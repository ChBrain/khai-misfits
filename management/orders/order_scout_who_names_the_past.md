# Order: Scout Who Names the Past

## Direction

The pointing was _the winners write history_, offered with two cases: the
Renaissance manufacturing the reputation of the Middle Ages, and the allocation
of blame for the Second World War. It is the second shape-shaped pointing in a
row, and it produced something the first could not.

**The slogan and the examples are different mechanisms.** Taken as a slogan the
candidate is The Survivors: the victors' record comes through and the losers'
does not, which is conditioning on survival, which this house has held since
early. Switch that off, stipulate a complete archive, and the slogan has nothing
left. **Both offered examples survive that switch-off untouched.** Nobody
destroyed the medieval archive. What Petrarch supplied was a frame, and the
frame's content was fixed by what its author needed to be true about himself: a
movement calling itself a rebirth has to assert a death.

So a pass that had deduped the slogan would have reported the region closed while
the region was open, and the correction is general: **run the switch-off on the
pointer's examples, never on the pointer's slogan.** A slogan is a compression,
and compressions drift toward whichever incumbent is most famous.

The pointing's gloss on the second example is worth correcting, and the
correction strengthens it rather than weakening it. The best-documented Second
World War case runs the other way from _took full blame_: the clean-Wehrmacht
account narrowed responsibility, because West German rearmament required an army
that had not been criminal. Article 231, where blame was assigned wholesale, is
the First World War and was drafted to ground a reparations claim. Opposite
directions, one engine, and nothing suppressed that had survived.

## Orders

Slate the surviving candidate P1 on the Playwright's standing slate,
[Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md), with its quantity, the compounding
step that makes it a trap rather than an observation, the seat that pays, the
reason no settlement is free, and the five cuts written as switch-off tests.

Site the candidate against Trouillot's four moments of historical production
rather than against the house's titles alone, since the first two moments are the
territory an incumbent already holds and the fourth is the one nothing holds.
Siting a candidate inside its own literature's decomposition is cheaper and
sharper than siting it against a list of house titles.

Record the verification state per source and never tidy an uncertain one up. Two
are confirmed bibliographically with their arguments corroborated from several
independent reports and neither was read in full. Two more are named as
candidates and are unverified at scout.

Carry the dissent into the slate line rather than leaving it for the author to
meet: the claim that _Dark Ages_ reflects Italian Renaissance attitudes sits in
tension with the phrase's near-absence from Italian before the twentieth century,
so the metaphor and the periodising move are Petrarch's and the English phrase
has a later history. Getting that wrong would overclaim the spine.

Record the methodological finding as an operating instruction, not as an
observation, since it is the second consecutive pass whose value was in how the
pointing was read rather than in what the sweep returned.
