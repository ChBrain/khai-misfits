# Order: Scout the Receiver Decides

## Direction

The pointing was communication science and _the receiver decides_, which in the
German-language tradition is Schulz von Thun's four-sides model: an utterance
carries factual content, self-revelation, a statement about the relationship and
an appeal, and the receiver chooses which ear to hear it with.

The region is open. Nothing in the house holds four sides, four ears, the Organon
model or the sender-receiver frame. But the probe returned **two constraints that
decide the candidate's shape before any authoring question is asked**, and both
would otherwise have been met at the pull request.

**The obvious spine is taken, and taken as a spine.** Watzlawick's _Pragmatics of
Human Communication_ is already cited by The Double Bind and marked SPINE, and
the whole Palo Alto group goes with it. A receiver-decides play cannot be built
on Watzlawick without failing the shared-work wall or being argued into the canon
list, and the canon exemption is for a field's foundational text shared across a
family rather than a device for reusing a spine.

**And the named model is a framework rather than a finding.** Schulz von Thun
assembled the four sides from Watzlawick's axiom and Bühler's Organon model, and
the standing criticism is that it oversimplifies. No empirical validation
surfaced. By the ordering rule this register already carries, that is a spine
question and not a coverage one.

So the line is slated P2 rather than P1, and its precondition is the spine.

## Orders

Slate the candidate P2 on the Playwright's standing slate,
[Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md), with the recursion that makes it a
trap stated plainly: a clarification is an utterance with the same sides, so
meta-communication iterates the structure rather than escaping it.

Run the cuts per distinctive feature rather than on the candidate, which is the
instruction the previous pass added, and record each at its true strength. The
cut against The Double Bind holds and is the good one. The cut against Anyone Can
See It appears to hold and wants care. The cut against The Illusion of Shared
Understanding is **contingent on the four-sidedness claim**, because grounding can
settle what was meant only if there is one thing meant, so it is not yet made and
is not makeable until the spine is.

Record that contingency as the precondition and as the whole precondition, rather
than listing it beside the others as though the three were alike.

Name where the spine would have to come from if it exists: not the diagram but
the pragmatics under it, Bühler's Organon model, Austin's illocutionary and
perlocutionary distinction, and relevance theory. Record which of those surnames
scanned clear and which is taken, so the next author starts from the scan rather
than repeating it.

Instruct a drop rather than an argument if none of those carries the
four-sidedness claim, because a play built on a teaching diagram is not a warrant.
