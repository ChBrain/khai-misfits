# Order: Scout Horsemanship

## Direction

The slate held six targets and no P1, every one carrying a cut to write or a condition
that could kill it. Two preceding passes had established that a domain named by
somebody who works in it outyields an instrument sweep by about ten to one.

This pass tests the edge of that rule, and widens it. The domain is horsemanship, which
the author knows from **outside** their profession, and it behaved exactly as the two
workplace domains did: one domain, one P1, on the same ratio. **The rule is therefore
not about employment but about knowing a practice from the inside**, and an avocation
qualifies on the same terms, which enlarges the pool of useful pointings considerably.

The domain is unusually unstaged. Its own vocabulary returns zero throughout, and so
does its mechanism vocabulary. The nearby terms that do appear belong to misfits about
something else entirely, none of which holds operant conditioning as a mechanism, so
the region is open rather than merely unnamed.

## Orders

Slate the candidate that survived the mechanism test as P1, carrying the inversion the
literature supplies rather than the weaker one the pass first assumed.

Record that the literature made the candidate **stronger** than the framing it arrived
with: a mistimed release does not merely fail to instruct, it punishes the response it
was asking for, and there is a described continuum from poorly timed negative
reinforcement into punishment with no boundary the rider can feel.

Slate the lesson horse at P2 with its precondition attached, and state plainly that the
cut against Alarm Fatigue may not be makeable, so the line is to be dropped rather than
argued if it cannot be cut clean.

Record the four further candidates the pass raised as **untested rather than struck**.
The pass tested one candidate hard rather than five lightly, and a strike asserted
without the switch-off test having been run is a claim. This register's value is that
its strikes are not claims.

Record the verification state honestly: the spine is verified and the specific journal,
volume and page details are not, and are to be fixed at authoring.

## Implementation

The batch is appended to [Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md) at the
thirty-seventh pass, in the register's established form: the refinement to the pointing
rule, the domain and its unstaged vocabulary, the incumbents that hold the nearby terms
for other mechanisms, the surviving candidate with the inversion the literature
supplies, the conditional second, and the four recorded untested.
