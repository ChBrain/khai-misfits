# Order: Scout the Third Pass

## Direction

The second scout came back five of ten invalid, and the reading of that failure was that the
candidates had been deduped by title rather than by source. That reading was right and incomplete. The
second batch also re-swept three of the first batch's five fields, so the duplicates were not bad luck
in the sifting: they were the predictable yield of returning to mined ground. This pass fixes both. The
domain is chosen against evidence rather than by taste, the roster probed before a scout is briefed so
that already-mined ground is ruled out in advance; and the candidate is cleared by its **leading author
surname first**, which is the single check four of the last batch's five duplicates would have failed.

Five scouts swept five fields orthogonal to the seven already worked: epidemiology and
causal-inference methodology, audit and accounting and ratings and compliance, international relations
and conflict and security studies, microbiology and immunology and antimicrobial resistance, and
climate and energy and environmental governance. Each was handed the two hundred and one canonical
concepts and the nine hundred and forty-one scholar surnames as lists, and each was required to write
out its surname check, its canonical-name check, and the cut against the two or three nearest staged
neighbours, dropping anything that could not clear all three. Citation honesty was made a hard
requirement, confirmed or uncertain marked per candidate, with the instruction never to tidy an
uncertain one up, because a warrant credits other people's work and a confidently wrong citation is
worse than no candidate at all.

The pass surfaced roughly forty-seven distinct candidates at a far lower duplicate rate than the last,
and every scout's surname claim was re-run against the files independently before anything was slated.
All five checked out, with no false clean claims.

## Orders

Slate the ten cleanest gaps on the Playwright's standing slate,
[Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md), tiered P1 and P2, two from each field so the roster does
not tilt, each carrying its concept, its source, its locally-rational reading, and the cut that clears
every staged misfit. Record the surplus as a reserve rather than discarding it, so the next reconcile
begins from a stocked slate and not a fresh sweep. Keep the rejections: what a pass refused, and
against which staged play, is the part of the record that stops the next scout re-proposing it.

Correct the brief where it failed. The held-off list handed to the five scouts was built by grepping
the plan and the grep missed a line, so Original Antigenic Sin and the Hygiene Hypothesis went out as
fair game though the plan already parks both. The microbiology scout read the plan itself rather than
the extract it was given and refused both on the house's own record. Both stay held, and the failure is
written down beside them.

Keep the three composition notes the scouts raised, since none of them is visible from a single
candidate: that the three epidemiology siblings are one mechanism told three ways and at most two
should ever be staged; that three further vaccine plays would tilt a house already carrying three; and
that the slated coup-proofing trap is adjacent to the coup-to-civil-war trade, which is to be staged
only if cut hard against it, or not at all.

## Implementation

The batch is appended to [Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md) at the two-hundred-and-one
reconcile, in the plan's established form: the pass and its method, then the correction, the reaffirmed
held and the retired-on-mechanism record, then the ten slated targets tiered P1 and P2, then the
reserve, the composition notes, and the honest remainder.

The P1 line is the Coup-Proofing Trap, Immortal Time Bias, Endogenous Risk, the Green Paradox, the
Conservation-Reward Paradox in antibiotics, and Competing Risks. The P2 line is the Market-Value
Decline of Variable Renewables, Bystander Selection, the Regulatory Licence, and the Alliance Security
Dilemma. Fifteen further candidates are held in the reserve, and four uncertain citations are named for
verification before they enter a warrant.

One finding is recorded above the rest because it is the method proving itself. The audit scout's
strongest first-pass candidate, ratings shopping, was killed by the scout itself on a full read of a
staged warrant: The Best on Paper claims the optimiser's-curse structure in general and not a domain,
so an issuer soliciting several ratings and publishing the best is that structure exactly, joined to
the selective disclosure The File Drawer already holds. The collision was invisible by name, by source,
and by field, and visible only in the mechanism. That is the dedup the first two passes did not have.
