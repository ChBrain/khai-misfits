# Order: Scout Automating Yourself Away

## Direction

The pointing was automating your own job away, put as a symmetry: succeed and
the role is gone, fail and the role is gone. It is the third shape-shaped
pointing in a row, and it decomposes further than anything this register has
handled. **Seven incumbents touch it.** That reads as a closed region and is not
one.

**A dense dedup is not a strike.** Seven incumbents touching a candidate is
evidence that the region matters, not evidence that it is covered, and the
temptation is to infer coverage from the count. The switch-off is still the only
instrument and it has to be run against each incumbent separately. Run that way,
all seven cleared.

**And the strike was avoided only by a literature search**, which is the
procedural finding. The dedup was complete and pointed at a strike. The search
found a named, peer-reviewed mechanism the dedup had no way to surface, because
none of the seven incumbents uses its vocabulary. **A dedup runs against the
house and can only say what the house holds; it cannot say whether a candidate
has a spine.** Those are two questions and this pass answered them in the wrong
order. It cost nothing here and it would have cost a misfit.

The pointing's symmetry is rhetorical, and saying so makes the case stronger
rather than weaker. Failing at a job and losing it is not a mechanism, it is
employment. The second arm is the **wall that closes the exit**: without it the
worker could decline to automate, and with it declining is legible as failure.
One trap and a closed door, not two traps, and only that reading admits a
no-fault account, since a symmetry would need somebody to have arranged it.

## Orders

Slate the surviving candidate P1 on the Playwright's standing slate,
[Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md), with its quantity, the inversion,
the loop that closes it, the seats that pay, the reason no settlement is free,
and the seven cuts, writing out the three that were closest.

Record the pointing's symmetry as rhetorical and give the better reading, so the
author does not try to build two mechanisms where the structure has one.

Record the density finding as an operating instruction rather than an
observation, and record the ordering finding with it: dedup against the house
answers coverage, and only the literature answers whether there is a spine, so
the literature goes first when the dedup is dense.

Record the verification state as thin, because it is. The foundational paper is
named from memory. The papers that carry the spine were seen only as search
listings with no author details. The trade-press survey figures are not warrant
and must not reach an Origin cell. State plainly that the whole Origin table is
owed a verification pass before authoring rather than a citation check after it.

Note the `Webster` collision now rather than at authoring, since the citation
form for a four-author paper is a decision with an index cost, as the
thirty-ninth pass recorded.
