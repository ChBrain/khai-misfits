# Order: Scout Further Afield

## Direction

The first scout swept five fields the staged set had barely entered and its ten
targets are now fully authored, the last struck alongside this order. This
second scout works the ground the first left standing: five fields chosen
for where the house is thinnest after that batch landed, law and
institutional design, distributed systems and networks and security,
medicine and clinical decision science, cognitive and social psychology, and
evolutionary and population biology, the last three returning to fields the
first scout touched but did not exhaust. Each scout was briefed with the
house standard, a structural trap where locally rational conduct sums to a
bad end with no villain, a real research spine credited and not claimed, a
costly remedy and no free hero, and a load-bearing vector no staged misfit
holds; each deduped its finds by engine, not by name, against the full one
hundred and ninety-six staged misfits and this slate's held-off list before
proposing. The pass surfaced roughly thirty distinct candidates. This order
records the scout and refills the slate with its ten cleanest.

triage note of method, then the slated targets tiered P1 and P2, each carrying

survivors lead as P1: metastable failures in distributed systems, the Will

ecological trap, the bystander effect, and the judgment-proof problem;

niche, more adjacent to a staged misfit, or needing more careful framing

here.
