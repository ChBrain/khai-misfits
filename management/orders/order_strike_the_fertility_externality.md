# Order: Strike the Fertility Externality

## Direction

The next open line carried a precondition and a drop instruction, and predicted
its own death: free-riding is the most heavily worked family in the house and a
fourth version of it is not a misfit. The line was right, and the source settles
it without any interpretive move being required.

Folbre's paper argues that the production of children's capabilities creates a
public good that cannot be priced, so that **individuals can free ride on the
efforts of parents in general and mothers in particular**. Those are the paper's
own words. The candidate is not merely annexable by the free-rider family: **its
literature names its mechanism as free-riding**, and the house stages four
versions of that already.

The distinctive features were tested one at a time and each was extinguished by a
different incumbent: the public-goods engine takes the under-provision, More
Mouths Than Meat takes the twenty-five-year dead time, Whoever Comes Instead
takes the non-identity twist, and the reflexive pension loop falls to Fixes that
Fail and to the public-goods engine both. Nothing survives.

**There is no residue, and that is worth saying.** The previous strike left a
successor candidate, and a register whose every strike produced one would teach
that a good candidate always hides inside a bad one. It does not.

## Orders

Strike the line by its own instruction, and record the source's own words rather
than the house's reading of them, since a strike grounded in what the paper calls
its mechanism needs no argument at all and is the cheapest kind to trust.

Record the switch-offs feature by feature rather than as a verdict on the
candidate, naming which incumbent extinguishes which feature, so that a later
author who thinks one of those features is the real spine can see it was tested
on its own.

Record that there is no residue, explicitly, against the pattern the previous
strike set.

Record the instruction this pass adds as the complement of the forty-first
pass's, and require them to be read together. That pass found seven incumbents
and correctly refused to strike; this one finds four and strikes. The count was
never the evidence in either direction. Touching is not annexing; extinguishing
is, and the switch-off must be pointed at each distinctive feature rather than at
the candidate as a whole, because a composite candidate can survive as a whole
while every part of it is separately owned.

## Implementation

The strike is appended in place on the Playwright's standing slate,
[Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md), beneath the line it closes, with
the source's wording, the four switch-offs written out, the explicit absence of a
residue, and the per-feature instruction stated against its complement.
