# Order: Resolve the Schoolmaster

## Direction

The P1 slate is empty, so the next work is the top open P2, and that line carried
an unresolved precondition and an explicit drop instruction. Resolving it either
way was the unit of work; arguing it was the one thing the line forbade.

**The precondition was wrong about which cut was hard.** It named Alarm Fatigue
and expected the candidate to die there. That cut holds comfortably: Alarm
Fatigue is a signal-detection result about thresholds and base rates, and a
beginner's constant grip is not a false alarm but a stimulus carrying no
information, so switching off the flood of false positives leaves the schoolmaster
untouched. Signal detection and habituation are different literatures.

**The annexer is a misfit that did not exist when the line was written.** It
Learns When You Stop was the same pass's P1, staged four misfits later, and its
quantity is the delay between the horse's try and the release. A beginner who
grips constantly never releases, so the schoolmaster is that misfit with the
delay set to infinity: switch the delay to zero and the harm goes quiet.

So the line is struck, obeying its own instruction, and the register gains
something it did not have. **A slate is a queue whose own output changes what is
left in it.** The thirty-seventh pass slated a P1 and a P2 from one domain and
staging the P1 closed the P2, which neither line could have recorded because the
annexing misfit was four builds in the future.

One reading of the dead line survived the switch-off, and it is a selection
mechanism rather than a learning one. It is recorded as a new candidate rather
than used to rescue the old one, because a rescue is the argument the drop
instruction forbids.

## Orders

Strike the line and record which cut was expected to kill it, which one did, and
why the expected one holds. A strike whose stated reason is wrong teaches the
next author the wrong thing even when the verdict is right.

Record the queue finding as a standing instruction rather than an observation:
before authoring a surviving slate line, dedup it against the misfits staged
since the line was written, not only against the house it was written against.
The older a line is, the more of the house it has never been checked against, and
the most likely annexer is a sibling slated beside it.

Raise the surviving reading as its own P2, marked untested and unwarranted, with
the switch-off it already passes written out and the work it still owes stated:
no literature has been opened, and the previous pass established that the dedup
answers coverage while only the literature answers whether there is a mechanism.

Note that the new candidate generalises past horses, and instruct that this be
checked first rather than last, since a shape claimed of simulators and practice
patients as well as lesson horses either has a spine in one of those literatures
or has none anywhere.

## Implementation

The strike and the successor are appended in place on the Playwright's standing
slate, [Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md), the strike beneath the line
it closes and the new candidate immediately after it, so the descent from one to
the other is readable without the register being searched.
