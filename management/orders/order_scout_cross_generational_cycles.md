# Order: Scout Cross-Generational Cycles

## Direction

The pointing was a **shape** and not a domain: things that repeat across
generations and appear inevitable. Every pass since the thirty-fifth has taken a
domain, and the rule those built is roughly one candidate per pointed domain
against roughly one per ten from an instrument sweep. **A shape is a different
instrument and the difference is structural.** A shape is what this house
collects, so the house already holds its best instances, and the probe confirmed
it at once: the financial forgetting cycle, the levee effect, shifting baselines
and fire suppression are all staged.

What is unstaged in this region is unstaged for a reason worth carrying: the
grand cycle theories, which are exactly what the word cycle attracts, mostly do
not survive the citation standard. **So a shape pass spends its effort on
striking rather than on finding**, and its strikes are the deliverable. Three
were raised and three were struck, at three different strengths, and recording
which strength each was struck at is the point: one on tested and failed
predictions, one on unfalsifiable method, one on the contested existence of the
phenomenon itself.

One candidate survived, and it survived because its mechanism is not the one the
adjacent incumbents run. A successful preventive programme does not change the
hazard, does not invite exposure and accumulates nothing. **It removes the
evidence for itself**, leaving its own honestly reported harms as the only
adverse events anybody can still see, and the period of the resulting cycle is
set by a cohort with no memory of the disease becoming the cohort that decides.

The pass also owes a correction to its predecessor, which read a reading failure
as a property of frontier domains when it is a property of the network.

## Orders

Slate the surviving candidate P1 on the Playwright's standing slate,
[Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md), with its quantity, the inversion,
the empirical row that measures the return leg, the seat that pays, the reason no
settlement is free, and the cuts against all five incumbents that look adjacent,
naming for each what is switched off and what survives.

State the prerequisite in full and in order. It is three pull requests deep,
which is the most this register has recorded, and the order is fixed by the
contract's table rather than by preference: the cell repair rides the misfit
lane, the declaration is governance, the misfit comes last, and nothing can be
folded because the guard will not put two lanes on one branch. Run the probe
rather than assuming the cost.

Strike the three grand-cycle candidates and record the **strength** of each
strike, with what would bring the strongest of them back.

Correct the thirty-eighth pass's reading of its own reading failures. At n equals
three it inferred a property of unstaged domains. At n equals five, one of them
open access, the cause is the session's egress policy, and the narrow reading
would mislead the next author into thinking familiar ground reads better.

Record that the Source cell's author list is index work as well as credit work,
since a many-authored paper's citation form decides how much namesake machinery
the pass must pay for.

## Implementation

The batch is appended to [Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md) at the
thirty-ninth pass, in the register's established form: the shape-against-domain
finding with the probe that shows it, the surviving candidate with its five cuts
written out, the three strikes at their three strengths, the prerequisite stated
in order, the correction to the previous pass, and the note on citation form.
