# Order: Scout the Neighbouring Fields

## Direction

The warrant-mine that filled the season worked the house's own REFERENCES, the
concepts each misfit is cut against; this order works the ground beyond them.
Five scouts swept five bodies of work the staged set had barely entered:
economics and mechanism design, distributed systems and control theory,
evolutionary and population biology, cognitive and social psychology, and the
theory of organizations. Each was briefed with the house standard, a structural
trap where locally rational conduct sums to a bad end with no villain, a real
research spine credited and not claimed, a costly remedy and no free hero, and a
load-bearing vector no staged misfit holds; and each deduped its finds by engine,
not by name, against the one hundred and eighty-six staged misfits, the
house-wide concordance, and the slate's own held-off list. The pass surfaced
roughly twenty distinct gaps, concentrated where the house is thinnest:
genetic-conflict biology, distributed-systems failure, and public-administration
theory. This order records the scout and refills the slate with its ten cleanest.

## Orders

[Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md), tiered P1 and P2, each with its
source and the cut that clears every staged misfit, worked from the top as every
batch is. Strike the stale: the slate's last open target, The Spotlight Effect,

to weigh.

## Implementation

The refill batch is appended to [Fill the Season](../plan_fill_the_season.md) at
the one-hundred-and-eighty-six reconcile, in the plan's established form: a triage
note of method, strike, and held, then the slated targets tiered P1 and P2, each
carrying the concept, its source, and its distinct-from cut. The strongest,
cleanest survivors lead as P1: Myerson and Satterthwaite's bilateral-trading
impossibility, costly signaling and the handicap principle, naive realism, the
garbage-can model, leaky-vaccine hypervirulence, and split-brain under partition;
the diamond paradox, Niskanen's budget-maximizing bureau, parent-offspring and
imprinting conflict, and reactive devaluation follow as P2. Roughly ten further
gaps the scout surfaced are held for a later reconcile as borderline against a
staged misfit, recorded in the pass notes and not slated here.
