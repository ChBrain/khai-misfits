# Plan: Find the Next One

## Taxonomy

A **procedure**: how the house finds the misfit it does not yet hold. The
Playwright holds it beside [Cut to Fit](plan_cut_to_fit.md), which sets how many
elements a misfit carries, and [Name It Once](plan_name_it_once.md), which sets
what each is called. Those two govern a play being written; this one governs
finding the play to write.

## Owner

- Owner: [The Playwright](position_playwright.md)

## Direction

**Six selectors, and the point of writing them down is the yield.** Every one of
them was worked out inside [Fill the Season](plan_fill_the_season.md), which is
now over a megabyte, so a selector rediscovered is a pass spent. Each entry below
says what the selector searches, what it has actually returned, and how it fails.

**1. The field count.** Count the misfits per field and read the thin ones. It
**orders a search and never answers one**: a thin field is a place to look, not a
gap to fill, and the register has said so of every counting instrument since.

**2. The domain survey.** Read the **place titles**, not the `voice:` line, which
describes tone and sorts uselessly. Roughly seven hundred places across the house,
and their frequent heads are institutional: the floor, the ledger, the table, the
review. Probing sixty domain markers returned forty empty and **one candidate**.
The discipline that makes the list readable is that **an absent domain is not an
absent mechanism**: the parole board's risk instrument is Metric Fixation, the
abandoned mine is Nothing to Lose. The list is a reading list and not a backlog.

**3. The concept list.** Take an external list of named effects and check each
against the house, rather than probing the house with a candidate's vocabulary.
One pass returned **four candidates**, all of which reached the stage. Most of the
list was already staged under the house's own titles, which is the ordinary
result and not a failure.

**4. `--families`.** Rank the canon works two or more misfits share and read the
members with no axis. The rationale is that misfits citing one work are in one
conversation, which is where an opposed pair would hide. **The yield is low and
the reason is instructive**: a shared conversation is exactly where an author
staging a second play had to justify it, so the distinctness paragraphs cluster
inside families. It is a high-yield selector for **finding an argument already
written** and a low-yield one for finding a new axis member.

**5. The empty end of a singleton dial.** For each axis carrying one play, ask
what happens at the other end and whether that is a play. This produced the
house's tightest oppositions, and it carries **two tests, both cheap and both
required**. The dial must be harmful at both ends **with different engines**,
since a one-dial-two-bad-ends structure has no sign and cannot declare. And the
play at the occupied end **must not already carry the other end as a vector**: a
production that stages a dilemma properly holds both horns, so read its **No Hero
paragraph and whatever it calls its lever** before slating anything. Most
singletons have one harmful end only and will stay singletons however long the
ratchet runs, which is a property of the dials and not a coverage failure.

**6. The joiner, by the dial.** Read the undeclared list for plays that would sit
on an axis the house already has. It declares rather than scouts, and it has found
pairs no warrant made, because two plays can share a quantity from different
literatures and neither author would meet the other. **Count the candidates read,
not the declarations made**: a pass that reads twenty and declares six has done
the same work as one that declares twelve and has half of them wrong.

## Orders

**A description proposes a candidate and only the warrant confirms one.** Expect
roughly half of what a one-line description suggests to be refused on reading, and
do the refusing before committing rather than after.

**Test the engine and not the domain, and know the hard form.** The easy form is a
new field for a staged mechanism. The hard form is a **resemblance**: two
structures can share their whole silhouette and run on different quantities, which
has caught three passes in a row. A ceiling that makes war unthinkable looks
exactly like a shield handing an actor a margin, and is not one.

**One engine in two domains is a row, not a play.** It belongs in an Origin table.
**And where two plays pass each other's switch-offs but still share a room, the
separation has to be found in the staging**: set the candidate where the shared
circumstance arises for an unshared reason, and if it can only be staged in the
incumbent's room it is a row whatever the logic says.

**A candidate's number of incumbents is not known until somebody tries to write
it.** A slate line records the neighbour the scouting pass could see; the settling
pass is the first to hold the mechanism in its hands and routinely finds more.

**Scan the surnames before authoring and again at the end.** The pre-authoring
scan reads the sources you planned and understates; the second source set is where
the namesakes are. Read the whole output of `science surname`, never its last
line: for a clear surname that is one line and for a taken one it hides the count
and the declaration state. **A slate line must carry the shape of the namesake
obligation and not the fact of one**, because the arm of the ordering table is the
whole instruction and "not clear" is worth nothing to whoever discharges it.

**A prohibition is not a task.** A slate line that nothing can complete is an
instruction and belongs in prose, or every refill pass reads it and decides again
that there is nothing to do.

## Implementation

The slate and the register are both [Fill the Season](plan_fill_the_season.md),
which rides the lane of the change it drives. Declaring an axis is `misfits/**`
and is the misfit lane; declaring a surname is `khai-guard.config.json` and is
governance, so **one namesake obligation cannot be discharged in one pull
request** and the ordering table decides which half goes first.

A pass runs one selector, not several. The register records the yield whether it
is six or zero, because **a refusal recorded is worth more than a candidate
slated**: four of the last ten passes turned on a candidate the warrants refused,
and each refusal is a distinction the house now holds.

## Targets

- [ ] every selector above has been run at least twice, so its yield is measured
      rather than estimated
- [ ] a pass that scouts records the number of candidates read, not only those
      slated
- [ ] no candidate is slated without its nearest incumbent named and its
      switch-off written
- [ ] no candidate is authored without the surname scan run twice, before and
      after
- [ ] the register stops being the only place a selector is written down, which is
      what this plan is for
