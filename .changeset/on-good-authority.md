Add the misfit On Good Authority, a staging of the Woozle effect (evidence by citation): a claim gains the look of established evidence not from data but from being cited and re-cited, each author trusting a prior source in good faith, so a weak or unsupported assertion accretes a dense network of references until it reads as settled fact, and belief in it is manufactured by the count of citations and not the weight of the evidence, no villain required since no one fabricates and to cite a source is the ordinary practice that makes the work possible. Held against its bound and hedged: this is a mechanism attested in case analyses of specific literatures and documented instances rather than a measured rate at which literatures distort, and a dense citation record is more often earned than manufactured, so the house stages the structure, that a claim can be cited into authority with no villain, and not a licence to distrust every well-cited fact.
