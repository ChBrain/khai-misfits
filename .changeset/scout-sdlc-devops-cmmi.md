Scout SDLC, DevOps and CMMI-Dev: the thirty-sixth pass, three more domains pointed by
the author.

Three domains produced one P1, against the thirty-fifth's one from one and the
thirty-fourth's one from ten. The ratio is the thing to carry: a pointed probe yields
about one candidate per domain, an instrument sweep about one per ten.

Most of the ground was already staged. Technical debt is Shifting the Burden, alerting
on everything is Alarm Fatigue, delivery metrics and maturity levels becoming targets
are Metric Fixation, automation nobody understands is The Irony of Automation, and the
DevOps team that becomes a third silo is an Origin row in The Mirror, Conway's law
applied to the remedy being still Conway's law. The long-lived branch is held with its
cut owed, since switching off sunk cost and short horizons takes most of the
postponement with them.

Slated: The Control Nobody Can Remove. A defined process accumulates practices, each
added for a real reason, and none is removed, because adding and removing are accounted
differently: an addition is distributed across small increments nobody signs for, and a
removal is one act with a named owner. One direction is a decision and the other is a
drift, and omission bias supplies the measured half.

The pass also found the Source-column defect a third time, in the form no wall can
catch: the index reads one column, so it records who the house cites there rather than
who the house cites. Register only; ships no package content.
