Five, or a Layer: the span of control trade.

There is a number of people one person can attend to and it is small. Graicunas worked out in 1933
why: what a manager handles is the relationships among the reports, which go 1, 6, 18, 44, 100, 244
as the reports go one to six. So an organisation faces one choice. Widen the span, and each person
gets a slice of a week already committed. Narrow it, and a layer goes in, and a layer exists
precisely so that less reaches the person above.

The loss needs nobody's interest. Give the summary's writer nothing to defend and no reason to shade

worth reading, so value and loss are one quantity.
