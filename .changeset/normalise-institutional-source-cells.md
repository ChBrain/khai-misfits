Normalise two institutional Source cells so the science index stops manufacturing scholars out of them. The forward map keys each Origin row by parsing its Source cell as an author list and taking the last word of each part, so a cell naming a body rather than a person is keyed on whatever institutional word happens to end it. Plausible Deniability cited "Church Committee" and was filed under a scholar named **Committee**; The Driest Wells cited "DHSS Working Group (the Black Report)" and was filed under a scholar named **Group**. Both sat in the index beside real surnames, and neither is one. The house already has a pattern for a source that is a record rather than a person, used by The Coinage in Loudest When Hushed and by the historical record of Semmelweis's finding in No Place in the Theory: end the cell in an ordinary lowercase noun, which the parser drops cleanly, and let the body's proper name sit inside the phrase where it reads as a citation and not as an author. The cells now read "The Church Committee record" and "The Black Report of the DHSS working group". No citation changes and no staging changes: the same reports are credited to the same bodies, spelled the same way, and only the shape of the phrase has moved, so the scholar count falls from nine hundred and forty-three to nine hundred and forty-one and both misfits keep their full entry in the per-misfit listing. These two are pre-existing, older than the recent batch, and are the same class of fault as the Project cell corrected alongside them and the Escape cell corrected once before in The Double Bind.
