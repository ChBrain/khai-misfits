Stage **Back to Even** (the disposition effect): because the price paid becomes the reference point and people are risk-averse over gains but risk-seeking over losses, investors sell winners too early and ride losers too long, so returns and taxes suffer. Credited to Shefrin & Statman, Odean, and Tversky & Kahneman, held with the tax-loss-selling bound.
