Add the misfit No Need to Ask, a staging of the political resource curse (the rentier state): a state funded by resource rents need not tax its citizens, so it need not answer to them, and the fiscal bargain that ties taxation to representation and forces accountable institutions is severed, so accountability atrophies and the windfall leaves governance weaker, no villain required.
