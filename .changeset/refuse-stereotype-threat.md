Read the state of the evidence on **stereotype threat**, as its slate line required, and **refuse it on the sourcing rule**. The line said the shape was good and the literature contested, and that the pass should be prepared to refuse on sourcing rather than on mechanism. It refuses on sourcing.

**The refusal is not that the literature is contested but that no direction survives the house's own test.** A warrant states a governing law drawn from a source's finding, and the two branches a play would rest on do not supply one. On the gender-and-mathematics branch, two meta-analyses report strong publication bias and a pre-registered replication in Dutch high schools, N of about two thousand, found neither an overall effect among girls nor any of the four theorised moderators. On the founding race branch, Sackett, Hardison and Cullen showed the 1995 result is routinely read as saying something it does not: the scores were adjusted for prior SAT, so the finding is that the gap absent threat is the gap the prior scores predict, and not that the gap closes. Serious defenders remain and the field is not dead, which is exactly the problem: **a warrant cannot state as a governing law a claim whose best-powered test returns null**, and a play whose Restrictions would have to say the effect may not exist has no engine to stage.

One ordering finding. The surname scan ran first, by habit, and flagged a `Quinn` collision the play would have created, Diane Quinn against the Kevin Quinn already in You Would Have to Tell. That obligation never had to be discharged, because the sourcing read killed the play. **Where a candidate carries a sourcing caution, the sourcing read is the cheaper filter and belongs before the surname scan**, and the same applies to the deindividuation line still on the slate. Ships nothing.
