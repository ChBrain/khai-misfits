The Last One to Answer: the tail at scale, where a request that needs every component is timed by the slowest of them,
so a rare per-machine delay becomes the ordinary case as the fan-out widens. Staged on Dean and Barroso's account of
tail latency and on Vulimiri and colleagues' general result that redundancy buys latency back.
