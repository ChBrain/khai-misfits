Scouts, because the slate had nothing left to author, and the field-count selector picked the field in one command.

**The slate held one authoring candidate and it carries a sourcing caution.** Eleven passes have gone by since the last play was staged, and the reason is not neglect: there was nothing to build. **A slate that empties of candidates is a slate that has stopped being refilled.**

**The register can compute where the house is thin, and this pass finally ran it.** Measurement 87, Markets 61, Safety 45, Collective action 41, Organisations 39, Ecology 38, **Groups, candour and face 28**, Senge 5.

**Twenty-eight concepts read.** The field is dense in one direction — what gets said, what gets withheld, how the saying is read — and empty in another: **no play in the house casts the group's memory.** Every memory play here is individual: shifting baselines, familiarity, face exposure, peak and end.

Five uncast seats considered, one taken forward: **collaborative recall**, which has a literature and no incumbent. A group meets to reconstruct an event, which is what every debrief is for. **Collaborative inhibition** — hearing others recall disrupts each person's retrieval order, so the group produces less than the same people separately (Weldon & Bellinger, 1997). **Memory conformity** — each account is exposed to the others before it is fixed, so one member's error is taken up and later recalled as first-hand (Roediger, Meade & Bergman, 2001).

**And the harm needs no claim about anybody's confidence.** The convergence is read afterwards as corroboration, by everyone receiving two agreeing accounts and by the group itself, and the agreement was manufactured by the meeting rather than observed. That is a claim about the _reader_ of the accounts, so the play can stand on two documented arms and carry no finding the pass could not source.

Nearest incumbents named: **Too Sure to Be True** (there the observations were independent and the agreement is the anomaly; here pooling _makes_ them dependent) and **The Trip to Abilene** (there a record of what is voiced misread as what is held; here a record of what is recalled together misread as what was seen apart).

Switch-off written before the authoring: elicit each account independently and in writing before anybody speaks, and both arms die. **Which is exactly what a debrief is not.**

Ships nothing.
