Declare Kelly and Barnett as shared surnames: two keys become four people.

`Kelly` is Dawn Kelly, with Amburgey and Barnett in As Good as New, against Kevin
Kelly in The Shirky Principle. `Barnett` is William P Barnett, on the same paper,
against Michael L Barnett in The Youngest in the Room. Both arrived with As Good as
New, and until now each carried two people under one bare key, which is what an
undeclared surname does: it collates, and it passes quietly rather than failing.

The pair was split rather than held together, as `Wilson` and `List` were. `Barnett`
owed nothing, both its cells having been written with given names already. `Kelly`
owed a cell, because The Shirky Principle's Source column read `Shirky, Kelly` with
the given names in the Key Work column where the index cannot see them; that repair
landed separately on the misfit lane it belonged to, and this declaration follows it.
