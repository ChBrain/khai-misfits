Add the misfit The Price Neither Will Name: a staging of the Myerson-Satterthwaite impossibility, where a buyer values a thing above what a seller would take for it, so a real gain sits between them, but each privately knows only its own value and shades toward its own side, the buyer stating below its ceiling and the seller above its floor, each the best reply and neither a lie, so the two figures need not cross though the true values would and the deal both would gain from dies in the gap. And it cannot be designed away: to make honest reporting the best reply any rule must concede each side an information rent, and on the marginal trade the two rents together exceed the gain, so no voluntary, truthful, budget-balanced rule clears every warranted trade, and efficiency is reachable only by an outside subsidy that breaks the balance of the books or by an equal ex-ante ownership, with no one cheating and no rule at fault. Cut to the private-information-forecloses-the-gain vector no staged misfit holds, distinct from Over a Barrel (no sunk specific investment, a one-shot arm's-length trade), The Market for Lemons (two-sided hidden value, not one-sided hidden quality), Loath to Part (a real overlap exists, not an inflated true value), and After You (both sides present and trading, not a coordination failure of timing), and held to its bound, since the impossibility needs two-sided private value over overlapping ranges and lifts under one-sided information, a known value, guaranteed gains, or an outside purse (Myerson and Satterthwaite; Myerson; Vickrey, Clarke, and Groves; Cramton, Gibbons, and Klemperer).
