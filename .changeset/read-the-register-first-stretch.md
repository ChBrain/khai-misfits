Runs the first stretch of the register read the slate has carried unstarted, and **seven of seven rules found had never reached a plan**.

**The filter is the reusable part.** A standing rule here is bold, at least eight words, carries a general marker, **contains no proper noun** past the first word, and is framed on the work rather than the subject — its subject is a pass, a warrant, a candidate, an axis, an instrument, a lane, a count. That cuts the pre-session register to **130** candidates, which is readable, where the raw count of 437 the slate quoted is not.

**The stretch is a rank, not a line range**, which corrects the slate's method. Each of the 130 was scored by how much of its vocabulary already appears in the six other plans, and the pass read the 45 lowest. Line ranges read whatever they happen to hold; the score orders the set by the thing being looked for. Next stretch is ranks 46–130.

The seven: a candidate that needs a wrongdoer is not a structure (and its positive form, no villain available being the candidate's strength); where an incumbent's remedy is what the candidate cannot obtain, the two are not competitors; a candidate whose remedy is undone in proportion to how well it works is not a coordination failure; a bound is a confession, so mine the file where authors record what they could not settle; a row that cannot be traced is dropped whatever it would have added; where the mechanism has moved, go back to the original rather than forward to the next. Four carried to Find the Next One, three to Cut to Fit.

**One of the seven is a rule this session re-derived and shipped as new.** _A shared word is the commonest false positive in this search_ was recorded eighty passes ago, with the test that settles it. Two passes ago the same thing was found again from the concept-line instrument, called a cross-field pun, and written into the same plan as a finding. Fourth re-derivation this run, and the first measured rather than noticed.

Two smaller corrections: the slate said four plans and there are seven; and the register holds **no standing rule at all before line three thousand** — 160 bold spans, zero rule-shaped — so a method is what a register grows, not what it starts with.

Ships nothing.
