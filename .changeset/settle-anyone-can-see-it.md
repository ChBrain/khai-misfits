Settle the slated question: **Anyone Can See It does not join The Trip to Abilene**, and the test that decides it also **corrects what the last pass asserted about it**. That pass wrote that both plays die under a stipulation of perfect mutual insight, which is the mark of a shared dial. It said so without running the stipulation, and it is false.

**Run it properly and only one dies.** Give every party perfect knowledge of the others' inner states. Pluralistic ignorance is gone: nobody can misread an assent that is fully legible. Naive realism is **untouched**, because its three tenets are about the self, not the other: I see reality as it is, a rational person would agree, and one who does not is biased. Knowing your mental state completely, I learn that you sincerely hold the other view, and the first tenet is undisturbed, so I conclude you are the distorted one. That is the harm, arrived at from perfect insight into you. **The quantity in Abilene is what a party can observe about others, and the quantity in naive realism is what a party can observe about itself**, and the second is the one the play needs.

**And the reading turned up Abilene's real nearest relative, which is not naive realism at all.** Safe to Say stages preference falsification, and its law is the same shape: each conceals, sees only the safe professions of others, believes itself more isolated than it is, and conceals the harder. Abilene's own law names the same asymmetry, that a voiced yes adds to the apparent consensus while a swallowed no never subtracts from it. But **stipulate that dissent carries no social cost and only one of those dies too.** Safe to Say needs the cost and is gone without it; Abilene runs at the costless end by construction, since Harvey's point is that people accommodate where there is nothing to fear. **Abilene is pinned at low cost and does not move along that dial**, which is the third case that rule has decided. Its engine is the observability asymmetry itself, and the house holds no play whose dial is that. Ships nothing.
