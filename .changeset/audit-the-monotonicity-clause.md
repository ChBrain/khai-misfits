Audits the register for the monotonicity clause the last pass thought it had used for the first time, and corrects the slate line that asked for the audit.

**The line said the clause had never once decided a row. It has decided at least eleven, since the sixty-sixth pass, under four names that never referred to each other.** This is the third correction in a run to a claim written into a slate line rather than into a finding, and the second by a pass that had already recorded the rule about it. Knowing the failure mode is not the same as being outside it.

The four names: **flat, not signed** (Pushing on a String, a response with no direction at the bound); **the second test, or holding both horns** (a dial harmful at both ends — five of six refusals in a single pass, and the most common refusal in the register); **the pinned rule** (four cases, not the three the register claims); and **monotonicity called by its name**, once, last pass.

**But the last pass had found something, and the audit says which half.** An axis wants one quantity in which each harm is monotone, and that is two requirements. _Does the harm move when the dial moves, in one direction?_ catches pinned, flat and both horns, and was already running under three names. _Does the harm stay put when the dial stays put?_ catches nothing those names catch, and was genuinely first used last pass. The first asks whether the harm responds to the dial; the second whether it responds to nothing else. Both carried into Find the Next One, replacing the pinned rule's paragraph with the pair it belongs to.

**And the pinned rule's own use-count is wrong in two places.** Good Enough to Leave was first and The Peace It Keeps second; the next pass called Better in Both "the second, after Good Enough to Leave", dropping The Peace It Keeps, and the pass after inherited the drop for Abilene. A running count kept in an append-only register is kept by whoever wrote it last, because the cheap way to find the number is to read the nearest previous statement rather than to count the set.

Ships nothing.
