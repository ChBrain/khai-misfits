Add the misfit **How Many They Have Done**: a hospital performs a complex operation four times a year, by a surgeon who trained properly, keeps up and does each one carefully, and its results are worse than a centre doing two hundred. Nothing about anybody is deficient; the thing that is missing is the operations they did not do. The capability is held by a group rather than a person, is made of repetitions, and decays in the months between cases, so four a year is a level and not a slow climb. Every party is right on its own terms: the referral goes to the nearest capable service because that is what patients ask for and nothing in a referral names volume, and the hospital holds the service because the emergency cover, the training rotation and the staff go with it.

Joins `knowledge-concentration` as a positive against The Bus Factor's negative, and both warrants name each other. The pair is as tight as the mechanism produces, since each play's remedy is the other's harm: spread the work and nobody accumulates enough of it to be good at it, concentrate it and the institution cannot survive their leaving. The discriminator is what the concentration is asked to produce, capability or continuity, which is the same routing decision judged against two obligations every organisation holds. Distinct from Only as Good as the Worst, since stipulating the tasks fully separable leaves the harm standing, and from The Ninth Woman, since nine surgeons who have each done four is not one who has done thirty-six. Also regularises `Gary S. Becker` to `Gary Becker`, the one cell of five that spelled him differently.
