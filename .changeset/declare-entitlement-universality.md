Declare `entitlement-universality` on **Too Much to Ask** at a positive sign, which fills the empty end of an axis **Nobody Was Left Out** has held alone, and the two now name each other. There a legislature funds a smaller class for every district on the same terms in the same year, refusing to write down which children wait, and the claim lands on a stock of teachers that takes years to make. Here the entitlement is gated instead, each gate a defensible step against a real fraud or error, so the claiming cost accumulates and screens the eligible by their capacity to bear it.

What sets the sign is what the entitlement is rationed by. A universal claim is rationed by the supply of the thing it buys and goes to whoever can attract that supply; a gated claim is rationed by the cost of proving eligibility and goes to whoever can bear that cost. Neither allocation is the need the scheme was written for, and each is the settlement the other refuses. Also gives `entitlement-universality` its sign comment, which it was missing.
