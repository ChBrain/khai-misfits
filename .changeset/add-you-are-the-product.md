Add the misfit **You Are the Product** (the audience commodity): an advertising-funded medium sells not its content but its audience, so its paying customer is the advertiser and its product is the reader, and it rationally shapes what it makes to gather and hold the audience advertisers will pay for, the reader's own interest served only where it coincides with attention-capture. After Smythe (1977; 1981), with Jhally & Livant on watching-as-working, Wu's Attention Merchants for the field, and the advertising filter of Herman & Chomsky credited narrowly to its structural claim, not the press-conspiracy reading. Held to its bound: the tilt bites where the funding is advertising and interests diverge, softening where they coincide or where the reader or public becomes the payer.
