Add the misfit **Only as Good as the Worst** (the O-ring theory): production as a chain of complementary tasks whose qualities multiply, so one weak link cuts the whole, the skilled are matched with the skilled, small skill gaps widen into large wage gaps, and the low-skill are stranded out of high-value work. After Kremer (1993) and Becker (1973), held to its bound: the trap is as strong as production is multiplicative and softens where tasks are substitutable or redundant.
