Add the misfit **Stranger Than True** (the differential spread of true and false news): people share what they find novel and surprising, and false stories, unconstrained by what is true, are on average more novel than true ones, so each rational share of the surprising amplifies falsehood over truth and the false travels farther, faster, and deeper, with no deceiver and no bot required. After Vosoughi, Roy & Aral (Science, 2018), with Berger & Milkman on the sharing motive and the lie-outruns-truth folk saying and Brandolini's bullshit asymmetry for the lag. Held to its bound: a tendency strongest in political news, novelty the authors' best-supported explanation, false more likely to spread and not all falsehood spreading; people prefer the novel, not the false.
