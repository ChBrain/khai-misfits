Add the misfit **Whatever You Call It**: a word for something shameful has become an insult, a better one is chosen by people acting for those it names, it works for a decade, and then a child uses it in a playground as an insult and the whole exercise begins again. The connotation is stored in the thing and not in the syllables, so any expression that reliably points at the thing collects it, and pointing reliably is the whole job. The three properties a replacement needs, clarity, adoption and sincere use, are the complete recipe for building the association that spoils it, so there is no more careful version. Nobody misused the word: the child learned it from adults using it correctly, and what it now means includes what people feel about its subject.

Distinct from The Hollow Word, since stipulating a perfectly stable referent makes this worse rather than absent; from Prevalence-Induced Concept Change, since the concept's boundary does not move at all; from Metric Fixation, since stipulating that nobody is optimising anything leaves the harm untouched, the contamination being done by ordinary sincere reference; and from More to Stand Still, since the word does not fade, it acquires a definite content it did not start with. Declares the axis `referent-stigma`, negative.
