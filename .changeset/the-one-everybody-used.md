The One Everybody Used: the popular sire effect, where a closed breed register narrows in proportion to how well its
own health evidence identifies the best animal. Staged on Calboli, Sampson, Fretwell and Balding's pedigree analysis of
ten breeds and Lewis, Abhayaratne and Blott's trends across all 215 Kennel Club breeds.
