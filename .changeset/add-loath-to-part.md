Stage **Loath to Part** (the endowment effect): merely owning a good raises the price one demands to give it up above the price one would pay to acquire the same good, so mutually beneficial trades go unmade and goods stick with their first holders. Credited to Thaler, Kahneman/Knetsch/Thaler, and Tversky & Kahneman, held with the List boundary.
