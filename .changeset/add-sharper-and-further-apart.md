Add the misfit Sharper and Further Apart: a staging of motivated numeracy, an instance of identity-protective cognition, where analytical skill is deployed to reach the conclusion a reasoner's identity requires rather than the one the evidence supports, so the most capable reasoners diverge furthest and shared evidence widens the disagreement it was meant to settle. Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic put a two-by-two contingency table to subjects whose numeracy had been measured separately. The table is a genuine trap, since the raw counts point one way and the ratios the other, so a reader who compares the large numbers instead of dividing arrives backwards. Labelled as a trial of a skin-rash cream it behaved as an ability test should, the more numerate getting it right and the less numerate caught by the counts. Relabelled, with the identical cells and the identical arithmetic, as a study of a gun-control ban, the answers sorted by politics rather than by skill and the gap between opposed groups was widest among the most numerate, so the capability that corrected the error in the neutral frame amplified the division in the charged one, without a single faked calculation. The mechanism is that being right about a table is worth almost nothing to an individual while being out of step with their own community costs standing, trust, and belonging, so the identity-consistent belief is the sound personal investment and a reasoner with more analytical power simply has more of it to spend on that investment. The remedy, stripping the identity cue so the data arrives unflagged, is the finding's own control condition and demonstrably works, and is close to useless where it is most wanted, since the questions a society must settle arrive already flagged, cannot be laundered, and are discounted by any audience that suspects the framing was managed. Cut to the skill-serves-the-side vector no staged misfit holds, distinct from Anyone Can See It (naive realism fails at the level of perception, each side unable to see it is construing; here both sides compute correctly whenever nothing rides on the answer), The Filter Bubble (there the information supply differs; here it is held identical by construction, the same cells on one page, so the divergence is produced entirely downstream of the evidence), Too Sure to Be True (there the signal is the absence of disagreement; here disagreement is the outcome and its correlation with skill is the finding), and Don't Look (there the trap is avoidance; here the reasoner engages the data harder than anyone and the engagement does the damage), and held clear of The Commons despite the source literature's own "tragedy of the science communication commons" phrasing, since nothing is depleted and no shared stock is drawn down. Held to its bound with the magnitude carried lightly: it needs a live identity stake and a question able to carry one, its size and generality are argued over in the literature, the finding is symmetric across sides and is staged symmetrically, and nothing here claims numeracy is generally a liability (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic; Kahan and the Cultural Cognition Project; Lord, Ross and Lepper).
