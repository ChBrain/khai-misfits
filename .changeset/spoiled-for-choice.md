Add the misfit Spoiled for Choice, a staging of the paradox of choice: past a point more options do not free the chooser but overwhelm, so a larger assortment lowers the odds of choosing at all and the satisfaction of the choice made, each option added in good faith to serve a varied taste summing to paralysis and regret, no villain required. Held against its bound and hedged hard: the effect is genuinely contested, a large meta-analysis finding no reliable average and later work locating it only under specific conditions of choice-set complexity, task difficulty, and preference uncertainty, so the strong more-is-less claim is overstated and the effect conditional.
