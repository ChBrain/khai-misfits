Retire Long After the Cause as a duplicate of Held Under, and fold its one additive vector into Held Under instead. Long After the Cause was authored from the refilled scout slate as a staging of metastable failure in distributed systems, but the house already stages that phenomenon as Held Under, on the same spine: Bronson and colleagues' metastable-failure framework, Huang and colleagues' field study, and Nagle's and Jacobson's congestion collapse. The duplication went undetected because the dedup was run against titles rather than against the canonical concept, and Held Under's title does not name its phenomenon; the slate's own struck entry recorded the staging and was not consulted. The misfit is removed and the count returns to 196.

What the duplicate did carry that Held Under did not is the hysteresis, and it is kept: Held Under gains **The Gap**, the distance between the load that tips the system into the failing state and the far lower load at which it could climb back out, since it must now clear the fresh load and the retries together. Between the two thresholds both states exist, and which one holds is set by where the system has been rather than by the load arriving now. The Gap is what makes waiting useless at any duration, since a return to ordinary traffic is nowhere near low enough and the system fails under a load it carried without noticing the day before, and it is what sets the price of the escape, since the Breaker must be thrown further and held longer than the situation appears to warrant and a proportionate cut leaves the loop fully fed. The piece is wired into The Hold, where it explains why the state does not clear itself, and into The Trip, where it sets how far the shedding must reach; the warrant carries it in the Encoding, and two stale lines describing the play and company as not yet authored are corrected, since both have been staged since (Bronson and colleagues; Huang, Magnusson, and colleagues).
