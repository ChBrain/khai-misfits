Add the misfit **Only From Someone Like Us**: a practice is genuinely better, the information about it reaches every holding, and it does not spread, because the influence channel runs only between people who resemble each other and everybody who has already adopted is, by construction, no longer comparable. The founding hybrid seed corn study supplies both halves: the salesmen informed and the neighbours persuaded, and the early adopters were younger, better educated and farming more land. The adoption destroys the comparability it would need to spread, the discount applied to an early adopter's success is an accurate reading of what that success is evidence for, and the change agent sent to close the gap is the least similar person available. Distinct from After You, since stipulating a single side removes that harm and leaves this one, because the wait is for a witness and not for a counterparty; from Information Cascade, since stipulating that everybody weights their own signal fully leaves this entire, because the private signal is accurate and is the difficulty; and from What Everyone Else Does, where a custom is being defended and here nothing is.

Declares the axis `witness-similarity` with a positive sign, on how persuasive an adoption is as the person who made it resembles the person deciding.
