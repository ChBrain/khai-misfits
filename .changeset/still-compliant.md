Adds **Still Compliant**, the three hundred and forty-fourth misfit, thirty-six files, undeclared.

A statute deems conformity to a named framework an affirmative defence. The uncertainty it answers is real: a firm cannot know in advance what a court will call reasonable care, and finds out after a breach, from a judge, with hindsight. But the protection attaches to matching a text rather than to meeting a hazard, because a defence that turned on the quality of the security would be the open-ended reasonableness the statute exists to replace. So every measure up to the text buys protection and every measure past it buys none, the officer who would propose more is proposing a cost with no legal return to a board that must justify its spend against competitors equally defended, and **nobody has to be told to stop**. The framework has a revision date and the hazard has none. The morning after the breach everybody is compliant, and the answer to a real injury is true in every particular.

The slate warned this might be a row on **The Letter of the Law**. It is not: specification gaming needs a gap between proxy and intent, and here conforming is exactly the conduct asked for. Stipulate the specification captures the drafters' intent perfectly and that play has nothing left, while this one is untouched, because its harm is the absence of anything above the specification rather than a perverse route through it.

The real neighbour is **What Everyone Else Does**, which the slate never named and whose warrant already contains this play's law as a corollary. The cut is what the standard is made of: custom is practice and cannot age relative to practice, so improving the calling improves the duty, while a framework is a document that can be years behind and still confer the defence. Stipulate that every firm spontaneously adopts a better method and custom's duty rises to meet them while the harbour sits where it was written. And where Hand left the crack open in 1932, that a calling may lag and courts must in the end say what is required, a statute closes it: custom was never conclusive and a harbour is.

Sourcing is thinner than usual and the warrant says so. The design is real and re-enacted (Ohio 2018, then Utah, Connecticut, Iowa, Tennessee, Texas) and the objection is on the record from a governor who vetoed the same design in 2024 rather than sign it. No study measuring the convergence was found, so the play makes no claim about magnitude.
