Read the house for signs declared on dials whose harm does not run one way, and withdraw one. **Sixteen similarity-shaped dials read, two warrants opened, one axis withdrawn**, taking the wall from 201 units declaring an axis to 200.

**Marked the Same by Anyone gives up `marker-agreement`.** Agreement is not a dial with a bad end and a good one. Raise it and the instrument narrows to what everybody can score alike, which suppresses the expertise it was paying for. Lower it and the mark becomes one an advocate can contest, which is survivable by a candidate with a school behind them and not by one without. Both ends are harmful and in different currencies, so there is no single good quantity to move in one direction, and a one-dial-two-bad-ends structure has no sign to declare.

**The screen that found it is mechanical and the house should keep it.** Of the 201 signs declared, **99 carry no comment naming what moves**. This one was among them, and the absence was the evidence: nobody could write the comment because two things were rising and they were not the same thing. A sign that cannot be given a comment is a sign that may have no single good quantity behind it.

Two rules sharpened rather than added. **The far end being ordinarily bad is not a second bad end**: alarm sensitivity, redundancy and training specificity all have a far end that is worse in the ordinary way, and none of that makes a two-bad-ends structure, because a second bad end has to be a structural trap and not merely a failure. And **a warrant that says "two signs" may mean two times rather than two ends**: Ready on the First Day carries "one property with two signs, twenty five years apart", which is the same end of the dial being good early and bad late, so its sign survives.
