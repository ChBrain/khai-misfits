Grep the warrants for their own kinship phrases and read the six that name two plays: one declaration, five refusals, and a rule that decides which is which.

**Plausible Deniability joins The Paper Trail, and the axis is renamed from `blame-for-deviation` to `blame-attachment` because the second member arrived.** The Paper Trail's warrant already called the pair "the other face of blame-avoidance": there everyone engineers process so no fault can attach, here a principal engineers ignorance so no knowledge can. The quantity is the same in both, how readily blame attaches to whoever can be shown to have known or decided, and the two engines are what a party builds against it, a record in one and an ignorance in the other.

**The rule the pass adds is where to cut.** A kinship phrase lives inside a distinctness paragraph, whose whole job is to separate, so the sentence after it is always a separation. The question is **what it separates**. If it separates the **engine**, the dial is shared and an axis is owed: a gatekeeper deciding against a hazard deciding, a record built against an ignorance built. If it separates the **quantity**, there is no axis however close the family: a product along a chain against a maximum over a set, an accumulation in the medium against a ratio in the product, a mastered tool reshaping a problem against a search steered by the cost of looking. Five of the six read this pass separated the quantity.

Refused: The Last One to Answer against Only as Good as the Worst, An Angle and a Count against Exactly One Kilogram, A Bigger Harvest Every Year against What the Water Leaves, The Law of the Instrument against The Streetlight Effect and The Cargo Cult, and The Dead Sea Effect against `adverse-selection-risk`.
