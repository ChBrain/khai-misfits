Add the misfit The Wasted Vote, a staging of Duverger's Law: under a plurality, winner-take-all electoral rule a vote for a third party is likely wasted, so each voter strategically deserts it for one of the two viable contenders, and this individually rational desertion sums to a self-reinforcing two-party lock that narrows the choices everyone then faces, the third deserted because judged unable to win and unable to win because deserted, no villain required. Held against its bound: the law is a tendency and not a determinism, strong under single-member plurality, overridden by regionally concentrated third parties, and shaped by social cleavages as much as by the rule.
