Add the misfit Starting Tomorrow, a staging of present bias and hyperbolic discounting: a person discounts the near future far more steeply than the far, so their ranking of a smaller-sooner against a larger-later reward reverses as the sooner nears, and they plan in the calm to save or quit and then, in the moment, take the immediate reward and break the plan, again and again, a present self overriding its own earlier plan, no weakness of will required.
