Runs the fifth stretch and finds the seam has changed shape.

**The pre-session register is nearly read out.** Nine rows remain below line eleven thousand that earlier stretches had not read, and most are narration. Four stretches took it from one carried rule to most of what it holds — **the first time a selector in this house has run out rather than been abandoned**.

**The seam that is not exhausted holds seven of one thing.** Every axis in the house with an opposition produced, at the pass that found it, a sentence beginning _what sets the sign is_. There are seven. **None was in any plan.** Some sit in the warrants of the pair they were written for, which is the right place for a pair and no place at all for the next candidate on that dial.

**A discriminator is what an opposition leaves behind**, and the register already holds the evidence that they generalise: one written for a pair answered the next without amendment. So seven reusable answers existed with nowhere to look them up, and a pass arguing a sign was arguing from scratch against a question the house had settled.

Carried, quoted, by axis, to Find the Next One: `codification`, `reviewer-proximity`, `removal-asymmetry`, `entitlement-universality`, `merit-legibility`, `deference-to-judgement`, `precommitment`.

**One carries a warning.** `reviewer-proximity` exists because two plays declared the same sign under two names for one quantity — two plays making opposite claims about one dial, each agreeing with nobody, each passing every gate. **A discriminator can be the residue of a merge as well as of an opposition.**

Two method rules to Keep Clean beside them, and the counted slate lines rewritten to name their instrument instead: **the instrument is current and the number never is.** The rewrites read better than the originals, which is what the slate asked to be checked.

Ships nothing.
