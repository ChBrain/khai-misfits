Add the misfit **Wherever You Draw It**: a relationship measured across a territory is computed over areas somebody else drew for enumeration or representation, and redrawing them returns a different coefficient that is also correct. The scale limb can be reasoned about and the zonation limb cannot, since nothing ranks two tilings of the same territory into the same number of pieces. Pre-registering the zoning fixes the analyst's standing and leaves the estimate exactly as dependent, which is what separates it from undisclosed flexibility.
