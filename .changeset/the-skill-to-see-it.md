Add the misfit The Skill to See It, a staging of the Dunning-Kruger effect: because judging how well a task is done draws on the very skill the task requires, those who lack the skill lack the means to see that they lack it, so the least able rate themselves near the best while the most able underrate themselves, and only gaining the skill lifts the self-insight with it, no vanity required.
