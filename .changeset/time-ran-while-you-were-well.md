Add the misfit Time Ran While You Were Well: the limitation bar on latent harm staged as a filter that sorts by latency rather than by merit, where a period measured from the defendant's act extinguishes claims in proportion to how long the injury took to become knowable, the claimant who did not delay by a day is barred anyway, and the discovery rule that corrects it hands the stale evidence back to the defendant while the statute of repose runs on beneath, immune by construction.
