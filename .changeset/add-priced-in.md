Add the misfit Priced In: a staging of the Grossman-Stiglitz paradox, where a market price aggregates the informed traders' costly research and reveals it to everyone for free the moment they act, so each uninformed trader rationally reads the answer off the price rather than pay to gather it, and if the price reflected everything no one would be paid to gather it and no one would, so a fully informative price is self-defeating and the market rests at an equilibrium degree of disequilibrium, informative enough to be useful and noisy enough that gathering still pays, the noise supplied by traders who on average lose.
