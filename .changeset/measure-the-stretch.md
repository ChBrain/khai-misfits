Measures whether the reading stretch is finished as a unit of work. **It is.**

**Thirty-five rule-shaped spans across the last eleven entries, twenty-three carried, twelve not.** A third uncarried looks like the standing order failing. It is not, and reading the twelve is what says so: **eleven of them are narration about one pass**. The count overstates by roughly four to one, so **a raw uncarried figure from this instrument is a reading list length and never a debt**.

**One genuine rule was left behind**, and two more were found beside it in the same reading:

- _Deferring a declaration a wall requires is not a scheduling choice, it is shipping the thing the wall exists to stop._
- _A declaration that costs nothing to make also ships nothing._
- _A wall that reads a diff needs a diff_ — which is why the same changeset check passes on a working tree and refuses the change once it is a commit.

All three to Keep Clean.

**So the bulk read is done, and the sweep that replaces it is small**: eleven passes' entries are thirty-five spans, against two hundred and thirteen for the whole register.

**And the residue that matters is where the rules were found.** All three came from passes where the house was **wrong about something** — a deferral the ratchet refused, a changeset the hook refused, a wall answering differently before and after a commit. **A pass refused by a wall leaves a rule nearly every time**, which is a better selector than any line range.

The converse is weaker, and this pass is its own counterexample: it was refused by nothing and still left one, carried before narration could hide it.
