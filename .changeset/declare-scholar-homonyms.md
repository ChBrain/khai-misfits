Declare the twelve surnames this house shares between more than one scholar. The science index keys an Origin row on the author's surname, which is how a scholar written "Kahneman" in one warrant and "Daniel Kahneman" in another collates to a single entry, and it is also how two different people collapse into one. A sweep of all nine hundred and fifty-odd keys found twelve surnames carrying more than one person: Adams is Scott, Gordon and John; Hart is 't Hart, Julian Tudor Hart and Oliver Hart; Rose is Geoffrey, Michael R and Todd; Wagner is Adolph and Richard inside a single misfit's Origin table; and Black, Freeman, Kessler, Luchins, Lynch, Martin, Schwartz and Wilde each carry two. Read as one entry apiece, those are twelve false statements about who wrote what. The separation cannot be computed and this corpus is what proves it, since "Buchanan", "James Buchanan" and "James M Buchanan" are one economist and "Schelling", "Thomas Schelling" and "Thomas C Schelling" are one strategist, so any rule that split the Harts would shatter those two as well; and keying on full names instead would trade a dozen false merges for hundreds of false splits and destroy the collation the index exists for. So the shared surnames are declared here, in the shape the house already uses for `memberPolicy.homonyms`, and everything else stays computed. This declaration is inert until the corresponding khai-tests change ships, since the installed build does not yet read it, and it activates on the next release with no further edit. Ships nothing: config only.
