Stage **A Reason Too Many** (motivation crowding-out): attaching an explicit reward or fine to a behaviour carried by an intrinsic or moral motive can crowd out that motive, so the incentive reduces the behaviour it meant to raise. Credited to Deci & Ryan, Lepper/Greene/Nisbett, Gneezy & Rustichini, Titmuss, and Frey/Jegen/Oberholzer-Gee, held with the conditional hedge.
