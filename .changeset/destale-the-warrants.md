Correct fifty-five warrants that claim their own play is unwritten. A warrant drafted ahead of its staging opened by saying so, "(play and company not yet staged; this warrant is drafted first, to prove the science and the distinction before authoring)", called its title provisional, and closed its Encoding section with "the play and its company are not yet authored; these are the proposed load-bearing pieces the warrant commits the staging to carry". That was true when each was written and false the moment the play landed, and nothing removed it afterwards, so fifty-five of the house's two hundred and three misfits, better than a quarter of the roster, carry a warrant that denies the existence of the thirty-odd files sitting beside it. Every one of the fifty-five was checked and every one is fully staged with a play and a complete company, so there is no case here of a warrant telling the truth about work still to come. The correction is mechanical and uniform: the parenthetical is removed, "the title is provisional, a reading of" becomes "the title is a reading of", and the Encoding preamble becomes the line the house now uses everywhere else, "Source to constraint, per element, one per load-bearing vector, no filler." Touched paragraphs are rewrapped to the hundred-column width the repository keeps, since prettier is configured to preserve prose wrapping and will not do it. Tables, headings, quotes and list items were left alone by construction. This matters beyond tidiness because these files are the dedup instrument: the house-wide concordance points a scout at a per-misfit warrant for the full distinctness argument, and a scout who followed that pointer to any of the fifty-five would have read that the concept was not staged yet, which is the same class of failure that let a duplicate misfit reach main earlier in this run. Two pre-existing dash violations found in the same sweep are fixed with it, an en-dash in a year range in The Calm You Pay For and an em-dash pair in The Paper Trail, taking the house to zero em and en dashes across every misfit file. No staging changes, no citation changes and no concept changes: only claims about the state of the work, and they are now true.
