# Position: Holder

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). The seat of the lowest-ranked work, which reached the shared resource first and holds it rightfully, needing only a little time to finish and release, and frozen mid-task by the order.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The lock, and the least rank in the system: exclusive hold of a resource only one may keep, and a place at the bottom of the order.
An honest claim: it took the lock when it was free, as it was entitled to, and has only a short task to finish before letting go.
No standing to keep running when outranked, so its hold on the resource outlasts its hold on the machine, the one thing it keeps when everything else is taken from it.

## Orders

Hold and release.
Take the lock, do the short work that needs it, and let go as fast as the work allows, wanting nothing but to finish and free the resource.
Yield the machine the instant a higher task is ready, because the order requires it, even though yielding mid-task leaves the lock still in hand.

## Loses

The machine, and with it the chance to finish and release.
Because it ranks below the middle work, the seat is set down mid-section the moment that work is ready, and cannot run again until the machine returns to it, so the release everything is waiting on is suspended not by its own doing but by the order applied correctly to it.
It keeps the lock it cannot use and loses the running that would let it give the lock up.

## Drives

Toward the lowest work holding what the highest needs and unable to move for the middle,
blameless and load-bearing at once, quick with the lock and frozen with it in hand,
so the seat that did nothing but take what it was owed becomes the still point the whole inversion turns on.
