# Process: Lending

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). Run by [The Dispatcher](persona_the_dispatcher.md) on [Serve the Highest Ready](plan_serve_the_highest_ready.md), at [The Critical Section](place_the_critical_section.md), in [The Lent Rank](plot_the_lent_rank.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Foremost blocking on a lock the Holder holds, detected by the Dispatcher as the moment a higher-ranked task is waiting on a lower-ranked one.
This is the one condition the remedy must be built to watch for beforehand; nothing in the ordinary run of the system raises it on its own.

## Direction

The Holder's priority raised to the Foremost's for the span of [The Critical Section](place_the_critical_section.md), then dropped back once the lock is released.
Carries [The Inheritance](piece_the_inheritance.md) onto the Holder, closing the gap [The Critical Section](place_the_critical_section.md) withholds by giving the resource, for as long as it is held, the rank of whoever needs it most.

## Lever

A task blocking a higher-priority task inherits that task's priority for the duration of the blocking, so no third task ranked between them can preempt it: the priority inheritance protocol (Sha, Rajkumar, and Lehoczky).
This bounds the Foremost's wait to at most one critical section's length; it does not remove the wait, and the priority ceiling protocol further bounds it to a single such section while preventing deadlock.

## Echo

The Middle held off the core for the section's duration, the Holder finishing and releasing, the Foremost entering, the Holder's borrowed rank withdrawn the instant it is no longer earning it.
The fix must be designed in before the fact, on Mars Pathfinder it was uploaded from Earth after the resets, and it costs bookkeeping on every lock acquired, a standing price for a wait that is now bounded rather than open (Reeves).
