# Process: Preempting

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). Run by [The Middle](persona_the_middle.md) on [Take My Turn](plan_take_my_turn.md), and by [The Dispatcher](persona_the_dispatcher.md) on [Serve the Highest Ready](plan_serve_the_highest_ready.md), at [The Core](place_the_core.md), in [The Cut-In](plot_the_cut_in.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Middle becoming ready while the Holder occupies the core, wanting nothing the Holder has and owing it nothing.
The Dispatcher does exactly what it is built to do: compare ranks and hand the seat to whichever is higher, with no view into what the displaced task is holding.

## Direction

The Holder off the core mid-section, frozen inside [The Critical Section](place_the_critical_section.md) with [the Lock](piece_the_lock.md) still in its grip, the Middle in its place.
Carries [The Inversion](piece_the_inversion.md) forward: a swap correct by rank leaves the lock the Foremost needs held by a task that now cannot run to release it.

## Lever

A fixed-priority preemptive scheduler always runs the highest-priority ready task, without regard to what resource the displaced task is holding (Sha, Rajkumar, and Lehoczky).
That single, individually correct rule is the whole mechanism; no fault in the Middle's request or the Dispatcher's judgment is needed to produce the freeze.

## Echo

The critical section stalled indefinitely, its only occupant unable to run, unable to finish, unable to open the door.
The stall persists for exactly as long as the Middle, and whatever after it, keeps outranking the Holder, a duration the Middle's own ordinary work never intended to set.
