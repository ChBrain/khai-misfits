# Plot: The Cut-In

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Core](place_the_core.md), where the Middle becomes ready, wanting nothing from anyone and owing nothing to anyone.
The Holder still occupies the seat, mid-section, [The Lock](piece_the_lock.md) in its grip; the Foremost still waits behind it.

## Action

[Preempting](process_preempting.md): [The Middle](persona_the_middle.md), on [Take My Turn](plan_take_my_turn.md) at [the Middle's seat](position_middle.md), and [The Dispatcher](persona_the_dispatcher.md), on [Serve the Highest Ready](plan_serve_the_highest_ready.md), together carry [The Order](piece_the_order.md) into effect and take the Holder off the core.

## Stage

The Middle needs nothing the Holder has and holds nothing itself; it is simply ready, and it outranks the Holder, and that is the entire case the Dispatcher weighs. Serving the highest ready task, exactly as built, the Dispatcher swaps the Holder out and the Middle in. The Holder freezes mid-section, [The Lock](piece_the_lock.md) still closed in its hand, unable to run and so unable to finish and so unable to release. The Foremost, which outranks both of them, is not on the core and not next in line either: it is still waiting on the lock the Holder cannot free, and the task now running is one it outranks completely. This is [The Inversion](piece_the_inversion.md), and from here the Foremost is [Starving](process_starving.md): its wait no longer ends when the Holder's short section would have finished, but runs as long as the Middle, and whatever follows it, keeps the core.

## Tension

A correct swap by rank has emptied the bound out of the Foremost's wait; nothing forces the Middle to yield, and nothing lets the Holder run until it does.
The highest-priority task in the system is now starving behind ordinary work it outranks, with every decision that produced this state individually right.
