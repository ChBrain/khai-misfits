# Place: The Ready Line

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). Where ready tasks wait to be dispatched by rank. Hosts [The Right Call](plot_the_right_call.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Tasks able to run but not running, ordered by nothing but rank: the Foremost once blocked and then ready, the Middle needing nothing from anyone.
What shows is a queue with no history in it, each task present only as its priority number, blind to how long it has waited or what it is waited on for.

## Holds

[The Order](piece_the_order.md), the standing rule that rank alone decides who leaves the line next.
Holds the Foremost and the Middle side by side despite one racing toward starvation and the other merely getting on with ordinary work, since the line keeps no record of stakes, only of rank.

## Offers

A place from which the highest-ranked waiter is always served next, exactly as the Dispatcher promises.
The line offers this guarantee faithfully and without exception, and that very faithfulness is what lets the Middle step past the Foremost the moment the Foremost's rank is not, in fact, the highest present.

## Withheld

Any distinction between a task waiting on ordinary scheduling and a task waiting, unbounded, on a lock held below it.
The line withholds cause: it shows that the Foremost is not running, never why, so nothing here alone can tell a routine wait from [Starving](process_starving.md).
