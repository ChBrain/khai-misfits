# Plot: The Block

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Core](place_the_core.md), where the Foremost becomes ready and reaches for what its own work requires.
The Holder is still inside [The Critical Section](place_the_critical_section.md); the door the Foremost needs open is shut.

## Action

[Blocking](process_blocking.md): [The Foremost](persona_the_foremost.md), on [Take What I Need](plan_take_what_i_need.md) and holding [the Foremost's seat](position_foremost.md), asks for [The Lock](piece_the_lock.md), finds it held, and steps off the core carrying [The Wait](piece_the_wait.md).

## Stage

The Foremost has done nothing to invite this: it is the highest-ranked task in the system, it arrived and asked for the resource at the exact moment its own work required it, and the answer is simply that another task already holds it. There is no seniority clause for the lock; occupancy, not rank, decides who is inside. The Foremost leaves the core and joins the ready line, suspended not by anything wrong but by the one rule mutual exclusion imposes on everyone alike. At this instant the wait is still bounded: the Holder is working through a short section and nothing else is pressing on it, so if it simply ran to completion, the lock would free and the Foremost would enter shortly after.

## Tension

The Foremost, ranked above every other task in the system, is now waiting on the one ranked lowest.
That much is by design and survivable, so long as nothing else arrives to keep the Holder from finishing; the wait's whole shape depends on what the ready line admits next.
