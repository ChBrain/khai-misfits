# Plot: The Handover

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Critical Section](place_the_critical_section.md), quiet before anyone is waiting on it.
The Holder reaches the point in its own ordinary work where the shared resource is needed, and nothing else in the system is asking for it yet.

## Action

[The Holder](persona_the_holder.md), holding [the Holder's seat](position_holder.md), runs [Hold and Release](plan_hold_and_release.md): it takes [The Lock](piece_the_lock.md) and enters [The Critical Section](place_the_critical_section.md) to do a brief, bounded piece of work before letting go.

## Stage

The Holder is the lowest ranked of the three, and nothing about that matters yet: no higher task is contending for anything, so the take is unremarkable, granted the instant it is asked for. It settles into the section with the door shut behind it, working through a short sequence that will finish and release the lock on its own, well within any bound its designers assumed for it. The Foremost has not arrived; the Middle has not stirred; the ready line holds nothing that outranks the Holder in this moment. What is underway is exactly what the system was built to allow, one task, briefly, with exclusive use of what it needs.

## Tension

The Lock is now held, and the section is occupied for as long as this piece of work takes, no longer.
Nothing here forecasts trouble: the arrangement is correct and ordinary, and its only exposure is timing, whether the Holder finishes and releases before anything higher-ranked comes to need what it is holding.
