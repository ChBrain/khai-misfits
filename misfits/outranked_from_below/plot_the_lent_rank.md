# Plot: The Lent Rank

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Critical Section](place_the_critical_section.md), now run under a protocol designed in before any of this happened.
The Holder takes the lock again, and this time the Foremost's rank is not the only thing standing outside the door.

## Action

[Lending](process_lending.md): [The Dispatcher](persona_the_dispatcher.md), on [Serve the Highest Ready](plan_serve_the_highest_ready.md) at [the Dispatcher's seat](position_dispatcher.md), detects [The Foremost](persona_the_foremost.md) blocked on [The Holder](persona_the_holder.md) and raises the Holder to the Foremost's rank for as long as [The Critical Section](place_the_critical_section.md) lasts, carrying [The Inheritance](piece_the_inheritance.md).

## Stage

The moment the Foremost blocks on the lock the Holder holds, the Dispatcher lends the Holder the Foremost's own priority for the span of the section, nothing more. When the Middle becomes ready this time, it finds the seat held by a task now ranked above it, and [The Order](piece_the_order.md), followed exactly as before, keeps the Middle off the core rather than putting it on. The Holder finishes undisturbed, releases the lock, drops back to its own rank the instant it is no longer earning the borrowed one, and the Foremost enters. The order the schedule was built to keep is restored, at a cost that had to be paid in advance: the bookkeeping of tracking who blocks whom, the protocol designed and tested before the failure it prevents, and a wait for the Foremost that is bounded now, one critical section's length, not abolished. In the flight case, this protection existed in the platform but shipped switched off; the fix was diagnosed under pressure and uploaded to the spacecraft from Earth (Reeves).

## Tension

The Foremost still waits, and still yields the core for a span it does not choose; the difference is that the span now has an edge.
Inheritance costs foresight nothing prevented until it was designed for, and it buys a system that can be trusted to bound its worst case rather than one that must simply hope the timing never lines up again.
