# Persona: The Dispatcher

## Taxonomy

[Dispatcher](position_dispatcher.md). The scheduler, which at every instant hands the one machine to the highest-ranked ready work and never once breaks its rule, and which cannot see that a held resource carries no rank.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Impartial, exact, always correct.
Is the rule made an actor: whenever the machine comes free it gives it to the highest-ranked task that is ready, immediately, without exception and without favour.
No error in it ever, at the level it works: every single decision it makes is the right decision by the order it is given, and it keeps that order flawlessly, instant after instant.

## Action

Serves the highest ready work on [Serve the Highest Ready](plan_serve_the_highest_ready.md), through [Preempting](process_preempting.md) at [the Core](place_the_core.md), dispatching by [the Order](piece_the_order.md) and nothing else.
Cannot read [the Lock](piece_the_lock.md): the Dispatcher sees rank and readiness, and a task holding a resource another task needs looks to it exactly like any other task of its rank, so it hands the machine to the Middle over the Holder without knowing what the Holder carries.
Does what a correct scheduler does: it enforces the priority order perfectly, and in enforcing it perfectly, dispatches the inversion.

## Shadow

Not broken and not misconfigured: the Dispatcher does precisely what a priority scheduler is supposed to do, and every audit of its decisions finds each one correct.
Because it dispatches on rank alone and a held lock confers no rank, the Dispatcher cannot tell that the low work it is passing over is standing in for the high work waiting on it, so the very fidelity to the order that makes it trustworthy is what lets the order invert beneath it, the fault, if there were one, being not in any ruling but in what the ruling is blind to.
It is the correct machine that produces the wrong end, and correcting it means not fixing an error but teaching it to see the one thing it was built not to need.

## Tell

Says, in effect, I ran the highest-ranked ready task, every time, and it is telling the truth.
Cannot answer for the wait, because nothing in its rule is the wait's cause and nothing in its view is the wait's shape, until it is given [the Inheritance](piece_the_inheritance.md) and taught to lend the holder the rank of the work it blocks, the one change that lets a correct order stay correct in effect.
