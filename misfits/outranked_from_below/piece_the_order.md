# Piece: The Order

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). The priority ranking honoured at every dispatch, never once broken, that holds the whole system together and yet inverts in its effect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Honoured by [The Dispatcher](persona_the_dispatcher.md) on [Serve the Highest Ready](plan_serve_the_highest_ready.md), the rule it applies without exception at [The Core](place_the_core.md).
It is the rank [The Middle](persona_the_middle.md) legitimately holds over [The Holder](persona_the_holder.md), and is replayed decision by decision, and found correct at each one, in [The Right Call](plot_the_right_call.md).

## Load Bearing

A fixed-priority preemptive scheduler always runs the highest-priority ready task, and that rule alone, applied consistently, is what produces unbounded priority inversion when a resource is held beneath it (Sha, Rajkumar, and Lehoczky). The Order is load bearing because it is the one thing in the misfit that is never wrong: every swap it authorizes, the Middle over the Holder included, is individually correct, which is precisely what makes the inversion a property of the composition and not of any single ruling. Guard against reading the Order as the villain in disguise, a rule that should have known better: it cannot see what a task holds, only what it ranks, and that blindness is not a defect to be patched but the definition of the scheduling discipline itself.

## Apparent

A simple ladder, whoever ranks highest goes first, so straightforward it seems to guarantee the outcome its name promises.
Because it is followed without exception, it looks like sufficient protection on its own, the kind of rule that, faithfully kept, should make it impossible for the highest-ranked work to ever wait on the lowest, which is exactly the appearance The Inversion exploits.

## Yearbook

Kept by [the Dispatcher](persona_the_dispatcher.md), exercised by [the Middle](persona_the_middle.md) in [The Cut-In](plot_the_cut_in.md), and audited without finding fault in [The Right Call](plot_the_right_call.md).
It is the standing rule that [The Ready Line](place_the_ready_line.md) enforces and [Preempting](process_preempting.md) invokes; [The Inheritance](piece_the_inheritance.md) does not break it but works underneath it, changing what rank the Holder is seen to carry rather than what the Order does with whatever rank it is given.
