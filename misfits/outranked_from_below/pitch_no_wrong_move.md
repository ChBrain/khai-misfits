# Pitch: No Wrong Move

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Orderly, correct, unhurried.
A system with a rule anyone would keep: one machine, one thing at a time, always the highest-ranked ready work first.
Nothing false in it, and nothing loose: the rule is right, and in nearly every instant it does exactly right by the order, serving the most important work first and the least last.

## Undertow

Under the order kept at every step, the order inverted in the sum.
The highest work needs a thing the lowest work is holding, so it waits; and a task of middle rank, needing none of it, takes its rightful turn ahead of the frozen holder, so the highest work waits behind the lowest for as long as the middle work runs.
Rank is what the machine dispatches on, and holding the lock confers no rank, so the one fact that would order it right, that the lowest work is standing in for the highest while it holds the lock, is the fact the rule cannot read.

## Nerve

The certainty that if every decision is correct the outcome must be, and the discovery that it need not be:
that a rule can keep faith with itself at every step and still starve the very work it exists to serve, without bound, behind work of no importance,
and that when you replay it to find the fault there is no fault to find, only right decision after right decision summing to the wrong end.

## Echo

After the run: the highest-priority work served last and a watchdog, counting on it, resetting a system with nothing broken in it, as one did in flight on Mars Pathfinder (Reeves).
And the one correction that sets the order right, lending the holder the rank of the work it blocks, a piece of foresight that must be built in before the timing ever lines up,
because the failure shows only when the three ranks and the one lock fall into step, rare enough to pass every test and surface, without warning, in the field.
