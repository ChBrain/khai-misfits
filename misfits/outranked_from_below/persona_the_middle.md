# Persona: The Middle

## Taxonomy

[Middle](position_middle.md). The task of middle rank, needing nothing from the others and holding nothing, whose only doing is its own ordinary business, and whose rightful turn ahead of the frozen holder is what makes the highest work's wait unbounded.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Middle priority, unremarkable, minding its own work.
Is the everyday task that becomes ready and has something to do, ranked above the low work and below the high, entitled to run when the machine is free and nothing higher is ready to take it.
No design in it toward anyone: it wants none of the lock, knows nothing of the wait it is causing, and is only doing the thing it exists to do, at the moment it is allowed to do it.

## Action

Runs its own work on [Take My Turn](plan_take_my_turn.md), through [Preempting](process_preempting.md) at [the Core](place_the_core.md), taking the machine ahead of [the Holder](persona_the_holder.md) because it outranks it.
Cannot see [the Inversion](piece_the_inversion.md) it opens: nothing tells the Middle that the low work it displaced was holding what the high work needs, so its ordinary progress is, unknown to it, the length of the highest work's wait.
Does what middle work does when its turn comes and nothing higher is running: it takes the machine and runs, correctly, for as long as it has work.

## Shadow

Not a queue-jumper and not a usurper: the Middle cut nothing in line, broke no rule, and took only the turn its rank entitles it to, ahead of lower work, as the order says it may.
Because it outranks the holder but not the blocked high work, and needs nothing anyone holds, the Middle is the third element that turns a short wait into an unbounded one, doing so entirely by running its own business, so the most consequential actor in the trap is the one with the least to do with it.
Its running is both wholly innocent and exactly the cost, and those are not in tension: they are the trap.

## Tell

Says nothing to anyone, announces nothing, just has work and does it.
Cannot know that while it runs the highest work is starving behind the holder it displaced, so it proceeds in good conscience and good order, and the longer its ordinary business lasts, the longer the most important work in the system is made to wait.
