# Position: Foremost

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). The seat of the highest-ranked work, the work the system exists to serve first, which needs a resource the lowest work holds and can only wait, outranking all and served last.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The top of the order, and a requirement it cannot fill by rank: the standing to preempt anything not holding what it needs, and a plain need for a resource that another task already holds.
Real precedence, enforced everywhere the machine dispatches: nothing of lower rank runs ahead of it while it is ready and unblocked.
No claim on a held lock, because a lock answers to holding and not to rank, so the seat's whole precedence goes silent at the one point it needs it.

## Orders

Take what you need.
Run at the top of the order, reach for the resource, and if it is free, proceed; if it is held, block and wait for it, because a lock cannot be taken by rank.
Do not stand down and do not lose precedence: keep the top of the order the whole time, ready and blocked, entitled to everything except the one thing that is held.

## Loses

The thing precedence was supposed to buy: being served first.
Because the resource is held by lower work and holding carries no rank, the seat waits behind that lower work, and when a middle task takes its rightful turn ahead of the frozen holder, the seat waits behind the middle work too, for as long as it runs.
It keeps the top of the order and loses the whole benefit of it, served last while ranked first.

## Drives

Toward the most important work waiting on the least,
its precedence intact at every instant and inert at the one that counts,
so the seat built to be served first is the seat the inversion starves, blocked on a resource its rank cannot reach.
