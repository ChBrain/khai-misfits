# Place: The Core

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). The single processor: one task runs here, and only ever one. Hosts [The Block](plot_the_block.md), [The Cut-In](plot_the_cut_in.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

One running task, whichever the Dispatcher has placed there, and beside it a line of others merely ready.
Nothing about which task is running discloses what it holds or what waits on it; the core shows only rank in motion, a slot filled and refilled by whoever currently outranks the rest.

## Holds

The single running slot, and every swap that fills it: the Middle's preemption of the Holder, the Foremost's entry once the lock frees.
Carries [The Inversion](piece_the_inversion.md) as it forms, since the core is where a technically correct swap, the Middle over the Holder, becomes the reason the Foremost cannot yet run at all.

## Offers

The one seat rank alone decides, awarded strictly and without memory of who owes whom.
Because the core asks only "who outranks whom, now," it offers the Middle a clean claim on the seat the instant it is ready, no notice taken of the Holder's unfinished business underneath.

## Withheld

Any record of what the departed task was in the middle of, and any veto for the task it left behind.
The core withholds continuity: it neither preserves the Holder's claim on the seat nor lets the Foremost skip the line by virtue of outranking everyone, since the Foremost's rank counts only once it too stands in the ready line.
