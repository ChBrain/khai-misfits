# Position: Dispatcher

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). The seat of the scheduler, which hands the one machine to the highest-ranked ready work at every instant and keeps its rule flawlessly, and which cannot read that a held resource carries no rank.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

One rule and total fidelity to it: give the machine, always and at once, to the highest-ranked task that is ready.
A perfect record at its own level: every decision it makes is the right decision by the order it holds, and it never once departs from it.
No sight of the lock, because it dispatches on rank and readiness alone, and a task holding what another needs looks to it like any other task of its rank.

## Orders

Serve the highest ready.
Whenever the machine comes free, hand it to the highest-ranked ready task immediately, without exception, preempting whatever runs beneath it.
Read only rank and readiness, because that is the rule, and the rule is correct, so serve it faithfully and let the order it enforces be the order that holds.

## Loses

Nothing it can see, and the whole outcome it cannot.
Because it reads rank and not holding, the seat cannot tell that the low work it passes over is standing in for the high work waiting on it, so its flawless enforcement of the order is exactly what lets the order invert beneath it, a correctness that costs everything without a single wrong decision to show for it.
It loses the outcome while keeping the rule, and cannot even locate the loss, until it is taught to lend the holder the waiter's rank.

## Drives

Toward the correct machine that produces the wrong end,
enforcing the order perfectly and dispatching the inversion in the same stroke,
so the seat that never makes a wrong decision is the seat through which every right one sums to the highest work served last.
