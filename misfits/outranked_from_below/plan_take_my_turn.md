# Plan: Take My Turn

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the middle's seat](position_middle.md), inside [Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Middle](position_middle.md)

## Direction

Run your own work whenever the machine is free and nothing higher is ready, taking it ahead of the low work as the order allows. Ask nothing of the lock and know nothing of the wait: do the thing you exist to do, at the moment you are entitled to do it, and let your rightful turn be as ordinary as it feels.

## Orders

([The Middle](persona_the_middle.md) takes the machine through [Preempting](process_preempting.md) ahead of [the Holder](persona_the_holder.md), opening [the Inversion](piece_the_inversion.md) it cannot see, its own work touching none of [the Lock](piece_the_lock.md).)

## Implementation

([The Core](place_the_core.md) hands the middle work its turn by rank; the [preempting](process_preempting.md) process displaces the frozen holder for as long as the middle work runs, so [the Inversion](piece_the_inversion.md) holds and the highest work's [Wait](piece_the_wait.md) lengthens by exactly the middle work's duration, all of it done in good order.)

## Targets

- [ ] the middle work runs its rightful turn whenever the machine is free and nothing higher is ready
- [ ] it asks nothing of the lock and blocks nothing, needing none of what the others hold
- [ ] its ordinary running displaces the frozen holder and sets the length of the highest work's wait
- [ ] no rule is broken and no cost is paid by the middle work, its innocence and the cost being one and the same
