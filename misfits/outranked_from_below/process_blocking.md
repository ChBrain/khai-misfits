# Process: Blocking

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). Run by [The Foremost](persona_the_foremost.md) on [Take What I Need](plan_take_what_i_need.md), at [The Critical Section](place_the_critical_section.md), in [The Block](plot_the_block.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Foremost reaching the point in its own work where it needs the shared resource, and finding [the Lock](piece_the_lock.md) already held.
Nothing the Foremost did was wrong: it asked for what its work required, at the moment its work required it, and the door was simply shut.

## Direction

Off the core and onto the ready line, unable to proceed until the lock frees.
Carries the Foremost out of [The Core](place_the_core.md) and leaves the Holder inside the critical section it cannot yet enter, so blocking hands control of when the Foremost resumes entirely to a task the Foremost outranks.

## Lever

Mutual exclusion on a shared resource necessarily suspends any task that arrives while another holds it, regardless of either task's priority (Sha, Rajkumar, and Lehoczky).
This much is exact and unavoidable in any locking scheme; what is not yet decided is how long the suspension lasts, which is the question [Starving](process_starving.md) answers.

## Echo

A highest-priority task now waiting on a lower one, its rank suspended along with its execution.
The Foremost leaves no trace of urgency behind it on the core, so the wait that follows looks, from outside, like any other task simply not yet ready to run.
