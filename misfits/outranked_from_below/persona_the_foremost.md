# Persona: The Foremost

## Taxonomy

[Foremost](position_foremost.md). The highest-ranked work, the work the whole system exists to serve first, which needs a resource the lowest work is holding and so can do nothing but wait, outranking everyone and served last.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Highest priority. Most important. First in every ordering.
Is the work the machine is built to run ahead of all else, the task no rule would ever knowingly delay, entitled by rank to preempt anything that is not holding what it needs.
Wants only to proceed, and would, in an instant, if the one thing it needs were free; there is no impatience in it and no fault, only the plain requirement of a resource another task happens to hold.

## Action

Reaches for [the Lock](piece_the_lock.md) on [Take What I Need](plan_take_what_i_need.md), through [Blocking](process_blocking.md) at [the Core](place_the_core.md), and finding it held, carries [the Wait](piece_the_wait.md).
Cannot take the lock by rank, because a lock is not dispatched by rank: it is held until released, and rank buys no exception, so the Foremost's whole standing counts for nothing against a resource already in another's hand.
Does what the most important work does when the thing it needs is held: it stops, and waits, and its rank cannot shorten the wait by a single instant.

## Shadow

Not a victim of anyone, and not wronged by a decision: no task took the lock from it, no scheduler passed it over, no rule was bent against it.
Because holding the resource confers no rank, the Foremost's precedence, real and correctly enforced everywhere else, is silent at exactly the point that matters, so the work most owed the machine is the work left waiting on it, and there is no misjudgement to blame for that, only the gap between what rank governs and what a lock governs.
It is served last not because it was ranked low but because ranking is not what was in play.

## Tell

Says nothing, asks for nothing unusual, only needs what it needs and is entitled to it.
Cannot make its rank tell where a lock is concerned, so it sits ready and blocked while lower work runs, and the longer the middle work has to do, the longer the most important work in the system waits, its precedence intact and inert.
