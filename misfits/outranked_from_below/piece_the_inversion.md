# Piece: The Inversion

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). The emergent state itself: a lower-ranked task running while the highest-ranked waits on it, the order intact at every single step and reversed in the sum.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Produced by [Preempting](process_preempting.md), the moment [The Middle](persona_the_middle.md) takes [The Core](place_the_core.md) from [The Holder](persona_the_holder.md).
Seen whole in [The Cut-In](plot_the_cut_in.md), the plot where the swap that was correct in isolation resolves into the state the entire play turns on.

## Load Bearing

The Inversion is not any one decision but the composed effect of several correct ones: the Holder correctly takes the lock, the Foremost correctly blocks on it, and the Dispatcher correctly runs the Middle over the Holder the instant the Middle is ready, and the sum of these is a highest-ranked task waiting on a lower one with no rule broken anywhere in the chain (Sha, Rajkumar, and Lehoczky). It is load bearing because it is the central object of the whole misfit, the thing every persona, plan, and process either produces, suffers, or exists to bound, and nothing in the mechanism up to this point is wrong, only composed into an order it was never designed to allow. Guard against looking for the moment it goes wrong: there is no such moment, only the moment several rights stack into a wrong.

## Apparent

A momentary shuffle on the core, one task swapped for another, nothing that looks, from any single vantage, like a failure of rank.
Watched step by step it is invisible as an inversion at all, each swap defensible on its own terms, and it is only when the Foremost's wait is measured against the Middle's ordinary business that the reversed order becomes visible, which is why it so often goes unnoticed until [The Wait](piece_the_wait.md) has already run long.
