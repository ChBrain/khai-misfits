# Position: Middle

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). The seat of the middle-ranked work, needing nothing the others hold and holding nothing, whose rightful turn ahead of the frozen holder is what turns a short wait into an unbounded one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A middle place in the order, and its own ordinary business: the standing to run ahead of the low work when the machine is free, and work of its own to do that touches none of the lock.
A clean claim: it needs nothing anyone holds, blocks nothing, and asks only to run its turn when its turn comes.
No knowledge of the wait it lengthens, because nothing tells the seat that the low work it displaced was holding what the high work needs.

## Orders

Take my turn.
Run your own work whenever the machine is free and nothing higher is ready, taking it ahead of the low work as the order allows.
Ask nothing of the lock and know nothing of the wait: just do the thing you exist to do, at the moment you are entitled to do it.

## Loses

Nothing, and that is the point.
The seat pays no cost and breaks no rule: it runs its turn, correctly, and in running lengthens the highest work's wait by exactly its own duration, without ever losing or spending anything of its own.
It is the one seat in the trap that comes through untouched, and its untouched running is the cost the others bear.

## Drives

Toward the third task that makes the wait unbounded,
doing so purely by taking a turn it is owed and running work of no relation to any of it,
so the seat with the least to do with the trap is the seat whose ordinary business sets the length of the starving.
