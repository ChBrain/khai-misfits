# Place: The Critical Section

## Taxonomy

[Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md). The locked region the Holder is inside and the Foremost needs. Hosts [The Handover](plot_the_handover.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A narrow stretch of work wrapped by [the Lock](piece_the_lock.md), one task inside it and every other claimant waiting outside.
What is visible is only occupancy: the Holder present, the door shut behind it, and no sign from outside of how much work remains before it opens.

## Holds

The Holder's grip on the resource, and the Foremost's need pressed against a door that will not move.
Holds the section's one unforgiving rule, at most one occupant, which is exactly what makes the Foremost's entry impossible while the Holder is inside, whatever else is true of either task's rank.

## Offers

Exclusive use of the shared resource, and nothing about who is entitled to jump the queue for it.
The section offers the Holder the same protection it would offer anyone: finish undisturbed by another occupant, a guarantee that has nothing to do with priority and everything to do with correctness of the resource itself.

## Withheld

Priority. The section carries no rank of its own and confers none on whoever is inside it.
It withholds any signal to the rest of the system that its current occupant matters more than usual right now, which is exactly the gap [Lending](process_lending.md) is built to close.
