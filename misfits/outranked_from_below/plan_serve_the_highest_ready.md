# Plan: Serve the Highest Ready

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the dispatcher's seat](position_dispatcher.md), inside [Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Dispatcher](position_dispatcher.md)

## Direction

Whenever the machine comes free, hand it at once to the highest-ranked ready task, without exception and reading only rank and readiness, because that rule is correct and doing right by the order in nearly every instant. Keep faith with it flawlessly, and know that faith alone will dispatch the inversion, until you are taught the one added discipline that lets a correct order stay correct in effect: while a low task holds what a high task needs, lend the holder the waiter's rank.

## Orders

([The Dispatcher](persona_the_dispatcher.md) serves by [the Order](piece_the_order.md) through [Preempting](process_preempting.md), blind to [the Lock](piece_the_lock.md) the holder carries, and only [Lending](process_lending.md) the holder [the Inheritance](piece_the_inheritance.md) closes the gap.)

## Implementation

([The Core](place_the_core.md) is served strictly by rank; the [preempting](process_preempting.md) process enforces the order faithfully and so opens [the Inversion](piece_the_inversion.md), which no ruling can catch because no ruling is wrong; the [lending](process_lending.md) remedy raises the holder to the waiter's rank for the span of the critical section, bounding the wait rather than abolishing it, and must be built in before the timing lines up.)

## Targets

- [ ] the machine is handed to the highest-ranked ready task at every instant, the rule kept flawlessly
- [ ] only rank and readiness are read, a held resource carrying no rank the dispatcher can see
- [ ] the faithful order dispatches the inversion, no wrong decision available to catch
- [ ] the added discipline of lending the holder the waiter's rank is the one fix, foreseen and built in, that keeps a correct order correct in effect
