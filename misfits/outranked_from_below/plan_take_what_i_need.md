# Plan: Take What I Need

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the foremost's seat](position_foremost.md), inside [Outranked from Below](play_outranked_from_below.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Foremost](position_foremost.md)

## Direction

Run at the top of the order and reach for the resource you need. If it is free, take it and proceed; if it is held, block on it and wait, because a lock is held until released and no rank can take it early. Keep your precedence the whole time, ready and blocked, and let it buy you everything except the one thing another task is holding.

## Orders

([The Foremost](persona_the_foremost.md) reaches for [the Lock](piece_the_lock.md) through [Blocking](process_blocking.md), finds it held by [the Holder](persona_the_holder.md), and carries [the Wait](piece_the_wait.md), its rank unable to shorten it.)

## Implementation

([The Core](place_the_core.md) dispatches by rank, and a lock does not; the [blocking](process_blocking.md) process suspends the highest work on a resource its precedence cannot reach, so [the Wait](piece_the_wait.md) opens, short while the holder could still run and unbounded once [the Middle](persona_the_middle.md) takes the machine.)

## Targets

- [ ] the highest work reaches for the resource and, finding it held, blocks rather than seizing it
- [ ] its precedence is kept intact, ready and blocked, never stood down
- [ ] rank buys no early claim on the held lock, the one need precedence cannot fill
- [ ] the wait is borne without fault, short at first and unbounded once a middle task runs ahead of the frozen holder
