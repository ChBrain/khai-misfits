## Tension

Here the failure stops being about anybody's conduct and becomes a property of the evidence.
Nothing was concealed from this seat and nothing was misreported to it. The right of attendance was real
and was honoured, the agenda was published, and the minutes afterwards confirm every observation the
watcher made.
[The watcher cannot tell](piece_the_watcher_cannot_tell.md): a short item, one speech, a unanimous vote and
an accurate minute are exactly what a well-run authority with an easy decision produces, so **the account
that would be true cannot be written and the account that can be written is thin**.
So the watcher writes the thin one, or writes nothing. And the silence that follows is later offered as
evidence that the sessions are satisfactory, which is a conclusion drawn from a difficulty rather than from
an absence of one.
