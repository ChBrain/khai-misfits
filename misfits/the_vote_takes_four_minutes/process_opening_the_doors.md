# Process: Opening the Doors

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). A statute defining an occasion and requiring that occasion to be public.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A legislature answering a real history of business done unseen.
At [the requirement seat](position_requirement.md), identically for every body in the jurisdiction.

## Direction

Toward an occasion anybody may attend, noticed in advance and minuted afterwards.
It succeeds. The notice goes up, the doors open, the minutes are accurate, and no exemption is abused.

## Lever

The definition, which is not adjustable without cost.
Sharpen it and it is enforceable and steppable; widen it and it reaches two colleagues talking in a
corridor, which is not a thing a legislature can forbid.

## Echo

A rule obeyed in full,
a boundary every competent body can see,
and the space immediately outside it becoming the most useful room in the building.
