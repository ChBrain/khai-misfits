# Persona: The Minute

## Taxonomy

[Record](position_record.md). The written account, accurate in every particular, standing in for a room it cannot carry.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Complete, correct, timely and public.
Names who moved, who seconded and who voted which way.
Has never been altered and would not be.

## Action

Records the item, the mover, the ninety seconds and the vote.
Publishes.
Is read a year later by somebody with one question.

## Shadow

That its accuracy is the whole of the difficulty.
**It reports what happened at the occasion and cannot report that the occasion was not where it happened**,
so it is a true document that supports a false inference, and correcting it is not possible because there
is nothing in it to correct.

## Tell

Is the evidence that the rule was obeyed.
Is the evidence that would look identical if it had not been.
