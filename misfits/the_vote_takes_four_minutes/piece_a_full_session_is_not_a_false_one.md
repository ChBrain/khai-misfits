# Piece: A Full Session Is Not a False One

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). The public meeting is complete, lawful and real, and the vote in it is the decision.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the chamber](place_the_chamber.md), on the notice, with the doors open.
In the minutes, which are accurate and would survive any audit.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play collapses into an exposé and the misfit disappears.
It is load bearing because **there is nothing here to expose**: no exemption was abused, no quorum met in
secret, no minute was altered, and a member who wished to reopen the item could have and would have been
heard. The occasion is not a performance of a decision, it **is** the decision, legally and in fact.

## Apparent

That a ratification must be a sham.
It is a real vote that a real body would be bound by, taken by people who have already heard the arguments
somewhere the rule does not go.

## Yearbook

The statutes themselves: a meeting defined by a quorum and a matter within the remit, required to be
noticed and open, with the definition doing the work that makes the requirement enforceable.
