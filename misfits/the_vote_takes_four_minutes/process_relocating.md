# Process: Relocating

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). The exchange of reasons finding the venue where it is cheapest, which is the one the rule does not name.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Work that still has to be done, and a price on doing it in the record.
At [the corridor](place_the_corridor.md), continuously, with no decision to relocate ever taken.

## Direction

Toward the briefing, the one-to-one and the call.
This is not selection by anybody: it is what a conversation does when one venue costs a career sentence and
the other costs nothing, and it would happen identically in any body under any comparable rule.

## Lever

None that anybody holds, and no moment at which it is chosen.
No member decides to move the deliberation; each takes the ordinary step of asking a colleague a question,
and the deliberation has moved by the time anybody could have noticed.

## Echo

Positions known before the doors open,
objections heard and answered where nothing is written,
and an item that takes four minutes because there is nothing left to say.
