# Piece: The Watcher Cannot Tell

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). A ratification and a genuine agreement leave the same record, so no inspection separates them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the gallery seat](position_gallery.md), which has the right and the afternoon and nothing else.
In the minutes, which are complete, and in the silence afterwards.

## Load Bearing

Without it the failure is discoverable and therefore fixable by somebody paying attention.
It is load bearing because **every observable fact is consistent with a body that simply agreed**: a short
item, one speech, a unanimous vote and an accurate minute are exactly what a well-run authority with an
easy decision produces, so the account that would be true cannot be written and the account that can be
written is thin.

## Apparent

That nobody complained, so there was nothing to complain about.
Nobody complained because a suspicion with no evidence behind it costs the person raising it and helps
nobody.

## Yearbook

The gap between an occasion and a decision: the record is a complete account of the first and has no
vocabulary for the second, so its accuracy is what makes the inference from it wrong.
