# Process: Attributing

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). A sentence being fixed to a person, kept, and handed to whoever asks for it later.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A minute, a transcript and a roll call, all of them correct.
At [the record seat](position_record.md), permanently, and at [the constituency seat](position_constituency.md) whenever anybody looks.

## Direction

Toward accountability, which is what the record is for and what it delivers.
The reader gets the sentence, the name and the date, and can hold the speaker to all three, which is not a
side effect of publication but its purpose.

## Lever

The reader's charity, which cannot be required and would defeat the point if it were.
Asking the constituency to read a tentative sentence as tentative is asking it to hold nobody to anything,
so the seat cannot be adjusted without removing the accountability the record exists for.

## Echo

A price on saying anything unfinished,
paid by whoever says it and by nobody who keeps quiet,
so the thinking moves and the record keeps only the conclusion.
