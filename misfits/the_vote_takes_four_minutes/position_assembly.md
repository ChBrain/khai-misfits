# Position: Assembly

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). The seat that must decide as a body and must be seen doing it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A remit, a quorum, and a calendar with a public session on it.
Members who will be judged separately for what the body does together.
Every hour that is not the session, and no rule about most of them.

## Orders

Arrive prepared, because arriving unprepared wastes the public's time.
Settle what can be settled before the item is called.
Meet in the open, on the notice, as the statute requires.

## Loses

Nothing, and it is obeying the rule in full.
**The two orders it is under do not conflict on their face**, since preparing is not deciding and a body
that has read its papers is a better body, so the seat can follow both completely and still leave the
session with nothing to do.

## Drives

Toward a public occasion that is complete and empty,
which no member intended and none can point to a moment of choosing,
because the preparation and the decision are the same conversation seen from two ends.
