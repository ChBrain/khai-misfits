## Tension

The play has to establish that the rule succeeded before anything else, and it succeeded completely.
The occasion is defined by a quorum and a matter within the remit, which is what makes it enforceable. The
notice goes up. The doors open. The minutes are accurate. No exemption is invoked and none is abused.
And the same sharpness that makes the definition enforceable is what leaves everything outside it
unreached. **A rule can attach to an occasion and cannot attach to a decision**, because a decision is not
an event anybody can point at, and no drafter can enumerate every way in which five people speak to one
another.
At the end of it the statute has done exactly what it says, and the space immediately outside its boundary
has become the most useful room in the building, which nobody chose and every version of the rule creates.
