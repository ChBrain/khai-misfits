# Position: Record

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). The seat that keeps what was said and hands it to whoever asks later.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A minute, a roll call, and in some bodies a full transcript.
Permanence, searchability, and no way to carry the room it was made in.
Readers who were not there and will not be told what the meeting felt like.

## Orders

Keep everything.
Attribute it.
Release it to anybody who asks, which is the point of keeping it.

## Loses

Nothing, and it is the seat doing the most work in the play.
**A record is not a report of a conversation, it is a different object**, because it is read later, by
people with their own reasons, without the context that made a tentative sentence tentative.

## Drives

Toward a price on saying anything unfinished,
paid by whoever says it and by nobody who keeps quiet,
so the seat that exists to preserve the deliberation is what makes it too expensive to hold there.
