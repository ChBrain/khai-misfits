# Position: Requirement

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). The seat where the rule's reach is fixed, occupied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A definition: a quorum, a matter within the remit, an occasion.
Enforceability, which comes entirely from that definition being sharp.
No opinion about anything that happens outside it.

## Orders

Attach to the occasion.
Do not attach to the decision, because a decision is not an event a rule can point at.
Apply identically to every body in the jurisdiction.

## Loses

Nothing, because it was never anybody's.
**The sharpness that makes it enforceable is the same sharpness that makes it avoidable without evasion**,
so a rule cannot be widened here without being blunted, and the trade was made at drafting by people who
had no better option.

## Drives

Toward a boundary that every competent body can see,
and toward the space immediately outside it becoming the most useful room in the building,
which no drafter chose and every drafter creates.
