# Process: Watching

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). Somebody exercising the right the statute created, and getting exactly what it promised.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An agenda, a free afternoon and a right of attendance.
At [the chamber](place_the_chamber.md) and [the livestream](place_the_livestream.md), whenever anybody chooses.

## Direction

Toward an accurate account of the occasion, which is delivered.
Nothing is concealed from the watcher, nothing is misreported, and the record afterwards confirms every
observation they made.

## Lever

None available to the watcher, who has no standing to ask anybody anything.
And no inference available either, because the observations are equally consistent with a body that agreed
and a body that settled elsewhere.

## Echo

A watcher who saw everything and learned nothing,
an account accurate in every particular and thin in the only one that matters,
and a silence afterwards that will be read as satisfaction.
