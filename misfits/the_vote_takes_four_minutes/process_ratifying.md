# Process: Ratifying

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). A settled matter being put through the occasion the statute requires, correctly and in full.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An item on a posted agenda, and a body that already knows where it stands.
At [the chamber](place_the_chamber.md), on the notice, in public.

## Direction

Toward a lawful decision, which it reaches.
The item is called, a member speaks, the vote is taken and recorded, and every element the statute
requires has occurred. Nothing here is a pretence: the vote is the vote, and it would bind even if
everybody in the room had changed their mind that morning.

## Lever

Nobody's, and nothing is being performed.
A member who wished to reopen the matter could, and would be heard, and the reason none does is that the
reopening already happened somewhere the record does not go.

## Echo

Four minutes,
a complete minute of them,
and an occasion indistinguishable from a body that simply agreed.
