# Persona: The One Who Must Vote

## Taxonomy

[Assembly](position_assembly.md). An elected member who reads the papers, has no settled view on item seven, and will be quoted.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Diligent, undecided, and willing to be argued with.
Would say "talk me out of it" to five colleagues in a room.
Believes in the open-meetings rule and voted for the last strengthening of it.

## Action

Asks the officer for a briefing.
Puts the doubt to two colleagues separately, and is answered.
Arrives at the session with a position, and speaks for ninety seconds.

## Shadow

That the reticence is a reading and not a failing.
**A sentence said into a transcript is read later, by people with their own reasons, without the room that
made it tentative**, so thinking aloud in the record is a thing done once, and the member who declines is
right about what it would cost and would be right in any body.

## Tell

Is the party the requirement was aimed at.
Is the party whose behaviour the requirement changed, in the direction nobody wanted.
