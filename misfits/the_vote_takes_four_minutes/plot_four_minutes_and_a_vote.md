## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

Item seven, called, on a nineteen-item agenda that has to be finished this evening.

## Action

[Ratifying](process_ratifying.md): [The Minute](persona_the_minute.md), in [the record seat](position_record.md), works through [Keep the Session Short](plan_keep_the_session_short.md).

## Stage

## Tension

This is the plot that has to be careful, because nothing here is a performance.
A member speaks for ninety seconds. Nobody dissents. The vote is taken and recorded, and it binds.
[A full session is not a false one](piece_a_full_session_is_not_a_false_one.md): a member who wished to
reopen the matter could have, and would have been heard, and none does because the reopening already
happened somewhere the record does not go.
And the brevity is a virtue by every measure the body is judged on. A nineteen-item agenda finished in an
evening is a well-run authority, the public's time is real, and **nothing distinguishes an efficient
session from an emptied one** in any figure anybody reports.
The minute is accurate. It names the item, the mover, the ninety seconds and the vote, and it would survive
any audit, because there is nothing in it that is not true.
