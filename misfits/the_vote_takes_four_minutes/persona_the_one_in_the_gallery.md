# Persona: The One in the Gallery

## Taxonomy

[Gallery](position_gallery.md). Somebody who took the afternoon off, sat at the back, and wrote down what was said.

Read the agenda and the papers beforehand.
Would report a breach of the rule if there were one, and there is not.

is unwriteable, the account that is writeable is thin, and a suspicion with no evidence behind it is worse
than nothing.
