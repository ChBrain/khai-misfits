## Tension

Each settlement gives back the thing it was for.
**Widen the definition** to catch serial contact, which several statutes have done and which is
enforceable, and the same words reach two colleagues asking each other a question, so a body that may not
confer arrives unprepared and the public's business is done worse in the open.
**Require the deliberation to be conducted in public** and not merely held there, and you have asked a rule
to observe the state of mind a member walks in with, which nothing can.
**Publish more**, transcripts as well as minutes, and
[dissent fell when the transcripts came](piece_dissent_fell_when_the_transcripts_came.md), so the move
everybody reaches for is the one measured to run the wrong way.
**Exempt preliminary discussion** by statute and you have conceded the thing the requirement was for.
[The Rule](persona_the_rule.md) is untouched by all four, because none of them changes what a stated
position costs the person who states it, and **the one that would is the one that asks the reader of a
record to hold nobody to anything.**
