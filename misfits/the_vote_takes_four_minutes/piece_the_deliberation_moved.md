# Piece: The Deliberation Moved

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). The exchange of reasons happened in full, and happened where the statute does not reach.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the corridor](place_the_corridor.md), in the days before the item is called.
In every briefing, call and separate conversation that a body needs and a rule cannot name.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is about a body that hides things, and stops being a misfit.
It is load bearing because **nothing was suppressed and nothing was concealed**: the doubt was raised, met
and answered, the member changed their mind, and every step of that is what a deliberating body is supposed
to do. What the rule lost is not the deliberation but the place it was held.

## Apparent

That a short session means a body with nothing to discuss.
It means a body that has discussed it. The two look identical from the gallery and produce the same
minutes.

## Yearbook

The bargaining result: a representative who must state a position in front of the constituents who will
judge it is pushed toward posturing and away from the concession a settlement needs, so the settlement is
reached where the constituents are not.
