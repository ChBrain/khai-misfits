# Piece: The Record Is the Cost

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). A position stated in a durable record is a different object from the same position stated among colleagues.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the record seat](position_record.md), which keeps it, and at [the constituency seat](position_constituency.md), which reads it.
In the head of every member deciding whether to think aloud.

## Load Bearing

Without it the members are timid and the play has a villain made of temperament.
It is load bearing because **the record cannot carry the room**: a sentence meant as an opening is read
later, by somebody with one question, without the five people it was addressed to or the tone it was said
in, so a tentative remark and a commitment are the same string of words on the page.

## Apparent

That publishing more would restore the candour.
Publishing more is what raises the price. The transcript is the record at its most complete and its most
expensive to speak into.

## Yearbook

The Federal Reserve evidence: after the committee began publishing full transcripts of its meetings, its
members were less willing to voice dissent in them, modelled as an expert advisor with career concerns
rather than as a loss of nerve.
