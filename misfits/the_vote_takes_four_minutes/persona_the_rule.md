# Persona: The Rule

## Taxonomy

[Requirement](position_requirement.md). The statute, doing exactly what it says and reaching exactly as far as it defines.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Clear, enforceable, popular and enforced.
Defines a meeting by a quorum and a matter within the remit.
Was strengthened twice and would be strengthened again if asked.

## Action

Requires notice.
Requires the doors open.
Requires the minutes, and stops.

## Shadow

That its reach and its enforceability are the same property.
**A definition sharp enough to be applied is sharp enough to be stepped outside without evasion**, so the
rule cannot be widened without becoming a rule about ordinary conversation, and the drafter who kept it
narrow was choosing enforceability rather than conceding anything.

## Tell

Is the only element in the play with no occupant.
Is the element every other one is obeying.
