# Plot: The Briefing Before

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Work that still has to be done, and a member with a genuine doubt about item seven.

## Action

[Relocating](process_relocating.md): [The One Who Must Vote](persona_the_one_who_must_vote.md), in [the assembly seat](position_assembly.md), works through [Know Where They Stand First](plan_know_where_they_stand_first.md).

## Stage

[The Corridor](place_the_corridor.md): an office, a call, ten minutes before the doors open, and a paper between two people.

## Tension

The second obvious answer closes here, and it closes on the member's diligence rather than in spite of it.
Nobody decides to move the deliberation. A member with a doubt asks the officer, then puts it to two
colleagues separately, which is what a prepared member does and what the statute plainly permits.
[The record is the cost](piece_the_record_is_the_cost.md): [attributing](process_attributing.md) a
sentence fixes it to a person, and [The Reader a Year Later](persona_the_reader_a_year_later.md), in
[the constituency seat](position_constituency.md), will find it with one question and without the room
that made it tentative, so thinking aloud is a thing done once and declining to do it is a reading rather
than a failing.
So [the deliberation moved](piece_the_deliberation_moved.md), and nothing about it was suppressed.
By the time the doors open the positions are known and the objections have been answered. **The
deliberation happened, in full, candidly, and left no account**, and the only party who could have stopped
it is the one whose whole reason for existing is that the body should be prepared.
