# Position: Gallery

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). The seat the requirement was written for, and the one with the least to work with.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A right of attendance, an agenda, a livestream and a minute.
An afternoon, and no standing to ask anybody anything.
A complete and accurate account of the occasion it was given.

## Orders

Come. Watch. Read the minutes.
Report what happened, which is what it saw.
Do not report what did not happen in front of it, because it has no way to know.

## Loses

The thing it came for, and it cannot establish that it lost anything.
**A ratification and an agreement leave the same record**, so the seat cannot distinguish a body that
settled elsewhere from a body that simply agreed, and an accusation it cannot support is worse than
silence.

## Drives

Toward a watcher who saw everything and learned nothing,
whose account is accurate in every particular,
and whose silence afterwards is read as satisfaction.
