# Pitch: Nothing Was Decided in the Room

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Precise about which gathering the statute defines and which it does not.
Unwilling to call any member a schemer or the public session a sham.
Clear that everything minuted actually happened.

## Undertow

Under the four minutes, one property of a record and one limit of a rule.
A position stated where it will be read later is a different object from a position stated among colleagues.
And a duty of publicity attaches to an occasion, which can be held while the deciding moves.

## Nerve

The unease of watching something real that is no longer where anything happens.
Nothing was hidden. Nothing was misreported. Nobody was kept out.
The reasons were exchanged, in full, in a room the law does not name.

## Echo

After the run: an agenda posted and a gallery open to anybody,
a briefing at which the positions were settled and which broke no rule,
a vote that took four minutes and was the real vote,
and a record that a ratification and an agreement would both have produced.
