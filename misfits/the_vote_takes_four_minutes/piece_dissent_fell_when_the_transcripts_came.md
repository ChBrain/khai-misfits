# Piece: Dissent Fell When the Transcripts Came

## Taxonomy

[The Vote Takes Four Minutes](play_the_vote_takes_four_minutes.md). The measured version: more publicity of debate, less willingness to voice a disagreement in it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the livestream](place_the_livestream.md), which is the same move in a smaller body.
In every proposal to answer the problem by publishing more.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play rests on an argument and not on a finding.
It is load bearing because **the remedy everybody reaches for is the one measured to run the wrong way**: a
committee that began publishing full transcripts of its meetings found its members less willing to voice
dissent in them, which turns the settlement of publishing more from an untried idea into a tested one.

## Apparent

That the members concerned were protecting themselves rather than doing their jobs.
The mechanism modelled is an expert advisor with career concerns, which is what anybody with a reputation
and a future has, and the response is rational at the level of the person and costly at the level of the
committee.

## Yearbook

Meade and Stasavage on the Federal Open Market Committee, using the 1993 decision to release full
transcripts as a change in publicity and reading the change in expressed dissent against it.
