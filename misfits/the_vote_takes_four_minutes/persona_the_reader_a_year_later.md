# Persona: The Reader a Year Later

## Taxonomy

[Constituency](position_constituency.md). Somebody with one question, a search box, and no memory of the evening.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Entitled, literate and doing exactly what the record was published for.
Was not in the room and was never going to be.
Would say, correctly, that a published transcript is there to be read.

## Action

Searches for the item.
Finds a sentence with a name against it.
Reads it as what the person thinks, because that is what a record of what a person said is.

## Shadow

That reading it that way is right and is what makes the room impossible.
**The record cannot carry the tone, the five people it was addressed to, or the fact that it was an
opening**, so a tentative remark and a settled commitment are the same string of words, and a reader who
allowed for the difference would be holding nobody to anything.

## Tell

Is the reason the record exists.
Is the reason nothing gets said into it.
