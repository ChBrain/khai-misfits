## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Load Bearing

Without it somebody could be told to use the word more carefully and the harm would go.
It is load bearing because **there is no careful version**: clarity fixes what the word points at,
adoption makes everybody point that way, and sincerity means the pointing is done about real people, and
those three are simultaneously the definition of a working replacement and the complete method for
building the association that ends it.

## Apparent

That the word could be used more sparingly to make it last.
A word used sparingly has not replaced the old one, which survives beside it and stays available.

## Yearbook

The X-phemism framework, which holds the referent constant across a set of synonyms and locates the whole
difference between them in connotation and in the circumstances they suit.
