# Piece: Nobody Misused It

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). The child in the playground learned the word from adults using it correctly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the playground](place_the_playground.md), the first time it is heard as an insult.
In the absence of any adult who taught it as one.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play has a culprit and stops being a misfit.
It is load bearing because **the lesson is what transmitted it**: the word arrived through correct use,
was learned correctly, and is being used for what it points at, which is the feeling attached to the
thing, so there is no misuse to find and no instruction that would have prevented it.

## Apparent

That children should be taught not to use it that way.
They were taught what it means, thoroughly and by everybody, and what it means now includes the feeling.

## Yearbook

Pejoration by association as historical linguistics has long described it, a neutral term acquiring
negative connotation through nothing but its subject.
