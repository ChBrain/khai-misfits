# Process: Souring

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). The word arriving at the feeling that was attached to the thing all along.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Nothing. It is the association reaching completion.
At [the playground](place_the_playground.md), audibly, some years after adoption.

## Direction

Toward a word that carries what its referent carries.
There is no wrong turn here to identify: the connotation travels because the reference is reliable, and
the reference is reliable because everybody did as they were asked.

## Lever

None at all.
The process has no participants and no decisions, which is why every intervention in this play is aimed
at the word before or the word after and never at this.

## Echo

A term that is now an insult,
a general agreement that a better word is needed,
and no one to say what should have been done differently.
