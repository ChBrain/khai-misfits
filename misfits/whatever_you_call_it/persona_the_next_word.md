# Persona: The Next Word

## Taxonomy

[Recourse](position_recourse.md). The replacement being drafted before anybody has finished learning the last one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Longer, usually. More technical, often. More recently coined, always.
Chosen with all the experience of the previous three rounds.
Better than what it replaces, which is not in dispute.

## Action

Arrives.
Buys an interval, proportional to how hard it is to say.
Enters the same sequence at the same point.

## Shadow

That its only advantage is unfamiliarity, which it spends by being used.
**The lever is awkwardness**, so the remedy for a short-lived word is a word people cannot quite manage,
and a word people cannot quite manage has not replaced anything yet.

## Tell

Is the correct response to the situation.
Is the situation, one turn on.
