# Process: Choosing

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). Selecting a word to replace one that has turned.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A current term that has become something you can be called in a corridor.
At [the style guide](place_the_style_guide.md), after enough people have said so.

## Direction

Toward an expression that is accurate, plain and not yet spoiled.
This is done well and is done by people acting for those the word names, often at their asking, and the
result is genuinely better than what it replaces.

## Lever

The syllables, which are the only part of a word this process can reach.
There is no lever for the connotation, because the connotation is not in the word yet and will be
supplied by something else.

## Echo

A better word,
an entry in the guide written as though it were permanent,
and a clock nobody in the room can see.
