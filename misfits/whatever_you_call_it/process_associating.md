# Process: Associating

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). Learners working out what the word points at, from correct examples.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The word, used accurately, in front of somebody who does not yet know it.
At [the form](place_the_form.md) and everywhere else, continuously, invisibly.

## Direction

Toward competent use of the language as it is spoken.
This is what learning a word is, is done by everybody, and cannot be done any other way: usage is the
only evidence there is about meaning.

## Lever

None, unless the word is withheld, which would make it useless.
The process cannot be slowed without making the word less clear, less adopted or less sincerely used,
and all three are the properties the replacement was for.

## Echo

A population that knows what the word means,
which is to say a population that knows what it points at,
and a connotation now attached to the pointer.
