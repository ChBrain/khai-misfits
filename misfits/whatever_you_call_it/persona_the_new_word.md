# Persona: The New Word

## Taxonomy

[Use](position_use.md). The chosen expression, in circulation, doing exactly what it was chosen to do.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Clear, dignified, and briefly weightless.
Carries no history, which is why it was picked.
Is written on forms, said in waiting rooms, taught to children.

## Action

Points at the thing.
Points at it accurately, consistently, and everywhere.
Becomes the word for it.

## Shadow

That being the word for it is the whole of the exposure.
**A word has no defence against its own referent**, and the better it does its job the faster it is
reached, so its competence and its lifespan are the same quantity read twice.

## Tell

Is the improvement, and is not pretending to be one.
Is the improvement that will be the insult.
