# Plot: And Then It Is the Insult

## Action

[Souring](process_souring.md): the association reaching completion, with nobody performing it.

## Stage

[The Playground](place_the_playground.md): one child using the current official term as an insult, and another understanding it at once.

## Tension

This is the plot with no actor in it, which is what makes it the turn.
The child did not learn the word as an insult. It was learned from adults using it properly, on forms and
in waiting rooms and in conversation, about real people, and it was learned correctly. What the child is
using it for is the feeling attached to the thing, because that is now part of what the word means.
There is no misuse to locate. There is no adult to correct, no lesson that would have prevented it, and
no moment at which somebody should have been more careful, because carefulness is the mechanism.
And the second child's immediate understanding is the proof. The word did not turn in one mouth. It
turned everywhere at once, quietly, over years, and the playground is simply where it became audible to
the people who chose it.
