# Plot: A Kinder Word Is Chosen

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The current term, heard in a corridor, used at somebody.

## Action

[Choosing](process_choosing.md): [The Advocate](persona_the_advocate.md), in [the naming seat](position_naming.md), works through [Choose a Word Without a History](plan_choose_a_word_without_a_history.md).

## Stage

[The Style Guide](place_the_style_guide.md): an entry, dated, with a short and persuasive reason.

## Tension

The play has to establish first that this was done well, because everything after it depends on there
being nothing to correct here.
The word was not chosen by a committee protecting itself from complaint. It was chosen for the people it
is used about, usually at their asking, after the previous one had become something you could be called.
It is accurate, it is plain, and it is better. Nobody in this plot is being precious and nobody is being
absurd.
And what is being selected is the **syllables**, which is the only part of a word a decision can reach.
The connotation is not in the word yet. It will be supplied later, by whatever the word turns out to
point at, and that is settled somewhere this seat has no access to at all.
The entry goes in written as permanent. So did the last one.
