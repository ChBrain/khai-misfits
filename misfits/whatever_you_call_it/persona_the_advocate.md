# Persona: The Advocate

## Taxonomy

[Naming](position_naming.md). The person who proposes the better word, for the people it will be used about.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, well-briefed, and acting on somebody else's behalf or their own.
Has watched the previous word turn into something said in corridors.
Chooses for accuracy and dignity, and gets it right.

## Action

Proposes the word.
Argues it into the style guide.
Moves on to other work.

## Shadow

That the property being chosen is not the property that decides.
**The syllables can be selected and the connotation cannot**, because the connotation will be supplied
later by what the word turns out to point at, and that is decided somewhere this seat has no access to.

## Tell

Is the party who did the kind thing.
Is the party who set the clock running.
