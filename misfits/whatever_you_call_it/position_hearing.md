# Position: Hearing

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). The seat that learns what a word means by hearing it used.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

No dictionary, and no need of one.
A stream of correct uses by adults who know what they are talking about.
The ordinary human machinery for working out what an expression points at.

## Orders

Work out the referent.
Attach to the word whatever is felt about the referent, which is what a connotation is.
Use the word for that feeling, since that is now what it carries.

## Loses

Any way to keep the two apart.
**A learner cannot separate the word from what it is used for**, because being used for it is the only
evidence available about what it means, so a word learned perfectly is a word that has taken on
everything attached to its subject.

## Drives

Toward competent use of the language as spoken,
which is the whole of what this seat is for,
so the seat that learns correctly is the seat that completes the transfer.
