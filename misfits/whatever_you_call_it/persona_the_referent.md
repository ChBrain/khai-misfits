# Persona: The Referent

## Taxonomy

[Reference](position_reference.md). The thing itself, unchanged, supplying the feeling that every word for it will collect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Constant, indifferent, and entirely uninvolved in the naming.
Carries whatever people feel about it, which nobody chose either.
Would be exactly the same under any expression whatever.

## Action

Stays what it is.
Is pointed at.
Supplies the connotation.

## Shadow

That it is the only thing that matters and the only thing untouched.
**Every remedy in the play is a move on the expression**, and the expression was never where the trouble
lived, so this persona sits through the entire production without being addressed once.

## Tell

Is not a party to anything.
Is the reason there is a play.
