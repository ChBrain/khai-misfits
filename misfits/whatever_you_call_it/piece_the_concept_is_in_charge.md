# Piece: The Concept Is in Charge

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). The connotation lives in the thing, not the word, so any word for the thing collects it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the reference seat](position_reference.md), which supplies it.
Wherever a replacement is chosen for its syllables and judged by what it comes to mean.

## Load Bearing

Without it the turnover looks like bad luck in the choosing, and a better committee would fix it.
It is load bearing because **a word has no defence against its own referent**: the connotation is not
stored where a naming decision could reach it, so no expression can be selected to resist a feeling that
will be supplied later by the thing it turns out to point at.

## Apparent

That a sufficiently neutral coinage would hold.
Neutrality is the starting condition of every replacement and is the thing being spent; a coinage holds
for as long as nobody knows what it means.

## Yearbook

The formulation that concepts and not words are in charge, so giving a concept a new name colours the
name, which is the sentence the play runs on.
