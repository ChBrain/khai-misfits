# Position: Use

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). The seat where the word is actually said, about real people, by people being careful.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A form to complete, a letter to write, a conversation to have.
A word that is currently correct and that everyone has agreed on.
A reason to be precise, because the subject is somebody's life.

## Orders

Use the current word.
Use it accurately, which means about exactly the thing it names.
Use it often, because consistency is what makes it the word.

## Loses

Nothing, and that is the difficulty.
**Every use here is correct and every use here is a deposit**, since accuracy is what fixes the
association between the word and the thing, and the association is the channel the feeling travels
along.

## Drives

Toward consistent, respectful, accurate speech,
which is exactly what was asked for and exactly what is delivered,
so the seat doing the right thing is the seat doing the damage.
