# Position: Recourse

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Has

Four options, all of them real, none of them free.
A word that is now being used as an insult by people who learned it properly.
Everybody's agreement that something should be done.

## Orders

Replace it, which works and starts the clock.
Or hold the line, which spares the effort and spends it on the people addressed.
Or address the stigma, which is the only move that reaches the mechanism.

## Loses

The distinction between the remedy and the next iteration.
**Replacing is both the fix and the thing being fixed**, so a seat that succeeds perfectly at its task
has produced exactly the situation it inherited, one word later, and will be occupied again.

## Drives

which is available, and works, and expires,
so the seat with the remedy is the seat that has the same job for ever.
