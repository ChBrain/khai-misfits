# Piece: It Is Not Overuse

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). The word is not worn out by repetition; it acquires a specific content it did not start with.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Wherever this is offered as an example of a word losing its force.
At [the reference seat](position_reference.md), which is where the content comes from.

## Load Bearing

Without it this collapses into semantic bleaching and into the house's **More to Stand Still**, and earns
no separate staging.
It is load bearing because **the change is an addition and not a wearing-off**: bleaching leaves a word
meaning less, and stipulating restrained use fixes it, whereas here the word ends up meaning something
quite definite that it did not mean before, and restraint changes nothing because the content comes from
the referent and not from the frequency.

## Apparent

That a word used less often would keep its edge.
It would keep its edge and lose its job, and the old word would still be the one in circulation.

## Yearbook

The distinction between a term whose force fades through overuse and a term that has taken on the
character of what it is used for.
