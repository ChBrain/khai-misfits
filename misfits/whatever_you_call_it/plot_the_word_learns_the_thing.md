## Tension

Here the transfer happens, and it happens through the machinery by which anybody learns anything.
A learner has no dictionary and needs none. The evidence about what an expression means is how it is
used, and it is being used correctly, by people who know what they are talking about, about exactly the
thing it names. So the referent is inferred, accurately, which is the whole of what learning a word is.
And **the reference cannot be acquired without what comes attached to it.** Whatever is felt about the
thing, fear or embarrassment or pity or contempt, is now what the word carries, because the word's entire
content is what it points at.
So the better the teaching, the more complete the transfer. [It Is Not Overuse](piece_it_is_not_overuse.md):
nothing here is being worn out. The word is not losing meaning, it is **gaining** one, quite definite,
that it did not have when it was chosen.
