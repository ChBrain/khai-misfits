## Tension

Each escape gives back the thing it was for.
Choose something longer and more technical, which is what everybody does by the third round, and you have
bought a longer interval with **awkwardness**, since unfamiliarity is what slows the association and
unfamiliarity is the same property as being hard to say.
Refuse to change at all, and you are addressing people in terms that have already turned, which is a real
cost and is not paid by whoever made the decision.
Reclaim the word, which does sometimes work and works well, and you have reached for the one move that is
available only to the people it is used against, cannot be organised on their behalf, and does not
generalise.
Address the stigma itself, which is the only remedy that touches [The Referent](persona_the_referent.md)
and therefore the only one that removes the engine, and you have named the thing everybody was already
trying to do when they changed the word in the first place.
And the entry goes in written as permanent, which is not naivety. Nobody chooses a temporary word.
