# Process: Adopting

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). Getting everybody to use the new word instead of the old one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A guide entry, and a general willingness to be considerate.
At [the form](place_the_form.md), across an institution, over a year or two.

## Direction

Toward consistent use everywhere the word is needed.
Consistency is not incidental: a word that only some people use has not replaced anything, and the old
one survives beside it and keeps being available.

## Lever

Guidance, training, and the ordinary wish not to give offence.
All three are effective, which is why adoption succeeds, and success is measured in exactly the quantity
that spoils the word.

## Echo

One word in use where there were two,
an interval in which it works,
and an association forming at the rate of the adoption.
