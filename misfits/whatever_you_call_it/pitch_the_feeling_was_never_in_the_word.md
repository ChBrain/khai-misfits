# Pitch: The Feeling Was Never in the Word

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact about what a word carries, and about where it carries it from.
Unwilling to fault either the people who choose the words or the people who spoil them.
Clear that the kindness was real for as long as it lasted.

## Undertow

Under the turnover, one relation from linguistics and one from ordinary life.
Concepts are in charge, so a new name for a concept is coloured by it.
And a replacement that is not used clearly, widely and in earnest has replaced nothing.

## Nerve

The unease of a good act that has to be performed again, and again, for ever.
Nobody spoiled the word. The word did its job.
Doing its job is what spoiled it.

## Echo

After the run: a term chosen with care by people acting for those it names,
an interval in which it worked exactly as intended,
a child in a playground using it correctly and cruelly,
and a meeting to choose the next one.
