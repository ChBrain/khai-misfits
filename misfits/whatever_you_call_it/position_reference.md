# Position: Reference

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). The seat held by the thing itself, which does not move and is not consulted.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Whatever people actually feel about it: fear, embarrassment, pity, contempt.
No stake in which expression is currently used for it.
Complete indifference to the naming, and complete control over the outcome.

## Orders

Stay what it is.
Be pointed at, by whatever is currently pointing.
Supply the connotation, which is the only thing this seat does.

## Loses

Nothing at all, which is precisely the problem.
**This seat is never the target of any remedy attempted in the play.** Every move made elsewhere is a
move on the expression, and the expression was never where the trouble lived, so the seat that decides
the outcome is the one seat nobody in the sequence is addressing.

## Drives

Toward remaining exactly what it was,
which requires no effort and receives none,
so the immovable seat is the seat that makes the treadmill turn.
