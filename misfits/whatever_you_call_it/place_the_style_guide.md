# Place: The Style Guide

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). Where the current word is written down as the current word.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An entry, dated, with a note about what it replaces.
A short reason, usually accurate and usually persuasive.
An implicit promise that this one is settled.

## Holds

The moment the choice becomes an institution.
**Everything in here is a decision about syllables**, and the guide has no field for the thing the
syllables will point at, because that is not a matter of style and is not anybody's to write down.

## Offers

A consistent, defensible, kinder vocabulary.

## Withheld

Any expiry date.
The entry is written as permanent because nobody in the room believes they are choosing a temporary word,
and each previous entry was written the same way.
