# Persona: The Listener

## Taxonomy

[Hearing](position_hearing.md). Anybody learning the word from hearing it used, which includes the child in the playground.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, attentive, and equipped with nothing but examples.
Never consults a dictionary and does not need to.
Learns what an expression points at, which is what learning a word is.

## Action

Hears the word used correctly.
Infers the referent.
Takes on whatever is felt about the referent, and uses the word for that.

## Shadow

That learning it properly is what completes the transfer.
**Being used for the thing is the only evidence there is about what a word means**, so there is no way
to acquire the reference without acquiring what comes attached to it, and a perfect learner is the most
efficient carrier.

## Tell

Is the party the play refuses to blame.
Is the party through whom the connotation arrives.
