# Position: Naming

## Taxonomy

[Whatever You Call It](play_whatever_you_call_it.md). The seat that picks the better word, after the last one turned.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A word that has become something you can be called in a corridor.
A brief from, or on behalf of, the people it names.
A shortlist of expressions that are accurate, plain and not yet spoiled.

## Orders

Choose something clear, so that it communicates.
Choose something dignified, so that it is an improvement.
Get it adopted, because a word nobody uses has replaced nothing.

## Loses

Any control over the property it is actually selecting for.
**The seat can choose the syllables and not the connotation**, because the connotation is not in the
word and cannot be put there: it will be supplied later, by what the word turns out to point at, and
that is settled outside this seat entirely.

## Drives

Toward a word that does not wound,
which it finds, and which does not wound, for a while,
so the seat that performs the kindness is the seat that sets the clock running.
