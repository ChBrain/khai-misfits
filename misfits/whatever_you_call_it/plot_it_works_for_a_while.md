## Tension

The interval is real and the production insists on it.
For a period that may run to a decade or more, the word does exactly what it was chosen to do: people are
described in terms that do not wound them, forms read decently, and the old word falls out of use and
takes its history with it. That is a good outcome and it was produced on purpose.
The word achieves this by being used **clearly**, so that everybody knows what it points at, and
**widely**, so that the old one has nowhere left to live, and **in earnest**, about real people, by
clinicians and officials and neighbours who have read the guidance and mean it.
Those three are the definition of a working replacement.
They are also, exactly and without remainder, the method for building the association that will end it,
which is why nothing in this plot can be done more carefully.
