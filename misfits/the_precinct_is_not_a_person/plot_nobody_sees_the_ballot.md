# Plot: Nobody Sees the Ballot

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Missing Record Is Guaranteed, Not Withheld](piece_the_missing_record_is_guaranteed_not_withheld.md): a curtain, a folded paper, and a procedure built so nothing can be reconstructed.

## Action

[Securing](process_securing.md): [The Guarantee](persona_the_guarantee.md), in [the secrecy seat](position_secrecy.md), works through [Let No One See It](plan_let_no_one_see_it.md).

## Stage

[The Booth](place_the_booth.md): no camera, no signature on the ballot, no register of what was marked.

## Tension

The play has to establish first that this is an achievement, because everything after it depends on there
being nothing here anybody would undo.
It was fought for against the watching, the buying and the punishing of voters. It admits no exception,
and the absence of an exception is what makes it a guarantee rather than a policy: a ballot that could be
opened for good purposes is a ballot that can be opened.
And **the absence it produces is the product.** The missing record is not a gap somebody failed to fill,
not a file held securely elsewhere, and not a dataset awaiting a sharing agreement. It was never made.
So every difficulty in the rest of this production traces back to a room that is working exactly as
intended, and no reform reaches it, because the reform is the thing being prevented.
