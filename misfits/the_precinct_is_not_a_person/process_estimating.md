# Process: Estimating

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). Recovering an individual quantity from aggregate totals, by the best method available.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A required finding about individuals and evidence about precincts.
At [the courtroom](place_the_courtroom.md), in preparation, weeks before the hearing.

## Direction

Toward the best estimate the field knows how to produce.
The estimators are not guesses: they recover the individual quantity exactly, provided one thing is true
about how groups behave from place to place, and that condition is written into every account of them.

## Lever

The assumption, which is the only adjustable part.
It cannot be tested against the data in hand, because the individual level is unobserved, and being
unobserved is the entire reason the process was started.

## Echo

A number that is correct about precincts,
an assumption stated in the report,
and no way, from these data, to find out whether it holds.
