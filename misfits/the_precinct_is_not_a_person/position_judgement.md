# Position: Judgement

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). The seat that must find, one way or the other, on a date.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A case brought, a statute that makes the finding a required element, and a hearing date.
Expert evidence on both sides, each competent and each qualified.
No power to obtain what does not exist.

## Orders

Weigh the evidence that is before it.
Find, because a finding is what the statute requires.
Give reasons, in terms the parties and an appellate court can follow.

## Loses

The option to wait for better evidence, which is the option every other seat still has.
**A court cannot adjourn until the individual data appear**, because they will not appear, so the
interval it is handed must be converted into a determination about people by a forum whose whole function
is to convert.

## Drives

Toward a decision on the best evidence there is,
which is what a court is for and what it does,
so the seat that must resolve it is the seat with the least room to hold the difficulty open.
