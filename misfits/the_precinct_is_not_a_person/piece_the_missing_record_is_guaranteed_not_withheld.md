# Piece: The Missing Record Is Guaranteed, Not Withheld

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). The individual data are absent because an institution exists to make them absent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the booth](place_the_booth.md), where the link is destroyed on purpose.
In every request for finer data, which is a request about the ballot rather than about a publication.

## Load Bearing

Without it this is the house's **Wherever You Draw It**, where a bureau published at its own units and
things could have gone otherwise.
It is load bearing because **the absence is the product**: an arrangement that let the analyst recover
how a person voted would be an arrangement that had abandoned the guarantee, so there is no reform, no
budget and no data-sharing agreement that reaches it.

## Apparent

That someone somewhere holds the finer file securely.
Nobody does. It was never made, which is the difference between a record protected and a record
prevented.

## Yearbook

The secret ballot as a deliberate institution, fought for against the watching, buying and punishing of
voters, and defended since the nineteenth century without exception for good purposes.
