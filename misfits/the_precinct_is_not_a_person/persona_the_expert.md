# Persona: The Expert

## Taxonomy

[Inference](position_inference.md). The witness who estimates individual behaviour from totals, and says so.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Qualified, careful, and candid about the method.
Uses the estimator the field regards as best, and reports its assumptions in the report.
Would give the same evidence for either party, and often has.

## Action

Computes the estimate.
States the assumption it rests on.
Answers questions about it, accurately, in a room that came for the number.

## Shadow

That the assumption is the one thing the evidence cannot speak to.
**The output and the validity are answerable from the same data**, and that data is aggregate, so a
witness who could verify the premise would not have needed the method, and the candour in the report
cannot be converted into a check.

## Tell

Is the party who understands the difficulty best.
Is the party who supplies the number anyway, because somebody must.
