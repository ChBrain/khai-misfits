## Tension

The methods are not guesses, which the play says plainly.
Ecological regression and its modern successors recover the individual quantity exactly, **provided** the
behaviour of a group does not depend on where that group lives. That premise is written into every
account of them and is stated in the report.
And it is the one thing the evidence cannot speak to. The individual level is unobserved, which is why
the estimate was attempted, so the output and the validity are answerable from the same data and neither
is. An analyst able to verify the premise would not have needed the method.
Nor do the diagnostics close it: tested against data where the individual truth is known, the estimates
have not always recovered it, and the warnings have not always fired.
What is left that depends on nothing is [The Bounds Are True and Wide](piece_the_bounds_are_true_and_wide.md):
an interval implied by the totals alone, unattackable in cross-examination, and often wide enough to
contain the finding and its opposite.
