# Piece: The Group Is Not the People in It

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). A relationship between group means is a different quantity from the one among individuals.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the return sheet](place_the_return_sheet.md), the moment two columns are correlated.
Wherever an aggregate result is quoted as a statement about the people counted in it.

## Load Bearing

Without it the ecological estimate is merely imprecise, and more precincts would fix it.
It is load bearing because **grouping removes the variation inside each group**, so what remains is a
relationship between averages: it can be far stronger than the individual one, can be weaker, and can run
the other way, and more precincts make it more precise about precincts and no closer to people.

## Apparent

That a large enough sample of areas converges on the individual answer.
It converges on the ecological answer, which is a different number, and converges very well.

## Yearbook

The demonstration that there need be no correspondence between an individual correlation and an
ecological one, with the case in which a higher share of foreign birth accompanied higher literacy while
the foreign-born were themselves less literate.
