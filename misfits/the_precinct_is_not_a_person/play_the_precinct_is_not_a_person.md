# Play: The Precinct Is Not a Person

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Precinct Is Not a Person.
A court has to decide whether a group of voters votes cohesively, because a statute makes that finding the hinge of the case.
The ballot is secret. No record connects any person to a vote, none ever will, and that is the point of the institution rather than a gap in it.
So the finest evidence there can be is the precinct return: a count of votes beside a count of residents. The relationship between them is computed, correctly, and it is a fact about precincts, and the court needs a fact about people.

## Arc

The bend is this: the guarantee that protects the voter is the same guarantee that makes the question unanswerable, and the question still has to be answered by Friday.

It runs on one relation from statistics and one from the institution. **A relationship between group means is a different quantity from the relationship among individuals**, because grouping removes the variation inside each group, so the two can differ in size and in direction and both be correct; and **the individual record cannot exist**, because a system that let anybody reconstruct it would have given up the thing it was built to protect.

Take the secrecy first, because the play has to establish that it is an achievement. It was fought for, it is defended, and its whole purpose is that nobody can find out how a person voted. An arrangement in which the expert could simply look would be an arrangement that had abandoned the ballot. Nothing in this production wants that back.

Then the fact that inverts it. **The estimate is not a worse measurement of the individual quantity, it is a measurement of something else.** Across states a higher share of foreign birth went with higher literacy, and the foreign-born were themselves less literate, and neither statement is a mistake. So a correct number arrives in a courtroom carrying a description it does not have.

Then the sharper half, which is why the methods do not close it. Every estimator that recovers the individual quantity does so **under an assumption about how groups behave across places**, and that assumption is exactly what an aggregate cannot report on, since the individual level is unobserved and that is the whole reason the estimate was attempted. The deterministic bounds are honest and are often too wide to decide anything; the models that choose within them have been tested where the truth is known and have not always found it, without their diagnostics saying so.

And nobody has done anything wrong. The secrecy is a guarantee. The count is complete. The expert used the best method the field has and stated its limits. The court cannot adjourn until the individual data appear, because they will not appear, because that was decided in advance and on purpose.

Reach for a settlement and each one gives back the thing that was wanted. Report the bounds, which is the honest move, and you have handed a court an interval that spans the finding and its opposite. Use survey data, which is real evidence, and you have answered a question about respondents. Run a sensitivity analysis across assumptions, which is right, and you have produced a range instead of a determination. And obtain the individual record, which would settle it entirely, and you have proposed dismantling the thing the case is about.

## Stakes

Whether a finding about people can rest on the only evidence a free election permits.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the secrecy is right, because it is and the production defends it, and not whether the method is wrong, because it is the best the field has, but whether a correct statement about precincts can be converted into a determination about voters by a forum that has no choice but to convert it. The production cannot settle it. It can only show that the record is missing on purpose, that the estimate measures a different quantity, that the assumption every method needs is the one the data cannot speak to, and that every escape returns a range where a finding was required.
