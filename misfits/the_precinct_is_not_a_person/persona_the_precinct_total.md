# Persona: The Precinct Total

## Taxonomy

[Tabulation](position_tabulation.md). A count of votes beside a count of residents, complete and correct.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Accurate, public, auditable, and coarse.
Is not a summary of a finer file, because there is no finer file.
Is the smallest lawful reporting unit and would be smaller only if the ballot were less secret.

## Action

Records what was cast.
Records who lives there.
Says nothing whatever about which of them cast what.

## Shadow

That being a floor is different from being a rounding.
**This is the most detailed record that can exist**, so an analyst who wants more is not asking for a
better extract, they are asking for the guarantee to be weaker, and there is nowhere else to look.

## Tell

Is complete and correct and beyond dispute.
Is about a place, and the case is about people.
