# Position: Recourse

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). The seat trying to make the estimate honest without making it useless.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Deterministic bounds that are true whatever the model says.
Survey data, exit polls, sensitivity analyses across assumptions.
A duty to overstate nothing, and an audience that needs a finding.

## Orders

Report what the totals actually permit.
Show how the answer moves as the assumptions move.
Refuse to present a point estimate as though it were observed.

## Loses

The distinction between honesty and unhelpfulness.
**The bounds are true and are often too wide to decide anything**, so the most defensible output of this
seat is the one that leaves the court exactly where it started, and every narrowing of it is bought with
an assumption the data cannot support.

## Drives

Toward an answer that does not claim more than it knows,
which is the correct professional posture,
so the seat that behaves best returns the least usable thing.
