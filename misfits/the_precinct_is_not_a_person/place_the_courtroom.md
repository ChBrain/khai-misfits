# Place: The Courtroom

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). Where an estimate about places is turned into a finding about people.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two qualified experts, disagreeing about an assumption rather than about arithmetic.
A statute that makes the finding a required element of the claim.
A hearing date, and no mechanism for adjourning until better evidence exists.

## Holds

The conversion, and no marker for it.
**This room's function is to turn uncertainty into determination**, which is what makes it a court, and
nothing in the procedure distinguishes a quantity that was measured from one that was inferred under a
premise nobody could test.

## Offers

A finding, reasoned, appealable, and on the date.

## Withheld

Any power to obtain the missing record.
A court can compel a great many documents and cannot compel one that was never permitted to exist.
