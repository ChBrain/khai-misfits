# Process: Deciding

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). Turning the evidence into a finding, because a statute requires one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A case brought, an element that must be proved, and a hearing date.
At [the courtroom](place_the_courtroom.md), on the day.

## Direction

Toward a reasoned determination on the best evidence available.
This is what a court is for, is done conscientiously, and would be a failure of duty if it were declined.

## Lever

The weight given to competing experts, which is the only thing the forum controls.
It cannot adjudicate an assumption that neither side can test, and it cannot compel a record that was
never permitted to exist.

## Echo

A finding about how a group of people voted,
resting on a number about precincts,
with nothing in the reasons marking the point at which one became the other.
