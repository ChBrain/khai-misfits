# Position: Inference

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). The seat asked to say how a group voted, from totals.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The returns, the roll, and every method the field has produced.
A question about individuals and evidence about precincts.
A professional obligation to state the limits, which it discharges.

## Orders

Estimate the individual quantity from the aggregate.
Use the best available estimator.
Report the assumptions and the uncertainty, in the report, where they belong.

## Loses

The ability to check the one thing the estimate depends on.
**Every method needs an assumption about how groups behave across places, and the aggregate cannot speak
to it**, because the individual level is unobserved and that is precisely why the estimate is being
attempted, so the method's validity and the method's output are answerable from the same data and
therefore neither is.

## Drives

which it produces, competently, with its limits written down,
so the seat that knows most about the difficulty is the seat that supplies the number.
