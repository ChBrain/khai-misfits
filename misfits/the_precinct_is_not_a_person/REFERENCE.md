# The Precinct Is Not a Person: Reference

The research warrant for The Precinct Is Not a Person.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the ecological fallacy, on the ecological inference problem
that follows from it, and on the secret ballot as the case where the individual record cannot exist even
in principle, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging a guarantee as **the reason a question cannot be answered**: a court has to decide whether a
group of voters votes cohesively, because a statute makes that finding the hinge of the case; the ballot
is secret, so no record of how any person voted exists or ever will; the finest evidence there can be is
the precinct return, a count of votes beside a count of residents; an expert computes the relationship
between them, correctly; and the number that comes out is a fact about precincts and is being used as a
fact about people. The play models the documented mechanics, the secrecy that is a guarantee rather than
an omission, the total that is the finest record the system can produce, the aggregation that inflates
the correlation without anybody's error, the assumption that every method needs and that no aggregate
can test, and the bounds that are honest and too wide to settle anything, as the load-bearing vectors of
the structure, not as a tale of a careless expert or a credulous court.

The governing law is Robinson's: there need be no correspondence between the individual correlation and
the ecological correlation. The two are different quantities, not one quantity measured well and badly.
Grouping removes the variation within each group, so what is left is a relationship between group means,
and a relationship between group means can be far stronger than the one among individuals, can be weaker,
and can run the other way. His own case is the one still taught: across states, a higher share of foreign
birth went with higher literacy, and among individuals the foreign-born were less literate, and both are
correct.

What makes it structural rather than a fault of technique is that no aggregate can check the assumption
that any method needs. Ecological regression and its modern successors are not guesses; they are
estimators that recover the individual quantity **if** the behaviour of a group does not vary with where
that group lives. That premise is exactly what an aggregate cannot report on, because the whole reason
the estimate is being attempted is that the individual level is unobserved. So the method's output and
the method's validity are answerable from the same data, which is to say neither is.

What removes any possibility of blame is that every party is doing the right thing and one of them is
doing a very good thing. **The secrecy is not an oversight.** It is a guarantee, deliberately built,
defended for a century and a half, and its whole point is that nobody can find out how a person voted;
a system that let the expert answer the question would be a system that had abandoned it. The count is
complete and correct. The expert applies the best method the field has, and reports its limits. The court
cannot decline to rule, because a case has been brought and a statute requires the finding.

And the harm is not a mistake in anybody's arithmetic. It arrives as a number that is exactly right about
one thing and is being asked about another, delivered into a forum that must convert it into a finding
about people, with no instrument anywhere in the room that could tell the two apart.

It runs against the house's **Only One Person Fits**, on the same axis and with the opposite sign. There
a body releases records that must be detailed enough to answer a question, and detail is what narrows a
row to one person, so granularity is the harm. Here granularity is the whole of what is missing, and the
absence that protects the voter is the absence that makes the finding unavailable. That warrant already
says so in prose, that a question about a rare condition or a small place cannot be answered from a table
of totals, and this play is that sentence staged.

**What sets the sign on this axis is whether the row is being used or protected.** Where the output is an
inference about individuals, detail supplies the variation the inference needs and its absence is the
harm. Where the output is the protection of the person in the row, detail supplies the uniqueness that
identifies them and its presence is the harm. Same dial, opposite sign, and the discriminator is which of
those two the record is for. The plays differ again in how the dial got where it is: there a body chooses
a release and is caught between two harms it can trade off, and here the setting was fixed once, by a
constitutional commitment, and cannot be revisited at all.

It is distinct from the house's **The Bigger Picture**, which stages Simpson's paradox: there the
quantity is a confounder that reverses an association when groups are combined, and stipulating no such
subgroup structure removes the harm. Stipulate that here, no confounder anywhere and no reversal, and the
harm survives, because the ecological correlation still exceeds the individual one by the plain
arithmetic of removing within-group variance. The reversal is this play's most vivid case and not its
engine.

It is distinct from the house's **Wherever You Draw It**, which stages the modifiable areal unit problem:
there the quantity is the freedom to cut the territory a different way, and the harm is that no cutting
is correct. Stipulate a single fixed partition that nobody may modify, and this harm survives entirely,
because it is about the **level** of the unit rather than the **choice** of it. And the two are staged
apart on purpose: there the individual data are missing because a bureau published at its own units,
which is an administrative fact that could have gone otherwise, and here they are missing because a
democracy guarantees they will be.

And it is distinct from the house's **The Abstraction Ladder**, which that neighbour's warrant already
names as the same dial turned the other way: there a report climbs toward the abstract through a reporting
chain, shedding at each hop the specifics that would let it be checked. Stipulate no chain at all, one
authority and nobody summarising for a superior, and this harm survives entirely, because the precinct total
is not a summary of anything. Nothing was shed. The finer record was never made.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the expert
  as careless or the court as credulous, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the
  secrecy is a guarantee whose whole point is that the question cannot be answered from records, the
  count is complete and correct, the method is the best the field has and is applied with its limits
  stated, and the court cannot decline to rule on a matter a statute puts before it. The failure is
  authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a correct number about
  precincts must be converted into a finding about people by a forum with no instrument that could tell
  the two apart.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Reporting the
  deterministic bounds is the honest move, is always available, and returns an interval that is often too
  wide to decide anything. Individual-level survey evidence exists, is genuinely informative, and answers
  a question about respondents rather than about voters, with its own selection problems. Exit polls
  sample and do not enumerate. Sensitivity analysis across model assumptions is right and produces a
  range rather than a finding. And the only complete remedy is the individual record, which is the thing
  the institution exists to prevent.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the sociology, the statistics and the political
  methodology are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: **the ecological fallacy is a hazard and not
  a theorem about every aggregate study**, since aggregate and individual relationships often do agree
  and the discipline of checking is what the literature actually asks for; **the methods are contested
  rather than discredited**, and their proponents answer the critiques at length, so the play stages a
  live dispute and does not award it; and the legal application is one jurisdiction's and is described
  here in general terms rather than as a claim about any particular case. **No magnitudes are carried
  here**, because the papers themselves could not be read in the pass that cited them: publisher hosts
  and the article archives are blocked to it, so the bibliography, the findings and their directions were
  verified by search and corroborated across independent reports, which is the treatment a citation
  survives and a reported figure is not. **The play makes no claim that aggregate analysis should not be
  done**, since for most questions about places there is nothing else, only that an answer about groups
  arrives in a forum that needs one about people.
