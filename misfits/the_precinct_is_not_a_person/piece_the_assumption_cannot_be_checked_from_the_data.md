# Piece: The Assumption Cannot Be Checked From the Data

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). Every estimator needs a premise about how groups behave across places, and the aggregate is silent on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the inference seat](position_inference.md), in the paragraph of the report that states the condition.
In the disagreement between two competent experts, which is about the premise and not about arithmetic.

## Load Bearing

Without it the difficulty is a technical one and the next method closes it.
It is load bearing because **the output and the validity are answerable from the same data**: the
individual level is unobserved, which is why the estimate is being attempted, so an analyst able to
verify the premise would not have needed the method.

## Apparent

That the model's own diagnostics warn when the premise fails.
Tested against data where the individual truth is known, the estimates have not always recovered it and
the diagnostics have not always said so.

## Yearbook

Ecological regression stated with its constancy condition, the modern model that chooses within
deterministic bounds, and the critiques reporting the condition as often inappropriate for real aggregate
data.
