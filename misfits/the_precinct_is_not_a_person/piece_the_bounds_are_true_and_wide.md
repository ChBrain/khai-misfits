## Place

## Load Bearing

Without it the play has an obvious remedy and no second half.
It is load bearing because **honesty and usefulness move in opposite directions here**: the interval that
depends on nothing is often wide enough to contain the finding and its opposite, and every narrowing of
it is bought with an assumption the totals cannot support.

## Apparent

That reporting the range discharges the difficulty.
It discharges the witness's duty and hands the court the same problem in a more honest form, with the
hearing date unchanged.

## Yearbook

The deterministic bounds that the totals imply whatever any model says, offered as the foundation of the
modern method and as the part of it nobody disputes.
