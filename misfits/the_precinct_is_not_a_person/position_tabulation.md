# Position: Tabulation

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). The seat that produces the finest record the system can lawfully make.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Ballots in a box and residents on a roll.
A duty to count accurately and to publish what it counted.
The smallest reporting unit the law allows, and no smaller one available anywhere.

## Orders

Count every vote.
Publish the total, by precinct, because that is what a public count is.
Go no finer, because finer is the link the previous seat destroyed.

## Loses

Any capacity to answer a question about a person.
**This seat produces a floor rather than a convenience**: the precinct total is not a rounded version of
something more detailed that exists elsewhere, it is the most detailed thing there is, and its coarseness
is inherited from a guarantee rather than chosen for cost.

## Drives

Toward a complete, correct, public count,
which is exactly what an election requires,
so the seat that produces the evidence produces it at the only resolution that is permitted.
