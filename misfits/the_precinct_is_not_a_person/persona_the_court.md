# Persona: The Court

## Taxonomy

[Judgement](position_judgement.md). The forum that has to find, on a date, on the evidence that exists.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Attentive, impartial, and bound by a statute that requires the finding.
Has heard two competent experts disagree about assumptions it cannot adjudicate from the record.
Cannot order into existence a document nobody is permitted to hold.

## Action

Weighs it.
Finds.
Gives reasons.

## Shadow

That its whole function is to convert an uncertainty into a determination.
**A court cannot adjourn until the individual data appear**, because they never will, so the interval it
receives leaves the room as a fact about people, and nothing in the procedure marks the conversion as
having happened.

## Tell

Is the party doing exactly what it exists to do.
Is where a statement about precincts becomes a statement about voters.
