# Process: Bounding

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). Saying what the totals permit, without a model.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A refusal to present a point estimate as though it had been observed.
At [the courtroom](place_the_courtroom.md), in the same report, a page later.

## Direction

Toward a statement that is true by arithmetic and depends on nothing.
This is the most defensible thing anybody in the play produces, cannot be attacked on its assumptions
because it has none, and is the correct professional posture.

## Lever

Width, which cannot be reduced without adding a premise.
Every narrowing is bought with an assumption the data cannot support, so the lever moves honesty and
usefulness in opposite directions.

## Echo

An interval that is beyond dispute,
which often contains the finding and its opposite,
and a point estimate on the page before it that is easier to quote.
