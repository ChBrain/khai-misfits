# Plot: And the Court Has to Rule

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A hearing date, a required element, and the settlements.

## Action

[Deciding](process_deciding.md): [The Court](persona_the_court.md), in [the judgement seat](position_judgement.md), works through [Decide on the Best Evidence There Is](plan_decide_on_the_best_evidence_there_is.md).

## Stage

[The Courtroom](place_the_courtroom.md): a statute that makes the finding necessary, and no mechanism for adjourning until better evidence exists.

## Tension

Each escape gives back the thing it was for.
Report the bounds, which is the honest move and the most defensible thing anybody produces, and the court
has an interval spanning the finding and its opposite, with the date unchanged.
Bring survey evidence, which is real and informative, and the court has an accurate answer about
respondents and its own selection problems to weigh.
Run a sensitivity analysis across assumptions, which is right, and the court has a range where an element
of a claim was required.
Obtain the individual record, which would settle it in an afternoon, and you have proposed dismantling
the institution the case is about.
And the court finds, because that is what a court is for and declining would be a failure of duty. It
weighs two competent experts who disagree about a premise neither can test, and it writes reasons.
**Nothing in the reasons marks the point at which a fact about precincts became a fact about voters**,
because the forum has no such marker and never had one: converting uncertainty into determination is the
whole of what it exists to do.
