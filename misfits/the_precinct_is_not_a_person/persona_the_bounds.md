# Persona: The Bounds

## Taxonomy

[Recourse](position_recourse.md). What the totals actually permit, stated without a model.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

True by arithmetic and dependent on nothing.
Requires no assumption about how anybody behaves, which is exactly its strength.
Is the one part of the analysis that cannot be attacked in cross-examination.

## Action

States the interval the totals allow.
Refuses to narrow it.
Sits in the report beside a point estimate that is easier to quote.

## Shadow

That being unimpeachable and being useful are different properties.
**The interval often spans the finding and its opposite**, so the only part of the evidence that is
beyond dispute is the part that decides nothing, and every narrowing of it is bought with a premise the
data cannot support.

## Tell

Is the honest answer.
Is not an answer to the question that was asked.
