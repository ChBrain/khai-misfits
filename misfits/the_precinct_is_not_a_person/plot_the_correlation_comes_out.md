## Tension

The estimate exists and is correct, and the correction the play has to make is to what it is correct
about.
Grouping removes the variation inside each group, so what is being measured is a relationship between
averages. That relationship can be far stronger than the one among individuals, can be weaker, and can
run the other way, and all of those are ordinary rather than pathological. Across states a higher share
of foreign birth went with higher literacy while the foreign-born were themselves less literate, and both
statements are true, about different things.
So more precincts do not help. They make the number more precise about precincts.
And [It Is Not the Confounder](piece_it_is_not_the_confounder.md): stipulate no subgroup structure and no
reversal anywhere, and the ecological figure still exceeds the individual one, because removing the
within-group variation is enough by itself. The reversals are what make the fallacy memorable. The
routine case is an estimate that is simply much stronger than the truth, and that is the one in evidence.
