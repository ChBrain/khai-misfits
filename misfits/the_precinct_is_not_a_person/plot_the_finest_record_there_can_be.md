## Tension

The count is complete, correct and public, and observers from every side watched it, and where the
numbers did not reconcile the discrepancy was published too.
Beside it sits a second table, of who lives there, from another authority and another year.
And there is no join between them at the level of a person, because **the join is what the booth
destroyed**.
This is where the play insists on a distinction that reads as pedantry and is the whole of it: the
precinct total is a **floor** and not a rounding. It is not a summary of a finer file kept somewhere for
safety. The finer file was never made. So an analyst asking for more detail is not asking the returning
officer for a better extract, they are asking for the ballot to be less secret, and there is nowhere else
to look.
