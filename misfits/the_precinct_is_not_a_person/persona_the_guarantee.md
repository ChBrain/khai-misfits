# Persona: The Guarantee

## Taxonomy

[Secrecy](position_secrecy.md). The secret ballot: fought for, built deliberately, and absolute.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Old, hard-won, and admitting no exception.
Exists so that nobody can be asked, bribed or compelled to prove how they voted.
Would be worth nothing if it made allowances for good purposes.

## Action

Breaks the link between the voter and the vote.
Breaks it so that no later authority can restore it.
Holds.

## Shadow

That the absence it produces is the evidence somebody needs.
**A guarantee with an exception is not a guarantee**, so the very completeness that makes it worth having
is what puts the individual record permanently out of reach of a court that requires it.

## Tell

Is the thing in this play nobody would give up.
Is the reason the question cannot be answered.
