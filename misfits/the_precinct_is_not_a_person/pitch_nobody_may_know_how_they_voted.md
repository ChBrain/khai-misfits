# Pitch: Nobody May Know How They Voted

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact about what was computed, and about what it was computed over.
Unwilling to call the secrecy a problem, because it is the achievement.
Quoting the interval wherever the interval is the honest answer.

## Undertow

Under the finding, one relation from statistics and one from the institution.
A relationship between group means is a different quantity from the one among individuals.
And the individual record cannot exist, because that was decided in advance and on purpose.

## Nerve

The unease of a number that is right about precincts in a room that needs people.
Nobody was careless. Nobody may know how they voted, and that is the point.
The court still has to rule on Friday.

## Echo

a count that is complete and correct and as fine as counting gets,
an estimate that would be excellent if the question were about precincts,
and a bound that spans the finding and its opposite.
