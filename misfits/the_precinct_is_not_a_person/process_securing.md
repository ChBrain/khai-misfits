# Process: Securing

## Taxonomy

[The Precinct Is Not a Person](play_the_precinct_is_not_a_person.md). Destroying the link between the voter and the vote, thoroughly and by design.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A history of voters being watched, bought and punished.
At [the booth](place_the_booth.md), at every election, without exception.

## Direction

Toward a vote that cannot be attributed to anybody.
This is done well and by people who understand exactly why: an exception for good purposes is the same
thing as no guarantee, so the completeness is the point rather than an excess of caution.

## Lever

None, and that is deliberate.
There is no authority anywhere that can be asked to make an exception, which is what distinguishes a
guarantee from a policy.

## Echo

A vote nobody can trace,
a record that was never made rather than one that was withheld,
and a question that no later evidence can reach.
