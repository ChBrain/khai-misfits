## Load Bearing

Without it this collapses into Simpson's paradox and earns no separate staging.
It is load bearing because **the switch-off runs cleanly**: stipulate no subgroup structure, no lurking
variable and no reversal at all, and the ecological correlation still exceeds the individual one, because
removing the within-group variation is enough on its own.

## Apparent

That the reversal cases are what the fallacy is about.
They are what makes it memorable. The routine case is an estimate that is merely much stronger than the
truth, produced without anybody's error, and it is the one that appears in evidence.

## Yearbook

The distinction between an association reversed by combining groups and a quantity that is simply
different at two levels of aggregation.
