# Pitch: Nobody Set the Date

## Taxonomy

[The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Civic and reasonable. A judiciary answerable to the people it sentences among, an electorate with a legitimate view about crime, a judge applying community standards, a sentence within the lawful range.
Every element of it chosen on purpose, defended in good faith, and working.

## Undertow

Under the civics, one wire with a virtue written at one end and a defect at the other, conducting hardest when it is shortest.
And a defendant assigned to a year of somebody else's term by arrest dates, continuances and laboratory backlogs, none of which ever looked at them.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a finding that is simultaneously proof the system is working and proof it is failing, with no measurement anywhere that can tell you which.

## Echo

Afterward: ten per cent, fifty per cent more departures above the range, and somewhere between eighteen hundred and twenty-seven hundred years of incarceration attributed to the distance to an election, every year of it imposed lawfully and none of it appealable.
