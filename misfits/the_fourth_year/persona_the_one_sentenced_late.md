# Persona: The One Sentenced Late

## Taxonomy

[One Sentenced Late](position_one_sentenced_late.md). The defendant whose case came up in the fourth year.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person convicted of the same offence as the other, before the same judge, in the year the term expires.
Distinguished from them by an arrest date, a continuance and a laboratory backlog.

## Action

Receives a longer sentence, lawfully imposed, within or reasonably above the range.
Appeals, and finds there is nothing to appeal.

## Shadow

Bears a cost that no document in the case records.
The sentence is one the judge was entitled to impose, the reasons given are sound reasons, and a difference visible only across hundreds of cases cannot be raised in one: so this persona is holding a real injury with no forum, since the harm exists as a coefficient in a study and never as an error in a file.

## Tell

Was assigned to a year by a process that never looked at them, and pays the difference between two numbers neither of which is wrong.
