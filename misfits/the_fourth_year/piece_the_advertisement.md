# Piece: The Advertisement

## Taxonomy

[The Campaign Trail](place_the_campaign_trail.md). Thirty seconds, and the reason the wire runs one way.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On [the campaign trail](place_the_campaign_trail.md), bought in the weeks before the vote, naming a single decision out of thousands.

## Load Bearing

It sets the direction of the whole mechanism.
A release that ended badly is a name, a face and a date, and it fits in half a minute: a sentence that ran a year longer than it should have has no victim who knows it, no name attached and no story shape at all, so the pressure the wire carries is not a preference for severity as such but a consequence of which errors can be told.

## Apparent

A lawful piece of political speech, frequently accurate in every particular it states.
One true case, correctly described.

## Yearbook

Needs no falsehood to work, and rarely contains one.
Has no counterpart in the other direction, because the harm it would have to describe is invisible to the person who suffered it and to everybody else.
