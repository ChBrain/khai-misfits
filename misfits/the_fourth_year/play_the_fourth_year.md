# Play: The Fourth Year

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Fourth Year.
Most American states elect their trial judges, and they do it on purpose, for a reason that is not foolish: a judiciary nobody can remove is a real problem in a democracy, and the answer chosen was to make judges answerable to the people whose community they sentence in. That answerability is a wire, and a wire conducts. What arrives along it is the community's view of how firmly wrongdoing should be met, which is exactly what it was installed to carry. It arrives most strongly when the wire is shortest, which is the year the term runs out. So the same judge, sentencing the same conduct, sentences it differently in the fourth year than in the first, and the person standing in front of the bench did not choose which year they were standing in.

## Arc

The bend is this: accountability and independence are not two dials but one, read from opposite ends, so every increment of one is exactly a decrement of the other.
It runs on a wire doing the job it was strung for.
The Term That Ends was installed deliberately. Elective judgeships were chosen over appointed ones by people who thought an unremovable judiciary was the greater danger, and who were not wrong to think so. Nothing about the arrangement is an oversight, and nobody smuggled it in.
The Ones Who Vote want a judge who takes crime seriously, which is a legitimate preference honestly held, and the only one they are given any instrument to express. They are not a mob. They are the constituency the design named as the source of the standard, doing what the design asked them to do.
The One Who Must Stand is where the misfit becomes impossible to allocate. As the term closes, sentences rise. In Washington State, across 265 superior court judges and three election cycles, sentences ran about ten per cent longer at the end of a judge's political cycle than at the beginning, and discretionary departures above the guideline range rose by around fifty per cent across the cycle. In Pennsylvania, using the random assignment of cases to judges on staggered terms, somewhere between eighteen hundred and twenty-seven hundred years of incarceration across twenty-two thousand cases were attributed to the electoral dynamic. But no measurement can say whether a given judge was chasing votes or sincerely reweighting toward the community's standards as the moment of accountability concentrated the mind, because those two produce identical numbers. And an elected judiciary exists precisely so that community standards do reach sentencing. So the same finding is evidence of the system working and evidence of it failing, and there is no instrument that separates them.
And the cost is assigned by the docket. The One Sentenced Early and The One Sentenced Late did the same thing, and the difference between them is where in a four-year term their case happened to come up, which is a matter of arrest date, continuances, laboratory backlogs and calendars. Nobody chose it, nobody can see it from inside a single case, and it is invisible on appeal, because a sentence inside the range or reasonably departing from it is a sentence a judge was entitled to impose.
And every correction turns the same dial the other way. Appoint judges instead and the sentences steady and the accountability goes, replaced by whoever does the appointing. Lengthen the terms and the cycle stretches but the answerability thins in proportion. Retention elections soften the contest and keep the calendar. Tighten the guidelines and the variance falls while the discretion moves to the prosecutor, who is also elected, and whose decisions almost nothing reviews. There is no setting of the dial that gives independence and accountability at once, because they are the same setting, named twice.

## Triggers

**[A Term With an End](plot_a_term_with_an_end.md)**
A state decides that an unremovable judiciary is the greater danger and makes its judges answerable to the community they sentence in. Closes when the wire is strung deliberately, for a reason that is not foolish, and begins conducting what it was strung to conduct.

**[Whichever Year You Land In](plot_whichever_year_you_land_in.md)**
Cue: an arrest date, a continuance, a laboratory backlog, a calendar. The case comes up when it comes up. Closes when a defendant has been assigned to a year of somebody else's term by a process with no view of them at all, and no way to notice what it just did.

**[Above the Range](plot_above_the_range.md)**
Cue: a sentence within a judge's lawful discretion, and the option of departing upward from the guideline band. Closes when departures above the range have risen by around half across the cycle, every one of them a decision the judge was entitled to make, and none of them reviewable as an error.

**[Two Men, Same Crime](plot_two_men_same_crime.md)**
Cue: the same conduct, the same court, the same judge, four years apart. Closes when the sentences differ by roughly a tenth, the difference is the calendar, and nothing in either file records a reason that could be appealed.

**[The Thirty Seconds That Ends a Career](plot_the_thirty_seconds_that_ends_a_career.md)**
Cue: a broadcast advertisement naming a judge who released someone who then did something terrible. Closes when the asymmetry is understood: severity is never the story, leniency is always the story, and the wire therefore carries pressure in one direction only.

**[The Same Dial](plot_the_same_dial.md)**
Cue: the structure understood, and the answers on the table: appoint, lengthen the terms, switch to retention, or tighten the guidelines. Closes when the sentences steady and the accountability goes with them, or the discretion moves to a prosecutor who is also elected and whom almost nothing reviews.

## Stakes

Whether a system can be held accountable to a public without becoming responsive to a calendar, when those are the same property described by people who disagree about it.
Every plot moves the same way: toward answerability that was chosen deliberately and works exactly as designed, and away from any correction that could take the defect without the virtue, since they are one wire. The production cannot give a defendant a different year, and it cannot ask an electorate to stop having a view about crime. What is left to fight over is not whether judges should answer to anybody, an unanswerable judiciary is its own misfit, but what is owed to the person whose sentence was set by the distance to an election, and whether a thing can be called a defect at all when it is the mechanism working.
