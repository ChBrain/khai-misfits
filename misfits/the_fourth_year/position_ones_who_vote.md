# Position: Ones Who Vote

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A legitimate preference about how firmly wrongdoing should be met, honestly held.
One instrument for expressing it, used rarely and with almost no information about any individual case.

## Orders

Prefer firmness, since firmness is what the channel can carry.

## Loses

Any ability to send a correction back.
An election offers roughly one bit every few years, and the only property of a judiciary legible through it is severity, so a released defendant who reoffends is a story and a sentence that ran a year too long is nothing at all: the seat is a legitimate authority with a lawful preference, wired to a channel too narrow to carry the answer to its own excess.

## Drives
