# Plan: Take the Dial Off

## Taxonomy

A **correction**: the second intent of [The Ones Who Vote](persona_the_ones_who_vote.md), inside [The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md), held by the same hand that supplies the demand.

## Owner

- Owner: [The Ones Who Vote](persona_the_ones_who_vote.md)

## Direction

Separate the sentence from the calendar. Change how judges are selected or retained, stretch the cycle, or narrow what discretion can do, and accept in each case that the accountability moves by the same amount.

## Orders

(Appoint rather than elect; or lengthen terms; or switch to retention rather than contested elections; or tighten [the guideline band](piece_the_guideline_band.md) and restrict [the departure](piece_the_departure.md).)

## Implementation

([The Term That Ends](persona_the_term_that_ends.md), which is the actual mechanism and can only be shortened or lengthened, never made to conduct one way; and an electorate that would have to vote away its own instrument.)

## Targets

- [ ] the price is accepted: appointment steadies the sentences and hands the selection to whoever appoints, which is a different unaccountability rather than none
- [ ] the price is accepted: lengthening the term stretches the cycle and thins the answerability in exact proportion, since they are one quantity
- [ ] the price is accepted: retention softens the contest and keeps the calendar, so it reduces the effect without removing it
- [ ] the price is accepted: tightening the band moves the discretion to the prosecutor, who is also elected and whom almost nothing reviews
