# Process: Drawing the Date

## Taxonomy

[The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md). A case being assigned to a year of somebody else's term.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An arrest date, a continuance, a laboratory backlog, a listing clerk.
Nothing that has ever considered the defendant, the judge, or the election.

## Direction

Toward a sentencing date somewhere in a four-year cycle, distributed by administration rather than by anything about the case.
Toward [The One Sentenced Early](persona_the_one_sentenced_early.md) or [The One Sentenced Late](persona_the_one_sentenced_late.md), with nothing distinguishing them.

## Lever

Scheduling, which is the least deliberate mechanism in the building and here decides part of the sentence.

## Echo

An allocation that is arbitrary in the strict sense, unobjectionable at every step, and never recorded anywhere as an allocation of anything.
