# Persona: The One Sentenced Early

## Taxonomy

[One Sentenced Early](position_one_sentenced_early.md). The defendant whose case came up at the far end of a term.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person convicted of an offence, sentenced in the first year after an election, by a judge with three years still to run.
Ordinary in every respect, and fortunate in exactly one.

## Action

Receives a sentence inside the range, reasoned properly and recorded fully.
Serves it, and never learns there was anything to learn.

## Shadow

Holds the control condition, and cannot know it.
Nothing in this persona's file differs from the other's except a date that appears in both and means nothing in either: the advantage is real, measurable across a population, and completely invisible from inside the one case where it happened, so it is never claimed, never contested and never named.

## Tell

Is the term of the comparison that makes the misfit visible, and is the only one who never had to be told.
