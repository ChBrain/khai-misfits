# Persona: The One Who Must Stand

## Taxonomy

[One Who Must Stand](position_one_who_must_stand.md). The judge, whose severity moves with the distance to the vote.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An elected trial judge, conscientious, experienced, sentencing within lawful discretion, and facing the electorate at a known date.
Not corrupt, and not obliged to be, for any of this to happen.

## Action

Sentences within the range, and sometimes above it, as the law permits.
Departs upward about half again as often at the end of a term as at the beginning.

## Shadow

Cannot be told apart from the version of itself that is behaving perfectly.
No measurement can distinguish a judge chasing votes from a judge sincerely reweighting toward the community's standards as accountability approaches, because the two produce identical numbers, and an elected judiciary exists precisely so that community standards do reach sentencing: so the same finding is evidence of this persona working and evidence of it failing, and the misfit cannot be allocated to a motive even in principle.

## Tell

Is unimpeachable in every individual case and describes a curve across four years.
