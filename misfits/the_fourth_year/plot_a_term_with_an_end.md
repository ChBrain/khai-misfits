# Plot: A Term With an End

## Owner

## Cue

A state deciding how to constitute its trial courts, and judging that a judiciary nobody can remove is the greater danger.

## Action

[The Term That Ends](persona_the_term_that_ends.md), holding [Term That Ends](position_term_that_ends.md), is written into the constitution: a fixed period of years with an election at the end of it.

## Stage

The wire is strung deliberately, for a reason that is not foolish, and begins carrying the community's standards to the bench, which is what it was strung to carry.

## Tension

The virtue and the defect are one conductance, read from the two ends.
Answerability to a community and sensitivity to a date are not two properties that happen to travel together: they are the same property, praised by the people who wanted the judiciary answerable and deplored by the people who want it independent, and no drafting anywhere could have supplied the first without the second. Nothing here was overlooked or smuggled in. The arrangement is doing exactly what its authors specified, and the complaint against it is a description of the specification.
