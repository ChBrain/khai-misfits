# Plot: Whichever Year You Land In

## Taxonomy

[The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An arrest date. A continuance granted for good reason. A laboratory backlog. A listing clerk with a diary.

## Action

[Drawing the date](process_drawing_the_date.md): the case is assigned a sentencing date somewhere in a four-year cycle, producing [The One Sentenced Early](persona_the_one_sentenced_early.md) or [The One Sentenced Late](persona_the_one_sentenced_late.md).

## Stage

Nothing in the assignment considered the defendant, the judge or the election, and nothing about it is irregular.

## Tension

The least deliberate mechanism in the building has just decided part of a sentence.
Scheduling is administration, not adjudication: it has no view of anybody, no discretion to exercise and no obligation to explain itself, which is exactly right for scheduling. But the year it lands on is now a term in the sentence, and because the allocation is recorded nowhere as an allocation of anything, no file in either case contains a fact that anyone could later point to.
