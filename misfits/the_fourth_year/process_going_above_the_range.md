# Process: Going Above the Range

## Taxonomy

[The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md). The discretionary step, taken half again as often late in a term.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A case the judge considers worse than its category, and [the guideline band](piece_the_guideline_band.md) that permits departing above it with reasons.
[The One Who Must Stand](persona_the_one_who_must_stand.md), sentencing in [the sentencing room](place_the_sentencing_room.md).

## Direction

Toward [the departure](piece_the_departure.md), stated on the record with sound reasons.
Toward a sentence that is lawful, reviewable only for the adequacy of those reasons, and never for its date.

## Lever

Discretion, preserved deliberately because a system that cannot depart cannot answer to a case genuinely worse than its category.

## Echo

Departures rising by around fifty per cent across the cycle, carrying much of the added severity, in the one move that requires a written reason.
