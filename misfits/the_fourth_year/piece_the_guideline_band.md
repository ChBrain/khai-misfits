# Piece: The Guideline Band

## Taxonomy

[The Sentencing Room](place_the_sentencing_room.md). The lawful range, wide enough to hold the whole effect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [the sentencing room](place_the_sentencing_room.md), governing every sentence, and satisfied by every sentence in this play.

## Load Bearing

It makes the variation lawful, which is what makes it unreachable.
The band exists to constrain arbitrariness and it does constrain it, but any point inside it is a sentence the judge was entitled to impose, so a decade of drift toward the top of the range is a decade of entirely correct decisions: the instrument built to bound discretion is the same instrument that certifies every exercise of it.

## Apparent

A published range for an offence and a criminal history, applied consistently and reviewable if breached.
A real and successful reform, which reduced disparity substantially when it arrived.

## Yearbook

Introduced against exactly this kind of unevenness, and effective against the crude version of it.
Leaves a lawful interval, because a band with no interval is not sentencing but arithmetic, and the interval is where the calendar lives.
