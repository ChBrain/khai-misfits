# Plot: The Same Dial

## Taxonomy

[The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The structure understood at last, and the answers on the table: appoint instead of electing, lengthen the terms, switch to retention, or tighten the band.

## Action

[Take the dial off](plan_take_the_dial_off.md): [The Ones Who Vote](persona_the_ones_who_vote.md) hold both the demand that turns the dial and the only power that could remove it, and find that every setting moves both quantities at once.

## Stage

Appointment steadies the sentences and hands the choosing to whoever appoints. Longer terms stretch the cycle and thin the answerability in exact proportion. Retention keeps the calendar. Tightening the band moves the discretion to the prosecutor, who is also elected.

## Tension

There is no setting that gives independence and accountability together, because they are one setting under two names.
Every correction is a trade of the same quantity against itself, so the argument can never be won on the merits: someone who values answerability and someone who values insulation are not disagreeing about facts, they are reading one number from opposite ends and calling it by the name they prefer. And the only body that could change the arrangement is the one whose legitimate preference supplies its force.
