# Plot: Above the Range

## Taxonomy

[The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A case the judge considers worse than its category, and a guideline system that permits departing above the band with reasons stated.

## Action

[Going above the range](process_going_above_the_range.md): [The One Who Must Stand](persona_the_one_who_must_stand.md), holding [One Who Must Stand](position_one_who_must_stand.md), takes [the departure](piece_the_departure.md) in [the sentencing room](place_the_sentencing_room.md), on the record.

## Stage

Across the electoral cycle in the Washington data, departures above the range rose by about fifty per cent, and carried much of the added severity.

## Tension

The effect concentrates in the one move that requires the judge to write down a reason, and the reasons written are sound.
It does not hide in a vague drift that could be dismissed as noise: it lives in explicit, reasoned, individually defensible decisions to exceed the band, each of them the exercise of a discretion preserved deliberately, because a system that cannot depart cannot answer to a case genuinely worse than its category. A departure is reviewable for the adequacy of its reasons. It is not reviewable for the year in which it was taken.
