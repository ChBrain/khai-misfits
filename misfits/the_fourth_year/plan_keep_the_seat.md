# Plan: Keep the Seat

## Taxonomy

A **positional necessity**: the intent of [The One Who Must Stand](persona_the_one_who_must_stand.md), inside [The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Must Stand](persona_the_one_who_must_stand.md)

## Direction

Sentence within the law, applying the standards of the community you sentence in, which is what an elected judiciary is for. Remain a judge, which requires a record that can be read back without a thirty-second answer to it.

## Orders

(Sentence inside [the guideline band](piece_the_guideline_band.md); take [the departure](piece_the_departure.md) where the case warrants it, with reasons on the record; do it in [the sentencing room](place_the_sentencing_room.md), one case at a time, as the office requires.)

## Implementation

([The Sentencing Room](place_the_sentencing_room.md); the [coming up for election](process_coming_up_for_election.md) and [going above the range](process_going_above_the_range.md) processes; discretion preserved on purpose, because a system that cannot depart cannot answer to a genuinely worse case.)

## Targets

- [ ] every sentence is lawful, reasoned and within discretion
- [ ] the community's standards reach the bench, which is the whole point of the arrangement
- [ ] no individual decision is available to be called an error, because none is one
- [ ] nothing distinguishes this plan from chasing votes, and nothing ever will, because the two produce identical numbers
