# Place: The Sentencing Room

## Taxonomy

[The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md). Where one case is decided, correctly, and the pattern cannot be seen.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A courtroom on an ordinary morning: a pre-sentence report, counsel on both sides, a victim statement, a judge who has read everything.
Reasons given aloud and recorded, all of them sound.

## Holds

[The Guideline Band](piece_the_guideline_band.md), which sets the lawful range for this offence and this history.
[The Departure](piece_the_departure.md), the step above the band that the judge is entitled to take with reasons.

## Offers

Individual justice, done attentively, on the facts of this person and this offence.
A decision that will withstand any review, because a sentence within discretion is not an error.

## Withheld

Every other case.
The room is built to consider one defendant thoroughly and holds no view of the four hundred sentenced before them or the four hundred after, so the one thing that would reveal the effect, comparison across a term, is the one thing this place is designed never to do: the pattern exists only at a scale no courtroom ever occupies.
