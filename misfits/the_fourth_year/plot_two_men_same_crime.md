# Plot: Two Men, Same Crime

## Taxonomy

[The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The same conduct, the same court, the same judge, four years apart.

## Action

[The One Sentenced Early](persona_the_one_sentenced_early.md) receives a sentence in the first year of the term. [The One Sentenced Late](persona_the_one_sentenced_late.md), holding [One Sentenced Late](position_one_sentenced_late.md), receives a longer one in the fourth, and appeals.

## Stage

Sentences run roughly ten per cent longer at the end of a cycle than at the beginning. Both files are complete, both sets of reasons are sound, and the appeal fails because there is nothing in it to allow.

## Tension

The injury is real, measurable, and has no forum.
A difference visible only across hundreds of cases cannot be raised inside one, so the harm exists as a coefficient in a study and never as an error in a file. The one who paid it cannot show it, the one who benefited never learns of it, and the judge who imposed both was entitled to impose both. In Pennsylvania, between eighteen hundred and twenty-seven hundred years of incarceration across twenty-two thousand cases were attributed to this dynamic: every year of it lawful, and none of it appealable.
