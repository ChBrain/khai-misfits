# Plan: Pick a Tough One

## Taxonomy

A **civic preference**: the intent of [The Ones Who Vote](persona_the_ones_who_vote.md), inside [The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The Ones Who Vote](persona_the_ones_who_vote.md)

## Direction

Get a judiciary that takes crime seriously, using the instrument provided. Reward firmness, remove the judge whose leniency produced a disaster, and do it on the small amount of information an election can carry.

## Orders

(Attend to [the advertisement](piece_the_advertisement.md) on [the campaign trail](place_the_campaign_trail.md); punish the release that turned out badly; vote through [standing again](process_standing_again.md), rarely and with almost no case-level knowledge.)

## Implementation

([The Campaign Trail](place_the_campaign_trail.md); a channel offering roughly one bit every few years; and severity as the only property of a judiciary legible through it.)

## Targets

- [ ] a legitimate preference about how firmly wrongdoing is met is expressed
- [ ] the judiciary is answerable to the community rather than unremovable
- [ ] the bad release is punished, since it is the only error the channel can carry
- [ ] the over-long sentence is not punished, because nobody who suffered it knows, and no story exists to be told about it
