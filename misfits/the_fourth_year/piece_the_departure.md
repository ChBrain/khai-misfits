# Piece: The Departure

## Taxonomy

[The Sentencing Room](place_the_sentencing_room.md). The step above the band, where most of the effect is carried.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [the sentencing room](place_the_sentencing_room.md), taken with stated reasons, in the cases the judge considers to warrant more than the band allows.

## Load Bearing

It is where the measurement actually landed.
Discretionary departures above the guideline range rose by about fifty per cent across the electoral cycle in the Washington data, and accounted for much of the added severity, so the effect does not hide in a general drift: it concentrates in the one move that requires the judge to write down a reason, and the reasons written are sound ones.

## Apparent

An exceptional sentence, justified on the record, in a case with features the band did not anticipate.
Exactly the flexibility a guideline system is supposed to preserve.

## Yearbook

Preserved deliberately, because a system that cannot depart cannot answer to a case that is genuinely worse than its category.
Reviewable in principle for adequacy of reasons, and never reviewable for the year in which it was taken.
