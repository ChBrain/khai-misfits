# Place: The Campaign Trail

## Taxonomy

[The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md). Where the record is read back, in one direction only.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Church halls, county fairs, a local paper, a broadcast buy. A judge explaining a judicial record to people with no reason to have followed it.
An opponent, and a few thousand decisions available to be quoted.

## Holds

[The Advertisement](piece_the_advertisement.md), thirty seconds naming one release that turned out badly.
A record in which every severe sentence is unmentionable and every lenient one is a liability.

## Offers

Democratic accountability, genuinely: a chance for a community to remove a judge it does not want.
The only moment at which the public's standards are actually transmitted.

## Withheld

Any channel running the other way.
There is no advertisement about a sentence that was a year too long, no constituency for the defendant who served it and no way for the public to learn it happened, so the wire that was strung to carry a standard turns out to carry pressure in a single direction: severity is never a story, leniency always is, and the asymmetry is a property of what is legible rather than of what anybody wants.
