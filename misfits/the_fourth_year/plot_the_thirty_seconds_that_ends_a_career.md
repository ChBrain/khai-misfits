# Plot: The Thirty Seconds That Ends a Career

## Taxonomy

[The Fourth Year](play_the_fourth_year.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An opponent, a broadcast buy, and one decision out of several thousand: a release that ended badly, with a name, a face and a date.

## Action

[Standing again](process_standing_again.md): [The Advertisement](piece_the_advertisement.md) runs on [the campaign trail](place_the_campaign_trail.md), and [The Ones Who Vote](persona_the_ones_who_vote.md), holding [Ones Who Vote](position_ones_who_vote.md), decide on it.

## Stage

The advertisement contains no falsehood. It describes one true case, correctly.

## Tension

Only one of the two errors has a shape that can be told, so the wire carries pressure in a single direction.
An over-long sentence has no victim who knows they are one, no name attached, no date and no story: nobody can make an advertisement about it, and no constituency exists to be moved by it. So the asymmetry is not a public preference for severity, it is a property of what is legible through a channel offering one bit every few years. The electorate is not choosing to ignore the other error. It has never been in a position to hear of it.
