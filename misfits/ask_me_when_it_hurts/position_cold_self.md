# Position: Cold Self

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The seat of the calm state, and marks that its honesty is no defense against a gap it cannot see across.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Everything a state free of hunger, pain, fear, or desire can access: what usually matters, what a reasonable person would choose with a clear head.
No access to the pull it has not yet felt, since a visceral state cannot be recalled or imagined with its true strength from outside itself.
A plan that is sincere and well reasoned, and no less honest for being wrong about what is coming.

## Orders

Reason from where you stand, on everything the calm state can access.
Make the plan the calm state can make, and mean it, since a plan made honestly is not a lesser plan for the state it cannot foresee.
Know that the forecast is built without what the coming state will feel, and that no amount of careful reasoning in the calm supplies what only the state itself can provide.

## Loses

Nothing of its own honesty, and commits no fault in the reasoning it performs.
Because its forecast is built from everything it can access, the seat takes no blame for what lies past that access, and yet the very completeness of its own account is what leaves it with nothing to say about the state it has not felt.
It keeps its honestly reasoned plan and loses no cause for blame, the calm state that reasons as well as any calm state could and still cannot reach across to the one that is coming.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honest plan is built on a state it will never get to feel from the inside,
reasoning soundly from everything within reach, sincere and well made and not weak,
so the seat that plans as well as it can is the seat that, by the same fact, plans without the one thing that will decide whether the plan holds.
