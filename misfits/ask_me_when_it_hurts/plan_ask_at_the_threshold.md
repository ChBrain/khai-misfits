# Plan: Ask at the Threshold

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), inside [Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Steward](position_steward.md)

## Direction

Ask as near the visceral state as the moment allows, since a preference taken closer to the threshold catches more of what the state will want than one taken from the calm alone. Know that the state cannot always be consulted in advance, that the remedy is real and still incomplete, and that the calm self who returns once the state passes will be no wiser than before.

## Orders

([The Steward](persona_the_steward.md) holds [the Return](piece_the_return.md) through [Asking](process_asking.md) at [the Bedside](place_the_bedside.md), asking nearer the threshold than a plan made purely in the calm allowed.)

## Implementation

([Asking](process_asking.md) at [the Bedside](place_the_bedside.md) holds [the Return](piece_the_return.md), because the state passes and the calm self resumes, no better informed than before, and the question asked nearest the threshold is carried forward into the next plan, imperfectly but more honestly than a forecast made from the calm alone (Loewenstein, 2005).)

## Targets

- [ ] the preference is asked as near the visceral state as the moment allows
- [ ] it is owned that the remedy is real but incomplete, since the state cannot always be consulted in advance
- [ ] the calm self's return is met honestly, no better informed than before the state arrived
- [ ] what the asking returns is carried forward into the next plan, decision, or directive
