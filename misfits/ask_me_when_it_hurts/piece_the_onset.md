# Piece: The Onset

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The turn: the visceral state's real and involuntary arrival, attention narrowed onto the drive and its relief.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Grip](place_the_grip.md), carried by [Wanting](process_wanting.md): fully present here, nothing held back, nothing softened by distance.

## Load Bearing

The state is not chosen or feigned; it arrives on its own terms and occupies the mind while it is present. Hunger, pain, fear, or desire, whichever it is, it is as real and legitimate a condition as the calm state that preceded it, and it does not announce itself as a distortion to be corrected (Loewenstein, 1996). It does not merely add urgency to the earlier plan's terms, a louder voice arguing the same case; it reorganizes what is weighed at all, narrowing attention onto the drive and its relief until other considerations, once clear, recede.

## Apparent

Looks like a lapse, a craving taking over, something to be resisted or ashamed of; is actually a state as honest as the one it replaces.
