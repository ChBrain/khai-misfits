# Piece: The Plan

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The setup: the plan made honestly in the calm, reasoned from everything a state free of hunger, pain, fear, or desire can access.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Calm](place_the_calm.md), carried by [Forecasting](process_forecasting.md): a room where nothing yet presses on the body, so nothing yet unsettles the reasoning that builds the plan.

## Load Bearing

The plan is sincere and well made, reasoned from everything the calm state can access. It weighs the facts on hand, the stated values, the future as best it can be pictured, and it arrives at terms that hold together. This is not a weak or careless plan; it is the best plan the calm state can make (Loewenstein, 1996). Nothing in it is false or thin, and nothing in its reasoning is skipped. What it cannot see, because it is not yet present to be seen, is the visceral state that will later decide whether the plan holds. The absence is not a defect in the plan's construction, only in its materials.

## Apparent

Looks like ordinary good sense: prudence, foresight, a plan any reasonable person would make and be glad to have made.

## Yearbook

Made by [the Cold Self](persona_the_cold_self.md) through [Forecasting](process_forecasting.md) at [the Calm](place_the_calm.md), dramatized in [What the Calm Can See](plot_what_the_calm_can_see.md). It meets [the Gap](piece_the_gap.md), and it is broken by [the Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md), not because anyone changed their mind but because the state under the plan changed.
