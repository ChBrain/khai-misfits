# Plan: Hold the True Scope

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the record's seat](position_record.md), inside [Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Record](position_record.md)

## Direction

Hold the accumulated cases to what they show, and generalize no further than the evidence reaches. Know that the gap is real and documented, that its size is not uniform, and that a claim held past its true scope is a weaker claim than the one the evidence actually supports.

## Orders

([The Record](persona_the_record.md) holds [the Conditions](piece_the_conditions.md) through [Bounding](process_bounding.md) at [the Bedside](place_the_bedside.md), keeping the pattern honest by keeping its edges.)

## Implementation

([Bounding](process_bounding.md) at [the Bedside](place_the_bedside.md) holds [the Conditions](piece_the_conditions.md), because the gap is strongest for acute and unfamiliar drive states and weaker for mild or well-rehearsed ones, so the pattern is staged as a documented and variable tendency and not a claim that all self-prediction fails (the visceral-influence and empathy-gap literature that followed Loewenstein).)

## Targets

- [ ] the pattern is held to what the accumulated cases actually show
- [ ] the gap's size is seen to vary by domain and by the state's intensity and familiarity
- [ ] the larger claim, that no calm plan can be trusted, is refused as broader than the evidence supports
- [ ] it is owned that a claim held past its true scope is weaker than the one the evidence actually makes
