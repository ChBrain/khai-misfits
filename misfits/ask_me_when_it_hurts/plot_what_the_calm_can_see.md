# Plot: What the Calm Can See

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At the Calm, a decision is coming that depends on how a future state, not yet felt, will change what is wanted. Nothing visceral is present yet to unsettle the reasoning.

## Action

[The Cold Self](persona_the_cold_self.md), from [the cold self's seat](position_cold_self.md), on [Plan From Here](plan_plan_from_here.md) through [Forecasting](process_forecasting.md), holds [The Plan](piece_the_plan.md) at [The Calm](place_the_calm.md): reasoning out, term by term, what usually matters and what a clear head would choose.

## Stage

The plan is sincere and well made, the best the calm state can do, reasoned from everything within its reach (Loewenstein, 1996). It weighs the terms it can see, checks them against what has usually mattered before, and sets down a choice that a clear head would stand behind. Nothing about it is thin or self-deceiving; no corner has been cut and no question waved past. What it cannot yet see, because it is not yet present in the body doing the reasoning, is the visceral state that will decide whether the plan holds once it arrives.

## Tension

A plan made for a state it has never had to answer to.
