# Position: Steward

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The seat of the third party who must plan around a gap that is not their own, and marks that asking nearer the threshold narrows the gap without closing it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The chance to ask nearer the visceral state than a plan made purely in the calm allowed, catching more of what the state will want.
No power to make the state available on demand, since the threshold cannot always be reached before a decision is needed.
A remedy that is real, imperfect, and the best available across a gap the Steward did not create and cannot fully close.

## Orders

Ask as close to the threshold as the moment allows, since a forecast taken nearer the state catches more of what it will want than one taken from the calm alone.
Know that even the best-timed question will sometimes land too soon or too late, and that this is a limit of the remedy and not a failure to apply it well.
Carry what the asking returns forward honestly, into the plan, the directive, the decision made on someone else's behalf.

## Loses

No fault for timing it cannot control, and commits no error in offering a remedy it knows to be incomplete.
Because the gap between two selves is not the Steward's to close alone, the seat takes no blame for what the threshold's uncertain arrival leaves unresolved, and yet the incompleteness remains, felt in every case that lands where the asking failed to reach.
It keeps its honestly offered remedy and loses no cause for blame, the seat that narrows the gap it did not create and cannot fully close.

## Drives

Toward the one who narrows the gap without ever closing it,
asking as near the threshold as a moment allows, and carrying forward what the asking returns,
so the seat that closes what distance it can is the seat that, by the same fact, cannot promise the distance closed for good.
