# Piece: The Gap

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The mechanism: the calm state's structural inability to simulate a visceral state's true pull from outside it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Calm](place_the_calm.md), carried by [Forecasting](process_forecasting.md): present even here, quiet, unnoticed, folded invisibly into a forecast that feels complete.

## Load Bearing

Hunger cannot be recalled with its true pull once sated, pain cannot be simulated with its true urgency once it has passed, and desire cannot be weighed at its true strength before it is felt (Loewenstein, 1996). This is not a failure of intelligence or effort. A careful mind and a careless one meet the same wall, because the gap is a limit of access, not a limit of diligence: a visceral state can only be known from inside itself, and the calm state, by definition, is not inside it. The forecast built without that access still feels whole from where it stands, which is exactly what makes the gap hard to notice until the state it could not simulate arrives.

## Apparent

Looks like a minor uncertainty, a detail the plan can safely round off; is actually a blind spot no amount of careful reasoning closes.
