# Plot: The Shape the Gap Takes

## Owner

## Cue

The pattern plain across cases, and the question of how far it truly reaches.

## Action

At [The Bedside](place_the_bedside.md), [The Record](persona_the_record.md) from [the record's seat](position_record.md), on [Hold the True Scope](plan_hold_the_true_scope.md) through [Bounding](process_bounding.md), holds [The Conditions](piece_the_conditions.md): the pattern held to its true scope, no wider than the cases actually show.

## Stage

The gap is strongest for acute and unfamiliar drive states and weaker for mild or well-rehearsed ones, so the pattern is a documented and variable tendency and not a claim that all self-prediction fails (the visceral-influence and empathy-gap literature that followed Loewenstein). No one in this account is at fault: the Cold Self reasoned soundly from everything a calm state could reach, the Hot Self wanted honestly from everything a gripped state could feel, the Steward asked as close to the threshold as timing allowed, and the Record holds the pattern to no more than the evidence shows. This is distinct from a sibling misfit called Starting Tomorrow, which stages hyperbolic discounting, where a reward's value spikes as it nears in time and the ranking of a smaller sooner reward against a larger later one reverses purely as a function of proximity, the same want discounted more steeply and not altered in kind. Here, in Ask Me When It Hurts, the preference itself changes: the cold self and the hot self are not weighing the same want on different clocks, they want different things, because a visceral state's presence or absence changes the content of what is wanted, not merely its urgency or its distance in time. The two patterns can even sit side by side in the same decision, one bending the clock and the other swapping the want, and telling them apart is most of what the Record is for. What remains, once the bound is drawn and the distinction held, is the play's own stakes: the truest answer to what a person will want lives on the far side of a state their calm self can plan around but never fully enter.

## Tension

A pattern real in its cases, and not a license to distrust every calm plan.
