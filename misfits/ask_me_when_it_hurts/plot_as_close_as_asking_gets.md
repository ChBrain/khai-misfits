# Plot: As Close as Asking Gets

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The gap carried into a decision that must be made for, or with, someone else.

## Action

At [The Bedside](place_the_bedside.md), [The Steward](persona_the_steward.md) from [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), on [Ask at the Threshold](plan_ask_at_the_threshold.md) through [Asking](process_asking.md), holds [The Return](piece_the_return.md): asking as near the threshold of the visceral state as timing allows, rather than only in the calm room before it or only once it has passed.

## Stage

A patient in a calm consultation room honestly underpredicts how much a future pain state will drive their own choices, and so may decline in the calm state an intervention the gripped state will urgently want, or accept in the gripped state what the calm state would have refused, with real consequences for consent, treatment, and the directives a person leaves for a future self they cannot yet fully imagine (Loewenstein, 2005). The Steward's answer is to move the asking as close to the threshold as the situation permits, catching the patient nearer the state the decision is actually about. The remedy is real and it is not a fix: the state cannot always be consulted in advance, and the calm self who returns once it passes still cannot fully recall what it just felt, so even the best timed question is answered by someone standing a little outside the moment it concerns.

## Tension

The closest anyone can get, and still not close enough.
