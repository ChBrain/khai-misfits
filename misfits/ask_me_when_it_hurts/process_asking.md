# Process: Asking

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). Borne at [The Bedside](place_the_bedside.md), precipitated by [The Steward](persona_the_steward.md), in [As Close as Asking Gets](plot_as_close_as_asking_gets.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A decision that must be made for, or with, someone whose preference will change with their state.

## Direction

From a plan made in the calm toward a preference taken as near the threshold as the moment allows.

## Lever

A preference asked nearer the state it will be enacted in captures more of what that state will actually want than one asked from the calm alone, though timing can never be guaranteed to land exactly at the threshold (Loewenstein, 2005).
