# Plot: A Gap No Forecast Crosses

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The plan meets the limit of what the calm state can actually know about the state that is coming.

## Action

At [the Calm](place_the_calm.md), [the Cold Self](persona_the_cold_self.md) from [the cold self's seat](position_cold_self.md) meets [The Gap](piece_the_gap.md): the structural blind spot where hunger, pain, fear, or desire cannot be simulated with their true pull from outside themselves.

## Stage

Hunger cannot be recalled with its true pull once sated, pain cannot be simulated with its true urgency once it has passed, and desire cannot be weighed at its true strength before it is felt (Loewenstein, 1996). The Cold Self reaches for the visceral state to include it in the plan and finds only its shape, not its weight; the memory or the anticipation stands in for the thing itself and stands in poorly. This is a limit of access, not of intelligence or care; the Cold Self has reasoned as well as any calm state could, and the gap would remain even for a sharper mind reasoning in the same chair.

## Tension

A forecast built honestly, and built without the one thing that will decide it.
