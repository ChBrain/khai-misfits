# Place: The Calm

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The setting free of any visceral pull, where the plan is made and its blind spot is found. Hosts [What the Calm Can See](plot_what_the_calm_can_see.md), [A Gap No Forecast Crosses](plot_a_gap_no_forecast_crosses.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A person reasoning clearly, no hunger, pain, fear, or desire in the room.
The plan taking shape, one careful consideration at a time.

## Holds

[The Plan](piece_the_plan.md). The honest plan is set down here, reasoned from everything within reach of a state that is, at this moment, free and clear.

[The Gap](piece_the_gap.md). The limit of what the calm state can know is already present here too, quiet, not yet felt as a problem, waiting on a state that has not yet arrived to expose it.

## Offers

To the Cold Self: a clear, calm view, nothing yet clouding the reasoning.

## Withheld

Any felt sense of the visceral pull itself.
From here it cannot be simulated, only reasoned about abstractly.
