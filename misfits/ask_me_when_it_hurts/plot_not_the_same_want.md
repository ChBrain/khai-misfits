# Plot: Not the Same Want

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The calm plan meets the state it was made without.

## Action

At [The Grip](place_the_grip.md), [The Hot Self](persona_the_hot_self.md) from [the hot self's seat](position_hot_self.md) meets [The Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md): the calm plan set aside, not weighed and beaten, simply no longer what is wanted.

## Stage

The plan breaks not from weakness but because the self holding it now honestly wants something else (Loewenstein, 1996). No villain: the Cold Self reasoned soundly and the Hot Self wants honestly, and neither is lying to the other, they are, in this moment, different selves, each answerable only to what it can feel and know from where it stands. This is distinct from a sibling misfit called Starting Tomorrow, where the same reward is merely discounted more steeply as it nears in time and the want itself never changes; here the want changes in kind because of the state's presence, not its timing.

## Tension

A promise broken by no one, since the self who made it is not the self who is here now.
