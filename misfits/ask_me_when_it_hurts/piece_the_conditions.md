# Piece: The Conditions

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The bound: the gap's true, variable scope, strongest for acute and unfamiliar drive states and weaker for mild or well rehearsed ones.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Bedside](place_the_bedside.md), carried by [Bounding](process_bounding.md): the accumulated cases weighed against each other here, not any single one taken as the whole pattern.

## Load Bearing

The gap is documented across many cases but is not uniform in size; it is strongest where the visceral state is acute and unfamiliar and weaker where it is mild or well rehearsed, so the pattern is a real and variable tendency and not a claim that all self-prediction fails (the visceral-influence and empathy-gap literature that followed Loewenstein). A state met many times before leaves a self that has, in effect, been asked before; a state that arrives suddenly and unfamiliar leaves a self with nothing to draw on. The bound is what keeps the claim honest to its own limits.

## Apparent

Looks like a hedge that undercuts the whole pattern; is actually what keeps the pattern honest.
