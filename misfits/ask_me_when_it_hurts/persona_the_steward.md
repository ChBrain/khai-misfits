# Persona: The Steward

## Taxonomy

[Steward](position_steward.md), inside [Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The archetype of the clinician or caregiver who must plan around a gap that is not their own, asking as near the threshold as the moment allows.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

It looks like the fix, the professional who knows to ask at the right moment and so closes the gap the two selves could not close alone, consent taken carefully, a directive drafted with the patient's true preference in view.

## Action

Ask at the threshold. At [the Bedside](place_the_bedside.md), on [Ask at the Threshold](plan_ask_at_the_threshold.md) through [Asking](process_asking.md), the Steward holds [the Return](piece_the_return.md), the visceral state passing and the calm self resuming, no better informed than before it began (Loewenstein, 2005).

## Shadow

It is not the fix it looks like. The Steward's remedy narrows the gap by asking as close to the state as the moment allows, but the state cannot always be consulted in advance, and the calm self who returns once it passes cannot fully recall what it just felt, so the next plan is made no wiser than the last.

## Tell

It asks as late as it can, and still, sometimes, asks too soon or too late to catch the truth.
