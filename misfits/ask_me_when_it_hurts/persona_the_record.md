# Persona: The Record

## Taxonomy

[Record](position_record.md), inside [Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The archetype of the accumulated cases, holding the pattern's true and bounded reach across domains, states, and people.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

It looks like the pattern proven beyond doubt, case after case of a calm plan overruled by a state it could not foresee, evidence enough, it would seem, to distrust every calm plan on principle.

## Action

Hold the true scope. At [the Bedside](place_the_bedside.md), on [Hold the True Scope](plan_hold_the_true_scope.md) through [Bounding](process_bounding.md), the Record holds [the Conditions](piece_the_conditions.md), the gap strongest for acute and unfamiliar drive states and weaker for mild or well-rehearsed ones (the visceral-influence and empathy-gap literature that followed Loewenstein).

## Shadow

Its evidence is real and its edges are just as real. The Record refuses the larger claim the cases seem to invite, that no calm plan can be trusted, since the gap it documents varies by domain and by the state's intensity and familiarity, and a calm plan made about a mild or well-rehearsed state holds up far better than one made about an acute or unfamiliar one.

## Tell

It keeps the pattern honest by keeping its edges, and refuses the bigger story the cases seem to want it to tell.
