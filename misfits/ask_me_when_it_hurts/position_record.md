# Position: Record

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The seat of the accumulated cases, and marks that the pattern's honesty is measured by what it refuses to claim as much as by what it documents.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The accumulated cases, real and repeatedly demonstrated, a calm plan overruled by a state it could not foresee.
No case that proves more than the state it documents, since the gap's size is not uniform across every domain, intensity, and level of familiarity.
The true, bounded scope, strongest where the state is acute and unfamiliar, weaker where it is mild or well rehearsed.

## Orders

Hold the pattern to what the cases show, and generalize no further than the evidence reaches.
Resist the larger claim the accumulated cases seem to invite, that no calm plan can ever be trusted, since a plan made about a mild or familiar state holds up far better than one made about an acute or unfamiliar one.
Keep the edges of the pattern as carefully as the pattern itself, since a claim held past its true scope is a different, weaker claim than the one the evidence supports.

## Loses

No confidence overclaimed, and commits no error by refusing the bigger story the cases seem to want it to tell.
Because its authority rests on holding exactly what the record supports, the seat takes no credit for a broader claim it declines to make, and yet the discipline of refusing it is easy to mistake for a weaker case than the one actually made.
It keeps its honestly bounded pattern and loses no cause for blame, the seat that documents the gap without overreaching it.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honesty is measured by what it refuses to claim as much as by what it documents,
holding case after case to its own true scope, strongest here and weaker there,
so the seat that proves the pattern real is the seat that, by the same fact, proves it is not everywhere the same.
