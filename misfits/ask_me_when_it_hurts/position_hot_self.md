# Position: Hot Self

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The seat of the visceral state, and marks that its want is as honest as the plan it overrules, and not weaker for overruling it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real and involuntary pull, present and demanding, attention narrowed onto the drive and its relief.
No access to the calm state's forecast in this moment, since the state that is here now crowds out the considerations the earlier plan weighed.
A want that has changed in kind and not only grown more urgent, honestly held and not feigned.

## Orders

Want what you want, now, honestly, since this state is as real as the one that made the plan it overrules.
Do not mistake the strength of the pull for a failure of character; it is the state occupying the mind while it is present, not a weaker self losing to a stronger impulse.
Know that the plan being set aside was sincerely made, and that setting it aside is not a betrayal of it.

## Loses

Nothing of its own honesty, and commits no fault for wanting what it wants while it is present.
Because the want is genuinely its own and not a corruption of the calm plan, the seat takes no blame for the reversal, and yet the plan it overrules loses ground it never dishonestly held.
It keeps its honestly felt want and loses no cause for blame, the gripped state that wants exactly what a state like this one wants and could not have wanted anything else.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honest want overrules an honest plan that neither self made false,
present and demanding, narrowing everything down to the drive and its relief,
so the seat that wants as fully as it can is the seat that, by the same fact, cannot hand its want back to the plan it replaces.
