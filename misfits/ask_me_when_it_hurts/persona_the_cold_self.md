# Persona: The Cold Self

## Taxonomy

[Cold Self](position_cold_self.md), inside [Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The archetype of the calm self, free of hunger, pain, fear, or desire, who plans honestly for a state it cannot enter.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person of ordinary good sense. It looks like prudence itself, weighing what usually matters, choosing what a reasonable person would choose with a clear head, the very picture of a plan made responsibly.

## Action

Reason from here. At [the Calm](place_the_calm.md), on [Plan From Here](plan_plan_from_here.md) through [Forecasting](process_forecasting.md), the Cold Self holds [the Plan](piece_the_plan.md), and it meets [the Gap](piece_the_gap.md), the limit of what a state free of the pull can know about the state that is coming (Loewenstein, 1996).

## Shadow

Its reasoning is sound and its sincerity is real, and neither saves the plan. The Cold Self forecasts as well as its access allows, but a visceral state is not fully imaginable from outside itself, so the plan it makes is built without the very thing that will decide whether it holds.

## Tell

It plans for a self it can never fully imagine, and calls the plan finished anyway.
