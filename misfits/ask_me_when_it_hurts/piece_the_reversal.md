# Piece: The Reversal

## Taxonomy

[Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The harm: the calm plan abandoned because what is wanted has changed, not merely how urgently it is wanted.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Grip](place_the_grip.md), carried by [Wanting](process_wanting.md): the reversal happens here, in the fullness of the state, not as a weighing against the plan but as a wanting that no longer runs through the plan's terms at all.

## Load Bearing

The plan is set aside not from weakness but because the self holding it now honestly wants something else (Loewenstein, 1996). This is distinct from Starting Tomorrow, its sibling misfit: there the same reward is discounted more steeply as it nears in time, the want unchanged and only its weighting shifting, while here the want itself has changed in kind because of the visceral state's presence, not its timing. Nobody lied and nobody caved. The plan was true for the self that made it, and the reversal is true for the self that meets it, and the two are simply not the same self.

## Apparent

Looks like a broken promise, weakness of will, a failure to hold the line; is actually a different, equally honest want overruling an equally honest plan.

## Yearbook

Met by [the Hot Self](persona_the_hot_self.md) through [Wanting](process_wanting.md) at [the Grip](place_the_grip.md), dramatized in [Not the Same Want](plot_not_the_same_want.md). It breaks [the Plan](piece_the_plan.md), and it is what [the Return](piece_the_return.md) must be carried past.
