# Persona: The Hot Self

## Taxonomy

[Hot Self](position_hot_self.md), inside [Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md). The archetype of the same person under a visceral state's grip, wanting honestly and, in that moment, differently.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

It looks like weakness from the outside, a plan abandoned, a promise broken. Up close it is nothing of the kind: a real and involuntary state, present and demanding, with attention narrowed onto the drive and its relief.

## Action

Want it now. At [the Grip](place_the_grip.md), on [Want This Now](plan_want_this_now.md) through [Wanting](process_wanting.md), the Hot Self holds [the Onset](piece_the_onset.md), and it meets [the Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md), the calm plan set aside by a want that has changed and not only grown more urgent (Loewenstein, 1996).

## Shadow

It is not overridden by a lesser impulse and it is not lying to itself. The Hot Self is, in this moment, honestly a different self, pursuing what it wants with the same sincerity the Cold Self brought to the plan it now sets aside, distinct from Starting Tomorrow's Present Self, who wants the same reward more urgently rather than a different thing entirely.

## Tell

It breaks a promise it never got to make, since the self that made it is, right now, someone else.
