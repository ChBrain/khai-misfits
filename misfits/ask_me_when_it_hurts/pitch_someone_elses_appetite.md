# Pitch: Someone Else's Appetite

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Ask Me When It Hurts](play_ask_me_when_it_hurts.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a calm plan is honestly wrong about a want that belongs to a state the calm self cannot enter.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Quiet and physical, the honest gap between who a person is calm and who they become in the grip of hunger, pain, fear, or desire. Nothing here is dishonesty or weakness; the calm plan is sincere and the gripped want is real.

## Undertow

A visceral state is not fully imaginable from outside itself, so a calm self's forecast of a gripped state is honestly made and reliably wrong. The gap runs in both directions, and a self that has just passed through a visceral state cannot fully recall, once calm again, how strongly it pulled.

## Nerve

That a visceral state changes what is wanted and not only how urgently it is wanted, so the plan the calm self makes is not overridden by weakness but by a want the calm self could never have felt on its own behalf, something closer to someone else's appetite than its own forgotten one. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since the gap is documented and varies by domain and by the intensity and familiarity of the state, strongest for acute and unfamiliar drive states and weaker for mild or well-rehearsed ones (Loewenstein, 1996; 2005).

## Echo

Plan honestly in the calm, and be overruled by a want the calm self never got to feel.
