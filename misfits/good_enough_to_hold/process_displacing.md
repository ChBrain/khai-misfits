# Process: Displacing

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md). Borne at [The Mandate](place_the_mandate.md), precipitated by [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) reading the schedule before the instrument, in [It Says Here We May Hold It](plot_it_says_here_we_may_hold_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A committee holding other people's money under a mandate keyed to a recognised grade, and a file that will be read by people who know the outcome.

## Direction

From investigating an instrument toward establishing authority for holding it, so the question becomes whether the position is permitted rather than whether it is sound.

## Lever

That overriding a rule-recognised grade with the committee's own analysis exceeds the authority the seat holds, so compliance is the safe course and analysis is the exposed one, and nobody has to get lazy.

## Echo

[The Displaced Diligence](piece_the_displaced_diligence.md), the research stopping at exactly the seat with the strongest reason to do it.
