# Process: Designating

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md). Borne at [The Rulebook](place_the_rulebook.md), precipitated by the rule having to say whose grade counts, in [Who Is Allowed to Say](plot_who_is_allowed_to_say.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A rule that relies on a grade and therefore cannot leave open who may issue one, since an unrestricted list would let anybody sell compliance.

## Direction

From an open market in opinions toward a named list of parties whose opinion has legal effect, so the second permission sits above the first.

## Lever

That the incumbency and the pricing power are creatures of the designation rather than of any record, so a more accurate newcomer cannot take business that was never about accuracy (Lawrence J. White).
