## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play overreaches into a claim it cannot support, that ratings are worthless or that the agencies acted in bad faith, neither of which the source literature says and neither of which the house will stage.
It bears the load as the limit that keeps this a design fault. Because the trap needs a rule referencing a private grade and does not arise where the grade is advisory, it is conditional and locatable; and because removing the reference obliges the regulator to fund a public assessment at a scale nobody has funded or to write the standard that becomes the next licence, the remedy is real, was ordered, and is not free.
