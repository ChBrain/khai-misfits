# Persona: The Issuer

## Taxonomy

[Issuer](position_issuer.md). The one buying a document about itself that it does not need to read.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Practical and unsentimental, treating the rating as a cost of access rather than as a verdict.
Knows precisely where the line is and precisely what falling below it would close off.
Has no view worth stating on whether the methodology is any good.

## Action

Runs [Buy What Makes It Holdable](plan_buy_what_makes_it_holdable.md): through [Licensing](process_licensing.md) at [the Issuance](place_the_issuance.md), taking [the Licence](piece_the_licence.md).
Procures the grade that makes the paper eligible for the institutions holding most of the money.
Prices the rating against the spread it unlocks rather than against the assessment it contains.

## Shadow

Not cynical and not evasive: a treasurer that declined to obtain the grade would be denying its own company access to the capital it was hired to raise, and would be replaced by one that obtained it.
Because what is being bought is legibility to the rulebook, the accuracy of the document is incidental to the transaction, and the seat would pay the same for a correct grade and an incorrect one.
It is the party the assessment is about and the party with the least reason to care what it says.

## Tell

Can name the threshold to the notch and cannot say what the notch measures.
Treats a downgrade as a funding event rather than as news about itself, which is exactly what the rule has made it.
