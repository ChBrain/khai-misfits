# Piece: The Licence

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md). The conversion: a rule says investment grade, and a private opinion becomes the permission an issuer needs to be held at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Rulebook](place_the_rulebook.md) where it is created and at [The Issuance](place_the_issuance.md) where it is bought (Partnoy).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing the grade stays advisory and no rule keys to it, and the whole play dissolves into an ordinary market for opinions that disciplines itself in the ordinary way.
It bears the load as the moment the trap is complete. Because the conversion happens at the act of reference and requires no misconduct by anybody, a perfectly incorruptible regulator produces it, and there is no later point at which somebody could have behaved better and prevented it.

## Apparent

Official recognition, and it looks like an endorsement of quality: a regulator has examined this standard and found it good enough to build a rule on.
