# Plan: Sell the Grade They Need

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the rater's seat](position_rater.md), inside [Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Rater](position_rater.md)

## Direction

Supply the recognised grade to the parties who must have one, at a price set by what the grade unlocks rather than by what the analysis cost, since that is the value the buyer is actually receiving. Maintain the methodology and publish it, because the record is genuine and this seat did earn it, and because a published method is the honest thing to have whether or not anybody in the chain reads it. Do not pretend the reputational channel is still doing the work: it built the record, and it stopped being the channel the moment the product became compulsory. Accept that accuracy cannot be converted into revenue here, that a more accurate rival cannot undercut this seat and cannot be recognised without the designation, and that the protection is from competition the seat would have won and from pressure it needed.

## Targets

- [ ] the grade is priced against what it unlocks, since that is what the buyer receives
- [ ] the methodology is maintained and published, whether or not anyone in the chain reads it
- [ ] no claim is made that reputational discipline still reaches the product
- [ ] it is recorded that accuracy has no revenue channel here, and why
- [ ] the designation is named as the source of the pricing power, rather than the record
