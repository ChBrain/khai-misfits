# Plan: Hold What the Rule Permits

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the investor's seat](position_investor.md), inside [Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Investor](position_investor.md)

## Direction

Manage money that is not this seat's own, under a mandate keyed to a recognised grade, and keep a file that will be read afterwards by people who know the outcome. So begin with the schedule rather than the instrument: take the position where the grade is recognised and the schedule permits it, and decline it where it is not. Do not override a rule-recognised grade with the committee's own analysis, however good the analysis is, because doing so exceeds the authority this committee holds and would have to be answered for if the position went wrong. Sell on the notch when the grade crosses the line, immediately, because the mandate requires it and requires it at once. Record that this is compliance rather than conviction, and that the sale is a rule event and not a judgement about the borrower.

## Orders

([The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) complies through [Displacing](process_displacing.md) at [the Mandate](place_the_mandate.md), setting [the Displaced Diligence](piece_the_displaced_diligence.md), and sells through [Triggering](process_triggering.md) at [the Downgrade](place_the_downgrade.md), meeting [the Cliff](piece_the_cliff.md).)

## Implementation

([The Mandate](place_the_mandate.md) is where authority replaces analysis; [Displacing](process_displacing.md) stops the research without anybody deciding to, and [Triggering](process_triggering.md) turns a one-notch opinion into a simultaneous sale.)

## Targets

- [ ] the schedule is read before the instrument, and the position follows the schedule
- [ ] no rule-recognised grade is overridden by the committee's own analysis
- [ ] the file records the authority for every holding
- [ ] the sale on the notch is executed at once, as the mandate requires
- [ ] it is recorded that the sale is a rule event and not a judgement about the borrower
