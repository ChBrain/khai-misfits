# Plan: Buy What Makes It Holdable

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the issuer's seat](position_issuer.md), inside [Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Issuer](position_issuer.md)

## Direction

Raise capital at the best available cost, which means getting the paper inside the eligibility schedule of the institutions that hold most of the money. Find the line, to the notch. Procure the recognised grade that puts the issue above it. Price the rating against the spread it unlocks rather than against the analysis it contains, because the spread is the quantity the purchase actually moves and the analysis is not what the buyers will read. Form no view on the methodology, since a view would not change the decision: the grade is required either way. Treat a downgrade as a funding event and plan for it as one. And do not pretend to be buying an assessment of the company, because what is being bought is legibility to the rulebook, and a treasurer that declined to buy it would be denying its own company the access it was hired to obtain.
