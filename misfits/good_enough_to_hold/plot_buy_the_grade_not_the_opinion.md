# Plot: Buy the Grade, Not the Opinion

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Issuance](place_the_issuance.md), where paper below the line cannot be held by the institutions that hold most of the money.

## Action

Through [Licensing](process_licensing.md), [the Issuer](persona_the_issuer.md), from [the issuer's seat](position_issuer.md), on [Buy What Makes It Holdable](plan_buy_what_makes_it_holdable.md) takes [the Licence](piece_the_licence.md): the threshold is located to the notch, the recognised grade above it is procured, and the fee is priced against the spread it unlocks rather than against the analysis it contains. No view is formed on the methodology, because no view would change the decision. The scene closes with a treasurer doing precisely the job it was hired to do, and with the assessment of the company incidental to the transaction.

## Stage

A treasurer that declined to obtain the grade would be denying its own company access to the capital it was hired to raise, would have to explain that to a board, and would be replaced by somebody who obtained it.

## Tension

The party the assessment is about is the party with least reason to read it, and would pay the same for a correct grade and an incorrect one, so the buyer best placed to challenge the analysis has no interest in its quality at all.
