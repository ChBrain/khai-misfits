# Piece: The Second Gate

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md). The incumbency: the designation deciding who may issue a recognised grade, so the pricing power belongs to the rule.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Rulebook](place_the_rulebook.md), in the list of whose grade counts, written because an unrestricted list would let anybody sell compliance (Lawrence J. White).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing anyone may issue a recognised grade, and the first objection any reader raises is answered: a more accurate rater would undercut the incumbents and the market would correct.
It bears the load as the reason competition does not repair this. Because the designation is itself a licence sitting above the first one, a newcomer cannot be recognised without it and cannot compete on accuracy where accuracy is not the product, so the protection is from the pressure the incumbents needed and the rivalry they would have won.

## Apparent

Necessary hygiene, and it is that: a rule relying on a grade cannot leave open whose grade counts without inviting anybody at all to sell permission.
