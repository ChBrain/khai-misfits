## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The correct remedy, ordered and agreed: the reference is the mechanism, so the reference comes out.

## Direction

From a rule that points at a private grade toward a rule that must point at something, since a rule pointing at nothing permits nothing.

## Lever

That the two available fillers both cost: a public assessment nobody has funded at the necessary scale, or a substitute standard that becomes the next licence the moment a rule points at it.
