# Plot: One Notch and We Are Sellers

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Downgrade](place_the_downgrade.md), where a grade moves one step and crosses the line a schedule was keyed to.

## Action

Through [Triggering](process_triggering.md), [the Investor](persona_the_investor.md) on [Hold What the Rule Permits](plan_hold_what_the_rule_permits.md) meets [the Cliff](piece_the_cliff.md): the mandate requires the sale and requires it at once, so every holder bound to that line is a seller in the same hour, in the same direction, without anybody having changed their mind. The scene closes with a one-notch private opinion converted into a synchronised mechanical sale, and with the selling pressure owed to the rule rather than to the news (the Financial Stability Board).

## Stage

It will be reported as a market reaction to bad news, which is what a market is supposed to do. Every seller is executing its mandate at the moment the mandate requires, and no one of them could have waited.

## Tension

Composure does not help and dispersion of holders does not help, because the synchronisation was written years earlier into every schedule at once, so it is perfect compliance that produces the disorder.
