# Plot: Who Is Allowed to Say

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Rulebook](place_the_rulebook.md) again, with the obvious follow-up question: if the rule relies on a grade, whose grade counts.

## Action

Through [Designating](process_designating.md), [the Regulator](persona_the_regulator.md) on [Reference the Standard That Exists](plan_reference_the_standard_that_exists.md) sets [the Second Gate](piece_the_second_gate.md): a named list of parties whose opinion has legal effect, drawn up because an unrestricted list would let anybody at all sell compliance. The scene closes with the incumbency and the pricing power belonging to the regulation rather than to any record, and a more accurate newcomer unable to take business that was never about accuracy (Lawrence J. White).

## Stage

Necessary hygiene, and genuinely necessary. A rule that relied on a grade while leaving open who may issue one would be an invitation, and the first person to accept it would not be a rating agency.

## Tension

This is the answer to the objection every reader raises, that competition should fix it, and the answer is that the same rule which made the product compulsory closed the market for it, in the same act and for a good reason.
