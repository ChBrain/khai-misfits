# Pitch: Somebody Already Checked

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reassuring and faintly administrative, the tone of a form that has already been signed.
A competent body with a method and a record has assessed this instrument, a regulator recognised the assessment, and the schedule permits the position, so the work has been done and it has been done by specialists.
Nothing false in it. The regulator could not staff an in-house assessment of every instrument in the market, the assessment it pointed at was the best that existed, and the committee relying on it is complying rather than shirking.

## Undertow

Under the recognition, a product whose value has stopped depending on being right.
The moment a rule says investment grade, the grade is a permission and not a forecast, and a permission does not have to be accurate to be necessary, so demand rises as informational content falls (Partnoy).
And the rule decides who may issue it, so the pricing power belongs to the regulation and a more accurate newcomer cannot take business that was never about accuracy (Lawrence J. White).

## Nerve

The sufficiency of the check, and the catch that the check is a permit:
that everyone in the chain has correctly relied on somebody else's authority, that the investment committee which did its own analysis instead would be exceeding its mandate,
and that somebody already checked is the sentence in which the last party who would have looked decides not to.

## Echo

After the run: the research no longer done by the parties best placed to do it, with nobody having got lazy and every one of them behaving correctly.
And the line as a mechanism, since a one-notch downgrade makes every threshold-bound holder a forced seller in the same hour, the pressure owed to the rule rather than the news (the Financial Stability Board).
And the bound held in view: taking the reference out was ordered and is right, and leaves the regulator to build the assessment at public expense or to write the standard that becomes the next licence.
