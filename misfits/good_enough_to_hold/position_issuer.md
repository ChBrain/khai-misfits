# Position: Issuer

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md). The seat the assessment is about, and the one with least reason to read it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Paper to sell and a class of buyers who may only hold what the schedule permits.
A line, known to the notch, and a spread on either side of it.
A mandate from its own board to raise capital at the best available cost.

## Orders

Buy what makes it holdable: procure the grade that puts the paper inside the eligibility schedule.
Price the rating against the spread it unlocks, since that is the quantity the purchase actually moves.
Accept what the seat does: a treasurer that declined would be denying its own company the capital it was hired to raise.

## Loses

Any interest in what the document says, since the purchase is of legibility to the rulebook and the seat would pay the same for a correct grade or an incorrect one.
Because the assessment is incidental to the transaction, the party best able to supply the underlying facts has no reason to press for a better analysis of them.
It keeps its access and loses the one reading of itself that an outsider was paid to produce.

## Drives

Toward the seat that buys a verdict on itself and never opens it,
finding the line, procuring the grade, pricing it against the spread, and treating a downgrade as a funding event,
so a purchase that any board would approve in a sentence is the point at which the rating stops being about the company at all.
