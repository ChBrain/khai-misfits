# Persona: The Rater

## Taxonomy

[Rater](position_rater.md). The one who would rather be right and cannot be paid for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Professional and a little defensive, insisting the methodology is published and the record is checkable.
Both of those are true, and neither is what the buyer is buying.
Would prefer, sincerely, that the work were the product.

## Action

Runs [Sell the Grade They Need](plan_sell_the_grade_they_need.md): through [Licensing](process_licensing.md) at [the Issuance](place_the_issuance.md), setting [the Inverted Demand](piece_the_inverted_demand.md).
Sells a recognised grade to an issuer that must have one, at a price set by what the grade unlocks.
Maintains the analysis, and watches the revenue detach from it.

## Shadow

Not corrupt and not indifferent: the discipline that built the record was reputational, and reputation stopped being the channel the moment the product became compulsory.
Because a permission need not be right to be necessary, the market grows as the informational content falls, so nothing this seat can do converts accuracy into revenue.
It holds a pricing power it did not win, granted by a designation it did not write.

## Tell

Publishes a methodology nobody in the chain has to read, and is correct that it published it.
Cannot be undercut by a more accurate rival, because the business is not accuracy and the rival cannot be recognised without the designation.
