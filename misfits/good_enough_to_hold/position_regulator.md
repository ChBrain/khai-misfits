# Position: Regulator

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md). The seat that made the delegation, and the only one that could end it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A rule to write, a market of instruments it cannot assess one by one, and no funded capability to try.
A private standard with a genuine record, available and already trusted by the people it regulates.
An industry that would rather the reference were not written at all.

## Orders

Write a rule that works: point it at the best assessment that exists rather than inventing one the agency cannot staff.
Say whose grade counts, since an unrestricted list would let anybody sell compliance.
Accept what the seat does: the delegation is the cheapest competent answer, and a regulator inventing its own standard would be asked who checks that one.

## Loses

The standard's quality, since the moment the rule points at it the grade's value stops depending on being right, and the discipline that produced the record no longer reaches the product.
Because the reference must also name who may issue it, the seat hands the incumbents a pricing power that belongs to the regulation and not to any record, so its own rule closes the market it was relying on.
It keeps a workable rule and loses the thing that made the rule work.

## Drives

Toward the seat that produces the whole trap without doing anything anybody could name as wrong,
pointing at the best assessment available, naming who may issue it, and being ordered years later to take every reference back out,
so a delegation made in good faith and against the industry becomes the reason nobody in the chain needs to know whether the instrument is any good.
