# Position: Investor

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md). The seat best placed to investigate and least free to act on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Money that is not its own, a mandate keyed to a recognised grade, and a schedule of what may be held.
The analysts, the data and the standing to form its own view of any instrument in the book.
A fiduciary duty, and a record of every decision that will be read afterwards by people who know the outcome.

## Orders

Hold what the rule permits: take the position because the grade is recognised and the schedule allows it.
Sell on the notch, because the mandate requires it and requires it immediately.
Accept what the seat does: overriding a rule-recognised grade with its own analysis would exceed the authority the committee holds.

## Loses

The use of its own judgement, since compliance is the safe course and analysis is the exposed one, so the party with the strongest reason to investigate has the strongest reason not to.
Because its selling is a rule event rather than a judgement about the borrower, it happens in the same hour as everybody else bound the same way, and the seat contributes to a forced sale it did not choose and could not time.
It keeps a defensible file and loses the research nobody else was placed to do.

## Drives

Toward the seat that is prudent in exactly the way that removes the prudence from the system,
reading the schedule rather than the prospectus, taking the permitted position, and selling on the notch without having changed its mind,
so a committee behaving correctly at every step is where the last independent look at the instrument was supposed to happen and does not.
