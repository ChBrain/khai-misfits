# Plan: Reference the Standard That Exists

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the regulator's seat](position_regulator.md), inside [Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Regulator](position_regulator.md)

## Direction

Write a rule that can actually be applied. The agency cannot assess every instrument in the market one by one and has not been funded to try, so point the capital requirement, the eligibility schedule and the mandate at the best assessment that already exists, which is a private one with a genuine record and one the regulated parties already use. Then say whose grade counts, because a rule relying on a grade without naming the issuers of it would let anybody sell compliance. Write it against the industry's preference rather than for it, and expect no thanks. And when the reference is later ordered out, take it out, then find something to stand where each reference stood: either an assessment built in house at a scale nobody has funded, or a substitute standard, knowing that the substitute becomes the next licence the moment a rule points at it.

## Orders

([The Regulator](persona_the_regulator.md) references through [Referencing](process_referencing.md) at [the Rulebook](place_the_rulebook.md), setting [the Licence](piece_the_licence.md); designates through [Designating](process_designating.md) at the same place, setting [the Second Gate](piece_the_second_gate.md); and replaces through [Replacing](process_replacing.md) at [the Repeal](place_the_repeal.md), meeting [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md).)

## Implementation

([The Rulebook](place_the_rulebook.md) is where a private grade becomes a legal threshold; [Referencing](process_referencing.md) converts the opinion into a permission, [Designating](process_designating.md) decides who may issue it, and [Replacing](process_replacing.md) discovers what the reference was holding up.)

## Targets

- [ ] the rule is written against an assessment that exists rather than one the agency cannot staff
- [ ] the issuers of a recognised grade are named, so compliance cannot be sold by anybody
- [ ] the reference is recorded as written against the industry's preference, not for it
- [ ] when ordered out, every statutory reference is removed rather than narrowed
- [ ] what stands in each reference's place is chosen knowingly: a public capability, or the next licence
