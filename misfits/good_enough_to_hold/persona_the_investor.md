# Persona: The Investor

## Taxonomy

[Investor](position_investor.md). The one best placed to investigate, and the one forbidden to act on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful and procedural, opening with the schedule rather than with the instrument.
Reads the mandate closely and the prospectus not at all, which is the correct allocation of its attention.
Would describe itself, accurately, as prudent.

## Action

Runs [Hold What the Rule Permits](plan_hold_what_the_rule_permits.md): through [Displacing](process_displacing.md) at [the Mandate](place_the_mandate.md), setting [the Displaced Diligence](piece_the_displaced_diligence.md), and through [Triggering](process_triggering.md) at [the Downgrade](place_the_downgrade.md), meeting [the Cliff](piece_the_cliff.md).
Takes the position because the grade is recognised and the schedule permits it.
Sells on the notch because the mandate requires it, at the same hour as everyone else bound the same way.

## Shadow

Not lazy: a committee that overrode a rule-recognised grade with its own analysis would be exceeding the authority it holds and would answer for the position if it went wrong, so the diligence is displaced rather than abandoned.
Because compliance is the safe course and analysis is the exposed one, the party with the strongest incentive to investigate has the strongest reason not to.
And its selling is not a judgement about the borrower at all, which is what makes it simultaneous.

## Tell

Can produce the authority for every holding and an opinion about none of them.
Becomes a forced seller without having changed its mind, since the mind was never what the position rested on.
