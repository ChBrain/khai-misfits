# Plot: Take the Reference Out

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Repeal](place_the_repeal.md), with a direction to strike every statutory reference to a rating and a list of the rules that carry one.

## Action

Through [Replacing](process_replacing.md), [the Regulator](persona_the_regulator.md) on [Reference the Standard That Exists](plan_reference_the_standard_that_exists.md) meets [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md): the references come out, correctly, and a blank appears where each one stood, because a rule that points at nothing permits nothing. Two fillers are available and no third: an assessment built in house at a scale nobody has funded, or a substitute standard that becomes the next licence the moment a rule points at it. And the limit is stated in the same breath, that the trap needs a rule referencing a private grade and does not arise where the grade is advisory.

## Stage

The remedy is correct, was agreed, and was ordered. The reference is the mechanism, so removing the reference is genuinely the thing that ends it, and nobody in the room is defending the old arrangement.

## Tension

Nothing in the mechanism is peculiar to credit ratings and everything in it follows from a rule pointing at a private grade, so the delegation keeps being remade under new names, and the room ending one licence is the room that will issue the next.
