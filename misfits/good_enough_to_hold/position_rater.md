# Position: Rater

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Hold](play_good_enough_to_hold.md). The seat holding a pricing power it did not win.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A published methodology, a checkable record, and analysts who would rather be right than wrong.
A designation, granted by rule, without which nothing it says would count.
Issuers who must have a grade and buyers who must not act without one.

## Orders

Sell the grade they need, at a price set by what the grade unlocks rather than by what the analysis cost.
Maintain the methodology and publish it, because the record is genuine and the seat did earn it.
Accept what the seat does: the discipline that built the record was reputational, and reputation stopped being the channel the moment the product became compulsory.

## Loses

Any way to convert accuracy into revenue, since a permission does not have to be right to be necessary, so the market grows as the informational content of the product falls.
Because the designation decides who may issue a recognised grade, the seat cannot be undercut by a more accurate rival and cannot use that safety for anything, the protection being from competition it would have won and from pressure it needed.
It keeps the franchise and loses the only mechanism that ever made the franchise worth holding.

## Drives

Toward the seat that would rather be right and cannot be paid for it,
selling the recognised grade, maintaining the analysis, and watching the revenue detach from the work,
so the party with the expertise ends up supplying a permission, having abandoned nothing and decided nothing.
