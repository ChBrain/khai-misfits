# Persona: The Regulator

## Taxonomy

[Regulator](position_regulator.md). The one who made the delegation, for reasons that were good then and are good now.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Plain and slightly weary, explaining a rule that has been misread as a favour to the industry.
Points out that the reference was written against the industry's preference and that nobody has funded the alternative.
Does not defend any particular rating and was never asked to.

## Action

Runs [Reference the Standard That Exists](plan_reference_the_standard_that_exists.md): through [Referencing](process_referencing.md) at [the Rulebook](place_the_rulebook.md), setting [the Licence](piece_the_licence.md); through [Designating](process_designating.md) at the same place, setting [the Second Gate](piece_the_second_gate.md); and through [Replacing](process_replacing.md) at [the Repeal](place_the_repeal.md), meeting [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md).
Writes investment grade into the capital rule because the alternative is an assessment it cannot staff.
Names who may issue a recognised grade, because an unrestricted list would let anyone sell compliance.

## Shadow

Not captured and not lazy: the delegation is the cheapest competent answer to a problem that has no cheap competent answer, and a regulator that invented its own standard would be asked who checks that one.
Because the conversion happens at the moment of reference rather than at any moment of misconduct, this seat produces the whole trap while doing nothing anybody could name as wrong.
And when it is ordered to take the reference back out, it discovers that something must stand where each reference stood.

## Tell

Can explain every clause and cannot explain what to put in place of one.
Removes the licence and issues its replacement in the same document, because a rule that points at nothing permits nothing.
