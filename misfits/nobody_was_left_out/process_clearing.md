# Process: Clearing

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). A short supply settling among many claimants by order of preference rather than by size of need.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An entitlement taken up at once by nearly everybody.
At [the hiring fair](place_the_hiring_fair.md), more posts than there are people qualified to fill them.

## Direction

Toward whoever is preferred, and only toward them.
The step nobody expects is the sharpness: a shortage does not distribute a proportionate share to each claimant, it satisfies them in order until it runs out, so **an advantage that is small still takes everything ahead of it**.
So a distribution of advantage that was almost flat, and a distribution of staffing that was almost flat with it, resolve into an outcome that is not flat at all.

## Lever

[The rank](piece_the_rank.md), which is an ordering and not a magnitude.
Because it is an ordering, the size of the edge does not moderate the size of the result, which is why nobody involved can see their own contribution to it.

## Echo

Full staffing at the front of the order and the entire shortfall at the back of it.
Nothing improper happened at any point in the process, and the process is the whole finding.
