# Position: Stock

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). The seat occupied by a quantity fixed years before the decision that needs it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A size, set by admissions and graduations already completed.
A growth rate of its own, which is not responsive to appropriation inside a school year.
An almost even distribution across schools, which is the state the scheme finds it in.

## Orders

Be however many it is.
Move, when the terms of one post differ from the terms of another, because moving is the only response it has.
Grow later, at its own pace, which is the only real remedy and arrives after the cohort has left.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and everything downstream: a constraint that binds on production time rather than on price cannot be relieved by money, so the entire funded demand resolves into a reordering of who holds the existing stock.
Because the seat is a count rather than a party, it cannot be negotiated with, appealed to or persuaded.
It ends the same size, differently placed.

## Drives

Toward nothing at all, having no intent,
never once acting,
so the seat that decides the outcome is the one seat in the play with no agency in it.
