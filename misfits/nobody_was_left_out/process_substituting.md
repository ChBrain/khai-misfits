# Process: Substituting

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). Filling the posts the auction left, with whoever is available before the term starts.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A room, twenty children, and a date that does not move.
At [the room of twenty](place_the_room_of_twenty.md), a post nobody with a completed credential applied for.

## Direction

Toward somebody at the front, which is unambiguously better than nobody.
The choice at this point is not between an experienced teacher and a new one, it is between a new one and an empty room, and every school makes it the same way and makes it correctly.
So the substitution is not a lowering of a standard by anybody, it is the only remaining move in a sequence whose earlier steps were all correct too.

## Lever

Time, which has run out.
Because the constraint at this step is the calendar rather than the budget, there is no version of this seat that waits for a better applicant.

## Echo

Under four per cent before, everywhere. More than twenty-one per cent in the lowest-income schools by the third year, against four point three in the highest.
The gradient was made here, out of a sequence in which no participant did anything they should not have.
