# Plan: Hire the Best Who Applies

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the preferred's seat](position_preferred.md), inside [Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Preferred](position_preferred.md)

## Direction

Advertise the posts, interview properly, and appoint the strongest applicant. Break no rule, offer nothing improper, and accept that more people apply here than elsewhere.

## Orders

([The District Up the Freeway](persona_the_district_up_the_freeway.md) posts its vacancies on the same day as everybody else, receives more applications than it has posts, and fills them early with experienced people.)

## Implementation

([The Hiring Fair](place_the_hiring_fair.md), where the same short supply is being sought by everybody at once; the [clearing](process_clearing.md) process settling by [the rank](piece_the_rank.md); the [drawing](process_drawing.md) that follows from it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the posts are advertised openly and filled on the merits
- [ ] the advantage is real, small, and not of this seat's making
- [ ] more applicants arrive here than there are posts, without anything being offered to attract them
- [ ] first refusal on the scarce supply follows from the order and not from the size of the edge
