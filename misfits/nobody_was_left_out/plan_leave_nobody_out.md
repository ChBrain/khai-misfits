# Plan: Leave Nobody Out

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the entitler's seat](position_entitler.md), inside [Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Entitler](position_entitler.md)

## Direction

Fund the benefit for every district on the same terms in the same year. Do not rank the schools, because ranking them means writing down which children wait, and the evidence does not support making any of them wait.

## Orders

([The Statute](persona_the_statute.md) offers money per class to any district bringing its early grades down to twenty, names no school, excludes none, and sets a date.)

## Implementation

([The Credential Programme](place_the_credential_programme.md), which the scheme does not touch; the [entitling](process_entitling.md) process creating a simultaneous claim on [the credential](piece_the_credential.md); the benefit itself delivered as [the class of twenty](piece_the_class_of_twenty.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the offer is open to every district on identical terms
- [ ] no school is named as waiting, because naming one is the thing being avoided
- [ ] the funding is per class, since a teacher is not the legislature's to appropriate
- [ ] the date is set, because a scheme without one is a statement of intent
