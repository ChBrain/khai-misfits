# Position: Mover

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). The seat where the scarce input relocates, one unremarkable career decision at a time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Experience, which is exactly what is short.
A vacancy elsewhere, advertised openly, that is better on terms anybody would weigh.
No obligation of any kind to weigh anything else.

## Orders

Apply.
Accept, if appointed.
Give notice, work it, and go.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat is the mechanism's only moving part: at the scale of one person this is a career, at the scale of a state hiring simultaneously it is the redistribution the whole outcome consists of.
Because no single move is visible as a transfer, there is no point at which the seat could reasonably be asked to decide otherwise, and no aggregate that anybody is responsible for.
It ends better off, blameless, and elsewhere.

## Drives

Toward a better post,
never once taking anything from anybody it could name,
so the seat that does nothing unusual is the seat the whole reallocation runs through.
