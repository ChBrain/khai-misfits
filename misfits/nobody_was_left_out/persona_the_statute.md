# Persona: The Statute

## Taxonomy

[Entitler](position_entitler.md). The funded universal claim: the same terms, to every district, in the same year.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Well drafted, well evidenced and generous.
Rests on experimental work that is genuinely good and that found the largest gains for the children who had least.
Nothing careless in it: the universality is a deliberate choice and the defensible one.

## Action

Offers money per class to any district that brings its early-grade classes down to twenty.
Sets a date, because a scheme without one is a statement of intent.
Names no school and excludes none, which is what makes it an entitlement rather than a grant.

## Shadow

Nothing, and it authors the whole structure without doing anything wrong: the alternative to universality is a list of the schools that go last, which is a worse document to have to write and a worse one to defend.
Because the statute can fund a class and cannot staff one, its full and equal uptake is precisely what turns the qualified teachers already in post into the thing being competed for.
It ends having delivered exactly what it promised, everywhere, on time.

## Tell

Says: this is for every child in the state.
Which is true, is the honourable part, and is the mechanism.
