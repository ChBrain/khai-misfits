# Plan: Take the Better Job

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the mover's seat](position_mover.md), inside [Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Mover](position_mover.md)

## Direction

Weigh the pay and the commute, apply for the better post, and take it if it is offered. Owe nothing to an aggregate you cannot see and could not act on if you could.

## Orders

([The Teacher Who Moves](persona_the_teacher_who_moves.md) sees a vacancy that pays a little more and takes forty minutes less a day, applies, is appointed, gives notice and works it.)

## Implementation

([The Hiring Fair](place_the_hiring_fair.md), where the move is one appointment among thousands; the [drawing](process_drawing.md) process moving [the credential](piece_the_credential.md) up the freeway; nothing in it visible at the scale a person acts on.)

## Targets

- [ ] the vacancy is real, advertised, and better on terms anybody would weigh
- [ ] the application and the appointment are ordinary in every respect
- [ ] no individual move is large enough to register as a transfer of anything
- [ ] the aggregate of such moves is the whole of the reallocation
