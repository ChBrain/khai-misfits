# Persona: The First-Year Permit

## Taxonomy

[Substitute](position_substitute.md). A real teacher in a real classroom on their first day, hired because the alternative was nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Willing, often very good, and at the start.
Holds a permit or a waiver rather than a completed credential, which is a statement about a date and not about a person.
Did not create the shortage and is the reason the room is not empty.

## Action

Applies for a post that a fully credentialed applicant did not apply for.
Is appointed, quickly, because September is close.
Stands at the front of a class of twenty and begins, learning the job while doing it, as everybody in it once did.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is where the play's cost is finally paid without anybody at it being at fault: what the children in this room get is a smaller class and a teacher in their first year, and the scheme's own evidence says a smaller class helps, so both halves are true at once.
Because a permit is a timing fact rather than a competence fact, the seat cannot be criticised and cannot be substituted for experience either.
It ends teaching, improving, and standing in the one in five where before it was one in twenty-five.

## Tell

Says: I start on Monday.
Which is the correct answer to a room that would otherwise have nobody in it.
