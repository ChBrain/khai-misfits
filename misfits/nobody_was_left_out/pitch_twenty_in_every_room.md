# Pitch: Twenty in Every Room

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry, administrative, procedural.
The register of a statute beside a staffing sheet: rooms counted, posts counted, a date by which both have to agree.
Nothing indignant anywhere in it, because the universal offer is the fair one and somebody had to write it.

## Undertow

Under the entitlement, two facts that do not negotiate.
A credential takes years, so the stock on the day is the stock. A school opens in September, so the post is filled with whoever is available.
Between those two, money offered to everybody at once is not a purchase. It is an auction, and an auction has an order.

## Nerve

The unease of watching a fair scheme, correctly designed, honestly funded, delivered in full to every school that asked,
produce in three years a gap that was not there before,
in the direction the scheme existed to close.

## Echo

After the run: a room of twenty children, exactly as promised, in every school in the state,
a teacher at the front of each one,
and, in the schools that needed the scheme most, a one-in-five chance that the teacher is on a permit rather than a credential, where before it was one in twenty-five everywhere.
