## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A finding that the benefit is largest for those with least, which is what makes acting on it urgent.
Two available forms, universal and targeted, of which only one avoids naming the schools that wait.

## Orders

Nothing, and it authors the structure without a misstep anywhere in it: the universal form is the fair form, and the fair form is what concentrates the whole state's demand on one morning.
Because the seat can appropriate money and cannot appropriate people, full uptake converts its generosity into an auction it never intended to run.
It ends having delivered the thing it promised, in every school, on time.

right in every single instance to refuse to rank them,
so the seat that will not choose who waits is the reason somebody does.
