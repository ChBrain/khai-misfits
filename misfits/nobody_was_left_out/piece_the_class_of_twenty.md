# Piece: The Class of Twenty

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). The benefit itself: real, evidenced, promised to every school and delivered to every school.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the room of twenty](place_the_room_of_twenty.md), in a wealthy district and a poor one alike.
Answered nowhere, because there is nothing here to answer: this part of the scheme worked.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is an account of a policy that failed, which is a different and much more ordinary story.
It is load bearing because it makes the misfit **survive the delivery being perfect**: the entitlement arrived in full, undiminished, everywhere, and the harm is not in what was delivered but in what delivering it everywhere at once required to be bought.

## Apparent

The scheme's success, and it is one.
It reads as the answer to any criticism, which is why the criticism has to be aimed at the staffing and not at the class, and why an evaluation of whether classes got smaller returns an unambiguous yes.

## Yearbook

Funded by [entitling](process_entitling.md), demanded everywhere at once.
Its universal delivery is what puts [the credential](piece_the_credential.md) into short supply and starts [clearing](process_clearing.md).
