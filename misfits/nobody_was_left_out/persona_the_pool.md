# Persona: The Pool

## Taxonomy

[Stock](position_stock.md). The qualified teachers who exist on the morning the money arrives: a number no statute can move.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not a shortage in the ordinary sense, and not anybody's failing.
The size of it is the output of programmes that ran years ago, admitted the people they admitted, and have already graduated them.
It grows, at its own rate, and the rate is not adjustable by appropriation.

## Action

Is however large it is.
Distributes itself, before the scheme, almost evenly: fewer than four per cent of the early-grade posts were held by somebody without a full credential, in even the state's hardest-served schools.
Responds to the new demand by moving, since moving is the only response available to it.

## Shadow

Nothing, and it is the binding constraint: money can raise what a teacher is paid and cannot raise how many teachers there are this year, so an offer made to everybody simultaneously reaches a quantity that is already fixed.
Because the constraint is a production time rather than a price, no amount of funding converts into supply inside the window the statute set.
It ends the same size it started, standing in different rooms.

## Tell

Says nothing, having no voice and no representative.
Its size is a fact reported afterwards, in a table nobody read before the vote.
