# Nobody Was Left Out: Reference

The research warrant for Nobody Was Left Out.
The term is the house's; the play runs on California's class size reduction programme and on what a simultaneous
universal entitlement does to an input in fixed supply, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the funded entitlement as **a claim that can only be met out of what already exists**: a legislature reads good
experimental evidence that smaller classes help, and help most the children who have least, funds the smaller class for
every district on the same terms in the same year, and every district takes it up. The thing a smaller class is made of
is a teacher, and a teacher takes years to make, so the money reaches a quantity that was fixed before it was
appropriated. The play models the documented mechanics, the universal form chosen because the alternative is a list of
the schools that wait, the stock that cannot grow inside the window, the ordering by which a shortage is actually
settled, the ordinary career move that carries the whole reallocation, the room that has to open in September with
somebody at the front of it, and the state average in which a redistribution cancels, as the load-bearing vectors of the
structure, not as a tale of a wasteful scheme or a school that would not try.

The governing law is that a funded claim raised everywhere at once cannot enlarge an input whose constraint is a
production time, so it can only reorder who holds it, and a reordering settles by who can attract rather than by who was
meant to be helped.

**Nobody in the sequence falls short, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The legislature acts
on good evidence and refuses to rank the children. The receiving district advertises openly and appoints on the merits.
The teacher takes a better post. The school that lost them hires quickly rather than open an empty room. The new teacher
begins.

**And the scheme is delivered in full, which the play establishes before anything else.** No money went missing, no
district refused, no promise was broken and no class stayed large. The rooms of twenty appeared in the wealthiest
districts and the poorest ones alike, and they are a real benefit resting on real evidence. Every later difficulty rests
on how completely this succeeded, which is what puts the misfit outside anybody's competence as well as outside their
conduct, since there is no failure of delivery here to point at.

**Then the fair construction is the mechanism, which is the hardest thing in the play.** Universality was not an
oversight. A targeted scheme requires a document naming the schools that go last, and the evidence gives no ground for
putting any particular child on it, so the honourable form is the one that opens to everybody at once. That form
concentrates an entire state's demand for one input into a single hiring season. **The generosity and the mechanism are
the same decision**, and no amount of care in the drafting separates them.

**And a small advantage takes everything ahead of it, which is the sharp part.** A shortage is not shared out in
proportion to anything: claimants are satisfied in order until the supply runs out. So a district that is slightly
better paid, slightly easier to reach and slightly less demanding does not receive a slightly larger share of the
qualified teachers, it receives first refusal on them, and the school behind it on each of those margins receives what
is left. **A nearly level field yields a steeply tilted result**, through thousands of separate appointments each of
which is open, correct and unremarkable, and none of which is visible as a transfer to the person making it.

**Then the record says the gradient was made and not revealed.** Before the programme, fewer than four per cent of
teachers in the funded grades were not fully credentialed in even the state's most disadvantaged schools. By the third
year, more than twenty-one per cent were not fully credentialed in the lowest-income schools, against four point three
per cent in the highest, which is roughly where everybody had stood at the start. The shortfall was not distributed. It
was delivered whole, to one end of the distribution, and to the end the reform was written for.

**And it does not appear in the number the scheme is reported against.** The harm here is a redistribution rather than a
reduction, and a redistribution nets to nothing in a mean, so the state-wide figure is honest, is the correct answer to
the question that was put, and contains no trace of the finding. Seeing it at all required somebody to split the data by
the income of the school.

It is distinct from the house's **The Driest Wells**, which is the nearest incumbent and stages the inverse care law,
where provision follows the slope of reward rather than the slope of need, more completely the more it is exposed to
market forces, and where universal provision is drawn on further by the advantaged for their longer reach. Remove that
engine entirely, by stipulating no standing inverse relation between need and provision at all, which is approximately
what the pre-programme figures describe, and this misfit stands whole: **the gradient here begins at zero.** That play
needs an existing slope for supply to run down; this one needs only that the claimants are not identical and the input
is fixed, since under rationing by order any difference at all, however small and however far from a standing care
gradient, allocates the entire shortfall. And the two differ on which quantity is unequal: there the advantaged draw
more of the service, here every school received exactly the same service and the inequality is in what the service had
to be built out of.

It is distinct from the house's **All at Once**, which stages the cache stampede, where a shared expiry synchronises
every request into one flood that overwhelms the backend the cache existed to spare. Remove that engine, by stipulating
the demand arrives smoothly and never synchronises, phasing the scheme across a decade instead of a season, and this
stands: **the rate is not what decides the incidence.** Phasing changes the size of the shortfall and not who bears it,
because the ordering that allocates it is unchanged, and the finding here is the incidence rather than the size. There
the claimants are interchangeable and the harm falls on a shared resource; here the claimants differ and the harm falls
on the weakest of them.

It is distinct from the house's **The Dead Sea Effect**, where the strongest people leave an organisation first and the
residue is selected downward. Remove that engine, by stipulating that nobody who leaves is any better than anybody who
stays and that mobility carries no relation to quality, and this stands entire, because **the loss here is a count
before it is a calibre**: the school is short of teachers rather than short of good ones, and would still be short if
every teacher in the state were identical. What that play needs is a differential in who moves; what this one needs is
that more posts were opened than there are people to fill them.

It is distinct from the house's **Proved on Somebody Else**, which stages the efficacy-effectiveness gap, where a result
established under trial conditions does not survive the move to ordinary practice. Remove that engine, by stipulating
the trial population and setting are identical to the field in every respect, and this stands: **the difference is not
between the trial and the field, it is between the small and the universal.** The original experiment was run in a
minority of classrooms and drew its teachers from a market it did not disturb, so what fails on scale-up is not the
representativeness of the finding but the elasticity of an input the finding assumed.

It is distinct from the house's **The Head Start**, which stages the Matthew effect, where an early advantage compounds
so that a lead widens by accumulation. Remove that engine, by stipulating no advantage compounds and every period begins
fresh, and this stands, because it is **one clearing event and not an accumulation**: the gradient appears within three
years out of a market that cleared once each hiring season, and nothing in it needs a previous round's outcome to feed
the next.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the legislature as
  profligate, the suburban district as a poacher or the school that hires on a permit as lowering a standard, because
  nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the statute acts on good randomised evidence and chooses the
  universal form because the targeted one requires listing the children who wait; the receiving district advertises
  openly, interviews properly and appoints the strongest applicant, offering nothing improper to anybody; the teacher who
  moves weighs pay and commute and decides as anybody would, in a move far too small to register as a transfer of
  anything; the school that loses them appoints quickly because the choice at that point is a new teacher or an empty
  room, and it makes it correctly; the new teacher takes a post nobody else applied for and begins, holding a permit that
  describes a date and never a person; and the evaluation reports the state-wide mean, which is the honest answer to the
  question that was asked. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a
  claim raised everywhere at once can only be met out of what already exists.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Phasing the scheme requires somebody to
  write down which children go last, which is the document the universal form was chosen to avoid. Targeting it at the
  poorest schools stops it being an entitlement, and the districts left out have a case that is not obviously worse.
  Raising pay to draw teachers in favours whichever districts can raise it furthest, which are the ones already at the
  front of the order. Enlarging the credential programmes is the only measure that actually relieves the constraint and
  it delivers after the cohort the scheme was written for has left the grades it covers. And the state average rose, so
  on the number the scheme is reported against there is nothing to answer for, and the gradient exists only for as long
  as somebody keeps splitting the data.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed as the
  house's. The staging is the house's; the randomised class size finding is Mosteller's account of Project STAR, the
  statute and its appropriations are the state's, the staffing figures are the evaluating consortium's, and the measured
  trade between class size and teacher qualification is Jepsen and Rivkin's. The bounds are carried with them:
  **whether class size reduction is a good use of public money is genuinely contested**, and the play takes the
  experimental premise as the reform's own rather than settling the argument; the figures are one state in one period and
  are offered as the documented shape of the reallocation rather than as constants, and a programme run elsewhere or at
  another pace need not produce the same distribution; certification and prior experience are indicators of a teacher's
  standing rather than measures of how well anybody teaches, and the play claims nothing about any teacher holding
  either; the account treats districts as comparable where they differ in size, funding base and labour market, which is
  its own simplification; and the mechanism is stated for an input whose constraint is a production time, which is not
  every input. **The play makes no claim about any school, any district, any teacher or any child, and gives no
  educational, employment or policy advice of any kind**, only that a benefit funded for everybody on the same morning is
  bought out of a stock that was finished years earlier, and that a shortage settles by order rather than by margin.
