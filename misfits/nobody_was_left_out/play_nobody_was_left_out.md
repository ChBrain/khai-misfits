# Play: Nobody Was Left Out

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Nobody Was Left Out.
The reform excluded nobody. That is the point of the title and it is also the mechanism. A funded entitlement was written for smaller classes and offered to every district in the state on the same terms in the same year, because that is what an entitlement means and because the alternative, phasing it by need, is a decision about which children wait. Every district could take it. Almost every district did. Every room got its smaller class.
And the thing a smaller class is made of is a teacher, and a teacher takes years to make. On the day the money arrived, the number of qualified teachers in the state was whatever it was. So the money did not buy teachers. It bid for them.

## Arc

The bend is this: an entitlement funded everywhere at once cannot enlarge an input that takes years to produce, so it can only reallocate the input that exists, and a reallocation settles by who can attract rather than by who was meant to be helped.
It runs on two facts, neither of them anybody's fault: a qualification takes years, and a school opens in September whether or not it is staffed.
Take the offer first. It is generous, it is evidenced, and it is fair. The experimental work behind smaller classes is real: pupils and teachers were randomly assigned, the small classes did better, and the gains were largest for the children who had least. A legislature reading that and funding it for everybody is doing the competent thing, and the universality is the honourable part, because a scheme that reached only some schools would have required somebody to name the schools it did not reach.
Then take the stock. Nothing in the statute makes a teacher. Every district is now short of the same person on the same morning, so the qualified teachers already in post become the thing being competed for, and they were not distributed evenly to begin with, only nearly so.
And **nearly so is enough**, which is the sharp part. When a fixed supply is short, it is rationed by order and not by margin. A district that is slightly easier to reach, slightly better paid, slightly less demanding does not take a slightly larger share: it takes its pick, and the school that is a little behind on every one of those takes what is left. So a very flat distribution of advantage produces a very steep distribution of outcome, and it does so with nobody discriminating and nobody being unfair.
The record is specific. Before the programme, fewer than four per cent of teachers in these grades were not fully credentialed in even the state's most disadvantaged schools. By the third year, more than twenty-one per cent were not fully credentialed in the lowest-income schools, against four point three per cent in the highest-income ones. The gradient was not there before. The reform did not reveal it. **The reform made it.**
Reach for a settlement and there is not one. Phase the scheme and somebody has to say which children go last. Target it at the poorest schools and it is no longer an entitlement, and the districts left out have a case. Raise pay to draw teachers in, and the districts that can raise it furthest are the ones already winning. Wait for the pipeline, which is the only thing that actually works, and the children in the first cohort are in secondary school by the time it delivers. And the state average went up, so on the number the scheme is reported against, it worked.

## Triggers

**[The Bill Passes](plot_the_bill_passes.md)**
An evidenced finding that smaller classes help most the children who have least, and a legislature that funds it for everybody. The offer goes to every district on the same terms in the same year. Closes when the universality is established as the fair construction rather than as an oversight, since the alternative requires naming the schools that wait.

**[Every Room in the State, by September](plot_every_room_in_the_state_by_september.md)**
Cue: the money arriving everywhere at once, against a count of qualified teachers that no statute can change. Every district is short of the same person on the same morning. Closes when the offer is shown to be a bid rather than a purchase, because nothing in it makes a teacher.

**[A Shorter Commute](plot_a_shorter_commute.md)**
Cue: a vacancy up the freeway, better paid and easier to reach, filled by somebody already teaching. Nothing improper anywhere: the district advertises, the teacher applies, the better offer wins. Closes when a movement that is unremarkable one person at a time is shown to be the allocation of the entire shortfall.

**[Somebody Has to Be at the Front](plot_somebody_has_to_be_at_the_front.md)**
Cue: a school that has lost two experienced teachers and must open with twenty to a room. It hires on an emergency permit, choosing that over a class with nobody in front of it. Closes when the choice is shown to be the right one available and the only one available.

**[Under Four, and Then Twenty-One](plot_under_four_and_then_twenty_one.md)**
Cue: the evaluation, three years in, disaggregated by the income of the school. Fewer than four per cent uncredentialed everywhere before; more than twenty-one against four point three after. Closes when the gradient is shown to have been created by the equal treatment rather than exposed by it, and to be invisible in the state average the scheme is reported against.

## Stakes

Whether a benefit can be given to everybody at once without taking it from somebody.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the reform was wise, because the evidence behind it is real and the universal form is the fair one, but whether a funded claim on an input that takes years to make can do anything other than reorder who holds it. The production cannot find a version that does. It can only show that the offer was open to all and taken by all, that the thing it had to be spent on was already made and could not be made faster, that a shortage rations by order rather than by margin so a nearly level field yields a steeply tilted result, that every person who moved was right to move and every school that hired was right to hire, and that the gradient which appeared in three years where none had stood before does not appear at all in the average the scheme is judged by.
