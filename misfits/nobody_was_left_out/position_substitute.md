# Position: Substitute

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). The seat that fills the room the credentialed applicant did not apply for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Willingness, ability, and a start date rather than a finished qualification.
A post that is open because somebody left and nobody senior applied.
A class of twenty children who are there on Monday either way.

## Orders

Apply for the post nobody else took.
Be appointed quickly, because the date is close.
Stand at the front, and learn the job while doing it.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat is where the cost lands with no fault attached to it: the children here have a smaller class, which the evidence says helps, and a teacher in their first year, which is what the auction left, and both are true simultaneously.
Because a permit describes a date and not a person, the seat is beyond criticism and is also not a substitute for the years it does not yet have.
It ends teaching well or badly as anybody might, and counted in a statistic about somebody else's scheme.

## Drives

Toward a classroom that has somebody in it,
right in every single instance to take the post,
so the seat that prevents the worst outcome is the one the evaluation measures the shortfall by.
