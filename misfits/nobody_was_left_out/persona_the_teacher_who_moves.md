# Persona: The Teacher Who Moves

## Taxonomy

[Mover](position_mover.md). An experienced teacher who takes a better post, which anybody would, and thereby moves the scarce thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Good at the job, and years into it.
Not leaving in frustration and not leaving anybody down: the post they are going to was advertised and they applied for it like anybody else.
Has a commute, a mortgage and a reasonable interest in both.

## Action

Sees a vacancy up the freeway that pays a little more and takes forty minutes less a day.
Applies. Is appointed, because they are experienced and the appointing district can afford to prefer experience.
Gives notice, works it, and is replaced.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is the one that carries the mechanism without being able to see it: a career decision that is entirely ordinary at the scale of one person is, at the scale of a state hiring at once, the redistribution of the input the scheme depends on.
Because no individual move is large enough to be visible as a transfer, there is no point at which anybody could reasonably be asked to decide differently.
It ends better off, teaching a class of twenty, having harmed nobody identifiable.

## Tell

Says: it was a better job.
It was. That is the whole of the account and there is nothing else in it.
