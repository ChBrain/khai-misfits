# Piece: The Rank

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). The ordering by which a shortage is settled: claimants satisfied in turn until the supply runs out.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the hiring fair](place_the_hiring_fair.md), implicit in every separate appointment.
Answered nowhere: nobody sets it, states it or could act on it if they saw it.

## Load Bearing

Without it a small advantage produces a small effect and there is no misfit.
It is load bearing because it is the reason **a nearly level field yields a steeply tilted result**: rationing by order means the size of an edge does not moderate the size of the outcome, so a district that is marginally easier in every respect does not take a marginally larger share, it takes first refusal on the whole of it.

## Apparent

Ordinary competitive hiring, which is what it is.
It reads as a fair market operating fairly, and it is one, which is why no participant can locate their own contribution to the result.
