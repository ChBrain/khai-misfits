# Persona: The School by the Yard

## Taxonomy

[Residual](position_residual.md). The employer the scheme was most for, which must staff the same rooms out of whatever the auction leaves.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Committed, experienced in its own difficulties, and the intended beneficiary of the whole reform.
Serves the children the evidence says gain most from a smaller class.
Wants exactly what the statute offers and takes it up in full.

## Action

Reduces its classes to twenty, as the scheme intends, which means it needs more teachers than it has.
Loses two of the experienced ones it already had, to posts advertised elsewhere.
Opens in September regardless, because the date is not negotiable and the children arrive either way.

## Shadow

The staff it started with, and then the difference between the applicants it wanted and the applicants it got: the seat is not outbid at a single moment but at every moment, on a margin small enough that nobody notices it and consistent enough that it decides everything.
Because the shortfall is allocated by order, the seat that is last in the order absorbs the whole of it rather than a share of it.
It ends with the smaller classes it was promised and a staffroom it would not have recognised three years earlier.

## Tell

Says: we got the funding, same as everyone.
Which is accurate, and is the reason.
