# Place: The Credential Programme

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). Where teachers are actually made, at a pace the statute does not reach and cannot accelerate.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Admissions, coursework, supervised practice and examinations, running over years.
Cohorts already enrolled, whose size was fixed before anybody drafted the scheme.

## Holds

The only genuine remedy, and its timetable.
It is the reason the shortage is not a pricing problem: a programme cannot be told to graduate its people sooner, so an appropriation made in one year meets a supply set by decisions taken in earlier ones.

## Offers

More teachers, eventually, and reliably, at the rate the programmes run.

## Withheld

Speed.
Nothing here responds to urgency, so the one intervention that would actually enlarge the stock delivers after the children the scheme was written for have left the grades it covers.
