# Place: The Hiring Fair

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). Where the fixed stock is allocated, and where every district in the state turns out to be looking for the same person on the same morning.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Advertisements, applications, interviews and offers, all of them proper.
Thousands of appointments, none of which is remarkable and no two of which are coordinated.

## Holds

The whole of the reallocation, and none of it visibly.
It is where the scheme stops being money and becomes an order of preference, and where the fact that a shortage is settled by rank rather than by margin does its work without anybody stating it.

## Offers

An open, fair and entirely legitimate market in posts: everything advertised, everything on the merits, nothing offered under the table anywhere.

## Withheld

The aggregate.
No party here sees more than its own vacancies and its own applicants, so the transfer that is happening across the state is not visible from any seat that could act on it.
