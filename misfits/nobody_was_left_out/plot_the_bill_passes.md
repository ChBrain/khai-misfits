# Plot: The Bill Passes

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An experimental finding that smaller classes help, and help most the children who have least.
A legislature with the money to act on it, and two ways of writing the scheme.

## Action

[Entitling](process_entitling.md): [The Statute](persona_the_statute.md), holding [the entitler's seat](position_entitler.md), chooses the universal form over the targeted one.

## Stage

[The Statute](persona_the_statute.md), on [Leave Nobody Out](plan_leave_nobody_out.md), offers money per class to every district on identical terms, names no school and sets a date.
The evidence backs it: pupils and teachers were randomly assigned in the original trial, the small classes did better, and the gains were largest for the children with least.

## Tension

The universality is the honourable choice, and that is what makes everything after it structural.
This is not a story about a careless scheme. A targeted version requires a document listing the schools that wait, and nothing in the evidence justifies putting any particular child at the end of that list. So the fair form is chosen, deliberately, for good reasons, and the fair form is the one that concentrates an entire state's demand for one input into a single hiring season. **The generosity and the mechanism are the same decision.**
