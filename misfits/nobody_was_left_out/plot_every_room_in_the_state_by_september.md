# Plot: Every Room in the State, by September

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The money arriving everywhere at once, against a count of qualified teachers that no statute reaches.
[The Credential Programme](place_the_credential_programme.md), running at the pace it has always run at.

## Action

[Clearing](process_clearing.md): [The Pool](persona_the_pool.md), in [the stock's seat](position_stock.md), meets a demand it cannot answer, while every district in the state advertises in the same week.

## Stage

[The Pool](persona_the_pool.md), on [Be However Many We Are](plan_be_however_many_we_are.md), is the size the previous decade's admissions made it and grows no faster for being wanted.
[The Statute](persona_the_statute.md) has funded a class and cannot appropriate a teacher, so what it has actually opened is an auction.

## Tension

The offer is a bid and not a purchase, and nothing in the drafting could have made it otherwise.
The constraint binds on production time rather than on price: a credential takes years, so the teachers who will be teaching this September are already teaching somewhere this June. Money can change what a teacher is paid and cannot change how many there are, which means a funded demand raised everywhere simultaneously has exactly one thing it can do, and that is move people. **The whole budget resolves into an order of preference.**
