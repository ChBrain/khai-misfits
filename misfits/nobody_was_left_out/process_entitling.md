# Process: Entitling

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). Creating a funded claim that everybody may exercise at the same time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Good evidence that the benefit is real and largest for those with least.
At [the credential programme](place_the_credential_programme.md)'s downstream end, a demand that programme did not plan for.

## Direction

Toward a benefit that excludes nobody.
This is the fair construction and the defensible one: a targeted scheme requires a list of the schools it does not reach, and the evidence gives no ground for putting any particular child at the end of it.
And the same construction concentrates the entire state's demand for one input into a single hiring season, which no drafting can avoid, because the alternative is the ranking that was refused.

## Lever

Money, which is what a legislature has.
Because the binding constraint is a production time rather than a price, the instrument available is not the instrument the constraint responds to.

## Echo

Full uptake, everywhere, and an auction nobody intended to open.
The scheme is delivered in every school and is simultaneously a simultaneous bid for a stock that cannot grow this year.
