# Plot: A Shorter Commute

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A vacancy up the freeway: a little better paid, forty minutes a day closer, advertised openly like every other.
[The Rank](piece_the_rank.md), operating without being stated.

## Action

[Drawing](process_drawing.md): [The Teacher Who Moves](persona_the_teacher_who_moves.md), in [the mover's seat](position_mover.md), applies and is appointed by [The District Up the Freeway](persona_the_district_up_the_freeway.md) from [the preferred seat](position_preferred.md).

## Stage

[The Teacher Who Moves](persona_the_teacher_who_moves.md), on [Take the Better Job](plan_take_the_better_job.md), weighs the pay and the commute and decides as anybody would.
[The District Up the Freeway](persona_the_district_up_the_freeway.md), on [Hire the Best Who Applies](plan_hire_the_best_who_applies.md), interviews properly and appoints the strongest applicant, having offered nothing improper to anybody.

## Tension

An advantage that is small still takes everything ahead of it, and that is the plot the whole play turns on.
A shortage is not shared out in proportion. It is satisfied in order, until it runs out. So the district that is slightly easier to reach, slightly better paid and slightly less demanding does not receive a slightly larger share of the scarce teachers: it receives first refusal on them, and the school behind it on every one of those margins receives what is left over. **A nearly level field produces a steeply tilted result**, and it does so through thousands of appointments each of which is correct, open, and unremarkable. The record is specific: experienced teachers left the schools serving the poorest children for suburban posts, and teachers moved out of the later grades into the funded early ones.
