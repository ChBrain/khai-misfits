# Process: Averaging

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). Reporting the scheme against the state-wide number, in which the incidence does not appear.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An evaluation, and a legislature that needs to know whether the money worked.
At the level the scheme was legislated on: the state.

## Direction

Toward the mean, which is the honest summary of a state-wide programme and is what was asked for.
Smaller classes did raise attainment, and the careful work finds the rise dampened by the increase in the share of teachers with neither prior experience nor full certification, and dampened most in the schools serving the poorest children.
So the average is not wrong and is not hiding anything on purpose: it is answering the question that was put, which was about the state.

## Lever

Aggregation, which is what makes a state-wide result readable at all.
Because the harm is a redistribution rather than a reduction, it cancels in the mean by construction, and no amount of care with the mean recovers it.

## Echo

A programme that reports as a modest success and contains a reversal at one end of it.
The gradient exists in the disaggregation and nowhere else, so seeing it at all required somebody to split the data by the income of the school.
