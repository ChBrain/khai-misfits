## Action

## Stage

[The School by the Yard](persona_the_school_by_the_yard.md), on [Have Somebody in Every Room by September](plan_have_somebody_in_every_room_by_september.md), makes the appointment quickly because the date is close.

## Tension

The choice here is not between an experienced teacher and a new one.
It is between a new one and an empty room, and every school in the position makes it the same way and makes it correctly. So no standard was lowered by anybody: this is the last move in a sequence whose earlier moves were all right as well, and it is made under a calendar rather than a budget, so there is no version of the seat that waits. And the children in this room do get the smaller class, which the scheme's own evidence says helps them. **Both halves are true at once**, and neither cancels the other.
