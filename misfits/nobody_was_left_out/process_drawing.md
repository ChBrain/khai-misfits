# Process: Drawing

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). Experienced teachers relocating toward the posts that are better on ordinary terms.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Vacancies advertised everywhere at once, differing in pay, commute and intake.
At [the hiring fair](place_the_hiring_fair.md), thousands of separate and unremarkable appointments.

## Direction

Toward the better post, which is where anybody would go.
Nothing here is a flight and nothing is a judgement about the school being left: the vacancy up the freeway pays a little more and costs forty minutes a day less, and that is a sufficient and honourable reason on its own.
And the movement is invisible as a transfer at the scale on which it is decided, since one teacher taking one job is not an allocation of anything.

## Lever

The difference in terms between two posts, which existed before the scheme and mattered less when nobody was hiring.
Because the scheme made everybody hire at once, a difference that had been latent became the thing that decided where the stock sat.

## Echo

The schools that were behind on those terms lose staff they had, and gain applicants they did not want to have to consider.
The record is specific: experienced teachers left the schools serving the poorest children for suburban posts, and teachers in the later grades moved into the funded early ones.
