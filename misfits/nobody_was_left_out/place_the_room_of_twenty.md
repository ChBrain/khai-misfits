# Place: The Room of Twenty

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). Where the benefit is delivered, in full, identically, everywhere: the part of the scheme that worked exactly as written.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Twenty children and one teacher, in a school in a wealthy district and in a school in a poor one alike.
Both rooms are what the statute promised and both are smaller than they were.

## Holds

The proof that the scheme was not a failure of delivery.
It holds the play's hardest fact: the entitlement arrived everywhere, undiminished, and the difference between the two rooms is not in the count of children but in the years standing at the front.

## Offers

A smaller class, to every child in the state, which is a real benefit supported by real evidence and was not taken away from anybody.

## Withheld

The staffing.
The room shows how many children are in it and shows nothing at all about how the person in front of them was recruited, so the dimension the scheme delivered on is visible in it and the dimension it spent is not.
