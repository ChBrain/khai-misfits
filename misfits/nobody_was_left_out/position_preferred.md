# Position: Preferred

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). The seat that holds a small, blameless advantage and therefore receives first call on a short supply.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A shorter commute, a slightly higher scale, a slightly easier intake.
Ordinary hiring practice and no rule broken anywhere.
More applicants than posts, which it did nothing in particular to arrange.

## Orders

Advertise. Interview. Appoint the strongest applicant.
Fill early, since strong applicants accept early.
Reduce classes to twenty, on the same funding as everybody else.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat is the play's quietest problem: it is not taking advantage, it is having one, and under a shortage that is the same thing in its effects and nothing like it in its conduct.
Because a shortage rations by order, the seat's small edge converts into first refusal on the whole of the scarce supply rather than into a proportionate share of it.
It ends fully staffed, having behaved impeccably throughout.

## Drives

Toward filling its own posts well,
never once bidding against anybody it can see,
so the seat that competes with nobody in particular outcompetes everybody in general.
