# Persona: The District Up the Freeway

## Taxonomy

[Preferred](position_preferred.md). The employer that is slightly easier in every respect, and therefore takes its pick of a short supply.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An ordinary district doing ordinary things.
Advertises its posts, interviews properly, hires the strongest applicant, and breaks no rule at any point.
Would be surprised, and reasonably so, to be told it had taken anything from anybody.

## Action

Posts its vacancies on the same day as everybody else.
Receives more applications than it has posts, because the commute is shorter, the pay a little better and the intake a little easier.
Fills them, early, with experienced people.

## Shadow

Nothing whatever, and that is the difficulty: this seat is not exploiting an advantage, it is merely having one, and under a shortage having one at all is sufficient.
Because the rationing is by order rather than by margin, a small and entirely blameless edge does not win a proportionate share, it wins first refusal on all of it.
It ends fully staffed, with classes of twenty, having done nothing but hire well.

## Tell

Says: we had forty applicants for six posts.
True, unremarkable, and the same sentence read from the other end of the county is a school that had none.
