# Piece: The Flat Before

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Left Out](play_nobody_was_left_out.md). The state of the staffing when the money arrived: fewer than four per cent without a full credential, in even the hardest-served schools.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the record, before the first funded year.
Answered nowhere, because nobody thought to treat it as the baseline it turned out to be.

## Load Bearing

Without it the outcome reads as an existing inequality being measured for the first time.
It is load bearing because it establishes that **the gradient was not there beforehand**: the schools serving the poorest children were staffed about as well as the rest on this dimension, so what follows is not an old pattern surfacing but a new one being produced, by a reform designed to help those schools most.

## Apparent

An unremarkable baseline figure in an evaluation appendix.
It reads as context rather than as evidence, which is why the finding is usually stated as a disparity rather than as a change.
