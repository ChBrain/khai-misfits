# Persona: The One Who Did What Others Do

## Taxonomy

[One Who Did What Others Do](position_one_who_did_what_others_do.md). The practitioner, not negligent and not wrong.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A competent professional who did the thing the way it is done, as they were taught, as their colleagues do, on a busy day among many others.
Not careless, not rushed, and not cutting anything.

## Action

Follows the practice accepted as proper by a responsible body of colleagues.
Is found not negligent, even though another body of opinion takes a contrary view.

## Shadow

Is genuinely blameless, which is what leaves the harm unowned.
There is no departure to identify here, no shortcut and no lapse: this persona did what any of their colleagues would have done and what their training required, so the injury cannot be traced to a choice anyone made badly, and the practice that caused it is the only candidate for blame and is not a person.

## Tell

Is exonerated correctly, and the exoneration is what makes the practice unreachable.
