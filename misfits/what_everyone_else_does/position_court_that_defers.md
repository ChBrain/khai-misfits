# Position: Court That Defers

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md). The seat whose modesty is competence rather than abdication.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A decision to make about professional care, no clinical training, and no independent standard of its own.
Experts of standing on both sides, disagreeing honestly.

## Orders

Ask what a responsible body would have done.
Satisfy yourself that the opinion withstands logical analysis.

## Loses

The ability to raise the standard, as the price of not lowering the quality of the decision.
The alternative is a judge settling contested clinical questions on their own reading, which is worse in every case anybody has proposed and would expose careful practitioners to judgement by hindsight: so the deference is right, its bounds are real, and they remain bounds on an opinion produced from inside the profession being judged.

## Drives

Toward refusing to write the standard, and thereby adopting whatever the standard already was.
