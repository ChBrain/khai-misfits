# Plot: A Responsible Body

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A practitioner who did the thing the way it is done, as taught, on a busy day, with nothing rushed and nothing cut.

## Action

[Doing it the usual way](process_doing_it_the_usual_way.md): [The One Who Did What Others Do](persona_the_one_who_did_what_others_do.md), holding [One Who Did What Others Do](position_one_who_did_what_others_do.md), acts in accordance with [the body of opinion](piece_the_body_of_opinion.md).

## Stage

Not negligent, and not made negligent by the existence of a body of opinion that takes a contrary view.

## Tension

The exoneration is correct, and the correctness is what puts the practice out of reach.
There is no departure here to identify, no shortcut and no lapse: this practitioner did what any of their colleagues would have done and what their training required. So the harm cannot be traced to a choice anybody made badly, and the only remaining candidate for blame is the practice itself, which is not a person, cannot be sued, and was never on trial.
