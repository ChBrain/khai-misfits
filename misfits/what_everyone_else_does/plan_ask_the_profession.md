# Plan: Ask the Profession

## Taxonomy

A **judicial method**: the intent of [The Court That Defers](persona_the_court_that_defers.md), inside [What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The Court That Defers](persona_the_court_that_defers.md)

## Direction

Decide the claim without pretending to a competence you do not hold. Establish what a responsible body of practitioners would have done, and hold the practitioner to that rather than to the outcome.

## Orders

(Hear both experts at [the expert table](place_the_expert_table.md); establish [the body of opinion](piece_the_body_of_opinion.md); satisfy yourself it withstands logical analysis; apply it through [standing back](process_standing_back.md).)

## Implementation

([The Expert Table](place_the_expert_table.md), where every witness qualified to answer is a member of the profession being judged; and a tribunal with no independent standard of its own.)

## Targets

- [ ] the practitioner is protected from judgement by hindsight and by outcome
- [ ] contested clinical questions are not decided on a judge's own reading, which would be worse
- [ ] the opinion relied on is responsible, and capable of withstanding logical analysis
- [ ] the standard is adopted rather than written, from inside the profession under examination
