# Plot: The Court Steps Back

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A claim that turns on whether a professional decision was careless, and a judge with no clinical training and no independent standard to apply.

## Action

[Standing back](process_standing_back.md): [The Court That Defers](persona_the_court_that_defers.md), holding [Court That Defers](position_court_that_defers.md), takes the question to [the expert table](place_the_expert_table.md) and asks what a responsible body of practitioners would have done.

## Stage

The deference is bounded: the opinion must be responsible, and must be capable of withstanding logical analysis.

## Tension

The modesty is genuine competence rather than abdication, which is why it cannot simply be withdrawn.
The alternative is a judge settling contested clinical questions on their own reading, which is worse in every case anybody has proposed, and it would expose careful practitioners to judgement by outcome and by hindsight. So the step back is right. And it means the standard will be adopted rather than written, from inside the profession being judged, by the only witnesses qualified to speak to it.
