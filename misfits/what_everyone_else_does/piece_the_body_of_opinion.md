# Piece: The Body of Opinion

## Taxonomy

[The Expert Table](place_the_expert_table.md). The test itself: what a responsible body of practitioners accepts.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the expert table](place_the_expert_table.md), where the question is whether a responsible body of colleagues would have accepted this as proper.

## Load Bearing

It converts a description of practice into a legal duty.
A practitioner acting in accordance with a practice accepted as proper by a responsible body is not negligent, and is not made negligent by the existence of a contrary body of opinion: so the duty is set by what some competent colleagues do, which means it can only ever be as high as some competent colleagues have already reached.

## Apparent

A sensible, humble test, refined over decades and applied by careful judges to honest evidence.
The alternative to judges writing clinical guidance, which nobody wants.

## Yearbook

Qualified in 1997 so that the opinion relied on must be capable of withstanding logical analysis, which is a genuine bound and requires a judge to overrule expert evidence to use it.
Still measured, after the qualification, against opinion produced inside the profession under examination.
