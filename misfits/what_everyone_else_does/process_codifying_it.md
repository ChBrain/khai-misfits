# Process: Codifying It

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md). Best practice written down, and thereby fixed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A body assembling the evidence and publishing [the guideline](piece_the_guideline.md).
A profession that wants its standard explicit rather than carried in habit.

## Direction

Toward an immediate and genuine raising of the floor, which is what codification achieves.
Toward the document becoming what a responsible body follows, errors and omissions included.

## Lever

Writing it down, which makes the standard both visible and static.

## Echo

Departure from the guideline becoming the act that must be justified, so the practitioner who has read newer evidence is the one now carrying the risk.
