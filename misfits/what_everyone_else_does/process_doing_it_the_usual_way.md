# Process: Doing It the Usual Way

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md). Competent practice, performed as taught.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A patient, a list, and a method every practitioner in the department can perform reliably under pressure.
[The One Who Did What Others Do](persona_the_one_who_did_what_others_do.md), in [the treatment room](place_the_treatment_room.md).

## Direction

Toward the practice as it is taught, supervised and corrected, which is what training exists to produce.
Toward an outcome that is occasionally a harm, with nothing done differently from any other day.

## Lever

Consistency, which is a genuine virtue and is what makes the harm untraceable to a decision.

## Echo

An injury with a cause and no departure, in a room organised entirely around performing the method well and not at all around reviewing it.
