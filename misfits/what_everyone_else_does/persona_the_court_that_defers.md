# Persona: The Court That Defers

## Taxonomy

[Court That Defers](position_court_that_defers.md). The tribunal, modest for good reason.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A judge who must decide whether a professional was careless, holding no clinical training and no independent standard to apply.
Experts of standing on both sides, disagreeing.

## Action

Asks what a responsible body of practitioners would have done, and applies the answer.
Requires, since 1997, that the opinion relied on be capable of withstanding logical analysis.

## Shadow

Is right to step back, which is why the deference cannot simply be withdrawn.
The alternative is a judge deciding contested clinical questions on their own reading, which is worse in every case anybody has proposed, and it would expose careful practitioners to judgement by outcome and hindsight: so the modesty is genuine competence rather than abdication, and its bounds, real as they are, still bound an opinion produced inside the profession being judged.

## Tell

Refuses to write the standard, and thereby adopts whatever the standard already was.
