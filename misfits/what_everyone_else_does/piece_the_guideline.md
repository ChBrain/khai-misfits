# Piece: The Guideline

## Taxonomy

[The Expert Table](place_the_expert_table.md). The written best practice, which becomes the new custom.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Cited at [the expert table](place_the_expert_table.md), and pinned to the wall of [the treatment room](place_the_treatment_room.md).

## Load Bearing

It fixes the standard in writing, and fixing it is what freezes it.
Codifying best practice raises the floor immediately and genuinely, and then the document becomes the thing a responsible body follows, errors and omissions included: so departure from it becomes the act that must be justified, and the practitioner who has read newer evidence is now the one taking the risk.

## Apparent

Evidence-based, peer-reviewed, periodically updated, and a real improvement on unwritten habit.
The most effective single answer anybody has to this misfit.

## Yearbook

Written to make good practice explicit, and used afterwards as the definition of what a responsible body does.
Updated on a cycle measured in years, during which it is the standard regardless of what has been published since.
