# Place: The Treatment Room

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md). Where the practice is done, correctly, by everybody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Equipment, protocols on the wall, a checklist, a busy list and colleagues who all work the same way.
Training, supervision, and a registrar being corrected toward the usual method.

## Holds

[The Custom](persona_the_custom.md), carried in how things are taught rather than written anywhere as a rule.
[The New Device](piece_the_new_device.md), in the catalogue and not in the room.

## Offers

Competence, consistency, and a method every practitioner here can perform reliably under pressure.
The reassurance that whatever happens, it was done properly.

## Withheld

Any occasion to ask whether the method is the best one.
The room is organised entirely around performing the practice well, which it does, and holds no procedure for reviewing the practice itself: so improvement has to arrive from outside it, and nothing inside is designed to notice its absence.
