# Position: One Who Was Harmed

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md). The seat with everything proved except a person.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real injury, a provable cause, and competent representation.
A case that would succeed against any individual departure.

## Orders

Bring the claim.
Hear that this is how it is done.

## Loses

On the one element the practice can supply and the facts cannot.
The harm is real and the causation is established, and the defence is not a denial but a description: no departure, no shortcut, nobody who chose wrongly and no moment at which anything went other than as intended, so the claim fails for want of somebody to attach the facts to rather than for want of facts.

## Drives

Toward proving everything except that anybody should have done otherwise.
