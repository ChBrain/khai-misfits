# Persona: The One Who Was Harmed

## Taxonomy

[One Who Was Harmed](position_one_who_was_harmed.md). The injured party, defeated by a practice rather than a person.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Somebody with a real injury, a provable cause and a competent lawyer.
Holding a case that would succeed against any individual departure and fails against a universal one.

## Action

Brings the claim, and hears the answer: this is how it is done.
Loses, on evidence that is accurate.

## Shadow

Has nobody to sue, because the thing that injured them is not a person.
The harm is real and the causation is established, and the defence is not a denial but a description: there was no departure to point at, no shortcut taken, nobody who chose wrongly and no moment at which anything went other than as intended, so the claim fails not on the facts but on the absence of anyone to attach them to.

## Tell

Proves everything except that somebody should have done otherwise, which is the only element the practice can supply.
