# Position: One Who Went First

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Has

## Orders

## Loses

Every legal benefit of the improvement it paid for.
Protection under this test comes from what others do rather than from what you do, so being ahead confers none, while the colleague who declined is fully covered for as long as enough others also decline: and the only mechanism by which the method ever becomes compulsory is the accumulation of exactly these unrewarded adoptions.
