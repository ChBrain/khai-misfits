# Place: The Expert Table

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md). Where the standard is established, by the people who are the standard.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two experts of real standing, reports exchanged, a joint statement of agreement and disagreement, and honest testimony from both.
A judge listening carefully to a question they cannot answer themselves.

## Holds

[The Body of Opinion](piece_the_body_of_opinion.md), on which the whole test turns.
The question of whether that opinion withstands logical analysis, which is the only lever the room contains.

## Offers

The best available account of what competent practitioners do, given under oath and tested in cross-examination.
A fair, careful and reviewable route to an answer.

## Withheld

Any witness who is not a member of the profession being judged.
The question of whether the practice as a whole is good enough can only be put to people whose practice it is, so the table can establish beyond argument what is done and has no independent instrument for what ought to be: every voice in it is honest, and every voice in it comes from inside.
