# Plot: The Cost of Going First

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The New Device](piece_the_new_device.md): obtainable, affordable, demonstrably better, and used by almost nobody.

## Action

[Going first](process_going_first.md): [The One Who Went First](persona_the_one_who_went_first.md), holding [One Who Went First](position_one_who_went_first.md), buys the equipment, does the training and absorbs a slower first year.

## Stage

The adopter receives no legal protection whatever for having done so. The colleague who declined remains fully covered while enough others decline.

## Tension

The incentive runs backwards, and the only route out of the misfit is built entirely from unrewarded acts.
Protection comes from what others do rather than from what you do, so being ahead confers none: the device becomes compulsory at the moment it stops being an improvement and starts being the usual thing, which means it protects everybody who adopts it afterwards and nobody who adopted it before. The people who make the standard rise are the only people it never covers.
