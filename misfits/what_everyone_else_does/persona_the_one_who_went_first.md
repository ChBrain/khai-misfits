# Persona: The One Who Went First

## Taxonomy

[One Who Went First](position_one_who_went_first.md). The early adopter, paying for a protection that covers other people.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A practitioner or a department that has read the evidence and adopted the safer method before it is usual.
Has bought the equipment, done the training, and absorbed the slower first year.

## Action

Adopts, ahead of the profession, at their own cost.
Receives no legal advantage whatever for having done so.

## Shadow

Buys the thing that will eventually protect everybody except themselves.
Protection under this test comes from what others do rather than from what you do, so adoption confers none, while the colleague who declines is fully covered for as long as enough others also decline: and the only route by which the method ever becomes compulsory is the accumulation of exactly the unrewarded adoptions this persona is making.

## Tell

Is doing the thing that will one day be the standard, and is the only person who gains nothing from it.
