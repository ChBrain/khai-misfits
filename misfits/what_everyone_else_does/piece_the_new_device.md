# Piece: The New Device

## Taxonomy

[The Treatment Room](place_the_treatment_room.md). The better method, available and not required.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the catalogue, in the literature, and in a few departments: everywhere except [the treatment room](place_the_treatment_room.md) where the harm occurs.

## Load Bearing

Its availability is not enough, and its adoption is what makes it compulsory.
Being obtainable, affordable and demonstrably better places no obligation on anybody, because the duty is set by what is done rather than by what could be done: so the device sits in a state of being clearly worth adopting and legally optional, and it leaves that state only when enough practitioners have already adopted it for no legal reason at all.

## Apparent

A radio on a tug in 1932, or its equivalent in any decade since: obtainable, affordable, and plainly useful.
Bought by some, not by most.

## Yearbook

Becomes mandatory at the moment it stops being an improvement and starts being the usual thing.
Confers protection on everybody who adopts it after that point and on nobody who adopted it before.
