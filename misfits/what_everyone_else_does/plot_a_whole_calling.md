# Plot: A Whole Calling

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

1932. Two tugs lose their barges in a storm. Radios existed, worked, and were not generally carried.

## Action

A court declines to treat [the custom](persona_the_custom.md) as the answer, holding that a whole calling may have unduly lagged in the adoption of new and available devices, and that courts must in the end say what is required.

## Stage

There are precautions so imperative that even their universal disregard will not excuse their omission. The tugs were held liable for not carrying what nobody carried.

## Tension

The crack was named ninety years ago and has not become the working rule.
The principle is quotable, correct and available to any judge who wants it, and in most of the places this misfit actually operates the standard is still what a responsible body of practitioners accepts. Saying what is required means a tribunal overruling the only witnesses qualified to speak to the question, on its own assessment, which is the very step [The Court That Defers](persona_the_court_that_defers.md) declined for reasons that were good then and are good now.
