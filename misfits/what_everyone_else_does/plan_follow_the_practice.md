# Plan: Follow the Practice

## Taxonomy

A **professional norm**: the intent of [The One Who Did What Others Do](persona_the_one_who_did_what_others_do.md), inside [What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Did What Others Do](persona_the_one_who_did_what_others_do.md)

## Direction

Do the thing the way it is done. Follow the method you were taught, that your colleagues use and that your department is set up to perform reliably, because consistency under pressure is what keeps patients safe.

## Orders

(Work to the practice in [the treatment room](place_the_treatment_room.md); follow [the guideline](piece_the_guideline.md) where one exists; do not improvise on a busy list; be judged against colleagues rather than against outcomes.)

## Implementation

([The Treatment Room](place_the_treatment_room.md); the [doing it the usual way](process_doing_it_the_usual_way.md) process; training, supervision and a method every practitioner here can perform.)

## Targets

- [ ] the method is performed competently and consistently, which it is
- [ ] no shortcut is taken and no lapse occurs
- [ ] the practice followed is one accepted as proper by a responsible body of colleagues
- [ ] nothing in the plan asks whether the practice itself is the best available, because no part of the day provides for it
