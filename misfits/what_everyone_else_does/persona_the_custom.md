# Persona: The Custom

## Taxonomy

[Custom](position_custom.md). What is done, standing in for what ought to be.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The ordinary way a thing is done in a profession, held in training, in supervision, in the way a registrar is corrected on a ward round.
Nobody's decision, and everybody's.

## Action

Sets what a responsible body of practitioners would have done.
Is read by the law and returned as the duty of care.

## Shadow

Seals itself at both ends, and has no aperture for criticism.
A method nobody uses is not required, because no responsible body uses it, and a method everybody uses cannot be negligent, because a responsible body does: so the mechanism reads the average and hands it back as the standard, and there is no input anywhere in it through which the average could be informed that it is too low.

## Tell

Is the thing being measured and the ruler at the same time.
