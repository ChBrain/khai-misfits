# Plot: When the Guideline Becomes the Custom

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The answers on the table: reject an opinion that will not withstand logical analysis, or write the practice down so the standard is explicit rather than habitual.

## Action

[Codifying it](process_codifying_it.md): [the guideline](piece_the_guideline.md) is published, evidence-based and peer-reviewed, and pinned to the wall of [the treatment room](place_the_treatment_room.md).

## Stage

The floor rises immediately and genuinely. This is the most effective single answer anybody has.

## Tension

Writing the standard down is what freezes it, and the qualification requires a judge to overrule expert evidence to use it.
The document becomes the thing a responsible body follows, errors and omissions included, so departure becomes the act that must be justified and the practitioner who has read newer evidence is now the one carrying the risk. Updates run on a cycle measured in years, during which the guideline is the standard regardless of what has been published since. The correction raises the floor and installs a new ceiling in the same motion.
