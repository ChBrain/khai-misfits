# Pitch: Nobody Fell Below the Standard

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Judicious and modest. A court declining to write clinical guidance from the bench, experts of standing on both sides, a practitioner who did exactly what any competent colleague would have done, a test refined and qualified over decades.
Everything careful, everything reasoned, everything correct.

## Undertow

Under the reasoning, a legal floor set by the profession's own habit, and no input anywhere through which the habit could be told it is too low.
A safer method available for years, obligatory only once enough people have already adopted it for nothing.

## Nerve

The vertigo of an injury with a cause, a mechanism and a remedy, and nobody who did anything wrong.

## Echo

Afterward: the adopter who paid for the improvement and gained no protection, the colleague who declined and was covered, and a ninety-year-old judgment saying that a whole calling may have unduly lagged, still quoted and still not the working rule.
