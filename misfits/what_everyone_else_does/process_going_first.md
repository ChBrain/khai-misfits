# Process: Going First

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md). Adoption ahead of the profession, unrewarded.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

## Direction

Toward equipment bought, training done and a slower first year absorbed.
Toward no legal protection whatever, since protection comes from what others do.

## Lever

An incentive that runs backwards: declining is covered while enough others decline, and adopting is covered by nothing.

## Echo

The only route by which the method ever becomes compulsory, built entirely out of adoptions nobody is rewarded for making.
