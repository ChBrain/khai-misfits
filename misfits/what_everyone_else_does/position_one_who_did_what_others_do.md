# Position: One Who Did What Others Do

## Taxonomy

[What Everyone Else Does](play_what_everyone_else_does.md). The seat that is blameless, and whose blamelessness closes the case.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Training, competence, a busy list, and the practice as it is taught and supervised.
A practice accepted as proper by a responsible body of colleagues.

## Orders

Do it the way it is done.
Be judged against colleagues rather than against outcomes.

## Loses

Nothing, and that is precisely the difficulty.
There is no departure here to identify, no shortcut and no lapse, and another body of opinion taking a contrary view does not make the seat negligent: so the injury cannot be traced to a choice anybody made badly, and the only remaining candidate for blame is the practice itself, which is not a person and cannot be sued.

## Drives

Toward a correct exoneration, which is the thing that puts the practice beyond reach.
