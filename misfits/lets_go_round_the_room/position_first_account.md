# Position: First Account

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). The seat that speaks first, occupied by whoever begins.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The room's attention and an empty record.
No advantage of rank, volume or confidence, and none needed.
The property that everything said afterwards is heard against what it said.

## Orders

Begin, because somebody has to.
Say what you remember, in your own order, as fully as you can.
Do not wait for a better rememberer, because the meeting cannot start until somebody starts it.

## Loses

Nothing, and it is not taking anything either.
**The first speaker's effect is a property of order and not of character**, so replacing the occupant changes
who has the effect and not whether anybody does, and the seat cannot be emptied without the meeting failing
to begin.

## Drives

Toward an order the room will follow and details the room will have available,
by somebody doing the one thing the meeting cannot proceed without,
so the influence is the sequence's and not the speaker's.
