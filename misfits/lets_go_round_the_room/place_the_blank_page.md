# Place: The Blank Page

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). The account each person did not write before anybody spoke.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Nothing, because it was never used.
It costs ten minutes and a pen, and it is available at every debrief this play stages.
It is the one arrangement in which both mechanisms are absent.

## Holds

The way out, and the reason nobody takes it.
**Write first and pool afterwards and there is no other order cutting across yours and no detail available
before yours is fixed**, so the pooled account is longer and its agreements are real. And it reads as
distrust of people who came to help, it contradicts the whole intuition that talking it through together is
the point, and it makes a meeting begin in silence.

## Offers

Everything the room offers, longer, with the agreement meaning what the reader will take it to mean.

## Withheld

Itself, at almost every meeting, for reasons that are about the room and not about the memory.
