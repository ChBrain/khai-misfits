# Pitch: More Heads, More of It

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Conscientious, and right to be.
Exact about what was said, what was heard, and which of the two anybody can tell apart afterwards.
Unwilling to call the first speaker domineering or the one who adopts a detail suggestible, because neither is required.

## Undertow

Under a well-run debrief, one fact about memory and one about meetings.
A memory is reached by a path, and somebody else's path cuts across yours.
And hearing each other is not a step in pooling: it is what pooling is.

## Nerve

The unease of a document everybody signed.
Nobody lied. Nobody was bullied. Nobody failed to contribute.
The shorter half is the half nobody reached, and no line in the account marks where it would have gone.

## Echo

After the run: a room that did the right thing,
an order of recall that was somebody else's,
a detail arriving with no seam in it,
and an agreement made here, read later as several people agreeing.
