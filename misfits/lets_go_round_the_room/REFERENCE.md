# Let's Go Round the Room: Reference

The research warrant for [Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md).
The title is the house's; the play runs on the collaborative-memory literature, on the retrieval-disruption
account of why groups recall less than their members do apart, and on the social-contagion work on how one
person's detail becomes another's memory, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the group debrief as **a pooling that shortens what it pools**: several people who were there sit
down together to work out what happened, which is what a debrief is for and what any careful team would do.
The play models the documented mechanics, the retrieval path each person's memory is reached by, the other
person's order cutting across it, the shortfall against the same people recalling apart, the account exposed
to the others before it is fixed, the detail taken up and later reported as one's own, the shorter and more
uniform document that leaves the room, and the reader who takes its agreement for corroboration, as the
load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a domineering colleague or a suggestible one.

The governing law is that **the same act that pools the accounts is the act that shortens and aligns them**,
so a group assembled to recall an event more fully recalls it less fully and more uniformly, and the
uniformity is then read as the corroboration that the meeting destroyed.

What makes it structural is that **hearing is what a meeting is**. There is no version of a joint recall in
which the members pool what they hold and do not hear each other, because hearing each other is the pooling.
The convener is not choosing between a good meeting and a bad one. They are calling the meeting, correctly,
and the disruption and the pooling are the one act read two ways.

What makes it costly is that **nobody in the room does anything wrong at any point**. The one who speaks
first has the largest effect because everything afterwards is heard against what they said, which is a
property of order and not of character. The one who adopts a detail is not credulous: a memory adopted is a
memory from inside, with no felt seam between the thing seen and the thing heard and now recalled. And the
reader who takes several agreeing accounts for corroboration is doing what agreement is for.

It is distinct from the house's **Too Sure to Be True**, which stages agreement past a threshold as evidence
of a common error. There the observations were genuinely independent and the excess agreement is the anomaly
to be explained, so the remedy is to notice that too much agreement is itself information. **Here the
observations are made dependent by the act of pooling**, so the agreement is not an anomaly at all: it is the
meeting's own product, arriving at exactly the level a meeting produces, with nothing statistically strange
about it and nothing for a suspicious reader to catch.

It is distinct from the house's **The Trip to Abilene**, and the cut is what is being read off the room.
There a record of what is **voiced** is misread as a record of what is **held**, and every party knows their
own mind and withholds it. Here nobody withholds anything and there is no private position being concealed:
a record of what is recalled **together** is misread as a record of what was seen **apart**, and the parties
have no truer account they are keeping back, because the truer account was never formed.

It joins the house's **Information Cascade** on the axis `observed-predecessors`, at the same sign, and the
quantity is the one both turn on: **how much of a party's own information reaches the record, as the number
of others' contributions they have taken in first rises**. Set the number of prior accounts to zero, so that
everybody reports at once and privately, and both plays are gone: the cascade has no predecessors to copy
and this room has no other order to be cut across and no detail available to adopt. An axis shares a dial and
not a mechanism, and these two mechanisms are as far apart as an inference and a retrieval.

**Which is exactly the cut.** In the cascade the chooser's own information is intact and is **set aside**, by
sound reasoning, because the weight of the visible prior choices fairly outweighs one contrary hint, so the
record of actions is complete and what it fails to carry is the hint behind each action. Here nothing is set
aside and there is no intact private account being withheld: the parts each person's own order would have
reached are **missing from the record** because the retrieval did not happen, and no party in the room could
name what is absent or knows that anything is.

And it is distinct from the house's **The Illusion of Shared Understanding**, which turns on a speaker
believing they were understood. **Nothing here depends on anybody misjudging what got across**: the
communication succeeds completely, every account is heard exactly as given, and the harm is produced by the
hearing landing rather than by its failing to.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the convener as
  careless, the first speaker as domineering, or the one who adopts a detail as suggestible. The meeting is
  the right thing to call and a team that did not call it would be answering for that instead. **The first
  speaker's effect is a property of order and not of character**, and everything said after them is heard
  against what they said whoever they are. A memory adopted is a memory from inside, with no felt seam in
  it. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that **the same act
  that pools the accounts is the act that shortens and aligns them**.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a better-chaired meeting. Taking separate written
  accounts first works and is almost never done, because a meeting is what a group does and the writing
  reads as distrust of people who came to help. Having each person cover a different part works, and
  requires knowing in advance who saw what, which is the thing the meeting was called to find out. Recording
  who said each detail first works, and asks a room to minute its own recall as evidence rather than as
  content. And warning the room about the effect works on nobody, since neither arm runs on anybody's belief
  about it.
- **Held against its own limit**: the collaborative-inhibition arm is **measured on word lists and staged
  scenes** rather than on the open-ended events a real debrief covers, and it **weakens or vanishes** where
  members retrieve non-overlapping parts or are made to organise their recall the same way. Collaboration
  also **prunes some errors**, so the room is not simply worse than the same people apart. The play stages a
  **shorter and more uniform** account rather than a less accurate one, and locates the harm in how that
  uniformity is later read.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the collaborative-memory findings are theirs, and the
  bounds are carried with them. **No magnitude is carried**: the play states that the group produces less
  than the same people apart and that reports converge, and it states no proportion, no effect size and no
  count, because the pass that wrote it could not read the papers. **The works themselves could not be read
  in the pass that cited them**, since the publisher hosts and the article archives are blocked to it, so the
  bibliography, the findings and their directions were verified by search and corroborated across independent
  reports, which is the treatment a citation survives and a reported figure is not. **And no claim is made
  about anybody's confidence.** The play's harm runs through the reader of the accounts and not through the
  felt certainty of the rememberers, which is a claim the pass could not source and did not need.
