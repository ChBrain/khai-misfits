# Place: The Later Reading

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). Wherever the account is used afterwards, by somebody who was not in the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

One version of events with several names on it.
No dissent recorded, correctly, because there was none.
A reader applying the ordinary rule that agreement among accounts is evidence.

## Holds

The misread, and it needs no error to produce it.
**Agreement observed in the world and agreement produced in a room look identical on paper**, and the
document records no order of speaking, no adoption and no comparison with what was never reached, so the
distinction the reading turns on was never written down and could not have been.

## Offers

A confident basis for whatever is decided next.

## Withheld

The one fact that would change the weight: where the agreement came from.
