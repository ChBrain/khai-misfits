# Process: Adopting

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). Taking up a detail somebody else reported and later recalling it as your own.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A detail offered before your account is fixed.
At [the room](place_the_room.md), from the first account onward, by everybody who heard it.

## Direction

Toward converged reports.
Nobody is credulous and nobody is lying: **a memory adopted is a memory, from inside**, with no felt seam
between the thing seen and the thing heard and now recalled, which is why no amount of sincerity separates
them.

## Lever

Sequence, since the earlier an account is given the more of the room's later accounts are heard against it.

## Echo

A detail arriving with no source attached,
two people whose reports now match,
and neither of them able to say which of them had it first.
