# Process: Hearing

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). Attending to somebody else's account of the same event.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The first person beginning, and everybody else listening.
At [the room](place_the_room.md), continuously, by people doing exactly what they came to do.

## Direction

Toward the room's shared version.
It is the only thing anybody in the room is doing wrong, and it is not wrong: listening is why they came,
and a person who did not listen would be the one at fault.

## Lever

Attention, which cannot be given to two orders at once.

## Echo

An account received in full,
an order that is not yours,
and a detail now available to everybody who heard it.
