# Piece: My Own Thread

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). The idiosyncratic route a person reaches their own memory by, and the thing the room cuts across.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Carried into [the room](place_the_room.md) by every person in it, invisibly.
Interrupted by [hearing](process_hearing.md) and lost through [disrupting](process_disrupting.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the shortfall has to be explained by effort, and the play becomes about loafing.
It is load bearing because a memory is **reached by a path rather than read off a list**: the organisation is
part of how the thing is got to at all, so an order that is not yours does not merely slow the retrieval, it
reaches different material.

## Apparent

That a person could simply hold their thread and listen at the same time.
Attention is the one resource that cannot be given to two orders at once, and the person doing it is
listening, which is what they came for. The instruction to do both is the one the seat cannot carry out.

## Yearbook

The retrieval-disruption account of collaborative inhibition, which is the standard explanation and the one
tested against effort and motivation.
