# Piece: The Shorter Account

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). What leaves the room: less of the event than walked in, and more uniform.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Written at [the room](place_the_room.md) through [Write the Agreed Version](plan_write_the_agreed_version.md).
Read at [the later reading](place_the_later_reading.md), where it is the only version of the event that exists.

## Load Bearing

Without it the two mechanisms produce nothing that travels.
It is load bearing because **a faithful record of a shortened recall is a shortened record, faithfully
made**: nothing said is left out, nothing unsaid is added, and there is no line the writer could add marking
the unreached parts, because nobody in the room knows there were any.

## Apparent

That somebody would notice it was short.
Short against what? The comparison that would show it is the same people recalling at separate desks and
being pooled afterwards, which is exactly the arrangement the meeting replaced, so the measure is the one
thing the room does not have.

## Yearbook

The nominal group: the same number of people, recalling separately, pooled after, which is the comparison the
literature makes and no real meeting ever does.
