# Plot: A Sensible Meeting

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Something happened, several people saw it, and none of them holds the whole of it.

## Action

[Convening](process_convening.md): [The Sensible Reason to Meet](persona_the_sensible_reason_to_meet.md), in [the room seat](position_room.md), works [Get It Straight Together](plan_get_it_straight_together.md) and holds [the reason to meet](piece_the_reason_to_meet.md).

## Stage

[The Room](place_the_room.md): everybody who was present, an hour somebody thought to set aside, and goodwill.

## Tension

This is the plot that has to establish there is no fault here, or nothing after it reads correctly.
[The reason to meet](piece_the_reason_to_meet.md) is true: each person holds a piece and the pieces belong
together, and a team that did not call this meeting would be answering for that instead.
And the harder half is that **hearing each other is not a step in pooling: it is what pooling is**. There is
no arrangement of this room in which the members contribute what they hold and do not hear what the others
hold, so the convener is not choosing between a good meeting and a bad one. They are calling the meeting,
correctly, and **the disruption and the pooling are the one act read two ways**.
