# Position: Record

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). The seat that writes down what the meeting produced, occupied by nobody in particular.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Everything said in the room and nothing that was not.
An obligation to be faithful to the meeting, which it discharges completely.
No way to record what nobody reached.

## Orders

Write what the room agreed.
Mark the disagreements, which is what a careful record does.
Do not editorialise, and do not add what was not said.

## Loses

Nothing it was asked to hold.
**A faithful record of a shortened recall is a shortened record, faithfully made**, and there is no line the
writer could add marking where the unreached parts would have gone, because nobody knows where they would
have gone or that there were any.

## Drives

Toward a document shorter and more uniform than what walked into the room,
by a writer doing the job exactly and leaving nothing out that was in,
so the shortfall passes into the record as though it were the event.
