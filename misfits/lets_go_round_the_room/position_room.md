# Position: Room

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). The seat that convenes the joint recall, occupied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Several people who were there and one event none of them holds whole.
The obvious and correct reason to put them in one place.
No way to pool what they hold except by letting them hear each other.

## Orders

Get everybody who was there into a room.
Work out what happened between you, since each holds a piece and the pieces belong together.
Do it soon, because memory does not improve with waiting.

## Loses

Nothing it knows about.
**Hearing each other is not a step in pooling: it is what pooling is**, so the seat cannot convene the
meeting and withhold the exposure, and there is no version of a joint recall in which the members contribute
what they hold without hearing what the others hold.

## Drives

Toward an account fuller than any one person could give,
by a convener doing what a careful team requires and would be answerable for not doing,
so what follows is a property of the arrangement rather than of anybody's conduct inside it.
