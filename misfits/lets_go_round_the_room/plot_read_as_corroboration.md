# Plot: Read as Corroboration

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The account reaches somebody who was not in the room, which is what it was written for.

## Action

[Corroborating](process_corroborating.md): [The One Who Was Not There](persona_the_one_who_was_not_there.md), in [the reader seat](position_reader.md), works [Take It as Corroborated](plan_take_it_as_corroborated.md) and meets [the agreement made here](piece_the_agreement_made_here.md).

## Stage

[The Later Reading](place_the_later_reading.md): one version of events, several names on it, no dissent recorded and none to record.

## Tension

The plot has to land the harm on a reader who is reasoning correctly, and then hold the whole play against
its limit.
**Agreement observed in the world and agreement produced in a room look identical on paper.** The reader
applies the rule that agreement among accounts is evidence, which is the right rule and what agreement is
for, to a record that is faithful in every line, and gets a confidence the account cannot support. Suspicion
has nothing to work on: no excess agreement to notice, no anomaly, no suppressed dissent.
And the bound. The shortening is measured on **word lists and staged scenes** rather than the open-ended
events a real debrief covers, and it weakens or vanishes where the people in the room retrieved
non-overlapping parts or were made to organise their recall the same way. **Collaboration also prunes some
errors**, since a wrong detail can be challenged by somebody who knows better, so the room is not simply
worse: it is shorter and more uniform, and the uniformity is what gets misread.
Closes on [the blank page](place_the_blank_page.md), which would have removed both arms for ten minutes and a
pen, and which almost nobody uses, for reasons that are about the room and not about the memory.
