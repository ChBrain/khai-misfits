# Plot: The Agreed Version

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The meeting ends and somebody writes it up.

## Action

[What We Wrote Down](persona_what_we_wrote_down.md), in [the record seat](position_record.md), works [Write the Agreed Version](plan_write_the_agreed_version.md) and produces [the shorter account](piece_the_shorter_account.md).

## Stage

[The Room](place_the_room.md): a whiteboard photographed, a document circulated, everybody content that it says what was said.

## Tension

The plot has to make the record's fidelity the problem rather than its carelessness.
Nothing said is left out and nothing unsaid is added, and the document would survive any audit of whether it
says what was said. **A faithful record of a shortened recall is a shortened record, faithfully made**, and
there is no line the writer could add marking the unreached parts, because nobody in the room knows there
were any.
Short against what, in any case. The comparison that would show it is these same people at separate desks,
pooled afterwards, which is **exactly the arrangement the meeting replaced**.
Closes with a document shorter and more uniform than what walked in, and no line in it marking either.
