# Plot: Where Did That Come From

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A detail offered by one person, before anybody else's account is fixed.

## Action

[Adopting](process_adopting.md): [The One Who Speaks First](persona_the_one_who_speaks_first.md), in [the first-account seat](position_first_account.md), works [Start Us Off](plan_start_us_off.md), and what was said becomes [the detail that was not mine](piece_the_detail_that_was_not_mine.md).

## Stage

[The Room](place_the_room.md): an account given in good faith and heard in good faith.

## Tension

The plot has to show the adoption happening with nobody credulous and nobody lying.
**A memory adopted is a memory, from inside.** There is no felt seam between the thing seen and the thing
heard and now recalled, so no amount of care or self-examination separates them, and the person who reports
it later is telling the truth about their own memory.
And the first speaker is doing nothing but speaking first. **The influence belongs to the sequence**, since
everything said afterwards is heard against what they said, so replacing the person changes who has the
effect and not whether anybody does, and the only way to remove it is to have nobody speak first, which is to
have no meeting.
Closes with two accounts converged and neither party able to say which of them had the detail.
