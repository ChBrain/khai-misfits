# Process: Convening

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). Putting the people who were there in one place to work out what happened.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An event several people saw and none of them holds whole.
At [the room](place_the_room.md), soon afterwards, by somebody doing what a careful team requires.

## Direction

Toward an account fuller than any one person could give.
This is the whole content of the process and not a step in it: a debrief that does not pool is not a weaker
debrief, it is several people each keeping a piece.

## Lever

Presence, which is the only thing that can be turned and cannot be turned down.
Convening people who do not hear each other is not a quieter meeting, it is no meeting.

## Echo

Everybody who was there, in one place,
willing, prompt and well disposed,
about to hear each other, which is the pooling and the disruption at once.
