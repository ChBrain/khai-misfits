# Position: Own Order

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). The seat of a person's private route to their own memory, occupied by each of them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An event reached by a path rather than read off a list.
An organisation idiosyncratic to the person holding it, this bit leading to that one.
No way to follow it while attending to somebody else's.

## Orders

Follow your own thread, because it is how you get to what you have.
Attend to the others, because that is what you came for and what the meeting is.
Do both, which is the instruction the seat cannot carry out.

## Loses

The parts its own route would have reached.
**What is retrieved is what the room's order reaches and not what your own would have**, so the loss is
silent: nothing is forgotten, nothing is suppressed, and the missing pieces leave no gap anybody in the room
can point to.

## Drives

Toward a contribution shaped by the order the room happened to take,
by a person listening politely and waiting their turn,
so the shortfall is authored by nobody and visible to no one in the room.
