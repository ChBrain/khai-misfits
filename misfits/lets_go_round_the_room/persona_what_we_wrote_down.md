# Persona: What We Wrote Down

## Taxonomy

[Record](position_record.md). The account the meeting produced, faithful in every line.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Accurate, complete against the meeting, and signed.
Would survive any audit of whether it says what was said.
Is the only version of the event that will exist from here on.

## Action

Sets down what the room agreed and marks where it did not.
Leaves out nothing that anybody said.
Records no order of speaking, no adoption, and no comparison with what was never reached.

## Shadow

That being faithful to the meeting is the whole of the problem.
**A faithful record of a shortened recall is a shortened record, faithfully made**, and no line the writer
could add would mark the unreached parts, because nobody in the room knows there were any.

## Tell

Is the element that would pass any check the house can run on it.
Is the element that carries the shortfall forward as though it were the event.
