# Position: Reader

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). The seat that receives the account later, occupied by whoever was not there.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A document several people put their names to.
The ordinary and sound rule that agreement among accounts is evidence.
No way to tell, from the document, where the agreement was made.

## Orders

Weigh the account by how well its parts are corroborated.
Treat several people saying the same thing as stronger than one person saying it.
Ask whether anybody dissented, and note that nobody did.

## Loses

The distinction the whole reading turns on.
**Agreement observed in the world and agreement produced in a room look identical on paper**, so the reader
is not being careless: the information that would separate them was never written down and could not have
been.

## Drives

Toward a confidence the account cannot support,
by a reader applying the correct rule to the document in front of them,
so the misread needs no error at all, only the document.
