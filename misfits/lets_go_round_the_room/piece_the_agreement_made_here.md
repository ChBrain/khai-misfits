# Piece: The Agreement Made Here

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). Convergence produced by the meeting, read afterwards as convergence observed in the world.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Manufactured at [the room](place_the_room.md) by [hearing](process_hearing.md) and [adopting](process_adopting.md).
Cashed at [the later reading](place_the_later_reading.md) through [corroborating](process_corroborating.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is only about a shorter document, and nothing turns on it.
It is load bearing because **agreement observed in the world and agreement produced in a room look identical
on paper**, so the harm needs no error by anybody: the reader applies the correct rule to a faithful record
and gets a confidence the account cannot support.

## Apparent

That this is the reader's mistake and a more suspicious reader would catch it.
Suspicion has nothing to work on. There is no excess of agreement to notice, no statistical anomaly, and no
dissent that was suppressed: the agreement arrives at exactly the level a meeting produces, in a document
that records no order of speaking and no adoption.

## Yearbook

Every setting where a jointly produced account is later weighed by how many people signed it, which is most
of the settings a debrief is written for.
