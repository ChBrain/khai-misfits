# Persona: The One Who Was Not There

## Taxonomy

[Reader](position_reader.md). Whoever receives the account afterwards, including the group itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, and reasoning correctly.
Weighs the account by how well corroborated its parts are, which is the right way to weigh an account.
Is the party the whole exercise was for.

## Action

Reads several names against one version of events.
Treats the agreement as strength, because agreement among accounts is strength.
Notes that nobody dissented, and is right that nobody did.

## Shadow

That the document cannot tell them what they need.
**Agreement observed in the world and agreement produced in a room look identical on paper**, so this element
is not careless and does not improve with care: the information that would separate the two was never
written down and could not have been.

## Tell

Is the element where the harm lands.
Is the element that never enters the room and cannot be warned.
