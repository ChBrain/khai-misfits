# Process: Corroborating

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). Reading several agreeing accounts as several people agreeing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The record leaving the room.
At [the later reading](place_the_later_reading.md), whenever the account is used, by whoever was not there.

## Direction

Toward a confidence proportioned to the agreement.
The rule is correct and this is what agreement is for: several independent accounts converging is the
strongest ordinary evidence there is, and a reader who ignored it would be reasoning worse.

## Lever

Independence, which the reader assumes and the document does not record either way.

## Echo

One version of events with several names on it,
no dissent, correctly noted,
and a decision taken on a strength the account cannot carry.
