# Persona: The Sensible Reason to Meet

## Taxonomy

[Room](position_room.md). The reason the meeting is called, present in every debrief and embodied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Unarguable, and rightly so.
Is what anybody would say if asked why the meeting is happening.
Would be defended by everybody in the play, including the people it costs.

## Action

Puts the people who were there in one place, soon.
Asks them to work out what happened between them, since each holds a piece.
Does not ask them to write anything down first, because nobody does.

## Shadow

That it is the mechanism and cannot be softened for that reason.
**Hearing each other is not a step in pooling: it is what pooling is**, so relaxing this element does not
reduce the harm, it cancels the meeting and leaves several people each holding a piece of an event and
nobody putting them together.

## Tell

Is the element nobody would remove.
Is the element without which the play does not run.
