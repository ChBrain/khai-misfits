# Piece: The Detail That Was Not Mine

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). Something another person reported, taken up, and later recalled as one's own.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Offered at [the room](place_the_room.md) by whoever spoke first, or by anybody speaking before you.
Made yours through [adopting](process_adopting.md), and carried out of the room in your account.

## Load Bearing

Without it the room only shortens the account, and the second arm is missing.
It is load bearing because **a memory adopted is a memory, from inside**: there is no felt seam between the
thing seen and the thing heard and now recalled, so no amount of care, honesty or self-examination separates
them, and the person reporting it is telling the truth about their own memory.

## Apparent

That somebody in the room could say where each detail came from.
They could, at the time, for a little while. The record does not ask, the meeting has no reason to minute
its own order of speaking, and by the later reading nobody can.

## Yearbook

The social-contagion finding: an item another person reports is later reported by the hearer as their own,
and the reports of two people who have talked converge on each other.
