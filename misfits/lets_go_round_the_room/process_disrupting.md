# Process: Disrupting

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). Somebody else's order cutting across the route you reach your own memory by.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Nothing anybody does on purpose. It runs whenever [hearing](process_hearing.md) does.
At [the room](place_the_room.md), throughout, by nobody.

## Direction

Toward less of the event than the same people would have produced apart.
The group is not lazier and nobody is loafing: **what is retrieved is what the room's order reaches and not
what your own would have**, and the account each person gives is honest and complete against what they
reached.

## Lever

Order, which the room has one of and each person has another.

## Echo

A contribution shaped by the route the room took,
parts that were never mentioned because they were never reached,
and no gap in the record where they would have gone.
