# Plot: Losing the Thread

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The first person begins, and everybody else listens, which is what they came to do.

## Action

[Hearing](process_hearing.md) then [Disrupting](process_disrupting.md): [The Way I Had It](persona_the_way_i_had_it.md), in [the own-order seat](position_own_order.md), works [Follow My Own Thread](plan_follow_my_own_thread.md) and loses [my own thread](piece_my_own_thread.md).

## Stage

[The Room](place_the_room.md): people taking turns, politely, in an order somebody's account happened to set.

## Tension

The plot has to make the shortfall land without anybody being at fault for it.
A memory is **reached by a path rather than read off a list**, and the organisation is part of how the thing
is got to at all, so an order that is not yours does not slow the retrieval: it reaches different material.
Attention cannot be given to two orders at once, and the person losing their thread is **listening**, which
is why they came and what a person who did not listen would be blamed for.
Closes with the group producing less than the same people at separate desks would have produced pooled, and
**the loss leaving no gap anybody in the room can point to**, because nothing was forgotten and nothing was
suppressed: the unreached parts were simply never mentioned.
