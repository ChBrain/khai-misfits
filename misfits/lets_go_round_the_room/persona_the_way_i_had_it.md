# Persona: The Way I Had It

## Taxonomy

[Own Order](position_own_order.md). A person's private route to their own memory, carried by each of them into the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Invisible, including to the person carrying it.
Feels like nothing at all: not a method, not a strategy, just how the thing comes back.
Is the only route to a large part of what that person holds.

## Action

Reaches one detail through another, in an order nobody chose.
Is interrupted by attending to somebody else's order, which is what listening is.
Does not resume, because the room has moved on and the thread is not picked back up.

## Shadow

That the loss leaves no gap anybody can see.
**Nothing is forgotten and nothing is suppressed**: the unreached parts were never mentioned, so there is no
silence to notice, no hesitation to read, and no person in the room who could say what is missing.

## Tell

Is the element the room cannot observe.
Is the element whose absence is only visible against a comparison the room does not have.
