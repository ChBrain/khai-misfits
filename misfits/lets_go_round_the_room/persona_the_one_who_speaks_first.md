# Persona: The One Who Speaks First

## Taxonomy

[First Account](position_first_account.md). Whoever begins, which somebody has to.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary in every respect that would explain the effect.
Not senior, not loud, not certain: just first, because the room was waiting and somebody had to start.
Would be replaced by somebody who had exactly the same effect.

## Action

Gives an account, in their own order, as fully as they can.
Offers details, some of which the others do not have.
Sets the order the room will follow, without intending to and without noticing.

## Shadow

That the influence belongs to the sequence.
**Everything said afterwards is heard against what they said**, so nothing about this element improves if you
replace the person, and the only way to remove the effect is to have nobody speak first, which is to have no
meeting.

## Tell

Is the element people reach for when they want somebody to blame.
Is the element that behaves identically whoever occupies it.
