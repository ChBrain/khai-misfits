# Piece: The Reason to Meet

## Taxonomy

[Let's Go Round the Room](play_lets_go_round_the_room.md). Each person holds a piece and the pieces belong together, which is true and is why the meeting is right.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the room](place_the_room.md), stated or assumed by everyone who walks in.
Behind [Get It Straight Together](plan_get_it_straight_together.md), which is the only sensible mandate for the seat.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a story about a badly run meeting, and the remedy is a better chair.
It is load bearing because **hearing each other is not a step in pooling: it is what pooling is**, so the
element cannot be relaxed to reduce the harm. Relaxing it cancels the meeting and leaves several people each
holding a piece and nobody putting them together, which is worse and is what the meeting exists to prevent.

## Apparent

That a well-chaired meeting would avoid this.
Chairing changes the order; it does not remove the fact that there is one. Neither mechanism runs on
interruption, dominance or poor facilitation, and both run in a room where everybody is polite and takes
turns.

## Yearbook

The debrief, the post-incident review, the family working out what happened: every setting in which getting
people together is the obviously correct first move.
