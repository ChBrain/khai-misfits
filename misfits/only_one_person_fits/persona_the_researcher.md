# Persona: The Researcher

## Taxonomy

[Researcher](position_researcher.md). The one who needs the detail because the question is made of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An investigator with a question about small areas, rare outcomes or particular subgroups, and no way to answer it from a table of totals.

## Action

Asks for the variables the question requires.
Analyses at the resolution the question is posed at, and publishes what is found.

## Shadow

Asks for exactly the granularity that makes the file identifying, and needs it.
The seat is not overreaching: an outcome that differs by place cannot be studied without places, a subgroup effect cannot be found without the subgroup, and a rare condition has few enough people that any real cell is small. Coarsening the request until the file is safe is coarsening it until the question changes into a different question. The request that makes the study possible and the request that makes the rows unique are the same request.

## Tell

Asks for what the study needs, which is what the release cannot safely give.
