# Piece: The Quasi-Identifier

## Taxonomy

[The Release](place_the_release.md). The attribute that is neither a name nor noise.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Left behind by [stripping the names](process_stripping_the_names.md), and required by [keeping what answers the question](process_keeping_what_answers_the_question.md).

## Load Bearing

It is what the question is made of and what the rule does not name.
An age, a postcode, a date of birth, an admission date, the times a card was used. No list of identifiers written for a person to read includes these, because a person does not recognise them as identifying, and any list that did include them would forbid the studies the release exists for. So the seat that removes identifiers removes the right things and leaves the operative ones, and the seat that requests variables requests the operative ones because the question is about them.
