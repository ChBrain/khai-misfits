# Plot: The Remedy Prices the Trade and Cannot End It

## Taxonomy

[Only One Person Fits](play_only_one_person_fits.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A formal guarantee adopted, and a parameter to choose.

## Action

[The Privacy Budget](piece_the_privacy_budget.md) is set, noise is calibrated, and the worst case is bounded in advance.

## Stage

A genuine advance, which converts an unstated hope into a stated exchange.

## Tension

Making the trade explicit is the achievement and is not an escape from it.
A formal guarantee holds against an adversary with arbitrary outside information, which nothing before it did, and it forces the exchange to be chosen deliberately rather than discovered by somebody else later. It still requires a parameter, the accuracy given up is real, and at the largest deployment yet attempted the published analyses put the loss on the smallest geographies and the smallest populations. That is where the data was most needed and least substitutable, so the remedy's cost lands closest to the reason for releasing at all.
