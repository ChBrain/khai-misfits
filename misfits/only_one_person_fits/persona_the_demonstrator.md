# Persona: The Demonstrator

## Taxonomy

[Demonstrator](position_demonstrator.md). The one who performs the re-identification in order to stop it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A researcher who believes a released file is not as anonymous as it is said to be, and can establish that in only one way.

## Action

Buys an ordinary outside list, joins it to the release, and names somebody.
Publishes the method, tells the holder, and argues for a stronger standard.

## Shadow

Is the reason the risk is known and performs the harm to show it.
The seat is not an attacker in any ordinary sense: the demonstration exists to protect the people in the file, is published rather than exploited, and has repeatedly been what moved a standard. It also has no substitute, because an argument that a file could be re-identified is answered by the claim that nobody has, and only a re-identification refutes that. So the evidence that the release is unsafe can be produced only by doing the unsafe thing once, deliberately, in the open.

## Tell

Proves the danger by being the danger, once, on purpose.
