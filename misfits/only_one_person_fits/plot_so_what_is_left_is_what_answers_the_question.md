# Plot: So What Is Left Is What Answers the Question

## Taxonomy

[Only One Person Fits](play_only_one_person_fits.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A researcher who needs an age, a place and a date because the question is about ages, places and dates.

## Action

[The Quasi-Identifier](piece_the_quasi_identifier.md) is retained, deliberately, because removing it removes the study.

## Stage

A residue that is not a residue but the entire point of the release.

## Tension

The detail cannot be given up without giving up the question.
An outcome that differs by place cannot be studied without places. A subgroup effect cannot be found without the subgroup. A rare condition has few enough people that any honest cell is small. Coarsening until the file is safe does not produce a safer version of the study, it produces a different study that does not answer what was asked. So the seat asking for granularity is not overreaching: it is describing its question.
