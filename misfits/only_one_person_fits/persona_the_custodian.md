# Persona: The Custodian

## Taxonomy

[Custodian](position_custodian.md). The one who holds the records and has to let them out.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A registry, agency or hospital holding records collected for another purpose, facing a research request, a transparency duty and a reproducibility requirement.

## Action

Releases the data, under the applicable rule.
Applies the de-identification standard, keeps a record of the release, and takes the undertakings the rule asks for.

## Shadow

Cannot withhold and cannot release without doing this.
The seat is not being cavalier: the alternative to release is a body of findings nobody outside can check, questions about rare conditions and small places that go unanswered, and a public that funded the collection and cannot see what it bought. Withholding has victims too and they are not hypothetical. The seat is choosing between two real costs with no third option, and the rule it follows was written by people who knew that.

## Tell

Does the defensible thing, which is the thing that sets the mechanism running.
