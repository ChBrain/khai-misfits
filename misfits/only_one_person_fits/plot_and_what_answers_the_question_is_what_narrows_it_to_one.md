# Plot: And What Answers the Question Is What Narrows It to One

## Taxonomy

[Only One Person Fits](play_only_one_person_fits.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An ordinary outside table with names in it, and a join.

## Action

At [the linkage](place_the_linkage.md), [the unique row](piece_the_unique_row.md) is matched, and a person is named from a file with no names.

## Stage

Uniqueness produced by counting attributes, with no identifier involved anywhere.

## Tension

The row was one person before anybody looked, and looking only reads off which.
Postcode, sex and date of birth were estimated to single out a large majority of a national population, a figure later re-examined downward and still a majority. Four approximate points distinguish almost everyone in a mobility trace. Identifiability is therefore a property of the file against everything else, not of the file, so no inspection of the release by its holder can settle it, and the answer keeps moving as the world outside acquires more tables.
