# Persona: The De-identifier

## Taxonomy

[De-identifier](position_de_identifier.md). The one who applies the standard.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A statistician or disclosure-control officer working to a written rule: an enumerated list of identifiers to remove, a minimum cell size, a suppression threshold.

## Action

Removes the direct identifiers.
Suppresses small cells, coarsens where the rule says to coarsen, and signs off that the file meets the standard.

## Shadow

Meets the standard exactly, and the standard is not the mechanism.
The seat is not cutting corners: what it removes is what the rule enumerates, and the rule enumerates names, numbers and addresses because those are what a person recognises as identifying. What remains is age, place, date, sequence, which nobody would call an identifier and which is why they were left. The seat cannot remove them without destroying the release, and cannot keep them without leaving what makes a row rare. There is no version of this seat that could have done better under the rule it is given.

## Tell

Signs off correctly on a file that is exactly as unique as it is useful.
