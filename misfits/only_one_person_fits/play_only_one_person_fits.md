# Play: Only One Person Fits

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Only One Person Fits.
A body holds records about people and is obliged, or plainly right, to release them: for research, for scrutiny, for the reproducibility of results already published on them. It removes the names first, competently, to the standard of the day. What remains is what a question can be asked of, and what a question can be asked of is detail: an age, a place, a date, a sequence of visits. Detail is what makes a row answer, and detail is what makes a row rare, and past a certain point rare means one.

## Arc

The bend is this: the granularity that lets a record answer a question is the granularity that narrows it to a person, so a release is useful and identifying by the same measure.
It begins with a release nobody sensible opposes.
[The Record Must Be Released, and That Is Right](plot_the_record_must_be_released_and_that_is_right.md). The question it answers is real and the alternative is a finding nobody can check.
And the names come off properly.
[And the Names Come Off, Correctly](plot_and_the_names_come_off_correctly.md). [Stripping the names](process_stripping_the_names.md) is done to the applicable standard and is not where this fails.
So what is left is what answers.
[So What Is Left Is What Answers the Question](plot_so_what_is_left_is_what_answers_the_question.md). [The Quasi-Identifier](piece_the_quasi_identifier.md) is neither name nor noise, and removing it removes the study.
And [what answers the question is what narrows it to one](plot_and_what_answers_the_question_is_what_narrows_it_to_one.md).
[The Unique Row](piece_the_unique_row.md) needs no name to be a person, and [the linkage](place_the_linkage.md) is where an outside table finishes the sentence.
Then [the remedy prices the trade and cannot end it](plot_the_remedy_prices_the_trade_and_cannot_end_it.md). [The Privacy Budget](piece_the_privacy_budget.md) makes the exchange explicit and finite, which is an achievement and is not an escape.
And [the row is anonymous and is you](plot_so_the_row_is_anonymous_and_is_you.md). [The Subject](persona_the_subject.md) was party to nothing and cannot know.

## Triggers

**[The Record Must Be Released, and That Is Right](plot_the_record_must_be_released_and_that_is_right.md)**
Cue: a question that can only be answered from records already collected, and a duty or a plain public interest in releasing them. Closes when the release is established as the defensible act rather than a lapse.

**[And the Names Come Off, Correctly](plot_and_the_names_come_off_correctly.md)**
Cue: a variable list, a suppression rule, and a standard to meet. Closes when the removal is shown to be competent and not the point of failure.

**[So What Is Left Is What Answers the Question](plot_so_what_is_left_is_what_answers_the_question.md)**
Cue: a researcher who needs an age, a place and a date because the question is about ages, places and dates. Closes when the remaining detail is shown to be the study rather than a residue.

**[And What Answers the Question Is What Narrows It to One](plot_and_what_answers_the_question_is_what_narrows_it_to_one.md)**
Cue: an ordinary outside table with names in it, and a join. Closes when the row is shown to be one person without ever having carried a name.

**[The Remedy Prices the Trade and Cannot End It](plot_the_remedy_prices_the_trade_and_cannot_end_it.md)**
Cue: a formal guarantee adopted, and a budget to set. Closes when the exchange is found to be quantified rather than removed, and its cost found to fall on the smallest groups.

**[So the Row Is Anonymous and Is You](plot_so_the_row_is_anonymous_and_is_you.md)**
Cue: a person who answered a form or attended an appointment, years ago. Closes when a correctly de-identified record is found to be both anonymous and a particular person.

## Stakes

Whether a person can be counted without being found.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a release that is obliged or plainly right, a removal of names that meets the standard, a residue that is the study itself, a uniqueness that arrives from detail rather than from identifiers, a formal remedy that prices the exchange without ending it, and a subject who is party to nothing, and away from any account in which a custodian was careless or an attacker was malicious. The production cannot fault the de-identifier, who applied the rule as written; and it cannot fault the demonstrator, who performed the re-identification in order to show that it was possible and had no other way to show it. What is left to fight over is not whether anybody erred but what to call a record that carries no name and admits one person.
