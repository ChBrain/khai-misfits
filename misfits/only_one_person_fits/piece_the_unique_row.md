## Load Bearing

Uniqueness arrives from the number of attributes rather than from any of them, so nothing in the row looks dangerous.
Postcode, sex and date of birth were estimated to single out a large majority of a national population, a figure later re-examined downward and still a majority. Four approximate points in time and space distinguish almost everyone in a mobility trace. Fifteen ordinary demographic attributes were estimated to identify essentially any American, and sampling does not rescue the file, because a rare combination is rare in the sample too. No single value here is an identifier and the combination is one, which is a property of arithmetic rather than of anybody's conduct.
