## Withheld

The pretence that the release was ever anonymous on its own.
The place shows that identifiability is not a property of the file but of the file against everything else, so it cannot be certified by looking at the file. The uniqueness was not created here: the row admitted one person from the moment it was written, and the join only reads off which one. That is why a custodian's inspection of its own release, however careful, cannot answer the question, and why the answer keeps changing as the world outside acquires more tables.
