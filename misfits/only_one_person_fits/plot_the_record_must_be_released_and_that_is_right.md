# Plot: The Record Must Be Released, and That Is Right

## Taxonomy

[Only One Person Fits](play_only_one_person_fits.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A question answerable only from records already collected, and a duty or plain public interest in releasing them.

## Action

[The Custodian](persona_the_custodian.md) prepares a release: a request accepted, a file assembled, a standard to be met.

## Stage

A decision between two real costs, with no third option available.

## Tension

Withholding is not the safe choice, it is the other costly one.
A registry that releases nothing leaves findings nobody outside can check, questions about rare conditions and small places unanswered, and a public that paid for the collection unable to see what it bought. Those costs fall on people too, and they are not hypothetical. The rule the seat follows was written by people who understood that, which is why it prescribes release under conditions rather than refusal.
