# Position: Front-Runner

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The seat of one of the two viable contenders who gathers the deserters and consolidates, and marks that the lock it holds is competition's sum and not a conspiracy.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

One of the two viable candidacies, a real chance to finish first.
The deserters of the third, voters seeking a say among those who can win.
A simple aim, to gather the votes on offer and win the seat.

## Orders

Consolidate, and gather the votes the rule sends your way.
Seek the voters who have deserted the third for a viable choice, present yourself as one of the only two who can win, and win the seat, because that is what a candidate does.
Know that the deserters come to you because the rule has wasted the third and not because you conspired with your rival, that the two-party field you help hold is the sum of the electorate's sensible desertions, and that you consolidate a lock you did not design.

## Loses

Nothing it counts as a loss, and this is the point.
Because the seat only gathers the votes on offer and wins the seat it sought, it takes no loss it can name, and yet the field it consolidates is narrowed to two, the third view unrepresented, a public loss authored by no one who willed it and carried by the whole.
It keeps its victory and its clean competition and loses no cause for blame, a contender that only sought the votes the rule sent its way.

## Drives

Toward the viable contender that gathers the deserters and wins,
seeking the votes on offer and consolidating a field the rule has narrowed, in fair competition and not conspiracy,
so the seat that only competes to win holds the lock to two, an outcome no one designed built from the sum of sensible votes.
