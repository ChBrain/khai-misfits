# Piece: The Third

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The third candidate with real but insufficient support, genuinely preferred by some and unable to finish first.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Hustings](place_the_hustings.md), carried by [Standing](process_standing.md), voiced by [the Challenger](persona_the_challenger.md): the platform raised in the open contest, before any ballot is cast.

## Apparent

A hopeless spoiler splitting the vote.
