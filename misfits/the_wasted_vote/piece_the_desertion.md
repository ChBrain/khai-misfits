# Piece: The Desertion

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The act: the strategic switch to the viable lesser evil.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Booth](place_the_booth.md), carried by [Deserting](process_deserting.md), voiced by [the Elector](persona_the_elector.md): the mark made, not for the preferred, but for the one who can win.

## Load Bearing

The voter abandons its true preference for whichever of the two front runners is the lesser evil, so as to be heard, keeping the preference intact and abandoning only its expression, a rational best-response to the count and not a want of nerve (Duverger; Cox).

## Apparent

Cowardice, a sellout of conviction.

## Yearbook

Carried by [Deserting](process_deserting.md), staged at [the Booth](place_the_booth.md), voiced by [the Elector](persona_the_elector.md), dramatized in [The Lesser Evil](plot_the_lesser_evil.md). It is the switch each voter makes; summed across the electorate it builds [The Lock](piece_the_lock.md).
