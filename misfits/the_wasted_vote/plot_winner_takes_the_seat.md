# Plot: Winner Takes the Seat

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The field is set, three candidates before the electorate, and the counting rule that will decide among them is already in force.

## Action

At [the Hustings](place_the_hustings.md), [the Elector](persona_the_elector.md) meets [the Count](piece_the_count.md), the plurality winner-take-all rule that awards the single seat to whoever finishes first and nothing to anyone else.

## Stage

This is the mechanical effect (Duverger). The rule is plain and neutral on its face: count every vote, seat whoever has the most. But its plainness hides an asymmetry that falls hardest on a third party. A candidate who finishes second with a large share wins exactly as much as one who finishes third with almost none: nothing. There is no proportional consolation, no partial seat for partial support. However much sincere backing the third draws, the rule under-rewards it in the only currency that matters, seats won. The Elector, standing before this rule for the first time in this telling, meets it not as an opinion to weigh but as an arithmetic fact to reckon with. No villain: the rule does not target the third candidate, it simply counts, and would treat any candidate the same were the standings reversed.

## Tension

A vote for the third, however sincerely cast, is worth nothing at all under this count.
