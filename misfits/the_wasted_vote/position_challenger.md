# Position: Challenger

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The seat of the third candidate with real but insufficient support, and marks that its hopelessness is made by the desertion it draws and not given in the standing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real platform and a genuine choice the two front runners do not offer.
Real support, the sincere preference of some part of the electorate.
Not the most, and under a rule that seats the first alone, no path to finish first.

## Orders

Stand anyway, and offer the third choice the two do not.
Raise your platform and draw the voters who genuinely prefer it, giving the electorate a real alternative to the two front runners, because a view held by some deserves a candidate to carry it.
Know that under a rule that seats the first alone your real support will bleed away to the viable, that you will be called a spoiler for splitting the vote, and that you are judged unable to win and so deserted, the verdict writing itself, your hopelessness made and not given.

## Loses

The support it truly has, drained away to the viable by the rule.
Because the count seats the first alone and your voters, not wanting to waste a vote, desert you for the lesser of the two who can win, the seat holds a real preference that never becomes a real tally, its numbers judged too few to win and made too few by the judging.
It keeps its genuine platform and its honest standing and loses no cause for blame, a third choice stranded by a rule that rewards only the first.

## Drives

Toward the third candidate that offers a real choice and cannot win,
standing on genuine support and drawing the sincere preference of some, only to see it desert to the viable under the rule,
so the seat that carries the third view is deserted for being sure to lose and made sure to lose by the deserting, a spoiler only in the counting.
