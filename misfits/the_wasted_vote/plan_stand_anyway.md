# Plan: Stand Anyway

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the challenger's seat](position_challenger.md), inside [The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Challenger](position_challenger.md)

## Direction

Raise your platform and draw the voters who genuinely prefer it, giving the electorate a real alternative to the two front runners, because a view held by some deserves a candidate to carry it. Know that under a rule that seats the first alone your real support will bleed away to the viable, that you will be called a spoiler for splitting the vote, and that you are judged unable to win and so deserted, the verdict writing itself, your hopelessness made and not given.
