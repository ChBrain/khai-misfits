# Plan: Count It Different

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reformer's seat](position_reformer.md), inside [The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reformer](position_reformer.md)

## Direction

Change how the votes are counted, to proportional or ranked or runoff rules under which a vote for a third candidate is no longer thrown away, so the pressure to desert is relieved and more than two parties can be viable. Know that this spends a real cost, resisted by the two parties the current rule favours and who must legislate the change, that the multi-party systems it opens carry their own costs of fragmented coalitions and slower government, and that no ballot removes the need for voters to coordinate.
