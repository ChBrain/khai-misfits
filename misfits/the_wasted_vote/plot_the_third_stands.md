# Plot: The Third Stands

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An election runs under a winner-take-all rule, the single seat going to whoever finishes first. A third candidate enters the field.

## Action

At [the Hustings](place_the_hustings.md), [the Challenger](persona_the_challenger.md) from [the Challenger's seat](position_challenger.md) on [Stand Anyway](plan_stand_anyway.md) through [Standing](process_standing.md) raises [the Third](piece_the_third.md), the third candidate with real but insufficient support.

## Stage

The third candidate offers a genuine choice that the two established contenders do not: a distinct platform, a sincere alternative, drawing real support from those who prefer it outright. But the support is real and not the most, and this is the seed of everything that follows. The third stands on its merits, unable yet to know that the rule waiting to count it cares nothing for those merits, only for who finishes first (Duverger). What it draws today is the sincere preference of some; what the rule will make of that preference, later in the count, is another matter entirely. No villain here: the Challenger stands honestly, offering a real choice, and nothing in the standing itself does harm.

## Tension

The third is in the field, genuinely preferred by some, and the rule ahead rewards only the first.
