# Plot: The Lesser Evil

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The wasted vote is feared now, and a say in the contest that will be decided is wanted.

## Action

At [the Booth](place_the_booth.md), [the Elector](persona_the_elector.md) from [the Elector's seat](position_elector.md) on [Back a Winner](plan_back_a_winner.md) through [Deserting](process_deserting.md) casts [the Desertion](piece_the_desertion.md), abandoning its true preference for the lesser of the two who can win.

## Stage

The desertion does not touch the preference itself; it touches only its expression on the ballot. The Elector still, privately, prefers the third; nothing about that private ranking has changed. What changes is the mark made at the Booth: a vote cast for whichever of the two front runners is judged the lesser evil, so as to have a say in the contest that will actually be decided. This is a rational best response to the rule met at the Count and the fear formed around the Wasted Vote, not a want of nerve or a betrayal of conviction (Cox). Coordinating on one of the two viable contenders, rather than splitting sincere support across a field of no-hopers, is exactly what a rule that seats only the first makes sensible for a voter who wants any influence at all on who is seated. No villain: the Elector deserts to be heard, and there is nothing dishonest in the arithmetic that leads there.

## Tension

The preference is kept, and the vote is cast against it, so as to be heard.
