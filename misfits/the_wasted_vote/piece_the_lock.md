# Piece: The Lock

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The harm: the self-reinforcing two-party equilibrium.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Return](place_the_return.md), carried by [Consolidating](process_consolidating.md), voiced by [the Front-Runner](persona_the_front_runner.md): the tally that comes back down to two.

## Load Bearing

The third is deserted because judged unable to win and cannot win because deserted, so the judgement fulfils itself and the field settles to two, the choices narrowed to a pair, the third view unrepresented and the voter voting against rather than for, a lock the sum of sensible desertions and designed by no one (Duverger; Riker).

This is distinct from The Crowded Middle: there two candidates edge to the centre spatially to win the median voter, so both cluster alike, and the engine is candidate positioning. Here the engine is voters' strategic desertion of a third under a counting rule, and the field is reduced in number to two, not converged in position; the two can co-occur but this misfit is the locking-to-two, not the clustering-alike. It is distinct from The Beauty Contest: there players price opinion about opinion in a market with no counting rule; here the coordination is on which candidates are viable under a specific electoral rule, and the vote is not a price. And it is distinct from Information Cascade: there each copies others' visible choices and discards a private signal, updating belief from the crowd; here the voter keeps its private preference intact, still preferring the third, and abandons only its expression, best-responding to a counting rule, so it is strategic voting and not belief-updating herd behaviour.
