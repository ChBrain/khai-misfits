# Position: Reformer

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The seat of the one who would change the count so a third vote is not wasted, and marks that the remedy is costly, resisted by the incumbents, and not without its own trade.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A sight of the cause, the knowledge that the desertion is forced by a rule that wastes any vote but the first.
Another ballot to reach for: proportional representation, or ranked and runoff rules under which a third vote counts.
A clear view of the price, the incumbents who must pass the change and the costs the new rule carries.

## Orders

Count it different, since the rule that seats the first alone is what wastes the third.
Change how the votes are counted, to proportional or ranked or runoff rules under which a vote for a third candidate is no longer thrown away, so the pressure to desert is relieved and more than two parties can be viable.
Know that this spends a real cost, resisted by the two parties the current rule favours and who must legislate the change, that the multi-party systems it opens carry their own costs of fragmented coalitions and slower government, and that no ballot removes the need for voters to coordinate.

## Loses

The ease of the rule as it stands, spent to open the field.
Because it will not accept a count that wastes the third choice, the seat takes on the hard cost of changing the rule against the incumbents it favours, and trades the clean decisiveness of two-party government for the coalitions and delay of a field of more, buying a wider choice at a real price.
It keeps its sight of the cause and its honest reach for the cure and loses no cause for blame, the one who would count the third vote in and pays for the counting.

## Drives

Toward the one who would change the count so the third vote is not wasted,
reaching for proportional or ranked or runoff rules that let more than two be viable, against the incumbents and at a real cost,
so the seat that alone can open the field opens it only by spending the ease of the rule that closed it, and only so far as the incumbents allow.
