# Position: Elector

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The seat of the voter who prefers the third but deserts it for the viable lesser evil, and marks that the desertion is the rational way to be heard and not a want of principle.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A sincere first preference, a genuine liking for the third candidate.
One vote, and under the rule a say only if it falls on a candidate who can win.
A reading of the field, the knowledge that the third cannot finish first and the two front runners can.

## Orders

Back a winner, since a vote for the one who cannot win wins nothing.
Weigh your sincere vote for the third, likely thrown away under a rule that seats the first alone, against a vote for the lesser of the two who can win, and choose to be heard in the contest that will actually be decided.
Know that your desertion keeps your true preference and abandons only its expression, that it is the rational answer to the count and not a failure of nerve, and that the sum of such sensible desertions is the very lock that wastes the third choice for all.

## Loses

The sincere vote it would have cast, given up to keep a say.
Because a vote for a candidate who cannot win forfeits any say in the real contest, the seat deserts its true preference for the viable lesser evil, and buys a voice in the outcome at the price of voting against the worse rather than for the best.
It keeps its real preference and its rational judgement and loses no cause for blame, a voter heard only by deserting the choice it holds.

## Drives

Toward the voter who prefers the third and votes for the second,
reading that the third cannot win and deserting it for the viable lesser evil, so as to have a say, sensibly and without fault,
so the seat whose every rational vote not to be wasted is a vote that helps lock the field to two, its say bought by the surrender of its choice.
