# Place: The Booth

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The voting booth, where the voter faces the wasted vote and deserts. Hosts [A Vote Thrown Away](plot_a_vote_thrown_away.md), [The Lesser Evil](plot_the_lesser_evil.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The voter alone with the ballot, its sincere preference marked in the mind.
The reading, arrived at unaided, that the preference cannot win.

## Holds

[The Thrown Vote](piece_the_thrown_vote.md).
The belief that a sincere vote for a sure loser is thrown away.

[The Desertion](piece_the_desertion.md).
The strategic switch from the true preference to the viable lesser evil.

## Offers

To the Elector, a say in the contest that will actually be decided, if it votes for one who can win.

## Withheld

Any way to be heard while voting for a candidate who cannot finish first.
