# Plot: A Vote Thrown Away

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The rule is understood now, and the running reckoning shows the third trailing behind the two front runners.

## Action

At [the Booth](place_the_booth.md), [the Elector](persona_the_elector.md) meets [the Wasted Vote](piece_the_wasted_vote.md), the belief that a sincere vote for a sure loser is thrown away and forfeits a say in the contest that will be decided.

## Stage

This is the psychological effect (Duverger; Cox). It is one thing to know the mechanical rule in the abstract, another to feel its weight at the moment of marking a ballot. The Elector still prefers the third, sincerely and without doubt. But standing at the Booth, the arithmetic of the Count becomes a live anticipation: if the third cannot finish first, a vote for it changes nothing in the contest that will actually be decided, and so amounts, in effect, to no vote cast at all on the outcome. This anticipation is not free floating; it needs the Elector to share, with enough of the electorate, a common read on who is viable and who is not (Cox), the polls, the talk, the visible trailing in the running count. No villain: the fear of the wasted vote is a rational reading of the rule just met, not a failure of conviction or a cynic's shrug.

## Tension

The sincere vote stands about to be surrendered, so as to have a say in the contest that will be heard.
