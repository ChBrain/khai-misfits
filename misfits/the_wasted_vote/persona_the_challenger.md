# Persona: The Challenger

## Taxonomy

[Challenger](position_challenger.md), inside [The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The archetype of the third candidate with real but insufficient support, genuinely preferred by some and unable to finish first, the option the winner-take-all rule will strand.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A hopeless spoiler, a vanity candidate who should stand aside, splitting the vote and helping the worse of the two win, as though standing at all were an indulgence and the sensible thing were to fold into one of the two and not divide the like-minded.

## Action

Stand anyway. At [the Hustings](place_the_hustings.md), the Challenger on [Stand Anyway](plan_stand_anyway.md) through [Standing](process_standing.md) raises [the Third](piece_the_third.md), the third candidate with real support and a genuine platform, offering a choice the two front runners do not, and drawing the preference of some without the numbers to finish first (Duverger).

## Shadow

Its hopelessness is made, not given. The Challenger is not a vanity or a spoiler by nature: it draws real support and offers a real choice, and it is unable to win only because it is judged unable to win and so deserted, the verdict writing itself. It stands not to split but to represent a view the two do not, and that its support bleeds away to the viable is the rule's doing and the voters' sensible response, not a fault in the standing. It offers the third choice in good faith, stranded by a count that rewards only the first.

## Tell

It offers a real choice and draws real support, and is called a spoiler only because the rule strands it.
