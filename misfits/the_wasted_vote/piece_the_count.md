# Piece: The Count

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The mechanical effect: the plurality winner-take-all rule that seats the first alone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Hustings](place_the_hustings.md), met by [the Elector](persona_the_elector.md): the rule standing over the contest before a single vote is cast under it.

## Load Bearing

The rule awards the single seat to whoever finishes first and nothing to anyone else, so a vote for a candidate who cannot come first wins nothing, the mechanical effect that under-rewards a third party in seats however much support it draws (Duverger).

## Apparent

A neutral technicality of how ballots are tallied.

## Yearbook

Met by [the Elector](persona_the_elector.md), staged at [the Hustings](place_the_hustings.md), dramatized in [Winner Takes the Seat](plot_winner_takes_the_seat.md). The rule that makes a third vote wasted; it gives rise to [The Thrown Vote](piece_the_thrown_vote.md).
