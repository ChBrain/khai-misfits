# Persona: The Elector

## Taxonomy

[Elector](position_elector.md), inside [The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The archetype of the voter who prefers the third candidate but, under the rule that a vote for a loser wins nothing, deserts it for the lesser of the two who can win, so as to be heard.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A coward or a sellout, betraying its own conviction, folding to the frontrunner out of timidity or a wish to back a winner, as though a firmer voter would simply vote its heart and the desertion were a failure of principle.

## Action

Back a winner. At [the Hustings](place_the_hustings.md), the Elector meets [the Count](piece_the_count.md), the rule that seats the first alone, then at [the Booth](place_the_booth.md) meets [The Thrown Vote](piece_the_thrown_vote.md) and on [Back a Winner](plan_back_a_winner.md) through [Deserting](process_deserting.md) casts [the Desertion](piece_the_desertion.md), abandoning its true preference for the viable lesser evil, so as to have a say (Duverger; Cox).

## Shadow

Its desertion is rational, not a want of principle. The Elector is not betraying its conviction lightly: under a rule where a vote for a candidate who cannot win wins nothing, a sincere vote forfeits any say in the contest that will actually be decided, so to be heard it must choose among those who can win. It keeps its true preference intact and abandons only its expression, best-responding to the count and not folding from timidity. Its vote against the worse is the only way the rule leaves it to be heard, and the fault is not the Elector's for taking it.

## Tell

It still prefers the third, and votes for the second, because the rule makes a vote for the first-choice a vote for no one.
