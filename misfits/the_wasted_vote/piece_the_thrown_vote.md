# Piece: The Thrown Vote

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The psychological effect: the belief that a sincere vote for a sure loser is thrown away.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Booth](place_the_booth.md), met by [the Elector](persona_the_elector.md): the belief that arrives with the voter before the mark is made.

## Load Bearing

Following from the count, the anticipation that a vote for a candidate who cannot win forfeits a say in the contest that will actually be decided, the belief that drives the sincere preference out, needing voters to share information about who is viable (Duverger; Cox).

## Apparent

Cynicism, a failure to vote one's heart.

## Yearbook

Met by [the Elector](persona_the_elector.md), staged at [the Booth](place_the_booth.md), dramatized in [A Vote Thrown Away](plot_a_vote_thrown_away.md). The fear that forces the switch; it precipitates [The Desertion](piece_the_desertion.md).
