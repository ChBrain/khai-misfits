# Piece: The Other Ballot

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Place

At [the Return](place_the_return.md), carried by [Reforming](process_reforming.md), voiced by [the Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md): the count itself put up for revision, once the tally is in.

## Load Bearing

The proportional or ranked and runoff rule under which a vote for a third candidate is no longer wasted and more than two parties can be viable, the one real cure, costly because it is resisted by the two parties the current rule favours and who must legislate it and because multi-party systems carry their own costs of fragmented coalitions and slower government (Duverger's corollary; Cox). And it carries the bound: the law is a tendency and not a determinism, holding at the single-member district but overridden nationally by a regionally concentrated third party (Canada, India), needing shared information to bite, and shaped by social cleavages as much as by the rule (Riker; Lipset and Rokkan).

## Apparent

A sore loser rigging the rules.
