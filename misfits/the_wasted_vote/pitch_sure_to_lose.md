# Pitch: Sure to Lose

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a third candidate is deserted because it is judged sure to lose, and deserted it is sure to lose.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Grave and measured, the civic scene told without blame. An ordinary voter who genuinely prefers a third candidate and, seeing under the rule that it cannot win, votes instead for the lesser of the two who can, so as to have a say. Nothing here is cowardice or apathy; the desertion is the sensible way to be heard.

## Undertow

Under a plurality, winner-take-all rule the single seat goes to whoever finishes first and nothing to anyone else, so a vote for a candidate who cannot come first wins nothing, the mechanical effect (Duverger). Foreseeing the waste, the voter deserts the true preference for a viable one, the psychological effect, and the anticipation of the wasted vote drives the sincere choice out (Duverger; Cox).

## Nerve

That the third is deserted because judged unable to win and cannot win because deserted, so the judgement fulfils itself and the field locks to two by the sum of votes cast, sensibly, not to be wasted. No one is a villain; it is the rule that counts the votes, held against its limit, since the law is a tendency softened by regional third parties and by the cleavages that share the cause (Riker; Lipset and Rokkan).

## Echo

A vote for the one who cannot win is thrown away, so each backs a winner, and only two ever can.
