# Plan: Back a Winner

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the elector's seat](position_elector.md), inside [The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Elector](position_elector.md)

## Direction

Weigh your sincere vote for the third, likely thrown away under a rule that seats the first alone, against a vote for the lesser of the two who can win, and choose to be heard in the contest that will actually be decided. Know that your desertion keeps your true preference and abandons only its expression, that it is the rational answer to the count and not a failure of nerve, and that the sum of such sensible desertions is the very lock that wastes the third choice for all.
