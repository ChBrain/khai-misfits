# Plot: Down to Two

## Taxonomy

[The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The desertions cast at the Booth are not one Elector's alone; across the whole electorate, they sum.

## Action

At [the Return](place_the_return.md), [the Front-Runner](persona_the_front_runner.md) from [the Front-Runner's seat](position_front_runner.md) on [Consolidate](plan_consolidate.md) through [Consolidating](process_consolidating.md) draws [the Lock](piece_the_lock.md), the self-reinforcing equilibrium in which the third is deserted because judged unable to win and cannot win because deserted.

## Stage

Here is the harm. The Third is deserted because it is judged unable to win; and being deserted is exactly what makes it unable to win. The judgement fulfils itself, and it fulfils itself not once but at scale, each Elector's individually rational desertion adding to the next, until the field settles to two and the third view goes unrepresented in the Return that counts the result. Nobody chose this outcome as an outcome; it is the sum of many separately sensible choices, arrived at without any single hand steering it there (Duverger; Riker). This is distinct from three neighbouring shapes it might be mistaken for. It is not The Crowded Middle, where two candidates converge spatially toward a shared centre; here the field is reduced in number, not converged in position, the third candidate's platform never moves, only its support drains away. It is not The Beauty Contest, where a price forms around opinion about others' opinion in a market; here voters coordinate on viability under a counting rule, not on guessing what other guessers will guess. And it is not Information Cascade, where belief updates as each observer copies the herd; here the Elector's private preference for the third is never revised or abandoned, only its public expression on the ballot deserts, strategic voting and not belief-updating herd behaviour. No villain: the front runners only compete to win, as candidates do, and the loss of the third view is authored by no one in particular.

## Tension

The field is locked to two, not by any hand, but by the sum of votes each cast so as not to be wasted.
