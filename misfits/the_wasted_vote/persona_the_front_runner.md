# Persona: The Front-Runner

## Taxonomy

[Front-Runner](position_front_runner.md), inside [The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The archetype of one of the two viable contenders, who gathers the deserters and consolidates, so the field settles to a pair and the lock holds.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A cynical machine closing the field, colluding with the other big party to shut the outsiders out, as though the two-party lock were a conspiracy of the powerful rather than the sum of many voters' choices.

## Action

Consolidate. At [the Return](place_the_return.md), the Front-Runner on [Consolidate](plan_consolidate.md) through [Consolidating](process_consolidating.md) draws [the Lock](piece_the_lock.md), gathering the voters who deserted the third and presenting itself as one of the only two who can win, so the field settles to two and the equilibrium holds (Duverger; Riker).

## Shadow

Its consolidation is competition, not conspiracy. The Front-Runner is not colluding to close the field: it is only doing what a candidate does, seeking the votes on offer, and the deserters come to it because the rule has made the third a wasted vote, not because it conspired with its rival to shut the third out. The lock is not its design but the sum of the electorate's sensible desertions, which it gathers as any contender would. It competes to win in good faith, and the two-party field it helps hold is authored by no one who willed it.

## Tell

It only gathers the votes on offer, and the votes on offer are only two candidates' because the rule wasted the third.
