## Direction

Seek the voters who have deserted the third for a viable choice, present yourself as one of the only two who can win, and win the seat, because that is what a candidate does. Know that the deserters come to you because the rule has wasted the third and not because you conspired with your rival, that the two-party field you help hold is the sum of the electorate's sensible desertions, and that you consolidate a lock you did not design.
