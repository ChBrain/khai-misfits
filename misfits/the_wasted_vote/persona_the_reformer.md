# Persona: The Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Reformer](position_reformer.md), inside [The Wasted Vote](play_the_wasted_vote.md). The archetype of the one who would change the ballot so a third vote is no longer wasted, and who marks that the remedy is costly, resisted by the incumbents, and not without its own trade.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A sore loser rigging the rules, a partisan who could not win the game as played and so would change the game, as though reform were only the losers' gambit and the honest course were to win under the rule as it stands.

## Action

Count it different. At [the Return](place_the_return.md), the Reformer on [Count It Different](plan_count_it_different.md) through [Reforming](process_reforming.md) holds [the Other Ballot](piece_the_other_ballot.md), the proportional or ranked and runoff rule under which a vote for a third candidate is no longer thrown away and more than two parties can be viable (Duverger's corollary; Cox).

## Shadow

Its reform is the one real cure, and it is dearly bought. The Reformer is not a sore loser gaming the rules for advantage: the desertion is forced by a rule that wastes any vote but the first, so the only way to keep the third choice alive is to change how the votes are counted. But this spends a real cost, resisted by the two parties the current rule favours and who must pass the change, and the multi-party systems it opens carry their own costs of fragmented coalitions and slower government, and no ballot removes the need for voters to coordinate. Its remedy works against the grain of the incumbents it must persuade, and it is not free.

## Tell

It would change the count so the third vote is not wasted, and the two the rule favours are the ones who must allow it.
