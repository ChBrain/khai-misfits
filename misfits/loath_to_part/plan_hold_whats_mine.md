# Plan: Hold What's Mine

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the owner's seat](position_owner.md), inside [Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Owner](position_owner.md)

## Direction

Do not part with the good below what it is worth to you now that it is yours, which is more than you would have paid to get it. Letting it go is a real loss, and a loss weighs more than the equal gain of acquiring, so name your price as the true measure of that loss and hold to it. Accept that this price sits above what the same good is worth to anyone who does not own it, and that the reference point which raised it is not a pose but the ground you now stand on.

## Orders

([The Owner](persona_the_owner.md) receives [the Holding](piece_the_holding.md) through [Endowing](process_endowing.md) at [the Draw](place_the_draw.md), where ownership sets [the Reference Point](piece_the_reference_point.md), so keeping the good is the baseline and parting is a loss, and the seat holds to a price above the buyer's.)

## Implementation

([Endowing](process_endowing.md) at [the Draw](place_the_draw.md) moves the reference point to include [the Holding](piece_the_holding.md), so [the Loss](piece_the_loss.md) of parting is felt more than the gain of acquiring, and the owner's honest price rises above what a non-owner would pay, a figure true to the loss and not a bargaining ploy.)

## Targets

- [ ] the good is held at a price true to the loss of parting, above what the seat would have paid to acquire it
- [ ] the price is named honestly, not as a ploy, but as the measure of a loss genuinely felt
- [ ] the reference point set by ownership is recognised as the ground of the value, not a difference in taste
- [ ] the higher price sits above what the good is worth to any non-owner, and the seat does not see this as unreasonable
