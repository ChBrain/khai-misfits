# Plan: Trust the Trade

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the allocator's seat](position_allocator.md), inside [Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Allocator](position_allocator.md)

## Direction

Assign the good to a first holder, by lot or by rule, and step back, trusting that free trade will carry it to whoever values it most, so the choice of first holder need not be laboured. This is the Coase theorem, and it is sound wherever ownership leaves value unchanged. Treat the initial allocation as provisional, a starting point the market will correct, and do not reckon on the possibility that the first holder, once endowed, will be loath to part.

## Orders

([The Allocator](persona_the_allocator.md) assigns [the Holding](piece_the_holding.md) at [the Draw](place_the_draw.md), trusting trade to reallocate it, but through [Freezing](process_freezing.md) the allocation sticks where it was handed, so [the Trade Unmade](piece_the_trade_unmade.md) leaves the good with its first holder.)

## Implementation

([Freezing](process_freezing.md) at [the Draw](place_the_draw.md) sets the allocation to whoever received [the Holding](piece_the_holding.md), because the endowment makes the first holder loath to part and [the Trade Unmade](piece_the_trade_unmade.md) never reshuffles it, so the Coase theorem's independence of the initial allocation fails and the arbitrary start becomes the final rest.)

## Targets

- [ ] the good is assigned to a first holder and the market trusted to reallocate it
- [ ] the initial allocation is treated as provisional, per a theorem sound where ownership leaves value unchanged
- [ ] the endowment makes the first holder loath to part, so the good does not reshuffle by trade
- [ ] the provisional assignment freezes into the final one, the start deciding the end the theorem said it would not
