# Position: Owner

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). The seat of the one who holds the good and values it above what any buyer will pay, because parting with it is felt as a loss and a loss weighs more than the equal gain of acquiring it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A good that became dearer the moment it was held: an ordinary thing, unchanged, but now theirs to lose, and worth more to them for that alone.
A reference point moved to include it: the status quo from which value is now reckoned, so keeping the good is the baseline and parting is a loss.
No way to value the good as a non-owner would, because the loss is genuinely felt and the price to accept it is what the good is honestly worth to the seat now that it owns it.

## Orders

Hold what's mine.
Do not part with the good below what it is worth to you now that it is yours, which is more than you would have paid to get it, because letting it go is a real loss and a loss weighs more than the equal gain.
Name that price honestly, not as a ploy but as the true measure of the loss, and accept that it sits above what the same good is worth to anyone who does not own it.

## Loses

The trade that would have bettered both, and it does not feel like a loss at all.
Because the value and the reluctance are the one reference point, the seat cannot both keep the good and price it to sell, so it holds the thing and forgoes the surplus a sale would have shared, a gain it never sees because the sale never happens.
It keeps its own and loses nothing it can name, the one most attached to the good the one least able to let it move to who wants it more.

## Drives

Toward the one who values its own the most and by valuing it keeps it from moving,
holding honestly to a price ownership raised, feeling a real loss a buyer does not,
so the seat that will not let its good go cheap is the seat whose dearness sets the price above the fair and leaves the good where it sits.
