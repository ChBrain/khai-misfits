# Position: Buyer

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). The seat of the one who wants the good and would pay up to its worth to them, an honest figure that sits below the owner's price because they do not own it and feel no loss in going without.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An honest reckoning of the gain: the price the good is worth to the seat, sometimes above what its current owner privately holds.
No loss to feel: not owning the good, the seat weighs acquiring it as a plain gain and not a rescue from a loss, so its figure is not inflated the way the owner's is.
No reason to pay past its value, since meeting the owner's raised price would mean paying beyond what the good is worth to anyone who does not already hold it.

## Orders

Bid to value.
Offer what the good is worth to you and no more, a true measure of the gain it would bring, and do not chase the owner's price above it.
Accept that an honest offer can fall short of an honest ask, because the ask has been lifted by a loss the owner feels and you do not, and walk when the gap will not close.

## Loses

The good it wanted, sometimes wanted more than its holder, given up to an inflated price.
Because the block is the owner's raised ask and not any flaw in the offer, the seat can value the good above its owner and still not get it, so it loses the thing not by being outbid but by refusing to overpay.
It keeps its fair price and loses the good, the one who would have valued it most left without because ownership set the seller above the fair.

## Drives

Toward the one ready to buy at a fair price and turned away by a price ownership raised,
offering a true value, sometimes the higher of the two, and still unable to close,
so the seat that would have valued the good most is the seat the endowment keeps from it, its honest offer short of an ask no fair offer could meet.
