# Plot: No Sale

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Stall](place_the_stall.md), the ask sits above the bid, and neither side moves.

## Action

Through [Sticking](process_sticking.md), [The Trade Unmade](piece_the_trade_unmade.md) takes its shape: the sale that would have left both sides better off does not happen, and the good stays with [The Owner](persona_the_owner.md) rather than moving to [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md), who values it more. The scene closes with the good unsold and the surplus foregone: a gain no one realises, because nothing visibly went wrong.

## Stage

The trade fails not for want of a willing buyer but because ownership lifted the owner's price above the buyer's, so fewer trades occur than a frictionless market predicts (Kahneman, Knetsch, and Thaler). Keep the cut clean: this is not the Market for Lemons. No hidden quality is feared and nothing about the good is unknown to either side. The block is bare possession, not information.

## Tension

A trade both would have gained from is left undone. The loss is invisible, because nothing was taken from anyone.
