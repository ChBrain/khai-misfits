# Piece: The Holding

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). The good once it is owned (Thaler): an ordinary thing, a mug, a ticket, a house, whose worth to its holder rises the moment it is theirs, not for anything changed in it but for the bare fact of possession.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Draw](place_the_draw.md), where the good is first handed to [The Owner](persona_the_owner.md) through [Endowing](process_endowing.md). Offered later at [The Stall](place_the_stall.md), where its raised worth is set against a buyer's price.

## Load Bearing

The object is unchanged, identical to the one a buyer would weigh; only its ownership differs, and that alone lifts its value to the holder (Thaler). Guard against reading the raised value as anything in the good itself, a quality or a use: it is possession and not the object that changed.

## Apparent

Just a thing someone owns, worth what it is worth.

Received by [The Owner](persona_the_owner.md) through [Endowing](process_endowing.md), assigned by [The Allocator](persona_the_allocator.md). Sets [The Reference Point](piece_the_reference_point.md), in [The Mug in Hand](plot_the_mug_in_hand.md).
