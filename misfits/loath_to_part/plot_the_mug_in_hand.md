# Plot: The Mug in Hand

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Draw](place_the_draw.md), a good is handed to someone who did not choose it: a mug dealt at random, no more.

## Action

[The Owner](persona_the_owner.md), from [the owner's seat](position_owner.md), receives [The Holding](piece_the_holding.md) through [Endowing](process_endowing.md). The moment it is theirs its worth rises: ownership sets [The Reference Point](piece_the_reference_point.md), so that keeping the mug becomes the baseline and parting with it would be a loss. The scene closes on the good unchanged, and yet worth more, for nothing but the having.

## Stage

The endowment effect: merely owning a thing raises the value one places on it, above what the same thing was worth moments before it was handed over (Thaler). The mechanism is loss aversion and reference-dependence: once owned, the good becomes the point from which gains and losses are measured, and a loss looms larger than the equivalent gain (Tversky and Kahneman). No one has erred and no one has been fooled; the price simply moves with the having.

## Tension

Nothing in the mug has changed. It is already worth more to the one now holding it, and worth exactly what it was to everyone else.
