# Persona: The Buyer

## Taxonomy

[Buyer](position_buyer.md). The one who wants the good and would pay up to its worth to them, an honest figure that sits below the owner's price because they do not own it and feel no loss in going without.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Willing, sober, honest.
Wants the good and would gladly buy it, sometimes wants it more than the current owner does, and offers what it is worth to them.
No stinginess in it: the price offered is a true reckoning of the gain acquiring the good would bring, and there is no reason to pay past that.

## Action

Bids to value on [Bid to Value](plan_bid_to_value.md), through [Sticking](process_sticking.md) at [the Stall](place_the_stall.md), where its honest offer meets the owner's higher ask and [the Trade Unmade](piece_the_trade_unmade.md) is the result, the sale that would have bettered both left undone.
Cannot reach the owner's price without overpaying, because that price is inflated by a loss the owner feels and the buyer does not, so meeting it would mean paying past what the good is worth to anyone who does not already hold it.
Does what a sober buyer does: it offers what the good is worth to it and no more, and the offer, honest, falls short of an ask that ownership has lifted.

## Shadow

Not mean for holding its price and not the cause of the failed trade: the Buyer offers a true value, sometimes above what the owner privately holds, and is turned away only by a price the endowment raised.
Because the block is the owner's inflated ask and not any flaw in the offer, the Buyer can want the good more than its holder and still not get it, so the one who would value the good most can be the one the effect keeps from it.
It is the one ready to buy at a fair price, and the one left without because ownership set the seller's price above the fair.

## Tell

Says it would pay what the thing is worth and no more, and is being entirely reasonable, and still cannot close the sale.
Cannot be blamed for the gap, because its offer is honest and the shortfall is the owner's inflation, so it bids soundly and walks away, the good it wanted staying with a holder who valued it less.
