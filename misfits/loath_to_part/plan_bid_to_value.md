# Plan: Bid to Value

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the buyer's seat](position_buyer.md), inside [Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Buyer](position_buyer.md)

## Direction

Offer what the good is worth to you and no more, a true measure of the gain acquiring it would bring, sometimes above what its current owner privately holds. Do not chase the owner's price past your value, because that price has been lifted by a loss the owner feels and you do not, and paying it would mean paying beyond what the good is worth to anyone who does not already hold it. Where the ask will not come down to a fair offer, walk.

## Orders

([The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) makes an honest offer that, through [Sticking](process_sticking.md) at [the Stall](place_the_stall.md), meets the owner's higher ask across [the Gap](piece_the_gap.md), so [the Trade Unmade](piece_the_trade_unmade.md) leaves the sale undone.)

## Implementation

([The Gap](piece_the_gap.md) at [the Stall](place_the_stall.md) sits between the buyer's honest offer and the owner's raised ask; [Sticking](process_sticking.md) is the sale not closing across it, so [the Trade Unmade](piece_the_trade_unmade.md) forms, the buyer refusing to overpay and the good staying with a holder who may value it less.)

## Targets

- [ ] the offer is set to the good's true worth to the seat, the gain of acquiring, and no higher
- [ ] the owner's inflated ask is not chased past that value
- [ ] where the ask will not fall to a fair offer, the seat walks rather than overpays
- [ ] the good is lost not by being outbid but by an ask ownership lifted above any fair offer
