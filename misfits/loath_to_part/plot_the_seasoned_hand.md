# Plot: The Seasoned Hand

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Floor](place_the_floor.md), a dealer works among goods that are stock, not keepsakes.

## Action

[The Trader](persona_the_trader.md), from [the trader's seat](position_trader.md), runs [Hold to Sell](plan_hold_to_sell.md) through [Seasoning](process_seasoning.md), holding [The Exchange](piece_the_exchange.md): pricing a holding at its market worth and letting it go without a pang, the gap between ask and bid gone. The scene closes with trades clearing at prices where an ordinary owner would have balked.

## Stage

Experienced traders, and goods held for exchange rather than for use, show little or no endowment effect; market experience attenuates it, so the effect is bound rather than universal (List). Part of the measured gap in the original experiments is also contested as an artifact of how willingness-to-accept and willingness-to-pay were elicited, rather than a pure effect of ownership (Plott and Zeiler).

## Tension

The effect that freezes an ordinary owner scarcely touches the seasoned hand. The trap, it turns out, has an edge.
