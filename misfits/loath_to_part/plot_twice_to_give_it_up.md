# Plot: Twice to Give It Up

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Stall](place_the_stall.md), owner and buyer meet over the same good: one to sell it, one to buy it.

## Action

[The Owner](persona_the_owner.md) runs [Hold What's Mine](plan_hold_whats_mine.md), carrying [The Loss](piece_the_loss.md) and naming a price to part with the good. [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md), from [the buyer's seat](position_buyer.md), runs [Bid to Value](plan_bid_to_value.md) and names a price to acquire it. [The Gap](piece_the_gap.md) opens between them, wide: the owner asks about twice what the buyer will pay. The scene closes with the two prices set far apart, and neither party unreasonable.

## Stage

In the mug and token experiments, owners demanded roughly twice what buyers would pay for the identical good; the willingness-to-accept and willingness-to-pay gap is opened by loss aversion (Kahneman, Knetsch, and Thaler; Knetsch). Buyer and owner are drawn from the same pool of people, so the gap is not a difference in taste between two kinds of person. It is the endowment alone.

## Tension

Both name an honest price. The honest prices do not meet.
