# Position: Trader

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). The seat of the seasoned exchanger who holds goods as stock and not as keepsakes, showing little or none of the effect, the bound that keeps the trap from being a law of all ownership.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A practised detachment: goods held as inventory bound for sale, so a holding in the seat's hand is stock and not a possession, and parting costs no more than the price it fetches.
A reference point worn loose: repeated trading has kept ownership from gripping, so a good's worth to the seat is its market worth and not a figure inflated by having it.
No special virtue and no coldness, only the seasoning that comes of treating every good as held-for-exchange, which most owners of most goods never acquire.

## Orders

Hold to sell.
Treat every holding as stock bound for exchange, not as a thing to keep, so the reference point never forms around it and parting is no loss.
Price each good at its market worth and let it go for that, clearing the trade an ordinary owner would balk at, and know the exemption is bought with experience, not owed.

## Loses

Nothing on the floor, and yet the seat marks the trap's edge rather than its absence.
Because the ease comes only of a seasoning most owners never get, the seat cannot lend its detachment to the owner of a home or an heirloom, so what it shows is the bound of the effect and not that the effect is unreal.
It keeps its clear trades and loses any power to free the ordinary owner, its exemption exactly the practice the keepsake's holder has no way to buy.

## Drives

Toward the one the endowment scarcely touches and whose exemption is bought, not owed,
pricing to the market and trading freely where an ordinary owner would freeze,
so the seat that trades without a pang is the seat that marks the bound of the trap, real for the keepsake and gone for the stock, a law of neither.
