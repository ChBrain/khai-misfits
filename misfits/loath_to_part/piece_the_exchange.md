# Piece: The Exchange

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). The boundary and the hedge (List; Plott and Zeiler): the market experience and the goods-held-for-trade that shrink or erase the gap, so the effect is real for ordinary owners and fades for seasoned traders and routine goods, bound and not universal.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Floor](place_the_floor.md), where goods are stock and not keepsakes, held by [The Trader](persona_the_trader.md) through [Seasoning](process_seasoning.md).

## Load Bearing

This is the hedge: experienced traders and people handling goods held for exchange show little or no endowment effect, so the trap is real but bounded, not a law of all ownership (List); and part of the measured gap is contested as an artifact of the elicitation (Plott and Zeiler). Guard against reading the misfit as a claim that all trade fails or that owners can never sell: seasoning and holding-for-exchange dissolve the gap.

## Apparent

Just a market working normally, ask and bid meeting.
