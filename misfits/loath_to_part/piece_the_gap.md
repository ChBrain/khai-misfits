# Piece: The Gap

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). The observable (Kahneman, Knetsch, and Thaler): the wedge between willingness-to-accept, the sum demanded to give the good up, and willingness-to-pay, the sum offered to get it, the measurable size of the endowment effect and the room in which trades fail.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Named at [The Stall](place_the_stall.md), where [The Owner](persona_the_owner.md)'s asking price and [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md)'s offer are set apart.

## Load Bearing

Owners in the mug experiments demanded about twice what buyers would pay for the same good, a gap wide enough that trades which should occur do not (Kahneman, Knetsch, and Thaler). Guard against reading the gap as a difference in the good's worth to two people with different tastes: buyer and owner are drawn from the same pool, and only the endowment differs.

## Apparent

A normal spread between ask and bid, the ordinary distance in any haggle.
