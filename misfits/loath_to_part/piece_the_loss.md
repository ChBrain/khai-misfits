# Piece: The Loss

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). The engine (Thaler; Tversky and Kahneman): the coding of giving up the owned good as a loss, and the asymmetry by which losses loom larger than equal gains, the reason the price to surrender exceeds the price to acquire.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Felt at [The Stall](place_the_stall.md), when [The Owner](persona_the_owner.md) is asked to part with the good, carried there on [Hold What's Mine](plan_hold_whats_mine.md).

## Load Bearing

A loss of a given size weighs more than a gain of the same size, so surrendering the good hurts more than acquiring it would please, and the owner therefore demands more to part than a buyer will pay (Thaler; Tversky and Kahneman). Guard against reading the owner's higher price as greed or a bargaining ploy: the loss is genuinely felt, and the price is what the good is now worth to them.

## Apparent

Ordinary reluctance to sell, mistaken for holding out for more.
