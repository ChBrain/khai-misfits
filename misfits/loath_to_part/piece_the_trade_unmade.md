# Piece: The Trade Unmade

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). The consequence (Kahneman, Knetsch, and Thaler): the mutually beneficial exchange that does not happen because ownership lifted the seller's price above the buyer's, the good stuck with its holder and the Coase theorem's allocation-independence broken.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Fails at [The Stall](place_the_stall.md), where [The Owner](persona_the_owner.md) and [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) part without a sale. Freezes at [The Draw](place_the_draw.md), where the allocation set by [The Allocator](persona_the_allocator.md) then holds.

## Load Bearing

This is the load-bearing harm: a trade that would have made both sides better off left undone, and goods coming to rest with whoever first held them rather than with whoever values them most (Kahneman, Knetsch, and Thaler). Guard against reading it as no loss because nothing was taken: the foregone surplus is real and invisible, a gain that was never realised.

## Apparent

Simply a sale that did not go through, easily read as no one wanting it enough.
