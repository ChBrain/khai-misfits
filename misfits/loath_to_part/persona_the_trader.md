# Persona: The Trader

## Taxonomy

[Trader](position_trader.md). The seasoned exchanger who holds goods as stock and not as keepsakes, and shows little or none of the effect, pricing a holding at its market worth and letting it go without a pang, the bound that keeps the trap from being a law.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Practised, detached, clear.
Handles goods as things held for exchange, so a mug in its hand is inventory and not a possession, and parting with it costs no more than the price it fetches.
No coldness in it and no special virtue: repeated trading has simply worn the reference point loose, so ownership no longer inflates what a good is worth to it.

## Action

Holds to sell on [Hold to Sell](plan_hold_to_sell.md), through [Seasoning](process_seasoning.md) at [the Floor](place_the_floor.md), holding [the Exchange](piece_the_exchange.md), pricing each good at its market worth and letting it go without the pang that grips an ordinary owner, so trades clear.
Cannot be gripped by the endowment because it never lets the reference point form around a holding, treating every good as bound for sale, so parting registers as no loss and the ask does not rise above the bid.
Does what a seasoned hand does: it prices to the market and trades freely, showing that the effect which freezes an ordinary owner has an edge and is not a law of all ownership (List).

## Shadow

Not immune by nature and not proof the effect is unreal: the Trader escapes it only through the very experience most owners of most goods never get, so its ease marks the bound of the trap, not its absence.
Because seasoning is what dissolves the grip, the one who can trade freely is the one for whom the good was never a keepsake, and that detachment is exactly what the owner of a home or an heirloom cannot summon.
It is the one the endowment scarcely touches, and the one whose exemption is bought with a practice the ordinary owner has no way to buy.

## Tell

Says a mug is worth what it fetches and hands it over for the price, feeling no loss, and takes that ease as ordinary.
Cannot lend the ease to the owner of a thing held to keep, because the reference point re-forms around whatever is not held for exchange, so it clears its trades and the ordinary owner, beside it, still cannot part with theirs.
