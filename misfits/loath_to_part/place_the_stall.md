# Place: The Stall

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). Where owner and buyer meet to trade, the good offered, the price named, the sale made or not made. Hosts [Twice to Give It Up](plot_twice_to_give_it_up.md), [No Sale](plot_no_sale.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The owner naming a price to part and the buyer naming a price to acquire, the two set far apart.

## Holds

[The Gap](piece_the_gap.md) between what the owner will accept and the buyer will pay, and [The Loss](piece_the_loss.md) the owner feels at parting.

## Offers

A trade that should happen, both sides able to gain from it.

## Withheld

The trade itself, since the owner's price sits above the buyer's and neither is being unreasonable.
