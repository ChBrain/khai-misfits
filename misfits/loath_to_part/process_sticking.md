# Process: Sticking

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). Borne at [The Stall](place_the_stall.md), precipitated by [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) bidding below the owner's ask, [The Trade Unmade](piece_the_trade_unmade.md), in [No Sale](plot_no_sale.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The owner's price, lifted by ownership, sitting above the buyer's honest value.

## Direction

From a mutually beneficial trade that should happen toward one that does not, the good staying where it sits.

## Lever

The willingness-to-accept and willingness-to-pay gap opened by loss aversion, about twice the price to part as to acquire (Kahneman, Knetsch, and Thaler).
