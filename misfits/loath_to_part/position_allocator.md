# Position: Allocator

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). The seat that assigns who first holds the good, trusting trade to move it to whoever values it most, and unwittingly deciding where it comes to rest because the endowment makes the first holder loath to part.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The power to hand the good to someone first: the lot, the rule, the default that lands a holding on one party rather than another.
A sound belief behind the power: the Coase theorem, that with free trade the first assignment does not decide the last, because the good will move to whoever values it most whichever way it started.
No sight of the flaw in that belief, because the theorem holds wherever ownership leaves value unchanged, and only the endowment effect breaks that premise.

## Orders

Trust the trade.
Assign the good to a first holder, by lot or by rule, and step back, trusting that trade will carry it to whoever values it most, so the choice of first holder need not be laboured.
Treat the initial allocation as provisional, a starting point the market will correct, which is what the theorem promises.

## Loses

The irrelevance it counted on: the first assignment, meant to be idle, turns out to decide the whole.
Because the good will not move once held, the seat's provisional choice becomes final, so the allocation it thought the market would correct is frozen where it fell, and the good rests with who was handed it, not who wants it most.
It keeps its fair procedure and loses the outcome it trusted the procedure to reach, the one who thought the start did not matter the one whose start settled the end.

## Drives

Toward the one who trusted a sound theorem and was caught where its premise failed,
assigning in good faith and stepping back, believing trade would correct any start,
so the seat that thought the first holder did not matter is the seat whose first assignment, made idle in theory, decides in fact where the good comes to rest.
