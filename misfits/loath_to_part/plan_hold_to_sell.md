# Plan: Hold to Sell

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the trader's seat](position_trader.md), inside [Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Trader](position_trader.md)

## Direction

Treat every holding as stock bound for exchange, not as a thing to keep, so the reference point never forms around it and parting is no loss. Price each good at its market worth and let it go for that, clearing the trade an ordinary owner would balk at. Know that this ease is the seasoning of repeated trading and not a virtue owed, that it dissolves the gap only for goods held for exchange, and that it cannot be lent to the owner of a thing held to keep.

## Orders

([The Trader](persona_the_trader.md) holds [the Exchange](piece_the_exchange.md) through [Seasoning](process_seasoning.md) at [the Floor](place_the_floor.md), pricing each good at its market worth and letting it go without the pang that grips an ordinary owner, so trades clear.)

## Implementation

([Seasoning](process_seasoning.md) at [the Floor](place_the_floor.md) keeps ownership from gripping, so [the Exchange](piece_the_exchange.md) prices a holding at its market worth and no reference point inflates it, the gap gone and trades clearing, the bound that keeps the effect from being a law of all ownership, real still for the keepsake the floor never handles.)
