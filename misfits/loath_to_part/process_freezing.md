# Process: Freezing

## Taxonomy

[Loath to Part](play_loath_to_part.md). Borne at [The Draw](place_the_draw.md), precipitated by [The Allocator](persona_the_allocator.md) assigning [The Holding](piece_the_holding.md), in [Where It Was Handed](plot_where_it_was_handed.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The good being first assigned to someone.

## Direction

From an allocation that trade should reshuffle toward whoever values the good most, to one frozen wherever it was first handed.

## Lever

The endowment makes the initial allocation sticky, so the Coase theorem's independence of first ownership fails and where the good was handed is where it stays (Kahneman, Knetsch, and Thaler).
