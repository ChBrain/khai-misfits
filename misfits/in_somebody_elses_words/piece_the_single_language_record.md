## Load Bearing

It removes the comparison, so nothing downstream can establish what the misfit consists of.
The transcript is not wrong and omits nothing that was said in the language it keeps. What it does not hold is the original utterance, so an appeal, a review or a later reader has one text and no second text to set beside it. A court records its own language because that is the language it acts in, and a two-language record would require a second transcriber, a second certification and a second set of disputes about it. The consequence is that the durable document is precisely the one that cannot show the substitution.
