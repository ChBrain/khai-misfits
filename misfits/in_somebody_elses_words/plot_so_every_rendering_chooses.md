# Plot: So Every Rendering Chooses

## Taxonomy

[In Somebody Else's Words](play_in_somebody_elses_words.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Seconds to render an answer with three hedges in it.

## Action

[Rendering the answer](process_rendering_the_answer.md): [the interpreter](persona_the_interpreter.md) trades fluency against register at [the witness box](place_the_witness_box.md), several times a sentence.

## Stage

A faithful rendering that is faithful in one of two ways.

## Tension

There is no neutral version, so the choice is continuous, competent and unrecorded.
Keep every hedge and the witness reads as incoherent in a language they do not speak. Drop them and the witness reads as more certain than they were. Both renderings are defensible, both are what a good interpreter produces under time, and nothing in the room, the code or the transcript marks which was taken.
