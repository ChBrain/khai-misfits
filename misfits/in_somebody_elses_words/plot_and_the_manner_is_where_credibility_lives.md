# Plot: And the Manner Is Where Credibility Lives

## Taxonomy

[In Somebody Else's Words](play_in_somebody_elses_words.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A witness hedging where the memory is partial, and pausing where the question is hard.

## Action

[The Pragmatic Layer](piece_the_pragmatic_layer.md) is identified at [the witness box](place_the_witness_box.md): hedging, hesitation, politeness, length and voice, running beside the content.

## Stage

A second channel, informative, and the one the room is weighing.

## Tension

The manner is not ornament, which is why it cannot be the half that is discarded for convenience.
A hedge tells a listener how firmly a thing is held, and a listener who attends to it is reading correctly rather than being misled. That is the whole difficulty: the layer is genuinely informative, so preserving it matters, and it is also the layer a second speaker must re-produce from scratch.
