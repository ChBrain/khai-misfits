# Play: In Somebody Else's Words

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

In Somebody Else's Words.
An interpreter is required to convey what was said without addition or omission, and to be invisible. Both requirements are right. But hedging, hesitation and register are where a room reads credibility from, and no rendering carries them and the fluency at once, so the interpreter chooses several times a sentence and the fact-finder weighs the choice as if it were the witness.

## Arc

The bend is this: the party being assessed for credibility has no access to the object being assessed, and nobody in the room did anything wrong.
It begins with two duties that are each correct.
[The Code Requires Two Things](plot_the_code_requires_two_things.md). [The Two Duties](piece_the_two_duties.md) are accuracy and invisibility, and a code missing either would be worse.
And the manner is not ornament.
[And the Manner Is Where Credibility Lives](plot_and_the_manner_is_where_credibility_lives.md). [The Pragmatic Layer](piece_the_pragmatic_layer.md) tells a listener how firmly a thing is held, and attending to it is reading correctly.
So there is no neutral rendering.
[So Every Rendering Chooses](plot_so_every_rendering_chooses.md). Keep the hedges and the witness reads as incoherent; drop them and the witness reads as more certain than they were.
Then [the room reads the choice as the witness](plot_and_the_room_reads_the_choice_as_the_witness.md).
[The Fact-Finder](persona_the_fact_finder.md) is instructed to weigh manner, and [the advocate](persona_the_advocate.md) presses a pause that may be processing.
And [the record keeps only one language](plot_the_record_keeps_only_one_language.md). [The Single-Language Record](piece_the_single_language_record.md) is accurate, durable, and holds no original to compare against.
And [the guise shows it moves the verdict](plot_and_the_guise_shows_it_moves_the_verdict.md). [The Researcher](persona_the_researcher.md) demonstrates the effect and closes the only clean repair.

## Stakes

Whether a person can be assessed on their own account of themselves when the account reaching the room is authored twice.
Every plot moves the same way: toward two duties that are each right, a manner that is genuinely informative, a rendering with no neutral version, a room performing its instructed task on merged material, a record that keeps one side, and a demonstration that closes the obvious repair, and away from any account in which the interpreter was careless or the court indifferent. The production cannot fault the interpreter, who is the only party who can see the problem and has no move that avoids it; and it cannot fault the fact-finder, who is doing precisely what the role requires and would be doing less if they ignored how an answer was given. What is left to fight over is not whether the rendering was accurate but whose manner the room was weighing.
