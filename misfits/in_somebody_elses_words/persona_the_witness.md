# Persona: The Witness

## Taxonomy

[Witness](position_witness.md). The one weighed on words that are not theirs.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person answering questions in the language they think in, in a room that will decide something about them in another.

## Action

Answers.
Hesitates where the question is hard, hedges where the memory is partial, and is heard doing neither.

## Shadow

Produces the signal and does not own the version of it the room receives.
Every hedge and pause is doing work: it marks uncertainty honestly and is the thing a careful listener wants. What the room hears is a rendering, and the seat cannot check it, cannot object to it and in most cases cannot follow it. So the person being assessed for credibility has no access to the object being assessed, which is not a disadvantage in the proceedings but a fact about the proceedings.

## Tell

Says it exactly, and is judged on the second version.
