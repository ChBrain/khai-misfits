# Persona: The Fact-Finder

## Taxonomy

[Fact-Finder](position_fact_finder.md). The one assessing credibility off a rendering.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A juror or a judge, listening, and doing what the role explicitly asks: forming a view of whether this person is to be believed.

## Action

Weighs the manner along with the matter.
Cannot separate the two, and is not asked to.

## Shadow

Performs the assigned task competently on material that has passed through somebody else.
Assessing credibility from demeanour and delivery is not a lapse: it is what the seat is instructed to do, and no instruction tells anybody to disregard hesitation, because hesitation is genuinely informative. The seat has no way to know which pauses were the witness's, and no way to ask. What reaches it is a single stream in one language, and the room offers nothing that would let it be decomposed.

## Tell

Does exactly what was asked, with the layers already mixed.
