# Place: The Record

## Taxonomy

[In Somebody Else's Words](play_in_somebody_elses_words.md). One language, kept perfectly, and the other not kept at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A transcript. Every question and every answer, in the language of the court, complete and accurate.
No omission and no error: what was said in that language is what appears.

## Holds

[The Advocate](persona_the_advocate.md), building on qualifiers and pauses as they appear here.
[The Single-Language Record](piece_the_single_language_record.md), which is the whole of what any appeal can read.

## Offers

A complete and checkable account of the proceedings, in the language the proceedings were conducted in.

## Withheld

The original.
The place keeps the rendering and not the thing rendered, so a later reader has no object to compare against and no way to ask whether a hedge was the witness's. That is not a defect anybody introduced: a court records its own language, which is the language it acts in, and a transcript in two languages would need a second transcriber, a second certification and a second set of disputes. The consequence is that the one document that outlasts the hearing is the one that cannot show what this misfit is about.
