# Process: Rendering the Answer

## Taxonomy

[In Somebody Else's Words](play_in_somebody_elses_words.md). The choice, made well, several times a sentence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A question put, and an answer given in the other language.
[The Interpreter](persona_the_interpreter.md), holding [Interpreter](position_interpreter.md), at [the witness box](place_the_witness_box.md).

## Direction

Toward a faithful version of the answer in the language of the court.

## Lever

Fluency and register, which cannot both be preserved and must be traded in the seconds available.

## Echo

An accurate rendering that carries somebody's manner, and no marker anywhere saying whose.
