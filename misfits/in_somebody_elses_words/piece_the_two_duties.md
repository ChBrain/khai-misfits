## Load Bearing

Accuracy and invisibility are each right on their own and pull apart on every sentence with a hedge in it.
The codes require the interpreter to convey what was said without addition or omission, and to be a conduit rather than a participant. Neither could be dropped: an interpreter licensed to summarise would be deciding the case, and one licensed to advocate would be a party. But a hedge is content in one sense and manner in another, so preserving it is an addition to fluency and dropping it is an omission from the answer, and the seat is out of compliance whichever way it moves.
