# Process: Making the Record

## Taxonomy

[In Somebody Else's Words](play_in_somebody_elses_words.md). Keeping one language, and losing the comparison.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A hearing under way, and a transcriber working in the language of the court.
[The Advocate](persona_the_advocate.md), holding [Advocate](position_advocate.md), at [the record](place_the_record.md).

## Direction

Toward a complete and checkable account of the proceedings.

## Lever

The court's own language, which is the language it acts in and the only one it certifies.

## Echo

A document that outlasts the hearing, is accurate, and holds no object to compare the rendering against.
