# Position: Interpreter

## Taxonomy

[In Somebody Else's Words](play_in_somebody_elses_words.md). The seat holding two correct duties that diverge.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Certification, a code requiring accuracy and impartiality, and a few seconds per answer.

## Orders

Render what was said. Add nothing. Be invisible.

## Loses

Any rendering that is neutral.
Manner and matter are two channels and no version of the sentence carries both, so invisibility is the one instruction that cannot be obeyed.

## Drives

Toward a faithful answer that is faithful in one of two ways and must pick.
