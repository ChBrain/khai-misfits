# Persona: The Researcher

## Taxonomy

[Researcher](position_researcher.md). The one who shows the effect is real.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Recordings of real proceedings, and an experiment in which the same testimony is presented in more than one interpreted style.

## Action

Varies the style, holds the content fixed, and measures what listeners conclude.

## Shadow

Establishes the finding completely and supplies no way to act on it.
The design is the strong one: hold the content constant, vary only the pragmatic layer, and read the difference in how listeners rate the witness. It shows the effect is there and is large enough to matter, which is what takes this from a plausible worry to a demonstrated one. What it cannot produce is a rendering that has no style, because there is no such rendering, so the result names the problem exactly and rules out the only repair that would have been clean.

## Tell

Proves it moves, and cannot say which way to hold it.
