# Persona: The Advocate

## Taxonomy

[Advocate](position_advocate.md). The one who examines the rendering as if it were the answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A line of questions built to open a gap, and a transcript in which every answer appears in one language.

## Action

Presses on a hesitation.
Builds on a hedge, or on its absence.

## Shadow

Is doing the job properly and is working on material one layer removed from the source.
Cross-examination is supposed to press exactly here, on the pause before the answer and the qualifier inside it, and a seat that ignored them would be doing less than the role requires. The difficulty is that the pause the seat presses on may be the interpreter's processing and the qualifier may be the interpreter's grammar, and nothing in the room marks which. So the technique is applied correctly to an object it was designed for and is no longer holding.

## Tell

Presses on the right thing, one speaker late.
