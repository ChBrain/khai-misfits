# Persona: The Interpreter

## Taxonomy

[Interpreter](position_interpreter.md). The one required to do two things that diverge.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A certified professional standing beside the witness box, holding a code of conduct that requires accuracy and impartiality at once.

## Action

Renders the answer.
Chooses, several times a sentence, between what was said and how it was said.

## Shadow

Is the only party in the room who can see the problem, and has no move that avoids it.
The code is not badly written and the training is not thin: accuracy and invisibility are exactly the right requirements, and any code lacking either would be worse. What cannot be delivered is both, because a witness's hedging, hesitation and register are not decoration on the content but a second channel running beside it, and a rendering either carries them and reads as incoherent or drops them and reads as a fluency the witness did not have. The seat makes that choice continuously, competently, and without a defensible way to record having made it.

## Tell

Does the job correctly and cannot do it neutrally.
