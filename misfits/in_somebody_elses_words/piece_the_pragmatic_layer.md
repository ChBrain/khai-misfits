## Load Bearing

It is where credibility lives, which is why it cannot be treated as the disposable half of the sentence.
Hedging, hesitation, politeness, the length of an answer, the use of the passive: these are not ornament on the content but a running commentary on how firmly it is held. A listener uses them, correctly, and a witness who hedges honestly is telling the room something true. That is exactly why a rendering that smooths them produces a more confident witness than the one in the box, and a rendering that keeps them all produces a less coherent one.
