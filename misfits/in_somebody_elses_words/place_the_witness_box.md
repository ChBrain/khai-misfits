# Place: The Witness Box

## Taxonomy

[In Somebody Else's Words](play_in_somebody_elses_words.md). Where both channels are present and only one survives the crossing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A question in one language, a pause, an answer, and the same answer again in another.
Ordinary competent work: the interpreter is certified, the terminology is right, and nobody in the room detects an error.

## Holds

[The Witness](persona_the_witness.md), producing hedges and hesitation that are doing honest work.
[The Interpreter](persona_the_interpreter.md), choosing between them and fluency, several times a sentence.
[The Pragmatic Layer](piece_the_pragmatic_layer.md), which is what the choosing is about.

## Offers

Access to testimony that would otherwise be unavailable to the court at all, which is why interpreters are there.

## Withheld

The witness's own manner, in any version anybody can inspect.
The place is where two channels arrive and one is re-made in transit, and there is no version of the crossing that leaves both intact. So what the room receives is a single stream whose fluency, pace and hedging are jointly authored, and nothing in the setting marks the seam. The absence is not a failure of the place: it is what a place for rendering one language into another is.
