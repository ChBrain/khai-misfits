## Cue

## Stage

A durable document holding the rendering and not the thing rendered.

## Tension

The one artefact that outlasts the hearing is the one that cannot show the substitution.
Nothing was omitted from it: everything said in the court's language appears. But an appeal, a review or a later reader has one text and no second text to set beside it, so the question of whose hedge it was cannot be asked afterwards either. A court records the language it acts in, and a two-language record would need a second transcriber, a second certification and its own disputes.
