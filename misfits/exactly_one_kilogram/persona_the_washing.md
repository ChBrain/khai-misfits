# Persona: The Washing

## Taxonomy

[Washing](position_washing.md). The cleaning written into the definition, which removes more than it reveals.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A prescribed procedure: chamois soaked in ether, then ethanol, then steam. Written down to the cloth and the solvent, performed by hand.
Part of the definition itself, since the unit was the mass of the artefact immediately after it.

## Action

Removes the accumulated surface contamination, which is exactly what a reference object needs.
Removes about seventeen micrograms from the prototype, and thirteen to seventeen from each copy.

## Shadow

Takes off a quantity of the same order as the drift it exists to defeat.
A century of divergence measures around fifty micrograms and one wash accounts for roughly a third of that, so the operation that establishes the exact mass is also one of the largest single perturbations of it: the reference is only exactly itself in the moments just after being altered by the act that made it so.

## Tell

Is the definition and the interference at once, and cannot be dropped from either role.
