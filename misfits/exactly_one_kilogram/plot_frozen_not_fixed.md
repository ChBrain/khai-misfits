# Plot: Frozen, Not Fixed

## Taxonomy

[Exactly One Kilogram](play_exactly_one_kilogram.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

2019, and decades of work on the Kibble balance and on spheres of isotopically enriched silicon converging well enough to displace an object.

## Action

[Fix it to the universe](plan_fix_it_to_the_universe.md): [The Keepers](persona_the_keepers.md) retire the artefact and set [the fixed number](piece_the_fixed_number.md), the Planck constant, to an exact value, untying the kilogram from any object permanently.

## Stage

The value is chosen to match the prototype as it stood at the changeover, because any other choice would have shifted every mass measurement on Earth at once.

## Tension

The correction ratifies the error rather than reversing it, and was right to.
A century of accumulated drift is not undone: it is adopted as correct and made permanent, because continuity had to be preferred over correctness when the alternative was a discontinuity propagating into every weighed transaction in the world. And the liberation carries its own distance: realising the new definition takes a Kibble balance or an enriched silicon sphere, instruments perhaps a handful of laboratories possess, so the ordinary user now stands further from the definition than when it was an object in a safe they could in principle have gone to look at.
