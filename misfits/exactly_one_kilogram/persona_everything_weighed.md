# Persona: Everything Weighed

## Taxonomy

[Everything on the Chain](position_everything_on_the_chain.md). Every mass on Earth, riding an unbroken chain back to the safe.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Every scale, every dose, every cargo, every transaction by weight, on a chain of calibrations running national standard by national standard back to one object outside Paris.
The largest party in the play, and the one with no representation in it.

## Action

Measures, correctly, by a definition that is correct by construction.
Moves, exactly and instantly, whenever the reference moves.

## Shadow

Absorbs the entire error and cannot register any of it.
Each mass is defined by its ratio to the thing that shifted, so the shift propagates through every measurement in perfect proportion and perfect silence: a pharmacist's balance and a national standard both stay right, in the only sense of right available, and an error distributed across everything is an error observable nowhere.

## Tell

Is where the whole of the consequence went, and is the one persona that never appears in any comparison.
