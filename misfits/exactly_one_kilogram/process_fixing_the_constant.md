# Process: Fixing the Constant

## Taxonomy

[Exactly One Kilogram](play_exactly_one_kilogram.md). The 2019 redefinition, freeing the unit and keeping the drift.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Decades of work on the Kibble balance and on spheres of isotopically enriched silicon, converging well enough to replace an object.
[The Keepers](persona_the_keepers.md), retiring the artefact they had kept.

## Direction

Toward [the fixed number](piece_the_fixed_number.md): the Planck constant set to an exact value, so the kilogram is tied to the universe rather than to a lump of metal.
Toward a value chosen to match the prototype as it stood, because any other choice would have shifted every mass on Earth at once.

## Lever

Continuity, preferred over correction deliberately and rightly, since a discontinuity would have propagated into every weighed transaction in the world.

## Echo

A century of drift adopted as correct and made permanent, and a definition realised by instruments perhaps a handful of laboratories possess.
