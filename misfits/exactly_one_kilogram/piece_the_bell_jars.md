## Owner

## Place

## Load Bearing

They are the reason the drift is slow and the reason nobody watched it.
Protection and isolation are the same act here: each layer that keeps contamination off the surface also keeps the object out of reach, so the piece that limits the rate of change is also the piece that limits the number of times anyone can measure the change, and there is no arrangement of glass that does one without the other.

## Apparent

Three covers of glass, nested, doing exactly what covers do.
Simple, effective, and entirely correct as engineering.

## Yearbook

Slowed the accumulation to something like a microgram a year, which is why the artefact lasted a century as a usable reference at all.
Did not stop it, because a surface in any atmosphere adsorbs, and because the alloy outgasses from the inside where no cover reaches.
