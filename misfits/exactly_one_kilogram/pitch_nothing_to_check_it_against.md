# Pitch: Nothing to Check It Against

## Taxonomy

[Exactly One Kilogram](play_exactly_one_kilogram.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Meticulous and reverent. A vault, keys held separately, three nested bell jars, a cleaning procedure written down to the cloth and the solvent, and a century of custody that no one has ever faulted.
An object that was, on every single day of its life, exactly what it was supposed to be.

## Undertow

Under the reverence, a definition with no outside.
The care that protected the artefact is the reason it was observed three times in a hundred years, and the ritual that made it exact removed seventeen micrograms of it, which is about a third of the drift the whole discipline was hunting.

## Nerve

The vertigo of an object that cannot be wrong, in a world where everything else is measured against it.

## Echo

Afterward: fifty micrograms of divergence with no way to say which member moved, a redefinition that fixes the unit to the universe by matching the number to the artefact as it stood, and a kilogram that is now realised by instruments a handful of laboratories own.
