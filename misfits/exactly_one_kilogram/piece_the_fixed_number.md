# Piece: The Fixed Number

## Taxonomy

[The Comparison Room](place_the_comparison_room.md). The constant fixed by decree in 2019, which frees the unit and freezes the error.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In every laboratory at once, replacing the object in [the comparison room](place_the_comparison_room.md) with a value that has no location.

## Load Bearing

It ends the artefact's reign and ratifies its drift in the same stroke.
The Planck constant was fixed at an exact value, which unties the kilogram from any object permanently: but the value was chosen to match the prototype as it stood at the changeover, because any other choice would have shifted every mass measurement on Earth at once, so a century of accumulated drift was not reversed, it was adopted as correct and made permanent.

## Apparent

An exact number, defined rather than measured, identical everywhere in the universe and for all time.
A genuine and long-sought liberation from a lump of metal in a safe.

## Yearbook

Made continuity the priority over correction, deliberately and rightly, since a discontinuity in the kilogram would have propagated into every weighed transaction in the world.
Realised in practice by a Kibble balance or a sphere of isotopically enriched silicon, instruments perhaps a handful of laboratories possess.
