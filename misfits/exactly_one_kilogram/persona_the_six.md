# Persona: The Six

## Taxonomy

[Six](position_six.md). The official copies, kept so the set can at least be compared with itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sister cylinders struck from the same alloy and stored alongside the prototype, held back from ordinary work for this purpose alone.
The discipline's answer to the problem of an unmeasurable reference, and a good answer.

## Action

Sit alongside, and are brought out for the same rare comparisons.
Diverge, slowly, from the prototype and from each other, by something on the order of fifty micrograms across a century.

## Shadow

Can show that the set has moved and never which member did.
Attributing the divergence needs a third thing standing outside the whole group, and there is no outside: so these copies convert an undetectable problem into a visible and unanswerable one, which is real progress and is not a solution, since the reading is identical whether the prototype fell or the copies rose.

## Tell

Provide the only evidence in the play, and the evidence has no subject.
