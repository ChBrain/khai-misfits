# Play: Exactly One Kilogram

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Exactly One Kilogram.
From 1889 until 2019 the kilogram was not a quantity. It was a thing: a cylinder of platinum and iridium, about the size of a plum, sitting under three nested bell jars in a safe outside Paris. Its mass was not measured to be one kilogram. Its mass was one kilogram, in the way a metre of ribbon is a metre long if the ribbon is the metre. Which means the cylinder could not be too heavy, could not be too light, and could not drift, since there existed nothing it could drift with respect to. Every scale on Earth, every dose, every cargo, every transaction by weight, ran back through an unbroken chain of calibrations to that object, and the object was correct by construction, on every day of its life, no matter what happened to it.

## Arc

The bend is this: a definition has no outside, so a reference that moves does not become wrong, it makes everything else wrong instead.
It runs on a thing that cannot be measured.
The Cylinder was exactly one kilogram at every moment, and this is not a convention politely agreed but the actual content of the definition. If it gained a microgram of atmospheric hydrocarbon, the kilogram gained a microgram. If it lost one to a cloth, the kilogram lost one. There is no experiment anybody could have run, at any expense, that would have shown it to be off, because off is measured against something and it was the something.
The Six exist for exactly this reason, and they cannot solve it either. Official copies were struck from the same alloy and kept alongside, so that the set could at least be compared with itself. Over a century they came apart, by something on the order of fifty micrograms. That divergence is real and it is unattributable: the group tells you that the members have separated and cannot tell you which one moved, because answering that needs a third thing outside the set, and there was no outside.
The Keepers did everything right, and the care is part of the mechanism. Three nested bell jars, a vault, keys held separately, and the object removed only for official comparisons: three of them in a hundred years. That is exemplary custody. It is also why there are three data points in a century, so the very diligence that protected the artefact is the reason the record of it is nearly empty.
And The Washing is where it turns. The definition did not say the mass of the cylinder. It said the mass of the cylinder after a particular cleaning: chamois soaked in ether, then ethanol, then steam. That procedure removed about seventeen micrograms from the international prototype and thirteen to seventeen from each of the copies. So the unit was defined as the mass of an object immediately after an operation which itself takes off a quantity comparable to the century of drift everyone was trying to detect, and there is no way to look at the artefact without handling it, and no way to handle it without changing it.
And Everything Weighed carried the consequence, invisibly. If the reference moved, every mass on the planet moved with it, in perfect proportion and perfect silence, since each was defined by its ratio to the thing that moved. An error that propagates everywhere is an error that shows up nowhere.
And the correction did not undo it. In 2019 the kilogram was redefined by fixing the Planck constant to an exact value, which frees the unit from any object at last. But the number chosen was chosen to match the international prototype as it stood at the moment of the change, because a redefinition that shifted the kilogram would have shifted every mass measurement on Earth at once. So a century of drift was not reversed. It was ratified, deliberately and correctly, and made permanent. And realising the new definition takes a Kibble balance or a sphere of isotopically enriched silicon, which perhaps a handful of laboratories on Earth possess, so an ordinary user is now further from the definition than they were when it was a plum-sized object in a safe.

## Stakes

Whether a system of measurement can be sound when its foundation is the one quantity in it that nothing can measure.
Every plot moves the same way: toward exemplary care, correct procedure and an object that is always exactly what it is defined to be, and away from any instrument that could show the foundation moving, because such an instrument would have to stand outside a definition that has no outside. The production cannot weigh the kilogram, and it cannot look at the artefact without touching it. What is left to fight over is not whether the metrologists did their work well, they did it beautifully, but what is owed to a century of measurements that were all correct by construction, and whether a unit tied at last to the universe is any closer to the person holding the scale.
