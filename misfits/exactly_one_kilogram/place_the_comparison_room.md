# Place: The Comparison Room

## Taxonomy

[Exactly One Kilogram](play_exactly_one_kilogram.md). Where the set meets itself, and finds out only that it has separated.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A balance capable of resolving a microgram, in a room with the temperature, pressure and humidity held and recorded.
The prototype and its official copies, out of their jars, side by side, three times in a century.

## Holds

[The Chamois and the Ether](piece_the_chamois_and_the_ether.md), because nothing is weighed here until it has been cleaned.
A comparison that can rank the members of a set against one another with extraordinary precision.

## Offers

The finest mass measurement anybody can make, and a real answer: the group has drifted apart by something like fifty micrograms.

## Withheld

Any way to say who moved.
The instrument compares members of a set to each other and would need a third thing outside the set to attribute the change, and there is no outside, so the room produces a result of great precision and no subject: the prototype falling and the copies rising give the identical reading, and nothing that could be built here would tell them apart.
