# Exactly One Kilogram: Reference

The research warrant for Exactly One Kilogram.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the international prototype of the kilogram, its measured
divergence from its official copies, the cleaning procedure written into the definition, and the 2019
redefinition of the SI base units, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the artefact kilogram as **a reference with no outside**: from 1889 until 2019 the kilogram was not
a quantity but an object, a platinum-iridium cylinder kept under nested bell jars in a safe outside Paris,
and the definition said that this object is the kilogram rather than that it weighs one. The play models
the documented mechanics, the definitional immunity that makes the artefact incapable of error, the copies
that make the divergence visible without making it attributable, the custody so careful that it produced
three observations in a century, the cleaning ritual inside the definition that removes a third of the
signal it is hunting, the whole world of measurement absorbing the drift in silence, and the redefinition
that ratifies the accumulated error rather than reversing it, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure,
not as a tale of sloppy metrology.

The governing law is that a definition has no outside. A reference that moves does not become wrong: it
makes everything else wrong instead, silently and in exact proportion, because every quantity in the system
is defined by its ratio to the thing that moved. So the error propagates to every mass on Earth and is
therefore visible in no measurement of any mass on Earth.

What removes the easy reading is that the custody was exemplary. Three nested bell jars, a vault requiring
separately held keys, and removal only for official verification: that is correct practice, and it is the
reason the century holds three comparisons. Every removal is a handling and every handling is a
perturbation, so the correct policy is to look as seldom as possible, which means the diligence that
preserved the artefact and the poverty of the evidence about it are one act rather than two. There was no
version of doing the job well that also produced data.

What sharpens it is that the observation is a cause of the thing observed. The definition specified the
mass of the artefact immediately after a prescribed cleaning: chamois soaked in ether, then ethanol, then
steam. That procedure removed about 17 micrograms from the international prototype and 13 to 17 from each
official copy, against a century of divergence of roughly 50, so a single cleaning accounts for about a
third of the entire signal. The cleaning cannot be dropped, since a reference carrying a hundred years of
adsorbed hydrocarbon is not a reference, so the unit is exactly itself only in the moments just after the
act that altered it.

And the copies are the discipline's honest answer, which converts an undetectable problem into a visible
and unanswerable one. The set diverged; which member moved is a question that requires a third thing
outside the set, and the set contains its own definition.

It is distinct from the house's **Metric Fixation**, where a number chosen to stand for a goal is hit
exactly and truthfully while the goal quietly dies: there the measure and the thing measured come apart,
and the failure is that the proxy was never the target. Here nothing is a proxy for anything. The artefact
is not standing for the kilogram, it is the kilogram, and the failure is not substitution but the absence
of any vantage point from which substitution could even be checked.

It is distinct from the house's **The New Normal**, where each small deviation is tolerated once and
becomes the standard, so the line of acceptable creeps until it fails: there somebody tolerates something,
and a judgement is being made and remade. Here no judgement occurs at any point, nobody tolerates anything,
and the drift is physical adsorption on a surface nobody is permitted to touch.

It is distinct from the house's **Model Collapse**, where each model trained on the last one's output
drifts to a self-referential mean: there the reference degrades through repeated copying, generation by
generation. Here there is no copying and no generations. A single object drifts, and the reason nobody can
see it is not accumulated resampling but the logical position of the object in the definition.

It is distinct from the house's **The Horizon Problem**, where every plan ends at its instrument's edge so
what lies past carries no cost: there the instrument has a limit and the world extends beyond it. Here the
instrument is superb and its limit is not a range but a direction, since a comparison inside a closed set
can rank its members and can never locate the set.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the custodians
  as negligent or the definition as naive, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: an
  artefact definition was the only kind available in 1889, when no constant of nature could be realised to
  anything like the needed precision; the custody was as good as custody gets, and its rarity of handling
  was the correct policy rather than a lapse; the official copies were the right response to an
  unmeasurable reference; the cleaning had to be inside the definition, because a contaminated reference is
  not one. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that the one
  quantity the whole system rests on is the one quantity the system contains no way to measure, and that
  looking at it at all is a cause of the thing being looked for.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Fixing the Planck
  constant frees the kilogram from any object permanently, which was the whole ambition and is a real
  achievement, and the value was chosen to match the prototype as it stood, so a century of drift is
  adopted as correct and made permanent rather than reversed. That choice was right: a discontinuity in the
  kilogram would have moved every weighed transaction on Earth at once. And realisation now requires a
  Kibble balance or a sphere of isotopically enriched silicon, so the practical chain still runs through
  physical artefacts calibrated by a handful of laboratories, and the ordinary user stands further from the
  definition than when it was an object in a safe.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the prototype, the verification campaigns, the
  contamination studies, the cleaning research and the redefinition are theirs. The bounds are carried with
  them: the roughly 50 microgram figure is a characterisation of a divergence across an ensemble measured
  in a small number of campaigns, not a precise trajectory, and reasonable summaries of it differ; the
  direction of the drift is conventionally described as the prototype losing mass relative to its copies,
  and that description is a choice of frame rather than a finding, since the comparison cannot attribute
  it; the contamination rates and cleaning losses vary between artefacts and with surface finish; and the
  dates of the periodic verifications are given differently in different sources depending on whether the
  campaign or its sanction is meant. **The play makes no claim that the artefact system was a mistake**, it
  held the world's commerce and science together for 130 years, only that its foundation was the one thing
  in it that nothing could weigh.
