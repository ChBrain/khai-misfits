# Plot: It Weighs What It Weighs

## Taxonomy

[Exactly One Kilogram](play_exactly_one_kilogram.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

1889. A cylinder of platinum and iridium is not declared to weigh one kilogram. It is declared to be the kilogram.

## Action

[The Cylinder](persona_the_cylinder.md) takes [Cylinder](position_cylinder.md), and every scale on Earth begins tracing back to it through an unbroken chain of calibrations.

## Stage

The object is exactly one kilogram, on every day of its life, whatever physically happens to its surface.

## Tension

The correctness is a property of grammar rather than an achievement of care, and it cannot be revoked.
Error is a comparison, and this is where comparisons terminate: no experiment at any expense could have shown the artefact to be off, because off requires a something to be off against and it was the something. If it takes on a microgram of atmospheric hydrocarbon, the kilogram takes on a microgram. The definition holds on the days the object is visibly soiled, and it is not being generous when it does.
