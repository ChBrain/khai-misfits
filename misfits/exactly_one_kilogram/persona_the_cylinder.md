# Persona: The Cylinder

## Taxonomy

[Cylinder](position_cylinder.md). The international prototype, which is the kilogram rather than weighing one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A cylinder of platinum and iridium about the size of a plum, cast in 1889, sitting under bell jars in a safe outside Paris.
Not a very good realisation of the kilogram. The kilogram.

## Action

Sits still, for decades at a time.
Adsorbs atmospheric hydrocarbons and mercury vapour, and outgasses volatile impurities from its own alloy, at something like a microgram a year.

## Shadow

Cannot be wrong, which is not a compliment.
Error is a comparison and this persona is the thing comparisons terminate at, so no experiment at any expense could have shown it to be off: if it took on a microgram, the kilogram took on a microgram, and every mass on Earth adjusted silently to match. Its correctness is not an achievement of care but a property of grammar.

## Tell

Is exactly one kilogram on every day of its life, including the days it is visibly dirty.
