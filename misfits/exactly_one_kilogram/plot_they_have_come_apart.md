# Plot: They Have Come Apart

## Taxonomy

[Exactly One Kilogram](play_exactly_one_kilogram.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The prototype and its six official copies out of their jars together, on a balance that resolves a microgram, in a room with everything held and recorded.

## Action

[Weighing them against each other](process_weighing_them_against_each_other.md): [The Six](persona_the_six.md), holding [Six](position_six.md), are compared with the prototype and with one another, as well as mass measurement can be done.

## Stage

The set has separated by something on the order of fifty micrograms across a century. The measurement is superb.

## Tension

A result of great precision, and no subject.
Attributing the divergence needs a third thing standing outside the whole group, and the group contains its own definition, so there is no outside to stand in. The prototype falling and the copies rising produce the identical reading, and nothing that could be built in this room would separate them. The copies were the discipline's answer to an unmeasurable reference, and they are a good answer: they convert an undetectable problem into a visible and unanswerable one, which is real progress and is not a solution.
