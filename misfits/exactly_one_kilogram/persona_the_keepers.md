# Persona: The Keepers

## Taxonomy

[Keepers](position_keepers.md). The custodians, whose diligence is part of the mechanism.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Metrologists of the international bureau, holding the artefact under three nested bell jars in a safe that takes separately held keys to open.
Among the most careful people who have ever handled an object.

## Action

Protect it: from air, from handling, from temperature, from everybody, including themselves.
Bring it out for official comparison three times in a hundred years, and otherwise leave it entirely alone.

## Shadow

Bought the artefact's stability with the record's emptiness, and could not have done otherwise.
Every removal is a handling and every handling is a perturbation, so the correct custodial policy is to look as rarely as possible: which means the discipline holds three observations across a century, and the diligence that preserved the object and the sparseness of the evidence about it are not two facts but one.

## Tell

Did everything right, and the rightness is why so little is known.
