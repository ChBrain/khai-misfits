# Plan: Wash It First

## Taxonomy

A **definitional requirement**: the intent of [The Washing](persona_the_washing.md), inside [Exactly One Kilogram](play_exactly_one_kilogram.md).

## Owner

## Direction

Make the artefact exactly itself before anything is compared to it. Remove the accumulated surface contamination by a standardised, non-abrasive, written-down procedure, because a reference carrying a century of atmospheric hydrocarbon is no reference at all.

## Orders

(Chamois soaked in ether, then ethanol, then steam; applied by hand in [the comparison room](place_the_comparison_room.md); performed before every weighing, since the unit is the mass immediately afterwards.)

## Implementation
