# Plan: Fix It to the Universe

## Taxonomy

A **correction**: the second intent of [The Keepers](persona_the_keepers.md), inside [Exactly One Kilogram](play_exactly_one_kilogram.md), once the artefact's limits are accepted.

## Owner

- Owner: [The Keepers](persona_the_keepers.md)

## Direction

Untie the unit from any object, permanently. Fix a constant of nature to an exact value so that the kilogram can be realised anywhere, by anyone with the right instrument, without reference to a cylinder in a safe.

## Orders

(Fix the Planck constant to an exact value through [the fixed number](piece_the_fixed_number.md); realise the unit by Kibble balance or by a sphere of isotopically enriched silicon; retire the prototype from its defining role in 2019.)

## Implementation

([The Comparison Room](place_the_comparison_room.md), whose object is replaced by a value with no location; the [fixing the constant](process_fixing_the_constant.md) process; decades of work converging well enough to displace an artefact.)

## Targets

- [ ] the unit is freed from any physical object, which was the whole ambition and is achieved
- [ ] the price is accepted: the value is chosen to match the prototype as it stood, so the century of drift is ratified rather than reversed
- [ ] the price is accepted: continuity is preferred over correction, deliberately, because a discontinuity would have moved every weighed transaction on Earth
- [ ] the price is accepted: realisation now needs instruments perhaps a handful of laboratories own, so the ordinary user stands further from the definition than when it was an object they could in principle visit
