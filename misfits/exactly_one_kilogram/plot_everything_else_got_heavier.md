# Plot: Everything Else Got Heavier

## Taxonomy

[Exactly One Kilogram](play_exactly_one_kilogram.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An unbroken chain of calibration running from a pharmacist's balance, through a national standard, back to the safe outside Paris.

## Action

[Everything Weighed](persona_everything_weighed.md), holding [Everything on the Chain](position_everything_on_the_chain.md), measures correctly by a definition that is correct by construction, everywhere, continuously.

## Stage

The reference moves. Every mass on Earth moves with it, in exact proportion, at the same instant.

## Tension

An error that propagates everywhere is an error observable nowhere.
Each mass is defined by its ratio to the thing that shifted, so the shift cancels out of every comparison anybody could make: the pharmacist's balance stays right, the national standard stays right, and right is the only sense of right there is. The largest party in the play absorbs the entire consequence and is the one persona that never appears in a single measurement, because a measurement is a comparison and it has nothing to be compared with.
