# Place: The Wordlist

## Taxonomy

[Erased by What Divided Them](play_erased_by_what_divided_them.md). Where the signal is, while there is one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A few hundred meanings, the same in each language, set out in columns, with the sound correspondences ruled between them.
Ordinary excellent work: the data are attested, the correspondences are regular, and anybody can check them.

## Holds

[The Comparativist](persona_the_comparativist.md), establishing the rules.
[The Correspondence](piece_the_correspondence.md), which is the whole of the evidence.
[The Replacement Rate](piece_the_replacement_rate.md), quietly at work in the background of every column.

## Offers

A reconstruction of an unwritten language, precise enough that later discoveries have confirmed it.

## Withheld

Any column older than the material allows.
The place holds modern and attested forms, and everything about the past is inferred from what those forms preserve. Preservation is not a constant: vocabulary turns over, sounds merge, and each merger removes a distinction that a correspondence needed. So the place is a window whose depth is set by how much has survived rather than by how carefully anybody reads it.
