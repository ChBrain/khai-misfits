# Plot: Which Also Consumes It, at a Rate

## Taxonomy

[Erased by What Divided Them](play_erased_by_what_divided_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The same process, four thousand years later, still running.

## Action

[The Replacement Rate](piece_the_replacement_rate.md) works behind [the wordlist](place_the_wordlist.md): inherited words are lost, borrowed over or narrowed out, and sounds merge.

## Stage

A signal that declines steadily and for the same reason it appeared.

## Tension

The engine of the evidence and the engine of its erasure are one engine, which is the whole of the misfit.
Every lost word removes a place a correspondence could have been seen, and every merger destroys a distinction some correspondence depended on. So the signal does not sit and wait: it decays because the process that made it has not stopped, and the depth at which it becomes unreadable is set by that rate. Nothing anybody does to the method changes the rate, because the rate is the subject of the discipline rather than an obstacle to it.
