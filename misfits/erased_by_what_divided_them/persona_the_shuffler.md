# Persona: The Shuffler

## Taxonomy

[Shuffler](position_shuffler.md). The one who counts what chance alone would give.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Two wordlists from languages nobody claims are related, and a procedure for counting matches under the same rules.

## Action

Applies the long ranger's criteria to unrelated pairs.
Counts.

## Shadow

Establishes the noise floor and thereby establishes what cannot be settled.
The result is not a refutation of anybody: it is a measurement of how many matches the method's own standards produce from languages known to be unrelated, which turns out to be many, because with enough languages, enough semantic latitude and enough phonetic tolerance, resemblances are abundant. So this seat does not show the deep proposal false. It shows that a true one and a false one would look the same.

## Tell

Proves nothing wrong and removes the possibility of proving anything right.
