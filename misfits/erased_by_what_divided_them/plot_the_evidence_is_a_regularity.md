## Tension

The evidence is a rule rather than a likeness, and that is what makes it evidence at all.
Related languages do not preserve similar words but **systematic** ones, where a sound in one answers to a particular sound in the other everywhere the two appear. A likeness on its own is what coincidence produces; a regularity holding across a whole vocabulary is not. So the method is not a matter of noticing resemblances and never was, and its successes are among the most impressive in the historical sciences.
