# Plot: And Contact Supplies the Rest

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

Four thousand years of neighbours, trade and intermarriage.

## Action

[The Borrower](persona_the_borrower.md), holding [Borrower](position_borrower.md), takes words from the language next door and passes them on, for millennia.

## Stage

Real resemblances, meaning something other than descent.

## Tension

A word borrowed early enough wears the regularity an inherited word wears, and no record separates them at depth.
Loans taken before a sound change undergo it, so they arrive in the modern language obeying the same correspondences as inherited vocabulary. At shallow depths the strata are visible and loans can be traced, which is ordinary competent work. At depth they cannot, and this is not a gap in anybody's archives: it is what long contact does. So a second source of true resemblance sits underneath the chance one, and it is not noise at all, which makes it worse.
