# Plot: And the Regularity Is Made by the Divergence

## Taxonomy

[Erased by What Divided Them](play_erased_by_what_divided_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two communities separating, and their speech going on changing.

## Action

[The Correspondence](piece_the_correspondence.md) is traced to its cause: sound changes running through a whole vocabulary at once, on each side of a separation.

## Stage

A proof of common descent manufactured by the act of ceasing to be common.

## Tension

The same event that creates two languages creates the evidence that they were one, which is why the evidence exists to be found.
Nothing was recorded, nobody kept anything, and no witness survives: what survives is the pattern that regular change stamps on an inherited vocabulary. So the discipline's entire evidentiary base is a by-product of divergence, produced free and without intent, and the amount of it depends on how the divergence went rather than on how hard anybody has looked.
