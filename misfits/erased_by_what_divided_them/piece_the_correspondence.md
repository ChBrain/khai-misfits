## Load Bearing

It is made by the divergence and is the only thing that distinguishes descent from coincidence.
Two related languages do not preserve similar words: they preserve **systematic** ones, where a sound in one answers to a particular sound in the other everywhere the two appear. That regularity is produced by sound changes running through a whole vocabulary at once, which is what separation does, so the same event that creates two languages creates the proof that they were one. Nothing else in the discipline carries this weight, and nothing else could, because a likeness on its own is what coincidence also produces.

## Apparent

A resemblance between words. The obvious sign that two languages are related.
