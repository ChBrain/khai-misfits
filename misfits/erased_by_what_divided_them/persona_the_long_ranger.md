# Persona: The Long Ranger

## Taxonomy

[Long Ranger](position_long_ranger.md). The one proposing a family below the floor.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A proposal grouping several established families under one deeper ancestor, with hundreds of assembled comparisons.

## Action

Publishes the deeper grouping.
Answers each objection and gains no ground.

## Shadow

Is not being unrigorous, and is working where the instrument has no resolution.
The comparisons are collected honestly and some of them may well be real: the proposal is not a fantasy and its author is often the person who knows the material best. But at that depth the number of matches a genuine relation would leave is not distinguishable from the number chance and contact supply, so the evidence offered cannot support the claim however much of it is offered, and offering more of it changes nothing.

## Tell

Argues in good faith, at a depth where good faith is not the constraint.
