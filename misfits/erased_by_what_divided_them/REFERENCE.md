# Erased by What Divided Them: Reference

The research warrant for Erased by What Divided Them.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the comparative method in historical linguistics and on what happens
to its evidence as the depth it is asked to reach increases, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the proof of common descent as **a signal made and eaten by one process**: two languages are shown to
share an ancestor by sound correspondences that recur across a whole inherited vocabulary, and those
correspondences are stamped there by the very changes that separated the two communities. The changes do not
stop when the proof is made. They go on replacing inherited words with new ones and merging distinct sounds into
one, so the number of places a correspondence can still be seen falls with time, and below a certain depth what
survives is no more than chance resemblance and old contact would have supplied in any case. The play models the
documented mechanics, the regularity that makes descent distinguishable from likeness, the divergence that
manufactures it free of charge, the replacement that consumes it at a rate, the chance floor that arrives in the
same quantity whether the deep relation is real or not, the ancient borrowing that wears inherited regularity,
and the referee's verdict that a field mishears, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of
a careless long ranger or a closed-minded sceptic.

The governing law is that the evidence of a family is manufactured by the divergence and consumed by the same
process continuing, so how far it reaches is set by the thing it is evidence of.

**The method is the strong party here, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's competence.** The
comparative method is among the great achievements of the historical sciences: it reconstructs an unattested
language from its descendants, and it has been confirmed by material found afterwards, most famously when
Hittite was deciphered and turned out to carry consonants a reconstruction had already required. Nothing in the
play doubts it, and the horizon is not a defect in it. It is the range of an instrument that was never asked to
choose one.

What removes any possibility of blame from the comparativist is that the regularity is a rule rather than a
likeness. Two words that resemble each other prove nothing, since any two vocabularies contain some. What
proves descent is a correspondence that holds systematically, so that a sound in one language answers a
particular sound in the other across the inherited stock, which is a pattern nobody could have produced on
purpose and coincidence does not supply in bulk. The demand for that rule is exactly what makes the method
strong and exactly what makes it run out, because a rule needs instances and the instances are being spent.

What removes it from the long ranger is that assembling more comparisons cannot repair the case. Below the
floor, a true deep relation and a false one produce comparisons in the same quantity, since chance is supplying
them either way. A list of three hundred honest-looking matches is therefore consistent with both answers, and
adding a fourth hundred moves nothing, so the difficulty is not that the deep proposals are argued badly but
that the observation which would decide them is not available at that depth.

And contact closes the case against the last party, because it is a second source of **true** resemblance
meaning something else. Words borrowed early enough are carried through the borrower's own later sound changes
and come to wear inherited regularity, so the cleanest available signal is not uniquely produced by descent. The
techniques for separating loan from inheritance work well when the contact is recent and shallow, and they lose
purchase in the same direction and for the same reason everything else here does.

**And the symmetry is the part a field most often mishears.** A referee who writes that the evidence does not
support the conclusion has written the only defensible sentence, and it is not a finding that the proposal is
false. The measurement that denies the long ranger a verdict denies the sceptic one too: nobody has shown the
languages unrelated, and at that depth nobody can. Every human language descends from something, so some deep
relations are simply true, and the horizon is a statement about what can be established rather than about what
happened.

It is distinct from the house's **A Hundred Years to Vouch for a Thousand**, which the slate line required it to
be named clear of: there a short record is extrapolated to a long horizon, so the trouble is that a fitted
relation is carried beyond the range that produced it. Here nothing is fitted and nothing is extrapolated. The
signal is present and readable inside the range and physically absent outside it, and the honest move is not a
wider interval but a refusal to answer.

It is distinct from the house's **Taken Apart to Be Read**, where the measurement destroys the specimen, so the
information is lost by the act of collecting it and the loss is somebody's operation. Here nobody destroys
anything and nobody performs any act: the erasure is ordinary language change running in speakers who are doing
nothing but speaking, and it has been happening for the whole interval before any linguist arrives.

It is distinct from the house's **Nothing Known Against It**, where an absence of evidence is read as clearance
because nobody has looked. Here the absence is symmetric and the looking has been done: the evidence is missing
for the affirmative and for the negative alike, which is why neither a proposal nor its refusal can be recorded,
where the incumbent's absence points one way only and is filled by doing the study.

It is distinct from the house's **Nobody Stopped Speaking It**, the house's other misfit on language, which
stages language shift, a community's move to another tongue with no decision anybody would recognise as one.
That is a process among living speakers with an outcome in the present. This one is about what a much older
process leaves behind for somebody trying to read it, and the two share neither a scholar nor a work.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the long ranger as
  careless or the sceptic as closed-minded, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the
  comparative method is a great achievement with a range it did not choose; the regularity that makes it strong
  is the same demand that makes it run out; the speakers replacing words and merging sounds are doing nothing
  but speaking; the chance floor arrives in equal quantity whether the deep relation is real or not, so more
  comparisons cannot help whoever is right; ancient contact supplies true resemblances that mean something
  else; and the referee who writes that the evidence does not support the conclusion has written the only
  defensible sentence. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that
  the process which makes the evidence of a family is the process that eats it.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Computing the chance floor is
  the real advance, works, and establishes that the question cannot be answered rather than answering it.
  Statistical and phylogenetic methods borrowed from biology extend the reach and inherit the same thinning
  data. Typological and structural features are more stable than vocabulary and are also more likely to be
  shared by chance or by contact, which is the trade rather than an escape. Ancient DNA and archaeology
  reconstruct populations and movements and do not recover a language. Better wordlists and more fieldwork are
  worth doing and add languages rather than depth. And knowing the whole structure, which every party does,
  leaves a proposal on the desk and a form asking whether the evidence supports it.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the comparative method, the replacement-rate work, the
  chance-factor computation, the treatment of borrowing and the methodological critique are theirs. The bounds
  are carried with them: **where the horizon falls is genuinely contested**, commonly discussed in the region of
  several thousand years and not a line anybody can draw, and the play carries the argument rather than settling
  it; glottochronology's constant-rate assumption is widely rejected and only the fact of replacement is relied
  on here, never a date; the long-range proposals differ greatly from each other in method and in care, and the
  play treats them as one class, which is its own simplification; and the techniques for detecting loans are
  stronger than this account's single sentence allows. **The play makes no claim that any proposed deep family
  is false, or that any proposal should not have been made**, only that the instrument which established the
  families we have is consumed by the same process that wrote them, and that beyond its range a true claim and a
  false one look alike.
