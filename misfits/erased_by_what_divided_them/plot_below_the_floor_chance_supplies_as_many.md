# Plot: Below the Floor, Chance Supplies as Many

## Taxonomy

[Erased by What Divided Them](play_erased_by_what_divided_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A deep proposal, with hundreds of comparisons assembled honestly.

## Action

[Shuffling the lists](process_shuffling_the_lists.md): [the shuffler](persona_the_shuffler.md), holding [Shuffler](position_shuffler.md), applies the same criteria to languages nobody claims are related, and counts.

## Stage

A measured noise floor, and it is not low.

## Tension

The count does not refute the proposal: it shows a true one and a false one would look the same.
With enough languages, enough phonetic latitude and enough semantic latitude, matches accumulate from unrelated material, and the criteria have to be loosened that far to find anything at all at that depth. So the quantity of evidence offered is a quantity the null hypothesis also predicts, and [the long ranger](persona_the_long_ranger.md) cannot repair this by assembling more, because more of the same kind is exactly what both hypotheses predict.
