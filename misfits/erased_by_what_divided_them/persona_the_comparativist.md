# Persona: The Comparativist

## Taxonomy

[Comparativist](position_comparativist.md). The one who finds the correspondences and reconstructs the ancestor.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Wordlists from a dozen languages, a table of consonants, and a set of correspondences that hold across the whole list.

## Action

Establishes the regular correspondences.
Reconstructs a proto-form and writes an asterisk in front of it.

## Shadow

Has an instrument that works superbly and has a range.
The method is one of the great achievements of nineteenth century science and it is not in question: where the signal is present the reconstruction is precise, testable and repeatedly confirmed by material found later. What it cannot do is work below a certain depth, and that is not a defect of the technique but a property of the material, so no refinement extends it and the seat is not being asked to try harder.

## Tell

Reconstructs the unwritten past exactly, down to a floor it did not choose.
