# Persona: The Borrower

## Taxonomy

[Borrower](position_borrower.md). The one whose contact makes inheritance and diffusion converge.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Four thousand years of neighbours, trade, intermarriage and shared technology, and the words that came with all of it.

## Action

Takes words from the language next door.
Passes some on, over and over, for millennia.

## Shadow

Produces exactly the pattern the method reads as inheritance, and does it without anybody's intent.
A word borrowed early enough undergoes the borrower's later sound changes, so it arrives in the modern language wearing the same regularity an inherited word wears. At shallow depths the two can be told apart, because the strata are visible and the loans are recent enough to trace. At depth they are not, and the ambiguity is not a gap in anybody's records: it is what long contact does.

## Tell

Makes a true resemblance that means something other than what it looks like.
