# Persona: The Referee

## Taxonomy

[Referee](position_referee.md). The one who must assess the claim with no instrument for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A submitted paper proposing a deep family, a reviewer's form, and a deadline.

## Action

Reads it.
Reports that the evidence does not support the conclusion, and cannot report that the conclusion is false.

## Shadow

Is right about the evidence and is not entitled to the verdict everybody reads into the report.
Saying that a claim is unsupported is not saying it is wrong, and the distinction matters here more than anywhere, because the method that cannot confirm the deep grouping also cannot exclude it. So the seat's honest assessment is systematically read as a rejection of the proposal, and the seat cannot correct that reading without asserting something it also has no evidence for.

## Tell

Writes the only defensible report and is understood to have said more.
