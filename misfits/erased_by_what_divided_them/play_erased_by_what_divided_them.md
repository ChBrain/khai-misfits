# Play: Erased by What Divided Them

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Erased by What Divided Them.
Two languages are shown to share an ancestor by sound correspondences that hold across a whole vocabulary, and those correspondences are stamped there by the changes that separated them. The changes do not stop. They go on replacing words and merging sounds, so the proof thins at a rate, and below a certain depth what is left is no more than chance and old borrowing would have supplied anyway.

## Arc

The bend is this: the evidence of a family is manufactured by the divergence and consumed by the same process continuing, so its reach is set by the thing it is evidence of.
It runs on a rule rather than a likeness.
[The Evidence Is a Regularity](plot_the_evidence_is_a_regularity.md). [The Correspondence](piece_the_correspondence.md) is systematic, holds across a whole vocabulary, and is what separates descent from coincidence, which is why the method's reconstructions have been confirmed by things found afterwards.
And the rule is made by the splitting.
[And the Regularity Is Made by the Divergence](plot_and_the_regularity_is_made_by_the_divergence.md). Nothing was recorded and no witness survives: what survives is the pattern regular change stamps on an inherited vocabulary, produced free and without intent.
And the same process eats it.
[Which Also Consumes It, at a Rate](plot_which_also_consumes_it_at_a_rate.md). [The Replacement Rate](piece_the_replacement_rate.md) loses inherited words and merges sounds, and each loss removes a place a correspondence could have been seen.
Then [Below the Floor, Chance Supplies as Many](plot_below_the_floor_chance_supplies_as_many.md).
[The Chance Match](piece_the_chance_match.md) arrives in the same quantity whether the deep relation is real or not, so [the long ranger](persona_the_long_ranger.md) cannot repair the case by assembling more of it.
And [contact supplies the rest](plot_and_contact_supplies_the_rest.md). [The Borrower](persona_the_borrower.md)'s ancient loans wear inherited regularity, which is a second source of true resemblance meaning something else.
And [the region beyond answers neither way](plot_so_the_region_beyond_answers_neither_way.md). [The Referee](persona_the_referee.md) writes the only defensible sentence, that the evidence does not support the conclusion, and a field hears that the proposal has been refuted.

## Triggers

**[The Evidence Is a Regularity](plot_the_evidence_is_a_regularity.md)**
Cue: a dozen languages that look as though they might be related. Closes when the evidence is shown to be a rule rather than a likeness, which is what makes it evidence at all.

**[And the Regularity Is Made by the Divergence](plot_and_the_regularity_is_made_by_the_divergence.md)**
Cue: two communities separating, and their speech going on changing. Closes when the proof of common descent is found to be manufactured by the act of ceasing to be common.

**[Which Also Consumes It, at a Rate](plot_which_also_consumes_it_at_a_rate.md)**
Cue: the same process, four thousand years later, still running. Closes when the engine of the evidence and the engine of its erasure are shown to be one engine.

**[Below the Floor, Chance Supplies as Many](plot_below_the_floor_chance_supplies_as_many.md)**
Cue: a deep proposal with hundreds of honest comparisons. Closes when the count shows that a true claim and a false one would look the same, so more evidence of the same kind cannot help.

**[And Contact Supplies the Rest](plot_and_contact_supplies_the_rest.md)**
Cue: four thousand years of neighbours. Closes when early loans are found wearing inherited regularity, adding a second source of true resemblance that means something else.

**[So the Region Beyond Answers Neither Way](plot_so_the_region_beyond_answers_neither_way.md)**
Cue: a referee's form and a deadline. Closes when the symmetry is established, since the measurement that denies one side a verdict denies the other one too.

## Stakes

Whether a question with an answer can be closed when the only instrument runs out before reaching it.
Every plot moves the same way: toward evidence that is a rule rather than a likeness, a rule manufactured by the divergence it proves, a process that eats it at the rate it made it, a noise floor that chance alone supplies, a contact history that supplies real resemblances meaning something else, and a symmetry in which neither the claim nor its denial is established, and away from any account in which the long ranger was careless or the sceptic was closed-minded. The production cannot fault the comparativist, whose method is among the great achievements of the historical sciences and has a range it did not choose; and it cannot fault the long ranger, who is working where the instrument has no resolution rather than misreading a signal. What is left to fight over is not who is right about the deep past but what to call a question that is certainly settled somewhere and cannot be settled here.
