## Load Bearing

It is the same accumulation that produced the correspondences, continued, and it removes them at a rate.
Languages replace vocabulary steadily: an inherited word is lost, borrowed over or narrowed out of the basic list, and each loss removes a place a correspondence could have been seen. Sounds merge as well, and every merger destroys a distinction some correspondence depended on. So the signal does not sit still and wait to be found: it declines, and it declines because the process that made it has not stopped.
