# Piece: The Blank Column

## Taxonomy

[The Table of Alternatives](place_the_table_of_alternatives.md). The absence that reads as a clearance.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Standing beside the filled one on [the table of alternatives](place_the_table_of_alternatives.md), and read at [reaching for the analogue](process_reaching_for_the_analogue.md).

## Load Bearing

It is a fact about the literature presented in a column reserved for facts about the compound.
Every molecule has this column before anybody has spent twenty years looking, including the one just restricted, so the blank reports how long the search has run rather than what the search found: and because the table has no field for the age of the evidence, the honest reading of an honest document favours whatever has been examined least.

## Apparent

The strongest entry on the page. No findings, no exceedances, no reviews.
Exactly what a clean compound would look like.

## Yearbook

Fills in slowly, at the rate exposure allows.
Is indistinguishable, on the day of the decision, from a genuine clearance.
