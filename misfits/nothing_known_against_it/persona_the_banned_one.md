# Persona: The Banned One

## Taxonomy

[Banned One](position_banned_one.md). The compound with a record, restricted on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Twenty years of studies, a replication, a review, and a restriction that followed from them.
The best-characterised molecule in the room.

## Action

Comes off the market.
Leaves a hole in the formulation.

## Shadow

Is dangerous because it is known, and known because it was used.
Every finding against it was bought with exposure, so its dossier is a record of the years it spent in products and in people: what marks it out from its replacement is not that it is worse but that somebody has had time to find out.

## Tell

Is the only compound on the table anybody can say anything about.
