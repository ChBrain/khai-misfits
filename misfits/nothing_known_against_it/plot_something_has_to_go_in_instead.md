# Plot: Something Has to Go In Instead

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Known Against It](play_nothing_known_against_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A product that still has to do what it did, and a formulation with a hole in it.

## Action

[The Formulator](persona_the_formulator.md), holding [Formulator](position_formulator.md), opens [the table of alternatives](place_the_table_of_alternatives.md).

## Stage

The choice is which compound, not whether. Leaving the formulation empty is not on the table.

## Tension

The one option that would break the sequence is the one nobody is offering.
A flame retardant, a plasticiser or a surfactant is doing a job the product needs and often a job a standard requires, so declining to substitute means declining to make the product, which is a decision taken somewhere else and usually not at all. The seat that picks the replacement never holds the question of whether the function is necessary, and the sequence runs entirely inside that seat.
