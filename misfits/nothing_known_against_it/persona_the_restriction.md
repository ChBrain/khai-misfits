# Persona: The Restriction

## Taxonomy

[Restriction](position_restriction.md). The instrument, which acts on names.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A listed substance, a CAS number, a date, and a scope.
Earned, reviewed, and enforceable.

## Action

Removes the named compound.
Does not reach the one that replaces it.

## Shadow

Can only bind what has been characterised, which is what it has just finished doing.
Regulating a class rather than a substance is the standing proposal and is hard for a real reason, since a class wide enough to catch the analogue catches compounds nobody has evidence against, which is the same objection the restriction itself was built to answer.

## Tell

Works exactly as designed and moves the problem one molecule along.
