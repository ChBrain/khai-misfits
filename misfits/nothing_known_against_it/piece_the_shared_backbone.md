## Load Bearing

It is the reason the substitute performs and the reason it is likely to behave alike in a body, and the search selects for it without asking about the second.
A compound is chosen because it does the same job in the same formulation, and doing the same job usually means a shared structure: so the technically best answer and the structurally closest relative are the same candidate, and the property that makes the substitution work is the property that makes the finding predictable.
