## Taxonomy

## Cue

## Action

## Stage

[The Substitute](persona_the_substitute.md) goes in, chosen by following the evidence.

## Tension

The blank is a fact about the literature sitting in a column reserved for facts about the compound.
Every molecule has it before anybody has spent twenty years looking, including the one just restricted, so it reports how long the search has run rather than what the search found. And there is no field on the page for the age of the evidence, so the honest reading of an honest document favours whatever has been examined least: choosing otherwise would mean preferring known harms over none, which no procedure asks for and no auditor would accept.
