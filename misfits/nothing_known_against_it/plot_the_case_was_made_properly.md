# Plot: The Case Was Made Properly

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Known Against It](play_nothing_known_against_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Twenty years of studies on one compound, and a regulator with a decision to take.

## Action

[Restricting it](process_restricting_it.md): [the restriction](persona_the_restriction.md), holding [Restriction](position_restriction.md), lists [the banned one](persona_the_banned_one.md) by name.

## Stage

The compound comes off the market. The finding replicated, the review was held, and the decision follows from the evidence.

## Tension

Nothing downstream of this is an argument against acting on evidence.
The play states it here so that it cannot be borrowed later: the restriction is earned, the science behind it is honest work that found real things, and a version of this production that treated the ban as the error would be a different play with a different cast. What is worth noticing is only where the dossier came from. Every finding in it was bought with exposure, so the compound is dangerous because it is known and known because it was used.
