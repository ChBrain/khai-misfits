# Persona: The Record

## Taxonomy

[Record](position_record.md). The evidence, which grows only with use.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Studies, exceedances, biomonitoring, reviews, and the years all of it took.
Honest work, done properly, by people who found real things.

## Action

Accumulates against whatever is in use.
Says nothing about anything else.

## Shadow

Is a function of exposure time before it is a function of hazard.
A compound cannot acquire a record without being used, and it cannot be used at scale before it is chosen, so the instrument that decides which compounds are dangerous can only report on the ones already in people, and its silence about the rest is structural rather than reassuring.

## Tell

Is the reason the restriction is right and the reason the replacement looks safe.
