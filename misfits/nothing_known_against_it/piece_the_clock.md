# Piece: The Clock

## Taxonomy

[The Twentieth Year](place_the_twentieth_year.md). The time evidence takes, restarting at every substitution.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Running from the day the substitute goes in, and read only at [the twentieth year](place_the_twentieth_year.md).

## Load Bearing

It resets on every substitution, so the system's knowledge never accumulates ahead of its exposure.
Studies need years and biomonitoring needs a population that has been exposed, so the interval between a compound entering use and being characterised is roughly fixed: each restriction hands that interval back in full, and a sequence of correct restrictions is a sequence of resets rather than a march toward safety.

## Apparent

Nothing. Ordinary scientific timescales, which nobody disputes.
The unremarkable pace at which any dossier is built.

## Yearbook

Is spent with the compound in the product and in people.
Cannot be shortened by caring more, since what it is waiting for is exposure.
