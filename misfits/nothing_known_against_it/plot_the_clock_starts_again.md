# Plot: The Clock Starts Again

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Known Against It](play_nothing_known_against_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The substitute in the product, and the first studies beginning on it.

## Action

[Waiting for the record](process_waiting_for_the_record.md): [the clock](piece_the_clock.md), reset on the day of the substitution, runs.

## Stage

The dossier on the substitute will take about as long as the last one, and all of it is spent with the compound in use.

## Tension

A sequence of correct restrictions is a sequence of resets.
Studies need years and biomonitoring needs a population that has already been exposed, so the interval between a compound entering use and being characterised is roughly fixed and cannot be shortened by caring more: what it is waiting for is the exposure. Each restriction hands that interval back in full, which means the system's knowledge never gets ahead of its exposure and every correct decision buys the same delay again.
