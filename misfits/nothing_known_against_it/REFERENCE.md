# Nothing Known Against It: Reference

The research warrant for Nothing Known Against It.
The term is the field's; the play runs on regrettable substitution and the evidence asymmetry that produces it,
all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging substitution as **a comparison between a filled column and a blank one, where the blank reports the age
of the literature and is read as a property of the compound**: a substance is restricted on evidence that took
two decades to accumulate, the product still has to work, and the formulator picks the nearest molecule that
does the same job. Against that one there is nothing recorded, and there is nothing recorded because nothing
has had time to be recorded. The play models the documented mechanics, the restriction that is earned, the
substitution that is compulsory rather than chosen, the structural resemblance that makes the nearest answer a
near relative, the absence that reads as a clearance, the interval that resets, and the finding that arrives
after the exposure it describes, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of an industry
evading a rule.

The governing law is that evidence about a compound is produced by exposure to it, so a record is a function of
time in use before it is a function of hazard. A molecule cannot acquire findings without being used, and it
cannot be used at scale before it is chosen, which means the instrument that decides what is dangerous can only
report on what is already in people. Its silence about everything else is structural, and there is no field in
any comparison table that distinguishes a silence of that kind from a clearance.

What removes any possibility of blame from the regulator is that the restriction is earned and the instrument
is limited by the same standard that earned it. The dossier meets the bar, the review was held, and acting on
it is what a regulator is for. Binding a class rather than a substance is the standing proposal and is hard for
a real reason: a class wide enough to catch the analogue catches compounds nobody has evidence against, which
is the objection the restriction itself was built to answer.

What removes it from the formulator is that they are choosing which and never whether, and must justify the
choice against the data available. The data favours whatever has been studied least, and preferring a compound
with known harms over one with none would fail an audit, so the procedure that keeps the choice honest is the
procedure that moves it toward the dark. The resemblance that makes the substitute work is the best available
reason to expect it to behave alike, and it is not a hazard argument on its own and supports no restriction at
the time.

And the interval cannot be shortened. Studies need years and biomonitoring needs a population that has already
been exposed, so the gap between a compound entering use and being characterised is roughly fixed. Each
restriction hands that gap back in full, which makes a sequence of correct decisions a sequence of resets
rather than a march toward safety.

It is distinct from the house's **Known Too Late**, where a technology can be steered while its harms are
unknown and has set too hard to steer by the time they show: there the trap is entrenchment, a one-way
hardening in which the window closes for good. Here nothing entrenches at all. The substitute can be restricted
in its turn, on the same standard, as easily as its parent was, and that is precisely the difficulty: the move
remains available and restarts the clock every time it is used, so the shape is a cycle rather than a closing
window.

It is distinct from the house's **First Do Harm**, where a remedy applied in good faith treats the complaint
and inflicts a new, separate harm the condition never carried: there the intervention itself does the damage,
to tissue the disease had left whole. Here the restriction inflicts nothing and succeeds completely against its
target. The new harm is not created by the remedy but **left unmeasured** by it, and it was already going to be
whatever it was: what the remedy supplied was only the reason to reach for something nobody had looked at.

It is distinct from the house's **The Balloon Effect**, where squeezing a banned trade on one patch bulges it
to the next so local wins move the trade rather than cut it: there the same activity relocates in space and the
aggregate is conserved under enforcement. Here nothing moves anywhere. The same use continues in the same
place, by the same people, for the same purpose, with a different molecule in it, and what is displaced is not
the activity but the evidence base underneath it.

It is distinct from the house's **The Cobra Effect**, where a bounty set to reduce a problem is farmed so the
reward breeds the very thing it pays to remove: there somebody responds to an incentive and produces the target
deliberately. Here nobody is farming anything and no incentive is being gamed. The formulator is complying
fully, wants the safest available compound, and reaches the worse outcome by reading an honest table honestly.

It is distinct from the house's **The Streetlight Effect**, where the search runs where it is easy to look
rather than where the answer is: there somebody chooses the lit ground and the remedy is to look in the dark.
Here nobody chooses where to search. The light falls on whatever has been in use longest, because use is what
makes light, and a searcher who insisted on examining the dark would be asking for findings that do not exist
yet about a compound nobody has been exposed to.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the industry as
  evading the rule and the regulator as naive, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the
  restriction is earned, replicated and reviewed, and acting on it is what a regulator is for; the instrument
  binds a name because a name is what can be enforced, and widening it catches compounds nobody has evidence
  against, which is the objection the restriction was built to honour; the formulator chooses which compound
  and never whether, and must justify the choice against data that favours whatever has been studied least;
  the resemblance that makes the substitute work is a technical requirement and is not a hazard argument on its
  own; and the interval before a record exists cannot be shortened, since what it waits for is exposure. The
  failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a record is a function of
  time in use before it is a function of hazard.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Assessing alternatives before
  adoption is the real remedy and wants toxicological, exposure and life-cycle machinery that no formulation
  table carries on the day, applied to compounds with no data to assess. Restricting by class reaches the
  analogue and sweeps in compounds against which nothing is known, which is the standard the restricted
  compound was itself protected by for twenty years. Declining to substitute means declining the function,
  which is a decision taken somewhere other than the seat that picks the replacement, and often not taken at
  all. And knowing the mechanism completely changes nothing about a table with one column filled in.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the bisphenol, flame-retardant and fluorinated sequences, the
  green-toxicology argument, the alternatives-assessment machinery and the regulatory record are theirs. The
  bounds are carried with them: the play makes **no claim about how often** substitution goes this way, since
  the documented cases are selected for having turned out badly and the base rate is genuinely disputed;
  substitutions that worked exist and are not staged here, so nothing in this production should be read as an
  argument against substitution as a policy; whether a given analogue is as harmful as its parent is a live
  scientific question decided compound by compound and is not settled by structural resemblance, which the play
  carries as an expectation and never as a finding; the twenty-year interval is an order of magnitude drawn
  from the cited sequences rather than a constant; and comparing compounds on unequal evidence is itself a
  methodological problem with a literature of its own, which the play stages as the shape of the table rather
  than adjudicating. **The play makes no claim that the restrictions were mistakes**, only that a record is
  bought with exposure, so the option with nothing recorded against it is the option nobody has had time to
  examine.
