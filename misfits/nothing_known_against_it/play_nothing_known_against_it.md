# Play: Nothing Known Against It

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Nothing Known Against It.
Twenty years of studies accumulate against a compound and a restriction follows, correctly and on the evidence. The product still has to work, so a formulator opens the table of alternatives and picks the nearest molecule that does the same job. Against that one there is nothing: no positive findings, no exceedances, no reviews. The column is blank, and the column is blank because the compound is new.

## Arc

The bend is this: evidence accrues with use, so the cleanest record in the room always belongs to whatever has been used least.
It runs on a restriction that is earned.
[The Banned One](persona_the_banned_one.md) is not restricted on suspicion. Twenty years of work built [the record](persona_the_record.md) against it, the finding replicated, the review held, and acting on that is what a regulator is for. Nothing in this play argues the restriction was wrong, and a version that did would be a different play.
And something has to go in instead.
[The Formulator](persona_the_formulator.md) is not choosing whether to use a chemical, they are choosing which one, and the product has to do what it did. So the search runs over compounds that behave alike, and behaving alike in a formulation usually means [the shared backbone](piece_the_shared_backbone.md): the nearest answer is the nearest molecule.
Then [Read as a Clearance](plot_read_as_a_clearance.md), which is a fact about the literature.
[The Substitute](persona_the_substitute.md) arrives with [a blank column](piece_the_blank_column.md), and the blank is read across the table as the safest entry. It is not evidence of safety. It is the absence that any compound has before anybody has spent twenty years looking, and there is no field in the table that says so.
And [the clock](piece_the_clock.md) starts again. Studies begin, exposure begins with them, and the record on the substitute will take about as long as the last one did, during which the compound is in the product and in everybody.
So [the twentieth year](place_the_twentieth_year.md) arrives, the findings come in, and the analogue turns out to do much what its parent did, which the shared backbone always made likely and which nothing in the table asked.
And the same move is available again, at the same price, on the next molecule along.

## Triggers

**[The Case Was Made Properly](plot_the_case_was_made_properly.md)**
Cue: twenty years of studies on one compound, and a regulator with a decision to take. Closes when the restriction has been shown to be earned, replicated and reviewed, so nothing downstream of it can be read as an argument against acting on evidence.

**[Something Has to Go In Instead](plot_something_has_to_go_in_instead.md)**
Cue: a product that still has to do what it did, and a formulation with a hole in it. Closes when the choice has been found to be which compound rather than whether, so declining to substitute is not among the options on the table.

**[The Nearest Molecule Is the Nearest Answer](plot_the_nearest_molecule_is_the_nearest_answer.md)**
Cue: the search for something that behaves the same way in the same formulation. Closes when behaving alike has been shown to run through a shared structure, so the technically best answer and the structurally closest relative are the same candidate.

**[Read as a Clearance](plot_read_as_a_clearance.md)**
Cue: the table of alternatives, with one column filled in and one blank. Closes when the blank has been found to be a fact about the literature rather than about the compound, with no field in the table that says which.

**[The Clock Starts Again](plot_the_clock_starts_again.md)**
Cue: the substitute in the product, and the first studies beginning on it. Closes when the record on it has been shown to need about as long as the last one, all of it spent with the compound in use.

**[And the Twentieth Year Arrives](plot_and_the_twentieth_year_arrives.md)**
Cue: the findings, coming in on the substitute. Closes when the analogue has been found to do much what its parent did, which the shared backbone made likely and nothing in the table asked, and when the same move is available again on the next molecule along.

## Stakes

Whether a rule that acts on names can act on a hazard.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a restriction that is earned, a substitution that is compulsory rather than chosen, a nearest answer that is a near relative, a blank that reads as a clearance, a clock that restarts with the compound already in use, and a finding that arrives after the exposure it describes, and away from any account in which somebody knew. The production cannot fault the regulator, who acted on the evidence there was, and it cannot fault the formulator, who had to put something in. What is left to fight over is not whether the substitute was tested, everybody agrees it was not, but what to call a system in which the least examined option is the one that looks best.
