# Place: The Table of Alternatives

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Known Against It](play_nothing_known_against_it.md). Where the comparison is made, honestly, on the only data there is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Candidate compounds down one side, endpoints across the top, and a column of findings against the restricted one.
A defensible document, prepared by competent people, that would survive an audit.

## Holds

[The Formulator](persona_the_formulator.md), choosing which and never whether.
[The Substitute](persona_the_substitute.md), arriving with nothing recorded against it.
[The Blank Column](piece_the_blank_column.md), which is the strongest entry on the page.

## Offers

Every endpoint anybody has measured, laid out side by side and correctly attributed.
A choice that can be justified to a regulator, a customer and a court.

## Withheld

Any field for how long each compound has been looked at.
The place compares findings against findings and treats an absence as a value, so the row with the least work behind it reads as the best row, and there is nowhere on the page to write that the difference is the age of the literature rather than the behaviour of the molecule.
