# Persona: The Formulator

## Taxonomy

[Formulator](position_formulator.md). The party choosing which, never whether.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A product that has to do what it did, a hole where a compound was, and a table of alternatives.
Complete compliance, and a deadline.

## Action

Picks the nearest thing that works.
Documents the choice, correctly, against the data available.

## Shadow

Is asked to compare a filled column with a blank one.
The comparison the table supports is between a compound with findings and a compound without, and the honest reading of that comparison is the one that moves the product toward whatever has been studied least: choosing otherwise would mean preferring the option with known harms over the option with none, which no procedure asks for and no auditor would accept.

## Tell

Follows the data and is carried by it in the wrong direction.
