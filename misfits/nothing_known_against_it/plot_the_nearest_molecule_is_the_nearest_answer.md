# Plot: The Nearest Molecule Is the Nearest Answer

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Known Against It](play_nothing_known_against_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The search for something that behaves the same way in the same formulation.

## Action

Candidates are screened on performance, and [the shared backbone](piece_the_shared_backbone.md) is what most of them have in common.

## Stage

The technically best answer and the structurally closest relative turn out to be the same candidate.

## Tension

The property that makes the substitution work is the property that makes the finding predictable.
Doing the same job in the same formulation usually runs through a shared structure, and a shared structure is also the best available reason to expect similar behaviour in a body. Nobody is ignoring that: it is simply not a hazard argument on its own, it supports no restriction, and it would not survive a challenge from a compound that has no findings against it. So the search selects for the resemblance and the table has no way to price it.
