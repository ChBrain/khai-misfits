# Position: Formulator

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Known Against It](play_nothing_known_against_it.md). The seat that chooses which and never whether.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A hole in a formulation, a deadline, and a table of alternatives.

## Orders

Pick something that works. Document it against the data.

## Loses

The option of preferring the known.
The seat must justify its choice against the available data, and the available data favours whatever has been studied least: choosing the compound with findings over the compound without would fail an audit, so the procedure that keeps the seat honest is the procedure that moves it toward the dark.

## Drives

Toward following the evidence into the least examined option in the room.
