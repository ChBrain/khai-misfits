# Position: Restriction

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Known Against It](play_nothing_known_against_it.md). The seat that binds names rather than hazards.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A listed substance, a number, a scope and enforcement behind it.

## Orders

Remove the named compound. Hold the line.

## Loses

Everything one substitution away.
The seat can only bind what has been characterised, which is what characterising takes decades to do, and widening it to a class catches compounds nobody has evidence against, which is the objection the seat exists to honour: so it is not failing to anticipate the analogue, it is applying to the analogue the same standard that protected the analogue's parent for twenty years.

## Drives

Toward working exactly as designed and moving the problem one molecule along.
