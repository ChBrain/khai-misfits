# Piece: The Premium

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). A full egg's investment, paid every season, whether or not the policy is claimed on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Paid at [the scrape](place_the_scrape.md), at laying.
Answered nowhere: it falls due before anybody knows whether it will be needed.

## Load Bearing

Without it the second egg is a free option and there is no misfit.
It is load bearing because the females invest **about the same in each egg**, so this is a real cost rather than a token, and it is what makes the arrangement an insurance policy rather than an abundance of eggs.

## Apparent

An extra egg, which looks like fecundity.
It reads as a species laying more than it needs, and the fact that it is a priced bet against a specific and measured failure rate is not visible from the clutch.

## Yearbook

Paid by [underwriting](process_underwriting.md), against [the failure rate](piece_the_failure_rate.md).
It is redeemed as [the claim](piece_the_claim.md) in four seasons in ten and written off in the other six.
