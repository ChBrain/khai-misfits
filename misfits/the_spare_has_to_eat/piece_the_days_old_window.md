# Piece: The Days-Old Window

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). The few days between the two hatchings, in which the contest is settled.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the scrape](place_the_scrape.md), inside the first week.
Answered nowhere: the asynchrony that creates it was arranged at laying.

## Load Bearing

Without it two chicks share one ration for weeks and both do badly.
It is load bearing because it makes the resolution **quick rather than protracted**: the staggered laying gives the elder a decisive head start, so the outcome is days of contest rather than a season of divided food, and what is lost is an egg's investment rather than half a season's.

## Apparent

The cruel part, and the part the account is usually told for.
It reads as the whole phenomenon, and it is the cheapest step in it: the expensive decision was made weeks earlier, when the second egg was laid.

## Yearbook

Created by the asynchrony in [underwriting](process_underwriting.md), executed in [culling](process_culling.md).
It is why the arrangement costs a premium rather than a ruined season.
