# Process: Culling

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). The elder chick removing the younger, within days, in a nest that feeds one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two chicks and one ration.
At [the scrape](place_the_scrape.md), inside the first week.

## Direction

Toward one chick that fledges rather than two that do not.
This is the step the play most refuses to sentimentalise: the elder is not vicious, it is competing for a supply that cannot carry two, and a tolerant version of it produces two underfed chicks instead of one fledged one.
And the parent does not intervene, which is not indifference: the ration raises one, and the arrangement it laid the eggs under is exactly this one.

## Lever

The head start, which is days of hatching order rather than any superiority, and was arranged by the staggered laying.
Because the asynchrony makes the contest short, the resolution costs days rather than a season of divided food.

## Echo

One chick, and a premium spent.
The younger was not surplus to a plan: it was the plan, for a season that did not happen.
