# Plot: The Premium Was Paid Anyway

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The six seasons in ten when the first egg hatches and the insurance is redundant.
[The Premium](piece_the_premium.md), spent.

## Action

[Provisioning](process_provisioning.md): [The Foraging](persona_the_foraging.md), in [the constraint seat](position_constraint.md), delivers a ration that raises one, exactly as it was always going to.

## Stage

[The Foraging](persona_the_foraging.md), on [Bring Back What There Is](plan_bring_back_what_there_is.md), yields what the season yields, indifferent to how many eggs were laid, and reveals itself only after hatching.
[The Second Egg](persona_the_second_egg.md) is removed, having been entirely capable of fledging.

## Tension

Redundancy is not free when the redundancy eats, and that is the whole misfit.
An unused spare part can sit on a shelf. A spare chick draws on the very ration it was held against, so it cannot be kept in reserve and the policy has to be **cancelled rather than shelved**. And the cancellation is not for any failure: the second egg is removed precisely because it succeeded, in a season when the thing it was insuring against did not happen. **It was not surplus to the plan. It was the plan, for a year that did not come.**
