# Piece: The Claim

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). The four seasons in ten when the first egg fails and the spare is the entire year.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the scrape](place_the_scrape.md), when the first egg does not hatch.
Answered nowhere in advance: nothing distinguishes these seasons until hatching.

## Load Bearing

Without it the premium buys nothing and the practice is inexplicable.
It is load bearing because it is **where the whole arrangement is paid for**: every fledgling of those seasons exists only because the second egg was laid, so the six wasted premiums are bought by these four and the account balances across seasons rather than within one.

## Apparent

A lucky escape, or a good year rescued.
It reads as the exception, and it is the justification: the practice exists for these seasons and is a dead loss in all the others.

## Yearbook

Redeemed in [hatching](process_hatching.md), against [the premium](piece_the_premium.md) paid at [underwriting](process_underwriting.md).
It is the branch that [the days-old window](piece_the_days_old_window.md) is the alternative to.
