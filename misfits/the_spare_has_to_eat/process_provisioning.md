# Process: Provisioning

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). Carrying back what the range yielded, which raises one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A food supply decided at [the foraging range](place_the_foraging_range.md).
Adults travelling out and returning through the rearing period.

## Direction

Toward one fledgling.
Nothing here is stinted and no adult is idle: the quantity is set by conditions across a sea or a territory rather than by effort, and working harder does not make the range yield more.
So the constraint the clutch was chosen against turns out to be exactly what it was feared to be, and turns out too late to change the clutch.

## Lever

The range's yield, which is neither observable in advance nor movable by anything at the nest.
Because it is revealed only after hatching, it cannot inform the decision that depended on it.

## Echo

One chick fed to fledging.
The ration was always going to raise one, and the only open question was whether there would be a chick left to take it.
