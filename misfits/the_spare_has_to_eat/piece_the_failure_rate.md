# Piece: The Failure Rate

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). First eggs hatching about fifty-one to sixty-one per cent of the time in the species that lay a spare.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Counted at [the colony record](place_the_colony_record.md), across years.
Answered nowhere: it is a property of the species rather than of any nest.

## Load Bearing

Without it the second egg is unexplained and the practice looks like waste.
It is load bearing because it **prices the premium**: at a rate near a hundred per cent the spare buys nothing, and at a rate near half it buys the difference between a season and nothing, so this number is what decides whether the arrangement exists at all.

## Apparent

A poor hatching record, and a sign of a struggling population.
It reads as something wrong with the species, and it is the ordinary condition of these species and the reason for the trait rather than a symptom of anything.

## Yearbook

Measured by [hatching](process_hatching.md), read against [the eighty-five per cent](piece_the_eighty_five_per_cent.md) in [comparing](process_comparing.md).
It sets the value of [the premium](piece_the_premium.md) and therefore whether it is worth paying.
