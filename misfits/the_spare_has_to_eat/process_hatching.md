# Process: Hatching

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). The first egg succeeding or failing, and the second following it out days later.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two incubated eggs, laid a few days apart.
At [the scrape](place_the_scrape.md), over the hatching period.

## Direction

Toward one chick or two, decided by the first egg.
About four times in ten the first does not hatch, and the second becomes the entire season: every fledgling of those years exists because the premium was paid.
About six times in ten it does hatch, and the second becomes a redundancy in a nest with one ration.

## Lever

Nothing anybody controls: the rate is a property of the species rather than of any nest, and no care at a particular scrape moves it.
Because it is fixed in advance and revealed only here, this step is the point at which the bet is settled.

## Echo

Either a season saved, or an insurance policy that has come due for cancellation.
[The claim](piece_the_claim.md) and [the days-old window](piece_the_days_old_window.md) are the two branches, and nothing distinguishes them until now.
