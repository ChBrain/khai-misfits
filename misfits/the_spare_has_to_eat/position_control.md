# Position: Control

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). The seat of the related species that does not need the insurance and does not buy it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

First eggs that hatch at least eighty-five per cent of the time.
About the same per-egg investment as its siblicidal relatives, so the cost of a spare would be similar.
A single-egg clutch, and no siblicide.

## Orders

Lay one.
Hatch it, nearly always.
Fledge it.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat is what turns the account into a test: the premium costs about the same in both kinds of species and only the benefit differs, so the trait appears precisely where the failure rate makes it worth paying for.
Because that pattern was predicted before it was checked across species rather than read off one, this seat is evidence rather than illustration.
It ends with one fledgling and no dead chick, for reasons that have nothing to do with temperament.

## Drives

Toward one fledgling a season,
never once facing the choice,
so the seat that proves the mechanism is the one the mechanism is absent from.
