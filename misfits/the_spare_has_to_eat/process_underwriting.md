# Process: Underwriting

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). Laying a second egg against the chance that the first does not hatch.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A first-egg hatching rate of roughly half to six in ten.
At [the scrape](place_the_scrape.md), a few days after the first egg.

## Direction

Toward a season that survives the first egg failing.
This is the right decision and the comparative record is what shows it: the premium costs about the same across species because females invest similarly per egg, so where first eggs hatch reliably the spare is not laid, and where they do not, it is.
And it is made under an ignorance that cannot be lifted, since the food that will decide how many can be raised is settled far away and later.

## Lever

[The premium](piece_the_premium.md), which is a full egg's investment and falls due every season.
Because it is paid before the outcome is known, it is paid in the six seasons where it turns out to be unnecessary as well as the four where it is not.

## Echo

Two eggs in a nest that will fledge one.
Nothing has gone wrong at this step and nothing could have been decided better.
