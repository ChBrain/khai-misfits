# Plan: Remove the Competition

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Direction

Compete for a ration that raises one, using the head start the staggered laying gave you, and remove what else is in the nest within days.

## Orders

([The Elder Chick](persona_the_elder_chick.md) hatches first, is larger by days, competes, and removes the younger without being prevented.)

## Implementation

([The Scrape](place_the_scrape.md), where the ration arrives; the [culling](process_culling.md) process inside [the days-old window](piece_the_days_old_window.md); the parent not intervening, because the ration raises one.)

## Targets

- [ ] the parent's laying and this removal are one arrangement seen from either end
