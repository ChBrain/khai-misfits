# Position: Spare

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). The seat of a working insurance that has to be destroyed when the risk does not materialise.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

About the same investment as the first egg, so it is a premium rather than a token.
The capacity to raise a perfectly good fledgling, exercised in four seasons in ten.
An appetite, drawn on the same ration as the elder.

## Orders

Be laid, a few days behind.
Hatch.
Be removed within days, in the seasons where the first hatched too.

## Loses

Its existence in six seasons of ten, and the seat is where the misfit lands: it is not destroyed for failing but for succeeding at a time when it was not needed.
Because it eats from the ration it was held against, it cannot be kept in reserve the way an unused spare can be kept, so redundancy that consumes must be resolved rather than shelved.
It ends as the whole of a season, or as nothing, decided by an egg it never met.

## Drives

Toward fledging, which it is entirely capable of,
never once given the chance in the ordinary case,
so the seat that saves four seasons in ten is the one that is killed in the other six.
