# Position: Primary

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). The seat the season is meant to run on, and which fails about four times in ten.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

First place in the laying order and every advantage the nest has.
An ordinary failure rate, from infertility, predation, breakage or chill.
No defect of its own.

## Orders

Hatch, about six times in ten.
Fail, about four.
Take the season with it when it fails, unless something else was laid.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and its unreliability is why everything else exists: a rate near a hundred per cent makes the second egg pure waste, and a rate near half makes it the difference between a season and nothing.
Because the rate is a property of the species rather than of any nest, no care at any particular scrape moves it.
It ends as the chick that removes its sibling, or as an egg that quietly did not hatch.

## Drives

Toward hatching,
never once at fault when it does not,
so the seat that is meant to carry the season is the reason a second one is bought.
