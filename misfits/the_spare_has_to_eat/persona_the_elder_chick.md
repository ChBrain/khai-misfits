# Persona: The Elder Chick

## Taxonomy

[Survivor](position_survivor.md). Hatched first, larger by days, and removes the younger from a nest that feeds one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Healthy and ordinary, doing what a chick in its position does.
Larger only because it hatched earlier, which is what the staggered laying arranged.
Not vicious and not aberrant: this is the species' normal course and not an incident.

## Action

Hatches first.
Competes with whatever else is in the scrape for a ration that raises one.
Removes it, within days, and is not prevented.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is where the play refuses its easiest villain: the elder is not cruel, it is competing for a food supply that cannot carry two, and a version of it that tolerated the younger would raise two underfed chicks instead of one fledged one.
Because the parent laid the second egg deliberately and the elder removes it deliberately, both acts are correct and they are the same arrangement seen from two ends.
It ends fledged, on a ration that would not have stretched.

## Tell

Says nothing, being a chick.
Its whole statement is that it is there in the autumn and the other one is not.
