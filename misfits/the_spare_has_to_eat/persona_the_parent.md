# Persona: The Parent

## Taxonomy

[Underwriter](position_underwriter.md). Lays two eggs in a season that will fledge one, which is the correct number to lay.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Experienced, well fed and doing exactly what the comparative record says pays.
Invests about the same in each egg, so the second is a real cost and not a token.
Not overreaching and not confused about capacity: it knows the ration raises one.

## Action

Lays the first egg, and then the second, a few days later.
Incubates both.
Provisions whatever hatches, out of a supply it does not control.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is where the whole structure is authored without an error in it: laying one egg loses four seasons in ten, laying two costs a premium every season, and there is no third number.
Because the food available cannot be known when the eggs are laid, the decision is made under an ignorance nothing at the nest can remove.
It ends having done the arithmetic correctly and having produced a chick that will not survive the week.

## Tell

Says nothing, and does not intervene when the elder removes the younger.
Which is not indifference: the ration raises one, and it knows which one it is.
