# Piece: The Unknowable Year

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). Which kind of season this is, decided far from the nest and revealed only after the eggs have hatched.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [the foraging range](place_the_foraging_range.md), read at [the scrape](place_the_scrape.md) far too late.
Answered nowhere: this is the one thing that would resolve the play, and it is not available.

## Load Bearing

Without it the parent lays one egg in good seasons and two in bad, and there is no cost anywhere.
It is load bearing because it names **the only real remedy and its unavailability**: everything else in the play follows from a decision that has to be made before the information arrives, and no competence, care or attention at any seat brings the information forward.

## Apparent

Uncertainty, of the ordinary kind every organism faces.
It reads as background noise rather than as the binding constraint, and the fact that the entire arrangement is a response to it and not to anything else is not visible from any single season.

## Yearbook

Imposed by [the foraging range](place_the_foraging_range.md) on [underwriting](process_underwriting.md).
It is why the clutch is a bet, and why the bet is placed the same way every season.
