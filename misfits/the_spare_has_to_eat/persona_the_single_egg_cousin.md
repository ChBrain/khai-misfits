# Persona: The Single-Egg Cousin

## Taxonomy

[Control](position_control.md). The related species whose first eggs hatch at least eighty-five per cent of the time, lays one, and has no siblicide.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not better parented and not more careful.
Differs in one measured respect: its first eggs almost always hatch.
Lays a single egg, raises it, and the question never arises.

## Action

Lays one.
Hatches it, nearly always.
Fledges it, with no sibling to remove and no premium to pay.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is the play's control: the premium costs about the same in both kinds of species, since females invest similarly per egg, and only the benefit differs, so the trait appears exactly where the failure rate makes it worth buying.
Because that pattern was predicted and then found across species rather than asserted from one, this seat converts the account from a story into a test.
It ends with one fledgling and no dead chick, for a reason that is nothing to do with temperament.

## Tell

Says nothing, and its clutch says it:
one egg, because one egg hatches.
