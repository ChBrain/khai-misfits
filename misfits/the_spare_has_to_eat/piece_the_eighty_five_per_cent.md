## Load Bearing

Without it the account is a story told about one kind of bird.
It is load bearing because it is **the control the comparison supplies**: per-egg investment is similar in both kinds, so the cost of a spare would be about the same, and only the benefit differs, which means the trait appearing exactly where first eggs fail often is a prediction confirmed rather than a pattern noticed.

## Apparent

It reads as the normal case against which the others look damaged, and what it actually is is the other end of a measured gradient in one quantity.
