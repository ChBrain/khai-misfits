# Plot: Within Days of Hatching

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Both eggs hatching, days apart. One ration, two chicks, and an elder with a head start.
[The Days-Old Window](piece_the_days_old_window.md), open.

## Action

[Culling](process_culling.md): [The Elder Chick](persona_the_elder_chick.md), in [the survivor seat](position_survivor.md), removes [The Second Egg](persona_the_second_egg.md)'s chick from [the scrape](place_the_scrape.md).

## Stage

[The Elder Chick](persona_the_elder_chick.md), on [Remove the Competition](plan_remove_the_competition.md), competes for a supply that cannot carry two and is not prevented.
[The Parent](persona_the_parent.md) does not intervene, which is not indifference: it laid the eggs under exactly this arrangement and it knows the ration raises one.

## Tension

The play refuses its easiest villain here, and the refusal is the point.
The elder is not vicious. It is larger only because it hatched days earlier, which the staggered laying arranged, and it is competing for a ration that cannot carry two: a tolerant version of it produces two underfed chicks rather than one fledged. And the window is short by design, so what is lost is an egg's investment rather than a season of divided food. **The parent's laying and this removal are one arrangement seen from either end**, and both are correct.
