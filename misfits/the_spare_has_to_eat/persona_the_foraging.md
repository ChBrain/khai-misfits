# Persona: The Foraging

## Taxonomy

[Constraint](position_constraint.md). The food supply, decided far from the nest, unknown at laying, and sufficient for one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Variable between seasons and not adjustable within one.
Set by conditions at sea or across a range, tens or hundreds of kilometres from the scrape.
Indifferent to how many eggs were laid.

## Action

Yields whatever it yields.
Is carried back, at whatever rate the adults can manage.
Raises one chick, in the ordinary case.

## Shadow

Nothing, and it is the binding constraint the whole play rests on: the clutch is decided before this is known, so the number of eggs is a bet placed against a quantity that will not be revealed until the chicks are already hatched.
Because it is neither observable in advance nor influenced by effort at the nest, no competence at any seat converts into knowing which season needs the spare.
It ends having fed one chick, as it was always going to.

## Tell

Says nothing until it is too late to change the clutch.
Which is the whole of the ignorance the second egg exists to cover.
