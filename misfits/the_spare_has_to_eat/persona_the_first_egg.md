# Persona: The First Egg

## Taxonomy

[Primary](position_primary.md). The one the season is meant to run on, which fails to hatch about four times in ten.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary in every respect and not defective.
Laid first, incubated properly, and given every advantage the nest has.
Fails for reasons that are ordinary too: infertility, predation, breakage, chill.

## Action

Is laid.
Hatches, about six times in ten.
Does not hatch, about four times in ten, and takes the season with it unless something else is there.

## Shadow

Nothing of its own, and its unreliability is the reason for everything else: a hatching rate near one hundred per cent would make the second egg a pure waste, and a rate near half makes it the difference between a season and nothing.
Because the rate is a property of the species rather than of any clutch, no care at any particular nest moves it.
It ends either as the chick that removes its sibling, or as an egg that never hatched and was quietly replaced.

## Tell

Says nothing, being an egg.
Its whole statement is a number in a colony table: fifty-one to sixty-one per cent.
