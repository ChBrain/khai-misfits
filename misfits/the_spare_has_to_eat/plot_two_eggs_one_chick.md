# Plot: Two Eggs, One Chick

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A clutch of two, in a species that fledges one.
[The Premium](piece_the_premium.md), about a full egg's investment, falling due at laying.

## Action

[Underwriting](process_underwriting.md): [The Parent](persona_the_parent.md), in [the underwriter's seat](position_underwriter.md), lays the second egg a few days after the first.

## Stage

[The Parent](persona_the_parent.md), on [Lay Two and Raise One](plan_lay_two_and_raise_one.md), invests about the same in each egg and incubates both.
[The Foraging](persona_the_foraging.md), in [the constraint seat](position_constraint.md), has already decided how many can be raised, tens or hundreds of kilometres away, and will not say.

## Tension

The second egg is insurance, not excess, and establishing that first is what makes the rest structural.
Females invest about the same in each egg, so this is a **priced bet** rather than an abundance of eggs. And it is placed in ignorance that nothing can lift: the food that will decide how many chicks can be raised is settled far from the nest and revealed only after they hatch. So the clutch is chosen against a quantity nobody at the scrape can consult, which is not a failure of attention and is the condition the whole arrangement answers.
