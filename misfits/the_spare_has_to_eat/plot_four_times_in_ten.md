## Cue

## Action

[Hatching](process_hatching.md): [The First Egg](persona_the_first_egg.md), in [the primary seat](position_primary.md), does not hatch, and [The Second Egg](persona_the_second_egg.md) becomes the season.

## Stage

[The First Egg](persona_the_first_egg.md), on [Fail Four Times in Ten](plan_fail_four_times_in_ten.md), fails from infertility, predation, breakage or chill, with no defect of its own and no carelessness at the nest.
[The Second Egg](persona_the_second_egg.md), on [Be There in Case](plan_be_there_in_case.md), hatches and raises the only fledgling of the year.

## Tension

This is where the whole practice is paid for, and it is worth saying before the difficult part.
About four seasons in ten the first egg produces nothing. In those seasons the second is not a spare: **it is the entire year**, and every fledgling of those years exists only because the premium was laid. So the arrangement balances across seasons rather than within one, and the six wasted premiums are bought by these four. Any account that starts with the killing has started in the middle.
