# Place: The Colony Record

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). Where hatching success is counted across nests, seasons and species, and where the practice stops being a curiosity.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Tables of eggs laid, eggs hatched and chicks fledged, gathered over years.
Two kinds of species side by side: those that lay two and reduce, and those that lay one.

## Holds

The test, and the only place it exists.
It holds the comparison that makes the account evidence: first-egg hatching success of roughly fifty-one to sixty-one per cent where the second egg is laid, against at least eighty-five per cent where it is not, with similar per-egg investment in both, so the cost is near constant and the benefit is what varies.

## Offers

A pattern across species that no single nest could show and that was predicted before it was counted.

## Withheld

Any account of the individual case.
The record says why the practice exists and never why this clutch failed, so the thing it explains is a species' arrangement rather than any season's outcome.
