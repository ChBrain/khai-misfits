# The Spare Has to Eat: Reference

The research warrant for The Spare Has to Eat.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the insurance-egg hypothesis and obligate siblicide, and on what happens to
a redundancy that draws on the very supply it was held against, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the second egg as **a policy whose premium falls due every season and whose claim is made in four of them**:
first eggs in these species hatch only about half to six times in ten, so a spare is worth laying; the spare carries
about the same investment as the first, so it is a real premium; and when the first does hatch, the spare is a chick
with an appetite drawn on a ration that raises one, so it is removed within days. The play models the documented
mechanics, the clutch chosen before the food is known, the failure rate that prices the premium, the seasons the spare
is the entire year, the ration set far from the nest, the days-old window in which the contest is settled, and the
related species whose first eggs hatch reliably and which lays one egg, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure,
not as a tale of a cruel chick or a negligent parent.

The governing law is that redundancy which consumes the resource it insures cannot be held once the risk has passed,
so a policy that works must be destroyed in exactly the cases where the thing it covered went well.

**Nobody in the sequence falls short, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The parent lays
the number that pays across seasons. The first egg fails for ordinary reasons. The spare is entirely capable of
fledging. The elder competes for a supply that cannot carry two. The ration is what the range yielded.

**And the premium is real, which the play establishes before anything else.** Females invest about the same in each
egg, so the second is a priced bet rather than an abundance of eggs. That is what makes the arrangement an insurance
policy and not fecundity, and it is what the comparative test turns on.

**Then the claim is where the whole practice is paid for.** About four seasons in ten the first egg produces nothing,
and in those seasons the second is not a spare but the entire year: every fledgling of those years exists only because
the premium was laid. The account balances **across** seasons rather than within one, so the six wasted premiums are
bought by these four, and any telling that begins with the killing has begun in the middle.

**And the decision is made in an ignorance nothing can lift.** The food that decides how many chicks can be raised is
settled tens or hundreds of kilometres from the nest and is revealed only after they have hatched. So the clutch is a
bet placed against a quantity nobody at the scrape can consult, which is not a failure of attention but the condition
the whole arrangement exists to answer.

**Then redundancy that eats cannot be shelved, which is the whole misfit.** An unused spare part sits on a shelf. A
spare chick draws on the very ration it was held against, so the policy has to be **cancelled rather than stored**. And
the cancellation is not for any failure: the second egg is removed precisely because it succeeded, in a season when the
risk it covered did not materialise. **It was not surplus to the plan. It was the plan, for a year that did not come.**

**And the removal is competition rather than cruelty.** The elder is larger only because it hatched days earlier, which
the staggered laying arranged, and it is competing for a supply that cannot carry two: a tolerant version of it
produces two underfed chicks instead of one fledged. The parent does not intervene, which is not indifference, since it
laid the eggs under exactly this arrangement. The window is short by design, so what is lost is an egg's investment
rather than a season of divided food.

**Then the comparison is the test, and it is what makes this evidence rather than a story.** Because per-egg investment
is similar across species, the **cost** of an insurance egg is roughly constant while the **benefit** depends entirely
on how often first eggs fail. The trait should therefore appear where the failure rate is high and be absent where it
is low. That is what the colonies show: first-egg hatching success of roughly **fifty-one to sixty-one per cent** in
the obligately siblicidal species, against **at least eighty-five per cent** in the single-egg species. The species
that does not need the insurance does not buy it, which is arithmetic rather than restraint.

It is distinct from the house's **Belt and Braces**, which is the nearest incumbent and stages the redundancy paradox,
where adding backups lowers reliability through common-mode failure, added complexity and the complacency a backup
invites. Remove that engine entirely, by stipulating that redundancy is perfectly effective, that no backup ever
introduces a failure mode and that nobody is made careless by having one, and this misfit stands whole: **the
redundancy here works.** The second egg genuinely produces a fledgling in the seasons the first fails. The difficulty
is not that the backup betrays its purpose but that it consumes the resource it was held against, so **there
redundancy fails, and here it succeeds and is killed.**

It is distinct from the house's **Not All Hers to Give**, which stages parent-offspring conflict, where parent and
offspring are selected to disagree over how much is transferred. Remove that engine, by stipulating that parent and
surviving chick agree completely that only one can be raised and that no interest diverges anywhere, and this stands
entire, because **the insurance structure needs no disagreement.** Two eggs are still laid, the spare is still the
season four times in ten, and it is still removed in the other six. That conflict is a real complication of this case,
and it is a complication rather than the engine, which is why the warrant cites it separately below and leaves the
disagreement to the incumbent.

It is distinct from the house's **The Brittle Optimum**, which stages the fragility of the lean, where slack is
optimised away and the system loses its capacity to absorb a disturbance. Remove that engine, by stipulating that no
slack is ever trimmed and that nothing has been optimised at all, and this stands, because **the slack here is
deliberately provided and then destroyed.** Nobody removed a margin to save cost; a margin was bought at full price and
had to be given up because it could not be carried, which is the opposite motion arriving at a related place.

It is distinct from the house's **Kept for the Worst**, which stages the broken antibiotic market, where a drug
conserved for the cases that most need it earns nothing while it is held in reserve. Remove that engine, by stipulating
that holding something in reserve costs its holder nothing and is fully rewarded, and this stands, because **the
reserve here cannot be held at all.** There the reserve sits unused and unpaid; here it eats, so the question is never
how to reward keeping it but whether keeping it is possible.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the elder chick as cruel
  or the parent as negligent, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the parent lays the number
  that pays across seasons, invests about the same in each egg, and cannot know at laying how much food the season
  will yield; the first egg fails for ordinary reasons and at a rate that is a property of the species rather than of
  any nest; the second egg is entirely capable of fledging and does so in the seasons it is needed; the elder is
  larger only because it hatched days earlier and is competing for a ration that cannot carry two, so tolerance would
  produce two underfed chicks instead of one fledged; and the parent does not intervene because the arrangement it
  laid the eggs under is exactly this one. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the
  misfit is that a redundancy which eats cannot be kept once it is no longer needed.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Keeping the second chick divides a
  ration that raises one and fledges neither well. Laying a single egg forfeits roughly four seasons in ten. Laying
  the second later is what the hatching asynchrony already does, and the asynchrony is precisely what makes the
  removal quick rather than a season of shared starvation. And the one measure that would actually resolve it,
  knowing in advance which season the first egg will fail, is set far from the nest and revealed only after the eggs
  have hatched, so no attention, effort or condition at any nest brings it forward.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed as the
  house's. The staging is the house's; the brood reduction frame is Lack's and the insurance-egg test and the
  parent-offspring strand are Anderson's. The bounds are carried with them: **how far obligate siblicide is explained
  by insurance rather than by food limitation, parental interest or other functions of the marginal offspring is
  genuinely contested**, and the play carries that argument rather than settling it; the hatching figures are ranges
  drawn from particular species and colonies over particular periods and are offered as the documented shape of the
  comparison rather than as constants; per-egg investment being similar across species is an assumption of the
  comparative argument and is stated as such; the account treats a season's food as one quantity where it is many, and
  treats obligately siblicidal species as one kind where they differ, which are its own simplifications; and the
  mechanism is stated for a redundancy that draws on the resource it insures, which is not every redundancy. **The
  play makes no claim about any species, any population or any nest, gives no biological or animal husbandry advice of
  any kind, and concerns birds and nothing else: nothing in it is about people, families or human siblings, and no
  reading of it in those terms is intended or supported.**
