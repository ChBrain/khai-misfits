# Place: The Scrape

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). The nest: two eggs laid days apart, and one chick in it by the end of the first week.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A bare hollow, two eggs, then two chicks briefly, then one.
A parent that incubates both and does not intervene when the number falls.

## Holds

The whole sequence, compressed into days.
It holds the fact that makes the arrangement legible rather than shocking: the removal happens within days of hatching, before much has been invested in the younger chick beyond the egg, which is what the staggered laying arranged and is why the resolution is quick.

## Offers

One fledgling, reliably, out of whatever the season turned out to be.

## Withheld

Any sign of which season this was going to be.
The eggs were laid before the food was known, so the scrape holds a decision made in ignorance and its consequence, and nothing in it distinguishes the two at the time it mattered.
