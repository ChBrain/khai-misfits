# Plan: Fail Four Times in Ten

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the primary seat](position_primary.md), inside [The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Primary](position_primary.md)

## Direction

Hatch about six times in ten and fail about four, from infertility, predation, breakage or chill, without any defect of your own and without any nest being at fault.

## Orders

([The First Egg](persona_the_first_egg.md) is laid first, incubated properly, given every advantage the nest has, and does not hatch about four times in ten.)

## Implementation

([The Scrape](place_the_scrape.md), where it is incubated; the [hatching](process_hatching.md) process recorded as [the failure rate](piece_the_failure_rate.md); against [the eighty-five per cent](piece_the_eighty_five_per_cent.md) of the species that never needed a spare.)

## Targets

- [ ] the failure is ordinary and is nobody's carelessness
- [ ] the rate is a property of the species rather than of any clutch
- [ ] a rate near a hundred per cent would make the second egg pure waste
- [ ] a rate near half makes it the difference between a season and nothing
