# Pitch: Bought Every Year, Needed in Four

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry, observational, unhurried.
The register of a field notebook beside a hatching table: how many laid, how many hatched, how many fledged, in which season, at which colony.
Nothing indignant anywhere in it, because there is no decision in the play that anybody gets wrong.

## Undertow

Under the clutch, two facts that do not negotiate.
An egg costs something to make. The food a parent can bring back is decided far from the nest and is not known when the eggs are laid.
Between those two, a spare that eats is a policy whose premium falls due every season and whose claim is made in four of them.

## Nerve

The unease of a redundancy that works,
is paid for honestly,
and has to be destroyed precisely in the years when the thing it was insuring against did not happen.

## Echo

After the run: one fledgling on a bare scrape,
a second egg that hatched and was gone inside a week,
and a related species down the coast that lays one egg, because its first eggs hatch nearly always and it never needed the policy at all.
