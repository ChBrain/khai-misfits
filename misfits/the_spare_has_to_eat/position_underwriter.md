# Position: Underwriter

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). The seat that decides how many eggs to lay against a food supply it cannot yet see.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A clutch size to choose, and about the same investment available for each egg.
A hatching rate for first eggs of roughly half to six in ten.
No information about the coming season's food.

## Orders

Lay two.
Incubate both.
Provision whatever hatches, out of whatever the range yields.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat authors the structure without an error in it: one egg loses four seasons in ten, two costs a premium every season, and there is no third number available.
Because the constraint is revealed only after the chicks have hatched, the decision is made under an ignorance nothing at the nest can remove.
It ends having chosen correctly and having produced a chick that will not last the week.

## Drives

Toward as many fledglings as the range will carry,
right in every single instance,
so the seat that gets the number right is the reason a chick is killed.
