# Position: Constraint

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). The seat of a food supply set far away, unknowable at laying, and sufficient for one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Variability between seasons and none within one.
A location tens or hundreds of kilometres from the nest.
Complete indifference to how many eggs were laid.

## Orders

Yield what it yields.
Be carried back at whatever rate the adults manage.
Raise one chick.

## Loses

Nothing, and it is what the whole play rests on: the clutch is decided before this is known, so the number of eggs is a bet against a quantity revealed only after the chicks hatch.
Because it is neither observable in advance nor movable by effort, no competence anywhere converts into knowing which season needs the spare.
It ends having fed one chick, as it was always going to.

## Drives

Toward nothing, having no intent,
never once announcing itself in time,
so the seat that decides the clutch is the one nobody can consult.
