# Persona: The Second Egg

## Taxonomy

[Spare](position_spare.md). The insurance: real, effective, and doomed in the majority of seasons.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not surplus, not an accident and not a lesser egg.
Carries about the same investment as the first, which is what makes it a premium rather than a token.
Would raise a perfectly good fledgling, and in four seasons in ten does exactly that.

## Action

Is laid a few days after the first.
Hatches into a chick.
Is removed within days, in the seasons where the first hatched too.

## Shadow

Its existence, in six seasons out of ten, and the seat is where the misfit lands: it is not destroyed because it failed but because it succeeded at the wrong time, having been held against a risk that did not materialise.
Because it eats from the same ration as the elder, it cannot be kept in reserve the way an unused thing can be kept, and redundancy that consumes is redundancy that must be resolved.
It ends as the whole of the season, or as nothing at all, and which one is decided by an egg it never met.

## Tell

Says nothing.
It was the plan, for a year that did not happen.
