# Process: Comparing

## Taxonomy

[The Spare Has to Eat](play_the_spare_has_to_eat.md). Setting the species that lay two beside the species that lay one, and reading the difference.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Hatching records across colonies, seasons and species.
At [the colony record](place_the_colony_record.md), aggregated.

## Direction

Toward the test rather than the story.
The prediction is specific: because females invest about the same in each egg, the **cost** of an insurance egg is roughly constant across species while the **benefit** depends on how often first eggs fail, so the practice should appear where the failure rate is high and be absent where it is low.
That is what the record shows: roughly fifty-one to sixty-one per cent first-egg hatching where the spare is laid, at least eighty-five per cent where it is not.

## Lever

Aggregation across species, which is the only level at which the pattern exists.
Because no single nest can display it, the account had to be built from many colonies over many years.

## Echo

An arrangement that is exactly where the arithmetic says it should be.
[The eighty-five per cent](piece_the_eighty_five_per_cent.md) is the half of the comparison that is easy to overlook and is the half that makes it evidence.
