# Process: Stratifying

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). Borne at [The Trial](place_the_trial.md), precipitated by [The Modeller](persona_the_modeller.md) reopening finished data to ask what the treatment does at each level of risk, in [Sort Them by Risk](plot_sort_them_by_risk.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A skew that is real and measurable, and someone unwilling to let the average stand as the last word on who benefits.

## Direction

From one effect for the whole population toward an effect read within strata of predicted baseline risk, so the minority who collect the benefit separate from the majority who do not.

## Lever

That predicting risk and stratifying on the prediction is not the subgroup analysis that fails, since it uses one multivariable prediction rather than testing covariates one at a time, and where applied it has separated who benefits (Kent, Hayward and colleagues).

## Echo

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the gap narrowed and the bill presented in data, validation, and residual uncertainty.
