# Persona: The Trialist

## Taxonomy

[Trialist](position_trialist.md). The one who reports the average because it is the honest ceiling of the design, and refuses the finer answer because it would not hold.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Rigorous and unshowy, running the study properly and reporting what it supports and nothing past it.
Randomises, follows up, counts, and publishes a figure that is exactly what was measured.
No overreach on show: it declines to slice the data into claims that will not survive replication, which is discipline and not caution.

## Action

Runs [Report the Average](plan_report_the_average.md): through [Averaging](process_averaging.md) at [the Trial](place_the_trial.md) it collapses thousands of unlike patients into [the Average](piece_the_average.md), a valid estimate of what the treatment does to a population.
Cannot report what the treatment does to a person, since the design does not estimate that and no amount of care extracts it.
Does the correct thing twice over: it produces the estimate the study supports and withholds the one it does not.

## Shadow

Not timid: subgroup claims made one variable at a time are documented to produce findings that do not replicate, so refusing them protects the literature rather than the seat.
Because the honest ceiling of the design is a population figure, its restraint is what sends a group-shaped number downstream to a decision that is person-shaped, and its integrity is the origin of the mismatch.
It is the most disciplined actor in the play and the one that hands the trap to everyone after it.

## Tell

Publishes an effect size and a confidence interval and considers the question answered, which within the study it is.
Cannot mark the figure with the fact that it describes nobody in particular, since there is no such field and the number would be no less correct if there were.
