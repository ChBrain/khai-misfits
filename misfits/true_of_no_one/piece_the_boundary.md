# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). The condition and the hedge: the trap needs real heterogeneity and a trial unable to resolve it, and lifts under risk-stratified analysis bought with data, validation, and residual uncertainty.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held at [The Trial](place_the_trial.md) when the data is reopened, where the remedy is demonstrated and its bill is itemised (Kent, Hayward and colleagues).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overclaims dangerously: without the boundary it reads as a case against guidelines, against trials, or as a licence for a clinician to substitute intuition, none of which the source supports and the last of which is the specific failure the whole discipline was built to end.
It bears the load as the honest limit on a result that invites exactly the wrong conclusion. It also prices the remedy: individual participant data pooled across trials that never expected to be pooled, a risk model externally validated or the overfitting merely moves, and an output that comes back a probability, so the gap narrows at real recurring cost and never closes.

## Apparent

A footnote, even: the conditions at the end of a finding whose headline travels far more easily than its qualifications.
