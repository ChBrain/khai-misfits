# Plan: Write One Rule

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the panel's seat](position_panel.md), inside [True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Panel](position_panel.md)

## Direction

Turn the estimate into something a clinic can act on: one recommendation, statable, followable, and auditable, graded honestly for the strength of the evidence beneath it. Do not hedge it into conditional branches the evidence has not established and no practitioner could apply at the bedside, since guidance nobody can follow is not guidance and will simply be ignored in favour of habit, which is worse. Accept that making the estimate operational requires making it uniform.

## Orders

([The Panel](persona_the_panel.md) converts through [Ruling](process_ruling.md) at [the Committee](place_the_committee.md), turning [the Average](piece_the_average.md) into [the Rule](piece_the_rule.md).)

## Implementation

([The Committee](place_the_committee.md) is where an estimate becomes an instruction; [Ruling](process_ruling.md) requires a single form to be followable, so the last representation of the distribution is removed here, and what reaches [the Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md) applies to everyone alike.)

## Targets

- [ ] one recommendation is issued, in a form a clinic can follow and an auditor can check
- [ ] the evidence grade honestly reflects the estimate the rule rests on
- [ ] no branch is added that the evidence has not established or the bedside cannot compute
- [ ] the rule is uniform, that being the price of being usable
