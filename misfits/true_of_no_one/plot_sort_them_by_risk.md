# Plot: Sort Them by Risk

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Trial](place_the_trial.md), reopened, with someone asking not what the treatment does on average but what it does at each level of risk.

## Action

[The Modeller](persona_the_modeller.md) on [Sort by Risk](plan_sort_by_risk.md) through [Stratifying](process_stratifying.md) holds [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): predict each patient's baseline risk and read the effect within strata of that prediction rather than one covariate at a time, and the minority who collect the benefit become separable from the majority who do not. The price is itemised: individual participant data rather than published summaries, often pooled across trials that never expected to be pooled; a risk model externally validated, or the overfitting is merely relocated; and an output that comes back as a probability. The scene closes with the gap narrowable at a real and recurring cost, the trap bounded (Kent, Hayward and colleagues).

## Stage

This is not the subgroup analysis that fails, since it stratifies on one multivariable prediction rather than testing covariates one at a time, and where it has been applied the minority who benefit have been separable.

## Tension

The honest output is a position in a distribution and the question asked was whether to treat this person, so the remedy narrows the gap without closing it and hands the judgement back to the room it came from.
