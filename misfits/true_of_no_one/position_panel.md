# Position: Panel

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). The seat that converts an estimate into guidance, which requires it to be uniform to be usable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A valid population estimate and a mandate to make it actionable.
A requirement that the output be statable, followable, and auditable, or it will not function as guidance at all.
No stratification the evidence has established and the clinic can compute, so no honest way to condition the rule.

## Orders

Write one rule: issue a single recommendation in the form the evidence supports, gradeable and applicable across the population studied.
Do not hedge it into branches the evidence has not earned and no practitioner could follow, since guidance nobody can apply is not guidance.
Accept what the seat does: it makes the estimate operational, and operational means the same for everyone.

## Loses

The spread, entirely and by necessity.
Because followability requires uniformity, the seat loses the last representation of the distribution the evidence rested on, and loses it not through carelessness but as the price of producing something a clinic can act on.
It keeps a rule the field can follow and loses every trace of who it does not fit.

## Drives

Toward the one whose diligence strips out the variation it was working from,
writing one rule because one rule is what guidance means and, by doing so, sending to the clinic a recommendation that applies to people the evidence never described,
so the seat that made the evidence usable is the seat that made it universal, honest in its grading and total in its reach.
