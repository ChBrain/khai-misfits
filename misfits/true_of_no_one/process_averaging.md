# Process: Averaging

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). Borne at [The Trial](place_the_trial.md), precipitated by [The Trialist](persona_the_trialist.md) reporting the estimate the design supports, in [One Number for Everyone](plot_one_number_for_everyone.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A randomised design whose valid output is the average effect across its participants and nothing narrower.

## Direction

From thousands of patients differing in baseline risk and biology toward one figure with an interval, sound as an estimate and silent about every member.

## Lever

That the finer answer is not merely unavailable but unreliable, since one-variable-at-a-time subgroup analysis produces claims that do not replicate, so the average is the honest ceiling and not a failure of nerve (Rothwell).

## Echo

[The Average](piece_the_average.md), valid and lossy in the same act.
