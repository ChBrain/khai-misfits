# Pitch: What the Evidence Says

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Careful and evidence-led.
A well-run trial, a real effect, a guideline written from it, and a clinician doing the disciplined thing by following the evidence rather than a hunch.
Nothing false in it: the trial is sound, the number is correct, and following validated evidence over intuition is the whole hard-won lesson of the discipline.

## Undertow

Under the sound chain, a number the wrong shape for the decision it is asked to make.
The one figure a trial can validly produce is an average over patients who differed enormously in what they stood to gain, and outcome risk is skewed, so a high-risk minority collected most of the benefit while harms fell evenly on everyone (Kent, Hayward and colleagues).
No fraud and no laziness: the trialist gives the honest ceiling of the design, the panel writes the only rule a clinic can follow, and the clinician applies the best evidence there is.

## Nerve

The confidence of standing on real evidence, and the catch that the evidence is about a crowd and the patient is one person:
that the validity which lets the number travel from trial to guideline is the same property that empties it for anyone in particular, the estimate and its inapplicability the one average seen twice,
and what the evidence says is a true sentence about a population being asked to decide for someone who is not it.

## Echo

After the run: a low-risk patient a decade into a preventive drug, carrying its side effects and its cost for a gain near zero, inside a chain where nobody misreported and nobody misapplied (Kravitz, Duan and Braslow).
And the remedy, at a real price and never complete: predict baseline risk and read the effect within strata of it, which wants individual participant data pooled across trials never designed to be pooled, an externally validated model or it merely relocates the overfitting, and still comes back a probability.
And the bound held in view: it needs a genuinely heterogeneous population and a trial unable to resolve who is who, so the whole of it is that a number can be right about everyone together and wrong about each of them apart.
