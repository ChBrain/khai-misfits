# Piece: The Average

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). The one estimate a randomised design can validly produce: correct about the population, silent about every member of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Produced at [The Trial](place_the_trial.md) and carried unchanged to [The Committee](place_the_committee.md), where it becomes an instruction (Kravitz, Duan and Braslow).

## Load Bearing

Remove its validity and there is no misfit, only a bad study: a wrong number misapplied is an error with an obvious remedy, and nobody needs a play about it.
It bears the load precisely because it is right. The trap requires an estimate sound enough to travel the whole chain unchallenged, since it is the soundness that carries it into the guideline and the guideline into the prescription, and no step along the way has grounds to stop it.

## Apparent

Knowledge, and truly so: it is what the treatment does, measured rather than asserted, and the alternative to having it is not a better number but no number.
