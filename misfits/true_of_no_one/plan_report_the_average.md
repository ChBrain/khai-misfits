# Plan: Report the Average

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the trialist's seat](position_trialist.md), inside [True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Trialist](position_trialist.md)

## Direction

Publish the estimate the design supports, with its interval, and let it stand as what was measured. Do not slice the data into subgroup claims tested one variable at a time, since those are documented to produce findings that do not replicate, and supplying an unreliable finer answer would corrupt the literature this seat exists to serve. Claim nothing about what the treatment did for any particular participant: randomisation was adopted precisely so that no such claim would be needed, and it does not deliver one.

## Orders

([The Trialist](persona_the_trialist.md) collapses the participants through [Averaging](process_averaging.md) at [the Trial](place_the_trial.md), producing [the Average](piece_the_average.md).)

## Implementation

([The Trial](place_the_trial.md) is where thousands of unlike patients become one figure; [Averaging](process_averaging.md) is valid and lossy at once, delivering a sound estimate of the population effect while discarding every trace of [the Spread](piece_the_spread.md) it was computed over.)

## Targets

- [ ] the average effect is reported with its interval, as measured
- [ ] no one-variable-at-a-time subgroup claim is published as a finding
- [ ] nothing is claimed about any individual participant's effect
- [ ] the honest ceiling of the design is given, and nothing past it
