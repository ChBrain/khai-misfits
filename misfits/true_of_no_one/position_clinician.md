# Position: Clinician

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). The seat where a rule about a population meets a person, with only one lever for telling them apart and that lever discredited.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The best validated evidence available, in the form of a rule that says what to do.
A patient who may sit anywhere in a distribution the rule does not mention.
One means of individualising, clinical impression, which the discipline has good evidence performs worse across a practice.

## Orders

Follow the evidence: treat as the rule directs, since the rule rests on a randomised finding and the alternative rests on a feeling.
Do not substitute an impression about this patient for a validated result, that substitution being the specific failure evidence-based practice was built to end.
Accept what the seat does: it applies a population rule to an individual, correctly, having no validated instrument for doing otherwise.

## Loses

The patient's own case, which it cannot see and cannot ask after.
Because the only lever for individualising is the one the seat is right to distrust, it loses every low-risk patient to years of side effects and cost for a gain near zero, and loses them without a record, since no field on the chart holds the mismatch.
It keeps a defensible decision and loses the person the decision was for.

## Drives

Toward the one standing closest to the harm with the fewest means of avoiding it,
following the evidence because following the evidence is right and the alternative is worse and, by doing so, delivering the treatment to the patient it will not help,
so the seat that did the disciplined thing is the seat at which the cost is finally paid, correct in the general case and mistaken in this one, with no way from here to tell which case this is.
