# Persona: The Modeller

## Taxonomy

[Modeller](position_modeller.md). The one who asks what the treatment does at each level of risk rather than on average, and pays for the answer in data, validation, and residual uncertainty.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Patient and methodical, reopening finished trials to ask a question they were not designed to answer.
Builds a model of baseline risk and reads the treatment effect within strata of that prediction, rather than testing one variable at a time.
No showmanship on show: the work is slow, technical, and produces a distribution where everyone wanted a number.

## Action

Runs [Sort by Risk](plan_sort_by_risk.md): through [Stratifying](process_stratifying.md) at [the Trial](place_the_trial.md) it separates the minority who collect the benefit from the majority who do not, holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) against [the Skew](piece_the_skew.md).
Cannot proceed on published summaries, since the method needs individual participant data, often pooled across trials that were never designed to be pooled and do not agree on what they recorded.
Does the one thing that reaches the mechanism, and hands back a probability rather than an answer.

## Shadow

Not a perfectionist obstructing practice: the skew is real and measurable, and where the method has been applied the minority who benefit have been separable, so this is a demonstrated correction and not a methodological wish.
Because an unvalidated risk model relocates the overfitting rather than curing it, its remedy is only as good as an external validation that is itself expensive and often unavailable, so the fix carries the failure mode it was built to end.
It is the play's only remedy and it returns the decision to the clinician still uncertain.

## Tell

Asks for the raw data and is told the trial reported its results, which it did, completely and correctly.
Cannot deliver what the consulting room actually wants, a yes or a no for this person, since the honest output is a position in a distribution and the decision remains a judgement.
