# Persona: The Clinician

## Taxonomy

[Clinician](position_clinician.md). The one who applies the rule to a person, because departing from validated evidence on a feeling is the thing the discipline exists to prevent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Conscientious and current, following guidance rather than habit, and able to say which evidence supports what it does.
Treats the patient in front of it according to the best available rule, which is what a careful practitioner is asked to do.
No arrogance on show: it does not substitute its own impression for a randomised finding, having been trained precisely out of that.

## Action

Runs [Follow the Evidence](plan_follow_the_evidence.md): through [Applying](process_applying.md) at [the Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md) it treats as [the Rule](piece_the_rule.md) directs, and [the Treated Regardless](piece_the_treated_regardless.md) follows for a patient who stood to gain almost nothing.
Cannot compute this patient's position in the distribution, since the risk model does not exist, is not validated, or is not available at the bedside, and the guideline offers no place to enter one.
Does the disciplined act: it applies the evidence rather than an intuition, which is right in general and wrong here without any way to tell the difference.

## Shadow

Not thoughtless: the alternative on offer is clinical impression, which is exactly what evidence-based medicine was built to displace and which performs worse across a practice, so the seat's compliance is well-founded.
Because the only lever it has for individualising is the intuition it was trained to distrust, its correct refusal to use that lever is what delivers the treatment to the patient it will not help.
It is the actor closest to the harm and the one with the least available means to avoid it.

## Tell

Documents the guideline the decision rests on, which is complete and accurate documentation and settles any audit.
Cannot record that this patient was unlike the average, since the chart has no field for it and the evidence supplies no number to enter, so the mismatch leaves no trace anywhere in the record.
