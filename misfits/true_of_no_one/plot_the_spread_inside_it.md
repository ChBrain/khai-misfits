# Plot: The Spread Inside It

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Trial](place_the_trial.md), with the one figure published and somebody asking what it was computed over.

## Action

[The Spread](piece_the_spread.md) shows: patients differing enormously in baseline risk of the outcome and in the biology the treatment acts on, so the effect was never one quantity but a distribution. And [the Skew](piece_the_skew.md) beneath it: outcome risk is unevenly distributed, so a high-risk minority stood to gain substantially while the low-risk majority stood to gain very little, and the harms fall far more evenly, since a side effect does not check baseline risk before arriving. The scene closes with most of the trial's benefit revealed as the property of a minority, and the average revealed as their lift (Kent, Hayward and colleagues).

## Stage

None of this contradicts the published figure. The average is exactly what it claims to be, and the distribution behind it is exactly what an average is computed over, so there is nothing here to correct.

## Tension

The shape of the distribution is what turns a technicality into a loss, and it is precisely the property that the reported figure, being a single number, has no way to carry forward.
