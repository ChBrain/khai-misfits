# Plan: Follow the Evidence

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the clinician's seat](position_clinician.md), inside [True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Clinician](position_clinician.md)

## Direction

Treat as the rule directs. The rule rests on a randomised finding and the alternative rests on your impression of the patient in front of you, and impression performs worse across a practice, which is the whole hard-won lesson the discipline is built on. Do not substitute a feeling about this case for a validated result. Where you cannot compute where this patient sits in the distribution, and you cannot, since the model does not exist or is not at the bedside, apply the rule as written and document the guidance it rests on.

## Orders

([The Clinician](persona_the_clinician.md) treats through [Applying](process_applying.md) at [the Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md), as [the Rule](piece_the_rule.md) directs, producing [the Treated Regardless](piece_the_treated_regardless.md).)

## Implementation

([The Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md) is where a population rule meets a person; [Applying](process_applying.md) carries the recommendation onto a patient who may sit anywhere in a distribution the rule does not mention, and the chart records the guidance followed and has no field for the fit.)

## Targets

- [ ] the rule is applied as written, and the guidance it rests on is documented
- [ ] no clinical impression is substituted for a validated finding
- [ ] where the patient's position in the distribution cannot be computed, the rule still governs
- [ ] the decision is defensible on audit, which it is, completely
