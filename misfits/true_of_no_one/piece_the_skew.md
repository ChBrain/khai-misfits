# Piece: The Skew

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). The unevenness of outcome risk: a high-risk minority collecting most of the benefit while harms and costs fall evenly across everyone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Latent in [The Trial](place_the_trial.md) and visible only once the data is reopened by risk rather than by covariate (Kent, Hayward and colleagues).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the spread is survivable: variation distributed symmetrically would leave the average a fair description of a typical patient, most people near it, and treating everyone alike a reasonable approximation rather than a systematic misallocation.
It bears the load as the thing that converts variation into harm. Because benefit concentrates where risk is high and harm does not concentrate anywhere, the low-risk majority carry a full share of the cost against a fraction of the gain, and the average that justified their treatment was lifted by patients who are not them.

## Apparent

A technical detail of distribution shape, the sort of thing that belongs in an appendix rather than in a decision about whether to take a drug for ten years.
