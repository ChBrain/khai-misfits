# Position: Modeller

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). The seat that recovers who benefits, at the price of data it must beg for and certainty it cannot supply.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A method that reads treatment effect within strata of predicted baseline risk rather than one variable at a time.
A requirement for individual participant data, often pooled across trials that never expected to be pooled.
No output better than a probability, the honest answer being a position in a distribution.

## Orders

Sort by risk: predict each patient's baseline risk, then read the effect within strata of that prediction, so the minority who collect the benefit separate from the majority who do not.
Validate the risk model externally, since an unvalidated one relocates the overfitting instead of curing it.
Accept what the seat does: it narrows the gap and returns the decision to the clinician still uncertain.

## Loses

The clean answer everyone downstream was waiting for.
Because the honest output is a probability and the question asked was whether to treat this person, the seat loses the one form of result that would actually settle the consulting room, and loses it necessarily rather than through any shortfall of method.
It keeps a real correction and loses the ability to end the judgement it was meant to inform.

## Drives

Toward the one whose demonstrated remedy arrives expensive, partial, and late,
stratifying because stratifying is the only thing that reaches the skew and, by doing so, buying visibility of who benefits with data that must be pooled and a model that must be validated,
so the seat holding the play's one correction is the seat that narrows the gap without closing it, and hands back a distribution where a decision was wanted.
