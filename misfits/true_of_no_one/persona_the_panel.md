# Persona: The Panel

## Taxonomy

[Panel](position_panel.md). The one who turns a valid estimate into a recommendation a clinic can actually follow, which means one rule for everyone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Deliberate and consultative, weighing the evidence and issuing guidance the field can act on.
Produces something statable, followable, and auditable, which is the whole point of guidance and the reason anyone waited for it.
No shortcut on show: the rule is drawn from the best available evidence and says what that evidence supports.

## Action

Runs [Write One Rule](plan_write_one_rule.md): through [Ruling](process_ruling.md) at [the Committee](place_the_committee.md) it converts [the Average](piece_the_average.md) into [the Rule](piece_the_rule.md), a single recommendation in the form the evidence supports.
Cannot issue guidance conditioned on a risk stratification the evidence has not established and the clinic cannot compute, since a rule nobody can apply is not guidance.
Does what the seat exists for: it makes a population estimate operational, and operational means uniform.

## Shadow

Not lazy: a guideline hedged into fifty conditional branches is not a guideline, it is a literature review, and the panel that issued one would be replaced by one that did not.
Because followability requires uniformity and the evidence beneath it is a distribution, its diligence is what strips the last trace of the spread out of the thing that reaches the clinic.
It is the actor that makes the evidence usable and, in the same act, makes it apply to people it was never about.

## Tell

Publishes a recommendation with a strength rating and an evidence grade, both honestly assigned to the average it rests on.
Cannot grade the rule for the patient it will meet, since strength of evidence describes the estimate and not the fit, so a strong recommendation and a poor fit sit together without contradiction.
