## Shown

## Holds

## Offers

To the field, a sound estimate of what the treatment does to a population, which is what the design was built to deliver.

## Withheld

What the treatment did for any particular participant, which randomisation was adopted precisely to avoid needing, so the one thing the clinic will want is the one thing never recorded.
