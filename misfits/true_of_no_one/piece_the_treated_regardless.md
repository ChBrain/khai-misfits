# Piece: The Treated Regardless

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). The low-risk patient carrying years of side effects and cost for a gain near zero, inside a chain where every step was correct.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Arriving at [The Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md), documented completely as guideline-concordant care, and recorded nowhere as a mismatch (Kravitz, Duan and Braslow).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit is a seminar point: a gap between averages and individuals that cost nobody anything would be a methodological curiosity, and the chain from trial to clinic could be left exactly as it is.
It bears the load as the actual harm, and as the harm's invisibility. Because the treatment is guideline-concordant, the decision defensible, and the documentation complete, nothing in the record marks the patient who should not have been there, so the cost accumulates across a practice while every individual case passes audit.

## Apparent

Good care, and by every available standard it is: the best evidence, correctly applied, properly documented, by a clinician who declined to guess.

## Yearbook

Stands over the close of [The Patient in Front of You](plot_the_patient_in_front_of_you.md), the cost arriving where nobody can see it.
It works together with [The Rule](piece_the_rule.md), the instruction and its landing, correct at one end and wrong at the other.
