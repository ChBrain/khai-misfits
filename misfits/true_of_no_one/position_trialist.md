# Position: Trialist

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). The seat that produces the one estimate a randomised design can honestly support, and withholds the finer one it cannot.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A design that delivers a valid estimate of the average effect and of nothing narrower.
Documented evidence that one-variable-at-a-time subgroup claims do not replicate.
No means of estimating what the treatment did for any particular participant, the trial having randomised precisely to avoid needing to.

## Orders

Report the average: publish what the study supports, with its interval, and let it stand as what was measured.
Withhold the finer answer, since supplying an unreliable subgroup claim would corrupt the literature the seat exists to serve.
Accept what the seat does: it gives the honest ceiling, and the honest ceiling is a statement about a population.

## Loses

Nothing in its own terms, which is the shape of it.
Because the seat is judged on the validity of its estimate and its estimate is valid, it loses nothing it is accountable for, and the cost it originates lands entirely in a decision it never makes and cannot see from here.
It keeps a sound result and loses only the applicability that was never the study's to supply.

## Drives

Toward the one whose restraint is the origin of the mismatch,
reporting the average because the average is what the design supports and, by doing so, sending a group-shaped number down a chain that ends at a person,
so the seat most beyond reproach is the seat from which the trap is issued, correct in every claim and the wrong shape at the far end.
