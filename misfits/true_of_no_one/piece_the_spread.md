# Piece: The Spread

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). The real variation the average was computed over: patients differing in baseline risk of the outcome and in the biology the treatment acts on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Present throughout [The Trial](place_the_trial.md) and represented nowhere in what leaves it, since the reported figure carries no trace of the distribution behind it (Kravitz, Duan and Braslow).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the average fits everyone: a population genuinely homogeneous in what it stood to gain would be perfectly described by its mean, the guideline would be right about each member, and the chain from trial to prescription would work exactly as it is supposed to.
It bears the load as the condition that makes a valid number inapplicable. Because the effect was never one quantity but a distribution, the figure that survives into practice is a summary of something that has no single value, and everything downstream inherits that.

## Apparent

Noise, or nothing at all: variation around a mean reads as the ordinary scatter every estimate has, rather than as differences in who the treatment was ever going to help.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [The Spread Inside It](plot_the_spread_inside_it.md), the distribution opened up.
It works together with [The Skew](piece_the_skew.md), the variation and the shape of it, the second turning the first from a technicality into a loss.
