# Piece: The Rule

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). The average made operational: one recommendation, statable, followable, auditable, and therefore the same for everyone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Drafted at [The Committee](place_the_committee.md) and carried to [The Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md), where it is the only form of the evidence anyone actually meets.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the estimate never reaches a patient: without translation into a followable instruction the trial's finding stays in the literature, and whatever happens in the clinic happens on habit instead, which is the worse outcome and the reason guidelines exist.
It bears the load as the necessary step that completes the trap. Because usability demands uniformity, this is where the last representation of the distribution is stripped out, so the thing that arrives at the bedside is not a summary of a distribution but an instruction about a person.

## Apparent

Rigour operationalised: a graded, evidence-based recommendation is the visible proof that practice is being governed by findings rather than by custom.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Write It for the Room](plot_write_it_for_the_room.md), the estimate becoming an instruction.
It works together with [The Treated Regardless](piece_the_treated_regardless.md), the instruction and the patient it lands on.
