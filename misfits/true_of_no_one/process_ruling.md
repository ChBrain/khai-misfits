# Process: Ruling

## Taxonomy

[True of No One](play_true_of_no_one.md). Borne at [The Committee](place_the_committee.md), precipitated by [The Panel](persona_the_panel.md) converting an estimate into guidance a clinic can follow, in [Write It for the Room](plot_write_it_for_the_room.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A valid population estimate and a mandate to make it actionable in settings that must be able to apply and audit it.

## Direction

From an estimate with a distribution behind it toward a single recommendation with none, statable, followable, and the same for everyone.

## Lever

That followability requires uniformity: a guideline hedged into branches the evidence has not established and the bedside cannot compute is not guidance, and would be displaced by habit, which is worse.

## Echo

[The Rule](piece_the_rule.md), operational at last, and applying to people the evidence never described.
