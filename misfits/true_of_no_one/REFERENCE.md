# True of No One: Reference

The research warrant for **True of No One**, a staging of the phenomenon the literature calls
**heterogeneity of treatment effects** (Kravitz, Duan and Braslow): that a randomised trial can validly
estimate only the average effect across its participants, while those participants differ substantially
in what they stood to gain, so a guideline written from the average and applied to an individual treats
many patients for a benefit that accrued, in the trial itself, mostly to a minority at high risk, with
nobody misreporting and nobody misapplying. It is held with the finding that the effect is conditional
on a genuinely heterogeneous population and a trial unable to resolve who is who, and that it lifts
under risk-stratified analysis, which costs pooled individual participant data, external validation, and
still returns a probability rather than an answer.

## Line of Work

Staging heterogeneity of treatment effects as a **valid-average-wrong-shape structure**: a trial is run
properly and yields a number that is correct, the reduction in the bad outcome the treatment produces
across the randomised population. That number is an average, and the patients averaged over were not
interchangeable, differing in baseline risk of the outcome and in the biology the treatment acts on, so
the effect was never one quantity but a distribution across them (Kravitz, Duan and Braslow). The trial
reports the average because the average is the honest ceiling of what its design supports, and this is
not timidity: conventional subgroup analysis, testing one variable at a time, is well documented to
produce claims that do not replicate, so a trialist who supplied the finer answer would be supplying an
unreliable one (Rothwell). The spread inside the average is not gentle. Risk of the outcome is typically
skewed across a population, so a high-risk minority stand to gain substantially while the low-risk
majority stand to gain very little, and harms and costs fall far more evenly, since a side effect does
not check baseline risk before arriving; put together, most of the total benefit observed in a trial is
often collected by a minority of its participants, and the average is their lift (Kent, Hayward and
colleagues). A guideline panel then converts the average into a recommendation, because a recommendation
must be statable, followable, and auditable to function at all, and a guideline hedged into fifty
conditional branches is not one. And a clinician applies it, because departing from the best validated
evidence on a feeling about a particular patient is precisely what evidence-based practice was built to
prevent. The play models the documented mechanics: the population that genuinely differs, the design
whose valid output is one number, the skew that concentrates benefit in a minority while harm spreads
evenly, the rule that must be written in a followable form, the application of that rule to an
individual, and the risk-stratified analysis that alone separates the minority who benefit, as the
load-bearing vectors of the structure, and not as a claim that trials mislead, that guidelines are
wrong, or that clinicians should follow intuition instead. The ordinary expectation is that a valid
estimate is a usable one; the finding is that validity and applicability are different properties, and
the number that travels soundly from trial to guideline is a description of a population being asked to
decide for a person. The governing law is that **where treatment effect varies across a population and
the design can estimate only its average, that average is the estimate that survives into practice, so
patients unlike the average are treated as though they were it, carrying the harms while the benefit
that justified the rule accrued mostly elsewhere**.

It is an inference-and-decision structure no staged misfit holds, and it is carefully distinct from its
nearest neighbours. It is distinct from The Proxy, statistical discrimination, where an agent unable to
observe an individual's quality prices them by their group's average, the rated-low member rationally
under-invests in signalling, and the group's real average falls to meet the rating: there the engine is
a **self-confirming loop** that changes the world, the assumed average becoming the produced average,
whereas here nothing is confirmed and nothing changes, the average being simply correct and the trap
lying entirely in applying a true group figure to a member who differs from it. It is distinct from
Better in Both, the Will Rogers phenomenon, where improved detection migrates cases between fixed
categories and lifts every category's average with no individual altered: there the average is an
**artefact** produced by reclassification, whereas here the average is accurate, undistorted, and
exactly what it claims to be, and the failure is downstream of a correct number. It is distinct from The
Bigger Picture, Simpson's paradox, where an unevenly distributed confounder makes a trend reverse
between the pooled and the stratified reading: there **two levels of one table disagree** and the
question is which to trust, whereas here the levels do not conflict, the average and the strata being
consistent, and the difficulty is that the level the evidence can support is not the level the decision
requires. And it is distinct from Overfitting, where a model fitted to a sample's noise fails on new
data: there the fault is **too much fitting**, whereas here it is deliberate under-fitting, one
parameter for a whole population, adopted precisely because fitting further is known to produce findings
that do not hold.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast a
  misleading trialist, a lazy panel, or a thoughtless clinician, because the finding it stages is that
  each is doing the disciplined thing: the trialist reports the average because it is the honest ceiling
  of the design and the finer answer would not replicate (Rothwell); the panel writes one followable
  rule because a recommendation hedged into fifty branches cannot function as a recommendation; and the
  clinician applies the best validated evidence rather than a feeling about this patient, which is
  precisely what evidence-based medicine was built to enforce. The loss is authored by no one: it is a
  population that genuinely differs, a design that can estimate only its average, and a skew that
  concentrates the benefit, so a patient unlike the average is treated as though they were it.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Risk-stratified
  analysis does work, and it is expensive: it wants individual participant data rather than published
  summaries, often pooled across trials never designed to be pooled; it wants the risk model externally
  validated, without which it merely relocates the overfitting it was meant to cure; and when done well
  it returns a probability rather than an answer, so the clinician is left deciding for a person the
  evidence can describe only in distribution (Kent, Hayward and colleagues). The reckoning is that the
  gap between a valid estimate and an individual decision can be narrowed at real and recurring cost and
  never closed.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The naming and the account of heterogeneity of treatment effects are Kravitz,
  Duan, and Braslow's; the finding that outcome risk is skewed so benefit concentrates in a minority,
  and the risk-stratified method that recovers it, are Kent, Hayward, and colleagues'; and the
  documentation that conventional subgroup analysis produces unreliable findings is Rothwell's. The
  scope is held to the structural gap between an average effect and an individual decision, and the play
  is not offered as medical advice, as a reason for any patient to stop a treatment, or as a claim that
  guidelines are generally wrong or that trials generally mislead. The opposite is nearer the point: the
  trial is sound, the guideline is drawn correctly from it, and the difficulty survives all of that
  soundness, which is what makes it a structural trap rather than an error to be corrected.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per element, one per load-bearing vector, no filler.

- **The Average**: carries the valid estimate (Kravitz, Duan and Braslow; Rothwell), the one number a
  randomised design can honestly produce, correct about the population and silent about any member, the
  honest ceiling rather than a shortfall.
- **The Spread**: carries the heterogeneity (Kravitz, Duan and Braslow), the real variation in baseline
  risk and in the biology the treatment acts on, so the effect was never one quantity but a distribution
  collapsed into one figure.
- **The Skew**: carries the concentration (Kent, Hayward and colleagues), outcome risk unevenly
  distributed so a high-risk minority collect most of the benefit while harms fall evenly across
  everyone, which is what turns a spread into a loss.
- **The Rule**: carries the translation, the average converted into a statable, followable, auditable
  recommendation, in the only form a clinic can act on and therefore in a form that applies to all.
- **The Treated Regardless**: carries the phenomenon whole, the low-risk patient carrying years of side
  effects and cost for a gain near zero, inside a chain where every step was correct.
- **The Boundary**: carries the condition and the hedge (Kent, Hayward and colleagues), that the trap
  needs genuine heterogeneity and a trial unable to resolve it, and lifts under risk-stratified analysis
  bought with pooled individual data, external validation, and an answer that returns as a probability.
