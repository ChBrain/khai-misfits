# Plot: The Swallowed Signal

## Taxonomy

[Too Kind to Drop](play_too_kind_to_drop.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Loop](place_the_loop.md), the feedback path from bottleneck back to sender, where a lost packet should be reporting in and none is.

## Action

[Hiding](process_hiding.md): [The Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md), holding [the keeper's seat](position_keeper.md), on [Never Drop](plan_never_drop.md), takes into [The Buffer](piece_the_buffer.md) what should have fallen, carrying [The Concealment](piece_the_concealment.md) between [The Signal](piece_the_signal.md) and [The Sender](persona_the_sender.md).

## Stage

Every packet that arrives past the point the link can carry is absorbed rather than dropped, exactly what the deep buffer was stocked for. No loss reaches the Sender, so no instruction to slow down is ever sent: the Signal is not blocked or falsified, it simply never comes into being, concealed inside a buffer doing its job (Gettys and Nichols). The Sender, told nothing, reads silence as room and keeps raising its rate. The Buffer fills, packet by packet, until there is no more room left to absorb into.

## Tension

The Keeper has not erred and the Sender has not been deceived; each has simply kept doing what it was built to do, with nothing between them to say stop. The Buffer now sits full, holding a debt of unsent signal it cannot discharge, and every packet still arriving has to wait behind everything already there.
