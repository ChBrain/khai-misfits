# Plot: The Good Queue

## Taxonomy

[Too Kind to Drop](play_too_kind_to_drop.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Bottleneck](place_the_bottleneck.md), the standing queue laid open for diagnosis, the burst it once absorbed long since gone.

## Action

The reckoning, not a further move: [The Buffer](piece_the_buffer.md) did exactly the job it was given, and [The Standing Queue](piece_the_standing_queue.md) is that same job continued past the point it was needed, with [The Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md) on [Never Drop](plan_never_drop.md) and [The Sender](persona_the_sender.md) on [Fill the Pipe](plan_fill_the_pipe.md) both still doing exactly what each was built to do.

## Stage

No link is overloaded on average, no one misconfigured anything, no one behaved badly: a queue that absorbs a burst and drains between bursts is a good queue, and this one never drains, a bad, standing queue built from the same buffer by the same correct choices (Nichols and Jacobson). The diagnosis names the difference precisely: transient versus persistent, not present versus absent capacity. The buffer's protection worked too well and never let go.

## Tension

If nothing is broken and no one is at fault, the fix cannot be to scold the Keeper or shame the Sender, and shrinking the buffer only trades one failure for another under a real burst. The only lever left is the signal itself: get it back to the Sender before the buffer fills, not after.
