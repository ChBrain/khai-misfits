# Process: Bloating

## Taxonomy

[Too Kind to Drop](play_too_kind_to_drop.md). Run by [the Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md) on [Never Drop](plan_never_drop.md), borne by [the Interactive](persona_the_interactive.md), at [The Bottleneck](place_the_bottleneck.md), inside [The Stall](plot_the_stall.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Buffer](piece_the_buffer.md) reaching the top with [Hiding](process_hiding.md) still in force: once memory runs out at the same moment the signal that would have stopped the climb has been concealed, the queue that was draining stops draining and does not resume on its own.

## Direction

From transient to persistent: what began as [The Good Queue](plot_the_good_queue.md), a burst absorbed and about to drain, does not drain, and every packet arriving behind it, including [the Interactive](persona_the_interactive.md)'s, now crosses a full buffer rather than an emptying one, so the wait grows for all of them together, not for the flow that filled it.

## Lever

A standing queue substituting for a transient one at a link whose throughput reading never falters (Gettys and Nichols): the buffer keeps moving bytes at full capacity the whole time, so nothing on the usual meter shows what changed, only the wait behind the full buffer, hundreds of milliseconds or seconds, that every crossing packet now pays.

## Echo

[The Latency](piece_the_latency.md) borne first by [the Interactive](persona_the_interactive.md), the flow with the least room to absorb delay: a call that stutters, a page that stalls, the instant someone on the same link starts a large upload, throughput still reading full the whole time.
