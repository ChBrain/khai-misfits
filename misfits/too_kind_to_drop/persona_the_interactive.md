# Persona: The Interactive

## Taxonomy

[Interactive](position_interactive.md). The latency-sensitive flow that needs only to cross quickly to be useful, a video call, a keystroke, a click, and that bears the ballooned delay behind a full buffer it never filled and cannot clear.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Light, urgent, small.
Is the flow whose whole value is timeliness: a voice that must arrive as it is spoken, a key that must show as it is pressed, carrying little data and asking only to be quick.
No demand in it beyond promptness: it does not want the link's capacity, only its responsiveness, and on an unbloated link it would have it with room to spare.

## Action

Takes its place on [Get Through](plan_get_through.md), bearing [the Latency](piece_the_latency.md) behind [the Standing Queue](piece_the_standing_queue.md) at [the Bottleneck](place_the_bottleneck.md), and suffers [Bloating](process_bloating.md).
Cannot jump the buffer: its packets enter the same full buffer as everyone's and wait out the same standing queue, so a flow that needs milliseconds is made to wait hundreds of them, or seconds, for want of any way past the backlog.
Does what a timely flow does behind a full buffer it did not fill: it stutters, lags, and stalls, its value spent in the wait.

## Shadow

Not a victim of anyone and not wronged by a decision: no flow attacked it, no buffer singled it out, no rule was bent against it.
Because the standing queue delays every packet alike and its value is timeliness alone, the Interactive bears the whole cost of a bloat it had no part in making, sharing a link with a bulk flow whose ramp filled a buffer that now taxes them both, and there is no misconduct to blame for that, only a queue that does not know a call from an upload.
It is the plainest reading of the cost, and it is caused by nothing but a buffer too deep to drain.

## Tell

Says the connection is slow, though the speed test reads full and the download finishes fine.
Cannot point to a fault, because there is none to point to, only a standing queue between its every packet and the far end, turning a link that measures healthy into one that cannot carry a conversation.
