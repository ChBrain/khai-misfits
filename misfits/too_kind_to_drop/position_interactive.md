# Position: Interactive

## Taxonomy

[Too Kind to Drop](play_too_kind_to_drop.md). The seat of the latency-sensitive flow, whose whole value is timeliness and which shares the link with a bulk flow, bearing the standing queue's delay behind a buffer it never filled.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Little data and one need: to cross the link quickly, a voice or a keystroke or a click whose worth is entirely in arriving on time.
A modest claim: it asks nothing of the link's capacity, only its responsiveness, and on a link whose queue stays short it would have that easily.
No way past the backlog, because its packets enter the same buffer as the bulk flow's and must wait out the same standing queue, with no lane of its own.

## Orders

Get through.
Send the small, timely packets the moment they are ready and ask only that they arrive promptly, taking a place in the queue like any other traffic.
Depend on a short queue, because timeliness is the whole value, and behave no differently when the queue is long, having no means to jump it.

## Loses

The one thing it needed, promptness, and with it its entire worth.
Because the standing queue delays every packet alike and the seat has no way around it, a flow that needed milliseconds waits hundreds of them, or seconds, and a conversation or a keypress that must be timely to mean anything is made late.
It loses everything to a delay it had no hand in causing, on a link whose throughput still reads full.

## Drives

Toward the plainest bearer of the cost,
a timely flow stalled behind a buffer a bulk flow filled and it cannot clear,
so the seat that asked only to be quick is the seat the bloat taxes hardest, wronged by no one and slowed by a queue that cannot tell a call from an upload.
