# Plan: Get Through

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the interactive's seat](position_interactive.md), inside [Too Kind to Drop](play_too_kind_to_drop.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Interactive](position_interactive.md)

## Direction

Send the small, timely packets the moment they are ready and ask only that they arrive promptly, taking a place in the queue like any other traffic. Depend on a short queue, because timeliness is the whole value, and behave no differently when the queue is long, having no lane of your own and no way to jump the backlog.

## Orders

([The Interactive](persona_the_interactive.md) sends its timely packets into [the Bottleneck](place_the_bottleneck.md), and behind [the Standing Queue](piece_the_standing_queue.md) bears [the Latency](piece_the_latency.md) through [Bloating](process_bloating.md).)

## Implementation

([The Bottleneck](place_the_bottleneck.md) mixes the timely flow with the bulk one in a single buffer; the [bloating](process_bloating.md) process holds them behind a standing queue that delays every packet alike, so a flow that needed milliseconds waits hundreds of them, its whole worth spent in a delay it had no part in causing.)

## Targets

- [ ] the small, timely packets are sent the moment they are ready, asking only promptness
- [ ] a place is taken in the shared queue, with no lane of its own and no way past the backlog
- [ ] a short queue is depended on, timeliness being the entire value of the flow
- [ ] the standing queue delays every packet alike, so the timely flow bears a cost it did not cause
