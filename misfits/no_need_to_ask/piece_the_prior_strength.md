# Piece: The Prior Strength

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The remedy and its bound together: the accountable institutions kept or built before the windfall lands, and the acknowledgment that not every resource-rich state needs them, because not every resource-rich state is cursed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Ministry](place_the_ministry.md), carried by [Building](process_building.md), voiced by [The Steward](persona_the_steward.md), who taxes where taxing is unnecessary and binds a treasury that could simply be spent.

## Load Bearing

Hold the fiscal link open by design where the rents would otherwise let it close by default: tax the citizens even when the rents make taxing needless, bind the windfall in a transparent fund, publish the accounts, so the state answers to its people by design where it need not by necessity. The curse itself is conditional on the institutions the windfall meets: producer-friendly institutions turn the resource into a blessing and grabber-friendly ones turn it into a curse, so a state that entered the boom already accountable escapes it, Norway and Botswana the standing counterexamples (Mehlum, Moene and Torvik). It carries the bound the whole misfit stands under: the very cross-national link between resource wealth and unaccountable rule has been challenged on long runs of within-country data, which finds little support for it (Haber and Menaldo), so the curse is contested and conditional, not a determinism, and not every resource-rich state is cursed. The remedy is costly and rare precisely because it asks a state to lay on itself the discipline the rents were meant to spare it, and it works best exactly where it is least needed, where the institutions were already strong before the ground paid out.

## Apparent

Looks like needless self-denial: a government taxing a public it could simply subsidise, locking away money it could spend today, discipline with no visible enemy to justify it.
