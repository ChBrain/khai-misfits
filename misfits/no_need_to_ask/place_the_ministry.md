# Place: The Ministry

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). Where the accountability institutions, a capable bureaucracy, courts, a legislature that holds the purse, would sit, and where instead they thin for want of the pressure that builds them; where a steward would build them anyway. Hosts [The Checks Wither](plot_the_checks_wither.md), [Build It First](plot_build_it_first.md).

## Owner

## Shown

The offices of the checks, half staffed and half built: a budget office with no mandate to question the budget, a legislature that never learned to ask where the money came from because it never had to.

## Holds

[The Hollow Institutions](piece_the_hollow_institutions.md). [The Prior Strength](piece_the_prior_strength.md).

## Offers

To whoever would build them, the place the checks belong, standing open and waiting to be filled in earnest.

## Withheld

The pressure from a taxed public that alone builds and keeps institutions strong. No one presses the ministry to grow, so absent a deliberate hand it does not.
