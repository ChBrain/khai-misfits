# Persona: The Well

## Taxonomy

[Well](position_well.md), inside [No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The archetype of the resource itself, the source that pays the state directly from the ground, so the treasury is filled without the treasury ever turning to the people.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A pure blessing, a gift from the earth. It looks like nothing but fortune, wealth welling up from below at no one's expense, the luck of a nation that happened to sit on riches.

## Action

Flow to the state. On [Pay the State](plan_pay_the_state.md) through [Flowing In](process_flowing_in.md), the Well delivers [the Rents](piece_the_rents.md) to [the Treasury](place_the_treasury.md), the money sold abroad and reaching the state without passing through its citizens' pockets, so that what fills the treasury never had to be asked of anyone (Mahdavy).

## Shadow

Its blessing is the very thing that severs the link. Because the Well pays the state and not the people pay it, the state is loosed from having to tax them, and so from having to bargain with or answer to them, so the gift from the earth quietly cuts the tie that a taxing state cannot escape. It means nothing by it and is neither good nor ill, only a source that happens to pay the state instead of the state's own citizens, and the harm is in the direction of the flow and not in any intent.

## Tell

It fills the treasury from the ground, so the treasury never has to turn to the people.
