# Piece: The Hollow Institutions

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The harm made visible: the capable bureaucracy, the honest court, the legislature that holds the purse, none of them built, none of them forced into being by a pressure that never arrived.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Ministry](place_the_ministry.md), met by [The Sovereign](persona_the_sovereign.md), who finds the checks it never built and never needed to.

## Load Bearing

Institutions of accountability are not simply willed into existence; they are forced into being by a public that must be answered because it is taxed, and where no taxed public presses, the checks atrophy for want of the pressure that builds them. The same funding that spares the state a tax also spares it the more distasteful trade, since a treasury filled from the ground can afford repression without ever raising a rate: the rentier effect and the repression effect running side by side to the same end, a state neither pressed from below nor priced out of holding the line (Ross). Worth holding apart from Kleptocracy, the tale of a thief in the palace: this is not that tale. The harm bites a government of ordinary good will as surely as a greedy one, because it is the funding and not the character that severs the link, and a ruler who means well finds the checks no more built than one who means ill, for the same structural reason.

## Apparent

Looks like a lean or half-finished state, understaffed ministries and a young bureaucracy still catching up, nothing sinister in an institution simply not yet grown.
