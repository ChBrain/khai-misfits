# Piece: The Rents

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The windfall itself: the resource sold abroad and the payment for it landing in the treasury, revenue the state need ask no one for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Treasury](place_the_treasury.md), carried in by [Flowing In](process_flowing_in.md), voiced by [The Well](persona_the_well.md): the coffers filling from the ground and the foreign sale, never from an assessment sent to a citizen.

## Load Bearing

The state receives rent rather than extracting revenue, and the difference is the whole of the mechanism: rent arrives already made, asked of no one, owed to no one's ledger, so the state that lives on it is loosed from the domestic economy and from the need to tax those it governs (Mahdavy). Nothing yet is spent or withheld; only that the money's origin, the ground and the foreign buyer, bypasses the citizen entirely, and every piece the misfit builds afterward stands on that one fact.

## Apparent

Pure good fortune: wealth welling up at no one's expense, a discovery under the ground doing what decades of taxation could not, and no citizen a cent the poorer for the treasury's new abundance.

## Yearbook

Carried by [Flowing In](process_flowing_in.md), staged at [The Treasury](place_the_treasury.md), voiced by [The Well](persona_the_well.md), dramatized in [The Money Arrives](plot_the_money_arrives.md). It severs into [The Severed Link](piece_the_severed_link.md), the bargain the rents make needless, and it is spent onward as [The Handout](piece_the_handout.md), the sensible use a state loosed from taxing finds for the money it never had to ask for.
