# Plot: The Checks Wither

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ministry](place_the_ministry.md), where the institutions were never forced into being.
Nothing built them, because nothing ever pressed for them.

## Action

[The Sovereign](persona_the_sovereign.md), from [the sovereign's seat](position_sovereign.md), meets [The Hollow Institutions](piece_the_hollow_institutions.md) at [The Ministry](place_the_ministry.md): the checks atrophied because no taxed public ever forced them into being, and repression affordable to a state that need not tax the very people it might have to repress.

## Stage

The rentier channel and the repression channel, running together (Ross). Neither is authored by a villain: the sovereign in this seat wants only what any sovereign wants, and would find the same hollowness waiting whatever his character.
This is not kleptocracy. The harm bites a government of ordinary good will as surely as it bites a greedy one, because it is the funding and not the character that severs the link; this is not the tale of a thief in the palace, it is the tale of a treasury that never had to answer to anyone.

## Tension

The wealth that should have freed a people leaves the power over them less answerable than poverty would have.
