# Plot: Buying Quiet

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Square](place_the_square.md), the rents already in hand.
There is quiet to keep, and it is cheaper to buy than to earn.

## Action

[The Sovereign](persona_the_sovereign.md), from [the sovereign's seat](position_sovereign.md), on [Spend the Rents](plan_spend_the_rents.md) through [Distributing](process_distributing.md), hands out [The Handout](piece_the_handout.md) at [The Square](place_the_square.md): subsidy and patronage that buy quiet in place of the slow institutions a taxing state would otherwise have had to build.

## Stage

The distributive state: buying quiet costs less than building the checks a taxed public would eventually have demanded (Karl).
This is not rent-seeking. There, real resources are burned in the scramble for a transfer that makes nothing. Here the wealth is not dissipated but misdirected: it is spent, and spent well, only on silence rather than on the institutions that would check it. Rent-seeking may ride along in the scramble for the handout, but it is a component here, not the load-bearing vector; the vector is where the money goes, not where it is lost.

## Tension

The wealth is not destroyed. It is spent on silence, in place of the checks that silence replaces.
