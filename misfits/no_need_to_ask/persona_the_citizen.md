# Persona: The Citizen

## Taxonomy

[Citizen](position_citizen.md), inside [No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The archetype of the untaxed and subsidised subject who presses no claim to hold power to account, because the lever ordinary publics hold, the withholding of revenue, is not theirs to pull.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A contented beneficiary, well looked after. It looks like someone with little to complain of, taxes light or none and the subsidy arriving, a person sensibly grateful for a state that gives and does not take.

## Action

Take, and ask nothing. At [the Square](place_the_square.md), on [Take the Handout](plan_take_the_handout.md) through [Standing Down](process_standing_down.md), the Citizen holds [the Unasked](piece_the_unasked.md), accepting the subsidy and pressing no hard claim to account, since it funds nothing the state depends on and so holds no lever to withhold (Beblawi and Luciani).

## Shadow

Its contentment is rational and it is the quiet half of the harm. The Citizen is not servile or cowed but is answering the incentives before it: with no tax paid, it holds no purse to close against the state, so demanding an account would cost it much and win it little, and it trades a voice it cannot easily raise for a benefit in hand. So the pressure that builds accountable institutions never comes from below, and the not-asking is no failing of courage but the sound reading of a bargain it was never offered.

## Tell

It pays no tax and holds no lever, so it asks for no account and is given none.
