# Piece: The Unasked

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The demand side of the severed bargain: the citizen, untaxed and subsidised, who presses no claim against the power over them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Square](place_the_square.md), carried by [Standing Down](process_standing_down.md), voiced by [The Citizen](persona_the_citizen.md), who takes the handout and asks nothing further.

## Load Bearing

The lever an ordinary public holds over its government is the granting or the withholding of the revenue the government needs, and a public that pays no tax holds no such lever to pull. So accountability is not demanded from below, not because the citizen has lost the taste for it but because the bargain that would ground the demand, revenue offered in exchange for representation, was never struck (Beblawi and Luciani). The quiescence is rational, not servile: a citizen weighing a hard claim against a government they do not fund, against a handout already in hand from a government they do, chooses reasonably, and no failure of courage need enter the account.

## Apparent

A contented, well-looked-after public, little to complain of and little complaining, the picture of a citizenry at ease with its government.
