# Persona: The Steward

## Taxonomy

[Steward](position_steward.md), inside [No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The archetype of the one who would hold the fiscal link open by design, taxing the people and building the checks the rents make needless, and who marks that the remedy is costly, rare, and against the grain.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A prudent guardian of the future. It looks like the far-sighted hand that sets up the sovereign fund and the public accounts, the sober counsel that will not let a windfall be simply spent.

## Action

Build the checks. At [the Ministry](place_the_ministry.md), on [Build the Checks](plan_build_the_checks.md) through [Building](process_building.md), the Steward holds [the Prior Strength](piece_the_prior_strength.md), keeping or building the accountable institutions before the windfall lands, taxing the citizens even when the rents make it needless, binding the rents in a transparent fund so the state answers by design (Mehlum, Moene and Torvik).

## Shadow

Its remedy asks the state to spurn the ease the rents offer, and leans on a strength most did not have. To build the checks is to lay on the state the very discipline the windfall was meant to spare it, to tax where taxing is needless and to bind a treasury that could simply be spent, and it works best where accountable institutions already stood before the boom, so where they did not the remedy is hardest exactly where it is most needed. It knows the hedge it stands on and claims no certainty, only a way of holding the link open against the pull of the rents.

## Tell

It taxes the people though the rents make it needless, so that the state must still answer to them.
