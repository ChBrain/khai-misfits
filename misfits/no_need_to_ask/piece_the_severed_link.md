# Piece: The Severed Link

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The broken fiscal bargain itself: the tie between taxation and accountability, cut not by decree but by the simple fact that the state need not tax to live.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Treasury](place_the_treasury.md), held by [The Sovereign](persona_the_sovereign.md), who need bargain with no one for the money already arrived.

## Load Bearing

Through most of history the power to tax and the power to hold power to account grew up together, no taxation without representation, because a ruler who must ask for money must answer for how it is used. Rent funding removes the asking, and with it the pressure to answer: a state that need not tax its people need not bargain with them, and the link that would have tied the treasury to the citizen is cut before it is ever tied (Beblawi and Luciani). The channel here is fiscal and political, running through the treasury and the citizen-state bargain, and it is worth holding apart from Dutch Disease, the same windfall's economic channel, where the money instead lifts the real exchange rate and prices the country's own factories and farms out of their markets. A state can suffer either channel without the other: the tradable economy can hollow while the treasury still answers to its people, or the treasury can go unanswerable in a country whose exchange rate never moves. Here it is the bargain that breaks, not the balance of trade.

## Apparent

Looks like mere fiscal good luck: taxes low or waived outright, a government sparing its people the collector's visit, nothing more sinister than a lighter bill.
