# Plot: Nothing to Withhold

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Square](place_the_square.md), the handout already passed among the crowd.
The citizen here is untaxed, and subsidised besides.

## Action

[The Citizen](persona_the_citizen.md), from [the citizen's seat](position_citizen.md), on [Take the Handout](plan_take_the_handout.md) through [Standing Down](process_standing_down.md), holds [The Unasked](piece_the_unasked.md) at [The Square](place_the_square.md): pressing no claim to an account, holding no lever of withheld revenue to pull.

## Stage

A public that pays no tax holds no revenue to withhold, and so cannot compel an account from the state that governs it. Its quiet is rational, not servile: a bargain of revenue for representation that was never offered it in the first place (Beblawi and Luciani).

## Tension

The public that funds nothing can demand nothing.
