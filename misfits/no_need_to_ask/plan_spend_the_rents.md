# Plan: Spend the Rents

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the sovereign's seat](position_sovereign.md), inside [No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Sovereign](position_sovereign.md)

## Direction

Distribute the windfall on subsidy and patronage that content the people, and on the means to keep order, rather than on the thankless institutions a taxing state is forced to build, since you need answer to no one who does not fund you. Know that the funding and not your character severs the link, that even meaning well you will find patronage cheaper than checks, and that the accountability you never build is the accountability no one below you has the lever to demand.

## Orders

([The Sovereign](persona_the_sovereign.md), holding [the Severed Link](piece_the_severed_link.md), hands out [the Handout](piece_the_handout.md) through [Distributing](process_distributing.md) at [the Square](place_the_square.md), subsidy and patronage in place of the slow institutions.)

## Implementation

([Distributing](process_distributing.md) at [the Square](place_the_square.md) spends the rents as [the Handout](piece_the_handout.md), because buying quiet is cheaper than building checks, so [the Hollow Institutions](piece_the_hollow_institutions.md) are left unbuilt at [the Ministry](place_the_ministry.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the windfall is distributed on subsidy and patronage that buy quiet
- [ ] the distributing is understood as locally sensible, quiet cheaper than institutions
- [ ] the checks a taxing state must build are seen to go unbuilt for want of the pressure
- [ ] the harm is owned as the funding's and not the ruler's character, biting a government of good will
