# Position: Sovereign

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The seat of the rent-funded state, loosed by its funding from the need to tax and so from the need to answer, spending the windfall on quiet rather than on the checks a taxing state must build.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A treasury filled from the ground, revenue it need ask no citizen for.
A cheap way to keep the peace: the handout, the subsidy, the public post, the patronage, that buys quiet without building anything slow.
No creditor among its people, since it does not depend on their revenue, so no public with a lever to compel it to account.

## Orders

Spend the rents, and keep the peace.
Distribute the windfall on subsidy and patronage that content the people, and on the means to keep order, rather than on the thankless institutions a taxing state is forced to build, since you need answer to no one who does not fund you.
Know that the funding and not your character severs the link, that even meaning well you will find patronage cheaper than checks, and that the accountability you never build is the accountability no one below you has the lever to demand.

## Loses

Nothing it feels, and it forfeits the checks that would have made its power answerable.
Because the rents free it from taxing, the seat is freed from the bargain that would have forced it to build the courts and the bureau and the legislature that hold power to account, and it lets them wither for want of the pressure, keeping its ease at the cost of a hollowed state.
It keeps its windfall and its good name and loses no cause for blame, the funding the author of the loss and not any villainy of its own.

## Drives

Toward the state paid by the ground and not the people,
spending the rents on quiet and not on checks, no ill will needed,
so the seat that need not ask its citizens for money need not answer to them, and the wealth that freed it looses it from account.
