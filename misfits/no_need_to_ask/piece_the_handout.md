# Piece: The Handout

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The rents spent outward: subsidy, public jobs, patronage, the state distributing what it need not have earned from its own people.

## Owner

## Place

## Load Bearing

A rentier state is a distributive state before it is anything else, allocating rather than extracting, and distributing is simply cheaper: buying quiet with a subsidy or a public post costs less, in effort and in time, than building the capable bureaucracy, the honest court, the legislature that holds the purse, the slow institutions a taxing state is forced to build because it must answer to the taxed (Karl). Worth holding apart from Rent-Seeking, where real resources are burned competing to capture a transfer that creates nothing: here nothing is dissipated in the scramble, the wealth is simply misdirected, spent on quiet rather than on checks. Rent-seeking can ride along as one channel through which a curse like this bites, but it is not the load-bearing vector; the vector is the severed tax-link that made spending on quiet the cheaper and more rational course in the first place.

## Apparent

A generous government sharing the nation's good fortune: subsidised fuel, a public salary for a citizen who wants one, a new road through a town that never asked for one, gratitude the obvious response.
