# Plan: Take the Handout

## Taxonomy

## Direction

Accept the subsidy and the light bill, and press no hard claim to hold the rulers to account, since you fund nothing they depend on and so hold no lever to withhold, and demanding an account would cost you much and win you little. Know that your quiet is rational and not servile, that the bargain other publics strike, revenue for representation, was never offered you, and that the pressure that would build the checks cannot come from a public that pays no tax.
