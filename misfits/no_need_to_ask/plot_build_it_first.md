# Plot: Build It First

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ministry](place_the_ministry.md), before the windfall lands, or early enough still to matter.
The discipline the rents would gladly spare a state is, here, still being kept.

## Action

[The Steward](persona_the_steward.md), from [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), on [Build the Checks](plan_build_the_checks.md) through [Building](process_building.md), holds [The Prior Strength](piece_the_prior_strength.md) at [The Ministry](place_the_ministry.md): keeping or building the accountable institutions before the windfall arrives, taxing the people even where the rents would make it needless, binding the money in a transparent fund rather than a treasury that answers to no one.

## Stage

The fiscal link is held open by design, not left to close on its own. The curse is conditional on the institutions the windfall meets: a blessing where they are producer-friendly, a curse where they are grabber-friendly, so a state already accountable before the money arrived can escape it, as Norway and Botswana did (Mehlum, Moene and Torvik).
Hold the bound: the curse is contested, not a determinism. The cross-national link itself has been challenged on long within-country data (Haber and Menaldo).

## Tension

The only escape is to spurn the ease the rents offer, and to do it before the windfall lands; it leans on a prior strength most states did not have.
