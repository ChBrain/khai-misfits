# Pitch: The Ground Pays, So the People Don't Ask

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that when the treasury is filled from the ground rather than from the people, the state need not ask them, and so need not answer them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Sober, reasonable, even fortunate. A government blessed with a great resource, spending the proceeds on subsidy and jobs and public works, a citizenry content and untaxed. Nothing on the face of it but good luck well used.

## Undertow

The money reaches the treasury without passing through the citizens' pockets, so the state need not tax them (Mahdavy). And a state that need not ask its people for money need not bargain with them or answer to them, the old tie between taxation and accountability quietly cut (Beblawi and Luciani). So it spends the rents on the handout that buys quiet, not on the checks a taxing state must build, and the checks wither for want of the pressure (Karl; Ross).

## Nerve

That the wealth which should have freed a people is the very thing that looses the power over them from having to answer to them. No greedy despot is needed: the funding severs the link whoever holds the throne, and even a ruler of good will finds patronage cheaper than institutions.

## Echo

When the ground pays the state's bills, the people are never asked, and so are never answered.
