# Plan: Build the Checks

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), inside [No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Steward](position_steward.md)

## Direction

Tax the people though the rents make it needless, bind the rents in a fund and publish the accounts, keep or raise the courts and the bureau and the legislature that hold power to account, so the state answers by design where it need not by necessity. Know that the remedy asks the state to lay on itself the discipline the rents were meant to spare it, that it leans on a prior strength most did not have, and that the pull to spend the rents on quiet returns with every boom, the hedge you stand on and the bound you must name.
