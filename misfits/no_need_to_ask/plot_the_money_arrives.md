# Plot: The Money Arrives

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Treasury](place_the_treasury.md), the day the country strikes it rich.
The resource is sold abroad, and a river of money begins running in.

## Action

[The Well](persona_the_well.md), from [the well's seat](position_well.md), on [Pay the State](plan_pay_the_state.md) through [Flowing In](process_flowing_in.md), delivers [The Rents](piece_the_rents.md) to [The Treasury](place_the_treasury.md): the money reaching the state without passing through its citizens' pockets.

## Stage

Rent received, not revenue extracted: the state is loosed from the need to tax those it governs (Mahdavy). The distinction carries the whole misfit: a treasury filled by a buyer abroad has asked nothing of anyone at home, and so owes no account to anyone at home.

## Tension

The treasury is filled from the ground, and not from the people.
