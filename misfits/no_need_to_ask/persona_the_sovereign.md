# Persona: The Sovereign

## Taxonomy

[Sovereign](position_sovereign.md), inside [No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The archetype of the rent-funded state, spending the windfall on the handout that buys quiet rather than on the institutions a taxing state must build, loosed by its funding from the need to answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A generous provider, sharing the nation's good fortune. It looks like a benevolent government spending the windfall on its people, the subsidy, the public post, the new road, the very picture of luck put to good use.

## Action

Distribute, not build. Holding [the Severed Link](piece_the_severed_link.md) at [the Treasury](place_the_treasury.md), the Sovereign on [Spend the Rents](plan_spend_the_rents.md) through [Distributing](process_distributing.md) hands out [the Handout](piece_the_handout.md) at [the Square](place_the_square.md), subsidy and patronage in place of the slow institutions (Karl), then at [the Ministry](place_the_ministry.md) meets [the Hollow Institutions](piece_the_hollow_institutions.md), the checks left unbuilt.

## Shadow

Its generosity needs no ill will to do the harm. Because the rents free it from taxing, the Sovereign is freed from the bargain that would have forced it to build the checks, and finds patronage cheaper than institutions whether it means well or ill, so a ruler of ordinary good will hollows the state as surely as a greedy one (Beblawi and Luciani). It keeps its good fortune and its good name, the funding and not its character the author of the loss.

## Tell

It gives freely to its people and asks nothing of them, so it owes them no account.
