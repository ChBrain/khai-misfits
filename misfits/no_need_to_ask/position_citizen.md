# Position: Citizen

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md). The seat of the untaxed and subsidised subject who presses no claim to hold power to account, holding no lever of withheld revenue to pull.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A subsidy in hand and a light tax bill or none, the windfall reaching it as benefit rather than levy.
No purse the state depends on, since it funds little or nothing of the treasury, so no revenue it could withhold to compel an account.
A high price on dissent and a low return to it, since demanding accountability wins little when the state does not need its money.

## Orders

Take the handout, and ask nothing.
Accept the subsidy and the light bill, and press no hard claim to hold the rulers to account, since you fund nothing they depend on and so hold no lever to withhold, and demanding an account would cost you much and win you little.
Know that your quiet is rational and not servile, that the bargain other publics strike, revenue for representation, was never offered you, and that the pressure that would build the checks cannot come from a public that pays no tax.

## Loses

Nothing it can feel now, and it forfeits the account it has no lever to demand.
Because it pays no tax and holds no purse to close, the seat cannot compel the state to answer, so it trades a voice it could not easily raise for the benefit in hand, and the accountability that its withheld revenue might have bought is never bought.
It keeps its subsidy and its quiet and loses no cause for blame, the subject answering the incentives of a bargain it was never offered.

## Drives

Toward the one who takes the subsidy and asks nothing,
untaxed and holding no lever, quiet by reason and not by fear,
so the seat that funds nothing the state depends on can demand nothing of it, and the pressure that builds the checks never rises from below.
