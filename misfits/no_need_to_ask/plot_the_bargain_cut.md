# Plot: The Bargain Cut

## Taxonomy

[No Need to Ask](play_no_need_to_ask.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Treasury](place_the_treasury.md), now full.
The state that stands before it need not ask anyone for the money it holds.

## Action

[The Sovereign](persona_the_sovereign.md), from [the sovereign's seat](position_sovereign.md), holds [The Severed Link](piece_the_severed_link.md) at [The Treasury](place_the_treasury.md): the tie between taxation and accountability cut, because a state that need not tax its people need not bargain with them, and need not answer to them either.

## Stage

Historically the power to tax and the power to hold power to account grew up together: no taxation without representation. Rent-funding removes the pressure that grew them (Beblawi and Luciani).
This is not Dutch Disease. That is the economic channel of the same windfall: the exchange rate rises and the tradable sectors hollow out. Here the channel is fiscal and political, running through the treasury and the bargain between citizen and state, and a country can suffer either without the other.

## Tension

A state that need not ask its people need not answer them.
