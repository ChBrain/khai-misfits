# Process: Deferring

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). The appeal court declining to re-decide a fact, and declining hardest on credibility.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An appeal that challenges a finding rather than a ruling.
At [the appeal](place_the_appeal.md), under a standard written for the purpose.

## Direction

Toward not substituting a worse-placed judgement for a better-placed one.
That is a real discipline and a necessary one: a court further from the evidence, re-deciding on paper, would be slower and no more accurate, and a system permitting it would never reach an end.
And the standard is not uniform. Where the finding rests on the credibility of a witness, it demands **greater** deference still.

## Lever

The account of what the appeal court did not see, which is accurate.
And which is calibrated to a channel measured at barely above a coin, so **the humility is well founded in principle and aimed away from the evidence.**

## Echo

The finding was open to the judge below.
True, and not the same as right.
