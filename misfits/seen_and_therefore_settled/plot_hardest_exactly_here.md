## Action

## Stage

[The Judge Who Was There](persona_the_judge_who_was_there.md)'s finding becomes, at this point, unappealable in practice.

## Tension

This is the detail that turns a general principle into a specific trap, and it is written into the rule rather than left to practice.
The deference is **not uniform**. It is weakest where a finding rests on documents that the appeal court can read for itself, and strongest where the finding rests on the credibility of a witness, because credibility is exactly what the appeal court could not observe.
That is the argument for the grading and it is a good one. It is also why the grading runs **inversely to the measurement**: the protection is firmest on the thinnest ground, and the refinement was made without the measurement in view.
And nothing here is anybody's error. The rule was written by people trying to allocate a decision to whoever was best placed to make it, using the same account of what watching yields that the judge, the appeal court and the losing party all hold.
