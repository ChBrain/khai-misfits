# Process: Watching

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). The trial judge observing a witness, and an impression forming.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A witness taking the oath.
At [the courtroom](place_the_courtroom.md), for as long as the evidence lasts.

## Direction

Toward a view about whether this person is telling the truth.
The impression is not a voluntary act. It forms in anybody who watches, it forms in the appeal judges too when they sit at first instance, and it forms in the losing party watching from the back of the court.
So this is not a lapse in professional standards: it is the ordinary human view of what watching somebody tells you, held by everybody in the building.

## Lever

Presence, which is real and exclusive and cannot be transmitted.
The question the play asks is not whether the judge watched properly but what watching properly yields.

## Echo

A finding, written in a sentence,
that the witness did not impress the court,
which describes something that genuinely happened in the room.
