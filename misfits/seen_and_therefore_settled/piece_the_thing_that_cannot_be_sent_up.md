# Piece: The Thing That Cannot Be Sent Up

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). Manner's non-transmissibility: writable only as a conclusion.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Between [the courtroom](place_the_courtroom.md) and [the bundle](place_the_bundle.md).
Enacted by [transmitting](process_transmitting.md), which carries everything else.

## Load Bearing

Without it the appeal court could examine the reading and the rule would need no special deference.
It is load bearing because it makes the finding **unauditable rather than merely unreviewed**: what leaves the room is the conclusion and not the material, so nobody downstream can check the reading against what was read, and there is nothing for a standard to be applied to.

## Apparent

A limit of paper, to be fixed by recording the hearing.
Recording supplies the visible channel, which is the one the measurement says is worse, so the fix hands the appeal court more of the evidence that does not work and leaves the grading exactly where it was.

## Yearbook

A property of what writing can hold, spent at [the bundle](place_the_bundle.md).
It is why the review that exists has nothing to grip.
