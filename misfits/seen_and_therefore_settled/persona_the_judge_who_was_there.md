# Persona: The Judge Who Was There

## Taxonomy

[Presence](position_presence.md). The trial judge: the only person in the system who watched the witness answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, experienced, and doing the job as it is meant to be done.
Heard the question put and the answer arrive, watched the pause before it, and formed a view.
Not guessing, and not pretending to more certainty than they feel.

## Action

Sits through it.
Forms an impression, which is not a voluntary act.
Writes the finding, and gives reasons.

## Shadow

That the faculty being exercised has been measured and comes in at about a coin.
Nothing here is careless: the impression forms in anybody who watches, the judge believes in it because everybody does, and the belief is not a lapse in professional standards but the ordinary human view of what watching somebody tells you.
It ends having decided the case on the part of the evidence that carries least.

## Tell

Says the witness did not impress them.
Means it, and is describing something real that happened in the room.
