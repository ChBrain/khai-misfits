# Position: Remedy

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). The seat of what is available to the party who lost on the facts.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A full appellate system, functioning, with review of fact genuinely available.
A standard designed to catch what is plainly wrong.
Nothing that reaches an impression formed by an experienced judge who heard the evidence.

## Orders

Receive the appeal.
Look for a ground.
Answer honestly.

## Loses

Nothing, and the absence is not a gap anybody left.
Findings of fact **are** reviewable, and a credibility finding by a judge who was present is by construction not plainly wrong, so this seat can be complete, functioning and correctly applied and still contain **review without a route.**
It ends giving an accurate answer, which is nothing.

## Drives

Toward correcting what was got wrong,
which it does, wherever the standard can reach,
so the seat that exists to supply the remedy is the seat with none to supply here.
