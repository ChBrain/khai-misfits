## Tension

Every settlement costs the thing that made the rule worth having.
Reviewing credibility afresh means re-deciding the trial on paper, which is what the deference exists to prevent and which never reaches an end. Recording the hearing gives the appeal court the video, which is **the channel the measurement says is worse**, so the fix supplies more of the evidence that does not work. Telling judges the research exists does not stop an impression forming, because forming it is not voluntary.
And the last one is the reason this stays a misfit rather than becoming a proposal. A rule that allocated the finding to whoever holds the transcript would be a rule that **the party who was in the room is the one who should not decide**, and that is a sentence no system has been willing to write.
Necessity explains why the deference exists. It says nothing about the deference being graded highest on the weakest channel, and that is the part with no defence.
