# Persona: The Transcript

## Taxonomy

[Transmission](position_transmission.md). What survives to the appeal: the words, the answers, and the order they came in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Complete in what it holds, and famously incomplete in what it does not.
Every question, every answer, every evasion that took the form of a sentence.
Universally described as the poor relation of having been there.

## Action

Records.
Travels.
Arrives intact, and is read by people who were not present.

## Shadow

That it may be the better document and is treated as the worse.
The measurement runs the other way from the reputation: people judge audible lies more accurately than visible ones, and a scholar of the rule put it plainly, that a transcript is probably a **better** basis for credibility than the live performance, because it strips the misleading nonverbal data and keeps the reliable verbal content.
It ends carrying the stronger evidence into a court that defers on the ground of lacking the weaker.

## Tell

Nobody ever says the appeal court has the better record.
Nobody has shown that it does not.
