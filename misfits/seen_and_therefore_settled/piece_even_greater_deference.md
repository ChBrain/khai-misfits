# Piece: Even Greater Deference

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). The standard's own emphasis: the deference rises where the finding rests on credibility.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the wording of the rule and in the cases construing it.
Applied at [the appeal](place_the_appeal.md), correctly, every time.

## Load Bearing

Without it the deference is flat and the misfit is a general observation about appellate procedure.
It is load bearing because the protection is **graded, and graded the wrong way**: it is weakest where a finding rests on documents and strongest where it rests on a witness being believed, so the rule reserves its firmest ground for its thinnest premise.

## Apparent

A sensible refinement, since credibility is exactly what the appeal court could not observe.
That is the argument, and it is why the grading exists. It is also why the grading tracks the measurement inversely, and the refinement was made without the measurement being in view.

## Yearbook

Written into the standard rather than left to practice, spent at [the appeal](place_the_appeal.md).
It is the detail that turns a general principle into a specific trap.
