# Process: Appealing

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). The losing party looking for a ground, and being answered accurately.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A judgment that turned on being believed, and went the other way.
At [the appeal](place_the_appeal.md), with competent advice.

## Direction

Toward whatever can be argued.
The law is checked and the law was applied correctly. The procedure is checked and the procedure was fair. The finding is read, and it rests on one sentence about impression.
There is no ground available that says the judge's reading of a face is worth about as much as a coin, because no system has written that ground and none would know how to phrase it.

## Lever

A search of the available grounds, done properly, returning nothing.
The absence is not a gap anybody left: it is what a functioning system looks like at this particular point.

## Echo

An accurate answer to what can be done,
which is nothing.
