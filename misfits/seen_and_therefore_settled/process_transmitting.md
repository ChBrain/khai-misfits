# Process: Transmitting

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). The record going up: what the appeal is allowed to have.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A notice of appeal.
At [the bundle](place_the_bundle.md), assembled by people being thorough.

## Direction

Toward a complete account of what was said.
Every question, every answer, every evasion that took the form of a sentence, in order and verbatim: nothing is withheld and nothing is summarised away.
What does not travel is the manner, because manner cannot be written down except as a conclusion, and the conclusion is what appears in the judgment.

## Lever

Writing, which preserves the audible channel entirely and the visible one not at all.
**Which is the better half by the measurement and the worse half by the reputation.**

## Echo

A bundle nobody calls sufficient,
holding the evidence that measures best.
