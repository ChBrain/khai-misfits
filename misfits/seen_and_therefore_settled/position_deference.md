# Position: Deference

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). The seat of the rule: correct the law, decline the facts, and decline hardest on credibility.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Full power over questions of law, exercised without hesitation.
An accurate account of what it did not see.
A standard that raises its own threshold where a finding rests on a witness being believed.

## Orders

Review.
Correct the law.
Do not substitute your own view of who was telling the truth.

## Loses

Nothing to timidity, which is what makes this seat difficult rather than culpable.
The restraint is real and is well founded in principle, and it is calibrated to what could not be seen, so **it points away from the evidence**: the channel it lacks measures worse and the channel it holds measures better.
It ends most restrained exactly where the standard directs, which is where the ground is thinnest.

## Drives

Toward not re-deciding what it is worse placed to decide,
which is a genuine and necessary discipline,
so the seat exercising the right humility aims it at the wrong quantity.
