## Tension

This plot's work is to establish that the deference is **right**, because a production that opens by treating it as a defect has nothing to say later.
A trial cannot be re-run on appeal. Somebody had to hear the answers arrive and form a view, and no appellate bench can do that from a bundle. A system that let every finding of fact be re-decided by a court further from the evidence would be slower, no more accurate, and would leave litigation without an end.
And the impression is not a voluntary act. It forms in anybody who watches: in the trial judge, in the appeal judges when they sit at first instance, and in the losing party at the back of the court. **This is the ordinary human view of what watching somebody tells you**, held by everybody in the building, and not a lapse in professional standards by anyone.
