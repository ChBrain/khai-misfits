# Pitch: The Judge Saw the Witness

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact and unhurried, explaining a standard of review to somebody who has read the rule and not yet believed it.
Quite willing to say that a good rule rests on a bad premise.
Not indignant, and quoting the rule wherever the rule is the point.

## Undertow

Under the standard, two facts that do not negotiate.
The trial judge's presence is real, unrepeatable and impossible to write down.
What people get from watching a witness has been measured, and they do better on what they hear.

## Nerve

The unease of a sentence everybody accepts.
The judge below saw the witness and we have only the paper: true, said in good faith, and pointing at the half of the evidence that carries less,
while the paper holds the half that carries more.

## Echo

After the run: a trial that could not have been held anywhere else,
an appeal court correcting the law and declining the facts, exactly as it should,
a rule that protects hardest where its ground is thinnest,
and a party told accurately that there is nothing to be done.
