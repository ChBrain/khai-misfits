# Persona: The Reviewing Court

## Taxonomy

[Deference](position_deference.md). The appeal bench: entirely willing to correct the law, and correctly conscious of what it did not see.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not timid and not deferential by temperament.
Reverses on law without hesitation, reads the papers closely, and knows exactly what a trial is for.
Aware, accurately, that it was not in the room.

## Action

Reviews.
Corrects the law.
Declines to substitute its own view of who was telling the truth.

## Shadow

That its modesty is calibrated to the wrong quantity.
It defers in proportion to what it could not see, and what it could not see is the channel that measures worse, while the channel it does have measures better, so **the humility is real, is well founded in principle, and points away from the evidence.**
It ends most restrained exactly where the standard tells it to be, which is where the ground is thinnest.

## Tell

Will say the finding was open to the judge below.
Which is true, and is not the same as saying it was right.
