# Process: Reviewing

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). What review actually does, which is a great deal, and not this.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Any appeal at all.
At [the appeal](place_the_appeal.md), by a bench with full power over the law.

## Direction

Toward correcting what was got wrong.
Errors of law are corrected without hesitation. Findings of fact are genuinely reviewable too, on a standard built to catch what is plainly wrong, and appeals on fact do succeed.
**So the trap is not that review is absent.** It is that a credibility finding by an experienced judge who heard the evidence is, by construction, not plainly wrong.

## Lever

A threshold set where a finding must be demonstrably mistaken rather than merely doubted.
Which is the correct threshold, and which no impression-based finding can cross, because an impression leaves nothing to demonstrate against.

## Echo

Review, functioning, complete,
and no route through it for this.
