## Load Bearing

Without it the appeal court simply has less and defers for a thin but real reason.
It is load bearing because it makes the deficit **the wrong way round**: what travels is the channel that measures better and what stays behind is the channel that measures worse, so the appeal court defers on account of lacking the weaker evidence while holding the stronger.

## Apparent

A technicality inside a psychology paper.
It is the whole inversion. A scholar of the rule drew the conclusion directly, that a transcript is probably a better basis for credibility than the live performance, because it strips the misleading nonverbal data and keeps the reliable verbal content.
