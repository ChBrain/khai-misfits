# Piece: The Trial That Cannot Be Re-Run

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). Why the deference is right whatever else is true: nobody can hold the hearing again.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Under the whole of the rule, at [the appeal](place_the_appeal.md).
The reason the play refuses to ask for the deference to be abolished.

## Load Bearing

Without it the misfit resolves into a reform and stops being a misfit.
It is load bearing because it makes the rule **necessary and not merely traditional**: a court further from the evidence re-deciding every finding would be slower, no more accurate, and would leave litigation without an end.

## Apparent

The answer to the whole play, since if the rule is necessary the difficulty is priced in.
It is not priced in. Necessity explains why deference exists and says nothing about it being **graded highest** on the weakest channel, which is the part with no defence and the part the play stages.

## Yearbook

True of every appellate system there has ever been, spent as the reason no settlement is free.
It is what keeps this from being a proposal.
