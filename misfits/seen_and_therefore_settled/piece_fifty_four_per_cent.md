# Piece: Fifty-Four Per Cent

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md). The measured accuracy of judging truth from manner, across two hundred and six studies.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the deception-detection literature, synthesised across twenty-four thousand judges.
Carried, unknowingly, by everybody at [the courtroom](place_the_courtroom.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the deference is owed to a real advantage and the play is a complaint about appeals being hard.
It is load bearing because it makes the exclusive advantage **nearly empty**: the thing the appeal court cannot have is a channel that classifies truth and lies at barely above a coin, so the rule protects access to something that does not carry what it is credited with.

## Apparent

A finding about untrained observers, which judges are not.
The synthesis covers many kinds of judge and does not find the profession that escapes it, and the play claims only what the measurement claims: that this is what people get from watching, not that any particular judge is worse than anybody else.

## Yearbook

Measured in psychology, spent in law, and connecting the two is the play's extension rather than the authors'.
It is the number the rule was written without.
