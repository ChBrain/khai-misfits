# Persona: The Demeanour

## Taxonomy

[Channel](position_channel.md). The visible manner of a witness: vivid, compelling, and nearly empty.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The most persuasive thing in the room.
A hesitation, a look away, a shift in the chair, a voice that goes quiet at the wrong moment.
Everybody in the room reads it, and everybody in the room believes they can.

## Action

Presents itself.
Is read, confidently, by whoever is watching.
Cannot be written down.

## Shadow

That it has been measured and it does not carry what it appears to.
Across two hundred and six studies and twenty-four thousand judges, people classify truth and lies at fifty-four per cent, and **they do better on the audible than on the visible**, so this channel is not merely thin but the thinner of the two available.
It ends the sole ground of a rule, on the strength of being the thing nobody else can have.

## Tell

Impossible to describe in a judgment except as a conclusion.
Which is why the conclusion is what goes on the page.
