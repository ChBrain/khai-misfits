# Persona: The Litigant

## Taxonomy

[Remedy](position_remedy.md). The party who lost on the facts, reading a judgment they cannot appeal.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Somebody with a case that turned on being believed.
Not a vexatious appellant and not a conspiracy theorist: a person whose account was preferred against, in one sentence, on grounds of impression.
Advised, correctly, that there is nothing here.

## Action

Reads the judgment.
Looks for a ground.
Finds law reviewable and fact not.

## Shadow

That the review which exists is not addressed to this and never was.
Findings of fact are reviewable, on a standard designed to catch the plainly wrong, and a credibility finding formed by an experienced judge who heard the evidence is by construction not plainly wrong: **so there is review, and no route.**
It ends without a ground of appeal that says the judge's impression of a face is worth about a coin.

## Tell

Asks what can be done.
Gets an accurate answer, which is nothing.
