# Plot: Nothing to Appeal On

## Taxonomy

[Seen, and Therefore Settled](play_seen_and_therefore_settled.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Litigant](persona_the_litigant.md), reading a judgment that turned on being believed.

## Action

[Appealing](process_appealing.md): [the remedy seat](position_remedy.md) searches the grounds at [the appeal](place_the_appeal.md) on [Offer What There Is](plan_offer_what_there_is.md).

## Stage

[Reviewing](process_reviewing.md) runs completely and correctly, and does not reach it.

## Tension

Here the harm lands, and it lands inside a system that is working.
**The trap is not that review is absent.** Errors of law are corrected without hesitation, procedure is checked, findings of fact are genuinely reviewable on a standard built to catch what is plainly wrong, and appeals on fact do succeed.
It is that a credibility finding by an experienced judge who heard the evidence is, by construction, **not plainly wrong**. The threshold is the correct one and no impression-based finding can cross it, because an impression leaves nothing to demonstrate against: what left the courtroom was the conclusion and not the material, so nobody downstream can check the reading against what was read.
And there is no ground available that says the judge's reading of a face is worth about as much as a coin, because no system has written that ground and none would know how to phrase it. The party asks what can be done and is told, accurately, that there is nothing.
