# Position: Verifier

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). The seat that can confirm an artefact and not an absence, so the controls that leave paper are the controls that count.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Independence, method and access to the records.
A test that works cleanly on anything producing a document, a signature or a register.
No test at all for a hazard that was never introduced, because absences generate no evidence.

## Orders

Check that what was recorded as done was done.
Confirm the issue sheets, the dated procedure, the attended briefing.
Record the corrective action as complete, which it is.

## Loses

Nothing, and it quietly reprices the ladder: verification is easy in inverse proportion to effectiveness, so the strong controls are the hard ones to be credited for and the weak ones close cleanly.
Because the system rewards demonstrated action, it rewards the bottom of the hierarchy, without any party intending that and without any entry in the log being false.
It ends holding an accurate record of correct work that says nothing about whether the hazard is still there.

## Drives

Toward evidence that an action was completed,
right in every single instance to require it,
so the seat that keeps everybody honest is the seat that makes the countable control the attractive one.
