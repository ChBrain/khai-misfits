## Stage

## Tension

The record is honest and the floor has not changed, and there is no field in which that difference could be entered.
This is the last turn and it is the one that makes the arrangement stable: the lower rungs generate documents and the upper ones generate absences, so verification is easy in inverse proportion to effectiveness, and a system that quite properly rewards demonstrated action ends up rewarding the weak end of the ladder with nobody intending it. Across a field record the distribution comes out as [the tally](piece_the_tally.md), from coordinators chosen for being successful, who were stopped seventy-one times: which is why this is a fact about the shape of the ladder and not about the people on it. Mandate the top rung and you have mandated a capital programme against an incident deadline. Train harder and you have chosen the second-weakest rung and called it rigour. Blame the coordinator and you have blamed the one who kept climbing.
