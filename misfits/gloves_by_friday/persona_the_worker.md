# Persona: The Worker

## Taxonomy

[Bearer](position_bearer.md). The person the bottom two rungs land on, whose continuous correct effort is the entire mechanism by which they work.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, experienced, and now carrying a new requirement.
Follows the revised method statement, wears what is issued, attends the briefing.
No resistance in it: the measures are reasonable and they are followed.

## Action

Absorbs a procedure and a piece of equipment into the way the job is done.
Performs both correctly, on the shift after the incident and on every shift after that, because that is what makes them protective at all.
Continues doing so while the process runs, which is indefinitely.

## Shadow

Not careless, and load bearing in a way the log never records: a rule and a respirator do nothing on their own, so the protection is not the control, it is the worker performing the control, continuously and without lapse.
So the measure that was cheap to install is expensive to sustain, and the whole of that expense is paid in attention by one person who was not in the room when the rung was chosen.
Any lapse over years reads afterwards as their error, and the design that made attention the safeguard does not appear in the finding.

## Tell

Says: understood, and signs the sheet.
Wears the equipment, follows the method, and is the reason it works, which is a fact about the control and not a compliment to them.
