# Persona: The Auditor

## Taxonomy

[Verifier](position_verifier.md). Can verify a control that leaves a record and cannot point at an absence, so the countable controls are the ones that earn credit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Independent, thorough, and interested in evidence.
Checks that what was said to be done was done: issue sheets, signatures, training registers, dated procedures.
No laxity in it: this is verification working exactly as designed.

## Action

Confirms that the respirators were issued, that the method statement was revised and dated, that the briefing was delivered and attended.
Records the corrective action as complete, which it is.
Cannot, by the same method, confirm the presence of a hazard that was never introduced, because there is no document for a thing that does not exist.

## Shadow

Not superficial, and asymmetric by construction: the bottom rungs generate artefacts and the top rungs generate absences, so verification is easy in inverse proportion to effectiveness.
So the control that is hardest to demonstrate is the one most worth having, and a system that rewards demonstrated action rewards the weak end of the ladder without anybody intending it.
The log ends up an accurate record of correct work and a poor record of whether anybody is safer.

## Tell

Says: implemented, verified, closed.
Every word of which is true, and none of which is about the hazard.
