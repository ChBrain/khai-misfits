# Place: The Action Log

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). Where corrective actions are raised, recorded and closed, and where only the controls that leave artefacts can appear.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A register of items: date raised, hazard, control selected, control implemented, evidence, date closed.
Every line in it true, approved and verifiable.

## Holds

The organisation's entire visible account of what it did about safety, and therefore the surface everything is judged on.
It also holds the asymmetry: a signature on an issue sheet enters cleanly, and a hazard that was designed out enters as nothing at all.

## Offers

Accountability, genuinely, and evidence that correct work was done by competent people inside the time available.
Nothing in it is false and nothing in it is padding.

## Withheld

Any account of whether the hazard is still there.
Because the log records actions rather than states, a full page of completed corrective actions is consistent with a floor that has not changed, and the log has no field in which that difference could be entered.
