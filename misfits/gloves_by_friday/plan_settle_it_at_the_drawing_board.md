# Plan: Settle It at the Drawing Board

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the origin's seat](position_origin.md), inside [Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Origin](position_origin.md)

## Direction

Specify the process to the standards and knowledge of the day, deliver it on the brief, and finish. Do not carry any duty toward the hazards the specification fixes, because there is no mechanism by which a closed design answers a later incident.

## Orders

([The Designer](persona_the_designer.md) fixes which hazards will exist, at the only moment when removing them costs a drawing revision, then signs off and goes, appearing in no corrective action afterwards.)

## Implementation

([The Drawing Board](place_the_drawing_board.md), closed years ago and elsewhere; the top two rungs of [the ladder](piece_the_ladder.md) held there and nowhere else; the ceiling this puts over [the reach](piece_the_reach.md) of everybody downstream.)

## Targets

- [ ] the process is specified competently, to the standards of its day
- [ ] the hazards that will exist for the life of the plant are fixed by that specification
- [ ] elimination and substitution are affordable at exactly this moment and at no later one
- [ ] the seat closes, and is absent from every corrective action it determines
