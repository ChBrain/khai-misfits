# Pitch: The Rung Within Reach

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Level, procedural, faintly clerical.
The register of a corrective action log: date raised, hazard, control selected, control implemented, date closed.
No alarm in the telling, because every entry in it is correct and was made by somebody competent inside the time they had.

## Undertow

Under the log, a second ranking nobody wrote down.
The five rungs are ordered by how well they work, and they are almost exactly reverse-ordered by how quickly they can be installed and how far up the organisation the decision sits, so the list that tells you what to do is the list that tells you what you cannot have.
The strong control and the slow control are not two things. They are one property, read from two ends.

## Nerve

The unease of watching a correct procedure, applied correctly by a competent person, arrive reliably at the weakest available answer,
with a written record at the end of it showing an action raised, actioned and closed,
and the hazard still exactly where it was on Tuesday.

## Echo

After the run: a log full of completed corrective actions, none of them wrong,
a coordinator who was stopped seventy-one times and kept going,
and a person on the floor who now has to be careful in a particular way, on every shift, for as long as the process runs.
