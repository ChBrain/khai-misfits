# Gloves by Friday: Reference

The research warrant for Gloves by Friday.
The term is the house's; the play runs on occupational safety practice and on which control gets chosen when a correct
ranking meets a deadline, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging hazard control as **a correct ranking applied by people ranked the other way**: the profession orders controls
by effectiveness, elimination first, then substitution, then engineering controls, then administrative controls, then
equipment on the person. That ordering is not in dispute and rests on a real distinction rather than a convention,
which is that the upper rungs work without needing continuous human action and the lower ones work only for as long as
somebody keeps performing them. What the ladder does not carry is a second ordering that runs very nearly the reverse
of it: a control that works without human effort has to be built into the thing, so it needs the design office, the
capital cycle and a shutdown, while a control that needs human effort can be introduced on any afternoon by anybody
with a budget code, because all it requires is the person, who is already there. The play models the documented
mechanics, the ladder that is right, the lead time that runs against it, the reach that stops at the fourth rung, the
attempts that are blocked and leave no record, the distribution that results, and the attention left on one person
forever, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a lax safety function.

The governing law is that a control's strength and its lead time are one property read from two ends, so the ranking
that says which control is best is close to the reverse of the ranking that says which control is available.

**Nobody in the sequence falls short, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The coordinator
applies the hierarchy in order and starts at the top. The designer specified competently to the standards of the day
and finished. The board asks what has been done and asks it this week. The worker follows the method and wears what is
issued. The auditor verifies what was recorded and records it complete.

What removes any possibility of blame from the coordinator is that **the field record is of the successful ones.** In
107 days of observation with 12 coordinators selected as successful, 280 measures were implemented and the coordinators
were **prevented 71 times**. Most of what went in was administrative, at 53.6 per cent, with engineering controls at
35 per cent, leaving the top of the ladder as a remainder. That distribution is not what a lax safety function looks
like: it is what the shape of the ladder looks like when somebody good climbs it against a deadline they did not set.

What removes it from the board is that the demand is legitimate and the deadline is the correct part of it. An
organisation that asks what has been done about an injury slowly is a worse organisation. **The urgency that makes it
responsive is the same urgency that sets a window no capital programme can enter**, and there is no version of the
question that both arrives promptly and reaches the drawing board.

What removes it from the designer is that their window was the only cheap one and it closed on schedule. Elimination
and substitution cost a drawing revision before anything is built and a capital programme afterwards, so the rung the
hierarchy ranks first belongs to a party who signed off years earlier, possibly at a company that no longer exists,
and who has no standing in the corrective action at all.

**And the verification is asymmetric, which is what makes the arrangement stable.** A respirator issued produces an
issue sheet, a signature and a date. A hazard that was designed out produces nothing, because absences generate no
evidence. So a system that quite properly requires proof that an action was completed is easiest to satisfy at exactly
the end of the ladder that works least, and the log fills with real, correct, verified corrective actions while the
floor is unchanged. No entry is false and there is no field in which the difference could be recorded.

**Then the cost does not disappear, it changes payer.** The lower rungs are inexpensive to establish and expensive to
sustain, because they rely on conscious decisions and consistent behaviour, so what is avoided as a capital cost paid
once is converted into an attention cost paid every shift, indefinitely, by somebody who was not in the room when the
rung was selected. A lapse across years is then recorded as that person's error, and the choice that made attention the
safeguard does not appear in the finding.

**The strict ordering is itself contested, and the play does not dispute that either.** The professional body's own
current statement cautions that the hierarchy is a general consensus that may not hold in every circumstance, and notes
that the five levels are sometimes called principles of control precisely to avoid the determinative sense of the word
hierarchy. The play stages the ordering as the profession's own and adjudicates none of that.

It is distinct from the house's **The Unmarked Edge**, which is the nearest incumbent and stages drift into failure,
where cost and effort gradients migrate the operating point toward the boundary without anybody deciding to move it.
Remove that engine, by stipulating an operating point held exactly where it was designed and no gradient acting on it
at all, and this misfit stands entire: **nothing here drifts.** The control is selected explicitly, on the first day,
in writing, by a competent person applying the correct method, and recorded. The weakness is in which rung was
available, not in a position that moved while nobody watched.

It is distinct from the house's **The New Normal**, which stages the normalization of deviance, where an unchanged act
is progressively reclassified as acceptable. Nothing is reclassified here and nothing changes over time: the procedure
and the equipment are the approved controls on the first day and on the last, hazard-assessed, documented and correct
from the outset, and the misfit is fully present in the first week.

It is distinct from the house's **Regrettable Substitution**, which stages the evidence asymmetry, where an alternative
with less data reads as safer and a hazard is swapped for an uncharacterised one. Remove that engine, by stipulating
complete toxicological data on every candidate substitute, and this stands: **the difficulty is not which substance but
which tier.** Even with perfect information about a milder alternative, substitution still requires the process
changed, the plant requalified and a party at the drawing board who is not reachable, so the coordinator still issues
the equipment on Friday.

It is distinct from the house's **Stopping Is the Hard Part**, which stages transient operating mode risk, where
protection specified for steady state is least present during the traversal between states. Remove that engine, by
stipulating a plant that never starts up or shuts down and simply runs, and this stands: the inversion between
effectiveness and availability holds in steady state exactly as it does in a transition, because it is a property of
what controls are rather than of what the plant is doing.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the safety function as
  lax or the employer as indifferent, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the coordinator
  applies the hierarchy in order and names the top rung, and the field record is of coordinators selected as
  successful, who implemented 280 measures and were prevented 71 times; the board asks what has been done and asks it
  promptly, which is the correct thing for a board to do and is the same act that sets the window; the designer
  specified the process competently to the standards of the day at the only moment when removing a hazard cost a
  drawing revision, and has no standing in a corrective action years later; the worker follows the revised method and
  wears what is issued, and is thereby the mechanism by which either measure protects anybody; and the auditor
  verifies the evidence an action produced, which is what keeps the record honest and is also why the controls that
  leave artefacts are the ones that close cleanly. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure,
  so the misfit is that the ranking by effectiveness is close to the reverse of the ranking by availability.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Mandating the top of the ladder
  mandates a capital programme against a deadline set by an incident, and the capital cycle cannot be convened by
  Friday. Training harder selects the second-weakest rung and calls it rigour, which is the move the hierarchy exists
  to discourage. Designing hazards out at the drawing board is exactly right, is what the profession has recommended
  since about 1950, and is unavailable to everybody downstream of a board that has closed. Auditing for outcomes
  rather than actions is correct and runs into the asymmetry that made the arrangement stable, since an absence
  produces no evidence to audit. Giving the coordinator authority over capital makes them a different role in a
  different organisation. And knowing the whole structure, which the field does and has published, leaves an incident
  on Tuesday, an answer due on Friday, and two rungs that fit inside a week.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed as the
  house's. The staging is the house's; the hierarchy, the field observation study, the guidance on implementation
  difficulty and sustainment cost, and the professional body's own cautions are theirs. The bounds are carried with
  them: **whether the ordering is strict is genuinely contested**, the profession's own white paper describes it as a
  general consensus that may not hold in every circumstance and notes the levels are sometimes called principles
  rather than a hierarchy, and the play carries that argument rather than settling it; the distribution figures come
  from one observational study of 12 coordinators in a particular national and sectoral context and are offered as the
  documented shape of what gets installed rather than as a constant; the account treats hazard control across
  industries as one structure, which is its own simplification; and the play does not claim the distribution has
  failed to improve, only that it is what has been observed. **The play makes no claim about any employer, any safety
  professional, any regulator or any incident, and gives no occupational health, engineering or legal advice of any
  kind**, only that the ladder is right, and that it is close to upside down against the calendar and the org chart.
