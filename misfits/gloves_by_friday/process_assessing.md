# Process: Assessing

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). The hazard identified and characterised after the incident, correctly and quickly, which is the part of the work that goes right.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An incident, and a duty to respond.
At [the floor](place_the_floor.md), the hazard examined, the exposure understood, the mechanism of harm written down.

## Direction

Toward an accurate statement of what the hazard is.
This step is done well and is not the difficulty: the coordinator is competent, the hazard is real and identifiable, and nothing about the assessment is rushed into error by the deadline.
It is worth staging precisely because it succeeds, since the failure downstream cannot then be attributed to not having understood the problem.

## Lever

Competence and access, both of which are present.
Because the assessment is not the constraint, improving it changes nothing about what happens next, which is the first sign that the difficulty is not epistemic.

## Echo

A correct problem statement, handed to a ranking that will also be correct.
Everything up to this point looks like a safety function working exactly as designed, and it is one.
