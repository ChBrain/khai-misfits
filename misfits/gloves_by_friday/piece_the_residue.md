# Piece: The Residue

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). The continuous attention the installed rungs require, paid by one person, forever, and recorded nowhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the floor](place_the_floor.md), on every shift, for as long as the process runs.
Answered nowhere: [the action log](place_the_action_log.md) has a field for a completed action and none for a permanently running one.

## Load Bearing

Without it the lower rungs really would be cheap and the ladder's ordering would be a matter of degree.
It is load bearing because it is what the ordering is actually about: the upper rungs work without anybody doing anything and the lower ones are a standing requirement on a person, so choosing the bottom of the ladder converts a capital cost that would have been paid once into an attention cost paid indefinitely by somebody who was not consulted.

## Apparent

Just the job, ordinary care, what a competent person does anyway.
Because it is absorbed into how the work is done, it never appears as a cost of the decision that created it, and its eventual failure appears as one person's lapse rather than as the rung that made attention the safeguard.

## Yearbook

Created by [issuing](process_issuing.md) and carried by [sustaining](process_sustaining.md), which never completes.
It is borne entirely by [the worker](persona_the_worker.md), is invisible to [the auditor](persona_the_auditor.md), and is the difference between the control [the ladder](piece_the_ladder.md) ranked first and the one that went in.
