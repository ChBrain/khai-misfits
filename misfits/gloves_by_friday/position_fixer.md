# Position: Fixer

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). The seat that applies the ranking correctly and can only buy from the bottom of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Command of the hierarchy, and the discipline to start at the top of it every time.
A budget code, a purchase order and the authority to revise a procedure.
No authority whatever over process design, installed plant or capital, and a deadline set by somebody else.

## Orders

Assess the hazard.
Rank the controls in the order the profession specifies, starting at elimination.
Implement what can be implemented inside the window, and record it.

## Loses

The top of its own ranking, every time, and roughly half its measures to the administrative rung.
Because its reach stops where the strong controls begin, the correctness of its method cannot improve its result: applying the ladder better delivers the same purchase order.
It ends the week having done the job properly and having installed the control the ladder ranks fourth.

## Drives

Toward a control in place before the deadline,
right in every single instance to start at the top and right to finish where it can,
so the seat that knows the ranking best is the seat that demonstrates most reliably that knowing it is not enough.
