# Piece: The Lead Time

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). What each rung takes to install, which runs very nearly inverse to the ladder's own ordering.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Measured at [costing](process_costing.md), against a window set by [the board](persona_the_board.md).
Answered nowhere: no party can shorten it, because it is a property of what makes a control strong.

## Load Bearing

Without it the ladder can simply be applied top down and the misfit does not exist.
It is load bearing because it is the governing law itself made concrete: strength and slowness are one property read from two ends, since a control that works without human effort must be built into the thing, and building into the thing means the drawing board, capital and a shutdown.

## Apparent

Ordinary project reality, the plain fact that big changes take longer than small ones.
Nothing about it looks like a mechanism of harm, and it never appears in the ladder's own presentation, which is drawn as a pyramid with no time axis on it at all.
