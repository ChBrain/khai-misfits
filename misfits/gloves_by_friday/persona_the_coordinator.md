# Persona: The Coordinator

## Taxonomy

[Fixer](position_fixer.md). Applies the hierarchy properly, reaches the rung their authority reaches, and is stopped above it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, unhurried under pressure, and genuinely good at the job.
Knows the ladder, applies it in order, and starts at the top every time because that is what the ladder is for.
Nothing cynical in it: this is the successful version of the role, not a caricature of it.

## Action

Assesses the hazard, ranks the controls in the order the profession specifies, and names the correct one, which is at or near the top.
Costs it, and finds the top rungs need a person, a budget and a shutdown that are not available inside the window.
Puts in what can go in: a revised procedure, a briefing, equipment on the person, all of it hazard-assessed and approved.

## Shadow

Not lax, and structurally short: their authority reaches the bottom two rungs cleanly, the third with a fight, and the top two not at all.
So the ranking they apply correctly delivers an answer they cannot buy, and the answer they can buy is the one the same ranking puts last.
In the field record the coordinators were the successful ones and were prevented seventy-one times, which is what being good at this looks like from outside.

## Tell

Says: the right control is elimination, and I have written that down.
Then issues the gloves, on Friday, because Friday is when the answer is due and gloves are what Friday can hold.
