# Plot: Something Happened on Tuesday

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Floor](place_the_floor.md), and an exposure: a near miss, a hand in the wrong place, a reading nobody wants to see twice.
Nothing has been decided yet, and a clock has started that was set by the incident rather than by anything about the plant.

## Action

[Assessing](process_assessing.md): [The Coordinator](persona_the_coordinator.md), holding [the fixer's seat](position_fixer.md), examines the hazard and characterises it properly, while [The Board](persona_the_board.md), holding [the principal's seat](position_principal.md), asks what will have been done by the end of the week.

## Stage

[The Board](persona_the_board.md), on [Be Seen to Have Answered](plan_be_seen_to_have_answered.md), asks the question an organisation ought to ask and asks it promptly, which is the correct thing to do and is not a pressure anybody should want removed.
[The Coordinator](persona_the_coordinator.md), on [Put Something in Place This Week](plan_put_something_in_place_this_week.md), begins at the top of the method, because that is what the method is for.

## Tension

Everything here is right, and one thing has already been decided.
There is no fault to find: the hazard is real, the assessment is competent, the question is legitimate, and asking it slowly would be worse. But the deadline inside the question was set by when somebody was nearly hurt, and the rungs of [the ladder](piece_the_ladder.md) are separated by lead times of months and years. The window has been fixed before anybody has looked at a single control, and it will decide the outcome more completely than the assessment will.
