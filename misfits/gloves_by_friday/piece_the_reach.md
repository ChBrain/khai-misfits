# Piece: The Reach

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). How far up the ladder the person at the scene can act, which is the fourth rung cleanly, the third with a fight, and no higher.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the floor](place_the_floor.md), bounded by a purchase order and the authority to revise a procedure.
Bounded above by [the drawing board](place_the_drawing_board.md), which is closed.

## Load Bearing

Without it the coordinator's competence would translate into the control the ranking names, and the play would be a story about doing the assessment properly.
It is load bearing because it is what makes competence insufficient: the assessment is right, the ranking is right, and the result is decided by where the pen's authority stops rather than by anything either got wrong.

## Apparent

An organisational detail, a matter of who signs what.
It reads as administration rather than as the deciding variable, which is why the shortfall is nearly always written up afterwards as a safety culture problem rather than as a question of which rung anybody could actually buy.

## Yearbook

Measured at [costing](process_costing.md) against [the lead time](piece_the_lead_time.md).
Where it is contested rather than simply short it appears as [the block](piece_the_block.md), and where it holds it produces the distribution recorded in [the tally](piece_the_tally.md).
