# Plot: Nobody in the Building Can Do That

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The named control, taken to be costed.
[The Drawing Board](place_the_drawing_board.md), where elimination and substitution actually live, closed nine years ago and possibly in another country.

## Action

[Costing](process_costing.md): each rung priced against the window, so that elimination is found to need the process redesigned, a guard is found to need installed plant altered and a line stopped, and [The Designer](persona_the_designer.md), holding [the origin's seat](position_origin.md), is found to be unreachable.

## Stage

[The Designer](persona_the_designer.md), having long since completed [Settle It at the Drawing Board](plan_settle_it_at_the_drawing_board.md), fixed which hazards would exist at the only moment when removing them cost a drawing revision, and is absent from this and every subsequent corrective action.
[The Coordinator](persona_the_coordinator.md) measures [the reach](piece_the_reach.md) against [the lead time](piece_the_lead_time.md) of each rung and finds their authority ends at the fourth cleanly, the third with a fight, and no higher.

## Tension

The ordering turns over, and it turns over for the same reason it exists.
What makes the upper rungs strong is that they work without human effort, and a control that works without human effort has to be built into the thing: so strength and lead time are not two properties that happen to correlate, they are one property read from two ends. The ladder has no time axis drawn on it and never has. Nobody has done anything wrong, the design was competent for its day, the coordinator is good, the board is right to want an answer, and the top of the correct ranking has just left the window on entirely honest grounds.
