# Position: Principal

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). The seat that legitimately demands a fast answer, and in doing so sets the window that excludes the strong controls.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The authority to require a response and the standing to expect one this week.
Capital, released on an annual cycle that cannot be convened by Friday.
A genuine and correct interest in people not being hurt.

## Orders

Ask what has been done.
Ask it promptly, because an organisation that asks it slowly is worse.
Accept a real, approved, implemented corrective action, and close the item.

## Loses

Nothing it can see, and the whole top of the ladder: the demand is legitimate and the deadline in it is what does the selecting, so the seat cannot get both speed and reach out of one request.
Because its urgency is the correct response to an injury, the thing that makes it responsible is the thing that narrows the answer.
It ends satisfied, truthfully, by the weakest control on the list.

## Drives

Toward a prompt and visible answer,
right in every single instance to want one,
so the seat that is most clearly doing its duty is the seat whose timescale picks the rung.
