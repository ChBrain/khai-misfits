# Piece: The Ladder

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). The hierarchy of controls, five rungs ordered by effectiveness, correct, agreed and taught everywhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Applied at [the floor](place_the_floor.md) by whoever holds [the fixer's seat](position_fixer.md), in order, from the top.
Held whole nowhere: its top two rungs live at [the drawing board](place_the_drawing_board.md) and its bottom two at the floor.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is no standard at all and every control looks equivalent, which is worse in every respect.
It is load bearing because it is **right**: the ordering rests on a real distinction, that the upper rungs work without continuous human action and the lower ones do not, and the misfit is not that the ladder is wrong but that it is silent about who can reach which rung.

## Apparent

A complete answer, and a settled one.
Because the ranking is genuinely correct and universally taught, it reads as the whole of the guidance, and the second ordering it says nothing about is invisible precisely because the first one is so sound.

## Yearbook

Applied through [ranking](process_ranking.md) and defeated at [costing](process_costing.md).
It names the control that [the reach](piece_the_reach.md) cannot buy, and its lower rungs are the ones that survive into [the action log](place_the_action_log.md) and into [the tally](piece_the_tally.md).
