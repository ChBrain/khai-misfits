# Persona: The Designer

## Taxonomy

[Origin](position_origin.md). The only party who could have removed the hazard, who did their work years ago, elsewhere, and is not reachable from the incident.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Absent, competent, and long since finished.
Specified the process at a point when elimination and substitution were cheap, which is to say before anything was built.
Not negligent: designing to the standards and knowledge of the day, on a brief that was closed and signed off.

## Action

Made the decisions that fixed which hazards would exist, at the only moment when removing them cost a drawing revision rather than a capital programme.
Delivered, and moved on, and the company may no longer exist.
Is not present at the incident, is not consulted, and has no standing in the corrective action at all.

## Shadow

Not at fault, and holding the only rung that matters: the top of the ladder is theirs and it was theirs nine years ago.
So the control the hierarchy ranks first is the one whose window shut before anybody in the current scene arrived, which is not a failing of theirs and is not recoverable by anybody else.
Their absence is the reason the ladder has a ceiling for everyone below.

## Tell

Says nothing, having said everything in a specification that is now installed plant.
Is invisible in the log, appears in no corrective action, and decides the whole of what is available.
