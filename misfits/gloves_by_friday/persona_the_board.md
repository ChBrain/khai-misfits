# Persona: The Board

## Taxonomy

[Principal](position_principal.md). Requires a visible answer on a timescale set by the incident, which is exactly what a board is for and is what selects the rung.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Serious, responsible, and correctly impatient.
Something happened, people are exposed, and the question is what has been done about it, asked this week rather than next year.
Nothing performative about it: an organisation that did not ask this would be worse.

## Action

Asks what has been done, by the end of the week.
Accepts a corrective action that is real, approved and implemented, and is right to accept it, since it is real, approved and implemented.
Funds capital work on capital timescales, through a process that runs annually and cannot be convened by Friday.

## Shadow

Not overbearing, and decisive by accident: the demand for a timely answer is legitimate, and the window it sets is the window that excludes the top of the ladder.
So the urgency that makes an organisation responsive is the same urgency that selects the weakest available control, and there is no version of the demand that both arrives fast and reaches the drawing board.
It gets a true answer to the question it asked, and the question it asked had a deadline in it.

## Tell

Says: what have we done about Tuesday.
Is told, truthfully, that a procedure has been revised and equipment issued, and closes the item, correctly.
