# Piece: The Block

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). The seventy-one occasions on which the coordinators were prevented from implementing a measure, which is what trying looks like in the record.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Between [the floor](place_the_floor.md) and everything above it, recorded in the field study rather than in any company's own log.
Answered nowhere: a prevented measure leaves no artefact, so it does not enter [the action log](place_the_action_log.md) at all.

## Load Bearing

Without it the distribution could be read as preference, and the coordinators as people who simply reach for gloves.
It is load bearing because it is the direct evidence that they did not: these were the successful coordinators, they put 280 measures in, and they were stopped seventy-one times, which places the shortfall outside their conduct in a way no argument about culture could.

## Apparent

Friction, ordinary organisational resistance, the sort of thing everybody has.
It is the least visible quantity in the play, because the record of an action is generated by its completion, so the attempts that fail are exactly the ones that leave nothing behind.

## Yearbook

Produced whenever [the reach](piece_the_reach.md) is contested rather than simply absent.
It is invisible to [the auditor](persona_the_auditor.md) by construction, and it is the reason [the tally](piece_the_tally.md) is a fact about structure rather than about the people in it.
