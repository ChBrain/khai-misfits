# Process: Sustaining

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). The continuous human effort the installed rungs require, which never completes and is where the cost actually falls.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A procedure and a piece of equipment now part of the job.
At [the floor](place_the_floor.md), on the shift after the incident and on every shift after that.

## Direction

Toward protection that exists only while it is being performed.
The lower rungs are cheap to establish and expensive to sustain, because they rely on conscious decisions and consistent behaviour, so the protection is not provided by the control, it is provided by [the worker](persona_the_worker.md) executing the control, indefinitely.
This is the residue in motion: a capital cost avoided, converted into an attention cost paid forever by somebody else.

## Lever

Attention, which is finite, and vigilance, which decays.
Because nothing in the arrangement ever completes, there is no point at which the cost stops being paid, and no entry anywhere that records it being paid at all.

## Echo

A safeguard that looks free and is not.
Read from the log the measure is closed; read from the floor it is running, every shift, and a lapse across years will be recorded afterwards as a person's error rather than as the rung that was chosen.
