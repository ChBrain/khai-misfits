# Process: Costing

## Taxonomy

[Gloves by Friday](play_gloves_by_friday.md). Each rung priced in money, downtime and authority, where the ordering turns over and the top of the ladder leaves the window.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A named control and a deadline.
At [the floor](place_the_floor.md) and against [the drawing board](place_the_drawing_board.md), each rung costed against what is available before the answer is due.

## Direction

Toward the discovery that the ranking runs backwards against the calendar.
Elimination needs the process redesigned, which needs a party at [the drawing board](place_the_drawing_board.md) who finished years ago; a guard needs installed plant altered and a line stopped; a procedure needs a meeting; equipment needs a purchase order.
This is the misfit's engine in motion: strength and lead time are the same property read from two ends, because a control that works without human effort has to be built into the thing.

## Lever

The window, set by [the board](persona_the_board.md) at the incident and not by the engineering.
Because the deadline is legitimate and the capital cycle is annual, the constraint cannot be argued away by anybody acting correctly inside it.

## Echo

A shortlist of two rungs, both at the bottom.
Nothing in the costing is wrong and nothing in it is padded, and it eliminates, on entirely honest grounds, everything the ranking put first.
