# Plan: Put Something in Place This Week

## Taxonomy

## Direction

Apply the hierarchy in order, starting at elimination, name the correct control, cost it, and then install whatever can actually be installed before the answer is due. Do not skip the ranking, because the ranking is right, and do not expect it to change what is purchasable by Friday.
