# Piece: Nobody Is Being Reckless

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). Both parties calculate correctly on real interests, and the recklessness is what the two correct calculations produce.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the provocation seat](position_provocation.md), where the reading is arithmetic.
At [the response seat](position_response.md), where declining to escalate is the only defensible choice.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play has a culprit and stops being a misfit.
It is load bearing because **each party would be criticised for behaving otherwise**: a claimant that
never pressed a legitimate claim would be failing its own people, and a government that traded a country
for a district would be arraigned for it, so the sequence is built entirely out of decisions that would
be endorsed one at a time.

## Apparent

That somebody must be gambling.
Nobody is. The claimant's reading requires no assumption of irrationality on the other side, and the
government's restraint requires no assumption of restraint on the claimant's.

## Yearbook

The quantitative finding that nuclear-capable pairs show fewer wars and more minor disputes, which is a
pattern rather than a run of individual misjudgements.
