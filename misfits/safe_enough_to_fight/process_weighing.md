# Process: Weighing

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). A government deciding what a district is worth, with a population behind it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A real provocation, and a duty that comes before everything else.
At [the cabinet room](place_the_cabinet_room.md), under time pressure, with the facts agreed.

## Direction

Toward a response that is defensible to its own people.
Declining to trade a country for a district is not weakness and would be endorsed by anybody in the seat;
a government that did otherwise would be arraigned for it, correctly.

## Lever

The options list, whose middle is short.
There is no lever that is both proportionate and believed, and resolve does not manufacture one.

## Echo

A proportionate answer,
a correct decision,
and a demonstration the other side will price the next provocation against.
