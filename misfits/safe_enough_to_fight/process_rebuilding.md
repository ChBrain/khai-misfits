# Process: Rebuilding

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). Putting rungs back into the ladder so that an answer exists that can be believed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A gap between doing nothing and ending everything.
At [the cabinet room](place_the_cabinet_room.md), after the second or third demonstration.

## Direction

Toward a proportionate response, which is the only kind that deters.
This is the answer the strategy literature actually gave and it is not naive: graduated options,
conventional strength, and a declaratory policy that matches what would really be done.

## Lever

Usability, which is what makes an answer believed.
The lever cannot be pulled one-sidedly: an answer usable by one party is a level of violence available to
both, so this process buys credibility with the thing the ceiling had taken away.

## Echo

A ladder with a middle,
deterrence restored at the low end,
and a war that is thinkable again at every level below the top.
