# Piece: Presence Is Not Credibility

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). The deterrent is available at every rung and effective at one, which is not a coverage problem.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the silo](place_the_silo.md), where readiness is verified for a case that has not arisen.
Wherever displacement is offered as the explanation for conflict moving to the low end.

## Load Bearing

Without it this collapses into the house's **The Balloon Effect** and earns no separate staging.
It is load bearing because **the switch-off runs cleanly**: stipulate unlimited coverage, the deterrent
present at every rung at once, which it already is, and the harm is untouched, because what fails is not
the reach of the threat but the belief in it.

## Apparent

That more readiness, more range or more resolve would reach further down.
None of them addresses the disproportion, and the disproportion is the whole of it.

## Yearbook

The distinction between a threat that is absent, which leaves uncertainty, and one that is present and
disproportionate, which removes it.
