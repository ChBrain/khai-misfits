# Piece: The Ones It Happens To Did Not Build It

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). The ceiling protects the parties that built it, and the fighting it clears the ground for happens elsewhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the theatre seat](position_theatre.md), which was at neither table.
In the border province, the line of control, and the third country.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play reads as a ledger between two states, and the ledger balances.
It is load bearing because **the beneficiaries and the payers are different people**: the two principals
receive a peace between themselves that is real and durable, and the cost of it is a recurring war
somewhere that had no capability, no representation and no part in either calculation.

## Apparent

That the accounting is between the two principals and comes out positive.
It comes out positive for them. The transfer to the theatre appears in no account of the peace, which is
why the peace is usually described without it.

## Yearbook

The proxy conflicts and border wars that ran through the decades in which the principals did not fight
each other, and the places they ran in.
