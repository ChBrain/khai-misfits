# Position: Parity

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). The seat that holds the ceiling: the capacity that makes the largest war unwinnable for anybody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A capability sufficient to destroy the other side after absorbing a first blow.
A record of decades in which the war it was built to prevent did not happen.
No graduated version of itself.

## Orders

Deter the largest war.
Remain survivable, so that the deterrence does not depend on going first.
Be believed, which at this level it entirely is.

## Loses

Any application below the thing it prevents.
**The capability has one setting and it is the whole of the setting**, so this seat cannot lend its
credibility downward: what makes it believed at the top is precisely the magnitude that makes it
unbelievable anywhere else.

## Drives

Toward a war that cannot be started,
which it achieves and has kept achieving,
so the seat that removed the worst outcome is the seat that cleared the ground beneath it.
