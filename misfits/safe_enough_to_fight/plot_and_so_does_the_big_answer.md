## Tension

Here the achievement turns over, and the turn is a single fact read twice.
The war is unthinkable because it would destroy both. Therefore the threat to start one is not credible
for anything that is worth less than both. And almost everything is worth less than both.
**This is not a gap in coverage.** The weapons are present at every rung: they could be used for a border
incursion, technically, tomorrow. What cannot happen is a government **wanting** to, and that is a
judgement about worth rather than about capability, so it is not reachable by readiness, range, resolve
or declaration.
And the disproportion is not a secret. Both sides can do the arithmetic, each knows the other can, and
neither needs to say anything, which is why [Presence Is Not Credibility](piece_presence_is_not_credibility.md):
the threat has not become distant or uncertain, it has become **legible**, and a legible refusal deters
less than an ambiguous one.
