# Persona: The Middle Option

## Taxonomy

[Recourse](position_recourse.md). The believable answer somebody is trying to build between doing nothing and ending everything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sensible, well-argued, and the answer the subject actually gave.
Takes the form of graduated options, conventional strength, or a stated policy.
Is aimed precisely at the gap the ceiling opened.

## Action

Adds a rung.
Makes a proportionate response available, and therefore believable.
Restores deterrence at the low end.

## Shadow

That a rung is a thing both parties can stand on.
**A usable answer is usable by whoever holds one**, so credibility at the low end is bought by putting
back the violence the ceiling had removed, and the remedy and the harm are the same object read from
two ends.

## Tell

Is the correct response to the problem.
Is the correct response that reopens the war.
