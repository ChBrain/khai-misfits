# Process: Probing

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). Pressing an old claim at a level the other side will not answer at full strength.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A grievance with no remaining route, and a reading of the ceiling.
At [the border](place_the_border.md), once the arithmetic is clear to both sides.

## Direction

Toward a claim advanced at a bearable cost.
The reading is accurate: it requires no misjudgement of the adversary, no assumption of irrationality,
and no luck, because the disproportion between a district and a country is visible to everybody.

## Lever

The gap between what is worth taking and what is worth annihilation.
The lever exists because the ceiling created it, so this process is operating on a peace it did not build.

## Echo

Something small, taken,
an answer that comes back proportionate,
and a reading confirmed as fact rather than estimate.
