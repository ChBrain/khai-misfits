# Place: The Border

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). The line of control: disputed, inhabited, and small enough to fight over.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A line on a map that is a ridge, a river, or an armistice position nobody signed as final.
Posts, patrols, and villages on both sides of something provisional.
A dispute with a documented history and no agreed resolution.

## Holds

The whole mechanism, at a scale the principals can absorb.
**This is the largest thing that is smaller than the ceiling**, which is what makes it the natural site:
big enough to be worth taking and small enough that nobody would end a country over it, and both of those
are true at once and to everybody.

## Offers

A place where the dispute can be conducted.

## Withheld

Any influence over the level at which it is settled.
The people here are inside the mechanism and outside every decision that operates it.
