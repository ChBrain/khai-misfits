# Process: Absorbing

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). The place taking the conflict that the ceiling made survivable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A column, a shelling, or a war conducted through somebody else's country.
At [the border](place_the_border.md), repeatedly, across decades.

## Direction

Toward survival at the level available.
The people here are competent at this and have had practice: they evacuate, rebuild, farm between rounds,
and go on living somewhere that neither principal would trade a city for.

## Lever

Local, and it does not touch the mechanism.
This process has no representation at either table and no instrument at the level where any of it is
decided.

## Echo

Casualties at a scale both principals can absorb,
a peace between the principals that is genuine and is not shared,
and a transfer from the parties that are safe to the party that is not, appearing in no account of it.
