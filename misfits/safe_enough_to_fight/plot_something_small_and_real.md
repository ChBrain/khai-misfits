## Tension

The claim is usually not frivolous, which the production says plainly and does not soften.
It has a history, it has been raised, and the routes that exist have been tried and are closed. A state
that abandoned it would be failing the people who hold it.
What has changed is not the claim but the price of pressing it. **The reading is arithmetic and not a
gamble**: the disproportion between a district and a country is visible to both sides, so acting on it
requires no misjudgement of the adversary, no assumption that anybody will behave irrationally, and no
luck at all.
And the scale is chosen deliberately, which is the part that makes it structural rather than opportunist:
the action is sized to stay under the level at which the answer becomes the ceiling, so the ceiling is
not being defied, it is being **used as a boundary**, and the more reliable it is the more precisely it
can be used.
