# Persona: The Balance

## Taxonomy

[Parity](position_parity.md). The ceiling itself: survivable, mutual, and entirely believed at the level it operates.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Stable, patient, and successful at the thing it was built for.
Survives a first blow, which is what makes it credible without going first.
Has been holding for decades and shows no sign of failing.

## Action

Removes the largest war from the set of things a government could choose.
Keeps removing it.
Does nothing else.

## Shadow

That it has exactly one setting and cannot lend it downward.
**What makes it believed at the top is the magnitude that makes it unbelievable below**, so its
credibility is not a stock that can be spent on smaller matters: it is a property of the size of the
thing, and the size is the problem.

## Tell

Is the reason there has been no war.
Is the reason there has been so much fighting.
