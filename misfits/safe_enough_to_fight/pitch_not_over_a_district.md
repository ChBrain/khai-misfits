# Pitch: Not Over a District

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact about what the ceiling covers, and about the ground it does not.
Unwilling to call either side reckless, because both are calculating correctly.
Naming the rung wherever the rung is the point.

## Undertow

Under the peace, one relation from strategy and one from ordinary judgement.
A war that could once be won cannot be, so no government begins one.
And a threat out of all measure with a provocation is not believed.

## Nerve

The unease of an achievement that is real and has a floor beneath it.
Nobody would end the world over a district. Everybody knows it.
That sentence is the peace, and it is also the opening.

## Echo

After the run: decades without the war the apparatus was built to prevent,
a deterrent ready at every rung and usable at one,
a column across a line of control that nobody will answer at full strength,
and a province that was party to neither calculation.
