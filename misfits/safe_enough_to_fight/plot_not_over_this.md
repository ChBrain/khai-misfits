# Plot: Not Over This

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The provocation, real and outrageous, in front of a government that holds an enormous answer.

## Action

[Weighing](process_weighing.md): [The Threat](persona_the_threat.md) stays where it is while [the response seat](position_response.md) works through [Do Not Answer This With That](plan_do_not_answer_this_with_that.md).

## Stage

[The Cabinet Room](place_the_cabinet_room.md): a map, a casualty figure, and a list of options with a gap in the middle of it.

## Tension

This is the step that spends the deterrent, and it is the step that could not have gone otherwise.
The first duty is to the population, and it is binding. A government that traded a country for a district
would be arraigned for it, correctly, by its own people first. Everybody in the room agrees on the facts
and on the arithmetic, and the disagreement, where there is one, is about which of the small options is
least bad.
So a proportionate answer goes back, and it is the right answer.
And **the correct decision is also the demonstration.** Whatever was said in public beforehand, what the
other side now observes is that this level of provocation drew this level of answer, so the next
provocation is priced against an observation rather than against a declaration, and the estimate that was
a reading has become a fact.
Both parties were right. That is the whole of the plot.
