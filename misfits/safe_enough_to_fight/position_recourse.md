# Position: Recourse

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). The seat trying to put something believable between doing nothing and ending everything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Every remedy the strategy literature produced, and each of them is real.
Intermediate options, conventional forces, declaratory policy, and negotiation.
A ladder with a very high top and nothing convincing in the middle.

## Orders

Make the answer proportionate, so that it can be believed.
Reach the low end, where the harm now lives.
Do it without making the largest war thinkable again.

## Loses

The distinction between fixing the problem and rebuilding it.
**Every rung added to the ladder is a rung somebody can climb.** The remedy for an unusable answer is a
usable one, and a usable answer is usable by both parties, so credibility at the low end is bought by
restoring exactly the violence the ceiling had removed.

## Drives

Toward a threat proportionate enough to be believed,
which is available and is the answer the subject actually gave,
so the seat with the remedy is the seat whose remedy is the harm's other name.
