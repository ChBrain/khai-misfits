## Tension

The play has to establish first that this is an achievement, because everything after it depends on there
being nothing to regret here.
A war that could once be won cannot be won. No government begins one. Decades pass. The apparatus was
built by serious people trying to prevent exactly one thing, and it prevented it, and the production
disputes none of that.
And the capability has **one setting**. There is no smaller version of it to reach for, no graduated
form, no way to lend its credibility downward, because what makes it believed at the top is the magnitude
that makes it unbelievable anywhere else.
So the ceiling is not a partial solution with a gap under it. It is complete, at the level it operates,
and the level it operates at is the only one it can reach.
