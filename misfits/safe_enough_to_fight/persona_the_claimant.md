# Persona: The Claimant

## Taxonomy

[Provocation](position_provocation.md). The state with an old grievance and a newly available way to press it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Aggrieved, patient, and usually holding a case that is not frivolous.
Has tried the routes that exist and found them closed.
Reads the other side accurately, which is the only skill the seat requires.

## Action

Takes something small.
Keeps it below the level at which the answer becomes the ceiling.
Waits, and reads the response, and prices the next one against it.

## Shadow

That the reading is a calculation and not a gamble.
**The disproportion between a district and a country is not hidden from anybody**, so acting on it
requires no misjudgement of the adversary, no assumption of irrationality, and no luck: it requires only
arithmetic that both sides can do.

## Tell

Is the party the play declines to call reckless.
Is the party whose correctness is the harm.
