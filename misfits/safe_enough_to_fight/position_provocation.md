# Position: Provocation

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). The seat with a claim it has never been able to press, and an opening it has correctly identified.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A grievance of long standing, usually documented, often with a case behind it.
No route to pressing it that the other side would concede.
An accurate reading of what will and will not be answered at full strength.

## Orders

Press the claim.
Stay below the level at which the answer becomes the ceiling.
Keep the action deniable, limited, or local, which is the same instruction three ways.

## Loses

Nothing it was going to have anyway, which is why the seat is occupied.
**The calculation is correct rather than lucky**: the disproportion between a district and a country is
not hidden, so the reading requires no gamble, no misjudgement of the adversary and no assumption that
anybody will behave irrationally.

## Drives

Toward a claim advanced at a cost it can bear,
which the ceiling has made possible for the first time,
so the seat that acts is acting on a peace it did not build and did not want.
