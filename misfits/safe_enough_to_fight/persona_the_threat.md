# Persona: The Threat

## Taxonomy

[Response](position_response.md). The very large answer that exists, is ready, and will not be used for this.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ready, capable, and sincerely meant at the level it was built for.
Could be applied to any provocation whatever, technically speaking.
Is applied to one, and everybody knows which.

## Action

Deters the thing it is proportionate to.
Sits, fully available, through everything else.
Says nothing that anybody at the low end acts on.

## Shadow

That being unusable is not the same as being absent, and is worse.
**An absent threat leaves uncertainty; a present and disproportionate one removes it**, because the
adversary is no longer guessing at capability, it is reading a decision anybody in that seat would make.

## Tell

Is the most powerful instrument either side holds.
Is the instrument that decides nothing below the top.
