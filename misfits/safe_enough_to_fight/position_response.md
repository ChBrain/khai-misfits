# Position: Response

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). The seat that has to decide what to do about something small, holding an answer that is very large.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The full capability, ready, and a population behind it.
A provocation that is real, is an outrage, and is worth a fraction of what the answer would cost.
Nothing in between that the other side finds convincing.

## Orders

Protect the population, which is the first duty and the binding one.
Answer proportionately, if a proportionate answer exists.
Do not trade a country for a district.

## Loses

Its entire deterrent below the threshold, at the moment it behaves correctly.
**Declining to escalate is the right decision and it is also the confirmation**, because the reading the
other side made is now a demonstrated fact rather than an estimate, and the next provocation is priced
against it.

## Drives

Toward a response that is defensible to its own people,
which means a response smaller than the one it holds,
so the seat that behaves responsibly is the seat that ratifies the opening.
