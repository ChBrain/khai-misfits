# Place: The Cabinet Room

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). Where a government decides what to do about something real and small, holding something enormous.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A map, a casualty figure, and a list of options with a gap in the middle of it.
Advisers who agree on the facts and on the arithmetic.
A duty to a population that is not in the room.

## Holds

The moment the reading is confirmed.
**The decision is correct and it is also the demonstration**: whatever the government judged in private,
what the other side observes is that this level of provocation drew this level of answer, and the next
one is priced against the observation rather than against the declaration.

## Offers

A defensible response, smaller than the one available.

## Withheld

Any option that is both proportionate and believed.
The middle of the list is where the room is short, and no amount of resolve in this room puts anything
there.
