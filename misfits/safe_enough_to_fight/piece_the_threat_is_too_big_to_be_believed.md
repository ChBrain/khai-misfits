# Piece: The Threat Is Too Big to Be Believed

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). Belief tracks proportion, so a response out of all measure with a provocation does not deter it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the cabinet room](place_the_cabinet_room.md), in the gap in the middle of the options list.
Wherever a very large answer is the only answer available to a very small question.

## Load Bearing

Without it this is a story about a state that cannot commit, and the house already stages that.
It is load bearing because **the block is proportionality and not capacity**: the weapons are ready and
could answer anything, the government could bind itself to anything, and what nobody would do is **want**
to end a country over a district, which is a judgement about worth rather than about ability and cannot
be repaired by resolve.

## Apparent

That a firm enough declaration closes the gap.
A declaration is free, and being free is exactly why it carries no information: the other side is reading
what the government would choose, not what it has said.

## Yearbook

Snyder's statement that the greater the stability of the balance of terror, the lower the stability of
the balance at its lower levels, offered as a restatement of what the field already held.
