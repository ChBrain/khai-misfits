## Tension

First the cost, because it does not fall where the decisions were taken.
The principals receive a peace between themselves that is real and has held for decades. The fighting the
ceiling cleared the ground for happens on a line of control, in a border province, or in a third country
that had no capability at the level that decided any of it and no representation at either table. The
transfer appears in no account of the peace, which is why the peace is usually described without it.
Then the settlements, each giving back the thing it was for.
Build the middle rung, which is the answer the subject actually gave, and you have made a proportionate
answer available and therefore believed, which is the point, and **a rung is a thing both parties can
stand on**, so the violence the ceiling removed has been put back deliberately.
Rearm conventionally, and you reach the low end at permanent cost, and the other side reads a build-up as
preparation and matches it.
Declare that any incursion at all will be met at full strength, which is free, and nobody believes a free
statement about a choice they can compute for themselves.
Settle the claim, which is the only remedy that removes the mechanism rather than relocating it, and you
have proposed the thing both parties have been failing at for as long as the claim has existed.
