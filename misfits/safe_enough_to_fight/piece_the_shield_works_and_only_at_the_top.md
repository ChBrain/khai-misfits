# Piece: The Shield Works, and Only at the Top

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). The ceiling is a genuine achievement and it is the same fact that clears the ground beneath it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the silo](place_the_silo.md), in the decades of readiness with no war.
At [the border](place_the_border.md), in the decades of fighting.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play reads as an argument that the ceiling was a mistake, which it is not making.
It is load bearing because **the achievement and the harm are one fact and not two**: the war that could
once be won cannot be, which is what everybody wanted, and a war that cannot be won is one nobody will
threaten over a district, which is what nobody wanted, and no version of the first exists without the
second.

## Apparent

That a better-designed ceiling would cover more.
Coverage is not the variable. A ceiling wide enough to be wanted for small stakes is one small enough to
be survivable, and a survivable one is not a ceiling.

## Yearbook

The argument that advantage at the strategic level does not convert into leverage below it, so the top
rung cannot be spent on anything smaller than the thing it prevents.
