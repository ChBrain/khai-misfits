# Process: Deterring

## Taxonomy

[Safe Enough to Fight](play_safe_enough_to_fight.md). Holding a survivable capability so that the largest war is not worth starting.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A war that could be won, and a determination that it should not be winnable.
At [the silo](place_the_silo.md), over years, by people trying to prevent exactly one thing.

## Direction

Toward a largest war that no government would choose.
This is done well and is done by serious people, and it succeeds: the war it was built against has not
happened, across decades, which is the outcome the whole apparatus was for.

## Lever

Magnitude, which is the only lever this process has and the source of its credibility.
There is no smaller setting, so the process cannot be aimed at anything it is not proportionate to.

## Echo

A ceiling that holds,
an answer that exists at every rung and is wanted at one,
and a stretch of ground below it that is now survivable to contest.
