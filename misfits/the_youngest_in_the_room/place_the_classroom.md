# Place: The Classroom

## Taxonomy

[The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md). Where the comparison group is the furniture.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Thirty children grouped by date of birth, a teacher who knows them all, and a year's worth of daily observation of how each of them behaves.
Concern, competence, and a real duty not to leave a child unsupported.

## Holds

[The Referral Form](piece_the_referral_form.md), and behind it criteria asking about the child's developmental level.
The only sample of ordinary five-year-old behaviour anyone in the building can actually watch.

## Offers

Close, sustained, expert attention to every child in it, of a kind almost no other setting provides.
A reliable sense of what is usual here, built from a year of watching.

## Withheld

Any group that is not this one.
The standard written in the criteria is developmental level and the standard available in the room is the other twenty-nine children, so the substitution happens without anyone choosing it: the place cannot supply an age-matched comparison because it was assembled by the calendar, and the more attentively it is observed the more confidently the wrong baseline is applied.
