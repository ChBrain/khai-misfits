# Process: Being Assessed

## Taxonomy

[The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md). The right criteria meeting the only available sample.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A child who cannot sit still, and an adult with a duty of concern.
[The One Who Assesses](persona_the_one_who_assesses.md), in [the classroom](place_the_classroom.md), with [the referral form](piece_the_referral_form.md).

## Direction

Toward a comparison with what is usual, drawn from the only sample anybody can observe, which is the class.
Toward referral, assessment and sometimes diagnosis and treatment.

## Lever

A substitution nobody performs deliberately: developmental level is the standard written down, and the room is the standard available.

## Echo

In states requiring a child to be five by the first of September, about 85 diagnoses per ten thousand for August births against about 64 for September, with no such gap in states whose cutoff falls elsewhere.
