# Persona: The One Born in September

## Taxonomy

[One Born in September](position_one_born_in_september.md). The oldest in the room, and the beneficiary who never learns of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A child who happens to have been born just after the cutoff, and is therefore the oldest in every room they will be in for the next thirteen years.
At five, nearly a fifth of a life ahead of the youngest.

## Action

Runs faster, sits still longer, reads sooner and is picked more often, all of which is true.
Receives the coaching, the minutes and the expectation that follow being picked.

## Shadow

Is genuinely better, and the reason is not available to anybody.
Nothing about this persona is fraudulent and no adult is deceived: the superiority is real on every day it is measured, and its origin is a date on a form that no coach, teacher or clinician has any occasion to consult, so the advantage is invisible precisely because it is not a claim anyone is making.

## Tell

Is right about being good at it, and would have been about equally good either way.
