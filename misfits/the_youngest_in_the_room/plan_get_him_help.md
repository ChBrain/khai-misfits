# Plan: Get Him Help

## Taxonomy

A **duty of care**: the intent of [The One Who Assesses](persona_the_one_who_assesses.md), inside [The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Assesses](persona_the_one_who_assesses.md)

## Direction

Do not leave a struggling child unsupported. Notice what is in front of you, describe it accurately, compare it with what is usual, and get the child assessed by someone qualified.

## Orders

(Observe daily in [the classroom](place_the_classroom.md); complete [the referral form](piece_the_referral_form.md) accurately; apply criteria asking whether the behaviour is inappropriate for the developmental level; refer through [being assessed](process_being_assessed.md).)

## Implementation

([The Classroom](place_the_classroom.md), where the only observable sample of ordinary behaviour is twenty-nine children grouped by date of birth; concern, competence and a real professional obligation.)

## Targets

- [ ] the child who is struggling is noticed rather than left
- [ ] the behaviour is described accurately and completely, which it is
- [ ] the published criteria are applied carefully by someone qualified
- [ ] the comparison group is the room, because no other sample exists, and nothing on the form marks where the substitution happened
