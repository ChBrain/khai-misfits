# Process: Drawing the Line

## Taxonomy

[The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md). A date chosen for timetables, deciding a childhood.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The need to group children at all, and an authority with a timetable to write.
[The Line in the Calendar](persona_the_line_in_the_calendar.md), holding [Line in the Calendar](position_line_in_the_calendar.md).

## Direction

Toward twelve-month bands, which is the standard and sensible way to organise schooling and junior sport.
Toward an oldest and a youngest in every band, separated by up to a year.

## Lever

Arbitrariness, which is genuine: nothing turns on which date is picked, and that is exactly why nobody returns to it.

## Echo

At five years old, a gap of nearly a fifth of a life inside a group that will be treated as one kind of child.
