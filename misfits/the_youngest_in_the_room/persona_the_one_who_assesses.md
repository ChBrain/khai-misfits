# Persona: The One Who Assesses

## Taxonomy

[One Who Assesses](position_one_who_assesses.md). The teacher and the clinician, applying the criteria properly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A teacher who has noticed a child struggling, and behind them a clinician applying published diagnostic criteria with care.
People acting out of concern, with a real duty not to leave a child unsupported.

## Action

Observes the behaviour, compares it against what is usual, and refers.
Applies criteria that ask whether the behaviour is inappropriate for the child's developmental level.

## Shadow

Has only one comparison group available, and it is the wrong one.
The criteria say developmental level and the evidence to hand is the rest of the class, so the standard being applied in practice is the room: this is not a misapplication of the test, it is what applying the test looks like when the only sample of normal behaviour a teacher can observe is thirty children the school put together by date of birth.

## Tell

Would be negligent to ignore what they are seeing, and what they are seeing is a birthday.
