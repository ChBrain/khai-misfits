# Piece: The Referral Form

## Taxonomy

[The Classroom](place_the_classroom.md). The instrument that asks about age and is answered by the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [the classroom](place_the_classroom.md), completed by a teacher out of genuine concern, and passed to a clinician who applies the criteria carefully.

## Load Bearing

It contains the substitution, and contains it invisibly.
The criteria ask whether the behaviour is inappropriate for the child's developmental level, which is the right question: the only evidence a teacher can supply is how this child compares with the others in the class, which is a different question, and nothing on the form marks the point where one has been answered in place of the other.

## Apparent

A careful, concerned, professionally proper document, describing behaviour accurately and completely.
Exactly what a responsible adult should complete when a child appears to be struggling.

## Yearbook

Built on criteria that name developmental level precisely because their authors understood the difference.
Administered in the one setting where an age-matched comparison group cannot exist, since the setting was assembled by date of birth.
