# Plan: Move the Line

## Taxonomy

A **correction**: the second intent of [The One Who Picks](persona_the_one_who_picks.md), inside [The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md), once the pattern is named.

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Picks](persona_the_one_who_picks.md)

## Direction

Stop reading the calendar as ability. Shift the cutoff, band by maturity rather than by birthday, rotate the date so no cohort carries it permanently, or at least tell the people doing the selecting what they are looking at.

## Orders

(Move [the line in the calendar](persona_the_line_in_the_calendar.md); or band by biological maturity; or rotate the cutoff between years; or publish the effect to everyone who writes [the squad list](piece_the_squad_list.md) or [the referral form](piece_the_referral_form.md).)

## Implementation

([The Trial Session](place_the_trial_session.md) and [The Classroom](place_the_classroom.md), which each need a comparison group neither can assemble; and a grouping that cannot be abolished, only redrawn.)

## Targets

- [ ] the price is accepted: shifting the date makes a different set of children youngest, and helps nobody
- [ ] the price is accepted: banding by maturity means measuring children's bodies, and invites gaming by adults with an interest in the answer
- [ ] the price is accepted: rotating the cutoff protects no child and spreads the harm thinly across all of them
- [ ] the price is accepted: warning the selectors lowers confidence in every young assessment without identifying which ones were wrong
