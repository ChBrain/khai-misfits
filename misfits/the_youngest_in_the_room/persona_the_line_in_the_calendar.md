# Persona: The Line in the Calendar

## Taxonomy

[Line in the Calendar](position_line_in_the_calendar.md). The cutoff, chosen for convenience and consequential for life.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A date: born before it, you are in this year. Set by an education authority or a sporting body for administrative reasons, and nothing turning on which date it is.
The most consequential arbitrary decision in the play, made by people thinking about timetables.

## Action

Sorts children into twelve-month bands.
Produces, in every band, an oldest and a youngest separated by up to a year.

## Shadow

Cannot be removed by being moved.
Any line at all creates a youngest, so shifting the date changes which children are disadvantaged and not whether any are: the only arrangement with no youngest child is no grouping of children at all, which nobody proposes and nothing could deliver.

## Tell

Is the cause of everything that follows, and is the one element in the play nobody ever looks at twice.
