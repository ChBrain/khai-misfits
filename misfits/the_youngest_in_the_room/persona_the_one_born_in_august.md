# Persona: The One Born in August

## Taxonomy

[One Born in August](position_one_born_in_august.md). The youngest in the room, assessed against it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A child born just before the cutoff, and therefore the youngest in every room for the next thirteen years.
Ordinary in every respect, and eleven months behind the child at the next desk.

## Action

Is slower, smaller, less able to sit still and quicker to lose the thread, all of which is accurate.
Is not picked, or is picked and referred, and receives whichever of those follows.

## Shadow

Presents identically to a child who is struggling, and there is no test that separates them.
Restlessness, distraction and difficulty sitting still are what eleven months younger looks like at five, so the behaviour is real, the observation is correct and the inference is the only one the available comparison supports: the youngest child and the child with a difficulty produce the same evidence, and nothing in the room can tell them apart.

## Tell

Is measured accurately, against the wrong thing, by people who are trying to help.
