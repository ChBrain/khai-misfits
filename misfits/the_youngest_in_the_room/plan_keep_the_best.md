# Plan: Keep the Best

## Taxonomy

A **professional method**: the intent of [The One Who Picks](persona_the_one_who_picks.md), inside [The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Picks](persona_the_one_who_picks.md)

## Direction

Fill the limited places with the best available players, judged on what they can do, and then develop the ones you chose, because a squad you do not invest in is not a squad.

## Orders

(Run the same drills for everybody with the same time each in [the trial session](place_the_trial_session.md); rank on performance; write [the squad list](piece_the_squad_list.md); give [the extra hour](piece_the_extra_hour.md) to the players on it.)

## Implementation

([The Trial Session](place_the_trial_session.md); the [being picked](process_being_picked.md) and [getting the coaching](process_getting_the_coaching.md) processes; a registration form with dates of birth on it that nobody has any occasion to open.)

## Targets

- [ ] every child gets the same drills, the same watching and the same chance to show what they can do
- [ ] the ranking reflects performance on the day accurately
- [ ] the selected players are developed, which is the point of selecting them
- [ ] nothing in the session could distinguish a good player from an old one, so nothing does
