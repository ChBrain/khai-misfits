# Piece: The Squad List

## Taxonomy

[The Trial Session](place_the_trial_session.md). A correct record of one morning, which becomes a forecast.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Written at the end of the session in [the trial session](place_the_trial_session.md), and read afterwards as a statement about the children rather than about the morning.

## Load Bearing

It converts an observation into an allocation.
The list is accurate about who performed better that day, and from the moment it exists it stops being a record and starts being a distribution of coaching, minutes and expectation: nothing in it is false, and everything downstream treats it as a claim about ability rather than a claim about a Saturday.

## Apparent

A dozen names, chosen on the evidence, by someone with no reason to favour anybody.
Defensible in every particular, and revisable in principle at the next trial.

## Yearbook

Revisable in principle and rarely revised in practice, because the children on it are by then genuinely better, having had the coaching.
Reproduced upward at every level, so the same sorting is applied again to a pool that has already been sorted.
