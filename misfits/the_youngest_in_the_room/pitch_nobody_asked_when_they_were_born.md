# Pitch: Nobody Asked When They Were Born

## Taxonomy

[The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Kindly and competent. A fair trial with everybody given the same chance, an experienced coach picking the best players on the day, a teacher who notices a child struggling and does something about it, a clinician applying published criteria carefully.
Every observation accurate, every intention good, every judgement defensible.

## Undertow

Under the competence, a date chosen for administrative convenience, and a comparison group that is the room rather than the age.
At five years old, eleven months is a fifth of a life, and it presents as the child rather than as the calendar.

## Nerve

The vertigo of an assessment that is entirely correct about what it measured, and measured the wrong thing without any way of knowing.

## Echo

Afterward: professional rosters stacked toward the early months of the selection year, about 85 diagnoses per ten thousand for August births against 64 for September, and no such gap at all in the states whose cutoff falls elsewhere.
