# Plot: Behind by Eleven Months

## Taxonomy

[The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A child who cannot sit still, in a room where the only sample of ordinary behaviour anybody can watch is the other twenty-nine children.

## Action

[Being assessed](process_being_assessed.md): [The One Who Assesses](persona_the_one_who_assesses.md), holding [One Who Assesses](position_one_who_assesses.md), completes [the referral form](piece_the_referral_form.md) out of genuine concern, and a clinician applies the published criteria with care.

## Stage

In states requiring a child to be five by the first of September, about 85 diagnoses per ten thousand among August births against about 64 among September births, with treatment rates about a third higher.

## Tension

The criteria ask about developmental level and the evidence available answers a different question, and nothing marks the substitution.
The behaviour is real, the description is accurate and ignoring it would be the negligence: restlessness and difficulty sitting still are simply what eleven months younger looks like at five, so the youngest child and a struggling child produce identical evidence. What settles the cause is the control the researchers had for free: no such gap appears in states whose cutoff falls in another month.
