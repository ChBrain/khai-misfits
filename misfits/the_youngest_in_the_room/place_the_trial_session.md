# Place: The Trial Session

## Taxonomy

[The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md). Where the pick is made, fairly, on everything that can be seen.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A pitch or a rink on a Saturday, numbered bibs, the same drills for everybody and the same amount of time each.
An experienced selector watching closely, and a fair process by every standard anyone would apply to it.

## Holds

[The Squad List](piece_the_squad_list.md), which will be written at the end of the morning.
Children up to a year apart in age, standing in the same queue, indistinguishable by anything on display.

## Offers

Genuine equality of opportunity in the only sense a session can provide: the same drills, the same watching, the same chance to show what you can do.
An accurate ranking of who performed better this morning.

## Withheld

The one column that would explain the ranking.
Dates of birth are on a registration form nobody has any occasion to open, and no drill anywhere distinguishes a good player from an old one, so this place produces a correct measurement of performance and holds nothing capable of showing what the performance was made of.
