# Persona: The One Who Picks

## Taxonomy

[One Who Picks](position_one_who_picks.md). The selector, observing accurately and choosing correctly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An experienced coach or scout at a trial session, with limited places and a duty to fill them with the best available players.
Not biased, not lazy, and not fooled by anything.

## Action

Watches, compares, and picks the children who are faster, stronger, steadier and better at the thing being tested.
Invests in the ones picked, which is the sensible thing to do with a squad.

## Shadow

Is right on the day, and the day is the whole of what can be seen.
There is nothing false to catch here, because nothing false has been said: the older child genuinely is better at that trial, so a selector who ignored it would be selecting worse players on purpose, and the information that would distinguish a good player from an old one does not exist anywhere in the session.

## Tell

Makes an accurate assessment that becomes accurate about a different thing, through the coaching that follows it.
