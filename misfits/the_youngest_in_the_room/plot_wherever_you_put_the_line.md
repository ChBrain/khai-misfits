## Cue

## Action

[Drawing the line](process_drawing_the_line.md): [The Line in the Calendar](persona_the_line_in_the_calendar.md), holding [Line in the Calendar](position_line_in_the_calendar.md), fixes a date, and everyone born before it is in this year.

## Stage

Twelve-month bands, which is the standard and sensible arrangement. Inside each one, children up to twelve months apart.

## Tension

The arbitrariness is genuine, and it is exactly why nobody ever goes back to look at it.
Nothing turns on which date is chosen, so the choice is made by people thinking about terms and timetables and is never revisited: and yet at five years old the gap it opens is nearly a fifth of a life. The seat cannot be made harmless by being moved, because any line produces a youngest, and the only arrangement with no youngest child is no grouping of children at all.
