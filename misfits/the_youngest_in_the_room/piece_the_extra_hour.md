# Piece: The Extra Hour

## Taxonomy

[The Trial Session](place_the_trial_session.md). The coaching that follows selection, and makes the difference real.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Everywhere after [the trial session](place_the_trial_session.md): the better team, the stronger opponents, the extra minutes, the expectation of being one of the good ones.

## Load Bearing

It is what turns a transient age gap into a permanent ability gap.
A difference made of eleven months would close on its own within a few years, since children converge as they grow: this piece prevents that by feeding the gap continuously, so the advantage that was going to disappear is instead compounded into something that will still be there at twenty.

## Apparent

Ordinary good practice. You invest in the players you have selected, because that is what selection is for and the alternative is coaching nobody in particular.
Generous, well meant, and correct as coaching.
