## Tension

There is nothing false here to catch, because nothing false has been said.
The older child genuinely is better at that trial, so a selector who set it aside would be choosing worse players on purpose, and no drill anywhere distinguishes a good player from an old one. The dates of birth sit on a registration form nobody has any occasion to open. So the place produces a correct measurement of performance while holding nothing at all that could show what the performance was made of.
