# Position: One Born in August

## Taxonomy

[The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md). The seat that pays the calendar in a currency nobody labels.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A birthday just before the cutoff, and therefore up to eleven months less of life than the child at the next desk.
The behaviour that eleven months less of life produces.

## Orders

Be the youngest in the room.
Be assessed against it.

## Loses

The distinction between being young and being behind.
Restlessness, distraction and difficulty sitting still are exactly what eleven months younger looks like at five, so this seat and a genuinely struggling child produce identical evidence, and nothing available in the room separates them: the observation is accurate, the inference is the only one the comparison supports, and the answer is wrong.

## Drives

Toward accurate measurement of the wrong quantity, by people trying to help, with no mechanism anywhere for the mistake to surface.
