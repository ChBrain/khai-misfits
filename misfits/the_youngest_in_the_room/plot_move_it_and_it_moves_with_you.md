# Plot: Move It and It Moves With You

## Taxonomy

[The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The effect understood at last, and the answers on the table: shift the date, band by maturity, rotate the cutoff, or warn the people doing the judging.

## Action

[Move the line](plan_move_the_line.md): [The One Who Picks](persona_the_one_who_picks.md) reaches for each in turn, and finds that the grouping cannot be abolished, only redrawn.

## Stage

Shifting the date makes a different set of children the youngest. Rotating it spreads the harm thinly over all of them and protects none.

## Tension

Every correction relocates the disadvantage, because the disadvantage is the grouping rather than the date.
Banding by biological maturity works and means measuring children's bodies, and invites gaming by adults with an interest in the answer. Warning the selectors is the one cheap intervention and it lowers confidence in every young player's assessment without identifying which ones were mismeasured, so it buys general doubt rather than particular correction. There is no version of the line that has no youngest child standing at the end of it.
