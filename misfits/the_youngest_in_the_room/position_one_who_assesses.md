# Position: One Who Assesses

## Taxonomy

[The Youngest in the Room](play_the_youngest_in_the_room.md). The seat with the right criteria and the wrong sample.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Published criteria asking whether behaviour is inappropriate for the child's developmental level.
A duty of concern, and one observable comparison group: the class.

## Orders

Notice the child who is struggling.
Compare against what is usual, and refer.

## Loses

The gap between the standard written down and the standard available.
The criteria say developmental level and the evidence to hand is thirty children the school grouped by date of birth, so what is actually applied is the room: this is not a misapplication of the test but what applying it looks like when no other sample of normal behaviour exists, and ignoring what is plainly visible would be the negligence.

## Drives

Toward a referral that is careful, concerned, professionally correct, and triggered by a birthday.
