# Position: One Who Strung It

## Taxonomy

[The Far End of the Wire](play_the_far_end_of_the_wire.md). The seat that compensates correctly for a flow that will one day reverse.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A conductor, a length of run, a load at the end of it, and the standard practice for serving all three.
Authority over [the tap setting](piece_the_tap_setting.md), and none at all over who will later install what.

## Orders

Size the cable for the load.
Lift the supply end so the far end still arrives inside limits under load.
Serve every customer on the road properly, which it does, for decades.

## Loses

Any way to compensate for one direction that is not a miscompensation for the other.
The lift is a fixed quantity applied to a variable that has only ever had one sign, so the seat cannot hedge without under-serving the far end for the whole life of the network against a reversal that may never come.

## Drives

Toward a road where the last house is properly supplied, which is the whole of the brief and is achieved, and into the one arrangement that makes that same house the first to be cut off when the current turns.
