# Piece: The Ceiling

## Taxonomy

[The Line Out](place_the_line_out.md). The hosting limit: a number belonging to a cable, which nobody on it is told.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

A property of this conductor, this length and this transformer, present on [the line out](place_the_line_out.md) from the day it was strung.

## Load Bearing

Without it every roof could export freely and there is no structure; with it disclosed at application there is a queue people could see.
It is the piece that makes the harm unappealable: the limit is real, fixed and knowable to the network operator, and it appears on no application form, so it is crossed by the arrival of a neighbour rather than by anything the household that pays for it did.

## Apparent

Nothing. It has no meter, no indicator and no notice.
Measured in the literature as a proportion of customers exporting, with effects appearing at low penetration and becoming significant above roughly a third of the street.

## Yearbook

Present and irrelevant for the whole life of the road, when nothing exported.
Reached quietly, one approval at a time, by a sequence of decisions none of which was ever asked to consider it.
