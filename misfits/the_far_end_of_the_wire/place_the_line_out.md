# Place: The Line Out

## Taxonomy

[The Far End of the Wire](play_the_far_end_of_the_wire.md). The feeder itself, along which everything happens by distance.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A single cable running out from a substation along a road, with houses tapped off it in order.
Nothing visible changes along its length. It looks identical at the first house and the last.

## Holds

[The Tap Setting](piece_the_tap_setting.md) at its supply end, lifted to compensate for the run.
[The Ceiling](piece_the_ceiling.md), which belongs to this conductor and this length and is written down nowhere the street can see.

## Offers

Power to every house on it, reliably, for decades, and now a path back for whatever those houses generate.
No obstruction to anybody and no discrimination between them.

## Withheld

Any equality of position.
Distance is the one property it distributes unevenly, and it distributes it by address: the same current produces a different voltage at every point along the run, so a road that treats everyone identically produces radically different outcomes at each end of itself.
