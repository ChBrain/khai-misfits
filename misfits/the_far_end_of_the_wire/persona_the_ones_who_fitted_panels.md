# Persona: The Ones Who Fitted Panels

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Fitted Panels](position_ones_who_fitted_panels.md). Each install legal, correctly sized, approved, and separately harmless.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary households on an ordinary street. A fair tariff, a good installer, a sensible thing to do with a south-facing roof.
Each one applies, is assessed on its own merits, and is approved.

## Action

Fit panels, one roof at a time, over several years.
Export whatever is not used, which is what the scheme is for and what the meter is there to record.

## Shadow

Cannot see the street, only the roof.
One installation exporting does nothing whatever, and there is no rule against it and no reason for one, so nothing in any single approval could have been decided differently. The quantity that matters is how many of them there are, which is not a fact about any of them and appears on nobody's application.

## Tell

Says "it is only my roof", correctly, in a street where the same correct sentence is being said by everybody.
