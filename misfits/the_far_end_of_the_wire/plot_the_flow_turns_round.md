# Plot: The Flow Turns Round

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A still bright noon in spring. The sun over the whole street at once, and almost nobody home.

## Action

[Turning back](process_turning_back.md): generation exceeds local demand at the same moment along the road, and the current on [the line out](place_the_line_out.md) reverses.

## Stage

Nobody stages this. No coordination occurs, no limit is exceeded, and no equipment does anything other than what it does every day.

## Tension

The reversal requires nothing but simultaneity, which is weather.
There is no act here to attribute, no threshold anyone crossed and no decision that could have gone differently, because the sun arriving over a whole street at the same time is not an arrangement anybody made. And with the current reversed, the network is now running a relationship it has always run, in a direction it has never run it.
