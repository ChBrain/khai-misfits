# Process: Cutting Out

## Taxonomy

[The Far End of the Wire](play_the_far_end_of_the_wire.md). The protection operating, and the loss landing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Voltage above the permissible limit at the electrical end of the feeder.
[The Safety Trip](piece_the_safety_trip.md), inside the inverter at [the last house](persona_the_last_house.md).

## Direction

Downward: first reducing output as the voltage climbs, then disconnecting altogether if it stays high.
Aimed at safety, and achieving it exactly.

## Lever

A certified threshold, identical in every unit on the street, doing precisely what it was fitted to do.

## Echo

Legal generation from correct equipment switched off by its own protection, in one household, chosen by distance.
