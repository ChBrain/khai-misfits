# Persona: The Last House

## Taxonomy

[Last House](position_last_house.md). Furthest from the substation, and therefore the one that pays.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An ordinary household at the end of an ordinary road. Same panels as the neighbours, same installer, same tariff, approved on the same terms.
Distinguished from everyone else on the street by nothing but distance.

## Action

Generates, exports, and watches the inverter reduce output through the middle of a bright afternoon.
Loses the generation entirely when [the safety trip](piece_the_safety_trip.md) disconnects it, then reconnects, then drops out again.

## Shadow

Cannot change the thing that selected it.
Position on a wire is not a choice, not a rule broken, and not a fault that can be remedied by better equipment: the same inverter anywhere else on the road would run all afternoon. So the household bearing the whole cost is the one with the least ability to do anything about it, and no misconduct anywhere in the sequence to appeal against.

## Tell

Rings the installer, who finds nothing wrong, because there is nothing wrong: the equipment is working correctly and is being switched off by its own protection.
