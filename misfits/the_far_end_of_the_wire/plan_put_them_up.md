# Plan: Put Them Up

## Taxonomy

A **personal scheme**: the intent of [The Ones Who Fitted Panels](persona_the_ones_who_fitted_panels.md), inside [The Far End of the Wire](play_the_far_end_of_the_wire.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The Ones Who Fitted Panels](persona_the_ones_who_fitted_panels.md)

## Direction

Put panels on a south-facing roof at a fair price, under a scheme built to encourage exactly that. Apply properly, be assessed, be approved, and export whatever the household does not use.

## Orders

(Each occupant of the seat applies, is assessed on the merits of its own application, is approved, installs correctly sized equipment, and exports the surplus.)

## Implementation

([The Line Out](place_the_line_out.md); the [putting panels up](process_putting_panels_up.md) process; nothing in the application asks about [the ceiling](piece_the_ceiling.md), because no form has a field for it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the installation is correctly sized for the roof
- [ ] the application is assessed and approved on its own merits
- [ ] the surplus is exported, which is what the scheme is for
- [ ] nothing anywhere in the process counts how many neighbours have already done the same
