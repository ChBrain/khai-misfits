# Persona: The Climbing Volt

## Taxonomy

[Climbing Volt](position_climbing_volt.md). The same relationship, running with one sign reversed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not a fault, not a surge and not a defect. Simply what voltage does along a conductor carrying current, which is to change with distance.
Indifferent to direction, because the relationship it obeys has no term for intent.

## Action

Falls along [the line out](place_the_line_out.md) whenever power flows outward, which is what the network was built around.
Rises along the same run whenever power flows back, which requires nothing but the current reversing.

## Shadow

Cannot be prevented without preventing either the export or the distance.
There is no third option in the arithmetic: current, impedance and length are what it is made of, and all three are physical facts of a street that already exists. So the rise is not a thing to be fixed at its source, only paid for somewhere.

## Tell

Announces nothing. It is highest exactly where there is least equipment to notice it, at the far end of a road, in a house with no instrument and no reason to look.
