# Piece: The Tap Setting

## Taxonomy

[The Substation](place_the_substation.md). The lift that protects the far end under load and pushes it over under export.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the substation](place_the_substation.md), applied to the whole of [the line out](place_the_line_out.md), set once and correct ever since.

## Load Bearing

Without it the far end is under-served for the life of the network, and with it the far end is the first to be cut off.
It is the piece that carries the inversion: a fixed lift applied to a variable that has only ever had one sign, so the same number is the right answer in one direction and the wrong one in the other, without being touched.

## Apparent

A number on a regulator. Standard practice, defensible to anybody, and unchanged since commissioning.
Not a bodge, not a shortcut, and exactly what a competent engineer would set for a long run.

## Yearbook

Chosen for a road where power went one way.
Still chosen for a road where it goes both, because there is one setting and two directions, and no adjustment helps the far end at noon without hurting the near end at seven.
