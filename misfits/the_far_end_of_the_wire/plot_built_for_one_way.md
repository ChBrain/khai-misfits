## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

A street to be supplied, a substation at one end, and power that has only ever travelled outward.

## Action

[Stringing](process_stringing.md): [The One Who Strung It](persona_the_one_who_strung_it.md), holding [One Who Strung It](position_one_who_strung_it.md), sizes the conductor and lifts [the tap setting](piece_the_tap_setting.md) to compensate for the sag along the run.

## Stage

[The One Who Strung It](persona_the_one_who_strung_it.md), having tabled [Build It for the Load](plan_build_it_for_the_load.md), delivers a road on which every house, including the furthest, arrives inside limits under load.

## Tension

The compensation is correct and is the whole of the problem.
Lifting the supply end is not a bodge, it is how a long run is served, and it would be done the same way today by anybody competent. What has also just been created, and is on no drawing, is a fixed quantity sitting on a variable that has only ever carried one sign, in a network where nothing yet suggests the sign could change.
