# Pitch: Nobody Chose the Order

## Taxonomy

[The Far End of the Wire](play_the_far_end_of_the_wire.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Plain and municipal. Good engineering, a sunny street, panels going up one roof at a time and everybody pleased about it.
Nothing adversarial anywhere: a network built properly, a scheme working as intended, neighbours doing a sensible thing at a fair price.

## Undertow

Under the ordinary competence, one line of arithmetic changing sign.
Nothing breaks and nothing degrades. The same relationship runs, backwards, and the compensation that was protecting the far end becomes the thing that pushes it over, without anyone touching a setting or exceeding a limit.

## Nerve

The particular unfairness of a cost that is assigned by where you live on a road, and that arrives because a neighbour did something entirely reasonable.

## Echo

Afterward: a street full of working panels, one household whose inverter drops out most afternoons in spring, a ceiling that appears on no application form, and no component anywhere that an inspection could call faulty.
