# Plot: Everyone Fits Panels

## Taxonomy

[The Far End of the Wire](play_the_far_end_of_the_wire.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A sunny region, a fair tariff, a good installer, and a street of south-facing roofs.

## Action

[Putting panels up](process_putting_panels_up.md): [The Ones Who Fitted Panels](persona_the_ones_who_fitted_panels.md), holding [Ones Who Fitted Panels](position_ones_who_fitted_panels.md), apply one household at a time and are approved on the merits of each application.

## Stage

[The Ones Who Fitted Panels](persona_the_ones_who_fitted_panels.md), working [Put Them Up](plan_put_them_up.md), install correctly sized equipment and export the surplus, which is exactly what the scheme exists to encourage.

## Tension

Every approval is right and no approval could have been decided otherwise.
One roof exporting has no measurable effect, there is no rule against it and no evidence on which to refuse, so nothing in any individual case is available to be reconsidered. The quantity that will matter is how many of them there are, and that is a fact about the street rather than about any household, so it appears on no application and is nobody's to weigh.
