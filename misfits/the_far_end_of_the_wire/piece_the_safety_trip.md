# Piece: The Safety Trip

## Taxonomy

[The Line Out](place_the_line_out.md). The protection that removes the generation it was fitted to protect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Inside the inverter at [the last house](persona_the_last_house.md), and inside every other inverter on the road identically.

## Load Bearing

Without it an overvoltage is a hazard, and with it the overvoltage becomes a curtailment.
It is the piece that converts a physical quantity into a loss borne by one household: the device does not fail, malfunction or misjudge, it performs exactly to specification, and performing to specification is what switches off legal generation from correct equipment.

## Apparent

A threshold and a relay. Reduce output as voltage climbs; disconnect if it stays above the permissible limit; reconnect when it is safe.
Required, certified, and identical in every unit on the street.

## Yearbook

Never once operated for forty years, because nothing exported.
Operating most bright afternoons in spring now, in one house, while the identical device in every other house on the road sits untroubled.
