# Persona: The One Who Strung It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Strung It](position_one_who_strung_it.md). Engineers a feeder correctly for the only flow that exists, and lifts the supply end to protect the far end.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent and municipal. Knows the conductor, the length of the run and the load at the end of it.
Sizes the cable, sets the supply voltage high enough that the last house on the road still arrives inside limits, and signs off a network that serves every customer properly.

## Action

Runs [the line out](place_the_line_out.md) from [the substation](place_the_substation.md) and sets [the tap setting](piece_the_tap_setting.md) deliberately high, which is the standard and correct compensation for voltage sagging along a long run.
Serves the street for decades without a complaint.

## Shadow

Cannot build a conductor that knows which way the current is going.
The compensation is right, is standard, and would be done the same way today, and it is a fixed lift applied to a variable that will one day run the other way: so the seat's best work is the quantity that later becomes the pressure, and no revision of the design could have avoided it without under-serving the far end for the whole life of the network.

## Tell

Says "the far end needed the extra volts", which was true for forty years and is the exact sentence that explains the overvoltage.
