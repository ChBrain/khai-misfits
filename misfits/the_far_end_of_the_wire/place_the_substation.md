# Place: The Substation

## Taxonomy

[The Far End of the Wire](play_the_far_end_of_the_wire.md). Where the compensation is set, correctly, once.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A fenced enclosure, a transformer, a regulator with a setting on it.
Nothing dramatic and nothing neglected: inspected, maintained, and doing what it has always done.

## Holds

[The Tap Setting](piece_the_tap_setting.md), the lift applied to the whole road.
The authority to change it, which is real and which cannot help the far end without hurting the near end.

## Offers

A supply voltage chosen so that every house on the run, including the furthest, arrives inside limits under load.
Exactly the service the network was built to give.

## Withheld

A second setting for the other direction.
There is one lift for one cable, and it must serve a road that now runs both ways, so the same number is simultaneously the right answer for the evening and the wrong one for the middle of a bright spring day.
