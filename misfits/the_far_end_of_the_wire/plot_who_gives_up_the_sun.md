# Plot: Who Gives Up the Sun

## Taxonomy

[The Far End of the Wire](play_the_far_end_of_the_wire.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The hosting limit finally understood, and three answers on the table: strengthen the road, share the curtailment, or close the road to new export.

## Action

[Cutting out](process_cutting_out.md) redistributed rather than removed: [The One Who Strung It](persona_the_one_who_strung_it.md), holding [One Who Strung It](position_one_who_strung_it.md), chooses which of the three to pay for.

## Stage

[The One Who Strung It](persona_the_one_who_strung_it.md) tables [Share the Ceiling](plan_share_the_ceiling.md), and whichever door is opened, somebody is standing behind it.

## Tension

Every answer works and every answer charges a different innocent party.
Reconductoring raises the ceiling and is paid by every customer on the tariff, including those with no panels and no export. Volt-var and droop-based sharing spread the loss across all the exporters, and spreading it raises the total energy curtailed rather than lowering it, so the fairer arrangement is the more wasteful one. Capping export on a saturated feeder protects everyone already connected and hands the entire bill to whoever applies next, refused a lawful upgrade for their position in a queue that was never published.
