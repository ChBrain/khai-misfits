# Persona: The Ally

## Taxonomy

[Ally](position_ally.md). The one whose survival is the subject of somebody else's sentence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Direct and slightly too insistent, asking the question a state in its position is obliged to ask.
Wants the territory named, the contingency named, the forces named, and a timetable.
Concedes nothing to the argument that vagueness is useful, because vagueness is useful to the other party.

## Action

Runs [Ask for It in Writing](plan_ask_for_it_in_writing.md): through [Reassuring](process_reassuring.md) at [the Ally's Capital](place_the_allys_capital.md), setting [the Dial](piece_the_dial.md), and through [Arming](process_arming.md) at the same place, setting [the Hedge](piece_the_hedge.md).
Presses for specifics while the specifics are still obtainable.
Reads every softening of the language as data, and acts on it.

## Shadow

Not a client and not a supplicant: it is doing the arithmetic any government would do, and the arithmetic says that a guarantee it cannot quote is a guarantee it cannot rely on.
Because the answer it needs is exactly the answer the patron cannot safely give, its most reasonable act is the one that starts the trap.
And when the answer softens it does not sulk, it hedges, and every hedge available to it is prudent and makes the guarantor's position worse.

## Tell

Asks for a document and gets a communique.
Cannot be reassured by a formula that does not name its territory, and cannot be blamed for that, since a formula that names no territory reassures nobody.
