# Process: Reassuring

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). Borne at [The Ally's Capital](place_the_allys_capital.md) and at [The Treaty Text](place_the_treaty_text.md), precipitated by [The Ally](persona_the_ally.md) asking what is actually covered, in [Exactly How Far Will You Go](plot_exactly_how_far_will_you_go.md) and [Put It in the Text](plot_put_it_in_the_text.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A state whose survival rests on somebody else's decision, asking for the one thing that would let it plan.

## Direction

From a general assurance toward a specific one, since nothing vague comforts anybody: the territory named, the contingency named, the forces named, in words a legislature can be held to.

## Lever

That specificity is the only currency reassurance is sold in, so the more the promise is made to do its job the more exactly it states its own edges (Glenn Snyder; Schelling).

## Echo

[The Dial](piece_the_dial.md), one setting with a bad end at each stop, and [The Published Map](piece_the_published_map.md), the same sentence read by the party it was written against.
