# Persona: The Partner

## Taxonomy

[Partner](position_partner.md). The other ally, who was not in the room and read the whole thing anyway.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Courteous and watchful, raising the matter obliquely and meaning it entirely.
Has no quarrel with the patron and no complaint to make, and is nonetheless recalculating.
Says the words about unity, and orders the study about alternatives.

## Action

Runs [Watch How They Treat Them](plan_watch_how_they_treat_them.md): through [Watching](process_watching.md) at [the Summit](place_the_summit.md), setting [the Watchers](piece_the_watchers.md).
Reads the patron's conduct toward another partner as the only evidence available about its own guarantee.
Prices that evidence into its own posture without ever announcing that it has.

## Shadow

Not disloyal and not opportunistic: it is reasoning correctly from the only evidence it has, since no patron has ever demonstrated a guarantee except by keeping one.
Because reputation is indivisible across the network, it converts a bilateral softening into a general doubt without anybody intending that, and without a single accusation being made.
It is the reason the patron cannot buy back its freedom quietly, and it is not doing anything to the patron at all.

## Tell

Signs the communique on unity and commissions the paper on independent capability in the same week, and both are sincere.
Cannot be reassured in private, because the thing it is reading is not what it is told but what it is shown.
