# Position: Ally

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). The seat whose most reasonable request is the one that cannot be granted.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A survival that depends on somebody else's decision, and no way to make that decision itself.
A guarantee written in language it did not draft, and a legislature that will ask what the language means.
Every option a doubting state has: arm, go forward, move early, or open a line to the other side.

## Orders

Get the answer while it can still be got: name the territory, name the contingency, name the forces, and get it in a document that can be quoted.
Read every softening as data, since a formula that names nothing reassures nobody and was not meant to.
Accept what the seat does: a state that did not press for the answer would be failing at the only task that finally matters.

## Loses

The reassurance it asked for, since the only answer specific enough to reassure it is the answer that prints the map of where nothing will meet it.
Because a doubting ally must act and every act available to it is prudent, its own prudence is what makes the patron's position worse, and it cannot be asked to stop being prudent.
It keeps a rational hedge and loses the guarantee the hedge was a substitute for.

## Drives

Toward the seat that asks the question any government in its position must ask,
pressing for specifics, reading the softening, doing the arithmetic, and beginning the programme,
so the most defensible request in the play is the one that sets the whole mechanism running.
