# Position: Partner

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). The seat that was not in the room and read the whole thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Its own guarantee, in similar language, from the same patron.
No quarrel with the patron, no accusation to make, and no evidence about its own guarantee except how another partner's was handled.
A study it can commission quietly and a communique it can sign sincerely.

## Orders

Watch how they treat them, because a patron has never demonstrated a guarantee except by keeping one.
Price what is shown rather than what is said, and adjust the national posture accordingly.
Accept what the seat does: this is correct reasoning from the only evidence in existence, and a state that ignored it would be negligent.

## Loses

The privacy of the patron's decision, which it destroys simply by being a competent observer, so the patron cannot buy its freedom back in one capital without paying for it in all of them.
Because reputation across the network is indivisible, the seat converts a bilateral softening into a general doubt while intending nothing of the kind and making no complaint at all.
It keeps its own prudence and loses the alliance's coherence, which was the thing its guarantee rested on.

## Drives

Toward the seat that does nothing and changes everything,
observing, inferring, signing the words about unity, and commissioning the paper about alternatives,
so a chain of reasoning no one can fault turns one softened clause into a network of hedges, with nobody having acted against anybody.
