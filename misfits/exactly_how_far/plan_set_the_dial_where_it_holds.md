# Plan: Set the Dial Where It Holds

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the patron's seat](position_patron.md), inside [Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Patron](position_patron.md)

## Direction

Find the setting that reassures the ally without surrendering the decision of when this country goes to war. Write the guarantee precise enough to be believed, because an alliance nobody believes in is not an alliance and a promise nobody can quote is not a promise. Then read what has been published, in the way the party it was written against will read it. Where the exposure is intolerable, soften the language back toward consultation, toward appropriate action, toward all means it deems necessary, and recover the choice. Do not pretend either setting is free. Refuse to sign away the choice of when to fight, because that would be reckless with the lives this seat is holding, and refuse to say so plainly, because saying so plainly is the abandonment signal in its purest form. Hold in view that the same words answer three readers who need opposite things from them, so no setting is right for more than one at a time, and that on the morning the crisis reads it there will be no adjustment left that anybody believes.

## Orders

([The Patron](persona_the_patron.md) reassures through [Reassuring](process_reassuring.md) at [the Treaty Text](place_the_treaty_text.md), setting [the Published Map](piece_the_published_map.md); hedges through [Hedging](process_hedging.md) at the same place, setting [the Restored Choice](piece_the_restored_choice.md); and is read through [Testing](process_testing.md) at [the Crisis](place_the_crisis.md), meeting [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md).)

## Implementation

([The Treaty Text](place_the_treaty_text.md) is where precision is decided and published in one act; [Reassuring](process_reassuring.md) buys belief with specificity, [Hedging](process_hedging.md) buys the choice back with the certainty it was made of, and [Testing](process_testing.md) reads whatever setting is standing.)

## Targets

- [ ] the guarantee is written specific enough that the ally can quote it
- [ ] what the wording covers and what it therefore does not are both read before it is published
- [ ] neither setting is presented as free, and the price of each is stated in the seat's own papers
- [ ] the choice of when to fight is not signed away, and the refusal is not announced
- [ ] it is held in view that the crisis reads the standing setting and permits no adjustment
