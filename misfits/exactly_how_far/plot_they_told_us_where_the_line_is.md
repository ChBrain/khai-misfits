# Plot: They Told Us Where the Line Is

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Adversary's Desk](place_the_adversarys_desk.md), with the treaty, the ratification debate and the announced postures all obtained from open sources.

## Action

Through [Reading](process_reading.md), [the Adversary](persona_the_adversary.md) on [Read What They Wrote](plan_read_what_they_wrote.md) takes [the Published Map](piece_the_published_map.md) as intelligence: two lists are drawn up, one of what is covered and one of what is not, and only the second is worked in. The omissions are treated as deliberate, because a guarantee naming everything would guarantee nothing and whoever drafted this had to leave something out. The scene closes with a move sited exactly where the words stop, and with the treaty citable, accurately, in the justification.

## Stage

Reading a public treaty for what it omits is the work an adversary exists to do, and a service that did not do it would be incompetent. Nothing was concealed, nothing was stolen, and nobody misunderstood anything.

## Tension

A firmer guarantee does not close this seat out but relocates it, since a line drawn anywhere has an outside, so the remedy the ally is pressing for improves the quality of the map rather than removing it.
