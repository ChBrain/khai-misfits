# Position: Patron

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). The seat that holds one dial and owes three answers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The commitment, and sole authority over how tightly it is written.
A duty to people who will do the fighting, and no desire to have their war chosen by a partner with a border dispute.
Two settings, both available, both public, and no third.

## Orders

Reassure the ally, because an alliance nobody believes in is not an alliance.
Keep the choice of when to fight, because handing it away is reckless with the lives the seat is holding.
Accept what the seat does: the same words answer three readers who need opposite things, so every setting is right for one of them.

## Loses

Either the confidence or the freedom, since precision buys the first with a published map and vagueness buys the second with the certainty the deterrent was made of.
Because its conduct toward one partner is the only evidence the others have about their own guarantee, it cannot even spend that choice privately, and a bilateral softening is priced in every capital at once.
It keeps a defensible position and loses the deterrent, which was never a capability but a belief in somebody else's head.

## Drives

Toward the seat that must set the dial before knowing what will read it,
writing the guarantee precise enough to reassure, seeing the map it printed, softening the language to get the choice back, and finding what the choice cost,
so an ordinary act of statecraft, explicable to any legislature in a sentence, is the act that arms the ally and sites the adversary's move.
