# Persona: The Patron

## Taxonomy

[Patron](position_patron.md). The one holding the dial, who must set it before knowing what will read it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful and unhurried, conscious that every word will be quoted in four capitals.
Will not hand the decision of when it goes to war to a smaller partner with a border dispute it did not choose.
Will not say so plainly either, because saying so plainly is the abandonment signal in its purest form.

## Action

Runs [Set the Dial Where It Holds](plan_set_the_dial_where_it_holds.md): through [Reassuring](process_reassuring.md) at [the Treaty Text](place_the_treaty_text.md), setting [the Published Map](piece_the_published_map.md); through [Hedging](process_hedging.md) at the same place, setting [the Restored Choice](piece_the_restored_choice.md); and through [Testing](process_testing.md) at [the Crisis](place_the_crisis.md), meeting [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md).
Writes the guarantee precise enough to reassure, then reads what it has published.
Softens the language to get the choice back, then discovers what the choice was bought from.

## Shadow

Not faithless and not weak: a state that signed away its choice of when to fight would be reckless with the lives it holds, and one that would not reassure at all would have no alliance to hold.
Because the same words answer three readers who need opposite things, its every setting of the dial is correct for one of them and wrong for the other two.
It cannot hedge privately, because its conduct toward one partner is the only evidence the rest have about their own guarantee.

## Tell

Produces language that a legislature will accept, an ally can quote, and an adversary can map, and cannot produce language that does only the first two.
Turns the dial once more in the crisis and finds that a promise improved under fire persuades nobody.
