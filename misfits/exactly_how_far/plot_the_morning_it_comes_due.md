# Plot: The Morning It Comes Due

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Crisis](place_the_crisis.md), with a move already made, a text already published, a partner already armed, and hours in which to decide what was set over years.

## Action

Through [Testing](process_testing.md), [the Patron](persona_the_patron.md) on [Set the Dial Where It Holds](plan_set_the_dial_where_it_holds.md) meets [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md): the dial is read once, at whatever value it happens to hold, and any adjustment offered now persuades nobody, because a promise improved under fire is not a promise but a reaction. And the limit is stated in the same breath, that the trap needs an ally whose security genuinely rests on the guarantee and an adversary reading it, and that entrapment is in practice far rarer than the theory predicts, because great powers routinely evade what they signed. The scene closes with the dilemma held as real and the size of its tighter horn held as disputed (Beckley).

## Stage

This is the only moment the setting matters and the only moment it cannot be changed. Everything anybody did to reach it was defensible on the day they did it, and the record will show that.

## Tension

The bound is what keeps the play a mechanism rather than an argument against alliances, and it arrives at the point of maximum cost, where a qualifying paragraph is the hardest thing in the room to hear.
