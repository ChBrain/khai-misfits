# Piece: The Watchers

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). The network: a patron's conduct toward one partner is the only evidence the rest have about their own guarantee.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Summit](place_the_summit.md), among delegations who make no complaint and have already recalculated (Christensen and Jack Snyder).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing each guarantee were read on its own, and the patron can soften one commitment quietly and pay for it only there, which would make the dilemma bilateral and manageable.
It bears the load as the reason the trap is general rather than local. Because no patron has ever demonstrated a guarantee except by keeping one, reputation across the network is indivisible, so a doubt raised in one capital is priced in all of them, and the alliance is chained to its weakest quarrel or comes apart in pieces, with nobody having acted against anybody.

## Apparent

Solidarity, and the solidarity is sincere: the communique is meant, the reaffirmations are real, and no delegation in the room is working against the patron.
