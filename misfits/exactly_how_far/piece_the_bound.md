# Piece: The Bound

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). The condition and the contested magnitude: what the trap requires, and how large its worse horn actually is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Crisis](place_the_crisis.md), stated at the moment the setting is finally read, so the scope is set where the cost is (Beckley).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play overreaches into a claim it cannot support, that alliances are unwise or that guarantees are generally hollow, neither of which the source literature says and neither of which the house will stage.
It bears the load as the limit that keeps this a structural dilemma rather than an indictment. Because the trap needs an ally whose security genuinely rests on the guarantee and an adversary reading it, it does not arise everywhere; and because great powers routinely evade the commitments they signed, entrapment is in practice far rarer than the theory predicts, so the dilemma is real and the size of its tighter horn is disputed.

## Apparent
