# Plan: Watch How They Treat Them

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the partner's seat](position_partner.md), inside [Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Partner](position_partner.md)

## Direction

Read the patron's conduct toward another partner as evidence about this one's guarantee, because it is the only evidence that exists: no patron has ever demonstrated a guarantee except by keeping one, and words about unity are available to every patron regardless of what it will do. Price what is shown rather than what is said. Make no accusation, raise no dispute, and sign the communique on unity sincerely, since none of that is inconsistent with recalculating. Commission the study on independent capability quietly and let its conclusions inform the national posture. Accept that this is correct reasoning from the only material in existence, and that a state which ignored the material would be negligent, while noting that the same reasoning conducted in every capital at once is what turns one softened clause into a network of hedges with nobody having acted against anybody.

- [ ] no accusation is made and the communique is signed sincerely
- [ ] the study on independent capability is commissioned without announcement
- [ ] it is noted that the same reasoning in every capital is what makes the doubt general
