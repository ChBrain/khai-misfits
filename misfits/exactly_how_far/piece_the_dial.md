# Piece: The Dial

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). The structure itself: commitment as one continuous setting, with abandonment at the loose end and entrapment at the tight one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Ally's Capital](place_the_allys_capital.md), where the setting is first read by the people whose survival it describes (Glenn Snyder).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing commitment were a switch rather than a setting, and the whole play collapses into a question of whether the patron is honest, which is a smaller thing and not a structure.
It bears the load as the reason no answer exists. Because both fears are well founded and both sit on one variable, every increment spent against one is taken from the other, so the seat holding it is not choosing between a good option and a bad one but between two costs, and cannot decline to choose, since not setting the dial leaves it set.

## Apparent

A judgement call, and it will be argued as one: how firm to be, a matter of nerve, of temperament, of how much one values the partner.

## Yearbook

Stands over the setup in [Exactly How Far Will You Go](plot_exactly_how_far_will_you_go.md), where the question is asked and turns out to have no safe answer.
It works together with [The Bound](piece_the_bound.md), the structure and the conditions under which it actually bites.
