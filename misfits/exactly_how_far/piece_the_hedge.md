# Piece: The Hedge

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). The ally's rational response: a doubting state arms, goes forward, moves early, or opens a line to the other side.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Ally's Capital](place_the_allys_capital.md), begun quietly and defended openly the moment anybody objects.

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing the ally sits still while the guarantee thins, and the loose end costs the guarantor nothing but the partner's disappointment, which no state has ever gone to war over.
It bears the load as the reason the loose end is expensive rather than merely disappointing. Because each option available to the ally is prudent from where it stands, the guarantor cannot object without conceding it should have closed the gap instead, so the ally's rational self-help is precisely what converts the patron's caution into a worse strategic position for the patron.

## Apparent

Ingratitude, and it will be read that way in the patron's press: the partner it protected, arming behind its back and talking to the other side.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Then We Will Need Our Own](plot_then_we_will_need_our_own.md), where prudence on one side is damage on the other.
It works together with [The Watchers](piece_the_watchers.md), one capital's arithmetic and the same arithmetic done everywhere at once.
