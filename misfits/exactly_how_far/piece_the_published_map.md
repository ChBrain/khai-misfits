# Piece: The Published Map

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). The cost of the tight end: a promise exact enough to reassure names the ground it covers, and so names the ground it does not.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Treaty Text](place_the_treaty_text.md) where it is written and at [The Adversary's Desk](place_the_adversarys_desk.md) where it is read as intelligence (Schelling; Glenn Snyder).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing a guarantee could be specific to one reader and opaque to another, and the tight end of the dial is simply correct and the ally should always be given what it asks for.
It bears the load as the reason precision is not the remedy. Because the document must be public to deter and public to reassure, the drafter cannot choose an audience, so the better it is written the sharper the boundary it draws, and the improvement the ally asked for is delivered intact to the party the treaty was written against.

## Apparent

Diplomatic craftsmanship, and it is that: a clause that a legislature will ratify, an ally can quote, and a court could construe.
