# Position: Adversary

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). The seat the document was not addressed to and is entirely about.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The published text, which was published in order to be read, and unlimited time to read it.
An interest in the ground the text does not name, and no interest at all in the ground it does.
A working assumption that serious people who had to leave something out left out what they meant to.

## Orders

Read what they wrote: list what is covered, list what is not, and operate only in the second list.
Take the omissions as the product, since a guarantee that named everything would guarantee nothing and its drafters knew that.
Accept what the seat does: this is the work an adversary exists to do, and one that did not do it would be incompetent.

## Loses

Nothing in this play, which is why the seat is here: it is the reader that converts the patron's care into the ally's exposure, and it pays no price for the conversion.
Because a line drawn anywhere has an outside, a firmer guarantee does not remove its opportunity but moves it, so the seat cannot be closed out by better drafting.
It keeps the whole benefit of somebody else's precision, having done nothing but read.

## Drives

Toward the seat that needs no deception and receives no secret,
taking a public document at its word, planning where the words stop, and citing them accurately afterwards,
so the guarantee's greatest virtue, that it is exact enough to be believed, is what makes it useful to the party it was written against.
