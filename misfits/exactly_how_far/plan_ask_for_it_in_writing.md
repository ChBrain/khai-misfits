# Plan: Ask for It in Writing

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the ally's seat](position_ally.md), inside [Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Ally](position_ally.md)

## Direction

Get the answer while it can still be got. Ask exactly how far: what territory, which contingencies, whose forces, on what timetable, and what happens on the morning the shooting starts. Insist on a document that can be quoted in a legislature rather than a communique that can be read three ways, since a formula naming no territory reassures nobody and was not drafted to. Concede nothing to the argument that ambiguity is useful, because the usefulness accrues to the other party. Then read every softening of the language as data, and when the data says the guarantee is thinning, act on it: begin the national programme, take the forward posture, consider moving while moving is possible, or open the quiet line. Do not apologise for any of that. A state whose survival rests on somebody else's decision and that did not press for the answer would be failing at the only task that finally matters.

## Orders

([The Ally](persona_the_ally.md) presses through [Reassuring](process_reassuring.md) at [the Ally's Capital](place_the_allys_capital.md), setting [the Dial](piece_the_dial.md), and hedges through [Arming](process_arming.md) at the same place, setting [the Hedge](piece_the_hedge.md).)

## Implementation

([The Ally's Capital](place_the_allys_capital.md) is where the guarantee is weighed by the people it is about; [Reassuring](process_reassuring.md) turns the request into a specific promise, and [Arming](process_arming.md) turns a thinning promise into a substitute for it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the question is put in the form that admits only a specific answer
- [ ] the territory, the contingency, the forces and the timetable are each named or each recorded as unnamed
- [ ] the promise obtained is one the legislature can quote, not one three readings can survive
- [ ] every softening of the language is read as data rather than as reassurance
- [ ] when the data says the guarantee is thinning, the substitute is begun without waiting to be released
