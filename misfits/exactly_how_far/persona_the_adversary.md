# Persona: The Adversary

## Taxonomy

[Adversary](position_adversary.md). The reader the document was not written for, and the one it is finally about.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Unhurried and literal, working from open sources because the open sources are the point.
Treats the guarantee as a serious document written by serious people, which is why the omissions are read as deliberate.
Claims no insight and needs none: everything it uses was published in order to be read.

## Action

Runs [Read What They Wrote](plan_read_what_they_wrote.md): through [Reading](process_reading.md) at [the Adversary's Desk](place_the_adversarys_desk.md), taking [the Published Map](piece_the_published_map.md) as intelligence.
Lists what is named, then lists what is not, and works only in the second list.
Plans a move that the guarantee, read exactly as written, does not cover.

## Shadow

Not a villain and not a schemer: reading a public treaty for what it omits is the work an adversary exists to do, and one that did not would be incompetent.
Because the precision that reassured the ally is the same precision that drew the line, the better the guarantee is written the better the map it hands over.
It needs nobody to have been deceived, since nothing here was concealed and nothing was misunderstood.

## Tell

Cites the treaty accurately when justifying the move, and the citation holds.
Cannot be caught out by a firmer guarantee, only relocated by it, since a line drawn anywhere is a line with an outside.
