# Piece: The Restored Choice

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). The cost of the loose end: ambiguity returns the guarantor's decision, and the decision is what the deterrent was made of.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Treaty Text](place_the_treaty_text.md), in the alternative phrasings in the margin: consultation, appropriate action, all means it deems necessary.

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing vagueness were merely weak rather than actively self-defeating, and the play resolves into an argument for firmer commitments, which is the advice the tight end has already refuted.
It bears the load as the mirror that closes the trap. Because a deterrent is not a capability but a belief in another party's head, the freedom recovered here is taken directly from the certainty that did the deterring, so the guarantor buys back its choice with the only thing that made the choice worth having.

## Apparent

Prudence, and it is prudence: a state declining to have its wars chosen by a smaller partner with a border dispute is behaving responsibly toward the people who would do the fighting.
