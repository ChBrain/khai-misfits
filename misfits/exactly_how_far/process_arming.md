# Process: Arming

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). Borne at [The Ally's Capital](place_the_allys_capital.md), precipitated by [The Ally](persona_the_ally.md) reading the softened language, in [Then We Will Need Our Own](plot_then_we_will_need_our_own.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A state that has done the arithmetic on a thinning guarantee and found the answer unacceptable.

## Direction

From reliance on somebody else's promise toward a substitute for it: a national programme, a forward posture, an early move, or a quiet line to the other side.

## Lever

That every one of those is prudent from where the ally stands, so the guarantor has no standing to object, having itself declined to close the gap the ally is filling.

## Echo

[The Hedge](piece_the_hedge.md), a rational response that worsens the position of the party whose caution produced it.
