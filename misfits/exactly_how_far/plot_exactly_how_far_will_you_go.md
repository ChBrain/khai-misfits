# Plot: Exactly How Far Will You Go

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ally's Capital](place_the_allys_capital.md), with a text on the table that the government did not draft and a legislature that will ask what it means.

## Action

Through [Reassuring](process_reassuring.md), [the Ally](persona_the_ally.md) on [Ask for It in Writing](plan_ask_for_it_in_writing.md) sets [the Dial](piece_the_dial.md): what territory, which contingencies, whose forces, on what timetable, and what happens on the morning the shooting starts. It will not accept a formula that names nothing, because a formula that names nothing reassures nobody and was not drafted to. The scene closes with the question established as one any government in this position is obliged to ask, and with the answer established as a setting rather than a switch, carrying abandonment at one end and entrapment at the other, both fears well founded (Glenn Snyder).

## Stage

Nothing here is grasping. A state whose continued existence depends on somebody else's decision, and which did not press for the terms of that decision, would be failing at the only task that finally matters.

## Tension

The most defensible request in the play is the one that starts the mechanism, because the answer specific enough to satisfy it is the answer that cannot be given safely, and the seat asking has no way to want less.
