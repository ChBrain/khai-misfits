# Plan: Read What They Wrote

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the adversary's seat](position_adversary.md), inside [Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Adversary](position_adversary.md)

## Direction

Work from the published text, because the published text was published in order to be read. Take it as a serious document written by serious people under legislative constraint, which is the reason its omissions are informative: a guarantee that named everything would guarantee nothing, so whoever drafted it had to leave something out and chose what. List what is named. List what is not. Then operate only in the second list. Require no deception, obtain no secret, and make no assumption that anybody misunderstood anything, since nothing here was concealed. Plan the move where the words stop, and be able to cite the treaty accurately in justifying it afterwards. Expect a firmer guarantee to relocate the opportunity rather than remove it, because a line drawn anywhere has an outside.
