# Process: Watching

## Taxonomy

[Exactly How Far](play_exactly_how_far.md). Borne at [The Summit](place_the_summit.md), precipitated by [The Partner](persona_the_partner.md) observing how another partner was handled, in [We Are Watching How You Treat Them](plot_we_are_watching_how_you_treat_them.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A state holding a similar guarantee from the same patron, and no evidence about it except somebody else's case.

## Direction

From a bilateral softening toward a general doubt, as each capital prices the same conduct and adjusts its own posture without announcing that it has.

## Lever

That no patron has ever demonstrated a guarantee except by keeping one, so conduct is the only evidence and words about unity are available to every patron regardless (Christensen and Jack Snyder).

## Echo

[The Watchers](piece_the_watchers.md), reputation indivisible across the network, and a hedge that cannot be spent in one capital without being charged in all of them.
