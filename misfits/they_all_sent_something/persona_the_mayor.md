# Persona: The Mayor

## Taxonomy

[Mayor](position_mayor.md). The one who must accept it and be seen to be grateful.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A local official with a destroyed town, a runway, and shipments already inbound from people who meant well.

## Action

Accepts what arrives.
Thanks the senders publicly, finds somewhere to put it, and pays to dispose of what cannot be used.

## Shadow

Holds a refusal that is available on paper and not in the world.
The seat could in principle decline consignments, and the cost of doing so falls immediately and entirely on it: senders who spent their own money are told their gift was unwanted, the refusal is reported, and a town that has just been destroyed acquires a reputation for ingratitude at the moment it most needs sympathy and support. Meanwhile the shipment is already on the tarmac and refusing it does not make it leave. So the seat accepts, which is the only move available, and the acceptance is what commits the handling capacity.

## Tell

Says thank you, and pays for the disposal.
