# Piece: The Five Per Cent

## Place

## Load Bearing

The useful part is real and present, and its proportion is what makes it expensive to reach.
Of what arrives at a large disaster, roughly three-fifths is non-priority and should not have been sent, a further third is low-priority and belongs in storage for later, and something between a twentieth and a tenth is what was needed on the day it landed. That last fraction is not missing and is not the wrong thing: it is genuinely there, and it is dispersed through a volume many times its size, in cartons nobody labelled because nobody was coordinating. So the response's problem is not scarcity, which is legible and can be appealed against, but a composition that has to be undone before anything in it can be used.
