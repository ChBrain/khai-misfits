# Persona: The Broadcaster

## Taxonomy

[Broadcaster](position_broadcaster.md). The one who calls for help and cannot narrow the call.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An agency or a newsroom issuing an appeal within hours of an event nobody has assessed yet.

## Action

Announces the disaster and asks for support.
Says what is known, which is that a place has been destroyed and people need help.

## Shadow

Must appeal before anybody knows what is needed, and the appeal is what summons the wrong things.
The seat is not being vague by choice: a needs assessment takes days and the response has to begin in hours, so the only honest appeal at the time it must be made is a general one. Naming specific items would be guessing, and the guess would be wrong and would then be shipped in bulk. Waiting for the assessment means the first week has no response in it. So the seat issues the appeal it can defend, and a general appeal is answered generally, by everybody, with whatever they have.

## Tell

Asks correctly, before the answer exists.
