# Persona: The Logistician

## Taxonomy

[Logistician](position_logistician.md). The one who must open everything to find anything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A coordinator at the receiving end with one runway, a warehouse, a forklift, a handful of trucks and a queue outside.

## Action

Receives, opens, identifies, sorts, stores or discards.
Finds the priority items inside the rest, and moves them out.

## Shadow

Performs the bottleneck and cannot perform anything else instead.
The seat is not slow and is not disorganised: sorting is not an overhead laid on top of the work, it is the only route to the work, because the priority items are physically inside the pile and cannot be identified without opening it. The same trucks, staff and hours do both jobs, so every carton sorted is a delivery not made and every delivery made is a carton still sealed. There is no version of this seat that reaches the affected population faster while an unsorted mountain stands between them.

## Tell

Works flat out at the thing that is in the way.
