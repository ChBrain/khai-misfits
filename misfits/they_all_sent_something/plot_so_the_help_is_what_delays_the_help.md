# Plot: So the Help Is What Delays the Help

## Taxonomy

[They All Sent Something](play_they_all_sent_something.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A queue outside a warehouse that is full.

## Action

[The Affected Person](persona_the_affected_person.md) waits for five things, two hundred metres from everything.

## Stage

A response that obstructed itself, with nobody having erred.

## Tension

Every seat performed correctly and the outcome is the sum of the correct performances.
The donor gave, at their own cost, in the only form the impulse comes in, having probably heard the argument against it. The broadcaster issued the only appeal defensible before an assessment. The convergence followed from the announcement, with nobody coordinating and nobody able to. The logistician is working flat out at the one task that leads to every other. The mayor accepted because refusing costs a destroyed town its goodwill and does not move the pallets. And the person it was all for waits, not because anything is scarce but because the abundance has the wrong composition and must be undone before it can be used.
