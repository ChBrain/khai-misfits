# Play: They All Sent Something

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

They All Sent Something.
A disaster is announced and people give, in enormous numbers, immediately, and toward exactly the right place. What they give is largely things: clothes, food, water, toys, medicines. Every carton has to be received, opened, identified, sorted, stored or discarded before any of it can reach anybody, and the people and vehicles doing that sorting are the people and vehicles that would otherwise be distributing. The great majority of what arrives was never needed.

## Arc

The bend is this: aid arrives in proportion to how much people care, and the capacity to sort it does not, so the caring is what buries the response.
It begins with a response nobody would want less of.
[A Disaster Is Announced and People Give](plot_a_disaster_is_announced_and_people_give.md). [The Donor](persona_the_donor.md) acts within hours, at their own cost, toward strangers.
And what they give is things.
[And What They Give Is Things, Not Money](plot_and_what_they_give_is_things_not_money.md). [The Tangible Gift](piece_the_tangible_gift.md) is what makes the giving happen, and it is what makes it unusable.
So it all arrives at once.
[So It All Arrives at Once, at the One Airstrip](plot_so_it_all_arrives_at_once_at_the_one_airstrip.md). [The Airstrip](place_the_airstrip.md) is one runway, and every consignment in the world is coming to it.
And [everything must be opened before anything can be used](plot_and_everything_must_be_opened_before_anything_can_be_used.md).
[The Handling Capacity](piece_the_handling_capacity.md) is finite and is the same capacity that distributes, so sorting and delivering compete for one forklift.
Then [nobody can refuse it](plot_nobody_can_refuse_it.md). [The Mayor](persona_the_mayor.md) must accept what arrives and be seen to be grateful for it.
And [the help is what delays the help](plot_so_the_help_is_what_delays_the_help.md). [The Affected Person](persona_the_affected_person.md) waits, inside a warehouse full of gifts.

## Triggers

**[A Disaster Is Announced and People Give](plot_a_disaster_is_announced_and_people_give.md)**
Cue: a broadcast, and millions of people who want to do something today. Closes when the response is established as generous, immediate and correctly aimed.

**[And What They Give Is Things, Not Money](plot_and_what_they_give_is_things_not_money.md)**
Cue: a box being packed in a garage. Closes when the tangibility is shown to be what produces the gift rather than a misunderstanding about what helps.

**[So It All Arrives at Once, at the One Airstrip](plot_so_it_all_arrives_at_once_at_the_one_airstrip.md)**
Cue: a runway, a port, and consignments converging from everywhere. Closes when the simultaneity is shown to follow from the announcement rather than from any failure of scheduling.

**[And Everything Must Be Opened Before Anything Can Be Used](plot_and_everything_must_be_opened_before_anything_can_be_used.md)**
Cue: an unlabelled pallet. Closes when sorting and distributing are found to draw on one capacity.

**[Nobody Can Refuse It](plot_nobody_can_refuse_it.md)**
Cue: a shipment already on the tarmac, sent by people who meant well. Closes when refusal is found to be unavailable in practice rather than merely unattractive.

**[So the Help Is What Delays the Help](plot_so_the_help_is_what_delays_the_help.md)**
Cue: a queue outside a warehouse that is full. Closes when the response is found to have obstructed itself with nobody having erred.

## Stakes

Whether a place that has just been destroyed can be reached by the people trying to reach it.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a response that is generous and immediate, a gift whose tangibility is the reason it exists, a convergence that follows from the announcement, a sorting burden that consumes the distribution capacity, an acceptance that cannot be declined, and an affected person waiting inside a full warehouse, and away from any account in which donors were foolish or responders were incompetent. The production cannot fault the donor, whose giving is real, is aimed correctly, and is produced by exactly the impulse that makes it the wrong shape; and it cannot fault the logistician, whose sorting is the bottleneck and is also the only way to find what was needed. What is left to fight over is not whether anybody erred but what to call a response that arrives in the quantity people care and is handled at the rate one runway allows.
