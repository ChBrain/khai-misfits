## Withheld

The place is where the arithmetic of the response is settled. Of what arrives at a large disaster, roughly three-fifths is non-priority and should not have been sent, another third is low-priority and belongs in storage for later, and something between a twentieth and a tenth is what was actually needed on the day it landed. Those proportions are the whole difficulty: the useful fraction is real and is present, and it is distributed through a volume many times its size, and the only instrument for extracting it is the same people and vehicles that would be delivering it.
