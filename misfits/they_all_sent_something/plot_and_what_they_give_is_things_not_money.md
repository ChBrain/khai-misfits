# Plot: And What They Give Is Things, Not Money

## Taxonomy

[They All Sent Something](play_they_all_sent_something.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A box being packed in a garage.

## Action

[The Tangible Gift](piece_the_tangible_gift.md) is chosen: clothes, food, water, medicine, toys, and the carriage paid.

## Stage

A form that is not a misunderstanding but a condition of the gift.

## Tension

The argument against the form turns out to be an argument against the motive.
Relief professionals have asked for money instead in every large disaster for decades, and the goods keep coming, which means the explanation cannot be that donors have not heard. What moves somebody to act within hours at their own cost is the prospect of a definite thing reaching a definite person, and a transfer is neither definite nor visibly theirs once made. Some of the hesitation about cash is well founded too, since a donor cannot verify that it arrives. Remove the tangibility and a large part of the giving goes with it.
