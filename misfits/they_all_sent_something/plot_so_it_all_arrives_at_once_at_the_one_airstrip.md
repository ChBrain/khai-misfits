# Plot: So It All Arrives at Once, at the One Airstrip

## Taxonomy

[They All Sent Something](play_they_all_sent_something.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A runway, a port, and consignments converging from everywhere.

## Action

At [the airstrip](place_the_airstrip.md), everything lands in the same few days, faster than anything leaves.

## Stage

A convergence that follows from the announcement rather than from any scheduling failure.

## Tension

Nobody is coordinating and nobody could be, which is why the timing is not anybody's fault.
A disaster is announced once, to everybody, and everybody who responds does so within the same short window, because that is what responding to an announcement means. There is no register of who is sending what, no authority the senders are subject to, and no mechanism by which a person packing a box could learn what has already been packed. Meanwhile the receiving capacity is smaller than usual rather than larger, because the disaster removed some of it, and the runway is one because the other is under water.
