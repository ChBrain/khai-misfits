# Plot: And Everything Must Be Opened Before Anything Can Be Used

## Taxonomy

[They All Sent Something](play_they_all_sent_something.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An unlabelled pallet.

## Action

[The Logistician](persona_the_logistician.md) begins sorting, because [the five per cent](piece_the_five_per_cent.md) is inside and cannot be identified any other way.

## Stage

One capacity doing two jobs, at the expense of the second.

## Tension

Sorting is not an overhead on the work: it is the only route to it.
Roughly three-fifths of what arrives at a large disaster is non-priority and should not have been sent, a further third belongs in storage for later, and between a twentieth and a tenth is what was needed on the day. That fraction is genuinely present and is dispersed through a volume many times its size, in cartons nobody labelled because nobody was coordinating. The same people, trucks and hours do the sorting and the delivering, so every carton opened is a delivery not made, and deferring the sorting is not available because perishables set the clock.
