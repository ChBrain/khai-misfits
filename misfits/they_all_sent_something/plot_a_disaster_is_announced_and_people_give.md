# Plot: A Disaster Is Announced and People Give

## Taxonomy

[They All Sent Something](play_they_all_sent_something.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A broadcast, and millions of people who want to do something today.

## Action

[The Broadcaster](persona_the_broadcaster.md) issues the appeal it can defend, and [the donor](persona_the_donor.md) responds within hours.

## Stage

A response that is generous, immediate and correctly aimed.

## Tension

This is the part nobody would want less of, and it is where the mechanism starts.
The giving is real: people spend their own money, on their own initiative, for strangers they will never meet, at the moment it is needed and toward the place that needs it. Nothing in this play is an argument that they should give less or care less. The appeal that summons them is also correct, and could not be narrower: a needs assessment takes days and the response has to begin in hours, so naming particular items would be guessing, and the guess would be shipped in bulk.
