# Piece: The Handling Capacity

## Taxonomy

[The Warehouse](place_the_warehouse.md). Finite, and the same capacity that would distribute.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Committed at [the airstrip](place_the_airstrip.md), and consumed by [sorting what came](process_sorting_what_came.md).

## Load Bearing

Sorting and delivering are not two jobs competing for attention: they are one set of people, trucks and hours doing either.
A disaster removes capacity by definition, so the receiving end has one usable runway, a warehouse, a forklift and a handful of vehicles. Every carton opened is a delivery not made, and every delivery made leaves a carton sealed. There is no arrangement in which the sorting happens elsewhere, because the goods are already here, and none in which it happens later, because perishables set the clock and the queue is outside now. The capacity is the scarce thing, and the response consumes it before it can use it.

## Apparent

A staffing problem, which more volunteers would relieve.

## Yearbook

Is the bottleneck the aid arrives into, and the aid is what fills it.
