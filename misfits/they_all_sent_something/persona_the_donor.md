# Persona: The Donor

## Taxonomy

[Donor](position_donor.md). The one who gives immediately, generously, and in kind.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Somebody watching a broadcast, with a car, a spare afternoon, and things in a cupboard.

## Action

Packs a box and sends it.
Gives clothes, food, water, medicine, toys, at their own cost, toward strangers, within hours.

## Shadow

Gives what makes giving possible, and it is the wrong shape.
The seat is not foolish and is not ignorant: the appeal to send money instead is broadcast in every disaster and has been for decades, and this seat has usually heard it. What moves somebody to act at all is the wish to do a definite thing for a definite person, and money is neither definite nor visibly theirs once given. Some of the seat's reluctance is also well founded, since it cannot verify that cash reaches anybody and has read about the cases where it did not. So the impulse that produces the gift is the same impulse that determines its form, and removing the second removes the first.

## Tell

Does the generous thing, in the only form the generosity comes in.
