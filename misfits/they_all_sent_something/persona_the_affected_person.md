# Persona: The Affected Person

## Taxonomy

[Affected Person](position_affected_person.md). The one waiting inside a warehouse full of gifts.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Somebody whose house is gone, in a queue, needing water and a tarpaulin today.

## Action

Waits.
Queues, asks, and waits again, while a mountain of goods sits two hundred metres away.

## Shadow

Is surrounded by help and cannot be reached by it.
The seat is the reason every other seat acted and is the last to be reached by any of it. What it needs is a short list and is genuinely present somewhere in the pile, and finding it means going through everything else first. Nothing the seat can do affects the order: it cannot direct the sorting, cannot tell anybody which carton to open, and cannot decline the gifts on its own behalf. The delay is not caused by scarcity, which would at least be legible, but by abundance of the wrong composition.

## Tell

Needs five things and is sent everything.
