# Plot: Nobody Can Refuse It

## Taxonomy

[They All Sent Something](play_they_all_sent_something.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A shipment already on the tarmac, sent by people who meant well.

## Action

[The Mayor](persona_the_mayor.md) accepts it, thanks the senders publicly, and finds somewhere to put it.

## Stage

A refusal that exists on paper and not in the world.

## Tension

The cost of declining falls entirely on the party least able to bear it.
A town that has just been destroyed and is asking for sympathy, volunteers and long-term support cannot afford to be reported as having turned away gifts. Senders who spent their own money would be told their generosity was unwanted, and the next appeal would be answered by fewer of them. And the shipment is on the tarmac in any case, so refusal does not make it leave: it makes it somebody's problem in a worse location. So acceptance is the only available move, and acceptance is what commits the handling capacity.
