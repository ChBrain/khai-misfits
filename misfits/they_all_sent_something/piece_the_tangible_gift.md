## Load Bearing

Its form is not a misunderstanding to be corrected: it is the condition on which the gift exists at all.
Relief professionals have asked for money rather than goods in every large disaster for decades, and the goods keep coming. The reason is not that donors have not heard. What moves somebody to act within hours, at their own cost, for strangers, is the prospect of a definite thing reaching a definite person, and a bank transfer is neither definite nor visibly theirs once made. Part of the reluctance is also well founded, since a donor cannot verify that cash arrives and has read about occasions when it did not. So the argument against the form is an argument against the motive.
