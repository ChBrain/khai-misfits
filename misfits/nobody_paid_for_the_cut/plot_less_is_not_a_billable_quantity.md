# Plot: Less Is Not a Billable Quantity

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The piece filed, and the editor's marks coming back on it.
Two hundred words that should not be there, correctly identified.

## Action

[Cutting](process_cutting.md): [The Editor](persona_the_editor.md), holding [the shaper's seat](position_shaper.md), requires the piece tighter, and [The Writer](persona_the_writer.md) removes the words, and the invoice falls by the amount removed.

## Stage

[The Editor](persona_the_editor.md), on [Make It Tight Anyway](plan_make_it_tight_anyway.md), asks for exactly the right thing, which is what editors are for and is correct every time it is exercised.
[The Writer](persona_the_writer.md) makes the cut, because they are a professional and the note is good, and funds it, because the invoice is written against what ran.

## Tension

The best available improvement is the only act the arrangement charges for.
Verification pays nothing, which is bad enough. **Cutting pays less than nothing.** So the arrangement does not merely neglect the thing that most reliably improves a piece of writing, it prices it below zero, and the person requesting it has authority over the copy and none whatever over the card. The better the editor, the larger the unbilled transfer they are unknowingly requesting, and there is no version of either of them doing their job well that also settles it.
