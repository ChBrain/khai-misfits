# Persona: The Writer

## Taxonomy

[Filer](position_filer.md). Has a finite day and a per-unit rate, and allocates hours the way anybody paid per unit allocates them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, quick, and doing correct arithmetic.
Knows what a good piece is, has written many, and is not confused about the difference between four hundred words and four hundred good words.
No cynicism in it: the rate is the rate, the day is the day, and rent is due.

## Action

Takes the commission at the agreed rate and files at the length the rate makes sense at.
Spends hours where hours produce units, because that is what the agreement pays for and there is no other agreement.
Cuts when asked, and absorbs the cut, because the invoice is written against what ran.

## Shadow

Not lazy, and not free to be otherwise: the parts of the job that would most improve it are the parts that produce no units, so doing them is a donation out of a fee that was not set to include one.
So the writer capable of the better piece and the writer who files this one are the same person under the same contract, and the difference between the two is unpaid hours nobody agreed to buy.
Any shortfall reads afterwards as their standard, and the rate that set the allocation is not in the byline.

## Tell

Says: what length do you want it.
Which is the only question the agreement gives them a way to ask, and answers itself the moment the rate is named.
