# Process: Cutting

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). Tightening the piece, which improves it reliably and produces a negative quantity of the unit being paid for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An editor's correct request.
At [the wire](place_the_wire.md), two hundred words removed and the piece better for it.

## Direction

Toward a shorter, stronger piece and a smaller invoice.
This is the sharpest term in the production: verification pays zero, which is bad, and cutting pays **less than zero**, so the single most reliable improvement available to any piece of writing is the only act in the arrangement the writer is charged for performing.
Nothing about that is anybody's fault. It falls directly out of pricing a job by the unit of its own bulk.

## Lever

The editorial requirement, which is legitimate and is met.
Because the writer is a professional and the request is right, the cut happens, and because the invoice is written against what ran, the writer funds it.

## Echo

A better piece and a lower fee, on the same afternoon.
Read from [the rate card](place_the_rate_card.md) the transaction is a reduction in delivered units; read from the page it is the best thing that happened to the piece.
