# Position: Payer

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). The seat that can only contract on terms a party outside the room could verify, and knows it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A budget, and the authority to set a rate.
Full awareness that the countable unit is not the thing they want.
No capacity to supervise the work, and therefore no enforceable term for anything that leaves no artefact.

## Orders

Name a countable unit and a rate against it.
Pay in full for every unit delivered.
Decline to contract on what cannot be checked, because a term neither party can enforce is not a term.

## Loses

The thing it actually wanted, in exchange for the thing it could write down: the contract buys the countable half and is silent on the rest, and silence in an agreement is not neutrality, it is an allocation.
Because carefulness leaves no artefact verifiable at a distance, there is no rate card that reaches it, and a better publisher writes the same card.
It ends having bought exactly what it specified and wanted something else.

## Drives

Toward an agreement that can be enforced,
right in every single instance to require a term that can be checked,
so the seat that most wants the better piece is the one whose only instrument cannot name it.
