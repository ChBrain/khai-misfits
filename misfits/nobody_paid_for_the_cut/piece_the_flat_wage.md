# Piece: The Flat Wage

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). The theory's own answer, a weak or absent incentive on the measurable dimension, which costs the gain that counting really does buy.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Off the rate card entirely, in the staff job the arrangement replaced.
Answered by nobody in the production, since it is not a term either party can reach from inside a commission.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play has no remedy at all and reads as a complaint.
It is load bearing because the remedy exists, is precise, and is unwanted: where dimensions differ in measurability the optimal incentive on the measurable ones is weaker and can be flat, which means pay a wage and stop counting, and that is the arrangement that has been disappearing for thirty years.

## Apparent

Softness, or a failure of nerve, or a refusal to reward performance.
It reads as the absence of good management rather than as the output of a formal result, which is why it is argued about in the register of attitude rather than of design.

## Yearbook

The limit of [the intensity](piece_the_intensity.md) taken to zero.
It buys back [the rest of the job](piece_the_rest_of_the_job.md), it forgoes the productivity that piece rates genuinely deliver (Lazear), and it requires the supervision [the publisher](persona_the_publisher.md) does not have, which is where the whole arrangement started.
