# Process: Allocating

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). The writer dividing a finite day across the parts of the job, correctly, in the proportions the rate implies.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A commission at a per-unit rate, and a day with a floor under it.
At [the rate card](place_the_rate_card.md)'s terms, the hours apportioned.

## Direction

Toward the priced dimension, in proportion to how strongly it is priced.
An hour writing produces four hundred words; an hour on [the phone](place_the_phone.md) produces none; an hour cutting produces a negative number. The ordering of those returns is the ordering of the day, and no step in that reasoning is an error.
This is the mechanism entire: not a lapse, not a temptation resisted or not, but arithmetic performed correctly against the only numbers available.

## Lever

[The fixed day](piece_the_fixed_day.md), which is the binding constraint and is not adjustable.
Because the residue is finite, every unbilled improvement is a transfer out of the writer's own fee, and the size of the transfer they can afford is set by how much they need the rent.

## Echo

A piece of the length the rate implied.
From outside it reads as a professional judgement about how much the subject warranted, and it is a fact about a number on a card.
