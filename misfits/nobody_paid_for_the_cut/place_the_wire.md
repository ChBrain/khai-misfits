# Place: The Wire

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). Where copy lands, is measured, and is paid against, and where the filed piece and the better piece look identical.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An inbox, a queue, a content management system: pieces arriving with a word count attached.
Professional copy, correctly filed, on time.

## Holds

The delivered work, and the impossibility of reading the arrangement off it.
What separates the piece that was filed from the one that would have been filed is a call that left no mark, so the wire carries the loss in a form that cannot be inspected.

## Offers

An exact measurement of the contracted dimension, instantly and without dispute.
The count is right, is agreed, and is available the moment the copy arrives, which is precisely what made it the term in the first place.

## Withheld

Any measurement of the rest.
The wire can weigh a piece and cannot weigh whether it is true, so the number it produces is both perfectly accurate and silent on everything the number was standing next to.
