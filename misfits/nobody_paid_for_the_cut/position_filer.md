# Position: Filer

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). The seat that converts a finite day into billable units, correctly, and funds everything else out of what is left.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A day with a fixed number of hours in it.
A rate per countable unit, agreed and honoured.
Full professional knowledge of what would make the piece better, and no term in the agreement that pays for any of it.

## Orders

Take the commission at the rate.
Spend hours where hours produce units.
File at the length the rate makes sense at, and cut when asked.

## Loses

The unbilled half of the job, every time: the second call, the paragraph removed, the hour spent establishing that a claim will not stand up.
Because the day is finite and only one dimension is priced, doing the rest is a donation out of a fee that was never set to include it, and the seat cannot both make that donation and make rent.
It ends with an accurate invoice and a piece it knows is not the piece.

## Drives

Toward units filed against a rate,
right in every single instance to spend the hour where the hour pays,
so the seat that knows best what the piece needs is the seat least able to afford to give it.
