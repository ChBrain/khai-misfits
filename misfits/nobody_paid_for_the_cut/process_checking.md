# Process: Checking

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). Confirming the figure with the source, which costs four minutes, improves accuracy, and produces nothing billable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A number in the copy that is close and might be wrong.
At [the phone](place_the_phone.md), a willing source and an unmade call.

## Direction

Toward accuracy, at zero return.
The call is cheap, the source is willing, and the improvement is real, and none of it produces a unit, so under [allocating](process_allocating.md) it sits last and is reached only if the day has slack, which a per-unit day does not.
And the omission is untraceable: an unmade call leaves no artefact, so no later audit distinguishes the piece that skipped it from one that did not.

## Lever

Four minutes, which is the whole cost, against a day that has already been fully priced.
Because the constraint is the hour rather than the willingness, no amount of source goodwill closes it.

## Echo

A figure that runs close and wrong, indefinitely.
It is the cheapest improvement in the play, it is not bought, and its absence is the one loss here that cannot be recovered even in principle after the fact.
