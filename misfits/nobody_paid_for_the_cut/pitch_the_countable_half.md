# Pitch: The Countable Half

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry, transactional, itemised.
The register of a rate card and a filed invoice: units at a rate, a total, a payment date.
Nothing raised anywhere in it, because there is nothing to raise: every figure on the page is correct and every party agrees it is.

## Undertow

Under the invoice, the half of the job that has no line on it.
The second call to the source, the paragraph taken out, the hour spent deciding a claim will not stand up: all of it done or not done inside the same finite day, none of it appearing in any column, and none of it therefore free.
The countable half and the rest are not two jobs. They are one job, of which a contract can name only the part a stranger could check.

## Nerve

The unease of watching an entirely honest rate produce an entirely predictable piece,
every party doing the arithmetic correctly and none of them wrong about anything,
and the best single revision available being the one the writer pays for out of their own fee.

## Echo

After the run: an invoice that is accurate, a piece that is longer than it should be and thinner than it looks,
a source who was not rung back because ringing back pays nothing,
and a rate card that never once mentioned any of it, because a rate card can only mention what can be counted from somewhere else.
