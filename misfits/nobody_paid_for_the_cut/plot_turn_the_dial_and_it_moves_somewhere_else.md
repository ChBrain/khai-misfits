# Plot: Turn the Dial and It Moves Somewhere Else

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A randomised trial at a Kenyan news outlet, writers assigned to a piece rate or to pay per click.
[The Intensity](piece_the_intensity.md), pointed at a different dimension, deliberately, under measurement.

## Action

[Rating](process_rating.md) run as an experiment: the contracted unit changed from the article to the pageview, and every downstream quantity measured against a control arm that kept the piece rate.

## Stage

[The Publisher](persona_the_publisher.md)'s seat points the contract at attention instead of at volume, which is a reasonable thing to try and is what much of the industry did.
[The Writer](persona_the_writer.md)'s seat re-allocates, correctly, again: pageviews per article triple, published articles fall by nearly three quarters, and the copy moves off local news and onto national politics.

## Tension

The loss relocated. It did not lift.
This is the plot that stops the misfit being about quantity. Under the piece rate the writers produced volume and thin sourcing; under pay per click they produced fewer, sharper, more national pieces and stopped covering the town. Nobody became lazier and nothing was gamed: **effort went where the contract pointed, which is all it has ever done.** So the law is not that per-unit pay buys quantity instead of quality. It is that a contract buys the dimension it can name, entirely, and the dimensions it cannot name are funded out of what is left of somebody's day. Turn the dial and you choose which ones those are. The one setting that does not choose is [the flat wage](piece_the_flat_wage.md), which the theory recommends, which costs the productivity that counting genuinely buys, and which is the arrangement that has been going away.
