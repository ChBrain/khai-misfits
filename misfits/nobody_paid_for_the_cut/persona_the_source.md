# Persona: The Source

## Taxonomy

[Uncalled](position_uncalled.md). The second call that would have confirmed the figure, not made, because making it produces nothing billable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Reachable, willing, and not contacted.
Would have taken the call, would have corrected the number, and has no idea any of this is happening.
Nothing withheld: there is simply no call.

## Action

Waits, without knowing they are waiting.
Is not rung, because an hour on the phone produces zero units and the day has a floor under it.
Reads the piece later, if at all, and sees a figure that is close and wrong.

## Shadow

Not unavailable, and absent from the account entirely: the check that did not happen leaves no trace, so the difference between the piece that was filed and the piece that would have been filed is invisible to every party including the writer.
So the cost lands on accuracy, which nobody measured, rather than on effort, which everybody did.
Their non-appearance is the clearest thing in the play and appears in no column of it.

## Tell

Says nothing, having been asked nothing.
Is the whole of what the rate card did not buy, and is not mentioned on it.
