# Place: The Phone

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). Where the check that produces no units would have happened, and where the play's cheapest available improvement sits unbought.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A number in a notebook, and four minutes.
A source who would answer, would confirm or correct the figure, and would think nothing of it.

## Holds

The cheapest improvement available anywhere in the production, and the one nothing is pointed at.
It also holds the asymmetry that makes the loss permanent: a call not made leaves no record, so its absence cannot be found later by anybody auditing anything.

## Offers

Accuracy, for four minutes, from a willing party at no cost to anybody but the clock.
Nothing is scarce here except the hour, and the hour is the only thing the rate card priced.

## Withheld

Any unit at all.
The call produces nothing that can be counted, invoiced or verified from outside, so under a per-unit agreement it is the first thing to go and the last thing anybody would notice going.
