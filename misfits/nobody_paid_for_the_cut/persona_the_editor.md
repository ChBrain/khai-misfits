# Persona: The Editor

## Taxonomy

[Shaper](position_shaper.md). Asks for it tighter and better sourced, which is right and is what editors are for, and holds no budget line for either.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sharp, experienced, and entirely on the reader's side.
Reads a piece and sees at once what it would be two hundred words shorter and one phone call firmer.
Nothing adversarial in it: the request is the job, and the writer knows it is.

## Action

Reads, marks, and asks for the cut and the check.
Gets both, usually, because the writer is a professional and does them.
Commissions the next piece at the same rate, because the rate card is not theirs and the budget was set elsewhere.

## Shadow

Not demanding, and asking for what the arrangement charges somebody else for: every improvement they request is real, is correct, and is payable only out of the writer's own fee.
So the editorial function and the payment function point opposite ways, and the editor has authority over the first and none over the second.
The better they are at their job, the larger the unpaid transfer they are unknowingly requesting.

## Tell

Says: can we lose two hundred words and can you stand that figure up.
Both requests are right. Neither has a line on the rate card.
