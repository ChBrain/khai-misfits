# Plan: File What the Rate Counts

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the filer's seat](position_filer.md), inside [Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Filer](position_filer.md)

## Direction

Take the commission, spend the day's hours where hours produce billable units, and file at the length the rate makes sense at. Do the unbilled parts to the extent the fee can absorb them, which is not to the extent the piece deserves.

## Orders

([The Writer](persona_the_writer.md) allocates a finite day across the parts of the job, correctly, and the allocation follows the rate because the rate is the only term with a number against it.)

## Implementation

([The Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md), setting what a unit is; the [allocating](process_allocating.md) process running against [the fixed day](piece_the_fixed_day.md); [filing](process_filing.md) producing [the countable](piece_the_countable.md) while [the rest of the job](piece_the_rest_of_the_job.md) is funded from the residue.)

## Targets

- [ ] the commission is accepted at a rate both parties agree is fair
- [ ] hours are spent where hours produce units, which is correct arithmetic
- [ ] the piece is filed at the length the rate makes sense at
- [ ] the unbilled parts are done to the extent the fee absorbs them, and no further
