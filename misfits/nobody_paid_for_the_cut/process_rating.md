# Process: Rating

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). Choosing which dimension of the job the agreement will name, which is decided by verifiability and not by importance.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A commission to a party nobody can supervise.
At [the rate card](place_the_rate_card.md), a unit selected and a price set against it.

## Direction

Toward a term both sides can enforce.
The selection runs on one question, whether a third party could count it without being present, and that question is unrelated to how much the dimension matters: word count scores perfectly and matters little, carefulness scores zero and matters most.
So the naming is decided by a property that has nothing to do with value, and the result is not a compromise but a filter.

## Lever

Enforceability, which is not optional.
Because an unenforceable term is not a term, the filter cannot be relaxed by goodwill, and a publisher who cared twice as much would write the same card.

## Echo

An agreement that is honest, plain and complete on its face.
Nothing in it is hidden, and everything it does not mention is thereby assigned to whoever has hours left.
