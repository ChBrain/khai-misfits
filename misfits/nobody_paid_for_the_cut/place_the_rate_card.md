# Place: The Rate Card

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). The schedule of what is paid for and at what rate, which can only list things a party outside the room could count.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A short document, or a line in an email: eighteen pence a word, forty pounds a piece, a fraction of a penny a click.
Agreed by both sides, honoured in full, and containing no term either party would dispute.

## Holds

The entire allocation of the writer's day, decided by which dimension it names.
It also holds its own limit: every term on it is enforceable precisely because it is countable at a distance, and nothing that fails that test can appear, however much it matters.

## Offers

Enforceability, which is what a contract with a stranger has to have.
That is genuinely what it offers and it is the reason it exists: without a checkable term there is no agreement at all, only a hope.

## Withheld

Every dimension that leaves no artefact.
The card cannot mention the second call or the removed paragraph, not from meanness but because a term nobody could verify is not a term, so the parts of the job that most need funding are the parts it is structurally incapable of naming.
