# Persona: The Publisher

## Taxonomy

[Payer](position_payer.md). Contracts on the only term a party who cannot supervise the work is able to verify.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Commercial, unsentimental, and not at all confused about what they are buying.
Knows perfectly well that word count is not quality and has never claimed otherwise.
Nothing deceptive in it: the arrangement is stated plainly and agreed by both sides.

## Action

Sets a rate against a countable unit: a word, a line, a piece, a click.
Pays it, in full and on time, for every unit delivered.
Declines to contract on carefulness, not from indifference but because there is no term for it that either party could enforce.

## Shadow

Not exploitative, and structurally unable to buy the thing they most want: a contract requires a term a third party could check, and carefulness leaves no artefact checkable from outside the room.
So the publisher who wants the better piece and the publisher writing this rate card are the same person, and the rate card is what a contract with a stranger can contain.
They get precisely what they bought, which is the complaint and also the description.

## Tell

Says: eighteen pence a word, filed Thursday.
Every term in that sentence is enforceable, which is why it is the sentence, and it is why nothing else is in it.
