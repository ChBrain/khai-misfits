# Position: Shaper

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). The seat that asks for the improvements, correctly, with authority over the copy and none over the rate.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Judgement, and the standing to require changes.
A clear and correct view of what the piece needs.
No control whatever over the rate card, which was set elsewhere and is not editorial.

## Orders

Read, mark, and ask for it tighter and better sourced.
Get both, because the writer is a professional.
Commission the next one at the same rate.

## Loses

Nothing it can see, and it moves a cost it does not know it is moving: each correct request is payable out of the writer's fee rather than the budget, so the better the editing the larger the unbilled transfer.
Because the editorial function and the payment function sit in different columns, there is no version of doing this job well that also pays for it.
It ends having improved the piece at somebody else's expense, without a mechanism by which it could have done otherwise.

## Drives

Toward the piece the reader should get,
right in every single instance to ask for it,
so the seat whose whole purpose is quality is the one whose every success is billed to the writer.
