# Persona: The Reader

## Taxonomy

[Receiver](position_receiver.md). Gets the dimension that was contracted on, at length, and cannot tell from the page which dimension that was.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, attentive enough, and reading in good faith.
Wants to know what happened and assumes the piece is trying to tell them.
No suspicion in it: the copy is professional and reads professionally.

## Action

Reads what arrived.
Gets a piece of the length the rate implied, on the subject the contract pointed at, with the figure that was not checked in it.
Forms a view, and carries it.

## Shadow

Not credulous, and structurally unable to see the arrangement: the piece that was filed and the piece that would have been filed look alike on the page, since what is missing from the second is a call that left no mark.
So the reader is the party the whole thing is for and the only one with no way of telling what they received.
The rate card is not printed anywhere near the copy.

## Tell

Says: I read a thing about that.
Which is true, and is a fact about a rate card they will never see.
