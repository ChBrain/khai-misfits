# Process: Filing

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Paid for the Cut](play_nobody_paid_for_the_cut.md). Producing the countable units, at pace, which is the part of the job the agreement can see.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Hours allocated to the priced dimension.
At [the wire](place_the_wire.md), copy arriving with a count attached.

## Direction

Toward units, delivered and measured.
This step works perfectly: the copy is professional, the count is exact, the invoice is correct and the payment is prompt.
It is staged because it succeeds, so the loss downstream cannot be attributed to anybody failing to do what was agreed.

## Lever

Competence and pace, both of which are present in full.
Because the contracted performance is delivered without shortfall, improving it changes nothing about what is missing.

## Echo

An accurate invoice against an honest agreement.
Every party is satisfied on every term that exists, which is the condition the rest of the play takes place under.
