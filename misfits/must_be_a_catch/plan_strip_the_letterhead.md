# Plan: Strip the Letterhead

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the mediator's seat](position_mediator.md), inside [Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Mediator](position_mediator.md)

## Direction

Carry the identical terms across the gap without the name that would trigger the discount, relaying the offer as terms on the table rather than as one side's proposal to the other. Hold the boundary of what the seat can do: restore the offer's evaluated worth close to a neutral reading, and claim no more than that, since the seat cannot resolve the adversarial relationship the discount comes from. Accept what the plan costs: the remedy exists only where both sides already trust the channel, and it is unavailable in exactly the relationships that need it most.

## Orders

([The Mediator](persona_the_mediator.md) relays the terms through [Relaying](process_relaying.md) at [the Relay](place_the_relay.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md).)

## Implementation

([The Relay](place_the_relay.md) is where the offer's source is set aside; [Relaying](process_relaying.md) carries the terms to each side as terms rather than as the other's proposal, restoring the reading [the Discount](piece_the_discount.md) had taken away.)

## Targets

- [ ] the terms carried are identical to what either side set down directly
- [ ] the source is stripped, not disguised, so the relay is honest about what it is doing
- [ ] the mediator's own neutrality is verifiable by both sides, not merely claimed
- [ ] the remedy is not extended into a claim that the adversarial relationship itself is resolved
