# Piece: The Backlash

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). The political cost a negotiator pays for appearing to take a rival's offer at face value, reinforcing a private inference with a public one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held at [The Caucus](place_the_caucus.md), out of sight of the offer itself: a negotiator who looks trusting of the rival answers to a base judging posture rather than terms (Ross and Stillinger).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the discount stays a private matter: without a real cost for appearing to trust the rival's offer, a negotiator who privately judged the offer sound could act on that judgment freely, and the trap would be weaker, and easier to see through, than it is.
It bears the load as the reinforcement that keeps the discount in place even against a negotiator's own better judgment. Because the cost is political and not merely cognitive, it operates on a negotiator who has already seen through the attribution and cannot afford to act on having done so.

## Apparent

Accountability, even: a constituency holding its negotiator to account looks like ordinary democratic or organizational oversight, not like a mechanism of the trap.

## Yearbook

Stands over the close in [Don't Look Convinced](plot_dont_look_convinced.md), the cost that outlasts the private judgment.
It works together with [The Discount](piece_the_discount.md), the public cost the private one's reinforcement.
