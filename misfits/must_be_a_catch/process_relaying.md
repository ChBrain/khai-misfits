# Process: Relaying

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). Borne at [The Relay](place_the_relay.md), precipitated by [The Mediator](persona_the_mediator.md) carrying terms without their source, in [Strip the Letterhead](plot_strip_the_letterhead.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A trust neither side is obliged to extend but has, in this instance, granted to a third party neither adversary.

## Direction

From an offer marked by its source toward the same offer carried as terms alone, the evaluated worth moving back toward what a neutral reading would give it.

## Lever

That an identical proposal, attributed to a trusted or neutral party instead of a rival, is read close to its actual content, shown directly in the disarmament-proposal demonstration (Stillinger, Epelbaum, Keltner and Ross).

## Echo

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), marking what the relay restores and what it leaves untouched.
