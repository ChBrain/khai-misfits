# Place: The Caucus

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). Where a negotiator answers to its own side for how it read a rival's offer. Hosts [Don't Look Convinced](plot_dont_look_convinced.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A negotiator reporting back, a constituency listening for signs of how the other side's offer was taken.

## Holds

[The Backlash](piece_the_backlash.md), the cost of looking taken in, held here rather than at the table where the offer itself was made.

## Offers

To the base, a chance to judge posture and reinforce the discount, and to the negotiator, a reminder of what a soft read would cost.

## Withheld

Any view of the offer's actual terms, so the judgment made here rests on how the negotiator looked rather than on what was actually on the table.
