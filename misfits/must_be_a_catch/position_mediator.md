# Position: Mediator

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). The seat of the trusted third party who carries an offer stripped of its source, restoring much of what the discount took.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Standing, where both sides grant it, to relay terms without either side reading its own motive into them.
No terms of its own to offer, only the terms either side has already set.
No power over the adversarial relationship itself, only over how one offer within it is read.

## Orders

Strip the letterhead: carry the identical terms across the gap without the name that would trigger the discount.
Hold the boundary of what the seat can do: restore an offer's evaluated worth close to a neutral reading, and go no further.
Accept what the seat does: relay one offer past the trap, and leave the trap itself standing for the next one made directly.

## Loses

Any standing at all, the moment either side comes to suspect the seat favors the other.
Because its whole leverage is a trust neither side is obliged to extend, the seat loses everything it does the instant that trust is withdrawn, its neutrality an asset it does not itself control.
It keeps whatever deals it closes while trusted, and loses every deal in a relationship that has no trusted channel to spare.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound neutrality can carry a good offer past a discount neither side could remove on its own,
relaying because relaying is the one act its trusted standing allows and, by doing so, recovering a deal that would otherwise have gone unmade,
so the seat that only did well by staying trusted is the seat through which the trap, for one offer at a time, is set aside rather than solved.
