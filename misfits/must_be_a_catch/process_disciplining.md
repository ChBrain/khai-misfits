# Process: Disciplining

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). Borne at [The Caucus](place_the_caucus.md), precipitated by [The Base](persona_the_base.md) holding the backlash over a negotiator's posture, in [Don't Look Convinced](plot_dont_look_convinced.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A constituency that cannot see the offer's terms directly and judges its negotiator by how trusting that negotiator appears toward the rival.

## Direction

From a negotiator's private read of the offer toward a public posture shaped by what its own side will tolerate, private judgment and public position pulled apart.

## Lever

That looking duped by the enemy carries a real political cost, so even a negotiator who privately judges the offer sound has reason to discount it publicly, private suspicion and political pressure reinforcing each other (Ross and Stillinger).

## Echo

[The Backlash](piece_the_backlash.md), reinforcing [The Discount](piece_the_discount.md) whether or not the negotiator believes it.
