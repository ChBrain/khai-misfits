# Position: Rival

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). The seat that reads the proposer's offer backward from its source, discounting it for no reason but who made it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A relationship genuinely adversarial in enough of its scope to make suspicion of the proposer's motives, in general, sound.
An inference ready before any offer arrives: a self-interested party proposes only what serves it.
No way to weigh an offer's content without the source coloring the weighing first.

## Orders

Read for the catch: apply the standing inference to whatever the proposer sets down, generous terms read as the largest gift from the least trusted source.
Discount before evaluating, since the source is known before the terms are, and the order of information is not something the seat can reverse.
Accept what the seat does: that suspecting a rival's motive is sound practice, and that the same soundness cannot tell a good offer from a bad one dressed to look good.

## Loses

The ability to take a fair offer at its word, even when it is in fact fair.
Because the seat's caution is not calibrated to this offer but to the relationship as a whole, it loses exactly the deals the relationship's own logic would otherwise allow, discounted alongside every bad offer the same caution correctly catches.
It keeps its protection against a genuine trap, and loses the one deal that was not a trap at all.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound wariness of a genuine adversary marks a genuine concession as suspect,
discounting because discounting is the ordinary discipline the seat rewards and, by doing so, turning the best offer on the table into the one least likely to be taken,
so the seat that only did well by staying careful is the seat through which a real deal becomes indistinguishable from a trap.
