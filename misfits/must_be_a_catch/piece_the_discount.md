# Piece: The Discount

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). The reduced evaluated worth an offer takes on once attributed to a rival, falling hardest on the most generous terms.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Measured at [The Table](place_the_table.md): the identical terms rated less favorable when attributed to the rival than to a neutral or trusted party, shown directly in the disarmament-proposal demonstration, an identical arms reduction plan rated more balanced when attributed to Reagan than to Gorbachev (Stillinger, Epelbaum, Keltner and Ross).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the attribution costs nothing: a suspicion that did not lower an offer's evaluated worth would leave the deal exactly as good as it was before anyone weighed the source, and the trap would have no teeth.
It bears the load as the measurable damage the attribution does. Because the discount is largest on the most generous terms, it is precisely the deals most likely to close that pay the heaviest price, the mechanism specific in its effect and not a uniform tax on every offer alike.

## Apparent

Skepticism, even: a lower valuation reads as prudent hedging rather than as an error, since some discount really would be warranted against a genuine trap.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Must Be a Catch](plot_must_be_a_catch.md), the value taken as the attribution lands.
It works together with [The Backlash](piece_the_backlash.md), the private discount reinforced by a public one.
