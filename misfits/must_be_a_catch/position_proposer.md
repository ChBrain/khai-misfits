# Position: Proposer

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). The seat that offers a genuine concession, terms a neutral hand would call fair, discounted by the rival for no reason but who made them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Real terms to offer, weighed and set in good faith, worth what a neutral hand would say they are worth.
No control over how the offer is read once it reaches the rival's side of the table.
Nothing to prove beyond the terms themselves, which is exactly what the seat cannot make count.

## Orders

Offer in good faith: set terms a neutral hand would call fair, and set them plainly, without disguising the concession as anything other than what it is.
Accept that the offer will be read backward from the seat's own name, and that no amount of good faith changes what the name alone already implies.
Move first if moving first is what the deal requires, knowing the best terms draw the heaviest discount.

## Loses

The benefit of the doubt, before a single term is read.
Because the rival infers content from source before weighing either, the seat loses exactly the credit its good faith should earn, discounted for the one fact it cannot change, which side of the table it sits on.
It keeps the terms it set, and loses the reading those terms deserved.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound offer of fair terms is read, by a rival correctly wary of a genuine adversary, as evidence of a catch,
proposing because proposing is what a negotiator trying to close a real deal does and, by doing so, marking its own best offer as its least believable one,
so the seat that only did well by offering fairly is the seat through which the good deal becomes the one no one will take.
