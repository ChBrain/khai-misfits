# Persona: The Base

## Taxonomy

[Base](position_base.md). The constituency either negotiator answers to, punishing any sign of taking a rival's offer at face value.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Watchful and loyal, the side that backs its negotiator and expects to be defended in turn.
Reads as ordinary vigilance, an audience that does not want its side taken advantage of at the table.
No cruelty on show: wanting a negotiator not to be fooled by the enemy is a reasonable thing for a constituency to want.

## Action

Runs [Punish the Soft Read](plan_punish_the_soft_read.md): through [Disciplining](process_disciplining.md) at [the Caucus](place_the_caucus.md), holds [the Backlash](piece_the_backlash.md) over any negotiator who looks to take a rival's offer seriously.
Cannot see the offer's actual terms as closely as the negotiator at the table does, and judges instead by how trusting the negotiator's posture looks.
Does what the seat rewards: punishes the appearance of being taken in, whether or not the offer was in fact sound.

## Shadow

Not vindictive: the cost it threatens is a real political cost, and a negotiator who looks duped by the enemy really does lose standing with the side it answers to, so the threat is not empty.
Because it judges posture rather than terms, its sound vigilance falls just as hard on a negotiator correctly recognizing a good offer as on one actually fooled by a bad one, protection and punishment indistinguishable from outside the room.
It is the reason a discount felt privately becomes a position held publicly, whether or not the two agree.

## Tell

Reads a negotiator's calm response to a generous rival offer as a warning sign, and a hard, suspicious response as reassurance, regardless of which offer was actually the better one.
Cannot be shown the terms directly without also showing whose terms they are, and once the source is known, the same watchfulness the seat runs on takes over.
