# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). The condition and the hedge: an adversarial relationship and a traceable source are needed, and the trap lifts where the offer is relayed blind, a trusted mediator carries it, or the relationship stops being adversarial.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held at [The Relay](place_the_relay.md), where a mediator's carriage restores much of what the discount took, but only where both sides already trust the channel and its neutrality is not itself contested (Ross and Stillinger; Stillinger, Epelbaum, Keltner and Ross).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overclaims: without the boundary, the discount would read as a fate of every negotiation rather than a conditional structure, needing a relationship genuinely adversarial and an offer with a traceable source, and weakening wherever those conditions do not hold.
It bears the load as the honest limit on the trap's reach. Because a trusted mediator is not always available and a genuinely bad offer deserves its discount, the boundary is also what keeps the staging from implying that every rejected offer traces to this bias rather than to the offer's own merits.

## Apparent

A footnote, even: the fine print at the end of a mechanism that otherwise reads as settled and complete.

## Yearbook

Stands over the close in [Carried by a Third Party](plot_carried_by_a_third_party.md), the remedy named and its limit named beside it.
It works together with [The Foregone Deal](piece_the_foregone_deal.md), the deal recoverable here and nowhere else in the play.
