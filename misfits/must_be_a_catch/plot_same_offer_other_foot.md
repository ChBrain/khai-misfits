# Plot: Same Offer, Other Foot

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Table](place_the_table.md), where the roles that just played out reverse.

## Action

[The Rival](persona_the_rival.md) now proposes its own concession, terms as fair, in their own right, as the proposer's were, and [the Proposer](persona_the_proposer.md), through the same [Discounting](process_discounting.md), applies the same [Attribution](piece_the_attribution.md) in return. [The Mirror](piece_the_mirror.md) stands revealed: neither side is the villain, both apply the identical, sound suspicion to the other. The scene closes with [the Foregone Deal](piece_the_foregone_deal.md), terms neither side will take from the other though both would take them from a stranger.

## Stage

Nobody switches sides in bad faith: the rival's offer is as genuine as the proposer's was, and the proposer's discount of it is as reasonable as the rival's discount was.

## Tension

Each side has now stood in both positions, offering and discounting in turn, so neither can plausibly claim the other alone is at fault, and the deal both would take from a stranger stays exactly where it started.
