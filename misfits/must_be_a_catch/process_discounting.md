# Process: Discounting

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). Borne at [The Table](place_the_table.md), precipitated by [The Rival](persona_the_rival.md) applying the standing attribution to the proposer's offer, in [Must Be a Catch](plot_must_be_a_catch.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An adversarial relationship in which suspecting the other side's motives is, in general, sound practice.

## Direction

From an offer's terms as set toward an evaluated worth read down from them, the reduction scaling with how generous the terms appear.

## Lever

That a party assumed to act in its own interest is assumed to have chosen terms that serve it, so the offer is read backward from its source before its content is weighed at all (Stillinger, Epelbaum, Keltner and Ross).

## Echo

[The Discount](piece_the_discount.md), attached before the deal is ever discussed on its merits.
