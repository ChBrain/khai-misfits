# Persona: The Rival

## Taxonomy

[Rival](position_rival.md). The side that reads the proposer's offer backward from its source, discounting it for no reason but who made it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful and unfooled, a negotiator who has seen offers before and does not take one at face value.
Reads as prudent, the party unwilling to be the one who got outmaneuvered by trusting too quickly.
No bad faith on show: scrutinizing a rival's offer is exactly what a competent negotiator is supposed to do.

## Action

Runs [Read for the Catch](plan_read_for_the_catch.md): through [Discounting](process_discounting.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md), applies [the Attribution](piece_the_attribution.md) to [the Proposer](persona_the_proposer.md)'s offer, a self-interested party offered this, so it must serve that interest.
Cannot separate the offer's content from its source, since the inference runs from source to content before the terms are weighed at all.
Does what a rival relationship rewards: treats an adversary's offer with suspicion, the same suspicion that correctly protects it from a bad offer also falling on a good one.

## Shadow

Not paranoid: the relationship really is adversarial in enough of its scope to make suspicion of the proposer's motives generally sound, so reading for a catch is not an overreaction but the ordinary discipline of the seat.
Because the discount is heaviest on the most generous offers, its soundest caution is exactly what marks the best deal on the table as the least trustworthy one, prudence and blindness the same act.
It is the reason the deal that would actually close reads as the one most worth refusing.

## Tell

Names a hidden motive for every generous term the proposer sets, and the more generous the term, the more certain the motive.
Cannot see the offer as the proposer intended it, since by the time it reaches [the Rival](persona_the_rival.md) it has already been reread through the one lens the seat is built to use.
