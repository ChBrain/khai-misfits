# Piece: The Mirror

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). The fact that the discount is not one side's error but a structure both sides apply to each other in turn, each reasonable about a rival whose opposed interest is, in general, real.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Shown at [The Table](place_the_table.md) once the roles reverse: whichever side proposes, the other discounts, the identical reasoning run from both directions at once (Ross and Stillinger).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the trap reads as one side's flaw: without the reversal, the discounting could be told as a story about a particularly suspicious rival rather than about the structure of the relationship itself, and the misfit would misplace its fault.
It bears the load as the proof that no side is the villain. Because the same offer, made by either party, meets the same discount from the other, the mechanism is shown to be a property of the adversarial relationship and not a defect in either negotiator's character.

## Apparent

An impasse of temperament, even: two stubborn sides, read from outside as simply unwilling to trust, rather than as two sound reasoners caught in the same structure.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Same Offer, Other Foot](plot_same_offer_other_foot.md), the symmetry made visible.
It works together with [The Foregone Deal](piece_the_foregone_deal.md), the mirror the reason the deal is lost from both directions at once.
