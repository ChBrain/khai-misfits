# Plan: Punish the Soft Read

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the base's seat](position_base.md), inside [Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Base](position_base.md)

## Direction

Withdraw confidence from a negotiator who looks to accept a rival's offer at face value, since a posture of easy trust toward an adversary reads, from outside the room, as a sign of being taken in. Reward the hard read, backing a negotiator who treats the rival's offer with visible suspicion, since that is the posture the seat can verify without seeing the terms itself. Accept what the plan costs: judging posture rather than terms will sometimes punish a negotiator who read the offer correctly, and the seat has no way to tell that case from the one where the negotiator was actually fooled.

## Orders

([The Base](persona_the_base.md) holds [the Backlash](piece_the_backlash.md) through [Disciplining](process_disciplining.md) at [the Caucus](place_the_caucus.md).)

## Implementation

([The Caucus](place_the_caucus.md) is where the negotiator answers for its posture, not for the terms themselves; [Disciplining](process_disciplining.md) converts a private inclination to trust the offer into a public cost, reinforcing [the Discount](piece_the_discount.md) whether or not the negotiator privately believes it.)

## Targets

- [ ] any visible sign of trusting the rival's offer draws a real cost with the base
- [ ] a visibly suspicious posture toward the rival's offer is rewarded, regardless of the offer's actual terms
- [ ] the pressure is applied consistently, since the base cannot verify the terms directly
- [ ] private judgment and public position are pulled apart by the cost of appearing soft
