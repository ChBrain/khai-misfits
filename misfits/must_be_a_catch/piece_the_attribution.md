# Piece: The Attribution

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). The inference that a party assumed to act in its own interest offered only what serves that interest, read into an offer before its content is weighed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Applied at [The Table](place_the_table.md), the instant an offer's source is known: a self-interested party proposed this, so it must serve that interest (Stillinger, Epelbaum, Keltner and Ross).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the offer is read for what it says: without the standing inference from source to content, the same terms would be evaluated on their own merits, and a fair offer would be recognized as fair regardless of who set it down.
It bears the load as the mechanism that converts a general suspicion of the relationship into a specific judgment about this offer. Because the inference runs before the terms are weighed, it decides the reading in advance, and everything downstream, the discount, the backlash, the foregone deal, follows from this single step.

## Apparent

Due diligence, even: reading an offer's likely motive before accepting it looks like exactly the caution any negotiator should exercise.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Fair Terms, Read Wrong](plot_fair_terms_read_wrong.md), the moment source outweighs content.
It works together with [The Discount](piece_the_discount.md), the inference the reason the value falls at all.
