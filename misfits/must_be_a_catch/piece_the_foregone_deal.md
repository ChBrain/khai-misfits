# Piece: The Foregone Deal

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). The terms both sides would accept from a neutral hand and will not take from each other, the agreement genuinely available and genuinely unmade.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Left on [The Table](place_the_table.md), untaken: the same terms a stranger's proposal would have closed, refused twice over once each side's own offer is discounted by the other (Ross and Stillinger).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit is only a curiosity: without a real deal genuinely lost, the discount would be a bias with no cost attached, an interesting misreading rather than a structural trap with real stakes.
It bears the load as the proof the mechanism matters. Because the deal was actually available, not merely imagined, its loss is what turns an attribution error into a misfit worth staging: locally sound reasoning on every side summing to an outcome worse than either side, alone, would have chosen.

## Apparent

Irreconcilable differences, even: a deal that failed reads, from outside, as proof the two sides simply wanted incompatible things.

## Yearbook

Stands over the close in [Same Offer, Other Foot](plot_same_offer_other_foot.md), the cost of the mirror made concrete.
It works together with [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the deal recoverable only where the boundary's conditions for a remedy are met.
