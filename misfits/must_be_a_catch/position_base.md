# Position: Base

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). The seat of the constituency either negotiator answers to, punishing any sign of taking a rival's offer at face value.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Standing to reward or withdraw support from its own negotiator, judged by posture rather than by terms it rarely sees in full.
A real stake in not being taken advantage of, which a negotiator who looks trusting of the enemy appears to threaten.
No direct view of the offer, only of how its own negotiator responds to it.

## Orders

Punish the soft read: withdraw confidence from a negotiator who appears to accept a rival's offer without the expected suspicion.
Reward the hard read: back a negotiator who treats the rival's offer as the trap the seat expects it to be.
Accept what the seat does: that it judges the appearance of trust rather than the offer's actual worth, since the offer is not the seat's to weigh directly.

## Loses

The ability to distinguish a negotiator correctly recognizing a good offer from one actually fooled by a bad one.
Because it judges posture and not terms, the seat loses exactly the cases where the right response looked, from outside the room, like the wrong one, punishing good judgment it cannot tell apart from bad.
It keeps its defense against a negotiator truly duped, and loses the deal a less anxious posture might have closed.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound demand not to be taken advantage of turns every soft-looking response into a liability,
disciplining because disciplining is what protects the side it answers for and, by doing so, forcing even a negotiator who privately judges the offer sound to discount it in public,
so the seat that only did well by staying vigilant is the seat through which a private suspicion becomes a public position no one can walk back.
