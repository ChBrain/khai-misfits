# Process: Offering

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). Borne at [The Table](place_the_table.md), precipitated by [The Proposer](persona_the_proposer.md) setting terms a neutral hand would call fair, in [Must Be a Catch](plot_must_be_a_catch.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A negotiator wanting the deal it is offering, moving first with real terms rather than waiting to be moved first.

## Direction

From no terms on the table toward a concrete offer, the terms fixed and attributable the moment they are set down.

## Lever

That the offer, once made, cannot be separated from the name that made it, so the same act that proposes the deal also supplies the rival everything it needs to discount it.

## Echo

[The Attribution](piece_the_attribution.md), waiting on the other side of the table to be applied.
