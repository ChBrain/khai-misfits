# Place: The Table

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md). Where offers are made directly between the two sides, source and terms arriving together. Hosts [Good Enough From a Stranger](plot_good_enough_from_a_stranger.md), [Must Be a Catch](plot_must_be_a_catch.md), and [Same Offer, Other Foot](plot_same_offer_other_foot.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two sides across from each other, terms set out, the ordinary business of a negotiation in progress.

## Holds

[The Attribution](piece_the_attribution.md), read into every offer the moment it lands, and [The Discount](piece_the_discount.md), the value the reading takes away.

## Offers

To either side, the chance to close a deal both would take from a neutral hand.

## Withheld

Any way to separate an offer's terms from its source, since here the two arrive in the same motion and are read together whether either side wants them to be.
