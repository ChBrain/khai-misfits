# Plan: Read for the Catch

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the rival's seat](position_rival.md), inside [Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Rival](position_rival.md)

## Direction

Apply the standing inference to whatever the proposer sets down: a self-interested party offered this, so assume it serves that interest until shown otherwise, and read the terms with that assumption first rather than last. Discount hardest where the offer is most generous, since a large gift from an adversary is the shape a real catch would take. Accept what the plan costs: a fair offer, read this way, will be discounted along with every unfair one, and the seat has no way from inside the relationship to tell the two apart in advance.

## Orders

([The Rival](persona_the_rival.md) applies [the Attribution](piece_the_attribution.md) through [Discounting](process_discounting.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md).)

## Implementation

([The Table](place_the_table.md) is where the offer arrives already carrying its source; [Discounting](process_discounting.md) reads [the Proposer](persona_the_proposer.md)'s terms backward from that source before their content is weighed, producing [the Discount](piece_the_discount.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] every offer from the proposer is read through the standing attribution first
- [ ] the discount scales with the offer's generosity, not against it
- [ ] no offer is taken at face value on the strength of its terms alone
- [ ] the caution is applied consistently, whether or not this particular offer happens to be sound
