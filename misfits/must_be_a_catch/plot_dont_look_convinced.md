# Plot: Don't Look Convinced

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Caucus](place_the_caucus.md), where the rival's negotiator answers for how the offer was read.

## Action

Through [Disciplining](process_disciplining.md), [the Base](persona_the_base.md), from [the base's seat](position_base.md), on [Punish the Soft Read](plan_punish_the_soft_read.md) holds [the Backlash](piece_the_backlash.md) over [the Rival](persona_the_rival.md), from [the rival's seat](position_rival.md): accept the proposer's offer at anything close to face value, and answer for looking duped by the enemy. The scene closes with private judgment and public position pulled apart, the discount reinforced whether or not the rival privately believes it (Ross and Stillinger).

## Stage

The base is not cruel: it defends against a real risk, that a negotiator trusted with the relationship might in fact be taken in, and it has no way to see the offer's terms directly to judge otherwise.

## Tension

A negotiator who has already seen through the attribution and judges the offer sound must still discount it in public, so the very insight that could have closed the deal is the insight the seat cannot afford to act on.
