# Persona: The Proposer

## Taxonomy

[Proposer](position_proposer.md). The side that offers a genuine concession, terms a neutral hand would call fair, discounted by the rival for no reason but who made them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Reasonable and forthcoming, a negotiator putting real terms on the table, giving something up to get somewhere.
Reads as the party trying to close the deal, willing to move first and take the risk of moving first.
No trick on show: the terms are exactly what they appear to be, weighed and offered in good faith.

## Action

Runs [Offer in Good Faith](plan_offer_in_good_faith.md): through [Offering](process_offering.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md), sets terms a neutral hand would call fair.
Cannot control how the offer is read once it leaves its hands, since the discount attaches at the rival's end, to the name at the bottom of the page and not to anything the proposer can change about the terms.
Does what good-faith negotiation asks: offers real value, and gets read as though it must be hiding something for the trouble.

## Shadow

Not naive: the terms are sound, and offering them is the correct move for a negotiator trying to close a deal it also wants closed.
Because the very generosity of the offer is what makes it read as the biggest gift from the least trusted source, its soundest move is the one most heavily discounted, virtue and suspicion the same fact seen from two sides.
It is the reason a foregone deal is foregone, without doing anything wrong.

## Tell

Offers its best terms early, expecting good terms to be recognized as good terms, and watches them read instead as evidence of a catch.
Cannot tell, from its own side of the table, that the very quality of its offer is what triggers the discount, since a worse offer would have drawn less suspicion and moved the deal no further.
