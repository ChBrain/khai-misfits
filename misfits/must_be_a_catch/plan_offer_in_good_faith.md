# Plan: Offer in Good Faith

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the proposer's seat](position_proposer.md), inside [Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Proposer](position_proposer.md)

## Direction

Set terms a neutral hand would call fair, and set them plainly, since disguising a real concession as anything less than it is would only give the rival more reason for suspicion, not less. Move first if the deal requires it, accepting that the best terms draw the heaviest discount and offering them anyway, because a negotiator who withholds its best terms to avoid the discount forecloses the deal just as surely. Take nothing you are not owed: this is not a bluff to be seen through, and the plan is simply to mean what the offer says.

## Orders

([The Proposer](persona_the_proposer.md) sets the terms through [Offering](process_offering.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md).)

## Implementation

([The Table](place_the_table.md) is where the offer is made and first read; [Offering](process_offering.md) carries terms weighed in good faith across to [the Rival](persona_the_rival.md), who applies [the Attribution](piece_the_attribution.md) before weighing them in turn.)

## Targets

- [ ] terms are set at what a neutral hand would call fair, not padded against an expected discount
- [ ] the offer is made plainly, disguised as nothing other than what it is
- [ ] the best available terms are offered, even knowing they draw the heaviest suspicion
- [ ] good faith is kept regardless of how the offer is read on the other side
