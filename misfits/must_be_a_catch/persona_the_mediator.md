# Persona: The Mediator

## Taxonomy

[Mediator](position_mediator.md). The trusted third party who carries an offer stripped of its source, restoring much of what the discount took.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Neutral and procedural, a channel rather than a party, relaying terms without a stake in which side wins them.
Reads as the honest broker, present to help the deal close rather than to win it for either side.
No favor on show: the terms it carries are the same terms either side could have set down itself.

## Action

Runs [Strip the Letterhead](plan_strip_the_letterhead.md): through [Relaying](process_relaying.md) at [the Relay](place_the_relay.md), holds [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), carrying the identical terms without the name that would trigger [the Discount](piece_the_discount.md).
Cannot manufacture trust it does not already have, since its whole leverage is the two sides' prior confidence that it favors neither.
Does what the seat allows: restores an offer's evaluated worth close to what a neutral reading would give it, when both sides actually grant it that standing.

## Shadow

Not a fixer of the underlying relationship: it can carry one offer past the discount, but it does nothing to the adversarial relationship that produces the discount in the first place, so the same trap is waiting for the next offer made directly.
Because its whole value depends on a trust neither side is obligated to extend, its remedy is available only where it has already been earned, absent exactly where the discount is worst.
It is the closest thing the play allows to a remedy, and it is bounded on every side.

## Tell

Is sought out only once direct offers have already failed, called in after the discount has already cost a round or more of negotiation.
Cannot be trusted by fiat, since either side's suspicion of the mediator's own neutrality reattaches the discount to the channel exactly as it attached to the rival before.
