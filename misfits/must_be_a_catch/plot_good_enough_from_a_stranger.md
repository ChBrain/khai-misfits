# Plot: Good Enough From a Stranger

## Taxonomy

[Must Be a Catch](play_must_be_a_catch.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Table](place_the_table.md), where an adversarial relationship sits over terms that would, from a neutral hand, be accepted by both sides.

## Action

The relationship stands as it is: two parties with interests genuinely opposed in enough of their dealings to make suspicion of each other's motives, in general, well founded, and terms exist that a stranger could set down and have both sides take. The scene closes with the terms available and the relationship, not the terms, the obstacle (Ross and Stillinger).

## Stage

Neither side is wrong to be wary in general: the adversarial relationship is real, and the suspicion it grounds is not manufactured.

## Tension

The one fact that would let the terms be judged on their own, that they are, in fact, the same terms a stranger could offer, is exactly the fact neither side can supply for the other.
