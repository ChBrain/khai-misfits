# Play: The Scaffold Left Up

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Scaffold Left Up.
A course gives beginners fully worked examples instead of problems to struggle through, and it works: they learn more, faster, and the finding replicates. Weeks later the same support is still there, still delivered the same way, and it is now costing the same people something, because a learner who holds the procedure must read the explanation of it anyway and hold both at once. Nothing announced the change. It happened on a different afternoon for each of them.

## Arc

The bend is this: the support fails by succeeding, and the moment it turns is not observable from anywhere.
It runs on an intervention that is genuinely good.
[The Support](persona_the_support.md) is not a crutch and not indulgence. Given a problem with no procedure to hand, a beginner spends everything on search and has nothing left to learn the structure with, so [the worked example](piece_the_worked_example.md) is the documented fix, measured against the alternative and replicated.
And it works, which is the input.
[Building the schema](process_building_the_schema.md) is what the support is for and what it delivers: [the beginner](persona_the_beginner.md) comes to hold the procedure. Nothing about that arrival is visible, since a schema is not a milestone and nobody in the room announces having got it.
Then [Now It Is in the Way](plot_now_it_is_in_the_way.md).
[The One Who Knows It Now](persona_the_one_who_knows_it_now.md) is handed an explanation of what they already hold, and must reconcile the two rather than skip one, so the guidance that removed a load now adds one. The support did not degrade and is not being misapplied: it is the same support, doing the same thing, to somebody it no longer describes.
And [The Missing Signal](piece_the_missing_signal.md) is why nobody catches it. Expertise arrives continuously, at a different point for every learner, and there is no test the room is running that reports it, so a support delivered to a class cannot be timed to a person even in principle.
And the obvious answer is the other error. Taking it away early is the documented harm the support was introduced to fix, at the same size, so there is no safe default to fall back to and nothing to do by policy.
And [The Evidence](persona_the_evidence.md) cannot settle it either, because the trial was run on the population that needed the support, which is the population before it worked.

## Stakes

Whether an intervention can be trusted past the point where it starts working.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a support that is genuinely good, an improvement that is the support's own product, a reversal with no event in it, a turning point that no available instrument reports, a withdrawal that reinstates the original harm, and evidence gathered on the state the support removes, and away from any account in which somebody was careless. The production cannot fault the designer, who did the measured thing, and it cannot produce the afternoon on which the support turned, because that afternoon is different for every person in the room. What is left to fight over is not whether the support helped, everybody agrees it did, but what to call an intervention whose validity expires quietly, one learner at a time, as a direct result of it working.
