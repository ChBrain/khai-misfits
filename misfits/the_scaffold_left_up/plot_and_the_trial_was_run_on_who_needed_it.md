## Tension

It measured the population before the support worked.
The sample had to be beginners, since that is who the question was about, and the effect it found in them is genuine: so the strongest case for continuing is drawn entirely from the state that continuing exists to end, and every learner the support succeeds on leaves the population the study describes. This is not a weak study and no better-powered version of it helps, because the question at issue is when a person stops being a beginner, and no trial of that design is addressed to it.
