## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

The weeks after, with the support working exactly as intended.

## Action

[Building the schema](process_building_the_schema.md): [the beginner](persona_the_beginner.md) comes to hold the procedure, by the support's own action.

## Stage

[The Schema](piece_the_schema.md) is there. Nothing in the room marks its arrival.

## Tension

The success is private and undated, and it is the input to everything that follows.
A schema is not a milestone: nobody in a class announces having got it, no exercise in the course is designed to report it, and the learner themselves would struggle to name the afternoon. So the support has produced, silently, the exact condition under which it stops being the right instrument, and it has done so at a different point for every person receiving it.
