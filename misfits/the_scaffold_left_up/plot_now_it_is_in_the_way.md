# Plot: Now It Is in the Way

## Taxonomy

[The Scaffold Left Up](play_the_scaffold_left_up.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The same support, delivered the same way, to somebody who now holds what it explains.

## Action

[The One Who Knows It Now](persona_the_one_who_knows_it_now.md), holding [One Who Knows It Now](position_one_who_knows_it_now.md), reads an explanation of the procedure they are already carrying.

## Stage

The guidance adds a load rather than removing one, without having changed in any respect.

## Tension

It cannot be skipped, which is the whole of why it costs.
Guidance presented beside a schema has to be checked against the schema rather than ignored, so the load is incurred in the act of establishing that the guidance is unnecessary, and the more securely the procedure is held the more reliably that check is run. The support did not degrade, is not being misapplied and is not being overused: it is the same instrument doing the same thing to somebody it no longer describes, and the harm scales with how well it worked.
