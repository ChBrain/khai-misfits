## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Projection

Still in the same seat, still given the same handout.

## Action

Holds both, and reconciles them.

## Shadow

Guidance presented alongside a schema must be checked against the schema rather than ignored, so the load is incurred in the act of confirming it is unnecessary, and the better the learner the more reliably they do the checking.
