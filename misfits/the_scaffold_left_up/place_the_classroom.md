# Place: The Classroom

## Taxonomy

[The Scaffold Left Up](play_the_scaffold_left_up.md). Where the support is delivered to a group, correctly, and where the group is not the unit that matters.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A course built on the literature, a handout with every step shown, and results that improved.
Competence, at every level, with the reasoning available on request.

## Holds

[The Designer](persona_the_designer.md), watching for a moment to stop.
[The Support](persona_the_support.md), performing the identical operation on everyone.
[The Worked Example](piece_the_worked_example.md), which is the documented fix and is not a crutch.

## Offers

One instrument, delivered to everybody at once, which is what makes a course possible at all.
A measured advantage over the alternative, real and replicated.

## Withheld

Any per-person timing.
The place runs on a schedule and a handout, and the quantity that decides whether the handout helps is held privately by each learner and changes on a different afternoon for each of them, so the unit the place can act on and the unit the mechanism runs on are not the same unit and no amount of care reconciles them.
