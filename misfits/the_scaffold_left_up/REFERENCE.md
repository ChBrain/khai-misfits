# The Scaffold Left Up: Reference

The research warrant for The Scaffold Left Up.
The term is the field's; the play runs on the expertise reversal effect and the cognitive load account that
explains it, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging instructional support as **an intervention whose validity expires as a direct result of it working**: a
beginner facing a problem with no procedure to hand spends the whole of a limited capacity on search and has
none left to acquire the structure with, so showing the worked steps is the documented way of freeing the
capacity that does the learning, measured against the alternative and replicated. What the support then
produces is a learner who holds the procedure, and guidance presented beside a held procedure must be
reconciled with it rather than skipped, so the same instrument that removed a load begins to add one. The play
models the documented mechanics, the support that is genuinely right, the acquisition that marks nothing, the
reversal with no event in it, the turning point no instrument in the room reports, the withdrawal that
reinstates the original harm, and the trial run on the state the support removes, as the load-bearing vectors
of the structure, not as a tale of a teacher who failed to notice.

The governing law is that guidance and schema are substitutes rather than complements, so support that
compensates for a missing schema is redundant once the schema exists, and redundant information is not free.
Processing it is not optional in the way ignoring an irrelevant object is optional: material presented as
relevant has to be checked against what is already held, and the check is where the cost falls. The better the
schema, the more reliably it is run.

What removes any possibility of blame from the designer is that they did the measured thing and the moment to
stop is not available. The turn is continuous, arrives at a different point for every learner, and is reported
by no test the course runs, so a support delivered to a class cannot be timed to a person even in principle:
the unit the room can act on and the unit the mechanism runs on are not the same unit. A designer looking
harder finds the same nothing.

What removes it from the obvious remedy is that withdrawal is the other error at the same size. Removing the
support is right for whoever holds the procedure and is exactly the documented harm for whoever does not, and
from the second week onward both are in the room at once. The error is symmetric and unavoidable rather than a
matter of judgement.

And the evidence cannot settle it. The trial had to be run on beginners, since that is who the question was
about, and the effect it found in them is real and replicates. So the strongest case for continuing is drawn
entirely from the state that continuing exists to end, every learner the support succeeds on leaves the
population the study describes, and no better-powered version of the same design is addressed to when a person
stops being one.

It is distinct from the house's **Second Nature**, where knowing a thing so well you cannot picture not knowing
it makes you explain over the heads of those who do not: there the failing party is the explainer and the
remedy is to model the novice better. Here the explainer models the novice correctly and the instrument is
right for the novice; what changes is the recipient, and the same explanation delivered with perfect empathy to
a person who has acquired the procedure costs them anyway.

It is distinct from the house's **Shifting the Burden**, where reaching for the quick fix lets the capacity for
the real cure wither: there something atrophies from disuse and the remedy is to exercise it. Here nothing
withers. The learner gets better, by the support's own action, and that improvement is precisely what breaks
the arrangement, so the corresponding move is not to restore a decayed capacity but to withdraw an intact one
at a moment nobody can identify.

It is distinct from the house's **The Good Move**, where a good answer arrives first and works so the search
stops and the better one in plain sight is never found: there the block is a solution the solver generated
themselves and the loss is a road not taken. Here nothing is blocked and no alternative is missed. The learner
reaches the right answer by the right route and pays a load on the way for reading a description of what they
were already doing.

It is distinct from the house's **More to Stand Still**, where the dose stops working so it is raised and the
raise becomes the level needed to stand still: there the effect decays toward zero with repetition and the
response is escalation. Here the effect does not decay, it **inverts**: the support goes from positive to
negative, more of it is worse rather than merely weaker, and nothing about the sequence is habituation to a
stimulus.

It is distinct from the house's **The Irony of Automation**, where automating the routine rusts the operator so
the rare failure lands on a hand too cold to catch it: there the support degrades the human and the harm
arrives at a later event. Here the human is improved rather than degraded, the harm is continuous rather than
saved up for an emergency, and the party that stops fitting is the instrument.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the designer as
  inattentive or the support as coddling, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the
  worked example is the documented fix and is measured against the alternative; the schema is the support's own
  product and arrives privately, on a different afternoon for each learner, marked by nothing; the guidance
  cannot be skipped once the schema exists, so the cost is incurred in confirming the guidance is unnecessary
  and rises with how well the support worked; the turning point is reported by no instrument the room has, and
  a designer who looked harder would find the same nothing; and withdrawal is the original harm for whoever is
  still short. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that an
  intervention can expire as a direct result of succeeding, quietly, one learner at a time.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Adaptive instruction is the
  real remedy and it wants rapid per-person diagnosis of expertise, repeated as expertise moves, which is a
  measurement the format of an ordinary course does not admit and which costs the thing that makes a course
  possible. Fading the support on a schedule replaces one wrong timing with another, since the schedule is
  per-room and the turn is per-person. Withdrawing early reinstates the documented harm at its original size.
  And knowing the mechanism completely, which everybody in the play does, changes none of the inputs to the
  decision about this week's handout.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the reversal, the worked-example and redundancy effects, the
  cognitive architecture and the adaptive remedy are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: the effect is
  established for **well-structured domains** and instructional formats of the kind studied, mathematics,
  procedures and technical training among them, and does not transfer without argument to open-ended or
  ill-structured learning; the size and even the direction of the reversal depend on the specific format,
  domain and measure, so the play stages the structure and not a magnitude; cognitive load theory's
  measurement is genuinely disputed, with subjective, dual-task and physiological methods giving different
  pictures and critics holding parts of the framework difficult to falsify, which the play carries rather than
  settles; and expertise is treated here as a matter of degree with no threshold, which is the play's own
  reading of a literature that often speaks in levels. **The play makes no claim that instructional support is
  generally counterproductive**, only that a support validated on the state it removes has no marker for the
  moment it stops applying, and that the moment is different for every person in the room.
