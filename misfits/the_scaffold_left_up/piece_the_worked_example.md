# Piece: The Worked Example

## Taxonomy

[The Classroom](place_the_classroom.md). The instrument, documented and correct.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Handed out in [the classroom](place_the_classroom.md) by [giving the support](process_giving_the_support.md), unchanged from week to week.

## Load Bearing

It is the established fix, not an indulgence, which is what makes the reversal a misfit rather than a mistake.
A beginner facing a problem with no procedure to hand spends everything on search and has nothing left to learn the structure with, so showing the steps is what frees the capacity to acquire them: the instrument is right, is measured against the alternative, and replicates, and none of that is what fails.

## Apparent

A handout. Every step shown, clearly, by somebody who knows the material.
The most obviously helpful object in the room.

## Yearbook

Never changes, and never needs to.
Becomes a cost without being altered, by the action of the person receiving it.
