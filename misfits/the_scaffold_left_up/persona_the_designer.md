# Persona: The Designer

## Taxonomy

[Designer](position_designer.md). The party who did the measured thing and has no move left.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A literature read properly, a course built on it, and an outcome that improved.
No shortcut taken anywhere.

## Action

Delivers the support to the room.
Watches for the moment to stop, and does not see one.

## Shadow

Is asked to time a per-person event with a per-room instrument.
The turn arrives continuously and at a different point for each learner, no test in the course reports it, and the two available policies, keep it and drop it, are each right for part of the room and wrong for the rest at any moment after the first week.

## Tell
