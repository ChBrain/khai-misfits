## Tension

Here the failure stops being about anybody's conduct and becomes a property of what arrived.
One term can be interrogated and the other cannot, so the comparison favours the inspectable side, and it
would come out the same way round for either party in either chair.
[Not arrogance](piece_not_arrogance.md): the judge rates the colleague highly and says so unprompted, and
the discounting is unaffected, because **it is not a verdict on anybody**. Swap the two people and it swaps
with them.
And equal weighting is not available as an act of fairness. It would mean acting on the strength of
reasoning nobody has seen, which is a guess about something invisible rather than a correction, so the
weighting that results is an accurate reading of an incomplete pair.
