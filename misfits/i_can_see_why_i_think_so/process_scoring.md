# Process: Scoring

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). The review that runs afterwards, correctly, on the one estimate that exists.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A year ending and a table of forecasts against outcomes.
At [the post-mortem](place_the_post_mortem.md), by people doing the job properly.

## Direction

Toward accurate feedback on what was decided.
It succeeds completely. Every error is computed correctly and reported honestly, and a bad call is named as
one.

## Lever

Constructing the counterfactual, which nobody is paid to do.
The midpoint of each pair could be computed and scored, and it would take a person a week and answer a
question nobody in the organisation has asked.

## Echo

A complete and honest record,
with no gap in it anywhere,
and no information at all about whether the weighting was the right one.
