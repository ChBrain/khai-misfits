# Plot: The Estimate Before the Advice

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A question worth two days of work, and somebody competent to do it.

## Action

[Estimating](process_estimating.md): [The One Who Already Thought About It](persona_the_one_who_already_thought_about_it.md), in [the estimate seat](position_estimate.md), works through [Use What I Actually Know](plan_use_what_i_actually_know.md).

## Stage

[The Desk](place_the_desk.md): working, notes, a discarded approach and the one that survived, and a number at the end of it.

## Tension

The play has to establish that nothing is being hidden from this party by anybody, and it establishes it
completely.
The work is good and the steps are checkable. The person can point at each one and say where the answer is
weak, which is the difference between an estimate and a guess.
And [the reasons are only mine](piece_the_reasons_are_only_mine.md): **forming the view was the same act as
assembling the case for it**, so the case is attached here permanently, at no cost, and by construction
rather than by any preference for one's own opinion.
At the end of it there is a claim that can be turned over, interrogated and defended, and no way at all to
hand that over at the speed the question is being asked.
