# Position: Access

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). The seat where the two inputs are made unlike, occupied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

One rule, applied to everybody: your reasons are yours and nobody else's are.
No occupant, no discretion and nothing to appeal to.
Complete control over what the weighting will come out as.

## Orders

Attach the case to whoever built it.
Detach it from everybody else.
Do this identically for the judge and the adviser, so that each is opaque to the other.

## Loses

Nothing, because it was never anybody's.
**The asymmetry is symmetric**, which is what puts it outside anybody's conduct: the adviser can no more
inspect the judge's reasons than the judge can inspect theirs, and if the roles were swapped the same
weighting would come out the other way round.

## Drives

Toward a pair of estimates that cannot be compared on the merits,
by a rule nobody wrote and nobody can suspend,
so the discounting is a property of the arrangement rather than of the people in it.
