# Process: Comparing

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). Two estimates being set against each other, one of which can be examined.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An estimate with its case attached and a number with none.
At [the access seat](position_access.md), by a rule nobody wrote, with no participants.

## Direction

Toward a weighting that favours the inspectable side.
This is not selection by anybody: it is what a comparison does when one term can be interrogated and the
other cannot, and it would come out the same way round for either party in either chair.

## Lever

None that either party holds, and no moment at which anybody decides.
The judge cannot see the reasoning that is missing, so they cannot weight it; that is not a failure of
effort and no amount of care supplies it.

## Echo

A supported claim on one side and a bare one on the other,
a weighting that is a correct reading of the pair,
and an asymmetry that swaps rather than dissolving when the roles do.
