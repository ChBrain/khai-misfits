# Pitch: They Sent Me a Number

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact about what each party sent and about what stayed behind.
Unwilling to call the judge proud or the adviser careless, because neither is required.
Naming the number wherever the number is the point.

## Undertow

Under the small revision, one property of an opinion and one property of errors.
A judgement carries its reasons for the person who made it and loses them for everybody else.
And two independent errors average closer to the truth than a typical one of them does.

## Nerve

The unease of a comparison that cannot be made and has to be made anyway.
Nobody was proud. Nobody was careless. Nobody was wrong about anybody's competence.
The move that would have paid had no case for it on the table.

## Echo

After the run: an estimate that arrived with its whole case attached,
a competent number that arrived alone because a conclusion is what was asked for,
a revision of exactly the size a person makes toward something they cannot inspect,
and a midpoint that nobody computed then or afterwards.
