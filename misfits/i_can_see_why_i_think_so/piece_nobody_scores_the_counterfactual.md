# Piece: Nobody Scores the Counterfactual

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). The judge sees what their revision did and never what the midpoint would have done.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the post-mortem](place_the_post_mortem.md), where the year is scored accurately.
At [the outcome seat](position_outcome.md), which is never asked the question that matters.

## Load Bearing

Without it experience corrects the weighting and the structure is temporary.
It is load bearing because **a counterfactual nobody computes leaves no gap in the record**: the review is
complete on its own terms, reports the truth about every decision taken, and cannot distinguish this
weighting from a better one, so a person can do the work for a career and accumulate no evidence about it.

## Apparent

That a good analyst would notice the pattern over time.
There is no pattern to notice. The comparison that would show it is not in the data, because only one of
the two estimates was ever run against the world.

## Yearbook

The structure of the paradigm itself: the revised judgement is scored, and the alternative combinations are
computed by the researchers rather than by anybody doing the job.
