# Position: Estimate

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). The seat holding a judgement and everything that produced it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A number, and the whole case for it, permanently and at no cost.
The ability to turn it over, find the weak step, and say why it landed there.
No way to hand any of that to anybody at the speed the question is being asked.

## Orders

Form the view.
Keep the reasons, which is not a decision but a property of having formed it.
Weigh anything arriving against what you can see of it.

## Loses

Nothing, and it is the seat behaving correctly throughout.
**Its advantage is not a preference for itself**: the reasons are attached because forming an opinion is
assembling them, so the asymmetry it enjoys was never chosen and cannot be given up by an act of modesty.

## Drives

Toward a comparison in which one side is inspectable and the other is not,
which is a fact about what arrived rather than about who is better,
so the weighting that follows is a correct reading of an incomplete pair.
