# Process: Advising

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). A competent colleague answering the question that was asked.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A request for a second opinion, made in good faith and meant seriously.
At [the email](place_the_email.md), within the hour, by somebody with reasons of their own.

## Direction

Toward a conclusion, delivered.
This is not a shortcut and not a discourtesy: a view is what was requested, a view is a conclusion, and
supplying the derivation would answer a different and much larger question.

## Lever

The request, which could have asked for the reasoning and would then have been asking for an analysis.
Nothing in the seat can send more without the asker having wanted more, and an asker who wanted more would
not have wanted a second opinion.

## Echo

A number that is exactly as good as the reasoning behind it,
arriving without any of it,
and indistinguishable on arrival from a number with nothing behind it at all.
