# Process: Estimating

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). A person forming a view, and thereby assembling the case for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A question worth two days of work and somebody competent to do it.
At [the desk](place_the_desk.md), before any advice is sought.

## Direction

Toward a number with a visible derivation behind it.
It succeeds. The work is good, the steps are checkable, and the person can say where the answer is weak,
which is the whole difference between an estimate and a guess.

## Lever

None that anybody would want to pull.
Forming the view without keeping the reasons is not a more modest version of this process, it is not
forming a view.

## Echo

An opinion held together with its evidence,
inspectable at any moment by exactly one person,
and no faster to transmit than it was to build.
