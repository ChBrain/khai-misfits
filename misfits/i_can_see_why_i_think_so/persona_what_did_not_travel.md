# Persona: What Did Not Travel

## Taxonomy

[Access](position_access.md). The reasoning that stayed behind, wearing the shape of a party because everything answers to its absence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Real, sound and undelivered.
Exists in full on the adviser's side of the desk.
Was not withheld, not summarised away and not thought unimportant.

## Action

Stays where it was made.
Leaves the number to travel alone.
Does the same in the other direction, so neither party can see the other's.

## Shadow

That its absence is symmetric and therefore nobody's fault.
**The adviser can no more inspect the judge's reasons than the judge can theirs**, so swapping the roles
swaps the discounting rather than removing it, and no version of either person changes the outcome.

## Tell

Is the only element in the play with no occupant.
Is the element the whole weighting is a response to.
