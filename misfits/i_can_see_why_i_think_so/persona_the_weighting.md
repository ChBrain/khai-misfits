# Persona: The Weighting

## Taxonomy

[Revision](position_revision.md). The judgement about how far to move, made honestly and on the evidence available.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Reasonable, defensible and open to argument.
Would move further if given a reason to.
Would be defended by any colleague asked to review it.

## Action

Weighs a claim it can inspect against a claim it cannot.
Moves toward the one it cannot, a distance the visible evidence supports.
Stops.

## Shadow

That the case for going further is arithmetic and was never on the table.
**Two independent errors average closer to the truth than a typical one of them**, which is a property of
errors rather than a claim about who is right, so it is a reason with nothing local in it and nothing to
cite in the meeting.

## Tell

Is the element everybody would say behaved correctly.
Is the element that cost the accuracy.
