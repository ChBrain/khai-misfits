# Persona: The One Who Already Thought About It

## Taxonomy

[Estimate](position_estimate.md). An analyst who has done the work, holds a number, and can say exactly why.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, unhurried and genuinely open to being wrong.
Asked for the second opinion themselves, and wanted it.
Rates the colleague who sent it highly, and says so without prompting.

## Action

Forms the estimate, which is the same act as assembling the case.
Reads the number that comes back.
Moves toward it, a short way, and records the revision.

## Shadow

That the short move is a reading and not a preference.
**They can inspect one of the two claims and not the other**, so moving all the way would mean acting on
the strength of reasoning they have not seen, which is a guess about something invisible rather than an act
of modesty.

## Tell

Is the party who asked for the advice.
Is the party the advice does least good to.
