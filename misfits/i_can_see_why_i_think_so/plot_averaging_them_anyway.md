# Plot: Averaging Them Anyway

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The settlements, each of them serious and one of them already written as a rule somewhere.

## Action

[Scoring](process_scoring.md): [The Score Nobody Waits For](persona_the_score_nobody_waits_for.md), in [the outcome seat](position_outcome.md), works through [Average Them and Say So](plan_average_them_and_say_so.md).

## Stage

[The Post-Mortem](place_the_post_mortem.md): every revised estimate, every outcome, an error for each, and no column for the midpoint.

## Tension

Each settlement gives back the thing it was for.
**Ask for the reasoning**, and the second opinion becomes a second piece of work, which is what an opinion
was got instead of.
**Average by rule**, and you surrender the cases where the judge really does know more, which are the cases
a judge is employed for.
**Take more advisers**, and the same discounting runs on each of them, because each arrives the same way.
**Explain the effect**, and you have asked the judge to weight something they still cannot see.
And [nobody scores the counterfactual](piece_nobody_scores_the_counterfactual.md), which is why none of this
gets settled by experience: the review is complete, honest and accurate about every decision taken, and
**only one of the two estimates was ever run against the world.**
