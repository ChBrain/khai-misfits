# Persona: The One Who Sent a Number

## Taxonomy

[Counsel](position_counsel.md). A colleague who was asked, thought about it properly, and replied.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, willing and quick.
Has reasons as good as the asker's and could give them if asked.
Would be surprised to hear that anything was missing from what they sent.

## Action

Reads the question.
Forms a view, with a case behind it.
Sends the view.

## Shadow

That sending the conclusion is what an opinion is.
**Nobody commissioned the derivation**, the request asked for a view, writing the reasoning is a different
and longer piece of work, and an opinion delivered with its full case attached is not a second opinion but a
second analysis.

## Tell

Is the party who did nothing wrong at any point.
Is the party whose contribution arrives in the one form that cannot be weighed.
