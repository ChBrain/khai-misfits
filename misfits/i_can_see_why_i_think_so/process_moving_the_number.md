# Process: Moving the Number

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). The revision itself, made honestly, and smaller than the one that would have paid.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Respect for a competent colleague and a genuine willingness to be moved.
At [the desk](place_the_desk.md), in the hour after the reply arrives.

## Direction

Toward the adviser's number, part of the way.
The distance is the distance the visible evidence supports, and a reviewer asked to check it would agree
with it, because there is nothing on the table arguing for more.

## Lever

The arithmetic, which nobody in the room can cite as being about this case.
Averaging pays because independent errors cancel, which is a property of errors rather than a claim about
either forecaster, so the argument for the midpoint has no local evidence in it at all.

## Echo

A revision everybody would defend,
a midpoint that would have been closer,
and no way to tell the two apart from inside the decision.
