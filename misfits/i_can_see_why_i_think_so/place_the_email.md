# Place: The Email

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). Where the second opinion arrives, complete and one line long.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A question asked in one sentence and answered in one.
A number, and a name, and nothing else.
A reply time short enough that asking was worth it.

## Holds

The stripping, performed by nobody.
**The reasoning did not travel because nothing asked it to**: the request wanted a view, a view is a
conclusion, and an opinion arriving with its full derivation is not a second opinion but a second analysis
and costs what one costs.

## Offers

An independent judgement, quickly, from somebody competent.

## Withheld

Everything that would make it comparable to the judgement already on the desk.
