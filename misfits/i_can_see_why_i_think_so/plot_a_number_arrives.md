# Plot: A Number Arrives

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A request for a second opinion, made in good faith and meant seriously.

## Action

[Advising](process_advising.md): [The One Who Sent a Number](persona_the_one_who_sent_a_number.md), in [the counsel seat](position_counsel.md), works through [Give Them My Best Number](plan_give_them_my_best_number.md).

## Stage

[The Email](place_the_email.md): a question asked in one sentence and answered in one, within the hour, by somebody competent.

## Tension

The second obvious answer closes here, on the adviser's competence rather than in spite of it.
They read the question, formed a view with a case behind it, and sent the view. Nothing was withheld,
nothing was summarised away, and nothing was thought unimportant.
[A number is not an argument](piece_a_number_is_not_an_argument.md), and that is not a failing: **an opinion
delivered with its full derivation is not a second opinion but a second analysis**, it costs what one costs,
and an asker who wanted that would not have asked for a view.
So what arrives is exactly as good as the reasoning behind it and carries none of it, and on arrival it is
indistinguishable from a number with nothing behind it at all.
