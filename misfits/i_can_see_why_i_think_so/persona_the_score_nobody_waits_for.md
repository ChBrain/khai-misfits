# Persona: The Score Nobody Waits For

## Taxonomy

[Outcome](position_outcome.md). The review that runs afterwards, correctly, on the wrong question.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Rigorous, timely and honest.
Scores what was decided against what happened.
Would report a bad call plainly and has done.

## Action

Takes the revised estimate.
Takes the outcome.
Reports the error, and stops, because there is nothing else recorded to compare.

## Shadow

That the comparison that would teach the lesson was never computed.
**A counterfactual nobody calculates leaves no gap in the record**, so the review is complete on its own
terms and a person can do this for a career while accumulating no evidence at all about their weighting.

## Tell

Is the element that exists to produce learning.
Is the element that guarantees this particular thing is never learned.
