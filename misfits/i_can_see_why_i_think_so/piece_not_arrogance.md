# Piece: Not Arrogance

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). The discounting survives where the judge rates the adviser highly, so it is not a verdict on anybody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the comparing](process_comparing.md) process, which runs the same way for either party in either chair.
In the judge's own account of the adviser, which is warm and accurate.

## Load Bearing

Without it the remedy is humility and the misfit dissolves into a character note.
It is load bearing because **the asymmetry is symmetric**: swap the two people and the discounting swaps
with them, so it cannot be a fact about either of them, and a judge who thought less of themselves would
still be holding one inspectable claim and one bare one.

## Apparent

That a person who valued their colleagues properly would move further.
They would move further only if they could see what they are being asked to weight. Valuing the colleague
is not the same as having their reasoning, and the play's judge does the first completely.

## Yearbook

The finding that egocentric discounting persists where the adviser's competence is known and acknowledged,
which is what separates it from a misjudgement of the adviser.
