# Piece: A Number Is Not an Argument

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). What an adviser sends is a conclusion, because a conclusion is what an opinion is and what was asked for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the email](place_the_email.md), where the reply arrives complete and one line long.
At [the counsel seat](position_counsel.md), which has not withheld anything.

## Load Bearing

Without it the adviser is terse or lazy, and the play has its second villain.
It is load bearing because **an opinion delivered with its full derivation is not a second opinion**: it is
a second analysis, it costs what one costs, and an asker who wanted that would not have asked for a view.
The stripping is what asking for an opinion consists of.

## Apparent

That the adviser could easily have written a few lines of reasoning.
A few lines is a summary, and a summary is another conclusion. What would make the number weighable is the
derivation, which is the work itself.

## Yearbook

The advice-taking paradigm: a judge holds an estimate, receives another, and revises, with the exchange
consisting of the two numbers and nothing else.
