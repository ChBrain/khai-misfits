# Plot: The Small Move

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The revision, made honestly, in the hour after the reply.

## Action

[Moving the Number](process_moving_the_number.md): [The Weighting](persona_the_weighting.md), in [the revision seat](position_revision.md), works through [Weigh Them Properly](plan_weigh_them_properly.md).

## Stage

[The Desk](place_the_desk.md): two numbers, a distance chosen between them, and a note of it in the file.

## Tension

This is the plot that has to be careful, because the revision is not a mistake anybody made.
The move is genuine, the distance is the distance the visible evidence supports, and a reviewer asked to
check it would agree, since there is nothing on the table arguing for more.
[The average would have won](piece_the_average_would_have_won.md), and the argument for it is not in the
room: two independent errors cancel against each other, which is a property of errors rather than a claim
about either forecaster, and **it holds even where the judge is the better of the two**.
So the case for the midpoint has no local evidence in it at all. It could not have been made in the meeting,
it would have sounded like splitting the difference between a right answer and a wrong one, and the person
who declined to make it was reading their own situation correctly.
