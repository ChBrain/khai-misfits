# Piece: The Reasons Are Only Mine

## Taxonomy

[I Can See Why I Think So](play_i_can_see_why_i_think_so.md). A judgement carries its reasons for the person who made it and loses them for everybody else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the desk](place_the_desk.md), where forming the view was the same act as assembling the case.
At [the access seat](position_access.md), which applies the same rule to both parties.

## Load Bearing

Without it the discounting is a preference for oneself, and the play has a villain made of vanity.
It is load bearing because **the attachment was never chosen**: an opinion is held together with what
produced it, by construction, so the judge's advantage over the adviser is a property of where the work was
done rather than an act of self-regard, and it runs in the other direction with equal force.

## Apparent

That a fair-minded person could set their own reasons aside.
Setting them aside does not supply the adviser's. It replaces a supported claim with a bare one and leaves
two bare claims, which is worse and not more modest.

## Yearbook

The differential-information account: advice is discounted because a person has access to the reasons
behind their own opinion and not to the reasons behind somebody else's.
