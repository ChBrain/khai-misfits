# Plan: Bring Something In From Outside

## Taxonomy

## Direction

Introduce an animal from outside the recorded descent, restoring variation the closed population cannot generate. Accept that the descent the register certifies is broken in the same act.

## Orders

## Targets

- [ ] admitting an outside animal removes what the rule produces rather than relaxing the rule
- [ ] the alternatives cost too: capping litters means using a worse animal, better testing sharpens the evidence again
