# Plan: Admit Only the Registered

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the closed seat](position_closed.md), inside [The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Closed](position_closed.md)

## Direction

Record parentage, verify it, and admit only the offspring of registered animals, because a register that admitted anything else would certify nothing.

## Orders

([The Studbook](persona_the_studbook.md) records, verifies, refuses everything outside the recorded descent, and holds the boundary for as long as the breed exists.)

## Implementation

([The Register](place_the_register.md), where the boundary is kept; the [closing](process_closing.md) process producing [the closed register](piece_the_closed_register.md); the pool it encloses shrinking at whatever rate the matings set.)

## Targets

- [ ] only the offspring of two registered animals are admitted
- [ ] the closure is the definition of a breed rather than a policy anybody chose
- [ ] a population receiving nothing from outside can only lose variation
- [ ] there is no relaxed version of the rule that still certifies descent
