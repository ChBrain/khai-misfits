# Persona: The Breeder

## Taxonomy

[Chooser](position_chooser.md). The one seat with a decision in it, making it on the best evidence available, which is the diligent thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Conscientious, informed, and doing exactly what the breed's health guidance asks.
Reads the published results rather than relying on hearsay, and pays for the tests.
Not chasing a fashion: the animal chosen is the one the evidence identifies.

## Action

Reads the scores, the certificates, the panels and the progeny records.
Selects the best-documented available sire.
Books one mating, which is one mating.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is the mechanism's only moving part: at the scale of one litter this is the responsible choice and the one the guidance recommends, and at the scale of a breed it is the convergence.
Because no single booking moves the effective number perceptibly, there is no point at which the seat could reasonably choose the worse animal, and nothing it could be asked to refrain from.
It ends having bred responsibly, and having contributed to a breed with fewer ancestors than it had.

## Tell

Says: he is the best one available and he is clear on everything.
He is. That is the whole account and there is nothing else in it.
