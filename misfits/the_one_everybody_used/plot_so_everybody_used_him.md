# Plot: So Everybody Used Him

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

One stud clear on every test that exists, with a progeny record to match.
A hundred breeders reading the same published results, separately, none of them consulting the others.

## Action

[Converging](process_converging.md): [The Breeder](persona_the_breeder.md), in [the chooser seat](position_chooser.md), selects and books, a hundred times over, at [the litter](place_the_litter.md).

## Stage

[The Breeder](persona_the_breeder.md), on [Use the Best One I Can Find](plan_use_the_best_one_i_can_find.md), reads the evidence, selects the best-documented available sire and books one mating.
[The Sire](persona_the_sire.md), on [Be Available to Everyone Who Asks](plan_be_available_to_everyone_who_asks.md), is used once by each of them.

## Tension

No copying is required, and that is the plot the whole play turns on.
Give every breeder complete independence: no knowledge of anybody else's choice, no fashion, no reputation, no watching each other. **They still converge**, because the evidence is public and correct and there is one best answer in it, so a hundred people who never speak arrive where a hundred imitators would and arrive faster. This is not herding and cannot be corrected by telling anybody to think for themselves, since thinking for themselves is what produces it. And nobody overuses him: each breeder uses him once, which is the only quantity any of them controls, and the aggregate belongs to nobody who arranged a mating.
