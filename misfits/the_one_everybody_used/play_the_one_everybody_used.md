# Play: The One Everybody Used

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The One Everybody Used.
A stud is examined, scored, X-rayed, DNA tested against every variant anybody has found, and shown. He comes through it all clear. His progeny are recorded and they come through it too. So he is not merely the one everybody liked: he is the one the evidence identifies, and a breeder who consults the evidence and uses him is doing the diligent thing rather than the lazy one.
Then everybody consults the same evidence, because it is published, and everybody arrives at the same animal.

## Arc

The bend is this: a closed population loses variation in proportion to how few individuals contribute, and how few contribute is decided by how well the best one can be identified.
It runs on two facts, neither of them anybody's doing: a breed is defined by descent from registered ancestors, so nothing comes in; and a dog can sire a great many litters while a bitch cannot.
Take the register first. Closing the stud book is what makes a breed a breed. There is no version of a registry that admits outside animals and still certifies descent, so the boundary is not an oversight and cannot be relaxed without giving up the thing the register exists to record.
Then take the evidence. Hip scores, eye tests, cardiologist certificates, DNA panels, progeny records, published results: this is the machinery built to raise the health of the breed, it works, and it does exactly what it was designed to do, which is tell a breeder which animal is the best bet. **Every improvement to it sharpens the answer.**
And a sharper answer is a narrower one. When nobody could tell which stud was better, the matings scattered across many of them, and the breed's ancestry stayed broad by accident. When everybody can tell, the matings concentrate, and the breed's ancestry narrows by diligence. So a pedigree analysis of ten breeds from a national register, eight generations deep, put the inbreeding effective population size at **between forty and eighty** for all but two of them: a breed with tens of thousands of living dogs behaving genetically like a population of fifty. And across all two hundred and fifteen breeds of that register over thirty-four years, the rate of inbreeding **was not correlated with how many dogs there were.** The census count reassures and means nothing.
Then the part nobody can see at the time. A test exists only for a variant somebody has already found. A carrier of a variant nobody has found is indistinguishable from a clear dog by every instrument in the room, including the ones that just certified him. So the same use that spreads his merit spreads whatever else he has, and both arrive together, and only one of them is visible for another two generations.
Reach for a settlement and there is one, and it costs the register. Cap the number of litters a sire may have, and you are telling breeders to use an animal they can see is worse. Bring in an outside dog, and the descent the register certifies is broken, which is the register's whole function. Test for more variants, and you have improved the evidence again, which is the thing that concentrates the choice. And the rates did fall after 2000, which is real and is worth saying, and they fell by breeders choosing to spread the matings rather than by any of the difficulty going away.

## Triggers

**[Only the Registered](plot_only_the_registered.md)**
A breed defined as the descendants of a recorded set of animals, and a register that admits nothing else. Closes when the closure is established as the definition rather than a policy, since a register that admitted outside animals would certify nothing.

**[Now You Can Tell Which One Is Best](plot_now_you_can_tell_which_one_is_best.md)**
Cue: the health machinery working. Hip scores, eye tests, cardiac certificates, DNA panels, progeny records, all published. Closes when the evidence is shown to be sound, useful and doing precisely what it was built to do, which is identify the best animal.

**[So Everybody Used Him](plot_so_everybody_used_him.md)**
Cue: one stud clear on every test, with the progeny record to match, and a hundred breeders reading the same published results. Closes when the convergence is shown to require no copying, no fashion and no laziness, only each breeder independently reading the evidence correctly.

**[Fifty Ancestors](plot_fifty_ancestors.md)**
Cue: the pedigree analysis, eight generations deep. Inbreeding effective population size between forty and eighty for all but two of ten breeds, and across all two hundred and fifteen breeds the rate of inbreeding uncorrelated with the number of dogs. Closes when the census count is shown to carry no information about the thing everybody uses it to reassure themselves with.

**[Two Carriers Meet](plot_two_carriers_meet.md)**
Cue: a litter, two or three generations on, with a disorder in it that no test in the world could have predicted, because the test for that variant did not exist when the mating was arranged. Closes when the invisibility is shown to be structural: a test exists only for a variant already found, so the best-certified animal is certified against the known list and against nothing else.

## Stakes

Whether a closed population can be improved without being narrowed.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether any breeder chooses badly, because every choice in the play is the informed one and the machinery producing the information is doing its job, but whether better evidence about which individual is best can fail to concentrate the choice on that individual. The production cannot find a way. It can only show that closing the register is what makes a breed a breed, that the health machinery works and each improvement to it sharpens the answer, that a sharper answer is a narrower one and the narrowing needs no copying and no fashion, that the resulting effective number bears no relation to how many dogs there are, that whatever the best animal carries and nobody has yet named travels with his merit and arrives two generations later, and that every remedy is a decision either to use a worse animal on purpose or to stop certifying descent.
