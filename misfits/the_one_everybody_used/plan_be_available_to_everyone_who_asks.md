# Plan: Be Available to Everyone Who Asks

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the chosen seat](position_chosen.md), inside [The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md).

## Owner

## Direction

Come through every test, and be used by whoever asks. Contribute a whole genome each time, the part that was certified and the part that was not looked at.

## Orders

([The Sire](persona_the_sire.md) is tested and passes, is used once by each of a hundred breeders rather than overused by any one of them, and contributes his whole genome to each.)

([The Litter](place_the_litter.md), a hundred times over; [concentrating](process_concentrating.md) running against [the effective number](piece_the_effective_number.md); [the carried variant](piece_the_carried_variant.md) travelling with the merit.)
