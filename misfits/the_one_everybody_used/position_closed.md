# Position: Closed

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). The seat that admits only recorded descent, which is what a breed register is for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Parentage records, verified, across generations.
A definition of the breed as the descendants of a recorded set.
No mechanism for admitting anything else, because admitting anything else would certify nothing.

## Orders

Record.
Verify.
Refuse everything outside the recorded descent.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat supplies the condition the whole structure needs: a population that receives nothing from outside can only lose variation, at whatever rate the matings inside it set.
Because the closure is the definition rather than a policy, there is no relaxed version of this seat that is still this seat.
It ends having recorded faithfully, over a pool that shrinks.

## Drives

Toward an accurate record,
never once at fault,
so the seat that makes the breed possible is the one that makes the loss irreversible.
