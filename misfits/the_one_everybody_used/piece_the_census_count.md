# Piece: The Census Count

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). How many dogs of the breed there are: the number everybody reassures themselves with, and it carries no information.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the register, as annual registrations.
Answered nowhere, because nobody thinks to check what it predicts.

## Load Bearing

Without it the concentration would be obvious and somebody would have acted sooner.
It is load bearing because of a measured fact: across all two hundred and fifteen breeds of a national register from 1980 to 2014, **the rate of inbreeding was not correlated with census population size**, so a breed being numerous is not evidence of anything about its genetics.

## Apparent

Reassurance, and it is the natural place to look.
It reads as the obvious measure of a population's health, and it is the wrong one, which is precisely why the finding had to be measured rather than assumed.

## Yearbook

Recorded alongside [the effective number](piece_the_effective_number.md) and unrelated to it.
It is what makes [concentrating](process_concentrating.md) invisible without a pedigree analysis.
