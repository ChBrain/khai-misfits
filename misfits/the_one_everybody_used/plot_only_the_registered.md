# Plot: Only the Registered

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A breed defined as the descendants of a recorded set of animals.
[The Closed Register](piece_the_closed_register.md), admitting nothing else.

## Action

[Closing](process_closing.md): [The Studbook](persona_the_studbook.md), in [the closed seat](position_closed.md), records, verifies and refuses.

## Stage

[The Studbook](persona_the_studbook.md), on [Admit Only the Registered](plan_admit_only_the_registered.md), admits the offspring of two registered animals and nothing else, faithfully, for as long as the breed exists.

## Tension

The closure is the definition and not a policy, which is what makes everything after it structural.
Nobody imposed this on the breed and nobody is defending it out of tradition: a register that admitted outside animals would not be a laxer register, it would be a different kind of document, certifying nothing. So the population is closed by the same act that makes it a population, and **variation inside it can be redistributed and cannot be replenished**. Everything that follows is a question of how fast, and by whose decisions.
