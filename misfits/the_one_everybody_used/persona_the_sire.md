# Persona: The Sire

## Taxonomy

[Chosen](position_chosen.md). The animal the evidence identifies: clear on every test that exists, with the progeny record behind it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Genuinely good, and not merely fashionable.
Passed every examination, scored well, and produced offspring who did the same.
Does nothing at all, being a dog: the seat is occupied rather than played.

## Action

Is tested, and comes through.
Is used, by whoever asks, which is a great many people.
Contributes his whole genome to each of them, the certified part and the rest.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is where the concentration physically happens: he is not overused by any single breeder, he is used once by each of a hundred of them, and the aggregate belongs to nobody.
Because a male can sire a great many litters while a female cannot, the same popularity in a bitch would be arithmetically impossible, so the asymmetry is a fact about biology rather than about anybody's preference.
It ends the best-documented animal of his generation and a large share of the next one's ancestry.

## Tell

Says nothing.
His entry in the register says it: a column of dates, and a column of litters, longer than anybody else's.
