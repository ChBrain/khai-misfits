# Persona: The Health Scheme

## Taxonomy

[Evaluation](position_evaluation.md). The machinery that makes merit legible: scores, certificates, DNA panels, progeny records, published.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Well designed, well run and genuinely effective.
Built by people trying to reduce inherited disease in the breed, and it does reduce it.
Nothing complacent in it: it is extended whenever a new variant is characterised.

## Action

Examines, scores, tests and records.
Publishes, so that any breeder can read the results rather than rely on reputation.
Improves, and each improvement makes the best animal easier to identify.

## Shadow

Nothing, and it is the engine: the seat's whole purpose is to answer the question which animal is the best bet, and answering it better is what narrows the set of animals used.
Because a sharper answer is a narrower one, the seat's success and the breed's concentration are the same quantity, and the seat has no version of itself that informs without discriminating.
It ends having raised the health of the breed and reduced the number of dogs it descends from.

## Tell

Says: hips scored, eyes clear, panel clear, progeny followed.
All true, all useful, and all read by everybody at once.
