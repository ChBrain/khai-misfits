# Process: Converging

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). Every informed breeder independently arriving at the same sire.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Published results and a mandate to use them.
At [the ring](place_the_ring.md), read separately by people who do not consult each other.

## Direction

Toward the best-documented animal, from every seat at once.
The step that is not what it looks like is this one: **no copying is required**. Give each breeder complete independence, no knowledge of anybody else's choice, no fashion and no reputation, and they still converge, because the evidence is public and correct and there is one best answer in it.
So this is not herding, and it cannot be corrected by telling anybody to think for themselves. Thinking for themselves is what produces it.

## Lever

The correctness of the evidence, which is the input.
Because the convergence is driven by the answer being right rather than by anybody watching anybody, improving the evidence strengthens it.

## Echo

One animal used by a great many people, none of whom overused him.
The aggregate belongs to nobody and no participant can see it from where they sit.
