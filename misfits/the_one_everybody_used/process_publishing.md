# Process: Publishing

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). Merit being measured and made public, better each year.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A mandate to reduce inherited disease.
At [the ring](place_the_ring.md), as scores, certificates, panels and progeny records.

## Direction

Toward a breeder who can tell which animal is the best bet.
The machinery is sound and it works: prevalence of screened disorders does fall where schemes are followed, and the answer to the question it was built to answer gets more precise every time a variant is characterised or a scheme extended.
And a more precise answer is a narrower one, so **the improvement and the concentration are the same event**, and no design of the scheme separates them.

## Lever

Information, which is the one thing the seat produces.
Because its usefulness is exactly its power to discriminate between animals, there is no setting at which it helps and does not narrow.

## Echo

A hundred breeders holding the same correct answer.
The seat has done its job perfectly and has told everybody the same thing.
