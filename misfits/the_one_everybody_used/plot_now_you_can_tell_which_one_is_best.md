# Plot: Now You Can Tell Which One Is Best

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The health machinery working: hip scores, eye tests, cardiac certificates, DNA panels, progeny records, published.
[The Published Result](piece_the_published_result.md), accurate and freely available.

## Action

[Publishing](process_publishing.md): [The Health Scheme](persona_the_health_scheme.md), in [the evaluation seat](position_evaluation.md), measures and makes public, and gets better at it every year.

## Stage

[The Health Scheme](persona_the_health_scheme.md), on [Tell Them Which One Is Best](plan_tell_them_which_one_is_best.md), extends the panel whenever a new variant is characterised and publishes so that breeders read results rather than reputation.
[The Sire](persona_the_sire.md), in [the chosen seat](position_chosen.md), is tested against all of it and comes through clear.

## Tension

The scheme is good, it works, and every improvement to it sharpens the answer.
There is nothing to criticise here and the play does not try. Prevalence of screened disorders does fall where the schemes are followed; that is what they were built for and it is what they do. The whole difficulty is contained in a sentence that reads like praise: **the better the evidence, the more precisely it identifies one animal.** A scheme that could not discriminate between sires would be a useless scheme, so there is no version of this seat that helps and does not narrow.
