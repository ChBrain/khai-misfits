# Persona: The Studbook

## Taxonomy

[Closed](position_closed.md). The register that admits only the descendants of registered animals, which is what makes a breed a breed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, well kept and entirely straightforward about what it is.
Records parentage, verifies it, and certifies that an animal descends from a recorded set.
Not defensive: the closure is the definition rather than a policy anybody chose to defend.

## Action

Admits the offspring of two registered animals.
Refuses everything else, which is the only way the certification means anything.
Holds the boundary across generations, faithfully, for as long as the breed exists.

## Shadow

Nothing, and it supplies the condition everything else needs: a population that cannot receive anything from outside can only lose variation, at whatever rate the matings inside it set.
Because a register admitting outside animals would certify nothing, there is no relaxed version of this seat that is still this seat.
It ends having recorded exactly what it promised to record, over a pool that gets smaller.

## Tell

Says: both parents registered.
Which is true, is the whole of its function, and is the reason nothing new can arrive.
