# Persona: The Outcross

## Taxonomy

[Reopening](position_reopening.md). The remedy that works: an animal from outside, and the end of the certification that made the breed a breed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Effective, and not controversial as biology.
Brings variation the closed population cannot generate for itself, immediately and in quantity.
Nothing wrong with the animal: the difficulty is entirely about the register.

## Action

Arrives from outside the recorded descent.
Restores variation, genuinely and at once.
Breaks the descent the register exists to certify, in the same act.

## Shadow

Nothing of its own, and its cost is the play's honest bound: the register's whole function is to record that an animal descends from a recorded set, so admitting one that does not is not a relaxation of the rule but the removal of the thing the rule produces.
Because the alternatives are equally structural, capping a sire's litters means instructing breeders to use a demonstrably worse animal and testing for more variants means sharpening the evidence again, there is no remedy here that costs nothing.
It ends having fixed the genetics and unsettled the definition.

## Tell

Says: this is the same breed, with something else in it.
Which is a sentence the register has no way to write.
