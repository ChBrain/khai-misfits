# Place: The Litter

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). Where the choice becomes animals, and where whatever was not tested for eventually appears.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Puppies, from one mating arranged on the best available evidence.
Repeated, a hundred times over, by a hundred people who never spoke to each other.

## Holds

Both halves of what was transmitted, arriving together and becoming visible at different times.
The certified merit shows at once and is confirmed; whatever else was carried shows two or three generations later, in a litter belonging to somebody who did everything correctly.

## Offers

Healthy puppies out of a well-documented sire, which is what the evidence predicted and usually what arrives.

## Withheld

The timing.
Nothing in a litter announces what it carries, so the interval between the mating and the consequence is longer than the interval between the mating and the next mating.
