# Piece: The Published Result

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). The score, the certificate, the panel and the progeny record: correct, public, and read by everybody at once.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Produced at [the ring](place_the_ring.md), by a scheme that works.
Answered nowhere: it is accurate, and its accuracy is the difficulty.

## Load Bearing

Without it breeders choose on reputation, the matings scatter, and the breed stays broad by accident.
It is load bearing because it makes the convergence **independent rather than imitative**: the evidence is public and there is one best answer in it, so a hundred people who never speak converge as fast as a hundred who copy, and telling anybody to think for themselves changes nothing.

## Apparent

A breed health programme working, and it is one.
It reads as unambiguously good, which it is, and the fact that every improvement to it narrows the breed further does not appear on any certificate.
