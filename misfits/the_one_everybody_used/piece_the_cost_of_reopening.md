# Piece: The Cost of Reopening

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). What an outcross spends: the certified descent that the register exists to record.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the register](place_the_register.md), which has no way to enter it.
Answered nowhere: the biology is uncontroversial and the bookkeeping is the whole difficulty.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play has a free remedy and no misfit.
It is load bearing because it makes every exit structural: outcrossing removes what the register produces rather than relaxing a rule, capping a sire's litters instructs breeders to use an animal they can see is worse, and extending the panel improves the evidence, which is the thing that concentrates the choice.

## Apparent

Controversy about tradition, or resistance from breed clubs.
It reads as a cultural objection, and the objection that actually binds is definitional: a register cannot certify descent from a set an animal does not descend from.

## Yearbook

Weighed against [the closed register](piece_the_closed_register.md) that made the loss one-way.
It is the reason the rates that did fall after 2000 fell by spreading matings rather than by any of this being resolved.
