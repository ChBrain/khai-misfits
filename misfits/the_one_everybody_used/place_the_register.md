# Place: The Register

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). Where descent is recorded and the boundary is kept, which is what makes a breed a breed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Entries, parentage, dates, and a rule admitting only the offspring of registered animals.
A record maintained faithfully across generations.

## Holds

The condition the whole structure rests on, and the reason the loss cannot be undone from inside.
It holds the fact that closure is not a policy but a definition: a register admitting outside animals would certify nothing, so there is no version of it that keeps its function and lets anything in.

## Offers

A verifiable account of what an animal is descended from, which is the entire value of a pedigree.

## Withheld

Any route in.
Nothing can arrive here that was not already here, so the pool can be redistributed and cannot be replenished, and every remedy that adds variation has to be recorded somewhere else.
