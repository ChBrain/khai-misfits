# Plan: Use the Best One I Can Find

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the chooser seat](position_chooser.md), inside [The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Chooser](position_chooser.md)

## Direction

Read the published results, select the best-documented available sire, and book the mating. Do the diligent thing rather than the convenient one, which is what the guidance asks and what the evidence supports.

## Orders

([The Breeder](persona_the_breeder.md) reads scores, certificates, panels and progeny records, selects the best-documented sire, and books one mating.)

## Implementation

([The Ring](place_the_ring.md), where the evidence is read; [converging](process_converging.md) toward the same animal as everybody else, independently; the result appearing only in [the effective number](piece_the_effective_number.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the choice is made on published evidence rather than on reputation
- [ ] the animal selected genuinely is the best-documented available
- [ ] no single booking moves the effective number perceptibly
- [ ] the aggregate of the bookings is the convergence, and nobody arranged it
