# Piece: The Closed Register

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). The rule admitting only recorded descent: the definition of a breed, and the seal on the pool.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the register](place_the_register.md), from the breed's founding.
Answered nowhere: a register admitting outside animals certifies nothing.

## Load Bearing

Without it variation lost inside can be replaced from outside and there is no misfit.
It is load bearing because it is **constitutive rather than regulatory**: nobody imposed it on the breed and nobody can relax it, since relaxing it does not produce a laxer register but a different kind of document.

## Apparent

A verifiable pedigree, which is the entire value of the thing.
It reads as the guarantee, and its being the reason the loss is one-way is not visible from any entry in it.

## Yearbook

Sealed by [closing](process_closing.md), and the condition [concentrating](process_concentrating.md) acts inside.
It is what [the cost of reopening](piece_the_cost_of_reopening.md) has to be weighed against.
