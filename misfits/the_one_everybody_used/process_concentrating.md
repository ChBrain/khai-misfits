# Process: Concentrating

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). The effective number of ancestors falling away from the number of animals.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A great many matings to one sire.
At [the litter](place_the_litter.md), multiplied across a breed and a generation.

## Direction

Toward a genetic population far smaller than the visible one.
The measurement is specific: a pedigree analysis of ten breeds from a national register, eight generations deep, found extremely inbred animals in every breed except one and put the inbreeding effective population size at **between forty and eighty** for all but two of them.
And the reassuring number is the wrong one: across all two hundred and fifteen breeds of that register over the period from 1980 to 2014, the rate of inbreeding was **not correlated with census population size**, so how many dogs there are says nothing about how much variation is being kept.

## Lever

The distribution of matings across sires, which nobody sets and everybody contributes to.
Because effective size depends on how evenly the contributions are spread rather than on how many animals exist, a large breed used unevenly behaves like a small one.

## Echo

A breed of tens of thousands with the genetics of about fifty.
The rate did fall after 2000, which is real, and it fell by breeders spreading the matings rather than by the mechanism weakening.
