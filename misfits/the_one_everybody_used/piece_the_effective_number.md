# Piece: The Effective Number

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). The genetic size of the breed: forty to eighty, against a census in the tens of thousands.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Computed from [the register](place_the_register.md), eight generations deep.
Answered nowhere in any single mating, since it is a property of the distribution.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a worry rather than a finding.
It is load bearing because it puts a number on the gap: a pedigree analysis of ten breeds found extremely inbred animals in every one but the greyhound and an inbreeding effective population size **between forty and eighty** for all but two, with popular sires identified as the major contributor to the rate.

## Apparent

A technical parameter from population genetics.
It reads as a modelling quantity rather than as a statement about how many ancestors a living breed actually has, which is what it is.

## Yearbook

Produced by [concentrating](process_concentrating.md), against [the census count](piece_the_census_count.md) which does not predict it.
It depends on how evenly matings are spread, which is what [converging](process_converging.md) decides.
