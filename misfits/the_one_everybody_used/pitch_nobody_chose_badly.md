# Pitch: Nobody Chose Badly

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry, clerical, procedural.
The register of a stud book beside a health certificate: who is entered, what he scored, how many litters, whose progeny were followed up.
Nothing indignant anywhere in it, because every decision in the play is the informed one and the information is good.

## Undertow

Under the pedigree, two facts that do not negotiate.
A breed is descent from a recorded set, so nothing comes in. A dog can sire a great many litters and a bitch cannot.
Between those two, the quality of the evidence decides how many ancestors a breed has, and better evidence means fewer.

## Nerve

The unease of watching a health scheme work exactly as designed,
be consulted exactly as intended,
and narrow the thing it was built to protect, a little further with every improvement to it.

## Echo

After the run: a breed of tens of thousands of dogs with the genetics of about fifty,
a stud certified clear against every variant anybody had found by the year he was used,
and a litter three generations later with something in it that no test in the world could have named at the time.
