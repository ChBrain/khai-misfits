# Plot: Two Carriers Meet

## Taxonomy

[The One Everybody Used](play_the_one_everybody_used.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A litter, two or three generations on, with a disorder in it.
Every test at the time was clear, and every test at the time was correct.

## Action

[Surfacing](process_surfacing.md): [The Untested Variant](persona_the_untested_variant.md), in [the unseen seat](position_unseen.md), becomes common enough to meet itself.

## Stage

[The Untested Variant](persona_the_untested_variant.md), on [Be Carried Without Being Looked For](plan_be_carried_without_being_looked_for.md), was carried silently through every certification and transmitted with everything else.
[The Outcross](persona_the_outcross.md), in [the reopening seat](position_reopening.md), is now the measure that would restore what was lost.

## Tension

Nothing was missed, and the certification was never the claim people read it as.
**A test exists only for a variant somebody has already characterised.** So an animal clear on every existing panel is clear on every existing panel, which is a smaller statement than it sounds and is the only statement anybody made. The merit and this travelled together at the same rate, and only one of them was visible at the time, so no diligence available then could have separated them, and the interval before the consequence is longer than the interval before the next mating. And every exit costs: outcrossing removes what the register produces rather than relaxing a rule, capping litters instructs breeders to use an animal they can see is worse, and characterising the new variant adds a test to the panel, which improves the evidence, which sharpens the answer, **which is where the play began.**
