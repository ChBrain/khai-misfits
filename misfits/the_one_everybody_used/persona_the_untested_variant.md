# Persona: The Untested Variant

## Taxonomy

[Unseen](position_unseen.md). Whatever the certified animal carries that nobody has yet found, and therefore nobody can test for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not hidden, not concealed and not missed by carelessness.
Recessive, so a single copy shows nothing at all in the animal carrying it.
Invisible to every instrument in the room, including the ones that have just certified him.

## Action

Is carried, silently.
Is transmitted with everything else, to a large share of a generation.
Waits, until it is common enough that two carriers are mated, which takes two or three generations.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is why the evidence cannot save the case: a test exists only for a variant somebody has already characterised, so certification is against the known list and against nothing else.
Because the same use that spreads the merit spreads this, and only the merit is visible at the time, the two cannot be separated by any amount of diligence available then.
It ends common, named at last, and named after the breed.

## Tell

Says nothing for two generations.
Then says it in a litter, all at once, to somebody who did everything correctly.
