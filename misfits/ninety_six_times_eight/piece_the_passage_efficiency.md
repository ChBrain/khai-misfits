## Load Bearing

It is excellent, and being excellent is exactly what makes it a fraction.
Measured across fifteen years, eight large dams and nearly twenty-seven thousand radio-tagged salmon and steelhead, the average tailrace-to-forebay figure at a single dam is about ninety-seven per cent, with the authors describing it as among the highest recorded for any migratory species and crediting sustained adaptive management of design and maintenance. There is no weak link here to be found and removed. The number is what a very good structure achieves after decades of deliberate improvement, and it is still a number less than one.
