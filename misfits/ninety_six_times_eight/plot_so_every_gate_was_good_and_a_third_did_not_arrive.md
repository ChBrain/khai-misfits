# Plot: So Every Gate Was Good and a Third Did Not Arrive

## Taxonomy

[Ninety-Six Times Eight](play_ninety_six_times_eight.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A season's count, written on a board.

## Action

[The Counter](persona_the_counter.md) records what came past, and [the fishery manager](persona_the_fishery_manager.md) closes the season early.

## Stage

A river of correct structures, and a shortfall with nobody's name on it.

## Tension

Every seat performed correctly and the outcome is the sum of the correct performances.
The engineer built one of the best passage structures in the world and has no lever on a product across eight. The operator meets every condition on the only licence it holds. The regulator enforces at the only scale its powers reach, which is the scale ownership comes in. The counter produces the one number that describes the run and can attribute none of it, because the difference accumulated rather than happening anywhere. The fishery manager absorbs the whole of it with the only lever available, which is to take less. What is left is not a fault to allocate but a river in which every gate was a good gate.
