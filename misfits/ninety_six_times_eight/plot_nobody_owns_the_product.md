# Plot: Nobody Owns the Product

## Taxonomy

[Ninety-Six Times Eight](play_ninety_six_times_eight.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A licence renewal, for one structure.

## Action

[The Regulator](persona_the_regulator.md) sets a condition, checks it, and renews; [the permit](piece_the_permit.md) describes this dam and no other.

## Stage

A deciding quantity that appears in no instrument anybody holds.

## Tension

The per-structure scope follows from ownership rather than from any failure of drafting.
A licence attaches to a structure because a structure is what a party applies for, operates, and can be held to. A condition about the whole river would fall on parties who are not applicants in that proceeding and could not be enforced against any one of them, and renewals arrive decades apart from different owners. So there is no seat that could be asked to answer for the product without being asked to answer for somebody else's asset, and no seat is asked.
