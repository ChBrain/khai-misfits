# Persona: The Fishery Manager

## Taxonomy

[Fishery Manager](position_fishery_manager.md). The one who sets a harvest against what gets through.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A manager with an escapement target, a treaty or allocation, a fleet waiting and a forecast built on last year.

## Action

Sets the season from the escapement.
Closes it early when the count comes in short.

## Shadow

Absorbs the whole shortfall with the only instrument it holds.
The seat cannot raise passage at any dam, cannot alter a licence, and cannot make the river shorter. What it can do is take less, so the arithmetic of eight structures arrives at this seat as a closed season and a fleet tied up. The people who lose the fishing did not build anything, own anything or permit anything, and the reduction they accept is the only place in the system where the product is answered at all.

## Tell

Pays the product with the one lever that has nothing to do with it.
