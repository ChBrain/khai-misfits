# Persona: The Fishway Engineer

## Taxonomy

[Fishway Engineer](position_fishway_engineer.md). The one who builds the structure and keeps improving it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A designer with hydraulic models, entrance attraction flows, decades of monitoring data and a standard to beat.

## Action

Designs the ladder, evaluates it against tagged fish, and changes it when the data says to.
Raises the figure year after year.

## Shadow

Produces one of the best passage structures ever built, and the run still loses a third.
The seat is not falling short of anything: the figure achieved is among the highest recorded for any migratory species anywhere, and it was reached by sustained adaptive management rather than by luck. The seat has no lever on the number that matters, because that number is a property of the river and the seat is responsible for a structure. Raising ninety-seven to ninety-nine at this dam is difficult, expensive and worth doing, and it does not change the fact that the quantity in question is a product over eight of them.

## Tell

Beats the standard and cannot reach the quantity.
