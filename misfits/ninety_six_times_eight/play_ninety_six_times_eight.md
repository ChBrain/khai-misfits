# Play: Ninety-Six Times Eight

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Ninety-Six Times Eight.
A dam stops a migration, so a fishway is required, designed, built, monitored and improved for decades. It works: across fifteen years, eight large dams and nearly twenty-seven thousand tagged fish, the average passage efficiency at a single dam is about ninety-seven per cent, which is among the highest ever recorded for any migratory species. Every structure in the river is excellent. The run has to cross all of them.

## Arc

The bend is this: excellence is a property of each structure and arrival is a property of all of them, so a river of individually excellent gates is not an excellent river.
It begins with a requirement everybody agrees on.
[A Dam Needs a Ladder, and the Ladder Is Built Well](plot_a_dam_needs_a_ladder_and_the_ladder_is_built_well.md). [The Fishway Engineer](persona_the_fishway_engineer.md) designs to a standard and then keeps improving it.
And it passes nearly all of them.
[And It Passes Nearly All of Them](plot_and_it_passes_nearly_all_of_them.md). [The Passage Efficiency](piece_the_passage_efficiency.md) is measured, honest and very high.
So the number is among the best ever recorded.
[So the Number Is Among the Best Ever Recorded](plot_so_the_number_is_among_the_best_ever_recorded.md). There is no weak link to find, which is what makes this not a story about a bad ladder.
And [the run crosses eight of them](plot_and_the_run_crosses_eight_of_them.md).
[The Product](piece_the_product.md) is what those numbers do in series, and it is not a number anybody designed.
Then [nobody owns the product](plot_nobody_owns_the_product.md). [The Permit](piece_the_permit.md) describes one structure, and every operator meets the one they hold.
And [every gate was good and a third did not arrive](plot_so_every_gate_was_good_and_a_third_did_not_arrive.md). [The Counter](persona_the_counter.md) writes the number on the board.

## Stakes

Whether a run that has to pass every gate can be protected by rules written one gate at a time.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a fishway obligation nobody disputes, a per-structure figure among the highest ever recorded, an absence of any weak link to find, an arithmetic of series that no single structure performs, a permit that describes one dam, and a count on a board at the top of the river, and away from any account in which somebody built a bad ladder or an operator cut a corner. The production cannot fault the engineer, whose structure is better than almost any comparable one anywhere; and it cannot fault the operator or the regulator, each of whom meets and enforces the instrument they actually hold. What is left to fight over is not whether anybody erred but what to call a river in which every gate was a good gate.
