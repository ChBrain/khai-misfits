# Plot: And It Passes Nearly All of Them

## Taxonomy

[Ninety-Six Times Eight](play_ninety_six_times_eight.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A radio tag entering a tailrace.

## Action

[The Passage Efficiency](piece_the_passage_efficiency.md) is measured: about ninety-seven in every hundred reach the forebay above the dam.

## Stage

A figure established over fifteen years rather than claimed.

## Tension

The number is high enough to look like an answer, and it is a fraction.
Across eight large dams, fifteen years and nearly twenty-seven thousand tagged salmon and steelhead, the average tailrace-to-forebay figure sits at about ninety-seven per cent. Read as a pass mark it settles the question at this dam, and read as what it is, a proportion less than one, it is the beginning of a different question entirely. Which of the two readings is correct depends on how many dams there are, which is not a fact about this dam.
