# Persona: The Counter

## Taxonomy

[Counter](position_counter.md). The one at the window who sees what actually arrived.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A biologist at a counting window near the top of the system, with a tally sheet, a season, and a view of the ladder.

## Action

Counts what comes past.
Records it, publishes it, and compares it with the count downriver.

## Shadow

Holds the only number that describes the run, and it explains nothing about any structure.
The seat is the first place in the whole system where the quantity that matters is visible, and it arrives with no attribution attached. The count is lower than the count eight dams down and there is no line in it saying where the difference went, because the difference did not go anywhere in particular: it accumulated. So the seat produces the evidence that something is wrong and cannot produce evidence that anything is at fault, and those are different findings that look alike on a board.

## Tell

Records the harm and can attribute none of it.
