# Plot: A Dam Needs a Ladder, and the Ladder Is Built Well

## Taxonomy

[Ninety-Six Times Eight](play_ninety_six_times_eight.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A barrier across a migration, and an obligation to get the fish past it.

## Action

[The Fishway Engineer](persona_the_fishway_engineer.md) designs, builds and then spends decades improving a structure at [the ladder](place_the_ladder.md).

## Stage

An obligation nobody disputes, met seriously and at length.

## Tension

This is the part everybody agrees about, and it is where the mechanism starts.
A dam without passage ends a migration, so passage is required, and the requirement has been taken seriously: hydraulic modelling, entrance attraction flows, tagged-fish evaluation, and revision on evidence over decades. Nothing in this play argues that fishways are a bad idea or that the people building them are unserious. The opposite is what makes it work as a misfit: the structures are good, the effort is real, and the difficulty survives all of it.
