## Tension

There is no weak link here to find, which is what the usual account of a system like this needs.
The familiar story about a chain of structures is that one of them is bad, and that finding and fixing it restores the whole. That story is unavailable. These figures are not merely adequate: they are at the top of what has been achieved for any migratory species, and they got there through decades of deliberate revision rather than by accident. Any account that goes looking for the weak dam is looking for something the evidence says is not there.
