## Place

## Load Bearing

Its scope is set by who owns what, and what the run needs is not owned by anybody.
A licence attaches to a structure because a structure is what a party applies for, operates and can be held to. A condition written about a whole river would fall on parties who are not applicants in that proceeding, could not be enforced against any one of them, and would arrive at each owner decades apart as their own renewals came up. So the per-structure scope is not a drafting failure and not a captured regulator: it follows from the fact that rivers are crossed by many owners and instruments run against one owner at a time.
