# Persona: The Dam Operator

## Taxonomy

[Dam Operator](position_dam_operator.md). The one who runs one dam and meets its licence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An operator with a generating licence, spill obligations, a fishway to maintain and a compliance record to keep.

## Action

Runs the fishway to specification, funds the monitoring, and reports the numbers.
Meets every condition attached to this structure.

## Shadow

Is fully compliant with an instrument that cannot describe the harm.
The seat is not evading anything: the conditions on its licence concern this dam, it meets them, and the figures it reports are good and are published. There is no clause it could satisfy that would address the product, because the product is a property of a river with several owners and this seat owns one structure. Asking it to answer for the other seven is asking it to answer for somebody else's asset, and no seat in the system is asked to answer for all eight.

## Tell

Complies completely, with the only thing there is to comply with.
