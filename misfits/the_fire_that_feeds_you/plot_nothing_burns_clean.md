# Plot: Nothing Burns Clean

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The fire lit and running, every beat and repair and thought drawn from it.

## Action

At [the Hearth](place_the_hearth.md), [the Body](persona_the_body.md) meets [the Ash](piece_the_ash.md), the reactive byproducts the fire throws off, the oxidative wear the making of energy lays into the very cell that made it.

## Stage

No fire burns clean. The making of energy throws off reactive byproducts, the reactive oxygen species of oxidative metabolism, that damage the very cell that produced them, so the fire that warms the body also scorches it, an account carried from Pearl's rate of living to Harman's free radical theory, and carried too with its serious challenge: antioxidant trials show no life extension, and some of these same reactive byproducts turn out to be signals the body needs rather than pure damage (Harman). No villain: the ash is done by nothing outside the body, no attacker reaching in, only the ordinary residue of the same chemistry that keeps the body from the cold.

## Tension

The wear thrown off by the very fire that keeps the body alive.
