# Plan: Spend It on Living

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the housekeeper's seat](position_housekeeper.md), inside [The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Housekeeper](position_housekeeper.md)

## Direction

Put the budget where the return is, into the reproduction that carries the line on, and into maintenance only so far as it keeps the body whole through its span of breeding, and then let the soma go. Know that this is no carelessness but the allocation the world selected, since a body it will end anyway is not worth building to last forever, and that the wearing you leave is the price of a budget spent wisely and not squandered.

## Orders

([The Housekeeper](persona_the_housekeeper.md) sets [the Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md) through [Spending](process_spending.md) at [the Household](place_the_household.md), funding living and reproduction now and starving the repair of the soma for later.)

## Implementation

([Spending](process_spending.md) at [the Household](place_the_household.md) sets [the Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md), because the return is on reproduction and the world ends the body regardless, so the budget funds the living and leaves the repair underfunded.)

## Targets

- [ ] the budget is set, funding living and reproduction now and starving the repair of the soma
- [ ] the allocation is understood as sound, since a body the world will end anyway is not worth building to last
- [ ] it is seen that the wearing follows from the repair the budget put elsewhere
- [ ] it is owned that the starving of repair is the measure the world selected and not a miser's neglect
