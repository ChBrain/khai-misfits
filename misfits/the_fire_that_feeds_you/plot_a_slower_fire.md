# Plot: A Slower Fire

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The wearing plain now, and a way sought to slow what cannot be stopped without ending the life it wears.

## Action

At [the Household](place_the_household.md), [the Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md) from [the Keeper's seat](position_keeper.md) on [Buy Time](plan_buy_time.md) through [Sparing](process_sparing.md) holds [the Bought Time](piece_the_bought_time.md), the slower burning and greater maintenance that win the body time.

## Stage

This is the remedy, and the bound. A body can burn a little slower and spend a little more on maintenance, as caloric restriction and the repair pathways it engages extend life in many creatures, and so buy itself time, but the time is bought at the cost of the growth and reproduction the budget would otherwise fund, the very trade the Ledger already named (Kirkwood). The tempting shortcut, to simply scavenge the Ash away with antioxidants, is refused by the evidence: antioxidant supplementation does not extend life, and some of the reactive byproducts are signals the body needs rather than damage to be mopped up. And the bound is held harder than most: the mechanism is genuinely contested, with mitohormesis and the hyperfunction alternative offering serious rivals to the crude damage account, so ageing is not simply rust to be scavenged away but a cost intrinsic to living itself (Kirkwood). No villain: the Keeper spends nothing on a shortcut that would work, only on the one trade that genuinely does.

## Tension

The time real but bought in living, and the cost of being alive moved but never lifted.
