# Pitch: The Warmth Wears You

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that the warmth which holds a body off the cold is the same warmth that wears it down.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Grave and elegiac, the long view of a life told without blame. A body doing the one thing a body must, burning to stay alive, and wearing for the doing of it. Nothing here is neglect or disease from without; the wearing is the plain price of the living, and the cold is no enemy, only the rest all matter keeps.

## Undertow

A living body holds off the cold, still default of death only by burning fuel without pause, and the fire throws off reactive byproducts that damage the very cell that made them (Pearl; Harman). Beneath the chemistry an evolved economy funds living and reproducing over repair, since the world ends bodies regardless, so the soma is left to wear (Kirkwood).

## Nerve

That a body ages not despite staying alive but because of it, the fire that feeds it the fire that consumes, so no wearing could be lifted that would not also be the life. No villain and nothing is, held against its limit and hedged harder than most, since the mechanism is contested and ageing is not simply rust to be scavenged away.

## Echo

To hold off the cold a body must burn, and the burning is the wearing, so to live at all is to be consumed by the living.
