# Plot: The Cold That Waits

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A body is not the default of its matter. The default is the cold, and everything drifts toward it in the end.

## Action

At [the Cooling](place_the_cooling.md), [the Cold](persona_the_cold.md) from [the Cold's seat](position_cold.md) on [Pull to Still](plan_pull_to_still.md) through [Stilling](process_stilling.md) holds [the Cold Default](piece_the_cold_default.md), the still, low-energy equilibrium all matter drifts toward.

## Stage

The default is no attacker. It is only the rest a body would keep if nothing worked to hold it elsewhere, the still, low-energy equilibrium all matter settles toward once nothing spends against it, the state a body must burn without pause to escape (Pearl). Nothing pulls; nothing hunts. The cold does not come for the body: it is simply what remains when the burning stops, the ground state under all of it, waiting the way a slope waits for whatever is not held up it. No villain: the cold takes nothing from the body, it only keeps what was never being spent to stay away.

## Tension

The rest waiting under all things, and the fire that must be lit against it.
