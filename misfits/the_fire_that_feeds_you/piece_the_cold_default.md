# Piece: The Cold Default

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The setting: the still, low-energy equilibrium that death is, waiting for no one to arrive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Cooling](place_the_cooling.md), carried by [Stilling](process_stilling.md), voiced by [the Cold](persona_the_cold.md): the quiet the body would already be in, had it never lit itself against it.

## Load Bearing

The Cold Default is load bearing because it is the baseline every fire is lit against and no attacker: the low-energy equilibrium toward which all matter drifts left to itself, the rest a body returns to the moment its own fire goes out. It presses on nothing and stalks no one; it is simply the thing that is there when the burning stops, the rest all matter keeps, and so the ground the whole play stands on (Pearl).

## Apparent

The enemy: death read as a force pressing in on the body, something that comes for it, hunts it, wants it.

## Yearbook

Carried by [Stilling](process_stilling.md), staged at [the Cooling](place_the_cooling.md), voiced by [the Cold](persona_the_cold.md), dramatized in [The Cold That Waits](plot_the_cold_that_waits.md). It is the default the body burns to defer; it is what [The Fire](piece_the_fire.md) holds off.
