# Position: Housekeeper

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The seat of the evolved economy that splits a finite budget between living and repair, and marks that funding the living over the mending is a sound allocation and not a miser's neglect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A finite budget of energy and material, and two ends to spend it on: living and reproducing now, and repairing the soma for later.
A world that ends bodies regardless, by predation and accident and cold, whatever their upkeep.
The measure that selected it, the return on reproduction that carries the line on past any single body.

## Orders

Spend it on the living, and fund only enough repair to last the reproductive span.
Put the budget where the return is, into the reproduction that carries the line on, and into maintenance only so far as it keeps the body whole through its span of breeding, and then let the soma go.
Know that this is no carelessness but the allocation the world selected, since a body it will end anyway is not worth building to last forever, and that the wearing you leave is the price of a budget spent wisely and not squandered.

## Loses

The lasting soma, spent down to fund the living, and it spends it knowingly.
Because the return is on reproduction and the world ends the body regardless, the seat funds the living and starves the repair, so the soma wears for want of the maintenance the budget put elsewhere, a body given only as much endurance as its breeding needs and no more.
It keeps its sound allocation and its clear measure and loses no cause for blame, an economy that funded the living because the mending would not have paid.

## Drives

Toward the evolved economy that funds the living and lets the soma wear,
spending the finite budget on the reproduction that carries the line and starving the repair that would only outlast the breeding, wisely and not miserly,
so the seat whose sound allocation is the very thing that leaves the body to wear starves the repair not by neglect but by the measure the world selected.
