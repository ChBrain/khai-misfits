# Plot: Nothing Left for Repair

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A finite budget of energy and material, and two ends waiting to draw on it.

## Action

At [the Household](place_the_household.md), [the Housekeeper](persona_the_housekeeper.md) from [the Housekeeper's seat](position_housekeeper.md) on [Spend It on Living](plan_spend_it_on_living.md) through [Spending](process_spending.md) sets [the Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md), funding living and reproducing now and starving the repair of the soma.

## Stage

Beneath the chemistry sits an economy. A body has only so much energy and material, and it must divide that budget between living and reproducing now, and repairing and maintaining the soma for later. Because the world ends bodies anyway, by predation and accident and cold, selection does not fund a body built to last forever: it funds only enough repair to carry the body through its expected span of reproduction, disposing of the soma once the copies are made (Kirkwood). The allocation is reasonable and not miserly, the measure the world itself selected, since a body destined to end regardless is not worth building for a permanence it will never use. No villain: the Housekeeper spends nothing wastefully, she simply keeps the ledger the world's own arithmetic wrote.

## Tension

The repair starved, and the wearing it leaves unmet.
