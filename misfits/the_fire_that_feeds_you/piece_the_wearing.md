# Piece: The Wearing

## Load Bearing

The Wearing is load bearing as the body worn down by the very living that sustains it: the warmth that holds off the cold consuming, by the same act, the one it warms. It is the Ash accrued past what the Ledger's starved repair can keep pace with, drifting the body back toward the cold default, authored by no one and by no external cause, only by the ordinary running of its own necessary fire (Kirkwood; Harman).

This piece is distinct from three neighbouring accounts it is easily mistaken for. It is distinct from Early and Uncounted (antagonistic pleiotropy): there, a single gene favoured for an early benefit is never billed for its late cost because too few of the cohort survive to pay it, the declining force of selection at work on one gene's unbilled bill. Here, the engine is the metabolic cost of life itself and the disposable-soma allocation of the whole body's budget, not one gene escaping notice; the two are complementary evolutionary accounts cut to different vectors, not rivals. It is distinct from The Load It Always Carried (mechanical fatigue): there, increments of load sum in a material until a part fractures, a mechanical account. Here, the damage is the chemical byproduct of the life process and the starving of repair, a metabolic and economic wearing, not a mechanical one. It is distinct from In Its Own Defence (immunopathology): there, the body's own defence escalates correctly against a real invader but its weapons wreck the host in the process. Here, there is no threat and no response, only the plain, unremarkable cost of being alive.

## Apparent

A rust, or a hard use done to the body from without: something applied to it, some wear visited on it, rather than run through it from the inside.
