# Plan: Burn to Live

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the body's seat](position_body.md), inside [The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Body](position_body.md)

## Direction

Keep the metabolism running that powers your every beat and repair and thought, because the moment it stops you return to the still default, and to live at all is to burn. Know that the fire throws off the damage that wears you, that the thing consuming you is the thing sustaining you, and that you cannot put out the fire to spare yourself the wearing without putting out the life.

## Orders

([The Body](persona_the_body.md) lights [the Fire](piece_the_fire.md) through [Burning](process_burning.md) at [the Hearth](place_the_hearth.md), the metabolism that holds off the cold, and meets [the Ash](piece_the_ash.md), the damage the fire throws off.)

## Implementation

([Burning](process_burning.md) at [the Hearth](place_the_hearth.md) lights [the Fire](piece_the_fire.md), because only the burning holds the body from the cold, and the same fire throws off [the Ash](piece_the_ash.md) that wears the cell that made it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the fire is kept, the metabolism burning without pause to hold the body from the cold
- [ ] the burning is understood as the whole of living, the body held from the default only while it burns
- [ ] it is seen that the fire throws off the damage that wears the body, done by nothing outside it
- [ ] it is owned that the fire cannot be put out to stop the wearing without returning to the cold
