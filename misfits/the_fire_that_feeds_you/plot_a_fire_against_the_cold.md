# Plot: A Fire Against the Cold

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The cold default a living body must hold off, not once but continuously, or return to it.

## Action

At [the Hearth](place_the_hearth.md), [the Body](persona_the_body.md) from [the Body's seat](position_body.md) on [Burn to Live](plan_burn_to_live.md) through [Burning](process_burning.md) lights [the Fire](piece_the_fire.md), the metabolism that powers every beat and holds the body from the cold.

## Stage

The fire is the whole of living: not a feature of the body but the condition of it, the metabolism that burns fuel to power every beat and repair and thought, and the body is held from the cold default only for as long as it keeps burning. To live at all is to burn (Pearl). Stop the fire and there is no body left to hold the line, only the matter returning to the rest it was always going to keep. No villain: the fire is lit by the body's own need to stay itself, nothing forcing the burn but the plain fact of what a living thing must do to remain living.

## Tension

The warmth that sustains, and the cost it has not yet shown.
