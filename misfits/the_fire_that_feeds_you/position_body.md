# Position: Body

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The seat of the living thing that burns to hold off the cold, and marks that the fire which sustains it is the fire that wears it, done by nothing outside it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A fire to keep, the metabolism it must burn without pause to hold itself from the cold default.
A wholeness at the start, an unworn body the living has not yet marked.
No way to stop the wearing but to stop the fire, which is to return to the cold.

## Orders

Burn to live, since only the fire holds you from the cold.
Keep the metabolism running that powers your every beat and repair and thought, because the moment it stops you return to the still default, and to live at all is to burn.
Know that the fire throws off the damage that wears you, that the thing consuming you is the thing sustaining you, and that you cannot put out the fire to spare yourself the wearing without putting out the life.

## Loses

The wholeness it began with, worn away by its own necessary fire.
Because it must burn to hold off the cold and the burning throws off the damage, the seat is worn by the very warmth that keeps it alive, its unworn body marked and marked again by the living, and the only escape from the wearing the cold it was burning to defer.
It keeps its life and its blameless burning and loses no cause for blame, a body worn by nothing but its own sustaining fire.

## Drives

Toward the living thing worn by the fire that keeps it warm,
burning without pause to hold off the cold and taking on the damage the burning throws off, worn by its own warmth,
so the seat that could stop the wearing only by returning to the cold ages not despite its living but because of it, consumed by the very thing that sustains it.
