# Plan: Buy Time

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the keeper's seat](position_keeper.md), inside [The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md).

## Owner

## Direction

Shift the budget toward maintenance and slow the fire, winning the body time as restriction wins it in many creatures, and take the cost where it falls. Know that the time is bought with the growth and reproduction the budget would otherwise fund, that the damage cannot simply be scavenged away since the shortcut is refused by the evidence, and that you buy against the grain of the living and never lift the cost of being alive.
