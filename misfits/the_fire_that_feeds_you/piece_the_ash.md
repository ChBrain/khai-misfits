# Piece: The Ash

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The mechanism of damage: the byproduct the fire throws off on the very cell that lit it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Hearth](place_the_hearth.md), met by [the Body](persona_the_body.md): what settles on the flame's own walls while it burns, not carried off anywhere, not swept out.

## Load Bearing

The Ash is load bearing as the reactive byproducts of oxidative metabolism, the reactive oxygen species that damage the DNA and proteins and lipids of the very cell that made them, so that the fire warming the body also scorches it in the same breath. This piece is carried with its serious challenge attached and not staged as settled fact: the crude account is much disputed, antioxidant supplementation does not extend life, and some of these same reactive byproducts act as beneficial signals the body relies on, mitohormesis rather than plain poison (Harman). What the piece holds is the byproduct, not a verdict on exactly how it harms.

## Apparent

A contaminant: something foreign that could, in principle, be filtered out, scavenged clean, mopped up before it does its damage.
