# Piece: The Fire

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The life process itself: the metabolism that burns without pause to hold the body off the cold default.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Hearth](place_the_hearth.md), carried by [Burning](process_burning.md), voiced by [the Body](persona_the_body.md): the fuel taken in and spent, every beat and repair and thought paid for out of the same flame.

## Load Bearing

The Fire is load bearing because it is the whole of living made physical: the burning of fuel that powers every beat and repair and thought, and so holds the body from the cold default it would otherwise drift back to at once. The body is held away from that default only so long as it burns; stop the fire and the rest waiting underneath is immediate, not a slow return but the one already owed (Pearl). Nothing about the Fire is optional; it is the cost of being here at all.

## Apparent

Pure life: warmth and vigour and appetite, a flame with no cost attached to it, burning as a pure gift.
