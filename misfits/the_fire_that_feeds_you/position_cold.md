# Position: Cold

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The seat of the cold, still default of death, and marks that it is no attacker but only the waiting rest, the ground the drama stands on and not its villain.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The default state of all matter, the still, low-energy equilibrium toward which everything drifts.
No aim and no motion, only the waiting, taking nothing and pressing on nothing.
The claim on every body the moment its fire goes out, the rest it returns to and came from.

## Orders

Pull to still, being the default all matter keeps.
Be the low-energy equilibrium toward which everything settles, the rest a living body must burn without pause to hold itself away from, and take the body back the moment the fire goes out.
Know that you attack nothing and lay siege to nothing, that you are the ground of the drama and not its villain, and that the harm of the misfit comes not from your waiting but from the fire the body lights against you.

## Loses

Nothing, for it wants nothing and does nothing, and this is the point.
Because the seat is only the default state and takes nothing by force, it holds no stake and suffers no loss, and yet it is the rest the body spends its whole living to defer, so it frames the tragedy without being its cause, the waiting that makes the fire necessary and the fire that does the wearing.
It keeps its stillness and its blamelessness and loses no cause for blame, the default that only waits while the warmth lit against it wears the body down.

## Drives

Toward the still default that only waits and takes nothing,
the low-energy rest all matter drifts to, the ground the body burns without pause to hold itself from,
so the seat that is no attacker frames the whole misfit, since the harm is not in the cold's waiting but in the fire the body must light against it.
