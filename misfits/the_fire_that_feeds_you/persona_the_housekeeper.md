# Persona: The Housekeeper

## Taxonomy

[Housekeeper](position_housekeeper.md), inside [The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The archetype of the evolved economy of the body, which divides a finite budget between living and reproducing now and repairing the soma for later, and funds the first and starves the second.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A miser starving the house of repair, a careless steward letting the fabric rot to spend on show, as though the wearing were the fault of a budget badly kept and a wiser hand would fund the maintenance the body needs.

## Action

Spend it on living. At [the Household](place_the_household.md), the Housekeeper on [Spend It on Living](plan_spend_it_on_living.md) through [Spending](process_spending.md) sets [the Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md), the allocation that funds reproduction and living now and leaves the repair of the soma underfunded, since the world ends the body regardless of how well it is kept (Kirkwood).

## Shadow

Its miserliness is a sound allocation, not a fault. The Housekeeper is not starving the house through carelessness or greed: it spends where the return is, and since the world ends bodies by predation and accident and cold regardless of their upkeep, a body built to last forever would waste its budget on a soma that will not survive to use it. So the evolved and reasonable thing is to fund the reproduction that carries the line on and to spend on repair only enough to last the reproductive span, and then let the soma go. It keeps the house well by the only measure that selected it, and the wearing it leaves is the price of a budget spent wisely, not squandered.

## Tell

It funds the living over the mending because a body the world will end anyway is not worth building to last, a wise allocation and not a miser's neglect.
