# Piece: The Bought Time

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The remedy and its bound: a slower fire and a fuller ledger for repair, bought at a real cost and only partly effective.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Household](place_the_household.md), carried by [Sparing](process_sparing.md), voiced by [the Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md): the deliberate turning down of the flame, and the accounts rebalanced toward upkeep.

## Load Bearing

The Bought Time is load bearing as the slower burning and greater maintenance that win the body time: caloric restriction extends life in many creatures, bought at the direct cost of the growth and reproduction the same budget would otherwise fund, a trade and not a discount. The antioxidant shortcut is refused by the evidence; supplements do not extend life, and some reactive byproducts are signals the body needs rather than a pollutant it can simply scrub away (Kirkwood).

This piece also carries the bound the whole play stands under: the mechanism is genuinely contested. The crude damage account is much challenged, by mitohormesis, where some reactive species act as beneficial signals, and by the hyperfunction alternative, that ageing is runaway growth-programme activity continuing past its use rather than accumulated damage at all. So ageing is not simply rust to be scavenged away, and this piece, like the misfit it closes, stages the robust disposable-soma core and not the contested specifics of the damage it slows.

## Apparent

A cure: the promise that the right supplement, found and taken, will lift the ageing off the body rather than merely rent it more time at a price.
