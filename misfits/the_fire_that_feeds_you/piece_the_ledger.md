# Piece: The Ledger

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The economic driver: the disposable-soma allocation that splits the body's finite budget and starves the repair line.

## Owner

## Place

At [the Household](place_the_household.md), carried by [Spending](process_spending.md), voiced by [the Housekeeper](persona_the_housekeeper.md): the finite accounts kept and drawn against every day, living against repair.

## Load Bearing

The Ledger is load bearing as the finite budget split between living and reproducing now and repairing the soma for later, an economy evolved to fund the first line and starve the second because the world ends bodies by predation and accident and chance regardless of how well they are kept up, so a body built to last forever would simply waste its budget on upkeep it would rarely live to use. This is a sound allocation, computed against the odds a body actually faces, not a miser's neglect of its own house (Kirkwood).

## Apparent

A budget badly kept: repair the body could well afford, if only it chose to spend on it, negligence dressed as necessity.
