# Place: The Household

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The body's economy, the finite budget divided between living and repair. Hosts [Nothing Left for Repair](plot_nothing_left_for_repair.md), [A Slower Fire](plot_a_slower_fire.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A finite budget and two ends it must be split between.
The accounts that fund the living and starve the mending.

## Holds

[The Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md).
The evolved allocation that funds living and reproducing now over repair for later, since the world ends bodies regardless.

[The Bought Time](piece_the_bought_time.md).
The slower burning and greater maintenance that win time, bought at the cost of the growth and reproduction the same budget would fund.

## Offers

To the Housekeeper, a return on reproduction that carries the line on; to the Keeper, a little time bought by shifting the ledger.

## Withheld

A budget large enough to fund both the living and a soma built to last.
