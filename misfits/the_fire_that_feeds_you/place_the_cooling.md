## Shown

The still rest waiting under all things.
The body drifting back toward it as it wears.

## Holds

[The Cold Default](piece_the_cold_default.md).
The still, low-energy equilibrium all matter drifts to, the rest a body must spend without pause to escape.

[The Wearing](piece_the_wearing.md).
Senescence, the body worn down by its own necessary living, the warmth that holds off the cold consuming the one it warms.

## Offers

To the Cold, the return of every body whose fire goes out.

## Withheld

Any lasting away from the default that would not cost the burning.
