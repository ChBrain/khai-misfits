# Persona: The Cold

## Taxonomy

[Cold](position_cold.md), inside [The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The archetype of the cold, still, low-energy default of death, the equilibrium all matter drifts toward, the rest a living body spends without pause to hold itself away from.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The enemy, death come to take the body, a darkness pressing in that the fire must beat back, as though the cold were an attacker and the whole of the struggle were to keep it out.

## Action

Pull to still. At [the Cooling](place_the_cooling.md), the Cold on [Pull to Still](plan_pull_to_still.md) through [Stilling](process_stilling.md) holds [the Cold Default](piece_the_cold_default.md), the low-energy equilibrium toward which all matter drifts, the rest the body returns to the moment its fire goes out.

## Shadow

Its enmity is only physics, and it does not attack. The Cold is not an aggressor laying siege: it is the default state of matter, the still equilibrium everything settles into absent the fire, and it takes nothing, presses on nothing, only waits, as it waits under all things. The tragedy of the misfit is not that the Cold is strong but that the fire lit against it is what consumes the body: the harm comes from the warmth, not the waiting. The Cold is the ground the whole drama stands on and not its villain, the rest all matter keeps and the body must burn to defer.

## Tell

It only waits, taking nothing and pressing on nothing, the still rest a body must burn without pause to hold itself away from.
