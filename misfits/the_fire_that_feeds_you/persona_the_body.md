# Persona: The Body

## Taxonomy

[Body](position_body.md), inside [The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The archetype of the living thing itself, which burns fuel without pause to hold off the cold default, and is worn by the very fire that keeps it warm.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A thing rusting with time, or worn by the world's hard use, its ageing a damage done to it from without, as though something outside were grinding it down and a better shelter or a cleaner life would spare it.

## Action

Burn to live. At [the Hearth](place_the_hearth.md), the Body on [Burn to Live](plan_burn_to_live.md) through [Burning](process_burning.md) lights [the Fire](piece_the_fire.md), the metabolism that powers its every beat, then meets [the Ash](piece_the_ash.md), the damage the fire throws off, and at [the Cooling](place_the_cooling.md) meets [the Wearing](piece_the_wearing.md), worn by the warmth that sustains it (Pearl; Harman).

## Shadow

Its wearing is done by no one and by nothing outside it. The Body is not rusting under some external grinding it might be sheltered from: the damage is thrown off by its own fire, the reactive byproduct of the very metabolism that keeps it from the cold, so the thing wearing it is the thing sustaining it. It cannot put out the fire to stop the wearing, because to put out the fire is to return to the cold. It ages not despite its living but because of it, worn by its own warmth, and there is no enemy to blame and no shelter that would spare it the cost of being alive.

## Tell

It burns because it must live, and the burning is the wearing, so the thing that keeps it warm is the thing that wears it down.
