# Persona: The Keeper

## Taxonomy

[Keeper](position_keeper.md), inside [The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md). The archetype of the one who would slow the wearing by burning less and mending more, and who marks that the time so bought is paid for in growth and reproduction, and that the damage cannot simply be scavenged away.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A saviour with a cure, one who will lift the ageing off the body if only the right supplement or regimen is found, as though the wearing were a debt that could be cancelled outright and the fire made to burn free of its cost.

## Action

Buy time. At [the Household](place_the_household.md), the Keeper on [Buy Time](plan_buy_time.md) through [Sparing](process_sparing.md) holds [the Bought Time](piece_the_bought_time.md), the slower burning and greater maintenance that win the body time, at the cost of the growth and reproduction the budget would otherwise fund (Kirkwood).

## Shadow

Its cure is real but partial and dearly bought. The Keeper is not a saviour cancelling the debt of ageing: it can shift the ledger, burning a little slower and spending more on repair, as caloric restriction extends life in many creatures, and so win the body time. But the time is bought with the growth and reproduction the same budget would have funded, the trade the disposable soma names, and it is not the clean scavenging of damage the shortcut promises, since antioxidant supplements do not extend life and some of the reactive byproducts are signals the body needs. It buys time against the grain of the living, and it cannot lift the cost of being alive, only move a little of it.

## Tell

It slows the fire to win time, and pays for the time in growth and reproduction, buying a little against the cost of living and never lifting it.
