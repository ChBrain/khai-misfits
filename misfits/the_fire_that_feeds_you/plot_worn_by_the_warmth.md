# Plot: Worn by the Warmth

## Taxonomy

[The Fire That Feeds You](play_the_fire_that_feeds_you.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The fire burning without pause and the repair too starved to keep pace with what it throws off.

## Action

At [the Cooling](place_the_cooling.md), [the Body](persona_the_body.md) meets [the Wearing](piece_the_wearing.md), senescence, the body worn down by the very living that sustains it.

## Stage

This is the harm. The warmth that holds off the cold consumes the one it warms: the Ash accrues, cell by cell, and the repair the Ledger never funded is too starved to keep pace, so the body ages not despite staying alive but because of it, worn by the very fire that keeps it from the default it was always going to return to. It is authored by no one and by no external cause: the metabolism doing the damage is the same metabolism keeping the body alive, and there is no wearing here to be lifted that would not also be the life. This is distinct from Early and Uncounted's account, a single gene's antagonistic pleiotropy under the declining force of selection, whereas here it is the metabolic cost of life itself and the disposable-soma budget, a complementary account cut to a different vector; distinct from The Load It Always Carried's mechanical fatigue summing load cycles, whereas here it is a chemical byproduct and a starved repair, a metabolic and economic wearing; and distinct from In Its Own Defence's immunopathology, a defence overshooting a threat, whereas here it is the ordinary running of the body with no threat at all, the plain cost of being alive (Kirkwood; Harman).

## Tension

The body worn by the very fire that keeps it from the cold, no wearing to be lifted that is not also the life.
