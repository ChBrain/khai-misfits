# Piece: The Accurate Test

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The mechanism: an instrument performing exactly at its advertised rate, answering only the one question it was built to answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), carried by [Testing](process_testing.md), voiced by [the Instrument](persona_the_instrument.md): the reading taken, the result returned, the error rate holding at its stated figure, no more and no less.

## Load Bearing

The instrument does exactly what it says: given the disease, it returns a positive at its stated rate; given no disease, it returns a false positive at its stated, low rate. That is the whole of what it was built to answer, how likely a result like this is given the disease, and it answers that one question well (Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys, 1978). What it was never built to answer, and does not attempt to, is the other question: given this result, how likely is the disease. The instrument is faithful to its own design and blind to the population it is run against, and nothing in its performance changes that.

## Apparent

Reads as a source of near certainty about the world: a fine instrument, rarely wrong, so a result from it seems to settle the matter on its own.
