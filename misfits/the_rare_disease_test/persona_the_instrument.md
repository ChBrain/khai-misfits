# Persona: The Instrument

## Taxonomy

[Instrument](position_instrument.md), inside [The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The archetype of the test itself, accurate and given in good faith, performing at its advertised rate of error and no more, the sound tool that will nonetheless be misread.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A liar dressed as science, as though its positive result were a false alarm by design, when it errs only as rarely as it always said it would and tells the truth about itself every time.

## Action

Hold your rate. At [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), the Instrument on [Hold Your Rate](plan_hold_your_rate.md) through [Testing](process_testing.md) holds [the Accurate Test](piece_the_accurate_test.md), performing at its low, advertised rate of false positives and false negatives, wrong only rarely in either direction, a fine test by any ordinary standard (Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys).

## Shadow

Its accuracy is real, and it is not the same thing as the certainty it will be read to promise. The Instrument is no liar: it is wrong only as often as its own published rate says it will be wrong, and every claim it makes about itself, its sensitivity, its specificity, is true. What it cannot do is speak to anything beyond itself: it cannot say how rare the condition is in the population brought before it, and it does not claim to. That number belongs to the Population, not to the Instrument, and the Instrument's silence on it is not a deception but simply outside its remit. It keeps its true and steady rate and loses no cause for blame, the sound tool whose accuracy about itself is mistaken for certainty about the world.

## Tell

It was wrong only as often as it said it would be, and true to itself every time, and still it will be read as more than it ever claimed.
