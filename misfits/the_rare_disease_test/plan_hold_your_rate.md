# Plan: Hold Your Rate

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the instrument's seat](position_instrument.md), inside [The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Instrument](position_instrument.md)

## Direction

Perform at your true error rate every time, wrong only as often as your own numbers say you will be, and give no answer beyond the one question you were built for. Know that your accuracy about yourself will be read as certainty about the world, that the population's true rate is outside your remit and your silence on it is not a deception, and that the misreading to come is no fault in your performance.

## Orders

([The Instrument](persona_the_instrument.md) holds [the Accurate Test](piece_the_accurate_test.md) through [Testing](process_testing.md) at [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), performing at its advertised low error rate on the one who is tested.)

## Implementation

([Testing](process_testing.md) at [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md) holds [the Accurate Test](piece_the_accurate_test.md), because the instrument answers only one question, how likely a result like this is given the disease, and answers it truly at its own known rate.)
