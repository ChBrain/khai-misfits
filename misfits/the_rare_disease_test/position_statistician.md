# Position: Statistician

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The seat of the one who restores the prior to the reckoning, and marks that the true sum is real and correct, and that its power to persuade depends on how it is framed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A way to fold the population's true rate back against the instrument's own rates, the missing half of the sum.
The correct probability, often far smaller than the confidence the positive result inspired on its own.
A held bound, the knowledge that the same true sum persuades far less stated as a percentage than as a count out of a thousand.

## Orders

Do the sum, and restore the prior to the reckoning.
Fold the population's true rate back against the instrument's own error rates, and state the actual probability the disease is present given the result, the missing half of a calculation that was, without it, only ever half complete.
Know that the number you return is often smaller and less satisfying than the confidence it replaces, that the same true sum persuades far more when cast as a count out of a thousand than as a percentage, and that your correction is not always reached for.

## Loses

The ease with which the wrong confidence persuaded, and gains nothing to match it.
Because it restores the true probability by a correct and complete sum, the seat takes no loss in the arithmetic itself, and yet its correct answer competes against a wrong one that felt more vivid and arrived first, so its true sum must work harder to be believed than the false certainty it corrects.
It keeps its true sum and its held bound, and loses no cause for blame, the one whose correct answer must struggle against a false one that felt more real.

## Drives

Toward the one whose true sum struggles against a false confidence that arrived first,
folding the population's rate back against the instrument's own numbers to restore the real probability,
so the seat that alone completes the reckoning holds even its own remedy to a bound, a truth that persuades only as well as the framing lets it.
