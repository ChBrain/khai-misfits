# Plan: Do the Sum

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the statistician's seat](position_statistician.md), inside [The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Statistician](position_statistician.md)

## Direction

Fold the population's true rate back against the instrument's own error rates, and state the actual probability the disease is present given the result, the missing half of a calculation that was, without it, only ever half complete. Know that the number you return is often smaller and less satisfying than the confidence it replaces, that the same true sum persuades far more when cast as a count out of a thousand than as a percentage, and that your correction is not always reached for.

## Orders

([The Statistician](persona_the_statistician.md) holds [the Arithmetic](piece_the_arithmetic.md) through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) at [the Numbers](place_the_numbers.md), folding the population's rate back against the instrument's rates to restore the true probability.)

## Implementation

([Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) at [the Numbers](place_the_numbers.md) holds [the Arithmetic](piece_the_arithmetic.md), because the true probability requires the prior folded back against the test's own rates, a sum easiest done as a count out of a thousand rather than a percent (Bar-Hillel; Gigerenzer and Hoffrage).)

## Targets

- [ ] the population's true rate is folded back against the instrument's own error rates
- [ ] the actual probability of the disease given the result is stated, restoring the missing prior
- [ ] it is seen that the same true sum persuades more as a natural frequency than as a percentage
- [ ] it is owned that the correction is not always reached for and depends on how the question is framed
