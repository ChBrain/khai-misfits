# Piece: The Crowded-Out Prior

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The mechanism: the base rate set aside not by refusal but by the greater vividness of the result in hand.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), carried by [Reading](process_reading.md), voiced by [the Reader](persona_the_reader.md): the population's known rarity, still true, still available, simply not reached for while the slip of paper is in hand.

## Load Bearing

Nothing removes the base rate from the record; it is set aside because it is pallid and non-causal next to the vivid, specific evidence the reader is actually holding, and base rates are neglected in close proportion to how pallid they read against a concrete case (Bar-Hillel, 1980). The right question, how likely is the disease given this result, needs the prior folded in to be answered at all, and that is precisely the step that goes unasked. The prior was never hidden and never denied; it simply lost the competition for attention to the one piece of evidence that felt like it already carried the answer.

## Apparent

Reads as an irrelevant detail correctly ignored: a population-level number that seems to have nothing to say about the one case in front of the reader.
