# Piece: The Positive Result

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The mechanism: the specific, vivid evidence in hand, weighed by asking how well it fits the hypothesis.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), carried by [Reading](process_reading.md), voiced by [the Reader](persona_the_reader.md): one slip of paper, one line, positive, held and read in the moment it arrives.

## Load Bearing

The result in hand is concrete where the population is abstract, and the mind reaches for the question representativeness makes natural: how likely is a result like this, given the disease. That is a real and reasonable question, and the reader asks it first because it is the question the evidence itself seems to pose (Kahneman and Tversky, 1972; 1973). It is not the question that settles the matter, how likely is the disease given this result, but the two are close enough in shape that answering one feels like answering the other. Nothing about the reader's reasoning here is careless; it is the ordinary, natural way a specific case gets weighed against a category.

## Apparent

Reads as decisive proof on its own: a positive result, held up alone, looking like it answers the whole question by itself.
