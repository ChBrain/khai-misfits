# Plot: One in a Thousand

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Before any test is given, the many stand together, and among them the condition sits at its true, low rate.

## Action

At [The Field](place_the_field.md), [the Population](persona_the_population.md) from [the population's seat](position_population.md) on [Stay Few](plan_stay_few.md) through [Distributing](process_distributing.md) takes up [the Rare Condition](piece_the_rare_condition.md), holding it at a real, fixed rate, one in a thousand or fewer, among the sick and well standing together, undivided by any result yet.

## Stage

The condition is present in only a small fraction of the many, and that fraction is not a guess or a house style, it is a published, knowable rate, the kind a physician or a student is handed before any laboratory result enters the problem (Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys). The rate is fixed before a single test is administered: whoever is later found positive was already, this whole time, drawn from a population where being positive for the condition is the rare case and being well is the ordinary one. Nothing about the population's arithmetic depends on who will be tested, or how, or when, it holds regardless, a fact of record standing apart from any single case that will later be read against it. The rate is not hidden and not hard to obtain, it sits in the open the way population statistics do, available to anyone who thinks to ask for it before reasoning about a single result. No villain: the population authors nothing and refuses no one, it simply is what it is at the rate it is, offering the one number every later reading will need and most will not reach for. The scene closes exactly where it opened, the many standing at their true rate, waiting for a test that has not yet been given to any one of them.

## Tension

The true prior sits here, whole and available, and it is also the single fact most likely to go unasked once a case is in hand.
