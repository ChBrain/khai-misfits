# Persona: The Reader

## Taxonomy

[Reader](position_reader.md), inside [The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The archetype of the one who reads the positive result, weighing it by asking how likely the evidence is given the disease, the prior crowded out and the confidence inflated by the very naturalness of the question asked.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A careless reasoner who never learned probability, as though a plainer education would have caught the error, when the question the Reader asks is the reasonable one to ask and only the wrong one to answer with.

## Action

Weigh the evidence. At [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), the Reader on [Weigh the Evidence](plan_weigh_the_evidence.md) through [Reading](process_reading.md) takes up [the Positive Result](piece_the_positive_result.md), asking how likely this result is given the disease, then meets [the Crowded-Out Prior](piece_the_crowded_out_prior.md) and [the Inflated Confidence](piece_the_inflated_confidence.md), reading the result as near certain (Kahneman and Tversky; Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys).

## Shadow

It asks a fair question and answers it well, and the well-answered question is not the one that mattered. The Reader is no careless reasoner: to ask how likely a positive result is if the person has the disease is a sensible and ordinary question, and the Instrument answers it clearly, so the Reader's confidence tracks a real and correctly read number. What it does not do, and what almost no one does without training or a prompt, is turn the question around, to ask how likely the disease is given the result, which needs the Population's rate folded back in. The specific result sits in hand, vivid and answered; the base rate sits further off, abstract and unasked. It keeps its sound reading of the question it asked and loses no cause for blame, the reasoner undone not by a bad answer but by the right answer to the wrong question.

## Tell

It asked how well the evidence fit the disease, and the fit was strong, and the fit was not the same as the disease being there.
