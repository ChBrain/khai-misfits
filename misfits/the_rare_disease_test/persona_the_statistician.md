# Persona: The Statistician

## Taxonomy

[Statistician](position_statistician.md), inside [The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The archetype of the one who restores the prior to the reckoning, folding the population's true rate back against the instrument's own rates, and holds the finding to the bound of how it is framed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A pedant who complicates a plain result with numbers no one needs, as though the arithmetic were an academic nicety, when it is the only route back to the true probability the plain reading missed.

## Action

Do the sum. At [the Numbers](place_the_numbers.md), the Statistician on [Do the Sum](plan_do_the_sum.md) through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) holds [the Arithmetic](piece_the_arithmetic.md), folding the Population's true rate back against the Instrument's own rates, restoring the probability the plain reading crowded out (Bar-Hillel; Gigerenzer and Hoffrage).

## Shadow

Its sum is not pedantry, it is the missing half of the reckoning. The Statistician does what the Reader's fair question left undone, bringing the prior back into a calculation that was, without it, only half complete, and the number it returns, often far lower than the confidence the positive result inspired, is not a complication but the actual answer to the actual question. But it holds its own bound: the correction lands only when the numbers are held together and the same sum, stated as a percentage rather than a count out of a thousand, persuades far less even when it is right, so the Statistician's remedy depends on how the question is put and is not always reached for. It keeps its true sum and its held bound, and loses no cause for blame, the one who restores a probability the plain reading could not see and cannot always make felt.

## Tell

It folded the population's true rate back into the count, and the number it returned was smaller and truer, and harder to believe than the one it replaced.
