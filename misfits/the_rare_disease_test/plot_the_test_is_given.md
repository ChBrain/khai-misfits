# Plot: The Test Is Given

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A member of the population steps forward, and an instrument built to answer one question is put to it.

## Action

At [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), [the Instrument](persona_the_instrument.md) from [the instrument's seat](position_instrument.md) on [Hold Your Rate](plan_hold_your_rate.md) through [Testing](process_testing.md) takes up [the Accurate Test](piece_the_accurate_test.md), performing at its advertised, low rate of error, wrong only rarely in either direction.

## Stage

The test is administered and returns its result at exactly the rate it was built and validated to (Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys): a low false positive rate, a low false negative rate, a fine instrument by any ordinary clinical standard. It answers one question only, how likely is this result given that the person has the condition, or given that they do not, and it answers that one question accurately, holding to its own published numbers without drift or exaggeration. The instrument makes no claim about the person's actual probability of having the condition, that is a different question, one that would require the population's rate as well, and the instrument was never built to carry that number. It performs its narrow task faithfully and stops exactly there, the same way it would for the ten-thousandth patient as for the first. No villain: the instrument is sound, calibrated, and honest about what it measures, claiming nothing beyond the rate it was tested to, and offering that rate openly to whoever reads the result. The scene closes with a test correctly, faithfully performed, its accuracy real and its silence about the person's true odds also real.

## Tension

An honest performance, exactly at its stated rate, is about to be read as answering a larger question than the one it was built to answer.
