## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Instrument](persona_the_instrument.md), performing at its own advertised rate on the case brought before it.

## Direction

From the instrument's own advertised rate toward a result performed truly.

## Lever

The instrument answers only one question, how likely a result like this is given the disease, and answers it at its own known, low error rate, wrong only rarely in either direction (Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys).
