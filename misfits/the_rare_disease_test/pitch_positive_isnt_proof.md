# Pitch: Positive Isn't Proof

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a positive result for a rare condition is read as far more certain than the arithmetic supports, because the vivid specific evidence crowds out the abstract prior.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Cool and exacting, the quiet arithmetic of a mistaken confidence. A test that performs exactly as advertised, and a reader whose confidence is nonetheless wrong. Nothing here is a fraud or a fool; the instrument is sound and the reasoning feels sound while it happens.

## Undertow

A positive result is weighed by asking how likely the evidence is given the disease, and the test answers that clearly, so the base rate, the disease's true low rate in the population, is crowded out by the more vivid specific result (Kahneman and Tversky; Bar-Hillel). Physicians given the numbers mostly fail to compute the true probability, some reading near certainty where it sits under one in ten (Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys).

## Nerve

That the same clarity that makes a positive result feel like evidence at all is the clarity that crowds out an abstract rate expressed only in a percentage, so the more decisive the result feels, the further the true probability drifts from what is believed. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since the neglect is strongest under pallid framing and weakens sharply when the same numbers are cast as a count out of a thousand (Gigerenzer and Hoffrage).

## Echo

Test truly, read the one clear result, and be sure of a thing that is still, most likely, not so.
