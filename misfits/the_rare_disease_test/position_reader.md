# Position: Reader

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The seat of the one who reads the positive result, and marks that the question it asks is sensible and the answer it gets is correctly read, and that the error lies only in the question left unasked.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A positive result in hand, vivid and specific, the case directly before it.
A sensible question to ask, how likely is a result like this if the disease is present.
No settled habit of turning the question around, to ask how likely the disease is given the result.

## Orders

Weigh the evidence, ask how well the result fits the disease.
Read the positive result and ask the natural question, how likely is this evidence if the person has the condition, and take the clear answer as your measure of confidence.
Know that this is the wrong question answered instead of the right one, that the right question also needs the population's rate folded back in, and that without it your confidence will run far ahead of the true probability.

## Loses

A confidence it never earned, though it asked in good faith.
Because it asks the sensible question and reads the clear answer correctly, the seat takes no loss in its own reasoning, and yet the question it asked was not the one that mattered, so the confidence it forms tracks the fit of the evidence and not the truth of the diagnosis, a mistaken certainty built from a correctly answered question.
It keeps its sound reading of its own question, and loses no cause for blame, the reasoner undone by the right answer to the wrong question.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound question yields an unsound confidence,
weighing the positive result by how well it fits the disease, a sensible and ordinary question, correctly answered,
so the seat that reads its own evidence rightly is the seat whose confidence runs past the truth, undone not by a bad answer but by a question that was never the one that mattered.
