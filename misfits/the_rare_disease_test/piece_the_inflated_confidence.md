# Piece: The Inflated Confidence

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The mechanism: a positive result read as near certain when the true probability may sit far lower.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

## Load Bearing

A sound test and a sound-seeming line of reasoning together produce a confidently wrong conclusion: the positive result is read as near certain proof of the disease, when the true probability, once the rarity of the condition is folded back in, may sit under one in ten (Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys, 1978). Nothing in the chain up to this point was an error in the sense of a mistake anyone could point to and correct in the moment: the test performed as advertised, the result was read for what it seemed to show, the prior was merely crowded out rather than denied. The confidence is the sum of steps that were each locally reasonable, and it is precisely that sum that makes it wrong.

## Apparent

Reads as a justified, well-earned certainty: the natural conclusion of a good test and a careful reading, nothing left to question.
