# Persona: The Population

## Taxonomy

[Population](position_population.md), inside [The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The archetype of the many among whom a condition sits at a real, low rate, the true prior that any single test result must eventually be weighed against and so often is not.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An irrelevance to the case at hand, as though the wider count of the healthy and the sick mattered nothing beside the one result in front of the reader, when its true rate is the very number the reading needs and lacks.

## Action

Stay few. At [the Population](place_the_population.md), the Population on [Stay Few](plan_stay_few.md) through [Distributing](process_distributing.md) holds [the Rare Condition](piece_the_rare_condition.md), present in only a small fraction of its many, one in a thousand or fewer, the low real rate fixed before any test is given (Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys).

## Shadow

Its true rate is the missing number, and it offers it freely to anyone who asks. The Population is no irrelevance: it holds the condition at exactly the rate it holds it, no more and no fewer, and that rate is not background noise but the very prior the arithmetic needs to turn a positive result into a true probability. It hides nothing and obscures nothing; the rate is a matter of record, published and knowable. But a single reader facing a single result rarely turns to ask it, since the population and its true count sit further from the case at hand than the vivid result already in the reader's grasp. It keeps its honest true rate and loses no cause for blame, the many whose real count is the answer no one thought to want.

## Tell

It held the disease at exactly the rate it always held it, plain to anyone who asked, and almost no one asked.
