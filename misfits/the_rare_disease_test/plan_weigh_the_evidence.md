# Plan: Weigh the Evidence

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reader's seat](position_reader.md), inside [The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reader](position_reader.md)

## Direction

Read the positive result and ask the natural question, how likely is this evidence if the person has the condition, and take the clear answer as your measure of confidence. Know that this is the wrong question answered instead of the right one, that the right question also needs the population's rate folded back in, and that without it your confidence will run far ahead of the true probability.

## Orders

([The Reader](persona_the_reader.md) takes up [the Positive Result](piece_the_positive_result.md) through [Reading](process_reading.md) at [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), asking how likely the result is given the disease, and meets [the Crowded-Out Prior](piece_the_crowded_out_prior.md) and [the Inflated Confidence](piece_the_inflated_confidence.md).)

## Implementation

([Reading](process_reading.md) at [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md) takes up [the Positive Result](piece_the_positive_result.md), because the sensible question about how the evidence fits the disease crowds out the population's rate as [the Crowded-Out Prior](piece_the_crowded_out_prior.md), yielding [the Inflated Confidence](piece_the_inflated_confidence.md) (Kahneman and Tversky; Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys).)

## Targets

- [ ] the positive result is read by asking how likely it is given the disease
- [ ] it is seen that this question, though sensible, is not the question that determines the diagnosis
- [ ] the population's rate is understood to be crowded out by the vividness of the specific result
- [ ] it is owned that the resulting confidence runs ahead of the true probability without the prior folded back in
