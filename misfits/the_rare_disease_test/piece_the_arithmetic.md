## Load Bearing

The correction is not a different fact, only the missing step: the prior folded back against the test's true and false positive rates, which restores the probability the reader actually needs. It is done far more reliably as a count out of a thousand people than as a percent, since natural frequency framing, or a causal story for why the base rate is what it is, sharply improves how well the reckoning is done (Gigerenzer and Hoffrage, 1995). And the neglect this restores against is not a fixed incapacity: it is strongest under pallid, purely statistical framing and weakens markedly once the base rate is given a natural, countable, or causal shape, a documented tendency rather than a wall (Bar-Hillel, 1980; Gigerenzer and Hoffrage, 1995).
