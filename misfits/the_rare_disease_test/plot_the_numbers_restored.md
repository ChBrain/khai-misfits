# Plot: The Numbers Restored

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The confidence has been stated, and a sum that was never done still waits to be done.

## Action

At [the Numbers](place_the_numbers.md), [the Statistician](persona_the_statistician.md) from [the statistician's seat](position_statistician.md) on [Do the Sum](plan_do_the_sum.md) through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) takes up [the Arithmetic](piece_the_arithmetic.md), folding the population's rate back against the instrument's own rates to restore the true probability.

## Stage

Nothing in the record changes, only what is finally laid beside what: the population's real, low rate from the first scene, folded back against the accurate test's own false positive and false negative rates from the second, the same numbers that were true the whole time, simply brought together at last. The sum is easiest done as a count out of a thousand people rather than as a percent, since natural frequency framing, or a causal story for the base rate, sharply improves how reliably the reckoning is carried out (Gigerenzer and Hoffrage). Done this way, the true probability of the condition given a positive result sits far below the near certainty the reading had settled into, often under one in ten, because a small false positive rate applied to a large healthy majority still produces more false positives than true cases drawn from a small affected minority (Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys). The correction holds to a bound and is not a claim of general incapacity: the neglect it corrects is strongest under pallid, purely statistical framing and weakens sharply once the same information is cast as natural frequencies or given a causal shape, a documented tendency and not a fixed wall (Bar-Hillel; Gigerenzer and Hoffrage). No villain: the population offered its rate honestly, the instrument performed exactly as advertised, the reader weighed the vivid result the way representativeness makes natural, and the statistician's sum adds nothing new, it only restores what the honest chain, at every sound step, had crowded out.

## Tension

A true sum must work harder to be believed than the false certainty it corrects, since it restores an abstract number against a confidence that already felt settled. Distinct from The False Trade-Off, a spurious correlation manufactured by a selection rule applied across a whole population, a collider bias with no single judgment being made, whereas here one reasoner with all the relevant numbers available simply weighs specific evidence over a prior, an error of inference and not of sampling, and distinct from Larger in the Looking, where attention magnifies a factor's felt importance in a forward-looking judgment about well-being, whereas here a specific vivid piece of evidence crowds out an abstract prior in a backward-looking probability judgment about a hypothesis given data, attention playing no defining role in either the mechanism or the name.
