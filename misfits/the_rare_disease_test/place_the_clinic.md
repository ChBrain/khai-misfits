# Place: The Clinic

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). Where the test is administered, the positive result is read, the prior is crowded out, and confidence inflates. Hosts [The Test Is Given](plot_the_test_is_given.md), [Read as Proof](plot_read_as_proof.md), [The Wrong Question](plot_the_wrong_question.md), [Sure of It](plot_sure_of_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A single case stepped forward from the many: one member of the population tested, one result returned, one reading made of it, the whole scene narrowed to an instance where the wider population no longer appears in view.

## Holds

[The Accurate Test](piece_the_accurate_test.md).
The instrument itself, performing at its own advertised rate, wrong only rarely in either direction.

[The Positive Result](piece_the_positive_result.md).
The specific evidence returned, vivid and immediate, the thing the reading fastens onto first.

[The Crowded-Out Prior](piece_the_crowded_out_prior.md).
The population's true rate, still true, now set aside, pallid next to the result in hand.

[The Inflated Confidence](piece_the_inflated_confidence.md).
The wrong question answered clearly and read as though it were the right one, the positive taken as near certain.

## Offers

The Clinic offers its seats a case sharp enough to reason about: one instrument, one result, one moment of decision, everything a reader needs to feel certain of an answer.

## Withheld

The wider count. The Clinic gives no view back to the many the case was drawn from, no reminder of how rare the condition was among them, so the very clarity that makes the single case easy to reason about is what withholds the rate that would have kept it in proportion.
