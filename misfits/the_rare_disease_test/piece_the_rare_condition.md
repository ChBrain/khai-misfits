# Piece: The Rare Condition

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The mechanism: the true, low prior that sits beneath any test result, fixed before the test is ever given.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Field](place_the_field.md), carried by [Distributing](process_distributing.md), voiced by [the Population](persona_the_population.md): a great many people, and among them a small, fixed fraction who carry the condition, counted once and set down before anyone is tested.

## Load Bearing

Every test is asked to answer a question about a population it did not choose and cannot change: how many of these people, before any instrument touches them, already carry the condition. That fraction is small, and it is knowable. Physicians and students given exactly this number, alongside a test's false positive rate, mostly failed to fold it back into their answer, some estimating near certainty where the true probability sat under one in ten (Casscells, Schoenberger and Grayboys, 1978). The rarity is not hidden from anyone; it is published, taught, and available on request. It simply sits upstream of the moment that matters, in a place no single test result visits.

## Apparent

Reads as an irrelevant background statistic: a number for the epidemiologist, not for the person or the physician facing one result in front of them.
