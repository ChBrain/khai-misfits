# Position: Instrument

## Taxonomy

[The Rare Disease Test](play_the_rare_disease_test.md). The seat of the test itself, and marks that its accuracy is true and self-contained, silent on the population's rate by remit and not by deceit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A low, published rate of error, wrong only rarely in either direction, true to its own advertised performance.
An answer to one question only, how likely is a result like this if the person truly has the condition.
No claim on the population's rate, since that number is not the Instrument's to know or to state.

## Orders

Hold your rate, and answer only what you were built to answer.
Perform at your true error rate every time, wrong only as often as your own numbers say you will be, and give no answer beyond the one question you were built for.
Know that your accuracy about yourself will be read as certainty about the world, that the population's true rate is outside your remit and your silence on it is not a deception, and that the misreading to come is no fault in your performance.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and is misread all the same.
Because it performs exactly at its true, advertised rate and claims nothing beyond itself, the seat takes no loss and tells no falsehood, and yet its honest accuracy is taken as a certainty about the case at hand that it never claimed and could not supply, the misreading built from a truth correctly told.
It keeps its true rate and its honest silence, and loses no cause for blame, the sound tool mistaken for more than it ever said.

## Drives

Toward the one whose truth about itself is read as truth about the world,
performing at its own low error rate exactly as advertised, and claiming nothing about the population it is applied to,
so the seat that is accurate in every claim it makes is the seat whose accuracy is stretched by the reading into a certainty it never offered.
