# Place: The Wellhead

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The source, where the new money enters and the first hand takes it up near the spring. Hosts [Put In](plot_put_in.md), [First at the Old Prices](plot_first_at_the_old_prices.md).

## Shown

A point where money is entered: a mine yielding ore, a loan desk, a bond sale to the central bank, a government spend before the tax comes in, and a hand close enough to take the money up while the prices around it still hold.

## Holds

[The Injection](piece_the_injection.md).
The new money entered at a point, for the war to fund, the road to build, or the faltering economy to steady.

[The First Spend](piece_the_first_spend.md).
The money spent at the prices that still prevail, taking real goods before the rise reaches them.

## Offers

The old prices, still standing, and money new enough that no other spender has yet bid against it.

## Withheld

Any sight of what the money will cost the far hands, and any share of the advantage to a hand that does not stand here.
