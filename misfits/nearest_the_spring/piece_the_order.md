# Piece: The Order

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The mechanism: the sequence in which the money arrives is the whole determinant of who gains and who loses.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Overlook](place_the_overlook.md), carried by [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md), voiced by [the Reckoner](persona_the_reckoner.md): the ledger laid flat, every hand marked not by what it did but by when the money reached it.

## Load Bearing

Strip away every other variable, the size of the injection, the fairness of each trade, the goodwill of the one who spent, and one thing alone remains as the determinant of gain or loss: the position each hand occupied in the order of arrival (Cantillon; Mises). A hand early in the sequence spends before the rise it helps cause; a hand late in the sequence pays after that rise has already passed through. No transfer is intended between them, no ledger entry moves purchasing power from one to the other by design, and no single hand, transacting honestly at the price it finds, can see the order it stands in or the tilt that order produces. The order is not a rule anyone set; it is simply the shape a diffusion takes when it must travel through hands one at a time.

## Apparent

Reads as mere happenstance, an accident of timing, first or last no more consequential than which checkout line one happened to stand in.
