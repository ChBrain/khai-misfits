# Plot: Put In

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A war to fund, a road to build, a faltering economy to steady: the reason is settled, and the money must go in somewhere.

## Action

At [the Wellhead](place_the_wellhead.md), [the Spring](persona_the_spring.md) from [the spring's seat](position_spring.md) on [Put It In](plan_put_it_in.md) through [Injecting](process_injecting.md) takes up [the Injection](piece_the_injection.md), entering the new money at a point, through a mine or a loan or a bond bought by the central bank or a government spend ahead of the tax that will one day cover it.

## Stage

New money cannot be poured over an entire economy at once, in every hand, at the same instant; it must come in through some particular channel, a mine yielding ore, a loan drawn down, a bond bought, a government spend (Cantillon). Wherever it lands, someone holds the new sum first and everyone else holds none of it yet, and that asymmetry is not a policy choice or a design flaw but the plain shape of a point having a mouth at all. From this point it must spread outward by the ordinary business of buying and selling, since a fresh sum must be given to someone before it can be given to another. No villain: the money is injected to fund a war, to build a road, to steady a faltering economy, to extend credit, to bring ore up from the ground, needful reasons that have nothing to do with who stands nearest the source. The tilt that follows is no part of the aim and no one's design; it is the mere geometry of entering at a point, the plain consequence of the money having to start somewhere.

## Tension

The money now sits at a point, bound by nothing but the injection's need, and bound all the same to favour whoever stands nearest, before a single further exchange has occurred.
