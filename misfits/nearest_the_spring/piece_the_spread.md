# Piece: The Spread

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The mechanism: the new money moves hand to hand, lifting the prices it touches along the way it travels.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Overlook](place_the_overlook.md), carried by [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md), voiced by [the Reckoner](persona_the_reckoner.md): the long view of the wave, watched moving outward from the well across seller after seller, market after market.

## Load Bearing

Money spreads gradually, hand to hand, and prices rise only as it reaches them, not all together and not all at once (Hume). The first hand's payment becomes the next seller's receipt, spent again in turn, and each spend nudges the prices it touches a little higher before moving on. This is the interval: the stretch of time and the chain of transactions in which the new money is still working through the economy, lifting activity and prices unevenly as it goes, some markets feeling it early and others not yet at all. It is the bridge between the first hand's cheap purchase, made before the wave reached it, and the last hand's dear one, made after the wave had already passed through. The spread is not a single event; it is the whole traveling shape of the injection's path.

## Apparent

Reads as prices simply going up, uniformly and all together, as though the economy absorbed the new money everywhere in the same instant.
