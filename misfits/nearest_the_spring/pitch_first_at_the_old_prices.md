# Pitch: First at the Old Prices

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that the hand which spends the new money first spends it at the old prices, and the hand that gets it last meets the new.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Cool and even, the quiet arithmetic of a flow. New money entering at a point, for a needful reason, and spreading from it, so one hand spends before the prices rise and another only after. Nothing here is a theft or a counterfeit; every hand pays a fair price at the going rate, and the difference is only when it paid.

## Undertow

The hand nearest the source takes real goods at the prices that still hold, before the money has bid them up (Cantillon), and the money spreads hand to hand, lifting prices along its path (Hume), so the hand it reaches last meets the risen prices with money that has not grown and loses what the first hand gained (Mises). The whole of the difference is the order in which the money arrived.

## Nerve

That new money cannot be added without redistributing it, since it enters at a point and the prices rise along its path, so the order of arrival alone sets one hand ahead of the rise and another behind it. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since the non-neutral direction is standard while the magnitude and the net winners in any episode are contested (the distributional-policy debate).

## Echo

Stand nearest the spring and drink at the old price, and the one at the far end pays the new for the same cup.
