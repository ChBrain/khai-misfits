# Position: Last Hand

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The seat of the late receiver the money reaches last, and marks that its loss is real and that no one took a coin from it, the prices simply arriving before its money did.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A wage or a fixed income or a store of cash, set before the rise.
The risen prices to meet, since the money has spread and lifted them before reaching this far.
No coin taken from it, and no one to name, only prices that arrived ahead of its money.

## Orders

Hold the line, and meet the prices as they come.
Hold to the wage agreed for the year, the pension fixed, the cash saved, and pay the prices you meet, honestly and in full, as you always have.
Know that the prices have risen ahead of your money by the working of the flow, that your wage buys less than it would have before the money moved, and that the loss is real though no one took a coin from you.

## Loses

Real purchasing power, and no one to name for it.
Because it holds a nominal wage or income set before the rise and meets the risen prices with money that has not grown, the seat pays more for the same and its money buys less, a loss of real purchasing power by nothing but its place at the far end of the stream, last to the money and first to its price.
It keeps its honest holding and its fair dealing, and its loss is real and authored by no one, poorer only by having been far from the spring.

## Drives

Toward the one whose money buys less and who was robbed by no one,
holding a wage or a pension set before the rise and meeting the risen prices with money that has not grown,
so the seat that paid every price honestly is the seat the flow makes poorer, by the plain misfortune of standing last.
