## Holds

[The Spread](piece_the_spread.md).

[The Order](piece_the_order.md).

[The Tilt](piece_the_tilt.md).

## Offers

The whole flow read at once: what no single hand inside it, seeing only its own trade, is placed to see.

## Withheld

Any hand in the mechanism, and any power to move the money once it is entered. The Overlook reads the flow; it does not steer it.
