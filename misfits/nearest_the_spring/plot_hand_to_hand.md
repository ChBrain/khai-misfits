# Plot: Hand to Hand

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The gain at one end of the path and the loss at the other, and between them a stretch of the flow no single hand ever sees.

## Action

At [the Overlook](place_the_overlook.md), [the Reckoner](persona_the_reckoner.md) from [the reckoner's seat](position_reckoner.md) on [Read the Flow](plan_read_the_flow.md) through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) takes up [the Spread](piece_the_spread.md), tracing the money as it moves hand to hand and lifting the prices it touches along the path it travels.

## Stage

From the vantage, the money is seen moving hand to hand, not vanishing into the economy all at once but passing from the first spender to the next and the next after that, each passage lifting the prices it touches a little further along the path (Hume). The rise is not instantaneous and not uniform: it rolls outward from the spring the way a wave rolls out from a stone dropped in still water, reaching some prices sooner and others only later. What was the first hand's cheap purchase becomes, a few exchanges on, the next hand's higher price, and that higher price becomes the one after that, the interval between the spring and the far reach filled with this ordinary, continuous handing on. No villain: each hand in the chain passes the money in a fair exchange, buying and selling at whatever rate it finds, and the diffusion is nothing more than money doing the plain work it was made to do. Seen from inside any single trade, nothing is amiss; only from above does the trade reveal itself as one span of a bridge that begins at the first hand's cheap purchase and ends at the last hand's dear one.

## Tension

The invisible bridge connecting the cheap purchase to the dear one, walked by everyone and seen by no one standing on it.
