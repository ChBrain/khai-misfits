# Piece: The Injection

## Load Bearing

Money cannot enter an economy all at once, in every hand, at the same instant. It enters through a particular channel: a mine, a loan, a bond purchase, a public disbursement, and from that single point it must spread outward by the ordinary business of buying and selling (Cantillon). The injection is a point, not a plane. Wherever it lands, someone holds the new money first and everyone else holds none of it yet. That asymmetry is not a policy choice or a design flaw: it is the plain shape of how money moves, since a fresh sum must always be given to someone before it can be given to another, and the difference in when the water first appears at the well is baked into the well having a mouth at all.

## Apparent

Reads as a neutral addition, a rising tide meant to lift every price by the same measure at the same time, as if the new sum were poured evenly over the whole economy at once.
