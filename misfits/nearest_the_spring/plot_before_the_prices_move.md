# Plot: Before the Prices Move

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The new money sits in the first hand, still unspent, and the prices around it have not yet moved.

## Action

At [the Wellhead](place_the_wellhead.md), [the First Hand](persona_the_first_hand.md) from [the first hand's seat](position_first_hand.md) on [Spend It First](plan_spend_it_first.md) through [Spending](process_spending.md) takes up [the First Spend](piece_the_first_spend.md), spending the new money at the prices that still hold, before it has bid them up.

## Stage

The hand nearest the source spends the new money into a market that still carries the old prices, since those prices have not yet had time to answer the money's presence (Cantillon). The purchase is ordinary: goods taken at the going rate, nothing marked up, nothing negotiated in bad faith, the only prices there were when the money arrived. But the going rate is the old rate, set before this sum existed, so the first hand takes real goods more cheaply than the same sum will buy once it has circulated further. The gain is not a discount granted by anyone; it is the gap between the price now and the price the money's own arrival will eventually cause, a gap that exists only because this hand stood closest to the source and so transacted before the rise it is itself beginning. No villain: it buys as anyone would, at the only prices there were, and games no timing, since the prices had not yet risen and there was nothing yet to game. It could not have known it stood nearest, and did nothing to arrange the standing.

## Tension

A real gain from nothing it did but be near, richer by a difference it neither sought nor could decline.
