# Place: The Far Reach

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). Downstream, far from the source, where the money arrives last and the late hand meets the risen prices. Hosts [Last to the Well](plot_last_to_the_well.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A wage packet counted out at the sum it was set to a year before, a pension paid on schedule, a jar of savings left untouched, each met by prices that climbed while the money was still on its way.

## Holds

[The Last to Arrive](piece_the_last_to_arrive.md).
A wage or a fixed income set before the rise, meeting money that has not grown against prices that have.

## Offers

The same fair exchange offered to every hand along the path, at whatever the going rate has become by the time it reaches here.

## Withheld

The old prices, already spent elsewhere, and any warning that they were rising before the money reached this far.
