# Persona: The First Hand

## Taxonomy

[First Hand](position_first_hand.md), inside [Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The archetype of the early receiver nearest the injection, the one who spends the new money at the prices that still hold and takes real goods before the money has bid them up.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A profiteer who fattens on cheap money, as though it had gamed the timing, when it only spent what it received at the prices it found, exactly as anyone would.

## Action

Spend it first. At [the Wellhead](place_the_wellhead.md), the First Hand on [Spend It First](plan_spend_it_first.md) through [Spending](process_spending.md) takes up [the First Spend](piece_the_first_spend.md), spending the new money at the prevailing prices, before the money has circulated and lifted them, and taking real goods at the old rate (Cantillon).

## Shadow

Its gain is real and its conduct is ordinary. The First Hand is no profiteer: it received the new money and spent it at the prices it found, in a fair exchange at the going rate, the only prices there were when it bought. It did not know it stood nearest the spring, and it gamed no timing, for the prices had not yet risen and there was nothing yet to game. Its advantage is only that it spent before the money it spent had reached far enough to lift the prices, so it took real goods at the old rate and is richer by the difference to come, a gain it did not seek and could not decline. It keeps its ordinary conduct and its fair price, and loses no cause for blame, richer only by having been near.

## Tell

It paid the going price like anyone, and the going price was the old one, and that was the whole of its fortune.
