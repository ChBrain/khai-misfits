# Persona: The Spring

## Taxonomy

[Spring](position_spring.md), inside [Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The archetype of the one who injects the new money into the economy, the mine or the loan or the central bank or the government, for an ordinary and needful reason, the point from which the money must spread.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A schemer who prints for the benefit of its friends, as though the tilt were the injection's purpose, when the money is put in for a war or a road or a faltering economy and must enter somewhere whatever the aim.

## Action

Put it in. At [the Wellhead](place_the_wellhead.md), the Spring on [Put It In](plan_put_it_in.md) through [Injecting](process_injecting.md) holds [the Injection](piece_the_injection.md), entering the new money at a point, through a mine or a loan or a bond bought or a government spend, for a reason that has nothing to do with who stands nearest (Cantillon).

## Shadow

It injects for a purpose, and the purpose is not the tilt. The Spring is no schemer: the money is put in to fund a war, to build a road, to steady a faltering economy, to extend credit, to bring ore up from the ground, and the tilt that follows is no part of the aim and no one's design. But the money cannot enter everywhere at once: it must enter at a point, through some particular hand, and from that point it must spread, so the injection that serves a needful end also, by its mere geometry, sets some hands nearer the source than others. It keeps its needful purpose and its clean intent, and cannot enter the money without giving someone the first of it, the source that grants an advantage it never meant to grant.

## Tell

It put the money in for a reason that had nothing to do with who stood nearest, and someone stood nearest all the same.
