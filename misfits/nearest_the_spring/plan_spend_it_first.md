# Plan: Spend It First

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the first hand's seat](position_first_hand.md), inside [Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [First Hand](position_first_hand.md)

## Direction

Spend the new money on the goods you want, at the prevailing prices, since those are the only prices there are and you buy as anyone would. Know that you spend before the money has bid the prices up, that the goods you take at the old rate are a real gain by the difference to come, and that the advantage is only that you were near, not that you gamed the timing.

## Orders

([The First Hand](persona_the_first_hand.md) takes up [the First Spend](piece_the_first_spend.md) through [Spending](process_spending.md) at [the Wellhead](place_the_wellhead.md), spending the new money at the prices that still hold.)

## Implementation

([Spending](process_spending.md) at [the Wellhead](place_the_wellhead.md) takes up [the First Spend](piece_the_first_spend.md), because to spend before the money has circulated is to buy at the prices that still prevail, taking real goods at the old rate (Cantillon).)

## Targets

- [ ] the new money is spent at the prevailing prices, before they rise
- [ ] real goods are taken at the old rate, a gain by the difference to come
- [ ] it is seen that the spending is ordinary, a fair exchange at the going price
- [ ] it is owned that the advantage is only nearness, not a gamed timing
