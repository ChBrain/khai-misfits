# Piece: The First Spend

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The mechanism: the hand nearest the injection spends the new money before the prices it will meet have risen.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Wellhead](place_the_wellhead.md), carried by [Spending](process_spending.md), voiced by [the First Hand](persona_the_first_hand.md): a purchase made minutes or days after the new money arrives, paid at the counter that has not yet heard the news.

## Load Bearing

The hand that receives the new money first also spends it first, and it spends it into a market that still carries the old prices, since those prices have not yet had time to answer the new money's presence (Cantillon). The purchase is ordinary: goods taken at the going rate, nothing marked up, nothing negotiated in bad faith. But the going rate is the old rate, set before this sum existed, so the first hand takes real goods more cheaply than the same sum will buy once it has circulated. The gain is not a discount granted by anyone; it is the gap between the price now and the price the money's own arrival will eventually cause, a gap that exists only because this hand stood closest to the source and so transacted before the rise it is itself starting.

## Apparent

Reads as a plain and ordinary transaction: money changes hands for goods at a fair price, nothing gained beyond the goods received.
