# Piece: The Last to Arrive

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The mechanism: the hand the new money reaches last meets the risen prices before it meets the money.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Far Reach](place_the_far_reach.md), carried by [Holding](process_holding.md), voiced by [the Last Hand](persona_the_last_hand.md): a wage packet counted the same as last year's, a pension check unchanged, a jar of cash saved and untouched, set against a grocery bill that is not.

## Load Bearing

Not every hand sits near the spring. Some hold a wage set for the year, a fixed income, or plain cash savings, money that does not grow as the new sum diffuses through the economy (Mises). Those hands go on paying at the shop and the market while the prices there have already climbed, lifted by every hand that spent the new money before it reached them. So the last hand meets the risen world with income or savings fixed at the old level, and the same sum buys less than it would have before the injection began its work. No coin is taken from this hand, no transaction singles it out; it simply stands at the far end of a wave that arrived at the prices before it arrived at the wallet. The loss is exactly the gap the first hand gained, paid not in a taking but in a shortfall.

## Apparent

Reads as ordinary hardship, the plain cost of living having gone up, nothing more than the world getting a little more expensive.
