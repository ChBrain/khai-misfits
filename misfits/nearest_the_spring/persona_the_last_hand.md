# Persona: The Last Hand

## Taxonomy

[Last Hand](position_last_hand.md), inside [Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The archetype of the late receiver the money reaches last, on a wage or a fixed income set before the rise, who meets the risen prices with money that has not grown and loses real purchasing power.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A grumbler who blames the cost of living on plots, as though someone had reached into its pocket, when no one moved a coin from it and the prices simply rose before its money did.

## Action

Hold the line. At [the Far Reach](place_the_far_reach.md), the Last Hand on [Hold the Line](plan_hold_the_line.md) through [Holding](process_holding.md) takes up [the Last to Arrive](piece_the_last_to_arrive.md), holding a wage or a fixed income or cash set before the rise, and meeting the higher prices before the new money reaches it (Mises).

## Shadow

Its loss is real and no one took a coin from it. The Last Hand is no grumbler inventing a plot: it holds a wage agreed for the year, a pension fixed, a store of cash, and it meets the risen prices honestly and pays them, and its money buys less than it would have before the money moved. But nothing was taken from it: the prices rose ahead of its money by the plain working of the flow, and it lost real purchasing power by nothing but its place at the far end of the stream, last to the money and first to its price. It keeps its honest holding and its fair dealing, and its loss is real and authored by no one, poorer only by having been far.

## Tell

No one reached into its pocket, and yet its wage buys less, because the prices arrived before the money did.
