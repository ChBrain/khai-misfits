# Piece: The Tilt

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The mechanism: the net redistribution from the late receivers to the early, real in direction and disputed in size.

## Owner

## Load Bearing

Sum every early gain and every late loss and a net remains, running from the hands the money reached last to the hands it reached first, visible only from above and authored by no one below (Mises). No single transaction in the chain carries this pattern; each was a fair exchange at the going rate. The tilt exists only in the aggregate, the shape the whole path leaves behind once it is added up. And here is the bound that holds it honest: that the path is non-neutral, that relative prices and the distribution shift while the new money is still spreading, is standard across the accounts (Mises). But the size of that shift, and even who nets out ahead in a given episode, are genuinely contested, since the same expansion that reaches asset holders first may also lower unemployment and lift the wages of the hands it reaches last (the distributional-policy debate). The direction is the reckoning; the magnitude is not.

## Apparent

Reads as a settled robbery of the many by the few, a fixed sum quietly moved from the poor to the rich by the act of creating money.
