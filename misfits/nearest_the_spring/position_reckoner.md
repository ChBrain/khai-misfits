# Position: Reckoner

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The seat of the one who reads the whole flow from above, and marks that the tilt it sees is real and its direction plain, and that its size and net incidence in any episode are contested, so the reckoning is bounded.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A vantage over the whole flow, the sight of the spread and the order no hand inside it has.
A clear reading of the direction, the net running from the late receivers to the early.
A held bound, the knowledge that the size of the tilt and even the net winners in any episode are contested, so the reckoning is of a direction and not a settled sum.

## Orders

Read the flow, and name the tilt without inflating it.
Trace the money hand to hand from the Overlook, read the order of arrival that sets one ahead of the rise and another behind, and name the net redistribution from the late to the early that no hand in the flow can see.
Know that the direction is standard and real, that the magnitude and even the net winners in a given episode are contested since the same expansion may lift the wages of the last, and that to claim a settled robbery is to read more than the flow shows.

## Loses

Nothing it can name, and holds a truth it must not overstate.
Because it sees from above what no hand in the flow can see, the seat takes no loss, and yet it carries the harder discipline: to name a real tilt and its direction while refusing the settled sum, since the size and net incidence are contested and the same money that reaches the first may also lift the last.
It keeps its clear sight and its held bound, and loses no cause for blame, the one who reads a real tilt and will not inflate it into a robbery no one committed.

## Drives

Toward the one who sees the tilt the hands cannot and holds it to what it is,
tracing the spread and the order from above and naming the net from the late to the early,
so the seat that alone can read the redistribution holds even its own reading to a bound, a direction named and a size left contested.
