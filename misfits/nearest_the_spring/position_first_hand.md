# Position: First Hand

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The seat of the early receiver nearest the injection, and marks that its gain is real and its conduct ordinary, spending at the only prices there were.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The new money, early, before it has spread.
The prices that still prevail, since the money it holds has not yet circulated to lift them.
No sight of an advantage, since the prices have not risen and there is nothing yet to game.

## Orders

Spend it first, at the prices that still hold.
Spend the new money on the goods you want, at the prevailing prices, since those are the only prices there are and you buy as anyone would.
Know that you spend before the money has bid the prices up, that the goods you take at the old rate are a real gain by the difference to come, and that the advantage is only that you were near, not that you gamed the timing.

## Loses

Nothing, and gains what it did not seek.
Because it spends at the prices it finds, in a fair exchange at the going rate, the seat takes no loss and does nothing untoward, and yet by spending before the money it spent had lifted the prices it takes real goods at the old rate and is richer by the difference, a gain it did not seek and could not decline.
It keeps its ordinary conduct and its fair price, and loses no cause for blame, richer only by having been near the spring.

## Drives

Toward the one who spends the new money at the old prices and is enriched by nothing it did,
buying as anyone would at the only prices there were, before the money had bid them up,
so the seat that gamed no timing and paid the going rate is the seat the flow makes richer, by the plain fortune of standing first.
