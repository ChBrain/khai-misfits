# Process: Spending

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). Borne at [the Wellhead](place_the_wellhead.md), precipitated by [the First Hand](persona_the_first_hand.md), in [First at the Old Prices](plot_first_at_the_old_prices.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The First Hand](persona_the_first_hand.md), the one who receives the new money nearest the source and spends it.

## Direction

From the money received toward real goods taken at the old prices.

## Lever

To spend before the money has circulated is to buy at the prices that still prevail, taking real goods at the old rate (Cantillon).
