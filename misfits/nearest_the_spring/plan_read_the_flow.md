# Plan: Read the Flow

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reckoner's seat](position_reckoner.md), inside [Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reckoner](position_reckoner.md)

## Direction

Trace the money hand to hand from the Overlook, read the order of arrival that sets one ahead of the rise and another behind, and name the net redistribution from the late to the early that no hand in the flow can see. Know that the direction is standard and real, that the magnitude and even the net winners in a given episode are contested since the same expansion may lift the wages of the last, and that to claim a settled robbery is to read more than the flow shows.
