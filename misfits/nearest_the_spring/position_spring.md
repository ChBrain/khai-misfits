# Position: Spring

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The seat of the one who injects the new money, and marks that the injection serves a needful end and that the tilt is no part of its aim but the mere geometry of entering at a point.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

New money to put into the economy, and a needful reason to put it in.
A point of entry, since the money must come in through some particular hand and cannot come in everywhere at once.
No way to grant no one the first of it, since to enter at a point is to set some hands nearer the source than others.

## Orders

Put it in, at the point it must enter, for the reason it is needed.
Inject the new money through the mine or the loan or the bond or the government spend, for the war or the road or the faltering economy that calls for it, since the money must enter somewhere to do its work.
Know that it cannot enter everywhere at once, that the point of entry sets some hands nearest the source, and that the tilt to come is no part of your aim but the plain consequence of the money having to start somewhere.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and this is the point.
Because it injects the money for a needful end and the tilt is no part of its aim, the seat takes no loss and does its proper work, and yet the mere geometry of entering at a point grants the nearest hand an advantage it never meant to grant, so its needful injection is the source of a redistribution it did not design.
It keeps its needful purpose and its clean intent, and loses no cause for blame, the source that must give someone the first of the money.

## Drives

Toward the one who injects the money for a needful end and cannot help the tilt,
entering it at the point it must enter, for the war or the road or the economy that calls for it, blind to no one on purpose,
so the seat whose injection serves an aim that has nothing to do with nearness is the seat from which the nearest hand takes its unmeant advantage.
