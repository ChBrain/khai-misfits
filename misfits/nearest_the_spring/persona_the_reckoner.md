# Persona: The Reckoner

## Taxonomy

[Reckoner](position_reckoner.md), inside [Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md). The archetype of the one who reads the whole flow from above, seeing the spread, the order of arrival, and the net tilt no single hand can see, and holding the bound on what the tilt can be claimed to be.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An ideologue who reads a grand injustice into a bookkeeping detail, as though the tilt were a settled robbery of the many, when its size and even its net direction in any given episode are genuinely in dispute.

## Action

Read the flow. At [the Overlook](place_the_overlook.md), the Reckoner on [Read the Flow](plan_read_the_flow.md) through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) takes up [the Spread](piece_the_spread.md), tracing the money hand to hand and the prices lifting along its path, then meets [the Order](piece_the_order.md) and [the Tilt](piece_the_tilt.md), the net running from the late to the early (Hume; Mises).

## Shadow

It sees what no hand in the flow can see, and it must not claim more than it sees. The Reckoner is no ideologue: from the Overlook it can watch the money spread and the prices lift along its path, and read the order of arrival that sets one hand ahead of the rise and another behind it, a redistribution invisible from inside the flow because each transaction there is a fair exchange. What it sees is real, and it names the direction rightly, from the late to the early. But it holds its own bound: that money is non-neutral along its path is standard, yet the size of the tilt and even the net winners in any given episode are contested, since the same expansion that reaches asset holders first may also lower unemployment and lift the wages of the last, so the reckoning is of a direction and not of a settled sum. It keeps its clear sight and its held bound, and reads a real tilt without inflating it into a robbery no one committed.

## Tell

It sees the tilt the hands cannot, names its direction, and stops there, because the size of it is not yet settled.
