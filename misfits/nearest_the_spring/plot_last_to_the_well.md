# Plot: Last to the Well

## Taxonomy

[Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A wage counted out at the sum agreed a year before, a pension paid on schedule, a jar of savings left untouched, each about to meet prices that have already climbed.

## Action

At [the Far Reach](place_the_far_reach.md), [the Last Hand](persona_the_last_hand.md) from [the last hand's seat](position_last_hand.md) on [Hold the Line](plan_hold_the_line.md) through [Holding](process_holding.md) takes up [the Last to Arrive](piece_the_last_to_arrive.md), holding a wage or fixed income set before the rise and meeting the risen prices with money that has not grown.

## Stage

By the time the new money reaches the last hands, the wage earner whose pay was set for the year, the pensioner on a fixed income, the saver who held cash, the prices those hands must pay have already risen along the path the money has travelled (Mises). The wage or income these hands hold was fixed before the rise, agreed at a sum that made sense under the old prices, and it does not grow simply because prices elsewhere have. So the last hand meets the higher prices with money that has not grown, and buys less than the same sum would have bought before the money moved, poorer by the same difference the first hand gained. No villain: no coin was taken from it, no contract broken, no price unfairly charged; the prices simply arrived at this reach before the money did, by the plain working of the flow rather than any hand's design. It pays every price honestly, in full, as it always has, and the loss is real all the same.

## Tension

A real loss with no one to name for it, the wage unchanged and what it buys diminished.
