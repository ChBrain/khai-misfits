# Plan: Hold the Line

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the last hand's seat](position_last_hand.md), inside [Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Last Hand](position_last_hand.md)

## Direction

Hold to the wage agreed for the year, the pension fixed, the cash saved, and pay the prices you meet, honestly and in full, as you always have. Know that the prices have risen ahead of your money by the working of the flow, that your wage buys less than it would have before the money moved, and that the loss is real though no one took a coin from you.

## Orders

([The Last Hand](persona_the_last_hand.md) takes up [the Last to Arrive](piece_the_last_to_arrive.md) through [Holding](process_holding.md) at [the Far Reach](place_the_far_reach.md), holding a wage or fixed income set before the rise and meeting the risen prices.)

## Implementation

([Holding](process_holding.md) at [the Far Reach](place_the_far_reach.md) takes up [the Last to Arrive](piece_the_last_to_arrive.md), because a nominal wage or income set before the rise meets the risen prices with money that has not grown, so it buys less than it would have (Mises).)

## Targets

- [ ] a wage or fixed income or cash set before the rise is held
- [ ] the risen prices are met and paid honestly, with money that has not grown
- [ ] it is seen that the prices arrived ahead of the money by the working of the flow
- [ ] it is owned that the loss of real purchasing power is real though no one took a coin
