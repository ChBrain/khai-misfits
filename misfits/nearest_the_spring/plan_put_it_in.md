# Plan: Put It In

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the spring's seat](position_spring.md), inside [Nearest the Spring](play_nearest_the_spring.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Spring](position_spring.md)

## Direction

Inject the new money through the mine or the loan or the bond or the government spend, for the war or the road or the faltering economy that calls for it, since the money must enter somewhere to do its work. Know that it cannot enter everywhere at once, that the point of entry sets some hands nearest the source, and that the tilt to come is no part of your aim but the plain consequence of the money having to start somewhere.

## Orders

([The Spring](persona_the_spring.md) holds [the Injection](piece_the_injection.md) through [Injecting](process_injecting.md) at [the Wellhead](place_the_wellhead.md), entering the new money at a point for a needful reason.)

## Implementation

([Injecting](process_injecting.md) at [the Wellhead](place_the_wellhead.md) holds [the Injection](piece_the_injection.md), because the money must enter through some particular hand, so it enters at a point and from that point must spread (Cantillon).)

## Targets

- [ ] the new money is entered at a point, for the needful reason it is called for
- [ ] it is seen that the money cannot enter everywhere at once
- [ ] it is understood that the point of entry sets some hands nearest the source
- [ ] it is owned that the tilt is no part of the aim but the geometry of entering at a point
