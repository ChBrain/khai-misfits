# Plot: Nobody Is Performing

## Taxonomy

[Not While You Are Watching](play_not_while_you_are_watching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An hour given freely, and an instruction to speak naturally.

## Action

[Speaking to the person present](process_speaking_to_the_person_present.md): [the speaker](persona_the_speaker.md), holding [Speaker](position_speaker.md), talks to the visitor at [the interview](place_the_interview.md).

## Stage

An ordinary, honest conversation, and a register that is not the one wanted.

## Tension

Nothing strategic is happening, which is the whole of the boundary with every failure that resembles this.
Nobody is impressing, misleading or managing anybody: speech shifts toward whoever is being addressed automatically, below the level at which anybody decides things, and is invisible to the person producing it. So the instruction to be natural names something that cannot be done on request, and the request to ignore the machine cannot be complied with by anybody however willing. The change is not a response to being measured. It is a response to somebody being there, and full cooperation is what produces it.
