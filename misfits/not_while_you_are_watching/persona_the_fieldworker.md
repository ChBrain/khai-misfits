# Persona: The Fieldworker

## Taxonomy

[Fieldworker](position_fieldworker.md). The one who has to be in the room to get the data.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A grant, a schedule of interviews, a recorder, and a quantity that is defined as what happens when they are not there.

## Action

Arrives. Explains. Turns the machine on. Listens.

## Shadow

Is the instrument and the interference, and cannot be one without being the other.
There is no version of the work in which the data arrives without somebody collecting it, and the collector is a person, in a room, whom the speaker can see. So the seat is not making an error that better training would remove: it is occupying a position that the definition of the target quantity excludes, and it occupies it because that is the only position from which anything can be heard at all.
