## Load Bearing

It is the thing that makes the observation ethical and the thing that makes it observed, in one act.
Telling somebody they are being studied is what they are owed and is not negotiable, and it is also precisely the information that produces the shift. The two are not separable by any wording: a form that secured consent without conveying that a study was happening would not be consent. So the object is not an obstacle placed in front of good research, it is the point at which what is owed to a person and what the method needs turn out to be the same fact read two ways.
