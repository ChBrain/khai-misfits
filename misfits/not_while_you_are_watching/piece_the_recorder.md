# Piece: The Recorder

## Owner

## Place

## Load Bearing

Making it smaller, quieter or forgotten reduces the effect and never removes it, because the machine was never the whole of the problem.
A recorder that nobody could see would still leave a researcher in the room, and the shift is toward the person rather than toward the device. So every improvement to the object is a real improvement and a bounded one, and the residue after the best possible machine is the part contributed by somebody being there, which no machine addresses.

## Apparent

A neutral witness. The part of the method that simply captures what happens.
