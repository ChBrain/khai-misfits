# Persona: The Sceptic

## Taxonomy

[Sceptic](position_sceptic.md). The one who asks how big the effect actually is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A re-analysis, a thin original dataset, and a famous effect that shrank when the numbers were looked at again.

## Action

Asks for the magnitude rather than the mechanism.
Points out that a structure everybody agrees on has been carrying a quantity nobody has established.

## Shadow

Is asking the right question, and the question has no answer available to anybody.
Establishing the size of the residue means comparing observed behaviour against unobserved behaviour, and the second term is the thing that cannot be had: any measurement of it is an observation. So the challenge is correct, is unanswerable in principle rather than for want of effort, and the same structure that produces the difficulty also protects it from being sized.

## Tell

Demands a number the mechanism guarantees nobody can produce.
