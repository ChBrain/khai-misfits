# Plot: And the Residue Cannot Be Sized

## Taxonomy

[Not While You Are Watching](play_not_while_you_are_watching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A reasonable refusal to take the size of an effect on trust.

## Action

[Weighing whether the effect is real](process_weighing_whether_the_effect_is_real.md): [the sceptic](persona_the_sceptic.md), holding [Sceptic](position_sceptic.md), grants the mechanism and asks for the magnitude.

## Stage

A sharper account of what has been measured, and a comparison that cannot be made.

## Tension

The right question is unanswerable in principle, and the same structure that causes the difficulty is what hides its size.
Sizing the residue means holding observed behaviour against unobserved behaviour, and any measurement of the second term is an observation, so the comparison is not merely expensive or difficult but excluded. Re-analysis, larger samples and better design all sharpen the first term and none supplies the second. Which leaves a discipline that knows the direction of its error, cannot state the magnitude, and cannot be faulted for either, since the missing number is missing by the same mechanism the field is describing.
