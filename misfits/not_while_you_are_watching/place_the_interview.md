# Place: The Interview

## Taxonomy

[Not While You Are Watching](play_not_while_you_are_watching.md). The room the fieldworker can get into.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A table, two chairs, a consent form signed, a recorder with its light on, and an hour given freely.
Everything correct: the study explained, the questions fair, the transcription careful, the participant willing.

## Holds

[The Fieldworker](persona_the_fieldworker.md), who is the instrument.
[The Speaker](persona_the_speaker.md), who is not performing.
[The Recorder](piece_the_recorder.md), which is the access and the interference in one object.

## Offers

An accurate, complete and ethically obtained record of everything said in it.

## Withheld

The one register the room was entered to collect.
The place is constituted by somebody having come to study somebody, so the speech it contains is speech addressed to a researcher, and that is a real variety of speech and not the one wanted. No improvement to the room reaches this: a warmer manner, a smaller machine and a longer visit all change how far it is off, and none of them makes the room into a room the researcher is not in.
