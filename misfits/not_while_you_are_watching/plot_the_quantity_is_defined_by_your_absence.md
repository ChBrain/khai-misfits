# Plot: The Quantity Is Defined by Your Absence

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

A study whose object is how people speak when nobody is studying them.

## Action

[The Vernacular](piece_the_vernacular.md) is specified at [the kitchen](place_the_kitchen.md), and [the fieldworker](persona_the_fieldworker.md), holding [Fieldworker](position_fieldworker.md), reads what the specification excludes.

## Stage

A target that is abundant, continuous, and defined by a condition the study cannot satisfy.

## Tension

This is not a hard measurement problem, and treating it as one is the mistake that keeps the room busy.
Most difficult measurements are difficult contingently: the instrument is coarse, the sample is thin, the signal is buried, and better work reaches them. Here the specification of the target contains the absence of an observer, so the difficulty is in the definition rather than in the apparatus, and a perfect instrument in perfect hands returns the same class of object as a poor one. Nothing that counts as improvement in method is aimed at the place the difficulty is.
