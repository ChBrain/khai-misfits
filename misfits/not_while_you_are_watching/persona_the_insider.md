# Persona: The Insider

## Taxonomy

[Insider](position_insider.md). The one who joins the network instead of visiting it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Two years, a introduction from somebody's cousin, and a place at the table that was earned rather than arranged.

## Action

Comes in as a friend of a friend.
Stays long enough to stop being a visitor.

## Shadow

Has the best remedy anybody has found, and it works by changing what is being observed.
Speech to a member of the network is closer to the target than speech to a stranger, which is real progress and is why the method is used. But it is speech to a member, not speech unobserved, and the observer is now inside the thing they came to describe: the network has a new person in it, the researcher owes and is owed, and what can be said in front of them is bounded by that. The residue is smaller and it is no longer possible to say by how much.

## Tell

Reduces the distortion by becoming part of the setting that produces it.
