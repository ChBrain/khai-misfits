# Position: Speaker

## Taxonomy

[Not While You Are Watching](play_not_while_you_are_watching.md). The seat that is being observed and is not performing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An hour, a visitor, and no reason to be anything other than helpful.

## Orders

Answer. Talk. Be yourself.

## Loses

Nothing, and it has nothing it could give differently.
Speech shifts toward whoever is addressed automatically and below the level decisions are made at, so an instruction to speak naturally cannot be carried out by anybody.

## Drives

Toward an ordinary conversation with the person actually in the room.
