# Not While You Are Watching: Reference

The research warrant for Not While You Are Watching.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the observer's paradox as sociolinguistics states it, and on what a
target quantity specified by an absence does to the method that must reach it, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging fieldwork as **a method whose object is defined by the method's own absence**: the thing being studied
is how people speak when nobody has come to study them. It happens continuously, in every kitchen, and it is
the most abundant material in the world. The only way to collect it is for somebody to be there, and there is
no such thing as speech addressed to nobody. The play models the documented mechanics, the target whose
specification excludes the condition of measurement, the researcher who is instrument and interference at once,
the shift that nobody performs and nobody can suspend, the bar on the one clean method that is right for
reasons unconnected to any study, the remedy that works by joining the thing observed, and the magnitude the
same mechanism withholds, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a clumsy researcher or
a participant putting on a show.

The governing law is that the target is specified by the absence of an observer and observation is the only
access to it, so the act of measuring is the act that removes the quantity being measured.

**Nothing strategic is happening, which is the whole of the boundary with every failure that resembles this.**
Nobody is impressing, misleading or managing anybody. Speech shifts toward whoever is being addressed
automatically, below the level at which anybody decides things, and invisibly to the person producing it, which
is why the instruction to speak naturally names something that cannot be done on request and why asking
somebody to ignore the recorder cannot be complied with however willing they are. The change is not a response
to being measured; it is a response to somebody being there, and full cooperation is what produces it.

What removes any possibility of blame from the fieldworker is that the instrument and the interference are one
person. There is no version of the work in which the data arrives without somebody collecting it, and the
collector is a person in a room whom the speaker can see. Better method therefore selects among ways of being
present rather than escaping presence, and a perfect instrument in perfect hands returns the same class of
object as a poor one.

What removes it from the ethics reviewer is that the bar would be worth having if no study existed anywhere.
Covert recording obtains the target outright and is impermissible for reasons that have nothing to do with this
or any research question, and in most jurisdictions it is unlawful as well. There is no conflict of values here
to adjudicate: telling somebody they are being studied is both what they are owed and precisely the information
that produces the shift, so a form that secured consent without conveying that a study was happening would not
be consent.

And the sceptic closes the case against the last party, by asking the right question and finding it excluded.
Establishing the size of the residue means holding observed behaviour against unobserved behaviour, and any
measurement of the second term is an observation. Re-analysis, larger samples and better design sharpen the
first term and none of them supplies the second, which leaves a discipline that knows the direction of its
error, cannot state the magnitude, and cannot be faulted for either.

It is distinct from the house's **Metric Fixation**, which the slate line for this misfit required it to be
named clear of, and the line is clean. There a measure becomes a target and somebody with an interest in the
number optimises against it, so there is a pressure to relieve, a proxy to distort and an interested party.
Here there is no target, no incentive and no interest: the speaker gains nothing by any register and is not
responding to a measurement at all, only to a person being present. One failure is removed by taking the
incentive away, and the other is not touched by it.

It is distinct from the house's **The Same Print Twice**, where an examiner is contaminated by context they had
no reason to be given and the repair is to withhold it: there the **observer** is what moves and sequential
unmasking reaches the failure. Here the observer is unbiased, the transcription is accurate and the analysis is
sound, and it is the **observed** that moves. The two are the same joint approached from opposite sides, and
only one of them has a procedure that fixes it.

It is distinct from the house's **True Until It Was Used**, where an estimated relation is a summary of
behaviour under an arrangement and using it to change the arrangement ends what it summarised: there the
invalidation is caused by the **use** of a finding, arrives afterwards, and would happen with no observer
anywhere near the households. Here nothing is used and nothing is published: the interference is simultaneous
with the collection and is produced by presence rather than by consequence.

And it sits beside the house's **Sandbag**, which holds the Hawthorne studies themselves for something else
entirely, the bank wiring room's peer enforcement of an output norm. That is worth naming rather than leaving
implicit: the most famous body of evidence in this territory is already in the house, carrying a different
mechanism, and this warrant deliberately does not take it. The Hawthorne effect enters here only through the
re-analysis that disputes it, cited below as a bound and never as a spine.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the researcher as
  clumsy or the participant as performing, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the
  target's specification excludes the condition of measurement, so the difficulty is in the definition and not
  the apparatus; the instrument is a person and there is no data without one; the shift is automatic, invisible
  to the speaker and impossible to suspend on request, so cooperation is what produces it; the bar on covert
  work is right on grounds that would hold if no study existed; entering the network is the best method anybody
  has and works by making the observer a member; and the demand for a magnitude is correct and is excluded by
  the same structure. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that
  the act of measuring is the act that removes the quantity being measured.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Covert recording works and is
  barred, and the bar is not a cost to be traded off but a boundary that would stand anyway. A smaller or
  hidden machine reduces the effect and leaves the part contributed by a person being in the room, which is the
  larger part. Long acquaintance reduces it and buys the reduction by making the observer part of the setting.
  The rapid anonymous method genuinely escapes the interview frame and returns one variable in one context,
  which is a real result and not a general instrument. Emotionally engaging questions work by making the
  observation matter less rather than by removing it. And knowing the whole structure, which every party does,
  leaves the fieldworker still needing to be in the room.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the paradox, the department store study, the account of demand
  characteristics, the social network method and the re-analysis are theirs. The bounds are carried with them:
  **how large the effect is remains genuinely contested**, and the re-analysis cited above found little support
  for it in the original illumination data, which the play carries as a live objection and does not settle;
  the play therefore stages the **structure**, that a target specified by an observer's absence is reached only
  by an observer's presence, and never a claim about how far any particular observation sits from its target;
  the sociolinguistic case is used as the clearest statement rather than the only domain, since the same
  structure is met independently in experimental psychology and in field research generally; the account treats
  observation as one visit and one register, which is the play's own simplification of speech that varies
  continuously with audience and setting; and the ethical bar is described as it stands and is not evaluated
  here. **The play makes no claim that any study was badly done, or that consent requirements should be
  relaxed**, only that the quantity wanted is defined by nobody watching, and that somebody has to.
