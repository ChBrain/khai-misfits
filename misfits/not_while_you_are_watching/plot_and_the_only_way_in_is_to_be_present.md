## Action

## Tension

The instrument and the interference are the same person, so they cannot be separated by care.
There is no version of the work in which data arrives without somebody collecting it, and the collector is a person in a room whom the speaker can see. Improving [the recorder](piece_the_recorder.md) is real and bounded: a machine nobody could see would still leave somebody in the room, and the shift is toward the person rather than the device. So method here selects among ways of being present, and the seat cannot be relieved by being better at the seat.
