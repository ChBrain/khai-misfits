# Persona: The Speaker

## Taxonomy

[Speaker](position_speaker.md). The one being recorded, who is not performing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A visitor at the table, a machine with a light on it, and an hour of their afternoon given freely.

## Action

Talks to the person in front of them.
Talks the way anybody talks to somebody they have just met.

## Shadow

Is doing nothing strategic, which is what makes this different from every failure that looks like it.
There is no attempt to impress, mislead or manage anybody: speech shifts toward whoever is being addressed automatically and below the level anybody decides things at, so telling them to speak naturally does not help and asking them to ignore the recorder cannot be complied with. The change is not a response to being measured. It is a response to somebody being there.

## Tell

Cooperates completely, and cooperation is the interference.
