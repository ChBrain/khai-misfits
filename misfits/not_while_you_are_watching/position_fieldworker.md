# Position: Fieldworker

## Taxonomy

[Not While You Are Watching](play_not_while_you_are_watching.md). The seat that must be present to collect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A method, a recorder, a schedule, and a target quantity defined by its own absence.

## Orders

Arrive. Explain. Record. Transcribe.

## Loses

The thing it came for, in the act of coming for it.
No training removes this, because the seat is not making a mistake: it is occupying the one position from which anything can be heard, and that position is excluded by the definition of what is wanted.

## Drives

Toward an accurate record of speech addressed to a researcher.
