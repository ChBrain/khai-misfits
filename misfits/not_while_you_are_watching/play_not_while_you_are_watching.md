# Play: Not While You Are Watching

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Not While You Are Watching.
The thing being studied is how people speak when nobody has come to study them. It happens continuously, in every kitchen, and it is the most abundant material in the world. The only way to collect it is for somebody to be there, and there is no such thing as speech to nobody.

## Arc

The bend is this: the target quantity is specified by the absence of an observer, and observation is the only access to it.
It runs on a definition rather than an apparatus.
[The Quantity Is Defined by Your Absence](plot_the_quantity_is_defined_by_your_absence.md). [The Vernacular](piece_the_vernacular.md) is not hard to measure the way a faint signal is hard to measure: the specification of the target rules out the condition under which any measurement can be taken, so a perfect instrument returns the same class of object as a poor one.
And the only door is the wrong door.
[And the Only Way In Is to Be Present](plot_and_the_only_way_in_is_to_be_present.md). [The Fieldworker](persona_the_fieldworker.md) is the instrument and the interference, and improving [the recorder](piece_the_recorder.md) is real and bounded, since a machine nobody could see would still leave a person in the room.
And nobody is performing.
[Nobody Is Performing](plot_nobody_is_performing.md). [The Speaker](persona_the_speaker.md) is not impressing or managing anybody: speech shifts toward whoever is addressed automatically and below the level anybody decides at, so the instruction to be natural names a thing nobody can do on request.
Then [The Method That Works Is Barred](plot_the_method_that_works_is_barred.md).
[The Consent Form](piece_the_consent_form.md) closes the clean route, correctly: telling somebody they are being studied is what they are owed and is exactly the information that produces the shift, and a form that secured consent without conveying it would not be consent.
And [becoming part of it is the best remedy](plot_becoming_part_of_it_is_the_best_remedy.md). [The Insider](persona_the_insider.md) enters [the kitchen](place_the_kitchen.md) through the network, gets much nearer the target, and is now inside the thing they came to describe.
And [the residue cannot be sized](plot_and_the_residue_cannot_be_sized.md). [The Sceptic](persona_the_sceptic.md) is right to ask how large the effect is, and the comparison requires unobserved behaviour, which any measurement of would be an observation.

## Triggers

**[The Quantity Is Defined by Your Absence](plot_the_quantity_is_defined_by_your_absence.md)**
Cue: a study whose object is how people speak when nobody is studying them. Closes when the difficulty has been located in the specification rather than the apparatus, so no improvement in instruments is aimed at it.

**[And the Only Way In Is to Be Present](plot_and_the_only_way_in_is_to_be_present.md)**
Cue: a schedule of visits and a machine somebody has to switch on. Closes when the instrument and the interference are shown to be one person, so method selects among ways of being present.

**[Nobody Is Performing](plot_nobody_is_performing.md)**
Cue: an hour given freely, and an instruction to speak naturally. Closes when the shift is found to be automatic and invisible to the speaker, so nothing strategic is happening and cooperation is the interference.

**[The Method That Works Is Barred](plot_the_method_that_works_is_barred.md)**
Cue: the obvious solution, which is to record without telling anybody. Closes when the bar is found correct on grounds that would hold if no study existed, so there is no conflict of values to adjudicate.

**[Becoming Part of It Is the Best Remedy](plot_becoming_part_of_it_is_the_best_remedy.md)**
Cue: two years, and an introduction through somebody's family. Closes when the best available method is seen to work by changing the observed thing, leaving a smaller distortion of a different shape.

**[And the Residue Cannot Be Sized](plot_and_the_residue_cannot_be_sized.md)**
Cue: a reasonable refusal to take the size of an effect on trust. Closes when the comparison is found to require unobserved behaviour, so the number is excluded rather than merely expensive.

## Stakes

Whether a quantity defined by nobody watching can be known by anybody who is.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a target whose specification excludes the only available access, an instrument that is a person, a shift that nobody performs and nobody can suspend, a bar that is right for reasons unconnected to the study, a remedy that works by joining the thing observed, and a magnitude that the same mechanism withholds, and away from any account in which a researcher was clumsy or a participant was putting on a show. The production cannot fault the fieldworker for being in the room, because there is no data without somebody in it, and it cannot fault the ethics board for requiring consent, because what a person is owed does not bend to what a method needs. What is left to fight over is not whether the effect exists but what to call a discipline that knows the direction of its error and is structurally denied its size.
