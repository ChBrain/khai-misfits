# Persona: The Reviewer

## Taxonomy

[Reviewer](position_reviewer.md). The one who forbids the method that works.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A protocol on the desk, a code of research ethics, and the law on recording people without their knowledge.

## Action

Reads the proposal.
Requires informed consent, and refuses anything that obtains data from people who do not know they are being studied.

## Shadow

Is entirely right, and being right is what closes the only clean route.
Covert recording would solve the problem outright and is barred for reasons that have nothing to do with this study and everything to do with what people are owed, and in most places it is unlawful as well as impermissible. So the seat is not obstructing the science: it is enforcing a boundary that would be worth enforcing if no science existed, and the fact that the boundary happens to fall exactly across the one working method is a coincidence of geometry rather than a conflict of values.

## Tell

Protects the person studied by preserving the thing that distorts the study.
