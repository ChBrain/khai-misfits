# Position: Sceptic

## Taxonomy

[Not While You Are Watching](play_not_while_you_are_watching.md). The seat that asks for the magnitude.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A re-analysis, a thin original dataset, and a reasonable demand for a number.

## Orders

## Loses

The possibility of an answer, which the structure removes rather than withholds.
