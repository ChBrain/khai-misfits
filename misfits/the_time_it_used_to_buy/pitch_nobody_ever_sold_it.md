# Pitch: Nobody Ever Sold It

## Taxonomy

[The Time It Used to Buy](play_the_time_it_used_to_buy.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Measured and technical. An orderly transition: correct closures, compliant new plant, a protection scheme maintained to standard, an operator dispatching the cheapest available generation.
Everything documented, everything approved, everything defensible.

## Undertow

Under the order, a service that arrived free for a century and therefore appeared on nothing.
No invoice, no meter, no contract, no screen. It was ample while nobody looked, and the looking only begins after the first time it is missed.

## Nerve

The vertigo of discovering that the thing holding everything together was never on the list of things.

## Echo

Afterward: a rule requiring minimum inertia per sub-network, two large machines burning fuel to keep steel turning for its physics, a hundred years of accounts with no line for any of it, and a protection scheme whose every setting was correct.
