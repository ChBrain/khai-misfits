# Place: The Frequency Desk

## Taxonomy

[The Time It Used to Buy](play_the_time_it_used_to_buy.md). Where the system is watched, and where the missing quantity is not displayed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Screens: demand, dispatch, flows on the interconnectors, frequency to three decimal places.
Operators who know the system intimately and have run it correctly for years.

## Holds

[The Frequency Trace](piece_the_frequency_trace.md), which shows where frequency is and, after the fact, how fast it moved.
[The Trip Setting](piece_the_trip_setting.md) of the load-shedding scheme, staged and correct.

## Offers

Complete visibility of everything the system has ever thought to instrument.
Immediate, expert response to anything those instruments report.

## Withheld

The one number that would have mattered.
Inertia is a property of which machines happen to be running, not a flow anyone meters, so it was on no screen at the desk: the operators could see everything they had ever needed to see, and the quantity thinning underneath them was not among the things anyone had thought to build a display for.
