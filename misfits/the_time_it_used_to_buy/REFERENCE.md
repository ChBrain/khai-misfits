# The Time It Used to Buy: Reference

The research warrant for The Time It Used to Buy.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the loss of power-system inertia as synchronous generation is
displaced, anchored on the South Australian black system of 28 September 2016 and the rule changes that
followed it, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the loss of grid inertia as **a buffer that was never anyone's to give**: the spinning mass of
conventional generators stores kinetic energy and physically resists sudden changes in frequency, which
nobody ever installed it to do and nobody was ever paid for. As those machines retire on their own sound
economics and are replaced by generation with no rotating mass, built correctly to the standards of its
day, the pool shrinks, and what it was providing turns out to have been time. The play models the
documented mechanics, the buffer arriving free as a by-product of a machine bought for something else,
the closures that are each individually right, the replacement that is not declining to contribute but
physically constituted otherwise, the aggregate that no participant chooses or can see, the protection
scheme that stays correct and runs out of milliseconds, and the correction that spends the goal that drove
the change, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of reckless decarbonisation.

The governing law is that inertia does not prevent a disturbance and does not reduce its size. It slows the
rate at which frequency changes, and everything that responds to a disturbance, automatic and human alike,
is calibrated against the time that slowing provides. So the quantity being lost is not headroom against a
larger fault, it is the interval in which an adequate response can arrive, and a scheme that was correctly
designed and correctly maintained can fail without a single one of its settings being wrong.

What makes it structural rather than negligent is that nobody at any point owned the thing that
disappeared. Inertia was never specified, never contracted, never metered and never priced. It came free
with machines installed to make electricity, so its provision was invisible while it was ample, and its
withdrawal is not a decision anyone takes: a plant closes on economics or emissions rules, and the kinetic
energy goes with it as a side effect that appears on no line of the closure case.

South Australia on 28 September 2016 is the anchor because it separates the failure from any fault. Wind
was supplying roughly 40 per cent, synchronous machines were few, and system inertia was low. When the
Heywood interconnector tripped, the resulting rate of change of frequency ran above 6 Hz per second, which
**exceeded the ability of the under-frequency load-shedding scheme to arrest the fall** before frequency
dropped below 47 Hz. The scheme was not defective. It was outrun. Within weeks the operator imposed a
minimum of two large synchronous generators online at all times, and a rule change followed requiring
minimum inertia to be maintained in each sub-network.

It is distinct from the house's **Borrowed Immunity**, where each person skips a shot the herd already
covers until coverage slips below the line: there the protection is a contribution an individual chooses
whether to make, and the free rider benefits from the others' contributions while withholding their own.
Here nobody free-rides and nobody withholds. The retiring plant does not stay to enjoy a pool it stopped
feeding, it leaves the system entirely, and the replacement is not declining to contribute inertia but
physically constituted without it.

It is distinct from the house's **Safety in Numbers**, where a crowd confers real safety and a population
thinned past a threshold loses it: there the same population becomes fewer. Here the population is not
thinning at all, it is being **substituted**, member for member, by units that do the primary job as well or
better and the secondary one not at all.

It is distinct from the house's **The Brittle Optimum**, where slack is recognised as idle waste in calm
and trimmed away deliberately: there someone identifies the margin and removes it. Here nobody ever
identified it, nobody trims it, and it is not removed as a saving but shed as a by-product of a change made
for entirely unrelated reasons.

It is distinct from the house's **Onto the Survivors**, where a failed member's load is redistributed onto
the rest until the sharing ruptures: there something fails and its burden moves. Here nothing fails, no
burden is transferred, and what is lost is a property of the fleet's composition rather than a load.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the closing
  plant as reckless or the new fleet as a free rider, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get
  wrong: an old machine closing on economics or emissions rules is doing the correct and intended thing,
  every new unit was approved under the connection standards that existed when it was built and complies
  with them fully, and the operator dispatching the cheapest available generation is doing its job. Nor is
  the protection scheme at fault: it was correctly designed and correctly maintained, and it was outrun. The
  failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, a service that arrived free as a by-product
  and therefore was never specified, metered or priced, so the misfit is that the one quantity holding the
  system together was the one quantity no participant owned, provided deliberately, or could see.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Requiring synchronous
  machines to stay online works, and means burning fuel and emitting carbon to keep mass turning for its
  physics rather than its electricity, which spends exactly the goal that motivated the transition that
  caused the problem. Synchronous condensers and grid-forming inverters work and cost real capital, paid by
  the system rather than by whoever caused the shortfall, since nobody caused it. And every one of these
  answers requires first naming and measuring a quantity that ran unpriced for a century, which is itself a
  reform nobody is thanked for until after the event that proves it necessary.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the physics, the incident investigation, the operator
  directions and the rule change are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: the South Australian event
  had several contributing causes, among them successive tornado-driven transmission faults and the ride-
  through settings of wind farms at the time, and the operator's own report treats inertia as one factor in
  a sequence rather than the single cause. Inertia requirements, grid-forming inverter standards and
  synthetic-inertia products have all moved substantially since. **The play makes no claim that inverter-
  based generation should not displace synchronous plant**, only that a service which arrives free is a
  service nobody is watching.
