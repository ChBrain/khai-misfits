# Plot: Burning Coal for the Spin

## Taxonomy

[The Time It Used to Buy](play_the_time_it_used_to_buy.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The mechanism finally visible, weeks after the event, and the answers on the table.

## Action

[Running thin](process_running_thin.md) arrested by fiat rather than by price: a minimum of two large synchronous generators required online at all times, and a rule following that sets a floor for the quantity in each sub-network.

## Stage

[The Scheme](persona_the_scheme.md) tables [Keep Two Spinning](plan_keep_two_spinning.md), and the system starts paying for a property it received free for a hundred years.

## Tension

The correction spends the goal that motivated the change that caused it.
Holding synchronous machines online means burning fuel and emitting carbon to keep steel turning for its physics rather than its electricity, which is precisely the outcome the transition existed to avoid. Condensers and grid-forming inverters do it without the emissions and cost capital, paid by the whole system rather than by whoever caused the shortfall, because nobody caused it. And every route begins by naming and measuring something that ran unpriced for a century, which is a reform nobody is thanked for until the event that proved it necessary has already happened.
