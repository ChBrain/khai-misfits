# Persona: The One Who Closed It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Closed It](position_one_who_closed_it.md). Retires an old machine, correctly, for reasons that are entirely sound.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Unsentimental and numerate. Runs the closure case: fuel cost, staff, output, maintenance, carbon, the price of compliance with a new emissions rule.
Reaches the answer the numbers give, which is to shut it.

## Action

Closes the plant, on schedule, as the economics or the rule requires.
Stops the machine, and with it the spinning mass, which is not on the case and is not part of the decision.

## Shadow

Cannot count a quantity that no accounting recognises.
The closure case is complete and correct on every line it has, and the line it lacks was never omitted, it was never invented: nobody anywhere had a field for kinetic energy, so the loss is not overlooked so much as unrepresentable.

## Tell

Says "the plant was uneconomic", which is true, audited, and describes everything about the decision except its largest consequence.
