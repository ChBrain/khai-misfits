# Piece: The Frequency Trace

## Taxonomy

[The Frequency Desk](place_the_frequency_desk.md). The falling line, and the slope that is the whole story.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the screens at [the frequency desk](place_the_frequency_desk.md) in real time, and in the incident report afterward.

## Load Bearing

Without it the loss is abstract, and with it the loss is a gradient anyone can see.
It is the piece that makes the missing quantity finally legible, and only in retrospect: inertia is not on any display, but the steepness of this line is exactly what inertia was flattening, so the one visible record of the absent service is the shape of the failure it could not prevent.

## Apparent

A line falling from 50 Hz. On an ordinary day it dips and recovers.
On 28 September 2016 it falls at over 6 Hz per second and passes 47 Hz before the staged response completes.

## Yearbook

Recorded on every event for decades, and read for where the frequency went.
Read, after this one, for how fast it went, which turns out to have been the question all along.
