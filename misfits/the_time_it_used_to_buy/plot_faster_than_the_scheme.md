# Plot: Faster Than the Scheme

## Taxonomy

[The Time It Used to Buy](play_the_time_it_used_to_buy.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

28 September 2016. Wind at roughly 40 per cent of supply, few synchronous machines online, and the Heywood interconnector trips.

## Action

[Falling fast](process_falling_fast.md): [The Frequency Trace](piece_the_frequency_trace.md) falls at over 6 Hz per second, while [The Scheme](persona_the_scheme.md), holding [Scheme](position_scheme.md), acts at each of [the trip setting](piece_the_trip_setting.md)'s thresholds exactly as specified.

## Stage

The fall passes 47 Hz before the staged response can complete. The scheme does not malfunction at any point.

## Tension

A correct protection is outrun rather than defeated, and that is the whole of the misfit.
Every threshold in it would have worked at the rate of change the system used to have, so nothing is available to be blamed: examined in full afterward, the scheme is found right. What was lost was never adequacy, it was the interval in which adequacy could arrive, and no instrument anywhere was measuring intervals.
