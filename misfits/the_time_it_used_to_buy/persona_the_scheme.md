# Persona: The Scheme

## Taxonomy

[Scheme](position_scheme.md). Correct, maintained, properly set, and outrun.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The under-frequency load-shedding arrangement: staged thresholds that disconnect blocks of demand as frequency falls, to arrest the fall before the system collapses.
Designed carefully, tested, maintained, and never once shown to have a wrong setting.

## Action

Watches the frequency and acts at its thresholds, in order, exactly as specified.
Operates correctly on 28 September 2016, and does not arrest the fall.

## Shadow

Cannot act faster than it was built to, and was built against a rate of change that no longer applies.
Every threshold in it would have worked at the rate the system used to have, so the failure is not of adequacy but of time: above 6 Hz per second the fall reaches 47 Hz before the staged response can complete, and nothing about the scheme is available to be blamed.

## Tell

Is examined afterward in full and found correct, which is the finding that makes the misfit rather than closing it.
