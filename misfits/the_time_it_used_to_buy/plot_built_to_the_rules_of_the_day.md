## Tension

This is the beat where the easy story would find its villain, and there is not one.
Free riding requires the ability to provide and the choice to withhold, and this fleet has neither: there is no synchronised rotating mass here to withhold. Every unit met every requirement that existed, in full, and the requirement did not exist because when the standard was written a generator meant a turbine and spinning mass could not be absent, so nobody specified what nobody could omit.
