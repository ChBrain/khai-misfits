# Plot: It Came With the Machine

## Taxonomy

[The Time It Used to Buy](play_the_time_it_used_to_buy.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Machine Hall](place_the_machine_hall.md), and something very heavy turning inside it in step with the whole system.

## Action

[The Heavy Turning](persona_the_heavy_turning.md), holding [Heavy Turning](position_heavy_turning.md), stores kinetic energy and resists sudden change in frequency, continuously, without being asked.

## Stage

Nobody stages this. The mass is spinning because generating electricity happened, for a century, to require a large object spinning at speed.

## Tension

The service is load-bearing and is on nobody's invoice.
It was never specified, contracted, metered or priced, because it was never a product: it arrived free with the machine the way weight arrives free with a wall. And every response the system has to a sudden loss, automatic and human alike, has been quietly calibrated against the interval it provides, by people who had no reason to know they were relying on anything.
