# Process: Running Thin

## Taxonomy

[The Time It Used to Buy](play_the_time_it_used_to_buy.md). The pool shrinking as an emergent fact nobody holds.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Closures and connections, each correct, accumulating over years.
[The Heavy Turning](persona_the_heavy_turning.md), holding [Heavy Turning](position_heavy_turning.md), across the whole system at once.

## Direction

Downward, as a property of which machines happen to be running at any given moment.
Not chosen by any participant, not priced by any market, and not displayed at [the frequency desk](place_the_frequency_desk.md).

## Lever

Merit order, doing exactly what it is meant to do: dispatching the cheapest available generation.

## Echo

A system operating normally, correctly and economically, on a margin of time that is smaller every year and that nothing reports.
