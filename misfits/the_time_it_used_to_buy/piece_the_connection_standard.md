# Piece: The Connection Standard

## Taxonomy

[The Machine Hall](place_the_machine_hall.md). The rules a generator must meet, which never asked for the thing in question.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Governing every connection to the system, applied at [the machine hall](place_the_machine_hall.md) and to every new unit alike.

## Load Bearing

Without it the new fleet could be called negligent, and it cannot.
It is the piece that clears the replacement completely: every unit met every requirement in force at the time it connected, and inertia was not among them, because when the standard was written every generator had spinning mass by construction and there was no reason to require a property that could not be absent.

## Apparent

A technical document. Fault ride-through, reactive capability, protection settings, power quality.
Thorough, current for its era, and enforced.

## Yearbook

Written when a generator meant a turbine, so it specified what could vary and was silent about what could not.
Revised many times on everything it covered, and never once on this, until an event made the omission visible.
