# Plot: Nobody's Number

## Taxonomy

[The Time It Used to Buy](play_the_time_it_used_to_buy.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Years of closures and connections, each individually correct, accumulating quietly.

## Action

[Running thin](process_running_thin.md): the pool of turning mass shrinks as a property of which machines happen to be dispatched, driven by merit order doing exactly what it is meant to do.

## Stage

[The Frequency Desk](place_the_frequency_desk.md) shows demand, dispatch, flows and frequency to three decimal places, and does not show this, because it is not a flow anyone meters.

## Tension

The quantity that will decide the outcome is nobody's number.
No participant chooses it, no market prices it, no operator can see it, and no threshold anywhere announces its crossing. The operators can see everything they have ever needed to see, run the system correctly and economically, and the margin thinning underneath them is not among the things anyone ever thought to build a display for.
