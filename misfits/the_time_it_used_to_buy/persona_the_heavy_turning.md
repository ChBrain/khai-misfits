# Persona: The Heavy Turning

## Taxonomy

[Heavy Turning](position_heavy_turning.md). Thousands of tonnes of steel spinning in step with the grid, storing time nobody bought.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Incidental. Present because making electricity happened, for a century, to require a very large object spinning at speed.
Not a feature, not a product, and not a decision: a property of mass in motion, like weight coming free with a wall.

## Action

Resists any sudden change in frequency, physically and instantly, by trading kinetic energy against the disturbance.
Buys the interval that every response, automatic and human, is quietly calibrated against.

## Shadow

Cannot appear on anything, because it was never a product.
No invoice lists it, no contract requires it, no meter records it and no market prices it, so its abundance is invisible and its departure is invisible by exactly the same property. A thing that arrives free is a thing nobody is watching.

## Tell

Is noticed for the first time in the report written after it was insufficient.
