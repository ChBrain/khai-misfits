# Process: Closing Down

## Taxonomy

[The Time It Used to Buy](play_the_time_it_used_to_buy.md). Retiring a machine correctly, on a case with no line for what leaves with it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An old plant reaching the end of its economics, or an emissions rule arriving.
[The One Who Closed It](persona_the_one_who_closed_it.md), holding [One Who Closed It](position_one_who_closed_it.md), at [the machine hall](place_the_machine_hall.md).

## Direction

Toward shutdown, which is the correct and intended outcome of both the economics and the rule.
Aimed at cost and carbon, and achieving both.

## Lever

A closure case that is complete and correct on every line it has.

## Echo

A plant closed as intended, and thousands of tonnes of spinning mass stopped, which appears on nothing.
