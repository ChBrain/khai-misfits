# Plot: A Good Reason to Close

## Taxonomy

[The Time It Used to Buy](play_the_time_it_used_to_buy.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An old plant at the end of its economics, or a new emissions rule arriving, or both at once.

## Action

[Closing down](process_closing_down.md): [The One Who Closed It](persona_the_one_who_closed_it.md), holding [One Who Closed It](position_one_who_closed_it.md), runs the case and shuts the machine.

## Stage

[The One Who Closed It](persona_the_one_who_closed_it.md), having tabled [Close It on the Numbers](plan_close_it_on_the_numbers.md), reaches the answer the numbers give, which is the correct and intended outcome of both the economics and the rule.

## Tension

The case is complete and correct on every line it has.
Fuel, staff, output, maintenance, carbon, compliance: all counted, all audited, all right. The line it lacks was not omitted, it was never invented, so there is no version of this decision made by anyone at all that would have counted the quantity leaving with the machine. Closing is what both instruments were designed to cause, and closing is what happens.
