# Plot: Randomisation Repairs the Comparison Between People

## Taxonomy

[What Seven Means Now](play_what_seven_means_now.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two arms, concealed allocation, and hundreds of questionnaires.

## Action

[Randomising the arms](process_randomising_the_arms.md): [the trialist](persona_the_trialist.md), holding [Trialist](position_trialist.md), obtains a valid group difference at [the trial](place_the_trial.md).

## Stage

A sound causal estimate that survives everybody meaning something different by a seven.

## Tension

The obvious objection has a real answer, and the play grants it fully rather than working around it.
If Alice's seven and Bob's four are not on one scale, randomisation makes the distribution of those idiosyncrasies the same in expectation across arms, so the difference in group means is sound. That is why trials of these outcomes work, it is not a patch, and any account that ignores it is arguing with a straw instrument. The misfit has to survive this concession or it is not one.
