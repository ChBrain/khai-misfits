# Persona: The Methodologist

## Taxonomy

[Methodologist](position_methodologist.md). The one who named it and built the instrument for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A model separating a change in the thing from a change in the standard, a change in what is weighed, and a change in what the word means.

## Action

Names recalibration, reprioritisation and reconceptualisation.
Builds the then-test, and measures the shift.

## Shadow

Makes the problem visible and hands back a measurement with the same difficulty inside it.
The work is real and the effect is quantified rather than asserted: recalibration has been measured at around a tenth of an estimated quality-of-life score in one disease, and found significant on half the dimensions of a standard questionnaire at six months in another. The instrument that finds it asks a person, now, to rate how bad it was then, which requires reporting against a standard the seat has established that they no longer hold. It works, it is the best available, and it is the same act one layer down.

## Tell

Measures the moving ruler with a ruler that has moved.
