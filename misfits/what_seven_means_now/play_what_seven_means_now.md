# Play: What Seven Means Now

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

What Seven Means Now.
Pain, nausea, fatigue and mood are private by definition, so the only access is a number the person says, anchored to the worst they can imagine. Randomisation repairs the comparison between people and is a genuinely good answer. It does not reach the comparison inside one, because living with a condition changes what the worst is, so two honest answers a year apart are given against two different rulers.

## Arc

The bend is this: the adaptation that makes a condition bearable is the same movement that makes the before and after incomparable, so the outcome everybody wanted is what removes the evidence of it.
It begins with a quantity that has no reading outside the one who has it.
[The Quantity Is Private](plot_the_quantity_is_private.md). Substituting an observable measures a different thing that correlates, so [the number](piece_the_number.md) is the instrument rather than a stopgap.
And the report carries the whole weight.
[And the Report Is the Whole of the Access](plot_and_the_report_is_the_whole_of_the_access.md). The answer is honest, careful, and anchored to the reporter's own experience because there is nothing else to anchor it to.
And the obvious objection has a real answer.
[Randomisation Repairs the Comparison Between People](plot_randomisation_repairs_the_comparison_between_people.md). The play grants it fully: the difference in group means is sound even though nobody means the same by a seven.
Then [the standard moves inside one](plot_and_the_standard_moves_inside_one.md).
[The Moving Standard](piece_the_moving_standard.md) arrives during the experience rather than being carried into it, so it can arrive unequally and nothing balances it.
So [the change score is two scales subtracted](plot_so_the_change_score_is_two_scales_subtracted.md). [The Change Score](piece_the_change_score.md) is what almost every decision runs on, and a zero can mean no change or an improvement exactly matched by a standard that rose.
And [the then-test asks for a standard nobody holds](plot_and_the_then_test_asks_for_a_standard_nobody_holds.md). [The Methodologist](persona_the_methodologist.md) measures the shift with the same material, carefully, and does not escape it.

## Triggers

**[The Quantity Is Private](plot_the_quantity_is_private.md)**
Cue: a question about how bad the pain is, and no instrument in the room but a person. Closes when the report is established as the instrument rather than a stopgap for a better one.

**[And the Report Is the Whole of the Access](plot_and_the_report_is_the_whole_of_the_access.md)**
Cue: a person considering the question properly and answering it. Closes when the answer is found honest, careful, and anchored to an experience that can change.

**[Randomisation Repairs the Comparison Between People](plot_randomisation_repairs_the_comparison_between_people.md)**
Cue: two arms, concealed allocation, hundreds of questionnaires. Closes when the between-person objection is granted in full and answered soundly.

**[And the Standard Moves Inside One](plot_and_the_standard_moves_inside_one.md)**
Cue: months of living with the condition, and nobody deciding anything. Closes when the shift is shown to arrive during the experience and to be unbalanceable by allocation.

**[So the Change Score Is Two Scales Subtracted](plot_so_the_change_score_is_two_scales_subtracted.md)**
Cue: a note of last year's number, and this year's answer beside it. Closes when a zero is found to have two readings and no arithmetic to separate them.

**[And the Then-Test Asks for a Standard Nobody Holds](plot_and_the_then_test_asks_for_a_standard_nobody_holds.md)**
Cue: a suspicion that the before and after are not on one scale. Closes when the instrument for measuring the shift turns out to be made of the shift.

## Stakes

Whether a person can be shown to be better when the only witness to it is the person, and the witness has changed.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a quantity with no reading outside the one who has it, a report that is honest and load-bearing, a repair that works exactly as far as the arm boundary, a standard that moves because the person is coping, a change score made of two rulers, and an instrument for the shift that is made of the shift, and away from any account in which somebody exaggerates or somebody is careless. The production cannot fault the reporter, who answers truthfully twice; and it cannot fault the trialist, whose design is sound and whose repair is real and bounded. What is left to fight over is not whether the numbers are honest but whether a person who has learned to carry something has improved, stayed the same, or both.
