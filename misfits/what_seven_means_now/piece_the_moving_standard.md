## Load Bearing

It moves because the person is adapting, so the thing that corrupts the measurement is the thing everybody wanted to happen.
A scale is anchored to the worst the reporter can imagine, and living with a condition changes what that is. The literature separates this from two neighbours: what the person weighs most can shift, and what the word means to them can shift. All three are adaptive and none is an error. So the corruption of the instrument is not a lapse in the reporter and not a defect in the questionnaire: it is a person getting better at carrying something, which is the outcome everybody was working toward.
