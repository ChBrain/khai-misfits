# Process: Running the Then-Test

## Taxonomy

[What Seven Means Now](play_what_seven_means_now.md). Measuring the shift, with the material the shift is in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A suspicion that the before and after are not on one scale.
[The Methodologist](persona_the_methodologist.md), holding [Methodologist](position_methodologist.md).

## Direction

Toward an estimate of how far the standard has moved.

## Lever

A retrospective rating: asking the person now to say how bad it was then.

## Echo

A quantified shift, obtained by asking for a report against a standard this seat has shown the person no longer holds.
