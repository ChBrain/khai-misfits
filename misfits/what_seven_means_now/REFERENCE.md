# What Seven Means Now: Reference

The research warrant for What Seven Means Now.
The term is the house's; the play runs on self-reported outcomes and on what adaptation does to a scale
anchored inside the person reporting, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the self-report as **an instrument that adapts with its subject**: pain, nausea, fatigue and mood are
private by definition, so the only access to them is a number the person says, anchored to the worst they can
imagine. The report is honest, is given carefully, and is the best evidence about the quantity that exists
anywhere. Randomisation then repairs the comparison between people completely, which the play grants in full.
What it does not reach is the comparison inside one, because living with a condition changes what the worst is,
so two true answers a year apart are given against two different rulers. The play models the documented
mechanics, the quantity with no reading outside its owner, the report that carries the whole evidential weight,
the repair that works exactly as far as the arm boundary, the standard that moves because the person is coping,
the change score made of two rulers, and the instrument for measuring the shift that is made of the shift, as
the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of anybody exaggerating or anybody being careless.

The governing law is that the adaptation which makes a condition bearable is the same movement that makes the
before and after incomparable, so the outcome everybody wanted is what removes the evidence of it.

**Nobody in the sequence is unreliable, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The
reporter answers truthfully on each occasion. The trialist's design is sound and pre-specified. The
methodologist quantifies the difficulty rather than asserting it. The committee draws a line that has to exist.

What removes any possibility of blame from the reporter is that the scale must be anchored to something and
there is nothing else to anchor it to. A measurement that did not go through the person would be measuring a
different thing that correlates with this one, so substituting a biomarker, a grimace or a step count is not a
more objective reading of the same quantity. The report is the instrument rather than a stopgap for a better
one, and the anchor is a fact about a person at a moment.

What removes it from the trialist is that randomisation is a real answer given in full. If one person's seven
and another's four are not on one scale, concealed allocation makes the distribution of those idiosyncrasies
the same in expectation across arms, so the difference in group means is a sound causal estimate. That is why
trials of these outcomes work at all, it is not a patch, and any account that ignores it is arguing with a straw
instrument.

**And the concession runs out at a definite place, which is what the misfit consists of.** Randomisation
balances what people carry into a trial. It cannot balance an adjustment the experience itself produces, because
that adjustment arrives during the trial and can arrive unequally: the arm in which more happens has more to
adapt to. So the term is not distributed by allocation, and no amount of concealment reaches it.

What removes it from the clinician is that the repair is unavailable rather than neglected. There is one person
in the room and no arms to balance, so the difference between two numbers is the only quantitative object
present, and it subtracts a reading on one ruler from a reading on another. A score of zero can mean no change,
or a real improvement matched exactly by a standard that rose to meet it, and nothing in the arithmetic
separates them.

And the committee closes the case against the last party, because there is no second material. A guideline must
fix a smallest difference worth having or the question is answered by whoever is loudest, and the threshold is
derived from change scores anchored to people's own reports of feeling better, so both sides of the anchor are
the quantity in question.

**The effect is measured rather than argued, which is what makes this stageable.** Recalibration has been
estimated at around a tenth of a quality-of-life score in one disease, and found statistically significant on
about half the dimensions of a standard questionnaire at six months in another. The design that finds it is the
then-test, which asks a person now to rate how bad it was then, and therefore requires a report given against a
standard this same literature has shown the person no longer holds. That is not a flaw somebody introduced: it
is the only material available, used carefully, and it does not escape itself.

It is distinct from the house's **Not While You Are Watching**, which the slate line required it to be tested
against before authoring, and the test was run in the sweep. There the target quantity is specified by the
absence of an observer, so the access **alters** what is being measured and the difficulty is in the definition.
Here the access alters nothing: the question is asked and the state is unchanged by the asking. What moves is
the scale, on its own, in the months between two askings, and it would move whether or not anybody ever asked.

It is distinct from the house's **More to Stand Still**, which is the nearest incumbent and stages hedonic
adaptation, where satisfaction returns toward a neutral point so gains are neutralised and staying above neutral
takes ever more input. That misfit is about the **level**. This one is about the **ruler**. Remove the
incumbent's engine, by supposing a person whose actual state is durably and permanently improved with no
reversion at all, and this misfit stands entire: that person can still have recalibrated what a seven means and
still report the same number, which is the whole difficulty. One stages a gain that fades; this one stages a
gain that holds and cannot be shown.

It is distinct from the house's **In Somebody Else's Words**, where a second speaker re-authors the manner a
room reads credibility from, so the signal is authored twice and the seam is between two people. Here the signal
is authored once, honestly, by the only person who has access to it. What has two versions is not the speaker
but the scale, and the seam is between one person and their own past.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to suggest that anybody
  exaggerates, misremembers or is careless, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the
  quantity is private by definition and no observable substitutes for it without measuring something else; the
  report is given carefully and is the best evidence that exists; the scale must be anchored to the reporter's
  experience because there is nothing else to anchor it to; randomisation is a real repair, given in full, and
  its reach stops at the arm boundary for a reason rather than through an oversight; the recalibration is
  adaptive and is the outcome everybody was working toward; the clinician has no arms to balance and must still
  decide today; the methodologist quantifies the difficulty with the only material available; and the committee
  must draw a line or leave the question to whoever is loudest. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges
  from the structure, so the misfit is that adaptation and measurement failure are one movement.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. The then-test detects the
  shift and is itself a retrospective report. Statistical modelling of measurement invariance finds where the
  scale has moved and depends on assumptions about which items stayed fixed. Anchoring to an external
  observable measures a correlate rather than the quantity. Asking a global transition question, whether the
  person feels better than before, is a single report subject to the same recalibration. Individualised
  measures let people name what matters to them and make comparison across people harder in exchange. And
  knowing the whole structure, which the field does and has documented at length, leaves an appointment on
  Thursday and a question that has to be asked and answered.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the response-shift model, its components, the then-test and the
  measurement literature are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: **whether response shift is a bias to be
  adjusted out or part of the benefit itself is genuinely contested**, and the play carries that argument
  rather than settling it; the magnitudes cited are from particular diseases, instruments and follow-up
  intervals and are offered as evidence that the effect is large enough to matter rather than as a general
  rate; the then-test has its own well-documented limitations including recall, and the account simplifies a
  methodological literature that is far more developed than one row suggests; and the play treats a family of
  instruments and settings as one structure, which is its own simplification. **The play makes no claim about
  any treatment, any measurement decision, any guideline or any person's condition, and gives no advice of any
  kind**, only that the quantity has no reading outside its owner, that adaptation moves the ruler, and that
  the movement everybody hoped for is the movement that cannot be subtracted.
