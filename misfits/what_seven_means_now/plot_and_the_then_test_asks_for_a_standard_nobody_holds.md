# Plot: And the Then-Test Asks for a Standard Nobody Holds

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A suspicion that the before and after are not on one scale, and a design for checking.

## Action

[Running the then-test](process_running_the_then_test.md): [the methodologist](persona_the_methodologist.md), holding [Methodologist](position_methodologist.md), asks the person now to rate how bad it was then.

## Stage

A quantified shift, obtained by the same act one layer down.

## Tension

The instrument that measures the moving ruler is a ruler that has moved, and it is still the best there is.
The work is real and the effect is measured rather than asserted, at around a tenth of an estimated score in one disease and on half the dimensions of a standard questionnaire at six months in another. What the design requires is a retrospective rating: a report given against a standard this very literature has shown the person no longer holds. That is not a flaw somebody introduced. It is the only material available, used carefully, and it does not escape itself.
