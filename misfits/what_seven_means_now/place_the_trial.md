# Place: The Trial

## Taxonomy

[What Seven Means Now](play_what_seven_means_now.md). Where the repair works, and where its reach ends.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two arms, allocation concealed, hundreds of people, and one questionnaire administered to all of them.
Sound design throughout: the randomisation is real, the analysis is pre-specified, and the estimate is valid.

## Holds

[The Trialist](persona_the_trialist.md), obtaining a good answer to the between-person question.
[The Moving Standard](piece_the_moving_standard.md), arriving during the trial rather than being carried into it.
[The Committee](persona_the_committee.md), waiting downstream for a number to apply a threshold to.

## Offers

A causal estimate of the difference between arms that survives every person meaning something different by a seven.

## Withheld

Balance in the thing that changes while the trial runs.
The place succeeds at what it was built for and its reach stops at the arm boundary. Randomisation balances what people bring with them, which is exactly the between-person incomparability, and cannot balance an adjustment produced by what happens to them afterwards, because that adjustment can be larger in the arm where more happened. So the place holds a valid estimate with an unbalanced term inside it, and nothing about the design was done badly.
