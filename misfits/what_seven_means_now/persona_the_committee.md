# Persona: The Committee

## Taxonomy

[Committee](position_committee.md). The one that turns change scores into a threshold.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A guideline in draft, and a number below which a difference is agreed not to matter to a patient.

## Action

Fixes the smallest change worth having.
Applies it to trial results, and to the question of what will be offered.

## Shadow

Needs a threshold and can only build one out of the quantity in question.
Somebody has to say how much improvement counts, because without it every difference is either significant or not and neither answers what a person would notice. The threshold is derived from change scores, usually anchored to a person's own report of whether they feel better, so both sides of the anchor are made of the same material. The seat is not being careless: there is nothing else the threshold could be made of, and refusing to set one does not leave the question unanswered but leaves it answered by whoever is loudest.
