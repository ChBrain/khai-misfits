# Persona: The Reporter

## Taxonomy

[Reporter](position_reporter.md). The only access there is, and an honest one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person asked to put a number on something nobody else can reach: how bad the pain is, how tired, how sick, how low.

## Action

Considers it properly and answers.
Answers again a year later, just as carefully.

## Shadow

Is telling the truth twice, on a ruler that moved between the two.
Nothing here is exaggeration or forgetfulness and no version of the seat is unreliable: the answer is given with care and is the best evidence about this quantity that exists anywhere. What has happened in the interval is that the seat has learned what the condition is like, and the worst day it can now imagine is not the worst day it could imagine before. So the scale is anchored to experience, the experience has changed, and the number is honest against a standard the seat no longer shares with its own past self.

## Tell

Gives two true answers that cannot be subtracted.
