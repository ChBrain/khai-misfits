## Load Bearing

It is what almost every decision actually runs on, and it is the one quantity randomisation does not repair.
A clinician deciding about one person has no arms to balance. A guideline fixing the smallest difference worth having derives it from change scores anchored to people's own reports of feeling better, so both sides of the anchor are the quantity in question. And a person who has adapted may report the same number while carrying the condition far better, so a score of zero can mean no change, or a real improvement matched exactly by a standard that rose to meet it, with nothing in the arithmetic to tell them apart.
