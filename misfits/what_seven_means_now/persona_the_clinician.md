# Persona: The Clinician

## Taxonomy

[Clinician](position_clinician.md). The one deciding from one person's two numbers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A consulting room, a person in front of them, and a note recording what the last number was.

## Action

Asks the question.
Compares the answer with the one from before, and decides what to do next.

## Shadow

Has no group to average and cannot use the repair that works.
Randomisation is a real answer and it is not available here: there is one person, no arms, and nothing to balance idiosyncrasy across. What the seat has is a difference between two numbers, which is the only quantitative thing in the room, and which subtracts one scale from another. The seat knows this, asks around it, listens for what the person actually says, and still has to write something in the record and act.

## Tell

Uses the one comparison available, knowing what it is made of.
