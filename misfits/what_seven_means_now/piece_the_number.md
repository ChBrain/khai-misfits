## Load Bearing

It is the only access to the quantity, so nothing about it can be checked against anything outside the person.
Pain, nausea, fatigue and mood are private by definition: there is no thermometer, and a measurement that did not go through the person would be measuring something else. That is not a gap waiting for technology. It is what the quantity is. So the number carries the whole evidential weight, is given carefully, and has no external referent by which it could be shown to be too high, too low, or the same as somebody else's.
