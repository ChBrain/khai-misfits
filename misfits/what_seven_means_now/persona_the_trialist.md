# Persona: The Trialist

## Taxonomy

[Trialist](position_trialist.md). The one whose repair works, exactly as far as it goes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Two arms, allocation concealed, hundreds of people, and the same questionnaire administered to all of them.

## Action

Randomises.
Compares the group means, and obtains a valid estimate of the difference.

## Shadow

Solves the between-person problem completely and does not touch the other one.
This is not a seat with a weakness. Randomisation makes the distribution of private scales the same in expectation across arms, so the difference in group means is a sound causal estimate even though no two people mean the same thing by a seven. What it cannot balance is a change in the ruler that the experience itself produces, because that is not an idiosyncrasy carried into the trial: it arrives during it, and it can arrive differently in the arm where more has happened.

## Tell

Fixes the comparison between people and inherits the one inside them.
