# Plot: The Quantity Is Private

## Taxonomy

[What Seven Means Now](play_what_seven_means_now.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A question about how bad the pain is, and no instrument in the room but a person.

## Action

[The Number](piece_the_number.md) is established at [the consulting room](place_the_consulting_room.md) as the whole of the access: there is no thermometer, and a measurement that did not go through the person would be measuring something else.

## Stage

A quantity with no reading outside the one who has it.

## Tension

This is what the quantity is rather than a gap waiting for technology, which is what makes the rest structural.
Substituting an observable, whether a biomarker, a grimace or a step count, does not measure the thing more objectively: it measures a different thing that correlates with it. So the report is not a stopgap standing in for a better instrument. It is the instrument, and it is the only one there can be.
