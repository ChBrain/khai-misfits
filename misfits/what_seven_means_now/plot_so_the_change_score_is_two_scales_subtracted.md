# Plot: So the Change Score Is Two Scales Subtracted

## Taxonomy

[What Seven Means Now](play_what_seven_means_now.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A note recording last year's number, and this year's answer beside it.

## Action

[The Change Score](piece_the_change_score.md) is formed at [the consulting room](place_the_consulting_room.md) and carried to [the committee](persona_the_committee.md), which builds a threshold from it.

## Stage

The quantity almost every decision runs on, made of two readings from two rulers.

## Tension

A score of zero can mean no change, or a real improvement matched exactly by a standard that rose to meet it.
Nothing in the arithmetic separates them. The clinician has one person and no arms, so the repair that works is unavailable rather than neglected. The committee must fix a smallest difference worth having, and derives it from change scores anchored to people's reports of feeling better, so both sides of the anchor are the quantity in question. Neither seat is being careless: there is no second material to build any of it from.
