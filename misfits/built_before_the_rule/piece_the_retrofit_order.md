## Place

## Load Bearing

It is the only instrument in the play that actually reaches an exempt building, and it charges the people it protects.
Mandatory ordinances apply to structures built under codes enacted before a stated date and set compliance clocks by vulnerability class, commonly seven years for the most critical, ten and thirteen for the rest: and the work routinely costs more than the building is worth, so it is recovered from the rent or the building is sold, and the tenants move.

## Apparent

A public safety measure, correctly aimed, adopted after argument, with a reasonable timetable and clear technical standards.
The right answer, and known to be.

## Yearbook

Adopted in some cities decades after the hazard was catalogued, and never adopted in many others, since the cost falls locally and the earthquake is a probability.
Carries no funding in most versions, so the mandate and the means to comply with it are written by different bodies, or by only one.
