# Plot: A Percentage of What It's Worth

## Taxonomy

[Built Before the Rule](play_built_before_the_rule.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The same roof, the same rewiring, the same quote, presented on two buildings with different valuations.

## Action

[Costing the work](process_costing_the_work.md), run twice: on the valuable property [the contractor's number](piece_the_contractors_number.md) sits comfortably under [the fifty per cent line](piece_the_fifty_per_cent_line.md), and on the cheap one it trips it.

## Stage

The threshold is a percentage of market value, so the poorer the building, the less work it takes to trigger full compliance.

## Tension

The owner least able to fund compliance is the one most often required to fund it, and therefore most often deterred from repairing at all.
Nothing in the rule intends this and nothing in it could avoid it: a threshold has to be proportionate to something, and an absolute figure would fall even harder on the cheap building. So the denominator that makes the rule fair between properties is the same denominator that concentrates the deterrent effect on the oldest, poorest and most dangerous end of the stock.
