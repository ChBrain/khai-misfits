# Place: The Old Frontage

## Taxonomy

[Built Before the Rule](play_built_before_the_rule.md). The street the hazard stands over, in plain view and unreadable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A row of brick facades, parapets, shopfronts, flats above, and a pavement with people on it.
The most valued street in the district, and the most affordable.

## Holds

Unreinforced walls, put up before anybody required reinforcement, carrying floors that are not tied to them.
[The Retrofit Order](piece_the_retrofit_order.md) in the jurisdictions that have adopted one, and nothing at all in those that have not.

## Offers

Housing and shops at rents nothing newer can match, in buildings people are fond of and campaign to keep.
Full legal compliance, since everything here met the standard that existed when it went up.

## Withheld

Any signal of what it is.
Nothing on the frontage distinguishes a reinforced building from one that is not, no notice is served on the people living above, and the fondness the street attracts is a further reason not to touch it: so the hazard is public, catalogued in the city's own records, and invisible to every person standing under it.
