# Piece: The Contractor's Number

## Taxonomy

[The Permit Counter](place_the_permit_counter.md). The estimate, which is where the decision actually happens.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Prepared before anybody reaches [the permit counter](place_the_permit_counter.md), and specified against the threshold rather than against the building.

## Load Bearing

It is the instrument by which the scope is reduced, and it is honest.
A builder who knows the rule prices the work that can be done without crossing the line, because that is the work the client can pay for: nothing here is falsified, no valuation is fiddled and no inspector is deceived, and the reduction happens in the specification rather than in the paperwork, where no enforcement can reach it.

## Apparent

An ordinary estimate for an ordinary job, itemised, defensible, and slightly smaller than the one first discussed.
Competent professional advice, and correct advice.

## Yearbook

Written against a threshold rather than against a need, since a quote that triggers full compliance is a quote nobody accepts.
Repeated every few years on the same building, each round patching what has failed and leaving what has not.
