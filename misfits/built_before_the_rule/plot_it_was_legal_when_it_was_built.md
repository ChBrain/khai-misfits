# Plot: It Was Legal When It Was Built

## Taxonomy

[Built Before the Rule](play_built_before_the_rule.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The new requirement taking effect, and a district full of buildings that were already standing.

## Action

[Standing exempt](process_standing_exempt.md): [The Building That Predates It](persona_the_building_that_predates_it.md), holding [Building That Predates It](position_building_that_predates_it.md), continues lawfully on [the old frontage](place_the_old_frontage.md), brick, unreinforced, occupied.

## Stage

Applying the requirement backward would condemn the entire existing stock on the day of enactment, which no city can afford and no legislature will do.

## Tension

The exemption is granted by date of construction, and date of construction is what predicts the absence of reinforcement.
These are not two facts that happen to correlate: they are one fact read twice, so the relief lands exactly on the buildings the clause was written about. And the overlap cannot be narrowed from either end, because a rule that exempted fewer buildings would be a rule that condemned more occupied housing, and the people in it would have nowhere to go.
