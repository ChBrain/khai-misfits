# Pitch: Nobody Has to Fix It

## Taxonomy

[Built Before the Rule](play_built_before_the_rule.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Municipal and reasonable. A code improved after every disaster, an exemption for what was lawful when it went up, a threshold applied evenly to everybody, an official with no discretion to bend it.
All of it defensible at a council meeting, and most of it genuinely good.

## Undertow

Under the reasonableness, an exemption handed out by age, and age is the thing that predicts the hazard.
And a trigger that makes leaving the building alone the cheapest thing an owner can do with it.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a safety rule whose practical effect is that nobody goes near the unsafe thing.

## Echo

Afterward: brick walls from before 1945 with no steel in them, standing lawfully over a pavement; work specified at forty-nine per cent on advice; buildings declining or empty rather than repaired; and a retrofit ordinance the next city over has still not adopted.
