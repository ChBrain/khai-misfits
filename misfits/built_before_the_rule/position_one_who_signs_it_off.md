# Position: One Who Signs It Off

## Taxonomy

[Built Before the Rule](play_built_before_the_rule.md). The seat whose fairness is what makes it powerless.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A code, a valuation, an estimate, and a line that falls in the same place for everybody.
Full knowledge of what is wrong with the building in front of it.

## Orders

Compare the number against the threshold.
Apply the same line to every applicant.

## Loses

Any route to the outcome it can see coming.
A threshold that bent for a sympathetic owner would bend for a well-connected one, so the evenness that makes the rule fair is exactly the evenness that makes it unreachable: the seat explains the consequence honestly, watches the applicant reduce the scope in response, and would be acting improperly if it did anything else.

## Drives

Toward telling the truth at the counter, which is the thing that stops the work.
