# Plan: Apply the Rule

## Taxonomy

A **public duty**: the intent of [The One Who Signs It Off](persona_the_one_who_signs_it_off.md), inside [Built Before the Rule](play_built_before_the_rule.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Signs It Off](persona_the_one_who_signs_it_off.md)

## Direction

Apply the same threshold to everybody, explain its consequences honestly, and issue or refuse on the numbers. Even treatment is the whole of what makes the rule fair and reviewable.

## Orders

(Take the valuation and [the contractor's number](piece_the_contractors_number.md); measure against [the fifty per cent line](piece_the_fifty_per_cent_line.md); explain what crossing it requires; issue or refuse at [the permit counter](place_the_permit_counter.md).)

## Implementation

([The Permit Counter](place_the_permit_counter.md); the [standing exempt](process_standing_exempt.md) status the building already holds; a code that arrived after it and cannot be applied backwards.)

## Targets

- [ ] the line falls in the same place for every applicant, however sympathetic or well connected
- [ ] the consequence of crossing it is explained honestly and in advance
- [ ] the decision is reviewable, even and correct
- [ ] no partial improvement can be traded for a partial exemption, because no such power exists and granting it would end the evenness
