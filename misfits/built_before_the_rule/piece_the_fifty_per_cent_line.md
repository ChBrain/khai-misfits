# Piece: The Fifty Per Cent Line

## Taxonomy

[The Permit Counter](place_the_permit_counter.md). The threshold that converts improvement into liability.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the permit counter](place_the_permit_counter.md), applied to every application: work costing half or more of the building's market value before the damage triggers full compliance.

## Load Bearing

It makes the cheapest lawful action leaving the building alone.
Under the national flood programme the line sits at fifty per cent and communities may set it lower, at thirty or forty, and crossing it requires the entire structure to meet the standard of a new building: so improvement past a point stops being an improvement and becomes a condemnation notice with a price on it.

## Apparent

A clear, even, published threshold that treats every applicant alike and can be checked by anybody.
Exactly the kind of rule good regulation is supposed to consist of.
