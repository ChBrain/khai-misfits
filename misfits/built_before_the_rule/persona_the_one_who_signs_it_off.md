# Persona: The One Who Signs It Off

## Taxonomy

[One Who Signs It Off](position_one_who_signs_it_off.md). The building official, applying a threshold with no give in it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A plans examiner at a permit counter with a code, a valuation and an estimate, and a duty to apply the same line to everybody.
Knows the building, knows what is wrong with it, and knows exactly what the paperwork will say.

## Action

Compares the estimate against the threshold and issues or refuses accordingly.
Explains the consequence of crossing the line, honestly, to owners who then do not cross it.

## Shadow

Has no discretion, and discretion is the last thing anybody should want here.
A threshold that bent for sympathetic owners would be a threshold that bent for well-connected ones, so the evenness that makes the rule fair is the same evenness that makes it unreachable: the official can see the outcome coming, is required to produce it, and would be acting improperly to do anything else.

## Tell

Tells the truth at the counter, and the truth is what stops the work.
