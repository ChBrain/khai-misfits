# Process: Costing the Work

## Taxonomy

[Built Before the Rule](play_built_before_the_rule.md). An estimate measured against a threshold rather than against a building.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A maintenance list, and [the fifty per cent line](piece_the_fifty_per_cent_line.md) sitting at half the building's market value.
[The One Who Owns It](persona_the_one_who_owns_it.md), holding [One Who Owns It](position_one_who_owns_it.md).

## Direction

Toward [the contractor's number](piece_the_contractors_number.md), specified to land underneath the line.
Toward a scope reduced on professional advice, lawfully, before anyone reaches [the permit counter](place_the_permit_counter.md).

## Lever

Market value as the denominator, so the cheapest buildings reach the threshold on the least work.

## Echo

The same repair passing without comment on a valuable property and triggering full compliance on a poor one.
