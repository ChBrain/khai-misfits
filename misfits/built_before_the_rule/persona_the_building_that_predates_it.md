# Persona: The Building That Predates It

## Taxonomy

[Building That Predates It](position_building_that_predates_it.md). The exempt structure, and the dangerous one, being the same object.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Brick, three storeys, typically put up before 1945: unreinforced masonry, with no steel tying the walls to the floors.
Known since the 1930s to be among the most lethal things to stand next to in an earthquake.

## Action

Stands, lawfully, complying with everything that existed when it was built.
Holds shops on the ground floor and flats above, at rents nothing newer can match.

## Shadow

Is exempt for exactly the reason it is dangerous.
The relief is granted by the date of construction, and the date of construction is what predicts the absence of reinforcement, so the exemption and the hazard are not merely correlated: they are two readings of one fact, and any rule that exempted less would be a rule that condemned more of what people live in.

## Tell

Is legal, occupied, affordable and a known killer, all at once and with no contradiction anywhere in the paperwork.
