# Persona: The One Who Owns It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Owns It](position_one_who_owns_it.md). The owner, doing the arithmetic that the rule hands them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A landlord or small proprietor with an old building, a maintenance list, and a valuation that is mostly the land.
Not wealthy, and not a speculator: often somebody whose whole capital is this one structure.

## Action

Gets an estimate, and finds that the work as specified would cross the threshold.
Reduces the scope until it lands underneath, on professional advice, entirely lawfully.

## Shadow

Is behaving correctly and producing the worst available outcome.
Crossing the line means bringing the entire structure to the standard of a new building, which routinely costs more than the building is worth: so the rational, legal and frequently the only affordable course is to do less work than was planned, and the rule that exists to improve the stock is the reason the stock is not improved.

## Tell

Is deterred from repair by the regulation written to compel it, and cannot be faulted at any step.
