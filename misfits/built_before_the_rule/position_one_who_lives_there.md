# Position: One Who Lives There

## Taxonomy

[Built Before the Rule](play_built_before_the_rule.md). The seat the rule is for, appearing in it only as a cost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A flat above a shop at a rent nothing newer in the district can match.
A wall with no steel in it, undisclosed and undiscoverable.

## Orders

Live there.
Move out when the work is finally ordered.

## Loses

Whichever way it goes.
Leaving the building alone leaves this seat under the hazard the code was written about, and ordering the retrofit removes its home for the duration and usually permanently, since the work costs more than the building is worth and is recovered from the rent: so every option on the table trades a danger it cannot see against a displacement it cannot afford, and none of them ends with it safe and still housed.

## Drives

Toward being the reason for the rule and the party charged for every version of it.
