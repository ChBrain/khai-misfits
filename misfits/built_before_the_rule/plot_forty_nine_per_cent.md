# Plot: Forty-Nine Per Cent

## Taxonomy

[Built Before the Rule](play_built_before_the_rule.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A maintenance list, a builder, and a threshold at half the building's market value before the damage.

## Action

[Costing the work](process_costing_the_work.md): [The One Who Owns It](persona_the_one_who_owns_it.md), holding [One Who Owns It](position_one_who_owns_it.md), receives [the contractor's number](piece_the_contractors_number.md) and finds the job as specified crosses [the fifty per cent line](piece_the_fifty_per_cent_line.md).

## Stage

The scope is reduced until it lands underneath. Nothing is falsified, no valuation is fiddled, no inspector is deceived.

## Tension

The reduction happens in the specification, where no enforcement can reach it.
This is not evasion: crossing the line requires bringing the entire structure to the standard of a new building, which routinely costs more than the building is worth, so a builder who prices the work that can actually be paid for is giving correct advice and an owner who accepts it is behaving rationally and lawfully. The threshold was introduced to stop the stock being repeatedly patched and never brought up to standard, and the answer to it is to repeatedly patch the stock and never bring it up to standard.
