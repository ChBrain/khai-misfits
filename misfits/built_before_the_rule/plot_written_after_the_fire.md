## Tension

The requirement could not have existed one day earlier, and the lateness is not anybody's failing.
Nobody could have demanded reinforcement before the earthquake that demonstrated why it was needed, so hindsight is not a defect in the process but the only source the process has. The code is not slow, captured or complacent: it is doing the one thing available to it, learning from the disaster, and every clause it holds was bought at that price.
