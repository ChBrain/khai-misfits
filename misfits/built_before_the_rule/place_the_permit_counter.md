# Place: The Permit Counter

## Taxonomy

[Built Before the Rule](play_built_before_the_rule.md). Where the arithmetic is done, and the repair is talked out of existence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A counter, a queue, a plans examiner, a valuation and a contractor's estimate.
A code applied identically to everyone who walks in, which is the whole virtue of the place.

## Holds

[The Fifty Per Cent Line](piece_the_fifty_per_cent_line.md), against which every application is measured.
[The Contractor's Number](piece_the_contractors_number.md), which decides whether the building is touched at all.

## Offers

Even treatment, a clear rule, a reviewable decision, and an honest explanation of what crossing the line will require.
Everything one could reasonably ask of a regulatory counter.

## Withheld

Any outcome other than the one it can see coming.
The official knows the building, knows what is wrong with it and knows the applicant will reduce the scope rather than trigger full compliance, and has no power to trade a partial improvement for a partial exemption: so the place produces an even, correct, unimpeachable decision whose predictable effect is that the wall stays as it is.
