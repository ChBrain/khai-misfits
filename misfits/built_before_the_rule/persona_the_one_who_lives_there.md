# Persona: The One Who Lives There

## Taxonomy

[One Who Lives There](position_one_who_lives_there.md). The tenant, protected by the rule and displaced by its enforcement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A household in a flat above a shop, in a building put up before the requirement existed, paying a rent nothing newer in the district can match.
The person the code is for.

## Action

Lives there, under a wall with no steel in it, without being told and without any way of finding out.
Is moved out when the retrofit finally happens, and does not come back at the old rent.

## Shadow

Is the beneficiary of every correction and the one who pays for each of them.
Ordering the retrofit protects this persona by removing their home for the duration and usually for good, since the work costs more than the building is worth and is recovered from the rent: so the choice presented is a hazard they cannot see or a displacement they cannot afford, and there is no version on the table in which they simply end up safe and housed.

## Tell

Is the reason the rule exists, and appears in the decision only as a cost.
