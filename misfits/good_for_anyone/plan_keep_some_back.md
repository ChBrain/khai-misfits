# Plan: Keep Some Back

## Taxonomy

A **stewardship intent**: the intent of [The One Who Can Take Only This](persona_the_one_who_can_take_only_this.md), inside [Good for Anyone](play_good_for_anyone.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Can Take Only This](persona_the_one_who_can_take_only_this.md)

## Direction

Hold enough for the people who have no alternative. Reserve a floor of stock against the booked operations and the transfusion programmes, so that having no substitute is not the same as going last.

## Orders

(Set a reserve level in [the fridge](place_the_fridge.md); flag the booked cases in advance; share stock between hospitals; escalate when the shelf drops below the floor.)

## Implementation

([The Fridge](place_the_fridge.md), which has perfect information and no supply-side control; the [running the stock down](process_running_the_stock_down.md) process, drawing from every hospital in the region at once.)

## Targets

- [ ] the booked cases with no alternative are identified and provided for
- [ ] the shelf is watched accurately, rotated, and shared where sharing helps
- [ ] the price is accepted: a reserve held back is a reserve not available to somebody unidentified who arrives tonight
- [ ] the price is accepted: every unit protected here is protected from a patient whose reach for it would have been correct
