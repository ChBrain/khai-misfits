# Piece: The Emergency Unit

## Taxonomy

[The Resuscitation Bay](place_the_resuscitation_bay.md). The bag issued before anybody knows anything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held ready in [the resuscitation bay](place_the_resuscitation_bay.md) or a satellite fridge, issued uncrossmatched on the word of the team leader.

## Load Bearing

It converts a universal property into a universal draw.
Because it can be given without knowing anything, it is given whenever nothing is known, which is exactly the situation the stock exists for and also the situation in which most of it is spent on people who will turn out not to have needed it: the bag's virtue and its consumption are the same fact.

## Apparent

Two units in a cool box with a tag, ready to hang in under a minute, checked and rechecked.
The single most valuable object in the department during a major haemorrhage.

## Yearbook

Introduced and refined precisely so that nobody dies waiting for compatibility to be established, which it achieves.
Drawn against a stock that cannot be enlarged, by a decision rule that cannot ask who is on the other end of it.
