# Persona: The One Who Can Take Anything Else

## Taxonomy

[One Who Can Take Anything Else](position_one_who_can_take_anything_else.md). The patient who turned out to have alternatives.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The same person as the one who arrived unidentified, an hour later, once the laboratory has returned a type.
Compatible with several groups, of which the universal one is merely the first that arrived.

## Action

Received the universal bag while nobody knew, correctly.
Is changed over to their own group as soon as the type is known, which recovers nothing already given.

## Shadow

Is not a free rider and consumes most of the supply.
Every unit went in for the right reason and could not have gone in otherwise, so there is nobody here to charge with taking more than their share: the bulk of a stock reserved in principle for those with no alternative is used, unavoidably and properly, by people who had one and did not yet know it.

## Tell

Takes the thing they did not need, at the only moment when nobody could tell they did not need it.
