# Piece: The Change Over

## Taxonomy

[The Fridge](place_the_fridge.md). The switch to the patient's own group, which saves the next units and not the last.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Ordered from [the fridge](place_the_fridge.md) the moment the laboratory returns a type, and carried out as soon as the next unit is needed.

## Load Bearing

It is the standard conservation measure, it works, and it is entirely retrospective.
Moving a patient onto their own group the instant it is known stops further draw on the universal shelf, which recovers real units across a year: and it cannot recover a single unit already transfused, so the saving is always on the units after the answer and never on the ones that were given while waiting for it.

## Apparent

Routine good practice, taught everywhere, done without fuss and recorded in the notes.
The cheapest and least controversial thing in the play.

## Yearbook

Emphasised harder as shortages have deepened, together with advice to type patients as quickly as possible so the switch can happen sooner.
Bounded by the same minutes as the test, since the switch cannot precede the answer.
