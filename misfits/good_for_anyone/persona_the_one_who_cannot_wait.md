# Persona: The One Who Cannot Wait

## Taxonomy

[One Who Cannot Wait](position_one_who_cannot_wait.md). The unidentified patient, haemorrhaging now.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Somebody brought in bleeding, unconscious or unable to say anything useful, with no record and no wristband.
Losing volume faster than any test can be completed.

## Action

Is given the universal bag, immediately, before anybody knows what they are.
Is typed as soon as the laboratory can manage it.

## Shadow

Makes the reach correct, and the correctness is the whole engine.
Typing takes minutes this persona does not have, so reaching for blood that suits anybody is the right decision every single time it is made, taken under pressure by somebody who would be culpable for hesitating: there is no version of this play in which that reach is a mistake, which is why the play cannot be closed by improving anybody's judgement.

## Tell

Consumes the scarce thing for exactly the reason the scarce thing exists.
