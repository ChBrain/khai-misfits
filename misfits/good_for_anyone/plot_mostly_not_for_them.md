# Plot: Mostly Not for Them

## Taxonomy

[Good for Anyone](play_good_for_anyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An hour later, the laboratory returns a group.

## Action

[Typing them](process_typing_them.md): [The One Who Can Take Anything Else](persona_the_one_who_can_take_anything_else.md), holding [One Who Can Take Anything Else](position_one_who_can_take_anything_else.md), turns out to be compatible with several groups, and is moved onto their own through [the change over](piece_the_change_over.md).

## Stage

Every unit already transfused stays transfused. The switch prevents the next ones and recovers none of the last.

## Tension

Most of a stock reserved in principle for those with no alternative is consumed, unavoidably and properly, by people who had one.
There is nobody here to accuse of taking more than a share: the units went in for the right reason, could not have gone in otherwise, and were given at the only moment when the distinction was unavailable to anybody. The draw is not a failure of stewardship, it is what stewardship looks like when the information arrives after the decision.
