# Plot: Somebody Waits Either Way

## Taxonomy

[Good for Anyone](play_good_for_anyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The shelf below its floor, a ceiling on donors that no appeal can lift, and the corrections on the table.

## Action

[Keep some back](plan_keep_some_back.md): a reserve is set in [the fridge](place_the_fridge.md) against the booked cases, and every unit protected is a unit unavailable to whoever arrives tonight without a name.

## Stage

Supplies have fallen to critical levels every year since 2019, produced without a single incorrect decision anywhere in the chain.

## Tension

There is no arrangement that leaves nobody waiting, because the two claims are made of the same property.
Holding stock back leaves somebody unidentified waiting on a crossmatch they may not survive. Releasing it leaves the people with no substitute short and their operations postponed. Recruiting harder raises attendance and cannot raise the share of the population that is eligible. So the shelf can be watched perfectly, rotated perfectly and shared perfectly between hospitals, and every remaining decision available to anybody is a decision about who goes short.
