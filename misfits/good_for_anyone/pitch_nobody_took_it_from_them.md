# Pitch: Nobody Took It From Them

## Taxonomy

[Good for Anyone](play_good_for_anyone.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Clinical and unhurried. A shelf stocked to protocol, a doctor reaching for the right bag in the right seconds, a laboratory returning a type as fast as it can be returned, a service asking politely for donors.
Every action correct, and most of them admirable.

## Undertow

Under the protocol, a property that makes something useful to everybody and therefore claimable by everybody, against a supply capped by how many people are born with it.
And a few who can take nothing else, drawing on the same shelf as the entire population.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a shortage produced entirely by correct decisions, none of which took anything from anybody.

## Echo

Afterward: around seven people in a hundred who can receive only this, a donor ceiling set by genetics rather than by persuasion, and supplies falling to critical levels every year since 2019.
