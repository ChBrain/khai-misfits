# Persona: The Blood That Suits Everybody

## Taxonomy

[Blood That Suits Everybody](position_blood_that_suits_everybody.md). Red cells with none of the common markers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary red cells that happen to carry no A, no B and no RhD, so nothing in any recipient's body objects to them.
Not a refined or luxury product: the same thing as everybody else's blood, minus the markers.

## Action

Goes into anybody, safely, without knowing who they are.
Sits on one shelf, in one fridge, for all of those purposes at once.

## Shadow

Is claimable by everybody precisely because it harms nobody.
Compatibility is not a surplus but an invitation, so every patient in the building is a potential recipient of this and only a few are restricted to it: the property that makes it a gift to the whole population is the same property that makes the whole population a claimant on it.

## Tell

Is the most useful blood there is, and therefore the least likely to be there.
