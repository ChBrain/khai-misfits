# Persona: The One Who Gives It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Gives It](position_one_who_gives_it.md). The donor, capped by genetics rather than by willingness.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Somebody who carries none of the markers and is asked, repeatedly and by name, to come in more often than other donors.
Roughly seven in a hundred of the population, and rarer in several groups within it.

## Action

Donates as often as the interval allows, and is called again.
Cannot become more numerous.

## Shadow

Is the one input in the play that cannot be increased by doing anything better.
An appeal can raise the share of eligible donors who attend, which is real and worth doing, and it cannot raise the share of the population that is eligible, because that is fixed by what people are born with: so the supply has a ceiling that no campaign, budget or logistics improvement can lift, and demand on it comes from everybody.

## Tell

Is asked to give more than anybody, and the total is set before anybody asks.
