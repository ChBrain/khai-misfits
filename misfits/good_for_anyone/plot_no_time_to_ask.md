## Tension

The reach is right every single time, which is why nothing in the play can be fixed by improving judgement.
A team that hesitated to check compatibility while somebody emptied would be culpable, and the protocol exists precisely to remove hesitation from a moment where hesitation kills. So the consumption of the scarce thing is not an error being repeated: it is the correct decision being taken correctly, under the one condition that guarantees nobody can yet tell whether this patient needed it.
