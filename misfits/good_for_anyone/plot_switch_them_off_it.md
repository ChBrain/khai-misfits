# Plot: Switch Them Off It

## Taxonomy

[Good for Anyone](play_good_for_anyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Shortages deepening, and the conservation advice: type as fast as possible and change over, and in major trauma give ordinary O positive even when the group is unknown.

## Action

[Type them faster](plan_type_them_faster.md): the laboratory runs urgently, [the change over](piece_the_change_over.md) is ordered the moment a group returns, and many hospitals now hang O positive in major bleeding because the reaction risk is lower during ongoing blood loss.

## Stage

Real units are bought back across a year. This is the most effective thing anybody does.

## Tension

The saving lands only after the answer, and the substitution moves the question rather than settling it.
The change over cannot recover a unit already transfused, so it protects the next patient and never the one being treated. And giving O positive when the group is unknown quietly converts the decision from who is bleeding worse now into who the marker could harm later, which is a defensible clinical judgement and is also an allocation rule about the future rather than the emergency in front of anybody.
