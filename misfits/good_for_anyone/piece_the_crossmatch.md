# Piece: The Crossmatch

## Taxonomy

[The Fridge](place_the_fridge.md). The test that would answer everything, and takes time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the laboratory beside [the fridge](place_the_fridge.md), begun the moment a sample arrives and finished as fast as the chemistry allows.

## Load Bearing

It is the only thing that could prevent the misfit, and it is always slightly too slow.
Knowing the patient's group would let the shelf be spent only on those who need it, and the test cannot be completed in the seconds a haemorrhage allows: so the gap between the arrival of the patient and the arrival of the answer is the entire mechanism, and shortening it, which laboratories work hard at, narrows the gap without closing it.

## Apparent

A standard, reliable, well-validated procedure, run urgently and reported as soon as it is done.
Faster every decade, and never instantaneous.

## Yearbook

Improved steadily, with rapid typing methods that cut the wait substantially.
Still measured in minutes, against a bleed measured in seconds, so the reach happens first and the knowledge arrives afterwards.
