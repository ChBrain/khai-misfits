# Persona: The One Who Can Take Only This

## Taxonomy

[One Who Can Take Only This](position_one_who_can_take_only_this.md). The patient with no substitute, on a scheduled list.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Around seven people in a hundred, whose bodies recognise none of the markers and will attack any cells that carry them.
Often on a planned operation, a transfusion programme, or an obstetric pathway, with a date in a diary.

## Action

Waits, while the shelf is drawn on by everybody in the region who arrives without a name.
Has an operation postponed when the stock does not hold.

## Shadow

Competes with the entire population rather than with a rival group.
There is no other claimant to negotiate with and no committee to appeal to, because everybody alive is compatible with what this persona alone requires: the demand pressing on their supply is not a faction, it is the property of the substance itself, so there is nothing here that could be shared out differently by agreement.

## Tell

Is short of the one thing that suits everybody, for exactly the reason it suits everybody.
