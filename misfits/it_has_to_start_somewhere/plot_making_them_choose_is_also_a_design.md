## Tension

The one position that looks like standing outside the architecture is a position inside it.
Requiring an answer is a decision about how the question is put, it carries its own wording and its own order, and it produces its own distribution which is different again from both presumptions. It also imposes a decision on people who have no view and would rather not give one, which is a real cost borne by real people, and it raises the rate at which the form is abandoned unfinished. So the escape is a further piece of the thing being escaped, and this is the plot that closes the last exit rather than the first.
