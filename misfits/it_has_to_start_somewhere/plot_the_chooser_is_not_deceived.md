# Plot: The Chooser Is Not Deceived

## Taxonomy

[It Has to Start Somewhere](play_it_has_to_start_somewhere.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A person at the counter with ten minutes and a queue behind them.

## Action

[Filling it in](process_filling_it_in.md): [the chooser](persona_the_chooser.md), holding [Chooser](position_chooser.md), answers what they have a view about at [the counter](place_the_counter.md) and leaves the rest.

## Stage

An honest return, freely made, with nothing hidden from anybody.

## Tension

Nothing here is a failure of attention, and treating it as one is the misreading the play refuses.
The box is visible, the alternative is printed, and changing it costs a moment; what is missing is a preference to assert. A setting an official body chose reads as a recommendation and frequently is one, the effort of forming a view exceeds the stake on most of the questions a form asks, and on a question with no view to give, leaving the answer where it is reports that accurately. So the person who ends up somewhere they did not choose has chosen nothing wrongly, and no amount of care, literacy or attention would have produced a different return.
