# Persona: The Registrar

## Taxonomy

[Registrar](position_registrar.md). The one who must say what the form says before it is printed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A form going to press on Friday, a legal duty to issue it, and a page that has to be finished.

## Action

Sets the boxes, the wording and the order.
Sends it to print.

## Shadow

Cannot decline the act, and has nowhere neutral to stand while performing it.
Every arrangement of the page produces a different distribution of answers, including the arrangement that looks like no arrangement at all, so there is no version of the form that leaves the question to the public untouched. The seat is not choosing whether to influence the outcome: it is choosing which influence, from a list with no zero on it.

## Tell

Is asked for a neutral form, and no such object exists to hand over.
