# Persona: The Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Reformer](position_reformer.md). The one who says the answer is to make people choose.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A proposal to remove the default entirely and require an answer before the form can be filed.

## Action

Strikes the preselected box.
Requires an active answer from everybody.

## Shadow

Has found the only position that looks neutral, and it is a design like the others.
Forcing an answer is itself a choice about how the question is put, it still has an order and a wording, and it imposes a decision on people who would rather not make one and have no view to give. It raises the abandonment rate, it cannot be applied to every question a form asks, and where it is applied it produces its own distribution, which is different again. So the escape from the architecture is a further piece of it.

## Tell

Removes the default and installs one.
