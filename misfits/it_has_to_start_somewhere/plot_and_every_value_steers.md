# Plot: And Every Value Steers

## Taxonomy

[It Has to Start Somewhere](play_it_has_to_start_somewhere.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two countries, comparable populations, comparable stated attitudes, and one difference on the page.

## Action

[The Take-Up Rate](piece_the_take_up_rate.md) is compared across arrangements, and the gap is found at [the counter](place_the_counter.md) to be large rather than marginal.

## Stage

The same question, answered very differently, by people who differ in nothing else that was measured.

## Tension

The effect is not a rounding error at the edge of a policy: it is larger than the policy.
Where the setting has been varied and the populations are otherwise alike, the difference in outcome runs to multiples rather than percentage points, and it holds where the stated attitudes of the two populations are indistinguishable. So the arrangement of the page is not a detail beneath the decision, it is frequently the largest single determinant of it, and a body that spent a year on the substance of a policy settled more of the outcome in the afternoon it agreed the form.
