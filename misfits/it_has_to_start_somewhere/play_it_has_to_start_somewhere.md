# Play: It Has to Start Somewhere

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

It Has to Start Somewhere.
A form goes to press on Friday and a box on it is either ticked or it is not. Whichever way it goes to print, the answers come back different, from the same people, with the same views, freely given. There is no third way to print it, and the arrangement that looks like no arrangement is one of the arrangements.

## Arc

The bend is this: a question has to arrive in some condition, every condition moves the answer, and there is no null condition to fall back on.
It runs on an act that cannot be declined.
[The State Has to Have a Value](plot_the_state_has_to_have_a_value.md). [The Registrar](persona_the_registrar.md) must finish [the blank form](piece_the_blank_form.md) by the print date, choosing from presumed in, presumed out and made to answer, a list with no zero on it.
And the choice is not a detail.
[And Every Value Steers](plot_and_every_value_steers.md). Where the arrangement has been varied across comparable populations with indistinguishable stated attitudes, the outcome moves by multiples, so [the take-up rate](piece_the_take_up_rate.md) is settled more by the afternoon the form was agreed than by the year spent on the policy.
And the obvious repair is inside the problem.
[Making Them Choose Is Also a Design](plot_making_them_choose_is_also_a_design.md). [The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) strikes the preselection and installs a requirement, which has its own wording, its own distribution, and a real cost paid by people who have no view and would rather not give one.
Then [The Order Is a Setting Too](plot_the_order_is_a_setting_too.md).
[The Order of the Options](piece_the_order_of_the_options.md) survives every remedy aimed at preselection, because a list has to be in some order and position moves the answer, and randomising it spreads the effect rather than removing it.
And [the chooser is not deceived](plot_the_chooser_is_not_deceived.md). Nothing is hidden and the change costs a moment: what is absent is a preference to assert, so the honest return reports the setting on every question the person had no view about.
And [the evidence came from the last setting](plot_and_the_evidence_came_from_the_last_setting.md). [The Analyst](persona_the_analyst.md) proposes to follow revealed preference, and every observation available was produced on a form that already carried an arrangement.

## Stakes

Whether a question can be put to anybody without the putting of it deciding part of the answer.
Every plot moves the same way: toward an act that cannot be declined, an effect larger than the policy it sits under, a repair that is a further piece of the thing repaired, a setting that survives the removal of settings, a person who has chosen nothing wrongly, and an appeal to evidence that runs through the arrangement it would judge, and away from any account in which a body set out to steer anybody. The production cannot fault the registrar for finishing the page, because the page has to be finished and the list has no zero on it, and it cannot fault the person who left a box as it came, because on a question they had no view about that is the honest report. What is left to fight over is not whether to steer but what to call an outcome that nobody chose, arrived at by people all of whom chose correctly.
