## Load Bearing

It is a real measurement of what people did and is not a measurement of what they wanted.
Between comparable populations with comparable stated attitudes, the same question under a different arrangement returns a different rate, and the gap is not small. Every honest use of the number, to set policy, to follow revealed preference, to check whether an arrangement is serving anybody, runs through a quantity the arrangement produced. And asking people directly instead does not escape it, because the survey has an order and a wording of its own.
