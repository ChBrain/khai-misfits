# Persona: The Chooser

## Taxonomy

[Chooser](position_chooser.md). The one who fills it in and mostly leaves it as it came.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ten minutes, a queue behind them, and a question they have no settled view about.

## Action

Reads the page. Changes what they care about. Leaves the rest.

## Shadow

Is not deceived, is not lazy, and is not doing anything a careful person would do differently.
Nothing is hidden: the box is visible, the alternative is on the page, and changing it costs a moment. But a setting somebody official chose reads as a recommendation and often is one; the effort of forming a view exceeds the stake on most questions; and on a question they have no view about there is nothing to express, so accepting what is there is not a failure to decide but the honest report of having no preference.

## Tell

Ends up somewhere they did not choose, having chosen nothing wrongly.
