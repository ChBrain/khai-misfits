# Place: The Counter

## Taxonomy

[It Has to Start Somewhere](play_it_has_to_start_somewhere.md). Where the form is met, answered and filed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A queue, a few minutes each, a page of questions, and a pen.
No pressure and no concealment: the boxes are visible, the alternatives are printed, and anybody may change anything.

## Holds

[The Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md), answering what they have a view about.
[The Blank Form](piece_the_blank_form.md), already carrying its state.
[The Take-Up Rate](piece_the_take_up_rate.md), accumulating one return at a time.

## Offers

A full and free opportunity to answer differently, at the cost of a moment.

## Withheld

A reason to spend the moment, on the questions where there is no view to express.
The place cannot supply a preference that does not exist, so on those questions what is filed is the setting rather than an answer, and the return is indistinguishable afterwards from one somebody meant.
