# Persona: The Legislator

## Taxonomy

[Legislator](position_legislator.md). The one who must write which of the three it will be.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A bill, a committee, and three options: presumed in, presumed out, or made to answer.

## Action

Hears the arguments. Finds each of them sound. Writes one into the statute.

## Shadow

Is legislating a quantity nobody wanted to legislate, and cannot leave it out of the bill.
Silence is not available: a statute that says nothing about the setting leaves whoever prints the form to decide it, which is a delegation and not an abstention. So the seat is obliged to fix by law a thing that ought to be nobody's business, and every available wording moves the outcome by more than most of the policy the bill is actually about.

## Tell

Must legislate the shape of the question in order to legislate anything else.
