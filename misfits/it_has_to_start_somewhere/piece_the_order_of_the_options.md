## Load Bearing

It survives every remedy aimed at the first setting, because a list has to be in some order.
Strike the preselected box and the options are still printed one above another, and position moves the answer: measured on ballots, on menus and on ranked results, first place is worth something to whoever holds it. Randomising the order does not remove the effect, it distributes it, which is a different policy and not an absence of one. So the piece is what makes the difficulty structural rather than a feature of preselection.
