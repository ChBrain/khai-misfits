# Persona: The Analyst

## Taxonomy

[Analyst](position_analyst.md). The one who works out what people actually want, from what they actually did.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Years of returns, a clean dataset, and a brief to set the default to whatever most people choose.

## Action

Measures the distribution of answers.
Recommends the setting that matches it.

## Shadow

Reads a preference off a population the previous setting produced.
The proposal is exactly right in form: let the default follow what people want rather than what anybody thinks they should want. The data available to establish what people want is the record of answers given on forms that already had a setting on them, and that setting moved the answers, so the measurement recommends the arrangement that generated it. Asking people directly does not escape it either, since the question has an order and a wording of its own.

## Tell

Proposes to let the evidence decide, and the evidence is downstream of the decision.
