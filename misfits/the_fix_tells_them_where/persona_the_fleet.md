# Persona: The Fleet

## Taxonomy

[Fleet](position_fleet.md). The estate, updating on its own schedule and not on this one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Maintenance windows, change freezes, embedded devices, appliances, and things nobody knows they run.
Operators doing their jobs properly.

## Action

Updates. Slowly.
Is mostly unpatched on the day.

## Shadow

Is not slow through negligence and cannot be made fast.
An estate is a negotiation between availability and risk, and the parties running it have obligations the maintainer does not see, so the curve is long and shallow for reasons that are sound at every point on it and the publication lands at the start of it.

## Tell

Is the party the fix protects and the party the fix exposes, in that order.
