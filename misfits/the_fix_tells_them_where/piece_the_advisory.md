# Piece: The Advisory

## Taxonomy

[The Release](place_the_release.md). The part that can be made vaguer, and the part vagueness costs.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Written at [the release](place_the_release.md) and read by whoever must decide whether to open an emergency window.

## Load Bearing

Every degree of vagueness is paid for by the defender and refunded to nobody.
An operator uses it to decide whether this one justifies an unscheduled outage, which is the decision the whole system depends on, and somebody comparing builds does not need it at all: so withholding detail removes the input from the only party whose behaviour it changes, and leaves the specification exactly as available as it was.

## Apparent

A severity, an affected range, and a sentence about impact.
The one part of the release anybody argues about.

## Yearbook

Is the only lever available, and it points the wrong way.
Has no setting at which the diff becomes harder to read.
