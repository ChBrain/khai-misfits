# Persona: The Window

## Taxonomy

[Window](position_window.md). The exposure, owned by nobody who can shorten it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The interval between publication and the last machine applying it.
Measured in weeks at best and in years at worst.

## Action

Opens on release.
Closes on somebody else's schedule.

## Shadow

Cannot be closed by the party that opened it.
The maintainer ends it only for whoever chooses to update, which is a fact about other people's estates, so the one actor who could have chosen the timing has no purchase at all on the duration: what they control is when the exposure starts and nothing about when it stops.

## Tell

Is created by an act of repair and ended by strangers.
