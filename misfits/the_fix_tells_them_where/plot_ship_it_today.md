# Plot: Ship It Today

## Taxonomy

[The Fix Tells Them Where](play_the_fix_tells_them_where.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A fix ready for a flaw nobody outside has reported, and a maintainer deciding when to release.

## Action

[Shipping it](process_shipping_it.md): [the maintainer](persona_the_maintainer.md), holding [Maintainer](position_maintainer.md), publishes the build and the advisory.

## Stage

The estate can now close the hole. Waiting would have been worse on its own terms.

## Tension

There is no safer version of this decision.
[The Flaw](persona_the_flaw.md) is present in every deployed copy whether or not anybody has written a fix, somebody else may already hold it quietly, and every unshipped day is a day the estate is open with nobody able to close it. The maintainer was under no obligation to look and looked. So the most responsible available act is taken promptly and correctly, and the play stops there for a moment because everything that follows must not be read as an argument against taking it.
