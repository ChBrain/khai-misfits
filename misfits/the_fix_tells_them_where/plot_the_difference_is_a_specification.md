# Plot: The Difference Is a Specification

## Taxonomy

[The Fix Tells Them Where](play_the_fix_tells_them_where.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The old build and the new one, both public because one is what everybody runs and the other is what they must run.

## Action

[Reading the difference](process_reading_the_difference.md): [the diff](persona_the_diff.md) is assembled from [the two builds](piece_the_two_builds.md) by anybody who wants it.

## Stage

It locates the missing check, its path, its version and its condition. What is left is engineering rather than discovery.

## Tension

The pair is constituted by the release rather than leaked from it.
A patched build has to be published for the estate to apply it, and an unpatched one is already everywhere, so the comparison exists the moment the repair is available and no policy about wording reaches it. This is why the play declines to treat the reader as a party with a decision: the trap is complete against an entirely automated scanner, and nothing about it depends on anybody's character. The difficulty of using the flaw fell by exactly the amount the maintainer just supplied.
