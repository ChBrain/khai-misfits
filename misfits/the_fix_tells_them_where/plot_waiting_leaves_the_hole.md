# Plot: Waiting Leaves the Hole

## Taxonomy

[The Fix Tells Them Where](play_the_fix_tells_them_where.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The other obvious remedy, which is to hold the fix until the estate is ready.

## Action

Release is deferred, and [the flaw](persona_the_flaw.md) stays in [Flaw](position_flaw.md), where it already was.

## Stage

Nothing is protected. The flaw remains open for everybody, including whoever found it independently and said nothing.

## Tension

There is no date at which the estate is ready.
The curve has a tail measured in years and is made partly of machines whose operators do not know they exist, so waiting for readiness is waiting for an event that does not occur. And the delay is not neutral: it keeps a flaw closable-in-principle uncloseable-in-fact, which converts a known problem into an unknown one for everybody except whoever already has it. The real choice was never between an exposed estate and a safe one.
