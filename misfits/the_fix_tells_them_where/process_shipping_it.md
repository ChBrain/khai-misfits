# Process: Shipping It

## Taxonomy

[The Fix Tells Them Where](play_the_fix_tells_them_where.md). The repair, released promptly and correctly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A fix that works, an estate that cannot patch itself, and no reason to think nobody else has this.
[The Maintainer](persona_the_maintainer.md), holding [Maintainer](position_maintainer.md), at [the release](place_the_release.md).

## Direction

Toward a build the estate can apply, published where the estate looks.
Toward [the advisory](piece_the_advisory.md), saying enough for an operator to prioritise.

## Lever

The only mechanism there is for closing a hole on machines the maintainer does not own.

## Echo

[The Two Builds](piece_the_two_builds.md) in public, and a window opening that the maintainer cannot close.
