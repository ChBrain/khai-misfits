# Persona: The Flaw

## Taxonomy

[Flaw](position_flaw.md). The thing that was already there.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A missing check, present in every deployed copy, for as long as the version has shipped.
Unannounced and fully exploitable the whole time.

## Action

Sits.
Waits to be found, by anybody.

## Shadow

Is not created by the disclosure and is not ended by silence.
Holding the fix leaves it open for everybody including whoever already has it quietly, so the choice was never between an exposed estate and a safe one: it was between a flaw known to one party and a flaw known to all, with the second at least being closable by the people it belongs to.

## Tell

Makes both options worse and neither of them wrong.
