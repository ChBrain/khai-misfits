# Persona: The Maintainer

## Taxonomy

[Maintainer](position_maintainer.md). The party who must ship and has no safer option.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A fix ready, a flaw nobody outside has reported, and a release process that works.
No obligation to have looked, and they looked.

## Action

Ships it.
Writes an advisory saying enough to act on.

## Shadow

Creates the exposure by ending it.
There is no arrangement in which the repair travels and its description does not, because the description is what the repair is: so the most responsible available act is also the publication of the flaw, and the maintainer is the only party who could have done both and cannot do one.

## Tell

Does the right thing promptly and is the reason the day is dangerous.
