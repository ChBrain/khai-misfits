# Plot: And the Window Is Not Yours to Close

## Taxonomy

[The Fix Tells Them Where](play_the_fix_tells_them_where.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Everything understood, the fix out, and the exposure running.

## Action

[The Window](persona_the_window.md) holds [Window](position_window.md): opened by one decision, closing on thousands of others.

## Stage

The party who created the exposure by repairing the flaw has no purchase at all on how long it lasts.

## Tension

No seat anywhere holds both ends of it.
The maintainer chose the date and controls nothing about the duration; the operators control the duration and did not choose the date, and each of them closes it only for themselves. So the exposure is authored once and ended piecemeal, by parties who cannot coordinate and are not wrong. Understanding the whole mechanism, which everybody in the play does, changes none of the inputs: the next flaw will be found, the fix will be right, and shipping it will be the responsible thing to do.
