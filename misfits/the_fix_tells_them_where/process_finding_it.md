# Process: Finding It

## Taxonomy

[The Fix Tells Them Where](play_the_fix_tells_them_where.md). The discovery, done by somebody under no obligation to look.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A codebase, an audit, a fuzzer, or an afternoon of curiosity.
[The Flaw](persona_the_flaw.md), holding [Flaw](position_flaw.md), present in every deployed copy already.

## Direction

Toward knowing that a check is missing, and where.
Toward a fix, which is the only thing knowing is good for.

## Lever

Attention, spent by somebody who did not have to spend it and whose finding changes nothing about whether the flaw is there.

## Echo

One party holding a flaw that everybody has been running, and a decision about what to do with it.
