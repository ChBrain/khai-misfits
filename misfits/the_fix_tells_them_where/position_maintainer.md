# Position: Maintainer

## Taxonomy

[The Fix Tells Them Where](play_the_fix_tells_them_where.md). The seat that must act and cannot act quietly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A fix, a release process, and a flaw nobody outside has reported.

## Orders

Ship it. Say enough to act on.

## Loses

The option of a silent repair.
The seat can choose the date and cannot choose whether the repair describes itself, because the description is what the repair is: so every path out of the seat publishes the flaw, and the only variable left is when.

## Drives

Toward doing the responsible thing and opening the exposure by doing it.
