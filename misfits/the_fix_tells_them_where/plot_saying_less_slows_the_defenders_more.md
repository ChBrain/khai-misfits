# Plot: Saying Less Slows the Defenders More

## Taxonomy

[The Fix Tells Them Where](play_the_fix_tells_them_where.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The obvious remedy, which is to publish a vaguer advisory.

## Action

[The Advisory](piece_the_advisory.md) is softened: the severity stays, the impact goes, the affected paths are described in general terms.

## Stage

The operator can no longer tell whether this one justifies an unscheduled outage. The difference between the builds is exactly as readable.

## Tension

Every degree of vagueness is paid for by the defender and refunded to nobody.
The advisory is the input to the one decision the whole system depends on, whether to open an emergency window, and somebody comparing builds does not need it at all. So withholding detail removes the information from the only party whose behaviour it changes and leaves the specification untouched: it is the sole lever anybody can adjust and it has no setting at which the diff becomes harder to read.
