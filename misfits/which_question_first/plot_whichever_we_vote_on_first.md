# Plot: Whichever We Vote On First

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The panel settling how the conference will be run.
[The Route](piece_the_route.md), about to be chosen without being argued.

## Action

[Choosing the route](process_choosing_the_route.md): [The Chair](persona_the_chair.md), in [the procedure seat](position_procedure.md), puts the questions in an order.

## Stage

[The Chair](persona_the_chair.md), on [Decide What We Vote On](plan_decide_what_we_vote_on.md), orders the questions, takes the count that order implies, and records the outcome.
[The Losing Party](persona_the_losing_party.md), in [the bound seat](position_bound.md), is bound by the result.

## Tension

The case is decided here, and it does not look like a decision.
It looks like scheduling. It is discharged properly by somebody doing an ordinary administrative job, and in almost every case it changes nothing, because the two counts almost always agree. That is exactly why it is never examined: **the choice is invisible in the cases where it is decisive**, being indistinguishable from the many occasions on which it decided nothing. And nobody votes on it. The panel votes on the contract, on the breach and on the verdict, and never on which of those votes governs.
