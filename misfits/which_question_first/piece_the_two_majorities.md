# Piece: The Two Majorities

## Load Bearing

Without it one of the counts is wrong and somebody has made a mistake.
It is load bearing because **both are genuine**: the arithmetic can be checked rather than believed, every count is correct, and the contradiction therefore sits in the aggregation rather than in any participant, which is what makes this an impossibility and not a failure.

## Apparent

A close case, decided narrowly.
It reads as an ordinary two-to-one result, and the fact that there are two different two-to-one results pointing opposite ways is only visible if somebody sets them side by side.
