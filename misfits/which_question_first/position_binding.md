# Position: Binding

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). The seat of the agreed connective joining the questions to the verdict.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Universal acceptance: no party disputes it and no judge doubts it.
A logical form rather than a judgement.
Application to any set of answers, including the majority's.

## Orders

Join the two questions to the outcome.
Apply to each judge's own answers.
Apply to the majority's answers, if anybody takes them.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat is the load-bearing one nobody suspects: the agreement on the rule is what makes the counts diverge, since without a logical link between the questions there is nothing for a majority to be inconsistent about.
Because it is uncontested it is never examined, so the trouble it enables stays invisible to anybody looking for a disagreement about law.
It ends correctly applied twice, to two sets of answers, returning two verdicts.

## Drives

Toward a determinate outcome from settled law,
never once in dispute,
so the one thing everybody agrees on is the thing that makes agreement impossible.
