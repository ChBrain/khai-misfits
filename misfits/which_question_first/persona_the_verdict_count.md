# Persona: The Verdict Count

## Taxonomy

[Outcome](position_outcome.md). The tally taken on results: not liable, not liable, liable, so the defendant wins two to one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A genuine majority, arrived at by correct counting.
Faithful to what each judge would say if asked only how the case comes out.
The intuitive count, and the one most people assume is the only one.

## Action

Asks each judge for a verdict.
Counts them.
Returns not liable, by two to one.

## Shadow

Nothing of its own, and its result contradicts the reasons a majority of the panel holds: the court announces that the defendant wins while a majority of it believes there was a contract and a majority believes it was breached.
Because it records an outcome without a coherent set of reasons behind it, it leaves the next case with a result and no usable rule.
It ends with a verdict the panel voted for and reasoning it does not collectively hold.

## Tell

Says: the defendant succeeds.
And cannot say why, in any way a majority of the court would sign.
