# Position: Outcome

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). The seat of the verdict majority, faithful to the panel's results and not to its reasons.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A genuine majority on the outcome.
Correct counting.
The intuitive standing that makes it feel like the only count there is.

## Orders

Ask each judge how the case comes out.
Count the answers.
Return the majority verdict.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and its result contradicts the reasons a majority holds: the court announces the defendant wins while a majority of it finds a contract and a majority finds a breach.
Because it records an outcome with no coherent set of reasons behind it, the next case inherits a result and no usable rule.
It ends with a verdict the panel voted for and reasoning it does not collectively hold.

## Drives

Toward the answer the judges actually gave,
never once miscounting either,
so the route that honours the votes leaves the law with nothing to stand on.
