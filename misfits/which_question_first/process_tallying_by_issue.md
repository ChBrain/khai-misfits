# Process: Tallying by Issue

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). Taking a majority on each question separately and applying the rule to the answers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Three coherent views on two questions.
At [the tally](place_the_tally.md), column by column.

## Direction

Toward the answer the panel collectively holds about each element.
Two of the three found a contract, so the court finds a contract. Two of the three found a breach, so the court finds a breach. Apply [the connective](piece_the_connective.md) and the defendant is liable.
And this is not a manoeuvre: deciding an issue by majority is what a court does, and the result is faithful to what the panel actually believes about the law's elements.

## Lever

The connective, which is uncontested and is what makes the two findings compose into a verdict.
Because the rule is agreed, applying it to the majority's answers is as legitimate as applying it to any judge's.

## Echo

A verdict of liable, reasoned and defensible, which **two of the three judges would each have voted against**.
The route that produces usable reasons produces an outcome most of the court opposes.
