# Piece: The Route

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). Whether the panel counts issues or verdicts: the choice that decides the case and that nobody votes on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [the conference room](place_the_conference_room.md) by [choosing the route](process_choosing_the_route.md).
Answered nowhere: it is not argued before the decision and not reviewable after it.

## Load Bearing

Without it the two majorities stand unresolved and no verdict issues.
It is load bearing because it is **decisive and invisible at once**: the choice looks like scheduling, is made by habit, is discharged properly by somebody not aware of deciding anything substantive, and settles the outcome in exactly the cases where nobody notices it was made.

## Apparent

An administrative detail about the order of business.
It reads as procedure of the least consequential kind, and its being the ground of decision is precisely what a question of order does not look like.

## Yearbook

Chosen by [the chair](persona_the_chair.md), omitted from [the judgment](place_the_judgment.md).
Fixing it in advance decides every future case of the kind before any is heard.
