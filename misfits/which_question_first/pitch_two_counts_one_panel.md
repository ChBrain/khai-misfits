# Pitch: Two Counts, One Panel

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry, procedural, unhurried.
The register of a conference room and a vote table: who held what, on which question, and in what order it was put.
Nothing indignant anywhere in it, because every vote in the play is coherent and every count is correct.

## Undertow

Under the verdict, two facts that do not negotiate.
People hold consistent views. Majorities are taken one question at a time.
Between those two, a set of answers each carried by a majority need not hang together, and nothing in the counting will tell you that it does not.

## Nerve

The unease of two opposite judgments,
from the same three coherent people, the same agreed law and the same evidence,
separated by nothing except which question the panel happened to put first.

## Echo

After the run: a judgment on the record with an outcome and reasons,
a losing party bound by a route that was never argued and usually not minuted,
and a vote table that would have gone the other way if the conference had been run differently.
