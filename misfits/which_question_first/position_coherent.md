# Position: Coherent

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). The seat of an individual whose answers and verdict hang together.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An answer to each question, formed on the evidence.
The agreed rule, applied correctly to those answers.
Complete internal consistency, checkable and checked.

## Orders

Answer each question.
Derive the verdict.
Vote the same way if asked again.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat is why this is an impossibility rather than a failure: there is no error to find, no inconsistency to correct and no reasoning to improve at any of the three.
Because each individual is coherent, whatever goes wrong goes wrong in the joining and not in the parts.
It ends having voted correctly and having contributed to a result that contradicts either its answers or its verdict.

## Drives

Toward a correct decision on the case,
right in every single instance,
so the seat with nothing wrong with it is one of three that together produce a contradiction.
