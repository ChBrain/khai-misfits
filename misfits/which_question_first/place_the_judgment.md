# Place: The Judgment

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). Where the outcome is published, with reasons, and without the route that produced it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An order, and reasoning in support of it.
Nothing about whether the panel tallied issues or verdicts.

## Holds

The reason the mechanism is unappealable.
It holds the fact that the ground of decision is not in the document: appellate review addresses reasoning and outcome, and the aggregation rule a panel used to combine itself is neither, so the cause of the loss is not a reviewable thing.

## Offers

A public, reasoned, checkable account of why the case came out as it did.

## Withheld

The route.
Nothing in the judgment distinguishes a court that counted issues from one that counted verdicts, so the losing party reads an unimpeachable document and cannot name what beat it.
