# Process: Publishing

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). Recording the outcome and the reasons, and not the route.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A decided case.
At [the judgment](place_the_judgment.md), for the parties and for later courts.

## Direction

Toward a public, reasoned account of the decision, which is what a judgment is for and what it delivers.
Nothing is concealed on purpose: the aggregation rule a panel used to combine itself is not conventionally part of a judgment, and no drafter omits it in order to hide anything.
So the document is complete by its own standards and silent on the thing that decided the case.

## Lever

Convention about what a judgment contains, which nobody set with this in mind.
Because review addresses reasoning and outcome rather than the panel's internal arithmetic, the omission is not merely unrecorded but unreviewable.

## Echo

An unimpeachable judgment, and a losing party with nothing to cite.
[The silent record](piece_the_silent_record.md) is what makes the mechanism permanent rather than merely occasional.
