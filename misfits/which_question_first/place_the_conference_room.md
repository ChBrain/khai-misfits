# Place: The Conference Room

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). Where three coherent views are stated and where the order of the questions is settled without being argued.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Three judges, an agreed rule, and a case.
A chair deciding what is put and in what sequence.

## Holds

The decision, in the one form nobody recognises as one.
It holds the play's quietest fact: the substantive outcome is settled here, by an administrative choice about sequence, in a conversation that is not part of the hearing and is not usually minuted.

## Offers

An orderly deliberation among people who have all read the papers and hold considered views.

## Withheld

Any sense that a choice was made.
The two counts agree in nearly every case, so the sequence is set by habit, and the one occasion it decides the case is indistinguishable from the many where it decided nothing.
