## Load Bearing

Without it the losing party can point at the route and argue about it.
It is load bearing because it makes the mechanism **permanent rather than occasional**: nothing is concealed on purpose, the document is complete by its own standards, and review addresses reasoning and outcome rather than a panel's internal arithmetic, so the cause of the loss is not merely unrecorded but unreviewable.

## Apparent

A properly reasoned judgment.
It reads as complete, which by every existing convention it is, and the omission is invisible because nobody expects the item to be there.
