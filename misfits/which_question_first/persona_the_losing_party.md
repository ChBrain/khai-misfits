# Persona: The Losing Party

## Taxonomy

[Bound](position_bound.md). Whoever the route went against, bound by a procedure that was never argued and cannot be appealed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Represented properly, heard fully, and treated correctly on every point that was argued.
Would have won on the other count, and has no way to say so as a ground.
Not the victim of any error: there is no error.

## Action

Argues the case, on the law and the facts.
Loses, or wins, according to a count nobody put to them.
Reads a judgment that records the outcome and not the route.

## Shadow

The case, for a reason that appears nowhere in it: the ground of decision is a matter of internal practice rather than of law or fact, so there is nothing in the judgment to challenge and nothing in the record to point at.
Because appellate review addresses reasoning and outcome rather than the aggregation rule a panel used to combine itself, the actual cause of the loss is not a reviewable thing.
It ends bound, correctly, by something it never had the chance to address.

## Tell

Says nothing about it, having nothing to cite.
The judgment is unimpeachable on its face and would have gone the other way in the next room.
