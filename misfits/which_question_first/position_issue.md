# Position: Issue

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). The seat of the question-by-question majority, faithful to the panel's law and not to its verdicts.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A genuine majority on each question.
Correct counting, with nothing concealed.
The rule, available to apply to the result.

## Orders

Count each question separately.
Take the majority answer to each.
Apply the rule to those answers.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and its result is a verdict a majority of the panel would each reject: two of three would say not liable if asked directly.
Because it is faithful to what the panel holds about the elements, it is unfaithful to what the panel holds about the case, and the two cannot both be honoured.
It ends with a reasoned judgment the court's own majority disagrees with.

## Drives

Toward a coherent statement of the law,
never once miscounting,
so the route that produces usable reasons produces an outcome most of the court opposes.
