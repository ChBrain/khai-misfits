# Process: Choosing the Route

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). Settling which of the two counts governs, by practice rather than by argument.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two genuine majorities pointing opposite ways.
At [the conference room](place_the_conference_room.md), as a question of sequence.

## Direction

Toward whichever count the order of business implies.
This is the step where the case is actually decided, and it does not look like a decision: it looks like scheduling, it is discharged properly, and the person doing it is usually not aware of choosing anything substantive.
Because the two counts agree in almost every case, the choice is unexamined until it matters, and when it matters nothing marks it as having been made.

## Lever

[The route](piece_the_route.md), which nobody votes on and which no party is heard on.
Because it is internal practice rather than law, it is neither argued before the decision nor reviewable after it.

## Echo

A verdict, determined by the sequence of questions.
Fixing one route in advance does not dissolve this: it decides every future case of the kind before any of them is heard.
