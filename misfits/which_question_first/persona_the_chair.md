# Persona: The Chair

## Taxonomy

[Procedure](position_procedure.md). Whoever settles how the conference is run, and thereby settles the case without voting on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Doing an ordinary administrative job and doing it properly.
Deciding what is put to the panel and in what order, which is what presiding consists of.
Usually not aware of choosing anything substantive, because in most cases the two counts agree.

## Action

Puts the questions in an order.
Takes the count that order implies.
Records the outcome.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is where the decision actually happens: the route is chosen here, by practice or habit rather than by argument, and it is decisive in exactly the cases where it is invisible.
Because the two counts agree in almost every case, the choice is unexamined until it matters, and when it matters nobody notices that it was made.
It ends having presided correctly and having determined the result.

## Tell

Says: shall we take the issues first?
Which is a scheduling question, and is the judgment.
