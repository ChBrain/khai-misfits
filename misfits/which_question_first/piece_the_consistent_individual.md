# Piece: The Consistent Individual

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). Every judge coherent: answers that hang together and a verdict that follows from them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At each seat, throughout.
Answered nowhere: there is nothing to correct at any of the three.

## Load Bearing

Without it the contradiction has an author and somebody can be told to think again.
It is load bearing because it **locates the failure in the joining**: individual coherence is complete and collective coherence is unavailable, so no improvement in the reasoning, preparation or care of any participant touches the result.

## Apparent

Competence, and the ordinary expectation of a judge.
It reads as the baseline rather than as a finding, and the fact that full individual coherence is precisely what makes the collective contradiction inescapable is the opposite of how competence usually reads.

## Yearbook

Established in [judging](process_judging.md), unaffected by everything after it.
