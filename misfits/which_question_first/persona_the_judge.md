# Persona: The Judge

## Taxonomy

[Coherent](position_coherent.md). One of three, entirely consistent: an answer to each question and the verdict those answers require.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, well prepared and holding a coherent view.
Applies the agreed rule correctly to their own findings, so their verdict follows from their answers without a gap.
Not strategic and not confused: give them the same case again and they vote the same way.

## Action

Answers the contract question.
Answers the breach question.
Reaches the verdict those two answers require under a rule they accept.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is what makes the play an impossibility rather than a failure: there is no error to find here, no inconsistency to correct and no reasoning to improve.
Because each of the three is coherent, whatever goes wrong goes wrong in the joining of them rather than in any of them.
It ends having voted correctly on every question and having contributed to a result that contradicts either its answers or its verdict.

## Tell

Says: no contract, so not liable.
Coherent, correct, and about to be outvoted on the first half and joined on the second.
