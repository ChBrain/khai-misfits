## Stage

[The Judge](persona_the_judge.md) is coherent: answers that hang together and a verdict that follows.
[The Rule](persona_the_rule.md) is agreed and correctly applied, by each judge to their own row and by the court to its column majorities.

## Tension

There is no error, and that is what makes this an impossibility rather than a failure.
Every individual position is consistent. The rule is uncontested and applied correctly in both directions. Both counts are arithmetically right. So the contradiction is not in any participant and cannot be removed by better reasoning, more preparation or a longer conference: **individual coherence is complete and collective coherence is unavailable**. And the thing that makes it unavailable is the one thing nobody disputes, since without a logical link between the questions there would be nothing for a majority to be inconsistent about. **The agreement is the cause.**
