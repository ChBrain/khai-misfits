# Plan: Be Bound by Whichever It Was

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the bound seat](position_bound.md), inside [Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Bound](position_bound.md)

## Direction

Argue the law and the facts, receive a full hearing on every point, and be bound by a count that was never put to you and cannot be raised on appeal.

## Orders

([The Losing Party](persona_the_losing_party.md) argues the case, loses or wins according to the route, and reads a judgment recording the outcome and not the route.)

## Implementation

([The Judgment](place_the_judgment.md), which is unimpeachable on its face; [the silent record](piece_the_silent_record.md) omitting the route; nothing available as a ground because there is no error.)

## Targets

- [ ] the hearing is full and correct on every argued point
- [ ] the case would have gone the other way on the other count
- [ ] the ground of decision is internal practice rather than law or fact
- [ ] review addresses reasoning and outcome, not how a panel combined itself
