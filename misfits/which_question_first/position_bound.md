# Position: Bound

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). The seat the outcome lands on, bound by a route it was never allowed to address.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Full representation and a complete hearing on every argued point.
A case that would have gone the other way on the other count.
No ground of appeal, because there is no error.

## Orders

Argue the law and the facts.
Lose, or win, by a count nobody put to it.
Read a judgment that records the outcome and not the route.

## Loses

The case, for a cause that appears nowhere in it: the ground of decision is internal practice rather than law or fact, so there is nothing in the judgment to challenge and nothing in the record to point at.
Because review addresses reasoning and outcome rather than how a panel combined itself, the actual cause of the loss is not a reviewable thing.
It ends bound, correctly, by something it never had a chance to address.

## Drives

Toward a fair hearing, which it received,
never once able to name what beat it,
so the party with the strongest grievance is the one with nothing to cite.
