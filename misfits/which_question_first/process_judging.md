# Process: Judging

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). Each of three forming an answer to every question and the verdict those answers require.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A case, an agreed rule, and evidence.
At [the conference room](place_the_conference_room.md), independently.

## Direction

Toward a coherent individual position.
There is nothing to criticise at this step and the play does not try: each judge answers on the evidence, applies a rule nobody disputes, and reaches the verdict that follows, with no gap anywhere.
So the play's whole difficulty is assembled out of three people about whom nothing is wrong, which is what puts it outside anybody's competence as well as their conduct.

## Lever

The evidence, and the rule, both of which are available to all three equally.
Because the disagreement is about findings rather than about law, no amount of further argument between them removes it.

## Echo

[The three views](piece_the_three_views.md): contract and no breach, breach and no contract, both.
Three rows, each internally consistent, and no two alike.
