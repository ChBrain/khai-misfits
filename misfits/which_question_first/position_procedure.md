# Position: Procedure

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). The seat that settles how the panel combines itself, and thereby settles the case.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Authority over what is put and in what order.
An ordinary administrative role, discharged properly.
No sense of deciding anything substantive, because the counts almost always agree.

## Orders

Order the questions.
Take the count that order implies.
Record the outcome.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat is where the decision actually happens: the route is chosen by practice or habit rather than by argument, and it is decisive precisely in the cases where nobody notices it was chosen.
Because the two counts agree in nearly every case, the choice goes unexamined until it matters, and it is invisible when it does.
It ends having presided correctly and having determined the result.

## Drives

Toward an orderly conference,
never once intending to decide anything,
so the seat that arranges the meeting is the seat that gives the judgment.
