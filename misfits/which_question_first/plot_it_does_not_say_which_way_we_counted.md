# Plot: It Does Not Say Which Way We Counted

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The judgment as published: an order, and reasons.
[The Silent Record](piece_the_silent_record.md), complete by every existing convention.

## Action

[Publishing](process_publishing.md): the outcome and the reasoning recorded at [the judgment](place_the_judgment.md), and the route omitted.

## Stage

[The Losing Party](persona_the_losing_party.md), on [Be Bound by Whichever It Was](plan_be_bound_by_whichever_it_was.md), reads a document that records the outcome and not the route.
Nothing was concealed: the aggregation rule a panel used to combine itself is not conventionally part of a judgment, and no drafter omitted it in order to hide anything.

## Tension

This is what turns a curiosity about counting into something a party actually loses on.
The hearing was full and correct on every argued point. The case would have gone the other way on the other count. And the ground of decision is internal practice rather than law or fact, so there is nothing in the judgment to challenge and nothing in the record to point at: **review addresses reasoning and outcome, and a panel's internal arithmetic is neither.** The losing party reads an unimpeachable document and cannot name what beat it. And fixing one route by rule does not dissolve any of this, since a rule nobody voted on, applied to every case of the kind before any of them is heard, is the same choice made once instead of many times.
