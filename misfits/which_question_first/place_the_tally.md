# Place: The Tally

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md). Where the two counts are laid side by side and both turn out to be genuine majorities.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A table of three rows and three columns: contract, breach, verdict.
Two to one, two to one, and two to one the other way.

## Holds

The whole misfit, in nine cells.
It is where the arithmetic can be checked rather than believed: every column carries a real majority, every judge's row is coherent, and the rule applied to the column majorities and the rule applied to the row verdicts give opposite answers.

## Offers

Complete transparency about what everybody thought, on every question.

## Withheld

Any indication which count governs.
The table settles nothing by itself, so a document that contains the entire truth of the deliberation is silent on the only question that decides the case.
