# Plot: Three Judges, Two Questions

## Taxonomy

[Which Question First](play_which_question_first.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A claim before a panel of three, and a rule none of them disputes.
[The Connective](piece_the_connective.md): liable if and only if there was a contract and it was breached.

## Action

[Judging](process_judging.md): [The Judge](persona_the_judge.md), in [the coherent seat](position_coherent.md), forms an answer to each question and the verdict those answers require.

## Stage

[The Judge](persona_the_judge.md), on [Vote My View on Every Question](plan_vote_my_view_on_every_question.md), answers on the evidence and applies the agreed rule to their own findings.
[The Rule](persona_the_rule.md), in [the binding seat](position_binding.md), is accepted by all three and argued by no party.

## Tension

Nothing here is contrived, and establishing that is the plot's whole function.
An agreed rule, an undisputed logical connection, three prepared people who have each read the papers, and a disagreement about findings rather than about law. This is the ordinary situation that majority rule exists to resolve. **The play's entire difficulty will be assembled out of three individuals about whom nothing whatever is wrong**, which is what puts it outside competence as well as outside conduct.
