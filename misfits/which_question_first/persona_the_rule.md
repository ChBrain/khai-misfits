# Persona: The Rule

## Taxonomy

[Binding](position_binding.md). The agreed law: liable if and only if there was a contract and it was breached.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Settled, uncontested and applied identically by all three judges.
Not the thing in dispute: no party argues it and no judge doubts it.
A connective rather than a judgement, joining two findings into one outcome.

## Action

Joins the two questions to the verdict.
Applies to each judge's own answers, correctly.
Applies to the majority's answers too, if anybody chooses to apply it there.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is the load-bearing one nobody suspects: it is the agreement on the rule that makes the two counts diverge, since without a logical connection between the questions there is nothing for a majority to be inconsistent about.
Because it is uncontested, it is never examined, and the disagreement it produces is invisible as long as everybody is looking for a disagreement about law.
It ends having been correctly applied twice, to two different sets of answers, giving two different verdicts.

## Tell

Says: contract and breach, or nothing.
Which everybody accepts, and which is exactly what makes the trouble possible.
