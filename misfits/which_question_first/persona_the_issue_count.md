# Persona: The Issue Count

## Taxonomy

[Issue](position_issue.md). The tally taken question by question: two to one there was a contract, two to one it was breached.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A genuine majority on each question, arrived at by correct counting.
Not a manoeuvre and not a technicality: this is what deciding an issue by majority means.
Produces the answer a majority of the panel actually holds on each point.

## Action

Counts the contract question and finds a majority yes.
Counts the breach question and finds a majority yes.
Applies the agreed rule to those answers, and returns liable.

## Shadow

Nothing of its own, and its result is a verdict a majority of the panel would have voted against: two of the three would each say not liable if asked directly.
Because it is faithful to what the panel believes about the law's elements, it is unfaithful to what the panel believes about the case.
It ends with a defensible reasoned judgment and an outcome most of the court rejects.

## Tell

Says: on the findings of this court, the defendant is liable.
True of the findings. Not true of the judges.
