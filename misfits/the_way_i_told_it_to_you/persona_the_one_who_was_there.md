# Persona: The One Who Was There

## Taxonomy

[Telling](position_telling.md). The witness, describing the afternoon to somebody who was not at it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Straightforward, generous with detail, and easy to follow.
Would repeat any sentence of it under oath.
Is the person everybody would ask, because they were the one in the room.

## Action

Selects, among the true things, the ones this listener will be able to use.
Leans on what the two of them already share, because that is what a reference needs to land.
Stops when the listener has it.

## Shadow

That the selection is not felt as a selection.
**Every sentence was true of the afternoon**, so nothing in the experience of telling it resembles editing,
and the only signal that a version has been chosen would be the other versions, which were never said and are
now not held.

## Tell

Is the element that did the honest thing.
Is the element that ends up with one copy of five.
