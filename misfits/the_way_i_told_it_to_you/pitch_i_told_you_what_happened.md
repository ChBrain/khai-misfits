# Pitch: I Told You What Happened

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Warm about the telling, because the telling is the good part.
Exact about what was said and what was merely true and not said.
Unwilling to call the speaker a flatterer or the listener a manipulator, because neither is required.

## Undertow

Under an ordinary account of an ordinary afternoon, one property of events and one property of speech.
A real thing that happened admits several accurate descriptions, so choosing among them crosses no line.
And an utterance is built for the person hearing it, or it is not understood by anybody.

## Nerve

The unease of a conversation that went perfectly.
Nobody lied. Nobody argued. Nobody was persuaded of anything by anybody.
The exchange succeeded, and the succeeding is what did it.

## Echo

After the run: an afternoon that would have supported several tellings,
one telling built so that a particular person could take it,
a nod coming back that the taking had worked,
and, a year on, a single copy of the afternoon, in the shape it was given in.
