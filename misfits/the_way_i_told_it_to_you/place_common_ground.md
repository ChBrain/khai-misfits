# Place: Common Ground

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). What the two of them already hold between them, which is where every reference in the telling is anchored.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Years of it: shared acquaintances, shared history, a shared sense of what the listener thinks about this sort of thing.
No inventory, because nobody has ever needed one.
The reason a half-finished sentence is enough.

## Holds

The listener's view, as a fact about the world rather than as a claim in the conversation.
**Being known is the whole contribution**, which is why nothing here can be described as pressure: the view
sits in the shared background where the speaker's word choices are made, and it was put there by ordinary
acquaintance rather than by anybody's argument.

## Offers

The fastest, most accurate telling available: everything that does not have to be explained.

## Withheld

Any way to use it for reference and not use it for selection.
