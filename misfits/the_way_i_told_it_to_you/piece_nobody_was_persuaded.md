# Piece: Nobody Was Persuaded

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). The listener's view was known and never asserted, so nothing in the play is influence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the [reception](position_reception.md) seat, which makes no case at any point.
At [common ground](place_common_ground.md), where the view sits as a fact about the world rather than as a claim in the conversation.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a person talked round by a friend, which the house has and which this is not.
It is load bearing because **being known is the whole contribution**: the listener offers no evidence,
raises no objection and never mentions their opinion, so there is no argument anywhere in the play for the
speaker to have lost, and the drift runs anyway.

## Apparent

That the speaker adopted somebody else's view for want of one of their own.
They had one. The effect does not require the teller to arrive without a judgement of the matter, and the
later work is explicit that it does not depend on the communicator's own initial judgement, so this is not a
vacuum being filled.

## Yearbook

The finding that audience tuning shifts the communicator's own memory where the audience's attitude is
merely known to them, with no persuasive attempt made and none needed.
