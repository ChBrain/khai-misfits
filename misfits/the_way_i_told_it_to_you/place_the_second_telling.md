# Place: The Second Telling

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). A year on, in a different room, where the afternoon is wanted for something that matters.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A reference, a review, a question put by somebody entitled to ask it.
A witness doing their honest best and being believed, correctly, to be doing so.
An account delivered with the detail and confidence of somebody who was there, because they were.

## Holds

The residue, arriving as testimony.
**What comes back is what is held**, so the shaping done on a walk home a year ago is now indistinguishable,
to the speaker and to everybody listening, from what happened.

## Offers

A sincere and specific account from the only available witness.

## Withheld

Any means, for anyone in the room including the witness, of telling it from the untold one.
