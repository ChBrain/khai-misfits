# Play: The Way I Told It to You

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Way I Told It to You.
Somebody was there, and somebody else was not, and the first tells the second what happened.
They tell it to _them_: to a person whose view of the matter is already known, in the words that will land with that person, choosing among the descriptions the thing honestly admits.
It lands. And a year on, when the event is wanted again, what comes back is the version that landed.

## Arc

The bend is this: a message built so that one particular person could take it is the message its author is left holding.

Take the event first, because the play has to establish that nothing false is ever said. A real thing that happened is not a single sentence waiting to be reported: it admits several accurate descriptions, and which of them a person reaches for is not a choice between truth and untruth. **The material is ambiguous and the tellings are all true.** So there is no line the speaker crosses and no moment where they decide to shade anything.

Then take the listener, who has a view. The speaker knows it; they have known it for years; it was never argued for and is not being argued for now. The listener asks for nothing, presses nothing and offers no evidence. **Nobody is persuaded of anything in this play**, and that has to be established early, because the shape is otherwise mistaken for influence and it is not influence.

Then the tuning, which looks like the flaw and is the competence. A speaker designs each utterance for particular listeners, and listeners resolve what was meant by assuming precisely that: they arrive at one reading rather than another because they take the speaker to have built it so they would. **An untuned message is not a plainer message; it is an uninterpretable one.** So the selection among true accounts is not a concession made to the room. It is the thing that makes the account land at all, and a speaker who declined it would not be telling the unvarnished truth but failing to say anything usable.

Then the uptake, which is where the loss actually enters, and which runs the wrong way round. The residue does not follow the tuning by itself. It follows the tuning **when the communication worked**: when the signal comes back that the listener got it, that they identified the thing being described, that the two of them are now holding the same picture. Where that signal is absent, the memory does not move; where it arrives, it does, and it does so through the speaker's trust in the listener's reading of the world. **What makes the message good is what makes the memory move.** A speaker aiming to be understood is aiming, exactly, at the condition under which their own record of the event is quietly replaced.

Then the keeping, which nobody notices because there is nothing to notice. The speaker does not have two copies of the afternoon, one raw and one told. They have what they have. Asked again a year later, they produce the version that was successfully shared, and it is not a distortion they could catch, because catching it would need the original, and the original was never stored anywhere but in them.

And the accounting is the strange part: the room lost nothing. The listener received a true, well-built description and was not misled by any of it. Nobody was flattered, nobody lied, nobody was talked into anything, and no group was starved of a signal it needed. The whole cost falls on the one person who was actually there, and it falls after everybody has gone home.

Reach for a settlement and each one gives back the thing that was wanted. Tell it flat, to nobody in particular, and produce an account nobody in particular can use. Write it down first, and write the note for somebody too. Tell it to several people, and hold several tuned versions instead of one. Learn about the effect, and have nothing to compare against, because the unedited copy was never made.

## Triggers

**[Who I Am Telling](plot_who_i_am_telling.md)**
Cue: an event, and somebody who was not at it. Closes when it is established that the listener has a known view, that the view was never argued for and is not being argued for now, and that the speaker is about to describe something to a particular person rather than into the air.

**[The Version That Fits](plot_the_version_that_fits.md)**
Cue: the telling itself, at the pace of ordinary speech. Closes when the account has been built for this hearer out of descriptions the afternoon honestly supports, with nothing false asserted and nothing withheld for the speaker's own sake, and when it is clear that a message not built this way would not have been a plainer one but an unusable one.

**[They Got It](plot_they_got_it.md)**
Cue: the small signal coming back that the listener has it: the nod, the right question, the name supplied before it is given. Closes when the exchange is seen to have succeeded completely, and when the success, rather than the tuning, is located as the thing that fixes the version.

**[A Year Later](plot_a_year_later.md)**
Cue: the event wanted again, in another room, for another purpose. Closes when what comes back is the told version, when the speaker has no second copy to check it against, and when it is clear that this is not a lapse anybody could have caught from the inside.

**[Telling It Straight](plot_telling_it_straight.md)**
Cue: the settlements, tried in good faith. Closes when each gives back what it was for: an untuned account nobody can use, a written record written for somebody, several audiences and several kept versions, and a warning that names a drift the speaker has nothing to measure against.

## Stakes

Whether a person can describe what happened to somebody in particular and still keep what happened.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the speaker was honest, because every sentence was true of the afternoon, and not whether the listener applied pressure, because the listener made no case at all, but whether the act of building an account somebody else can take leaves the builder with the account they built. The production cannot settle it. It can only show that the material admitted more than one true telling, that designing for the hearer is what makes a telling land, that the memory moves when and because the landing succeeds, and that the speaker holds one copy of the afternoon and it is now the copy they made for you.
