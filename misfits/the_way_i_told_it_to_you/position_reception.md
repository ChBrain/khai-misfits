# Position: Reception

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). The seat that holds a view of the matter and never once puts it as a claim.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An opinion about the thing being described, formed elsewhere and long ago.
No evidence to offer and no intention of offering any.
Complete innocence of what its presence is doing.

## Orders

Listen. Ask the question that shows you are following.
Do not argue, do not correct, do not press, do not raise the subject of your own view at all.
Be somebody worth telling.

## Loses

Nothing, and gains nothing, which is the point.
**This seat makes no case**, so nothing that happens in the play can be described as persuasion: what the
seat contributes is not an argument but the plain fact of being a known reader, and it would contribute the
same amount asleep.

## Drives

Toward a telling shaped by a view that was never asserted,
by a party who did not ask for it and could not have withdrawn it,
so the influence is a property of being known rather than of anything the seat does.
