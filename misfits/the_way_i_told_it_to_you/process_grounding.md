# Process: Grounding

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). Establishing, between the two of them, that the thing has been understood well enough to go on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Any contribution that needs to be taken before the next one can be made.
At [the walk home](place_the_walk_home.md), continuously, by both parties without either noticing.

## Direction

Toward mutual belief that the contribution has landed.
Every conversation runs this, at every turn, and one that did not would stall on its first sentence, so
there is no version of the telling in which this is skipped or throttled.

## Lever

[The Nod](persona_the_nod.md), and its absence.
The absence is a real lever and does real work: where the signal of successful identification does not come
back, the residue does not form, which means the only way to avoid the effect is to fail.

## Echo

Two people who now hold what they take to be the same picture,
each correct about that,
and one of them changed by it.
