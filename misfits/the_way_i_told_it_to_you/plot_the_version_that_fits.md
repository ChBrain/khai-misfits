# Plot: The Version That Fits

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The telling itself, at the pace of ordinary speech, with nothing at stake.

## Action

[Tuning](process_tuning.md): [The One Who Was There](persona_the_one_who_was_there.md), in [the telling seat](position_telling.md), works through [Say It So They Get It](plan_say_it_so_they_get_it.md) and is held throughout to [Say Nothing That Is Not True](plan_say_nothing_that_is_not_true.md).

## Stage

[The Walk Home](place_the_walk_home.md): twenty minutes, no record kept, nobody else present, and no reason for anybody to be careful.

## Tension

This is the plot that has to be careful, because the telling is not a fault anybody commits.
[Every sentence was true](piece_every_sentence_was_true.md), a fact-checker with the afternoon in hand would
pass every line, and nothing is held back for the speaker's own sake.
And [the tuning is the competence](piece_the_tuning_is_the_competence.md): a hearer arrives at one reading
rather than another by taking the speaker to have built it for them, so the selection among true accounts is
not a softening but the thing that makes the account land at all.
Behind each sentence stands
[what the afternoon would also support](persona_what_the_afternoon_would_also_support.md), which has no line
in the play and never will, because the only party who could have staged it is the one now holding a single
version. The stakes are zero here, which the play needs: the shaping is not a response to pressure, and it
would run identically in a room with consequences in it.
