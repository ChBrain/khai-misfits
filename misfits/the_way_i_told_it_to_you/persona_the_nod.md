# Persona: The Nod

## Taxonomy

[Uptake](position_uptake.md). The signal coming back that the account was taken, given without thinking by everybody who listens to anything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Too small to be an event.
Costs nothing, means only that the thing went over, and is given by any competent listener a hundred times an hour.
Is the most ordinary object in the play.

## Action

Registers that the contribution has been understood well enough for present purposes.
Releases the speaker to go on.
Does this continuously, because a conversation that stopped to check would not be a conversation.

## Shadow

That it is the trigger and not the courtesy.
**The memory moves when the message lands and not when it is merely tuned**, and it moves through the
speaker's trust in this listener's reading of the world, so the residue is a function of how well the
exchange went rather than of how hard it was shaped.

## Tell

Is the element that means everything worked.
Is the element that fixes the version.
