# Plot: They Got It

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The signal coming back: the nod, the follow-up in the right key, the name supplied a beat before it is given.

## Action

[Trusting](process_trusting.md): [The Nod](persona_the_nod.md), in [the uptake seat](position_uptake.md), works through [Check That It Landed](plan_check_that_it_landed.md).

## Stage

[The Walk Home](place_the_walk_home.md): two people who now hold what they take to be the same picture, and are right about that.

## Tension

Everything worked, and the working is the mechanism.
[It only moves if it landed](piece_it_only_moves_if_it_landed.md): the memory follows the tuned account
after the audience is seen to have identified what was described, and not after they are seen to have
missed it, and what carries it inward is the speaker's trust in this listener's reading of the world rather
than any judgement about the account.
So the direction runs backwards from where a reader expects it. **What makes the message good is what makes
the memory move**, and a speaker who wanted to protect their copy of the afternoon would have to arrange to
be misunderstood by somebody they did not respect. The scene has no fault in it anywhere, and it is the
scene where the version is fixed.
