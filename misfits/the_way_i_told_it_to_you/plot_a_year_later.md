# Plot: A Year Later

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The afternoon wanted again, in another room, for something that matters.

## Action

[Recalling](process_recalling.md): [What I Have a Year Later](persona_what_i_have_a_year_later.md), in [the record seat](position_record.md), works through [Remember What Happened](plan_remember_what_happened.md), on a copy laid down by [keeping](process_keeping.md).

## Stage

[The Second Telling](place_the_second_telling.md): a reference, a review, a question put by somebody entitled to ask it, answered by the only available witness.

## Tension

The witness is honest, specific and believed, and is right to be believed about everything they can be
checked on.
[Keeping](process_keeping.md) overwrote nothing and deleted nothing, because there was never a second draft
to overwrite: the shared version is the available one, and availability is all a memory has.
So care does not reach it. A witness who resolves to be scrupulous is scrupulous about what comes back, and
what comes back is the account built on a walk home for one person whose view of the matter everyone had
known for years. Nobody in the room, the witness included, can tell it from the untold one, and the party who
is short something is the party nobody is worried about.
