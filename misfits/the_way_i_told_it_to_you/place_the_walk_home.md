# Place: The Walk Home

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). Where the account is given: no record, no audience beyond the one, and no reason for anyone to be careful.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two people, one of whom was there.
Twenty minutes, at the pace of ordinary speech, with nothing at stake and nobody else present.
A telling that both of them would call a nice talk about what happened.

## Holds

The whole mechanism, in a setting with no pressure in it at all.
**The tuning is not a response to stakes**, so it runs here exactly as it would run in front of a panel:
what is doing the work is a listener with a known view, not a room with consequences in it.

## Offers

The best conditions the play has: honesty, ease, time, and a hearer worth telling.

## Withheld

Any copy of what was said, and any copy of what was not.
