# Piece: It Only Moves If It Landed

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). The residue follows successful communication, not the shaping, which is what makes the play a trap and not a habit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the [uptake](position_uptake.md) seat, and in the [grounding](process_grounding.md) and [trusting](process_trusting.md) processes that run through it.
In [The Nod](persona_the_nod.md), which is too small to be an event and is the trigger.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is impression management and the house already has that.
It is load bearing because the direction is backwards: memory moved toward the tuned message **after
feedback that the audience had identified the target and not after failed identification**, mediated by the
speaker's epistemic trust in the audience's judgement. If the mechanism were about looking good, whether the
listener understood would not matter, and it is the only thing that does.

## Apparent

That a careful speaker could tune and then simply not let it stick.
The condition for it sticking is the condition they are speaking for. **What makes the message good is what
makes the memory move**, so the only reliable way to protect the copy is to be misunderstood.

## Yearbook

The shared-reality finding that audience-tuning effects on the communicator's memory are contingent on the
experience of a successful, trusted exchange rather than on the tuning itself.
