# Process: Tuning

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). Building the account for the person who is going to receive it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The decision to say anything at all to anybody in particular.
At [the walk home](place_the_walk_home.md), in the first sentence, by somebody who wants to be understood.

## Direction

Toward an account this hearer can take whole.
This is not a softening and not a compromise: a speaker designs each utterance for particular listeners, and
listeners resolve reference and word meaning by assuming exactly that, so the design is what makes a unique
reading available at all.

## Lever

Everything at [common ground](place_common_ground.md), which is what the selection is made out of.
The lever cannot be released without releasing the reference too, so a speaker who declined to tune would not
produce a plainer account but an account nobody could resolve.

## Echo

One telling, entirely true,
built out of a set of tellings that were also entirely true,
with no moment in it that felt like a choice.
