# Process: Recalling

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). The afternoon asked for again, later, by somebody entitled to ask.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A question that matters, put to the only witness.
At [the second telling](place_the_second_telling.md), a year on, in a room with something riding on it.

## Direction

Toward an honest, detailed, confident answer.
The witness is not performing and not hedging: they are reporting what they have, which is what a truthful
witness is asked to do, and they do it well.

## Lever

Care, which is available and does nothing.
A witness who resolves to be scrupulous will be scrupulous about the version they hold, since scruple
operates on what is retrieved and there is nothing else in the seat to operate on.

## Echo

Testimony that is sincere, specific and believed,
and that is the account built for one listener on a walk home,
with nobody in the room, the witness included, able to tell the difference.
