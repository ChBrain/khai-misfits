# Persona: The Person I Was Telling

## Taxonomy

[Reception](position_reception.md). The known reader, whose view of the matter is old news and never once said aloud.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Interested, unhurried, asking the question that shows they are following.
Has no stake in the account and wants nothing from it.
Would be astonished to hear that they had shaped anything.

## Action

Listens.
Supplies a name a beat before it is given, which is what following looks like.
Does not raise their own opinion, because nobody raised the subject.

## Shadow

That being known is enough.
**Nobody was persuaded**, and this element could not have argued the speaker into anything because it never
argued: what it contributed was its readership, and it would have contributed the same amount having said
nothing at all.

## Tell

Is the element with no case to answer.
Is the element the whole telling was built around.
