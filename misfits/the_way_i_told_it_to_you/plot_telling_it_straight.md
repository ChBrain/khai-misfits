# Plot: Telling It Straight

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The settlements, tried in good faith by somebody who has understood the problem.

## Action

[Tuning](process_tuning.md) again, and again, against every discipline that might suspend it, at [the telling seat](position_telling.md).

## Stage

[Common Ground](place_common_ground.md): the only material a telling can be built out of, and the reason none of the settlements gets clear of it.

## Tension

Each one gives back the thing it was for.
Tell it flat, to nobody in particular, and the words still have to be resolved by whoever is listening, so
the account is not undesigned but designed for a person who is not here, and no particular person can use it.
Write it down first, and the note is written for somebody too.
Tell it to several people, and hold several tuned versions rather than none.
Learn about the effect, and find there is nothing to compare against, because
[the room lost nothing](piece_the_room_lost_nothing.md) and neither did any record: the unedited copy was
never made, and the only seat that held one is [the record](position_record.md), which now holds the other.
The last settlement is the honest one and the play states it plainly: stop caring whether the listener
understood, and the drift stops, along with the reason anybody says anything to anybody.
