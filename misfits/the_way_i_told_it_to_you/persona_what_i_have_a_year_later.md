# Persona: What I Have a Year Later

## Taxonomy

[Record](position_record.md). What comes back when the afternoon is asked for again, in another room, for another purpose.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sincere, detailed and offered in complete good faith.
Is what the speaker actually remembers, not what they have decided to say.
Would survive cross-examination, because there is nothing in it the speaker is holding back.

## Action

Returns the afternoon on request.
Returns it in the shape it is held in.
Says, accurately, that this is what they remember.

## Shadow

That the shape is the shape it was given in.
**There is no second copy to check against**, so an honest witness reporting honestly reports the version
built for one listener a year ago, and no amount of care about accuracy reaches a draft that was never
stored.

## Tell

Is the element telling the truth.
Is the element carrying the whole cost.
