# Position: Uptake

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). The seat where it is settled that the contribution went over.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The small change that tells a speaker they were understood: the nod, the follow-up in the right key, the name supplied a beat before it was given.
A judgement about whether to let the account stand or ask again.
The gate on everything that happens afterwards.

## Orders

Signal receipt when the thing has been received.
Signal trouble when it has not.
Do this continuously and without ceremony, because a conversation that did not do it would stall on every sentence.

## Loses

Nothing it could have kept.
**This seat is the mechanism**, which is the part that runs backwards: the residue in the speaker follows
the successful pass and not the tuning, so a seat doing the one thing it is for is the seat that fixes the
version.

## Drives

Toward two people holding what they take to be the same picture,
with the speaker's trust in the other's reading of the world carrying the picture back into them,
so the better the exchange goes the more completely the told version becomes the kept one.
