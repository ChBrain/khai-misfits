# Position: Ambiguity

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). The seat that makes more than one true account available, occupied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The afternoon, which was not a sentence and does not reduce to one.
A supply of accurate descriptions, several of which point in different directions.
No occupant, no discretion, and nothing anyone can appeal to.

## Orders

Admit every reading the facts support.
Refuse to nominate one of them as the account.
Do this for any event whatever, since events are not propositions and never arrive pre-worded.

## Loses

Nothing, because it holds nothing anybody wanted.
**A description is a selection and there is no unselected one**, so the seat cannot be tightened by care or
by discipline: a speaker asked to describe the afternoon without choosing would be asked to say nothing.

## Drives

Toward a telling that is true and a telling that is also true and points elsewhere,
with no fact of the matter about which of them the event was,
so the tuning never has to cross into anything the speaker does not believe.
