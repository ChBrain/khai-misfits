# Piece: The Tuning Is the Competence

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). Designing an utterance for its hearer is what makes it interpretable, so there is no untuned version to have told instead.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the [tuning](process_tuning.md) process, which cannot be released without releasing the reference with it.
In every resolved pronoun and every half-finished sentence at [the walk home](place_the_walk_home.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the remedy is obvious and the misfit dissolves into advice.
It is load bearing because a speaker designs each utterance for particular listeners and **listeners reach a
unique reading by assuming exactly that**, so an untuned account is not a plainer account but one nobody can
resolve, and the discipline that would remove the drift removes the message.

## Apparent

That one could just tell it neutrally.
Neutral to whom. A description built for no one in particular still uses words, and the hearer will resolve
them against whatever they do know, so the choice is not between designed and undesigned but between
designed for this person and designed for a person who is not here.

## Yearbook

The audience-design account of meaning and reference, in which the hearer's route to a unique interpretation
runs through the assumption that the speaker built the utterance for them.
