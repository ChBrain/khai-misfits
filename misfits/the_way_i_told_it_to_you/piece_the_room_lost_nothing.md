# Piece: The Room Lost Nothing

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). The whole cost falls on the teller, which is what separates it from every play about what a group fails to hear.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the [record](position_record.md) seat, which is inside one person.
At [the second telling](place_the_second_telling.md), where the bill arrives, a year after everyone went home.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is the house's **The Front** in different clothes.
It is load bearing because the ledgers point opposite ways: there the speaker edits for how they will be
read, holds back what would cost them standing, and the group is starved of the burning thing while the
speaker's own belief is untouched. Here nothing is held back, the listener receives a true and well-built
account, the group is short of nothing, and the only party changed is the one who was actually there.

## Apparent

That an exchange with no loser in the room is an exchange with no loss.
The loss is a copy of an afternoon, held by one person, spent at the moment the exchange succeeded, and
payable at [the second telling](place_the_second_telling.md) to somebody who was not on the walk home and
has no way to know what they are being handed.

## Yearbook

The saying-is-believing effect stated where it lives: in the communicator's later memory and evaluation, and
not in what the audience took away.
