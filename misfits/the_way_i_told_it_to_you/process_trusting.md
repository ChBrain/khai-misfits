# Process: Trusting

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). Taking the listener as somebody whose reading of the world is worth having.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The exchange having gone well.
At [common ground](place_common_ground.md), over years, and confirmed in the minute the account lands.

## Direction

Toward holding the shared version as the good one.
The literature puts this in the middle of the mechanism rather than beside it: the memory effect is mediated
by the communicator's trust in the audience's judgement about the world, so what carries the tuned account
inward is an estimate of the listener rather than an estimate of the account.

## Lever

Who the listener is.
Nothing here is available to discipline: a speaker cannot decide to trust a trusted friend less for the
duration of a story about an afternoon, and would be worse off in every other respect if they could.

## Echo

A version endorsed by somebody worth being understood by,
which is the property that makes it worth keeping,
and the property that makes it the one that is kept.
