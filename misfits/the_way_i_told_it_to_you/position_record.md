# Position: Record

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). The seat that holds the afternoon afterwards, and holds exactly one of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

One copy, kept in the person who was there.
No index, no timestamps, no earlier draft retained for comparison.
Whatever comes back when the afternoon is asked for.

## Orders

Return the event on request.
Return it in the shape it is held in, without annotation.
Offer no account of how it came to be in that shape.

## Loses

The version that was not told, silently and without a receipt.
**There is no second copy to check against**, so the drift is not merely unnoticed but unnoticeable from
inside: an audit would need the original, and the original was only ever stored here.

## Drives

Toward an honest witness who will say what they remember,
who does remember it,
and whose memory of the afternoon is the account they once built for somebody in particular.
