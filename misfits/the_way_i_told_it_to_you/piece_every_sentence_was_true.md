# Piece: Every Sentence Was True

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). The material admits several accurate accounts, so the tuning never has to assert anything false.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the [ambiguity](position_ambiguity.md) seat, which holds the other true accounts and never names one as the account.
In every line of the telling at [the walk home](place_the_walk_home.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a person shading the truth and dissolves into a note about honesty.
It is load bearing because **a description is a selection and there is no unselected one**: the effect is
reported for evaluatively ambiguous material precisely because that is where the choice among true readings
lives, and a speaker held to strict truth is held to nothing that narrows it.

## Apparent

That a scrupulous person would simply say what happened.
There is no what-happened at the resolution a sentence needs. The afternoon was not a proposition, and the
speaker asked to report it without choosing among its true descriptions is being asked to stay silent.

## Yearbook

The saying-is-believing paradigm's use of stimulus material constructed to admit both a positive and a
negative reading, which is what separates the effect from misreporting.
