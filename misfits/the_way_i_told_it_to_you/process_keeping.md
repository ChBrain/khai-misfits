# Process: Keeping

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md). The told version settling into the only copy of the event there is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Nothing anybody does.
At [the record](position_record.md), in the hours and weeks after, without a step anyone could point at.

## Direction

Toward one account where there had been an afternoon.
No overwrite is performed and no earlier draft is deleted, because no earlier draft was ever written down:
what happens is that the version that was successfully shared is the version available, and availability is
all a memory has.

## Lever

None that the speaker holds.
The process has no interface: it cannot be paused, reviewed, or asked what it discarded, and a speaker who
knew about it in full detail would still have nothing to inspect.

## Echo

An event and a telling, arriving as one object,
in a person who would swear to it,
correctly, because it is what they have.
