# Plot: Who I Am Telling

## Taxonomy

[The Way I Told It to You](play_the_way_i_told_it_to_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An afternoon, and somebody walking home beside you who was not at it.

## Action

[Grounding](process_grounding.md): [The Person I Was Telling](persona_the_person_i_was_telling.md), in [the reception seat](position_reception.md), works through [Keep My View Out of It](plan_keep_my_view_out_of_it.md).

## Stage

[Common Ground](place_common_ground.md): years of shared acquaintance, and a view of this sort of thing that both of them have known about for as long as they have known each other.

## Tension

The play has to establish, before anything else, that there is no argument in it.
The listener will not make a case, will not offer evidence, will not correct and will not raise their own
opinion, because nobody raised the subject. [Nobody was persuaded](piece_nobody_was_persuaded.md) is a
statement about the whole run and not about this scene alone.
And the influence is present anyway, because what is doing the work is not an argument but a readership: the
view sits at common ground as a fact about the world, put there by ordinary acquaintance, and the speaker
will make every word choice against it without once treating it as a claim. The restraint that a decent
listener shows here removes the argument and leaves the influence exactly where it was.
