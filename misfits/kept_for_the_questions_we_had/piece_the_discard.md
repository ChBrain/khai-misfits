## Load Bearing

Its destruction removes the only evidence by which the decision to destroy it could be judged.
This is not an ordinary irreversibility. A demolished building can be photographed, a closed line has a map; here the thing lost is the information itself, so the counterfactual is not merely unobserved but destroyed, and no later method, technique or standard reaches it. The appraisal is therefore documented, reviewable as an argument, and unfalsifiable as a result.
