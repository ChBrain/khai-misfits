# Process: Asking the Record

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Questions We Had](play_kept_for_the_questions_we_had.md). Where a later question meets a shaped record.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A question, from whichever decade is asking.
[The Researcher](persona_the_researcher.md) or [the latecomer](persona_the_latecomer.md), at [the reading room](place_the_reading_room.md).

## Direction

Toward what the holdings can say, which is all any search can reach.

## Lever

An accurate catalogue, which is exhaustive about the holdings and cannot be about anything else.

## Echo

For one question, an answer and a citation. For another, nothing, which is what an absence of evidence and a removal both return.
