# Plot: The Record Improves by Every Check Available

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Questions We Had](play_kept_for_the_questions_we_had.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The annual report, and a profession asking honestly whether it is doing this well.

## Action

[The finding aid](piece_the_finding_aid.md) is measured against use at [the reading room](place_the_reading_room.md): requests served, backlog cleared, researchers satisfied, citations traced.

## Stage

Every indicator moving the right way, and every one of them correct.

## Tension

The instruments can only reach the kept part, so the feedback confirms the decision whatever the decision was.
Requests are served because what is requested is what is described; satisfaction is high among people whose questions the collection was selected to answer; and the backlog falls precisely to the degree that material was removed. None of these measurements is wrong, and none of them can be made about [the discard](piece_the_discard.md). So the loop runs entirely inside the surviving sample, and a profession doing this carefully, reviewing itself honestly and improving year on year would see exactly what it sees now whether the appraisals were excellent or catastrophic.
