# Play: Kept for the Questions We Had

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Kept for the Questions We Had.
More arrives than can ever be described, so a portion is kept and the rest is destroyed. The choice is made on written criteria, by trained people, in good faith, from the best evidence there is about what will be wanted: what is being asked for now. The record that results is better, by every measurement anybody can take of it, than the record would have been without the choice.

## Arc

The bend is this: the criterion is built from the present's questions and applied to the only copy, and the evidence that would show it was wrong is what the decision destroys.
It runs on selection being compulsory.
[Everything Cannot Be Kept](plot_everything_cannot_be_kept.md). The limit is [the cataloguer](persona_the_cataloguer.md) and not the shelving: an accession nobody has described cannot be asked for, so a larger undescribed record answers fewer questions than a smaller described one.
And the profession refuses to choose at random.
[So a Criterion Is Written Down](plot_so_a_criterion_is_written_down.md). [The Schedule](piece_the_schedule.md) is published, applied consistently and filed with its grounds, which is what makes the act professional and also what makes the loss patterned rather than even.
And the criterion is made of now.
[And the Criterion Is a Photograph of the Present](plot_and_the_criterion_is_a_photograph_of_the_present.md). Use, citation and the questions coming across the desk are honest measurements of what is wanted by the people who are here to want it, and nothing anywhere samples the people who are not.
Then [The Discard Is Not Recoverable](plot_the_discard_is_not_recoverable.md).
[The Discard](piece_the_discard.md) takes with it the only evidence by which the decision could be judged, so [the successor](persona_the_successor.md) may audit the reasoning forever and never the result.
And [the gap does not announce itself](plot_and_the_gap_does_not_announce_itself.md). [The Finding Aid](piece_the_finding_aid.md) is exhaustive about the holdings and cannot list an absence, so [the latecomer](persona_the_latecomer.md) reads an empty search as a practice that was rare rather than a record that was removed.
And [The Record Improves by Every Check Available](plot_the_record_improves_by_every_check_available.md). Requests served, backlog cleared, researchers satisfied: every instrument reaches only the kept part, so the feedback confirms the decision whatever the decision was.

## Triggers

**[Everything Cannot Be Kept](plot_everything_cannot_be_kept.md)**
Cue: an intake larger than the hours available to describe it. Closes when the binding cost has been located in description rather than storage, so a larger undescribed record answers fewer questions than a smaller described one.

**[So a Criterion Is Written Down](plot_so_a_criterion_is_written_down.md)**
Cue: a profession that refuses to select at random. Closes when the discipline that makes the act defensible is seen to be the same property that makes the loss patterned rather than even.

**[And the Criterion Is a Photograph of the Present](plot_and_the_criterion_is_a_photograph_of_the_present.md)**
Cue: the use statistics the schedule is built from. Closes when the measurement has been found sound and about the wrong population, since nothing samples people who are not yet here to be sampled.

**[The Discard Is Not Recoverable](plot_the_discard_is_not_recoverable.md)**
Cue: an appraisal signed off, and a lorry at the loading bay. Closes when what is destroyed turns out to be the evidence by which the destruction could be judged, so the decision is documented and unfalsifiable at once.

**[And the Gap Does Not Announce Itself](plot_and_the_gap_does_not_announce_itself.md)**
Cue: a question from a later decade, at the catalogue. Closes when an absence and a removal have been shown to return the same result, with nothing in the building able to separate them.

**[The Record Improves by Every Check Available](plot_the_record_improves_by_every_check_available.md)**
Cue: the annual report, and a profession honestly asking whether it is doing this well. Closes when every instrument is found to reach only the kept part, so the feedback would look like this whatever the appraisals were.

## Stakes

Whether a decision whose evidence it destroys can be got right, or even known to have been got wrong.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a selection that cannot be declined, a criterion that is professional precisely by being systematic, an estimator that is honest about the wrong population, a loss that removes the terms of its own audit, a gap that reads as an absence, and a feedback loop that runs entirely inside the surviving sample, and away from any account in which an archivist was careless or a criterion was ideological. The production cannot fault the seat for writing its reasons down, because writing them down is what raises the act above throwing things out, and it cannot fault the researcher for asking the questions of their own decade, because those are the only questions anybody has. What is left to fight over is not whether to select but what to call a judgement that is answerable to everything except its own result.
