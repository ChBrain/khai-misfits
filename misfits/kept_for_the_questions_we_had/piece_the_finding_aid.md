# Piece: The Finding Aid

## Taxonomy

[The Reading Room](place_the_reading_room.md). The description that makes a holding reachable, and the cost that makes selection compulsory.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Produced by [describing what is kept](process_describing_what_is_kept.md), and used at [the reading room](place_the_reading_room.md).

## Load Bearing

It is exhaustive about the holdings and structurally silent about everything else, which is what makes a gap unreadable.
A catalogue is a list of what is there. There is no form in which it could list what is not, because the description of a destroyed record is the record. And the same object is the reason everything cannot simply be kept: it costs skilled hours per box, so an undescribed backlog is a more complete archive that answers fewer questions.

## Apparent

A neutral index. The most innocent object in the building.

## Yearbook

Makes the kept part usable, and makes the unkept part indistinguishable from what never existed.
