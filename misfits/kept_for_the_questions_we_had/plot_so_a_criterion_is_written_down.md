## Stage

## Tension

Everything that makes the act professional also makes it systematic, which is the opposite of harmless.
Random loss is unbiased and merely thins a record. A stated criterion, applied consistently by trained people across an institution and a career, removes material in a pattern, and the pattern is the criterion. So the discipline that raises appraisal above throwing things out is exactly what converts an even thinning into a shaped one, and there is no version of doing this responsibly that does not have that property.
