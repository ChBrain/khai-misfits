# Place: The Appraisal Room

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Questions We Had](play_kept_for_the_questions_we_had.md). Where the intake is met and the schedule is applied.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Pallets of accessioned material, a written criterion, a form recording what was kept and on what grounds.
Nothing furtive: the standard is published, the reasoning is filed, and the decision is defensible to anybody who reads it.

## Holds

[The Archivist](persona_the_archivist.md), applying a stated criterion.
[The Schedule](piece_the_schedule.md), which is careful and is of its time.
[The Discard](piece_the_discard.md), briefly, on its way out.

## Offers

A collection that can be described, reached and used, and a written account of how it was arrived at.

## Withheld

Any way to test the criterion against what it excludes.
The place produces one comparison and destroys the other side of it: what is kept can be measured, used and cited forever, and what is not kept leaves the building with nothing recorded about it except that it went. So the room can prove its decisions were reasoned and can never learn whether they were right.
