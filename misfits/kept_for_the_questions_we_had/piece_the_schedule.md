## Load Bearing

It is built from the only evidence about future demand that exists, and that evidence is a photograph of the present.
Use statistics, citation patterns and the questions coming across the desk are real measurements, honestly taken, of what is wanted now. Nothing measures what will be wanted, because the people who will want it are not available to be surveyed. So the schedule is not a guess dressed as a standard: it is the best available estimator of the wrong quantity.
