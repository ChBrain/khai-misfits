# Persona: The Latecomer

## Taxonomy

[Latecomer](position_latecomer.md). The one who arrives with a question the record was not kept for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A question nobody was asking when the schedule was written, and a catalogue that is complete and searchable.

## Action

Searches. Finds little. Concludes that the thing was not much recorded.

## Shadow

Cannot tell a silence from a removal, and neither can anybody else.
The catalogue describes what is held and is accurate about all of it; nothing in it is a list of what is not there. So an absence reads as evidence that the practice was rare, the group was small or the matter was not written down, and the reading that it was appraised away is available only as a suspicion that no source in the building can settle.

## Tell

Draws a sound conclusion from a record that was shaped to answer somebody else.
