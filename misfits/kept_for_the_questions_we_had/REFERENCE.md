# Kept for the Questions We Had: Reference

The research warrant for Kept for the Questions We Had.
The term is the house's; the play runs on archival appraisal and on what a selection destroys besides the
records, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging appraisal as **a judgement answerable to everything except its own result**: more arrives than can ever
be described, so a portion is kept and the rest is destroyed. The choice is made on written criteria, by
trained people, in good faith, from the best evidence anybody has about what will be wanted, which is what is
being asked for now. The record that results is better by every measurement that can be taken of it. The play
models the documented mechanics, the description cost that makes selection compulsory, the written criterion
that makes the act professional and the loss patterned, the demand measurement that is sound and about the
wrong population, the destruction that removes the terms of its own audit, the gap that returns the same result
as an absence, and the feedback that runs entirely inside the surviving sample, as the load-bearing vectors of
the structure, not as a tale of a careless archivist or an ideological schedule.

The governing law is that the criterion is built from the present's questions and applied to the only copy, and
the evidence that would show it was wrong is what the decision destroys.

**The selection is deliberate, stated and defensible, and that is what makes it worse rather than better.**
Random loss is unbiased and merely thins a record. A criterion applied consistently by trained people across an
institution and a career removes material in a pattern, and the pattern is the criterion, so the discipline
that raises appraisal above throwing things out is the same property that converts an even thinning into a
shaped one. There is no version of doing this responsibly without that property.

What removes any possibility of blame from the archivist is that not deciding is not available. An accession
nobody has described cannot be asked for, so a larger undescribed record answers fewer questions than a smaller
described one; selecting at random would be worse than selecting on a reason; and the only reason available is
the demand the present emits. The seat is not choosing between a good criterion and a better one but between
the criterion of its own moment and no criterion at all.

What removes it from the researcher is that the questions are real and asked in good faith, and the record of
their asking is an honest measurement. It is simply a census of one moment's interests, and no instrument
anywhere samples the interests of people who are not yet here to be sampled. Every research turn of the last
century arrived after the appraisals that would have served it.

And the profession's own review closes the case against the last party, because every instrument reaches only
the kept part. Requests are served because what is requested is what is described, satisfaction is high among
people whose questions the collection was selected to answer, and the backlog falls precisely to the degree
that material was removed. None of those measurements is wrong and none can be made about what was destroyed,
so a profession reviewing itself honestly would see what it sees now whether the appraisals were excellent or
catastrophic.

It is distinct from the house's **The Survivors**, which is its nearest neighbour and the misfit it is most
likely to be mistaken for. There a sample is thinned by attrition nobody chose, so the bias follows whatever
made things fragile and the repair is to reason about the missing population, which can be characterised
because the process that removed it can be modelled. Here somebody chooses, by published criteria, in good
faith, and the choosing is the difference: the loss is not correlated with fragility but with a written
statement of what mattered in a particular decade, which is both more systematic and less recoverable, since
there is no attrition process to model and no rate to estimate. Passive loss can be corrected for. A reasoned
selection cannot, because the reason was the mechanism.

It is distinct from the house's **Nothing Known Against It**, where an absence of evidence is read as clearance
because nobody has looked: that is the closest mechanism in the house and the difference is what looking would
do. There the trouble is too little information and the repair is available, since the studies can be run and
the absence filled. Here looking is exhausted at the moment of the search: the material is not unstudied, it is
gone, so no method, technique or budget reaches it. One misfit is about a question nobody has asked yet; this
one is about a question that can never be answered again.

It is distinct from the house's **As Full As I Remember**, where each generation takes what it first
encountered as the natural baseline, so the reference point drifts with the observer: there the evidence
survives and the observer's memory is what moves, and a long series recovers it. Here the observer is not
misremembering anything and the series itself was cut. The two are the same failure from opposite ends, a
memory shortened against a record shortened, and only one of them can be repaired by finding older data.

It is distinct from the house's **Held Still to Keep It**, where inscribing a practice as heritage fixes a
living variability into one authorised form: there the thing preserved survives in a changed state and the
variation is suppressed rather than destroyed, so the loss is legible to anybody comparing the form to its
history. Here nothing is fixed in place and nothing is available for comparison, because what the decision
removes leaves no version of itself behind.

## Origin

The position that refused to select, the criteria that replaced it, the cost that makes selection compulsory,
the warning about whose questions are being served, the argument about whose values decide, and the modern
remedy with its dispute.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the archivist as
  careless or the schedule as ideological, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: not
  selecting is not available, since an undescribed mass answers nothing; selecting at random would be worse
  than selecting on a stated reason; the criterion is written, published and filed with its grounds, which is
  what raises the act above throwing things out; the demand it is built from is measured honestly and there is
  no instrument that could measure the other demand; the researcher whose questions form the census is asking
  real questions in good faith; and the profession's own review reaches only the kept part, so it would look
  like this whatever the appraisals were. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so
  the misfit is that the criterion is made of the present and the evidence against it is what the decision
  destroys.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Keeping everything is the
  obvious answer and is the one the description cost forecloses, since backlog is not a smaller archive but an
  unreachable one. Appraising by function rather than content is the discipline's real remedy, is argued
  carefully and moves the judgement to a level that is easier to state, without removing the judgement or its
  date. Sampling keeps a fraction of a series and preserves the shape while destroying exactly the individual
  cases most later questions turn out to want. Documenting the decision fully is right, is done, and produces a
  record that can be audited as an argument and never as a result. Digital storage removes the shelving
  constraint and leaves the description constraint, which was the binding one. And reappraisal can return to a
  decision only while the material still exists, which is the one condition the decision removes.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the custodial position, the appraisal criteria, the processing
  argument, the warning about research fashion, the case about values and the functional turn are theirs. The
  bounds are carried with them: **whether macroappraisal succeeds is genuinely contested** within the
  profession and the play carries the argument rather than settling it; the claim that description rather than
  storage is the binding constraint is drawn from the processing literature and varies by institution and
  format, so it is stated as the structural reason selection is compulsory and not as a universal ratio; the
  account treats appraisal as one decision taken once on an accession, which is the play's own simplification
  of schedules that operate continuously and of records destroyed long before any archivist sees them; the
  research turns cited as arriving after the appraisals that would have served them are offered as the shape of
  the difficulty and not as a measurement of how much was lost, which is the quantity the mechanism makes
  unmeasurable; and nothing here is an argument about any particular institution or any particular disposal.
  **The play makes no claim that archivists should keep more, or that any appraisal was wrong**, only that the
  criterion is built from the questions of its moment, and that the decision consumes the evidence by which it
  could be judged.
