# Plot: The Discard Is Not Recoverable

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Questions We Had](play_kept_for_the_questions_we_had.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An appraisal signed off, and a lorry at the loading bay.

## Action

[Destroying the rest](process_destroying_the_rest.md): [the archivist](persona_the_archivist.md) authorises the removal of [the discard](piece_the_discard.md) from [the appraisal room](place_the_appraisal_room.md).

## Stage

Space, a workable collection, and a decision that cannot be taken again.

## Tension

What is destroyed is the evidence by which the destruction could be judged, which is a stronger loss than irreversibility.
A demolished building can be photographed and a closed railway has a map, so the thing that is gone leaves a description of itself behind. Here the thing lost is the information, so nothing survives that stands in for it: [the successor](persona_the_successor.md) may read the reasoning, apply a later standard to it, and still hold no term to compare the result against. The appraisal is documented, reviewable as an argument, and permanently unfalsifiable as a result.
