# Persona: The Researcher

## Taxonomy

[Researcher](position_researcher.md). The one whose questions are the only evidence there is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A request slip, a thesis, and a subject that is what people are working on this decade.

## Action

Asks the record what this generation wants to know.
Leaves a trace of having asked, which is the data the schedule is built from.

## Shadow

Asks in good faith and is the entire sample.
Nothing about the questions is fashionable in a way anybody could be blamed for: they are real questions, well posed, and the demand they express is genuine. But demand recorded in a reading room is a census of one moment's interests, and there is no instrument anywhere that samples the interests of people not yet born.

## Tell

Is honestly consulted, and is honestly the wrong population.
