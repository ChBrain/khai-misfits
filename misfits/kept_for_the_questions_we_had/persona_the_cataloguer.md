# Persona: The Cataloguer

## Taxonomy

[Cataloguer](position_cataloguer.md). The one whose hours are the real limit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A backlog measured in shelf miles, and a description rate measured in boxes a week.

## Action

Arranges, describes, and makes a thing findable.
Falls further behind on everything not selected out.

## Shadow

Is the reason keeping everything is not the safe option it sounds like.
Storage is cheap and description is not: an accession nobody has described is not in the archive in any sense a researcher can use, so an undescribed mass is a more complete record that answers fewer questions than a smaller described one. The seat is what converts a choice about space into a choice about knowledge, and no budget yet proposed removes it.

## Tell

Makes the kept part real, and the unkept part is not the only thing that vanishes.
