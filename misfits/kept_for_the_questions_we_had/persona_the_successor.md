# Persona: The Successor

## Taxonomy

[Successor](position_successor.md). The one who inherits the decision and cannot review it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A collection, a file of appraisal reports, and a standard that has moved since they were written.

## Action

Reads what a predecessor kept and why.
Finds the reasoning sound, the paperwork complete, and the evidence gone.

## Shadow

Holds a decision that is fully documented and permanently unauditable.
The reports say what was destroyed and on what grounds, which is more than most professions leave, and it is not enough: reviewing an appraisal would mean comparing what was kept against what was not, and the second term was destroyed by the decision under review. So a later standard can be applied to the reasoning and never to the result.

## Tell

Can audit the argument and never the answer.
