# Place: The Reading Room

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Questions We Had](play_kept_for_the_questions_we_had.md). Where the record is asked, and where a gap looks like an answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A catalogue, a request slip, a table, and a box that arrives or does not.
Good service: the finding aid is accurate about everything it lists, and the staff know the collection.

## Holds

[The Researcher](persona_the_researcher.md), whose asking becomes the criterion.
[The Latecomer](persona_the_latecomer.md), whose question arrives too late to have counted.
[The Finding Aid](piece_the_finding_aid.md), which is complete about the holdings and silent about everything else.

## Offers

An accurate account of what is held, and prompt access to all of it.

## Withheld

Any mark distinguishing what was never there from what was taken away.
The catalogue is a list of holdings and cannot be a list of absences, so a search returning nothing is read as the practice being rare or the matter unrecorded, and the reading that it was appraised out is a suspicion no source in the building can settle. The place is not concealing anything: there is simply no record of a removal, because a record of it would have been the thing itself.
