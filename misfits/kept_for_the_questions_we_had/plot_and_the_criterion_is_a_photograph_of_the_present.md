# Plot: And the Criterion Is a Photograph of the Present

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The evidence base for the schedule: who came, what they asked for, what they cited.

## Action

[Asking the record](process_asking_the_record.md): [the researcher](persona_the_researcher.md), holding [Researcher](position_researcher.md), uses [the reading room](place_the_reading_room.md), and the use is counted.

## Stage

A real measurement of demand, honestly taken.

## Tension

The measurement is sound and is of the wrong population, and no better instrument exists or could.
Use statistics and citation patterns describe what is wanted now, by people who are here to want it. What the schedule needs is what will be wanted, by people who cannot be surveyed because they do not yet exist. Every research turn of the last century, from social history to family history to the histories of people whose paperwork nobody was keeping, arrived after the appraisals that would have served it. So the schedule is the best available estimator of a quantity it cannot observe, and improving the estimator does not change which quantity it is.
