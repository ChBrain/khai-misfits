# Plan: Reach the Other Hand

## Direction

Commit believably to higher future inflation so expected inflation lowers the real rate when the nominal one cannot fall, or turn to fiscal spending to fill the demand the rate cut cannot draw out, or reach for the unconventional tool. Know that each reach spends a real cost, that a bank built to be credibly conservative finds it hard to promise convincingly to be loose, and that the trap is contested, its remedies debated, and a determined bank arguably never truly out of ammunition.
