## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Shown

The lever worked and nothing moving, the dial at its end and the slump unanswered.
The slack string, pulled taut when drawn back, offering no resistance when pushed.

## Holds

[The String](piece_the_string.md).
The lever's impotence at the floor, the tool that can pull an economy back but cannot push it forward, no fault in the hand that holds it.

[The Other Hand](piece_the_other_hand.md).
The credible future inflation, or fiscal action, or unconventional tool, each costly and against the grain of the regime's own habits.

## Offers

To the reformer, a reach past the floor by another hand, when the familiar one has nothing left to turn.

## Withheld

The simple turn of the familiar dial, spent here and no longer useful.
