# Plot: Nothing Moving

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The lever worked, the rate cut, the money injected, and still nothing moving.

## Action

At [the Impasse](place_the_impasse.md), [the Banker](persona_the_banker.md) meets [the String](piece_the_string.md), the tool that can pull an economy back by raising the rate unable to push a frozen one forward by lowering it.

## Stage

This is the harm the whole misfit stages: at the floor, the lever's grip is gone exactly when the slump most needs it pulled. A rate cut can always cool an overheating economy by raising the cost of borrowing, a string that pulls; but at the floor, further cuts cannot lower an already-near-zero cost any further, so the same tool cannot push a frozen economy forward (Hicks; Keynes). This plot is distinct from the Paradox of Thrift: that misfit is a fallacy of composition, individually sound saving summing to a collective shortfall, no tool involved. It is distinct from Debt-Deflation: that misfit is falling prices raising the real burden of debt, a spiral of prices and balance sheets, not a rate pinned at its floor. Here the failure belongs to the lever at the bound, not to the wielder's will or skill. No villain: the loss is authored by no one, since the tool was worked correctly and simply has no purchase left to give.

## Tension

The tool strong to cool, and useless to warm.
