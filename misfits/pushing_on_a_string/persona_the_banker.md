# Persona: The Banker

## Taxonomy

[Banker](position_banker.md), inside [Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The archetype of the central bank in the slump, wielding the one tool it always wields, the rate cut and the money injected, and finding at the floor that the tool has no grip.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A timid banker, or a bold one, either way a matter of nerve. It looks like a question of will, a policymaker who should simply do more, cut harder, print faster, act with courage where a lesser one hesitates, as though the slump would lift if only the hand on the dial were firmer.

## Action

Cut the rate. At [the Bank](place_the_bank.md), the Banker on [Cut the Rate](plan_cut_the_rate.md) through [Cutting](process_cutting.md) works [the Lever](piece_the_lever.md), lowering the rate and injecting money to draw out spending, then meets [the Floor](piece_the_floor.md), the zero bound the rate cannot cross, and at [the Impasse](place_the_impasse.md) meets [the String](piece_the_string.md), the tool that cannot push a frozen economy forward (Keynes; Hicks).

## Shadow

Its impotence is a property of the tool and not a want of nerve. The Banker is not failing in its duty: it wields the lever correctly, and no boldness makes a rate that has reached its floor fall further, nor makes money that cash and bonds have rendered identical pass on into loans and spending. It does exactly what the downturn calls for, and the tool has no purchase at the floor. The failure is the lever's at the bound, not the wielder's of will, though a determined bank may reach by other means (Bernanke).

## Tell

It does the right and usual thing well, and at the floor the right and usual thing does nothing.
