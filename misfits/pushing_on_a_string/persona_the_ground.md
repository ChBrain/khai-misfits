# Persona: The Ground

## Taxonomy

[Ground](position_ground.md), inside [Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The archetype of the economy itself in the deep slump, the frozen ground that calls for stimulus and that, being frozen, has already driven the rate down to its floor.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A passing dip, a downturn like any other, cold now but sure to warm at the usual touch of the usual lever, as though this slump were no different from the ones a rate cut has always lifted, a season that will turn on its own.

## Action

Stay frozen. At [the Street](place_the_street.md), the Ground on [Stay Frozen](plan_stay_frozen.md) through [Freezing](process_freezing.md) becomes [the Slump](piece_the_slump.md), the deep downturn that calls for the cure and in which hoarding feels safe and prices may even fall, the cold economy the lever is most needed to lift (Keynes).

## Shadow

Its depth is the very thing that disarms the cure. The Ground is not a passing dip the usual lever will lift: it is the slump deep enough to have pushed the rate to its floor, and at that floor the lever it calls for has no grip, so the deeper it freezes the more it needs the cure and the less the cure can bite. It does nothing wrong by being cold; it is only the setting, the frozen ground that makes the lever's impotence bite exactly where warmth is most needed.

## Tell

The colder it is, the more it needs the lever, and the colder it is, the less the lever can do.
