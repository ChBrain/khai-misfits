# Plot: By Another Hand

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The rate cut spent, the floor reached, and the slump still unmoved.

## Action

At [the Impasse](place_the_impasse.md), [the Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) from [the Reformer's seat](position_reformer.md) on [Reach the Other Hand](plan_reach_the_other_hand.md) through [Reaching](process_reaching.md) holds [the Other Hand](piece_the_other_hand.md), the credible promise of future inflation or the fiscal action or the unconventional tool that reaches where the rate cut cannot.

## Stage

Where the rate cut has run out of room, this plot stages the remedy and the bound together. The remedy exists: a central bank can try to commit credibly to future inflation, or fiscal spending can step in directly, or unconventional tools can be tried, but each is costly and runs against the grain of the regime's own credibility and its own habits of thrift (Krugman). And the bound itself is not settled doctrine: the trap is a contested regime, not a hard wall or a permanent state, and a sufficiently determined bank is arguably never fully out of ammunition (Bernanke). No villain: the reformer reaches for the other hand not because the banker failed, but because the string alone cannot finish the job, and the finishing is dearly bought.

## Tension

The way out real but dearly bought, and the trap itself contested.
