# Piece: The Hoard

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The mechanism of failure: the injected money willingly held rather than spent or lent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Street](place_the_street.md), carried by [Hoarding](process_hoarding.md), voiced by [the Saver](persona_the_saver.md): cash taken in and simply kept.

## Load Bearing

Once a bond pays no more than cash, the money the bank injects is willingly held rather than lent or spent, absorbed into idle balances as liquidity preference turns absolute and soaks up whatever the bank supplies (Keynes). The holding is sensible, not blameworthy: a bond offers no premium over cash and the future is cold, so keeping the cash is the reasonable choice of the saver at the street, not a hoarder's greed. This is the piece where the lever's push disappears, not because anyone refuses it but because no one has a reason to pass it on.

## Apparent

Hoarding, a miser sitting on money the economy visibly needs.
