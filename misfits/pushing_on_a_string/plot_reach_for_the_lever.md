# Plot: Reach for the Lever

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The slump deep and calling for the cure that has always worked before.

## Action

At [the Bank](place_the_bank.md), [the Banker](persona_the_banker.md) from [the Banker's seat](position_banker.md) on [Cut the Rate](plan_cut_the_rate.md) through [Cutting](process_cutting.md) works [the Lever](piece_the_lever.md), cutting the rate and injecting money to lower borrowing and draw out spending.

## Stage

The tool reached for here is the correct one, the usual one, the sober and textbook response to a downturn: lower the rate, make holding cash less rewarding than lending it, and let cheaper borrowing draw spending back out. This is not overreach or panic, it is the ordinary machinery of monetary policy, wielded well and meant in full (Keynes). Nothing here is done half-heartedly or wrongly; the banker pulls the lever exactly as the office requires. No villain: the reach is competent, the cut is real.

## Tension

The right and usual thing done, and the floor still ahead.
