# Piece: The Other Hand

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The remedy and its bound: escape by a hand other than the usual one, at a real cost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Impasse](place_the_impasse.md), carried by [Reaching](process_reaching.md), voiced by [the Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md): the reach past the dial that will not turn.

## Load Bearing

The escape is by another hand than the usual one, and it costs something real: credible future inflation, so that expected inflation lowers the real rate when the nominal one cannot fall further, though a conservative bank finds such a promise hard to make convincingly; or fiscal spending, filling the demand the rate cut cannot draw out; or unconventional tools, whose power is itself debated (Krugman). Each of these works against the grain of the regime's own credibility and thrift, which is why the reach is costly rather than free.

Alongside the remedy sits the bound on the trap itself: this is a contested regime, not a hard wall or a permanent state, its remedies debated, and a determined bank is arguably never out of ammunition since it can inflate the currency by other means (Bernanke). The floor binds the ordinary lever; it does not bind every tool a determined institution might use.

## Apparent

A reckless spendthrift abandoning the sober rules of ordinary policy.
