# Position: Banker

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The seat of the central bank wielding the conventional lever in the slump, and marking that at the floor the lever's impotence is a property of the tool and not a failure of its nerve.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one tool it always wields, the rate it can cut and the money it can inject to lower the cost of borrowing and draw out spending.
A floor beneath the rate, the zero lower bound it cannot cross, since no one holds a bond that pays less than the cash they could hold instead.
The sober duty to act, the correct and usual response to a downturn, which it means and performs well.

## Orders

Cut the rate, and inject the money the slump calls for.
Lower the rate and supply the money to draw out investment and spending, the sober and correct response to a downturn, wielded well and meant in full.
Know that at the floor the rate falls no further however boldly you act, that the money injected is willingly held once a bond pays no more than cash, and that the lever's impotence there is a property of the tool and the bound and not a want of your nerve.

## Loses

The purchase of its one tool, exactly when the slump most needs it.
Because the rate has reached its floor and, there, cash and bonds are the same, the seat cuts and injects and moves nothing, the lever strong to cool an overheating economy and useless to warm a frozen one, pushing on a string.
It keeps its sober duty and its correct performance and loses no cause for blame, a bank that does the right thing well and finds the right thing does nothing at the floor.

## Drives

Toward the central bank that wields the lever correctly and finds it slack,
cutting the rate and injecting the money the downturn calls for, and meeting the floor where the tool has no grip,
so the seat that does exactly what the slump demands cannot lift it, the failure the lever's at the bound and not the wielder's of will.
