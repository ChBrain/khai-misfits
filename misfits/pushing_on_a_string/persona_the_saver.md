# Persona: The Saver

## Taxonomy

[Saver](position_saver.md), inside [Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The archetype of the holder of money in the slump, the household and the bank and the firm, who takes the injected cash and holds it rather than lends or spends it once a bond pays no more than the cash in hand.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A hoarder, a miser sitting on money the economy needs, someone who could lend or spend and simply will not, clutching the cash out of fear or greed while the slump deepens for want of the very spending they withhold.

## Action

Hold the cash. At [the Street](place_the_street.md), the Saver on [Hold the Cash](plan_hold_the_cash.md) through [Hoarding](process_hoarding.md) holds [the Hoard](piece_the_hoard.md), the injected money willingly kept rather than lent or spent, absorbed into idle balances, absolute liquidity preference taking up whatever the bank supplies (Keynes).

## Shadow

Its hoarding is sensible and not blameworthy. The Saver is not clutching cash against its own interest: when a bond pays no more than the cash it could hold instead, and the cold future makes the cash worth keeping, holding it is simply the rational thing. It is not withholding spending out of fear or greed but responding correctly to a world where money and bonds have become the same, so the injection meant to move it moves nothing, and the fault is not the Saver's for taking the only sensible course.

## Tell

It holds the money because the money and the bond are the same, and holding is the sensible thing.
