# Persona: The Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Reformer](position_reformer.md), inside [Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The archetype of the one who reaches by another hand when the rate cut is spent, the credible promise of future inflation, or the fiscal spending, or the unconventional tool, each reaching where the lever cannot.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A reckless spendthrift, a radical who would abandon the sober rules, promising inflation the prudent bank was built to prevent, or spending public money the careful state was built to husband, courting the very looseness the ordinary regime exists to hold back.

## Action

Reach the other hand. At [the Impasse](place_the_impasse.md), the Reformer on [Reach the Other Hand](plan_reach_the_other_hand.md) through [Reaching](process_reaching.md) holds [the Other Hand](piece_the_other_hand.md), the credible promise of future inflation or the fiscal action or the unconventional tool that reaches where the rate cut cannot, costly and against the grain (Krugman; Bernanke).

## Shadow

Its recklessness is the only reach the floor leaves open, and it is dearly bought. The Reformer is not abandoning the rules for their own sake: the rate cut is spent, so the escape must come from elsewhere, and each way out works against the grain of the very credibility or thrift the ordinary regime prizes. A bank built to be credibly conservative finds it hard to promise convincingly to be loose (Krugman); the fiscal reach spends real money and political capital; the unconventional tool's power is itself debated. The cost is real and the reach is not the familiar dial.

## Tell

It reaches where the lever cannot, and every reach spends the credibility or thrift the regime was built to keep.
