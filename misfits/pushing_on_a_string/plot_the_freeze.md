# Plot: The Freeze

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The economy falling into a deep downturn, spending drying up, prices beginning to sag.

## Action

At [the Street](place_the_street.md), [the Ground](persona_the_ground.md) from [the Ground's seat](position_ground.md) on [Stay Frozen](plan_stay_frozen.md) through [Freezing](process_freezing.md) becomes [the Slump](piece_the_slump.md), the deep downturn that calls for stimulus and in which hoarding feels safe and prices may fall.

## Stage

This is the setting that makes the coming impotence bite. The slump has to be deep enough to drive the rate down toward its floor before the lever can lose its grip at all: a shallow downturn leaves room for ordinary rate cuts to work, so the plot needs the ground genuinely frozen, spending withheld, prices soft or falling, for the trap to become reachable at all (Keynes). Nothing in this freezing is malicious or even unusual: it is the ordinary shape of a bad slump, households and firms pulling back because the future looks cold, before any bank has moved a lever. No villain: the ground simply stays still.

## Tension

The economy frozen, and the usual cure already demanded.
