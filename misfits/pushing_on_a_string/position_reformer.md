# Position: Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The seat of the one who reaches by another hand when the rate cut is spent, and who marks that every reach is costly, against the grain of the regime's own credibility and thrift, and its power debated.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The knowledge that the rate cut is spent, that the lever has met its floor and the escape must come from elsewhere.
Another hand to reach with: the credible promise of future inflation, or the fiscal spending, or the unconventional tool.
A regime built on the very credibility and thrift each reach must spend, so that every escape works against its grain.

## Orders

Reach the other hand, since the lever is spent and the slump unmoved.
Commit believably to higher future inflation so expected inflation lowers the real rate when the nominal one cannot fall, or turn to fiscal spending to fill the demand the rate cut cannot draw out, or reach for the unconventional tool.
Know that each reach spends a real cost, that a bank built to be credibly conservative finds it hard to promise convincingly to be loose, and that the trap is contested, its remedies debated, and a determined bank arguably never truly out of ammunition.

## Loses

The credibility or the thrift each reach must spend, and it spends them knowingly.
Because the escape works against the grain of the regime built to prize them, the seat buys a way out at the price of the promised prudence or the husbanded funds, none of it the simple turn of the familiar dial, each reach costly and its power debated.
It keeps its clear sight of the cost and its reach where the lever cannot go and loses no cause for blame, the self that spends the regime's own virtues to reach past the floor.

## Drives

Toward the one who reaches by another hand when the lever is spent,
committing to future inflation or turning to fiscal action or reaching for the unconventional tool, each against the grain and dearly bought,
so the seat that alone reaches past the floor reaches only by spending the regime's own credibility or thrift, and holds in view that the trap is contested and a determined bank may always have other means.
