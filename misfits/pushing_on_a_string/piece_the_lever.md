# Piece: The Lever

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The usual tool: the rate cut and the money injected to work it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Bank](place_the_bank.md), carried by [Cutting](process_cutting.md), voiced by [the Banker](persona_the_banker.md): the dial turned the way it has always been turned.

## Load Bearing

Lowering the rate and injecting money normally lowers the cost of borrowing and draws out investment and spending, the dial the bank always turns first, the sober and correct response to a downturn (Keynes). The Lever is not wielded badly here: it is the tool applied exactly as it should be, by a banker doing the job right. What happens to it next, at the floor and in the hoard, is not a failure of this piece's own working but a limit that lies beyond it.

## Apparent

The sure cure, the dial that always works if turned far enough.
