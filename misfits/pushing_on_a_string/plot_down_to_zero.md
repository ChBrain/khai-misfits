# Plot: Down to Zero

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The rate falling, cut after cut, toward its floor.

## Action

At [the Bank](place_the_bank.md), [the Banker](persona_the_banker.md) meets [the Floor](piece_the_floor.md), the zero lower bound the rate cannot cross since no one holds a bond paying less than cash.

## Stage

The rate reaches its floor and can fall no further: this is the wall the lever meets, the flat segment where further cuts stop changing anything, because once a bond pays no more than cash, no one need be paid to hold cash instead (Keynes; Hicks). The effective floor sits slightly below exactly zero, not precisely at it, but the shape is the same: a bound the tool cannot be pushed past. No villain here: this is a property of the bound itself, not a failure of the banker's nerve or skill. The lever has been cut correctly and cut all the way; it simply runs out of room.

## Tension

The rate at its floor, and falling no further.
