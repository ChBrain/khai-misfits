# Plan: Hold the Cash

## Taxonomy

## Direction

Take the injected money and keep it rather than lend or spend it, because when a bond pays nothing more than cash and the future looks cold, holding is simply the sensible course. Know that your holding is the mechanism that disarms the lever, that absolute liquidity preference absorbs whatever the bank supplies, and that this is no fault of yours but the rational answer to a world where money and bonds have become the same.
