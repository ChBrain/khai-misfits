# Piece: The Slump

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The setting: the deep downturn that calls for stimulus and drives the rate to its floor.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Street](place_the_street.md), carried by [Freezing](process_freezing.md), voiced by [the Ground](persona_the_ground.md): the cold spreading through ordinary trade, spending stalled before any lever is touched.

## Load Bearing

The Slump is the cold economy in which hoarding feels safe and prices may fall, deep enough that its own depth drives the rate down to the floor where the lever is most needed and least able to move anything (Keynes). It is not a passing dip: its depth is precisely what pushes the rate to the level at which money and bonds stop being distinguishable, setting up the very bind the rest of this misfit works through.

## Apparent

A passing dip, the kind the usual lever always lifts.
