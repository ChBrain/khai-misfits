# Plan: Cut the Rate

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the banker's seat](position_banker.md), inside [Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Banker](position_banker.md)

## Direction

Lower the rate and supply the money to draw out investment and spending, the sober and correct response to a downturn, wielded well and meant in full. Know that at the floor the rate falls no further however boldly you act, that the money injected is willingly held once a bond pays no more than cash, and that the lever's impotence there is a property of the tool and the bound and not a want of your nerve.

## Orders

([The Banker](persona_the_banker.md) works [the Lever](piece_the_lever.md) through [Cutting](process_cutting.md) at [the Bank](place_the_bank.md), cutting the rate and injecting money to draw out spending, and meets [the Floor](piece_the_floor.md) the rate cannot cross.)

## Implementation

([Cutting](process_cutting.md) at [the Bank](place_the_bank.md) works [the Lever](piece_the_lever.md), because lowering the rate and injecting money is the tool the downturn calls for, until [the Floor](piece_the_floor.md) stops the rate falling and [the String](piece_the_string.md) leaves the tool slack.)

## Targets

- [ ] the rate is cut and money injected, the sober and correct response to the slump
- [ ] the tool is understood as the right and usual one, wielded well and meant in full
- [ ] it is seen that at the floor the rate falls no further however boldly the bank acts
- [ ] the impotence is owned as a property of the tool and the bound, not a failure of nerve
