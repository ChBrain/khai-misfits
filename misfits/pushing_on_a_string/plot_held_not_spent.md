# Plot: Held, Not Spent

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The money injected at the floor, arriving in hands that have no reason to move it.

## Action

At [the Street](place_the_street.md), [the Saver](persona_the_saver.md) from [the Saver's seat](position_saver.md) on [Hold the Cash](plan_hold_the_cash.md) through [Hoarding](process_hoarding.md) holds [the Hoard](piece_the_hoard.md), the new money willingly kept rather than lent or spent once a bond pays no more than cash.

## Stage

Once the rate sits at its floor, money and bonds become near-perfect substitutes, and holding the newly injected cash costs nothing against holding a bond instead. The hoarding this plot stages is entirely sensible and not in any way blameworthy: liquidity preference has turned absolute, and the saver is simply responding rationally to a future that offers no reward for parting with cash (Keynes). Whatever the bank supplies, the street can absorb without ever putting it to work. No villain: the saver is not hoarding out of fear or spite, only out of a correct reading of the terms on offer.

## Tension

The injection absorbed, and nothing moving.
