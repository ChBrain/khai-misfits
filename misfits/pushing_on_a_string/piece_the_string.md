# Piece: The String

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The harm: the lever's impotence exactly when it is most needed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Impasse](place_the_impasse.md), met by [the Banker](persona_the_banker.md): the dial turned and turned, nothing on the other end moving.

## Load Bearing

The tool that can pull an overheating economy back by raising the rate cannot push a frozen one forward by lowering it: pushing on a string, an impotence worst exactly in the deep slump that drove the rate down to the floor in the first place (Hicks; Keynes).

This is distinct from three neighboring misfits it is easy to blur into. It is distinct from the Paradox of Thrift, where the engine is the fallacy of composition in saving, many prudent savers each cutting spending and together making a demand shortfall; here the engine is the failure of the monetary lever at the zero bound, the impotence of the very tool meant to cure such a shortfall. The two can stand together in one slump, but this misfit is the tool that cannot bite, not the thrift that made the wound. It is distinct from Debt-Deflation, where a falling price level lifts the real burden of fixed debt in a self-feeding spiral; here no falling price level is required, only a rate already at its floor and money already held, though a falling price level can deepen the trap once it is underway. And it is distinct from a mere failure of nerve: the lever's impotence at the floor is a property of the tool and the bound it has reached, not the banker's timidity or will.

## Apparent

A timid banker who simply will not act.
