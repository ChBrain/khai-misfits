# Place: The Street

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). Where the Ground becomes the Slump and the Saver holds the Hoard. Hosts [The Freeze](plot_the_freeze.md), [Held Not Spent](plot_held_not_spent.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The frozen economy, shuttered orders and idle hands.
The cash sitting in ledgers and pockets, counted, whole, and going nowhere.

## Holds

[The Slump](piece_the_slump.md).
The deep downturn, cold enough to drive the rate to its floor, hoarding felt as the only safe posture, prices maybe falling further still.

[The Hoard](piece_the_hoard.md).
The money the bank injected, willingly held rather than lent or spent, since a bond pays no more than cash and the future gives no reason to part with it.

## Offers

To the holder, the safety of cash when a bond pays no more and tomorrow is cold.

## Withheld

Any return on parting with the cash, the one incentive that would make spending or lending worth the risk.
