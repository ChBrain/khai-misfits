# Position: Saver

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The seat of the holder of money in the slump, household and bank and firm, who holds the injected cash rather than lends or spends it, and who does the sensible thing when a bond pays no more than cash.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The injected money, the new cash the bank supplies hoping it will be lent and spent.
A choice between holding and parting with it, between the cash in hand and a bond or a loan or a purchase.
A cold future and a bond that pays no more than cash, so that holding the money costs nothing and keeping it feels safe.

## Orders

Hold the cash, since a bond pays no more than the cash in your hand.
Take the injected money and keep it rather than lend or spend it, because when a bond pays nothing more than cash and the future looks cold, holding is simply the sensible course.
Know that your holding is the mechanism that disarms the lever, that absolute liquidity preference absorbs whatever the bank supplies, and that this is no fault of yours but the rational answer to a world where money and bonds have become the same.

## Loses

The spending and lending the injection meant to draw out, though the seat loses it blamelessly.
Because a bond pays no more than the cash it holds and the cold future makes the cash worth keeping, the seat holds the money the bank hoped it would part with, so the injection moves nothing and the slump goes unlifted, the cure absorbed into idle balances.
It keeps its sensible judgement and its safe cash and loses no cause for blame, a holder doing the only rational thing when cash and bonds pay the same.

## Drives

Toward the holder of money who keeps rather than parts with it,
taking the injected cash and holding it because a bond pays no more and the future is cold, sensibly and without fault,
so the seat that does the only rational thing absorbs the very cure meant to move it, the injection held not spent and the lever slack.
