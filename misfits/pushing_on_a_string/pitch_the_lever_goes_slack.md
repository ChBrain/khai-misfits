# Pitch: The Lever Goes Slack

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that the rate cut which can always cool an overheating economy cannot warm a frozen one, because at the floor the lever goes slack.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Grave and measured, sober and correct. A central bank doing exactly what it always does in a downturn, cutting the rate and injecting money, the right response soberly made. Nothing here is timidity or a failure of nerve; the tool is wielded well and simply has no grip.

## Undertow

The rate has a floor near zero, because no one holds a bond that pays less than the cash they could hold instead, so once it reaches that floor it falls no further (Keynes; Hicks). And at the floor a bond paying nothing and cash paying nothing are the same, near-perfect substitutes, so the injected money is not passed on into loans and spending but willingly held, absorbed into idle balances, liquidity preference become absolute (Keynes).

## Nerve

That the lever fails exactly when it is most needed, in the deep slump that drove the rate to the floor, so the tool that pulls an overheating economy back by raising the rate cannot push a frozen one forward by lowering it. No one is a villain; it is a property of the tool and the bound, held against its limit, since the trap is contested and a determined bank may always inflate by other means (Bernanke).

## Echo

Pull and the economy comes; push and the string goes slack, the lever strong to cool and useless to warm.
