# Position: Ground

## Taxonomy

[Pushing on a String](play_pushing_on_a_string.md). The seat of the economy itself in the deep slump, the frozen ground that calls for the cure and that, being frozen, has already driven the rate down to its floor.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A deep and cold downturn, the slump that calls for stimulus and in which hoarding feels safe and prices may even fall.
A depth that has already pushed the rate to its floor, the very freeze that leaves the lever nowhere lower to go.
No fault and no will, being only the setting, the ground on which the lever is worked and found slack.

## Orders

Stay frozen, and call for the cure the lever was made to give.
Be the deep downturn that demands stimulus, cold enough that hoarding feels safe and prices may fall, the slump the rate cut is meant to lift.
Know that your very depth has driven the rate to its floor, that at that floor the lever you call for has no grip, so the colder you are the more you need the cure and the less the cure can bite.

## Loses

The lift the cure was meant to give, forfeited by the very depth that demands it.
Because the slump is deep enough to have driven the rate to its floor, the seat calls for a lever that at the floor cannot move it, so the freeze that most needs warming is exactly the freeze the tool cannot warm.
It keeps its blameless cold and its honest call for the cure and loses no cause for blame, the ground that needs the lever most and can be moved by it least.

## Drives

Toward the frozen economy that calls for the cure it disarms,
being the deep slump that demands the lever and that has already driven the rate to the floor where the lever has no grip,
so the seat that most needs warming is the one the tool can least warm, the setting that makes the lever's impotence bite exactly where it hurts.
