# Plot: Nobody Needs a Fence Here

## Taxonomy

[A Fence in a Good Street](play_a_fence_in_a_good_street.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A team where trust is high and infringement is rare, working exactly as intended.

## Action

[The Trust](persona_the_trust.md) does its work on [the good team](place_the_good_team.md): people assume good faith, share readily, and rarely step on each other.

## Stage

Most marking disappears with most infringement, which is the achievement and is real.

## Tension

The rarity is the achievement and the mechanism, from one property.
A place where nobody has to guard anything is worth building and was built on purpose. It is also a place with no comparison cases, so whatever marking survives is not one signal among many: it is the only one of its kind, and it will be read against a background that contains nothing like it. The team cannot have the first without producing the second, and there is no version of the success that leaves the background intact.
