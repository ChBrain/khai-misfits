# Play: A Fence in a Good Street

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

A Fence in a Good Street.
Somebody comes to feel a piece of work is theirs: they built it, they know it, they are the reason it is any good. That feeling is the thing that turns a passenger into a contributor and everybody wants more of it. It also produces a small fence, a naming convention, a request to be asked first, a note left on the file, because somebody who has invested that much has good reason to expect a hand that does not know it. And in a team where trust is high, that fence is the only fence on the street, so it is the thing people see.

## Arc

The bend is this: the fence is made of the caring, so it appears on exactly the people the team most wants.
It runs on an ownership that is genuinely good.
[The Owner](persona_the_owner.md) is not possessive in any ordinary sense. Feeling a piece of work is yours is what produces the extra hour, the second look and the version nobody asked for, and every account of good work has that feeling somewhere in it.
And what ownership does is mark.
[The Marking](persona_the_marking.md) is not a separate impulse bolted on: it is what felt ownership consists of in practice, communicating that this is mine and, unavoidably in the same gesture, that it is not yours. [The Fence](piece_the_fence.md) is small and reasonable, and it is aimed at a real thing.
And the thing it is aimed at is real. [The Encroachment](persona_the_encroachment.md) does not have to have happened yet: a hand that does not know the code, a rewrite over a weekend, a decision taken while you were on leave, and anybody who has been through one of those is right to anticipate the next.
Then [Nobody Needs a Fence Here](plot_nobody_needs_a_fence_here.md).
[The Trust](persona_the_trust.md) is high, so infringement is rarer and marking is rarer with it, which is exactly what the team was built for and is a real achievement. And a rare thing is a visible thing. On [the good team](place_the_good_team.md) a fence is not one signal among many, it is the only one of its kind, and it is read against a background in which nobody expected to see one.
So [the colleagues](persona_the_colleagues.md) mark the owner down as a worse contributor than they would in a team that trusted each other less, and they are reading the room correctly.
And there is no way out that keeps the thing. Taking the fence down means accepting the encroachment it was aimed at, which is real. Caring less removes the fence and removes the contributor with it.

## Triggers

**[The Ones Who Care Do the Marking](plot_the_ones_who_care_do_the_marking.md)**
Cue: a piece of work somebody built, knows, and is the reason is any good. Closes when the feeling of ownership has been shown to produce both the extra hour and the small fence, from one source, so the team cannot select for the first alone.

**[The Encroachment Is Real](plot_the_encroachment_is_real.md)**
Cue: a hand that does not know the work, arriving without warning. Closes when the thing the fence is aimed at has been found to be an ordinary and recurring event rather than a suspicion, so anticipating it is competence rather than paranoia.

**[Nobody Needs a Fence Here](plot_nobody_needs_a_fence_here.md)**
Cue: a team where trust is high and infringement is rare, working as intended. Closes when the rarity has been shown to cut both ways, suppressing most marking and making whatever remains the only thing of its kind on the street.

**[So the Fence Is the Only Thing You See](plot_so_the_fence_is_the_only_thing_you_see.md)**
Cue: the small fence, in a place with no other fences. Closes when it has been found to be read against a background that contains no comparison for it, so its size stops mattering and its presence carries the whole signal.

**[Judged Hardest Where It Was Least Expected](plot_judged_hardest_where_it_was_least_expected.md)**
Cue: the colleagues, forming a view of the owner as a team contributor. Closes when the same behaviour has been marked down further here than it would be in a team that trusted each other less, by people reading the room correctly.

**[And Letting Go Costs the Thing That Made You Good](plot_and_letting_go_costs_the_thing_that_made_you_good.md)**
Cue: the obvious advice, which is to stop fencing. Closes when taking the fence down has been shown to mean accepting the encroachment it was aimed at, and caring less has been shown to remove the contributor along with the fence.

## Stakes

Whether the disposition a team most wants can be had without the behaviour it least tolerates.
Every plot moves the same way: toward an ownership that is a genuine good, a marking that is what that ownership consists of rather than an addition to it, a threat that is real and recurring, a rarity that makes the remaining signal loud, and a judgement made correctly by people reading their own room, and away from any account in which somebody is being territorial in the ordinary bad sense. The production cannot ask the owner to care less, since that is the thing being cultivated, and it cannot ask the colleagues to read the room wrongly. What is left to fight over is not whether the fence was small, everybody agrees it was, but what to call a signal whose meaning is set by the quality of the street it stands in.
