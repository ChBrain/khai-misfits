# A Fence in a Good Street: Reference

The research warrant for A Fence in a Good Street.
The term is the field's; the play runs on organisational territoriality, the psychological ownership it
expresses, and the trust moderation that prices it, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging territoriality as **a behaviour made of the disposition every team is trying to cultivate, priced by
the quality of the team it appears in**: feeling that a piece of work is yours is what produces the extra hour
and the second look, and it is also what produces the note on the file, because marking is what felt ownership
consists of in practice rather than a habit attached to it. The marking says this is mine and, in the same
gesture, that it is not yours. Where trust is high, infringement is rare and marking is rare with it, which is
the achievement; and a rare signal is a loud one, so what survives is read against a background containing
nothing like it. The play models the documented mechanics, the ownership that is a genuine good, the marking
that carries a second meaning it cannot drop, the encroachment that is real and absent at the moment of
judgement, the rarity that cuts both ways, the reading that is accurate, and the remedy that removes the
contributor with the fence, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of somebody being
territorial in the ordinary bad sense.

The governing law is that a signal's meaning is set by its base rate rather than by its size. A note asking to
be consulted is unremarkable where such notes are common and informative where they are not, and the difference
lies in the street rather than in the note, so the usual advice, to soften the wording or reduce the ask,
addresses a dimension nobody is reading. Presence has no smaller version of itself.

What removes any possibility of blame from the owner is that the team asked for this by name. Commitment,
investment and the sense that the work is yours are cultivated deliberately and appear in every appraisal as a
strength, and the marking is the same output seen from the other side, so an instruction to keep the first and
drop the second asks for an input nobody separates. What they are guarding against is real: a hand that does
not know the code, a rewrite over a weekend, a decision taken during leave, and anticipating a recurring event
is competence rather than insecurity.

What removes it from the colleagues is that they are reading their own room correctly. Drawing an inference
from a rare and deliberate signal is what a competent reader does, and the signal really is rare here and
really was deliberate, so the judgement is not a bias to be corrected, not a misunderstanding to be cleared up,
and not something better information would touch. The penalty is not applied by a harsher audience: it is
computed against a higher baseline that the audience built on purpose.

And the remedy reaches the wrong thing twice. Taking the fence down means accepting the encroachment it was
aimed at, which was never the imaginary part. Caring less removes the fence properly and removes the
contributor with it. So the team asks for a quality by name, penalises the only form that quality takes, and
offers as a remedy the removal of what it was asking for.

It is distinct from the house's **A Little Crooked and Mine**, where labour spent building a thing inflates its
value in the builder's own eyes so a fair trade goes unmade: that is the house's incumbent on felt ownership
and already carries the psychological-ownership literature as its explanatory row, so the line matters. There
the ownership inflates a **valuation** and the casualty is a transaction with an outside party who prices the
object correctly. Here the ownership produces a **behaviour**, and the casualty is the owner's standing among
people who are not trading with them at all. Different output, different injured party, and no price is being
misjudged by anybody.

It is distinct from the house's **The Front**, where each shows the resolved version and edits the raw away, so
the room holds only the finished thing: there the actor is managing an impression, staging what is shown and
withholding the rest, and the audience is the point of the exercise. Here the actor is not performing at all.
The marking is functional, aimed at an object rather than at anybody watching, and the adverse reading is a
side effect of a gesture that was never addressed to the people who receive it.

It is distinct from the house's **Not Invented Here**, where a good idea from outside is spurned for being
outside and the group builds a worse one of its own: there the boundary is around a group and the mechanism
runs on origin, so what is rejected is an input and the loss is the better idea. Here the boundary is around
one person's felt possession of a specific object, nothing is rejected on grounds of where it came from, and
the loss is not a worse artefact but a reading of the person who guarded a good one.

It is distinct from the house's **The Bus Factor**, where knowledge concentrates in one head so the work stops
when that head is unavailable: there the difficulty is the concentration itself and the remedy is to spread it.
Here spreading the knowledge is not resisted and would not help, because what is marked is not the knowledge
but the object, and an owner who documented everything perfectly would still leave the note asking to be
consulted first.

It is distinct from the house's **Saving Face**, where a courteous team keeps every mistake unspoken so it
fixes what it can name and never learns what it cannot say: there the trust and courtesy suppress a signal that
should have been sent, and the loss is information. Here the trust suppresses a behaviour rather than a
disclosure, nothing goes unsaid, and the loss falls on the one person whose signal was not suppressed.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the owner as
  possessive and the colleagues as unfair, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: felt
  ownership is asked for by name, appears in appraisals as a strength, and is the reliable source of work being
  better than it had to be; the marking is what that ownership consists of in practice, so the two cannot be
  selected apart; the encroachment it guards against is real, recurring and nobody's fault; the trust that
  makes marking rare is a genuine achievement and is the same property that makes a remaining signal loud; and
  the colleagues draw a correct inference from a signal that really is unusual here and really was deliberate.
  The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a signal's meaning is
  set by the quality of the place it appears in.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Softening the fence addresses
  a dimension nobody is reading, since what is legible is its presence and presence has no smaller version.
  Taking it down means accepting the encroachment it was aimed at. Caring less removes the fence properly and
  removes the contributor with it, because the disposition and the behaviour are one output. And explaining the
  fence to the colleagues asks them to weigh an event that sits in the past or the future against a note that
  is in the room, which is not a comparison the moment of judgement makes available.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the definition of territoriality, the theory of psychological
  ownership, the infringement responses and the trust moderation are theirs. The bounds are carried with them:
  the trust moderation is a **specific finding** rather than a general law and is stated as one, resting on
  field and survey evidence in particular organisational settings rather than on a broad experimental base; the
  literature is largely self-report and cross-sectional, so the play stages a documented tendency and not a
  measured constant, and the direction of causation between ownership, marking and reputation is not
  established by these designs; the boundary between psychological ownership and territoriality is itself
  argued over in the reviews, with some treating them as one construct, which the play carries rather than
  settles; and the base-rate reading, that a signal's meaning is set by its rarity, is the play's own framing
  of why the moderation runs the way it does rather than a claim any of these papers makes in those terms.
  **The play makes no claim that territorial behaviour is generally harmless or generally benign**, only that
  the form it takes here is made of the disposition the team asked for, and that it is priced hardest exactly
  where the team succeeded most.
