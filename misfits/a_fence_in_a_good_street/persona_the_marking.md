# Persona: The Marking

## Taxonomy

[Marking](position_marking.md). The behaviour, small, reasonable and unavoidably two-sided.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A naming convention, a request to be asked first, a note left on the file.
Nothing anybody would call an incident.

## Action

Says this is mine.
Says, in the same breath, that it is not yours.

## Shadow

Carries the second half whether or not it is meant.
There is no version of a claim that is only a claim: marking a thing as one person's marks it as not another's, so a gesture aimed entirely at protecting the work arrives at a colleague as a statement about them, and no amount of care in the wording removes the structure.

## Tell

Is aimed at an object and lands on a person.
