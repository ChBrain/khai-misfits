# Process: Reading the Fence

## Taxonomy

[A Fence in a Good Street](play_a_fence_in_a_good_street.md). The judgement, made correctly and against the team's own baseline.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A colleague picking up the work, and a note asking to be consulted first.
[The Colleagues](persona_the_colleagues.md), holding [Colleagues](position_colleagues.md), at [the handover](place_the_handover.md).

## Direction

Toward an inference about a person, drawn from a rare and deliberate signal.
Toward a view of the owner as a team contributor.

## Lever

[The Expectation](piece_the_expectation.md), which the team built on purpose and which is the baseline the fence is scored against.

## Echo

A markdown larger than the same behaviour would earn in a team that trusted each other less, delivered by people who are not wrong.
