# Persona: The Encroachment

## Taxonomy

[Encroachment](position_encroachment.md). The real thing the fence is for.

## Owner

## Projection

A hand that does not know the code, a rewrite over a weekend, a decision taken while you were on leave.
Ordinary, recurring, and nobody's fault either.

## Action

## Shadow

Is what makes the marking competence rather than paranoia.
Anticipating a recurring event is what an experienced person does, and the fence is proportionate to a documented risk: so any reading that makes the owner merely insecure has to first pretend the thing they are guarding against does not occur, which it does.

## Tell
