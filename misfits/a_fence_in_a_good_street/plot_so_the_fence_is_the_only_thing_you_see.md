# Plot: So the Fence Is the Only Thing You See

## Taxonomy

[A Fence in a Good Street](play_a_fence_in_a_good_street.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The small fence, in a place with no other fences.

## Action

[The Fence](piece_the_fence.md) is found at [the handover](place_the_handover.md) by a colleague who came to help.

## Stage

Its size stops carrying information. What remains legible is that somebody here thought one was needed.

## Tension

Making it smaller does not reach the thing being read.
A note asking to be consulted is unremarkable where such notes are common and is informative where they are not, and the difference is in the street rather than in the note. So the usual advice, to soften the wording or reduce the ask, addresses a dimension nobody is reading: what is legible is the presence of a marking in a place that has none, and presence has no smaller version of itself.
