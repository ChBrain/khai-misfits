# Plot: And Letting Go Costs the Thing That Made You Good

## Taxonomy

[A Fence in a Good Street](play_a_fence_in_a_good_street.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The obvious advice, offered kindly: stop fencing.

## Action

[Letting go](process_letting_go.md): the note comes off the file, and the owner stops asking to be consulted.

## Stage

The colleague reads as easy to work with. The weekend rewrite still happens, and there is less reason to spend the extra hour.

## Tension

Both halves of the advice reach the same thing.
Taking the fence down means accepting the encroachment it was aimed at, which is real and recurring and was never the imaginary part. Caring less removes the fence properly, and removes the contributor with it, because the disposition and the behaviour are one output. So the team is left asking for a quality by name, penalising the only form that quality takes, and offering as a remedy the removal of what it was asking for, with nobody in the sequence having done anything wrong.
