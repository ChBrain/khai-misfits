## Stage

## Tension

Anticipating a recurring event is competence, which is what makes the fence correct.
Any reading in which the owner is merely insecure has to begin by pretending the thing they are guarding against does not occur, and it does, to them, more than once. So the note on the file is proportionate to a documented risk, and the case for it is complete. What the case is not is present: the encroachment sits in the past or the future, and the fence sits in the room, so at the moment of judgement only one of the two is available to look at.
