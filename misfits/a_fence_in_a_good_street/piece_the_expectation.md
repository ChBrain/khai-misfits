## Place

## Load Bearing

It is what converts a small gesture into a large signal, and it is made of the achievement rather than of anybody's severity.
Where infringement is rare, guarding against it reads as a claim about the people around you, so the same note that is unremarkable in a suspicious team is informative in a trusting one: the penalty is not applied by a harsher audience, it is computed against a higher baseline that the audience built on purpose.

## Apparent

Good culture, which it is.

## Yearbook

Cannot be lowered without lowering the thing it is made of.
