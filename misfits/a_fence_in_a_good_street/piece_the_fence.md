# Piece: The Fence

## Taxonomy

[The Handover](place_the_handover.md). The marking itself, small and proportionate.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Left on the file, and found at [the handover](place_the_handover.md) by whoever came to pick the work up.

## Load Bearing

It is proportionate to a real risk and its size stops mattering the moment it is the only one.
A note asking to be consulted is a reasonable response to a documented recurring event, and in a place where such notes are common it would be read as one: here there is nothing to compare it against, so what carries the signal is not how big the fence is but that a fence exists at all, and no amount of making it smaller reaches that.

## Apparent

A line in a readme. A convention. A request, politely worded.
The least aggressive thing anybody did that week.

## Yearbook

Is read for its presence rather than its size, wherever fences are rare.
Cannot be softened into invisibility, since the softening does not change the fact of it.
