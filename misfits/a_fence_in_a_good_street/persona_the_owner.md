# Persona: The Owner

## Taxonomy

[Owner](position_owner.md). The party the team most wants, producing the behaviour it least wants.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A piece of work they built, know, and are the reason is any good.
The extra hour, the second look, the version nobody asked for.

## Action

Cares.
Marks, because caring is what marks.

## Shadow

Cannot separate the two, and neither can anybody else.
Felt ownership is not a disposition with a fencing habit attached: the marking is what the ownership consists of in practice, communicating that this is mine and in the same gesture that it is not yours, so a team selecting for the first is selecting for the second and has no instrument that takes one.

## Tell

Is the reason the work is good and the reason there is a fence on it.
