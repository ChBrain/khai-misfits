## Load Bearing

Without it the play has a culprit and stops being a misfit.
It is load bearing because **the cut is endorsed by the same economics that makes it harmful**: with
waiting time in the cost, fewer riders genuinely do justify fewer departures, so the step that carries
the entire harm is the step nobody would think to question and nobody could be criticised for.

## Apparent

That an operator with public duties should have held the line.
An operator required to cover its costs cannot, and one that is not so required is already the subsidy
remedy under another name.

## Yearbook

the sequence has no villain in it anywhere.
