## Cue

## Action

## Stage

[The Widened Road](place_the_widened_road.md): fresh surface, new signage, and a journey time board reading lower.

## Tension

The play has to establish first that the saving is real, because everything after it depends on there
being no error here.
The corridor was measured and was genuinely over capacity. The case was built by the published method,
with the assumptions stated and the sensitivity tests run. The lane was built to standard and inside the
estimate. On the first morning the journey is quicker by exactly the minutes the appraisal promised.
Nothing in this plot is disputed anywhere in the production.
And one line in the assumptions will be undone by the scheme itself. **Mode share is an input here and an
outcome out there**, held fixed because that is the convention and because the trains belong to another
organisation, so a document that speculated about their timetable would be overstepping its remit.
There is no error to find. There is a boundary, drawn where the method draws it, with the mechanism on
the other side of it.
