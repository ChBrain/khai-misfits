# Piece: Frequency Is Bought by Riders

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). Ridership is an input to the service and not only a payment for it, so losing riders makes the service slower.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the platform](place_the_platform.md), on the departure board.
In the scale economies that appear once waiting time is counted as part of a journey.

## Load Bearing

Without it a mode shift merely moves people and the play has no harm in it.
It is load bearing because **the wait is a share of the trip**: more riders justify more departures and
more departures shorten everybody's wait, so the mechanism runs in reverse just as readily and a service
that loses passengers does not get smaller, it gets slower.

## Apparent

That the operator could simply hold the frequency and wait for the riders to come back.
It could, at a loss, for as long as somebody funds the loss, which is the remedy and not a correction.

## Yearbook

The finding that urban public transport has substantial scale economies once users' waiting time is in
the cost function, and that unsubsidised frequency therefore sits below the optimum.
