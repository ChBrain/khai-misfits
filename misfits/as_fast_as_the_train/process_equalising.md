# Process: Equalising

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). The road refilling until driving is no better than the alternative.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Any gap at all between the two door-to-door times.
At [the equilibrium seat](position_equilibrium.md), continuously, with nobody running it.

## Direction

Toward one number where there were two.
This is not a decision and has no author: it is what a population of people each taking the quicker
option produces, and it would happen just as reliably if every one of them were a stranger to the others.

## Lever

None that anybody holds.
The process has no participants to consult, no document, and no owner, which is why it survives every
change of policy on either side of it.

## Echo

A road at the speed of the alternative,
an alternative slower than it was before the scheme,
and a corridor whose journey time is worse than the one that justified the work.
