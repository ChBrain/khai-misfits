# Piece: It Is Not the Extra Trips

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). The harm comes from a shift between modes at constant travel, not from journeys the capacity induced.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the widened road](place_the_widened_road.md), in the composition of the traffic that fills it.
Wherever induced demand is offered as the whole explanation of a road that got slower.

## Load Bearing

Without it this collapses into the house's **Jevons Paradox** and earns no separate staging.
It is load bearing because **the switch-off runs cleanly**: stipulate that the number of journeys is
fixed and that not one is induced, and the harm survives entire, because what damages the corridor is
where the existing travellers go and what their leaving does to the service they left.

## Apparent

That induced demand and this are two names for one thing.
They coexist and they compound, and they are separable: induced demand needs new trips, this needs a
competing service with scale economies, and either can be stipulated away without touching the other.

support it leans on, since its authors report no effect of transit provision on vehicle travel.
