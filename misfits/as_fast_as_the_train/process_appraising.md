# Process: Appraising

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). Turning a proposal into a number a committee can approve.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A scheme that needs a case, and a method that says how to build one.
At [the appraisal](place_the_appraisal.md), before anything is built.

## Direction

Toward a defensible benefit-cost ratio.
The method is published, the assumptions are stated, the sensitivity tests are run, and the practitioner
following it is doing the job correctly and could not be faulted on any line of it.

## Lever

The stated assumptions, of which mode share is one.
Holding it fixed is the convention rather than an oversight, and freeing it means modelling another
organisation's commercial response, which is a larger exercise and a weaker number.

## Echo

An approved scheme,
a promised saving that is delivered,
and an assumption that the scheme's own success will falsify.
