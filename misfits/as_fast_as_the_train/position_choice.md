# Position: Choice

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). The seat where one person, this morning, picks a way to work.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Two options and a clock.
A door-to-door time for each, including the walk and the wait.
No obligation to anybody else's timetable.

## Orders

Take the quicker way.
Reconsider when it stops being quicker.
Owe nothing to the option not taken.

## Loses

Any view of what its own switch does.
The seat is **correct and infinitesimal**: one commuter moving changes nothing, the change is the sum of
them, and no individual occupant of this seat is deciding the aggregate any more than a single voter
decides an election.

## Drives

Toward the fastest journey available this morning,
which is the only thing this seat was ever asked to do,
so the seat that carries the mechanism is the one seat that cannot see it.
