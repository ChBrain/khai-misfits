# Place: The Appraisal

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). The document that turned a proposal into a scheme: honest, conventional, and bounded.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A benefit-cost ratio, a time saving in millions of hours, a discount rate.
A stated assumption that the mode shares are as observed.
Sensitivity tests, on demand growth and on cost, and none on the alternative's frequency.

## Holds

The whole of the error, and none of it is an error.
**The one input the scheme itself will change is the one input held fixed**, which is not negligence but
convention: mode share is treated as a characteristic of the corridor, and there is nothing in the method
that makes it a variable.

## Offers

A defensible case, and a number a committee can approve.

## Withheld

Any account of another organisation's response.
The trains belong to somebody else, and a document that speculated about their timetable would be
overstepping.
