# Persona: The Timetable

## Taxonomy

[Service](position_service.md). The published frequency, matched to the riders who are actually there.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Responsive, costed, and defensible line by line.
Reads the farebox and adjusts, which is what an operator is supposed to do.
Would not survive a week of running trains nobody is on.

## Action

Takes two departures an hour out.
Publishes the change with notice.
Balances the account.

## Shadow

That it is part of the journey rather than a schedule for it.
**The wait is not a delay before the trip, it is a share of the trip**, so cutting the frequency does not
make the service smaller, it makes it slower, and the speed it lowers is the one the road is keyed to.

## Tell

Is the party that responded correctly to a real fall in demand.
Is the party through which the whole harm passes.
