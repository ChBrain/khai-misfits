# Persona: The Commuter

## Taxonomy

[Choice](position_choice.md). One person with two ways to work and a preference for the faster one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, rational, and owed nothing to anybody.
Counts the whole journey: the walk, the wait, the ride, the park.
Switches when the sum changes, and switches back when it changes again.

## Action

Takes the quicker way.
Repeats it, with everybody else, every morning.
Notices months later that both ways are slower.

## Shadow

That the switch is right and the switching is the mechanism.
**One commuter moving changes nothing**, which is true of each of them and false of all of them, so
nobody in this seat is deciding the aggregate and the aggregate is nothing but them.

## Tell

Is the party doing the sensible thing.
Is the party the sensible thing is done to.
