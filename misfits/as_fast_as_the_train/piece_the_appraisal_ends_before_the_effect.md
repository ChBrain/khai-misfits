# Piece: The Appraisal Ends Before the Effect

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). The case holds the mode shares fixed, and the scheme's own success is what changes them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the appraisal](place_the_appraisal.md), in the stated assumptions.
In the sensitivity tests, which cover demand growth and cost and not the alternative's frequency.

## Load Bearing

Without it somebody made a forecasting error and the play is about competence.
It is load bearing because **nothing in the method makes mode share a variable**: it is treated as a
characteristic of the corridor, holding it fixed is the convention rather than an oversight, and freeing
it would mean modelling another organisation's commercial response, which is a larger exercise returning
a weaker number.

## Apparent

That a better appraisal would have caught it.
A better appraisal would have reported a smaller benefit and a wider uncertainty, to a committee choosing
between schemes appraised the ordinary way.

## Yearbook

The convention of fixed mode shares in scheme appraisal, and the fact that it is written down rather than
assumed quietly.
