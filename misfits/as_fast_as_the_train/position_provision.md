# Position: Provision

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). The seat that adds capacity to the road, on an appraisal that says it is worth it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A congested link, a design standard, and a budget.
An appraisal method with a benefit-cost ratio and a set of assumptions.
Ownership of the carriageway and of nothing else in the journey.

## Orders

Relieve the congestion that was measured.
Build to standard and inside the estimate.
Deliver the time saving the case was approved on, which it does.

## Loses

Any purchase on the thing that will undo it.
The trains are **another organisation's asset** and the mode shares are an input to the appraisal rather
than an outcome of it, so this seat can neither foresee nor influence the response that takes the saving
back, and would exceed its remit if it tried.

## Drives

Toward a wider road and a faster morning,
which it achieves, exactly as promised, on the day it opens,
so the seat that bought the saving is the seat with no standing over what happens to it.
