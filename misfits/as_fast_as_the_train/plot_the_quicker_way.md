## Action

## Stage

[The Widened Road](place_the_widened_road.md): traffic that is lighter this week than it will be next month.

## Tension

Here the mechanism starts and there is nothing in it to object to.
The commuter counts the whole journey, the walk and the wait and the ride and the park, finds that
driving is now shorter, and drives. The comparison is accurate. The switch is correct on the day it is
made. Anybody advising that person would advise the same, and nobody is owed continued custom by a train
they have stopped needing.
**One commuter moving changes nothing.** That is true of each of them and false of all of them, which is
the whole difficulty: the aggregate is nothing but these decisions, and no occupant of this seat is in a
position to see it, any more than a single voter decides an election.
And the switching does not stop when the road is as quick as the train. It stops when the road is **no
quicker**, because that is when the reason to switch runs out, so the corridor is on its way back to
where it started before anybody has noticed anything has happened.
