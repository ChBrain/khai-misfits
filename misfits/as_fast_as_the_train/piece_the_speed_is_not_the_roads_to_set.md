# Piece: The Speed Is Not the Road's to Set

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). The equilibrium road speed is set by the door-to-door speed of the competing service.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the equilibrium seat](position_equilibrium.md), which nobody occupies.
Wherever two modes serve one corridor and travellers may move between them.

## Load Bearing

Without it a widening simply delivers minutes and keeps them.
It is load bearing because **any advantage the road holds recruits drivers until it is gone**, so the
corridor cannot settle above the alternative's speed however much capacity is added, and the only way to
lower the settling point is to raise the alternative.

## Apparent

That enough capacity would eventually outrun the switching.
Enough capacity would, and the quantity of it is what the argument is actually about; the paradox is that
the amount that gets built relocates travellers instead.

## Yearbook

Thomson's proposition, and Downs's earlier account of peak congestion re-establishing itself after
capacity is added by travellers converging from other times, other routes and other modes.
