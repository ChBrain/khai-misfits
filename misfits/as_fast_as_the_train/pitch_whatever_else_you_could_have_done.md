# Pitch: Whatever Else You Could Have Done

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Precise about what capacity buys, and about how long it keeps it.
Unwilling to call the timetable cut a mistake, because with waiting in the cost it is the efficient answer.
Quoting the journey time wherever the journey time is the point.

## Undertow

Under the scheme, one relation from equilibrium and one from operations.
Any advantage the road holds recruits drivers until it is gone.
And the thing it is measured against gets slower as they arrive.

## Nerve

The unease of a saving that is real, delivered, and spent on something else.
Nobody miscalculated. Nobody cut the service. The service followed the riders.
And the riders followed the road.

## Echo

After the run: an appraisal whose arithmetic still holds,
a platform with a longer wait than it had before the scheme,
a road at the speed of a train that is now slower,
and a seat at the table for everybody except the equilibrium.
