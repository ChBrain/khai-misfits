# Process: Widening

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). Building the lane that the congestion measurement said was needed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A corridor over capacity, and a case that clears the threshold.
At [the appraisal](place_the_appraisal.md), two or three years before the first morning.

## Direction

Toward a faster journey on a link that is demonstrably too small.
This is done well and is done by people who know what they are doing: to standard, inside the estimate,
and delivering the saving the case was approved on.

## Lever

Carriageway, which is the only thing this process owns.
There is no lever here for the alternative at all, because the trains are another organisation's asset
and this process has no standing to touch them.

## Echo

A real time saving, on the first morning,
a corridor that is quicker than the train for the first time in years,
and a reason for several thousand people to reconsider.
