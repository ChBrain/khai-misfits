# Persona: The Subsidy

## Taxonomy

[Recourse](position_recourse.md). The payment that holds a frequency the farebox no longer supports.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Correct, well-founded, and permanently unpopular.
Follows directly from scale economies: unsubsidised frequency sits below the optimum.
Is a line in a budget that has to be defended every year.

## Action

Keeps the departures on.
Keeps the wait short.
Keeps the road's benchmark where it was.

## Shadow

That everything it buys is something that does not happen.
**A decline prevented has no photograph**, so the cost is itemised annually and the benefit is a
counterfactual, which is the weakest position from which to hold a budget line.

## Tell

Is the remedy the economics implies.
Is the remedy that looks like money spent on empty seats.
