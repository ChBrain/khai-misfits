# Position: Service

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). The seat that decides how often the train runs, from what the farebox says.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Rolling stock, crew rosters, and a cost that has to be covered.
A demand figure that has just fallen.
A published timetable that everybody plans around.

## Orders

Match the service to the demand.
Cover the cost, because the seat is required to.
Do not run empty trains, which nobody would defend.

## Loses

The ability to hold a frequency it can no longer fill.
The cut is **efficient and not a retreat**: with waiting time counted in the cost of a journey, fewer
riders genuinely do justify fewer departures, so the response that carries the harm is the response the
economics endorses.

## Drives

Toward a timetable that fits the riders it has,
which is exactly what an operator is for,
so the seat that lowers the alternative's speed is doing the correct thing at every step.
