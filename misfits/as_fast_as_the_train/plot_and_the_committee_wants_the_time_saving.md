## Tension

Each escape gives back the thing it was for.
Subsidise the frequency, which is exactly what the scale economies imply and is not a distortion, and you
have taken on a permanent budget line whose entire benefit is **a decline that does not happen**, defended
annually against costs that are itemised.
Give the new lane to the buses, and you have built a different scheme and forfeited the saving the
committee approved, having spent the money for it.
Price the road, which reaches the equilibrium directly and is the oldest answer in the subject, and you
have proposed the one measure that is hardest to get agreed by the people who would pay it.
Appraise with the mode shares free instead of fixed, which is correct, and you have spent more to return
a **smaller** number to a committee comparing it against schemes appraised the conventional way.
And [It Is Not the Extra Trips](piece_it_is_not_the_extra_trips.md), so the remedies aimed at induced
demand do not reach this: stipulate that no journey is induced and the corridor still ends up slower,
because the damage was done by where the existing travellers went and by what their leaving did to the
service they left.
