# Persona: The Journey Time

## Taxonomy

[Equilibrium](position_equilibrium.md). The quantity that equalises across both modes, and belongs to neither.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Impersonal, patient, and completely reliable.
Does not care which mode is improved or by how much.
Settles wherever the better of the two options happens to sit.

## Action

Equalises.
Waits.
Equalises again after the next scheme.

## Shadow

That it can be moved down only by moving both, and is routinely moved up by moving one.
**Improving the road relocates travellers and improving the alternative is what would lower this
number**, so a city that spends on one mode at a time is spending on the distribution rather than on the
level.

## Tell

Is the number that decides the outcome.
Is the number no report contains.
