# Process: Switching

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). Several thousand people separately deciding to drive instead.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A door-to-door comparison that has changed sign.
At [the widened road](place_the_widened_road.md), over the weeks after opening.

## Direction

Toward the quicker journey.
Each decision is correct on the morning it is taken, is taken on accurate information, and would be taken
the same way by anybody advising the person taking it.

## Lever

The time difference, which is the only input this process reads.
Nobody here is weighing the aggregate, because the aggregate is not visible from a seat that contains one
commuter and one clock.

## Echo

A road filling up,
a farebox falling,
and no individual decision anybody would ask to have back.
