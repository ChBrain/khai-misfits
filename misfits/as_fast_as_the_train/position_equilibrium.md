# Position: Equilibrium

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). The seat where the two journey times are equalised, and which nobody occupies.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Both door-to-door times, and the only quantity that decides the outcome.
No budget, no staff, no remit and no address.
A result that arrives whatever anybody intends.

## Orders

Equalise.
Keep equalising, after every scheme, every timetable and every fare.
Answer to nobody, because nobody is asking.

## Loses

Nothing, because it was never anybody's to lose, which is the point.
**This seat is unoccupied by construction.** The authority owns the road, the operator owns the
timetable, the commuter owns the choice, and the equalised time is a property of all three together that
none of them appraises, reports on or is accountable for.

## Drives

Toward one number where there were two,
arrived at by nobody and binding on everybody,
so the seat that determines the answer is the seat with no chair in the room.
