# Plot: Worse Than Before

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The road, refilling, and an alternative that is slower than it was.

## Action

[Equalising](process_equalising.md): [The Journey Time](persona_the_journey_time.md), in [the equilibrium seat](position_equilibrium.md), works through [Equalise the Two Times](plan_equalise_the_two_times.md).

## Stage

[The Widened Road](place_the_widened_road.md): a journey time board reading higher than it did before the scheme.

## Tension

The equilibrium re-establishes itself, which it always does, and it re-establishes against a benchmark
that has moved the wrong way.
The road settles where driving is no better than the alternative. The alternative now includes a longer
wait. So the settling point is **below where it was before the lane was added**, and the corridor is
slower than the one whose congestion justified the work.
Nothing in the sequence would be undone by anybody who took part in it. The authority would build the
lane again. The commuters would switch again. The operator would cut again, and would be right to.
And the seat where this is decided has nobody in it. **The authority owns the road, the operator owns the
timetable, the commuter owns the choice, and the equalised journey time is a property of all three that
none of them appraises, reports or answers for.** There is no meeting at which it is on the agenda,
because there is no body it belongs to.
