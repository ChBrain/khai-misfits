# Process: Thinning

## Taxonomy

[As Fast as the Train](play_as_fast_as_the_train.md). Matching the timetable to the riders who are left.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A revenue line that no longer covers the service.
At [the platform](place_the_platform.md), at the next timetable change.

## Direction

Toward a frequency the demand supports.
With waiting time counted in the cost of a journey, this is not a retreat but the efficient response, and
an operator that ran the old frequency into an empty carriage would be criticised for it.

## Lever

Departures, which are the operator's only adjustable cost of any size.
There is no lever for the road, which is where the demand went, and no mechanism by which the operator
could be compensated by whoever took it.

## Echo

Two fewer trains an hour,
a longer wait that is part of the journey and not a delay before it,
and a slower alternative for the road to be measured against.
