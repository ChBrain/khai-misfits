## Action

[Reconciling by purpose](process_reconciling_by_purpose.md): [the court of justice](persona_the_court_of_justice.md), holding [Court of Justice](position_court_of_justice.md), declines to privilege a language and decides by purpose and general scheme.

## Stage

## Tension

Neither alternative is available, so the method is the only one and it moves the answer out of the words.
Preferring one language would make the rest secondary and end the arrangement. Counting versions would hand the meaning to whichever languages happen to be numerous, which is arbitrary in a different direction. Purpose and scheme are what remain, they keep the order uniform, and they are a sound and long-established method. What they are not is a reading of anything anybody was given.
