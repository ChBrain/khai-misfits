# Plot: So the One Written for You Is Authentic and Not Enough

## Taxonomy

[Read It in All of Them](play_read_it_in_all_of_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A reader who obtained the official text, read it, and complied with it.

## Action

[Reading the one you have](process_reading_the_one_you_have.md): [the reader](persona_the_reader.md), holding [Reader](position_reader.md), acts on an authentic version and finds the question settled elsewhere.

## Stage

Compliance that is complete as conduct and, by the stated method, incomplete.

## Tension

The version addressed to this reader is the law and reading only it is the wrong method, and both of those are true at once.
The doctrine never says the version is unofficial or that the reading was careless, and it does not need to. It says that no single version may be relied on in isolation, and the seat has one language. So a person who did the only available thing, correctly, is told that correctness required something else, and there is no version of that seat which could have done better without being a different seat.
