# Play: Read It in All of Them

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Read It in All of Them.
A rule is published simultaneously in every official language and each version is equally authentic, because a person should be bound by a text in a language they read. Languages do not carve the world the same way, so competent translation leaves versions that differ. Uniform application then makes it impossible to consider one version in isolation, and that duty reaches national courts too, so the ordinary act of reading the law in your own language becomes an incomplete method.

## Arc

The bend is this: the provision that guarantees you a text in your own language is the provision that removes any text able to settle a question by itself.
It begins with a rule that is plainly right.
[Every Version Is Authentic, and That Is Right](plot_every_version_is_authentic_and_that_is_right.md). [The Equal Authenticity](piece_the_equal_authenticity.md) stops anybody being bound through a translation of the law rather than through the law.
And competent work still leaves a difference.
[And Competent Translation Still Diverges](plot_and_competent_translation_still_diverges.md). [The Divergence](piece_the_divergence.md) is the distance between two languages rather than a slip, and it is there on the morning of publication.
So no version may be read alone.
[So No Version May Be Read Alone](plot_so_no_version_may_be_read_alone.md). [The Comparison Duty](piece_the_comparison_duty.md) follows necessarily, because otherwise one instrument means different things in different places.
And [the duty falls on the reader's own court](plot_and_the_duty_falls_on_the_readers_own_court.md).
[The National Court](persona_the_national_court.md) owes the same method and has a bench, a registry and the languages of its own jurisdiction.
Then [the text stops deciding and the purpose decides](plot_the_text_stops_deciding_and_the_purpose_decides.md). Preferring a language would end the arrangement and counting them would be arbitrary, so purpose and scheme are what remain.
And [the one written for you is authentic and not enough](plot_so_the_one_written_for_you_is_authentic_and_not_enough.md). [The Reader](persona_the_reader.md) did the only available thing, correctly.

Cue: a reader who obtained the official text, read it, and complied. Closes when the only available act is found complete as conduct and incomplete as method.

## Stakes

Whether a person can know a law that was written for them in their own language.
Every plot moves the same way: toward an authenticity rule that is plainly right, a divergence that competent work cannot remove, a comparison duty that uniformity requires, a duty binding courts without the languages for it, a settlement reached outside any reader's text, and an act of compliance that is complete and insufficient at once, and away from any account in which a translator slipped or a reader was lazy. The production cannot fault the translator, whose residue is the distance between two languages; and it cannot fault the Court, which cannot privilege a language without ending the arrangement or count them without handing meaning to whichever are numerous. What is left to fight over is not whether anybody erred but what to call a text that binds you, is yours, and may not be read alone.
