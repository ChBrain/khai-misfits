# Plot: Every Version Is Authentic, and That Is Right

## Taxonomy

[Read It in All of Them](play_read_it_in_all_of_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An instrument agreed, and an order whose people read two dozen languages.

## Action

[Enacting in every language](process_enacting_in_every_language.md): [the drafter](persona_the_drafter.md) and [the translator](persona_the_translator.md) issue it at [the official journal](place_the_official_journal.md), and [the equal authenticity](piece_the_equal_authenticity.md) is established.

## Stage

A rule that binds each person in a language they can read, with no version marked secondary.

## Tension

The rule is not a courtesy and cannot be softened, which is what makes everything after it structural.
A law published in only some languages binds everybody else through a translation of the law rather than through the law itself. Naming one version the source restores exactly that, so equal authenticity is the answer and is the whole case. What it also does, in the same provision, is remove any text that could settle a dispute by itself.
