# Persona: The National Court

## Taxonomy

[National Court](position_national_court.md). The one carrying a duty it cannot discharge.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A courtroom in one member state, a provision to apply, and a doctrine requiring it to be read against every other version.

## Action

Applies the provision.
Is required to compare the versions, and works in one or two languages.

## Shadow

Holds a duty stated seriously by a court that meant it, and no capacity to perform it.
The requirement is not rhetorical: it is the stated method, and it binds this seat as well as the one above. What the seat has is a bench, a registry and the languages its judges happen to read. Two dozen versions of every relevant instrument, compared line by line for a divergence that may not exist, is not a task any national court is resourced for, and the seat is not being criticised for failing to do it. The duty and the capacity were set by different considerations and never met.

## Tell

Is bound to a method it does not have the languages for.
