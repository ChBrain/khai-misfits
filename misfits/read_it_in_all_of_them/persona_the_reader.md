# Persona: The Reader

## Taxonomy

[Reader](position_reader.md). The one who reads the version written for them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A business or a person with an obligation, an official text in the language they were addressed in, and a decision to make.

## Action

Reads the law.
Complies with what it says, in the version that is authentic and is theirs.

## Shadow

Does exactly the right thing and is told afterwards that it was the wrong method.
The seat is not cutting a corner: the version consulted is authentic, was published for this seat, and is the only one this seat can read. The doctrine that follows does not say the version is unofficial or that the reading was careless. It says that no single version may be relied on in isolation, which converts the ordinary and only available act of reading the law into an incomplete one. There is no version of this seat that could have done better without becoming a different seat entirely.

## Tell

Reads the text that binds them, which is not enough.
