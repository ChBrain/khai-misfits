# Plot: So No Version May Be Read Alone

## Taxonomy

[Read It in All of Them](play_read_it_in_all_of_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A provision that reads one way here and another way somewhere else.

## Action

[The Comparison Duty](piece_the_comparison_duty.md) is stated: uniform application makes it impossible to consider one version in isolation, so a provision must be read against the others.

## Stage

A method that follows necessarily, and forbids the only reading most people can do.

## Tension

The doctrine is correct and its correctness is what does the damage.
If each forum read only its own text, one instrument would mean different things in different places and the order would not be one order. So the requirement is not fussiness: it is what uniformity costs. Its effect is to declare the ordinary act of reading the law in your own language to be an incomplete method, for reasons that have nothing to do with the reader.
