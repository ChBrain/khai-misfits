# Persona: The Drafter

## Taxonomy

[Drafter](position_drafter.md). The one who writes one thing that is published as many.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A single instrument, negotiated, agreed and then issued simultaneously in every official language of the order.

## Action

Settles the wording.
Sends it out in two dozen versions, none of which is marked as secondary.

## Shadow

Produces one rule and cannot produce one text.
The seat is not being careless and is not the author of the divergence: the negotiation is over meaning, and the meaning is agreed. What cannot be done is to hold that meaning in a single object, because the order binds people who read different languages and a rule published only in some of them would bind people in a language they do not read. So the instrument exists as a set, is agreed as one, and is issued as many, and nothing in the drafting is where the problem enters.

## Tell

Agrees one meaning and issues it as a set.
