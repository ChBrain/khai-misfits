# Plot: And the Duty Falls on the Reader's Own Court

## Taxonomy

[Read It in All of Them](play_read_it_in_all_of_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A national bench, a provision to apply, and the languages its judges happen to read.

## Action

[Comparing the versions](process_comparing_the_versions.md): [the national court](persona_the_national_court.md), holding [National Court](position_national_court.md), owes the same duty and works at [the referral](place_the_referral.md) in one or two languages.

## Stage

A stated method binding seats that have no capacity to perform it.

## Tension

The duty and the capacity were set by different considerations and never met, and nobody is at fault for the gap.
The obligation is not rhetorical and does not stop at the court that announced it. What a national court has is a bench, a registry and the languages of its own jurisdiction, and comparing every version of every relevant instrument for a divergence that may not exist is not a task it is resourced for. So the seat charged with applying the rule correctly cannot use the method that defines correct application.
