# Process: Enacting in Every Language

## Taxonomy

[Read It in All of Them](play_read_it_in_all_of_them.md). Making the rule readable by everybody it binds.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An instrument agreed and ready to issue.
[The Drafter](persona_the_drafter.md), holding [Drafter](position_drafter.md), with [the translator](persona_the_translator.md), at [the official journal](place_the_official_journal.md).

## Direction

Toward simultaneous publication in every official language, with none secondary.

## Lever

Equal authenticity, which is what stops anybody being bound by a translation of a law rather than by the law.

## Echo

One agreed meaning, existing as a set of texts that are each the instrument and are not identical.
