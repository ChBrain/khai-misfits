# Persona: The Translator

## Taxonomy

[Translator](position_translator.md). The one whose competent work still differs.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A lawyer-linguist working to a terminology database, a style guide and a deadline, on a text that will be law.

## Action

Renders the provision into a language whose categories are not the same categories.
Produces a version that is correct and is not identical.

## Shadow

Is expert, is careful, and cannot make two languages carry one text.
Nothing here is a slip. Where one language has a single word for two things and another has two words, a choice must be made and either choice is defensible; where a tense carries an implication in one grammar and nothing in another, the implication either appears or does not. The seat resolves these to the best professional standard available, which is high, and the residue is not error but the distance between two languages, which no standard closes because no standard can.

## Tell

Does the work perfectly and leaves a difference behind.
