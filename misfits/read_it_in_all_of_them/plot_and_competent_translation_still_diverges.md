## Cue

## Action

[The Divergence](piece_the_divergence.md) is produced: where one language joins what another splits a choice is made, and where a grammar carries an implication another cannot the implication appears or does not.

## Stage

Versions that are each correct and are not the same, present on the day of publication.

## Tension

The residue is what is left when the standard has been met, which is why no drafting or training removes it.
This is not a slip to be found and corrected, and treating it as one is the first thing the play refuses. The seat works to a high professional standard and the difference that remains is the distance between two languages. It is not a drift that grows with time either: it is there on the morning of publication, in every version at once.
