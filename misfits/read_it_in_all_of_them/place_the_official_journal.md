## Offers

## Withheld

An original.
The place holds no privileged text and could not hold one without giving up what it is for: the moment a version becomes the source, everybody who reads a different one is bound by a translation of a law rather than by the law. So the set is the instrument, and the difference between reading the instrument and reading a text has been introduced without anybody deciding to introduce it.
