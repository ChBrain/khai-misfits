## Load Bearing

It is the right rule and it is what removes the privileged text, so both halves of the difficulty come from the same decision.
A person should be bound by a text in a language they read, and a rule published in only some languages binds the rest through a translation of the law rather than through the law. Equal authenticity is the answer, is not a courtesy, and cannot be softened by naming one version the source, because that is precisely the thing it exists to refuse. What follows is that the instrument is a set, and a set can differ from itself.
