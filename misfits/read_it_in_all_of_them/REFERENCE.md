# Read It in All of Them: Reference

The research warrant for Read It in All of Them.
The term is the house's; the play runs on multilingual legal orders and on what equal authenticity costs the
person it was written to protect, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the authenticity rule as **a guarantee that removes the text it guarantees**: a rule is published
simultaneously in every official language and each version is equally authentic, because a person should be
bound by a text in a language they read and a rule published in only some languages binds everybody else
through a translation of the law rather than through the law. Languages do not carve the world the same way, so
competent translation leaves versions that differ on the morning of publication. Uniform application then makes
it impossible to consider one version in isolation, and that duty reaches national courts as well, so the
ordinary act of reading the law in your own language becomes an incomplete method. The play models the
documented mechanics, the authenticity rule that is plainly right, the divergence that survives a met standard,
the comparison duty that uniformity requires, the duty binding seats without the languages for it, the
settlement reached outside any reader's text, and the compliance that is complete and insufficient at once, as
the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a translator slipping or a reader being lazy.

The governing law is that the provision guaranteeing you a text in your own language is the provision that
removes any text able to settle a question by itself.

**Nobody in the sequence falls short, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The
drafter agrees one meaning. The lawyer-linguist works to a terminology database and a style guide, at a high
professional standard. The reader obtains the official text and complies with it. The national court applies the
provision. The Court of Justice answers the question referred to it.

What removes any possibility of blame from the authenticity rule is that it cannot be softened. Naming one
version the source restores exactly the thing it exists to refuse, since everybody reading a different language
would then be bound through a rendering of the law. So equal authenticity is the answer rather than a courtesy,
and it is in the same provision that no version can settle a dispute alone.

What removes it from the translator is that the residue is what remains **after** the standard is met rather
than what remains when it is not. Where one language has a single word for two things and another has two, a
defensible choice must be made; where a grammar carries an implication another cannot, the implication appears
or does not. That distance is not a slip to be found and corrected, and it does not grow with time: it is
present in every version at once on the day of publication.

And the comparison duty closes the case against the last party, because it follows necessarily. If each forum
read only its own text, one instrument would mean different things in different places and the order would not
be one order. So requiring that a provision be read against its other versions is what uniformity costs, and it
is not fussiness.

**And the duty reaches the reader's own court, which is where the structure becomes concrete.** The obligation
does not stop at the court that stated it: national courts owe it too. What a national bench has is a registry
and the languages of its own jurisdiction, and comparing every version of every relevant instrument for a
divergence that may not exist is not a task it is resourced for. So the seat charged with applying the rule
correctly does not have the means to use the method that defines correct application.

**Then the settlement comes from outside every text.** The Court cannot prefer one language without making the
rest secondary, and cannot count versions without handing the meaning to whichever languages happen to be
numerous. Purpose and general scheme are what remain, they are sound and long established, and they keep the
order uniform. What they are not is a reading of anything anybody was given.

It is distinct from the house's **An Angle and a Count**, which the slate line required it to be tested against
before authoring, and the test was run in the sweep. There two definitions of one quantity drift apart at a
physical rate, so a seat must keep inserting corrections and the difficulty accumulates. Remove that engine, by
supposing no drift at all, and this misfit stands entire: there is no rate here and nothing accumulates, since
the versions are simultaneously authentic and simultaneously different on the day they are issued.

It is distinct from the house's **Right Apart Wrong Together**, where two individually correct metals joined in
one circuit consume one of themselves, so the harm falls on a party to the joint and runs at an
electrochemical rate. Remove that engine and this stands: nothing degrades, neither version is consumed, no
current runs, and the cost falls on a **third party** who relied on one of them rather than on either of the
two joined things.

It is distinct from the house's **In Somebody Else's Words**, where a second speaker re-authors the manner a
room reads credibility from, so there is an original utterance and a rendering and the record keeps only one of
them. Here the law says there is no original: every version is the instrument, none is a rendering, and the
difficulty is not that one text was lost but that no text was ever privileged.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the translator as
  sloppy or the reader as lazy, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: equal authenticity
  is the only defensible arrangement and cannot be softened without restoring the thing it refuses; the
  divergence is the distance between two languages and is what remains after a high standard is met; the
  comparison duty follows necessarily from uniform application, since otherwise one instrument would mean
  different things in different places; the national court owes that duty and has the languages of its own
  jurisdiction; the Court of Justice cannot privilege a language or count them and must still answer; and the
  reader consults the authentic text addressed to them, which is the only one they can read. The failure is
  authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that the guarantee of a text in your own
  language is what removes any text that settles the question.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Designating one version as
  authoritative ends the arrangement and binds most readers through a translation of the law. Drafting in a
  single language and translating afterwards is what equal authenticity was adopted against, and moves the
  question rather than answering it. Aligning terminology across languages is real work, is done, and cannot
  close a gap that exists because the categories differ. Machine comparison of versions can flag candidate
  divergences and cannot say which reading the purpose supports. Publishing an official commentary adds a text
  that also needs translating. And knowing the whole structure, which the profession does and has written about
  at length, leaves an obligation falling due and one language to read it in.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the authenticity principle, the case law, the interpretive
  criteria and the critical scholarship are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: **whether the comparison
  requirement describes what courts do or idealises it is genuinely contested**, and the play carries that
  argument rather than settling it; the account describes one family of multilingual orders and treats
  arrangements that differ in detail as one structure, which is its own simplification, since states with two
  official languages and interpretation acts of their own resolve divergence by rules this play does not
  model; the frequency and practical severity of material divergence is not quantified here and the play makes
  no claim about how often a reader is actually caught by one; and the doctrine has developed over decades in
  ways one row cannot carry. **The play makes no claim that any judgment was wrongly decided, that any version
  was badly translated, or that any legal order should change its arrangements**, only that a text written for
  you in your own language can be authentic, binding, and insufficient to answer the question it was written to
  answer.
