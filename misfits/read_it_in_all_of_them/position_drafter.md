# Position: Drafter

## Taxonomy

[Read It in All of Them](play_read_it_in_all_of_them.md). The seat that agrees a meaning and issues a set.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A negotiated instrument, and an obligation to publish it in every official language.

## Orders

Settle the wording. Issue it, simultaneously, in all of them.

## Loses

The possibility of a single object.
A rule published in only some languages would bind people in a language they do not read, so the set is the only lawful form.

## Drives

Toward one agreed meaning, existing as two dozen authentic texts.
