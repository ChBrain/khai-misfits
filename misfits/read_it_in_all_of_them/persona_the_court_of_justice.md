# Persona: The Court of Justice

## Taxonomy

[Court of Justice](position_court_of_justice.md). The one who must settle it, and by something other than the text.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A referral, a divergence between versions that both say something clear, and a question that has to be answered.

## Action

Compares the versions.
Finds no version privileged, and decides by the purpose and the general scheme.

## Shadow

Reaches the only available answer, and it is not a reading of any text.
The seat cannot prefer one language, because preferring one would make the others secondary and the whole arrangement rests on none of them being. It cannot count versions, because a majority reading would hand the meaning to whichever languages happen to be numerous. What is left is purpose and scheme, which is a sound method and is the one thing that keeps the order uniform. Its consequence is that the words a party actually read stop being where the answer is.

## Tell

Settles it soundly, by a route that passes through no reader's text.
