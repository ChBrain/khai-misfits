## Load Bearing

It declares reading one version in isolation to be the wrong method, which is the only act most readers can perform.
The doctrine is not rhetorical and does not stop at the court that stated it: the obligation to read a provision against its other language versions is owed by national courts too. That is coherent, since a uniform rule cannot be maintained if each forum reads only its own text. It is also a requirement that no national bench and no ordinary reader has the languages for, so the method that preserves the order is one almost nobody in the order can carry out.
