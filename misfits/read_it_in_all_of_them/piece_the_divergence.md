## Load Bearing

It arrives from the distance between languages rather than from anybody's error, so no standard of drafting or translation removes it.
Where one language has a single word for two things and another has two, a defensible choice must be made; where a grammar carries an implication another cannot, the implication appears or does not. Lawyer-linguists working to terminology databases and style guides resolve these to a high standard, and the residue is what is left when the standard has been met rather than what is left when it has not. It is present on the day of publication, in every version, and it is not a drift that grows.

## Apparent

A translation error, to be found and corrected.

## Yearbook

Is what two languages leave when both are rendered correctly, which is why it cannot be drafted away.
