# Plot: The Second Cannot Bend

## Taxonomy

[An Angle and a Count](play_an_angle_and_a_count.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The obvious repair: make the second a little longer, and let the day come out even.

## Action

[The Second](piece_the_second.md) is held against [the rotation](piece_the_rotation.md) at [the bureau](place_the_bureau.md), and the repair is found to be unavailable.

## Stage

A unit that is rigid by construction, beside a quantity that is not.

## Tension

The rigidity is the whole value of the unit, so bending it destroys the thing being protected.
A second that could be stretched to fit a day would not be a unit, and every measurement ever expressed in seconds would move with it: a caesium standard is worth having precisely because it does not care what the Earth is doing. So the flexible side of the arrangement is fixed for the same reason the rigid side is, and the incompatibility cannot be met halfway.
