# Persona: The Delegate

## Taxonomy

[Delegate](position_delegate.md). The one who must vote on which requirement gets broken.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A conference room, a standing agenda item, and two constituencies who are both right.

## Action

Hears both cases, finds both sound, and votes.
Defers, when deferring is available.

## Shadow

Is not deciding badly, and is not deciding slowly for want of nerve.
The question in front of the seat has no answer inside it: it is a choice about who absorbs an incompatibility, and every option is a transfer rather than a repair. Forty years of the item returning is not a failure of the committee. It is what a body looks like when it is asked to resolve something that is not resolvable.

## Tell

Keeps arriving at the same agenda item, correctly.
