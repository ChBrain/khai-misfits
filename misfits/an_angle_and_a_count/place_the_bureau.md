# Place: The Bureau

## Taxonomy

[An Angle and a Count](play_an_angle_and_a_count.md). Where one number is published for everybody, six months ahead.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An ensemble of atomic clocks, a record of the Earth's orientation, a difference between them, and a bulletin.
No dispute about any measurement: both quantities are known to far better precision than the correction being argued over.

## Holds

[The Timekeeper](persona_the_timekeeper.md), who must publish something.
[The Rotation](piece_the_rotation.md) and [The Second](piece_the_second.md), which are what have to be reconciled.
[The Delegate](persona_the_delegate.md), when the reconciling becomes a vote.

## Offers

A single civil time, and six months' warning of the next correction.

## Withheld

Any way to know the correction after that.
The place cannot forecast the Earth: the drift comes from tides, from the flow of the core against the mantle and from ice moving off the crust, so the next correction can be announced half a year out and the one after it cannot be announced at all. Which means the number of seconds between now and a date some years away is not a knowable quantity, and no care at the bureau makes it one.
