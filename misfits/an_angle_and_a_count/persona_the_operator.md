# Persona: The Operator

## Taxonomy

[Operator](position_operator.md). The one who spreads the extra second so nothing sees it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A fleet of machines that must not stop, and a clock they all read.

## Action

Stretches the seconds across a day so the correction arrives without a discontinuity.
Keeps time monotonic, and keeps the fleet up.

## Shadow

Buys uniformity with accuracy, and it is the best move available.
During the smear the machines are keeping a second that is not a second, so a duration measured across it is wrong by a fraction that nothing in the system records. And because every operator picks its own window, two smeared clocks disagree with each other and with the published time, so the remedy that removes the discontinuity replaces it with a disagreement.

## Tell

Solves the problem locally by making the local clock quietly wrong.
