# Plot: So the Extra Second Is Inserted

## Taxonomy

[An Angle and a Count](play_an_angle_and_a_count.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The drift reaching the limit that was agreed.

## Action

[Inserting the second](process_inserting_the_second.md): [the timekeeper](persona_the_timekeeper.md) announces through [the bureau](place_the_bureau.md), and [the extra second](piece_the_extra_second.md) is put into the last minute of a day.

## Stage

Noon held near the sun, and a minute with sixty one seconds in it.

## Tension

The compromise works, and works by putting the incompatibility somewhere rather than removing it.
The angle is held, which is what the correction is for. In exchange a count that everything downstream reads as continuous acquires a discontinuity, at a moment set by a planet and therefore not movable, not softenable and not schedulable further out. Nobody designed this badly: it is the cheapest available place to put a contradiction, and the contradiction is still there afterwards.
