# Persona: The Timekeeper

## Taxonomy

[Timekeeper](position_timekeeper.md). The one who must publish a single civil time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A caesium ensemble, a record of the Earth's orientation, and a bulletin that the world sets its clocks by.

## Action

Compares the count against the angle.
Publishes one number, and announces a correction when the two have drifted far enough apart.

## Shadow

Is asked for one quantity that must satisfy two requirements, and has no third option to reach for.
Both requirements come from outside the seat and neither can be refused: the count must be uniform or it is not a measurement, and the angle must be tracked or noon is not noon. Whatever is published breaks one of them, so the seat's whole competence goes into choosing which.

## Tell

Publishes the correct answer to a question that has no correct answer.
