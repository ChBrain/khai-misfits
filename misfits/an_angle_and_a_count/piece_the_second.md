## Load Bearing

Its whole value is that it cannot bend, and that is what makes it useless for tracking a thing that does.
A unit that could be stretched to fit the day would not be a unit, so nothing about the count can be adjusted to accommodate the rotation. The uniformity is not a design choice with a trade behind it: it is the property that makes duration measurable at all, and a second that varied would take every measurement ever made down with it.
