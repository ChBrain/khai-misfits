# Play: An Angle and a Count

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

An Angle and a Count.
A day is a rotation of the Earth, which is an angle. A second is a fixed number of cycles of a caesium atom, which is a count. Civil time is asked to be one quantity that is both, and the Earth does not turn in a whole number of seconds, nor in the same number twice.

## Arc

The bend is this: two requirements on one quantity, both correct, both binding, and no third quantity to put either of them on.
It runs on requirements that are real on both sides.
[Both Requirements Are Real](plot_both_requirements_are_real.md). [The Navigator](persona_the_navigator.md) needs the angle because a second of time is a quarter of a mile of ground, and [the scheduler](persona_the_scheduler.md) needs the count because a tally that can decrease is not a measurement. Neither is a preference.
And the Earth will not oblige.
[The Earth Does Not Keep a Steady Beat](plot_the_earth_does_not_keep_a_steady_beat.md). [The Rotation](piece_the_rotation.md) is slowed by tides, pushed either way by the core against the mantle, and shifted again by ice moving off the crust, so the drift is real, uneven, and not forecastable.
And the obvious repair is closed.
[The Second Cannot Bend](plot_the_second_cannot_bend.md). [The Second](piece_the_second.md) is rigid because rigidity is the whole of its value, so it cannot be stretched to make the day come out even without taking every measurement ever made along with it.
Then [So the Extra Second Is Inserted](plot_so_the_extra_second_is_inserted.md).
[The Extra Second](piece_the_extra_second.md) holds the angle by putting a discontinuity into the count, at a moment a planet chooses, which is the cheapest available place to put a contradiction rather than a way of removing one.
And [the count was assumed to be a count](plot_and_the_count_was_assumed_to_be_a_count.md). What breaks at [the log](place_the_log.md) is not an unhandled case but the meaning of the unit, which is why review would not have found it and why the failure lands on work that was done correctly.
And [Every Remedy Pays in a Different Currency](plot_every_remedy_pays_in_a_different_currency.md). Abandon the correction and noon walks away from the sun forever; smear it and a second is briefly not a second; keep two timescales and the conversion needs a table of corrections nobody can write yet.

## Triggers

**[Both Requirements Are Real](plot_both_requirements_are_real.md)**
Cue: one published time, and two parties setting their instruments by it. Closes when neither requirement can be reduced to a preference, since the angle is a quantity on the ground and the count is what makes duration measurable.

**[The Earth Does Not Keep a Steady Beat](plot_the_earth_does_not_keep_a_steady_beat.md)**
Cue: the angle measured against the quasars, beside a count that never varies. Closes when the drift has been shown to be real, uneven and unforecastable, so a correction can be announced and never planned for.

**[The Second Cannot Bend](plot_the_second_cannot_bend.md)**
Cue: the obvious repair, of making the second a little longer. Closes when bending the unit is found to destroy the thing being protected, since a second that could be stretched would not be a unit.

**[So the Extra Second Is Inserted](plot_so_the_extra_second_is_inserted.md)**
Cue: the drift reaching the limit that was agreed. Closes when the compromise has been seen to hold the angle by giving the contradiction a place to happen, at a moment a planet sets and nobody can move.

**[And the Count Was Assumed to Be a Count](plot_and_the_count_was_assumed_to_be_a_count.md)**
Cue: a difference taken between two timestamps in the last minute of the year. Closes when what broke turns out to be the meaning of the unit rather than an unhandled case, so no review could have caught it.

**[Every Remedy Pays in a Different Currency](plot_every_remedy_pays_in_a_different_currency.md)**
Cue: three good answers on the table and a vote to cast. Closes when each has been priced in its own currency, drift, accuracy, or a conversion table that cannot be written yet, and the choice is still which requirement to break.

## Stakes

Whether one quantity can be asked to satisfy two correct requirements that exclude each other.
Every plot moves the same way: toward two requirements neither of which is a preference, a planet that will not turn evenly, a unit that cannot bend without taking every measurement with it, a correction that relocates the contradiction rather than removing it, a failure that lands on work done correctly, and three remedies that each pay somewhere else, and away from any account in which a standards body dithered or a programmer was careless. The production cannot fault the navigator for wanting the clock to be about the Earth, and it cannot fault the scheduler for assuming that time moves forward, because that is what the word means. What is left to fight over is not who was wrong but which of two right answers the world should be made to live without.
