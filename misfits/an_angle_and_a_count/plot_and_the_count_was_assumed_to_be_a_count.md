# Plot: And the Count Was Assumed to Be a Count

## Taxonomy

[An Angle and a Count](play_an_angle_and_a_count.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The last minute of the year, and a difference being taken between two timestamps.

## Action

[Subtracting two timestamps](process_subtracting_two_timestamps.md): [the scheduler](persona_the_scheduler.md) does the arithmetic at [the log](place_the_log.md) and gets an interval that cannot exist.

## Stage

A negative duration, a lock that is never released, a loop that will not finish.

## Tension

What broke was not an unchecked case but the meaning of the word, which is why care would not have caught it.
Nobody wrote down that time moves forward, in the way nobody writes down that a length is not negative: it is the definition the work rests on, and no clock any of these people had ever read behaved otherwise. Review does not find an assumption that nothing in the world has ever contradicted. And the failure lands on ordinary competent work, on systems whose arithmetic was right and whose clock was read correctly, which is what puts it outside anybody's conduct.
