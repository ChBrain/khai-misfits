## Load Bearing

It is irregular, and the irregularity is not noise to be averaged away.
Tides slow it by a couple of milliseconds a century, the core moving against the mantle pushes it either way over decades, and mass shifting between ice and ocean changes it again; recently it has been running fast enough that the next correction may have to be a second taken out rather than put in. So the drift is real, is not steady, and cannot be projected forward, which is why the correction can only ever be announced and never scheduled.
