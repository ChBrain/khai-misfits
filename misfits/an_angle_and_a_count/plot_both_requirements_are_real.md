# Plot: Both Requirements Are Real

## Taxonomy

[An Angle and a Count](play_an_angle_and_a_count.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

One published time, and two parties setting their instruments by it.

## Action

[The Navigator](persona_the_navigator.md) asks it for the Earth's orientation; [the scheduler](persona_the_scheduler.md) asks it for a duration. Both readings are taken from the same bulletin at [the bureau](place_the_bureau.md).

## Stage

Two requirements on one quantity, stated plainly and both sound.

## Tension

Neither requirement can be reduced to a preference, which is what shuts the ordinary exit.
The angle is a quantity: a second of time is a quarter of a mile of ground, so a clock that has drifted from the Earth points at the wrong place. The count is a definition: a second is a unit of duration, and a tally of them that can decrease is not a measurement of anything. There is no version of this in which one side is being sentimental about the old ways or fussy about a corner case, and that is why the difficulty never resolves into somebody yielding.
