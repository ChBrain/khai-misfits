# Persona: The Navigator

## Taxonomy

[Navigator](position_navigator.md). The one who needs to know where the Earth actually is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A dish that must be pointed, an ephemeris, and a spacecraft that will not wait.

## Action

Asks civil time for the Earth's orientation.
Points at where the ground station will be, not at where a uniform count says it should be.

## Shadow

Wants nothing unreasonable and is asking for the older of the two meanings.
A day was a rotation for as long as there have been days, and pointing at anything beyond the sky requires the angle and not a tally: an error of one second of time is an error of a quarter mile at the equator, so the requirement is a real quantity and not a preference for tradition.

## Tell

Needs the clock to be about the Earth, which is what a clock was for.
