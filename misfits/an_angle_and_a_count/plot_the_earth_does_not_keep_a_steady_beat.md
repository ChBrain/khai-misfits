# Plot: The Earth Does Not Keep a Steady Beat

## Taxonomy

[An Angle and a Count](play_an_angle_and_a_count.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The angle, measured against the quasars, against a count that never varies.

## Action

[Measuring the rotation](process_measuring_the_rotation.md): [the timekeeper](persona_the_timekeeper.md) reads [the rotation](piece_the_rotation.md) at [the bureau](place_the_bureau.md) and finds the difference growing unevenly.

## Stage

A drift that is real, is not constant, and runs in both directions.

## Tension

The irregularity is not measurement noise, so no amount of averaging removes it.
Tides take a couple of milliseconds a century off the turn, the core moving against the mantle adds and subtracts over decades, and mass moving between ice and ocean shifts it again; lately the Earth has run fast, so the next correction may be a second removed rather than added, a case the arrangement has never had to perform. None of it is forecastable, which is the property that matters: the correction can be announced half a year ahead and never planned for, and no instrument at the bureau changes that, because the thing being predicted is a planet.
