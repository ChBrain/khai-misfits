# Plan: Advise the Risk

## Taxonomy

A **duty to the institution**: the intent of [The One Who Advises Against It](persona_the_one_who_advises_against_it.md), inside [Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Advises Against It](persona_the_one_who_advises_against_it.md)

## Direction

Protect the institution from an exposure it cannot bound. Price the liability honestly, explain it plainly, and advise against proceeding where the risk is open-ended.

## Orders

(Assess [the statutory damages](piece_the_statutory_damages.md), which need no proof of loss and multiply by the number of works; note that the potential claimant cannot be ruled out; advise against; record the advice through [deciding not to](process_deciding_not_to.md).)

## Implementation

([The Rights Desk](place_the_rights_desk.md), where the search has already found nobody; sympathy for the project, and responsibility for an institution that cannot absorb an unbounded claim.)

## Targets

- [ ] the exposure is assessed honestly rather than minimised
- [ ] the institution is not asked to accept an open liability across a collection for a single reissue
- [ ] the advice given is the advice any competent lawyer would give
- [ ] the work stays dark, as the direct result of doing this correctly
