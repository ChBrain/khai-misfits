# Nobody to Ask: Reference

The research warrant for Nobody to Ask.
The term is the house's; the play runs on orphan works in copyright, the prohibition on formalities that
makes them possible, the diligent search requirement, and the measured absence of mid-century works from
circulation, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the orphan work as **a deadlock with no disagreement in it**: copyright attaches automatically at
fixation, because requiring registration would strip rights from exactly the authors least able to manage
paperwork, and the treaty accordingly forbids any formality as a condition of the right. A protection with
no formalities is a protection with no register, and a term running seventy years past the author's death
outlives every record that could have connected the work to a person. The play models the documented
mechanics, the humane rule that forbids the list, the holder who becomes unlocatable through ordinary
events, the willing licensee whose offer has no recipient, the counsel who correctly prices an unbounded
liability, the search whose unit is the work while the problem's unit is the collection, and the
corrections that either leave the deadlock standing or grant a permission nobody gave, as the load-bearing
vectors of the structure, not as a tale of greedy rightsholders or piratical archives.

The governing law is that the law cannot distinguish absence from refusal. A holder who would certainly have
said yes produces exactly the same legal position as one who said no, because consent is required and
silence is not consent, and there is no procedure by which an unanswered question becomes an answered one.

What makes it a misfit rather than an injustice is that it produces a loss on every side at once. The
holder earns no royalty, the archive gets no use, the public gets no access, and no competitor is excluded
from anything. There is no party being favoured over another, which means the usual remedy of rebalancing
between them has nothing to work on.

What locks it is the pairing of two rules that are each defensible alone. Automatic protection is right, and
statutory damages that need no proof of loss are right, because small rightsholders could not otherwise
enforce anything. Together they mean an institution cannot bound its exposure by asking what a use is
worth, while the person who might claim cannot be ruled out for precisely the reason the licence cannot be
sought. So counsel advises against proceeding, correctly, and the advice is what keeps the work dark.

It is distinct from the house's **The Anticommons**, where too many owners each hold a veto over one
resource so it sits idle: there the rights are fragmented and the vetoes are real, held by identifiable
people who can be approached and who may refuse. Here there is no fragmentation and no veto. There is one
owner and no refusal, and the transaction fails because consent cannot be sought rather than because it is
withheld, so the anticommons problem of assembling many yeses is replaced by the problem of finding anybody
at all to say one.

It is distinct from the house's **The Price Neither Will Name**, where a buyer and seller both stand to gain
but each hides its price so the deal dies in the gap: there both parties are present, informed and
strategic, and the failure is bargaining. Here one party is absent and nothing strategic occurs anywhere.

It is distinct from the house's **Too Much to Ask**, where each step is added for a good reason and the cost
of claiming deters the people the programme was built to serve: there the claimant can complete the process
and declines because it is not worth it. Here the process can be completed perfectly and produces no
entitlement, since a search that proves nobody can be found does not create a person to license from.

It is distinct from the house's **The Hollow Word**, where a word keeps circulating after the practice it
named has gone: there a form outlives its content and the field speaks an old language. Here nothing is
hollow: the right is fully alive, fully enforceable and fully valuable, and the only missing element is a
person to exercise it.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the
  rightsholder as obstructive or the archive as reckless, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to
  get wrong: the prohibition on formalities protects the authors least able to manage paperwork and was
  written against an era in which people lost everything they made by missing a renewal; the holder who
  cannot be found has done nothing but live an ordinary life containing a death, an inheritance or a
  company sale; the would-be licensee has budget, goodwill and every intention of paying; and counsel who
  advises against proceeding is correctly pricing an exposure that cannot be bounded and would be failing
  their duty to say otherwise. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the
  misfit is that a right nobody is exercising produces a loss for every party at once, including the person
  who holds it.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Capping liability for
  good-faith users after a documented search leaves a residual risk and still requires the per-work search,
  so collections remain uneconomic to clear. Extended collective licensing works by having a body grant
  permission on behalf of holders who never joined it and never agreed to its terms, which is exactly the
  objection to it. A register would dissolve the whole problem and cannot be made a condition of the right,
  because the treaty forbids formalities, so any register is voluntary and the unlocatable are precisely
  the people who do not join one. And shortening the term would strip protection from living authors in
  order to release works that nobody is claiming.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the treaty, the directive, the search requirements
  and the empirical work are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: orphan status is a matter of degree
  and of effort rather than a fixed property, and a work unfindable by one searcher may be cleared by
  another with better sources; estimates of how much of any collection is orphaned vary enormously by
  medium, period and method, and this play quotes none, since the figures in circulation are not consistent
  enough to rest on; the availability gap Heald measures is caused by copyright status together with
  publishers' own commercial choices, so orphan works are a substantial part of that hole rather than the
  whole of it; statutory damages and their scale are features of some systems and not others, so the
  chilling effect staged here is sharpest in those; and several jurisdictions have since enacted orphan
  works schemes or extended collective licensing that materially change the position. **The play makes no
  claim that authors should have to register to be protected**, only that a right with no record outlives
  every means of finding the person who holds it.
