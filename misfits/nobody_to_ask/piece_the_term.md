# Piece: The Term

## Taxonomy

[The Stacks](place_the_stacks.md). The duration that outlives everybody who could have been asked.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Running over everything in [the stacks](place_the_stacks.md), for the life of the author and seventy years after it.

## Load Bearing

It is what turns a traceable owner into an untraceable one, purely by lasting.
An address book is good for a decade, a company for a few, a family's memory of what a great-aunt photographed for perhaps two generations: a right that runs seventy years past a death will therefore outlast every record that could have connected it to a person, and the longer the term runs the larger the orphaned pool grows.

## Apparent

A single number in a statute, harmonised internationally, defended as securing an author's family after their death.
Uncontroversial in its own terms, and settled.

## Yearbook

Extended repeatedly, each time for reasons argued on their merits and each time enlarging the population of works whose owners have gone.
Falls identically on the work still earning and the work nobody has asked about since 1962, since the term does not know the difference.
