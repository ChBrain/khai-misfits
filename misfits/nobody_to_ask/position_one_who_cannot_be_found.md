# Position: One Who Cannot Be Found

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md). The seat that is absent without ever leaving.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A valuable right, held either unknowingly or through a chain nobody has written down.
A life that included a death, an inheritance, a company sale or a change of name.

## Orders

Hold the right.
Do nothing, which is what the law requires of you.

## Loses

The royalty it would have been glad of, and every chance of being asked.
Each step that broke the chain is an ordinary event that happens to everybody and none of them is a breach of any duty, so there is no negligence to allege and no bad faith to expose: the seat is not withholding consent, it is simply not present, and the law provides no way for absence to be treated differently from refusal.

## Drives

Toward holding a right that earns nothing, licenses nothing and refuses nothing, while being blamed for none of it.
