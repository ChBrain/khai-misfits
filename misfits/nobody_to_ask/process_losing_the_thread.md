# Process: Losing the Thread

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md). An owner becoming unlocatable through ordinary life.

## Owner

## Initiated by

A death, an inheritance, a company sale, a merger, a renaming, a winding up whose schedule of assets never itemised a backlist.
[The One Who Cannot Be Found](persona_the_one_who_cannot_be_found.md), holding [One Who Cannot Be Found](position_one_who_cannot_be_found.md).

## Direction

Toward a right held unknowingly, or through a chain nobody wrote down.
Toward the end of every record that could have connected the work to a person, well inside [the term](piece_the_term.md).

## Lever

Time, applied to institutions and families that do not last as long as the right does.

## Echo

An absence that is indistinguishable from refusal in law, and entirely unlike it in fact.
