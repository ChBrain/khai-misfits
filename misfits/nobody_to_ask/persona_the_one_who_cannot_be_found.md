# Persona: The One Who Cannot Be Found

## Taxonomy

[One Who Cannot Be Found](position_one_who_cannot_be_found.md). The holder, absent through ordinary events.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A photographer who died in 1981, whose estate passed to a niece who does not know what a copyright is.
Or a publisher bought, merged, renamed and wound up, its schedule of assets never itemising a 1958 backlist.

## Action

Holds a valuable right without knowing it, or holds it through a chain nobody has ever written down.
Earns nothing from it, licenses nothing, and refuses nothing.

## Shadow

Is not hiding, and has done nothing at all.
Every step that broke the chain is an ordinary event that happens to everybody, a death, an inheritance, a company sale, a change of name, and none of them is a failure of duty: so there is no negligence to allege, no bad faith to expose and nobody who could have kept the thread that was not simply living an ordinary life.

## Tell

Would very likely have said yes, and is the reason nobody can say anything.
