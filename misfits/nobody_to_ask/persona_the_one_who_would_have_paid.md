# Persona: The One Who Would Have Paid

## Taxonomy

[One Who Would Have Paid](position_one_who_would_have_paid.md). The willing licensee, with budget and nobody to give it to.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An archive that wants to digitise, a label that wants to reissue, a documentary that needs forty seconds of footage.
Funded, willing, and entirely prepared to pay whatever a licence would cost.

## Action

Attempts to clear the rights, at [the rights desk](place_the_rights_desk.md), through every source anybody has.
Finds nothing, and stops.

## Shadow

Fails for want of a counterparty rather than for want of agreement.
This is not a negotiation that broke down, a price that could not be met or a refusal that might be appealed: the request was never made, because there is nobody to make it to, so the one thing that would resolve everything, an offer and an acceptance, has no second party available anywhere in the world to complete it.

## Tell

Has the money, wants the thing, harms nobody by using it, and does not use it.
