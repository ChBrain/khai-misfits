# Plot: A Licence From Somebody Else

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The deadlock named at last, and the schemes on the table: cap the liability, licence collectively, or bring back a register.

## Action

[Let them use it](plan_let_them_use_it.md): [The One Who Would Have Paid](persona_the_one_who_would_have_paid.md) presses for each in turn, and finds every one of them carries the cost somewhere else.

## Stage

Limited liability for good-faith users still requires the per-work search first, so collections remain uneconomic to clear.

## Tension

Every answer either leaves the deadlock standing or grants a permission the owner never gave.
Extended collective licensing works by having a body licence on behalf of holders who never joined it and never agreed to its terms, which is precisely the objection. A register would solve the whole thing and cannot be made a condition of the right, because the treaty forbids formalities, so any register is voluntary and the people who cannot be found are the people who do not join it. And shortening the term would strip protection from living authors in order to release works that nobody is claiming.
