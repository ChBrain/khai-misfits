# Process: Protecting Without Paperwork

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md). A right attaching automatically, and leaving no trace of who holds it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Fixation: the moment a work is written down, recorded or exposed.
[The Rule Against Formalities](persona_the_rule_against_formalities.md), holding [Rule Against Formalities](position_rule_against_formalities.md).

## Direction

Toward protection for every author, including the careless, the poor and the unadvised, with nothing required of them.
Toward a system holding no list of who owns what.

## Lever

A treaty article providing that enjoyment and exercise shall not be subject to any formality, written against an era in which people lost their work by missing a renewal.

## Echo

The most humane provision in the field, and the reason nothing in the field can be traced.
