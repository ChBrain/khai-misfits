# Process: Searching Diligently

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md). The prescribed hunt, per work, ending where it began.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A relief available only to those who have looked properly.
[The One Who Would Have Paid](persona_the_one_who_would_have_paid.md), at [the rights desk](place_the_rights_desk.md), with [the diligent search](piece_the_diligent_search.md).

## Direction

Through registries, catalogues, trade directories, probate records and databases, documented item by item.
Toward a finding, almost always, that the holder cannot be identified or located.

## Lever

A remedy whose unit is the work, applied to a problem whose unit is the collection.

## Echo

A search that costs more than every licence it would have bought, succeeding perfectly at establishing that there is nobody to pay.
