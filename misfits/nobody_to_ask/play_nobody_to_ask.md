# Play: Nobody to Ask

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Nobody to Ask.
Copyright arrives the moment a work is fixed. No form, no fee, no registration, no notice: the protection attaches automatically and internationally, and it does so on purpose, because requiring paperwork would mean that the careless, the poor and the unadvised lost their rights while the organised kept theirs. It is one of the genuinely humane provisions in the whole of intellectual property. It also means there is no register. And a right with no register, running for the life of the author and seventy years afterwards, will outlive the address book that could have found them.

## Arc

The bend is this: protection without formalities is protection without a record of who holds it.
It runs on a kindness with no filing cabinet behind it.
The Rule Against Formalities is not an oversight. The treaty says plainly that the enjoyment and exercise of these rights shall not be subject to any formality, and the reason is good: a registration requirement is a tax on attention, paid by exactly the authors least able to pay it, and the century before it was abolished is full of people who lost everything they had made by missing a renewal.
The One Who Cannot Be Found does nothing wrong either, and mostly does nothing at all. A photographer dies and their estate passes to a niece who does not know what a copyright is. A publisher is bought, merged, renamed and wound up, and the schedule of assets does not itemise a 1958 backlist. Nobody hides. The thread simply goes, through ordinary events that happen to everybody.
The One Who Would Have Paid is willing, and this is what makes it a misfit rather than a shame. An archive wants to digitise, a label wants to reissue, a documentary wants forty seconds of footage. They have budget, they have goodwill, and they would pay whatever a licence cost. The transaction fails not because it is refused but because it cannot be proposed.
And The One Who Advises Against It is right to say no. Using a work without a licence exposes the institution to statutory damages that do not depend on any proof of loss, and the person who might appear to claim them cannot be ruled out precisely because they cannot be found. The lawyer is not being timid: they are pricing an unbounded risk with a hole where the counterparty should be, and no responsible institution takes that on for one film.
So The Diligent Search becomes the ritual. The law offers relief to those who look properly, and looking properly means a documented search of registries, catalogues, trade sources and databases, per work. For a collection of fifty thousand photographs, the search costs more than every use it would license, and it usually ends with nothing found, which is the outcome it was always most likely to reach.
And the hole is measurable. A random sample of new books for sale on Amazon holds more titles from the 1880s than from the 1980s, though roughly fourteen times as many books were published in the later decade. The nineteenth century is available because it is out of copyright. The twentieth is missing because it is in.

## Stakes

Whether a right can be said to be working when it produces nothing for its holder, nothing for the person who wants to pay, and nothing for anybody else.
Every plot moves the same way: toward automatic protection, prudent advice and properly documented searching, and away from any instrument that could complete a transaction both sides would have wanted, because one side is not there to want anything. The production cannot make a register the treaty forbids, and it cannot ask an institution to accept unbounded liability for one reissue. What is left to fight over is not whether authors should be protected without paperwork, they should, but what is owed to a work that is preserved, wanted, and unusable, and whether a permission nobody can refuse is a permission at all.
