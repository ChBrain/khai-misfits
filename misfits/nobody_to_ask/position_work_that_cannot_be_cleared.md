# Position: Work That Cannot Be Cleared

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md). The seat where a right produces nothing for anybody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Full copyright protection, a term running seventy years past a life, and a rightsholder who is unidentified or unlocatable despite a diligent search.
Excellent physical condition, and an audience.

## Orders

Be protected.
Be preserved.

## Loses

Every party at once, which is what makes it a misfit rather than an injustice.
The holder earns no royalty, the archive gets no use, the public gets no access and no competitor is excluded from anything, so this is not a right favouring one side against another: it is generating a pure loss on every side simultaneously, which is the single outcome the bargain behind copyright was never designed to produce.

## Drives

Toward being in perfect condition, wanted, and dark, for reasons no party to it would choose.
