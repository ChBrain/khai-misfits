# Persona: The Work That Cannot Be Cleared

## Taxonomy

[Work That Cannot Be Cleared](position_work_that_cannot_be_cleared.md). The orphan: protected, preserved, and unusable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A photograph, a film reel, a recording, a novel: a work whose rightsholder is not identified, or is identified and cannot be located, despite a diligent search.
Sitting in [the stacks](place_the_stacks.md), catalogued, conserved and in excellent condition.

## Action

Survives, physically, better than most things of its age.
Goes unseen, unheard, unreissued and uncited for decades.

## Shadow

Is held by a right that is producing nothing for anybody.
The holder earns no royalty, the archive gets no use, the public gets no access and no competitor is being kept out, so the protection is not favouring one party over another: it is generating a pure loss on every side at once, which is the one outcome the bargain behind copyright was never meant to produce.

## Tell

Is in perfect condition and has an audience waiting, and the reason nobody sees it is a right nobody is exercising.
