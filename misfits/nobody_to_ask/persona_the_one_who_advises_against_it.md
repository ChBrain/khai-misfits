# Persona: The One Who Advises Against It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Advises Against It](position_one_who_advises_against_it.md). Counsel to the institution, pricing a risk with a hole in it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A lawyer advising a library, a museum or a broadcaster on whether to proceed without a licence.
Sympathetic to the project, and responsible for the institution.

## Action

Assesses the exposure: statutory damages that need no proof of loss, multiplied by the number of works.
Advises against, and the project stops.

## Shadow

Is right, and the rightness is what closes the door.
The claimant who might appear cannot be ruled out for precisely the reason the licence cannot be obtained, that nobody knows who they are: so the risk is not small and unquantified but unbounded and unquantifiable, and no responsible institution accepts an open liability across a collection in exchange for one reissue.

## Tell

Gives the advice that any competent lawyer would give, and it is the advice that keeps the work dark.
