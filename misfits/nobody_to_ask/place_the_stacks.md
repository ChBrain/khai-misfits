# Place: The Stacks

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md). Where the work is kept perfectly and shown to nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Climate control, acid-free boxes, a catalogue, conservators, and a reading room with a request slip.
An institution doing preservation as well as preservation has ever been done.

## Holds

[The Work That Cannot Be Cleared](persona_the_work_that_cannot_be_cleared.md), in better condition than most things its age.
[The Term](piece_the_term.md), running quietly over everything on the shelves.

## Offers

Survival, indefinitely, and a complete record of what exists and where it is.
Access, to anybody who asks, in the reading room, one item at a time.

## Withheld

Every use that would reach more than one person at a time.
The place can preserve a work and describe it and let a scholar look at it, and cannot digitise it, publish it, screen it or reissue it, so its competence at the hard problem, keeping the object alive for a century, is complete, and its power over the easy one, letting anybody see it, is nil.
