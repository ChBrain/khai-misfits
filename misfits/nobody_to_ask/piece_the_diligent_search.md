# Piece: The Diligent Search

## Taxonomy

[The Rights Desk](place_the_rights_desk.md). The ritual that qualifies for relief and costs more than the use.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Conducted at [the rights desk](place_the_rights_desk.md), per work, through registries, catalogues, trade sources and databases, and documented throughout.

## Load Bearing

It is the price of the exemption and it scales the wrong way.
A work counts as orphaned when no rightsholder is identified, or none is located despite a diligent search, so the search is what unlocks any relief at all: and because it must be done for each work separately, a box of fifty thousand photographs costs more to search than every licence those photographs would ever have earned.

## Apparent

A reasonable requirement, and a fair one: nobody should be able to declare a work orphaned without having genuinely looked.
Proportionate for a single film, and honestly intended.

## Yearbook

Designed around the case of one valuable work with one missing owner, which it handles well.
Applied to collections, where the unit of the problem is the box and the unit of the remedy is the item.
