# Plot: What the Search Costs

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A relief available only to those who have looked properly, and a box holding fifty thousand photographs.

## Action

[Searching diligently](process_searching_diligently.md): [the diligent search](piece_the_diligent_search.md) is run through registries, catalogues, trade directories, probate records and databases, documented item by item as required.

## Stage

The search establishes beyond doubt that the holders cannot be identified or located, which is the finding it was always most likely to reach.

## Tension

The remedy is measured in works and the problem is measured in collections.
The requirement is fair and proportionate for a single valuable film with one missing owner, which is the case it was designed around and handles well. Applied to a box, the per-item search costs more than every licence those items would ever have earned, so the exemption is technically available and economically unreachable, and doing the thing correctly is what makes it impossible.
