# Plot: Nobody Says No

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An archive that wants to digitise, a label that wants to reissue, a documentary that needs forty seconds of footage. Funded, willing, and prepared to pay whatever it costs.

## Action

[The One Who Would Have Paid](persona_the_one_who_would_have_paid.md), holding [One Who Would Have Paid](position_one_who_would_have_paid.md), attempts to clear the rights at [the rights desk](place_the_rights_desk.md), and finds nobody.

## Stage

The licence fee sits ready for whoever can be shown to be entitled to it. Nobody can be shown to be entitled to it.

## Tension

The transaction fails for want of a counterparty rather than for want of agreement.
This is not a negotiation that broke down, a price that could not be met or a refusal that could be appealed: the offer was never communicated, because there is nobody to communicate it to. So the single act that would resolve the matter for every party at once, an offer and an acceptance, has no second party anywhere in the world available to complete it, and the deadlock has no disagreement in it at all.
