# Plot: More From the 1880s Than the 1980s

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A random sample of new books offered for sale, counted by the decade in which they were first published.

## Action

The count is made, and the dip falls exactly where [The Work That Cannot Be Cleared](persona_the_work_that_cannot_be_cleared.md), holding [Work That Cannot Be Cleared](position_work_that_cannot_be_cleared.md), sits in [the stacks](place_the_stacks.md) with [the term](piece_the_term.md) still running over it. More titles are on sale from the 1880s than from the 1980s, though roughly fourteen times as many books were published in the later decade.

## Stage

The nineteenth century is available because it is out of copyright. The twentieth is missing because it is in.

## Tension

The hole is measurable, and it is in the middle rather than at the far end.
Age is not what removes a book from circulation: the oldest works are the most available, because anybody may reprint them without asking. What removes a book is a live right with nobody answering for it, so the curve dips exactly where protection still runs and the holders have gone. The orphaned works are not all of that dip, and publishers' own commercial choices account for part of it, but the shape of the loss follows copyright status rather than demand or decay.
