# Persona: The Rule Against Formalities

## Taxonomy

[Rule Against Formalities](position_rule_against_formalities.md). The provision that grants the right and forbids the register.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A treaty article stating that the enjoyment and exercise of these rights shall not be subject to any formality.
Protection attaching the moment a work is fixed: no form, no fee, no notice, no renewal.

## Action

Protects everybody automatically, including the careless, the poor and the unadvised.
Leaves no list anywhere of who holds what.

## Shadow

Cannot be repealed without reinstating the harm it was written against.
A registration requirement is a tax on attention, paid by exactly the authors least able to pay it, and the era before this provision is full of people who lost everything they had made by missing a renewal: so the absence of a register is not carelessness but the direct cost of a protection nobody sensible wants to withdraw.

## Tell

Is the most humane rule in the field, and the reason nothing in the field can be traced.
