# Plan: Let Them Use It

## Taxonomy

A **correction**: the second intent of [The One Who Would Have Paid](persona_the_one_who_would_have_paid.md), inside [Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md), once the deadlock is named.

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Would Have Paid](persona_the_one_who_would_have_paid.md)

## Direction

Make the use lawful without the owner. Cap the liability for those who searched in good faith, licence collectively on behalf of holders who cannot be asked, or bring back a register so the thread is not lost in the first place.

## Orders

(Legislate limited liability for good-faith users after a documented search; adopt extended collective licensing so a body may licence on behalf of holders who never joined it; or reinstate registration, which the treaty forbids as a condition of the right.)

## Implementation

([The Rights Desk](place_the_rights_desk.md), which would still have to run [the diligent search](piece_the_diligent_search.md) first; and [The Rule Against Formalities](persona_the_rule_against_formalities.md), which no correction here can amend.)

## Targets

- [ ] the price is accepted: capping liability leaves a residual risk and still requires the per-work search, so collections stay uneconomic to clear
- [ ] the price is accepted: collective licensing grants permission on behalf of an owner who never joined the body and never agreed to its terms
- [ ] the price is accepted: a register cannot be made a condition of the right, since the treaty forbids formalities, so any register is voluntary and the unlocatable do not join it
- [ ] the price is accepted: shortening the term would strip protection from living authors to release works nobody is claiming
