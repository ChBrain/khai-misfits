## Action

## Stage

[The Term](piece_the_term.md) runs seventy years past a life, outlasting every record that could have connected the work to a person.

## Tension

Nobody hides, and every step that broke the chain is an ordinary event.
A death, an inheritance, a company sale, a change of name: none is a breach of any duty, so there is no negligence to allege and no bad faith to expose. And the law has no way of treating absence differently from refusal, so a holder who would almost certainly have said yes produces exactly the same legal situation as one who said no, which is the whole of the difficulty and none of it anybody's doing.
