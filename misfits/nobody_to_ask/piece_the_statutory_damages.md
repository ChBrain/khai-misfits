# Piece: The Statutory Damages

## Taxonomy

[The Rights Desk](place_the_rights_desk.md). The exposure that needs no proof, against a claimant who cannot be identified.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the advice given at [the rights desk](place_the_rights_desk.md), and in every decision not to proceed.

## Load Bearing

It converts an unknown owner into an unbounded liability.
Damages that do not depend on proving any actual loss are a sound answer to infringement that is hard to quantify, which is a real problem: and they mean the institution cannot bound its exposure by asking what the use is worth, while the person who might claim them cannot be ruled out for exactly the reason the licence cannot be sought.

## Apparent

A standard remedy, present in many systems, and defensible: without it, small rightsholders could not enforce anything.
Aimed squarely at commercial infringers, which is what it was written for.

## Yearbook

Written against a defendant who knew whose work they were taking, and applied to one who could not have found out.
Multiplies by the number of works, so it is mildest for the single infringement and harshest for the archive acting in good faith across a collection.
