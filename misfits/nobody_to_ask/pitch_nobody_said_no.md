# Pitch: Nobody Said No

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Careful and archival. A protection granted automatically to every author however poor or unadvised, an institution acting well within the law, a lawyer giving correct advice about genuine exposure, a search conducted properly and documented.
Everything humane, everything prudent, everything defensible.

## Undertow

Under the care, a permission that cannot be sought, because the treaty that spared authors the paperwork also spared the system any record of them.
A term running seventy years past a life, against an address book that lasts a decade.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a lock with no key and no keyholder, on a door nobody ever refused to open.

## Echo

Afterward: a box of fifty thousand photographs preserved perfectly and shown to nobody, a search that costs more than every licence it would buy, and more new books on sale from the 1880s than from the 1980s.
