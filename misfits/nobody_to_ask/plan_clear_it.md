# Plan: Clear It

## Taxonomy

A **professional method**: the intent of [The One Who Would Have Paid](persona_the_one_who_would_have_paid.md), inside [Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Would Have Paid](persona_the_one_who_would_have_paid.md)

## Direction

Find the rightsholder and pay them. Do the search properly, record it, and licence the work at whatever it costs, because the project is funded and the use harms nobody.

## Orders

(Run [the diligent search](piece_the_diligent_search.md) at [the rights desk](place_the_rights_desk.md), through registries, catalogues, trade sources, probate records and databases; document it per work; make the offer to whoever is found.)

## Implementation

([The Rights Desk](place_the_rights_desk.md); the [searching diligently](process_searching_diligently.md) process; real expertise, a real budget and a genuine willingness to pay.)

## Targets

- [ ] every route to a rightsholder that anyone has devised is applied competently
- [ ] the search is documented per work, as the relief requires
- [ ] the finding is reached beyond doubt: the holder cannot be identified or located
- [ ] the finding unlocks nothing, because there is nobody to make the offer to
