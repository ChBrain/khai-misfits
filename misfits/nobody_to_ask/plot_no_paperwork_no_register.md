# Plot: No Paperwork, No Register

## Taxonomy

[Nobody to Ask](play_nobody_to_ask.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A treaty article providing that the enjoyment and exercise of these rights shall not be subject to any formality.

## Action

[Protecting without paperwork](process_protecting_without_paperwork.md): [The Rule Against Formalities](persona_the_rule_against_formalities.md), holding [Rule Against Formalities](position_rule_against_formalities.md), attaches protection at the moment of fixation, with no form, fee, notice or renewal.

## Stage

Every author is protected, including the careless, the poor and the unadvised, and none of them has to do anything.

## Tension

The absence of a register is not an oversight, it is the price of the protection.
A registration requirement is a tax on attention, paid hardest by exactly the authors least able to pay it, and the century before this rule is full of people who lost everything they made by missing a renewal. So there is nothing here to fix: the most humane provision in the field is the same provision that leaves the system with no list of who holds what, and any correction that reinstates the list reinstates the harm.
