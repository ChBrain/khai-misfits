## Offers

The only route between a stopped plant and a running one, in either direction.

## Withheld

The protection that is in service everywhere else.
The place is where the design's specificity turns from a virtue into an absence. Trips set against operating conditions would fire continuously here, so they are deliberately overridden; instruments calibrated for a range are outside it; the mixture occupies compositions the plant is never meant to hold. None of that is an oversight, and all of it is assessed and written down. It is simply that this is the one place the plant goes where the thing built to protect it was built for somewhere else, and it goes there twice for every campaign it runs.
