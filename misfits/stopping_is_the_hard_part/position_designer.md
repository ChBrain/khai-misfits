# Position: Designer

## Taxonomy

[Stopping Is the Hard Part](play_stopping_is_the_hard_part.md). The seat whose specificity makes the plant work and makes the transition an outside.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A throughput target, an efficiency target, and an envelope to draw.

## Orders

Design for the operating point. Specify the equipment and the settings against it.

## Loses

Any coverage of the states between off and on.
A plant equally instrumented across every state it could occupy would be more expensive, less efficient and more complex, and the complexity would carry hazards of its own.

## Drives

Toward a controllable plant, which is a plant designed around a point.
