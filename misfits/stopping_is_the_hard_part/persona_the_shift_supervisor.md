# Persona: The Shift Supervisor

## Taxonomy

[Shift Supervisor](position_shift_supervisor.md). The one whose cautious decision is the dangerous operation.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A supervisor at three in the morning with a reading that is not right, a plant running, and the authority to stop it.

## Action

Decides.
Calls a controlled shutdown when something is wrong, which is what the seat exists to be able to do.

## Shadow

Holds an authority whose exercise is the riskiest thing available.
The seat is not hesitating out of weakness: both options are genuinely bad. Continuing to run a plant it is worried about is indefensible if the worry is right. Shutting down moves the plant deliberately out of the state everything is specified for, through the range the design has least to say about, with overrides active and manual actions multiplied, at three in the morning with a night shift. The seat cannot get the plant to a safe condition without passing through the least safe one, because a stopped plant is on the far side of the transition.

## Tell

Does the responsible thing, which is the operation that hurts people.
