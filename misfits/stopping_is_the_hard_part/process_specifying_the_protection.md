# Process: Specifying the Protection

## Taxonomy

[Stopping Is the Hard Part](play_stopping_is_the_hard_part.md). Setting the trips against the state the plant is meant to hold.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An operating point, and a hazard study.
[The Control Engineer](persona_the_control_engineer.md), holding [Control Engineer](position_control_engineer.md), at [the steady state](place_the_steady_state.md).

## Direction

Toward protection that fires when the plant leaves where it should be.

## Lever

Trip settings and interlock logic, chosen against the operating conditions.

## Echo

Protection that is exact at the operating point and is about the operating point.
