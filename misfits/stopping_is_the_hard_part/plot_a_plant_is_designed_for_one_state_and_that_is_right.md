# Plot: A Plant Is Designed for One State, and That Is Right

## Taxonomy

[Stopping Is the Hard Part](play_stopping_is_the_hard_part.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A process to be built, and an operating point to build it around.

## Action

[The Designer](persona_the_designer.md) fixes the point and sizes everything to it, producing [the design envelope](piece_the_design_envelope.md).

## Stage

The decision that makes process control tractable.

## Tension

This is not a compromise anybody should be talked out of, and it is where the mechanism begins.
Equipment is sized to a throughput, instruments are ranged to the conditions they will see, control loops are tuned to a point. A plant specified to be equally controllable and equally protected across every state it could ever occupy would cost far more, run less efficiently, and carry the hazards its own additional complexity introduced. Designing around a point is what makes the plant work at all, and everything that follows follows from having done it well.
