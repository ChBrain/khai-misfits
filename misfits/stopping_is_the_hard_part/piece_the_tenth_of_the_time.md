## Owner

## Place

## Load Bearing

The concentration is measured rather than argued, and its size is what makes this a structure rather than an impression.
A refining or petrochemical facility spends under a tenth of its time in transient operations, and more than half of process safety incidents occur during them; a study of five hundred incidents established the proportion, and separate work has put incidents around five times more frequent during start-up than during normal running. The ratio is not a claim about any plant or any operator. It is the shape of where in the operating year the harm sits, and it sits in the part the design has least to say about.
