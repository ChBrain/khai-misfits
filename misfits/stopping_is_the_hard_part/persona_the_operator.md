# Persona: The Operator

## Taxonomy

[Operator](position_operator.md). The one performing the sequence, rarely, with the most to do.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A panel operator taking a cold plant up, with a long procedure, many manual steps and a handful of these a year.

## Action

Runs the sequence.
Opens, closes, watches, holds, waits for a temperature, and does it in the order written.

## Shadow

Does the most, with the least practice, in the state with the least protection.
The seat is neither careless nor undertrained in any ordinary sense: it follows an approved procedure written and assessed for exactly this. What is against it is arithmetic. A transition demands many times the manual actions of an hour at steady state, so even at an unchanged rate of error per action the exposure rises with the count; and the seat performs this a few times a year rather than continuously, so it is least practised at the thing that asks most of it. Neither of those is a failing that more care removes.

## Tell

Acts more often, more rarely, with less watching over it.
