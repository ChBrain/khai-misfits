# Position: Shift Supervisor

## Taxonomy

[Stopping Is the Hard Part](play_stopping_is_the_hard_part.md). The seat whose authority is dangerous to exercise.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A reading that is not right, a running plant, and the power to stop it.

## Orders

Decide. Stop it if you are worried.

## Loses

A safe option.
Running on with a worry is indefensible if the worry is right, and stopping moves the plant deliberately through the state with least protection.

## Drives

Toward a safe condition, reachable only across the unsafe one.
