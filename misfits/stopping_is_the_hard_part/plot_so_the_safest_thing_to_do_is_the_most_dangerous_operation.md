# Plot: So the Safest Thing to Do Is the Most Dangerous Operation

## Taxonomy

[Stopping Is the Hard Part](play_stopping_is_the_hard_part.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A plant coming down, and a fence line beyond it.

## Action

[The Tenth of the Time](piece_the_tenth_of_the_time.md) is where it lands, and [the neighbour](persona_the_neighbour.md) is over the fence, unaware which night it is.

## Stage

A safety response that is the hazard, with nobody having erred.

## Tension

Every seat performed correctly and the outcome is the sum of the correct performances.
The designer's operating point is what makes the plant controllable. The control engineer's trips inherit that point because a trip must be set against something, and the overrides are engineered and governed because a plant without them cannot start. The operator follows an approved sequence and is exposed by the count of actions and the rarity of the task rather than by anything it could do differently. The supervisor stops the plant, which is what a supervisor is for. And the neighbour carries a risk concentrated into a tenth of the year with no way to know which nights those are. What is left is not a fault to allocate but a response that is correct and is the riskiest hour of the year.
