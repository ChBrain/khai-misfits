# Persona: The Neighbour

## Taxonomy

[Neighbour](position_neighbour.md). The one over the fence, party to none of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Somebody living beyond the fence line, who knows the plant is there and nothing else about it.

## Action

Nothing.
The seat takes no decision, holds no information, and is not consulted about when the plant runs or stops.

## Shadow

Carries a risk that peaks on a schedule nobody tells them.
The seat has no part in the design, the procedure, the override logbook or the shutdown decision, and no way to know that the hours when the plant is coming down are the hours that matter to it. It is not being kept in the dark by anybody: there is no mechanism by which a plant announces its transitions to a fence line, and nothing the seat could do with the information if it had it. The exposure is real, is concentrated into a small fraction of the year, and is invisible from where the seat stands.

## Tell

Bears the peak and is never told when it is.
