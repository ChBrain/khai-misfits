# Persona: The Designer

## Taxonomy

[Designer](position_designer.md). The one who builds it around an operating point.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A process engineer sizing vessels, selecting instruments and setting an envelope, against a throughput and an efficiency target.

## Action

Designs for the operating point.
Specifies the equipment, the control range and the protective settings against the conditions the plant is meant to hold.

## Shadow

Makes the plant possible by making it specific, and specificity is what the transition falls outside of.
The seat is not being narrow: a plant designed to be equally instrumented, equally controllable and equally protected across every state it could ever occupy would be enormously more expensive, less efficient and harder to run, and its extra complexity would carry hazards of its own. Designing around a point is what makes process control work. What follows from it is that every state which is not that point is a state the design has less to say about, and the plant must pass through those states twice for every campaign it runs.

## Tell

Specifies well, and the specification is what the transition is outside.
