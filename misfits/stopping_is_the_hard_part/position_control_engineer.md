# Position: Control Engineer

## Taxonomy

[Stopping Is the Hard Part](play_stopping_is_the_hard_part.md). The seat that removes protection by design because there is no other way.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Trip settings, interlock logic, and a start-up sequence to write.

## Orders

Set the trips for the operating conditions. Design the overrides. Assess, log, time-limit, review.

## Loses

The option of a start-up with everything in service.
A trip specified against steady state fires continuously on a rising temperature, so a plant with no start-up overrides cannot be started at all.

## Drives

Toward a startable plant, protected everywhere except while starting.
