# Plot: And the Interlocks Are Bypassed by Procedure, Correctly

## Taxonomy

[Stopping Is the Hard Part](play_stopping_is_the_hard_part.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A trip that would fire continuously on a rising temperature.

## Action

[The Authorised Bypass](piece_the_authorised_bypass.md) is applied: designed in, hazard-assessed, written into the approved procedure, scoped, annunciated, time-limited and logged.

## Stage

A designed feature with its own governance, which is the protection switched off.

## Tension

The profession's answer here is thorough and it does not change what is true.
A trip specified against steady-state conditions fires continuously during a start-up, so a plant with no overrides cannot be started at all, and pretending otherwise would produce a plant nobody could run or a set of trips everybody defeated informally. So the overrides are engineered, reviewed through hazard study, approved in the procedure, limited to the specific interlock, annunciated, time-bounded and reviewed at handover. All of that is good and none of it puts the protection back in service during the hours it is out.
