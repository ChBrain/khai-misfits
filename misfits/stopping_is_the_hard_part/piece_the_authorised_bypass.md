## Load Bearing

It is not a violation and not a workaround: it is a designed feature with its own governance, and it is still the protection switched off.
A trip specified against steady-state conditions fires continuously on a rising temperature or a part-filled vessel, so a plant with no start-up overrides cannot be started. The profession's answer is thorough: overrides are designed in and reviewed through hazard study, written into the approved operating procedure, scoped to the specific interlock, annunciated, time-limited and reviewed at handover. All of that is good practice and none of it changes the fact that during the operation which carries most of the risk, some of the plant's protection is deliberately not in service.

## Apparent

A lapse in discipline, of the sort a stricter regime would end.

## Yearbook

Is a protection correctly disabled because it was specified for somewhere the plant is not.
