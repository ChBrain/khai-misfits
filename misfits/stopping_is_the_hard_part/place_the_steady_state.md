## Withheld

Any way to stay here.
The place is the argument for the design and it is also the whole difficulty, because it cannot be occupied continuously. Catalysts are changed, vessels are inspected, campaigns end, feedstocks change and things go wrong, so a plant leaves this state and returns to it repeatedly, and every departure and every return is a passage through somewhere else. Nothing here is a warning sign: the steadiness is real, the protection is genuinely in service, and the place is exactly as safe as it looks.
