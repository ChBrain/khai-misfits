# Play: Stopping Is the Hard Part

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Stopping Is the Hard Part.
A process plant is designed to run at an operating point, and everything that protects it is specified for that point: the instruments, the alarm settings, the trips, the interlocks. That is right, and a plant designed to be equally safe in every conceivable state would be a different and worse plant. But nothing gets from off to on without passing through everything in between, and in between is where the mixture is in its explosive range, the vessel is at an intermediate pressure and the trips would fire continuously if they were not deliberately overridden.

## Arc

The bend is this: the protection is specified for the state the plant runs in, so the act of leaving that state is the act performed with least of it.
It begins with a design choice nobody disputes.
[A Plant Is Designed for One State, and That Is Right](plot_a_plant_is_designed_for_one_state_and_that_is_right.md). [The Design Envelope](piece_the_design_envelope.md) is what makes a plant controllable and efficient at all.
And the protection is specified for that state too.
[And the Protection Is Specified for That State Too](plot_and_the_protection_is_specified_for_that_state_too.md). The trips and interlocks are set against the conditions the plant is meant to hold.
So getting there passes through states it is not designed for.
[So Getting There Passes Through States It Is Not Designed For](plot_so_getting_there_passes_through_states_it_is_not_designed_for.md). [The Transition](place_the_transition.md) is unavoidable, because there is no route from off to on that skips the middle.
And [the interlocks are bypassed by procedure, correctly](plot_and_the_interlocks_are_bypassed_by_procedure_correctly.md).
[The Authorised Bypass](piece_the_authorised_bypass.md) is designed in, assessed, logged and time-limited, and it is still the protection being switched off.
Then [the response to a worry is a shutdown](plot_the_response_to_a_worry_is_a_shutdown.md). [The Shift Supervisor](persona_the_shift_supervisor.md) must decide, and the safe-looking decision moves the plant into its worst mode.
And [the safest thing to do is the most dangerous operation](plot_so_the_safest_thing_to_do_is_the_most_dangerous_operation.md). [The Neighbour](persona_the_neighbour.md) is over the fence and has no part in any of it.

## Stakes

Whether a plant can be stopped by the people responsible for stopping it without that being the most dangerous thing they do.
Every plot moves the same way: toward an operating point that is what makes a plant controllable, a protection specified against that point, a traversal that cannot be skipped, an override that is designed in and assessed and is still a protection removed, a cautious decision that is a transition, and a fence line with somebody behind it, and away from any account in which a procedure was sloppy or an operator was careless. The production cannot fault the designer, whose single operating point is the reason the plant works at all; and it cannot fault the supervisor, whose alternative to shutting down is to keep running a plant they are worried about. What is left to fight over is not whether anybody erred but what to call a response that is correct and is the riskiest hour of the year.
