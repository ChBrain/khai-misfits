# Piece: The Design Envelope

## Taxonomy

[The Steady State](place_the_steady_state.md). The state everything is specified for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Drawn by [specifying the protection](process_specifying_the_protection.md), and departed from at [the transition](place_the_transition.md).

## Load Bearing

Its narrowness is what makes the plant work, and the same narrowness is what the transition falls outside.
Equipment is sized to a throughput, instruments are ranged to the conditions they will see, control loops are tuned to a point, and trips are set against the state the plant is meant to hold. A plant specified to be equally controllable and equally protected across every state it could occupy would cost far more, run less efficiently and carry the hazards its own extra complexity brought. So designing around a point is not a compromise anybody should be talked out of: it is the thing that makes process control tractable.

## Apparent

A limitation, of the sort a more thorough design would remove.

## Yearbook

Is the reason the plant is safe where it lives and has less to say about the way in and the way out.
