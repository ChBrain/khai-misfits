# Plot: So Getting There Passes Through States It Is Not Designed For

## Taxonomy

[Stopping Is the Hard Part](play_stopping_is_the_hard_part.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A cold plant, and a sequence to bring it up.

## Action

At [the transition](place_the_transition.md), the plant occupies intermediate pressures, rising temperatures and mixtures inside their flammable range.

## Stage

A traversal that is unavoidable rather than badly planned.

## Tension

There is no route from off to on that skips the middle, and the middle is somewhere the plant is never meant to sit.
A mixture that is safe lean and safe rich passes through the range between them on the way. A vessel that is safe empty and safe full is at an intermediate level in between. A catalyst safe cold and safe hot goes through every temperature separating them. None of that can be designed away, because it is not a design choice: it is what changing state means. And a plant does it twice for every campaign it runs, plus every time anything goes wrong.
