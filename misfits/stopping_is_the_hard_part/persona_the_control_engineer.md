# Persona: The Control Engineer

## Taxonomy

[Control Engineer](position_control_engineer.md). The one who designs the overrides that switch the protection off.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An engineer setting trip points and interlock logic, and writing the start-up overrides into the sequence.

## Action

Sets the trips for the operating conditions.
Designs the start-up overrides, has them assessed, and writes them into the approved procedure with logging and time limits.

## Shadow

Removes protection by design, correctly, and there is no other way to start the plant.
The seat is not weakening anything improperly: a trip specified against steady-state conditions fires continuously on a rising temperature or a part-filled vessel, so a plant with no start-up overrides cannot be started at all. The overrides are designed in, reviewed through hazard study, approved in the operating procedure, annunciated, time-limited, scoped to the specific interlock and reviewed at handover, which is the profession's answer and is a good one. It remains true that during the riskiest operation the plant performs, some of its protection is deliberately not in service.

## Tell

Switches it off properly, because there is no proper way to leave it on.
