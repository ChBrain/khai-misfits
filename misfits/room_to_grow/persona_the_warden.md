# Persona: The Warden

## Taxonomy

[Warden](position_warden.md), inside [Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The archetype of the population's own density-dependence, the competition and crowding and cannibalism that hold the number below its ceiling, the brake whose grip the cull relaxes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A cruel hand starving its own, as though the regulation were a malice, when it is only the impersonal working of a limited food supply and a crowded range on a population too dense for them.

## Action

Hold them down. At [the Range](place_the_range.md), the Warden on [Hold Them Down](plan_hold_them_down.md) through [Regulating](process_regulating.md) holds [the Crowding](piece_the_crowding.md), pressing the number below the ceiling through the competition for food, the disease that moves in a dense crowd, and the adults that eat the young (Ricker; Nicholson).

## Shadow

Its grip is no malice, and it is the grip the cull unwittingly loosens. The Warden is not cruel: it is the impersonal working of a habitat, a food supply that only stretches so far, a density at which disease travels, a stock of adults that eat the young, and it presses hardest exactly when the population is densest, holding the number below what the range could briefly feed. It intends nothing and holds no one down on purpose; it is simply what a population does to itself when it is too many. And its grip is the thing the cull relaxes: thin the crowd and the competition eases, the disease slows, the cannibals dwindle, so the very force that held the number down is loosened by the deaths meant to lower it. It keeps its impersonal working and its honest pressure, and loses no cause for blame, the self-regulation whose easing gives the survivors their room.

## Tell

It held the number down by the crowd's own hunger, and the cull that thinned the crowd is the cull that set its grip loose.
