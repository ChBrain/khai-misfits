# Plan: Hold Them Down

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the warden's seat](position_warden.md), inside [Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Warden](position_warden.md)

## Direction

Regulate the number below the ceiling through the competition for a limited food, the disease that moves in a dense crowd, and the adults that eat the young, pressing hardest exactly when the population is densest. Know that you hold no one down on purpose, that you are only what a population does to itself when it is too many, and that your grip is the thing the cull relaxes, loosened by the very deaths meant to lower the number.

## Orders

([The Warden](persona_the_warden.md) holds [the Crowding](piece_the_crowding.md) through [Regulating](process_regulating.md) at [the Range](place_the_range.md), pressing the number below the ceiling by the crowd's own hunger and press.)

## Implementation

([Regulating](process_regulating.md) at [the Range](place_the_range.md) holds [the Crowding](piece_the_crowding.md), because the density-dependent forces press hardest when the crowd is dense, so the number is held below the ceiling and the grip eases the moment the crowd is thinned.)

## Targets

- [ ] the number is held below the ceiling by competition, disease, and cannibalism
- [ ] the density-dependence is understood to press hardest when the crowd is densest
- [ ] it is seen that the grip is no malice but the plain working of a limited habitat
