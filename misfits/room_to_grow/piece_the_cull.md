# Piece: The Cull

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The mechanism: the good-faith removal of a fraction of the population, meant only to subtract.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Range](place_the_range.md), carried by [Culling](process_culling.md), voiced by [the Culler](persona_the_culler.md): a tag on a clipboard, a quota filled, a truck loaded and driven off the range with fewer animals in it than were counted that morning.

## Load Bearing

The cull is a harvest or a poison or a removal, applied by the plain arithmetic that to kill some is to leave fewer, an arithmetic true of nearly every population under management. Nothing in that arithmetic is wrong: the count on the range does drop by the number taken. But the deaths meant only to subtract also fall on the crowded, self-limiting stage of the population, and in doing so they relax the same density-dependence that had been holding the numbers down (Zipkin, Kraft, Cooch and Sullivan, on removals of nuisance and invasive fish that raised abundance rather than lowering it). The cull is load bearing because it is the only intervention in the piece: everything that follows is not a second act by anyone, only the population's own regulation answering the subtraction.

## Apparent

It reads as a plain subtraction from the number, no different in kind from removing any other fixed quantity from a fixed total, so that the count after should simply be the count before, minus the take.
