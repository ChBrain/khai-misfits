# Piece: The Overcompensation

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The mechanism: the survivors' own response to eased pressure, producing more than the cull removed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Thinned](place_the_thinned.md), carried by [Filling](process_filling.md), voiced by [the Survivor](persona_the_survivor.md): a mother in better condition than last season, a litter that all lived past its first weeks, young that found food where a rival used to stand.

## Load Bearing

Each survivor does only what any organism does when the pressure that had been limiting it eases: it eats better, so it breeds more, and its young survive at a higher rate than young born into the crowded range before the cull. Summed across every survivor, this response produces more net recruitment than the number the cull removed, the classical overcompensatory pattern in stock-recruitment curves, where a thinned population's output exceeds the unthinned one (Abrams; Ricker). No survivor is doing anything unusual, and no survivor knows what any other survivor is doing; each is answering the room made around it, on its own account. The overcompensation is load bearing because it is the step where individual responses, harmless and separate, become a population-level surplus.

## Apparent

It reads as a mere recovery, the population climbing back toward its old, normal size, the ordinary and expected refilling of a gap that was always going to close.
