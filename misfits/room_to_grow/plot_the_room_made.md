# Plot: The Room Made

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Thinned](place_the_thinned.md), the same ground the cull left behind now carries fewer bodies than it did the morning before.

## Action

At [the Thinned](place_the_thinned.md), [the Survivor](persona_the_survivor.md) from [the survivor's seat](position_survivor.md) on [Fill the Room](plan_fill_the_room.md) through [Filling](process_filling.md) takes up [the Room Made](piece_the_room_made.md), the food and space and safety the cull's deaths have freed.

## Stage

Once the crowd is thinned, the same forces that had been suppressing the population reverse their sign. Food that had been contested now goes spare, ground that had been packed dense enough to carry disease now holds fewer bodies across the same stretch, and adults that would have displaced or eaten the young are fewer, so more young live past the season's start (Abrams). Nothing has been added to the range and its ceiling has not moved: what has changed is only the population's distance from that ceiling, wider now than it was the morning of the cull. This gap is not yet spent by anyone. It is only there, standing open, food and cover and safety with no claim on them yet. No villain: the room is not made on purpose, not by the Survivor and not by anyone else. It is the population's own density-dependence easing as the crowd it depended on for its grip grows thinner, the same mechanism that held the number down now running, unbidden, in reverse.

## Tension

The gap that will not simply refill to where it was, only wait to be overfilled.
