# Place: The Thinned

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The same range after the cull, its density relaxed, where the survivors find room and the count can run past where it started. Hosts [Fewer Bodies on the Ground](plot_fewer_bodies_on_the_ground.md), [The Thriving](plot_the_thriving.md), [Counted Again After the Cull](plot_counted_again_after_the_cull.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Fewer bodies on the same ground: less competition per survivor, more food and shelter to go around, the pressure that had held the population down now measurably eased.

## Holds

[The Room Made](piece_the_room_made.md).
The food, space and safety the cull has left behind, freed for whoever remains.

[The Overcompensation](piece_the_overcompensation.md).
The survivors' response to that room: eating better, breeding more, losing fewer young than the crowded range allowed.

[The Rebound](piece_the_rebound.md).
The summed result, a population climbing back past the number the cull removed it from.

## Offers

To the survivor, the Thinned offers exactly what the crowding had been withholding: uncontested food, open shelter, young that live instead of being eaten. Nothing here is offered on purpose; it is only what the range looks like once its own regulating pressure is relaxed.

## Withheld

The Thinned withholds any memory of the count the range carried before the cull, so nothing in the scene itself signals that the response is outrunning the removal. That comparison is made elsewhere, at the long count.
