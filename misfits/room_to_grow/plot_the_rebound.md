# Plot: The Rebound

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Thinned](place_the_thinned.md), the season closes and the count is taken again, on the same ground and by the same method as before the cull.

## Action

At [the Thinned](place_the_thinned.md), [the Survivor](persona_the_survivor.md) from [the survivor's seat](position_survivor.md) through [Filling](process_filling.md) meets [the Rebound](piece_the_rebound.md), the summed total of every survivor's own eating and breeding and surviving young.

## Stage

Summed across every survivor's own eating and breeding and surviving young, the population read at the season's end is not merely recovered but risen, more numerous than the count taken the day the cull began (Zipkin, Kraft, Cooch and Sullivan). No single survivor produced this outcome and none can be pointed to as its cause: it is a property of the total, invisible in any one animal and plain only when the count before is set against the count after. The removal that took a fraction of the population has left, on the same ground and by the same method of counting, a larger population than it started with, control backfiring not through any error in the cull but through the population's own regulation, first relaxed and then overcompensated (Abrams). No villain: no one raised the number. The Culler removed a fraction in good faith and the survivors thrived in the room that removal made, and the rise is nothing more than those two honest acts, summed.

## Tension

The number raised by the very mortality meant to lower it, visible only in the total and in no single act inside it.
