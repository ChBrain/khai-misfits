# Position: Ecologist

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The seat of the one who reads the net across the whole population, and marks that the rebound is real and its reason plain, and that the effect holds under specific conditions and is not a law.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A view of the total, the net across the whole population that the single removal cannot see.
A clear reading of the reason, the mortality falling on a crowded stage whose density-dependence overcompensated when it was relieved.
A held bound, the knowledge that the effect needs that overcompensating regulation and that placement of the mortality, so it holds under specific conditions and not everywhere.

## Orders

Read the numbers, and name the rebound and its conditions.
Count the total rather than the single death, name the rebound the culler cannot see, and trace it to the overcompensating density-dependence and the mortality on the self-limiting stage that produced it.
Know that the effect needs those conditions, that most culls reduce the population exactly as they intend, and that to declare culling always backfires is to over-read the case into a law it is not.

## Loses

Nothing it can name, and holds a truth it must not overstate.
Because it reads the total and names the rebound rightly, the seat takes no loss, and yet it carries the harder discipline: to name a real effect while holding it to the conditions it requires, since most culls lower the number as meant and the backfire is not a general law.
It keeps its true reading and its held bound, and loses no cause for blame, the one who names a real rebound and will not make it a rule.

## Drives

Toward the one who reads the net and holds it to its conditions,
counting the total the single death cannot see and naming the rebound and the density-dependence that made it,
so the seat that alone reads the whole holds even its own reading to a bound, a real effect named and its scope kept, not a law that culling always backfires.
