# Plot: The Thriving

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Thinned](place_the_thinned.md), the room the cull made does not sit empty for long.

## Action

At [the Thinned](place_the_thinned.md), [the Survivor](persona_the_survivor.md) from [the survivor's seat](position_survivor.md) through [Filling](process_filling.md) meets [the Overcompensation](piece_the_overcompensation.md), eating better in the room the cull made, breeding more, and losing fewer young to a crowd that once ate them.

## Stage

Each survivor answers only the room around it. Food that is no longer contested lets it carry young to term more often, and fewer adults pressing the same ground means its young live past an age the crowded range would have taken them. Summed across every survivor doing this on its own account, the response outproduces the cull, more recruits entering the population than the cull removed from it, the overcompensatory pattern first shown in stock-recruitment curves and in blowfly populations thinned below their crowded state (Abrams; Ricker). No survivor knows what any other survivor is doing, and none is answering anything beyond the food and space and safety directly in front of it. No villain: the survivors scheme at nothing. They do only what any creature does when the pressure that had been limiting it lifts, eating what is available and breeding at the rate that availability supports, with no plan to outproduce a cull none of them can perceive as a cull.

## Tension

The blameless thriving that undoes the cull, one survivor's ordinary year at a time.
