# Piece: The Room Made

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The mechanism: the density-dependent brake relaxed by the cull's deaths, the pressure of the crowd eased before anyone has bred a single new animal.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Thinned](place_the_thinned.md), carried by [Filling](process_filling.md), voiced by [the Survivor](persona_the_survivor.md): open ground at the feeding line where a body used to stand, a stretch of cover with no rival scent in it, a season with no outbreak moving through the group.

## Load Bearing

Once the cull has thinned the crowd, the same forces that had been suppressing the population reverse their sign. Food and space and safety, all scarce when the range was full, are freed as the crowd is thinned: fewer mouths compete at the food, fewer bodies pack the ground disease needs to travel, fewer adults remain to displace or consume the young (Abrams). Nothing new has been added to the range; the ceiling has not moved. What has changed is that the population is now further below its own brake than it was before the cull, and that gap is the room the piece is named for. The room made is load bearing because it is the precise quantity that the survivors will spend: how much room is made sets how far the population can be carried past its old mark.

## Apparent

It reads as a temporary gap, the kind any harvest leaves, expected to refill only back up to the old level and stop there, since that was the level the range was said to support.
