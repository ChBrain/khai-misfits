# Pitch: Kill Some, Get More

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a cull can raise the number it was meant to lower, because the killing relaxes the crowding that had held the population down.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Cool and clinical, the arithmetic of a population that regulates itself. A cull made in good faith by the plain logic that killing some leaves fewer, and a number that rises for it. Nothing here is a blunder or a monster growing heads; the removal is sound and the survivors do only what any creature does when the pressure eases.

## Undertow

A population is held below its ceiling by its own density, the competition for food, the crowding that spreads disease, the adults that eat the young (Ricker; Nicholson). Cull a fraction and the brake relaxes: fewer mouths, fewer cannibals, less disease, so the survivors eat better and breed more, and if the release outweighs the removal the population rebounds past where it began (Abrams; Zipkin and colleagues).

## Nerve

That a population held down by its own regulation can be raised by killing part of it, since the cull that removes the animals also removes the crowding that held them back. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since the effect needs overcompensating density-dependence and mortality on the crowded stage, real under those conditions and not a law that culling always backfires (Abrams).

## Echo

Thin the crowd that was starving itself, and feed the ones you left, and count more than you began with.
