# Process: Culling

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). Borne at [the Range](place_the_range.md), precipitated by [the Culler](persona_the_culler.md), in [The Culling](plot_the_culling.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Culler, reading a count judged too high against the range's ceiling and acting on the plain arithmetic that removing some lowers the number.

## Direction

From the number judged too high toward the fraction removed.

## Lever

To remove a fraction is, by arithmetic true of nearly every population, to lower the number: the Culler intends nothing beyond that subtraction, but the same deaths that remove bodies also remove the crowding those bodies were producing, so a cull meant only to subtract also relaxes a brake it never meant to touch (Zipkin, Kraft, Cooch and Sullivan 2009).
