# Plot: The Held Down

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Range](place_the_range.md), the herd sits at the size it has always sat at, no fence around it and no hand pressing down, and still it does not grow.

## Action

At [the Range](place_the_range.md), [the Warden](persona_the_warden.md) from [the warden's seat](position_warden.md) on [Hold Them Down](plan_hold_them_down.md) through [Regulating](process_regulating.md) takes up [the Crowding](piece_the_crowding.md), pressing the number below the ceiling the range could otherwise feed.

## Stage

The population sits below what the range could carry, and it sits there by its own doing. Competition for a limited food thins the animals at the margin of the feeding ground, disease moves faster and further through a dense press of bodies than through a sparse one, and adults that displace or eat the young when numbers run high take a share of every season's newborns (Ricker; Nicholson). These forces are density-dependent: they tighten as the population rises and loosen as it falls, so the same range yields fewer survivors per head when it is full than when it is nearly empty. Nothing outside the herd is holding it down: no predator brought in, no fence built, no hand on any single animal, only the crowd's own numbers acting on the crowd. No villain: the Warden holds no one down on purpose. It is only the impersonal working of a limited habitat on a population too dense for it, hunger and disease and cannibalism doing to this crowd what they would do to any crowd this size, with no intention behind them and no one to answer for them.

## Tension

The number held down by the crowd's own hunger, a brake that presses only because the crowd is dense enough to feel it.
