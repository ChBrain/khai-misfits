# Plot: When It Holds

## Cue

At [the Long Count](place_the_long_count.md), the rebound sits plain in the record, and the question left is when, and only when, it comes.

## Action

At [the Long Count](place_the_long_count.md), [the Ecologist](persona_the_ecologist.md) from [the ecologist's seat](position_ecologist.md) on [Read the Numbers](plan_read_the_numbers.md) through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) takes up [the Condition](piece_the_condition.md), naming the overcompensating density-dependence and the mortality on the self-limiting stage the rebound requires.

## Stage

The rebound is real, but it is not unconditional. It requires overcompensating density-dependence, a population whose net production bends back on itself so that a thinned stock yields more recruits than an unthinned one, and it requires that the mortality fall on the crowded, self-limiting stage where that overcompensation operates (Abrams). Where either is missing, where the density-dependence merely compensates rather than overcompensates, or the cull strikes a stage the crowding does not regulate, the population falls as the cull intended, which is the far more common case. The Ecologist reads across the whole population and the whole record, not any single cull, and holds the effect to the conditions the record actually supports. No villain: the Culler followed a logic sound for nearly every population, the survivors did only what any creature does when the pressure eased, and the effect that resulted holds only under its own conditions, real where those conditions are met and absent everywhere else, since most culls reduce the population exactly as they intend.

## Tension

The rebound real under its conditions, and not a law that culling always backfires.

This is distinct from The Cobra Effect, a human incentive gamed, a bounty farmed so people breed the very pest it pays to remove, where here there is no reward and no gaming, only an ecological response to relaxed density-dependence that no one breeds and no one intends; and distinct from The Balloon Effect, a banned trade squeezed on one patch bulging onto the next, the total unchanged and merely displaced, where here the rise is real in the very population that was culled, and in the same place.
