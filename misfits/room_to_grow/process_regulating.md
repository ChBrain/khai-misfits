# Process: Regulating

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). Borne at [the Range](place_the_range.md), precipitated by [the Warden](persona_the_warden.md), in [The Held Down](plot_the_held_down.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Warden, reading a range already pressed against its own ceiling, its numbers held down not by any hand but by the crowding of the population itself.

## Direction

From the crowd's density toward the number held down.

## Lever

Density-dependent forces, competition for food, disease that spreads faster in a dense crowd, adults that eat the young, press hardest exactly when the crowd is dense, so the population is held below the habitat's ceiling, and the grip eases the moment the crowd is thinned (Ricker 1954; Nicholson 1954, 1957).
