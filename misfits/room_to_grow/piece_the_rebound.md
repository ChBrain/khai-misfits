# Piece: The Rebound

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The mechanism: the net rise of the population past its pre-cull number, the whole of the piece read in one count.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Thinned](place_the_thinned.md), carried by [Filling](process_filling.md), voiced by [the Survivor](persona_the_survivor.md): a season's end tally that runs higher than the tally taken the day the cull began, counted on the same ground, by the same method.

## Load Bearing

Summed across every survivor's own eating, breeding, and surviving young, the population is now more numerous than it was before the cull began. This is not visible in any single animal, only in the total: the increase is a property of the count, raised by the very mortality that was meant to lower it (Abrams; Zipkin, Kraft, Cooch and Sullivan). The rebound is the hydra effect proper, the name Abrams gives the synthesis: added mortality raising abundance through relaxation of the population's own density-dependence, first shown in overcompensatory stock-recruitment curves and blowfly experiments (Ricker; Nicholson) and then confirmed in the field, in removals of nuisance and invasive fish that left more fish than before (Zipkin and colleagues). The rebound is load bearing because it is the outcome the whole piece has been building toward: no one willed it, and the count shows it anyway.

## Apparent

It reads as a coincidence, an unrelated good year, better rain or a mild winter or luck, anything but the cull itself, since nothing about killing animals looks like it should leave more of them.
