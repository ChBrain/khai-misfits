# Persona: The Ecologist

## Taxonomy

[Ecologist](position_ecologist.md), inside [Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The archetype of the one who reads the net across the whole population, names the rebound the single removal cannot see, and holds the effect to the conditions it requires.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A doomsayer who declares that culling always backfires, as though every removal fed the thing it struck, when the effect needs specific conditions and most culls reduce the population as they intend.

## Action

Read the numbers. At [the Long Count](place_the_long_count.md), the Ecologist on [Read the Numbers](plan_read_the_numbers.md) through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) holds [the Condition](piece_the_condition.md), naming the overcompensating density-dependence and the mortality on the self-limiting stage that the rebound requires (Abrams).

## Shadow

It sees the rebound the culler cannot, and must not make it a law. The Ecologist is no doomsayer: reading the total rather than the single death, it can see that the cull raised the number, and name the reason, that the mortality fell on a crowded stage whose density-dependence overcompensated when it was relieved. What it sees is real. But it holds its own bound: the effect needs that overcompensating regulation and that placement of the mortality, so it appears under specific conditions and not everywhere, and most culls reduce the population exactly as they intend, so to declare that culling always backfires is to over-read the case. It keeps its true reading and its held bound, and names a real effect without inflating it into a rule that killing a thing always makes more of it.

## Tell

It reads the rebound the culler missed, names the conditions that made it, and stops there, because most culls lower the number as meant.
