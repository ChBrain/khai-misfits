# Plan: Read the Numbers

## Taxonomy

## Direction

Count the total rather than the single death, name the rebound the culler cannot see, and trace it to the overcompensating density-dependence and the mortality on the self-limiting stage that produced it. Know that the effect needs those conditions, that most culls reduce the population exactly as they intend, and that to declare culling always backfires is to over-read the case into a law it is not.
