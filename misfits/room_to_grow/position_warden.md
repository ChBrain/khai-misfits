# Position: Warden

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The seat of the population's own density-dependence, and marks that its grip is no malice but the impersonal working of a limited habitat, and that the cull relaxes it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A limited habitat, a food supply that only stretches so far and a range that holds only so many.
The density-dependent forces that press hardest when the crowd is dense, the competition, the disease, the cannibal adults.
No design and no malice, only the impersonal holding of the number below what the range could briefly feed.

## Orders

Hold them down, by the crowd's own hunger and press.
Regulate the number below the ceiling through the competition for a limited food, the disease that moves in a dense crowd, and the adults that eat the young, pressing hardest exactly when the population is densest.
Know that you hold no one down on purpose, that you are only what a population does to itself when it is too many, and that your grip is the thing the cull relaxes, loosened by the very deaths meant to lower the number.

## Loses

Its grip, though it held by no malice, to the deaths that thin the crowd.
Because it presses only through the crowd's own density and eases the moment the crowd is thinned, the seat that held the number down is loosened by the cull, the competition and cannibalism and disease all slackening as the numbers drop, so the very force that regulated the population is the force the removal relaxes.
It keeps its impersonal working and its blameless pressure, and loses no cause for blame, the self-regulation whose easing gives the survivors their room.

## Drives

Toward the population's own density that holds it down and is loosened by the cull,
pressing the number below the ceiling through hunger and disease and cannibalism, hardest when the crowd is dense,
so the seat that is no malice but the plain working of a limited range is the grip the deaths relax, its easing the room the survivors fill.
