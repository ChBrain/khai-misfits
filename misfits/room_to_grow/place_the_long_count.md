# Place: The Long Count

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The ecologist's vantage over the whole population's total, before the cull and after, where the net is read and the conditions for a rebound are named. Hosts [When It Holds](plot_when_it_holds.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A ledger, not a scene: the count taken before the cull set against the count taken after, read across the whole population rather than at any one range.

## Holds

[The Condition](piece_the_condition.md).
The named requirements, an overcompensating density-dependence and a mortality that falls on the crowded, self-limiting stage, without which the net does not run backward.

## Offers

To the ecologist, the Long Count offers the only vantage wide enough to read a rebound as a rebound: not a range, not a single season, but the total measured against itself.

## Withheld

The Long Count withholds any verdict on a single cull from a single season's numbers alone, and withholds, just as firmly, any general claim that culling backfires. Most culls still reduce the population as intended; the net here names only the conditions under which this one did not.
