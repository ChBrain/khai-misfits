# Position: Culler

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The seat of the one who removes a fraction of the population, and marks that the logic is sound for nearly every population and that the removal relaxes the very brake that held the number down.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A number judged too high, and the means to remove a fraction of it.
The sound and ordinary logic that to kill some is to leave fewer, true of almost every population there is.
No sight, from the single death, of the brake the removal relaxes, since from where it stands each cull is one fewer.

## Orders

Bring it down, remove a fraction to lower the number.
Cull the population by harvest or poison or removal, in good faith and by the plain arithmetic that to kill some is to leave fewer, the logic that holds for nearly every population there is.
Know that this population holds itself down, that the deaths you deal relax the competition and cannibalism that kept the number low, and that your correct subtraction can be undone by a release you did not reckon and cannot watch.

## Loses

Its aim, though its logic was sound, to a release it could not see.
Because it culls by the arithmetic true of almost everything and cannot see from the single death the brake the removal relaxes, the seat lowers the number and finds it risen, its honest subtraction undone by the very density-dependence the deaths eased.
It keeps its sound logic and its honest aim, and loses no cause for blame, the one whose true arithmetic failed against a population that regulates itself.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound subtraction raises the number,
culling in good faith by the logic that to kill some leaves fewer, blind from the single death to the brake it relaxes,
so the seat whose arithmetic holds for nearly everything is the seat it fails against here, undone by a population that was holding itself down.
