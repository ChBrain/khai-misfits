# Persona: The Culler

## Taxonomy

[Culler](position_culler.md), inside [Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The archetype of the one who removes a fraction of the population in good faith, by the plain logic that to kill some is to leave fewer, the hand whose removal relaxes the very brake that held the number down.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A blunderer who cannot count, as though its cull were a plain mistake that any fool would foresee, when the logic it follows, that killing some leaves fewer, holds for nearly every population there is.

## Action

Bring it down. At [the Range](place_the_range.md), the Culler on [Bring It Down](plan_bring_it_down.md) through [Culling](process_culling.md) takes up [the Cull](piece_the_cull.md), removing a fraction of the population to lower the number, by the sound logic that to kill some is to leave fewer (Zipkin, Kraft, Cooch and Sullivan).

## Shadow

Its logic is sound almost everywhere, and it fails just here. The Culler is no blunderer: to remove a fraction of a population and expect fewer is the plain and correct arithmetic of nearly every population there is, and the cull it makes is honest and aimed only at the number. What it cannot see from where it stands is that this population was holding itself down, that the deaths it deals do not only subtract but relax the competition and the cannibalism that had kept the number low, so its correct subtraction is undone by a release it did not reckon and could not watch, each death from its vantage simply one fewer. It keeps its sound logic and its honest aim, and loses no cause for blame, the one whose true arithmetic failed against a population that regulates itself.

## Tell

It killed some to leave fewer, as the arithmetic says, and left more, because the ones it killed were the crowd that starved the rest.
