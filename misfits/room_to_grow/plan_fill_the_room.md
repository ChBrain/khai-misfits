# Plan: Fill the Room

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the survivor's seat](position_survivor.md), inside [Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md).

## Owner

## Direction

Take the food the thinning freed, let your young live now fewer adults remain to eat them, and breed more as the crowding that suppressed you lifts, which is only what any creature does when the press eases. Know that this ordinary response, summed across all who survived, produces more than the cull removed, so the population rebounds not to where it was but past it, raised by the very mortality meant to lower it.
