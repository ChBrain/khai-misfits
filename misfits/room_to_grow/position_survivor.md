# Position: Survivor

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The seat of the remainder that lives through the cull, and marks that its thriving is no cunning but the plain response of any creature to an eased pressure, and that summed it rebounds the number.

## Has

The room the cull made, the food and space and safety freed as the crowd was thinned.
The plain response of any creature to an eased pressure, to eat what is spare and breed while it can.
No plan and no design, only a suppression lifted and a living taken up again.

## Orders

Fill the room, eat what is spare and breed while the pressure is off.
Take the food the thinning freed, let your young live now fewer adults remain to eat them, and breed more as the crowding that suppressed you lifts, which is only what any creature does when the press eases.
Know that this ordinary response, summed across all who survived, produces more than the cull removed, so the population rebounds not to where it was but past it, raised by the very mortality meant to lower it.

Nothing, and raises the number without meaning to.
Because it only eats the spare food and breeds as the eased crowd allows, the seat takes no loss and schemes at nothing, and yet its blameless thriving, summed across the survivors, produces more than the cull took, so the population rebounds past its start, undone the reduction by a living no one designed.
It keeps its ordinary response and its blameless living, and loses no cause for blame, the remainder whose thriving in the room raised the number the cull meant to lower.

eating the spare food and breeding as the eased crowd allows, no cunning and no design in it,
