# Plot: The Culling

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Range](place_the_range.md), the count comes in high against the number the ground is judged able to bear, and a hand reaches to bring it down.

## Action

At [the Range](place_the_range.md), [the Culler](persona_the_culler.md) from [the culler's seat](position_culler.md) on [Bring It Down](plan_bring_it_down.md) through [Culling](process_culling.md) takes up [the Cull](piece_the_cull.md), removing a fraction of the population to lower the number.

## Stage

The Culler counts a number judged too high for the range to carry and moves to bring it down: a harvest quota filled, a poison laid, an invasive species removed, whichever the range calls for. The logic behind the act is the plain arithmetic that to kill some is to leave fewer, an arithmetic true of nearly every population under management and not invented for this one (Zipkin, Kraft, Cooch and Sullivan). Each animal taken is counted once and subtracted once, and the tally read at the end of the cull is lower than the tally read at its start, exactly as that arithmetic promises. Nothing about the method is hidden or careless: the cull is aimed only at the number, by the ordinary means wildlife management uses everywhere. No villain: the Culler's reasoning is sound for almost every population there is, and the cull itself is honest, undertaken in good faith to bring a high number down and carried out by no different logic than any harvest, poisoning, or removal elsewhere.

## Tension

The sound subtraction that will relax the brake it did not know it was pressing on.
