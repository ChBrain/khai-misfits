# Piece: The Crowding

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The mechanism: the population's own density-dependence, holding its numbers below the ceiling the habitat could otherwise carry.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Range](place_the_range.md), carried by [Regulating](process_regulating.md), voiced by [the Warden](persona_the_warden.md): the herd at its accustomed size, thinner animals at the back of the feeding ground, a sickness moving fast through the tight press of bodies, an old male taking a newborn that strayed too close.

## Load Bearing

The population sits below what the range could carry, and it sits there because of its own crowding, not because of any outside limit. Competition for limited food thins the ones at the margin, disease spreads faster and further through a dense group than a sparse one, and adults consume or displace the young when numbers press hardest (Ricker; Nicholson). This is density-dependence in its classical form: the brake tightens as the population rises and loosens as it falls, so the same habitat yields fewer survivors per head when it is full than when it is empty. The crowding is load bearing because it is the brake the cull will relax: everything that follows, the freed food, the eased disease, the surviving young, is this same mechanism running in reverse.

## Apparent

It reads as a fixed ceiling, a number the range simply will not exceed, as if the population were pinned there by the size of the land itself. A cull looks like it will only lower that ceiling further, taking the population down from where it already sat.
