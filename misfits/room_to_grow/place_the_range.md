# Place: The Range

## Taxonomy

[Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The habitat where the population lives, held below its own ceiling by its own density, and where the cull falls. Hosts [The Held Down](plot_the_held_down.md), [The Culling](plot_the_culling.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A crowded range: bodies pressed against a fixed ceiling of food, space and shelter, the population's own numbers doing the work of holding its numbers down.

## Holds

[The Crowding](piece_the_crowding.md).
The density itself: the competition, the disease, the cannibalism of the young that a full range imposes on its own occupants.

[The Cull](piece_the_cull.md).
The fraction removed, judged against a count read as too high for the range to carry.

## Offers

The Range offers the seat only what it can see: a ceiling, a density, a count judged too high, and a lever, mortality, that reads as plain subtraction.

## Withheld

What the Range withholds is any sight of the brake its own crowding applies, and so any warning that easing that brake, not only removing the fraction, is what the cull actually sets in motion. That accounting only surfaces once the range has been thinned.
