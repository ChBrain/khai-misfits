# Persona: The Survivor

## Taxonomy

[Survivor](position_survivor.md), inside [Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md). The archetype of the remainder that lives through the cull, fills the room the deaths made, overcompensates in the eased conditions, and rebounds the population past where it began.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An opportunist that exploits a slaughter, as though its thriving were a cunning, when it only eats the food now going spare and breeds as any creature does when the crowding lifts.

## Action

Fill the room. At [the Thinned](place_the_thinned.md), the Survivor on [Fill the Room](plan_fill_the_room.md) through [Filling](process_filling.md) takes up [the Room Made](piece_the_room_made.md), the freed food and space, then meets [the Overcompensation](piece_the_overcompensation.md), breeding more and its young surviving, and [the Rebound](piece_the_rebound.md), the population risen past its start (Abrams; Zipkin and colleagues).

## Shadow

Its thriving is no cunning, and it is the thriving that raises the number. The Survivor is no opportunist scheming on a slaughter: it eats the food that the thinning left going spare, its young live because fewer adults remain to eat them, and it breeds more because the pressure that had suppressed it lifted, which is only what any organism does when the crowding eases, no plan and no design in it. And yet, summed across all the survivors, this ordinary response produces more than the cull removed, so the population rebounds not to where it was but past it, raised by the very mortality meant to lower it. It keeps its ordinary response and its blameless living, and rebounds the number without meaning to, the remainder whose thriving in the room undoes the cull.

## Tell

It only ate the food now going spare and bred as the eased crowd lets it, and by that plain thriving it made the population more.
