# Plan: Bring It Down

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the culler's seat](position_culler.md), inside [Room to Grow](play_room_to_grow.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Culler](position_culler.md)

## Direction

Cull the population by harvest or poison or removal, in good faith and by the plain arithmetic that to kill some is to leave fewer, the logic that holds for nearly every population there is. Know that this population holds itself down, that the deaths you deal relax the competition and cannibalism that kept the number low, and that your correct subtraction can be undone by a release you did not reckon and cannot watch.
