# Piece: The Trade-Off Pair

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The mechanism: two options that trade off fairly against each other, with no dominance and no obvious winner, the ordinary shape of a choice before anything else joins it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Menu](place_the_menu.md), carried by [Offering](process_offering.md), voiced by [the Alternative](persona_the_alternative.md): two items set out plainly on the same shelf, a cheaper one against a better one, a nearer one against a bigger one, nothing on the display to recommend either over the other except what the chooser happens to weigh more.

## Load Bearing

This is the pair as it stands before the misfit begins: two options, each ahead of the other on a different dimension, so that neither one dominates and choosing between them means genuinely giving something up (Huber, Payne and Puto, Journal of Consumer Research, 1982). One is cheaper, the other better made; one is nearer, the other bigger; the trade is real and it runs both ways, so a chooser who prefers either option is not making an error. Nothing about the pair alone settles the outcome: whatever share falls to each, absent anything else on the menu, tracks how buyers weigh the two dimensions against each other, and no local comparison hands either one an easy win. The pair is the baseline the rest of the misfit needs, since the shift that comes later can only be read as a shift against a choice that was fair to begin with. Take away an honest trade-off here, and there is nothing left for a decoy to bend.

## Apparent

It reads as a simple, self-contained two-way choice, complete on its own terms, with nothing else in the world relevant to how it comes out.
