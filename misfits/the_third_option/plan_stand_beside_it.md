# Plan: Stand Beside It

## Direction

Hold your qualities exactly as they are while the decoy sits beside you, dominated on every count that matters, and let the clean win between you and it be seen for what it is. Know that this win, though true and unarranged, will lend its shine to the harder comparison against the Alternative that it was never part of, and that the ground you gain there is not ground you fairly took.
