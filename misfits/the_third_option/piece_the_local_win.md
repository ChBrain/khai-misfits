# Piece: The Local Win

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The mechanism: the clean, decisive comparison the Contender wins against the Decoy, its own merits unchanged, a win whose credibility is ready to be borrowed by a comparison it was never part of.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [Beside It](place_beside_it.md), carried by [Comparing](process_comparing.md), voiced by [the Contender](persona_the_contender.md): the moment a shopper's eye lands on the Contender next to the Decoy and sees, without effort, which of the two is plainly better, a comparison so clean it settles itself.

## Load Bearing

Because the Decoy is worse than the Contender on every dimension that matters, the comparison between them is unusually easy: the Contender beats it outright, on every count, a win that costs nothing to see and nothing to doubt (Huber, Payne and Puto, Journal of Consumer Research, 1982). The Contender does nothing to earn this win beyond standing where it stands; its own qualities are exactly what they were before the Decoy arrived. What makes the win load bearing is not the comparison itself, which decides nothing about the real choice against the Alternative, but what happens to its credibility next: a clear local win is read as a signal about quality in general, and that signal is carried into a harder comparison it was never actually part of (Huber, Payne and Puto; the attraction-effect literature). The win is real and trivial at once, real in that the Contender does beat the Decoy, trivial in that beating a dominated option says nothing about beating a fairly trading-off one.

## Apparent

It reads as trivial and self-evidently irrelevant to the real choice, a comparison too lopsided to tell anyone anything worth knowing.
