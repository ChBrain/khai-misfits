# Plot: Governing Unchosen

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The choice made, and the decoy still standing unchosen, off to the side.

## Action

At [the Choice](place_the_choice.md), [the Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md) from [the chooser's seat](position_chooser.md) through [Choosing](process_choosing.md) meets [the Governed Choice](piece_the_governed_choice.md), the outcome decided by [the Decoy](persona_the_decoy.md), which drew almost no share of the choice and governs the result anyway.

## Stage

The Decoy draws almost none of the choice for itself, exactly as expected of an option no rational chooser would take, and governs the outcome anyway, deciding between the Contender and the Alternative without ever being a contender for either seat (the attraction-effect literature). What decided the Choice was not the Decoy's own appeal, which never existed, but its relation to the Contender, an asymmetry no one arranged and no one voted on. No villain: the Alternative's own standing never moved, the Contender never arranged its win or asked the Decoy to stand beside it, and the Decoy never sought to be chosen, drew no share, and wanted nothing from the outcome it ended up governing. The governing is an emergent fact, produced by the shape of the set and not by any actor's intent, authored by no one in the room and traceable to all of them at once. The Chooser walks away believing the Contender simply won, and in one sense it did, only not for the reason the Chooser would give if asked.

## Tension

The true author of the outcome is the one option nobody picked.
