# Persona: The Alternative

## Taxonomy

[Alternative](position_alternative.md), inside [The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The archetype of the option that keeps its honest trade-off character against the other original, offering something real that the Contender does not, and loses share when the choice tilts away from it though its own merits never changed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An inferior choice fairly beaten, as though it lost the comparison it was always in, when the comparison that actually moved the choice was never the one it was part of at all.

## Action

Trade fairly. At [the Menu](place_the_menu.md), the Alternative on [Trade Fairly](plan_trade_fairly.md) through [Offering](process_offering.md) holds [the Trade-Off Pair](piece_the_trade_off_pair.md), offering itself as genuinely better on one dimension and worse on another against the Contender, with no dominance between them (Huber, Payne and Puto).

## Shadow

Its trade-off is real, and the loss it takes has nothing to do with it. The Alternative is no inferior choice fairly beaten: it is better than the Contender on some real dimension, worse on another, exactly the honest balance that makes the original choice hard and genuine. Nothing about its offer weakens when the decoy arrives, since the decoy is not dominated by the Alternative and wins no clean comparison against it either way. What changes is not its own standing but the comparison the chooser leans on, a different, easier one the Alternative was never part of. It keeps its true trade-off intact and loses no cause for blame, the fair option whose share falls though its merit stands exactly where it always stood.

## Tell

It offered the same honest trade-off it always offered, untouched by the newcomer, and lost ground anyway, to a comparison it was never in.
