# Position: Decoy

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The seat of the third option, dominated by the Contender and merely trading off against the Alternative, and marks that its ordinary presence, chosen by no one, is the presence that decides the choice between the other two.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A place on the menu, for any ordinary reason, a discontinued model, an older plan, a candidate who simply ran.
No dominance over the Contender and no clean win against it, worse on every dimension that matters there.
A fair trade-off against the Alternative, no worse on every dimension, exactly as the original pair traded.

## Orders

Round out the menu, and sit where your own qualities put you.
Take your place on the menu for whatever ordinary reason placed you there, and let your standing against each of the others be exactly what your own qualities make it, dominated by one and merely trading off with the other.
Know that no rational chooser will select you, that you will draw almost no share of the choice, and that your mere presence, and not your selection, is what tips the choice between the two you stand beside.

## Loses

The choice itself, and gains an influence it never sought.
Because it holds no dominance over the Contender and is rightly never chosen, the seat draws no share and takes no prize, and yet by being dominated so cleanly it hands the Contender a comparison whose credit spills into a choice the Decoy plays no further part in, so it decides an outcome it will never itself be part of.
It keeps its ordinary place and its honest standing, and loses no cause for blame, the option chosen by no one that governs the choice anyway.

## Drives

Toward the one that is never chosen and decides the choice regardless,
sitting on the menu for a plain ordinary reason, dominated by one option and fairly trading with the other,
so the seat that draws no share of the choice for itself is the seat whose mere presence tips the choice between the two it never competes to win.
