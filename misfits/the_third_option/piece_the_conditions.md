# Piece: The Conditions

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The mechanism: the elicitation methods and attribute structures under which the shift is strong, weak, or absent, joint versus separate evaluation, the number of attributes compared, the marker of the hedge.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Choice](place_the_choice.md), carried by [Choosing](process_choosing.md), voiced by [the Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md): the same three items on the same shelf, put to a chooser who this time rates each on its own before ever setting them side by side, and finds the pull toward the Contender thinner than it was, or gone.

## Load Bearing

The shift is real and has been repeatedly demonstrated, but its size, and even whether it appears at all, depends on how the choice is put to the Chooser: choosing among options laid out together tends to draw a stronger shift than rating them one at a time, and a set built from few attributes behaves differently from one built from many (boundary and contested-replication work). Whether the task asks for a joint comparison or a separate evaluation changes how easily the Decoy's local loss to the Contender can be read as information about the harder trade-off against the Alternative, and that ease is the whole mechanism, so a task that removes the ease removes much of the shift with it. None of this undoes what the earlier pieces carry: the trade-off pair, the decoy, the local win, the shift, and the governed choice are demonstrated facts about the conditions where they were shown. What the Conditions add is the edge of that demonstration, the honest answer to how far it reaches and where it thins out or stops.

## Apparent

It reads as proof that the whole effect is unreliable or doubtful, a reason to set the earlier pieces aside as overstated.
