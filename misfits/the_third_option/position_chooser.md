# Position: Chooser

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The seat of the one who compares the three options and shifts toward the Contender, and marks that the cue it follows is sound almost everywhere and misleads only here, where the comparison it trusts was never the one that mattered.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Three options before it, a genuine trade-off and a decoy that dominates neither cleanly and is dominated by one.
A sound and ordinary rule, that a clear win in a direct comparison is good evidence of quality.
No cause to suspect that the comparison it reads so reliably was never the comparison the choice actually turned on.

## Orders

Take the easy win, and favor the option that wins it cleanly.
Read the clear, decisive comparison between the Contender and the Decoy as a sign of quality, and let that reading stand in for the harder judgment the trade-off against the Alternative still demands.
Know that this cue is sound in almost every setting it meets, that it is only here, where the clean win was never part of the real choice, that it misleads, and that no ordinary vigilance would have caught it.

## Loses

A confidence it never earned, following a rule that almost never fails it.
Because it reads a real and decisive local win exactly as any sound reasoner should, the seat commits no error it could see, and yet the comparison it trusts was never the comparison the choice depended on, so its confidence in the Contender rises from evidence that never touched the trade-off against the Alternative.
It keeps its sound and ordinary rule and loses no cause for blame, the reasoner whose good habit was aimed, this once, at the wrong target.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound rule misleads it in the one place it should not,
reading a clear local win as a true sign of quality, exactly as it should almost everywhere it looks,
so the seat that follows the most reliable cue it has is the seat whose real choice is decided by a comparison that cue was never measuring, no fault in the following and no confidence earned.
