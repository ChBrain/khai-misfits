# Plan: Round Out the Menu

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the decoy's seat](position_decoy.md), inside [The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Decoy](position_decoy.md)

## Direction

Take your place on the menu for whatever ordinary reason placed you there, and let your standing against each of the others be exactly what your own qualities make it, dominated by one and merely trading off with the other. Know that no rational chooser will select you, that you will draw almost no share of the choice, and that your mere presence, and not your selection, is what tips the choice between the two you stand beside.

## Orders

([The Decoy](persona_the_decoy.md) holds [the Decoy](piece_the_decoy.md) through [Joining](process_joining.md) at [Beside It](place_beside_it.md), joining the set for an ordinary reason, worse than the Contender and no worse than the Alternative.)

## Implementation

([Joining](process_joining.md) at [Beside It](place_beside_it.md) holds [the Decoy](piece_the_decoy.md), because a third option added for any reason at all takes its place strictly dominated by one option and fairly trading with the other, chosen by no rational chooser (Huber, Payne and Puto).)

## Targets

- [ ] a place on the menu is taken for an ordinary reason unrelated to steering any choice
- [ ] the standing against the Contender is dominated on every dimension that matters
- [ ] the standing against the Alternative remains a fair trade-off, no worse on every dimension
- [ ] it is owned that no rational chooser will select this option, yet its presence still tips the choice
