# Persona: The Decoy

## Taxonomy

[Decoy](position_decoy.md), inside [The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The archetype of the third option, dominated by the Contender and merely trading off against the Alternative, chosen by no rational chooser and yet the presence that decides the choice between the other two.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A plant placed to deceive, as though its whole purpose were the comparison it hands to the Contender, when it may sit on the menu for any ordinary reason and want nothing of the choice it ends up swaying.

## Action

Round out the menu. At [Beside It](place_beside_it.md), the Decoy on [Round Out the Menu](plan_round_out_the_menu.md) through [Joining](process_joining.md) holds [the Decoy](piece_the_decoy.md), added for any reason at all, worse than the Contender on every dimension that matters and no worse than the Alternative on every dimension (Huber, Payne and Puto).

## Shadow

Its presence is ordinary, and its effect is not what placed it there. The Decoy is no plant staged to deceive: a discontinued model kept in the catalog, an older plan not yet retired, a candidate who simply chose to run, any of these explains its place on the menu without a trace of intent to steer anyone's choice, and it is drawn to no share of the choice itself, since no rational chooser would take a strictly worse option over one already offered. It does not know it dominates or is dominated, does not seek the comparison it loses to the Contender or the one it draws even with against the Alternative; it simply sits where its own qualities put it. It keeps its ordinary reason for being there and loses no cause for blame, the option no one picks that still tips the choice between the two that were never about it.

## Tell

It sat on the menu for a plain, unremarkable reason, was chosen by no one, and decided the choice all the same.
