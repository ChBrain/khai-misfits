# Position: Alternative

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The seat of the option that keeps its honest trade-off against the Contender, and marks that its own merits never change and the loss it takes comes from a comparison it was never part of.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A genuine trade-off against the Contender, better on one dimension and worse on another, no dominance between them.
A standing that never shifts on its own account, since the decoy is dominated by neither of them cleanly on both sides.
No part in the comparison that actually decides the choice, the Contender's easy win against a third option entirely.

## Orders

Trade fairly, and offer exactly the balance you always offered.
Hold your honest trade-off against the Contender steady, better here, worse there, and let the chooser weigh the real difference between you as it always had to.
Know that your standing has not changed when the decoy arrives, that the decoy wins no clean comparison against you either way, and that the ground you lose will come from a comparison you were never in.

## Loses

Share of the choice, though nothing about it changed.
Because its trade-off stays exactly as honest and exactly as balanced as it always was, the seat gives no ground on its own account, and yet the chooser's confidence in the Contender rises from a comparison the Alternative never entered, so its share falls to a contest it was not part of and could not have won or lost.
It keeps its true, unchanged trade-off and loses no cause for blame, the fair option undone by a comparison elsewhere.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honest trade-off never moves and still loses ground,
offering the same real balance against the Contender it always offered, untouched by what the decoy does,
so the seat whose own merit stays exactly where it stood is the seat whose share falls anyway, to a comparison it never entered and could not have swayed.
