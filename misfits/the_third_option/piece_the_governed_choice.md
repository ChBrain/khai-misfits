# Piece: The Governed Choice

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The mechanism: the outcome decided by an option no one selected, the Decoy drawing almost no share of the choice and governing the result anyway.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Choice](place_the_choice.md), carried by [Choosing](process_choosing.md), voiced by [the Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md): the Decoy still sitting unpicked on the shelf after the purchase is made, its ballot line unmarked, its plan unchosen, present at the count and absent from the total.

## Load Bearing

Across the attraction-effect literature that followed Huber, Payne and Puto's original demonstration, the pattern holds across consumer, political, and other choice domains: the dominated option itself takes almost none of the choice, and the option it flatters takes more than it would have taken alone. This is the harm of the whole structure laid bare: an alternative that draws close to no share still governs which of the other two wins, because its role was never to be chosen but to change how one of the others is seen beside it. No single act in the chain looks like a cause on its own. The menu offered a discontinued item for an ordinary reason, the Contender won a comparison it did not seek, the Chooser used a shortcut that is usually sound, and the sum of these ordinary acts is a result authored by none of them and traceable to an option none of them picked.

## Apparent

It reads as an unremarkable, ordinary preference with no hidden cause, a chooser getting the thing it wanted.
