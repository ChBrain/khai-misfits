# Plan: Trade Fairly

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the alternative's seat](position_alternative.md), inside [The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Alternative](position_alternative.md)

## Direction

Hold your honest trade-off against the Contender steady, better here, worse there, and let the chooser weigh the real difference between you as it always had to. Know that your standing has not changed when the decoy arrives, that the decoy wins no clean comparison against you either way, and that the ground you lose will come from a comparison you were never in.

## Orders

([The Alternative](persona_the_alternative.md) holds [the Trade-Off Pair](piece_the_trade_off_pair.md) through [Offering](process_offering.md) at [the Menu](place_the_menu.md), offering its genuine trade-off against the Contender with no dominance either way.)

## Implementation

([Offering](process_offering.md) at [the Menu](place_the_menu.md) holds [the Trade-Off Pair](piece_the_trade_off_pair.md), because two options that trade off with no dominance set the hard, honest choice the whole misfit turns on before any decoy joins (Huber, Payne and Puto).)

## Targets

- [ ] the genuine trade-off against the Contender is offered, better on one dimension and worse on another
- [ ] the standing is held to be unchanged by the decoy's arrival
- [ ] it is seen that the decoy wins no clean comparison against the Alternative either way
- [ ] it is owned that any ground lost will come from a comparison the Alternative was never part of
