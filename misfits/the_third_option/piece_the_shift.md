# Piece: The Shift

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The mechanism: the choice moving toward the Contender, violating regularity and independence from irrelevant alternatives, though nothing about the Contender's own merit changed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Choice](place_the_choice.md), carried by [Choosing](process_choosing.md), voiced by [the Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md): a hand reaching for the Contender that, on a menu without the Decoy, might just as easily have reached for the Alternative, the reach itself the only visible sign that anything moved at all.

## Load Bearing

The share that falls to the Contender rises once the Decoy joins the set, and rises specifically because the Decoy is dominated by the Contender and not by the Alternative (Huber, Payne and Puto, Journal of Consumer Research, 1982). This is the shift proper: regularity says adding an option to a set should never raise the odds of choosing something already there, since a new option can only draw share away and never add to what was present, and independence says the choice between two options ought not turn on the presence of a third that neither chooser nor either option asked for. Both are violated at once, and neither the Contender nor the Alternative has changed in any way that would justify it: same price, same features, same trade-off between them as before the Decoy arrived. What moved is the comparison available to the Chooser, not the comparison that actually decides between the two real options, and the shift is that easy comparison substituting for the harder one.

## Apparent

It reads as the Chooser simply preferring the better option, an outcome no different from any ordinary purchase or vote.
