# Plan: Take the Easy Win

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the chooser's seat](position_chooser.md), inside [The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Chooser](position_chooser.md)

## Direction

Read the clear, decisive comparison between the Contender and the Decoy as a sign of quality, and let that reading stand in for the harder judgment the trade-off against the Alternative still demands. Know that this cue is sound in almost every setting it meets, that it is only here, where the clean win was never part of the real choice, that it misleads, and that no ordinary vigilance would have caught it.

## Orders

([The Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md) takes up [the Shift](piece_the_shift.md) through [Choosing](process_choosing.md) at [the Choice](place_the_choice.md), favoring the Contender for its clear win over the Decoy, and meets [the Governed Choice](piece_the_governed_choice.md) and [the Conditions](piece_the_conditions.md).)

## Implementation

([Choosing](process_choosing.md) at [the Choice](place_the_choice.md) takes up [the Shift](piece_the_shift.md), because the local win against the Decoy is read as a sign of quality that stands in for the harder judgment, so [the Governed Choice](piece_the_governed_choice.md) follows and [the Conditions](piece_the_conditions.md) hold the finding to its bound (Huber, Payne and Puto).)

## Targets
