# Place: The Choice

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). Where the Chooser actually decides, the choice shifts, an option no one picked governs the outcome, and the bound on the whole effect is held. Hosts [A Clear Win Stands In](plot_a_clear_win_stands_in.md), [Governing Unchosen](plot_governing_unchosen.md), [Real But Not Always](plot_real_but_not_always.md).

## Shown

The Chooser deciding, the decoy drawing almost no share, the Contender's share risen and the Alternative's fallen, though neither option's own merits moved at all.

## Holds

[The Shift](piece_the_shift.md).
The movement of the choice toward the Contender, traced back to a local win that never involved the Alternative.

[The Governed Choice](piece_the_governed_choice.md).
The outcome steered by an option that was itself chosen by no one.

[The Conditions](piece_the_conditions.md).
The bound on how far and how reliably the shift travels, holding the effect to its evidence.

## Offers

A verdict. The Chooser leaves with a pick made, and from the inside, well justified.

## Withheld

Proof that the pick tracked what it should have. Nothing here shows the Chooser that a comparison it was never part of did the deciding.
