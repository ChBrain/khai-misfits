# Pitch: Chosen by No One

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that an option no rational chooser would ever select can still decide the choice between two others.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry and observant, the small sleight of a menu. Two fair options and a third that beats neither and loses to one, present for any ordinary reason. Nothing here is a rigged comparison staged on purpose; the decoy needs no intent and the chooser no gullibility.

## Undertow

A decoy dominated by one of two fairly trading-off options hands that option a comparison it cannot lose, and the ease of that local win substitutes for the harder judgment the original pair demanded, shifting the choice toward the option the decoy flatters (Huber, Payne and Puto). The decoy itself draws almost no share and governs the outcome anyway.

## Nerve

That comparative ease can stand in for a harder trade-off judgment, so an alternative no one chooses still decides the choice between two others, violating the plain expectation that adding an option should never favor one already there. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since the effect is real and repeatedly demonstrated but its size depends on how the choice is put to the chooser and it weakens or vanishes under some conditions (boundary and contested-replication work).

## Echo

Add a third thing worse than one and no worse than the other, and watch the choice tilt toward the one it lost to.
