# Persona: The Contender

## Taxonomy

[Contender](position_contender.md), inside [The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The archetype of the option that dominates the decoy outright, winning an easy comparison it did nothing to seek, and gaining share in the choice against the Alternative though its own merits never changed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A schemer who planted a weaker rival to look good beside it, as though it arranged its own flattering comparison, when the decoy's presence and its dominance over it are none of the Contender's doing.

## Action

Stand beside it. At [Beside It](place_beside_it.md), the Contender on [Stand Beside It](plan_stand_beside_it.md) through [Comparing](process_comparing.md) takes up [the Local Win](piece_the_local_win.md), winning a clear and decisive comparison against the decoy standing beside it, unchanged in its own qualities the whole time (Huber, Payne and Puto).

## Shadow

Its win is real, and it did nothing to earn the credit that win will be given. The Contender is no schemer: it does not place the decoy, does not choose to dominate it, and holds exactly the same qualities before and after the decoy arrives. That it beats the decoy on every dimension is simply true, a fact about the decoy's weakness and not a maneuver of the Contender's. What follows, that this clean local win lends its shine to the harder, unrelated comparison against the Alternative, is not something the Contender contrives; it only stands where it stands and wins what it plainly wins. It keeps its unchanged merits and its honestly won comparison, and loses no cause for blame, the option that gains ground it did not seek by winning a contest it did not arrange.

## Tell

It beat the decoy cleanly, as it always would have, and was favored over an alternative it never actually out-traded.
