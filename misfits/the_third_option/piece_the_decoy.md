# Piece: The Decoy

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The mechanism: a third option added for any ordinary reason, worse than the Contender on every dimension that matters and not worse than the Alternative, chosen by no rational chooser.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [Beside It](place_beside_it.md), carried by [Joining](process_joining.md), voiced by [the Decoy](persona_the_decoy.md): a third item set on the shelf next to the pair, a discontinued model still listed in the catalog, an older plan not yet retired, a candidate who simply chose to run, sitting there for no reason connected to the choice it will end up shaping.

## Load Bearing

The Decoy is defined entirely by its relation to the other two, not by anything special in itself: it is worse than the Contender on every dimension that matters, and not worse than the Alternative, so it strictly loses to one option and merely trades off against the other, exactly as the pair traded off between themselves (Huber, Payne and Puto, Journal of Consumer Research, 1982). No rational chooser would ever take it, since a strictly better option already sits beside it on every count, and choosers rarely do. Its place on the menu needs no design behind it: a catalog keeps an old model on the shelf, a plan is not yet pulled from the list, a weaker candidate files to run because running costs little, and none of these ordinary reasons has anything to do with steering anyone's choice. What matters is only the asymmetry it introduces: dominated by one option, not by the other, present without needing to be wanted.

## Apparent

It reads as a harmless, irrelevant addition to the menu, since no one will pick it and its presence seems to cost the choice nothing.
