# Position: Contender

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). The seat of the option that dominates the decoy outright, and marks that the win is real and unsought, and that the credit it lends to a harder, unrelated comparison is none of the Contender's doing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Every dimension that beats the decoy, an outright dominance it neither sought nor arranged.
The same qualities before and after the decoy arrives, nothing in its own standing shifted.
No hand in placing the decoy or in the comparison against the Alternative that its local win comes to flatter.

## Orders

Stand beside it, and win the comparison that is simply yours to win.
Hold your qualities exactly as they are while the decoy sits beside you, dominated on every count that matters, and let the clean win between you and it be seen for what it is.
Know that this win, though true and unarranged, will lend its shine to the harder comparison against the Alternative that it was never part of, and that the ground you gain there is not ground you fairly took.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and gains ground it did not earn by the comparison that mattered.
Because it does not place the decoy and does not choose to dominate it, the seat takes no loss and commits no maneuver, and yet the clean win it holds against the decoy lends unearned credibility to the harder comparison against the Alternative, so it gains a share it did not win by trading fairly.
It keeps its unchanged merits and its honestly won local win, and loses no cause for blame, the option favored by a contest it did not need to enter.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honest local win borrows credit for a harder comparison it never fought,
standing unchanged beside a decoy it did not place and beating it cleanly, exactly as its own merits always would,
so the seat that wins only the comparison it is plainly owed is the seat that gains ground in the comparison it never had to win, favored past its own honest trade.
