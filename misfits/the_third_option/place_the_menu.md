# Place: The Menu

## Taxonomy

[The Third Option](play_the_third_option.md). Where the two original options sit, trading off fairly, no decoy present yet. Hosts [Two Fair Options](plot_two_fair_options.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two options, side by side, and nothing else. One ahead on a dimension the other lacks, the second ahead on a dimension the first lacks, no line drawn under either as the winner.

## Holds

[The Trade-Off Pair](piece_the_trade_off_pair.md).
Two options that trade off cleanly, dominance on neither side, is what makes the choice standing between them a genuinely hard one.

## Offers

A fair fight. Whichever way the Chooser leans here, the lean is theirs to own, since nothing on the menu yet beats the other outright.

## Withheld

A tiebreaker. The menu holds the trade-off open rather than closed, and offers no third thing for a chooser to lean on instead of deciding.
