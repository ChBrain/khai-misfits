# Plot: Neither of Them Is Right

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The population's actual trait value, resting where the two opposing selections cancel.
[The Two Optima](piece_the_two_optima.md), both of them unreached.

## Action

[Settling](process_settling.md): [The Compromise](persona_the_compromise.md), in [the displaced seat](position_displaced.md), holds the balance point on behalf of everybody and to the benefit of nobody.

## Stage

[The Compromise](persona_the_compromise.md), on [Sit Where the Pull Balances](plan_sit_where_the_pull_balances.md), sits between the two optima and at neither, moving only when one of the pressures changes.
Both sexes are displaced. Both are being selected on correctly.

## Tension

This is not an agreement, and calling it a compromise is the mistake it invites.
Nothing was negotiated and nothing conceded, because there is no party here: the value is the resting point of two forces, so it has no claim to being a good outcome for anybody and is not a bad decision by anybody either. And it cannot be improved, because moving it toward one sex's optimum moves it away from the other's **through the same locus**. What remains is [the load](piece_the_load.md): the fitness both sexes are short of, carried by every individual in the population, set down by none of them, and attached to no event that ever occurred.
