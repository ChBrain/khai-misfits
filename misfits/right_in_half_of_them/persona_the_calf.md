# Persona: The Calf

## Taxonomy

[Undifferentiated](position_undifferentiated.md). The stage at which both sexes need the same things, where the conflict is measurably absent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Small, growing, and not yet differentiated in any way the trait acts on.
Wants exactly what the other sex's calves want: to survive to breeding.
Carries the variant, and it is not yet pulling in two directions.

## Action

Survives, or does not.
Does so at a rate that depends on the same alleles in both sexes and depends on them the same way.
Grows into one of the two bodies that will need opposite things.

## Shadow

Nothing at all, which is the point of the seat: it is here to show that the conflict has a start, and that the start is the divergence of the phenotypes rather than the sharing of the genome.
Because juvenile survival is strongly and positively correlated across the sexes while adult reproductive success is strongly and negatively correlated, the shared genome is demonstrably not sufficient on its own.
It ends by becoming either the seat the variant suits or the seat it does not.

## Tell

Says: we are the same so far.
Which is true, and is measurable, and stops being true at maturity.
