# Process: Sharing

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). One locus being expressed in two bodies that will need opposite things from it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Ordinary inheritance.
At [the genome](place_the_genome.md), at every locus outside the small sex-specific region.

## Direction

Toward the same effect in both sexes, because there is nothing else available.
This is not a flaw in the arrangement: a shared inheritance is what makes two sexes one species, and the alternative would be two genomes rather than a resolved conflict.
And it is the necessary condition for everything that follows, since a variant that could arrive only where it helped would generate no misfit at all.

## Lever

The absence of any label.
Because a genome carries no annotation for the sex it will be read in, the one item of information that would resolve the conflict is the one that is not there.

## Echo

A variant favoured in one sex, present at the same frequency in the other.
Nothing has gone wrong at this step and nothing could have gone otherwise.
