# Persona: The Stag

## Taxonomy

[Selected](position_selected.md). The sex the variant suits: leaves more offspring for carrying it, and passes it to daughters it will not suit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Successful, by the only measure selection uses.
Larger, or better armed, or faster to mature, in whatever direction the variant pushes.
Doing nothing to anybody: the advantage is over other males and is not taken from any female.

## Action

Competes, and wins more than his share of matings.
Leaves more offspring than his rivals, which is what being favoured means.
Passes the variant to half of them, including the daughters.

## Shadow

Nothing of his own, and he is the vector of the whole cost: the seat's success is what propagates the variant, so the better this seat does the more of the other sex carries what will lower its fitness.
Because there is no mechanism by which a father transmits to sons only, the seat cannot succeed selectively and cannot moderate the transmission.
It ends with more descendants than anybody, and with daughters that leave fewer than average.

## Tell

Says nothing, and the pedigree says it for him:
the stags that left the most offspring fathered the daughters that left the fewest.
