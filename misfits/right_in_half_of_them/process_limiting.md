# Process: Limiting

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). Expression becoming restricted to the sex an allele benefits, which is the real remedy.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A modifier that biases or limits expression by sex.
At [the genome](place_the_genome.md), at whichever loci it reaches.

## Direction

Toward dimorphism, and toward genuine resolution at that locus.
This works. Where expression becomes sex-limited the conflict ends there, one sex can approach its optimum without dragging the other away, and the difference between the sexes that results is the visible record of a conflict that used to exist.
And it is slow, partial and itself constrained: most loci are not sex-limited, the modifiers must arise and spread like any other variant, and work following the process has found sex-specific adaptation arrested partway rather than run to completion.

## Lever

Evolutionary time, which is the one lever available and the one that cannot be hurried.
Because the load is borne every generation while the remedy is paid in instalments, resolution and burden are simultaneous states rather than alternatives.

## Echo

Dimorphism where it has reached, and the compromise everywhere else.
The remedy separates the two sexes' expression rather than settling anything between them, which is the only shape a resolution can take when the difficulty is that they are the same.
