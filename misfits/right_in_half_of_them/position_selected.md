# Position: Selected

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). The seat the variant favours, whose success is the mechanism by which the other sex acquires it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An advantage on the shared trait, in the direction this sex is selected in.
More offspring than rivals, which is the definition of being favoured.
Transmission to daughters as well as sons, which is not optional.

## Orders

Compete.
Leave more offspring than the alternative.
Transmit, indiscriminately by sex.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat propagates the cost precisely in proportion to its own success: the more this seat wins, the more of the other sex carries a variant that lowers its fitness, and the winning is neither aggression nor exploitation, only leaving more descendants.
Because transmission cannot be made sex-selective by any behaviour, the seat has no moderate version of itself.
It ends with the most descendants in the population, half of whom are worse off for the inheritance.

## Drives

Toward more offspring,
never once taking anything from the other sex,
so the seat that harms nobody is the one that carries the harm outward.
