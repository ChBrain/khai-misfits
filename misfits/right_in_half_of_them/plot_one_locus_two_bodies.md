# Plot: One Locus, Two Bodies

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A shared genome, and two sexes that do not need the same things out of it.
[The Shared Locus](piece_the_shared_locus.md), expressed identically wherever it is read.

## Action

[Sharing](process_sharing.md): [The Allele](persona_the_allele.md), in [the shared seat](position_shared.md), is transmitted to offspring of both sexes and does the same thing in each.

## Stage

[The Allele](persona_the_allele.md), on [Do the Same Thing in Both](plan_do_the_same_thing_in_both.md), produces its phenotype without any information about the body it is in.
[The Stag](persona_the_stag.md) and [The Hind](persona_the_hind.md) both carry it, and it raises the offspring count of one and lowers the offspring count of the other.

## Tension

The conflict is a property of inheritance rather than of anybody's behaviour.
Nothing in a genome is labelled for the sex it will be read in, so there is no step at which anything could have been decided otherwise and no party that could have decided it. **There is no antagonist in this play at all**: nothing is competing, nothing is exploiting anything, and nothing is even aware of the other side. The two sexes are built from one set of instructions and read them in bodies that need opposite answers, which is the whole of it.
