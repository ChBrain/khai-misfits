# Persona: The Allele

## Taxonomy

[Shared](position_shared.md). One variant at one locus, expressed in whichever body it lands in, with no way to know which that is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not a strategy and not an agent: a sequence that has an effect.
Raises the number of offspring left by one sex and lowers the number left by the other, and does both by the same mechanism.
Nothing conflicted about it internally: it does one thing, consistently.

## Action

Is inherited, by sons and daughters alike, outside the small region where inheritance is sex-specific.
Is expressed, and produces its phenotype.
Is selected for in half the population and against in the other half, simultaneously.

## Shadow

Nothing, and it is the reason nobody is at fault: the seat has no preference, no target and no capacity to do anything other than what it does, so the harm attaches to no decision anywhere.
Because nothing in a genome is labelled for the sex it will be read in, there is no version of this seat that arrives only where it is wanted.
It ends at whatever frequency the two opposing pressures leave it, which is a compromise rather than an answer.

## Tell

Says nothing, being a sequence.
Its whole statement is a phenotype, made identically in two bodies that need opposite things from it.
