# Piece: The Shared Locus

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). One position in a genome held by both sexes, expressed in both, and labelled for neither.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the genome](place_the_genome.md), which is to say almost everywhere.
Answered nowhere: outside the small sex-specific region there is no mechanism by which transmission or expression can distinguish.

## Load Bearing

Without it the two sexes are two problems and there is no conflict at all.
It is load bearing because it makes the antagonism **structural rather than behavioural**: the sexes are not competing, negotiating or exploiting each other, they are built from one set of instructions and read it in bodies that need different answers.

## Apparent

Ordinary inheritance, and it is.
It reads as the thing that makes a species one species, which is exactly what it is, and its being the cause of the difficulty is not visible from anything about it.

## Yearbook

Operated on by [sharing](process_sharing.md), pulled by [pulling](process_pulling.md).
It is the reason [the two optima](piece_the_two_optima.md) cannot both be reached, and the thing [the sex-limited switch](piece_the_sex_limited_switch.md) removes one locus at a time.
