# Piece: The Negative Correlation

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). The intersex genetic correlation for lifetime fitness in a wild red deer population: about minus nought point four eight.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the pedigree](place_the_pedigree.md), computable only from decades of individual records.
Answered nowhere: it is a property of a population and appears in no animal's life.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a laboratory result and an argument about wild populations.
It is load bearing because it puts the mechanism **in a long-lived wild animal nobody arranged**: the stags that left the most offspring fathered the daughters that left the fewest, so selection was running against males carrying the alleles for high female fitness, in a population under no experimental manipulation at all.

## Apparent

A correlation coefficient in a table.
It reads as one statistic among many, and the sentence it stands for, that being a successful father predicts having unsuccessful daughters, is not what a coefficient looks like.
