# Position: Burdened

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). The seat the variant does not suit, holding a trait value set by selection on somebody else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The variant, acquired by descent.
Its phenotype, at a value optimal for a body this is not.
Ordinary competence in everything the trait does not touch.

## Orders

Live and breed, at whatever rate the displaced trait allows.
Transmit the variant onward, to sons it helps and daughters it does not.
Be selected against, correctly, for carrying it.

## Loses

Fitness, for a cause located wholly outside the seat: nothing was done to it, nothing was withheld from it, and the deficit is the distance between its own optimum and a specification written by selection on the other sex.
Because transmitting the variant benefits this seat's sons, even the passing on is not an error available for correction.
It ends with fewer offspring than an uncarrying individual, and no event to point at.

## Drives

Toward its own optimum,
never once reaching it,
so the seat that bears the cost is the one with nothing it could have done differently.
