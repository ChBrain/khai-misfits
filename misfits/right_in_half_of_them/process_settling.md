# Process: Settling

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). The population arriving at the trait value where the two pulls cancel.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two opposing pressures of unequal strength.
At [the genome](place_the_genome.md), at every locus where the sexes disagree.

## Direction

Toward a balance point, and toward nobody's optimum.
The result is not a compromise in the sense of an agreement: nothing negotiated, nothing conceded and no party present, only a resting value of two forces.
And it is stable in a way that is worse than instability would be, since a value that moved would at least visit somebody's optimum on the way.

## Lever

The relative strength of the two pressures, which nothing in the population controls.
Because the balance shifts only when one pressure changes, no individual and no lineage has any purchase on it.

## Echo

[The load](piece_the_load.md): the fitness both sexes are short of, carried by every individual and set down by none of them.
In the wild it reads as a negative genetic correlation for lifetime fitness between the sexes, about minus nought point four eight in a red deer population followed across decades.
