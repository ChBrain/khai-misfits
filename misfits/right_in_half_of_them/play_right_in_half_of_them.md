# Play: Right in Half of Them

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Right in Half of Them.
Males and females of one species carry very nearly the same genome. They do not need the same things out of it. A variant that builds a heavier body, a longer horn, a faster metabolism can raise the number of offspring one sex leaves and lower the number the other leaves, and because the locus is shared it is expressed in both of them regardless.
So it is right in half of them. Not right on average, not right in the long run: right in one body and wrong in the other, at the same moment, in the same population, and there is no version of the allele that is only in the half it suits.

## Arc

The bend is this: selection acts on individuals and inheritance acts on a genome the two sexes hold in common, so a variant favoured in one is imposed on the other, and a population settles where neither sex is where it should be.
It runs on two facts, neither of them anybody's doing: the sexes share their loci, and they do not share their optima.
Take the sharing first. Outside a small sex-specific region, an allele in a father can arrive in a daughter, and it does what it does there. Nothing in the genome is labelled for the body it will land in. The selection acting on the male is real and correct and increases his descendants; the selection acting on the female is real and correct too, and pulls the other way on the very same locus.
Then take the measurement, because the whole thing would be a theory otherwise. In fruit flies, forty haploid genomes were cloned and their fitness read in both sexes: as juveniles, when the two sexes want the same things, survival was **strongly positively correlated** across the sexes. As adults, when they do not, reproductive success was **strongly negatively correlated**. The conflict is not a permanent property of a shared genome. It switches on when the two bodies start needing different things.
And it is not a laboratory artefact. On the Isle of Rum, in a red deer population followed across decades of pedigree, the intersex genetic correlation for lifetime fitness came out at about minus nought point four eight: **the stags that left the most offspring fathered daughters that left the fewest.** So a female is selected against for carrying what made her father successful, and a male is selected against for carrying what would have made his sisters so.
The result is a population sitting at a compromise, displaced from both optima, carrying a load that no individual can put down and no selection can remove, because removing it in one sex is imposing it on the other.
Reach for a settlement and there is one, and it is slow and it is partial. Expression can become sex-limited, and where it does the conflict is genuinely resolved and dimorphism appears. But most loci are not sex-limited, the modifiers that would limit them have to arise and spread like anything else, and the work that has looked at the process finds sex-specific adaptation arrested partway rather than completed. So the load is paid down over evolutionary time, in instalments, while it is being borne in full every generation.

## Triggers

**[One Locus, Two Bodies](plot_one_locus_two_bodies.md)**
A shared genome, and two sexes that do not want the same things out of it. Nothing in a locus is labelled for the body it will land in, so an allele favoured in one sex arrives in the other and does what it does there. Closes when the conflict is established as a property of inheritance rather than of anybody's behaviour.

**[As Calves They Agree](plot_as_calves_they_agree.md)**
Cue: forty cloned haploid genomes, their fitness read in both sexes at two stages. Juvenile survival correlates strongly and positively across the sexes; adult reproductive success correlates strongly and negatively. Closes when the conflict is shown to switch on at the point the two bodies begin needing different things, rather than to have been there all along.

**[The Best Stags Had the Worst Daughters](plot_the_best_stags_had_the_worst_daughters.md)**
Cue: a red deer population on an island, followed across decades, with a pedigree deep enough to set a father's lifetime fitness beside his daughter's. The intersex correlation comes out at about minus nought point four eight. Closes when the effect is out of the laboratory and into a wild population of long-lived animals nobody arranged.

**[Neither of Them Is Right](plot_neither_of_them_is_right.md)**
Cue: the population trait value, sitting where the two pulls balance. Both sexes are displaced from their own optimum and both are being selected on correctly. Closes when the compromise is shown to be nobody's choice and nobody's error, and to be unimprovable by any move that does not separate the sexes.

**[Only in the One It Suits](plot_only_in_the_one_it_suits.md)**
Cue: a modifier that would limit expression to the sex the allele benefits, which is the real remedy and does genuinely work. Closes when the remedy is shown to be slow, incomplete and itself subject to arrest, so the load is paid down over evolutionary time and borne in full in the meantime.

## Stakes

Whether two things selected in opposite directions can both arrive when they are built out of one set of instructions.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether either sex is doing something wrong, because selection is not a party and there is nobody in the play with an interest, but whether a shared inheritance can deliver two different answers at once. The production cannot find a way. It can only show that nothing in a genome is labelled for the sex it will be read in, that the conflict is absent while the two bodies want the same things and present the moment they do not, that the effect is measurable in a wild pedigree as a negative correlation between a father's fitness and his daughter's, that the population therefore sits displaced from both optima carrying a load no individual can set down, and that the one real remedy separates the expression rather than the interests, arrives slowly, and has been found stalled partway through.
