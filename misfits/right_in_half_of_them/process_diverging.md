# Process: Diverging

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). The two sexes' optima moving apart as their phenotypes differentiate, which is where the conflict starts.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Maturation, and the divergence of what the two bodies are for.
At [the genome](place_the_genome.md), acting on the same loci as before.

## Direction

Toward two different best values for one trait.
The measured location of this step is the play's cleanest fact: with forty cloned haploid genomes read in both sexes, juvenile survival correlated **strongly and positively** across the sexes and adult reproductive success correlated **strongly and negatively**.
So the shared genome is present in full at both stages and the conflict is present at only one, which means sharing alone does not cause it and the divergence of the phenotypes does.

## Lever

The difference between what the two bodies are selected for, which is a fact about the species rather than about any individual.
Because the divergence is what sexual reproduction consists of, removing it is not an available move.

## Echo

A conflict with a start, at maturity, rather than a permanent condition of a shared genome.
[The juvenile agreement](piece_the_juvenile_agreement.md) is what shows it, by being the case where everything else is true and the conflict is not.
