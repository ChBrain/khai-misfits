# Persona: The Hind

## Taxonomy

[Burdened](position_burdened.md). The sex the variant does not suit: carries it because of who her father was, and leaves fewer offspring for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, healthy and unremarkable in every respect she controls.
Displaced from her own optimum on the shared trait, by an amount she did not choose and cannot alter.
Not a victim of anybody: nothing was done to her and nothing was withheld.

## Action

Lives, breeds, and leaves fewer offspring than a female without the variant would have.
Passes it on herself, to sons in whom it will help and daughters in whom it will not.
Is selected against, correctly, for carrying it.

## Shadow

Fitness, for a reason located entirely in another body: the seat is not less able, it is built to a specification set by selection on males, and the difference between that specification and her own is the whole of the deficit.
Because the variant helps her sons, her transmitting it is not an error either, so nothing in the seat's own behaviour is available to be corrected.
It ends with fewer offspring than she would have had, and with sons who do better than average.

## Tell

Says nothing, and would have nothing to report if she could:
there is no event in her life that this happened at.
