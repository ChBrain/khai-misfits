# Piece: The Load

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). The fitness both sexes are short of, carried by every individual and set down by none of them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Everywhere in the population, at every locus where the sexes disagree.
Answered nowhere in any generation: only [limiting](process_limiting.md) touches it, and only at the loci it has reached.

## Load Bearing

Without it the compromise is merely a value rather than a cost.
It is load bearing because it names **what is actually lost**: not a resource, not an opportunity and not anybody's advantage, but the distance between where each sex sits and where it would be if the other did not exist, which is a permanent shortfall with no event behind it.

## Apparent

Genetic variation for fitness, which population genetics expects to find.
It reads as ordinary standing variation, and the fact that it is being maintained by two correct pressures pulling opposite ways rather than by drift or mutation is not visible in the variance itself.

## Yearbook

Produced by [settling](process_settling.md), measured through [the negative correlation](piece_the_negative_correlation.md).
It is paid down only by [the sex-limited switch](piece_the_sex_limited_switch.md), locus by locus, over evolutionary time.
