# Pitch: The Half It Suits

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry, biological, quantitative.
The register of a pedigree beside a fitness table: who left more, whose daughters left fewer, counted over twenty years of an island population.
Nothing indignant anywhere in it, because selection is not a party and there is nobody here to hold responsible.

## Undertow

Under the trait, two facts that do not negotiate.
The sexes share their loci. The sexes do not share their optima.
Between those two, a variant cannot be favoured in one body without being imposed on the other, so every gain has a matching cost in a relative who did not choose it and cannot decline it.

## Nerve

The unease of a correlation that runs the wrong way and cannot be argued with:
the stags that left the most offspring were the ones whose daughters left the fewest,
and both of them were being selected on correctly.

## Echo

After the run: a population sitting between two optima and standing at neither,
a load carried in full every generation and paid down only across evolutionary time,
and a remedy that works by cutting the two sexes apart rather than by settling anything between them.
