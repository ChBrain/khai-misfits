# Piece: The Juvenile Agreement

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). The stage at which the two sexes' fitness runs together: survival correlated strongly and positively across the sexes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Before differentiation, in the same shared genome as everything else.
Answered nowhere, because there is nothing here to answer.

## Load Bearing

Without it the conflict could be dismissed as an artefact of sharing a genome and nothing more.
It is load bearing because it is **the control the mechanism supplies about itself**: everything else is identical at this stage and the conflict is absent, so sharing is not sufficient and the divergence of the phenotypes is what switches it on.

## Apparent

An unremarkable positive correlation, of the kind fitness measures usually show.
It reads as the expected result rather than as the informative one, which is why the finding is usually stated as the negative adult correlation alone.

## Yearbook

Measured alongside [the negative correlation](piece_the_negative_correlation.md), in the same forty cloned genomes.
It is what [diverging](process_diverging.md) ends, and the reason the conflict can be given a starting point at all.
