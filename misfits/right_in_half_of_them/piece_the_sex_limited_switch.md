# Piece: The Sex-Limited Switch

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). Expression restricted to the sex an allele benefits: the one real remedy, and a slow one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the genome](place_the_genome.md), at whichever loci a modifier has reached.
Answered on evolutionary time, which is the only schedule available.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play has no remedy and the misfit is merely permanent.
It is load bearing because the remedy **works by separating rather than reconciling**: nothing is settled between the sexes, the sharing is simply ended at one point, and the dimorphism that results is the visible record of a conflict that used to be there.

## Apparent

Sexual dimorphism, which looks like a design.
It reads as two sexes built differently on purpose, and the fact that each difference marks a conflict resolved rather than a specification met is not legible from the animal.

## Yearbook

Run by [limiting](process_limiting.md), against [the shared locus](piece_the_shared_locus.md).
Most loci are not sex-limited, and work following the process has found sex-specific adaptation arrested partway rather than complete.
