# Place: The Rut

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). Where fitness is decided for one of the two sexes, and where the advantage is unmistakably an advantage.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Competition among males over access to mating, settled on size, condition and armament.
An outcome that is a real difference in offspring left.

## Holds

The selective pressure in one direction, at full strength and entirely legitimate.
It holds the reason the variant spreads at all: nothing here is a defect, an accident or an exploitation, only one sex being better than another at the thing that sex is selected on.

## Offers

A clear, measurable advantage to whoever holds the variant, taken from rivals of the same sex and from nobody else.

## Withheld

Any sight of the other half.
Nothing at this place registers what the same variant will do in a daughter, so the pressure operating here is blind to the cost it is generating elsewhere.
