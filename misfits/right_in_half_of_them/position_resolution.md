# Position: Resolution

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). The seat of sex-limited expression: the remedy that ends the sharing rather than settling the conflict.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A modifier that restricts expression to one sex.
Real effect where it arises: the conflict at that locus ends and dimorphism follows.
The same timescale and the same constraints as any other variant.

## Orders

Arise, rarely.
Spread, if favoured.
Resolve the conflict at the loci it reaches, and nowhere else.

## Loses

Nothing, and its bound is the play's honesty: most loci are not sex-limited, the modifiers must arise and spread like anything else, and the process has been found arrested partway rather than complete.
Because the remedy runs on evolutionary time while the load is borne every generation, resolution and burden are not alternatives but simultaneous states.
It ends having genuinely fixed part of it, on a schedule nothing can accelerate.

## Drives

Toward expression in the sex it suits,
right in every single instance,
so the only real remedy works by cutting the two apart and never by reconciling them.
