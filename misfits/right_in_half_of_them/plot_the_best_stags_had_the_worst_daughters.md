# Plot: The Best Stags Had the Worst Daughters

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A red deer population on an island, followed across decades, with parentage and lifetime offspring counts for individual animals.
[The Pedigree](place_the_pedigree.md), deep enough to correlate a father's fitness with his daughter's.

## Action

[Pulling](process_pulling.md) and [settling](process_settling.md), read off from the record: [The Stag](persona_the_stag.md) in [the selected seat](position_selected.md) and [The Hind](persona_the_hind.md) in [the burdened seat](position_burdened.md), across generations.

## Stage

[The Stag](persona_the_stag.md), on [Leave More Than the Others](plan_leave_more_than_the_others.md), competes and wins and leaves more offspring than his rivals, taking nothing from any female.
[The Hind](persona_the_hind.md), on [Do the Best I Can With It](plan_do_the_best_i_can_with_it.md), leaves fewer than a female without the variant, with no event in her life at which this happened.

## Tension

The intersex genetic correlation for lifetime fitness came out at about minus nought point four eight, in the wild.
That is the sentence the whole play exists for: **the stags that left the most offspring fathered the daughters that left the fewest**, so selection was running against males carrying the alleles for high female fitness, in a long-lived animal in a population nobody arranged and nobody manipulated. And it is invisible from inside any single life. No hind is aware of a deficit, no stag is aware of imposing one, and the quantity exists only as a property of a population measured across decades.
