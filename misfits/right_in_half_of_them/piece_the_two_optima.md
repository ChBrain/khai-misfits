# Piece: The Two Optima

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). The best value of one shared trait for each sex, which are different numbers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set by what each sex is selected on, at [the rut](place_the_rut.md) for one and at breeding for the other.
Answered nowhere: nothing reconciles them, because they are properties of two different jobs.

## Load Bearing

Without it the shared locus is harmless and everybody arrives.
It is load bearing because it supplies the **only disagreement in the play**: there is no interest, no strategy and no antagonist anywhere here, and the entire misfit is that one quantity has two correct answers and one setting.

## Apparent

A trade-off, of the sort every organism makes.
It reads as ordinary biological compromise, and the fact that the two ends of it sit in different individuals who cannot pool anything is what does not show.

## Yearbook

Separated by [diverging](process_diverging.md), balanced by [settling](process_settling.md).
Their distance apart is what [the load](piece_the_load.md) is made of, and closing it is what [the sex-limited switch](piece_the_sex_limited_switch.md) achieves without moving either.
