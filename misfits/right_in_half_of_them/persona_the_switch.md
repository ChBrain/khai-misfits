# Persona: The Switch

## Taxonomy

[Resolution](position_resolution.md). Sex-limited expression: the one real remedy, which works by separating rather than by settling.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A modifier that restricts an allele's expression to the sex it benefits.
Genuinely effective where it arises: the conflict at that locus is resolved and dimorphism appears.
Not a compromise and not a truce, but an end to the sharing at one point.

## Action

Arises, rarely, and spreads if it is favoured, like anything else.
Frees one sex to approach its optimum without dragging the other away from theirs.
Leaves every locus it has not reached exactly as it was.

## Shadow

Nothing, and its limit is the play's honest bound: the remedy is real and it is slow, most loci are not sex-limited, and the work that has followed the process finds sex-specific adaptation arrested partway rather than run to completion.
Because it must arise and spread on the same timescale as anything else, the load is paid down in instalments while it is borne in full every generation.
It ends having resolved the conflict at the loci it reached, on a schedule nothing can be done about.

## Tell

Says: only in the one it suits.
Which is the answer, and arrives long after the question.
