# Position: Undifferentiated

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). The seat at which the two sexes want the same things, and where the correlation is measurably positive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A shared genome, as at every other stage.
A single objective, survival to breeding, held identically by both sexes.
No divergence in the phenotype for selection to act on differently.

## Orders

Survive, or not.
Do so under selection that runs the same way in both sexes.
Mature into one of the two seats that need opposite things.

## Loses

Nothing, and its emptiness is the finding: the shared genome is present here in full and the conflict is not, so sharing alone does not produce it and the divergence of the phenotypes must.
Because juvenile survival correlates strongly and positively across the sexes while adult reproductive success correlates strongly and negatively, the switch has a location and it has been measured.
It ends by differentiating, which is the moment the conflict begins.

## Drives

Toward survival, which both sexes want equally,
never once in tension,
so the seat that proves the mechanism is the one the mechanism is absent from.
