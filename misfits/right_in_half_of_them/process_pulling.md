# Process: Pulling

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). Selection acting on one locus in two directions at once, correctly in both.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A variant with opposite fitness effects in the two sexes.
At [the rut](place_the_rut.md) in one direction, and everywhere the other sex breeds in the other.

## Direction

Toward the male optimum and the female optimum simultaneously.
Neither pressure is weak, mistaken or perverse: each is selection doing exactly what selection does, on individuals that differ in the number of offspring they leave.
And they cannot be reconciled, because they act through the same locus, so any change that improves one sex's position degrades the other's by the same act.

## Lever

The shared locus, which is what makes the two pressures antagonistic rather than independent.
Because both are acting on one quantity, this is not two problems that happen to conflict but one quantity with two correct answers.

## Echo

A variant selected for and against at the same time.
Experimental evolution in fruit flies over twenty-nine generations established that such variants are not rare, by releasing a locus from expression in one sex and watching what happened.
