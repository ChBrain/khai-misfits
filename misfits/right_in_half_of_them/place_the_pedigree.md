# Place: The Pedigree

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md). Where the conflict becomes visible: a father's lifetime fitness set beside his daughter's, across decades of one population.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Parentage, lifespans and offspring counts, recorded individually over many years in a wild population on an island.
A record deep enough to compute a correlation between the sexes.

## Holds

The only view from which the misfit exists as a fact rather than an argument.
It holds the finding that the stags that left the most offspring fathered the daughters that left the fewest, an intersex genetic correlation for lifetime fitness of about minus nought point four eight.

## Offers

A measurement of something no individual animal experiences and no single generation displays.

## Withheld

Any account of when it happened.
The correlation is a property of a population across decades, so there is no moment in any animal's life at which the cost was incurred and nothing an observer of one animal would ever see.
