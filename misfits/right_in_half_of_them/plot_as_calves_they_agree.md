# Plot: As Calves They Agree

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Forty haploid genomes, cloned, and their fitness read in both sexes at two stages of life.
[The Juvenile Agreement](piece_the_juvenile_agreement.md), about to end.

## Action

[Diverging](process_diverging.md): [The Calf](persona_the_calf.md), in [the undifferentiated seat](position_undifferentiated.md), matures into either [The Stag](persona_the_stag.md) or [The Hind](persona_the_hind.md).

## Stage

[The Calf](persona_the_calf.md), on [Need the Same Things for Now](plan_need_the_same_things_for_now.md), is selected on identically in both sexes: juvenile survival correlates **strongly and positively** across them.
Then the phenotypes differentiate, and adult reproductive success correlates **strongly and negatively**.

## Tension

The mechanism supplies its own control, and this is it.
The shared genome is present in full at both stages. The conflict is present at only one. So sharing a genome is **not sufficient** to produce the antagonism, and what produces it is the divergence of what the two bodies are for. That makes the conflict a thing with a start rather than a permanent condition of sexual reproduction, and it locates the start precisely: at the point where the same trait stops having one best value.
