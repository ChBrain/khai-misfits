# Plan: Leave More Than the Others

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the selected seat](position_selected.md), inside [Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Selected](position_selected.md)

## Direction

Compete, win, and leave more offspring than your rivals, which is the whole of what being favoured means. Take nothing from the other sex and transmit to it anyway, because transmission cannot be made selective.

## Orders

([The Stag](persona_the_stag.md) wins more than his share of matings, leaves more offspring than his rivals, and passes the variant to half of them including the daughters.)

## Implementation

([The Rut](place_the_rut.md), where the advantage is realised; the [pulling](process_pulling.md) process acting in this direction; the outcome recorded in [the pedigree](place_the_pedigree.md) as [the negative correlation](piece_the_negative_correlation.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the advantage is over other males and is taken from no female
- [ ] more offspring than rivals is the definition of being favoured
- [ ] half of those offspring are the sex the variant does not suit
- [ ] the transmission cannot be moderated by anything the seat does
