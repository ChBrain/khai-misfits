# Plot: Only in the One It Suits

## Taxonomy

[Right in Half of Them](play_right_in_half_of_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A modifier that would restrict expression to the sex the allele benefits.
[The Sex-Limited Switch](piece_the_sex_limited_switch.md), which is the real remedy and does genuinely work.

## Action

[Limiting](process_limiting.md): [The Switch](persona_the_switch.md), in [the resolution seat](position_resolution.md), reaches a locus and ends the conflict there.

## Stage

[The Switch](persona_the_switch.md), on [Turn It Off in One of Them](plan_turn_it_off_in_one_of_them.md), frees one sex to approach its optimum without dragging the other away, and leaves every locus it has not reached exactly as it was.
Dimorphism appears where it has been, and is the visible record of a conflict that used to exist.

## Tension

The remedy works by separating the two sexes rather than by settling anything between them, and that is as good as it gets.
Nothing is reconciled here. The sharing is ended at one point, which is the only shape a resolution can take when the difficulty is that the two parties are the same. And the schedule is the honest bound: most loci are not sex-limited, the modifiers must arise and spread like any other variant, and work following the process has found sex-specific adaptation **arrested partway** rather than run to completion. So the load is paid down in instalments over evolutionary time and borne in full every generation, which are not alternatives but the same state described on two clocks.
