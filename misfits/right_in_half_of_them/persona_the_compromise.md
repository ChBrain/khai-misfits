# Persona: The Compromise

## Taxonomy

[Displaced](position_displaced.md). The trait value the population actually sits at: between two optima and at neither.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A number, arrived at rather than decided.
The point where the pull from one sex balances the pull from the other, weighted by how hard each pulls.
Nobody's preference and nobody's proposal.

## Action

Sits where the opposing selections balance.
Moves when either pressure changes, toward the sex that is pulling harder.
Is never at either optimum, at any point, under any circumstance.

## Shadow

The fitness both sexes are short of, which is a real and permanent quantity: the seat is not a settlement between parties but the resting point of two forces, so it has no claim to being a good outcome for anybody and is not a bad decision by anybody either.
Because it cannot be improved for one sex without being worsened for the other by the same locus, there is nothing here for optimisation to work on.
It ends holding a load that every individual in the population carries and none of them can put down.

## Tell

Says nothing, being a value rather than a party.
It is what everybody has, and what nobody would have chosen.
