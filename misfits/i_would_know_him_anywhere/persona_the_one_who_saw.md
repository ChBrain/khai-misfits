# Persona: The One Who Saw

## Taxonomy

[One Who Saw](position_one_who_saw.md). The witness, honest, helpful, and as sure as anyone ever is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Someone who saw a stranger for a few seconds under stress, had no reason at the time to be memorising anything, and wants very much to help.
Nothing to gain, nothing against anybody, and no idea that the task in front of them is harder than the one they think they are doing.

## Action

Looks at the row, picks the face that feels right, and says how certain they are.
Repeats it afterward, in good faith, every time asked.

## Shadow

Has no private sensation of being in the harder condition.
The certainty is produced by the same machinery that produced the pick, so it cannot report on that machinery's blind spot, and there is nothing available to introspect: the experience of a mistaken cross-race identification and a correct own-race one are, from the inside, the same experience.

## Tell

Says the truest thing they know, which is how sure they are, and it is not evidence of the thing the room takes it for.
