# Plot: As Sure as Anyone

## Taxonomy

[I Would Know Him Anywhere](play_i_would_know_him_anywhere.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The question of how certain, asked at the moment of the pick, before anybody knows whether it was right.

## Action

[The words at the time](piece_the_words_at_the_time.md) are recorded verbatim, honestly, and then [firming up](process_firming_up.md) begins: the account repeated to detectives, to counsel, in preparation, and at last in [the witness box](place_the_witness_box.md).

## Stage

The witness in court is more certain than the witness in the viewing room was, and has learned nothing new in between.

## Tension

The certainty is manufactured by the same machinery that manufactured the pick, so it cannot report on that machinery's blind spot.
Confidence at a first clean identification genuinely does carry information about accuracy, which is why recording it early was a real advance and why relying on it is sound rather than naive. What it cannot register is this. There is no private sensation of having been in the harder condition: from the inside, a mistaken identification across the line and a correct one within it are the same experience, so the witness's honesty is perfectly intact and their report is uninformative in precisely the way nobody present can detect.
