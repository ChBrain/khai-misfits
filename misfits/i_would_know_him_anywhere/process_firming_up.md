# Process: Firming Up

## Taxonomy

[I Would Know Him Anywhere](play_i_would_know_him_anywhere.md). Certainty growing between the viewing room and the courtroom.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Repetition: the account given again to detectives, to counsel, in preparation, and at last in [the witness box](place_the_witness_box.md).
Feedback of every kind, including the simple fact that a charge was brought.

## Direction

Toward an identification recalled more easily each time, and therefore experienced as stronger.
Toward a witness in court who is more certain than the witness in the viewing room was, having learned nothing new.

## Lever

Fluency mistaken for evidence, which is why the confidence taken at the pick is worth so much more than the confidence offered at trial.

## Echo

A sincere witness whose certainty has risen while their information has not, testifying honestly to both.
