# Process: Seeing It Happen

## Taxonomy

[I Would Know Him Anywhere](play_i_would_know_him_anywhere.md). A thin encoding, made thinner by the pairing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A few seconds of a stranger's face, under stress, with no intention of remembering it.
[The One Who Saw](persona_the_one_who_saw.md), who did not know they were about to become a witness.

## Direction

Toward a weak trace, which is what a brief frightening glimpse produces for anybody.
Toward a weaker one across the line, degraded in both directions at once.

## Lever

The mirror effect: fewer correct identifications and more false ones, the second being what puts a particular innocent person in a cell.

## Echo

A memory that feels no different from a strong one, because nothing in the experience reports on its own quality.
