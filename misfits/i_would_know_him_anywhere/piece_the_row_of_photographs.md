# Piece: The Row of Photographs

## Taxonomy

[The Viewing Room](place_the_viewing_room.md). The array, composed correctly, asking a question harder than it looks.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the table in [the viewing room](place_the_viewing_room.md), fillers matched to the description, administered by someone who does not know the answer.

## Load Bearing

It is a discrimination task disguised as a recognition task.
Fair composition requires the fillers to resemble the description, so the array deliberately presents several similar faces and asks the witness to tell them apart: that is precisely the operation the effect degrades, which means the fairer the array, the more load it places on the one faculty that is weaker here.

## Apparent

Six photographs, evenly lit, properly matched, with nothing to distinguish the suspect from the fillers.
The state of the art, and genuinely better than what came before.

## Yearbook

Improved steadily for decades against suggestion, bias in composition and administrator influence, all of which were real problems and are now largely solved.
Never modified for the relationship between the witness and the faces, because no procedure can change what the witness brought with them.
