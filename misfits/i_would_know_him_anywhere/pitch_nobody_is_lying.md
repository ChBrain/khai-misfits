# Pitch: Nobody Is Lying

## Taxonomy

[I Would Know Him Anywhere](play_i_would_know_him_anywhere.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Careful and procedural. A witness who wants to help and has nothing to gain, an array composed properly, an administrator who does not know the answer, a confidence recorded at the time, a jury doing exactly what reasonable people do with strong evidence.
Every account truthful, every safeguard observed, every step defensible on review.

## Undertow

Under the care, a skill built unevenly by a lifetime of looking, which is not a belief and cannot be corrected by having better ones.
An error rate that belongs to a pair rather than to a person, and certainty produced by the same machinery that produced the mistake.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a room in which every single person is telling the truth, and the truth is not enough to get the answer.

## Echo

Afterward: the identification holding up on every review because there was nothing wrong with it, and a large share of the eyewitness cases later undone by DNA turning out to have crossed the same line.
