# Persona: The One in the Row

## Taxonomy

[One in the Row](position_one_in_the_row.md). The person picked, who did nothing and can prove nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Somebody who resembles a description, placed in an array for that reason, and selected by an honest witness.
Innocent, and holding no evidence of it beyond an account nobody can check.

## Action

Denies it, consistently, from the first moment and afterward.
Faces a witness who is telling the truth and an accusation with no defect in it anywhere.

## Shadow

Cannot attack the identification without attacking a person the room can see is sincere.
There is no suggestion to expose, no lying witness to break, no procedural breach to appeal and no motive to impugn, so every ordinary route to challenging identification evidence is closed by the very cleanliness of the process: the case against this persona is strong on every dimension that can be examined and wrong on the one that cannot.

## Tell

Is convicted on true testimony, which is the outcome no safeguard in the building was designed to prevent.
