# Position: Twelve

## Taxonomy

[I Would Know Him Anywhere](play_i_would_know_him_anywhere.md). The seat that must weigh, with only one scale.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An honest witness, a clean procedure, and a confident identification.
No expertise in memory, and until now no reason to need any.

## Orders

Weigh the evidence.
Use certainty as the measure of reliability, as everybody does.

## Loses

The one dimension, to the absence of any instrument for it.
Confidence at a first clean identification genuinely does carry information, so relying on it is sound practice rather than an error being committed here: what it does not carry is this, and there is no question the seat could have asked, no demeanour it could have read and no test available in the room that would separate the accurate identification from the mistaken one.

## Drives

Toward the verdict the evidence supports, on evidence that is true and insufficient in a way nothing present could reveal.
