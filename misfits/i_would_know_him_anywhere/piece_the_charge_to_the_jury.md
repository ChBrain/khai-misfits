# Piece: The Charge to the Jury

## Taxonomy

[The Witness Box](place_the_witness_box.md). The warning that works, and does not stay where it is aimed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Delivered in [the witness box](place_the_witness_box.md)'s courtroom at the close, where some jurisdictions instruct juries on cross-racial identification and others do not.

## Load Bearing

It is the only correction that reaches the room where the decision is made.
Telling twelve people that identification across this line is less reliable is true, useful, and the product of decades of work: the difficulty is that an instrument which lowers the weight given to a class of evidence cannot pick out the members of that class which were right, so it discounts the accurate identification by the same amount as the mistaken one.

## Apparent

A short passage of law, accurate, cautious, and hard-won.
Adopted in some jurisdictions after long argument and refused in others on the ground that it invades the jury's province.

## Yearbook

Fought for over decades, and still uneven: available in some courts, unavailable in others, and delivered at the end of a trial to people who have already watched a sincere witness point across a room.
Carries no way of saying which case this is, because nothing does.
