# Piece: The Words at the Time

## Taxonomy

[The Viewing Room](place_the_viewing_room.md). The confidence recorded at the pick, which is good evidence of the wrong thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Written down in [the viewing room](place_the_viewing_room.md) at the moment of the identification, before anybody says whether it was right.

## Load Bearing

It is the room's only reliability instrument, and it is genuinely a good one.
Confidence expressed at a first clean identification does carry real information about accuracy, which is why taking it early and verbatim is a real advance and why the room is right to rely on it: what it cannot register is this effect, because the certainty is produced by the same machinery as the pick and has no separate access to that machinery's limits.

## Apparent

A sentence in the witness's own words, taken before contamination, exactly as the research recommends.
A safeguard working properly.

## Yearbook

Introduced to solve a real problem: confidence stated months later, after feedback and repetition, tells you almost nothing.
Solved that problem, and inherited the assumption underneath it, that certainty is the quantity worth recording.
