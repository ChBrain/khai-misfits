# Plot: A Stranger, for Nine Seconds

## Taxonomy

[I Would Know Him Anywhere](play_i_would_know_him_anywhere.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A brief look at an unfamiliar face, under stress, with no reason at the time to be memorising anything at all.

## Action

[Seeing it happen](process_seeing_it_happen.md): [The One Who Saw](persona_the_one_who_saw.md), holding [One Who Saw](position_one_who_saw.md), encodes what a frightened person encodes in a few seconds.

## Stage

The trace is thin, as it would be for anybody, and thinner here in both directions at once.

## Tension

The degradation runs both ways, and the second way is the one that convicts somebody.
Across the line there are fewer correct identifications and more false ones, which is the pattern the research calls a mirror effect. A drop in correct identifications only lets a guilty person go. A rise in false ones puts a particular innocent person in a row of photographs and then in a cell. And the memory itself feels no different from a strong one, because nothing in the experience of remembering reports on the quality of what is being remembered.
