## Tension

The explanation the room reaches for first is the wrong one, and removing it changes nothing.
The effect appears in infants long before anybody holds an opinion about anything, it tracks contact rather than conviction, and it sits at full strength in witnesses with no measurable prejudice of any kind. So this is not a story that can be closed by finding the bigot: strip every bad motive out of the play and the mechanism is exactly where it was, which is what makes it structural rather than moral.
