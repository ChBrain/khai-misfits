# Persona: The Years of Looking

## Taxonomy

[Years of Looking](position_years_of_looking.md). The tuning that built the skill, out of whatever faces were around.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A lifetime of ordinary looking: family, school, street, screen. Not training, not effort, and nobody's decision.
The quiet accumulation that makes one person's eye sharp for differences another's eye passes over.

## Action

Tunes recognition to the crowd it was given, sharpening the distinctions that mattered there.
Delivers a skill that feels uniform from the inside and is not.

## Shadow

Is not an attitude, which is the first thing everyone assumes it is.
The effect appears in infants long before anyone holds an opinion about anything, it tracks contact rather than belief, and it sits identically in a witness with no measurable prejudice at all: so the explanation the room reaches for first is the wrong one, and every bad motive can be removed without touching the mechanism.

## Tell

Cannot be corrected by holding better beliefs, and is not improved at all by knowing about it.
