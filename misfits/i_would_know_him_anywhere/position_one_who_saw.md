# Position: One Who Saw

## Taxonomy

[I Would Know Him Anywhere](play_i_would_know_him_anywhere.md). The seat that reports honestly on what it cannot inspect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A few seconds of a stranger's face, under stress, encoded without any intention of encoding it.
Complete good faith, and a real desire to get it right.

## Orders

Look at the row.
Pick the one that feels right, and say how sure you are.

## Loses

Any access to the thing that determines whether it worked.
The certainty is generated by the same machinery that generated the pick, so it cannot report on that machinery's limits, and the seat has no private sensation of being in the harder condition: from the inside, a mistaken identification across the line and a correct one within it are indistinguishable, which means the seat's honesty is intact and its report is uninformative in exactly the way nobody can detect.

## Drives

Toward the truest statement it is able to make, which is how sure it is, and which the room will hear as something else.
