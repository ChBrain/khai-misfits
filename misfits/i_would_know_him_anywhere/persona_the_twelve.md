# Persona: The Twelve

## Taxonomy

[Twelve](position_twelve.md). The jury, weighting the strongest evidence in front of them, correctly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Twelve reasonable people, attentive, taking the task seriously, with no expertise in memory and no reason to need any.
Given an honest witness, a clean procedure, and a confident identification.

## Action

Weighs the identification heavily, because on every visible dimension it deserves it.
Uses confidence as the measure of reliability, which is what everyone does and what it usually is.

## Shadow

Has no instrument for the invisible dimension, and is not wrong to lack one.
Confidence at a first clean identification does carry real information, so listening to it is sound practice rather than a mistake being made here: what it does not carry is this particular effect, and there is no question the jury could have asked, no demeanour it could have read and no test in the room that would have separated an accurate identification from a mistaken one.

## Tell

Reaches the verdict that the evidence supports, on evidence that is true and insufficient in a way nothing available could show.
