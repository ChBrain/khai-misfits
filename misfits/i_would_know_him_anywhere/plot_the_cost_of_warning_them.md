# Plot: The Cost of Warning Them

## Taxonomy

[I Would Know Him Anywhere](play_i_would_know_him_anywhere.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The effect understood at last, and the answers on the table: change the presentation, instruct the jury, require corroboration, or build the contact that would have prevented it.

## Action

[Get it right this time](plan_get_it_right_this_time.md): [The Twelve](persona_the_twelve.md) reach for the only correction that arrives where the decision is made, which is [the charge to the jury](piece_the_charge_to_the_jury.md).

## Stage

Each answer works, and each one is paid for by somebody the answer was not aimed at.

## Tension

An instrument that lowers the weight on a class of evidence cannot pick out the members of that class that were right.
Presenting the photographs one at a time lowers false identifications and lowers correct ones with them, so fewer innocent people are picked and more guilty ones walk free. The instruction discounts the accurate identification by exactly as much as the mistaken one, and is given at the close of a trial to people who have already watched a sincere witness point across a room. Requiring corroboration protects the wrongly identified by abandoning the correctly identified. And contact does predict improvement, at a relationship the research puts near minus fifteen hundredths and strongest in childhood, which is not a remedy available to anybody on the day.
