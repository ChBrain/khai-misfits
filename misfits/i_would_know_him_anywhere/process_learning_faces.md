# Process: Learning Faces

## Taxonomy

[I Would Know Him Anywhere](play_i_would_know_him_anywhere.md). A skill tuning itself, over a lifetime, to whoever was around.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Ordinary exposure, beginning in infancy and never stopping.
[The Years of Looking](persona_the_years_of_looking.md), holding [Years of Looking](position_years_of_looking.md).

## Direction

Toward sharp discrimination among the faces seen often, and blunter discrimination among the faces seen rarely.
Toward a competence that feels uniform from the inside and is not.

## Lever

Exposure, which is not a belief and cannot be revised by holding better ones.

## Echo

An uneven skill nobody chose, present in infants before anyone has an opinion, and unimproved by learning that it exists.
