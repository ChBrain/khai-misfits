# Plot: The Column That Works

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A vessel alongside, an inspector with authority to board, and published criteria to check it against.
[The Detention Rate](piece_the_detention_rate.md), accumulating across ports and years.

## Action

[Inspecting](process_inspecting.md) then [ranking](process_ranking.md): [The Inspector](persona_the_inspector.md), holding [the ranker's seat](position_ranker.md), records real deficiencies, and [The List](persona_the_list.md) in [the signal's seat](position_signal.md) publishes the three-year rate by flag.

## Stage

[The Inspector](persona_the_inspector.md), on [Inspect What Can Be Inspected](plan_inspect_what_can_be_inspected.md), confines the finding to what is aboard, which is why it is trustworthy.
[The List](persona_the_list.md), on [Publish the One Column](plan_publish_the_one_column.md), makes the comparison public, and insurers, charterers and authorities act on it.

## Tension

This is a regulation working, and the play needs that established before anything else.
No proxy has decayed and nothing is being gamed: the deficiencies are real, the detentions are warranted, the criteria are published and the findings are appealable. Ships got safer. **Every later difficulty rests on how completely this succeeds**, which is what puts the misfit outside anybody's competence as well as outside their conduct, since there is no weak instrument here to point at.
