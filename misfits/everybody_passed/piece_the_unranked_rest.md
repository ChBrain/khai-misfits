## Load Bearing

Without it the decision has nothing left to run on and the convergence is harmless.
It is load bearing because it is where **all** the remaining information sits: once the published column is flat, these are the only dimensions that can distinguish two candidates, so they necessarily settle the choice, whatever anybody's priorities are.

## Apparent

Nothing about weighing them looks like avoidance, and in the presence of a flat safety column, weighing them is simply what deciding means.
