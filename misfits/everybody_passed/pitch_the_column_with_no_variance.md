# Pitch: The Column With No Variance

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Flat, administrative, tabular.
The register of a performance list: flag, inspections, detentions, a three-year rate, a band.
Nothing accusing anywhere in it, because every figure is accurate and the quantity it tracks improved.

## Undertow

Under the table, a column that has stopped saying anything.
Every serious candidate reads the same, which is the outcome the whole regime was built to produce, and which leaves an owner reading down it with no reason to prefer one flag over another on the only dimension anybody publishes.
Success and usefulness are not two settings on one dial. Here they run in opposite directions.

## Nerve

The unease of watching an inspection regime work exactly as intended,
raise the thing it measures everywhere it reaches,
and thereby hand the entire decision to the columns it never covered.

## Echo

After the run: a table that is correct, a fleet that is safer than it was, a flag chosen on price and paperwork and disclosure,
and a line in a convention requiring a genuine link that nobody has ever had to define,
because agreeing what it meant was the one thing that would have stopped the convention being signed.
