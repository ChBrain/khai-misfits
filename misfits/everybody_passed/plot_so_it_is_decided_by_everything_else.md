## Cue

## Action

## Stage

[The Owner](persona_the_owner.md) selects on the columns with variance still in them, lawfully, on terms each registry states openly.
[The Seafarer](persona_the_seafarer.md), on [Sail Under Whichever It Is](plan_sail_under_whichever_it_is.md), takes the genuine benefit of the measured dimension and the terms the unmeasured ones settled.

## Tension

Nobody avoided anything, and the whole decision has moved.
The owner checked the safety column first and was right to. Having checked it, they found it silent, and something had to decide. So the flag is chosen on cost and speed and crewing and disclosure, none of which is ranked, none of which is aboard a ship, and none of which any inspector has standing over. **The dimensions that settle the choice are exactly the ones the working regime cannot reach**, and the seafarer, who gains most directly from the part that works, has no view of the part that decided.
