# Plot: The Line Nobody Defined

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The rule that was written to reach precisely this.
[The Genuine Link](piece_the_genuine_link.md): a flag state and its ships must be genuinely connected.

## Action

[Sailing](process_sailing.md) continues at [the open sea](place_the_open_sea.md), where no port authority is in reach and the flag state's own diligence is the whole of the oversight.

## Stage

The provision is binding on every party and has no agreed definition, so no test exists for whether any particular registration satisfies it.
The reason is not neglect: any definition would have suited some maritime states and cost others, so leaving it open was the price of agreement, and a convention that fixed it would not have been signed.

## Tension

The safeguard is present, and that is worse than absent.
An absent rule is a gap somebody could propose filling. A rule in force with no applicable test **occupies the place a check would go**: it can be cited, it satisfies the question of whether the matter is regulated, and it does no work. And the ambiguity is not a drafting failure to be corrected later, it is the consideration that bought the agreement, so proposing a definition now reopens the negotiation the omission was designed to close. Publish a second list and you need a party with standing to inspect a share register, which nobody has. Widen the safety index until it discriminates again and you have asked an inspection regime to stop succeeding. **The fix in every direction is the success run backwards.**
