# Position: Ranker

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md). The seat that can verify one dimension reliably and has no standing over any of the others.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Access to a vessel in port and the authority to detain it.
Published criteria, an appealable finding, and a three-year rolling count.
No access whatever to a share register, a tax position or a corporate domicile, none of which is aboard.

## Orders

Inspect what is aboard.
Record deficiencies and detain where they warrant it.
Contribute the count to the flag's rate.

## Loses

Nothing, and it takes the play's scope with it: the reach that makes the finding trustworthy is the reach that leaves every other column unmeasured, so the regime is authoritative about one thing and silent about the rest.
Because the silence follows from where an inspector can stand rather than from what anybody chose, no better version of the seat covers more.
It ends having measured the measurable dimension well.

## Drives

Toward a verifiable finding on a real deficiency,
right in every single instance to confine itself to what it can check,
so the seat that earns the regime its authority is the seat that fixes how narrow the authority is.
