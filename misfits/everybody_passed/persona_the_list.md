# Persona: The List

## Taxonomy

[Signal](position_signal.md). The published ranking itself: accurate, effective, and emptied of information by its own effect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A table. Flag, inspections, detentions, a three-year rate, a band.
Updated annually, used by insurers, charterers and port authorities, and taken seriously precisely because it is accurate.
Nothing rhetorical in it: it reports what happened.

## Action

Publishes the rate, which causes the registries to improve, which is the intended effect and the observed one.
Goes on publishing after they have improved.
Reports, correctly, that the serious candidates now sit in the same band.

## Shadow

Not misleading, and self-defeating as an instrument of choice: the improvement it caused is what removed the spread that made it useful for choosing, so the more completely it works the less it can distinguish.
Because the variance it lost was ships being detained, the loss is the outcome everybody wanted and cannot be undone or regretted.
It ends a true and successful table that decides nothing.

## Tell

Says: forty flags on the white list.
The best sentence in the play and the reason the decision happens elsewhere.
