# Piece: The Tonnage

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md). The fleet itself, most of it registered openly, and the quantity that makes the whole arrangement matter.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Spread across registries, the large majority of it by deadweight in open registers.
Counted everywhere and explained, usually, by the wrong mechanism.

## Load Bearing

Without it this is a curiosity about a table rather than an account of where the world's shipping is flagged.
It is load bearing because its **distribution is the evidence**: it sits overwhelmingly with registries in the top band, so its movement cannot be explained by a search for laxity and must be explained by the columns nobody publishes.

## Apparent

Evidence of a flight from standards, which is how it is usually read.
The reading is natural, is wrong on this data, and is corrected only by putting the fleet's distribution and the performance bands on the same page.
