# Persona: The Inspector

## Taxonomy

[Ranker](position_ranker.md). Verifies what can be verified from a deck, counts it honestly, and has no standing anywhere else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Practical, thorough, and correctly limited.
Boards a ship in port, works through the condition of the hull, the equipment, the documents and the crew's certificates, and records deficiencies.
Nothing arbitrary in it: the criteria are published and the findings are appealable.

## Action

Inspects, records deficiencies, detains where the deficiencies warrant it.
Contributes the detention to a three-year rolling count by flag.
Does not, and cannot, inspect a share register, a tax treatment or a corporate domicile, none of which is aboard the vessel.

## Shadow

Not narrow by choice, and decisive by that narrowness: the regime reaches exactly what a person standing on a deck can see, which is why it works and why it covers one dimension of the several a flag is chosen on.
So the scope that makes the finding reliable is the scope that leaves the other columns unmeasured, and no better inspector reaches them.
It ends having measured the measurable thing accurately, which is all anybody asked and less than the decision requires.

## Tell

Says: this vessel is detained until the deficiency is closed.
True, checkable, effective, and about the one dimension that can be checked this way.
