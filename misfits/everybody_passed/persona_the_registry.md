# Persona: The Registry

## Taxonomy

[Flag](position_flag.md). Answers the published ranking because a place at the top is worth money, and competes on everything the ranking does not contain.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Commercial, responsive, and genuinely compliant.
Runs a register, delegates survey work, and watches its own detention rate the way a business watches a rating.
No delinquency in it: the serious registries meet the standard and are measured meeting it.

## Action

Keeps its detention rate down, because a white-list flag is inspected less and trades more easily.
Invests in survey capacity and in shedding bad tonnage, which is the response the publication was designed to produce.
Competes for registrations on cost, speed, crewing rules and disclosure, because those are what is left to compete on.

## Shadow

Not lax, and not the villain the phrase open registry invites: the largest of them sit at the top of the table and one of them is the best-performing flag there is.
So the reading in which tonnage chases laxity is refuted by the very instrument built to detect laxity, and what the tonnage actually chases is the unranked columns.
It ends compliant on the published dimension and differentiated on every other, which is exactly what a published dimension does to a market.

## Tell

Says: we are on the white list.
Which is true, is the point, and is now true of every registry an owner would seriously consider.
