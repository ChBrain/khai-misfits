# Process: Choosing

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md). An owner selecting a flag, finding the published dimension identical across the shortlist, and deciding on the rest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A registration to be made and a table to consult.
At [the register](place_the_register.md), a shortlist of top-band flags.

## Direction

Toward a decision that has to be reached on something.
The owner does the prudent thing first and narrows by the published ranking, which is what it is for. Then the criterion runs out: **a column that reads the same everywhere cannot choose between anywhere**, so the remaining variance decides, and it is all in cost, processing time, crewing nationality rules and disclosure.
No step of that is evasive, and consulting the table more carefully changes none of it.

## Lever

The variance left in the unpublished columns, which is the only thing with information in it.
Because information for choosing lives in difference rather than in quality, the dimension everybody improved is the dimension that stopped mattering to the choice.

## Echo

A flag selected responsibly on safety and substantively on everything else.
The two are not in tension: they are consecutive steps of one proper process.
