# Position: Chooser

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md). The seat that consults the published column, finds no variance in it, and must still decide.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A commercial decision that has to be made and a published table to inform it.
Genuine reason to care about flag safety: detentions cost time, money and insurance.
A shortlist on which the safety column reads identically, and other columns that do not.

## Orders

Consult the ranking and narrow to the top band.
Find the shortlist indistinguishable on that dimension.
Decide on cost, speed, crewing and disclosure, because something has to decide it.

## Loses

The use of the one instrument built to inform it: having done the prudent thing and read the table, the seat is left with a criterion that separates nothing, and no fault of its own put it there.
Because a converged quantity carries no information for choosing, consulting it more carefully changes no outcome, and the decision necessarily falls to whatever still varies.
It ends having chosen properly on the measured dimension and entirely elsewhere in substance.

## Drives

Toward a flag that will not cost it detentions,
right in every single instance to check the list first,
so the seat that takes the published measure most seriously is the one it can help least.
