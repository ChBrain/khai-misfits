# Process: Converging

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md). Every registry that matters answering the ranking, so the spread the ranking depended on closes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A published band worth being in and a commercial reason to reach it.
At [the register](place_the_register.md), survey capacity funded and poor tonnage shed.

## Direction

Toward a top band that contains all the serious candidates.
This is the regime working, not failing: the three largest open registries sit in it and the best-performing flag of all belongs to an offshore financial centre, which rules out the reading in which tonnage runs to laxity.
And the same movement empties the column, since a quantity every candidate shares has no variance left to distinguish them by.

## Lever

Money, straightforwardly: fewer inspections, easier trade, better terms.
Because the incentive is real and the response is correct, the convergence cannot be prevented without weakening the regime that produced it.

## Echo

An improved fleet and an uninformative column, from one movement.
The improvement is genuine and is the point; the emptying is its shadow and was nobody's aim.
