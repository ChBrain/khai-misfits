# Process: Ranking

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md). Aggregating detentions by flag over three rolling years and publishing the result in bands.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Inspections accumulated across many ports.
At [the port](place_the_port.md) and beyond it, a rate computed for each flag with enough inspections to support one.

## Direction

Toward a public, comparative, actionable figure.
Publication is what gives the regime its force, because insurers, charterers and authorities act on the bands, so a place at the top is worth money and a place at the bottom costs it.
That is the mechanism of improvement and it is a good one.

## Lever

Comparability, which requires a common scale and enough observations, and which is what turns a set of local findings into pressure on a registry.
Because the pressure works through the comparison, the ranking's usefulness depends on there being differences to rank.

## Echo

A table that changes behaviour, at first by showing a spread.
Every later property of the play follows from how completely it succeeds at that.
