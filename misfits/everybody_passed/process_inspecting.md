# Process: Inspecting

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md). Boarding a vessel and verifying what is aboard, which is the part of the arrangement that plainly works.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A vessel alongside and an inspector with authority to board.
At [the port](place_the_port.md), hull, equipment, documents and certificates checked against published criteria.

## Direction

Toward a verifiable finding.
This step succeeds and is staged because it succeeds: the deficiencies are real, the detentions are warranted, the criteria are published and the finding is appealable.
Nothing downstream can be attributed to a weak instrument, which is what puts the misfit outside anybody's competence as well as outside anybody's conduct.

## Lever

Physical access to the vessel, which is the whole basis of the regime's authority and the whole of its limit.
Because what can be verified is what is aboard, the scope is set by where a person can stand rather than by what anybody chose to cover.

## Echo

A true record of one dimension.
The regime is authoritative about it and silent about the rest, and both follow from the same fact about berths.
