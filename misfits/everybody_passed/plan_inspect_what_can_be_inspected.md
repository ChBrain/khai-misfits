# Plan: Inspect What Can Be Inspected

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the ranker's seat](position_ranker.md), inside [Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Ranker](position_ranker.md)

## Direction

Board the vessel, work through what is aboard, record the deficiencies and detain where they warrant it. Do not reach for what is not on the ship, because there is no way to verify it from a deck and an unverifiable finding is not a finding.

## Orders

([The Inspector](persona_the_inspector.md) checks hull, equipment, documents and certificates, records deficiencies, and contributes detentions to the flag's three-year rolling count.)

## Implementation

([The Port](place_the_port.md), where a vessel can be boarded; the [inspecting](process_inspecting.md) process feeding [ranking](process_ranking.md); the finding recorded as [the detention rate](piece_the_detention_rate.md), and everything not aboard left as [the unranked rest](piece_the_unranked_rest.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the vessel is boarded and what is aboard is verified against published criteria
- [ ] deficiencies are recorded and detentions issued where warranted
- [ ] the count enters the flag's three-year rolling rate
- [ ] nothing off the vessel is assessed, there being no way to verify it from a deck
