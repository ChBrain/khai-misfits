# Persona: The Seafarer

## Taxonomy

[Bearer](position_bearer.md). Sails under whichever flag was chosen, on terms set by columns that were never ranked.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Professional, mobile, and working to a contract.
Serves on a vessel whose flag was decided before they joined it, by parties they will never meet.
Nothing resigned about it: the ship is seaworthy and the inspection regime is why.

## Action

Joins the vessel and sails.
Benefits directly from the converged column, since the ship is genuinely safer than the same ship would have been before the regime existed.
Works under nationality rules, contractual terms and an ownership structure decided by the unranked columns.

## Shadow

Not neglected on the measured dimension, and reached by no instrument on the others: the regime that made the ship safe is the regime whose success moved the decision to ground it does not cover.
So the seat gains, really, from the part that is published, and takes whatever the unpublished part settled, and both come from the same working system.
It ends safer than before and on terms nothing ranks.

## Tell

Says nothing about the flag, having had no part in choosing it.
Sails on a vessel that passes inspection, which is true and is not the whole of what the flag decided.
