# Plot: Where the Tonnage Actually Is

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The obvious reading, that tonnage runs to whichever flag asks least.
[The Tonnage](piece_the_tonnage.md), the large majority of it by deadweight in open registers, and the performance bands beside it.

## Action

[Converging](process_converging.md) has already run: [The Registry](persona_the_registry.md), holding [the flag's seat](position_flag.md), funded survey capacity and shed poor tonnage, because [the white list](piece_the_white_list.md) is worth money.

## Stage

[The Registry](persona_the_registry.md), on [Stay on the White List](plan_stay_on_the_white_list.md), answers the ranking exactly as publishing it intended.
The result is checkable: the three largest open registries sit in the top band, the best-performing flag of all belongs to an offshore financial centre, and the bottom band holds small registries carrying very little.

## Tension

The instrument built to detect a flight from standards reports that there is not one.
This is the plot that removes the story everybody arrives with. If tonnage were chasing laxity it would be at the bottom of the table, and it is at the top. So **open registry and low standard have come apart**, and whatever is moving the world's fleet is not the safety column, because on the safety column the destinations are the good ones. The question the rest of the play answers is what is moving it instead.
