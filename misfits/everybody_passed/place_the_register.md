# Place: The Register

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md). Where a ship is entered on a flag, and where the choice among converged candidates is actually settled.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An administrative office, a fee schedule, a processing time and a set of conditions.
An owner completing a registration that any of several flags would have accepted.

## Holds

The decision itself, and the columns that decide it.
It holds what the published table does not: what registration costs, how quickly it is granted, which nationalities may crew the vessel and how much of the ownership must be disclosed.

## Offers

A lawful and ordinary commercial transaction, completed quickly, on terms stated openly by the registry.

## Withheld

Any ranking of its own terms.
There is no white list for cost, speed, crewing or disclosure, so the dimensions that settle the choice are the ones with no published band and no party positioned to inspect them.
