# Everybody Passed: Reference

The research warrant for Everybody Passed.
The term is the house's; the play runs on port state control and ship registration, and on what happens to a published
measure after everybody meets it, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the performance table as **a measure that spends its own usefulness by working**: ships are inspected in port,
deficiencies are recorded, detentions are counted over three rolling years, and flags are ranked from best to worst and
published. The registries carrying the world's tonnage answer it, because a place in the top band means fewer
inspections and easier trade, and the answer shows in the figures. Then an owner choosing a flag reads down the safety
column and finds every serious candidate in the same band, so the column is accurate, the improvement behind it is
real, and it cannot separate two options. The play models the documented mechanics, the inspection confined to what is
aboard, the ranking that gives the finding commercial force, the convergence that force produces, the column emptied of
variance by its own success, the unranked dimensions that then decide everything, and the treaty rule written to reach
them and never defined, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a flag of convenience or a lax
inspector.

The governing law is that information for choosing lives in difference rather than in quality, so a measure that
succeeds well enough to be met everywhere stops being able to distinguish anywhere, and the decision it was built to
inform falls to whatever went unmeasured.

**Nobody in the sequence falls short, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The inspector
verifies what is aboard. The registry meets the standard because meeting it pays. The list publishes accurately. The
owner consults it before choosing. The seafarer sails a ship that is genuinely safer for all of it.

**And the instrument is sound, which the play establishes before anything else.** No proxy has decayed and nothing is
gamed: the deficiencies are real, the detentions are warranted, the criteria are published, the findings are
appealable, and ships got safer. Every later difficulty rests on how completely this succeeds, which is what puts the
misfit outside anybody's competence as well as outside their conduct, since there is no weak measurement here to point
at.

**Then the reading everybody arrives with is refuted by the instrument built to detect it.** The account in which
tonnage runs to whichever flag asks least predicts that the fleet sits at the bottom of the table. It does not. The
three largest open registries are all on the white list, the best-performing flag in the world belongs to an offshore
financial centre, and the bottom band holds small registries carrying very little. **Open registry and low standard
have come apart**, so whatever moves the world's fleet is not the safety column, because on the safety column the
destinations are the good ones.

**What moves it is that the column has no variance left.** When every candidate a serious owner would consider sits in
the same band, the figure separates none of them, and a quantity that is the same everywhere cannot decide between
anywhere. Reading the table more carefully does not help and caring more about safety does not help. So the choice
falls to the dimensions with variance still in them: what a registration costs, how quickly it is granted, which
nationalities may crew the vessel, and how much of the ownership behind it must be disclosed. None of those is ranked
and none is aboard a ship, so no inspector has standing over any of them.

**And the variance cannot be restored, because the variance was the harm.** A spread in detention rates meant ships
were being detained. Closing it is the outcome the whole regime exists to produce, so there is nothing here to regret
and nothing to undo, only a property to notice: **being good and being informative are different properties, and here
the first was achieved by spending the second.**

**Then the rule written to reach the other columns is present and inoperative.** The convention requires a genuine link
between a ship and its flag state. There is no agreed definition of what that means and the term is read loosely
wherever it is read at all. That is not neglect: any definition would have suited some maritime states and cost others,
so leaving it open was the price of agreement, and a convention that fixed it would not have been signed. A rule in
force with no applicable test is worse than an absent one, because **it occupies the place a check would go**: it can
be cited, it settles the question of whether the matter is regulated, and it does no work.

It is distinct from the house's **Metric Fixation**, which is the nearest incumbent and stages Goodhart's Law, where a
proxy adopted for an unmeasurable goal ceases to measure it because it is targeted. Remove that engine entirely, by
stipulating that every measure is a flawless measure of exactly what it measures, that nothing decays and nothing is
gamed, and this misfit stands whole: **it requires the measure to be working.** The detention rate never stopped
measuring ship safety, and ship safety genuinely improved. The difficulty is not a corrupted proxy but an accurate one
that every candidate now shares.

It is distinct from the house's **Nobody Paid for the Cut**, which stages the multitask distortion, where a fixed
effort budget is drawn to whichever dimension a contract can name. Remove that engine, by stipulating an unlimited
effort budget so that attending to one dimension costs nothing on another, and this stands: **a shipowner choosing a
registry is not allocating scarce effort.** It is a single discrete choice among alternatives, and the ranked dimension
has converged so it carries no information for choosing. That is the collapse of a criterion rather than the diversion
of effort, and it would happen to a chooser with infinite attention.

It is distinct from the house's **The Lowest Bar**, which stages the race to the bottom, where jurisdictions compete
for business by lowering standards. Remove that engine, by stipulating that no jurisdiction ever lowers a standard to
attract anybody, and this stands entire, because **the data already rules the race out**: the registries holding the
tonnage are in the top band, not the bottom. The movement here is selection across a distribution that converged
upward on the one measured dimension and was never coordinated on any other.

It is distinct from the house's **Hotelling's Law**, which stages minimum differentiation, where sellers converge on
the same position to capture the median customer and variety is what is lost. Nobody converged here to capture
anybody: the registries converged because an external inspection regime worked, the convergence is an improvement
rather than a loss, and what goes missing is not variety in the offering but information in a column.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the open registry as a
  flag of convenience in the pejorative sense or the owner as an evader, because nobody in the sequence has a decision
  to get wrong: the inspector verifies what is aboard and cannot verify what is not, which is why the finding is
  trustworthy; the registry meets the published standard because meeting it pays, which is exactly the response
  publishing it was designed to produce, and the largest of them are in the top band; the list publishes accurately and
  goes on being accurate after it has worked; the owner consults the ranking first, which is prudent and is what it is
  for, and decides on the remaining columns because the consulted one separates nothing; and the seafarer sails a ship
  that is genuinely safer for the whole arrangement. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure,
  so the misfit is that a measure met everywhere decides nothing anywhere.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Publishing a second list for the
  other columns requires a party with standing to inspect a share register or a tax position, which port state control
  has for what is on the deck and nothing has for what is in a corporate filing. Enforcing the genuine link requires
  first agreeing the definition that was avoided in order to reach agreement at all, which reopens the negotiation the
  omission was designed to close. Widening the safety index until it discriminates again is asking an inspection
  regime to stop succeeding. Restoring the variance means ships being detained, which is the harm the regime removed.
  And knowing the whole structure changes nothing at the counter, where a registration has to be completed today on
  whichever columns still differ.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed as the
  house's. The staging is the house's; the inspection regime, the performance lists, the registry history and the
  convention are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: **whether published performance lists raise standards or
  chiefly relocate poorer tonnage is genuinely contested**, and the play carries that argument rather than settling it;
  the figures are one regional regime in one period and are offered as the documented shape of the convergence rather
  than as constants, and a flag's band under one memorandum need not match its record elsewhere; port state control
  covers more than structural safety and the play does not claim the index is silent on everything but hull and
  equipment, only that registration cost, processing time, crewing nationality rules and ownership disclosure are
  outside it; and the account treats registries as comparable where they differ in size, history and function, which
  is its own simplification. **The play makes no claim about any registry, any flag state, any owner or any vessel, and
  gives no maritime, legal or commercial advice of any kind**, only that a column everybody satisfies has stopped
  choosing between them, and that the rule written to reach the others was left undefined to get it signed.
