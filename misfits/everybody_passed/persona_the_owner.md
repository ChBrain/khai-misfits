# Persona: The Owner

## Taxonomy

[Chooser](position_chooser.md). Reads the safety column first, finds it identical across every serious candidate, and decides on the columns nobody publishes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Commercial, diligent, and not indifferent to safety.
Checks the flag performance list before registering, because a poor flag means detentions, delays and higher insurance.
Nothing furtive in it: every step of the choice is lawful and most of it is ordinary business.

## Action

Consults the published ranking and narrows to white-list flags, which is prudent and is what the list is for.
Finds the shortlist indistinguishable on that dimension, because they all met the standard.
Chooses among them on registration cost, processing time, crewing nationality rules and disclosure requirements.

## Shadow

Not evasive, and steered by an absence: having done exactly what the regime asks by consulting the table, the owner is left with a column that separates nothing and a decision that still has to be made on something.
So diligence about safety and selection on tax and disclosure are not in tension here, they are sequential steps of one entirely proper process.
It ends having chosen responsibly on the measured dimension and freely on all the others.

## Tell

Says: they are all white list, so it comes down to cost and crewing.
Both halves accurate, and the second is only available because the first is.
