# Piece: The Genuine Link

## Taxonomy

[Everybody Passed](play_everybody_passed.md). The convention's requirement that a flag state and its ships be genuinely connected: binding, undefined, and occupying the place a check would go.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the treaty text, and in no test anybody can apply.
Answered nowhere: there is no agreed definition and the term is read loosely wherever it is read at all.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play has a gap and no account of why nothing fills it.
It is load bearing because the absence is **structural rather than accidental**: any definition would have suited some maritime states and cost others, so the ambiguity was the price of agreement, and a convention that defined it would not have been signed. The safeguard is therefore not missing, which could be remedied, but present and inoperative, which cannot.

## Apparent

A settled provision of international law, cited in every textbook.
It reads as a requirement in force, and the fact that no party can say what would satisfy it is a matter for specialists rather than a visible hole.

## Yearbook

Written to reach [the unranked rest](piece_the_unranked_rest.md) and reaching none of it.
It governs [the open sea](place_the_open_sea.md), where nothing else does, and it is the reason the interval between port calls has a rule and no test.
