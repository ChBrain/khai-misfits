## Action

## Stage

[The Owner](persona_the_owner.md), on [Choose on What Still Differs](plan_choose_on_what_still_differs.md), does the responsible thing first and consults the list, having real commercial reason to care since detentions cost time, money and insurance.

## Tension

The measure is true, is successful, and has nothing left to say.
This is the misfit standing still. **Information for choosing lives in difference, not in quality**, so a column every candidate shares cannot separate them however good the number is, and the number is good precisely because everybody reached it. Reading the table more carefully does not help. Caring more about safety does not help. The variance that would have made the column useful was ships being detained, so its loss is the outcome the whole regime was built to produce, and there is nothing here to regret and nothing to undo.
