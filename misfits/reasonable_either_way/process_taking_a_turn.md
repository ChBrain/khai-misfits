# Process: Taking a Turn

## Taxonomy

[Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md). Making the defensible choice, once, and recording it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A decision the analysis cannot proceed without, and data that make one answer look right.
[The One at the Keyboard](persona_the_one_at_the_keyboard.md), holding [One at the Keyboard](position_one_at_the_keyboard.md), writing [the exclusion](piece_the_exclusion.md) and [the covariate](piece_the_covariate.md).

## Direction

Toward the route a careful person would take through these particular numbers.
Each turn taken once, justified, and written down, with no alternative run and discarded.

## Lever

Judgement, applied to what is visible: the same faculty that makes the analysis good is the one that makes it data-dependent.

## Echo

One route walked, fully documented, and indistinguishable in the record from a route that had been fixed in advance.
