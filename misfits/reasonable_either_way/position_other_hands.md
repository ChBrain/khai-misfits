# Position: Other Hands

## Taxonomy

[Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md). The seat that shows the garden exists by walking different routes through it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The same file, the same question, and its own judgement.
Full sight of its own reasoning, and a willingness to publish it.

## Orders

Analyse independently, without conferring.
Show the route as well as the result.
Report the spread as it comes, including when the spread is embarrassing.

## Loses

The power to resolve what it reveals.
Having demonstrated that competent analysts split twenty to nine on the same numbers, the seat cannot then say which of the twenty-nine was right, because the disagreement is about judgement and not about correctness: so the finding is left less trusted and no better established.

## Drives

Toward transparency about how much the route matters, and away from any consensus the transparency was hoped to produce, since the honest answer turns out to be that one dataset supports several defensible conclusions.
