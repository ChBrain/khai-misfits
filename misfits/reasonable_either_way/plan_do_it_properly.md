# Plan: Do It Properly

## Taxonomy

A **personal scheme**: the intent of [The One at the Keyboard](persona_the_one_at_the_keyboard.md), inside [Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One at the Keyboard](persona_the_one_at_the_keyboard.md)

## Direction

Write the question down first. Look at the data properly before deciding anything. Make each choice the way a careful person would, justify it, record it, run the analysis once, and report all of it.

## Orders

(The One at the Keyboard fixes the hypothesis in advance, opens the file, writes [the exclusion](piece_the_exclusion.md) once the bad records are seen, adds [the covariate](piece_the_covariate.md) once the groups are seen to differ, runs the test once, and writes up every step into [the written account](place_the_written_account.md).)

## Implementation

([The Long Table](place_the_long_table.md); the [looking first](process_looking_first.md), [taking a turn](process_taking_a_turn.md) and [writing it up](process_writing_it_up.md) processes; [the line at point oh five](piece_the_line_at_point_oh_five.md) is what the result will be judged against.)

## Targets

- [ ] the hypothesis is written down before the data arrive
- [ ] every choice is made deliberately, justified, and recorded
- [ ] exactly one analysis is run, and no result is discarded unreported
- [ ] the account discloses every step that was taken
