# Persona: The One at the Keyboard

## Taxonomy

[One at the Keyboard](position_one_at_the_keyboard.md). Runs one analysis, honestly, and makes every choice in it after seeing the data.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful and unhurried. Writes the hypothesis down before the file arrives, and keeps the note.
Explains each decision as it is made and records it: the exclusion, the transform, the grouping, the control. Would show any of it to anybody.

## Action

Opens the file, looks at what is there, and begins deciding.
Writes [the exclusion](piece_the_exclusion.md) that any careful person would write, and adds [the covariate](piece_the_covariate.md) that is obviously necessary once the groups are visible.
Runs the analysis once, and reports it in full.

## Shadow

Cannot know what they would have done with different numbers, and neither can anyone else.
The care is genuine and is the mechanism: every choice was good _because_ it answered what was on the screen, and answering what is on the screen is exactly how the path came to be selected by the data. There is no version of being careful here that does not also do this.

## Tell

Says "I only ran it once", and is telling the truth, and it is the wrong count.
Would say, if asked whether a different file would have changed the analysis, "of course, that is what judgement is for", without hearing that as the admission it is.
