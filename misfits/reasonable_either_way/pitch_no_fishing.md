# Pitch: No Fishing

## Taxonomy

[Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Careful and unhurried. A good analyst working slowly, explaining each decision as it is made, writing it down as it is explained.
The pleasure of watching competence: nothing rushed, nothing skipped, nothing hidden. Everything a reviewer could ask for is already on the page before the reviewer asks.

## Undertow

Under the competence, a count that cannot be taken.
Because what would settle the question is not what was done but what would have been done with a different file, and nobody knows that, including the analyst. So the diligence and the flaw are the same act seen twice: the choices are careful _because_ the data were looked at, and they are compromised for exactly that reason.

## Nerve

The small vertigo of realising that a thing you did once, and did right, is being counted as though you had done it many times.

## Echo

Afterward: a clean write-up with every step recorded, a result that will not reproduce, a replicator who cannot say what they did differently, and no one anywhere in the record who did anything wrong.
