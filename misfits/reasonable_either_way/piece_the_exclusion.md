# Piece: The Exclusion

## Taxonomy

[The Long Table](place_the_long_table.md). The rule that removes the bad records, written after the bad records were seen.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On [the long table](place_the_long_table.md), written in the first hour, and copied into [the written account](place_the_written_account.md) with its justification.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the misfit would need a hidden step, and there is none.
It is the piece that carries the whole structure in miniature: a rule anyone would agree with, defensible on its face, disclosed in full, and selected by the very data it is applied to.

## Apparent

A sentence. Responses below a threshold that no attentive person could have produced, and three records with impossible timestamps, are removed.
Reviewers agree with it on sight, because it is right.

## Yearbook

Written to fit what was on the screen that morning.
Would have been written differently, and just as defensibly, had the impossible timestamps not been there and a cluster of implausible durations been there instead. That other rule was never written and is not anywhere.
