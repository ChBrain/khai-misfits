# Place: The Written Account

## Taxonomy

[Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md). Where one route becomes the story of the analysis, truthfully.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Methods, in order. The exclusion rule and its justification. The transform and why. The controls and the reason each was chosen.
One result, one test, one number.
Nothing omitted, nothing softened, no second analysis quietly dropped.

## Holds

The full and accurate record of the route walked.
[The Line at Point Oh Five](piece_the_line_at_point_oh_five.md), against which the number is judged, and which was fixed before any of this began.

## Offers

Complete accountability for everything that happened.
A reviewer can check every step, query every choice, and be answered.

## Withheld

The size of what did not happen.
The account is true and finishes there, and a true account of one route reads identically whether the garden held two branches or two thousand, so the document that discloses everything discloses nothing about the only quantity that would price the result.
