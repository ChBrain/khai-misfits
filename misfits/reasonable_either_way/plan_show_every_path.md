# Plan: Show Every Path

## Taxonomy

A **correction**: the intent of [The One at the Keyboard](persona_the_one_at_the_keyboard.md), inside [Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md), once the garden is understood.

## Owner

- Owner: [The One at the Keyboard](persona_the_one_at_the_keyboard.md)

## Direction

Empty the garden by walking all of it: enumerate every defensible choice at every decision point, run the whole grid of combinations, and publish the landscape of results instead of one number from one route.

## Orders

(The One at the Keyboard lists the defensible options at each turn, computes the full set of resulting analyses, and reports the distribution of outcomes rather than selecting from it.)

## Implementation

([The Written Account](place_the_written_account.md); the [branching](process_branching.md) process, made explicit and executed instead of left implicit; [the line at point oh five](piece_the_line_at_point_oh_five.md) applied to a spread rather than to a point.)

## Targets

- [ ] the defensible options at each decision point are enumerated openly
- [ ] the whole grid is run, not a route through it
- [ ] the distribution of results is published, including the unflattering regions
- [ ] the price is accepted: the answer is now a landscape, and a landscape is read selectively or not at all
