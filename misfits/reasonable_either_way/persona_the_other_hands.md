# Persona: The Other Hands

## Taxonomy

[Other Hands](position_other_hands.md). The other competent analysts, who would have chosen differently and can prove it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Numerous, qualified, and disagreeing.
Sixty-one of them across twenty-nine teams on one question about referees and red cards. Seventy teams on one imaging dataset. All working openly, all showing their reasoning, none of them cheating.

## Action

Take the same file and the same question, and route through it differently.
Return effect sizes from 0.89 to 2.93, twenty teams finding an effect and nine finding none, with twenty-one distinct combinations of controls between them.
On the imaging file, produce seventy workflows, no two identical.

## Shadow

Cannot settle it by being right, because they are not wrong.
Their spread is not a marker of poor work to be trained away: it is what competent judgement looks like when judgement is what the step requires. So the demonstration proves the garden exists and cannot say which path through it was correct, which leaves the finding no better off than before it was checked.

## Tell

Each explains their route and each explanation is convincing, including the ones that reach opposite conclusions.
