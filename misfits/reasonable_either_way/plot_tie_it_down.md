# Plot: Tie It Down

## Taxonomy

[Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The structure understood at last, and the two real instruments on the table.

## Action

[Taking a turn](process_taking_a_turn.md) before looking, or [branching](process_branching.md) made explicit and run in full: [The One at the Keyboard](persona_the_one_at_the_keyboard.md), holding [One at the Keyboard](position_one_at_the_keyboard.md), chooses between fixing the route in advance and publishing the whole landscape.

## Stage

[The One at the Keyboard](persona_the_one_at_the_keyboard.md) tables [Say It in Advance](plan_say_it_in_advance.md) or [Show Every Path](plan_show_every_path.md), and pays for whichever is chosen.

## Tension

Both instruments work, and each is bought with something the field wanted.
Fixing the route in advance means fixing it at the moment of least knowledge, so a real surprise in the data can no longer be answered properly and must be reported as a departure or ignored. Showing every path answers honestly and turns a claim into a landscape, which is read selectively or not read, handing the choosing to the reader instead of removing it. Neither recovers what the unwalked branches would have said, because they were never walked, and that is the one thing no instrument can reach.
