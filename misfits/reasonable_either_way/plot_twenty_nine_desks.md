# Plot: Twenty-Nine Desks

## Taxonomy

[Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The same file and the same question, sent out to other competent hands: twenty-nine teams, sixty-one analysts, no conferring.

## Action

[Taking a turn](process_taking_a_turn.md), many times over: [The Other Hands](persona_the_other_hands.md), holding [Other Hands](position_other_hands.md), each route through the same numbers by their own judgement and publish the route with the result.

## Stage

The answers come back spread from 0.89 to 2.93 in odds-ratio units, twenty teams finding a significant effect and nine finding none, with twenty-one distinct combinations of controls between them.
On an imaging dataset, seventy teams return seventy workflows, no two identical.

## Tension

The garden is now demonstrated and is no more resolvable than before.
Every route is explained, every explanation convinces, and several of them reach opposite conclusions. This is not sixty-one lapses to be trained away: it is what judgement looks like where the step requires judgement. So the exercise proves the multiplicity exists and cannot say which path was right, leaving the original finding less trusted and no better established.
