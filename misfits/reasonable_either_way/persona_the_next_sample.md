# Persona: The Next Sample

## Taxonomy

[Next Sample](position_next_sample.md). The fresh data, on which the finding does not appear.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Indifferent and unarranged. Collected properly, by people with no stake in the outcome, on the same question.
Carries its own idiosyncrasies, which are different ones, and around which the garden would have grown a different path.

## Action

Fails to reproduce the finding when the original analysis is run on it exactly as written.
Prompts the question of what the replicator did differently, and supplies no answer, because nothing was done differently.

## Shadow

Cannot exonerate itself, and takes the blame by default.
A failed replication is read as a fact about the replication, since the original is documented and clean and the replicator is the new party in the room, so the one event that reveals the garden is the event most likely to be charged to the person who triggered it.
