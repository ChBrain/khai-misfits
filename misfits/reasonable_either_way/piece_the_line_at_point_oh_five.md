## Load Bearing

Without it there is a spread of numbers and no claim, and so no misfit.
It is the piece that converts the garden into a false statement: the line means how rarely a result like this arises by chance, and how rarely depends on how many routes were open, so a threshold computed for one fixed path is applied to a path that was chosen by the data.

## Apparent

A convention. A number everybody knows, applied identically to every study, requiring no judgement at all.
The one element in the whole production that nobody chose and nobody can be blamed for.
