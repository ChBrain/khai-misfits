# Process: Looking First

## Taxonomy

[Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md). Seeing the data before deciding, which is what good practice requires.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A file arriving after the question was written down.
[The One at the Keyboard](persona_the_one_at_the_keyboard.md), holding [One at the Keyboard](position_one_at_the_keyboard.md), at [the long table](place_the_long_table.md).

## Direction

Toward understanding what is actually there: the bad records, the long tail, the group with eleven members.
An analyst who did not do this would decide worse, so the process is not a lapse to be trained out but the thing competence consists of.

## Lever

Sight. Nothing is withheld at the table, and everything visible is legitimately usable in deciding how to proceed.

## Echo

A set of choices about to be made that are better for having looked, and are, for exactly that reason, contingent on what was seen.
