# Plot: It Does Not Come Back

## Taxonomy

[Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Fresh data, properly collected on the same question, and [the written account](place_the_written_account.md) followed exactly.

## Action

[Taking a turn](process_taking_a_turn.md), with every turn already specified: [The Next Sample](persona_the_next_sample.md), holding [Next Sample](position_next_sample.md), yields no effect where the original found one.

## Stage

The replicator reports the null and is asked what they did differently.
They did nothing differently. They followed the route as written, which is the whole of what replication means.

## Tension

The one event that reveals the garden arrives already discredited.
The original is documented, clean and first; the replication is the new party in the room and its analyst cannot name a single departure, which sounds like evasion and is the literal truth. The finding did not fail because the replication was worse, it failed because the route was grown around numbers that are not these numbers, and the garden that grew it left nothing behind to point at.
