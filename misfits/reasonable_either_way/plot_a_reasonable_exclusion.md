# Plot: A Reasonable Exclusion

## Taxonomy

[Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The file open on [the long table](place_the_long_table.md), and three records with impossible timestamps visible in the first minutes.

## Action

[Looking first](process_looking_first.md) and then [taking a turn](process_taking_a_turn.md): [The One at the Keyboard](persona_the_one_at_the_keyboard.md), holding [One at the Keyboard](position_one_at_the_keyboard.md), writes [the exclusion](piece_the_exclusion.md) that any careful person would write, and writes down why.

## Stage

[The One at the Keyboard](persona_the_one_at_the_keyboard.md), working [Do It Properly](plan_do_it_properly.md), sets a rule that a reviewer will agree with on sight, because it is right.

## Tension

The rule is correct, and the data chose it.
Not in any sinister sense: the impossible timestamps were there, removing them is right, and an analyst who left them in would be doing worse work. But the rule exists in this shape because of what was on the screen this morning, and a different file would have prompted a different rule, equally right and equally unremarkable. Nothing about the moment feels like a fork, which is why it does not get counted as one.
