# Process: Writing It Up

## Taxonomy

[Reasonable Either Way](play_reasonable_either_way.md). Reporting the route walked, completely and truthfully.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An analysis finished and a result in hand.
[The One at the Keyboard](persona_the_one_at_the_keyboard.md), holding [One at the Keyboard](position_one_at_the_keyboard.md), at [the written account](place_the_written_account.md).

## Direction

Toward full disclosure of everything that happened: every rule, every control, every justification, one result.
Nothing omitted and nothing dressed, because there is nothing to omit or dress.

## Lever

Honesty, which is present in full and is not sufficient, since the account can only report events and the problem is not made of events.

## Echo

A methods section a reviewer can check line by line and approve, describing a route whose selection it has no way to describe.
