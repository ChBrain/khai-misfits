# Persona: The Home Language

## Taxonomy

[Home Language](position_home_language.md). The good that passes only one way.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Every word intact, songs, jokes, a way of naming things nothing else names.
Not endangered in any sense a dictionary could show.

## Action

Persists in every use except the one that matters.
Waits to be spoken to somebody small.

## Shadow

Cannot ask for the only thing that keeps it.
Its survival is not a function of how many people know it, how well it is documented or how much it is loved, but of whether it is the first thing said to a child, and there is no way for it to press that claim in the room where the claim is decided.

## Tell

Is complete, cherished, recorded and finished, all at once.
