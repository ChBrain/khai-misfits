# Plot: Nothing Looks Lost

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Stopped Speaking It](play_nobody_stopped_speaking_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The years after, with the elders in the next room and the language spoken around the child daily.

## Action

[Keeping it alive](process_keeping_it_alive.md): [the elders](persona_the_elders.md) speak it at every meal, every funeral and every song, and are understood.

## Stage

It passes every test anybody thinks to run. It is alive.

## Tension

The health is real and is exactly why nobody looks.
What everybody means by a language being alive is that there are people speaking it, and there are, so the household contains no evidence of a problem and would not produce any if asked. The check that would have caught it is not whether it is spoken but whether anybody is acquiring it, and that is not a question a room full of fluent speakers prompts. The safety is complete for precisely as long as [the elders](persona_the_elders.md) last.
