## Tension

This is what separates the loss from every reversible one.
A standard that locked in can be unlocked at a price, a preference that tipped can tip back, and a resource drawn down can be left to recover. Here the thing that was skipped was an acquisition window, and windows do not reopen for the people who were in them: what is available afterwards is instruction, which produces something else. So goodwill, funding and total agreement about what should happen are all present, and none of them is the missing input.
