# Piece: The Understanding

## Taxonomy

[The Kitchen](place_the_kitchen.md). The comfortable state that transmits nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Acquired in [the kitchen](place_the_kitchen.md) by [the child](persona_the_child.md), from years of hearing it and answering in the other language.

## Load Bearing

It is why the failure is invisible from inside the family for a whole generation.
A person who follows every word at the table is not experienced by anybody as a non-speaker, least of all by themselves, and a life is entirely liveable from there, so the household contains no evidence that anything has gone wrong and would not produce any if asked.

## Apparent

Fluency. The child understands the grandparents perfectly.
Everybody at the table would say the language is being passed on.

## Yearbook

Is durable, and is not a stage on the way to speaking.
Cannot be given to anybody else, because what passes is what is said, not what is followed.
