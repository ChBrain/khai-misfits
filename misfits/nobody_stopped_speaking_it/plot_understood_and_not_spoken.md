# Plot: Understood and Not Spoken

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Stopped Speaking It](play_nobody_stopped_speaking_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A child who hears it constantly and answers in the other one.

## Action

[The Child](persona_the_child.md) grows up in [Child](position_child.md) and arrives at [the understanding](piece_the_understanding.md): every word followed, none produced.

## Stage

A comfortable state, liveable for a whole life, and not a state anything passes on from.

## Tension

Nobody experiences this person as a non-speaker, least of all themselves.
They follow the table, laugh at the jokes and answer the grandparents, and a life is entirely available from there, so nothing presents as a loss to the person holding it or to anyone watching. What transmits is what is said to a child, not what a child follows, and the difference between those two has no symptom at the age it would still matter.
