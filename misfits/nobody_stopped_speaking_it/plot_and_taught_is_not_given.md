# Plot: And Taught Is Not Given

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Stopped Speaking It](play_nobody_stopped_speaking_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The remedy, funded, staffed and full of willing adults.

## Action

[Teaching it back](process_teaching_it_back.md): [the evening class](place_the_evening_class.md) runs, and the students, who have understood the language all their lives, start from grammar.

## Stage

It makes real speakers. It does not reopen the channel.

## Tension

The repair is genuine and is aimed at a different loss than the one that happened.
Instruction produces a second language held by adults, and what went was a first language held by anybody, so a fully successful programme leaves the language carried by people who learned it rather than by people it was spoken to. That is not nothing and it is not the same thing, and the honest form of the outcome is that the documentary loss is repaired, the record is safe, and the mechanism is not: nobody stopped speaking it, and nobody was given it.
