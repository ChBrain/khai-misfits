## Place

## Load Bearing

It is the entire mechanism of transmission and it is not institutional.
A language passes by being the thing said to a child before the child can weigh it, which means the decision sits with whoever is holding the baby and nowhere else: no school, ministry, academy or community body has any access to it, so a good that looks collective is dispensed privately, once per household, by people deciding something else.

## Apparent

Nothing. Talking to a baby.
The least deliberate act in the house.

## Yearbook

Opens once per person and does not reopen.
Closes without a signal, since the household that closes it is speaking a language the whole time.
