# Plot: The Right Language for the Child

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Stopped Speaking It](play_nobody_stopped_speaking_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A kitchen, a baby, and two languages the parent holds.

## Action

[Choosing at home](process_choosing_at_home.md): [the parent](persona_the_parent.md), holding [Parent](position_parent.md), speaks the one the school runs in.

## Stage

The child will enter its first classroom understanding everything, instead of catching up for six years.

## Tension

The parent is not choosing between a heritage and a job.
They are choosing which of those two classrooms their own child sits in, and the evidence is their own childhood, which is not in dispute and is not sentimental. [The Dominant Language](persona_the_dominant_language.md) needs no policy behind it and no enforcer: it is where the work, the forms and the friends are, and preferring it for a child you love is not a failure of loyalty. Nobody in this kitchen is weighing a language, and the language is what is being weighed.
