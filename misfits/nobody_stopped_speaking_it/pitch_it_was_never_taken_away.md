# Pitch: It Was Never Taken Away

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Stopped Speaking It](play_nobody_stopped_speaking_it.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Domestic and warm. A kitchen, a baby, grandparents in the next room, a child doing well at school.
Love, competence and good sense, in every decision, at every point.

## Undertow

Under the kitchen, a channel one household wide, closed separately by each household for its own good reasons.
So the sum has no author and no meeting, and the thing being lost is alive the whole time it is being lost.

## Nerve

The particular vertigo of a door that is not shut but absent, found by people pushing on it.

## Echo

Afterward: a language nobody stopped speaking, a generation that understood it and could not say it, and a class full of adults learning what they were not given.
