# Nobody Stopped Speaking It: Reference

The research warrant for Nobody Stopped Speaking It.
The term is the field's; the play runs on language shift and the intergenerational transmission it turns on,
all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging shift as **a good that passes through a channel one household wide, closed separately by each household
for reasons that are good**: a language survives by being the first thing said to a child, which puts the whole
mechanism in a kitchen and none of it in a school, a ministry or an academy. A parent choosing the dominant
language is not weighing a heritage against a job, they are deciding whether their own child sits in a first
classroom understanding everything or catching up for six years, and their evidence is their own childhood. So
the sum is computed nowhere, by parties each computing their own part correctly. The play models the documented
mechanics, the choice that is right for the child, the fluent elders whose health is the reason nobody looks,
the generation that understands and cannot produce, the acquisition window that does not reopen, the return
that falls with every closure, and the remedy that makes speakers rather than transmission, as the load-bearing
vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a people who let their language go.

The governing law is that transmission is private, individual and one-directional, while the good it carries is
collective. Nothing else about a language substitutes for it: not how many people know it, not how well it is
documented, not how much it is loved, and not how much of it is in daily use around a child. A language can be
complete, cherished, recorded and finished at the same time, and the only test that would have caught it is
whether anybody is acquiring it, which is not a question a room full of fluent speakers prompts anybody to ask.

What removes any possibility of blame from the parent is that they are answerable for a child and are right
about that child. The advantage they are handing over is measurable and the disadvantage they are sparing was
theirs. They are also never told they are the mechanism: there is no register of who is transmitting, no
notification when a household stops, and no forum in which the language is on an agenda, so the one party who
can close the channel is the one party with no view of the aggregate and no reason to look for one.

What removes it from anybody else is that no enforcer is required. The dominant language wins by being where
the work, the forms, the schooling and most of the friends are, and a parent who loves a child prefers the
language with more doors behind it. Suppression of a minority language is a real and separate phenomenon with
an agent in it, and a play that reached for one would be describing something else with a different cast, so
this production declines it entirely.

And the repair reaches a different loss than the one that happened. Teaching works: classes make real speakers,
documentation makes the record safe, and none of it reopens an acquisition window for the people who were in
it. What comes out is a second language held by adults where what went was a first language held by anybody, so
a fully successful programme is honest about repairing the documentary loss and not the mechanism.

It is distinct from the house's **The Tyranny of Small Decisions**, where each small choice is rational and
free and their sum is an outcome nobody chose because nothing ever decides the whole: that is the nearest
misfit in the house and the shape is genuinely shared, so the line has to be the irreversibility rather than
the aggregation. There, an aggregate that nothing decided could in principle be decided, and the standing
remedy is to convene the choice that was never convened. Here convening it changes nothing, because what was
skipped is an acquisition window and windows do not reopen for the people who were in them: total agreement
about what should happen is present in the evening class, and it is not the missing input.

It is distinct from the house's **Path Dependence**, where a lead won by accident compounds as each adopter
picks what others picked until a worse standard locks in: there the engine is switching costs, and a locked-in
standard can be unlocked at a price that is high and payable. Here there is no price. A parent who wants to
switch back cannot buy the thing they need, because the input is a first language given to somebody who is now
forty, and the falling return is not a switching cost but a change in what the good is worth.

It is distinct from the house's **The Sort**, where mild preferences about who lives nearby tip past a
threshold into a segregation nobody chose: there the preferences are about the aggregate, however mildly, and
the pattern is reversible in principle by the same moves that made it. Here nobody holds a preference about the
aggregate at all, mild or otherwise, since the parent is not thinking about a language, and no rearrangement of
who lives where reaches a generation that was not spoken to.

It is distinct from the house's **The Commons**, where everyone takes a fair share of the shared field and the
field goes bare because the cost of one more is split by all: there the good is destroyed by being used, and
restraint is the remedy. Here the good is not rivalrous and is destroyed by not being used in one specific way,
so restraint is not available as a move and the parent is not taking anything from anybody.

It is distinct from the house's **Bad Coin**, where good and shoddy pass at the same bar so the shoddy
circulates and the careful work is withdrawn: there the two are substitutes at a fixed rate and the good one is
hoarded rather than lost. Here the home language is not withdrawn to be kept safe, and nobody is holding a
reserve of it: the elders spend it freely every day of their lives, which is exactly what makes the failure
invisible.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the parent as
  disloyal or the dominant language as an aggressor, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get
  wrong: the parent is answerable for one child and is right about that child, with their own childhood as the
  evidence; the household holds the whole channel and is never told it is the mechanism, since no register, no
  notification and no aggregate exists anywhere; the elders are fluent daily and their health is the exact
  reason nobody runs the one check that would catch it; the child who understands and does not speak is not
  experienced as a non-speaker by anybody including themselves; and each closure makes the next parent's
  identical answer cleaner. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is
  that a collective good is dispensed privately, one baby at a time, by people deciding something else.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Teaching it back works and
  makes a second language held by adults where a first language held by anybody was lost, which is a real
  repair of a different thing. Documentation makes the record safe and the record was never the requirement.
  Official status, signage and media are downstream of the home and do not reach it. And asking a parent to
  choose otherwise is asking them to hand their child six years of catching up for a benefit that lands on
  nobody in that kitchen, which is not a request the production is willing to make of them.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the transmission scale, the Oberwart and East Sutherland and
  Gapun studies, the semi-speaker finding and the revitalisation survey are theirs. The bounds are carried with
  them: this is the **voluntary** shift case and the play stages only that, since coerced suppression of a
  language is a distinct phenomenon with an agent in it and is deliberately not cast here, which means nothing
  in this production should be read as an account of any case where a language was suppressed by policy or
  force; the sharpness of the acquisition window is a matter of degree rather than a cliff and adult learners
  do reach high proficiency, so the play stages the difference between acquisition and instruction as a
  difference in what transmits and not as a claim that adults cannot learn; whether transmission can be
  restarted is genuinely contested, with real revitalisation successes claimed and their generality argued
  over, so the evening class is staged as a repair of the documentary loss without a verdict on the larger
  question; and the case studies are ethnographies of particular communities rather than a general law, so the
  play carries the pattern as documented and recurring rather than as universal. **The play makes no claim that
  any community should have chosen differently**, only that the channel is one household wide, that it opens
  once, and that a language can be alive in every checkable sense while it is not being passed on.
