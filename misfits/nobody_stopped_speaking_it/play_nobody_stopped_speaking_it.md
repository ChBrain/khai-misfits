# Play: Nobody Stopped Speaking It

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Nobody Stopped Speaking It.
A parent stands in a kitchen with a baby and picks which language to speak to it. One is the language of the school, the clinic, the forms, the work and everything on a screen. The other is the language of this kitchen and the two rooms behind it. Speaking the first gives the child a start that can be measured, and the parent knows this because they were the child who did not have it. So they speak the first. So does everyone else, separately, for their own child, and nobody anywhere decides anything about the second.

## Arc

The bend is this: the language passes only through a channel one household wide, and each household closes it for reasons that are good.
It runs on a choice that is right for the child.
[The Parent](persona_the_parent.md) is not choosing between a heritage and a job. They are choosing whether their own child will sit in a classroom understanding everything from the first day or catching up for six years, and the evidence for that is their own childhood. [The Dominant Language](persona_the_dominant_language.md) does not have to be imposed on anybody: it is where the work, the forms and the friends are, and preferring it for a child you love is not a failure of loyalty.
And nothing looks lost, because nothing is.
[The Elders](persona_the_elders.md) are in the next room speaking it daily, the songs still exist, the words are all still there. [The Home Language](persona_the_home_language.md) is alive in every sense a person can check at the time, and the one sense in which it is not is invisible until decades later.
Then [Understood and Not Spoken](plot_understood_and_not_spoken.md) is where the machinery turns.
[The Child](persona_the_child.md) grows up hearing it and answering in the other one, and ends fluent in comprehension and unable to produce. That is a perfectly comfortable state to live a life in. It is not a state anything can be passed on from, because what a child acquires is what is spoken to it, and understanding is not the thing that gets transmitted.
And [the first years](piece_the_first_years.md) are the whole channel, so [the gate only opens one way](plot_the_gate_only_opens_one_way.md). A generation that did not acquire it cannot hand it on by trying harder, since the mechanism that would have done it is not available to adults, and there is no second entrance.
And each closure makes the next choice cleaner. [The Falling Return](piece_the_falling_return.md) is not a punishment: with fewer speakers of the child's generation, keeping it buys less, so the parent after this one faces the same decision with a better answer, and is right too.
So it ends in [the evening class](place_the_evening_class.md), which produces speakers and does not reopen the channel, and in a language that nobody stopped speaking and nobody was given.

## Stakes

Whether a good that passes through one household at a time can be held by anyone at all.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a choice that is right for the child in front of it, a loss with nothing to see at the time, a channel that is one household wide, a gate that does not open backwards, and a remedy that makes speakers rather than transmission, and away from any account in which somebody took it. The production cannot ask a parent to choose worse for their child, and it cannot produce the moment the language stopped, because there is no moment: there is only a long series of kitchens. What is left to fight over is not whether the loss is real, everybody agrees it is, but what to call the disappearance of a thing that nobody stopped speaking.
