## Shown

The volunteer's station, the donor's chair, the day-care doorway at pickup: the act given freely, the parent on time out of consideration, the donor giving. Later the same place, with less of it.

## Holds

[The Intrinsic Motive](piece_the_intrinsic_motive.md) that carried the act before any price touched it, and, once the price arrives, [The Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md) that is already at work under the visible shortfall.

## Offers

To the Willing, the chance to act for its own reason: unpaid, unpenalised, answerable to nothing but the interest or the duty or the care that brought them here.

## Withheld

Any guarantee the free motive survives a price laid on it.
