# Plan: Count on the Willing

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the served's seat](position_served.md), inside [A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Served](position_served.md)

## Direction

Rely on the freely-given act as one reasonably may while a motive carries it: build on the goodwill that is reliably there, the parents on time, the donors who give for nothing. And when an incentive displaces that goodwill, absorb the shortfall, because there is no one to appeal to and nothing was breached, only a motive crowded out by a price you never set and had no hand in.

## Orders

([The Served](persona_the_served.md) counts on the willing act at [the Post](place_the_post.md), and through [Displacing](process_displacing.md) meets [the Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md), receiving less of the act than the free motive gave once the price has crowded that motive out.)

## Implementation

([The Post](place_the_post.md) is where the reliance rests and the shortfall lands; [Displacing](process_displacing.md) withers the willing act as [the Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md) takes hold, so the seat that neither gave the motive nor priced it away is the one that carries the gap, with no breach to name.)

## Targets

- [ ] the willing act is relied on soundly while a motive carries it
- [ ] the shortfall is absorbed when the incentive displaces the goodwill relied on
- [ ] no breach is found and no culprit named, the loss a motive crowded out and not a service refused
- [ ] the harm lands on the party with the least hand in causing it
