## Initiated by

The wish to get more of a willing act, by rewarding or penalising it.

## Direction

From a freely-given or obligated act toward a priced one.

## Lever

The ordinary and usually correct expectation that an incentive adds to a motive and raises the behaviour, the standard price effect, which is why the move looks sensible before it is made.
