# Piece: The Reframe

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). The mechanism of displacement: the shift by which a self-chosen or obligated act becomes a priced transaction.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Post](place_the_post.md), where the price meets the act (Gneezy and Rustichini; Lepper, Greene, and Nisbett). A fine for lateness is read as a price for lateness; a paid pastime is read as work. The place does not change; what the act means there does.

## Load Bearing

The incentive changes what the act MEANS, not only what it pays. Once the act is a transaction, the non-priced motive that used to carry it has no place in it: duty and price do not stack, they compete for the same slot.
Guard against reading this as the actor turning cynical or greedy. Answering a fine as the price it now is, is honest and rational: the Willing has not become worse, the meaning of the act has changed under them, and the shift is a fact about the incentive, not a moral failure in the person paying it.

## Apparent

Just a person responding sensibly to a new price. Nothing to see, nothing that looks like a loss, only an ordinary adjustment to an ordinary signal.
