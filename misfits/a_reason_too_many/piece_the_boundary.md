# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). The condition and the hedge: crowding-out is not a law but a conditional effect, and the incentive that displaces under one condition can add, or even crowd motivation in, under another.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), where the Steward weighs it before The Payer sets a price at [The Counter](place_the_counter.md) (Frey and Jegen; Gneezy and Rustichini). It holds where the prior motive is strong and the incentive is small or controlling, and it reverses toward a plain price effect, or toward crowding motivation in, where the incentive is large enough or framed as support and recognition rather than as a control.

## Load Bearing

This is the hedge, and it is load bearing in its own right: incentives ordinarily raise behaviour, and crowd out a motive only under specific conditions, so the trap the play stages is real but bounded, not universal.
Guard against reading the misfit as a claim that rewards never work or that paying people is always a mistake. Most incentives add to behaviour as intended; only some, laid on a strong prior motive and framed as a control, displace it. The boundary is what tells the two apart.

## Apparent

A mere caveat, easy to drop for a simpler and more dramatic rule, the sort of qualification a retelling trims first.
