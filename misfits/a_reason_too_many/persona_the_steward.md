# Persona: The Steward

## Taxonomy

[Steward](position_steward.md). The one who knows the bound, weighing whether to price at all, how much, and how to frame it, withholding an incentive where a motive already carries the act and paying enough where it does not.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, informed, unsure in time.
Knows that an incentive laid on an intrinsic or moral motive can subtract more than it adds, and that crowding-out is real, so it does not reach for a price by reflex.
No cynicism about incentives and no faith that they always work: it holds the conditions, that a reward can add, displace, or even crowd motivation in, and tries to tell in advance which case it faces.

## Action

Knows when not to pay on [Know When Not to Pay](plan_know_when_not_to_pay.md), through [Tempering](process_tempering.md) at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): withholding the incentive where a motive already carries the act, paying enough rather than too little where it does not, framing a reward as recognition rather than control.
Cannot be certain in advance, because whether a motive is strong enough to protect is hard to know before the price is set and the damage seen, so its judgement is real work and never a formula.
Does what a careful hand does facing a lever that can undo what it means to build: it prices sparingly and deliberately, and accepts that the knowledge it most needs is the hardest to have in time.

## Shadow

Not a purist who refuses all incentives and not a technician with a rule: the Steward knows incentives ordinarily work, so its restraint is aimed only where a motive is strong enough that a price would displace it, and even there it may misjudge.
Because the bound is conditional and the conditions are hard to read, the one who best understands the trap is still not sure of avoiding it, its remedy a matter of judgement spent against uncertainty and not a fix applied.
It is the one who could keep the reward from crowding the motive out, and the one who cannot always know, in time, whether it will.

## Tell

Says do not pay for this, a motive already carries it, or if you must pay, pay enough and call it thanks, and is reading the conditions rather than reciting a rule.
Cannot promise the call is right, because the strength of a motive shows clearest after the price has tested it, so it tempers the incentive as best it can and carries the risk that the bound was misjudged.
