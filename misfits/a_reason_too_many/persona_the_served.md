# Persona: The Served

## Taxonomy

[Served](position_served.md). The one the willing act was for, the teacher kept waiting, the patient who needs the blood, who counted on a freely-given behaviour and, once it is priced and crowded out, gets less of it than before.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Reliant, unassuming, short-changed.
Depends on the willing act without having to ask for it: the teacher who trusted parents to come on time, the recipient who relied on donors who gave for nothing, the many served by a motive they never had to pay for.
No entitlement in it: it counted on goodwill because goodwill was reliably there, and it is left short not by anyone's malice but by an incentive that displaced the goodwill it relied on.

## Action

Counts on the willing on [Count on the Willing](plan_count_on_the_willing.md), and through [Displacing](process_displacing.md) at [the Post](place_the_post.md) meets [the Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md), receiving less of the act than the free motive gave once the price has crowded that motive out.
Cannot restore the goodwill it relied on, because the motive was another's and the price that displaced it was a third party's, so the seat that bears the shortfall is the one with the least hand in causing it.
Does what the one who relied on a willing act does when the act is priced away: it absorbs the loss, the day-care waiting later, the supply run thin, the coverage the free motive gave now gone.

## Shadow

Not owed the willing act and not unharmed by its loss: the Served had no claim on goodwill, but it truly relied on it, so when the incentive crowds the goodwill out, the harm lands on the party that neither gave the motive nor priced it.
Because the loss is a motive displaced and not a service withdrawn in spite, there is no one to appeal to and nothing was breached, so the figure most dependent on the free act is the figure the crowding-out most quietly costs.
It is the one the willing act was for, and the one left short when a reward turned that act into a transaction.

## Tell

Says the parents used to be on time and wonders why a fine made it worse, feeling the shortfall without seeing the reframing that caused it.
Cannot name a culprit, because no one withdrew the act in bad faith, so it reads the loss as bad luck or bad character, when it is a motive crowded out by a price it never set.
