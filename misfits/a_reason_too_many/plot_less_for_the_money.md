# Plot: Less for the Money

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Post](place_the_post.md), where the count comes in below where it stood before the incentive.

## Action

Through [Displacing](process_displacing.md), [The Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md) takes hold: the behaviour falls, and [The Served](persona_the_served.md), from [the served's seat](position_served.md), who once ran on [Count on the Willing](plan_count_on_the_willing.md), gets less than the free motive gave, the day-care fuller of late pickups, the blood supply thinner. The scene closes with the incentive having bought less of the act, not more.

## Stage

Motivation is not additive, so the intrinsic or moral motive is lost by more than the extrinsic one adds (Deci and Ryan; Frey and Jegen). This is not Metric Fixation: no proxy is gamed here, a motive is simply displaced.

## Tension

The reward meant to raise the act is what lowered it, and the loss is easily misread as merely paying too little.
