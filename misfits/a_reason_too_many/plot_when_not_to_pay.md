# Plot: When Not to Pay

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), where the Steward weighs whether to price the act at all, how much, and how to frame it.

## Action

[The Steward](persona_the_steward.md), from [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), runs [Know When Not to Pay](plan_know_when_not_to_pay.md) through [Tempering](process_tempering.md), holding [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): withhold the incentive where a motive already carries the act, pay enough where it does not, frame a reward as recognition rather than control. The scene closes with the incentive bounded, and the reminder that crowding-out is conditional and not a law.

## Stage

Crowding theory bounds the effect: incentives ordinarily raise behaviour and crowd out a motive only under specific conditions, sometimes crowding it in instead (Frey and Jegen; Gneezy and Rustichini, pay enough or do not pay at all). The remedy is real but uncertain, since whether a motive is strong enough to protect is hard to know in advance.

## Tension

The fix is to know when not to pay, and that knowledge is exactly what is hard to have in time.
