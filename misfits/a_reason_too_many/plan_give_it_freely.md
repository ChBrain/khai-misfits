# Plan: Give It Freely

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the willing's seat](position_willing.md), inside [A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Willing](position_willing.md)

## Direction

Do the act for its own reason and ask no pay: carry it on an interest taken for its own sake, a duty felt, an act of care, the motive that needs no reward. And when a price is later attached, answer it honestly as the price it now is, since a duty priced becomes a service and a pleasure paid becomes work, and know that responding sensibly to the incentive is exactly what displaces the reason you acted on before.

## Orders

([The Willing](persona_the_willing.md) carries [the Intrinsic Motive](piece_the_intrinsic_motive.md) at [the Post](place_the_post.md), giving the act freely, until a price arrives and, through [Transacting](process_transacting.md), it answers [the Reframe](piece_the_reframe.md), the free motive displaced by the transaction the act has become.)

## Implementation

([The Post](place_the_post.md) is where the act is given for nothing; once [the Incentive](piece_the_incentive.md) reaches it, [Transacting](process_transacting.md) turns the act into a transaction, so [the Reframe](piece_the_reframe.md) leaves no place in it for the intrinsic motive, and the willing seat gives less by responding to the price exactly as a reasonable person would.)

## Targets

- [ ] the act is done for its own reason, asking no pay, while a motive carries it
- [ ] once priced, the act is answered honestly as the price it has become, not gamed or refused
- [ ] the free motive is displaced by the reframing, not stacked with the reward
- [ ] the change is seen as honest and not a fall, the seat giving less by acting reasonably
