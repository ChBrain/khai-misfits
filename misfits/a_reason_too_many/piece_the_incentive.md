# Piece: The Incentive

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). The extrinsic reward or fine attached to a behaviour, offered in the ordinary expectation that it will add to the existing motive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [The Counter](place_the_counter.md): the payment for doing the thing, or the penalty for not (Gneezy and Rustichini). It is fixed there, as a number, before it ever reaches [The Post](place_the_post.md) where the Willing act it is meant to raise is actually done.

## Load Bearing

This is the ordinary, usually sensible lever: incentives ordinarily do raise behaviour, and most of the time attaching one is exactly the straightforward improvement it looks like.
Guard against reading it as always additive. Laid on a behaviour already carried by an intrinsic or moral motive, the same incentive can displace more than it adds (Gneezy and Rustichini), so the ordinary case is not a guarantee for this one.

## Apparent

A straightforward reward or penalty, the obvious way to get more of an act: pay more, get more; fine more, get less.
