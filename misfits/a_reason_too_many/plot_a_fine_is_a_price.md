# Plot: A Fine Is a Price

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Post](place_the_post.md), where the Willing meets the new price where it used to act for free.

## Action

[The Willing](persona_the_willing.md) meets [The Incentive](piece_the_incentive.md) and, through [Transacting](process_transacting.md), answers [The Reframe](piece_the_reframe.md): the parent reads the fine as a price and buys the extra time with a clear conscience, the enthusiast comes to see a paid pastime as work. The scene closes with the act now a transaction, the moral or intrinsic reason quietly displaced.

## Stage

A fine is a price, so the duty becomes a purchasable service and the act is answered honestly as the transaction it has become, the incentive changing what the act means (Gneezy and Rustichini; Lepper, Greene, and Nisbett). This is not The Cobra Effect: nothing is gamed here, the price is answered honestly and exactly as posted.

## Tension

No one cheats, and the honest answer to the price is exactly what removes the stronger reason.
