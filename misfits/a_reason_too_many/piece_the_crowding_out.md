# Piece: The Crowding-Out

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). The net displacement: the intrinsic or moral motive lost by more than the extrinsic one adds, so the behaviour falls instead of rising.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Post](place_the_post.md), where the behaviour falls and [The Served](persona_the_served.md) gets less than before the incentive arrived (Deci and Ryan; Frey and Jegen). The reason too many has subtracted the reason that mattered.

## Load Bearing

Motivation is not additive. An incentive laid on a motive it did not create can subtract more than it adds, so the sum of an intrinsic reason plus an extrinsic one can be smaller than the intrinsic reason alone.
Guard against reading the fall as proof the reward was merely too small. More pay can help at the margin, but the displaced motive is a real and separate loss, not simply an under-priced incentive waiting for a bigger number; Gneezy and Rustichini found small payments could lower effort below the level with no payment at all.

## Apparent

A disappointing result, easily blamed on paying too little, easily answered by raising the price rather than by questioning whether a price belonged there at all.
