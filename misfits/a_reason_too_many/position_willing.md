# Position: Willing

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). The seat of the one who does the act for its own sake or out of duty, needing no payment, and who, once the act is priced, answers the price honestly as a price, the free motive displaced.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A motive that needs no reward: an interest taken for its own sake, a duty felt, an act of care, the reason that was carrying the behaviour before any price.
An honest response to a price, once one is set: the act, now priced, is a transaction, and answering it as the price it has become is reasonable and not a betrayal.
No way to keep the free motive inside a priced act, because the price changes what the act means, so the seat cannot both treat the act as a transaction and hold the reason it acted before.

## Orders

Give it freely, until it is priced.
Do the act for its own reason and ask no pay, and when a fine or a reward is attached, answer it as the price it now is, since a duty priced is a service and a pleasure paid is work.
Accept what the pricing does: that responding sensibly to the incentive is exactly what crowds out the motive that needed none, and that the change is honest, not a fall.

## Loses

The free motive, displaced by the very reward meant to reinforce it.
Because motivation is not additive, the seat cannot add the extrinsic reason to the intrinsic one but has the stronger displaced by the weaker, so what it loses is the reason it acted before, and it loses it by responding reasonably to the price.
It keeps its good sense and loses its goodwill, the willing act it gave for nothing turned, once paid, into a transaction it gives less of.

## Drives

Toward the one most willing when nothing is paid and most changed when something is,
answering the price honestly because the price is real, and by answering it crowding out the reason that needed no price,
so the seat that gave the act freely is the seat the reward quietly turns into a buyer, the motive that carried the behaviour put out by the reason laid on top of it.
