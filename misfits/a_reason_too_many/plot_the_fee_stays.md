## Action

The fine is dropped, but [The Residue](piece_the_residue.md) remains: the lateness stays high, the motive does not return, because the act has been reframed and the shared understanding that once carried it is gone. The scene closes with the norm not restored by simply removing the price that broke it.

## Stage

Removing the incentive does not restore the motive; the day-care lateness stayed high after the fine was removed (Gneezy and Rustichini). This is not Moral Licensing: no credit was banked and spent here, the motive was displaced and did not come back.

## Tension

The trap is not undone by stopping the payment, because the reframing outlasts the price that caused it.
