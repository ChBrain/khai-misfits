# Persona: The Willing

## Taxonomy

[Willing](position_willing.md). The one who does the act for its own sake or out of duty, needing no payment, and who, once the act is priced, honestly answers the price as a price, the free motive quietly displaced.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Glad, dutiful, unpaid.
Does the thing for a reason that needs no reward: an interest taken for its own sake, a duty felt to another, an act of care or giving, and does it well because the reason is its own.
No martyrdom in it and no calculation: it is simply carried by a motive no one had to buy, the parent on time from consideration, the donor giving for nothing, the enthusiast who would do it anyway.

## Action

Gives freely on [Give It Freely](plan_give_it_freely.md), carrying [the Intrinsic Motive](piece_the_intrinsic_motive.md) at [the Post](place_the_post.md), until a price arrives and, through [Transacting](process_transacting.md), it answers [the Reframe](piece_the_reframe.md), reading the fine as a price and buying the extra time, or seeing the once-loved task as paid work.
Cannot hold the free motive once the act is priced, because the price changes what the act means, a duty into a service and a pleasure into a wage, so the transaction it now honestly answers has no place left in it for the reason it acted before.
Does what a reasonable person does facing a price: it responds to the price as the price it is, and the responding is exactly what crowds the old reason out.

## Shadow

Not a cynic for answering the price and not a saint for having given freely: the Willing did the act for a real motive, and once that act is priced, treating it as the transaction it has become is honest, not a fall from grace.
Because motivation is not additive, the added reason does not stack on its own but displaces it, so the one who was most willing when nothing was paid is the one the payment most changes, its free motive the very thing the price puts out.
It is the one who gave without being asked, and the one the reward quietly turns into a buyer.

## Tell

Says the fine is only fair and pays it without guilt, or says the task feels like work now that it is paid, and in both is answering the price honestly.
Cannot be blamed for the change, because the price is real and its response is sound, so it acts reasonably and gives less, the motive that carried the act crowded out by the reason laid on top of it.
