# Piece: The Intrinsic Motive

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). The pre-existing internal or moral reason the behaviour already ran on, before any incentive touched it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Post](place_the_post.md), where the act is given: an interest taken for its own sake, a duty felt, an act of care or altruism (Deci and Ryan; Titmuss). It is the reason the Willing is already there, doing the thing, before anyone offers to pay for it.

## Load Bearing

This is the thing that was doing the work before any incentive arrived, and the thing the incentive puts at risk (Deci and Ryan). It is not a residual or a nice-to-have alongside a "real" lever: for as long as it holds, it is the whole mechanism carrying the behaviour.
Guard against reading it as weak or unreliable because it asks no pay. An unpriced motive often carries more of the behaviour, more reliably, than a price can buy, which is exactly why attaching a price to it is a risk and not a free addition.

## Apparent

Ordinary goodwill. Easily taken for granted, easily assumed to be background rather than the mechanism itself, easily assumed to be improvable by adding a reward on top.
