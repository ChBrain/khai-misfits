# Process: Tempering

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). Borne at [The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), precipitated by [The Steward](persona_the_steward.md) weighing [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), in [When Not to Pay](plot_when_not_to_pay.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The recognition that an incentive can subtract more than it adds.

## Direction

From a naive incentive toward a bounded one: withheld where a motive already carries the act, made large enough where it does not, framed as recognition rather than control.

## Lever

Crowding theory's conditions, that the effect is conditional and reverses to a plain price effect or even crowds motivation in under the right frame or amount (Frey and Jegen; Gneezy and Rustichini, pay enough or do not pay at all).
