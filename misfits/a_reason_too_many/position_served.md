# Position: Served

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). The seat of the one the willing act was for, who counted on a freely-given behaviour and, once it is priced and crowded out, receives less of it than before, the shortfall landing on the party with the least hand in causing it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A reliance without a claim: a dependence on the willing act, the teacher trusting the parents on time, the recipient counting on donors who gave for nothing, real but never contracted.
A trust that was well placed until the price came: goodwill was reliably there because a motive carried it, so counting on it was sound.
No hand on either lever, because the motive belonged to another and the price was set by a third, so the seat that bears the loss neither gave the goodwill nor priced it away.

## Orders

Count on the willing.
Rely on the freely-given act as one reasonably may while a motive carries it, building on the goodwill that was reliably there.
Absorb the shortfall when the incentive displaces that goodwill, because there is no one to appeal to and nothing was breached, only a motive crowded out by a price the seat never set.

## Loses

The coverage the free motive gave, withdrawn by a reframing it had no part in.
Because the loss is a motive displaced and not a service refused, the seat cannot demand the act back or blame a breach, so it loses the reliable goodwill it counted on and is left to carry the gap.
It keeps its reasonable reliance and loses what it relied on, the party most dependent on the willing act the one the crowding-out most quietly costs.

## Drives

Toward the one the willing act was for and the one left short when a reward turned that act into a transaction,
having relied soundly on a goodwill that a price then displaced, with no lever of its own and no breach to name,
so the seat that neither gave the motive nor priced it is the seat that bears the loss when the reason that carried the act is crowded out.
