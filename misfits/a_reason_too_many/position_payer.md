# Position: Payer

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). The seat of the one who attaches a reward or a fine to a willing behaviour to get more of it, in the ordinary and usually correct expectation that a price added to a motive raises the act.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The obvious lever: a payment for doing the act or a penalty for not, the sensible means of getting more of a behaviour or less of its lack.
A sound expectation behind it: incentives ordinarily do raise the behaviour they price, so the move is the one that mostly works and looks like pure gain.
No sight, from the counter, of whether the motive it prices is intrinsic and strong, so no way to tell in advance whether the incentive will add to that motive or displace it.

## Orders

Pay for more.
Attach the reward or the fine to the behaviour you want more of, in the expectation that the price adds to the motives already there and raises the act.
Do not look for the catch, because from the counter there is none to see: the incentive appears purely additive, and nothing at the moment of setting it shows that it will reframe the act rather than reinforce it.

## Loses

The rise it paid for, where the motive it priced was one a price displaces.
Because the seat cannot tell from the counter whether the behaviour ran on an intrinsic motive, it cannot tell whether its incentive will strengthen or crowd out, so where it crowds out, the payer loses the very increase it spent to buy.
It keeps its reasonable expectation and loses the outcome, the one trying hardest to strengthen the act the one who may quietly undo it.

## Drives

Toward the one who meant only to buy more of a good behaviour,
pricing it soundly and by the ordinary rule, unable to see in advance that the price would displace the motive rather than add to it,
so the seat that reached for the sensible lever is the seat whose price, laid on a willing motive, bought less of the act and not more.
