# Piece: The Residue

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). The irreversibility: the motive that does not simply return when the incentive is withdrawn.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Post](place_the_post.md), where the act stays diminished after the price is gone, and the shortfall is recorded at [The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md). The day-care lateness that stayed high after the fine was removed (Gneezy and Rustichini) is the exemplar: the number the fine was meant to move never went back down once the fine went away.

## Load Bearing

Removing the incentive does not restore the norm. The act has been reframed as a transaction, and the shared understanding that once carried it for its own sake is gone, not merely suspended, so there is nothing left for the withdrawal to hand back.
Guard against assuming the trap is undone by simply dropping the incentive. The reframing outlasts the price that caused it; ending the price is necessary to stop paying for the worse behaviour, but it is not sufficient to bring the old motive back.

## Apparent

A lingering bad habit, mistaken for unrelated to the fee that started it, as though the behaviour simply drifted rather than having been reframed and left there.

## Yearbook

Left behind by [The Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md), the reason the remedy is not merely to stop paying.
Weighed by [The Steward](persona_the_steward.md) against [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) at [The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), the standing fact of [The Fee Stays](plot_the_fee_stays.md).
