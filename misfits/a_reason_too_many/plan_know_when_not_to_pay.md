# Plan: Know When Not to Pay

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), inside [A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Steward](position_steward.md)

## Direction

Weigh the motive before pricing it. Withhold the incentive where an intrinsic or moral reason already carries the act, and where you must pay, pay enough that the price outweighs the crowding rather than too little, and frame the reward as recognition rather than control, and move before the motive hardens into a habit of being paid (Frey and Jegen; Gneezy and Rustichini). Hold the bound honestly: incentives ordinarily work and crowd out only under particular conditions, and the judgement of which case you face is the hard part and may be wrong.

## Orders

([The Steward](persona_the_steward.md) holds [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) through [Tempering](process_tempering.md) at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), withholding the incentive where a motive already carries the act, paying enough where it does not, and framing a reward as recognition rather than control.)

## Implementation

([The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md) is where the conditions are weighed; [Tempering](process_tempering.md) bounds the incentive to them, so [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) keeps [the Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md) from being universal, the remedy real but uncertain because the strength of a motive shows clearest only after a price has tested it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the motive is weighed before it is priced, the incentive withheld where a motive already carries the act
- [ ] where payment is needed it is enough rather than too little, and framed as recognition not control
- [ ] the bound is held honestly, crowding-out treated as conditional and not a law
- [ ] the judgement is accepted as uncertain, the strength of a motive hard to know before a price has tested it
