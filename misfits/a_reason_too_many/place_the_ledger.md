# Place: The Ledger

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). Where the accounting is kept and the bound is weighed. Hosts [When Not to Pay](plot_when_not_to_pay.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The books, the behaviour lower than before the incentive, the conditions under which a price helps or harms laid out for the reckoning.

## Holds

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the conditional bound that keeps crowding-out from being a law, and the standing record of [The Residue](piece_the_residue.md) that never simply cleared.

## Offers

To the Steward, the judgement of when to withhold the incentive, when to pay enough, when to frame a reward as recognition rather than control.

## Withheld

Any simple rule that fits every case, since whether a motive is strong enough to protect is hard to know in advance.
