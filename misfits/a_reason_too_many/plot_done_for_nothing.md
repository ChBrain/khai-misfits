# Plot: Done for Nothing

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Post](place_the_post.md), where the willing act is given freely: a parent on time out of consideration, a volunteer at their station, a donor giving blood for nothing in return.

## Action

[The Willing](persona_the_willing.md), from [the willing's seat](position_willing.md), runs [Give It Freely](plan_give_it_freely.md), carrying [The Intrinsic Motive](piece_the_intrinsic_motive.md): the act done for its own sake, the duty felt, no pay asked and none expected. The scene closes with the behaviour carried, willingly and well, by a motive no one had to buy.

## Stage

The behaviour is sustained by an intrinsic or moral motive: an interest taken for itself, a duty felt, an act of care or altruism (Deci and Ryan; Titmuss). Nothing here is thin or fragile. It is simply not yet priced.

## Tension

A thing done well for no reward, and about to be given one.
