# Place: The Counter

## Taxonomy

[A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md). Where the incentive is set and the act is rung up as a transaction. Hosts [A Price to Help](plot_a_price_to_help.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The fine posted, the pay offered, the favour turned into a service: the Payer setting a fine or a payment, meaning to get more of the act.

## Holds

[The Incentive](piece_the_incentive.md), offered in the ordinary expectation that it adds to the motive already doing the work.

## Offers

To the Payer, the sensible-looking lever of a reward or a penalty: the standard, usually correct move to raise a behaviour by pricing it.

## Withheld

Any sign, at the moment of setting it, that the price will displace the motive rather than reinforce it.
