# Plan: Pay for More

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the payer's seat](position_payer.md), inside [A Reason Too Many](play_a_reason_too_many.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Payer](position_payer.md)

## Direction

Attach a reward or a fine to the behaviour you want more of, in the ordinary and usually correct expectation that a price added to a motive raises the act. Reach for the obvious lever, a payment for doing the thing or a penalty for not, and do not look for a catch, because from the counter the incentive appears purely additive, a reason laid on top of the reasons already there, with nothing to show that it will reframe the act rather than reinforce it.

## Orders

([The Payer](persona_the_payer.md) sets [the Incentive](piece_the_incentive.md) through [Pricing](process_pricing.md) at [the Counter](place_the_counter.md), the fine for lateness or the pay for donating, expecting the behaviour to rise.)

## Implementation

([The Counter](place_the_counter.md) is where the price is set; [Pricing](process_pricing.md) attaches [the Incentive](piece_the_incentive.md) in the sound expectation that it adds to the motive, and only later, where the motive it priced was intrinsic and strong, does the incentive reframe the act instead, the payer unable to see the difference at the moment of setting it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the reward or fine is attached to the behaviour sought, by the ordinary rule
- [ ] the move is made in the sound expectation that a price adds to a motive and raises the act
- [ ] the catch is invisible from the counter, the incentive appearing purely additive
- [ ] where the priced motive was intrinsic and strong, the price displaces it, and the rise paid for is lost
