# Plot: And the Loss Is Only Visible From Outside a Lifetime

## Taxonomy

[As Full As I Remember](play_as_full_as_i_remember.md)

## Owner

## Cue

The port records, the photographs, the log books, the middens.

## Action

[Reaching back](process_reaching_back.md): [the archive](persona_the_archive.md) recovers the earlier state and publishes it.

## Stage

It works. It is real historical science, and it settles what was there.

## Tension

The record is repaired and the thing that would carry a decision is not.
The number arrives to a room in which nobody has ever seen the sea it describes, so it lands as history rather than as loss, and a target set from it has to be defended against everybody's own accurate experience of a normal that is lower. That is not obstruction and not denial: the fisher, the minister and the scientist are each correctly calibrated to what they saw, and being asked to manage toward a state none of them can picture is a different request from being asked to restore one they remember.
