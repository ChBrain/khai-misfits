## Load Bearing

Every ground for discarding it is sound, which is why the discipline that keeps the science honest is the same discipline that removes the evidence.
Memory inflates, the people left in a fishery are a selected sample, numbers recalled decades later are unreliable, and no assessment can be built on recollection: a scientist who used it would be doing something indefensible, so the refusal is not deafness and cannot be corrected by asking anybody to listen harder.

## Apparent

A story. A nice one, told by somebody who was there.
The least admissible kind of evidence there is.
