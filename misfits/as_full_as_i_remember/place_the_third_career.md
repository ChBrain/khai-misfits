# Place: The Third Career

## Taxonomy

[As Full As I Remember](play_as_full_as_i_remember.md). Where three honest calibrations are laid end to end and the loss appears between them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Three sets of assessments, each internally consistent, each written by somebody careful.
No drift anywhere, no error, no lowered standard.

## Holds

[The Decline](persona_the_decline.md), which fits inside none of the three.
[The Short Series](piece_the_short_series.md), whose zero is the second observer's already-thinned sea.
[The Old-Timer](persona_the_old_timer.md), whose account spans them and cannot be used.

## Offers

A complete demonstration that nothing went wrong inside any career.
Everything needed to see the trend, once the three are set beside each other by somebody outside all of them.

## Withheld

An observer who was present for all of it.
The place cannot produce one, because the interval exceeds a working life, so the comparison that would show the trend requires an instrument nobody has: careers are replaced rather than extended, and the loss lives in the joins.
