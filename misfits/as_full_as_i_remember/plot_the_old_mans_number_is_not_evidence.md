## Cue

## Action

## Stage

Correctly. Memory inflates, the people still in a fishery are a selected sample, and recalled numbers are unreliable.

## Tension

The discipline that keeps the science honest is the discipline that removes the only spanning witness.
Every objection is sound, and a scientist who built an assessment on recollection would be doing something indefensible that the profession is right to forbid. So this is not deafness and cannot be corrected by asking anybody to listen harder: it is rigour, working exactly as intended, on the one testimony that was present at both ends of the interval. And it dies with its holder, while the next person offering it will be remembering a thinner sea.
