# Persona: The Assessment

## Taxonomy

[Assessment](position_assessment.md). The instrument, whose zero is already a reduced state.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A survey series, standardised, quality controlled, and beginning at the first survey.
The best evidence anybody has.

## Action

Measures change since it started.
Reports honestly on that interval.

## Shadow

Cannot see before its own first point, and does not say so.
The series opens decades after the first taking, so its baseline is somebody's already-thinned sea, and nothing inside a time series reports what preceded it: the instrument is not wrong about anything it covers and is silent, structurally, about the only comparison that would show the trend.

## Tell

Is the best evidence there is and starts too late by construction.
