# Plot: The Series Begins When the Science Did

## Taxonomy

[As Full As I Remember](play_as_full_as_i_remember.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The survey record, consulted for what the stock used to be.

## Action

[The Assessment](persona_the_assessment.md), holding [Assessment](position_assessment.md), reads [the short series](piece_the_short_series.md) back to its first point.

## Stage

The first point is decades or centuries after the first taking. It is somebody's already-thinned sea.

## Tension

A time series is silent about what preceded it and does not report the silence.
Every quantity computed from the record, the reference points, the targets, the recovery goals, is anchored to that opening value, and nothing inside the instrument distinguishes an opening value from a natural one. The assessment is correct about every interval it covers and structurally mute about the only comparison that would show the trend, so relying on it is right and produces a target set at a state that was already reduced when it was first written down.
