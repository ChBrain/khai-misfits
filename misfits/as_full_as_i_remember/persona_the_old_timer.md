# Persona: The Old-Timer

## Taxonomy

[Old-Timer](position_old_timer.md). The one witness who spans the gap, rightly discounted.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A memory of something larger, told without exaggeration as far as they know.
The only person present at both ends.

## Action

Says what it was like.
Is listened to politely and not used.

## Shadow

Is discarded by the discipline that keeps the science honest.
Memory inflates, the survivors of a fishery are a selected sample, and no assessment can rest on recollection: every reason to set the testimony aside is a good reason, so the profession is not being deaf, it is being rigorous, and rigour is what removes the only evidence spanning the interval.

## Tell
