# Persona: The Newcomer

## Taxonomy

[Newcomer](position_newcomer.md). The observer, correctly calibrated to whatever was there.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A first season on a stock, and a need to know what a normal one looks like.
Forty careful years ahead.

## Action

Takes what is there as the reference.
Holds it, accurately, for a career.

## Shadow

Is not drifting, and that is what makes them invisible to every check.
Nothing slides, no standard is lowered and no category widens: the calibration is stable, correct against a real sea, and internally consistent for a lifetime, so an audit of this observer finds nothing at all and the failure is not located anywhere inside them.

## Tell

Is right for forty years about a sea that is not the sea.
