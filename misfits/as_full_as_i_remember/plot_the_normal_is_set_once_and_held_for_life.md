# Plot: The Normal Is Set Once and Held for Life

## Taxonomy

[As Full As I Remember](play_as_full_as_i_remember.md)

## Owner

## Cue

Forty careful years, and every assessment in them honest and well made.

## Action

[Holding the normal](process_holding_the_normal.md): the calibration stays put, and professional standards keep it there.

## Stage

Nothing drifts. No standard is lowered, no category widens, and an audit of any single career comes back clean.

## Tension

This is what separates the play from every slide the house holds.
A drifting standard can be caught by somebody comparing an observer against themselves, which is what makes normalisation of deviance and a widening concept detectable in principle. Here there is nothing to catch: the seat is stably and correctly calibrated against a real sea for a lifetime, its judgements agree with each other and with the data, and no check anybody could run on one career would find anything, because nothing inside one career is wrong.
