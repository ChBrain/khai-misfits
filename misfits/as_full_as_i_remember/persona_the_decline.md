# Persona: The Decline

## Taxonomy

[Decline](position_decline.md). The trend that fits inside no career.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A slope measured in centuries, crossing three or four working lives.
Real, large, and settled by the archives.

## Action

Continues.
Appears in nobody's experience as a change.

## Shadow

Exists only in the comparison between observers.
It is not hidden by anybody's error and would be visible to a person who lived four hundred years: what it needs is a single instrument spanning the interval, and the instruments available are careers, which are replaced rather than extended, so the trend is not concealed but unaddressed.

## Tell

Is agreed by everybody afterwards and detected by nobody at the time.
