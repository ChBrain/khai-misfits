## Cue

A scientist beginning work on a stock, needing a sense of what is usual.

## Action

## Stage

[The First Sight](piece_the_first_sight.md) becomes the reference, because there is nowhere else to get one.

## Tension

The calibration is what the work requires rather than an error in it.
Nobody can judge whether a stock is healthy without a sense of what healthy looks like, and a state carries no history inside it: arriving in year sixty looks exactly like arriving in year twenty, with the same fish, the same gear and the same competent observation. So the reference is taken accurately, from a real sea, by somebody doing precisely what a professional is supposed to do, and it is anchored to a point on a slope that cannot be read from the point.
