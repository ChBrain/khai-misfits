# As Full As I Remember: Reference

The research warrant for As Full As I Remember.
The term is Pauly's; the play runs on generational amnesia, the half of shifting baseline syndrome in which
nobody's own judgement moves, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the shifting baseline as **a reference point taken once at first contact and held accurately for a
lifetime, so a decline slower than a career lives in the joins between observers**: a scientist beginning work
on a stock needs a sense of what is usual, and the only source is what is there on arrival. That calibration is
correct, is honestly held, and does not move. The one before them held a different one against a fuller sea and
the one after will hold a thinner one, and the loss across the three of them is registered by none of them. The
play models the documented mechanics, the calibration the work requires, the stability that keeps every
observer clean, the one spanning testimony that rigour is right to discard, the series whose zero is already a
reduced state, the trend that fits inside no career, and the archive that repairs the record without repairing
the sense of it, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of anybody's standards slipping.

The governing law is that a state carries no history inside it. Arriving in year sixty looks exactly like
arriving in year twenty, with the same fish, the same gear and the same competent observation, so the reference
is anchored to a point on a slope that cannot be read from the point. The failure is therefore not located in
any observer and cannot be found by examining one.

**The play stages the generational half only.** The literature distinguishes generational amnesia, in which
knowledge of the earlier state does not pass between cohorts, from personal amnesia, in which an individual's
own sense of the past updates as conditions change. The second is a within-observer adaptation and belongs to
the house's Prevalence-Induced Concept Change, so the production declines it entirely and casts nothing that
requires anybody's line to move. That refusal is what makes the line true rather than asserted.

What removes any possibility of blame from the observer is that nothing about them slips. No standard is
lowered, no category widens, judgements agree with each other and with the data across forty years, and an
audit of any single career comes back clean. A drifting standard is detectable in principle by comparing an
observer against themselves; here there is nothing to catch.

What removes it from the profession is that the one witness spanning the interval is rightly refused. Memory
inflates, the people still in a fishery are a selected sample, and recalled numbers are unreliable, so a
scientist building an assessment on recollection would be doing something indefensible. The discipline that
keeps the science honest is the same discipline that discards the only evidence crossing the gap, and asking
anybody to listen harder is not a correction.

And the instrument's own zero is already a reduced state. A time series is silent about what preceded it and
does not report the silence, so every reference point, target and recovery goal computed from it is anchored to
an opening value that was itself somebody's thinned sea, with nothing inside the record distinguishing that
value from a natural one.

It is distinct from the house's **Prevalence-Induced Concept Change**, where a comparative eye widens the
concept as true cases dwindle so the count never falls: that is the nearest misfit and the two are mirror
images at first glance, one hiding an improvement and one hiding a decline. The line is the **dynamics of the
comparison class**. There it updates continuously against recent company, so the judge's own line drifts
outward and the failure is inside a single observer. Here the comparison class is frozen at first contact and
never updates at all: nobody's line moves, every observer is stably correct for a lifetime, and the change
happens only when one observer is replaced by another. Those are opposite behaviours of the same component,
and they put the failure in different places.

It is distinct from the house's **Nobody Stopped Speaking It**, where a language passes only through a channel
one household wide and each household closes it for good reasons: the shape is closer than the slate line
anticipated, since both turn on something that passes by first-hand acquisition and is not replaced by a
description of it. The line is that there transmission is **an act somebody performs**, and a parent chooses,
so the channel is closed by decisions that could have gone otherwise. Here there is no act to perform or omit,
because what would have to pass is first-hand experience of a state that no longer exists, and no one can hand
that over at any price. And the casualties differ: there the loss is the good itself, while here the good is
already gone and what is lost is the **ability to register how much**, so the casualty is the instrument.

It is distinct from the house's **The New Normal**, where each successful deviation relaxes the standard until
the tolerated drift is the rule: there a judgement moves inside an organisation that could in principle notice
it moving, and the remedy is to fix the standard in advance. Here no judgement moves, so a fixed standard
declared by any one cohort fixes it at that cohort's already-reduced reference, and the remedy addresses a
mechanism that is not running.

It is distinct from the house's **The Made Ground**, where living well on a place raises a floor its heirs must
clear: there the inheritance is physical and the heir is unfit for a real, altered world. Here nothing about
the world's alteration is in dispute and no heir is unfit for anything: what is inherited is a **reference
point**, and the difficulty is not that the successor cannot meet the new conditions but that they cannot see
them as new.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the scientist as
  short-sighted or the profession as deaf, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the
  reference point is the calibration the work requires and has exactly one available source; it is stably and
  correctly held for a career, so nothing drifts and an audit of any observer comes back clean; the anecdote is
  set aside on grounds that are individually sound and collectively decisive; the series is the best evidence
  anybody has and is silent about what preceded it without announcing the silence; and the trend fits inside no
  working life, so it appears in nobody's experience as a change. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges
  from the structure, so the misfit is that a reference point taken once at first contact makes a long decline
  invisible to every honest observer of it.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Reaching back into archives
  works, is real historical science, and delivers a number to a room in which nobody has ever seen the state it
  describes, so it arrives as history rather than as loss and a target set from it must be defended against
  everybody's accurate experience of a lower normal. Fixing a standard in advance is the remedy for a drifting
  line and there is no drifting line here, so it fixes the standard at whichever cohort declares it. Recording
  the old-timer's account does not make it admissible. And knowing the mechanism completely does not supply
  anybody with the experience of the earlier sea, which is what would carry the decision.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the naming, the generational and personal split, the synthesis
  and the photographic reconstruction are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: the play stages
  **generational amnesia only** and explicitly declines personal amnesia, which is a real half of the
  literature and belongs to a mechanism the house already holds, so nothing here should be read as a claim
  about how individuals remember their own pasts; how far baselines shift is **genuinely disputed** and the
  empirical base is smaller than the concept's reach, so the play stages the structure and never a magnitude;
  historical reconstruction is itself contested in reliability, and whether a deep-history state is an
  achievable or even desirable target is argued over, which the play carries rather than settling; the
  fisheries case is where the term was coined and the generalisation beyond it is younger and thinner; and
  treating a career as the unit of calibration is the play's own simplification of a literature that does not
  fix the interval. **The play makes no claim that any assessment was wrong**, only that a reference point has
  one source, that it is taken once, and that a decline slower than a working life is therefore measured
  correctly by everybody and seen by nobody.
