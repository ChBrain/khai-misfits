## Load Bearing

A time series is silent about what preceded it and does not report the silence, so its zero passes as the natural state.
The survey begins when the science began, which is decades or centuries after the first taking, and every quantity computed from it, the reference points, the targets, the recovery goals, is anchored to that opening value: the instrument is correct about everything it covers and structurally mute about the only comparison that would show the trend.

## Apparent

The best evidence anybody has. Standardised, quality controlled, defensible.
The thing a manager is right to rely on.

## Yearbook

Lengthens by one year each year and never reaches backwards.
Makes the earliest observed state the implicit target, which is somebody's already-thinned sea.
