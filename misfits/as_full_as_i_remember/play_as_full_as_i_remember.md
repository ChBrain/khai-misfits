# Play: As Full As I Remember

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

As Full As I Remember.
A scientist begins work on a stock and sees what is there in their first seasons, which becomes what a normal stock looks like to them. They are careful for forty years, and every assessment they write is honest and well made. The one before them did the same, against a fuller sea. The one after will do the same, against a thinner one. Nobody's judgement drifts, nobody is fooled, and the decline across the three of them is registered by none of them.

## Arc

The bend is this: the reference point is set by first contact and held for life, so the loss lives between careers rather than inside one.
It runs on a normal that has to come from somewhere.
[The Newcomer](persona_the_newcomer.md) cannot begin without a sense of what is usual, and there is only one place to get it: what is there when they arrive. [The First Sight](piece_the_first_sight.md) is not a bias, it is the calibration the work requires, and every observer in every discipline has one.
And it does not move afterwards.
That is what separates this from a drift. [The Newcomer](persona_the_newcomer.md) does not slide, does not lower a standard and does not widen a category: they are correctly and stably calibrated to a real sea, for a whole career, and a fair audit of any single one of them finds nothing wrong.
Then [The Old Man's Number Is Not Evidence](plot_the_old_mans_number_is_not_evidence.md).
[The Old-Timer](persona_the_old_timer.md) remembers something larger and says so, and the profession is right to discount it: memory inflates, survivors of a fishery are selected, and no assessment can rest on what somebody recalls. The discipline that keeps the science honest is the same discipline that discards the only testimony spanning the gap.
And [The Assessment](persona_the_assessment.md) begins when the science began. [The Short Series](piece_the_short_series.md) starts at the first survey, which is decades after the first taking, so the instrument's own zero is somebody's already-reduced sea and nothing inside it reports that.
And the loss is visible only from outside a working life. It needs the port records, the photographs, the archaeology, the log books: [reaching back](process_reaching_back.md) works, is real historical science, and is neither what an assessment runs on nor what a fisher or a minister can feel.

## Stakes

Whether a decline slower than a career can be registered by the people living through it.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a reference point that must come from first contact, a calibration that stays correct and stable for a lifetime, a testimony that is rightly discarded, a series whose zero is already a reduced state, and a loss that exists only in the comparison between observers, and away from any account in which somebody's judgement slipped. The production cannot fault a scientist whose line never moved, and it cannot use the one witness who saw the fuller sea, because using them would be the unscientific thing. What is left to fight over is not whether the sea emptied, the archives settle that, but what to call a loss that every honest observer measured correctly and none of them could see.
