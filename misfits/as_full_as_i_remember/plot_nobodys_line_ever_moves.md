# Plot: Nobody's Line Ever Moves

## Taxonomy

[As Full As I Remember](play_as_full_as_i_remember.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Three careers laid end to end, each internally consistent.

## Action

[The Third Career](place_the_third_career.md) sets them beside each other, which nobody inside them ever did.

## Stage

[The Decline](persona_the_decline.md) appears in the joins. It is inside none of the three.

## Tension

The loss is produced by replacement of the observer and by nothing anybody did.
Each calibration was correct against the sea it was taken from, each career was consistent, and the change happened at the handovers, where no instrument was pointed because a handover is not an observation. So the trend is not concealed by anybody's error and would be plain to somebody who lived four hundred years: what it needs is one instrument spanning the interval, and the instruments available are careers, which are replaced rather than extended.
