## Place

## Load Bearing

It is complete, accurate and honestly given, and its completeness is not the quantity in question.
Every specification, drawing, procedure and setting is present, the training was delivered, and the supplier's engineers answered what was asked. A supplier with a commercial interest in the transfer succeeding has no reason to hold anything back and did not. What the document cannot carry is the weighting: which of its lines is critical this week, which of its tolerances is the one that actually binds, which of its warnings is boilerplate. That weighting is not omitted from the text, because it is not the kind of thing a text has. It belongs to the reader.

## Apparent
