# Place: The Bench

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Know It to Learn It](play_you_have_to_know_it_to_learn_it.md). Where it fails to be used, months later.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The equipment installed, the procedure open on the screen, a run that comes out wrong in a way the manual does not describe.
Everything proper: the steps were followed, the settings match, nothing has been improvised.

## Holds

[The Engineer](persona_the_engineer.md), who understood every sentence.
[The Prior Related Knowledge](piece_the_prior_related_knowledge.md), absent.
[The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md), out of budget and out of time.

## Offers

A demonstration that the package was not the problem.

## Withheld

Any way to tell which variable to move.
The place is where the deficit becomes concrete and where every account somebody will reach for is wrong. The engineer is competent and read the document correctly. The document is complete and describes the process accurately. What is missing is the surrounding knowledge that would make one of a hundred plausible next moves obviously the right one, and that knowledge is a residue of years of related work. So the failure is in neither the package nor the reading, and there is no third object in the room to point at.
