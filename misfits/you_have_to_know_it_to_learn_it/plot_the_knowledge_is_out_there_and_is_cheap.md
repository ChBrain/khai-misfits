# Plot: The Knowledge Is Out There and Is Cheap

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Know It to Learn It](play_you_have_to_know_it_to_learn_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A published literature, a licensable process, and an organisation that needs it.

## Action

[The Supplier](persona_the_supplier.md) will transfer everything, and a market exists in which to buy it.

## Stage

An abundance that is real rather than formal.

## Tension

Nothing is being withheld anywhere, which is where the difficulty has to start.
The literature is published and searchable. Processes are licensable and suppliers compete to license them. Consultants, equipment vendors and former employees are all available. Any account that opens by saying the knowledge is locked up has described a different problem, and it is a problem that has real remedies. This one does not, and the reason is that the obstacle is not on the supply side at all.
