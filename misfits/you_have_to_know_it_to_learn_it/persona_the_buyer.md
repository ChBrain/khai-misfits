# Persona: The Buyer

## Taxonomy

[Buyer](position_buyer.md). The one who is behind and acquires what it lacks.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An organisation without the capability, with a budget, a board, and competitors who already have it.

## Action

Buys the technology, the licence, the process, the training.
Specifies carefully, negotiates well, and takes delivery of everything specified.

## Shadow

Makes the correct decision and it does not work.
The seat is not cutting a corner: building the capability itself means funding years of research to arrive where others already are, on a budget that does not stretch to it, against competitors who will not wait. Every adviser it could reasonably consult would tell it to buy, and buying is what an organisation in this position does. What buying does not do is produce the by-product that research produces, which is the ability to see which part of what arrives matters. So the seat's rational choice is the one that leaves it unable to use its own purchase.

## Tell

Chooses correctly, and the correct choice is the one that does not build the capacity.
