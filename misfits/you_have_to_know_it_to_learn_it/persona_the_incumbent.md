# Persona: The Incumbent

## Taxonomy

[Incumbent](position_incumbent.md). The one who absorbs the same material effortlessly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A competitor with its own research programme, reading the same published literature the buyer bought.

## Action

Reads the paper.
Recognises what is new in it, sees where it fits, and has it in production within the year.

## Shadow

Gets the outside knowledge nearly free, because it already paid for the capacity.
The seat is not receiving a favour and has no advantage anybody could withdraw. It funded its own research for its own reasons, and the ability to use everybody else's work arrived as a by-product it did not buy and cannot sell. That is why the gap widens without either party doing anything to the other: the same published literature is worth a great deal to this seat and nearly nothing to the buyer, and the difference is entirely in what each already knew.

## Tell

Pays nothing for what the buyer cannot get at any price.
