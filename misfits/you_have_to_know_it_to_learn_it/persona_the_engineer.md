# Persona: The Engineer

## Taxonomy

[Engineer](position_engineer.md). The one reading it correctly and unable to use it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A capable engineer inside the buyer, with the documentation, the equipment and the time to read.

## Action

Reads what arrived.
Understands every sentence, follows every procedure, and runs the process as written.

## Shadow

Comprehends completely and cannot tell which detail is load-bearing.
The seat is not failing to understand and is not lazy: it can follow the text, reproduce the steps and explain them back. What it lacks is the surrounding knowledge that makes one line of a procedure obviously the critical one and another obviously boilerplate, and that surrounding knowledge is not in the document by anybody's fault. It comes from having worked in the area, which is the thing this organisation has not done. So the seat reads the answer without being able to recognise it as the answer.

## Tell

Understands the words and not which of them carry the weight.
