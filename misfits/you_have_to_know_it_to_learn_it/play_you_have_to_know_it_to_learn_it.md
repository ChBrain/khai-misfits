# Play: You Have to Know It to Learn It

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

You Have to Know It to Learn It.
Knowledge from outside is abundant, published, licensable and often cheap, and an organisation that is behind can acquire it. What it cannot acquire is the ability to recognise which of it matters, assimilate it and apply it, because that ability is largely a function of related knowledge the organisation already has, and that knowledge is a by-product of having done the work itself. So the organisation with the least of its own is the least able to use anybody else's, and buying instead of building is the rational thing for it to do.

## Arc

The bend is this: the capacity to absorb outside knowledge is a by-product of producing knowledge yourself, so the organisation that most needs to buy is the one least able to use what it buys.

Cue: an acceptance certificate signed, and a process that will not run. Closes when the shortfall is found to be in neither the package nor the reading of it.

**[So It Looks Like Refusal and Is Inability](plot_so_it_looks_like_refusal_and_is_inability.md)**
Cue: an evaluation asking why the transfer did not take. Closes when the diagnosis everybody reaches for is found to describe a different organisation entirely.

## Stakes

Whether an organisation that is behind can stop being behind by buying what it lacks.
Every plot moves the same way: toward knowledge that is genuinely available, a capacity to use it that rests on knowledge already held, a research investment whose second output nobody buys it for, a decision to buy that is correct on the information available, a delivery that omits nothing, and a bench where the process will not run, and away from any account in which somebody withheld something or somebody refused to learn. The production cannot fault the supplier, who handed over everything asked for and more; and it cannot fault the buyer, whose alternative was years of research to arrive where others already were. What is left to fight over is not whether anybody erred but what to call a deficit that is fed by the only rational response to it.
