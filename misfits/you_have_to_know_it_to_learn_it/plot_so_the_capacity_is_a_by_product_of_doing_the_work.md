# Plot: So the Capacity Is a By-Product of Doing the Work

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Know It to Learn It](play_you_have_to_know_it_to_learn_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two organisations reading the same published paper.

## Action

[The Second Face of Research](piece_the_second_face_of_research.md) shows: one of them has it in production within the year, and the other cannot see what is new in it.

## Stage

A second output of research that nobody funds research for.

## Tension

The by-product is what makes the same free literature worth everything and nothing.
Research produces the knowledge it was funded to produce, and it also produces the capacity to use knowledge produced by everybody else. No budget line names the second one, no supplier sells it, and the organisation that has it did not buy it. That is why the distance between an organisation that researches and one that does not widens without either acting on the other, and why the widening cannot be attributed to a strategy, a refusal or a market failure.
