## Load Bearing

It is the thing that decides whether outside knowledge can be used, and it cannot be bought because it is a property of the reader.
The capacity to recognise the value of new external information, assimilate it and apply it is largely a function of related knowledge already held. That is what lets somebody glance at a procedure and know which line is load-bearing, which paper in a literature is the one that matters, and which of a hundred plausible next moves is the right one. It is not tacit knowledge somebody is refusing to write down and it is not a trade secret: it is the accumulated residue of having worked in the area, and it sits in the person reading rather than in anything they read.
