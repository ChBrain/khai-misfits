# Persona: The Supplier

## Taxonomy

[Supplier](position_supplier.md). The one who hands over everything and is not withholding.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A firm, laboratory or licensor with a working process, full documentation and a commercial interest in the transfer succeeding.

## Action

Delivers the package: specifications, drawings, procedures, training, support.
Answers questions, sends engineers, and signs the acceptance.

## Shadow

Transfers everything transferable and the important part does not move.
The seat is not holding anything back, and has every reason not to: a transfer that fails costs it the follow-on work, the reference and the reputation. It can hand over what is written down and can send people to explain it. What it cannot hand over is the twenty years of related work that lets its own engineers know which of those pages matters this week, because that is not in the package and is not the sort of thing a package holds. The seat's completeness is genuine and is not sufficient.

## Tell

Withholds nothing and cannot deliver the part that decides it.
