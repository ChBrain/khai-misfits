# Piece: The Second Face of Research

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Know It to Learn It](play_you_have_to_know_it_to_learn_it.md). The output nobody funds research for, and the one that decides this.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Accumulated by [the incumbent](persona_the_incumbent.md), and absent wherever [choosing to buy rather than build](process_choosing_to_buy_rather_than_build.md) was the right call.

## Load Bearing

Research produces two things, and the organisation buying instead of researching forgoes the one it did not know it needed.
The obvious output is the knowledge created. The second is the capacity to use knowledge created by everybody else, which arrives as a by-product and is what makes the same published literature worth a great deal to one organisation and nearly nothing to another. Nobody buys research for that, no budget line names it, and no supplier can sell it. It is why the gap between an organisation that researches and one that buys widens without either doing anything to the other, and why the widening is not anybody's strategy.

## Apparent

An externality, and somebody else's problem.

## Yearbook

Is the reason the deficit feeds itself: the only affordable response to being behind is the one that does not produce it.
