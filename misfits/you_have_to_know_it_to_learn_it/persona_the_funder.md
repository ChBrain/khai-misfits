# Persona: The Funder

## Taxonomy

[Funder](position_funder.md). The one financing the transfer, measuring the transaction.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An agency or development body with a budget for capability transfer, an evaluation framework and a reporting cycle.

## Action

Funds the acquisition.
Verifies that the technology was delivered, the training was given and the acceptance was signed, and reports the transfer complete.

## Shadow

Measures the only thing that is measurable at the point of delivery.
The seat is not being lax: a transfer is an event with a date, a cost and a signature, and absorption is a process with no completion and no observable moment. Asking for evidence of absorption at handover asks for something that does not exist yet, and waiting for it means holding budgets open across reporting cycles that were not designed to hold them. So the framework counts deliveries, which is the honest thing it can count, and a project in which everything was delivered and nothing was absorbed is recorded as a success.

## Tell

Records a transfer that took place, which is the true statement.
