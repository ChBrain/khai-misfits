# Plot: The Delivery Is Complete and the Bench Is Silent

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Know It to Learn It](play_you_have_to_know_it_to_learn_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An acceptance certificate signed, and a process that will not run right.

## Action

At [the bench](place_the_bench.md), [the engineer](persona_the_engineer.md) follows an accurate procedure and gets a result the manual does not describe.

## Stage

A failure located in neither the package nor the reading.

## Tension

Every object in the room checks out, which is what leaves nothing to point at.
The documentation is complete, because a supplier whose reputation depends on the transfer had every reason to make it so. The engineer is competent and read it correctly, and can reproduce and explain every step. What is missing is the weighting that would make one of a hundred plausible next moves obviously right, and that is not absent from the text: it is not the kind of thing a text carries. There is no third object in the room, which is why the diagnosis that follows will be about people.
