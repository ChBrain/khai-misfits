# Plot: And Using It Needs Knowledge You Already Have

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Know It to Learn It](play_you_have_to_know_it_to_learn_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A manual open at the right page.

## Action

[The Prior Related Knowledge](piece_the_prior_related_knowledge.md) is what decides whether the page is recognised as the right one.

## Stage

A capacity that rests on what the reader brought with them.

## Tension

The ability to recognise, assimilate and apply is largely a function of related knowledge already held.
That is what lets somebody glance at a procedure and see which line bites, know which paper in a literature is the one, and pick the right move out of a hundred plausible ones. It is not a secret being kept and not tacit knowledge somebody declines to write down: it is the residue of having worked in the area, and it lives in the reader rather than the text. So what an organisation can get out of a document is bounded by what it already knew.
