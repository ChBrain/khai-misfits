# Plot: And the Firm That Is Behind Buys Instead, Correctly

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Know It to Learn It](play_you_have_to_know_it_to_learn_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A board choosing between years of research and a licence available now.

## Action

[The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) takes the licence, through [choosing to buy rather than build](process_choosing_to_buy_rather_than_build.md).

## Stage

The right decision, made on the information available.

## Tension

This is the step where the account usually finds a villain, and there is none.
Building the capability means funding years of research to arrive where competitors already are, on a budget that does not stretch to it, while those competitors keep moving. Every adviser the seat could reasonably consult would tell it to buy. The decision is not short-termism, is not a failure of ambition, and would be defended by anybody assessing it at the time. It is simply the decision that does not produce the by-product, and the by-product is what would have let the purchase work.
