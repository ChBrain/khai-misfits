# Play: Taken Apart to Be Read

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Taken Apart to Be Read.
The order of the layers is most of what a site can tell anybody, and the order is established by taking them off one at a time. So the reading and the destruction are one motion, the site can be asked once, and whoever holds the trowel that season is the only person who will ever see it.

## Arc

The bend is this: the case for waiting is correct and never expires, and so is the decay it is weighed against, so there is no moment at which both favour acting.
It runs on a method that consumes its object.
[Reading It Is Taking It Apart](plot_reading_it_is_taking_it_apart.md). [The Section](piece_the_section.md) exists while the cut is open and not afterwards, so every later claim is made from a drawing and no dispute about it can be settled by looking.
And every later method would do better.
[And Every Later Method Would Do Better](plot_and_every_later_method_would_do_better.md). [The Method Not Yet Invented](piece_the_method_not_yet_invented.md) is certain to arrive and unknowable in content, and the argument is available in identical form to every seat that follows, so it condemns any excavation whatever.
And the ground does not wait.
[But the Ground Does Not Wait](plot_but_the_ground_does_not_wait.md). [The Curator](persona_the_curator.md) holds a deposit whose water table has fallen and whose ploughsoil deepens, and the only instrument that would establish its condition is the act being deferred.
Then [Leaving It Is a Decision Too](plot_leaving_it_is_a_decision_too.md).
An excavation produces a report and a bill and a signature; a deferral produces a note that the surface is unchanged, so the quieter course is quieter because its losses are unobserved rather than smaller.
And [the timetable belongs to somebody else](plot_and_the_timetable_belongs_to_somebody_else.md). [The Planner](persona_the_planner.md) settles the question no archaeologist can settle, correctly and at scale, without ever putting it.
And [the record is only as good as the questions](plot_the_record_is_only_as_good_as_the_questions.md). [The Site Report](piece_the_site_report.md) is complete with respect to a question set rather than to the ground, and it is the only thing that will exist.

## Stakes

Whether a thing that can be examined once should be examined now.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a method that consumes its object, an argument for waiting that never expires, a decay that never stops, a deferral that is a position rather than an abstention, a timetable owned by somebody not asking the question, and a record dated at the moment it is made, and away from any account in which an excavator was hasty or a curator was negligent. The production cannot fault the excavator, because reading the sequence is taking it apart and this season's method is the best there has ever been; and it cannot fault the curator, because deferring to better hands is the discipline's own answer and is written into law. What is left to fight over is not whether to dig but what to call a choice between two courses whose costs are measured on different instruments, only one of which reports.
