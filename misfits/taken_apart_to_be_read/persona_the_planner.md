# Persona: The Planner

## Taxonomy

[Planner](position_planner.md). The one who fixes when it happens, for reasons unrelated to it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A consent, a construction programme, a condition requiring archaeological work before the ground is broken.

## Action

Sets the date on which the road is built.
Requires, correctly, that the site be excavated first.

## Shadow

Has produced most of the archaeology of the last forty years and chose none of it.
Developer-funded work is the reason the record exists at the scale it does, and the condition attached to the consent is a genuine protection that did not exist before. It also means the moment of reading is set by a construction programme, so the choice between now and later is not made by anybody weighing method against decay: it is made by whoever needs the ground, and the answer is always now.

## Tell

Funds the discipline and sets its clock, meaning neither.
