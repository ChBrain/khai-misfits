## Load Bearing

It is certain to arrive and its content is unknowable, which is what makes the deferral argument correct and unbounded.
Every decade has read the same earth better than the last, from pollen to radiocarbon to isotopes to micromorphology to sedimentary DNA, so the claim that later hands will do better is not optimism but the plainest induction the discipline has. And it never stops being true: there is no point at which a seat can say the methods are now good enough, because the same argument is available to every seat after it. So the reason for waiting does not expire, and neither does the decay it is being weighed against.
