## Load Bearing

It is complete with respect to a question set rather than to the ground, and no care improves that.
Everything in it is measured well and the archive behind it is deposited properly: the deficiency is not in the execution. It is that nobody sampled for pollen before pollen could be read, nobody sieved for fish bone before anybody wondered what people ate, and nobody kept the sediment before it could be read for the DNA of what walked over it. So the record's completeness would have to have been completeness about questions not yet asked, which is not a standard anybody could meet.
