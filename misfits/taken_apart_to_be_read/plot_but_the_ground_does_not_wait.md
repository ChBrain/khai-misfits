# Plot: But the Ground Does Not Wait

## Taxonomy

[Taken Apart to Be Read](play_taken_apart_to_be_read.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A field of barley over a deposit, and a decade passing.

## Action

[Leaving it in the ground](process_leaving_it_in_the_ground.md): [the curator](persona_the_curator.md), holding [Curator](position_curator.md), files another annual note at [the scheduled field](place_the_scheduled_field.md) saying the surface is unchanged.

## Stage

A site protected in law, and a deposit nobody has seen for forty years.

## Tension

The argument for waiting is unbounded and so is the decay, so the two curves never cross and there is no correct moment.
The water table falls, oxygen reaches what was waterlogged, the ploughsoil deepens a little each decade, and material is taken by people who do not report it. None of that is visible from the surface, and the only instrument that would settle the condition of the deposit is the excavation being deferred. So the seat holds something whose survival it is not permitted to verify, and a deferral that is right in principle is unverifiable in fact, which is a different thing from safe.
