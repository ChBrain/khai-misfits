## Action

## Stage

## Tension

The party that answers the question nobody can answer is the one party not asking it.
Developer-funded work is a genuine protection and is the reason the record exists at the scale it does, so this is not a complaint about it. But the choice between now and later, which no archaeologist can settle, is settled in practice by whoever needs the ground, and the answer is always now. So the trade is resolved constantly, at scale, correctly under the law, by a seat that is not weighing method against decay and has no reason to.
