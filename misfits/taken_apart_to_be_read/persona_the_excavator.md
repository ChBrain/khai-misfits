# Persona: The Excavator

## Taxonomy

[Excavator](position_excavator.md). The one who decides whether to open the ground.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A funded season, a licence, a team, and a field with something under it.

## Action

Opens the trench.
Removes each layer in the order it was laid down, recording as it goes.

## Shadow

Reads by dismantling, and there is no other way to read.
The stratigraphy is the evidence and the relation between the layers is most of the information, so it can only be established by taking them off one at a time and can only be established once. This seat records more carefully than any predecessor, which is true of every generation of the seat, and the recording and the destruction are the same motion rather than two things that happen to occur together.

## Tell

Does the best work ever done here, once, on material that will never be read again.
