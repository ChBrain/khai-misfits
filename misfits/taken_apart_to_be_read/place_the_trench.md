## Withheld

A second reading.
The place is constituted by removal, so it cannot be revisited, re-sampled or checked: the layers are the evidence, their order is most of the information, and establishing the order consists of taking them apart. There is no careful version of this that leaves the ground legible, because legibility here is a property of the sequence and the sequence is consumed by being followed.
