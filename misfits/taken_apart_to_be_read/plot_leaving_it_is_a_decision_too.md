# Plot: Leaving It Is a Decision Too

## Taxonomy

[Taken Apart to Be Read](play_taken_apart_to_be_read.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The discipline's own policy, that the best place for a site is where it is.

## Action

[The Scheduled Field](place_the_scheduled_field.md) is compared with [the trench](place_the_trench.md), and the two are found to be two readings of one question rather than an act and an abstention.

## Stage

Two courses, both defensible, and no third.

## Tension

Not digging is a choice with a cost, and it is the one whose cost nobody has to account for.
An excavation produces a report, a bill and a set of decisions somebody signed; a deferral produces a note that the surface is unchanged. So the two are held to different standards of proof although they are the same kind of decision about the same object, and the quieter one is quieter because its losses are unobserved rather than because they are smaller. This is the plot that closes the obvious exit, since the exit is not an abstention from the trade but a position within it.
