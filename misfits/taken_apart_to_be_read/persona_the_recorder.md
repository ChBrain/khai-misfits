# Persona: The Recorder

## Taxonomy

[Recorder](position_recorder.md). The one who makes the only thing that survives.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Context sheets, a drawn section, photogrammetry, sample tubes, and a report that will outlive the trench by centuries.

## Action

Writes down everything.
Publishes.

## Shadow

Records exhaustively against a list, and the list is of its own moment.
Everything measured is measured well, and what is measured is what this decade knows to ask for: nobody sampled for pollen before pollen could be read, and nobody sieved for fish bone before anybody cared what people ate. So the record is complete with respect to a question set rather than to the ground, and the completeness cannot be improved by care, only by knowing in advance what will later be wanted.

## Tell

Produces a faultless account of the questions that were current.
