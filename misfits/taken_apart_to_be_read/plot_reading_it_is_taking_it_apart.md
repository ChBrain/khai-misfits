# Plot: Reading It Is Taking It Apart

## Taxonomy

[Taken Apart to Be Read](play_taken_apart_to_be_read.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An open trench, and a layer that has to come off before the one beneath it can be seen.

## Action

[Lifting the layer](process_lifting_the_layer.md): [the excavator](persona_the_excavator.md), holding [Excavator](position_excavator.md), removes each context in order at [the trench](place_the_trench.md).

## Stage

A sequence established, by the best method the discipline has.

## Tension

The method and the destruction are one motion, so there is no careful version that leaves the ground legible.
The relation between the layers is most of the information, and it is established by taking them off one at a time: legibility here is a property of the sequence, and the sequence is consumed by being followed. [The Section](piece_the_section.md) exists while the cut is open and not afterwards, so every later claim about the site is made from a drawing of a thing that is not there, and a dispute about what it showed cannot be settled by looking.
