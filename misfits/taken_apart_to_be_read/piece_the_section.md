## Load Bearing

It is the primary evidence and it exists only while the trench is open, so every claim about the site is made from a drawing of a thing that is no longer there.
The face shows the order of deposition, which is the information, and it is produced by cutting through the deposit rather than found lying about. Nothing survives it: the drawing, the photographs and the samples are the record, and they are answers to the questions asked at the face. A later dispute about what the face showed cannot be settled by looking, because there is nothing to look at.
