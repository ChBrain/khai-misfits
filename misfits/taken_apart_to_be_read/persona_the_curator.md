# Persona: The Curator

## Taxonomy

[Curator](position_curator.md). The one who keeps a site by not looking at it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A scheduled monument, a management agreement, a field of barley, and a policy that says the best place for it is where it is.

## Action

Leaves it in the ground.
Files an annual condition report based on what can be seen from the surface.

## Shadow

Holds the discipline's own answer and cannot say whether it is working.
Preservation in situ is right in principle, is written into law and treaty, and defers the reading to hands that will do it better. What it cannot do is confirm the deposit is still there: the water table has fallen, the ploughsoil deepens a little each decade, and the only way to check the condition of the layers is the act being deferred. So the seat keeps something whose survival it is not permitted to verify.

## Tell

Preserves it perfectly, as far as anybody is allowed to know.
