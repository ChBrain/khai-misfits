# Process: Accumulating

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). The procedure growing, by increments nobody weighs against the whole.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Every addition, and no removal.
At [the procedure](place_the_procedure.md), continuously and without a decision.

## Direction

Toward a document that is defensible line by line and indefensible whole.
The total was never proposed and so was never approved: it accrued, each increment defensible on its own day, and nobody signed for the sum because the sum was never on an agenda.
A removal cannot accrue, being a single act with an owner, so the two directions are not opposite decisions.

## Lever

Time, and the absence of any step that runs the other way.
Because each line is separately justified, an argument against the whole must be made against every part, which is a fight nobody has the standing or the hours to pick.

## Echo

Ninety pages, every one of them with a reason,
agreed to by nobody.
