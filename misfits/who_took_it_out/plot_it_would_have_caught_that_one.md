# Plot: It Would Have Caught That One

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Incident](persona_the_incident.md), which really happened and really cost something.
[The Review](place_the_review.md), convened properly.

## Action

[Justifying](process_justifying.md): [The Incident](persona_the_incident.md), in [the warrant seat](position_warrant.md), is examined at [the review](place_the_review.md) and yields a step that would have caught it.

## Stage

[The Control](persona_the_control.md), on [Catch What Was Missed Last Time](plan_catch_what_was_missed_last_time.md), is added to [the procedure](place_the_procedure.md) and costs an afternoon a month.

## Tension

Every addition is right, and the play has to establish that before any of them can be the problem.
Something went wrong. A review was held by competent people acting in good faith. It found a step that genuinely addresses what happened, and the step is cheap on its own terms.
**There is no line in the document that is wrong.** So the trap cannot be sprung by finding a silly control or a nervous committee, and the production never argues that any particular step should not have been added. It has to be sprung with fifteen years of correct decisions and no bad one anywhere in the sequence.
