# Process: Justifying

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). The incident being examined, and yielding a step nobody can argue with.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Harm, real and recent.
At [the review](place_the_review.md), by people trying to prevent a recurrence.

## Direction

Toward a lesson learned from the most legitimate source a lesson can have: something that actually happened.
The warrant is an event rather than a projection, so the control it produces carries the authority of the event.
And that authority does not decay when the event does: the step remains attached to a real occurrence years after the conditions have gone.

## Lever

A true story, which is the strongest warrant available.
Because anybody proposing removal is proposing to discard the lesson of something that genuinely happened, the strength of this step is what makes the control unanswerable later.

## Echo

A step everybody agrees with,
outliving the circumstances that made it right.
