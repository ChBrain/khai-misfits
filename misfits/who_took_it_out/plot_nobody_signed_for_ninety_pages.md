## Stage

## Tension

The asymmetry is structural before it is psychological, and this is where it is stated.
**An addition is a drift and a removal is a decision.** The ninety pages were never proposed, so they were never approved: they accrued in increments each defensible on its own day, and nobody signed for the sum because the sum was never on an agenda.
A removal cannot accrue. It is a single act, it happens at one meeting, it has an owner, and it enters the record with a date and a name against it. So the two directions are not opposite decisions: **one of them is a decision and the other is weather.** And because each line is separately justified, an argument against the whole has to be made against every part, which is a fight nobody has the standing or the hours to pick.
