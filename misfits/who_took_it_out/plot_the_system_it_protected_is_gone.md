# Plot: The System It Protected Is Gone

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Cause That Went Away](piece_the_cause_that_went_away.md): the system decommissioned four years ago.
[The Control](persona_the_control.md), still performed monthly.

## Action

[Adding](process_adding.md) having long since finished, the step persists at [the procedure](place_the_procedure.md), performed by people who know what it guards and know it is gone.

## Stage

[The Remover](persona_the_remover.md), on [Propose That We Stop Doing This](plan_propose_that_we_stop_doing_this.md), can prove the step is pointless, and does it anyway.

## Tension

This is the case that removes every remaining excuse, and it is the one the mechanism handles worst.
Here there is no uncertainty about whether the control is needed: it is demonstrably not. Nobody would defend it on the merits and nobody has to, because **nobody is asked to defend it.** The obsolete step is not winning an argument; there is no argument.
**The cheapest thing anybody can do with an obsolete control is comply with it.** Compliance costs an afternoon a month and no signature. Removal costs one signature and the standing exposure that goes with it. So the step outlives the thing it protected, not through inertia or affection, but because the accounting makes doing the pointless work the dominant move for every individual who could stop it.
