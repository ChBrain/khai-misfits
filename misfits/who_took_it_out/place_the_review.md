# Place: The Review

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). Where an incident is examined and a control is produced: competent, well run, and only ever additive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

People who were there, working out what would have caught it.
A step found, agreed and written into the procedure.

## Holds

The play's quietest fact: this room works.
It is convened properly, staffed by competent people acting in good faith, and it reliably produces controls that genuinely address what went wrong. Nothing that happens here is a failure.

## Offers

A lesson learned from the most legitimate source there is, something that actually happened.

## Withheld

Any occasion on which the question runs the other way.
No incident ever produces the finding that a step should come out, because an incident is evidence about what was missing and never about what was surplus, so this room has no reverse gear and was not built with one.
