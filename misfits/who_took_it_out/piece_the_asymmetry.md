# Piece: The Asymmetry

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). Harm from an act judged more harshly than the same harm from an omission: measured, not assumed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the judgement of everybody who will review the decision afterwards.
Read accurately by [the remover](persona_the_remover.md), before anything is proposed.

## Load Bearing

Without it the reluctance is timidity and the remedy is encouragement.
It is load bearing because it is **studied**: people asked to judge identical outcomes, one reached by doing and one by not doing, do not judge them alike, so the person declining to propose a removal is reading a real feature of their world.

## Apparent

A bias, and therefore something that ought to yield to being pointed out.
It does not. **A bias that is true about how one will actually be treated cannot be argued away**, and naming it to the person who would have to sign changes nothing about what happens to them.

## Yearbook

Measured in the judgement literature, enacted at [the inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md).
It is the cognitive half of a mechanism whose other half is simply what records contain.
