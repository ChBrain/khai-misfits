# Place: The Inquiry

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). Where harm is examined afterwards, and where a removal has a name and a non-addition has none.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A proper enquiry, conducted in good faith, with a duty to establish what happened.
A record produced: name, date, form, wherever a control was taken out.

## Holds

Why the deterrence needs nobody to intend it.
Where a control was removed there is somebody to ask; where one was never added there is nothing to find, because a non-addition leaves no record. The same enquiry reaches a person in one case and an absence in the other.

## Offers

Accountability, honestly established, which is what an inquiry is for.

## Withheld

Any symmetry between the two cases.
That difference is a property of what records are rather than of how the question is put, so no improvement in the enquiry's fairness evens it out, and it operates on every removal that was never proposed.
