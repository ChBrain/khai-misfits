# Position: Warrant

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). The seat of the incident: real, costly, remembered, and unarguable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An event that actually happened and actually cost something.
People who were there.
The strongest kind of warrant a control can have.

## Orders

Occur.
Be reviewed properly by people trying to prevent a recurrence.
Yield a step that would have caught it.

## Loses

Nothing, and its strength is what makes the step it produced unanswerable afterwards.
Because the warrant is a real event rather than a projection, anybody proposing removal is proposing to discard the lesson of something that genuinely happened, which is a bad position to argue from however good the case.
It ends fifteen years past, cited by people who were not there.

## Drives

Toward a lesson learned,
from the most legitimate source a lesson can have,
so the seat that justifies the control is the seat that outlives it.
