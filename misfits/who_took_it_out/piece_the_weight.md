# Piece: The Weight

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). The cost of the whole procedure: real, borne by everybody, and itemised nowhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Carried at [the procedure](place_the_procedure.md), by whoever works under it.
Absent from every review that added to it.

## Load Bearing

Without it the accumulation is harmless and the play is about tidiness.
It is load bearing because it is **paid in a currency nobody counts**: each step costs an afternoon a month, no step's cost is ever set against the total, and no meeting has the whole figure in front of it.

## Apparent

Overhead. The price of being careful.
It reads as the natural cost of a mature organisation, which is how it is described in the documents that add to it, and that description is not obviously wrong.

## Yearbook

Summed by [accumulating](process_accumulating.md), never presented anywhere.
It is what [the total](persona_the_total.md) actually is, expressed in the hours of people who never attended any of the meetings that produced it.
