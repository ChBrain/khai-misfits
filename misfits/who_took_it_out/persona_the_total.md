# Persona: The Total

## Taxonomy

[Aggregate](position_aggregate.md). Ninety pages: never proposed, never approved, and nobody's decision.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Enormous, and unauthored.
Not the product of a policy or a philosophy: the sum of fifteen years of individually correct increments.
Defensible line by line and indefensible as a whole, which no single conversation is ever about.

## Action

Accrues.
Is complied with, at a cost that is real and is never itemised anywhere.
Grows.

## Shadow

That it was never anybody's decision, which is what removes the villain and also the remedy.
Because it accrued rather than being adopted, there is no author to persuade, no meeting at which it was approved, and no proposal on record to revisit; and because each line is separately justified, an argument against the whole must be made against every part.
It ends larger than last year, having been agreed to by nobody.

## Tell

Says nothing.
It is ninety pages, and every page has a reason.
