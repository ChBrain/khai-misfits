# Persona: The Remover

## Taxonomy

[Signatory](position_signatory.md). Whoever would have to propose taking a step out, and put their name on the proposal.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Correct, and unwilling.
Can see plainly that the step guards nothing, has read the history, and would be right to remove it.
Reading their own position accurately, which is what stops them.

## Action

Notices the obsolete step.
Works out what proposing its removal would involve.
Complies with it instead, which takes an afternoon a month and requires no signature.

## Shadow

Nothing they can be faulted for, and the seat is where the play's mechanism becomes a person.
Because leaving the step is an omission and removing it is an act, and harm following an act is judged more harshly than the same harm following an omission, the asymmetry they are reading is real and knowing about it does not dissolve it.
It ends performing a monthly step it can prove is pointless.

## Tell

Says: I would rather just do it than have that conversation.
Entirely rational, and it is how ninety pages happen.
