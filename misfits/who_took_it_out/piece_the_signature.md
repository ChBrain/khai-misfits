## Load Bearing

Without it the two directions are symmetrical and the process would shed as readily as it grows.
It is load bearing because it is **the whole of the asymmetry made concrete**: an addition is distributed across many small justified acts nobody signs for, and a removal is one act with an owner, so the directions differ in kind and not in degree.

## Apparent

Good governance. Accountability for changes.
That reading is correct, which is the difficulty: requiring a name for a consequential act is right, and it is what makes the one-way ratchet turn.
