# Persona: The Question

## Taxonomy

[Hindsight](position_hindsight.md). Who took it out: asked after every harm, and never asked about anything that was left in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Fair, and one-directional.
A proper question after an incident, asked by people with a duty to ask it, and one that would find a real answer if the answer existed.
Never paired with its opposite, because its opposite has no defendant.

## Action

Is asked, after harm.
Finds a name, a date and a form, if a control was removed.
Finds nothing at all if a control was merely never added, there being nobody to find.

## Shadow

Its own reasonableness, which is what makes it impossible to object to.
Because a removal leaves a record with an owner and a non-addition leaves no record at all, the same enquiry conducted honestly reaches a person in one case and an absence in the other, and that difference is not an oversight in how it is asked.
It ends having deterred every removal that never happened, which nobody counts.

## Tell

Says: who signed off on taking that out.
A fair question, with an answer, in exactly one of the two cases where it should be asked.
