## Tension

The asymmetry is measured, which is why knowing about it does not dissolve it.
People asked to judge identical outcomes, one produced by an act and one by an omission, **do not judge them alike**: harm that follows doing is condemned more than the same harm that follows not doing. This is not a folk belief about how committees behave; it is a studied effect with a name.
So the person contemplating a removal is **reading their situation correctly**. They are not timid and they are not wrong about the risk to themselves: the risk is real and is asymmetric, and the accurate reading is what produces the inaction. **A bias that is true about the world cannot be argued away by pointing it out.**
