# Pitch: Added Following the Incident of March

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Documentary, mild, unhurried.
The register of a procedure read aloud from a binder: section, subsection, added following the incident of March.
Nothing indignant anywhere in it, because every line in the document was somebody being careful.

## Undertow

Under the procedure, two facts that do not negotiate.
A control is added one line at a time, in answer to something real. A control is removed all at once, by somebody in particular.
Between those two, a document grows for fifteen years and nobody ever decided it should.

## Nerve

The unease of a step that protects a system decommissioned four years ago,
performed every month by people who know that,
because complying with it is cheaper than being the person who signed the form.

## Echo

After the run: ninety pages, every one of them justified,
a total nobody proposed and nobody approved,
and one question, asked in only one direction.
