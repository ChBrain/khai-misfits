# Position: Signatory

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). The seat of whoever would have to propose a removal, and put a name on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A clear view that the step guards nothing.
The history, read, and a case that would stand up.
An accurate reading of what proposing it would expose them to.

## Orders

Notice the obsolete step.
Weigh proposing its removal.
Comply with it instead.

## Loses

Nothing they can be faulted for, and this seat is where the mechanism becomes a person.
Because leaving the step is an omission and removing it is an act, and harm following an act is judged more harshly than the same harm following an omission, the asymmetry being read is real, and knowing about it does not dissolve it.
It ends performing a monthly step it can prove is pointless.

## Drives

Toward a process that makes sense,
by an assessment that is entirely correct,
so the seat that sees the waste is the seat that pays for it quietly.
