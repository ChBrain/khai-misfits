# Process: Asking

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). The enquiry after harm, finding a name in one direction and an absence in the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Harm, and a duty to establish what happened.
At [the inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md), properly and in good faith.

## Direction

Toward an honest account, which it produces.
Where a control was removed there is a record: a name, a date, a form, and a person who can be asked. Where a control was merely never added there is no record, because a non-addition leaves none.
So the same enquiry, conducted identically, reaches a person in one case and nothing in the other.

## Lever

The record, which exists for acts and not for omissions.
Because that difference is a property of what records are rather than of how the question is asked, no improvement in the enquiry's fairness evens it out.

## Echo

A fair question with an answer,
in exactly one of the two cases where it should be asked.
