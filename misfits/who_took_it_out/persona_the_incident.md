# Persona: The Incident

## Taxonomy

[Warrant](position_warrant.md). The thing that actually went wrong: real, costly, and the reason nobody can argue with the step it produced.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Real, and remembered.
Not a hypothetical risk raised by a nervous committee: something happened, it cost the organisation, and people were there for it.
The strongest kind of warrant there is.

## Action

Occurs.
Is reviewed, properly, by people trying to prevent a recurrence.
Produces a step that would have caught it.

## Shadow

Its own strength, which is what makes the step it produced unarguable afterwards.
Because the warrant is a real event rather than a projection, anybody proposing to remove the step is proposing to discard the lesson of something that genuinely happened, and that is a bad position to argue from however good the case.
It ends fifteen years past, cited in a document, by people who were not there.

## Tell

Says: this is not theoretical, it happened.
True then, true now, and the system it happened to was switched off in 2021.
