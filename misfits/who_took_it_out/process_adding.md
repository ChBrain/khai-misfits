# Process: Adding

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). A step entering the procedure, one line at a time, after something real.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An incident that happened and cost something.
At [the review](place_the_review.md), convened properly.

## Direction

Toward preventing a recurrence, which the step genuinely would.
Competent people acting in good faith find a control that addresses what went wrong, and it is cheap on its own terms.
So each pass through this step is correct, and fifteen years of correct passes is what produces the document.

## Lever

An increment, small enough that nobody weighs it against the total.
Because no single addition is large and every one is justified, there is no point in the sequence at which refusing would have been reasonable.

## Echo

A control that would have caught the last one,
and one more line in a document nobody is reading whole.
