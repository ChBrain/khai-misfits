# Process: Proposing

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md). A removal being considered, and not put on the agenda.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Somebody noticing a step that guards nothing.
At [the procedure](place_the_procedure.md), privately, and going no further.

## Direction

Toward a process that makes sense, by an assessment that is entirely correct.
The step is demonstrably obsolete, the case would stand up, and the person can prove it.
What stops the proposal is not doubt about the case but an accurate reading of what making it would expose them to.

## Lever

A signature, which a removal requires and an addition does not.
Because harm following an act is judged more harshly than the same harm following an omission, the exposure is real and asymmetric, and knowing that does not reduce it.

## Echo

An afternoon a month, spent,
by somebody who could prove it need not be.
