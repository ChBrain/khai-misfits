# Plot: Name the One You Are Removing

## Taxonomy

[Who Took It Out](play_who_took_it_out.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The settlements, arriving: sunset every clause, appoint a body to prune, require each addition to name a removal.
[The Signature](piece_the_signature.md), which each of them moves.

## Action

[Asking](process_asking.md): [The Question](persona_the_question.md), in [the hindsight seat](position_hindsight.md), is put at [the inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md) after harm, and finds a name in one direction and an absence in the other.

## Stage

[The Question](persona_the_question.md), on [Ask It Only in One Direction](plan_ask_it_only_in_one_direction.md), is a fair question with an answer in exactly one of the two cases where it should be asked.

## Tension

Every remedy relocates the signature and none abolishes it.
Sunset clauses work, and somebody must decline to renew, which is a signed act in different clothes. A pruning body works, and its members will be asked the same question about the same removals. Requiring each addition to name a removal genuinely works and is the strongest of them, and it buys its effect by **making addition harder at the exact moment somebody has just been hurt** and everybody in the room wants the control, which is the least defensible moment to introduce friction.
**There is no move that makes taking a control out cost the same as leaving it in**, because the difference does not live in the procedure. It lives in the fact that a removal produces a person who can be named afterwards and a non-removal produces nobody, and no rewriting of the process changes which of those two an inquiry can find.
