# Plot: The One Edge That Could Be Checked Came Apart

## Taxonomy

[A Hundred Years to Vouch for a Thousand](play_a_hundred_years_to_vouch_for_a_thousand.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The most recent decades of the overlap, where both records are densest.

## Action

[The Divergence](piece_the_divergence.md) is found at [the overlap](place_the_overlap.md): high-latitude density series that tracked temperature for centuries stop tracking it from around the middle of the twentieth century.

## Stage

The discipline's own most uncomfortable result, published by the people who found it.

## Tension

The single stretch where the stability of the relation could be examined is the stretch where it did not hold.
Why it happens is argued over and may be local, recent and specific to particular species and latitudes, and none of that is settled here. What is not arguable is the shape: an assumption that cannot be tested where it is used was tested at the one edge available, and failed there. That is neither proof the reconstructions are wrong nor a reason to set the assumption aside, and it is the only evidence anybody has in either direction.
