# A Hundred Years to Vouch for a Thousand: Reference

The research warrant for A Hundred Years to Vouch for a Thousand.
The term is the house's; the play runs on proxy calibration and on the span an instrument can be tested over
against the span it is used over, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging proxy reconstruction as **an instrument validated only where it is not needed**: a tree ring records
growth, and growth is not temperature, so the series has to be joined to a thermometer to mean anything.
Thermometers go back about a century and a half; the reconstruction goes back a thousand years. The join can
only be made, and can only be tested, on the span where both records exist, which is exactly the span for which
nobody needed a proxy. The play models the documented mechanics, the measurement that is exact and unlabelled,
the yardstick that is indispensable and short, the held-back verification that is correct and confined to the
window, the one edge at which the assumption was examinable and did not hold, the uncertainty stated honestly
about everything with a term in the data, and the convergence of independent archives that narrows the question
without closing it, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of anybody fitting carelessly or
claiming more than they knew.

The governing law is that a proxy is wanted precisely where the quantity was not directly observed, so the
period of application is by definition a period containing no test, and the instrument's purpose and its
unverifiability are one property stated twice.

**Everything that can be done properly is done properly, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's
conduct.** The ring widths are measured to a hundredth of a millimetre and cross-dated to the year. The fit is
performed on one half of the instrumental period and verified on the other, which the model has never seen. The
uncertainties are propagated and printed, and the method is published beside the series. None of it touches the
difficulty, because the difficulty is not in the execution.

What removes any possibility of blame from the statistician is that holding data back is the discipline's answer
to fitting noise, works exactly as intended, and cannot leave the window. There is nowhere outside the
instrumental period to hold anything back to, so a relation that holds throughout that era and fails before it
passes every available test honestly, with the skill reported and nothing concealed.

What removes it from the reconstructor is that the published band covers every uncertainty that has a term in
the data, and whether the relation itself extends is a statement about the world rather than about the sample.
There is no quantity inside the sample that bears on it and therefore nothing to propagate, so the stated
uncertainty is complete and is silent about the premise the series rests on.

**And the critic is right and gains nothing by it, which is what keeps this out of a controversy.** The
objection is not that anybody cheated: the rings, the split and the bands are all conceded. It is that the
relation's applicability outside its window is what no test inside the window can establish. The same argument
that stops the series being established stops it being refuted, so both parties are left holding an assumption
neither can reach.

The discipline's own most uncomfortable result belongs here and is carried as the spine rather than as an
embarrassment. High-latitude tree-ring density series that tracked temperature for centuries stopped tracking
it from around the middle of the twentieth century, while the thermometers kept rising. Why is argued over and
may be local, recent and particular to certain species and latitudes. The shape is not arguable: **the one
stretch in which the stability of the relation could be examined against an instrument is the stretch where it
did not hold**, and the people who found it published it.

It is distinct from the house's **True Until It Was Used**, which the slate line required it to be tested
against before authoring, and the test was run in the sweep. That misfit runs on **use**: an estimated relation
summarises behaviour under an arrangement, and employing it to change the arrangement ends what it summarised,
so the invalidation is caused by an act and arrives afterwards. Remove that engine entirely and this stands:
nobody uses a reconstruction to change the climate of 1300, no act invalidates anything, and the question of
whether the relation extends exists whether or not the series is ever read. One misfit is about a relation
destroyed by being acted on; this one is about a relation that may simply not reach.

It is distinct from the house's **Overfitting**, where a model captures noise in its training data and fails out
of sample: that is the failure the held-back verification is designed to catch, and it catches it. Here the
model is not overfitted, the verification years are genuinely unseen, and the relation may hold across every
year any instrument covers and still not extend. The standard repair is applied in full and is aimed at a
different failure, which is why its success here is not a false comfort anybody manufactured.

It is distinct from the house's **The Streetlight Effect**, where a search is conducted where looking is easy
rather than where the thing is: there an elsewhere exists and is merely inconvenient, so the reproach is
available. Here there is no second overlap anywhere, there will not be one, and the location of the light is
fixed by when the thermometer was invented.

It is distinct from the house's **As Full As I Remember**, which holds shifting baselines and works the same
palaeo ground: there each generation's reference point is set by what it first encountered, so the **observer**
moves and a long series recovers the loss. Here nobody's memory is at issue and the long series is the object
under discussion, so the two misfits meet at the point where one's remedy is the other's problem.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast anybody as fitting
  carelessly or claiming more than they knew, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the
  rings are measured exactly and dated to the year; the instrumental record is the only direct measurement there
  is and its length is the age of the thermometer; the verification is conducted on genuinely unseen years and
  there is nowhere outside the window to conduct it; the published band covers every uncertainty with a term in
  the data; the divergence was found and published by the discipline itself; and the critic's objection is
  correct and leaves them no better placed. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so
  the misfit is that the period a proxy is for is the period containing no test of it.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Convergence across
  independent archives is the real answer, is genuine evidence against a shared error, and leaves every archive
  calibrated on the same short record while establishing consistency rather than correctness. Longer
  instrumental series would help and cannot be created backwards. Better statistics guard against overfitting,
  which is not the failure here. Understanding the divergence physically would be the decisive advance and is an
  open research question rather than a step anybody has skipped. Modelling the proxy mechanism from first
  principles reduces the reliance on the fit and introduces a model with its own unexamined premises. And
  knowing the whole structure, which every party does, leaves the reconstruction as the best available account
  of a period there is no other way to reach.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the divergence result, the calibration method, the uniformitarian
  premise, the institutional review, the multiproxy comparisons and the dispute are theirs. The bounds are
  carried with them: **the causes of the divergence are genuinely unsettled** and may be local, recent and
  specific to particular species and latitudes, which the play carries and does not resolve; the play stages the
  **structure**, that an instrument is testable only over a span it is not needed for, and makes no claim about
  the accuracy of any reconstruction, about the climate of any century, or about any party to any dispute over
  this literature; the tree-ring case is used because it is where the difficulty has been examined most
  explicitly and the same structure holds for any calibrated proxy; and treating calibration as one fit made
  once is the play's own simplification of methods that nest, pool and regionalise. **The play makes no claim
  that past temperatures are unknowable or that any published series is wrong**, only that the assumption
  joining the measured span to the reconstructed one is the single thing the method contains no way to examine.
