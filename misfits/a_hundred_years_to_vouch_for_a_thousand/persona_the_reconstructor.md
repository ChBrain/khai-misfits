# Persona: The Reconstructor

## Taxonomy

[Reconstructor](position_reconstructor.md). The one who publishes the long series, with the bands on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A fitted relation, a proxy network going back a millennium, and a chart with a widening grey band behind the line.

## Action

Applies the relation to the years before the thermometers.
Publishes the series, the method and the uncertainties together.

## Shadow

States every uncertainty that can be quantified, and the one that matters is not among them.
The bands widen backwards and honestly, covering the noise in the proxies, the sparseness of the network and the error in the fit. What they cannot cover is whether the relation itself holds back there, because that is not a wider version of a known error: it is a question the data contains no term for. So the published uncertainty is complete with respect to everything measurable and silent about the assumption the whole series rests on.

## Tell

Publishes error bars that are honest and are about the wrong thing.
