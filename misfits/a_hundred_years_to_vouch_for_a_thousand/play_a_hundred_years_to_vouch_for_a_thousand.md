# Play: A Hundred Years to Vouch for a Thousand

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

A Hundred Years to Vouch for a Thousand.
A tree ring records growth, and growth is not temperature, so the series has to be joined to a thermometer to mean anything. Thermometers go back about a century and a half. The reconstruction goes back a thousand years. The join can only be made, and can only be tested, on the span where both exist, which is exactly the span nobody needed a proxy for.

## Arc

The bend is this: the instrument's purpose is to reach where no direct measurement exists, and that is the same property as its being uncheckable there.
It runs on a measurement that is exact and unlabelled.
[The Proxy Exists to Reach Past the Thermometers](plot_the_proxy_exists_to_reach_past_the_thermometers.md). [The Ring Reader](persona_the_ring_reader.md) dates every ring to the year, and growth answers to temperature, water, light, nutrients and age together, so a relation from elsewhere is required.
And the only yardstick is short.
[And Can Only Be Checked Against Them](plot_and_can_only_be_checked_against_them.md). [The Instrumental Record](piece_the_instrumental_record.md) is what makes the method possible and confines its testing to the century and a half nobody needs a proxy for.
And the standard guard does not reach it.
[Splitting the Record Does Not Leave the Window](plot_splitting_the_record_does_not_leave_the_window.md). [The Statistician](persona_the_statistician.md) holds data back properly, and both halves are inside the window, so a relation that fails only before it passes every test honestly.
Then [The One Edge That Could Be Checked Came Apart](plot_the_one_edge_that_could_be_checked_came_apart.md).
[The Divergence](piece_the_divergence.md): high-latitude density series that tracked temperature for centuries stopped tracking it after about 1960, found and published by the discipline itself, at the single edge where the assumption was examinable.
And [stationarity is the assumption and is not in the data](plot_stationarity_is_the_assumption_and_is_not_in_the_data.md). The bands widen honestly over everything with a term in them, and whether the relation holds back there has no term, so [the critic](persona_the_critic.md) is right and gains nothing by it.
And [convergence is the real answer and is bounded](plot_convergence_is_the_real_answer_and_is_bounded.md). Independent archives with different physics broadly agree, which is genuine evidence, and every one of them was calibrated against the same short record.

## Stakes

Whether an instrument can be trusted in the only place it was built to be used.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a measurement that is exact and unlabelled, a yardstick that is indispensable and short, a guard that is correct and aimed elsewhere, an assumption examinable at one edge and failing there, an uncertainty honestly stated about everything except the premise, and a convergence that narrows the question without closing it, and away from any account in which somebody fitted carelessly or published more than they knew. The production cannot fault the statistician, because holding data back is the right method and there is nowhere outside the window to hold anything back to; and it cannot fault the reconstructor, because every uncertainty with a term in the data is quantified and printed. What is left to fight over is not whether the work was done properly but what to call a result whose central assumption is the one thing the method contains no way to examine.
