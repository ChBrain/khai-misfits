# Plot: The Proxy Exists to Reach Past the Thermometers

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A question about the climate of the year 1300, and no thermometer that old.

## Action

[Measuring the rings](process_measuring_the_rings.md): [the ring reader](persona_the_ring_reader.md), holding [Ring Reader](position_ring_reader.md), builds an exact dated chronology from [the long before](place_the_long_before.md).

## Stage

A real, precise measurement of growth, in units nobody asked for.

## Tension

The whole point of the instrument is to reach where no direct measurement exists, which is also what makes it uncheckable there.
A ring records growth, and growth answers to temperature, water, light, nutrients and the tree's own age together, so a relation from somewhere else is needed to turn the series into degrees. And the place a proxy is wanted is by definition the place the quantity was not observed: if it had been, nobody would need the proxy. So the instrument's purpose and its unverifiability are the same property, stated twice.
