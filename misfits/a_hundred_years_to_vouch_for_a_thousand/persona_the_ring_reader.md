# Persona: The Ring Reader

## Taxonomy

[Ring Reader](position_ring_reader.md). The one who gets a number out of wood.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A core the width of a pencil, a bench, a measuring stage, and a thousand years of summers in it.

## Action

Measures each ring to a hundredth of a millimetre.
Cross-dates against neighbouring trees until the sequence is certain.

## Shadow

Produces a real measurement of something and cannot say by itself what.
The widths are accurate, the dating is exact and the series is genuine: what a ring does not carry is a label saying which summer it was and how warm. It records growth, and growth answers to temperature, water, light, nutrients and the tree's own age together, so turning the series into a temperature requires a relation borrowed from somewhere else, and this seat has none to supply.

## Tell

Measures precisely, in units nobody wanted.
