# Persona: The Critic

## Taxonomy

[Critic](position_critic.md). The one who asks whether the relation extends.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The published series, the method appendix, and a single question about the years outside the fit.

## Action

Grants the measurement, the fit and the verification.
Asks what establishes that the same relation held in 1300.

## Shadow

Asks the one question the structure is built to withhold, and is owed an answer nobody can give.
The objection is not that anybody cheated, since the seat concedes the rings, the split and the bands. It is that the relation's applicability outside its window is exactly what no test inside the window can establish, and there is no other window. So this seat is correct, is unanswerable, and is also not entitled to conclude the reconstruction is wrong, since the same argument leaves that unestablished too.

## Tell

Is right about the gap, and cannot fill it either.
