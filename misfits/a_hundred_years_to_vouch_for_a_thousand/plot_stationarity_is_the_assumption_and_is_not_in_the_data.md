## Tension

The band covers the noise and not the premise, and no better statistics would change that.
Widening bands account for scatter in the proxies, the thinning of the network and the error in the fit, all of which are quantities the data contains. Whether the relation itself holds back there is a statement about the world rather than about the sample, so nothing inside the sample bears on it and there is no term to propagate. [The Critic](persona_the_critic.md) is therefore right and gains nothing by it: the same argument that stops the series being established stops it being refuted, and both parties are left with an assumption neither can reach.
