# Plot: Convergence Is the Real Answer and Is Bounded

## Taxonomy

[A Hundred Years to Vouch for a Thousand](play_a_hundred_years_to_vouch_for_a_thousand.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Ice cores, corals, lake sediments and boreholes, each recording by a different physics.

## Action

Independent reconstructions built on unrelated proxies are laid over one another at [the long before](place_the_long_before.md) and found broadly to agree.

## Stage

The strongest argument available, and it is a good one.

## Tension

Agreement between independent instruments is real evidence and is not the test that is missing.
If four archives with different physics tell the same story, a shared error is less likely, and this is the discipline's genuine answer rather than a rhetorical one. It is also bounded in two ways that keep the difficulty open: each of them was calibrated against the same short instrumental record, so their independence is in the physics and not in the yardstick; and agreement establishes that they are consistent with one another rather than that any of them is right. So the best available answer narrows the question and does not close it, which is where the honest account stops.
