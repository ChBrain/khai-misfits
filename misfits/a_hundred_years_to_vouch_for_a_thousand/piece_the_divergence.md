## Load Bearing

It is the only place the assumption could be tested at all, and there it failed.
Tree-ring density series at high latitudes tracked temperature for centuries and then, from around the middle of the twentieth century, stopped tracking it, while the thermometers kept rising. The reason is argued over and may be local, recent and specific. What is not arguable is the shape of the finding: the single stretch where the stability of the relation could be examined against an instrument is the stretch where it did not hold, and the discipline found this itself and published it.

## Apparent
