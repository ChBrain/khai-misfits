# Persona: The Meteorologist

## Taxonomy

[Meteorologist](position_meteorologist.md). The one holding the only yardstick, and it is short.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A station register in a Stevenson screen, kept daily, going back to about the middle of the nineteenth century.

## Action

Reads the thermometer at the same hour every day.
Files it.

## Shadow

Holds the only direct measurement there is and cannot lend it backwards.
The instrumental record is what makes the whole enterprise possible: it is the one place a proxy can be compared with the quantity it is supposed to stand for. It is also about a century and a half long, which is not a shortcoming of this seat's diligence but the age of the instrument. So the yardstick exists, is excellent, and covers a tiny fraction of what wants measuring.

## Tell

Supplies the only test available, over the period the answer is not wanted for.
