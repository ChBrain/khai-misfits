# Plot: Both at the Door

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), [The Supply](persona_the_supply.md) and [The Demand](persona_the_demand.md) stand at the doorway, both looking in at the same empty floor, each waiting for the other to enter first.

## Action

[The Supply](persona_the_supply.md), holding [position supply](position_supply.md), runs [Withhold Until Wanted](plan_withhold_until_wanted.md); [The Demand](persona_the_demand.md), holding [position demand](position_demand.md), runs [Come When It's Worth It](plan_come_when_its_worth_it.md); both through [Waiting](process_waiting.md), each enacting [The Wait](piece_the_wait.md). The seller will not stock a market with no buyers in it, the buyer will not visit one with no sellers in it, and both are reading the identical, identically empty floor. Closes with each side politely, rationally, saying after you, and neither one going.

## Stage

Each side's wait is the correct reading of present value, since the platform is worth little to either side until the other is there (Caillaud and Jullien). This is not The Stag Hunt, where a single population fears a same-side defector: here two distinct sides each wait on the other, joined by a cross-side externality that a same-choice game has no analogue for.

## Tension

Two correct decisions, each right taken alone, composing into a door that no one walks through.
