# Piece: The Subsidy

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The remedy and its price: divide-and-conquer pricing that subsidises one side to build it and recoups from the other, plus the seeding and the marquee participant that carry it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), where [the Platform](persona_the_platform.md) sets the split: how much one side pays, how much the other is paid, and what it costs to hold that split until the floor fills.

## Load Bearing

The platform changes not the total price but its STRUCTURE, the split between the sides (Rochet and Tirole): cut one side below cost, even to zero or negative, to draw it in first, and recoup from the other side once the cross-side value exists to justify the charge (Caillaud and Jullien, the divide-and-conquer remedy). Guard against reading this as a costless trick or mere marketing flourish: the subsidy is a real cost carried against an uncertain launch, it can be spent on the wrong side (subsidising a side whose presence does not pull the other), and it can be taken by users who accept the inducement, extract it, and do not stay once it lifts, leaving the ledger spent and the floor still short.

## Apparent

Just a launch promotion. Free credits, a discount, an ordinary loss-leader.
