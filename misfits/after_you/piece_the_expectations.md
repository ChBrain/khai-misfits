# Piece: The Expectations

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The selector between the two outcomes: each side's belief about whether the other will come, self-fulfilling either way.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), carried by each side at the door, not written on [the Floor](place_the_floor.md) or in any contract but held privately by each side as it decides whether to step across.

## Load Bearing

THIS is the hedge (Caillaud and Jullien; Katz and Shapiro): the cold start is one of two equilibria, and expectations select between them. A side that believes the other will come, comes; a side that believes the other will stay away, stays away; both beliefs are self-fulfilling, and nothing about the platform's design or the sides' underlying wants has to change for the outcome to flip from empty to full. Guard against reading the failure to launch as a fate or a structural impossibility: it is a coordination problem with two solutions, and a shift in what the two sides expect, nothing more, can move the identical platform from [the Empty Platform](piece_the_empty_platform.md) to the full one.

## Apparent

Mere optimism or pessimism. Sentiment, easy to dismiss as mood rather than mechanism.
