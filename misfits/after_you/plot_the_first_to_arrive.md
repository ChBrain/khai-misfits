# Plot: The First to Arrive

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Floor](place_the_floor.md), one participant steps onto the empty floor before the other side has arrived, the room still short one side.

## Action

[The Marquee](persona_the_marquee.md), from [the marquee's seat](position_marquee.md), runs [Go First](plan_go_first.md) through [Tipping](process_tipping.md), its early presence shifting [The Expectations](piece_the_expectations.md). Seeded, subsidised, or courted, it commits before the other side arrives and absorbs the risk of standing alone on an empty floor, giving the waiting side a reason to believe the floor will fill. Closes with one side present, and the other side's belief beginning to turn.

## Stage

A first arrival, marquee or seeded, and self-fulfilling expectations: each side comes because it now believes the other will (Caillaud and Jullien; Katz and Shapiro).

## Tension

Someone must bear the cost of arriving to an empty room, and only then can the belief that fills it begin to turn.
