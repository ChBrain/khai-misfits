# Persona: The Marquee

## Taxonomy

[Marquee](position_marquee.md). The first to arrive, an anchor participant seeded, subsidised, or courted, whose committing before the other side is there absorbs the risk of the empty room and turns the other side's belief, tipping the platform toward full.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Present, credible, exposed.
Steps onto the floor while it is still empty on the other side, a marquee seller whose name is known, a first cohort seeded to make the room look alive, and its arrival is the one thing the waiting sides are waiting for.
No recklessness in it and no charity: it is usually paid to be first, subsidised or courted, because being first to an empty room is a real cost, and it takes that cost in exchange for the position its early presence buys.

## Action

Goes first on [Go First](plan_go_first.md), through [Tipping](process_tipping.md) at [the Floor](place_the_floor.md), shifting [the Expectations](piece_the_expectations.md), committing before the other side arrives so that the other side now has a reason to believe the floor will fill.
Cannot tip the platform by argument, only by presence: its being credibly there is what changes the other side's belief, and once one side is real the other's wait has nothing left to wait for, so the cross-side pull begins to reinforce itself.
Does what breaks the standstill: it bears the emptiness the two waiting sides would not, and its bearing it is what lets the belief that fills the room begin.

## Shadow

Not a hero for going first and not sure to be rewarded: the Marquee breaks the deadlock only by taking the very cost the two sides were right to avoid, so its arrival is a paid risk and not a triumph, and whether the room fills behind it depends on whether the other side's belief truly turns.
Because only a credible first presence shifts expectations, the platform must find and fund it, so the figure that opens the market is the figure that steps into the empty room for a price, and if the belief does not catch, the cost is spent and the floor is still bare.
It is the one that carries the launch across the gap the waiting could not, and the one that pays, at risk, to carry it.

## Tell

Is present where the two sides are absent, and is taken for a natural first mover when it was usually seeded or paid to be one.
Cannot guarantee the tip, because its presence buys a belief and not a certainty, so it stands on the empty floor as the credible first arrival, and the room fills behind it only if the other side comes to believe what its being there suggests.
