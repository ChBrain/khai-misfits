# Process: Stalling

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). Borne at [The Floor](place_the_floor.md), precipitated by [The Supply](persona_the_supply.md) and [The Demand](persona_the_demand.md) both waiting, in [Nobody Comes](plot_nobody_comes.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The two rational waits composing, each side's separate hold becoming, together, a single standstill.
No third party pushes the floor into silence. It sits idle because both waits are correct at once and neither breaks on its own.

## Direction

From a platform that could serve everyone toward one that never launches.
The floor stays exactly as ready as it was built, but readiness curdles into vacancy the longer both sides decline to be first.

## Lever

The bad equilibrium of the coordination problem, the cross-side value never forming because neither side will go first (Caillaud and Jullien).
This is one of two equilibria the same platform could reach, not a fate written into it: the identical floor, under different expectations, fills. Stalling is what the wait becomes when nothing intervenes to change what either side expects of the other.
