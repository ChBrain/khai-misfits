# Process: Waiting

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). Borne at [The Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), precipitated by [The Supply](persona_the_supply.md) and [The Demand](persona_the_demand.md) withholding [The Wait](piece_the_wait.md), in [Both at the Door](plot_both_at_the_door.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Each side facing a platform still empty on the other side.
Supply looks in and sees no demand, demand looks in and sees no supply, and each reads the same empty floor as the same reason to hold.

## Direction

From a decision to join toward a decision to hold back, on both sides at once.
Nothing forces the sequence: waiting is not one side stalling the other, it is both sides arriving at the same conclusion independently and simultaneously.

## Lever

The cross-side externality, since the platform is genuinely worth little to one side until the other is present, so waiting is the correct reading of present value (Caillaud and Jullien).
No side is wrong to wait. The floor really is worth close to nothing to a seller with no buyers on it, and close to nothing to a buyer with no sellers on it, so the wait is not error but arithmetic.
