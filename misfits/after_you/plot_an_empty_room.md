# Plot: An Empty Room

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Floor](place_the_floor.md), a platform stands ready: a venue built with room for both sides, valuable once full, and, for now, completely empty.

## Action

[The Platform](persona_the_platform.md), from [the platform's seat](position_platform.md), has built a meeting-ground bound by [The Cross-Side Pull](piece_the_cross_side_pull.md): worth everything once both sides are on it, worth nothing while it stands empty. The stalls are laid, the aisles are clear, the floor is finished and open for business. Closes with the room ready and no one in it, the value real and entirely unrealised.

## Stage

A two-sided market is valuable only when both distinct sides are present, its worth held entirely in the meeting it would host, not in either side alone (Rochet and Tirole). The platform has done everything a platform can do alone; what remains is not more building but an arrival neither side owns.

## Tension

A place worth everything when full and nothing when empty, waiting on an arrival no one here will make first.
