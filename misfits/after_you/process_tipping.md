# Process: Tipping

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). Borne at [The Floor](place_the_floor.md), precipitated by [The Marquee](persona_the_marquee.md) going first and [The Expectations](piece_the_expectations.md) shifting, in [The First to Arrive](plot_the_first_to_arrive.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A first side arriving, seeded or marquee, and the belief that follows it that the other side will come too.
The marquee crosses the threshold onto the floor. What changes is not just the floor's occupancy but what the waiting side now expects of it.

## Direction

From the empty equilibrium toward the full one, the cross-side pull turning self-reinforcing.
Each arrival on one side makes the floor more worth crossing for the other, and each arrival that draws makes the next arrival easier still, the same pull that held the floor empty now filling it.

## Lever

Self-fulfilling expectations, each side coming because it now believes the other will (Caillaud and Jullien; Katz and Shapiro).
This is the good equilibrium of the identical coordination problem that produced the stall: the hedge carried from the start, that the failure to launch was never a fate but one of two outcomes, and that a changed expectation is enough to reach the other.
