# Position: Supply

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The seat of the offering side, the sellers and drivers and hosts, willing to serve but worth nothing to a platform without buyers, so it waits at the door for the other side to arrive first.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A readiness to serve, and no reason to serve yet: the goods, the routes, the rooms it would bring to the platform the moment there were buyers for them.
A correct reading of the empty floor: because a stall earns only from buyers, stocking one where there are none is a cost for no return, so waiting is the sound response and not a failure of enterprise.
No power to summon the other side by arriving, because buyers come for goods only when there are buyers enough to matter to no seller, so its own presence draws none of the demand it needs.

## Orders

Withhold until wanted.
Do not stock a market with no buyers in it: hold the goods, keep the routes idle, wait at the threshold until the demand is there to be served, because coming first is paying now for a value that does not yet exist.
Accept the mirror: that the buyers are reasoning exactly the same way about you, so your sound wait meets their sound wait and the market neither of you refuses is the market neither of you begins.

## Loses

The trade it would gladly have done, foregone to avoid a cost.
Because the value of joining is the buyers' presence and its own arrival cannot create that, the seat cannot serve its way out of the standstill, so it keeps its goods unsold and loses the whole trade the full platform would have carried.
It loses nothing by waiting that it could have kept by coming, and takes half the market's failure with it, the launch unmade because it, correctly, would not be first.

## Drives

Toward the side most willing to serve and most surely kept from serving,
holding back soundly because an empty market is a real cost and its own arrival would not fill it,
so the seat that would have stocked every stall the moment there were buyers is the seat whose wait for buyers helps ensure there are none.
