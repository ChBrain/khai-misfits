# Plan: Come When It's Worth It

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the demand's seat](position_demand.md), inside [After You](play_after_you.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Demand](position_demand.md)

## Direction

Do not cross to a market with no sellers in it. Hold off, and wait at the threshold until there is something to find, because a visit is worth the trip only if there are goods to buy, and crossing to a market with none is effort for nothing. Arriving first spends effort on a value that does not yet exist, and your own arrival cannot summon the sellers, so waiting is the sound reading of the empty floor and not a failure of appetite.

## Orders

([The Demand](persona_the_demand.md) enacts [the Wait](piece_the_wait.md) through [Waiting](process_waiting.md) at [the Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), declining to visit a market with no sellers, so its custom stays held until the supply it needs is there.)

## Implementation

([Waiting](process_waiting.md) holds the Demand at [the Threshold](place_the_threshold.md) while the other side is absent; because [the Cross-Side Pull](piece_the_cross_side_pull.md) puts its value in the sellers' presence and not its own, its arrival cannot create the supply it waits for, so its sound wait meets the Supply's and composes into [the Empty Platform](piece_the_empty_platform.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the custom stays held while the floor is empty of sellers, the wait read as sound and not fickle
- [ ] the seat does not try to summon the sellers by arriving, since its own presence draws none
- [ ] its wait meets the Supply's identical wait, the mirror acknowledged
- [ ] the exchange it would gladly have made is foregone, the other half of the standstill authored by no one and correct on its own terms
