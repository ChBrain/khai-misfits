# Position: Demand

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The seat of the seeking side, the buyers and riders and guests, willing to come but with nothing to find on a platform without sellers, so it waits at the door for the sellers to arrive first.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An appetite to buy, and nothing yet to buy: the custom it would bring to the platform the moment there were sellers to serve it.
A correct reading of the empty floor: because a visit is worth the trip only if there are goods to find, crossing to a market with none is effort for nothing, so waiting is the sound response and not a failure of appetite.
No power to summon the other side by arriving, because sellers stock only where there are buyers enough to earn from, and one early buyer is not that, so its own presence draws none of the supply it needs.

## Orders

Come when it's worth it.
Do not cross to a market with no sellers in it: hold off, wait at the threshold until there is something to find, because arriving first is spending effort on a value that does not yet exist.
Accept the mirror: that the sellers are reasoning exactly the same way about you, so your sound wait meets their sound wait and the market neither of you refuses is the market neither of you begins.

## Loses

The custom it would gladly have brought, foregone to avoid a wasted trip.
Because the value of joining is the sellers' presence and its own arrival cannot create that, the seat cannot buy its way out of the standstill, so it keeps its custom and loses the whole exchange the full platform would have carried.
It loses nothing by waiting that it could have kept by coming, and takes the other half of the market's failure with it, the launch unmade because it, correctly, would not be first.

## Drives

Toward the side most ready to buy and most surely kept from buying,
holding off soundly because an empty market offers nothing and its own arrival would not stock it,
so the seat that would have filled every aisle the moment there were sellers is the seat whose wait for sellers helps ensure there are none.
