# Plan: Split the Price

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the platform's seat](position_platform.md), inside [After You](play_after_you.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Platform](position_platform.md)

## Direction

Move the price structure, not the level. The standstill cannot be waited out, because waiting is what both sides are doing, so use the one lever you hold: the split of the charge between the sides. Subsidise the side cheaper to attract and more wanted by the other, cut its price below cost to build it first, and recoup from the other side once that side has a reason to come (Rochet and Tirole; Caillaud and Jullien). Carry the cost forward knowing it buys an uncertain launch, can land on the wrong side, and can be taken by users who do not stay, and pay it anyway, because a standstill left alone is a market that never opens.

## Orders

([The Platform](persona_the_platform.md) takes [the Subsidy](piece_the_subsidy.md) through [Seeding](process_seeding.md) at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), cutting one side's charge below cost to build it and raising the other's to recoup, moving the split and not the total.)

## Implementation

([Seeding](process_seeding.md) bends the charge at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md) from a symmetric price toward an asymmetric one; the subsidised side, built first, gives the other side a reason to come, so the divide-and-conquer split works on [the Cross-Side Pull](piece_the_cross_side_pull.md), a real cost spent forward against a launch that may still not catch.)

## Targets

- [ ] the structure is moved, not the level, one side subsidised below cost and the other charged to recoup
- [ ] the subsidised side is the one cheaper to attract and more wanted by the other
- [ ] the cost is carried forward knowingly, against an uncertain launch and the risk of the wrong side or a non-staying user
- [ ] the standstill is not merely waited out, since waiting is what keeps the market shut
