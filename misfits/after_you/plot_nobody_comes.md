# Plot: Nobody Comes

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Floor](place_the_floor.md), time passes and the room stays empty, the stalls still laid, the aisles still clear, no one crossing the sill.

## Action

Through [Stalling](process_stalling.md), the two waits compose, and [The Empty Platform](piece_the_empty_platform.md) is the result: the cross-side value never forms, and a platform that would have served everyone fails to launch. No third party pushes the floor into silence; it sits idle because both waits are correct at once and neither breaks on its own. Closes with the venture written off as one nobody wanted, when the truth is that no one would be first.

## Stage

The loss is authored by no one, neither side wrong to wait, and nothing scarce but the coordination itself (Caillaud and Jullien). Distinguish lightly from Path Dependence: nothing has locked in here, no worse standard has formed and held; the right thing simply never started, a failure to launch rather than a failure to unwind.

## Tension

A real value lost with no culprit, a launch failing not for want of demand but for want of a first mover.
