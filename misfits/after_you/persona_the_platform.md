# Persona: The Platform

## Taxonomy

[Platform](position_platform.md). The intermediary that would join the two sides and holds the one real lever, the price structure, watching its ready floor stay empty because neither side will come first, and paying to seed one side to draw the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Built, ready, and stuck.
Has raised the whole meeting-ground, swept the stalls and opened the doors, and stands to earn everything once both sides are on it and nothing while they are not.
No fault in its building and no lack in its offer: the platform is good, the value real, and its only problem is that the two sides it would serve are each waiting for the other to arrive.

## Action

Splits the price on [Split the Price](plan_split_the_price.md), through [Seeding](process_seeding.md) at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), taking [the Subsidy](piece_the_subsidy.md), cutting one side's charge below cost to build it and raising the other's to recoup once that side has a reason to come.
Cannot simply wait the standstill out, because waiting is exactly what both sides are doing, so it must move the price structure it alone controls, the split between the sides and not the total, to make one side's arrival worth its while (Rochet and Tirole).
Does what an intermediary must to open a two-sided market: it pays to bring one side first, and carries that cost against a launch that is still uncertain.

## Shadow

Not a failure for the empty floor and not saved by the subsidy: the Platform built something genuinely valuable, and its emptiness is not a flaw in the building but a coordination its two sides cannot reach alone, so the intermediary with the best venue can still be the one that cannot open it.
Because the only lever it holds is the price structure, its remedy is a real cost spent against an uncertain launch, able to be laid on the wrong side or taken by users who do not stay, so the seat that would profit most from the meeting is the seat that must pay, at its own risk, to make the meeting begin.
It is the one whose platform would serve everyone, and the one that must buy the first move that neither side will make.

## Tell

Says the venue is ready and the value is plain, and finds that ready and plain are not enough, because a meeting-place is worth nothing until the meeting happens.
Reaches for the only tool it has, the split of the price, and spends it forward into an uncertain launch, knowing the subsidy is real money against a coordination that may still not hold.
