# Position: Platform

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The seat of the intermediary that would join the two sides, holding one real lever, the price structure, its ready floor empty because neither side will come first, and paying to seed one side to draw the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A meeting-ground worth everything full and nothing empty: the whole venue built, the stalls swept, the doors open, earning only once both sides are on it.
One real lever, the price structure: not the total charge but its split between the sides, the freedom to cut one side's price below cost and raise the other's, which is the platform's only means of moving a standstill it cannot wait out (Rochet and Tirole).
No power to command either side to come, because each side's arrival waits on the other's, so the seat that would profit from the meeting cannot simply order it to happen.

## Orders

Split the price.
Move the structure, not the level: subsidise the side that is cheaper to attract and whose presence the other most wants, build it first, and recoup from the other side once it has a reason to come (Caillaud and Jullien).
Carry the cost forward: know that the subsidy is real money spent against an uncertain launch, and that it can land on the wrong side or be taken by users who do not stay, and pay it anyway, because a standstill waited out is a market that never opens.

## Loses

The launch it built for, unless it pays to open it, and the payment itself if the launch does not catch.
Because the only lever it holds is the price structure, the seat cannot open the market for free, so it trades a real subsidy against an uncertain coordination and may lose both the subsidy and the launch if belief does not turn.
It keeps the venue and loses the certainty, the seat that would earn most from the meeting the seat that must fund, at its own risk, the first move neither side will make.

## Drives

Toward the intermediary whose platform would serve everyone and cannot open itself,
holding the one lever that can move the standstill and paying, forward and at risk, to move it,
so the seat that stands to gain the whole value of the meeting is the seat that must buy the beginning the two sides will not.
