# Plot: Splitting the Bill

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), [The Platform](persona_the_platform.md) reworks its books, the charge to each side written out separately, column by column.

## Action

[The Platform](persona_the_platform.md), from [the platform's seat](position_platform.md), runs [Split the Price](plan_split_the_price.md) through [Seeding](process_seeding.md), taking [The Subsidy](piece_the_subsidy.md): cutting one side's price below cost to build it, raising the other's to recoup once that side has a reason to come. Closes with the price structure bent, one side subsidised, the launch bought but not yet secured.

## Stage

The platform's lever is the price structure, not the level, divide-and-conquer pricing that subsidises one side to draw the other (Rochet and Tirole; Caillaud and Jullien). This is a real cost carried against an uncertain launch: it can be spent on the wrong side, or taken by a side that extracts the discount and does not stay once it lifts.

## Tension

The deadlock can be paid open, but the payment is real and the launch, even funded, is not yet secured.
