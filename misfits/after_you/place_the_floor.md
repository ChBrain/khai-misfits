# Place: The Floor

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The meeting-ground where the two sides would come together, valuable once full and empty because neither side comes first. Hosts [An Empty Room](plot_an_empty_room.md), [Nobody Comes](plot_nobody_comes.md), [The First to Arrive](plot_the_first_to_arrive.md), [The Room Fills](plot_the_room_fills.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A ready venue, built with room enough for both sides, standing empty.
The stalls are laid, the aisles are clear, the floor is finished and open for business, and no one is on it.

## Holds

The pull a full floor would exert, and the emptiness it exerts none of yet.
Here waits [the cross-side pull](piece_the_cross_side_pull.md) that would make the floor worth crossing once it is full, and here stands [the empty platform](piece_the_empty_platform.md) it remains while it is not, the same floor holding both its promise and its present state.

## Offers

To whoever steps onto it, the whole value of the meeting, conditional on the other side stepping on too.
The floor offers no partial worth: it is either the place where both sides transact or the place where one side stands alone, and which of the two it is depends on who else comes.

## Withheld

Any worth at all to a side that arrives while the other side is still absent.
A seller alone on the floor sells to no one, a buyer alone finds nothing to buy, so the floor withholds the very thing it was built to offer from the side that gets there first and finds it still empty.
