# Pitch: You First

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reasonable, patient, in good faith.
Two sides who would both gladly join a marketplace, and a platform that would serve them both well, nothing missing but the meeting, and no one refusing out of spite.
Nothing false in it: the market is real, the value is real, and each side declining to be first is reading the empty floor exactly right.

## Undertow

Under the good faith, a value that exists only once both sides are present.
Each side's benefit from joining is the other side's presence, a cross-side pull, so the first to arrive pays now for a worth that does not yet exist, and no one wants to be first into an empty room (Rochet and Tirole).
No stubbornness and no blunder: each side's wait is the correct reading of what the platform is worth today, and the two correct waits compose into a standstill, a chicken and egg with no first term (Caillaud and Jullien).

## Nerve

The plainness of two sides who would both come gladly, and the catch that neither will come first:
that the value of joining is the other side's presence, and the other side is not yet there, the worth and the emptiness the one cross-side pull seen from either door,
so the market that would serve everyone is written off as one nobody wanted, when the only thing missing was the coordination to bring both sides through the door at once.

## Echo

After the run: the market that never opened, the value real and unrealised, a launch that failed not for want of demand but for want of a first mover, a loss authored by no one and mistaken for a lack of interest (Caillaud and Jullien).
And the levers that open it, each at a price: subsidise one side below cost to build it and recoup from the other, court a marquee whose presence draws the rest, and above all shift what each side expects the other to do, each a real cost against an uncertain launch.
And the escape held in view, because the empty market is one of two outcomes and not a fate: the same platform fills or stays empty by what its two sides expect, self-fulfilling either way (Katz and Shapiro), so the whole difference between the full room and the empty one is a coordination bought at a price and never assured.
