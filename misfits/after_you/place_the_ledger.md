# Place: The Ledger

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). Where the platform sets the price structure, subsidising one side and charging the other. Hosts [Splitting the Bill](plot_splitting_the_bill.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The platform's books, the charge to each side written separately in its own column.
One side's price is cut below cost, the other's raised to recoup it, the same total split two different ways down two different rows.

## Holds

The lever that moves the split without moving the sum.
Here sits [the subsidy](piece_the_subsidy.md), the divide-and-conquer instrument (Rochet and Tirole; Caillaud and Jullien): not a discount for its own sake but a price structure built to attract one side first so the other follows.

## Offers

To the platform, the power to change not the total price but its structure, to build one side and draw the other.
The ledger lets the platform move value from column to column, spending on the side it needs to seed to earn back on the side that will pay once the other is present.

## Withheld

Any guarantee the subsidy lands.
The ledger records the charge, not its outcome: the subsidised side may take the discount and never return, or the wrong side may be chosen, so the ledger withholds certainty that the spend converts to the launch it was written to buy.
