# Plan: Go First

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the marquee's seat](position_marquee.md), inside [After You](play_after_you.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Marquee](position_marquee.md)

## Direction

Commit to the empty floor before the other side arrives. Be present, credibly and visibly, a name known or a cohort large enough to make the room look alive, so that the other side comes to believe the platform will fill and its wait has nothing left to wait for. Take the inducement that makes bearing the empty room worthwhile, since being first earns nothing on its own, and know the limit: your presence buys a belief and not a certainty, so the room fills behind you only if the other side's expectation truly turns.

## Orders

([The Marquee](persona_the_marquee.md) shifts [the Expectations](piece_the_expectations.md) through [Tipping](process_tipping.md) at [the Floor](place_the_floor.md), committing before the other side is there so the other side has a reason to believe the floor will fill.)

## Implementation

([Tipping](process_tipping.md) begins when the Marquee is credibly present at [the Floor](place_the_floor.md); its arrival turns [the Expectations](piece_the_expectations.md) the other side held, and once one side is real [the Cross-Side Pull](piece_the_cross_side_pull.md) reinforces itself, drawing the other side in, the belief self-fulfilling but bought and never assured, so the empty equilibrium gives way to the full one only if the turn catches.)

## Targets

- [ ] the seat is credibly present on the empty floor before the other side arrives
- [ ] the inducement to bear the empty room is taken, the cost of going first acknowledged
- [ ] the other side's expectation is turned, the cross-side pull beginning to reinforce itself
- [ ] the limit is held: presence buys belief and not certainty, so the room fills only if the turn catches, else the cost is spent and the floor still bare
