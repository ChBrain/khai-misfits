# Plot: The Room Fills

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Floor](place_the_floor.md), the first side present, the second beginning to arrive, the floor no longer reading as empty to whoever looks in from the threshold.

## Action

Through [Tipping](process_tipping.md), [The Expectations](piece_the_expectations.md) flip: the cross-side pull that once held both sides back turns self-reinforcing, and both sides come until the platform tips full. Closes with the room full, the same venue that stood empty now worth everything, the outcome having turned entirely on belief.

## Stage

The failure to launch was one of two equilibria, not a fate, and expectations selected between them; the good outcome was reachable all along, at the cost of the subsidy and the coordination that shifted belief (Caillaud and Jullien; Katz and Shapiro).

## Tension

The same platform, empty or full by what its two sides expected, the whole difference a coordination bought at a price and never guaranteed.
