# Piece: The Wait

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The rational withholding: each side declining to join first, because a platform empty on the other side is genuinely worth little.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the threshold](place_the_threshold.md), where each side stands and holds back, neither stepping onto [the floor](place_the_floor.md) first, each watching the door for the other.

## Load Bearing

The wait is RATIONAL, not stubborn (Caillaud and Jullien): joining an empty platform first means paying a cost now (attention, money, switching) for a cross-side value that does not yet exist, since that value depends on the other side's presence (the pull named in [the Cross-Side Pull](piece_the_cross_side_pull.md)). Each side's present-value reading is correct in isolation. The trap is that both readings are correct at once and compose into a standstill neither side authored. Guard against reading the waiting side as short-sighted, timid, or simply unmotivated: going first is the actual mistake under the present valuation, waiting is the correct move, and it is exactly two correct waits, not two errors, that produce the deadlock.

## Apparent

Ordinary caution. A party sensibly declining a bad deal, the same prudence anyone would praise in any other market.
