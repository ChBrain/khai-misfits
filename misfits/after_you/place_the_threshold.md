# Place: The Threshold

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The doorway where each side stands deciding whether to enter, looking in at the empty floor. Hosts [Both at the Door](plot_both_at_the_door.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The two sides at the door, each looking in, each waiting for the other to enter first.
Neither has crossed the sill. Both can see the same empty floor through the same open door, and both read it the same way.

## Holds

The rational withholding, and the belief each side carries about the other's move.
Here sits [the wait](piece_the_wait.md), the correct reading of a floor not yet worth crossing, and here sits [the expectations](piece_the_expectations.md) each side holds about whether the other will come, the belief that will decide, later, which way this resolves.

## Offers

To each side, the plain choice: enter first, or hold at the door.
The threshold puts the decision squarely on whoever stands at it, and asks nothing else of either side but to choose.

## Withheld

Any reason to enter first.
The floor beyond the door is worth nothing to one side until the other is on it, so the threshold withholds the incentive that would break the standoff, leaving each side with a correct reason to wait and no reason at all to go.
