# Piece: The Two Sides

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The two distinct populations a platform must join, each useless to the platform without the other: buyers and sellers, riders and drivers, cardholders and merchants.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the threshold](place_the_threshold.md), where both stand deciding whether to step onto the platform first. They are pointed at [the floor](place_the_floor.md), the ground they would meet on if both crossed, but the deciding happens at the threshold, before either crosses.

## Load Bearing

Two DISTINCT populations (Rochet and Tirole), not one group choosing a common action: a rider is not a driver, a cardholder is not a merchant, and each is joined to the other side only by its need of it. Their separateness is not incidental. It is what lets a platform charge one side and subsidise the other, an asymmetry with no meaning inside a single crowd. Guard against reading the two sides as one market, one undifferentiated set of participants deciding together, since that reading erases the very asymmetry that makes the coordination hard and the pricing structure (Rochet and Tirole) meaningful.

## Apparent

Just a buyer and a seller. An ordinary market, waiting to happen the way markets always do, one party wanting to sell, another wanting to buy, needing only to find each other.

## Yearbook

Bound to each other by [the cross-side pull](piece_the_cross_side_pull.md), each side enacting [the wait](piece_the_wait.md) in its own right. Embodied by [the Supply](persona_the_supply.md) and [the Demand](persona_the_demand.md), holding [position supply](position_supply.md) and [position demand](position_demand.md) respectively, in [Both at the Door](plot_both_at_the_door.md).
