# Process: Seeding

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). Borne at [The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), precipitated by [The Platform](persona_the_platform.md) splitting the price and taking [The Subsidy](piece_the_subsidy.md), in [Splitting the Bill](plot_splitting_the_bill.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The platform choosing to move the price structure rather than wait for the standstill to break on its own.
Holding the pricing lever, the platform reads the stall at the floor and reaches for the one thing in its power to change: not the total charge, but how it falls.

## Direction

From a symmetric charge toward an asymmetric one, one side subsidised below cost and the other charged to recoup it.
The total the platform needs to clear does not move; only the split does, drawn away from the side it wants to build and pushed onto the side it expects to pay once the first is present.

## Lever

Divide-and-conquer pricing, the price structure and not the level, the whole task of a two-sided platform (Rochet and Tirole; Caillaud and Jullien).
This is a real cost carried against an uncertain launch. The subsidy can be spent and the side it courts can still fail to convert into a floor the other side finds worth entering, so seeding risks its own spend without guaranteeing the tip.
