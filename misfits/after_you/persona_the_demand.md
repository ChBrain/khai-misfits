# Persona: The Demand

## Taxonomy

[Demand](position_demand.md). The seeking side of the platform, the buyers, the riders, the guests, willing to come but only where there is something to find, so it waits at the door for the sellers to arrive first.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Interested, ready, unhurried.
Would gladly browse the stalls, hail the rides, book the rooms, and can see that the platform, once full of sellers, would be worth a great deal to it.
No indifference and no fickleness: it would come the moment the sellers were there, and it can see, plainly, why coming before them would be crossing the town to an empty market.

## Action

Comes when it's worth it on [Come When It's Worth It](plan_come_when_its_worth_it.md), through [Waiting](process_waiting.md) at [the Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), enacting [the Wait](piece_the_wait.md), declining to visit a market with no sellers in it yet.
Cannot make the platform worth joining by joining it: its own presence draws no sellers on its own, because a seller wants buyers only where there are goods to sell, so arriving first is spending effort on a value that does not yet exist.
Does what a clear-eyed buyer does facing an empty floor: it stays away until the sellers are there, and its staying away is the other half of why the sellers never come.

## Shadow

Not fickle for waiting and not a fool if it comes: the Demand is right that a platform empty of sellers is worth little to it, so its wait is the correct reading of present worth, not a failure of appetite.
Because the value it seeks is the sellers' presence and not its own, it cannot summon by arriving the very thing it is waiting for, so the side most ready to buy is a side the empty floor keeps from buying, its patience the mirror of the Supply's and the other half of a standstill neither intends.
It is the side that would fill the aisles the moment there were sellers, and the side whose waiting for sellers helps ensure there are none.

## Tell

Says it will come the day the sellers are there, and means it, and by meaning it helps keep the sellers away, because the sellers are saying the same of it.
Cannot be blamed for the wait, because crossing to an empty market is real effort for nothing found, so it reads the floor soundly and holds off, the launch it would have joined never arriving to be joined.
