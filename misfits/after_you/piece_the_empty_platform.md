# Piece: The Empty Platform

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The bad equilibrium: the cross-side value never forming, a platform that would have served everyone failing to launch, a loss authored by no one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Floor](place_the_floor.md), standing empty: built, priced, open, and unvisited, the ground where both sides would have met had either crossed first.

## Load Bearing

This is the launch that never happens (Caillaud and Jullien): a real loss, with no culprit, because neither side was wrong to wait, and nothing scarce but the coordination itself. Guard against two misreadings. First, against reading it as a lack of demand or a failed product: the demand on both sides is real, and the value would exist the moment both sides were present; nothing but the timing coordination is missing. Second, against reading it as final or inevitable: the Empty Platform is ONE OF TWO EQUILIBRIA the same platform can settle into, not a fate. The other, full equilibrium is reachable by the same structure under different expectations.

## Apparent

A failed venture. Easily mistaken, by anyone looking only at the empty floor, for a platform nobody wanted.

## Yearbook

Formed from [the Wait](piece_the_wait.md) through [Stalling](process_stalling.md), as both sides that hold [position platform](position_platform.md) confront hold [position supply](position_supply.md) and [position demand](position_demand.md) frozen. The state that [the Expectations](piece_the_expectations.md) can flip out of, into the full equilibrium instead. Staged in [Nobody Comes](plot_nobody_comes.md).
