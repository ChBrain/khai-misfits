# Plan: Withhold Until Wanted

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the supply's seat](position_supply.md), inside [After You](play_after_you.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Supply](position_supply.md)

## Direction

Do not stock a market with no buyers in it. Hold the goods, keep the routes idle, and wait at the threshold until there is demand to serve, because a stall earns only from buyers and stocking one where there are none is a cost for no return. Coming first pays now for a value that does not yet exist, and your own arrival cannot summon the buyers, so waiting is the sound reading of the empty floor and not a failure of enterprise.

## Orders

([The Supply](persona_the_supply.md) enacts [the Wait](piece_the_wait.md) through [Waiting](process_waiting.md) at [the Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), declining to stock a market with no buyers, so its readiness stays withheld until the demand it needs is there.)

## Implementation

([Waiting](process_waiting.md) holds the Supply at [the Threshold](place_the_threshold.md) while the other side is absent; because [the Cross-Side Pull](piece_the_cross_side_pull.md) puts its value in the buyers' presence and not its own, its arrival cannot create the demand it waits for, so its sound wait meets the Demand's and composes into [the Empty Platform](piece_the_empty_platform.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the goods stay unstocked while the floor is empty of buyers, the wait read as sound and not timid
- [ ] the seat does not try to summon the buyers by arriving, since its own presence draws none
- [ ] its wait meets the Demand's identical wait, the mirror acknowledged
- [ ] the trade it would gladly have done is foregone, half the standstill authored by no one and correct on its own terms
