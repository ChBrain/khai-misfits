# Position: Marquee

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The seat of the first to arrive, an anchor participant seeded, subsidised, or courted, whose committing before the other side is there absorbs the risk of the empty room and turns the other side's belief, tipping the platform toward full.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A presence that carries weight: a name known, a cohort large enough to make the floor look alive, the one arrival the waiting sides are waiting for.
A cost it is usually paid to bear: being first to an empty room earns nothing on its own, so the seat is generally seeded or subsidised or courted to take the position, and it accepts the cost for the standing its early presence buys.
No power to compel the other side, only to persuade it by being credibly there, because what it changes is belief, and belief is what the other side's wait was waiting on.

## Orders

Go first.
Commit to the empty floor before the other side arrives, credibly and visibly enough that the other side comes to believe the platform will fill, and take the inducement that makes bearing the empty room worthwhile.
Know the limit: presence buys a belief and not a certainty, so arrive as the credible first mover, but expect the room to fill behind you only if the other side's expectation truly turns.

## Loses

The safety of waiting, given up to open the market, and the whole cost if the tip does not catch.
Because only a credible first presence shifts expectations, the seat must step into the very emptiness the two sides were right to avoid, so it bears a paid risk, and if belief does not turn behind it the cost is spent and the floor still bare.
It loses the shelter the waiting sides keep and gains the position of the one who opened the market, when the market opens, the seat that carries the launch across the gap the waiting could not.

## Drives

Toward the first arrival that breaks the standstill by bearing what the two sides would not,
present where they are absent, paid to be so, credible enough to turn belief,
so the seat that opens the market is the seat that steps into the empty room at a price, and tips it full only if the other side comes to believe what its being there suggests.
