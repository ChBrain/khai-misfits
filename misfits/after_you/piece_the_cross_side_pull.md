# Piece: The Cross-Side Pull

## Taxonomy

[After You](play_after_you.md). The cross-side network externality: each side's benefit from joining rises with the OTHER side's participation, not its own.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating across [the floor](place_the_floor.md), the ground between the two sides where the pull is felt: not a force either side exerts on itself, but the tug each side feels from the other's numbers, present the moment both are named and absent nowhere the platform is meant to work.

## Load Bearing

The value to one side sits in the other side's numbers (Rochet and Tirole, extending Katz and Shapiro's one-sided network externality to the two-sided case): a platform is valuable to riders roughly in proportion to its drivers, and valuable to drivers roughly in proportion to its riders, so the same platform is worthless when empty on either side and valuable when full on both. Guard against reading this as a same-side effect, one rider wanting more riders, since that is the one-sided case Katz and Shapiro described as the antecedent; the pull this piece names runs CROSS-side, one side's value keyed to the other side's count, which is exactly what makes each side wait on the other rather than on more of its own kind.

## Apparent

Just the obvious fact that a market needs both buyers and sellers, a truism too plain to name.
