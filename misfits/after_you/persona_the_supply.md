# Persona: The Supply

## Taxonomy

[Supply](position_supply.md). The offering side of the platform, the sellers, the drivers, the hosts, willing to serve but only where there are buyers to serve, so it waits at the door for the other side to arrive first.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Willing, equipped, unhurried.
Would gladly stock the stalls, run the routes, open the doors, and can see that the platform, once full of buyers, would be worth a great deal to it.
No reluctance in principle and no laziness: it would come the moment the buyers were there, and it can see, plainly, why coming before them would be paying to serve an empty floor.

## Action

Withholds until wanted on [Withhold Until Wanted](plan_withhold_until_wanted.md), through [Waiting](process_waiting.md) at [the Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), enacting [the Wait](piece_the_wait.md), declining to stock a market with no buyers in it yet.
Cannot make the platform worth joining by joining it: its own presence draws no buyers, because a buyer wants sellers only for the goods, and goods with no buyers earn nothing, so going first is spending on a value that does not yet exist.
Does what a clear-eyed supplier does facing an empty floor: it holds back until the demand is there, and its holding back is half of why the demand never comes.

## Shadow

Not obstinate for waiting and not a fool if it comes: the Supply is right that a platform empty of buyers is worth little to it, so its wait is the correct reading of present worth, not a failure of enterprise.
Because the value it seeks is the buyers' presence and not its own, it cannot create by arriving the very thing it is waiting for, so the side most willing to serve is a side the empty floor keeps from serving, its caution one half of a standstill it did not intend.
It is the side that would fill the stalls the moment there were buyers, and the side whose waiting for buyers helps ensure there are none.

## Tell

Says it will come the day the buyers are there, and means it, and by meaning it helps keep the buyers away, because the buyers are saying the same of it.
Cannot be blamed for the wait, because stocking an empty market is a real cost for no return, so it reads the floor soundly and holds back, the launch it would have joined never arriving to be joined.
