# Persona: The Outsider

## Taxonomy

[Outside](position_outside.md). The person with a result that contradicts the occupant, who reads the ground accurately and spends the career elsewhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Correct, and unheroic about it.
Not intimidated in any way they would describe as fear: this is arithmetic about referees, grants and meetings, done soberly.
Has other good work available, which is what makes the choice easy rather than agonising.

## Action

Reads the ground.
Works out what entering would cost and what being right would be worth.
Does something else, and does it well.

## Shadow

The result, which is never submitted and therefore never rejected and never wrong.
Because a paper that is not written leaves no trace, this seat produces no grievance, no anecdote and no record: the harm exists only as the absence of a thing nobody can name.
It ends with a good career elsewhere and a correct answer nobody ever read.

## Tell

Says: it is a fight I do not need.
True, and a career advisor would say the same, which is the whole difficulty.
