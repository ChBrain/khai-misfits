# Plot: A Death Nobody Chose

## Taxonomy

[Somebody Is Already There](play_somebody_is_already_there.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Vacancy](persona_the_vacancy.md), arriving without warning while the occupant is still working.
[The Premature Deaths](piece_the_premature_deaths.md), which nothing else selected.

## Action

[Vacating](process_vacating.md): [The Vacancy](persona_the_vacancy.md), in [the vacated seat](position_vacated.md), removes the occupancy at [the frontier](place_the_frontier.md) and settles no argument.

## Stage

[The Vacancy](persona_the_vacancy.md), on [Open What Was Closed](plan_open_what_was_closed.md), does in one afternoon what correct results had not done, and is nobody's victory.

## Tension

The quantity is exposed here, and it is the uncomfortable part of the play.
What was holding the subfield shut was **presence** rather than opposition, so what releases it is **absence** rather than evidence. No argument is won, no result is confirmed, and nothing about the science changes on that day: the only thing that changed is who is standing there.
And because such a death is unforeseen and selected by nothing, it makes the mechanism measurable: the field afterwards can be compared with the field before, and the comparison is not confounded by anybody's decision. **The uncomfortable claim and the testable one are the same claim**, which is rare and is why this can be evidence rather than complaint.
