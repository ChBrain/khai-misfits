# Piece: The Premature Deaths

## Taxonomy

[Somebody Is Already There](play_somebody_is_already_there.md). Eminent figures dying while still active: unforeseen, selected by nothing, and therefore evidence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the field after](place_the_field_after.md), as the line between before and after.
Produced by [vacating](process_vacating.md), and chosen by nobody.

## Load Bearing

Without it the account is a suspicion about how fields behave and cannot be tested.
It is load bearing because the event is **unselected**: nothing about the subfield or the science caused it, so the field before and the field after are comparable without anybody's decision confounding the comparison.

## Apparent

Morbid, and a strange thing to build an argument on.
That reading is fair and the play carries it: the design is uncomfortable precisely because the mechanism is, and the discomfort is the finding rather than an accident of method.

## Yearbook

The uncomfortable claim and the testable one turn out to be the same claim, which is rare.
What it measures is entry, by people who had never worked with the figure, rising once the figure is gone.
