# Position: Outside

## Taxonomy

[Somebody Is Already There](play_somebody_is_already_there.md). The seat of the person with the contradicting result, who reads the ground accurately and goes elsewhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A correct result that contradicts the occupant.
An accurate reading of what entering would cost: the referee, the panel, the meeting.
Other good work available, which is what makes the choice easy.

## Orders

Read the ground.
Weigh the cost of entering against the worth of being right.
Spend the career where it pays.

## Loses

The result, never submitted and therefore never rejected and never wrong.
Because a paper that is not written leaves no trace, this seat generates no grievance, no anecdote and no record, so the harm exists only as the absence of something nobody can name.
It ends with a good career elsewhere and a correct answer nobody ever read.

## Drives

Toward a career that repays the work put into it,
by an arithmetic any advisor would endorse,
so the seat that reasons correctly is the seat that takes the answer away.
