## Action

[Entering](process_entering.md): [The Newcomer](persona_the_newcomer.md), in [the arriving seat](position_arriving.md), publishes at [the field after](place_the_field_after.md) and cites work the subfield was not citing.

## Stage

[The Collaborator](persona_the_collaborator.md), on [Build on the Work I Know](plan_build_on_the_work_i_know.md), continues afterwards and produces less, which nothing in the opening compensates.

## Tension

Both halves have to be carried, and the second one refuses the happy ending.
The entrants are not marginal figures filling a gap: they had never worked with the occupant, they draw on a body of literature the subfield was not reading, and they are disproportionately highly cited. So what was missing was a **direction** rather than a few papers, which is what makes the closure expensive rather than merely slow.
And the same event takes something real from the people who were closest, whose own output falls once the collaboration is gone. **The funeral is not a remedy.** Nobody in this production wanted it, the field is smaller for it, and it is nonetheless the thing that worked.
