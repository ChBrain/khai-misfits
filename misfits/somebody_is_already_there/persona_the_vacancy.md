# Persona: The Vacancy

## Taxonomy

[Vacated](position_vacated.md). What the occupant's death makes: an opening nobody argued for and nobody wanted.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Unwelcome, and effective.
Not a victory for anybody: the field is smaller and sadder for it, and says so.
Selected by nothing, which is exactly what makes it evidence.

## Action

Arrives without warning, while the occupant is still working.
Removes the occupancy and nothing else: no argument is settled and no result is confirmed.
Opens the ground.

## Shadow

That it works, which is the play's most uncomfortable sentence.
Because what was holding the field shut was presence rather than opposition, the thing that releases it is absence rather than evidence, and no amount of better argument substitutes.
It ends having done what forty years of correct results could not, and having been a death.

## Tell

Says nothing.
There is an obituary, sincerely meant, and within two years the subfield has papers in it from people who were never there before.
