## Stage

[The Outsider](persona_the_outsider.md), on [Spend the Career Where It Pays](plan_spend_the_career_where_it_pays.md), goes elsewhere, and does good work there.

## Tension

Declining is the rational choice, which is why it cannot be answered with encouragement.
Entering a subfield against its leading figure means the paper is refereed in a small world where that figure is the obvious referee, the grant is assessed by their former students, and the result is discussed at meetings they chair. **Being right is not sufficient, because you must also be believed**, and belief in a small field routes through the person the result contradicts.
Set against that, there is other good work available, which is what makes the choice easy rather than agonising. **A career advisor would advise exactly this**, and would be giving good advice. So the deterrence needs no threat, no warning and no unpleasantness: it needs only arithmetic, done accurately by somebody with options.
