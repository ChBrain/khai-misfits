# Persona: The Occupant

## Taxonomy

[Occupied](position_occupied.md). The great figure standing in the middle of the subfield: eminent because the work was good, and no gatekeeper by temperament or intent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Deservedly eminent.
Right far more often than wrong, which is exactly why the judgement carries.
Generous to students, present at the meetings, raising the quality of everything nearby.

## Action

Works.
Is cited, invited, consulted and believed.
Occupies the frontier, which is not something done to anybody but simply where the work is.

## Shadow

Nothing chosen and nothing done, which is what makes the seat impossible to vacate honourably.
Because the deterrence is produced by presence rather than by conduct, there is no behaviour to correct: being scrupulously fair to everyone who does approach changes nothing about those who do not.
It ends having obstructed nobody, in a field that stayed shut while it was there.

## Tell

Says: of course I would look at it, send it to me.
Sincerely meant, and it is not sent.
