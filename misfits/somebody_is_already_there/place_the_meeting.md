# Place: The Meeting

## Taxonomy

[Somebody Is Already There](play_somebody_is_already_there.md). Where the field gathers: the room in which a result would have to be defended, and in front of whom.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A small world: the same faces, the obvious referee, the panel of former students.
Sessions chaired by the person a contradicting result would contradict.

## Holds

Why being right is not sufficient.
Belief in a small field routes through its central figure, so a correct result must not only be correct but be credited, and the credit runs through the person with most to lose by it.

## Offers

A fair hearing, sincerely, to anybody who comes.

## Withheld

Anonymity, and any route to acceptance that does not pass through the centre.
There is no second forum, no larger audience and no neutral arbiter, and that is what makes the reckoning outside it come out the way it does.
