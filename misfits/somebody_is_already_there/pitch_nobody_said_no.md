# Pitch: Nobody Said No

## Taxonomy

[Somebody Is Already There](play_somebody_is_already_there.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Careful, deferential, unhurried.
The register of a field that respects its seniors and is right to: measured praise, hedged disagreement, nothing said that could not be said to the person's face.
No resentment anywhere in it, because there is nothing to resent and everybody knows it.

## Undertow

Under the courtesy, two facts that do not negotiate.
Eminence is real and largely earned. A newcomer choosing where to spend a career reasons about the odds.
Between those two, a subfield closes without anybody closing it.

## Nerve

The unease of a result that is correct,
held by somebody who has read the ground accurately,
and will not be submitted anywhere, so it will never be rejected and never be wrong.

## Echo

After the run: a great figure, deservedly great, who obstructed nobody,
a set of papers with no authors and no titles,
and a field that opens on the day of a funeral nobody wanted.
