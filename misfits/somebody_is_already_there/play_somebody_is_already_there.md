# Play: Somebody Is Already There

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Somebody Is Already There.
A subfield with a great figure standing in the middle of it: genuinely great, right about most things, generous to students, and not a gatekeeper by temperament or by intent.
Somewhere outside the field is a person with a result that contradicts them.
That person looks at the ground, works out what entering would cost, and does something else instead. Nothing is rejected, because nothing is submitted.

## Arc

The bend is this: the obstacle is not opposition and never becomes opposition, because the work that would have been opposed is not done, and the quantity that governs it is nothing more than the great figure being alive.
It runs on two facts, neither of them anybody's doing: eminence is real and largely earned, and a newcomer choosing where to spend a career reasons about the odds.
Take the standing first, because the play has to establish that it is **deserved**. The occupant is eminent because the work was good. They are right far more often than they are wrong, which is exactly why their judgement carries, and their presence raises the quality of everything around them. There is no villain available here and the production never looks for one.
Then the reckoning, which is the outsider's and is correct. Entering a subfield against its leading figure means your paper is refereed in a small world where they are the obvious referee, your grant is assessed by their former students, your result is discussed at meetings they chair, and being right is not sufficient, because you must also be believed. Set against that, there is other good work available elsewhere. **Declining is the rational choice, and a career advisor would advise it.**
Then the harm, which is invisible by construction. Nothing is suppressed. No paper is rejected, no referee behaves badly, no meeting excludes anybody, and there is no record anywhere of a decision to keep the field closed. What exists instead is an absence: the work that was not attempted, by people who never announced they were not attempting it. **A field cannot notice the papers nobody wrote.**
Then the quantity, which is the uncomfortable part. What releases the field is not evidence and not argument: it is the occupant no longer being there. That is why the question can be tested at all, by looking at what happens to a subfield after an eminent figure dies while still active, which is a death nobody chose and nothing else selected. Entry by people who had never worked with the figure rises afterwards, and the entrants are not marginal: they draw on a different body of literature and are disproportionately highly cited.
And the vacancy is not a good, which the play carries rather than tidying. The same event that opens the field to outsiders takes something real from the people closest to it: those who worked with the figure produce less afterwards. **The funeral is not a remedy, and nobody in the play is glad of it.**

## Stakes

Whether a field can be corrected while the person it would correct is still standing in it.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the eminent figure is obstructive, because they are not and the play refuses to make them so, and not whether the outsider is timid, because the reckoning is sound and a career advisor would endorse it, but whether a subfield can take a result that contradicts its leading figure while that figure is present. The production cannot make it possible. It can only show that the standing is earned and mostly right, that declining to enter is the rational move for the person who would have to make it, that the resulting harm is an absence and therefore unobservable from inside, that what actually releases the field is a death rather than an argument, and that the release is not a good, since the same event takes real work from the people who were closest.
