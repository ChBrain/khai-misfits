# Process: Entering

## Taxonomy

[Somebody Is Already There](play_somebody_is_already_there.md). Outsiders arriving on the opened ground, and bringing another literature with them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The vacancy](persona_the_vacancy.md), which removed the occupancy and nothing else.
At [the field after](place_the_field_after.md).

## Direction

Toward work that was available for years and was not taken, done by people who had never worked with the occupant.
They are not marginal figures filling a gap: they draw on a body of literature the subfield was not reading, and they are disproportionately highly cited.
So what the closure cost was a direction rather than a few papers.

## Lever

Standing built elsewhere, which is what lets an outsider be taken seriously on arrival.
Because the entrants are strong rather than opportunistic, the size of the loss can be read off what they contribute.

## Echo

New work on old ground,
and a measure of what the field did without while it was shut.
