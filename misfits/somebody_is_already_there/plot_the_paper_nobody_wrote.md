# Plot: The Paper Nobody Wrote

## Taxonomy

[Somebody Is Already There](play_somebody_is_already_there.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Paper Not Written](piece_the_paper_not_written.md), which has no title and no author.
[The Field After](place_the_field_after.md), unaware of it.

## Action

[Declining](process_declining.md): the entry is not made, so nothing is submitted at [the frontier](place_the_frontier.md), and nothing is refused there.

## Stage

[The Occupant](persona_the_occupant.md) obstructs nobody throughout, and [the outsider](persona_the_outsider.md) complains to nobody, so no event occurs anywhere that anyone could record.

## Tension

The harm is invisible by construction, which is what makes it a structure rather than a scandal.
Nothing is suppressed. No paper is rejected, no referee behaves badly, no meeting excludes anybody, no student is discouraged, and there is no record anywhere of a decision to keep the field closed, **because no such decision was made**.
What exists instead is an absence: work not attempted, by people who never announced they were not attempting it. **A field cannot notice the papers nobody wrote.** So there is no grievance to investigate, no pattern of rejections to audit, and no complaint to uphold, and any body reviewing this subfield's conduct would correctly find nothing wrong with it.
