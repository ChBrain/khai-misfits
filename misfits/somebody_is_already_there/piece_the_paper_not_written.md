# Piece: The Paper Not Written

## Taxonomy

[Somebody Is Already There](play_somebody_is_already_there.md). The work never attempted: no title, no author, no record, and no way to count it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Nowhere, which is the point.
Absent from [the field after](place_the_field_after.md) and from every archive of it.

## Load Bearing

Without it the harm is a delay and somebody could have been asked about it.
It is load bearing because it is **an absence rather than an event**: nothing was suppressed, nothing rejected, nobody excluded, and no decision made, so there is no grievance to investigate and no pattern of refusals to audit.

## Apparent

Nothing at all.
It does not read as anything, because it is not there, and a review of the subfield's conduct would correctly find nothing wrong with it.

## Yearbook

Produced by [declining](process_declining.md), invisible to everybody including the person who did not write it.
A field cannot notice the papers nobody wrote, which is what makes this structural rather than scandalous.
