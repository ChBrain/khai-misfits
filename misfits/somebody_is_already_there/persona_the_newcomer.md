# Persona: The Newcomer

## Taxonomy

[Arriving](position_arriving.md). The entrant who never worked with the occupant, arriving after the ground opens, and bringing a different literature.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Outside the tradition entirely.
Not a student of the school, not a collaborator, not previously present in this subfield at any point.
Well regarded elsewhere, and arriving with the standing to be taken seriously here.

## Action

Enters ground that was available all along.
Cites work the subfield was not citing.
Publishes, and is read.

## Shadow

Nothing, and this seat is what makes the loss measurable rather than sentimental: the entrants are not marginal figures filling a gap but disproportionately highly cited people drawing on a different body of literature.
Because what they bring was outside the subfield's reading rather than merely absent from it, the field was missing a direction and not simply a few papers.
It ends contributing what was available for years, and was not.

## Tell

Says: I had never worked in this area.
Which is precisely why the contribution is worth what it is worth.
