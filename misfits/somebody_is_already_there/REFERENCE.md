# Somebody Is Already There: Reference

The research warrant for Somebody Is Already There.
The claim is Planck's; the play runs on the deterrence of entry by a living eminence, and on the natural
experiment that measures it, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging a scientific frontier as **ground that is closed by occupancy rather than by opposition**: an eminent
figure works at the centre of a subfield and is believed there, deservedly; an outsider holding a result that
contradicts them reads the referee pool, the funding panel and the conference programme, and spends the career
elsewhere; the work is therefore never attempted, so nothing is rejected and no record of any closure exists;
and what eventually opens the ground is the figure's death rather than any argument. The play models the
documented mechanics, the standing earned by good work, the arithmetic that makes declining correct, the paper
that has no title and no author, the unforeseen death that nothing selected, the entrants who arrive from
outside the tradition with a literature the subfield was not reading, and the collaborators whose output falls
when the same event occurs, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a domineering
professor or a timid junior.

The governing law is that a field's rate of correction depends on who is standing in it and not only on what
the evidence says, so a result that contradicts an eminent living incumbent waits on the incumbent rather than
on the evidence.

**Nobody in the sequence falls short, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The
occupant's eminence is earned and their judgement is usually right. The outsider's reckoning is accurate and
any advisor would endorse it. The referees behave properly. The meetings are open. No paper is refused,
because none is submitted.

**And the standing is deserved, which the play establishes before anything else.** The occupant is eminent
because the work was good; their judgement carries because it is usually correct, which is the only reason
judgement should ever carry; and their presence raises the quality of everything nearby. **There is no
gatekeeper here**: nobody is turned away, no paper is blocked, no student is warned off, and a direct approach
would be received courteously and read seriously. The production never looks for a villain, which is what keeps
the misfit from resolving into a complaint about one person.

**Then the reckoning, which is the outsider's and is sound.** Entering a subfield against its leading figure
means the paper is refereed in a small world where that figure is the obvious referee, the grant is assessed by
their former students, and the result is discussed at meetings they chair. **Being right is not sufficient,
because it is also necessary to be believed**, and belief in a small field routes through the person the result
contradicts. Against that sits other good work, available elsewhere, which makes the choice easy rather than
agonising. Declining is the rational move and no amount of encouragement changes a referee pool.

**Then the harm, which is invisible by construction.** Nothing is suppressed. No paper is rejected, no referee
misbehaves, no meeting excludes anybody, and no decision to keep the field closed is taken by anyone, because
no such decision exists. What exists is an absence, produced by people who never announced they were producing
it. **A field cannot notice the papers nobody wrote**, so there is no grievance to investigate and no pattern
of refusals to audit, and a review of the subfield's conduct would correctly find nothing wrong with it.

**Then the quantity, which is the uncomfortable part and is also what makes this testable.** What releases the
field is not evidence and not argument but the occupant no longer being there. Because a death that arrives
while a scientist is still active is unforeseen and selected by nothing about the science, the subfield before
and the subfield after are comparable without anybody's decision confounding them. **The uncomfortable claim
and the testable one are the same claim.** What is measured is entry: publications in the subfield by authors
who had never collaborated with the deceased rise afterwards.

**And the entrants are not marginal, which is what prices the closure.** They arrive from outside the
tradition, they draw on a body of literature the subfield was not citing, and they are disproportionately
highly cited. So what the occupancy cost was a **direction** rather than a few papers arriving late.

**And the vacancy is not a good, which the play carries rather than tidying away.** The same event that opens
the ground takes something real from those who were closest: the collaborators' own output falls afterwards.
**The funeral is not a remedy**, nobody in the production wanted it, the field is smaller for it, and it is
nonetheless the thing that worked.

It is distinct from the house's **No Place in the Theory**, which is the nearest incumbent and stages the
Semmelweis reflex, where evidence is rejected because no mechanism is available to account for it. Remove that
engine entirely, by stipulating a result whose mechanism the standing theory fully accepts, with nothing
missing from the account and nothing to explain, and this misfit is unchanged, because **nothing here turns on
the explanation being absent and everything turns on who is standing there.** That play needs a theoretical gap
and a body of evidence being argued over; this one needs no argument at all, since the work is not done and
therefore never contradicts anything in public.

It is distinct from the house's **Vetocracy**, which stages the proliferation of veto points, where too many
actors can block and so nothing passes. Remove that engine, by stipulating a field with no procedure, no vote,
no committee and nobody holding any formal power to block, and this stands entire, because **it needs no
procedure and confers no veto.** The occupant blocks nothing and could not: what deters is presence, and a
frontier with no decision rules at all produces the same result.

It is distinct from the house's **On Good Authority**, which stages citation distortion, where a claim acquires
unfounded authority through chains of citation that do not support it. Remove that engine, by stipulating that
every citation chain is accurate and no claim carries more support than it has earned, and this stands, because
**the authority here is real.** The occupant's standing is deserved and mostly correct; nothing about the record
is distorted, and the deterrence would be identical in a field with a perfect citation practice.

It is distinct from the house's **The File Drawer**, which stages publication bias, where results that were
obtained are selectively not published. Remove that engine, by stipulating that everything submitted is
published and nothing is filed away, and this stands whole, because **there is nothing to file.** There a result
exists and is withheld; here the result is never produced, so the loss is upstream of the literature entirely
and no reform of publishing reaches it.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the eminent figure
  as a gatekeeper or the outsider as timid, because nothing in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the
  occupant's standing is earned, their judgement is usually right, they turn nobody away and would read a direct
  approach seriously; the outsider's arithmetic about referees, panels and meetings is accurate, and any advisor
  would give the same counsel; the referees behave properly and the meetings are open; the collaborators take no
  part in anybody's exclusion; and the newcomers arrive only because ground became available. The failure is
  authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a frontier can be closed by presence
  while every person near it behaves impeccably.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Encouraging the outsider
  changes nothing, because the arithmetic they did is correct and boldness does not alter a referee pool.
  Asking the occupant to behave better changes nothing, since the deterrence follows from presence rather than
  conduct and they were already behaving well. Auditing the field finds nothing, because nothing was refused.
  Better evidence does not help, since the evidence was never brought. And the thing that does work is a death,
  which nobody may wish for, which settles no argument, and which takes real output from the people who were
  closest to the figure.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the claim is Planck's, the sociological account of resistance is
  Barber's, the astronomical episode belongs to Chandrasekhar and Eddington and to the historians who have
  written it up, and the measurement is Azoulay, Fons-Rosen and Graff Zivin's. The bounds are carried with them:
  **Planck's statement is an assertion from one person's experience and is offered as the proposition tested
  rather than as evidence**; the Eddington episode is a single illustrative case and proves nothing on its own;
  the measurement is of life-science subfields and one discipline's structure is not every discipline's;
  the mechanism the study identifies is entry by outsiders rather than any claim about who was right; whether
  senior figures obstruct heterodox work or supply necessary stewardship is genuinely contested and the play
  adjudicates none of it; and the same study reports a real cost to the deceased's collaborators, which the play
  carries. **The play makes no claim about any scientist, any field or any death, and gives no scientific,
  professional or institutional advice of any kind**, only that a frontier with somebody standing on it is
  entered less, by people who are not doing anything wrong.
