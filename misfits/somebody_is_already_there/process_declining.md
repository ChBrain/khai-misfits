# Process: Declining

## Taxonomy

[Somebody Is Already There](play_somebody_is_already_there.md). The entry not made, so that nothing is submitted and nothing is refused.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The odds](piece_the_odds.md), correctly computed.
Elsewhere, quietly, with no announcement of any kind.

## Direction

Toward other good work, which gets done and is worth doing.
Nothing is suppressed here: no paper is rejected, no referee behaves badly, no meeting excludes anybody, and no decision to keep the field closed is made by anyone, because no such decision exists.
What is produced is an absence, and an absence has no author.

## Lever

Non-participation, which requires nothing of anybody and leaves no trace.
Because a field cannot notice the papers nobody wrote, this step is unobservable from inside the subfield it harms.

## Echo

No grievance to investigate,
no pattern of rejections to audit,
and a review of the field's conduct that would correctly find nothing wrong.
