# Process: Vacating

## Taxonomy

[Somebody Is Already There](play_somebody_is_already_there.md). The occupancy ending, unforeseen, while the occupant is still working.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A death nobody chose and nothing else selected.
At [the frontier](place_the_frontier.md), without warning.

## Direction

Toward an open field, reached by the one route that reliably reaches it.
No argument is won, no result is confirmed, and nothing about the science changes on that day: what changes is who is standing there.
Because such an event is unforeseen and selected by nothing, it makes the mechanism measurable, the field afterwards being comparable with the field before without anybody's decision confounding it.

## Lever

Absence, which is what was actually holding things and is therefore what releases them.
Because presence was the obstacle, better evidence and better argument were never substitutes and are not now.

## Echo

An obituary, sincerely meant,
and within two years, papers from people who were never there before.
