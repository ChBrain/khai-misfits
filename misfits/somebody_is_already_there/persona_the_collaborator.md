# Persona: The Collaborator

## Taxonomy

[Attached](position_attached.md). The people who worked with the occupant, and whose own output falls when the occupant is gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Capable and genuinely productive.
Not coasting on a famous name: the collaboration was real work and the results were real results.
Closest to the figure, and the first to lose by the vacancy.

## Action

Works alongside the occupant, well.
Continues afterwards, and produces less.
Bears a cost that nothing in the field's opening compensates.

## Shadow

The plain fact that the release is not free, which the play carries rather than tidying away.
Because the same event that lets outsiders in takes something real from those who were closest, there is no reading of this in which the funeral is a remedy and no character in it who wanted the death.
It ends diminished, by an event the field partly benefited from.

## Tell

Says: it is not the same without them.
True in the ordinary human sense, and true in the output as well.
