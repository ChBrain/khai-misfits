# Process: Reckoning

## Taxonomy

[Somebody Is Already There](play_somebody_is_already_there.md). The outsider working out what entering would cost against what being right would be worth.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A result that contradicts the occupant.
At [the frontier](place_the_frontier.md), assessed from outside it.

## Direction

Toward a sound decision about where to spend a career, made soberly and with options.
The paper would be refereed in a small world where the occupant is the obvious referee, the grant assessed by their former students, the result discussed at meetings they chair. Being right is not sufficient, because it is also necessary to be believed, and belief in a small field routes through the person the result contradicts.
So this step is arithmetic rather than fear, and it is done correctly.

## Lever

Accurate reading of the ground, which is a professional skill and not a failing.
Because the arithmetic is sound, encouragement does not change it and a career advisor would reach the same answer.

## Echo

A correct decision,
by somebody with a correct result,
to take neither of them into that subfield.
