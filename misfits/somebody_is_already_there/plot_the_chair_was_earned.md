## Stage

## Tension

The eminence is deserved, and the play has to establish that before it can be the problem.
The occupant is eminent because the work was good. Their judgement carries because it is usually correct, which is the only reason judgement should ever carry, and their presence raises the quality of everything around them.
**There is no gatekeeper here.** Nobody is turned away, no paper is blocked, no student is warned off, and an approach made directly would be received courteously and read seriously. So the trap cannot be sprung by finding the occupant obstructive, and the production never looks for a villain: it has to be sprung with a great figure behaving impeccably in a field they improved.
