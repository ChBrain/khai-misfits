# Plot: So It Widens With the Odds That the Seller Knows More

## Taxonomy

[Widest When You Need It](play_widest_when_you_need_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

News the quoter cannot yet price.

## Action

[Widening as the odds change](process_widening_as_the_odds_change.md): the gap moves, the quote stays up, nothing else changes hands.

## Stage

The only instrument the seat holds, used as designed.

## Tension

Every alternative is unavailable rather than unattractive.
The quoter cannot ask an arriving seller what they know, because a seller who answered honestly would be answering against their own interest and there is no way to verify it. It cannot decline selectively, because declining to the people who look informed is declining to whoever is selling, which is not a market. And it cannot hold the gap fixed while the odds move, because doing so is an open invitation to exactly the counterparty it loses to, and it would be gone by the afternoon leaving no quote at all. Widening is the whole of the defence.
