# Plot: And the Spread Is a Defence and Not a Fee

## Taxonomy

[Widest When You Need It](play_widest_when_you_need_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A stream of arrivals, some of whom know what the price is about to do.

## Action

[The Spread](piece_the_spread.md) opens: a gap between the two posted prices, present before any cost is counted.

## Stage

A charge that exists for a reason unrelated to cost or profit.

## Tension

The model that established this removed every other explanation on purpose.
A risk-neutral quoter, zero transaction costs, zero expected profit, and a stream of traders of whom some are better informed: a positive gap still appears, because the quoter loses on every fill against the informed and can recoup only from everybody else. It cannot tell them apart at the moment of trade and has no way to ask. So the reading in which a dealer is taking a cut that competition ought to compress is not available: the gap is what standing between people who know different things costs, and it is paid by whoever knows less.
