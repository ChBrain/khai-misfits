# Plot: And Those Odds Peak When Everybody Sells

## Taxonomy

[Widest When You Need It](play_widest_when_you_need_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A day of one-way flow.

## Action

At [the day everyone sells](place_the_day_everyone_sells.md), the gap is at its widest and [the balance sheet](piece_the_balance_sheet.md) is filling.

## Stage

Two peaks arriving together, for one reason.

## Tension

The coincidence is not a coincidence and cannot be separated out.
A day when many holders want to sell is by definition a day when the reason to sell is shared, which is to say a day when it is most likely that any given seller is acting on something. So the odds that drive the gap peak precisely with the volume of people needing to trade. Independently and at the same time, the capacity behind the quote is consumed by the trades the quote invites, and on a one-way day nothing comes back. It was observed in the deepest market in the world: dealer capacity fell as demand for it soared, spreads widened, and past a level of balance-sheet use liquidity was worse than volatility alone explained.
