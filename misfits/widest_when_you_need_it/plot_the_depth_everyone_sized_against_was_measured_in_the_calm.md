# Plot: The Depth Everyone Sized Against Was Measured in the Calm

## Taxonomy

[Widest When You Need It](play_widest_when_you_need_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A risk limit set from a liquidity estimate.

## Action

[The Measured Depth](piece_the_measured_depth.md) is produced honestly at [the calm market](place_the_calm_market.md), and every position is sized against it.

## Stage

A true number, current and reviewed, about a different day.

## Tension

The measurement is not stale and not sloppy: it is correct about something else.
Depth observed when flow runs both ways describes a market in which the quoter's odds are ordinary. On the day the reason to sell is shared they are not ordinary, so the quantity estimated and the quantity wanted are different quantities and no amount of care collapses them. Assuming worse is available and costs return on every day it is not needed, and the seat that assumes worse answers for that cost too. So the honest number is the one that gets used, and it is the one that misleads.
