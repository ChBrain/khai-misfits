# Persona: The Informed Seller

## Taxonomy

[Informed Seller](position_informed_seller.md). The one who did the work and acts on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An analyst, a fund, or anybody who has read the filing carefully and reached a view before the price has.

## Action

Sells into the quote.
Takes the posted price, which is stale relative to what this seat knows.

## Shadow

Makes the price informative and makes the quote expensive for everybody else.
The seat is doing nothing wrong and is doing the thing prices depend on: a market where nobody researched anything would have prices that meant nothing, and the work is costly and has to be paid for somehow. The quoter's loss to this seat is exactly the transfer that rewards the research. The seat cannot be identified at the point of trade, has no obligation to announce itself, and would not be improved by being slower. Its existence is the reason the gap is not zero.

## Tell

Is indistinguishable from everybody else at the moment of the trade.
