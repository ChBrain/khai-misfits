# Play: Widest When You Need It

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Widest When You Need It.
An asset is sellable because somebody stands ready to buy it and to sell it, at prices posted in advance, to whoever arrives next. That quote is what a market is. The gap between the two prices is not a fee and not a margin: it is what the quoter must charge because some of the people arriving know something the quoter does not, and the quoter cannot tell which ones. It is therefore set by the odds that the next seller knows more, and those odds are highest on the day everybody wants to sell.

## Arc

The bend is this: the spread is the quoter's only defence against the counterparty who knows more, so it must be widest exactly when most people need to trade.
It begins with the thing that makes a market exist.
[Somebody Must Quote Both Sides, and That Is What a Market Is](plot_somebody_must_quote_both_sides_and_that_is_what_a_market_is.md). [The Quoter](persona_the_quoter.md) posts a price before knowing who will take it.
And the gap is a defence rather than a charge.
[And the Spread Is a Defence and Not a Fee](plot_and_the_spread_is_a_defence_and_not_a_fee.md). [The Spread](piece_the_spread.md) is positive in the model even at zero costs and zero expected profit, which is what removes the reading that somebody is taking a cut.
So it moves with the odds.
[So It Widens With the Odds That the Seller Knows More](plot_so_it_widens_with_the_odds_that_the_seller_knows_more.md). Nothing else is available: the quoter cannot ask, cannot refuse selectively, and loses to the informed on every fill.
And [those odds peak when everybody sells](plot_and_those_odds_peak_when_everybody_sells.md).
[The Day Everyone Sells](place_the_day_everyone_sells.md) is when the chance that any given seller knows something is highest and [the balance sheet](piece_the_balance_sheet.md) is fullest at once.
Then [the depth everyone sized against was measured in the calm](plot_the_depth_everyone_sized_against_was_measured_in_the_calm.md). [The Measured Depth](piece_the_measured_depth.md) is an honest number about a day nobody needed it.
And [the price was there until it was needed](plot_so_the_price_was_there_until_it_was_needed.md). [The Forced Seller](persona_the_forced_seller.md) is following a mandate and pays the difference.

## Stakes

Whether a holding can be turned into money on the day the money is wanted.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a quote that is what makes an asset sellable, a spread that is a defence against being systematically picked off, a widening that is the only instrument the quoter holds, a peak in asymmetry that coincides with a peak in need, a depth measured honestly on a day nobody needed it, and a forced seller following a mandate, and away from any account in which a dealer withdrew out of greed or a fund manager was reckless. The production cannot fault the quoter, whose narrower alternative is insolvency by the afternoon and no quote at all; and it cannot fault the informed seller, whose research is what makes a price mean anything. What is left to fight over is not whether anybody erred but what to call a price that is posted every day and is worst on the day it is taken.
