# Persona: The Quoter

## Taxonomy

[Quoter](position_quoter.md). The one who posts a price before knowing who will take it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A dealer or market maker standing ready on both sides of an asset, with a balance sheet, a limit on it, and no idea who arrives next.

## Action

Posts a bid and an offer, in size, continuously.
Honours them to whoever hits them, and adjusts the gap as the odds change.

## Shadow

Charges most exactly when the charge hurts most, and has nothing else to charge with.
The seat is not withdrawing and is not gouging: it loses on every trade against somebody better informed and can only recoup from the rest, so a positive gap survives even at zero costs and zero expected profit. It cannot ask an arriving seller what they know, cannot decline selectively without ceasing to be a market, and cannot hold the gap fixed while the odds move without being picked off into insolvency by the afternoon, after which there is no quote at all. Widening is what keeps the price on the screen.

## Tell

Stays open by charging most on the day it matters.
