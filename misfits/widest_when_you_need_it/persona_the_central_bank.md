# Persona: The Central Bank

## Taxonomy

[Central Bank](position_central_bank.md). The one who buys what nobody will quote, and moves the payer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An authority with an unlimited balance sheet in its own currency, a mandate for market functioning, and a decision to take within hours.

## Action

Buys, in size, at posted prices, until the market functions again.
Restores the ability to sell, which nothing else present can do.

## Shadow

Solves it, and by solving it becomes the thing everybody sizes against next time.
The seat's intervention works, is the correct decision, and is not free. The cost moves onto a public balance sheet, which is a real transfer to a party that was not in the trade. And an intervention that worked is now a fact everybody plans around, so the depth measured in the next calm quietly includes an expectation of this seat, which is the one component of it that cannot be observed until it is tested. There is no version of this seat that both rescues the market and leaves the next measurement honest.

## Tell

Fixes it, and is thereafter part of what is being measured.
