# Piece: The Spread

## Taxonomy

[Widening as the Odds Change](process_widening_as_the_odds_change.md). The defence, mistaken for a fee.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [the calm market](place_the_calm_market.md), and widened at [the day everyone sells](place_the_day_everyone_sells.md).

## Load Bearing

It is positive for a reason that has nothing to do with cost or profit, which is what removes every reading in which somebody is taking a cut.
In the model that established it, a risk-neutral quoter facing a stream of traders of whom some are better informed must post a gap even where transaction costs are zero and expected profit is zero. The quoter loses on every fill against the informed and can only recoup from everybody else, and cannot tell the two apart at the moment of trade. So the gap is not a charge for a service. It is the price of standing between people who know different things, paid by whoever does not know.

## Apparent

A dealer's margin, which competition should compress.

## Yearbook

Is what the quoter must charge to survive contact with the better informed, and is therefore largest when they are most likely present.
