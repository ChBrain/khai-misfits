# Position: Quoter

## Taxonomy

[Widest When You Need It](play_widest_when_you_need_it.md). The seat that must price before knowing who arrives.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A balance sheet with a limit, and two prices to post.

## Orders

Quote both sides in size. Honour them. Adjust the gap as the odds move.

## Loses

Every instrument except the gap.
It cannot ask what an arriving seller knows, cannot decline selectively and remain a market, and cannot hold the gap fixed while the odds move without being picked off.

## Drives

Toward staying on the screen, which requires charging most when it hurts most.
