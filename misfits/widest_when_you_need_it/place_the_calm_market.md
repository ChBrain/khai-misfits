# Place: The Calm Market

## Taxonomy

[Widest When You Need It](play_widest_when_you_need_it.md). Where the depth is measured, honestly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An ordinary week: tight quotes, flow in both directions, size available, a spread nobody notices.
Everything proper: the market works, the measurement is current, the estimate is the best one available.

## Holds

[The Risk Officer](persona_the_risk_officer.md), measuring what is there.
[The Measured Depth](piece_the_measured_depth.md), which is a true number.
[The Quoter](persona_the_quoter.md), profitable and unremarkable.

## Offers

A defensible estimate of how much can be sold in a day, and a limit built on it.

## Withheld

Any observation of the quantity that will actually be wanted.
The place is where the number everybody relies on is produced, and where it is clearest that producing it well is not the same as producing the right one. Depth on a day when flow is two-sided is a measurement of a market in which the quoter's odds are ordinary. On a day when the reason to sell is shared, the odds are not ordinary and the quantity is a different quantity. Assuming worse is possible and costs return on every day it is not needed, which somebody is also answerable for, so the honest number is the one that gets used.
