# Persona: The Forced Seller

## Taxonomy

[Forced Seller](position_forced_seller.md). The one who has to raise cash today and knows nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A fund meeting redemptions, a scheme paying benefits, a household with a bill: somebody with a date rather than a view.

## Action

Sells what can be sold, in the size required, by the date required.
Takes the quote, because the quote is what there is.

## Shadow

Pays the price of an asymmetry it is not on either side of.
The seat has no information and is not trading on any, which is precisely why it cannot be told apart from somebody who is. It is selling because a mandate, a redemption or a liability says so, and the date is not negotiable. It sized the holding against a depth that was honestly measured and is about a different day. Waiting is not available and selling something else means selling whatever is most liquid, which concentrates the damage rather than avoiding it.

## Tell

Pays for knowledge it does not have and cannot prove it lacks.
