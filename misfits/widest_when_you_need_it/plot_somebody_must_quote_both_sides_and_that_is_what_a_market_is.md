# Plot: Somebody Must Quote Both Sides, and That Is What a Market Is

## Taxonomy

[Widest When You Need It](play_widest_when_you_need_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An asset, and a holder who may one day want out of it.

## Action

[The Quoter](persona_the_quoter.md) posts a bid and an offer in size, before knowing who will take either.

## Stage

The act that turns a holding into something sellable.

## Tension

Nothing here is a service laid over a market: it is the market.
Without somebody standing ready on both sides, selling means finding a particular person who wants precisely what you have, at the moment you want to be rid of it. The standing quote removes that search, and removing it is what makes an asset a liquid one. So the seat is not an intermediary taking a slice of something that would otherwise happen unaided. It is the reason the thing happens, and everything that follows follows from the position it has to take.
