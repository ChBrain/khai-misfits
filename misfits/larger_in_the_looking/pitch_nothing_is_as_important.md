# Pitch: Nothing Is as Important

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that nothing in a life is as important as it seems while it is the thing being thought about.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Wry and reflective, the small comedy of a mind that magnifies whatever it looks at. A fair question about how much one thing matters, and an answer inflated by the very asking. Nothing here is a trick or a mind too foolish to know itself; to weigh a factor a mind must attend to it, and attention is what makes it loom.

## Undertow

The act of attending to a factor magnifies its apparent importance, so the estimate measures how much it fills the mind and not how much it will fill a life (Kahneman). Midwesterners and Californians report about the same satisfaction, yet both expect the Californians happier, fixing on the salient climate and neglecting the hundred things that fill a life the same in either place (Schkade and Kahneman).

## Nerve

That the only way to weigh a factor is to attend to it, and attending is what makes it loom, so a factor is overweighted every time it is the thing being thought about, larger in the looking than in the life. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since the factor is not nothing, only smaller in the living than in the judging, a documented bias in the judgments made while it is salient (Kahneman and colleagues).

## Echo

Bring the one thing to mind to weigh it, and it fills the mind, and you weigh a life you are not looking at.
