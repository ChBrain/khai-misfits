# Plan: Ask How Much

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the asker's seat](position_asker.md), inside [Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Asker](position_asker.md)

## Direction

Put the question of how much a single factor bears on a life, as a survey or a seller or a self weighing a choice must, since the question is legitimate and often necessary. Know that a question about a factor is a spotlight on the factor, that it can be weighed only by being brought to attention, and that the asking narrows the frame to the one thing before any answer is given.

## Orders

([The Asker](persona_the_asker.md) holds [the Question](piece_the_question.md) through [Asking](process_asking.md) at [the Asking](place_the_asking.md), putting the fair question that directs the mind to the factor.)

## Implementation

([Asking](process_asking.md) at [the Asking](place_the_asking.md) holds [the Question](piece_the_question.md), because to ask how much a factor matters is to bring it to attention, so the fair question is already the first turn of the illusion, the frame narrowed to the one thing.)
