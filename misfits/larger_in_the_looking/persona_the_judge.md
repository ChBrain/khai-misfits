# Persona: The Judge

## Taxonomy

[Judge](position_judge.md), inside [Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The archetype of the one who weighs how much the factor matters, bringing it to attention where it looms, and so overweighting it, the mind estimating importance the only way it can.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A fool who cannot know its own mind, as though a clearer head would simply weigh the factor rightly, when to weigh a factor at all is to attend to it, and attention is what inflates it.

## Action

Weigh the factor. At [the Asking](place_the_asking.md), the Judge on [Weigh the Factor](plan_weigh_the_factor.md) through [Focusing](process_focusing.md) takes up [the Factor](piece_the_factor.md), bringing the single thing to mind, then at [the Focus](place_the_focus.md) meets [the Magnification](piece_the_magnification.md) and [the Overweight](piece_the_overweight.md), rating it to matter far more than it will (Kahneman; Schkade and Kahneman).

## Shadow

It does the only thing a mind can do, and the doing is the error. The Judge is no fool: to gauge how much a factor bears on a life, it must bring the factor to attention and weigh it, for there is no other way a mind estimates importance than by holding the thing in view. But a thing held in view looms, and the looming is not a fault in the judging but the price of judging at all, so the estimate it returns is of how much the factor fills the mind while attended and not how much it will fill a life where it is one of many things. It weighs honestly and weighs too high, and cannot help the second by the first. It keeps its honest weighing and its only method, and overweights the factor without misjudging anything it could have judged better, the mind that magnifies exactly by attending.

## Tell

It brought the one thing to mind to weigh it well, and weighing it well was bringing it to mind, where it could only loom.
