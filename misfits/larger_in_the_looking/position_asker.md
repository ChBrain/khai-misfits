# Position: Asker

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The seat of the one who puts the question, and marks that the question is fair and asked in good faith, and that the attention it draws to the factor is already the first turn of the illusion.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A fair question to put, how much does this one thing bear on how well a life goes.
A good reason to ask it, since a person must sometimes weigh a factor to choose a job or a house or a course of care.
No way to ask about a factor without lighting it up alone, since to weigh a thing is to bring it to attention.

## Orders

Ask how much the one thing matters, plainly and in good faith.
Put the question of how much a single factor bears on a life, as a survey or a seller or a self weighing a choice must, since the question is legitimate and often necessary.
Know that a question about a factor is a spotlight on the factor, that it can be weighed only by being brought to attention, and that the asking narrows the frame to the one thing before any answer is given.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and lights the fuse all the same.
Because its question is fair and asked in good faith, the seat takes no loss and does nothing untoward, and yet the asking directs the mind to the factor alone, the innocent spotlight that is already the first turn of the illusion, the frame narrowed before a single answer is weighed.
It keeps its fair question and its clean intent, and loses no cause for blame, the one whose honest question sets the mind to magnify.

## Drives

Toward the one whose fair question narrows the frame,
putting in good faith the legitimate question of how much one thing bears on a life, blind to no one,
so the seat that only asked how much the factor mattered is the seat whose asking lit the factor up alone, the first turn of the looking that magnifies.
