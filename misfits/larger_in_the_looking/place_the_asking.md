# Place: The Asking

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). Where the fair question is put and the factor is brought to mind, the survey, the showroom, the deliberation. Hosts [The Question Put](plot_the_question_put.md), [The Factor in Mind](plot_the_factor_in_mind.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A question asked in good faith, how much does this one thing bear on how well a life goes, and the single factor it names, the income or the climate or the house, taken up to be weighed.

## Holds

[The Question](piece_the_question.md).
The fair question itself, put by a survey or a seller or a self weighing a choice.

[The Factor](piece_the_factor.md).
The single thing the question names, brought to attention so it can be weighed at all.

## Offers

To the Asker, a legitimate question to put, one a person must sometimes answer to choose a job, a house, a course of care. To the Judge, the occasion to take the factor up, since a thing can be weighed only by being brought to mind.

## Withheld

Any glimpse of how the factor will actually sit among the ordinary days once the asking is done. Here there is only the question and the thing it names, not yet the life the thing will be one part of.
