# Persona: The Dweller

## Taxonomy

[Dweller](position_dweller.md), inside [Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The archetype of the experiencing self that lives the actual days, for whom the factor is one of many things and mostly out of mind, the rest of the life the focused judgment neglects.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An ingrate blind to its own good fortune, as though it failed to enjoy the fine climate or the raise it was given, when the good is simply one of many things in a full day and not the day's whole substance.

## Action

Live the days. At [the Days](place_the_days.md), the Dweller on [Live the Days](plan_live_the_days.md) through [Dwelling](process_dwelling.md) holds [the Rest of Life](piece_the_rest_of_life.md), living the many diffuse things that fill the days the same whether the factor is present or not, the factor stepped through and rarely attended (Kahneman, Krueger, Schkade, Schwarz and Stone; Wilson and colleagues).

## Shadow

Its inattention to the factor is no ingratitude, but how a life is lived. The Dweller is not blind to its good fortune: the raise is real and the warm climate is pleasant, but a day is filled with the commute and the work and the child and the worry and the small hundred things, and the factor takes its place among them and is, most hours, simply not in mind. This is not a failure to appreciate but the plain shape of living, where no single circumstance holds the attention that a question about it commands, and the good is adapted to and folded into the ordinary. It keeps its full and various days and its honest living, and gives the factor exactly the place a life gives it, one of many, the rest of which the focused judgment never saw.

## Tell

It lived among a hundred things and gave the one its ordinary place, which the judgment, looking only at the one, mistook for neglect.
