# Piece: The Overweight

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The mechanism: the frame's size is handed over as the verdict, the factor rated as mattering far more than it will.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Focus](place_the_focus.md), carried by [Focusing](process_focusing.md), voiced by [the Judge](persona_the_judge.md): the number written down, the prediction spoken aloud, the estimate that closes the weighing.

## Load Bearing

Schkade and Kahneman (Psychological Science, 1998) found Midwesterners and Californians reporting about equal life satisfaction, yet both groups predicting that Californians must be happier, on the strength of a climate that, once named, filled the frame and produced a confident, mistaken verdict. The estimate of the frame is taken for the estimate of the life: what loomed large under attention is written down as though it will loom equally large in the living, day after ordinary day. Nothing dishonest happens at this step; the judge reports exactly what the frame showed. The frame was simply the wrong instrument for the question being asked.

## Apparent

Reads as a sound prediction, the considered answer to a fair question, given in good faith.
