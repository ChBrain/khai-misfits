# Piece: The Magnification

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The mechanism: attention does not merely locate the factor, it enlarges whatever it lands on, for as long as it lands there.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Focus](place_the_focus.md), carried by [Focusing](process_focusing.md), voiced by [the Judge](persona_the_judge.md): the factor filling the whole frame while it is being looked at, nothing else in the field of view.

## Load Bearing

Nothing in life is as important as you think it is while you are thinking about it (Kahneman): the aphorism names exactly what happens next. The factor, once brought to mind, does not sit there at its ordinary size; it swells to fill the frame, because the frame at that moment contains nothing else. This is focalism, the tendency to overweight a focal event or feature and neglect everything happening around it or after it that would otherwise compete for attention (Wilson, Wheatley, Meyers, Gilbert and Axsom, "Focalism...", JPSP, 2000). The estimate forming here is not yet a measure of the factor's weight in a life; it is a measure of how much the factor currently fills a mind, and the two are not the same thing, though in the moment of judging they feel identical.

## Apparent

Reads as clear sight of the factor's true weight, the moment of seeing it plainly for what it is worth.
