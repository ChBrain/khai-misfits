# Piece: The Small Difference

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The mechanism: measured against the days rather than against the frame, the factor settles to its true, modest size.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Days](place_the_days.md), carried by [Assessing](process_assessing.md), voiced by [the Assessor](persona_the_assessor.md): the two groups compared not on what they predicted of each other, but on what they actually reported feeling.

## Load Bearing

Schkade and Kahneman (Psychological Science, 1998) found that Midwesterners and Californians, asked to rate their own life satisfaction rather than predict a stranger's, reported about the same. The factor was not nothing: the climate is real, the income is real, the diagnosis is real, and each leaves some mark. But the mark left in the living is smaller than the mark drawn in the looking, because the living was never a frame with one thing in it. This is the bound the misfit sets and does not overrun: the focusing illusion is a documented bias in judgments made while a factor is held under attention, not a proof that the factor carries no weight at all. Cut to its true size, it is a small difference, real, present, and considerably less than it looked.

## Apparent

Reads as proof that the factor does not count at all, an equal and opposite error to the one it corrects.

## Yearbook

The factor cut to its true size, held between the looming estimate and nothing.
