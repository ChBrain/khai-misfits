# Plot: Checked Against the Days

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The judgment on record, and the days it predicted now run their course, ready at last to be checked against it.

## Action

At [the Days](place_the_days.md), [the Assessor](persona_the_assessor.md), from [the assessor's seat](position_assessor.md), on [Measure It Out](plan_measure_it_out.md) through [Assessing](process_assessing.md), holds [the Small Difference](piece_the_small_difference.md), setting the focused judgment against the lived reports.

## Stage

Read side by side, the two groups report about the same life satisfaction, the Californians no happier than the Midwesterners the earlier judgment predicted apart, and income moving the moods of actual days far less than the fixed attention foretold (Schkade and Kahneman). Every step that led here was sound on its own terms: a fair question, a weighing done the only way a mind can weigh, a looming that is the price of attending at all, and days lived fully around a factor mostly out of mind, and yet the sum of those sound steps is a prediction reliably too high. The bound holds both ways: the factor is not the looming weight the frame gave it, and it is not nothing either, since income does raise reported satisfaction somewhat and a warm climate is a genuine good. No villain: the asker asked in good faith, the judge weighed the only way a mind can, the dweller lived a full and ordinary life, and the factor is real, only overweighted against everything else that filled the days meanwhile.

## Tension

Distinct from The Worst and the Last, a retrospective memory that summarizes a lived episode by its peak and its end, whereas here no episode has yet been lived and nothing is yet remembered, only a prospective judgment inflated by the attention fixed on it. And distinct from More to Stand Still, where the felt value of a good genuinely fades as one adapts to it in the living, whereas here the effect is overjudged from the start by the attention, the adaptation itself one of the very things the focused judgment neglects. The factor real but smaller, larger in the looking than in the life.
