# Position: Dweller

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The seat of the experiencing self that lives the actual days, and marks that its inattention to the factor is no ingratitude but the plain shape of a full life, the rest the judgment never saw.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The actual days, filled with a hundred diffuse things, the work and the worry and the small ordinary hours.
A real but ordinary good in the factor, the raise or the climate that takes its place among the rest.
No room to hold the factor in constant view, since a lived day attends to what is before it and the factor is rarely before it.

## Orders

Live the days, and give the factor its ordinary place among the rest.
Live the full and various life, the commute and the work and the child and the hundred small things, and let the factor take its place among them, attended now and then and mostly not, since that is how a day is spent.
Know that this is no failure to appreciate the good, that a life gives no single circumstance the attention a question about it commands, and that the good is adapted to and folded into the ordinary as everything is.

## Loses

The looming weight the judgment gave the factor, and this is the truth of it.
Because it lives among a hundred things and gives the factor the ordinary place a full day gives it, the seat feels the factor as one of many and mostly not at all, so the weight the focused judgment predicted is simply not there in the living, lost not to ingratitude but to the plain shape of a life.
It keeps its full and various days and its honest living, and loses no cause for blame, the one who gives the factor exactly the place a life gives it.

## Drives

Toward the one for whom the factor is one of many things,
living the full days where the good takes its ordinary place among the work and the worry and the small hundred things,
so the seat that lives the life the judgment claimed to weigh gives the factor the modest place a life gives it, the rest of which the looking never saw.
