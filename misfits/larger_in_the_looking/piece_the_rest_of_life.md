## Load Bearing

Moment-to-moment mood is moved by income far less than the fixed attention of a survey question predicts; income looms large exactly when someone is made to attend to it and matters much less across the diffuse texture of an actual day (Kahneman, Krueger, Schkade, Schwarz and Stone, Science, 2006). The days are full of things that do not announce themselves for judgment: the errands, the weather that turns out not to matter as much as advertised, the friendships, the low hum of ordinary concerns, present alike whether the factor is high or low. And the factor itself is not static in the living the way it is in the frame; a person adapts to it, the way any focal change fades once it stops being the only thing looked at (Wilson, Wheatley, Meyers, Gilbert and Axsom). None of this was ignored out of carelessness. It simply was not asked about.
