# Piece: The Question

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The mechanism: to weigh a factor at all, a mind must first be turned toward it, and that turning is where the illusion begins.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Asking](place_the_asking.md), carried by [Asking](process_asking.md), voiced by [the Asker](persona_the_asker.md): a survey line, a seller's pitch, a private moment of weighing a move, each one putting the same fair question, how much does this bear on a life.

## Load Bearing

The question is not a trick. A survey that asks how income affects happiness, a seller who asks how the climate feels, a self turning over whether to take the job or make the move: none of these deceive, and none could be answered without first pointing the mind at the factor in question (Kahneman, Krueger, Schkade, Schwarz and Stone, Science, 2006). Weighing a thing requires attending to it; there is no other door in. That single act of turning attention toward the factor, ordinary and necessary as it is, is the first turn of the mechanism the rest of this misfit follows, though nothing has gone wrong yet in the asking itself.

## Apparent

Reads as a neutral request for information, the plain first step of finding something out.
