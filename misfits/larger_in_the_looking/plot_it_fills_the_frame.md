# Plot: It Fills the Frame

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The factor held in view a moment longer, and the moment does something to it that no one asked for.

## Action

At [the Focus](place_the_focus.md), [the Judge](persona_the_judge.md), from [the judge's seat](position_judge.md), through [Focusing](process_focusing.md), meets [the Magnification](piece_the_magnification.md), the factor swelling to fill the whole of a frame that, for as long as the looking lasts, holds nothing else.

## Stage

Nothing in life is as important as you think it is while you are thinking about it (Kahneman): the factor, once attended, does not sit at its ordinary size, it grows to fill the frame, because in that moment the frame contains nothing else to compete with it. This is focalism, the tendency to overweight whatever is focal and to neglect everything happening around it or after it that would otherwise share the attention (Wilson, Wheatley, Meyers, Gilbert and Axsom). What forms here is not yet a measure of the factor's weight in a life, only a measure of how much it currently fills a mind, and in the moment of judging the two feel like the same thing, though they are not. No villain: the looming is not a distortion the judge introduces, it is the ordinary behavior of attention itself, magnifying whatever it lands on for exactly as long as it lands there, the price of getting a look at anything at all.

## Tension

The factor filling the frame, mistaken, in the moment, for filling the life.
