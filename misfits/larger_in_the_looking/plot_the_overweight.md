# Plot: The Overweight

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The look completed, and a number or a verdict has to be given: this matters, and it matters this much.

## Action

At [the Focus](place_the_focus.md), [the Judge](persona_the_judge.md), from [the judge's seat](position_judge.md), through [Focusing](process_focusing.md), meets [the Overweight](piece_the_overweight.md), rating the factor to matter far more than it will.

## Stage

Schkade and Kahneman (Psychological Science, 1998) found Midwesterners and Californians reporting about equal life satisfaction, yet both groups predicting the Californians happier, the climate that filled the frame under questioning handed over as though it would fill the days the same way, though the lived days are moved by it little. The estimate of the frame is taken for the estimate of the life: what loomed while attended is written down as though it will loom just as large in the ordinary run of tomorrows. No villain: the judge weighs as honestly and as carefully as a mind can weigh, and cannot correct the overweight by weighing harder, since weighing harder is attending more, and attending more is looming more, so the very diligence brought to the question deepens the error it means to avoid.

## Tension

A verdict reached in good faith and given with care, systematically too high.
