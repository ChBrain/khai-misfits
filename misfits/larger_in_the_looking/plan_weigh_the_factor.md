# Plan: Weigh the Factor

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the judge's seat](position_judge.md), inside [Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Judge](position_judge.md)

## Direction

Estimate how much the single thing bears on the life by bringing it to attention and holding it in view, since there is no other way a mind gauges importance than by attending to the thing. Know that a thing held in view looms, that your estimate is of how much the factor fills the mind while attended and not how much it will fill the life, and that the overweight is the price of weighing at all and not a fault you could have avoided.

## Orders

([The Judge](persona_the_judge.md) takes up [the Factor](piece_the_factor.md) through [Focusing](process_focusing.md) at [the Asking](place_the_asking.md), bringing the single thing to mind, and meets [the Magnification](piece_the_magnification.md) and [the Overweight](piece_the_overweight.md) at [the Focus](place_the_focus.md).)

## Implementation

([Focusing](process_focusing.md) at [the Asking](place_the_asking.md) takes up [the Factor](piece_the_factor.md), because a mind gauges importance only by holding the thing in view, so the factor looms as [the Magnification](piece_the_magnification.md) and is rated too high as [the Overweight](piece_the_overweight.md) at [the Focus](place_the_focus.md) (Kahneman; Schkade and Kahneman).)

## Targets

- [ ] the factor is brought to mind and weighed, the only way a mind gauges importance
- [ ] it is seen that a thing held in view looms for as long as it is held
- [ ] it is understood that the estimate is of how much the factor fills the mind, not the life
- [ ] it is owned that the overweight is the price of weighing at all and not a fault in the judging
