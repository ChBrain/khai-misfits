## Load Bearing

Once named, the factor is what the mind has to work with: the raise, the coastline, the extra bedroom, the test result. There is no way to judge how much a thing matters without bringing that thing to mind, and bringing it to mind is the only instrument a judge has (Kahneman). Schkade and Kahneman (Psychological Science, 1998) asked people to rate how a single circumstance, living in California instead of the Midwest, bears on life satisfaction; the climate is a real thing, felt on real days, and naming it for judgment is the only way to find out what someone thinks it is worth. The factor, alone in the frame, is doing nothing wrong by being there. It is where the weighing has to start.
