# Place: The Days

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The actual lived life, where the factor is one of many diffuse things and the real difference it makes is small. Hosts [The Rest of It](plot_the_rest_of_it.md), [Checked Against the Days](plot_checked_against_the_days.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An ordinary run of days, the income earned but not attended most hours, the climate stepped through on the way to the day's real concerns, the factor present and almost never in the foreground.

## Holds

[The Rest of Life](piece_the_rest_of_life.md).
The many diffuse things that fill the days the same whether the factor is present or not, out of focus in the judging and thick underfoot in the living.

[The Small Difference](piece_the_small_difference.md).
The modest, real effect the factor turns out to make once it is set against how the days were actually lived.

## Offers

To the Dweller, the whole of an ordinary life to attend to, in which the factor takes its ordinary, unfocused place. To the Assessor, the lived reports to set against the focused prediction, the reckoning ground where the true size shows.

## Withheld

The narrowed frame's clarity. Nothing here fills the whole of attention the way the factor did at [The Focus](place_the_focus.md); the days keep no single thing in view long enough to let it loom.
