# Plot: The Rest of It

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The verdict delivered, and somewhere past the asking a life keeps being lived, unaware that a number has just been written down about it.

## Action

At [the Days](place_the_days.md), [the Dweller](persona_the_dweller.md), from [the dweller's seat](position_dweller.md), on [Live the Days](plan_live_the_days.md) through [Dwelling](process_dwelling.md), holds [the Rest of Life](piece_the_rest_of_life.md), the many diffuse things that fill the days the same whether the factor is present or not.

## Stage

The commute, the work, the child, the worry, the small hundred things: these fill an ordinary day whether or not the raise came through or the climate is fine, and the factor, once folded into that day, is stepped through rather than dwelt on, attended now and then and mostly not (Kahneman, Krueger, Schkade, Schwarz and Stone). The factor itself also changes in the living in a way the frame never showed: a good is adapted to, its felt charge fading as it becomes ordinary, so even the days it touches it touches less as they accumulate (Wilson, Wheatley, Meyers, Gilbert and Axsom). None of this was visible from the frame, where the factor stood alone with nothing to be diffused among and no time passing in which to be adapted to. No villain: the dweller's inattention is no ingratitude, it is the plain shape of how a day gets spent, no single circumstance holding the attention that a question about it once commanded.

## Tension

The rest of the life is the very thing the focused judgment crowded out, and it is most of what the days are made of.
