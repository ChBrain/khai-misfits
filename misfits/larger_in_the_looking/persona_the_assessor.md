# Persona: The Assessor

## Taxonomy

[Assessor](position_assessor.md), inside [Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The archetype of the one who sets the judgment against the lived reality, measures the small real difference the factor makes, and holds the effect to its bound, not nothing but smaller than the looking gave it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A cynic who says nothing matters, as though measuring the factor small were denying it counts at all, when the factor is real and the point is only that it is smaller than the focused judgment gives it.

## Action

Measure it out. At [the Days](place_the_days.md), the Assessor on [Measure It Out](plan_measure_it_out.md) through [Assessing](process_assessing.md) holds [the Small Difference](piece_the_small_difference.md), setting the judgment against the lived reality and finding the two groups reporting about the same satisfaction, the factor mattering far less in the living than in the looking (Schkade and Kahneman).

## Shadow

It measures the factor small and must not measure it to nothing. The Assessor is no cynic: reading the lived reports rather than the focused prediction, it finds that the two Californias come out about even, that income moves the moods of actual days far less than the fixed attention foretold, and it names the difference small and real. What it sees is honest. But it holds its own bound: the factor is not nothing, income does raise reported satisfaction somewhat and a warm climate is a genuine good, so the finding is that the attended factor is overweighted against everything else, not that it fails to count. It keeps its true measure and its held bound, and names a real but smaller effect without declaring that nothing matters, the one who cuts the factor to its size and no further.

## Tell

It set the judgment against the days and found the difference small, and called it small and real, neither the looming weight nor nothing.
