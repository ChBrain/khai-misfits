# Position: Judge

## Taxonomy

[Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The seat of the one who weighs the factor, and marks that to weigh is to attend and to attend is to magnify, so the overweight is the price of judging and not a fault in it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A factor to weigh, and the task of gauging how much it bears on a life.
The only method a mind has, to bring the thing to attention and hold it in view.
No way to weigh without magnifying, since a thing held in view looms for as long as it is held.

## Orders

Weigh the factor, bring it to mind and gauge its weight.
Estimate how much the single thing bears on the life by bringing it to attention and holding it in view, since there is no other way a mind gauges importance than by attending to the thing.
Know that a thing held in view looms, that your estimate is of how much the factor fills the mind while attended and not how much it will fill the life, and that the overweight is the price of weighing at all and not a fault you could have avoided.

## Loses

Its true estimate, though it weighed the only way it could.
Because to gauge the factor it must attend to it, and attention magnifies what it lands on, the seat returns a weight too high, an estimate of the frame taken for the estimate of the life, and it cannot correct this by weighing more carefully, since more careful weighing is more attention and more attention is more looming.
It keeps its honest weighing and its only method, and loses no cause for blame, the mind that magnifies exactly by attending.

## Drives

Toward the one whose only method is its error,
weighing the factor the sole way a mind can, by holding it in view, where it looms,
so the seat that judges as honestly and carefully as it can overweights the factor every time, magnified by the very attention that lets it judge at all.
