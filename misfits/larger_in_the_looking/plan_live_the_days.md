# Plan: Live the Days

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the dweller's seat](position_dweller.md), inside [Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Dweller](position_dweller.md)

## Direction

Live the full and various life, the commute and the work and the child and the hundred small things, and let the factor take its place among them, attended now and then and mostly not, since that is how a day is spent. Know that this is no failure to appreciate the good, that a life gives no single circumstance the attention a question about it commands, and that the good is adapted to and folded into the ordinary as everything is.
