# Persona: The Asker

## Taxonomy

[Asker](position_asker.md), inside [Larger in the Looking](play_larger_in_the_looking.md). The archetype of the one who puts the question, a survey or a seller or a self weighing a choice, and by asking how much a factor matters directs the mind to it, the innocent first turn of the illusion.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A manipulator who plants a fixation, as though asking about a thing were a trick to inflate it, when the question is fair and the attention it draws is only what any real question asks.

## Action

Ask how much. At [the Asking](place_the_asking.md), the Asker on [Ask How Much](plan_ask_how_much.md) through [Asking](process_asking.md) holds [the Question](piece_the_question.md), putting the fair question of how much one thing bears on how well a life goes, and by the asking directing the mind to the factor (Kahneman, Krueger, Schkade, Schwarz and Stone).

## Shadow

Its question is fair, and the fairness is what makes it work. The Asker is no manipulator: to ask how much a thing matters to a life is a legitimate question, one a person must sometimes answer to choose a job or a house or a course of care, and the survey or the seller or the deliberating self asks it in good faith. But a question about a factor is a spotlight on the factor: it can be weighed only by being brought to attention, and the asking brings it, so the innocent question is already the first turn of the illusion, the frame narrowed to the one thing asked about before a single answer is given. It keeps its fair question and its clean intent, and cannot ask how much a thing matters without lighting it up alone, the one whose honest question sets the mind to magnify.

## Tell

It only asked how much the one thing mattered, a fair question, and the asking was already the light that made the thing loom.
