# Persona: The Believer

## Taxonomy

[Believer](position_believer.md), inside [Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md). The archetype of the one who reads the unanimous verdict, takes each agreeing voice as another confirmation, and grows more certain the cleaner the agreement becomes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A rigorous mind, weighing the evidence and following it, its confidence growing with the count of independent confirmations exactly as sound reasoning says it should, the very picture of a careful judge persuaded by the weight of agreement.

## Action

Count the agreement. At [the Bench](place_the_bench.md), the Believer on [Count the Agreement](plan_count_the_agreement.md) through [Corroborating](process_corroborating.md) builds [the Corroboration](piece_the_corroboration.md), tallying each agreeing voice as one more confirmation, then at [the Review](place_the_review.md) meets [the False Certainty](piece_the_false_certainty.md), the overwhelming confidence just as the ground for it falls (Gunn et al.).

## Shadow

Its rigour is sound for the wrong case. The Believer is not careless or credulous: to count each independent confirmation as adding weight is exactly right when the judgments really are independent, and that is the rule a rigorous mind should follow. But it cannot tell from within whether the agreement it counts is the convergence of separate minds or the alignment of them by one hidden cause, and past a point the perfect agreement is better explained by the cause than by the truth, so the confidence it correctly builds rests on ground that has fallen away. It follows the evidence faithfully, and is led by its own rigour to be certain and wrong.

## Tell

It grows more sure the more who agree, following the right rule blind to whether the agreement is independent or tilted.
