# Plan: Say What I See

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the witness's seat](position_witness.md), inside [Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Witness](position_witness.md)

## Direction

Judge the question independently and in good faith, and return the answer you find, adding your voice to the verdict, because that is what an honest witness does and the scatter of many such honest errors is the signature of a real consensus. Know that a shared error you cannot see may tilt your judgment the same way it tilts every other, so the agreement you add to may be the alignment of many minds by one hidden cause and not their convergence on the truth, and your very honesty is what will make the false agreement look like proof.

## Orders

([The Witness](persona_the_witness.md) returns [the Perfect Agreement](piece_the_perfect_agreement.md) through [Judging](process_judging.md) at [the Bench](place_the_bench.md), its honest independent judgment fallen into line with the rest under a tilt it cannot see.)

## Implementation

([Judging](process_judging.md) at [the Bench](place_the_bench.md) returns [the Perfect Agreement](piece_the_perfect_agreement.md), because each honest witness reports what it finds, and a shared error leans all the same way, so the judgments fall into line rather than scatter.)

## Targets

- [ ] the question is judged honestly and independently, and the answer returned to the verdict
- [ ] the judging is understood as good-faith and reliable, exactly what an honest witness should do
- [ ] it is seen that a shared unseen error may tilt the judgment the same way as every other
- [ ] it is owned that the very honesty of the agreement is what makes the false verdict look like proof
