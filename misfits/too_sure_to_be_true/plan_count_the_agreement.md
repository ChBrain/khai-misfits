# Plan: Count the Agreement

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the believer's seat](position_believer.md), inside [Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Believer](position_believer.md)

## Direction

Weigh each agreeing voice as one more independent confirmation and follow the evidence where it leads, because that is exactly what a rigorous mind should do when the judgments are independent. Know that you cannot tell from within whether the agreement is the convergence of separate minds or their alignment by one hidden cause, that past a point a perfect agreement is better explained by the cause than the truth, so the confidence you correctly build may rest on ground that has fallen away.
