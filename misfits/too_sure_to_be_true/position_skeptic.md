# Position: Skeptic

## Taxonomy

[Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md). The seat of the one who distrusts the too-clean verdict and seeks dissent, and marks that the doubt is costly, runs only on some questions, and is a weight and not a proof.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A distrust of the too-clean verdict, the sense that an agreement can be too perfect for honest independent error.
The means to test it: a dissent sought, a line-up rebuilt, an instrument rechecked, a shared assumption named.
A clear view of the price, the effort and time and comfort spent, and the limit, that the test weighs a chance and never removes it.

## Orders

Test it for independence, and distrust the cleanest verdict most.
Seek a dissenting judgment, rebuild the line-up, recheck the instrument, hunt for the assumption held in common, to tell a real consensus from a correlated error, since the count of agreeing voices alone cannot.
Know that this spends real effort and time and the comfort of a clean answer, that it can be run on only some questions and never removes uncertainty entirely, and that its alarm is a weight on the chance of a hidden cause and not a proof that the verdict is wrong.

## Loses

The comfort of the clean answer, spent to test what the agreement was worth.
Because it will not take a perfect agreement for proof, the seat gives up the ease of a settled verdict and pays in effort and time to test an independence the count cannot show, buying a truer confidence at the price of the reassurance unanimity offers.
It keeps its clear doubt and its honest test and loses no cause for blame, the one who distrusts the cleanest verdict most and pays to find whether it was ever independent.

## Drives

Toward the one who distrusts the too-clean verdict and pays to test it,
seeking dissent and hunting the common cause to tell a real consensus from a tilted one, costly and against the grain,
so the seat that alone can catch the false agreement catches it only by spending the comfort of the clean answer, and only so far as the test can reach.
