# Pitch: When None Dissent

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that when not one of many honest independent judges dissents, the very absence of dissent is the mark against the verdict.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Grave and measured, the courtroom and the laboratory told without blame. Honest observers each judging the same question in good faith, and a reader taking their agreement, sensibly, as the strongest of proofs. Nothing here is dishonesty or collusion; the reading is the right one when the errors are truly independent.

## Undertow

Truly independent honest judgments should scatter, a spread of near misses around the truth, so a perfect agreement is better explained by a shared systematic error, a biased line-up, a miscalibrated instrument, a common assumption, than by truth plus honest noise, which would have left some dissent (Gunn et al.). Past a point each added agreeing voice should lower confidence, not raise it.

## Nerve

That the cleaner the verdict, the stronger the case against it, since a too-perfect agreement betrays the hidden tilt that no one in the chorus can hear from inside. No one is a villain; the shared error is unseen and unintended, held against its limit, since the alarm is a Bayesian weight on hidden correlation and not a proof of error, genuine evidence being able to command wide agreement.

## Echo

When honest judgments should scatter and instead all agree, doubt the agreement, for it is too clean for chance to have made it.
