# Position: Witness

## Taxonomy

[Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md). The seat of one of the many honest independent judges, and marks that its good-faith agreement, tilted the same way as every other, proves the hidden cause and not the truth it is taken for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An honest judgment, made independently and in good faith, its own small error and all.
No sight of the shared error beneath it, the tilt it carries in common with every other judge.
A verdict to add its voice to, one more among the many the reader will count.

## Orders

Say what you see, honestly and on your own, as a reliable witness should.
Judge the question independently and in good faith, and return the answer you find, adding your voice to the verdict, because that is what an honest witness does and the scatter of many such honest errors is the signature of a real consensus.
Know that a shared error you cannot see may tilt your judgment the same way it tilts every other, so the agreement you add to may be the alignment of many minds by one hidden cause and not their convergence on the truth, and your very honesty is what will make the false agreement look like proof.

## Loses

The independence its agreement is credited with, lost to a tilt it never saw.
Because a shared systematic error leans its honest judgment the same way as every other, the seat adds its voice to an agreement that corroborates only the hidden cause, so the reliability it is honoured for is the very thing that makes the too-clean verdict persuasive.
It keeps its honest judgment and its good faith and loses no cause for blame, a reliable witness whose agreement proves the tilt and not the truth.

## Drives

Toward the honest judge whose agreement means less than it seems,
judging independently and in good faith while a shared error tilts it the same way as all the rest, blind to the tilt,
so the seat whose very honesty adds weight to the verdict adds weight only to the hidden cause, corroborating a truth it never confirmed.
