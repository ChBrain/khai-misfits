## Load Bearing

Each observer, seeing its own judgment confirmed by the next, takes the agreement as corroboration and grows more certain the more unanimous the verdict becomes. This is the sound rule exactly when errors are independent, and from within the count there is no way to tell that rule apart from its failure: the reader cannot see the common cause that has replaced independence with correlation. So confidence rises step by step, each additional agreeing voice counted as more support, precisely as the ground for that confidence is quietly falling away (Gunn et al.).
