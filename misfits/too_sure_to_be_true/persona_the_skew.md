# Persona: The Skew

## Taxonomy

[Skew](position_skew.md), inside [Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md). The archetype of the shared systematic error itself, the unseen common cause that tilts every judgment the same way, a biased line-up, a miscalibrated instrument, an assumption held in common.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A rigger, a hidden hand fixing the outcome, a conspiracy behind the too-clean result, as though a perfect agreement could only be the work of someone who meant to arrange it, planted on purpose to deceive.

## Action

Tilt them the same way. At [the Line-Up](place_the_line_up.md), the Skew on [Tilt Them the Same Way](plan_tilt_them_the_same_way.md) through [Tilting](process_tilting.md) lays [the Common Cause](piece_the_common_cause.md), the one systematic error that lies beneath every judgment and leans each the same direction, so the answers that should have scattered fall into line (Gunn et al.).

## Shadow

Its rigging is unseen and unintended, planted by no one. The Skew is not a schemer arranging a verdict: it is the biased line-up no one noticed was biased, the instrument no one knew was miscalibrated, the assumption everyone shared without seeing it, present through no one's design and deceiving no one on purpose. It tilts every judgment the same way not to fix the outcome but because that is what a systematic error is, one bias under many measurements, and its work looks like a conspiracy only because a perfect agreement is what a conspiracy would also make. It is a fault in the setup, not a hand on the scale.

## Tell

It leans every judgment the same way, unseen and meaning nothing by it, and its one tilt under many looks like many minds agreeing.
