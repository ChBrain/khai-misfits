# Persona: The Skeptic

## Taxonomy

[Skeptic](position_skeptic.md), inside [Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md). The archetype of the one who distrusts the too-clean verdict and seeks dissent, testing whether the judgments were truly independent and hunting for the common cause, and who marks that the doubt is costly and its alarm a weight and not a proof.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A contrarian for its own sake, a spoiler who cannot accept a settled answer and picks at a consensus everyone else is satisfied with, as though doubt of a unanimous verdict were mere perversity or a refusal to be convinced.

## Action

Test it for independence. At [the Review](place_the_review.md), the Skeptic on [Test It for Independence](plan_test_it_for_independence.md) through [Testing](process_testing.md) holds [the Dissent Sought](piece_the_dissent_sought.md), seeking a dissenting judgment, rebuilding the line-up, checking the instrument, hunting the shared assumption, to tell a real consensus from a correlated error (Gunn et al.).

## Shadow

Its contrarianism is the one real check, and it is dearly bought. The Skeptic is not doubting for the sake of doubting: a perfect agreement is exactly what a hidden common cause produces, so the only way to tell a true consensus from a tilted one is to test the independence the count cannot show. But this spends real effort and time and the comfort of a clean answer, it can be run on only some questions and never removes uncertainty entirely, and it asks everyone to distrust the very agreement that feels like proof. Its doubt is not perversity but the correct response to an agreement too clean for independent error, held to a weight on the chance of a hidden cause and not a proof that the verdict is wrong.

## Tell

It distrusts the cleanest verdict most, and pays in effort and lost comfort to test whether the agreement was ever independent.
