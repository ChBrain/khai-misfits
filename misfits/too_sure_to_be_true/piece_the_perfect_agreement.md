# Piece: The Perfect Agreement

## Taxonomy

[Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md). The unanimity that appears where a scatter was expected.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Bench](place_the_bench.md), carried by [Judging](process_judging.md), voiced by [the Witness](persona_the_witness.md): the verdict where every independent look somehow lands the same.

## Load Bearing

The honest, independent judgments have fallen into line: the verdict with no dissent, too perfect for independent error to have produced. Truth plus honest noise would have left some scatter (Condorcet); its absence is not confirmation but a mark. Past a point the very perfection of the agreement is better explained by a shared systematic error than by the truth (Gunn et al.), and ancient precedent already knew the shape of it: at the Sanhedrin, a unanimous capital conviction acquits, because unanimity where dissent should exist is itself suspect.

## Apparent

Overwhelming confirmation, the strongest form the evidence could take.
