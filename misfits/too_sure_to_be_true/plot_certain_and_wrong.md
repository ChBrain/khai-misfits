# Plot: Certain and Wrong

## Taxonomy

[Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The corroboration has been mounting toward proof, and the count of agreeing voices is read as the strongest case there is.

## Action

At [the Review](place_the_review.md), [the Believer](persona_the_believer.md) meets [the False Certainty](piece_the_false_certainty.md), the overwhelming confidence placed in the conclusion just as the ground for it falls away.

## Stage

This is the harm. Past a point, the perfect agreement is better explained by the common cause than by the truth, so the more it agrees the less it should be trusted, and the cleaner the verdict, the stronger the case against it (Gunn et al.). The certainty and its collapse arrive together, in the same unanimous verdict, and nothing inside the count marks the turn. This is distinct from three neighboring failures it can be mistaken for. It is distinct from Information Cascade, where the judgments are not independent to begin with, each later voice herding by copying the ones before it; here the judgments were independent, and it is a common cause, not copying, that aligned them. It is distinct from Closing Ranks, where unanimity is manufactured by suppressing dissent that existed and was silenced; here there is no dissent to hide, only honest witnesses whose independent judgments were tilted the same way before any of them spoke. And it is distinct from The File Drawer, where the tell is what is missing, the suppressed null results never shown; here the tell is the opposite, the concordance of what is present, the very completeness and agreement of the record in hand. No villain authors this loss. Each observer judged honestly and independently, no one colluded, the shared error was unseen and unintended, and reading agreement as corroboration is the right rule exactly when errors are independent; the loss belongs to no one.

## Tension

The certainty highest exactly where its ground is weakest.
