# Persona: The Witness

## Taxonomy

[Witness](position_witness.md), inside [Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md). The archetype of one of the many honest independent judges, whose good-faith judgment is tilted the same way as every other by an unseen common cause, so the answers that should have scattered fall into line.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A reliable corroborator, one more honest voice confirming what all the others found, its agreement adding weight to the verdict, as though each independent witness saying the same thing could only mean the thing were true.

## Action

Say what I see. At [the Line-Up](place_the_line_up.md), the Witness meets [the Scatter](piece_the_scatter.md), the spread its honest error should show, then at [the Bench](place_the_bench.md) on [Say What I See](plan_say_what_i_see.md) through [Judging](process_judging.md) returns [the Perfect Agreement](piece_the_perfect_agreement.md), its judgment fallen into line with the rest under a tilt it cannot see (Gunn et al.).

## Shadow

Its agreement corroborates nothing it is taken to. The Witness is not a lying or colluding voice: it judges honestly and independently and in good faith, exactly as a reliable witness should. But a shared error it cannot see, a line-up built so one face stands out, an instrument miscalibrated the same for all, tilts its judgment the same way it tilts every other, so the agreement it adds to is not the convergence of separate minds on the truth but the alignment of separate minds by one hidden cause. It corroborates in good faith, and its very honesty is what makes the false agreement look like proof.

## Tell

It says honestly what it sees, and what it sees is tilted the same way as every other, so its agreement proves only the tilt.
