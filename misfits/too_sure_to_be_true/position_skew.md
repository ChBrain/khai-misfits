## Orders

It keeps its blamelessness and its lack of intent and loses no cause for blame, an unseen error that deceives without a deceiver.

leaning every judgment the same way, unseen and unintended, a fault in the setup and not a hand on the scale,
so the seat that plants no conspiracy makes the very agreement a conspiracy would make, one tilt under many mistaken for many minds as one.
