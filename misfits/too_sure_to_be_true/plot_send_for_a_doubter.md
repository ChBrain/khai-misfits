# Plot: Send for a Doubter

## Taxonomy

[Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The verdict is too clean to trust, and something must be done that the agreement itself will not volunteer.

## Action

At [the Review](place_the_review.md), [the Skeptic](persona_the_skeptic.md) from [the Skeptic's seat](position_skeptic.md) on [Test It for Independence](plan_test_it_for_independence.md) through [Testing](process_testing.md) holds [the Dissent Sought](piece_the_dissent_sought.md), the deliberate seeking of dissent and testing of independence and hunting of the common cause.

## Stage

This is the remedy, and the bound it is held to. The test is costly: it spends effort, time, and the comfort of a clean answer, and it can only be run on some questions, not all of them, since a shared line-up or instrument or assumption has to be found and checked before it can be ruled out (Gunn et al.). Sending for a doubter, asking whether the witnesses truly judged apart, hunting for the thumb on the scale, is the only way to tell a real consensus from a correlated error, and it is bought at a real price rather than read off the verdict for free. And the bound holds against overcorrection: the alarm this misfit raises is a Bayesian weight on the chance of a hidden common cause, not a proof that the agreement is wrong. Genuine strong evidence is able to command wide, even near-unanimous, agreement, and unanimity is not by itself a verdict of error, only a reason to look harder at where it came from (Gunn et al.). No villain stands in the way of the doubter, and none is needed to explain why the test is rarely run.

## Tension

The way out is real but dearly bought, and the alarm is a weight and not a proof.
