# Piece: The False Certainty

## Taxonomy

[Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md). The harm: overwhelming confidence delivered exactly as its ground gives way.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Review](place_the_review.md), met by [the Believer](persona_the_believer.md): the file closed on a verdict nobody disputed.

## Load Bearing

Past a point the perfect agreement is better explained by the common cause than by the truth, so the more a verdict agrees with itself the less it should be trusted, and the confidence placed in the conclusion is highest exactly where its ground is weakest: the cleaner the verdict, the stronger the case against it (Gunn et al.).

This is distinct from three neighboring failures that can look alike from outside. In an **Information Cascade**, each observer copies the visible choices of those ahead of it and discards its own private signal, so the agreement is manufactured by herding and the judgments are not independent at all. Here, by contrast, the judgments genuinely are independent, each honestly its own; it is their improbable agreement, given that independence, that indicts a shared bias. In **Closing Ranks**, unanimity is manufactured by loyalty and the self-silencing of private dissent, an illusion laid over a suppressed disagreement. Here there is no dissent to hide and no coordination to enforce it, only honest judgments aligned by a common cause none of them can see. In **The File Drawer**, null results are suppressed so the visible record over-represents the positive, and the tell is the studies that are missing. Here every judgment is present and every one of them is honest; the tell is not what is absent but the very concordance of what is there.
