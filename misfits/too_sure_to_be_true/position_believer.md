# Position: Believer

## Taxonomy

[Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md). The seat of the one who reads the unanimous verdict and grows more certain the cleaner it is, and marks that its rule is right for independence and blind to the tilt.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A verdict to read, the agreeing voices of the many laid before it.
A sound rule: to count each independent confirmation as adding weight, and grow more certain the more agree.
No way to tell, from within, whether the agreement it counts is independent or aligned by a hidden cause.

## Orders

Count the agreement, and let your confidence grow with the count of those who agree.
Weigh each agreeing voice as one more independent confirmation and follow the evidence where it leads, because that is exactly what a rigorous mind should do when the judgments are independent.
Know that you cannot tell from within whether the agreement is the convergence of separate minds or their alignment by one hidden cause, that past a point a perfect agreement is better explained by the cause than the truth, so the confidence you correctly build may rest on ground that has fallen away.

## Loses

The truth of its conclusion, lost to a rule sound only for the case it cannot check.
Because it counts each agreeing voice as an independent confirmation and cannot see the shared cause that aligned them, the seat grows most certain exactly where the agreement is most tilted, and its own rigour carries it to a conclusion whose unanimity is the mark against it.
It keeps its sound rule and its faithful reasoning and loses no cause for blame, a careful judge led by its own rigour to be certain and wrong.

## Drives

Toward the reader who grows surest where it should grow least,
counting each agreeing voice as confirmation by a rule that is right for independence and blind to the tilt,
so the seat whose faithful reasoning builds confidence with the count builds it fastest where the hidden cause has aligned them, certain in proportion to the error.
