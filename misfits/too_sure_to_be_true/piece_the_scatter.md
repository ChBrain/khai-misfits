# Piece: The Scatter

## Taxonomy

[Too Sure to Be True](play_too_sure_to_be_true.md). The spread that truly independent honest judgments should show.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Line-Up](place_the_line_up.md), met by [the Witness](persona_the_witness.md): the array of separate honest looks, each free to land where its own error takes it.

## Load Bearing

Many separate minds, each making its own honest error, produce a spread of near misses around the truth: some high, some low, rarely two alike. That scatter is not noise to be tidied away, it is the signature of independence, the shape a real consensus of separate minds would take if left to its own errors (Condorcet's independence assumption). Where the scatter is missing, something has replaced it.

## Apparent

Messy disagreement, a noisy read to be averaged and forgotten.

## Yearbook

Met by [the Witness](persona_the_witness.md) at [the Line-Up](place_the_line_up.md), dramatized in [Near Misses](plot_near_misses.md). It is the spread that should have been there; its absence is [The Perfect Agreement](piece_the_perfect_agreement.md).
