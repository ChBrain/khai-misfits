# Position: Payer

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). The seat that buys by the dose because that is what buying is, and the only one that could end the trap.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A budget it must account for, a tariff keyed to units dispensed, and an invoice it can check against the dispensing record.
A principle that is right almost everywhere: do not pay for what is not used.
One alternative instrument, a payment for access rather than volume, and no way to price it confidently.

## Orders

Get value for the budget: reimburse per course, refuse to pay for stock nobody took, and be able to account for every pound.
Consider, when it is put, a subscription that pays whether or not a single course is dispensed.
Accept what the seat does: the thrift that protects patients from shelf-warmers is the thrift that makes the most valuable compound unfundable.

## Loses

Its own principle, since ending the trap requires paying for a medicine precisely because nobody is taking it, which is the thing the seat's whole discipline exists to prevent.
Because nobody can price a compound decades before the resistance it is being kept for arrives, and because the scheme is hardest to defend in exactly the years when it is working, the seat cannot buy certainty along with the instrument.
It keeps a defensible ledger and loses the drug that was never made, which appears in no ledger at all.

## Drives

Toward the only actor who can end it and the one for whom ending it is a reversal,
paying for what is used, then being asked to pay for what is not, and finding the second impossible to defend at a budget hearing,
so the correction exists, works, and is a standing public purchase of something nobody hopes to need.
