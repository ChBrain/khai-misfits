## Action

The scene closes with worth and earnings pointing in opposite directions, arrived at without any party behaving badly and without the resistance forecasts being disputed at any point (Outterson and colleagues).

## Stage

Both halves are correct and neither can be given up. The stewardship is right, the payment rule is right, and the board is not proposing that either should change, only reading what the two of them come to when set beside each other.

## Tension

The comparison runs sharpest against the compounds most worth having, since the better the drug the more carefully it is withheld, so the market does not merely underprovide antibacterials but underprovides the best of them first, and the relation would hold just as firmly for a rival firm doing the same sum.
