## Load Bearing

Remove it, by using the new agent freely, and the market is healthy and the drug is spent in three years, which is a worse outcome by a wide margin and real harm to people not yet in the room.
It bears the load as the first half of the inversion and as the half that cannot be traded. Because the drug's worth is exactly the useful life the restraint preserves, the only lever that would keep its market alive is the one thing the seat must not pull, so the trap cannot be relieved from this side by anybody behaving better.
