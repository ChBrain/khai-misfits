# Process: Paying

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). Borne at [The Counter](place_the_counter.md), precipitated by [The Payer](persona_the_payer.md) reimbursing what was dispensed, in [Paid by the Dose](plot_paid_by_the_dose.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A public budget that must be accounted for, and the principle that one does not pay for what is not used.

## Direction

From a licensed product toward a revenue line: reimbursement per course, invoices checked against the dispensing record, and nothing paid for stock nobody took.

## Lever

That the rule is right almost everywhere and was designed to protect patients from paying for shelf-warmers, so the arrangement is not a hostility to anyone and cannot be abandoned in the general case.

## Echo

[The Tariff](piece_the_tariff.md), the second half of the inversion, supplied by ordinary thrift and defensible line by line in a ledger that has no entry for a drug that was never made.
