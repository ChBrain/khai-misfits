# Piece: The Exit

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). The portfolio decision: the antibacterial programme closed, the novel-mechanism projects first.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Boardroom](place_the_boardroom.md), as an ordinary fiduciary judgement explicable to anyone in a sentence (Projan).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the inversion is an interesting fact that costs nothing: a firm that stayed in antibacterials despite the arithmetic would keep the pipeline full and the play would have no consequence.
It bears the load as the point where the relation becomes a missing drug. Because the novel mechanisms are the dearest to pursue, they are cut first, so the exit is worst exactly where the science is most needed, and because a rival faces the same arithmetic there is nobody to step into the gap.

## Apparent

Discipline, and it is discipline: money put where it returns, by a board that would be criticised for doing otherwise and that says honestly it wishes the work paid.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Move the Programme](plot_move_the_programme.md), a loss on both sides of the table.
It works together with [The Empty Shelf](piece_the_empty_shelf.md), the decision and the address the bill arrives at.
