## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing a drug whose correct use is large, and the market works exactly as markets are supposed to: usefulness and revenue point the same way and the pipeline fills.
It bears the load as the whole mechanism, and as the reason the failure is selective. Because worth is measured in preserved years and revenue in doses sold, the comparison runs sharpest against the compounds most worth having, so the market does not merely underprovide antibacterials, it underprovides the best of them first.

## Apparent

An unfortunate fact about a particular class of drug, and it will be described that way in every account of it, which understates it: this is not a quirk of one product but the shape of the whole category.
