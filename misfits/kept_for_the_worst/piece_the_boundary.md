# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). The condition and the hedge: what the trap needs, and what separating the reward from the volume actually requires.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Tariff](place_the_tariff.md), stated with the correction rather than after it, so the instrument is priced rather than recommended.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play overreaches into a claim it cannot support, that pharmaceutical markets generally fail, that the firms which left acted improperly, or that stewardship is a mistake, none of which the source says and none of which the house will stage.
It bears the load as the limit that keeps this a design fault. Because the trap needs a drug whose correct use is minimal and a reward tied to volume, it does not reach a medicine people are meant to take, and because it lifts wherever those two are separated, it is a repairable arrangement rather than a verdict on markets or on the industry.

## Apparent

A qualification, and an easy one to skip: the sort of hedge that reads as caution and is in fact what separates a fixable market design from a denunciation of everybody in it.
