# Plot: Reserve It

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Formulary](place_the_formulary.md), on the day a new agent against a resistant organism is licensed.

## Action

Through [Reserving](process_reserving.md), [the Steward](persona_the_steward.md) on [Keep It in Reserve](plan_keep_it_in_reserve.md) sets [the Reserve](piece_the_reserve.md): the agent is restricted at once, the old generic is tried first, and the new one is held for the isolates nothing else will touch. Every dose withheld is counted as useful life preserved, because what this drug is worth is the years before resistance catches it. The scene closes with correct annual sales in the low hundreds of courses and a hospital that used it freely shown to be doing real harm to people not yet in the room.

## Stage

This is the guidance and the guidance is right. Nothing here is caution, rationing or timidity, and a committee that decided otherwise would be spending in three years a drug that could have lasted fifteen.

## Tension

Preservation and market shrinkage are one act rather than two, so the only lever that would keep the product's market alive is using more of it, which is precisely what this seat must not do, and the trap cannot be relieved from this side by anybody behaving better than they already are.
