# Plot: Nothing Left to Reach For

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Bedside](place_the_bedside.md), years later, and an isolate that answers to nothing on the list.

## Action

Through [Exiting](process_exiting.md), [the Steward](persona_the_steward.md) on [Keep It in Reserve](plan_keep_it_in_reserve.md) meets [the Empty Shelf](piece_the_empty_shelf.md): a susceptibility report, a list of remaining options with one line on it, and the line is blank. The drug that is missing did not fail in trials and was not withdrawn for harm. It was never made. The scene closes with the reason it was never made being that everybody agreed it had to be saved.

## Stage

It will be recorded as a difficult case: a resistant organism, an unlucky patient, and no decision in the notes that anyone would take back. Each of those descriptions is true and none of them reaches the cause.

## Tension

There is no withdrawal to reverse, no failure to investigate and no culprit to find, and the seat standing here is the same seat that restricted the agent correctly a decade ago, with no vantage joining the two ends of what it did.
