# Process: Delinking

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). Borne at [The Counter](place_the_counter.md), precipitated by [The Payer](persona_the_payer.md) considering a payment that is not keyed to doses, in [Pay for the Shelf](plot_pay_for_the_shelf.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The recognition that the volume rule is why the drug most worth having is the one nobody will develop.

## Direction

From payment for doses toward payment for access: a market-entry reward, a pull incentive, or a subscription that pays whether or not a single course is dispensed.

## Lever

That it works and reverses the seat's own principle, since it requires paying for a medicine precisely because nobody is taking it, and that it is hardest to defend in exactly the years when it is succeeding (Outterson and colleagues).

## Echo

[The Subscription](piece_the_subscription.md) and [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), a standing public purchase of the unused, priced honestly rather than recommended, and undone the moment it lapses.
