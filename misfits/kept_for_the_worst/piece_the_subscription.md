# Piece: The Subscription

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). The remedy: payment for access rather than volume, whether or not a single course is dispensed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Counter](place_the_counter.md), where the payment rule lives and where the only correction would have to be written (Outterson and colleagues).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play has no answer at all, and a misfit with no available remedy is a lament rather than a trap.
It bears the load as the no-free-hero. Because it requires the payer to fund a medicine precisely because nobody is taking it, it reverses the principle that seat exists to uphold; because nobody can price a compound decades before the resistance it is kept for arrives, it cannot be sized confidently; because compounds can qualify on the letter without adding a new mechanism, it must be policed where policing is hardest; and because a reward withdrawn restores the old arithmetic, it must be sustained through every budget that follows.

## Apparent

An obvious fix, and from outside it looks like one: pay for the thing you want to exist, which is what public procurement does every day for lighthouses, vaccines and fire engines.

It works together with [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the correction and the honest account of its terms.
