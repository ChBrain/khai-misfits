# Position: Steward

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). The seat whose correct restraint is half the inversion, and which stands at the bedside when there is nothing left.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Guidance that is correct, a formulary it controls, and a newly licensed agent it can restrict on the day it arrives.
A clear account of the drug's worth: the years before resistance catches it, which every withheld dose extends.
No view of anybody's revenue, and no occasion on which anyone has asked it for one.

## Orders

Keep it in reserve: put the old generic first, hold the new agent for the isolates nothing else will touch, and defend the restriction against pressure to relax it.
Count every dose withheld as useful life preserved, because that is what it is.
Accept what the seat does: it is preserving the drug and shrinking its market with the same act.

## Loses

Any lever that would keep the market alive, since the one available, using the drug more, is precisely what it must not do and would spend the years the restraint exists to buy.
Because the value it protects and the revenue it destroys are the same restraint seen from two sides, stewarding better makes the problem larger, and the ordinary remedy for a struggling product is the thing this seat is right to refuse.
It keeps the drug's useful life and loses the next drug entirely.

## Drives

Toward the actor with the clearest duty in the play, standing at both ends of the loop with no vantage joining them,
restricting on the day of licensing and, years later, reaching for a list of options with one blank line on it,
so the correct restraint and the empty shelf are the same policy read at two distances, and neither end can see the other.
