# Place: The Counter

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). Where payment is keyed to units dispensed, and where the only correction would have to be written. Hosts [Paid by the Dose](plot_paid_by_the_dose.md) and [Pay for the Shelf](plot_pay_for_the_shelf.md).

## Shown

An accountable arrangement: reimbursement per course, invoices checked against the dispensing record, and nothing paid for stock nobody took.

## Holds

[The Tariff](piece_the_tariff.md), the volume rule, and later [The Subscription](piece_the_subscription.md) and [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the correction and its price.

## Offers

To the public, a budget in which every pound can be accounted for, which is what a public budget is supposed to be.

## Withheld

Any line for the drug that was never made, since a ledger records what was spent and not what was never developed, so the cost of this arrangement appears nowhere in the account that would have to justify changing it.
