# Plan: Keep It in Reserve

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), inside [Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Steward](position_steward.md)

## Direction

Restrict the new agent on the day it is licensed. Put the old generic first, hold the new one for the isolates nothing else will touch, and defend the restriction against every pressure to relax it, because what this drug is worth is the years before resistance catches it and every dose given is selection pressure spent. Understand that a hospital using it freely would be doing real harm to people who are not in the room yet, so this is not caution or rationing but the correct clinical practice and what the guidance says. And note what the seat cannot do about the consequence: the one lever that would keep the product's market alive is using more of it, which is precisely the thing it must not do.

## Orders

([The Steward](persona_the_steward.md) reserves through [Reserving](process_reserving.md) at [the Formulary](place_the_formulary.md), setting [the Reserve](piece_the_reserve.md), and years later meets [the Empty Shelf](piece_the_empty_shelf.md) through [Exiting](process_exiting.md) at [the Bedside](place_the_bedside.md).)

## Implementation

([The Formulary](place_the_formulary.md) is where the restriction is written; [the Bedside](place_the_bedside.md) is where the consequence of a market that closed years earlier is finally met, and no vantage joins the two.)

## Targets

- [ ] the new agent is restricted on the day it is licensed
- [ ] the old generic is tried first, and the new one held for what nothing else will touch
- [ ] the restriction is defended against pressure to relax it
- [ ] every dose withheld is counted as useful life preserved, which is what it is
- [ ] the seat holds no lever that would keep the market alive, the only one available being forbidden
