# Plan: Get Value for the Budget

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the payer's seat](position_payer.md), inside [Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Payer](position_payer.md)

## Direction

Pay for what is used. Reimburse per course, check the invoice against the dispensing record, and refuse to pay for stock nobody took, because a budget that paid for medicines nobody used would be indefensible and the arrangement exists to protect patients from shelf-warmers. Then, when it is put to you that this arrangement is why the drug most worth having is the one nobody will develop, consider the other instrument honestly: a market-entry reward or a subscription that pays for access whether or not a single course is dispensed. Price it without flattering it. It means committing public money up front for a medicine you hope will never be needed, guessing a compound's worth decades before the resistance it is kept for arrives, policing claims from compounds that qualify on the letter and add no new mechanism, and sustaining the scheme, since a reward withdrawn returns the market to the arithmetic it escaped.

## Targets

- [ ] payment follows units dispensed, and every pound can be accounted for
- [ ] stock nobody took is not reimbursed, which in the general case is exactly right
- [ ] the delinked alternative is considered honestly when it is put
- [ ] its costs are priced rather than waved off: money up front, a valuation nobody can make, claims to police, and a scheme to sustain
- [ ] the correction requires paying because nobody is taking it, which is the reversal at the centre
