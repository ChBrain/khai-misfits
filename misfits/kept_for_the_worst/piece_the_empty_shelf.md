# Piece: The Empty Shelf

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). The cost: a resistant isolate, and a list of remaining options with one line on it that is blank.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Bedside](place_the_bedside.md), years after the room that caused it, and met by someone who was not in that room.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play is an argument about pharmaceutical economics with nothing at stake in it, since a pipeline that thins without anyone ever needing what it would have held costs nothing at all.
It bears the load as the proof that the failure is under-production. Because the missing drug did not fail in trials and was not withdrawn for harm but was never made, there is no withdrawal to reverse, no failure to investigate and no culprit to find, and the reason it was never made is that everybody agreed it had to be saved.

## Apparent

Bad luck, and it will be recorded as a difficult case: a resistant organism, an unlucky patient, and no obvious decision anyone would take back.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Nothing Left to Reach For](plot_nothing_left_to_reach_for.md), where the loop closes on the seat that opened it.
It works together with [The Reserve](piece_the_reserve.md), the restraint and its consequence at two distances, with no vantage joining them.
