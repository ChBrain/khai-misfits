# Pitch: Only When Nothing Else Works

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Responsible and clinical, the tone of a hospital that has learned its lesson about antibiotics.
Restricted on the day it is licensed, the old generic first, the new agent held for the isolates nothing else will touch, and every dose withheld counted as useful life preserved.
Nothing false in it: this is the guidance, it is right, and a hospital that used the new agent freely would be doing real harm to people who are not in the room yet.

## Undertow

Under the restraint, a revenue line running the other way.
The drug's worth is the years before resistance catches it and its earnings are doses dispensed, so the better the stewardship the smaller the market, and a board setting an antibacterial programme beside an oncology one is not facing a hard decision (Outterson and colleagues; Projan).
No rapacious industry and no mean health service: the payer is refusing to pay for what is not used, which is what a payer is for, and nobody wants the shelf empty, the firms least of all.

## Nerve

The correctness of the restraint, and the catch that the restraint is the market:
that a compound is most valuable exactly where it is least sold, so worth and earnings point in opposite directions without anybody behaving badly,
and only when nothing else works is the sales forecast as well as the instruction.

## Echo

After the run: a bedside years later where the list of options for a resistant isolate has one line and it is blank, and a missing drug that did not fail in trials and was not withdrawn for harm but was never made (Projan).
And the remedy, real and priced: delink the reward from the volume, a market-entry prize or a subscription that pays for access whether or not a course is dispensed, which works and means public money committed up front for a medicine nobody hopes to need, guessed at decades before the resistance arrives, and hardest to defend in the years when it is working (Outterson and colleagues).
And the bound held in view: it needs a drug whose correct use is minimal and a reward tied to volume, so the whole of it is that the medicine we most want to exist is the one we have arranged to make worthless.
