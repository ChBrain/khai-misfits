# Kept for the Worst: Reference

The research warrant for **Kept for the Worst**, a staging of the phenomenon the literature calls the
**broken market for antibiotic innovation**, the conservation-reward paradox of antimicrobial
development (Outterson and colleagues; Projan): that a last-resort antibiotic's value to the public
rises with how carefully it is withheld while its revenue rises only with how much of it is sold, so
correct stewardship drives the return on the most valuable drugs toward zero and the pipeline empties
exactly where the need is greatest. It is held with the finding that every party is doing its job
properly, that the failure is one of **under-production** rather than over-consumption, and that it
persists under perfect stewardship, so it cannot be cured by anyone prescribing better.

## Line of Work

Staging the antibiotic market failure as an **inverted-reward structure**: the ordinary expectation of
a medicine is that its usefulness and its earnings point the same way. A drug that helps many people
is taken by many people, and the revenue that follows is the signal that tells a firm to make more
such drugs and tells a rival to try to beat it. That alignment is what a pharmaceutical market is for,
and for most classes it holds. For a last-resort antibiotic it inverts, exactly and by design. The
right clinical practice on a newly licensed agent against a resistant organism is to **not use it**:
treat with the older generic first, reserve the new one for the cases nothing else will touch, and
keep its useful life as long as possible, because every dose given is selection pressure spent and the
drug's worth is measured in the years before resistance catches it. So the better the stewardship the
smaller the volume, and the more valuable the drug the more carefully it is withheld. Meanwhile the
payment runs on units dispensed, as payment for medicines does and as any payer with a budget would
sensibly arrange, since paying for what is used is how a health system avoids paying for what is not.
Put the two together and the arithmetic is unforgiving: a compound that would save the most lives in
the worst future is a compound with almost no sales, on a short acute course rather than a chronic
one, against a flat market, and a board comparing it with an oncology programme does not have a
difficult decision to make. The firms leave, and they leave first from the compounds with genuinely
novel mechanisms, which are the expensive ones. The play models the reserve that is correct, the
volume payment that is sensible, the inversion the two produce, the exit that follows, the empty shelf
at the bedside, and the delinked payment that could fix it and has to be bought, as the load-bearing
vectors of the structure, and not as a tale of a rapacious industry. The ordinary expectation is that a
market underprovides what people will not pay for; the finding is that this market underprovides
precisely what everyone agrees is most valuable, **because** they agree it must be saved. The governing
law is that **where a drug's worth to the public rises with how carefully it is withheld and its
revenue rises only with how much is sold, correct stewardship drives the return on the most valuable
compounds toward zero, so the medicines most worth having are the ones a volume market cannot fund**.

It is an under-production structure no staged misfit holds, and it is carefully distinct from its
nearest neighbours. It is distinct from The Commons, where a shared stock is drawn down because each
user takes the whole of their gain and a fraction of the harm: there the trouble is **over-consumption**
of something that exists, and the remedy is to restrain the taking, whereas here the restraint is
already perfect and the trouble is that the thing is never made at all, so this is the commons read in
a mirror, and stewardship, which is the commons' cure, is this trap's cause. It is distinct from The
Firefighter's Medal, where prevention goes uncredited because a disaster that did not happen is
invisible while a rescue is not: there both activities are performed inside one organisation and the
fault is in how credit is allocated between them, whereas here there is no allocation problem and no
second activity, the good is simply never manufactured, because no accounting of credit can fund a
product whose optimal consumption is zero. It is distinct from Not Worth My While, Rose's prevention
paradox, where a measure that gains little for each of many people is rejected by every one of them
individually while being worth a great deal in aggregate: there the benefit is **thin and spread**, and
the trouble is on the recipient's side, whereas here the benefit is enormous and concentrated on a few
desperate cases and the trouble is that it cannot be **billed**, since billing requires use and the
correct use is almost none. And it is distinct from Baumol's Cost Disease, where a sector's costs rise
because its productivity cannot: there the good keeps being produced and merely becomes dearer,
whereas here the production stops, and it stops for a reason unconnected to the cost of making it.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast a
  rapacious industry abandoning the sick for profit, or a health service too mean to pay, because the
  finding it stages needs neither. The steward restricting the new agent on the day it is licensed is
  following the guidance that saves lives and preserves the drug's useful years, and a steward who
  used it freely would be doing real harm. The payer paying for units dispensed is doing what any payer
  with a budget should do, since paying for what is used is how a health system avoids paying for what
  is not, and the alternative has to be argued for rather than assumed. The board closing the
  antibacterial programme is making an ordinary portfolio comparison on a short acute course against a
  flat market, and would be failing its duty to do otherwise. Nobody wants the shelf empty, the firms
  least of all, since a company with no antibacterial franchise sells no antibacterials at all.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Delinkage is the
  instrument that reaches the mechanism, and it works by severing the reward from the volume: a
  market-entry reward, a pull incentive, or a subscription that pays for access to a drug whether or
  not anybody takes it. Its costs are specific and awkward. It requires a payer to commit public money
  up front for medicines it hopes will never be needed, which is the hardest expenditure in the world
  to defend at a budget hearing and the first to be cut. It requires someone to guess a compound's
  worth decades before the resistance it is being kept for arrives, which nobody can do well. It can be
  claimed by compounds that qualify on the letter of the scheme without adding a genuinely new
  mechanism, which is the part the scheme is least able to police. And it must be sustained, since a
  reward withdrawn returns the market to exactly the arithmetic it was built to escape. The reckoning
  is that the fix is a standing public purchase of something nobody uses, and that it is politically
  hardest in precisely the years when it is working.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The statement of the conservation-reward conflict as a market design fault
  and the delinkage remedy are Outterson, Powers, Daniel and McClellan's; the account of why an
  antibacterial programme loses a portfolio comparison, and why the novel-mechanism compounds are
  abandoned first, is Projan's. The Origin cell leads with Outterson alone and names his co-authors in
  the key-work column, because the science index keys a source cell on surname and the house already
  cites a different **Powers**, Claire Powers on Marek's disease in Saved to Spread, so keying this row
  on Powers would merge two unrelated people under one entry; all four authors are credited by name and
  only the index key is held back. The scope is held to last-resort antibiotics whose correct use is
  minimal, and is not stretched into a claim that pharmaceutical markets generally fail, that the firms
  which exited acted improperly, or that stewardship is a mistake, since stewardship is correct and the
  source literature argues for repairing the market around it rather than for using the drugs more.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per element, one per load-bearing vector, no filler.

- **The Reserve**: carries the correct restraint, the newly licensed agent restricted on the day it
  arrives, the generic tried first, and every dose withheld counted as useful life preserved, which is
  right on every clinical ground and is the first half of the inversion.
- **The Tariff**: carries the payment architecture, revenue as a function of units dispensed, which is
  how medicines are paid for and how any payer with a budget avoids paying for what is not used, and is
  the second half.
- **The Inverse**: carries the conjunction (Outterson and colleagues), value rising with withholding
  and revenue rising only with selling, so the better the drug the smaller its market, and no party has
  to behave badly for the two to point in opposite directions.
- **The Exit**: carries the portfolio decision (Projan), an antibacterial programme losing an ordinary
  comparison on a short acute course against a flat market, with the genuinely novel mechanisms
  abandoned first because they are the most expensive to pursue.
- **The Empty Shelf**: carries the cost, arriving years later at a bedside, where the list of options
  for a resistant isolate has one line and it is blank, and the drug that is missing is the one nobody
  was ever paid to make.
- **The Subscription**: carries the remedy that costs (Outterson and colleagues), delinked payment for
  access rather than volume, a standing public purchase of a medicine nobody uses, defensible only in
  advance of a need that cannot be dated and hardest to defend in the years when it is working.
- **The Boundary**: carries the condition and the hedge, that the trap needs a drug whose correct use
  is minimal and a reward tied to volume, that it lifts where the two are separated, and that
  separating them requires guessing a compound's worth decades early, resisting claims from compounds
  that add no new mechanism, and sustaining the scheme, since a reward withdrawn restores the
  arithmetic it was built to escape.
