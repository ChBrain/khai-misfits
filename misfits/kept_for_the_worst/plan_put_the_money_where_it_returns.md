# Plan: Put the Money Where It Returns

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the board's seat](position_board.md), inside [Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Board](position_board.md)

## Direction

Compare the programmes on the numbers the programmes produce, and fund the ones that return. Set the antibacterial candidate honestly against the alternatives: a short acute course rather than a chronic therapy, a market that is flat and that everybody wants to stay flat, correct annual sales in the low hundreds of courses, and a clinical guidance that will restrict it on the day it is licensed. Do not dispute the resistance forecasts, which are not what the comparison turns on. Say plainly that you would rather the antibacterial work paid, because a company with no antibacterial franchise sells no antibacterials at all, and note that wishing does not make it pay. If it does not return, close it, and close the novel-mechanism projects first, because those are the dearest to pursue and the comparison is worst for them.
