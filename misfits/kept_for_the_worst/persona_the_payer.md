# Persona: The Payer

## Taxonomy

[Payer](position_payer.md). The one who buys by the dose because that is what buying is, and who would have to buy differently to end it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Accountable and unglamorous, paying for units dispensed as it pays for every medicine, and able to account for each of them.
Regards paying for what is not used as the thing a public budget exists to avoid, which in almost every other case it is.
Is not hostile to the industry and has funded plenty of expensive drugs that were actually taken.

## Action

Runs [Get Value for the Budget](plan_get_value_for_the_budget.md): through [Paying](process_paying.md) at [the Tariff](place_the_tariff.md), setting [the Tariff](piece_the_tariff.md), and later through [Delinking](process_delinking.md) at the same place, holding [the Subscription](piece_the_subscription.md) and [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md).
Reimburses per course, checks the invoice against the dispensing record, and refuses to pay for stock nobody took.
Considers, later, a subscription that pays for access whether or not a single course is dispensed.

## Shadow

Not mean and not obstructive: a payer that reimbursed medicines nobody used would be indefensible in the general case, and the arrangement it operates was designed to protect patients from paying for shelf-warmers.
Because the volume rule is the other half of the inversion, its correct thrift is what makes the most valuable compound the least fundable, and the fix it must consider is a standing purchase of a medicine it hopes will never be needed, which is the hardest line in any budget to defend and the first to be cut.
It is the only actor who can end the trap and can only end it by doing the thing its whole discipline exists to prevent.

## Tell

Can justify every pound spent and cannot justify the pounds that were never spent on a drug that was never made.
Cannot fund the subscription and be sure of it, since nobody can price a compound decades before the resistance it is being kept for arrives, and the scheme is hardest to defend in exactly the years when it is working.
