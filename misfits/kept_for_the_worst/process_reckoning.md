# Process: Reckoning

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). Borne at [The Boardroom](place_the_boardroom.md), precipitated by [The Board](persona_the_board.md) reading the restraint and the tariff on one page, in [Worth Most Unsold](plot_worth_most_unsold.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two correct arrangements meeting in a portfolio comparison that has to be made and cannot be deferred.

## Direction

From a compound's clinical worth toward its commercial value, and from those two toward the discovery that they run in opposite directions.

## Lever

That the better the drug the more carefully it is withheld and the less of it is sold, so the comparison is not merely negative about antibacterials but sharpest against the ones most worth having (Outterson and colleagues).

## Echo

[The Inverse](piece_the_inverse.md), worth and earnings pointing opposite ways with nobody having behaved badly to make them, and a number that is explicable to anyone in a sentence.
