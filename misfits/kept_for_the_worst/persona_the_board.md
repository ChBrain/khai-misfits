# Persona: The Board

## Taxonomy

[Board](position_board.md). The one that reads the two facts on the same page and makes the only decision the page permits.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sober and fiduciary, comparing programmes on the numbers the programmes produce, without appetite for a fight and without contempt for the science.
Would say honestly that it wishes the antibacterial work paid, and means it, since a company with no antibacterial franchise sells no antibacterials at all.
Does not dispute the resistance forecasts and has no need to: the forecasts are not what the comparison turns on.

## Action

Runs [Put the Money Where It Returns](plan_put_the_money_where_it_returns.md): through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) at [the Boardroom](place_the_boardroom.md), meeting [the Inverse](piece_the_inverse.md), and through [Exiting](process_exiting.md) at the same table, producing [the Exit](piece_the_exit.md).
Sets a short acute course against a flat market beside an oncology programme, and finds the comparison is not close.
Closes the antibacterial work, and closes the novel-mechanism projects first, because those are the dearest to pursue.

## Shadow

Not rapacious and not indifferent: it is discharging a duty it would be criticised for neglecting, and a board that funded the programme anyway would be spending other people's money against its own analysis.
Because the compound's value is measured in years of preserved usefulness and its revenue in doses sold, the better the drug the worse it scores here, so the decision is not merely negative about antibacterials but sharpest against the ones most worth having.
It is the seat where the inversion is finally read as a number, and the number is not close.

## Tell

Can show the comparison to anyone and be understood at once, which is the problem.
Cannot be argued round by an appeal to the public interest, since the public interest is exactly what the stewardship guidance is protecting and the guidance is what makes the volume small.
