# Plot: Pay for the Shelf

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Tariff](place_the_tariff.md), and someone asking what would actually work.

## Action

Through [Delinking](process_delinking.md), [the Payer](persona_the_payer.md) on [Get Value for the Budget](plan_get_value_for_the_budget.md) holds [the Subscription](piece_the_subscription.md): pay for access rather than volume, a market-entry reward or a standing payment made whether or not a single course is dispensed. It works. It then holds [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) and prices it: public money committed up front for a medicine nobody hopes to need, a compound valued decades before the resistance it is kept for arrives, claims to police from compounds that qualify on the letter and add no new mechanism, and a scheme that must be sustained, since a reward withdrawn restores the arithmetic it escaped (Outterson and colleagues).

## Stage

The instrument is real and is already being tried. This is not a thought experiment and not a plea for the industry: it is public procurement of a thing we want to exist, of the kind done every day for lighthouses, vaccines and fire engines.

## Tension

The correction requires the payer to fund a medicine precisely because nobody is taking it, reversing the principle that seat exists to uphold, and it is hardest to defend in exactly the years when it is working, since a subscription for a drug that has never been needed looks, from a budget hearing, indistinguishable from waste.
