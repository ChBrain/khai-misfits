# Position: Board

## Taxonomy

[Kept for the Worst](play_kept_for_the_worst.md). The seat where the inversion is finally read as a number, and the number is not close.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A portfolio, a duty to the people whose money it is, and two programmes to compare.
A short acute course against a flat market on one side, and a chronic therapy in a growing indication on the other.
No dispute with the resistance forecasts, which are not what the comparison turns on.

## Orders

Put the money where it returns: compare the programmes on the numbers the programmes produce, and fund the one that returns.
Close the antibacterial work if it does not, and close the novel-mechanism projects first, since those are the dearest to pursue.
Accept what the seat does: it would rather the antibacterial work paid, and cannot make it pay by wishing.

## Loses

Any room to decide otherwise, since funding the programme against its own analysis would be spending other people's money on a preference.
Because the compound's worth is measured in preserved years and its revenue in doses sold, the comparison runs sharpest against the drugs most worth having, so the seat is not merely negative about antibacterials but selectively hostile to the best of them.
It keeps a defensible decision and loses the franchise, a company with no antibacterial business selling no antibacterials at all.

## Drives

Toward the seat that reads the two facts on one page and finds only one answer written there,
setting the programmes side by side, seeing the comparison is not close, and closing the dearest projects first,
so an ordinary fiduciary judgement, explicable to anyone in a sentence, is the point at which the pipeline ends.
