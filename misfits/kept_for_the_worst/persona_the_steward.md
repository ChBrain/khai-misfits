# Persona: The Steward

## Taxonomy

[Steward](position_steward.md). The one whose correct restraint is half the inversion, and who stands at the bedside when there is nothing left.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Rigorous and unshowy, restricting the new agent on the day it is licensed and able to say exactly why.
Treats every dose withheld as useful life preserved, because what the drug is worth is the years before resistance catches it.
Has no view about anybody's revenue and has never been asked for one.

## Action

Runs [Keep It in Reserve](plan_keep_it_in_reserve.md): through [Reserving](process_reserving.md) at [the Formulary](place_the_formulary.md), setting [the Reserve](piece_the_reserve.md), and years later through [Exiting](process_exiting.md) at [the Bedside](place_the_bedside.md), meeting [the Empty Shelf](piece_the_empty_shelf.md).
Puts the old generic first, holds the new agent for the isolates nothing else will touch, and defends the restriction against pressure to use it.
Reaches, later, for a list of options that has one line on it and the line is blank.

## Shadow

Not over-cautious and not rationing: a hospital that used the new agent freely would be doing real harm to people who are not in the room yet, and the guidance it follows is correct in every particular.
Because the drug's value to the public is exactly what its restraint preserves, and its revenue is exactly what its restraint destroys, the seat's diligence is one half of the reason the next drug is never made, and stewarding better makes that half larger.
It is the actor with the clearest duty in the play and stands at both ends of the loop without a vantage joining them.

## Tell

Reports excellent stewardship metrics, which are excellent, and which describe a market shrinking.
Cannot use the drug more to keep the market alive, since that is the one thing it must not do and would waste the very years the restraint exists to buy.
