## Load Bearing

It shows the theory wrong about people and right about the shape, which is the honest position and not a compromise.
Subjects punish free-riders at their own expense far more than any calculation allows, contributions hold where punishment is permitted, and that is a real finding about real people. It is also bounded: punishing the non-punisher is far rarer than punishing the free-rider, contributions fall back over rounds when the option is removed, and what sustains cooperation in the field looks more like standing institutions than like the impulse. So the curve narrows the recursion and leaves it open.
