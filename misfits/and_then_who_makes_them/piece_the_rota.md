## Load Bearing

It states an obligation and contains no way of imposing one, which is what makes it the setup rather than the mechanism.
Everybody agreed it, everybody meant it, and it says exactly what it should. What it cannot do is act: an obligation on paper is discharged by whoever chooses to discharge it, and the only thing that converts it into a constraint is somebody being willing to be the person who mentions it. So the document is not deficient in any way a better document could remedy, and the difficulty begins where the paper stops.

## Apparent

The arrangement, settled. The part everybody agreed to.

## Yearbook
