# Persona: The Neighbour

## Taxonomy

[Neighbour](position_neighbour.md). The one who watched the witness say nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A landing, and a conversation that visibly did not happen.

## Action

Notices that nobody said anything.
Does not mention that either.

## Shadow

Stands one level further down, in a structure that is identical and has no floor.
Reproaching somebody for failing to reproach somebody costs this seat the same difficult conversation and delivers the same shared benefit, so the reasons are the reasons above it, unchanged. And the same is true of reproaching this seat, and of that, without limit. Which is why the arrangement does not fail at some particular level: there is no level at which the calculation comes out differently, so there is nothing for it to fail at.

## Tell

Reproduces the failure by descending one step and finding it the same.
