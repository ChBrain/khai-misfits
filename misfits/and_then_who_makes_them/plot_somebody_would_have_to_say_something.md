## Cue

Two neighbours meeting on a landing, and a fortnight everybody has noticed.

## Action

[Saying something](process_saying_something.md): [the witness](persona_the_witness.md), holding [Witness](position_witness.md), weighs the conversation at [the stair](place_the_stair.md) and says good morning.

## Stage

A repair that exists, is available, and is not taken.

## Tension

The repair has the shape of the thing it repairs, which is the whole finding and not an analogy.
Speaking costs this seat a difficult conversation, a soured landing and possibly a year of small hostilities, and the clean stair that follows is shared equally with everybody in the building including the three who said nothing. Cost concentrated, benefit spread: that is a public good exactly, sitting one level below the stair. So declining is not apathy, timidity or indifference. It is the same sound reasoning that produced the problem, applied to the cure.
