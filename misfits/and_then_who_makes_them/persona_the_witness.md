# Persona: The Witness

## Taxonomy

[Witness](position_witness.md). The one who could say something, and who would pay for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A landing, a neighbour, and a fortnight of missed turns that everybody has noticed.

## Action

Considers mentioning it.
Says good morning instead.

## Shadow

Is making the same calculation the missed turns were made on, and making it correctly.
Saying something costs this seat a difficult conversation, a soured landing and possibly a year of small hostilities, and the benefit is a clean stair shared equally by everybody in the building including the three who said nothing. That is the structure of a public good exactly, one level down from the stair itself, so declining is not apathy, timidity or indifference: it is the same sound reasoning that produced the problem, applied to the repair.

## Tell

Declines to fix a thing for the reason it broke.
