# Persona: The Contributor

## Taxonomy

[Contributor](position_contributor.md). The one who does their week.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A rota on the noticeboard, a bucket, and a Saturday morning that could have been something else.

## Action

Does the week that is theirs.
Notices, at the end of the month, that the stair is worse than it was.

## Shadow

Is doing exactly what the arrangement asks and has no way to make the arrangement work.
The contribution is genuine and is not in question: what the seat cannot supply is anybody else's, and its own compliance is worth almost nothing on a stair that four people use and one person sweeps. So the seat is not being taken advantage of by anybody in particular, it is holding up one corner of a thing that needs four, and it has no instrument for the other three.

## Tell

Keeps its side of a bargain that is not being kept.
