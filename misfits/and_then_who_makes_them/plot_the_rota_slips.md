# Plot: The Rota Slips

## Taxonomy

[And Then Who Makes Them](play_and_then_who_makes_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Four flats, one stair, and a rota everybody agreed to.

## Action

[Paying in](process_paying_in.md): [the contributor](persona_the_contributor.md), holding [Contributor](position_contributor.md), does its week at [the stair](place_the_stair.md), and the following three weeks are done badly or not at all.

## Stage

A shared thing that everybody wants and that is getting worse.

## Tension

[The Rota](piece_the_rota.md) states an obligation and contains no way of imposing one, which is where the play starts rather than what it is about.
Everybody agreed it and everybody meant it, and it says exactly what it should say. What it cannot do is act: an obligation on paper is discharged by whoever chooses to discharge it. So there is no better document to write, and the seat that keeps its own week holds up one corner of a thing that needs four, with no instrument by which to obtain the other three.
