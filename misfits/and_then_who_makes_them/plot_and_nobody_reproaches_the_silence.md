# Plot: And Nobody Reproaches the Silence

## Taxonomy

[And Then Who Makes Them](play_and_then_who_makes_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A conversation that visibly did not happen, in front of somebody.

## Action

[Letting the silence pass](process_letting_the_silence_pass.md): [the neighbour](persona_the_neighbour.md), holding [Neighbour](position_neighbour.md), considers reproaching the one who said nothing, and does not.

## Stage

The same decision, one level down, taken the same way for the same reasons.

## Tension

There is no level at which the calculation comes out differently, so there is no level at which it fails.
Reproaching somebody for failing to reproach somebody costs the same conversation and delivers the same shared benefit, and so does reproaching the one who let that pass, without limit. This is why the arrangement does not break at some identifiable point that a rule could be placed at: the structure is identical all the way down, and a fix applied at any level needs the same missing thing at the level beneath it.
