# Persona: The Experimenter

## Taxonomy

[Experimenter](position_experimenter.md). The one who finds that people punish anyway.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A public-goods game, a punishment stage, and subjects who spend their own money to fine a free-rider.

## Action

Finds punishment happening at real cost, far more than the theory allows.
Runs it longer, and watches the effect move.

## Shadow

Has the honest answer and it is a bound rather than a rescue.
People do punish at their own expense, contributions hold up where they can, and that is a real finding about real people that the play carries and does not talk away. It is also bounded: the second-order punishment is far rarer than the first, cooperation without it falls back over rounds, and what sustains it in the field looks like the institutions rather than the impulse. So the impulse narrows the recursion and does not close it.

## Tell

Shows the model is wrong about people, and right about the shape.
