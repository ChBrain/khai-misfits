# Persona: The Convenor

## Taxonomy

[Convenor](position_convenor.md). The one who proposes to pay somebody to do it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A quote from a cleaning firm, a proposed monthly charge, and a meeting to agree it.

## Action

Proposes the warden.
Waits for the levy to be agreed.

## Shadow

Has the answer, and the answer has to be bought by the people it is the answer for.
Paying somebody whose job it is removes the whole recursion at a stroke, because a warden enforces for wages rather than out of public spirit and is not choosing between a clean stair and a soured landing. What it costs is a standing charge that must be agreed unanimously or near it, collected from everybody, and defended at the meeting by somebody: which is a public good with the same shape as the stair, sitting on top of it. The escape is real and is not outside the structure.

## Tell

Solves it with an institution, which is the thing that has to be provided.
