# Plot: The One Who Declines Is the One Harmed

## Taxonomy

[And Then Who Makes Them](play_and_then_who_makes_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The end of the month, and a stair worse than it was.

## Action

[The Rota](piece_the_rota.md) is read again at [the stair](place_the_stair.md) by the same people who agreed it, all of whom use the stair every day.

## Stage

Everybody worse off, and nobody having done anything they should not have.

## Tension

Every party wants the outcome and every party's own move away from it is correct, which is what puts this outside anybody's conduct.
There is no free-rider enjoying a benefit at somebody else's expense here, or rather everybody is one and each is also the injured party: the seat that declined to speak walks up the same dirty stair as the rest. Nobody is exploiting anybody, nobody has been taken in, and the arrangement was not badly designed by whoever wrote it. What went wrong is that the only available repair costs its provider privately and pays the building collectively, and that is true of the repair to the repair as well.
