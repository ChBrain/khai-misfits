# Place: The Stair

## Taxonomy

[And Then Who Makes Them](play_and_then_who_makes_them.md). The shared thing, and the landing where it would be raised.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Four flats, one stair, a rota pinned to the board, a bucket in the cupboard under it.
Nothing sinister: everybody agreed the rota, everybody meant it, and everybody is perfectly pleasant on the landing.

## Holds

[The Contributor](persona_the_contributor.md), doing its week.
[The Witness](persona_the_witness.md), and [the neighbour](persona_the_neighbour.md), meeting and saying good morning.
[The Rota](piece_the_rota.md), accurate and ignored by degrees.

## Offers

A clean stair to everybody, on the days somebody has swept it.

## Withheld

Any way to raise it that does not cost the raiser.
The place is a landing between two front doors, so a word about the rota is a word to somebody who will be there tomorrow and next year, and the cost of saying it falls entirely on whoever says it. That is not a feature of these neighbours: it is what a shared space between people who cannot leave is, and it is why the repair has the same shape as the thing it repairs.
