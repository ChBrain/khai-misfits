# Pitch: Somebody Ought To

## Taxonomy

[And Then Who Makes Them](play_and_then_who_makes_them.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

## Tenor

Plain and domestic. A rota on a noticeboard, a stair that needs sweeping, a landing where two neighbours meet.
Reasonable people, none of whom is wrong about anything.

## Undertow

Under the rota, a repair whose cost falls on one person and whose benefit falls on everybody.

## Nerve

The flatness of a thought that is correct, sincere, and shared by every single person holding it.

## Echo

Afterward: a warden who would fix it, and a levy that has to be agreed by the same meeting.
