# Play: And Then Who Makes Them

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

And Then Who Makes Them.
Four flats share a stair and a rota everybody agreed to. The rota slips. The repair is that somebody mentions it, and mentioning it costs one person a difficult year and gives four people a clean stair. So nobody mentions it, and nobody mentions that nobody mentioned it, and there is no level at which that stops being true.

## Arc

The bend is this: the repair for an under-supplied public good is itself an under-supplied public good, and so is the repair for that.
It runs on an agreement that cannot act.
[The Rota Slips](plot_the_rota_slips.md). [The Rota](piece_the_rota.md) states an obligation and contains no way of imposing one, so there is no better document to write, and [the contributor](persona_the_contributor.md) holds up one corner of a thing that needs four.
And the repair costs one person.
[Somebody Would Have to Say Something](plot_somebody_would_have_to_say_something.md). [The Witness](persona_the_witness.md) would pay a soured landing and the building would get the clean stair, which is a public good exactly, one level below the first.
And it is the same one level further down.
[And Nobody Reproaches the Silence](plot_and_nobody_reproaches_the_silence.md). [The Neighbour](persona_the_neighbour.md) faces an identical calculation, and so does whoever watches them, so the structure does not break at a point a rule could be placed at.
Then [The One Who Declines Is the One Harmed](plot_the_one_who_declines_is_the_one_harmed.md).
Nobody is exploiting anybody: everybody wants the outcome, every move away from it is correct, and the seat that said nothing walks up the same dirty stair as the rest.
And [people punish anyway, and it is not enough](plot_people_punish_anyway_and_it_is_not_enough.md). [The Experimenter](persona_the_experimenter.md) finds real punishment at real cost, and finds [the decay curve](piece_the_decay_curve.md) beneath it: the second order is rarer than the first, and cooperation falls back without it.
And [the warden has to be paid for](plot_the_warden_has_to_be_paid_for.md). [The Levy](piece_the_levy.md) ends the recursion completely and has to be convened, argued and agreed by the same four people, so the institution sits on top of the structure rather than beyond it.

## Stakes

Whether a group can obtain the thing that would make it obtain things.
Every plot moves the same way: toward an agreement that cannot act, a repair priced to one person and paid to everybody, a second level identical to the first, a party that is both the one declining and the one harmed, an impulse that is real and bounded, and an institution that sits on top of the structure rather than outside it, and away from any account in which somebody was apathetic or somebody else was taking advantage. The production cannot fault the witness for saying good morning, because speaking costs a year and buys a share of a stair, and it cannot fault the contributor for keeping a bargain that is not working, because keeping it is the only instrument the seat has. What is left to fight over is not who should have spoken but what to call a difficulty whose every remedy is another instance of it.
