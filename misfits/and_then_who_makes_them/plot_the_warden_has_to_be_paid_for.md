## Tension

The escape works entirely and has to be provided in the way the recursion prevents.
A paid warden enforces for wages, does not choose between a clean stair and a soured landing, and is genuinely outside the difficulty: this is not a remedy the play is undermining. But somebody has to obtain the quote, put the notice up, chair the room and be argued with, and that labour is uncompensated and shared out afterwards among everybody including whoever stayed at home. So the institution sits on top of the structure rather than beyond it, which is why such arrangements are hard to start and comparatively easy to keep, and why the buildings that have one usually cannot say who set it up.
