# Plot: People Punish Anyway, and It Is Not Enough

## Taxonomy

[And Then Who Makes Them](play_and_then_who_makes_them.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A laboratory, a public-goods game, and a stage at which subjects may fine each other at their own expense.

## Action

[The Experimenter](persona_the_experimenter.md), holding [Experimenter](position_experimenter.md), records [the decay curve](piece_the_decay_curve.md) across many rounds.

## Stage

Punishment happening at real cost, far more of it than the theory allows.

## Tension

The finding is true, is carried, and is a bound rather than a rescue.
People do fine free-riders out of their own money, and contributions hold up where they can, which is a real fact about real people and the play does not talk it away. It is also bounded in three ways: punishing the non-punisher is far rarer than punishing the free-rider, contributions fall back over rounds where the option is withdrawn, and what sustains cooperation outside a laboratory looks more like a standing institution than like the impulse. So the impulse narrows the recursion and does not close it, which is the honest position rather than a compromise between two.
