# Place: The Meeting

## Taxonomy

[And Then Who Makes Them](play_and_then_who_makes_them.md). Where the way out would be agreed, and paid for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Six chairs, a quote from a cleaning firm, and a proposed monthly charge with a figure on it.
Good faith throughout: everybody wants the stair clean, nobody disputes the quote, and the arithmetic is not in doubt.

## Holds

[The Convenor](persona_the_convenor.md), proposing.
[The Levy](piece_the_levy.md), which would end the whole difficulty and has to be agreed.
Everybody else, each of whom would be better off if the levy passed and somebody else did the arguing.

## Offers

A complete escape from the recursion, available for money.

## Withheld

Anybody obliged to convene it.
The place cannot summon itself: somebody has to get the quote, put the notice up, chair the room and be argued with, and that labour is uncompensated and shared out afterwards among everybody including whoever stayed at home. So the institution that would remove the problem is provided the way the stair is, and the room is where that becomes visible rather than where it is escaped.
