# Plot: Move Them Before They Settle

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Roster](place_the_roster.md), a posting list signed on a schedule nobody finds remarkable.

## Action

Through [Shuffling](process_shuffling.md), [the Minder](persona_the_minder.md), from [the minder's seat](position_minder.md), on [Keep Them Strangers](plan_keep_them_strangers.md) works [the Shuffle](piece_the_shuffle.md): commanders moved in their second year, before the men begin to speak of the colonel rather than of the battalion, and, where the difference is not decisive, the officer whose family and region can be vouched for preferred over the officer who is merely better. Nobody is accused of anything, because the practice works on a capability and not on a person, and a perfectly loyal officer has the capability too. The scene closes with an officer corps in which no commander could take a unit with him, and in which no commander ever learns his ground, his men, or his trade (Quinlivan).

## Stage

The measure is doing precisely its work. A commander who stays long enough becomes a man his men would follow anywhere, and anywhere includes the capital, so rotation is not spite and the preference for the vouched-for is not bigotry but the cheapest available proxy for a thing that cannot be observed directly.

## Tension

The following that would carry a unit into the capital is the following that would carry it across a river under fire, so the practice cannot succeed narrowly and no test exists that tells cohesion from conspiracy, which leaves the most conscientious official in the play emptying the army by discharging his mandate exactly as written, with no accusation to rebut and no innocence that helps.
