# Piece: The Counterweight

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The structural lever: a second force answering separately, so that no single command can take the state.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Barracks](place_the_barracks.md), beside the army and never with it, holding the capital and the first call on supply (Quinlivan).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and one command holds the whole sword, which is the arrangement the ruler cannot survive, so the counterweight is not an excess of caution but the minimum the seat requires.
It bears the load as the first of three separate subtractions. Because its separateness is the entire value, it cannot be relaxed in the emergency, so the two forces cannot be concentrated by one order, cannot fight as one, and draw on a single stock of fuel and transport in which one of them has written priority.

## Apparent

Strength, and it is strength: the best equipped and best trained formation in the country, which is exactly what a counterweight has to be to weigh.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Raise the Other Army](plot_raise_the_other_army.md), where the capital is secured and the field army halved in the same act.
It works together with [The Bill](piece_the_bill.md), being the reason the force at the border cannot concentrate.
