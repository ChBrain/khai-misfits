# Position: Guard

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The seat that answers separately so that no single command can take the state, and that is subtracted from the force it stands beside.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Its own chain of command, running to the ruler and to nobody else, which is the whole of its value.
First call on fuel, transport and spare parts, written into its establishment rather than taken.
Real quality: better equipped, better paid and better trained than the army, because that is what it was raised to be.

## Orders

Stand apart: hold the capital, answer to one man, and come under no field commander in any circumstance.
Be excellent, which it is, and be separate, which is the point.
Accept what the seat does: its separateness is not a flaw in the arrangement, it is the arrangement.

## Loses

Any capacity to be combined with the army in the emergency, since a guard that can be folded in is a guard that can be commanded by the man who folds it.
Because it has never exercised alongside the units it would have to fight beside, the seat loses interoperability with the only other force in the country, and improving the guard does not close that gap but widens it.
It keeps the capital secure and takes the field army's half with it.

## Drives

Toward the best formation in the country and the reason the country cannot put a capable force in the field,
doing well the job it was given, drawing the supply its establishment grants it, and answering where it was told to answer,
so the excellence of the second army is subtracted from the first rather than added to the state.
