# Persona: The Ruler

## Taxonomy

[Ruler](position_ruler.md). The one who must fear the instrument he is holding, and who prices two dangers correctly against each other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Unhurried and clear-eyed, the manner of someone who has read the history of the neighbouring capitals and of his own.
Does not speak of enemies within: speaks of arrangements, of balance, of not putting too much in one pair of hands.
No paranoia on show, and none needed: in this region the coup is frequent, in this country it is recent, and it would end with him personally.

## Action

Runs [Hold the Throne](plan_hold_the_throne.md): through [Weighing](process_weighing.md) at [the Palace](place_the_palace.md), setting [the Two Clocks](piece_the_two_clocks.md) and, later, reading [the Same List](piece_the_same_list.md) and holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md).
Buys the counterweight, approves the rotation, and declines the formation exercise, each on its own merits and each correctly.
Wants a capable army and says so sincerely, an army that loses being a throne that falls, and cannot have one on any terms available to him.

## Shadow

Not a fool and not a coward: the trade he makes is the right trade on his own discount rate, since the coup is near and certain in kind and personally fatal, and the war is deferred, probabilistic, and borne mostly by other people.
Because the list that would make the army able to fight is identically the list that would make it able to depose, his prudence is what disarms him, and thinking harder about it produces the same answer faster.
He is the only actor who could take the other side of the trade and the one for whom taking it is worst.

## Tell

Approves everything the security of the throne requires and is surprised by nothing until the border moves.
Cannot test the arrangement against the war, since the only rehearsal that would test it is the division-scale exercise he has declined, and declining it is the arrangement.
