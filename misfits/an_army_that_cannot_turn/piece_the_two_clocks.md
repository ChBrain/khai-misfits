# Piece: The Two Clocks

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The two dangers running at different speeds: the coup near, personal and fatal to the occupant, the war deferred, probabilistic and borne mostly by others.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Palace](place_the_palace.md), before any measure is chosen, so the arrangement that follows is the correct answer to the question the seat is actually facing (Talmadge).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the ruler is simply irrational, preferring a weak army to a strong one for no reason, and the trap dissolves into a story about a bad man making a bad choice.
It bears the load as the reason the choice is right. Because the internal danger is frequent in the region, recent in the country, and would end with the occupant personally, while the external one may never arrive and would be borne by other people, the arithmetic any occupant would perform returns the disarming answer, and performing it more carefully returns it sooner.

## Apparent

Prudence, and it is prudence: the near and certain threat handled first, which is what a person is supposed to do.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [The Fear Is Not Imagined](plot_the_fear_is_not_imagined.md), setting the price that every later measure is bought at.
It works together with [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), since where the far clock runs fast enough the same arithmetic returns the other answer.
