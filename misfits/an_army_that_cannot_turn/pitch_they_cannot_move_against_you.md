# Pitch: They Cannot Move Against You

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Calm and competent, the tone of a man giving good advice.
The guard answers only to you, the generals are moved before they settle, no formation exercises above battalion, and the units do not talk to each other without coming through us.
Nothing false in it: the threat is real, the measures work, and a ruler in this region who ignored the danger from his own officers would not be principled but dead within the year.

## Undertow

Under each measure, the same measure read the other way.
Two forces that answer separately cannot fight as one; officers moved every eighteen months never learn their ground or their men; an army that has not exercised above battalion cannot combine arms; and units that may not speak sideways cannot manoeuvre, manoeuvre being lateral communication under fire (Quinlivan).
No tyrant and no traitors: the officers passed over are not suspected of anything, the practice selecting against a capability rather than against a person, and nobody in the room wants the army to lose.

## Nerve

The purpose of the advice, and the catch that the advice is sound:
that the capability which lets a force fight is the capability which lets it act, so the safety and the weakness are one purchase and not two,
and they cannot move against you is the sentence under which they also cannot move.

## Echo

After the run: a border, a smaller enemy that can concentrate and combine and manoeuvre, and a force that can do none of the three, beaten without any failure of courage by a design that was answering a different question.
And the remedy, real and priced: promote on merit, exercise at formation scale, push initiative down, let the units talk, which is not merely expensive but is identically the list of capabilities required to mount a coup, so the cure cannot be separated from the threat (Quinlivan).
And the bound held in view: it needs a credible danger from one's own armed forces, and it lifts where the danger from outside is worse, rulers under real external pressure taking the other side of the trade, fighting better for it, and becoming easier to overthrow (Talmadge), so the whole of it is that a sword held so it can never turn on you is a sword that does not turn.
