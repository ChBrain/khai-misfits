# Persona: The Colonel

## Taxonomy

[Colonel](position_colonel.md). The one who is loyal in fact, obstructed anyway, and left to fight with what the arrangement permits.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Diligent and unpolitical, wanting nothing except a unit that works: gunnery that hits, drivers who can move at night, and a signals net that reaches the flank.
Requests the exercise, the live ammunition, the joint rehearsal with the formation next door, and requests them again the following year.
Has no ambitions on the capital and would find the suggestion absurd.

## Action

Runs [Be Ready Anyway](plan_be_ready_anyway.md): through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) at [the Front](place_the_front.md), meeting [the Bill](piece_the_bill.md).
Trains inside the limits: to battalion and no further, with the officers he is sent, for the eighteen months he is likely to keep them.
Fights when it comes, with a force that cannot concentrate, cannot combine and cannot move, and does not fail for want of nerve.

## Shadow

Not a victim of malice and not a martyr: nobody has accused him, nobody wishes him ill, and the measures that hollow his unit were not aimed at him at all.
Because the readiness he wants is indistinguishable from the readiness the arrangement is designed to prevent, his most professional requests are the ones most reliably refused, and asking more insistently makes him look more like the thing being guarded against.
He is the one person in the play who is trying to make the army work and the one with no lever that would.

## Tell

Keeps his unit as good as the ceiling allows and knows precisely where the ceiling is.
Cannot rehearse with the formation on his flank, so he meets it for the first time in the war, and discovers there what the two of them cannot do together.
