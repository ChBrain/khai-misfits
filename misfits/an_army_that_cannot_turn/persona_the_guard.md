# Persona: The Guard

## Taxonomy

[Guard](position_guard.md). The second force that answers separately, competent at what it was raised for, and the halving of the first.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Professional, well turned out, and genuinely good: better equipped, better paid, better trained than the army it sits beside, because that is what it was raised to be.
Speaks of the army without contempt and without much interest, the way one service speaks of another it does not work with.
Has never done anything improper and would be insulted by the suggestion.

## Action

Runs [Stand Apart](plan_stand_apart.md): through [Counterweighting](process_counterweighting.md) at [the Barracks](place_the_barracks.md), standing as [the Counterweight](piece_the_counterweight.md).
Answers to [the Ruler](persona_the_ruler.md) alone, holds the capital, and does not come under the field army's command in any circumstance, which is the whole of its value.
Draws first on the fuel, the transport and the spare parts, not by theft but by the priority written into its establishment.

## Shadow

Not a rival and not a praetorian schemer: it is doing well the job it was given, and the job is to make sure no single command can take the state.
Because its separateness is the point, the two forces cannot fight as one, cannot be concentrated by one order, and compete for a single supply, so the excellence of the guard is subtracted from the army rather than added to the state.
It is the most capable formation in the country and the reason the country cannot field a capable force.

## Tell

Passes every inspection and has never exercised alongside the units it would have to fight beside.
Cannot be folded into the army in the emergency, since a guard that can be folded in is a guard that can be commanded by the man who folds it, which is the arrangement it exists to prevent.
