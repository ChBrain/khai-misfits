# Persona: The Minder

## Taxonomy

[Minder](position_minder.md). The one who keeps officers from settling and units from speaking, and who prevents exactly what he was appointed to prevent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Courteous, procedural, and entirely unmysterious: he signs the posting orders, he attends the exercise planning, and he asks that traffic between formations come through his office.
Frames every measure as routine administration, which is what it is, and none of it as suspicion of anyone.
Names no one as a risk, because the practice does not work on people.

## Action

Runs [Keep Them Strangers](plan_keep_them_strangers.md): through [Shuffling](process_shuffling.md) at [the Roster](place_the_roster.md), working [the Shuffle](piece_the_shuffle.md), and through [Silencing](process_silencing.md) at [the Manoeuvre Ground](place_the_manoeuvre_ground.md), holding [the Silence](piece_the_silence.md).
Moves commanders in their second year, before a following can form, and prefers the officer whose family and region can be vouched for over the officer who is merely better.
Declines the division-scale exercise on the reasonable ground that a division moving at scale cannot be distinguished from a division moving on the capital.

## Shadow

Not a persecutor: the officers he moves are not accused of anything and mostly are not suspected of anything, since what he is removing is a capability and not a disloyalty, and a perfectly loyal officer has it too.
Because a following that could carry a unit into the capital is the same following that could carry it across a river under fire, his success is exact and its cost is exact, and he has no instrument that separates the two.
He is the most conscientious official in the play and the seat through which the army is emptied.

## Tell

Reports, accurately, that no formation has developed a personal command and no two units have an unsupervised channel.
Cannot be asked to prevent conspiracy while permitting cohesion, since he has not been given a test that tells them apart and no such test exists.
