# Piece: The Silence

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The informational lever: the exercise above battalion scale not scheduled, and the lateral channel between units not opened.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Manoeuvre Ground](place_the_manoeuvre_ground.md), as an absence rather than an act, the larger serial proposed each year and struck each year for reasons that are sound (Quinlivan).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and two formations acquire the habit of arranging things between themselves at a scale that could carry them to the capital, which is the operation a coup is, so the suppression is not obstruction for its own sake but the measure working.
It bears the load as the third subtraction and the deepest. Because combined arms cannot be learned below the scale at which it is fought, and because manoeuvre is lateral communication under fire, the channel that would let two units conspire is identically the channel that would let them fight together, and there is nothing to grant that grants only one.

## Apparent

Order, and a well-run headquarters: traffic routed, exercises proportionate to need, nothing improvised between formations behind the staff's back.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Let No Two Units Speak](plot_let_no_two_units_speak.md), an empty field standing for everything the army will not be able to do.
It works together with [The Same List](piece_the_same_list.md), being the clearest case of a capability that cannot be issued in one direction only.
