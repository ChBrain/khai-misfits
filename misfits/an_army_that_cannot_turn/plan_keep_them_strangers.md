# Plan: Keep Them Strangers

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the minder's seat](position_minder.md), inside [An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Minder](position_minder.md)

## Direction

Prevent a following from forming and a channel from opening. Move commanders in their second year, before the men begin to speak of the colonel rather than of the battalion. Prefer, where the difference is not decisive, the officer whose family and region can be vouched for over the officer who is merely better. Route traffic between formations through this office, so that no two units acquire the habit of arranging things between themselves. And decline the exercise above battalion scale on the plain ground that a division moving at scale cannot be told apart from a division moving on the capital. Accuse nobody, and mean it: what is being removed is a capability, and a perfectly loyal officer has it too.

## Orders

([The Minder](persona_the_minder.md) works through [Shuffling](process_shuffling.md) at [the Roster](place_the_roster.md), on [the Shuffle](piece_the_shuffle.md), and through [Silencing](process_silencing.md) at [the Manoeuvre Ground](place_the_manoeuvre_ground.md), holding [the Silence](piece_the_silence.md).)

## Implementation

([The Roster](place_the_roster.md) is where a career is shortened before it becomes a base; [the Manoeuvre Ground](place_the_manoeuvre_ground.md) is where the formation exercise is not held. [Shuffling](process_shuffling.md) prevents the following, and [Silencing](process_silencing.md) prevents the channel, and neither has an instrument that distinguishes the conspiracy from the cohesion, because there is none to be had.)

## Targets

- [ ] no commander holds a unit long enough for a personal following to form
- [ ] no two formations hold an unsupervised channel to each other
- [ ] no exercise is run at a scale that could not be told from the thing it would rehearse
- [ ] nobody is accused, the practice working on a capability and not on a person
- [ ] the mandate is discharged exactly as written, which is the whole of the difficulty
