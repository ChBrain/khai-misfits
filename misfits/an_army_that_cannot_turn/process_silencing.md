# Process: Silencing

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). Borne at [The Manoeuvre Ground](place_the_manoeuvre_ground.md), precipitated by [The Minder](persona_the_minder.md) striking the larger serial from the training calendar and routing the traffic, in [Let No Two Units Speak](plot_let_no_two_units_speak.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The informational requirement that units be unable to arrange things between themselves, met by suppressing lateral communication and curtailing exercise above battalion scale.

## Direction

From an army toward a set of battalions: no formation rehearsing at a scale that could carry it to the capital, and no formation rehearsing at the scale it would have to fight at.

## Lever

That a division moving at scale cannot be distinguished from a division moving on the capital, and that manoeuvre is lateral communication under fire, so the channel that would let two units conspire is the channel that would let them fight together (Quinlivan).

## Echo

[The Silence](piece_the_silence.md), an army that cannot combine arms because it has never trained above the level at which combined arms begins, and cannot manoeuvre because it may not speak sideways.
