# Position: Colonel

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The seat that is loyal in fact, obstructed anyway, and left to present the bill it did not incur.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A unit, for about eighteen months, with the officers it is sent.
A ceiling it knows precisely: battalion-scale training, no joint rehearsal with the flank, no signals net it may use without routing.
No ambition on the capital, and no way of proving it that would change anything.

## Orders

Be ready anyway: train to the ceiling, keep the gunnery honest, and request the exercise, the ammunition and the joint rehearsal again this year.
Fight when it comes, with what the arrangement permits.
Accept what the seat does: it asks for readiness, and readiness is the capability the arrangement exists to withhold.

## Loses

Its own case, since the more professionally it presses for what a unit needs, the more it resembles the thing being guarded against.
Because the requests that would make it effective are the requests that would make it dangerous, the seat loses the ordinary remedy of making a better argument, and insisting harder is read as the symptom rather than the cure.
It keeps its integrity and loses every capability that depends on someone else agreeing.

## Drives

Toward the one seat trying to make the army work and holding no lever that would,
training to the ceiling, meeting the flanking formation for the first time in the war, and discovering there what the two of them cannot do together,
so the force fails without any failure of nerve, beaten by a design that was answering a different question.
