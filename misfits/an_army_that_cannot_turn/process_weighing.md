# Process: Weighing

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). Borne at [The Palace](place_the_palace.md), precipitated by [The Ruler](persona_the_ruler.md) pricing two dangers that do not run at the same speed, in [The Fear Is Not Imagined](plot_the_fear_is_not_imagined.md) and [The Only Cure Is the Threat](plot_the_only_cure_is_the_threat.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A ruler holding an instrument that can be turned on him, in a region where it recently has been.

## Direction

From two dangers set side by side toward the one that would arrive first, the near threat certain in kind and personally fatal, the far one deferred, probabilistic, and borne mostly by others.

## Lever

That the arithmetic is correct on the seat's own horizon, so the calculation cannot be improved by care, and that where the far danger grows large enough the same arithmetic returns the other answer, rulers under severe external threat professionalising and paying for it in exposure (Talmadge).

## Echo

[The Two Clocks](piece_the_two_clocks.md), and later [The Same List](piece_the_same_list.md), the discovery that the remedy and the danger are one enumeration.
