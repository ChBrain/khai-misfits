# Process: Reckoning

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). Borne at [The Front](place_the_front.md), precipitated by [The Colonel](persona_the_colonel.md) being asked to do the three things the arrangement removed, in [The Day the War Comes](plot_the_day_the_war_comes.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A war arriving at a force built to answer a different question, and asking it to concentrate, to combine and to move.

## Direction

From an army on paper toward what it can actually do: no concentration, because the other force answers elsewhere; no combination, because it has never exercised at this scale; and no movement, because it may not speak to its flank.

## Lever

That each of the three failures traces to a separate measure, and each of those measures was a proportionate answer to a real internal danger, so the defeat is the sum of sound decisions and contains no error to find.

## Echo

[The Bill](piece_the_bill.md), collected from men who did not strike the bargain, with no failure of courage anywhere in the account and nothing left that could be undone in time.
