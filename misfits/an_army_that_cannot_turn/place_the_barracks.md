# Place: The Barracks

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). Where the second force sits beside the first, better found than it, and does not train with it. Hosts [Raise the Other Army](plot_raise_the_other_army.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two gates on the same road: newer vehicles behind one of them, and behind the other a formation that has been waiting on spare parts since spring.

## Holds

[The Counterweight](piece_the_counterweight.md), a force answering separately so that no single command can take the state.

## Offers

To the ruler, a capital that cannot be seized by whoever holds the army, and a genuinely capable formation to hold it.

## Withheld

Any joint exercise between the two, and so any possibility of fighting as one, since a guard that can be combined with the army is a guard that can be commanded by whoever combines them.
