# Position: Minder

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The seat that prevents a following from forming and a channel from opening, and that has no instrument distinguishing either from readiness.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The posting orders, a seat at exercise planning, and the routing of traffic between formations.
A mandate that is precise, lawful, and exactly what it says: no personal command, no unsupervised lateral channel, no formation manoeuvre at a scale that cannot be told from a march on the capital.
No accusation against anyone, the practice working on a capability rather than on a person.

## Orders

Keep them strangers: move commanders in their second year, prefer the officer who can be vouched for, and let no two units build a habit of working together.
Decline the division-scale exercise on the ground that it cannot be distinguished from the thing it would rehearse.
Accept what the seat does: it removes a capability that a perfectly loyal officer also has, and it is meant to.

## Loses

Any test that separates cohesion from conspiracy, there being none, so the seat cannot deliver one without the other.
Because the following that would carry a unit into the capital is the following that would carry it across a river under fire, the seat loses the ability to succeed narrowly, and doing the job better empties the army faster.
It keeps a service that cannot be turned and loses the service that could be used.

## Drives

Toward the most conscientious official in the play and the seat through which the army is emptied,
signing the routine order, attending the planning meeting, and asking that the traffic come through the office,
so a mandate discharged exactly as written is the route by which the state disarms itself, correct in every posting and fatal in the aggregate.
