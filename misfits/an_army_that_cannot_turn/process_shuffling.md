# Process: Shuffling

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). Borne at [The Roster](place_the_roster.md), precipitated by [The Minder](persona_the_minder.md) signing a routine posting order, in [Move Them Before They Settle](plot_move_them_before_they_settle.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The personnel requirement that no officer become a man worth following, met by selecting on what can be vouched for and moving commanders in their second year.

## Direction

From an officer corps toward a roster: nobody in place long enough to be followed anywhere, and nobody in place long enough to learn the ground, the men, or the trade.

## Lever

That the following which would carry a unit into the capital is the following which would carry it across a river under fire, so the practice cannot succeed narrowly, and no test exists that tells cohesion from conspiracy (Quinlivan).

## Echo

[The Shuffle](piece_the_shuffle.md), commanders who cannot take a unit with them and units that never become units, with nobody accused of anything at any point.
