# Piece: The Shuffle

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The personnel lever: officers chosen for what can be vouched for, and moved on before a following can form.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Roster](place_the_roster.md), in signed and routine posting orders that nobody finds remarkable and that name no one as a risk (Quinlivan).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and a commander stays long enough to become a man his men would follow anywhere, which is the capability the arrangement exists to deny, so the rotation is not spite but the measure doing precisely its work.
It bears the load as the second subtraction, and as the reason nobody can be blamed. Because what is being removed is a capability rather than a disloyalty, a perfectly loyal officer has it too and is moved too, so there is no accusation to rebut and no innocence that helps.

## Apparent

Fairness, and something like it: commands circulated rather than held, careers broadened, no fiefdoms, which reads as sound personnel policy in any army.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Move Them Before They Settle](plot_move_them_before_they_settle.md), taking the years in which a unit would have become a unit.
It works together with [The Silence](piece_the_silence.md), the one preventing the following and the other the channel, neither with an instrument that separates the conspiracy from the readiness.
