# Plan: Stand Apart

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the guard's seat](position_guard.md), inside [An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Guard](position_guard.md)

## Direction

Hold the capital and answer to one man. Take the establishment you were given, which grants you first call on fuel, transport and spare parts, and take it openly, since it is written down and not stolen. Be excellent, because excellence is what you were raised for and what makes the arrangement credible. And come under no field commander in any circumstance whatever, including the emergency, especially the emergency: a guard that can be folded into the army in a crisis is a guard that can be commanded by the man who folds it, which is precisely the arrangement you exist to prevent.

## Orders

([The Guard](persona_the_guard.md) stands through [Counterweighting](process_counterweighting.md) at [the Barracks](place_the_barracks.md), as [the Counterweight](piece_the_counterweight.md).)

## Implementation

([The Barracks](place_the_barracks.md) is where the second force sits beside the first and does not train with it; [Counterweighting](process_counterweighting.md) splits the sword so no single hand can lift it, and the same split means the two halves cannot be lifted together by anyone, including by the man they both answer to.)

## Targets

- [ ] the capital is held and the chain of command runs to one man
- [ ] the establishment's priority on supply is taken openly, being written and not taken
- [ ] the formation is genuinely good, since a counterweight that could not fight would not weigh
- [ ] no field commander acquires authority over it, in the emergency or out of it
- [ ] the quality achieved is understood to be subtracted from the army rather than added to the state
