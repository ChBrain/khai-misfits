# Process: Counterweighting

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). Borne at [The Barracks](place_the_barracks.md), precipitated by [The Guard](persona_the_guard.md) answering to one man and to no field command, in [Raise the Other Army](plot_raise_the_other_army.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The structural requirement that no single command be able to seize the state, met by raising a second force that answers separately.

## Direction

From one sword toward two half-swords: a capital that cannot be taken by whoever holds the army, and a field force that has lost the men, the equipment and the supply priority now standing behind the other gate.

## Lever

That the separateness is the whole of the value and cannot be relaxed in the emergency, since a guard that can be folded into the army is a guard that can be commanded by the man who folds it (Quinlivan).

## Echo

[The Counterweight](piece_the_counterweight.md), two forces that cannot be concentrated by one order and compete for one supply, the best formation in the country subtracted from the state's usable strength.
