# Piece: The Bill

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). What the war collects: a force that cannot concentrate, cannot combine and cannot move, for three separate reasons that were three sound answers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Front](place_the_front.md), presented to men who were not in the room where the purchase was made and cannot decline it.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the arrangement is costless, a ruler buying security with something nobody needed, and the play has no second face to show.
It bears the load as the price made visible and as the proof that no error was made. Because each of the three failures traces to a separate measure and each measure was proportionate to a real internal danger, the defeat is the sum of sound decisions and contains no mistake to find, no incompetent to relieve, and no moment where a different person would have chosen otherwise.

## Apparent

A rout, and it will be written up as one: poor leadership, poor morale, an army that would not fight, which is the account that fits what was seen and misses every cause of it.
