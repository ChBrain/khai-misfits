# Position: Ruler

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The seat that must be defended from the instrument it holds, and that prices two dangers against each other correctly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The power to raise a second force, to sign a posting order, and to decline an exercise.
Two threats on different clocks, the coup near and personal and fatal to the occupant, the war deferred and probabilistic and borne mostly by others.
No arrangement of the army that is both safe to hold and useful to have.

## Orders

Hold the throne: survive the danger that would end this month before the danger that may never arrive.
Buy the counterweight, approve the rotation, decline the formation exercise, each on its own merits.
Accept what the seat does: it is choosing between two goods it cannot both have, and choosing rightly on its own horizon.

## Loses

The army, which it wanted and said so sincerely, an army that loses being a throne that falls.
Because the list that would restore the fighting is identically the list that would restore the deposing, the seat loses the option of simply buying competence, and deliberating longer returns the same answer sooner.
It keeps the palace and loses the border.

## Drives

Toward the one actor who could take the other side of the trade and for whom taking it is worst,
weighing a certain near death against an uncertain far defeat and choosing as any occupant of the seat would choose,
so the most clear-eyed calculation in the play is the one that disarms the state, correct in every step and ruinous in the sum.
