# Place: The Manoeuvre Ground

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The field where the formation exercise would be held, defined entirely by the fact that it is not. Hosts [Let No Two Units Speak](plot_let_no_two_units_speak.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Ground wide enough for a division and a training calendar that stops at battalion, with the larger serial proposed each year and struck each year for reasons that are sound.

## Holds

[The Silence](piece_the_silence.md), the exercise not scheduled and the lateral channel not opened.

## Offers

To the ruler, the certainty that no formation is rehearsing a movement at a scale that could carry it to the capital.

## Withheld

Combined arms, which cannot be learned below the scale at which it is fought, and manoeuvre, which is lateral communication under fire and cannot be practised by units forbidden to speak to each other out of it.
