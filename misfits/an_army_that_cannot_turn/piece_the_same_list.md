# Piece: The Same List

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The enumeration read twice: promote on merit, exercise at formation scale, push initiative down, let units speak freely, which is what an army needs to fight and what a faction needs to depose.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Palace](place_the_palace.md), at the moment someone proposes that the army simply be made good, and read there in full before the proposal is answered.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the trap is ordinary miserliness, correctable by the ordinary means: a ruler withholding capability out of stinginess or suspicion could be shown the cost, argued with, or outlived, and the problem would yield to a better case.
It bears the load as the reason no case works. Because the four things that restore the fighting are the four things that restore the deposing, the remedy cannot be separated from the danger by any amount of good faith on either side, and a ruler wholly convinced that his officers are loyal still cannot buy competence without buying the means of his own removal.

## Apparent

An oversight, and it reads as one from outside: a list of obvious reforms that any competent state would have adopted, which is exactly how the arrangement looks to anyone who has not read the second column.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [The Only Cure Is the Threat](plot_the_only_cure_is_the_threat.md), where the remedy is produced and found to be the danger itself.
It works together with [The Two Clocks](piece_the_two_clocks.md), the one making the trade rational and the other making it inescapable.
