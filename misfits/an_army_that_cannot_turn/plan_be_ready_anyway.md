# Plan: Be Ready Anyway

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the colonel's seat](position_colonel.md), inside [An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Colonel](position_colonel.md)

## Direction

Train to the ceiling and keep the unit as good as the ceiling allows. Get the gunnery honest, get the drivers moving at night, get the signals net reaching as far as it is permitted to reach. Request the live ammunition, the formation exercise and the joint rehearsal with the unit on the flank, and request them again next year when they are refused again. Understand what is being asked of the request and what it will look like: readiness is the capability the arrangement exists to withhold, so the more professionally the case is pressed the more it resembles the thing being guarded against, and there is no version of the argument that escapes this. Fight when it comes, with what is permitted.

## Orders

([The Colonel](persona_the_colonel.md) fights through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) at [the Front](place_the_front.md), meeting [the Bill](piece_the_bill.md).)

## Implementation

([The Front](place_the_front.md) is where the arrangement is tested by someone who was not consulted about it; [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) asks the force to concentrate, to combine and to move, and it can do none of the three, for three separate reasons each of which was a reasonable answer to a real internal danger.)

## Targets

- [ ] the unit is trained to the ceiling, and the ceiling is known precisely
- [ ] the exercise, the ammunition and the joint rehearsal are requested, and requested again
- [ ] the requests are understood to be indistinguishable from the danger, so pressing harder does not help
- [ ] the war is fought with what is permitted, and fought without failure of nerve
- [ ] the bill is presented to the ones who did not strike the bargain
