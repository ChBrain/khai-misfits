## Cue

## Action

Through [Silencing](process_silencing.md), [the Minder](persona_the_minder.md) on [Keep Them Strangers](plan_keep_them_strangers.md) holds [the Silence](piece_the_silence.md): the larger serial is proposed again this year and struck again, on the plain ground that a division moving at scale cannot be told apart from a division moving on the capital, and traffic between formations is routed through his office so that no two units acquire the habit of arranging things between themselves. The scene closes with an army that cannot combine arms, combined arms not being learnable below the scale at which it is fought, and cannot manoeuvre, manoeuvre being lateral communication under fire (Quinlivan).

## Stage

The ground for the refusal is the ground it appears to be. Two formations rehearsing a coordinated movement at scale are rehearsing the operation a coup is, and there is no version of the exercise that trains the one and not the other, so a headquarters that approved it would be approving the rehearsal of its own overthrow.

## Tension

This is the clearest case in the play of a capability that cannot be issued in one direction only: the channel that would let two units conspire is identically the channel that would let them fight together, so the emptiest field in the production carries everything the army will not be able to do, and the request that would fill it is indistinguishable, from the office that must rule on it, from the thing the office exists to prevent.
