# Plan: Hold the Throne

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the ruler's seat](position_ruler.md), inside [An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Ruler](position_ruler.md)

## Direction

Survive the danger that could end this month before the danger that may never arrive. The coup is frequent in this region, recent in this country, and would end with you personally; the war is deferred, uncertain, and would be borne mostly by other people, so weigh them on the clocks they actually run on and not on the clocks a foreign adviser prefers. Buy the counterweight, approve the rotation, decline the formation exercise, each on its own merits and each correctly. Want a capable army, and say so sincerely, an army that loses being a throne that falls. And when it is put to you that the army should simply be made good, read the list of what that requires before answering.

## Orders

([The Ruler](persona_the_ruler.md) weighs through [Weighing](process_weighing.md) at [the Palace](place_the_palace.md), setting [the Two Clocks](piece_the_two_clocks.md), and later reads [the Same List](piece_the_same_list.md) and holds [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md).)

## Implementation

([The Palace](place_the_palace.md) is where the two dangers are priced against each other; [Weighing](process_weighing.md) puts a near certainty against a far probability, so the arithmetic that any occupant of the seat would perform returns the answer that disarms the state, and performing it more carefully returns the same answer sooner.)

## Targets

- [ ] the internal danger is priced as what it is, frequent, recent, personal and fatal, and not inflated
- [ ] each measure is approved on its own merits, which each of them has
- [ ] the army is wanted, and wanted sincerely, since an army that loses is a throne that falls
- [ ] the list that would make the army able to fight is read in full, and found to be the list that would make it able to depose
- [ ] the trade is recognised as a trade and not as an oversight, which is the whole of the difficulty
