# Plot: Raise the Other Army

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Barracks](place_the_barracks.md), two gates on the same road, and the newer vehicles behind one of them.

## Action

Through [Counterweighting](process_counterweighting.md), [the Guard](persona_the_guard.md), from [the guard's seat](position_guard.md), on [Stand Apart](plan_stand_apart.md) becomes [the Counterweight](piece_the_counterweight.md): a force that holds the capital, answers to one man, comes under no field commander in any circumstance, and is better equipped and better trained than the army it sits beside, because a counterweight that could not fight would not weigh. It takes its priority on fuel and transport openly, that priority being written into its establishment rather than taken from anyone. The scene closes with the capital secured and the field army halved in the same act, two forces that cannot be concentrated by one order and draw on one stock (Quinlivan).

## Stage

The measure is the minimum the seat requires. Leave the whole sword in one hand and the ruler does not survive to have any other problem, so the counterweight is not an excess of caution but the structural condition of there being a state at all.

## Tension

The excellence of the second force is exactly what makes it useless to the first, since its separateness is its entire value and cannot be relaxed in the emergency, a guard that can be folded into the army being a guard that can be commanded by the man who folds it, so the best formation in the country is subtracted from the state's usable strength rather than added to it, and improving it widens the gap it was meant to cover.
