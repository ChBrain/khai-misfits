# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[An Army That Cannot Turn](play_an_army_that_cannot_turn.md). The condition and the hedge: the trap needs a credible internal threat, and it lifts where the external one dominates.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Palace](place_the_palace.md), inside the same weighing that produced the arrangement, since it is the same arithmetic returning a different answer (Talmadge).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play overreaches into a claim it cannot support, that authoritarian armies are uniformly incompetent and that coup-proofing explains every defeat, which the source literature does not say and the house will not stage.
It bears the load as the limit that keeps the finding true. Because rulers facing severe external threat do professionalise, do fight better for it, and do become easier to overthrow, and because one army can carry the trade struck differently in different units, what is staged is a trade with terms and not a law of nature, and the terms are what make it a misfit rather than a verdict on a form of government.

## Apparent

A qualification, and an easy one to skip: the sort of hedge that reads as academic caution and is in fact the difference between the finding and a slander.
