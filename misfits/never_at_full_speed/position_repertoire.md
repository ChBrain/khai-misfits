# Position: Repertoire

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). The seat of what is actually available: not what is known, but what has the repetitions.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Every technique the school was willing to test, at thousands of reps against resistance.
Every technique it was not, at zero.
No say in which list anything went on.

## Orders

Be built by whatever was safe to practise.
Be spent under duress.
Offer what is there.

## Loses

The distinction between what a person knows and what a person can do, which nobody notices until it matters.
Because the list was assembled by what a partner could survive rather than by what the moment would want, **the repertoire is a record of the club's safety and not of anybody's need**, and it is complete on the safe subset and empty on the rest.
It ends supplying a competent answer that was not the one held in reserve.

## Drives

Toward doing something that works,
out of what it actually contains,
so the seat that meets the threat is the seat the syllabus never asked.
