# Persona: The Partner

## Taxonomy

[Consent](position_consent.md). The person on the other side: the reason the exclusion is right, and the reason it cannot be relaxed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A colleague, a friend, somebody who came to train and expects to leave able to work on Monday.
Not an obstacle. The whole point of a club is that there is somebody to train with next week.
Their eyes, their throat, their fingers.

## Action

Turns up.
Offers resistance, at everything the school is willing to test.
Draws one line, and the line is not negotiable.

## Shadow

That the line is drawn exactly where the interesting techniques begin.
This is not squeamishness and it does not soften: a school that trained past it would have no members, and every remedy the play considers ends by asking this seat to absorb something, which is why every remedy stops short.
It ends protecting the very thing that makes the technique unknowable.

## Tell

Perfectly willing to be thrown hard, choked out, and armlocked to the point of tapping.
Not willing to be blinded, and correct.
