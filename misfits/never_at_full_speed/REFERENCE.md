# Never at Full Speed: Reference

The research warrant for Never at Full Speed.
The setting is a martial art with a syllabus; the play runs on the fact that a technique is excluded from
full-resistance practice in proportion to its severity, and on the measured finding that performance under
anxiety falls back on what has been practised most, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the excluded technique as **a capability that is believed and never made**: the most damaging techniques
are taken out of free practice precisely because they are damaging, which is the founding innovation of the
modern art and is what made pressure-testing anything possible; they are preserved honestly in the forms, named,
taught, graded and handed on by people who believe in them; a form transmits the shape and not the conditions,
so it is not a rehearsal; the sentence that explains the exclusion supplies the reason there is no evidence and
the claim of large effect at once; and under threat, attention goes to the threat and execution reverts to
whatever has the repetitions. The play models the founding exclusion, the partner whose limit makes it hold, the
custody that discharges its duty completely, the true sentence doing two jobs, the drilled subset that is
genuinely skilled, the narrowing under duress, and the extra session that measurably helps and measurably fails
to prevent the fall, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a fraudulent school or a
credulous student.

The governing law is that a technique's severity buys both its reputation and its exemption from rehearsal, so
confidence and the repetition count move in opposite directions off one quantity.

**Nobody in the sequence falls short, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The founder
removed the dangerous techniques for a good reason and the reason still holds. The instructor teaching the forms
believes in them and has no motive to mislead. The training partner who declines permanent damage is right and
is the reason a club exists at all. The student who infers a reserve is drawing the obvious inference from a true
statement. And the person under threat who reaches for the drilled thing is not losing their nerve.

**And the exclusion is right, which the play establishes before anything else.** Kano's move was to take the most
dangerous techniques out of free practice **so that free practice could be done at full effort**, and to keep them
in the forms. That is the innovation, not the compromise: it is what produced a body of technique tried thousands
of times against people trying to stop it. **A school that spars everything has no members left**, so the line is
held by arithmetic rather than by squeamishness and is not a policy a braver generation could revisit.

**Then the preservation, which is entirely open.** The excluded techniques are not hidden, denied or quietly
dropped. They are named in public, demonstrated, corrected, examined and passed on. Nothing in this account
requires anybody to conceal or exaggerate anything, which is what makes it a structure rather than a fraud.

**Then the fact that inverts it.** A form is not a rehearsal. Practice transfers to performance when it samples
the information and the demands the performer will actually meet, and a demonstration on a compliant partner
samples neither, because sampling them is exactly what the exclusion forbids. **So the transmission can be
faultless and carry none of what was needed**: the shape survives a century and was never the part that goes
missing.

**Then the sentence, which is where the trap seals.** _We do not spar that, because it would injure you._ It is
true, it is said in good faith, and it does two things in one breath: it supplies the reason there is no evidence
**and** it supplies the claim of large effect. A listener takes both away together, so **the belief accounts for
its own untestedness** and leaves no residue that would want explaining. Asked what the evidence is, it gives the
reason there is none, and that is a good answer.

**Then the night it happens, and the measurement is unkind.** Under anxiety attention shifts to the threat and
away from the task, and execution falls back on what has been performed most. Police officers who additionally
train a martial art once a week perform better under anxiety than officers relying on regular training alone,
**and that additional training does not prevent performance from falling under anxiety.** More practice buys
improvement and does not buy immunity, and it is practice of the permitted subset, so it deepens the list that
already had the repetitions and adds nothing to the other.

**And the deficit is not evidential, which is the sharpest thing here.** Stipulate the technique's efficacy
perfectly established by every means available, with no gap in the literature whatever, and nothing changes,
because **efficacy is not availability.** One is a fact about the technique and the other is a count of times this
particular body has performed it against unwilling opposition, and no fact established anywhere else adds a
single repetition to it. What is missing is a motor skill, not a warrant.

**And every settlement buys realism by giving up the thing that was excluded.** Protective equipment lets people
strike harder and changes what is being struck. Progressive resistance gets closer and stops short. Scenario
training under induced stress genuinely works and is the strongest of them, and it still cannot deliver the
excluded technique at full effect, because delivering it is precisely what was ruled out. **The one experiment
that would settle this is the one nobody may run, and the reason nobody may run it is the reason it is worth
running.**

It is distinct from the house's **A Lower Bar Set by a Higher One**, which is the nearest incumbent and stages the
surgical placebo problem, whose governing law is that the bar protecting a volunteer rises with the harm the
control arm would bear, so the evidence obtainable falls as that bar rises. Remove that engine entirely, by
stipulating a control arm that costs nothing and an ethics committee that never refuses a comparison, and this
misfit is unchanged, because **there it is the control that cannot be given and here it is the treatment.** A
surgeon performs the operation constantly and lacks only something to compare it against; the practitioner has
never once performed the technique at all. And the deficits are different in kind: that play's harm is that a
decision-maker lacks a warrant, and this one's is that a performer lacks the repetitions, which is why
establishing the efficacy by other means closes that play and leaves this one exactly where it was.

It is distinct from the house's **The Cargo Cult**, which stages form copied without cause, where the ritual is
reproduced by people who do not understand what made the original work. Remove that engine, by stipulating that
every instructor understands the mechanism of the technique completely and can explain the anatomy of it, and
this stands entire, because **understanding a technique does not rehearse it.** That play needs the cause to be
unknown; this one grants that it is fully known, taught accurately, and still never performed.

It is distinct from the house's **Proved on Somebody Else**, which stages the efficacy-effectiveness gap, where a
treatment is tested under conditions unlike the conditions of use. Remove that engine, by stipulating trial
conditions identical to use conditions in every respect, and this is untouched, because **here there is no trial
at all.** That play needs a test whose setting misleads; this one has no test, and the reason there is none is the
same fact that makes the technique worth having.

It is distinct from the house's **Kept for the Worst**, which stages the antibiotic conservation conflict, where a
drug's value is preserved by withholding it and the withholding destroys the case for making it. Remove that
engine, by stipulating no conservation motive anywhere and free use whenever anybody wishes, and this stands,
because **nothing here is being conserved.** The excluded technique is not being saved for later: it is
unpractisable now and would be unpractisable at any level of willingness to spend it.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the school as
  fraudulent, the instructor as self-deceiving or the student as credulous, because nothing in the sequence has a
  decision to get wrong: the founding exclusion was an advance and its reason still holds, the preservation is
  done in the open by people who believe in what they teach and have no motive to mislead, the training partner
  who declines permanent damage is right and is the reason a club exists, the inference of a reserve is the
  obvious reading of a true sentence, and the person who reaches for the drilled technique under threat is not
  losing their nerve. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that
  severity buys the confidence and the exemption with one coin.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Establishing the technique's
  efficacy by other means changes nothing, because efficacy is not availability. Explaining the mechanism in
  full changes nothing, because understanding is not rehearsal. Training more helps and was measured helping,
  and does not prevent the fall, and is training of the permitted subset. Protective equipment changes what is
  being struck, progressive resistance stops short, and scenario training under stress, which is the strongest
  remedy available, still cannot deliver the excluded technique at full effect, because delivering it is exactly
  what was ruled out.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed as
  the house's. The staging is the house's; the founding exclusion is Kano's, the measurement of arrest and
  self-defence performance under anxiety is Renden, Landman, Savelsbergh and Oudejans's, the model of how anxiety
  acts on perceptual-motor performance is Nieuwenhuys and Oudejans's, and representative learning design is
  Pinder, Davids, Renshaw and Araújo's. The bounds are carried with them, and one is owed plainly: **the account
  of Kano's exclusion is given as the historical record of how Kodokan judo was constituted**, and the 1932
  lecture is cited for where he set out his rationale rather than quoted, that text not having been read here.
  The others: **the anxiety studies are of police officers performing arrest and self-defence tasks**, so
  applying them to martial-arts curricula generally is the play's extension rather than the authors' finding;
  representative learning design is a framework for designing practice and not a measurement of any particular
  syllabus; the structural halves, that severity gates rehearsal and that the stated reason closes the enquiry,
  are offered as descriptions and not as measured results; how much ordinary instruction this accounts for is
  genuinely contested and the play adjudicates none of it; and nothing here is a judgement about whether any
  excluded technique works, since the play grants that it does and that granting it is the mechanism. **The play
  makes no claim about any school, any art, any instructor or any student, and gives no self-defence, training or
  safety advice of any kind**, only that a thing kept for the worst night is a thing nobody has ever done.
