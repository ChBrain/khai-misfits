# Process: Excluding

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). Taking the dangerous techniques out of free practice: the founding move, and an advance.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The observation that a school which spars everything runs out of members.
At [the mat](place_the_mat.md), once at the founding and again in every generation.

## Direction

Toward free practice that can be done at full effort.
This is the innovation and not the compromise. Kano removed the most dangerous techniques from randori precisely so randori could be practised without restraint, and that is what made pressure-testing anything possible at all.
The result is a body of technique that has been tried thousands of times against people trying to stop it, which almost nothing else in the field can say.

## Lever

The line at [consent](position_consent.md): what may be done to somebody who has to work on Monday.
It is not a policy and does not soften, so this is not a decision that gets revisited when the culture changes.

## Echo

An art that can test itself,
at the price of a list of things it may not test.
