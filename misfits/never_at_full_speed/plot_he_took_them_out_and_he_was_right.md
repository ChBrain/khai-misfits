# Plot: He Took Them Out, and He Was Right

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The founding: a school deciding which techniques may be used in free practice.
[The Partner](persona_the_partner.md), who has to be there again on Thursday.

## Action

[Excluding](process_excluding.md): the dangerous techniques come out of free practice at [the mat](place_the_mat.md), so that free practice can be done at full effort.

## Stage

[The Partner](persona_the_partner.md), in [the consent seat](position_consent.md), on [Set the Limit and Hold It](plan_set_the_limit_and_hold_it.md), draws the line and does not move it.

## Tension

This plot's work is to establish that the exclusion is **an advance**, because a play that opens by calling it a compromise has nothing to say later.
Kano removed the most dangerous techniques from randori precisely so that randori could be practised without restraint, and that is what made pressure-testing possible at all. The result is a body of technique tried thousands of times against people trying to stop it, which almost nothing else in the field can say.
And the line is not a policy that a braver generation could revisit. **A school that trained past it would have no members and no next week**, so the exclusion holds by arithmetic rather than by squeamishness, and it will hold for as long as clubs need people in them.
