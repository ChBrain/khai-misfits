# Persona: The Severe Technique

## Taxonomy

[Severity](position_severity.md). The move with the highest claimed effect and the lowest number of repetitions: the eye, the throat, the small joint, the neck past where a neck goes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not doubted by anybody, which is the whole difficulty.
The play grants that it works. Granting it is not a concession: it is the mechanism, because what makes it unpractisable is precisely that it works on the person you would practise it on.
Named in every syllabus, believed by everyone in the room.

## Action

Sits in the curriculum.
Gets demonstrated, slowly, to a compliant partner.
Waits.

## Shadow

That the same property doing the believing is the one doing the excluding.
Severity buys its reputation and buys its exemption from testing with one coin, so the more anybody credits it the less anybody has ever done it, and there is no version where the credit arrives without the exemption.
It ends the most trusted thing in the syllabus and the least performed.

## Tell

Everyone can describe it.
Nobody can say when they last did it to somebody who did not want it done.
