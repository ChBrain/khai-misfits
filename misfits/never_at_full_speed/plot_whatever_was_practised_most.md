## Tension

This is where the harm lands, and it lands on a person who trained hard and was told the truth.
Under anxiety attention shifts to the threat and away from the task, and what is executed is not what is known but what has been performed most often. This is measured, it happens to trained and experienced people, and knowing about it does not switch it off. **Selection under duress is by repetition and not by merit**, so the syllabus is never consulted.
And the deficit is not evidential. Stipulate the technique's efficacy perfectly established by every means available: the count is unchanged, because **efficacy is not availability.** One is a fact about the technique and the other is a fact about this body, and no fact established anywhere else adds a single repetition to it.
