# Persona: The Threat

## Taxonomy

[Duress](position_duress.md). The night it happens: the conditions of use, arriving without notice and deciding what is available.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not an opponent in any sporting sense.
Somebody who has not agreed to anything, in a place nobody chose, with no referee and no mat.
The only conditions that were ever the point.

## Action

Arrives.
Takes the attention.
Leaves whatever is left to do the work.

## Shadow

That it does not select the best technique available. It selects the most practised one.
Under anxiety attention goes to the threat rather than to the task, and execution falls back on what has the repetitions, which is a property of how people work under duress and not a failure of nerve in any particular person.
It ends spending a repertoire that was assembled by what was safe to drill.

## Tell

Everything the person knows is still known.
Almost none of it is reachable.
