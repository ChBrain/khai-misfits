# Piece: One Sentence, Two Jobs

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). _We do not spar that, because it would injure you._ True, said in good faith, and doing two things at once.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Said at [the mat](place_the_mat.md), the first time a student asks.
Carried afterwards by [the reserve](persona_the_reserve.md), unexamined.

## Load Bearing

Without it somebody notices the absence of evidence and goes looking, and looking would eventually find something.
It is load bearing because it **closes the enquiry**: it supplies the reason there is no test and the claim of large effect in one breath, so the belief accounts for its own untestedness and leaves no residue that would want explaining.

## Apparent

An honest disclosure, which is exactly what it is.
Nobody is being deceived and no claim is being smuggled: the sentence is true in both of its jobs, and that is what makes it durable rather than merely convenient.

## Yearbook

Spoken by instructors who believe it, spent at [the night it happens](place_the_night_it_happens.md).
Asked what the evidence is, it gives the reason there is none, and the room is satisfied because that is a good answer.
