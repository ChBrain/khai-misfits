# Piece: The Repetition Count

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Attached to every technique in the school, unwritten and known to nobody.
Thousands, at [the mat](place_the_mat.md). Zero, at [the syllabus](place_the_syllabus.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it this is an argument about evidence, and arguments about evidence can be settled by better arguments.
It is load bearing because it makes the deficit **quantitative and personal**: what is missing is not a warrant that the technique works, it is the count of times this particular body has performed it against unwilling opposition, and no fact established anywhere else adds one to it.

## Apparent

A gap in the evidence base, to be closed by research.
Stipulate the technique's efficacy perfectly established by every means available and the count is unchanged, because **efficacy is not availability.** One is about the technique and the other is about the person.
