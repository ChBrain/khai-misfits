# Plot: Once a Week Is Not Nothing

## Owner

## Cue

## Action

## Stage

[The Partner](persona_the_partner.md), in [the consent seat](position_consent.md), is asked by every proposed remedy to absorb something, and every remedy stops short.

## Tension

The settlement, which is real and measured, and its limit, which is in the same result.
Officers with an additional martial-arts session a week perform better under anxiety than officers relying on regular training alone, **and that additional training does not prevent performance falling under anxiety.** More practice buys improvement and does not buy immunity.
And it is practice of the permitted subset, so it deepens the list that already had the repetitions and adds nothing to the other one. Every other remedy trades the same way: protective gear lets people hit harder and changes what is being hit; progressive resistance gets closer and stops short; scenario training under induced stress is the best of them and still cannot deliver the excluded technique at full effect, because delivering it is what was ruled out.
**The one experiment that would settle this is the one nobody may run**, and the reason nobody may run it is the reason it is worth running.
