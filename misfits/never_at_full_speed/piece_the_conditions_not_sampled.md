# Piece: The Conditions Not Sampled

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). Practice that does not resemble use does not transfer to use: the formal version of why a form is not a rehearsal.

## Owner

## Place

Between [the syllabus](place_the_syllabus.md) and [the night it happens](place_the_night_it_happens.md).
Studied in the learning-design literature, enacted every week by [preserving](process_preserving.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the forms are a partial rehearsal and the deficit is a matter of degree.
It is load bearing because it says what a practice must contain to count: the information and the demands the performer will actually face. A form supplies neither, so **the transmission can be perfect and still carry none of what was needed.**

## Apparent

A quibble about training methods, of interest to coaches.
It is the reason the shape survives a century and the skill was never made, and it applies with full force to a curriculum that is entirely honest about what it contains.
