# Persona: The Reserve

## Taxonomy

[Repertoire](position_repertoire.md). The supposed contents of what is available: a set of techniques believed to be in reserve, and not in it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The most reasonable inference available from what everybody is told.
There is a set of techniques the school will not spar, and the stated reason is that they are too damaging. Anybody hearing that concludes there is capability in reserve, and the conclusion is not stupid.
It is what the sentence means.

## Action

Forms, unprompted.
Requires nobody to claim anything.
Persists.

## Shadow

That it is built out of the absence of a test and reads as the result of one.
_We do not spar that, because it would injure you_ supplies the reason there is no evidence and the claim of high effect in the same breath, so the belief accounts for its own untestedness and leaves no residue that would want explaining.
It ends held with a confidence that nothing in the record supports and nothing in the record contradicts.

## Tell

Asked what the evidence is, gives the reason there is none.
And that answer satisfies the room, because it is a good one.
