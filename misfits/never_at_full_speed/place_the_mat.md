# Place: The Mat

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). Where full resistance happens, honestly and hard, for the permitted subset only.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

People trying to stop each other, at full effort, several times a week.
Techniques failing, being adjusted, and failing better.

## Holds

The good half of the production, which the play does not undercut.
What is tested here is genuinely tested. Thousands of repetitions against unwilling opposition make something real and available, and the people who have it are not fooling themselves about it.

## Offers

The only thing that reliably makes a technique available later: doing it, at speed, to somebody who does not want it done.

## Withheld

The list that never comes here.
The line is drawn where permanent damage begins, so the techniques with the largest claimed effect are precisely the ones this room may not examine, and it is not a room that could be persuaded otherwise.
