# Piece: The Narrowing

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). Under threat, attention goes to the threat and the repertoire shrinks to what has been done most.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the person, at [the night it happens](place_the_night_it_happens.md).
Measured in the anxiety and perceptual-motor literature, enacted by [reaching](process_reaching.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it a person under pressure selects the best technique they know and the reserve is available.
It is load bearing because selection under duress is by **repetition and not by merit**: attention shifts to the threat and away from the task, and execution falls back on what has been performed most, so the syllabus is never consulted.

## Apparent

Panic, or a failure of nerve, and therefore something a braver person avoids.
It is neither. It is a measured property of people performing under anxiety, it happens to trained and experienced performers, and knowing about it does not switch it off.

## Yearbook

Studied in police and sport performance under pressure, spent where it matters.
Everything the person knows is still known. Almost none of it is reachable.
