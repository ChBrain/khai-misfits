# Piece: The Extra Session

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). The measured settlement: one more training a week helps, and does not prevent the fall.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On a training schedule, and in the results.
Produced by [Once a Week Is Not Nothing](plot_once_a_week_is_not_nothing.md), and by nothing else in the play.

## Load Bearing

Without it the remedy is untried and the play is staging a dilemma it has not tested.
It is load bearing because it is **both halves at once**: officers with an additional martial-arts session a week perform better under anxiety than those without, and that additional training does not prevent performance falling under anxiety. More practice buys improvement and does not buy immunity.

## Apparent

The answer, since the thing that helps is known and can simply be done more.
More of what, is the question it does not answer. The extra session is practice of the permitted subset, so it deepens the list that already had the repetitions and adds nothing at all to the other one.

## Yearbook

Measured in police arrest and self-defence performance under anxiety, spent at [the night it happens](place_the_night_it_happens.md).
It is the piece that keeps the misfit honest: there is a real improvement available, it was measured, and it is not an exit.
