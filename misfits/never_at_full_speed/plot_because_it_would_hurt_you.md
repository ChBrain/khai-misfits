# Plot: Because It Would Hurt You

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A student asking why they never spar that.
An instructor answering truthfully, in one sentence.

## Action

[Believing](process_believing.md): the answer is given at [the mat](place_the_mat.md), and [The Reserve](persona_the_reserve.md) forms in the room without anybody claiming anything.

## Stage

[One Sentence, Two Jobs](piece_one_sentence_two_jobs.md) does both of them at once, and the enquiry closes.

## Tension

Here the trap seals, and it seals with a true sentence said in good faith.
_We do not spar that, because it would injure you._ Nobody is being deceived and no claim is being smuggled. But the sentence supplies the reason there is no evidence **and** the claim of large effect in the same breath, so a listener takes both away together and **the belief accounts for its own untestedness.**
Which leaves no residue. There is nothing unexplained to go looking about, because the absence has already been explained, and asked what the evidence is the sentence gives the reason there is none, and the room is satisfied, because that is a good answer.
