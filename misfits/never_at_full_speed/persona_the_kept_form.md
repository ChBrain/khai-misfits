# Persona: The Kept Form

## Taxonomy

[Custody](position_custody.md). The technique preserved: named, taught, demonstrated, graded, and honestly passed on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful work by people who are not lying about anything.
The excluded techniques are not hidden, denied or quietly dropped. They are written down, shown, corrected and examined, by instructors who believe in them and have every reason to.
**Everything here is done in the open.**

## Action

Preserves the shape.
Transmits it, accurately, across generations.
Passes it on to somebody who will do the same.

## Shadow

That a shape is not a rehearsal, and it was never the shape that goes missing.
Rehearsal means doing the thing under the conditions of use, against somebody who does not want it to work, and that is exactly what custody may not permit: so the transmission can be perfect and carry nothing of what was needed.
It ends handing over a form that is complete and a skill that was never made.

## Tell

The syllabus is intact after a hundred years.
The number is still zero.
