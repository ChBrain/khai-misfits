# Process: Believing

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). The exclusion read as reserve: a reasonable inference from what everybody is told.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A stated reason for a visible absence.
At [the mat](place_the_mat.md), the first time a student asks why they never spar that.

## Direction

Toward the conclusion that capability is being held back.
And the conclusion is not stupid. It is what the sentence means: told that a technique is not sparred because it would cause permanent damage, a listener takes away both that there is no test and that there is a large effect, and takes them away together.
So the belief forms without anybody claiming anything and without anybody being deceived.

## Lever

[One sentence, two jobs](piece_one_sentence_two_jobs.md), true in both of them.
It supplies the reason there is no evidence and the claim of high effect in one breath, so **the belief accounts for its own untestedness** and leaves nothing over that would want explaining.

## Echo

A room satisfied by the answer,
which was a good answer,
to the question of what the evidence is.
