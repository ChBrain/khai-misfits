# Position: Custody

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). The seat of preservation: what keeps the excluded technique alive across generations, in the open and in good faith.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A syllabus, a grading, a lineage and a set of forms.
Instructors who believe in what they are teaching and have no motive to mislead.
Every part of the technique except the conditions.

## Orders

Name it.
Teach it, demonstrate it, examine it.
Hand it on unchanged.

## Loses

Nothing it was ever given, which is why it cannot be blamed for what is missing.
A form transmits the shape faultlessly and cannot transmit performance under resistance, because performing under resistance is the thing custody may not permit, so **perfect transmission and an unmade skill are entirely compatible.**
It ends having discharged its duty completely.

## Drives

Toward keeping something valuable from being lost,
which it does,
so the seat that preserves the technique is the seat that certifies it without ever having seen it work.
