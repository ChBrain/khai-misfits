## Tension

The preservation is honest, and the play insists on that before it does anything else.
Nothing is hidden or denied. The excluded techniques are named in public, demonstrated, corrected, examined and handed on by instructors who believe in them and have no motive to mislead. **The whole account is in the open**, which is what makes this a structure rather than a fraud.
And a form is not a rehearsal. Practice transfers to use when it samples the information and the demands the performer will actually face, and a demonstration on a compliant partner samples neither, because sampling them is exactly what custody may not permit. **So the transmission can be faultless and carry none of what was needed**: the syllabus is intact after a hundred years and the count has never left zero.
