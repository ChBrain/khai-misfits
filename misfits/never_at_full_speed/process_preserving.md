# Process: Preserving

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). Keeping the excluded techniques in the forms: honest, careful, and complete in the wrong dimension.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The refusal to lose something valuable.
At [the syllabus](place_the_syllabus.md), continuously, across generations.

## Direction

Toward accurate transmission of the shape.
Nothing here is hidden or denied. The techniques are named, demonstrated, corrected, graded and passed to people who will do the same, by instructors who believe in them and have no motive to mislead: **the whole account is in the open.**
And what is transmitted is the form, because the form is the only part that can be handed over without a body to hand it over on.

## Lever

Repetition of the shape, with a compliant partner, at a speed nobody would call full.
Which transmits everything except the one thing rehearsal is for.

## Echo

A syllabus intact after a century,
and a count that has never left zero.
