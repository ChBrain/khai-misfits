# Pitch: We Do Not Spar That

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Quiet, after class, to somebody who has trained long enough to be told.
Fond of the art and unsentimental about it.
Unwilling to say the comfortable thing, and not interested in scoring a point off anybody.

## Undertow

Under the syllabus, two facts that do not negotiate.
What makes a technique too dangerous to practise is that it works on the person you would practise it on.
A skill that has never been performed under the conditions of use is not there under the conditions of use.

## Nerve

The unease of a true sentence, said in good faith,
that hands you the reason there is no evidence and the claim of a large effect together,
so that the thing you trust most is the thing nobody has ever once done.

## Echo

After the run: an honest syllabus, an instructor who believed it, a hundred years of faithful transmission,
a repertoire built out of whatever a training partner could survive,
and a count, on the techniques kept for the worst night, that has never left zero.
