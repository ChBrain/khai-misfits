# Process: Drilling

## Taxonomy

[Never at Full Speed](play_never_at_full_speed.md). The permitted subset getting the repetitions, week after week, against people trying to stop it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A technique a partner can survive at full resistance.
At [the mat](place_the_mat.md), twice a week, for years.

## Direction

Toward genuine skill in whatever was safe to test.
This is the good half of the production and the play does not undercut it: thousands of repetitions against unwilling opposition produce something real, reliable and available, and the people who have it are not fooling themselves.
The list of what gets this treatment was written by [consent](position_consent.md) and by nothing else.

## Lever

Volume, under resistance, in conditions that resemble use.
Which is what makes a skill available later, and is exactly what the excluded techniques are never given.

## Echo

A repertoire that is complete on one list
and empty on the other,
built by what a partner could survive rather than by what the night would want.
