# Process: Briefing the Bench

## Taxonomy

[The Same Print Twice](play_the_same_print_twice.md). The assignment arriving, delivered by people doing their jobs.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A case, an item, and an expert who needs to know what is being asked.
[The Investigator](persona_the_investigator.md), holding [Investigator](position_investigator.md).

## Direction

Toward an examiner who knows which comparison is wanted.
Toward [the bench](place_the_bench.md), with the file and the covering note.

## Lever

The impossibility of a request with no question attached, which is not something anybody can work on.

## Echo

[The Briefing](piece_the_briefing.md) at the bench, carrying the condition of the work and an account of the case in one delivery.
