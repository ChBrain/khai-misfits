## Place

## Load Bearing

The fix is a role rather than a rule, which is why agreeing with it changes nothing on its own.
Sequential unmasking works by releasing the context in a controlled order, and somebody has to hold the file, decide what may be said and when, and answer for those decisions: so a laboratory persuaded of the remedy is exactly where it was until the post is funded, and the post buys a delay in every case and a new place for a mistake.

## Apparent

An administrative detail. A workflow change, on paper.
The cheapest-sounding part of the proposal.

## Yearbook
