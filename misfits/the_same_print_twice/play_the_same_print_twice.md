# Play: The Same Print Twice

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Same Print Twice.
An examiner is handed a mark and a question. To answer it they have to be told something: which comparison to make, what the item is, what was asked. The mark itself is partial and smudged, as marks from the world are, and in the range where it is partial the answer is a judgement rather than a reading. The briefing that made the work possible sits in the same head as the judgement. Years later the same examiner, given the same mark with a different account of the case around it, reaches the other conclusion, honestly, and is as certain both times.

## Arc

The bend is this: the information that makes the examination possible is the information that moves it, and it cannot be handed over separately.
It runs on a task that cannot start from nothing.
[The Examiner](persona_the_examiner.md) needs to know what is being asked before they can answer it. [The Briefing](piece_the_briefing.md) is not gossip, it is the assignment, and there is no version of the work that begins without one.
And the material is ambiguous where it matters.
[The Mark](persona_the_mark.md) is partial and distorted, because marks left by the world are, and [the ambiguous zone](piece_the_ambiguous_zone.md) is where the whole value of an expert sits: an unambiguous comparison needs no examiner and produces no disagreement.
Then [The Same Expert, the Other Answer](plot_the_same_expert_the_other_answer.md).
Given the same mark inside a different account of the case, the same examiner reaches the opposite conclusion, and is honest and confident on both occasions. [The Earlier Conclusion](persona_the_earlier_conclusion.md) is the only party that contradicts them, and it is them.
And trying harder does not reach it. The examiner cannot subtract what they were told, cannot detect that the judgement moved, and gets no signal at the moment of deciding, since the experience of a contaminated judgement is exactly the experience of a clean one.
And the remedy is real and is a person. [The Order](persona_the_order.md) works by giving the context in a controlled sequence rather than withholding it, which needs somebody standing between the case and the bench to decide what may be said and when, so the fix is not a rule but a role, and a role is a post, a salary and a delay.
So the bench is left with a task that requires a briefing, a judgement the briefing moves, an expert who cannot feel it, and a repair that costs a person the laboratory does not have.

## Stakes

Whether an expert judgement can be insulated from the assignment that makes it possible.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a task that cannot begin without a briefing, an ambiguity that is where the expertise lives, a context delivered by the ordinary working of a laboratory, a reversal produced by the same honest person, an introspection that returns nothing, and a repair that is a post rather than a procedure, and away from any account in which somebody leaned on the examiner or the examiner wanted a result. The production cannot ask for the examination to begin from nothing, and it cannot ask the expert to notice a movement that has no sensation attached. What is left to fight over is not whether the two answers differed, the record shows they did, but what to call a judgement that is honest, expert, confident, and set by what the examiner had to be told.
