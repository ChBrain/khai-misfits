# Plot: The Zone Where Judgement Lives

## Taxonomy

[The Same Print Twice](play_the_same_print_twice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A partial and distorted mark, of the kind the world actually leaves.

## Action

[Comparing the mark](process_comparing_the_mark.md): [the examiner](persona_the_examiner.md) applies the method to every feature it names, and runs out of material before running out of question.

## Stage

[The Ambiguous Zone](piece_the_ambiguous_zone.md) is where the answer now has to come from somewhere other than the mark.

## Tension

The value of the expert and the exposure to context are one property of the material.
An unambiguous comparison needs no examiner and produces no disagreement between competent ones, so everything an expert adds sits precisely where the material underdetermines the answer. That is also the only range in which an account of the case could settle anything: something has to, and the mark has declined. The zone cannot be removed without removing the reason for the examination.
