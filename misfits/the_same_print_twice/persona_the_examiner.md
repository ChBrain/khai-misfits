# Persona: The Examiner

## Taxonomy

[Examiner](position_examiner.md). The expert, honest, skilled, and unable to feel it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Twenty years at a bench, a good record, and no stake in any outcome.
A mark, a question, and the training to answer it.

## Action

Reads the mark.
Reaches a conclusion, and is confident in it.

## Shadow

Cannot subtract what they were told.
There is no operation available to a mind that removes a known fact from a judgement already formed, and no sensation that marks a judgement as having moved: the experience of a contaminated conclusion is exactly the experience of a clean one, so care produces certainty rather than correction.

## Tell

Is telling the truth on both occasions.
