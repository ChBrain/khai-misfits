# Persona: The Investigator

## Taxonomy

[Investigator](position_investigator.md). The party who supplies the context, meaning nothing by it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A file, a covering note, a conversation in the corridor about what the case is.
Briefing an expert, which is what briefing an expert looks like.

## Action

Says what the case is.
Expects the examination to answer it.

## Shadow

Is doing the necessary thing and is the delivery mechanism.
Nobody here is leaning on anybody: the context travels with the assignment by the ordinary operation of a laboratory, and an investigator who said nothing would be handing over an item with no question attached, which is not a request that can be worked on.

## Tell

Cannot brief less without briefing nothing.
