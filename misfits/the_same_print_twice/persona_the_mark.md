# Persona: The Mark

## Taxonomy

[Mark](position_mark.md). The material, ambiguous where it matters.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A partial impression, distorted, incomplete, left by the world rather than by a laboratory.
Enough to work with and not enough to settle.

## Action

Supports more than one reading.
Does not adjudicate between them.

## Shadow

Is where the expert is for.
An unambiguous comparison needs no examiner and produces no disagreement, so the whole value of expertise sits in the range where the material underdetermines the answer, which is the same range in which anything else in the room can tip it.

## Tell

Is the reason an expert is needed and the reason the expert can be moved.
