# Piece: The Briefing

## Taxonomy

[The Bench](place_the_bench.md). The assignment, which is the condition of the work.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Arriving at [the bench](place_the_bench.md) with the case, through [briefing the bench](process_briefing_the_bench.md).

## Load Bearing

It cannot be separated into the part that enables the work and the part that moves the answer, because they are one delivery.
An examiner has to know which comparison is wanted and what question is being asked, and that knowledge arrives carrying an account of the case: an item handed over with nothing attached is not a request anybody can act on, so there is no version of the assignment that informs without also framing.

## Apparent

A covering note, a file, a conversation in the corridor.
Briefing an expert, which is what briefing an expert looks like.

## Yearbook

Travels with the assignment under every staffing anybody has proposed.
Cannot be reduced without reducing the work to an item and no question.
