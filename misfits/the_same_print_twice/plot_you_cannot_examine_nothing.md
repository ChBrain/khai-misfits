# Plot: You Cannot Examine Nothing

## Taxonomy

[The Same Print Twice](play_the_same_print_twice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A mark, a question, and an examiner about to start.

## Action

[Briefing the bench](process_briefing_the_bench.md): [the investigator](persona_the_investigator.md), holding [Investigator](position_investigator.md), sends the item and says what is being asked.

## Stage

The work can now begin. It could not have begun without this.

## Tension

The briefing is the condition of the examination rather than an addition to it.
An item handed over with nothing attached is not a request anybody can act on, so an investigator who said less would be sending an object rather than a case. And the knowledge of what is wanted arrives carrying an account of why: there is no version of the assignment that names the comparison and frames nothing, because naming the comparison is already framing. Nobody here is leaning on anybody, and the context is not an opinion about the answer.
