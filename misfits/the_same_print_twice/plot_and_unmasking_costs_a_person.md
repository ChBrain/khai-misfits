# Plot: And Unmasking Costs a Person

## Taxonomy

[The Same Print Twice](play_the_same_print_twice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The real remedy, sequential unmasking, understood and agreed by everybody.

## Action

[Ordering what is told](process_ordering_what_is_told.md): [the order](persona_the_order.md) releases the context in sequence rather than withholding it.

## Stage

It works. It needs [the case manager](piece_the_case_manager.md), somebody standing between the case and the bench.

## Tension

The fix is a role rather than a rule, so agreeing with it changes nothing on its own.
Somebody has to hold the file, decide what may be said and when, and answer for those decisions, which is a post, a salary, a delay in every case and a new place for an error. A laboratory persuaded of the remedy completely is exactly where it was until the post is funded, and the cost scales with caseload, so it is dearest where the benches are busiest and the marks are worst. The remedy is not in doubt anywhere in this play. What is in doubt is who pays for it.
