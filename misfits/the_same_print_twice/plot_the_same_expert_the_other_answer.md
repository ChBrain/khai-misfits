# Plot: The Same Expert, the Other Answer

## Taxonomy

[The Same Print Twice](play_the_same_print_twice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The same mark, years later, inside a different account of the case.

## Action

[Reaching the conclusion](process_reaching_the_conclusion.md) runs again on [the second sitting](place_the_second_sitting.md), and [the earlier conclusion](persona_the_earlier_conclusion.md) is in the file.

## Stage

The opposite answer, reached honestly, by the same examiner, confident on both occasions.

## Tension

This is the comparison that removes every explanation needing a second party.
There is no opponent, no rival construal, no identity being served and no motive in play: both readings come from one expert with the same training and the same standards, and the only thing different between the two occasions is the account of the case around the mark. The party contradicting the examiner is the examiner, at another time, and neither of them was lying.
