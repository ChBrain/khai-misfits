# Place: The Books

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The balance sheets where cover is measured against obligation and the financing posture, hedge, speculative, or Ponzi, is written down in figures. Hosts [A Little More](plot_a_little_more.md) and [The Thinning Margin](plot_the_thinning_margin.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two columns, cash flow expected against debt service owed, restated every period as the loan is renewed on slightly easier terms than the one before it.

## Holds

[The Cushion](piece_the_cushion.md) and the borrower's place on [The Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md): whether income still covers interest and principal both, interest alone with the principal rolled, or neither, with a sale or a refinancing the only way out. The Books is where [Leveraging](process_leveraging.md) is legible, rung by rung.

## Offers

A precise reading of exposure, to anyone willing to look: at any moment the Books state exactly how much of this borrower's continued solvency depends on being able to borrow again.

## Withheld

Comparison across time. Each period's Books are read against that period's Cushion, never against the Cushion the borrower carried when the Boom began, so the erosion is visible rung by rung and invisible end to end.
