# Process: Forgetting

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). Run by [The Lender](persona_the_lender.md) on [Lend on the Record](plan_lend_on_the_record.md), at [The Boom](place_the_boom.md), in [The Good Years](plot_the_good_years.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Nothing more than time passing without a default. Each year [The Boom](place_the_boom.md) adds to the record without a loss booked against it, and that record is itself the trigger: no decision to relax is required, only the accumulation of evidence that caution was not needed.

## Direction

Toward laxer terms. The margin of safety The Lender demands on the next loan contracts a little from the margin demanded on the last, tracking the falling frequency of default in the record rather than the frequency that would hold once the record's own effect on behavior is counted.

## Lever

As the recorded rate of default falls, the risk premium a rational lender prices into the next loan falls with it (Minsky): the same evidence that once justified a wide margin now argues, correctly on its own terms, against holding one. HEDGE: the premium falls toward the level the calm record supports, not toward zero by any fixed schedule, and a lender who holds a wider margin than the record demands is not thereby shown wrong, only unrewarded for now.

## Echo

[The Cushion](piece_the_cushion.md), thinner on every new loan than on the last, handed forward into [The Books](place_the_books.md) as the falling margin against which [Leveraging](process_leveraging.md) takes its next step.
