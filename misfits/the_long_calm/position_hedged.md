# Position: Hedged

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The seat of the borrower whose income covers interest and principal both, the safe starting posture, whose margin the long calm makes look like overpriced caution.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real margin: income enough to service the interest and retire the principal, so the seat can pay its way whatever the market does.
An honest cover: it owes nothing it cannot itself repay, and depends on no one rolling its debt and no price rising to sustain it.
No exposure to the calm ending, and no evident use for the margin while the calm holds, so the very safety it carries reads, on the record, as slack.

## Orders

Cover it all.
Hold income enough for both interest and principal, keep the margin that lets you pay your way in any weather, and owe nothing you cannot yourself retire.
Read the record honestly, and where a decade without a crisis says the caution is now overpriced, ease it a sound step at a time, as the evidence supports.

## Loses

The margin, spent soundly, one reasonable step at a time.
Because the record genuinely rewards less caution, the seat eases the cover the last crisis taught it to keep, and each step is defensible alone, so it gives up its safety not to folly but to evidence, and starts up a ladder no single step commits it to.
It keeps its good sense the whole way and loses, gradually, the give the structure will need when the calm ends.

## Drives

Toward the safest posture becoming the first rung,
its margin let go soundly because a real record says the danger has passed,
so the seat that could weather anything is the seat whose reasonable easing seeds the fragility the calm will break on.
