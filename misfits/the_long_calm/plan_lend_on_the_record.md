# Plan: Lend on the Record

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the lender's seat](position_lender.md), inside [The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Lender](position_lender.md)

## Direction

Price the premium to the evidence, and where a decade of low losses says the old premium is too high, ease the terms to what the data supports. Meet the market your rivals set, because holding a caution the record no longer justifies only hands sound business to the lender beside you, and lend into the record of good outcomes the calm keeps producing.

## Orders

([The Lender](persona_the_lender.md) eases terms at [the Books](place_the_books.md) through [Forgetting](process_forgetting.md) and [Validating](process_validating.md), each repaid loan proving the easing right, funding the climb up [the Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md).)

## Implementation

([The Books](place_the_books.md) price the premium off a record of repaid loans; the [forgetting](process_forgetting.md) and [validating](process_validating.md) processes make the low loss rate keep justifying easier terms, so the soundest, most competitive pricing funds the migration, and the loans that will fail are booked on terms that looked good until [the Moment](piece_the_moment.md) reveals what the record could not.)

## Targets

- [ ] the premium is priced to the evidence of low defaults and repaid loans
- [ ] terms are eased where a decade of low losses says the old premium is too high
- [ ] the market the rivals set is met, sound business not left on the table
- [ ] the soundest pricing funds the fragility, the correction arriving only after the terms are set and the moment has come
