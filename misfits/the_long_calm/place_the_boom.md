# Place: The Boom

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The prolonged expansion itself, calmer the longer it runs, where the record of low defaults and rising prices gets made. Hosts [The Good Years](plot_the_good_years.md), [A Little More](plot_a_little_more.md), and [Nothing Broke It](plot_nothing_broke_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A run of years without a default: loans repaid, prices climbing, nobody at the table having lost money lately. The visible surface is unbroken good news, quarter after quarter, each one adding to a record that reads as vindication.

## Holds

[The Calm](piece_the_calm.md), accumulating as evidence. The Boom holds time itself compounding into a case that the caution the last downturn taught was excessive, since nothing since has required it.

## Offers

Confidence, and the terms confidence buys: cheaper credit, easier covenants, a willingness on every side to extend what the record has just finished justifying.

## Withheld

Its own arithmetic. The Boom shows what has not happened, defaults, shortfalls, forced sales, but nothing here shows what is being built on the strength of that absence; the ledger where the cost accrues is kept elsewhere, in [The Books](place_the_books.md).
