# Plot: The Thinning Margin

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Books](place_the_books.md), where each period's margin is read against the period just past and never against the margin the Boom opened with.

## Action

[Validating](process_validating.md): [The Market](place_the_market.md)'s climbing price confirms [The Speculator](persona_the_speculator.md)'s [Roll It Over](plan_roll_it_over.md) and [The Ponzi](persona_the_ponzi.md)'s [Ride the Price](plan_ride_the_price.md) again this period, and [The Lender](persona_the_lender.md), holding [the lender's seat](position_lender.md), reads the renewed repayment as fresh confirmation and extends [Lend on the Record](plan_lend_on_the_record.md) once more, [The Cushion](piece_the_cushion.md) thinner on the books than the reading shows.

## Stage

Every loan that rolls, every sale that clears, every quarter without a default is entered into the record as a point in favor of the risk that was taken to get there, and the record is not wrong: the risk did pay. What it does not enter is the comparison that would matter, this quarter's margin against the Boom's opening margin rather than against last quarter's, so an erosion that is continuous end to end reads as flat, period by period. The Speculator and the Ponzi are rewarded exactly as their positions predicted, which is the strongest argument the record can offer for taking the next position the same way.

## Tension

The longer the confirmation runs, the more leveraged and refinancing-dependent the structure grows beneath it, and the confirmation itself is what keeps that growth from being read as a cost rather than as proof.
