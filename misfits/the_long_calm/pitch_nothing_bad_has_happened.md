# Pitch: Nothing Bad Has Happened

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Steady, reassuring, evidenced.
An economy calm for years: defaults low, loans repaid, prices rising, every risk of the last decade rewarded, so more debt looks safe and gearing up looks like sober sense.
Nothing false in it: the record is real, and reading it to mean the old caution is now overpriced is the rational response, not a delusion.

## Undertow

Under the reassurance, a structure sliding from safe toward fragile.
Each reasonable step of added leverage moves a borrower from covering interest and principal, to covering only interest and rolling the rest, to covering neither and riding the price, and the system grows more dependent on credit that keeps flowing and prices that keep rising.
Everything that would test the leverage is exactly what the long calm has not produced, so the structure is most brittle precisely when it looks most secure.

## Nerve

The comfort of a record that says the danger has not come, and the catch that the record is what builds the danger:
that the leverage runs highest and the margin thinnest at the safest-seeming moment, so a small knock, the ordinary end of a calm, is enough to tip what took years to build,
and nothing in the chain of sound decisions that got there was the wrong one to make.

## Echo

After the run: a collapse that needed no villain and no vast shock, only a long enough calm and the prudence that reads it, the tip McCulley's Minsky moment and the spiral down Fisher's debt-deflation.
And the fixes hard for the reason the trap is: leaning against the boom forgoes real gains in the calm against a danger no one can see,
and the cushion that softens the fall validates the risk-taking and feeds the next long calm, so stability keeps ending, again, the way it does.
