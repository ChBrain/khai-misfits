# Piece: The Moment

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The tipping point where accumulated fragility unwinds: the mechanism Minsky's, the label McCulley's "Minsky moment".

## Load Bearing

The point at which speculative and Ponzi units, unable to refinance, are forced to sell, and a small shock is sufficient to produce it only because the financing structure has already migrated, across the length of the Calm, to where continued refinancing was load bearing (Minsky); the label is McCulley's, coined for the 1998 Russian debt crisis, the mechanism it names is Minsky's, and the two are credited separately, never merged. HEDGE: once the forced selling begins, the downward spiral of falling prices raising the real weight of debt is Fisher's debt-deflation, not a further Minsky addition; this piece is the tip, not the descent. Guard against treating the shock itself as the cause; the shock is small and interchangeable, the fragility that a small shock can tip is the thing that took years to build.

## Apparent

It looks like the moment something goes wrong, a discrete event with a triggering cause worth isolating and blaming, the failed refinancing or the falling price read as the story's origin. That reading inverts the order: the trigger is incidental, almost any would do, what makes it the Moment is the fragility already accumulated underneath it.
