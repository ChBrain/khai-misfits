## Load Bearing

Hedge finance, where cash flows cover interest and principal both; speculative finance, where they cover only the interest and the principal must be rolled over; Ponzi finance, where they cover neither and the borrower depends on a rising [Market](place_the_market.md) to refinance or sell: three postures, and a stretch of stability moves the financing structure up them rung by rung, not because anyone turns reckless but because each step is a reasonable read of a genuine record (Minsky). HEDGE: this is a tendency of the financing structure over a boom, not an iron law that every unit climbs or every calm ends at Ponzi; hedge units persist throughout, and the mix shifts, it does not vanish. Guard against reading the Ladder as a fixed sequence any one borrower must complete, it is the aggregate composition that migrates, not a fate assigned to each.

## Apparent

It looks like healthy diversification, a market efficiently sorting borrowers into the financing style that suits their appetite for risk, no different in kind from any spread of conservative and aggressive positions. That reading misses the direction: the mix is not stable, it is drifting upward as long as the Calm continues, and the drift is the misfit, not the spread itself.
