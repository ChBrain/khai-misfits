# Persona: The Hedged

## Taxonomy

[Hedged](position_hedged.md). The borrower whose income covers both interest and principal, the safe starting posture, that reads a genuine record of calm and reasonably concludes its caution is now overpriced.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Solid, covered, unworried.
Is the borrower that can pay its way whatever comes: income enough for the interest and the principal both, a real margin between what it earns and what it owes.
No timidity in staying safe and no folly in easing off it: the caution was earned by the last crisis, and after a decade without one the evidence genuinely says that caution now costs more than it is worth.

## Action

Covers interest and principal on [Cover It All](plan_cover_it_all.md), at [the Books](place_the_books.md), through [Forgetting](process_forgetting.md) as the last crisis fades and [the Cushion](piece_the_cushion.md) it holds comes to look like slack.
Cannot tell its prudence from overpricing: with the danger a decade absent, the margin it keeps reads on the evidence as caution overpaid, so easing it is not recklessness but the sound response to a real record.
Does what a covered borrower does when the record keeps rewarding less caution: it eases, reasonably, one sound step at a time, and starts up the ladder it did not mean to climb.

## Shadow

Not a coward for holding the margin and not a fool for spending it: the Hedged is the one posture that could weather anything, and its safety is exactly what the long calm makes look wasteful.
Because the record genuinely supports easing off, the Hedged's reasonable steps are the first rung of the migration, so the safest starting point is where the fragility begins, not from any bad call but from sound calls made against a calm that keeps paying them.
It is the model of prudence, and the seed of the structure that prudence, reading a real record, builds toward breaking.

## Tell

Says the old caution is overpriced now, and is right, on the evidence the calm has given it.
Cannot see that the margin it is reasonably letting go is the give the structure will need when the calm ends, so it spends its safety soundly, a step at a time, toward a fragility no single step contains.
