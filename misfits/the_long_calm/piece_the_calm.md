# Piece: The Calm

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The stretch of stability itself: low defaults, loans repaid, prices rising, the record every unit in the Boom reads, and reads rationally.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held at [The Boom](place_the_boom.md), where it accumulates year on year; read alike by [The Hedged](persona_the_hedged.md), [The Speculator](persona_the_speculator.md), and [The Ponzi](persona_the_ponzi.md), and lent against by [The Lender](persona_the_lender.md) through [Lend on the Record](plan_lend_on_the_record.md). It thins [The Cushion](piece_the_cushion.md) through [Forgetting](process_forgetting.md), the record itself doing the eroding.

## Load Bearing

The Calm is the incubator of the collapse it precedes, not evidence against one: a run of low defaults and rising prices is a genuine record, and reading it rationally, borrowers take on more debt and lenders extend more credit on easier terms, because the risk that justified caution simply has not shown up (Minsky). HEDGE: the record is real and the inference from it is not wrong on its own terms, only incomplete, it prices the years just past and nothing further out. Guard against reading a long calm as proof that the caution it is eroding was excessive.

## Apparent

It looks like safety achieved, a stretch earned by sound practice and confirmed by results, so that the longer it runs the more settled the case for easing looks. That reading mistakes the record for the risk: the absence of a default is not the absence of the conditions that produce one, only their postponement.
