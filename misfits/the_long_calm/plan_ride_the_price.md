# Plan: Ride the Price

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the ponzi's seat](position_ponzi.md), inside [The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Ponzi](position_ponzi.md)

## Direction

Carry debt your income cannot service at all, and meet it by refinancing against, or selling into, an asset whose price keeps climbing. Stay fully committed to the trend, because a market up for years rewards riding it, and treat a rising price as the plan itself and not merely the hope behind it.

## Orders

([The Ponzi](persona_the_ponzi.md) rides the price at [the Market](place_the_market.md) through [Validating](process_validating.md), each rise rewarding the last, wholly carrying [the Refinancing](piece_the_refinancing.md) at the top of [the Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md).)

## Implementation

([The Market](place_the_market.md) sustains the seat only while its price rises; the [validating](process_validating.md) process lets each rise prove the last one right and draw more onto the rung, so the position that covers neither interest nor principal is sound while the climb holds and destroyed the moment it merely stalls, its own forced selling part of the turn.)

## Targets

- [ ] debt is carried that income cannot service at all, met only by the rising price
- [ ] the position is refinanced against, or sold into, an asset whose price keeps climbing
- [ ] the trend is ridden fully, a rising market having rewarded the bet every time in the calm
- [ ] a mere pause in the rise is fatal, the seat forced to sell into the first flat tick and turning the market as it does
