# Plot: A Little More

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Books](place_the_books.md), where cover is measured against obligation and each new loan is set against the loan just before it.

## Action

[Leveraging](process_leveraging.md): [The Lender](persona_the_lender.md), holding [the lender's seat](position_lender.md), runs [Lend on the Record](plan_lend_on_the_record.md), easing terms because defaults have been low for years now, and [The Speculator](persona_the_speculator.md), holding [the speculator's seat](position_speculator.md), takes up [Roll It Over](plan_roll_it_over.md) on the strength of it, carrying [The Cushion](piece_the_cushion.md) a notch thinner than the loan it replaces.

## Stage

The Lender's covenant this year is looser than last year's, not from any change of judgment but from the same judgment applied to a longer record: years without a default earns the next borrower a wider loan against the same income. The Speculator takes it, and takes on a debt whose cash flow covers the interest alone, the principal to be rolled forward when it comes due rather than paid down now. Neither decision is reckless measured against what each party can see; the Lender is pricing the risk the record shows, and the Speculator is borrowing on terms the market is genuinely offering. What moves is the structure beneath both of them: a loan that would have wanted hedge cover two years ago clears as speculative cover now.

## Tension

Nothing here has failed and nothing here is unsound; that is exactly what the scene leaves standing. The Cushion is thinner than it was, on both sides of the desk, on the strength of nothing but the absence of a loss, and the same easing has room to happen again next year on the same grounds.
