# Place: The Market

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The asset market whose rising prices let a bet that could not otherwise pay, pay, and whose reversal is where the accumulated fragility is finally tested. Hosts [Roll It Forward](plot_roll_it_forward.md) and [The Break](plot_the_break.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A price, ticking upward, and a stream of refinancings and sales going through at that price without difficulty, each one a small confirmation that the price will still be there when the next one is due.

## Holds

The single number that Ponzi finance is built on: not income, but the belief that this price, or a higher one, will still be quotable when the debt next comes due. The Market is where [Validating](process_validating.md) runs.

## Offers

Liquidity, for as long as it lasts: a place to sell or refinance against, standing ready to absorb whatever the Boom's leverage has come to depend on it absorbing.

## Withheld

Any guarantee that the price holds. The Market offers no floor beneath itself, only the going rate, and a going rate can go the other way; that reversal is what [Tipping](process_tipping.md) needs and nothing here forbids it.
