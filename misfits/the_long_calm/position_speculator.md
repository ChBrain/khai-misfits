# Position: Speculator

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The seat of the borrower whose income covers the interest but not the principal, so the principal must be rolled over, sound while credit flows, the middle rung of the ladder.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Cover for the interest and not the principal: income enough to service the debt but not to retire it, so the principal stands until it can be refinanced.
A working posture on the record: rolling the principal has never failed in the whole calm, so the seat carries more debt than it can repay and does so as ordinary business.
No solvency of its own past the interest, only a standing reliance on the door of credit being open each time the debt comes due.

## Orders

Roll it over.
Service the interest in full from income, and refinance the principal each time it matures, gearing to where the debt exceeds what you could retire but not what you can roll.
Count on credit staying available, because in the whole of the calm it always has, and treat that availability as the ordinary condition of doing business.

## Loses

The ability to stand alone the moment the credit it counts on is not there.
Because its position rests on refinancing rather than on its own income, the seat is sound only while the door stays open, so a squeeze it did nothing to cause turns a working balance sheet into a forced seller overnight, through no change in itself.
It loses nothing while the calm holds and everything the instant rolling fails, its solvency never its own to keep.

## Drives

Toward the posture where the structure first becomes conditional,
sound on the record and dependent on a door the calm has made look permanently open,
so the seat that has always rolled its debt is the first the ending of the calm can reach.
