# Plot: Nothing Broke It

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Boom](place_the_boom.md), still unbroken, old enough now that almost no one working through it has priced a downturn from memory rather than from a model.

## Action

[Validating](process_validating.md) still running, unbroken: [The Hedged](persona_the_hedged.md), holding [the hedged's seat](position_hedged.md), on [Cover It All](plan_cover_it_all.md); [The Speculator](persona_the_speculator.md), holding [the speculator's seat](position_speculator.md), on [Roll It Over](plan_roll_it_over.md); and [The Ponzi](persona_the_ponzi.md), holding [the ponzi's seat](position_ponzi.md), on [Ride the Price](plan_ride_the_price.md), each meeting this quarter exactly what its posture requires, carrying [The Calm](piece_the_calm.md) one more unbroken quarter forward.

## Stage

No one in this scene is reckless and no one is lying to themselves. The Hedged still covers interest and principal both; the Speculator still rolls a principal that a market still willing to lend makes rollable; the Ponzi still sells or refinances against a price that is still, this quarter, there to be sold or refinanced against. Every decision in the chain, from the first lender who eased a covenant on a clean record to the Ponzi position taken out last month, was the reasonable reading of the evidence available when it was made, and the evidence has not yet stopped agreeing with any of them. This is Minsky's finding exactly: stability is not the reward the caution earned, it is the condition the caution's absence is quietly compounding, and nothing in a chain this sound is positioned to notice, let alone slow, what it is building.

## Tension

The safest this structure has ever looked is the same quarter in which it stands furthest from where safety was last measured. Nothing here is going to correct itself, because nothing here has yet been shown to need correcting.
