# Plot: The Good Years

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Boom](place_the_boom.md), years running without a default, loans repaid on schedule and prices climbing quarter after quarter. Nothing here has broken; the record is reading, so far, exactly as it looks.

## Action

[Forgetting](process_forgetting.md): [The Hedged](persona_the_hedged.md), holding [the hedged's seat](position_hedged.md), runs [Cover It All](plan_cover_it_all.md) at [The Boom](place_the_boom.md), income covering interest and principal both, the posture the whole structure still starts from, while [The Calm](piece_the_calm.md) accumulates behind it, one uneventful year at a time.

## Stage

The Hedged pays what it owes on schedule, the same schedule it has kept for years, and the payment looks no different this quarter than the last. Around it the record lengthens: no default booked anywhere nearby, no forced sale, prices a shade higher than they were. The caution the last downturn taught was set against a crisis memory now has to work to summon, and a caution nothing since has required reads, on the evidence in hand, as caution no longer owed. Nothing here is wrong: The Hedged still covers what it owes in full, and the record is simply, genuinely, safe.

## Tension

The safety is real, and so is what it is teaching. A stretch this clean stops being read as luck and starts being read as a rule, and a rule this reliable is an invitation, one nobody in this scene has yet accepted, to lean on it harder than the last one warranted.
