# Piece: The Backstop

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The remedy and its price: the support that cushions the collapse and, in cushioning it, feeds the confidence that begins the next Boom.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Applied after [The Moment](piece_the_moment.md), where a lender of last resort and countercyclical support meet the forced selling [Tipping](process_tipping.md) produced at [The Market](place_the_market.md); the leaning against the Boom that would have slowed [Leveraging](process_leveraging.md) earlier goes unapplied, because it is costly precisely during [The Calm](piece_the_calm.md), when nothing yet shows a reason to pay for it.

## Load Bearing

Minsky's own remedies, a lender of last resort and countercyclical fiscal support, cushion the collapse once it comes, and leaning against the boom would slow the build toward it, but neither is free: leaning against the boom means restraining credit while every visible sign says otherwise, a cost paid against a danger no one can yet see, and the cushion that limits the damage validates, after the fact, the risk-taking that produced it, underwriting the leverage that feeds the next cycle's Calm (Minsky). HEDGE: this is Minsky's own prescription, not an outside critique layered onto the hypothesis; the hypothesis carries its own remedy and its own reason the remedy is hard to apply in time. Guard against reading the Backstop as a fix that ends the pattern; it ends the collapse and reloads the incubator.

## Apparent

It looks like the story's resolution, the intervention that stops the fall and restores order, so that the sequence reads as a problem solved. That reading stops one cycle short: the same support that stopped the fall tells the next Boom's lenders and borrowers that the downside is covered, which is itself a fact the next Calm will be read against.

## Yearbook

Applied after [The Moment](piece_the_moment.md) and [Tipping](process_tipping.md) at [The Market](place_the_market.md); the unapplied alternative to slowing [Leveraging](process_leveraging.md) during [The Calm](piece_the_calm.md). It is the no-hero close: what cushions the fall also validates the leverage that will spend down [The Cushion](piece_the_cushion.md) again next Boom.
