# Process: Tipping

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). Borne at [The Market](place_the_market.md), precipitated by [The Ponzi](persona_the_ponzi.md) failing to roll, carrying [The Moment](piece_the_moment.md), in [The Break](plot_the_break.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A shock too small to trouble a hedge unit: a rate rise, a shortfall, a single refinancing that does not clear, arriving on a structure that [Leveraging](process_leveraging.md) has left with no cushion to absorb it.

## Direction

From refinancing-dependence toward forced sale: the speculative and Ponzi units that relied on rolling debt or a rising price to meet it can no longer do either, and sell into a market that Validating had assumed would keep rising, pushing the price the next unit needed away from them too.

## Lever

Stability is destabilizing (Minsky): because the calm has already thinned every cushion and migrated the structure up [The Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md), the shock required to tip it shrinks as the calm lengthens, so it is the accumulated fragility, not the size of the shock, that does the work. McCulley later named this tipping point the "Minsky moment" (1998, for the Russian debt crisis); the label is his, the mechanism is Minsky's own. HEDGE: the migration up the ladder is a tendency, not every long calm ends this way, and the downward spiral of forced sales driving prices lower still, once the tip has come, is Fisher's debt-deflation, the shape of the descent, not Minsky's own contribution.

## Echo

Collapse, and after it a cushion rebuilt chastened, standing ready to be spent again the next time a record of calm runs long enough to be believed.
