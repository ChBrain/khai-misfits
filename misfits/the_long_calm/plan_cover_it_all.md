# Plan: Cover It All

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the hedged's seat](position_hedged.md), inside [The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Hedged](position_hedged.md)

## Direction

Hold income enough to service both interest and principal, keep the margin that lets you pay your way in any weather, and owe nothing you cannot yourself retire. Read the record honestly, and where a decade without a crisis says the caution is now overpriced, ease it a sound step at a time, as the evidence supports, not as a nerve fails.

## Orders

([The Hedged](persona_the_hedged.md) covers interest and principal at [the Books](place_the_books.md) through [Forgetting](process_forgetting.md), holding [the Cushion](piece_the_cushion.md) that the lengthening calm makes look like slack.)

## Implementation

([The Books](place_the_books.md) show a margin between what is earned and what is owed; the [forgetting](process_forgetting.md) process lets the record of calm reprice that margin as excess caution, so each sound easing spends a little of [the Cushion](piece_the_cushion.md) and moves the seat toward the first rung of [the Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md), no single step a mistake.)

## Targets

- [ ] income covers both interest and principal, the seat able to pay its way in any weather
- [ ] a real margin is held, owing nothing that cannot be self-retired
- [ ] the record is read honestly, easing caution only where a decade without crisis genuinely supports it
- [ ] each sound step spends a little of the cushion, the safest posture starting up the ladder it did not mean to climb
