# Piece: The Cushion

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The margin of safety between what a borrower's cash flow earns and what its debt service demands.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Lives on [The Books](place_the_books.md), where cover is measured against obligation each period; thinned by [Forgetting](process_forgetting.md), which erodes the caution that once padded it, and by [Leveraging](process_leveraging.md), which spends it rung by rung as the borrower moves up [The Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md). Borne first and longest by [The Hedged](persona_the_hedged.md), whose position is defined by carrying more of it than the posture strictly requires, under [Cover It All](plan_cover_it_all.md), until it gives way to something thinner.

## Load Bearing

The Cushion is the quantity the financing structure is actually spending across the Boom: the gap between income and obligation that lets a borrower absorb a bad quarter without missing a payment, and every reasonable step of added leverage, taken on the strength of the Calm, narrows that gap a little more (Minsky). HEDGE: no single step spends much of it, and each looks small measured against the step before rather than against the Cushion the Boom began with. Guard against measuring the Cushion period to period instead of end to end, that comparison is exactly what hides how far it has thinned.

## Apparent

It looks like idle capacity, headroom sitting unused while the record shows it was never needed, so spending it down for better terms looks like ordinary efficiency rather than a cost. That reading only holds while the Cushion is thick enough that its absence has not yet been tested.

## Yearbook

Lives on [The Books](place_the_books.md), thinned by [Forgetting](process_forgetting.md) and [Leveraging](process_leveraging.md), borne by [The Hedged](persona_the_hedged.md) under [Cover It All](plan_cover_it_all.md). Its thinning is what [The Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md) measures rung by rung, and what running out means for [The Refinancing](piece_the_refinancing.md) once income alone no longer covers what is owed.
