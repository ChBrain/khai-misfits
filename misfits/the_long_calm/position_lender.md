# Position: Lender

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The seat of the one who extends the credit, pricing risk off the record of low defaults and repaid loans, easing terms as the calm continues and funding each rung the borrowers climb.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A record to price from: years of low defaults and repaid loans, the evidence on which it sets the premium it charges for danger.
A competitive floor: the terms its rivals offer, which it must at least meet or lose sound business to them.
No sight of the aggregate it is building, only loan by loan the record of good outcomes, and nothing in that record that shows the structure it funds growing brittle.

## Orders

Lend on the record.
Price the premium to the evidence, and where a decade of low losses says the old premium is too high, ease the terms to what the data supports.
Meet the market your rivals set, because holding a caution the record no longer justifies only hands sound business to the lender beside you.

## Loses

Nothing on the record it prices from, and its footing when the losses finally arrive.
Because the record it reads is the calm, and the calm is what breeds the fragility, the seat's soundest and most competitive pricing is what funds the migration, so the loans that will fail are booked on terms that looked good when they were set, the correction arriving only after the damage is done.
It loses nothing while it lends and discovers the cost only once the moment has come and the terms cannot be recalled.

## Drives

Toward the disciplined pricer of risk that finances the fragility its discipline cannot see,
easing terms soundly because the calm has produced no data on the danger it is building,
so the seat that lends most rationally is the seat whose rational lending funds the leverage the calm will break on.
