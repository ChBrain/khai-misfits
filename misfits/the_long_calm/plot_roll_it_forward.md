# Plot: Roll It Forward

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Market](place_the_market.md), a price ticking upward and a stream of refinancings and sales going through against it without difficulty.

## Action

[Leveraging](process_leveraging.md), a rung further: [The Speculator](persona_the_speculator.md), holding [the speculator's seat](position_speculator.md), holds [Roll It Over](plan_roll_it_over.md), covering interest and rolling the principal forward again, and [The Ponzi](persona_the_ponzi.md), holding [the ponzi's seat](position_ponzi.md), takes up [Ride the Price](plan_ride_the_price.md), covering neither and counting on the next sale or the next refinancing to close the gap, both carrying [The Refinancing](piece_the_refinancing.md) up another step of [The Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md).

## Stage

The Speculator's roll goes through the way it went through last time: the lender renews the principal, the interest is paid, the file closes until the next due date. The Ponzi's position clears too, not because its income covers what it owes but because the asset behind it is worth more than it was when the loan was written, enough to refinance against or sell outright if the refinancing does not come through. Both trades are unremarkable this quarter, and both are quietly a bet that a Market which has only gone one direction will keep going that direction long enough to be borrowed against again.

## Tension

The system's solvency, rung by rung, now runs through continued access to credit rather than through income earned and banked. Nothing has yet asked it to prove that access holds, and the Ladder has more rungs above this one.
