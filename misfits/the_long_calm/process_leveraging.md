## Initiated by

## Direction

From hedge finance toward speculative, and from speculative on toward Ponzi: cash flow that once covered interest and principal both comes to cover interest alone, with the principal rolled over, then comes to cover neither, with the position carried on the expectation of refinancing or a rising price.

## Lever

A calm that validates past leverage licenses the next increment of it, so the financing structure migrates up the postures as the boom lengthens (Minsky). HEDGE: this is the tendency of the structure summed across many borrowers, not a compulsion on any single one; a given unit can hold at hedge indefinitely, and the migration the hypothesis describes is a property of the aggregate, not a determinate path each balance sheet must walk.

## Echo

A financing structure now standing on [The Refinancing](piece_the_refinancing.md): obligations that can only be met by borrowing again, carried forward into [The Market](place_the_market.md) where that borrowing must, in fact, be found.
