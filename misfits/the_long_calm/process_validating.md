# Process: Validating

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). Run by [The Ponzi](persona_the_ponzi.md) on [Ride the Price](plan_ride_the_price.md), at [The Market](place_the_market.md), in [The Thinning Margin](plot_the_thinning_margin.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A price that keeps rising, meeting a position whose cash flow covers neither interest nor principal and so needs exactly that rise to refinance or sell out of the debt.

## Direction

Toward more of the same: each refinancing or sale that clears at a higher price is read as confirmation of the bet, drawing further speculative and Ponzi entrants into the market and lifting the price the next entrant will need.

## Lever

Rising asset prices validate the risk already taken, and that validation is itself the evidence that recommends taking more (Minsky): the loop is not a miscalculation repeated, it is the same rational reading of a genuine record applied one more time. HEDGE: the loop runs only while prices in fact rise; it supplies no floor and no promise that they continue to, and its own momentum is what leaves [The Ponzi](persona_the_ponzi.md) with nothing to fall back on once they stop.

## Echo

A market whose going price now carries the weight of debt it was never asked to carry, the margin between what is owed and what income covers thinned to nothing and standing entirely on [The Market](place_the_market.md) holding its level, the precondition [Tipping](process_tipping.md) needs only a small shock to unsettle.
