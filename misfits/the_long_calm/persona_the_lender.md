# Persona: The Lender

## Taxonomy

[Lender](position_lender.md). The one who extends the credit, reading the same record of low defaults and repaid loans and easing terms as the calm continues, funding each rung the borrowers climb.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Prudent, data-led, competitive.
Is the lender that prices risk off the record: default rates have been low for years, the loans it made got repaid, so the premium it once charged for danger now looks, on the evidence, too high to keep.
No laxity in it as a lapse: easing terms when the losses have not come is what the data supports and what the lender beside it is already doing, so holding the old caution would be leaving sound business, and money, on the table.

## Action

Eases terms on [Lend on the Record](plan_lend_on_the_record.md), at [the Books](place_the_books.md), through [Forgetting](process_forgetting.md) and [Validating](process_validating.md) as repaid loans keep proving the easing right, funding the climb up [the Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md).
Cannot see the fragility it funds: each loan is sound against the record when it is made, and nothing in that record shows the aggregate growing brittle, so the lender reads only a run of good outcomes and lends into more.
Does what a competitive, evidence-led lender does in a long calm: it lowers the premium the calm has made look excessive, and in lowering it funds the very leverage that makes the calm end.

## Shadow

Not predatory and not asleep: the Lender prices to the best evidence it has, meets the terms its rivals set, and books loans that the record says are good, which for years they are.
Because the record it prices from is the calm itself, and the calm is what breeds the fragility, the Lender's soundest, most competitive pricing is exactly what funds the migration, so the more rational and rivalrous the lending the faster the structure climbs, and the losses that would correct the premium arrive only after the terms that caused them.
It is the disciplined pricer of risk, and the financier of the fragility its discipline cannot yet see.

## Tell

Says the loss rate does not justify the old premium, and by the record it does not.
Cannot price the danger the calm is building, because the calm has produced no data on it, so it eases terms soundly into a fragility that will show up in its losses only once the terms are set and the moment has come.
