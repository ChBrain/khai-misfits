# Persona: The Ponzi

## Taxonomy

[Ponzi](position_ponzi.md). The borrower whose income covers neither interest nor principal, so only the rising price of the asset sustains it, the top rung of the ladder, sound while prices climb.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Bold, all-in, riding the trend.
Is the borrower that cannot service its debt from what it earns at all, and does not mean to: the plan is to refinance against, or sell into, an asset whose price keeps rising.
No fraud in it where the trend is real: in a market that has gone up for years, staking a position on more of the same is a bet the record has paid every time it was made.

## Action

Rides the price on [Ride the Price](plan_ride_the_price.md), at [the Market](place_the_market.md), through [Validating](process_validating.md) as each rise rewards the last, carrying [the Refinancing](piece_the_refinancing.md) it wholly depends on at the top of [the Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md).
Cannot stand still: with income covering neither interest nor principal, the position works only while the price rises fast enough to refinance or sell against, so a mere pause is not neutral but fatal to it.
Does what a trend-rider does while the trend holds: it rides, and the rising price keeps proving it right, until the rise is the one thing the calm stops delivering.

## Shadow

Not a con and not a dupe: the Ponzi is named for the shape of the financing, not for any deceit, and in a genuinely rising market its bet is the one the whole record has been rewarding.
Because it needs not just credit but a rising price, the Ponzi is the most fragile posture and the one the calm most lavishly pays, so a boom draws more onto this rung exactly as it lengthens, and the reversal that ends it need not be a crash, only a stop, a price that ceases to rise.
It is the fullest expression of the calm's logic, and the first thing the calm's ending destroys.

## Tell

Says the price will carry it, and for as long as the price rises, the price does.
Cannot survive a flat market, let alone a falling one, so the moment the rise it is staked on merely stalls, a position sound at the peak is forced to sell into the turn, and its selling is what turns the peak.
