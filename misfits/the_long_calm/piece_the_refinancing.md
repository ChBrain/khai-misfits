# Piece: The Refinancing

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The standing need to keep borrowing in order to service what is already owed, carried by the upper rungs of [The Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Carried by [The Speculator](persona_the_speculator.md) under [Roll It Over](plan_roll_it_over.md), who must find the principal again each period, and further by [The Ponzi](persona_the_ponzi.md) under [Ride the Price](plan_ride_the_price.md), who must find the interest as well, both leaning on [The Market](place_the_market.md) to stay open. It is the dependence [Tipping](process_tipping.md) strikes.

## Load Bearing

Speculative and Ponzi units do not fail because they cannot pay from income, they were never expected to; they fail when they cannot borrow again, so the debt they carry is only ever as sound as the market's continued willingness to extend it, and that willingness is precisely what a small shock can withdraw (Minsky). HEDGE: refinancing dependence is not itself a defect, it is the ordinary condition of these two postures throughout the Calm, and it costs nothing as long as credit stays open. Guard against locating the danger in the size of the debt; the danger is in the need to roll it, which a debt of any size can carry as long as credit never tightens.

## Apparent

It looks like routine treasury management, no different from any firm rolling commercial paper or refinancing on schedule, an unremarkable operational fact rather than a vulnerability. That reading holds exactly as long as the Market keeps absorbing the roll; it says nothing about what happens the one time it does not.

## Yearbook

It is the load the upper rungs of [The Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md) bear once [The Cushion](piece_the_cushion.md) no longer covers what is owed, and what fails, at [The Moment](piece_the_moment.md), all at once.
