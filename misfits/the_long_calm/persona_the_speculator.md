# Persona: The Speculator

## Taxonomy

[Speculator](position_speculator.md). The borrower whose income covers the interest but not the principal, so the principal must be rolled over, sound while credit keeps flowing, the middle rung of the ladder.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Confident, geared, unhurried.
Is the borrower that carries more debt than its income can retire, servicing the interest comfortably and rolling the principal forward each time it comes due.
No recklessness in it on the record: refinancing has never once failed in the whole of the calm, so counting on it is not a gamble but the ordinary way business has been done for years.

## Action

Rolls the principal on [Roll It Over](plan_roll_it_over.md), at [the Market](place_the_market.md), through [Leveraging](process_leveraging.md), carrying [the Refinancing](piece_the_refinancing.md) it depends on and standing a rung up [the Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md) from the Hedged.
Cannot pay down what it owes, only service and roll it: its whole position rests on credit staying available when the debt matures, a condition the long calm has made look permanent.
Does what a geared borrower does when rolling has always worked: it rolls, and gears a little further, sound as long as the door it refinances through stays open.

## Shadow

Not a swindler and not a mark: the Speculator services its debt in full and refinances the rest exactly as the market has always let it, and by the record that is a working, ordinary posture.
Because its solvency depends on continued access to credit rather than on its own income, the Speculator is where the structure first becomes conditional, sound only while the calm holds, so the more of the system that stands on this rung the more a single squeeze on credit can reach, and none of that shows while the rolling goes on.
It is the ordinary geared borrower, and the first posture a shock can find.

## Tell

Says it will roll the principal as it always has, and it always has.
Cannot service what it cannot refinance, so the day the credit it counts on is not there, a position that was sound the evening before must sell the morning after, through no change in itself.
