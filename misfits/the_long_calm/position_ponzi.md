# Position: Ponzi

## Taxonomy

[The Long Calm](play_the_long_calm.md). The seat of the borrower whose income covers neither interest nor principal, sustained only by the rising price of the asset, the top rung of the ladder, sound while prices climb.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A bet on the trend: income that covers neither interest nor principal, and a plan to refinance against or sell into an asset whose price keeps rising.
A record that has paid it: in a market up for years, staking on more of the same is the bet the calm has rewarded every time it was placed.
No footing without a rising price, so the seat holds not a margin but a direction, sound only while the market keeps moving the way it has.

## Orders

Ride the price.
Carry debt your income cannot service at all, and meet it by refinancing against, or selling into, an asset whose price keeps climbing.
Stay fully committed to the trend, because a market that has risen for years rewards riding it, and treat a rising price as the plan and not merely the hope.

## Loses

Everything the moment the price merely stops rising.
Because the seat needs not just open credit but a climbing market, a pause is as fatal as a fall: with no income to fall back on, a position sound at the peak must sell into the first flat tick, and its selling is part of what turns the market down.
It loses not to a crash it could see coming but to a stall, the gentlest possible end of the rise it was staked on.

## Drives

Toward the most fragile posture and the one the calm most richly pays,
needing a rising price and drawn onto the rung in greatest number just as the boom runs longest,
so the seat that is the fullest expression of the calm's logic is the first thing the calm's ending destroys.
