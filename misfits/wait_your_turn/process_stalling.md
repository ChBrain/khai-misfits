# Process: Stalling

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). The front of the queue waiting on a thing that is genuinely being fetched.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A signature, a lookup, a part from the back, a form somebody else has to bring.
At [the line](place_the_line.md), at the counter, in full view.

## Direction

Toward the completion of legitimate work at the speed legitimate work completes.
Nobody chose the delay and nobody can shorten it: removing it would mean removing the signature or the lookup or the part, each of which exists for a reason somebody could defend in a review.
So there is no one to hurry here, and no defect available to remove.

## Lever

Time, spent on something real.
Because the thing being fetched is not a resource anybody else in the queue wants, no amount of contention management touches this step.

## Echo

One person served correctly,
and a room that did not move while it happened.
