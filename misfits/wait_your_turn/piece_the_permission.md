# Piece: The Permission

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). The only missing part: leave for a ready customer to walk to a free counter, which does not exist and is not scarce.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Absent, between [the line](place_the_line.md) and [the doors](place_the_doors.md).
Four steps wide.

## Load Bearing

Without it the harm has no name that is not "congestion", and it is not congestion.
It is load bearing because naming it makes the diagnosis unavoidable: what is missing is **not capacity, not staff, not time and not money**, and no amount of any of those produces it.

## Apparent

Not a thing at all. Nobody notices an absent permission.
It reads as the natural state of a queue rather than as a withheld resource, which is exactly why the room looks well run.
