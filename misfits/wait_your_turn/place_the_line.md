# Place: The Line

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). The single sequence everybody joins at the back of and is served from the front of.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

One queue, orderly, kept without supervision.
A front that is stopped, and everybody else standing behind it in the order they arrived.

## Holds

The play's quietest fact: the discipline being kept here is the good one.
It needs no referee, cannot be worked by the confident, protects the timid, and costs nothing to run, which is why it is everywhere. Nothing in the production improves by relaxing it, and the production never asks for that.

## Offers

Fairness between any two people in it, exactly and reliably.

## Withheld

Any route past the front.
There is no provision for stepping aside, because a provision for stepping aside is the thing that makes a queue a queue where the loudest go first.
