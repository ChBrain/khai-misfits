# Plot: The Door Was Open

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Open Door](persona_the_open_door.md), standing free at [the doors](place_the_doors.md).
[The One Behind](persona_the_one_behind.md), ready this instant, four steps away.

## Action

[Blocking](process_blocking.md): [The One Behind](persona_the_one_behind.md), in [the held seat](position_held.md), is prevented from reaching [the open door](place_the_doors.md) by nothing except [the order they came in](piece_the_order_they_came_in.md).

## Stage

[The Open Door](persona_the_open_door.md), on [Stand Open](plan_stand_open.md), stays free, serves nobody, and is not being used by any other customer at any point in the scene.

## Tension

This is the whole play, and it is a defect of ordering rather than of capacity.
What stands between the one behind and the counter is not a busy server, not a shortage and not a competing customer: it is sequence. **The thing they are waiting for is not in use by anybody.** [The Permission](piece_the_permission.md) is the only missing part, and it is missing by rule.
And the test is available in the room. Empty the building of every other customer, leave one stalled person at the front and one ready person behind and every counter in the place standing free, and the ready person **still waits**. Nothing has been relieved by removing all the load, because load was never what was holding them. That is the defect in its pure state, and it is why this cannot be answered by building more counters: the counters are already empty.
