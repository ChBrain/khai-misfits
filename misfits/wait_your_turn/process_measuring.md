# Process: Measuring

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). Counter occupancy being computed into the number the room is judged by.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Every counter, sampled.
At [the meter](place_the_meter.md), summarised.

## Direction

Toward an honest account of how much of the capacity is being used, which is the natural thing to ask about a bank of servers.
Nobody picked a misleading statistic: the counters really are unoccupied, so a low figure is accurate, and an operator reading spare capacity from it has reasoned correctly to a true conclusion.
So an honest instrument sits above a stopped queue, and it is neither lying nor evidence.

## Lever

Occupancy, which is what makes a service's capacity readable at all.
Because the harm's whole nature is that the servers are free, no increase in the accuracy or frequency of this measurement can register it.

## Echo

Eleven per cent, correct,
above a line that has not moved in four minutes.
