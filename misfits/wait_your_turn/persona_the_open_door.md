# Persona: The Open Door

## Taxonomy

[Available](position_available.md). The counter across the room, free the whole time, serving nobody, and unreachable by rule rather than by distance.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Open. Staffed, ready, and not in use by any customer at any point in the scene.
The plainest fact in the room and the one nobody can act on.

## Action

Stands open.
Serves nobody.
Is not competed for, not contended, not scarce, and not reached.

## Shadow

Nothing, and this seat is the hinge of the play: the thing the queue is waiting for **is not in use by anybody**.
Because it is idle rather than busy, every remedy aimed at scarcity misses it: more counters, faster counters and shorter transactions all add capacity to a room whose capacity was never spent.
It ends as it began, open, having served nobody, in a building where people waited.

## Tell

Says nothing.
It is a counter with nobody at it, which is the whole argument.
