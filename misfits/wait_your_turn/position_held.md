# Position: Held

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). The seat behind the front: ready to be served, and stopped by sequence alone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Everything needed to be served, now.
A place in the order, which is a protection as much as a constraint.
A clear view of a counter it may not walk to.

## Orders

Keep the order.
Do not overtake.
Wait until the seat ahead is vacated.

## Loses

The whole of the time it waits, none of which was spent on contention.
Because what holds it is neither scarcity nor competition but sequence, nothing added to the system relieves it: more counters, faster counters and shorter transactions all leave this seat exactly where it was.
It ends served, having been ready throughout.

## Drives

Toward being served,
by keeping a rule that is genuinely fair to it,
so the seat that behaves correctly is the seat that pays.
