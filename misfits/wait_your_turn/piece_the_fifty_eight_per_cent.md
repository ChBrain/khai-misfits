# Piece: The Fifty-Eight Per Cent

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). The measure of what one ordered line costs: the fraction of the capacity that a single first-come queue per entrance can use at saturation.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the doors](place_the_doors.md), as the ceiling nobody reaches.
Recovered by [matching](process_matching.md), which is what makes the settlement worth its price.

## Load Bearing

Without it the loss is an anecdote about a bad afternoon.
It is load bearing because the figure is **derived rather than surveyed**: two minus the square root of two, about fifty-eight and a half per cent, so more than two fifths of the capacity is lost to the ordering alone, with the destinations those customers want standing free.

## Apparent

A performance number, of the kind that improves with better equipment.
It reads as an engineering limit to be pushed, and it is not one: faster counters raise the rate and leave the fraction exactly where it is.

## Yearbook

Produced by [blocking](process_blocking.md) under saturation, invisible to [measuring](process_measuring.md).
It is the arithmetic of the same structure the room shows at four minutes and one stalled customer.
