# Process: Ordering

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). Arrivals being placed in a single sequence and served from the front of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Each arrival, joining at the back.
At [the line](place_the_line.md), one sequence for everybody.

## Direction

Toward a fair and cheap discipline, which is what first come, first served is.
It needs no supervision, cannot be worked by the confident, treats the timid exactly as it treats the pushy, and is the rule a queue falls back to when nobody is enforcing anything.
So the ordering is not a compromise or an oversight: it is the thing a designer would choose, and would choose again.

## Lever

Sequence, which is what makes a shared resource usable without a referee.
Because the fairness and the blocking are the same property seen twice, no improvement to how the ordering is applied separates them.

## Echo

A queue everybody accepts,
in which being second to a stalled first is total.
