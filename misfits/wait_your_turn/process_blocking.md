# Process: Blocking

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). Everything behind the stalled front being held, by sequence and by nothing else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The order they came in](piece_the_order_they_came_in.md), applied without exception.
At [the line](place_the_line.md), while [the doors](place_the_doors.md) stand open.

## Direction

Toward the strict preservation of arrival order, which is the process working exactly as designed.
What it produces is that a customer who is ready, whose destination is free, and who is in competition with nobody, does not move.
**The resource they are waiting for is not in use by anybody**, which is what separates this from every form of contention.

## Lever

The prohibition on overtaking, which is the same prohibition that makes the queue fair.
Because load was never what was holding them, emptying the building relieves none of it: the harm survives at any level of demand and is at its purest at the lowest.

## Echo

A stopped line and open counters,
with nothing anywhere in the room being used.
