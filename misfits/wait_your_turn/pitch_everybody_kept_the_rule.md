# Pitch: Everybody Kept the Rule

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Plain, procedural, unhurried.
The register of a rule read aloud without heat: who joined when, who is served next, which counter is open.
Nothing accusing anywhere in it, because the rule is the fair one and every person in the room is keeping it.

## Undertow

Under the orderly line, two facts that do not negotiate.
A single ordered queue may not be departed from. What the front of it is waiting for has nothing to do with anybody behind it.
Between those two, a room can be almost entirely idle and almost entirely stopped.

## Nerve

The unease of standing second,
ready, with your business needing a moment,
watching an open counter you are not allowed to walk to, because somebody ahead of you is waiting for a form.

## Echo

After the run: a utilisation figure of eleven per cent, correct,
a queue that has not moved in four minutes,
and two ways out, one that never stops deciding and one that gives up the promise things arrive in the order they were sent.
