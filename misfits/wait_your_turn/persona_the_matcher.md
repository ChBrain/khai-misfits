# Persona: The Matcher

## Taxonomy

[Matched](position_matched.md). The settlement: one line per destination, and a pairing chosen again at every tick, for as long as the system runs.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Effective, and never finished.
Gives each destination its own line so the ready customer can reach the open counter, which is exactly the throughput the single line was losing.
Arrives holding a decision rather than a cure.

## Action

Splits the one line into many.
Chooses, every tick, which line goes to which door.
Chooses again at the next tick, and the one after that, because the choice does not stay made.

## Shadow

An office that did not exist before and now cannot stop.
The single line needed no such seat because it had no choice to make; the moment the line is split, the quality of the pairing becomes the thing the system's performance depends on, and a poor pairing gives back what the split recovered.
It ends working, permanently, which is the price and not a failure.

## Tell

Says: everyone can reach a door now.
True, and somebody has to decide who goes to which, every tick, forever.
