# Position: Available

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). The seat of the free counter: open, staffed, unused, and unreachable by rule.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Capacity, in full.
Staff, ready.
No customer, at any point in the scene, and no competitor for it.

## Orders

Stand open.
Serve whoever is sent.
Be sent nobody.

## Loses

Nothing it can feel, and this seat is the hinge: the thing the queue waits for **is not in use by anybody**.
Because it is idle rather than contended, every remedy aimed at scarcity misses the harm entirely, which is why the play cannot be answered by adding capacity to a system whose capacity was never spent.
It ends open, having served nobody, in a building where people waited.

## Drives

Toward being used,
with nothing preventing it but the order of a line elsewhere,
so the seat with everything to give is the seat that gives nothing.
