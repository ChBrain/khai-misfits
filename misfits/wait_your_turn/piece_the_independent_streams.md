# Piece: The Independent Streams

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). The second settlement: the single ordered stream cut into separate ones, so a stall in one does not hold the others.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Across [the doors](place_the_doors.md), replacing one sequence with several.
Bought at the cost of [the order they came in](piece_the_order_they_came_in.md), which is the thing it gives up.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play offers one remedy and reads as a scheduling problem.
It is load bearing because it is the **other** kind of answer: not a better way to run one queue but a decision to stop having one, and it shows that the two available settlements pay in different currencies.

## Apparent

A free win. Obviously better, once stated.
It reads as pure gain because what it spends was never a feature anybody listed: the guarantee that things arrive in the order they were sent.

## Yearbook

Enabled by giving up [the order they came in](piece_the_order_they_came_in.md), which everything built on top of it had assumed.
It relieves the blocking completely and returns the problem as a different one: what to do when order mattered after all.
