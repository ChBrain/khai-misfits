# Process: Matching

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). One line per destination, and a pairing of lines to doors chosen afresh at every tick.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The single line, split.
At [the doors](place_the_doors.md), every tick, without end.

## Direction

Toward letting a ready customer reach an open counter, which recovers exactly what the single line was losing.
It works, and it introduces a choice where there was none: which line is paired with which door, decided now and decided again immediately afterwards.
So the settlement is real and the cost is real, and the cost is a decision that never completes.

## Lever

Choice, which the single ordered line did not have and did not need.
Because the system's performance now depends on the quality of the pairing, this step can be done badly in a way the thing it replaced could not.

## Echo

Every ready customer reaching a door,
and an office that can never be vacated.
