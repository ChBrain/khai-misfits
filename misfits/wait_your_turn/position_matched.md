# Position: Matched

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). The seat of the settlement: one line per destination, and a pairing that has to be chosen again every tick.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A line for each destination instead of one line for all of them.
The throughput the single line was losing.
A choice to make, at every tick, that does not stay made.

## Orders

Split the line.
Pair lines to doors.
Pair them again, next tick, and the tick after that.

## Loses

The simplicity that made the single line cost nothing to run.
Because the pairing's quality now determines the system's performance, a seat has been created that can itself be done badly, where before there was no choice available to get wrong.
It ends working, permanently, which is the price rather than a failure.

## Drives

Toward every ready customer reaching an open counter,
by never stopping deciding who goes where,
so the seat that fixes the misfit is the seat that can never be vacated.
