# Plot: Waiting on Something Real

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The First in Line](persona_the_first_in_line.md), stopped at the counter.
[The Legitimate Wait](piece_the_legitimate_wait.md): a signature, a lookup, a part from the back, a form somebody else has to bring.

## Action

[Stalling](process_stalling.md): [The First in Line](persona_the_first_in_line.md), in [the head seat](position_head.md), waits at [the line](place_the_line.md) for a thing that is being fetched, and the thing is genuinely being fetched.

## Stage

[The First in Line](persona_the_first_in_line.md), on [Finish What I Am Waiting For](plan_finish_what_i_am_waiting_for.md), stands still and holds the head of the queue, doing nothing wrong and nothing slowly.

## Tension

There is no one to hurry and nothing to fix.
The wait is legitimate work proceeding at the speed legitimate work proceeds at. The person at the front did not choose it, cannot shorten it, and is not idling: they are waiting on something that has to happen. Removing the wait would mean removing the signature, the lookup or the part, each of which is there for a reason somebody could defend.
**And it has nothing whatever to do with anybody standing behind them.** The form being fetched is not a resource the queue is competing for. Nobody behind is waiting for the form. They are waiting for the person who is waiting for the form, which is a different thing and is the only thing holding them.
