# Plot: First Come, First Served

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Line](place_the_line.md), formed.
[The Order They Came In](piece_the_order_they_came_in.md), settled the moment each person joined and not revisited since.

## Action

[Ordering](process_ordering.md): everybody who arrives takes a place at [the line](place_the_line.md) behind everybody already there, and [the head seat](position_head.md) belongs to whoever has been waiting longest.

## Stage

[The One Behind](persona_the_one_behind.md), on [Go Now, I Am Ready](plan_go_now_i_am_ready.md), takes their place at the back without being asked and without resenting it, because the rule that put them there is the rule that protects them.

## Tension

The discipline is good, and the play has to establish that before it can be the problem.
First come, first served needs no supervision, cannot be worked by the confident, and treats the timid exactly as it treats the pushy: it is the rule we teach children, and a queue that abandons it becomes a queue where the loudest go first. It also costs almost nothing to run, which is why it is everywhere. **Nothing in this production improves by relaxing it**, and the production never asks for that.
So the trap cannot be sprung by finding fault with the rule. It has to be sprung with the rule intact, kept by everybody, and working exactly as designed.
