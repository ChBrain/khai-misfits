# Persona: The One Behind

## Taxonomy

[Held](position_held.md). Second in the line, ready this instant, four steps from a counter standing free.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ready. The business would take a moment and every part of it is in hand.
Keeping the rule without being asked, because the rule that is holding them is the same rule that protects them from the next confident person through the door.
Not aggrieved. Watching.

## Action

Stands.
Does not overtake, does not complain, does not walk to the open counter.
Waits for a thing that has nothing to do with them to finish happening to somebody else.

## Shadow

Nothing done and nothing to do, which is what makes the seat unbearable rather than unjust.
There is no one to appeal to: the person ahead is blameless, the counter is genuinely open, the rule is genuinely fair, and the wait is genuinely total.
It ends served, eventually, having been ready the entire time.

## Tell

Says: I only need a minute.
True, and the minute was available throughout.
