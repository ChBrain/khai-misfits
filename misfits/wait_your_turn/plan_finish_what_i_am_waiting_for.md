# Plan: Finish What I Am Waiting For

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the head seat](position_head.md), inside [Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Head](position_head.md)

## Direction

Wait for the thing being fetched, hold the front of the line while waiting because that is where standing happens, and be served when it arrives. Do nothing wrong and nothing slowly.

## Orders

([The First in Line](persona_the_first_in_line.md) waits at the counter on [the legitimate wait](piece_the_legitimate_wait.md), and holds the head of the queue by occupying it.)

## Implementation

([The Line](place_the_line.md), at the counter; the [stalling](process_stalling.md) process over a signature, a lookup or a part from the back; no provision anywhere for stepping aside.)

## Targets

- [x] the thing being fetched was requested, and is genuinely being fetched
- [ ] the wait completes at the speed legitimate work completes at
- [ ] the front of the queue is held for the whole of it, by occupation and not by choice
- [ ] nothing in the wait has any connection to anybody standing behind
