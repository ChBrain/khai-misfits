# Persona: The Utilisation

## Taxonomy

[Measured](position_measured.md). The figure for how busy the counters are: low, correct, and pointed exactly away from the harm.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Honest and well chosen.
The natural thing to measure about a bank of servers, computed the natural way.
Not hiding anything on purpose: nobody picked it in order to conceal a stopped queue.

## Action

Is computed over the counters and displayed.
Reports a low number, accurately.
Stays low while nothing moves, because the counters are in fact not being used.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is why the room passes inspection: the number is true, is the obvious one to compute, and an operator reading spare capacity from it has reasoned correctly to a true statement.
Because the harm's whole nature is that the servers are free, the figure that would raise an alarm in almost any other failure is here the signature of this one, read as its opposite.
It ends accurate and pointed the wrong way, which are not in tension.

## Tell

Says: eleven per cent.
True. And the line has not moved in four minutes.
