# Position: Head

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). The seat at the front of the line, which holds everything behind it by occupying it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The counter, and the right to it, earned by having waited longest.
A wait of its own on something legitimate that somebody else is fetching.
No means of stepping aside, because the rule has no provision for it.

## Orders

Wait for the thing being fetched.
Occupy the front of the line while waiting.
Be served when it arrives.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat is where the play's blamelessness is established: the wait is real work proceeding normally, chosen by nobody and shortenable by nobody.
Because it has no connection to anybody standing behind, there is no negotiation available and no fault to allocate.
It ends served and correct, having stopped a room.

## Drives

Toward its own completion,
by the only route open to it,
so the seat that is doing nothing wrong is the seat that holds the queue.
