# Persona: The First in Line

## Taxonomy

[Head](position_head.md). The one at the counter, stopped on something that is being fetched, holding the whole queue by standing where they are.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Patient and entirely in the right.
Waiting on a signature, a lookup, a part from the back: legitimate work, proceeding at the speed such work proceeds at.
Not idling, not dawdling, and not aware of holding anything.

## Action

Waits.
Holds the head of the queue while waiting, because the head of the queue is where they are standing.
Does not choose this, cannot shorten it, and would step aside if stepping aside were a thing the rule allowed.

## Shadow

Nothing, and that is the seat: there is no one to hurry here and no defect to remove.
The wait is a real thing being done by somebody else somewhere else, and it has no connection at all to anybody standing behind.
It ends served, correctly, having done nothing wrong, in a room where nobody moved for four minutes.

## Tell

Says: they have gone to get it, it will not be a moment.
True. And the room behind is at a standstill because of it.
