# Plot: Nothing Is Busy

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Meter](place_the_meter.md), read by whoever is answerable for the room.
[The Idle Reading](piece_the_idle_reading.md): counters idle, utilisation low, and the figure correct.

## Action

[Measuring](process_measuring.md): [The Utilisation](persona_the_utilisation.md), in [the measured seat](position_measured.md), is computed over [the doors](place_the_doors.md) and displayed at [the meter](place_the_meter.md).

## Stage

[The Utilisation](persona_the_utilisation.md), on [Report What Is Being Used](plan_report_what_is_being_used.md), reports a low number, accurately, while the queue does not move.

## Tension

The instrument is honest, well chosen, and structurally incapable of seeing this.
Nobody picked a misleading measure. A measure of how busy the servers are will report a system with idle servers as underused, and it is right: they **are** underused. Watching it more closely reports the same number more often, and watching it more often reports it more often still.
**The harm's whole nature is that the servers are free**, so the figure that would sound the alarm in almost any other failure is here the very signature of the failure, read as its opposite. An operator who responds to a low utilisation figure by concluding there is spare capacity has reasoned correctly from an accurate number to a true statement, and the queue has still not moved.
