# Piece: The Legitimate Wait

## Taxonomy

[Wait Your Turn](play_wait_your_turn.md). What the front of the queue is stopped on: a signature, a lookup, a part from the back, a form somebody else has to bring.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the line](place_the_line.md), at the counter, in full view.
Held by [the first in line](persona_the_first_in_line.md), who did not choose it and cannot shorten it.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is nothing to blame the delay on, and with it there is still nothing to blame the delay on.
It is load bearing because it is **real**: it is work proceeding normally, defensible in a review, and its legitimacy is what removes the villain from the room.

## Apparent

An inefficiency. Something a better-run counter would not have.
It reads as slack to be squeezed, which is why the first instinct is to make the front faster, and making the front faster leaves the structure exactly as it was.

## Yearbook

Produced by [stalling](process_stalling.md), transmitted by [blocking](process_blocking.md).
It has no relationship of any kind to anybody standing behind, which is the fact the whole play turns on.
