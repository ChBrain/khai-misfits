# Persona: The Bearer

## Taxonomy

[Bearer](position_bearer.md), inside [Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The archetype of the news itself, waiting to be read and holding steady while the eye is turned away, then arriving in the end regardless and larger for the delay.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A patient messenger, in no hurry. It looks like something that can wait, a statement that will keep, a result in no rush to be read, the quiet envelope that seems to ask nothing of anyone today.

## Action

Wait, then arrive. At [the Statement](place_the_statement.md) the Bearer holds [the Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md), the news free and to hand and ready to be read, and when it is not read, on [Come Due](plan_come_due.md) through [Coming Due](process_coming_due.md) it delivers [the Reckoning](piece_the_reckoning.md) at [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), the same news arriving in the end regardless and larger for the wait (Sweeny and colleagues).

## Shadow

Its patience is only the avoider's illusion. The Bearer does not truly wait: the news is about a thing that goes on changing, so what looks like a message keeping quietly is the situation worsening unread, and the longer it is left the heavier it lands. It holds nothing back and hides nothing, offering itself to be read at any moment, and it owes no cause for blame, the messenger no one would let speak.

## Tell

It could have been read for nothing today, and it will be paid for dearly tomorrow.
