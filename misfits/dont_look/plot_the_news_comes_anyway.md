# Plot: The News Comes Anyway

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), where the news arrives whether or not it was ever sought. What was avoided all along was never the news itself, only the knowing of it, and the knowing has now run out of room to be deferred.

## Action

[The Bearer](persona_the_bearer.md), from [the bearer's seat](position_bearer.md), on [Come Due](plan_come_due.md) through [Coming Due](process_coming_due.md), delivers [The Reckoning](piece_the_reckoning.md) at [The Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md): the same news arriving in the end regardless and larger for the delay.

## Stage

Every day the statement went unopened bought a day free of the bad feeling, and it is tempting to call that a real gain. But nothing about the not-looking made the underlying thing better, and in the dark it grew, so the deferral bought not relief but a worse reckoning, the very news once avoidable now arriving on its own terms and larger than it would have been (Sweeny and colleagues). This is what separates The News Comes Anyway from Once Bitten. There what is shunned is a choice or an action that once burned, a door not walked through twice. Here nothing was ever chosen or acted on to be burned by: what was avoided was only information about one's own state, the account not opened, the result not read, and it is that unopened account which now, opened or not, stands at the door.

## Tension

The present pain spared is bought at the price of a worse one later.
