# Plot: Don't Open It

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Statement](place_the_statement.md) still sits at the desk, and now the moment of choice has passed without a choice being made. The envelope stays sealed, the login is not made, and the day closes the way it opened, unread.

## Action

[The Ostrich](persona_the_ostrich.md), from [the ostrich's seat](position_ostrich.md), on [Look Away](plan_look_away.md) through [Looking Away](process_looking_away.md), turns [The Averted Eye](piece_the_averted_eye.md) at [The Statement](place_the_statement.md): not looking, and least of all when the news is worst.

## Stage

The looking does not track how much the news matters. It tracks how good the news is expected to be, checked often when the account is likely up and checked rarely when it is likely down, so attention rises and falls with the expected valence of the news rather than with the stakes riding on it (Karlsson, Loewenstein and Seppi). This is what tells Don't Open It apart from Plausible Deniability. There the not-knowing faces outward, held up as a shield before a judge who might otherwise assign blame. Here it faces inward: no judge is watching, no blame is at stake, and the only one spared by the averted eye is the one who averts it, and the only one harmed is the same.

## Tension

The looking stops exactly when the situation most needs watching.
