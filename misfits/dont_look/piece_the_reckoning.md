# Piece: The Reckoning

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The bad news arriving in the end regardless, and larger for the delay: the present pain that was spared bought at the price of a worse one later.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), carried by [Coming Due](process_coming_due.md), voiced by [The Bearer](persona_the_bearer.md).

## Load Bearing

What was deferred was only the knowing, never the news itself; the situation the news describes went on changing whether or not anyone read it. So when it finally arrives, unread and untended the whole while, it arrives heavier than it would have (Sweeny and colleagues). Draw this apart from Once Bitten, where what is avoided is a choice or an action that once caused harm and is afterward shunned. Here nothing was ever chosen or acted upon and then avoided. What was avoided was information about one's own state, the account not opened, the result not read, and the Reckoning is that same unread information returning on its own schedule rather than the reader's.

## Apparent

It looks like sudden bad luck, a blow from nowhere landing on someone who did nothing to deserve it. Nothing about its arrival looks like the outcome of a choice made earlier.

## Yearbook

Carried by [Coming Due](process_coming_due.md), staged at [The Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), voiced by [The Bearer](persona_the_bearer.md), dramatized in [The News Comes Anyway](plot_the_news_comes_anyway.md); grown from [The Blind Spell](piece_the_blind_spell.md), it is the unread [The Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md) come due, and it is what [The Forced Look](piece_the_forced_look.md) would have forestalled.
