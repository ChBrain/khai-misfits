# Piece: The Ledger

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The information about one's own state, free and to hand and ready to help if read: the account that could be opened, the login a tap away, the result already sitting at the desk.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Statement](place_the_statement.md), held by [The Bearer](persona_the_bearer.md): the envelope already delivered, the number already computed, waiting on nothing but the eye that would read it.

## Load Bearing

The Ledger is information that is free, to hand, and useful: no fee stands between the reader and their own situation, and knowing it would genuinely help (Galai and Sade). This is the hinge that separates the Ledger from Cheaper Not to Know: there the information is costly to acquire and would not change the decision even if had, so skipping it is sound. Here the looking costs almost nothing, a tap, a glance, and it would change what the reader does next. What keeps the Ledger unread is not its price. It is only the news it is expected to carry.

## Apparent

It looks like an ordinary statement, a result that can wait its turn, asking nothing of anyone today. Nothing about it seems urgent, and nothing about it seems to demand a today rather than a someday.

## Yearbook

Held by [The Bearer](persona_the_bearer.md) at [The Statement](place_the_statement.md), dramatized in [The Ledger Waits](plot_the_ledger_waits.md); its news is what [The Flinch](piece_the_flinch.md) recoils from before it is even seen, and left unread it becomes, in time, [The Reckoning](piece_the_reckoning.md).
