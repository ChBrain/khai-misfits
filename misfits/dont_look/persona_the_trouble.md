# Persona: The Trouble

## Taxonomy

[Trouble](position_trouble.md), inside [Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The archetype of the small, still-fixable problem that grows unwatched in the dark the not-looking makes, addressable all the while it is ignored.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A little thing, nothing to worry about. It looks like a minor matter that can safely be left, the small overdraft, the odd symptom, the slipping figure, easy to tell yourself is not yet worth the looking.

## Action

Grow. At [the Dark](place_the_dark.md), on [Grow Unwatched](plan_grow_unwatched.md) through [Worsening](process_worsening.md), the Trouble becomes [the Blind Spell](piece_the_blind_spell.md), swelling quietly for as long as the eye is averted, still within reach of a fix the whole time it goes untended.

## Shadow

Its smallness is a window, not a reprieve. For as long as it is watched the Trouble can be caught and mended cheaply, but the not-looking is exactly when it is worst, so it grows most in the dark that the flinch from it makes, and the window that was open while it was small closes unseen. It bears no ill will and springs from no malice, only the ordinary way untended things worsen, and it owes no cause for blame.

## Tell

It could have been fixed while it was small, and it grew most in the time no one looked.
