# Pitch: Better Not to Look

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Don't Look](play_dont_look.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that where the news about your own situation is likely to be bad, the free look feels not worth the pain, and you watch least exactly when it matters most.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Quiet, gentle, understood. An ordinary person who could open the statement or log in and simply does not, and no one blames them: learning bad news hurts, and the flinch from a coming blow is the most human thing there is. Nothing here is laziness or folly.

## Undertow

The information is free and to hand and would help, so declining it is not the cost-benefit call of rational ignorance (Galai and Sade). It is avoided because the news itself is likely to hurt, and free information is not free to receive when what it carries is pain (Golman, Hagmann and Loewenstein). So the eye turns away just as the news turns bad, and the looking stops precisely when it would matter most (Karlsson, Loewenstein and Seppi).

## Nerve

That the flinch is rational in its own coin, buying a real and present comfort, while the cost, a problem left to grow unwatched, is deferred and diffuse and falls due later, larger. No one is careless; the very moment the situation most needs watching is the moment it is hardest to look.

## Echo

You could know in a moment and for nothing, and precisely because the news is bad, better not to look.
