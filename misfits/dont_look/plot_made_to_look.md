# Plot: Made to Look

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), reached again, but this time by arrangement rather than by accident. The choice of whether to look has been taken out of the moment it would otherwise be lost in.

## Action

[The Steward](persona_the_steward.md), from [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), on [Look Anyway](plan_look_anyway.md) through [Forcing the Look](process_forcing_the_look.md), installs [The Forced Look](piece_the_forced_look.md) at [The Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md): the commitment or the automatic monitoring that makes the news arrive whether or not it is sought.

## Stage

The Flinch wins its contest one moment at a time, whenever the choice to look or not is left standing open in the moment the news might be bad. Take the looking out of that moment, set it on a schedule or bind it to a commitment made in advance, and the Flinch has no moment left to win in, since the news now arrives on its own regardless of what any single moment's feeling would have chosen. But the remedy is not free and not always right. To be made to face bad news that cannot be acted on is distress bought for no gain, and some news is genuinely better left unknown, so forcing the look lays down a real cost against a real but uncertain benefit. And the bound must hold here as everywhere in this house: the tendency to look away is real but of varying and debated magnitude, and it is not universal, since some watch their own situation more closely under stress rather than less, the meerkat that answers the ostrich, and not all not-looking is a flinch to begin with, some of it the sound and rational declining of information that costs more than it is worth.

## Tension

The only reliable look is the one arranged before the blow, and it is not free and not always right.
