# Piece: The Blind Spell

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The stretch during which the problem goes unwatched while it is still addressable, attention lowest exactly when it would matter most.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Dark](place_the_dark.md), carried by [Worsening](process_worsening.md), voiced by [The Trouble](persona_the_trouble.md).

## Load Bearing

Through the whole of the Blind Spell the problem stays fixable; nothing about it becomes unreachable while it is merely ignored. But the not-looking falls exactly when the problem is worst and least tended, since that is when the Flinch is sharpest and the eye turns away hardest, so the trouble grows most precisely in the dark that the flinch itself makes. The looking and the needing to look, which in a well-tended situation would meet, pull apart instead: attention withdraws just as the need for it rises.

## Apparent

It looks like quiet, nothing wrong on the surface, a calm stretch with no news to report. The stillness reads as safety rather than as the absence of anyone watching.
