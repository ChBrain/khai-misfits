# Position: Ostrich

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The seat of the one who could learn its own situation for nothing and looks away because the news is likely to hurt, watching least exactly when the looking matters most.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Information about its own state, free and to hand: the statement, the login, the result, all yours to read in a moment.
A fair idea the news is bad, and so an anticipated pain waiting in the looking, a blow it can see coming.
A present comfort within reach at no felt cost, the sparing of that pain, bought simply by not looking now.

## Orders

Keep your peace, and do not open it.
Leave the statement sealed and the login untouched, since looking will only hurt and not looking costs you nothing you can feel today, and least of all look when the news is likely worst.
Know that the comfort is a flinch and not composure, that the tell is the timing, freely looking when the news is good and stopping when it turns bad, and that what you defer is the knowing and not the news, no fault in it and no cowardice, only the human turn from a coming blow.

## Loses

Nothing it can feel now, and everything it declines to see.
Because the sparing of the present pain is the very not-watching, the seat that keeps its peace lets the problem it might have caught grow unseen, and forfeits the cheap early fix that only looking would have found.
It keeps its calm and its comfort and loses no cause for blame, the human eye that turns from the blow it sees coming.

## Drives

Toward the one who looks away to spare itself the hurt,
declining the free look because the news is bad, neither lazy nor a coward,
so the seat that could know for nothing does not, and least of all when the news matters most, the flinch immediate and its cost deferred.
