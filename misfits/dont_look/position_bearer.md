# Position: Bearer

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The seat of the news itself, free to be read at any moment and holding while the eye is turned away, then arriving in the end regardless and larger for the delay.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The news of the situation, complete and ready, offered to be read at no cost the instant it is sought.
No power to force itself on anyone: it can be left unopened for as long as the reader chooses, and it makes no demand.
A tie to a thing that keeps changing, so that what it reports goes on worsening while it waits, and the message it carries grows heavier the longer it is left.

## Orders

Wait to be read, and come due in the end.
Sit ready at the desk to be read for nothing today, and if you are not read, arrive later regardless, heavier for the wait, since the thing you report did not pause while the eye was away.
Know that your patience is only the avoider's illusion, that you hide nothing and hold nothing back, and that being left unread does not spare the reader the news but only defers the knowing and swells the bill.

## Loses

Nothing of itself, and it is powerless to be heard in time.
Because it can be left unopened at will, the seat that would have cost nothing to read today is refused, and it can only return later, larger, as the reckoning the delay made, never able to make itself known at the moment reading would have been cheap.
It keeps its full and honest message and loses no cause for blame, the messenger no one would let speak.

## Drives

Toward the news free to read and left unread,
waiting patiently and hiding nothing, tied to a thing that will not wait,
so the seat that could have been had for nothing today comes due tomorrow, heavier for every hour the eye stayed turned away.
