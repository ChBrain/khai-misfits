# Persona: The Ostrich

## Taxonomy

[Ostrich](position_ostrich.md), inside [Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The archetype of the one who could learn its own situation for nothing and looks away because the news is likely to hurt, watching least exactly when it matters most.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A calm person keeping their peace. It looks like someone unbothered, not fretting over every dip, sensibly declining to obsess, the very picture of composure that knows not to check the account ten times a day.

## Action

Look away. At [the Statement](place_the_statement.md), the Ostrich meets [the Flinch](piece_the_flinch.md), the anticipated pain of the likely-bad news, then on [Look Away](plan_look_away.md) through [Looking Away](process_looking_away.md) turns [the Averted Eye](piece_the_averted_eye.md), not opening the statement and not logging in, and least of all when the news is worst (Karlsson, Loewenstein and Seppi).

## Shadow

Its calm is a flinch, not composure. The Ostrich is not declining to obsess but sparing itself a present pain, and the tell is the timing: it looks freely when the news is likely good and stops looking just as the news turns bad, so the not-looking tracks the hurt and not the need (Karlsson, Loewenstein and Seppi). It is neither lazy nor a coward, only human in turning from a coming blow, and it owes no cause for blame.

## Tell

It checks happily when the market rises and stops checking the moment it falls.
