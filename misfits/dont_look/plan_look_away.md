# Plan: Look Away

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the ostrich's seat](position_ostrich.md), inside [Don't Look](play_dont_look.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Ostrich](position_ostrich.md)

## Direction

Leave the statement sealed and the login untouched, since looking will only hurt and not looking costs you nothing you can feel today, and least of all look when the news is likely worst. Know that the comfort is a flinch and not composure, that the tell is the timing, freely looking when the news is good and stopping when it turns bad, and that what you defer is the knowing and not the news, no fault in it and no cowardice, only the human turn from a coming blow.

## Orders

([The Ostrich](persona_the_ostrich.md), having met [the Flinch](piece_the_flinch.md) at [the Statement](place_the_statement.md), turns [the Averted Eye](piece_the_averted_eye.md) through [Looking Away](process_looking_away.md), not opening the statement and not logging in.)

## Implementation

([Looking Away](process_looking_away.md) at [the Statement](place_the_statement.md) turns the eye from the news, because [the Flinch](piece_the_flinch.md) makes the free information costly to receive, so the looking stops just as the news turns bad.)

- [ ] the declining is understood as a flinch from the anticipated pain, not composure
