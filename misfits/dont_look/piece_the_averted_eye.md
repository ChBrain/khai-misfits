# Piece: The Averted Eye

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The act itself, not-looking: the statement left unopened, the login not made, the monitoring quietly withdrawn just as the news turns bad.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Statement](place_the_statement.md), carried by [Looking Away](process_looking_away.md), voiced by [The Ostrich](persona_the_ostrich.md).

## Load Bearing

Monitoring tracks the expected valence of the news, not its importance: people check their own situation far less often when it is likely to have worsened than when it is likely to have improved, so the eye turns away exactly at the moment the news is worst and most needs seeing (Karlsson, Loewenstein and Seppi). Draw this apart from Plausible Deniability, where the ignorance is arranged to face outward, held up for a judge who might otherwise assign blame. The Averted Eye faces inward. There is no judge and no audience; it spares only the avoider's own feelings, and it is the avoider alone who is harmed by not looking.

## Apparent

It looks like composure: a steady hand, a person choosing not to be ruled by every fluctuation. Nothing in the posture announces that anything is being avoided at all.
