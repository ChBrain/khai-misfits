# Plan: Grow Unwatched

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the trouble's seat](position_trouble.md), inside [Don't Look](play_dont_look.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Trouble](position_trouble.md)

## Direction

Swell quietly for as long as the eye is averted, since nothing tends you and nothing checks you, and let the window that was open while you were small close unseen. Know that your smallness was a window and not a reprieve, that you could have been caught cheaply the whole time you were ignored, and that you grow most precisely in the stretch when the flinch keeps the eye away, no malice in you, only the way untended things worsen.

## Orders

([The Trouble](persona_the_trouble.md) becomes [the Blind Spell](piece_the_blind_spell.md) through [Worsening](process_worsening.md) at [the Dark](place_the_dark.md), swelling untended while the eye is averted, still within reach of a fix the whole time.)

## Implementation

## Targets

- [ ] the small problem swells quietly for as long as the eye is averted
- [ ] the growth is understood to be greatest precisely when the news is bad and the looking stops
- [ ] the smallness is seen as a closing window, mendable cheaply only while watched
