# Position: Steward

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The seat of the one who takes the looking out of the moment, committing in advance or setting the news to arrive on its own, and who marks that the forced look is costly and not always right.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A cool hour, away from the blow, in which looking can be chosen before the flinch is upon it.
Means to bind the later self: the standing check, the automatic alert, the schedule that makes the news arrive whether or not it is sought.
A clear sight of the cost: that forced attention on unactionable bad news is distress for no gain, and that some news is genuinely better unknown.

## Orders

Arrange the looking in advance, and make not-looking the harder path.
Set the monitoring on a schedule and the alert to come on its own, commit in the cool hour to the look the moment will not choose, so the news arrives before the trouble hardens and the flinch has no moment to win in.
Know that the remedy is not free and not always right, that being made to stare at bad news you cannot act on is a cost and not a cure, and that the pull to look away returns each time the news turns bad, the hedge you stand on and the bound you must name.

## Loses

The ease it spends to bind the future, and it loses that knowingly.
Because the looking must be paid for in advance, in the friction of the schedule and the discomfort of news that comes unbidden, the seat buys the watched situation at the price of the comfort of the averted eye, and where the news is unactionable it may buy only distress.
It keeps its clear sight of the cost and loses no cause for blame, the planner who moves the choice out of the worst moment.

## Drives

Toward the one who takes the looking out of the moment,
binding the later self with the schedule and the alert so the news comes whether sought or not, at the price of the comfort spent,
so the seat that alone breaks the flinch breaks it only by arranging the look before the blow, the remedy real and dear and not always right.
