# Plan: Look Anyway

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), inside [Don't Look](play_dont_look.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Steward](position_steward.md)

## Direction

Set the monitoring on a schedule and the alert to come on its own, commit in the cool hour to the look the moment will not choose, so the news arrives before the trouble hardens and the flinch has no moment to win in. Know that the remedy is not free and not always right, that being made to stare at bad news you cannot act on is a cost and not a cure, and that the pull to look away returns each time the news turns bad, the hedge you stand on and the bound you must name.

## Orders

([The Steward](persona_the_steward.md) installs [the Forced Look](piece_the_forced_look.md) through [Forcing the Look](process_forcing_the_look.md) at [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), the commitment or the automatic monitoring that makes the news arrive whether or not it is sought.)

## Implementation

([Forcing the Look](process_forcing_the_look.md) at [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md) sets [the Forced Look](piece_the_forced_look.md), because taking the choice out of the moment denies the flinch its moment, so the news comes on a schedule and not-looking is made the harder path.)

## Targets

- [ ] the looking is arranged in advance, the schedule set and the alert made to come on its own
- [ ] the remedy is understood to work by taking the choice out of the moment the flinch would win
- [ ] the cost is named, the friction of the forced look and the distress of unactionable news
- [ ] the bound is held, that where the news is better unknown or cannot be acted on the forced look is no cure
