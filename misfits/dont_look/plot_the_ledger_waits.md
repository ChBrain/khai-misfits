# Plot: The Ledger Waits

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Statement](place_the_statement.md), where the account sits unopened. The login is a tap away and the result is already computed, waiting at the desk for nothing but an eye.

## Action

[The Bearer](persona_the_bearer.md), from [the bearer's seat](position_bearer.md), holds [The Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md) at [The Statement](place_the_statement.md): the news free and to hand, ready to be read at no cost.

## Stage

The Ledger is information about one's own state, and it costs the reader almost nothing to obtain: no fee, no study, no wait, only the small act of looking (Galai and Sade). This is what separates the beat from Cheaper Not to Know. There the information is dear to acquire and, even had, would not change what is decided, so skipping it is the sound and rational move. Here the opposite holds on both counts: the looking is free and to hand, and the knowing would genuinely help, would show whether the account is sound or overdrawn, whether the result is the one hoped for. What keeps the Ledger closed is not its price. It is only the news it is expected to carry.

## Tension

The look would cost almost nothing, and the news is likely bad.
