# Piece: The Forced Look

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The remedy and the bound together: taking the choice out of the moment so the news arrives whether or not it is sought.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), carried by [Forcing the Look](process_forcing_the_look.md), voiced by [The Steward](persona_the_steward.md).

## Load Bearing

The Flinch wins because the choice to look is left to the moment, and in that moment the comfortable move always beats the painful one. So the remedy does not try to out-argue the Flinch in the moment at all; it removes the moment. A standing check made in advance, an alert set to arrive on its own, a commitment entered in a cool hour before any bad news is in view: these make the looking automatic rather than chosen, so there is no instant left in which the flinch could win. But the Forced Look is not free. It costs real friction to set up and keep, and being made to face bad news that cannot be acted on is distress bought for no gain at all. Nor is it always right, since some news genuinely is better left unknown. And even a good arrangement is not proof against the next flinch, which can undo tomorrow what was set up today. The Forced Look also carries the bound on the whole misfit: the tendency it answers is real but of varying and debated size, and it is not universal, since some watch more closely under stress rather than less, the meerkat set against the ostrich (Gherzi and colleagues). Not all not-looking is this flinch either; some of it is the sound rational ignorance the house stages on its own account, where the news would cost more to get than it is worth.

## Apparent

It looks like fuss, an anxious hovering, more checking than the situation seems to call for. Nothing about it looks like relief; it looks like someone who cannot let a thing alone.
