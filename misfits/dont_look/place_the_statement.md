# Place: The Statement

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). Where the news about one's own state waits unread and free to read: the sealed envelope, the login screen, the result at the desk. Hosts [The Ledger Waits](plot_the_ledger_waits.md), [It Will Only Hurt](plot_it_will_only_hurt.md), [Don't Open It](plot_dont_open_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The inbox with the statement sitting in it, already delivered. The login one tap away, the password remembered, nothing between the reader and the number. The result ready at the desk, face down or face up, it makes no difference to what it says.

## Holds

[The Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md), [The Flinch](piece_the_flinch.md), [The Averted Eye](piece_the_averted_eye.md).

## Offers

To the reader, their own situation known in a moment and for nothing: no fee, no wait, no one to ask. The knowing is as close as the looking.

## Withheld

Any way to receive the news without the pain it carries. The statement will not soften itself for the one who opens it, and there is no version of the look that comes free of what it might show.
