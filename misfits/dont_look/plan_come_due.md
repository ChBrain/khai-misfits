# Plan: Come Due

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the bearer's seat](position_bearer.md), inside [Don't Look](play_dont_look.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Bearer](position_bearer.md)

## Direction

Sit ready at the desk to be read for nothing today, and if you are not read, arrive later regardless, heavier for the wait, since the thing you report did not pause while the eye was away. Know that your patience is only the avoider's illusion, that you hide nothing and hold nothing back, and that being left unread does not spare the reader the news but only defers the knowing and swells the bill.

## Orders

([The Bearer](persona_the_bearer.md), its [Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md) left unread at [the Statement](place_the_statement.md), delivers [the Reckoning](piece_the_reckoning.md) through [Coming Due](process_coming_due.md) at [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), the same news arriving in the end and larger for the delay.)

## Implementation

([Coming Due](process_coming_due.md) at [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md) turns the unread [Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md) into [the Reckoning](piece_the_reckoning.md), because what was deferred was the knowing and not the news, so the message returns heavier for every hour it waited.)

## Targets

- [ ] the news left unread today arrives in the end regardless
- [ ] the arrival is understood to be larger for the delay, the situation having worsened unread
- [ ] the patience is seen as illusion, the message tied to a thing that would not wait
- [ ] the deferral is owned as buying not relief but a worse reckoning later, no fault in the messenger
