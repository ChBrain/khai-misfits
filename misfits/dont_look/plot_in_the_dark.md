# Plot: In the Dark

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Dark](place_the_dark.md), where no eye reaches while the statement stays closed. What the news would have been about does not pause for the not-looking; it keeps on being true whether or not anyone reads it.

## Action

[The Trouble](persona_the_trouble.md), from [the trouble's seat](position_trouble.md), on [Grow Unwatched](plan_grow_unwatched.md) through [Worsening](process_worsening.md), becomes [The Blind Spell](piece_the_blind_spell.md) at [The Dark](place_the_dark.md): the small problem swelling untended, still fixable all the while.

## Stage

The debt still accrues interest whether or not the statement is opened; the symptom still runs its course whether or not the result is read. Nothing about not-looking pauses the thing looked away from, and a problem left untended is a problem free to grow, addressable the whole while, since nothing about its size forecloses the fix, only the fix is never reached for. And it grows fastest in exactly the stretch the Flinch keeps the eye turned, because that stretch is also the one in which the news would have been worst and the tending most needed, so attention is lowest precisely where it would matter most. No hand pushes it along. It is only the ordinary way an untended thing worsens, in the dark that the not-looking itself made.

## Tension

The problem grew most in the very stretch no one looked.
