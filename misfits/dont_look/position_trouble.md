# Position: Trouble

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The seat of the small, still-fixable problem that grows unwatched in the dark the not-looking makes, addressable all the while it is ignored.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A small beginning, cheap to mend while it is caught early, the overdraft slight, the symptom faint, the figure just starting to slip.
An open window: for as long as it is watched it can be met and fixed before it hardens, its cure within reach.
A quiet way of worsening when untended, growing without noise or drama, drawing no attention to the fact that it is growing.

## Orders

Grow, and grow most in the dark.
Swell quietly for as long as the eye is averted, since nothing tends you and nothing checks you, and let the window that was open while you were small close unseen.
Know that your smallness was a window and not a reprieve, that you could have been caught cheaply the whole time you were ignored, and that you grow most precisely in the stretch when the flinch keeps the eye away, no malice in you, only the way untended things worsen.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and it costs the reprieve that only watching would have kept.
Because the not-looking is exactly when it is worst, the seat that could have been mended while small is instead left to harden, and the cheap early fix is forfeited to the growth the dark allowed.
It bears no ill will and loses no cause for blame, the ordinary problem that worsened only because no one looked.

## Drives

Toward the small thing left to grow in the dark,
mendable the whole time it is ignored, worsening without noise,
so the seat that a look would have caught cheaply hardens unseen, growing most in the very stretch the flinch keeps the eye turned away.
