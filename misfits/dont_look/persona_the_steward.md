# Persona: The Steward

## Taxonomy

[Steward](position_steward.md), inside [Don't Look](play_dont_look.md). The archetype of the one who takes the looking out of the moment, committing in advance or setting the news to arrive on its own, and who marks that the forced look is costly and not always right.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A sensible planner, ahead of the trouble. It looks like the prudent hand that sets up the standing order and the regular checkup, the calm foresight that arranges things so they cannot be forgotten.

## Action

Force the look. At [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), on [Look Anyway](plan_look_anyway.md) through [Forcing the Look](process_forcing_the_look.md), the Steward installs [the Forced Look](piece_the_forced_look.md), the commitment made in a cool hour or the automatic monitoring, so the news arrives whether or not it is sought and not-looking is made harder than looking.

## Shadow

Its remedy asks a price and is not always right. To be made to face bad news you can do nothing about is distress for no gain, some news is genuinely better not known, and even a good arrangement is undone by the same flinch the next time the news turns bad, since the pull to look away returns whenever the looking is left to the will. It knows the hedge it stands on and claims no cure, only a way of moving the choice out of the worst moment.

## Tell

It arranges the looking in advance, because it knows the moment itself will not choose to look.
