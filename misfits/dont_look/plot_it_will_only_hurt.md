# Plot: It Will Only Hurt

## Taxonomy

[Don't Look](play_dont_look.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Statement](place_the_statement.md) waits at the same desk, and now it is not the cost of looking that is weighed but the shape of what looking would show. There is no proof yet, only a fair and quiet idea that the number will be worse than hoped.

## Action

[The Ostrich](persona_the_ostrich.md), from [the ostrich's seat](position_ostrich.md), meets [The Flinch](piece_the_flinch.md) at [The Statement](place_the_statement.md): the anticipated pain that makes free information costly to receive, arriving before a single figure has been read.

## Stage

Learning bad news is not neutral: it carries a real psychological cost of its own, a bad feeling, a pressure to act, the knowledge that once known a thing cannot be unknown, and that cost attaches to the news whether or not the looking itself costs anything (Golman, Hagmann and Loewenstein; Sweeny and colleagues). So the Flinch is not squeamishness and not a failure of nerve. It is the ordinary and human response to a coming pain that is real, felt in advance of the fact it only anticipates, and it is understood here, not despised.

## Tension

Free information is not free to receive when what it carries is pain.
