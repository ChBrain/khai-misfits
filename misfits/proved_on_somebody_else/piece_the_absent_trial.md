# Piece: The Absent Trial

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). The pragmatic study that would answer the clinician's question, named in 1967, right, expensive, and mostly not run.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Nowhere. Named in the literature since Schwartz and Lellouch, and absent for most of what a clinic hands out.
Answered by no party in the play, none of whom is positioned to commission it.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a description of a bind with no way out, and the house does not stage those without naming the way that exists.
It is load bearing because the remedy is real and old and specific: run it in ordinary conditions with ordinary patients and measure what happens, which buys back relevance at the price of the precision that made the first answer worth having, and needs far more participants to reach the same confidence.

## Apparent

An obvious idea that somebody ought to fund.
It presents as an oversight, and it is a price: the trade between rigour and relevance is not an error in the system, it is the shape of the problem the system is solving.

## Yearbook

Named by Schwartz and Lellouch and pressed for since.
It would measure [the distance](piece_the_distance.md), it would spend [the power](piece_the_power.md) that [the criteria](piece_the_criteria.md) were protecting, and it is what [the clinician](persona_the_clinician.md) would read if it existed.
