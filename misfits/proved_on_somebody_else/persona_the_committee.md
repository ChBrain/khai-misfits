# Persona: The Committee

## Taxonomy

[Guardian](position_guardian.md). Refuses to approve a study that would expose people and answer nothing, which is the protective act that produces the narrow entry.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Conscientious, unhurried, and entirely on the participant's side.
Reads the protocol for whether the risk asked of participants is justified by what the study can deliver.
No obstruction in it: this is the body that exists so people are not used for nothing.

## Action

Asks whether the study is powered to answer its question.
Declines a broader entry that would cost the power to detect anything, because a study that exposes people to risk and returns no answer is not a weaker study but an unethical one.
Approves the narrow protocol, and is right to.

## Shadow

Nothing it can see, and it is the seat where the misfit is manufactured: **the safeguard and the unrepresentativeness are one act**, so protecting participants is what removes them from the evidence.
Because there is no version of the approval that both protects the enrolled and represents the treated, it cannot be argued out of the choice by anybody who understands the choice.
It ends having done its job exactly, and having narrowed the world the answer will describe.

## Tell

Says: this is not powered to answer that in that population.
Which is correct, is the reason the population is not in it, and is why the answer will later be used on them anyway.
