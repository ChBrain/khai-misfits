# Pitch: Clean Enough to Be About Nobody

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Careful, clinical, itemised.
The register of a protocol: inclusion criteria, exclusion criteria, primary endpoint, and a line of justification against each.
Nothing defensive in it, because nothing in it needs defending: every exclusion has a reason and the reasons are good ones.

## Undertow

Under the protocol, a filter that selects for the absence of ordinary life.
Three conditions at once, a long list of other medicines, eighty-six years: each of them a reason to be left out of the study and each of them a reason to be given the drug.
Rigour and relevance are not two settings on one dial. They are one act read from either end.

## Nerve

The unease of watching a genuinely decisive result arrive,
established beyond reasonable doubt, by people who did everything correctly,
about a population that does not walk into any clinic.

## Echo

After the run: a licence, a label carrying an instruction and not an inclusion list,
a woman of eighty-six on four other medicines taking a drug proved on somebody unlike her,
and a clinician who knows all of this and has no other trial to read.
