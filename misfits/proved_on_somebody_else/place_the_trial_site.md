# Place: The Trial Site

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). An expert centre where the conditions can be held still, which is what makes the demonstration possible and what makes it unlike a clinic.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A specialist unit with a protocol, a screening log, scheduled visits, monitoring and adherence support.
Selected patients, seen more often than ordinary care sees anyone, by staff with a research role.

## Holds

The only conditions under which the causal question can be answered at all, and every one of them a departure from ordinary practice.
It holds both halves of the misfit: the control that makes the effect visible, and the artificiality that is the same control described from the other side.

## Offers

A setting in which one thing varies and the rest is held, which is precisely what an experiment requires and precisely what nowhere else provides.

## Withheld

Resemblance to the place the answer will be used.
The site cannot both hold the conditions still and look like a clinic, so the fidelity that would make the result transferable is the fidelity that would destroy it.
