# Position: Approver

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). The seat that will not license on a contaminated signal, and so requires the design that cannot look like practice.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An evidentiary bar, and the authority to hold it.
A history of harm from approving on weak evidence, which is why the bar exists.
An indication written as a clinical category rather than as a roster of the enrolled.

## Orders

Require an effect shown without confounding.
License on the clean demonstration.
Write an indication that a clinician can act on.

## Loses

Nothing, and it sets the direction for everybody upstream: the bar it holds is what makes the explanatory design necessary, so the demand for certainty is what guarantees the narrow study.
Because lowering the bar to buy representativeness is the failure it was built to prevent, the trade is closed to it in both directions.
It ends having protected the public with evidence gathered on part of it.

## Drives

Toward certainty before exposure,
right in every single instance to demand it,
so the seat guarding against bad evidence is the reason the good evidence is about fewer people.
