# Process: Licensing

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). The regulator approving on the clean demonstration and issuing an indication in general clinical terms.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A clean result meeting an evidentiary bar.
At [the label](place_the_label.md), an indication, a dose and a set of warnings.

## Direction

Toward a usable instruction.
The bar is held because approving on a muddy signal is the failure regulation exists to prevent, and the indication is written as a clinical category because a licence restricted to the enrolled roster would be unusable and would also be arbitrary.
So the answer is released in a form that is actionable and does not carry the population it came from.

## Lever

The evidentiary standard, which cannot be lowered to buy representativeness without becoming the harm it guards against.
Because the standard is what forced the explanatory design, the regulator's rigour and the study's narrowness are the same requirement seen twice.

## Echo

A finding in general circulation, correct and unmoored.
From here on, the criteria live in a paper and the instruction lives in a clinic, and only one of them is in the room when a decision is made.
