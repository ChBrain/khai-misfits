# Persona: The Excluded

## Taxonomy

[Outside](position_outside.md). Kept out of the study for their own protection, and prescribed the result of it, being exactly the kind of person the drug is for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Eighty-six, three conditions, four other medicines, and entirely ordinary.
Not rare, not an edge case, not a complication: the modal patient in a great many clinics.
Nothing unreasonable about the exclusion that was applied to them.

## Action

Is screened and found ineligible, on grounds that are genuinely protective.
Does not enter the trial, does not appear in the analysis, and is not represented in the estimate.
Receives the drug, later, on the licence the trial produced.

## Shadow

Not overlooked, and excluded by the very features that make them likely to be treated: comorbidity, polypharmacy and age are simultaneously the commonest reasons to be kept out of a study and the commonest reasons to be given something.
So the filter runs in the opposite direction to the prescribing, and the further a person sits from the evidence the more likely they are to be handed it.
It ends taking a medicine whose effect in people like them has not been measured, for reasons that were entirely about their safety.

## Tell

Says nothing, having never been asked.
Appears in no forest plot, and in the prescribing figures for the same drug in very large numbers.
