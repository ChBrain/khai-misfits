# Plan: Do Not Enrol Who Cannot Be Answered For

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the guardian's seat](position_guardian.md), inside [Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Guardian](position_guardian.md)

## Direction

Refuse any protocol that would expose participants to risk without the power to answer its question. Do not accept a broader entry on the grounds that it is more representative, because representativeness is not a benefit to the person enrolled in a study that settles nothing.

## Orders

([The Committee](persona_the_committee.md) tests the protocol for power against the risk asked of participants, declines the broad version, and approves the narrow one, which is the protective decision.)

## Implementation

([The Trial Site](place_the_trial_site.md), whose entry it governs; the [narrowing](process_narrowing.md) it authorises; [the power](piece_the_power.md) that heterogeneity would have cost, and [the distance](piece_the_distance.md) that protecting the participant opens.)

## Targets

- [ ] the protocol is tested for whether it can answer its question at all
- [ ] a broader entry is declined because it would leave the study unpowered
- [ ] the refusal is made on the participants' behalf and not the sponsor's
- [ ] the narrow protocol is approved, correctly, and the treated population is thereby left out
