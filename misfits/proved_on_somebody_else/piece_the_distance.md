# Piece: The Distance

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). The gap between the population the effect was measured in and the population it is used on, running systematically in one direction.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Between [the trial site](place_the_trial_site.md) and [the clinic](place_the_clinic.md).
Recorded in neither: the protocol lists who was excluded, the prescribing records list who was treated, and nothing puts the two on one page.

## Load Bearing

Without it the finding transfers and there is nothing here.
It is load bearing because it is **not random**: comorbidity, polypharmacy and age are simultaneously the commonest grounds for exclusion and the commonest grounds for prescription, so the further a person sits from the evidence the likelier they are to receive it, which is the misfit in a sentence.

## Apparent

An ordinary caveat, the sort of limitation every paper carries.
It reads as a standard qualification rather than as a systematic inversion, and the direction, which is the whole point, is invisible from either end alone.
