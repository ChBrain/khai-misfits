# Process: Supporting

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). Monitoring, reminders and specialist delivery, added so that adherence and administration cannot explain the difference.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A requirement that the intervention actually be received as specified.
At [the trial site](place_the_trial_site.md), scheduled visits, reminders, and staff whose role is the study.

## Direction

Toward a faithfully delivered intervention.
Wandering adherence is one more thing that could have caused the result, so it must be removed, and removing it means a level of contact and prompting that no ordinary service provides.
So the trial measures the drug taken as intended, and the clinic will later administer the drug taken as people actually take it.

## Lever

The integrity of the comparison, which is not optional: an intervention half-received answers a different question from the one asked.
Because the support exists to protect the inference, it cannot be dropped without dropping the inference.

## Echo

An effect estimated under delivery nobody will reproduce.
This is the half of the gap that survives even if every patient were identical, since what differs is not the people but the care around them.
