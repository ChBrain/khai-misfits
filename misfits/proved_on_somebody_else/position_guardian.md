# Position: Guardian

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). The seat that protects participants from a study that cannot answer, and thereby writes them out of the answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The authority to refuse a protocol.
A standard that asks whether the risk to participants is justified by what the study can deliver.
No means of enlarging a study's power other than narrowing what it must account for.

## Orders

Ask whether the study is powered for its question.
Refuse the broad entry that would leave it unpowered, since exposing people for no answer is not a weaker study but an unethical one.
Approve the narrow protocol.

## Loses

Nothing, and it produces the misfit: the safeguard and the unrepresentativeness are the same act, so the seat protects the enrolled by removing the treated from the evidence.
Because no approval both protects participants and represents the prescribed population, understanding the trade better does not open a third option.
It ends correct on the only question it was given and having decided a different one by implication.

## Drives

Toward a study that does not use people for nothing,
right in every single instance to insist on it,
so the seat that exists to protect participants is where the exclusion is actually authored.
