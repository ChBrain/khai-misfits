## Taxonomy

## Load Bearing

Without it there is nothing to apply and no misfit, only a weak study.
It is load bearing because it is **genuinely good**: the estimate is unbiased for the people studied, the inference is sound, and the paper reports its own criteria, so the failure downstream cannot be pinned on overstatement, sloppiness or spin at any point.

## Apparent

Exactly what it is, a solid finding.
Nothing about it needs discounting, which is why the gap is so hard to see: there is no error in the result to point at.
