# Plan: Remove Everything That Could Explain It

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the prover's seat](position_prover.md), inside [Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Prover](position_prover.md)

## Direction

Hold every rival cause still so the one under test is the only thing left that could have produced the difference. Exclude what would confound, support what would wander, and report the criteria beside the result.

## Orders

([The Trialist](persona_the_trialist.md) writes eligibility criteria against comorbidity, interacting medication, extremes of age and inadequate organ function, and runs at sites with monitoring and adherence support.)

## Implementation

([The Trial Site](place_the_trial_site.md), where the conditions can be held; the [narrowing](process_narrowing.md) and [supporting](process_supporting.md) processes; the criteria recorded as [the criteria](piece_the_criteria.md), producing [the clean result](piece_the_clean_result.md) and, with it, [the distance](piece_the_distance.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] every rival cause of the outcome is excluded or held constant
- [ ] each exclusion carries a written and defensible justification
- [ ] adherence and delivery are supported so they cannot explain the difference
- [ ] the effect, when it appears, has nothing else it could be
