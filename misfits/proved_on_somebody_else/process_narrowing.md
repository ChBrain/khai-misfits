# Process: Narrowing

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). Writing the eligibility criteria, each exclusion carrying a real reason, most of them protective.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A causal question and a duty to answer it without confounding.
At [the trial site](place_the_trial_site.md), the screening criteria drafted and approved.

## Direction

Toward a population in which the effect can be seen.
Every exclusion removes a source of variation that would otherwise compete to explain the outcome, and the same exclusions remove the people whose lives contain that variation.
This is where the misfit is made, and nothing about it is a shortcut: the criteria are published, justified, and reviewed by a body whose function is to protect the people they exclude.

## Lever

Statistical power, which heterogeneity spends, and the ethical requirement that follows from it: a study that exposes participants and answers nothing is not weaker but wrong.
Because the lever is an obligation rather than a preference, it cannot be relaxed by caring more about representativeness.

## Echo

A study that can answer its question.
Read from the protocol it is a careful design; read from the clinic it is a list of the people the answer will not be about.
