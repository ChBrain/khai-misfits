# Place: The Label

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). Where the finding is written down as an instruction, and where the inclusion list is not.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An indication, a dose, contraindications and warnings.
A clinical category named in general terms, because that is what an instruction has to be.

## Holds

The finding's whole public form, and the point at which the population it was established on stops travelling with it.
A label is an instruction rather than a bibliography, and no version of it could carry the trial's screening log without ceasing to be usable.

## Offers

Actionable guidance to a clinician with a patient present, immediately and without research training.

## Withheld

The identity of the people studied.
The gap does not survive into the document that carries the answer, so a clinician who wants it must go and find the paper, and finding it changes nothing about the decision they have to make today.
