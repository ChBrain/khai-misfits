# Plot: You Cannot Consent Somebody to a Question You Cannot Answer

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The protocol before an ethics committee, with a proposal to broaden the entry so the study looks more like a clinic.
[The Power](piece_the_power.md), and a sample size that will not survive the broadening.

## Action

[The Committee](persona_the_committee.md), holding [the guardian's seat](position_guardian.md), tests the study for whether it can answer its question at the risk it asks of participants, and declines the broad version.

## Stage

[The Committee](persona_the_committee.md), on [Do Not Enrol Who Cannot Be Answered For](plan_do_not_enrol_who_cannot_be_answered_for.md), refuses on the participants' behalf: exposing people to risk and inconvenience in a study that settles nothing is not a weaker study, it is an unethical one.
[The Trialist](persona_the_trialist.md) accepts the narrowing, which they would have proposed anyway.

## Tension

This is where the misfit is manufactured, by the body that exists to prevent harm.
**The safeguard and the unrepresentativeness are one act.** Protecting the enrolled is what removes the treated from the evidence, and no version of the approval does both, so the choice cannot be argued out of anybody who understands it. And the refusal is correct. A committee that waved the broad protocol through would have exposed more people to reach a conclusion about none of them, which is the failure it was constituted to stop.
