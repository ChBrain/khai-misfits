# Position: Prescriber

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). The seat holding a patient the evidence excluded and no better-evidenced alternative than the one it must use.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A person needing treatment now.
Complete awareness that the trial population was narrower than this clinic.
No second trial, and no version of declining to extrapolate that produces an answer rather than an untreated patient.

## Orders

Read the label and the literature.
Note that this patient would not have been enrolled.
Prescribe, because the alternative arm is not better evidence but no treatment.

## Loses

Any evidenced option: the choice is between extrapolating and withholding, and withholding is not the cautious arm, it is an untested decision with its own harm.
Because knowing the gap does not close it, sophistication here converts into an accurate description of a bind rather than a way out of it.
It ends having made the best available decision on a finding that was never about this person.

## Drives

Toward treating the person who is here,
right in every single instance to treat them,
so the seat that can see the gap most clearly is the one with least room to act on seeing it.
