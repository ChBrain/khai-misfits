# Process: Prescribing

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). The drug given to whoever needs it, including everybody the protocol screened out.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A patient present, a condition to treat, and a licence.
At [the clinic](place_the_clinic.md), the prescription written.

## Direction

Toward treatment today.
The clinician knows the trial was narrower than the clinic and prescribes regardless, because declining to extrapolate does not produce a better-evidenced option, it produces an untreated patient with an untested decision behind them.
And the direction of the mismatch is systematic rather than random: comorbidity, polypharmacy and age exclude from studies and indicate for treatment, so the further from the evidence, the likelier the prescription.

## Lever

The absence of any alternative arm with better evidence behind it.
Because the choice is between extrapolating and withholding, awareness of the gap converts into an accurate description of a bind rather than a way out.

## Echo

A correct decision resting on a finding about somebody else.
Nothing in the encounter is careless, and nothing in it repairs [the distance](piece_the_distance.md).
