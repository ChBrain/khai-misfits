# Plot: The Label Does Not Say Who

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Licensing, and a label: an indication, a dose, contraindications, warnings.
No inclusion list, because a label is an instruction rather than a bibliography.

## Action

[Licensing](process_licensing.md) releases the finding as a usable instruction, and [prescribing](process_prescribing.md) carries it to [the clinic](place_the_clinic.md), where [The Excluded](persona_the_excluded.md) holds [the outside seat](position_outside.md).

## Stage

[The Clinician](persona_the_clinician.md), on [Treat the Person in Front of Me](plan_treat_the_person_in_front_of_me.md), reads the label, notes that the woman of eighty-six on four other medicines would not have been enrolled, and prescribes.
[The Excluded](persona_the_excluded.md), completing [Be Protected Out of the Evidence](plan_be_protected_out_of_the_evidence.md), receives the drug on the licence the study produced.

## Tension

The inversion closes, and it runs one way.
Comorbidity, polypharmacy and age are the commonest grounds for exclusion from a study and the commonest grounds for being prescribed something, so **the people held furthest from the evidence are the people it is most often used on**, systematically rather than by chance. Nobody arranged that. And the clinician's awareness buys nothing: declining to extrapolate is not the cautious arm, it is an untested decision with its own harm, so knowing the trial was narrow produces an accurate description of a bind and not a way out of it.
