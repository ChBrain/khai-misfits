# Process: Demonstrating

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). The effect appearing cleanly, beyond reasonable doubt, in the conditions built to let it appear.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A completed trial in a controlled population.
At [the trial site](place_the_trial_site.md), the analysis run and the result read.

## Direction

Toward a finding that has nothing else it could be.
This step succeeds, and it is staged because it succeeds: the estimate is unbiased for the population studied, the confidence interval is narrow, and the inference is sound.
Nothing downstream can be blamed on a failure here, which is what puts the misfit outside anybody's competence as well as outside anybody's conduct.

## Lever

The control established upstream, which is what makes the result decisive.
Because the quality of the answer rests on the narrowness that produced it, the two cannot be separated at this or any later stage.

## Echo

A true result about a defined population.
It is not overstated by anybody: the paper reports the criteria, and the estimate is correct for the people it was measured in.
