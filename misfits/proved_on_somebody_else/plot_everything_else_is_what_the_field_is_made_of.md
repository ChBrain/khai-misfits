# Plot: Everything Else Is What the Field Is Made Of

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A treatment to be tested, and a question answerable only by holding every other cause still.
[The Criteria](piece_the_criteria.md), blank, and a protocol to write.

## Action

[Narrowing](process_narrowing.md): [The Trialist](persona_the_trialist.md), holding [the prover's seat](position_prover.md), excludes severe comorbidity, interacting medication, extremes of age and inadequate organ function, each with a written justification.

## Stage

[The Trialist](persona_the_trialist.md), on [Remove Everything That Could Explain It](plan_remove_everything_that_could_explain_it.md), does exactly what showing a cause requires, and can name every exclusion and its reason at any point.
Nothing is hidden: the criteria will be published beside the result, and the paper will say plainly who was not in it.

## Tension

The list of confounders and the description of ordinary life are the same list.
There is no error here to find. Three conditions at once is a source of variation that would compete to explain the outcome, and it is also what being eighty-six is like. So the protocol is not choosing rigour over relevance, it is discovering that they are one property: **every source of variation removed to make the effect visible is a feature of the setting the effect will be used in**, and a better trialist produces a cleaner answer about a smaller slice of the world.
