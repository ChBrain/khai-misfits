# Persona: The Regulator

## Taxonomy

[Approver](position_approver.md). Requires a clean demonstration before licensing, because approving on a muddy signal exposes everybody who will later take the drug.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sceptical, procedural, and correctly demanding.
Wants an effect shown beyond reasonable doubt before a product reaches the market.
Nothing captured in it: the standard exists because the alternative harmed people.

## Action

Sets an evidentiary bar that a contaminated result cannot clear.
Licenses on the clean demonstration, and writes an indication.
Does not, and cannot practically, restrict the licence to the exact population enrolled, because the indication is a clinical category rather than a roster.

## Shadow

Not lax, and pushing in the same direction as everyone else: the bar it holds is what makes the explanatory design necessary, so the demand for certainty is the demand that produced the narrow study.
Because approving on a weak signal is the failure it was built to prevent, it cannot lower the bar to buy representativeness, and raising it buys less.
It ends having protected the public by requiring evidence gathered on a slice of it.

## Tell

Says: show me the effect without the confounding.
The correct instruction, and the one that guarantees the trial will not look like a clinic.
