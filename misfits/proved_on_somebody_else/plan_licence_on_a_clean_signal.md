# Plan: Licence on a Clean Signal

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the approver's seat](position_approver.md), inside [Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Approver](position_approver.md)

## Direction

Require an effect demonstrated without confounding before anything reaches the market, because licensing on a muddy signal exposes everyone who will later take it. Write an indication a clinician can act on.

## Orders

([The Regulator](persona_the_regulator.md) holds a bar that a contaminated result cannot clear, licenses on the clean demonstration, and issues an indication stated as a clinical category.)

## Implementation

([The Label](place_the_label.md), where the indication is written; the [licensing](process_licensing.md) process; the bar that makes [the clean result](piece_the_clean_result.md) necessary and therefore makes [the criteria](piece_the_criteria.md) necessary too.)

## Targets

- [ ] no product is licensed on evidence that could be explained another way
- [ ] the standard is held for the protection of everyone who will later take the drug
- [ ] the licence issues on the clean demonstration
- [ ] the indication names a clinical category, not the roster of people enrolled
