# Position: Prover

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). The seat that must remove every rival cause to show one, and narrows the finding's reach with every removal.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A question that can only be answered by holding other causes still.
The methods to do it: eligibility criteria, controlled delivery, monitored adherence, expert sites.
Full knowledge that each of these makes the study less like the world, and no method that avoids it.

## Orders

Exclude what would confound.
Support what would wander.
Report the effect, and report the criteria beside it.

## Loses

Generalisability, in exact proportion to the rigour bought: every source of variation removed to make the effect visible is a feature of the setting the effect will be used in.
Because the two move together and not against each other, no amount of skill separates them, and the better the design the narrower the claim it earns.
It ends with a result it can defend completely and apply to a slice.

## Drives

Toward an effect shown beyond doubt,
right in every single instance to remove what would confound,
so the seat that produces the certainty is the seat that spends the relevance to buy it.
