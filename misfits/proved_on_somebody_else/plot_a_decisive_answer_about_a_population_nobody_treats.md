# Plot: A Decisive Answer About a Population Nobody Treats

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

The trial running at expert centres, with monitoring, reminders and adherence support, because wandering adherence is one more thing that could explain the difference.
Selected patients, seen more often than ordinary care sees anyone.

## Action

[Supporting](process_supporting.md) protects the delivery, then [demonstrating](process_demonstrating.md) returns the estimate: the effect appears cleanly, the interval is narrow, and the inference is sound.

## Stage

[The Trialist](persona_the_trialist.md) completes the study and reports the criteria beside the result, as required and as intended.
[The Regulator](persona_the_regulator.md), holding [the approver's seat](position_approver.md) and working to [Licence on a Clean Signal](plan_licence_on_a_clean_signal.md), finds a demonstration that clears the bar.

## Tension

The result is genuinely good, and that is what makes the gap invisible.
There is no overstatement to discount, no spin to strip out, no methodological weakness to point at: the estimate is unbiased for the population measured and the paper says who that was. And half of what separates this from a clinic is not the patients at all. It is [the support](piece_the_support.md), the monitoring and prompting that made adherence uniform, **so the gap survives even if every patient were identical**, since what differs is the care around them as much as the people in it.
