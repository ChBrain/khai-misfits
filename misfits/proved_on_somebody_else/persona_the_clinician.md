# Persona: The Clinician

## Taxonomy

[Prescriber](position_prescriber.md). Has a person who needs treating today and the only evidence that exists, which was gathered on somebody else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Experienced, appropriately sceptical, and aware of the gap.
Can name the trial, its inclusion criteria and its limits without looking them up.
No naivety in it: the awareness is complete and changes nothing.

## Action

Reads the label and the literature.
Notes that the patient in front of them would not have been enrolled.
Prescribes anyway, because the alternative is not a better-evidenced treatment but no treatment, and the condition is present now.

## Shadow

Not credulous, and left with a choice that has no evidenced arm: knowing the trial was narrow does not produce a wider trial, and declining to extrapolate does not produce an answer, it produces an untreated patient.
So the sophistication that lets them see the problem is exactly the sophistication that shows them there is nothing else to do.
It ends having made the best available decision on evidence that was never about this person.

## Tell

Says: the trial excluded people like her, and I am going to prescribe it.
Both halves are correct, and the second does not follow from ignorance of the first.
