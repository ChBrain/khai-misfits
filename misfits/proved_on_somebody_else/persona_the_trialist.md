# Persona: The Trialist

## Taxonomy

[Prover](position_prover.md). Removes every other possible cause, because that is what showing a cause requires, and every removal narrows who the finding is about.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Rigorous, careful, and not at all naive about what the design costs.
Knows the trial population is narrower than the treated one and can say so at any point in the process.
Nothing evasive in it: the criteria are published and the justifications are written down.

## Action

Writes eligibility criteria that exclude severe comorbidity, interacting medication, extremes of age and inadequate organ function.
Runs at centres with monitoring and adherence support, because wandering adherence is one more thing that could explain the difference.
Reports the result, and reports the criteria alongside it.

## Shadow

Not blinkered, and structurally unable to have both: every source of variation removed to make the effect visible is a feature of the setting the effect will be used in.
So the design's quality and its narrowness are the same property, and a better trialist produces a cleaner result about a smaller slice of the world.
It ends holding a finding it can defend entirely and cannot generalise.

## Tell

Says: we excluded severe renal impairment, and here is why.
Every word of that is true and correct, and it is also a description of who the answer is not about.
