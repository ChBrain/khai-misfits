# Piece: The Power

## Taxonomy

[Proved on Somebody Else](play_proved_on_somebody_else.md). What heterogeneity spends, and therefore the reason the exclusion is an ethical requirement rather than a preference.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Behind [narrowing](process_narrowing.md), and in front of [the committee](persona_the_committee.md) at the moment of approval.
Answered nowhere else: only more participants or less variation buys it, and one of those is affordable.

## Load Bearing

Without it the exclusions look like fastidiousness and the play collapses into a complaint about lazy design.
It is load bearing because it converts the narrowing into an obligation: a study that exposes people to risk and cannot answer its question is not a weaker study, it is an unethical one, so **the protective act and the unrepresentative act are the same act**.

## Apparent

A statistical technicality, a sample-size calculation in an appendix.
It presents as arithmetic and functions as ethics, which is why the exclusion cannot be argued away by anybody who cares more about the excluded.

## Yearbook

Spent by variation, defended by [the criteria](piece_the_criteria.md).
It is the reason [the committee](persona_the_committee.md) refuses the broad entry, and the reason [the absent trial](piece_the_absent_trial.md) needs far more people than anyone will fund.
