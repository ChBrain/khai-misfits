# Piece: The Overlap

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). The range where the buyer's value exceeds the seller's cost, the real gain a trade would split.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Sitting on [The Table](place_the_table.md), between the two private values and belonging to neither: the span from the seller's floor to the buyer's ceiling, every price in it leaving both better off than no deal, the gain that makes this the easy case.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no loss to mourn: if the buyer's value fell below the seller's cost, no trade should happen and none failing to happen is a tragedy.
It is the overlap that makes the miss a loss. The gain is genuinely there, so a deal that dies leaves real value on the table and not merely a bargain that was never worth striking, and the whole point is that the surplus was real and went unclaimed.

## Apparent

The obvious deal: a buyer who would pay more than a seller would take, a gain plainly worth splitting, a price that surely exists somewhere in the middle.

## Yearbook

Shown at [The Table](place_the_table.md) in [A Deal Both Would Take](plot_a_deal_both_would_take.md), the gain both parties come to claim.
It is what [The Rent](piece_the_rent.md) forecloses and what [The Unmade Trade](piece_the_unmade_trade.md) is the loss of, the surplus that was really there and still went to no one.
