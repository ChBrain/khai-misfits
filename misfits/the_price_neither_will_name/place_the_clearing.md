# Place: The Clearing

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). Where a rule is run over the two reports to try to match them into a trade. Hosts [No Rule Clears It](plot_no_rule_clears_it.md) and [The Deal Dies](plot_the_deal_dies.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A mechanism at work: sealed figures, a rule that should read them and name a price, the orderly machinery of a market meant to clear.

## Holds

[The Rent](piece_the_rent.md) the rule must pay for the truth, and [The Unmade Trade](piece_the_unmade_trade.md) left when the rule cannot pay it and balance too.

## Offers

To the broker, the revelation principle and a free hand to design: a sound way to build a rule for honest reports.

## Withheld

Any rule that is efficient, voluntary, truthful, and self-funding all at once, since the machinery that clears the trade must either run a deficit or let the edge trades fall.
