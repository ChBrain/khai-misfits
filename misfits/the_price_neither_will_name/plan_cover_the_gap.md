# Plan: Cover the Gap

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the sponsor's seat](position_sponsor.md), inside [The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Sponsor](position_sponsor.md)

## Direction

Pay the shortfall from a purse outside the gain, to clear a trade the balanced rule forecloses, or decline it and let the trade fall. An outside dollar can pay each side its rent without drawing on the gain, so efficiency is reachable the moment budget balance is given up; the reckoning is whether the lost trade is worth more than the subsidy, and the knowledge that where information is one-sided or a value is known or the gain is guaranteed, no dollar is needed at all.

## Orders

([The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md) weighs the outside dollar through [Underwriting](process_underwriting.md) at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) and paying each rent from outside the gain, or holding back.)

## Implementation

([The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md) is where the shortfall is reckoned; [Underwriting](process_underwriting.md) pays each rent from a purse that is not the gain and clears the trade, at the cost of a book that no longer balances, the deficit the standing price of efficiency where no easier structure is at hand.)
