# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). The condition the impossibility needs and the edges where it lifts, the hedge that keeps it real but bounded.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held at [The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), where the sponsor reckons what would clear the trade: the outside dollar that pays each rent from a purse that is not the gain, and the starting structures under which no dollar is needed at all.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overreaches into a claim that bargaining is always doomed, which is false: the boundary is what keeps the trap honest.
It carries the condition and the escape. The impossibility needs two-sided private value over overlapping ranges; give it one-sided information, a known value, or gains guaranteed over disjoint ranges and the trade clears on its own, and even in the hard case an outside subsidy (Vickrey; Clarke; Groves) or an equal ex-ante ownership (Cramton, Gibbons, and Klemperer) restores the efficient trade, each at its own cost.
