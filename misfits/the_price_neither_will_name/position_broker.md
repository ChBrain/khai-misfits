# Position: Broker

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). The seat of the one who runs a rule to clear the trade, and who discovers that no rule can be efficient, voluntary, truthful, and self-funding at once.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A mechanism, and the standing to set the terms both parties trade under.
The revelation principle as a tool: the knowledge that any rule the parties would game reduces to a truthful one, so it may design for honest reports.
No rule that holds all four properties, because to buy the truth it must pay a rent, and on the marginal trade the rents exceed the gain.

## Orders

Find a rule that clears every trade the values warrant, that both parties join willingly, that makes honest reporting their best reply, and that pays for itself.
Design for truth, since the revelation principle says you may, and then see what the truth costs.
Accept what the designing shows: that the four cannot be had together, and that your rule is not broken but bound.

## Loses

The fourth property, always, whichever three it keeps.
Because it cannot make truth free, it must concede each side a rent, and the rents on the marginal trade exceed the gain, so a self-funding rule must let those trades fall and an efficient rule must run a deficit: what it loses is the belief that a clever enough mechanism escapes the wall.
It keeps its craft and loses the wall, proving by its own soundness that the loss is structural and not a failure of design.

## Drives

Toward the honest designer who meets the impossibility head on,
running rule after rule and finding each gives up one of the four, and by proving it names the trap exactly,
so the broker who tried hardest to clear the trade is the one who shows most clearly it cannot be cleared for free, the wall found by the hand that pushed on it.
