# Plot: A Deal Both Would Take

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Table](place_the_table.md), where a buyer and a seller sit over a thing worth trading, each willing and a gain plainly between them.

## Action

[The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md), from [the buyer's seat](position_buyer.md), and [The Seller](persona_the_seller.md), from [the seller's seat](position_seller.md), meet over [The Overlap](piece_the_overlap.md): the buyer would pay as much as ten, the seller would take as little as six, so a surplus of four sits between them that any price from six to ten would split. The scene closes with the gain real and unclaimed, a deal that plainly should be made.

## Stage

This is the easy case, the one where a price surely exists: every number in the overlap leaves both better off than no deal at all, so nothing about the values themselves stands in the way of a trade both parties want.

## Tension

The gain both can feel is a gain neither can point to, because each holds [The Private Value](piece_the_private_value.md) that fixes the overlap's edges, and neither will show it, so the very surplus that makes the deal easy is hidden from the two who would split it.
