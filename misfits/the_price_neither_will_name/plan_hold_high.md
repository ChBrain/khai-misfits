# Plan: Hold High

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the seller's seat](position_seller.md), inside [The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Seller](position_seller.md)

## Direction

State a figure above what you would truly take, to keep more of the gain for yourself. The buyer cannot see your floor, only guess at it, so every unit you seem to need is a unit of the surplus you hold, and opening over your true cost is the plain best reply to a buyer who is offering under its ceiling. Do not name your cost, because naming it sets the price against you and hands the whole gain across the table.

## Orders

([The Seller](persona_the_seller.md) lays [the Shade](piece_the_shade.md) over [the Private Value](piece_the_private_value.md) it holds, through [Shading](process_shading.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md), asking nine where it would have taken six.)

## Implementation

([The Table](place_the_table.md) is where the ask is set; [Shading](process_shading.md) sets it over the true floor as the sound best reply, invisible as anything but an opening position, so that only when it meets the buyer's own offered-low figure does the gap between them foreclose a trade the true values warranted.)

## Targets

- [ ] the ask is set above the true floor, by the ordinary best reply
- [ ] the true cost is not named, since naming it hands the gain away
- [ ] the move is sound and honest, a position and not a lie
- [ ] met by the buyer offering low, the two figures need not cross and the deal may fall
