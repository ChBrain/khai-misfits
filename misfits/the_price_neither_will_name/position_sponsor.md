# Position: Sponsor

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). The seat of the one outside the trade who could cover its shortfall, and who must choose between a balanced book and a cleared trade.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An outside purse: money that is not the gain and can pay each side its rent.
The power to break budget balance, and so to buy the efficient trade the parties alone cannot reach.
No way to both balance the books and clear every warranted trade, because the only rule that clears them all runs a deficit someone must fund.

## Orders

Weigh what efficiency costs, and pay it or decline it.
Cover the gap where a lost trade is worth more than the subsidy it takes, and let the trade fall where it is not, and know that neither choice is free.
Accept what the weighing shows: that the deal both parties would have gained from can be had, but only from a purse outside their gain.

## Loses

The balance of the books, if it clears the trade; the trade, if it keeps the balance.
Because the efficient rule must run a deficit, funding it spends real money on a gain that goes to others, so what it loses by clearing the trade is the balance, and what it loses by keeping the balance is the trade.
It keeps whichever it chooses and loses the other, the costless correction the one thing it cannot have.

## Drives

Toward the reckoner who names the price of efficiency out loud,
paying the rent from outside where the gain is worth it and holding back where it is not, and by choosing shows the remedy is real and never free,
so the sponsor who could save the deal is the one who proves saving it has a cost, and that the balanced book and the cleared trade cannot both be kept.
