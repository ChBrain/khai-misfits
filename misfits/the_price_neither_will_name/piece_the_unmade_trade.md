# Piece: The Unmade Trade

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). The efficient trade that does not happen, the surplus lost in the gap between the two shaded figures.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Left at [The Clearing](place_the_clearing.md), where the rule was run and the trade did not clear: the deal that should have been struck and was not, the gain that was really there and went to no one, the thing the whole production is the shape of.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no misfit, only a market clearing as markets do: it is the trade foreclosed that makes the structure a trap and not a success.
It carries the phenomenon whole. The buyer offered low, the seller held high, the broker ran a sound rule, and the trade both would have gained from still did not happen, so the loss is real and authored by no one, a gain that no rule could buy into being for less than it was worth.

## Apparent

Two parties who somehow could not agree: from outside it looks like ordinary stubbornness, a deal that fell through, nobody's fault and nothing structural.
