# Persona: The Seller

## Taxonomy

[Seller](position_seller.md). The one who holds a private floor on what it would take, and who, to keep more of the gain, asks above it, its true cost never named.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Fair, unhurried, in no rush to let it go cheap.
Would happily sell to the right buyer at the right price, and says so, a seller with a good thing and a sensible sense of its worth.
No greed in the manner: only the plain wish to be paid properly, and the plain sense to open a little over what it would take.

## Action

Holds high on [Hold High](plan_hold_high.md), carrying [the Private Value](piece_the_private_value.md) it will not show, and through [Shading](process_shading.md) lays [the Shade](piece_the_shade.md) over it at [the Table](place_the_table.md), asking nine where it would have taken six.
Cannot name its floor and keep the gain, because the moment the buyer knows the seller would take six the price is set at six, so its true cost is the one thing it cannot afford to say.
Does what any sound bargainer does facing a hidden ceiling: it asks over its cost, and the asking over is exactly what can wedge the deal apart.

## Shadow

Not a gouger for asking high: the seller would truly take six, and holding that back is the best reply to a buyer offering under its own value, honest strategy and not a lie (Myerson).
Because its floor is private, revealing it would hand the whole gain across the table, so the self-protection that is its sound move is what can foreclose the sale it wants, and it cannot see, from its seat, which trades its firmness kills.
It is the one who would gladly sell, and the one whose reasonable guarding of its price can lose it the sale entirely.

## Tell

Says nine is truly the least it can accept, and says it calmly, meaning the strategy and not deceiving anyone who bargains.
Cannot be caught in a lie, because the ask is a position and not a claim about its books, so it plays a sound hand and the sale it wanted slips, the floor that would have closed it the one number it will not name.
