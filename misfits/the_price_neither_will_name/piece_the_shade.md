# Piece: The Shade

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). The gap between a party's true value and the figure it states, the buyer below its value and the seller above its cost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Laid over each private value at [The Table](place_the_table.md): the buyer's offer set under the price it would pay, the seller's ask set over the cost it would take, each a deliberate distance from the truth and each looking like a plain opening position.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the two would name their true numbers, the overlap would show, and the deal would clear: the shade is what hides the gain from both.
It bears the load precisely because it is not a lie. Under any rule that asks a party to report, stating below your value or above your cost is the best reply, since naming the true number hands the gain to the other side, so the shade is sound play and cannot be scolded away (Myerson).

## Apparent

Ordinary bargaining: everyone opens a little low or a little high, and no one faults a buyer for offering under or a seller for asking over.
