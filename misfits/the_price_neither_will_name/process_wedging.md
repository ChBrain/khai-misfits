# Process: Wedging

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). Borne at [The Clearing](place_the_clearing.md), precipitated by [The Rent](piece_the_rent.md) driving the shaded figures apart, in [The Deal Dies](plot_the_deal_dies.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The rent coming, on the trades near the edge of the overlap, to more than the gain those trades hold.

## Direction

From a real overlap toward a gap: the two shaded figures held apart by the rent so they do not cross, the marginal trades let fall.

## Lever

That the rent is taken out of the gain, not added to it, so a rule that will not run a deficit must forgo exactly the trades whose gain the rent would swallow (Myerson and Satterthwaite).

## Echo

[The Unmade Trade](piece_the_unmade_trade.md), the surplus foreclosed and the deal left unstruck, with no one having lied.
