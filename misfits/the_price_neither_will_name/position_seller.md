# Position: Seller

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). The seat of the one who holds a private floor on what it would take, and who, to keep more of the gain, states a figure above it, the true cost withheld.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A floor known only to itself: the least it would accept, real and unprovable to the other side.
A sound reason to state more than it, since every unit it can seem to need is a unit of the gain it keeps.
No way to reveal its floor and still hold the gain, because the moment the buyer knows the seller would take six, the price is set at six and the gain is the buyer's.

## Orders

Hold high, and keep your floor back.
State a figure over what you would truly take, because that is the best reply to a buyer who is offering low, and do not name your cost, since naming it hands the gain across the table.
Accept what the holding does: that guarding your number is sound, and that the sound move, met by the buyer's, is what can leave the deal unmade.

## Loses

The deal, sometimes, and never knowably which times.
Because it cannot both reveal its cost and keep the gain, it must hold the truth back, and the holding that protects its share is the same act that can foreclose the trade, so what it loses is a sale it would have made, lost by playing its hand well.
It keeps its whole floor secret and loses the certainty of the sale, the gain it guarded guarding the gain away.

## Drives

Toward the side that would let the thing go and most must overstate to let it go dear,
holding high because high is the best reply, and by holding high helping wedge the two figures apart,
so the seller who would have taken six is the seller who asks nine and means it as strategy, the floor that would have closed the deal the one number it cannot afford to name.
