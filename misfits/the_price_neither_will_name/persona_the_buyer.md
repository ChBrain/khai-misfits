# Persona: The Buyer

## Taxonomy

[Buyer](position_buyer.md). The one who holds a private ceiling on what it would pay, and who, to keep more of the gain, offers below it, its true value never named.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Keen, reasonable, good for the money.
Wants the thing and would pay well for it, and says so warmly, a buyer any seller would be glad to have found.
No hard bargaining in the manner: only the plain wish to make a fair deal, and the plain sense to open a little under what it would go to.

## Action

Offers low on [Offer Low](plan_offer_low.md), carrying [the Private Value](piece_the_private_value.md) it will not show, and through [Shading](process_shading.md) lays [the Shade](piece_the_shade.md) over it at [the Table](place_the_table.md), stating seven where it would have paid ten.
Cannot name its ceiling and keep the gain, because the moment the seller knows the buyer would pay ten the price is set at ten, so the truth is the one thing it cannot afford to say.
Does what any sound bargainer does facing a hidden floor: it offers under its value, and the offering under is exactly what can wedge the deal apart.

## Shadow

Not a chiseller for offering low: the buyer would truly pay ten, and holding that back is the best reply to a seller holding its own number back, honest strategy and not a lie (Myerson).
Because its ceiling is private, revealing it would hand the whole gain across the table, so the very self-protection that is its sound move is what can foreclose the deal it wants, and it cannot see, from its seat, which trades its caution kills.
It is the one who most wants the thing, and the one whose reasonable guarding of its price can lose it the thing entirely.

## Tell

Says seven is truly the most it can do, and says it evenly, meaning the strategy and not deceiving anyone who bargains.
Cannot be caught in a lie, because the offer is a position and not a claim about its heart, so it plays a sound hand and the deal it wanted slips, the ceiling that would have closed it the one number it will not name.
