# Plot: The Deal Dies

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Clearing](place_the_clearing.md), where the gap holds and the rule, sound as it is, does not clear the trade.

## Action

Through [Wedging](process_wedging.md), [The Rent](piece_the_rent.md) drives the shaded figures apart so they do not cross, and [The Unmade Trade](piece_the_unmade_trade.md) is what is left: the surplus of [The Overlap](piece_the_overlap.md) lost, the deal both would have gained from unstruck. The scene closes with the gain gone to no one's pocket, the miss authored by no one.

## Stage

The buyer offered low, the seller held high, and the broker ran a sound rule, so there is no wrongdoer to point to: this is not Over a Barrel, where a sunk, specific investment leaves one side to be squeezed; nothing is sunk here and no one is held, the barrier being only the rent that two-sided private value commands.

## Tension

The deal that plainly should have been made is not, and the loss reads as mere stubbornness when it is a consequence: the rent to reveal the two true numbers is larger, at the margin, than the gain those numbers hold, so the gain cannot pay for its own revealing and dies unclaimed.
