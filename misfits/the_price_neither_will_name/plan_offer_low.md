# Plan: Offer Low

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the buyer's seat](position_buyer.md), inside [The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Buyer](position_buyer.md)

## Direction

State a figure below what you would truly pay, to keep more of the gain for yourself. The seller cannot see your ceiling, only guess at it, so every unit you seem not to value is a unit of the surplus you hold, and opening under your true price is the plain best reply to a seller who is doing the same with its floor. Do not name your value, because naming it sets the price against you and hands the whole gain across the table.

## Orders

([The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) lays [the Shade](piece_the_shade.md) over [the Private Value](piece_the_private_value.md) it holds, through [Shading](process_shading.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md), stating seven where it would have paid ten.)

## Implementation

([The Table](place_the_table.md) is where the offer is made; [Shading](process_shading.md) sets it under the true ceiling as the sound best reply, invisible as anything but an opening position, so that only when it meets the seller's own held-high figure does the gap between them foreclose a trade the true values warranted.)

## Targets

- [ ] the offer is set below the true ceiling, by the ordinary best reply
- [ ] the true value is not named, since naming it hands the gain away
- [ ] the move is sound and honest, a position and not a lie
- [ ] met by the seller holding high, the two figures need not cross and the deal may fall
