# Process: Shading

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). Borne at [The Table](place_the_table.md), precipitated by [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) and [The Seller](persona_the_seller.md) laying [The Shade](piece_the_shade.md) over [The Private Value](piece_the_private_value.md), in [Each Shades](plot_each_shades.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The wish to keep more of the gain, once each party sees the other's number is hidden.

## Direction

From a true value toward a self-serving stated figure: the buyer's offer down under its ceiling, the seller's ask up over its floor.

## Lever

That naming your true value hands the gain to the other side, so stating short of it is simply the best reply, the sound bargaining move that looks like nothing more than an opening position (Myerson).

## Echo

[The Shade](piece_the_shade.md), now on both sides, two figures that need not cross though the true values would.
