# Position: Buyer

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). The seat of the one who holds a private ceiling on what it would pay, and who, to keep more of the gain, states a figure below it, the true value withheld.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A top price known only to itself: the most it would pay, real and unprovable to the other side.
A sound reason to state less than it, since every unit it can seem not to value is a unit of the gain it keeps.
No way to reveal its ceiling and still hold the gain, because the moment the seller knows the buyer would pay ten, the price is set at ten and the gain is the seller's.

## Orders

Offer low, and hold your ceiling back.
State a figure under what you would truly pay, because that is the best reply to a seller who is doing the same, and do not name your value, since naming it is the one move that costs you the whole gain.
Accept what the offering does: that protecting your number is sound, and that the sound move, met by the seller's, is what can leave the deal unmade.

## Loses

The deal, sometimes, and never knowably which times.
Because it cannot both reveal its value and keep the gain, it must withhold the truth, and the withholding that protects its share is the same act that can foreclose the trade, so what it loses is a deal it would have taken, lost by playing its hand well.
It keeps its whole ceiling secret and loses the certainty of the trade, the gain it guarded guarding the gain away.

## Drives

Toward the side that most wants the thing and most must hide how much,
offering low because low is the best reply, and by offering low helping wedge the two figures apart,
so the buyer who would have paid ten is the buyer who says seven and means it as strategy, the ceiling that would have closed the deal the one number it cannot afford to name.
