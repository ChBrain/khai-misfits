# Persona: The Sponsor

## Taxonomy

[Sponsor](position_sponsor.md). The one outside the trade who could cover its shortfall, and who must choose between a balanced book and a cleared trade.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Steady, solvent, willing to help a good deal happen.
Sits outside the trade with a purse and no stake in the gain, and would gladly see two willing parties get their deal done, a backer glad to be useful.
No reluctance in the manner: only the calm of someone who can afford to close the gap, until the ledger is opened.

## Action

Weighs [Cover the Gap](plan_cover_the_gap.md) through [Underwriting](process_underwriting.md) at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): an outside dollar can pay each side its rent and clear the trade, and where information is one-sided or a value known or the gain guaranteed, no dollar is needed at all.
Cannot both cover the gap and keep the books balanced, because the only rule that clears every warranted trade runs a deficit, so the help it can give costs it the balance it would keep.
Does what a careful backer does: it reckons whether the lost trade is worth more than the subsidy, and pays or declines, neither choice free.

## Shadow

Not a miser for hesitating and not a fool for paying: the deficit is real and so is the gain, and weighing one against the other is the honest work of the seat, not coldness.
Because efficiency is reachable only by breaking budget balance, the remedy it holds is genuine and never costless, so the sponsor is the proof that the deal can be saved and the proof that saving it has a price, both at once.
It is the one who could close the gap, and the one who shows the gap has a cost that someone, always, must carry.

## Tell

Reaches for the purse, then sets it down to name the deficit out loud, then decides.
Cannot pretend the correction is free, because the books will not let it, so it speaks the price of efficiency plainly and chooses with open eyes, the backer who makes the cost of the cure visible.
