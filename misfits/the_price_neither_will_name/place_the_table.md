# Place: The Table

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). Where the buyer and the seller meet over a gain and trade figures, each true value kept in hand. Hosts [A Deal Both Would Take](plot_a_deal_both_would_take.md) and [Each Shades](plot_each_shades.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two willing parties across a table, a thing worth trading between them, and the easy confidence that a gain this plain will find a price.

## Holds

[The Private Value](piece_the_private_value.md) in each hand, [The Overlap](piece_the_overlap.md) between them, and [The Shade](piece_the_shade.md) each lays over its own number.

## Offers

The plainest expectation there is: that a buyer who would pay more than a seller would take will meet somewhere in the middle and split the difference.

## Withheld

Each party's true number, and so the very location of the overlap, so that the gain both can feel is a gain neither can point to.
