# Process: Clearing

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). Borne at [The Clearing](place_the_clearing.md), precipitated by [The Broker](persona_the_broker.md) running a rule on [Find the Rule](plan_find_the_rule.md), in [No Rule Clears It](plot_no_rule_clears_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The attempt to make the trade happen by a rule, once plain bargaining has stalled.

## Direction

From two private reports toward a matched trade and a settled price, by whatever mechanism the broker chooses.

## Lever

The revelation principle: any mechanism the parties would game reduces to a truthful one that asks them to report, so the broker may design for honest reports, and this sound step is exactly what forces the rule to pay the rent (Myerson).

## Echo

[The Rent](piece_the_rent.md), the slice each side must be left for its truth, and the discovery that on the marginal trade the two slices exceed the gain.
