# Plot: Cover the Gap

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), where a careful hand outside the trade weighs what clearing it would cost.

## Action

[The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md) on [Cover the Gap](plan_cover_the_gap.md) through [Underwriting](process_underwriting.md) holds [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): an outside dollar can pay each side its rent and clear the trade, but the books no longer balance, and where information is one-sided, a value is known, or the gain is guaranteed, the trap lifts with no dollar at all. The scene closes with efficiency bought only at a subsidy, and the trap shown real but bounded.

## Stage

Efficiency is reachable, and never free: a mechanism can clear every warranted trade if an outside party covers the deficit it runs (Vickrey; Clarke; Groves), and an equal ex-ante ownership can restore a balanced efficient rule (Cramton, Gibbons, and Klemperer), but an ordinary trade carries no outside purse and no chosen starting structure.

## Tension

The remedy exists and cannot be had for nothing, so the choice is real and unavoidable: break the balance of the books to clear the trade, or keep the books balanced and lose the trade the values warranted, the cure and its price arriving together.
