# Plot: Each Shades

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Table](place_the_table.md), where each party moves to claim more of the gain than an even split would give it.

## Action

Through [Shading](process_shading.md), [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) on [Offer Low](plan_offer_low.md) states seven where it would have paid ten, and [The Seller](persona_the_seller.md) on [Hold High](plan_hold_high.md) asks nine where it would have taken six, each laying [The Shade](piece_the_shade.md) over the value it holds. The scene closes with two figures on the table, seven and nine, that do not cross though ten and six would.

## Stage

Shading is the best reply and not a lie: to name your true value is to hand the whole gain across the table, so offering under and asking over is sound bargaining, a position each is entitled to take and neither can be caught in (Myerson).

## Tension

The two sound moves, taken together, put the deal out of reach: each protects its own number correctly, and the correct self-protection on both sides leaves a gap between the shaded figures where the true values would have met.
