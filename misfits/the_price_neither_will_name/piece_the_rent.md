# Piece: The Rent

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). The information rent any truthful, voluntary rule must concede each side to make revealing its value the best reply.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Weighed at [The Clearing](place_the_clearing.md), where the broker's rule is run: the slice of the gain the rule must leave the buyer to make it admit a high value, and the slice it must leave the seller to make it admit a low cost, the price of pulling a private number into the open.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the impossibility is gone: if the truth could be had for nothing, a rule could clear every warranted trade and balance its books.
It is the rent that carries the whole theorem. To make honest reporting the best reply, each side must be left a slice of the gain, and on the trades near the edge of the overlap the two slices together come to more than the gain itself, so no rule that pays itself can cover both rents, and one of efficient, voluntary, truthful, or self-funding has to give (Myerson and Satterthwaite).

## Apparent

A fee, almost, for honesty: the little that each party is allowed to keep for admitting what it knows. From outside it looks like a solvable friction, a cost to be trimmed rather than a wall.
