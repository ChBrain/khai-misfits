# Plot: No Rule Clears It

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Clearing](place_the_clearing.md), where a broker steps in to design the deal into being that plain bargaining could not reach.

## Action

[The Broker](persona_the_broker.md) on [Find the Rule](plan_find_the_rule.md) runs [Clearing](process_clearing.md), designing for honest reports by the revelation principle, and meets [The Rent](piece_the_rent.md): to make the buyer admit a high value and the seller a low cost, the rule must leave each a slice of the gain. The scene closes with the broker out of rules, since on the marginal trade the two slices together exceed the gain, so efficient, voluntary, truthful, and self-funding cannot all be kept and one must give (Myerson and Satterthwaite).

## Stage

The rule is sound and honestly run, and still it cannot hold all four: this is not a metric gamed or an incentive farmed, but a wall proven, the broker's own competence showing that no cleverness of design buys the truth for less than the truth costs.

## Tension

The harder the broker pushes, the plainer the wall: every rule it builds gives up one of the four properties, so its skill does not defeat the impossibility but demonstrates it, and there is no rule left to try that escapes the rent.
