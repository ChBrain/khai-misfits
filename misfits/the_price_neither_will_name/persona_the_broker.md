# Persona: The Broker

## Taxonomy

[Broker](position_broker.md). The one who runs a rule to clear the trade, and who finds, rule by rule, that no mechanism can be efficient, voluntary, truthful, and self-funding at once.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Methodical, unruffled, sure a good rule will do it.
Has seen deals stall on nothing but bargaining and believes a well-built mechanism can cut through: sealed figures, a fair split, the right incentives, and the trade will clear.
No doubt in the manner at the start: only the confidence of a designer with a sound tool and a clear problem.

## Action

Runs [Find the Rule](plan_find_the_rule.md) through [Clearing](process_clearing.md) at [the Clearing](place_the_clearing.md), designing for honest reports by the revelation principle, and meets [the Rent](piece_the_rent.md) it must concede each side to make the truth their best reply.
Cannot hold all four properties at once, because buying the truth costs a rent and on the marginal trade the two rents exceed the gain, so every rule it builds gives up one of efficient, voluntary, truthful, or self-funding.
Does what a good designer does: it tries the next rule, and the next, and by exhausting them proves the wall is real (Myerson and Satterthwaite).

## Shadow

Not a bungler for failing to clear the trade: its rules are sound and honestly run, and the wall it meets is a theorem and not a mistake it made.
Because the rent on the marginal trade is larger than the gain, no cleverness of design escapes it, so the broker's own competence is what proves the loss is structural, the harder it pushes the plainer the wall.
It is the one who most believed a rule would do it, and the one whose careful work shows most clearly that no rule can, for free.

## Tell

Proposes one more mechanism, then names which property it just gave up, and then, quieter, stops proposing.
Cannot fault its own craft, because the craft is sound, so it ends by conceding the wall rather than its skill, the designer who mapped the trap by walking every wall of it.
