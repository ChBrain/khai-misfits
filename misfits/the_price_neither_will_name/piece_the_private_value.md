# Piece: The Private Value

## Taxonomy

[The Price Neither Will Name](play_the_price_neither_will_name.md). Each party's true reservation, the buyer's top price and the seller's floor, known only to the one who holds it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held at [The Table](place_the_table.md), one in each hand and neither on show: the buyer's own ceiling, the seller's own floor, each a real number and each invisible to the other, who can only guess at the range it falls in (Myerson and Satterthwaite).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is nothing to hide, and no trap: if each could see the other's number, a clearing price would be plain and the deal would simply be made.
It is the two-sidedness that bears the load. Both numbers are private at once, so neither party and no rule can locate the overlap for certain, and the whole impossibility rests here: a value only its holder knows, on both sides of the table.

## Apparent

A simple fact each party has: what the thing is worth to me. From outside it looks like the most ordinary knowledge, the one thing in the deal that ought to be easy.

## Yearbook

Held by [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) and [The Seller](persona_the_seller.md) at [The Table](place_the_table.md), each its own and neither shared.
It is what [The Shade](piece_the_shade.md) covers and what [Shading](process_shading.md) moves away from, the true number under the stated one, the thing the price neither will name is the name of.
