# Persona: The Soil

## Taxonomy

[Soil](position_soil.md). The supply of everything the air does not provide.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Zinc, iron, magnesium and the rest, at whatever the ground holds and a root can lift.
A season no different from the last.

## Action

Sends up minerals at its own rate.
Does not respond to what is in the air.

## Shadow

Is not failing and is not depleted, and it still cannot keep pace.
Nothing here requires poor ground: a well-managed soil delivers exactly what it delivered before, which is the whole difficulty, since the vessel it is filling has grown and its rate has not. There is no fault to find and no restoration that would answer this.

## Tell

Does its job unchanged, into something larger.
