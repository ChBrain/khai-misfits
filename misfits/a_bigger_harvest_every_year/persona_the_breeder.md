# Persona: The Breeder

## Taxonomy

[Breeder](position_breeder.md). The one who selects for the trait that keeps people alive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Trial plots, a target, and a region whose margin against hunger is thin.
Decades of gains behind them, all real.

## Action

Selects for yield, generation after generation.
Releases the variety that carries more.

## Shadow

Is pursuing the correct trait and is compounding the dilution.
Yield is what has stood between populations and famine, so no version of this seat selects for anything else first; and selecting for more bulk from the same soil is selecting, in the same act, for a lower concentration of everything the soil supplies.

## Tell

Wins the argument they were right to have, and thins the grain by winning it.
