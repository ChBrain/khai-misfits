# Persona: The Eater

## Taxonomy

[Eater](position_eater.md). The household taking most of one nutrient from one grain.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A diet built on a staple, because that is what is grown, affordable and available.
More of it on the plate than last decade.

## Action

Eats the staple.
Takes protein and zinc in proportion to its concentration.

## Shadow

Loses exactly in proportion to the dilution and has the least room to move.
A varied diet absorbs a thinner staple without noticing, which is why the loss is invisible where anybody can afford one; and the households where a single grain carries most of the intake are the households where widening the diet is a matter of money rather than of advice.

## Tell

Is the only party who feels it, and the one with no adjustment available.
