# Plot: The Soil Does Not Keep Up

## Taxonomy

[A Bigger Harvest Every Year](play_a_bigger_harvest_every_year.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The same crop's zinc, iron and magnesium, which came from the ground.

## Action

[The Soil](persona_the_soil.md), holding [Soil](position_soil.md), supplies at whatever rate it always did.

## Stage

The minerals arrive at the old rate into a vessel that has grown.

## Tension

Nothing here requires poor ground, and no restoration answers it.
The soil is not depleted, not mismanaged and not failing: it delivers exactly what it delivered before, at a rate set by what it holds and what a root can lift, and there is no mechanism by which a richer atmosphere raises that. So the shortfall is not a degradation anybody caused and cannot be repaired by fixing the ground, because the ground is not what changed.
