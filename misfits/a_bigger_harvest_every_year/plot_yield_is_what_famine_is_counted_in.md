# Plot: Yield Is What Famine Is Counted In

## Taxonomy

[A Bigger Harvest Every Year](play_a_bigger_harvest_every_year.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A breeding programme, a national statistic and a food-security budget.

## Action

[Selecting for yield](process_selecting_for_yield.md) and [weighing the harvest](process_weighing_the_harvest.md) run, and [the tonnage](piece_the_tonnage.md) carries upward.

## Stage

Every instrument in the chain is denominated in the quantity that grew.

## Tension

Choosing that denominator is correct rather than careless, which is what makes it irreducible.
Hunger is a shortage of calories before it is a shortage of anything else, tonnes are comparable across crops and countable at national scale, and no programme can put a trait ahead of the one that has kept populations alive. So the instrument is right, is used correctly, and is silent about a ratio, and the assay that would carry the other half runs on samples and reports where no budget line reads it.
