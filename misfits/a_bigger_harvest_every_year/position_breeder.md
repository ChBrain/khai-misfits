## Taxonomy

[A Bigger Harvest Every Year](play_a_bigger_harvest_every_year.md). The seat that selects for the trait standing between a region and hunger.

## Loses

Nothing, and that is the difficulty: the trait is the right one.
The seat cannot select for something else first without giving up the thing that has kept people alive, and selecting for more bulk out of the same ground is, in the same act, selecting for a lower concentration of everything the ground supplies.

## Drives
