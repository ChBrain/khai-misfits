# A Bigger Harvest Every Year: Reference

The research warrant for A Bigger Harvest Every Year.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the shift in plant mineral concentration under elevated carbon
dioxide, and on the instrument that reports the harvest as a mass, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging nutrient dilution as **a gain and a loss that are the same matter counted twice**: carbon dioxide is
the substrate of photosynthesis, so more of it in the air means more sugar and starch per plant, and the yield
rises. Zinc, iron, magnesium and the nitrogen behind the protein come from the ground, at a rate set by what
the soil holds and what a root can lift, and nothing about a richer atmosphere raises that. So the same
milligrams of mineral end up inside more milligrams of everything else, and the grain is honestly more food and
honestly thinner food at once. The play models the documented mechanics, the carbon that arrives free from the
air, the soil that supplies at its unchanged rate, the grain in which the two descriptions are both true, the
breeding decision that compounds it and is correct, the tonnage every budget is written in, the household with
no room to eat around it, and the mineral bred back one variety at a time, as the load-bearing vectors of the
structure, not as a tale of anybody mismeasuring or selling a worse crop.

The governing law is that the extra mass **is** the dilution. There is no practice that takes the yield without
taking the fall in concentration, because they are not two consequences of one input but one quantity described
against two denominators. That is why nothing here is repaired by better husbandry, better soil or better
measurement: none of them separates a thing from itself.

**The play declines to stage the emission decision, and that refusal is what keeps it a misfit.** Who put the
carbon in the air, and whether they should have, is a different mechanism, and the house already holds it in
The Commons, in Fair Warning and in Known Too Late. This production takes an atmospheric concentration as
given, in the way a stock assessment takes a sea as given, and stages only what happens downstream of it. A
version that reached for the emitter would be describing a wrong rather than a structure, and would lose the
thing that makes this one work: that every party in the field, the laboratory and the ministry is doing the
right thing and the food still gets thinner.

What removes any possibility of blame from the breeder is that yield is the trait that has stood between
populations and famine, so no programme can put another first. And selecting for more bulk out of the same
ground is, in the same act, selecting for a lower concentration of everything the ground had to supply, which
means the correct decision compounds the mechanism rather than resisting it.

What removes it from the measurement is that tonnes are the right primary instrument. Hunger is a shortage of
calories before it is a shortage of anything else, and mass is comparable across crops, countable at national
scale and the unit a food-security budget can actually be written in. The assay that would carry the other half
is a laboratory procedure run on samples and reported in a literature no budget line reads, so the silence is
structural rather than chosen.

And the soil closes the case against a second party, because it is not failing. It delivers exactly what it
delivered before, on well-managed ground as much as on poor, which is the whole of the difficulty: the vessel
has grown and the rate has not, so there is no degradation to reverse and no restoration that answers this.

It is distinct from the house's **What the Water Leaves**, where irrigation delivers the water a crop needs and
the salt it carried stays behind until the ground will not grow anything: it is the nearest relative in the
house and the two are inverses of one shape. There the input **accumulates** something unwanted and the medium
degrades, so the damage is to the land and is visible in it. Here nothing accumulates and nothing degrades: the
soil is unchanged, the crop is healthy and larger, and what falls is a **ratio inside the product**, which
leaves no mark on the field at all.

It is distinct from the house's **Best Day for It**, where each additional unit of variable renewable
generation lowers the market value of every unit, so the quantity rises and the value factor falls: the shape
is the same and the objects are not. There the falling quantity is a **price**, set by a market that could in
principle be redesigned, and the mechanism runs through the simultaneity of output. Here the falling quantity
is a **physical concentration**, set by stoichiometry, and no market design reaches it.

It is distinct from the house's **Metric Fixation**, where a measure becomes a target and is gamed until it
stops measuring: there somebody optimises against the proxy and the remedy is to relieve the pressure or change
it. Here nobody games anything. Yield is pursued because yield is the substantively right goal, not because it
is what gets counted, and the tonnage is not a proxy for nourishment that has drifted: it is an accurate
measure of a different and genuinely important quantity, which was never claiming to be the other one.

It is distinct from the house's **Model Collapse**, where a generative system trained on its own output loses
the tails until the distribution narrows: both are a quality that erodes while the quantity holds or grows, and
the causes are unrelated. There the degradation is **recursive**, produced by feeding the output back in, and
the remedy is fresh data from outside the loop. Here there is no loop and no feedback: a single exogenous
change in one input dilutes one harvest, and the next harvest is not thinner because the last one was.

It is distinct from the house's **Missing from the Count**, where a population is measurably undercounted and
the correction is barred from being used: there the instrument is **wrong** and is known to be wrong, and the
difficulty is that the repair is forbidden. Here the instrument is right. The tonnage is accurate, nobody is
prevented from measuring composition, and the assay is performed and published: what is missing is not
permission but a place in the arithmetic anybody plans in.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the breeder as
  short-sighted or the statistician as counting the wrong thing, and **refuses outright to stage whoever put
  the carbon in the air**, because that is a different mechanism the house already holds and reaching for it
  would turn a structure into a wrong. Nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the extra growth is
  fixed carbon and is a genuine gain measured in open field trials; the soil supplies at its unchanged rate on
  good ground as much as on poor, so nothing is depleted and nothing needs restoring; the grain is honestly
  more food and honestly thinner food at once, with no measurement wrong anywhere; yield is the trait that has
  kept populations alive and cannot be put second; and tonnes are the correct primary instrument because a
  famine is a shortage of mass before it is anything else. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from
  the structure, so the misfit is that the input which makes a crop bigger is the input that dilutes it.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Breeding the mineral back
  works, is released and is adopted, and it is varietal, so it must reach each crop, then each locally adapted
  variety, then each farmer's seed, and then be done again, because the concentration keeps falling underneath
  the programme while the programme runs. Fertilising with zinc reaches some soils and is an input cost falling
  on the farmers with least of it. Widening the diet is the correct advice and is precisely what poverty
  prevents, so it is unavailable exactly where the loss lands. Measuring composition instead of mass answers a
  different question from the one a food-security budget is funded to ask, and would have to displace the
  instrument that catches famine. And knowing the mechanism completely changes nothing about what a plant does
  with a carbon atom.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the stoichiometric prediction, the ionome meta-analysis, the
  open-air crop results, the yield synthesis and the breeding programme are theirs. The bounds are carried with
  them: **the effect has been genuinely contested**, on the ground that much of the early evidence came from
  chambers and glasshouses and that many individual results are not statistically significant, which is why the
  meta-analytic and open-air evidence is what the play rests on and why it stages a direction rather than a
  figure; the **physiological mechanism is still argued over** and is not settled by anything cited here; the
  magnitudes vary by crop, by nutrient, by cultivar and by experimental design, so the eight percent is a mean
  across a heterogeneous body and not a rate anybody should carry to a particular field; the play stages **no
  projection of health outcomes at all**, since translating a concentration into a deficiency burden requires
  assumptions about diets and populations that are a separate literature with its own disputes; and treating
  the harvest as one instrument is the play's own simplification of a measurement system with several. **The
  play makes no claim that food is becoming scarce, that any breeding programme erred, or that any statistic
  was falsified**, only that the mass and the nourishment are supplied by different sources at different rates,
  and that everything anybody counts is denominated in the one that grew.
