# Persona: The Agronomist

## Taxonomy

[Agronomist](position_agronomist.md). The one who measures the harvest and reports what it was.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A weighbridge, a survey, a national statistic, a budget written in tonnes.
Every figure accurate.

## Action

Weighs it. Reports it up.
Records a good year.

## Shadow

Measures correctly, in the unit that matters most, and the unit is the half that grew.
Tonnes are comparable across crops, countable at scale and the thing a shortage is a shortage of, so denominating in them is right rather than lazy; and a composition assay is a different instrument, run on samples, reported nowhere the budget can read it.

## Tell

Files an accurate report of a good year that is also a thinner one.
