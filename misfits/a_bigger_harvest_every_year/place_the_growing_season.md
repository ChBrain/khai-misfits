# Place: The Growing Season

## Taxonomy

[A Bigger Harvest Every Year](play_a_bigger_harvest_every_year.md). Where carbon becomes bulk, and the ground supplies the rest at its own rate.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A field under an atmosphere with more carbon dioxide in it than the last generation's.
Everything working: good ground, good husbandry, a sound variety, a better yield than before.

## Holds

[The Carbon](piece_the_carbon.md), arriving free and without limit from the air.
[The Soil](persona_the_soil.md), sending up zinc and iron at whatever rate it always did.
[The Grain](piece_the_grain.md), filling with more of the first and the same of the second.

## Offers

A real gain, measured in open field trials rather than in a chamber.
More food off the same hectare, which is the outcome the whole apparatus exists to produce.

## Withheld

Any signal that the two inputs are not scaling together.
The place presents one crop growing well, and a plant that is larger is what a good season looks like from inside it: nothing in the field, the eye or the yield monitor distinguishes a heavier grain that is as rich as its parent from a heavier grain that is not, because the difference is a ratio and the place reports a quantity.
