# Plot: And the Zinc Goes Back One Variety at a Time

## Taxonomy

[A Bigger Harvest Every Year](play_a_bigger_harvest_every_year.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A released biofortified variety with the mineral bred back into it.

## Action

[Breeding the zinc back](process_breeding_the_zinc_back.md): [the fortifier](persona_the_fortifier.md), holding [Fortifier](position_fortifier.md), crosses for concentration and holds the yield.

## Stage

It works, it is released, and it is adopted.

## Tension

The remedy succeeds and is varietal, so it runs at the pace of plant breeding against something that does not pause.
It must reach each crop, then each locally adapted variety of that crop, then each farmer's seed, which is an adoption problem rather than a breeding one, and then be done again, because the concentration keeps falling underneath the programme while the programme runs. That is a real repair with a real price and a standing obligation, rather than a fix that ends the mechanism.
