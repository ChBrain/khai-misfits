# Place: The Weighbridge

## Taxonomy

[A Bigger Harvest Every Year](play_a_bigger_harvest_every_year.md). Where the harvest becomes a number, and the number is a mass.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A load weighed, recorded, aggregated into a district figure and then a national one.
No error anywhere: accurate scales, honest returns, a budget and a food-security assessment written in the result.

## Holds

[The Agronomist](persona_the_agronomist.md), reporting a good year, correctly.
[The Tonnage](piece_the_tonnage.md), comparable across crops and countable at scale.
[The Grain](piece_the_grain.md), passing over as a mass and never as a composition.

## Offers

The right unit for the question anybody is funded to ask, which is whether there is enough.
A figure that can be compared, planned against and argued over.

## Withheld

The denominator.
The place measures what there is and never what is in it, and that is not an oversight: hunger is a shortage of calories before it is a shortage of anything else, so tonnes are the correct primary instrument, and the assay that would show the other half is a laboratory procedure run on samples, reported in a literature no budget line reads.
