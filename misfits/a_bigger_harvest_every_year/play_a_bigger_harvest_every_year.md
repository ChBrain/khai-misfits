# Play: A Bigger Harvest Every Year

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

A Bigger Harvest Every Year.
The grain gets larger and there is more of it, which is the outcome every agricultural institution exists to produce and the one that has kept famine away. The zinc and the iron and the protein in it are drawn from the soil, and the soil does not send up more of them because the air has more carbon in it. So the same grain is more food and thinner food, both statements true of one object, and the harvest is weighed in tonnes because tonnes are what a famine is counted in.

## Arc

The bend is this: the bulk is carbon and the nourishment is not, so the input that makes the crop bigger is the input that dilutes it.
It runs on a gas that is plant food.
[The Carbon](piece_the_carbon.md) is fixed into sugar and starch in [the growing season](place_the_growing_season.md), and more of it in the air means more of that per plant. This is real, is measured in the field, and is why yields under raised carbon dioxide are higher.
And the soil does not keep up.
[The Soil](persona_the_soil.md) sends up zinc, iron, magnesium and the rest at a rate set by what is in it and what a root can take, and nothing about a richer atmosphere raises that. So the minerals arrive at the old rate into a larger vessel.
Then [A Bigger Grain Is Not a Richer One](plot_a_bigger_grain_is_not_a_richer_one.md).
[The Grain](piece_the_grain.md) carries near enough the same milligrams in more milligrams of everything else, so its concentration falls. Nobody has measured anything wrongly: more food and less nourishing food are the same observation of the same object.
And [Yield Is What Famine Is Counted In](plot_yield_is_what_famine_is_counted_in.md).
[The Breeder](persona_the_breeder.md) selects for yield because yield is what stands between a region and hunger, and [The Agronomist](persona_the_agronomist.md) reports [the tonnage](piece_the_tonnage.md) at [the weighbridge](place_the_weighbridge.md) because tonnes are comparable, countable and what the budget is written in. Every instrument in the chain is denominated in the quantity that grew.
And [The Eater](persona_the_eater.md) carries it. A household taking most of its protein and most of its zinc from one staple has its intake fall with the concentration, and is the household with the least room to eat around it.
And [the zinc goes back one variety at a time](plot_and_the_zinc_goes_back_one_variety_at_a_time.md). [The Fortifier](persona_the_fortifier.md) breeds it in, which works and is released and is adopted, and has to be done again for the next crop, the next region and the next decade, because the mechanism does not stop while the breeding happens.

## Triggers

**[The Carbon Is the Bulk](plot_the_carbon_is_the_bulk.md)**
Cue: a crop in a season with more carbon dioxide in the air over it. Closes when the extra growth has been located in fixed carbon, so the gain is real, is measured in open field trials, and is made of the one element that arrives from the atmosphere.

**[The Soil Does Not Keep Up](plot_the_soil_does_not_keep_up.md)**
Cue: the same crop's zinc, iron and magnesium, which came from the ground. Closes when the mineral supply has been shown to be set by the soil and the root rather than by the air, so nothing about a richer atmosphere raises it.

**[A Bigger Grain Is Not a Richer One](plot_a_bigger_grain_is_not_a_richer_one.md)**
Cue: a harvested grain, larger than its parent variety's. Closes when the concentration has been found to fall while the content holds, with no measurement wrong anywhere, so more food and thinner food are one observation.

**[Yield Is What Famine Is Counted In](plot_yield_is_what_famine_is_counted_in.md)**
Cue: a breeding programme, a national statistic and a food-security budget. Closes when every one of them has been found to be denominated in the quantity that grew, correctly, because hunger is a shortage of calories before it is anything else.

**[The Person Who Eats the Staple Carries It](plot_the_person_who_eats_the_staple_carries_it.md)**
Cue: a household taking most of its protein and zinc from one grain. Closes when the loss has been located there rather than anywhere else, because eating around a thinner staple is what a wider diet does and a wider diet is what poverty prevents.

**[And the Zinc Goes Back One Variety at a Time](plot_and_the_zinc_goes_back_one_variety_at_a_time.md)**
Cue: a released biofortified variety with the mineral bred back into it. Closes when the remedy has been shown to work, to be varietal, and to need repeating for the next crop, the next region and the next decade, since nothing stops while it is done.

## Stakes

Whether a food supply can register a loss that arrives inside a gain.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a bulk made of carbon, a mineral supply that does not scale with it, a grain that is honestly both more and thinner, an instrument denominated in the half that grew, and a remedy that must be bred in again and again, and away from any account in which somebody mismeasured, cut a corner or sold a worse crop. The production cannot fault a breeder for chasing yield, because yield is what has kept people alive, and it cannot fault a statistic for counting tonnes, because tonnes are what a famine is counted in. What is left to fight over is not whether there is more food, there is, but what to call a shortfall that every honest instrument in the chain reports as a good year.
