# Plot: The Person Who Eats the Staple Carries It

## Taxonomy

[A Bigger Harvest Every Year](play_a_bigger_harvest_every_year.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A household taking most of its protein and zinc from one grain.

## Action

[The Eater](persona_the_eater.md), holding [Eater](position_eater.md), eats what is grown, affordable and there.

## Stage

The intake falls in exact proportion to the concentration.

## Tension

The loss lands where there is least room to move, and the advice that would answer it is what poverty prevents.
A varied diet absorbs a thinner staple without registering it, so wherever anybody can afford one the mechanism is invisible. The seat that carries the whole of it is the one for which widening the diet is a question of money rather than of knowledge, so the remedy everybody would name first is the remedy this seat has already been unable to take for other reasons.
