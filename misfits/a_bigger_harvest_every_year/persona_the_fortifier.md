# Persona: The Fortifier

## Taxonomy

[Fortifier](position_fortifier.md). The breeding programme that puts the mineral back.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A target concentration, a gene pool, and years of crossing.
Released varieties, adopted in the field.

## Action

Breeds the zinc and the iron back into the crop.
Gets it into farmers' hands.

## Shadow

Works, and works one variety at a time, against something that does not pause.
The remedy is real and released and taken up, and it is varietal: it must be bred into each crop, then into each locally adapted variety of that crop, then adopted seed by seed, and then done again as the concentration keeps falling underneath it.

## Tell

Restores what was lost, at the pace of plant breeding, to whichever crop got there first.
