## Load Bearing

It is the growth and the dilution in one substance, so the two cannot be separated by any practice.
More carbon in the air means more carbohydrate per plant, which is the yield gain and is genuine. The minerals and the protein nitrogen do not arrive with it. So the extra mass **is** the thing lowering the concentration: there is no way to take the gain without taking the dilution, because they are the same matter counted twice.
