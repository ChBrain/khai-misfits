# Process: Trading

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). Shares moving between willing parties at an agreed price.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Allocating](process_allocating.md), and a right that may be sold.
At [the register](place_the_register.md), continuously, for as long as the scheme runs.

## Direction

Toward whoever will pay most, which is usually whoever can fish it most cheaply.
This is the mechanism that retires the surplus capacity the race built, and it is not a side effect:
a scheme with secure but untradable shares leaves the over-built fleet in place and forgoes most of
the gain that justified the change.
So there is nothing to switch off here without switching off the point.

## Lever

Price, and access to credit.
The question is not whether the trades are fair, which they are, but that **the two buyers a seller
cannot tell apart are the whole of the outcome**.

## Echo

Fewer vessels, fishing better,
fewer names on the register each year,
and a growing share of the fishery held by people who do not go to sea.
