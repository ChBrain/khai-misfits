# Place: The Register

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). The ledger of who holds what: the fishery's real map, kept ashore.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Names, tonnages, transfer dates, and a quoted price.
Every entry lawful, every transfer consented to, every accumulation cap satisfied on its face.
A list that gets shorter.

## Holds

The outcome, written down in advance of anybody noticing it.
This is the only place the mechanism is visible while it is running, and it is visible as **nothing
but a series of ordinary purchases**, each unremarkable, each between willing parties, and the
pattern legible only in the aggregate that nobody is charged with reading.

## Offers

Complete transparency about the thing nobody has grounds to object to.

## Withheld

The affiliation behind a name.
A cap written against a holder is satisfied by a holder, and the register records holders, so the
question the cap was written to ask is the one thing this place cannot answer.
