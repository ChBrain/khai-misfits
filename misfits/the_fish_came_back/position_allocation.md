# Position: Allocation

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). The seat where the shares are first issued: a public resource divided among the people who were fishing it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A total allowable catch, and a formula for cutting it into shares.
A catch history for every vessel in the fishery, earned in the race the scheme is about to end.
One moment in which the whole future ownership of the fishery is decided.

## Orders

Divide the catch on the record that exists.
Make each share secure enough to be worth conserving.
Make it transferable, because that is what retires the capacity the race built.

## Loses

The chance to be revisited.
This seat is occupied **once**: the initial grant is free, every subsequent one is a purchase, and so
the line between those who were present in one particular year and everybody who came after is drawn
here and cannot be redrawn without taking property from somebody.

## Drives

Toward a fishery that will stop racing,
which is what it is for and what it achieves,
so the seat that ends the ruin is the seat that fixes who is in the fishery forever.
