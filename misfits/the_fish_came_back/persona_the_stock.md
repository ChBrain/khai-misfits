# Persona: The Stock

## Taxonomy

[Recovery](position_recovery.md). The biomass on the grounds, which was going down and is now going up.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The reason for all of it, and the only party in the production that is unambiguously better off.
Counted every year, modelled, surveyed, and rebuilding on the schedule the plan predicted.
Reported as the vindication of the scheme, which it is.

## Action

Rebuilds when it is not raced for.
Raises what a share of it is worth.
Says nothing about who should hold the share.

## Shadow

That its recovery is the mechanism.
Nothing about a healthier stock is a cost to anybody, and yet **the price of entry is computed from
it**: a share is worth the fishing it permits, so every tonne of recovery is added to the wall, and
the fishery's best year is the hardest year to buy into.

## Tell

Is what everybody in the room wanted.
Is what the deckhand cannot afford.
