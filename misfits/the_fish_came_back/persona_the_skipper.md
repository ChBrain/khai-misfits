# Persona: The Skipper

## Taxonomy

[Allocation](position_allocation.md). The owner-operator who was in the fishery when the shares were issued, and holds one because of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Forty years on the water, a boat that owes nothing, and no child who wants the life.
Was given the share on the strength of a catch history that was earned in the race the share ended.
Has a pension that consists entirely of one asset.

## Action

Fishes the share while there is a reason to.
Takes the best offer when there is not.
Signs a transfer that no rule forbids and no conscience troubles.

## Shadow

That the sale is the mechanism and is also the point of having an asset.
**There is nothing to counsel here**: a person who worked a fishery for a career and is offered its
capital value has no obligation to refuse it, and any rule that stops them is a rule confiscating the
only thing the scheme gave them.

## Tell

Is the party the scheme was designed to protect.
Is the party whose last correct act removes the fishery from the town.
