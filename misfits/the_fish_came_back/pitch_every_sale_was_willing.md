# Pitch: Every Sale Was Willing

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Precise about who holds what, in a hall where half the room used to fish and does not now.
Careful never to say that anybody was cheated, because nobody was.
Quoting the price wherever the price is the point.

## Undertow

Under the recovery, one relation from economics and one from the ground.
A secure share removes the reason to race, which is the whole of why it worked.
And a secure share of a recovering fishery is a capital asset, priced on what it permits.

## Nerve

The unease of a scheme that succeeded on its own terms and closed the door behind it.
Every sale was willing: true of every transfer, said by every party to one,
and the harbour has a fishery it can see and cannot enter.

## Echo

After the run: a stock in better condition than any of them remember,
a season long enough to fish in weather a person would choose,
a register of shares with fewer names on it each year,
and a deckhand doing the arithmetic on a right that costs more than a boat.
