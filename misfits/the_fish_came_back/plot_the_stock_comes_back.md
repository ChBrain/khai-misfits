# Plot: The Stock Comes Back

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Price Follows the Fish](piece_the_price_follows_the_fish.md): a good survey, a raised total allowable catch, and a scheme proving itself.

## Action

[Rebuilding](process_rebuilding.md): [The Stock](persona_the_stock.md), in [the recovery seat](position_recovery.md), rises on [Let It Rebuild](plan_let_it_rebuild.md).

## Stage

[The Register](place_the_register.md): the quoted price of a share moves with the survey, in the same direction, for the same reason.

## Tension

Here the loop closes, out of nothing but good news.
A share is priced on the fishing it permits, so a rebuilt stock is a dearer share. And confidence that
the right will still be honoured in twenty years is itself a conservation success, and it is the
second input to the price.
**Every instrument that makes the scheme work harder makes the share more expensive.**
So the deckhand doing the arithmetic is not looking at a fishery in trouble. They are looking at one
in excellent condition and a number they cannot meet, and those are the same fact stated twice.
Waiting is no answer: a fall in the price is a fall in the stock or a doubt about the scheme, so the
moment the right becomes affordable is the moment it stops being worth having.
