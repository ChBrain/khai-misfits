# Position: Tenure

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). The seat of the harbour's hold on its own fishery: the ice plant, the gear store, the crew, the reason the town is where it is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A fleet that shrinks as the shares consolidate.
Shoreside businesses whose volume is somebody else's landing decision.
No legal interest of any kind in the shares that used to be landed here.

## Orders

Keep the fishery in the town.
Without a claim on any share.
Against buyers who are richer and are breaking no rule.

## Loses

Standing.
The community was never a party: **the scheme allocated to vessels and not to places**, so a harbour
watching its shares leave has nothing to appeal, nothing to enforce and nobody to accuse, and its
interest exists only in the sense that it is obviously real.

## Drives

Toward a reserve or a rule,
each of which is carved from the same total or forbids somebody a sale,
so the seat with the strongest case is the seat with the fewest instruments.
