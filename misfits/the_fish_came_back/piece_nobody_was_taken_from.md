# Piece: Nobody Was Taken From

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). The absence of any transaction that could be undone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the register](place_the_register.md), in every line of it.
In each transfer: a seller who wanted to sell, a buyer who paid, a price both agreed.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is about a fraud or a loophole and the remedy is enforcement.
It is load bearing because **there is no wrongful act anywhere in the sequence**: the allocation used
the only record that existed, the sales were voluntary and well priced, the purchases were lawful, and
the caps were satisfied. An investigator sent to find the moment it went wrong returns with a list of
ordinary contracts.

## Apparent

That the willingness of the sellers settles the question.
It settles the moral question about the sellers and no other. Each transfer was good for both
parties to it, and the aggregate of those transfers is a fishery the town cannot enter, which is not
a thing any of them agreed to or could have declined.

## Yearbook

The gap between the fairness of each transaction and the character of what they sum to.
The play uses it to remove blame without removing the harm.
