# Position: Transfer

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). The seat of the market in shares: buyers, sellers, brokers, lenders and a price.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Willing sellers, mostly old, mostly without successors.
Willing buyers, mostly larger, mostly able to borrow.
A price that both sides agree is fair, because both sides chose it.

## Orders

Move the share to whoever values it most.
Retire the surplus vessels the race built.
Let a fisher realise the value of a career.

## Loses

Its capacity to distinguish two buyers.
**The market cannot see who is standing behind the money**, and has no reason to: a share sold to the
skipper next door and a share sold to a fund three time zones away are the same transaction at the
same price, and the difference between them is invisible to every party at the moment it matters.

## Drives

Toward efficiency,
which is the gain the scheme was costed on,
so the seat that removes the wasted boats is the seat that removes the fishery from the harbour.
