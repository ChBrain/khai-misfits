# Play: The Fish Came Back

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Fish Came Back.
The stock was being fished down and the fleet was racing for it. A share of the catch was made into a thing a person could own, and the racing stopped, and the fish came back.
Every share that changed hands was sold by somebody who wanted to sell it, at a price they were glad of, to somebody who paid it.
And there is now no way for a deckhand in that harbour to become a skipper, because the right to fish costs more than a boat and rises with the stock.

## Arc

The bend is this: the instrument works, and the property that makes it work is the property that closes the door.

It runs on one relation from economics and one from the ground. **A common-pool resource is overfished because nobody's restraint is worth anything to them**, and giving each participant a secure share of the catch removes the reason to race: the fish left in the water are still yours next year, so leaving them there becomes the rational act. And on the ground, **a secure share of a recovering fishery is a capital asset**, priced at what the fishing it permits is worth, and it is bought and sold like one.

Take the instrument first, because the play has to establish that it **works**. The race to fish is real and it is ruinous: seasons compressed into days, gear stacked against gear, boats built for speed rather than for the sea, and a stock taken down by a fleet that would all have preferred to slow down and could not. A secure share ends it. The catch improves, the season lengthens, the deaths at sea fall, and the stock rebuilds. Nothing in the design is a wrong done to anybody, and the fishery is in better condition than the one it replaced.

Then the fact that inverts it. **The right had to be tradable to do the job, and a tradable right accumulates.** Transferability is not a decoration on the scheme: it is the part that moves the fishing to whoever can do it best and retires the surplus capacity that the race built. So shares move, willingly, at good prices, from people who are old or tired or have no successor, to people who can raise the money. Nobody is dispossessed. Nobody is even disadvantaged in the transaction. And after twenty years the shares are held by fewer people, more of them ashore, and some of them by nobody who has ever been on the water.

Then the sharper half, which is why it cannot be waited out. **The better the conservation works, the higher the wall.** A share is priced on the fishing it permits, so a rebuilt stock is a more expensive share. The recovery that vindicates the whole design is the same event that puts the right out of reach of the person who would have bought in. A young fisher in that harbour is not looking at a fishery in trouble. They are looking at a fishery in excellent condition and a price they cannot meet, and the two are the same fact.

And nobody has done anything wrong. The council designed the scheme the economics recommended. The skipper sold a career's asset at its worth, which is what an asset is for. The buyer paid the asking price. The deckhand is not owed a share by anybody, and the fish are there, and there is no way in.

Reach for a settlement and each one gives back the thing it was bought with. Cap what any one holder may own and the cap binds on exactly the consolidation that retired the surplus fleet, and is met by holding through affiliated companies. Require the owner to be aboard and you forbid the ageing skipper the sale that was their pension, and you are answered with a name on a crew list. Reserve shares for the community and you have carved them from the same total and moved the allocation argument indoors. Make the right untradable and the concentration stops with the efficiency. And do none of it and the harbour keeps its fishery on paper.

## Triggers

**[The Derby Stops](plot_the_derby_stops.md)**
The instrument established as sound rather than as the villain: a secure share removes the reason to race, the season lengthens, the gear conflict ends, the boats stop being built for speed, and the stock begins to rebuild. Closes when it is clear the scheme did what it was for and that the room would design it again.

**[A Share Becomes a Price](plot_a_share_becomes_a_price.md)**
Cue: the first transfer. Closes when the share is visible as a capital asset rather than as a permission: it is priced on the fishing it permits, it can be borrowed against, and it is now the most valuable thing in the fishery, worth more than the boat that catches with it.

**[The Stock Comes Back](plot_the_stock_comes_back.md)**
Cue: a rebuilt biomass and a raised total catch. Closes when the loop is in the open: the better the stock, the more each share permits, so the price of a share rises with the conservation success, and the wall against a new entrant is built out of the thing everybody wanted.

**[The Last Boat Sold](plot_the_last_boat_sold.md)**
Cue: a skipper with no successor and a good offer. Closes when the transfer is shown to be wholly willing on both sides and to leave the harbour one fishery poorer anyway: no clause was broken, no party was wronged, and the shares are ashore.

**[And Someone Still Has to Fish](plot_and_someone_still_has_to_fish.md)**
Cue: the remedies. Closes when each is found to hand back what it was bought with: an accumulation cap that binds on the consolidation that retired the surplus fleet and is met through affiliates, an owner-aboard rule that forbids the skipper their pension and is answered by a name on a crew list, a community reserve carved from the same total, and untradable shares that stop the concentration with the efficiency.

## Stakes

Whether a resource can be saved by making it property without the property becoming the new scarcity.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the scheme was wrong, because the racing stopped and the stock recovered and the room would build it again, and not whether anybody was cheated, because every share was sold willingly at its worth, but whether a fishery can be held by the people who fish it once the right to fish it is worth more than the fishing. The production cannot settle it. It can only show that the instrument worked, that transferability is the part that made it work, that the recovery is what raises the wall, that every transfer was between willing parties, and that each remedy returns the efficiency it was paid for.
