# Process: Leasing

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). A share rented for a season to the person who will actually catch the fish.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Capitalising](process_capitalising.md), and a price no working fisher can meet outright.
At [the harbour](place_the_harbour.md), every season, before the boat leaves.

## Direction

Toward fishing without owning, which is the one route left in.
It is a genuine accommodation and it is used: a person with no capital can fish this year by paying
the holder a rent, and many do, and the fish are landed.

## Lever

The rent, which is set by what the fishing is worth and therefore takes most of what the fishing is
worth.
The lessee carries the weather, the gear, the crew and the risk, and keeps the residue after the
holder is paid, so **the route that is open is the one that cannot accumulate**.

## Echo

Fish landed by people who own none of the fishery,
a rent leaving the harbour every season,
and a way in that is also a way of never getting in.
