# Position: Entry

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). The seat of getting in: what a person must now buy before they may fish.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A price, publicly quoted, for a right that used to be a licence and a willingness to work.
A lender who will finance a boat and is warier of financing a share.
Skill, which was the old currency here and is no longer the binding one.

## Orders

Buy in at the market price.
Or crew for somebody who did.
Or leave.

## Loses

The ladder.
The route from deckhand to skipper ran through a boat and a licence, both of which could be bought
out of a good few seasons; **it now runs through an asset priced on a fishery's whole future**, which
no amount of crewing accumulates, so the progression is not slowed but severed.

## Drives

Toward whoever has capital,
which is not a corruption of the market but the market working,
so the seat that decides who fishes next is decided somewhere other than on the water.
