# Process: Capitalising

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). The fishery's future earnings collapsing into the present price of a share.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Rebuilding](process_rebuilding.md), and a right that is secure enough to price.
At [the register](place_the_register.md), the moment the first share is quoted.

## Direction

Toward a price that equals what the fishing is worth.
This is what a functioning asset market does and there is no version of a secure tradable right that
avoids it: the rent the resource yields has to sit somewhere, and once the right is ownable it sits
in the right.

## Lever

The expected catch, and confidence that the share will still be honoured in twenty years.
**Both of those are conservation successes.** The more credible the management and the healthier the
stock, the higher the price, so every instrument that makes the scheme work harder also makes the
share dearer.

## Echo

A right worth more than the vessel that fishes it,
a fisher whose largest asset is a permission,
and an entry cost that rises every time the news is good.
