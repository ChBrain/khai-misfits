# Piece: The Price Follows the Fish

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). The loop: conservation success raising the cost of getting in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the register](place_the_register.md), every time the survey is good.
Between [rebuilding](process_rebuilding.md) and [capitalising](process_capitalising.md), with nothing in between.

## Load Bearing

Without it the barrier is a one-off consequence of the initial grant and time would erode it.
It is load bearing because **time makes it worse**: a share is priced on the fishing it permits, the
fishing improves as the stock rebuilds, and confidence that the right will be honoured rises as the
scheme proves itself, so the two things that mark the plan a success are the two inputs to the price.
The wall is built out of good news and grows with it.

## Apparent

That a young fisher should wait for a dip.
A dip in the price is a dip in the stock or a doubt about the scheme, so the moment the right is
affordable is the moment it is not worth having, and waiting is not a strategy but a description of
the trap.

## Yearbook

The capitalisation of resource rent into the price of the right that carries it.
The play uses it for one thing: that the entry cost is computed from the conservation outcome.
