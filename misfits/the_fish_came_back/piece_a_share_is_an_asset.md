# Piece: A Share Is an Asset

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). The moment a management permission becomes property.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the register](place_the_register.md), from the first transfer onward.
On a balance sheet, in a loan file, and in a will.

## Load Bearing

Without it the shares stay with the people they were issued to and the play has no motion.
It is load bearing because **security and transferability are the two properties that make the scheme
work**, and they are the same two properties that make a share ownable, financeable and worth
accumulating. The instrument cannot have one pair without the other.

## Apparent

That this is a regrettable extra, bolted on for convenience and removable.
Remove it and the over-built fleet stays in the water, which is most of the gain gone: transferability
is load bearing in the economics, not decorative.

## Yearbook

The distinction between a licence, which permits, and a right, which is held.
Naming which one was created is the play's whole use of it.
