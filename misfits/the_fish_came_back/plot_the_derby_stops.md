# Plot: The Derby Stops

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Race Is Over](piece_the_race_is_over.md): a season compressed into days, a fleet built for speed, and a stock going down.

## Action

[Allocating](process_allocating.md): [The Skipper](persona_the_skipper.md), in [the allocation seat](position_allocation.md), receives a share on [End the Race](plan_end_the_race.md).

## Stage

[The Register](place_the_register.md): a catch history becomes a tonnage, and every vessel in the fishery is given a reason to stop racing.

## Tension

Here the play establishes that the scheme is right, at full strength, because a weak version would
make the ending cheap.
The racing was ruinous and nobody in it could stop unilaterally: a fish left in the water was a fish
for the next boat, so restraint cost the person exercising it and bought them nothing.
A secure share reverses that in one move. The season lengthens, the gear conflict ends, the boats stop
being built for a sprint, fewer people drown, and the stock begins to rebuild.
**A room shown this evidence would vote for the scheme again, and should.**
The allocation goes on catch history because that is the only record that exists, which is the fairest
available rule and is also a prize for the behaviour being ended, and it draws a line around one
year's participants that no later rule can cross without a purchase.
