# Persona: The Deckhand

## Taxonomy

[Entry](position_entry.md). The next fisher, who has the skill and the years and no share.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Crews for whoever is fishing, on a share of the catch that is smaller than it was.
Would have bought a boat and worked up, which is how everybody in the hall over fifty did it.
Is not owed anything by anybody and is not the subject of any clause.

## Action

Learns the grounds.
Prices the right to fish them.
Crews for somebody else, or leaves.

## Shadow

That there was never a moment of exclusion to point at.
**No door was closed**: the door was priced, by a market working properly, on an asset that got more
valuable because the fishery got healthier, so there is no decision in the record that could have
gone the other way and nobody to ask.

## Tell

Is the only party with a claim that is not a legal claim.
Is the reason the fishery has an age profile and not a future.
