# Piece: The Race Is Over

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). The fact that the instrument works, which the whole production rests on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the grounds](place_the_grounds.md), in every season since the shares were issued.
In the standard result: a secure share removes the reason to fish before somebody else does.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is an argument against catch shares and there is nothing structural in it.
It is load bearing because **the scheme has to be right for the trap to be one**: the racing was
ruinous, the fleet was over-built, the seasons were compressed into days, and a secure share ended
all three and let the stock rebuild. A room shown this evidence would vote for the scheme again, and
should.

## Apparent

That the harm therefore shows the scheme was a mistake.
It does not. The harm is produced by the part that worked, so any account that treats the design as
an error has to explain why the fishery it replaced was better, and it was not.

## Yearbook

The standard case for rights-based fisheries management, stated as its proponents state it.
The play needs it stated at full strength, because a weakened version would make the ending cheap.
