# Process: Allocating

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). Cutting a public catch into private shares on the record of who was fishing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A stock in decline and a fleet that cannot stop racing for it.
At [the register](place_the_register.md), once, in one season.

## Direction

Toward a defensible division of something that was nobody's.
Catch history is the only record that exists and the only basis that survives challenge, so the
shares go to the vessels that fished hardest in the years before the scheme, which is the fairest
available rule and also an award for the behaviour the scheme is ending.

## Lever

The formula, and the reference years it reads.
There is no lever here for anybody who was not in the fishery then, because there is no record of
them, and none for the harbour, because the record is kept by vessel.

## Echo

A fishery with owners,
each of whom now has a reason to leave fish in the water,
and a boundary drawn around a single year's participants that no later rule can cross without a
purchase.
