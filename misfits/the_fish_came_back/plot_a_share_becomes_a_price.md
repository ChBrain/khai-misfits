## Tension

Here the instrument's second property arrives, and it is not an add-on.
Transferability is what retires the vessels the race built, which is most of the gain the scheme was
costed on: a secure but untradable share leaves the over-built fleet in the water and forgoes it.
So the right has to be sellable, and a sellable secure claim on a resource is an asset. It has a
price, a market, and a lender who will take it as collateral.
**The share is now worth more than the boat.**
Nothing has gone wrong. The rent a fishery yields has to sit somewhere, and once the right is ownable
it sits in the right, which is the ordinary behaviour of a functioning asset market and not a
defect in it.
