# Persona: The Share

## Taxonomy

[Transfer](position_transfer.md). A fraction of the annual catch, made into a thing a person can own, hold, borrow against and sell.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Issued as a management device and behaving as a security from the first day it could be sold.
Has a price, a market, a lender who will take it as collateral and a value on a balance sheet.
Appears in the regulation as an allocation and in the harbour as the most expensive object in the fishery.

## Action

Removes the reason to race, which is what it was made for.
Moves to whoever will pay most for it.
Appreciates with the stock.

## Shadow

That it cannot do the first without the second.
**Security is what stops the racing and transferability is what retires the surplus fleet**, and the
same two properties are what make it accumulate: a thing that is safe to hold and free to sell is a
thing that ends up held by whoever can hold most of it.

## Tell

Is the successful part of the design.
Is the barrier.
