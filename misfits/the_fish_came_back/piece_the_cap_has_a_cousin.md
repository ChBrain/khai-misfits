# Piece: The Cap Has a Cousin

## Taxonomy

[The Fish Came Back](play_the_fish_came_back.md). Why the obvious fix does not hold: a limit written against a holder, met by another holder.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the register](place_the_register.md), in the compliance column.
In the accumulation cap, the owner-aboard rule, and the affiliation test written to police them.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play ends at the first remedy and is about regulatory laziness.
It is load bearing because **each fix is answered in kind rather than defeated**: a cap on holdings is
met by a second company, an owner-aboard rule by a name on a crew list, and each answer is itself
lawful, so the regulator is not facing evasion so much as the ordinary flexibility of corporate form
applied to a rule that has to name somebody.

## Apparent

That a tighter definition would close it.
Tighter definitions are written and are useful and each one narrows the fishery's legitimate
arrangements too, so the cost of the enforcement is paid by exactly the small operators the rule was
written to keep.

## Yearbook

The difficulty of writing an ownership limit against a legal person who can be more than one.
The play uses it only for the shape of the failure, not to accuse anybody of gaming it.
