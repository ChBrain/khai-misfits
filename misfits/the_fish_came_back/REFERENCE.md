# The Fish Came Back: Reference

The research warrant for The Fish Came Back.
The term is the house's; the play runs on individual transferable quotas in fisheries management, on
the capitalisation of resource rent into the price of the share, and on the documented distributional
consequences, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging rights-based fisheries management as **a remedy that is paid for in the currency it creates**:
a stock is being fished down by a fleet that cannot stop racing for it, a secure share of the annual
catch is made into something a person can own and sell, the racing stops, and the stock rebuilds. That
is right and is one of the better-evidenced instruments in resource management. The play models the
documented mechanics, the instrument that works, the transferability that is load bearing rather than
decorative, the capitalisation that follows from a right being secure, the recovery that raises the
price of the right, the transfers that are voluntary on both sides, and the community that was never a
party to any of it, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a bad scheme or of
somebody gaming it.

The governing law is that a right which is worth conserving is a right worth buying. Security is what
removes the reason to race, because a fish left in the water is still yours next year; transferability
is what retires the vessels the race built, because the share moves to whoever will pay most for it.
Those two properties are the whole of why the instrument works, and a claim that is safe to hold and
free to sell is by construction an asset, priced on what it permits and available to whoever can raise
the money.

What removes any possibility of blame is that every transaction is between willing parties. The
allocation went on catch history because that is the only record that exists and the only basis that
survives challenge. The sale is made by a fisher at the end of a career, offered the capital value of
that career, with no successor and no obligation to refuse. The purchase is lawful and at the asking
price. An investigator sent to find the moment it went wrong returns with a list of ordinary contracts.

What makes it structural is the direction of the loop. A share is priced on the fishing it permits, so
a rebuilt stock is a dearer share, and confidence that the right will still be honoured in twenty
years is itself a conservation success and is the second input to the price. **Every instrument that
makes the scheme work harder makes the share more expensive.** So the barrier is not a residue of the
initial grant that time erodes; it is built out of good news and grows with it, and the moment the
right becomes affordable is the moment it stops being worth having.

And the correction hands back what it was bought with. An accumulation cap binds on exactly the
consolidation that retired the surplus fleet, and is met by a second company. An owner-aboard rule
forbids the ageing skipper the sale that was their pension, and is answered by a name on a crew list.
A community reserve is carved from the same total and moves the allocation argument indoors, which is
a real instrument and does hold some of the fishery in some towns. Untradable shares stop the
concentration and the efficiency together. The route that stays open is leasing, which lands fish and
cannot accumulate, because the rent is set by what the fishing is worth.

It is distinct from the house's **The Commons**, which stages the problem this instrument
solves rather than a rival account of the harm: there the quantity is the divergence between private
and social cost under open access, and setting it to zero removes the reason to introduce shares at
all. Here the quantity is the transferability of the right that was created, and the two are
independent: a fishery can have full open-access ruin and non-transferable quota, with no consolidation
at all, or a perfectly healthy stock whose shares are the more expensive for it.

It is distinct from the house's **The Head Start**, where advantage compounds because holding more
makes acquiring more easier: that is the nearest neighbour and it is not the engine here. Stipulate
flat returns to scale, so that a large holder enjoys no advantage whatever in acquiring the next share,
and the concentration proceeds anyway, out of heterogeneous efficiency and out of exit, because an
ageing owner with no successor sells to whoever can raise the capital.

It is distinct from the house's **The Only Basket**, where a benefit capitalises into a single
household asset and the holder then defends it politically: there the instrument of harm is a veto, and
the harm is exercised. Here the holders exercise nothing; the barrier is a price, and it would stand in
a fishery whose quota holders had no political voice at all.

It is distinct from the house's **Good Enough to Leave**, where a qualification that can only be
certified at home becomes the licence to leave: there the harm is that the certificate travels and the
trained person with it. Here nothing travels except a share, the fishing stays exactly where the fish
are, and what leaves is the ownership of it.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the
  scheme as a mistake or the buyers as predators, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get
  wrong: the managers implemented the instrument the evidence recommended and it worked; the allocation
  used the only record that existed; the seller was offered the capital value of a career and owed
  nobody a refusal; the buyer paid the asking price and satisfied every cap on its face; and the
  concentration would proceed in a fishery of scrupulous participants, because it follows from
  transferability and from people retiring. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the
  structure, so the misfit is that saving a common resource by making it property makes the property
  the new scarcity.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Accumulation caps
  bind on the consolidation that retired the surplus fleet and are answered by affiliated holdings.
  Owner-aboard rules forbid a retiring fisher the sale that was their pension and are answered by a
  crew list. Community quota programmes are real, are used, and do hold some of the fishery in some
  towns, and they are carved from the same total and re-open the allocation question inside the
  community. Making the right untradable removes the concentration and the efficiency gain together.
  Leasing keeps a route in that lands fish and cannot end in ownership, because the rent is set by what
  the fishing is worth.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the fisheries economics and the ethnography are
  theirs. The bounds are carried with them: outcomes differ sharply between fisheries, and the
  consolidation a scheme produces depends on how it was designed, on what caps and residency conditions
  it carried from the start, and on how concentrated the fleet already was; several jurisdictions have
  built community-holding mechanisms into their schemes from the outset and report better distributional
  outcomes than the retrofitted cases; the conservation record of catch shares is strong and this play
  does not dispute it; and the ethnographic work cited is deep in one archipelago rather than broad
  across many, which is what gives it its detail and what limits its reach. **The magnitudes those
  papers report are deliberately not carried here**, because the papers themselves could not be read in
  the pass that cited them: publisher hosts and the article archive are blocked to it, so the
  bibliography, the study designs and the directions were verified by search and corroborated across
  independent reports, which is the treatment a citation survives and a number is the one thing that
  does not. **The play makes no claim that catch shares should not be used**, since the alternative is
  usually the race, only that the right which stops the race is a thing that has to be bought.
