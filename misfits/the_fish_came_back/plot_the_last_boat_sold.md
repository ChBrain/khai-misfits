## Stage

[The Harbour](place_the_harbour.md): a berth empties, the ice plant loses a season's volume, and the register is one name shorter.

## Tension

Here the harm is done and there is nobody to accuse of doing it.
A person who fished for forty years is offered the capital value of that career, and has no obligation
to refuse it; the share was the only thing the scheme gave them, and any rule that stops the sale is a
rule confiscating it.
The buyer paid the asking price. The transfer satisfied every cap on its face.
**No clause was broken and no party was wronged, and the shares are ashore.**
The harbour is not a party to any of it: the scheme allocated to vessels and not to places, so a town
watching its fishery leave has nothing to appeal, nothing to enforce, and an interest that is obviously
real and entirely outside the instrument.
