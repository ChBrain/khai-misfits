# Plot: Take the Copy Now

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Archive](persona_the_archive.md), arriving with a remedy that works.
[The Moment of Citing](piece_the_moment_of_citing.md), which is its condition.

## Action

[Preserving](process_preserving.md): [The Archive](persona_the_archive.md), in [the preserved seat](position_preserved.md), takes a copy as the citation is made and serves that copy afterwards.

## Stage

[The Archive](persona_the_archive.md), on [Take a Copy at the Time](plan_take_a_copy_at_the_time.md), protects everything it is pointed at, and is pointed at nothing that was written before it existed.

## Tension

The cure is real, is in use, and is not retrospective.
Institutions do this and libraries run it, and a citation to a preserved copy survives the loss of the original entirely. There is no scepticism to register about whether it works: it does.
Its condition is the play's last turn. The copy must be taken **at the moment of citing**, because once the target is gone there is nothing left to preserve and no amount of later diligence recovers it. **So the remedy protects the corpus written from here and reaches none of the corpus already written**, and the seventy per cent that has already gone stays gone. A fix that arrives after the damage is a fix for somebody else's damage.
