# Plan: Say Exactly Where It Is

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the cited seat](position_cited.md), inside [It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Cited](position_cited.md)

## Direction

Read the source, represent it accurately, and record the author, the title, the date and the address. Check before writing it down. Give the reader the only handle the thing has.

## Orders

([The Citation](persona_the_citation.md) records the reference at [the footnote](place_the_footnote.md), having read and verified the source.)

## Implementation

([The Footnote](place_the_footnote.md), at the time of writing; the [citing](process_citing.md) process; [the address](piece_the_address.md) as the only available handle.)

## Targets

- [x] the source was read and says what it is reported to say
- [x] the address was checked at the time of writing
- [ ] the reference remains exact and well formed for as long as the document lasts
- [ ] its accuracy is irrelevant to whether it will still resolve, since it fixes a place and not a thing
