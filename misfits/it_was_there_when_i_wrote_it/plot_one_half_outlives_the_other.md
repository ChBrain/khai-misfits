## Action

## Stage

[The Standing Text](persona_the_standing_text.md), on [Remain in Force](plan_remain_in_force.md), does exactly what the institutions around it were built to make it do.

## Tension

The asymmetry is the whole play, and it runs the wrong way with quality.
The citing document is durable **by design**: printed, deposited, catalogued, legible in a century, and that permanence is the entire point of the apparatus around it. The cited target is durable by **nobody's design at all**, kept only as long as somebody finds it convenient.
So the two halves of a single reference are held by different parties under different obligations and decay at completely different rates. **The better the citing institution does its job, the longer the document spends pointing at nothing**, which means the effect is worst in exactly the places built to last: the law reports, the archives, the permanent record.
