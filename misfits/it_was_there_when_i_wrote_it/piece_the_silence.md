# Piece: The Silence

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md). The absence of any signal that a reference has failed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the footnote](place_the_footnote.md), permanently.
Broken only at [the later reading](place_the_later_reading.md), and only for one reader.

## Load Bearing

Without it the damage is noticed as it happens and is repaired at a manageable size.
It is load bearing because it is what makes the loss **cumulative**: the text is unchanged, the footnote well formed, nothing in the document false, and no part of the apparatus ever checks, so a body of work can rot through entirely while every copy reads perfectly.

## Apparent

Nothing, by definition.
It presents as a document in good order, which is precisely what it is, and there is no reading of the page that discloses it.

## Yearbook

Produced by [persisting](process_persisting.md) alongside [reorganising](process_reorganising.md), broken only by [checking](process_checking.md).
It is why the count, when somebody finally makes it, comes out at most of the corpus rather than a few per cent.
