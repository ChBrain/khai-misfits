## Owner

## Shown

A reader, checking.
An address that resolves to nothing: not a contradiction, not a retraction, an absence.

## Holds

The only detection the system has.
A broken reference announces nothing, so the failure exists undiscovered until somebody actually looks, and a reference is normally taken on trust, which is what a reference is for.

## Offers

The discovery, to whoever troubles to make it.

## Withheld

Any means of repair.
The reader learns that the support is gone and has no way to reconstruct what was there, so detection and remedy are separated completely.
