# Piece: The Seventy Per Cent

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md). The measurement: how much of a counted corpus already reaches nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Outside the documents, in the study that counted them.
Produced by somebody occupying [the following seat](position_following.md) at scale, which individual readers do not.

## Load Bearing

Without it the harm is a plausible worry about a footnote somebody once found broken.
It is load bearing because it is **already past**: more than seventy per cent of the addresses in one set of law reviews and half of those in United States Supreme Court opinions no longer reach what was cited, so this is a completed loss and not a projected risk.

## Apparent

A technical statistic about hyperlinks.
It reads as an IT problem, which understates it: the corpus counted is the permanent legal record, and the fraction is most of it.

## Yearbook

Counted once, by people who went and looked at every reference.
It is what [the silence](piece_the_silence.md) looks like when somebody finally measures it.
