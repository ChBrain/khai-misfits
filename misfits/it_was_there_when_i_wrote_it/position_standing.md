# Position: Standing

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md). The durable document's seat: printed, deposited, in force, and carrying its footnote unchanged.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Permanence, deliberately engineered by the institutions around it.
Authority, and the reliance of everybody who cites it in turn.
A footnote that will read the same in a century.

## Orders

Remain in force.
Be relied upon.
Carry the reference exactly as written, indefinitely.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and its virtue is what sets the size of the damage.
Because this half of the reference is kept by an institution built for permanence and the other half is kept by nobody in particular, **the better this seat performs the longer it points at nothing**, so the effect is worst in the places built to last.
It ends authoritative, unaltered, and resting on an address.

## Drives

Toward permanence, achieved,
in a record designed to outlast everybody in it,
so the seat that keeps the record is the seat that preserves the broken pointer.
