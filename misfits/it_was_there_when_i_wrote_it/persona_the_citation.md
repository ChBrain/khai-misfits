# Persona: The Citation

## Taxonomy

[Cited](position_cited.md). The reference as made: precise, checked at the time, honest about what the source said, and pointing at an address.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Exact, and made in good faith.
The author read the source, represented it accurately, and recorded where it could be found.
Nothing careless anywhere in it, and no better practice was available.

## Action

Names the author, the title, the date and the address.
Is checked before it is written down.
Fixes a location, because a location was the only handle the thing had.

## Shadow

Nothing done wrong and nothing that could have been done better, which is what makes this seat impossible to improve.
Because a citation can only fix an address, and the thing at that address is kept by somebody else under no obligation, the accuracy of the citation is irrelevant to whether it will still work.
It ends perfectly formed and pointing at nothing.

## Tell

Says: it was there when I wrote it.
True, and checked, and it is not there now.
