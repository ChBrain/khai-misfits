## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Place

At [the footnote](place_the_footnote.md), recorded exactly.
Pointing at [the server](place_the_server.md), which owes it nothing.

## Load Bearing

Without it the citation is vague and the play is about sloppy scholarship.
It is load bearing because of what it **is**: a reference to a location, not to a document, so its correctness says nothing about whether the thing will still be at that location, and no care taken over it changes that.

## Apparent

The most precise part of the whole reference.
It reads as the element that removes ambiguity, and it does, which is why nobody treats it as the fragile one.
