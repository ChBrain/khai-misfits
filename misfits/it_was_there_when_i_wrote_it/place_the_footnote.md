# Place: The Footnote

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md). Where the reference lives: exact, well formed, and unchanged for as long as the document lasts.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An author, a title, a date, an address.
Correct on the day it was written and correct in its form ever afterwards.

## Holds

The play's quietest fact: nothing here ever becomes false.
The text does not change, the formatting does not decay, and no statement in the document is wrong. What fails is outside it, and the footnote goes on reading perfectly regardless.

## Offers

Everything a reference is supposed to offer: attribution, verifiability, and a route to the source.

## Withheld

Any signal that the route no longer goes anywhere.
There is no mechanism by which a footnote reports its own failure, so the place that would raise the alarm is structurally silent.
