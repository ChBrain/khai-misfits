# Process: Persisting

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md). The citing document remaining in force, deposited and unchanged, for as long as its institutions intend.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Publication, deposit and indexing.
At [the footnote](place_the_footnote.md), which will read the same in a century.

## Direction

Toward permanence, which is precisely what the apparatus around a record is built to achieve.
It succeeds: the document is printed, catalogued, legible and authoritative, and is relied on by everything that cites it in turn.
And because the other half of the reference is kept by nobody in particular, the success of this step is what sets the duration of the mismatch.

## Lever

Durability, engineered.
Because the two halves are held by different parties under different obligations, **improving this step lengthens the exposure rather than reducing it**.

## Echo

A record that will outlast everybody in it,
carrying an address that outlasted nothing.
