## Tension

The failure is silent, which is what lets it accumulate to the point the measurement finds.
The text reads exactly as it did. The footnote is still well formed. Nothing in the document has become false, and no part of the apparatus reports a fault, because none of it ever checks. **A broken reference does not announce itself.**
So detection depends entirely on somebody going to look, and most readers never do: a reference is usually taken on trust, which is what a reference is for. A body of work can therefore rot through completely while every copy of it sits on a shelf reading perfectly, and [the seventy per cent](piece_the_seventy_per_cent.md) is what that looks like once somebody finally counts.
