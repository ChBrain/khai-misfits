# Persona: The Host

## Taxonomy

[Hosting](position_hosting.md). Whoever holds the cited thing: reorganising, renaming, retiring, and under no undertaking to anybody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, and competent.
A site tidied, a department renamed, a project closed, a company bought, a server retired, a domain allowed to lapse.
Every one of those is administration any reasonable body performs, and none was done to anybody.

## Action

Reorganises.
Moves or retires what it holds.
Gives no notice, because there is nobody it knows to notify.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the blamelessness here is total: no undertaking was given, none was requested, and the citing party never made itself known.
Because the obligation does not exist rather than being neglected, there is no standard this seat falls short of and no policy that would have caught it.
It ends having kept its own affairs in good order, and having broken somebody else's argument.

## Tell

Says: we moved to a new site in 2019.
Announced properly at the time, to the people who were reading then.
