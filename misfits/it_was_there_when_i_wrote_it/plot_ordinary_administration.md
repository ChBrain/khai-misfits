## Stage

## Tension

The host is blameless completely, and not merely excusable.
A site is tidied, a department renamed, a project closed, a company bought, a server retired, a domain allowed to lapse. Every one of those is administration any reasonable body performs, and performing it well looks exactly like this.
**No undertaking was given and none was requested.** The obligation does not exist rather than being neglected, so there is no standard this seat falls short of, no policy that would have caught it, and nobody to complain to. And the citing party never made itself known: a host does not learn who has cited it, so even a host that wanted to keep faith would not know which of its pages somebody's argument now depends on.
