# Position: Following

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md). The later reader's seat: the only party who discovers the failure, and the rarest to be occupied.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The diligence to take a reference seriously enough to check it.
An address, and an expectation of finding something at it.
No way to tell what was there.

## Orders

Follow the reference.
Arrive.
Find nothing, and have no means of reconstructing what was lost.

## Loses

The check itself, which is the only detection mechanism the whole apparatus has.
Because a broken reference announces nothing and most readers take a citation on trust, which is what citations are for, this seat is rarely occupied and the damage accumulates in its absence.
It ends holding a document that reads perfectly and rests on nothing checkable.

## Drives

Toward verifying what a document claims,
which is what the apparatus exists to permit,
so the seat that would catch this is the one almost nobody sits in.
