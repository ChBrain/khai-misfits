# Persona: The Archive

## Taxonomy

[Preserved](position_preserved.md). The remedy: a copy taken at the moment of citing, and cited in place of the address.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Real, and in use.
Not a proposal: institutions do this, libraries run it, and a citation to a preserved copy survives the loss of the original.
Effective on everything it touches.

## Action

Takes a copy at the moment the citation is made.
Serves that copy afterwards, permanently.
Reaches nothing it was not pointed at.

## Shadow

Its condition, which is the play's last turn: the copy has to be taken **then**.
Because once the target is gone there is nothing left to preserve, this seat cannot be applied to any existing damage, so it protects the corpus written from here and none of the corpus already written.
It ends a working cure that is not retrospective.

## Tell
