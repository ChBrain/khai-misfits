# Process: Citing

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md). A source read, represented accurately, and recorded with the address at which it sits.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An argument that rests on something outside itself.
At [the footnote](place_the_footnote.md), at the time of writing.

## Direction

Toward saying exactly where a thing is, which is the whole of what a reference can do.
The source was read, it said what it is reported to say, and the address was checked before it was written down.
No better practice was available, because a location was the only handle the thing had.

## Lever

Precision about a place.
Because what sits at that place is kept by another party under no obligation, no increase in this step's accuracy affects whether it will still resolve.

## Echo

A reference that is exact,
about somewhere that need not stay as it is.
