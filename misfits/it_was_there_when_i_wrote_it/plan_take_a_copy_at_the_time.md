# Plan: Take a Copy at the Time

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the preserved seat](position_preserved.md), inside [It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Preserved](position_preserved.md)

## Direction

Take a copy of the target as the citation is made, and serve that copy permanently thereafter, so the reference survives the loss of the original. Act only in the one window in which acting is possible.

## Orders

([The Archive](persona_the_archive.md) preserves the target at [the moment of citing](piece_the_moment_of_citing.md) and serves it afterwards.)

## Implementation

([The Footnote](place_the_footnote.md), as it is written; the [preserving](process_preserving.md) process, run by institutions and libraries; nothing available afterwards to copy.)
