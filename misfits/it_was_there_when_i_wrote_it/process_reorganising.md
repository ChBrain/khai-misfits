# Process: Reorganising

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md). A site tidied, renamed, retired or allowed to lapse, as any reasonable body does.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Ordinary administration: a department renamed, a project closed, a company bought, a server retired.
At [the server](place_the_server.md), announced to whoever is reading at the time.

## Direction

Toward keeping its own estate in good order, which is the whole of what a host owes.
No undertaking was given to anybody and none was requested, so this is not neglect of a duty but the absence of one.
And the host does not learn who has cited it, so even one that wished to keep faith would not know which pages an argument depends on.

## Lever

Change, which is what maintaining anything consists of.
Because the obligation does not exist rather than being ignored, no policy or standard reaches this step.

## Echo

A well-kept site,
and somebody else's argument, quietly unsupported.
