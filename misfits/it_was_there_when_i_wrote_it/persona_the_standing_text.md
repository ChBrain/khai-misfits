# Persona: The Standing Text

## Taxonomy

[Standing](position_standing.md). The judgement or the paper: durable by design, deposited and indexed, still in force, and still saying what it said.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Permanent, deliberately.
Printed, deposited, catalogued, and legible in a century, which is the whole point of the institutions around it.
Authoritative, and rightly so.

## Action

Remains in force.
Is cited in turn, and relied upon.
Carries its footnote unchanged, word for word, for as long as it lasts.

## Shadow

Its own durability, which is what determines how long it spends pointing at nothing.
Because this half of the reference is kept by an institution built for permanence and the other half is kept by nobody in particular, **the better this seat does its job the worse the mismatch becomes**.
It ends authoritative, unaltered, and resting on an address.

## Tell

Says nothing new, ever, which is its virtue.
The sentence is the same sentence; only the world at the other end of it has moved.
