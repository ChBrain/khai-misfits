# Process: Checking

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md). A reader going to the address given, and arriving at nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Diligence, which is unusual.
At [the later reading](place_the_later_reading.md), years afterwards.

## Direction

Toward verifying what a document claims, which is what the whole apparatus exists to permit.
What is found is an absence rather than a disagreement: not a contradiction, not a retraction, not a different claim, and no way to reconstruct what was there.
So the check succeeds at detecting the fault and fails at repairing it.

## Lever

Actually looking, which is the only detection this system has.
Because a reference is normally taken on trust, which is what a reference is for, this step is rarely performed and the damage accumulates in its absence.

## Echo

One reader who knows,
and every other copy of the document reading exactly as it always did.
