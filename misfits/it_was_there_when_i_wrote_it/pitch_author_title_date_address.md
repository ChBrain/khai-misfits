# Pitch: Author, Title, Date, Address

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Bibliographic, unbothered, exact.
The register of a footnote read aloud: an author, a title, a date, an address, in that order and no adjectives.
Nothing accusing anywhere in it, because every party did the correct thing at the moment they did it.

## Undertow

Under the apparatus, two facts that do not negotiate.
A citation fixes an address rather than a thing. Nobody owes anybody a permanent address.
Between those two, a document outlives what it rests on and says nothing about it.

## Nerve

The unease of a judgement still in force,
whose support is a string of characters,
in a sentence that has not changed a word since the day it was true.

## Echo

After the run: a footnote perfectly formed,
seventy per cent of a corpus reaching nothing,
and a fix that works on everything written from now on and on none of what is already there.
