# It Was There When I Wrote It: Reference

The research warrant for It Was There When I Wrote It.
The term is the law librarians' and the legal scholars'; the play runs on reference rot in citation, and on what
happens to a durable document resting on a support that nobody is keeping, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the citation as **two halves kept by different parties under different obligations**: a reference is made
exactly, checked at the time, and fixes an address because an address is the only handle the thing has; the host
of that address reorganises, renames, retires or lapses, which is ordinary administration and owed to nobody; the
citing document persists by design, deposited and authoritative, carrying the reference unchanged; and a reader
who follows it years later arrives at nothing. The play models the documented mechanics, the citation that is
good scholarship, the host that gave no undertaking and cannot know who depends on it, the asymmetry by which
the more durable half outlives the other, the silence that lets the loss accumulate unnoticed, the measured
fraction of a permanent record that has already gone, and the remedy that works and must be applied at the moment
of citing, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a careless author or a negligent host.

The governing law is that a citation fixes a location rather than a thing, so the reference survives only as long
as a party with no obligation to the citing document chooses to leave that location as it is.

**Nobody in the sequence falls short, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The author
read the source and recorded it accurately. The host kept its own estate in good order. The publishing
institution achieved exactly the permanence it exists to achieve. The reader did the diligent thing. No statement
anywhere became false.

**And the citation is good scholarship, which the play establishes before anything else.** The source was read,
it says what it is reported to say, and the address was verified before it was written down. **There is no
carelessness available to find**, and no better practice existed, because a location was the only way to say
where the thing was. So the trap cannot be sprung by improving the reference: it must be sprung with one that is
exact, honest and correctly formed.

**And the host is blameless completely rather than merely excusable.** A site tidied, a department renamed, a
project closed, a company bought, a server retired, a domain allowed to lapse: each is what any reasonable body
does, and doing it well looks exactly like this. **No undertaking was given and none was requested**, so the
obligation is absent rather than breached, and no policy or audit standard reaches it. And a host does not learn
who has cited it, so even one wishing to keep faith would not know which of its pages an argument now rests on.

**Then the asymmetry, which is the whole play and runs the wrong way with quality.** The citing document is
durable by design: printed, deposited, catalogued, legible in a century, which is the entire point of the
apparatus around it. The cited target is durable by nobody's design at all. So the two halves of one reference
decay at completely different rates, and **the better the citing institution does its job, the longer the
document spends pointing at nothing.** The effect is therefore worst in exactly the places built to last.

**And the failure is silent, which is what makes it cumulative.** A broken reference does not announce itself:
the text is unchanged, the footnote is still well formed, nothing in the document has become false, and no part
of the apparatus ever checks. Detection depends entirely on somebody going to look, and a reference is normally
taken on trust, which is what a reference is for. A body of work can rot through completely while every copy of
it reads perfectly.

**Then the measurement, which makes this a finding rather than a worry.** More than **seventy per cent** of the
addresses cited in one set of law reviews, and **fifty per cent** of those in United States Supreme Court
opinions, no longer reach the material that was cited. The corpus counted is the permanent legal record, and the
fraction is most of it. **This is a completed loss rather than a projected risk.**

**And the remedy is real and is not retrospective.** Archiving the target at the moment of citing, and citing the
preserved copy, works: institutions do it, libraries run it, and such a citation survives the loss of the
original. Its condition is timing. Once the target is gone there is nothing left to preserve, so no later
diligence, funding or policy recovers any of it. **The cure protects the corpus written from here and reaches
none of the corpus already written**, and the measured fraction that has gone stays gone.

It is distinct from the house's **Longer Than Its Meaning**, which is the nearest incumbent and stages long-term
nuclear semiotics, where a marker outlasts the literacy that would read it. Remove that engine entirely, by
stipulating a literacy that persists for ever, so that anything found can be read and understood perfectly, and
this misfit is unchanged, because **nothing here is unreadable and the target is simply not there.** Run it the
other way for the confirmation: remove **this** engine, by stipulating that every cited target persists for ever
at its original address, and this collapses to no harm at all, while that misfit is untouched, since its marker
still cannot be read. The two separate on the cleanest available line: **there the thing survives and cannot be
understood; here it is understood perfectly and is not there.**

It is distinct from the house's **The Hollow Word**, which stages semantic drift, where a term's meaning bleaches
as it is used. Remove that engine, by stipulating that no word ever changes meaning and every term denotes now
exactly what it denoted then, and this stands entire, because **nothing here turns on meaning at all.** The
footnote's words are unambiguous throughout; what fails is a pointer, and a perfectly stable vocabulary does not
keep a server running.

It is distinct from the house's **The Bus Factor**, which stages key-person risk, where knowledge held by one
person is lost when that person goes. Remove that engine, by stipulating that every person's knowledge is fully
documented and nothing depends on any individual, and this stands, because **the loss here is not knowledge and
not tacit.** The cited material was published, public and often widely held; what vanished is the route to it,
and documenting who knows what does not restore an address.

It is distinct from the house's **The File Drawer**, which stages publication bias, where results that were
obtained are selectively withheld. Remove that engine, by stipulating that everything obtained is published and
nothing is filed away, and this stands whole, because **the thing here was published.** There a result exists and
is kept back; here it was made public, was cited, and then ceased to be reachable, so the loss is downstream of
publication rather than upstream of it.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the author as
  careless or the host as negligent, because nothing in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the source was
  read and accurately represented, the address was checked at the time, and no better practice existed; the host
  gave no undertaking, was asked for none, performed only ordinary administration, and could not have known which
  of its pages an argument depended on; the publishing institution achieved precisely the permanence it exists
  for; and the reader did the diligent thing and found an absence. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges
  from the structure, so the misfit is that the two halves of a reference are kept by different parties with
  different obligations and outlive each other by different spans.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. A more careful citation changes
  nothing, since the address was already correct. A more responsible host changes nothing in general, since the
  obligation does not exist and no host can learn who cites it. Auditing the document finds nothing, because
  nothing in it is false. Making the citing record more durable makes the mismatch longer rather than shorter.
  And archiving at the moment of citing genuinely works and cannot be applied backwards, so it protects what has
  not yet been written and leaves the measured loss exactly where it is.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed as
  the house's. The staging is the house's; the scoping of link and reference rot in legal citation, and the
  counts on which this rests, are Zittrain, Albert and Lessig's. The bounds are carried with them: **the measured
  fractions are of specific corpora at a specific time**, law reviews and United States Supreme Court opinions,
  and are offered as the shape and scale of the loss rather than as constants of any other literature; the
  authors are proposing a remedy as well as reporting a problem, which the play notes rather than treats as
  neutrality; who should bear the cost of permanence, on what legal footing, and whether any archive is more
  durable than what it replaces are genuinely contested and the play adjudicates none of it; and the account is
  stated for citations to addressed online material, which is not every citation. **The play makes no claim about
  any court, any journal, any host or any archive, and gives no legal, bibliographic or archival advice of any
  kind**, only that a reference fixes a place, and that nobody is keeping the place.
