# Persona: The Reader

## Taxonomy

[Following](position_following.md). Whoever comes later and actually checks: the only party who finds out, and the rarest one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Diligent, and unusual in being so.
Doing what the apparatus is for: taking the reference seriously enough to go and look.
Expecting to find a source and to disagree with it or not.

## Action

Follows the reference.
Arrives at nothing: not a contradiction, not a retraction, not a different claim.
Has no way to tell what was there.

## Shadow

The discovery, which is theirs alone and which the document does not make.
Because a broken reference does not announce itself, this seat is the only mechanism by which the damage is ever detected, and most readers never occupy it.
It ends knowing the argument rests on nothing checkable, and holding a document that reads exactly as it always did.

## Tell

Says: this link is dead.
A small remark about a footnote, and the only report the failure ever generates.
