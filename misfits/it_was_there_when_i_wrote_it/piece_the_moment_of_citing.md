# Piece: The Moment of Citing

## Taxonomy

[It Was There When I Wrote It](play_it_was_there_when_i_wrote_it.md). The only window in which the remedy can be applied: while the target still exists.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the footnote](place_the_footnote.md), as it is being written and not afterwards.
The single point at which [preserving](process_preserving.md) can act.

## Load Bearing

Without it the remedy is general and the play ends in a solution.
It is load bearing because it makes the cure **non-retrospective**: once the target is gone there is nothing left to copy, so no later diligence, funding or policy recovers any of what is already lost.

## Apparent

An implementation detail about when to press a button.
It reads as a matter of workflow, and it is the whole boundary of what can be saved: everything on one side of it is protected and everything on the other is not.

## Yearbook

Named by [the archive](persona_the_archive.md), who can act only here.
It divides the corpus into what will survive and [the seventy per cent](piece_the_seventy_per_cent.md), and nothing moves across the line.
