# Position: Hosting

## Taxonomy

## Has

Its own affairs to keep in order.
No undertaking to anybody, because none was given and none was requested.
No knowledge of who has cited it, since a host does not learn that.

## Orders

Announce changes to the people reading at the time.
Carry on.

## Loses

Nothing, and the blamelessness is total rather than merely excusable.
Because the obligation does not exist rather than being neglected, no policy would have caught this and there is no standard the seat falls short of; and a host that wanted to keep faith could not, not knowing which of its pages an argument now rests on.
It ends having kept its own house well and broken somebody else's argument.

which is the whole of what it owes,
so the seat doing nothing wrong is the seat that removes the support.
