# Position: Surveyor

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The seat of the one who measures how a life is going by what the person says they want or how satisfied they report, and so takes the honest yes at face value.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A measure built on the stated want: how satisfied are you, how much do you have of what you wanted, taken from the person's own mouth.
A deference it holds as a virtue, the refusal to presume it knows a person's good better than they do.
No window onto the wanting's history, so it cannot see that the want it records was shrunk to fit the wall before it ever asked.

## Orders

Take the yes, and write it down.
Ask each person how content they are and trust the answer, since who are you to overrule how they feel about their own life, and score the satisfaction they report as the measure of how well they fare.
Know that your deference is exactly what blinds you, that the want you record was revised to fit the constraint, and that by trusting the shrunken want you certify the deprivation as contentment and let the wall go unseen.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and it forfeits the truth it was built to find.
Because it trusts the stated want, the seat records the adapted contentment as wellbeing and misses the deprivation entirely, so the instrument meant to find how people fare returns the loss as a life well lived, its respect for the person the very thing that fails them.
It keeps its fairness and its good name and loses no cause for blame, a neutral counter that certifies the cage.

## Drives

Toward the one who measures by the stated want,
asking how content and taking the true yes, deferring as a virtue,
so the seat that honours the person's own voice is the seat that records their deprivation as their contentment, the constraint invisible to the measure meant to find it.
