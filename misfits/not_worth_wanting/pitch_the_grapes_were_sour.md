# Pitch: The Grapes Were Sour

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that when a good is walled off, the wanting for it quietly shrinks to fit, so the deprivation is felt as contentment.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Gentle, humane, at peace. A person content with their lot, wanting little and easy in it, and the peace is real. Nothing here is bitterness or self-pity; the contentment is honest, and honestly reported.

## Undertow

The good was walled off, and the mind, behind the person's own back, revised the wanting to fit the reaching, so the unattainable came to seem not worth wanting, the fox's grapes reframed as sour (Elster). What changed was the wanting, not the wall; so any measure that trusts what people say they want reads the narrowed life as chosen and the deprivation as wellbeing (Sen; Nussbaum).

## Nerve

That the kindest thing the mind can do, sparing the ache of an unmeetable longing, is the very thing that hides the constraint from the person inside it and from everyone measuring their contentment. No one planted the smaller want; it is held against its limit, since not every lowered want is a hidden loss.

## Echo

Wall off the good and the wanting quietly follows, so the fox, in perfect honesty, reports the grapes were sour.
