# Piece: The Wall

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The constraint that forecloses the good: poverty, custom, or plain circumstance, the fixed term the wanting will quietly bend around.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Reach](place_the_reach.md), met by [The Wanter](persona_the_wanter.md).

## Load Bearing

The immovable constraint. What matters is that the wall itself never moves: it is the wanting that bends, not the wall, so nothing about the person's real options changes even as the ache of wanting them fades. The wall is the fixed fact the whole misfit turns on; hold it steady and watch the wanting move instead (Elster).

## Apparent

Just the way things are. Unremarkable, the kind of limit nobody thinks to name because everybody around has always lived inside it.

## Yearbook
