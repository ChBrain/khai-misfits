# Place: The Reach

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). Where the good is wanted and found out of reach, and where the wanting itself is quietly revised to fit. Hosts [The Grapes Out of Reach](plot_the_grapes_out_of_reach.md), [Walled Off](plot_walled_off.md), [The Sour Turn](plot_the_sour_turn.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The good beyond the wall. The reaching hand, still lifted. The ache that sits between the wanting and the wall, before anyone has thought to move either.

## Holds

[The Grapes](piece_the_grapes.md), [The Wall](piece_the_wall.md), [The Turn](piece_the_turn.md).

## Offers

To the mind, of the two things standing apart, the easier one to move: not the wall, the wanting.

## Withheld

The good itself, kept out of reach exactly as it was. And any moving of the wall, which stays precisely where it stood.
