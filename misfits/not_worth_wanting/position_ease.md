# Position: Ease

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The seat of the mind's own tension-easing, the unbidden adjustment that revises the wanting to fit the reaching, working behind the Wanter's back and asking no leave.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A tension to relieve, the ache between a wanting and a wall that will not move.
The easier of the two things to change: not the wall, which stands, but the wanting, which can quietly be shrunk to fit.
No need of the person's consent, since it works behind their back, so the wanting is revised before any choice to give the good up is ever made.

## Orders

Ease the ache, and ask no leave.
Relieve the tension between the wanting and the wall by moving the wanting down to fit, so the good beyond reach comes to seem not worth wanting, and do it unbidden, before the person weighs or chooses anything.
Know that your mercy is the whole of the trap, that by easing the ache you surrender the good with no decision made, and that the peace you bring is a narrowing quietly done, the constraint left standing and unseen.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and it gives away the good on the person's behalf.
Because it moves the wanting and not the wall, the seat resolves the ache at the cost of the walled-off good, spending what the person had reason to value to buy them a peace they did not ask for, and doing it so quietly that no loss is ever felt.
It keeps its kind intent and loses no cause for blame, a mercy that wrongs no one and yet leaves the cage in place.

## Drives

Toward the unbidden easing that moves the wanting, not the wall,
relieving the ache behind the person's back and asking no leave, meaning only to spare them,
so the seat that brings the peace is the seat that hides the loss, its very kindness the thing that keeps the wall unseen.
