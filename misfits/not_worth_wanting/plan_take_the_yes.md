# Plan: Take the Yes

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the surveyor's seat](position_surveyor.md), inside [Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Surveyor](position_surveyor.md)

## Direction

Ask each person how content they are and trust the answer, since who are you to overrule how they feel about their own life, and score the satisfaction they report as the measure of how well they fare. Know that your deference is exactly what blinds you, that the want you record was revised to fit the constraint, and that by trusting the shrunken want you certify the deprivation as contentment and let the wall go unseen.

## Orders

([The Surveyor](persona_the_surveyor.md) records [the Masked Loss](piece_the_masked_loss.md) through [Surveying](process_surveying.md) at [the Measure](place_the_measure.md), asking the Wanter how satisfied it is and taking the honest yes at face value.)
