# Persona: The Wanter

## Taxonomy

[Wanter](position_wanter.md), inside [Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The archetype of the one who wanted a good put out of reach, and whose wanting was quietly revised to fit the reaching, so they rest content and report, in honesty, that they did not want it anyway.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person at peace, wanting little. It looks like contentment well earned, someone unbothered by what they lack and easy in their lot, the picture of a soul that has made its peace.

## Action

Want, then want less. At [the Reach](place_the_reach.md), through [Reaching](process_reaching.md), the Wanter wants [the Grapes](piece_the_grapes.md) and meets [the Wall](piece_the_wall.md); then, its wanting revised by [the Turn](piece_the_turn.md), on [Make Peace](plan_make_peace.md) it holds [the Contentment](piece_the_contentment.md) at [the Hearth](place_the_hearth.md), resting easy and content.

## Shadow

Its contentment is honest and it is the mask. The Wanter is not lying and not weak: the wanting-less is real and its ease a true mercy, sparing the ache of a longing that could never be met. But what changed was the wanting and not the wall, so the good goes unmissed while the constraint stands, and the person's own sincere report of contentment is exactly what hides the deprivation from anyone who would measure whether they are well.

## Tell

It says, and means, that it did not want the thing anyway, and the walls are still standing.
