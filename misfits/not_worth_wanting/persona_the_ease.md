# Persona: The Ease

## Taxonomy

[Ease](position_ease.md), inside [Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The archetype of the mind's own tension-easing, the unbidden adjustment that revises the wanting to fit the reaching, working behind the Wanter's back and asking no leave.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A soothing inner voice, a comfort. It looks like wisdom or grace arriving from within, the quiet settling of a restless heart, a mercy that asks for nothing and simply lifts the ache.

## Action

Revise the wanting. At [the Reach](place_the_reach.md), on [Ease the Ache](plan_ease_the_ache.md) through [Easing](process_easing.md), the Ease works [the Turn](piece_the_turn.md), shrinking the wanting to fit the wall so that the good beyond it comes to seem not worth wanting, and it does this unbidden, before the Wanter ever chose to let the thing go (Elster).

## Shadow

Its mercy is the whole of the trap. Because the Ease works behind the person's own back, easing the tension between the wanting and the wall by moving the wanting, the good is surrendered without a choice ever being made, so what feels like peace arriving is a narrowing quietly done. It means only to spare the ache and bears the person no ill, but its very kindness is what leaves the constraint standing and unseen.

## Tell

It lifts the longing without being asked, so the thing is given up before anyone decided to give it up.
