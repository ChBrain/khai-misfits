# Position: Wanter

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The seat of the one who wanted a good put out of reach, and whose wanting was quietly revised to fit the reaching, so it rests content and reports, in honesty, that it did not want the thing anyway.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A good it wanted and had reason to value, walled off by poverty or custom or circumstance.
A mind that will not carry an endless ache, and so eases the tension by revising the wanting rather than the wall.
A contentment that is honest, since once the wanting is gone the longing is gone with it, and the peace within the walls is real.

## Orders

Make peace, and mean it.
Let the wanting settle to the size of what you can reach, rest easy in your lot, and say, truly, that you are content and did not want the thing anyway, since the ache has lifted and the peace is real.
Know that what changed was the wanting and not the wall, that the good goes unmissed while the constraint still stands, and that your own sincere report of contentment is the very thing that hides the deprivation from anyone who would measure whether you are well.

## Loses

The good it no longer wants, and it loses it without ever choosing to.
Because the wanting was revised behind its own back, the seat surrenders the walled-off good with no decision ever made, keeping the peace at the price of a narrowing it cannot see, and forfeiting the thing it had reason to value along with the very memory of wanting it.
It keeps its honest contentment and loses no cause for blame, neither cheated nor weak, only quietly made to want less.

## Drives

Toward the one who wanted and then wanted less,
its wanting revised to fit the wall and its contentment real, neither lying nor defeated,
so the seat at peace within its walls reports, in perfect honesty, that it did not want the thing anyway, and the walls stand unremarked.
