# Piece: The Contentment

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The honest satisfaction that follows the turn: the person wants less, and says, and means, that they are content and did not want the thing anyway.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Hearth](place_the_hearth.md), voiced by [The Wanter](persona_the_wanter.md).

## Load Bearing

The report is genuine, no lie anywhere in it. Wanting less is a real mercy, sparing a person the ache of a longing that could never have been met, and that is precisely why it is no safe guide to how the life actually fares: a wanting shrunk to fit a wall reads back as satisfaction no matter how narrow the wall has made the life (Nussbaum).

## Apparent

Contentment well earned. A soul at peace, the kind of ease that looks like the reward of good character rather than the residue of a constraint.

## Yearbook

Held by [The Wanter](persona_the_wanter.md) at [The Hearth](place_the_hearth.md), dramatized in [Content Within the Walls](plot_content_within_the_walls.md); it follows [The Turn](piece_the_turn.md) and it is what [The Masked Loss](piece_the_masked_loss.md) records as wellbeing.
