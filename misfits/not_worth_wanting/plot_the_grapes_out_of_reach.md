# Plot: The Grapes Out of Reach

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Reach](place_the_reach.md), a good is wanted, and beyond the wall. The reaching hand is still lifted, and nothing yet has moved to close the distance.

## Action

[The Wanter](persona_the_wanter.md), from [the wanter's seat](position_wanter.md), through [Reaching](process_reaching.md), wants [The Grapes](piece_the_grapes.md) at [The Reach](place_the_reach.md), a work or a freedom or a life, and finds it out of reach.

## Stage

The good is one the person had reason to value, not a whim picked up and set down, and the wanting is real before it ever shrinks: the whole weight of what follows rests on the grapes having been genuinely worth wanting first (Elster).

## Tension

The good is wanted, and it is walled off.
