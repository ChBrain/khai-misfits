# Plan: Ask What They Can Do

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the advocate's seat](position_advocate.md), inside [Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Advocate](position_advocate.md)

## Direction

Weigh the life by the capabilities the person actually holds, the doings and beings open to them, since these do not shrink when the wanting does, and do not let a reported contentment settle the matter. Know that to look past a person's own account is to risk telling them they are worse off than they feel, a paternalism that may override a real and freely-held want, and that you must draw by hand the line between a masked loss and a made peace, and may draw it wrong.

## Orders

([The Advocate](persona_the_advocate.md) holds [the Capability](piece_the_capability.md) through [Assaying](process_assaying.md) at [the Measure](place_the_measure.md), weighing the life by what the person is able to do and be rather than what they say they want.)

## Implementation

([Assaying](process_assaying.md) at [the Measure](place_the_measure.md) reads the capabilities and not the stated want, because what a person can do and be can be seen from outside and does not shrink with the wanting, so [the Capability](piece_the_capability.md) looks past the adapted contentment.)

## Targets

- [ ] the life is weighed by the capabilities the person holds, the doings and beings open to them
- [ ] the reported contentment is not taken as final, the stated want distrusted where it may be adapted
- [ ] the risk of paternalism is named, the danger of overruling a real and freely-held want
- [ ] the bound is held, the hard line between a masked loss and a made peace drawn by hand and fallible
