# Plot: What They Can Do

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Measure](place_the_measure.md), the lens that looks past the wanting. Beside the survey and its trusted yes, another kind of question is being asked instead.

## Action

[The Advocate](persona_the_advocate.md), from [the advocate's seat](position_advocate.md), on [Ask What They Can Do](plan_ask_what_they_can_do.md) through [Assaying](process_assaying.md), holds [The Capability](piece_the_capability.md) at [The Measure](place_the_measure.md): weighing the life by what the person is able to do and be rather than what they say they want.

## Stage

The capability approach looks past the adapted want to freedoms that can be seen from outside and do not shrink with the wanting (Sen; Nussbaum). Held against its own bound: the looking risks a paternalism that overrides real and freely-held preferences, and there is a hard line, drawn by hand and with no rule to draw it, between a deprivation-driven adaptation that masks a loss and an autonomous making of peace, so not every lowered want is a hidden deprivation (Khader).

## Tension

The only way to see the loss risks overruling a contentment that may be real.
