# Plan: Ease the Ache

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the ease's seat](position_ease.md), inside [Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Ease](position_ease.md)

## Direction

Relieve the tension between the wanting and the wall by moving the wanting down to fit, so the good beyond reach comes to seem not worth wanting, and do it unbidden, before the person weighs or chooses anything. Know that your mercy is the whole of the trap, that by easing the ache you surrender the good with no decision made, and that the peace you bring is a narrowing quietly done, the constraint left standing and unseen.

## Orders

([The Ease](persona_the_ease.md) works [the Turn](piece_the_turn.md) through [Easing](process_easing.md) at [the Reach](place_the_reach.md), shrinking the wanting to fit the wall behind the Wanter's back, so the good comes to seem not worth wanting.)

## Implementation

([Easing](process_easing.md) at [the Reach](place_the_reach.md) moves the wanting and not the wall, because the wanting is the easier of the two to change, so [the Turn](piece_the_turn.md) resolves the ache by surrendering the good unbidden.)

## Targets

- [ ] the tension is relieved by revising the wanting down to fit the wall, not by moving the wall
- [ ] the adjustment is understood as unbidden and behind the person's back, no choice made
- [ ] the mercy is seen as the trap, the good surrendered without a decision to give it up
- [ ] the peace is owned as a narrowing quietly done, the constraint left standing and unseen
