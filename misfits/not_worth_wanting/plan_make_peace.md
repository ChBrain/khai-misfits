# Plan: Make Peace

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the wanter's seat](position_wanter.md), inside [Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Wanter](position_wanter.md)

## Direction

Let the wanting settle to the size of what you can reach, rest easy in your lot, and say, truly, that you are content and did not want the thing anyway, since the ache has lifted and the peace is real. Know that what changed was the wanting and not the wall, that the good goes unmissed while the constraint still stands, and that your own sincere report of contentment is the very thing that hides the deprivation from anyone who would measure whether you are well.

## Orders

([The Wanter](persona_the_wanter.md), its wanting revised by [the Turn](piece_the_turn.md), holds [the Contentment](piece_the_contentment.md) at [the Hearth](place_the_hearth.md), resting easy and reporting in honesty that it did not want the thing anyway.)

## Implementation

([The Contentment](piece_the_contentment.md) at [the Hearth](place_the_hearth.md) follows the revised wanting, because once the wanting is gone the longing is gone with it, so the peace within the walls is real and honestly reported.)
