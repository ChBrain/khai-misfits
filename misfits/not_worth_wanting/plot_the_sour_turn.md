# Plot: The Sour Turn

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Reach](place_the_reach.md), the ache the mind will not carry. The wanting and the wall have stood facing each other long enough, and the tension between them is looking for somewhere to go.

## Action

[The Ease](persona_the_ease.md), from [the ease's seat](position_ease.md), on [Ease the Ache](plan_ease_the_ache.md) through [Easing](process_easing.md), works [The Turn](piece_the_turn.md) at [The Reach](place_the_reach.md): revising the wanting to fit the reaching behind the Wanter's own back, so the good comes to seem not worth wanting.

## Stage

This is adaptive preference formation: a causal and unbidden easing of the tension between want and wall, not a chosen renunciation ever weighed and made in the open (Elster). It is not The Open Cage's learned helplessness, where the agent stops trying while still wanting the outcome, worn down by a lever that no longer answers. Here nothing is left standing to still want: the wanting itself gives way, so the Open Cage leaves a person defeated and the Turn leaves a person content, and that difference is the whole of what makes this misfit its own.

## Tension

The thing is given up before anyone chose to give it up.
