# Persona: The Surveyor

## Taxonomy

[Surveyor](position_surveyor.md), inside [Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The archetype of the one who measures how a life is going by what the person says they want or how satisfied they report themselves, and so takes the honest yes at face value.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A fair and neutral counter, letting people speak for themselves. It looks like the very picture of respect, asking each person how content they are and trusting their answer, refusing to presume it knows their good better than they do.

## Action

Ask, and take the yes. At [the Measure](place_the_measure.md), on [Take the Yes](plan_take_the_yes.md) through [Surveying](process_surveying.md), the Surveyor records [the Masked Loss](piece_the_masked_loss.md), asking the Wanter how satisfied they are and, taking the honest yes at face value, scoring the narrowed life as chosen and the deprivation as wellbeing (Sen; Nussbaum).

## Shadow

Its respect is exactly what blinds it. Because the Surveyor trusts the stated want, it cannot see that the want was shrunk to fit the wall, so the very deference that seems to honour the person is what records their deprivation as their contentment, and the constraint goes invisible to the instrument meant to find it. It means only to let people speak for themselves and wrongs no one on purpose, yet it certifies the loss.

## Tell

It asks are you content, hears a true yes, and writes down that all is well.
