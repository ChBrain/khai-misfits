# Piece: The Turn

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The engine of the misfit: the unconscious revision by which the wanting shrinks to fit the reaching, so the unattainable good comes to seem not worth wanting at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Reach](place_the_reach.md), carried by [Easing](process_easing.md), voiced by [The Ease](persona_the_ease.md).

## Load Bearing

Adaptive preference formation: a causal easing of the tension between want and wall, worked behind the person's own back and not a considered renunciation. No one chooses to give the grapes up; the wanting is revised before any such choice could be made, and the person who ends up content never felt themselves decide (Elster).

This is not The Open Cage's learned helplessness, where the agent stops trying while still wanting the outcome, caged by a dead lever it no longer bothers to pull. Here the wanting itself is what gives way: nothing is left standing to still want. The Open Cage leaves a person defeated; the Turn leaves a person content, and the difference is the whole of what makes this misfit its own.

## Apparent

Wisdom, or grace. It reads as a restless heart quietly settling, the kind of maturity that is praised: someone who has stopped chasing what was never meant for them.
