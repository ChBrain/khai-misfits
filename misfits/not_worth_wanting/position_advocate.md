# Position: Advocate

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The seat of the one who looks past what people say they want to what they are able to do and be, and who marks that the looking risks paternalism and demands the hard line between a masked loss and a made peace.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A better measure to hand: what a person is actually able to do and be, the capabilities open to them, which can be seen from outside and do not shrink with the wanting.
A distrust of the stated want, knowing it may have been revised to fit the wall, so the reported contentment is no safe guide to how a life fares.
A hard line to draw with no rule to draw it, between the deprivation-driven adaptation that masks a loss and the autonomous making of peace that is part of a good life.

## Orders

Ask what they can do, not what they say they want.
Weigh the life by the capabilities the person actually holds, the doings and beings open to them, since these do not shrink when the wanting does, and do not let a reported contentment settle the matter.
Know that to look past a person's own account is to risk telling them they are worse off than they feel, a paternalism that may override a real and freely-held want, and that you must draw by hand the line between a masked loss and a made peace, and may draw it wrong.

## Loses

The safety of the plain answer, and it takes on the risk of judging for another.
Because it will not take the stated want as final, the seat gives up the neutral ground the survey stands on and shoulders the danger of overruling a sincere contentment, buying a truer measure at the price of a paternalism it cannot fully escape.
It keeps its clear sight and its good intent and loses no cause for blame, a champion who sees the loss but cannot always prove it.

## Drives

Toward the one who measures the doing and not the wanting,
weighing capabilities that do not shrink with the want and distrusting the reported yes, at the risk of paternalism,
so the seat that alone can see past the adapted contentment must draw by hand the line between a masked loss and a made peace, and may draw it wrong.
