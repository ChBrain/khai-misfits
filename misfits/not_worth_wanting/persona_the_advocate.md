# Persona: The Advocate

## Taxonomy

[Advocate](position_advocate.md), inside [Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The archetype of the one who looks past what people say they want to what they are able to do and be, and who marks that the looking risks paternalism and demands the hard line between a masked loss and a made peace.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A champion of the overlooked, seeing further than the survey. It looks like the clear-eyed friend who will not be fooled by a brave face, insisting on real freedom and not mere reported contentment.

## Action

Measure the doing, not the wanting. At [the Measure](place_the_measure.md), on [Ask What They Can Do](plan_ask_what_they_can_do.md) through [Assaying](process_assaying.md), the Advocate holds [the Capability](piece_the_capability.md), weighing the life by what the person is actually able to do and be rather than what they say they want, since capabilities can be seen from outside and do not shrink with the wanting (Sen; Nussbaum).

## Shadow

Its clear sight carries its own danger. To look past a person's sincere account of their contentment is to risk telling them they are worse off than they feel, a paternalism that overrides real and freely-held wants, and the Advocate has no rule that tells a deprivation-driven adaptation from an autonomous making of peace, only a hard judgement to make case by case. It means well and sees truly, but it stands on a line it must draw by hand, and may draw wrong.

## Tell

It will not take the yes for an answer, and it cannot always prove the yes was false.
