# Piece: The Grapes

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The good a person wants and finds out of reach: a work, a freedom, a life they had real reason to value, not a whim picked up and set down.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Reach](place_the_reach.md), wanted through [Reaching](process_reaching.md), voiced by [The Wanter](persona_the_wanter.md).

## Load Bearing

The fable's grapes: a good foreclosed by circumstance, real and worth having before the wanting shrinks to fit what is left. It is a good with standing, chosen for reasons that would survive scrutiny, not an idle craving; the whole weight of what follows rests on the grapes having been genuinely worth wanting before the wanting itself gives way (Elster).

## Apparent

An ordinary thing. Nothing exotic in it, nothing anyone would think twice about wanting: work worth doing, a door worth walking through, a life worth having.

## Yearbook

Wanted by [The Wanter](persona_the_wanter.md) through [Reaching](process_reaching.md) at [The Reach](place_the_reach.md), dramatized in [The Grapes Out of Reach](plot_the_grapes_out_of_reach.md); it is foreclosed by [The Wall](piece_the_wall.md) and it is what [The Turn](piece_the_turn.md) reframes as not worth wanting.
