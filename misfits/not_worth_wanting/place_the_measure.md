# Place: The Measure

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). Where a life is assessed, by what the person says they want or by what they are able to do and be. Hosts [Counted as Content](plot_counted_as_content.md), [What They Can Do](plot_what_they_can_do.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The survey, asking how content a person is. Beside it, the capability list: the doings and beings a life could hold, set down whether or not they are wanted.

## Holds

[The Masked Loss](piece_the_masked_loss.md), [The Capability](piece_the_capability.md).

## Offers
