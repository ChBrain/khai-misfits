# Piece: The Capability

## Taxonomy

[Not Worth Wanting](play_not_worth_wanting.md). The remedy and its bound: measuring a life by what the person is actually able to do and be, rather than by what they say they want.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Measure](place_the_measure.md), carried by [Assaying](process_assaying.md), voiced by [The Advocate](persona_the_advocate.md).

## Load Bearing

The capability approach looks past the adapted want to the freedoms a person actually holds, freedoms that can be seen from outside and that do not shrink just because the wanting has (Sen; Nussbaum).

It carries its own bound. To look past a person's sincere contentment risks a paternalism that overrides preferences that are real and freely held, and there is a hard line, drawn by hand and with no rule to draw it by, between a deprivation-driven adaptation that masks a loss and an autonomous making of peace that belongs in any good life. Not every lowered want is a hidden deprivation (Khader).

## Apparent

Needless second-guessing. It reads as an outsider refusing to take a content person at their word, doubting a peace that was never theirs to doubt.
