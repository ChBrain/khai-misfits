# Position: Contender

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The seat of the competitor drawn to the center, whose best reply to where the rival stands is always to edge inward, so that two rivals reading the same spread-out market both settle at the middle, alike where it wanted them apart.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The power to win by moving, and no way to win by staying put: the freedom to choose its position along the market, and a rival choosing too, so that where it stands is only ever right relative to where the other is.
A sound reason to move inward: edging toward the center keeps every customer behind it, who still have no one nearer, and takes a share of those between the rivals, so the move wins the whole span for the loss of only the thin tail.
No winning distinct position, because holding its own ground cedes the middle, and the middle is most of the market.

## Orders

Take the center.
Move toward the median, best-replying to the rival, because that is the position that captures the larger side of any split and the one no distinct stand can beat while the rival is free to take it.
Accept the arrival: that two seats both following this order end up together at the middle, indistinguishable, the sameness the equilibrium and not a failure to be avoided.

## Loses

Its distinct ground, given up to win.
Because the pull that makes the center its best reply is the same pull on the rival, the seat cannot hold a position of its own and still compete, so it trades the variety it might have offered for the share the center commands, and lands beside its rival offering the same thing.
It loses nothing it could have kept while winning, and takes the whole market's variety down with it, the ends left to no one because both seats are at the middle.

## Drives

Toward the competitor whose winning move is the one that abandons its own position,
edging inward soundly because the center is where the win is and the rival will take it otherwise,
so the seat that competes best is the seat that ends up alike, its distinct offering foregone not by weakness but by the plain logic of where the customers are.
