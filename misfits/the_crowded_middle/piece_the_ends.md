# Piece: The Ends

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The social cost: the dispersed extremes of [The Line](piece_the_line.md) left unserved once both competitors have moved to [The Center](place_the_center.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Left behind at [The Margin](place_the_margin.md), the far edges of the Line no one stands on once [The Huddle](piece_the_huddle.md) has formed.
Not a place anyone is driven from directly: it empties because attention and position both move toward the middle, and nothing is left standing at the edges to hold it.

## Load Bearing

This is the load-bearing harm of the whole misfit: the lost variety and coverage, the gap between the center that competition actually reaches and the quartiles that would minimize buyers' total travel across the Line (Hotelling), and the caveat Downs adds, that it is exactly this abandonment of the alienated extremes that gives them a lever: to stay home, to walk, to not buy.
It is NOT wasted effort. Both competitors work exactly as hard huddled at the center as they would spread across the Line; nothing is squandered on the sellers' side.
It is NOT a lowered standard either. Neither offering gets worse at what it does; each is fully competent at serving the middle.
Guard against locating the harm in the competitors. Their positions are individually optimal, chosen correctly given what a Move at the Center wins. The loss falls on the customers and voters at the extremes, who wanted something the huddle no longer offers, and on the variety that a spread-out market never gets built at all.

## Apparent

A few marginal customers, easily written off: the kind of thin, unprofitable custom no seller optimizes for on purpose.

## Yearbook
