# Position: Pivot

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The seat at the median of the spread, as many on one side as the other, whose position is the point that beats any other under majority rule, so both rivals are drawn to court it and it is the best served of all (Black).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A position that decides, and no need to move to use it: the place at the middle of the market where half the tastes lie to each side, which is the point a rival must hold to win the larger side of any split.
A pull it exerts without acting: because the seat's position is the one no majority can defeat, it sets the target both rivals aim for, and they converge on it while it merely stands where it is.
No cost to being courted, only the fortune of standing where winning is, so the competition shapes itself to fit it.

## Orders

Hold the balance.
Stand at the median and let the contest come, because the position that decides the outcome is the position both offerings must reach, so the seat wins its perfect service by doing nothing but occupying the point that matters.
Take the service as the market working: from the middle both rivals look attentive and near, and there is no view from here of the ends they abandoned to arrive.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and yet the whole market's variety turns on it.
Because both rivals shape themselves to the median, the entire offering bends to this one seat's position, so the seat that loses nothing is the seat whose centrality costs everyone else, the coverage at the edges given up so the middle can be courted.
It keeps its perfect fit and cannot see the price, the variety lost at the ends invisible from the center it occupies.

## Drives

Toward the seat the competition serves best because it happens to stand where winning is,
courted without lifting a hand, decisive without deciding,
so the position that draws both rivals to itself is the position whose pull empties the ends, the fit it enjoys and the coverage the market loses the same convergence seen from the middle.
