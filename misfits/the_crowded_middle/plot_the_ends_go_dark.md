# Plot: The Ends Go Dark

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Margin](place_the_margin.md): the customer at the far end, the voter at the extreme, looking down the length of the street at two offerings that both sit far away, huddled at the center.

## Action

Through [Abandoning](process_abandoning.md), [The Ends](piece_the_ends.md) go unserved. Both competitors are chasing the median, and the variety and coverage the spread-out market wanted was never on either of their routes.
No one decided to leave the ends dark. It is simply what remains once every rational step has pointed the other way.
Closes with the far end served by no one, and no one on the street responsible for it.

## Stage

Hotelling's quartile social optimum, the arrangement that would minimize buyers' total travel, sits far from the center the competition actually reaches. The gap between the two is the load-bearing harm.
It falls on no one's account. Each competitor's position is individually optimal, chosen correctly given what the center wins, so this is not The Lowest Bar: no standard has slipped, nothing has been lowered, only the ends left standing where the huddle no longer reaches.

## Tension

A real loss with no culprit, invisible for the simple reason that it is variety that was never offered.
