# Piece: The Line

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The dimension of preference the market is spread across: the street, the flavor, the policy axis along which position is chosen and customers are distributed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Laid out whole at [The Street](place_the_street.md), end to end, before either competitor stands anywhere on it.
The Line is not created there so much as revealed there: the street is the ground on which its length becomes a place two can occupy.

## Load Bearing

Customers are spread along it, uniformly or smoothly (Hotelling), and each buys from the nearer offering: distance on the Line is the whole of the choice, nothing else differentiates one seller from another.
This is the terrain the sellers move across, not an actor in its own right; it does no choosing.
But its shape is exactly what pulls them: where the mass of customers sits on the Line determines where standing wins the most, and that is what draws every best reply toward the center.
Guard against reading the Line as neutral scenery. It looks like a plain map of tastes laid out for reference, but the distribution along it is the entire cause of the convergence that follows: a Line with the mass humped at the middle produces the pull toward the middle, and a Line shaped otherwise would produce a different pull. The terrain is inert only in the sense that it does not move; it is never inert in the sense of not mattering.

## Apparent

A plain map of tastes: here is what people want, spread out for anyone to read.

## Yearbook

Along it [The Move](piece_the_move.md) happens, each competitor's best reply edging inward.
At its center [The Median](piece_the_median.md) sits, the point that captures more of the Line than any other.
Its ends are [The Ends](piece_the_ends.md), the sparse tails a reposition toward the middle gives up in exchange for the larger span it wins.
