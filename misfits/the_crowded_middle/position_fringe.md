# Position: Fringe

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The seat at the end of the spread, the edge of the range, taken for granted before the rivals converge and abandoned after, whose one lever is to refuse the offering, and whose number is the variety the market has lost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A distinct want and no near offering: a taste at the edge of the market, as real as any at the center but rarer, and two rivals both sitting far away at the middle.
One lever only, the refusal: because it cannot draw the rivals by wanting, the seat can send just one signal, to withhold itself and go unserved rather than take the distant middle.
No pull of its own while it stays, because sparse custom at the edge is a rounding error to a rival chasing the mass, so its taste moves nothing until its absence is felt.

## Orders

Threaten to walk.
Refuse the offering that is not near enough, and where abstention or exit is possible and felt, leave, so that a rival counting on the edge feels the ground shift and edges back to hold it (Downs).
Know the limit: the lever opens the huddle only a little, and only where the walk can be made and is noticed, and where it cannot the convergence stands and the seat is simply left.

## Loses

The offering fitted to its taste, lost before it was ever made.
Because both rivals rationally chase the median, the seat's distinct want is served by neither, so what it loses is not a deal struck badly but a variety never offered, present only in the count of the market's abandoned range.
It keeps its taste and loses any near answer to it, disciplining the middle only by the one refusal it can make, and only so far.

## Drives

Toward the seat the pull to the center leaves at the edge with no one,
counted on to take what is nearest and offered nothing near, able to signal only by its absence,
so the seat with the most distinct want is the seat the competition most reliably abandons, its discontent read as apathy or extremity when it is a taste the market has simply left unserved.
