# Persona: The Pivot

## Taxonomy

[Pivot](position_pivot.md). The one in the middle of the spread, as many on one side as the other, whose position is the point that beats any other under majority rule, so both rivals are drawn to court it and it is the best served of all (Black).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Central, decisive, comfortable.
Sits at the median of the market, the customer with half the tastes to one side and half to the other, and finds both rivals arriving to offer exactly what it wants.
Nothing grasping in it: it did not ask to be courted, it simply stands where the mass is, and the point where the mass is happens to be the point that wins, so the competition comes to it.

## Action

Holds the balance on [Hold the Balance](plan_hold_the_balance.md), through [Crowding](process_crowding.md) at [the Center](place_the_center.md), sitting at [the Median](piece_the_median.md), the position no majority can defeat, so both rivals must reach it or lose.
Does nothing to draw them but stand where it is: because a rival that wins the median wins the larger side of any split, the median's position sets the target, and the two converge on it without it lifting a hand.
Is served best precisely because it is decisive: the point that decides the contest is the point both offerings are shaped to fit.

## Shadow

Not a manipulator for being courted and not owed its good fortune: the Pivot is well served because it happens to stand where winning is, and the same convergence that serves it so well is what abandons the ends.
Because both rivals shape themselves to the median, the market's whole offering bends to one customer's position, so the figure best served by the huddle is the figure whose centrality caused it, and the coverage lost at the edges is the price of the fit it enjoys.
It is the one the competition serves perfectly, and the one whose place at the center is why everyone else is served worse.

## Tell

Finds both offerings fit it exactly, and takes that as the market working, not as the market narrowing.
Cannot see the cost from where it sits, because from the median both rivals look attentive and close, and the customers at the ends, offered nothing near them, are out of its view, well served itself while the variety at the edges quietly goes.
