# Piece: The Huddle

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The clustered outcome: both competitors together at the middle, alike where the spread-out market wanted them apart.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Formed at [The Center](place_the_center.md), the point on [The Line](piece_the_line.md) where the last Move from each side lands and neither finds a further reply worth making.
It is reached, not built: no one designs the Huddle, it is simply where the sequence of moves stops improving on itself.

## Load Bearing

The Huddle is Hotelling's result named by Boulding: minimum differentiation, two sellers stable at the center though the arrangement that would minimize buyers' total travel puts them at the quartiles instead (Hotelling). It is the sum of every prior [Move](piece_the_move.md), sameness as equilibrium and not as error: each competitor's best reply, once both have made it, leaves neither able to do better by moving alone.
Guard against reading the sameness as laziness, cowardice, or conspiracy. It is what each side's own rational self-interest produces, not a failure of nerve or a secret arrangement: unilaterally leaving the Huddle costs the mover the larger half of the market it currently holds, so staying is the individually correct choice for both, even though the joint result serves the market worse than either could see from where it stands.

## Apparent

Two similar choices, which on the surface looks like plenty: competition, options, a market working as intended.
