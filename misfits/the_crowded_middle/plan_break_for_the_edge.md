# Plan: Break for the Edge

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the maverick's seat](position_maverick.md), inside [The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Maverick](position_maverick.md)

## Direction

Take the ground the huddle left open. Serve the abandoned end with something genuinely different, and accept the smaller market as the price of the distinct one, since to serve the end is to forgo the center's mass. Do not ask the two rivals to spread, because their convergence is each one's best reply; change the game instead, by being the position they abandoned, by making the difference real enough to keep its own customers, or by backing a rule that rewards distinctness (Downs), knowing that under a steep enough travel cost the rivals spread on their own (d'Aspremont, Gabszewicz, and Thisse).

## Orders

([The Maverick](persona_the_maverick.md) takes [the Spread](piece_the_spread.md) through [Spreading](process_spreading.md) along [the Street](place_the_street.md), differentiating to serve the end the huddle left open, trading the crowded center for a distinct hold.)

## Implementation

([Spreading](process_spreading.md) carries the seat away from the center back toward the abandoned end, taking [the Spread](piece_the_spread.md); the variety is restored at the price of the mass forgone, and the hold rests on whether the end can sustain it, so the correction is real but costly and never a return to a costless middle, proof the huddle was a result under assumptions and not a law.)

## Targets

- [ ] the abandoned end is served, the seat differentiating rather than crowding the center
- [ ] the smaller market is accepted as the price of the distinct one, the center's mass forgone
- [ ] the game is changed where standing apart alone will not hold: a third entrant, real difference, or a rule that rewards distinctness
- [ ] the variety is restored at a cost, the convergence shown conditional and not an iron law
