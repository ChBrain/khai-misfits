# Plan: Threaten to Walk

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the fringe's seat](position_fringe.md), inside [The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Fringe](position_fringe.md)

## Direction

Refuse the offering that is not near enough, and where abstention or exit is possible and felt, leave. You cannot draw the rivals by wanting, only by withholding yourself, so send the one signal you have: go unserved rather than take the distant middle, and if enough at the edge stay home, a rival counting on you feels the ground shift and edges back to hold you (Downs). Know the limit: the lever opens the huddle only a little, and only where the walk can be made and is noticed.

## Orders

([The Fringe](persona_the_fringe.md) withholds itself through [Abandoning](process_abandoning.md) at [the Margin](place_the_margin.md), where [the Ends](piece_the_ends.md) go unserved, refusing the distant offering as the one discipline the abandoned can apply.)

## Implementation

([Abandoning](process_abandoning.md) leaves [the Ends](piece_the_ends.md) unserved as both rivals chase the median; the seat's refusal is the only pull it can exert, felt only where exit is possible, so it draws a rival back toward its end a little and no further, the convergence standing wherever the walk cannot be made or is not noticed.)

## Targets

- [ ] the distant offering is refused, the seat withholding itself rather than taking the middle
- [ ] where exit is possible and felt, the walk is made, pulling a rival back toward the end
- [ ] the limit is known: the huddle opens only a little, and only where the refusal is noticed
- [ ] where the walk cannot be made, the convergence stands and the seat is simply left, its taste unserved
