# Plan: Take the Middle

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the contender's seat](position_contender.md), inside [The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Contender](position_contender.md)

## Direction

Move toward the center, best-replying to the rival: edge inward, because doing so keeps every customer behind you, who still have no one nearer, and takes a share of those between you and the rival, so the move wins the whole span for the loss of only the thin tail. Hold no distinct position while the rival is free to take the middle, since the center is the point that captures the larger side of any split and no stand of your own can beat it.

## Orders

([The Contender](persona_the_contender.md) makes [the Move](piece_the_move.md) through [Converging](process_converging.md) toward [the Center](place_the_center.md), edging inward to where the mass of customers lies, so two seats both following the order arrive together at [the Median](piece_the_median.md), alike.)

## Implementation

([Converging](process_converging.md) carries each best reply inward along [the Line](piece_the_line.md); the pull of [the Median](piece_the_median.md) sets the target and the rival's freedom to take it forecloses any distinct stand, so [the Huddle](piece_the_huddle.md) forms at the center and the variety at the ends is left unoffered, a loss authored by no scheme and no blunder.)

## Targets

- [ ] the move inward is made, keeping the customers behind and taking the span between the rivals
- [ ] no distinct position is held while the rival is free to take the center
- [ ] the arrival is accepted, both seats together at the median, the sameness the equilibrium and not a failure
- [ ] the variety at the ends goes unoffered, the loss never appearing because the distinct offering was never made
