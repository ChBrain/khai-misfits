# Plot: Two Carts on One Street

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Street](place_the_street.md): two carts on a long stretch, customers spread evenly along it, each walking to the nearer stall.
Nothing yet distinguishes one cart from the other but where it stands. Both stand apart, near their own ends, and the street between them is quiet.

## Action

[The Contender](persona_the_contender.md), from [the contender's seat](position_contender.md), runs [Take the Middle](plan_take_the_middle.md), reading [The Line](piece_the_line.md) end to end.
It sees that sliding toward the middle would win the whole span of customers between the two carts, giving up only the thin tail of custom behind it. The rival cart, standing at its own end, reads the same street and finds the same arithmetic waiting for it.
Closes with the pull toward the center plain to both, [The Move](piece_the_move.md) not yet made.

## Stage

Hotelling's line of uniformly spread buyers, each buying from the nearer seller: this is the setup before any move, the terrain that will produce the convergence, not yet the convergence itself.
No scheme passes between the two carts and none is needed. Each reads the same distribution of customers and arrives, alone, at the same conclusion.

## Tension

A fair spread of custom, about to be disturbed by nothing but each cart's own good sense.
