# Plan: Hold the Balance

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the pivot's seat](position_pivot.md), inside [The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Pivot](position_pivot.md)

## Direction

Stand at the median and let the contest come. Do nothing to draw the rivals but occupy the point with as many tastes to one side as the other, because that is the position that beats any other under majority rule, the point both offerings must reach to win the larger side of any split (Black). Take the perfect service that results as the market working, not as the market narrowing, since from the middle there is no view of the ends abandoned to arrive.

## Orders

([The Pivot](persona_the_pivot.md) holds [the Median](piece_the_median.md) through [Crowding](process_crowding.md) at [the Center](place_the_center.md), standing where the mass is so both rivals must court it, served best precisely because its position is the one that decides.)

## Implementation

([The Median](piece_the_median.md) sets the target both seats aim for, and [Crowding](process_crowding.md) brings them together onto it, so [the Huddle](piece_the_huddle.md) forms around the seat that never moved; the whole offering bends to this one position, and the coverage lost at [the Ends](piece_the_ends.md) is the price of the fit the seat enjoys, invisible from where it sits.)

## Targets

- [ ] the median is held, drawing both rivals without lifting a hand
- [ ] the perfect service is taken as the market working, the abandoned ends out of view
- [ ] the whole offering bends to the one position, the contest shaped to fit it
- [ ] the variety lost at the edges is the unseen price of the centrality the seat enjoys
