# Process: Spreading

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). Borne at [The Street](place_the_street.md), precipitated by [The Maverick](persona_the_maverick.md) differentiating and taking [The Spread](piece_the_spread.md), in [The One Who Broke Ranks](plot_the_one_who_broke_ranks.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A differentiator entering the huddle, a third competitor joining the race, or a cost of travel steep enough to punish closeness.
Any one of these is enough to start positions moving apart again.

## Direction

From the huddle at the middle back toward distinct positions, in the extreme case all the way to maximal differentiation.
It reverses converging outright: the same line, the same competitors, now spreading rather than massing.

## Lever

Product differentiation, added entrants, and proportional representation pulling the alienated back in (Downs), and above all the correction that under quadratic transport costs sellers repel to the extremes rather than cluster at the center, Hotelling's own convergence result reversing entirely (d'Aspremont, Gabszewicz, and Thisse).
This is what keeps minimum differentiation conditional rather than a law: it holds where position is chosen and price or platform is fixed, and it reverses where that is not so.

## Echo

[The Spread](piece_the_spread.md).
What spreading leaves behind is coverage restored at the cost of the mass the center used to guarantee.
