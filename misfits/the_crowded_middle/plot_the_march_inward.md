# Plot: The March Inward

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Street](place_the_street.md): each cart edges a step toward the middle, and then the other answers with a step of its own.
No word passes between them. Each simply reads where the rival now stands and replies.

## Action

[The Contender](persona_the_contender.md), from [the contender's seat](position_contender.md), runs [Take the Middle](plan_take_the_middle.md) through [Converging](process_converging.md), making [The Move](piece_the_move.md) again and again, each time best-replying to wherever the other has just landed.
Every step captures real span: the customers between the old position and the new one, won outright. Neither cart is chasing a phantom; each gain is a real gain, right up until the next step erases it and calls for another.
Closes with both settled together at [The Median](piece_the_median.md), side by side where the street once held two separate positions.

## Stage

Best-reply dynamics, one seller answering the other's current position until neither can improve by moving alone (Hotelling).
Each step here is locally correct and share-capturing, which sets it apart from The Arms Race, where the moves cancel and the net is only effort spent. It is also not Information Cascade: neither cart is reading the other's move as a signal to imitate, each is computing its own best reply to the same distribution of customers, and would arrive at the same step even blind to why the rival stood where it did.

## Tension

Every move correct on its own terms, and the sum a huddle that neither cart set out to build.
