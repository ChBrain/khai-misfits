# Piece: The Median

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The attractor: the point on [The Line](piece_the_line.md) that captures the most of the market and that majority rule selects outright.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Found at [The Center](place_the_center.md), not built there: it is where the distribution and the voting rule already put it before anyone moves.
[The Pivot](persona_the_pivot.md) stands for it, the customer or voter whose position the Median names.

## Load Bearing

The median, not the mean: under single-peaked preferences and majority rule, the median voter's ideal is the Condorcet winner, the one position no other can beat in a head-to-head vote (Black). On a market Line it is the point that wins the larger share of any split, which is why every best reply bends toward it (Hotelling), and it is the point two vote-maximizing parties converge on and come to resemble (Downs).
Guard against reading the Median as a compromise anyone chose or a fair blend struck between extremes. No one selected it and no negotiation produced it: it is simply where the mass of customers or voters already sits, and where the winning majority already is. Calling it a compromise credits deliberation to an outcome that arithmetic alone determines.

## Apparent

Just the middle of the road: the safe, moderate spot anyone sensible would pick.
