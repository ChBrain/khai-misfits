# Place: The Margin

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The abandoned edges of the line, furthest from wherever the competitors have converged. Hosts [The Ends Go Dark](plot_the_ends_go_dark.md), [The Threat to Stay Home](plot_the_threat_to_stay_home.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The customer at the far end served by no one, and the voter at the extreme whose preference nobody courts.
Both stand at the edge of the line watching the competitors huddle somewhere else entirely.

## Holds

[The Ends](piece_the_ends.md), the variety and coverage the dispersed market wanted and lost, and the one lever left to it: abstention or exit.
What the margin holds is not a demand unmet by accident; it is the tail every best reply chose to give up.

## Offers

To the Fringe, only the choice to endure the poor fit or to walk.
To a Maverick willing to stand here, an unserved market with no one else on it.

## Withheld

An offering fitted to the end rather than to the median.
Nothing at the margin is built for the edge; whatever reaches this far was built for the middle and simply carries.
