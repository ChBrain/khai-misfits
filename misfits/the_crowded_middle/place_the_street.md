# Place: The Street

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The spatial line itself, Hotelling's Main Street, where position is location and nothing else. Hosts [Two Carts on One Street](plot_two_carts_on_one_street.md), [The March Inward](plot_the_march_inward.md), [The One Who Broke Ranks](plot_the_one_who_broke_ranks.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two vendors on a line of evenly spread customers, each customer walking to the nearer stall.
The whole length of the street is visible at once: thin custom at both ends, thick custom nowhere until someone stands there.

## Holds

[The Line](piece_the_line.md), the dimension of preference laid out end to end, and the sparse tails at each far edge.
What the street holds latent is the whole span neither vendor is standing on yet.

## Offers

To each competitor, a position to choose along the line, and nothing else to compete on.
Whoever stands nearer the middle takes the larger half of the street; the offer is remade every time either one moves.

## Withheld

Any way to hold the middle without leaving an end open to no one.
The street gives each vendor only one line to stand on, so covering the center and covering the edges are never both available to the same stall.
