# Process: Abandoning

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). Borne at [The Margin](place_the_margin.md), precipitated by [The Contender](persona_the_contender.md) chasing the median, in [The Ends Go Dark](plot_the_ends_go_dark.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The convergence already underway at the center.
Nobody decides to abandon the ends; the ends are simply what is left once both competitors stop chasing them.

## Direction

From covered ends to uncovered ones.
It runs outward from the center in reverse: the ground the competitors vacate on their way toward the median.

## Lever

The median holds the mass and the ends hold only the sparse tail, and the social optimum that would minimize buyers' travel sits at the quartiles, not the point competition actually reaches (Hotelling).
What is efficient for the whole street and what is rational for each competitor are two different points, and the market finds the second.

## Echo

[The Ends](piece_the_ends.md), the load-bearing harm: the variety and coverage the dispersed market wanted and did not get.
Nothing is wasted here and no standard is lowered; what is lost is simply left unserved.
