# Position: Maverick

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The seat that breaks from the huddle to serve the abandoned end, a third entrant or a rival willing to be different, restoring the variety the convergence lost, at the cost of the mass at the center.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A ground the others left, and a price to stand on it: the abandoned end is a real market, unserved by the two at the center, and the seat can take it by offering something genuinely different.
A trade, not a windfall: to serve the end is to forgo the middle, so the seat holds a smaller, surer market bought with the largest one, and it holds only where its difference is real and the end can sustain it.
No way to make the two rivals spread, because their convergence is each one's best reply, so the seat changes the market by being the position they abandoned, not by asking them to move.

## Orders

Break for the edge.
Take the ground the huddle left open, differentiating to serve the end rather than crowding the center, and accept the smaller market as the price of the distinct one.
Change the game where standing apart alone will not hold: add the third entrant the two cannot both crowd out, make the difference real enough to keep its own customers, or back a rule that rewards distinctness (Downs), knowing that under a steep enough travel cost the rivals spread on their own (d'Aspremont, Gabszewicz, and Thisse).

## Loses

The center's mass, given up to restore the variety.
Because the largest market is the one it forgoes to stand at the edge, the seat that reopens the range pays for it in the share it surrenders, so its stand is a trade and its hold uncertain, resting on whether the abandoned end is large enough to keep it.
It loses the middle and gains the variety the convergence lost, one market surrendered so the range can be served.

## Drives

Toward the seat that carries the lost variety back into the market and pays for carrying it,
standing where the mass is not, holding a real but smaller ground,
so the seat that breaks the crowded middle is the seat that proves it was a result under assumptions and not a law, the huddle staying crowded only while the conditions that crowd it hold.
