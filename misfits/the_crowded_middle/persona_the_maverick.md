# Persona: The Maverick

## Taxonomy

[Maverick](position_maverick.md). The one who breaks from the huddle to serve the abandoned end, a third entrant or a rival willing to be different, restoring the variety the convergence lost, at the cost of the mass at the center.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Distinct, deliberate, exposed.
Looks at the two rivals crowding the center and at the customers at the ends served by no one, and takes the ground the others left, offering something genuinely different.
No romance in it and no folly: it does not stand apart out of pride but because the abandoned end is a real market, and it knows the price of standing there is the center's mass it gives up to do so.

## Action

Breaks for the edge on [Break for the Edge](plan_break_for_the_edge.md), through [Spreading](process_spreading.md) along [the Street](place_the_street.md), taking [the Spread](piece_the_spread.md), differentiating to serve the end the huddle left open and trading the crowded center for a distinct hold.
Cannot restore the variety for free: to serve the end is to forgo the middle, so it takes a smaller, surer market at the cost of the largest one, and it holds only where its difference is real and the end it serves can sustain it.
Does what breaks the crowded middle: not by asking the others to spread, which they will not, but by being the position they abandoned, or by changing the game with a third entrant, real differentiation, or a rule that rewards distinctness (Downs).

## Shadow

Not a hero for breaking ranks and not certain to win: the Maverick restores variety, but it does so by paying the center's mass, so its stand is a trade and not a triumph, and whether it holds depends on whether the abandoned end is large enough to keep it.
Because the convergence was each rival's best reply, only a change in the game reverses it, so the figure that reopens the market is the figure that steps outside the two-rival logic, and the deepest form of its move is not a choice at all but a change in the conditions: under a steep enough travel cost the rivals fly apart on their own, maximally differentiated (d'Aspremont, Gabszewicz, and Thisse).
It is the one that carries the lost variety back into the market, and the one that pays for carrying it.

## Tell

Says the end is worth serving and serves it, and is called niche or marginal for standing where the mass is not.
Cannot be sure of the reward, because the variety it restores is bought with the center it forgoes, so it holds a real but smaller ground, proof that the huddle was a result under assumptions and not a law, and that the middle only stays crowded while the conditions that crowd it hold.
