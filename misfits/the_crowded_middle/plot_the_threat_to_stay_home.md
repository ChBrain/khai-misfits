# Plot: The Threat to Stay Home

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Margin](place_the_margin.md): the alienated extreme, offered nothing near what it wants, watching both carts hold the center and neither one turn its way.

## Action

[The Fringe](persona_the_fringe.md), from [the fringe's seat](position_fringe.md), runs [Threaten to Walk](plan_threaten_to_walk.md): abstaining, exiting, refusing the custom neither offering earned.
The loss registers. A contender that had counted the fringe's business, or its vote, in its total finds the total short, and edges back a step toward the end it had left dark.
Closes with the one discipline the ends hold shown in full, and its limit shown alongside it.

## Stage

Downs's caveat to the convergence: the abstention of alienated extremes pulls vote-maximizing parties back off the pure median, some distance toward the ends they had abandoned.
It is a real correction, not a full one. It works only where exit or abstention is actually possible, and it moves a contender only as far as losing the fringe outweighs holding the center, so the huddle loosens without breaking.

## Tension

The abandoned can discipline the huddle only by refusing it, and only so far as refusal costs the huddle more than it costs them.
