# Place: The Center

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The crowded convergence ground at the median, where both competitors end up standing. Hosts [Both Sound Alike](plot_both_sound_alike.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Both competitors arrived at the middle, near enough to touch, indistinguishable in what they offer.
Between them stands the Pivot, the median customer, courted and well-served from both sides at once.

## Holds

[The Median](piece_the_median.md), the attractor and the Condorcet winner no majority can beat (Black), and beneath it [The Huddle](piece_the_huddle.md).
What the center holds is the reason both arrived: the one point that wins against any position off it.

## Offers

The largest catchment on the whole line to whoever stands there.
Standing at the median wins the full span between wherever the two competitors are, which is why both are drawn to it and neither can afford to leave.

## Withheld

Room for two to stand there and still differ.
The center seats one attractor, not two; once both hold it, there is nothing left between them for a customer to choose on.
