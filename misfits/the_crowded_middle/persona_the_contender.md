# Persona: The Contender

## Taxonomy

[Contender](position_contender.md). The competitor drawn to the center, whose best reply to the rival is always to edge inward, so that two rivals reading the same spread-out market both end up at the middle, alike where the market wanted them apart.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Able, honest, competitive.
Reads the market as it is, sees where the customers are, and moves to win more of them, which is exactly what a good competitor does.
No cynicism in it and no laziness: it would gladly hold a distinct position if that were the way to win, and it can see, plainly, why holding one is the way to lose.

## Action

Takes the middle on [Take the Middle](plan_take_the_middle.md), through [Converging](process_converging.md) toward [the Center](place_the_center.md), making [the Move](piece_the_move.md), edging inward because the center wins the whole span between the rivals for the loss of only the thin tail behind.
Cannot win and stay distinct at once: to hold its own end is to hand the rival everything in the middle, which is most of the market, so the move that wins is the move that abandons its ground.
Does what a clear-eyed competitor does facing a rival that will take the center if it does not: it edges in too, and the two arrive together, alike.

## Shadow

Not a sellout for crowding the middle and not a fool if it refuses to: the Contender wins by moving to the center, and its move is the correct reply to where the rival stands.
Because the pull that makes the center its best reply is the same pull acting on the rival, the two cannot both hold distinct ground, so the competitor doing exactly what competition rewards is the competitor that ends up indistinguishable from its rival, and the sameness is the equilibrium, not a weakness in either.
It is the one that could have offered something of its own, and the one the logic of winning keeps from offering it.

## Tell

Says the distinct position would serve part of the market better, and takes the center anyway, because the center is where the win is.
Cannot be blamed for the move, because the pull it follows is real and the rival follows it too, so it competes soundly and lands at the middle, the variety it might have carried left unoffered.
