# Process: Converging

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). Borne at [The Center](place_the_center.md), precipitated by [The Contender](persona_the_contender.md) best-replying and making [The Move](piece_the_move.md), in [The March Inward](plot_the_march_inward.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Each competitor's best reply to wherever the other currently stands.
No signal passes between them; each reads only the rival's position and answers it.

## Direction

From spread positions toward the median, one best reply at a time.
It runs from wherever the two started, apart on the line, to the same point in the middle.

## Lever

The center wins the whole span between the two while giving up only the sparse tail beyond it (Hotelling).
Nothing is coordinated and nothing is copied; each move is independently and locally correct, which is what makes the pattern durable.

## Echo

[The Huddle](piece_the_huddle.md).
What converging leaves behind is two positions no longer different enough for a customer to tell apart.
