# Plot: The One Who Broke Ranks

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Street](place_the_street.md): a maverick, or a third entrant, eyeing the empty end of the street while the two carts stand crowded together at the middle.

## Action

[The Maverick](persona_the_maverick.md), from [the maverick's seat](position_maverick.md), runs [Break for the Edge](plan_break_for_the_edge.md) through [Spreading](process_spreading.md), taking [The Spread](piece_the_spread.md).
It differentiates outright, planting itself where the abandoned end has been waiting, and trades the center's mass for a distinct hold no one else on the street contests.
Closes with variety restored at the edge, at a price, and the convergence shown to depend on the conditions that produced it rather than to be fixed for all time.

## Stage

Product differentiation, added entrants, and proportional representation all restore some variety, each by changing the game rather than dissolving the pull toward the center, and each at a real cost (Downs).
The deeper correction is d'Aspremont, Gabszewicz, and Thisse's: under quadratic transport costs, sellers do not cluster at the center at all, they maximally differentiate, Hotelling's own convergence result reversing outright. Minimum differentiation is conditional on the model, position chosen and price or platform fixed, not a law that holds regardless.

## Tension

The huddle can be broken, but only by paying away the center's mass, or by changing the terms of the game itself.
