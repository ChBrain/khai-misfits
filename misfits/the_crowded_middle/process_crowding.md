# Process: Crowding

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). Borne at [The Center](place_the_center.md), precipitated by both contenders reaching the median, in [Both Sound Alike](plot_both_sound_alike.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Both competitors reaching the middle at the same time, or near enough.
The last gap closes, and nothing separates them but the sign above the stall.

## Direction

From two distinct offerings to one indistinguishable pair.
It moves the market from a choice between different things to a choice between the same thing twice.

## Lever

Minimum differentiation, the name Boulding gave Hotelling's result.
Sameness is the equilibrium here, not an error either competitor made; each would lose ground by moving off it alone.

## Echo

[The Huddle](piece_the_huddle.md), and the variety lost at [The Ends](piece_the_ends.md).
What crowding leaves behind is a market with one real position filled twice, and two now empty.
