# Piece: The Move

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Middle](play_the_crowded_middle.md). The individually rational reposition: each competitor's best reply, edging inward along [The Line](piece_the_line.md) toward [The Median](piece_the_median.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Made along [The Street](place_the_street.md), wherever a competitor currently stands, each time a rival's position changes what the best reply is.
It is not a place so much as a crossing of the Street: a step taken, then the ground it lands on becomes the new position from which the next move is judged.

## Load Bearing

The Move edges inward because standing nearer the center wins the whole span between the two competitors while giving up only the sparse tail beyond (Hotelling): the arithmetic of a best reply rewards the step every time, regardless of where the rival stands.
It is share-capturing and locally correct, not a blunder and not imitation. Each Move is computed against the rival's current position, not against the rival's past choice.
Guard against reading the Move as collusion or as herding on another's decision. Nothing is agreed and nothing is copied: each competitor edges inward because it is individually the more profitable place to stand, the same reasoning any single seller alone on the Line would still find compelling if placed there. The convergence is a sum of independent best replies, not a coordinated plan.

## Apparent

Just chasing customers: the ordinary, blameless work of competing for share.
