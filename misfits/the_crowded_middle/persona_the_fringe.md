# Persona: The Fringe

## Taxonomy

[Fringe](position_fringe.md). The customer at the end of the spread, the voter at the edge, taken for granted before the rivals converge and abandoned after, offered nothing near what it wants, its number the very variety the market has lost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Distinct, distant, taken for granted.
Wants something at the edge of the range, a flavor, a position, an hour, that sits far from the center, and is counted on to take whatever is nearest because it has nowhere nearer to go.
No unreasonableness in it: its taste is as real as the median's, only rarer, and the market that once might have reached it now offers two versions of a middle that is not what it wanted.

## Action

Threatens to walk on [Threaten to Walk](plan_threaten_to_walk.md), through [Abandoning](process_abandoning.md) at [the Margin](place_the_margin.md), where [the Ends](piece_the_ends.md) go unserved, holding the one lever the abandoned have: to refuse the offering entirely rather than accept the distant middle.
Cannot pull the rivals back by wanting: only by leaving, and only where leaving is possible and felt, so if enough at the edge stay home a party edges back to hold them, and if they cannot or do not, the convergence stands (Downs).
Does what the abandoned can do and no more: it withholds itself, disciplining the huddle a little, unable to open it far.

## Shadow

Not a malcontent for refusing the middle and not served by staying: the Fringe is offered nothing near its taste because both rivals rationally chase the median, so its refusal is the correct reading of a market that has left it, not a tantrum.
Because its custom is sparse and its exit the only signal it can send, the market treats its taste as a rounding error, so the customer with the most distinct want is the customer the competition most reliably ignores, present in the count of the lost variety and absent from every offering.
It is the one the market could have served by staying spread, and the one the pull to the center leaves at the edge with no one.

## Tell

Says neither offering is for it, and is told the market has spoken, when what has spoken is the pull of the median on both rivals at once.
Can make itself felt only by its absence, so its discontent reads as apathy or extremity rather than as a taste unserved, the variety it represents scored as noise and left at the margin.
