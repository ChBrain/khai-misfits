# Piece: Now It Is the Reason

## Taxonomy

[The Mirror](play_the_mirror.md). The return arrow: once the architecture exists it constrains the communication that can happen next, so the seam is load-bearing in both structures at once.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the interface](piece_the_interface.md), on the day it stops being a record and starts being a cause.
In every conversation that does not happen because there is an API where it would have gone.

## Load Bearing

Without it the law runs one way and the play is a story about a copy: the org shapes the system, the
system sits there, and reshaping the org is a free move against an inert artefact.
It is load bearing because **the copy answers back**. The boundary was drawn where two teams did not
talk, and now the two teams do not need to talk, because the boundary handles it: the contract is
written, the queue is in place, the escalation path goes through the interface rather than through a
person. What began as a **record of a silence becomes the reason the silence continues**, and after a
release or two nobody can tell which it is, because both structures hold the same seam up.

## Apparent

That the architecture is the passive half, waiting to be corrected once the org is fixed.
It is the half with the longer memory. People move, teams re-form, reporting lines are redrawn in an
afternoon; the module boundary is in the build, the deploy, the on-call rota and three years of code
written against it, and it outlasts every person who agreed to it.

## Yearbook

The duality the mirroring work actually reports, product and organisational architecture as two views of
one structure rather than one copying the other, and the modularity literature's account of an
architecture that creates and then limits the organisational moves available afterwards.
It is why [the reorg](piece_the_reorg.md) is a last resort rather than a lever: the reorganiser is not
moving a boundary onto an empty field, they are moving it against an artefact that is holding it where it
is.
