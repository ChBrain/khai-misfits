# Place: Two Centuries On

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Comes Instead](play_whoever_comes_instead.md). Where the cost lands, on the only people there are.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A poorer world. Harder lives, thinner resources, and a full complement of ordinary human goods.
People who are glad to be alive and have no wish to have been someone else.

## Holds

[The Ones Who Come](persona_the_ones_who_come.md), bearing the cost and disqualified from reporting it.
[The Life Worth Living](piece_the_life_worth_living.md), which is what closes the complaint.

## Offers

The only witnesses available, and the only testimony that could settle anything.
A clear view of what was lost, held by people for whom nothing was lost.

## Withheld

Any overlap with the other future.
The two populations share nobody, so the place cannot produce a person who is worse off here than there, which is the one thing that would make the loss assignable: what it has instead is everybody agreeing the other world was better and no one able to say it was better for them.
