# Process: Choosing It

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Comes Instead](play_whoever_comes_instead.md). The decision, taken properly, on the merits, by people with the projections in front of them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two policies, a resource, and an obligation to pick one.
[The Ones Who Must Decide](persona_the_ones_who_must_decide.md), holding [Ones Who Must Decide](position_ones_who_must_decide.md), in [the committee room](place_the_committee_room.md).

## Direction

Toward the policy the comparison supports, with the discount rate argued and the projection run out two centuries.
Toward [the choice](persona_the_choice.md) being made rather than deferred, since abstaining selects too.

## Lever

The ordinary method for weighing a policy: what happens to people under each option, compared across the options.

## Echo

A decision reached correctly, and a comparison whose subject did not survive it.
