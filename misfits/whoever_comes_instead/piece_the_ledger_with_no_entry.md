# Piece: The Ledger With No Entry

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Place

## Load Bearing

It is a real quantity with no column to sit in, and that is the whole misfit.
Everybody agrees the world is worse by an amount that could be stated, and the accounting that morality actually uses posts entries against persons: so the sum exists and there is no line for it, which is not a failure of measurement but of the ledger's design, and redesigning the ledger is the expensive repair.

## Apparent

An absence. No claimant, no complaint, no case.
Indistinguishable, in the file, from nothing having happened.

## Yearbook

Grows with the size of the policy, since bigger measures shift more conceptions.
Is the reason the committee's conviction survives the argument that defeats it.
