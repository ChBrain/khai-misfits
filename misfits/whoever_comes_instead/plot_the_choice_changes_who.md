# Plot: The Choice Changes Who

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Comes Instead](play_whoever_comes_instead.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A policy large enough to matter, and the ordinary business of who moves where and which year a couple decides they can afford a child.

## Action

[Shifting who comes](process_shifting_who_comes.md): [the timing](piece_the_timing.md) works through everything the policy touches, from [the committee room](place_the_committee_room.md) outward.

## Stage

Two centuries on, the two futures are not one population living differently. They are two populations, overlapping in nobody.

## Tension

The mechanism belongs to no one and cannot be switched off.
It is not exotic and not the price of recklessness: any measure consequential enough to change a projection reaches into employment, housing and the timing of families, so identity-dependence is a property of acting at scale rather than of acting badly. The only policies exempt from it are the ones too small to change anything, and the room is not choosing between those.
