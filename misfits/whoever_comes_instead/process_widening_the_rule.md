# Process: Widening the Rule

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Comes Instead](play_whoever_comes_instead.md). The repair, which works, and goes on working after it should have stopped.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A verdict of not wrong that nobody accepts, and the obvious amendment.
[The Ones Who Must Decide](persona_the_ones_who_must_decide.md), after [looking for the injured party](process_looking_for_the_injured_party.md) has come back empty.

## Direction

Toward a standard on which an outcome can be worse without being worse for anyone.
Toward condemning the policy, which it does, immediately and cleanly.

## Lever

The impersonal comparison of outcomes, which needs no subject and therefore cannot be stopped by the absence of one.

## Echo
