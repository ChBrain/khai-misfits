# Plot: Glad to Be Here

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Comes Instead](play_whoever_comes_instead.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The poorer world, arrived at, with people in it.

## Action

[The Ones Who Come](persona_the_ones_who_come.md), holding [Ones Who Come](position_ones_who_come.md), are asked on [two centuries on](place_two_centuries_on.md) whether they wish the other policy had been chosen.

## Stage

They decline. [The Life Worth Living](piece_the_life_worth_living.md) holds: the lives are harder than they might have been and are worth having.

## Tension

The only party bearing the cost is the one party with no standing to report it.
The complaint available to them is that the rationing policy would have been better for them, and it would not: under it they are absent, and non-existence is not a worse condition they would have been in. So the testimony that could settle the case is refused by the witnesses, not withheld from them, and refusing it is the correct answer rather than a failure of nerve.
