# Persona: The Ones Who Must Decide

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Must Decide](position_ones_who_must_decide.md). The committee, holding a worse world and a sound argument.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The projections, the two columns, the meeting, and an obligation to choose.
Agreement in the room that the second world is better.

## Action

Look for the injured party. Fail to find one.
Choose anyway, because not choosing is choosing.

## Shadow

Cannot decline the decision or the reasoning.
Abstaining selects a policy as surely as voting does, the better world is plainly identified and cannot be un-seen, and the tool that would justify preferring it returns nothing: so the room is not confused, and being less confused would not help, since every step it took was correct.

## Tell

Holds a clearly worse outcome and a clearly valid argument, and nobody to name.
