# Persona: The Ones Who Would Have Been

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Would Have Been](position_ones_who_would_have_been.md). The alternative population, which cannot be produced.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The people of the better world: better off, never conceived, and not a set anybody can name.
Not a group that lost something.

## Action

Do not exist.
Cannot be represented, since there is nobody to represent.

## Shadow

Are not a wronged party and cannot be made into one.
A claim needs a claimant, and non-existence is not a condition somebody is in: so the intuition that they were deprived cannot be cashed as a wrong done to them, and every attempt to speak for them turns out to be speaking about an outcome rather than to a person.

## Tell

Are the reason the world is worse and can be nobody's grievance.
