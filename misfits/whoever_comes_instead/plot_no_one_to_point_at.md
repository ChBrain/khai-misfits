# Plot: No One to Point At

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Comes Instead](play_whoever_comes_instead.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The ordinary rule that an act is wrong because it is worse for somebody, applied to find the injured party.

## Action

[Looking for the injured party](process_looking_for_the_injured_party.md): [the rule](persona_the_rule.md) runs, correctly, over a world everybody agrees is worse.

## Stage

It returns not wrong. Not that the harm was small: that it has no bearer.

## Tension

The machinery seizes where it is working exactly as designed.
[The Ones Who Would Have Been](persona_the_ones_who_would_have_been.md) cannot supply a claimant, because they are not people who lost something, and a claim needs somebody to be in the condition being complained of. So what is left is [the ledger with no entry](piece_the_ledger_with_no_entry.md): a quantity everybody agrees on, stated to the decimal, with no column to post it in.
