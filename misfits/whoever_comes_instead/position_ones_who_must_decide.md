# Position: Ones Who Must Decide

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Comes Instead](play_whoever_comes_instead.md). The seat with the obligation and no ground.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Projections, agreement about which world is better, and no option to abstain.

## Orders

Decide.

## Loses

The justification, not the judgement.
The seat can see which world is better and cannot say who is wronged by producing the other, so what it loses is not its view but its warrant: it must choose on a conviction it cannot cash, which is a different and worse position than being uncertain.

## Drives

Toward acting rightly for a reason it cannot give.
