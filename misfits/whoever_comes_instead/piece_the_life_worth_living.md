# Piece: The Life Worth Living

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held by [the ones who come](persona_the_ones_who_come.md) on [two centuries on](place_two_centuries_on.md), and true of them.

## Load Bearing

It is what makes the case a paradox rather than an atrocity.
If the poorer world were not worth living in, the ones who come could say they had been wronged by being made at all, and the ordinary rule would have something to work with: it is precisely because their lives are good enough to be glad of that they cannot object, so the harm survives only in the range where nobody suffers enough to have a claim.

## Apparent

An ordinary good life, harder than it might have been.
The thing everybody wanted for them, delivered.

## Yearbook

Is the condition under which the argument bites, and it holds across most of the interesting cases.
Cannot be removed by making the outcome worse, since that hands the complaint back and dissolves the puzzle rather than solving it.
