# Plot: And the Loss Is Real

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Comes Instead](play_whoever_comes_instead.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Everything checked, and the poorer world still poorer.

## Action

[The Ones Who Must Decide](persona_the_ones_who_must_decide.md) return to [the committee room](place_the_committee_room.md) with the search closed and the repair priced.

## Stage

They hold a worse world, a valid argument that it wronged nobody, and no one to name.

## Tension

Both halves survive, and neither dissolves the other.
The loss is real: foreseen, chosen, and agreed on by everybody who ran the numbers. It is also unassignable, and the reasoning that makes it unassignable is the reasoning that makes wrongs answerable to people in the first place. So the room is not confused and being less confused would not help, since every step it took was correct. What is left to argue about is not whether the world is worse, but what to call a harm that is real and borne by no one.
