# Plot: Widening It Costs More

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The better suggestion, that an outcome can be worse without being worse for anyone.

## Action

[Widening the rule](process_widening_the_rule.md): the impersonal standard is adopted, and condemns the policy immediately.

## Stage

Then it goes on ranking. A vast population with lives barely worth living is placed above a small one that flourishes, and nothing in the standard objects.

## Tension

The repair works by removing the thing that would have stopped it.
What blocked the verdict was the requirement that a comparison have a subject, and lifting it is exactly what lets outcomes be ranked at all: so the same move that reaches this case reaches every case, including the ones where the ranking is monstrous. There is no version calibrated to condemn the depletion policy and decline the vast barely-living world, because the two follow from one step, and nobody in the room has found the amendment that takes only the first.
