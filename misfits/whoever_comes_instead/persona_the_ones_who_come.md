# Persona: The Ones Who Come

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Come](position_ones_who_come.md). The people the choice made, who are the only ones to ask.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The population of the poorer world, two centuries on: harder lives, fewer resources, and a full complement of ordinary human goods.
The only people there are.

## Action

Live. Prefer living to not living.
Decline, when asked, to wish the other policy had been chosen, since it would not have produced them.

## Shadow

Cannot complain without wishing themselves away.
The complaint available to them is that a different policy would have been better for them, and it would not: under that policy they are absent, so the comparison they would need has no other side, and their standing to object is destroyed by the same fact that makes them the ones bearing the cost.

## Tell

Are the injured party and have no injury to report.
