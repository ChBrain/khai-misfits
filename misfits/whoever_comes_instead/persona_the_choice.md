# Persona: The Choice

## Taxonomy

[Choice](position_choice.md). The policy, which constitutes its own subjects.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Two options on a table: spend the resource now, or ration it. One leaves the far future poorer and everybody can see which.
Ordinary, deliberate, and taken by people with the projections in front of them.

## Action

Gets made, on the merits, by a committee doing its job.
Reaches into who moves where, who meets whom, and which year a couple decides they can afford a child.

## Shadow

Does not act on a population, it selects one.
Every instrument the room has is built to compare what happens to people across options, and this choice changes the people, so the comparison has no fixed subject: the two columns are not two futures for the same population, they are two populations, and nothing in the method notices the substitution.

## Tell

Is evaluated with a tool that assumes the thing it destroys.
