# Place: The Committee Room

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Comes Instead](play_whoever_comes_instead.md). Where the two columns are compared, by an instrument that assumes what the choice removes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two policies, a discount rate, a projection running two centuries out, and agreement about which world is better.
People doing the job properly, with the numbers in front of them.

## Holds

[The Choice](persona_the_choice.md), which is about to select a population rather than act on one.
[The Rule](persona_the_rule.md), waiting to be applied and certain to return not wrong.

## Offers

Every ordinary instrument for weighing a policy: the columns, the comparison, the identified better outcome.
An obligation to decide, since abstaining selects as surely as voting.

## Withheld

A fixed population to compare across.
The method works by asking what happens to people under each option, and here the options contain different people, so the substitution takes place underneath the comparison rather than inside it: nothing in the projection is wrong and nothing in it can show what has gone missing.
