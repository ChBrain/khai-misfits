# Plot: The Rule Is Not Optional

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Comes Instead](play_whoever_comes_instead.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The obvious suggestion, that the rule should simply be dropped.

## Action

[The Rule](persona_the_rule.md) is examined for what it is doing elsewhere, by [the ones who must decide](persona_the_ones_who_must_decide.md).

## Stage

Dropping it costs the connection between a wrong and the person it was done to, everywhere and for everything.

## Tension

It is not a technicality standing in the way of a verdict.
It is the reason a victim can demand an answer and a bystander cannot be charged, the thing that makes morality answerable to people rather than to a total. Abandoning it to condemn one policy does not condemn that policy: it removes the standard under which anybody was ever owed anything, and hands back a verdict on every other case in exchange for the one.
