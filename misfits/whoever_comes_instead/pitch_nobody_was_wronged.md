# Pitch: Nobody Was Wronged

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Comes Instead](play_whoever_comes_instead.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reasonable and administrative. A committee weighing two policies, a discount rate, a projection two centuries out.
Everybody in the room meaning well and reasoning carefully, with the better world plainly identified.

## Undertow

Under the projection, a choice that does not decide what happens to people but which people there are.
So the ones who bear the cost are the ones the choice made, and they are not worse off than they would have been.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a harm that is real, foreseen, chosen, and borne by nobody.

## Echo

Afterward: a poorer world with people in it who are glad to be here, a rule that returns not wrong, and a repair that condemns the policy by giving up the idea that wrongs are owed to anyone in particular.
