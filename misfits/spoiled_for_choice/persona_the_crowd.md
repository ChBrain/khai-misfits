# Persona: The Crowd

## Taxonomy

[Crowd](position_crowd.md), inside [Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The archetype of the many with their varied tastes, whose real diversity of wanting is the good reason a wider range is offered, no two of them wanting quite the same.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A fickle mob impossible to please, forever demanding more kinds and never satisfied, as though the abundance were the fault of a crowd too greedy or too scattered in its wants to be served by anything less than everything.

## Action

Each to its own. At [the Market](place_the_market.md), the Crowd on [Each to Its Own](plan_each_to_its_own.md) through [Differing](process_differing.md) holds [the Varied Taste](piece_the_varied_taste.md), the real spread of preferences across its many, each wanting a slightly different thing, so that a wider range gives more of them a nearer fit.

## Shadow

Its fickleness is honest diversity, not a greed for everything. The Crowd is not an impossible mob: its many simply want different things, as people do, and to serve that real spread of taste a wider range is the reasonable answer, since somewhere in it more of them find a nearer fit. It does not demand the abundance out of scattered greed; the abundance is offered to it in good faith, a courtesy to its genuine variety. The diversity that makes the wide range worth stocking is no fault, only the plain fact that no two want quite the same, and it is from this honest variety, not any greed, that the abundance grows.

## Tell

It wants many different things because its many are different, an honest variety and not a greed, the good reason a wide range is offered.
