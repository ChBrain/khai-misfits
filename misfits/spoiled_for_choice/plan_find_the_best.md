# Plan: Find the Best

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the chooser's seat](position_chooser.md), inside [Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Chooser](position_chooser.md)

## Direction

Compare the options to choose the one that fits you best, since with so wide a range the perfect fit must be somewhere in it, and settle for nothing less while a better might yet be unseen. Know that the burden of comparing grows with the count until you cannot weigh them all, that to defer is to choose none and to choose is to forgo the rest, and that whatever you pick will be shadowed by all you did not, any shortfall read as your own fault.

## Orders

([The Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md) takes up [the Weighing](piece_the_weighing.md) through [Weighing](process_weighing.md) at [the Aisle](place_the_aisle.md), comparing each option against all the others, and later meets [the Freeze](piece_the_freeze.md) and [the Regret](piece_the_regret.md).)

## Implementation

([Weighing](process_weighing.md) at [the Aisle](place_the_aisle.md) takes up [the Weighing](piece_the_weighing.md), because the wish to find the best sets each option against all the others, so the burden grows with the count and the hope of a perfect fit is lifted too high.)

## Targets

- [ ] the options are weighed, each against all the others, in the wish to find the best
- [ ] the weighing is understood as a real burden that grows with the count of options
- [ ] it is seen that the range lifts the expectation of a perfect fit too high to meet
- [ ] it is owned that the paralysis and regret that follow are a real burden and ache, not a feeble will
