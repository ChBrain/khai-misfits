# Persona: The Stocker

## Taxonomy

[Stocker](position_stocker.md), inside [Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The archetype of the one who lays out the abundance, offering many kinds in good faith to serve the varied taste, by the plain logic that a wider range gives each a better chance at a perfect fit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A manipulator flooding the shelves to dazzle and confuse, piling on options to overwhelm the chooser into buying, as though the abundance were a trap laid to bewilder rather than a range offered to fit.

## Action

Stock it all. At [the Market](place_the_market.md), the Stocker on [Stock It All](plan_stock_it_all.md) through [Stocking](process_stocking.md) lays out [the Abundance](piece_the_abundance.md), the large assortment, each option put there in good faith to fit some taste, the wide range that by the ordinary logic of freedom can only help (Iyengar and Lepper).

## Shadow

Its flooding is a courtesy following a sound logic. The Stocker is not laying a trap to dazzle: it adds each option to fit some real taste in the varied crowd, and it follows the plain and reasonable logic that a wider range can only help, since a chooser who did not want the extra options could simply ignore them. It cannot see that past a point the range it offers as a service becomes a burden, that the options meant to free the chooser will instead weigh it down, because the harm is not in any one option, each of which is a genuine fit for someone, but in their number, which no single good intention adds on purpose.

## Tell

It stocks every kind to fit every taste, by the sound logic that more can only help, and cannot see the number it offers as a service become a burden.
