## Shown

The chooser before the shelves, comparing each option against all the others. The hand that reaches and withdraws.

## Holds

The rising burden of comparison, each option set against every other in the search for the best.

[The Freeze](piece_the_freeze.md).
The paralysis, the chooser who defers or declines rather than settle on one.

## Offers

To the Chooser, the whole range to compare in the hope of a perfect fit.

## Withheld
