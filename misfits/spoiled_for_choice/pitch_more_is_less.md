# Pitch: More Is Less

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that past a point more options give the chooser less, not more, both less choosing and less joy in the choice.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Wry and humane, the small ache of abundance. A world offering more kinds to fit more tastes, exactly as one should, and a chooser meeting the wider range with less ease and not more. Nothing here is a trick or a chooser too feeble for freedom; the abundance is a courtesy and the burden is real.

## Undertow

The burden of comparing rises with the count of options, each to be weighed against all the others, and the range lifts the expectation that a perfect fit must be found, so the chooser defers or declines, the larger display drawing more lookers but fewer buyers (Iyengar and Lepper). And the choice made is shadowed by all the forgone, expectations raised too high and any shortfall its own fault (Schwartz).

## Nerve

That the very range meant to fit every taste is the range that overwhelms the one who chooses, so more options leave it choosing less and enjoying it less. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged hard, since the effect is contested, absent on average and real only under specific conditions of complexity and difficulty (Scheibehenne, Greifeneder and Todd; Chernev, Bockenholt and Goodman).

## Echo

Offer a chooser more than it can weigh, and it chooses none, or chooses one and mourns the rest.
