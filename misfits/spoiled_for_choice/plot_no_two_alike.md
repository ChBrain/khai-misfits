# Plot: No Two Alike

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A crowd of varied tastes, no two quite the same, waiting to be served.

## Action

At [the Market](place_the_market.md), [the Crowd](persona_the_crowd.md) from [the Crowd's seat](position_crowd.md) on [Each to Its Own](plan_each_to_its_own.md) through [Differing](process_differing.md) holds [the Varied Taste](piece_the_varied_taste.md), the real diversity of preferences that makes a wider range reasonable.

## Stage

The varied taste is the good reason for the abundance to come. No two in the crowd want quite the same thing: one wants tart, one wants sweet, one wants a taste no one else has named yet. This is the honest starting condition, not a flaw to be corrected but a fact to be served, and it is the reason a market reaches for range in the first place, a wider assortment giving each preference a better chance at a perfect fit (Iyengar and Lepper). No villain here: the diversity is simply what a crowd is, and wanting it served is simply what a market does.

## Tension

The honest variety in the crowd, and the wide range it makes reasonable to offer.
