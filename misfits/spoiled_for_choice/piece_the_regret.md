# Piece: The Regret

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The harm: the choice shadowed by the forgone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Afterward](place_the_afterward.md), met by [the Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md): the jar carried home, and the shelf of forgone jars carried home with it, uninvited.

## Load Bearing

The chosen option is measured against every forgone alternative, each a road not taken and each free, in memory, to have been the better one. The range itself raised expectations too high for any single jar to meet, so a shortfall is read not as bad luck but as the chooser's own fault: with so much on offer, an unhappy outcome must mean she failed to choose well. Satisfaction falls and self-blame rises, the more that was offered the less that is enjoyed (Schwartz).

This is not The Anticommons, where too many OWNERS each holding a veto leave a resource unused, control fragmented and gridlocked; here the many are OPTIONS before a single chooser, and the harm is her own paralysis and regret, no resource blocked and no veto held by anyone. Nor is it Not Worth Wanting, the adaptive preference where the WANTING itself is quietly revised to fit what is available, sour grapes; here the desire stayed intact throughout; it is the CHOOSING that was overwhelmed, the chooser still wanting the good thing and simply unable to pick it out of the crowd. Nor is it The Tyranny of Small Decisions, where many small choices, each sound on its own, sum over TIME to an aggregate no one would have chosen whole; here the harm is the overwhelm of a SINGLE decision faced among too many options AT ONCE, a paralysis and a regret in the moment, not a drift across a sequence.

## Apparent

Reads as ingratitude, a chooser too spoiled to be happy with all that plenty.
