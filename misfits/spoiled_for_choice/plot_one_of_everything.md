# Plot: One of Everything

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A varied taste in the crowd, and a market reaching to serve all of it at once.

## Action

At [the Market](place_the_market.md), [the Stocker](persona_the_stocker.md) from [the Stocker's seat](position_stocker.md) on [Stock It All](plan_stock_it_all.md) through [Stocking](process_stocking.md) lays out [the Abundance](piece_the_abundance.md), the large assortment offered in good faith to fit some taste.

## Stage

By the ordinary logic of freedom, more options can only help: a chooser who did not want the extra jam, the extra pair of jeans, the extra plan, can simply pass it by, and a chooser who did want it now finds it there. So the Stocker lays out one of everything, each addition made to answer some real preference in the crowd, no different in spirit from the last. The harm, when it comes, will not live in any single option added this way. It will live in their number, a large assortment drawing more lookers than a small one but, past a point, fewer buyers (Iyengar and Lepper). No villain: the Stocker is following a sound logic, one item at a time, each one a courtesy.

## Tension

The range offered as a service to every taste in the crowd, and the number quietly becoming a burden.
