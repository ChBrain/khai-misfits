# Plan: Each to Its Own

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the crowd's seat](position_crowd.md), inside [Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Crowd](position_crowd.md)

## Direction

Let each of your many want the thing that fits it, and by that honest variety make it reasonable to offer a wider range, since a larger assortment gives more of you a nearer fit. Know that your diversity is no greed and no fault, that the abundance is offered to you in good faith as a courtesy to your real variety, and that the harm to come is not in your wanting but in the number the wanting calls forth.
