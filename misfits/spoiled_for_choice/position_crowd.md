# Position: Crowd

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The seat of the many with their varied tastes, and marks that its diversity is honest and the good reason a wider range is offered, not a greed for everything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real spread of preferences across its many, no two wanting quite the same.
A genuine claim on variety, since each of its many finds a nearer fit in a wider range.
No wish for the abundance as such, only for its own several tastes to be met.

## Orders

Want each to its own, since your many are genuinely different.
Let each of your many want the thing that fits it, and by that honest variety make it reasonable to offer a wider range, since a larger assortment gives more of you a nearer fit.
Know that your diversity is no greed and no fault, that the abundance is offered to you in good faith as a courtesy to your real variety, and that the harm to come is not in your wanting but in the number the wanting calls forth.

## Loses

Nothing of its own wanting, and this is the point.
Because the seat only wants its several honest things and asks for no abundance as such, it takes no loss it can name, and yet its real diversity is the good reason the wide range is stocked, and the wide range is what will overwhelm the one who must choose from it, so its blameless variety sets the whole in motion.
It keeps its honest wanting and loses no cause for blame, the varied taste that made a wider range worth offering.

## Drives

Toward the many whose honest variety is the reason for the abundance,
each wanting its own thing so that a wider range fits more of them, a real diversity and not a greed,
so the seat whose blameless spread of taste makes the wide range reasonable is the seat from whose honest wanting the overwhelming number grows.
