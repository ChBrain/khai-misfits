# Piece: The Abundance

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The phenomenon: the large assortment offered in good faith.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Market](place_the_market.md), carried by [Stocking](process_stocking.md), voiced by [the Stocker](persona_the_stocker.md): the shelf filled out, jar by jar, until the varied taste has something to answer it.

## Load Bearing

The wide range, each option put there in good faith to fit some taste named by [The Varied Taste](piece_the_varied_taste.md), offered by the ordinary logic of freedom: that more choice can only help, since an unwanted option costs the shopper nothing but a glance past it. The harm, when it comes, is not in any one option, every jar on the shelf earns its place. It is in their number: a display of twenty four jams draws more lookers than a display of six, but far fewer buyers (Iyengar and Lepper). The stocker means only to serve, and the shelf grows heavy with that service.

## Apparent

Reads as a plain good, a range that can only help and never hurt the shopper standing in front of it.
