# Persona: The Curator

## Taxonomy

[Curator](position_curator.md), inside [Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The archetype of the one who eases the burden by cutting the assortment to a few well-chosen options, setting a default, or counselling the good enough over the best, and who marks that each spends the freedom the abundance offered and that the effect it steers by is contested.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A gatekeeper narrowing the world, a paternalist deciding for others what they may choose from, as though the curation were only a taking away of freedom and the range would be better left whole.

## Action

Cut it to a few. At [the Afterward](place_the_afterward.md), the Curator on [Cut It to a Few](plan_cut_it_to_a_few.md) through [Curating](process_curating.md) holds [the Curated Few](piece_the_curated_few.md), the cut assortment, the sensible default, or the satisficing that takes the first good-enough option rather than searching for the best (Schwartz).

## Shadow

Its gatekeeping is a real relief bought at a real price. The Curator is not narrowing the world out of paternalism for its own sake: cutting the assortment to a few well-chosen options, or setting a default, or counselling the good enough, genuinely eases the burden of comparison and the ache of the forgone. But each spends the very freedom the abundance offered: curation takes the choice of what to omit out of the chooser's hands and risks omitting the one they would have wanted, a default steers them, and satisficing asks them to give up the search for the perfect fit. And it holds its own remedy loosely, since the effect it steers by is contested, absent on average and real only under specific conditions, so the cut is a relief where the burden is real and an overcorrection where it is not.

## Tell

It cuts the range to ease the choosing, and pays for the ease in the freedom the range offered, holding even its own remedy against a contested effect.
