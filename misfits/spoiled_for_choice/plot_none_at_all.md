# Plot: None at All

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The weighing has run past what the Chooser can carry, and still no winner named.

## Action

At [the Aisle](place_the_aisle.md), [the Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md) meets [the Freeze](piece_the_freeze.md), the paralysis, unable to compare them all and unwilling to settle while a better might be unseen.

## Stage

The comparison that Weighing began does not resolve, it accumulates, and past a point the Chooser cannot hold the full assortment in mind at once, cannot rule any option firmly out, and so defers the choice or declines it outright rather than risk having missed the better one still sitting on the shelf. The larger display, the very thing meant to serve every taste in the crowd, draws more lookers than a small one and fewer buyers, choice turned demotivating where it was meant to be freeing (Iyengar and Lepper). No villain: the Freeze is not a weak will giving up too soon, it is what a real cognitive burden looks like once it exceeds what any chooser can carry.

## Tension

The Chooser standing frozen before the aisle, choosing none of the many.
