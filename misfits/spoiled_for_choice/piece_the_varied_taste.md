# Piece: The Varied Taste

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The setting: the real diversity of preferences.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Market](place_the_market.md), carried by [Differing](process_differing.md), voiced by [the Crowd](persona_the_crowd.md): the honest spread of what people want, laid out before a single shelf has to answer for it.

## Load Bearing

The honest spread of preferences across the many, no two wanting quite the same thing: one wants sweet, one wants tart, one wants a jam that reminds her of a particular summer. This is the good reason a wider range is offered at all, since somewhere in a larger assortment more shoppers find a nearer fit to what they already wanted (Iyengar and Lepper). The variety is not manufactured or indulged, it is simply true, and it is the premise every later strain in this misfit stands on.

## Apparent

Reads as a fickle crowd demanding endless variety, never satisfied with what is already on the shelf.

## Yearbook

Carried by [Differing](process_differing.md), staged at [the Market](place_the_market.md), voiced by [the Crowd](persona_the_crowd.md), dramatized in [No Two Alike](plot_no_two_alike.md). It is the diversity that makes the abundance reasonable; it calls forth [The Abundance](piece_the_abundance.md).
