# Plot: What Might Have Been

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The choice is made at last, one thing taken from among the many.

## Action

At [the Afterward](place_the_afterward.md), [the Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md) meets [the Regret](piece_the_regret.md), the chosen option shadowed by all the forgone alternatives, expectations raised too high and any shortfall read as its own fault.

## Stage

This is the harm proper: not the freeze alone but what follows a choice that did get made. The wide range that lifted expectations before the choosing now lowers satisfaction after it, the chosen option compared not to nothing but to every alternative that was passed over, each one free to be imagined as the better pick in hindsight. Where a narrow range let a decent choice feel like a good one, a wide range makes a good choice feel merely decent, and the shortfall lands on the Chooser as self-blame rather than on the range itself, since with so many options surely the perfect one was there for the finding (Schwartz). This is distinct from The Anticommons, where too many owners hold vetoes over one resource and it is the gridlock among them that stalls things, not a single chooser's own paralysis and regret. It is distinct from Not Worth Wanting, where the wanting itself is revised to fit what is available; here the desire stays intact and only the choosing is overwhelmed. And it is distinct from The Tyranny of Small Decisions, where many small decisions taken separately sum to a bad outcome over time; here it is one decision, taken once, among too many options offered at once. No villain: the Regret is not proof of an unwise choice, only of a choice made inside a range built too wide.

## Tension

The choice made and mourned in the same breath, shadowed by all it might have been.
