# Plan: Stock It All

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the stocker's seat](position_stocker.md), inside [Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Stocker](position_stocker.md)

## Direction

Lay out every kind that fits some real preference in the varied crowd, since a wider range gives each a better chance of a perfect fit, and a chooser who does not want the extra can simply ignore it. Know that past a point the range you offer as a service becomes a burden, that the options meant to free the chooser will weigh it down, and that the harm is not in any one option but in their number, which your good intention adds without meaning to.
