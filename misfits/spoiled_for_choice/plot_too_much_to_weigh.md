# Plot: Too Much to Weigh

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The chooser stands before the full assortment, meaning only to pick well.

## Action

At [the Aisle](place_the_aisle.md), [the Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md) from [the Chooser's seat](position_chooser.md) on [Find the Best](plan_find_the_best.md) through [Weighing](process_weighing.md) takes up [the Weighing](piece_the_weighing.md), comparing each option against all the others in the hope of a perfect fit.

## Stage

To choose well among many is to compare each against the rest, and the labour of that comparison grows with the count: twenty four options do not cost four times what six cost, they cost far more, every new entrant weighed against every one already held in mind. Worse, the range itself lifts what the chooser expects to find, a wide enough aisle promising that the perfect fit must be in here somewhere, if only it can be located (Iyengar and Lepper; Schwartz). No villain: the Chooser is doing exactly what Find the Best asks, carefully, in good faith, and the burden is simply what that plan costs once the count runs high.

## Tension

The burden mounting with every option weighed, and the perfect fit hoped for but still unfound.
