# Position: Curator

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The seat of the one who eases the burden by cutting the range, setting a default, or counselling the good enough, and marks that each spends the freedom the abundance offered and the effect it steers by is contested.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A way to ease the burden: to cut the assortment to a few well-chosen options, to set a sensible default, or to counsel satisficing over maximizing.
A clear sight of the price, the freedom the abundance offered that each remedy spends.
A held doubt, the knowledge that the effect it steers by is contested, absent on average and real only under specific conditions.

## Orders

Cut it to a few, or set a default, or take the good enough, to ease the burden.
Trim the assortment to a few well-chosen options, or set a sensible default, or counsel the chooser to take the first good-enough thing rather than search for the best, and so lift the burden of comparison and the ache of the forgone.
Know that each of these spends the freedom the abundance offered, that curation takes the omitted options out of the chooser's hands and risks omitting the very one it would have wanted, and that the effect you steer by is contested, so your cut is a relief where the burden is real and an overcorrection where it is not.

## Loses

The freedom of the full range, spent to buy the ease of choosing.
Because it will not leave the chooser to drown in the abundance, the seat cuts the range or sets the default or counsels the good enough, and so gives up the full freedom the abundance offered, buying the ease of choosing at the price of the options omitted and the search foregone.
It keeps its real relief and its clear sight of the cost and loses no cause for blame, the one who eased the burden by spending the freedom that made it.

## Drives

Toward the one who eases the choosing by spending the freedom that overwhelmed it,
cutting the range or setting a default or counselling the good enough, each a real relief bought with the freedom the abundance offered,
so the seat that alone lifts the burden lifts it only by narrowing the range, and holds even its own remedy loosely against an effect that is contested and conditional.
