# Persona: The Chooser

## Taxonomy

[Chooser](position_chooser.md), inside [Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The archetype of the one who stands before the abundance and must choose, taking on the burden of comparison, freezing before it, and regretting the choice at last made.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A weakling who cannot handle freedom, paralysed by plenty and ungrateful for the range, as though a stronger person would simply pick and be happy and the fault were a will too feeble for the abundance offered.

## Action

Find the best. At [the Aisle](place_the_aisle.md), the Chooser on [Find the Best](plan_find_the_best.md) through [Weighing](process_weighing.md) takes up [the Weighing](piece_the_weighing.md), comparing each option against all the others, then meets [the Freeze](piece_the_freeze.md), deferring or declining, and at [the Afterward](place_the_afterward.md) meets [the Regret](piece_the_regret.md), the choice shadowed by all it forwent (Iyengar and Lepper; Schwartz).

## Shadow

Its paralysis is a real burden, not a feeble will. The Chooser is not too weak for freedom: the work of comparing many options each against all the others truly grows with their number, and the range truly lifts the expectation that a perfect fit must be in there, so to weigh them all is a labour and to settle while a better might be unseen is a real loss. And its regret is not ingratitude: with so much forgone, the chosen thing is measured against all it might have been, and any shortfall reads as its own fault, since with so much on offer an unhappy outcome must be a failure to have chosen well. It meets a genuine cognitive burden and a genuine ache, not a weakness, and it chooses less and enjoys it less for a reason outside its will.

## Tell

It cannot weigh them all and cannot bear to settle, so it chooses none or chooses one and mourns the rest, from a real burden and not a weak will.
