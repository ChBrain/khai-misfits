# Piece: The Freeze

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The paralysis: the chooser who defers or declines.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Aisle](place_the_aisle.md), met by [the Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md): the weighing that never resolves, the hand that does not close on a jar.

## Load Bearing

Unable to compare them all and unwilling to settle while a better fit might still be unseen further down the shelf, the chooser defers the choice, or declines it outright, and walks away with nothing. The larger display draws more lookers than the smaller one, but far fewer buyers: sixty percent of shoppers stopped at the twenty four jam table, only three percent of them bought, against forty percent who stopped at the six jam table and thirty percent of them buying (Iyengar and Lepper). Choice, past this point, turns demotivating rather than freeing, the very range meant to serve her leaving her standing with empty hands.

## Apparent

Reads as a will too feeble for freedom, a shopper who cannot handle the plenty laid out for her.
