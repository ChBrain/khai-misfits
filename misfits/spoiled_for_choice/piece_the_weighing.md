# Piece: The Weighing

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The mechanism: the rising burden of comparison.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Aisle](place_the_aisle.md), carried by [Weighing](process_weighing.md), voiced by [the Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md): jar held against jar, the comparing that the shelf now demands of someone standing in front of it.

## Load Bearing

The burden of comparing each option against all the others grows with their count, not in a straight line but faster, every new jar added to the shelf multiplying the pairs a careful chooser feels obliged to weigh. And the range itself lifts expectations: a shelf this full seems to promise that the perfect jar is somewhere on it, so the chooser feels she owes it a proper search rather than a quick pick. The labour of choosing well swells past what she can comfortably bear, past what [The Abundance](piece_the_abundance.md) looked like it cost when it was still just jars on a shelf (Iyengar and Lepper; Schwartz).

## Apparent

Reads as mere indecision, a chooser dithering who simply needs to make up her mind.

## Yearbook

Carried by [Weighing](process_weighing.md), staged at [the Aisle](place_the_aisle.md), voiced by [the Chooser](persona_the_chooser.md), dramatized in [Too Much to Weigh](plot_too_much_to_weigh.md). It is the burden that grows with the count; it tips into [The Freeze](piece_the_freeze.md).
