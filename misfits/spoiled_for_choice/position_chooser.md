# Position: Chooser

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The seat of the one who must choose from the abundance, and marks that its paralysis and regret are a real burden and a real ache, not a will too feeble for freedom.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The whole abundance before it, every option to be weighed against all the others.
A wish to choose well, to find the fit that is best and not merely good.
An expectation the range has raised, that with so much on offer a perfect fit must be in there to be found.

## Orders

Find the best, weighing each option against all the rest.
Compare the options to choose the one that fits you best, since with so wide a range the perfect fit must be somewhere in it, and settle for nothing less while a better might yet be unseen.
Know that the burden of comparing grows with the count until you cannot weigh them all, that to defer is to choose none and to choose is to forgo the rest, and that whatever you pick will be shadowed by all you did not, any shortfall read as your own fault.

## Loses

The choosing and the joy of it both, to a burden outside its will.
Because the work of comparing grows past what it can bear and the expectation is lifted too high to meet, the seat defers or declines and so chooses none, or chooses one and finds it shadowed by all the forgone, so it loses the easy choosing the abundance promised and the satisfaction of the choice it makes.
It keeps its wish to choose well and its real burden and loses no cause for blame, a chooser overwhelmed by a range meant to serve it.

## Drives

Toward the one overwhelmed by the abundance meant to serve it,
weighing each option against all the rest until it can weigh no more, so it chooses none or chooses one and mourns the forgone,
so the seat that only wished to choose well is left choosing less and enjoying it less, from a real burden and a real ache and not a feeble will.
