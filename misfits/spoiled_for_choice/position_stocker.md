# Position: Stocker

## Taxonomy

[Spoiled for Choice](play_spoiled_for_choice.md). The seat of the one who lays out the abundance, and marks that each option is a courtesy following a sound logic and the harm is in the number, which no one adds on purpose.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A varied taste to serve, and a wide range with which to serve it.
The sound and ordinary logic that more choice can only help, since the unwanted option can simply be ignored.
No sight, from behind the shelves, of the burden the number will lay on the one who must choose from it.

## Orders

Stock it all, to fit every taste, by the logic that more choice can only help.
Lay out every kind that fits some real preference in the varied crowd, since a wider range gives each a better chance of a perfect fit, and a chooser who does not want the extra can simply ignore it.
Know that past a point the range you offer as a service becomes a burden, that the options meant to free the chooser will weigh it down, and that the harm is not in any one option but in their number, which your good intention adds without meaning to.

## Loses

The service it meant to give, turned to a burden by the number.
Because it stocks each option in good faith to fit some taste and follows the sound logic that more can only help, the seat lays out a range that past a point overwhelms the chooser, so its courtesy becomes the burden and the freedom it offered the paralysis, a harm in the number no single good intention added on purpose.
It keeps its good faith and its sound logic and loses no cause for blame, a stocker whose service was undone by the count it did not weigh.

## Drives

Toward the one who lays out the abundance as a courtesy and finds it a burden,
stocking each kind in good faith by the logic that more can only help, blind from behind the shelves to the weight of the number,
so the seat whose every option is a genuine fit for someone offers, in their sum, the range that overwhelms the chooser, harmed by a number no one added on purpose.
