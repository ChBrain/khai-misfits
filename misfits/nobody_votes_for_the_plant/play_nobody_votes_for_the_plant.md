# Play: Nobody Votes for the Plant

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Nobody Votes for the Plant.
The vote goes to the party paid last, because the party paid last wants the whole thing to do well. That party is a pension fund holding a fraction of a percent of this company and comparable fractions of several hundred others, because concentrating other people's retirement money would be a breach of duty. Everything the firm asked somebody to learn is in one building, and there is no instrument for it.

## Arc

The bend is this: the vote was placed with the residual claimant because that party is exposed to the outcome, and the instrument carrying the vote is built so that nobody need be.
It runs on accountability needing a single objective.
[Somebody Has to Be Answerable](plot_somebody_has_to_be_answerable.md). [The Director](persona_the_director.md) can be held to account because there is one thing to hold the seat to, and widening the duty to weigh several parties is the same act as removing the test.
And the residual claimant is the right answer.
[And the Residual Claimant Is the Right Answer](plot_and_the_residual_claimant_is_the_right_answer.md). Wages, invoices and interest are fixed claims that are indifferent to any surplus above them; [the share](piece_the_share.md) is paid out of what is left, so its interest tracks the whole.
And the share is made to be divided.
[But the Share Is Made to Be Divided](plot_but_the_share_is_made_to_be_divided.md). [The Trustee](persona_the_trustee.md) must thin the exposure and may not thin the vote, so the argument that placed the decision assumed a party the instrument is designed to make unnecessary.
Then [And the Skill Is Not](plot_and_the_skill_is_not.md).
[The Firm-Specific Skill](piece_the_firm_specific_skill.md) is worth more here and no more elsewhere by definition, so [the specialist](persona_the_specialist.md) could not have spread it, and [the board room](place_the_board_room.md) has no mechanism by which an untradable stake could be represented.
And [giving the exposed a vote costs more than it saves](plot_giving_the_exposed_a_vote_costs_more_than_it_saves.md). [The Co-owner](persona_the_co_owner.md) is right about the diagnosis and the remedy is priced: a meeting of parties who want different things is slow, under-invests, and cannot wind up when winding up is right.
And [what is lost was never priced](plot_and_what_is_lost_was_never_priced.md). [The Valuer](persona_the_valuer.md) reaches everything that has ever been traded, and the concentrated stakes have never been traded anywhere, so one side of the comparison arrives as a number and the other as an assertion.

## Stakes

Whether a decision right can sit with the party the instrument was designed to leave barely exposed.
Every plot moves the same way: toward accountability that requires one objective, a residual claimant correctly chosen, an instrument built so that prudence thins the holding and not the vote, a stake that is concentrated by definition and unrepresented by construction, a repair that is real and expensive, and a loss with no price because no transaction in it exists, and away from any account in which a fund was greedy or a board was indifferent. The production cannot fault the trustee for diversifying, because that is what it owes the people whose money it holds, and it cannot fault the director for answering to one party, because answering to several is answering to none. What is left to fight over is not who should have decided but what to call an outcome that everybody involved arrived at correctly.
