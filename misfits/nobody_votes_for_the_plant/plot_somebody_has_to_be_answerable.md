# Plot: Somebody Has to Be Answerable

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Votes for the Plant](play_nobody_votes_for_the_plant.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A body of people who may spend other people's money, and a demand that they be answerable for it.

## Action

[Deciding for the residual claimant](process_deciding_for_the_residual_claimant.md): [the director](persona_the_director.md), holding [Director](position_director.md), weighs a proposal at [the board room](place_the_board_room.md) against one objective.

## Stage

A seat that can be held to account, because there is something to hold it to.

## Tension

Accountability requires a single objective, so widening the duty is the same act as removing it.
A board told to weigh members, staff, suppliers and a town has been given no test at all: every decision becomes defensible and none becomes checkable, so the seat grows unaccountable in exactly the degree that it grows even-handed. This is not a preference for shareholders over anybody. It is what answerability is made of, and it is the reason the arrangement cannot simply be widened, since the widening removes the property that made anybody trust the seat.
