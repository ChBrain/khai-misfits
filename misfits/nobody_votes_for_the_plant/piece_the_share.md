# Piece: The Share

## Taxonomy

[The Board Room](place_the_board_room.md). The stake that is made to be divided, and carries the vote.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held in fractions by [the trustee](persona_the_trustee.md), and voted at [the board room](place_the_board_room.md).

## Load Bearing

It is divisible and tradable, which is what lets prudence thin the exposure, and the vote does not thin with it.
The whole design of the instrument is that a stake in it can be made arbitrarily small and sold at any time, and that is exactly what makes equity investable by people who cannot afford to lose. But the control right travels with the share proportionally and is exercised in full, so an owner holding a fraction of a percent still helps decide the whole. The thinness is a feature working correctly, and the vote is a feature working correctly, and the two together are the mechanism.

## Apparent

An ownership stake. The thing that makes somebody care about the company.

## Yearbook

Is designed so that nobody has to have much at stake, and carries the decision anyway.
