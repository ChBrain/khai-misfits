# Persona: The Valuer

## Taxonomy

[Valuer](position_valuer.md). The one who puts a number on what is at stake.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A model, a discount rate, comparable transactions, and a deadline before the vote.

## Action

Prices the assets, the contracts and the going concern.
Reports a range, honestly.

## Shadow

Can reach whatever has a market, and the concentrated stakes have none.
A share has a price because it is traded, and a working knowledge of which machine drifts in July has never been traded anywhere, so there is no comparable, no rate and no line for it. This is not an omission the model could be extended to correct: the missing number is missing because no transaction in it has ever occurred. So the decision is taken on a complete valuation of everything that is priceable, and what is at stake outside that set enters the room as an assertion.

## Tell

Produces an honest number for one side of a comparison nobody can complete.
