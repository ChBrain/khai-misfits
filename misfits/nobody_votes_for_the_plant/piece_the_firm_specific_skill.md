## Load Bearing

It is indivisible and untradable by definition, so no prudence could have spread it and no instrument represents it.
Firm-specific means worth more here and no more elsewhere: that is what the term denotes, which is why the firm wanted it built and why no market for it exists. Spreading it across employers would consist of not having it. So there is no failure of diversification to point at, and the loss when the firm ends shows up as earnings that do not return rather than as a fall in any quoted price.
