# Place: The Board Room

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Votes for the Plant](play_nobody_votes_for_the_plant.md). Where it is decided, correctly, by the people entitled to.

## Owner

## Shown

A board pack, a valuation, a register of members, a resolution and a minute.
Nothing irregular: the duty is discharged, the process is proper, the numbers are sound and the decision is better for the members than the alternatives put beside it.

## Holds

[The Director](persona_the_director.md), answerable to one party and therefore answerable.

## Offers

A tested decision, a documented reason, and a party that can be held to account for it.

## Withheld

A seat for the stake that cannot be traded.
The place is constituted by a register of members, so the parties in it are the parties who hold [the share](piece_the_share.md), and the room is not excluding anybody: it has no mechanism by which an untradable stake could be represented, because representation here runs through an instrument that only a tradable stake has.
