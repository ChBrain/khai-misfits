# Persona: The Trustee

## Taxonomy

[Trustee](position_trustee.md). The one who holds the shares, on behalf of somebody else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A pension fund, thousands of beneficiaries, a mandate, and a legal duty not to concentrate their money anywhere.

## Action

Holds a fraction of a percent of this company, and comparable fractions of several hundred others.
Votes the shares.

## Shadow

Is diversified because it is obliged to be, and the obligation is the mechanism.
Concentrating the fund in one firm would be a breach of duty to people whose retirement it holds, so spreading the risk is not indifference to this company but care for them. What follows is arithmetic rather than attitude: a decision that ends this firm removes a fraction of a percent from the portfolio and everything from the town, and the seat is answerable for the first quantity only.

## Tell

Discharges its duty perfectly, and its duty is to be barely exposed.
