# Position: Director

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Votes for the Plant](play_nobody_votes_for_the_plant.md). The seat that decides, and answers to one party.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A duty, a board pack, and one objective against which a decision can be tested.

## Orders

Weigh. Decide. Account for it to the members.

## Loses

Its accountability, if the objective is widened.
A seat told to weigh several parties can defend any decision and be tested on none, so even-handedness and answerability move in opposite directions.

## Drives

Toward a decision that can be checked, which is why it may consider only one of the parties.
