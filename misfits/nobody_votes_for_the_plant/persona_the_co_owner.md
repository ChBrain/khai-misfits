# Persona: The Co-owner

## Taxonomy

[Co-owner](position_co_owner.md). The one who proposes giving the exposed a vote.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A proposal for employee ownership, a worked precedent, and a room that is willing to hear it.

## Action

Puts the decision right where the exposure is.
Waits for the meeting to reach a decision.

## Shadow

Has the correct diagnosis and a remedy that has been priced and is expensive.
Where the owning class is homogeneous a vote is cheap, and where it is not the meeting has to reconcile people whose interests genuinely differ, which costs time, splits the firm and produces decisions late or not at all. Such a firm also under-invests, because the return arrives after the members have gone, and it cannot bring itself to wind up when winding up is right. The remedy is real, is used, and buys the alignment with a governance cost that falls on the same people.

## Tell

Moves the vote to the exposure, and the meeting is what has to pay.
