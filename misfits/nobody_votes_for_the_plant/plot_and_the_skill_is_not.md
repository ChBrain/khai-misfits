## Tension

The stake that cannot be spread belongs to the party with no vote, and neither half of that is anybody's error.
Firm-specific denotes worth more here and no more elsewhere, so spreading it across employers would consist of not having it, and there is no prudence this seat declined to exercise. Meanwhile the board room is constituted by a register of members and has no mechanism by which an untradable stake could be represented, because representation there runs through an instrument only a tradable stake has. So the exposure is concentrated by definition and unrepresented by construction, and the two facts have no author between them.
