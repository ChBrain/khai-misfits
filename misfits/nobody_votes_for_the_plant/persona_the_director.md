# Persona: The Director

## Taxonomy

[Director](position_director.md). The one who decides, answerable to one claimant.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A board pack, a fiduciary duty, and a proposal that is better for the members than the alternatives.

## Action

Weighs the options against one objective.
Takes the decision that the objective requires.

## Shadow

Is answerable because it answers to one party, and would be answerable to nobody if it answered to several.
A board told to weigh members, staff, suppliers and a town has been given no objective at all: any decision can be defended and none can be tested, so the seat becomes unaccountable in exactly the degree that it becomes even-handed. Answering to the residual claimant is what makes the seat checkable, and the checkability is what makes the failure land where it does.

## Tell

Can be held to account precisely because it may consider only one of the parties.
