# Plot: But the Share Is Made to Be Divided

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Votes for the Plant](play_nobody_votes_for_the_plant.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A pension fund holding other people's retirement money.

## Action

[Holding a fraction of everything](process_holding_a_fraction_of_everything.md): [the trustee](persona_the_trustee.md), holding [Trustee](position_trustee.md), spreads the fund across several hundred companies and votes each holding.

## Stage

A portfolio properly diversified, and a full vote attached to each fraction of it.

## Tension

The party chosen because it bears the residual risk is the party whose exposure prudence obliges it to thin.
Concentrating a pension fund in one firm would be a breach of duty to the people whose money it is, so the thinness is care rather than indifference. But the decision right travels with the share proportionally and is exercised in full, so an owner feeling a fraction of a percent helps decide the whole. The argument that placed the vote assumed a party exposed to the outcome; the instrument that carries the vote is designed so that nobody need be.
