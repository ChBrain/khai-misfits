# Persona: The Specialist

## Taxonomy

[Specialist](position_specialist.md). The one who learned what only works here.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Nineteen years, a process nobody else runs, and a working knowledge of which machine drifts in July.

## Action

Learns the place deeply, because the place needs that and pays for it.
Becomes worth more here and no more anywhere else.

## Shadow

Made the investment the firm asked for, and the investment is what cannot be moved.
Firm-specific means untransferable by definition: the knowledge is valuable and is valuable in one location, which is why the firm wanted it built and why no market exists to sell it into. Diversifying it is not something this seat declined to do; it is not a thing that can be done, since spreading the skill across employers would consist of not having it. And the loss when the firm ends is measured in the earnings that do not come back.

## Tell

Did exactly what was asked, and holds the whole of it in one place.
