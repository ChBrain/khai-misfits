# Plot: And the Residual Claimant Is the Right Answer

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Votes for the Plant](play_nobody_votes_for_the_plant.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The question of which single party the seat should answer to.

## Action

[The Share](piece_the_share.md) is identified at [the board room](place_the_board_room.md) as the claim that is paid last, after wages, suppliers and lenders.

## Stage

A principled answer, arrived at by argument rather than by custom.

## Tension

The party paid last is the party that wants the total maximised, which makes the answer correct and not arbitrary.
Everybody else holds a fixed claim: the wage, the invoice, the interest are agreed in advance and are indifferent to any surplus above them, so a party protected by a contract has no reason to care whether the firm does well or merely well enough to pay. The residual claimant is paid out of what is left, so its interest tracks the whole. That is why the vote sits where it sits, and it is why this misfit cannot be resolved by moving the vote for the reason it was placed there.
