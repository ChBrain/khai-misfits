# Nobody Votes for the Plant: Reference

The research warrant for Nobody Votes for the Plant.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the placement of the corporate control right and on what firm-specific
investment does to the argument that placed it, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging corporate control as **a right placed on an exposure the instrument was designed to remove**: the vote
goes to the party paid last, on the sound argument that a party paid out of what is left is the one whose
interest tracks the whole, since wages, invoices and interest are fixed claims indifferent to any surplus above
them. That party is a pension fund holding a fraction of a percent of this company and comparable fractions of
several hundred others, because concentrating other people's retirement money in one firm would be a breach of
the duty owed to them. Meanwhile everything the firm asked somebody to learn sits in one building, and there is
no instrument for it. The play models the documented mechanics, the accountability that requires a single
objective, the residual claim that is the principled answer to which objective, the share that is divisible and
carries an undiminished vote, the firm-specific capital that is indivisible by definition and unrepresented by
construction, the repair that exists and is priced, and the valuation that reaches whatever has been traded, as
the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a greedy fund or an indifferent board.

The governing law is that the decision right was placed with the residual claimant because that party is
exposed to the outcome, and the instrument carrying the right is built so that no holder need be.

**Every party in the sequence is discharging a duty, and the duties are the mechanism.** The trustee is
diversified because concentrating would breach what it owes its beneficiaries. The director answers to one
claimant because a seat answering to several can defend any decision and be tested on none. The specialist
built exactly the capability the firm asked for and paid for. None of these is a choice that could have gone
the other way without a worse failure taking its place.

What removes any possibility of blame from the board is that accountability requires a single objective, so
widening the duty and removing the test are one act. A board told to weigh members, staff, suppliers and a town
has been given nothing to be held to, and grows unaccountable in exactly the degree that it grows even-handed.
The seat's answerability is what fixes which party it may consider.

What removes it from the specialist is that firm-specific denotes worth more here and no more elsewhere, so
spreading the stake would consist of not holding it. There is no prudence declined and no diversification
forgone: the concentration is what the term means, and it is the property that made the investment worth the
firm's while to request.

And the room closes the case against the last party, because it is not excluding anybody. The board room is
constituted by a register of members, and representation there runs through an instrument that only a tradable
stake possesses, so there is no mechanism by which an untradable stake could hold a seat. The exposure is
concentrated by definition and unrepresented by construction, and the two facts have no author between them.

It is distinct from the house's **Seed Corn**, which the slate line for this misfit required it to be named
clear of, and the test was run before authoring rather than after. That misfit runs on measurement: when only
the near term is measured and rewarded, managers spend the far term to deliver it. Remove that engine entirely,
by taking a private firm with no quarterly reporting and no near-term bonus, and this failure stands: a fund
with a five-year mandate values the year-five price, and value appearing in year twelve is worth nothing to it
**even if perfectly known**. Nothing is measured, nothing is rewarded, and the divergence survives, because the
quantity here is not the length of anybody's view but the concentration of the deciding party's exposure.

It is distinct from the house's **Metric Fixation**, where a measure becomes a target and an interested party
optimises against it: there is no proxy here and nothing is being gamed. The share price is not a distorted
measure of the members' interest, it is an accurate one, and the difficulty is that an accurate measure of one
party's interest is what the seat is required to serve.

It is distinct from the house's **The Best on Paper**, which holds the optimizer's curse: there a decision is
taken on noisy estimates and the winner disappoints because selection favours overstatement. Here the estimates
are not noisy and nothing is overstated. The valuation is honest and complete about everything it covers, and
what is missing from it is missing because no transaction in that thing has ever occurred.

And it stands **opposed** to the house's **The Only Basket**, on the same axis and with the opposite sign, which
is the pairing that makes both legible. There a household holds its whole wealth in one undiversifiable asset
**and holds the local vote**, so it rationally uses the ballot to defend the one thing it cannot spread, and the
sum of those sound defences chokes a region's housing supply. Here the undiversifiable parties hold no vote at
all, and the party that does hold it is diversified by duty. So the quantity is the same, the diversification of
whoever holds the decision right, and the outcomes move in opposite directions. **What sets the sign is whether
the concentrated exposure and the decision right sit in the same hands.** Where they do, the concentration
produces over-defence of the single asset; where they do not, the diversification produces under-weighting of
the stake nobody voted for. Neither misfit is an argument for the other's arrangement, and reading them together
is the point: moving a vote toward an exposure and away from one are the same lever, and it has a failure at
each end.

## Origin

The question as it was first put, the spine, why shareholders own at all, the argument that closes the obvious
repair, what the concentrated stake costs when it is lost, and the dissent.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the fund as greedy
  or the board as indifferent, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the trustee is
  diversified because concentrating other people's retirement money would breach the duty owed to them; the
  director answers to one claimant because a seat answering to several can defend anything and be tested on
  nothing; the residual claim is the principled place for the vote, since every other claim is fixed and
  indifferent to any surplus; the specialist built exactly the capability the firm asked for, and firm-specific
  denotes a stake that cannot be spread; the board room has no mechanism by which an untradable stake could be
  represented; and the valuer reaches everything that has ever been traded. The failure is authored by nobody
  and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that the vote was placed on an exposure the instrument was
  built to remove.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Moving the decision right to
  the exposed parties is the real remedy, exists, is used, and is priced: a meeting of members who want
  genuinely different things is slow and divisive, such a firm under-invests because the return arrives after
  the present members have gone, and it cannot bring itself to wind up when winding up is right, which is the
  case the arrangement exists to handle. Widening the board's duty removes the test that made the seat
  answerable. Contracting for the specific investment is what the parties already try, and the difficulty is the
  part that cannot be written down in advance, which is the part that made it specific. Long-term shareholding
  reduces the thinness and does not restore concentration, since a patient holder of a fraction of a percent is
  still holding a fraction of a percent. And pricing the missing side is unavailable, because a price needs a
  transaction and none has occurred.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the separation of ownership and control, the team-production
  analysis, the account of why ownership settles where it does, the single-objective argument, the displacement
  evidence and the dissent are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: **whether the non-shareholder parties
  are in fact under-protected is genuinely contested**, since they hold contracts, statutory rights and often
  organisation, and the dissent cited above argues that insulating boards to protect them harms the members
  without demonstrably helping anybody, which the play carries as a live objection and does not settle; the
  legal position differs by jurisdiction and the play describes a structure rather than any country's company
  law; the displacement evidence measures what a concentrated stake was worth by observing its loss and is not
  offered as a valuation of anything or as a claim about how often such losses occur; the account treats the
  firm as facing one decision taken once, which is the play's own simplification of continuous choices about
  investment, location and closure; and **the play makes no claim that any company was wrongly run, or that
  ownership should be arranged differently**, only that the vote sits with the party the instrument was designed
  to leave barely exposed, and that the parties who cannot spread their stake have no seat in the room.
