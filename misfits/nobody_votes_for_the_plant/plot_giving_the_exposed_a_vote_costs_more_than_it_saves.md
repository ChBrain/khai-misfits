# Plot: Giving the Exposed a Vote Costs More Than It Saves

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Votes for the Plant](play_nobody_votes_for_the_plant.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The obvious repair, put by somebody who has thought about it.

## Action

[The Co-owner](persona_the_co_owner.md), holding [Co-owner](position_co_owner.md), proposes at [the shop floor](place_the_shop_floor.md) that the decision right be moved to where the exposure is.

## Stage

A remedy that exists, is used, and has been studied where it is used.

## Tension

The repair is correct and priced, which is what makes it a cost rather than an answer.
A vote is cheap where the owning class wants the same thing and dear where it does not, and the exposed parties here want genuinely different things, so the meeting must reconcile them: slowly, divisively, and sometimes not at all. Such a firm also under-invests, because the return arrives after the present members have gone, and it cannot bring itself to wind up when winding up is right, which is the case the whole arrangement exists to handle. So the alignment is bought with a governance cost falling on the very people it aligns.
