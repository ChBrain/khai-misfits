# Kept From Burning: Reference

The research warrant for Kept From Burning.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the fire suppression paradox and the fire deficit it produces, all
cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging suppression as **a protection whose input is the removal of the small releases that used to keep the
load down**: a fire caught in the first hour is caught correctly, because it is cheap now and dear by evening
and there are houses in the draw below, and the thing that fire would have done is eat the year's needles,
brush and small stems. So the load builds by exactly the act that keeps everyone safe this season, without an
event to notice, until the flame length past a certain load exceeds what anybody can stand in front of and
suppression stops being an option rather than merely getting harder. The play models the documented mechanics,
the call that is right every time it is made, the accumulation with no steps in it, the record that reads the
same whether the policy is working or storing the bill, the threshold that is a change in kind, the remedy
priced by the thing it removes, and the impossibility of stopping, as the load-bearing vectors of the
structure, not as a tale of an agency that failed to think ahead.

The governing law is that the disturbance being excluded was the process that kept the system inside the range
where exclusion was cheap. Frequent light fire is not damage the ground tolerates: it is the mechanism that
holds the fuel array at a level where a fire stays on the ground and a crew can hold it. Remove it and nothing
else removes it, so the array climbs, and the climb has no threshold anybody can be present at, since a season
of growth is not an event.

What removes any possibility of blame from the crew is that every step is the step it should take. The fire is
genuinely cheap in the first hour and genuinely expensive in the fourth, the liability for what burns sits in
that seat while the fifty-year horizon does not, and a crew that let it run would be answering for a house
rather than for a policy. So the decision that compounds is taken by the only party with no access to what it
compounds into, and taken rightly, once a year, for as long as the policy runs.

What removes it from the agency is that the evidence is generated by the mechanism. Fifty years without a
catastrophic fire is exactly what a working policy produces and exactly what a policy quietly storing the bill
produces, and the two series diverge only at the point where reading them is no longer useful. That is not a
failure of measurement and cannot be repaired by measuring better, because the record is answering a question
the question at issue does not reduce to.

And the repair is real and is priced by the accumulation. Putting fire back deliberately is the one input that
takes the load down, and on a light load it wants an ordinary afternoon and a small liability, while on decades
of fuel it wants weather that is rarer, a margin nobody will underwrite, and a public that has spent those
decades correctly learning that fire near houses is the emergency. So the cure was widest when nobody needed it
and is narrowest when everybody does, and every season it is deferred the deficit grows while the seasons
available to clear it do not.

It is distinct from the house's **Dry Enough to Build On**, where a wall stops the small floods so the plain
behind it fills up and the flood it cannot stop finds a town: there the protection works exactly as promised
and the loss is manufactured by what people then build behind it, so the mechanism runs through a behavioural
response and would not run at all on an empty plain. Here nothing is built and nobody moves in. The hazard
itself grows, physically, on ground that no one has touched, because the small releases that consumed it were
prevented, and the play deliberately declines to cast the spread of houses into the wildland edge, which is a
real and separate phenomenon and is that misfit's, not this one's.

It is distinct from the house's **The Brittle Optimum**, where every slice of slack is idle waste in calm and
is trimmed away until the leanest system shatters on the first shock: there something valuable is removed by
somebody optimising, and the repair is to stop trimming and pay for the margin. Here nothing is removed at all.
Something accumulates, and it accumulates because a protective act succeeded, so there is no margin to restore
and no optimiser to call off: the corresponding repair is to add a disturbance rather than to stop subtracting
a buffer.

It is distinct from the house's **The Firefighter's Medal**, where prevention is invisible and the dramatic
save gets the medal, so effort flows to firefighting and the fires never stop: there prevention is starved by a
reward asymmetry and the remedy is to fund and credit it. Here suppression **is** the prevention, it is
lavishly funded, superbly executed and publicly credited, and it is the accumulating input. No reallocation of
effort or credit touches this, because the problem is not that the preventive work is neglected but that it
works.

It is distinct from the house's **More to Stand Still**, where the dose stops working so it is raised, and the
raise becomes the level needed to stand where you started: there the system habituates to a stimulus and the
escalation buys a flat effect. Here nothing habituates and the effect is not flat. Every year of suppression
succeeds completely on its own terms and the escalation is not required to hold the outcome, it is required
only at the end, once, when the outcome is no longer available at any dose.

It is distinct from the house's **The New Normal**, where each successful deviation relaxes the standard until
the tolerated drift is the rule: there a judgement moves, incrementally, inside an organisation that could in
principle notice it moving. Here no judgement moves at all. The standard is constant, the practice is constant,
the execution is constant, and the only thing that changes is a physical quantity on a hillside that no policy
was ever expressed in terms of.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the crew as
  short-sighted or the agency as negligent, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the
  fire is cheap in the first hour and dear in the fourth and the houses below the slope are real; the seat that
  carries the liability for what burns does not carry the horizon; the fuel is the ordinary annual growth and
  is on nobody's incident form; the record is true in every figure and cannot be read against itself; and the
  threshold is crossed by addition, so there is no year in which the mistake was made and nobody is present at
  the crossing. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that the act
  which keeps everyone safe this season is the same act that stores the load.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Putting fire back is the one
  repair that reaches the load and it is priced by the load, so it is dearest exactly when it is necessary and
  a skipped season is a deficit carried forward rather than a delay. Letting a fire run is not available for
  the same reasons it was never available, and those reasons are stronger now, since a fire let go today is not
  the light one the ground wants. Mechanical thinning reaches part of the array and not the part that carries a
  crown fire, at a cost per acre that no programme has been funded to apply at landscape scale. Understanding
  the mechanism completely, which everybody in the play does, changes none of the inputs to this afternoon's
  decision.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the fire history, the Baja comparison, the naming of the paradox,
  the charcoal record and the feedback analysis are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: the mechanism is
  established for frequent-fire forest types such as ponderosa pine and dry mixed conifer and does **not**
  generalise across all vegetation, since chaparral and lodgepole systems burn in high-severity regimes
  naturally and a fuel-accumulation reading is contested or wrong for them; how much of recent severity is
  accumulated fuel against warming, drought and wind is genuinely unsettled and varies by forest type, so the
  play stages the accumulation mechanism without assigning it a share; the Minnich comparison has been argued
  over for four decades and is carried as the observation that made the mechanism visible rather than as a
  settled measurement; and the fire regimes the ground grew up under were in many places maintained by
  deliberate Indigenous burning rather than by lightning alone, which the play credits and does not stage,
  since staging it would require a cast this production does not carry. **The play makes no claim that
  suppression should be withheld from any particular fire**, only that the small fires were the mechanism that
  kept the load low, and that a policy can be right every afternoon and wrong across fifty years.
