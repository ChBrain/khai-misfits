# Plot: The Window Narrowed

## Taxonomy

[Kept From Burning](play_kept_from_burning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The obvious remedy: put fire back on the ground deliberately, before the weather does it.

## Action

[Putting fire back](process_putting_fire_back.md) is planned, measured and costed against [the narrowing window](piece_the_narrowing_window.md).

## Stage

It is the one repair that reaches the load, and it is dearer and rarer than it was every year the policy worked.

## Tension

The cure is indexed to the disease.
A deliberate burn on a light load wanted an ordinary afternoon and carried a small liability; the same act on fifty years of fuel wants weather that is rarer, a margin nobody will underwrite, and a public that has spent fifty years correctly learning that fire near houses is the emergency. So the remedy was widest when nobody needed it and narrowest when everybody does, and each season it is deferred the deficit to be cleared grows while the seasons available to clear it do not.
