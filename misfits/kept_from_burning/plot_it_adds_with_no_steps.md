# Plot: It Adds With No Steps

## Taxonomy

[Kept From Burning](play_kept_from_burning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The seasons after, and the ordinary annual growth that a fire used to take off.

## Action

[Standing the fuel](process_standing_the_fuel.md): [the land](persona_the_land.md) grows its usual load and keeps it, because the only thing that removed it is now caught in the first hour.

## Stage

[The Fuel That Stayed](piece_the_fuel_that_stayed.md) builds without an event: no season is a change, and there is nothing to notice at the time.

## Tension

The input to the load is the protection itself.
Needles, brush and small stems accumulate whether or not anybody is watching, so nothing here is neglect and nobody is failing to do a job: the fuel is not on the incident form, was never anybody's to report, and a sum with no steps is still a sum. Fifty years on, the slope is thicker than any living person has seen it, and the year in which that happened does not exist.
