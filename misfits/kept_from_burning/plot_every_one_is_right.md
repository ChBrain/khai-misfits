# Plot: Every One Is Right

## Taxonomy

[Kept From Burning](play_kept_from_burning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A fire started small on a slope with houses below it, and a crew that can reach it inside the hour.

## Action

[Putting it out](process_putting_it_out.md): [the crew](persona_the_crew.md), holding [Crew](position_crew.md), cuts the line before the wind turns.

## Stage

The fire is out by evening and nothing below the slope is lost. The call was correct and it was not close.

## Tension

There is no version of that call that comes out the other way.
[The Small Fire](persona_the_small_fire.md) is cheap in the first hour and expensive in the fourth, the houses are real, and the liability for what burns sits in this seat while the horizon does not. Nobody standing on the slope in the afternoon is being asked to weigh a century, and a crew that let it run would be answering for a house rather than for a policy. So the decision that compounds is taken by the only party with no access to what it compounds into, and taken rightly.
