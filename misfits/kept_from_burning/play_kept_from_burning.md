# Play: Kept From Burning

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Kept From Burning.
A fire starts small on a slope in the afternoon. A crew reaches it in the first hour and puts it out, which is the right call and is not a close one. It is made again the next season, and the season after, for fifty years, by people who are good at it. What a small fire used to do was eat: the needles, the brush and the small stems that a burn every seven or twelve years took off. Caught early, that fuel stays. Nobody adds it and no year's growth is a change, and after fifty years the slope carries a load that cannot be put out by anybody.

## Arc

The bend is this: the small fires were what consumed the fuel, so stopping them is the same act as storing it.
It runs on a decision that is right every single time.
[The Small Fire](persona_the_small_fire.md) is cheap in the first hour and expensive in the fourth, there are houses below the slope, and nobody in the moment is being asked to weigh a century against an afternoon. A crew that let it run would be answering for the house it let burn. There is no version of that call that comes out the other way, which is why it keeps being made.
And the fuel is not something anybody put there.
[The Fuel That Stayed](piece_the_fuel_that_stayed.md) is the ordinary annual growth that used to be taken off and now is not, so the load builds with no visible step: no single season's needles are a change, and a sum with no steps is still a sum. [The Land](persona_the_land.md) is not being neglected, it is being protected, and protection is the input.
Then [The Record](persona_the_record.md) reads as proof, and reads that way either way.
Fifty years without a catastrophic fire is exactly what the policy promised. It is also exactly what a policy quietly storing the bill would produce, right up until it stopped. So the evidence for continuing is generated by the mechanism that makes continuing worse, and nothing inside the record separates the two readings.
And [Past What Can Be Held](plot_past_what_can_be_held.md) is where the machinery seizes.
[The Threshold](piece_the_threshold.md) is not a bigger version of the small fire. Past a load the flame length exceeds what anybody can stand in front of, the fire moves into the crowns, and it stops being a thing that is fought and becomes a thing that is fled. The same weather that gave a ground fire in year ten gives a crown fire in year sixty.
And the remedy narrowed while the policy worked. Putting fire back was cheap and safe early. On a loaded slope it wants a weather window that is rarer, a liability nobody will carry, and a public that has learned that fire near houses is the emergency, so [the narrowing window](piece_the_narrowing_window.md) is priced by exactly the accumulation it exists to remove.
So the ground holds a load nobody laid, a record nobody can read against itself, and a cure that got dearer every year it worked.

## Stakes

Whether a policy whose evidence is generated by its own mechanism can be assessed at all.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a call that is correct each time it is made, a load that accumulates without an event, a record that cannot be read against itself, a fire that changes in kind rather than degree, and a remedy that is priced by the thing it removes, and away from any account in which somebody was careless. The production cannot ask the crew to let the house burn, and it cannot produce the year in which the mistake was made, because there is no such year. What is left to fight over is not whether the load is there, everybody can measure it, but what to call a policy that is right every afternoon and wrong across fifty years.
