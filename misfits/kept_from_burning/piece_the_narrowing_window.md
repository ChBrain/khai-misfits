# Piece: The Narrowing Window

## Taxonomy

[The Fiftieth Year](place_the_fiftieth_year.md). The remedy, priced by the thing it removes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held open on [the fiftieth year](place_the_fiftieth_year.md), narrower each season, and worked through [putting fire back](process_putting_fire_back.md).

## Load Bearing

It is the one repair that reaches the load, and it gets dearer in exact proportion to how long the policy has been working.
A deliberate burn on a light load wants an ordinary afternoon and carries a small liability; the same act on fifty years of fuel wants weather that is rarer, a margin nobody will underwrite, and a public that has spent fifty years being told that fire near houses is the emergency, so the cure is indexed to the disease it treats.

## Apparent

A permit, a burn plan, and a forecast that does not come.
A reasonable process, correctly cautious.

## Yearbook

Narrows while the policy succeeds and would have been widest when nobody needed it.
Closes for a season on one bad forecast and does not reopen the deficit it skipped.
