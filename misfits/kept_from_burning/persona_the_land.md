# Persona: The Land

## Taxonomy

[Land](position_land.md). The ground, protected rather than neglected.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A slope that used to burn every seven or twelve years, lightly, and has not burned in fifty.
Thicker than any living person has seen it.

## Action

Grows. Keeps what it grows.
Looks well.

## Shadow

Is carrying a regime it was never adapted to.
The species on it are the ones that came up under frequent light fire, so the thing being protected is not the ground as it was but the ground as fifty years of protection has made it, and there is no year in which the substitution took place.

## Tell

Is healthier by every measure taken and holds more than it can survive.
