# Plot: Past What Can Be Held

## Taxonomy

[Kept From Burning](play_kept_from_burning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The same weather as any other year, arriving on a slope that has not burned in fifty.

## Action

[The One That Comes](persona_the_one_that_comes.md) enters [One That Comes](position_one_that_comes.md) on [the fiftieth year](place_the_fiftieth_year.md), and moves into the crowns.

## Stage

It stops being a thing that is fought and becomes a thing that is fled. [The Threshold](piece_the_threshold.md) was crossed on a date nobody recorded.

## Tension

The change is in kind, not in degree, which is why competence does not reach it.
Past a load the flame length exceeds what a person can stand in front of, so the question is no longer whether the crew is good enough but whether anybody is there at all, and the answer does not depend on any equipment that exists. The crews who held a hundred fires cannot hold this one, and they are the same crews, no worse than they were.
