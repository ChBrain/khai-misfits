# Piece: The Threshold

## Taxonomy

[The Fiftieth Year](place_the_fiftieth_year.md). The point past which suppression is not an option.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Crossed somewhere on [the fiftieth year](place_the_fiftieth_year.md), on a date nobody recorded.

## Load Bearing

It converts a difference of degree into a difference of kind, which is why no amount of getting better at the job reaches it.
Past a load the flame length exceeds what a person can stand in front of and the fire moves into the crowns, so the question stops being whether the crew is good enough and becomes whether anybody is there at all, and the answer does not depend on equipment that exists.

## Apparent

Nothing. It has no marker and announces no crossing.
The slope looks the same the year after as the year before.

## Yearbook

Moves closer with every successful season, and is not visible from either side.
Is reached by addition, so nobody is present at the moment it is passed.
