# Piece: The Fuel That Stayed

## Taxonomy

[The Slope](place_the_slope.md). The load, which nobody added.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Left on [the slope](place_the_slope.md) by [putting it out](process_putting_it_out.md), once a year, for fifty years.

## Load Bearing

It is not waste and not neglect: it is the ordinary annual growth a light fire used to take off.
Needles, brush and small stems accumulate whether or not anybody is watching, and the only thing that ever removed them was the fire that is now caught in the first hour, so the input to the load is the protection itself and there is no negligent party anywhere in the sequence.

## Apparent

Undergrowth. A thicker, greener, healthier-looking stand.
The visible result of fifty years of care.

## Yearbook

Adds by the season and subtracts only by burning.
Can be measured exactly, at any time, by anybody who walks out with a tape.
