# Persona: The Crew

## Taxonomy

[Crew](position_crew.md). The party who cannot decline, and does the job properly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Hand tools, an engine, a line cut before the wind turns.
Good at a dangerous job and answerable for the houses below.

## Action

Arrives, cuts, holds, mops up.
Goes home before the fire is a fire.

## Shadow

Has no version of the call that runs the other way.
Letting it burn is not an option a crew can take: the liability is theirs, the houses are real, and nobody standing on that slope in the afternoon is being asked to weigh a century, so the decision that compounds is taken by people with no access to the thing it compounds into.

## Tell

Is blameless at every step and is the mechanism.
