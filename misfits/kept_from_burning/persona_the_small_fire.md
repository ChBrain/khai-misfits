# Persona: The Small Fire

## Taxonomy

[Small Fire](position_small_fire.md). The decision, correct on its own terms and made again every year.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A quarter acre on a slope in the afternoon, reachable inside the hour, with houses below it.
Cheap now and expensive by evening.

## Action

Gets put out.
Gets put out again next season, and the season after.

## Shadow

Was the thing that ate the fuel.
Every fire small enough to be caught easily is a fire doing the work that keeps the next one small, so catching it is not a neutral act with a delayed cost, it is the same act as storing the load, performed once a year by people who are right each time.

## Tell

Is cheapest to stop in exactly the years it is most worth letting run.
