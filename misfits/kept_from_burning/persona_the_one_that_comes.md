# Persona: The One That Comes

## Taxonomy

[One That Comes](position_one_that_comes.md). The fire that is not a bigger version of the small one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The same weather as any other year, arriving on fifty years of standing fuel.
A front, a wind, an afternoon.

## Action

Moves into the crowns.
Stops being fought and starts being fled.

## Shadow

Is a change in kind rather than in degree.
Past a load the flame length exceeds what anybody can stand in front of, so suppression is not merely harder but absent as an option, and the crew that could hold a hundred such fires at year ten cannot hold this one with any equipment that exists.

## Tell

Arrives on ordinary weather and is not an ordinary event.
