# Position: Supervisor

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). The seat whose correct question has one good answer, and whose asking of it couples the system together.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The power to require a measure and to wire it to a capital rule, which is what makes it a control rather than an opinion.
A duty to inspect, compare across firms, and answer for the regime afterwards.
No instrument that can see, or object to, the fact that every firm's answer is the same answer.

## Orders

Make it measurable: require a risk measure that is quantitative, auditable and sensitive to conditions, and reject a discretion that cannot be checked in advance or defended after.
Demand validation, backtesting and annual review, all of which will be supplied and none of which reaches the fault.
Accept what the seat does: a well-drafted requirement has a best answer, and everyone will find it.

## Loses

The independence of the things it supervises, since asking each firm for the right number gets the same number from all of them.
Because the harmonisation is the direct product of a good rule rather than of anyone's laziness, the seat cannot cure it by enforcing harder, and enforcing harder tightens it.
It keeps a system it can audit firm by firm and loses the only property that would have made the audits add up.

## Drives

Toward the actor with the clearest public duty and the one whose discharge of it wires the system as one body,
asking for the number it can inspect, refusing the discretion it cannot, and getting exactly what it asked for from everyone at once,
so the requirement that makes each institution legible is the requirement that makes them all move together.
