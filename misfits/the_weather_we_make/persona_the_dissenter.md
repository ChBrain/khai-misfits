# Persona: The Dissenter

## Taxonomy

[Dissenter](position_dissenter.md). The one who names the trade correctly and has nothing to offer that is not a purchase.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Academic in manner and specific in argument, saying the thing in advance rather than afterwards: that harmonising the measure raises systemic risk rather than lowering it.
Does not claim the models are wrong about any single firm, and concedes at once that each is a good answer to the question asked.
Is listened to politely and is asking for something nobody can write down.

## Action

Runs [Make Them Different](plan_make_them_different.md): through [Diversifying](process_diversifying.md) at [the Committee](place_the_committee.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md).
Sets out the three available corrections and prices each: mandated model diversity, countercyclical buffers, and a risk-insensitive leverage cap.
Refuses to present any of them as free, which is what makes the argument hard to act on and honest.

## Shadow

Not a prophet and not vindicated by the event: it is proposing measures with real costs against a harm that has not happened, to people who must justify every cost that has.
Because diversity means ordering named institutions to run models measurably worse at describing their own risk, and buffers mean idle capital released on the judgement hardest to make at the moment it is needed, and a crude cap means punishing the safe balance sheet with the dangerous one, the seat cannot offer a correction that a supervisor can defend in the year it is imposed.
It is the only actor who sees the whole shape and the one with nothing costless to sell.

## Tell

Is agreed with in principle and not adopted, and the agreement is sincere.
Cannot name which institution should hold the worse model, since any answer is a supervisor telling a named firm to measure itself badly, and no answer at all is what leaves the room with the arrangement it walked in with.
