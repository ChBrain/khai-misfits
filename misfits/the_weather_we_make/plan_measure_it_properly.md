# Plan: Measure It Properly

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the risk officer's seat](position_risk_officer.md), inside [The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Risk Officer](position_risk_officer.md)

## Direction

Fit the model to the observable history, backtest it honestly, validate it, and report the number it produces. Report that number and no other. State the assumptions plainly, the exogeneity of the price among them, and understand what that assumption is: not an error inside the model but a scope condition outside it, exactly true for this institution acting alone and untestable from this institution's data, since the sample contains no episode in which this firm's own selling moved the market for the reason everyone was selling. Do the work yourself and expect to arrive where your counterparts arrived, because a well-posed question on shared data has a best answer and competence converges on it.

## Orders

([The Risk Officer](persona_the_risk_officer.md) standardises through [Standardising](process_standardising.md) at [the Model Room](place_the_model_room.md), setting [the Same Model](piece_the_same_model.md), and calibrates through [Calibrating](process_calibrating.md), baking in [the Weather](piece_the_weather.md).)

## Implementation

([The Model Room](place_the_model_room.md) is where the good answer is found independently and identically; [Standardising](process_standardising.md) produces the agreement and [Calibrating](process_calibrating.md) fixes the premise, so the two things the trap needs are both delivered by doing the job well.)

## Targets

- [ ] the model is fitted, backtested and validated against the observable history
- [ ] the number reported is the number produced, and no other
- [ ] the exogeneity assumption is stated, and recognised as untestable from inside one firm's data
- [ ] the work is done independently, and arrives where every counterpart independently arrived
- [ ] the convergence is not visible from this seat, there being nothing about this firm that is wrong
