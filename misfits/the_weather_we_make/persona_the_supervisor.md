# Persona: The Supervisor

## Taxonomy

[Supervisor](position_supervisor.md). The one who asks for a number it can inspect, which is the right thing to ask for and the thing that harmonises the answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful and institutional, wanting a measure that is quantitative, auditable and sensitive to conditions rather than a judgement it must take on trust.
Can explain precisely why discretion was rejected: it cannot be checked in advance, compared across firms, or defended to a legislature afterwards.
Has no view about which model, only that there be one and that it be inspectable.

## Action

Runs [Make It Measurable](plan_make_it_measurable.md): through [Requiring](process_requiring.md) at [the Committee](place_the_committee.md), setting [the Limit](piece_the_limit.md).
Wires the measure to a capital rule, so the number obliges rather than advises, which is what makes it a control at all.
Requires validation, backtesting and annual review, all of which the industry supplies and none of which touches the fault.

## Shadow

Not captured and not lazy: it is asking the correct question, and a supervisor who accepted an uninspectable discretion would be failing plainly and would deserve to be told so.
Because the good answers to its question converge, its requirement is what makes every institution hold the same model, so the harmonisation is not an accident of industry laziness but the direct product of a well-drafted rule.
It is the actor with the clearest public duty in the play and the one whose discharge of it couples the system together.

## Tell

Can audit any single firm's model and cannot audit the fact that they are all the same model.
Cannot ask for diversity without asking someone to be measurably worse at describing their own risk, which is not a thing it can put in a rulebook or defend to the firm it names.
