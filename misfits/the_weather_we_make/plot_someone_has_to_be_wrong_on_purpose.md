# Plot: Someone Has to Be Wrong on Purpose

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Committee](place_the_committee.md), and the obvious question of why the models are not simply fixed.

## Action

Through [Diversifying](process_diversifying.md), [the Dissenter](persona_the_dissenter.md) on [Make Them Different](plan_make_them_different.md) concedes at once that every firm's model is a good answer to the question asked, and argues the aggregate instead, then holds [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) by pricing all three corrections. Break the agreement, and you are ordering named institutions to run models measurably worse at describing their own risk. Break the wire, and you are holding capital idle through the good years to be released on the judgement hardest to make at the moment it is needed. Take a crude leverage cap, and you punish the safe balance sheet alongside the dangerous one and push risk to wherever the cap does not reach. The scene closes with the room agreeing sincerely and not acting (Daníelsson).

## Stage

The question deserves the answer it gets, and the dissent is not vindicated by the event. It asks people to bear a certain present cost against a harm that has not happened and cannot be attributed in advance, which is the hardest case any supervisor is ever asked to make.

## Tension

There is no correction that survives contact with the named firm it would fall on, since diversity requires a supervisor to tell a particular institution to measure itself badly and no such sentence can be written into a rulebook, so the play closes on a trade a supervisor must choose rather than an error anyone failed to notice, and the accurate measure and the steady system remain in tension with nobody at fault for it.
