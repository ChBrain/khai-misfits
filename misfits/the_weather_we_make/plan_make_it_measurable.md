# Plan: Make It Measurable

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the supervisor's seat](position_supervisor.md), inside [The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Supervisor](position_supervisor.md)

## Direction

Require a risk measure that is quantitative, auditable and sensitive to conditions, and wire it to a capital rule so that it obliges rather than advises. Reject the alternative and be able to say why: a discretion cannot be checked in advance, cannot be compared across firms, and cannot be defended to a legislature after a failure, so a regime built on it is a regime that cannot be supervised at all. Demand validation, backtesting and annual review. Expect the industry to supply all three, and note that none of them examines whether every firm has arrived at the same model, because that is not a question about any firm and there is no form on which it could be asked.

## Orders

([The Supervisor](persona_the_supervisor.md) requires through [Requiring](process_requiring.md) at [the Committee](place_the_committee.md), setting [the Limit](piece_the_limit.md).)

## Implementation

([The Committee](place_the_committee.md) is where a number becomes an instruction; [Requiring](process_requiring.md) converts a measure into a mandated action, so that agreement between models will become simultaneity of trade, which is not a consequence anyone in the room is in a position to see.)

## Targets

- [ ] the measure is quantitative, auditable and sensitive to conditions
- [ ] it is wired to a capital rule, so it obliges rather than advises
- [ ] the discretionary alternative is rejected, and for the right reasons
- [ ] validation, backtesting and annual review are required and are supplied
- [ ] no instrument exists that would notice the answers converging, which is the whole of the difficulty
