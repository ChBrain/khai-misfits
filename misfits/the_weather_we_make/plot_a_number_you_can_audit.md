# Plot: A Number You Can Audit

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Committee](place_the_committee.md), and the question of what a supervisor is entitled to rely on.

## Action

Through [Requiring](process_requiring.md), [the Supervisor](persona_the_supervisor.md), from [the supervisor's seat](position_supervisor.md), on [Make It Measurable](plan_make_it_measurable.md) sets [the Limit](piece_the_limit.md): a risk measure that is quantitative, auditable and sensitive to conditions, wired to a capital rule so that it obliges rather than advises. The discretionary alternative is rejected and the reasons are given: a judgement cannot be checked in advance, compared across firms, or defended to a legislature after a failure. The scene closes with a number that is now an instruction, and with validation, backtesting and annual review all required, supplied, and touching nothing that matters here.

## Stage

The request is correct and the rejection is correct. A regime resting on a discretion nobody can inspect is a regime that cannot be supervised, and a supervisor who accepted one would be failing plainly.

## Tension

The wire is what makes the measure a control at all, and it is also the half of the mechanism that turns agreement between models into simultaneity of trade, so the seat cannot keep the control and drop the coupling, and nothing in the drafting room is positioned to notice that it has just made every future reading of the measure into a synchronised order.
