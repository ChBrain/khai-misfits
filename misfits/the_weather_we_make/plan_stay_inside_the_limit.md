# Plan: Stay Inside the Limit

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the desk's seat](position_desk.md), inside [The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Desk](position_desk.md)

## Direction

Watch the limit and reduce the moment it binds, into whatever bid is there. Do not hesitate: hesitating is criticised, breaching is a removal, and neither the rulebook nor the seat treats this as a judgement call. If the measure worsens again, cut again. Do not read the thin bid as information about the asset, and understand that you have no way to tell it from the sum of every other desk obeying the same instruction in the same hour, because you can see your own screen and no one else's mandate. Note the one action that would break the loop, which is holding through the move, and note that it is a breach.

## Orders

([The Desk](persona_the_desk.md) unwinds through [Unwinding](process_unwinding.md) at [the Floor](place_the_floor.md), meeting [the Rush](piece_the_rush.md), and again at [the Market](place_the_market.md), answered by [the Tail](piece_the_tail.md).)

## Implementation

([The Floor](place_the_floor.md) is where the instruction lands and is obeyed; [the Market](place_the_market.md) is where every such obedience meets every other, so the same act is prudence at one scale and the price move at the next, and no vantage on the floor reaches the second.)

## Targets

- [ ] the limit is watched and the position reduced the moment it binds
- [ ] there is no hesitation, hesitating being criticised and breaching a removal
- [ ] a worsening measure produces a further cut, each one correct on its own terms
- [ ] the thin bid is not distinguishable from everyone else's compliance, and is not distinguished
- [ ] the one move that would break the loop, holding through it, is the one move forbidden
