# Plot: The Move That Could Not Happen

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Market](place_the_market.md), where the only counterparties available are the others doing the same thing.

## Action

Through [Unwinding](process_unwinding.md), [the Desk](persona_the_desk.md) on [Stay Inside the Limit](plan_stay_inside_the_limit.md) is answered by [the Tail](piece_the_tail.md): the price falls because they are selling, measured volatility rises because the price fell, every model reports that risk has risen again, every limit binds harder, and the loop closes on itself. The scene ends with an extreme that the models had priced as a once-in-many-lifetimes event, delivered on schedule, manufactured by the models rather than missed by them (Daníelsson and Shin).

## Stage

No rule was broken by anyone. Every reduction was correct, mandated and promptly executed, and the account written afterwards, that liquidity vanished, is an accurate description of every other institution obeying its own limits.

## Tension

The failure is not one of foresight, so the ordinary remedy reaches nothing: a better estimate of the same distribution buys no protection, and refitting the models on the crisis itself teaches every one of them to agree about the next one too, which is the same fault carrying more data, and there is no participant standing anywhere in the play from whose position the aggregate is visible at all.
