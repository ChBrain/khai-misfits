# Persona: The Risk Officer

## Taxonomy

[Risk Officer](position_risk_officer.md). The one who builds the best available model and, by building it well, builds everyone else's.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Technical and conscientious, fitting the model to the observable history, backtesting it honestly, and reporting the number it produces.
Would be removed for reporting any other number, and says so plainly when asked why the figure is what it is.
Knows the model's stated assumptions and can recite them, the exogeneity of the price among them.

## Action

Runs [Measure It Properly](plan_measure_it_properly.md): through [Standardising](process_standardising.md) at [the Model Room](place_the_model_room.md), setting [the Same Model](piece_the_same_model.md), and through [Calibrating](process_calibrating.md), baking in [the Weather](piece_the_weather.md).
Estimates on the history available, which is the same history available to everyone else, and validates against it.
Arrives independently at the model its counterparts arrived at independently, because it is the good answer to the question that was asked.

## Shadow

Not a copier and not negligent: it did the work itself, and the convergence is what competence looks like when everyone is answering the same well-posed question on the same data.
Because the exogeneity premise is exactly true for its own institution acting alone, it is not an error in the model but a scope condition, and nothing in this seat's own data could ever falsify it, since a single firm's history contains no instance of everyone selling at once for the reason everyone is selling.
It is the most technically careful actor in the play and the seat where the shared blind spot is manufactured.

## Tell

Reports a number that is correct, validated, reproducible and, in the hour it matters, an instruction issued simultaneously to every competitor.
Cannot test the one assumption that fails, since testing it would require the firm's own actions to have moved the market in the sample, and that is precisely the case the sample does not contain.
