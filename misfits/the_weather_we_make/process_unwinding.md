# Process: Unwinding

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). Borne at [The Floor](place_the_floor.md) and completed at [The Market](place_the_market.md), precipitated by [The Desk](persona_the_desk.md) obeying a binding limit, in [Every Screen Says Sell](plot_every_screen_says_sell.md) and [The Move That Could Not Happen](plot_the_move_that_could_not_happen.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A shock, a correctly measured rise in volatility, and a limit that binds on every desk running the same model in the same hour.

## Direction

From a mandated reduction on one book toward the same reduction on every book, and from there to a price that falls because they are selling and a measured volatility that rises because it fell.

## Lever

That the loop needs no belief about anyone: each desk is executing an instruction, not forecasting what others will do, so the process runs in full even under common knowledge of every other institution's position.

## Echo

[The Rush](piece_the_rush.md) and then [The Tail](piece_the_tail.md), an extreme priced as nearly impossible and produced on schedule by the orderly conduct of everybody holding it.
