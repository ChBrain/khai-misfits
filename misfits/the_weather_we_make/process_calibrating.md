# Process: Calibrating

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). Borne at [The Model Room](place_the_model_room.md), precipitated by [The Risk Officer](persona_the_risk_officer.md) fitting the model to the history it has, in [Priced as Weather](plot_priced_as_weather.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An estimate that must come from somewhere, and a history in which this firm's own trading never moved the market for the reason everyone was trading.

## Direction

From observed returns toward a distribution, carrying in with it the premise that the price is given from outside and does not answer back.

## Lever

That the premise is exactly true for one institution acting alone, so it is a scope condition rather than an error, and no amount of better fitting reaches it, the falsifying case being absent from every sample a single firm can hold (Daníelsson and Shin).

## Echo

[The Weather](piece_the_weather.md), the one assumption the whole apparatus rests on, correct for each and false for all, and untestable from where it is made.
