# Position: Dissenter

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). The seat that sees the whole shape and has nothing costless to offer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The argument, made in advance rather than afterwards: that harmonising the measure raises systemic risk rather than lowering it.
Three real corrections: mandated model diversity, countercyclical buffers, and a risk-insensitive leverage cap.
No version of any of them that a supervisor can defend in the year it is imposed.

## Orders

Make them different: break the agreement between the models, or break the wire from the number to the hand, or accept a cruder measure that cannot be shared into a common signal.
Concede that each firm's model is a good answer to the question asked, because it is, and argue the aggregate rather than the instance.
Accept what the seat does: it prices every correction honestly, which is what makes the argument hard to adopt.

## Loses

The hearing it deserves, since it asks people to bear a certain present cost against a harm that has not happened and cannot be attributed in advance.
Because diversity means naming an institution and ordering it to measure itself badly, and buffers mean idle capital released on the judgement hardest to make at the moment it is needed, and a crude cap means punishing the safe balance sheet alongside the dangerous one, the seat has no proposal that survives contact with the firm it would fall on.
It keeps the correct account and loses the room.

## Drives

Toward the only actor who can name the trade and the only one with nothing free to sell,
setting out three corrections and refusing to call any of them a repair,
so the play closes on a choice a supervisor must make rather than an error somebody failed to notice, and being agreed with sincerely is not the same as being adopted.
