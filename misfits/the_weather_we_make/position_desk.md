# Position: Desk

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). The seat where correct individual conduct and the aggregate outcome are one act seen at two scales.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A book, a binding limit, and a model output that has just worsened.
An unambiguous rulebook: reduce when the limit binds, without argument and without delay.
A view of its own screen and no view of anyone else's instruction.

## Orders

Stay inside the limit: cut the flagged exposure into whatever bid is there, and cut further if the measure worsens.
Do not hesitate, since hesitating is criticised and breaching is a removal.
Accept what the seat does: it is executing a mandated instruction and is not being asked to judge.

## Loses

The option of holding through the move, since holding is a breach, so the one action that would break the loop is the one action forbidden to it.
Because every other desk received the same instruction in the same hour from the same class of model, the seat loses any way to tell a thin bid from the sum of everyone else's compliance, and moving faster to get ahead of the fall is what makes the fall.
It keeps a clean compliance record and loses the price it was selling at.

## Drives

Toward the seat that is right every time it acts and wrong in what its actions come to,
cutting because the limit binds, finding the bid thin, and cutting again,
so the prudence is the market, and the report afterwards that liquidity vanished is an accurate description of everybody else obeying their limits.
