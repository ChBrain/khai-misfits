# Piece: The Same Model

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). Harmonisation: not that the models resemble one another but that they agree and update together, because a well-posed question on shared data has one good answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Model Room](place_the_model_room.md), arrived at independently in every firm and chosen by nobody (Daníelsson and Shin).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the same shock produces a scatter of different readings, some limits binding and others not, so the selling is staggered and the market has counterparties who are not sellers.
It bears the load as the vector the neighbouring plays do not hold. Because the convergence is what competence looks like rather than what laziness looks like, no firm can be faulted for it, no audit of any single model detects it, and nobody working harder cures it, since working harder is what produces it sooner.

## Apparent

Best practice, and it is best practice: every institution using the method the field agrees is correct, which is ordinarily the strongest sign an industry has that it is doing the right thing.
