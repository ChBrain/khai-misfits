# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). The condition and the hedge: it needs a common measure wired to a binding constraint, and every correction buys stability with accuracy.

## Owner

## Place

Operating at [The Committee](place_the_committee.md), where the trade would have to be struck and where each correction is priced rather than recommended (Daníelsson).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play overreaches into a claim it cannot support, that risk measurement is useless or capital regulation a mistake, which the source literature does not say and the house will not stage.
It bears the load as the limit that keeps the finding a finding. Because the trap needs both conditions and lifts where either is missing, and because each of the three corrections is real and each spends something, what is staged is a choice a supervisor must make rather than an error someone failed to notice: diversity means ordering a named firm to measure itself badly, buffers mean idle capital released on the judgement hardest to make when it is needed, and a crude cap means punishing the safe balance sheet with the dangerous one.

## Apparent

A qualification, and an easy one to skip: the sort of hedge that reads as academic caution and is in fact the difference between a design problem and a denunciation.
