# Persona: The Desk

## Taxonomy

[Desk](position_desk.md). The one whose prudence is the market it is selling into, and who cannot see that from where it sits.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Disciplined and unromantic, watching the limit and reducing the position the moment it binds, without argument and without delay.
Would be criticised for hesitating and removed for breaching, so the response is not a judgement call and is not treated as one.
Sees its own screen, its own book, and a price that is falling.

## Action

Runs [Stay Inside the Limit](plan_stay_inside_the_limit.md): through [Unwinding](process_unwinding.md) at [the Floor](place_the_floor.md), meeting [the Rush](piece_the_rush.md), and again at [the Market](place_the_market.md), answered by [the Tail](piece_the_tail.md).
Cuts the exposure the model has flagged, into whatever bid is there.
Finds the bid thin, cuts further as the measure worsens, and does the right thing each time it does it.

## Shadow

Not panicking and not gaming: it is executing a mandated instruction, and an institution whose desks ignored binding limits would be the scandal instead of the case study.
Because every other desk received the same instruction in the same hour from the same class of model, the only counterparties available are the others doing exactly this, so its prudence is the price move, and reducing faster to get ahead of the fall makes the fall.
It is the seat where correct individual conduct and the aggregate outcome are the same act seen at two scales.

## Tell

Reports afterwards that liquidity vanished, which is true, and which describes the other desks obeying their limits.
Cannot hold the position through the move, since holding is a breach, and cannot know that the thin bid is the sum of everyone else's compliance rather than a signal about the asset.
