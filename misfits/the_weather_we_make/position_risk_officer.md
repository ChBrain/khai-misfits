# Position: Risk Officer

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). The seat that does the work itself and arrives where everyone else arrived, because it is the good answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The observable history, which is the same history everyone else has.
A well-posed question from the supervisor, and the competence to answer it correctly.
An assumption it can state and cannot test: that the price is given from outside and does not respond to what this firm does about it.

## Orders

Measure it properly: fit the model to the history, backtest it honestly, validate it, and report the number it produces.
Report that number and no other, since reporting any other is the thing this seat exists not to do.
Accept what the seat does: it answers a shared question on shared data, and competence converges.

## Loses

The ability to falsify the one premise that fails, since its own firm's history contains no episode in which its own selling moved the market for the reason everyone was selling.
Because the exogeneity of the price is exactly true for this institution acting alone, the assumption is not an error inside the model but a scope condition outside it, and no amount of better fitting reaches it.
It keeps a model that is correct about its own firm and loses any view of the model it shares.

## Drives

Toward the most technically careful actor in the play and the seat where the shared blind spot is made,
fitting, testing, validating, and arriving independently at what every counterpart independently arrived at,
so doing the work properly is what produces the agreement, and doing it better would produce it sooner.
