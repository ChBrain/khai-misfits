# Plot: Priced as Weather

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Model Room](place_the_model_room.md), and a distribution that has to be estimated from something.

## Action

Through [Calibrating](process_calibrating.md), [the Risk Officer](persona_the_risk_officer.md) on [Measure It Properly](plan_measure_it_properly.md) bakes in [the Weather](piece_the_weather.md): the price is taken as given from outside, a thing that happens to the holder rather than a thing the holder helps make. It is stated in the documentation and disputed by nobody, because for this institution acting alone it is exactly true. The scene closes with the whole apparatus resting on one sentence, correct at the scale of a firm and false at the scale of the system.

## Stage

It is a modelling convention and an unremarkable one. Prices are the environment and positions are the decision, no textbook treats that as controversial, and the officer who wrote it down was not making a claim so much as describing what a fitted distribution is.

## Tension

The premise cannot be tested from where it is made, since this firm's history contains no episode in which its own selling moved the market for the reason everyone was selling, so the falsifying case is precisely the one no single institution's data can hold, and a scope condition that no available evidence can reach will not be revised by anyone who is being careful.
