# Plan: Make Them Different

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the dissenter's seat](position_dissenter.md), inside [The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Dissenter](position_dissenter.md)

## Direction

Say it in advance rather than afterwards: harmonising the measure raises systemic risk rather than lowering it. Concede immediately that each firm's model is a good answer to the question asked, because it is, and argue the aggregate rather than the instance, since an argument that any single model is wrong will be refuted by the backtest and will deserve to be. Then set out the three corrections and price each honestly. Break the agreement between the models, which means ordering named institutions to run models measurably worse at describing their own risk. Break the wire from the number to the hand, which means countercyclical buffers, capital idle through the good years and released by the judgement hardest to make at the moment it is needed. Or accept a cruder measure that cannot be shared into a common signal, which punishes the safe balance sheet alongside the dangerous one and pushes risk to wherever the cap does not reach. Do not present any of them as free.
