# Process: Requiring

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). Borne at [The Committee](place_the_committee.md), precipitated by [The Supervisor](persona_the_supervisor.md) rejecting an uninspectable discretion, in [A Number You Can Audit](plot_a_number_you_can_audit.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A duty to supervise something that can be checked in advance, compared across firms, and defended after a failure.

## Direction

From a judgement toward an instruction: a quantitative, auditable, condition-sensitive measure, wired to a capital rule so that it obliges rather than advises.

## Lever

That without the wire a shared model would produce only shared opinions, and with it agreement between models becomes simultaneity of trade, which is not a consequence visible from inside a drafting room (Daníelsson and colleagues).

## Echo

[The Limit](piece_the_limit.md), a number that now compels, and a regime auditable firm by firm and blind to the only property that would make the audits add up.
