# Piece: The Weather

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). The exogeneity premise: the price treated as given from outside, a thing that happens to the holder rather than a thing the holder helps make.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Model Room](place_the_model_room.md), carried into the estimate rather than stated as a claim, since every fitted distribution assumes it (Daníelsson and Shin).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by modelling the price as responding to the holder's own reaction, and the measure stops being a barometer and starts being a mirror, which is a different instrument and one nobody has been asked for.
It bears the load as the premise that is true and false at two different scales. Because it holds exactly for one institution acting alone, it is a scope condition rather than an error, and it cannot be falsified from any single firm's history, since that history contains no episode in which the firm's own selling moved the market for the reason everyone was selling.

## Apparent

A modelling convention, and an unremarkable one: prices are the environment, positions are the decision, and no textbook treats that as controversial.
