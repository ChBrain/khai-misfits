# Pitch: The Model Is Validated

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Assured and procedural, the tone of a control that works.
The model is validated, backtested against the history, reviewed annually, and wired to a limit so the number obliges rather than advises.
Nothing false in it: the measure is quantitative, auditable and sensitive to conditions, which is exactly what a supervisor should require, since the alternative is a discretion nobody can inspect in advance or defend afterwards.

## Undertow

Under the validation, a premise that holds for one holder and fails for all of them at once.
The model treats the price as weather, given from outside, which is true while you are the only one acting on it, and every good answer to an auditable risk-sensitive measure converges on the same model estimated on the same history, so they do not merely resemble each other, they agree and update together (Daníelsson and Shin).
No reckless bank and no captured supervisor: nobody games anything, nobody hides anything, and the loop runs on institutions doing precisely what they were told to do.

## Nerve

The comfort of a number you can check, and the catch that everyone checked the same one:
that a measure shared by all and wired to a limit turns agreement between models into simultaneity of trade, so the prudence is the market,
and the model is validated is the sentence under which a system arranges to move as one body.

## Echo

After the run: an extreme priced as a once-in-many-lifetimes event, arriving for the second time in a decade, manufactured by the models rather than missed by them, and no rule broken by anybody (Daníelsson and Shin).
And the remedy, real and priced: require model diversity and you are ordering some institutions to run models measurably worse at describing their own risk; hold countercyclical buffers and capital sits idle for years to be released on the judgement hardest to make at the moment it is needed; impose a crude leverage cap and the safe balance sheet is punished with the dangerous one (Daníelsson).
And the bound held in view: it needs a common measure wired to a binding constraint and lifts where either is missing, so the whole of it is that a barometer everybody obeys at once stops describing the weather and starts making it.
