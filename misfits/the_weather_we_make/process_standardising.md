# Process: Standardising

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). Borne at [The Model Room](place_the_model_room.md), precipitated by [The Risk Officer](persona_the_risk_officer.md) answering a well-posed question correctly, in [Everyone Buys the Same Instrument](plot_everyone_buys_the_same_instrument.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A shared question, shared data, and competence: the good answers to an auditable risk-sensitive measure are the same answer.

## Direction

From many independent modellers toward one model: not resemblance but agreement, the outputs moving together because the inputs and the method are common.

## Lever

That the convergence is what competence looks like rather than what laziness looks like, so it cannot be corrected by anyone working harder, and working harder produces it sooner (Daníelsson and Shin).

## Echo

[The Same Model](piece_the_same_model.md), the load-bearing vector, an agreement nobody chose and no single firm's audit can see, since it is not a fact about any firm.
