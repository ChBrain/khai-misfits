# Piece: The Limit

## Taxonomy

[The Weather We Make](play_the_weather_we_make.md). The wire from the number to the hand: a capital or exposure rule keyed to the measure, so the measure obliges rather than advises.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Committee](place_the_committee.md), written into the regime for the right reasons, since a control that only advises is not a control.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the shared model produces shared opinions and nothing else: a room full of institutions that think the same thing and trade whenever they choose, which harms no one.
It bears the load as the converter. Because a measure tied to a rule is an instruction, agreement between models becomes simultaneity of trade, so this piece is what turns a common belief into a common act, and it is the half of the mechanism that a supervisor could in principle remove and cannot remove without giving up the control itself.

## Apparent

Rigour, and it is rigour: the difference between a risk framework that binds and one that is filed, which every supervisor and every board has learned to insist on.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [A Number You Can Audit](plot_a_number_you_can_audit.md), where a measure becomes a mandate.
It works together with [The Same Model](piece_the_same_model.md), the two conditions the trap needs, neither of which is sufficient alone.
