# Position: Component

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). The seat of one machine among many, meeting its target and occasionally busy with correct work.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A response time of about ten milliseconds and a ninety-ninth percentile of a second.
Correct configuration, adequate provisioning and no fault of any kind.
Scheduled activities that occupy it briefly and leave it better.

## Orders

Answer.
Occasionally take a second instead.
Return a correct result either way.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat establishes the play's innocence: there is no threshold this fails, no defect to remove, and a better machine of the same kind changes the rate and not the structure.
Because the slow case is maintenance rather than malfunction, no engineering attention converts this seat into one that is never slow.
It ends having met its target while the service did not.

## Drives

Toward answering quickly,
right in every single instance,
so the seat that is good enough is the one the whole difficulty is built out of.
