# Plot: Ten Milliseconds, Almost Always

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

One machine of many, correctly configured and meeting its target.

## Action

[Answering](process_answering.md): [The Server](persona_the_server.md), in [the component seat](position_component.md), receives a call and returns a correct result.

## Stage

[The Server](persona_the_server.md), on [Answer in Ten Milliseconds](plan_answer_in_ten_milliseconds.md), answers in about ten milliseconds ninety-nine times in a hundred, and takes a second on the hundredth.

## Tension

This is a good server, and that is the whole point of establishing it first.
Ninety-nine times in a hundred is a figure most systems would be pleased with. Nothing here fails a threshold, nothing is under-provisioned, nothing is neglected, and there is no defect available to remove. **The play's entire cause is going to be assembled out of components that pass every test anybody would set them**, which is what puts the failure outside competence as well as outside conduct.
