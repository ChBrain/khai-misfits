# Position: Duplicate

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). The seat of the second copy: cheap, effective, and a bet rather than a cure.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Spare capacity, which a fan-out system has.
A short delay before issuing, so the copy goes only where the first looks like going long.
Two machines whose interruptions are independent.

## Orders

Send again.
Take the first answer.
Discard the other.

## Loses

Nothing, and its bound is the play's ending: the variability is untouched, and what is bought is the improbability of two independent machines pausing at the same instant.
Because the interruptions are correct work nobody wants to stop, the design goal becomes tolerating the tail rather than removing it, which is what the people who named the problem recommend.
It ends having improved the service greatly and changed none of its causes.

## Drives

Toward a faster answer,
never once addressing why the first was slow,
so the best available remedy is one that covers the problem rather than solving it.
