# Pitch: Every One of Them Was Fast

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry, instrumented, unhurried.
The register of a latency histogram beside a timeline: how long, how often, at which percentile, on which machine.
Nothing indignant anywhere in it, because nothing failed and every part did the job it was built for.

## Undertow

Under the response time, two facts that do not negotiate.
A request that needs every answer is not finished until the last answer. Every machine is occasionally busy with correct work.
Between those two, the rare case stops being rare in exact proportion to how many machines are asked.

## Nerve

The unease of a system where every component meets its target,
every slow moment is a machine doing something necessary,
and the service is slow most of the time with nothing anywhere to point at.

## Echo

After the run: a mean response time of eleven milliseconds, accurate,
a service that misses a second on nearly two requests in three,
and a fix that consists of asking the question twice and ignoring the second answer.
