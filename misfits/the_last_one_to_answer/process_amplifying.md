# Process: Amplifying

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). A per-machine rate becoming a service rate, by the requirement that all of them be fast at once.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A fan-out, and a per-call probability of being slow.
At [the wait](place_the_wait.md), on every request.

## Direction

Toward the rare case becoming the ordinary one.
The step can be checked rather than believed, which is why the play states it in the open: the request is fast only if **all** hundred are fast, which happens at ninety-nine hundredths raised to the hundredth power, about thirty-seven per cent, so **sixty-three per cent** of requests are slow.
And it runs the wrong way with every improvement to the system's scale: more shards means more throughput and a worse tail, from the same components.

## Lever

[The arithmetic](piece_the_arithmetic.md), which is not a model and has no parameters to argue about.
Because it is arithmetic rather than a claim about any system, no better engineering of the components changes its form, only the base it is raised to.

## Echo

A service that is slow most of the time, made entirely of parts that are fast.
Nothing in the fleet has changed, and the whole has.
