# Place: The Fleet

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). Where the hundred machines are, each meeting its target, each occasionally busy with something necessary.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Machines, correctly configured and adequately provisioned.
Scheduled activities running independently on each of them: reclamation, compaction, rebuilds, daemons, power states.

## Holds

The play's whole supply of causes, none of which is a fault.
It holds the fact that makes the mechanism scale the wrong way: the activities are independent across machines, so the chance that **some** machine is busy at a given instant rises with the number of machines and does not fall with the quality of any one of them.

## Offers

Throughput, which is the reason to have a hundred machines rather than one, and it delivers it.

## Withheld

Any single point to look at.
No machine here is the problem, no machine is the same problem twice, and the property that matters belongs to the set rather than to any member of it.
