# Plan: Ask Twice and Take the First

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the duplicate seat](position_duplicate.md), inside [The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Duplicate](position_duplicate.md)

## Direction

Send the request again, elsewhere, either at once or after a short wait, and take whichever answer arrives first. Spend a few per cent of extra work to buy the tail back.

## Orders

([The Hedge](persona_the_hedge.md) issues a duplicate, takes the first answer to arrive, and discards the other.)

## Implementation

([The Fleet](place_the_fleet.md), which has the spare capacity; the [hedging](process_hedging.md) process costing [the extra two per cent](piece_the_extra_two_per_cent.md); against [the tail](piece_the_tail.md) rather than against its causes.)

## Targets

- [ ] it works, and works in ordinary systems rather than only in one company's
- [ ] issuing the copy after a short wait keeps the extra total work to a few per cent
- [ ] what is bought is the improbability of two independent machines pausing at once
- [ ] the variability itself is untouched, so the tail is covered rather than removed
