# Piece: The Extra Two Per Cent

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). The additional total work a delayed duplicate costs, against the tail it removes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the fleet](place_the_fleet.md), as capacity spent on answers that will be discarded.
Answered nowhere: it is a price rather than a problem.

## Load Bearing

Without it the remedy is expensive and the play ends without one.
It is load bearing because the bargain is **so good that it exposes what is really going on**: a few per cent of duplicated work buys back most of the tail, which is only possible because the underlying causes are independent and brief, and it treats none of them.

## Apparent

An efficiency, and it is one.
It reads as a clever optimisation, and what it actually is, a bet that two machines will not pause at the same instant, is a different kind of thing from a fix.

## Yearbook

Spent in [hedging](process_hedging.md), against [the tail](piece_the_tail.md).
The variability remains exactly as it was, which is why the goal becomes tolerating the tail rather than removing it.
