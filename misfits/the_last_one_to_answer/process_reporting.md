# Process: Reporting

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). The response times being aggregated into the number people look at.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Every answer, timed.
At [the dashboard](place_the_dashboard.md), summarised.

## Direction

Toward an honest and useful summary, which is what a mean is.
Nobody chose an average in order to conceal a tail: it is the natural statistic, it is correctly computed, and it is dominated by the ninety-nine because that is what dominance by the majority means.
So an accurate figure sits on a screen while the service misses its target most of the time, and the figure is not lying and is not evidence.

## Lever

Aggregation, which is what makes a fleet's behaviour readable at all.
Because the harm is a property of the distribution's shape, no increase in accuracy or frequency at this step recovers it.

## Echo

A flat graph and a slow service.
Seeing the difference requires somebody to ask for a high percentile, which is a different question from the one the instrument was pointed at.
