# Process: Hedging

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). Sending the request twice and taking whichever answer arrives first.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Spare capacity, which a fan-out system has.
At [the fleet](place_the_fleet.md), as a duplicate issued at once or after a short delay.

## Direction

Toward a much shorter tail, cheaply.
This works, and the general result is not one company's trick: replicating an operation across diverse resources and taking the first to complete reduces mean and tail latency in ordinary systems, in name lookups, in database queries and in packet forwarding.
Issuing the copy only after a brief wait confines the duplication to the requests that look like going long, which is what keeps the extra total work to a few per cent.

## Lever

Capacity, traded deliberately for latency, which is the seat's whole design.
Because the causes are correct work nobody wants to stop, this is the lever that exists rather than the one anybody would prefer.

## Echo

A service that meets its target, built on components that still pause exactly as often as before.
What was bought is the improbability of two independent machines pausing at the same instant, which is usually a good bet and is a bet.
