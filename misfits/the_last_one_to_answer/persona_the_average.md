# Persona: The Average

## Taxonomy

[Reported](position_reported.md). The mean response time: excellent, accurate, watched by everybody, and silent about all of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Honest and well computed.
Dominated by the ninety-nine fast answers, because that is what a mean is.
Not hiding anything on purpose: nobody chose it in order to conceal a tail.

## Action

Is measured, aggregated and displayed.
Reports a small number, correctly.
Stays small while the service degrades, because the degradation lives in a part of the distribution it does not weight.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is why the structure survives being watched: the number is true, is the natural one to compute, and moves hardly at all when nearly two requests in three miss their target.
Because the harm is in the shape of the distribution rather than in its centre, no improvement to the accuracy or the frequency of this measurement recovers it.
It ends accurate and uninformative, which are not in tension.

## Tell

Says: eleven milliseconds.
True. And the service missed a second on most requests that hour.
