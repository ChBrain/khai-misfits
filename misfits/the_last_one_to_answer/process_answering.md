# Process: Answering

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). One machine receiving a call and returning a correct result, quickly almost always.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A call arriving at [the fleet](place_the_fleet.md).
A machine that is correctly configured and meeting its target.

## Direction

Toward a correct answer in about ten milliseconds.
There is nothing to criticise at this step and the play does not try: ninety-nine times in a hundred is a figure most systems would be pleased with, and the hundredth is not a failure either, since the machine is reclaiming memory or compacting a log or yielding a cache to a neighbour.
So the whole of the play's cause is assembled here out of things that are working.

## Lever

The machine's own time, which is occupied briefly by activities that improve it.
Because those activities are correct, there is no lever here that makes the seat never slow.

## Echo

A correct answer, on time or a second late.
[The ninety-ninth percentile](piece_the_ninety_ninth_percentile.md) is the only record that the second case happened at all.
