# Persona: The Hedge

## Taxonomy

[Duplicate](position_duplicate.md). The second copy of the request, sent elsewhere, with whichever answer arrives first taken and the other discarded.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Effective, cheap and general: it works in ordinary systems and not only in one company's.
Costs a few per cent of extra total work when the second copy is sent only after a short wait.
Not a trick and not a workaround: it converts spare capacity into lower latency, deliberately.

## Action

Issues a duplicate, either immediately or after a brief delay.
Takes the first answer to arrive.
Cancels or ignores the other.

## Shadow

Nothing, and its bound is the play's honest ending: the variability is untouched, and what the seat buys is a bet that two independent machines will not be interrupted at the same instant, which is usually a good bet and is a bet.
Because the underlying interruptions are correct work that nobody wants to stop, the design goal becomes tolerating the tail rather than removing it, which the people who named the problem say plainly.
It ends having improved the service greatly and having changed none of the causes.

## Tell

Says: ask twice, keep the first.
Which is a strange sentence to have to write, and is the best answer there is.
