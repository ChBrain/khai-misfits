# Persona: The Last Reply

## Taxonomy

[Pacer](position_pacer.md). Whichever answer happens to arrive last: the one that sets the time, having done nothing different from the others.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Correct, complete and no worse than any other reply.
Slow only on this occasion, and by an amount that would be unremarkable on its own.
Not identifiable in advance and not the same machine twice.

## Action

Arrives.
Sets the response time of the whole request, alone.
Is indistinguishable, afterwards, from the ninety-nine that were quick.

## Shadow

Nothing of its own, and the seat is why the harm has no address: the machine that paced the request is not a bad machine, is not the same one next time, and did nothing that any of the others would not do eventually.
Because the seat is occupied by a different party on every occasion, there is no component to fix, remove or hold responsible.
It ends having decided the outcome and being untraceable as a cause.

## Tell

Says: here is your answer.
Correct, complete, and nine hundred and ninety milliseconds after the other ninety-nine.
