# Position: Interruption

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). The seat of correct work that occupies a machine at an inconvenient moment.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A legitimate purpose: reclaiming memory, compacting a log, rebuilding data, yielding a shared resource, leaving a power-saving state.
Low average cost and a brief burst when it runs.
A designer who chose it and would choose it again.

## Orders

Run when due.
Occupy the machine.
Leave it better than before.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat removes the last place to point: the slow moment is maintenance, contention or power management rather than failure, so eliminating it would cost more than the pause does.
Because such activities are many and independent across a fleet, the chance that some machine is busy rises with the number of machines rather than falling with the quality of any one.
It ends having improved a machine and lengthened a request.

## Drives

Toward a healthier machine,
right in every single instance,
so the seat that maintains the system is the seat that paces it.
