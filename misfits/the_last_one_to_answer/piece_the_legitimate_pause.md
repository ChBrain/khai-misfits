# Piece: The Legitimate Pause

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). Reclamation, compaction, rebuilds, daemons, contention and power states: correct work at an inconvenient instant.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the fleet](place_the_fleet.md), independently on every machine.
Answered nowhere: removing the activity costs more than the pause does.

## Load Bearing

Without it the slow case is a defect and somebody fixes it.
It is load bearing because it puts the cause **outside anybody's competence as well as their conduct**: every interruption is a thing a designer chose, would choose again, and could defend in a review, so there is no bad component anywhere in the fleet to find.

## Apparent

Overhead, or jitter, or noise.
It reads as the sort of thing better engineering removes, and the reason better engineering does not, that each item on the list is doing something necessary, is not what jitter looks like on a graph.

## Yearbook

Produced during [answering](process_answering.md), recorded only as [the ninety-ninth percentile](piece_the_ninety_ninth_percentile.md).
Its independence across machines is what makes the chance of some machine being busy rise with the fleet.
