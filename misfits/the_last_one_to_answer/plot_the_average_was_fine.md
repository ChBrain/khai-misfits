## Stage

## Tension

The number is true, and that is why the structure survives being watched.
Nobody chose a mean in order to conceal a tail. It is the natural statistic, it is honestly computed, and it is dominated by the ninety-nine fast answers because that is what a mean does. So an accurate figure sits on a screen above a service that is slow most of the time, and the figure is neither lying nor evidence. **The harm is in the shape of the distribution rather than in its centre**, so measuring more often or more precisely recovers none of it, and seeing it at all requires somebody to ask for a high percentile, which is a different question from the one the instrument was pointed at.
