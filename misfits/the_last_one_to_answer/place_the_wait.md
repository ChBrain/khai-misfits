# Place: The Wait

## Taxonomy

[The Last One to Answer](play_the_last_one_to_answer.md). Where a request sits between issuing a hundred calls and receiving the hundredth reply.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A request with ninety-nine answers in hand and one outstanding.
Milliseconds, usually. Occasionally most of a second.

## Holds

The conversion, and the only place it happens.
It is where a distribution of per-machine times becomes a single service time, by the rule that the last one governs, and where a property that was rare per call becomes ordinary per request.

## Offers

A complete and correct result, which is what fanning out was for.

## Withheld

Any partial credit.
Ninety-nine fast replies are worth nothing here without the hundredth, so all the excellence in the fleet is held hostage to one arrival nobody can predict.
