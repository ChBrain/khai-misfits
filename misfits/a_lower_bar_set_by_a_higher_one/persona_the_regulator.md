# Persona: The Regulator

## Taxonomy

[Regulator](position_regulator.md). The one with a gate for pills and none for procedures.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A licensing dossier for a compound, requiring randomised controlled evidence before anybody may sell it.

## Action

Refuses the drug without the trials.
Has no equivalent instrument standing between a procedure and its adoption.

## Shadow

Holds a gate that was built where a gate could be built.
The pharmaceutical requirement is one of the great public health achievements and nobody proposes weakening it, and it exists partly because a placebo pill is cheap and harmless, so demanding the comparison was demanding something obtainable. No such thing could be required of a procedure without requiring the sham, so the asymmetry is not an oversight in the law: it is the law declining to compel what the ethics committee would refuse.

## Tell

Guards one door thoroughly, because the other has no frame.
