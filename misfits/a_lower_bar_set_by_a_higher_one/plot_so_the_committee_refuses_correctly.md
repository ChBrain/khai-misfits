# Plot: So the Committee Refuses, Correctly

## Taxonomy

[A Lower Bar Set by a Higher One](play_a_lower_bar_set_by_a_higher_one.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A protocol proposing to anaesthetise volunteers and do nothing inside them.

## Action

[Reviewing the protocol](process_reviewing_the_protocol.md): [the committee](persona_the_committee.md), holding [Committee](position_committee.md), weighs the harm against what would be learned and refuses.

## Stage

A protected volunteer, a minuted reason, and a question left open.

## Tension

The rule is right, is old, and closes the only clean comparison, and those three facts are unrelated to one another.
Harm to a participant must be justified by what is learned, which is the oldest rule in research ethics and would stand if this question had never been asked. The bar therefore rises with the cost of the control arm, and the evidence available falls as it rises: **one movement rather than two.** There is nobody to persuade and no conflict of values to adjudicate, because the seat refusing is not weighing science against ethics but applying a proportionality test that comes out where it comes out.
