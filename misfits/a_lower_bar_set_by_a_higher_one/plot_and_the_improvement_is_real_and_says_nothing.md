## Tension

The most abundant evidence in the field is compatible with either answer and always will be.
People present at their worst and drift back toward their average, painful conditions fluctuate, and a serious intervention by somebody trusted moves reported symptoms by a measurable amount. Each is sufficient alone to produce exactly this report from an operation that did nothing. So nobody is being fooled by a weak observation: the observation is strong, is accurate, and is of a quantity that does not distinguish the cases.
