# Persona: The Surgeon

## Taxonomy

[Surgeon](position_surgeon.md). The one who does the operation and wants the answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A theatre list, a technique learned from somebody who learned it from somebody, and patients who come back better.

## Action

Operates.
Sees the improvement, week after week, year after year.

## Shadow

Has the best evidence available to the seat and it is the evidence that cannot distinguish the cases.
Patients do improve, reliably and visibly, and that is a fact rather than an impression. It is also exactly what would be seen if the operation did nothing, because people come when they are worst, recover on their own, and believe in what was done to them. So the seat's whole clinical experience, however long and however honest, is silent on the question, and the seat is usually the first to say so.

## Tell

Watches people get better and cannot tell why.
