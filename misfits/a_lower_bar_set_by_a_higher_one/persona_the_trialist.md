# Persona: The Trialist

## Taxonomy

[Trialist](position_trialist.md). The one designing round the missing arm.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A protocol, a funder, a statistician, and a comparison that cannot be written into it.

## Action

Designs the best available study: expertise-based, registry-linked, staged.
Publishes what it can support.

## Shadow

Has good instruments for every question except the one at issue.
Comparing two techniques, following outcomes at scale, staging the introduction of a procedure: all of these are real and answer real questions. None of them controls for the thing that needs controlling, which is what the patient and the surgeon both believe is happening, because that belief is present in every arm of every design that does not include a sham. So the substitutes are not weaker versions of the missing comparison. They are answers to adjacent questions.

## Tell

Runs excellent trials beside the one that would settle it.
