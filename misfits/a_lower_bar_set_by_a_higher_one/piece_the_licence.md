# Piece: The Licence

## Taxonomy

[A Lower Bar Set by a Higher One](play_a_lower_bar_set_by_a_higher_one.md). The gate on one class, and the absence of one on the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Required by [the regulator](persona_the_regulator.md) through [licensing on what there is](process_licensing_on_what_there_is.md), for compounds and not for procedures.

## Load Bearing

The asymmetry is not an oversight in the law but the law declining to compel what an ethics committee would refuse.
Requiring randomised controlled evidence before a compound may be sold is among the great public health achievements, and it was demandable partly because the comparison it demands is cheap and harmless to make. The same requirement placed on a procedure would be a requirement to run the sham. So one class is held to the strongest standard in medicine and the class beside it to what its ethics permit, and the difference between them tracks how the control arm is made rather than anything about how well either works.

## Apparent

An inconsistency somebody should tidy up.

## Yearbook

Is a good rule where it applies, absent where it cannot be, and the whole of the asymmetry.
