# Play: A Lower Bar Set by a Higher One

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

A Lower Bar Set by a Higher One.
A drug is compared with a dummy as a matter of course, because the dummy costs its control arm a swallow. The same comparison for an operation costs the control arm an anaesthetic, a wound and a recovery, so an ethics committee usually refuses it, correctly. One class of treatment is therefore held to the strongest standard in medicine and the class beside it to whatever its ethics permit.

## Arc

The bend is this: the rule that protects the volunteer is the rule that decides how much anybody knows, and it is right.
It runs on a comparison that is cheap in one class.
[A Pill Costs Its Control Arm Nothing](plot_a_pill_costs_its_control_arm_nothing.md). [The Licence](piece_the_licence.md) exists because a placebo tablet asks almost nothing of anybody, which is a fact about pills rather than about evidence.
And expensive in the next.
[And a Procedure's Control Costs Them an Operation](plot_and_a_procedures_control_costs_them_an_operation.md). [The Sham Incision](piece_the_sham_incision.md) requires the control arm to believe it was treated, and there is no lesser version, since an unblinded control measures the thing being controlled for.
So the committee refuses.
[So the Committee Refuses, Correctly](plot_so_the_committee_refuses_correctly.md). The bar rises with the harm and the evidence falls as it rises, which is one movement and not two, and there is no conflict of values in it to adjudicate.
Then [And the Improvement Is Real and Says Nothing](plot_and_the_improvement_is_real_and_says_nothing.md).
[The Improvement](piece_the_improvement.md) is genuine and is exactly what an operation doing nothing would produce, so the most abundant evidence in the field is compatible with either answer.
And [when the trial does run it sometimes overturns the practice](plot_when_the_trial_does_run_it_sometimes_overturns_the_practice.md). Where the comparison has been made, established operations have failed it, which is how anybody knows the gap carries real error rather than a formality.
And [the substitutes cannot reach it](plot_and_the_substitutes_cannot_reach_it.md). [The Trialist](persona_the_trialist.md) builds every design that does not need a sham, and belief about what was done sits in every arm of all of them.

## Triggers

**[A Pill Costs Its Control Arm Nothing](plot_a_pill_costs_its_control_arm_nothing.md)**
Cue: a compound seeking a licence, and a placebo that looks identical. Closes when the great requirement is seen to rest on the comparison being cheap and harmless, which is a fact about pills.

**[And a Procedure's Control Costs Them an Operation](plot_and_a_procedures_control_costs_them_an_operation.md)**
Cue: the same design, proposed for a procedure. Closes when blinding is found to require an anaesthetic and a wound, with no lesser version available.

**[So the Committee Refuses, Correctly](plot_so_the_committee_refuses_correctly.md)**
Cue: a protocol proposing to cut volunteers and do nothing inside. Closes when the bar rising with the harm and the evidence falling as it rises are shown to be one movement.

**[And the Improvement Is Real and Says Nothing](plot_and_the_improvement_is_real_and_says_nothing.md)**
Cue: a patient with less pain six weeks after. Closes when the strongest impression available is found to be the weakest evidence there is.

**[When the Trial Does Run It Sometimes Overturns the Practice](plot_when_the_trial_does_run_it_sometimes_overturns_the_practice.md)**
Cue: the rare approved sham-controlled comparison. Closes when established procedures are found to have failed it, so the gap carries demonstrated error.

**[And the Substitutes Cannot Reach It](plot_and_the_substitutes_cannot_reach_it.md)**
Cue: every good design that does not need a sham. Closes when belief about what was done is found in every arm of all of them, so no accumulation converges on the missing comparison.

## Stakes

Whether a class of treatment can be known to work when the test that would show it may not be run.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a requirement that was demandable because it was cheap, a comparison priced by what the intervention is, a proportionality rule that is right and closes the question, an improvement that is real and uninformative, a handful of trials that did run and overturned what they tested, and substitutes that answer adjacent questions, and away from any account in which surgeons resisted scrutiny or a committee obstructed science. The production cannot fault the committee, because harm to a volunteer must be justified by what is learned and that rule would stand if nobody had ever asked this question; and it cannot fault the surgeon, because the improvement is real and a career of watching it settles nothing. What is left to fight over is not whether to test but what to call a standard of evidence set by the cost of a control arm rather than by anything about the treatment.
