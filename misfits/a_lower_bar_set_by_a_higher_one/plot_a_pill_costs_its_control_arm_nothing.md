# Plot: A Pill Costs Its Control Arm Nothing

## Taxonomy

[A Lower Bar Set by a Higher One](play_a_lower_bar_set_by_a_higher_one.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A compound seeking a licence, and a placebo that looks identical.

## Action

[Licensing on what there is](process_licensing_on_what_there_is.md): [the regulator](persona_the_regulator.md), holding [Regulator](position_regulator.md), refuses the compound without randomised controlled evidence.

## Stage

The strongest standard in medicine, routinely met.

## Tension

The requirement was demandable because the comparison it demands is cheap and harmless, which is a fact about pills rather than about evidence.
A placebo tablet costs its control arm a swallow and some inconvenience, so asking for the comparison asks almost nothing of anybody, and the rule that followed is among the great public health achievements. Nothing about the logic of the trial depends on the intervention being a pill: what depends on it is whether the comparison can be obtained at all.
