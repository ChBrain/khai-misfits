# Persona: The Patient

## Taxonomy

[Patient](position_patient.md). The one who gets better, genuinely.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Two years of pain, a waiting list, an operation, and a knee that hurts less afterwards.

## Action

Reports the improvement, accurately.
Recommends the surgeon to a neighbour.

## Shadow

Is telling the truth and the truth is not evidence for what everybody takes it to be evidence for.
The pain really is less: nothing here is imagined, exaggerated or misremembered. But the seat arrived at its worst, which is when people present; the condition fluctuates; and a great deal was done to this person by somebody they trusted, which alters reported pain by a measurable amount. All three would produce this same report from an operation that did nothing at all.

## Tell

Gives an honest account that cannot be used.
