# Position: Offtaker

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md). The seat that funds the fleet by hiding the fact.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A balance sheet able to carry price risk, and lenders who will not lend without somebody carrying it.
A captured price nobody can forecast far enough ahead to finance against.
One instrument that solves that: a long contract at a fixed price.

## Orders

Guarantee the price, because nothing gets financed against a revenue line nobody can predict.
Take the price risk onto this book, which is the service being paid for and the reason the seat exists.
Accept what the seat does: without this instrument the fleet does not get built at all.

## Loses

The signal, since a contract paying the same whatever hour the power arrives in removes the price information from the party best placed to respond to it.
Because the risk it was hired to take away is exactly the signal that would fund the correlation-breaking assets, no contract exists that both finances the build and preserves the incentive, and the seat cannot write one.
It keeps a bankable project and loses the only pressure that would have produced the storage.

## Drives

Toward the seat whose remedy is the one anybody will actually pay for,
offering the fixed price, reaching financial close, and delivering a generator with no reason to care when it generates,
so the instrument that makes the build possible is the instrument that makes the problem invisible to the people who could solve it.
