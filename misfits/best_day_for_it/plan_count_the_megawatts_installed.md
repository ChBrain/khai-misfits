# Plan: Count the Megawatts Installed

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the policymaker's seat](position_policymaker.md), inside [Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Policymaker](position_policymaker.md)

## Direction

Report progress on the two measures the field standardised on: installed capacity and levelised cost. Use them correctly and report them accurately, because both are genuinely improving and a departure from the standard measures would have to be justified to people who would want to know why. Hold in view what they cannot do: a levelised cost averages over output without ever asking when the output arrives, so it compares an intermittent technology with a dispatchable one as though the two produced the same product, and no amount of better data repairs that. So when the question is whether the build pays, change the measure rather than gathering more of the same: measure the captured price, the value factor, the ratio of what the fleet actually earns to the average price. And when that measure shows a decline, report it as what it is, real and quantified and a function of penetration rather than a fixed property of the technology, mitigable by flexibility elsewhere in the system, and a reason to count value rather than a reason to stop building.

## Targets

- [ ] installed capacity and levelised cost are reported accurately, since both genuinely improve
- [ ] the limit of a levelised cost is stated: it averages over output and cannot ask when the output arrives
- [ ] the captured price and the value factor are measured alongside the standard series
- [ ] any decline found is reported as a function of penetration, not as a property of the technology
- [ ] the finding is framed as a reason to count value rather than as a reason to stop building
