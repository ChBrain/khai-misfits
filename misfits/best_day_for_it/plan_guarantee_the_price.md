# Plan: Guarantee the Price

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the offtaker's seat](position_offtaker.md), inside [Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Offtaker](position_offtaker.md)

## Direction

Make the project financeable, which means giving the lenders a revenue line they can underwrite. Nothing is financed against a captured price that nobody can forecast a decade ahead, so write the long contract at a fixed price and take the price risk onto this balance sheet, since carrying that risk is the service being paid for and the reason the seat exists. Do not pretend the instrument is neutral. A contract paying the same whatever hour the power arrives in removes the price information from the generator, which is the party best placed to act on it, so financing the build and suppressing the signal are one instrument and not two. State that plainly rather than treating it as an implementation detail, and note that no contract exists which both funds the build and preserves the incentive, because the risk this seat was hired to take away is exactly the signal that would fund the correlation-breaking assets.
