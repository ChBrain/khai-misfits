# Process: Clearing

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md). Borne at [The Merit Order](place_the_merit_order.md), precipitated by a fuel-free fleet bidding into a cheapest-first dispatch, in [Bid Zero, Because It Is Zero](plot_bid_zero_because_it_is_zero.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A plant with no marginal cost meeting a mechanism designed to take the cheapest offer first.

## Direction

From two separately correct behaviours toward a clearing price at the bottom of the stack in exactly the hours the fleet is running.

## Lever

That the zero bid is a true statement and the cheapest-first rule is the whole point of a merit order, so there is no step here to reform without breaking what keeps system costs down (Sensfuss, Ragwitz and Genoese).

## Echo

[The Zero Bid](piece_the_zero_bid.md), arithmetic rather than aggression, producing a floor price nobody chose and nobody disputes.
