# Process: Cannibalising

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md). Borne at [The Windy Afternoon](place_the_windy_afternoon.md), precipitated by the fleet running at full output, in [Nobody Pays in the Hours You Produce](plot_nobody_pays_in_the_hours_you_produce.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The plain fact that what makes one unit produce makes every unit of the class produce, in the same hours, at the same time.

## Direction

From a growing fleet toward a falling captured price, the suppression landing in exactly the hours the technology has to earn in.

## Lever

That the decline tracks deployment rather than deterioration, so it would continue even if the technology got cheaper every year, cost and value being independent quantities (Hirth).

## Echo

[The Value Factor](piece_the_value_factor.md), a plant performing exactly as modelled while earning less than the model assumed.
