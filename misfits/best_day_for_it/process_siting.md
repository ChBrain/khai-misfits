# Process: Siting

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md). Borne at [The Best Site](place_the_best_site.md), precipitated by [The Developer](persona_the_developer.md) choosing the best available resource, in [Build Where the Wind Is](plot_build_where_the_wind_is.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A resource map, capital that must be put where the returns are, and every rival developer holding the same map.

## Direction

From an individual siting decision toward a class whose output is very nearly simultaneous, without any coordination and without anybody intending it.

## Lever

That decorrelating means building on worse ground, which lowers the builder's own returns in order to raise its rivals', so the correlation is chosen by nobody and undoable by nobody alone.

## Echo

[The Correlated Fleet](piece_the_correlated_fleet.md), a whole technology producing in the same hours because every one of its builders was right.
