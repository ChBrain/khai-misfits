# Piece: The Contract That Hides It

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md). The remedy that is paid for and makes it worse: a fixed price that funds the build by removing the signal.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Financing](place_the_financing.md), in the one instrument the lenders will accept.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the fleet does not get built at all, so the play would have no subject: this is the instrument that made the deployment possible and it is not optional.
It bears the load as the no-free-hero, and exactly. Because a contract paying the same whatever hour the power arrives in removes the price information from the generator, which is the party best placed to act on it, financing the build and suppressing the fix are one instrument and not two, and no contract exists that does the first without the second.

## Apparent

Prudent risk transfer, and it is that: nobody finances a project against a revenue line nobody can forecast, and the seat carrying the risk is being paid to carry it.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [We Will Guarantee the Price](plot_we_will_guarantee_the_price.md), where the enabling instrument is the concealing one.
It works together with [The Unfunded Fix](piece_the_unfunded_fix.md), the two remedies that fail in opposite directions.
