# Position: Developer

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md). The seat whose correct decision, taken by everybody, is the mechanism.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A resource map, capital, and a duty to put the capital where the resource is best.
A plant with no fuel, so a marginal cost of nothing and a true bid of zero.
Competitors reading the same maps and reaching the same conclusions.

## Orders

Build where the wind is: site on the best available resource and take the capacity factor that comes with it.
Bid zero, because zero is what it costs to run and any other number is a lie about the economics.
Accept what the seat does: decorrelating on purpose would destroy its own returns in order to raise its rivals'.

## Loses

The revenue per unit, since the fleet suppresses the price in exactly the hours it produces, and the decline tracks the deployment rather than any deterioration in the plant.
Because storage, transmission and demand shifting each raise every rival's revenue as much as the funder's, the seat cannot buy its way out either, and a board asked to spend shareholders' money improving competitors' returns would refuse.
It keeps a plant performing exactly as modelled and loses the price the model assumed.

## Drives

Toward the seat that does its job perfectly and is punished by everybody else doing the same,
reading the map, taking the best ground, bidding what it costs, and watching the captured price fall as the fleet grows,
so a decision no board could fault, taken by every board at once, is the whole of the mechanism.
