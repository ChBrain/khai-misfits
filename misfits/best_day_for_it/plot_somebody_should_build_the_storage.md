# Plot: Somebody Should Build the Storage

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Best Site](place_the_best_site.md), with the remedy on the table, agreed to work, and a question about who pays for it.

## Action

Through [Declining](process_declining.md), [the Developer](persona_the_developer.md) on [Build Where the Wind Is](plan_build_where_the_wind_is.md) meets [the Unfunded Fix](piece_the_unfunded_fix.md): storage, transmission across weather regimes and demand shifting each break the correlation and each work, and each raises every rival's revenue exactly as much as the funder's. The scene closes with a board that would have to explain why it spent shareholders' money improving competitors' returns, and with a refusal that is correct governance rather than short-sightedness.

## Stage

It will be reported as an industry declining to invest in its own future while asking for support, and the seat has no good answer to that framing, because the framing is not wrong about what it did.

## Tension

The fix fails on ownership rather than on engineering, so no amount of technical progress reaches it, and every party who would benefit has the same reason as every other not to be the one who pays.
