# Persona: The Offtaker

## Taxonomy

[Offtaker](position_offtaker.md). The one who makes the build possible by removing the thing that would fix it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Steady and bankable, offering the one thing a lender will accept: a price that does not move.
Points out, correctly, that nothing gets financed against a captured price nobody can forecast.
Has no wish to suppress any signal and is not thinking about signals at all.

## Action

Runs [Guarantee the Price](plan_guarantee_the_price.md): through [Insuring](process_insuring.md) at [the Financing](place_the_financing.md), setting [the Contract That Hides It](piece_the_contract_that_hides_it.md).
Writes a long contract at a fixed price and the project reaches financial close.
Takes the price risk onto its own book, which is what it is being paid for.

## Shadow

Not a fixer and not a distortion: without this instrument the fleet does not get built at all, and the seat is supplying exactly the certainty the capital market requires.
Because the contract pays the same whatever hour the power arrives in, it removes the price signal from the party best placed to act on it, so financing the build and suppressing the fix are one instrument and not two.
It is the only remedy anybody will actually pay for, and it is the one that makes the underlying problem invisible to the people who could solve it.

## Tell

Delivers a bankable project and a generator with no reason to care when it generates.
Cannot write a contract that both funds the build and preserves the signal, because the signal is the risk it was hired to take away.
