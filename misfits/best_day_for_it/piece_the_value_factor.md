# Piece: The Value Factor

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md). The finding: revenue per unit falls as a function of the technology's own deployment, independently of its cost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Windy Afternoon](place_the_windy_afternoon.md), on the best generating day of the month and the worst earning one (Hirth).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play is a description of how a merit order works, which is a textbook page and not a misfit.
It bears the load as the inversion the play is named against. Because cost and value are independent quantities and only one of them is being driven by the build, the technology would go on becoming less valuable even while becoming cheaper every year, so success is the cause of the decline and cheapness cannot answer it.

## Apparent

A market downturn, and it will be argued as one: prices are soft, the sector is maturing, and better technology will fix it in due course.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Nobody Pays in the Hours You Produce](plot_nobody_pays_in_the_hours_you_produce.md), where the plant performs exactly as modelled and earns less than the model assumed.
It works together with [The Wrong Metric](piece_the_wrong_metric.md), the quantity that decides whether the build pays and the instruments that cannot see it.
