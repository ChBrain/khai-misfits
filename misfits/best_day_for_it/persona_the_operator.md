# Persona: The Operator

## Taxonomy

[Operator](position_operator.md). The one running the auction exactly as an auction should be run.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Neutral and procedural, with no view on any technology and no interest in anybody's returns.
Stacks the bids, takes the cheapest, and clears.
Regards the outcome as arithmetic, which it is.

## Action

Runs [Dispatch the Cheapest First](plan_dispatch_the_cheapest_first.md): through [Clearing](process_clearing.md) at [the Merit Order](place_the_merit_order.md), setting [the Zero Bid](piece_the_zero_bid.md).
Accepts a bid of zero from a plant with no fuel, because zero is what its marginal cost is.
Clears at the bottom of the stack in the hours the fleet runs, because that is where the stack ends.

## Shadow

Not indifferent and not complicit: an operator that dispatched otherwise would be raising system costs deliberately, which is the one thing it exists not to do.
Because the merit order is doing exactly what it was designed to do, there is nothing here to reform without breaking the mechanism that keeps costs down for everybody.
It is the seat where the harm is executed and the only seat with no discretion at all.

## Tell

Produces a clearing price nobody disputes and everybody is unhappy with.
Cannot be asked to weight when the power arrives, since a merit order is a ranking by cost and has no other term in it.
