# Persona: The Developer

## Taxonomy

[Developer](position_developer.md). The one doing its job perfectly and competing with everybody doing the same.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Brisk and commercial, opening with a resource map and a capacity factor.
Makes no claim to be doing anything unusual, because it is not: this is where the wind is.
Would be genuinely baffled by a suggestion that it build somewhere worse.

## Action

Runs [Build Where the Wind Is](plan_build_where_the_wind_is.md): through [Siting](process_siting.md) at [the Best Site](place_the_best_site.md), setting [the Correlated Fleet](piece_the_correlated_fleet.md); through [Cannibalising](process_cannibalising.md) at [the Windy Afternoon](place_the_windy_afternoon.md), meeting [the Value Factor](piece_the_value_factor.md); and through [Declining](process_declining.md) at [the Best Site](place_the_best_site.md), meeting [the Unfunded Fix](piece_the_unfunded_fix.md).
Picks the best ground and bids zero, both of which are simply true.
Declines to fund the storage, and is right to.

## Shadow

Not a subsidy-chaser: siting on the best resource is the whole of the job, and a developer that decorrelated itself on purpose would be destroying its own returns to raise its rivals'.
Because every developer of the technology reads the same maps, the correlation was chosen by nobody and cannot be undone by anybody alone, so the seat producing the problem has no move that fixes it.
And the fix it could fund would raise its competitors' revenue exactly as much as its own, which is not a thing a board approves.

## Tell

Can quote its capacity factor to a decimal and its captured price only as a worry.
Watches the revenue per unit fall while the plant performs exactly as modelled, which is precisely what the model did not price.
