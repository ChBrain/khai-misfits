# Piece: The Wrong Metric

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md). The invisibility: installed capacity and levelised cost both improve, honestly, and neither has a term for when the output arrives.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Capacity Table](place_the_capacity_table.md), in two accurate charts that mislead only together (Joskow; Ueckerdt, Hirth, Luderer and Edenhofer).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing the standard measures could show the effect, and the trap is a known cost that policy weighs like any other, which is management rather than a misfit.
It bears the load as the reason nothing prompts the question. Because a levelised cost averages over output without asking when the output arrives, it compares an intermittent technology with a dispatchable one as though the two produced the same product, so the blindness is structural and no quantity of better data repairs it.

## Apparent

Rigour, and it is rigour: the two measures the field itself standardised on, correctly computed and honestly reported by people nobody is spinning.
