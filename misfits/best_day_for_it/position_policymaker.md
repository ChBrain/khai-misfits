# Position: Policymaker

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md). The seat holding two honest instruments that are blind to the thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Installed capacity and levelised cost, the two measures the field itself standardised on.
A record of both improving, year on year, accurately reported.
The authority to change what is measured, and no particular reason to.

## Orders

Count the megawatts installed and track the cost curve, since those are the standard measures and a departure would have to be justified.
Report both accurately, which they are, and take the improvement as the improvement it genuinely is.
Accept what the seat does: an average over output cannot ask when the output arrives, so the instruments are blind rather than imprecise.

## Loses

Sight of the effect, since the two correct charts together mislead and nothing in either would ever prompt the question that reveals it.
Because a levelised cost compares an intermittent technology with a dispatchable one as though the two produced the same product, the seat's central comparison is not a comparison of like with like and cannot be repaired by better data.
It keeps two accurate series and loses the quantity that decides whether the build pays.

## Drives

Toward the seat that must change the measure rather than gather more of it,
reporting capacity and cost, finding both excellent, and eventually measuring the captured price instead,
so the discovery arrives from a different question rather than from new evidence, and what it finds is real, quantified and mitigable, which is harder to say than either good news or bad.
