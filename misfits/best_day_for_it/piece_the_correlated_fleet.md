# Piece: The Correlated Fleet

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md). The root: every developer reads the same resource maps, so the class's output is very nearly simultaneous.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Best Site](place_the_best_site.md), created by three rival developers looking at the same shaded ground and all being right.

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing the units produced at unrelated times, and zero marginal cost is harmless: the fleet would earn the average price and there would be no trap at all.
It bears the load as the thing nobody chose and nobody can undo. Because decorrelating means siting on worse ground, any single builder that did it would lower its own returns to raise its rivals', so the correlation is not a coordination failure anyone could fix by behaving better and is not a decision anybody made.

## Apparent

Good siting, and it is good siting: the resource is real, the capacity factor is real, and the case for building on the best ground is unanswerable.

## Yearbook

It works together with [The Zero Bid](piece_the_zero_bid.md), the two conditions that are harmless apart and are the trap together.
