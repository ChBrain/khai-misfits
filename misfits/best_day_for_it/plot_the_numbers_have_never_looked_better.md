# Plot: The Numbers Have Never Looked Better

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Capacity Table](place_the_capacity_table.md), the annual review, with two charts on the wall that are both correct.

## Action

Through [Measuring](process_measuring.md), [the Policymaker](persona_the_policymaker.md) on [Count the Megawatts Installed](plan_count_the_megawatts_installed.md) sets [the Wrong Metric](piece_the_wrong_metric.md): installed capacity up and to the right, levelised cost down and to the right, both reported accurately on the two measures the field itself standardised on. The scene closes with neither instrument capable of showing the effect, because an average over output never asks when the output arrives, and a comparison of that average across an intermittent and a dispatchable technology is not a comparison of like with like (Joskow; Ueckerdt, Hirth, Luderer and Edenhofer).

## Stage

Nobody is being spun here. The build is real, the cost fall is real, and an official who departed from the standard measures would have been asked to justify the departure to people entitled to ask.

## Tension

The blindness is structural rather than a shortage of data, so nothing in either series will ever prompt the question that reveals it, and the room can only find the effect by changing what it measures.
