# Position: Operator

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md). The seat with no discretion, where the arithmetic is executed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A stack of bids, a demand to meet, and a rule that says take the cheapest first.
No view on any technology and no interest in any generator's returns.
A mandate to keep system costs down, which the merit order exists to serve.

## Orders

Dispatch the cheapest first, which is what a merit order is and the reason costs stay down for everybody.
Accept a bid of zero from a plant with no fuel, because that is a true statement of its marginal cost.
Accept what the seat does: an operator that dispatched otherwise would be raising costs deliberately, which is the one thing it exists not to do.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, which is why the seat is here: it is the point at which two correct behaviours become a clearing price at the bottom of the stack, and it pays none of the cost of that.
Because a merit order is a ranking by cost and contains no term for when the power arrives, the seat cannot weight scarcity into the outcome without ceasing to be a merit order.
It keeps the mechanism that holds costs down and hands the fleet a price in exactly its earning hours.

## Drives

Toward the seat that has no choice to make and makes the outcome anyway,
stacking, taking the cheapest, clearing at the bottom, and producing a price nobody disputes and everybody is unhappy with,
so the harm passes through the one actor in the play with no discretion at all.
