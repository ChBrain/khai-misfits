# Persona: The Policymaker

## Taxonomy

[Policymaker](position_policymaker.md). The one reading two honest charts that cannot show the thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Confident and well briefed, leading with installed capacity and the falling cost curve.
Uses the two measures the field itself standardised on, and uses them correctly.
Is not being spun by anybody, which is the difficulty.

## Action

Runs [Count the Megawatts Installed](plan_count_the_megawatts_installed.md): through [Measuring](process_measuring.md) at [the Capacity Table](place_the_capacity_table.md), setting [the Wrong Metric](piece_the_wrong_metric.md) and later meeting [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md).
Reports capacity up and levelised cost down, both accurately.
Eventually measures the captured price instead, and finds the decline was there all along.

## Shadow

Not naive: the measures are the standard ones, the figures are right, and an official using different numbers would have been asked to justify the departure.
Because a levelised cost averages over output without asking when the output arrives, the seat's instruments are structurally blind to the effect rather than merely imprecise.
And when it does look, what it finds is a real decline that is a function of penetration and mitigable, which is a harder thing to say than either good news or bad.

## Tell

Can show two charts that are both correct and together mislead.
Discovers the problem by changing the measure rather than by receiving new data, since the data was always there.
