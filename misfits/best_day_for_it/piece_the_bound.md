## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play overreaches into a claim it cannot support, that renewables are uneconomic or that deployment should slow, which is the opposite of what the source literature says.
It bears the load as the limit that keeps this a finding rather than a verdict. Because the trap needs units sharing both zero marginal cost and a common weather driver, it is conditional and locatable; and because the decline is a function of penetration rather than a fixed property, and is mitigable by flexibility elsewhere in the system, it is a reason to count value rather than a reason to stop building.

## Apparent

A hedge, and an easy one to read as either retreat or reassurance, when it is neither: it is the difference between a mechanism and a slogan, in a subject where both sides want a slogan.
