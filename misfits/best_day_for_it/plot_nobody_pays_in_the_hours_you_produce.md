# Plot: Nobody Pays in the Hours You Produce

## Taxonomy

[Best Day for It](play_best_day_for_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Windy Afternoon](place_the_windy_afternoon.md), the best generating day of the month, with the whole fleet at full output.

## Action

Through [Cannibalising](process_cannibalising.md), [the Developer](persona_the_developer.md) on [Build Where the Wind Is](plan_build_where_the_wind_is.md) meets [the Value Factor](piece_the_value_factor.md): what makes one unit produce makes every unit of the class produce, so the fleet suppresses the price in exactly the hours it has to earn in, and revenue per unit falls as a function of how much of the technology has been built. The scene closes with the plant performing precisely as modelled while earning less than the model assumed, and with the decline owed to the fleet's success rather than to any deterioration (Hirth).

## Stage

It will be argued as a downturn: soft prices, a maturing sector, and better technology along shortly to fix it. Each of those is a reasonable thing to say and none of them reaches the cause.

## Tension

Cost and value are independent quantities and only one is being driven by the build, so the technology would go on becoming less valuable even while becoming cheaper every year, and cheapness cannot answer it.
