# Plot: Only the Ones They Turned Down

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An investor with no view of the sector, applying for everything on offer because there is no basis on which to choose.

## Action

[The Ones Who Cannot Know](persona_the_ones_who_cannot_know.md), holding [Ones Who Cannot Know](position_ones_who_cannot_know.md), are filled in full wherever demand was weak and cut back wherever it was strong.

## Stage

Their realised average is worse than the average issue, without any error on their part, so a discount is required for them to break even at all.

## Tension

The sorting is done by the information they do not have.
Rationing bites hardest exactly where the informed wanted shares, so the uninformed end up holding least of what turned out well and all of what turned out badly, and no amount of care corrects it, since correcting it would require the very view they are defined by lacking. This is why the discount cannot be set to zero even in a world with no [holding back](process_holding_back.md) at all: [the one who asks](persona_the_one_who_asks.md) needs these accounts to keep applying, and an offering that loses them stops being a public offering.
