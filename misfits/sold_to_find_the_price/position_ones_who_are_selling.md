# Position: Ones Who Are Selling

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md). The seat that funds the payment.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An asset with no traded price, a date, and most of the shares still in hand.
One estimate of its own worth, which is the least reliable estimate in the room.

## Orders

Agree a range, sit through the asking, sign a price the night before.
Fund the discount out of the shares it is keeping.

## Loses

An amount larger than the fee, and cannot buy the alternative.
Pricing at the level the asking produced removes the reason anybody answered the asking; declining the asking returns it to valuing itself from the inside, which is guessing; so the seat is not choosing between paying and not paying, it is choosing between a known cost and an unknown error, and there is no third column.

## Drives

Toward a listing, a price, and a morning on which it is both shortchanged and richer than it has ever been.
