# Process: Reading the Pop

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md). The number in public, and the argument it cannot settle.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An opening bell and a closing print, one day after the price had to be written down.
[The Ones Who Are Selling](persona_the_ones_who_are_selling.md) and everybody else, on [the first day](place_the_first_day.md).

## Direction

Toward a percentage, quoted everywhere by lunchtime, read as money the company threw away.
Toward [the discount](piece_the_discount.md) becoming arithmetic that anybody can perform.

## Lever

Subtraction, which produces one figure for three different things: the payment the mechanism required, the rent the allocating hand took, and any plain error of judgement.

## Echo

A gap larger than the fee, a founder richer than they have ever been, and nobody at the issuer complaining about either.
