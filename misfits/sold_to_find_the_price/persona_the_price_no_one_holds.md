# Persona: The Price No One Holds

## Taxonomy

[Price No One Holds](position_price_no_one_holds.md). The number the whole exercise is for, resident in nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The level at which the shares will clear, which exists as the sum of what several dozen institutions would separately pay.
Not written down anywhere, not withheld by anybody, and not available to the party selling.

## Action

Sits in fragments across the buy side, one fragment per holder, none of them able to see the rest.
Comes into existence in public only after the sale that was supposed to be priced by it.

## Shadow

Is distributed rather than hidden, which is why no amount of diligence reaches it.
An auditor can establish what the company owns and a lawyer what it owes, and neither of those is the number: the number is an opinion about the future held by the people on the other side of the trade, so the seller cannot find it by looking harder at itself, and the one method that would produce it, asking the holders, is the method that gives them a reason to shade.

## Tell

Appears for the first time on the day after it was needed.
