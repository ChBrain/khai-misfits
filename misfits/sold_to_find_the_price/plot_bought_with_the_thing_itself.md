# Plot: Bought with the Thing Itself

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A bank that needs an answer, a book that will stay useless without one, and no cash anywhere with which to buy it.

## Action

[Paying for the answer](process_paying_for_the_answer.md): [the one who asks](persona_the_one_who_asks.md) sets [the discount](piece_the_discount.md) below the level the book implies and hands [the allocation](piece_the_allocation.md) to the participants whose bids moved the price.

## Stage

The consideration is transferred, in shares, from [the ones who are selling](persona_the_ones_who_are_selling.md) to [the ones who know](persona_the_ones_who_know.md), and the price is thereby discovered by selling under it.

## Tension

The currency and the commodity are the same asset, which is why this cannot be tidied.
There is no cash to pay with, no way to value the information except by the price it produces, and no way to make the payment mean anything within a single issue: it works only because the same names come back, so the discretion to allocate is not an ornament but the instrument itself. And because the payment is made in the thing being priced, it is never recorded as a payment: it appears on [the first day](place_the_first_day.md) as a shortfall, sitting beside a stated fee that is fixed, visible and far smaller than it.
