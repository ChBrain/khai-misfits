# Piece: The Discount

## Taxonomy

[The Book](place_the_book.md). The consideration, drawn on the asset it is meant to value.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set in [the book](place_the_book.md) the night before, and converted into a public number on [the first day](place_the_first_day.md).

## Load Bearing

It is a payment, and it looks exactly like a loss.
The bank has no cash to buy the view with and the issuer has no other coin, so the price is set below the level the book implies and the difference is the fee for the information: it is charged to the shares the seller is keeping, it never appears in any statement of costs, and it is the only currency in the arrangement that the informed will accept.

## Apparent

A shortfall. The gap between what was received and what the market paid the next day, quoted as a percentage and read by everybody as money thrown away.
Sitting beside a stated underwriting fee that is fixed, modest, and far smaller.

## Yearbook

Cannot be set to zero without emptying the book, since nobody pays for the privilege of raising their own cost.
Cannot be audited as a payment, because it is not recorded as one anywhere: which means the amount that was necessary and the amount that was captured are added together before anyone sees either.
