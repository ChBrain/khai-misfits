# Persona: The Ones Who Are Selling

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Are Selling](position_ones_who_are_selling.md). The issuer, whose asset is the currency.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A company and its existing holders, selling a slice to raise money and to become traded.
Keeping most of the shares, which is why the discount is charged to them and not to the buyer of the slice.

## Action

Signs the range, sits through the roadshow, agrees the price the night before, and rings the bell.
Reads the closing print the following evening and does not complain about it.

## Shadow

Cannot refuse the process without pricing itself from the inside.
Its own estimate of its own worth is the single least reliable number available, since it is the only one with no market behind it, so declining the asking does not save the discount: it replaces a known cost with an unknown error, and the alternative of setting the offer at the level the asking produced simply removes the reason anybody answered the asking, which returns it to pricing blind.

## Tell

Is poorer by an amount larger than the fee, and richer than it has ever been, on the same morning.
