# Piece: The Indication

## Taxonomy

[The Book](place_the_book.md). The thing being purchased, disguised as a formality.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Given in [the book](place_the_book.md) by [the ones who know](persona_the_ones_who_know.md), over a fortnight, without obligation.

## Load Bearing

It is the commodity, and everybody involved calls it paperwork.
An indication that names a quantity and a limit price is a disclosure of private value dressed as an administrative step, and it is the only input the offer price can be built from: so the central transaction of the whole exercise is conducted in a form that carries no consideration, no signature and no enforcement, which is exactly why it has to be paid for afterwards.

## Apparent

A phone call and a line in a ledger. Non-binding, revocable, and free to give.
Indistinguishable, on the page, from an indication given carelessly by somebody with no view at all.

## Yearbook

Costs its giver money in exact proportion to its truthfulness, since naming a higher limit raises what they will pay.
Is worth more to the asker when it comes from a large and frequent participant, which is the same set of people who have most to lose from candour and most to gain from being asked again.
