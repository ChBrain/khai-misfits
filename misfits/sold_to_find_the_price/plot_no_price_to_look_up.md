# Plot: No Price to Look Up

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A company with no traded shares, a date, and a number that has to be written on a document by Thursday.

## Action

[The Ones Who Are Selling](persona_the_ones_who_are_selling.md), holding [Ones Who Are Selling](position_ones_who_are_selling.md), discover that the one estimate they own is the one with no market behind it.

## Stage

[The Price No One Holds](persona_the_price_no_one_holds.md) turns out to be distributed rather than hidden: it is the aggregate of what several dozen institutions would separately pay, and none of them can see the whole of it either.

## Tension

Diligence does not reach it, because it is not a fact about the company.
An auditor establishes what is owned and a lawyer what is owed, and neither is the number: the number is an opinion about the future held by the people on the other side of the trade. So the seller cannot find it by looking harder at itself, and cannot find it by looking harder at the market, since the market for this thing does not exist until the sale it is meant to price is over.
