## Targets

- [ ] the money is raised and the listing exists
- [ ] the price is set from evidence rather than from the seller's own opinion, which is the point
- [ ] the cost of that evidence is paid, in the asset, and appears in no statement of costs
- [ ] the alternative is priced honestly: not paying means guessing, so there is no column with a zero in it
