# Piece: The Allocation

## Taxonomy

[The Book](place_the_book.md). The instrument that makes the payment credible, and the one that makes it divertible.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Decided in [the book](place_the_book.md) at the end, by [the one who asks](persona_the_one_who_asks.md), at their discretion and against no published rule.

## Load Bearing

It carries the payment across issues, which is the only way a discount can buy anything.
A price set low benefits whoever happens to receive shares, so without the power to choose the recipients the discount rewards nobody in particular and buys no information at all: the discretion is not an ornament on the mechanism, it is the mechanism, and it is what lets the asker settle a debt incurred in one issue with an allocation in the next.

## Apparent

An administrative decision taken on the last evening, disclosed to nobody, and explained by nobody.
A favoured account receiving a good fill, which looks identical whatever the reason.

## Yearbook

Is granted preferentially to the participants whose bids moved the price, which is what makes truthful bidding pay.
Is granted preferentially to the participants who are valuable to the asker for other reasons, which is what makes it a rent: and since the two motives produce the same entry in the same ledger, removing the second requires removing the first.
