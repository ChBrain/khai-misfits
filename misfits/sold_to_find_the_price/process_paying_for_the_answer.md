# Process: Paying for the Answer

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md). The settlement, made in the asset because there is nothing else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A book that will stay empty of anything useful unless candour is compensated.
[The One Who Asks](persona_the_one_who_asks.md) setting [the discount](piece_the_discount.md) and handing out [the allocation](piece_the_allocation.md), funded by [the ones who are selling](persona_the_ones_who_are_selling.md).

## Direction

Toward an offer price below the level the book implies, which is the consideration.
Toward preferential fills for the participants whose bids moved the price, which is what carries the consideration into the next issue.

## Lever

The repeat: the same names come back, so a payment made now is a reason to answer truthfully later, and a single issue on its own could not buy anything.

## Echo

A price discovered by selling under it, charged to the shares the seller kept, and recorded in no statement of costs anywhere.
