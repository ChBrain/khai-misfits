# Plot: The Cost of Saying Yes

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An investor asked, without obligation, how much they want and what they would pay for it.

## Action

[Holding back](process_holding_back.md): [the ones who know](persona_the_ones_who_know.md) give [an indication](piece_the_indication.md) below the view behind it, every one of them, in [the book](place_the_book.md).

## Stage

The book fills with statements that are honest in form and low in substance, and the asking has produced evidence that is systematically understated without anyone having lied.

## Tension

The tax on candour is arithmetic, not character.
Naming a higher limit raises the price the namer will pay, so the truthful answer is the expensive one and the shaded answer is free, and this holds as firmly for a scrupulous holder as for a cynical one. [The One Who Asks](persona_the_one_who_asks.md) cannot fix it by asking better, because the flaw is not in the wording: it is that the valuer and the buyer are the same person, and no form of words separates them.
