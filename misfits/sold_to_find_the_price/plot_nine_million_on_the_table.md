# Plot: Nine Million on the Table

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A first day of trading, a closing price beside an offer price, and a percentage quoted in every account of it by lunchtime.

## Action

[Reading the pop](process_reading_the_pop.md): on [the first day](place_the_first_day.md), [the discount](piece_the_discount.md) stops being an arrangement and becomes arithmetic anybody can perform.

## Stage

The gap runs to roughly twice what the underwriters were paid, in public, and nobody at the issuer complains about it.

## Tension

The silence is the strange part, and it has a mechanism.
The people who signed the price are shareholders too, and the pop that shortchanged the company made them richer than they had ever been on the same morning, so the shortfall arrives wrapped in a personal windfall: the loss falls on the shares the firm retained, and the gain lands in the pockets of the ones who agreed to it. [The Ones Who Are Selling](persona_the_ones_who_are_selling.md) are therefore not being fooled and not being brave, they are simply not the party the money came from, and there is nobody in the room whose own position points the other way.
