# Place: The Book

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md). The room where the answer is bought.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A fortnight of calls and meetings, a range printed on a cover, and a ledger of interest filling up on the bank's side of the wall.
Nothing binding anywhere in it.

## Holds

[The Indication](piece_the_indication.md), given without obligation and therefore worth only what the giver's future costs them.
[The Allocation](piece_the_allocation.md), promised to nobody and decided at the end.

## Offers

The only route to [the price no one holds](persona_the_price_no_one_holds.md), since the holders are in this room and nowhere else.
A record of who said what, kept by the asker, which is what turns a single exchange into a repeated one.

## Withheld

Any way of receiving a view from somebody without also inviting their bid.
The place cannot separate the two, because the participants are valuers and buyers in the same breath, so every honest number in the ledger arrives attached to a person who will pay more for having written it: the room can be run scrupulously, and it will still be a room in which the truth has a price on it.
