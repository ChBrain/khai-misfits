# Process: Asking Around

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md). The only route to the number, and the one that spoils it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A range printed on a cover page, a fortnight of meetings, and a company with nothing to price itself against.
[The One Who Asks](persona_the_one_who_asks.md), holding [One Who Asks](position_one_who_asks.md), in [the book](place_the_book.md).

## Direction

Toward [the price no one holds](persona_the_price_no_one_holds.md), which exists only in the people being asked.
Toward [the indication](piece_the_indication.md), collected from each of them without obligation.

## Lever

The question itself, which cannot be put to a valuer without also being put to a buyer, since they are the same person.

## Echo

An answer that has been contaminated by the act of requesting it, and no version of the request that avoids this.
