# Pitch: Nobody Knows the Number

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Procedural and unhurried. A company going public, a bank taking soundings over two weeks, a range on a cover page, a book filling, a price agreed the night before.
Every step conventional, disclosed, and done exactly as it is always done.

## Undertow

Under the procedure, a number that is in nobody's possession: not the company's, not the bank's, only the aggregate of what the buyers would each pay.
It can be assembled only by asking them, and asking gives every one of them a reason to understate, so the answer has to be bought.

## Nerve

The vertigo of paying for a thing with the thing you are trying to value.

## Echo

Afterward: a first day that prints the number the asking was for, a gap running to roughly twice the fee, and a room in which nobody is angry because the people who signed for the shortfall got rich on the same morning.
