# Persona: The Ones Who Cannot Know

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Cannot Know](position_ones_who_cannot_know.md). The uninformed side, and the second reason the discount cannot be zero.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Everybody without a view: the smaller funds, the retail accounts, the ones applying for whatever is on offer.
Necessary to the offering, since an issue placed with the informed alone is not a public offering.

## Action

Applies for everything, uniformly, having no basis on which to choose.
Receives a fraction of the issues the informed wanted, and the whole of the issues they did not.

## Shadow

Is sorted by the very thing it cannot see.
Because allocation is rationed exactly where demand is strong, the uninformed hold least of what turned out well and most of what turned out badly, so their realised return is worse than the average issue even when every issue is fairly priced: the penalty is produced by the rationing rather than by any error of theirs, and no amount of care removes it, since caring more would require the information they are defined by not having.

## Tell

Stops applying if the average fill loses money, and the offering needs it to keep applying.
