# Persona: The One Who Asks

## Taxonomy

[One Who Asks](position_one_who_asks.md). The bank running the book, and the only hand that can make the payment.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The lead underwriter: takes the soundings, keeps [the book](place_the_book.md), sets the price with the issuer the night before, and decides who gets what.
Paid a fee that is fixed, visible and modest beside the payment it is arranging.

## Action

Asks the informed what they think, and pays them for saying it out of the issuer's shares.
Allocates at its own discretion, which is the instrument that makes the payment credible across issues rather than only within one.

## Shadow

Holds discretion over a payment it does not fund.
The one thing that makes the mechanism work, a hand free to reward the investors who told the truth, is the same thing that leaves it free to reward the investors who are worth most to it for other reasons: the two are indistinguishable from outside, since both look like a favoured account receiving a good allocation, and no rule that removes the discretion leaves the mechanism standing.

## Tell

Is asked to be a fiduciary to the seller while being the seller's counterparty's most valuable long-term relationship.
