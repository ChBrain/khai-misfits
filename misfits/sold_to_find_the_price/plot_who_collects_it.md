# Plot: Who Collects It

## Taxonomy

[Sold to Find the Price](play_sold_to_find_the_price.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A payment the mechanism requires, passing through the one party with discretion over where it lands, and funded by somebody who is not in the room when it is decided.

## Action

[The One Who Asks](persona_the_one_who_asks.md), holding [One Who Asks](position_one_who_asks.md), hands out [the allocation](piece_the_allocation.md) in [the book](place_the_book.md), against no published rule and to nobody's account but its own judgement.

## Stage

The discount is shown to be collectable by the hand that allocates it, in commissions routed to the lead and in shares placed with people it wants something from, so a necessary payment becomes an available rent.

## Tension

Removing the abuse means removing the instrument, because they are the same instrument.
The discretion that lets truthful bidders be rewarded is the discretion that lets valuable relationships be rewarded, and from outside a favoured account with a good fill looks identical either way: [the first day](place_the_first_day.md) prints one number for the consideration, the rent and any plain misjudgement together. So the argument about whether a given discount was too large cannot be settled from the evidence the process produces, which is not a gap in the record but a property of paying in the asset.
