# Persona: The Ones Who Know

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Know](position_ones_who_know.md). The institutions holding the fragments, and the reason they are expensive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A few dozen funds that follow the sector, meet the management, and carry a view worth having.
The same names, issue after issue, which is the only thing that makes any of this workable.

## Action

Answers the phone, gives [an indication](piece_the_indication.md), and understates it.
Comes back for the next one, and the one after that, which is where the leverage over them lives.

## Shadow

Is taxed for candour by simple arithmetic.
An investor who says the issue is worth more than the range has, in the act of saying so, raised the price they are about to pay for it, so the truthful answer is the costly one and the shaded answer is free: this is not greed, deceit or bad faith, it is what happens when you ask somebody to bid on a thing and to value it in the same sentence, and no appeal to honesty survives contact with it.

## Tell

Will tell you what it is worth, at a price, and the price is paid in the thing.
