# Piece: The Clock

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The mortality of the newly founded: high at the start, declining as the repeats accumulate, and returned to the beginning by a rebuild.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the long run](place_the_long_run.md), where a hazard rate can be read at all.
Reset by [resetting](process_resetting.md), the moment the rebuild completes.

## Load Bearing

Without it the cost of the attempt has no unit and the play trades in unease.
It is load bearing because it makes the cost **actuarial**: what the organisation spends is not confidence or morale but position on a mortality curve, and it is returned to the position of a body founded last Tuesday.

## Apparent

A metaphor about feeling new.
It reads as a figure of speech, which is exactly the reading the evidence refuses: the rate is measured, against age and against time since change.

## Yearbook

Wound by [reproducing](process_reproducing.md) over decades, returned to zero by [reorganising](process_reorganising.md).
It runs down again afterwards, which is the mercy in the play, unless [the second time](persona_the_second_time.md) arrives first.
