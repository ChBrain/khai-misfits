# Persona: The Record

## Taxonomy

[Reproduced](position_reproduced.md). Forty years of coming out the same way, which is why the organisation can promise anything and explain anything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Dependable, and not out of caution.
Delivers what it said it would; renders an account afterwards of what it did and why.
Both rest on one foundation, a structure that runs again and comes out as it did last time.

## Action

Repeats.
Is relied upon, and is worth relying upon.
Accumulates the legitimacy and the institutional support that only a long run of repeats can accumulate.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is the play's whole difficulty: the sameness is the mechanism of the competence rather than a symptom of complacency.
A body without this fidelity can promise nothing, since a promise is a claim about a repeat, and can explain nothing, since an account is a claim about a procedure actually followed.
It ends spent, by an attempt that was correct, having been the thing worth keeping.

## Tell

Says: we have done this every year since the sixties.
True, and it is the reason anybody believes the next sentence.
