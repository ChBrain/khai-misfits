# Place: The Changeover

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). Inside the organisation while it is being rebuilt: the interval in which it is neither the old body nor yet the new one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A structure being taken apart and put back differently.
Work proceeding competently, on a sound answer, with support throughout.

## Holds

What the attempt actually spends.
The body cannot come out the same way while it is being remade, its account stops describing the thing rendering it, and the legitimacy built on repetition rests on a claim about something currently being replaced. None of that is a failure of the rebuild: it is the definition of one.

## Offers

The form the world now requires, actually reached.

## Withheld

Any way of carrying the record across.
There is no more careful execution that buys the new structure without spending the old one: care changes the size of the payment, never its kind.
