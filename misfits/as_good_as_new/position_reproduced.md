# Position: Reproduced

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The seat of the structure that comes out the same way each time, which is what reliability and accountability are made of.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A structure that runs again and comes out as it did last time.
Delivery that can be depended on, because a promise is a claim about a repeat.
An account that can be rendered, because an account is a claim about a procedure actually followed.

## Orders

Run again.
Come out the same.
Be worth relying on, and be relied on.

## Loses

Nothing while it holds, and this seat is the play's difficulty: the sameness is the mechanism of the competence and not a symptom of complacency.
Because both valuable properties rest on this one foundation, there is no way to keep them through a rebuild of it.
It ends spent by a correct decision, having been the thing worth keeping.

## Drives

Toward being depended upon,
by never varying,
so the seat that earns the trust is the seat that cannot be moved.
