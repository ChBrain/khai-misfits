# Process: Reorganising

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The structure taken apart and rebuilt in the agreed form.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The right answer](persona_the_right_answer.md), accepted, which obliges action.
At [the changeover](place_the_changeover.md), for as long as the work takes.

## Direction

Toward the form the world now requires, by the only route that reaches it.
While the work runs the body cannot come out the same way each time, its account no longer describes the thing rendering it, and the legitimacy built on repetition rests on a claim about something being replaced.
None of that is a failure of the rebuild: it is what a rebuild is.

## Lever

Dismantling, which is the only way to reach a different structure.
Because the currency spent is the record itself, no more careful execution buys the change at a lower price: it changes the size of the payment and not its kind.

## Echo

The form that was needed, achieved,
and a body carrying the mortality of one founded last Tuesday.
