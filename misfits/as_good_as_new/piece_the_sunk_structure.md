# Piece: The Sunk Structure

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). What the inertia is actually made of: commitments in plant and people, coalitions that had to be built, and precedents that became the standard.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Throughout the organisation, load bearing in the literal sense.
Taken apart at [the changeover](place_the_changeover.md), piece by piece.

## Load Bearing

Without it the inertia is a mood and the play is about reluctance.
It is load bearing because every item in it is **defensible**: the plant was bought to do the work, the coalitions were assembled because nothing gets done without them, and the precedents became standards precisely because following them was how the thing was reliably done.

## Apparent

Baggage. Accumulated weight to be shed by a decisive leadership.
It reads as the residue of past decisions rather than as the current mechanism of the competence, which is what makes shedding it sound free.

## Yearbook

Laid down by [reproducing](process_reproducing.md), rewarded by [selecting](process_selecting.md).
It is the reason the rebuild is expensive and the reason the body was worth rebuilding.
