# Piece: The Two Readings

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The finding's two halves: change is disruptive, and change is also what makes an organisation able to change.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the long run](place_the_long_run.md), inseparable from [the thousand and eleven](piece_the_thousand_and_eleven.md).
Carried by the play and settled by it nowhere.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play overstates its case and becomes an argument for never moving, which the evidence does not support.
It is load bearing because it **includes the case where the harm relents**: the hazard declines with time since the change, so a body left alone recovers, and a finding that names its own limit is stronger than one that does not.

## Apparent

A hedge. The qualification a careful author adds at the end.
It reads as softening, and it is the opposite: it is what removes the last exit, since the same account that makes change disruptive makes it more available next time.

## Yearbook

Produced by [the thousand and eleven](piece_the_thousand_and_eleven.md), enacted by [the second time](persona_the_second_time.md).
Read alone the first half says wait and recover; read together they say waiting is not reliably what happens.
