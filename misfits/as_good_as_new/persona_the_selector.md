# Persona: The Selector

## Taxonomy

[Selector](position_selector.md). The environment, keeping the organisations that can be relied on and can account for themselves, and manufacturing a third property it never asked for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Indifferent and consistent.
Rewards two things only: delivery that can be depended on, and conduct that can be explained afterwards.
Wants nothing else, and has no view at all about how any organisation is built inside.

## Action

Keeps the dependable.
Does not keep the others.
Presses harder where the environment is harsher, and gets more of what it rewards.

## Shadow

Nothing chosen, and this is the inversion the play turns on: the two properties it selects for are bought with reproducibility, and reproducibility **is** inertia, so an environment that keeps choosing dependable bodies is an environment that produces bodies which cannot easily be changed.
Because the inertia is the consequence of the selection rather than a residue that escaped it, pressing harder makes it worse: a fiercer environment yields more inert organisations, not fewer.
It ends having produced exactly what it rewarded, and something else besides.

## Tell

Says nothing, being an environment.
It keeps the ones that can be relied on, and what that means underneath is not its concern.
