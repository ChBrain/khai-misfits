## Stage

## Tension

Both halves of the finding have to be carried, and the second one closes the last exit.
The first half is a relief and must be said plainly: the hazard is a **window**, not a doom. It rises when the change is made and then declines with time since the change, in the same shape age-dependence always has, so an organisation that survives long enough to reproduce again earns its reliability back.
The second half is [the two readings](piece_the_two_readings.md), and it is why the relief is partial: a body that has changed once is **more likely to change again**. The same account that makes change disruptive makes it, over time, more available. So the exit of simply waiting the window out is not reliably open: the next correct move arrives, is still correct, and is now likelier to be taken, and it reopens the window before the previous one has shut.
