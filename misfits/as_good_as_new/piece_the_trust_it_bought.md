# Piece: The Trust It Bought

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The legitimacy and institutional support that only a long run of repeats can accumulate.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the population](place_the_population.md), where it is what keeps the organisation in the field.
Suspended at [the changeover](place_the_changeover.md), for the duration.

## Load Bearing

Without it the cost of the attempt is internal and the world outside does not notice.
It is load bearing because it is **held by others**: it lives in the expectations of everybody who deals with the organisation, so it cannot be protected from inside, and no amount of internal care during a rebuild preserves a belief somebody else holds.

## Apparent

An asset, of the kind that persists.
It reads as banked, as something earned and kept, which is exactly what makes its suspension during a rebuild unexpected.

## Yearbook

Accrued by [reproducing](process_reproducing.md) over decades, rewarded by [selecting](process_selecting.md).
It rests on a claim about a body that is, during the work, being replaced.
