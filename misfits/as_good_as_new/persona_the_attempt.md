# Persona: The Attempt

## Taxonomy

[Attempted](position_attempted.md). The reorganisation: correct, agreed, successful at what it set out to do, and paid for with the record.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent and well founded.
Acting on a sound answer with support throughout the body.
Not reckless, not hasty and not a gamble: the thing a responsible organisation does when it knows what it must become.

## Action

Takes the structure apart and rebuilds it in the agreed form.
Suspends, for the duration, the claim that the body comes out the same way each time.
Succeeds.

## Shadow

The record, spent, and no cheaper currency was available.
During the work the organisation cannot be relied on to reproduce, which is not a failure of the rebuild but what a rebuild is; the account it could render stops describing the body rendering it; and the legitimacy built on decades of repetition rests on a claim about something currently being replaced.
It ends successful, with the body carrying the hazard of one founded last Tuesday.

## Tell

Says: it went well, we are through it.
True. And the mortality table does not care that it went well.
