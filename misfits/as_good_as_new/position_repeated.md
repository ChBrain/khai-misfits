# Position: Repeated

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The seat of the next change: likelier for the last one, and arriving before the previous window has shut.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Another turn in the world, and another sound answer to it.
A body more practised at moving than it was.
A hazard from the previous move that has not finished declining.

## Orders

Move again.
Be as well justified as the first move was.
Arrive sooner than a body that had never moved.

## Loses

The exit that waiting would have supplied.
Because the hazard declines with time since the change, an organisation left alone earns its reliability back; because change makes further change more available, being left alone is not reliably what happens.
It ends having reopened the window for reasons nobody can fault.

## Drives

Toward the form the world requires now,
having learned that this is a thing the body can do,
so the seat that proves the capability spends the recovery.
