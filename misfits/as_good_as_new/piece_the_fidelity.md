# Piece: The Fidelity

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The capacity to run again and come out the same, which is what reliability and accountability are both made of.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the population](place_the_population.md), being tested every time the organisation is asked to deliver.
Spent at [the changeover](place_the_changeover.md), which is the only place it can be spent.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is no competence to lose and the play is about a body that was never worth much.
It is load bearing because it is **one purchase serving two properties**: a promise is a claim about a repeat and an account is a claim about a procedure followed, so both fail together the moment the repeats stop.

## Apparent

Conservatism. Doing it the old way.
It reads as the absence of ambition rather than as the mechanism of the trust, which is why the first instinct is to loosen it.

## Yearbook

Built by [reproducing](process_reproducing.md), rewarded by [selecting](process_selecting.md), spent by [reorganising](process_reorganising.md).
It is the same property under two names: read forwards it is reliability, read backwards it is inertia.
