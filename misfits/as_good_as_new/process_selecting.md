# Process: Selecting

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The environment keeping the dependable and discarding the rest, and manufacturing inertia by doing so.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Every occasion an organisation is chosen or not chosen.
At [the population](place_the_population.md), continuously, on two criteria.

## Direction

Toward a population of organisations that deliver dependably and can account for themselves, which is what any environment worth operating in rewards.
It has no view about internal structure and never selects for rigidity.
What it produces anyway is rigidity, because the properties it does select for require reproducibility and reproducibility is inertia under another name.

## Lever

Consistency of the two tests, which is what makes the environment worth adapting to.
Because the inertia is the consequence of the selection rather than a residue escaping it, harsher pressure yields more of it and not less.

## Echo

Exactly the organisations that were asked for,
and none of them able to become anything else cheaply.
