# Persona: The Second Time

## Taxonomy

[Repeated](position_repeated.md). The next change, arriving sooner because the last one was made, and reopening a window that had not finished closing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Correct again, which is the trouble.
Not recklessness and not a taste for upheaval: the world turned again, the answer is sound again, and the body is now more practised at moving.
Likelier for the first attempt having happened.

## Action

Moves the organisation again.
Does so while the hazard from the previous move is still running down.
Is as well justified as the first one was.

## Shadow

The exit that simply waiting would have provided.
Because the hazard declines with time since the change, an organisation left alone earns its reliability back; because change makes further change more available, being left alone is not reliably what happens.
It ends having reopened the window, for reasons nobody can fault.

## Tell

Says: we know how to do this now.
True, and that is precisely the finding that removes the last way out.
