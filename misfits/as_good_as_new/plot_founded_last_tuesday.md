## Stage

[The Attempt](persona_the_attempt.md), on [Become the Thing That Is Needed](plan_become_the_thing_that_is_needed.md), succeeds at what it set out to do and spends [the record](persona_the_record.md) doing it, so [the clock](piece_the_clock.md) goes back.

## Tension

The cost is the record itself, which is why nothing cheaper was available.
The structure that reproduced is the structure being taken apart, so during the work the organisation cannot be relied on to come out the same way: that is not a failure of the rebuild, it is what a rebuild is. The account it could render no longer describes the body rendering it. And the legitimacy and institutional support built on decades of repetition rest on a claim about a body that is currently being replaced.
So it emerges **young**. Not young as a figure of speech: young in the sense the mortality tables mean, carrying the elevated hazard of a thing that has not yet demonstrated it can repeat. **The attempt did not fail. Succeeding is what cost this.**
