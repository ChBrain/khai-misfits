# Piece: The Thousand and Eleven

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The evidence: a thousand and eleven newspaper organisations, followed across a hundred and ninety-three years.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the long run](place_the_long_run.md), which is the only place a population this size is legible.
Read against age, and against time since the last change.

## Load Bearing

Without it the account is a claim about how organisations feel during upheaval.
It is load bearing because it is **a population rather than a case**: the pattern is estimated across a thousand bodies over nearly two centuries, so it is a rate and not an anecdote, and it can say by how much and for how long rather than only that it hurts.

## Apparent

Historical, and about newspapers.
It reads as remote from any organisation a reader has in mind, which understates it: the length is what makes the shape of the risk visible at all.

## Yearbook

Read by [resetting](process_resetting.md), and carrying [the two readings](piece_the_two_readings.md) that the play refuses to separate.
It is the reason the cost has a shape and a duration rather than only a direction.
