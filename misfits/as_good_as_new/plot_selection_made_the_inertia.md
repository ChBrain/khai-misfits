## Action

## Stage

[The Selector](persona_the_selector.md), on [Keep the Ones That Can Be Relied On](plan_keep_the_ones_that_can_be_relied_on.md), rewards two properties and thereby manufactures a third it never chose.

## Tension

This is the inversion, and it is easy to get backwards.
The obvious reading is that inertia is a flaw which selection failed to weed out, a residue of slack in bodies that were not pressed hard enough. It is the opposite. Reliability and accountability require reproducibility; reproducibility **is** inertia; so an environment that keeps choosing dependable organisations is an environment that produces organisations which cannot easily be changed. **The inertia is the consequence of selection and not a precondition of it.**
Which means pressure does not relieve it. A harsher environment, selecting harder on exactly the properties that matter, produces bodies that are more inert rather than less, and the sources are ordinary and defensible: sunk commitments in plant and people, coalitions that had to be built to get anything done, and precedents that became the standard because following them was how the thing was reliably done.
