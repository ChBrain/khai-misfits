# Play: As Good as New

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

As Good as New.
An organisation that has done the same thing the same way for forty years, and is trusted for exactly that: it delivers what it says, it can account for what it did, and both of those are true because the structure underneath reproduces itself with very little drift.
It can see what it must become. Nobody inside disputes it.
And the reorganisation that would take it there returns it to the death rate of a body founded last Tuesday.

## Arc

The bend is this: the trap is not ignorance and cannot be answered by seeing more clearly, because the organisation already sees perfectly, and the thing that kills it is the move it makes after it sees.
It runs on two facts, neither of them anybody's doing: reliability and accountability are bought with reproducibility, and reproducibility is inertia under another name.
Take the competence first, because the play has to establish that it is **real**. An organisation that can be relied on to deliver, and that can render an account of what it did, holds two things most bodies do not. Both rest on the same foundation: a structure that comes out the same way each time it is run. That fidelity is not laziness and not complacency: it is the mechanism of the competence, and a body without it can promise nothing and explain nothing.
Then the inversion, which is the finding and is easy to get backwards. The inertia is not a flaw that survived selection. It is what selection **produced**: the environment rewards the reliable and the accountable, those properties require reproducibility, so an environment that keeps choosing dependable organisations is an environment that manufactures organisations which cannot easily be changed. **Inertia is the consequence, not the precondition.**
Then the hinge, which is the whole play. The organisation sees the turn. It is right about it. It agrees internally on what it must become. And the attempt itself dismantles the record: the structure that reproduced is taken apart, what could be relied on is in question, the account that could be rendered no longer describes the body rendering it, and the legitimacy and support built on decades of repetition are suspended while the work is done. The body emerges young. Not metaphorically young: young in the way the actuarial tables mean, carrying the elevated hazard of a thing that has not yet proved it can repeat.
And the evidence is a population rather than a story. A thousand and eleven newspaper organisations, followed over a hundred and ninety-three years, and the finding has two halves that must both be carried: change raises the hazard of failure immediately after it is made, and that hazard then declines with time since the change, exactly as age-dependence works. It is a window, not a doom.
Reach for a settlement and there is none that is free. Do not change, and the organisation is what it was in a world that is not. Change, and it enters the window. Change slowly, and the window is longer and shallower rather than absent. And the second half of the same finding closes the last exit: an organisation that has changed once is **more likely to change again**, so the window can be reopened before the previous one has finished closing. **The move that is correct is the move that is dangerous, and knowing this does not make it less correct.**

## Stakes

Whether a body can become something else without dying of it.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the organisation is complacent, because it is not and its sameness is the mechanism of its competence, and not whether it understands its situation, because it understands it exactly, but whether the properties that made it worth keeping can survive being rebuilt. The production cannot make them survive. It can only show that reliability and accountability are bought with reproducibility and that reproducibility is inertia under another name, that selection manufactured the inertia rather than overlooking it, that seeing the turn perfectly and agreeing on the answer changes nothing about what follows, that the attempt spends the record and returns the body to the mortality of a new one, and that the window this opens declines with time and can be reopened by the next attempt, which is likelier for having made this one.
