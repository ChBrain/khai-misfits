# Position: Selector

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The environment's seat: it keeps what can be relied on and accounted for, and produces inertia without ever selecting for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Two criteria and no others: dependable delivery, and conduct that can be explained afterwards.
No view whatever about how an organisation is built inside.
More force where the environment is harsher.

## Orders

Keep the dependable.
Discard the rest.
Apply the same two tests, indefinitely.

## Loses

Nothing, and the inversion sits here: the properties it rewards are bought with reproducibility, so selecting for them manufactures bodies that cannot easily change.
Because the inertia is the consequence of the selection rather than a residue escaping it, pressure makes it worse, and a fiercer environment yields organisations that are more inert rather than fewer.
It ends having produced precisely what it asked for, and something else with it.

## Drives

Toward a population of dependable organisations,
by the only test that identifies them,
so the seat that raises the standard is the seat that builds the trap.
