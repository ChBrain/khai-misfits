## Cue

## Action

[Reproducing](process_reproducing.md): [The Record](persona_the_record.md), in [the reproduced seat](position_reproduced.md), runs again at [the population](place_the_population.md) and comes out as it did last time.

## Stage

[The Record](persona_the_record.md), on [Come Out the Same Way Every Time](plan_come_out_the_same_way_every_time.md), delivers what it said it would and renders an account of what it did, both of which rest on the same foundation.

## Tension

The competence is real, and the play has to establish that before it can be the problem.
An organisation that can be relied on to deliver, and that can explain afterwards what it did and why, holds two things most bodies do not, and both are bought with the same coin: a structure that reproduces with very little drift. **The sameness is the mechanism of the competence and not a symptom of complacency.** A body without it can promise nothing, because a promise is a claim about a repeat, and can explain nothing, because an account is a claim about a procedure that was actually followed.
So the trap cannot be sprung by finding the organisation lazy. It has to be sprung with the competence intact and doing exactly what it is for.
