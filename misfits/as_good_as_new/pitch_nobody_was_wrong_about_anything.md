# Pitch: Nobody Was Wrong About Anything

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Actuarial, unsentimental, patient.
The register of a hazard rate read off a population: how many were alive at the start of the year, how many at the end, and how old they were.
Nothing scornful anywhere in it, because the organisation is right about what it needs and right to want it.

## Undertow

Under the competence, two facts that do not negotiate.
Reliability and accountability are bought with reproducibility. Reproducibility is inertia under another name.
Between those two, the properties worth keeping are the properties that cannot be carried across a rebuild.

## Nerve

The unease of being right,
agreeing with everybody who matters,
and finding that the correct move is the one that puts the body back on the mortality table it spent forty years climbing off.

## Echo

After the run: a structure that does what it needs to do,
a hazard rate at the level of a body founded last Tuesday,
and a finding that the window closes with time, and that the next attempt is likelier for this one having been made.
