# Process: Resetting

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The hazard returning to its founding level, declining with time since the change, and reopening when the body moves again.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The rebuild, completed.
At [the long run](place_the_long_run.md), where a hazard rate is a thing that can be read at all.

## Direction

Toward a body that must earn its reliability again from the beginning, on the same terms as any new organisation: repeat, and keep repeating, until the repeats are evidence.
The hazard rises at the change and then declines with time since it, which is the shape age-dependence always has, so this is a window rather than a doom.
And an organisation that has changed once is more available to change again, so the window can reopen before it has shut.

## Lever

Time since the last change, which is the only thing that closes it.
Because the same account that makes change disruptive makes it more likely to recur, the remedy of simply waiting is not reliably available.

## Echo

Reliability, earned back, on the same terms as the first time,
unless the next correct move arrives first.
