# Plot: They Could See It Perfectly

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The turn in the world, arrived and unambiguous.
[The Right Answer](persona_the_right_answer.md), known inside the organisation and disputed by nobody.

## Action

[Seeing](process_seeing.md): [The Right Answer](persona_the_right_answer.md), in [the sighted seat](position_sighted.md), is worked out at [the long run](place_the_long_run.md) and agreed across the body that would have to enact it.

## Stage

[The Right Answer](persona_the_right_answer.md), on [Be Right About What Is Needed](plan_be_right_about_what_is_needed.md), names the form the organisation must take, correctly, and is accepted without a fight.

## Tension

Perception is eliminated as the problem, and this is the plot that does it.
There is no faction denying the turn, no incumbent defending a comfortable position, no report that failed to reach the top, and no blindness anywhere: the organisation understands its situation exactly and agrees on what it must become. **None of that helps.**
So every remedy addressed to awareness is spent before the play begins. Better information does nothing, because the information is already good. A clearer view does nothing, because the view is already clear. Alignment does nothing, because everybody is aligned. **The danger is downstream of the insight**, and lives entirely in the act of acting on it, which is the one thing being right obliges the organisation to do.
