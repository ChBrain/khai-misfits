# Place: The Long Run

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The span over which a turn is legible as a turn and a hazard rate is a thing that can be read at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A hundred and ninety-three years, and a thousand and eleven organisations inside them.
Rates of failure, against age and against time since the last change.

## Holds

Both halves of the finding, which have to be carried together.
The hazard rises when the change is made and declines with time since it, so this is a window rather than a doom; and a body that has changed once is more likely to change again, so the window can reopen before it has shut.

## Offers

Evidence rather than a story: a population followed long enough that the shape of the risk is measurable.

## Withheld

The comfort of the first half on its own.
Read alone, the decline with time says wait and recover; read with the second half, waiting is not reliably what happens.
