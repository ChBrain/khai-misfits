# Process: Reproducing

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The structure running again and coming out as it did last time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Each occasion the organisation is asked to do the thing it does.
At [the population](place_the_population.md), among bodies being judged on whether they can be depended on.

## Direction

Toward delivery that can be relied on and conduct that can be explained afterwards, which are the two properties worth having.
Both are bought with the same coin: low drift between one running and the next.
So the fidelity is the mechanism of the competence and not a symptom of complacency, and a body without it can neither promise nor explain.

## Lever

Low drift, which is what makes a claim about the future or the past possible at all.
Because both valuable properties rest on this one foundation, nothing preserves them across a rebuild of it.

## Echo

An organisation worth believing,
and a structure that cannot be moved without spending what makes it worth believing.
