# Persona: The Right Answer

## Taxonomy

[Sighted](position_sighted.md). The form the organisation must take: correctly identified, understood throughout, and disputed by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Correct, and known to be.
Not a proposal fighting for air: the turn in the world is unambiguous, the analysis is sound, and the body that would have to enact it agrees.
Arrives with no opposition to overcome.

## Action

Names what the organisation must become.
Is accepted.
Obliges the organisation to act, because being right about this is what an obligation to act consists of.

## Shadow

Nothing, and this seat exists to eliminate perception as the explanation.
There is no denying faction, no defended incumbency, no report that failed to arrive and no blindness anywhere, so every remedy addressed to awareness is spent before the play opens: better information changes nothing when the information is good, and alignment changes nothing when everybody is aligned.
It ends correct, and having been correct is what starts the harm.

## Tell

Says: this is what we have to become, and everyone agrees.
True on both counts, and the danger is entirely downstream of it.
