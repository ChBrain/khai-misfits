# Process: Seeing

## Taxonomy

[As Good as New](play_as_good_as_new.md). The turn perceived, the new form worked out, and the body agreeing on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An unambiguous change in what the world requires.
At [the long run](place_the_long_run.md), where the turn is visible as a turn and not as noise.

## Direction

Toward a correct account of what the organisation must now become, arrived at soundly and accepted throughout.
There is no denying faction, no defended incumbency and no report that failed to arrive: this step works.
So every remedy addressed to awareness is spent here, and none of them touches what follows.

## Lever

Accuracy, achieved.
Because the danger lives downstream in the acting rather than upstream in the knowing, improving this step further changes nothing at all.

## Echo

A body that understands its situation exactly,
and is obliged by that understanding to do the dangerous thing.
