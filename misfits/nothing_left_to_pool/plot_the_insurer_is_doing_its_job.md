# Plot: The Insurer Is Doing Its Job

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Left to Pool](play_nothing_left_to_pool.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An underwriter pricing what it can see.

## Action

[Pricing the risk](process_pricing_the_risk.md): [the underwriter](persona_the_underwriter.md), holding [Underwriter](position_underwriter.md), rates the application at [the underwriting desk](place_the_underwriting_desk.md).

## Stage

A price that matches the risk, and a book that stays solvent.

## Tension

Pricing accurately and declining to pool are one act, so the duty and the mechanism cannot be separated.
The regulator requires it, the people already covered are owed it, and nothing is being extracted that the applicant does not already hold. Which means the seat cannot be asked to be better at its job in order to save the arrangement, because being better at its job is what ends it, and an underwriter who ignored what it could see would be failing everybody in the book to protect one applicant.
