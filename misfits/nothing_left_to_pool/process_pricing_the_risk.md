# Process: Pricing the Risk

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Left to Pool](play_nothing_left_to_pool.md). The insurer's whole duty, performed, which is the same act as declining to pool.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An application carrying a fact the applicant already holds.
[The Underwriter](persona_the_underwriter.md), holding [Underwriter](position_underwriter.md), at [the underwriting desk](place_the_underwriting_desk.md).

## Direction

Toward a price that matches the risk.
Toward a solvent book and a fair charge to everybody in it.

## Lever

The regulator's requirement and the low group's entitlement, which point the same way and admit no discretion.

## Echo

An accurate price, and one pool sorted into two that do not need each other.
