# Play: Nothing Left to Pool

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Nothing Left to Pool.
A thousand people each face a risk and none of them knows whose turn it will be, so they put money together and whoever it lands on is carried by the rest. What they are trading is not money but the not knowing. Then a test arrives that says whose turn it is, and it is a good test, wanted for good reasons, and everybody may read it. There is now nothing left to share.

## Arc

The bend is this: the good being sold is a shared ignorance, so measuring the risk consumes the thing being sold.
It runs on a pool that exists because nobody knows.
[The Pool](piece_the_pool.md) works by [The Insured](persona_the_insured.md) and nine hundred and ninety nine others being, for this purpose, the same person. That is not a convenient approximation: it is the whole of what makes the trade worth either side's while.
And the test is good and is wanted.
[The Test](piece_the_test.md) tells somebody something true about themselves, which they wanted for reasons that have nothing to do with any policy: whether to be screened, what to tell their children, how to plan. [The Clinician](persona_the_clinician.md) is right to offer it and they are right to take it.
Then [A Measured Risk Is Not a Shared One](plot_a_measured_risk_is_not_a_shared_one.md).
The test does not raise anybody's risk. It sorts one pool into two, and the two do not need each other: the low group is paying for a certainty and the high group is buying one, so what was insurance on both sides becomes a schedule of payments.
And [The Insurer Is Doing Its Job](plot_the_insurer_is_doing_its_job.md).
[The Underwriter](persona_the_underwriter.md) prices what it can see, which is what an insurer is for and what its regulator requires of it, and pricing accurately is the same act as declining to pool.
And [Nobody's Conduct Produced It](plot_nobodys_conduct_produced_it.md). The person now uninsurable did nothing, chose nothing and can change nothing: what has altered is only how much is known about a fact that was already true, so there is not even the thin consolation of a decision to have taken differently.
And [the bar on using it restores the pool and hides the fact](plot_and_the_bar_on_using_it_restores_the_pool_and_hides_the_fact.md). [The Bar](piece_the_bar.md) is the real remedy, is law in several countries, and works by forbidding one side to use information the other side has, which is the arrangement the house already stages as an entirely different failure.

## Stakes

Whether a thing that exists only while nobody knows can survive people finding out.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a good made of shared ignorance, a test that is accurate and wanted for its own reasons, a sorting that changes no risk and ends a trade, an underwriter doing exactly what it is for, a casualty with no conduct to regret, and a remedy that works by forbidding one side to know what the other does, and away from any account in which an insurer hid behind a test or a customer concealed one. The production cannot fault a clinician for offering a good test, and it cannot fault an underwriter for pricing what it can see, because that is the same act as protecting everybody it already covers. What is left to fight over is not whether knowing is better than not knowing, it usually is, but what to call a market that is dissolved by the arrival of the truth.
