# Persona: The Low Group

## Taxonomy

[Low Group](position_low_group.md). The ones the test cleared, who no longer need the others.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A result that says the risk is small, and a premium set for a pool that assumed otherwise.
No wish to harm anybody.

## Action

Declines to pay for a risk it now knows it does not carry.
Leaves, or asks for its own price.

## Shadow

Is not defecting, since there is nothing left to defect from.
The seat is not free-riding, hiding a fact or exploiting anybody: it is declining to buy something it has been shown it does not need, which is what anybody does about anything, and the pool it leaves was only a pool while its own position was unknown to it.

## Tell

Ends the arrangement by acting on good news honestly.
