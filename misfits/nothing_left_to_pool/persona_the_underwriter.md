# Persona: The Underwriter

## Taxonomy

[Underwriter](position_underwriter.md). The one who prices what can be seen.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A book of policies, a solvency requirement, and a duty to the people already covered.
Information that is available to the applicant and to anybody else who asks.

## Action

Prices the risk it can observe.
Declines or loads what it now knows.

## Shadow

Is doing the whole of its job, and its job is the mechanism.
Pricing accurately is what a regulator requires, what the low group is entitled to, and what keeps the book solvent for everybody in it; and pricing accurately and declining to pool are one act rather than two, so there is no version of this seat that does its duty and preserves the pool.

## Tell

Protects the people it covers by ending the thing that covered them.
