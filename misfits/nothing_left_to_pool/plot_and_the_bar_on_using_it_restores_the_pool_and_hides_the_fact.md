# Plot: And the Bar on Using It Restores the Pool and Hides the Fact

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Left to Pool](play_nothing_left_to_pool.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A law forbidding insurers to ask.

## Action

[Forbidding the question](process_forbidding_the_question.md): [the legislator](persona_the_legislator.md) enacts [the bar](piece_the_bar.md), and the pool stands again.

## Stage

The applicant may know and the insurer may not ask.

## Tension

The remedy works by manufacturing the asymmetry the house stages elsewhere as its own failure.
The state cannot make a fact unknown, so it makes it unusable on one side only, and the pool that returns is not the symmetric one that existed before: it is one in which the parties hold different information deliberately. That is a trade rather than a repair, and how much selection follows from it is argued rather than settled, which is the honest end of the play: the best available answer to a problem of too much shared knowledge is a legislated ignorance on one side of the table.
