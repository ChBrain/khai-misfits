# Persona: The Clinician

## Taxonomy

[Clinician](position_clinician.md). The one who offers a good test to somebody who wants it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An accurate test, a patient with questions, and reasons that have nothing to do with insurance.
Screening intervals, family, planning, treatment that starts earlier.

## Shadow

Withholding a useful result to protect somebody's insurability would be its own kind of harm and no code permits it, so the seat cannot decline; and the same knowledge that improves the care removes the ignorance the cover was made of.

## Tell

Does good medicine and ends an insurance.
