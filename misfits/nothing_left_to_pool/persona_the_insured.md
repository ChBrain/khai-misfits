# Persona: The Insured

## Taxonomy

[Insured](position_insured.md). The one who was interchangeable, and is not any more.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A risk faced in common with a thousand others, and no way to know whose turn it is.
A test available, and reasons of their own for wanting it.

## Action

Pools while nobody knows.
Takes the test, for reasons unconnected to any policy.

## Shadow

Loses the cover by learning something true, with no conduct to regret.
Nothing they did raised the risk, the fact was true before the test and is true after it, and there was no decision available that would have kept both the knowledge and the cover: wanting to know is the ordinary thing to want, and the price of it is set by a market that cannot carry a known outcome.

## Tell

Is priced out by a fact that was always the case.
