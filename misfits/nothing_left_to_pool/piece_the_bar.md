## Load Bearing

It restores the pool by manufacturing an asymmetry, which is the arrangement the house stages elsewhere as its own failure.
The state cannot make a fact unknown, so it makes it unusable by one side: the applicant may know and the insurer may not ask. That does restore a pool, and the pool it restores is not the symmetric one that existed before but one in which the two parties hold different information on purpose. Whether the selection that follows is large enough to unravel the market is argued rather than settled, which the play carries.

## Apparent

A protection. A fairness measure.

## Yearbook

Turns a symmetric problem into an asymmetric one deliberately, which is a trade rather than a repair.
