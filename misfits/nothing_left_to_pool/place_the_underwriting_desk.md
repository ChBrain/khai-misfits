# Place: The Underwriting Desk

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Left to Pool](play_nothing_left_to_pool.md). Where what can be seen is what is charged for, correctly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An application, a set of observable facts, a rating table, a price or a refusal.
No bad faith: the same rules for everybody, a solvency requirement behind them, and nothing extracted that the applicant does not already have.

## Holds

[The Underwriter](persona_the_underwriter.md), doing exactly what the seat is for.
[The Test](piece_the_test.md) result, which the applicant holds and may be asked for.
[The Insured](persona_the_insured.md), whose risk has not changed at all.

## Offers

A price that reflects the risk, which is what the regulator requires and what everybody already covered is owed.

## Withheld

Any way to price accurately and pool at the same time.
The place makes the two into one act: every increment of accuracy is an increment of separation, so the desk cannot be asked to be better at its job in order to save the arrangement, since being better at its job is what ends it.
