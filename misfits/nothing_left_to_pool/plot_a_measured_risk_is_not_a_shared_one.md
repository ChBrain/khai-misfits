# Plot: A Measured Risk Is Not a Shared One

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Left to Pool](play_nothing_left_to_pool.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The result, which changes nobody's risk.

## Action

One pool is sorted into two by a fact that was already true of everybody in it.

## Stage

[The Low Group](persona_the_low_group.md) no longer needs the others, and the others cannot be carried by it.

## Tension

The two groups do not need each other, so what was insurance on both sides becomes a schedule of payments.
The low group is paying for a certainty and the high group is buying one, and neither is a transfer. Nothing here required anybody to defect: the low group declines to buy a thing it has been shown it does not need, which is what anybody does about anything, and the pool it leaves was a pool only while its own position was unknown to it.
