# Nothing Left to Pool: Reference

The research warrant for Nothing Left to Pool.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the destruction of insurance opportunities by information that
arrives before the risk resolves, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging insurance as **a good made of a shared ignorance, which measurement consumes**: a thousand people face
a risk and none of them can say whose turn it will be, so they put money together and whoever it lands on is
carried by the rest. Money is only the vehicle. What is actually being exchanged is the mutual inability to
say which of them it is, and that is why the price is bearable and why both sides gain. Then a test arrives
that says whose turn it is. It is accurate, it is wanted for reasons that have nothing to do with any policy,
and everybody may read it. The play models the documented mechanics, the pool that rests on symmetric
ignorance, the test that is good clinical practice, the sorting that raises nobody's risk and ends a trade,
the underwriter whose duty and whose effect are one act, the casualty with no conduct to regret, and the legal
bar that restores the pool by manufacturing an asymmetry, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as
a tale of an insurer hiding behind a test.

The governing law is that the good being sold is the not knowing, so any accurate account of whose turn it is
removes the substance and leaves the vehicle. The payments can continue and there is no longer a transfer in
them.

**The test changes nobody's risk, which is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The fact was true
before it and is true after it, so nothing about the world has worsened: only how much is known has moved. The
person now uninsurable did not smoke, drive badly or conceal anything. They asked a question about their own
body and received an accurate answer, and every account of misfortune that offers a lesson is unavailable here.

What removes any possibility of blame from the underwriter is that pricing accurately and declining to pool
are one act rather than two. The regulator requires the first, the people already covered are owed it, and
nothing is extracted that the applicant does not already hold. So the seat cannot be asked to be better at its
job in order to save the arrangement, because being better at its job is what ends it.

What removes it from the clinician is that the reasons are clinical and would stand if no insurer existed
anywhere. Screening intervals, what to tell a family, when treatment should begin: each is sufficient alone,
and withholding a useful result to protect somebody's insurability is a harm of its own that no code permits.

And the low group closes the case against the last party, because it is not defecting. It declines to buy a
thing it has been shown it does not need, which is what anybody does about anything, and what it leaves was a
pool only while its own position was unknown to it.

It is distinct from the house's **The Market for Lemons**, which is its nearest neighbour and its **other
half**: there one side knows what the other does not, so the informed party's private knowledge drives the
good out of the market, and the repair is to close the gap with warranties, certification or disclosure. Here
there is no gap to close. The information is symmetric, everybody may read the same result, and the pool
dissolves anyway. The two are the halves of one bind rather than variants of one mechanism, and the proof of it
is that **the remedy for this misfit creates the other one**: barring the insurer from asking restores a pool
by putting the applicant in possession of what the underwriter may not have. That work's spine sits there and
not here, which is why this warrant rests on Hirshleifer instead.

It is distinct from the house's **Moral Hazard**, where cover changes the behaviour of the covered so the
insured party takes more of the risk they are protected from: there the insurance **causes** something, and
the remedy is to leave some exposure with the insured. Here the insurance causes nothing and the test causes
nothing. Nobody's behaviour changes at all, and what moves is a quantity of knowledge about a fact that was
already settled.

It is distinct from the house's **The Proxy**, where a group statistic stands in for an individual and becomes
self-fulfilling: there the difficulty is that the individual is being priced by a number about other people,
and better individual information would **help** them. Here better individual information is what harms them,
so the two run in opposite directions on the same act, and a reform that replaced group rating with individual
measurement would repair one misfit by causing this one.

It is distinct from the house's **Borrowed Immunity**, where a person declines a small private cost because
others have made the risk remote, so voluntary coverage stalls below what everybody needs: there somebody free
rides on a protection they still benefit from. Here the low group is not free riding on anything, because once
the sorting is done the protection does not extend to them and there is nothing left to ride on.

It is distinct from the house's **Nothing Known Against It**, where an absence of evidence is read as clearance
and the substitute passes because nobody has looked: there the trouble is **too little** information, and
looking would fix it. Here the trouble is arriving information that is accurate, and looking is what did it,
which is the same pair of misfits pointing in opposite directions on the value of finding out.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the insurer as
  hiding behind a test or the customer as concealing one, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get
  wrong: the pool rests on an ignorance every party shares rather than on a secret anybody keeps; the test is
  accurate, is wanted on clinical grounds alone, and raises nobody's risk; the underwriter's duty to price
  accurately is owed to the people already covered and is the same act as declining to pool; the low group
  declines to buy a thing it has been shown it does not need; and the person left uninsurable did nothing,
  chose nothing and can change nothing. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so
  the misfit is that the good being sold is a shared ignorance and measurement consumes it.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Barring the use of the
  result is the real remedy, is law in several countries and does restore a pool, and it works by making the
  information unusable on one side rather than unknown, so the pool that returns is deliberately asymmetric and
  is the arrangement the house stages as a separate failure. Insuring against reclassification is the answer
  the mechanism invites and requires everybody to commit before anybody knows, which is exactly the window the
  test closes. Declining to be tested protects the cover and forgoes the medicine. Subsidising the high group
  from public funds works and is a transfer rather than an insurance, which is a different thing honestly
  named. And knowing the mechanism completely leaves the clinician still right to offer the test.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the information result, the account of risk-bearing as the traded
  good, the time-consistent contract, the analysis of categorical discrimination and the statutes are theirs.
  The bounds are carried with them: **how much adverse selection follows a legal bar is genuinely contested**
  and varies by line of business, so the play stages the structure and never a magnitude, and nothing here is
  an argument for or against any particular statute; the Hirshleifer result concerns information arriving
  **before** an uncertainty resolves and does not say that information is generally harmful, which is a
  misreading the play declines; the account treats the pool as one arrangement dissolving once, which is the
  play's own simplification of markets that segment continuously and by degrees; and genetic testing is used
  as the clearest case rather than the only one, since the mechanism is about the precision of any risk
  measurement. **The play makes no claim that people should not be tested, or that any insurer acted
  wrongly**, only that the thing being pooled is the not knowing, and that finding out is what spends it.
