# Plot: The Pool Is Made of Not Knowing

## Taxonomy

[Nothing Left to Pool](play_nothing_left_to_pool.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A thousand people facing one risk, none of them knowing whose turn it is.

## Action

[Sharing what nobody knows](process_sharing_what_nobody_knows.md): [the insured](persona_the_insured.md), holding [Insured](position_insured.md), pools in [the pool before the test](place_the_pool_before_the_test.md).

## Stage

[The Pool](piece_the_pool.md) works, and pays people through the worst year of their lives.

## Tension

The good being traded is the shared ignorance, and the money is only the vehicle.
Being interchangeable for this purpose is not a convenient approximation: it is true, it is why the price is bearable, and it is why both sides gain. Which means the arrangement rests on a symmetric ignorance rather than on a secret, so nobody's discretion protects it and nothing anybody promises can keep it alive once the fact arrives.
