# Piece: The Gap

## Taxonomy

[Held Under](play_held_under.md). The distance between the two thresholds: the load that tips the system in, and the far lower load at which it could climb back out.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Stretched across [The Queue](place_the_queue.md), invisible on any board. Above the upper threshold the system tips; below the lower one it recovers; and between them both states exist at once, so which one holds is set by where the system has been and not by the load arriving now.

## Load Bearing

It is load bearing as the reason waiting is not a strategy at any duration, and the reason the escape must be violent. Going in, it took a transient above the ordinary to cross the line. Coming out, ordinary is nowhere near low enough, because the Service must now clear the fresh load and [The Sustaining Load](piece_the_sustaining_load.md) together, so the level that would starve the loop sits far beneath the level the system carried comfortably all week (Bronson and colleagues). This is what makes the trap counterintuitive from the outside: a system failing under a load it handled yesterday reads as a contradiction, when it is the plain signature of two thresholds instead of one. And it is what makes a proportionate response useless: shedding back to normal leaves the loop fully fed, so the Breaker must be thrown further and held longer than the situation appears to warrant, and anything short of the lower threshold reads, wrongly, as the shedding having failed (Huang, Magnusson, and colleagues).

## Apparent

Reads as nothing at all, since neither threshold is drawn on any dashboard and only one of them is ever crossed on the way in. What shows instead is the puzzle it produces: a service refusing a load it served without noticing an hour ago.

## Yearbook

Opened by [Sustaining](process_sustaining.md), which is what separates the two thresholds in the first place: without the retry load summing onto the fresh, they would sit at the same level and the system would lift the moment [The Trigger](piece_the_trigger.md) passed. It is what holds [The Metastable State](piece_the_metastable_state.md) shut against time, and it sets the setting of [The Breaker](piece_the_breaker.md): how far below normal the Operator must shed, and for how long, before [The Queue](place_the_queue.md) can drain.
