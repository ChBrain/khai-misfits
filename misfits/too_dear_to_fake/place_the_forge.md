# Place: The Forge

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). Where the signal is burned into being and its cost is paid, differently by holder and pretender. Hosts [The Mimic Falls Short](plot_the_mimic_falls_short.md) and [The Waste Is the Proof](plot_the_waste_is_the_proof.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The making of the proof: the years of the degree, the risk of the tail, the runway of the spend, resource poured into a display whose worth is its cost.

## Holds

[The Signal](piece_the_signal.md) in the making, [The Differential](piece_the_differential.md) that prices it apart, and [The Deadweight](piece_the_deadweight.md) burned across all who make it.

## Offers

To the holder, a proof too dear for a pretender to match: a cost that excludes exactly the one who lacks the quality.

## Withheld

Any cheap way to make the same proof, since a signal that cost little would convince no one, so the forge offers only the dear road and no other.
