# Plan: Trust the Dear

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reader's seat](position_reader.md), inside [Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reader](position_reader.md)

## Direction

Believe only a signal too costly to fake, and discount the cheap claim. A quality you cannot see must be read from a proof, and the only proof you can trust is one a pretender could not have afforded, so grant your trust to the dear signal and withhold it from the bare word. This is sound, since faking the dear proof would cost more than the prize, and it is also the rule that obliges every holder to burn to be read.
