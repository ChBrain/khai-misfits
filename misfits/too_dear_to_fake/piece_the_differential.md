# Piece: The Differential

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). The cost that falls harder on the party lacking the quality, the single condition that makes the signal honest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Fixed at [The Forge](place_the_forge.md), in the very cost of making the signal: the same degree cheaper for the able to earn, the same tail deadlier for the unfit to carry, so the price of the proof is not one price but two, lower for the holder and higher for the pretender (Spence; Grafen).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the signal is cheap talk: if the proof cost the pretender no more than the holder, the pretender would send it too and the receiver could believe nothing.
It is the differential that carries the truth, not the display. The signal separates the quality from its absence only because the one who lacks it cannot profitably match the burn, so this gap in cost, and not the credential itself, is the whole of the honesty (Spence; Grafen).

## Apparent

A detail of the cost, easily overlooked: that the proof is a little harder for some than others. From outside it looks like a footnote, and it is the load-bearing beam.
