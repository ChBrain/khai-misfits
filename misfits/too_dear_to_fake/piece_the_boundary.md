# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). The condition the honest signal needs and the edges where the waste lifts, the hedge that keeps the trap real but bounded.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Weighed at [The Assay](place_the_assay.md), where the quality might be tested directly and a cheaper proof considered: the reformer's ledger of what could replace the burn, and of when no burn was needed at all (Spence; Grafen).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overreaches into a claim that every credential is empty and every display pure waste, which is false: the boundary is what keeps the trap honest.
It carries the condition and the escape. The honest signal needs a cost that falls harder on the pretender and a quality that cannot be seen; give it a signal cheap to fake, a quality plain to observe, or a cost that is also productive, and the waste falls or disappears, a degree that truly trains, a test that truly checks, a proof that is not pure burn.

## Apparent

A list of exceptions where the deal is fairer, easy to read as a softening that lets signaling off, rather than the line that says exactly when the burn is waste and when it is not.
