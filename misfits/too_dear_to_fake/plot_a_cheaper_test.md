# Plot: A Cheaper Test

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Assay](place_the_assay.md), where a reformer weighs whether the waste can be cut.

## Action

[The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) on [Cut the Waste](plan_cut_the_waste.md) through [Reforming](process_reforming.md) holds [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): a cheaper test that cannot be faked would spare the burn, but a fakeable one collapses to cheap talk, direct verification spends its own resource, and a banned signal breeds another. The scene closes with the waste hard to cut and the trap shown bounded (Spence; Grafen).

## Stage

The honest signal needs a differential cost and a hidden quality: where the quality is plain to see, the test cannot be gamed, or the cost is productive, a degree that truly trains, a check that truly reads, the burn lifts, so the waste is real but bounded and not every credential is empty.

## Tension

The reformer is right that the burn is waste and still cannot make the belief cheaper than the truth it rests on, so each shortcut either gets faked or spends a cost of its own, and the cure moves the deadweight rather than removing it.
