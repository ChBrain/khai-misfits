# Persona: The Reader

## Taxonomy

[Reader](position_reader.md). The one who must judge a hidden quality and can trust only a signal too dear to fake, so its trust is what makes the burn worth spending.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Discerning, fair, a good judge of the real thing.
Weighs the proof before it and trusts what is earned, passing over the empty claim, proud of a standard that lets no pretender through.
No prejudice in it: the standard is sound, since a proof too dear to fake really is more reliable than a word that costs nothing.

## Action

Reads [the Signal](piece_the_signal.md) through [Reading](process_reading.md) at [the Gate](place_the_gate.md), granting trust to the costly proof and withholding it from the bare claim, on [Trust the Dear](plan_trust_the_dear.md).
Cannot trust a cheap claim without being fooled, because anyone could make it, so it must demand the dear proof and read the quality from the cost.
Does what a sound judge does facing an unseen quality: it believes what could not have been faked, and the believing sets the price every holder must meet.

## Shadow

Not a snob for demanding the dear proof: the cheap claim really is unreliable, and requiring a costly one is the only sound way to tell holders from pretenders.
Because its trust rests on the cost, its fair standard is what obliges every holder to burn, so the very discernment it takes pride in is what makes the waste mandatory, the judge and the toll one and the same.
It is the one who reads the quality rightly, and the one whose right reading forces the burning.

## Tell

Waves off the uncredentialed sight unseen, sure that the absence of a dear proof is the absence of the quality, and says so as fairness.
Cannot be faulted for the rule, because the rule is sound, so it enforces the burn while believing it merely rewards merit, the toll it sets read by itself as justice.
