# Piece: The Separation

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). The equilibrium in which the holders signal and the pretenders do not, so the receiver reads the signal as truth.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Standing at [The Gate](place_the_gate.md), where the sort holds: the able carry the credential and the rest do not, so the reader, seeing the signal, knows the quality is there, and seeing none, knows to doubt (Spence).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the signal means nothing: if holders and pretenders both sent it, or neither did, the receiver could read no quality from it, and the burn would buy no belief.
It is the separation that makes the signal informative. Because the differential cost sorts the two into signalers and non-signalers, the presence of the signal carries the quality, and the whole point of the burn is to hold this sort in place.

## Apparent

A clean reading of the room: those with the mark are trusted, those without are passed over, the world sorted tidily by an honest badge.
