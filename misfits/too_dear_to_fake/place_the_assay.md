# Place: The Assay

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). Where the quality might be tested directly and a cheaper proof is weighed against the burn. Hosts [A Cheaper Test](plot_a_cheaper_test.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The reformer's bench: the alternative tests laid out, the quality probed for a direct read, the deadweight reckoned against what a cheaper proof would cost.

## Holds

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the conditions under which a cheaper test holds and those under which it collapses back to the burn.

## Offers

The hope of a cheaper proof: a test unfakeable and cheap, a direct check of the quality, a signal that is also productive.

## Withheld

A costless correction, since a cheap test that can be gamed proves nothing and a real check spends its own resource, so no free way to end the burn is on offer here.
