# Plan: Cut the Waste

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reformer's seat](position_reformer.md), inside [Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reformer](position_reformer.md)

## Direction

Replace the dear signal with a cheaper proof that cannot be faked, or verify the quality directly, and cut the deadweight where you can. The burn is real waste and worth ending, but the belief can be no cheaper than the unfakeable proof it rests on, so measure each shortcut: a fakeable cheap test collapses to cheap talk, a direct check spends its own resource, and a banned signal breeds another. Cut the burn where the quality is plain or the test cannot be gamed, and only there.

## Orders

([The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) weighs a cheaper test through [Reforming](process_reforming.md) at [the Assay](place_the_assay.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) between where the burn can be cut and where it cannot.)

## Implementation

([The Assay](place_the_assay.md) is where the reform is weighed; [Reforming](process_reforming.md) tries a cheaper proof and finds it holds only where it cannot be faked, so the reformer maps the boundary rather than removing the burn, cutting waste where the quality is observable or the cost is productive.)

## Targets

- [ ] a cheaper proof is sought that cannot be faked
- [ ] a fakeable cheap test collapses to the cheap talk it replaced
- [ ] direct verification spends its own resource, the cost moved not removed
- [ ] where the quality is plain or the cost productive, the burn can be cut, and only there
