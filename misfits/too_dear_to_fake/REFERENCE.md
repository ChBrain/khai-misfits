# Too Dear to Fake: Reference

The research warrant for **Too Dear to Fake**, a staging of the phenomenon the literature calls
**costly signaling** and, in biology, the **handicap principle**: that where one party holds a quality
another cannot observe and both would gain from its being believed, only a signal too expensive to
fake is credible, so the quality is proven by burning real resource that conveys nothing but its own
cost, and everyone who holds the quality must burn or be taken for the one who does not. It is held
with the finding that the honesty of the signal is conditional on a differential cost, since where the
signal is cheap to fake, the quality is directly observable, or the cost falls the same on all, the
signal collapses to cheap talk or to pure waste.

## Line of Work

Staging costly signaling as a **waste-guarantees-honesty structure**: one party holds a quality that
another would pay to know, high ability, good health, real wealth, genuine commitment, and the other
cannot see it directly, so a mere claim proves nothing, since anyone can claim it for free. The only
claim that carries is one too expensive to make falsely: a display, a credential, a handicap that
costs so much to produce that the party without the quality could not profitably match it. So the
holder burns real resource, years of schooling that need not teach, a tail that only weighs the bird
down, a spend that buys nothing but its own visibility, and the receiver believes precisely because
faking it would have cost more than it was worth to the faker (Spence; Zahavi; Grafen). The ordinary
reading is that the credential or the display is earned and the cost is its worth. But the cost is not
the worth: it is the toll that makes the worth believable, a toll paid to no one and producing
nothing, and because the signal only works by being dear, everyone who holds the quality is forced to
pay it or be pooled with those who do not, so the resource burned across the whole field is deadweight
that no one can unilaterally stop spending. The governing law is that **where a hidden quality must be
believed and can be signaled only at a cost that falls harder on those who lack it, the signal is
honest exactly because it is wasteful, so real resource is burned to prove what it cannot otherwise
show, and each holder must burn or be taken for a pretender, the waste the price of the belief**. The
play models the documented mechanics: the separating equilibrium in which education signals ability by
its differential cost rather than its content (Spence), the handicap that is honest because only the
fit can bear it (Zahavi), the formal condition under which such signals are stable (Grafen), and the
conspicuous burning of resource as a proof of standing (Veblen), as the load-bearing vectors of the
structure, and not as a claim that all credentials are empty or all display is waste.

It is a signaling structure no staged misfit holds, and it is carefully distinct from its nearest
neighbours. It is distinct from The Arms Race, positional spending for rank, where each escalation
only cancels against the others and all stand higher for no one's gain: there the prize is relative
position and the spend nets to zero, conveying nothing, whereas here the spend conveys real
information, separating the quality from its absence, and it persists with a single signaler and a
single receiver where there is no rank to contest, the waste buying honesty and not position. It is
distinct from The Done Thing, institutional isomorphism, where organisations adopt a recognised form
for legitimacy rather than fit: there the form is copied for the standing that conformity confers, and
the cost is incidental, whereas here it is the cost itself, differentially borne, that carries the
information, and a cheaper form would defeat the whole purpose. It is distinct from The Market for
Lemons, adverse selection, where hidden quality drives good goods out because buyers cannot tell them
apart: there the information stays hidden and the market unravels toward the bad, whereas here the
information is revealed, at a price, by a signal that sorts the good from the bad, the very unraveling
the burn is spent to prevent. It is distinct from The Leadership Script, fluency mistaken for
competence, where the sound of leadership is read as the substance: there a cheap signal is wrongly
trusted, whereas here the signal is trusted rightly and only because it is dear, an honest proof
bought with real waste rather than a cheap proxy mistaken for the thing.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the one
  who burns resource to signal as a poseur or the one who trusts the costly signal as a snob, because
  the finding it stages is that both act soundly: a holder of the quality who declines to signal is
  pooled with those who lack it and loses, so burning is the rational move, and a receiver who trusts
  only what is too dear to fake is right to, since the cheap claim really is unreliable (Spence;
  Zahavi). The one who cannot afford the signal is not a fraud for failing to send it, and the one who
  would reform the waste acts in good faith. The loss is authored by no one: it is the deadweight that
  honest signaling requires, a resource burned to make a hidden quality believable, spent because the
  quality cannot be shown any cheaper way.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. The waste can be
  cut, but not for free: a cheaper test can replace the costly signal only if it cannot itself be
  faked, and a cheap test that can be gamed collapses back to the cheap talk it replaced; the quality
  can be verified directly, but direct verification spends its own resource and is often what the
  signal existed to avoid; and cutting one costly signal tends to raise another, since the pressure to
  prove a hidden quality does not vanish when its current proof is banned (Spence; Grafen). The
  reckoning is that a hidden quality that must be believed has a price, and where the only credible
  proof is a dear one, the belief is bought with waste that no reform removes for nothing.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here,
  never claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the signaling equilibrium and the
  differential cost of the credential are Spence's, the handicap principle is Zahavi's, the formal
  stability condition is Grafen's, and the reading of conspicuous consumption as a wealth signal is
  Veblen's; the contrast with adverse selection is Akerlof's and is drawn only to mark the line. The
  mechanism is carried as carefully as the finding: that the signal is honest only where its cost
  falls harder on those who lack the quality, that the burn conveys nothing but its own expense, that
  each holder must signal or be pooled with the pretenders, and above all that the effect is
  conditional, since a cheap-to-fake signal, a directly observable quality, or a productive cost each
  changes the case, and not every credential is empty nor every display pure waste. The scope is held
  to hidden qualities proven by differentially costly signals, and not stretched into a claim that all
  education is signaling or all spending is display.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per element, one per load-bearing vector, no filler.

- **The Quality**: carries the hidden trait one party holds and the other would pay to know, ability,
  fitness, wealth, commitment (Spence; Zahavi), unobservable directly and worthless to either unless it
  can be believed.
- **The Signal**: carries the costly display that stands in for the quality (Zahavi; Veblen), the
  credential, the handicap, the conspicuous spend, believed not for what it contains but for what it
  cost.
- **The Differential**: carries the engine of the honesty, the cost that falls harder on the party
  lacking the quality, so the signal separates the two and the low type cannot profitably match it
  (Spence; Grafen), the single condition without which the signal is cheap talk.
- **The Separation**: carries the equilibrium that results, high types signaling and low types not, so
  the receiver reads the signal as truth and the sort holds (Spence), the credibility the burn buys.
- **The Deadweight**: carries the phenomenon whole, the resource burned to prove and producing nothing
  else, and the trap that everyone with the quality must burn or be pooled with the pretenders, so the
  waste is field-wide and no one can stop it alone (Zahavi; Veblen).
- **The Boundary**: carries the condition and the hedge, that the honest signal needs a differential
  cost and a hidden quality, and dissolves where the signal is cheap to fake, the quality is directly
  observable, or the cost is productive rather than pure burn (Spence; Grafen), so the waste is real
  but bounded and not every credential is empty.
