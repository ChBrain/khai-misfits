# Process: Reading

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). Borne at [The Gate](place_the_gate.md), precipitated by [The Reader](persona_the_reader.md) inferring the quality from [The Signal](piece_the_signal.md), in [Burn to Be Believed](plot_burn_to_be_believed.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The wish to know a quality that cannot be seen, faced with a signal that can.

## Direction

From a costly signal toward a trusted judgement of the quality behind it, belief granted to the dear and withheld from the cheap.

## Lever

That the signal could not have been faked without costing the faker more than the prize, so trusting it is sound and trusting a cheap claim is not (Spence).

## Echo

Trust placed in the dear signal, the belief that lets [The Separation](piece_the_separation.md) hold.
