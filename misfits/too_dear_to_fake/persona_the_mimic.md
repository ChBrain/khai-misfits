# Persona: The Mimic

## Taxonomy

[Mimic](position_mimic.md). The one without the quality who would pass as having it, and whose failure to afford the signal is what makes the signal honest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Eager, plausible, claiming the quality it wants believed.
Presents itself as the real thing and would send the same proof the holder sends, sure it belongs on the trusted side of the gate.
No malice in it: it simply wants the prize, and would gladly pay for the signal if the signal were within its reach.

## Action

Attempts [the Signal](piece_the_signal.md) on [Match the Signal](plan_match_the_signal.md), and through [Sorting](process_sorting.md) at [the Forge](place_the_forge.md) meets [the Differential](piece_the_differential.md): the cost that the holder could bear falls harder on it, so the match is a losing spend and the proof does not hold.
Cannot profitably send the signal, because it is priced to exclude exactly the one who lacks the quality, so the attempt costs more than being believed is worth.
Does what a sound pretender does: it tries the cheaper version, finds it does not read, and abandons the climb, priced out rather than caught.

## Shadow

Not a fraud unmasked but a bidder priced out: it is not caught in a lie, it simply cannot afford the truth's proof, and that inability is not its shame but the signal's guarantee.
Because the differential defeats it, its failure is what the reader trusts, so the pretender serves the very sort it loses to, its exclusion the thing that makes every holder's burn believable.
It is the one who wanted in, and the one whose falling short measures the honesty of the whole.

## Tell

Sends a cut-rate version of the proof and it does not carry: the cheaper degree, the thinner display, the borrowed show, and the cheapness reads.
Cannot pass, because the cost that excludes it is the point, so it reveals the differential by failing to meet it, the pretender whose short proof proves the long one honest.
