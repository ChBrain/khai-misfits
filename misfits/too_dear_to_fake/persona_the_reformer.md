# Persona: The Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Reformer](position_reformer.md). The one who sees the burn as waste and would cut it, and who finds the belief cannot be made cheaper than the truth it stands for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Clear-eyed, practical, impatient with waste.
Sees the field burning resource on proofs that produce nothing and means to fix it: a cheaper test, a direct check, an end to the pointless spend.
No cynicism in it: it is right that the burn is deadweight, and its wish to cut it is sound.

## Action

Weighs [Cut the Waste](plan_cut_the_waste.md) through [Reforming](process_reforming.md) at [the Assay](place_the_assay.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): it proposes a cheaper test, tries a direct verification, and finds a fakeable cheap test collapses to cheap talk, a real check spends its own resource, and a banned signal breeds another.
Cannot make the belief free, because the pressure to prove a hidden quality does not vanish when its current proof is banned, so the waste moves rather than ends.
Does what a sound reformer does: it maps exactly where a cheaper proof holds and where it fails, and cuts the burn where the quality is plain or the test cannot be gamed, and only there.

## Shadow

Not naive and not defeated: it is right that the burn is waste, and it is also unable to remove it for nothing, both truths at once.
Because a belief can be no cheaper than the unfakeable proof it rests on, its every shortcut either gets gamed or spends a cost of its own, so the reformer proves, against its own wish, how dearly honest belief is bought.
It is the one who most wants the waste gone, and the one who shows most clearly why it resists.

## Tell

Proposes a cheaper test with confidence, then watches it get gamed, then quietly says where the old burn must stay after all.
Cannot fault its own aim, because the waste is real, so it ends by bounding the reform rather than winning it, the one who named the deadweight and marked exactly how much of it is load-bearing.
