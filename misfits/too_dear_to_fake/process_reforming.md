# Process: Reforming

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). Borne at [The Assay](place_the_assay.md), precipitated by [The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) weighing a cheaper proof, in [A Cheaper Test](plot_a_cheaper_test.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The wish to cut the deadweight, once the burn is seen to prove and produce nothing.

## Direction

From a costly signal toward a cheaper proof or a direct check of the quality, the burn traded for something less wasteful.

## Lever

That a proof both unfakeable and cheap would spare the resource, if one can be found, since the waste is worth cutting wherever the belief can be had for less (Spence; Grafen).

## Echo

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the reform working only where the cheaper test cannot be faked and failing back to the burn where it can.
