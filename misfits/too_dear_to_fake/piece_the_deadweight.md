# Piece: The Deadweight

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). The resource burned across every signaler to prove and produce nothing, and the trap that no one can stop burning alone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Spent at [The Forge](place_the_forge.md), across the whole field of signalers: the years, the risk, the runway, poured into proofs that do nothing but cost, and never recovered, since the burning is the point and the ash is the product (Zahavi; Veblen).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no misfit, only an honest market with a costless signal, which cannot exist: it is the waste that makes the structure a trap and not a tidy sort.
It carries the phenomenon whole. The signal is honest only by being wasteful, and every holder must burn or be pooled with the pretenders, so the deadweight is field-wide and self-locking, spent by all because quitting alone reads as lacking, a loss authored by no one and stoppable by no one.

## Apparent

The natural price of excellence: the effort, the training, the investment that of course the worthy make. From outside it looks like earned cost, and it is cost earning nothing but belief.

## Yearbook

Spent by every [Signaler](position_signaler.md) on [Burn to Prove](plan_burn_to_prove.md), the sum of all the burning [Signaling](process_signaling.md) does, laid bare in [The Waste Is the Proof](plot_the_waste_is_the_proof.md).
It is what [The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) would cut and what [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) marks the limits of, the waste that holds because no one can drop the signal and still be believed.
