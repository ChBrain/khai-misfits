# Position: Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). The seat of the one who sees the burn as waste and would cut it, and who finds the belief cannot be made cheaper than the truth it stands for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The sight of the deadweight, the field-wide resource spent to prove and produce nothing.
The power to propose a cheaper test, a direct check, a productive signal in place of the pure burn.
No way to make the belief free, because every cheaper proof it reaches for either can be faked or spends a cost of its own.

## Orders

Cut the waste: find a proof that costs less and still cannot be faked.
Replace the dear signal with a cheaper test, or verify the quality directly, and measure whether the new proof holds or collapses.
Accept what the reform shows: that the waste is real and worth cutting, and that cutting it is never free, since a fakeable cheap test is no proof at all.

## Loses

The costless correction, always.
Because a cheap test that can be gamed collapses to the cheap talk it replaced, and a real check spends its own resource, and a banned signal breeds another, what it loses is the hope of ending the burn for nothing, met at every turn by a cost that moves rather than vanishes.
It keeps the truth that the burn is waste and loses the power to remove it freely, the reformer right about the cost and unable to spare it.

## Drives

Toward the one who names the deadweight and finds it resists,
proposing a cheaper proof and watching it fake or spend, and by trying maps exactly where the burn can be cut and where it cannot,
so the seat that most wants to end the waste is the seat that proves how dearly honest belief is bought, the reform bounded by the same logic that set the burn.
