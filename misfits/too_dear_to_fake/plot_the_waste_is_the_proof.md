# Plot: The Waste Is the Proof

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Forge](place_the_forge.md), where the burning spreads to everyone who holds the quality.

## Action

[The Deadweight](piece_the_deadweight.md) is the resource spent across every [Signaler](position_signaler.md) to prove and produce nothing, the years and risk and runway poured into proofs that only cost, and each holder must burn or be pooled with the pretenders, so none can stop alone. The scene closes with the belief bought at pure waste, field-wide and unquittable (Veblen).

## Stage

This is not The Arms Race, where each rational escalation cancels against the others for a rank no one gains: here the spend conveys real information, sorting the quality from its absence, and it persists with a single sender and a single receiver where there is no rank to contest, the waste the price of honesty and not a positional externality.

## Tension

Everyone burns to be believed and no one can stop, because to drop the signal alone is read not as thrift but as lacking the quality, so the deadweight is self-locking, a waste each holder must keep spending precisely because it proves them.
