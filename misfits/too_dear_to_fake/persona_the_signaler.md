# Persona: The Signaler

## Taxonomy

[Signaler](position_signaler.md). The one who holds a quality no one can see and burns real resource on a proof too dear for a pretender to match, to be believed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Accomplished, credentialed, plainly the real thing.
Carries a proof of worth that any onlooker respects: the degree, the record, the visible mark of having done what few can, and wears it as earned.
No bluff in it: the quality is real, and the proof is real, so the respect is not misplaced, only bought at a price that does not show.

## Action

Burns on [Burn to Prove](plan_burn_to_prove.md), and through [Signaling](process_signaling.md) makes [the Signal](piece_the_signal.md) at [the Forge](place_the_forge.md), spending years or risk or runway on a display too expensive for a pretender to send.
Cannot be believed without burning, because the cheap word it might say for nothing is the very word a pretender could say too, so only the dear proof will carry.
Does what a holder of a hidden quality soundly does: it pays the cost that excludes the pretender, and the paying is exactly what makes it believed.

## Shadow

Not a poseur for spending: the quality is real, and burning to prove it is the sound move when no cheaper proof would be trusted.
Because the cost and not the content is what convinces, it has spent real resource on a proof that produces nothing but the belief, so its accomplishment is partly a receipt, the merit genuine and the price of showing it pure waste.
It is the one who truly has the worth, and the one who burns most to have it seen, the proof earned by buying nothing else.

## Tell

Names the cost of its proof as if the cost were the merit: the years it took, the price it paid, the difficulty of the climb, offered as the point.
Cannot be caught in a lie, because the quality is real, so it speaks the burn with pride and never quite says that the burn bought only the believing, the receipt worn as the achievement.
