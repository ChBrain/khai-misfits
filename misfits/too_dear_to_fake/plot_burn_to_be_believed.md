# Plot: Burn to Be Believed

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Forge](place_the_forge.md), where the holder pays a price a pretender would not.

## Action

[The Signaler](persona_the_signaler.md) on [Burn to Prove](plan_burn_to_prove.md) through [Signaling](process_signaling.md) makes [The Signal](piece_the_signal.md), spending years or risk or runway on a display too dear to fake, and at [The Gate](place_the_gate.md) [The Reader](persona_the_reader.md), from [the reader's seat](position_reader.md), through [Reading](process_reading.md) believes it, precisely because faking it would have cost more than the prize. The scene closes with the signal read as truth, bought with real burn (Spence; Zahavi).

## Stage

The cost, not the content, carries the truth: what convinces is not what the degree teaches or how the tail looks, but that a pretender could not have afforded it, so a signal made cheap would convince no one and the burn is the whole of the power.

## Tension

The proof is believed exactly because it wasted resource, so the honesty and the waste are the same act: make the signal cheaper and the pretender sends it too, so the only believable proof is the one that costs enough to hurt.
