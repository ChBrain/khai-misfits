# Piece: The Signal

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). The costly display that stands in for the hidden quality, believed not for what it contains but for what it cost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Made at [The Forge](place_the_forge.md) and read at [The Gate](place_the_gate.md): the degree earned over years that need not teach the job, the tail grown at real risk, the spend staged to be seen, a display whose worth to the receiver is its price to the sender (Spence; Zahavi).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the quality stays unprovable, the claim stays free, and the able cannot be told from the rest: the signal is the only bridge across the hidden worth.
It bears the load by its cost and not its content. What convinces is not what the degree teaches or how the tail looks, but that a pretender could not have afforded it, so a signal made cheap would carry nothing at all.

## Apparent

An earned mark of worth: a qualification, a fine display, an achievement respected on sight, the honest badge of a real quality.
