# Position: Reader

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). The seat of the one who must judge a hidden quality, and who can trust only a signal too dear to fake, so its trust is what makes the burn worth spending.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The power to grant or withhold trust, and a quality before it that it cannot see directly.
A sound rule: believe only what is too expensive to fake, and discount the cheap claim, since the cheap claim really is unreliable.
No way to get the quality for free, because the only proof it can trust is one the sender had to burn to make.

## Orders

Trust only the dear signal, and doubt the cheap word.
Read the costly proof as the quality it stands for, because a pretender could not have afforded it, and pass over the one who sends only a claim, because a claim costs nothing and so tells nothing.
Accept what the reading does: that requiring a dear proof is sound, and that the soundness is exactly what obliges every holder to burn.

## Loses

The chance of a costless read, and any signal it lets go cheap.
Because it cannot trust a free claim without being fooled, it must demand the burn, so what it loses is the cheaper world where the quality could be told without waste, given up for the reliability the dear signal buys.
It keeps a trustworthy read and loses the cheap one, the belief bought at the sender's cost because no cheaper belief would hold.

## Drives

Toward the judge who believes the costly proof and by believing it makes the spend pay,
trusting the dear signal because the cheap one cannot be trusted, and by trusting it setting the price every holder must meet,
so the one who reads the proof rightly is the one whose right reading obliges the burn, the belief and the waste held together by the same sound rule.
