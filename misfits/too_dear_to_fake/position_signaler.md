# Position: Signaler

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). The seat of the one who holds a quality that cannot be seen, and who burns real resource on a proof too dear for a pretender to match, to be believed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real quality no one can see: ability, health, wealth, commitment, invisible from outside.
A reason to spend on a signal too expensive to fake, since a free claim carries nothing and only a dear proof will be trusted.
No way to be believed without burning, because the cheap word it could say for nothing is exactly the word a pretender could say too.

## Orders

Burn to prove, or be taken for a pretender.
Spend real resource on a display the one lacking the quality could not afford, and accept that the cost, not the content, is what carries the truth, so a cheaper proof would convince no one.
Do not expect the burn back: it buys the belief and nothing else, and declining to spend it is declining to be believed.

## Loses

The resource burned, which produces nothing but the proof.
Because it cannot both keep the resource and be believed, it must spend, and the spend earns only the belief, so what it loses is the burn itself, a pure cost paid to no one, and it loses it precisely by doing the sound thing.
It keeps its quality proven and loses the resource that proved it, the toll on a worth it could not show any cheaper way.

## Drives

Toward the holder who spends most to be believed and is believed for the spend,
burning because to decline is to be pooled with those who cannot, and by burning holding the sort in place,
so the one with the real quality is the one who pays the real cost, the proof earned exactly because it bought nothing but the proving.
