# Plot: The Unprovable Worth

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Gate](place_the_gate.md), where a hidden quality must be judged and cannot be seen.

## Action

[The Signaler](persona_the_signaler.md) holds [The Quality](piece_the_quality.md), a real worth the reader would pay to know, and [The Reader](persona_the_reader.md) cannot see it, so a bare claim, free to make and free to fake, convinces no one. The scene closes with the worth real and unprovable, a word worth nothing because anyone could say it.

## Stage

A claim costs nothing, so it tells nothing: everyone says they are able, and the saying is free, so no receiver can read the quality from a word, and the holder of a real worth stands no higher than a pretender who claims the same.

## Tension

The worth is real and invisible at once, so the very thing that would win the prize cannot be shown to win it, and the holder gains nothing from a quality that cannot be believed.
