# Plan: Burn to Prove

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the signaler's seat](position_signaler.md), inside [Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Signaler](position_signaler.md)

## Direction

Spend real resource on a proof too dear for a pretender to afford, to be believed. A free claim carries nothing, since anyone could make it, so reach instead for a display whose whole force is its cost: a degree earned over years, a handicap borne at risk, a spend staged to be seen. Do not look for a cheaper proof, because a cheaper one would convince no one, and accept that the burn returns nothing but the belief.

## Orders

([The Signaler](persona_the_signaler.md) makes [the Signal](piece_the_signal.md) through [Signaling](process_signaling.md) at [the Forge](place_the_forge.md), spending the resource that a pretender could not match.)

## Implementation

([The Forge](place_the_forge.md) is where the proof is burned into being; [Signaling](process_signaling.md) pours real resource into a dear display, so that the cost and not the content is what will be trusted, and only later is it plain that the burn bought the belief and produced nothing else.)

## Targets

- [ ] a proof is made too dear for a pretender to afford
- [ ] its cost, not its content, is what will be trusted
- [ ] the resource burned returns nothing but the belief
- [ ] declining to burn pools the holder with the pretenders, so the burn is not optional
