# Piece: The Quality

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). The hidden worth one party holds and another would pay to know, invisible from outside.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held by the signaler and needed at [The Gate](place_the_gate.md), where it cannot be shown: real ability, sound health, true wealth, genuine commitment, a worth the reader would trust if only it could be seen, and it cannot (Spence; Zahavi).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is nothing to prove, and no trap: if the reader could see the quality directly, no costly signal would be needed and none spent.
It is the hiddenness that bears the load. The worth is real and unobservable at once, so a bare claim about it is free to make and therefore proves nothing, and the whole burn exists only because this quality cannot be shown any cheaper way.

## Apparent

A simple fact about the holder: what they are worth, how able, how sound. From outside it looks like something that ought to speak for itself, and it is exactly the thing that cannot.

## Yearbook

Held by [The Signaler](persona_the_signaler.md) and unseen by [The Reader](persona_the_reader.md) at [The Gate](place_the_gate.md).
It is what [The Signal](piece_the_signal.md) stands in for and what [The Differential](piece_the_differential.md) sorts on, the worth the whole production is spent to make believable.
