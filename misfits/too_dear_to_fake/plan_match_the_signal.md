# Plan: Match the Signal

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the mimic's seat](position_mimic.md), inside [Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Mimic](position_mimic.md)

## Direction

Send the same signal the holder sends, to pass as having the quality, and find the cost defeats you. Attempt the proof that earns belief, since the prize is real and you want it, but the burn is priced to fall harder on exactly the one who lacks the quality, so matching it costs you more than being believed is worth. The attempt is not caught as a lie; it is priced out, and the falling short is what keeps the signal honest.

## Orders
