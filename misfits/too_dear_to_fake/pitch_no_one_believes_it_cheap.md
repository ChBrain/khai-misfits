# Pitch: No One Believes It Cheap

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Impressive and earned.
A credential, a display, a proof of worth that any onlooker respects, and the respect is not hollow: the signal really does sort the able from the rest.
Nothing false in it: a receiver who trusts only what is too dear to fake is right to, because the cheap claim really is unreliable.

## Undertow

Under the earned proof, a resource burned to nothing but the proving.
The cost is not the worth: it is the toll that makes the worth believable, paid to no one and producing nothing, so the degree that need not teach and the tail that only weighs are burned to be believed and for no other end (Spence; Zahavi).
No poseur and no snob: the holder burns because to decline is to be pooled with the pretenders, and the receiver trusts the dear signal because the cheap one cannot be trusted, and both are right.

## Nerve

The plainness of an earned proof, and the catch that the earning is pure waste:
that the signal is honest exactly because it is costly, so removing the burn would let the pretender send it too, the belief sought and the waste that secures it the one cost seen twice,
and everyone who holds the quality must burn or be taken for lacking it, so the resource spent across the field buys nothing but the belief and no single hand can stop it.

## Echo

After the run: the office full of degrees the jobs never needed, the bird slowed by the tail that won it a mate, the runway burned on a party to look funded, a resource spent to prove and not to do, and a loss authored by no one (Veblen).
And the remedies, each at a cost and none certain: a cheaper test that cannot be faked, a direct check of the quality, a signal that is also productive, but a fakeable cheap test collapses to cheap talk, verification spends what the signal spared, and a banned proof breeds another (Spence; Grafen).
And the bound held in view, because the honesty is conditional and not a law: it needs a cost that falls harder on the pretender and a quality that cannot be seen, and lifts where the signal is cheap to fake, the quality is plain, or the cost is productive, so the whole of it is that to be believed you must burn.
