# Position: Mimic

## Taxonomy

[Too Dear to Fake](play_too_dear_to_fake.md). The seat of the one without the quality who would pass as having it, and whose failure to afford the signal is what makes the signal honest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The same wish to be believed, and none of the quality to back it.
No affordable way to send the signal, since the cost that the holder can bear falls harder on it.
A clear view of the prize and no path to it that pays, because matching the proof would cost it more than being believed is worth.

## Orders

Match the signal if you can, and find you cannot.
Attempt the same proof the holder sends, and meet the differential that defeats it, since the burn is priced to exclude exactly you.
Accept what the attempt shows: that the signal is honest not by catching liars but by pricing them out, and that your falling short is what the receiver trusts.

## Loses

The attempt, and the resource spent attempting it.
Because the cost falls harder on it, matching the signal is a losing spend, so what it loses is the try itself, and its inability to profit from the proof is precisely the thing that keeps the proof honest.
It keeps nothing and proves everything, the pretender whose failure is the signal's guarantee.

## Drives

Toward the one whose falling short measures the differential,
attempting the proof and abandoning it because it cannot pay, and by abandoning it confirming the sort,
so the seat that cannot afford the signal is the seat that makes the signal worth trusting, honest by the very cost that shuts it out.
