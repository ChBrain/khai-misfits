# Pitch: It Held Last Time

## Taxonomy

[Dry Enough to Build On](play_dry_enough_to_build_on.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Level and unhurried. Good engineering, good ground, ordinary families over eighty years.
The satisfaction of a thing that works: dry springs where there used to be mud, a plain that has become useful, a decision to build there that anyone would call sound and that the record supports.

## Undertow

Under the soundness, a quiet that is doing the damage.
Because the wall stops the small floods, and the small floods were the only thing keeping the memory alive, the safety and the forgetting arrive as one event and cannot be separated. Nobody notices a memory going. There is no moment where it goes.

## Nerve

The particular unease of a place that feels safer every year for the same reason it is becoming more exposed.

## Echo

Afterward: a plain full of houses, a wall that did everything asked of it, a rating that was published all along, and a flood that took what would not have been standing there.
