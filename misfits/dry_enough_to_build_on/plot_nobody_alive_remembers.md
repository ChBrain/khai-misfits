# Plot: Nobody Alive Remembers

## Taxonomy

[Dry Enough to Build On](play_dry_enough_to_build_on.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Forty dry springs in a row, and [the old mark](piece_the_old_mark.md) still chest high on the gable of the one house that predates the wall.

## Action

[Going quiet](process_going_quiet.md): [The Last Flood](persona_the_last_flood.md), holding [Last Flood](position_last_flood.md), recedes from the practice, then from the reason, then from the last people who held it.

## Stage

No one stages this either. There is no meeting at which the caution is dropped, no vote, and no moment: the habit of leaving the low ground alone simply stops having anyone who keeps it.

## Tension

The record survives and does not work.
The mark is legible, correctly placed, and walked past by hundreds of people, and it means nothing, because what made it mean something was the practice of being flooded, and that practice was stopped on purpose and for good reasons. So the forgetting cannot be fixed by better records: it was never a records problem, and the thing that would refresh it is the thing the wall exists to prevent.
