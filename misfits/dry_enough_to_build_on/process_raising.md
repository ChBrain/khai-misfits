# Process: Raising

## Taxonomy

[Dry Enough to Build On](play_dry_enough_to_build_on.md). Building the defence, correctly, against a real hazard.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A river that takes the low ground every few years, and a case for stopping it.
[The One Who Raised It](persona_the_one_who_raised_it.md), holding [One Who Raised It](position_one_who_raised_it.md), at [the crest](place_the_crest.md).

## Direction

Toward the water, and only the water.
Sized to [the design flood](piece_the_design_flood.md), built to that height and no further, with the limit published.

## Lever

A century of gauge readings, and the competence to turn them into a height.

## Echo

Small floods stopped, springs dry, a plain measurably safer than it was, and a promise kept exactly as worded.
