# Plot: The Wall Goes Up

## Taxonomy

[Dry Enough to Build On](play_dry_enough_to_build_on.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A river that has taken the low ground every few years for as long as the gauge has been read, and a case for stopping it that nobody sensible disputes.

## Action

[Raising](process_raising.md): [The One Who Raised It](persona_the_one_who_raised_it.md), holding [One Who Raised It](position_one_who_raised_it.md), sizes the work to [the design flood](piece_the_design_flood.md) and builds [the crest](place_the_crest.md) to that height.

## Stage

[The One Who Raised It](persona_the_one_who_raised_it.md), having tabled [Hold the Water Back](plan_hold_the_water_back.md), publishes the limit in the same breath as the promise, and delivers the promise exactly.

## Tension

The wall works, and working is the whole of the problem.
There is no flaw to find: the rating is honest, the construction is sound, the small floods stop, and the plain is safer than it was by any measure anyone can take. What has also just happened, and is on no drawing, is that the one mechanism which kept people off the low ground has been removed, and removing it was the point.
