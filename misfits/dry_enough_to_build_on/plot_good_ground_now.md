# Plot: Good Ground Now

## Taxonomy

[Dry Enough to Build On](play_dry_enough_to_build_on.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Level, serviced land beside a river, dry every spring since the wall went up, priced as though it were still the old low ground.

## Action

[Filling in](process_filling_in.md): [The One Who Builds There](persona_the_one_who_builds_there.md), holding [One Who Builds There](position_one_who_builds_there.md), checks the maps and the rating, finds the ground genuinely safer, and buys.

## Stage

[The One Who Builds There](persona_the_one_who_builds_there.md), having tabled [Build Where It Is Dry](plan_build_where_it_is_dry.md), takes [the deed](piece_the_deed.md), is lent to by a lender reading the same true fact, and builds under a permission granted on the same grounds.

## Tension

The check was done properly and returned the right answer.
This is not a gamble and not a lapse: the plain really is drier, the improvement really was made, and building on ground that has become safer is precisely what an improvement is for. The only thing the check cannot ask is what happens to the answer when ten thousand people run it, correctly, on the same plain.
