# Persona: The Bigger Water

## Taxonomy

[Bigger Water](position_bigger_water.md). The flood beyond the rating, which the rating always said would come.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, in its own terms. Larger than the design flood, and no rarer than the design flood said it would be.
Announced in advance, in writing, for the whole life of the wall, in a number nobody read as a limit.

## Action

Goes where water goes: over, or through, or around, into the lowest ground available.
Finds a town on it.

## Shadow

Cannot be blamed, and takes the blame anyway, since the event is the only visible thing in the whole sequence.
The loss is charged to the flood being exceptional when the flood is exactly what was forecast, and the eighty quiet years that put a town in its path are not read as a cause at all, because nothing happened in them.

## Tell

Arrives once, and is called unprecedented by people who have the precedent written down.
