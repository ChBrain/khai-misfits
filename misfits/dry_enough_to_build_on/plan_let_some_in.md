# Plan: Let Some In

## Taxonomy

A **correction**: the intent of [The One Who Raised It](persona_the_one_who_raised_it.md), inside [Dry Enough to Build On](play_dry_enough_to_build_on.md), once the structure is understood.

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Raised It](persona_the_one_who_raised_it.md)

## Direction

Stop removing the small floods. Give the river room, set the defences back from the channel, buy out and move what stands in the way, and let the plain flood often enough that it stays cheap, stays empty, and stays remembered.

## Orders

(The One Who Raised It moves the line back rather than up, accepts inundation of ground that is now dry, funds the relocation of what is already there, and defends the decision in the years when no flood comes to justify it.)

## Implementation

([The Dry Side](place_the_dry_side.md), given back in part; the [going quiet](process_going_quiet.md) process reversed by recurrence rather than by record; [the old mark](piece_the_old_mark.md) made legible again by water rather than by explanation.)

## Targets

- [ ] the defence is set back rather than raised
- [ ] the plain is allowed to flood at a frequency that sustains the memory
- [ ] what already stands in the way is bought out and moved, and paid for now
- [ ] the price is accepted: visible damage today against a loss that, if this works, nobody will ever see
