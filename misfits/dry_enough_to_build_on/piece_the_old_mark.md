# Piece: The Old Mark

## Taxonomy

[The Dry Side](place_the_dry_side.md). A line cut on a gable at the height the water reached, kept and unread.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Chest high on the one house that predates the wall, on [the dry side](place_the_dry_side.md), where hundreds of people walk past it.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the forgetting could be blamed on a lost record, and the misfit would dissolve into a filing problem.
It is the piece that shows the record survives and does not work: the information is present, legible, and physically located at the right height, and it means nothing to anyone, because what made it meaningful was the practice of being flooded and that practice has been stopped.

## Apparent

A chiselled line and a year, weathered but plain.
Read by visitors as decoration, and by residents not at all, since a mark of water on a street that has never had water on it is a curiosity.

## Yearbook

Cut by someone who expected it to be needed.
Meaningful to everyone for thirty years, then to the older half, then to nobody, without ever being removed, disputed, or contradicted.
