# Process: Going Quiet

## Taxonomy

[Dry Enough to Build On](play_dry_enough_to_build_on.md). The decay of a memory that only recurrence can refresh.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Nothing happening, for long enough.
[The Last Flood](persona_the_last_flood.md), holding [Last Flood](position_last_flood.md), everywhere on [the dry side](place_the_dry_side.md).

## Direction

Downward, without an event.
The habit of leaving the low ground alone loses its reason, then its practice, then its holders, in that order and over about a generation.

## Lever

The wall working. There is no other lever: a memory of flooding is sustained by flooding, so removing the small floods removes the mechanism.

## Echo

[The Old Mark](piece_the_old_mark.md) still legible on the gable and read by nobody, and a plain with no practice on it that says what it is.
