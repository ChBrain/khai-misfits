# Persona: The Last Flood

## Taxonomy

[Last Flood](position_last_flood.md). The memory that only floods refresh, receding at the rate the wall succeeds.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Present at first, in everyone: a year people name, a line on a wall, a habit of keeping the low ground for grazing that nobody has to justify.
Fainter each decade. Held last by people who were children then, and by nothing else once they are gone.

## Action

Fades, without an event to mark the fading.
Takes with it the reason the low ground was cheap, the reason the old houses stand where they stand, and the unspoken rule that made [the old mark](piece_the_old_mark.md) legible.

## Shadow

Cannot be sustained by a record, only by recurrence.
Where the river keeps coming, this persona is renewed and a place stays adapted without anyone deciding to be. Where a wall stops the small floods, there is nothing left to renew it, so the wall's success and this persona's disappearance are one event and not two.

## Tell

Is not spoken. Its absence is the whole of its presence: nobody says the flood is forgotten, because there is nobody left who would notice that it had been.
