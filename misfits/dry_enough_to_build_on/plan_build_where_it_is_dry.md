# Plan: Build Where It Is Dry

## Taxonomy

A **personal scheme**: the intent of [The One Who Builds There](persona_the_one_who_builds_there.md), inside [Dry Enough to Build On](play_dry_enough_to_build_on.md).

## Direction

Put a house on the best ground available, having checked that it is. Read the maps, ask about the wall, confirm the plain is drier than it was, and act on a real improvement the way an improvement is meant to be acted on.

## Orders

(The One Who Builds There checks the maps and the rating, buys on [the dry side](place_the_dry_side.md), takes [the deed](piece_the_deed.md), borrows against it, and builds under a permission granted on the same true facts.)
