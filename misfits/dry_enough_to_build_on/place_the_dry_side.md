# Place: The Dry Side

## Taxonomy

## Shown

Level land, close to everything, serviced and cheap for what it is.
Dry every spring for as long as anyone can remember, which is as long as the wall has stood.
No standing water, no watermarks on anything built since, no habit anywhere of leaving it alone.

## Offers

Everything it appears to offer, honestly: ground that is genuinely safer than it was, at a price that has not yet caught up with the improvement.
No obstacle to anyone who checks, because checking confirms it.

## Withheld

Any sign of what it is for.
Not concealed: erased. The plain is a floodplain and looks like a field, because the one thing that ever marked it as a floodplain was being flooded, and that has been stopped on purpose.
