# Persona: The One Who Raised It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Raised It](position_one_who_raised_it.md). Builds a real defence against a real hazard, and builds it well.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent and unromantic. Surveys the river, reads a century of gauge records, sizes the work against the flood that comes once in a hundred years, and publishes the number.
Promises exactly what the wall will do and nothing beyond it.

## Action

Raises the crest to the rating and no higher, because higher costs more than the case supports.
Writes [the design flood](piece_the_design_flood.md) into the record, where anyone can read what the wall is and is not for.
Watches the small floods stop, which is the wall working.

## Shadow

Cannot see the town as one of the wall's outputs.
The brief was the water and the wall answers it exactly: nothing in the survey, the rating or the sign-off has a place to record what will come to stand behind it, so the one effect that will decide the eventual loss is the one effect the work was never asked to estimate.

## Tell

Says "it holds to the hundred year flood", which is true, precise, and heard by everyone downstream as "it holds".
