# Position: Last Flood

## Taxonomy

[Dry Enough to Build On](play_dry_enough_to_build_on.md). The seat that holds the caution, and can only be refilled by the thing the wall prevents.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A year people name, a line on a wall, a habit of leaving the low ground alone that nobody has to argue for.
No archive that can do its work, because the caution it carries is practice rather than information.

## Orders

Keep the low ground cheap.
Keep the old houses where the old houses are.
Make [the old mark](piece_the_old_mark.md) mean something to whoever walks past it.

## Loses

Everything, quietly, at the exact rate the wall succeeds.
A memory of flooding is refreshed by flooding, so the removal of the small floods removes the only mechanism that sustains this seat, and it empties without an event, a decision, or anyone noticing that it has.

## Drives

Toward an adaptation that costs nothing and needs no enforcement while the river keeps arriving, and out of existence the moment the river is stopped, so the protection and the forgetting are one act seen from two sides.
