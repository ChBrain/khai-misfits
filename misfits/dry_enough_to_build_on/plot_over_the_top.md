# Plot: Over the Top

## Taxonomy

[Dry Enough to Build On](play_dry_enough_to_build_on.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A flood larger than [the design flood](piece_the_design_flood.md), arriving at about the frequency the rating stated it would.

## Action

[Overtopping](process_overtopping.md): [The Bigger Water](persona_the_bigger_water.md), holding [Bigger Water](position_bigger_water.md), passes [the crest](place_the_crest.md) and goes to the lowest ground available.

## Stage

The water takes a town. On the plain as it stood eighty years earlier, the same flood would have taken a field.

## Tension

The event that everyone will explain is the only one that was ever announced.
The rating said this would happen, in writing, from the first day, and the loss is charged to the flood being exceptional rather than to the eighty quiet years that put a town in its path, because nothing happened in those years and nothing is hard to blame. The wall did not fail. It did exactly what it said, up to exactly where it said, and the damage is the difference between a field and a town.
