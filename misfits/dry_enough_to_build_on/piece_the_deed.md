# Piece: The Deed

## Taxonomy

[The Dry Side](place_the_dry_side.md). Title to land that is genuinely safer, at a price that says so.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held by [the one who builds there](persona_the_one_who_builds_there.md), lodged with a lender, recorded against a parcel on [the dry side](place_the_dry_side.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the settlement is a choice that could be reversed, and it is not.
It is the piece that makes the exposure permanent: a deed is borrowed against, built on, inherited and insured, so what the wall invited becomes a thing with owners, and a plain with owners cannot be given back to the river the way a driver can slow down.

## Apparent

An ordinary title to an ordinary parcel. Checked, financed, permitted, registered.
Nothing on its face distinguishes it from a deed to land that was never a floodplain.

## Yearbook

Worth little when the ground was wet, and the price rose as the springs stayed dry, which is the improvement being capitalised exactly as it should be.
Multiplied across the plain into a total that appears on no single document.
