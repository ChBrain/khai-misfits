## Stage

## Tension

Everything the correction buys is invisible and everything it costs is not.
The damage is paid now, in flooded ground, moved households and land given back, against a loss that arrives after the payers are gone and that, if the plan works, nobody will ever witness. And the alternative is worse in a way that looks better: raising the wall also works, and buys a drier plain, a longer quiet, a fainter memory and more standing behind it, so the instrument that most plainly answers the complaint is the one that deepens the structure underneath it.
