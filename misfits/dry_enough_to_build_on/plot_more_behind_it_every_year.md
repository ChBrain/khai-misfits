# Plot: More Behind It Every Year

## Taxonomy

[Dry Enough to Build On](play_dry_enough_to_build_on.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An ordinary decade. Permissions granted, mortgages written, services extended, all on ground that is dry and has been dry for as long as the applicants can remember.

## Action

[Filling in](process_filling_in.md), repeated: [The One Who Builds There](persona_the_one_who_builds_there.md), holding [One Who Builds There](position_one_who_builds_there.md), occupied ten thousand times over, each occupant checking and each check passing.

## Stage

Across the contiguous United States, building levees is associated with floodplain urban expansion accelerating by around sixty-two per cent, which is this plot happening at scale and in public.

## Tension

The exposure is accumulating in the open and appears on no document.
Every permission is sound, every valuation is honest, every mortgage is prudently written against ground that has genuinely improved. What is missing is not diligence but an aggregate: no party in the sequence is asked what is now standing on the plain, because no party's decision requires the answer, and the total is therefore held by nobody at the exact moment it becomes the only number that matters.
