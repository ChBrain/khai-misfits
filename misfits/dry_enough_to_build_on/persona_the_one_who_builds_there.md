# Persona: The One Who Builds There

## Taxonomy

[One Who Builds There](position_one_who_builds_there.md). Reads a real improvement correctly and acts on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful. Not a gambler and not a fool: checks the maps, asks about the wall, reads the rating, and finds that the plain is drier than it was, which is true.
Wants level ground close to work, near services, at a price that reflects what the land used to be.

## Action

Buys, borrows against, and builds on [the dry side](place_the_dry_side.md), holding [the deed](piece_the_deed.md) to land that is genuinely safer than it was.
Is lent to by a lender reading the same fact, and permitted by a planner reading it too.

## Shadow

Cannot see the total, because the total is nobody's line item.
Each decision is individually correct and the sum of them is a town on a floodplain, arrived at without a single unsound step. Across the contiguous United States, building levees is associated with floodplain urban expansion accelerating by around sixty-two per cent, which is this persona repeated, sensibly, at scale.

## Tell

Says "there has never been a flood here", meaning it as evidence, and meaning something that is true and has been true for exactly as long as the wall has stood.
