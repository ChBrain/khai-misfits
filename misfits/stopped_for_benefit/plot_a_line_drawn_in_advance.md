# Plot: A Line Drawn in Advance

## Taxonomy

[Stopped for Benefit](play_stopped_for_benefit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The protocol written before the first participant is enrolled, with the looks scheduled and the line computed.

## Action

[The Stopping Boundary](piece_the_stopping_boundary.md) is fixed in the charter and will be applied at each [interim look](persona_the_interim_look.md) without discretion.

## Stage

Repeated looking will not inflate the chance of a false positive. The boundary succeeds completely at the thing it was built for.

## Tension

The rigour is real, aimed at this exact problem, and insufficient to it.
Boundaries were developed to stop investigators peeking until they saw what they wanted, a genuine abuse that they genuinely prevent. What no boundary can do is make the estimate at a crossing unbiased, because any rule that stops on a large observed effect will stop preferentially when the observation was helped by chance: the rules govern how often you stop wrongly, not how big the number is when you stop rightly.
