# Piece: The Stopping Boundary

## Taxonomy

[The Closed Session](place_the_closed_session.md). The pre-specified line, rigorous and insufficient.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Written into the charter before the first participant is enrolled, and applied at each [interim look](persona_the_interim_look.md) in [the closed session](place_the_closed_session.md).

## Load Bearing

It is the discipline's answer to this exact problem, and it does not solve it.
The boundary is designed so that repeated looking does not inflate the chance of a false positive, and it succeeds at that: what it cannot do is make the estimate at a crossing unbiased, because any rule that stops on a large observed effect will stop preferentially when the observed effect has been helped by chance, and the measured overestimate is independent of whether formal rules were used.

## Apparent

Principled, computed in advance, published in the protocol, and applied without discretion.
Exactly what methodological rigour looks like, because it is.

## Yearbook

Developed precisely to stop investigators peeking until they saw what they wanted, a real abuse that it genuinely prevents.
Controls the probability of stopping wrongly, and not the size of what is reported when stopping is right.
