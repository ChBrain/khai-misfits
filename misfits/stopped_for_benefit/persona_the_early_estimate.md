# Persona: The Early Estimate

## Taxonomy

[Early Estimate](position_early_estimate.md). The number as it stood at the stop, and as it will stand forever.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A relative risk with a confidence interval, computed correctly from the data that existed at the moment the boundary was crossed.
Honest arithmetic on a truncated sample.

## Action

Is published, cited, meta-analysed and written into guidance.
Is never revised, because there is nothing further to revise it with.

## Shadow

Is the final answer to a question that was never finished being asked.
Across truncated trials the relative benefit is overstated by about thirty per cent on average, worst where the events are fewest, and once the trial stops the control arm is offered the treatment, so the comparison cannot be completed by this trial or by anybody looking back at it: this is not a provisional figure awaiting refinement, it is a measurement interrupted permanently.

## Tell

Is arithmetically correct, professionally reported, and larger than the truth by an amount nobody can now determine.
