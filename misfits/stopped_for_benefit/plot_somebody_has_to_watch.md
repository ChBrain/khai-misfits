# Plot: Somebody Has to Watch

## Taxonomy

[Stopped for Benefit](play_stopped_for_benefit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A trial with people in it, half of them receiving the other thing, running for years.

## Action

[The Board That Must Stop It](persona_the_board_that_must_stop_it.md), holding [Board That Must Stop It](position_board_that_must_stop_it.md), is constituted independently and takes the only unblinded view, in [the closed session](place_the_closed_session.md).

## Stage

Interim monitoring is an obligation rather than a courtesy: a trial that never looks cannot protect anybody inside it.

## Tension

The instrument that protects the participants is the same instrument that will choose when the number is taken.
Nobody proposes running blind to accumulating harm or benefit, and nobody should: the alternative is leaving people on an inferior arm for the convenience of a clean result, which is the abuse monitoring boards exist to prevent. So the looking is not optional and is not a compromise, and every consequence that follows in this play follows from doing it properly.
