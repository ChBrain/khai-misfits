# Plot: What It Costs to Be Sure

## Taxonomy

[Stopped for Benefit](play_stopped_for_benefit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The problem understood, and the corrections on the table: require more events before acting on a crossing, shrink the published figure, or keep following participants after the stop.

## Action

[Wait for the events](plan_wait_for_the_events.md): [The Board That Must Stop It](persona_the_board_that_must_stop_it.md) examines each in turn and finds every one of them paid for by somebody.

## Stage

Waiting means leaving people on the arm the board believes is worse, deliberately, for longer.

## Tension

The measurement cannot be completed, because completing it is the thing that was refused for the best reason there is.
Shrinking the estimate means publishing a number nobody measured while clinicians go on using the one that was. Following participants after the stop is confounded from the moment the control arm is offered the treatment, which follows automatically from the same reasoning that justified stopping. And [The One Treated After](persona_the_one_treated_after.md), who bears all of it, had no representative in the room, no consent form and no board watching over them.
