# Place: The Closed Session

## Taxonomy

[Stopped for Benefit](play_stopped_for_benefit.md). Where the only unblinded view exists, and the decision is made.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A room with the independent members, an unblinded statistician, and tables nobody else in the trial is allowed to see.
A charter setting out in advance what will be looked at and what will be done about it.

## Holds

[The Stopping Boundary](piece_the_stopping_boundary.md), computed before enrolment began.
[The Event Count](piece_the_event_count.md), which is small, and which decides how much of what is on the table is noise.

## Offers

Genuine independence, genuine expertise, and a decision made by people with nothing to gain from either answer.
Protection for the participants that exists nowhere else in the structure.

## Withheld

Any permitted route to the consideration that matters.
The room can see that an estimate at a crossing is likely inflated, and cannot act on it, because acting on it means continuing to allocate people by lot against the trial's own belief: so the one place in the play equipped to understand the problem is the place least free to respond to it.
