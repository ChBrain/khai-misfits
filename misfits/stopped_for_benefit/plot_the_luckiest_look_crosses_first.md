# Plot: The Luckiest Look Crosses First

## Taxonomy

[Stopped for Benefit](play_stopped_for_benefit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Few events on the table, and an estimate that wanders widely around whatever the truth is.

## Action

[Looking early](process_looking_early.md): [The Interim Look](persona_the_interim_look.md), holding [Interim Look](position_interim_look.md), computes from [the event count](piece_the_event_count.md) and compares, again and again, until one crossing occurs.

## Stage

The looks at which the treatment appears dramatically better are disproportionately the looks at which chance ran its way.

## Tension

The trial does not stop when the drug is good, it stops when the drug is good and the run has been kind, and at that instant the two cannot be separated.
Nothing available in the room distinguishes a real effect of that size from a smaller effect having a favourable spell, because both produce the identical table. And the smaller the event count the cheaper a dramatic separation is for chance to manufacture, so the trials that stop soonest and look most impressive are precisely the ones whose numbers are worth least.
