# Position: Interim Look

## Taxonomy

[Stopped for Benefit](play_stopped_for_benefit.md). The seat that protects and chooses in one act.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The accumulating data at a fixed point, and a boundary to compare it against.
Few events, and an estimate that wanders widely around the truth.

## Orders

Look.
Compare, and repeat until something crosses.

## Loses

The separation between a good treatment and a kind run of events.
Early estimates are noisy, so the looks at which the treatment appears dramatically better are disproportionately those where chance ran its way: the moment of stopping is therefore selected on luck as well as on merit, and no examination of the data at that instant can tell which contributed what.

## Drives

Toward a stop that is necessary for the participants and chosen by the noise.
