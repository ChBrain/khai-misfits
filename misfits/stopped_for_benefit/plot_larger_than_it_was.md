# Plot: Larger Than It Was

## Taxonomy

[Stopped for Benefit](play_stopped_for_benefit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Truncated trials assembled and compared against trials that ran to completion on the same questions.

## Action

[Carrying the number forward](process_carrying_the_number_forward.md): [The Early Estimate](persona_the_early_estimate.md), holding [Early Estimate](position_early_estimate.md), travels through citation and meta-analysis into [the prescribing guideline](place_the_prescribing_guideline.md).

## Stage

Trials stopped early overstate the relative benefit by around thirty per cent on average, the difference is independent of whether formal stopping rules were used, and it is largest where the studies are smallest.

## Tension

Every step of the transmission is accurate, so nothing along the chain can catch it.
The paper reports what the data showed, discloses that the trial was stopped early, and computes the interval honestly. The guideline committee quotes it correctly and applies its methods properly. The number the trial would have produced had it finished does not exist anywhere and cannot be obtained, so there is no better source being overlooked: the inflation travels intact because everybody handling it is doing their job well.
