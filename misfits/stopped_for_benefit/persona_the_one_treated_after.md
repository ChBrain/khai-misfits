# Persona: The One Treated After

## Taxonomy

[One Treated After](position_one_treated_after.md). The patient outside the trial, treated on the inflated number.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Somebody who will be prescribed this treatment in three years, on the strength of a guideline, in a clinic that has never heard of the monitoring board.
Not a participant, not consented, and not counted anywhere.

## Action

Receives the treatment, and whatever benefit it actually confers.
Is told a figure describing a larger one.

## Shadow

Bears the cost of a protection extended to somebody else, and has no standing anywhere in the process.
No board was constituted to watch over this persona, no consent form mentions them, and the trade that was made on their behalf, a better outcome for the enrolled against a worse estimate for the treated, was made by people who were right to make it: so the loss is real, distributed and owned by nobody at all.

## Tell

Is the reason the trial was run, and the only party with no representative in the room where it ended.
