# Piece: The Crossover

## Taxonomy

[The Prescribing Guideline](place_the_prescribing_guideline.md). The offer to the control arm, which closes the question permanently.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Made immediately after the stop, before anything reaches [the prescribing guideline](place_the_prescribing_guideline.md): the participants on the control arm are unblinded and offered the treatment.

## Load Bearing

It is the act that makes the estimate final, and it is not optional.
Having concluded that one arm is better, the trial cannot leave its own participants on the other, so everybody is offered the treatment: and from that moment there is no untreated comparison group anywhere, so the measurement cannot be completed by this trial, resumed later, or reconstructed by anybody looking back.

## Apparent

The obviously right thing to do, done promptly, and the thing every participant would want.
Ethically unarguable, and welcomed by everybody involved.

## Yearbook

Follows automatically from the stop, since the reasoning that justifies stopping justifies treating the controls.
Converts a provisional figure into a permanent one, without anybody intending to fix a number.
