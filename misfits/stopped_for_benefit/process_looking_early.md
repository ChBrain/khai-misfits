# Process: Looking Early

## Taxonomy

[Stopped for Benefit](play_stopped_for_benefit.md). Scheduled monitoring, which is obligatory and noisy.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A trial with people in it, half receiving the other thing, and a duty not to leave them there uninspected.
[The Interim Look](persona_the_interim_look.md), holding [Interim Look](position_interim_look.md), in [the closed session](place_the_closed_session.md).

## Direction

Toward an estimate computed on [the event count](piece_the_event_count.md) as it stands, which is small.
Toward a comparison against [the stopping boundary](piece_the_stopping_boundary.md), repeated at intervals.

## Lever

Few events, which make the estimate wander widely, so a dramatic separation is cheap for chance to produce.

## Echo

An instrument that protects the participants and, in the same motion, decides which moment the number will be taken at.
