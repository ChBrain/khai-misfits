# Pitch: Nobody Could Let It Run

## Taxonomy

[Stopped for Benefit](play_stopped_for_benefit.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Rigorous and humane. A protocol written before enrolment, a boundary computed properly, an independent board meeting in closed session, and an obligation to the people already in the trial that nobody would want relaxed.
Everything pre-specified, everything defensible, everything done as the discipline requires.

## Undertow

Under the rigour, a stopping time chosen by the data, and early data that is mostly noise.
And a comparison that ends the moment it is stopped, because the control arm is offered the drug and nobody can ever finish the measurement.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a number spoiled by the one act in the whole trial that nobody could have refused to perform.

## Echo

Afterward: a relative benefit overstated by around thirty per cent on average, unchanged by whether formal stopping rules were used, worst where the events were fewest, and printed in a guideline that will treat people who were never in the room.
