# Process: Crossing the Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Stopped for Benefit](play_stopped_for_benefit.md). The moment selected, partly on merit and partly on luck.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An estimate that has wandered far enough to exceed the pre-specified line.
[The Stopping Boundary](piece_the_stopping_boundary.md), applied without discretion, as designed.

## Direction

Toward a recommendation to stop, which the board is constituted to make.
Toward a crossing that is disproportionately drawn from the runs where chance favoured the treatment.

## Lever

Selection on an observed extreme, which no boundary can remove, since any rule that stops on a large observed effect stops preferentially when the observation was helped.

## Echo

An overestimate that is independent of whether formal stopping rules were used, because the rules govern the false positive rate rather than the reported magnitude.
