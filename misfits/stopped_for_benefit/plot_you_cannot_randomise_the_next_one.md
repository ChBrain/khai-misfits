# Plot: You Cannot Randomise the Next One

## Taxonomy

[Stopped for Benefit](play_stopped_for_benefit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The boundary crossed, and somebody due to be enrolled tomorrow with an envelope that would decide their treatment by lot.

## Action

[Stopping it](process_stopping_it.md): [The One Randomised Next](persona_the_one_randomised_next.md), holding [One Randomised Next](position_one_randomised_next.md), makes the recommendation compulsory, and the trial halts, unblinds, and offers [the crossover](piece_the_crossover.md) to the control arm.

## Stage

The participants are protected completely and correctly, which is what everybody would want.

## Tension

The one consideration that would argue for continuing is the one nobody is permitted to act on.
The premise of randomisation is genuine uncertainty, and the moment the board believes one arm is better that premise has failed for this person specifically. A board declining to stop because the effect size would be tidier later is a board deliberately allocating somebody by lot against its own conviction, which is indefensible. So the body best equipped to understand the statistical damage is the body least free to avoid it.
