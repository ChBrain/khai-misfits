# Process: Carrying the Number Forward

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Initiated by

Publication of [the early estimate](persona_the_early_estimate.md), correctly computed and correctly reported.
Committees weighing the literature and applying their methods properly.

## Direction

Toward citation, meta-analysis and [the prescribing guideline](place_the_prescribing_guideline.md).

## Lever

Accuracy of transmission: every step quotes the source correctly, so nothing along the chain introduces or removes the inflation.

## Echo
