# Persona: The Board That Must Stop It

## Taxonomy

[Board That Must Stop It](position_board_that_must_stop_it.md). The monitoring committee, holding the only unblinded view.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Independent clinicians and statisticians meeting in closed session, seeing what nobody running the trial is allowed to see.
Constituted precisely so that participants are not left on an inferior arm for the convenience of a clean result.

## Action

Reviews each [interim look](persona_the_interim_look.md) against the boundary.
Recommends stopping when the boundary is crossed, which is what it exists to do.

## Shadow

Holds a duty that points one way and knows what the stop costs.
Its members understand the statistics perfectly well, including that an estimate at a crossing is likely to be inflated, and none of that alters what they must do: continuing means allocating the next participant by lot to a treatment they now believe is worse, and no board can defend that on the ground that the effect size will be tidier later.

## Tell

Is the only body in the trial competent to see the problem and the one body obliged to act against it.
