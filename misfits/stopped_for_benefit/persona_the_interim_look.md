# Persona: The Interim Look

## Taxonomy

[Interim Look](position_interim_look.md). The scheduled peek, which selects a moment out of noise.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A pre-planned analysis of the accumulating data at a fixed point: so many events, so many participants, a boundary to be compared against.
Necessary, because a trial that never looks cannot protect anybody in it.

## Action

Computes the current estimate and holds it against [the stopping boundary](piece_the_stopping_boundary.md).
Repeats, at intervals, until one of the looks crosses.

## Shadow

Selects the moment on luck, and the selection cannot be undone by looking harder.
Early in a trial there are few events and the estimate wanders widely, so the looks at which the treatment appears dramatically better are disproportionately those where chance has run its way: the trial therefore stops when the drug is good and the run has been kind, and no examination of the data at that instant can separate the two contributions.

## Tell

Is the instrument that protects the participants and the instrument that chooses the number.
