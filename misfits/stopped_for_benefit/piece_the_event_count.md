# Piece: The Event Count

## Taxonomy

[The Closed Session](place_the_closed_session.md). The small number that decides how much of this is noise.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the table in [the closed session](place_the_closed_session.md): so many events in one arm, so many in the other, and everything computed from the difference.

## Load Bearing

It governs the whole misfit, and it is smallest exactly when the stop is most tempting.
An estimate built on few events wanders widely, so a dramatic early separation is the cheapest thing for chance to produce: the overestimate in truncated trials is largest where the events are fewest, which means the trials that stop soonest and look most impressive are the ones whose numbers are worth least.

## Apparent

Two integers and a ratio, reported precisely, with an interval that honestly reflects the uncertainty in them.
Nothing hidden and nothing misstated.

## Yearbook

Grows as the trial runs, which is the only thing that would settle the question.
Stops growing at the moment the trial stops, which is triggered by it being small enough to have produced a dramatic ratio.
