# Persona: The One Randomised Next

## Taxonomy

[One Randomised Next](position_one_randomised_next.md). The participant who makes the stop compulsory.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Somebody due to be enrolled tomorrow and allocated by lot, who consented on the understanding that nobody yet knew which arm was better.
Present, identifiable, and about to be assigned.

## Action

Arrives, and would receive whichever arm the envelope gives.
Is offered the treatment instead, because the trial has stopped.

## Shadow

Is the strongest argument in the play and is not wrong.
The premise of randomisation is that nobody knows which arm is better, and once the board believes one is, that premise has failed for this persona specifically: so continuing would mean allocating a person by lot against the trial's own current belief, which nobody can defend and nobody should be asked to.

## Tell

Is protected completely, correctly, and at the cost of everybody who is not yet in the room.
