# Plan: Bring the Numbers Down

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the warden's seat](position_warden.md), inside [More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Warden](position_warden.md)

## Direction

Read the record of widening swings and lay a single measure on both counted stocks together, by the same amount, at the same time, meaning the shared cause to produce a shared, smaller effect. Hold no reach into any fold of the range that sits outside the count, because the measure can only act on what is counted.

## Orders

([The Warden](persona_the_warden.md) suppresses hunter and herd alike across the counted range, expects an even effect from the even cause, and reads the widened swing that follows against the record rather than against which side answered first.)

## Implementation

([The Range](place_the_range.md), the counted portion of it; the [suppressing](process_suppressing.md) process; the outcome is [the release](piece_the_release.md), the herd freed sooner than the hunters can be, widening the swing the measure was meant to flatten.)

## Targets

- [ ] a single measure is laid on predator and prey together, across the counted range
- [ ] the herd answers the measure within a season
- [ ] the predator stock cannot answer within that season, needing several instead
- [ ] the swing that follows is read, and the fold of range the measure never reached is named for what it held steady on its own
