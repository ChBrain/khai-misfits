# Plot: Bred on a Plenty Already Spent

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Range](place_the_range.md), visibly thinner now, the herd's numbers turning down under a season's worth of honest takes.
A litter conceived back when the range still had food to spare is finishing its growth, off in whatever cover raises a litter, unseen by anything counting the range today.

## Action

[Maturing](process_maturing.md): [The Litter](persona_the_litter.md), holding [the litter's seat](position_litter.md), comes up hunting on schedule, its growth funded entirely by a range that no longer stands as it did when the growing began.

## Stage

[The Litter](persona_the_litter.md), having tabled [Come Up Fed](plan_come_up_fed.md), adds its mouths to the hunt and starts taking its own share the moment it is grown.
[The Hunter](persona_the_hunter.md), still on [Eat While It Lasts](plan_eat_while_it_lasts.md), reads the litter's arrival as more hands at a table already setting fewer places, and [the overhang](piece_the_overhang.md) becomes visible on the range: more predator than the current stock of prey can feed.

## Tension

No one erred, and the range is overhung all the same.
The litter is only doing what a well-fed litter does, and it was fed honestly, on food that was real at the time it was eaten. It has simply arrived, on schedule, into a range that spent the meantime growing thinner, so the very success of the season that raised it is exactly what stages this season's overshoot: a predator count that keeps climbing past the point the prey beneath it can still answer for.
