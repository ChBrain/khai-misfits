# Position: Predator

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). The seat that eats, and whose numbers answer a range that has already changed by the time they do.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The capacity to hunt, at whatever rate the range's current stock of prey allows.
A body that converts a good season into more of itself, on a delay it cannot shorten.
No say over how fast the range refills, only over how much of it is taken while it lasts.

## Orders

Eat what the range provides.
Convert each fed season into growth, and let the growth arrive whenever a body takes to grow.
Keep hunting at the same rate once the range has thinned, because the seat has no slower setting built in.

## Loses

The very numbers its own good season bought, once they arrive on a range that season already spent.
Because the growth it earns shows up late, it keeps adding hunters to a stock the range can no longer feed, so its own success is what the shortfall is measured against.
It ends the cycle smaller than its peak, by exactly the amount the range could not carry.

## Drives

Toward a headcount that always answers yesterday's range and never today's,
right in every single season to hunt what is there,
so the seat that eats fastest when food is plenty is the same seat that starves fastest once it is not.
