# Plan: Outbreed the Pressure

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the prey's seat](position_prey.md), inside [More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Prey](position_prey.md)

## Direction

Breed at the rate the range's food and cover allow, replacing losses as they come, whatever is doing the taking and however much of it there is. Hold no rate back on purpose, because there is no signal available that would tell the seat how much predation pressure it is currently under. Refill the range at that same rate the moment the pressure eases.

## Orders

([The Herd](persona_the_herd.md) replaces its losses at the range's own pace, keeps breeding through a season of heavy taking, and rebuilds close to full rate once the hunters thin.)

## Implementation

([The Range](place_the_range.md); the [hunting](process_hunting.md) it absorbs and the [rebounding](process_rebounding.md) it later drives; neither process changes the seat's own breeding rate, which answers only the range's food and cover.)

## Targets

- [ ] losses to hunting are replaced at the range's own pace, through a season of heavy taking
- [ ] the herd's numbers fall exactly as far as the taking, and no further on their own account
- [ ] once the pressure eases, the herd rebuilds toward the range's full rate
- [ ] the rebuilding runs under whatever rate [the throttle](piece_the_throttle.md) leaves in the dams breeding it
