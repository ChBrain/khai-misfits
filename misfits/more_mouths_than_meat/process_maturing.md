# Process: Maturing

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). A litter growing into hunting age on reserves banked at conception, arriving on whatever range exists by the time it is done.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A fed conception and a growth clock that runs at its own pace.
At [the range](place_the_range.md), a litter conceived while the stock was still plentiful, beginning the season or more it takes to grow into a hunter.

## Direction

Toward a full-strength arrival, regardless of what happens on the range meanwhile.
The maturing runs on the reserves it was given at the start, not on the range's current state, so it proceeds at the same pace whether the range is thickening or thinning underneath it, and finishes exactly when its own clock says it finishes.
This is the reproductive delay in motion: the gap between a plenty and the mouths that plenty eventually funds.

## Lever

A growth rate fixed by biology, not by the range's condition.
Because the maturing cannot be sped up by scarcity or slowed by it either, once the reserves are banked the process has no way to answer what the range is doing in the meantime, so the lever is time itself, ticking at a pace nothing on the range can adjust.

## Echo

A predator stock that grows in a single step, late.
While the litter matures, the range shows no new hunters at all, only the ones already counted, so what will land as a sudden jump in mouths reads, right up until it lands, as a range that has not yet felt the plenty it was fed.
