# Pitch: Feast Then Famine

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Plain, unhurried, procedural.
The record of a range across seasons: how many mouths, how much meat, and how long it takes for one figure to answer the other.
Nothing urgent in the telling itself, only a lag that nobody in the scene can feel passing.

## Undertow

Under the calm record, a table that is never set for the party that shows up to it.
Every rise looks, in the season it happens, like simple plenty rewarded, and every fall looks like simple scarcity enforced, and both readings are true and both arrive too late to matter, because the number that would have explained either was already fixed a season before anyone could see it.
The feast and the famine are not two events. They are one curve, read at two different points.

## Nerve

The unease of watching a good season stack the next bad one without either side doing anything to deserve it,
sure, in the moment, that plenty means plenty and scarcity means scarcity,
while the record shows the two were always the same debt, coming due on its own schedule.

## Echo

After the run: a range that has swung wide and swung back, no fatter and no leaner than it would have been left alone,
every rise explained by the fall before it and every fall explained by the rise before that,
and the one quiet stretch that held its shape through the whole of it, the fold nobody touched, is the only part of the record that was never a debt to anyone.
