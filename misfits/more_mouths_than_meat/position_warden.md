# Position: Warden

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). The seat that lays a measure on both stocks at once to flatten the swing, and hands the advantage to whichever side recovers fastest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The reach to suppress both the predator stock and the prey stock across the counted range, by one shared measure.
No reach at all into any part of the range that sits outside the count.
The record of past swings, and no way to read from it that equal is not equivalent.

## Orders

Lay the measure evenly, on predator and prey together.
Expect an even effect from an even cause.
Read what follows against the record, not against which side answered first.

## Loses

The steadying it aimed for, because the measure it can lay evenly is not answered evenly: the herd rebuilds within a season and the hunters need several.
Because the seat can only act on what is counted, the fold of range nobody counts is never touched by its measure at all, and never credited for holding the line the measure could not.
It ends having widened exactly the swing it was brought in to flatten.

## Drives

Toward a correction applied equally to a system that answers unequally,
right to treat like causes alike,
so the seat built to be fair to both sides is the seat that hands the next peak to the one side, without knowing it did.
