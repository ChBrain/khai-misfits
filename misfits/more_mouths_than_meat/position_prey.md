# Position: Prey

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). The seat that feeds the predator and is thinned by it, with no lever to hold its own breeding back against a hunger it cannot count.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A breeding rate set by its own food and cover, not by how many hunters are drawing on it.
The capacity to refill fast once the hunting eases, close to its full rate.
No way to sense how large the predator stock feeding on it has grown, only the losses already taken.

## Orders

Breed at the rate the range allows.
Replace what is lost, at that same rate, whether the loss is light or heavy.
Refill the range once the taking eases, without waiting for a signal that never comes.

## Loses

Numbers to a predator stock it cannot see coming, because the stock that will overshoot it was funded by meals it already gave up.
Because it has no lever to slow its own breeding on purpose, it goes on feeding a hunger already too large for the range to carry, and falls exactly as far as that hunger takes it.
It ends a season thinner than the range's food alone would explain, the difference eaten by mouths its own plenty helped grow.

## Drives

Toward a rebuilding that runs at its own steady rate whenever the pressure lifts,
right in every single season to breed what the range allows,
so the seat that never withholds anything on purpose is the seat every overshoot is finally paid out of.
