# Play: More Mouths Than Meat

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

More Mouths Than Meat.
A hunter eats well, and eats well again, and every meal is a fair trade for the effort it cost. What the meal buys does not arrive at once: a season passes before a full belly becomes another mouth on the range, and it is exactly that season in which the herd being eaten from stops being able to feed so many. By the time the new litter comes up hunting, the plenty that raised it is already gone, and the predator's numbers keep climbing on a memory of food that no longer exists. Nobody overate and nobody misjudged anything, there was nothing to misjudge: a body that eats well breeds, that is what a body does, on whatever the range happens to be by the time the breeding shows up.

## Arc

The bend is this: eating well is what raises a predator's numbers, and the eating happens on time, but the rise it buys never does, so a hunter's numbers always answer a range that has already moved on from the one that fed them.
It runs on a control that only ever sees the past.
Two stocks govern each other and neither from outside: the herd's size sets how well the hunters eat, and the hunters' eating sets how fast the herd falls, so each is the other's ceiling and the other's floor, with nothing standing above either to hold a balance for them. But every signal in that loop is stale by the time it is acted on. A litter conceived on a full range is born and grown, a season or more later, into whatever the range has become by then, and it is always leaner, because the very success that filled the mother is the pressure that was already thinning the herd she fed from.
So the predator's numbers keep rising past the point the range can still feed them, not from any miscount but from bodies doing exactly what fed bodies do, arriving late to a table that was cleared while they were still growing up. The mouths outnumber the meat, and once they do, hunting fails more than it succeeds and the predator's own numbers give way, hard and fast, the fall always steeper than the rise that caused it. In the quiet that follows, with the range short of hunters, the herd multiplies into an emptiness it never had to earn either: and would recover clean, if nothing else were carried forward. Something is. The stress of having been hunted lowers what a surviving mother breeds, and she hands the lowered rate to her young whether the hunters are still there to press her or not, so the rebound runs slower than the bare arithmetic of an empty range would predict, throttled by a season already over.
Reach for the obvious fix, a broad suppression laid on both sides at once, a cull, a poison, and the swing does not settle, it widens: the herd answers a lighter hand within a season, the hunters need several before they can answer at all, so the very evenness of the measure is what hands the advantage to whichever side rebuilds faster. What would actually hold the swing down is not a hand on the numbers at all, but a fold of the range kept out of the count on purpose, defended in every season it looks like it is doing nothing, because that is the one season it is doing everything.

## Triggers

**[The Feeding](plot_the_feeding.md)**
A hunter takes from a herd that can, for now, afford it. The Hunter hunts, the Herd keeps breeding through the loss, and the meal is honestly bought, its full cost not yet posted. Closes when a season's worth of honest takes has been made.

**[Bred on a Plenty Already Spent](plot_bred_on_a_plenty_already_spent.md)**
Cue: the range is visibly thinner, and a litter conceived on the plenty of the last plot comes of age and starts hunting too. The predator's numbers keep climbing on a stock already turning down. Closes when the mismatch between mouths and meat is plain on the range.

**[Meat Runs Out First](plot_meat_runs_out_first.md)**
Cue: the mismatch is read off the larder, more predator than the herd can feed. Hunts fail more than they succeed, and the predator's own numbers give way, faster than they rose. Closes when the crash has run its course.

**[Slower to Return](plot_slower_to_return.md)**
Cue: the range stands empty of hunters, and the herd breeds back into it. The rebound is real, and it is not as fast as an empty range alone would predict, because a stress bred into the mothers rides along into their young. Closes when the herd's numbers are climbing again, at a rate the danger it survived has already dented.

**[The Net That Catches Both](plot_the_net_that_catches_both.md)**
Cue: a fold of the range that nobody hunts or counts, sitting through every swing so far looking like nothing. A broad measure is laid on the counted range to flatten the swings, and it flattens nothing: the herd answers within a season, the hunters cannot, and the swing widens under the very hand meant to steady it. Closes when the swing is wider than before, and the one thing that would have held it, left alone, is named for what it did.

## Stakes

Whether more eating ever settles into a stable more, or only ever a wider swing and a harder crash on the far side of it.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether either side errs, because neither does, but whether a control that only ever answers a past state can ever come to rest instead of circle one. The production cannot close the gap between a meal and its consequence, because the gap is a body's own time to grow, not a miscalculation either side could correct by trying harder or eating less. It can only show that the rise earns its own overshoot, that the crash is the overshoot resolving on schedule, that the recovery carries forward a debt neither side chose to owe, and that the hand reaching for the numbers to steady them is reaching for the one lever guaranteed to widen the swing, while the one thing that would hold it, a piece of the range spent on nothing, has to be defended hardest in the seasons it is proving nothing at all.
