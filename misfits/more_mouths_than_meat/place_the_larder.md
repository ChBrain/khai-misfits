# Place: The Larder

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). Where the mismatch between mouths and meat is read plainly, the count that the starving answers to.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two figures set beside each other: how many predators the range currently carries, and how much prey remains to feed them.
The gap between the two, growing across the seasons the litter matured in, until it stands wide enough that hunts start failing more than they succeed.

## Holds

The reckoning the range itself never shows in real time.
Here the overshoot becomes a number instead of a feeling, so the larder holds the plain arithmetic that the mouths and the meat now disagree, and it is where the shortfall stops being a coming thing and starts being a current one.

## Offers

Nothing that can be spent. Only the true state of the account.
Because the larder counts what is there and what is needed, it offers a clean picture of the mismatch, and clean is not the same as useful: no hunter can eat the larder's honesty, so the offer is a reading, never a remedy.

## Withheld

A way to close the gap without the starving that follows.
The larder can show the shortfall down to its exact size, and it withholds any means of paying it off gently, because the debt is the debt, and the only thing that clears it is the predator count coming back down to what the range can carry.
