# Plot: The Net That Catches Both

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Refuge](place_the_refuge.md), a fold of the range left out of every count, sitting through swing after swing looking like nothing, while elsewhere the record of those swings is read as too wide to leave alone.
A case is made, off the refuge entirely, for a broad measure to flatten what the counted range keeps doing.

## Action

[Suppressing](process_suppressing.md): [The Warden](persona_the_warden.md), holding [the warden's seat](position_warden.md), lays an even measure on both counted stocks at once, while [The Herd](persona_the_herd.md), holding [the prey's seat](position_prey.md), answers it within a season and [The Hunter](persona_the_hunter.md), holding [the predator's seat](position_predator.md), cannot answer that quickly at all.

## Stage

[The Warden](persona_the_warden.md), having tabled [Bring the Numbers Down](plan_bring_the_numbers_down.md), suppresses hunter and herd alike across the counted range, expecting the shared cut to produce a shared, smaller swing.
[The Herd](persona_the_herd.md), still on [Outbreed the Pressure](plan_outbreed_the_pressure.md), rebuilds almost at once, and [the release](piece_the_release.md) follows: freed of pressure sooner than the hunters can be, the herd comes back larger than the measure meant to leave it, while the refuge, never touched because it was never worth counting, goes on quietly carrying breeding stock the crash and the suppression both missed.

## Tension

The measure is not a mistake either. An even hand looks like fairness, and the suppression truly does fall equally.
But equal is not equivalent when one side rebuilds in a season and the other needs several, so the fix reached for because it seemed neutral turns out to be the least neutral thing on offer, and the next swing runs wider than the one it was meant to flatten. The one thing that would have actually held the swing down was never touched at all: the fold of range that did nothing, on purpose, in every season it was asked to prove it mattered, and proved it only in the one season nobody was counting.
