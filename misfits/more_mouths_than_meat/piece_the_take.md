# Piece: The Take

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). One honest meal, paid for in prey lost right now and owed back later, in mouths, on a range that has not yet felt it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the range](place_the_range.md), where a hunt succeeds and the herd's count drops by exactly what was taken.
Set against [the larder](place_the_larder.md), where every take like it is finally added up against what the range can carry.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is no coupling at all, only two populations sitting beside each other with nothing moving between them.
With it the whole mechanism has its first motion: a real loss to the prey and a real gain to the predator, honestly matched in the moment, and nothing hidden except when the gain shows up. The Take is load bearing because it is the coupling's smallest unit (Lotka; Volterra): every overshoot the play stages is nothing more than this one exchange, repeated, with its consequence arriving late.

## Apparent

A single, unremarkable meal, the plainest fact of the range.
Seen in the moment, a take looks like nothing but a hunter eating, and the truth that it is also a deposit against a headcount not due for a season or more stays hidden behind how ordinary the meal looks while it is happening.

## Yearbook

Made through [hunting](process_hunting.md), the coupling in its smallest form.
It is what [the delay](piece_the_delay.md) sits between and what it postpones, what [the overhang](piece_the_overhang.md) is built from take by take, and the reason [the larder](place_the_larder.md) has anything to count at all.
