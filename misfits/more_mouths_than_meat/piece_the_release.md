# Piece: The Release

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). The herd freed of pressure faster than the predator can be, by the very measure meant to hold both stocks down evenly, so the suppression that was meant to flatten the swing hands it to the side that answers first.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the range](place_the_range.md), where an equal cut lands on both stocks and only one of them rebuilds inside the season it takes to notice.
Set against [the refuge](place_the_refuge.md), which was never touched by the cut at all and needed no releasing from it.

## Load Bearing

Without it the obvious fix would simply work, a shared correction producing a shared, smaller swing, and the misfit would have a costless remedy.
With it the correction backfires precisely because it is even-handed: the prey answers within a season and the predator needs several, so the same cut that looked fair widens the gap it was meant to close. The Release is load bearing because it is Volterra's principle staged directly (Volterra; the paradox of the pesticides): the remedy that suppresses both parties frees the one that recovers faster.

## Apparent

A correction working as intended, both counts down together, the record showing shared sacrifice.
Seen in the season it is applied, the release looks like the measure succeeding, both stocks reduced by the same hand, and the truth that one side is already climbing back while the other has barely started stays hidden behind how symmetrical the cut looked at the moment it was made.

## Yearbook

Produced by [suppressing](process_suppressing.md), the even hand that does not land evenly.
It is what [the warden](persona_the_warden.md) could not see coming, the same shape as [the overhang](piece_the_overhang.md) staged by a different cause, and the reason [the refuge](place_the_refuge.md) is worth more than anything the suppressing could touch: a capacity that was never counted cannot be released into overshoot, because it was never suppressed to begin with.
