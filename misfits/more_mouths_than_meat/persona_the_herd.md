# Persona: The Herd

## Taxonomy

[Prey](position_prey.md). Breeds at the rate it always breeds, whatever is being taken from it, and cannot hold that rate back against a hunger it has no way to perceive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Steady, replaceable, indifferent to how many hunters are on the range.
Breeds because breeding is what it does, at a pace set by the range's own food and cover, not by any count of how many mouths are working against it.
No improvidence in it: only a rate it has no lever to lower on purpose.

## Action

Replaces its losses at the rate the range's food and cover allow, season after season, whether the losses are light or heavy.
Falls when the taking outpaces the replacing, and keeps falling as long as it does, with no signal telling it to slow the breeding further or the hunting less.
Rebuilds, once the hunters thin, at close to its full rate again, filling the range back in.

## Shadow

Not reckless, incapable of a different setting: it has no way to read how many hunters are drawing it down, only the food and cover in front of it right now.
So it keeps feeding a predator stock that is, by the time the range shows the strain, already bigger than the strain can support, and its own steady replacing is exactly what keeps that predator stock viable long enough to overshoot.
It is the fuel and the ceiling at once, and neither post is one it set on purpose.

## Tell

Says, in effect, nothing: there is no count of hunters it consults before breeding.
Falls exactly as far as it is taken, and rises again exactly as far as the range still lets it.
