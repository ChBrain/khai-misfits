# Process: Starving

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). A predation pressure outliving the prey base that paid for it, and the predator stock giving way, hard and fast, to match.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A predator headcount already set by a plenty that has since passed, and a range that can no longer feed it.
At [the larder](place_the_larder.md), the mismatch plain: more mouths counted than there is meat to answer them.

## Direction

Toward a predator stock cut down to whatever the range can currently carry, and no further than that.
Each failed hunt is a hunter unfed, and an unfed hunter breeds less or dies outright, so the starving runs exactly as far as the shortfall demands and then stops, because there is nothing left in the mismatch to keep driving it once the predator count and the range's true capacity meet again.
It is the overshoot resolving, not a separate misfortune landing on top of it.

## Lever

A shortfall that every hunter feels at once, regardless of how it was earned.
Because the range cannot tell an honestly grown litter from an old hand at hunting, the starving falls on the whole predator stock together, so the correction is blunt by nature: it does not spare the newly arrived mouths that caused the mismatch, it simply removes mouths until the count fits again.

## Echo

A range that reads, afterward, as a disaster with no author.
The predator numbers that a season ago looked like a healthy, well-fed stock are, once the larder runs short, the same numbers reading as an overshoot that had to correct, so what changes between the two readings is only the calendar, not anything anyone did differently.
