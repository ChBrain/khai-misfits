# Persona: The Hunter

## Taxonomy

[Predator](position_predator.md). Takes from the herd at whatever rate the range allows, and is right that the meal is fairly earned.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Fed, capable, unhurried.
Hunts because hunting is what it is built to do, not out of any want beyond the next meal, and reads a full belly as nothing more than the day's honest wage.
No greed in it: only a body doing what a body of its kind does with the food in front of it.

## Action

Takes prey at the rate the range currently supports, no more than that and no less.
Turns each good season into more of itself, a season or two down the line, in the form of a litter it will not see hunting until the range has already changed underneath it.
Keeps hunting exactly as hard once the range has thinned, because slower is not a setting it has.

## Shadow

Not gluttonous, thorough: it eats what is there and converts it, faithfully, into more mouths to feed later.
But the litter its good season buys arrives on a range that season already spent, so its own success is what stocks the shortfall it will starve into, and by the time hunting fails more than it succeeds, the numbers doing the failing were earned honestly, every one of them.
It is at its largest exactly when the range can least afford it, and did nothing wrong to get there.

## Tell

Says, in effect, nothing: it does not reason about the range at all, only takes what a hunt yields.
Is fullest the season before the hunts start failing, and cannot tell the two seasons apart from the inside.
