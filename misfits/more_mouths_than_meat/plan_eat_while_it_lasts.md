# Plan: Eat While It Lasts

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the predator's seat](position_predator.md), inside [More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Predator](position_predator.md)

## Direction

Take from the range at whatever rate it currently allows, and let each fed season become more hunters whenever a body takes to grow one. There is no slower setting to reach for and no faster one either: hunt as hard as the range supports, this season and the next, whatever the next one turns out to hold.

## Orders

([The Hunter](persona_the_hunter.md) takes prey at the rate the range allows, feeds the litter that will hunt later, and keeps hunting at the same rate once the range has thinned, because the seat has no other setting.)

## Implementation

([The Range](place_the_range.md); the [hunting](process_hunting.md) process; [the take](piece_the_take.md) is the honest unit of it, and [the delay](piece_the_delay.md) is why its full cost does not land until later.)

## Targets

- [ ] a fed season is taken from the range, honestly, while the range can support it
- [ ] the season's growth arrives, as more hunters, on whatever range exists by then
- [ ] hunting continues at the same rate once the range has thinned
- [ ] the mismatch resolves itself in [starving](process_starving.md), because no order here says to stop first
