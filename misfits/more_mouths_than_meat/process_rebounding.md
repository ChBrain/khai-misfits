# Process: Rebounding

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). The herd multiplying into a range briefly emptied of predators, at a rate the season it survived has already dented.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A predator stock thinned by starving, and a range with food and cover to spare again.
At [the range](place_the_range.md), the herd breeding back up with almost nothing left to take the losses.

## Direction

Toward a herd refilling the range, close to, but short of, the rate an untouched population would.
The rebounding runs on the food and cover now available, so it climbs fast by the herd's own standard, and it climbs slower than the bare arithmetic would predict, because part of the breeding stock is carrying a lowered rate forward from the pressure it survived.
It is recovery and inheritance running at once, in the same season.

## Lever

Abundance on one side, and a marked breeding rate riding along on the other.
Because the food and cover no longer limit the herd the way the predator pressure just did, the rebounding is driven mainly by what is available, tempered by however many dams in the breeding stock are carrying the throttle, so the lever is mixed: mostly opportunity, partly a debt still being paid.

## Echo

A range reading as recovered before the record shows it fully is.
The herd is visibly climbing, cover fills back in, hunters are scarce, and it looks, from inside the season, like a clean return to the range's old numbers, so what the record will eventually show as a rebound running under pace is, for now, simply read as recovery, no slower than expected because nobody on the range is counting against an expectation.
