# Place: The Range

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). Where the hunting, the maturing and the rebounding all happen, the shared ground both stocks are governed on and by.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Predator and prey, sharing one stretch of ground, each visible to the other only as numbers taken or numbers left.
The season's condition: thick with cover and food, or thinned by a season of honest hunting, changing under both stocks at the same pace neither of them set alone.

## Holds

The whole loop in one place: what is eaten, what breeds, what matures, what starves, what refills.
Here the coupling is not an idea but a shared floor, so the range holds both curves at once and lets neither move without moving the other, the predator's rise drawn straight from the prey's fall and the prey's fall setting, later, the predator's own ceiling.

## Offers

A season that always reads as the plain continuation of the one before it.
Because the range shows only its current state and never the reserves already banked or the debts already owed, it offers each season as if it stood on its own, so a good season for the hunters looks like nothing more than a good season, not the funding of a shortfall still a season away.

## Withheld

Any sign of what has already been committed and not yet arrived.
The range cannot show a litter still maturing, or a stress still working its way through a dam's next breeding, so it withholds the very information that would let either stock act on its own future instead of its own present, holding both to a read that is always, structurally, a season behind.
