# Persona: The Dam

## Taxonomy

[Dam](position_dam.md). Survives the hunting and carries what it cost her into the rate she breeds at, whether the hunters are still there to press her or not.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Wary, worn, still breeding.
Lived through a season under real pressure, and the pressure left something in her that outlasted the pressure itself.
No fault in it: a body under threat that long changes what it can afford to spend on the next litter, and she did not choose the change.

## Action

Breeds, once the danger has passed, at a rate lower than an unthreatened dam would.
Passes that lowered rate to her own young before they are born, not as a lesson taught but as a condition carried.
Cannot raise the rate back by trying, because it was never a decision she made in the first place.

## Shadow

Not overcautious, marked: the stress she carried through the hunted season set something that does not reset the moment the hunters thin.
So the range's rebound, which looks like it should run at the herd's full rate the instant the predators are gone, is quietly capped by however many dams like her are breeding into it, a debt paid forward from a danger already over.
She survived the thing that should have slowed her, and it slowed her anyway, afterward, on her own young.

## Tell

Says, in effect, "the danger is over," and breeds as if it is not, because some part of what the danger did to her never got the news.
