# Piece: The Crash

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). The predator stock giving way, hard and fast, to a shortfall its own honest overshoot created, the fall always steeper than the rise that caused it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the range](place_the_range.md), where hunts start failing more than they succeed and the predator count gives way in a single, sharp season.
Measured at [the larder](place_the_larder.md), where the mismatch that caused it was already plain before the fall began.

## Load Bearing

Without it the overhang would simply stand, a permanent excess with nothing to resolve it, and the system would not cycle, it would just be wrong.
With it the mismatch is corrected the only way the range can correct it, by removing mouths until the count fits again, all at once rather than gradually. The Crash is load bearing because it is the starvation vector completed (Elton and Nicholson): the reason the record shows a ten-year cycle and not a slow, steady climb.

## Apparent

Calamity, a sudden and severe collapse with the shape of a disaster.
Seen from inside the season it happens, the crash looks like misfortune striking a healthy population, and the truth that it is the exact, proportionate correction of a shortfall the population itself grew into stays hidden behind how abrupt and total the fall reads.

## Yearbook

Reached through [starving](process_starving.md), the process that removes what the larder shows is owed.
It is what [the overhang](piece_the_overhang.md) was always going to become, what clears the range for [rebounding](process_rebounding.md) to begin, and what leaves behind the dams whose [throttle](piece_the_throttle.md) will slow that same rebound once it starts.
