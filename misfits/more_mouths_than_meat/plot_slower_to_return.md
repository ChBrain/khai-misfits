# Plot: Slower to Return

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Range](place_the_range.md), hunters scarce now after the crash, prey multiplying into an emptiness it did not have to earn and does not need to understand.
Food and cover both standing open again, more than the herd's current numbers use.

## Action

[Rebounding](process_rebounding.md): [The Herd](persona_the_herd.md), holding [the prey's seat](position_prey.md), breeds back up with almost nothing left to thin it, while [The Dam](persona_the_dam.md), holding [the dam's seat](position_dam.md), carries forward a lowered rate set by a season of pressure that has already passed.

## Stage

[The Herd](persona_the_herd.md), on [Outbreed the Pressure](plan_outbreed_the_pressure.md), refills the range at close to its full rate, the numbers climbing season over season.
[The Dam](persona_the_dam.md), having tabled [Carry It Forward](plan_carry_it_forward.md), breeds her own litter at less than that rate, because [the throttle](piece_the_throttle.md) set in her while the hunters were still pressing her, and it travels with her regardless of whether they still are.

## Tension

The recovery is real, and it is not as fast as an empty range and a fed herd alone would predict.
There is no wrong choice in it: the herd is breeding at its own honest rate, and the dam is breeding at hers, marked as it is. But part of what is refilling the range is a generation whose mothers carried a danger that lifted before their young were even born, so the rebound is throttled by a season already over, a debt paid forward by bodies that had no say in owing it.
