# Plot: Meat Runs Out First

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Larder](place_the_larder.md), the mismatch shown plain: predator numbers at their high point, the herd already short of what that many mouths need.
The two figures no longer agree, and the gap between them is wide enough that the next hunt is no longer a sure thing.

## Action

[Starving](process_starving.md): [The Hunter](persona_the_hunter.md), now the litter-joined predator stock at its peak, hunts exactly as hard as ever and finds less to take with every round, because the range beneath it has already been spent.

## Stage

[The Hunter](persona_the_hunter.md), still holding [Eat While It Lasts](plan_eat_while_it_lasts.md) because a hunter has no other plan to fall back on, fails more hunts than it completes, and [the crash](piece_the_crash.md) follows: predator numbers give way, fast, faster than they rose.
[The Herd](persona_the_herd.md), thinned to its own low point, holds on [Outbreed the Pressure](plan_outbreed_the_pressure.md), breeding whatever the remaining pressure still allows.

## Tension

The fall is not a punishment. It is the same honest arithmetic, run the other way.
A stock that grew past what its food could carry sheds exactly the numbers the shortfall demands, no more and no less, and it sheds them from a population that, a season ago, was only doing what a well-fed population does. There is no better decision the hunters could have made mid-crash: the mismatch was set before any of them could hunt differently, and the crash is simply that mismatch resolving on its own schedule.
