# Persona: The Warden

## Taxonomy

[Warden](position_warden.md). Lays an even measure on both stocks at once to flatten the swing, and cannot see that even is not equivalent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Reasonable, even-handed, working from the record.
Sees the swing widening season over season and reaches for the one lever that looks fair, a measure that touches predator and prey alike, by the same amount, at the same time.
No overreach in it: only the sound instinct that a shared cause should get a shared correction.

## Action

Suppresses both stocks across the counted range by an equal measure, meaning the same equally to be a symmetrical fix.
Watches the herd answer within a season, breeding back to where it stood before, while the hunters, needing a season or more to raise a litter of their own, cannot answer at all in that time.
Reads the widened swing that follows as a puzzle in the numbers, not a fault in the measure, because the measure really did fall equally.

## Shadow

Not careless, misled by the wrong symmetry: equal is the fair word and the wrong one, because the two stocks do not answer a lightened hand on the same clock.
So the measure that was meant to hold the swing down hands the advantage to whichever side rebuilds first, and the herd rebuilds first every time, so the next peak the Warden's own correction produces is higher than the one it was called in to prevent.
It reached for the lever every record recommends, and the record is exactly what the two stocks do not share.

## Tell

Says "the same to both, so it is fair to both," and the range answers that the same is never the same when one side needs a season and the other needs three.
