# Process: Hunting

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). The predator taking from the herd at the rate the range currently allows, the coupling that governs both stocks from the inside.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A herd to draw on and a predator built to draw on it.
At [the range](place_the_range.md), the hunters taking prey at whatever rate the current stock supports, the herd losing exactly what is taken and no more.

## Direction

Toward a meal honestly earned, and a growth it will not pay out until later.
Each successful hunt feeds a body that will, in due course, become more hunters, so the hunting drives its own future headcount without any hunter intending that, and it runs as hard as the range allows because there is no signal in a single hunt that says the range as a whole is being drawn down too far.
The taking and the future overshoot are the same motion, read at two different times.

## Lever

A hunger that is satisfied immediately and a consequence that is not.
Because the eating and the effect of the eating happen on different clocks, the hunting has every reason to continue exactly as it has, no reason inside any single hunt to slow, so the process turns on how well fed the hunter is right now, never on how the herd's whole stock is standing.

## Echo

A range drawn down by an amount that reads, in the moment, as sustainable.
The meal is taken, the herd absorbs the loss, and the hunt looks, from inside the season, like the range holding steady, so what shows on the range as a normal season of feeding is, unseen, the funding of a predator headcount the same range will not be able to support once it arrives.
