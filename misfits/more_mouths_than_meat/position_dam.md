# Position: Dam

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). The seat that survives the hunted season and carries what it cost her forward into the next generation's breeding rate.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A breeding rate that the stress of a hunted season can lower, and keep lowered, past the season itself.
The means to pass that lowered rate to her own young before they are born.
No control over when the marking happens or how long it lasts, only over the young she raises after it has.

## Orders

Survive the hunted season.
Breed afterward at whatever rate the marking left her.
Hand that rate forward, unaltered, to the next litter.

## Loses

The clean rebound an unmarked dam would have, because the danger she lived through did not end when the hunting stopped.
Because the lowering was never a choice, she cannot raise her own rate back by trying, and the range's recovery is capped by however many seats like hers are breeding into it.
She ends the season a survivor, and the survival itself is what slows what comes after her.

## Drives

Toward a rebound that runs under the bare arithmetic of an empty range,
right to breed at whatever rate she was left with,
so the seat that carried the danger longest is the seat still paying for it after the danger is gone.
