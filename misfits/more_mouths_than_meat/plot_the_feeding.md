# Plot: The Feeding

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Range](place_the_range.md), stocked well within what it can currently feed, predator and prey both holding steady numbers.
Nothing strained yet: every hunt that is made this season is one the range can plainly absorb.

## Action

[Hunting](process_hunting.md): [The Hunter](persona_the_hunter.md), holding [the predator's seat](position_predator.md), takes prey at the rate the range allows, while [The Herd](persona_the_herd.md), holding [the prey's seat](position_prey.md), keeps breeding at the rate it always does, losses and all.

## Stage

[The Hunter](persona_the_hunter.md), having tabled [Eat While It Lasts](plan_eat_while_it_lasts.md), makes [the take](piece_the_take.md), a full meal, earned clean, and reads it as nothing more than the season's ordinary wage.
[The Herd](persona_the_herd.md), on [Outbreed the Pressure](plan_outbreed_the_pressure.md), keeps the range stocked despite what is taken, replacing its losses at the pace the range still allows.

## Tension

The meal is honest, and honest is not the same as free.
There is no fault to find here: the hunter takes exactly what the range currently supports, the herd loses exactly what is taken and no more, and by every measure available this season, the range is holding steady. But [the delay](piece_the_delay.md) sits underneath the whole exchange: what this season's good hunting buys will not arrive as more hunters for a season or more yet, so the books that look balanced right now are already carrying an entry that has not been posted, a debit the range has not yet been asked to pay.
