# Piece: The Delay

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). The gap between a meal being eaten and there being more mouths for it, physiological and not decisional, the reason nothing in the loop can act on the range as it currently stands.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the range](place_the_range.md), spanning every season between a fed conception and a hunting-age arrival.
Answered nowhere: no place on the range closes it faster, because it is a body's own time to grow, not a distance any place could shorten.

## Load Bearing

Without it a rise in eating would show up as a rise in numbers at once, and the system could track itself and hold steady.
With it every gain and every loss is read against a range that has already moved on, so The Delay is load bearing because it is the governing law itself made concrete (Lotka; Volterra): a control acting through it cannot stop at the right level, because by the time it perceives the level, the level has changed.

## Apparent

Simple growth time, unremarkable, the plain fact that a litter takes a season to become a hunter.
Seen from inside any single season, the delay looks like nothing but biology proceeding normally, and the truth that this normal proceeding is what makes the whole system circle instead of settle stays hidden behind how ordinary a growing season looks while it is passing.

## Yearbook

Run through [maturing](process_maturing.md), the clock that ticks regardless of what the range is doing.
It is what turns [the take](piece_the_take.md) into [the overhang](piece_the_overhang.md), what makes [the crash](piece_the_crash.md) arrive on schedule rather than on warning, and the reason [the refuge](place_the_refuge.md) matters: nothing counted can outrun a delay built into growing up, but something never counted was never waiting on the count in the first place.
