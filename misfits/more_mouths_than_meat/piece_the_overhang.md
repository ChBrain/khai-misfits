# Piece: The Overhang

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). More predator than the current range can feed, the overshoot made visible, built entirely from meals that were honest when they were eaten.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the range](place_the_range.md), where the litter's arrival raises the predator count past what the thinned herd can support.
Read plainly at [the larder](place_the_larder.md), where the mismatch between mouths and meat is finally set down as a figure.

## Load Bearing

Without it the cycle would be a single rise and a single fall, proportionate, and nothing would call the misfit a misfit.
With it the system carries past the level it should have stopped at, not because anything pushed harder than it needed to, but because the mouths that arrive were promised by a plenty that no longer exists. The Overhang is load bearing because it is the overshoot itself (Elton and Nicholson; Krebs, Boutin and Boonstra): the visible proof that a control through a delay cannot stop at the right level, only pass through it.

## Apparent

A predator stock simply doing well, numerous, capable, feeding itself.
Seen from inside the season it appears, the overhang looks like health, a strong hunting population, and the truth that it is already more than the range beneath it can carry stays hidden behind how robust the numbers look right up until the hunts start failing.

## Yearbook

Built by [maturing](process_maturing.md), one arriving litter at a time.
It is what [the delay](piece_the_delay.md) produces once it runs its course, what [the crash](piece_the_crash.md) exists to correct, and what [the release](piece_the_release.md) shows in mirror image: the same structure, mouths outrunning meat, staged once by an honest overshoot and once by a fix that made it worse.
