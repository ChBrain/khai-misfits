# Process: Suppressing

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). A measure laid on predator and prey together to flatten the swing between them, and the widening of that swing instead, because the predator rebuilds slower than the thing it eats.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A record of swings read as too wide to leave alone, and a wish for a fix that touches nobody unfairly.
At [the range](place_the_range.md), a measure applied equally across the counted stock, predator and prey together, by the same amount at the same time.

## Direction

Toward what looks, at the moment it is laid, like a shared and therefore fair correction.
The suppressing removes numbers from both sides by the same measure, so it drives toward an apparent balance, an equal cut applied to an unequal system, and it does not know, because nothing in the measure can know, that the two sides answer a lightened hand on different clocks.

## Lever

A single shared cause, expected to produce a single shared effect.
Because the measure cannot distinguish a stock that rebuilds in one season from a stock that needs several, the suppressing runs on the false symmetry of equal treatment, and the lever that seemed to guarantee fairness is exactly what guarantees the two sides drift apart once the measure lifts.

## Echo

A range that reads, in the season the measure is laid, as visibly calmer.
Both counts drop together, the record shows a shared sacrifice borne evenly, so what will become, a season or two later, a wider swing than before is, at the moment of the cut, indistinguishable from the correction working exactly as intended.
