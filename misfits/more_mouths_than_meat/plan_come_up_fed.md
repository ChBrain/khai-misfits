# Plan: Come Up Fed

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the litter's seat](position_litter.md), inside [More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md).

## Direction

Grow on the reserves banked at conception, on schedule, regardless of what the range does in the meantime. Come of age at full strength and join the hunt the moment growth is complete, without checking the range first, because there is no way to check it that would change the growing.

## Orders

([The Litter](persona_the_litter.md) matures on reserves from a fed conception, comes up hunting on schedule, and adds its mouths to the predator count without adjusting for what that count already stands at.)

## Implementation

([The Range](place_the_range.md); the [maturing](process_maturing.md) process, running on its own clock; the arrival lands as [the overhang](piece_the_overhang.md) once the range beneath it has already thinned.)

## Targets

- [ ] the litter matures on reserves banked while the range still had them to spare
- [ ] the growing runs its full course, unaffected by the range's condition in the meantime
- [ ] the litter comes up hunting at full strength, on schedule
- [ ] its arrival lands as more mouths than the current range can feed
