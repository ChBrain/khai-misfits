# Persona: The Litter

## Taxonomy

[Litter](position_litter.md). Comes of age funded by a plenty that has already gone, and hunts into a range it never saw at its best.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Young, well grown, arriving.
Was fed, before it could hunt for itself, on a range that had food to spare, and grows into a hunter exactly as that feeding promised it would.
No presumption in it: it simply becomes what its own good start made it.

## Action

Matures on reserves banked during a season of plenty, out of sight of the range as it is now.
Comes up hunting on schedule, joining the predator stock at full strength, whatever the range has done in the meantime.
Adds its mouths to the count without any way of knowing the count it is joining is already too high.

## Shadow

Not premature, exactly on time: its growth took the season it always takes, no faster and no slower.
But the season it took is the same season the range spent turning thinner, so the moment it is ready to hunt is the moment its hunting can least be supported, and its arrival is what tips a rising predator stock into one the range can no longer carry.
It is the overshoot made flesh, and it was funded honestly, every meal of it, before the shortfall existed.

## Tell

Says, in effect, "I grew where I was fed," and means it: the range it grew on and the range it hunts on are not the same range, and it has no way to tell.
