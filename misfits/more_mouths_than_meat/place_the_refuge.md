# Place: The Refuge

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). A fold of the range kept out of the count on purpose, doing nothing visible through every swing, and holding the breeding stock that survives them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A corner of the range no hunter reaches and no larder counts, through feast seasons and famine seasons alike.
Prey sitting there in numbers too small to matter to any single season's tally, undisturbed by whatever the counted range is doing.

## Holds

The one piece of the herd that neither the crash nor the suppression can touch.
Here a small breeding stock rides out every swing untouched by either, so the refuge holds continuity itself, the thing that lets the counted range refill from something rather than from nothing when the crash empties it.

## Offers

Nothing, for as long as it can be watched.
Because the whole value of the refuge is that it is not being drawn on, it offers no return anyone can point to in the season they are looking, so its contribution is invisible exactly when someone is asking what it is worth.

## Withheld

Itself, from the count, and that withholding is the whole of what it does.
The refuge cannot hold its ground while also being counted as available capacity, so it withholds its own numbers from every tally that would otherwise fold it into the next hunt, or the next broad measure meant to flatten the swing.
