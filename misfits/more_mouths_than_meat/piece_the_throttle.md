# Piece: The Throttle

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). A lowered breeding rate set by a hunted season and carried maternally into the next generation, slowing a rebound the danger it answers to has already left.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the range](place_the_range.md), riding along inside the herd's own rebounding, invisible in any single season's headcount.
Set by the season under real predation pressure, and paid out in the season after, once the pressure is already gone.

## Load Bearing

Without it the rebound would run exactly as fast as an empty range and a fed herd predict, a clean bounce back to the old numbers.
With it the recovery is quietly capped by however many dams are breeding under the marking, so the cycle's rising edge is never as steep as the falling edge that produced it. The Throttle is load bearing because it is the inherited vector itself (Boonstra; Sheriff): the reason a population can go on answering a danger that is no longer there to press it.

## Apparent

Simply a slower season, unremarkable, the kind of variation any wild population shows.
Seen from inside the range, a throttled rebound looks like nothing more than an ordinary dip in the rate, and the truth that it is a debt from a specific danger, paid by a specific generation of mothers, stays hidden behind how normal a slightly slower season looks next to a slightly faster one.

## Yearbook

Carried through no process the play names directly, set instead within [the dam](persona_the_dam.md) herself and handed forward as she breeds.
It is what keeps [rebounding](process_rebounding.md) from running at its bare-arithmetic pace, what makes the recovery from [the crash](piece_the_crash.md) slower than the crash alone would predict, and the quiet cousin of [the release](piece_the_release.md): one throttle nobody chose, one release nobody meant, both riding a delay no one in the loop can see across.
