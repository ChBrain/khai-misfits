# Position: Litter

## Taxonomy

[More Mouths Than Meat](play_more_mouths_than_meat.md). The seat that matures on a plenty already spent, and joins the hunt on schedule whatever the range has become.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A growth clock set at conception, running on reserves banked while the range was still able to spare them.
No visibility into the range as it stands the day it finishes growing.
The same appetite and the same hunting capacity as any other predator, the moment it comes of age.

## Orders

Mature on schedule.
Join the hunt at full strength once grown, regardless of what the range can currently support.
Add to the predator count without adjusting for what that count has already become.

## Loses

The chance to arrive when the range could still carry it, because its growing season is exactly the season the range spent turning thin.
Because the clock that sets its maturing cannot be slowed by anything happening on the range meanwhile, it lands, on time, in the one moment its landing does the most damage.
It ends the plot as the visible overshoot, the mouths the meat can no longer answer.

## Drives

Toward a full-strength arrival timed by its own growth and by nothing else,
right to have matured on what it was fed,
so the seat that comes of age exactly on schedule is the seat that turns a fed season into next season's shortfall.
