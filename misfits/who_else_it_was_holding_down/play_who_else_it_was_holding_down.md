# Play: Who Else It Was Holding Down

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Who Else It Was Holding Down.
An apex predator takes stock, the losses are counted, and it is removed. The removal works. What is not counted is that the same appetite had been killing and harrying every smaller predator in the range, so those rise, and they take nests and small vertebrates in numbers the large one never did. The cost lands on a species that was not the subject of the request, not the object of the intervention, and has no column in the ledger.

## Arc

The bend is this: the intervention succeeds completely on its target and produces the opposite of its purpose on a party the accounting has no place for.
It begins with a real loss and a competent programme.
[The Loss Is Real and the Removal Works](plot_the_loss_is_real_and_the_removal_works.md). The takes are counted, the target comes down, and every figure in the return is correct.
And the animal was doing two things with one appetite.
[And the Apex Was Also Holding Something Down](plot_and_the_apex_was_also_holding_something_down.md). [The Suppression](piece_the_suppression.md) is the same act as the offence, so no version of the intervention keeps it.
So the ground fills.
[So the Ground It Held Is Filled](plot_so_the_ground_it_held_is_filled.md). [The Released Middle](piece_the_released_middle.md) rises with nothing applied to it, responding to a subtraction rather than an agent.
Then [the cost lands on a third party](plot_and_the_cost_lands_on_a_third_party.md).
Many small predators take more nests than one large one did, so removing a predator increases predation.
And [no ledger has a column for it](plot_which_no_ledger_has_a_column_for.md). [The Missing Column](piece_the_missing_column.md) is a gap in the schema rather than in the entries, which is why no audit reaches it.
And [putting it back does not simply reverse](plot_and_putting_it_back_does_not_simply_reverse.md). [The Restorer](persona_the_restorer.md) argues a sound structure against an uneven record.

## Stakes

Whether an intervention that succeeds at everything it measures can be told from one that works.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a real loss competently relieved, a service that is the same act as the offence, a release produced by subtraction rather than by any agent, a third party whose cost exceeds what was prevented, a ledger with no column to record it in, and a repair that is right in principle and uneven in effect, and away from any account in which the stockman exaggerated or the agency was careless. The production cannot fault the stockman, who is asked what was lost and answers accurately; and it cannot fault the controller, whose return would survive any audit, since an audit checks the columns that exist against the world and never checks the world for columns. What is left to fight over is not whether the programme did what it said but who was ever going to be counting the thing it cost.
