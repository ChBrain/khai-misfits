# Place: The Ledger

## Taxonomy

[Who Else It Was Holding Down](play_who_else_it_was_holding_down.md). The accounting that has two columns, and the cost that is in neither.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A programme return: stock losses before and after, target animals taken, cost per unit, outcome delivered.
No creative accounting and no missing receipts. Everything entered is correct.

## Holds

[The Stockman](persona_the_stockman.md), whose losses are the first column.
[The Controller](persona_the_controller.md), whose takes are the second.
[The Missing Column](piece_the_missing_column.md), which is the whole misfit.

## Offers

An honest and complete account of everything the programme was set up to account for.

## Withheld

A line for whoever pays.
The place is not falsified and is not incomplete by its own standard: it answers the question it was built to answer, and answers it correctly. What it cannot do is register a cost falling on a party that is not a stakeholder, not a target and not a cost centre, because a ledger has the columns it was given and no ledger acquires one by the cost arriving. So the failure is not that somebody looked away but that nowhere in the instrument is there a place to look.
