## Load Bearing

It takes more small prey than the apex did, so removing one predator increases predation rather than reducing it.
Mesopredators are smaller, far more numerous and take nests, eggs and small vertebrates the apex largely ignored. Released, they range further, forage in daylight and breed up. The arithmetic runs against intuition: one large predator removed is replaced by many small ones whose combined take on the third party exceeds anything the apex was doing to it. Across two centuries the pattern is one-directional, with mesopredator ranges expanding while apex ranges contracted.
