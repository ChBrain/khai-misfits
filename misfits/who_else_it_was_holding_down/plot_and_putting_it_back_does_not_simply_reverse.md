## Tension

The obvious remedy is the one the structure implies and cannot be promised to work.
Reviews find the mesopredator response matching prediction in roughly half the pairings examined, and one large fenced removal experiment found no mesopredator increase at all. Productivity, other human pressure and which mesopredator is present all modulate it, and most of the evidence is correlative rather than manipulative. So restoration is argued for on a structure that is sound and a record that is uneven, by people reading the same papers as those who doubt it.
