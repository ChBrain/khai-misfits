# Place: The Fragment

## Taxonomy

[Who Else It Was Holding Down](play_who_else_it_was_holding_down.md). Where it was first measured, and where the chain is short enough to see.

## Owner

## Shown

A canyon of native scrub, cut off by housing, one of dozens ranging from half a hectare to a hundred.
Ordinary careful work: area and age since isolation recorded, birds counted on transects, predators tracked.

## Holds

## Offers

A place small enough that the whole chain can be counted: the apex, the middle, and the birds.

## Withheld

Any confusion about what is causing what.
The place is where the structure stopped being an argument, because fragmentation supplied the removal without anybody intending one: canyons that lost their coyotes are the canyons with more cats and foxes, and they are the canyons where the scrub-breeding birds went. That is why the observation is credited, and it is also why the generality is contested, since a canyon is not a continent and the natural experiment was not designed.
