## Load Bearing

It is the same behaviour as the predation on stock, aimed at different bodies, which is why one intervention ends both.
A large predator kills and harries smaller predators that eat the same prey, which the literature calls intraguild predation: a blend of competition and predation in which potential competitors eat each other. It is not a favour, is not intended as one and is not directed at any interest of anybody's. But it holds the mesopredator population and its behaviour down continuously, for free, and it is delivered by the same appetite that takes lambs. So the animal cannot be removed for one and left in place for the other.
