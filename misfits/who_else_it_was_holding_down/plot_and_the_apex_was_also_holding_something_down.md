# Plot: And the Apex Was Also Holding Something Down

## Taxonomy

[Who Else It Was Holding Down](play_who_else_it_was_holding_down.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The range, empty of its largest predator for the first time.

## Action

[The Suppression](piece_the_suppression.md) is identified: the same appetite that took lambs was killing and harrying every smaller predator in the range, continuously and for nothing.

## Stage

A service ending, which no party contracted, priced or recorded.

## Tension

Predation on stock and suppression of mesopredators are one behaviour aimed at different bodies, so ending one ends the other.
The animal is guilty of what the tally says and no exoneration is offered. The point is that the thing it was doing to smaller predators is not a separable second activity that could have been retained: it is the same act, and it was never a favour, never intended and never charged for. So there is no version of the intervention that keeps it.
