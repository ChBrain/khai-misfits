# Persona: The Controller

## Taxonomy

[Controller](position_controller.md). The one who runs the programme and measures it honestly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A control programme with a target species, a method, a budget and an annual return showing the target down.

## Action

Removes the apex predator across the range.
Reports the reduction, which is real.

## Shadow

Succeeds completely at the thing being measured, and the measurement is not wrong.
The programme is not a failure and does not look like one from any position inside it: the target is reduced, the reduction is verified, and stock losses fall. Every number in the return is correct. What the return has no line for is a species that was never a target, never a cost centre and never a subject of the programme, and the absence of that line is not a concealment. Nobody removed the column. There was never a column.

## Tell

Delivers exactly what was commissioned, and the commission was the shape of the ledger.
