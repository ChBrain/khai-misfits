# Persona: The Stockman

## Taxonomy

[Stockman](position_stockman.md). The one whose losses are real and documented.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A season's tally of lambs and calves taken, and a request to the agency that runs predator control.

## Action

Reports the losses.
Asks for the animal that took them to be removed.

## Shadow

Is right about the loss, and the loss is the only thing anybody asked about.
Nothing here is imagined or inflated: apex predators do take stock, the takes are counted, and the person carrying that cost is entitled to ask for it to stop. What the request cannot contain is the rest of what the animal was doing, because a stockman is not positioned to observe it and was never asked to. The question put to the seat is what it lost, and the seat answers that question accurately.

## Tell

Answers the question asked, which is the whole of the question anybody asks.
