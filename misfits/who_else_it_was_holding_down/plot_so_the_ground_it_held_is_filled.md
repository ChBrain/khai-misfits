# Plot: So the Ground It Held Is Filled

## Taxonomy

[Who Else It Was Holding Down](play_who_else_it_was_holding_down.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Foxes, cats and raccoons in a range with nothing above them.

## Action

[Filling the ground](process_filling_the_ground.md): [the middle hunter](persona_the_middle_hunter.md) ranges further, forages by day and breeds up, and [the released middle](piece_the_released_middle.md) is what the counts resolve into.

## Stage

More predators than before the programme, of a different size.

## Tension

Nothing was applied here, and the rise is a response to a subtraction rather than to an agent.
No poison, no bounty and no incentive reaches the mesopredator: what reaches it is the absence of the thing that was pressing down. That is why no line of the programme reads as its cause and why the seat cannot be described as responding badly. Across two centuries the direction is consistent, mesopredator ranges expanding as apex ranges contract.
