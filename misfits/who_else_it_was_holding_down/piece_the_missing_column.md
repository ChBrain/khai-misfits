# Piece: The Missing Column

## Taxonomy

[The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md). The shape of the instrument, and the whole of the misfit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

The absence at [the ledger](place_the_ledger.md), left standing by [counting what was removed](process_counting_what_was_removed.md).

## Load Bearing

The party that pays is not a stakeholder, not a target and not a cost centre, so the accounting has nowhere to record it.
The programme has a column for stock lost and a column for animals taken, and both are filled correctly. A scrub-breeding bird is neither: it is not the subject of the request, not the object of the intervention and not represented at any point in the sequence. So the cost does not appear as a small figure or a disputed one. It does not appear. And no audit finds it, because an audit checks the columns that exist against the world rather than checking the world for columns.

## Apparent

An oversight. Something somebody forgot to include.

## Yearbook

Is not a gap in the entries but a gap in the schema, which is why nobody in the sequence could have caught it.
