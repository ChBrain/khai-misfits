# Plot: Which No Ledger Has a Column For

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

The annual return, complete and correct.

## Action

[The Missing Column](piece_the_missing_column.md) is located at [the ledger](place_the_ledger.md): stock lost, animals taken, and nowhere to enter a bird.

## Stage

A cost that does not appear as small or disputed, but does not appear.

## Tension

The gap is in the schema rather than in the entries, which is why nobody in the sequence could have caught it.
The stockman is asked what was lost and answers accurately. The controller is asked whether the target came down and answers accurately. An audit compares the columns against the world and finds them right. The third party is not a stakeholder, not a target and not a cost centre, so there is no position in the arrangement from which its loss is a number anybody is responsible for producing.
