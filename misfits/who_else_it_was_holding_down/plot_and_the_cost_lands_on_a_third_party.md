# Plot: And the Cost Lands on a Third Party

## Taxonomy

[Who Else It Was Holding Down](play_who_else_it_was_holding_down.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Transect counts in canyons that lost their coyotes.

## Action

The scrub-breeding birds are counted on transects at [the fragment](place_the_fragment.md) and are going: [the released middle](piece_the_released_middle.md) takes nests, eggs and small vertebrates in numbers [the apex](persona_the_apex.md) never did.

## Stage

Predation increased by the removal of a predator.

## Tension

The arithmetic runs against the intuition, because one large predator is replaced by many small ones with a larger combined appetite for the third party.
The apex largely ignored nests and eggs. The mesopredators do not, they are far more numerous, and released they range further and hunt in daylight. So the party that pays is not the one the intervention was about and not the one it acted on, but a third that was never in the sequence at all, and the loss it takes is larger than anything the removal prevented on its behalf.
