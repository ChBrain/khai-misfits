# Persona: The Apex

## Taxonomy

[Apex](position_apex.md). The animal that was doing two things and was asked about one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A coyote, a dingo, a wolf. Takes stock, and also kills and harries every smaller predator in its range.

## Action

Takes lambs, which is counted.
Kills and displaces foxes, cats and raccoons, which is not.

## Shadow

Does the harm it is accused of, and the same appetite performs a service nobody has priced.
There is no exoneration to offer and none is wanted: the animal is guilty of exactly what the tally says. The point is that predation on stock and suppression of smaller predators are the same behaviour pointed at different bodies, so an intervention that ends the first ends the second, and the accounting that justified the intervention has a line for one and not the other. The service was never a favour, was never intended and was never contracted.

## Tell

Is removed for what it did, and takes with it what it was also doing.
