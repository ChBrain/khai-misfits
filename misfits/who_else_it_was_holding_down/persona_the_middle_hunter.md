# Persona: The Middle Hunter

## Taxonomy

[Middle Hunter](position_middle_hunter.md). The one who fills ground that opened.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A fox, a feral cat, a raccoon. Smaller, more numerous, and until now killed and harried by something bigger.

## Action

Ranges further, forages by day, breeds up.
Takes nests, eggs and small vertebrates in numbers the apex never did.

## Shadow

Is doing nothing but occupying ground, and nobody applied anything to it.
No agent was aimed here and no incentive was offered: the change is the removal of a thing that was pressing down, which is a subtraction rather than an intervention. The seat responds as any released population does, and its per-head take of small prey is higher than the apex's was, because it is smaller and there are far more of it. So the total predation on the third party rises when the top predator goes, which is arithmetic rather than misbehaviour.

## Tell

Rises because something stopped, not because anything started.
