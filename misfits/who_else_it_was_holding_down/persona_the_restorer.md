# Persona: The Restorer

## Taxonomy

[Restorer](position_restorer.md). The one who finds the reversal is not symmetric.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A proposal to return the apex predator to a range it held, and a literature that will not say plainly what will happen.

## Action

Argues for restoration.
Reads the evidence, and finds it mixed.

## Shadow

Proposes the obvious repair and cannot promise it works.
The move is right in principle and is what the structure implies, and the record is genuinely uneven: reviews find the mesopredator response matching prediction in roughly half the pairings examined, and one large fenced removal experiment found no mesopredator increase at all. The reasons are real, since productivity, other human pressure and which mesopredator is present all modulate it. So the seat is arguing for a repair whose effect is contested by people who are reading the same papers.

## Tell

Offers the reversal, and cannot say it reverses.
