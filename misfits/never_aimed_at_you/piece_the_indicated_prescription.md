# Piece: The Indicated Prescription

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md). The thing there is nothing wrong with: the right drug, the right dose, a patient who needed it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md), written against a guideline and defensible line by line.

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing the mass of prescriptions were inappropriate, and the play collapses into the ordinary account of resistance driven by misuse, which is a solved problem with a known remedy and not a misfit.
It bears the load as the reason nothing can be corrected. Because the prescriptions generating the bulk of the pressure are the appropriate ones, and would be written again by a clinician who understood the whole mechanism, there is no behaviour anywhere in the mass to name, reform or audit away.

## Apparent

Good medicine, and it is good medicine: the prescription a guideline recommends, an auditor passes and a colleague would have written.
