# Plan: Cover It Now

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the prescriber's seat](position_prescriber.md), inside [Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Prescriber](position_prescriber.md)

## Direction

Treat the patient who is present. Identify the likely organism from the presentation, and give the drug the guideline names for it, at the standard dose, without waiting for a culture that will take days to return. Do not narrow empirically before identification: narrowing on a guess kills patients, which is not a rhetorical point but the reason the practice exists, and a clinician who did it to reduce a population-level pressure would be trading a certain harm here for a diffuse one elsewhere. Form no view on what else the patient is carrying, because nothing in the consultation concerns it and no test at the point of care would name it. Write the prescription that a guideline would recommend, an auditor would pass and a colleague would have written, and be prepared to write the same one again with the entire mechanism laid out, because the alternative at this moment is not a narrower drug but no drug.

([The Prescriber](persona_the_prescriber.md) covers through [Covering](process_covering.md) at [the Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md), setting [the Indicated Prescription](piece_the_indicated_prescription.md) and [the Blind Hour](piece_the_blind_hour.md), and meets [the Stranger's Isolate](piece_the_strangers_isolate.md) through [Surfacing](process_surfacing.md) at [the Resistance Report](place_the_resistance_report.md).)

## Implementation

([The Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md) is where a correct decision is taken without the information that would narrow it; [Covering](process_covering.md) treats the likely organism now, and [Surfacing](process_surfacing.md) returns the consequence years later to a seat that cannot connect it to anything.)

## Targets

- [ ] the likely organism is identified from the presentation and the guideline drug is given
- [ ] the dose and duration are standard, and the decision is documented against the guideline
- [ ] no empiric narrowing is done ahead of identification, and the reason is recorded
- [ ] no view is formed on the rest of the patient's flora, since no test here would name it
- [ ] the prescription is one this seat would write again with the whole mechanism in front of it
