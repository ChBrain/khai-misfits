# Piece: The Organism Nobody Is Treating

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md). The exposure itself: a whole commensal community taking the dose, none of it causing anything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Flora](place_the_flora.md), in a throat, on skin and in a gut, and in nobody's mind at all (Dethlefsen and Relman).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing a drug reached only its target, and there is no bystander exposure and no play: the whole structure rests on the fact that a systemic agent cannot be told what it was prescribed for.
It bears the load as the point where the harm becomes invisible. Because the patient feels nothing, the clinician sees nothing, and no test at the point of care names it, the event that matters produces no evidence at the moment it happens, and the community does not return to its prior state afterwards.

## Apparent

Nothing at all, which is the difficulty: there is no symptom, no sign, no reading and no entry in any record.
