# Position: Steward

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md). The seat holding the one correction that was actually built.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Guidelines, an audit, a rate to improve, and the standing to challenge a prescription.
A programme that is funded, staffed and effective at what it does.
An instrument whose whole logic is appropriateness.

## Orders

Cut the ones that should not have been written: audit against the guidelines, challenge what fails, and improve the rate.
Report honestly that most prescribing is appropriate, because it is and that is a good finding.
Accept what the seat does: the work is necessary, the harms it prevents are real, and it has never claimed to have solved resistance.

## Loses

Reach, since the mass generating the pressure is appropriate and the instrument selects on appropriateness, so the intervention is aimed at the tail by construction.
Because widening it would mean removing prescriptions the guidelines require, the seat cannot extend its own remit without ceasing to be stewardship and starting to withhold indicated treatment.
It keeps a rising appropriateness rate and loses any claim on the bulk of what it was set up to address.

## Drives

Toward the seat doing genuine work on the fraction while the bulk is untouched,
auditing, challenging, improving the rate, and reporting a resistance curve that does not answer,
so the only correction anybody built is measuring the wrong denominator, through no fault of its own and with no better instrument available.
