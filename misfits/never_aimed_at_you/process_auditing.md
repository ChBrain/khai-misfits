# Process: Auditing

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md). Borne at [The Stewardship Round](place_the_stewardship_round.md), precipitated by [The Steward](persona_the_steward.md) testing prescriptions against the guidelines, in [Cut the Ones That Should Not Have Been Written](plot_cut_the_ones_that_should_not_have_been_written.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A funded, staffed programme with a guideline, an audit sample and the standing to challenge a prescription.

## Direction

From a body of prescribing toward the removal of what should not have been written, and toward an appropriateness rate that rises year on year.

## Lever

That the instrument selects on appropriateness, so it reaches the inappropriate tail by construction while the mass generating the pressure passes the audit, and widening it would mean removing prescriptions the guidelines require.

## Echo

[The Trimmed Tail](piece_the_trimmed_tail.md), genuine and necessary work aimed at the fraction, and a resistance curve that does not answer to it.
