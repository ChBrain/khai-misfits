# Plot: Count Where the Exposure Falls

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Model](place_the_model.md), where somebody stops counting prescriptions and starts counting exposure.

## Action

Through [Apportioning](process_apportioning.md), [the Epidemiologist](persona_the_epidemiologist.md) on [Count Where the Exposure Falls](plan_count_where_the_exposure_falls.md) sets [the Bystander Mass](piece_the_bystander_mass.md): the antibiotic exposure a species receives is split into the share generated by prescriptions aimed at it and the share generated by prescriptions aimed at something else, using prescribing data, carriage prevalence and the indications each drug is written for. The scene closes with the large majority in the second column for most species and most drugs, so the pressure that matters is produced by correct use and the standard account of resistance is pointed at the smaller share (Tedijanto, Olesen, Grad and Lipsitch).

## Stage

The method is stated before the headline and the assumptions are conceded at once. This is not a contrarian result and it offers nothing at all to anybody who would rather prescribe freely.

## Tension

The finding names a target and no culprit, because every prescription in the mass it identifies is one that should have been written, so the seat can say what the problem is and cannot say what anybody should have done differently.
