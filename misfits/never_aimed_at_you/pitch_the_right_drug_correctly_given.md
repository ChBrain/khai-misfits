# Pitch: The Right Drug, Correctly Given

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Calm and clinical, the tone of a decision nobody would query.
A patient with a real infection, a drug that covers the organism causing it, a dose that is standard, and a course that is completed as instructed.
Nothing false in it. This is the prescription a guideline would recommend, an auditor would pass and a colleague would have written.

## Undertow

Under the correct decision, a drug that does not know what it was prescribed for.
Everything in that person's flora takes the dose, none of it causing anything, and the community does not return to what it was afterwards (Dethlefsen and Relman).
Count the exposure across a population and most of the pressure on any given species turns out to come from prescriptions aimed at something else entirely (Tedijanto, Olesen, Grad and Lipsitch).

## Nerve

The correctness of the decision, and the catch that correctness is what generates the pressure:
that the alternative at the moment of decision is not a narrower drug but no drug, since identification takes days and narrowing empirically before the culture returns kills people,
and the right drug, correctly given is the sentence in which the bulk of the harm is written and nothing in it can be improved.

## Echo

After the run: an isolate years later in a person who never took the drug, with no feedback reaching the clinician who wrote it (Lipsitch and Samore).
And a correction aimed at the tail, since appropriateness stewardship removes what should not have been written and the mass was appropriate, so working harder at that job cannot reach the bulk.
And the bound held in view: the estimates are model-based and vary by species and drug, withdrawing the drug does not reliably restore susceptibility, and none of it argues against stewardship or against treating the patient (Andersson and Hughes).
