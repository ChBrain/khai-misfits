# Place: The Consulting Room

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md). Where a correct decision is taken without the information that would narrow it. Hosts [Cover It Now](plot_cover_it_now.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A patient present now, a presentation that names a likely organism, a guideline that names a drug, and a laboratory that will answer in days.

## Holds

[The Indicated Prescription](piece_the_indicated_prescription.md), the thing there is nothing wrong with, and [The Blind Hour](piece_the_blind_hour.md), the clinical fact that blocks the obvious remedy.

## Offers

The right treatment for the person in the room, at the moment they need it, which is the whole purpose of the encounter and is genuinely delivered.

## Withheld

Any identification in time to matter, since the culture takes days and the patient does not have days, so the room cannot aim and cannot wait, and both of those are facts about the world rather than the clinician.
