# Persona: The Steward

## Taxonomy

[Steward](position_steward.md). The one doing real work on the part that can be worked on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Methodical and quietly effective, arriving with a guideline, an audit and a rate to improve.
Reports honestly that most prescribing in the audit is appropriate, which is a good finding and is reported as one.
Is not claiming to have solved resistance and has never claimed it.

## Action

Runs [Cut the Ones That Should Not Have Been Written](plan_cut_the_ones_that_should_not_have_been_written.md): through [Auditing](process_auditing.md) at [the Stewardship Round](place_the_stewardship_round.md), meeting [the Trimmed Tail](piece_the_trimmed_tail.md).
Audits prescriptions against the guidelines and removes the ones that should not have been written.
Improves the appropriateness rate, genuinely, year on year.

## Shadow

Not a box-ticker and not a fig leaf: the work is necessary, the harms it prevents are real, and the seat would be missed if it stopped.
Because the instrument it holds is appropriateness and the mass generating the pressure is appropriate, the intervention is aimed at the tail by construction, and no amount of doing it better reaches the bulk.
It is the one correction that was actually built, funded and staffed, and it is measuring the wrong denominator through no fault of its own.

## Tell

Reports a rising appropriateness rate and a resistance curve that does not answer it.
Cannot widen its instrument without ceasing to be stewardship, since the prescriptions it would have to remove are the ones the guidelines require.
