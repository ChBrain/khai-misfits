# Piece: The Bystander Mass

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md). The finding: for most species and most drugs, the large majority of exposure comes from prescriptions aimed at something else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Model](place_the_model.md), in a split between exposure aimed at a species and exposure aimed elsewhere (Tedijanto, Olesen, Grad and Lipsitch).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play is an observation that antibiotics have collateral effects, which everybody already knows and which changes nothing about where effort should go.
It bears the load as the inversion the play is named against. Because the bulk of the pressure is generated by correct use aimed elsewhere, the standard account of resistance as a consequence of misuse is not merely incomplete but pointed at the smaller share, so the target moves without any culprit appearing.

## Apparent

A statistic, and one that will be quoted by both sides: as evidence that prescribing restraint is futile, and as evidence that it matters more than ever.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Count Where the Exposure Falls](plot_count_where_the_exposure_falls.md), where the ordinary account is inverted by a change of question.
It works together with [The Trimmed Tail](piece_the_trimmed_tail.md), the mass and the instrument that cannot reach it.
