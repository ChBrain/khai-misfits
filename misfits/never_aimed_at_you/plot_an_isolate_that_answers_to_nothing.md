# Plot: An Isolate That Answers to Nothing

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Resistance Report](place_the_resistance_report.md), years later and somewhere else, with a susceptibility list and very little left on it.

## Action

Through [Surfacing](process_surfacing.md), [the Prescriber](persona_the_prescriber.md) on [Cover It Now](plan_cover_it_now.md) meets [the Stranger's Isolate](piece_the_strangers_isolate.md): the patient carrying the organism never took the drug that selected it, and the prescriptions that generated the pressure were written for other people, for other infections, across other years. The scene closes with the decision and the damage sharing no patient, no clinician and no decade, and with nothing travelling back to any seat that could have acted differently (Lipsitch and Samore).

## Stage

It will be recorded as a difficult case: a resistant organism, an unlucky patient, and no decision in any note that anybody would take back. Each of those is true and none of them reaches the cause.

## Tension

No clinician can learn this from their own practice however carefully they attend to it, because the feedback that would teach it is distributed across thousands of correct decisions and arrives attached to none of them.
