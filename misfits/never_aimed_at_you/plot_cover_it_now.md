# Plot: Cover It Now

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md), with a patient present, a presentation that names a likely organism, and a laboratory that will answer in days.

## Action

Through [Covering](process_covering.md), [the Prescriber](persona_the_prescriber.md) on [Cover It Now](plan_cover_it_now.md) sets [the Indicated Prescription](piece_the_indicated_prescription.md) and [the Blind Hour](piece_the_blind_hour.md): the guideline drug for the likely organism, at the standard dose, written before any culture returns. No narrowing is attempted, because narrowing on a guess before identification kills patients, and that is why the practice exists rather than a point of rhetoric. The scene closes with the broad choice established as the correct decision under the information that exists, and with a clinician who would write exactly the same thing again with the entire mechanism laid out.

## Stage

This is the prescription a guideline recommends, an auditor passes and a colleague would have written. Nothing in it is hasty, nothing is generous, and the patient needed it.

## Tension

The alternative at this moment is not a narrower drug but no drug, so the remedy that would fix everything is blocked by a fact about the world rather than by a habit anybody could reform.
