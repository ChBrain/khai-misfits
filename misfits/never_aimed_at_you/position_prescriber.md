# Position: Prescriber

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md). The seat whose correct decision generates the pressure.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A patient present now, a likely organism, and a guideline that names the drug.
A laboratory that will identify the organism in days, and a clinical situation measured in hours.
A duty to the person in the room and no information at all about anything else they are carrying.

## Orders

Cover it now: give the drug that covers the likely organism, at the standard dose, before the culture returns.
Do not narrow empirically, because narrowing before identification kills patients and that is why the practice exists.
Accept what the seat does: this is the prescription a guideline recommends, an auditor passes and a colleague would have written.

## Loses

Any way to aim, since the drug is systemic and reaches everything the patient carries, and the seat cannot select what it cannot identify.
Because the harm surfaces years later in a different person, no feedback ever reaches this seat, so nothing in its own practice could teach it otherwise even in principle.
It keeps a record it can justify prescription by prescription and loses any account of what those prescriptions did.

## Drives

Toward the seat that would write the same thing again knowing everything,
naming the organism, covering it now, completing a correct consultation, and years later reading somebody else's report with nothing on it,
so the decision that generates the bulk of the pressure is the one there is no argument against.
