# Persona: The Prescriber

## Taxonomy

[Prescriber](position_prescriber.md). The one making the right call with the information that exists.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Unhurried and clear, naming the likely organism and the drug that covers it.
Would write the same prescription again with the entire mechanism laid out, and says so without defensiveness.
Has no view at all on the patient's throat flora, because nothing in the consultation is about it.

## Action

Runs [Cover It Now](plan_cover_it_now.md): through [Covering](process_covering.md) at [the Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md), setting [the Indicated Prescription](piece_the_indicated_prescription.md) and [the Blind Hour](piece_the_blind_hour.md), and through [Surfacing](process_surfacing.md) at [the Resistance Report](place_the_resistance_report.md), meeting [the Stranger's Isolate](piece_the_strangers_isolate.md).
Covers the likely organism now, because identification takes days and the patient is here.
Reads, years later and in another patient, a susceptibility report with nothing on it.

## Shadow

Not careless and not free with the prescription pad: this is the drug a guideline would recommend, an auditor would pass and a colleague would have written.
Because the alternative at the moment of decision is not a narrower drug but no drug, and narrowing empirically before the culture returns kills people, the seat has no better option and would not want one it could not identify with.
And the pressure it generates never comes back to it, so there is nothing in its own practice that could teach it otherwise.

## Tell

Can justify every prescription in its record and has never once been asked about the organism that paid for one.
Meets the consequence only as somebody else's report, about somebody else's patient, in a decade where it cannot be traced to any prescription at all.
