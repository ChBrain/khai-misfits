# Persona: The Epidemiologist

## Taxonomy

[Epidemiologist](position_epidemiologist.md). The one who apportions the exposure and finds the ordinary account inverted.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Precise and careful with what the numbers will carry, leading with the method rather than the headline.
Concedes at once that the estimate is model-based and varies by species and by drug.
Declines to convert the finding into a slogan in either direction.

## Action

Runs [Count Where the Exposure Falls](plan_count_where_the_exposure_falls.md): through [Apportioning](process_apportioning.md) at [the Model](place_the_model.md), setting [the Bystander Mass](piece_the_bystander_mass.md) and later meeting [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md).
Splits the exposure a species receives into the share aimed at it and the share aimed elsewhere.
Finds the second share is the large majority, for most species and most drugs.

## Shadow

Not a contrarian and not an apologist: the finding embarrasses the standard story about misuse and offers no comfort at all to anybody who would rather prescribe freely.
Because the pressure is generated by correct decisions, the seat can name what the problem is and cannot name a decision anybody should have taken differently.
And the honest statement it has to make, that the estimate is real and bounded and does not argue against stewardship, is harder to carry than either good news or bad.

## Tell

Produces a number that redirects the target and licenses no accusation.
Cannot hand anybody an action item, because every prescription in the mass it identified was one that should have been written.
