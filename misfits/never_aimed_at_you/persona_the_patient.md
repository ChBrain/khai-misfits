# Persona: The Patient

## Taxonomy

[Patient](position_patient.md). The one who does exactly as instructed and is where it all happens.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Cooperative and slightly anxious, wanting the infection gone and asking whether to finish the course.
Finishes it, because that is the instruction and has been for a lifetime.
Would be baffled to learn that anything in this concerns an organism that was not making them ill.

## Action

Runs [Finish the Course](plan_finish_the_course.md): through [Exposing](process_exposing.md) at [the Flora](place_the_flora.md), setting [the Organism Nobody Is Treating](piece_the_organism_nobody_is_treating.md).
Takes the drug as prescribed, so it reaches the infection and everything else being carried.
Recovers, correctly and as expected, and carries a changed community afterwards.

## Shadow

Not non-compliant and not over-demanding: this seat did what it was told, and a patient who stopped early would be criticised for it by the same system.
Because the drug is systemic and the flora is everywhere, the seat is not a decider at all but the place where the exposure happens, and there is no version of complying better that avoids it.
And the community does not return to what it was, so the effect outlasts the course that was correctly completed.

## Tell

Reports the infection gone, which it is, and reports nothing else, because there is nothing else to feel.
Carries the consequence out of the building without ever being told it exists, and could not have been told anything actionable if they had been.
