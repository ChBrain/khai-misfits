# Place: The Stewardship Round

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md). Where appropriateness is tested, improved, and turns out to be the wrong denominator. Hosts [Trimming the Tail](plot_trimming_the_tail.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A guideline, an audit sample, a list of challenges raised, and an appropriateness rate that has risen every year the programme has run.

## Holds

[The Trimmed Tail](piece_the_trimmed_tail.md), real work aimed at the fraction that fails the audit.

## Offers

Genuine prevention: the antibiotic written for a viral illness, the course that ran too long, the broad agent used where a narrow one was identified, all removed, and each of those is a real harm avoided.

## Withheld

Any reach into the mass, because the instrument selects on appropriateness and the mass passes, and widening it would mean removing prescriptions the guidelines require, which would stop this being stewardship at all.
