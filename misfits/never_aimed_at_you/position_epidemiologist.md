# Position: Epidemiologist

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md). The seat that finds the target and cannot name a culprit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Prescribing data, carriage data, and a way to apportion exposure between the organism aimed at and the rest.
A model, with assumptions it can state and ranges it can give.
A standard account of resistance, driven by misuse, that its own numbers do not support.

## Orders

Count where the exposure falls: split what a species receives into the share aimed at it and the share aimed elsewhere.
Report the split honestly, including that it is model-based and varies by species and by drug.
Accept what the seat does: the finding redirects the target and licenses no accusation against anybody.

## Loses

The action item, since every prescription in the mass it has identified is one that should have been written, so there is no behaviour to name and nobody to ask to stop.
Because the result embarrasses the standard story while offering nothing to anybody who would rather prescribe freely, it satisfies neither side and will be quoted by both.
It keeps a correct and careful number and loses any recommendation that follows straightforwardly from it.

## Drives

Toward the seat that inverts the ordinary account and hands nobody an instruction,
splitting the exposure, finding the majority aimed elsewhere, stating the bound, and declining the slogan,
so the discovery that most of the pressure comes from correct use is a redirection of effort rather than a finding of fault.
