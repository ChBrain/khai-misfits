# Plan: Cut the Ones That Should Not Have Been Written

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), inside [Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Steward](position_steward.md)

## Direction

Audit prescribing against the guidelines, challenge what fails, and improve the appropriateness rate. Remove the antibiotic written for a viral illness, the course that ran too long, the broad agent used where a narrow one was indicated and identified. This is genuine work, the harms it prevents are real, and the programme would be missed if it stopped. Report the audit honestly, including the finding that most prescribing is appropriate, which is a good result and should be stated as one rather than buried. Hold in view what the instrument is: it selects on appropriateness, so it reaches the inappropriate tail by construction, and the mass generating the selection pressure is appropriate. Do not widen the remit to cover that mass, because the prescriptions in it are the ones the guidelines require, and a stewardship programme that started removing indicated treatment would have stopped being stewardship. Claim no more than the programme does, and never that resistance has been addressed.

## Orders

([The Steward](persona_the_steward.md) audits through [Auditing](process_auditing.md) at [the Stewardship Round](place_the_stewardship_round.md), meeting [the Trimmed Tail](piece_the_trimmed_tail.md).)

## Implementation

([The Stewardship Round](place_the_stewardship_round.md) is where appropriateness is tested and improved; [Auditing](process_auditing.md) reaches the tail by construction and leaves the mass, because the mass passes.)

## Targets

- [ ] prescriptions are audited against the guidelines and failures are challenged
- [ ] the appropriateness rate is measured and improved year on year
- [ ] the finding that most prescribing is appropriate is reported plainly as a good result
- [ ] the remit is not widened to prescriptions the guidelines require
- [ ] no claim is made that the programme addresses the bulk of the selection pressure
