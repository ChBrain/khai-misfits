# Plan: Count Where the Exposure Falls

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the epidemiologist's seat](position_epidemiologist.md), inside [Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Epidemiologist](position_epidemiologist.md)

## Direction

Stop counting prescriptions and start counting exposure. For a given species, split the antibiotic exposure it receives into the share generated by prescriptions aimed at it and the share generated by prescriptions aimed at something else, using prescribing data, carriage prevalence and the indications each drug is written for. Report the split as it comes out, which for most species and most drugs puts the large majority in the second category. State the method before the headline and concede at once what the estimate rests on: it is model-based, it depends on carriage assumptions, and it varies by species and by drug. Then say plainly what does and does not follow. It redirects the target, because the pressure is generated by correct use. It licenses no accusation, because every prescription in the mass is one that should have been written. And it argues against neither stewardship nor treatment, since the source literature says neither and the number will be quoted by people who want it to say both.
