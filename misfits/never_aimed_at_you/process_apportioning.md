# Process: Apportioning

## Taxonomy

[Never Aimed at You](play_never_aimed_at_you.md). Borne at [The Model](place_the_model.md), precipitated by [The Epidemiologist](persona_the_epidemiologist.md) counting exposure rather than prescriptions, in [Counting Exposure, Not Scripts](plot_counting_exposure_not_scripts.md) and [What the Estimate Carries](plot_what_the_estimate_carries.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A standard account of resistance driven by misuse, meeting a question nobody had asked: where does the exposure actually fall.

## Direction

From a count of prescriptions toward an attribution of exposure, split per species between the share aimed at it and the share aimed at something else.

## Lever

That for most species and most drugs the large majority lands in the second column, so the pressure that matters is generated by correct use and the standard account is inverted (Tedijanto, Olesen, Grad and Lipsitch).

## Echo

[The Bystander Mass](piece_the_bystander_mass.md), a target named without a culprit, and [The Bound](piece_the_bound.md), what the estimate rests on and what it does not license.
