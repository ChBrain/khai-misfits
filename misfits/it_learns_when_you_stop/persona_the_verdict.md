# Persona: The Verdict

## Taxonomy

[Diagnosis](position_diagnosis.md). Stubborn: the explanation that fits every observation and is not the explanation.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Reasonable, and evidentially sound.
The horse ignores the aids, resists more the firmer the ask, and does nothing when asked politely. That is exactly what stubborn looks like.
Arrived at honestly by somebody weighing what they have seen.

## Action

Explains everything.
Predicts correctly: firmer asks will produce more resistance, which they do.
Recommends more of what caused it.

## Shadow

That it is consistent with the whole record and wrong about all of it.
Because a punished try produces precisely the behaviour an unwilling horse would produce, the evidence cannot separate them from inside, and the diagnosis that fits is the one that closes the loop rather than opens it.
It ends confirmed by every subsequent ride.

## Tell

Says: this one has always been difficult.
True as a description, false as a cause, and impossible to distinguish without knowing what happened to the tries.
