# Persona: The Release

## Taxonomy

[Reinforcer](position_reinforcer.md). The removal of the pressure: the whole of what the horse learns from, and the rider's own cessation.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The entire lesson, in an event that looks like nothing.
Not a reward given but a demand withdrawn, which is why it is so easy to overlook as an act.
The only thing in the exchange that says yes.

## Action

Arrives, or does not.
Marks whatever the horse was doing at the instant it happens.
Teaches that, precisely, whether or not that is what anybody wanted.

## Shadow

That it is made of the rider's own restraint, which is what puts it out of reach at the moment it is needed.
Because it consists of stopping rather than doing, a rider who has resolved to try harder is by that resolution withholding the only instruction available.
It ends late, or absent, having been the whole of the teaching.

## Tell

Says nothing at all.
It is the absence of the thing that was there a moment ago, and it is the only sentence the horse can read.
