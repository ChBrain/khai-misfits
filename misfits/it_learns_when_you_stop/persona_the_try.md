# Persona: The Try

## Taxonomy

[Attempt](position_attempt.md). The horse's first offer: a shift of weight, a fractional softening, the beginning of the thing asked for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Small, and correct.
Not the movement, the shift before the movement: the horse working out what is wanted and starting to give it.
Exactly the thing a rider should be waiting for, and easy to miss entirely.

## Action

Offers.
Waits to learn what happens next.
Repeats, or stops repeating, according to what it finds.

## Shadow

That it arrives inside the firmer ask, which is where a rider is least able to notice it.
Because it appears before the movement rather than as the movement, a rider watching for the answer sees nothing yet and keeps asking, so what follows the offer is pressure.
It ends punished, and then it ends rarer.

## Tell

Says nothing anybody is listening for.
It is a quarter of an inch and a softening, and it was the answer.
