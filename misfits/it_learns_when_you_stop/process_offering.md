# Process: Offering

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The horse trying: the answer arriving in its first and smallest instalment.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A question the horse has understood well enough to begin answering.
At [the contact](place_the_contact.md), before any movement is visible.

## Direction

Toward the thing that was asked for, starting at the beginning of it.
A horse does not produce the finished answer whole: it shifts its weight, softens a fraction, starts. That start is [the shift before the movement](piece_the_shift_before_the_movement.md), and it is correct.
The rate at which the horse repeats this is set entirely by what it finds waiting on the other side.

## Lever

Whatever follows the offer, which the horse reads as a verdict and reads accurately.
Release following an offer makes offers more frequent; pressure following an offer makes them rarer. Nothing about that is the horse's error, and nothing about it can be overridden by intention.

## Echo

A quarter of an inch that was the answer,
counted, by the only party keeping the count.
