# Pitch: Half a Pound, Now, There

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Close, physical, quiet.
The register of a lesson given from the middle of an arena: half a pound, now, there, that was it.
Nothing scolding anywhere in it, because the rider is trying and the horse is not being difficult.

## Undertow

Under the contact, two facts that do not negotiate.
The pressure asks and the release teaches. A horse's first offer is small enough to be missed.
Between those two, the harder somebody asks the more certainly they punish the answer.

## Nerve

The unease of doing the obvious reasonable thing,
asking again and meaning it,
and finding out years later that this was the one move guaranteeing the answer would never come.

## Echo

After the run: a rider who tried hard,
a horse that has stopped offering anything,
and a word, stubborn, that fits every single thing either of them did.
