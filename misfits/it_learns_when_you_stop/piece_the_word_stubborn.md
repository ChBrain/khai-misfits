# Piece: The Word Stubborn

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The explanation that fits every observation, is what a reasonable person concludes, and is wrong.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the rider's account of the horse, reached at [the arena](place_the_arena.md) after months.
Held by [the verdict](persona_the_verdict.md), on the evidence.

## Load Bearing

Without it the rider notices the trouble and looks for a cause, and looking would eventually find one.
It is load bearing because it **closes the enquiry**: one word covers the ignored aids, the resistance that grows with firmness and the nothing that follows a polite ask, so there is no residue left over to want explaining.

## Apparent

A judgement about the horse's character, and therefore unfair to the horse.
Unfairness is the small half. The large half is that it is **consistent with the evidence**, which is what makes it durable, and acting on it produces more of the behaviour it predicts.

## Yearbook

Spoken at [the arena](place_the_arena.md), confirmed by [escalating](process_escalating.md).
It is the piece that makes the trap self-sealing: the wrong explanation is never disconfirmed, because following it manufactures its own evidence.
