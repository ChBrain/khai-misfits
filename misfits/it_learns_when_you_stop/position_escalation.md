# Position: Escalation

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The seat of asking again and meaning it: the reasonable move when a question goes unanswered.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An unanswered question and a duty to make it clearer.
Every instinct a person has about being ignored.
Instruction, from good teachers, to be firm rather than nagging.

## Orders

Notice that the answer has not come.
Increase.
Hold, until it does.

## Loses

Nothing it can see, and that is the hinge of the whole production.
Because firmness is expressed as **holding**, and the horse's offer arrives inside the holding, the more committed this seat is to being clear the more reliably it is still applying pressure at the instant the try appears.
It ends having punished the answer it was asking for, by doing exactly what it was told to do.

## Drives

Toward a question the horse cannot mistake,
by the one method available for making a question firmer,
so the seat that tries hardest is the seat that does the damage.
