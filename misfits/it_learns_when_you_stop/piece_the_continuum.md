# Piece: The Continuum

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). Poorly timed negative reinforcement running into punishment, with no step between them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Along [the contact](place_the_contact.md), in the same act the rider experiences as being firm.
Described in the equitation-science literature as a continuum rather than as two categories.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a lesson about being late, and lateness is a skill problem with a training answer.
It is load bearing because it makes the two things **the same act at different timings**: there is no moment at which correcting stops and punishing starts, so a rider cannot detect the crossing by attending harder to what they are doing.

## Apparent

A matter of degree, and therefore something a considerate rider stays on the safe side of.
There is no safe side. The position on the continuum is set by when the horse offers, which the rider does not know, so it is not a dial anybody is holding.

## Yearbook

Named in the study of how horses are trained, enacted at [the contact](place_the_contact.md).
Its far end has a name of its own, and the name is learned helplessness.
