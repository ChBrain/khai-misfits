# Piece: The Shift Before the Movement

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The try, in its physical form: a redistribution of weight, a softening, arriving ahead of anything visible.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the contact](place_the_contact.md) and under the seat, a fraction of a second before the transition.
Offered by [the try](persona_the_try.md), unprompted, as soon as the question is understood.

## Load Bearing

Without it a rider could simply release on the answer and the play would close.
It is load bearing because the answer **has a beginning that is not the answer**: the horse starts before it moves, so the correct instant to release is one at which the thing asked for is still absent, and releasing then feels like giving up.

## Apparent

Too small to be the point, and therefore something to wait past.
Waiting past it is the fault. It is the whole of what the horse has to offer at that moment, and it is exactly what a rider should be marking.

## Yearbook

Felt rather than seen, taught at [the long apprenticeship](place_the_long_apprenticeship.md).
It is the reason the settlement is expensive: the remedy is aimed at a thing that has not happened yet.
