# Place: The Long Apprenticeship

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The years it takes to feel a shift before it becomes a movement: the settlement's price, paid in time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The same exercise, thousands of times, with somebody on the ground saying now, and there, and no, that was late.
A rider slowly acquiring a perception they did not have.

## Holds

The reason the remedy does not close the play.
Releasing at the try works and is measured. It requires feeling something that has not happened yet, and no amount of understanding the mechanism shortens the acquisition, because what is being learned is a perception rather than a fact.

## Offers

A real answer, for those who get there: lower rein tension, fewer conflict behaviours, a horse that keeps offering.

## Withheld

Any way to arrive early.
Knowing that the release teaches does not let a rider feel the try, and a rider who knows the whole mechanism and cannot yet feel it will hold through the try exactly as before, now with an explanation.
