# Persona: The Pressure

## Taxonomy

[Signal](position_signal.md). The rein and the leg: a question and a motivation, and no part of the answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Legitimate, and necessary.
Tells the horse what is wanted and supplies a reason to answer, which is what a signal is for.
Not cruelty and not a shortcut: a rider who never asks teaches nothing at all.

## Action

Asks.
Motivates.
Continues, for as long as the answer has not arrived.

## Shadow

Nothing while it is a question, and everything once it outlasts the answer.
Because it carries no instructional content of its own, more of it conveys nothing more, and its only teaching effect is the one it has by **ending**.
It ends held too long, having asked correctly and taught the opposite.

## Tell

Says: slow down.
Clearly, and it will go on saying it, which is the problem.
