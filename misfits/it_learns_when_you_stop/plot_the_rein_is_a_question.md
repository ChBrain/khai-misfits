# Plot: The Rein Is a Question

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Arena](place_the_arena.md), an ordinary lesson.
[The Contact](place_the_contact.md), taken up between hand and mouth.

## Action

[Asking](process_asking.md): [The Pressure](persona_the_pressure.md), in [the signal seat](position_signal.md), tells the horse what is wanted at [the contact](place_the_contact.md) and supplies a reason to answer.

## Stage

[The Pressure](persona_the_pressure.md), on [Make the Question Unmistakable](plan_make_the_question_unmistakable.md), is clear, motivating, and entirely legitimate.

## Tension

The asking is right, and the play has to establish that before it can be the problem.
The rein and the leg are signals. They say what is wanted and they give the horse a reason to answer, which is what signals are for. **A rider who never asks teaches nothing at all**, and the production never argues for a horse left alone.
So the trap cannot be sprung by finding the pressure cruel or the rider heavy-handed. It has to be sprung with a fair question, clearly put, by somebody who wants the horse to succeed.
