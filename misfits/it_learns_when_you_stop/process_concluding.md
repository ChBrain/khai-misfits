# Process: Concluding

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The rider explaining the horse: ordinary reasoning from accurate observation, reaching the wrong place.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A pattern that has repeated often enough to want explaining.
At [the arena](place_the_arena.md), over months.

## Direction

Toward [the word stubborn](piece_the_word_stubborn.md).
The observations are real: the horse ignores the aids, resists more the firmer the ask, and does nothing when asked politely. One word covers all three, it is available, and it is what a reasonable person concludes.
The competing account requires knowing that the reinforcer is the release, and that the horse's try was met with pressure months ago, neither of which is visible from here.

## Lever

Consistency with the evidence, which the wrong explanation has in full.
And acting on it produces more of the behaviour it predicts, so the conclusion is confirmed by its own consequences and never has to survive a test it could fail.

## Echo

A horse that has stopped offering anything at all,
which is called stubbornness, and is a correct reading of its own record.
