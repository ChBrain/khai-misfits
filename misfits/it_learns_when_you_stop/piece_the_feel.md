# Piece: The Feel

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The perception the settlement requires: knowing the try has started before it shows.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the rider's hands and seat, or not there.
Acquired only at [the long apprenticeship](place_the_long_apprenticeship.md), over years, with somebody on the ground.

## Load Bearing

Without it the mechanism would be closed by explaining it, and explanation is cheap.
It is load bearing because what stands between a rider and the remedy is **a perception and not a fact**: understanding the whole play changes nothing about whether the hand knows when to open.

## Apparent

Mystique, the kind of thing horse people say instead of explaining.
It is not mystique. It is a discrimination between two states of a few ounces, learnable and slow, and the slowness is the point rather than the mystery.

## Yearbook

Taught by repetition and correction, spent at [the contact](place_the_contact.md).
It is why a rider can know exactly what they are doing wrong and go on doing it.
