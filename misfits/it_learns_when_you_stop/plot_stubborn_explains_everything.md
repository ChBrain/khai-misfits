## Tension

The diagnosis is consistent with the whole record and wrong about all of it.
The horse ignores the aids, resists more the firmer the ask, and does nothing when asked politely. **That is exactly what stubborn looks like**, and it is what a reasonable person weighing the evidence concludes.
It is also exactly what a punished try looks like, and **the evidence cannot separate them from inside**. Both accounts predict the same rides. The difference is not visible in the horse's behaviour but in the history of what happened to its offers, which nobody recorded because nobody knew to watch for them.
So the explanation that fits is the one that closes the loop: it recommends firmness, firmness holds through more tries, and every subsequent ride confirms it.
