# Plan: Make the Question Unmistakable

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the signal seat](position_signal.md), inside [It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md).

## Owner

## Direction

Put the question in the language the horse reads, with enough behind it that answering matters, and get it right, because a rider who never asks teaches nothing at all.

## Orders

([The Pressure](persona_the_pressure.md) asks at [the contact](place_the_contact.md), clearly, and motivates the answer.)

## Implementation

([The Contact](place_the_contact.md), a few ounces; the [asking](process_asking.md) process, taught and practised; the rein and the leg, carrying question and motivation and no part of the answer.)
