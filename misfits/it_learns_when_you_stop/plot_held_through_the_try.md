# Plot: Held Through the Try

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Try](persona_the_try.md), a shift of weight inside a firmer ask.
[The Continuum](piece_the_continuum.md), with no boundary anybody can feel.

## Action

[Escalating](process_escalating.md): [The Firmer Ask](persona_the_firmer_ask.md), in [the escalation seat](position_escalation.md), increases and holds the pressure at [the contact](place_the_contact.md), waiting for the movement.

## Stage

[The Try](persona_the_try.md), on [Offer Something and See](plan_offer_something_and_see.md), arrives before the movement, is met by more pressure, and learns what that means.

## Tension

This is where the harm lands, and it lands on the correct answer.
The horse offers: a shift of weight, a fractional softening, the beginning of the thing that was asked for. If the pressure is still there when the offer comes, then **what followed the offer was pressure**. The try was punished, and the horse makes fewer of them.
And there is no line the rider can feel. The literature describes a **continuum** running from poorly timed negative reinforcement into punishment, and nothing marks the crossing: the rider experiences one continuous act of being firm. Its far end is a horse that has stopped offering anything at all, which is not obstinacy but the correct conclusion from the evidence it was given.
