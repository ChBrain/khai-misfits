# Persona: The Firmer Ask

## Taxonomy

[Escalation](position_escalation.md). Asking again and meaning it: the reasonable response to an unanswered question, and the one that holds through the answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Reasonable, and universal.
It is what any person does when a thing does not happen: ask again, more clearly, with more insistence.
Not temper and not force for its own sake: resolve.

## Action

Increases the pressure.
Holds it, because holding is what firmness consists of.
Waits for the movement, which is the wrong thing to be waiting for.

## Shadow

The try, arriving somewhere inside the holding and being met by more of it.
Because firmness is defined by continuing, this seat cannot be occupied and vacated at the same instant, and the instant it would need to vacate is the one nobody has yet learned to feel.
It ends having punished the answer it was asking for.

## Tell

Says: I mean it.
Sincerely, and the horse has just been told that offering was a mistake.
