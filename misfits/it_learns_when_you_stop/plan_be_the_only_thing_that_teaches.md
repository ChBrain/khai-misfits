# Plan: Be the Only Thing That Teaches

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the reinforcer seat](position_reinforcer.md), inside [It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reinforcer](position_reinforcer.md)

## Direction

Arrive at the instant the horse was right, and say yes by ceasing. Carry the entire instructional content of the exchange, in a moment that does not come back.

## Orders

([The Release](persona_the_release.md) removes the pressure at [the contact](place_the_contact.md), and that removal is what the horse learns from.)

## Implementation

([The Contact](place_the_contact.md), in an instant; the [releasing](process_releasing.md) process, which is cessation rather than action; nothing visible, because an absence cannot be watched.)

## Targets

- [x] the reinforcing event is the removal of the pressure and not its application
- [x] the lesson is dated by when this happens and not by how well the question was put
- [ ] it cannot be rehearsed as an action, being made of not doing
- [ ] a mistimed yes still teaches something
