# Position: Attempt

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The seat of the horse's first offer: a shift, a softening, the beginning of the answer and not yet the answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The correct response to the question, in its earliest form.
A size of about a quarter of an inch.
Nothing to announce it, because it happens before the movement it precedes.

## Orders

Work out what is wanted.
Offer a little of it.
Watch what follows, and adjust the rate accordingly.

## Loses

Everything, and it loses it by being right too early.
Because it appears before the movement rather than as the movement, a rider waiting for the answer sees nothing yet and keeps asking, so what follows the offer is pressure, and pressure following an offer is a verdict on the offer.
It ends punished, and then it ends rare, and a horse that has stopped offering is reading its own record correctly.

## Drives

Toward answering the question,
in the only increment it has to give,
so the seat that holds the right answer is the seat that gets corrected for it.
