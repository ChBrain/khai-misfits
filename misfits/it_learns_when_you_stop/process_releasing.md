# Process: Releasing

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The aid coming off: the answer being marked, which is the only teaching that happens.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The rider deciding the horse has done enough.
At [the contact](place_the_contact.md), in an instant that does not come back.

## Direction

Toward telling the horse it was right.
In pressure-release work the reinforcing event is the **removal** of the pressure and not its application, so this process carries the entire instructional content of the exchange and everything before it is only the question.
Which means the lesson is dated by when this happens, not by how well the question was put.

## Lever

Cessation, and its timing.
Because the lever is the rider stopping rather than the rider doing, it cannot be rehearsed as an action, and a rider watching their own hands is watching the wrong process.

## Echo

There, that was it,
said by nothing happening, at a moment already gone.
