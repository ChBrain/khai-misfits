# Position: Reinforcer

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The seat of the release: the removal of the pressure, which is the whole of what the horse learns from.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The entire instructional content of the exchange.
A single moment, unrepeatable, with no way to correct it afterwards.
No presence of its own: it is an absence, and absences are hard to aim.

## Orders

Arrive.
Arrive at the right instant.
Say yes, and nothing else.

## Loses

Its own visibility, because a rider watching their hands is watching what they are doing rather than what they have stopped doing.
Since it consists of the rider ceasing to act, it cannot be practised the way an aid can be practised, and it is felt by the horse in a moment the rider has already moved past.
It ends mistimed far more often than it ends absent, which is worse, because a mistimed yes teaches something.

## Drives

Toward telling the horse it was right,
which is the only way a horse can be told,
so the seat that does all the teaching is the seat made entirely of not doing.
