# Process: Asking

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The aid going on: a question put in the only language available, and correctly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A rider wanting something from a horse.
At [the contact](place_the_contact.md), a few ounces at a time.

## Direction

Toward a question the horse can read.
The rein and the leg carry both halves of a signal: what is wanted, and a reason to answer. Neither half is a fault, and the production establishes this before it establishes anything else, because a play that begins by blaming the ask has nothing left to say about the release.
This process is the part of riding that is taught, watched, corrected and practised.

## Lever

Pressure, applied and sustained.
It is the only instrument the rider has for making a question, and it works: horses answer questions put this way, every day, everywhere.

## Echo

Half a pound, and a horse that knows it has been asked something,
which is the whole of what asking achieves.
