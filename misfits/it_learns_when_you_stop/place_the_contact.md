# Place: The Contact

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The few ounces between hand and mouth: where the question, the answer and the verdict all happen, in the same place and within a second.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Rein tension, rising and falling.
A horse's jaw, a rider's fingers, and a quantity that can be measured and has been.

## Holds

Everything the production turns on, compressed into one channel.
The ask goes down it, the try comes up it, and the release travels down it again, so there is no separate place to look for any of the three: **the instruction and the punishment occupy the same wire.**

## Offers

The only language available, and it is a real one: horses answer questions put this way every day, and the play never suggests otherwise.

## Withheld

Any boundary a rider can feel.
Firm correction and punishment are the same act at different timings, and timing is not something the hand reports back, so the crossing happens here and leaves no mark here.
