# Plot: Only the Stopping Teaches

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Release](persona_the_release.md), arriving as an absence.
The horse, reading it.

## Action

[Releasing](process_releasing.md): [The Release](persona_the_release.md), in [the reinforcer seat](position_reinforcer.md), removes the pressure and marks whatever the horse was doing at that instant.

## Stage

[The Release](persona_the_release.md), on [Be the Only Thing That Teaches](plan_be_the_only_thing_that_teaches.md), says yes, and is the only thing in the exchange that can.

## Tension

The inversion is stated here and everything else follows from it.
**The pressure says ask and its removal says yes.** So the whole instructional content of the exchange lives in the moment the rider stops, and everything before that moment is only the question, however well or badly it is put.
Which means the teaching is made of the rider's **restraint** rather than their effort. More pressure conveys nothing more, because pressure carries no instruction of its own: its only teaching effect is the one it has by ending. **A rider who resolves to try harder is, by that resolution, withholding the single instruction available to them**, and nothing about trying harder feels like withholding anything.
