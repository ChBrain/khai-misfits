# Position: Signal

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). The seat of the ask: the rein and the leg, carrying the question and the motivation and none of the answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A clear question, put in a language the horse can read.
Enough motivation behind it that answering matters.
The rider's full attention, because this is the part a rider is taught to get right.

## Orders

Ask.
Be unmistakable.
Be strong enough to be worth answering.

## Loses

Nothing, and this seat is where the play gives the rider their due: **there is nothing wrong with asking.**
A rider who never asks teaches nothing at all, so the ask is not the fault and cannot be made the fault; it is a legitimate signal doing legitimate work.
It ends correct, and insufficient, because being right about the question says nothing about the answer.

## Drives

Toward making the question clear,
which is what every lesson concentrates on,
so the seat that gets the attention is the seat that carries none of the teaching.
