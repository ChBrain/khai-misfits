# Process: Escalating

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md). Asking again and meaning it: the reasonable move, and the mechanism.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An answer that has not come.
At [the contact](place_the_contact.md), immediately and without deliberation.

## Direction

Toward a question the horse cannot mistake.
There is only one way to make a sustained signal firmer, which is to increase it and to keep it there, so firmness and **holding** are the same act described twice.
And [offering](process_offering.md) happens inside that holding, which puts the horse's correct answer and the rider's increased pressure in the same instant by construction.

## Lever

More, for longer.
It is the move any reasonable person makes when a thing does not happen, it is what good teachers tell riders to do rather than nag, and it is right about the question and fatal to the answer.

## Echo

Pressure still present when the try arrived,
so the try was answered, and the answer was no.
