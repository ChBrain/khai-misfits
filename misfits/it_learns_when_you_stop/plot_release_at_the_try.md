# Plot: Release at the Try

## Taxonomy

[It Learns When You Stop](play_it_learns_when_you_stop.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Feel](piece_the_feel.md), which takes years.
[The Lower Tension](piece_the_lower_tension.md), which is what it buys.

## Action

[Offering](process_offering.md) is met, this time, by a release timed to the shift rather than to the movement, at [the long apprenticeship](place_the_long_apprenticeship.md).

## Stage

[The Try](persona_the_try.md) is answered at the moment it appears, and repeats, because what followed it was the pressure ending.

## Tension

The settlement works, is measured, and cannot be hurried.
Releasing at the horse's first attempt rather than at the completed movement produces lower rein tension and fewer conflict behaviours, which is not a claim about feel but a measurement.
What it costs is the thing no willingness supplies. The rider must feel **the shift before the movement**, a thing that has not happened yet, and must give up the pressure at the instant the thing asked for is still absent. **They have to stop asking exactly when they most want to keep asking**, and against an instinct that is correct almost everywhere else in life.
So the remedy is real and is not available on demand: it is a perceptual skill acquired over years, and no amount of wanting it, caring about the horse, or trying harder shortens the acquisition.
