# Plot: The Courtship

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Field](place_the_field.md), thick with comers, before a single spade goes into the ground. Nothing here is broken and nothing is owed: this is what a live market looks like from the inside.

## Action

[The Rival](persona_the_rival.md), holding [the rival's seat](position_rival.md), runs [Bid for the Deal](plan_bid_for_the_deal.md) alongside every other bidder crowding the same offer, and that crowd is what keeps [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) free to choose and the terms at [The Table](place_the_table.md) honest, while [The Investment](piece_the_investment.md) is still only a proposal on a page.

## Stage

Several suppliers price the same job, several buyers price the same output, and no single pair is stuck with the other: whoever names the sharpest terms wins the round, and everyone passed over stands ready to take the deal if the winner reaches too far. The Investor weighs offers the way anyone weighs a market with options in it, unhurried, since leaving one table for another still costs nothing. Whatever gets signed here is set under the discipline of everyone who could have signed instead.

## Tension

The contract about to be written can only be as good as what it can name in advance, and it is about to meet the one thing it cannot: what happens to these same terms once the field that priced them fairly is gone.
