# Position: Owner

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). The seat of the remedy: the party who brings both sides under one ownership so there is no separate counterparty left to hold up, removing the exposure at the cost of the market's sharp incentives.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A lever on the boundary of the firm: the power to change who owns what, buying the supplier or the buyer so the specific asset and the party that needs it sit inside one ownership.
A real answer: with no second party across a table, there is no one to reopen the terms, so the specific investment can be sunk without a squeeze to fear.
No costless version of it, because enclosing the deal replaces the market's sharp incentives with the firm's dulled ones and its oversight, and reassigning ownership secures one incentive only by weakening another.

## Orders

Own both sides.
Where the specificity is worth it, move the boundary of the firm to enclose the deal, buying the counterparty so the hold-up has no separate party to run through.
Accept the price: the dulled incentives of hierarchy, the cost of oversight, and the fact that ownership can protect one party's keenness to invest only by lowering the other's.

## Loses

The market's discipline, traded for the firm's.
Because integration removes the counterparty by removing the counterparty's independence, the seat gives up the sharp incentives the market kept and takes on the softer, costlier discipline of hierarchy, and cannot make everyone secure and keen at once.
It loses the price of the market it enclosed and gains a hold-up removed, one distortion answered with another.

## Drives

Toward the remedy that relocates the tension rather than dissolving it,
enclosing the specific deal at the boundary of the firm and paying for the enclosure in incentive and oversight,
so the seat that removes the hold-up is the seat that trades the sharp discipline of the market for the dulled discipline of the roof it brings both under.
