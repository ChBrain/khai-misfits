# Persona: The Investor

## Taxonomy

[Investor](position_investor.md). The party whose relationship-specific investment would create the most value, and who, foreseeing that once it is sunk the surplus can be taken, rationally builds general or less rather than expose itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Capable, willing, clear-eyed.
Is the party who could create the most value by fitting itself tightly to this one relationship: building the plant beside the factory, cutting the tool to the spec, learning the craft the firm needs.
No timidity in it and no greed: it would gladly make the specific investment if it could keep its share of what that investment creates, and it can see, plainly, why it might not.

## Action

Guards the exit on [Guard the Exit](plan_guard_the_exit.md), through [Withholding](process_withholding.md) at [the Site](place_the_site.md), building general rather than specific, or less, rather than sink an asset it could then be squeezed on.
Cannot make the fit tight and keep itself safe: the specificity that would create the value is the same specificity that strips its outside option, so to protect its share it must give up the very investment that would have created the most.
Does what a clear-eyed party does facing a hold-up it can foresee: it declines the specific investment, and the largest gains go unmade.

## Shadow

Not a coward for holding back and not a fool if it does not: the Investor would create the most value by sinking the specific asset, and its refusal is the correct reading of what would happen if it did.
Because the value and the vulnerability are the same tight fit, the Investor cannot separate them, so the party best able to create the surplus is the party the surplus most exposes, and its rational caution is exactly what leaves the best deal undone.
It is the one who could have built the most, and the one the prospect of the squeeze keeps from building it.

## Tell

Says the general plant is worth less than the specific one, and builds it anyway, because the specific one it could be squeezed on.
Cannot be blamed for the retreat, because the squeeze it foresees is real and within the rules, so it protects its share soundly and takes the smaller, safer deal, the larger one never made.
