# Place: The Field

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). The competing alternatives, crowded before the investment and empty after it. Hosts [No Other Buyer](plot_no_other_buyer.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Before the sinking, a field thick with rivals: other buyers who would take the deal, other suppliers who would take the contract, each a live bid holding the terms honest.
After the sinking, the same field surveyed again, and it reads as empty, not because anyone left it, but because none of them fit what is now built.

## Holds

The count of comparable outside options open to the investor at a given moment, the number that large-numbers competition depends on and that bilateral monopoly is what happens without.

## Offers

Ex-ante, a floor under the terms: any rival could still win the deal, so no single partner can demand more than the field would bear.
Ex-post, nothing comparable, since the asset now built fits nowhere else the field can point to.

## Withheld

A credible alternative once the asset is sunk. The field does not turn anyone away; it simply has no one left suited to what was built for one relationship alone.
