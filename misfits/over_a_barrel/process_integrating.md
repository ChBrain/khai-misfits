# Process: Integrating

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). Run by [The Owner](persona_the_owner.md) on [Own Both Sides](plan_own_both_sides.md), at [The Table](place_the_table.md) and [The Site](place_the_site.md), in [One Roof](plot_one_roof.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The standing cost of [Squeezing](process_squeezing.md) and [Withholding](process_withholding.md), recognized as the price of leaving investment and relationship in two hands over an asset that only works joined.

## Direction

Toward one ownership over both the investment and the counterparty who could hold it up, replacing a negotiation between two parties with an instruction inside one.

## Lever

Common ownership of both sides of the specific asset removes the party who could appropriate the quasi-rent: there is no longer a counterparty across the table to squeeze, since there is no longer a table (Williamson; Grossman and Hart).

## Echo

[The Merger](piece_the_merger.md): the hold-up removed at the cost of market incentives dulled and bureaucracy added, a cure that trades one distortion for another, not a return to what the field once priced.
