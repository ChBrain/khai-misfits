# Plot: Built for One

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Site](place_the_site.md), ground broken on a design that fits one buyer and nobody else: the courting settled at [The Field](place_the_field.md) is now becoming concrete.

## Action

[The Investor](persona_the_investor.md), from [the investor's seat](position_investor.md), sinks [The Investment](piece_the_investment.md): a plant raised beside this one buyer's factory, a tool cut to this one buyer's spec, built to the fit that creates the most value precisely because it serves this relationship and no other.

## Stage

The general-purpose version was cheaper and safer, sellable to anyone; the Investor passes it over for the specific one, since the extra value is made of that very specificity, a tank sized to this pipeline, a die shaped to this part, a technician trained to this line, worth far more here than it would fetch stripped down and sold as scrap or retrained elsewhere. The foundation goes in facing one factory. The die is cut to one drawing. Once poured, once cut, neither can be quietly resized back into something the wider trade would pay full price for.

## Tension

The asset now exists only in relation to one buyer, and the same fact that makes it worth building is the fact that will make it hard to walk away from.
