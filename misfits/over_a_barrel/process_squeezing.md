# Process: Squeezing

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). Run by [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) on [Reopen the Terms](plan_reopen_the_terms.md), at [The Table](place_the_table.md), in [The Squeeze](plot_the_squeeze.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Transformation](piece_the_transformation.md), already borne: the investment is sunk, the rivals gone from [The Field](place_the_field.md), and the Buyer is now the only outlet the investment has.

## Direction

Toward terms redrawn in the Buyer's favor, pressed up to, but not past, the point where the Investor's return still exceeds what the asset would fetch salvaged elsewhere.

## Lever

The quasi-rent, the gap between the asset's value inside this relationship and its value at the next-best use, appropriated because the contract could not name every contingency and renegotiation stays within its rules (Klein, Crawford, and Alchian).
HEDGE: whether this is what happened in the historic Fisher Body and General Motors case is contested (Coase; Casadesus-Masanell and Spulber); the mechanism does not depend on that case being the true account.

## Echo

[The Hold-Up](piece_the_hold_up.md): terms shifted, nothing breached, nothing hidden, the Investor answering to a counterparty it has no comparable alternative to.
