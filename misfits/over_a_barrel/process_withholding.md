# Process: Withholding

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). Run by [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) on [Guard the Exit](plan_guard_the_exit.md), at [The Site](place_the_site.md), in [The Plant Not Built](plot_the_plant_not_built.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The foresight that [Squeezing](process_squeezing.md) will follow [Narrowing](process_narrowing.md), reasoned backward from the hold-up to the investment before either has happened.

## Direction

Away from the specific asset that would create the most value inside the relationship, toward a general or smaller one that keeps an outside option intact.

## Lever

The rational anticipation of an appropriable quasi-rent, foreseen before it exists: since part of the return to the best investment would later be captured by the counterparty, less than the best gets built now (Grossman and Hart).

## Echo

[The Underinvestment](piece_the_underinvestment.md), the largest gains going unmade, with no contract broken and no party at fault: the correct response to a correctly foreseen squeeze, not a failure of nerve.
