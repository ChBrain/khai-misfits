# Piece: The Underinvestment

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). The harm the whole misfit turns on: the investment never made, or made smaller and less specific than the deal would reward, because the hold-up is foreseen before it happens.

## Load Bearing

Foreseeing that any quasi-rent it creates will later be reachable by the Buyer, the Investor rationally builds less, or builds general rather than specific, trading away the largest value the relationship could have created for a smaller value that cannot be held up (Grossman and Hart; Williamson). This is the load-bearing harm of the whole structure: nothing is stolen and no contract is breached, because the surplus that would have been fought over is never made at all, foregone before it could exist to be taken. Guard against centering the ex-post transfer in the Hold-Up as the true cost; the transfer only moves value already created, while the Underinvestment is the value that never comes into being, and it is reliably the larger loss.

## Apparent

Looks like ordinary prudence: a conservative budget, a cautious rollout, nothing visibly wrong and nothing that shows up as a failure on any ledger.
