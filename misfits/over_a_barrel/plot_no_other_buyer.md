# Plot: No Other Buyer

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Field](place_the_field.md), surveyed again now that [The Investment](piece_the_investment.md) is sunk: the same ground, the crowd that stood on it gone.

## Action

[Narrowing](process_narrowing.md) runs on its own, precipitated by the sinking itself: [The Rival](persona_the_rival.md), still holding [the rival's seat](position_rival.md), finds [Bid for the Deal](plan_bid_for_the_deal.md) has nowhere left to land, and [The Transformation](piece_the_transformation.md) is what the field now shows in the crowd's place.

## Stage

Nobody left and nobody was driven off; the field simply stopped being a place any of them could still win from. A plant raised beside one factory has no next-best buyer waiting nearby; a die cut to one part fits no other order on any other rival's floor. What was a market of many possible partners is now a standoff between the two already locked in, a large-numbers competition collapsed to a small-numbers pair (Williamson). The Investor scans the same names that once bid freely and finds every one of them unsuited to what got built.

## Tension

[The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) has nowhere left to sell, and everyone waiting at [The Table](place_the_table.md) now knows it too.
