# Piece: The Merger

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). The remedy and its price: vertical integration bringing both parties under one owner so there is no one left outside to hold up, and the family of lesser remedies beside it.

## Load Bearing

Under one owner there is no counterparty across the table to renegotiate with, so the specific asset can be built at its full, most valuable size without exposing it to appropriation (Williamson). What integration buys back in investment incentive it spends in the sharper discipline the market once supplied, replaced by the duller ones of internal bureaucracy: a cure, not a return to what the field once priced. Short of full integration, allocating ownership of the asset protects the incentive of whichever party holds it, and only that party, since only an owner keeps a residual claim worth investing for; a long contract, a hostage, or a bond reaches for a cheaper version of the same fix but cannot foresee any more of the future than the original agreement did (Grossman and Hart). Guard against reading the Merger as free or strictly better: it forecloses the Hold-Up by trading it for a different, smaller cost elsewhere, never by removing cost altogether.

## Apparent

Reads as tidy consolidation: one company instead of two, efficiency, the natural next step for a relationship that has worked well.
