# Place: The Site

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). Where the relationship-specific investment goes into the ground, or does not. Hosts [Built for One](plot_built_for_one.md), [The Plant Not Built](plot_the_plant_not_built.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The plant raised beside one buyer's factory, the tool cut to one buyer's spec: whatever gets built, and how tightly it is fitted to this one relationship rather than to the trade in general.
Where nothing is built to that fit instead, a plainer, general-purpose version standing in its place.

## Holds

The gap between what the asset is worth here, inside this one relationship, and what it would fetch salvaged anywhere else, the quasi-rent that exists only once the specific version is chosen and sunk.

## Offers

A choice, before ground is broken: build to the fit that would create the most value if the relationship holds, or build to the fit that keeps a way out if it does not.

## Withheld

Any guarantee, once the specific asset stands, that the terms which made building it worthwhile will still be the terms on offer afterward.
