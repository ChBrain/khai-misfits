# Process: Narrowing

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). Borne at [The Field](place_the_field.md), precipitated by [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) sinking [The Investment](piece_the_investment.md), in [No Other Buyer](plot_no_other_buyer.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Investment, sunk. The instant the asset is committed and fitted to one relationship, the field of rivals it might otherwise have served stops being a field it can still turn to.

## Direction

From many comparable partners to one: large-numbers competition among rivals collapses into a small-numbers standoff between the two parties already committed.

## Lever

The fundamental transformation, ex-ante competition replaced ex-post by bilateral monopoly, the moment specificity removes the outside option (Williamson).
Nothing on the field changes hands to cause it: the rivals do not withdraw, they simply stop being comparable once the asset fits only where it was built.

## Echo

[The Transformation](piece_the_transformation.md): a market of many narrowed to a standoff of two, the condition that [Squeezing](process_squeezing.md) exploits and [Withholding](process_withholding.md) is a defense against.
