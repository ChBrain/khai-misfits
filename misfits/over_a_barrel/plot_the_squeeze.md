# Plot: The Squeeze

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Table](place_the_table.md), the same two chairs as before, the same document, reopened now that [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) has nowhere else to take the deal.

## Action

[The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md), from [the buyer's seat](position_buyer.md), runs [Reopen the Terms](plan_reopen_the_terms.md) through [Squeezing](process_squeezing.md): pressing for a larger share of [The Quasi-Rent](piece_the_quasi_rent.md), the margin [The Investment](piece_the_investment.md) is worth here over what it would fetch anywhere else, and naming [The Hold-Up](piece_the_hold_up.md) plainly, a threat to walk unless the terms are redrawn.

## Stage

The Buyer breaches no clause and conceals no fact: the original contract, written honestly while [The Field](place_the_field.md) was still crowded, could not name every contingency that might arise once it wasn't, and this renegotiation lives entirely in that unnamed gap (Klein, Crawford, and Alchian). The ask stops short of the Investor's true floor, the salvage value of a plant that fits nowhere else, because taking everything would leave nothing left to reach for next time; it still lands well past anything the pre-investment competition would ever have allowed. A case like Fisher Body and General Motors is often told this way, though the telling is contested and carried lightly here. The Investor can refuse and take the salvage price, or accept and keep most of a relationship it can no longer replace.

## Tension

The terms move, nothing is torn up, and the surplus the specific investment was built to earn is being redivided by whoever now holds the leverage.
