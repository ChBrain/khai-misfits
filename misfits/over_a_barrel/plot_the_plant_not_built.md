# Plot: The Plant Not Built

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Site](place_the_site.md), before ground is broken, the same choice [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) once made freely in [Built for One](plot_built_for_one.md) comes back changed, read now in the light [The Squeeze](plot_the_squeeze.md) made foreseeable.

## Action

[The Investor](persona_the_investor.md), from [the investor's seat](position_investor.md), runs [Guard the Exit](plan_guard_the_exit.md) through [Withholding](process_withholding.md): building general instead of specific, or building less than the deal could use, so [The Underinvestment](piece_the_underinvestment.md) stands where the fullest version of the plant would have gone (Grossman and Hart).

## Stage

Nothing about this choice is a failure of nerve: the Investor has watched, or can simply reason through, what specificity hands a counterparty once it is sunk, and prices that exposure in before a single foundation is poured. The plant that would have sat closest to the buyer's line, the die cut tightest to the part, the training aimed narrowest at this one employer, none of it gets built, since building it would only hand more to be reclaimed later. A plainer, general-purpose version goes up instead, worth less to this relationship and worth more everywhere else, safer for being worse fitted.

## Tension

No contract was broken and no one was deceived, and still the largest gains this deal could have made go permanently unmade, foregone before there was anything left to fight over.
