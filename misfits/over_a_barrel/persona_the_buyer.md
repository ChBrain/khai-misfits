# Persona: The Buyer

## Taxonomy

[Buyer](position_buyer.md). The counterparty who, once the other has sunk a relationship-specific asset, can reopen the terms and appropriate the quasi-rent, openly and within the rules, because the investor now has no comparable outside option.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, straight-dealing, no worse than the market.
Is the party on the other side of the deal, who bargained fairly before the investment was made and is offered, after it, an advantage it did not manufacture.
No malice in it: the Buyer made no threat before the plant was built, and pressing the terms once it is built is taking a gain the situation hands it, not springing a trap it laid.

## Action

Reopens the terms on [Reopen the Terms](plan_reopen_the_terms.md), through [Squeezing](process_squeezing.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md), reaching for [the Quasi-Rent](piece_the_quasi_rent.md) the sunk [Investment](piece_the_investment.md) exposed.
Cannot be held to terms the contract never named: no agreement could foresee every contingency, so when circumstances shift the Buyer may renegotiate within the rules, and the investor, with nowhere else to go, must take almost any split.
Does what a counterparty does when the outside option across the table has vanished: it asks for more, and gets it, because the alternative for the other side is losing everything.

## Shadow

Not a swindler and not a villain: the Buyer breaks no promise, since the promise was necessarily incomplete, and it seizes no asset, only the surplus the other's specificity left on the table.
Because it can foresee the squeeze as clearly as the investor can, the Buyer's very availability to reopen the terms is what deters the investment in the first place, so the party that would gain from the specific asset is also the party whose reachable advantage keeps it from being built.
It is the one who would take the surplus, and, by being able to, the one who ensures there is no surplus to take.

## Tell

Says the terms were never fixed against this turn of events, and it is right, the contract could not have named it.
Cannot promise not to squeeze in a way the investor can trust, because nothing binds the promise, so its openness to renegotiation is exactly what the investor prices in and retreats from.
