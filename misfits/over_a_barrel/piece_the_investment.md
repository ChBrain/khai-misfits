# Piece: The Investment

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). The relationship-specific asset: worth far more inside this one relationship than in any other use it could be put to.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Sunk by [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) at [The Site](place_the_site.md), before the terms have any reason to move: the plant raised beside one buyer's factory, the tool cut to one buyer's spec, the skill learned for one employer's process, in [Built for One](plot_built_for_one.md).

## Load Bearing

Physical, dedicated, site-bound, or a human skill, the Investment's defining fact is not its size but its fit: built to serve this counterparty and worth markedly less redeployed to any other (Klein, Crawford, and Alchian; Williamson). Before it is sunk it is still a choice among many possible shapes, general or specific, and many possible partners. Once made, the choice cannot be unmade: the asset stays fitted to this relationship whether or not the relationship stays fair. Guard against reading the Investment as the problem; it is the source of the whole surplus at stake, and the misfit is what happens to that surplus after, not the act of building.

## Apparent

Ordinary capital expansion, a vote of confidence in the deal, business as usual. It reads as no different from any other investment right up to the moment the alternatives it gave up stop mattering.
