# Piece: The Transformation

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). Williamson's fundamental transformation: the switch the sinking works, from a large-numbers competition before the investment to a small-numbers bilateral monopoly after.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Produced by [Narrowing](process_narrowing.md) at [The Field](place_the_field.md), in [No Other Buyer](plot_no_other_buyer.md), where the many partners [The Rival](persona_the_rival.md) once stood among stop being comparable to the one relationship the asset now fits.

## Load Bearing

Before the Investment is sunk, the investor can choose among many counterparties, and that choice, live and credible, is what holds the terms honest: this is large-numbers ex-ante competition (Williamson). The instant the asset is committed, the alternatives are still standing in the field, but none of them fit what was built for one relationship alone, so the market for it collapses from many bidders to the two parties already locked together, a bilateral monopoly. Guard against reading the Transformation as a change in either party's character or honesty: nothing about them moves, only the structure of what else they could do.

## Apparent

From outside, nothing has changed: same two firms, same signed contract, same table. The room reads as continuous even though the leverage in it has already flipped.
