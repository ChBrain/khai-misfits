# Position: Investor

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). The seat of the party whose relationship-specific investment would create the most value, and who, foreseeing that once it is sunk the surplus can be taken, builds general or less rather than expose itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The power to create the most value, and no way to keep its share of it: the capacity to fit tightly to this one relationship, and only an incomplete contract and a vanishing outside option to protect what the fit would earn.
An honest position: it would gladly sink the specific asset if it could hold its share of what that asset creates.
No security once the asset is specific, because the specificity that makes it valuable is the specificity that leaves it nowhere else to go.

## Orders

Guard the exit.
Keep an outside option: build general rather than specific, or invest less, or not at all, so that whatever the terms become, you are never left with an asset worth almost nothing anywhere but here.
Weigh the value the tight fit would create against the squeeze it would expose you to, and where the squeeze is foreseeable, take the smaller, safer deal.

## Loses

The surplus of the best deal, given up to stay safe.
Because the value and the vulnerability are the same tight fit, the seat cannot keep one without the other, so protecting its share means declining the specific investment, and the most valuable version of the deal is foregone before it is ever made.
It keeps its outside option and loses the gains that only specificity would have created, a loss that never shows because the thing that would have caused it was never built.

## Drives

Toward the party best able to create the surplus and most exposed by creating it,
declining the tight fit soundly because the squeeze it would open is real and foreseeable,
so the seat that could have built the most is the seat the prospect of the hold-up keeps from building it at all.
