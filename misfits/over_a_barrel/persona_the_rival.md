# Persona: The Rival

## Taxonomy

[Rival](position_rival.md). The competing alternatives, the other buyers and other suppliers, whose presence keeps the terms honest before the investment is made, and who cannot bid on a specific asset once it is sunk, so the competition simply empties.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Present, plural, disciplining.
Is everyone else the parties could have dealt with instead: the other buyers for the supplier's output, the other suppliers for the buyer's need, the outside options that set what a fair deal is.
No design in it toward either party: the Rival does not take sides, it only exists, and its mere existence is what holds both to terms neither could improve on by walking.

## Action

Bids for the deal on [Bid for the Deal](plan_bid_for_the_deal.md) at [the Field](place_the_field.md) while the asset is still general, keeping [the Table](place_the_table.md) honest, and falls silent through [Narrowing](process_narrowing.md) once [the Investment](piece_the_investment.md) is sunk.
Cannot bid on what fits only one relationship: a plant beside one factory or a tool cut to one spec is worth almost nothing to the Rival, so after the sinking it has no comparable offer to make and cannot discipline the terms.
Does what competition does when the asset stops being general: it withdraws, not in anger but in irrelevance, leaving the two parties alone.

## Shadow

Not a party to the deal and not a loser in it: the Rival is the market itself, and its going is no one's doing and no one's fault, only the consequence of an asset made worth far more in one place than any other.
Because its presence is what made the terms fair and its absence is what makes the squeeze possible, the Rival is the whole difference between before and after, so the fundamental transformation is nothing but the Rival ceasing to be able to bid, the discipline gone the instant the specificity is real.
It is the competition that held both sides honest, and the competition the specific investment quietly removes.

## Tell

Says nothing and offers nothing after the sinking, because there is nothing for it to offer on an asset it cannot use.
Cannot be summoned back once the fit is tight, so the outside option the investor counted on is simply not there when the terms are reopened, and the Table that was fair is a standoff of two.
