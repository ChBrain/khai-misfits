# Position: Rival

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). The seat of the competing alternatives whose presence keeps the terms honest before the investment, and which cannot bid on a specific asset once it is sunk, so the competition empties and the discipline is gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Presence, and no stake: the other buyers and other suppliers the parties could deal with instead, whose mere availability sets what a fair deal is.
A disciplining weight: while the asset is still general, the seat's standing offers hold both parties to terms neither could improve on by walking.
No bid to make on a specific asset, because a plant beside one factory or a tool cut to one spec is worth almost nothing to it, so once the fit is tight it has nothing to offer.

## Orders

Bid for the deal.
While the investment is still general and could serve anyone, compete for it, and by competing set the fair terms that discipline the table.
Withdraw when the asset is sunk, not in protest but in irrelevance, because there is nothing to bid on an asset you cannot use.

## Loses

Its relevance, the instant the asset becomes specific.
Because the seat can only discipline terms it could plausibly win, the sinking of a relationship-specific asset removes its bid, so the outside option the investor counted on is simply not there when the terms are reopened.
It loses nothing of its own and takes the whole market's discipline with it, the fair terms it enforced gone the moment the fit is real.

## Drives

Toward the competition that is the whole difference between a fair deal and a squeeze,
present and disciplining before the sinking, silent and irrelevant after,
so the seat that held both sides honest is the seat the specific investment quietly removes, the fundamental transformation nothing but its going.
