# Piece: The Quasi-Rent

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). The appropriable surplus: the gap between what the sunk asset is worth inside this relationship and what it would fetch, salvaged, in its next-best use outside it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Created the moment [The Investment](piece_the_investment.md) is sunk at [The Site](place_the_site.md), and reached for by [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) through [Squeezing](process_squeezing.md) at [The Table](place_the_table.md).

## Load Bearing

Not the asset's whole value, only the margin: the amount by which staying in this relationship beats the best the investor could do anywhere else (Klein, Crawford, and Alchian). That margin is exactly what the investor loses by walking, and exactly what a counterparty with no better options to fear can threaten to redraw the terms around, since conceding still costs the investor less than refusing does. Guard against mistaking the Quasi-Rent for profit, or for the deal's full worth: it is the narrow difference specificity creates, and only that difference is what the hold-up ever puts at risk.

## Apparent

Looks like plain negotiating room, the normal give in any price, nothing that should alarm anyone at the table.

## Yearbook

Born of [The Investment](piece_the_investment.md), it is the lever named in [Squeezing](process_squeezing.md) and the prize taken in [The Hold-Up](piece_the_hold_up.md), pressed by [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) on [Reopen the Terms](plan_reopen_the_terms.md), in [The Squeeze](plot_the_squeeze.md).
