# Piece: The Hold-Up

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). The ex-post squeeze itself: the counterparty reopening agreed terms once the specific investment is sunk and the alternatives that once disciplined the deal are gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Run by [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) through [Squeezing](process_squeezing.md), staged on [Reopen the Terms](plan_reopen_the_terms.md) at [The Table](place_the_table.md), in [The Squeeze](plot_the_squeeze.md).

## Load Bearing

No contract is broken and nothing is hidden: it could not name every future contingency in advance, so when the Buyer comes back to the Table asking for different terms, the request sits inside a gap the agreement itself left open, not outside the letter of it (Klein, Crawford, and Alchian). It is pressed because the Investor's next-best alternative is now worth far less than staying in, a leverage the Transformation, not any deception, has handed the Buyer. Guard against reading the Hold-Up as fraud or theft: it needs both an unrecoverable investment and a contract that genuinely could not close every door, and it stays lawful precisely because both conditions are real, not staged.

## Apparent

Reads as an ordinary renegotiation: circumstances changed, the ask sounds reasonable, nothing on paper looks like a squeeze from either side of the table.
