# Persona: The Owner

## Taxonomy

[Owner](position_owner.md). The remedy made an actor: the party who brings both sides under one ownership so there is no separate counterparty left to hold up, removing the exposure at the cost of the market's sharp incentives.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Deliberate, structural, weighing the trade.
Is the one who answers the hold-up by changing who owns what: buying the supplier, or the buyer, so the specific investment and the party that needs it sit inside one firm, and there is no one across a table to reopen the terms.
No cleverness for its own sake: integration does not make the investment cheaper or the fit looser, it only removes the second party whose reachable advantage deterred the investment.

## Action

Owns both sides on [Own Both Sides](plan_own_both_sides.md), through [Integrating](process_integrating.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md), laying [the Merger](piece_the_merger.md) so the specific asset can be sunk without a counterparty to squeeze it.
Cannot remove the exposure without cost: bringing the deal inside one firm replaces the market's sharp incentives with the dulled ones of the organisation and its bureaucracy, and reassigning ownership secures one party's incentive to invest only by weakening the other's.
Does what a remedy does with an exposure inherent in specificity: it moves the boundary of the firm to enclose the hold-up, and pays for the enclosure in incentive and in oversight.

## Shadow

Not a hero and not a free fix: the Owner does not abolish the tension between value and vulnerability, it relocates it, trading the sharp discipline of the market for the softer, costlier discipline of hierarchy, and it must be arranged before the fact, at real expense.
Because ownership can protect only one incentive at a time and integration dulls what the market kept sharp, the Owner cannot make everyone secure and keen at once, so the fix that removes the hold-up brings its own frictions, chosen only when the specificity is worth the price of enclosing it.
It works, and it is neither obvious nor free, and it answers one distortion with another.

## Tell

Says it brought the two under one roof so no one could be held up, and it is right, and the incentives inside the roof are duller for it.
Cannot integrate a whole market or foresee every contingency a long contract would need, so it encloses the specific deal at the boundary of the firm and carries, from then on, the cost of running what the market used to price.
