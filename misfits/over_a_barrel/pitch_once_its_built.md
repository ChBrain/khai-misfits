# Pitch: Once It's Built

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reasonable, businesslike, in good faith.
Two parties with a genuinely valuable deal to do, the terms fair because the alternatives keep them fair, nothing hidden and nothing owed that is not paid.
Nothing false in it: the deal is real, the value is real, and the tight fit that would create the most of it is exactly what a good bargain looks for.

## Undertow

Under the fair terms, an investment that fits one relationship and no other.
The moment the plant is built beside the one factory, or the tool cut to the one spec, its worth everywhere else collapses, and the difference is a surplus the other side can now reach for, because the competition that held the terms honest is gone.
No breach and no lie: the contract could not name every contingency, so reopening the terms is within the rules, and the party that created the most value by fitting so tightly is the party the fit has left exposed.

## Nerve

The plainness of a good deal fairly struck, and the catch that striking it well means sinking something you can then be squeezed on:
that the specificity which creates the value is the specificity which strips your outside option, the two the same fit measured twice,
so the safest move is to build general, or less, or not at all, and the best version of the deal is quietly never made.

## Echo

After the run: the plant beside the factory never built, the tool never cut, the largest gains foregone before anyone could fight over them, a loss authored by no one and never seen (Grossman and Hart).
And the fixes that cost what the trap costs: bring both under one owner and dull the market's incentives to the firm's, write a contract long enough to foreclose the squeeze and still miss the unforeseen, post a hostage and raise the price of the squeeze without abolishing the exposure,
which is inherent in the specificity itself, so the most valuable deal is so often the one not done.
