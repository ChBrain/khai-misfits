# Plan: Bid for the Deal

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the rival's seat](position_rival.md), inside [Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Rival](position_rival.md)

## Direction

While the investment is still general and could serve anyone, compete for it, and by competing set the fair terms that discipline the table. Withdraw when the asset is sunk, not in protest but in irrelevance, because there is nothing to bid on an asset you cannot use.

## Orders

([The Rival](persona_the_rival.md) bids at [the Field](place_the_field.md) while the asset is general, keeping [the Table](place_the_table.md) honest, and falls silent through [Narrowing](process_narrowing.md) once [the Investment](piece_the_investment.md) is specific, carrying [the Transformation](piece_the_transformation.md).)

## Implementation

([The Field](place_the_field.md) is full of alternatives while the asset could serve anyone; the [narrowing](process_narrowing.md) process empties it the instant the asset fits only one relationship, so the discipline the Rival's bid supplied is gone and a market of many becomes a standoff of two.)

## Targets

- [ ] the Rival competes for the deal while the asset is still general and could serve anyone
- [ ] its competing sets the fair terms that discipline the table before the sinking
- [ ] it withdraws once the asset is specific, in irrelevance and not in protest, having nothing to bid on it
- [ ] its going is the fundamental transformation, the outside option removed and the squeeze made possible
