# Position: Buyer

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). The seat of the counterparty who, once the other has sunk a specific asset, can reopen the terms and take the quasi-rent, openly and within the rules, because the investor has no comparable outside option left.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An advantage it did not manufacture: the ability, once the specific asset is sunk, to reopen the terms and threaten to walk, against an investor who now has nowhere else nearly as good to go.
A clean standing: it made no threat before the investment and breaks no promise after, because the contract could not have named the contingency it now presses.
No way to bind itself not to squeeze, because nothing the investor could trust holds the promise, so its very freedom to renegotiate is a fact the investor must price in.

## Orders

Reopen the terms.
Once the asset is sunk and the outside option across the table is gone, ask for a larger share, up to the whole quasi-rent, since the investor's alternative is to lose everything the specific asset is worth here.
Stay within the rules: renegotiate only what the incomplete contract left unnamed, and take the gain the situation offers rather than one you engineered.

## Loses

Nothing in the squeeze, and the deal that would have been worth squeezing.
Because it can foresee its own advantage as clearly as the investor can, the seat's reachable power to reopen the terms is exactly what deters the specific investment, so the surplus it would have taken is never created for it to take.
It keeps every gain it can reach and loses the larger one that its own reachability kept from being built.

## Drives

Toward the counterparty whose available advantage is what empties the table it would have profited from,
able to take the surplus and, by being able, ensuring there is none,
so the seat that would gain from the specific asset is the seat whose power to squeeze it keeps it unbuilt.
