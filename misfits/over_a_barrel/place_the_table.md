# Place: The Table

## Taxonomy

[Over a Barrel](play_over_a_barrel.md). Where the deal's terms are set before the investment and reopened after it. Hosts [The Courtship](plot_the_courtship.md), [The Squeeze](plot_the_squeeze.md), [One Roof](plot_one_roof.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Before the sinking, a crowded table: several bidders, an open price, a contract being drafted with rivals still in the room.
After the sinking, the same table, the same two chairs, the same document. Nothing about the furniture announces that the room has changed.

## Holds

The incomplete contract, the terms it could write down and the contingencies it could not, which is exactly the room renegotiation moves in.
What the table holds is not a secret but a balance: who, at this moment, has somewhere else to go, and who does not.

## Offers

A place to set terms once, with alternatives still pricing them honestly, and a place to reopen them later, within the letter of what was signed.

## Withheld

Any clause that could have named every future contingency in advance. The contract is incomplete because it could not be otherwise, and that gap is what the table leaves open to be renegotiated, not concealed, later.
