# Plot: Water You Cannot Spare

## Taxonomy

[What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The mechanism understood at last, and the three remedies on the table: wash it down, drain it away, or rest the ground.

## Action

[Leaving behind](process_leaving_behind.md) and [rising](process_rising.md) worked against directly: [The One Who Waters](persona_the_one_who_waters.md), holding [One Who Waters](position_one_who_waters.md), applies water past the crop's need and cuts a drain to take the surplus off.

## Stage

[The One Who Waters](persona_the_one_who_waters.md) tables [Wash It Through](plan_wash_it_through.md), and pays for it out of the one thing the place is short of.

## Tension

Every remedy works and every remedy is paid at the wrong end.
Leaching needs more water than the crop needs, in the single kind of place where water is scarce. Drains yield nothing anyone can point to in a good year and must be maintained in every year. Fallow gives up a harvest against a harm that has not appeared and may not appear in this lifetime. Each cost falls on someone who will not live to see what it prevented, and if the remedy works nobody will ever learn what it prevented either, so the whole of the reward is invisible and the whole of the price is not.
