# Process: Rising

## Taxonomy

[What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md). The table climbing under a field that is watered and not drained.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Water applied to ground with nowhere to drain, season after season.
[The Water Below](place_the_water_below.md), under [the watered field](place_the_watered_field.md).

## Direction

Upward, slowly, without any level that would announce itself.
Then, once near enough the surface, upward through the soil itself, wicked and concentrated by the sun.

## Lever

The absence of a drain, which is not an oversight: a dry country builds works to bring water to ground and none to take it off.

## Echo

Salt that had been spread thinly through the soil delivered back to exactly the depth the roots are in.
