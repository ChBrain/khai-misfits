# Pitch: Sweet Enough to Pour

## Taxonomy

[What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Warm and agricultural. A good season, a skilled hand, water running down a ditch into ground that needs it and answering with grain.
Nothing anxious in it. This is competence in a hard place, and it works, and the people who do it are good at it.

## Undertow

Under the competence, an arithmetic with no subtraction in it.
Salt arrives with every watering and leaves with nothing, so the ledger only ever runs one way, at a rate too small to be an event. There is no bad year to point at and no moment that could be called the turn.

## Nerve

The unease of a sum that never once looks wrong while it is being done.

## Echo

Afterward: a hardier grain than the one before it, then a hardier one than that, then a white bloom on the ground after a dry night, and a chain of good farming with no bad decision anywhere in it.
