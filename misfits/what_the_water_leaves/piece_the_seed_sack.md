# Piece: The Seed Sack

## Taxonomy

[The Watered Field](place_the_watered_field.md). What is sown, and what the choice of it records.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the store, opened at sowing, carried out to [the watered field](place_the_watered_field.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the adaptation has no object and the record has no trace.
It is the piece that both answers the problem and hides it: what is in the sack changes as the ground changes, each change is a sound decision, and the sequence of changes is the only written record the whole process leaves.

## Apparent

Grain, of whichever kind the ground will still bear.
Wheat, once. Barley, later, sown by someone doing the sensible thing with a field that has begun to disappoint.

## Yearbook

Wheat and barley in roughly equal measure at the earliest tallies.
Wheat down to a fraction over the centuries that follow, which reads like a change in appetite and is a change in what the ground will carry.
