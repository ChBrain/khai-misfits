# Plot: What Stays

## Taxonomy

[What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Water standing on the furrows, the crop above it and the sun above that.

## Action

[Leaving behind](process_leaving_behind.md): [The Salt](persona_the_salt.md), holding [Salt](position_salt.md), stays where it was put while the water goes two ways and takes none of it.

## Stage

Nobody stages this. The crop takes water and the sun takes water, and both are indifferent to what was dissolved in it, so the deposit is made by physics and not by anybody's decision.

## Tension

The increment is real and is below every threshold in the setting.
Nobody can see it, taste it, or measure it with anything they have, and there is no year in which the ground is meaningfully worse than the year before. So there is no year in which anyone could have been asked to act, and no moment that anyone could later point to and call the turn: the ledger runs one way, and every entry in it is too small to be an event.
