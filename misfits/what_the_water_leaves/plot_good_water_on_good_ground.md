## Cue

A crop that will not exist unless the water comes.

## Action

[Watering](process_watering.md): [The One Who Waters](persona_the_one_who_waters.md), holding [One Who Waters](position_one_who_waters.md), opens the ditch, spreads the water, and lets it soak.

## Stage

[The One Who Waters](persona_the_one_who_waters.md), having tabled [Water the Field](plan_water_the_field.md), judges the amount and the timing the way a skilled person does, and the grain comes up because of it.

## Tension

There is no other way to farm here, and no more careful version of this.
The water is sweet enough to drink and carries only the salt any river carries. Too little and the crop fails, too much and it drowns, so the skill is real and is being exercised well. What has also happened, and is not a second act, is that a quantity of salt has been moved from the river into the ground and will not be going back.
