# Persona: The White Ground

## Taxonomy

[White Ground](position_white_ground.md). The field at the end, legible at last and long past use.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Pale after a dry night, crusted in patches, hard where it used to be soft.
Unmistakable to anyone who sees it, and requiring no instrument, no record and no argument.

## Action

Fails to grow the hardier grain, having already failed the softer one.
Is walked out to, looked at, and understood immediately and completely by whoever is standing on it.

## Shadow

Cannot arrive early enough to be of any use.
It is the first plainly visible sign in a process that has been running for generations, so the moment the evidence finally becomes legible is the moment there is nothing left to do with it, and everyone who could have acted on it is dead.

## Tell

Needs no explanation, which is the whole of the problem: understanding arrives complete, at once, and too late, and everything before it was invisible.
