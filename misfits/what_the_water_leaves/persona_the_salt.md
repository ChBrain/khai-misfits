# Persona: The Salt

## Taxonomy

[Salt](position_salt.md). Arrives dissolved, stays behind, and accumulates below the threshold of notice.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary. The quantity any river carries, far too little to taste, and no different from the salt in any water anyone has ever drunk.
Not a contaminant and not anyone's doing: simply what water is when it has run over ground.

## Action

Comes in with every watering and leaves with none of it.
Spreads thinly through the soil, then, once [the water below](place_the_water_below.md) has risen far enough, is carried back up and concentrated where the roots are.

## Shadow

Cannot be noticed at the rate it arrives, and cannot be removed by anything the place has spare.
Its only exit is downward, in more water than the crop needs, drained away, which is exactly the water a dry country does not have: so the one thing that would undo it is the one thing the setting cannot supply.

## Tell

Shows nothing at all until it shows [the white bloom](piece_the_white_bloom.md), by which point it has been arriving for longer than anyone present has been alive.
