# Position: One Who Waters

## Taxonomy

[What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md). The seat that must water, and cannot water without depositing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A river, a ditch, a field, and the skill to use them well.
A harvest that depends entirely on the watering, and a household that depends on the harvest.

## Orders

Open [the head ditch](piece_the_head_ditch.md) when the crop needs it.
Spread the water and let it soak.
Take the harvest, and do it again next season.

## Loses

Any way of performing the work that does not also perform the harm.
There is no more careful watering available: the water carries what it carries and the sun takes back only water, so the seat's competence and its damage are not separable acts but one act under two descriptions.

## Drives

Toward a crop that will not exist without the watering, and into a ground that is fractionally less waterable each time, at a rate that gives the seat no year in which it could have been asked to stop.
