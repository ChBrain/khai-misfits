# Process: Watering

## Taxonomy

[What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md). The necessary act, performed well.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A dry field and a crop that needs water.
[The One Who Waters](persona_the_one_who_waters.md), holding [One Who Waters](position_one_who_waters.md), at [the head ditch](piece_the_head_ditch.md).

## Direction

Toward the crop, and only the crop.
Aimed at a harvest, achieving one, and requiring skill to do properly: too little and the grain fails, too much and it drowns.

## Lever

A river running past a dry field, and the works to bring one to the other.

## Echo

Water standing and soaking, grain that will come up because of it, and a quantity of dissolved salt now in the ground rather than in the river.
