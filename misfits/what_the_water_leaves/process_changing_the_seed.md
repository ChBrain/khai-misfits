# Process: Changing the Seed

## Taxonomy

[What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md). The competent adaptation that also removes the signal.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Wheat beginning to thin where it did not use to.
[The Hardier Grain](persona_the_hardier_grain.md), holding [Hardier Grain](position_hardier_grain.md), sown from [the seed sack](piece_the_seed_sack.md).

## Direction

Toward a crop the ground will still carry, chosen by someone paying close attention.
Aimed at holding the harvest steady, and succeeding.

## Lever

Real tolerance: barley bears salt far better than wheat, so the switch is not a hope but a fact about the plant.

## Echo

A field that yields again, a household that keeps eating, and no remaining sign that anything is wrong with the ground.
