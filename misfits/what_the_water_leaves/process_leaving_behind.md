# Process: Leaving Behind

## Taxonomy

[What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md). The deposit: what the water carried and does not take away.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Water standing on ground, with a crop above it and the sun above that.
[The Salt](persona_the_salt.md), holding [Salt](position_salt.md), in [the watered field](place_the_watered_field.md).

## Direction

Downward and inward, into the soil, and nowhere out.
The crop takes water, the sun takes water, and neither takes what was dissolved in it.

## Lever

Evaporation and uptake: two processes that are selective for water and indifferent to everything carried in it.

## Echo

A season's residue in the ground, far below what any person could see, taste or measure, and identical to last season's, and to next season's.
