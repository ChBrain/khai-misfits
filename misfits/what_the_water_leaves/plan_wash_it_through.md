# Plan: Wash It Through

## Direction

Give the salt an exit. Apply more water than the crop needs so it carries the salt down past the roots, dig and maintain drains so the surplus has somewhere to go, and rest the field when it needs resting.

## Orders

(The One Who Waters applies water beyond the crop's requirement, cuts a drain to take the surplus away from [the watered field](place_the_watered_field.md), and leaves the ground fallow for a season when the reckoning calls for it.)

## Implementation

## Targets

- [ ] the price is accepted: each cost is paid now by someone who will not see what it prevented, and if it works nobody will know what it prevented either
