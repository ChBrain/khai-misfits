# Place: The Watered Field

## Taxonomy

[What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md). Where the water is spread and the crop comes up.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Level ground in a dry country, cut by furrows, with a ditch at the head of it and a river beyond.
Water standing and soaking. Grain, in the season, that would not be there without it.

## Holds

[The Head Ditch](piece_the_head_ditch.md), at the top, bringing water in.
[The Seed Sack](piece_the_seed_sack.md), holding whichever kind the ground will still bear.
[The White Bloom](piece_the_white_bloom.md), eventually, on the surface after a dry night.

## Offers

A harvest, in exchange for the watering, honestly and every season it is done properly.
Nothing withheld from anyone who works it well.

## Withheld

Any drain. There is nowhere for surplus water to go, and no surplus to send there.
Not by neglect: a place dry enough to need irrigating is a place without spare water, so the one route that would carry the salt away is closed by the same fact that makes the watering necessary.
