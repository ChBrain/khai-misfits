# Plot: The Water Comes Up

## Taxonomy

[What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Seasons of water applied to ground that has nowhere to drain, and [the water below](place_the_water_below.md) with only one direction available to it.

## Action

[Rising](process_rising.md): the table climbs a little each season, comes near enough for the soil to wick it, and the sun concentrates what arrives at the surface.

## Stage

No one is present for this and no one measures it. It happens underground, in a place nobody goes, at a rate that gives no season a character different from the last.

## Tension

The mechanism most responsible for the ending is the one part of the production nobody ever sees.
And the absence of a drain is not an oversight: works in a dry country are built to bring water to ground, and a ditch that carried water away from a field would be a strange thing to want. So the salt spread thinly through the soil is delivered back to exactly the depth the roots are in, by a process that has no threshold to watch for and no instrument in the setting that would read it.
