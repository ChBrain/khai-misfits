# Persona: The Hardier Grain

## Taxonomy

[Hardier Grain](position_hardier_grain.md). The good answer that works, and holds the yield up while the trend runs on beneath it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Barley where wheat used to stand. Tolerates salt far better, asks less of the ground, and gives a crop where the other kind has begun to thin.
Not a lesser choice: the right one, made by someone paying attention.

## Action

Is sown from [the seed sack](piece_the_seed_sack.md) when wheat starts to fail, and bears.
Holds the harvest steady, so the field goes on being a field and the household goes on eating.

## Shadow

Cannot be told apart from a solution, because it is one.
It answers the problem in front of it completely, and in doing so removes the only signal the trend was producing: a field that still yields is not a field anyone argues about, so the correct adaptation is also what buys the accumulation another few centuries of quiet.

## Tell

Reads, in the tallies, as a change in taste or in demand: wheat and barley roughly level at the start and wheat down to a fraction later, which looks like agriculture developing and is agriculture retreating.
