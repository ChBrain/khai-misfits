# Persona: The One Who Waters

## Taxonomy

[One Who Waters](position_one_who_waters.md). Irrigates a dry field, necessarily and skilfully, and by doing so salts it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Skilled, and unsentimental about it. Knows when to open the ditch and how long to let the water stand.
Reads the crop, reads the weather, and would be recognised by any farmer anywhere as someone who knows the work.

## Action

Opens [the head ditch](piece_the_head_ditch.md) and lets the water spread across [the watered field](place_the_watered_field.md).
Lets it soak, closes the ditch, and takes the harvest that follows.
Does it again the next season, because a field in a dry place is watered or it is nothing.

## Shadow

Cannot separate the watering from the salting, because they are not two acts.
There is no more careful way to do this that does not do it: the water carries what it carries, the crop takes the water, the sun takes the rest, and the residue is below anything this persona could see, taste or measure with anything they have.

## Tell

Says "it took the water well", meaning the field drank it and the grain came, which is true and is also the description of the deposit being made.
