# Piece: The White Bloom

## Taxonomy

[The Watered Field](place_the_watered_field.md). The crust on the surface: the first visible sign, and far too late.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the surface of [the watered field](place_the_watered_field.md), after a dry night, in patches that widen year on year.

## Load Bearing

Without it the process is never legible at all, and with it the legibility comes too late to matter.
It is the piece that fixes the timing of the misfit: everything before it was invisible and everything after it is finished, so the production has exactly one moment of evidence and it is positioned where evidence is no use.

## Apparent

A pale crust, obvious to anyone, requiring no instrument and no expertise.
Hard underfoot where the ground used to be soft.

## Yearbook

Absent for generations while the whole of the process ran.
Present now, complete, and unambiguous, on a field that will not grow the hardier grain either.
