## Action

## Stage

## Tension

The correct adaptation removes the only signal the trend was producing.
This is not denial: barley genuinely bears salt better than wheat, the switch is a fact about the plant rather than a hope, and the household keeps eating because of it. And a field that still yields is not a field anyone argues about, so the ground goes on changing with nothing left to report it. In the tallies it reads as a change in appetite, wheat and barley level at the start and wheat down to a fraction after, which looks like agriculture developing and is agriculture retreating.
