# Place: The Water Below

## Taxonomy

[What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md). The table under the field, rising a little each season.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Nothing, for most of the play. It is underground and no one goes there.
Its level is not measured, because measuring it is not something anyone has reason or means to do.

## Holds

Every watering that did not leave as vapour or as grain, gathered and held.
The dissolved salt of all of them, waiting to be brought back up.

## Offers

A rise so slow that no season differs from the last.
Then, once it is near enough the surface, a route upward: the soil wicks it, the sun concentrates it, and the salt is delivered to the depth the roots are in.

## Withheld

Any signal on the way up.
There is no level at which it announces itself, no depth that is a threshold anyone could watch for, and no instrument in the setting that would read it, so the mechanism most responsible for the ending is the one part of the production nobody ever sees.
