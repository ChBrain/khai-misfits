# Plan: Water the Field

## Taxonomy

A **personal scheme**: the intent of [The One Who Waters](persona_the_one_who_waters.md), inside [What the Water Leaves](play_what_the_water_leaves.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Waters](persona_the_one_who_waters.md)

## Direction

Grow grain in a dry place, which means bringing the river to the field and doing it well: enough water for the crop, not so much that it drowns, timed to what the season is doing.

## Orders

(The One Who Waters opens [the head ditch](piece_the_head_ditch.md) when the crop needs it, spreads the water across the furrows, lets it soak, closes the ditch, and takes the harvest.)

## Implementation

([The Watered Field](place_the_watered_field.md); the [watering](process_watering.md) process; [the head ditch](piece_the_head_ditch.md) is the works that make it possible, and there is no counterpart to it running the other way.)

## Targets

- [ ] the water is let in when the crop needs it, and not before
- [ ] enough is applied to carry the crop, and not so much as to drown it
- [ ] the harvest is taken
- [ ] the same is done next season, because a field here is watered or it is nothing
