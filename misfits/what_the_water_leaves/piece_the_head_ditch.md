# Piece: The Head Ditch

## Taxonomy

[The Watered Field](place_the_watered_field.md). The channel that brings water in, with no counterpart taking it out.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the top of [the watered field](place_the_watered_field.md), between the river and the furrows, opened and closed by hand.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is no field, and with it there is no exit.
It is the piece that makes the asymmetry physical: the works a dry country builds are works for bringing water to ground, and nothing in them is for taking water off it, because taking water off ground is the last thing anyone in a dry country wants to build.

## Apparent

Earth, dug and banked, a season's maintenance a year.
Obviously useful, obviously worth its upkeep, and admired when it is well kept.

## Yearbook

Dug by someone whose name is not remembered, extended twice, and repaired every year since.
Never once accompanied by a ditch running the other way, and nobody has ever proposed one, because a ditch that carried water away from a field would be a strange thing to want.
