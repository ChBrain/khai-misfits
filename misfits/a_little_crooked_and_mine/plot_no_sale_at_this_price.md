# Plot: No Sale at This Price

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The two valuations meet, and do not match.

## Action

At [The Stall](place_the_stall.md), [The Appraiser](persona_the_appraiser.md) from [the appraiser's seat](position_appraiser.md) meets [The Impasse](piece_the_impasse.md): a trade offered, considered, and left unmade.

## Stage

The trade both sides would gain from goes unmade, since the Builder's honestly felt price sits above what any buyer, feeling none of the labor, will pay (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012). No villain here: the Builder is not stubborn and the Appraiser is not stingy, both valuations are honestly arrived at, each true from where it stands, and the impasse is authored by no one, only by a value that only one side's labor produced. The Appraiser names a fair number and means it. The gap between that number and what the object is felt to be worth does not close, because nothing on the Stall can supply the labor that would close it. This is distinct from a sibling misfit called Loath to Part, where mere possession alone, no labor required, is enough to inflate a value above what a buyer will pay; here the inflation is specifically the labor of construction, and possession without completing a build is not enough to produce it.

## Tension

Two honest prices, and no trade that can hold them both.
