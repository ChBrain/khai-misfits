# Piece: The Ownership

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The mechanism: the felt sense that a finished object is meaningfully one's own because of the labor that made it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), carried by [Assembling](process_assembling.md): the feeling settles in the moment the last piece is set and the thing stands finished, made rather than merely held.

## Load Bearing

Labor invested in building or assembling an object generates a felt ownership that inflates its value in the builder's own eyes, independent of the object's objective quality, so a non-expert's imperfect, even crooked, creation is regarded by its maker as comparable in worth to an expert's polished one (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012). The feeling arrives with the last joint fitted, the last piece set, and it does not check itself against a level or a straightedge. This is not a failure of judgment; the feeling is sincerely earned by the labor spent, and it asks nothing more than to be recognized as the fair return on the hours it cost.

## Apparent

Looks like ordinary pride in one's own work; is actually a real inflation of value that a buyer will not share.
