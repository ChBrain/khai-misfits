# Plan: Hold the True Proportion

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the witness's seat](position_witness.md), inside [A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Witness](position_witness.md)

## Direction

Hold the accumulated cases to what they show, and generalize no further than the evidence reaches. Know that the effect is real and documented, that its size tracks task difficulty, and that a claim held past its true scope is a weaker claim than the one the evidence actually supports.

## Orders

([The Witness](persona_the_witness.md) holds [the Difficulty](piece_the_difficulty.md) through [Comparing](process_comparing.md) at [the Shelf](place_the_shelf.md), keeping the pattern honest by keeping its edges.)

## Implementation

([Comparing](process_comparing.md) at [the Shelf](place_the_shelf.md) holds [the Difficulty](piece_the_difficulty.md), because the inflation is roughly proportional to the labor felt as meaningfully one's own, stronger where the building demanded more and weaker where it demanded little, so the pattern is staged as a documented and variable tendency and not a flat, universal inflation (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012; the psychological-ownership and effort-justification literature that followed).)

## Targets

- [ ] the pattern is held to what the accumulated cases actually show
- [ ] the effect's size is seen to vary with task difficulty and the labor felt as one's own
- [ ] the larger claim, that all effort breeds attachment regardless of outcome, is refused as broader than the evidence supports
- [ ] it is owned that a claim held past its true scope is weaker than the one the evidence actually makes
