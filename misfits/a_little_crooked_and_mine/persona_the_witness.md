# Persona: The Witness

## Taxonomy

[Witness](position_witness.md), inside [A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The archetype of the accumulated cases, holding the effect's true, bounded proportion across easy builds and hard ones.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

It looks like the pattern proven beyond doubt, build after build, the maker's love outrunning the market's price every time, evidence enough, it would seem, to call every act of building an inflation waiting to happen.

## Action

Hold the true proportion. At [the Shelf](place_the_shelf.md), on [Hold the True Proportion](plan_hold_the_true_proportion.md) through [Comparing](process_comparing.md), the Witness holds [the Difficulty](piece_the_difficulty.md), the effect's size tracking task difficulty and the labor felt as meaningfully one's own (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012).

## Shadow

Its evidence is real and its edges are just as real. The Witness refuses the larger claim the accumulated cases seem to invite, that all effort breeds attachment regardless of outcome, since a simple task finished with little labor inflates its value far less than a hard one that demanded everything the builder had.

## Tell

It keeps the pattern honest by keeping its edges, and refuses to call every hour spent on a thing an hour that will be loved.
