# Plot: Left Half Done

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At the Shelf, a build begun, and never finished.

## Action

[The Abandoner](persona_the_abandoner.md), from [the abandoner's seat](position_abandoner.md), on [Leave It Half Done](plan_leave_it_half_done.md) through [Abandoning](process_abandoning.md), holds [The Unfinished](piece_the_unfinished.md) at [The Shelf](place_the_shelf.md): the object possessed exactly as long as a finished one, and showing none of the extra inflation a finished one carries.

## Stage

An abandoned build, begun but never finished, shows none of the inflation a completed one shows, so the effect is not the mere passage of time spent on a task or simple exposure to it, but requires a completed, successfully assembled object the builder can call finished and their own (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012). The pieces on the Shelf sit half joined, set down partway through, and the Abandoner looks at them without the warmth a finished build would have earned. There was a reason to stop, ordinary and sufficient, and no failure in stopping. The Abandoner has done nothing wrong in setting the task aside; the absence of feeling here is not a failure but the clearest evidence of what the feeling actually requires, since the same hours were spent, the same hands were used, and still nothing extra was generated by them.

## Tension

The same hours spent, and none of the love that finishing would have earned.
