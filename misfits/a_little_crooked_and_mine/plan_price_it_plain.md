# Plan: Price It Plain

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the appraiser's seat](position_appraiser.md), inside [A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Appraiser](position_appraiser.md)

## Direction

Judge the finished object by what it plainly is, since no history of the labor spent on it is visible to you. Know that a fair, honest price may still fall short of what the maker feels it is worth, and that this shortfall is not unkindness but the plain truth of a value you were never in a position to inflate.

## Orders

([The Appraiser](persona_the_appraiser.md) holds [the Verdict](piece_the_verdict.md) through [Appraising](process_appraising.md) at [the Stall](place_the_stall.md), judging the object at what it is objectively worth, and meets [the Impasse](piece_the_impasse.md).)

## Implementation

([Appraising](process_appraising.md) at [the Stall](place_the_stall.md) holds [the Verdict](piece_the_verdict.md), because the object is judged on everything actually visible and nothing more, and meets [the Impasse](piece_the_impasse.md), the trade going unmade since the builder's honestly felt price sits above what any buyer will pay (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012).)

## Targets

- [ ] the object is judged plainly, on its own visible quality and nothing else
- [ ] the price offered is fair and honest, for exactly what can be seen and held
- [ ] the impasse is met, the two valuations too far apart for the trade either side would gain from
- [ ] it is owned that the gap is no one's fault, only a value only one side's labor produced
