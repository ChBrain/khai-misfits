# Plot: Mine Because I Made It

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The build finished, and regarded for the first time by the one who made it.

## Action

At [The Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), [The Builder](persona_the_builder.md) from [the builder's seat](position_builder.md) meets [The Ownership](piece_the_ownership.md): a felt sense, immediate and warm, that the standing object is meaningfully theirs.

## Stage

Labor invested in building or assembling an object generates a felt ownership that inflates its value in the builder's own eyes, independent of the object's objective quality, so a non-expert's imperfect, even crooked, creation is regarded by its maker as comparable in worth to an expert's polished one (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012). The Builder looks the finished thing over, a joint not quite square, a seam a little proud of its neighbor, and none of it diminishes what is felt standing in front of it. This is not a failure of judgment or a trick of vanity. The feeling is sincerely earned by the labor spent, hour by hour, piece by piece, and the Builder has done nothing wrong in feeling it: the object carries the hands that made it, and that carrying is exactly what ownership, felt this way, is.

## Tension

A value earned honestly, and felt by no one who did not earn it.
