# Position: Abandoner

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The seat of the unfinished build, and marks that time spent on a task is not what inflates its value, completion is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An object left incomplete, held and handled exactly as long as a finished one.
No feeling of inflated ownership, since the labor never crossed into a finished, claimable result.
Proof, by its own plain case, of what the effect actually requires.

## Orders

Hold the unfinished object honestly, neither pretending it is done nor discarding it out of shame.
Feel exactly what the labor so far has earned, which is nothing extra, since nothing has been completed.
Know that this absence is not a failure of feeling but the clearest evidence of what feeling requires.

## Loses

Nothing of its own honesty, and commits no fault for feeling nothing extra about an unfinished thing.
Because it has completed nothing, the seat takes no blame for lacking a value it never earned, and yet its case is easy to mistake for a builder simply less attached, when it is instead the plainest proof of what attachment costs.
It keeps its honestly unfinished object and loses no cause for blame, the seat that shows, by feeling nothing extra, what finishing actually earns.

## Drives

Toward the one whose unfinished labor proves what finished labor alone can earn,
holding the same parts, spending real hours, and feeling none of the extra pride,
so the seat that stops short of done is the seat that, by the same fact, never crosses into what the effect requires.
