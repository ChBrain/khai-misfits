# Piece: The Unfinished

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The bound's sharpest edge: an abandoned build, possessed as long as a finished one, showing none of the extra inflation.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Shelf](place_the_shelf.md), carried by [Abandoning](process_abandoning.md): the incomplete object sits here as it was left, gathering dust, held but never carried past the last step.

## Load Bearing

An abandoned build, begun but never finished, shows none of the inflation a completed one shows, so the effect is not the mere passage of time spent on a task or simple exposure to it, but requires a completed, successfully assembled object the builder can call finished and their own (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012). The hours put into it were real, the parts handled just as they would have been handled toward a finished thing, and still no extra value settles on it while it waits half done. What is missing is not more time on the shelf but the last step that would have let it be called complete.

## Apparent

Looks like an ordinary unfinished project, nothing to explain; is actually the clearest evidence of what the effect requires.
