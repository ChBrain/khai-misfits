# Plan: Make It Mine

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the builder's seat](position_builder.md), inside [A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Builder](position_builder.md)

## Direction

Spend real effort building the thing, honestly, from the first piece to the last. Know that finishing it will generate a felt ownership that inflates its worth in your own eyes, and that this feeling, however sincere, is earned by the labor and will not travel to anyone who did not spend it.

## Orders

([The Builder](persona_the_builder.md) holds [the Labor](piece_the_labor.md) through [Assembling](process_assembling.md) at [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), spending real effort on the build, and meets [the Ownership](piece_the_ownership.md).)

## Implementation

([Assembling](process_assembling.md) at [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md) holds [the Labor](piece_the_labor.md), because the effort is spent honestly and for its own sake, and meets [the Ownership](piece_the_ownership.md), the finished object felt as meaningfully one's own because of what it cost to make (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012).)

## Targets

- [ ] the labor is spent honestly, from the first piece of the build to the last
- [ ] the build reaches completion, not merely partial engagement
- [ ] the ownership is met: the object felt as meaningfully one's own because of the labor spent on it
- [ ] it is owned that this felt ownership belongs to no one who did not spend the labor that earned it
