# Position: Appraiser

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The seat of the outside eye, and marks that its plain judgment is honest even where it cannot meet a maker's price.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A clear, uninvested view of the finished object, judged on what it plainly is.
No history with the labor that made it, and so no access to the feeling that labor generated.
A fair price, honestly offered, for exactly what can be seen and held.

## Orders

Judge the object as it stands, by its own quality, since no history of labor is visible to you.
Offer a fair price for what you can see, and no more, since paying for a history you did not live is not honesty but guessing.
Know that a low offer is not unkindness, only the plain truth of a value you were never in a position to inflate.

## Loses

Nothing of its own fairness, and commits no fault in offering a plain, honest price.
Because its judgment is made from everything actually available to it, the seat takes no blame for a gap it did not create, and yet the gap remains, felt by the maker as coldness though none was intended.
It keeps its honestly offered price and loses no cause for blame, the eye that values a thing exactly as much as it can actually see.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honest verdict cannot meet a maker's honest pride,
judging plainly, by what is visible and nothing more,
so the seat that prices fairly is the seat that, by the same fact, can never pay for a labor it did not spend.
