# Piece: The Verdict

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The outside read: an appraiser or buyer with no stake in the labor judging the same object plainly, at what it is objectively worth.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Stall](place_the_stall.md), carried by [Appraising](process_appraising.md): the object is seen here stripped of its history, as it stands, with no memory of the hours that went into it.

## Load Bearing

The appraiser judges the object by what it plainly is, having spent none of the labor that would let it feel any other way, and this is not coldness but an honest account of an object it did not build (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012). It reads the joints, the finish, the fit, and prices what it sees, nothing added for a history it was not present for. The price offered is fair by every measure available to someone standing outside the labor, even where it lands well under what the maker expects to hear.

## Apparent

Looks like an unfairly low offer, a failure to appreciate what went into the thing; is actually the plain truth of a value never spent by the one now judging it.
