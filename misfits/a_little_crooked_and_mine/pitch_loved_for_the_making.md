# Pitch: Loved for the Making

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that labor spent building a thing is loved for the making, and that love does not transfer to anyone who did not spend it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Warm and tactile, the honest pride of a thing made with one's own hands, and the honest limit of that pride. Nothing here is vanity or unkindness; the builder's feeling is real and the appraiser's plain read is real too.

## Undertow

Labor invested in building or assembling something generates a felt sense that the finished object is meaningfully one's own, inflating its value in the builder's own eyes independent of the object's objective quality. An outside eye, with no stake in the labor spent, judges the same object at what it is plainly worth.

## Nerve

That a trade both sides would gain from can go unmade because only one side's labor set the price, no deception or poor taste required. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since the effect requires a completed build and tracks task difficulty, not a claim that all effort breeds attachment regardless of outcome (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012).

## Echo

Build it with your own hands, and love it for the making, in a way no buyer ever will.
