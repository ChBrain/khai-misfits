## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A finished object offered for trade, met by someone with no history of the labor that made it.

## Direction

From the object's plain appearance toward a fair price judged on visible quality alone.

## Lever

An outside eye, having spent none of the labor, judges the object by what it plainly is, and a price fair by that measure still falls short of what the maker's own labor makes them feel it is worth (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012).
