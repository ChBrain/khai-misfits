# Persona: The Builder

## Taxonomy

[Builder](position_builder.md), inside [A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The archetype of the maker whose own labor turns a finished object into something felt as meaningfully theirs.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person absorbed in a task, hands full, attention on the work. It looks like ordinary effort, nothing remarkable, the kind of building anyone might do for themselves.

## Action

Make it mine. At [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), on [Make It Mine](plan_make_it_mine.md) through [Assembling](process_assembling.md), the Builder holds [the Labor](piece_the_labor.md), and it meets [the Ownership](piece_the_ownership.md), the finished object felt as theirs because of what it cost to make (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012).

## Shadow

Its pride is sincere and its blind spot is exactly that sincerity. The Builder does not inflate the object's value to deceive anyone, including themselves; the value is honestly felt, generated by real labor. What it cannot see is that the feeling was earned by spending the labor, not by the object itself, and so belongs to no one who did not spend it.

## Tell

It loves a thing for the making, and expects the love to travel with the object into a stranger's hands.
