# Persona: The Abandoner

## Taxonomy

[Abandoner](position_abandoner.md), inside [A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The archetype of the builder who begins a build and never finishes it, and so shows what the effect actually requires.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

It looks like an ordinary unfinished project, a box of parts half assembled and set aside, nothing dramatic, nothing to explain.

## Action

Leave it half done. At [the Shelf](place_the_shelf.md), on [Leave It Half Done](plan_leave_it_half_done.md) through [Abandoning](process_abandoning.md), the Abandoner holds [the Unfinished](piece_the_unfinished.md), possessing the incomplete object exactly as long as a finished one but showing none of the extra inflation (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012).

## Shadow

It is not a failed builder and it is not proof the effect is false. The Abandoner shows, by holding an unfinished thing without loving it any more than a stranger would, that the value the Builder feels is not the mere fact of time spent or hands laid on a task, but the fact of finishing it.

## Tell

It holds the same parts, spends real hours on them, and feels nothing extra for any of it, since it never crossed the line into done.
