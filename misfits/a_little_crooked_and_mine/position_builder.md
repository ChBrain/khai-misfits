# Position: Builder

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The seat of the maker, and marks that the labor spent building a thing is the very labor that inflates its value in the maker's own eyes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Real effort spent on a task, honestly engaged, from the first piece to the last.
A finished object, however imperfect, that is felt as meaningfully its own because of what it cost to make.
No access to how the object looks to anyone who did not spend that labor.

## Orders

Build honestly, spending real effort on the task from start to finish.
Feel what the labor earns: a sense of ownership that inflates the object's worth in your own eyes, independent of its objective quality.
Know that this feeling, however sincere, was earned by the labor and belongs to no one who did not spend it.

## Loses

Nothing of its own honest pride, and commits no fault in feeling what real labor earns.
Because its valuation is generated by effort actually spent, the seat takes no blame for the size of the feeling, and yet the very labor that earns it is what a buyer, having spent none of it, will never share.
It keeps its honestly earned pride and loses no cause for blame, the maker who values a thing exactly as much as the labor makes it feel worth.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honest labor becomes a value only it can feel,
building for its own sake, effort spent and felt as meaningfully its own,
so the seat that earns its pride most honestly is the seat that, by the same fact, earns a pride no market will pay for.
