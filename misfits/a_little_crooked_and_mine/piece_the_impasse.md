# Piece: The Impasse

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The harm: a trade both sides would gain from going unmade, since the builder's honestly felt price sits above what any buyer will pay.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Stall](place_the_stall.md), carried by [Appraising](process_appraising.md): the two prices meet here, spoken plainly, one against the other, and do not match.

## Load Bearing

The builder's price and the appraiser's price are both honestly arrived at, and still too far apart for a trade either side would gain from, since only one side's labor produced the value the price reflects (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012). This is distinct from Loath to Part: there, mere possession alone, no labor required, is enough to inflate a value above what a buyer will pay; here the inflation is specifically the labor of construction, and possession without completing a build is not enough to produce it. Neither side is wrong about what they see; they are simply pricing two different histories of the same object.

## Apparent

Looks like stubbornness on the builder's side or stinginess on the appraiser's; is actually two honest valuations that were never going to meet.
