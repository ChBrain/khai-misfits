# Plot: By My Own Hands

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At the Workshop, a build is undertaken, parts and materials waiting to become something finished.

## Action

[The Builder](persona_the_builder.md), from [the builder's seat](position_builder.md), on [Make It Mine](plan_make_it_mine.md) through [Assembling](process_assembling.md), holds [The Labor](piece_the_labor.md) at [The Workshop](place_the_workshop.md): hands moving from the first piece to the last, real effort spent honestly across every joint and fitting.

## Stage

The labor here is genuine, spent honestly and not performed for show, and it is the raw input the whole effect will run on (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012). There is nothing remarkable in it yet. The Builder measures, fits, checks, and fits again, the way any builder works through a task for themselves, with no audience and no display in mind, only the plain requirement of getting each piece to sit where it belongs. The pieces resist a little, the way parts do, and are worked into place by hand rather than forced. Nothing about this effort announces what it will become. What it will generate, once the build stands finished, is not yet in view; for now there is only the work, done carefully, for its own sake, one piece meeting the next.

## Tension

Effort spent for its own sake, and about to become something more.
