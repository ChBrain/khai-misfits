# Piece: The Difficulty

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The bound: the effect's true, variable scope, tracking task difficulty and the labor felt as meaningfully one's own.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Shelf](place_the_shelf.md), carried by [Comparing](process_comparing.md): case set beside case, an easy build beside a hard one, weighed for what each one's labor actually earned.

## Load Bearing

The effect is not uniform; it varies with task difficulty and is roughly proportional to the labor felt as meaningfully one's own, stronger where the building demanded more of the builder and weaker where it demanded little, so the pattern is a real and variable tendency and not a claim that all effort breeds attachment regardless of outcome (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012; the psychological-ownership and effort-justification literature that followed). A few minutes of trivial assembly does not earn the same weight as an afternoon spent puzzling through a hard one, and the difference shows up honestly in what the builder later feels the thing is worth. The hedge is not a retreat from the effect; it is the shape the effect actually has.

## Apparent

Looks like a hedge that undercuts the whole pattern; is actually what keeps the pattern honest.
