# Plot: Priced Plain

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At the Stall, the finished object meets an eye that did not build it.

## Action

[The Appraiser](persona_the_appraiser.md), from [the appraiser's seat](position_appraiser.md), on [Price It Plain](plan_price_it_plain.md) through [Appraising](process_appraising.md), holds [The Verdict](piece_the_verdict.md) at [The Stall](place_the_stall.md): the object turned over, weighed, and judged at what it is objectively worth.

## Stage

The Appraiser judges the object by what it plainly is, having spent none of the labor that would let it feel any other way, and this is not coldness but an honest account of an object it did not build (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012). A joint checked, a seam noted, a finish compared against what the market already knows how to price, and a number arrived at that owes nothing to hours the Appraiser never spent. The price offered is fair by every measure available to someone standing outside the labor that made the thing: it reflects the object as it stands, not the hands that stood behind it. There is no unkindness in the figure named. It is simply what an outside eye, honest and unhurried, can see.

## Tension

A fair price for everything visible, and nothing for what is not.
