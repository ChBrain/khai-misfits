# Process: Abandoning

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). Borne at [The Shelf](place_the_shelf.md), precipitated by [The Abandoner](persona_the_abandoner.md), in [Left Half Done](plot_left_half_done.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A build set aside before it is finished, for any ordinary reason.

## Direction

From partial labor toward an object held, but never completed, and so never inflated in value.

## Lever

The felt ownership that inflates a builder's valuation requires a completed, successfully assembled object, and does not attach to time spent or partial effort alone (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012).

[The Unfinished](piece_the_unfinished.md).
