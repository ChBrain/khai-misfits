# Position: Witness

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The seat of the accumulated cases, and marks that the effect's honesty is measured by what it refuses to claim as much as by what it documents.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The accumulated cases, real and repeatedly demonstrated, a builder's love outrunning a market's plain price.
No case that proves more than the difficulty it documents, since the inflation is not the same size for every build.
The true, bounded scope, roughly proportional to the labor felt as meaningfully one's own.

## Orders

Hold the pattern to what the cases show, and generalize no further than the evidence reaches.
Resist the larger claim the accumulated cases seem to invite, that all effort breeds attachment regardless of outcome, since a simple, easy build inflates far less than a hard one.
Keep the edges of the pattern as carefully as the pattern itself, since a claim held past its true scope is a different, weaker claim than the one the evidence supports.

## Loses

No confidence overclaimed, and commits no error by refusing the bigger story the cases seem to want it to tell.
Because its authority rests on holding exactly what the record supports, the seat takes no credit for a broader claim it declines to make, and yet the discipline of refusing it is easy to mistake for a weaker case than the one actually made.
It keeps its honestly bounded pattern and loses no cause for blame, the seat that documents the effect without overreaching it.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honesty is measured by what it refuses to claim as much as by what it documents,
holding case after case to its own true scope, strongest where the labor was hardest and weakest where it was least,
so the seat that proves the pattern real is the seat that, by the same fact, proves it is not everywhere the same size.
