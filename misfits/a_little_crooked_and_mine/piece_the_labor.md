# Piece: The Labor

## Taxonomy

[A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The setup: real effort spent building or assembling something, honestly, for its own sake.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), carried by [Assembling](process_assembling.md): parts laid out, tools at hand, and time enough to work through the steps without hurry.

## Load Bearing

The labor is genuine, spent honestly and not performed for show; there is no audience to impress and no shortcut taken for effect. It is the raw input the whole effect runs on, the hours and hands that will later be felt as the reason the finished thing belongs to its maker (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012). Nothing about the labor itself is remarkable: parts fitted to parts, steps followed in order, the ordinary friction of putting something together. What it will generate is another matter entirely, but the labor does not know that yet. It is spent the same way whether the result turns out plumb and square or a little crooked.

## Apparent

Looks like ordinary work, nothing unusual, the kind of building anyone might do for themselves on an afternoon with nowhere else to be.

## Yearbook

Spent by [the Builder](persona_the_builder.md) through [Assembling](process_assembling.md) at [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), dramatized in [By My Own Hands](plot_by_my_own_hands.md). It becomes [the Ownership](piece_the_ownership.md), and, left short of completion, it is what [the Unfinished](piece_the_unfinished.md) shows is not enough on its own.
