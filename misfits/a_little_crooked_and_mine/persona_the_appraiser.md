# Persona: The Appraiser

## Taxonomy

[Appraiser](position_appraiser.md), inside [A Little Crooked, and Mine](play_a_little_crooked_and_mine.md). The archetype of the outside eye, buyer or stranger, who judges a built object at what it is plainly worth, with no stake in the labor that made it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

It looks like coldness, a low offer for something that plainly cost someone real effort, an unwillingness to pay for what the maker clearly poured into the thing.

## Action

Price it plain. At [the Stall](place_the_stall.md), on [Price It Plain](plan_price_it_plain.md) through [Appraising](process_appraising.md), the Appraiser holds [the Verdict](piece_the_verdict.md), and it meets [the Impasse](piece_the_impasse.md), the trade going unmade because the builder's honestly felt price sits above what any buyer will pay (Norton, Mochon and Ariely, 2012).

## Shadow

It is not cold and it is not wrong. The Appraiser judges the object by what it plainly is, since it never spent the labor that would let it feel the object any other way, and there is no dishonesty in valuing a thing by what one can actually see and hold rather than by a history one did not live.

## Tell

It offers a fair price for what it can see, and cannot offer a cent for what it did not make.
