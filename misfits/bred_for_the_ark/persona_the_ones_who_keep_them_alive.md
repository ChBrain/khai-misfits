# Persona: The Ones Who Keep Them Alive

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Keep Them Alive](position_ones_who_keep_them_alive.md). The keepers, whose duty runs to the animals present.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Keepers and veterinary staff who know each animal by name, its history, its temperament and its bloodline.
Working to standards that took a century to develop and that nobody would want lowered.

## Action

Feeds, treats, shelters, excludes predators and parasites, and intervenes at every birth that needs it.
Loses almost nobody, which is the measure they are held to and the right one.

## Shadow

Has no version of the work that avoids the effect.
Predators cannot be introduced, animals cannot be left hungry to keep their foraging sharp, and every hardship that might preserve a wild trait is weighed against a population that may be the last: so the duty to the animals in front of them is discharged properly and completely, and discharging it is what does the selecting.

## Tell

Is judged on survival, achieves it, and cannot be asked to trade any of it for a trait nobody can see.
