# Piece: The Soft Release Pen

## Taxonomy

[The Range They Came From](place_the_range_they_came_from.md). The halfway structure, which helps and cannot teach a history.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the edge of [the range they came from](place_the_range_they_came_from.md), where animals are held, acclimatised and provisioned before and after the gate is opened.

## Load Bearing

It is the best bridge anybody has built and it bridges the wrong gap.
Acclimatisation to weather, terrain and local conditions is real and improves survival, and what it cannot supply is the outcome of generations of selection: a few weeks of support can teach an animal where it is, and cannot give it back a wariness that was bred out over fourteen generations, because that was never knowledge in the first place.

## Apparent

Careful, staged, monitored, provisioned, and far better than opening a crate and driving away.
An honest attempt at the hardest part of the whole programme.

## Yearbook

Developed after early reintroductions failed at rates nobody had expected, and it has improved them.
Works on what an individual can learn, against a deficit that accumulated in a lineage.
