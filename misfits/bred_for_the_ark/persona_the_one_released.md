# Persona: The One Released

## Taxonomy

[One Released](position_one_released.md). The animal put back, into a place it has never been.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An individual from the fourth or the fourteenth captive generation, health-checked, radio-collared and carried out in a crate.
Never wild, and descended from animals that were.

## Action

Is soft-released, supported, monitored and provisioned for as long as the programme can afford.
Does less well than a wild-born animal, in ways that are measured and expected.

## Shadow

Is returned to a range that did not wait, carrying traits fitted to a place that no longer applies.
Adaptation to one environment reduces fitness in others, so what was gained inside is a liability outside, and the habitat itself has gone on changing throughout: this persona is therefore mismatched twice over, once because it changed and once because home did, and neither shift was anybody's decision.

## Tell

Is the point of the whole programme, and the least prepared member of its lineage for the thing the programme was for.
