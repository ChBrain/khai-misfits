# Piece: The Studbook

## Taxonomy

[The Enclosure](place_the_enclosure.md). The pedigree record, managing the one thing that can be managed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held across institutions and consulted before every pairing at [the enclosure](place_the_enclosure.md), with animals moved between countries on its authority.

## Load Bearing

It controls who breeds with whom, which is genuinely powerful, and not what the breeding is being judged against.
Equalising family sizes and avoiding close pairings preserve diversity and slow the accumulation of adaptation to captivity, which is real and measurable work: and the slowing does not reduce the deleterious effect on the population once reintroduced, because the selecting environment is the enclosure and no pairing decision changes what the enclosure rewards.

## Apparent

Meticulous, international, decades deep, and the reason many programmes retain any diversity at all.
The most sophisticated instrument in the play.

## Yearbook

Built to solve inbreeding and the loss of diversity, which were the first problems captive programmes met, and which it does solve.
Silent on the direction of selection, since it can allocate parentage and cannot allocate an environment.
