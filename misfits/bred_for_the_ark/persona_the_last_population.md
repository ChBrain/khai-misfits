# Persona: The Last Population

## Taxonomy

[Last Population](position_last_population.md). The animals taken in, and everything the species now has.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A few dozen individuals, catalogued, health-screened, and held because the wild count was going to zero.
The entire remaining stock of a lineage, in one set of buildings.

## Action

Lives, breeds, and is kept from every hazard that was killing them.
Produces generations, each of which is a sample of the one before.

## Shadow

Cannot be held still, because it is alive.
A population that breeds is a population being resampled every generation, and resampling under conditions is selection whether anybody intends it or not: so the one property that makes this persona worth saving, that it is a living lineage rather than a specimen, is the property that guarantees it will not be the same lineage when it is given back.

## Tell

Is preserved successfully, and is not the thing that was preserved.
