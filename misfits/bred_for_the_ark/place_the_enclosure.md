# Place: The Enclosure

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md). The place that was meant to be a pause and is an environment.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Heat, shelter, a quarantine block, a veterinary suite, enrichment designed by people who have thought hard about it.
No predators, no droughts, no failed seasons, and food that arrives.

## Holds

[The Provided Food](piece_the_provided_food.md), reliable, adequate and identical in composition week after week.
[The Last Population](persona_the_last_population.md), breeding, generation on generation.

## Offers

Survival at rates the range could never produce, and a lineage that would otherwise have ended.
Conditions that improve every decade as the field learns more.

## Withheld

Any of the pressures that made the animals what they were.
The place cannot supply scarcity, danger or seasonal failure without harming the animals it exists to protect, so everything that shaped the species is absent by design and the absences are the achievement: which means the better this place becomes at its purpose, the further what lives in it moves from what was brought in.
