# Pitch: Nobody Meant to Change Them

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Patient and careful. A studbook kept meticulously, pairings computed to hold the genetics broad, keepers who know every animal by name and its whole history, veterinary records nobody could fault.
A population that would otherwise be zero, maintained by people doing everything right.

## Undertow

Under the care, an enclosure that is an environment like any other, quietly rewarding whatever does well in it, generation after generation.
And a range that goes on changing while the animals are held still on purpose.

## Nerve

The vertigo of realising that keeping something safe is a thing you do to it.

## Echo

Afterward: wariness gone because it cost energy and bought nothing, family sizes equalised in a way that slows the drift without lessening the harm, and advice to release within a few generations into the habitat that is still the reason they were taken in.
