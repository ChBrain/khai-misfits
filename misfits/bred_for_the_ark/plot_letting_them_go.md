# Plot: Letting Them Go

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The problem understood, and the corrections on the table: equalise the families, cross replicate populations, release within a few generations, or freeze the material instead.

## Action

[Put them back](plan_put_them_back.md): [The One Released](persona_the_one_released.md) goes out through [the soft release pen](piece_the_soft_release_pen.md), supported, provisioned and monitored for as long as the budget allows.

## Stage

Equalising family sizes reduces how fast adaptation accumulates, and does not reduce its deleterious effect on the reintroduced population.

## Tension

Every correction reaches the rate, the timing or the material, and none of them reaches the harm.
[The Studbook](piece_the_studbook.md) can allocate parentage and cannot allocate an environment. Releasing early is the one measure that touches the mechanism, and it spends precious founders into a habitat where the original threat usually persists. Cryopreservation stops the adaptation completely by stopping the life, and fixes the lineage at one instant of a world that goes on moving. The soft release pen teaches an individual where it is, against a deficit that accumulated across a lineage.
