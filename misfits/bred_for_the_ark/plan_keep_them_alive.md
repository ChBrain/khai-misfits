# Plan: Keep Them Alive

## Taxonomy

A **duty of care**: the intent of [The Ones Who Keep Them Alive](persona_the_ones_who_keep_them_alive.md), inside [Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The Ones Who Keep Them Alive](persona_the_ones_who_keep_them_alive.md)

## Direction

Do not lose any of them. Feed them properly, treat what ails them, exclude what kills them, and intervene wherever intervention improves survival, because these may be the last.

## Orders

(Provide [the provided food](piece_the_provided_food.md) daily to specification; maintain quarantine and veterinary care at [the enclosure](place_the_enclosure.md); exclude predators and parasites; assist at every birth that needs it.)

## Implementation

([The Enclosure](place_the_enclosure.md), improving every decade as the field learns more; the [taking them in](process_taking_them_in.md) process; standards developed over a century that nobody would want lowered.)

## Targets

- [ ] survival rates far above anything the range could produce
- [ ] health, nutrition and breeding success all improving with each revision of practice
- [ ] no hardship imposed to preserve a trait, since these may be the last of them
- [ ] every improvement in care increases the effect, because the care is what the animals are being fitted to
