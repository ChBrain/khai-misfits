# Plot: Selection Does Not Switch Off

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An enclosure with a roof, no predators, a fixed breeding schedule, and generations passing inside it.

## Action

[Fitting the enclosure](process_fitting_the_enclosure.md): [The Selection That Does Not Stop](persona_the_selection_that_does_not_stop.md), holding [Selection That Does Not Stop](position_selection_that_does_not_stop.md), rewards whatever does well here, generation on generation.

## Stage

Animals that tolerate people, breed on the available schedule and thrive on what is provided leave more descendants than animals that do not.

## Tension

Captivity is not a pause, it is a place, and no standard of care reaches this.
The process would run identically in a perfectly managed facility, because it is not a symptom of husbandry but the arithmetic of who leaves descendants applied to an environment. The only way to stop it is to stop the population breeding, which is indistinguishable from not having the population. So there is nothing here to improve and nobody selecting for anything.
