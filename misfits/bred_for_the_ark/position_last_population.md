# Position: Last Population

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md). The seat that cannot be held still because it is alive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A few dozen individuals, catalogued and screened, and the whole remaining stock of a lineage.
Generations, arriving whether anybody wants them or not.

## Orders

Live.
Breed.

## Loses

Its own identity across time, as the price of continuing.
A population that breeds is resampled every generation, and resampling under conditions is selection whether or not anybody intends it: so the property that makes this seat worth saving, that it is a living lineage rather than a specimen in a jar, is exactly the property that guarantees it will not be the same lineage when it is handed back.

## Drives

Toward being preserved successfully, and not being the thing that was preserved.
