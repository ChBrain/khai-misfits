# Plot: Tame Is the Fit One

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

Wariness, which costs energy and buys nothing where there are no predators, and a foraging repertoire that is worthless where [the provided food](piece_the_provided_food.md) arrives daily.

## Action

[The Selection That Does Not Stop](persona_the_selection_that_does_not_stop.md) discounts both, quietly, while [The Ones Who Keep Them Alive](persona_the_ones_who_keep_them_alive.md), holding [Ones Who Keep Them Alive](position_ones_who_keep_them_alive.md), do everything correctly.

## Stage

The traits the range requires are exactly the traits the enclosure charges for and does not pay for.

## Tension

The keepers cannot impose the pressures that would preserve what is being lost, and should not be asked to.
Predators cannot be introduced. Animals cannot be left hungry to keep their foraging sharp. Every such measure is weighed against the possibility that these are the last of them, and loses. So the duty to the animals in front of them is discharged properly and completely, and discharging it is the whole of the mechanism: every improvement in care makes the effect larger, not smaller.
