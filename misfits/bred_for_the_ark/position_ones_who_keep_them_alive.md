# Position: Ones Who Keep Them Alive

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md). The seat whose correct discharge of duty does the selecting.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Veterinary standards developed over a century, a duty to the animals present, and a population that may be the last.
Every tool for keeping animals alive and none for keeping them wild.

## Orders

Feed them, treat them, shelter them, exclude what kills them.
Lose nobody.

## Loses

The option of imposing the hardship that would preserve the trait.
Predators cannot be introduced, animals cannot be left hungry to keep their foraging sharp, and every such measure is weighed against the possibility that these are the last of them: so the duty is discharged properly and completely, and discharging it properly is the mechanism, which means no improvement in care reduces the effect and every improvement increases it.

## Drives

Toward survival, which is what the seat is judged on and what it should be judged on.
