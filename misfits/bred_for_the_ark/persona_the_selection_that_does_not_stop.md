# Persona: The Selection That Does Not Stop

## Taxonomy

[Selection That Does Not Stop](position_selection_that_does_not_stop.md). The process with no setting for held in trust.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Differential survival and reproduction, operating exactly as it does everywhere else, in a building with a roof.
Not a force anybody switched on, and not one anybody can switch off.

## Action

Rewards whatever does well here: tolerating people, breeding on the available schedule, thriving on what is provided.
Discounts whatever costs energy and returns nothing here, which is most of what the range requires.

## Shadow

Would operate identically in a perfectly run facility, so no standard of care reaches it.
This persona is not a symptom of poor husbandry that better husbandry would remove: it is the arithmetic of who leaves descendants, applied to an environment, and the only way to stop it is to stop the population breeding, which is the same as not having it.

## Tell

Selects continuously, on nobody's behalf, toward fitness for the place that was meant to be temporary.
