# Process: Opening the Gate

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md). The return, into a place that changed meanwhile.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A habitat judged ready enough, or a programme that cannot wait any longer.
[The One Released](persona_the_one_released.md), holding [One Released](position_one_released.md), from [the soft release pen](piece_the_soft_release_pen.md).

## Direction

Toward [the range they came from](place_the_range_they_came_from.md), with support, provisioning and monitoring for as long as the budget allows.
Toward survival rates below those of wild-born animals, in ways that are measured and expected.

## Lever

A double mismatch: the lineage moved while it was held, and the range moved while it waited.

## Echo

The purpose of the whole programme, carried out by the least prepared members its line has produced.
