# Plot: Home Moved Too

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Twenty years passing in [the range they came from](place_the_range_they_came_from.md): land use, climate, introduced competitors, new diseases.

## Action

The population is held still by design in [the enclosure](place_the_enclosure.md), while nothing outside is under any such arrangement.

## Stage

The animals are being returned to a second environment, which is not the one their founders left.

## Tension

The target moved throughout the period of protection, and holding still was the whole point.
Preservation assumes a thing worth returning to, fixed enough to be returned to, and a range is not that: it is a set of pressures that carries on operating and changing whether or not anybody is out there experiencing them. So the programme produces a double mismatch, once because the lineage changed and once because home did, and neither displacement was chosen or noticed by anybody at the time.
