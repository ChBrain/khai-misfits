# Process: Breeding Them On

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md). Generations arriving, each a sample of the last.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Pairings computed from [the studbook](piece_the_studbook.md), and animals moved between institutions to make them.
[The Ones Who Manage the Line](persona_the_ones_who_manage_the_line.md), holding [Ones Who Manage the Line](position_ones_who_manage_the_line.md).

## Direction

Toward preserved diversity, equalised families and avoided close pairings, all of which succeed.
Toward a population resampled every generation, which is the thing that cannot be prevented.

## Lever

Reproduction, which is what makes the lineage worth saving and what makes it move.

## Echo

Diversity held, drift slowed, and the direction of selection untouched by any of it.
