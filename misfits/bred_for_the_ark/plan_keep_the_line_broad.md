# Plan: Keep the Line Broad

## Taxonomy

A **genetic management**: the intent of [The Ones Who Manage the Line](persona_the_ones_who_manage_the_line.md), inside [Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The Ones Who Manage the Line](persona_the_ones_who_manage_the_line.md)

## Direction

Hold the diversity the founders brought. Equalise contributions, avoid close pairings, keep replicate populations apart and cross them, and move animals between institutions to make the pairings that matter.

## Orders

(Maintain [the studbook](piece_the_studbook.md) across institutions; compute pairings from relatedness; equalise family sizes; hold replicates separately and cross them; run it through [breeding them on](process_breeding_them_on.md).)

## Implementation

([The Enclosure](place_the_enclosure.md), and every other institution holding part of the population; decades of pedigree data and well-founded methods.)

## Targets

- [ ] inbreeding is avoided and founder representation is kept even
- [ ] genetic diversity is preserved measurably better than it would otherwise be
- [ ] the rate at which adaptation to captivity accumulates is reduced
- [ ] the deleterious effect on the reintroduced population is not reduced, because the environment doing the selecting is unchanged by any pairing decision
