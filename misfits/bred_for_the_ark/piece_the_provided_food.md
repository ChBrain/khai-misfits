# Piece: The Provided Food

## Taxonomy

[The Enclosure](place_the_enclosure.md). The ration, which is care and is also a change of world.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Delivered daily in [the enclosure](place_the_enclosure.md), formulated by nutritionists, adequate, reliable and much the same each week.

## Load Bearing

It removes the single largest source of selection the species ever faced.
Finding food across seasons, competitors and failures is most of what a wild life consists of, and a ration ends that entirely: so the repertoire of skills and preferences that took the lineage its whole history to assemble stops being tested, stops being paid for, and therefore stops being maintained.

## Apparent

Excellent husbandry, improving with every decade of nutritional research, and plainly good for the animals.
The most obviously benevolent object in the enclosure.

## Yearbook

Refined constantly toward better health, longer life and higher breeding success, all of which it achieves.
Cannot be made to vary in the way the range varies without imposing hunger on a population that may be the last.
