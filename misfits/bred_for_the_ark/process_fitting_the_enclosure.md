# Process: Fitting the Enclosure

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md). Adaptation to the place that was meant to be temporary.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An environment with a roof, no predators, and [the provided food](piece_the_provided_food.md) arriving daily.
[The Selection That Does Not Stop](persona_the_selection_that_does_not_stop.md), holding [Selection That Does Not Stop](position_selection_that_does_not_stop.md).

## Direction

Toward tolerance of people, breeding on the available schedule, and thriving on what is given.
Away from wariness and a broad foraging repertoire, which cost energy and return nothing here.

## Lever

Cost without benefit: the traits the range requires are exactly the traits the enclosure charges for and does not pay for.

## Echo

Adaptation accumulating with generations held, so the longer the programme succeeds the less the population can do what it was saved to do.
