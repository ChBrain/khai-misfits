# Persona: The Ones Who Manage the Line

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Manage the Line](position_ones_who_manage_the_line.md). The population geneticists, doing the only work available.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The people who hold the studbook, compute the pairings, track relatedness across institutions and decide who is moved where.
Sophisticated, well-founded work, and the reason many programmes have any diversity left.

## Action

Equalises family sizes, avoids close pairings, maintains and crosses replicate populations.
Slows the loss of genetic diversity, measurably.

## Shadow

Reaches the rate and not the harm.
Equalising family sizes reduces how fast adaptation to captivity accumulates and does not reduce its deleterious effect on the population once reintroduced, so this persona is not failing at its task and is succeeding at a task adjacent to the one that matters: what can be managed is who breeds with whom, and what cannot is that the place doing the choosing is the enclosure.

## Tell

Does everything the field knows how to do, and the animals still come out fitted to the building.
