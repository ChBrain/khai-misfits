# Process: Taking Them In

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md). The intervention, made because the alternative is zero.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A wild count falling toward nothing, and a cause that nobody has yet fixed.
[The Ones Who Keep Them Alive](persona_the_ones_who_keep_them_alive.md), acting on the only option left.

## Direction

Toward [the enclosure](place_the_enclosure.md): warmth, veterinary care, quarantine, and the exclusion of everything that was killing them.
Toward a founding group that is now the whole species.

## Lever

Necessity, which is genuine: this is usually not a choice between captivity and the wild but between captivity and nothing.

## Echo

A lineage that exists because somebody intervened, and a first generation already living somewhere new.
