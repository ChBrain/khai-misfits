# Plot: The Only Ones Left

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A wild count falling toward nothing, and a cause nobody has yet fixed.

## Action

[Taking them in](process_taking_them_in.md): [The Ones Who Keep Them Alive](persona_the_ones_who_keep_them_alive.md) bring a founding group into [the enclosure](place_the_enclosure.md), where [The Last Population](persona_the_last_population.md) is warm, fed, treated and safe from everything that was killing it.

## Stage

Survival rates rise immediately to levels the range could never produce. The lineage exists because somebody intervened.

## Tension

This is not a choice between captivity and the wild, and pretending otherwise is where most arguments about it go wrong.
The alternative on the table was nothing, so the intervention is right and is usually the only thing available. Everything that follows in this play follows from doing it, and none of it is an argument against having done it: the misfit begins at the moment the decision is correct, which is why it cannot be avoided by deciding better.
