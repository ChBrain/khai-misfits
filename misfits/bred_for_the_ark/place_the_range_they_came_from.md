# Place: The Range They Came From

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md). Home, which did not wait.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The country the founders were taken from: its predators, its seasons, its competitors and its diseases.
Twenty years of change since, in land use, climate, introduced species and everything else that moves.

## Holds

[The Soft Release Pen](piece_the_soft_release_pen.md), at the edge of it, where the transition is supposed to happen.
The reason the animals were removed, usually still present in some form.

## Offers

The only place the species can ultimately be a species rather than a collection.
Every pressure that made it what it was, still operating, on whoever is put into it.

## Withheld

Any obligation to stay as it was.
The population was held still deliberately and this place was under no such arrangement, so the target moved throughout the period of protection: the programme is therefore returning animals shaped by one environment into a second environment that is not the one their founders left, and neither of those two divergences was chosen by anybody.
