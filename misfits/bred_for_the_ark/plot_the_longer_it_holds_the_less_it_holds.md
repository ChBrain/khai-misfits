# Plot: The Longer It Holds, the Less It Holds

## Taxonomy

[Bred for the Ark](play_bred_for_the_ark.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Adaptation to captivity accumulating with the number of generations held, and a habitat that is not yet fixed.

## Action

[Breeding them on](process_breeding_them_on.md): [The Last Population](persona_the_last_population.md), holding [Last Population](position_last_population.md), continues successfully, year after year, exactly as the programme intends.

## Stage

The standard advice is to release within a few generations, into the range that is usually still the reason the animals were removed.

## Tension

The programme's success and its loss of purpose are the same quantity.
A captive population maintained for thirty years is thirty years of insurance held and thirty years of adaptation accrued, so the better it does at the thing it was built for, the less able it becomes to deliver what it was built for. And the release-early advice collides with the only reason the population is captive: the threat outside has usually not been dealt with, so the window that protects the genetics is the window in which the habitat is still lethal.
