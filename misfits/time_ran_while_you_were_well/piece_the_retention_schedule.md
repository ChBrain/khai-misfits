# Piece: The Retention Schedule

## Taxonomy

[The Records Room](place_the_records_room.md). The document that lawfully destroys the case, either way.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the wall of [the records room](place_the_records_room.md), and in the policy manual, approved by counsel and audited annually.

## Load Bearing

It converts the passage of time into the impossibility of proof, on purpose and correctly.
Retention periods exist for real reasons, storage cost, data protection obligations, the duty not to hold personal information longer than needed, and they are frequently mandatory: so the destruction that makes an old claim unanswerable is not neglect or concealment but compliance, performed by people who would be criticised for doing anything else.

## Apparent

A table of document classes and periods, unremarkable, in every organisation of any size.
Sensible, defensible and entirely ordinary.

## Yearbook

Written against risks that were legible when it was drafted: cost, clutter, privacy, and the danger of keeping what should not be kept.
Silent on latency, because a schedule that anticipated a claim arriving forty years later would have to keep everything forever, which is the thing it exists to prevent.
