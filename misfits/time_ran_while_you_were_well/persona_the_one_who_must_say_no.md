# Persona: The One Who Must Say No

## Taxonomy

[One Who Must Say No](position_one_who_must_say_no.md). The judge, who may believe the claim entirely and end it anyway.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A judge trying limitation as a preliminary issue, before the evidence, the witnesses or the merits.
Fully capable of seeing what the case is probably worth, and entirely without a route to it.

## Action

Hears the point, applies the statute, and dismisses.
Sometimes says, in the judgment, that the outcome is a hard one, which changes nothing.

## Shadow

Is not exercising a discretion and mostly has none to exercise.
The bar is a rule rather than a balancing exercise in most systems, so a claim that is meritorious, provable and serious ends without its merits being heard, and the judge is not making a mistake, softening a hard case or failing to notice: the correct application of a good rule is the whole of what happens.

## Tell

Dismisses a claim without ever finding out whether it was true.
