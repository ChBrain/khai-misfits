# Persona: The One Who Did Not Know

## Taxonomy

[One Who Did Not Know](position_one_who_did_not_know.md). The claimant, who did not delay by a single day.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person who inhaled a fibre in 1974 and was diagnosed in 2016, mesothelioma commonly taking twenty to fifty years to appear.
Well, working and untroubled for four decades, with nothing anywhere to investigate.

## Action

Brings the claim as soon as there is a claim to bring, which is the month of the diagnosis.
Is met with a period that expired while they were healthy.

## Shadow

Does not resemble the person the rule was written about, in any particular.
Limitation exists to stop claimants sleeping on their rights, and this persona had no right to sleep on: there was nothing to know, nothing to investigate and nothing to bring, so the conduct the bar is designed to discourage did not occur here and the bar applies at full strength anyway.

## Tell

Is barred for delay, having delayed by nothing, in a period spent entirely in good health.
