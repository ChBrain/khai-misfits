# Plot: When Should You Have Known

## Taxonomy

[Time Ran While You Were Well](play_time_ran_while_you_were_well.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The correction, adopted almost everywhere for latent disease: start the clock when the injury was, or reasonably should have been, discovered.

## Action

[Start the clock later](plan_start_the_clock_later.md): [the discovery rule](piece_the_discovery_rule.md) is applied, and the question at [the preliminary point](place_the_preliminary_point.md) becomes what this person ought to have suspected decades ago.

## Stage

The claimant with nothing to know is no longer barred before the injury exists, which is the right outcome and is now the law in most places for latent disease.

## Tension

The conflict is not dissolved by the fix, it is decided the other way, and the cost moves to the other party.
The defendant now faces exactly the forty-year-old allegation with no records that the period existed to prevent. The courts must determine when somebody ought to have known something, which is a finding about a state of knowledge that never existed and generates litigation of its own, and it leaves the claimant who suspected early worse off than the one who suspected nothing. And underneath it all [the statute of repose](piece_the_statute_of_repose.md) runs from the defendant's own act, immune by construction, since a period that discovery could extend would not be a repose: so the claim can be dead before the disease is alive, in a jurisdiction that has the discovery rule and applies it faithfully.
