# Plot: The Point Is Taken

## Taxonomy

[Time Ran While You Were Well](play_time_ran_while_you_were_well.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A claim issued the month of the diagnosis, and a defendant holding no records, no witnesses and no memory of the 1970s.

## Action

[Taking the point](process_taking_the_point.md): [The One Who Did It](persona_the_one_who_did_it.md), holding [One Who Did It](position_one_who_did_it.md), pleads limitation, which the law provided deliberately and expects to be used.

## Stage

The defence is asserted properly, by a party entitled to assert it, and says nothing at all about what happened.

## Tension

The party pleading the bar may be entirely innocent, and this is the version the play insists on.
It is not necessarily a wrongdoer sheltering behind a technicality: it may be a company that never did the thing, whose proof of that went into a shredder on a schedule its own lawyers approved and an auditor checked. For that party the bar is not an escape but the only defence still available, and winning on it leaves the accusation permanently unresolved rather than refuted, which is a poor prize for somebody who did nothing.
