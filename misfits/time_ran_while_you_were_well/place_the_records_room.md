# Place: The Records Room

## Taxonomy

[Time Ran While You Were Well](play_time_ran_while_you_were_well.md). Where the proof is destroyed, correctly and on time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Shelving, boxes, a schedule on the wall, and a contractor who comes on the appointed day with a shredder.
Everything documented, signed off, and done exactly as the policy requires.

## Holds

[The Retention Schedule](piece_the_retention_schedule.md), which says how long each class of document is kept and when it goes.
Certificates of destruction, which are themselves retained longer than most of what they destroyed.

## Offers

Compliance, storage costs contained, and the privacy obligations that require deletion honoured properly.
A defensible answer to any auditor who asks why a document no longer exists.

## Withheld

Everything, eventually, to everybody.
The room takes the defence and the claim together and cannot be asked to keep one and not the other, since nobody knows in advance which box holds the exoneration and which holds the admission: so the place that makes an old allegation unanswerable is also the place that makes it unrefutable, operating lawfully and on a schedule somebody was obliged to write.
