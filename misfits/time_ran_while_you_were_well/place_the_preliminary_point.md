# Place: The Preliminary Point

## Taxonomy

[Time Ran While You Were Well](play_time_ran_while_you_were_well.md). Where the case ends before it starts.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A hearing listed for a day, on one issue, before any evidence about the facts is called.
Counsel on both sides, a judge, dates and a statute, and nobody present who saw anything happen.

## Holds

[The Statute of Repose](piece_the_statute_of_repose.md), and the argument about whether [the discovery rule](piece_the_discovery_rule.md) applies at all.
A case that will be decided entirely on calendars.

## Offers

Efficiency, genuinely: an issue that can dispose of the whole matter is properly tried first, sparing everybody the cost of a trial that could not have mattered.
A clean, reviewable answer to a question with a right answer.

## Withheld

The facts, permanently.
Nothing that happened in 1974 will be examined here and nothing about it will be examined anywhere afterwards either, so the place produces a correct and final disposal of a dispute whose subject matter it never touches: the claim is not rejected, it is left untried, and there is no forum in which it becomes triable later.
