# Persona: The One Who Did It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Did It](position_one_who_did_it.md). The defendant, entitled to the bar, and possibly blameless.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A company, a contractor, an authority, or a successor to one, facing an allegation about the 1970s.
Holding no records, no witnesses and no institutional memory of the period, all of which is compliant rather than convenient.

## Action

Pleads limitation, which is a defence the law provided deliberately and expects to be used.
Answers the point without touching the facts, because the facts are not reachable.

## Shadow

May be entirely innocent and has no way of showing it.
The persona is not necessarily a wrongdoer sheltering behind a technicality: it may be a party that never did the thing, whose proof of that went into a shredder on a schedule its lawyers signed off, so asserting the bar can be the only defence available to somebody with a complete answer they can no longer evidence.

## Tell

Wins on a point that is correct, without anybody ever learning whether it should have won on the facts.
