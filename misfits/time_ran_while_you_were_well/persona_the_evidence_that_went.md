# Persona: The Evidence That Went

## Taxonomy

[Evidence That Went](position_evidence_that_went.md). The proof that decays, which is why the rule is right.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Files shredded on a retention schedule, a site demolished, a supplier dissolved, a supervisor dead, and a colleague certain of something nobody can check.
The reason repose exists, present in the play as a fact rather than as an argument.

## Action

Disappears, lawfully, on schedule, and often because a rule required it to.
Takes with it the defence as reliably as the claim.

## Shadow

Destroys the innocent defendant's protection first.
A company that did nothing wrong cannot now show that it did nothing wrong, having complied with every retention rule it was given: so the decay is not a convenience for the guilty, it is a symmetrical loss that makes the old allegation genuinely unanswerable, which is exactly what makes the argument for the bar so hard to answer.

## Tell

Is the strongest case for closing the books, and it closes them on the claimant who could not have come sooner.
