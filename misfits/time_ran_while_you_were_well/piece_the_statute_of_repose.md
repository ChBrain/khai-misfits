# Piece: The Statute of Repose

## Taxonomy

[The Preliminary Point](place_the_preliminary_point.md). The second clock, which the correction does not reach.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Underneath everything argued at [the preliminary point](place_the_preliminary_point.md), running from the defendant's own act regardless of what anybody knew.

## Load Bearing

It is the reason the fix is incomplete by design rather than by oversight.
A statute of repose runs from the completion of the work or the sale of the product and is generally not subject to [the discovery rule](piece_the_discovery_rule.md) at all, so in construction and product cases the claim can be extinguished before the disease exists, in a jurisdiction that has the discovery rule and applies it faithfully.

## Apparent

A second period, shorter in effect than the biology it governs, enacted deliberately by legislatures that knew what they were doing.
Defended on the ground that some parties must eventually be free of exposure altogether, which is a real argument.

## Yearbook

Enacted precisely because the discovery rule made liability open-ended, so it is not a survival of the old regime but a response to the new one.
Immune to the correction by construction, since a period that could be extended by discovery would be a limitation period and not a repose.
