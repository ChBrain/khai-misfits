# Plot: Running From the Wrong Day

## Taxonomy

[Time Ran While You Were Well](play_time_ran_while_you_were_well.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The drafting choice: the period begins on the day the thing was done.

## Action

[Running from the act](process_running_from_the_act.md): the clock starts at the release, the sale or the completion, and runs without reference to anybody's knowledge.

## Stage

For almost every harm this is correct, because the act and the injury arrive together and the claimant knows at once.

## Tension

The assumption is right about nearly everything and silent about the exceptions, which are the serious ones.
Nothing in the rule looks at knowledge, because when it was written there was no gap to look at: injury followed conduct closely enough that measuring from either gave the same answer. So the start date is fixed to the defendant's behaviour and asked to govern the claimant's rights, and the cases where those two come apart are not a random selection of cases.
