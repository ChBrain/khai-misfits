# Plan: Start the Clock Later

## Taxonomy

A **correction**: the intent of [The One Who Must Say No](persona_the_one_who_must_say_no.md), inside [Time Ran While You Were Well](play_time_ran_while_you_were_well.md), once the mismatch is named.

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Must Say No](persona_the_one_who_must_say_no.md)

## Direction

Measure from knowledge rather than from conduct. Start the period when the injury was, or reasonably should have been, discovered, so that a claimant with nothing to know is not losing a right they never had a chance to use.

## Orders

(Apply [the discovery rule](piece_the_discovery_rule.md) to latent injury; determine when the claimant knew or ought to have known; leave [the statute of repose](piece_the_statute_of_repose.md) where the legislature put it, since a court cannot reach it.)

## Implementation

([The Preliminary Point](place_the_preliminary_point.md), where the question becomes what somebody ought to have suspected decades ago; and a [records room](place_the_records_room.md) that emptied long before either clock mattered.)

## Targets

- [ ] the claimant with nothing to know is no longer barred before the injury exists, which is the right outcome and is now the law almost everywhere for latent disease
- [ ] the price is accepted: the defendant now faces the forty-year-old allegation with no records, which is exactly what the period existed to prevent
- [ ] the price is accepted: courts must find when a person ought to have known something, a question about a state of knowledge that never existed
- [ ] the price is accepted: the statute of repose runs on underneath, immune by construction, so the correction is incomplete by design rather than by oversight
