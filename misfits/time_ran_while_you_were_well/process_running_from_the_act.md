Toward expiry, on a schedule fixed entirely by the defendant's conduct.

## Lever

A drafting assumption that is right for almost every harm: that the injury and the act arrive together.

## Echo

A period that closes without reference to knowledge, in a system where knowledge is the only thing that makes a claim possible.
