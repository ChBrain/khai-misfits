# Piece: The Discovery Rule

## Taxonomy

[The Preliminary Point](place_the_preliminary_point.md). The correction that works, and hands back the problem it solved.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Argued at [the preliminary point](place_the_preliminary_point.md), where the question is when the claimant knew or ought reasonably to have known.

## Load Bearing

It moves the start of the clock from the act to the discovery, and thereby reimports the stale evidence the period existed to exclude.
Most jurisdictions now apply it to latent disease, which is the right answer: and the defendant then faces exactly the forty-year-old allegation with no records that the bar was designed to prevent, so the correction does not dissolve the conflict, it decides it the other way and hands the cost to the other party.

## Apparent

A humane and obviously correct reform, adopted almost everywhere for latent injury.
The single most effective answer anyone has to this misfit.

## Yearbook

Requires courts to determine when a person ought to have known something, which is a finding about a state of knowledge that never existed and generates its own litigation.
Leaves the claimant who suspected something early worse off than the one who suspected nothing, since the clock starts on reasonable suspicion rather than on proof.
