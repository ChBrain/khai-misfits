# Plot: Forty Years of Nothing

## Taxonomy

[Time Ran While You Were Well](play_time_ran_while_you_were_well.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A fibre inhaled in 1974, in a building nobody thought twice about, by a person who was entirely well the next morning and for four decades afterwards.

## Action

[Growing quietly](process_growing_quietly.md): [The One Who Did Not Know](persona_the_one_who_did_not_know.md) lives an ordinary life while mesothelioma takes the twenty to fifty years it commonly takes, and [the records room](place_the_records_room.md) empties on schedule for unrelated reasons.

## Stage

Two clocks run in parallel with nothing connecting them: one to expiry, one to diagnosis. The first finishes first, almost always.

## Tension

The bar exists to stop claimants sleeping on their rights, and this one held no right to sleep on.
There was nothing to know, nothing to investigate and nothing to bring, so the conduct the rule discourages did not occur here in any degree: and the rule applies at full strength anyway, because it measures elapsed years and not neglect. The filter therefore sorts by how long a harm takes to become knowable, which selects hardest against the injuries that hide best, and those are not a random sample of injuries.
