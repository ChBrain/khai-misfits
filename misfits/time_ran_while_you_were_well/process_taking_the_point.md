# Process: Taking the Point

## Taxonomy

[Time Ran While You Were Well](play_time_ran_while_you_were_well.md). The bar pleaded, and the case ended without its facts.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A claim issued, and a defendant with a complete answer that does not touch what happened.
[The One Who Did It](persona_the_one_who_did_it.md), holding [One Who Did It](position_one_who_did_it.md), asserting a defence the law provided on purpose.

## Direction

Toward [the preliminary point](place_the_preliminary_point.md), listed before any evidence about the facts is heard.
Toward [The One Who Must Say No](persona_the_one_who_must_say_no.md), applying a statute rather than exercising a discretion.

## Lever

An issue capable of disposing of the whole matter, which is properly tried first, since trying it later would waste everybody's money.

## Echo

A correct and final disposal of a dispute whose subject matter nobody in the room ever examines, and no later forum will.
