# Plot: A Reason to Close the Books

## Taxonomy

[Time Ran While You Were Well](play_time_ran_while_you_were_well.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A legal system deciding that claims cannot be brought forever, and setting a period.

## Action

[The Clock That Started Then](persona_the_clock_that_started_then.md) takes [Clock That Started Then](position_clock_that_started_then.md), while [The Evidence That Went](persona_the_evidence_that_went.md) supplies the reason: files shredded on [the retention schedule](piece_the_retention_schedule.md), a site demolished, a supervisor dead.

## Stage

The arguments for the bar are laid out, and they are the strongest arguments anybody makes in this play.

## Tension

The case for the rule is not about protecting anyone who did anything, which is what makes it unanswerable.
An old allegation genuinely cannot be answered: the decay takes the defence as reliably as the claim, so a party that did nothing wrong can no longer show it, having complied with every retention rule it was given. A defendant who remains liable indefinitely can never be told they are finished, and a system in which nothing closes cannot be insured, audited or planned under. So there is no version of this rule that can simply be removed.
