# Pitch: Nothing Was Concealed

## Taxonomy

[Time Ran While You Were Well](play_time_ran_while_you_were_well.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Orderly and just. A limitation period resting on the strongest arguments anyone makes about fairness to defendants, records destroyed on a schedule lawyers approved, a party entitled to peace asserting it, a judge applying a rule nobody thinks is wrong.
Every step correct, and none of it designed to protect anyone who did anything.

## Undertow

Under the order, a clock that started on a day the claimant knew nothing about, running through decades in which there was nothing to know.
And a filter that sorts injuries by how long they take to appear, which is not a property any court would call merit.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a right that expired during the years you were well, without anybody hiding anything from you.

## Echo

Afterward: a fibre from 1974 and a tumour from 2016, a claim dismissed on a preliminary point without the facts being heard, and a statute of repose running quietly on beneath the rule that was supposed to have fixed all this.
