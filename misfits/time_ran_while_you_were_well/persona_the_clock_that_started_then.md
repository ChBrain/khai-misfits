# Persona: The Clock That Started Then

## Taxonomy

[Clock That Started Then](position_clock_that_started_then.md). The limitation period, running from the act.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A fixed number of years, written into statute, beginning on the day the thing was done.
Drafted in an era when the act and the injury were assumed to arrive together, which for most harms they do.

## Action

Runs, from the date of the conduct, without reference to anybody's knowledge.
Expires, and having expired cannot be restarted by anything the claimant later discovers.

## Shadow

Is not a loophole, and this is the difficulty rather than a defence of it.
The arguments for repose are the strongest arguments in the play: an old allegation cannot be answered, an old record cannot be produced, and a defendant who remains liable indefinitely can never be told they are finished. None of that is about shielding wrongdoers, so there is no version of this persona that can be removed without removing something a just system needs.

## Tell

Measures from the defendant's conduct, and is asked to govern the claimant's rights.
