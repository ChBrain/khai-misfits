# Position: Need for an Object

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Has

## Orders

Identify before protecting. Refuse what cannot be identified.

## Loses

Nothing it is free to give up.
The seat cannot waive identification without ceasing to be an instrument of protection, since protection is the ability to say later whether the thing is still there, and that sentence cannot be written about something with no fixed content.

## Drives

Toward making the living thing into a particular thing, as the first act of saving it.
