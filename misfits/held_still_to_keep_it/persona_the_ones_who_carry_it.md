# Persona: The Ones Who Carry It

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Carry It](position_ones_who_carry_it.md). The practitioners, who wanted this and gain from it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The few hundred who know how, ageing, with no successors and no standing.
Asking for the recognition, and right to ask.

## Action

Agree to be recorded. Take the grant. Teach the school.
Go on doing it their own way at home, for a while.

## Shadow

Find their own variation reclassified as error, by an instrument they asked for.
Once there is an authorised version every other way of doing it is measured against it, so a difference that used to be the practice living reads as a mistake being made, and the reclassification is done by nobody: there is no ban, only a standard where there was none.

## Tell

Are the only ones who could have refused, and had the best reasons not to.
