# Persona: The Need for an Object

## Taxonomy

[Need for an Object](position_need_for_an_object.md). The demand that turns care into arrest, and cannot be waived.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The requirement, in any office that protects anything, for a name, a boundary and a record.
A thing that can be entered on a list and pointed at next year.

## Action

Asks what exactly is being safeguarded.
Declines to fund, teach or defend what cannot be identified.

## Shadow

Is not clumsiness and is not bureaucracy for its own sake.
Nothing that safeguards can safeguard a moving target: an instrument must be able to say next year whether the thing it protected is still there, and that sentence cannot be written about something with no fixed content, so identification is not a step before protection but the first half of it.

## Tell

Is the one participant with no discretion at all.
