# Persona: The Audience It Gains

## Taxonomy

[Audience It Gains](position_audience_it_gains.md). The second life, which is real and is not the first.

## Owner

## Projection

People who came to see it, having heard it is important.
A hall, a season, a programme note explaining what they are about to watch.

## Action

Funds, by watching, everything that keeps the practice going.

## Shadow

Changes what the performance is for, without asking anything of anybody.
The practice used to answer an occasion, a harvest, a death, a season, and it now answers an audience that has come to see the practice: so what is performed is the practice being itself, a demonstration of a way of life rather than the living of one, and the substitution is invisible from inside because every note is correct.
