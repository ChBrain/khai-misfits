# Persona: The Version Written Down

## Taxonomy

[Version Written Down](position_version_written_down.md). One form, from many, at one moment, by accident.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The performance that happened to be recorded: by the practitioner who agreed, in the year the funding came, on the day somebody arrived with the equipment.
Accurate, complete, and in no way chosen for its merit.

## Action

Sits in [the entry](piece_the_entry.md) and gets taught.
Becomes the version, because it is the only one anybody can consult.

## Shadow

Acquires an authority nobody granted it.
It was not selected as a canon and nothing in the record claims canonical standing: it becomes one by being the sole survivor in writing, so the authority is manufactured by the absence of rivals rather than by any judgement about which performance was best.

## Tell

Is a true record of an occasion and a false record of a practice.
