## Cue

Somebody arriving with a recorder, in a particular year, to a particular practitioner who said yes.

## Action

[Fixing it](process_fixing_it.md) produces [the entry](piece_the_entry.md), accurate and complete, in [the inventory](place_the_inventory.md).

## Stage

A version is fixed by availability rather than by judgement, and becomes the version because it is the only one anybody can consult.

## Tension

Nobody selected a canon, and a canon is what appears anyway.
[The Version Written Down](persona_the_version_written_down.md) makes no claim to authority and does not need to: the authority accrues from the absence of rivals in writing. Which means the elevation cannot be objected to, since there is no act to object to and no one who performed it, and cannot be undone by adding a second recording, since that makes two versions and not a practice.
