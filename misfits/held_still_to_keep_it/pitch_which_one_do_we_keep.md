# Pitch: Which One Do We Keep

## Taxonomy

[Held Still to Keep It](play_held_still_to_keep_it.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Careful and well meant. A practice worth keeping, people who want it kept, an instrument built to keep it, a form filled in properly and a grant that arrives.
Every step taken by somebody trying to help, and every step the right one.

## Undertow

Under the procedure, a thing that was alive because it was never the same twice, and an office that cannot protect what it cannot name.
So a version is fixed by whatever was to hand, and becomes the version because it is the only one written down.

## Nerve

The vertigo of saving a thing by stopping the one motion that was keeping it.

## Echo

Afterward: a hall, an audience, young people who learned it properly, and a practice performed as itself for people who came to see it, with the occasion it used to answer gone.
