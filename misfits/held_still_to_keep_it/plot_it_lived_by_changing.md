# Plot: It Lived by Changing

## Taxonomy

[Held Still to Keep It](play_held_still_to_keep_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A practice carried in a few hundred people who each do it slightly differently.

## Action

[Making it again](process_making_it_again.md): [the practice](persona_the_practice.md), holding [Practice](position_practice.md), answers whatever occasion has called for it.

## Stage

The differences are the process rather than corruption of it, so there is no true version underneath waiting to be recovered.

## Tension

The health of the thing is exactly the property that makes it unprotectable.
A faithful record of any single performance is a record of one occasion and not of the practice, because what the practice consists in is the differing. So there is nothing to be faithful to that is not a moment, and the absence of a canonical instance is not a gap in the record but the condition of it being alive.
