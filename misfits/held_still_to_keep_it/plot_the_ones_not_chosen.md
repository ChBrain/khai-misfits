# Plot: The Ones Not Chosen

## Taxonomy

[Held Still to Keep It](play_held_still_to_keep_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An authorised version, and everybody else still doing it their own way at home.

## Action

[The Variant](piece_the_variant.md) is measured against [the entry](piece_the_entry.md), by everybody at once and by nobody in particular.

## Stage

A difference that used to be the practice living has become an error being made, without anybody banning anything.

## Tension

The loss removes the faculty rather than a sample of it.
Variation was the mechanism by which the practice answered a new occasion, so what goes is not some of the practice but its capacity to go on being worth doing. And nothing addresses it, because nothing was done: no rule was made, no performance was forbidden, and [the ones who carry it](persona_the_ones_who_carry_it.md) are the ones who asked for the instrument that did it.
