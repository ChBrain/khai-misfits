# Play: Held Still to Keep It

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Held Still to Keep It.
A song that has never been sung the same way twice is going out of the world, because the people who sang it are dying and the ones after them are not learning it. So it is written down, and an inventory entry is made, and a grant follows, and a school is founded to teach it. Everything in that sentence was done by somebody trying to help, and each step was the right step. What is taught in the school is one version: the one that was sung on the day somebody came with a recorder. It is now the version, and the others, which used to be the song, are now mistakes.

## Arc

The bend is this: the thing was alive because it varied, and nothing can be protected until it has been made into something in particular.
It runs on a practice with no canonical form.
[The Practice](persona_the_practice.md) was never one object. It was a way of going on, carried in a few hundred people who each did it slightly differently, and the differences were not corruption of a true version: they were the process, the thing that let it answer a new occasion and go on being worth doing.
[The Need for an Object](persona_the_need_for_an_object.md) is what turns care into harm, and it is not negotiable. Protection is administered: it wants a name, a boundary, a record, a thing that can be entered on a list and pointed at next year. You cannot fund what you cannot identify, cannot teach what has no content, and cannot defend in a ministry what changes while you describe it. The instrument is not clumsy. **Nothing that safeguards can safeguard a moving target.**
So [One Version, at One Moment](plot_one_version_at_one_moment.md) happens, and it is chosen by whatever was available: the practitioner who agreed to be recorded, the year the funding came, the performance that happened to be good. Nobody selects a canon. A canon is what a record becomes once it is the only one.
[The Ones Not Chosen](plot_the_ones_not_chosen.md) follows without anyone deciding it. The moment there is an authorised version, every other way of doing it is measured against it, and a difference that used to be the practice living becomes an error being made. The variation does not go because it is banned. It goes because it now reads as wrong.
And [A Second Life as Itself](plot_a_second_life_as_itself.md) is the strangest turn. The listing works. Money arrives, a hall is built, young people learn it, an audience comes. What they come to see is the practice being the practice, performed as the thing it is now known to be, and the occasion it used to answer is gone, so what is kept is a demonstration of a way of life rather than the way of life.
Then there is the price of refusing, and it is real. Do nothing and it goes in a generation, with no record, no learners and no claim on anybody's attention. There is no arrangement in which the practice is both protected and left alone.

## Stakes

Whether a thing that exists only in being remade can be held without being stopped.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a practice whose variation was its life, an instrument that cannot act until it has an object, a version fixed by whatever was to hand, and a second life in which the practice is performed as itself, and away from any arrangement that protects without first identifying. The production cannot ask a ministry to fund an unnamed thing, and it cannot ask a practitioner to keep going unrecorded while the last carriers die. What is left to fight over is not whether the safeguarding was well meant, it was, and not whether it worked, it did, but who owns the difference between a practice and a record of one.
