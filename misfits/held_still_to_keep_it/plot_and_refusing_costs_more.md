# Plot: And Refusing Costs More

## Taxonomy

[Held Still to Keep It](play_held_still_to_keep_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The option of leaving it alone, considered honestly rather than raised to be dismissed.

## Action

[The Ones Who Carry It](persona_the_ones_who_carry_it.md) weigh [the recognition](piece_the_recognition.md) against an average age past sixty and no successors.

## Stage

Doing nothing ends it inside a generation, with no record, no learners and no claim on anybody's attention.

## Tension

The arrest is a price and not a mistake, which is what closes every exit.
There is no arrangement in which the practice is both protected and left alone, because protection begins with identification and identification is what stops it. Documenting without listing still selects a version. Listing without teaching leaves nobody to do it. So the choice is between a practice that ends and a record that survives, and the participants who understand this best are the ones who chose the record.
