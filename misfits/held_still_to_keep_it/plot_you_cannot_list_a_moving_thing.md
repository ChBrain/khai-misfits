# Plot: You Cannot List a Moving Thing

## Taxonomy

[Held Still to Keep It](play_held_still_to_keep_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A ministry, a form, and a practice that is going out of the world inside a generation.

## Action

[Naming it](process_naming_it.md): [the need for an object](persona_the_need_for_an_object.md) asks what exactly is being safeguarded, in [the inventory](place_the_inventory.md).

## Stage

Protection is shown to require a name, a boundary and a record, and no instrument is found that safeguards without first identifying.

## Tension

The demand is not bureaucratic clumsiness and cannot be waived.
To protect a thing is to be able to say next year whether it is still there, and that sentence cannot be written about something with no fixed content: so identification is not a step before protection, it is the first half of it. [The Inventory](place_the_inventory.md) has no field for the variation, and giving it one would produce a description that cannot be checked and therefore protects nothing.
