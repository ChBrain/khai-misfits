# Held Still to Keep It: Reference

The research warrant for Held Still to Keep It.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the safeguarding of intangible cultural heritage and on the
sociolinguistics of standardisation, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging safeguarding as **an identification that is also an arrest**: a practice that exists only in being
remade is going out of the world, and the only instruments that can help are ones that must first say what
exactly is being helped. The play models the documented mechanics, the practice with no authoritative
instance, the office that cannot fund or defend what it cannot name, the version fixed by whichever
performance happened to be recorded, the variant reclassified as error by the mere existence of a standard,
the recognition that is real and is the reason anybody consents, and the second life in which the practice is
performed as itself, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of officials spoiling
something.

The governing law is that protection is the ability to say, next year, whether the thing is still there, and
that sentence cannot be written about something with no fixed content. So identification is not a step taken
before protection begins: it is the first half of protection. An inventory can carry what was done and cannot
carry that it is done differently every time, since a description saying so cannot be checked against
anything and therefore protects nothing.

What removes any possibility of blame from the office is that no gentler instrument exists. A budget cannot
be spent on an unnamed thing, a school cannot teach a syllabus with no content, and a ministry cannot defend
in public an element it declines to describe. Safeguarding a moving target is not a harder version of the
task; it is a different one, and nothing performs it.

What removes it from the practitioners is that they asked, and were right to. The carriers are ageing, the
successors do not exist, and the recognition brings learners, money and standing that no other route
supplies. Their own way of doing it is then reclassified as a departure from a version they helped create,
by nobody, through the arrival of a standard where there had been none, and the reclassification cannot be
withdrawn without giving back the only thing keeping the practice in the world.

And the version is not a judgement anybody made. It is whichever performance was captured, by the
practitioner who agreed, in the year the money came. It claims no canonical standing on its face and acquires
it from the absence of rivals in writing, which is why there is no act to object to and no one who performed
it. Adding a second recording produces two versions rather than a practice.

It is distinct from the house's **Bred for the Ark**, where a species kept safe from its world is selected by
the place that keeps it, so the ark breeds animals for the ark: there the thing goes on changing and the harm
is that it changes toward the wrong world, so the conserved population is adapting when it should not be.
Here the harm runs the opposite way. The thing stops changing, and the stopping is the injury, because the
changing was the whole of how it stayed worth doing.

It is distinct from the house's **The Hollow Word**, where a word keeps circulating after the practice it
named has gone, so the field speaks the old language and does the new: there the name outlives the thing and
the loss is hidden by continuity of vocabulary. Here the thing outlives its own making, in a form that is
accurate and taught and performed correctly, so nothing is hidden at all and every particular is right.

It is distinct from the house's **Longer Than Its Meaning**, where a warning cut to outlast its maker
survives intact and reaches no one who can read it right: there the artefact endures and the capacity to
interpret it decays on the far side. Here the capacity is deliberately maintained, learners are taught and an
audience is assembled, and what is lost is upstream of interpretation, in the occasion the practice used to
answer.

It is distinct from the house's **The Cargo Cult**, where a team copies the exact form of a proven success
and no cargo lands, because the form was never what made it work: there the copyists mistake the form for the
cause and the copy fails on its own terms. Here nobody is mistaken about causation and the copy succeeds:
learners really can do it, the practice really does survive, and the misfit is what the surviving thing is
for rather than whether it works.

It is distinct from the house's **Metric Fixation**, where a number chosen to stand for a goal is hit exactly
and truthfully and the goal it stood for quietly dies: there a proxy displaces an end and effort bends toward
the number. Here no measurement is involved and nothing is being optimised. What displaces the practice is a
description of it, entered honestly, by people with no target to hit.

It runs directly against the house's **No One in Charge**, on the same axis and with the opposite sign,
and the two are worth reading together. Both turn on one quantity, **how much of an arrangement is made
explicit and fixed** so that somebody can check it next year. Here that is the harm: a practice whose worth
is that it is done differently every time cannot be written into a description it must then match, so the
inventory fixes content the practice never had. There it is the remedy: a group that refuses formal
structure does not abolish power but hides it, and power that is named can be argued with where power that
is denied cannot be reached.

**What sets the sign is what is being made explicit.** Where the thing is a living practice, writing it
down is a claim about its content, and the claim becomes the standard the practice is held to. Where the
thing is a relation of power, writing it down subtracts nothing from it and adds a handle. So the same
move, recommended by both literatures in good faith, is the loss in one and the correction in the other,
and neither play is wrong about its own case.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the office as
  meddling or the practitioners as naive, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: an
  instrument of protection must be able to say next year whether the thing is still there, and that requires
  identification, so no gentler instrument is being declined; the carriers asked for the recognition and were
  right to, since the alternative was ending inside a generation; the recorded version claims no authority
  and acquires it from having no rival in writing; and the reclassification of every other way of doing it is
  performed by nobody, through the arrival of a standard where there had been none. The loss is authored by
  no one and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a thing which exists only in being remade
  cannot be held without being stopped.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Recording several versions
  produces several records and not a practice, since what is lost is the making rather than any particular
  outcome of it. Documenting without listing still selects whichever performance was captured. Listing
  without teaching leaves nobody able to do it. Leaving it alone works perfectly and ends it inside a
  generation with no record, no learners and no claim on anybody, which is why the participants who
  understand the trade best are the ones who chose the record.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the convention, the critique and the sociolinguistics
  are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: whether inscription actually freezes what it lists is
  contested and argued case by case, the Convention's own text warns against fixing living heritage, and
  outcomes differ widely between elements and states, so the play stages a mechanism rather than a verdict on
  any listing; standardisation in language is a centuries-long process with many drivers and is not caused by
  heritage instruments, and is cited as a parallel rather than an instance; the recognition is a real benefit
  and the play does not treat it as a disguise; and many safeguarding programmes are designed by and run by
  the bearer communities themselves, which the staging does not deny. **The play makes no claim that any
  particular element has been damaged by being listed**, only that a description that can be checked next
  year cannot carry the one property that made the practice alive.
