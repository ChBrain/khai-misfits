# Plot: A Second Life as Itself

## Taxonomy

[Held Still to Keep It](play_held_still_to_keep_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The grant, the hall, the young learners, and an audience that has come to see it.

## Action

[Showing it](process_showing_it.md): the practice is performed in [the hall after](place_the_hall_after.md), correctly, note for note.

## Stage

What is performed is the practice being the practice, and the occasion it used to answer has gone.

## Tension

The substitution is invisible from inside, because every note is right.
The practice used to answer something outside itself that called for it, and the thing it now answers is a room of people who have come to watch it: so it survives as a demonstration of a way of life rather than the living of one. No performer could detect the change by examining the performance, and no audit could find a fault, since nothing in the execution is wrong.
