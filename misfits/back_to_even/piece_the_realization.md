# Piece: The Realization

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). The act of closing a position and booking its result: realising a gain banks it, realising a loss admits it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Book](place_the_book.md), where a sale is written down as a gain or a loss, the ledger [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) reaches readily for a winner and avoids for a loser.

## Load Bearing

A paper loss costs nothing to the ego until it is realised, so closing a loser turns a private hope into an admitted mistake, and admitting a loss means closing the account at a loss, which loss aversion and regret make painful (Shefrin and Statman). Guard against reading the reluctance as laziness: it is the sting of admitting a loss, a real cost the mind pays to close the account, not mere inertia.

## Apparent

Just the moment a trade is settled, a neutral piece of bookkeeping. It looks like paperwork, not a decision the mind resists.
