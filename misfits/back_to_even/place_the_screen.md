# Place: The Screen

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). Where the investor watches each holding against the price paid, green above the line and red below, and decides what to sell. Hosts [What You Paid](plot_what_you_paid.md), [Wait for It to Come Back](plot_wait_for_it_to_come_back.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A portfolio of positions, each marked as a winner or a loser by reference to what was paid for it, not by what it might do next.

## Holds

[The Purchase Price](piece_the_purchase_price.md), the line the screen draws between green and red, and [The Break-Even](piece_the_break_even.md), the level the loser must regain before the investor will let it go.

## Offers

A clear view of gain and loss measured against the entry price, position by position.

## Withheld

Any reason the entry price should bear on the decision. It is sunk, and the future the holding is about to have does not know what was once paid for it.
