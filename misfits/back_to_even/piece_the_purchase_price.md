# Piece: The Purchase Price

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). The reference point: the price paid for a holding, kept as the baseline that divides it into a winner above or a loser below.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Screen](place_the_screen.md), the line every position is marked against, green above it and red below. [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) reads it there before any sale, and it is the level [The Break-Even](piece_the_break_even.md) waits to regain.

## Load Bearing

Mental accounting opens an account at the purchase price, so a position is a gain or a loss only relative to it, and that framing, not the holding's own future, drives the sale (Shefrin and Statman). Guard against reading the purchase price as relevant to the decision: it is sunk, and the market's future does not know or care what anyone paid for a position.

## Apparent

Just the cost basis, the natural figure to compare a price against. It looks like the plainest fact on the screen, not a lever on the decision.

## Yearbook

Kept as the reference point behind [The Turn](piece_the_turn.md), the line [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) watches on [The Screen](place_the_screen.md), the level [The Break-Even](piece_the_break_even.md) waits to regain, in [What You Paid](plot_what_you_paid.md).
