# Piece: The Bound

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). The boundary and the hedge: the tax-loss selling that reverses the effect at year-end, the sophistication that lessens it, and the rule that overrides it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Long Run](place_the_long_run.md) and [The Book](place_the_book.md), where a rule would have governed the sale and the year-end reversal shows against the pattern it bounds.

## Load Bearing

At year-end tax-loss selling reverses the effect, since losers are then sold for the deduction, professionals show it less, and a rule that closes positions on their prospects overrides it (Odean; Shefrin and Statman). Holding a fallen asset is not always wrong, since a price sometimes truly recovers, so only the systematic asymmetry is the error. Guard against reading the misfit as a claim that every held loser is a mistake or that owners can never rightly hold a fallen asset.

## Apparent

A mere caveat, easy to drop for a simpler rule. It looks like a footnote, not the line that keeps the finding honest.
