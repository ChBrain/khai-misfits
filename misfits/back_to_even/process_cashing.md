# Process: Cashing

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). Borne at [The Book](place_the_book.md), precipitated by [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) selling [The Winner](persona_the_winner.md), in [Take the Gain](plot_take_the_gain.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A holding risen above the price paid: a gain that is only a number until it is taken, and now sure if taken.

## Direction

From a rising position held toward one sold, the gain locked in and the further climb foregone.

## Lever

Risk aversion over gains: the sure gain is preferred to the gamble of holding on, so the winner is cashed while it is still rising (Tversky and Kahneman; Shefrin and Statman).
