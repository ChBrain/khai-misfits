# Position: Investor

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). The seat of the one who holds the portfolio and, taking the price paid as the line, sells the winners and holds the losers, so gains are cut short and losses ridden in hope of getting back to even.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A portfolio and a reference point it did not choose to use: the price paid for each holding, which the mind keeps as the line dividing a gain to secure from a loss to avoid realising.
A sensible reason for each move: take a sure gain while it is there, and do not sell a holding you still believe in just because it is down.
No way to judge a holding free of that price, because it is the reference point that makes one position a winner to cash and another a loser to ride, so the sale turns on it.

## Orders

Sell when you're up.
Take the gain on a holding that has risen, locking the sure thing, and keep a holding that has fallen, waiting for it to come back to what you paid rather than realising the loss.
Give each move its reasonable name, taking profit and staying patient, and do not look for the pattern, since no single trade shows it.

## Loses

The return and the tax the pattern costs, worn as the market's doing.
Because the same reference point cashes the winner and rides the loser, the seat cannot correct one half without the other, so it cuts its gains short and lets its losses run, and the sold winners climb on while the held losers sink.
It keeps its sense of discipline and loses the account's ground to the market, the cost a pattern no one trade reveals.

## Drives

Toward the one who does what feels most disciplined and whose account quietly bleeds,
taking sure gains and nursing losses by the shape of value and the fear of a realised loss,
so the seat that means to invest well is the seat the price it paid keeps from letting winners run and losers go, the loss authored by no one and blamed on the market.
