# Position: Adviser

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). The seat that judges each holding by its prospects and not by the price paid, letting winners run and cutting losers on their merits, the remedy that bounds the trap at the cost of the discipline it asks.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A rule that drops the price paid: the insistence that the entry price is sunk and irrelevant, and that a holding be judged only on what it is worth keeping now.
A set of levers that bound the effect: a rule that closes positions on their outlook, the discipline to realise a loss, and the year-end tax-loss selling that turns the effect around (Odean; Shefrin and Statman).
No gift for prophecy and no painless cure, since it cannot know which loser recovers, and judging by prospects still means admitting the losses the purchase price was hiding.

## Orders

Judge by prospects.
Weigh each holding on its outlook, not on what was paid: let the winner run while its prospects are good, cut the loser whose prospects are poor, and take the loss where it should be taken.
Set the rule and keep it against the pull of the reference point, lean on the tax calendar, and know that holding a fallen holding is not always wrong.

## Loses

The comfort the disposition bought, spent on the sting of the realised loss.
Because dropping the purchase price means booking the losses it hid, the seat pays in admitted mistakes for the return it saves, and even then may misjudge a holding's prospects.
It keeps the account's ground and loses the ease of never realising a loss, its remedy a discipline held against a pull that returns whenever the guard is down.

## Drives

Toward the seat that could keep the winner running and the loser cut, and whose fix asks the hardest thing,
judging on prospects and not on entry price, taking the losses the reference point was nursing,
so the seat that best sees the trap is the seat whose remedy is discipline spent against a pull it is not itself free of, the bound real but never a cure applied once.
