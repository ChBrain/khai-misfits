# Plot: The Account Bleeds

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Long Run](place_the_long_run.md), time passing over the sold winners and the held losers.

## Action

Through [Bleeding](process_bleeding.md), [The Cost](piece_the_cost.md) accrues: the winners [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) sold climb on out of reach, the losers it kept sink further, and realising gains rather than losses worsens the tax, so the account falls behind the market. The scene closes with the disposition's price paid in return and in tax, and easily blamed on the market rather than the pattern.

## Stage

In Odean's accounts the winners sold went on to outperform the losers kept, so selling winners and holding losers lowered returns, and realising gains not losses raised taxes (Odean). This is not Loath to Part: no ownership inflates a price, the holding is readily sold when it is a winner.

## Tension

A real and systematic cost, worn as bad luck because each trade alone looked reasonable.
