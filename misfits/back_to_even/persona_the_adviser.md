# Persona: The Adviser

## Taxonomy

[Adviser](position_adviser.md). The one who judges each holding by its prospects and not by the price paid, letting winners run and cutting losers on their merits, the remedy that bounds the trap at the cost of the discipline it asks.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Detached, disciplined, exacting.
Treats the price paid as sunk and irrelevant, and asks of each holding only what it is worth keeping now, letting a winner run while its outlook is good and cutting a loser whose outlook is poor.
No coldness in it and no gift for prophecy: it cannot know which holding will recover, only that the entry price is the wrong thing to decide by, so it judges on prospects and accepts being sometimes wrong.

## Action

Judges by prospects on [Judge by Prospects](plan_judge_by_prospects.md), through [Ruling](process_ruling.md) at [the Book](place_the_book.md), holding [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md): closing each position on its outlook, taking the loss where the outlook is poor, and leaning on the year-end tax-loss selling that turns the effect around.
Cannot spare the investor the sting, because judging by prospects means realising the losses the purchase price was hiding, one painful admission at a time, so the remedy costs the very thing the disposition avoids.
Does what bounds the trap: it sets a rule that drops the price paid from the sale, knowing the rule must be kept against the pull of the reference point and that holding a fallen holding is not always wrong.

## Shadow

Not a scold who says always cut and not a seer who knows which loser recovers: the Adviser only insists the sale be judged on prospects, not on entry price, and even then it may misjudge, since a fallen holding sometimes truly comes back.
Because the bias is the pull of a reference point the mind will not easily drop, the one who best sees the trap is still not free of it, its remedy a discipline spent against a pull that returns whenever the guard is down.
It is the one who could keep the winner running and the loser cut, and the one whose fix asks the investor to admit, at last, the losses it was nursing.

## Tell

Says the price you paid is gone, judge the holding on where it is headed, and is stating the one thing the reference point resists.
Cannot make the discipline painless, because dropping the purchase price means realising the losses it hid, so it prescribes a rule and the tax calendar and carries the risk that the judgement of prospects was wrong.
