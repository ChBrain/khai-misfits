# Process: Ruling

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). Borne at [The Book](place_the_book.md), precipitated by [The Adviser](persona_the_adviser.md) weighing [The Bound](piece_the_bound.md), in [Judge by Prospects](plot_judge_by_prospects.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The recognition that the purchase price is sunk and should not rule the sale.

## Direction

From decisions ruled by the entry price toward decisions ruled by each holding's own outlook, gains let run and losers cut on their merits.

## Lever

A rule that closes positions on their prospects rather than their price paid overrides the effect, tax-loss selling reverses it at year-end, and sophistication lessens it in professionals: the bound that keeps the disposition from being a law (Odean; Shefrin and Statman). Holding a fallen asset is not always wrong, since a price sometimes truly recovers; only the systematic asymmetry is the trap.
