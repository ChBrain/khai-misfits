# Piece: The Break-Even

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). The purchase-price level the held loser must regain before it will be sold: the fixation on getting back to even.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Screen](place_the_screen.md), the line the loser is watched to reclaim, held there for [The Loser](persona_the_loser.md) by [Riding](process_riding.md) as long as it stays below.

## Load Bearing

The investor resolves to sell the loser once it returns to the purchase price, so the sale waits on a level the market has no reason to honour, and the loser is ridden while it may fall further (Shefrin and Statman). Guard against reading "back to even" as a sound target: the purchase price is sunk and irrelevant to whether the holding is worth keeping now.

## Apparent

A patient plan to sell without a loss, which sounds disciplined. It reads as a rule, not as the refusal to realise a loss that it actually is.

## Yearbook

Set by [The Purchase Price](piece_the_purchase_price.md), the goal of [The Loser](persona_the_loser.md) on [Wait for the Rebound](plan_wait_for_the_rebound.md) through [Riding](process_riding.md), keeping [The Realization](piece_the_realization.md) deferred, in [Wait for It to Come Back](plot_wait_for_it_to_come_back.md).
