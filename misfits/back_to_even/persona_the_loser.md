# Persona: The Loser

## Taxonomy

[Loser](position_loser.md). The holding fallen below the price paid, the loss sure only if realised, embodying the risk-seeking pull over losses to gamble on a recovery, so it is held too long and sinks on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Fallen, hopeful, kept.
Stands below the line the price paid drew, a loss that is only on paper while it is held, and it offers the investor the hope of a return to what was paid.
No villainy in it: it may be a sound holding in a dip, and holding it can be right, only that it is kept less for its outlook than for the pain of closing it at a loss.

## Action

Wait for the rebound on [Wait for the Rebound](plan_wait_for_the_rebound.md), through [Riding](process_riding.md) at [the Screen](place_the_screen.md), fixing on [the Break-Even](piece_the_break_even.md), the price paid it is resolved to be sold at and no lower, so it is watched to reclaim a level the market need not return to.
Cannot be closed as easily as the winner is cashed, because over a loss the investor is risk-seeking and prefers the gamble of holding to the sure loss, and realising the loss would mean admitting it.
Does what a paper loss does to a mind that cannot bear to make it real: it is ridden down, kept for a rebound to even, and in the long run it sinks further, the loss nursed and grown.

## Shadow

Not the wrong holding to have and not proof that cutting losers is always right: the Loser may recover, and holding it is sometimes sound, but it is kept not on its outlook but because selling it would realise the loss, the gamble preferred to the sure admission.
Because the risk seeking over losses that rides it is the other half of the reflected value function, the Loser is held for the same reason the winner is sold, so the holding with the worst recent run is the one the investor is slowest to release.
It is the position kept for a return to even, and the one the fear of a realised loss rides all the way down.

## Tell

Shows red on the screen and is kept until it is back to even, and the keeping is called patience, which sounds like conviction.
Cannot be judged on its prospects while the price paid is the target, because it is held for a level the market has no reason to honour, so it is ridden down, the loss deferred and deepened.
