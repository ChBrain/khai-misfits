# Plot: What You Paid

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Screen](place_the_screen.md), a portfolio of holdings, each marked green or red against the price paid for it: a line drawn under every position, waiting to be read.

## Action

[The Investor](persona_the_investor.md), from [the investor's seat](position_investor.md), takes [The Purchase Price](piece_the_purchase_price.md) as the line: each holding above it a winner, each below it a loser, and the sell decision comes to rest on that line rather than on where the holding is headed. The screen is read position by position, not by what lies ahead for any of them, but by what was once paid for each. The scene closes with the portfolio split into winners and losers by what was paid, not by what lies ahead.

## Stage

Mental accounting opens an account at the purchase price, so a holding becomes a gain or a loss only relative to it, though the price paid is sunk and the market's future does not know it (Shefrin and Statman; Tversky and Kahneman). No one has sold or held anything wrongly yet: the line is only drawn here, the misfit is that it will be read as though it mattered to what comes next.

## Tension

The figure that should not matter, what was paid, is the one the decision will turn on.
