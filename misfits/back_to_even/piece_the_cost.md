# Piece: The Cost

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). The reckoning: the return given up when winners are cut short and losers ridden, and the tax worsened by realising gains and not losses.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Long Run](place_the_long_run.md), where the sold winners keep rising and the held losers keep falling, the stretch of time over which the reckoning shows.

## Load Bearing

In Odean's accounts the winners sold went on to outperform the losers kept, so the disposition lowered returns, and realising gains rather than losses raised the tax bill besides (Odean). Guard against reading the cost as bad luck on particular trades: it is the systematic asymmetry, cutting gains and riding losses, that bleeds the account on average, not a run of unlucky picks.

## Apparent

An ordinary run of results, easily blamed on the market rather than the pattern. It looks like the account simply had a mediocre stretch.
