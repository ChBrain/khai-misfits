# Persona: The Winner

## Taxonomy

[Winner](position_winner.md). The holding risen above the price paid, the sure gain in hand, embodying the risk-averse pull over gains to lock it in, so it is cashed too early and climbs on out of reach.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Risen, tempting, soon gone.
Stands above the line the price paid drew, a gain that is real and would be sure if taken, and it offers the investor the comfort of a win booked.
No trick in it: the gain is genuine and taking it is not foolish, only that the taking is prompted by the fear of giving a sure thing back, not by any judgement that its climb is done.

## Action

Take the sure thing on [Take the Sure Thing](plan_take_the_sure_thing.md), through [Cashing](process_cashing.md) at [the Book](place_the_book.md), where the investor sells to lock the gain and the position is closed, the win realised.
Cannot be held as easily as it is cashed, because over a gain the investor is risk-averse and prefers the sure thing to the gamble of holding, so the winner is the holding most readily sold, whatever its prospects.
Does what a sure gain does to a mind that fears losing it: it is taken early, and then, in the long run, it climbs on past the price it was sold at, the gain cut short.

## Shadow

Not the wrong holding to own and not proof that taking gains is folly: the Winner is a good position, and cashing it is sometimes right, but it is sold not on its outlook but because it is up, the sure gain preferred to its uncertain further climb.
Because the risk aversion over gains that cashes it is one half of the reflected value function, the Winner is sold for the same reason the loser is held, so the holding with the best recent run is the one the investor is quickest to let go.
It is the position that had more to give, and the one the fear of a surrendered gain takes off the board too soon.

## Tell

Shows green on the screen and is sold to bank the win, and the selling is called taking profit, which sounds like sense.
Cannot keep the investor in for its prospects, because it is judged by its gain and not its future, so it is cashed while it is up and its later climb is watched from outside, the gain foregone.
