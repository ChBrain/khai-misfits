# Persona: The Investor

## Taxonomy

[Investor](position_investor.md). The one who holds the portfolio and, taking the price paid as the line, sells the winners and holds the losers, cutting gains short and riding losses in hope of getting back to even.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Prudent, disciplined, honest.
Takes a gain when it is there and will not sell a holding it still believes in at a loss, and can give a sensible reason for each move.
No recklessness in it and no greed: it means to invest well, and the pull it follows, take the sure gain and hold through a dip, feels like discipline, not a bias.

## Action

Sells when it's up on [Sell When You're Up](plan_sell_when_youre_up.md), at [the Screen](place_the_screen.md), cashing the winners and keeping the losers, and through [Bleeding](process_bleeding.md) its account, over the long run, falls behind the market as the sold winners climb and the held losers sink.
Cannot judge a holding free of the price paid, because that price is the reference point that makes one holding a gain to secure and another a loss to avoid realising, so the sale turns on where each stands against what was paid rather than on where it is headed.
Does what the shape of value and the fear of a realised loss counsel: it locks the sure gain and rides the loss for a rebound, and the pattern, not any one trade, is what costs it.

## Shadow

Not careless for taking gains and not stubborn for holding losers: each move follows the value function, risk-averse over a gain and risk-seeking over a loss, and the reluctance to sell a loser is the real sting of admitting the loss, so the behaviour is a systematic bias and not a lapse.
Because the same reference point cashes the winner and rides the loser, the Investor cannot correct one half without the other, so the one doing what feels most disciplined is the one whose account quietly bleeds.
It is the one who takes its gains and nurses its losses soundly, and the one the purchase price keeps from letting winners run and losers go.

## Tell

Says it will sell the loser once it is back to even, and takes the gain now while it is sure, and hears in both only good sense.
Cannot see the pattern from inside any one trade, because each looked reasonable alone, so it reads the account's shortfall as the market's doing and not the disposition's, the cost worn as luck.
