# Plot: No Law of the Ledger

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Long Run](place_the_long_run.md), the disciplined account beside the disposition-ridden one.

## Action

The bound is drawn: [The Bound](piece_the_bound.md) holds where tax-loss selling, sophistication, and a rule govern the sale, so the effect is robust for the ordinary investor left to the pull of the purchase price and fades where discipline or the tax calendar cuts across it. The scene closes with the effect bound but not banished, real for the untutored account and gone for the ruled one.

## Stage

The disposition effect is robust but bounded, reversed at year-end by tax-loss selling and lessened by sophistication and rules (Odean; Shefrin and Statman). Holding a fallen asset is not always the error, only the systematic asymmetry is; no claim that every held loser is a mistake.

## Tension

The trap is not a law of the ledger but a pull on whoever lets the purchase price rule the sale.
