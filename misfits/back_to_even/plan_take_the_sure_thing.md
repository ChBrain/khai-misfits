# Plan: Take the Sure Thing

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the winner's seat](position_winner.md), inside [Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Winner](position_winner.md)

## Direction

Offer the investor a gain it can lock now, and be cashed for it. Standing above the line the price paid drew, in the domain of gains where the investor is risk-averse, present the certain win as preferable to the gamble of holding for more, so the position is closed while it is up. Be sold on the fact of the gain and not on the judgement of prospects, the win booked and called taking profit, whatever the climb still left in you.

## Orders

([The Winner](persona_the_winner.md) is cashed through [Cashing](process_cashing.md) at [the Book](place_the_book.md), the sure gain locked in as the investor, risk-averse over gains through [the Turn](piece_the_turn.md), prefers the certain to the gamble.)

## Implementation

([The Turn](piece_the_turn.md), risk-averse above the reference point, makes the sure gain preferable to holding, so [Cashing](process_cashing.md) at [the Book](place_the_book.md) closes the winner early, its further climb foregone and, over the long run, watched from outside as part of [the Cost](piece_the_cost.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] a real, sure gain is presented above the line the price paid drew
- [ ] the position is cashed for being up, not judged on its prospects
- [ ] the sale is called taking profit and feels like sense
- [ ] the further climb is foregone, the winner's later rise the account's lost gain
