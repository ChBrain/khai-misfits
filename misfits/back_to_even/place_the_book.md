# Place: The Book

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). Where a sale is booked and the gain or loss becomes real: the ledger on which a position is closed and its result written down. Hosts [Take the Gain](plot_take_the_gain.md), [Weighed on the Outlook](plot_weighed_on_the_outlook.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A winner sold and its gain entered as realised, beside a losing position that could be closed the same way and is not.

## Holds

[The Realization](piece_the_realization.md), the act of closing a position and admitting, on the ledger, what it actually returned.

## Offers

To close any position on its merits and book the outcome, gain or loss alike, the same ledger for both.

## Withheld

Comfort in booking a loss. Realising it means closing the account and admitting the result, and loss aversion and regret make that admission painful (Shefrin and Statman).
