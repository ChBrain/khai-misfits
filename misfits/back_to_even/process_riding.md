# Process: Riding

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). Borne at [The Screen](place_the_screen.md), precipitated by [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) holding [The Loser](persona_the_loser.md) toward [The Break-Even](piece_the_break_even.md), in [Wait for It to Come Back](plot_wait_for_it_to_come_back.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A holding fallen below the price paid: a loss that is only a number until it is taken, and now sure if taken.

## Direction

From a position that should be judged on its own prospects toward one kept instead for the hope of getting back to even.

## Lever

Risk seeking over losses, and the pain of realising one: the loser is ridden rather than closed, the gamble on recovery preferred to the sure loss (Shefrin and Statman; Tversky and Kahneman).
