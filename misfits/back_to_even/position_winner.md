# Position: Winner

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). The seat of the holding risen above the price paid, the sure gain in hand, embodying the risk-averse pull over gains to lock it in, so it is cashed too early and climbs on out of reach.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real gain above the line the price paid drew: a win that would be sure if taken, and the comfort it offers a holder who fears giving a sure thing back.
A place in the domain of gains, where the investor is risk-averse and prefers the certain to the gamble, so of all the holdings it is the readiest to be sold.
No claim on being held for its prospects, because it is judged by its gain and not its future, so its outlook does not keep it on the board.

## Orders

Take the sure thing.
Offer the investor a gain it can lock now, and be cashed for it, closing the position while it is up rather than holding it for an uncertain further climb.
Be sold on the fact of the gain and not on the judgement of prospects, the win booked and called taking profit.

## Loses

The climb it still had in it, cut off at the sale.
Because it is cashed for being up and not for having run its course, the seat gives up the gain it would have made past the price it was sold at, and that later climb is watched from outside.
It keeps the sure gain and loses the greater one, the holding with the best recent run let go soonest.

## Drives

Toward the seat sold not on its outlook but because it is up,
a real gain taken early by a holder who fears surrendering it, the sure thing preferred to the further climb,
so the position that had more to give is the seat the risk aversion over gains takes off the board too soon, its later rise the account's foregone gain.
