# Plot: Weighed on the Outlook

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Book](place_the_book.md), the same holdings, weighed now on their outlook rather than their entry price.

## Action

[The Adviser](persona_the_adviser.md), from [the adviser's seat](position_adviser.md), runs [Judge by Prospects](plan_judge_by_prospects.md) through [Ruling](process_ruling.md), holding [The Bound](piece_the_bound.md): close each position on what it is worth keeping now, let winners run and cut losers on their merits, take the loss where the outlook is poor, and let a rule and year-end tax-loss selling override the pull of the purchase price. The scene closes with the sale governed by prospects, not by what was paid, at the cost of the discipline it takes to admit a loss.

## Stage

A rule that closes on prospects, tax-loss selling that reverses the effect at year-end, and the sophistication that lessens it all bound the trap (Odean; Shefrin and Statman). Holding a fallen asset is not always wrong, since a price sometimes truly recovers.

## Tension

The fix is to drop the purchase price from the decision, and dropping it means admitting the losses it was hiding.
