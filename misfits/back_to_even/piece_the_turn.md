# Piece: The Turn

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). The reflection in the value function: the flip from risk-averse over gains to risk-seeking over losses across the reference point.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Screen](place_the_screen.md), acting above and below the line drawn by [The Purchase Price](piece_the_purchase_price.md): the same screen, the two opposite pulls it produces on either side of that line.

## Load Bearing

The value function is concave in gains and convex in losses, so over a gain the sure thing is preferred and the winner is sold, and over a loss the gamble is preferred and the loser is held, one asymmetry producing both halves of the disposition (Tversky and Kahneman). Guard against reading the two behaviours as separate quirks: selling winners and holding losers are the single reflected value function seen on its two sides, not two unrelated habits.

## Apparent

Ordinary prudence over gains and ordinary hope over losses. Locking in a sure thing looks like sense, and waiting out a dip looks like patience.
