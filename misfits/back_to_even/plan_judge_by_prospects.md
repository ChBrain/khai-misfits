# Plan: Judge by Prospects

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the adviser's seat](position_adviser.md), inside [Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Adviser](position_adviser.md)

## Direction

Weigh each holding on its outlook, not on what was paid. Treat the entry price as sunk and irrelevant: let a winner run while its prospects are good, cut a loser whose prospects are poor, and take the loss where it should be taken. Set a rule that closes positions on their merits and keep it against the pull of the reference point, lean on the year-end tax-loss selling that turns the effect around, and know that judging by prospects still means admitting the losses the purchase price was hiding, and that holding a fallen holding is not always wrong.

## Orders

([The Adviser](persona_the_adviser.md) rules through [Ruling](process_ruling.md) at [the Book](place_the_book.md), holding [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md): closing each position on its outlook, taking the loss where it is warranted, and dropping the price paid from the sale.)

## Implementation

([Ruling](process_ruling.md) at [the Book](place_the_book.md) governs the sale by prospects and not by [the Purchase Price](piece_the_purchase_price.md), so [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md) holds where a rule, sophistication, or the tax calendar overrides the pull, keeping [the Cost](piece_the_cost.md) from being universal, the remedy real but paid in the sting of each realised loss.)

## Targets

- [ ] each holding is judged on its outlook, the entry price dropped as sunk
- [ ] winners are let run while their prospects are good and losers cut when theirs are poor
- [ ] a rule and year-end tax-loss selling are leaned on to override the pull of the reference point
- [ ] the sting of realising a loss is accepted, and holding a fallen holding is not treated as always wrong
