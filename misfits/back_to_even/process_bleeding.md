# Process: Bleeding

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). Borne at [The Long Run](place_the_long_run.md), precipitated by [The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) cutting gains and riding losses, [The Cost](piece_the_cost.md), in [The Account Bleeds](plot_the_account_bleeds.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Winners sold and losers held, position after position, as the market carries on regardless.

## Direction

From a portfolio that could have compounded toward one that underperforms it, the sold winners rising away and the held losers sinking further.

## Lever

The winners sold go on to outperform the losers kept, and selling winners rather than losers raises the tax bill besides, so the account's returns fall behind (Odean).
