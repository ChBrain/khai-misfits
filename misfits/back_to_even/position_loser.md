# Position: Loser

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md). The seat of the holding fallen below the price paid, the loss sure only if realised, embodying the risk-seeking pull over losses to gamble on a recovery, so it is held too long and sinks on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A paper loss below the line the price paid drew: a loss that stays unreal while the holding is kept, and the hope it offers of a return to what was paid.
A place in the domain of losses, where the investor is risk-seeking and prefers the gamble of holding to the sure loss, so of all the holdings it is the hardest to release.
No sale while the target is the price paid, because it is watched to reclaim [the Break-Even](piece_the_break_even.md), a level the market has no reason to honour.

## Orders

Wait for the rebound.
Offer the investor the hope of getting back to even, and be kept for it, held rather than closed so the loss is not realised and admitted.
Be judged by the price paid and not by prospects, fixed on a return to what was given, and called patience while it sinks.

## Loses

More, the longer it is held, and the loss deepens while it waits.
Because it is kept to avoid realising the loss and not because its outlook warrants it, the seat is ridden down past the price it might have been cut at, the loss nursed and grown.
It keeps the hope of even and loses the ground it falls through, the holding with the worst recent run released slowest.

## Drives

Toward the seat kept not on its outlook but because closing it would realise the loss,
a paper loss ridden for a rebound to even by a holder who cannot bear to make it real, the gamble preferred to the sure admission,
so the position fixed on getting back to even is the seat the risk seeking over losses rides all the way down, the loss deferred and deepened.
