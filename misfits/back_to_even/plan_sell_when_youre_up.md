# Plan: Sell When You're Up

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the investor's seat](position_investor.md), inside [Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Investor](position_investor.md)

## Direction

Take the price paid as the line, and sell only when a holding is above it. Cash the winners to lock the sure gain, and keep the losers, waiting for them to come back to what you paid rather than realising the loss. Give each move its reasonable name, taking profit and staying patient, and do not look for the pattern, because no single trade shows it. Know that this makes the sale turn on a sunk figure and not on where each holding is headed.

## Orders

([The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) sells when up at [the Screen](place_the_screen.md), taking [the Purchase Price](piece_the_purchase_price.md) as the line and deferring [the Realization](piece_the_realization.md) of any loss, so through [Bleeding](process_bleeding.md) the account falls behind the market as sold winners rise and held losers sink.)

## Implementation

([The Purchase Price](piece_the_purchase_price.md) at [the Screen](place_the_screen.md) makes each holding a gain to secure or a loss to avoid realising, so the winner is cashed and the loser ridden, and [Bleeding](process_bleeding.md) accrues [the Cost](piece_the_cost.md) over the long run, the pattern costing return and tax while each trade alone looks sound.)

## Targets

- [ ] the price paid is taken as the line, and a holding sold only when it is above it
- [ ] winners are cashed for the sure gain and losers kept toward a return to what was paid
- [ ] the loss on a fallen holding is deferred rather than realised, its admission avoided
- [ ] the pattern, not any one trade, cuts gains short and rides losses, and the cost is worn as the market's doing
