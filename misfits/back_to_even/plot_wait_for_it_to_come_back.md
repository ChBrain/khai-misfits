# Plot: Wait for It to Come Back

## Taxonomy

[Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Screen](place_the_screen.md), a holding fallen below the price paid, a loss now sure if taken.

## Action

[The Investor](persona_the_investor.md) holds [The Loser](persona_the_loser.md), from [the loser's seat](position_loser.md) running [Wait for the Rebound](plan_wait_for_the_rebound.md), through [Riding](process_riding.md), fixing on [The Break-Even](piece_the_break_even.md): the purchase price it resolves to sell at, and no lower. The scene closes with the loser kept, the sale waiting on a level the market has no reason to honour.

## Stage

Over a loss people are risk-seeking, preferring the gamble of holding to the sure loss, and realising the loss is painful to admit, so the loser is ridden (Shefrin and Statman; Tversky and Kahneman). This is not Good Money After Bad: no new money is put in, only a losing position left unclosed.

## Tension

The resolve to sell "once it is back to even" waits on a price the market may never return to.
