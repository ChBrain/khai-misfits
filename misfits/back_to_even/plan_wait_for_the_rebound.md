# Plan: Wait for the Rebound

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the loser's seat](position_loser.md), inside [Back to Even](play_back_to_even.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Loser](position_loser.md)

## Direction

Offer the investor the hope of getting back to even, and be kept for it. Standing below the line the price paid drew, in the domain of losses where the investor is risk-seeking, present the gamble of holding for a recovery as preferable to the sure loss of selling now. Be held rather than closed, so the loss stays on paper and unadmitted, fixed on a return to what was paid and called patience, whatever the outlook.

## Orders

([The Loser](persona_the_loser.md) is ridden through [Riding](process_riding.md) at [the Screen](place_the_screen.md), fixing on [the Break-Even](piece_the_break_even.md) as the investor, risk-seeking over losses through [the Turn](piece_the_turn.md), prefers the gamble to the sure loss and defers [the Realization](piece_the_realization.md).)

## Implementation

([The Turn](piece_the_turn.md), risk-seeking below the reference point, makes holding preferable to a sure loss, so [Riding](process_riding.md) at [the Screen](place_the_screen.md) keeps the loser fixed on [the Break-Even](piece_the_break_even.md), a level the market need not honour, and the loss is deferred and, over the long run, deepened.)

## Targets

- [ ] the hope of a return to the price paid is offered, and the holding kept for it
- [ ] the loss is left on paper rather than realised, its admission avoided
- [ ] the sale is fixed on getting back to even, a level the market has no reason to honour
- [ ] the loss is deferred and deepened, the loser ridden down while it waits
