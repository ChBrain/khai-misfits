# Position: Seller

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). The seat of a seller who can raise its price by less than the search cost and lose no customers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A price to set on a good many others also sell.
The knowledge that a raise smaller than the cost of looking loses no one, since no shopper will walk away over it.
No punishment for raising, so long as the raise stays under what it would cost a shopper to compare.

## Orders

Raise it a hair, since no one will leave over it.
Lift the price by less than the search cost, because the shopper before you will not pay to walk for a smaller saving, so the raise is safe and the margin is yours.
Accept what the seat does: that a raise no one walks over is riskless, and a riskless raise, made by all, carries the price to monopoly.

## Loses

Nothing by raising, and that is the trap.
Because the raise is riskless and unpunished, the seat loses nothing it can feel, so it raises again and again, and what is lost is not the seller's margin but the discipline of the market, competition gone though the seat only ever did the sound thing.
It keeps every hair it adds and loses only the low price that competition would have forced, which was never its to keep.

## Drives

Toward the one whose safe raise starts the climb,
lifting the price a hair no one will walk over and, by lifting it, inviting every rival to do the same,
so the seat that only took a riskless margin is the seat that begins the creep to monopoly, sound in each raise and disastrous in their sum.
