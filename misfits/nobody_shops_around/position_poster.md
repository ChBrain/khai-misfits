# Position: Poster

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). The seat of the one who would make comparison free and restore the discipline, at the cost of the transparency it takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The levers of transparency: a way to post, advertise, or compare prices so that looking costs nothing.
The knowledge that free comparison brings the discipline back, since a raised price seen at no cost is a price left.
No costless or lasting way to hold it, since transparency must be built and maintained and sellers have reasons to obscure and complicate what they charge.

## Orders

Post the price, and make comparison free.
Advertise the prices or give the shopper a way to compare at no cost, so the seller that raises is seen and left and the discipline of competition returns.
Accept what the seat spends: that the transparency is real work to build and keep, that it is resisted, and that any residual cost of comparing leaves room for the price to lift again.

## Loses

The ease of the fix, always.
Because transparency is costly to build and maintain and sellers obscure what they charge, the seat cannot make comparison free for nothing, so what it loses is the costless correction, the discipline bought only with the work of posting and the fight against obscured prices.
It keeps the remedy and loses its cheapness, competition restorable and never free.

## Drives

Toward the one who restores competition at the cost of transparency,
posting the prices so a raise is seen and left and, by making looking free, bringing the discipline back,
so the seat that would fix the market is the seat that proves the fix is dear, the low price returned only where comparison is made to cost nothing.
