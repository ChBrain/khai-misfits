# Plot: A Penny More

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Shop](place_the_shop.md), where a seller sets its price.

## Action

Through [Nudging](process_nudging.md), [The Seller](persona_the_seller.md), from [the seller's seat](position_seller.md), on [Raise It a Hair](plan_raise_it_a_hair.md) sets [The Nudge](piece_the_nudge.md): it lifts its price from the competitive level by a hair, by less than the cost of looking, so small that no shopper will pay to walk away from it. The scene closes with the higher price set and no one yet with a reason to leave (Diamond).

## Stage

The raise is trivial and sound: a penny on the tag, less than it would cost a shopper to go and check elsewhere, so the seller keeps every customer and takes the margin, doing nothing a fair shopkeeper would not.

## Tension

The raise is riskless precisely because the saving from walking is smaller than the walk, so the very thing that makes it fair, that no one will miss it, is what makes it stick, the first safe rung of a ladder no one yet sees.
