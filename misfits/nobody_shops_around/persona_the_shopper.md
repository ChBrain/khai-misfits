# Persona: The Shopper

## Taxonomy

[Shopper](position_shopper.md). The buyer free to compare prices but facing a small cost to do it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Practical, unbothered, free to shop anywhere.
Wants the good at a fair price and knows it could walk to the next seller, but sees no sense in spending time to save a trifle.
No laziness in it: it is simply weighing the walk against the saving, as any sensible buyer would.

## Action

Runs [Buy Where You Stand](plan_buy_where_you_stand.md), and through [Staying](process_staying.md) at [the Shop](place_the_shop.md) takes [the Stay](piece_the_stay.md): facing the higher price, it weighs the walk against the hair it would save, finds the walk costs more, and buys where it is.
Cannot gain by searching for a saving smaller than the search, so it correctly declines to walk.
Does what a reasonable buyer does: it stays rather than pay to look for a trifle, and the staying leaves the raise unpunished.

## Shadow

Not lazy and not loyal: it has compared the saving to the cost and chosen rightly, and staying is the sound call, not a failure to bother.
Because its correct choice not to walk is exactly what removes the threat that would have disciplined the price, the shopper that acts sensibly is the shopper that lets the market climb, its good sense the very thing that pays the monopoly price.
It is the buyer who did the smart thing, and the buyer who thereby loses the low price.

## Tell

Says it is not worth the trip, and it is right, and pays the higher price without a second thought.
Cannot see that every shopper says the same, because each stays alone, so its individually sound decision is the market's undoing, the practical buyer who reasons its way to the monopolist's till.
