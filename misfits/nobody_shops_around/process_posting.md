# Process: Posting

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). Borne at [The Board](place_the_board.md), precipitated by [The Poster](persona_the_poster.md) advertising prices to make comparison free, in [Make Looking Free](plot_make_looking_free.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The wish to restore the discipline of competition, once the cost of looking is seen to hold the price up.

## Direction

From prices you must go and find toward prices posted, so comparison costs nothing.

## Lever

That free comparison lets a raised price be seen and left, so the punishment a raise should carry returns, at the cost of building and keeping the transparency (Salop and Stiglitz; Varian).

## Echo

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), competition restored where comparison is free and slipping back where any cost of looking remains.
