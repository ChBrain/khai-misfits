# Piece: The Search Cost

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). The small cost to compare prices, the friction that does all the work even when it is vanishingly small.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Sitting over [The Street](place_the_street.md), between every shop and the next: the time, the effort, the walk, the phone held up, whatever small price a shopper must pay to learn what another seller charges (Stigler; Diamond).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and competition returns: if comparing were free, a seller that raised its price would be seen and left, and the discipline of rivalry would hold.
It bears the load precisely by being small. Because any positive cost of looking, however tiny, is enough to make a raise smaller than it not worth walking away from, the search cost need not be large to collapse competition, and the discontinuity is the point: free search gives the competitive price, any cost at all gives the monopoly one.

## Apparent

A trivial inconvenience: the minor hassle of checking another price, so small it looks like nothing, and it is exactly that smallness that is load-bearing.

## Yearbook

Sits over the market from [The Cost of Looking](plot_the_cost_of_looking.md), the friction the whole trap stands on.
It is what makes [The Nudge](piece_the_nudge.md) safe to make and [The Stay](piece_the_stay.md) rational to choose, the small price of looking that keeps anyone from looking.
