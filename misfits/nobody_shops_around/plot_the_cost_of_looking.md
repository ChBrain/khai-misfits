# Plot: The Cost of Looking

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Street](place_the_street.md), a market of many sellers offering the same good.

## Action

Many sellers line the street with the same good, the very picture of competition, and [The Search Cost](piece_the_search_cost.md) sits over it all: to check the next seller's price a shopper must pay a little time, effort, or a walk. The scene closes with competition that should discipline the price, and a tiny friction standing in the way of it (Stigler; Diamond).

## Stage

On paper the market is open: rivals everywhere, a shopper free to walk. The only thing between the shopper and the next price is a small cost of comparing, so trivial it looks like nothing, and that smallness is exactly what will matter.

## Tension

The one force that would hold the price down, a shopper walking to a cheaper seller, depends on comparison being worth the trouble, and the cost of looking, however small, is enough to make it not, so the discipline is disarmed before a single price moves.
