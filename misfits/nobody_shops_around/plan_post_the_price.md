# Plan: Post the Price

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the poster's seat](position_poster.md), inside [Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Poster](position_poster.md)

## Direction

Make comparison free, and the discipline returns. Post and advertise the prices, or give the shopper a way to see every price at once at no cost, so a seller that raises is seen and left and the price falls back toward cost. Expect the transparency to be dear and contested: it must be built and maintained, sellers will bundle and complicate what they charge to hide it, and any residual cost of comparing leaves room for the price to lift again.

## Orders

([The Poster](persona_the_poster.md) advertises the prices through [Posting](process_posting.md) at [the Board](place_the_board.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) between where comparison is free and where the cost of looking returns.)

## Implementation

([The Board](place_the_board.md) is where the prices are posted; [Posting](process_posting.md) makes comparison cost nothing, so a raise is seen and left and the discipline of competition comes back, at the cost of building and defending the transparency against sellers who obscure.)

## Targets

- [ ] the prices are posted so comparison costs nothing
- [ ] a seller that raises is seen and left, and the price falls back toward cost
- [ ] the transparency is costly to build and is resisted by sellers who obscure
- [ ] any residual cost of comparing leaves room for the price to lift again
