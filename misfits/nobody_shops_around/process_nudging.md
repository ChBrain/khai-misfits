# Process: Nudging

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). Borne at [The Shop](place_the_shop.md), precipitated by [The Seller](persona_the_seller.md) raising its price by a hair, in [A Penny More](plot_a_penny_more.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The chance to raise a price by less than it would cost a shopper to check elsewhere.

## Direction

From the competitive price toward a hair higher, a raise small enough that no shopper will walk away from it.

## Lever

That a raise smaller than the search cost loses the seller no customers, since leaving over it would cost the shopper more than it saves, so the raise is riskless (Diamond).

## Echo

[The Nudge](piece_the_nudge.md), the small safe raise that sticks.
