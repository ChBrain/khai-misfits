# Piece: The Nudge

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). A single seller's tiny price raise, smaller than the search cost, that no consumer will walk over.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [The Shop](place_the_shop.md): a price lifted by a hair from the competitive level, by less than it would cost a shopper to go and check elsewhere, so small that leaving over it would cost more than it saves (Diamond).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the price sits still: it is the nudge, the small safe raise, that starts the climb, and its safety, that no one will walk over it, is what lets it stick.
It bears the load as the unit of the ratchet. Because a raise smaller than the search cost loses the seller no customers, the nudge is riskless, and a riskless raise will always be made and then remade, so the whole creep to monopoly is the nudge repeated by every seller in turn.

## Apparent

A rounding up: from outside it looks like a trivial price tweak, a penny on the tag, not the first step of a march to the monopoly price.
