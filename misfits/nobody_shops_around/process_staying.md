# Process: Staying

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). Borne at [The Shop](place_the_shop.md), precipitated by [The Shopper](persona_the_shopper.md) declining to search at the higher price, in [Not Worth the Walk](plot_not_worth_the_walk.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The higher price met, and the small cost of walking to compare it against another.

## Direction

From a possible search toward buying where one stands, the walk weighed and declined.

## Lever

That the saving from searching is smaller than the cost of the search, so staying is the correct choice, and the correct choice leaves the raise unpunished (Diamond).
