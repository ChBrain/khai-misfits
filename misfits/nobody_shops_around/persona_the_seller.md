# Persona: The Seller

## Taxonomy

[Seller](position_seller.md). A seller who can raise its price by less than the search cost and lose no customers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, fair, a shopkeeper like any other.
Sells the same good the shop next door sells and prices it sensibly, with no wish to gouge and no sense of doing anything but running a business.
No greed on show: it means only to make a reasonable margin, and raising a hair no one will miss looks like exactly that.

## Action

Runs [Raise It a Hair](plan_raise_it_a_hair.md), and through [Nudging](process_nudging.md) at [the Shop](place_the_shop.md) sets [the Nudge](piece_the_nudge.md): a price lifted by less than the cost of looking, so small no shopper will walk away from it.
Cannot lose customers by the raise, since leaving over it would cost the shopper more than it saves, so the raise is safe and it takes the margin.
Does what any sensible seller does: it raises where a raise is riskless, and the riskless raise, made by all, begins the climb.

## Shadow

Not a gouger: each raise is small, sound, and unpunished, and the shopkeeper is right that no one will walk over it.
Because the raise is riskless and every rival will make the same one, its individually sensible margin is the first step of a march to the monopoly price, so its fairness in the small is disaster in the sum.
It is the seller doing nothing wrong, and the seller that starts the creep.

## Tell

Adds a penny and shrugs, sure no one will notice or mind, and no one does.
Cannot see the ladder, because each rung is trivial, so it raises and raises in good conscience, the fair shopkeeper whose safe small margins add up to the price of a monopoly.
