# Position: Shopper

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). The seat of the buyer free to compare prices but facing a small cost to do it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The freedom to take its custom to any seller.
A small cost to compare: the time, effort, or walk it takes to learn another price.
No reason to pay that cost for a saving smaller than it, so it buys where it stands when the difference is a hair.

## Orders

Buy where you stand when the saving is smaller than the look.
Weigh the walk against the difference, and when the difference is less than the cost of walking, stay and buy, since that is the correct call.
Accept what the seat cannot help: that the correct call, made by every shopper, is exactly what leaves each seller's raise unpunished.

## Loses

The low price competition would have given.
Because it rationally declines to search for a saving smaller than the search, the seat leaves the raise unpunished, so what it loses is the discipline that would have held the price down, and it loses it by choosing correctly not to walk.
It keeps its time and effort and loses the low price, the sound stay that lets the market climb.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound stay leaves the raise unpunished,
declining to search for a saving smaller than the walk and, by declining, removing the one threat that disciplined the price,
so the seat that only spent its time wisely is the seat through which competition loses its lever, correct in each choice and paying the monopoly price for all of them.
