# Place: The Shop

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). Where a seller sets its price and a shopper decides to stay or search. Hosts [A Penny More](plot_a_penny_more.md) and [Not Worth the Walk](plot_not_worth_the_walk.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A price on the tag and a shopper before it: the ordinary moment of a purchase, one seller's price and one buyer's choice to take it or go.

## Holds

[The Nudge](piece_the_nudge.md), the small raise set here, and [The Stay](piece_the_stay.md), the shopper's decision not to walk.

## Offers

To the seller, a safe raise no shopper will leave over; to the shopper, the good at the price shown, here and now without a walk.

## Withheld

What the next shop charges: the one fact that would discipline the price, hidden behind the small cost of going to look.
