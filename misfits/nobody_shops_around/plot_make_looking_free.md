# Plot: Make Looking Free

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Board](place_the_board.md), where someone tries to restore the discipline.

## Action

[The Poster](persona_the_poster.md), from [the poster's seat](position_poster.md), on [Post the Price](plan_post_the_price.md) through [Posting](process_posting.md) holds [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): it posts and advertises the prices so comparison is free, and the seller that raises is seen and left, so the price falls back toward cost, and where some shoppers search at no cost a spread of prices returns. The scene closes with competition restorable at the cost of making search free, the trap bounded (Salop and Stiglitz; Varian).

## Stage

The remedy is cheap comparison, and it is not free: post the prices and a raise is seen and punished, so the discipline comes back, but the transparency must be built and kept, and sellers bundle and obscure what they charge to make looking cost again.

## Tension

The poster is right that free comparison restores competition and cannot make comparison free for nothing or for good, so the low price returns only where looking is made cheap, and slips back wherever any cost of comparing remains, the fix real and dear and never quite settled.
