# Plan: Match the Market

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the rival's seat](position_rival.md), inside [Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Rival](position_rival.md)

## Direction

Hold your price with the others rather than undercut, since undercutting wins nothing. A shopper already in another shop will not walk to you over a hair, and one not yet shopping will not cross the street to save one, so a cut only throws away margin for custom the search cost keeps from coming. Match the market instead: it is the sound course, and it means the discipline that a lower price should bring is gone.

## Orders

([The Rival](persona_the_rival.md) matches the going price rather than cut, letting the whole market rise together, in [Not Worth the Walk](plot_not_worth_the_walk.md).)
