# Plan: Raise It a Hair

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the seller's seat](position_seller.md), inside [Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Seller](position_seller.md)

## Direction

Lift your price by less than the cost of looking, and lose no one. A shopper before you will not walk to the next seller to save a difference smaller than the walk, so a raise kept under the search cost is riskless: you take the margin and keep the custom. Do not fear the loss of customers, since none will leave over a hair, and do not stop at one, since the next raise is as safe as the last, all the way up to the price no rival could beat anyway.

## Orders

([The Seller](persona_the_seller.md) sets [the Nudge](piece_the_nudge.md) through [Nudging](process_nudging.md) at [the Shop](place_the_shop.md), a raise smaller than the search cost.)

## Implementation

([The Shop](place_the_shop.md) is where the price is set; [Nudging](process_nudging.md) lifts it by less than the cost of looking, so no shopper walks, and because every rival can and will do the same, the safe small raise is the first rung of the climb to monopoly.)

## Targets

- [ ] the price is raised by less than the cost of looking
- [ ] no shopper walks, since leaving over it costs more than it saves
- [ ] the raise is riskless, so it is made and then remade
- [ ] every rival can raise the same, so the safe raise begins the creep to monopoly
