# Place: The Board

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). Where prices might be posted and comparison made free, restoring the discipline. Hosts [Make Looking Free](plot_make_looking_free.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A price board, an advertisement, a comparison anyone can read: the prices of many sellers laid side by side at no cost to look.

## Holds

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the conditions under which free comparison restores competition and those under which the cost of looking returns.

## Offers

Free comparison: a way to see every price at once, so a seller that raises is seen and left and the discipline of competition comes back.

## Withheld

A costless and lasting transparency, since the board must be built and maintained, sellers obscure what they charge, and any residual cost of comparing leaves room for the price to lift.
