# Piece: The Monopoly Price

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). The unique equilibrium at the full monopoly price, many competitors charging what a single monopolist would.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Reached across [The Street](place_the_street.md), the same high price at every shop: the level at which no seller wants to raise further, the price a single owner of the whole market would set, arrived at by many sellers each raising a hair (Diamond).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no misfit, only a market that competes: it is the monopoly price, reached by competitors and not a monopolist, that makes the structure a trap.
It carries the phenomenon whole. Because the creep stops only where a monopolist would stop, the many sellers settle exactly at the monopoly price, so a market that looks fully competitive charges as if it had one owner, a discontinuous jump from the competitive price produced by nothing but a vanishing cost of looking.

## Apparent

A firm market price: from outside it looks like the going rate, the price the good simply costs, indistinguishable from a competitive price except that it is the monopolist's.
