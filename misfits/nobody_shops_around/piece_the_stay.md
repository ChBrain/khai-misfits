# Piece: The Stay

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). The shopper's rational choice not to search, buying where it stands because the saving is smaller than the look.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Made at [The Shop](place_the_shop.md), at the moment of the higher price: the shopper weighs the walk to the next seller against the hair it would save, finds the walk costs more, and stays (Diamond).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the nudge fails: if the shopper walked, the raise would lose the seller its custom and the price would fall back, so it is the stay that lets the raise stick.
It bears the load as the sound non-response. Because searching costs more than a raise smaller than the search cost saves, the shopper is right to stay, and its correct choice not to walk is exactly what removes the punishment a raise would otherwise carry, so the discipline of competition dies in a reasonable decision.

## Apparent

Laziness, or loyalty: from outside the shopper looks like it could not be bothered to compare, when it has compared the saving to the cost and chosen correctly.
