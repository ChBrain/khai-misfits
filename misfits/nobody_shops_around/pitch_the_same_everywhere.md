# Pitch: The Same Everywhere

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Ordinary and competitive.
A market of many sellers of the same good, rivals on every corner, prices that look set by competition and a shopper free to take their custom anywhere.
Nothing false in it: the sellers really do compete, the shopper really is free to walk, and on paper the market is exactly as open as it looks.

## Undertow

Under the open market, a small cost of comparing that no one pays.
Because looking costs a little, a seller can raise its price by less than the look and lose no one, and a rival gains nothing by undercutting, so the prices sit high and the same at every shop, set as if by one owner (Diamond).
No gouger and no fool: raising a hair no one will walk over is sound, and declining to search for a saving smaller than the walk is sound, and both are exactly what hold the price up.

## Nerve

The freedom to shop anywhere, and the catch that shopping anywhere is pointless:
that the prices are the same everywhere because no one compares, and no one compares because the prices are the same everywhere, the small cost of looking and the monopoly price the one friction seen twice,
so a market of many competitors charges the price of one, and the openness that looks like competition is exactly what makes looking not worth the trouble.

## Echo

After the run: the identical prices at every filling station and airport kiosk, a competitive market on paper charging as if by a single owner, a monopoly price with no monopolist and no conspiracy behind it (Diamond).
And the remedy, at a cost and resisted: make search free by posting and advertising prices, or a market with enough zero-cost searchers, so the seller that raises is seen and left and a spread returns, though transparency must be built and kept and sellers have reasons to obscure what they charge (Salop and Stiglitz; Varian).
And the bound held in view, because the result is conditional and not a law: it needs a positive search cost, a homogeneous good, and prices you must go and find, so the whole of it is that where nobody shops around, many sellers charge the price of one.
