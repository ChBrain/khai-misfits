# Plot: All the Way Up

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Street](place_the_street.md), where what one seller did, every seller does.

## Action

Through [Creeping](process_creeping.md), [The Creep](piece_the_creep.md) carries the raises across the market: each seller lifts a hair, each raise sticks and is followed, and no rival undercuts, so the prices climb together until [The Monopoly Price](piece_the_monopoly_price.md), the level a single owner would set. The scene closes with the market at the monopoly price, competition disciplining nothing, the jump made by a vanishing friction (Diamond).

## Stage

The climb ends only at the top: the sole price at which no seller wants to raise further is the monopoly price, so the many competitors settle exactly where one owner would, not by collusion and not by any seller charging more than the rest, but by all of them, a hair at a time.

## Tension

A vanishing cost of looking has produced the full monopoly price, so the market that is open in every visible way charges as if closed, the gap between the competitive price and the monopoly one crossed by a friction almost too small to name.
