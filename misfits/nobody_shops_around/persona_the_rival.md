# Persona: The Rival

## Taxonomy

[Rival](position_rival.md). The competitor who could undercut to win customers, and gains nothing by it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competitive, shrewd, always eyeing the others' prices.
Sells the same good as the shop across the way and would gladly take its customers, watching for the chance to win business by charging less.
No timidity in it: it is a keen competitor, and undercutting is exactly the move it would make if the move paid.

## Action

Runs [Match the Market](plan_match_the_market.md), and finding it can win no customers by cutting, holds its price with the rest rather than undercut, since a shopper in another shop will not walk over for a hair and one outside will not come for one.
Cannot draw custom by cutting, because the search cost keeps the customers it would court from coming, so a cut only forfeits margin for nothing.
Does what a shrewd competitor does: it declines a cut that would gain no one, and by declining lets the whole market rise together.

## Shadow

Not timid and not colluding: it would undercut in a heartbeat if it paid, and matching the market is the sound call precisely because a cut wins nothing.
Because the discipline of competition, a lower price pulling buyers over, is worth nothing once the difference is smaller than the cost of coming, the keen competitor cannot punish a rival's raise, so its shrewdness confirms the failure rather than fixing it.
It is the competitor who wanted to compete, and the competitor who proves competition has no teeth here.

## Tell

Eyes the others' prices, calculates the cut, and pockets the pen: no gain in it.
Cannot make the undercut pay, because the search cost withholds the customers it would win, so it matches instead, the keen rival whose sound refusal to cut is one more seller at the monopoly price.
