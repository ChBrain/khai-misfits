# Piece: The Creep

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). The induction, every seller nudging and none undercut, so the raises repeat and the price ratchets upward.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Spreading across [The Street](place_the_street.md), shop by shop: what one seller raised without loss, every seller raises, and no rival cuts it back, so the whole market climbs together, each a hair at a time (Diamond).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and one raise is a curiosity: it is the creep, the raise made by all and followed step on step, that turns a single nudge into a market at the monopoly price.
It bears the load as the ratchet. Because every seller can nudge safely and no rival is punished for following rather than undercutting, the raises do not stop at one, they repeat across the market and up the ladder, so the creep is the mechanism that carries the price all the way to the top.

## Apparent

Prices drifting up: from outside it looks like ordinary inflation or a market firming, not a ratchet with a fixed destination.

## Yearbook

Carried by [Creeping](process_creeping.md) in [All the Way Up](plot_all_the_way_up.md), the nudges repeating across the market.
It is [The Nudge](piece_the_nudge.md) made general, and it ends only at [The Monopoly Price](piece_the_monopoly_price.md), the top of the ladder no seller wants to climb past.
