# Persona: The Poster

## Taxonomy

[Poster](position_poster.md). The one who would make comparison free and restore the discipline, at the cost of the transparency it takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Clear-eyed, practical, out to make the market work.
Sees prices held high by nothing but the trouble of comparing them, and means to fix it by making comparison free: a board, an advertisement, a way to see every price at once.
No naivety in it: it is right that transparency brings the discipline back, and its aim is sound.

## Action

Runs [Post the Price](plan_post_the_price.md), and through [Posting](process_posting.md) at [the Board](place_the_board.md) holds [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): it advertises the prices so comparison costs nothing, and the seller that raises is seen and left, so the discipline returns and the price falls back toward cost.
Cannot make the transparency free or lasting, since it must be built and maintained and sellers have reasons to obscure and complicate what they charge.
Does what a sound reformer does: it makes looking cheap where it can, and finds the price lifting again wherever any cost of comparing remains.

## Shadow

Not naive and not defeated: it is right that free comparison restores competition, and it is also unable to make comparison free for nothing or for good.
Because transparency is costly to build and sellers work to obscure prices, the poster proves that the discipline of competition is not free but bought, real and dear, so its fix bounds the trap rather than dissolving it.
It is the one who can bring the low price back, and the one who shows how dearly cheap comparison is won.

## Tell

Puts every price on the board and watches them fall, then watches the sellers bundle, complicate, and obscure until comparison costs again.
Cannot hold the transparency for nothing, because it is fought, so it posts and reposts against a market that keeps hiding its prices, the reformer whose win lasts exactly as long as looking stays free.
