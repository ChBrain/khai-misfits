# Process: Creeping

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). Borne at [The Street](place_the_street.md), precipitated by every seller nudging and no rival undercutting, in [All the Way Up](plot_all_the_way_up.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The first nudge sticking unpunished, so what one seller did, all can.

## Direction

From one safe raise toward the whole market climbing, each seller lifting a hair and each raise followed.

## Lever

That every seller can nudge without losing customers and no rival gains by undercutting, so the raises repeat and none is punished, carrying the price up the ladder (Diamond).

## Echo

[The Creep](piece_the_creep.md), and at its top [The Monopoly Price](piece_the_monopoly_price.md), the level no seller wants to pass.
