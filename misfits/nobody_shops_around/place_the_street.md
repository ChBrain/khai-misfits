# Place: The Street

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). The market of many shops, where the creep spreads and search would happen. Hosts [The Cost of Looking](plot_the_cost_of_looking.md) and [All the Way Up](plot_all_the_way_up.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A row of sellers offering the same good, rivals up and down the street, the very picture of a competitive market.

## Holds

[The Search Cost](piece_the_search_cost.md) between each shop and the next, [The Creep](piece_the_creep.md) as it spreads, and [The Monopoly Price](piece_the_monopoly_price.md) they all reach.

## Offers

The appearance of competition: many rivals to choose among, and the freedom to walk from any one to another.

## Withheld

Any price cheaper than the rest worth the walk to find, so the freedom to compare is a freedom no one profits by using.
