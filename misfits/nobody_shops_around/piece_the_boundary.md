# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). The condition the result needs and the edges where it lifts, the hedge that keeps the trap real but bounded.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Weighed at [The Board](place_the_board.md), where prices might be posted and search made free: the conditions under which the monopoly price holds and those under which competition returns (Salop and Stiglitz; Varian).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overreaches into a claim that all prices are monopoly prices, which is false: the boundary is what keeps the trap honest.
It carries the condition and the escape. The result needs a positive search cost, a homogeneous good, and prices you must go and find; make search free by posting and advertising, let a fraction of shoppers search at no cost, or advertise the prices, and the seller that raises is seen and left and a spread of prices returns, so the trap is bounded and competition can be bought back, at the cost of the transparency it takes.

## Apparent

A set of exceptions where the market does compete, easy to read as softening the result rather than as the line that says exactly when the cost of looking collapses competition and when it does not.
