# Plan: Buy Where You Stand

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the shopper's seat](position_shopper.md), inside [Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Shopper](position_shopper.md)

## Direction

Weigh the walk against the saving, and when the saving is smaller, stay and buy. To compare prices costs you time or effort, and if the difference you might find is less than that cost, searching is a loss, so buy where you stand. This is the correct call every time the raise is a hair, and do not fault yourself for it: paying to look for a trifle is the real waste, and staying is the sound choice.

## Orders

([The Shopper](persona_the_shopper.md) takes [the Stay](piece_the_stay.md) through [Staying](process_staying.md) at [the Shop](place_the_shop.md), declining a search that would save less than it costs.)

## Implementation

([The Shop](place_the_shop.md) is where the choice is made; [Staying](process_staying.md) weighs the walk against the hair and declines it, the correct call, and because every shopper makes the same correct call the raise goes unpunished.)

## Targets

- [ ] the walk is weighed against the saving it would bring
- [ ] the saving is smaller than the search, so staying is correct
- [ ] the shopper buys where it stands rather than pay to look for a trifle
- [ ] the sound stay, made by every shopper, leaves the raise unpunished
