# Position: Rival

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Shops Around](play_nobody_shops_around.md). The seat of the competitor who could undercut to win customers, and gains nothing by it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The ability to cut its price below the others.
No customers to win by doing so, since a shopper in another shop will not walk over for a hair and one outside will not come for one.
A safer course in matching the market, since a cut only throws away margin for custom it cannot draw.

## Orders

Match the market, and do not undercut.
Hold your price with the rest rather than cut, because cutting wins no customers a search cost keeps from coming, so it only forfeits margin for nothing.
Accept what the seat confirms: that the discipline of competition, a lower price pulling buyers over, is worth nothing once the difference is smaller than the cost of coming.

## Loses

The power to discipline the market by undercutting.
Because a cut draws no customers when comparison is costly, the seat cannot punish a rival's raise by underpricing it, so what it loses is the very lever competition is supposed to give it, and it loses it by declining a cut that would gain nothing.
It keeps its margin and loses its threat, the undercut that cannot buy what the search cost withholds.

## Drives

Toward the one whose declining to undercut completes the failure,
matching the creeping price rather than cutting for customers it cannot win and, by matching, letting the whole market rise together,
so the seat that could have been competition's discipline is the seat that proves the discipline is gone, right not to cut and thereby part of the climb.
