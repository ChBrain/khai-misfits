## Cue

[The Shop](place_the_shop.md), where the shopper weighs the walk and the rival weighs the cut.

## Action

Through [Staying](process_staying.md), [The Shopper](persona_the_shopper.md), from [the shopper's seat](position_shopper.md), on [Buy Where You Stand](plan_buy_where_you_stand.md) takes [The Stay](piece_the_stay.md): the saving is smaller than the search, so it buys where it stands. And [The Rival](persona_the_rival.md), from [the rival's seat](position_rival.md), on [Match the Market](plan_match_the_market.md) declines to undercut, since it can win no customer a search cost keeps from coming. The scene closes with the nudge stuck, no shopper walking and no rival cutting (Diamond).

## Stage

Both do the sound thing: the shopper stays because paying to look for a trifle is the real waste, and the rival matches because a cut only forfeits margin for custom it cannot draw, so the raise meets neither of the two forces that should have punished it.

## Tension

The nudge holds not through anyone's error but through two correct choices, the shopper's not to walk and the rival's not to cut, so the discipline of competition dies in a pair of reasonable decisions.
