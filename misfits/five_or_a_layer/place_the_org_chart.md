# Place: The Org Chart

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). Where the shape is set, and where one of the two costs is visible and the other is not.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Boxes and lines, a count of levels, a count of reports per box.
Redrawn every few years and circulated widely.

## Holds

The decision itself, made once and paid at both ends.
It also holds the asymmetry that keeps the cycle running: **depth is visible on a chart and attention is not**, so the layer cost can be pointed at in a meeting and the span cost has to be felt by somebody before anybody sees it.

## Offers

A legible picture of the organisation that anybody can read and argue about, which is what makes the decision discussable at all.

## Withheld

The hours.
No chart shows how much of a manager's week each report actually gets, so the setting that looks cleanest on paper is the one whose cost does not appear on it.
