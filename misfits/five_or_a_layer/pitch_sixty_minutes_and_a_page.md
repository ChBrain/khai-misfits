# Pitch: Sixty Minutes and a Page

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry, structural, administrative.
The register of an organisation chart beside a diary: how many report to whom, how many hours there are in the week, what fits on a page before somebody stops reading.
Nothing indignant anywhere in it, because both settings are defensible and one of them has to be chosen.

## Undertow

Under the chart, two facts that do not negotiate.
An hour has sixty minutes, so attention divides. A summary is shorter than its source, so a layer delivers less. Every arrangement of people spends one to buy the other, and there is no third arrangement.
Supervision and fidelity are not two dials. They are one dial with a cost written at each end.

## Nerve

The unease of watching a reorganisation fix a real problem,
correctly, on evidence, against a complaint that was justified,
and produce the other problem in the same motion, on a three-year cycle, forever.

## Echo

After the run: a chart redrawn, a span that is either too wide to watch or narrow enough to need another row of boxes above it,
a summary that is honest and short,
and an arithmetic published in 1933 that tells anybody which cost they are choosing and has never told anybody how to avoid choosing.
