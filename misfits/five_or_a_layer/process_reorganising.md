# Process: Reorganising

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). Redrawing the shape to answer whichever cost has become visible, and thereby commissioning the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An accumulated complaint with evidence behind it.
At [the org chart](place_the_org_chart.md), levels removed or managers added.

## Direction

Toward the cost currently being felt, and away from it.
Firms did flatten and the evidence is specific: across three hundred large American firms the count reporting directly to the chief executive rose from about four and a half in 1986 to nearly seven by 1999, with levels between division heads and the top removed, in response to competition. That is the layer cost being paid down.
And the follow-up work found the flattening was not what it had been sold as, reading less like delegation than like centralisation, because removing the levels also removes the places where decisions used to be made below the top.

## Lever

Whichever complaint is loud, which is decided by which cost is visible rather than by which is larger.
Because depth shows on a chart and attention does not, the layer cost is easier to raise in a meeting, so the cycle has a slight bias toward flattening.

## Echo

A correct answer, and a clock started on its counterpart.
The sequence looks like an organisation that cannot make up its mind and is a run of right decisions against alternating complaints.
