# Piece: The Swap

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). The exchange every reorganisation makes: one cost paid down, its counterpart taken on, in the same act.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the org chart](place_the_org_chart.md), each time it is redrawn.
Recorded nowhere as an exchange: each redraw is minuted as a solution to the complaint it answered.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a static trade and there is nothing to watch.
It is load bearing because it shows the trade is **not settled by knowing about it**: the flattening across three hundred firms was evidenced, correct, and raised chief-executive spans from about four and a half to nearly seven, and the follow-up found it read less like the delegation it was sold as than like centralisation.

## Apparent

Organisational churn, or a management fashion cycle.
It reads as indecision, and it is a sequence of correct answers to whichever cost is currently visible, which is a different thing and cannot be cured by resolve.

## Yearbook

Executed by [reorganising](process_reorganising.md), between [the span](piece_the_span.md) and [the layer](piece_the_layer.md).
It runs slightly more often toward flattening, because depth is legible on [the org chart](place_the_org_chart.md) and attention is not.
