# Piece: The Shorter Version

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). The honest summary: accurate in all it contains, silent on all it does not, and useful in exactly the degree it is lossy.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Written at [the floor below](place_the_floor_below.md), read above.
Answered nowhere: an omission cannot announce itself, so nobody above learns what they were not told.

## Load Bearing

Without it the layer delivers nothing and the week above is not protected.
It is load bearing because **value and loss are the same quantity here**: a summary as long as its source has not summarised, so the shortening that makes it worth reading is the shortening that drops things, and this needs no interest, no incentive and no distortion, only the definition of a summary.

## Apparent

Good communication, and it is.
It reads as competence and consideration, and the fact that what was dropped was chosen by somebody who could not know what the reader would need does not appear in the document.

## Yearbook

Produced by [summarising](process_summarising.md) at each [layer](piece_the_layer.md), and compressed again at the next.
It is what [the finite hour](piece_the_finite_hour.md) required, and what makes depth cost fidelity.
