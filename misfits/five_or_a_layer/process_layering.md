# Process: Layering

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). Inserting a level so that spans can be small, and thereby inserting a point at which less passes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A span too wide to hold.
At [the org chart](place_the_org_chart.md), four managers appearing where one stood.

## Direction

Toward supervision that actually happens.
This step works: the teams are now small enough to know, the one-to-ones happen, and the people on the floor are genuinely better managed than they were.
And the same step creates a compression, because the person above now reads four accounts instead of holding twenty conversations, which is the reason the layer was worth adding.

## Lever

The finite week above, which is what made the wide span unmanageable in the first place.
Because the layer exists to protect that week, it must deliver less than the floor contains, or it has not protected anything.

## Echo

Better supervision and a shorter account, from one act.
Neither is a side effect of the other: they are the same structural change described from below and from above.
