# Plot: So You Add a Layer

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A span too wide for anybody to hold, and the obvious remedy.
Four managers appearing on [the org chart](place_the_org_chart.md) where one stood.

## Action

[Layering](process_layering.md): [The Manager](persona_the_manager.md), holding [the relay's seat](position_relay.md), takes a team small enough to know, while [The Report](persona_the_report.md) in [the supervised seat](position_supervised.md) gets a real one-to-one for the first time in months.

## Stage

[The Manager](persona_the_manager.md), on [Pass Up What Matters](plan_pass_up_what_matters.md), supervises properly and then writes up the week for somebody with less time than it takes to recount.
[The Report](persona_the_report.md), on [Be Seen Often Enough](plan_be_seen_often_enough.md), is genuinely better managed than before, which is not a small thing and is the whole reason the layer was added.

## Tension

The supervision is real, and the compression arrived in the same act.
Nothing here is a side effect. The person above now reads four accounts instead of holding twenty conversations, and **that is not a cost of the layer, it is the layer**: a level that passed everything through would be a longer meeting, and the week it was created to protect would be unprotected. So the organisation has bought attention on the floor with a point at which less travels, and both halves were delivered by one correct decision.
