# Piece: The Span

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). The count of people reporting to one person: the free variable, and the one whose cost no chart displays.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [the org chart](place_the_org_chart.md), felt at [the one-to-one](place_the_one_to_one.md).
Answered nowhere: widening it thins attention and narrowing it adds a compression, with no third setting.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is nothing to choose and no structure to the play.
It is load bearing because it is **one dial with a cost at each end**: setting a span is the same act as setting a depth, so supervision and fidelity are not two things to be balanced but one quantity spent in one direction or the other.

## Apparent

An organisational parameter, adjustable and neutral.
It looks like a setting with a right answer somewhere, and the reason there is no right answer, that both directions spend something, is not visible from the number itself.

## Yearbook

Set by [spanning](process_spanning.md), divided by [attending](process_attending.md), and bounded by [Graicunas's number](piece_graicunass_number.md).
Narrowing it produces [the layer](piece_the_layer.md); widening it produces a rescheduled one-to-one.
