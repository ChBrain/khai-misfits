# Position: Compression

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). The seat where value and loss are the same quantity, because a summary's usefulness is its shortness.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Whatever happened, and a page to put it on.
Honest intent, and no way to signal an omission.
A reader with less time than the source requires.

## Orders

Select.
Keep what seems to matter, drop what does not.
Stand in, above, for the week it describes.

## Loses

Precisely what makes it worth reading, in the act of becoming worth reading: a summary the length of its source has not summarised, so the loss is not a defect in the compression but the compression itself.
Because the omission is judged by somebody who cannot know what the reader will need, more care does not recover it and only length would, which is what was unavailable.
It ends true, useful, and missing whatever turns out to matter.

## Drives

Toward an account somebody will actually read,
never once inaccurate,
so the seat whose whole merit is being shorter is the seat where what was dropped stops existing.
