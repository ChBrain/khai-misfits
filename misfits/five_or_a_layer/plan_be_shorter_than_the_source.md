# Plan: Be Shorter Than the Source

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the compression's seat](position_compression.md), inside [Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Compression](position_compression.md)

## Direction

Be shorter than what you describe, because that is the whole of your usefulness. Be accurate in everything you contain and silent about everything you do not, there being no way for an omission to announce itself.

## Orders

([The Summary](persona_the_summary.md) selects, keeps what seems to matter, drops what does not, and stands in above for a week of work nobody there will otherwise hear about.)

## Implementation

([The Floor Below](place_the_floor_below.md), which it represents; the [summarising](process_summarising.md) process at each [layering](process_layering.md); compressed again at the next layer if there is one.)

## Targets

- [ ] the account is shorter than its source, which is what makes it worth reading
- [ ] everything in it is accurate
- [ ] what was dropped leaves no trace, an omission having no way to announce itself
- [ ] the value and the loss are the same quantity, so care cannot separate them
