# Piece: The Layer

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). A level inserted so that spans can be small, whose defining function is that less reaches the person above.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the org chart](place_the_org_chart.md), as a row of boxes.
Between [the floor below](place_the_floor_below.md) and whoever decides.

## Load Bearing

Without it nobody past a certain size is supervised at all.
It is load bearing because it is **not a defect**: a layer that passed everything through would be a longer meeting rather than a layer, so the compression is the definition and not a failure mode, and organisations have layers precisely because the week above is finite.

## Apparent

Bureaucracy, or distance from the floor, or a middle that has got too thick.
It is the cost that shows on a chart, which is why it is the one that gets removed, and its benefit is invisible on the same chart.

## Yearbook

Created by [layering](process_layering.md) to relieve [the span](piece_the_span.md).
Each one adds a [summarising](process_summarising.md) step, and removing one is [the swap](piece_the_swap.md) run in the flattening direction.
