# Position: Redesign

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). The seat that answers whichever cost is visible and thereby commissions the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real, accumulated complaint and the authority to redraw.
Evidence, usually good: distance from the floor, or spans nobody can manage.
No option that addresses both, since they are the same dial.

## Orders

Flatten when the complaint is distance.
Deepen when the complaint is neglect.
Be called again about the other one.

## Loses

Nothing, and it generates the cycle: each redraw is the correct answer to the cost being felt and the cause of the cost that will be felt next, so what reads as organisational indecision is a run of right decisions against alternating complaints.
Because no shape holds both, there is nothing for the cycle to converge on.
It ends having solved the stated problem and started the clock on its counterpart.

## Drives

Toward a shape that answers the complaint on the table,
right in every single instance to answer it,
so the seat that repeatedly fixes the organisation is the reason it is repeatedly fixed.
