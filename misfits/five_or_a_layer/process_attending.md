# Process: Attending

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). Dividing a fixed week across however many people report directly, at whatever slice that leaves.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A set span and a diary.
At [the one-to-one](place_the_one_to_one.md), recurring half hours that either happen or are moved.

## Direction

Toward supervision, in proportion to how few people there are to supervise.
The arithmetic is unforgiving and is not about effort: a manager with fifteen reports working flat out gives each of them a fifteenth of whatever is left after everything else, and working harder does not create a sixteenth hour.
This is the cost that does not appear on any chart, and it is the one the next reorganisation will eventually be called about.

## Lever

[The finite hour](piece_the_finite_hour.md), which does not stretch.
Because the constraint is time rather than willingness, no amount of commitment at this seat converts into more of it.

## Echo

Either a manager who knows what somebody is working on, or a meeting that keeps being rescheduled.
The difference is a number set on a chart by somebody else.
