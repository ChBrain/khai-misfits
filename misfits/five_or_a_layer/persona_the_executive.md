# Persona: The Executive

## Taxonomy

[Attender](position_attender.md). Has a finite week and more people below than a week holds, so must choose which cost to pay.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Capable, experienced, and entirely aware of the trade.
Can state both halves of it before being asked: too many reports and nobody is watched, too few and the news arrives pre-chewed.
Nothing complacent in it: the choice is made deliberately and reviewed.

## Action

Sets a span, and thereby sets a depth, since the two are the same decision made once.
Spends the week across whoever reports directly, at whatever slice that leaves each of them.
Reads what the layers below send up, which is less than what happened.

## Shadow

Not overstretched by poor planning, and structurally unable to buy both: widening the span thins the attention, narrowing it inserts a compression, and the week does not lengthen either way.
So the seat's competence shows in knowing which cost it is choosing and never in escaping the choice.
It ends supervising fewer people properly or more people thinly, and reading a shorter account of everything either way.

## Tell

Says: I can hold about six of these properly.
Which is true, is roughly Graicunas's number, and settles the shape of everything beneath it.
