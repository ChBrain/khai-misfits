# Position: Relay

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). The seat created to make supervision possible, whose function is to deliver less upward.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A span small enough to know properly.
Honest intent and nothing whatever to protect: no delivery target, no continuity mandate, no career at stake.
A recipient above with less time than the material takes to read.

## Orders

Supervise the small span properly.
Gather what happened and write it shorter.
Decide what does not go in.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and everything the compression drops: the seat is not harmed, is not culpable, and is the point at which the material shrinks, so what is lost is lost by a party doing exactly what it was created to do.
Because the shortening is the seat's purpose rather than its failure, doing the job better compresses more confidently.
It ends having supervised well and transmitted less, which are one act.

## Drives

Toward a team that is properly managed and an account that fits on a page,
never once shading anything,
so the seat that makes supervision possible is the seat where the information goes.
