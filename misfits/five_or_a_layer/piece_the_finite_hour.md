# Piece: The Finite Hour

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). The week that does not lengthen, which is one of the two facts the whole structure rests on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Behind [attending](process_attending.md), binding every division of it.
Answered nowhere: no shape, no tooling and no commitment adds an hour.

## Load Bearing

Without it a manager could hold any number of reports properly and there is no trade at all.
It is load bearing because it makes the constraint **arithmetic rather than motivational**: a manager with fifteen reports is not failing, they are dividing, and no version of trying harder produces a sixteenth hour.

## Apparent

The ordinary fact that people are busy.
It reads as a workload complaint rather than as a structural limit, which is why the span cost is usually discussed as though somebody could simply manage better.

## Yearbook

Divided at [attending](process_attending.md), across whatever [the span](piece_the_span.md) is set to.
It is what makes [the layer](piece_the_layer.md) worth adding, and therefore what causes [the shorter version](piece_the_shorter_version.md).
