# Plan: Fix Whichever One Is Hurting

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the redesign's seat](position_redesign.md), inside [Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Redesign](position_redesign.md)

## Direction

Answer the complaint that has accumulated. Flatten when the top is too far from the floor, deepen when nobody is being managed, and do it on the evidence, because the evidence is real either way.

## Orders

([The Reorganisation](persona_the_reorganisation.md) removes levels and widens spans, or cuts spans and adds managers, each correct against the cost currently being felt.)

## Implementation

([The Org Chart](place_the_org_chart.md), redrawn; the [reorganising](process_reorganising.md) process moving between [the span](piece_the_span.md) and [the layer](piece_the_layer.md); the exchange recorded as [the swap](piece_the_swap.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] a real complaint has accumulated and is evidenced
- [ ] the redraw answers exactly that complaint and answers it correctly
- [ ] the same redraw creates the counterpart cost, necessarily and immediately
- [ ] the next reorganisation is called, in a few years, about the other one
