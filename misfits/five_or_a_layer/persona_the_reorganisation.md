# Persona: The Reorganisation

## Taxonomy

[Redesign](position_redesign.md). The periodic redraw that solves whichever cost is currently being felt and produces the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Well-founded, evidenced, and genuinely responsive.
Called because a real complaint has accumulated: the middle is too thick, or nobody is being managed.
No cynicism in it: each redraw answers the thing it was called for.

## Action

Flattens when the complaint is distance, removing levels and widening spans, as large firms did through the nineties.
Deepens when the complaint is neglect, cutting spans and adding managers.
Is called again in a few years, about the other one.

## Shadow

Nothing, and it is the engine's visible form: each move is the correct answer to the cost being felt and the cause of the cost that will be felt next, so a sequence that looks like indecision is a sequence of right decisions.
Because there is no setting holding both, the cycle is not a failure to settle but the absence of anything to settle on.
It ends having fixed the problem it was called for, on schedule, and started the clock on the next one.

## Tell

Says: we are removing a layer to get closer to the customer.
True, correct, evidenced, and a decision to widen every span in the company.
