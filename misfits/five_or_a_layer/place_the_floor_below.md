# Place: The Floor Below

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). Where the work happens and where everything the summary dropped stays, unreported and unrecoverable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Teams doing the actual work, one or several layers beneath anybody who decides.
A week of events, most of which will not be written up.

## Holds

The source material, in full, and the difference between it and what travels.
It holds the part of the account that was true, was left out for good reasons, and will not be asked about because nobody above knows it exists.

## Offers

Everything that happened, to anybody standing on it.
The information is not hidden here and is not scarce here: it is simply longer than the channel above it.

## Withheld

Its own volume, at every layer above.
The floor cannot send itself upward, only an account of itself, and the account is shorter by construction, so the withholding is a property of the channel rather than a decision by anybody on the floor.
