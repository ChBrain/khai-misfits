# Process: Summarising

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). Writing an account shorter than what it describes, honestly, and choosing what does not go in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A week of work and a reader with less time than it takes to recount.
At [the floor below](place_the_floor_below.md), gathered up into a page.

## Direction

Toward something that will actually be read.
The compression is the value: an account as long as its source has not summarised anything and protects nobody's week.
So the omission is the function rather than the failure, and **it needs no interest whatever**: give the writer nothing to protect and no reason to shade, and a faithful summary is still shorter than its source.

## Lever

Length, which is the only thing that would recover what was dropped, and which is precisely what was not available.
Because the selection is made by somebody who cannot know what the reader will later need, care improves accuracy and cannot restore coverage.

## Echo

A true, useful, shorter account.
What was left out leaves no trace, since an omission has no way to announce itself, so nobody above learns what they were not told.
