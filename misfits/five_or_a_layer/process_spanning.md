# Process: Spanning

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). Setting how many report to one person, which is also setting how many layers the organisation has.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

More people than one person can attend to.
At [the org chart](place_the_org_chart.md), a number chosen for each box.

## Direction

Toward a shape that can be operated.
The number is not a style preference: [Graicunas's number](piece_graicunass_number.md) shows the load rising far faster than the count, because the relationships among the reports are the manager's problem too.
And the choice is single: a span and a depth are the same decision, since the people have to be arranged somehow and narrowing one widens the other.

## Lever

The count of people to be arranged, which is given, and the length of a week, which is fixed.
Because neither is adjustable, the only free variable is which of the two costs to pay.

## Echo

A chart, and a commitment nobody can see the whole of.
The depth is legible on the page; the attention per report is not.
