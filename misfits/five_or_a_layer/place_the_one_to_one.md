# Place: The One-to-One

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). Where attention is actually spent, half an hour at a time, and where a wide span shows up as a meeting that keeps being moved.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A recurring half hour in a diary, or a slot that has been rescheduled three times.
Two people, one of whom has a number of these to get through.

## Holds

The whole of what supervision concretely is, and the quantity that divides.
It is where the span stops being a number on a chart and becomes either a conversation that happens or one that does not.

## Offers

Genuine attention, where the span leaves room for it: a manager who knows what somebody is working on and can act on what they hear.

## Withheld

Duplication.
The half hour given to one report is not available to another, so the place cannot be in two spans at once, which is the arithmetic the whole play runs on.
