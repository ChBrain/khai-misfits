# Plot: The Shorter Version Is the Point

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A summary being written by somebody with nothing to protect: no delivery target, no continuity mandate, no career at stake.
A week of work, and a page.

## Action

[Summarising](process_summarising.md): [The Summary](persona_the_summary.md), holding [the compression seat](position_compression.md), selects what goes in and what does not, and travels upward standing in for everything at [the floor below](place_the_floor_below.md).

## Stage

[The Summary](persona_the_summary.md), on [Be Shorter Than the Source](plan_be_shorter_than_the_source.md), is accurate in everything it contains.
[The Manager](persona_the_manager.md) writes it in good faith and would write the same one if the organisation had no politics in it at all.

## Tension

Strip out every motive and the loss is untouched.
This is the plot that separates the play from every account of information distorted by people with something to gain. Give the writer nothing to defend and no reason to shade a single word, and **a faithful summary is still shorter than its source**. The shortening is what makes it worth reading, so value and loss are one quantity, and the selection is made by somebody who cannot know what the reader will later need. More care buys accuracy and cannot buy coverage: only length would, and length is exactly what was unavailable. And an omission has no way to announce itself, so nobody above ever learns what they were not told.
