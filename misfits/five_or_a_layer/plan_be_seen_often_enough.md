# Plan: Be Seen Often Enough

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the supervised's seat](position_supervised.md), inside [Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Supervised](position_supervised.md)

## Direction

Do the work and be seen as often as the span allows. Ask for a reasonable share of a manager's time, which is available under one setting and not the other, and have no say in which is running.

## Orders

([The Report](persona_the_report.md) works, receives whatever slice of attention the span leaves, and is represented upward by an account they neither write nor read.)

## Implementation

([The One-to-One](place_the_one_to_one.md) where it is granted or crowded out; [attending](process_attending.md) divided by [the span](piece_the_span.md); their account travelling as [the shorter version](piece_the_shorter_version.md) through however many layers there are.)

## Targets

- [ ] the work is done and a reasonable share of attention is wanted
- [ ] the share received is fixed by a number decided above them
- [ ] under a wide span the manager cannot get to them
- [ ] under a narrow span the manager can, and their account reaches the top one compression further away
