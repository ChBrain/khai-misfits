# Position: Attender

## Taxonomy

[Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md). The seat with a finite week and more people beneath it than the week holds, choosing which cost to pay.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A week, of fixed length, already committed.
Authority to set the span, which is the same act as setting the depth.
Full understanding of the trade and no instrument that avoids it.

## Orders

Set the span.
Spend the week across the direct reports.
Read what arrives from below, which is less than what happened.

## Loses

One of two things, always: attention per person if the span is wide, or fidelity of what arrives if it is narrow.
Because the week does not lengthen and a layer is a device for delivering less, no competence at this seat converts into having both.
It ends having chosen deliberately and paid one of them.

## Drives

Toward a shape it can actually operate,
right in every single instance to acknowledge the limit,
so the seat that understands the trade best is still the one that has to make it.
