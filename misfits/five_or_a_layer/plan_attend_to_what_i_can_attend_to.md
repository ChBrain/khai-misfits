# Plan: Attend to What I Can Attend To

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the attender's seat](position_attender.md), inside [Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Attender](position_attender.md)

## Direction

Set a span you can actually hold, knowing that setting it is also setting the depth, and that whichever you choose you will pay for at the other end. Do not pretend the week can be stretched.

## Orders

([The Executive](persona_the_executive.md) fixes the number of direct reports, spends the week across them, and reads what arrives from below knowing it is less than what happened.)

## Implementation

([The Org Chart](place_the_org_chart.md), where the shape is set; the [spanning](process_spanning.md) and [attending](process_attending.md) processes running against [the finite hour](piece_the_finite_hour.md); the limit computed as [Graicunas's number](piece_graicunass_number.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the span is set deliberately rather than by accretion
- [ ] the week is divided across the direct reports at whatever slice that allows
- [ ] setting the span is understood to be the same act as setting the depth
- [ ] the cost being chosen is named, and is paid at one end or the other
