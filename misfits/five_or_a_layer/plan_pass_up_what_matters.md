# Plan: Pass Up What Matters

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the relay's seat](position_relay.md), inside [Five, or a Layer](play_five_or_a_layer.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Relay](position_relay.md)

## Direction

Supervise a span small enough to know properly, then write up what happened for somebody with less time than the material takes. Choose honestly what does not go in, because something must not go in or the layer has no purpose.

## Orders

([The Manager](persona_the_manager.md) holds real one-to-ones with a small team, gathers what they are doing, and produces an account shorter than the week it describes.)

## Implementation

([The One-to-One](place_the_one_to_one.md), where the supervision actually happens; the [summarising](process_summarising.md) process producing [the shorter version](piece_the_shorter_version.md); [the layer](piece_the_layer.md) doing exactly what a layer is for.)

## Targets

- [ ] the span is small enough that everyone on it is genuinely known
- [ ] what happened is gathered and written up honestly
- [ ] what does not fit is left out, deliberately, because that is the layer's function
- [ ] the omission is chosen by somebody who cannot know what the reader will need
