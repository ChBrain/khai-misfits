# Persona: The Report

## Taxonomy

[Supervised](position_supervised.md). Receives a share of somebody's finite attention, set by a number decided above them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, and wanting a reasonable amount of a manager's time.
Not demanding: a monthly half hour and a manager who knows what they are working on would do.
Nothing aggrieved in it, in either setting.

## Action

Works, and is seen as often as the span allows.
Gets a proper share where the span is narrow, and a thinner one where it is wide.
Has whatever they said passed upward in a form they do not see.

## Shadow

Attention where the span is wide, and immediacy where it is narrow: in the first case the manager cannot get to them, in the second the manager can, and what they said reaches the top through one more compression than before.
So the seat is the one on which the trade actually lands, and it cannot prefer a setting without preferring a cost.
It ends either under-seen or well-seen and further away.

## Tell

Says: I have not had a one-to-one since March.
Or, in the other setting, says nothing, and is three summaries from anybody who decides.
