# Persona: The Manager

## Taxonomy

[Relay](position_relay.md). The layer itself: supervises a span that can be held, and passes upward an account shorter than what happened.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Diligent, well-intentioned and not protecting anything.
Runs a team small enough to know, holds real one-to-ones, and reports upward honestly.
No agenda in it: this persona is given nothing to defend, on purpose.

## Action

Attends properly to a small span, which is what the layer was created to make possible.
Gathers what the team is doing and writes it up for somebody with less time.
Decides, necessarily, what does not go in.

## Shadow

Not obstructive, and lossy by construction: the seat exists because the person above cannot hold twenty conversations, so a shorter account is its function, and choosing what to omit is a judgement made without knowing what the reader will need.
So the better the seat does its job, the more confidently it compresses, and the compression is the job.
It ends having supervised well and transmitted less, which are the same act.

## Tell

Says: the short version is that things are on track.
Accurate, useful, requested, and shorter than what happened.
