# Persona: The Summary

## Taxonomy

[Compression](position_compression.md). The shorter account itself: faithful, requested, and necessarily missing what did not fit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A page, or a paragraph, or a line in a deck.
Written honestly, read gratefully, and shorter than the thing it describes.
Nothing false in it anywhere.

## Action

Selects. Keeps what seems to matter and drops what does not.
Travels upward and stands in for a week of work nobody above will otherwise hear about.
Is compressed again at the next layer, if there is one.

## Shadow

Not deceptive, and lossy in exactly the amount that makes it useful: a summary the same length as its source has not summarised, so the value and the loss are one quantity.
Because the omission is a judgement made by somebody who cannot know what the reader will need, the loss is not correctable by care, only by length, which is what was not available.
It ends true, shorter, and missing the thing that turns out to matter.

## Tell

Says everything it says accurately.
Says nothing about what is not in it, there being no way for an omission to announce itself.
