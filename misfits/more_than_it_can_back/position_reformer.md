# Position: Reformer

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). The seat that would lift the reserve role off any one nation, proposing a supranational asset issued by none, the costly and barely-built remedy that alone resolves the bind.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one true exit the bind allows: a reserve asset issued by no single nation, which the world can accumulate without draining any one country's backing.
A design already drawn: Keynes's bancor, put forward at the founding and passed over for the dollar, and the Special Drawing Right the IMF later built in part.
No cheap or easy path to it, because a shared reserve asks every nation to cede a piece of its monetary sovereignty and to govern the money together, the collective undertaking the world has kept declining.

## Orders

Raise a money above the nations.
Propose the reserve asset issued by none, since only an asset no single country backs lets the world's reserves grow without thinning anyone's backing, and that alone dissolves the bind.
Accept that the price is sovereignty, and know that the remedy is refused not because it fails but because nations will not surrender the control it asks, so the exit stays drawn and unbuilt.

## Loses

The adoption of the one cure, for a price the nations will not pay.
Because the supranational asset asks each country to give up a piece of its own money and govern a shared one together, the remedy that would end the bind is exactly the one the world declined when it chose the dollar over the bancor, so the seat holds a working answer that goes unused.
It keeps the only exit and loses its realisation, the one whose cure is sound and unbought.

## Drives

Toward the one who holds the true exit and cannot make it cheap,
proposing the money no nation issues against a price of sovereignty none will pay, no flaw in the cure itself,
so the seat with the one real remedy is the seat whose remedy the world keeps refusing, the bound bought only at the cost of the sovereignty it asks.
