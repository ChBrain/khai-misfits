# Piece: The Thinning Backing

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). The claims held abroad outrunning the backing behind them, so the ratio underwriting the currency's value steadily falls.

## Place

At [the Window](place_the_window.md), carried by [Diluting](process_diluting.md).
Where the foreign claims accumulated through [the Outflow](piece_the_outflow.md) are set against the gold, and later the fiscal capacity, that stands behind them.

## Load Bearing

The Thinning Backing is load bearing because it is the arithmetic core of Triffin's dilemma made visible: foreign dollar holdings grew past the U.S. gold stock through the 1960s exactly as predicted, so the more the issuer supplied to meet [the World's Thirst](piece_the_worlds_thirst.md), the thinner the backing behind each unit already abroad became (Triffin). Under Bretton Woods the backing was gold, finite and countable; under the modern fiat system, floating money, the same tension softens into the sustainability of the issuer's external position against a still growing demand for safe assets (Farhi, Gourinchas & Rey). Guard against reading the thinning as the issuer secretly debasing its money: nothing is debased on purpose; the backing thins because supplying the world's reserves requires issuing past the backing, an arithmetic of the reserve role, not a hidden act.

## Apparent

A technicality, a ratio for accountants to track, not a matter that touches the currency's daily standing in anyone's hands.
The falling ratio looks like a bookkeeping detail well behind the scenes, until someone asks what stands behind the claims and finds the answer thinner than expected.
