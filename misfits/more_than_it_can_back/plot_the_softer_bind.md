# Plot: The Softer Bind

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Council](place_the_council.md), the old gold promise set beside the fiat, floating regime that replaced it.
Nations here weigh not a broken window but a standing question of how much can safely be owed.

## Action

The bound is drawn: [the Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md), reading the modern record, holds that [the Lost Confidence](piece_the_lost_confidence.md) bites hardest under a fixed convertibility promise, a finite gold backing that can run out, and softens under fiat, floating money into a standing tension between the world's growing demand for safe assets and the sustainability of the issuer's external position.

## Stage

This is the "new Triffin dilemma" (Farhi, Gourinchas and Rey; Obstfeld): the sharp, gold-convertibility form of the bind, a promise that can be exhausted and closed as in 1971, softens under fiat money into a supply-versus-confidence tension that is real and debated, not an iron law of imminent collapse. The hedge is load bearing: the conflict is sharpest where a fixed backing can be counted and run dry, and milder, though not absent, where none is promised; it is also why the issuer of a floating reserve currency enjoys an exorbitant privilege it has no wish to relinquish.

## Tension

The bind loosens when no gold must be paid on demand, but it does not vanish, only trades a convertibility crisis for a slow question of sustainability.
