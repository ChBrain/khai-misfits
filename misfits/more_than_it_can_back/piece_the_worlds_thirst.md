# Piece: The World's Thirst

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). A growing world economy's rising need for reserves, to settle trade and to hold against shocks.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Mint](place_the_mint.md), pressed by [the World](persona_the_world.md).
Where trade expands faster than any one country's gold or output, and the shortfall is felt as a standing need for more of the reserve currency.

## Load Bearing

The World's Thirst is load bearing because it is the demand side of Triffin's dilemma: a growing world trades more, holds more against shocks, and needs reserves to do both, and only the reserve currency can supply that need at the scale required (Triffin). The thirst does not level off; it grows with the world economy itself, so it is a standing pressure on the [Reserve Asset](piece_the_reserve_asset.md) that never resolves on its own, only by more of the currency leaving the issuer through [the Outflow](piece_the_outflow.md). Guard against reading the thirst as greed or as the issuer's fault: the world's need for reserves is genuine and grows with trade, and it is what pulls the outflow, so the demand is no one's misdeed.

## Apparent

Ordinary prudence: a nation holding reserves against a bad year looks like nothing more than sound housekeeping, the same caution any prudent household would keep.
Each holder is only doing what a well run economy should, so the thirst reads as virtue, never as the force straining the one currency asked to satisfy it all.
