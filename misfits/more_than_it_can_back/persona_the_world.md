# Persona: The World

## Taxonomy

[World](position_world.md), inside [More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). The archetype of the rest of the world: it needs reserves to settle its trade and cushion its shocks, so it demands and accumulates the reserve currency, and it holds the claims that thin as they mount.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A prudent saver, sensibly holding the safest money there is. It looks like nothing but good housekeeping, a world keeping the reserves any economy needs against a rainy day, in the one currency everyone trusts.

## Action

Thirst, and hold. Its growing trade presses [the World's Thirst](piece_the_worlds_thirst.md) at [the Mint](place_the_mint.md), and on [Hold the Reserve](plan_hold_the_reserve.md) it accumulates the reserve currency the issuer sends out, holding ever more of it as its own reserves (Triffin). It takes the reserves it genuinely needs, from the only source that can supply them.

## Shadow

Its sound demand is what pulls the outflow that thins the backing. Because its need for reserves can be met only by the issuer's deficits, the very holding that keeps the world liquid is what piles up the claims against a backing that cannot keep pace, so its prudence feeds the erosion, and one day the reserves it counted safe are backed by less than it thought (Triffin). It keeps its reserves and shares no fault, the saver whose need drives the bind.

## Tell

It holds more of another's money than that money can honour, and needed every bit of it.
