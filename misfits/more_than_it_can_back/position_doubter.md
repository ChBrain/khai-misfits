# Position: Doubter

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). The seat of the holder who reads the thinning correctly and moves to convert or diversify before the others, the one in whom confidence fails, mistaken for the cause of a collapse that was already built.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A clear reading of the numbers: the claims held abroad have outrun the backing, and the promise to convert them all cannot be kept.
A sound reason to move first: while the backing lasts it can convert its own claim, and the holder who waits until last gets nothing, so converting early is self-protection and not sabotage.
No power to have prevented the thinning, because the erosion was built by the supplying long before it moved, so all it can do is read the deterioration and act on it.

## Orders

Convert before the rest.
Present your claim for the backing while the backing lasts, since the claims have outrun it and the last to move is left holding what cannot be honoured, so moving first is the sound protection of what is yours.
Know that your flight is a true reading and not a false alarm, that the backing has genuinely thinned, and that the collapse you seem to trigger was made certain by the supplying and not by your nerve.

## Loses

Its good name, blamed for a break it only saw coming.
Because it is the one who moves when confidence fails, the seat is taken for the cause of the collapse, though the cause was the reserve role's supplying past its backing and the doubter merely the first to read it, so it carries a blame that belongs to no one and least of all to the clear eye.
It keeps its converted claim and loses its innocence in the story, the messenger charged with the message.

## Drives

Toward the one who reads a real thinning and is called the one who caused it,
converting first because the backing is genuinely outrun, no panic and no malice in it,
so the seat that sees the erosion clearest is the seat the collapse is pinned on, the run of true cause and no villain.
