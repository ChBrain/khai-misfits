# Plot: To Furnish Enough

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Mint](place_the_mint.md), the World's Thirst pressing and the only channel to answer it a deficit the issuer must run.
Nothing else in the system can put more of the reserve currency into the world's hands.

## Action

[The Issuer](persona_the_issuer.md) runs [Supply the World](plan_supply_the_world.md) through [Supplying](process_supplying.md), sending [the Outflow](piece_the_outflow.md) of currency abroad by persistent balance-of-payments deficits, repaid in the privilege of borrowing cheaply in its own money, while [the World](persona_the_world.md), on [Hold the Reserve](plan_hold_the_reserve.md), accumulates what flows out.

## Stage

The only channel through which the rest of the world can accumulate the reserve currency is the issuer sending it abroad, so furnishing enough reserves requires running deficits that pile up foreign claims against the issuer (Triffin). The deficits are the mechanism of supply itself, not a nation living beyond its means or spending carelessly; the outflow is what the reserve role demands.

## Tension

To give the world the reserves it needs, the issuer must send out claims it will struggle to honour in full.
