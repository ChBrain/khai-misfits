# Place: The Council

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). Where nations meet to weigh a shared reserve asset issued by none of them. Hosts [Money No Nation Issues](plot_money_no_nation_issues.md), [The Softer Bind](plot_the_softer_bind.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A proposal on the table, reserves that could grow without draining any one country's backing, argued nation by nation.

## Holds

[The Bancor](piece_the_bancor.md), the shared asset put forward as the remedy.

## Offers

The only real exit from the bind: a money no nation issues, so the world's reserves can grow without loading the burden on one currency's backing (Keynes).

## Withheld

An easy path to it; the council can propose the asset but not compel the sovereignty it asks nations to cede, so the bind returns wherever the proposal stalls.
