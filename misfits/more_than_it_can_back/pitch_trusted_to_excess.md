# Pitch: Trusted to Excess

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that the trust which crowns a currency the world's reserve is what the world's demand for it undoes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reasonable, even admiring. A currency so trusted the whole world holds it as reserve, an issuer meeting a real need, holders keeping the safe money they require. Nothing here is reckless; the currency is strong, and that is the trouble.

## Undertow

The world can only accumulate the reserve currency if the issuer sends it out by deficits, and every unit sent out is a claim on a backing that grows slower, so the more the world is furnished the thinner the backing behind each claim (Triffin). To supply enough is to erode confidence; to hold confidence is to supply too little.

## Nerve

That a currency can be undone not by weakness but by success, by being wanted so much the world holds more of it than it can ever back. No spendthrift, no panic without cause, only a reserve role whose two jobs cannot both be done.

## Echo

Trusted enough to be everyone's reserve, and supplied past what it can back.
