# Plan: Convert Before the Rest

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the doubter's seat](position_doubter.md), inside [More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Doubter](position_doubter.md)

## Direction

Present your claim for the backing while the backing lasts, since the claims have outrun it and the last to move is left holding what cannot be honoured, so moving first is the sound protection of what is yours. Know that your flight is a true reading and not a false alarm, that the backing has genuinely thinned, and that the collapse you seem to trigger was made certain by the supplying and not by your nerve.

## Orders

([The Doubter](persona_the_doubter.md) reads [the Thinning Backing](piece_the_thinning_backing.md) at [the Window](place_the_window.md) and through [Doubting](process_doubting.md) meets [the Lost Confidence](piece_the_lost_confidence.md), converting or diversifying its claim before the others while the backing can still honour it.)

## Implementation

([Doubting](process_doubting.md) at [the Window](place_the_window.md) turns a read of [the Thinning Backing](piece_the_thinning_backing.md) into [the Lost Confidence](piece_the_lost_confidence.md), because a rational holder converts first when the claims outrun the backing, a sound response to a real erosion and not a groundless panic.)
