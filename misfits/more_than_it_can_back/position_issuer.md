# Position: Issuer

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). The seat of the reserve-currency country, which must supply the world its reserves and, in supplying enough, thins the backing on which its currency's credibility rests.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A currency the world trusts and wants to hold as its reserve, and the privilege that comes with it: to borrow from the world cheaply, in money it prints itself.
A single channel to furnish that reserve: to send its currency abroad by running persistent external deficits, the only way the world can accumulate it.
No way to supply enough without piling up claims, because every unit that meets the world's demand is a claim on a backing that grows slower, so furnishing the reserve and covering it fully cannot both be done.

## Orders

Supply the world.
Run the deficits that send your currency abroad, since the world needs your money for its reserves and only your outflow can furnish it, and take the privilege the role repays you.
Know that this supplying is the very act that thins your backing, so you cannot both furnish enough and keep confidence whole, and the bind is the reserve role's and not a failing of your management.

## Loses

Its credibility, not to any recklessness but to the reserve role itself.
Because the only channel to supply the world is to send out more of its currency than its backing can cover, the seat that meets the world's demand is the seat whose backing thins beneath the claims, so its very success as the world's reserve is what erodes confidence in it.
It keeps its privilege and loses its surety, the trusted anchor undone by being trusted past what it can back.

## Drives

Toward the one whose supplying erodes the trust it lives on,
furnishing the world the only way it can and thinning its backing by the same act, no recklessness in it,
so the seat the world crowns its reserve is the seat that must issue more than it can back, the bind a structure's and not its own.
