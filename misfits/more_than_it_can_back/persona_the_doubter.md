# Persona: The Doubter

## Taxonomy

[Doubter](position_doubter.md), inside [More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). The archetype of the holder who reads the thinning: seeing the claims outrun the backing, it moves to convert or diversify before the others, the one in whom confidence fails.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A nervous defector, easily taken for the one who broke the system by losing its nerve. It looks like the first to run, the holder whose flight triggered the collapse the others were spared until it moved.

## Action

Convert before the rest. Reading [the Thinning Backing](piece_the_thinning_backing.md) at [the Window](place_the_window.md), the Doubter meets [the Lost Confidence](piece_the_lost_confidence.md) through [Doubting](process_doubting.md), and on [Convert Before the Rest](plan_convert_before_the_rest.md) it presents its claim for the backing while the backing lasts (Triffin). It protects itself soundly against a deterioration it did not make.

## Shadow

Its flight is a true reading, not a false alarm. Because the backing really has thinned, converting first is the correct response to a real erosion, so the Doubter is not a panicker undoing a sound system but a clear eye seeing what the supplying built, and the collapse it seems to trigger was made inevitable before it moved (Triffin). It keeps its claim's value and carries the blame that belongs to no one, the messenger mistaken for the cause.

## Tell

It is called the one who ran, and it only read the numbers right.
