# Plot: Money No Nation Issues

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Council](place_the_council.md), nations gathered to weigh a reserve asset that no one of them would issue.
The proposal sits on the table beside the record of a currency asked to be both one nation's money and everyone's reserve.

## Action

[The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md), from [the reformer's seat](position_reformer.md), runs [Above the Nations](plan_above_the_nations.md) through [Reforming](process_reforming.md), proposing [the Bancor](piece_the_bancor.md), a reserve asset issued by no single nation so the world's reserves can grow without draining any one country's backing.

## Stage

Keynes's Bancor, proposed in 1944, and the IMF's Special Drawing Right that followed in 1969, mark the real exit from the bind: a reserve asset lodged on no single nation's currency, so supplying it never thins any one issuer's backing (Keynes). It is costly precisely because it asks nations to cede monetary sovereignty and govern the asset together, which is why the SDR has only marginally achieved the role.

## Tension

The one true way out asks every nation to give up a piece of its own sovereignty, so the bind endures for want of the surrender.
