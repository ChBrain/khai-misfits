# Place: The Window

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). Where a holder presents the currency against its backing, and where the backing is seen to thin and confidence to fail. Hosts [The Backing Thins](plot_the_backing_thins.md), [The Window Closes](plot_the_window_closes.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The claims held abroad, measured against the gold or the fiscal capacity behind them: a ledger of what is owed set beside a ledger of what could actually be paid.

## Holds

[The Thinning Backing](piece_the_thinning_backing.md), as the claims outrun it, and [The Lost Confidence](piece_the_lost_confidence.md) that follows once the gap is seen.

## Offers

To a holder, conversion of the claim into the backing, for as long as the backing lasts.

## Withheld

Backing enough to honour every claim presented at once; supplying the world required issuing past what the window could cover, so the window offers first-come conversion, never universal convertibility.
