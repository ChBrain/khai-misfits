# Plan: Supply the World

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the issuer's seat](position_issuer.md), inside [More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Issuer](position_issuer.md)

## Direction

Run the deficits that send your currency abroad, since the world needs your money for its reserves and only your outflow can furnish it, and take the privilege the role repays you, to borrow from the world cheaply in money you print yourself. Know that this supplying is the very act that thins your backing, so you cannot both furnish enough and keep confidence whole, and the bind is the reserve role's and not a failing of your management.

## Orders

([The Issuer](persona_the_issuer.md) issues [the Reserve Asset](piece_the_reserve_asset.md) and sends [the Outflow](piece_the_outflow.md) abroad through [Supplying](process_supplying.md) at [the Mint](place_the_mint.md), furnishing the world the reserves it thirsts for by the only channel there is, its own persistent deficits.)

## Implementation

([Supplying](process_supplying.md) at [the Mint](place_the_mint.md) meets [the World's Thirst](piece_the_worlds_thirst.md) by sending [the Outflow](piece_the_outflow.md) abroad, because the world can accumulate the reserve only if the issuer sends it out, so furnishing enough piles up claims the backing must cover and cannot.)

## Targets

- [ ] the deficits that furnish the world its reserves are run, as the only channel of supply
- [ ] the privilege of borrowing cheaply in its own money is taken as the role's repayment
- [ ] the supplying is understood to thin the backing, not as profligacy but as the reserve role
- [ ] the bind of furnishing enough while staying credible is owned as structural, not mismanagement
