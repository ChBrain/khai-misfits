# Process: Diluting

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). Borne at [The Window](place_the_window.md), precipitated by [The World](persona_the_world.md) holding the claims that accumulate, in [The Backing Thins](plot_the_backing_thins.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The claims accumulating abroad as [The World](persona_the_world.md) holds them, each one sound on its own and cumulative in its effect.

## Direction

From a currency amply backed toward one whose claims outrun the backing behind them.

## Lever

Because supplying the world requires issuing past the backing, the ratio of backing to claims falls the more the asset is supplied, so the currency's credibility erodes by the very act that furnishes it (Triffin).
