# Persona: The Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Reformer](position_reformer.md), inside [More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). The archetype of the one who would lift the reserve role off any one nation: it proposes a supranational asset issued by none, the costly remedy that alone resolves the bind.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A visionary, or a dreamer, depending on who is listening. It looks like the one holding out a clean solution the world need only adopt, a money above the nations that would settle the whole trouble at a stroke.

## Action

Propose a money no nation issues. At [the Council](place_the_council.md), the Reformer works through [Reforming](process_reforming.md) on [Above the Nations](plan_above_the_nations.md), putting forward [the Bancor](piece_the_bancor.md), a reserve asset issued by none so the world's reserves can grow without draining any one country's backing (Keynes). It names the only true exit from the bind.

## Shadow

Its clean solution asks a price the nations will not easily pay. Because a shared reserve asks each nation to cede a piece of its monetary sovereignty and to govern the money together, the remedy that would dissolve the bind is exactly the one the world declined when it chose the dollar over the bancor, and the SDR that followed has only marginally been built, so the exit stays proposed and unrealised (Keynes; the SDR). It keeps the one real cure and cannot make it cheap, the answer the world keeps refusing.

## Tell

It holds the only exit, and its price is the sovereignty no nation will give up.
