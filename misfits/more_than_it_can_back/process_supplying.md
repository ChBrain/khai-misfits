# Process: Supplying

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). Borne at [The Mint](place_the_mint.md), precipitated by [The Issuer](persona_the_issuer.md) meeting [The World's Thirst](piece_the_worlds_thirst.md), in [To Furnish Enough](plot_to_furnish_enough.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Issuer](persona_the_issuer.md), drawn on by [The World's Thirst](piece_the_worlds_thirst.md) for more reserves than it already holds.

## Direction

From a world short of reserves toward a world supplied, as the issuer runs persistent external deficits to send its currency abroad.

## Lever

The only channel through which the world can accumulate the reserve currency is the issuer sending it abroad, so meeting the thirst requires deficits that pile up claims against the issuer (Triffin).
