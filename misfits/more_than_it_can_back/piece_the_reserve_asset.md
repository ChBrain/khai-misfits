# Piece: The Reserve Asset

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). A single national currency asked to be, at once, one country's money and the whole world's store of reserves.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Mint](place_the_mint.md), issued by [the Issuer](persona_the_issuer.md).
Where a currency struck for one nation's domestic use is also handed the world's second job of being what everyone else holds.

## Load Bearing

The Reserve Asset is load bearing because it is the object in which Triffin's dilemma occurs: one currency serving two jobs at once, the issuer's own money and the reserve everyone else needs, and the two jobs pull in opposite directions from the same supply (Triffin). To be enough of the world's reserve it must be issued freely; to remain sound as one nation's money it must be issued with restraint; the currency cannot do both, and it is the single asset both demands land on. Guard against reading its trouble as weakness or mismanagement: the currency is trusted, wanted, and strong, and the bind falls on it precisely because it is trusted enough to be everyone's reserve, not because it is weak.

## Apparent

A mark of success: the world's demand to hold this currency looks like nothing but proof of its strength and stability.
The more the world wants it, the more secure it seems, so the trouble that follows from being wanted reads as no trouble at all, until the two jobs start to conflict.
