# Plan: Hold the Reserve

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the world's seat](position_world.md), inside [More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [World](position_world.md)

## Direction

Accumulate the reserve currency the issuer sends out, since your trade and your safety need reserves and this is the safest money there is and the only one you can gather in the amounts you require. Know that your sound holding is what pulls the outflow that thins the backing, so the very prudence that keeps you liquid feeds the erosion, and the reserves you count safe rest on less than they seem.

## Orders

([The World](persona_the_world.md) presses [the World's Thirst](piece_the_worlds_thirst.md) at [the Mint](place_the_mint.md) and accumulates [the Reserve Asset](piece_the_reserve_asset.md) the issuer sends out, holding ever more of it as its own reserves against its trade and its shocks.)

## Implementation

([the World's Thirst](piece_the_worlds_thirst.md) at [the Mint](place_the_mint.md) can be slaked only by [the Outflow](piece_the_outflow.md) the issuer sends, so the world's holding pulls the deficits that pile the claims onto [the Thinning Backing](piece_the_thinning_backing.md), its prudence feeding the erosion.)

## Targets

- [ ] the reserves the world's trade and safety require are accumulated, from the only source
- [ ] the reserve currency is held as the safest money and the one gatherable at scale
- [ ] the demand is understood to pull the outflow that thins the backing behind the reserves
