# Plot: The Backing Thins

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Window](place_the_window.md), where the claims held abroad are set against the gold, and later the fiscal capacity, that stands behind them.
Each claim was sound when it was issued; set beside all the others, the sum outgrows what backs it.

## Action

As [the World](persona_the_world.md) holds ever more of what [the Outflow](piece_the_outflow.md) has sent it, [Diluting](process_diluting.md) leaves [the Thinning Backing](piece_the_thinning_backing.md), the foreign claims outrunning the gold, and later the fiscal capacity, behind them.

## Stage

The ratio of backing to claims falls the more the reserve asset is supplied, so credibility erodes by the very act that furnishes it (Triffin). This is not Debt-Deflation: nothing here deflates, and the currency is wanted more than ever, not less; only its backing thins, and it thins because the currency succeeded at its second job.

## Tension

The currency is more wanted than ever and less able than ever to honour all it has promised, and both because it succeeded.
