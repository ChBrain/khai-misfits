# Piece: The Bancor

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). A supranational reserve asset issued by no single nation, the one remedy that resolves the bind rather than merely delaying it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Council](place_the_council.md), carried by [Reforming](process_reforming.md).
Where nations would meet to issue and govern a reserve asset that belongs to none of them alone.

## Load Bearing

The Bancor is load bearing because it names the structural fix to Triffin's dilemma rather than a patch on it: if reserves are issued by no single nation, the world's growing thirst can be met without draining any one country's own backing, since there is no national currency left to overload with two jobs (Keynes proposed it in 1944; the IMF's SDR, launched in 1969, is its modest heir). It stands apart from every other piece in this misfit precisely because it dissolves the bind at its source, the [Reserve Asset](piece_the_reserve_asset.md) itself, instead of managing the consequences of one nation carrying it. Guard against reading it as a ready fix on the shelf: it asks nations to cede monetary sovereignty and govern a shared asset together, a collective undertaking the SDR has only marginally achieved, so the remedy is real but dear and unbuilt.

## Apparent

An obvious, overdue upgrade, the sort of fix that looks like it should have been adopted decades ago once the trouble with a national reserve currency is understood.
Naming the solution looks like nearly the same thing as having it, when the distance between the two is a council of nations agreeing to share what each would rather keep.
