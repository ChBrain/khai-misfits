# Piece: The Outflow

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). The persistent external deficits by which the issuer sends its currency abroad.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Mint](place_the_mint.md), carried by [Supplying](process_supplying.md).
Where the issuer's balance of payments runs deficit after deficit, the currency leaving faster than it returns.

## Load Bearing

The Outflow is load bearing because it is the only channel through which [the World's Thirst](piece_the_worlds_thirst.md) can be met: for the rest of the world to accumulate reserves, the issuer must run persistent balance-of-payments deficits, sending currency out faster than it comes back (Triffin). The same channel that supplies the world's reserves is the channel that piles up foreign claims against the issuer, so every unit that satisfies the thirst also thins [the Backing](piece_the_thinning_backing.md) behind it. Guard against reading the deficits as profligacy or overspending: they are the mechanism by which the world is supplied its reserves, structurally required by the reserve role, not a sign of a nation living beyond its means.

## Apparent

Generosity, or a nation simply spending freely and letting others benefit; a deficit read the ordinary way, as a sign a country is buying more than it sells.
The steady flow of currency abroad looks like largesse or carelessness with the accounts, when it is the fixed price of supplying reserves the world has asked for.
