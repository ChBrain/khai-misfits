# Position: World

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). The seat of the rest of the world, which needs reserves and can get them only by accumulating the issuer's currency, so its sound demand pulls the outflow that thins the backing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real and growing need for reserves, to settle its trade and hold against its shocks, that rises as its economy grows.
A single source that can meet it: the reserve currency the issuer sends out, there being no better reserve to hold and no other channel to accumulate one.
No way to build its reserves without loading claims on the issuer, because every unit it holds is a claim the issuer's backing must cover, so the world's prudence and the issuer's solvency pull against each other.

## Orders

Hold the reserve.
Accumulate the reserve currency the issuer sends out, since your trade and your safety need reserves and this is the safest money there is and the only one you can gather in the amounts you require.
Know that your sound holding is what pulls the outflow that thins the backing, so the very prudence that keeps you liquid feeds the erosion, and the reserves you count safe rest on less than they seem.

## Loses

The safety it thought it was buying, thinned by the scale of its own demand.
Because its need can be met only by the issuer's deficits, the seat that holds ever more reserves is the seat whose reserves are backed by ever less, so the prudence that gathered them is what left them under-covered, and the rainy-day money is worth less than counted when the rain comes.
It keeps its reserves and loses their surety, the saver whose genuine need drives the bind that undoes its savings.

## Drives

Toward the one whose prudent need feeds the erosion of what it holds,
gathering the reserves it truly requires from the only source there is, no greed in it,
so the seat that saves most soundly is the seat whose demand thins the backing behind its own reserves, the bind a structure's and not its fault.
