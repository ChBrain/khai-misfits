# Process: Reforming

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). Borne at [The Council](place_the_council.md), precipitated by [The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) proposing the shared asset, in [Money No Nation Issues](plot_money_no_nation_issues.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md), pressing the case for a reserve asset lodged on no single nation.

## Direction

From a reserve role lodged on one nation's currency toward a supranational asset issued by none.

## Lever

Only a reserve asset no single nation issues lets the world's reserves grow without draining any one country's backing, and it is costly because it asks nations to cede sovereignty and govern it together (Keynes; the SDR).
