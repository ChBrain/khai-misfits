# Process: Doubting

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). Borne at [The Window](place_the_window.md), precipitated by [The Doubter](persona_the_doubter.md) reading the backing thin, in [The Window Closes](plot_the_window_closes.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Doubter](persona_the_doubter.md), the holder who sees the claims outrunning the backing before the rest do.

## Direction

From a held claim toward a claim converted or diversified away, ahead of the others.

## Lever

Seeing the claims outrun the backing, a rational holder converts first rather than last, and the loss of confidence is a sound reading of a real deterioration, not a groundless panic (Triffin).
