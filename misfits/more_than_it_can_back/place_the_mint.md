# Place: The Mint

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). Where the issuer creates the reserve currency and sends it abroad to meet the world's demand. Hosts [Double Duty](plot_double_duty.md), [To Furnish Enough](plot_to_furnish_enough.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A national currency issued and sent abroad to serve as everyone's reserve: the press that strikes it, the ledger that sends it out, the same money doing the issuer's own business and the world's.

## Holds

[The Reserve Asset](piece_the_reserve_asset.md) it strikes, [The World's Thirst](piece_the_worlds_thirst.md) that draws on it, and [The Outflow](piece_the_outflow.md) already under way.

## Offers

To the world, the reserves it thirsts for; to the issuer, the privilege of borrowing cheaply in its own money, because the whole world wants to hold what it issues.

## Withheld

Any way to supply the world without piling up claims against the issuer; the mint can furnish liquidity or hold its backing steady, never both at once (Triffin).
