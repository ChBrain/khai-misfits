# Plot: The Window Closes

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Window](place_the_window.md), the claims held abroad now visibly outrunning the backing behind them, and a holder watching the gap.
Conversion is still offered, first come, for as long as the backing lasts.

## Action

[The Doubter](persona_the_doubter.md), from [the doubter's seat](position_doubter.md), runs [Convert Before the Rest](plan_convert_before_the_rest.md) through [Doubting](process_doubting.md), meeting [the Lost Confidence](piece_the_lost_confidence.md) and moving to convert its claim ahead of the others, until convertibility is suspended, as the United States closed the gold window in 1971.

## Stage

Foreign dollar holdings grew past the U.S. gold stock through the 1960s exactly as Triffin had warned, and convertibility became incredible before it was formally ended. This is not The Bank Run: the backing here genuinely thinned, so converting first is a sound reading of a real deterioration, not a groundless panic on a solvent institution; the vulnerability was built by the supplying itself, not imagined by the doubters.

## Tension

No one panics without cause and the system falls all the same, because the cause was built into supplying it.
