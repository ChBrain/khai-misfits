# Piece: The Lost Confidence

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). The credibility that fails once the backing is seen to be outrun, and the rational scramble to convert or diversify that follows.

## Place

At [the Window](place_the_window.md), carried by [Doubting](process_doubting.md).
Where holders watch [the Thinning Backing](piece_the_thinning_backing.md) and decide whether the promise of convertibility still holds.

## Load Bearing

The Lost Confidence is load bearing because it is the point where Triffin's dilemma turns from arithmetic to action: once the backing is seen to be outrun, a holder who converts or diversifies first is reading a real deterioration correctly, and the run this creates is built by the supplying that preceded it, not sprung by fear alone (Triffin). It is the hinge that closed Bretton Woods in 1971, when the gap between foreign dollar claims and the U.S. gold stock became too visible to ignore. Guard against reading the loss of confidence as a groundless panic like a bank run: here the backing genuinely thins, so converting first is a sound reading of a real deterioration, not fear feeding on itself over a sound institution.

## Apparent

Nervousness, an overreaction by jumpy markets to a currency that still looks strong on the surface.
The first movers look like they are panicking ahead of the evidence, when the evidence, the thinning ratio itself, is already there for anyone who checks.
