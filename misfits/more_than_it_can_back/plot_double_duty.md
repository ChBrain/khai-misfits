# Plot: Double Duty

## Taxonomy

[More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Mint](place_the_mint.md), where a single national currency is struck for one country's own use and, in the same stroke, handed the world's second job of being what everyone else holds.
The press runs for the issuer's own economy; the ledger it feeds is read by every nation that keeps its reserves in this one money.

## Action

[The Issuer](persona_the_issuer.md), from [the issuer's seat](position_issuer.md), issues [the Reserve Asset](piece_the_reserve_asset.md), the same currency asked to serve as domestic money and as the world's store of reserves at once, while [the World](persona_the_world.md), from [the world's seat](position_world.md), presses [the World's Thirst](piece_the_worlds_thirst.md) against it, a growing economy's standing need for more of it to hold.

## Stage

Triffin's dilemma begins here, in the object itself: one currency carrying two jobs that pull in opposite directions from the same supply, the issuer's own money and the reserve everyone else needs (Triffin). The currency is not asked to do this because it is weak; it is asked because it is trusted, strong, and wanted, and the two duties land on it for that very reason.

## Tension

The very trust that makes a currency the world's reserve is what the world's demand for it will undo.
