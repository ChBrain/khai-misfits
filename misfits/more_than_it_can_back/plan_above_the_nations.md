# Plan: Above the Nations

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reformer's seat](position_reformer.md), inside [More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reformer](position_reformer.md)

## Direction

Propose the reserve asset issued by none, since only an asset no single country backs lets the world's reserves grow without thinning anyone's backing, and that alone dissolves the bind. Accept that the price is sovereignty, and know that the remedy is refused not because it fails but because nations will not surrender the control it asks, so the exit stays drawn and unbuilt.

## Orders

([The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) works through [Reforming](process_reforming.md) at [the Council](place_the_council.md), putting forward [the Bancor](piece_the_bancor.md), a reserve asset issued by no single nation so the world's reserves grow without draining any one country's backing.)

## Implementation

([Reforming](process_reforming.md) at [the Council](place_the_council.md) offers [the Bancor](piece_the_bancor.md) as the one true exit, because a reserve no nation issues lifts the role off any one currency, and it stays unbuilt because it asks nations to cede sovereignty and govern a shared money together.)

## Targets

- [ ] the reserve asset issued by no single nation is proposed as the one true exit
- [ ] the world's reserves are shown to grow, under it, without thinning anyone's backing
- [ ] the price of sovereignty is named honestly, the reason the remedy goes unadopted
- [ ] the exit is held to be sound and unbuilt, refused for its cost and not for any failing
