# Persona: The Issuer

## Taxonomy

[Issuer](position_issuer.md), inside [More Than It Can Back](play_more_than_it_can_back.md). The archetype of the reserve-currency country: it supplies the world its reserves, is repaid in a real privilege, and bears the bind that supplying enough erodes the confidence its currency depends on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The trusted anchor of the system, the strong currency everyone is glad to hold. It looks like the country doing the world a service and reaping only the honour of it, its money good as gold and wanted everywhere.

## Action

Supply the world, and be paid a privilege. Drawn on by the world's demand, the Issuer runs deficits and sends [the Outflow](piece_the_outflow.md) abroad through [Supplying](process_supplying.md) at [the Mint](place_the_mint.md), on [Supply the World](plan_supply_the_world.md), issuing [the Reserve Asset](piece_the_reserve_asset.md) and borrowing cheaply in its own money (Triffin). It furnishes the reserves the world thirsts for the only way it can.

## Shadow

Its supplying is what thins its backing. Because the only channel to furnish the world is to send its currency out, every unit that meets the demand is a claim against a backing that grows slower, so the act that makes it the world's reserve is the act that erodes confidence in it, and it cannot both supply enough and stay credible (Triffin). It keeps its privilege and owes no cause for blame, undone not by weakness but by the reserve role it was trusted with.

## Tell

Its money is wanted everywhere, and there is more of it abroad than it can back.
