# Pitch: Nobody Wrote It Down

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Engineering and matter-of-fact. A contract written down and published, a reasonable implementation of it, a defect reported in good faith, a patch prepared and reviewed, a test suite that passes.
Every step ordinary, competent, and exactly what the job asks for.

## Undertow

Under the ordinariness, a promise that was never the thing anybody was using.
People built against what the system does, and what it does includes every accident nobody documented, so the accidents are load-bearing and none of the people leaning on them knows it.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a repair that is indistinguishable, from outside, from an outage.

## Echo

Afterward: a compliant implementation that users call broken, a correct fix backed out within a day, and a standard finally written by reverse-engineering what the implementations already did.
