# Plot: The Correct One Looks Broken

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A second implementation, written strictly and carefully to the published contract, meeting content that was built for the first one.

## Action

[Matching it](process_matching_it.md): [The One Who Must Match](persona_the_one_who_must_match.md), holding [One Who Must Match](position_one_who_must_match.md), fails on real content and begins reproducing the accidents deliberately, one at a time.

## Stage

Users compare the two systems, observe that this one does not work, and are not wrong about the observation.

## Tension

Correctness is the thing that presents as a defect.
Nobody reads a specification to apportion blame between implementations, and nobody should have to: the compliant one is the broken one in every sense that reaches an actual person. So matching the accident stops being a compromise and becomes the condition of being usable at all, and the second implementer ends up bug for bug compatible on purpose with a system whose bugs were never written down anywhere.
