# Place: Where They Meet

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md). The surface between two systems, showing everything and labelling nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Calls going in and results coming back: values, orderings, timings, error text, everything the running system does.
Reproducible on every attempt, by anybody, without permission.

## Holds

[The Behaviour Nobody Specified](persona_the_behaviour_nobody_specified.md), present and identical each time.
[The Written Contract](piece_the_written_contract.md), which is not here but in a document somewhere else.

## Offers

Complete visibility of what the system actually does, which is the only thing a caller can build against.
Perfect reproducibility, which is what makes it trustworthy.

## Withheld

The line between the promise and the accident.
Nothing crossing this surface is marked as guaranteed or incidental, and no experiment conducted here can separate them, so the place shows a caller everything and tells them nothing about which parts will still be true next year: the distinction exists only in a document that is not executable and that most callers will never open.
