# Process: Building On It

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md). Dependency forming by use rather than by agreement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Somebody who needed a thing to work, wrote it, ran it, and found that it did.
[The Ones Who Depend On It](persona_the_ones_who_depend_on_it.md), at [where they meet](place_where_they_meet.md).

## Direction

Toward [the code written against it](piece_the_code_written_against_it.md), accumulating across thousands of systems.
Toward every observable behaviour being load-bearing for somebody, whatever the contract says.

## Lever

Observation, which is the only thing a caller has, and which does not distinguish promises from accidents.

## Echo

A real specification that nobody holds a copy of, discoverable only by breaking it.
