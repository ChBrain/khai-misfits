# Process: Shipping It

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md). A contract turned into something that runs.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Written Contract](piece_the_written_contract.md), a deadline, and tests covering what it states.
[The One Who Shipped It](persona_the_one_who_shipped_it.md), holding [One Who Shipped It](position_one_who_shipped_it.md).

## Direction

Toward correct implementation of every guarantee.
Toward a hundred behaviours nobody specified, because a running system cannot decline to behave in the cases the document does not mention.

## Lever

Incompleteness: every contract reserves more than it promises, and every implementation must fill the gaps somehow.

## Echo

[The Behaviour Nobody Specified](persona_the_behaviour_nobody_specified.md), present on every run, absent from every document, and indistinguishable at the surface from a guarantee.
