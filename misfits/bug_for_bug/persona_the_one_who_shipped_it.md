# Persona: The One Who Shipped It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Shipped It](position_one_who_shipped_it.md). The implementer, who had nothing to get wrong.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An engineer writing a reasonable implementation of a documented contract, on a deadline, with tests that cover what the contract says.
Competent, conscientious, and finished on Friday.

## Action

Implements the guarantees, correctly.
Produces, alongside them, a hundred behaviours nobody specified, because a running system has no way to do less.

## Shadow

Had no decision available here to make badly.
Every implementation of an incomplete contract fills the gaps somehow, and there is no version of the work that leaves the unspecified parts unobservable: so the accidents are not carelessness, they are the necessary residue of turning a document into something that runs, and the alternative on offer was not a cleaner system but no system.

## Tell

Ships exactly what was asked for, and a great deal more that nobody asked about.
