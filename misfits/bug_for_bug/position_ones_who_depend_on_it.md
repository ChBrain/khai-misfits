# Position: Ones Who Depend On It

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md). The seat that agreed to nothing and holds everything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Something that needed to work, code that was written, a run that succeeded.
No audit of which behaviours the contract guaranteed, and no way to have performed one.

## Orders

Build against what it does.
Ship, and forget which parts were promised.

## Loses

Nothing, and immobilises everything.
With enough users of an interface, all observable behaviours will be depended on by somebody whatever the contract says, and it holds without any of them consenting: so this seat cannot be accused of relying on what it was warned against, having been warned of nothing and being unable to discover the difference by any test it could have run.

## Drives

Toward a dependency formed entirely by use, held by people who could not name it if asked.
