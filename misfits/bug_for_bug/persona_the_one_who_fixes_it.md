# Persona: The One Who Fixes It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Fixes It](position_one_who_fixes_it.md). The maintainer, whose repair is the outage.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A maintainer with a genuine defect in their own code, a patch, a review and a test that demonstrates the fix.
Acting entirely within the contract they published, which reserved this behaviour to nobody.

## Action

Corrects the accident, and releases.
Watches breakage arrive from every direction within a day.

## Shadow

Cannot repair anything without changing what is observable.
Every fix is a change to behaviour and every behaviour is somebody's dependency, so the maintainer who does the responsible thing on their own code stops working software belonging to people who are not doing anything wrong and never asked for the change: being right about the contract is no help at all to the systems that are now down.

## Tell

Does exactly what a good maintainer should, and reverts it by Tuesday.
