# Place: The Issue Tracker

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md). Where the repair is agreed, shipped, and undone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A report describing the defect accurately, a discussion establishing that it is one, a patch, a review and a merge.
Then, within a day, a queue of reports saying that the release has broken things.

## Holds

[The Compatibility Mode](piece_the_compatibility_mode.md), proposed at the end as the way out.
The revert, and a comment explaining that the behaviour is now load-bearing.

## Offers

A complete, public, timestamped record of a decision that was correct at every step.
Everything a good engineering process is supposed to produce.

## Withheld

Any account of who was relying on the behaviour before it changed.
Nobody registers a dependency, so the affected parties are unknown until they are broken, and they arrive as incident reports rather than as stakeholders: the place can establish perfectly that the fix was right and cannot discover, in advance, what it will cost or to whom.
