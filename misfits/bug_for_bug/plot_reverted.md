# Plot: Reverted

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Breakage arriving from every direction within a day, from parties nobody could have identified beforehand.

## Action

[Reverting the fix](process_reverting_the_fix.md): the patch is backed out at [the issue tracker](place_the_issue_tracker.md), and a comment is added explaining that [the behaviour nobody specified](persona_the_behaviour_nobody_specified.md) is now load-bearing.

## Stage

The defect is deliberately restored, and the record shows a decision that was correct at every step and has been undone.

## Tension

The accident stops being a bug and becomes a requirement, without anybody deciding that it should.
No meeting was held on the question, no vote taken, no promise given: the status changed because enough systems came to rest on it, and the moment of change is invisible even in hindsight. What was optional is now mandatory, the document still says otherwise, and the document is now the least accurate description of the system that anybody holds.
