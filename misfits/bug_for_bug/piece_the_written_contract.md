# Piece: The Written Contract

## Taxonomy

[Where They Meet](place_where_they_meet.md). The promise, narrower than the thing it describes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In a document beside [where they meet](place_where_they_meet.md), rather than at the surface itself, and read by a small fraction of the people using it.

## Load Bearing

It is the only place the distinction between guarantee and accident exists, and it is not executable.
The contract states what will hold and reserves everything else, which is exactly the right way to write one: and since nothing at the running surface marks which behaviours it covers, the reservation is invisible to precisely the people it is meant to warn, so the document governs a set of expectations that were never formed by reading it.

## Apparent

Clear, careful, and explicit about what is guaranteed and what is an implementation detail subject to change.
Everything a specification is supposed to be.

## Yearbook

Written to make change possible later, which is its whole purpose and the reason it reserves so much.
Overtaken by use, since what people build against is the system rather than the sentence about the system.
