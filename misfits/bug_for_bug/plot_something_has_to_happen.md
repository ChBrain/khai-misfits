# Plot: Something Has to Happen

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A documented contract, a deadline, and an engineer writing a reasonable implementation of it.

## Action

[Shipping it](process_shipping_it.md): [The One Who Shipped It](persona_the_one_who_shipped_it.md), holding [One Who Shipped It](position_one_who_shipped_it.md), implements every guarantee in [the written contract](piece_the_written_contract.md) and decides something sensible in each case it does not mention.

## Stage

[The Behaviour Nobody Specified](persona_the_behaviour_nobody_specified.md) exists: a stable sort, a particular wording, an order of fields. Undocumented, unintended, not forbidden, true on every run.

## Tension

There was no decision here available to be made badly.
Every contract reserves more than it promises, and a running system cannot decline to behave in the cases the document leaves out: something has to happen, so something does. The accidents are the necessary residue of turning a specification into something that executes, and the alternative on offer was not a cleaner system but no system at all.
