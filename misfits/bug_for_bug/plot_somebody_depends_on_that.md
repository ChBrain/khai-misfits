# Plot: Somebody Depends On That

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Enough users. Each of them needed something to work, wrote it, ran it, and found that it did.

## Action

[Building on it](process_building_on_it.md): [The Ones Who Depend On It](persona_the_ones_who_depend_on_it.md), holding [Ones Who Depend On It](position_ones_who_depend_on_it.md), accumulate [the code written against it](piece_the_code_written_against_it.md) across thousands of systems.

## Stage

With a sufficient number of users of an interface, it does not matter what the contract promises: all observable behaviours will be depended on by somebody.

## Tension

The dependency forms by use, and nobody consents to it or can even name it.
Nothing at [where they meet](place_where_they_meet.md) marks which behaviours were guaranteed and which merely occurred, so no experiment a caller could run would separate them: they cannot be accused of relying on what they were warned against, having been warned of nothing. And because no dependency is registered anywhere, the real specification is now distributed across thousands of systems and held in full by nobody.
