# Persona: The Ones Who Depend On It

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Depend On It](position_ones_who_depend_on_it.md). The users, holding dependencies they never agreed to.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Thousands of people who needed something to work, wrote it, tried it, found it worked, and moved on.
Most of them have not read the contract closely, and none of them audited which behaviours it guaranteed.

## Action

Builds against what the system does.
Ships, and forgets which parts were promised.

## Shadow

Formed the dependency by use rather than by agreement.
With enough users of an interface, all observable behaviours will be depended on by somebody whatever the contract says, and that holds without anybody consenting to it: so this persona cannot be accused of relying on something it was told not to rely on, because it was never told anything, and could not have discovered the difference by any test it might have run.

## Tell

Is doing nothing wrong and is the reason nothing can change.
