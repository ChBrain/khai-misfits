# Position: One Who Fixes It

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md). The seat whose correct act is the incident.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A genuine defect in its own code, a patch, a review, and a test demonstrating the repair.
A published contract that reserved this behaviour to nobody.

## Orders

Fix your bugs.
Release.

## Loses

The distinction between repairing and breaking.
Every fix changes what is observable and every observable thing is somebody's dependency, so doing the responsible thing on its own code stops working software belonging to people who are not at fault and never asked for the change: being right about the contract does nothing whatever for the systems that are now down.

## Drives

Toward doing exactly what a good maintainer should, and backing it out by Tuesday.
