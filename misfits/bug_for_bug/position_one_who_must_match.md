# Position: One Who Must Match

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md). The seat where being right is the defect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A careful, strict implementation of the published contract.
Tests against the document, and users who have never read it.

## Orders

Follow the specification.
Work with the content that exists.

## Loses

The two orders against each other, and must abandon the first.
Real content is built for the other implementation, so a strict reading fails on it, and users apportion blame by observation rather than by document: they see that this one does not work, which is true. So correctness is what presents as a defect, and reproducing the accidents deliberately stops being a compromise and becomes the condition of being usable.

## Drives

Toward being bug for bug compatible, on purpose, with a system whose bugs were never written down.
