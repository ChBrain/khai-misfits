# Plot: The Fix Is the Outage

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The accident recognised at last as what it is: a defect, in the maintainer's own code, reserved to nobody by the contract.

## Action

[The One Who Fixes It](persona_the_one_who_fixes_it.md), holding [One Who Fixes It](position_one_who_fixes_it.md), prepares a patch at [the issue tracker](place_the_issue_tracker.md), has it reviewed, and releases.

## Stage

Working software belonging to other people stops.

## Tension

Every repair is a change to what is observable, and everything observable is somebody's dependency.
The maintainer is doing precisely what a responsible maintainer should, on their own code, within a contract that permitted it explicitly: and the people now broken are not at fault either, having relied on behaviour nobody told them not to rely on. Being right about the contract does nothing at all for the systems that are down, and there was no way to learn the cost in advance, because nobody registers what they depend on.
