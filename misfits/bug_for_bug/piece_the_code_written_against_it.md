# Piece: The Code Written Against It

## Taxonomy

[Where They Meet](place_where_they_meet.md). The accumulated dependency, invisible until it breaks.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Everywhere else: in thousands of systems that call across [where they meet](place_where_they_meet.md) and were tested against what came back.

## Load Bearing

It is the real specification, and nobody holds a copy of it.
Each piece of it was written by somebody who tried something and found that it worked, so the aggregate encodes every accident of the implementation as a requirement: and because no dependency is ever registered anywhere, this piece cannot be inspected, counted or consulted before a change, only discovered afterwards by the breakage.

## Apparent

Ordinary working code, tested, in production, doing its job.
Not reckless, not clever, and not relying on anything it was told to avoid.

## Yearbook

Grows with every new user, and each addition narrows what the implementation may ever change.
Never written down as a whole, so the constraint it imposes is known only by violating it.
