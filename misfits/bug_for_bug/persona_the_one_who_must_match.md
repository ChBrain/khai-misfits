# Persona: The One Who Must Match

## Taxonomy

[One Who Must Match](position_one_who_must_match.md). The second implementation, punished for compliance.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A competing or successor implementation, written strictly and carefully to the published contract.
Correct in every particular, and tested against the document rather than against the world.

## Action

Meets real content built for the first implementation, and fails on it.
Reproduces the accidents deliberately, one at a time, until it works.

## Shadow

Discovers that correctness is what looks like a defect.
Users do not read specifications to apportion blame: they observe that this one does not work and the other one does, and they are not wrong about the observation. So the compliant implementation is the broken one in every sense that reaches anybody, and matching the accident stops being a compromise and becomes the condition of being usable at all.

## Tell

Ends up bug for bug compatible, on purpose, with a system whose bugs nobody ever wrote down.
