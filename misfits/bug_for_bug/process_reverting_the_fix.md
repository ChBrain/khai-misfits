# Process: Reverting the Fix

## Taxonomy

[Bug for Bug](play_bug_for_bug.md). A correct repair undone, and the defect made permanent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A genuine defect, a patch, a review and a release, all correct.
[The One Who Fixes It](persona_the_one_who_fixes_it.md), holding [One Who Fixes It](position_one_who_fixes_it.md), at [the issue tracker](place_the_issue_tracker.md).

## Direction

Toward breakage arriving from every direction within a day, from parties nobody could have identified in advance.
Toward the fix being backed out and the defect deliberately restored.

## Lever

Unregistered dependency: nobody records what they rely on, so the cost of a change is unknowable until it is paid.

## Echo

An accident that has stopped being a defect and become a requirement, without anybody deciding that it should.
