# Process: Marking to the Rubric

## Taxonomy

[Marked the Same by Anyone](play_marked_the_same_by_anyone.md). The application, which settles most of the pile and not the part that matters.

## Owner

[The Marker](persona_the_marker.md), holding [Marker](position_marker.md), in [the marking room](place_the_marking_room.md).

## Direction

Toward a mark for each script.

The rubric's coverage: it settles what it named, and what it named is what somebody could anticipate.
