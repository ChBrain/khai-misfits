# Place: The Marking Room

## Taxonomy

[Marked the Same by Anyone](play_marked_the_same_by_anyone.md). Where agreement is manufactured, checked and reported, and where the ceiling is set.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Trained markers, a rubric, sample scripts marked in common, a moderation meeting, a reported agreement statistic.
Everything working: careful people, a well-built instrument, a procedure designed to be fair.

## Holds

[The Marker](persona_the_marker.md), whose judgement is worth paying for.
[The Second Marker](persona_the_second_marker.md), whose agreement is the instrument's standing.
[The Rubric](piece_the_rubric.md), which does work and names what it thought to name.

## Offers

A mark that anybody could have given, which is the only kind that can be defended to somebody with no advocate.
Real agreement, bought honestly with training and moderation.

## Withheld

Any way to keep the judgements the agreement costs.
The place is built to remove variation between markers, and the variation between competent markers **is** the hard scripts: so every increment of consistency is bought from the same pile the expertise was for, and nothing in the room distinguishes noise between markers from a real difficulty two good readers would properly disagree about.
