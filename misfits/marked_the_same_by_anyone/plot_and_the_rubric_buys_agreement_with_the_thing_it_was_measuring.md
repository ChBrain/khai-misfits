# Plot: And the Rubric Buys Agreement With the Thing It Was Measuring

## Taxonomy

[Marked the Same by Anyone](play_marked_the_same_by_anyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A well-built rubric and a week of rater training, which do work.

## Action

[The Rubric](piece_the_rubric.md) is written and the markers are trained, and usable agreement on open work is reached.

## Stage

The remedy succeeds, on the dimensions the rubric names.

## Tension

What it cannot reach is whatever nobody anticipated, at a cost that scales with the caseload.
This is a real remedy and not a straw one: rubrics and trained raters do deliver defensible agreement on extended work, which is why performance assessment exists at all. The agreement is about the axes somebody listed before the scripts arrived, so a performance excellent along an unlisted axis is not marked down but unseen; and the rater training that produced the agreement recurs every session and costs most where the benches are busiest, which is where the candidates with no advocate are.
