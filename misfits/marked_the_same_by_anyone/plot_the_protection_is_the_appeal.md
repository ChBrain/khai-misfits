# Plot: The Protection Is the Appeal

## Taxonomy

[Marked the Same by Anyone](play_marked_the_same_by_anyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two candidates, one with a school and an advocate behind them and one without.

## Action

[Hearing the appeal](process_hearing_the_appeal.md): [the candidate](persona_the_candidate.md), holding [Candidate](position_candidate.md), argues the script against the stated criteria in [the appeal](place_the_appeal.md).

## Stage

The mark can be reproduced, shown and contested without anybody knowing anybody.

## Tension

Consistency is the poorer candidate's guarantee before it is anybody's convenience, which is what makes it impossible to trade away.
An arbitrary mark is survivable by a candidate with advocates, money and a school pressing the case, and is not survivable by one without. So the property that narrows what can be assessed is the same property that stops the outcome being decided by who can complain loudest, and the person it protects is the person with the least to gain from a looser instrument and the most to lose.
