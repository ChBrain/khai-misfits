# Piece: The Ceiling

## Taxonomy

[The Marking Room](place_the_marking_room.md). The 1904 bound: an instrument cannot be more valid than it is consistent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Accepted in [setting the paper](process_setting_the_paper.md), and enforced in [the marking room](place_the_marking_room.md) by every agreement statistic.

## Load Bearing

It converts a trade-off everybody argues about into a constraint nobody can argue with.
The correlation between an instrument and the thing it is measuring is bounded by how consistently the instrument measures anything at all, so an assessment that cannot be applied twice the same way cannot be valid however well it captures what matters. That removes the move the whole debate rests on: you cannot accept lower reliability in exchange for a truer measure, because the second is capped by the first.

## Apparent

A technical note in a manual. A statistician's concern.

## Yearbook

Is a theorem rather than a policy, so no reform reaches it.
Binds hardest exactly where a judgement is worth having, since that is where consistency is lowest.
