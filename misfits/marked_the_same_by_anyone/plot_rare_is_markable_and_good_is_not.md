# Plot: Rare Is Markable and Good Is Not

## Taxonomy

[Marked the Same by Anyone](play_marked_the_same_by_anyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An answer nobody else in the cohort gave.

## Action

[The Norm Sample](persona_the_norm_sample.md), holding [Norm Sample](position_norm_sample.md), is counted against, and originality becomes a lookup two markers agree on.

## Stage

Agreement is achieved, honestly, about frequency.

## Tension

A count cannot say whether an answer is unusual because it is brilliant or unusual because it is wrong.
Both are one observation to a frequency table, and the instrument has no further resource: separating them takes the judgement that was given up in order to become countable. So the move that made originality assessable at scale is real and is a change of question, from whether an answer is good to how often it appears, and the standard has left the script and gone to live in a corpus.
