# Persona: The Norm Sample

## Taxonomy

[Norm Sample](position_norm_sample.md). The corpus a rare answer is rare against.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Thousands of previous scripts, tabulated.
A question that markers could not otherwise agree on: is this original?

## Action

Turns a judgement into a lookup.
Makes rarity countable, and therefore markable.

## Shadow

Buys the agreement honestly and moves the standard out of the script.
Two markers can both count how often an answer appears and will get the same number, which is a genuine solution to a genuine problem; and what is then being measured is the answer's relation to a corpus rather than anything about the answer, so the instrument becomes reliable by changing the question to one that has a fact for an answer.

## Tell

Solves the disagreement by measuring something else, accurately.
