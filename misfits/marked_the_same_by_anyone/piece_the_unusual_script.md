## Load Bearing

It is simultaneously the reason for expert marking and the thing that lowers the ceiling.
A script two competent markers score identically without hesitating did not need either of them, so the whole value of expert judgement sits in the scripts they come apart on, and every one of those is recorded as a disagreement and reduces what the instrument may claim. The valuable judgements and the reliability-destroying ones are not merely correlated: they are the same set.

## Apparent

A hard case. Something for the moderation meeting.
