# Plot: The Judgement Worth Having Is the One They Split On

## Taxonomy

[Marked the Same by Anyone](play_marked_the_same_by_anyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A pile of scripts, most of them settling themselves.

## Action

[Marking to the rubric](process_marking_to_the_rubric.md): [the marker](persona_the_marker.md) works down the pile and reaches [the unusual script](piece_the_unusual_script.md).

## Stage

The expertise earns its keep, and the second marker lands somewhere else.

## Tension

The valuable judgements and the reliability-destroying ones are the same set, not merely correlated.
A script two competent markers score identically without hesitating is a script that needed neither of them, so everything expert marking adds sits exactly where they come apart. Which means the instrument cannot suppress its inconsistency without suppressing its expertise, and the moderation meeting that resolves the disagreement is resolving the only part of the process anybody was paying for.
