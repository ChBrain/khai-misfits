# Persona: The Examiner

## Taxonomy

[Examiner](position_examiner.md). The one who must make a mark defensible before it is anything else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A paper to set, a cohort to rank, and a decision that will change lives.
An appeals process, a regulator, and every mark open to challenge.

## Action

Builds the instrument so anybody can apply it the same way.
Defends what comes out of it.

## Shadow

Is doing the right thing and is choosing the ceiling by doing it.
Reproducibility is what makes a mark contestable, auditable and safe for the candidate with nobody to complain for them, so no version of this seat can trade it away; and the same choice caps what the instrument is able to reach, by a bound the seat did not set and cannot lift.

## Tell

Protects the weakest candidate by narrowing what anybody can be measured on.
