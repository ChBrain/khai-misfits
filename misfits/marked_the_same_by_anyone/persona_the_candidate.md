# Persona: The Candidate

## Taxonomy

[Candidate](position_candidate.md). The one with nobody to complain on their behalf.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A mark that decides a place, a bursary, a career.
No school pressing the case, no adviser, no money for a re-mark.

## Action

Sits the paper. Takes the mark.
Appeals, if there is anything to appeal on.

## Shadow

Is protected by the very constraint that narrows what they can show.
An arbitrary mark is survivable by a candidate with advocates and is not survivable by this one, so reproducibility is their guarantee rather than an administrative taste; and it is bought by an instrument that declines to assess whatever two markers would argue about, which is a real part of what they can do.

## Tell

Is shielded and thinned by one property, and would not give it up.
