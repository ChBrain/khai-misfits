# Play: Marked the Same by Anyone

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Marked the Same by Anyone.
A mark that a second marker would not have given is not a mark anybody can defend, appeal or audit, and the candidate with no advocate is the one who needs it defensible. So the instrument is built to be applied the same way by anybody. And the judgements worth having are the ones two competent markers would split on, because a script they both score identically is a script that did not need either of them.

## Arc

The bend is this: consistency is not one virtue among several, it is the ceiling on the others.
It runs on a result from 1904.
[The Ceiling](piece_the_ceiling.md) is Spearman's: the correlation an instrument can have with the thing it is trying to measure is bounded by how consistently the instrument measures anything at all. So an assessment nobody can apply twice the same way cannot be valid, however well it captures what matters, and reliability stops being a preference to be traded off.
And consistency means a second marker.
[The Second Marker](persona_the_second_marker.md) exists in [the marking room](place_the_marking_room.md) to agree, and the whole of the instrument's standing is whether they do. This is not bureaucracy: it is the only operational form the ceiling takes.
Then [The Protection Is the Appeal](plot_the_protection_is_the_appeal.md).
[The Candidate](persona_the_candidate.md) with money, a school behind them and somebody to write letters can survive an arbitrary mark. The one without cannot, and what protects them is that the mark can be reproduced, shown and argued in [the appeal](place_the_appeal.md). Consistency is the poorer candidate's guarantee before it is anybody's convenience.
And [The Judgement Worth Having Is the One They Split On](plot_the_judgement_worth_having_is_the_one_they_split_on.md).
[The Unusual Script](piece_the_unusual_script.md) is where the marking actually costs something, and it is exactly where the two markers come apart. A script both score identically without hesitating is a script that needed neither of them.
And [The Norm Sample](persona_the_norm_sample.md) is how the gap gets closed. Originality is made markable by asking how rarely an answer appears in a reference corpus, which two markers can look up and agree on: the judgement is replaced by a count, and the standard moves out of the script and into the sample.
And a count cannot tell the unusual from the good. [Rare Is Markable and Good Is Not](plot_rare_is_markable_and_good_is_not.md): an answer nobody else gave because it is brilliant and an answer nobody else gave because it is wrong are the same observation to a frequency table, and separating them takes the judgement the instrument gave up to become reliable.

## Triggers

**[A Mark Has to Survive a Second Marker](plot_a_mark_has_to_survive_a_second_marker.md)**
Cue: a script, a mark, and somebody asking whether anybody else would have given it. Closes when agreement between markers has been located as the operational form of the instrument's standing, rather than as a procedural nicety somebody could relax.

**[Reliability Is a Ceiling and Not a Preference](plot_reliability_is_a_ceiling_and_not_a_preference.md)**
Cue: an argument that an assessment should capture what matters even if it is hard to score consistently. Closes when the 1904 result has been shown to cap the second by the first, so an instrument cannot be more valid than it is consistent and the trade-off everybody assumes is not available.

**[The Protection Is the Appeal](plot_the_protection_is_the_appeal.md)**
Cue: two candidates, one with a school and an advocate behind them and one without. Closes when reproducibility has been found to be what shields the second, so consistency is the poorer candidate's guarantee before it is anybody's convenience.

**[The Judgement Worth Having Is the One They Split On](plot_the_judgement_worth_having_is_the_one_they_split_on.md)**
Cue: a pile of scripts, most of them settling themselves. Closes when the marking that costs something has been located exactly where the markers disagree, so the valuable judgements and the reliability-destroying ones are the same set.

**[Rare Is Markable and Good Is Not](plot_rare_is_markable_and_good_is_not.md)**
Cue: an answer nobody else in the cohort gave. Closes when a frequency count has been shown unable to say whether it is unusual because it is brilliant or unusual because it is wrong, since both are one observation to a table.

**[And the Rubric Buys Agreement With the Thing It Was Measuring](plot_and_the_rubric_buys_agreement_with_the_thing_it_was_measuring.md)**
Cue: a well-built rubric and a week of rater training, which do work. Closes when the agreement has been found to be about the dimensions the rubric named, so what it cannot reach is whatever nobody anticipated, at a cost that scales with the caseload.

## Stakes

Whether an assessment can reward the thing it exists to find without becoming impossible to defend.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a bound that caps validity by consistency, an agreement between markers that is the only operational form that bound takes, a reproducibility that shields the candidate with no advocate, a judgement that is valuable exactly where it is inconsistent, and a count that makes rarity markable and cannot make quality markable, and away from any account in which an examiner was lazy or a system indifferent to what matters. The production cannot fault an examinations office for insisting a mark survive a second marker, because that insistence is what protects the person with nobody to complain on their behalf. What is left to fight over is not whether consistency matters, a theorem settles that, but what to call an instrument that can be trusted about what it can agree on and can only agree on what it did not need a judgement for.
