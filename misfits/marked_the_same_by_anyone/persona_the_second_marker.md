# Persona: The Second Marker

## Taxonomy

[Second Marker](position_second_marker.md). The one whose agreement is the instrument's standing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The same script, marked independently.
A statistic that will be reported and will decide whether the paper stands.

## Action

Marks it again, blind.
Agrees, or does not.

## Shadow

Exists to agree, and is informative only when they fail to.
Nothing about this seat is bureaucracy: it is the only operational form the bound takes, since consistency that is never checked is an assumption rather than a property. And every disagreement it records is both a real finding about a hard script and a reduction in what the instrument may claim.

## Tell

Is the measurement of the measurement, and lowers it by working.
