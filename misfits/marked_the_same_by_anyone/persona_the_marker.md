# Persona: The Marker

## Taxonomy

[Marker](position_marker.md). The one applying the instrument, script by script.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A pile of scripts, a rubric, a deadline, and a professional judgement worth having.
Most of the pile settling itself.

## Action

Reads. Applies the rubric. Awards the mark.
Flags what the rubric does not fit.

## Shadow

Is most valuable exactly where they are least reproducible.
Their expertise earns its keep on the scripts a rubric does not settle, and those are precisely the scripts on which another equally competent marker would land somewhere else, so the seat's contribution and the instrument's inconsistency are the same quantity read twice.

## Tell

Is worth paying for on the scripts that make the instrument harder to defend.
