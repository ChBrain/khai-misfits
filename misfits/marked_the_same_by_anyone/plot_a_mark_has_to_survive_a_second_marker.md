# Plot: A Mark Has to Survive a Second Marker

## Taxonomy

[Marked the Same by Anyone](play_marked_the_same_by_anyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A script, a mark, and somebody asking whether anybody else would have given it.

## Action

[Checking the second marker](process_checking_the_second_marker.md): the same script is marked blind in [the marking room](place_the_marking_room.md) and the agreement is reported.

## Stage

The instrument's standing is the statistic that comes out.

## Tension

Agreement between markers is the only operational form the bound takes, so this is not procedure anybody could relax.
Consistency that is never checked is an assumption rather than a property, which is why a claim about an instrument has to be a claim about what two independent applications of it produce. And the check is honest in both directions: every disagreement it records is a true finding about a genuinely hard script and a reduction in what the instrument may claim about anything.
