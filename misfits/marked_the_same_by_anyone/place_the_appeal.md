# Place: The Appeal

## Taxonomy

[Marked the Same by Anyone](play_marked_the_same_by_anyone.md). Where the constraint pays off, and shows what it was bought with.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A candidate asking for a mark to be reconsidered, against stated criteria, in writing.
No favouritism anywhere: a stated standard, a reproducible mark, and a process that does not need anybody to know anybody.

## Holds

[The Candidate](persona_the_candidate.md), with nobody pressing the case but themselves.
[The Rubric](piece_the_rubric.md), which is the whole of what can be argued about.
[The Examiner](persona_the_examiner.md), who must be able to show why the mark is the mark.

## Offers

The protection: a decision that can be reproduced, shown and contested without an advocate, which is the thing money and connections would otherwise buy.

## Withheld

Any ground to stand on outside the criteria.
The place can only hear an argument that the script met a stated standard, which is exactly why it is safe for somebody with no advocate, and it means a script that was good in a way the criteria did not name has nothing to appeal with: the protection is real, is worth having, and is available only in the range the instrument agreed to measure.
