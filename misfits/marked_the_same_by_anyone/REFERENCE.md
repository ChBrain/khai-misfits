# Marked the Same by Anyone: Reference

The research warrant for Marked the Same by Anyone.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the attenuation bound of classical test theory and on what
inter-rater agreement costs the thing an assessment was built to find, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging assessment as **a protection and a narrowing that arrive in one property**: a mark that a second marker
would not have given cannot be defended, appealed or audited, and the candidate who needs it defensible is the
one with no school, no adviser and nobody to write letters. So an instrument is built to be applied the same
way by anybody, and that is right. It is also a ceiling: the correlation an instrument can have with the thing
it is trying to measure is bounded by how consistently it measures anything at all, so an assessment nobody can
apply twice alike cannot be valid however well it captures what matters. And the judgements worth paying for
are the ones two competent markers come apart on, because a script they both score identically without
hesitating did not need either of them. The play models the documented mechanics, the bound that caps validity
by consistency, the second marker whose agreement is the instrument's standing, the reproducibility that
shields the candidate with no advocate, the script that is valuable exactly where it is inconsistent, the
corpus that makes rarity countable, and the rubric that agrees about what somebody thought to name, as the
load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of an examinations office preferring what is easy to
count.

The governing law is that consistency is not one virtue among several but the ceiling on the others. That
removes the move every argument about assessment rests on: nobody can accept a looser instrument in exchange
for a truer measure, because the second quantity is bounded by the first.

**And this play declares no axis, which is a correction rather than an omission.** It carried
`marker-agreement` at a negative sign, and the sign was wrong: agreement is not a dial with a bad end and a
good one. Raise it and the instrument narrows to what everybody can score alike, which suppresses the
expertise it was paying for. Lower it and the mark becomes one an advocate can contest, which is survivable
by a candidate with a school behind them and not by one without. **Both ends are harmful and in different
currencies**, so there is no single good quantity to move in one direction, and a one-dial-two-bad-ends
structure has no sign to declare. The sign carried no comment for exactly that reason: nobody could name
what was supposed to be rising, because two things were and they were not the same thing.

**The valuable judgements and the reliability-destroying ones are not merely correlated: they are the same
set.** Everything expert marking adds sits on the scripts two competent markers would score differently, so an
instrument cannot suppress its inconsistency without suppressing its expertise, and the moderation meeting that
settles the disagreement is settling the only part of the process anybody was paying for.

What removes any possibility of blame from the examiner is that reproducibility is the poorer candidate's
guarantee. An arbitrary mark is survivable by somebody with advocates, money and a school pressing the case,
and is not survivable by somebody without, so the property that narrows what can be assessed is the same
property that stops the outcome being decided by who can complain loudest. No version of that seat can trade it
away, and the person it protects has the most to lose from a looser instrument.

What removes it from the marker is that they are most valuable where they are least reproducible. The
expertise earns its keep on the scripts the rubric does not settle, which are precisely the scripts on which
another equally competent marker would land somewhere else, so the contribution and the inconsistency are one
quantity read twice.

And the way the gap actually gets closed is a change of question rather than a failure. Originality is made
markable by asking how rarely an answer appears in a reference corpus, which two markers can look up and agree
on: real agreement, honestly bought, about frequency. A count then cannot say whether an answer is unusual
because it is brilliant or unusual because it is wrong, since both are one observation to a table, and
separating them takes the judgement that was given up in order to become countable.

It is distinct from the house's **Right About the Big One**, its nearest sibling, where a proper scoring rule
stops rewarding honesty once the evaluation is conditioned on extreme outcomes: both stage a scoring
instrument whose guarantee carries a condition nobody carries with it, and the conditions differ. There the
rule is intact and the **sample** breaks it, so the repair is a different rule chosen before the outcome.
Here the sample is not the problem and the **requirement of agreement** is what binds, so no choice of rule
escapes it: the constraint is on any instrument two people must apply alike, and it holds before a single
script is scored.

It is distinct from the house's **Metric Fixation**, where a measure becomes a target and is gamed until it
stops measuring: there somebody optimises against a proxy under pressure and the remedy is to relieve the
pressure. Here nothing is gamed and there is no pressure to relieve. The binding constraint is a theorem about
what an instrument can correlate with, and it would hold in a system where no candidate had any interest in
the result at all.

It is distinct from the house's **The Straight Rows**, where a state simplifies what it governs into a legible
form and the practical local knowledge is lost with the detail: the shape is close and the beneficiary is
not. There the simplification serves the **administrator**, who gains a governable map while the governed lose
their metis. Here it serves the **assessed**, and specifically the one with no advocate, so the party who
benefits from the narrowing is the party it narrows, and there is nobody standing outside the trade collecting
on it.

It is distinct from the house's **The Proxy**, where a group statistic stands in for an individual and becomes
self-fulfilling: scoring originality by frequency against a norm sample is formally a group statistic doing an
individual's work, and the difference is what it does next. There the proxy **feeds back**, shaping the
behaviour it predicts. Here it does not: a frequency table changes nothing about the script it scores, and the
loss is confined to a distinction the count cannot draw.

It is distinct from the house's **Missing from the Count**, where a population is measurably undercounted and
the correction is forbidden: there the quantity **can** be measured and the repair is barred by a rule
somebody wrote. Here nothing forbids anything, and the difficulty is that the quantity cannot be measured
consistently by two people, which no permission would change.

## Origin

The bound, the property it excludes, how the exclusion was worked around, what validity was later held to
include, and the argument that is still open.

| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Charles Spearman**                   | "The Proof and Measurement of Association between Two Things" (American Journal of Psychology, 1904)                     | **The spine**: the correction for attenuation, which bounds the correlation between two measures by their reliabilities, so an instrument cannot correlate with what it is measuring beyond what its own consistency allows.            |
| **J. P. Guilford**                     | "Creativity" (American Psychologist, 1950), the presidential address to the American Psychological Association           | **The property left outside**: divergent production named as a capacity the tests then in use did not reach, which is the account of what the ceiling excludes, given by the field to itself at the moment it noticed.                  |

Paul Torrance**                   | the Torrance Tests of Creative Thinking and their scoring manuals, from 1966                                             | **The workaround, and what it costs**: originality operationalised as statistical infrequency against a norm sample, alongside fluency, flexibility and elaboration, which makes the judgement countable by replacing it with a lookup. |
| **Samuel Messick**                     | "Validity of Psychological Assessment" (American Psychologist, 1995)                                                     | **What validity was held to include**: validity as one unified judgement covering the meaning and the social consequences of a score's use, which is what puts appealability inside the instrument rather than beside it.               |
| **The constructed-response record**    | inter-rater reliability procedures in large-scale assessment, and the standing preference for selected-response formats  | **The practice, in the field's own words**: open-response formats favoured by test constructors and teachers on grounds of ecological validity, and restricted in standardised testing because of rater variation.                      |

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the examinations
  office as preferring what is easy to count, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: a
  mark that cannot be reproduced cannot be defended to the candidate who most needs it defensible; the bound
  from consistency to validity is a result rather than a policy, so no reform reaches it; the second marker is
  not procedure for its own sake, since consistency never checked is an assumption rather than a property; the
  marker is most valuable exactly where they are least reproducible; and scoring by frequency is real
  agreement, honestly bought, about a real quantity. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the
  structure, so the misfit is that the property protecting the candidate with no advocate is the property that
  narrows what any candidate can be measured on.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Rubrics and trained raters
  are the real remedy, do reach defensible agreement on extended work, and are why performance assessment
  exists at all: what they agree about is the axes somebody listed before the scripts arrived, so a
  performance excellent along an unlisted axis is not marked down but unseen, and the training that produces
  the agreement recurs every session and costs most where the benches are busiest, which is where the
  candidates with no advocate are. Accepting a looser instrument to capture more is the trade the attenuation
  bound will not deliver. Scoring originality by frequency works and answers a different question. Removing
  the appeal would free the instrument and remove the protection it was built for. And knowing the mechanism
  completely leaves the next paper needing to be markable by two people.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the attenuation result, the naming of divergent production, the
  infrequency scoring, the unified account of validity and the reliability practice are theirs. The bounds are
  carried with them: the attenuation bound is a result **within classical test theory** and under its
  assumptions, and later measurement frameworks treat reliability differently, so the play stages the
  constraint as that theory states it and not as a claim about every possible instrument; **how far trained
  raters and well-designed rubrics close the gap is genuinely disputed** and varies by task, by scale and by
  scoring design, so the play stages the direction and never a rate; the treatment of originality as
  infrequency is one operationalisation among several and is cited for what making a judgement countable
  requires rather than as the last word on assessing creativity; and treating assessment as one instrument
  with one marking room is the play's own simplification of a practice with many forms. **The play makes no
  claim that examinations measure nothing worth measuring, or that any assessment system was badly designed**,
  only that validity is capped by consistency, that consistency means agreement between markers, and that the
  scripts markers agree about are the ones that did not need them.
