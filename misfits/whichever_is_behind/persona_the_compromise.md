# Persona: The Compromise

## Taxonomy

[Settlement](position_settlement.md). The paper proposing a standing split, tabled at every meeting and carried at none.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Four pages, well written, the same four pages each year with the figures refreshed.
Sixty of the capital programme to development and forty to condition, held for three years.
Universally described as sensible.

## Action

Is tabled.
Is discussed with respect.
Is not moved to a vote, and is carried forward to the next meeting.

## Shadow

That nothing is wrong with it and it still cannot pass.
Adopting it requires the organisation to state a rate of exchange between a family housed and a
home kept dry, in a minute, in public, and that sentence has no author: not the officers, who have
no basis for it, and not the members, who would have to defend it to whichever side it went against.

## Tell

Every person in the room believes it is roughly right.
No person in the room can say why sixty rather than fifty-five.
