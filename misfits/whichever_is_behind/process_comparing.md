# Process: Comparing

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). The table at the back of the pack being assembled: this authority against six like it, and against itself last year.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The quarterly return, and the survey cycle.
At [the committee room](place_the_committee_room.md), a week before anybody reads it.

## Direction

Toward one comparable question out of two incomparable goods.
It is not an evaluation and does not claim to be: it reports position, not worth, and everybody
using it knows that.
So this is not a measurement error or a proxy going wrong: the comparison is accurate about the
thing it measures, and the thing it measures is who is behind.

## Lever

Peer data, which exists, is published and is not disputed.
The question the play asks is not whether the comparison is right but what an organisation does
when it is the only thing in the pack that answers without argument.

## Echo

An arrow beside one of two rows,
which nobody voted for,
and which has settled what this year is about.
