# Process: Turning

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). The organisation reversing between its two goals: one programme stood down, the other stood up.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Releasing](process_releasing.md), and the next arrow.
Across [the site](place_the_site.md) and [the estate](place_the_estate.md), over about two quarters.

## Direction

Toward the corner, and never toward the middle.
Each turn is a correct response to a real shortfall, and the sequence of them is a policy nobody
proposed, because the sequence has never been an item.
There is no meeting at which the organisation decided to alternate.

## Lever

The capital programme, which is one pot and can be pointed at one thing.
The question is not whether each turn was justified, because each was, but what the turns cost
that neither destination costs.

## Echo

A contractor released and re-procured,
a design team dispersed and rebuilt,
a site held and insured through a year in which nothing was done on it.
