# Persona: The Agenda

## Taxonomy

[Attention](position_attention.md). One monthly meeting, and one line on it for a decision of substance.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ten items, of which eight are noting and one is a report back.
The remaining line carries whatever the organisation is actually going to do this year.
Assembled by officers from what has come up the channels, and circulated a week ahead.

## Action

Admits one thing.
Carries it until it is settled.
Admits the next thing.

## Shadow

That the constraint is not a shortage of time but the shape of deciding.
Lengthen the meeting, add a second committee, and each new body carries one thing of substance
in its turn, so what looks like a scheduling problem reproduces itself at whatever size it is given.

## Tell

Nobody has ever tabled an item called which of our two goals we are on.
It would not be a decision. It would be the thing the decisions have been doing.
