# Whichever Is Behind: Reference

The research warrant for [Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md).
The setting is a housing authority holding two standing goals, development and condition; the play runs on the
fact that an organisation which cannot weigh its goals attends to whichever is below its threshold, and that the
trigger to stop attending is therefore success, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging conflicting organisational goals as **a sequence that is right at every step and is at no step the
policy**: two goals are owed to two different sets of people and there is no rate of exchange between them, so
the organisation treats each as a constraint and attends to whichever is behind, which is cheaper than bargaining
and genuinely responsive; the threshold that decides which is behind is a comparison with peers and with last
year rather than anybody's ambition; attention is singular and is distributed by the channels that raise issues,
so the order is not a preference and survives a change of personnel; the shortfall that pulls attention in means
the absence of one pushes it out, so a goal is released at the moment it stops being behind rather than the
moment it is safe; the realised policy is therefore a sequence of corners while the interior split sits on the
table unadopted, because adopting it means minuting a price for one goal in the other; and the reversals cost
what neither destination costs. The play models the two owed goals, the singular agenda, the comparison that
ranks by shortfall, the release triggered by success, the split that cannot be signed, and the bill for turning
round, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of weak officers or a capital programme that
is merely too small.

The governing law is that goals are satisfied in an order set by shortfall rather than weighed against one
another, so an organisation with two of them occupies each corner in turn and the middle never.

It is distinct from the house's **Fixes that Fail**, whose engine is a symptomatic fix whose relief is bought
with a delayed side-effect that returns the problem worse. Remove that engine, by stipulating that every
programme here does exactly what it promises with no side-effect whatever, and this is untouched, because
**nothing in it degrades as a consequence of a fix**: the harm is that no interior point is ever occupied, and it
would stand if every development and every repair were perfect.

It is distinct from the house's **Accidental Adversaries**, where two parties each take locally sensible action
that undermines the other and each reads the response as hostility. Remove that engine, by stipulating perfect
goodwill and correct attribution between the two constituencies, and this stands entire, because **the
alternation is performed by one actor**: neither constituency acts on the other at all, and neither needs to
misread anything for the sequence to run.

It is distinct from the house's **The Cobweb Theorem**, whose quantity is the lag between a price signal and the
supply that answers it. Stipulate zero lag, with every response instantaneous, and this is unchanged, because
the switch fires the month the threshold is crossed and **nothing here is being forecast**.

It is distinct from the house's **The Bullwhip Effect**, whose quantity is amplification of a signal along a
chain of stages. Stipulate no amplification at any stage, and this is unchanged, because **there is no chain**:
one body reverses between two destinations, and the oscillation is not passed to anybody.

It is distinct from the house's **Priority Inversion**, where a low-priority holder of a shared resource blocks a
high-priority task. Stipulate no shared resource and nothing held, and this runs as written, because **nothing
here is blocked**: attention is reallocated by a rule that is working correctly, and the goal that is not
attended to is not waiting on the goal that is.

It is distinct from the house's **Coup-Proofing**, which stages a standing trade-off between loyalty and
effectiveness. Set its quantity to zero, stipulating no risk of a coup at all, and this is untouched. The sharper
line is the other way round: coup-proofing **settles**, at a point on a frontier, and stays there. This never
settles, and the whole cost is in the not settling.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the officers as
  weak, the members as short-termist, or either constituency as unreasonable, because nothing in the sequence has
  a decision to get wrong: each item was decided correctly on the paper in front of it and would be decided the
  same way again; the comparison table is accurate about the thing it measures; closing an item because it
  succeeded is right at every meeting where it happened; and the order in which issues reach the room is a
  property of the channels, so a chief executive with opposite views inherits the same sequence. The failure is
  authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a rule ranking goals by shortfall
  cannot occupy the middle.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Weighing the goals requires a
  rate of exchange between a family housed and a home kept dry, which nobody holds and no constituency has
  signed. Fixing the split removes the responsiveness that made attending to the shortfall sensible, so a real
  emergency in one goal cannot pull anything toward it, and the fixed ratio is itself a weighing nobody signed.
  Adding attention reproduces the sequence inside each new body, because attention is singular wherever it is
  exercised, and it adds a coordination cost. Lengthening the aspiration slows the switching without removing the
  switch, so each goal is neglected for longer before it is behind enough to be seen.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the quasi-resolution of conflict and the treatment of goals as
  constraints satisfied in sequence are Cyert and March's, the measured account of sequential attention to size
  and performance goals is Greve's, the study of two conflicting goals under shortfall is Gaba and Greve's, the
  structural distribution of limited attention is Ocasio's, and the incremental sequence held against this one is
  Lindblom's. The bounds are carried with them, and two are owed plainly. **None of the cited works was read in
  full**, each being behind publisher access from this environment, so each is cited for the question it
  addresses and its findings as corroborated from several independent reports, and **no numerical parameter
  appears anywhere in this production that those reports did not state**: the play carries no figure for the cost
  of a reversal, for a split, or for any threshold, and does not need one. And the second bound is a boundary
  between what is measured and what is drawn: the literature establishes that goals are attended by shortfall and
  released on satisfaction. **That the realised policy is therefore a sequence of corners, that the release
  schedules the next shortfall, and that the alternation costs more than either destination, are the play's
  readings of that mechanism and are not reported findings.** They follow from the trigger's shape rather than
  from a measurement, and they are marked as the house's throughout: in [the cost of turning
  round](piece_the_cost_of_turning_round.md), in [satisfied is silent](piece_satisfied_is_silent.md), and here.
  The setting is the house's own extension too: the cited studies are of firms, insurers and airlines, and a
  housing authority is where the play puts the mechanism, not where anybody measured it.
