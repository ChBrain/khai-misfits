# Process: Planning

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). What the people downstream of the programme try to do, and what the sequence does to it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A published forward programme, believed.
At [the estate](place_the_estate.md) and [the site](place_the_site.md), by everybody who has to commit before the next arrow.

## Direction

Toward commitments that outlast an agenda item.
A contractor prices a three-year framework, a household two years off an offer arranges a life
around it, a tenant is told the year their block is due.
Each of them is planning against a policy that is not a policy but a position, and nobody has
misled them, because the forward programme was true when it was published.

## Lever

The forward plan, which is honest and is superseded.
The question is not whether anybody lied but what a stated intention is worth when the thing that
supersedes it is a threshold rather than a decision.

## Echo

A framework priced for continuity and delivered in stops,
an offer that recedes,
and a repair date that moves, once, without a letter.
