# Piece: Constraints, Not Weights

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). How the organisation actually treats its two goals: as thresholds to be satisfied in order, and not as quantities to be traded off.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the behavioural account of the firm, as the quasi-resolution of conflict.
Carried, without being named, by everybody at [the committee room](place_the_committee_room.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a complaint about indecisive management.
It is load bearing because it makes the sequence **the honest move**: an organisation that cannot
price one goal in the other can still tell whether a goal is met, so treating each as a constraint
and satisfying it in turn is what remains available, and it is cheaper than bargaining and less
destructive than a fight over worth.

## Apparent

That this is a failure to prioritise.
It is a way of proceeding without a priority, which is not the same thing: a priority ranks goals
by worth and this ranks them by shortfall, and the second is the only ranking the organisation
has the information to make.

## Yearbook

Described in organisation theory in the nineteen sixties and measured since.
It is the finding the play stages, not a diagnosis the play brings.
