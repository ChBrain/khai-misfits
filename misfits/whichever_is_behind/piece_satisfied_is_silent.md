# Piece: Satisfied Is Silent

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). The inversion: attention is released by success, so a goal stops being watched at the moment it stops being behind.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [releasing](process_releasing.md), in a sentence that reads as good news.
Structurally, in the same rule that made the attention right in the first place.

## Load Bearing

Without it the sequence would be merely slow and the play would have no bend.
It is load bearing because the trigger runs **both ways**: a shortfall pulls attention in, so the
absence of a shortfall pushes it out, and there is no state in which a goal is met and still watched.
The goal that is doing well is therefore the goal that is being left, and it is left in the
condition that produces the next shortfall.

## Apparent

That somebody took their eye off it.
The item was closed because it succeeded, and closing a succeeded item is correct at every meeting
where it happened.

## Yearbook

The plain consequence of goals attended by shortfall, drawn out here rather than reported as a finding.
The literature establishes the trigger; the play states what a trigger of that shape does to the goal it releases.
