## Tension

Here the selection rule is put in the open, and it has to be shown to be **right**.
The organisation cannot weigh its goals, because weighing needs a rate of exchange between a family
housed and a home kept dry and nobody has one. What it can do is ask whether each goal is met, and
that question the peer table answers without argument.
So goals are treated as constraints satisfied in an order of priority rather than as quantities
traded off, which is cheaper than bargaining, spills less blood than a fight about worth, and is
genuinely responsive: whichever thing is actually going wrong is what gets the room.
**The rule ranks by shortfall and not by worth**, and that is not a defect in it. It is the only
ranking the organisation has the information to make.
