# Persona: The Waiting List

## Taxonomy

[Development](position_development.md). The people who have no home from this authority yet, counted monthly and compared with the neighbours.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A number that is always slightly worse than anybody would like and never catastrophic.
Behind it, households, each with a date on which they joined and no date on which they leave.
The one figure a councillor can be asked about in the street.

## Action

Grows.
Is counted, published and set against the peer authorities.
Crosses a line, and becomes the thing the committee is for.

## Shadow

That its claim on the agenda is a claim about being behind and not about being owed.
It is attended to when it is worse than the comparison, released when it is better, and the release
arrives long before anybody on it is housed.

## Tell

Everyone can say what the number is.
Nobody can say what it should be, except by naming another authority.
