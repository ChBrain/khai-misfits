# Persona: The Stock

## Taxonomy

[Condition](position_condition.md). The homes the authority already has, and the people already living in them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Twelve thousand roofs, and the slow arithmetic under them.
Surveyed on a cycle, scored against a standard, reported as a percentage that moves by less than a point a year.
The goal that never has an emergency until it does.

## Action

Ages.
Absorbs a deferred programme without complaint, for a while.
Arrives at the top of the agenda in exactly the state that three years of not being at the top produces.

## Shadow

That it is the goal best able to wait, which is what guarantees it will be asked to.
Nothing about a roof announces itself on the month it stops being fine, so the shortfall that
finally pulls the agenda across is one that has been accumulating since the last time it was here.

## Tell

The survey is always a little out of date.
Nobody has ever proposed doing it more often, because nothing would be done with the answer.
