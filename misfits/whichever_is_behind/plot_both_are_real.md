# Plot: Both Are Real

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two rows in the same pack: a waiting list, and a stock condition percentage.
One capital programme, and one line on [the agenda](persona_the_agenda.md).

## Action

[Attending](process_attending.md): [The Agenda](persona_the_agenda.md), in [the attention seat](position_attention.md), takes up one item at [the committee room](place_the_committee_room.md) on [Take One Thing at a Time](plan_take_one_thing_at_a_time.md).

## Stage

[The Waiting List](persona_the_waiting_list.md), on [Get Ahead of the List](plan_get_ahead_of_the_list.md), and [The Stock](persona_the_stock.md), on [Keep the Roof On](plan_keep_the_roof_on.md), each owed to a different set of people.

## Tension

The conflict has to be established as genuine before anything else is worth saying, because the
easy reading of this play is that somebody is being weak.
Nobody is. Both goals are owed. The people on the list have no home from this authority and the
people in the stock have one that is getting older, and there is no version of the argument in
which either side is asking for too much.
And the two cannot be advanced with the same money or decided in the same item, so the organisation
is not choosing between a good and a bad thing. **It is holding two goods with one hand.**
The play does not ask anybody to care less about either, and it would run identically on a larger
capital programme, because what is scarce in it is not money but the capacity to decide differently.
