# Process: Releasing

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). Attention leaving a goal, at the moment the goal clears its threshold.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The arrow turning, from [comparing](process_comparing.md).
At [the committee room](place_the_committee_room.md), in a sentence that reads as good news.

## Direction

Toward the other goal, which is now the one behind.
Nothing is abandoned in anybody's intention: the item is closed because it succeeded, and closing
a succeeded item is correct.
So this is not neglect as a failing. It is the same rule that made the attention right, running
in the direction nobody watches.

## Lever

Success, which is the trigger.
The play's question is what it means that a goal stops being watched when it stops being behind,
rather than when it is safe.

## Echo

A line struck from the forward plan,
a team redeployed to the goal that is now urgent,
and a standard that begins, quietly, to fall from the level that ended the attention.
