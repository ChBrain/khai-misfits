# Piece: The Order Nobody Chose

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). The sequence as a property of the organisation's channels rather than of anybody's preference in the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In how issues reach [the agenda](persona_the_agenda.md): the returns, the survey cycle, the peer table.
In the attention-based account of the firm, as the structural distribution of attention.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play has a villain, and the villain is whoever chairs the committee.
It is load bearing because it **survives a change of personnel**: the order is set by what the
channels put in front of the room, so a chief executive with opposite views inherits the same
sequence and is right to, and there is no seat from which a different answer was available.

## Apparent

That leadership could simply hold the line.
Holding the line means declining to attend to a goal that is measurably behind, which is not
leadership but a refusal, and it would be reversed by the first constituency to notice.

## Yearbook

Attention as a scarce resource distributed by structure is the attention-based view's claim.
The play uses it to remove the last candidate for blame, which is what the house standard requires of it.
