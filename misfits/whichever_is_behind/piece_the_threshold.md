# Piece: The Threshold

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). The aspiration level: the line a goal is above or below, set by the peers and by last year rather than by anybody's ambition.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the comparison](persona_the_comparison.md), in the table at the back of the pack.
In the literature, as the aspiration level against which performance is read.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is nothing to make one goal urgent rather than the other, and the play has no engine.
It is load bearing because it is **not a target**: it moves as the neighbours move and as last
year recedes, so a goal can become behind without anything about it changing, and can stop being
behind without becoming safe.

## Apparent

That somebody chose it.
Nobody did. It is assembled from published returns by officers doing exactly what they should,
which is why the play has no author to blame for the year the arrow points at.
