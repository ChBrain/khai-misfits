# Persona: The Comparison

## Taxonomy

[Aspiration](position_aspiration.md). The peer figure and last year's figure, which between them say which goal is behind.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A table at the back of the pack: this authority, six like it, and the same six last year.
Two rows that matter, the list and the standard, each with an arrow.
Nobody's opinion, and the only thing in the pack that answers a question without being argued.

## Action

Ranks.
Names one goal as behind.
Moves, quietly, as the neighbours move.

## Shadow

That it decides the year without being a decision.
It converts two incomparable goods into one comparable question, which is the only reason the
organisation can act at all, and it does so by discarding exactly the thing a compromise would need:
how much of one is worth how much of the other.

## Tell

It is never the item.
It is the page everybody turns to first.
