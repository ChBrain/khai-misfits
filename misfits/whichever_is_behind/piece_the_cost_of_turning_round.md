## Load Bearing

Without it the alternation is merely a different route to the same place and the play is about taste.
It is load bearing because it makes the sequence **worse than either policy it alternates between**,
and worse than the split: continuity has a value that is spent at every turn, and it is spent by a
decision nobody took, since the alternation has never been an item.

## Apparent

sum has no owner because it is not a decision but a shape.
