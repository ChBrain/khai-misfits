## Tension

Here the rule turns over, and it turns over by running in the direction nobody watches.
A shortfall pulls attention in. Therefore the absence of a shortfall pushes it out. **There is no
state in which a goal is met and still attended to**, so the trigger to stop is success and not safety.
The list leaves the agenda the month it stops being worse than the neighbours, which is long before
anybody on it is housed, and it leaves by a decision that reads as a closure rather than an abandonment.
And the goal that has been quietly waiting does not arrive fresh. It arrives with everything that
accumulated while it was above the line, because being above the line is precisely what took it
off the agenda. **The years of doing well are the years of not being looked at**, and that is what
schedules the next emergency.
