# Place: The Committee Room

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). Where the year is settled: one meeting, one pack, one line for a decision of substance.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Ten items, eight of them noting, a report back, and the one that matters.
Officers who have prepared honestly, members who have read the pack, and a comparison table at the
back that nobody argues with.

## Holds

The whole of the organisation's capacity to change what it is doing.
Not its capacity to act, which is large, but its capacity to decide to act differently, which is
one item at a time and is the constraint the play runs on.

## Offers

A correct decision, every time, on the paper in front of it.

## Withheld

Its own sequence.
No pack has ever contained an item on what the last four years have added up to, because that is
not a decision anybody can take: it is the shape the decisions have made, and it is visible only
from outside the room.
