# Process: Attending

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). The agenda taking up the goal that is behind, and carrying it until it is settled.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The arrow, from [comparing](process_comparing.md).
At [the committee room](place_the_committee_room.md), monthly, for as long as the shortfall lasts.

## Direction

Toward satisfying one goal, treated as a constraint rather than as a weight.
This is the cheap and honest move: an organisation that cannot price one goal in the other can
still tell whether a goal is met, and meeting it is something it knows how to do.
It is also genuinely responsive, since whatever is actually going wrong is what gets the room.

## Lever

The single item, which is the whole of the organisation's capacity to change course.
The question is not whether it attends properly but what happens to the goal it is not attending to.

## Echo

A programme, funded and running,
a constituency that feels heard and is,
and a second goal that is not on the agenda and is not therefore fine.
