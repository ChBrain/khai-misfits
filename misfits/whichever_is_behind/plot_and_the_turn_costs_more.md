# Plot: And the Turn Costs More

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Cost of Turning Round](piece_the_cost_of_turning_round.md): a contractor released and re-procured, a design team dispersed, a fence insured through a dead year.

## Action

[Planning](process_planning.md): everybody downstream at [the estate](place_the_estate.md) and [the site](place_the_site.md) commits against a forward programme that was true when it was published.

## Stage

[The Compromise](persona_the_compromise.md), on [Offer the Split](plan_offer_the_split.md), remains the thing that would have let them, and remains uncarried.

## Tension

Here the settlements are tried, and each costs what the sequence was for.
**Weigh the goals**: this needs the rate of exchange, which nobody holds and no constituency has signed.
**Fix the split**: this removes the responsiveness that made attending to the shortfall sensible, so
a real emergency in one goal cannot pull anything toward it, and the fixed ratio is itself a
weighing that nobody signed.
**Add attention**: a second committee or a longer agenda carries one thing of substance in its turn,
because attention is singular wherever it is exercised, so the sequence reproduces itself one level
down and gains a coordination cost.
**Slow the switching**: lengthening the aspiration does not remove the switch, it lengthens the
neglect, so each goal waits longer before it is behind enough to be seen.
And underneath all four, the bill that is nobody's: the alternation is dearer than either policy it
alternates between and dearer than the split, spent a turn at a time, in papers that each cover one
turn. **No meeting decided to alternate**, so there is no decision to reverse and no author to ask.
