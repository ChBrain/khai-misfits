# Place: The Site

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). Where the build goal lands: land held, a design drawn, a contractor engaged or released.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Hoarding, a planning notice, and a programme board with dates on it.
In the good years, cranes. In the other years, a fence and a monthly invoice for insuring it.

## Holds

The cost of the turn, in its most legible form.
A pipeline stood down is not paused: the contractor takes other work, the design team disperses,
and the same homes are dearer when they are started again.

## Offers

Homes that did not exist, and a figure that moves against the neighbours.

## Withheld

Continuity, which nobody withheld from it.
Every stand-down was a correct response to the other goal being behind, and the accumulated cost
of them appears in no paper, because no paper covers more than one turn.
