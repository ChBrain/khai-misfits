# Pitch: We Are Doing Both

## Taxonomy

[Whichever Is Behind](play_whichever_is_behind.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Patient and unembarrassed, walking a newcomer through four years of minutes.
Precise about every decision having been the right one on the paper in front of it.
Willing to say that a sequence of right decisions is not a policy.

## Undertow

Under the programme, two facts that do not negotiate.
Attention is one thing at a time, whoever is holding it.
A goal is a line you are above or below, and there is no third answer.

## Nerve

The unease of a sentence that is true.
We are doing both: said in good faith, minuted every year, and describing an organisation
that has been at one end of the room and then the other and never once in the middle.

## Echo

After the run: a list that cleared and stopped being watched,
a stock that reached the agenda in the state that not being watched produces,
a paper proposing sixty and forty, tabled at every meeting and carried at none,
and a bill for turning round that neither goal would have run up.
