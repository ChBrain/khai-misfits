# Place: The Estate

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Shown

Blocks on a survey cycle, scored against a standard, most of them fine.
A repairs contract running at whatever level the last committee set, and a forward programme with
years written beside block names.

## Holds

The slow half of the mechanism.
Nothing here announces the month it stops being fine, so a deferral is absorbed silently and the
shortfall that eventually pulls the agenda back has been accumulating since the last time it was here.

## Offers

Absorption. It is the goal that can wait, which is why it is asked to.

## Withheld

Any moment at which its neglect became a decision.
The years in which it was above the standard are the years in which it was not on the agenda, and
being above the standard is what took it off.
