# Plot: It Went Over

## Taxonomy

[Back to Where It Was Fine](play_back_to_where_it_was_fine.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An increment no larger than any of the hundreds before it, arriving after the feedbacks holding the clear state have worn thin.

## Action

[Tipping over](process_tipping_over.md): [The Two States](persona_the_two_states.md) switch in [the shallow water](place_the_shallow_water.md), and the weed goes, and the bed stays stirred, and the light stops arriving.

## Stage

The change is rapid and large. Nothing about the input that caused it distinguishes it from the inputs that did not.

## Tension

The response has stopped being proportional to the push, so nothing in the record marks the day.
A slope can be watched and extrapolated; a switch cannot, because the thing that changed was not the size of the disturbance but the capacity of the system to absorb it. So there is no early warning available in the monitoring, no last straw identifiable afterwards, and no moment at which anybody could have said that this discharge, unlike the previous one, was the one that mattered.
