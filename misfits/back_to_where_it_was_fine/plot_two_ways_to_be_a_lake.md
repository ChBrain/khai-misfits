# Plot: Two Ways to Be a Lake

## Taxonomy

[Back to Where It Was Fine](play_back_to_where_it_was_fine.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A few metres of water over a soft bed, and two arrangements it can settle into.

## Action

[The Two States](persona_the_two_states.md), holding [Two States](position_two_states.md), occupy [the shallow water](place_the_shallow_water.md): clear, with weed holding the bed down and the nutrients up, or turbid, with no weed because no light reaches the bed.

## Stage

Each arrangement maintains itself across a range of loading, so each is a place the system can live rather than a moment it passes through.

## Tension

Two stable states mean the system has somewhere else to be, and no record of how to get back.
The point at which the clear state fails and the point at which the turbid state fails are far apart, which is a property of the feedbacks rather than an entry in anybody's data. So a lake can be measured accurately for forty years and yield no information whatever about the quantity that will govern its recovery, because every one of those measurements was taken on the way in.
