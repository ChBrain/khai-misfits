# Plan: Cut the Load

## Taxonomy

A **statutory duty**: the intent of [The One Who Sets the Target](persona_the_one_who_sets_the_target.md), inside [Back to Where It Was Fine](play_back_to_where_it_was_fine.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Sets the Target](persona_the_one_who_sets_the_target.md)

## Direction

Restore the water body by removing the cause. Establish the loading at which the lake was clear, set that as the target, and require the catchment to reach it.

## Orders

(Read forty years of monitoring from [the shallow water](place_the_shallow_water.md); identify the historical loading; issue [the target number](piece_the_target_number.md) to [the catchment](place_the_catchment.md); defend it through consultation and appeal.)

## Implementation

([The Catchment](place_the_catchment.md), where every available lever sits; the [cutting it back](process_cutting_it_back.md) process; an obligation to justify every figure imposed on anybody.)

## Targets

- [ ] the target is derived from evidence rather than from assertion
- [ ] it is proportionate, defensible, and upheld at every stage
- [ ] the catchment is required to reach it, and does
- [ ] the figure identifies where the clear state failed rather than where the turbid state fails, and nothing in the record distinguishes the two
