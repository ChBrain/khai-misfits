# Persona: The Sediment

## Taxonomy

[Sediment](position_sediment.md). The bed, which took the phosphorus in and now gives it back.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Decades of accumulated phosphorus lying in the bed of a shallow lake, within reach of the water above it.
Stirred by fish that feed in it, and lit by nothing, because the water above no longer passes light.

## Action

Releases nutrient back into the water column, independently of anything arriving from the catchment.
Stays suspended, keeping the light out and the weed from re-rooting.

## Shadow

Turns the lake into its own supplier, so the pipes stop being the thing to fix.
Every instrument the catchment holds acts on what flows in, and the load that now matters is already inside: so a programme that controls the inputs perfectly is a programme aimed at a source that has been superseded, and the more completely it succeeds the more clearly this persona is revealed as the one that was holding the state.

## Tell

Is the record of the loading and the reason removing the loading does nothing.
