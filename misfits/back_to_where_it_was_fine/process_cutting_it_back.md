# Process: Cutting It Back

## Taxonomy

[Back to Where It Was Fine](play_back_to_where_it_was_fine.md). The reduction, achieved in full and to no effect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Target Number](piece_the_target_number.md), derived from the loading at which the lake was demonstrably clear.
[The Ones Who Paid for It](persona_the_ones_who_paid_for_it.md), changing practice and rebuilding a treatment works.

## Direction

Toward the historical loading, reached and verified by monitoring.
Toward a moderate decrease in turbidity along the branch the lake is on, and no switch.

## Lever

A target that identifies where the clear state failed rather than where the turbid state fails, those two lying far apart.

## Echo

A programme that delivers exactly what it promised, with a catchment looking at exactly the same water.
