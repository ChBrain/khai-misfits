# Piece: The Sediment Store

## Taxonomy

[The Shallow Water](place_the_shallow_water.md). Forty years of loading, held where the catchment cannot reach it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Lying in the bed of [the shallow water](place_the_shallow_water.md), shallow enough to be stirred and shallow enough to release.

## Load Bearing

It converts the lake from a recipient into a source.
Phosphorus accumulated over decades is released back into the water column and kept in circulation by fish feeding in the bed, so the internal supply sustains the turbid state whatever the catchment does: the pipes stop being the thing to fix at the exact moment the catchment finishes fixing them.

## Apparent

Soft grey material, sampled routinely, with its nutrient content well characterised and its release rates measurable.
Nothing hidden about it, and nothing surprising in the chemistry.

## Yearbook

Built up quietly across the whole period in which the lake looked fine, which is the period nobody was worried.
Persists for years or decades after external loading falls, which is why reductions upstream so often fail to bring the weed back.
