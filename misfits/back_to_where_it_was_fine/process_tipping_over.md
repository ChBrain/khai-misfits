# Process: Tipping Over

## Taxonomy

[Back to Where It Was Fine](play_back_to_where_it_was_fine.md). A small push producing a large move.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An increment no larger than the hundreds before it, arriving after the clear-state feedbacks have worn thin.
[The Two States](persona_the_two_states.md), holding [Two States](position_two_states.md), in [the shallow water](place_the_shallow_water.md).

## Direction

Toward the turbid arrangement: no weed, no light on the bed, sediment stirred and staying stirred.
Toward a change that is a switch rather than a slope.

## Lever

Loss of the feedback that was holding the clear state, so the response stops being proportional to the push.

## Echo

Nothing in the record marking the day, since the input that crossed it is indistinguishable from every input that did not.
