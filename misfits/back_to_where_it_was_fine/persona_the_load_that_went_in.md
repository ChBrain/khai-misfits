# Persona: The Load That Went In

## Taxonomy

[Load That Went In](position_load_that_went_in.md). Decades of phosphorus, every gram of it lawful.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Discharge from a treatment works under consent, and runoff from fields fertilised at the rate the guidance recommends.
Arriving continuously, from many sources, over forty years.

## Action

Enters the water, and mostly settles into the bed.
Accumulates without producing any visible change for a long time.

## Shadow

Is exonerated year after year by the lake's own resistance.
A system in a stable state absorbs disturbance and looks unchanged, which reads from outside as evidence that the loading is harmless, so the absence of an effect is produced by the very mechanism that is being worn down: every year of no visible harm is a year of the buffer being spent, and the two are indistinguishable from the bank.

## Tell

Has no member that did it, since the last increment was no larger than any of the hundreds before it.
