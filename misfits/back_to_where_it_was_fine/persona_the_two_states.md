# Persona: The Two States

## Taxonomy

[Two States](position_two_states.md). Clear and turbid, each holding itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Two arrangements a shallow lake can settle into: weed on the bed with water you can see through, or no weed and water you cannot.
Both stable across a range of loading, so both are places the system can live rather than moments it passes through.

## Action

Holds whichever arrangement it is in, against disturbance, by its own feedbacks.
Switches between them abruptly, and not at the same point in each direction.

## Shadow

Puts the backward switch a long way below the forward one, and nothing observable says where.
The hysteresis is a property of the feedbacks rather than a fact recorded anywhere, so a catchment with forty years of measurements has forty years of one branch: everything anybody has measured about this lake was measured while it was on the way in, and the number that would matter on the way out has never been an observable of this system.

## Tell

Is the reason the repair is specified correctly and set at the wrong value.
