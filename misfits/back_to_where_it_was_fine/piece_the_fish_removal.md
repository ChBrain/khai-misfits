# Piece: The Fish Removal

## Taxonomy

[The Shallow Water](place_the_shallow_water.md). The intervention inside the lake, which forces the switch and must be repeated.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Carried out in [the shallow water](place_the_shallow_water.md) itself: netting out the fish that stir the bed and graze the animals that would graze the algae.

## Load Bearing

It is the one measure that acts on the state rather than on the inputs, and it works by force rather than by removing a cause.
Taking out the bottom-feeding and plankton-eating fish lets the water clear enough for light to reach the bed, weed to re-root and the clear-state feedbacks to re-establish: which is a switch imposed from outside, not a system returning, so it holds only while the new arrangement can hold itself and fails if the loading is still too high.

## Apparent

Dramatic, visible, and satisfying: nets, boats, tonnes of fish removed, and water that clears within a season.
The intervention that finally produces a result after years of upstream work produced none.

## Yearbook

Developed because reducing external load repeatedly failed to restore the weed, which is the finding that made the whole approach necessary.
Requires the external load to be low enough for the clear state to hold afterwards, so it does not replace the catchment work: it is what the catchment work turns out to have been a precondition for.
