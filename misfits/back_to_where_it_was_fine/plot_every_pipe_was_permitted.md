## Cue

## Action

[Loading it](process_loading_it.md): [The Load That Went In](persona_the_load_that_went_in.md), holding [Load That Went In](position_load_that_went_in.md), enters [the catchment](place_the_catchment.md)'s waters and settles mostly into the bed.

## Stage

Nothing visible changes for a very long time. The lake looks entirely fine throughout.

## Tension

The absence of harm is produced by the mechanism the loading is wearing down, so the two are the same observation.
A system in a stable state absorbs disturbance and looks unchanged, which reads from outside as evidence that the loading is harmless: every year of no visible effect is a year of the buffer being spent. And no member of the loading is the culprit, since the last increment was no larger than the hundreds before it, so there is a real, cumulative, wholly permitted cause attributable to nobody at all.
