## Stage

## Tension

The number is right on the evidence and wrong for the system, and no better number was available to be set.
The historical loading identifies where the clear state failed, not where the turbid state fails, and those lie far apart. Setting the target lower would have meant charging a catchment on the strength of a threshold nobody had measured, which no regulator may impose and none should. So the reasoning is correct at every step, the delivery is complete, and there is no breach to allege against anybody.
