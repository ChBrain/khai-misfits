# Persona: The One Who Sets the Target

## Taxonomy

[One Who Sets the Target](position_one_who_sets_the_target.md). The regulator, reading the evidence correctly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An authority with forty years of monitoring, a statutory duty to restore the water body, and a requirement to justify every number it imposes.
Answerable for the cost of whatever it sets.

## Action

Identifies the loading at which the lake was demonstrably clear, and sets that as the target.
Defends the figure successfully at every stage, because it is the figure the evidence supports.

## Shadow

Chooses the number that is right on the record and wrong for the system.
The record covers only the branch the lake came in on, and the backward switch lies far below anything ever observed, so a target derived from the historical loading is derived correctly from the only data that exists: setting it lower would have meant imposing a cost on the catchment on the basis of a threshold nobody had measured, which no regulator can do and none should.

## Tell

Would be overturned on appeal for setting the number that might have worked.
