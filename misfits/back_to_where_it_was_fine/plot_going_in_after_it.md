# Plot: Going In After It

## Taxonomy

[Back to Where It Was Fine](play_back_to_where_it_was_fine.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Years of upstream work having produced no result, and the measures that act inside the lake instead.

## Action

[Go into the lake](plan_go_into_the_lake.md): [The Fish Removal](piece_the_fish_removal.md) is carried out in [the shallow water](place_the_shallow_water.md), the water clears within a season, light reaches the bed and weed re-roots.

## Stage

It works. This is the intervention that finally produces a result, and it acts on the state rather than on the cause.

## Tension

The switch has been imposed by force rather than by a system returning, so it holds only while the new arrangement can hold itself.
If the loading is still too high the lake goes back, which means the catchment work was not an alternative to this but a precondition for it, discovered in that order and paid for in the reverse one. Dredging or capping the bed removes the store outright and costs more than the whole catchment programme. And cutting the load further means charging people who have already delivered in full, on the strength of a threshold nobody had measured when the first bill was issued.
