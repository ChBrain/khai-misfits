# Process: Warning

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). The onward telling: what the duty exists to produce, in the cases that reach it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A threat against an identifiable person, judged serious.
At [the consulting room](place_the_consulting_room.md), rarely, and correctly.

## Direction

Toward somebody being told who would not otherwise have been told.
This is the duty working, and it works: the warnings it produces are real, and a profession that
made none of them would be offering a room where such things can be said and nothing follows.
So this is not the failure. It is the thing every settlement below has to replace and cannot.

## Lever

The threshold, above which there is no discretion.
The question is not whether the warning should be given but what the certainty of it does to the
sentence that would trigger it.

## Echo

A person warned,
a duty discharged,
and a rule whose whole visible record is its successes.
