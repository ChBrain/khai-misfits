## Tension

Here the settlements are tried, and each costs what the duty was for.
**A firewall**, a promise that nothing said here goes anywhere, is the duty's own removal: it buys
the disclosures and gives up the warnings, which is the trade the first plot already refused.
**A higher threshold** narrows what must be passed on and leaves the patient exactly where they were,
since the limit is still disclosed and they still cannot place themselves against it.
**Not disclosing the limit** buys candour by removing consent, and no profession will write that down.
**Telling patients it is rarely invoked** invites them to gamble on a stranger's reading of a
threshold, offered by the person who would be doing the reading.
And underneath all four, the measurement that makes this a play rather than a worry: mandatory
duty-to-warn laws are associated with more of the outcome the duty exists to prevent. **The harm can
only ever be a rate**, because the whole of it consists of sentences not said and appointments not
booked, so no case file contains it and no clinician can be shown it.
