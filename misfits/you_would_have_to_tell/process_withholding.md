# Process: Withholding

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). A subject not raised, in an hour where everything else is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Forewarning](process_forewarning.md), heard and understood.
At [the consulting room](place_the_consulting_room.md), silently, from the first session onward.

## Direction

Toward a course of treatment that goes well and has a hole in it.
Nothing is evaded, because nothing is asked that would reach it. The patient answers every question
honestly and the notes are complete.
This is not deception and cannot be caught at, since **there is no moment at which a refusal
occurs**: a subject that is never approached produces no observable event.

## Lever

The certainty of onward telling, which the patient was correctly informed of.
The question is not whether the patient is being straight, because they are, but what a straight
person does with accurate information about their listener.

## Echo

A full record with nothing missing from it,
sessions that help,
and the one subject the duty exists to catch, not among the subjects.
