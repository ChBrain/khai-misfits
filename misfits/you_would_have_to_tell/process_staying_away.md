# Process: Staying Away

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). An appointment not booked, by somebody who worked out what would happen.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A page about confidentiality, or a friend's account of it.
Nowhere the profession can see, before any [first session](place_the_first_session.md) exists.

## Direction

Toward not entering at all.
The reasoning is sound and takes about a minute: I might say something that would have to be passed
on, and I would rather not be in a position where that is decided by somebody else's reading of a
threshold.
Nobody has been deterred by a misunderstanding. They have been deterred by understanding it.

## Lever

Publicity, which the duty requires: the limit is disclosed and is therefore known before anybody
arrives.
The question the play asks is what a rule costs when the fact of it travels further than the rule.

## Echo

No file,
no clinician who could report it,
and a loss the profession cannot observe and no survey of the profession can reach.
