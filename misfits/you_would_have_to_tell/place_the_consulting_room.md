# Place: The Consulting Room

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). Where the hour happens: private, careful, and bounded by a limit everybody in it knows.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two people, fifty minutes, and a set of notes that will be complete.
Everything discussed honestly: the week, the sleep, the job, the marriage.

## Holds

The hole, which nothing in the room reveals.
A record with the only thing that mattered missing from it is indistinguishable from a record with
nothing missing, because an absence leaves no entry and no question was evaded.

## Offers

Treatment that works, on everything it reaches.

## Withheld

Its own limit, which was disclosed and is therefore operating.
The patient is not doubting the clinician: above the threshold there is no discretion to doubt, so
the room's safety is bounded by a rule rather than by anybody's character.
