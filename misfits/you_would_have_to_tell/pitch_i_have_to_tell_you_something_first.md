# Pitch: I Have to Tell You Something First

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Careful and unhurried, explaining the limits of confidentiality to somebody who has read the statute and not yet seen what it does.
Entirely in favour of the duty, and unwilling to pretend that being in favour of it settles anything.
Quoting the statute wherever the statute is the point.

## Undertow

Under the first five minutes, two facts that do not negotiate.
Above the threshold the listener has no discretion, which is what makes the rule worth having.
And the limit must be disclosed, so the warning arrives before anything it would deter.

## Nerve

The unease of doing it properly.
I have to tell you something first: owed, correct, said kindly, and heard by somebody
who is now deciding what not to bring in here.

## Echo

After the run: an hour that went well and had a hole in it,
an appointment that was never booked and can appear in no record,
a duty that everybody followed exactly,
and a line on a chart moving in the direction the duty was written to prevent.
