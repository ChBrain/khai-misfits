# You Would Have to Tell: Reference

The research warrant for [You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md).
The setting is psychotherapy under a mandatory duty to warn; the play runs on the fact that a duty about what
must be passed on operates entirely on what is said in the first place, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging a mandatory reporting duty as **a rule that narrows the channel it was written to open**: the duty is
owed, since it exists because an intention was heard and the person it concerned was never told; it is mandatory
above the threshold, and the absence of discretion is what makes it work and what makes the room unsafe to speak
in; consent requires the limit to be disclosed, so the deterrent is delivered before any disclosure it would
deter, by a clinician acting correctly and in the only honest order; the patient neither lies nor conceals but
does not raise it, which produces no refusal, no hesitation and no entry in a complete record; some never enter
the profession's sight at all, which is the part no practitioner can observe; and the outcome the duty exists to
prevent is measured higher where it binds. The play models the compelled telling, the disclosed limit, the
subject not raised, the appointment not booked and the rate that moves, as the load-bearing vectors of the
structure, not as an argument for absolute confidentiality and not as a story about a careless profession.

The governing law is that a duty concerning onward disclosure acts on prior disclosure, and prior disclosure is
where the information it wants comes from.

It is distinct from the house's **Watermelon Reporting**, whose engine is a reporter's exposure to blame for the
news they carry, so bad news does not travel upward. Switch that engine off, by stipulating that nobody is ever
penalised for passing something up, and this play stands entire, because **it is not the reporter who goes
silent but the source**, and the source goes silent because the listener is compelled rather than because the
listener would punish them. There the message dies in the person holding it; here it never enters that person.

It is distinct from the house's **Defensive Medicine**, whose quantity is liability exposure producing goal
displacement in a practitioner. Set it to zero, stipulating no liability of any kind, and this is unchanged: the
patient withholds on account of the disclosure itself, the clinician here displaces no goal and is not
practising defensively, and the duty would deter exactly as much if it carried no sanction at all and were
simply always obeyed.

It is distinct from the house's **The Paper Trail**, where effort migrates into documenting that one complied.
Stipulate that nobody ever needs to show compliance and this runs as written, since nothing here is being
documented and the loss is in what was never available to document.

It is distinct from the house's **First Do Harm**, which is the nearest family and stages an intervention whose
own action harms. Switch that engine off, stipulating an intervention that inflicts nothing, and this survives,
because **nothing here is inflicted**: the harm is the absence of what never arrived, and the duty's own action,
the warning, is beneficial in every case that reaches it.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the court as
  careless of the therapeutic relationship, the clinician as defensive, the patient as deceitful or the
  legislature as thoughtless, because nothing in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the duty exists
  because somebody should have been warned and was not; the mandatory form removes exactly the discretion
  through which that happened; disclosing the limit is required by consent and every other ordering is worse;
  the patient evades no question and answers honestly; and the person who never books has reasoned correctly
  from accurate information. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is
  that a duty about passing something on acts on whether it is said.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. A confidentiality firewall is
  the duty's own removal and gives up the warnings it does produce. Raising the threshold narrows what must be
  passed on and leaves the patient unable to place themselves against a line they still know exists. Not
  disclosing the limit buys candour by removing consent, which no profession will write down. And telling
  patients the duty is rarely invoked invites them to gamble on a stranger's reading of a threshold, offered by
  the person who would be doing the reading.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the duty and its reasoning are the court's and the legislatures'
  that codified it, the identification from the timing and form of state laws and both measured outcomes are
  Griffin Edwards's, the survey of the profession shortly after the ruling is Toni Pryor Wise's, the practice
  account is Quinn's, and the reading that the anticipated chilling effect did not materialise is Buckner and
  Firestone's. The bounds are carried with them, and three are owed plainly. **None of the cited works was read
  in full**, each being behind publisher access from this environment, so each is cited for the question it
  addresses and its findings as corroborated from several independent reports, and **no figure appears anywhere
  in this production that those reports did not state.** The second: the measured effect is an estimate from a
  natural experiment in law, with the usual limits of that design, and it is **contested**, which the warrant
  carries as a row rather than argues away; the play claims the sign and an order of magnitude and nothing
  finer. And the third is a limit on the staging rather than on the evidence. **The person the case is named for
  is the origin of the duty and is not staged**, appearing here as the reason the rule exists and nowhere in the
  production, because this play's payer is a rate and putting a real death on the board to make a structural
  point would be borrowing it. The house holds that a play which cannot be staged without a real victim in the
  room should not be staged, and this one can be.
