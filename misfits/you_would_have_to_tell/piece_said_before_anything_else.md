# Piece: Said Before Anything Else

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). The ordering that cannot be improved: the limit disclosed before anything it limits.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the first session](place_the_first_session.md), in the opening minutes.
In the consent requirement that every ethics board would insist on.

## Load Bearing

Without it the deterrent would arrive too late to deter and the play would be about tact.
It is load bearing because it fixes the sequence: **the warning about the duty precedes every
disclosure the duty would reach**, so the deterrence is not an unfortunate side effect of an
awkward conversation but a structural consequence of doing it in the only honest order.

## Apparent

That a better wording would soften it.
The profession has refined the wording for decades. Softening changes how it is felt and not what it
says, and what it says is the operative part.

## Yearbook

An obligation grounded in consent rather than in caution.
The play takes it as owed and shows what being owed costs.
