# Persona: The Clinician

## Taxonomy

[Duty](position_duty.md). The one who must pass it on, and who wants the rule to exist.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Experienced, careful, and in favour of the duty on the merits.
Would defend it to a sceptical colleague and has.
Has never had a case that reached the threshold and expects never to.

## Action

Discloses the limit, first, before anything else.
Listens.
Would tell, if it came to it, and knows it.

## Shadow

That being trustworthy is not what is in question.
The patient is not doubting their discretion, judgement or good faith: **above the threshold there
is no discretion to trust**, and the clinician's own reliability is the thing that makes the passing
on certain rather than likely.

## Tell

Says the sentence in the first five minutes, every time, exactly as required.
Has no way of knowing what it cost, in that hour or in any hour that never happened.
