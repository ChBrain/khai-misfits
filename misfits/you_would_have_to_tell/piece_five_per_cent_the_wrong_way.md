# Piece: Five Per Cent the Wrong Way

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). The measured movement in the outcome the duty was written to prevent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the law and economics literature, read across states by the timing and form of their duties.
At [the state line](place_the_state_line.md), where it is the only thing visible.

## Load Bearing

Without it this is a worry that clinicians have and the play is a complaint about paperwork.
It is load bearing because it makes the inversion **measured rather than argued**: mandatory
duty-to-warn laws are reported as associated with a homicide rate about five per cent higher, with
a movement in suicide alongside it, so the rule is not merely uncomfortable but is associated with
more of the thing it exists to prevent.

## Apparent

That an estimate this size could be anything.
The play claims what the design claims and no more: a sign and an order of magnitude, from variation
in the law across states, with the usual caveats of that method carried in the warrant rather than
argued away here.

## Yearbook

Measured in economics, spent in clinical ethics, and connecting the two is the play's extension
rather than the author's.
It is the number the duty was written without, because it could not have existed yet.
