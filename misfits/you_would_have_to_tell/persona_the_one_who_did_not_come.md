# Persona: The One Who Did Not Come

## Taxonomy

[Absence](position_absence.md). The person who worked out what they might say, and what would happen if they said it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not a patient, not a case, not a file.
Somebody who read the page about confidentiality, or was told by a friend, and did not make the call.
Indistinguishable in every dataset from a person who simply did not need help.

## Action

Considers it.
Learns what the limit is.
Does not book.

## Shadow

That the loss happens **upstream of the profession's sight entirely**.
There is no session in which their absence could be noticed, no clinician who could report it and no
record with a hole in it, so the narrowing that matters most is the part no practitioner can ever
observe and no survey of practitioners can reach.

## Tell

Appears in nothing.
Is the reason the numbers have to be read across states rather than inside a clinic.
