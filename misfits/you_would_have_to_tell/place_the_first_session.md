# Place: The First Session

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). Where the limit is said: in the opening minutes, before anything it governs.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A form, sometimes, and three or four sentences said aloud.
Wording the profession has worked on for decades to make it land as gently as it can.

## Holds

The ordering that cannot be changed.
Consent requires the limit to precede what it limits, so **the deterrent arrives before the
disclosure it deters**, and every alternative is worse: later it is a trap, and never is candour
bought by removing consent.

## Offers

An honest account of what the room does and does not hold.

## Withheld

Any measurement of what the twenty seconds after it cost.
Surveys reach the clinicians and not the patients, and reach nobody who left before the second
appointment.
