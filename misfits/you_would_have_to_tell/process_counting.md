# Process: Counting

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). Reading the outcome across states, because there is nowhere else it can be read.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Duties adopted at different times and in different forms.
At [the state line](place_the_state_line.md), across years, in the aggregate.

## Direction

Toward a comparison the profession cannot make from inside itself.
No clinic holds the evidence, because the evidence consists of what did not happen, so the only
available design is to compare places that adopted with places that had not yet.
Read that way, the mandatory duty is associated with more of the outcome it was written to prevent.

## Lever

Variation in the law, which exists by accident of federalism and is what makes any of this knowable.
The question is not whether the estimate is exact but that the sign is the wrong one.

## Echo

A small movement in a large number,
attributable to nobody,
and the only form the harm was ever going to take.
