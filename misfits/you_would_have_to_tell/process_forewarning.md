# Process: Forewarning

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). The limit being disclosed, at the start, to everybody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A first appointment.
At [the first session](place_the_first_session.md), in the opening minutes, before anything else.

## Direction

Toward informed consent, which requires it.
Nobody can agree to treatment without knowing what it does not cover, and the profession has worked
hard on the wording to make it land as gently as it can.
So this is not a lapse in tact or an excess of caution: it is the obligation, discharged in the only
order that is honest.

## Lever

Timing, which cannot be changed.
Disclosed later the limit is a trap; not disclosed it buys candour by removing consent, which no
profession will write down.

## Echo

Three or four sentences, understood,
and a patient who now knows, before speaking, what the room does not hold.
