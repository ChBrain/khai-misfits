# Plot: What Never Reaches the Room

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Sentence Not Said](piece_the_sentence_not_said.md): an hour that goes well.

## Action

[Withholding](process_withholding.md): [The Patient](persona_the_patient.md), in [the silence seat](position_silence.md), discusses everything else on [Keep It Out of the Room](plan_keep_it_out_of_the_room.md).

## Stage

[The Clinician](persona_the_clinician.md), on [Tell Whoever Must Be Told](plan_tell_whoever_must_be_told.md), taking complete notes with nothing missing from them.

## Tension

Here the harm arrives, and it arrives without an event.
The patient does not lie and does not conceal. **No question is evaded, because no question reaches
it.** Everything asked is answered honestly, the sessions help, and the subject that would have been
the whole point of the hour is simply not among the subjects.
So there is nothing to catch anybody at, in either direction. A record missing the only thing that
mattered is indistinguishable from a record missing nothing, since an absence leaves no entry.
The surveys taken after the ruling record clinicians reporting patients more reluctant to discuss
violent thoughts, and patients lost at the moment the limit was explained. **That is the profession
seeing the class and not the case**: knowing that it happens does not locate it in an hour, and no
amount of skill turns a subject that was never approached into an observation.
