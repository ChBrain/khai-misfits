# Piece: Somebody Had to Be Warned

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). Why the duty exists, and why the play does not ask for it to be repealed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the ruling that created it, and in the statutes that followed across most of the country.
Carried by [the clinician](persona_the_clinician.md), who is in favour of it on the merits.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is an argument for absolute confidentiality and does not need any of the rest.
It is load bearing because it makes every settlement expensive: a profession that hears intentions
and passes none of them on is **not offering confidentiality but a room where such things can be
said and nothing follows**, and no remedy below gets past that.

## Apparent

That granting this concedes the argument.
It concedes that the duty is owed and denies nothing about its effect, which is the whole position:
the play holds that the rule is right and that it is also associated with more of what it prevents.

## Yearbook

A rule with a reason, credited to the reason.
The person the case is named for is the origin of the duty and is not staged here, because the
play's payer is a rate and putting her in the room would be borrowing a death to make a point.
