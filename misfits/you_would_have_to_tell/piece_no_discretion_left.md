# Piece: No Discretion Left

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). The property that makes the duty work, and the property that empties the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Above the threshold, in every statute that makes the duty mandatory.
Understood exactly by [the patient](persona_the_patient.md), who has been told.

## Load Bearing

Without it the patient could rely on the clinician's judgement and the play has no engine.
It is load bearing because a duty that could be exercised at discretion would not be a duty: **the
certainty is the point**, and the certainty is what a person weighs before deciding whether a
subject is for this room.

## Apparent

That the patient distrusts the clinician.
They do not, and that is the difficulty. Trustworthiness makes the passing on certain rather than
likely, so **the better the clinician, the more reliable the deterrent**.

## Yearbook

Written into the mandatory form of the law deliberately, to remove the variation that let the
original harm happen.
The play stages what removing that variation does further upstream.
