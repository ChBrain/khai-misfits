## Tension

The duty has to be established as **owed** before anything else is worth saying, because the easy
reading of this play is that it wants confidentiality to be absolute.
It does not, and it says so here. A profession that hears intentions and passes none of them on is
not offering confidentiality: it is offering a room where such things can be said and nothing
follows. The warnings the duty produces are real and land on people who would otherwise not have
been told.
And the property that makes it work is the absence of discretion. A duty exercisable at judgement
would not be a duty, and removing that variation is exactly what the mandatory form was for, since
variation is how the original harm happened.
**Everything that follows is the cost of that, and none of it is an argument for repeal**, which is
the constraint every settlement in the last plot has to satisfy and none of them does.
