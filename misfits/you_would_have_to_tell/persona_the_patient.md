# Persona: The Patient

## Taxonomy

[Silence](position_silence.md). The one who came, and who does not raise it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Attending, engaged and honest.
Talks about the week, the sleep, the job and the marriage, all truthfully.
Would pass any test of candour anybody thought to set.

## Action

Hears the preamble.
Decides, without deciding, what is not for this room.
Discusses everything else.

## Shadow

That there is nothing to catch them at.
**This is not lying and not concealment**: no question is evaded, because the question was never
reached, and the subject that would have been the whole point of the hour is simply not one of the
subjects. The record is complete and shows nothing missing, since an absence leaves no entry.

## Tell

Comes back next week.
Says the sessions are helping, and means it.
