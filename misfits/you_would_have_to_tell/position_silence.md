# Position: Silence

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). The seat of what is not raised: not a lie, not concealment, and not visible in any record.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An hour a week, used honestly, on everything else.
A complete set of notes with nothing missing from them.
One subject that is not among the subjects.

## Orders

Keep it out of the room.
Answer everything asked.
Come back next week.

## Loses

Its own detectability.
Nothing is evaded, because nothing is asked that would reach it, so there is no moment at which a
clinician could notice a refusal: **an absence leaves no entry**, and a record that is complete and
a record that is missing the only thing that mattered look identical.

## Drives

Toward the treatment continuing,
which it does, usefully,
so the seat that is getting better is the seat the duty was written for.
