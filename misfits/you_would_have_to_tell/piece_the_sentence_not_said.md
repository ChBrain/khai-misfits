# Piece: The Sentence Not Said

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). The withheld subject: not a lie, not concealment, and not an event.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Nowhere. That is the difficulty.
Absent from [the consulting room](place_the_consulting_room.md), and from the complete notes taken in it.

## Load Bearing

Without it the harm would be detectable and somebody could act on it.
It is load bearing because **no question is evaded**: a subject that is never approached produces no
refusal, no hesitation and no entry, so a record missing the only thing that mattered is
indistinguishable from a record missing nothing.

## Apparent

That a skilled clinician would sense it.
The surveys after the ruling record clinicians reporting patients more reluctant to discuss violent
thoughts, which is the profession noticing the class and not the case: knowing it happens does not
locate it in an hour.

## Yearbook

The reason the evidence had to be found across states rather than inside practices.
A harm made of absences produces no observation where the absences occur.
