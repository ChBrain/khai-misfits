# Position: Duty

## Taxonomy

[You Would Have to Tell](play_you_would_have_to_tell.md). The seat of the compelled onward telling: owed, defensible, and without discretion above the line.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A rule with a reason: somebody heard an intention and the person it concerned was never told.
Statutes in most states, differing in timing and in form.
No latitude at all, once the threshold is met.

## Orders

Tell whoever must be told.
Do it whatever the relationship costs.
Do not exercise judgement above the line, because that is the point.

## Loses

The room it operates in.
The absence of discretion is what makes the duty worth having and is exactly what makes the room
unsafe to speak in, so **the property that makes the rule work is the property that empties it**.

## Drives

Toward the warning it exists to produce,
which it does produce, in the cases that reach it,
so the seat that is right about every case it sees is silent about the ones it never gets.
