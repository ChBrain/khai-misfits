# Persona: The Preamble

## Taxonomy

[Notice](position_notice.md). The sentence about the limits of confidentiality, said before anything else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Three or four sentences at the start of a first session, sometimes on a form as well.
Written by the profession, required by consent, and phrased as gently as it can be.
The thing every ethics board would insist on.

## Action

Arrives first.
Is understood.
Cannot be unsaid.

## Shadow

That its correctness is what gives it force.
**The deterrent is delivered before anything it would deter**, by somebody discharging an obligation
they are right to have, and a version that came later would be worse rather than better, since a
limit disclosed after the disclosure is not a limit but a trap.

## Tell

Everybody agrees it is owed.
Nobody counts what happens in the twenty seconds after it.
