# Position: Skilled

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The seat of the high-quality input, worth most beside others like it and little beside a weak link, sorted into high-value production and richly rewarded.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A quality that multiplies with quality: a reliable, high task that is worth far more on a chain of peers than on a chain with a weak link.
A sound reason to gather: on a flawed chain its excellence is multiplied by another's failure and comes to little, so its worth is highest among the skilled.
No full value beside the low, because there the multiplication of a weak link drags its quality down, so it is not worth its best where the quality around it is not high.

## Orders

Keep good company.
Seek the chains and firms where the other tasks are done as well as yours, since that is where your quality is multiplied by quality and worth its most.
Take the reward that gathers there, and know that the clustering is warranted by the multiplication, not owed to any disdain for the low.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and yet its clustering helps open the divide.
Because a skilled input is worth most beside skill and little beside a weak link, the seat is drawn to its peers, and that warranted drawing-together is what leaves the low pole to the low, so its sound choice helps strand the rest.
It keeps its high reward and loses no cause for blame, the one the multiplication rewards and the one whose gathering widens the gap.

## Drives

Toward the one worth most among its peers and drawn to gather with them,
seeking the company where quality multiplies quality, no arrogance in it,
so the seat the multiplication rewards so richly is the seat whose warranted clustering helps open the divide the low are stranded across.
