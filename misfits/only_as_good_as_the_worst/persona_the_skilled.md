# Persona: The Skilled

## Taxonomy

[Skilled](position_skilled.md). The high-quality input, worth most beside others like it and little beside a weak link, sorted into high-value production and richly rewarded, the peer the good gather with.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Able, sought, well-placed.
Does its task reliably and well, and is worth far more on a chain of others like it than on a chain with a weak link that would multiply its excellence down to little.
No arrogance in it: it seeks skilled peers not from disdain but because that is where its quality is worth most, and the reward there is real.

## Action

Keeps good company on [Keep Good Company](plan_keep_good_company.md), combining through [Multiplying](process_multiplying.md) at [the Assembly](place_the_assembly.md) into [the Product](piece_the_product.md), whose value is the product of the qualities, so its excellence is multiplied by its peers' and reaches a high worth none could alone.
Cannot be worth its full value beside a weak link, because on a flawed chain its quality is multiplied by another's failure and comes to little, so it gathers where the quality around it is high.
Does what a high-quality input does under multiplication: it clusters with its peers, where the best are worth most together.

## Shadow

Not a snob for keeping to its peers and not the cause of the divide: the Skilled seeks the company where its quality is genuinely worth most, and the clustering is warranted by the multiplication, not by contempt for the low.
Because a skilled input is worth most beside skill and little beside a weak link, the Skilled is drawn to gather with its own, and that same drawing-together is what leaves the low pole to the low, the reward of the best together and the stranding of the rest one structure.
It is the one the multiplication rewards so richly, and the one whose warranted clustering helps open the divide.

## Tell

Says it works best with people as good as itself, and is right, its quality is worth most there.
Cannot lift the low-skill by joining them, because on a weak chain its excellence would be multiplied down to little, so it keeps good company and the sorting it seeks helps strand the rest.
