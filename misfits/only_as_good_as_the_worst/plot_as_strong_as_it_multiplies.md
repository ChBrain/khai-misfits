# Plot: As Strong as It Multiplies

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Divide](place_the_divide.md), the multiplicative chain of the earlier scenes set now beside a chain whose tasks are substitutable, not complementary.

## Action

The bound is drawn: [The Bound](piece_the_bound.md) holds that the sorting and the stranding are as strong as production is multiplicative, no stronger and no weaker. Where a weak task can be substituted for, backed up by a redundant one, or where the tasks combine only additively rather than as a product, its damage no longer multiplies through the whole and the pull toward [Matching](process_matching.md) and away through [Stranding](process_stranding.md) both soften. The scene closes with the trap standing real for chains of complements, and standing down where the chain is not one.

## Stage

The O-ring result holds insofar as production is multiplicative, tasks genuinely complementary and each necessary to the others, and it softens where tasks are substitutable, redundant, or merely additive (Kremer). No claim is made here that all production is a weakest-link chain, nor that every workforce sorts as cleanly as [The Match](piece_the_match.md) suggests. The hedge is load-bearing: it is the condition, not a footnote to it.

## Tension

The trap is exactly as strong as the tasks are complementary, and it claims nothing beyond that line.
