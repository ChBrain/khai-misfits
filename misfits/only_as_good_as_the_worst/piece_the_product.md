# Piece: The Product

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The multiplicative rule: the value of the output is the product of the tasks' qualities, not their sum.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Reached at [The Assembly](place_the_assembly.md), the value [The Chain](piece_the_chain.md) combines to once every task has run. [The Firm](persona_the_firm.md) is who the value accrues to.

## Load Bearing

Because qualities multiply rather than add, a task at half quality roughly halves the whole product however excellent the rest, so the worst task dominates the value and no amount of excellence elsewhere buys it back (Kremer). This is the O-ring theory's central claim: production as a product of qualities, not a sum, named for the Challenger, where one faulty seal destroyed a shuttle built otherwise to the highest standard. Guard against reading production as additive, where a weak task would only subtract its own share from the total: the whole point of the multiplicative rule is that a weak task does not subtract, it multiplies through and cuts everything alongside it.

## Apparent

An ordinary finished product, worth whatever it is worth on the market.
