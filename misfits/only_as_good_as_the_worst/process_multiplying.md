# Process: Multiplying

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). Borne at [The Assembly](place_the_assembly.md), precipitated by [The Skilled](persona_the_skilled.md) and the weak link combining in [The Product](piece_the_product.md), in [The Whole Product](plot_the_whole_product.md).

## Owner

## Initiated by

A chain of complementary tasks combining into one output.

## Direction

From tasks valued separately toward a value that is their product, so the worst quality dominates the whole.

## Lever

Production is multiplicative and not additive, so a low quality anywhere cuts the whole in proportion, one weak link ruining the product (Kremer).
