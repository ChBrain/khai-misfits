# Persona: The Stranded

## Taxonomy

[Stranded](position_stranded.md). The lower-quality input, willing but shut out of high-value production because it would be the weak link, confined to low-value work, its small deficit in skill widened into a vast gap in wage and worth.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Willing, capable, kept out.
Would gladly do the work and is not far below the skilled, only a little less reliable, a little lower in quality.
No fault in it: it is turned from high-value chains not for anything it did wrong but because, on a chain where the value multiplies, its quality would cut the whole.

## Action

Takes what's left on [Take What's Left](plan_take_whats_left.md), through [Stranding](process_stranding.md) at [the Divide](place_the_divide.md), meeting [the Amplifier](piece_the_amplifier.md), shut out of high-value production and confined to low-value work where a weak link does less damage, its small skill gap multiplied into a large gap in life.
Cannot enter the high-value chain, because there its quality would be multiplied through and lower the whole, so no willingness or effort on its part opens the gate the multiplication has closed.
Does what the excluded do: it takes the low-value work that is left, the only place it is not the weak link.

## Shadow

Not a failure and not the author of its plight: the Stranded is only a little below the skilled, and it is shut out not by any wrong of its own but by a structure in which one low quality cuts the whole, so its exclusion is a multiplication's doing and not a just desert.
Because a small gap in skill is amplified and sorted into a large gap in outcome, the one who differs least from the skilled can still be stranded far below them, the poverty trap made not by its lack but by the multiplication of its lack.
It is the one high-value work cannot risk, and the one the structure strands for a deficit far smaller than the gap it opens in its life.

## Tell

Says it would do the work well enough, and is nearly right, only a little short of the mark the chain requires.
Cannot argue its way onto the high-value chain, because a little short is enough to cut a product whose value multiplies, so it takes what is left and its small deficit costs it a vast share of what it might have earned.
