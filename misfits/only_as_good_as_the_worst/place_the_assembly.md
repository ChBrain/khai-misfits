# Place: The Assembly

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The line where a chain of complementary tasks combines into one product, and one weak link cuts the whole. Hosts [The Whole Product](plot_the_whole_product.md), [One Bad Seal](plot_one_bad_seal.md), [The Long Way Up](plot_the_long_way_up.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Many tasks done in sequence, each one necessary.
The product only as good as its worst part.

## Holds

[The Chain](piece_the_chain.md), the long run of complementary tasks, and [The Product](piece_the_product.md), the multiplicative value that one weak link cuts.

## Offers

To a chain of tasks all done well, a high value none of them could reach alone.

## Withheld

Any forgiveness for a single low-quality task, since the value is the product of the qualities and not their sum.
