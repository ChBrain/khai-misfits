# Piece: The Bound

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The boundary of the theory: the result holds only as strongly as production is multiplicative.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Tested at [The Assembly](place_the_assembly.md), where a chain might be shortened, backed up, or made redundant instead of run in a single fragile sequence, and its effect shows at [The Divide](place_the_divide.md), where the gap between high and low would soften.

## Load Bearing

This is the hedge, and it is as load bearing as the harm it bounds. The sorting and the poverty trap hold only insofar as production is multiplicative and complementary: where a weak task can be substituted, backed up, or made to matter only additively, its damage no longer multiplies through the whole, so the weakest link no longer dominates and the sorting and the stranding soften (Kremer). The trap is real for complementary chains and not a law of all production. Guard against reading the misfit as a claim that all production is a weakest-link chain, or that every workforce sorts perfectly by skill: the theory names the condition under which the trap holds, and outside that condition the trap does not follow.
