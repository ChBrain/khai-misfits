# Piece: The Amplifier

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The multiplicative widening of small skill differences into large differences in wage and firm value, and the exclusion this drives.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Felt at [The Divide](place_the_divide.md), the distance between the high pole [The Skilled](persona_the_skilled.md) reaches and the low pole [The Stranded](persona_the_stranded.md) is held to.

## Load Bearing

This is the load-bearing harm of the whole misfit. A small gap in skill, multiplied through a chain and sorted by [Matching](process_matching.md) into different firms, becomes a vast gap in wage and in the worth of what can be built, and the low-skill are stranded out of high-value production that needs every task done well (Kremer). The harm is not one irreplaceable person failing at a single point, and it is not a trimmed buffer shattering under a shock: it is the sorting itself, and the exclusion it enforces, amplifying a difference that started small. Guard against reading the wage gap as proportional to the skill gap, a fair reflection of how much more able one worker is than another: multiplicative complementarity amplifies a small difference into a large one, so the gap in outcome overstates the gap in ability.

## Apparent

Ordinary inequality, taken for a fair reflection of ability honestly rewarded.
