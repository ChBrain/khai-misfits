# Plan: No Weak Links

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the firm's seat](position_firm.md), inside [Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Firm](position_firm.md)

## Direction

Admit only inputs whose quality your chain can bear, and match skill with skill. Because the value of your output multiplies rather than adds, the worst task caps the whole, so a poor one placed anywhere ruins it, and a skilled input is worth far more beside peers than beside a weak link. Leave the lower quality to lesser chains, not from disdain but because a weak link does less damage there and its quality is worth what it is. Know that this sorting, sound as it is, shuts the low-skill out.

## Orders

([The Firm](persona_the_firm.md) takes [the Match](piece_the_match.md) through [Matching](process_matching.md) at [the Gate](place_the_gate.md), admitting only high-quality inputs and pairing skill with skill, refusing any that would be [the Weak Link](piece_the_weak_link.md) in [the Product](piece_the_product.md).)

## Implementation

([Matching](process_matching.md) at [the Gate](place_the_gate.md) sorts inputs by quality because [the Product](piece_the_product.md) multiplies, so a skilled input is worth most beside peers, and [the Match](piece_the_match.md) of high with high maximises the value, leaving the low pole to lesser chains and stranding those turned away.)

## Targets

- [ ] only inputs the chain can bear are admitted, skill matched with skill
- [ ] any input that would be the weak link is refused, since one poor task caps a multiplicative whole
- [ ] the lower quality is left to lesser chains, where a weak link does less damage
- [ ] the sort is sound and value-maximising, and the exclusion it makes of the low-skill is real, not intended as malice
