# Plan: Keep Good Company

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the skilled seat](position_skilled.md), inside [Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md).

## Owner

## Direction

Seek the chains where the other tasks are done as well as yours, since that is where your quality is multiplied by quality and worth its most. On a flawed chain your excellence is multiplied by another's failure and comes to little, so your worth is highest among peers, not beside a weak link. Take the reward that gathers there, and know that the clustering is warranted by the multiplication and not owed to any disdain for the low. Know too that this sound drawing-together is what leaves the low pole to the low.
