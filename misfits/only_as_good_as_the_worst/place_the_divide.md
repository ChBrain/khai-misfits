# Place: The Divide

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The gap between high-value production, where the skilled cluster, and low-value work, where the stranded go. Hosts [Shut Out](plot_shut_out.md), [As Strong as It Multiplies](plot_as_strong_as_it_multiplies.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The skilled richly rewarded in high-value chains, the low-skill confined to low-value work.
Small differences in skill become vast differences in wage and worth.

## Holds

[The Amplifier](piece_the_amplifier.md), the multiplicative widening of small skill gaps into large outcome gaps, and the exclusion it makes.

## Offers

To the stranded, only the low-value work where they are not the weak link.

## Withheld

Any path from the low pole to the high, while the chain will not bear a weak link.
