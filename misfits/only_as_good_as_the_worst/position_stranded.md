# Position: Stranded

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The seat of the lower-quality input, willing but shut out of high-value production because it would be the weak link, confined to low-value work, its small deficit widened into a vast gap in wage and worth.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A willingness and a small deficit: it would gladly do the work and is only a little below the skilled, a little less reliable.
No entry to the high-value chain, because there its quality would be multiplied through and cut the whole, so the gate is closed to it whatever its effort.
Only the low-value work that is left, where a weak link does less damage and where its quality is worth what it is.

## Orders

Take what's left.
Accept the low-value work the sorting leaves, the only place you are not the weak link, since the high-value chain cannot bear the quality you bring.
Know that the exclusion is a structure's doing and not a just desert, and that no willingness of yours opens a gate the multiplication has shut.

## Loses

A vast share of what it might have earned, for a deficit far smaller than the gap it opens.
Because a small difference in skill is multiplied and sorted into a large difference in outcome, the seat loses not in proportion to how far it falls short but far more, stranded at the low pole for being a little below the mark.
It keeps its willingness and loses the high-value work, the one high value cannot risk and the one the structure costs most.

## Drives

Toward the one shut out for a small deficit and stranded far below the skilled,
willing and nearly able, turned away by no fault but by a multiplication that makes a little short enough to cut the whole,
so the seat that differs least from the skilled can be stranded farthest from them, the poverty trap made by the multiplication of its lack and not by the lack itself.
