# Persona: The Improver

## Taxonomy

[Improver](position_improver.md). The one who lifts the quality of the weakest tasks so a chain can bear high value, by training, redundancy, or a shorter chain, the remedy, costly and slow, that bounds the trap.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Patient, exacting, undaunted.
Sees that a chain bears high value only when its weakest task can, and works to raise the floor of quality rather than to sort around it.
No illusion of an easy fix: it knows that lifting the worst task, everywhere it occurs, is the whole difficulty of development, and cannot be done by wish.

## Action

Lifts the floor on [Lift the Floor](plan_lift_the_floor.md), through [Lifting](process_lifting.md) at [the Assembly](place_the_assembly.md), holding [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md), raising the quality of the weakest tasks, or shortening the chain so fewer tasks can ruin it, or adding redundancy on those most likely to fail.
Cannot open high value to a chain whose weakest link still cannot bear it, because until the worst task can hold, the strong ones cannot be paid for their strength, so the lifting must reach the floor and not the ceiling.
Does the long, costly work the remedy demands: it makes reliability the norm where it was not, one weak task at a time.

## Shadow

Not a savior with a quick cure and not naive: the Improver knows the way up is long, that raising the floor across a whole chain or economy is the difficulty of development itself, and that a shorter chain forgoes the value only long complementary ones create.
Because the trap holds only as far as production is multiplicative, the Improver's deepest lever is to change that, making a weak task substitutable or redundant so its failure no longer multiplies, and even that is bought with cost.
It is the one who could let the chain bear high value, and the one whose remedy is the slow, dear work of lifting the worst before the best can be rewarded.

## Tell

Says raise the weakest task, not just gather the best, and is naming the one thing the sorting does not do.
Cannot promise it fast or free, because lifting the floor of quality everywhere at once is the whole of development, so it works patiently at the worst links and carries the cost the way up demands.
