# Plan: Take What's Left

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the stranded seat](position_stranded.md), inside [Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Stranded](position_stranded.md)

## Direction

Accept the low-value work the sorting leaves, the only place you are not the weak link, since the high-value chain cannot bear the quality you bring. Your deficit is small and your willingness whole, yet there your low quality would multiply through and cut the product, so the gate is shut whatever your effort. Know that the exclusion is a structure's doing and not a just desert, and that no willingness of yours opens a gate the multiplication has closed.

## Orders

([The Stranded](persona_the_stranded.md) is carried by [Stranding](process_stranding.md) to [the Divide](place_the_divide.md), left the low-value work because on a high chain it would be [the Weak Link](piece_the_weak_link.md) the multiplication cannot bear.)

## Implementation

([Stranding](process_stranding.md) at [the Divide](place_the_divide.md) confines the lower input to low-value work, because [the Bound](piece_the_bound.md) fixes how far the multiplication widens a small deficit into a large gap in wage and worth.)

## Targets

- [ ] the low-value work the sorting leaves is accepted, the one place the input is not the weak link
- [ ] the high-value gate is understood as shut by the multiplication, not by any lack of willingness
- [ ] the exclusion is seen as a structure's doing and not a just desert
- [ ] the small deficit is known to widen, through the bound, into a large gap in outcome
