# Plot: Shut Out

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Divide](place_the_divide.md), where a worker willing to work, but of lower quality, is turned from the high-value chain.

## Action

[The Stranded](persona_the_stranded.md), from [the stranded's seat](position_stranded.md), runs [Take What's Left](plan_take_whats_left.md) through [Stranding](process_stranding.md), meeting [The Amplifier](piece_the_amplifier.md): shut out of high-value production because admitting it would make it the weak link and cut the whole chain's value, it is confined instead to low-value work, and a small gap in skill widens, through the chain it is barred from, into a vast gap in wage and worth. The scene closes with the low-skill stranded at the low pole, held there not by any single decision but by the structure that keeps producing the same divide.

## Stage

The low-quality are excluded from high-value production because that production needs every task done well, and small skill differences are amplified through the chain into large differences in outcome (Kremer). This is not the Brittle Optimum: no buffer was trimmed out of the chain and no shock struck it. Nothing needs to break here. The sensitivity to the weakest task is inherent in a product of qualities, present the moment the chain is built this way, waiting for no accident to reveal it.

## Tension

A small difference in skill becomes a vast difference in a life, and no one did wrong, at any single point, to make it so.
