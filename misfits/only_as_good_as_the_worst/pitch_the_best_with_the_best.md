# Pitch: The Best with the Best

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reasonable and meritocratic.
A firm hiring the best it can, skilled people working with other skilled people, each choosing sensibly and no one acting from malice.
Nothing false in it: a firm doing exacting work is right to want every task done well, and skilled workers are genuinely worth most together.

## Undertow

Under the merit, a multiplication that makes one weak link ruinous.
The value of a product is the product of its tasks' qualities, not their sum, so a single low-quality task cuts the whole however good the rest, as one faulty seal destroyed the shuttle (Kremer).
No snobbery and no cruelty: because a skilled worker is worth most beside peers and little beside a weak link, firms match skill with skill and low with low, the value-maximising response to a chain where the worst task caps the whole (Kremer; Becker).

## Nerve

The plainness of the best working with the best, and the catch that the same sorting shuts the rest out:
that the multiplication which makes quality so valuable in company with quality is what makes one low quality ruinous, the reward of skill together and the exclusion of the unskilled the one structure seen twice,
so a small difference in skill becomes a vast difference in wage and worth, and the low-skill are stranded out of high-value work that cannot risk a weak link.

## Echo

After the run: the skilled richly rewarded on chains of their peers, the low-skill confined to low-value work, a small gap in skill widened into a vast gap in life, a poverty trap authored by no one (Kremer).
And the way out, long and costly: raise the quality of the weakest tasks, shorten the chain, or add redundancy, each spending real cost, and lifting the floor across a whole chain the whole difficulty of development.
And the bound held in view, because the trap is as strong as the tasks are complementary and no stronger: where a weak task can be substituted, backed up, or made to matter only additively, its damage no longer multiplies, so the sorting softens and the misfit is real for complementary chains and not a law of all production.
