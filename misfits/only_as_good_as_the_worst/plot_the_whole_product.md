# Plot: The Whole Product

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Assembly](place_the_assembly.md), a product built of many complementary tasks, each one necessary and none able to stand in for another.

## Action

[Multiplying](process_multiplying.md): [The Skilled](persona_the_skilled.md) does its task well, and every other task in the chain does its own well beside it, and together they combine into [The Product](piece_the_product.md). Its value is not the sum of the tasks' qualities but their product: excellence in nine tasks and mediocrity in one does not average out, it multiplies through. The scene closes on a high value, the kind a long chain of tasks all done well can reach and no lesser chain can.

## Stage

Production here is multiplicative: value is the product of the tasks' qualities, not their sum (Kremer). Under a product, a low quality anywhere in the chain cuts the whole in proportion to how low it is, not merely by its own small share. Nothing has failed yet in this scene. The chain is simply built the way such chains are built, and that construction is already the whole of the trap.

## Tension

A great value is raised by many hands working well together, and any one of those hands, weak in only its own task, can undo what all the others built.
