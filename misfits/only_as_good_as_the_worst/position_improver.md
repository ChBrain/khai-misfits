# Position: Improver

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The seat that lifts the quality of the weakest tasks so a chain can bear high value, by training, redundancy, or a shorter chain, the remedy, costly and slow, that bounds the trap.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one lever the sorting lacks: to raise the floor of quality rather than to match around it, lifting the weakest tasks so the chain can hold high value.
A set of costly ways: training that makes reliability the norm, redundancy on the tasks most likely to fail, or a shorter chain in which fewer tasks can each ruin the whole.
No quick or free version, because lifting the worst task everywhere it occurs is the difficulty of development itself, and until the weakest link can bear high value none of the strong ones can be paid for their strength.

## Orders

Lift the floor.
Raise the quality of the weakest tasks, not merely gather the best, since a chain bears high value only when its worst task can, and the sorting that clusters the skilled does nothing for the stranded.
Accept the cost and the time, shorten the chain or add redundancy where you can, and know that the deepest fix is to make a weak task matter less, substitutable or backed up, so its failure no longer multiplies.

## Loses

The ease of a quick cure, spent on the long work of raising the worst.
Because the trap holds as far as production is multiplicative, the seat cannot lift the low by wish but only by the slow, dear work of making reliability the norm or the chain shorter, and a shorter chain forgoes the value only long complementary ones create.
It keeps the one real remedy and loses any shortcut, the one who could let the chain bear high value and must pay the whole cost of development to do it.

## Drives

Toward the one whose remedy is to lift the worst before the best can be rewarded,
raising the floor of quality against the whole difficulty of development, no quick cure and no illusion of one,
so the seat that could open high value to a rising chain is the seat whose fix is the long, costly lifting of the weakest link, the bound on the trap bought at the price of the work.
