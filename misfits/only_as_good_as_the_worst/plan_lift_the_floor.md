# Plan: Lift the Floor

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the improver seat](position_improver.md), inside [Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Improver](position_improver.md)

## Direction

Raise the quality of the weakest tasks, not merely gather the best, since a chain bears high value only when its worst task can, and the sorting that clusters the skilled does nothing for the stranded. Accept the cost and the time. Train reliability into the norm, back up the tasks most likely to fail, or shorten the chain so fewer tasks can each ruin the whole. Know that the deepest fix is to make a weak task matter less, substitutable or redundant, so its failure no longer multiplies, and that this remedy, costly and slow, is the one thing that bounds the trap.

## Orders

([The Improver](persona_the_improver.md) works through [Lifting](process_lifting.md) at [the Assembly](place_the_assembly.md), raising the weakest tasks so [the Chain](piece_the_chain.md) can bear high value and the low are no longer stranded across [the Divide](place_the_divide.md).)

## Implementation

([Lifting](process_lifting.md) at [the Assembly](place_the_assembly.md) raises the floor of quality against the whole difficulty of development, because only when the worst task can bear high value does [the Chain](piece_the_chain.md) hold, and the cost of that lifting is what makes the remedy slow and dear.)

## Targets

- [ ] the weakest tasks are raised, not merely the best gathered
- [ ] the cost and time are accepted, through training, redundancy, or a shorter chain
- [ ] the deepest fix is made to matter, letting a weak task fail without multiplying through
- [ ] the remedy is understood as the one bound on the trap, bought at the price of the work
