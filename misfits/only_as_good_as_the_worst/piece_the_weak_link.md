# Piece: The Weak Link

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The single low-quality task that cuts the whole, wherever in the chain it falls.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Found anywhere in [The Chain](piece_the_chain.md) at [The Assembly](place_the_assembly.md), and turned away at [The Gate](place_the_gate.md), where [The Firm](persona_the_firm.md) sorts inputs by quality before they can enter a chain at all.

## Load Bearing

Since the value of the product is a product of qualities, the lowest-quality task sets a ceiling on the whole: one weak link ruins an otherwise excellent chain of work (Kremer). This is why a single faulty O-ring seal, one component among thousands built to standard, destroyed the entire Challenger: not because it was the largest or costliest part, but because its failure multiplied through everything built around it. Guard against reading the weak link as a small or local fault, a flaw contained to its own corner of the chain: in multiplicative production its damage is never local, it multiplies through the entire product and lands on every task done well beside it.

## Apparent

One small flaw among many good parts, easily thought minor and easily overlooked.
