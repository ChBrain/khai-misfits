# Position: Firm

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The seat of the one who builds high-value output that needs every task done well, and so matches skill with skill, admitting only high-quality inputs and turning away any that would be the weak link.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Work whose value multiplies: a chain of tasks whose qualities combine as a product, so the seat needs every task done well and cannot let one spoil the whole.
A sound reason to sort: a skilled input adds far more beside peers than beside a weak link, so pairing high with high maximises the value it can make.
No way to carry a weak link on a high-value chain, because there its low quality would multiply through and cut the product, wasting the excellence around it.

## Orders

Keep no weak links.
Admit only inputs whose quality the chain can bear, and match skill with skill, because on multiplicative work the worst task caps the whole and a poor one placed anywhere ruins it.
Leave the lower quality to lesser chains, not from disdain but because that is where a weak link does less damage and where its quality is worth what it is.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and yet its sorting strands the low.
Because the value multiplies and one weak link would cut the whole, the seat cannot open its chain to the low-skill without wasting all the excellent work on it, so it must turn them away, and the exclusion it makes is real though it intends only to protect the product.
It keeps its high value and loses no cause for blame, the maker of the best the gate that shuts the rest out.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound refusal strands the low-skill,
matching skill with skill because the multiplication makes one weak link ruinous, no snobbery in it and no malice,
so the seat that builds high value is the seat whose value-maximising sort leaves the low pole to the low, the exclusion a structure's doing and not its own.
