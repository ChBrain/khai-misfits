# Piece: The Match

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). The positive assortative matching of quality with quality: high paired with high, low with low.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Made at [The Gate](place_the_gate.md), where inputs are sorted by quality before they are allowed to join a chain, and sought by [The Skilled](persona_the_skilled.md) as the surest route to a high-value chain.

## Load Bearing

Because quality multiplies, a skilled worker adds far more to a chain of other skilled workers than to a chain with a weak link, so firms match high with high and low with low, and this sorting maximises total output across the economy (Kremer). It extends what Becker showed for marriage: that complements sort positively, the high paired with the high and the low with the low, because a good match multiplies the value each partner brings rather than merely adding it (Becker). Guard against reading the sorting as snobbery, taste, or exclusion pursued for its own sake: it is the value-maximising response to a production function where the worst task caps the whole, not a preference that could be reasoned away.

## Apparent

Just firms hiring the best they can afford, or like keeping company with like.
