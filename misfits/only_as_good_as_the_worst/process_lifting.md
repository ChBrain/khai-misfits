# Process: Lifting

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). Borne at [The Assembly](place_the_assembly.md), precipitated by [The Improver](persona_the_improver.md) raising the weakest tasks toward [The Bound](piece_the_bound.md), in [The Long Way Up](plot_the_long_way_up.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The recognition that a chain can bear high value only when its weakest task can.

## Direction

From a chain that cannot sustain high value toward one that can, by lifting the quality of the weakest tasks, shortening the chain, or adding redundancy.

## Lever

Raising every weak link is costly and hard, and the bound holds only as far as production is multiplicative, softening where tasks are substitutable or redundant (Kremer).
