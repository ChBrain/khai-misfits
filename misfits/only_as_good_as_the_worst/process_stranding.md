# Process: Stranding

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md). Borne at [The Divide](place_the_divide.md), precipitated by [The Stranded](persona_the_stranded.md) being refused the high-value chain, [The Amplifier](piece_the_amplifier.md), in [Shut Out](plot_shut_out.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A low-quality input that would cut the product being excluded from it.

## Direction

From a worker who could contribute toward one confined to low-value work, and from a small gap in skill toward a large gap in wage and worth.

## Lever

Because a low quality would multiply through and cut the whole, the low-skill are stranded out of high-value production, and small skill differences are amplified into large outcome differences (Kremer).
