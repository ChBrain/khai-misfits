# Plot: One Bad Seal

## Taxonomy

[Only as Good as the Worst](play_only_as_good_as_the_worst.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Assembly](place_the_assembly.md), the same chain of tasks, one of them now done poorly among many still done well.

## Action

Through [Multiplying](process_multiplying.md), [The Weak Link](piece_the_weak_link.md) cuts [The Product](piece_the_product.md): the single low-quality task lowers the value of all the excellent work standing around it, not by its own small weight but by multiplying against every other quality in the chain. It is named for the Challenger, whose one faulty O-ring seal did not merely underperform its own function; it destroyed the whole shuttle. The scene closes on a product otherwise excellent, ruined by its worst part.

## Stage

Because the value is a product, the lowest-quality task sets a ceiling on the whole chain, and its damage does not stay local to its own task, it multiplies through every task beside it (Kremer). This is not the Bus Factor: no irreplaceable person is lost here, nothing depends on one person's uncopied knowledge. Any task done poorly is the weak link, whoever performs it and however replaceable they are.

## Tension

The flaw is small and local, one seal among thousands of parts, and its damage is neither: it reaches the whole and it stops nowhere short of it.
