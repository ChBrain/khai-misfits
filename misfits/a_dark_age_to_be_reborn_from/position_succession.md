# Position: Succession

## Taxonomy

[A Dark Age to Be Reborn From](play_a_dark_age_to_be_reborn_from.md). The seat of whoever comes next: the only party positioned to name the period, and the one party built out of the difference.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A case about its own moment, argued in public with reasons.
The vantage point, which nobody standing inside the period ever has.
An account of itself that requires the predecessor to have been a particular way.

## Orders

Declare a break.
Name what it broke from.
Date it.

## Loses

Nothing to any dishonesty, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.
**A movement cannot call itself a rebirth without asserting a death**, so this seat cannot be indifferent to how the predecessor comes out, and no motive to deceive is needed anywhere: the interest is structural and would be present in the most scrupulous occupant.
It ends having described somebody else in the terms it needed for itself.

## Drives

Toward an account of what has just begun,
which is a legitimate thing to want and to argue,
so the seat that alone can name the period is the seat that cannot be neutral about it.
