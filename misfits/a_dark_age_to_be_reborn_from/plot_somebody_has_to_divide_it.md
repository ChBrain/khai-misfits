# Plot: Somebody Has to Divide It

## Taxonomy

[A Dark Age to Be Reborn From](play_a_dark_age_to_be_reborn_from.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A past too long to hold in a sentence.
A syllabus with twelve weeks in it.

## Action

[Dividing](process_dividing.md): a line is drawn across time at [the break](place_the_break.md), with a name on each side of it.

## Stage

[The Long Middle](place_the_long_middle.md) becomes a period, teachable and citable, which it was not before.

## Tension

This plot's work is to establish that the cutting is **necessary**, because a play that opens by calling periodisation a mistake has nothing to say about anything that follows.
Without periods there is no history, only a list. The divisions carry real change: something did end and something did begin, and saying so is the work rather than a failure of it. The medievalist who spent a career objecting to how this particular cut was made answered his own title question with a **yes**.
So the production is not asking anybody to stop, and could not: **the alternative to a bad division is a different division**, never none, which is the constraint every later plot runs inside.
