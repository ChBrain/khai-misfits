# Place: The Break

## Taxonomy

[A Dark Age to Be Reborn From](play_a_dark_age_to_be_reborn_from.md). Where a movement declares itself new, and names what it broke from in the same breath.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Somebody making a case about their own moment, in public, with reasons.
A line drawn across time, and a word written on the far side of it.

## Holds

The play's quietest fact: **nothing here is deceit.**
The case is argued rather than smuggled, the argument is a real argument, and the man who reached for darkness said what he was doing and why. A production that opens by calling this propaganda has nothing to say about the four centuries that follow.

## Offers

A vantage point, which is genuinely required: nobody standing inside a period can name it.

## Withheld

Any namer without a stake.
The vantage is reserved by construction to somebody standing after the cut, whose account of themselves is built out of the difference, so the room contains no disinterested occupant and never could.
