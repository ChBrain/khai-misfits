## Tension

Here the interest appears, and the plot's difficulty is that it is not a motive to condemn.
Nothing at [the break](place_the_break.md) is deceit. The case is argued rather than smuggled, and the man who reached for darkness said what he was doing and why. But **a movement cannot call itself a rebirth without asserting a death**, so the successor's account of itself contains a claim about the predecessor by construction.
Which makes the interest **structural**: a scrupulous occupant of that seat has exactly the same one as a careless occupant, and there is no disinterested namer to appoint instead, because the vantage point required to name a period belongs only to somebody standing after the cut, and standing after the cut is what supplies the interest. **There is nothing neutral about cutting time into parts.**
