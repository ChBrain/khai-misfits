# Piece: The Fourth Moment

## Taxonomy

[A Dark Age to Be Reborn From](play_a_dark_age_to_be_reborn_from.md). Retrospective significance: the last of the four points at which the production of history admits a silence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

After the sources, after the archive and after the narrative.
Named in the study of how history is produced, and located exactly where this play sits.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play cannot say which part of the process it is about, and the answer matters.
It is load bearing because the earlier moments, the making of sources and the making of archives, are where a record is lost, and **this play needs the record kept**: the silence it stages enters at the point where significance is assigned, which is the one moment a complete archive does not protect.

## Apparent

A refinement of the same idea, so that suppression and framing are two versions of one complaint.
They are not, and the decomposition is what shows it: the first two moments run on what survives, the fourth runs on what a surviving thing is taken to mean, and switching off the first two leaves the fourth exactly where it was.

## Yearbook

Named in the literature on the production of history, where the silences are described as some unconscious and others quite deliberate.
It is the play's own location, borrowed from the field that mapped it.
