# Process: Naming

## Taxonomy

[A Dark Age to Be Reborn From](play_a_dark_age_to_be_reborn_from.md). Choosing the word for the other side of the line, by the party standing on this side.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A movement with a case about its own moment.
At [the break](place_the_break.md), while the case is being made.

## Direction

Toward a word that carries the argument.
**There is nothing neutral about cutting time into parts**, and the name is where the cut states what it means: a character, a boundary and a verdict, asserted together.
And the party choosing is the party whose own account is built out of the difference, which requires no dishonesty and does not go away when the namer is scrupulous.

## Lever

The vantage point, which only a successor has, and which comes with an interest attached.
Nobody inside a period can name it, so the naming is reserved, structurally, to somebody who cannot be indifferent.

## Echo

Darkness, chosen in the fourteenth century,
by a man arguing that something had ended,
and needing both halves of the argument.
