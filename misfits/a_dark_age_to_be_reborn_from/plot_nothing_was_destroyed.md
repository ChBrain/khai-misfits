## Tension

This plot exists to remove the reading that would otherwise absorb the whole play.
The usual account of a maligned period is that the record was lost, suppressed or burned, and that the surviving version is the one somebody wanted. **That account is unavailable here.** Nothing was destroyed. The medieval archive is enormous, it survived, it was catalogued by people who valued it, and nothing is missing that would settle the question.
So the distortion cannot be a selection on the evidence and must be a frame applied to all of it. **A name decides what is taken down and not what is on the shelf**, and a perfect record offers no resistance whatever to that: the pattern of wear on the catalogue is a map of the label rather than of the period.
