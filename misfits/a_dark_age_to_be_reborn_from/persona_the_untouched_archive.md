# Persona: The Untouched Archive

## Taxonomy

[Record](position_record.md). The archive itself: enormous, intact, and available the entire time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not a ruin and not a remnant.
Chronicles, charters, cartularies, letters, accounts, fabric, glass, law and music, in quantities nobody has finished working through.
Everything anybody would need to answer the question.

## Action

Survives.
Waits.
Answers whatever it is actually asked.

## Shadow

That being complete protects it from nothing.
**No victor suppressed this and no fire took it**, so the usual explanation is unavailable, and the frame did its work without needing a single document to be missing: a name decides what gets taken down from a shelf, not what is on it.
It ends fully preserved and unevenly read.

## Tell

The catalogue is longer than anybody's reading life.
Which parts of it are worn is a map of the label.
