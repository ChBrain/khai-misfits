# Pitch: The Age That Named You

## Taxonomy

[A Dark Age to Be Reborn From](play_a_dark_age_to_be_reborn_from.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry, precise, and slightly unfashionable.
The register of a historian at the end of a seminar, talking to people who already know the period.
Willing to name who said what and when, and unwilling to let a good story stand in for a citation.

## Undertow

Under the syllabus, two facts that do not negotiate.
History has to be cut into periods before it can be written or taught at all.
The cutting is done by people standing on one side of the cut, whose account of themselves is built out of the difference.

## Nerve

The unease of an intact archive.
Nothing was burned, nothing was suppressed, everything needed to settle it survived and is catalogued,
and a word chosen in the fourteenth century decided for four hundred years which parts of it anybody took down.

## Echo

After the run: a thousand years under one heading picked to serve an argument about a different century,
a literature that honestly agrees with the heading, having read what the heading selected,
and a correction that works, one monograph at a time, made by somebody who also came after.
