# Piece: The Thousand Years

## Taxonomy

[A Dark Age to Be Reborn From](play_a_dark_age_to_be_reborn_from.md). The span the word covers, which is longer than the era that chose the word.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the long middle](place_the_long_middle.md), from one end of it to the other.
Covered by a single heading, chosen at one moment near its close.

## Load Bearing

Without it the label is a rough characterisation and roughness is forgivable.
It is load bearing because of the disproportion: **no single word could be true of a thousand years**, so the question of fit was never the criterion, and the word was selected to do work in an argument about a different century entirely.

## Apparent

A necessary simplification, which every period name is.
Every period name is, and that is the play's point rather than its exception: the simplification is unavoidable and the choosing of it is not neutral, so the two facts have to be held together instead of one excusing the other.

## Yearbook

Spanned by a heading, taught in twelve weeks, worn into every general account.
It is why lengthening the middle and moving the boundaries was real work and not pedantry.
