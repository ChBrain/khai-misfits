# Persona: The Name It Gave

## Taxonomy

[Priority](position_priority.md). The word for the predecessor: chosen at the break, before anybody read the shelf.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

One word, doing a great deal of work.
It fixes a character, a boundary and a verdict at once, and it does all three before any of them has been examined.
Cheap to say, and afterwards very expensive to move.

## Action

Arrives.
Sets the terms the period will be examined under.
Stays, long after the people who chose it.

## Shadow

That its priority is the whole of its power.
It is not defended by evidence and does not need to be, because it is in place before the evidence is gathered and it decides which evidence anybody gathers.
It ends outliving both the argument that produced it and the profession that abandoned it.

## Tell

Everybody outside the field still uses it.
Everybody inside the field stopped, and it made no difference.
