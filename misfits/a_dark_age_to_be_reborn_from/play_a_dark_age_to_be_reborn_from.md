# Play: A Dark Age to Be Reborn From

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

A Dark Age to Be Reborn From.
Nobody burned the medieval archive. It is enormous, it survived, and it sat on the shelf the whole time.
What happened instead is that the people who came next needed a word for what they were not, and they chose darkness, and the word arrived before anybody had read the shelf.
A movement cannot call itself a rebirth without asserting a death. So the death got asserted, by the only party with a reason to assert it, and then it decided for four centuries which parts of the shelf anybody bothered to take down.

## Arc

The bend is this: an era's name, boundaries and character are supplied by whoever comes after it, and whoever comes after it has an identity that depends on the contrast, so the frame is authored by the one party that cannot be indifferent to it.
It runs on two facts, neither of them anybody's doing: history has to be cut into periods before it can be written or taught at all; and the cutting is done by people standing on one side of the cut.
Take the dividing first, because the play has to establish that it is **necessary**. Without periods there is no history, only a list. The medievalist who spent a career objecting to how the cut was made still answered his own title question with a yes: the divisions are needed, they carry real change, and the argument was never that we should stop.
Then the fact that inverts it. **There is nothing neutral about cutting time into parts.** The cut is an assertion about what changed and when, and it is made by somebody positioned after it, whose account of themselves is built out of the difference. Petrarch reached for darkness because he was arguing that something had ended and something had begun, and the argument required both halves.
Then the priority, which is what makes this a structure and not an opinion. **The name arrives before the reading.** It is set at the moment of the break, by people making a claim about their own moment, and everything the period is then examined for is examined under it.
Then the routing, which is where the damage compounds. The label decides what is studied, what is funded, what is taught, what is catalogued and what is thought worth keeping. A period called dark is a period whose brightness nobody is paid to look for. So a century later the literature agrees with the label, and it agrees **using material the label selected**.
And nothing was destroyed, which is the whole point and is what separates this from the ordinary complaint. **The archive is complete.** No victor suppressed it, no fire took it, nothing is missing that would settle the question. The distortion sits entirely in the frame, and a frame survives an intact record without difficulty.
Reach for a settlement and the ones that work are slow and partial. The period can be renamed, and historians largely have: the term is out of professional use and is still doing its work in every syllabus and museum label outside the profession. The frame can be argued with, which is a career's work against four hundred years of accumulated selection. **And whoever renames it will be somebody who comes after, with an account of themselves that needs the renaming to come out a particular way**, which is not a reason to stop and is a reason to notice.

## Stakes

Whether a period can be described by anybody who is not standing after it.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether Petrarch was dishonest, because he was making an argument and said so, and not whether the Middle Ages were good or bad, because the play takes no position on that and does not need one, but whether the division of time, which nobody can do without, can be performed by a party with no stake in where the line falls. The production cannot find one. It can only show that the cutting is necessary, that there is nothing neutral in it, that the namer is always positioned after the cut and built out of the difference, that the name arrives before the reading and then decides what gets read, that the record was never touched and the frame did not need it to be, and that every correction will be made by another successor with an account of themselves to keep.
