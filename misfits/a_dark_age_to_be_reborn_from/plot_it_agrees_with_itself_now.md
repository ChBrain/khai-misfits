There is no fraud and no motivated reasoning: the evidence is real and the conclusion follows from it. And the material reached the desk through four centuries of allocations made under a heading, so **the frame is confirmed by work that could not have disconfirmed it** without first having been funded to look somewhere else. The agreement is genuine and settles nothing.
Then the correction, which is real and partial. The profession largely abandoned the term, argued the continuities, moved the boundaries and lengthened the middle, and none of it was wasted. The word is still in every museum label and general syllabus outside the profession, which is where the routing actually happens, and **the frame was set in an afternoon and is unset one monograph at a time.**
And whoever finally renames it will be somebody who comes after, with an account of themselves that needs the renaming to come out a particular way. That is not a reason to stop. It is the reason there is no seat in this play from which the period can be described by anybody with nothing at stake.
