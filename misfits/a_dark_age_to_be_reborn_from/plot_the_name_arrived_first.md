## Tension

This is the hinge, and it is a claim about sequence rather than about content.
The word is **prior rather than derived**. It is in place before the evidence is gathered and it decides which evidence anybody gathers, so it is never put to a test it could fail: four centuries of allocation intervene between the naming and any examination general enough to challenge it.
And the allocation is where the damage compounds without a decision to blame. Somebody has to choose what fills twelve weeks, what the fund pays for and what goes on the wall, and every one of those choices is defensible on the merits as understood. **The merits as understood are the ones the name supplied.** A period called dark is a period whose brightness nobody is paid to look for, so no censor is required and none exists.
