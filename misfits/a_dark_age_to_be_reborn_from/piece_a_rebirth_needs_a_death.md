## Load Bearing

Without it the naming is arbitrary and could as easily have been generous.
It is load bearing because it makes the interest **structural rather than personal**: the successor's account of itself contains a claim about the predecessor by construction, so the party doing the naming cannot be indifferent to how it comes out, and a scrupulous occupant of that seat has exactly the same interest as a careless one.

## Apparent

A motive, and therefore something to be corrected by finding an honest namer.
There is no disinterested namer available. The vantage point required to name a period belongs only to somebody standing after the cut, and standing after the cut is what supplies the interest.

## Yearbook
