# Process: Dividing

## Taxonomy

[A Dark Age to Be Reborn From](play_a_dark_age_to_be_reborn_from.md). Cutting time into periods: unavoidable, and the thing the play does not ask anybody to stop.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The need to write or teach any history at all.
At [the break](place_the_break.md), and in every syllabus since.

## Direction

Toward a past that can be held in a sentence.
Without periods there is no history, only a list, and the divisions carry real change: something did end and something did begin, and saying so is the work rather than a failure of it.
The medievalist who spent a career objecting to how this particular cut was made answered his own title question with a **yes**.

## Lever

A line drawn across time, with a name on each side.
Cheap to draw, and afterwards very expensive to move.

## Echo

A period, teachable in twelve weeks,
and a claim about what changed, which is what a period is.
