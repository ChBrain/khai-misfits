# Process: Routing

## Taxonomy

[A Dark Age to Be Reborn From](play_a_dark_age_to_be_reborn_from.md). The label deciding what gets studied, funded, taught, catalogued and kept.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Finite time, finite money and finite wall.
At [the shelf](place_the_shelf.md) and in every allocation made since the name arrived.

## Direction

Toward whatever the heading suggests is worth the effort.
Each allocation is made by a competent person on the merits as they understand them, and the merits as understood are the ones the name supplied, so **no censor is required and none exists.**
A period called dark is a period whose brightness nobody is paid to look for.

## Lever

Reasonableness, applied a hundred thousand times under an unexamined heading.
It compounds, because next year's selection is made from what last year's produced.

## Echo

A worn part of the catalogue and an unworn part,
which together are a map of the label
rather than of the period.
