# Persona: The Rebirth

## Taxonomy

[Succession](position_succession.md). The movement that comes next and defines itself as a beginning.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Genuinely new, and genuinely arguing for it.
Not a propagandist and not a liar: somebody with a case about their own moment, making it in public, with reasons.
The case is that something has ended and something has begun.

## Action

Declares a break.
Names what it broke from.
Dates it.

## Shadow

That the case cannot be made without the other half.
**A movement cannot call itself a rebirth without asserting a death**, so the party supplying the predecessor's character is the one party whose own account depends on how that character comes out, and no dishonesty is required anywhere in it.
It ends having described somebody else in terms it needed for itself.

## Tell

Says what it is.
Which is a sentence about what it is not.
