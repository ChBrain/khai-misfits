# Persona: The Reading List

## Taxonomy

[Curriculum](position_curriculum.md). What the name routes attention to: the syllabus, the grant, the gallery, the shelf people actually take down.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary academic and institutional life, working correctly.
Somebody has to choose what is taught in the twelve weeks there are, what the fund pays for, what the museum displays and what the press commissions.
Every one of those choices is made by a competent person on the merits as they understand them.

## Action

Selects.
Selects again next year, from what last year produced.
Compounds.

## Shadow

That the merits as understood are the ones the name supplied.
**A period called dark is a period whose brightness nobody is paid to look for**, so the selection is not censorship and needs no censor: it is a long series of reasonable allocations, each made under a heading nobody chose to examine.
It ends having produced a literature shaped like its own title.

## Tell

Nobody ever decided not to study it.
There was simply always something else with a better case.
