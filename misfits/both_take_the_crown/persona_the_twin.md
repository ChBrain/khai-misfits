# Persona: The Twin

## Taxonomy

[Twin](position_twin.md). The node on the far side of the partition, alive and reasoning identically, that promotes itself too, so its correctness mirrors the Isolated's into two heads.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The very image of the other, healthy and correct.
A live node on the far side of the break, as capable and as sensible, doing precisely what the first does and for precisely the same reasons.
No difference in it that matters: it is the Isolated seen from the other side of the wire, reasoning the same and just as right.

## Action

Faces the same [Silence](piece_the_silence.md) and, through [Promoting](process_promoting.md) at [the Cluster](place_the_cluster.md), crowns itself to keep serving, exactly as the Isolated does, so together they raise [the Two Heads](piece_the_two_heads.md).
Cannot see the node it mirrors, so its identical reasoning runs blind to the twin it completes.
Does the same right thing: it promotes rather than freeze, and its correctness, matched to the other's, is what makes two primaries out of one rule.

## Shadow

Not the wrong one and not the copy to be blamed: it is as correct as the Isolated, and which of the two is called the twin is only a matter of where you stand.
Because two nodes doing the identical right thing is exactly what makes two heads, its rightness does not avert the split but completes it, the symmetry invisible from inside either side.
It is the mirror that proves the point: not one node erring, but two nodes correct, and that is enough to fork the state.

## Tell

Also reports itself primary and healthy, the same claim from the other side.
Cannot see the contradiction, because the wire is down between them, so both crowns are worn in good faith, each certain and each the other's unseen match.
