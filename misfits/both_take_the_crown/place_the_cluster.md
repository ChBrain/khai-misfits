# Place: The Cluster

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md). The replicated system meant to hold one shared state and agree on it. Hosts [Two Primaries](plot_two_primaries.md), [Two Truths](plot_two_truths.md), and [Pick Two, Get One](plot_pick_two_get_one.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A set of replicas serving together, one primary taking writes and the rest following, built to stay both up and correct.

## Holds

[The Two Heads](piece_the_two_heads.md) once the sides promote, [The Fork](piece_the_fork.md) once the writes diverge, and [The Trade](piece_the_trade.md) that stands over it all as a law.

## Offers

To its clients, one state to read and write, and the appearance of a single system however many nodes it runs on.

## Withheld

The guarantee that it stays single under a partition: the moment the wire splits, the one state it presents can quietly become two.
