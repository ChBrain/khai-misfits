# Plan: Write to the Primary

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the writer's seat](position_writer.md), inside [Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Writer](position_writer.md)

## Direction

Send your writes to the primary that answers you, and trust the acknowledgement it returns. This is exactly how the system is meant to be used: address the leader, record your write, move on when it confirms. Do not check for a second primary, because from outside you cannot see one, so you write to whichever head responds and take its confirmation as the truth recorded, unaware that another head may be recording a different one.

## Orders

([The Writer](persona_the_writer.md) sends its writes through [Forking](process_forking.md) at [the Cluster](place_the_cluster.md) to whichever primary answers, and both answer.)

## Implementation

([The Cluster](place_the_cluster.md) is where the writes land; [Forking](process_forking.md) records each on the head that received it, and because both heads accept and no channel joins them, the client's correct writes split across two histories into [the Fork](piece_the_fork.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] writes are sent to whichever primary answers, as the system intends
- [ ] the acknowledgement is trusted as the truth recorded
- [ ] both primaries answer, so writes land on both heads
- [ ] the correct writes split across two histories, each real on its side and absent from the other
