# Process: Fencing

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md). Borne at [The Blueprint](place_the_blueprint.md), precipitated by [The Architect](persona_the_architect.md) imposing a majority rule that stands the minority down, in [Fence It Off](plot_fence_it_off.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The wish to keep the state single, once the forced trade is understood.

## Direction

From two sides both serving toward one side serving, the minority refusing writes so at most one primary acts.

## Lever

That a quorum lets only the majority act and a fencing token turns away a stale primary's writes, so consistency is bought by standing the minority down, at the cost of its availability (Brewer; Lamport).

## Echo

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the fork prevented where a majority holds and the price of it paid in availability.
