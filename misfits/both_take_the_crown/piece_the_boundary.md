# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md). The remedy and the condition, the quorum that prevents two heads and the edges where the trap does not bite.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Weighed at [The Blueprint](place_the_blueprint.md), where the architect sets the rule: a quorum that lets only the majority act, a fencing token that turns away a stale primary, and the conditions under which no such guard is needed (Brewer; Gilbert and Lynch; Lamport).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overreaches into a claim that distributed systems cannot work, which is false: the boundary is what keeps the trap honest and the remedy in view.
It carries the condition and the escape. Preventing the fork means taking the consistency horn, a quorum that stands the minority down or a fencing token that rejects its writes, buying single state with lost availability; and the trap needs a partition and a state that must agree, so it lifts where the network holds, a majority rule is imposed, or the workload can reconcile divergent writes later.

## Apparent

A pile of caveats and knobs, easy to read as fine print, when it is the line that says exactly what the fork costs to prevent and where it never threatened at all.
