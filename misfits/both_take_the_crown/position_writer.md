# Position: Writer

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md). The seat of the client that writes to whichever primary answers, so its writes land on both heads and the state forks.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Writes to make, and a system that is supposed to accept them.
A primary to send them to, and no way, from outside, to know there are now two.
No sight of the partition, so it addresses whichever primary answers and takes the acknowledgement as the truth recorded.

## Orders

Write to the primary, and trust the acknowledgement.
Send your writes to the leader that answers you, since that is exactly how the system is meant to be used, and move on when it confirms.
Accept what the seat cannot see: that the leader answering you may be one of two, so your correct write may be recorded on one history and never the other.

## Loses

The guarantee that its write is the state.
Because it writes to whichever primary answers and both do, its correct writes are split across two histories, so what it loses is the assurance that what it recorded is the single truth, and it loses it by using the system exactly as intended.
It keeps its acknowledgements and loses their singularity, each write real on its side and absent from the other.

## Drives

Toward the client whose ordinary writes become the fork,
sending them to whichever head answers and, by writing to both across the split, diverging the state,
so the seat that only used the system as designed is the seat through which the two heads become two truths, correct and split at once.
