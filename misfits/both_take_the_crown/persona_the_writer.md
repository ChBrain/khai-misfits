# Persona: The Writer

## Taxonomy

[Writer](position_writer.md). The client that writes to whichever primary answers, so its writes land on both heads and the state forks.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, trusting, just using the system.
Has writes to make and a service to make them to, and sends them to the leader that answers, taking the acknowledgement as proof the truth is recorded.
No fault in it: it uses the cluster exactly as designed, writing to the primary and trusting the confirmation.

## Action

Runs [Write to the Primary](plan_write_to_the_primary.md), and through [Forking](process_forking.md) at [the Cluster](place_the_cluster.md), sends its writes to whichever primary answers, and both answer, so [the Fork](piece_the_fork.md) opens.
Cannot see that there are now two primaries, so it addresses whichever responds and trusts the acknowledgement it gets back.
Does what any client does: it writes to the leader and moves on, and its correct writes, split across two heads, become two truths.

## Shadow

Not careless and not to blame: writing to the primary that answers is exactly right, and the confirmation it trusts is genuinely given.
Because both primaries answer and record, its ordinary writes are split across two histories, so the very correctness of its use is what diverges the state, each write real on its side and missing from the other.
It is the client that did nothing wrong, and the client through whom the two heads become two truths.

## Tell

Gets its acknowledgements and trusts them, from whichever side it reached.
Cannot tell that another write, to the other head, has recorded a different truth, so it carries on confident its data is safe, right on its side and split without knowing it.
