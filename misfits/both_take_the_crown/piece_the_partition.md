# Piece: The Partition

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md). The network split, one group of nodes unable to reach another though every node is alive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Opening at [The Link](place_the_link.md): a failed path between two groups of nodes, so each group can reach itself and not the other, every node up and working and simply out of touch across the break (Gilbert and Lynch).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no trap: with the network whole, one primary is reachable by all and the cluster stays single, so the partition is the precipitating fact the whole misfit stands on.
It bears the load as a fact of networks, not a fault of nodes. Because links do fail while the machines they join keep running, the partition creates a live cluster with a hole through the middle, two halves alive and unable to coordinate, and everything after follows from that.

## Apparent

A blip: from outside it can look like a momentary glitch, a node gone quiet, when it is a clean cut leaving two working halves that cannot see each other.

## Yearbook

Cut by [Partitioning](process_partitioning.md) in [The Wire Goes Quiet](plot_the_wire_goes_quiet.md).
It brings [The Silence](piece_the_silence.md) that no node can read, the split from which two crowns and a fork follow.
