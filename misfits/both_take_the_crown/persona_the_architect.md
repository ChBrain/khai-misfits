# Persona: The Architect

## Taxonomy

[Architect](position_architect.md). The one who designs the rule that prevents two heads, and pays for it in availability.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Deliberate, clear about the trade, out to keep the state single.
Understands that a partition forces a choice and means to take the consistency horn, with a quorum that lets only the majority act and a fencing token that rejects a stale primary.
No illusion in it: it does not pretend to escape the trade, only to choose which side to surrender.

## Action

Works [Fence the Minority](plan_fence_the_minority.md) through [Fencing](process_fencing.md) at [the Blueprint](place_the_blueprint.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): it imposes a quorum so at most one side proceeds and turns away the stale primary's writes.
Cannot keep both consistency and availability under a partition, since [the Trade](piece_the_trade.md) is a theorem, so it buys the single state with the service the minority must refuse.
Does the sound thing: it stands the minority down, accepting that on the isolated side the correct behaviour becomes to stop serving.

## Shadow

Not timid for fencing and not defeated: refusing to serve on the minority side is correct, and it is also a real loss of availability, both at once.
Because the theorem forbids both, the architect proves that the fork is prevented only by paying, so its fix is not an escape from the trade but a clear-eyed choice of horn, consistency bought with uptime.
It is the one who keeps the state single, and the one who shows what keeping it single costs.

## Tell

Names the trade out loud and picks the horn: single state, and no service on the minority side while the partition lasts.
Cannot pretend the fix is free, because the theorem will not allow it, so it states the price plainly and designs to pay it, the one who fixes the split by choosing what to give up.
