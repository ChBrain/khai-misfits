# Plan: Keep Serving

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the isolated's seat](position_isolated.md), inside [Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md), and the same intent the Twin runs unseen on the far side.

## Owner

- Owner: [Isolated](position_isolated.md)

## Direction

When you cannot reach the leader and cannot tell whether it crashed or is merely unreachable, promote yourself and keep serving. Do not freeze waiting for a truth that may never come, since a system that stalls on every network hiccup has failed too, so take the available choice on the partial information the partition leaves you. Know that this is the correct move, and that the peer you cannot reach may be alive and making it too, so your right promotion may be one of two.
