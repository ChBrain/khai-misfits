# Place: The Blueprint

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md). Where the cluster's behaviour under partition is designed and the trade is chosen. Hosts [Fence It Off](plot_fence_it_off.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The design under the running system: the quorum rules, the fencing tokens, the choice of which horn of the trade to take when the wire splits.

## Holds

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the conditions under which a quorum prevents the fork and those under which no guard is needed at all.

## Offers

The levers to keep the state single: a majority rule that stands the minority down, a fencing token that rejects a stale write.

## Withheld

A design that keeps both consistency and availability under a partition, since the trade is a theorem and the blueprint can only choose which to surrender.
