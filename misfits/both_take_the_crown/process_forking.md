# Process: Forking

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md). Borne at [The Cluster](place_the_cluster.md), precipitated by [The Writer](persona_the_writer.md) sending writes to both primaries at once, in [Two Truths](plot_two_truths.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Writes arriving at both primaries, each answering as the sole leader.

## Direction

From one shared state toward two diverging histories, each side recording writes the other never sees.

## Lever

That both primaries accept their writes correctly and no channel joins them, so the divergence is not an error but the sum of two valid, unshared histories (Gilbert and Lynch).

## Echo

[The Fork](piece_the_fork.md), the one state become two that cannot be merged without losing what each recorded.
