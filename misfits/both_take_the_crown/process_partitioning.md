# Process: Partitioning

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md). Borne at [The Link](place_the_link.md), precipitated by a network path failing between two groups of nodes, in [The Wire Goes Quiet](plot_the_wire_goes_quiet.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A link failing, so one group of live nodes can no longer reach another.

## Direction

From one connected cluster toward two isolated halves, each alive and each unable to coordinate with the other.

## Lever

That networks fail while the machines they join keep running, so a partition leaves not a dead cluster but a live one with a hole through the middle (Gilbert and Lynch).

## Echo

[The Partition](piece_the_partition.md) and, with it, [The Silence](piece_the_silence.md) that no node can read.
