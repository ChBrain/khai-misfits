# Piece: The Fork

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md). Two primaries accepting conflicting writes so the shared state diverges into two irreconcilable histories, the split-brain itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Opening in [The Cluster](place_the_cluster.md): the one state pulling into two, a key set to two values, an account debited on one side and not the other, an order shipped twice, each history correct on its own side and irreconcilable with the other (Gilbert and Lynch).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and two heads are a curiosity that does no harm: it is the fork, the divergence of the state under conflicting writes, that turns two primaries into a real and costly split.
It carries the phenomenon whole. Because both primaries accept writes and no channel joins them, the single truth the cluster was built to keep becomes two, and they cannot be merged without losing some of what each recorded, so the fork is where the trap becomes damage that outlasts the partition.

## Apparent

Two systems, each fine: from either side the data looks consistent and correct, the divergence invisible until the halves are rejoined and the two truths collide.
