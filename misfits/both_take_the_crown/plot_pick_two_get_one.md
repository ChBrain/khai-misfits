# Plot: Pick Two, Get One

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Cluster](place_the_cluster.md), where the trade behind the fork is named.

## Action

[The Trade](piece_the_trade.md) is stated plainly: under a partition a system can be consistent or available but not both, so the fork was not a bug but the available horn of a forced choice, and preventing it means letting one side refuse to serve. The scene closes with the choice shown proved and inescapable, consistency and availability not both to be had while the partition lasts (Brewer; Gilbert and Lynch).

## Stage

This is not The Price Neither Will Name, a bargaining impossibility over private information: it is a distributed-systems impossibility over a network partition, the same shape of proof that you cannot have every good property at once, applied to what a replicated system can guarantee rather than what a mechanism can reveal.

## Tension

The fork can be read as a bug to be patched, but it is a theorem to be paid: keeping the state single requires that at most one side act, staying available requires that both do, and under a partition those cannot both hold, so the only real choice is which to surrender.
