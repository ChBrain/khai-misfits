# Position: Twin

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md). The seat on the far side of the partition, alive and reasoning identically, that promotes itself too, so its correctness mirrors the Isolated's into two heads.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A live node on the other side of the break, as capable and as correct as the first.
The same silence, read the same way: it cannot tell whether its peer crashed or is unreachable.
No shared view with the Isolated, so its identical reasoning runs blind to the very node it mirrors.

## Orders

Reason as the other reasons, and promote as the other promotes.
Face the same unreadable silence and take the same available choice, crowning yourself to keep serving, because from your side it is exactly as right as from the other.
Accept what the mirror means: that two correct nodes doing the identical right thing is precisely what makes two heads, and that the symmetry is invisible from inside it.

## Loses

The same singularity, in the same way.
Because it reasons identically and cannot see the Isolated, it crowns itself in the belief that it is the primary, so what it loses is the truth that another correct node has done the same, and it loses it by being right in the mirror.
It keeps its correctness and loses its uniqueness, the second crown that proves the first was never alone.

## Drives

Toward the mirror node whose right choice completes the split,
promoting exactly as its twin does and, by the symmetry, turning one correct promotion into two primaries,
so the seat that only did what the other did is the seat that makes the two heads real, correctness doubled into a fork.
