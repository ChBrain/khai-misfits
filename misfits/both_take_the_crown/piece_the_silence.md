# Piece: The Silence

## Taxonomy

[Both Take the Crown](play_both_take_the_crown.md). The fundamental ambiguity, a node's inability to tell a crashed peer from an unreachable one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Falling across [The Link](place_the_link.md): the unanswered heartbeat, the ping with no reply, silence on the wire that means the same whether the peer has died or is merely cut off, and that a node cannot see behind (Fischer, Lynch, and Paterson).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and a node could just wait: if it could tell a dead peer from an unreachable one, it would know whether to promote or to hold, and the trap would not spring.
It bears the load by forcing a blind choice. Because silence is indistinguishable from death and freezing is itself a failure, the node cannot wait to learn the truth, so it must act on partial information, and the correct action, keep serving, is exactly what leads to two heads.

## Apparent

No answer: from outside it looks like a simple timeout, a peer not responding, when it is an ambiguity with no way through, death and delay wearing the same face.

## Yearbook

Falls with [The Partition](piece_the_partition.md) in [The Wire Goes Quiet](plot_the_wire_goes_quiet.md).
It is what forces [The Isolated](persona_the_isolated.md) to choose, and so what precipitates [The Two Heads](piece_the_two_heads.md), the quiet that cannot be read and must still be answered.
