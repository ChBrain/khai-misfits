# Plan: Give Them Time to Adapt

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the legislator's seat](position_legislator.md), inside [Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Legislator](position_legislator.md)

## Direction

Set the date far enough out that nobody is stranded and firmly enough that nobody can wait it out. Fifteen years, not five, because five ruins the towns and the workers and the people who built their lives around the thing being ended, and a government that does that has done something wrong quite apart from what it achieved. And in statute, not in a speech, because a deadline nobody believes in changes nobody's plans, and a policy that changes nobody's plans is not a policy. Refuse the exemptions that would soften it and fund the transition for the regions. Note that these two requirements are not in tension with each other: fairness wants the notice long, effectiveness wants it believed, and the announcement has to be both. Note also that there is no version of it that is fair, effective, and silent about what the future price will be.

## Orders

([The Legislator](persona_the_legislator.md) announces through [Announcing](process_announcing.md) at [the Chamber](place_the_chamber.md), setting [the Notice](piece_the_notice.md).)

## Implementation

([The Chamber](place_the_chamber.md) is where the date is set and where the only correction would later have to be bought; [Announcing](process_announcing.md) issues one statement that is read as a policy in the country and as a price forecast in the ledger of anybody holding the asset.)

## Targets

- [ ] the date is far enough out that the towns and the investors can move
- [ ] it is in statute and stated plainly, since a deadline nobody credits changes nobody's plans
- [ ] the exemptions are refused and the transition is funded
- [ ] both requirements are met, neither having been traded against the other
- [ ] the announcement is understood to be a forecast as well as a rule, which is the whole of the difficulty
