# Process: Retiring

## Taxonomy

[Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md). Borne at [The Chamber](place_the_chamber.md), precipitated by [The Economist](persona_the_economist.md) naming the supply side and pricing what reaching it costs, in [Buy the Coal and Leave It](plot_buy_the_coal_and_leave_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The observation that every instrument in the package acts on demand while the mechanism runs through supply.

## Direction

Toward the stock rather than the flow: reserves bought and retired, a tax on what is held rather than on what is burned, or a demand cartel with no unregulated buyer left.

## Lever

That each route requires paying the owner for wealth the policy is extinguishing, a transfer to exactly the party the public believes should bear the loss, and that the cartel is useless unless it is very nearly universal.

## Echo

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the only correction that reaches the mechanism, growing politically harder the more technically correct it is, and shelved by everyone who has to stand for election.
