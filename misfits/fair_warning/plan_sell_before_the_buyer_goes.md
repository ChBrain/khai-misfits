# Plan: Sell Before the Buyer Goes

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Owner: [Owner](position_owner.md)

## Direction

Work out when to lift the stock, which is the only question there is, since the stuff does not spoil and there is no version of this in which it is never sold. The only reason to leave it in the ground another year is that it will be worth more next year than this year, net of what it costs to lift. That expectation has just changed: a credible statement that demand will be cut in fifteen years is a statement that the price in fifteen years will be lower, so the return to waiting has fallen and the optimal path has moved forward. Bring the field into production. Do not lobby against the date, do not dispute the science, and do not treat the announcement as an insult, none of which bears on the calculation. Note that this is the same arithmetic you would do for timber, a patent or a lease, and that you are not defying the policy but believing it.
