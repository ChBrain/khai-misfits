# Piece: The Bringing Forward

## Taxonomy

[Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md). The outcome: near-term emissions rising, and where the stock is ultimately lifted anyway, a schedule altered and a total unchanged.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Air](place_the_air.md), the one place in the play that keeps the account that matters and that no participant reads before deciding.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play has no cost: an owner rearranging its production calendar is nobody's business, and a policy that shifted some extraction between decades without consequence would be a curiosity.
It bears the load as the reason the timing is the harm. Because the atmosphere is a stock problem, what arrives and when is most of what matters, so a policy that lowered the eventual total by nothing and raised the near-term flow is scored on the second of those, and the intention behind it earns no credit in the only ledger that settles the question.

## Apparent

A blip, and it will be read as one: a rise in output in the years after a phase-out was announced, explicable as a boom, a price cycle, or a licence being used before it lapses.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [The Same Carbon, Sooner](plot_the_same_carbon_sooner.md), where the calendar the policy promised has no standing.
It works together with [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the harm and the honest account of how large it is and when it does not occur.
