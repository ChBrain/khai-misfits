# Piece: The Notice

## Taxonomy

[Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md). The announcement: a future cut in demand, dated far enough ahead to be fair and stated plainly enough to be believed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Chamber](place_the_chamber.md), issued once and read twice, as a rule in the country and as a forecast in the ledger of anyone holding the asset (Sinn).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by imposing the cut without warning, and the trap does not form: there is nothing to revalue in advance and no years in which to bring the extraction forward. But that is not an option the play can offer, because it strands the towns, the workers and the investors overnight, which is a different wrong and a plain one.
It bears the load as the fact that both of its virtues are required. A shorter notice is cruel and a vaguer one is ineffective, so the two available directions of retreat are each worse on their own terms, and there is no third version that is fair, effective, and silent about the future price.

## Apparent

Decency, and it is decency: a government doing the difficult thing properly, with time given, exemptions refused and a transition funded.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Say It Early and Mean It](plot_say_it_early_and_mean_it.md), a good policy correctly made and the origin of everything after it.
It works together with [The Certainty](piece_the_certainty.md), the announcement and every later hardening of it doing the same work in the same ledger.
