# Plan: Buy It and Leave It

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the economist's seat](position_economist.md), inside [Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Economist](position_economist.md)

## Direction

Point out that every instrument in the package acts on demand and the mechanism runs through supply, then name the three that reach it. Buy the reserves and retire them. Tax the stock rather than the flow. Or assemble a demand cartel comprehensive enough that no unregulated buyer remains. Concede at once that the announcement was right to be early and right to be credible, and do not propose that it should have been either late or vague, because that is not the argument and pretending it is will lose the room for the wrong reason. Price each instrument honestly, including the part nobody wants to hear, which is that all three require paying the owner for wealth the policy is extinguishing, and that the cartel is useless unless it is very nearly universal. Be honest about the magnitude too: it is contested, it turns on extraction costs and on how much of the stock is ultimately economic, and overstating it would be the easiest way to be dismissed.
