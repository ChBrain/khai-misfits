## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play overreaches into a claim it cannot support, that climate policy is counterproductive or that phase-outs should be announced late or vaguely, which the source does not say and the house will not stage.
It bears the load as the limit that keeps this a design problem. Because the trap needs an exhaustible stock, a forward-looking owner able to accelerate and a credible announcement, and because its size turns on extraction costs and on how much of the stock is ultimately economic and is contested in the literature, what is staged is a difficulty inside climate policy rather than a case against it, and the corrections are real: buy the reserves and retire them, tax the stock rather than the flow, or build a demand cartel with no unregulated buyer left, each of them a payment to the owner for wealth the policy is extinguishing.
