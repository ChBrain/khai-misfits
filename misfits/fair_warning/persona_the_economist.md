# Persona: The Economist

## Taxonomy

[Economist](position_economist.md). The one who names the instrument that would work and the reason nobody will pay for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Precise and unwelcome, pointing out that every instrument in the package acts on demand and the problem is on the supply side.
Concedes at once that the announcement was right to be early and right to be credible, and does not propose that it should have been either late or vague.
Is honest about the size of the effect, which is contested, and says so rather than overstating a case it believes.

## Action

Runs [Buy It and Leave It](plan_buy_it_and_leave_it.md): through [Retiring](process_retiring.md) at [the Chamber](place_the_chamber.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md).
Sets out the three instruments that reach the mechanism: buy the reserves and retire them, tax the stock rather than the flow, or assemble a demand cartel with no unregulated buyer left.
Prices each one honestly, including the part nobody wants to hear.

## Shadow

Not a contrarian and not a defender of the industry: what it proposes is more interventionist than the policy on the table, not less.
Because each instrument requires paying the owner for wealth the policy is extinguishing, it is asking a public that believes the polluter should bear the loss to write the polluter a cheque, and the cartel version is useless unless it is very nearly universal, so the proposal is politically worse the more correct it is.
It is the only actor holding an instrument that reaches the mechanism and the one nobody can afford to adopt.

## Tell

Is agreed with by the technically minded and shelved by everyone who has to stand for election.
Cannot offer a demand-side fix that does not run through the same announcement, since the announcement is the channel, and cannot make the supply-side fix cheap, since the cost is a transfer and transfers do not get cheaper by being explained.
