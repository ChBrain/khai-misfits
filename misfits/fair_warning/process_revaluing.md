# Process: Revaluing

## Taxonomy

[Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md). Borne at [The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), precipitated by [The Owner](persona_the_owner.md) reading the announcement as a statement about a future price, in [Worth Less in the Ground](plot_worth_less_in_the_ground.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An exhaustible stock that does not spoil, so the only question is when, and one line in the valuation that carries the whole reason to wait.

## Direction

From an expectation that the net price would rise toward an expectation that it will fall, and so from waiting being the better trade to lifting being the better trade.

## Lever

That the only return to leaving a resource in the ground is the expected rise in its net price (Hotelling), so a credible statement that demand will be cut is arithmetic and not politics, and the arithmetic is the ordinary one.

## Echo

[The Devaluation](piece_the_devaluation.md), a correct answer supplied by the policy itself, with the science, the transition funding and the moral case entering the calculation at no point and none of them being ignored on purpose.
