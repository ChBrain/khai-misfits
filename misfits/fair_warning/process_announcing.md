# Process: Announcing

## Taxonomy

[Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md). Borne at [The Chamber](place_the_chamber.md), precipitated by [The Legislator](persona_the_legislator.md) dating a phase-out far enough out to be fair and plainly enough to be believed, in [Say It Early and Mean It](plot_say_it_early_and_mean_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two requirements that are each non-negotiable and not in tension: enough notice that nobody is stranded, and enough firmness that nobody waits it out.

## Direction

From an intention toward a dated, statutory commitment that the country can plan around, which is exactly what it was meant to become.

## Lever

That there is no version of the announcement that is fair, effective, and silent about the future price, so the one statement is read as a rule in the country and as a forecast in the ledger of anyone holding the asset (Sinn).

## Echo

[The Notice](piece_the_notice.md), a good policy correctly made, and the origin of everything that follows, with both directions of retreat, shorter notice or vaguer notice, worse on their own terms.
