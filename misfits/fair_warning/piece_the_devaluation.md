# Piece: The Devaluation

## Taxonomy

[Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md). The revaluation: the expected future net price falling, and with it the entire return to leaving the stock in the ground.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), on one line of an ordinary valuation, arrived at by the arithmetic any holder of a depleting asset performs (Hotelling).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by giving the owner some other reason to wait, and the announcement produces no acceleration: a stock that must be held for a reason unconnected to its price does not respond to a price forecast.
It bears the load as the translation step, and as the reason nobody can be blamed. Because the only return to leaving an exhaustible resource where it is was the expected rise in its net price, the policy did not tempt the owner into anything: it changed the one number the decision turns on, and the owner read it correctly.

## Apparent

Bookkeeping, and it is bookkeeping: a line in a valuation, updated on new information, of the kind updated a hundred times a year for a hundred unremarkable reasons.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Worth Less in the Ground](plot_worth_less_in_the_ground.md), where the policy supplies the answer that undoes it.
