# Position: Campaigner

## Taxonomy

[Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md). The seat pressing for the exact property the policy needs, whose every victory lands in a ledger it never aimed at.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A long record of phase-outs that died quietly through vagueness, deferral and quiet renewal, and it is an accurate record.
The specific asks: a firmer date, a statutory backstop, an end to the exemptions, a minister who will say it plainly.
One announcement and one credibility, with no way to raise the public's belief while leaving the owner's untouched.

## Orders

Make it certain: close the exemptions, win the backstop, and get the commitment stated in terms nobody can walk back.
Treat every hardening as progress, which on the only axis this seat can measure it is.
Accept what the seat does: certainty about a demand cut is certainty about a price, and the seat cannot issue one without the other.

## Loses

Any way to want less of what it is asking for, since asking for less credibility is asking for the policy to fail, and the seat exists because soft commitments do fail.
Because its wins are read as forecasts by the party holding the stock, the more completely it succeeds the further forward the extraction moves, so success and harm are measured on the same dial.
It keeps a commitment nobody can walk back and loses the years it was trying to buy.

## Drives

Toward the actor working hardest for the outcome and the one whose success is the accelerant,
winning the backstop, closing the exemptions, and counting each as the real progress it is,
so a campaign that is right in every particular and wins on every particular is the campaign that moves the carbon forward, with nothing in its position to revise.
