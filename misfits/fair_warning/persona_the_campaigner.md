# Persona: The Campaigner

## Taxonomy

[Campaigner](position_campaigner.md). The one pressing for the exact property the policy needs, each increment of which lands in a ledger it was not aimed at.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Tireless and specific, asking for a firmer date, a statutory backstop, an end to the exemptions and a minister who will say the word out loud.
Argues from a long record of phase-outs that died quietly through vagueness, deferral and quiet renewal, and the record supports it.
Wants the thing to actually happen, which is the whole of its position and is not a disguise for anything.

## Action

Runs [Make It Certain](plan_make_it_certain.md): through [Pressing](process_pressing.md) at [the Chamber](place_the_chamber.md), setting [the Certainty](piece_the_certainty.md).
Wins the backstop, closes two exemptions, and gets the date restated in terms nobody can walk back.
Treats every increase in credibility as a gain, which by every measure the campaign has it is.

## Shadow

Not a zealot and not mistaken: credibility is the property that makes a deadline bite, and a campaign that settled for a soft date would have been settling for nothing.
Because certainty about a future demand cut is certainty about a future price, the campaign's victories are read as forecasts by the party holding the stock, and the more completely it wins the further forward the extraction moves.
It is the actor working hardest for the outcome and the one whose success is the accelerant.

## Tell

Counts as progress every hardening of the commitment, and each hardening is real progress on the only axis the campaign can see.
Cannot ask for a policy that is believed by the public and doubted by the owner, there being one announcement and one credibility, and cannot ask for less credibility without asking for the policy to fail.
