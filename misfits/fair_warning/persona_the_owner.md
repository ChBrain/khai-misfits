# Persona: The Owner

## Taxonomy

[Owner](position_owner.md). The one who believes the policy, and whose believing it is the harm.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Unexcited and commercial, holding a stock that does not spoil and deciding only when to lift it.
Does not argue with the science, does not lobby against the date, and does not treat the announcement as an insult.
Explains the decision in one sentence if asked: the return to leaving it there was the price going up, and the price is not going up now.

## Action

Runs [Sell Before the Buyer Goes](plan_sell_before_the_buyer_goes.md): through [Revaluing](process_revaluing.md) at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), meeting [the Devaluation](piece_the_devaluation.md); then through [Extracting](process_extracting.md) at [the Field](place_the_field.md), producing [the Acceleration](piece_the_acceleration.md), and at [the Air](place_the_air.md), delivering [the Bringing Forward](piece_the_bringing_forward.md).
Brings the field into production in the year the date is set, and lifts over fifteen years what it would have lifted over forty.
Sells to buyers who are still buying, which is the only kind there will be.

## Shadow

Not defiant and not greedy in any sense the play needs: it is doing the arithmetic every holder of a depleting asset does, and it would do the same if the asset were timber, patents or a lease.
Because the announcement told it credibly that the future price would be lower, its compliance with the policy's own logic is the acceleration, so an owner who disbelieved the government would have left the carbon in the ground longer.
It is the only actor who could slow the harm by doubting the state, and doubt is the one thing the policy worked hardest to remove.

## Tell

Cites the announcement as the reason for the investment decision, accurately, in documents anyone can read.
Cannot be argued out of it by an appeal to the climate, since the climate is not what changed: the expected price changed, and it changed because the policy said it would.
