# Position: Economist

## Taxonomy

[Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md). The seat holding the only instrument that reaches the mechanism, and the reason nobody will pay for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The observation that every instrument in the package acts on demand while the mechanism runs through supply.
Three corrections that reach it: buy the reserves and retire them, tax the stock rather than the flow, or assemble a demand cartel with no unregulated buyer left.
An honest account of the magnitude, which is contested, and no wish to overstate it.

## Orders

Buy it and leave it: act on the stock rather than the flow, and pay what that costs rather than pretending the demand-side package will reach it.
Concede that the announcement was right to be early and right to be credible, and do not propose that it should have been either late or vague.
Accept what the seat does: it is asking for more intervention than the policy on the table, not less.

## Loses

The room, because each instrument means paying the owner for wealth the policy is extinguishing, which asks a public that believes the polluter should bear the loss to write the polluter a cheque.
Because the cartel version is useless unless it is very nearly universal, and the buy-and-retire version is a transfer that does not get cheaper by being explained, the seat's proposal becomes politically harder the more technically correct it is.
It keeps the only answer that reaches the mechanism and loses every hearing in which it could be adopted.

## Drives

Toward the actor whose correctness is the reason it is shelved,
naming the supply side, pricing all three instruments, and refusing to call any of them cheap,
so the play closes on a real instrument nobody can afford rather than on an error anybody made, and being agreed with by the technically minded is not the same as being enacted.
