# Plan: Make It Certain

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the campaigner's seat](position_campaigner.md), inside [Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Campaigner](position_campaigner.md)

## Direction

Harden the commitment until it cannot be walked back. Win the statutory backstop, close the exemptions one at a time, and get a minister to say the date out loud in terms that will be quoted against any successor who tries to move it. Argue from the record, which is a long list of phase-outs that died quietly through vagueness, deferral and quiet renewal, and which is accurate. Do not settle for a soft date, because a soft date is how this ends up meaning nothing. And understand what cannot be asked for: there is one announcement and one credibility, so there is no way to raise the public's belief in the deadline while leaving untouched the belief of the party holding the stock, and asking for less credibility is asking for the policy to fail.
