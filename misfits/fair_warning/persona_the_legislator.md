# Persona: The Legislator

## Taxonomy

[Legislator](position_legislator.md). The one who must give notice and must be believed, and whose two virtues combine into the mechanism.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Serious and humane, choosing a date far enough out that nobody is stranded and firm enough that nobody can wait it out.
Can defend both halves separately: five years would ruin the towns, thirty would mean nothing, and vagueness is how a phase-out dies quietly.
Has no quarrel with the industry and says so, the point being to end a practice rather than to punish anybody.

## Action

Runs [Give Them Time to Adapt](plan_give_them_time_to_adapt.md): through [Announcing](process_announcing.md) at [the Chamber](place_the_chamber.md), setting [the Notice](piece_the_notice.md).
Puts the date in statute, refuses the exemptions that would have softened it, and funds the transition for the regions.
Says plainly that this will happen, because a policy that might not happen is not a policy.

## Shadow

Not naive and not captured: the announcement is the correct instrument, made the correct way, and a legislator who gave no notice or gave vague notice would have done worse on every count that can be named in advance.
Because credibility is the property that makes the deadline bite, and the same property is a price forecast to anyone holding the asset, its two virtues are one act read in two ledgers, and there is no version of the announcement that is fair, effective, and silent about the future price.
It is the actor with the clearest good intent in the play and the origin of the acceleration.

## Tell

Measures success by whether the date is believed, which it is, and which is the problem.
Cannot make the notice shorter without stranding people or vaguer without losing the effect, so the two available directions of retreat are each worse on their own terms.
