## Cue

## Action

Through [Revaluing](process_revaluing.md), [the Owner](persona_the_owner.md) on [Sell Before the Buyer Goes](plan_sell_before_the_buyer_goes.md) meets [the Devaluation](piece_the_devaluation.md): the resource does not spoil, so the only reason to leave it where it is was that it would be worth more later, net of the cost of lifting it, and a credible statement that demand will be cut in fifteen years is a statement that it will be worth less. The scene closes with waiting no longer the better trade, by an arithmetic that is ordinary, correct, and supplied by the policy itself (Hotelling).

## Stage

Nothing here is a manoeuvre. The valuation is the one any holder of a depleting asset performs, the same calculation would be made for timber, a patent or a lease, and the owner is not disputing the science, lobbying against the date, or treating the announcement as an insult.

## Tension

The policy did not tempt the owner into anything: it changed the single number the decision turns on, and the owner read it correctly, so the harm passes through the seat with the least to answer for, and the science, the transition funding and the moral case enter this calculation at no point without any of them being ignored on purpose.
