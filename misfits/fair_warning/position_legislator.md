# Position: Legislator

## Taxonomy

[Fair Warning](play_fair_warning.md). The seat that must give notice and must be believed, whose two virtues are one act read in two ledgers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The power to set a date, put it in statute, and refuse the exemptions that would soften it.
Two requirements that are not in tension with each other and are each non-negotiable: enough notice that nobody is stranded, and enough firmness that nobody waits it out.
No version of the announcement that is fair, effective, and silent about the future price.

## Orders

Give them time to adapt: date the phase-out far enough out that the towns and the investors can move, and state it plainly enough that they will.
Fund the transition, refuse the carve-outs, and say the thing out loud, since a policy that might not happen is not a policy.
Accept what the seat does: it is issuing a forecast to anyone who holds the asset, whether or not it is issuing one to anyone else.

## Loses

Both directions of retreat, since a shorter notice strands people and a vaguer one loses the effect, so the two available ways to reduce the acceleration are each worse on their own terms.
Because credibility is what makes the deadline bite and is also what moves the extraction forward, the seat cannot separate the property it needs from the property that costs, and doing the job better does both.
It keeps a fair and effective announcement and loses the schedule it was trying to protect.

## Drives

Toward the actor with the clearest good intent in the play and the origin of the acceleration,
choosing fifteen years because five would be cruel and thirty would mean nothing, and saying it in terms nobody can walk back,
so a decision correct on both of its faces is the decision that brings the carbon up sooner, with no third face available to choose instead.
