# Position: Many

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The seat of the diffuse interest, a large group that shares a real interest but whose members each hold too thin a share to justify acting, so it free-rides and stays latent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real shared interest and a thin share of it: a good all would gain, divided so fine across the many that each one's portion is small.
A sound reason to sit out: the good is non-excludable, so it comes whether or not this member pays, and one contribution is too slight to decide whether it comes at all, so free-riding is the rational move.
No way to be worth acting for alone, because the cost of acting falls whole on the one who acts while the gain is split among all, so no member's own arithmetic ever favours stepping up.

## Orders

Leave it to the rest.
Weigh your thin share against the cost of acting and decline, since the good will come without you if it comes and your single effort cannot swing it, so paying in is to spend a real cost for a sliver.
Know that your sitting out is sound and not apathy, and that the interest you share goes unadvanced not because you do not care but because each of you, caring, rightly waits for the others.

## Loses

A sum that dwarfs every share, left on the table for want of assembling.
Because each rational sitting-out is right and they all sit out together, the many forgo a collective good far larger than the few they lose to ever win, undone not in proportion to any member's small share but by the whole of what none of them combined to claim.
It keeps its reason and loses the outcome, the group whose interest is real and whose diffuseness leaves it silent.

## Drives

Toward the one who sits out soundly and loses in sum,
each share too thin and each voice too slight to make acting pay, no apathy in it,
so the seat that shares the largest interest can be the seat that advances none of it, the majority left unrepresented not because it is weak but because it is diffuse.
