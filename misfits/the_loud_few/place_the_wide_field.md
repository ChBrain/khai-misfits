# Place: The Wide Field

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). Where the many are scattered thin, each too far from the rest to combine, hosting [Not My Nickel](plot_not_my_nickel.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The many, spread across open ground.
Each stands too far from the next to compare notes or pool an effort, and each does the same private sum in isolation: what the share is worth, what acting would cost.

## Holds

The arithmetic that keeps the field from combining.
[The Thin Share](piece_the_thin_share.md) is what each member finds on doing the sum, [the Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) is the option that sum always leaves open, and [the Sum](piece_the_sum.md) is what the field would be worth in total if anyone ever added it up.

## Offers

The free ride, to every member of the field.
Because the good is non-excludable once made, the field lets each stand still and take the benefit of whatever the others provide, at no cost to standing still.

## Withheld

Any share large enough to make acting worth it.
No one on this field holds a stake that alone justifies the cost of organising the rest, so the good the field could make in sum stays unmade, wanted by everyone and provided by no one.
