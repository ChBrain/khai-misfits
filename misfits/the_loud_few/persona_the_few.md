# Persona: The Few

## Taxonomy

[Few](position_few.md), inside [The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The archetype of the concentrated interest: a small group whose members each hold a stake large enough to be worth acting on, so it organises and prevails where the many cannot.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Reasonable citizens defending a legitimate interest of their own. Each has much at stake and says so plainly, and there is nothing hidden in it: they would tell you exactly what they want and why it is worth their while to ask.

## Action

Combine and press the case. Because each of the few holds a stake large enough to justify the whole cost of acting, the small group is privileged and forms, and it carries [the Stake](piece_the_stake.md) through [Organising](process_organising.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md), pressing on [Press Our Case](plan_press_our_case.md) an interest concentrated enough that acting pays (Olson). It is the only voice that reliably arrives, so it is heard.

## Shadow

The tilt it wins leaves the many silent. Its organising is sound and warranted by its stake, and that very soundness is what bends the outcome its way through [Tilting](process_tilting.md) while the diffuse many, each with too thin a share to act, send no one, so [the Tilt](piece_the_tilt.md) it secures is the mirror of their latency, not a theft. It keeps its gain and owes no cause for blame, the winner the asymmetry rewards.

## Tell

It is few, and it is always in the room.
