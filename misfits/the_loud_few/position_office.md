# Position: Office

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The seat that decides, the office that acts on whoever organises to reach it, so the tilt to concentrated interests runs through it without any bribe.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A duty to answer the case made to it: it weighs and grants the interests that are brought before it, and it does so in good faith.
A room that only the organised enter: the few reliably arrive to press their stake, and the diffuse many, each with too thin a share to act, send no one.
No sight of the absent, because it can answer only the voice that reaches it, and the interest that never organised makes no case for it to weigh.

## Orders

Answer who asks.
Hear the case that is brought and grant what the weighing warrants, since that is the office's honest work and the interest that comes before you is the interest you can serve.
Know that you cannot answer a voice that never arrives, so when only the few press their case, you serve the few, not from favour but because the room holds no one else.

## Loses

Its evenhandedness, not by corruption but by the room it sits in.
Because it answers whoever shows up and only the few show up, its fair hearing of the case before it becomes, across every decision, a standing tilt to the concentrated interest, so the impartiality it keeps is the very channel the tilt runs through.
It keeps its good faith and loses its balance, the honest seat that bends because the room is not full.

## Drives

Toward the one who answers fairly and tilts all the same,
serving whoever makes the case because that is its duty, no bribe in it,
so the seat that would weigh every interest evenly is the seat through which the few prevail, the tilt a structure's doing and not the office's will.
