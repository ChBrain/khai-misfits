# Plot: A Private Reason

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Turnstile](place_the_turnstile.md), where joining costs something, and the collective cause on its own has not been enough to draw a crowd through it.

## Action

[The Organiser](persona_the_organiser.md), from [the organiser's seat](position_organiser.md), runs [Sweeten the Ask](plan_sweeten_the_ask.md) through [Sweetening](process_sweetening.md), handing [the Selective Incentive](piece_the_selective_incentive.md).
The many finally chip in, drawn by a private benefit the cause itself does not give, at real cost to whoever supplies it.

## Stage

Olson's selective incentive, the one lever that moves a large group (Olson); costly to supply and prone to capture the organisation for its own sake, so the remedy is a real one and no free hero.

## Tension

The many act only when bought a private reason, and the organisation risks becoming the sweetener rather than the cause.
