# Piece: The Sum

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The large aggregate benefit the many would win if they combined: real, and unclaimed only because it is never assembled.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Spanning [the Wide Field](place_the_wide_field.md), held collectively by [the Many](persona_the_many.md) but never gathered by them: the total sits latent across the whole group, present in principle, absent in practice.
Not a place the group occupies together; a total that could only be reached by an act of combination the field's own arithmetic keeps from happening.

## Load Bearing

Olson's account of the large latent group (Olson, _The Logic of Collective Action_, 1965): the aggregate a large group could secure by acting together is often bigger than what any small privileged group wins, but the same size that makes the aggregate large is what stops it from being organised.
Guard against reading the many's loss as small because each individual share is small: the SUM is large; only the per-head share is thin, and what is left on the table is the whole sum, not a trivial remainder.

## Apparent

It looks like a modest loss, hardly worth the trouble to recover, since what any one member forgoes is a sliver.
That reading stops at the individual's share and never adds the shares back up; the aggregate the field is quietly foregoing dwarfs it.
