# Place: The Turnstile

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). Where selective incentives are handed to contributors and free-riders are left outside the private benefit, hosting [A Private Reason](plot_a_private_reason.md) and [When the Many Move](plot_when_the_many_move.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A gate with two sides.
Those who pay in pass through to a private benefit waiting on the other side; those who do not pay stand outside it, in plain view of what they are missing.

## Holds

The lever that pays for a contribution the cause alone could not buy.
[The Selective Incentive](piece_the_selective_incentive.md) is what the turnstile hands out, a private good separate from the collective one, given only for passing through.

## Offers

A private benefit the cause itself does not give.
Whoever contributes gets something for themselves at the turnstile, on top of whatever share of the collective good their contribution helps produce.

## Withheld

That same private benefit from anyone who only free-rides.
The turnstile is what makes the ride no longer free: the collective good still reaches the free-rider once made, but the private good waiting here does not.
