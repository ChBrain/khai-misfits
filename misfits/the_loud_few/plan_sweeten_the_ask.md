# Plan: Sweeten the Ask

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the organiser's seat](position_organiser.md), inside [The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Organiser](position_organiser.md)

## Direction

Do not appeal to the shared good alone, which each will free-ride on, but attach to it a private benefit only contributors receive. That selective incentive is the one thing that makes acting pay for a member of a large group, the members-only good the free-rider does not get, so it is what moves the many where the cause itself cannot. Accept the cost and the risk, and know that the deepest danger is that the sweetener becomes the point, the body filling with members who came for the private good while the cause it was built for slips to a pretext.

## Orders

([The Organiser](persona_the_organiser.md) hands [the Selective Incentive](piece_the_selective_incentive.md) through [Sweetening](process_sweetening.md) at [the Turnstile](place_the_turnstile.md), bundling a private benefit with the cause so the many chip in for the benefit the collective good could not make them pay for.)

## Implementation

([Sweetening](process_sweetening.md) at [the Turnstile](place_the_turnstile.md) supplies the motive the shared good cannot, because [the Selective Incentive](piece_the_selective_incentive.md) is a private benefit given only to contributors, dear to supply and prone to capture the body, so the many move at last and the cause risks becoming the pretext.)

## Targets

- [ ] the appeal is not to the shared good alone but to a private benefit only contributors get
- [ ] the selective incentive is supplied at its real cost, the one lever that moves a large group
- [ ] the many are moved to chip in, drawn by the private reason the cause could not give
- [ ] the risk that the sweetener captures the cause is held in view, not wished away
