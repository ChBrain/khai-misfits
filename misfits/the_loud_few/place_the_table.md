# Place: The Table

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). Where the organised few are seated and the decision is made, the many nowhere in the room, hosting [Worth Their While](plot_worth_their_while.md), [Who's in the Room](plot_whos_in_the_room.md), and [A Drag No One Chose](plot_a_drag_no_one_chose.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A small table, fully seated.
The organised few sit around it, each with a stake worth the trip, and no chair stands empty for the many, because the many never asked for one.

## Holds

The weight that brought the few here and the bend it leaves behind.
[The Stake](piece_the_stake.md) is what seated every chair at this table, and [the Tilt](piece_the_tilt.md) is what the table produces once the sitting is done.

## Offers

A hearing, to whoever shows up.
The table does not discriminate by name or cause, only by presence: any interest organised enough to send someone gets a seat and a fair audience once seated.

## Withheld

A voice for those who did not organise to be here.
The table keeps no proxy for the absent many, so their real and larger interest goes unspoken, not refused, simply never carried through the door.
