# Pitch: Who Shows Up Wins

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a diffuse majority loses to a concentrated few not because it is weak but because it is diffuse.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reasonable, even civic. Everyone acts soundly on the size of their own stake: a few who organise because it plainly pays them, and many who stay home because it plainly does not. No conspiracy, no apathy, just arithmetic, run correctly by each.

## Undertow

The good at issue is a collective one, non-excludable, so each would rather let the others pay and enjoy it for free (Samuelson). In a large group each share is thin and each voice too slight to matter, so each rationally sits out and the group stays latent, while a small group whose members each hold a concentrated stake finds it worth acting, so the few prevail and outcomes tilt to them (Olson; Wilson).

## Nerve

That the many lose a sum that dwarfs anything the few win, and lose it while every one of them is being perfectly reasonable. The tilt is authored by no one: the few's organising is sound, the many's sitting out is sound, and the office hears only who came.

## Echo

Who shows up wins, and only the few ever show up.
