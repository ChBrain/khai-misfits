# Plot: Worth Their While

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Table](place_the_table.md), already seated, every chair filled by someone whose stake was worth the trip.
No one had to be summoned; the size of the stake did the summoning.

## Action

[The Few](persona_the_few.md), from [the few's seat](position_few.md), run [Press Our Case](plan_press_our_case.md) through [Organising](process_organising.md), taking [the Stake](piece_the_stake.md).
Each member's share of the good at issue is large enough, on its own, to be worth the whole cost of organising and pressing the case, so the group needs no coercion and no outside help to act.

## Stage

Olson's privileged small group: at least one member's stake justifies bearing the whole cost of the collective good alone, so the small group organises where the large one would not (Olson).
Not greed: the stake is just large enough per head to be worth acting on, the few doing the same rational sum the many do at the Wide Field, only with a far larger numerator.

## Tension

The very asymmetry that makes the few act is what leaves the many silent.
