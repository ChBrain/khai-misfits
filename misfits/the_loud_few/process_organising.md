# Process: Organising

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). Borne at [the Table](place_the_table.md), initiated by [the Few](persona_the_few.md), in [Worth Their While](plot_worth_their_while.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Few](persona_the_few.md), each already holding a stake worth the trip.
Begins the moment a concentrated interest, scattered until now, finds it has members who would each gain enough alone to justify acting.

## Direction

From a scattered concentrated interest toward a combined force.
The few move from separate holders of the same stake to a single organised body seated at [the Table](place_the_table.md), the group small enough that coordinating it costs little against what each member stands to gain.

## Lever

A stake large enough to justify the whole cost, held by at least one member.
Where one member's share of the good is worth bearing the entire cost of organising alone, the small group is privileged and forms, needing no coercion and no outside inducement to act (Olson).

## Echo

A privileged few, seated and ready to press their case.
What organising leaves behind is [the Stake](piece_the_stake.md), now carried by a body compact enough to act on it and loud enough to be heard.
