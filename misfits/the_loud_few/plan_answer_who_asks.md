# Plan: Answer Who Asks

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the office's seat](position_office.md), inside [The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Office](position_office.md)

## Direction

Hear the case that is brought and grant what the weighing warrants, since that is the office's honest work and the interest that comes before you is the interest you can serve. You cannot answer a voice that never arrives, so when only the few press their case, you serve the few, not from favour but because the room holds no one else. Know that this fair hearing of whoever shows up, in a room only the organised enter, becomes across every decision a standing tilt to the concentrated interest, the channel the tilt runs through even as your good faith holds.

## Orders

([The Office](persona_the_office.md) takes the case the few bring through [Tilting](process_tilting.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md), answering the only organised voice in the room and so meeting [the Tilt](piece_the_tilt.md), while the diffuse many, absent, are not weighed because they sent no one.)

## Implementation

([Tilting](process_tilting.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md) bends the outcome to whoever organised to reach it, because the office answers the case before it and only the few bring one, so [the Tilt](piece_the_tilt.md) follows from an honest hearing in a room the many never enter.)

## Targets

- [ ] the case that is brought is heard and granted as the weighing warrants
- [ ] the absent interest is understood as unanswerable, having made no case
- [ ] the few are served because the room holds no one else, not from favour
- [ ] the fair hearing is seen to tilt across every decision, the office the channel not the author
