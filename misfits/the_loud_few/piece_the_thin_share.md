# Piece: The Thin Share

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The sliver of the collective benefit that falls to each member of the large group, too small on its own to justify the cost of chasing it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Wide Field](place_the_wide_field.md), carried by [Thinning](process_thinning.md): the arithmetic that spreads one benefit across every member of a large group until each member's portion is nearly nothing.
Not a decision anyone makes; a division that happens automatically the moment the group is large, before anyone has weighed in at all.

## Load Bearing

Olson's group-size logic (Olson, _The Logic of Collective Action_, 1965): as membership grows, the per-member slice of a collective good shrinks, and any one member's own contribution has no perceptible effect on whether the good is won at all. Weighing a real cost against a vanishing, unmoved-by-me benefit, the individually rational answer is to withhold the effort.
Guard against reading the many's sitting out as apathy or ignorance: they are neither. The share really is too small and the single contribution really is too slight to swing the outcome, so sitting out is the rational read of the arithmetic, not a failure to care or to understand it.

## Apparent

It looks like indifference: the many just cannot be bothered, do not grasp what is at stake, or have grown too passive to show up.
That reading mistakes a correct calculation for a character flaw. Anyone doing the same division would land on the same answer.
