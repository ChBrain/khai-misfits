# Piece: The Stake

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The concentrated interest the few hold, large enough per head that at least one of them will bear the whole cost of acting.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Table](place_the_table.md), carried by [Organising](process_organising.md): the few sit close enough to the good, and their portion of it large enough, that showing up and pressing the case pays for itself.
Not a favor granted them; a position they arrive at because the arithmetic that thins the many's share never thins theirs the same way.

## Load Bearing

Olson's privileged group (Olson, _The Logic of Collective Action_, 1965): when a group is small enough, or the good concentrated enough, that one member's own share exceeds the cost of securing it, that member has reason to act alone if it must, and the group organises without needing coercion or inducement.
Guard against reading the few's organising as greed or conspiracy: the stake is simply large enough per head to be worth acting on, the same calculus the many run and rationally decline. No secret coordination is required when the arithmetic already favors action.

## Apparent

It looks like self-interest dressed up as principle, a small clique angling for advantage while claiming to represent a wider cause.
That reading assumes bad faith where the actual asymmetry is just size: a stake this concentrated would move anyone who held it.
