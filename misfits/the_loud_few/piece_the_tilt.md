# Piece: The Tilt

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The systematic bend of outcomes toward concentrated interests and away from the diffuse majority they are drawn from.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Table](place_the_table.md), carried by [Tilting](process_tilting.md): the decision leans toward whoever is present and organised, and only [the Few](persona_the_few.md) arrived organised.
Not a thumb on the scale placed by hand; the shape the scale takes when only one side ever shows up to be weighed.

## Load Bearing

Wilson's concentrated-benefits, diffuse-costs typology (Wilson, 1973), resting on Olson's logic of collective action (Olson, 1965): where a benefit concentrates on a few and its cost spreads thin over many, the few organise and the many do not, so the official hears one organised voice and answers it, and outcomes tilt toward concentrated interests as a rule, not an exception.
Guard against reading each narrow win as an isolated injustice authored by someone: the tilt is the accreted sum of many rational narrow wins, each one following the same logic of collective action, none of them a single villain's doing (Olson, _The Rise and Decline of Nations_, 1982). The official at [the Office](persona_the_office.md) is not bribed; it answers the only voice present, per [plan_answer_who_asks.md](plan_answer_who_asks.md).

## Apparent

It looks like corruption, a fix put in by someone who should have known better and did not.
That reading looks for a hand on the scale and finds none; what tilted the outcome was attendance, not intent.
