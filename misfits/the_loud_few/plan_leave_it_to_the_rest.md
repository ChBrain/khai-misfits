# Plan: Leave It to the Rest

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the many's seat](position_many.md), inside [The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Many](position_many.md)

## Direction

Weigh your thin share against the cost of acting, and decline. The good is non-excludable, so it will come whether or not you pay, and your single effort is too slight to decide whether it comes at all, so paying in spends a real cost for a sliver. Leave it to the rest. Know that your sitting out is sound and not apathy, and that the interest you share goes unadvanced not because you do not care but because each of you, caring, rightly waits for the others, and the sum you all forgo is left on the table for want of assembling.

## Orders

([The Many](persona_the_many.md) take [the Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) on [the Thin Share](piece_the_thin_share.md) through [Thinning](process_thinning.md) at [the Wide Field](place_the_wide_field.md), each computing a portion too small to act on and sitting out, so the good is never made and [the Sum](piece_the_sum.md) goes unclaimed.)

## Implementation

([Thinning](process_thinning.md) at [the Wide Field](place_the_wide_field.md) leaves each member of the large group with too small a share to justify the cost, because [the Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) is there for the taking on a non-excludable good, so all sit out and [the Sum](piece_the_sum.md) is forgone though it dwarfs every share.)

## Targets
