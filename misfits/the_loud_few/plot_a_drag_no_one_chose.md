# Plot: A Drag No One Chose

## Owner

## Cue

[The Table](place_the_table.md), over years, one narrow win after another, each seated and each fair on its own terms.

## Action

Many narrow wins accrete through [Tilting](process_tilting.md) into [the Tilt](piece_the_tilt.md) compounded, and [the Sum](piece_the_sum.md) the many forgo grows with every round, each coalition rational for its own members and the accreted drag chosen by no one.

## Stage

Olson's _The Rise and Decline of Nations_ (1982): stable societies accrete narrow distributional coalitions, each rational for its members, summing to institutional sclerosis (Olson).

## Tension

Every narrow win is defensible on its own, and a society ossifies from their sum.
