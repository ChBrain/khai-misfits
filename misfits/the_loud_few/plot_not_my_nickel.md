# Plot: Not My Nickel

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Wide Field](place_the_wide_field.md), where the many stand, a real shared interest spread thin across all of them.
Nobody has moved on it yet, and nobody steps forward now.

## Action

[The Many](persona_the_many.md), from [the many's seat](position_many.md), run [Leave It to the Rest](plan_leave_it_to_the_rest.md) through [Thinning](process_thinning.md), taking [the Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) on [the Thin Share](piece_the_thin_share.md).
Each does the same private sum: a slice too thin to justify the cost of acting, a contribution too small to be felt in the outcome, and a good that cannot exclude them whether they pay toward it or not. Each computes their sliver and sits out, the good unmade, [the Sum](piece_the_sum.md) left on the table.

## Stage

Olson's group-size free-ride: in a large group, a member's share of a collective good and their influence over whether it is provided both shrink until acting costs more than the sliver it buys (Olson).
Not Not Worth My While: there the benefit is symmetric and genuinely small to everyone; here the asymmetry is the whole point, the Sum is large and only the per-head share is thin.
Not The Commons: nothing is depleted, no stock drawn down; the good is simply never made.

## Tension

Each is right to sit out, and because each is right the many forgo a sum that dwarfs every share.
