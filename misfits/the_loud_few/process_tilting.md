# Process: Tilting

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). Borne at [the Table](place_the_table.md), initiated by [the Office](persona_the_office.md), in [Who's in the Room](plot_whos_in_the_room.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Office](persona_the_office.md), answering the room as it is actually seated.
Begins once the few have organised and arrive at [the Table](place_the_table.md) as the only voice the office has heard on the matter.

## Direction

From an even field toward outcomes bent to the organised few.
The office weighs what it has been shown, and what it has been shown is one side of the interest and not the other, so the decision moves from where an unbiased weighing would land toward where the room in front of it points.

## Lever

An office that acts on whoever organised to reach it, and only the few did.
No bribe changes hands and no favour is asked outright: the office simply answers the only organised voice in the room, and because the many never organised to be in it, the tilt follows from that asymmetry alone (Wilson; Olson).

## Echo

A decision bent toward concentrated interests, made in good faith.
What tilting leaves behind is [the Tilt](piece_the_tilt.md), an outcome no one at the table needed to buy because no one else was there to weigh against it.
