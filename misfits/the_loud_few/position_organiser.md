# Position: Organiser

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The seat that would rouse the many, the entrepreneur who cannot move a large group with the collective good itself and so must supply a private reason to act, the costly remedy that bounds the trap.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one lever that moves a large group: a selective incentive, a private benefit given only to those who contribute, separate from the collective good the many would free-ride on.
A set of costly means: a members-only good to hand out, or the coercion of compulsory membership, or an appeal bundled with private inducements, each an inducement the free-rider does not get.
No cheap or general version, because the private benefit is dear to supply and must be worth more to each than the cost of joining, and building a body encompassing enough to internalise the whole interest is exactly what the asymmetry makes hardest.

## Orders

Sweeten the ask.
Do not appeal to the shared good alone, which each will free-ride on, but attach to it a private benefit only contributors receive, since that is the one thing that makes acting pay for a member of a large group.
Accept the cost and the risk, and know that the deepest danger is that the sweetener becomes the point, the body filling with members who came for the private good while the cause it was built for slips to a pretext.

## Loses

The purity of the cause, spent to buy the many's motion.
Because a large group moves only on a private reason and not on the shared good, the seat must trade the cause it serves for the inducement that fills its ranks, and that inducement can capture the body, so the means it needs threatens the end it holds.
It keeps the one remedy that works and loses the cause's innocence, the mover whose lever can swallow what it levers.

## Drives

Toward the one who alone can rouse the many and must pay to do it,
supplying the private reason the bare collective good cannot, no free version of it,
so the seat that holds the one real remedy is the seat whose sweetener can become the cause, the bound on the trap bought at the price of the thing it means to save.
