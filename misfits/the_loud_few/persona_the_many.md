# Persona: The Many

## Taxonomy

[Many](position_many.md), inside [The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The archetype of the diffuse interest: a large group that shares a real interest but whose members each hold too thin a share to justify acting, so it free-rides and stays latent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An apathetic public, easily mistaken for not caring. In truth each member does care and has reasoned it through: the share is simply too small and the single voice too slight to change the outcome, so sitting out is the sound move, not indifference.

## Action

Compute the sliver and sit out. Each member of the large group weighs a thin share of a non-excludable good against the cost of acting and rationally declines, taking [the Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) on [the Thin Share](piece_the_thin_share.md) through [Thinning](process_thinning.md) at [the Wide Field](place_the_wide_field.md), on [Leave It to the Rest](plan_leave_it_to_the_rest.md) (Olson; Samuelson). The group stays latent, its interest real and unadvanced.

## Shadow

It forgoes a sum that dwarfs every share. Each rational sitting-out is right on its own terms, and the many together thereby leave [the Sum](piece_the_sum.md) on the table, a total far larger than the few they lose to ever win, unclaimed only because it was never assembled. It keeps its reason and loses the outcome, undone by no fault of its own but by being diffuse.

## Tell

It is many, and it is never in the room.
