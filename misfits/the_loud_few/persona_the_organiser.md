# Persona: The Organiser

## Taxonomy

[Organiser](position_organiser.md), inside [The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The archetype of the one who would rouse the many: the entrepreneur who cannot move a large group with the collective good itself and so must supply a private reason to act.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A champion of the cause, and sincerely so. It wants the many's shared interest advanced and works to build the body that would advance it, appealing to the good all would win together.

## Action

Bundle a private benefit with the cause. Because the collective good is one each would free-ride on, the appeal to it alone moves no one, so the organiser hands [the Selective Incentive](piece_the_selective_incentive.md) through [Sweetening](process_sweetening.md) at [the Turnstile](place_the_turnstile.md), on [Sweeten the Ask](plan_sweeten_the_ask.md), a members-only good the free-rider does not get, and the many finally chip in for it (Olson). It pays the whole cost of that inducement.

## Shadow

The organisation can become the sweetener rather than the cause. The selective incentive that alone moves the many is costly to supply and pulls the body toward serving it, so the members come for the private good and the shared interest it was built for slips to a pretext. It keeps the one lever that works and risks the cause it works for, the mover whose means can swallow its end.

## Tell

It offers a members-only good, never the cause alone.
