# Piece: The Free Ride

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The non-excludable collective good, enjoyed whether or not one paid toward it, so each member would rather let the others carry the cost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Wide Field](place_the_wide_field.md), where [the Many](persona_the_many.md) stand: once the good is provided, no member can be kept from it, paid up or not.
No gate, no ticket, no way to sort contributors from non-contributors after the fact; the good arrives the same for everyone the moment it arrives for anyone.

## Load Bearing

Samuelson's non-excludable public good (Samuelson, 1954): where nobody can be shut out of a benefit once it exists, each rational actor prefers that someone else pay for it, since paying buys nothing that not paying would not also deliver.
Guard against reading the free-rider as a cheat or a shirker: free-riding is the rational response to a good from which no one can be excluded, not a moral failing. The same non-excludability that makes the good worth wanting is what makes paying for it individually pointless.

## Apparent

It looks like freeloading, taking without contributing, letting others do the work and collecting the reward anyway.
That reading assumes a queue-jump that never happened: there is no line to jump, because the good was never fenced off from anyone in the first place.
