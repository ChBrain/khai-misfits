# Position: Few

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The seat of the concentrated interest, a small group whose members each hold a stake large enough to be worth the whole cost of acting, so it organises and prevails.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A stake concentrated enough to act on: an interest large per head, so that acting to secure it pays even when few share the cost.
A sound reason to organise: because the group is small, each member's contribution is not lost in a crowd and each carries a real share of the cost and the gain, so combining is worth it.
No need of the many, because the good it presses for is won by whoever shows up to press, and it reliably shows up while the diffuse many do not.

## Orders

Press the case.
Combine with the others who hold the same concentrated stake and carry it before the office, because a stake large enough per head makes acting pay and the small group is privileged to form where the large one cannot.
Know that the field is yours not by any theft but because you are the only voice that arrives, so ask plainly and take what the asking wins.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and yet its winning helps open the tilt.
Because it alone organises and the many stay latent, the outcome bends its way through no bribe and no malice, and that warranted victory is what leaves the diffuse majority unrepresented, so its sound pressing helps tilt the field.
It keeps its gain and loses no cause for blame, the winner the asymmetry rewards and the one whose showing up decides.

## Drives

Toward the one whose stake is worth acting on and who therefore acts,
combining because the concentration of its interest makes the cost worth bearing, no conspiracy in it,
so the seat the asymmetry rewards so reliably is the seat whose warranted organising is the mirror of the many's silence, the few who win because only the few show up.
