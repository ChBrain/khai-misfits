# Plot: When the Many Move

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Turnstile](place_the_turnstile.md), or a sudden rousing across [the Wide Field](place_the_wide_field.md), where the latent many stop being latent.

## Action

A moral entrepreneur, a mass movement, or a fall in coordination cost rouses the latent many, so [the Many](persona_the_many.md) do act, and the trap does not always hold.
The break comes where a [Selective Incentive](piece_the_selective_incentive.md), an entrepreneur, or cheap coordination supplies what the bare collective good cannot.

## Stage

The hedge, and it is load-bearing: the failure is a tendency conditioned on group size, non-excludability, and the absence of selective incentives, not a law that collective action always fails (Olson).
Large groups do mobilise, through moral entrepreneurs, mass movements, or falling coordination costs, whenever something beyond the bare collective good is supplied.

## Tension

The latency breaks exactly when a private reason or cheap coordination arrives, so the trap is real, common, and bounded.
