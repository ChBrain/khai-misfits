# Process: Sweetening

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). Borne at [the Turnstile](place_the_turnstile.md), initiated by [the Organiser](persona_the_organiser.md), in [A Private Reason](plot_a_private_reason.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Organiser](persona_the_organiser.md), unable to move a latent group on the collective good alone.
Begins once a shared interest exists that no amount of appealing to it in the abstract has moved anyone to act on.

## Direction

From a latent many toward a mobilised one, at cost.
Sweetening carries the many from sitting out to passing through [the Turnstile](place_the_turnstile.md), each now acting for a private reason attached to the collective cause rather than for the cause on its own.

## Lever

A selective incentive, a private benefit given only to those who contribute.
Where the collective good cannot supply a motive strong enough for any one member, a benefit withheld from free-riders and handed only at the turnstile supplies it instead, the one lever proven to move a large group (Olson).

## Echo

A group in motion, and a cost the organiser must keep paying.
What sweetening leaves behind is [the Selective Incentive](piece_the_selective_incentive.md), now doing the work the shared interest alone could not, for as long as the organiser can keep supplying it.
