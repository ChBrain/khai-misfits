# Persona: The Office

## Taxonomy

[Office](position_office.md), inside [The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). The archetype of the seat that decides: the office that acts on whoever organises to reach it, so the tilt to concentrated interests runs through it without any bribe.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An impartial servant of the public, and honestly meant. It would weigh every interest fairly if every interest came before it, and it believes it does exactly that: it answers the case that is made.

## Action

Hear and answer the room. The office acts on the organised voice that reaches it, taking the case the few bring through [Tilting](process_tilting.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md), on [Answer Who Asks](plan_answer_who_asks.md), and only the few reliably arrive, so it answers them and meets [the Tilt](piece_the_tilt.md) (Wilson). It is not bought; it simply hears one voice where the many sent none.

## Shadow

Only the few are ever present, so its fairness tilts. It would serve the many if the many came, and that is just what the asymmetry prevents, so its evenhanded answering of whoever shows up becomes, in a room only the few enter, a standing bias toward the few. It keeps its good faith and still bends the outcome, the impartial seat through which the tilt is done.

## Tell

It is not bought, and it still bends to the few.
