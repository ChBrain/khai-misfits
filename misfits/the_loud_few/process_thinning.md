# Process: Thinning

## Taxonomy

[The Loud Few](play_the_loud_few.md). Borne at [the Wide Field](place_the_wide_field.md), initiated by [the Many](persona_the_many.md), in [Not My Nickel](plot_not_my_nickel.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Many](persona_the_many.md), each doing the same private sum across [the Wide Field](place_the_wide_field.md).
Begins the moment a real shared interest is held by a group large enough that no single member's stake decides anything.

## Direction

From a real shared interest toward each member sitting out.
As the group grows, each member's share of the good and each member's influence over whether it is provided both shrink, so thinning carries the field from a genuine common cause toward a standing absence of anyone acting on it.

## Lever

A share and an influence that both shrink with group size.
A member's slice of a non-excludable good grows thinner as the group grows larger, and their own contribution grows less perceptible in the outcome, until acting costs more than the sliver it buys and sitting out is the rational call (Olson; Samuelson).

## Echo

A field where the interest is real and no one has moved on it.
What thinning leaves behind is [the Thin Share](piece_the_thin_share.md), the residue in every member's hands once the sum has been done and found not worth acting on.
