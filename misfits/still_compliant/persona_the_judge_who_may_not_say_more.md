# Persona: The Judge Who May Not Say More

## Taxonomy

[Bench](position_bench.md). The court that would once have asked whether more was required.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Correct, and correctly bounded.
Would once have been able to hold that a whole calling had unduly lagged.
Is not deferring to anybody: is applying a statute, which is the job.

## Action

Reads the deeming clause and gives it the effect it was enacted to have.
Finds the programme conformed, the attestation current, the defence made out.
Does not reach the question of whether the framework was enough, because the legislature reached it first.

## Shadow

That the door was closed by legislation rather than by deference.
**Custom was never conclusive and a harbour is**, so being a more searching judge does not reopen it: Hand's
crack survives everywhere the legislature has not spoken and nowhere it has, and this is a case where it has.

## Tell

Is the element that shows what the play is not.
Is the element that would have been the way out in 1932 and is the statute's own subject in 2018.
