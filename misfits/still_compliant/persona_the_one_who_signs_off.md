# Persona: The One Who Signs Off

## Taxonomy

[Budget](position_budget.md). The officer who must decide how much is enough and defend the number.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, competent, and accountable for the spend.
Wants the firm secure and wants to be able to say why each pound went where it did.
Would be replaced by somebody who behaved identically.

## Action

Maps the programme onto the framework, control by control, completely.
Puts the cost of doing so to a board, and gets it, because the return is legible.
Does not propose the measure past the line, because there is nothing to say about its return.

## Shadow

That nobody had to tell them to stop.
**Past the line the spend buys nothing the law will recognise**, and the competitor who declines is equally
defended, so stopping is the recommendation any careful officer makes and there is no meeting at which
anybody chooses to be less secure.

## Tell

Is the element people reach for when they want somebody to blame.
Is the element that behaves the same way whoever is sitting in it.
