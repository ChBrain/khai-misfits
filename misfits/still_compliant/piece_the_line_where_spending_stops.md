# Piece: The Line Where Spending Stops

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md). The point past which effort buys no protection, so no seat can justify crossing it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the board paper](place_the_board_paper.md), priced, with a column that has nothing to say beside it.
Reached through [stopping](process_stopping.md), by an officer recommending what any officer would.

## Load Bearing

Without it the convergence is a choice, and somebody can be blamed for it.
It is load bearing because **nobody has to be told to stop**: the measure past the line has no legal return
to state, the board must be told why each pound is spent, and the competitor who declines is defended
exactly as well, so there is no meeting at which anybody chooses to be less secure.

## Apparent

That a firm which cared about security would spend past it anyway.
Some do, where a customer, an insurer or a regulator with other powers pays them for it. Where nothing
outside the law does, spending past the line is a cost with no stated return, and an officer who proposes it
is asking a board to fund what they cannot justify.

## Yearbook

The ordinary discipline of a budget that must be defended, which is the same discipline in every firm and
not a symptom of any of them.
