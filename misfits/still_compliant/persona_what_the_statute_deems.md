# Persona: What the Statute Deems

## Taxonomy

[Harbour](position_harbour.md). The deeming clause itself, present in every case and embodied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Helpful, and genuinely so.
Is what a firm would ask for if asked, and what a legislature would offer if not.
Would be defended by everybody in the play, including the person it costs.

## Action

Converts a written standard into an affirmative defence.
Says in advance what will count, so that a firm need not learn it from a judge after a breach.
Does not look at the security, because it was drafted not to need to.

## Shadow

That it cannot supply the certainty without specifying the conduct.
**A defence that turned on the quality of the security would be the open-ended reasonableness this clause
exists to replace**, so softening it does not reduce the harm: it returns the firm to not knowing, which is
itself a cause of spending badly.

## Tell

Is the element nobody would remove.
Is the element without which the play does not run.
