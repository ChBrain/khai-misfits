# Position: Budget

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md). The seat that must decide how much is enough, occupied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A finite sum, competing claims on it, and an obligation to justify what it spends.
A legal test it can now read in advance, which is what it always wanted and never had.
Competitors reading the same test, spending against the same line.

## Orders

Fund what protects the firm, and be able to say why each pound was spent.
Meet the named framework, completely, because that is what the defence requires.
Do not fund what buys no protection, because that is what it means to hold a budget.

## Loses

The reason to keep going past the line.
**Every measure up to the text buys protection and every measure past it buys none**, so the seat is not
choosing to be less secure: it is declining to fund a cost with no legal return, against competitors equally
defended, which is the recommendation any careful holder of this seat would make.

## Drives

Toward a programme mapped exactly onto the framework and no further,
by an officer doing the job the seat exists for and a board doing the job it exists for,
so the convergence is a property of the test rather than of anybody's appetite for risk.
