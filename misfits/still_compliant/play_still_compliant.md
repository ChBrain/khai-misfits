# Play: Still Compliant

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Still Compliant.
Here is a real problem, and a sensible answer to it. A firm holding other people's data cannot know in advance what a court will one day call reasonable care, and it will find out only after a breach, from a judge, with hindsight and a plaintiff in the room. That uncertainty is not merely uncomfortable: it is a genuine reason to spend badly, because a firm that cannot tell which spending will count for it has no way to choose between the measures that would help and the measures that merely look diligent. So a legislature does the obvious kind thing. It names a published framework, written by people who know the subject, and says that a firm whose written programme reasonably conforms to it has an affirmative defence. Ohio did this first, in 2018, and the states that followed copied the shape. Certainty is supplied, the firm now knows what is being asked, and the stated purpose is to move firms upward toward a standard many of them were below.
And here is what the certainty does to the spending. The defence attaches to conformity with the text. It does not attach to the quality of the security, and there is no version of it that could, because a defence that turned on quality would be the open-ended reasonableness the statute was written to replace. So every pound spent up to the line buys protection and every pound spent past it buys none. The officer who proposes the extra measure is proposing a cost with no legal return, to a board that must justify its spend, in a market where the competitor who declines is equally defended. **Nobody has to be told to stop.** Stopping at the line is what a careful officer recommends and what a careful board approves, and there is no meeting at which anybody chooses to be less secure.
And here is the part that makes it a structure and not a complaint. The framework is a document, and a document has a date. The hazard has no date. It moves, continuously, driven by people whose whole occupation is to move it, and it does not consult the revision schedule of a standards body. So the distance between what the text asks and what the hazard now requires opens quietly and by nobody's decision, and it opens fastest in exactly the places the text has not yet learned to name. The programme is not decaying. It is doing precisely what it was built to do, against a world that has moved on.
And here is the morning after. The breach lands, and the firm is still compliant. The mapping is complete, the attestation is current, the auditor signed it, and the defence holds, because it was designed to hold and this is the case it was designed for. The person whose data went has a real injury, provable causation, and an answer that is entirely true: the programme conformed to the named framework, which the legislature said was enough. There is no departure to point at, no corner cut, and no moment at which anybody chose wrongly.
And here is the door that used to be open. In 1932 Learned Hand held that a whole calling may have unduly lagged in the adoption of new and available devices, and that courts must in the end say what is required. That crack has stayed open for ninety years, rarely used and always available. A statutory harbour closes it by statute: the court is not being asked to defer to a practice, it is being told by the legislature that conformity is a defence, which is the one thing Hand refused to allow custom to be. When Florida's legislature passed the same design in 2024, its governor vetoed it, saying the bill could result in Floridians' data being less secure because it provides across-the-board protections for only substantially complying with standards, and that it incentivises doing the minimum. He named the misfit and declined to sign it, and the states either side of him signed theirs.
And here is the bound that keeps it honest. The harbour is a ceiling only where the conformity is conclusive rather than evidential, only where the standard revises more slowly than the hazard moves, and only where nothing outside the law, no customer, no insurer, no regulator with other powers, pays a firm for exceeding it. Where those fail, a harbour can raise a floor and raise nothing else, which is what its drafters intended and what its supporters observe. The house stages the case where they hold, and holds the bound in view: that where the protection is for matching the text, the text is what gets matched, and what the text has not caught up with is nobody's job.

## Arc

The bend is this: where a statute makes conformity to a written standard a defence, the protection attaches to matching the text and not to meeting the hazard, so effort converges on the text and stops, and the text does not move while the hazard does.
It runs on a certainty that was worth supplying.
A firm cannot know in advance what a court will later call reasonable, and finds out only after a breach, so the uncertainty is itself a reason to spend badly, and a legislature that names a published framework and deems conformity a defence is solving a real problem in the obvious way (the Ohio Data Protection Act).
And the defence attaches to the text, because it cannot attach to anything else. A defence that turned on the quality of the security would restore the open-ended reasonableness the statute exists to replace, so conformity is the only thing a harbour can be made of, and the framework's words are the whole of what the law will read.
And so the spending stops at the line. Every measure up to the text buys protection and every measure past it buys none, so the officer proposing more is proposing a cost with no legal return to a board that must justify it, against competitors equally defended, and stopping is what a careful person recommends.
And the standard has a date and the hazard has not. The text is fixed at the revision that named it; the threat is worked on continuously by people paid to move it, and the gap opens by nobody's decision, widest where the text has not yet learned to name the thing.
And the morning after, everyone is compliant. The mapping is complete, the attestation current, the defence holds because this is the case it was built for, and the answer to a real injury is that the programme conformed to the framework the legislature named.
And the door that used to be open is shut by statute. Hand held in 1932 that a calling may lag and that courts must in the end say what is required; a harbour tells the court that conformity is a defence, which is precisely what he refused to let custom be (The T.J. Hooper).
And the whole is held against its limit. It needs the conformity to be conclusive rather than evidential, the standard to revise more slowly than the hazard moves, and nothing outside the law to pay for exceeding it: where those fail, the same statute raises a floor and does nothing else, which is what its drafters meant by it.

## Stakes

Whether a rule that tells you what is enough can avoid being a rule about what is plenty.
Every plot but the last moves the same way: toward the text, which is legible, costed, auditable and defended, and away from the hazard, which is none of those things and does not stop. The structure cannot supply certainty without specifying conduct, and cannot specify conduct without making the specification the thing that is met, because a defence that turned on the quality of the security would be the open-ended reasonableness the harbour exists to replace: the certainty and the ceiling are the one clause read twice. The production cannot make the spending continue past the line, because past the line it buys nothing the law will recognise, and the officer who proposes it is asking a board to fund a cost with no return against competitors who are equally defended. It can only show that nobody is a villain, that the legislature is solving a real problem and says so in the statute's own purpose, that the framework is good and its authors knew their subject, that the officer stopping at the line is doing what any officer would, and that the court declining to ask for more has been told not to by the legislature. And it holds the bound in view, because the harbour is a ceiling only where the conformity is conclusive rather than evidential, only where the standard revises more slowly than the hazard moves, and only where nothing outside the law pays a firm for exceeding it, so a harbour can and sometimes does raise a floor and nothing else. The whole of the misfit is that where the protection is for matching the text, the text is what gets matched, and what the text has not caught up with is nobody's job.
