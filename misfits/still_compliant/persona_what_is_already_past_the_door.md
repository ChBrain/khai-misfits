# Persona: What Is Already Past the Door

## Taxonomy

[Hazard](position_hazard.md). The technique that did not exist when the list was written, embodied by nobody in the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Absent from every document in the play.
Is not mentioned at the board, not named in the framework, and not covered by the attestation.
Is the only party in the production that is not spending anything to stay where it is.

## Action

Works continuously, driven by people whose occupation is to move it.
Prefers what is not written down, because what is written down is what is defended.
Arrives without notice and without malice toward anybody in particular.

## Shadow

That it needs no plan against the firm to defeat it.
**The gap opens fastest in exactly the places the text has not yet learned to name**, so the programme is
not decaying and nobody has become careless: the programme is doing precisely what it was built to do,
against a world that has moved on.

## Tell

Is the element no audit can find.
Is the element that appears in the play only once, at the end, and by then it has already happened.
