# Piece: The Deeming Clause

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md). Conformity with a named standard is a defence, so protection attaches to the text and not to the security.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the attestation](place_the_attestation.md), operating by law on every covered entity.
Read once at [the morning after](place_the_morning_after.md), where it does the thing it was written to do.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a story about a lazy firm, and the remedy is a better officer.
It is load bearing because **a defence that turned on the quality of the security would be the open-ended
reasonableness the clause exists to replace**: the certainty and the ceiling are one clause read twice, and
there is no draft of the statute that supplies the first and withholds the second.

## Apparent

That the drafters could simply have required more, or required the latest revision, or required a good
outcome.
Requiring more moves the line. Requiring the latest revision moves it on the framework's schedule. Requiring
a good outcome is the uncertainty again under another name, and the firm is back to finding out from a judge.

## Yearbook

Ohio's Data Protection Act of 2018 and the statutes that copied its shape, whose stated purpose is to move
firms upward toward a standard many were below.
