# Position: Bench

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md). The seat that would once have asked whether more was required, occupied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A statute telling it that conformity is a defence.
A doctrine, ninety years old, holding that a whole calling may lag and that courts must in the end say what
is required.
No way to apply the second without disapplying the first.

## Orders

Apply the statute the legislature wrote.
Do not substitute a judicial view of adequacy for a legislative one, because that is not the seat's job.
Give the defence the effect it was enacted to have.

## Loses

The question it used to be able to ask.
**Custom was never conclusive and a harbour is**, so this is not deference to a practice: the legislature
has answered in advance the one question Hand reserved to the courts, and the seat cannot reopen it by
being more careful.

## Drives

Toward a judgment that is correct, applies the law as written, and cannot reach the thing that went wrong,
by a court doing exactly what a court is for,
so the closing of the door is an act of the legislature and not a failure of the bench.
