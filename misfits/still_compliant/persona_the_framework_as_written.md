# Persona: The Framework as Written

## Taxonomy

[Standard](position_standard.md). The published control catalogue, current at the revision that named it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, thorough, and worth conforming to.
Was written by people who knew the subject and would be worth following if no statute mentioned it.
Is the part of the play that would survive any audit of its quality.

## Action

Sets out what a competent programme contains, item by item, checkably.
Is revised when the body that maintains it meets.
Says nothing at all about anything it has not yet learned to name.

## Shadow

That it has a date, and the thing it was written against does not.
**The interval between revisions is a property of the institution and not of the threat**, so a better
framework does not fix this: it moves the line, on its own schedule, and remains a document while the hazard
remains a process.

## Tell

Is the element everybody in the play would praise.
Is the element whose quality is not what is failing.
