# Still Compliant: Reference

The research warrant for [Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md).
The term is the house's; the play runs on the statutory safe-harbour design introduced by Ohio in 2018 and
copied since, on the tort doctrine it displaces, and on the objection a governor put on the record when he
declined to sign the same design, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the compliance safe harbour as **a certainty that can only be supplied by specifying conduct**: a
legislature, facing firms that cannot know in advance what a court will call reasonable care, names a
published framework and deems a programme that reasonably conforms to it a defence. The problem is real, the
framework is good, and the statute's stated purpose is to move firms upward. The play models the documented
mechanics, the deeming clause that attaches protection to conformity, the mapping of a programme onto a
control list, the point past which further effort buys no legal return, the standard's fixed revision date
against a hazard with none, the defence that holds the morning after a breach, and the judicial question
that the legislature has answered in advance, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of
a lazy firm or a captured legislature.

The governing law is that **a protection for matching a text is a protection the text collects**, so effort
converges on the specified conduct and stops there, and the specified conduct is fixed at the date somebody
wrote it while the hazard it was written against is not fixed at all.

What makes it structural is that **the certainty and the ceiling are one clause read twice**. A defence that
turned on the quality of the security would be the open-ended reasonableness the harbour exists to replace,
so conformity is the only material a harbour can be built from. There is no version of the statute that
supplies the certainty and withholds the ceiling, which is why this is a trap and not a drafting fault.

What makes it costly is that **nobody has to be told to stop**. The officer proposing a measure beyond the
framework is proposing a cost with no legal return, to a board that must justify its spend, in a market
where the competitor who declines is equally defended. Stopping at the line is what a careful officer
recommends and a careful board approves, and there is no meeting at which anybody chooses to be less secure.

It is distinct from the house's **What Everyone Else Does**, which is the nearest law in the house and shares
one arm of the mechanism: there too the party who goes first pays for the improvement and gets nothing for
it. The cut is in what the standard is made of. **Custom is practice, so it cannot age relative to
practice**: if the calling improves, the duty improves with it, and the play's engine is that the duty
tracks the average too faithfully. A framework is a document with a revision date, so it can be five years
behind the hazard and still confer the defence, and improvement by the whole field moves it not at all.
Stipulate that every firm spontaneously adopts a better method and custom's duty rises to meet them, while
the harbour sits exactly where it was written. And where Hand left the crack open, that a calling may lag
and courts must in the end say what is required, a statute closes it: **the court is not deferring to a
practice, it is being told by the legislature that conformity is a defence**, which is the one thing he
refused to let custom be.

It is distinct from the house's **The Letter of the Law**, and the slate that proposed this play named that
as the risk. It is not the risk. Specification gaming needs a gap between the proxy and the intent, and a
route that scores the objective while missing the goal. **Here there is no gap and no route**: conforming to
the framework is exactly the conduct the legislature asked for, the firm does precisely that, and nothing is
gamed, exploited or routed around. Stipulate that the specified conduct captures the drafters' intent
perfectly and completely and The Letter of the Law has nothing left, while this play is untouched, because
its harm is the absence of anything above the specification rather than the presence of a perverse route
through it.

It is distinct from the house's **Good Enough to Hold**, where a rule points at a private grade and the
grade becomes a permit: there a third party sells the assessment, its accuracy stops being convertible into
revenue, and the rule creates the grader's incumbency. **Here there is no grader and nothing is bought**: the
framework is public, its authors are paid by nobody in the transaction, and it does not become less accurate
as it becomes more used. It becomes older, which is a different failure with a different remedy.

And it is distinct from the house's **Nobody Sues for Taking It Down**, which turns on the same statutory
word. There a safe harbour prices two errors differently and the host takes the cheap one at a third party's
expense; the harm is over-removal and the party who pays was not in the transaction. Here the two errors are
not priced against each other at all: the firm does the thing the statute asked, at the level the statute
asked, and the harm is what was never done.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the
  legislature as captured, the framework's authors as careless, or the firm as cynical. The uncertainty the
  statute answers is real and is itself a cause of bad spending. The framework is written by people who know
  the subject and would be worth conforming to if no statute mentioned it. The officer who stops at the line
  is recommending what any officer would recommend, because past the line the spend buys nothing the law
  will recognise and the competitor who declines is equally defended. The failure is authored by nobody and
  emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that **a protection for matching a text is a protection the
  text collects**.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into better drafting. Make the harbour evidential
  rather than conclusive and it works, and hands back the certainty the statute existed to supply. Require
  the latest revision of the framework and it works, and moves the ceiling without removing it, at a cost
  borne on the framework's schedule rather than the hazard's. Condition the defence on outcomes and it
  works, and is the open-ended reasonableness again under another name. Let a court find conformity
  insufficient and it works, and is Hand's crack, ninety years open and rarely used, now with a statute
  arguing the other way.
- **Held against its own limit**: the harbour is a ceiling only where the conformity is **conclusive rather
  than evidential**, only where the standard **revises more slowly than the hazard moves**, and only where
  **nothing outside the law**, no customer, no insurer, no regulator holding other powers, pays a firm for
  exceeding it. Where those conditions fail, the same statute can raise a floor and do nothing else, which
  is what its drafters intended and what its supporters observe. The play stages the case where they hold
  and says so.
- **Credit, not claim**: the statutes, the case and the veto above are other people's acts, packaged and
  attributed here, never claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's. **No empirical claim is made
  about magnitude**: the play does not assert how far spending actually converges under these statutes, how
  many firms sit at the line, or that any breach has been shown to have been caused by the design, because
  the pass that wrote it found no study measuring any of that. It stages a mechanism named on the record by
  a governor who declined to sign it, against a statutory purpose that says the opposite, and lets the
  contradiction stand as the misfit. **The primary texts could not be read in the pass that cited them**,
  because the statutory codes, the law-review archives and the publisher hosts are all blocked to it, so the
  statutes, the case, the veto and its stated reason were verified by search and corroborated across
  independent reports, and Kaplow is cited for the bibliography and the direction of his argument and for
  no proposition, figure or result.
