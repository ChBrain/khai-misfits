# Process: Stopping

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md). Reaching the point at which further effort buys no protection, and going no further.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A completed mapping and a budget that must be justified.
At [the board paper](place_the_board_paper.md), each round, by an officer recommending what any officer would.

## Direction

Toward the line, and no further.
**Nobody has to be told to stop.** The measure past the line has no legal return to state, to a board that
must be told why, in a market where the competitor who declines is defended exactly as well.

## Lever

The legal return, which is the currency the room reads and the only one the statute pays in.

## Echo

A spend converged on exactly the text,
recommended by a careful officer and approved by a careful board,
with no meeting at which anybody chose to be less secure.
