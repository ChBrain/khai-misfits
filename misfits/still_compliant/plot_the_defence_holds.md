## Tension

The plot has to make the verdict land as correct rather than as an escape.
The defence is not a loophole and not a technicality: it is the clause working exactly as enacted, in the
case it was enacted for, and any other outcome would mean the certainty the statute promised had not been
supplied.
So the answer to the injury is true in every particular. The programme conformed to the framework the
legislature named, which the legislature said was enough. **There is no departure to point at and no moment
at which anybody chose wrongly**, which is why there is nothing further to say, and why the harm has to be
attributed to the arrangement or to nobody.
