## Load Bearing

Without it the failure has an author and the play has a villain.
It is load bearing because **there is no departure to point at and no moment at which anybody chose
wrongly**: the mapping was complete, the attestation current, the framework good, the officer careful and
the judge correct, so the harm has to be attributed to the arrangement or to nobody.

## Apparent

That the defence is a loophole, and closing it would fix this.
It is not a loophole. It is the clause working exactly as enacted, in the case it was enacted for, and any
other outcome would mean the certainty the statute promised had not been supplied.

## Yearbook

A governor's veto message of 2024, which put the objection on the record before any such case arose: that
across-the-board protection for only substantially complying with standards could leave data less secure,
and incentivises doing the minimum.
