# Position: Hazard

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md). The seat that moves, occupied by nobody in the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

No revision date, no committee, and no obligation to be legible.
Continuous effort behind it, from people whose whole occupation is to move it.
An interest in exactly the places a published list has not yet learned to name.

## Orders

Go where the door is.
Prefer what nobody has written down, because what is written down is what is defended.
Consult no publication cycle, because none applies.

## Loses

Nothing. It is the only seat in the play that is not spending anything to stay where it is.

## Drives

Toward the distance between a fixed text and a moving threat,
by nobody's decision inside the production and no failure anybody could be shown,
so the gap is not a lapse in the programme but the programme working as built against a world that moved.
