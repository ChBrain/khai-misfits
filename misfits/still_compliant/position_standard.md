# Position: Standard

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md). The seat that says what good looks like, occupied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A published catalogue of controls, written by people who knew the subject.
A revision date, and a committee that will meet again on its own schedule.
No mechanism for noticing that the world has moved, other than the next revision.

## Orders

Set out what a competent programme contains, in terms an auditor can check.
Be specific enough to be conformed to, because a standard nobody can conform to is not a standard.
Revise when the body meets, which is the only rate at which a document can change.

## Loses

Its currency, continuously and by nobody's fault.
**A standard is a document and a document has a date**, so the interval between revisions is a property of
the institution that maintains it and not of the threat it was written against, and the gap opens fastest in
exactly the places the text has not yet learned to name.

## Drives

Toward a programme that is checkable and complete against a list,
by a body doing careful work at the pace careful work is done,
so the distance between the list and the hazard is authored by no one and visible to no audit.
