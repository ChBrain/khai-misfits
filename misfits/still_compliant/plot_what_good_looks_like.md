# Plot: What Good Looks Like

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Firms that cannot know in advance what a court will call reasonable, and a legislature willing to tell them.

## Action

[Deeming](process_deeming.md): [What the Statute Deems](persona_what_the_statute_deems.md), in [the harbour seat](position_harbour.md), works [Deem It Enough](plan_deem_it_enough.md), while [The Framework as Written](persona_the_framework_as_written.md), in [the standard seat](position_standard.md), supplies [the control list](piece_the_control_list.md) on [Say What Good Looks Like](plan_say_what_good_looks_like.md).

## Stage

[The Attestation](place_the_attestation.md): a published catalogue, a statute naming it, and a defence available to whoever conforms.

## Tension

This is the plot that has to establish there is no fault here, or nothing after it reads correctly.
The uncertainty is real, and is itself a cause of spending badly: a firm that cannot tell which measures will
count for it has no way to choose between the ones that help and the ones that merely look diligent.
[The control list](piece_the_control_list.md) is good, written by people who knew the subject, and worth
conforming to if no statute mentioned it.
And [the deeming clause](piece_the_deeming_clause.md) is the harder half. **A defence that turned on the
quality of the security would be the open-ended reasonableness the clause exists to replace**, so conformity
is the only material a harbour can be built from. The legislature is not choosing between certainty with a
ceiling and certainty without one. It is supplying certainty, in the only way certainty can be supplied,
and **the certainty and the ceiling are one clause read twice**.
