# Plot: The Line, Priced

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The programme costed against the named framework, and the line visible for the first time.

## Action

[Conforming](process_conforming.md) then [Stopping](process_stopping.md): [The One Who Signs Off](persona_the_one_who_signs_off.md), in [the budget seat](position_budget.md), works [Buy the Defence](plan_buy_the_defence.md) and meets [the line where spending stops](piece_the_line_where_spending_stops.md).

## Stage

[The Board Paper](place_the_board_paper.md): a mapping, a cost, a return stated in the currency the room reads, and a recommendation.

## Tension

The plot has to show the convergence happening without anybody choosing it.
Every measure up to the text buys protection and every measure past it buys none, so the paper has a column
for the legal return of what it proposes and nothing whatever to write in that column beside the measure
that would go further.
**Nobody has to be told to stop.** The officer is not indifferent to security and the board is not reckless:
the officer is declining to propose a cost they cannot justify, the board is declining to fund one it has
not been given a reason for, and the competitor who declines the same measure is defended exactly as well.
Closes with the spend converged on exactly the text, recommended by a careful officer and approved by a
careful board, and no meeting anywhere in the firm at which anybody chose to be less secure.
