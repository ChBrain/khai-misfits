# Process: Deeming

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md). Converting conformity with a written standard into a defence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A legislature facing firms that cannot know in advance what a court will call reasonable.
By statute, at [the attestation](place_the_attestation.md), before any breach and without looking at any programme.

## Direction

Toward a firm that knows what is being asked.
This is the whole content of the process and not a step in it: certainty supplied after the fact is not
weaker certainty, it is a judgment, which is the thing the statute exists to pre-empt.

## Lever

Specificity, which is the only thing that can be turned and cannot be turned down.
Deeming something vaguely does not reduce the harm; it produces a firm that still cannot tell what will
count, which is the condition the statute was written to end.

## Echo

A protection stated in advance,
attached to conformity because nothing else can be settled in advance,
and collected, in full, by whoever matches the text.
