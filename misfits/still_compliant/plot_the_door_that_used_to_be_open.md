## Tension

The plot has to show that the closing was legislative and not judicial, and then hold the whole play against
its own limit.
In 1932 Learned Hand held that a whole calling may have unduly lagged in the adoption of new and available
devices, that courts must in the end say what is required, and that some precautions are so imperative that
even universal disregard will not excuse omission. **Custom was never conclusive and a harbour is**, so this
is not deference to a practice: the legislature has answered in advance the one question he reserved, and a
more searching judge cannot reopen it.
And the bound. The harbour is a ceiling only where the conformity is conclusive rather than evidential, only
where the standard revises more slowly than the hazard moves, and only where nothing outside the law, no
customer, no insurer, no regulator holding other powers, pays a firm for exceeding it. Where those fail the
same statute raises a floor and does nothing else, which is what its drafters intended and what its
supporters observe.
