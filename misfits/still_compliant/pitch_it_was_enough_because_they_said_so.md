# Pitch: It Was Enough Because They Said So

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reasonable throughout, and audited.
Exact about what was asked, what was done, and the distance between the two, which is nothing.
Unwilling to call the legislature captured or the firm cynical, because neither is required.

## Undertow

Under a well-run programme, one fact about protection and one fact about documents.
A protection for matching a text is a protection the text collects.
And a text has a revision date, while the thing it was written against does not.

## Nerve

The unease of a defence that works.
Nobody cut a corner. Nobody was warned and ignored it. Nobody chose to be less secure.
The one thing missing is everything above the line, and there was never a seat that wanted it.

## Echo

After the run: a control list mapped item by item,
an attestation an auditor would pass on any morning,
a technique that did not exist when the list was written,
and a firm that is still compliant, saying so, truthfully.
