# Position: Harbour

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md). The seat that deems conformity enough, occupied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

One clause, applied to every covered entity and every programme.
The power to convert a written standard into a defence, which is the only power it has.
No capacity whatever to look at a firm's security, because it was written not to need to.

## Orders

Say in advance what will count, so that a firm can know before the breach rather than after it.
Attach the protection to conformity, because conformity is the only thing that can be settled in advance.
Do not condition it on the outcome, because that is the uncertainty this clause exists to remove.

## Loses

Nothing it wanted, and it wanted certainty.
**A defence that turned on the quality of the security would be the open-ended reasonableness the clause
exists to replace**, so the seat cannot supply the certainty and withhold the ceiling: they are one clause
read twice, and there is no draft of it that separates them.

## Drives

Toward a firm that knows what is being asked and can plan against it,
by a legislature solving a real problem in the only way a legislature can,
so what follows is a property of the instrument rather than of anybody's motive in writing it.
