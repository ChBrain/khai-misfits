# Plot: The Years Pass

## Taxonomy

[Still Compliant](play_still_compliant.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Nothing happens. The programme runs, the attestation is renewed, and the framework is not revised.

## Action

[Ageing](process_ageing.md): [What Is Already Past the Door](persona_what_is_already_past_the_door.md), in [the hazard seat](position_hazard.md), works [Go Where the Door Is](plan_go_where_the_door_is.md), and [the gap the standard does not name](piece_the_gap_the_standard_does_not_name.md) opens.

## Stage

[The Attestation](place_the_attestation.md): a document current at its revision, renewed each cycle against a list that has not changed.

## Tension

This is the plot that separates the play from every play about spending less.
**A standard is a document and a hazard is a process.** The framework's revision interval is a property of
the body that maintains it, and the technique that will be used is worked on continuously by people
consulting no publication cycle at all, so the distance opens by nobody's decision and fastest in exactly
the places the text has not yet learned to name.
And no audit can find it, because an audit is a comparison with the list, and a thing the list does not name
is not a finding. Seeing it would take somebody paid to look past the list, and the seat that would fund
that is [the seat that stopped at the line](position_budget.md).
Closes with the programme doing precisely what it was built to do, against a world that has moved on, and
the two facts not in tension anywhere.
