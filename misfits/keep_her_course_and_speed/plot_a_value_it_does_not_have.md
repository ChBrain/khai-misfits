## Tension

The settlement, which is the best-resourced anybody has proposed, and its price, which is the mechanism itself.
A ship that steers itself would obey the rule more consistently than people do, and that would be a real improvement. Which ship gives way, which side to alter to, how large the alteration must be: all precise, all implementable. Then the release condition, and **an algorithm cannot execute a judgement**, so the implementing work asks for the ambiguity to be removed and is entirely right to ask.
But the ambiguity is the store. **What is being asked for and what is being relied on are the same quantity**, and any value chosen becomes a moment other vessels must predict, so the fill produces the problem it was meant to remove at whatever value is chosen.
Which is why the strongest remedy available demonstrates the structure rather than leaving it: Rules 15 and 16 implement in an afternoon, and 17 is still being asked about.
