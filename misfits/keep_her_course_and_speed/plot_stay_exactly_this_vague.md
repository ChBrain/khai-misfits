## Tension

Here the play turns, and it turns on the rule's apparent weakness being its function.
The stand-on ship may act as soon as it becomes apparent that the other is not taking appropriate action, and _apparent_ is a judgement. Almost everybody who meets this reads it as a drafting failure and reaches for a criterion: a range, a time to closest approach, a closing rate.
Any of them would work as a criterion and none of them can be used. **A precise threshold is a moment at which the stand-on ship's behaviour changes discontinuously**, and the give-way ship would then have to predict that moment, which is exactly the unpredictability the holding was bought to remove.
So the vagueness is not where the rule leaks. **It is the last place the rule's own benefit is stored**, and every proposal to fix it is a proposal to spend it.
