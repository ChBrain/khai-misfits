# Plot: Somebody Has to Be Predictable

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two ships on converging courses, a steady bearing, a closing range.
[The Two-Body Problem](piece_the_two_body_problem.md), present before any rule is applied.

## Action

[Holding](process_holding.md): [The Held Course](persona_the_held_course.md), in [the predictability seat](position_predictability.md), keeps course and speed at [the crossing](place_the_crossing.md), on [Be Somewhere Knowable](plan_be_somewhere_knowable.md).

## Stage

[The Late Turn](persona_the_late_turn.md), in [the obligation seat](position_obligation.md), plans a way round something that is not planning a way round her.

## Tension

This plot's work is to establish that holding is **the achievement**, because a play that reads it as passivity has nothing to say later.
An avoidance problem with two moving parties is not determinate: each plan invalidates the other. Fix one trajectory and the encounter has an answer in it, so keeping course and speed is not deference but the thing that turns the other bridge's guess into a calculation. It resolves crossings tens of thousands of times a day and nobody writes anything down.
And it is not a convention that could have gone otherwise. **Whatever the regulations had said, somebody would have had to stop responding**, so the cost is a property of the geometry rather than of these particular words.
