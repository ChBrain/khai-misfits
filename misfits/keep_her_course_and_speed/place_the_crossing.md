# Place: The Crossing

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). Two ships on converging courses: ordinary, daily, and almost always uneventful.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two vessels, a steady bearing, a decreasing range.
One of them required to keep clear, the other required to hold.

## Holds

The play's first fact, which everything else depends on: **this works.**
The arrangement resolves crossings tens of thousands of times a day, without incident and without anybody writing anything down, because fixing one trajectory turns the other bridge's guess into a calculation.

## Offers

Determinacy, in a problem that has none until somebody stops manoeuvring.

## Withheld

Any response from the ship that may be watching most closely.
Her whole contribution is to be findable, so the arrangement's benefit is stored in one party's inaction and is redeemable only if the other party performs.
