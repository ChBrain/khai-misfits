# Persona: The Closing Window

## Taxonomy

[Margin](position_margin.md). The shrinking time in which any coordinated answer still works.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A quantity nobody is spending and everybody is losing.
It is not consumed by hesitation or by argument: it goes because two ships are approaching each other and time passes.
Wide enough, early on, that anything works. Narrow enough, later, that only the right thing does.

## Action

Narrows.
Narrows faster as the range closes, because closing speed is not constant in what it costs.
Reaches a width where two manoeuvres cannot both be absorbed.

## Shadow

That the rule spends its widest part on holding.
The stand-on ship's duty is heaviest exactly while there is most room, and lightest when there is least, so the arrangement is at its most restrictive in the stretch where a response would have been cheapest.
It ends having been spent entirely on being legible.

## Tell

Six miles is a conversation.
Six cables is not.
