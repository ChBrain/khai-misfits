# Piece: Both Turns at Once

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). The failure, made of two lawful acts arriving together.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the last two minutes](place_the_last_two_minutes.md), in the same few seconds.
Composed of [giving way](process_giving_way.md) discharged late and [judging](process_judging.md) concluded soundly.

## Load Bearing

Without it the harm needs somebody to have got something wrong, and the play becomes a story about a bad watch.
It is load bearing because **neither act is an error at the moment it is taken**: the give-way ship acted as her duty required, the stand-on ship acted as the rule permits on a conclusion sound on her evidence, and the collision is composed entirely of compliance.

## Apparent

A coincidence, and therefore rare enough to be tolerable.
It is not a coincidence. The two acts are triggered by the same event, the approach getting late, so they are correlated by construction and arrive together far more often than independence would suggest.

## Yearbook

Assembled from two obediences, spent at [the last two minutes](place_the_last_two_minutes.md).
Each manoeuvre is made against a picture the other has already invalidated.
