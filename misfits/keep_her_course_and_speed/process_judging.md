# Process: Judging

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). Deciding whether it has become apparent that the other ship is not acting.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A range that has closed further than expected with no alteration observed.
At [the bridge](place_the_bridge.md), against what is on the screen and out of the window.

## Direction

Toward a conclusion the rule requires and does not specify.
The stand-on ship may act as soon as it becomes apparent that the give-way ship is not taking appropriate action, so this seat must distinguish a ship that is late from a ship that is not coming, using an observation that is identical in both cases.
There is no criterion to apply. There is a judgement to make, and the rule says so.

## Lever

Experience, the picture, and the closing rate, weighed by somebody who knows they may be wrong.
And whichever way it goes, the reasoning is sound: acting too early spends the predictability, acting too late spends the room.

## Echo

A correct decision, on the available evidence,
that the other ship was not going to turn.
