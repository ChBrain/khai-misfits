# Piece: The Two-Body Problem

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). Why one party must be held fixed: an avoidance problem with two movers has no determinate answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the geometry of any encounter, before any rule is applied.
Solved at [the crossing](place_the_crossing.md), by one ship agreeing not to move.

## Load Bearing

Without it the stand-on duty is a convention that could as easily have gone the other way, or been dropped.
It is load bearing because it makes the holding **necessary rather than customary**: if both ships plan around each other, each plan invalidates the other, and the encounter becomes determinate the instant one trajectory is fixed.

## Apparent

An argument for having a rule, which it is.
It is also an argument that the rule's cost is not a design choice: whatever the regulations had said, somebody would have had to stop responding, so the mechanism is not an artefact of these particular words.

## Yearbook

A property of the geometry, exploited by the collision regulations, spent at [the crossing](place_the_crossing.md).
It is why this cannot be fixed by writing a better rule.
