# Process: Holding

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). Keeping course and speed: the duty that makes the other ship's problem solvable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A crossing in which this ship is the one to be kept clear of.
At [the crossing](place_the_crossing.md), from the moment risk of collision exists.

## Direction

Toward being a fixed object that somebody else can plan around.
This is the good half of the production and the play does not undercut it: an avoidance problem with two moving parties is not determinate, and it becomes determinate the instant one is held. Holding is what turns the other bridge's guess into a calculation.
It works. Crossings resolve on it tens of thousands of times a day and nobody writes anything down.

## Lever

Not moving, exactly and visibly, for as long as it takes.
Which is the whole instrument this seat has, so every second of it is margin converted into legibility.

## Echo

A bearing that does not change,
which is what the other ship needs to see
and is also the definition of a collision course.
