# Position: Margin

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). The seat of room: the time in which a coordinated answer still works.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Distance, converting into time at the closing rate.
Enough of it, early, that almost any manoeuvre works.
Not enough, later, for two manoeuvres to be absorbed at once.

## Orders

Be wide.
Narrow, as the range closes.
Reach a width where the encounter no longer tolerates being got wrong.

## Loses

Everything, and it loses it to the correct behaviour of both ships.
The stand-on duty is heaviest exactly while there is most room and lightest when there is least, so **the arrangement is at its most restrictive in the stretch where a response would have cost least**, and the reserve is gone by the time it is worth having.
It ends having been spent entirely on being legible.

## Drives

Toward giving everybody time,
which it does, for a while,
so the seat that holds the whole encounter's slack is the seat the rule spends first.
