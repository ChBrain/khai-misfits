# Position: Predictability

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). The seat of what holding buys: one trajectory fixed, so that another ship's problem has an answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A course and a speed, maintained exactly.
The entire determinacy of the encounter, supplied by not moving.
No other instrument, because holding is the whole of what this seat does.

## Orders

Keep course.
Keep speed.
Keep both while the range closes.

## Loses

Nothing to any error, and this seat is where the play gives the rule its due: **holding is the achievement.**
An avoidance problem with two moving parties is not determinate and becomes determinate the moment one is fixed, so this is not passivity or deference but the thing that turns a guess into a calculation, and it resolves crossings tens of thousands of times a day.
It ends having converted every second of margin into legibility for somebody else.

## Drives

Toward being findable, so that somebody can plan around you,
which is the only cooperation available at that range,
so the seat that solves the encounter is the seat with nothing left to solve it with.
