# Pitch: Hold What You Are Doing

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact, unhurried, said to somebody who has just qualified.
The register of a watchkeeper who knows the rule word for word and is about to show you where the words stop.
Technical without any jargon for its own sake, and quoting the rule wherever the rule is the point.

## Undertow

Under the regulation, two facts that do not negotiate.
An avoidance problem with two moving parties has no determinate answer, and gets one the instant somebody stops moving.
Any precise moment at which the held party may start moving again is a moment the other party must now predict.

## Nerve

The unease of a rule that is exact everywhere except at the one place it matters,
finding out that the exactness stops there deliberately,
and that everybody who has tried to fix it has been asking for the thing that was keeping them safe.

## Echo

After the run: two competent bridges, a rule known word for word and obeyed by both,
a give-way turn made lawfully at the last moment of its lawful range,
and a stand-on turn made in the same few seconds, on a conclusion that was sound.
