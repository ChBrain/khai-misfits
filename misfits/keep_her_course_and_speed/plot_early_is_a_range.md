# Plot: Early Is a Range

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The give-way duty, stated: take action early and substantially.
[Early and Substantial](piece_early_and_substantial.md), of which only one word is a specification.

## Action

[Giving Way](process_giving_way.md): [The Late Turn](persona_the_late_turn.md), on [Act Early and Substantially](plan_act_early_and_substantially.md), waits lawfully at [the crossing](place_the_crossing.md).

## Stage

[The Held Course](persona_the_held_course.md) watches a stretch of approach in which nothing has happened and nothing is owed.

## Tension

The give-way duty is real, and this plot insists on it: it is discharged by nearly everybody nearly always, which is why the production is about two ships that comply.
But the two adjectives are not alike. **Substantial is a size** and can be checked against a protractor, because a small alteration is unreadable at a distance and the rule says so. **Early is a range**, because what counts as early depends on the encounter, and no rule that had to cover every encounter could say otherwise.
So there is a stretch of every approach during which the give-way ship has not acted and is in breach of nothing, and **nothing observable separates that stretch from the beginning of a failure.** From the other bridge, a ship that is late and a ship that is not coming are the same picture.
