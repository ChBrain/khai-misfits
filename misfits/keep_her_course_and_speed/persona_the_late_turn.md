# Persona: The Late Turn

## Taxonomy

[Obligation](position_obligation.md). The give-way ship's action: required, substantial, and not yet made.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Coming. Almost always coming.
The duty is real and is discharged by nearly everybody nearly always, which is why crossings resolve without incident tens of thousands of times a day.
Nothing here is a ship declining to comply.

## Action

Waits, lawfully.
Turns, substantially.
Turns at a moment inside a range rather than at an instant.

## Shadow

That from the other bridge it is indistinguishable, until it happens, from a turn that is not coming.
_Early_ is a range: there is a stretch of every approach in which this ship has not acted and is in breach of nothing, and nothing observable separates that stretch from the beginning of a failure.
It ends arriving correctly, at the same few seconds as somebody else's correct decision that it would not.

## Tell

Nothing, for a long time.
Then a large alteration, exactly as the rule asks for.
