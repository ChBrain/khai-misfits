# Piece: Early and Substantial

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). The give-way duty's two adjectives, and only one of them is a specification.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the rule, as the give-way ship's standing obligation.
Discharged by [the late turn](persona_the_late_turn.md), correctly, at a moment of her choosing.

## Load Bearing

Without it the give-way ship either acts at a stated instant or is in breach, and there is nothing to misread.
It is load bearing because **substantial is a size and early is a range**: the alteration must be large enough to be seen, which is specifiable, and the timing must suit the encounter, which is not, so a lawful gap exists in every approach.

## Apparent

Two words asking for the same thing, promptness and clarity.
They are not alike. One can be checked against a protractor and the other cannot be checked against anything, and the play turns entirely on the second.

## Yearbook

Written into the collision regulations, spent at [the crossing](place_the_crossing.md).
It is why a compliant ship and a failing ship are the same picture for a while.
