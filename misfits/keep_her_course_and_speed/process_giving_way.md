# Process: Giving Way

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). The other ship's duty: action taken early and substantially, inside a range.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Risk of collision with a ship this one must keep clear of.
At [the crossing](place_the_crossing.md), at a moment of this ship's choosing within what the rule allows.

## Direction

Toward an alteration large enough to be unmistakable from the other bridge.
The duty is real and is discharged by nearly everybody nearly always, which is why this is a production about ships that comply rather than ships that do not.
And the timing is a range: **early** is a judgement about the encounter and not a mark on a clock.

## Lever

A substantial alteration, made in time.
Substantial is specified because a small alteration is unreadable at a distance; early is not, because what counts as early depends on the encounter.

## Echo

Nothing, for a long time,
then a large turn, exactly as asked for,
and no way for anybody watching to have told the wait from a failure.
