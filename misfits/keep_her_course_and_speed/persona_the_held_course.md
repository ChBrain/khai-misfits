# Persona: The Held Course

## Taxonomy

[Predictability](position_predictability.md). The stand-on ship's straight line: the gift she gives the other ship, and the only thing she has to give.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not passivity, and not deference.
A fixed trajectory, offered on purpose, so that somebody else's problem has an answer in it.
The play establishes this before anything else, because a production that reads holding as timidity has nothing to say about the rest.

## Action

Keeps course.
Keeps speed.
Keeps both while the range closes and the other ship does nothing yet.

## Shadow

That the same fixity is the whole of her response and she may not spend any of it.
Every second she holds is a second of margin converted into legibility, and legibility is only redeemable if the other ship performs, so she carries no reserve into the part of the encounter where a reserve is what is wanted.
It ends having given away her manoeuvre to buy a calculation for somebody who may not do the sum.

## Tell

The bearing does not change.
That is the good news and it is also the definition of a collision course.
