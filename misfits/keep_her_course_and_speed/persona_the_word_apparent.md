# Persona: The Word Apparent

## Taxonomy

[Threshold](position_threshold.md). The release condition: _as soon as it becomes apparent_, and it must stay a judgement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The place the rule stops being exact, and it stops there on purpose.
Everything around it is precise: which ship gives way, which side to alter to, how substantial the alteration must be. This one word is not, and it is the hinge the whole arrangement turns on.
Read as a drafting weakness by almost everybody who meets it.

## Action

Releases the stand-on ship.
Declines to say when.
Stays a judgement, under every pressure to become a number.

## Shadow

That making it precise would return the problem the rule was built to solve.
Any threshold is a moment at which the stand-on ship's behaviour changes discontinuously, and the give-way ship would then have to predict that moment, which is the unpredictability the holding was purchased to remove.
It ends the last store of the rule's own benefit, and everybody who needs the rule to work asks for it to be spent.

## Tell

Cannot be defined.
Has never once been improved by somebody trying.
