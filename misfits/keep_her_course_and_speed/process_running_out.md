# Process: Running Out

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). The room going, at the closing rate, while everybody complies.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two ships approaching each other.
At [the crossing](place_the_crossing.md), continuously, with nobody spending anything.

## Direction

Toward a width that will not absorb two manoeuvres.
It is not consumed by hesitation, by argument or by anybody being slow: it goes because distance converts into time and time passes. Early on almost anything works. Later only the right thing does.
And the stand-on duty is heaviest while the room is widest, so the arrangement restricts most where a response would have cost least.

## Lever

Closing speed, which is not linear in what it costs.
Six miles is a conversation. Six cables is not.

## Echo

A reserve entirely spent on being legible,
by the time being legible has stopped helping.
