# Process: Formalising

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). Turning the rule into something a machine can execute, and finding one word that will not convert.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A ship that steers itself, or an aid that advises one.
At [the bridge](place_the_bridge.md), by people who have read the rule closely and want it obeyed better.

## Direction

Toward a rule applied the same way every time, which would be a genuine improvement.
Nearly all of it converts. Which ship gives way, which side to alter to, how large the alteration must be: all of it is precise and all of it implements.
Then the release condition, which is a judgement, and an algorithm cannot execute a judgement.

## Lever

A request to remove the ambiguity, which the implementing literature makes explicitly and is right to make.
And the ambiguity is the store: **what is being asked for and what is being relied on are the same quantity**, and nobody has found a way to have it in both places.

## Echo

Rules 15 and 16, implemented in an afternoon.
Rule 17, still being asked about.
