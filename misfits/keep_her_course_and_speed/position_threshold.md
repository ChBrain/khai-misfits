# Position: Threshold

## Taxonomy

[Keep Her Course and Speed](play_keep_her_course_and_speed.md). The seat of the release: _as soon as it becomes apparent_, and no more exact than that.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The power to free the stand-on ship.
No criterion, no distance, no time, no closing rate.
A whole rule of precise provisions on either side of it.

## Orders

Release, when it becomes apparent.
Decline to say when that is.
Remain a judgement.

## Loses

Nothing, and its apparent weakness is its function: **the vagueness is load-bearing.**
Any precise threshold is a moment at which the stand-on ship's behaviour changes discontinuously, so the give-way ship must predict that moment, which restores exactly the unpredictability the holding was bought to remove.
It ends the last store of the rule's own benefit, and everybody who needs the rule to work asks for it to be spent.

## Drives

Toward letting a ship save herself when nobody else will,
without telling anybody when that is,
so the seat that supplies the escape is the one seat that cannot be written down.
