# Position: Expectation

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). The seat that holds what the patient now knows may happen to them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An accurate list, correctly understood and correctly remembered.
A named set of sensations to attend to, and the reasonable belief that attending is sensible.
No error of any kind in its contents.

## Orders

Hold what you were told.
Attend to the things you were told to attend to, since that is what being told is for.
Do not dismiss a sensation you were specifically warned about.

## Loses

Its innocence, without any of its accuracy.
**An expectation of a symptom is itself a cause of that symptom**, so this seat does its work correctly and
is, in doing it, one of the reasons the work is needed.

## Drives

Toward a body under competent observation by the person who lives in it,
with the observation aimed at exactly the right places,
so the finding rate rises for reasons that have nothing to do with what the tablet is doing.
