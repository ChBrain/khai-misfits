# Persona: The Prescription Not Refilled

## Taxonomy

[Tolerance](position_tolerance.md). The decision to stop, made humanely and on everything anybody knows.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Responsive, respectful, and exactly what a patient wants from a clinician.
Believes the patient about their own body, which is right.
Would be endorsed by any colleague shown the notes.

## Action

Weighs a felt harm against an unfelt benefit.
Stops the drug rather than making a person argue for their own experience.
Records the patient as intolerant of it, which is now true in the notes and follows them.

## Shadow

That the comparison is not between two things of the same kind.
**A felt symptom is vivid and a prevented event is nothing at all**, so the vivid side wins for reasons that
are not about the evidence, and the risk that comes back will not be experienced as an event either.

## Tell

Is the element everybody would call good care.
Is the element that carries the cost, which was never the ache.
