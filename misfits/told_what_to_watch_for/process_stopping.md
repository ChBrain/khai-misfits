# Process: Stopping

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). Discontinuing a drug that is reported to be harming somebody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A reported harm, taken seriously because taking it seriously is right.
At [the consulting room](place_the_consulting_room.md), at the review, by a clinician who believes the patient.

## Direction

Toward relief of a present, felt harm.
The rule being applied is correct almost everywhere and is the rule a patient would want applied: if the
treatment is hurting you, the treatment stops.

## Lever

The comparison, which is between two things of unlike kind.
**A felt symptom is vivid and a prevented event is nothing at all**, so the weighing is not close and is not
decided by the evidence, and the risk restored will not announce itself either.

## Echo

A discontinuation that every colleague would endorse,
for a harm the tablet did not do,
and a note in the record saying this patient does not tolerate it.
