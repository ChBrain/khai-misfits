# Persona: The One Who Has to Tell You

## Taxonomy

[Prescription](position_prescription.md). The clinician, recommending a drug and naming what it may do.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, unhurried by the standards of the day they are having, and plainly on the patient's side.
Believes the drug will help, and says so.
Would be praised by any reviewer for the thoroughness of what follows.

## Action

Recommends the treatment on the balance of benefit and harm.
Names the harms, accurately and in full, in terms the patient can use.
Answers the question that gets asked, without softening the answer.

## Shadow

That the two halves of the job pull against each other and both are required.
**The recommendation and the warning are the same act of care**, so there is no version of this consultation
in which only the useful half happens, and the thoroughness that a reviewer would praise is the quantity the
harm scales with.

## Tell

Is the element doing everything right.
Is the element supplying the cause.
