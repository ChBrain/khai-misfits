# Piece: The Same Body, Both Months

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). The within-patient design that separates the drug from the expectation of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Outside the play, in the evidence the play rests on.
Nowhere any patient or clinician in the production can reach, which is the point of it.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a hypothesis about attribution with no way to test it, and a reader is entitled to
say the tablet probably did cause the ache.
It is load bearing because the comparison is **within one person**: the same patients took the drug, an
identical placebo and no tablet at all, in randomised months, scoring symptoms daily. Most of the burden
recorded on the drug was recorded on the placebo too, and the placebo months were clearly worse than the
months with nothing.

## Apparent

That this could be explained by the kind of patient who volunteers for such a study.
It could not, because the design does not compare kinds of patient. Each person is their own control, so a
difference between their drug months and their placebo months cannot be a difference between people.

## Yearbook

The n-of-1 crossover trial of a statin, a placebo and no treatment, which is the design that makes the
attribution testable at all and is unavailable inside any single consultation.
