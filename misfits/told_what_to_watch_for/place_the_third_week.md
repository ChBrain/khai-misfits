## Taxonomy

## Shown

Nothing that would have been mentioned to anybody in any other month.

## Holds

The coincidence that is not a coincidence and is not an effect either.
**A body asked to report on itself has always got something to report**, so the arrival of a symptom in this
week needs no cause beyond the passage of three weeks and the fact of being asked.

## Offers

## Withheld
