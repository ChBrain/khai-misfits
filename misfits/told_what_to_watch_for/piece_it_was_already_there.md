# Piece: It Was Already There

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). The mechanism needs no new sensation, only a new reason to notice an old one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the third week](place_the_third_week.md), in a body that has been aching intermittently for forty years.
In the [watching](process_watching.md) process, which points attention at exactly the right places.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play needs the drug to be doing something, and the whole cut against a genuine adverse effect
collapses.
It is load bearing because **a body asked to report on itself has always got something to report**: the
background rate of ordinary sensation is not zero, so the reported rate can rise with nothing whatever
happening in the tissue.

## Apparent

That a symptom appearing after a drug is started is evidence the drug caused it.
It is weak evidence at best, and this is the reason. The three weeks supply the sensation on their own, and
the warning supplies the heading it gets filed under, so the sequence proves only that both happened.

## Yearbook

The observation that adverse effects are reported on inert treatment at rates that track what the recipient
was led to expect, which is the rate this piece is made of.
