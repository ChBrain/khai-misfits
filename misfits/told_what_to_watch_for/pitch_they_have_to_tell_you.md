# Pitch: They Have to Tell You

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Careful with a case nobody can be faulted for.
Exact about what was said, what was felt, and which of the two is in doubt.
Unwilling to call the patient suggestible or the prescriber defensive, because neither is required.

## Undertow

Under a routine consultation, one fact about duties and one fact about bodies.
A risk that is not disclosed is a risk the patient never got to decline.
And a body asked to report on itself has always got something to report.

## Nerve

The unease of a conversation in which every sentence was true.
Nobody lied. Nobody imagined anything. Nobody failed to do their job.
The one false thing is a link between two real things, and it cannot be checked from either end.

## Echo

After the run: a list of effects, each of them documented,
a person attending to their body exactly as instructed,
an ache of the kind that was always there arriving with somewhere to be filed,
and a tablet stopped for a harm it did not do, with the risk it was for coming back.
