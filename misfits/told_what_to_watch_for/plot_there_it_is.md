# Plot: There It Is

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An ache, in the third week, of the kind that adults have and do not mention.

## Action

[Ascribing](process_ascribing.md): [The Ache That Was Always There](persona_the_ache_that_was_always_there.md), in [the symptom seat](position_symptom.md), works through [Notice What Changes](plan_notice_what_changes.md), and is then carried out by [reporting](process_reporting.md).

## Stage

[The Third Week](place_the_third_week.md): a bad fortnight of sleep, a long drive, a shoulder that has always done that.

## Tension

[It was already there](piece_it_was_already_there.md), and that is the whole of what this plot has to
establish: the mechanism needs no new sensation, only a new reason to notice an old one, so the tablet can
be doing nothing at all and the reported rate still rises.
[The symptom is real](piece_the_symptom_is_real.md) and nobody is imagining anything. The muscle hurts. The
report is honest, and a more scrupulous patient makes it more carefully rather than less.
**The one false thing in the play is the link between the two**, and it is the one thing nobody in the
production can test: a body does not label which of its sensations came from where, and the clinician has
one patient and no counterfactual. Only [the same body, both months](piece_the_same_body_both_months.md)
could settle it, and that comparison lives outside the consulting room and always will.
