# Piece: The Duty Is the Mechanism

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). The disclosure cannot be dropped, because dropping it takes the patient's decision with it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the [duty](position_duty.md) seat, which has no occupant and no discretion.
In the [consenting](process_consenting.md) process, whose only lever is detail and whose detail cannot be turned down.

## Load Bearing

Without it the remedy is obvious and the misfit dissolves into advice about saying less.
It is load bearing because **a risk not disclosed is a risk the patient never got to decline**: relaxing the
duty does not reduce the harm, it converts the patient's decision into the clinician's and leaves them to
find out afterwards. The one move that would stop the mechanism is the move that ends consent.

## Apparent

That a clinician could simply be more selective about what they mention.
That is the literature's own proposal and it is honest about the price: it asks a clinician to judge, case by
case, how much truth a particular person can carry. **That discretion is precisely what consent exists to
remove**, and exercising it well is still exercising it.

## Yearbook

The bioethics argument that the duty to disclose and the duty not to harm are in genuine tension here, and
the proposed resolution of a contextualised consent rather than a lesser one.
