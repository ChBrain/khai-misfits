# Process: Watching

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). A person attending to their own body in the places they were told to attend to.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Having been told.
At [the leaflet](place_the_leaflet.md) and everywhere afterwards, continuously, by somebody being sensible.

## Direction

Toward early detection of a real adverse effect, which is the point and which sometimes works.
A patient who did not do this would be the one behaving oddly, and the clinician asked them to do it because
catching a genuine reaction early is worth catching.

## Lever

Where the attention is pointed, which the warning sets and nothing afterwards resets.
There is no instruction that directs attention at a symptom and not at the ordinary sensation next to it.

## Echo

A body under competent observation by the person who lives in it,
observed in exactly the right places,
with a finding rate that rises for reasons unrelated to what the tablet is doing.
