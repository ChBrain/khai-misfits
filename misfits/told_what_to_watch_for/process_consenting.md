# Process: Consenting

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). Making the decision the patient's by telling them what they are deciding about.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A drug worth prescribing and a patient who has not yet agreed to take it.
At [the consulting room](place_the_consulting_room.md), before the prescription, by somebody discharging an obligation.

## Direction

Toward a patient who could say no.
This is the whole content of the process and not a step in it: a consent given without the risks is not a
weaker consent but a different thing, a decision the clinician made and the patient signed.

## Lever

Detail, which is the only thing that can be turned and cannot be turned down.
Naming an effect vaguely does not reduce the harm; it produces a patient who cannot recognise the thing they
were warned about, which is the failure the requirement exists to prevent.

## Echo

A list delivered accurately and in full,
understood exactly as intended,
by somebody who will now watch for every item on it.
