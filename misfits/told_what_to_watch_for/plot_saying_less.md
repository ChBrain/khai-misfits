# Plot: Saying Less

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The settlements, tried in good faith by somebody who has understood the problem exactly.

## Action

[Consenting](process_consenting.md) again, against every discipline that might shorten it, at [the duty seat](position_duty.md) and [the prescription seat](position_prescription.md).

## Stage

[The Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md): the same ten minutes, with somebody trying to spend them differently.

## Tension

Each one gives back the thing it was for.
Say less, and produce a patient who did not consent to what they took, which is
[the duty is the mechanism](piece_the_duty_is_the_mechanism.md) stated as a cost.
Frame the numbers positively, and it works, and it is a decision about a person's beliefs taken without
telling them you are taking it.
Judge case by case how much truth this particular patient can carry, which is what the literature proposes
and which is honest about its own price: **that discretion is precisely what consent exists to remove**, and
exercising it well is still exercising it.
Use an open-label approach, which has a real evidence base and is not available for a first prescription of
a drug nobody has taken yet.
And explain the nocebo effect itself, which is the settlement that looks cleverest and is one more thing
handed to somebody who will now watch for it.
