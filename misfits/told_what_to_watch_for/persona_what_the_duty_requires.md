# Persona: What the Duty Requires

## Taxonomy

[Duty](position_duty.md). The obligation itself, present in every consultation and embodied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Unarguable, and rightly so.
Is what a patient would insist on if asked, and what a regulator would insist on if not.
Would be defended by everybody in the play, including the person it costs.

## Action

Requires that the risks be named before the drug is taken.
Requires that they be named specifically enough to be acted on.
Does not care whether the naming is comfortable.

## Shadow

That it is the mechanism and cannot be softened for that reason.
**A risk not disclosed is a risk the patient never got to decline**, so relaxing this element does not
reduce the harm: it converts the patient's decision into somebody else's and leaves them to find out later.

## Tell

Is the element nobody would remove.
Is the element without which the play does not run.
