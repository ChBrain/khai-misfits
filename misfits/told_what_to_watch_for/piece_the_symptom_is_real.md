# Piece: The Symptom Is Real

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). The ache is felt and the report is honest, which is what keeps the play outside anybody's conduct.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the [symptom](position_symptom.md) seat, which reports what it feels.
In the [reporting](process_reporting.md) process, where honesty is present in full and does not help.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is malingering and dissolves into a note about difficult patients.
It is load bearing because the experience is not in question: the muscle hurts, the tiredness is there, and
a scrupulous patient reports it more carefully rather than less. **What is false is the attribution and
nothing else**, and the attribution is the part neither party can check.

## Apparent

That a symptom produced by expectation is a symptom somebody is imagining.
It is not, and the distinction matters for how the patient is treated. Expectation acts on what is perceived
and how it is interpreted, and a sensation arrived at that way is felt exactly like any other, because it is
one.

## Yearbook

The finding that nocebo responses are ordinary expectancy and learning acting on symptom perception, which
is what removes the fault from the person having them.
