## Stage

## Tension

The attention is correct and it is the mechanism.
A person warned about muscle ache attends to their muscles, and a patient who ignored a specific warning
would be the one behaving oddly. The clinician asked for exactly this, because catching a genuine reaction
early is worth catching.
And the accuracy offers no protection. [What I Now Know to Look For](persona_what_i_now_know_to_look_for.md)
contains no error at all: nothing misremembered, nothing exaggerated, nothing invented. **A patient who
understood the explanation perfectly is the patient in whom it works best**, so the element with nothing
wrong in it is the element doing the damage, and there is no instruction that points attention at a symptom
without pointing it at the ordinary sensation next to it.
