# Process: Ascribing

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). Assigning a felt sensation to the one cause that has been offered for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An ache, and a candidate explanation supplied three weeks earlier in writing.
At [the third week](place_the_third_week.md), silently, by somebody not aware they are doing anything.

## Direction

Toward an explanation, because a sensation with a name attached is easier to hold than one without.
The available explanation is the one that was named, and no competing explanation was ever offered, since
nobody hands a patient a list of the ordinary reasons a shoulder aches.

## Lever

None that either party holds.
**A body does not label which of its sensations came from where**, so this is not a judgement made badly but
a judgement made without an instrument, and the clinician has one patient and no counterfactual either.

## Echo

A real sensation and a false attribution, arriving as one object,
in a person who will report it accurately,
to a clinician who is right to believe them.
