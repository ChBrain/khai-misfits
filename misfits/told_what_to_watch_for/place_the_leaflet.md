# Place: The Leaflet

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). The folded paper in the box, where the list is written down and can be read again.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Every effect observed in anybody, in order of how often.
Nothing invented, nothing exaggerated, nothing left out.
A document that would survive any audit of its accuracy.

## Holds

The list, made permanent and re-readable at three in the morning.
**Each named effect is on it because it happened to somebody**, so there is no line a regulator could strike
and no sentence anybody could call untrue, and the accuracy is exactly what makes it usable as a checklist
for symptoms.

## Offers

A complete and honest account of what may happen, available whenever it is wanted.

## Withheld

Any indication of which of the listed things is happening to this reader, now, and which is the shoulder.
