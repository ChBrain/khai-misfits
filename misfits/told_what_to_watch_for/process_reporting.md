# Process: Reporting

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). Telling the clinician what has been happening, in the words they supplied.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A symptom the patient was asked to report, doing what it was asked to do.
At [the consulting room](place_the_consulting_room.md), at the review, by somebody being conscientious.

## Direction

Toward a clinician who knows what is happening to their patient.
The report is accurate about the experience and carries an attribution the patient cannot separate from it,
because nobody experiences a sensation and its cause as two facts.

## Lever

Honesty, which is present in full and does not help.
A more scrupulous patient reports more carefully, in the vocabulary they were given, which is the vocabulary
of the drug's known effects.

## Echo

A truthful account of a real experience,
filed under a heading supplied by the warning,
and correctly believed.
