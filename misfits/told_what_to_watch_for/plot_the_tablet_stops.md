# Plot: The Tablet Stops

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The review, and a symptom reported in good faith by somebody who was asked to report it.

## Action

[Stopping](process_stopping.md): [The Prescription Not Refilled](persona_the_prescription_not_refilled.md), in [the tolerance seat](position_tolerance.md), works through [Stop If It Is Harming Me](plan_stop_if_it_is_harming_me.md).

## Stage

[The Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md): a clinician who believes their patient, which is right, and a decision any colleague would endorse.

## Tension

This is where the cost lands, and the cost was never the ache.
The rule being applied is correct almost everywhere and is the rule anybody would want applied to them: if
the treatment is hurting you, the treatment stops. Making a patient argue for their own experience would be
worse medicine and worse manners.
But the weighing is between things of unlike kind. **A felt symptom is vivid and a prevented event is
nothing at all**, so a present discomfort is set against an absence that by construction never announces
itself, and the vivid side wins for reasons that are not about the evidence. The drug goes, the risk it was
preventing comes back, and the record now says this patient does not tolerate it, which follows them.
