# Position: Duty

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). The seat that requires the risks be named, occupied by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

One requirement, applied to every prescriber and every prescription.
No discretion, no exceptions worth the name, and nothing anybody can appeal to.
The power to make a refusal possible, which is the only power it has and the whole of its point.

## Orders

Say what the drug may do, before it is taken.
Say it in terms the patient can act on, which means specifically and not in the abstract.
Do this whether or not the saying will be comfortable, because the alternative is deciding on their behalf.

## Loses

Nothing, because it holds nothing anybody wanted for themselves.
**A risk not disclosed is a risk the patient never got to decline**, so the seat cannot be relaxed without
converting a patient's decision into a clinician's, which is the exact thing it exists to prevent.

## Drives

Toward a patient who knows what may happen and can therefore say no,
by a requirement nobody wrote for this consultation and nobody present can suspend,
so what follows is a property of the arrangement rather than of anybody's judgement inside it.
