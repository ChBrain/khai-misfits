# Position: Symptom

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). The seat where a sensation becomes a complaint with a cause attached.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An ache, genuinely felt, of a kind that adults have.
A candidate explanation, supplied three weeks ago, in writing, by somebody qualified.
No means whatever of testing one against the other.

## Orders

Report what you feel, honestly and in the words you were given.
Attach it to the thing that was named, because that is the only cause on offer.
Do not exaggerate, and do not stay quiet about it either.

## Loses

The distinction between a sensation and its cause, which it never had.
**A body does not label which of its sensations came from where**, so the attribution is not a judgement the
seat makes badly: it is a judgement the seat has no instrument for and must make anyway.

## Drives

Toward a truthful report of a real experience,
carrying an explanation that is the one false thing in the play,
offered in good faith to somebody who is right to take it seriously.
