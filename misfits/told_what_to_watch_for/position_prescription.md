# Position: Prescription

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). The clinician's seat, where the drug is offered and the risks are named.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A drug that will do this patient good, on the evidence, more likely than not.
A list of what it may do instead, every line of it observed in somebody.
Ten minutes, and an obligation to use some of them on the list.

## Orders

Recommend the treatment, because the balance of benefit and harm supports it.
Name the harms, accurately and in full, because the patient is the one taking them.
Answer what is asked, and do not soften what was asked about.

## Loses

Any way to do the first without doing the second.
**The recommendation and the warning are the same act of care**, and a clinician who gave one without the
other would be offering a decision the patient could not have made.

## Drives

Toward a patient properly informed and properly worried,
by a seat that is doing two right things that pull against each other,
with no version of the consultation available in which only the useful half happens.
