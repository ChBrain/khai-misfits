# Persona: What I Now Know to Look For

## Taxonomy

[Expectation](position_expectation.md). What the patient carries out of the room, accurate in every particular.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Correct. Nothing in it is wrong, exaggerated or misremembered.
Is exactly what a careful listener takes from a careful explanation.
Is what the consultation was for.

## Action

Holds the named effects and their names.
Directs attention to the parts of the body they concern.
Refuses to dismiss a sensation it was specifically warned about, which is the right refusal.

## Shadow

That being right is not the same as being harmless.
**An expectation of a symptom is itself a cause of that symptom**, so this element's accuracy is not a
defence against it: a patient who understood the explanation perfectly is the patient in whom it works best.

## Tell

Is the element with no error in it.
Is the element that does the damage.
