# Place: The Consulting Room

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). Ten minutes, two people, and a decision that has to be the patient's.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A recommendation the evidence supports, made by somebody who means it.
A list of possible effects, read out because it has to be.
A patient listening properly, which is what everyone hopes for.

## Holds

The whole mechanism, in a room where everybody is doing their job well.
**The recommendation and the warning are the same act of care**, so the room cannot produce the first
without the second, and the better the second is done the more of the play there is.

## Offers

The best conditions available: competence, honesty, time, and a patient who understands.

## Withheld

Any version of the ten minutes in which the patient can refuse without being told what they are refusing.
