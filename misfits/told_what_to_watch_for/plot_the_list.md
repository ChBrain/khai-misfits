# Plot: The List

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A prescription about to be written, and a duty that says the risks are named first.

## Action

[Consenting](process_consenting.md): [The One Who Has to Tell You](persona_the_one_who_has_to_tell_you.md), in [the prescription seat](position_prescription.md), works through [Tell Them Properly](plan_tell_them_properly.md) under [Leave Nothing Out](plan_leave_nothing_out.md).

## Stage

[The Consulting Room](place_the_consulting_room.md): ten minutes, a recommendation somebody means, and a list read out because it has to be.

## Tension

This is the plot that has to establish there is no fault here, or nothing after it reads correctly.
[Nobody lied](piece_nobody_lied.md): every named effect is on the label because it happened to somebody, and
there is no line a regulator could strike.
And [the duty is the mechanism](piece_the_duty_is_the_mechanism.md), which is the harder half.
[What the Duty Requires](persona_what_the_duty_requires.md) is not a formality and not defensive medicine:
it is what makes a refusal possible, and a refusal that is not possible is not a consent. So the clinician
is not choosing between telling and not telling. They are discharging an obligation, thoroughly, in the way
any reviewer would praise, and **the thoroughness is the quantity everything downstream scales with**.
