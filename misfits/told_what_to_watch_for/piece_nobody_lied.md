# Piece: Nobody Lied

## Taxonomy

[Told What to Watch For](play_told_what_to_watch_for.md). Every named effect is documented, so the disclosure is complete and honest and still causes what it names.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the leaflet](place_the_leaflet.md), which would survive any audit of its accuracy.
In every sentence spoken at [the consulting room](place_the_consulting_room.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a scare story badly told, and the remedy is proportion.
It is load bearing because **each named effect is on the list because it happened to somebody**: there is no
line a regulator could strike, no figure inflated for caution, and no sentence anybody could call untrue, so
the harm is produced by an accurate document doing its job.

## Apparent

That the list must be overstating things, or the problem would not arise.
It is not. The effects are real, they occur, and a patient has a right to know about each one. What the list
cannot do is say which of them is happening to this reader in this week, and that is not a defect a more
careful list could repair.

## Yearbook

The regulatory requirement that adverse effects observed in trials be disclosed, which is why the document
is both exhaustive and true.
