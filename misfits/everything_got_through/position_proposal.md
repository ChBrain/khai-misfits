# Position: Proposal

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The seat where an hour is chosen and committed to paper.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A map, an enemy strength, and a defensible judgement about when to go.
The authority to name the time.
No way to learn anything by naming it.

## Orders

Choose the hour.
Write it plainly.
Send it across.

## Loses

Its own certainty, at the moment it acts.
Before the note goes, this seat knows only what it knows. **After the note goes it knows one thing
less than it needs**, because the question is no longer what the hour should be but whether the other
hill has it, and that is a question about somewhere else.

## Drives

Toward a decision,
which is what the seat is for and what a commander is paid to make,
so the seat that ends the deliberation is the seat that opens the gap.
