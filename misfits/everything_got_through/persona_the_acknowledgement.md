# Persona: The Acknowledgement

## Taxonomy

[Exposure](position_exposure.md). The reply that is supposed to close the matter, and whose sending is what puts its author in this seat.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Short, immediate, and unambiguous.
Answers exactly the doubt it was sent to answer, and answers it correctly.
Arrives.

## Action

Removes one doubt.
Creates one doubt.
Requires an acknowledgement of its own.

## Shadow

That it is not an instrument that can be applied harder.
Each one is a strict improvement and none of them is the last: **the tower it builds is taller after
every crossing and is never finished**, because finishing would mean a message whose arrival the
sender could observe, and there is no such message in this or any protocol.

## Tell

Is always worth sending.
Is never worth waiting for.
