# Persona: The Messenger

## Taxonomy

[Delivery](position_delivery.md). The rider who carries each note across the valley, and in this production always arrives.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Fast, careful, and successful on every single crossing of the run.
Carries the note exactly as written and hands it to the right officer.
Is the one element of the situation that never fails.

## Action

Takes the note.
Crosses.
Delivers, and cannot report back that they delivered.

## Shadow

That arriving is not the same as being known to have arrived.
**The messenger's success is invisible from the hill they left**, and it is that invisibility rather
than any risk to them which produces the whole structure: a rider who always got through, and whose
getting through cannot be observed from the far side, does exactly as much damage as one who
sometimes does not.

## Tell

Is the only party who knows the message landed.
Is the only party the message is not addressed to.
