# Piece: The Channel Only Might Fail

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The condition: possibility of loss, not rate of loss.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the valley](place_the_valley.md), which in this production loses nothing.
In the one assumption the result actually needs.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play collapses into a story about a bad road, and better riders would fix it.
It is load bearing because **the quantity is binary and is already at its minimum**: what the result
requires is that a message might not arrive, and a valley crossed successfully a hundred times still
might not deliver the hundred and first. Reducing the loss rate towards zero does not reduce the
distance to common knowledge, because that distance was never a function of the rate.

## Apparent

That a sufficiently reliable channel is effectively a perfect one.
It is not, and that is the whole content: a channel known with certainty to be perfect closes the
tower in a single crossing, and no channel in the field is known that way.

## Yearbook

The distinction between a channel that is reliable and a channel that is known to be reliable.
Naming which one exists is the play's whole use of it.
