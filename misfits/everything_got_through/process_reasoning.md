# Process: Reasoning

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). Each officer working out what the other can and cannot know.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A plan that pays only if both move, which makes the other's state of mind part of one's own decision.
At [the two hills](place_the_two_hills.md), continuously, by both parties.

## Direction

Toward a correct account of somebody else's position.
Both officers do this perfectly. Each concludes truly that the other agrees, truly that the other
knows they agree, and truly that at the bottom of the tower somebody is holding a message they cannot
confirm.

## Lever

Rigour, which both have and which is the problem.
**A less careful officer would attack**, and the play refuses that reading: the reasoning is valid,
the conclusion is correct, and treating the difficulty as excessive caution mistakes a proof for a
temperament.

## Echo

Two correct chains of inference,
arriving at the same place from opposite hills,
and neither of them ending in a reason to move.
