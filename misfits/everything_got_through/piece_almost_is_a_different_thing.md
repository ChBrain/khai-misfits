# Piece: Almost Is a Different Thing

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The discontinuity: near common knowledge does not behave like a near version of common knowledge.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the appointed hour](place_the_appointed_hour.md), after any number of confirmations.
In the electronic mail game, where the same structure is written as an economic one.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play concedes that enough messages would do, and the ending becomes a matter of
patience.
It is load bearing because **the behaviour under many confirmations is the behaviour under none**:
each party is reasoning about the other's near certainty one floor down, and the argument unravels
from the bottom whatever the height, so the situation with a large but finite tower is not a slightly
worse version of the situation with a complete one.

## Apparent

That this shows the parties are being irrational.
It is the opposite: the unravelling is what correct reasoning produces, and an officer who attacked on
the ninety-ninth confirmation would be acting on a hope rather than on the tower they have.

## Yearbook

The result that arbitrarily deep mutual knowledge is strategically unlike common knowledge.
The play uses it to close the door on effort, which is the door every reader tries.
