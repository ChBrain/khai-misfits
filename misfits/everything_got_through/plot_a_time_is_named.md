## Cue

## Stage

[The Two Hills](place_the_two_hills.md): a good hour on paper, a rider on the slope, and two forces that both want to go.

## Tension

Here the play establishes that nothing is wrong, which it must do at full strength.
The plan is sound: the simultaneity is a fact about the enemy and not a preference of the planners, and
a version of the attack that tolerated one force going first would not work.
The hour is well chosen. The officer who chose it is decisive and correct and sent it without delay.
**The only thing that has happened is that a decision was made and put into a channel.**
And with that, the question changes under the commander's feet. It is no longer which hour, which was
answerable from here, but whether the other hill holds it, which is a fact about somewhere else and
cannot be established by any amount of judgement on this one.
