# Position: Commitment

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The seat where somebody must actually move, at the hour, with the force they have.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An hour, agreed by both parties in fact.
Two forces that are willing and ready.
A payoff that exists only if they move together.

## Orders

Go on the hour.
With the other force.
Or not at all.

## Loses

The possibility of being conditional.
An order to attack cannot be made contingent on the other party's state of mind, because that state
is what cannot be established: **the condition the commander would like to attach is exactly the one
the channel cannot report**, so the seat has to be occupied unconditionally or vacated.

## Drives

Toward action,
which both officers want and neither can justify,
so the seat that decides the outcome is the seat with nothing left to decide on.
