## Tension

Here the channel is shown working perfectly and the difficulty untouched.
The note is delivered. It is read, understood and agreed with. There is no interception, no forgery,
no delay and no ambiguity, and there will not be at any point in the run.
**The play never rests on a lost message and does not need one.**
And the first general knows none of this. The success of the crossing is a fact held at the far end
and by the rider, and neither is in a position to report it to the hill it came from, because a rider
sent back is not a report but another crossing under exactly the same condition.
So the one party who could settle the matter is the one party the matter is not addressed to.
