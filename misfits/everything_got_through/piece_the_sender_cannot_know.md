# Piece: The Sender Cannot Know

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The asymmetry that locates the harm: whoever wrote last is the one who is short.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the exposure seat](position_exposure.md), which is always occupied and never by both.
On whichever hill the most recent messenger left from.

## Load Bearing

Without it the difficulty is symmetrical and both parties are simply uncertain.
It is load bearing because **the exposure has an address**: at every moment exactly one officer holds
a message they cannot confirm, and the other can see them holding it. Sending again does not empty the
seat, it moves it across the valley, which is why the situation looks like progress from either side
and is stationary from above.

## Apparent

That the exposed party should simply act on what they have.
They may, and that is the standing-order answer, and it is not agreement: it is one party moving
without knowing, which is the thing the plan was trying to avoid rather than a way of achieving it.

## Yearbook

The observation that a message's arrival is knowable at the receiving end and nowhere else.
The play uses it to give the harm a location, so it can be shown moving rather than shrinking.
