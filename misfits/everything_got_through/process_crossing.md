# Process: Crossing

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). A rider carrying one note from one hill to the other, successfully.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A note that somebody needs the other side to have.
At [the valley](place_the_valley.md), as many times as there are messengers.

## Direction

Toward a note in the right hand.
Every crossing in this production achieves it. The valley is dangerous in principle and delivers in
practice, so the play never rests on a loss and never needs one: **the structure is produced by
crossings that all succeed.**

## Lever

Speed and care, which the riders have in full.
Neither reaches the difficulty, because the difficulty is not on this side of the valley and is not
about the rider at all.

## Echo

A note delivered,
read, understood and agreed with,
and a hill behind it that has learned nothing.
