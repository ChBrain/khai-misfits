# Pitch: Nobody Lost a Message

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Patient and exact about what each side knows and when it came to know it.
Unwilling to promise that one more messenger will settle it, because it will not.
Quoting the count of crossings wherever the count is the point.

## Undertow

Under the attack order, one relation from logic and one from the ground.
Acting together needs each to know that the other knows, without end.
And every message that arrives raises that tower by exactly one floor.

## Nerve

The unease of a channel that worked perfectly and settled nothing.
Nobody lost a message: true of every crossing, checkable by both generals,
and the attack is still not agreed at first light.

## Echo

After the run: a valley crossed a hundred times without loss,
two officers each certain the other means to go,
a plan neither of them will start,
and a set of tracks in the snow that prove only that somebody set out.
