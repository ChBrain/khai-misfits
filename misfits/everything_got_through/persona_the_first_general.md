# Persona: The First General

## Taxonomy

[Proposal](position_proposal.md). The officer who names the hour and sends the first messenger.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, decisive, and correct about the tactical situation.
Picks a good hour for good reasons and commits to it without hesitation.
Has done the only thing that can be done to begin.

## Action

Names the hour.
Sends.
Waits for a confirmation that will not finish the job.

## Shadow

That being first is being exposed.
Having sent, this officer knows the hour and does not know it was received: **the act of proposing
creates the very gap the proposal was meant to close**, and no amount of decisiveness reaches across
it, because what is missing is not resolve but a fact about somebody else.

## Tell

Is the only party who has acted.
Is therefore the first to be unable to act.
