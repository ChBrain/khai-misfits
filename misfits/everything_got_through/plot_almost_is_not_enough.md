## Tension

Here the door that every reader tries is closed, and it is closed by a result rather than by a mood.
Near certainty is not the currency the plan takes. Each officer is reasoning about the other's near
certainty one floor down, and the argument unravels from the bottom whatever the height, so **the
behaviour under many confirmations is the behaviour under none.**
That reads as absurd and is exactly what correct reasoning produces. An officer who attacked on the
ninety-ninth confirmation would be acting on a hope rather than on the tower they actually hold, and
**a less careful officer would attack**, which the play refuses as a reading: treating this as
excessive caution mistakes a proof for a temperament.
And a reliable channel does not rescue it either, because the condition was never the rate of loss. It
is that a message might not arrive, and a valley crossed successfully a hundred times might still not
deliver the hundred and first.
