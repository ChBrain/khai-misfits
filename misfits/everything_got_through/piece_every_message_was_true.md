# Piece: Every Message Was True

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The absence of any deceit, error or delay anywhere in the run.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the two hills](place_the_two_hills.md), in every note either officer wrote.
In the delivery record: no interception, no forgery, no misreading, no lateness.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is about trust or about security and the remedy is authentication.
It is load bearing because **honesty is assumed and changes nothing**: two parties who agree, who
never mislead each other, whose messages are all correct and all delivered, are in exactly the
position described. An adversary would add a second problem and would not add this one.

## Apparent

That a trusted channel or a signed message solves it.
Signing establishes who wrote a note, which was never in doubt here. What is missing is whether it
arrived, and no property of the message's contents reports on its own delivery.

## Yearbook

The stipulation, standard in the treatment, that the parties are honest and only the channel is
imperfect.
The play uses it to remove every reading in which somebody is at fault.
