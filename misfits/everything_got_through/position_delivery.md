# Position: Delivery

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The seat of the crossing: the valley, the rider, and whether the note is in somebody's hand.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A rider who is fast and careful.
A valley that is dangerous in principle and, in this production, is crossed successfully every time.
Complete knowledge of its own outcome, held by the one party who is not a decision maker.

## Orders

Carry.
Arrive.
Hand it over.

## Loses

Its own report.
The outcome of a crossing is known at the far end and nowhere else: **success here is
unobservable from the side that needed to observe it**, and a rider sent back to say so is not a
report but another crossing, subject in its turn to exactly the same condition.

## Drives

Toward arrival,
which it achieves without exception,
so the seat that never fails is the seat whose success cannot be established.
