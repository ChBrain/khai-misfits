# Persona: The Second General

## Taxonomy

[Confirmation](position_confirmation.md). The officer who receives the hour, agrees with it, and confirms at once.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Reads the order, judges it sound, and replies within the minute.
Wants the attack, agrees with the hour, and has no reservation of any kind.
Is in possession of more information than the sender is.

## Action

Receives.
Agrees.
Confirms, and thereby becomes the exposed one.

## Shadow

That agreeing is what puts this officer in the other's position.
Before replying they knew the hour and knew the first general did not know they knew it. **Having
replied, they know the hour and do not know their reply arrived**, which is precisely the state the
first general was in a moment ago, reached by doing the correct thing promptly.

## Tell

Is the party who removed a doubt.
Is the party who now holds the same doubt, one floor up.
