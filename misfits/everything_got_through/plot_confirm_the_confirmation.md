## Tension

Here the regress is in the open and it is not a failure of effort.
Each crossing adds one true floor to the tower: the sender knew, the receiver knew, and now the sender
knows the receiver knew. That is a real gain and it happens every single time.
**And the shortfall is exactly one floor at every height.** A protocol that has run a thousand times
is a thousand floors up and precisely as far from the top as it was at the start, because what acting
together requires is the whole tower and not a tall one.
Meanwhile the exposure has an address. At every moment one officer holds a message they cannot confirm
and the other can see them holding it, and sending again does not empty that seat: **it moves it across
the valley.** Which is why the situation looks like progress from either hill and is stationary from
above.
