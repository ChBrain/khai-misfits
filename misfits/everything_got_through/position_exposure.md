# Position: Exposure

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The seat occupied by whoever sent the most recent message, and by nobody else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Everything that has been agreed.
One missing fact, always the same fact: whether the last thing sent was received.
Occupancy that changes hands with every crossing.

## Orders

Hold.
Until the thing that cannot be established is established.
Which is never.

## Loses

The prospect of being vacated.
Sending again does not empty this seat, it **moves it to the other hill**: the party who has just
written is now the one who cannot know, and the party who has just read is the one who can see them
in it. There is no crossing after which nobody is here.

## Drives

Toward one more message,
which is always a reasonable thing to send,
so the seat that effort is aimed at is the seat effort relocates.
