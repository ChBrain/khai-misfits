# Process: Committing

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). Somebody actually moving, at the hour, on what they have.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The appointed hour](place_the_appointed_hour.md) arriving, whether or not anything is settled.
At [the two hills](place_the_two_hills.md), once, and not repeatable.

## Direction

Toward an attack, or toward nothing.
This is the only process in the production that cannot be run again: a crossing can be repeated and a
reply can be repeated, and the hour comes once, so the asymmetry between the endlessly repeatable and
the strictly single is the whole of the pressure.

## Lever

A standing rule decided before the channel was needed, which is the one instrument that works.
It works by **replacing the agreement with an instruction**: whoever holds the rule moves without
knowing, which removes the requirement for common knowledge by removing the requirement to know
anything at all.

## Echo

Either two forces in the valley,
or one force in the valley,
or two hills at first light and a plan that was agreed by both and started by neither.
