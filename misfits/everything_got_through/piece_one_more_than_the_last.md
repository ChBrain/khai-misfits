# Piece: One More Than the Last

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The regress: every acknowledgement needs an acknowledgement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the valley](place_the_valley.md), on every crossing after the first.
In the tower of nested knowing that acting together requires.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is about two officers who did not send enough messengers.
It is load bearing because **the shortfall is exactly one floor at every height**: each crossing adds
a true statement of the form each knows that the other knows, and the requirement is the whole
infinite tower, so a protocol that has run a thousand times is a thousand floors up and precisely as
far from the top as it was at the start.

## Apparent

That the gap closes asymptotically and can be treated as closed.
It does not close at all. The quantity that would have to go to zero is the number of floors
remaining, and that number is unchanged by every message ever sent.

## Yearbook

The standard statement that coordinated action requires common knowledge, and that no finite exchange
over a channel that may fail produces it.
The play uses it for one thing: that effort here is real and is not cumulative.
