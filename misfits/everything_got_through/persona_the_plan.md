# Persona: The Plan

## Taxonomy

[Commitment](position_commitment.md). The attack itself: successful if both forces move together and disastrous if one moves alone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sound, well judged, and the correct response to the ground.
Requires simultaneity because the enemy in the valley beats either force on its own.
Would be approved by any officer shown the map.

## Action

Pays when both go.
Destroys whoever goes alone.
Asks for a thing the channel cannot supply.

## Shadow

That its one demand is the whole of the difficulty.
**Nothing about the plan is a mistake**: the simultaneity is a fact about the enemy, not a preference
of the planners, and a plan that tolerated one force going first would not be this plan and would not
work. So there is no revision here that saves it and keeps it.

## Tell

Is the reason agreement is needed.
Is the reason agreement is not enough.
