## Tension

Here every settlement gives up the thing that was wanted.
Agree in person, before there is a channel to fail, and it works completely and cannot be done for
anything that arises after the two forces separate.
Issue a standing order to attack at the named hour regardless of confirmation, and the requirement for
common knowledge is removed by **removing the requirement to know anything at all**: whoever holds the
rule moves without knowing, and if the other did not receive the hour they go alone.
Write a rule about which officer attacks on an unconfirmed message and you have **assigned the risk
rather than dissolved it**, which is a real instrument and is not agreement.
Wait for certainty and it is first light on two hills, with a plan both officers agreed to and neither
started, and a set of tracks in the snow that prove only that somebody set out.
**The asymmetry is the whole ending**: the messages can be sent for ever and the hour comes once.
