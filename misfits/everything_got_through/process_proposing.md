# Process: Proposing

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). Choosing an hour and putting it into the channel.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An enemy in the valley strong enough to beat either force alone.
At [the two hills](place_the_two_hills.md), once, before anything else can happen.

## Direction

Toward a named time that both forces can work to.
This is the correct opening move and there is no better one: somebody has to say an hour, the
judgement behind it is sound, and a plan with no proposed time is not a plan.

## Lever

The choice of hour, which is the only thing this process controls.
It has no lever on the thing that will matter, because what matters next is not which hour was chosen
but whether the choice is held on both hills, and that is not a property of the choosing.

## Echo

A good hour, plainly written,
a rider on the slope,
and a commander who now needs a fact that is not available from here.
