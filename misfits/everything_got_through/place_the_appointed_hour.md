# Place: The Appointed Hour

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The moment itself, treated as somewhere both forces have to be.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A time on two pieces of paper, identical, agreed by both officers.
A deadline that does not care how many crossings have been made.
Dawn.

## Holds

The asymmetry that produces the ending.
Messages can be sent without limit and the hour arrives once: **the process that can be repeated for
ever is racing a process that happens exactly once**, and the tower is not finished at any point
before the deadline or at any point after it.

## Offers

A shared reference that both parties genuinely hold.

## Withheld

The one property that would make it usable.
Both know the hour; what neither has is the knowledge that the knowing is shared all the way down, and
an hour that both know and cannot rely on the other relying on is a time nobody moves at.
