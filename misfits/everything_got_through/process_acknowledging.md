# Process: Acknowledging

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). Replying to say the note was received, and thereby needing a reply.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Crossing](process_crossing.md), and a note that has arrived.
At [the two hills](place_the_two_hills.md), alternately, without limit.

## Direction

Toward a shared record of what is known.
Each reply is a genuine improvement and adds exactly one floor: the sender knew, the receiver knew,
now the sender knows the receiver knew. That is more than there was, every time, and it is never all
of it.

## Lever

Sending another one, which is always available and always reasonable.
The lever works and does not close: **the improvement is real and the shortfall is constant**, which
is what makes effort here feel productive and be futile at the same time.

## Echo

A taller tower after every crossing,
an exposure that has moved rather than shrunk,
and two officers each more confident and no more able to go.
