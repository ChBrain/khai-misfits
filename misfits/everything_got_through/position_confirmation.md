# Position: Confirmation

## Taxonomy

[Everything Got Through](play_everything_got_through.md). The seat that is supposed to settle the matter by answering.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The hour, correctly received.
Agreement with it, immediately and without reservation.
The means to say so.

## Orders

Reply at once.
Say yes plainly.
Do it again if asked.

## Loses

The property of being final.
Every reply this seat sends is a strict improvement and none of them is the last one: **a
confirmation that closed the matter would have to be a message whose arrival its sender could see**,
and there is no such message, so the seat can answer for ever and never finish.

## Drives

Toward reassurance,
which each reply genuinely provides,
so the seat that removes a doubt is the seat that inherits it.
