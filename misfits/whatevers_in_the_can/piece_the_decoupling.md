# Piece: The Decoupling

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). The problem and the choice coming apart, the phenomenon whole.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Left in [The Meeting](place_the_meeting.md) once the choice is closed: a decision made by oversight or flight, a solution attached that answers a question never really asked here, and a problem still out there, in another can or in none (Cohen, March, and Olsen).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no misfit, only a meeting that solved what it met to solve: it is the decoupling that makes the structure a trap, the decision made and the problem not.
It carries the phenomenon whole. Because the choice is reached by oversight, decided before the problems attach, or by flight, the problems drifting off to a better can, the decision and the problem it named come apart, so the meeting is productive and the concern goes unsolved, a loss no one authored and easily read as progress.

## Apparent

A decision reached: from outside it looks like the problem handled, the item closed, the matter settled, when the matter has only been left behind.

## Yearbook

Produced by [Coinciding](process_coinciding.md) in [Decided by Oversight](plot_decided_by_oversight.md), the choice closed and [The Problem](piece_the_problem.md) left unsolved.
It is what [The Convener](persona_the_convener.md) would tighten and what [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) marks the limits of, the gap between the decision and the problem it did not solve.
