# Process: Coinciding

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). Borne at [The Meeting](place_the_meeting.md), precipitated by [The Chair](persona_the_chair.md) reaching a decision from what is present, in [Decided by Oversight](plot_decided_by_oversight.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A decision due from the can, to be produced from whatever coincided in it.

## Direction

From a loaded can toward a decision, reached by resolution, or by oversight, or by flight.

## Lever

That the decision turns on the mix that coincided and is often closed by oversight, decided before problems attach, or by flight, the problems drifting off, rather than by resolving anything (Cohen, March, and Olsen).

## Echo

[The Decoupling](piece_the_decoupling.md), the choice made and the problem it named left behind.
