# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). The condition the dynamic needs and the edges where it lifts, the hedge that keeps the trap real but bounded.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Weighed at [The Agenda](place_the_agenda.md), where the convener sets what may enter which choice and when: the access and decision rules and the deadlines that time the streams, and the conditions under which no such structure is needed (Cohen, March, and Olsen; March and Olsen).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overreaches into a claim that all organizational choice is chaos, which is false: the boundary is what keeps the trap honest.
It carries the condition and the escape. The dynamic needs an organized anarchy, ambiguous goals, unclear means, fluid participation; give it clear goals, understood means, stable participation, or an imposed access and timing, and the streams march closer to step and the coupling tightens, so the trap is bounded and structure can buy it back, at a cost.

## Apparent

A footnote of exceptions where the meeting does solve its problem, easy to read as a softening rather than the line that says when choice couples by fit and when by timing.
