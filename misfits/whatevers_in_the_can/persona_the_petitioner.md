# Persona: The Petitioner

## Taxonomy

[Petitioner](position_petitioner.md). The one carrying a concern that looks for a forum, whose problem is aired where a choice opens and carried off again where none does.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Earnest, patient, carrying a concern that matters.
Has a real problem and wants it addressed, and takes it from meeting to meeting looking for the occasion where it will finally be weighed and settled.
No agenda beyond the concern itself: only the reasonable wish to see a genuine matter get its hearing and its answer.

## Action

Works [Find a Forum](plan_find_a_forum.md), carrying [the Problem](piece_the_problem.md) through [Streaming](process_streaming.md) at [the Backlog](place_the_backlog.md), and when a can opens, through [Dumping](process_dumping.md), tips it into [the Meeting](place_the_meeting.md).
Cannot control which choice opportunity opens or what else is in it, so it raises the concern where it can and hopes the timing serves.
Does what a reasonable person with a problem does: it looks for a forum, and takes what forum it finds, since that is the only way a concern gets addressed here at all.

## Shadow

Not naive for raising the concern: airing a problem where a choice opens is the sound move, and the only one available in a place with no shared clock.
Because it cannot command the timing, its concern's fate is chance: aired and then decided past by oversight, or drifting off to the next can, so the matter it most wants solved is the matter the room is least reliably built to solve.
It is the one who brought the real problem, and the one whose problem the meeting is least likely to actually answer.

## Tell

Raises the same concern again in the next meeting, and the next, sure that this time the room will take it up.
Cannot force the hearing, because the timing is not in its hands, so it re-tables the matter can after can, the earnest voice for a problem the streams keep carrying past.
