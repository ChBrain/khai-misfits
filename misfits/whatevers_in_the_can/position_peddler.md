# Position: Peddler

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). The seat of the one carrying a ready solution that looks for a use, deployed wherever a choice opens, whatever the question.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An answer that predates its question: a pet proposal, a favoured framework, a product looking for a problem.
The readiness to deploy it the moment a choice opportunity opens.
No need of a matching problem, since the solution attaches by being present when the can opens, not by fitting what is in it.

## Orders

Find it a problem, and deploy it wherever a choice opens.
Carry the answer to every occasion and offer it, since a solution present when the can opens is a solution adopted, and the question can be found afterward.
Accept what the seat trades: that the win is by timing and not by fit, and that the answer may be chosen for a problem it does not solve.

## Loses

Nothing it feels, and that is the trap.
Because its solution attaches by coincidence and it counts the attachment a success, the seat loses nothing it can see, so the cost falls elsewhere, on the problem the answer did not fit, and the peddler carries on rewarded.
It keeps its win and loses only what it does not bear, the fit that was never made.

## Drives

Toward the one whose answer wins by being present, not by being right,
carrying the solution to every can and attaching it wherever one opens, and by attaching it loading the choice with a fit that was never checked,
so the seat with a solution looking for a problem is the seat the coincidence most rewards, its readiness the very thing that couples the decision to the timing.
