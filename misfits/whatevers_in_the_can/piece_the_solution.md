# Piece: The Solution

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). An answer that exists before its question, a pet proposal or ready framework looking for a use.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Waiting in [The Backlog](place_the_backlog.md) and deployed at [The Meeting](place_the_meeting.md) when a can opens: the proposal already written, the framework already favoured, the product looking for a problem, carried in ready to attach to whatever choice is going (Cohen, March, and Olsen; Kingdon).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and a choice waits on a real solution to a real problem: it is the answer that predates its question that lets a decision be loaded with a fit that was never made.
It bears the load by being a stream too. Because a solution can flow independently, looking for a use rather than being sought for a need, it is available to couple to any can that opens, and its readiness is the other half of what lets choice and problem come apart.

## Apparent

The chosen answer: the proposal the meeting adopts, looking for all the world like the solution the problem called for.

## Yearbook

Carried by [The Peddler](persona_the_peddler.md) on [Find It a Problem](plan_find_it_a_problem.md), through [Streaming](process_streaming.md), the whole of [A Solution Looking for a Problem](plot_a_solution_looking_for_a_problem.md).
It is attached to [The Can](piece_the_can.md) by [The Coincidence](piece_the_coincidence.md), the ready answer that meets a choice by timing and not by fit.
