# Position: Chair

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). The seat of the one who presides over a choice and must produce a decision from whatever is present, by resolution, oversight, or flight.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The duty to decide, and the can's contents to decide from.
The styles a decision can take: resolution, actually solving; oversight, closing quickly before problems attach; flight, letting problems drift off and deciding what is left.
No power to summon the right problem and the right solution to the can, only to close on the mix that coincided.

## Orders

Clear the docket, and produce a decision from what is in front of you.
Take the can as it is and reach a decision, by resolving where you can and by oversight or flight where you must, since a decision is what the seat is there to deliver.
Accept what the seat delivers: that a decision closed is not a problem solved, and that the brisk clearing of items is exactly what leaves the problems behind.

## Loses

The guarantee that the decision solves the problem.
Because it decides from the mix that coincided, and often by oversight or flight, the seat cannot ensure the choice tracks the concern, so what it loses is the tie between deciding and solving, and it loses it by doing its job, producing decisions on time.
It keeps a cleared docket and loses the solved problems, productive and decoupled at once.

## Drives

Toward the one who closes items briskly and leaves problems unsolved,
producing a decision from whatever is in the can, by oversight or flight as often as resolution, and by producing it parting the choice from the problem,
so the seat that most reliably decides is the seat that most reliably decouples, the meeting productive and the concern still out there.
