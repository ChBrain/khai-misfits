# Process: Streaming

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). Borne at [The Backlog](place_the_backlog.md), precipitated by [The Petitioner](persona_the_petitioner.md) and [The Peddler](persona_the_peddler.md) carrying a problem and a solution on their own clocks, in [Four Streams](plot_four_streams.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An organized anarchy, where goals are ambiguous, means unclear, and participation fluid, so nothing marches in step.

## Direction

From a shared sequence toward four independent flows: problems, solutions, participants, and choice opportunities each on its own timing.

## Lever

That in such a place a solution can exist before its question and a problem can drift without a forum, so each stream moves when it moves and not when the others do (Cohen, March, and Olsen).

## Echo

Four streams flowing free, ready to couple by whatever coincidence a choice opportunity brings.
