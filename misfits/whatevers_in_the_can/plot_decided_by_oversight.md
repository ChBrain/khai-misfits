# Plot: Decided by Oversight

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Meeting](place_the_meeting.md), where the choice is closed and the problem is not.

## Action

[The Chair](persona_the_chair.md) on [Clear the Docket](plan_clear_the_docket.md) through [Coinciding](process_coinciding.md) reaches a decision by oversight, closing before the problems attach, or by flight, letting them drift to a better can, so [The Decoupling](piece_the_decoupling.md): a solution is adopted that answers a question never really asked here, and the problem is left unsolved. The scene closes with the decision made and the problem left behind, no one having erred (Cohen, March, and Olsen).

## Stage

This is not Everybody's Business: the decision is not dropped for want of an owner, it is briskly made. The trap is that it is made by oversight or flight, so a choice closes and a concern stays open, productive and unsolved at once, the meeting decoupled from the problem it named.

## Tension

The very briskness that clears the docket is what parts the choice from the problem, so the more reliably the room decides, the more reliably it leaves its concerns unsolved, a success that reads as progress and is not.
