# Piece: The Can

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). The choice opportunity itself, the occasion on which the organization is expected to decide.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Opening at [The Meeting](place_the_meeting.md): the scheduled decision, the agenda item, the committee convened to choose, an empty vessel into which the participants present tip whatever problems and solutions they are carrying (Cohen, March, and Olsen).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no occasion for the streams to meet: it is the can, the moment a decision is due, that gathers whatever happens to be present into a choice.
It bears the load as a container, not a process. Because the choice opportunity is a vessel filled by whoever is there rather than a march that seeks out the right inputs, what ends up in it is a matter of timing and attendance, and the decision is only ever as fitting as the mix that coincided.

## Apparent

A decision point: a meeting called to settle something, the proper place where a problem meets its solution and a choice is made.

## Yearbook

Opens in [Whoever's in the Room](plot_whoevers_in_the_room.md) and is filled by [Dumping](process_dumping.md), as [The Petitioner](persona_the_petitioner.md) and [The Peddler](persona_the_peddler.md) tip in what they carry.
What it holds is loaded by [The Coincidence](piece_the_coincidence.md), so the decision it yields turns on who was present and what they brought.
