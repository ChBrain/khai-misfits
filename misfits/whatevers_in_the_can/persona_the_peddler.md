# Persona: The Peddler

## Taxonomy

[Peddler](position_peddler.md). The one carrying a ready solution that looks for a use, deployed wherever a choice opens, whatever the question.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Helpful, ready, always with an answer.
Carries a proposal it believes in, a framework it favours, a solution already worked out, and offers it eagerly the moment a decision is on the table.
No cynicism in it: it genuinely thinks its answer is good, and bringing a good answer to a meeting looks like exactly what a helpful person should do.

## Action

Works [Find It a Problem](plan_find_it_a_problem.md), carrying [the Solution](piece_the_solution.md) through [Streaming](process_streaming.md) at [the Backlog](place_the_backlog.md), and deploys it at [the Meeting](place_the_meeting.md) the moment a can opens.
Does not need a matching problem, because the solution attaches by being present when the choice opens, not by fitting what the choice is about.
Does what a person with a ready answer does: it takes the answer to every occasion, since a solution in the room when the can opens is a solution adopted.

## Shadow

Not a schemer: it means well, and offering a worked-out answer is a reasonable and useful thing to do.
Because its solution wins by presence and not by fit, and because it bears none of the cost when the fit is wrong, it is rewarded for a coupling it never checked, so its helpfulness is exactly what loads a choice with an answer to a question no one asked.
It is the one with the ready answer, and the one whose readiness the coincidence most rewards.

## Tell

Has the same proposal for every problem, and offers it with equal confidence each time.
Cannot see the misfit, because the attachment counts to it as success, so it carries its one answer from can to can, the eager solution forever finding a use and never quite a question.
