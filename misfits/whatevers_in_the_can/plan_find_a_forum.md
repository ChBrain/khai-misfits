# Plan: Find a Forum

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the petitioner's seat](position_petitioner.md), inside [Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Petitioner](position_petitioner.md)

## Direction

Carry your concern until a choice opportunity opens, then raise it there. In a place with no shared clock, a problem gets addressed only where it lands in a can that happens to be open, so take the matter from meeting to meeting and tip it in wherever you can, since airing it is the only route to a hearing. Do not expect to command the timing: the can may close before the concern is weighed, or the concern may drift to the next, and neither is in your hands.

## Orders

([The Petitioner](persona_the_petitioner.md) carries [the Problem](piece_the_problem.md) through [Streaming](process_streaming.md) at [the Backlog](place_the_backlog.md), and tips it into [the Can](piece_the_can.md) through [Dumping](process_dumping.md) when a choice opens.)

## Implementation

([The Backlog](place_the_backlog.md) is where the concern waits and drifts; [Streaming](process_streaming.md) carries it on its own clock, so it is aired at [the Meeting](place_the_meeting.md) only when a can opens near it, and addressed or abandoned by the timing of the room.)

## Targets

- [ ] the concern is carried until a choice opportunity opens
- [ ] it is raised in whatever can is open, since that is the only route to a hearing
- [ ] the timing of the room is not in the seat's hands
- [ ] the concern may be decided past by oversight or drift to the next can, unsolved
