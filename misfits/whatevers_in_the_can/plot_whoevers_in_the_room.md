# Plot: Whoever's in the Room

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Meeting](place_the_meeting.md), where a choice opportunity opens.

## Action

[The Can](piece_the_can.md) opens, and through [Dumping](process_dumping.md) the participants present tip in whatever they carry, [The Petitioner](persona_the_petitioner.md) on [Find a Forum](plan_find_a_forum.md) its concern and [The Peddler](persona_the_peddler.md) its ready answer, so [The Coincidence](piece_the_coincidence.md) loads the choice by who was there and what they brought. The scene closes with the decision loaded by timing, not by fit (Cohen, March, and Olsen).

## Stage

A choice opportunity is a vessel, filled by attendance: what ends up in it is the mix of problems and solutions the people who happened to be present were carrying, so the choice is only ever as fitting as the coincidence that loaded it.

## Tension

The room will decide from what is in the can, and what is in the can is an accident of who came and what they held, so the decision to come is already hostage to timing before anyone weighs a thing.
