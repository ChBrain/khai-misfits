# Persona: The Chair

## Taxonomy

[Chair](position_chair.md). The one who presides over a choice and must produce a decision from whatever is present, by resolution, oversight, or flight.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Efficient, decisive, keeps things moving.
Runs the meeting, works the agenda, and delivers decisions on time, proud of a room that does not get bogged down and always closes its items.
No neglect in it: producing decisions is the job, and this chair does it briskly and well.

## Action

Works [Clear the Docket](plan_clear_the_docket.md) through [Coinciding](process_coinciding.md) at [the Meeting](place_the_meeting.md), reaching a decision from whatever is in [the Can](piece_the_can.md), by resolution where it can and by oversight or flight where it must.
Cannot summon the right problem and the right solution to the can, so it decides from the mix that coincided and moves on.
Does what a chair is there to do: it produces a decision, closing quickly before problems attach or letting them drift so the choice is free, since a decision delivered is the measure of the seat.

## Shadow

Not a shirker: it decides, reliably and on time, and deciding is precisely its duty.
Because a decision closed is not a problem solved, and because it often closes by oversight or flight, the very briskness it takes pride in is what parts the choice from the problem, so its competence is the engine of the decoupling.
It is the one who most reliably decides, and the one who most reliably leaves the problems behind.

## Tell

Reports a short meeting and a full slate of decisions, and counts that a good day's work.
Cannot see the gap, because the docket is clear and the minutes are full, so it measures the meeting by decisions produced and never by problems solved, the efficient hand that closes items over concerns it did not settle.
