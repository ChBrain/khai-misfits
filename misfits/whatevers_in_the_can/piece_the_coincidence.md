# Piece: The Coincidence

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). The temporal coupling by which a decision is produced, the streams meeting by timing rather than by logic.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At work in [The Meeting](place_the_meeting.md) the moment a can opens: the chance meeting of which problems, which solutions, and which participants are present at once, the mix that will decide what the choice becomes (Cohen, March, and Olsen; Kingdon).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and decisions would couple by fit: it is the coincidence, the joining by simultaneity rather than by the match of solution to problem, that makes the choice a product of timing.
It carries the engine. Because what is decided turns on the mix that happened to coincide, a waiting solution attaches to whatever choice opened and a problem present is addressed or not by accident of arrival, so the coincidence is the whole reason the decision fits the timing and not the need.

## Apparent

Good timing, or bad: it looks like the meeting simply happening to have the right people and the right proposal on hand, a lucky convergence rather than the mechanism it is.

## Yearbook

The coupling struck in [Whoever's in the Room](plot_whoevers_in_the_room.md), joining [The Solution](piece_the_solution.md) to [The Can](piece_the_can.md) by timing.
It is what produces [The Decoupling](piece_the_decoupling.md), the decision loaded by what coincided and so parted from the problem it was meant to solve.
