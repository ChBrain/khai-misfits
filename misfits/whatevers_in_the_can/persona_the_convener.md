# Persona: The Convener

## Taxonomy

[Convener](position_convener.md). The one who would structure access and timing to make choices track problems, and finds the structure costly and partial.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Organized, deliberate, out to bring order.
Sees the meetings deciding without solving and means to fix it, with access rules, decision rules, and deadlines that will bring the right problem and the right solution to the same table.
No fussiness in it: it is right that the coupling is loose, and its wish to tighten it is sound.

## Action

Works [Structure the Access](plan_structure_the_access.md) through [Structuring](process_structuring.md) at [the Agenda](place_the_agenda.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): it sets who and what may enter which choice and when, so problems and fitting solutions are brought together rather than left to coincide.
Cannot fully impose it, because goals stay ambiguous and participation stays fluid, and the structure it sets narrows the openness that let good couplings happen too.
Does what a sound organizer does: it channels the streams where it can, tightening the coupling toward resolution, and finds the anarchy routing around the parts it cannot hold.

## Shadow

Not a bureaucrat for trying and not defeated: it is right that structure tightens the coupling, and it is also unable to make that free or complete.
Because an organized anarchy resists being fully ordered, and because structure closes off useful chance couplings along with the useless ones, the convener proves, against the wish for a clean fix, how dearly the coupling is bought.
It is the one who would make choices track problems, and the one who shows how much and how partly that can be done.

## Tell

Adds a rule, watches the streams find their way around it, and adds another.
Cannot close the anarchy, because its goals and its people will not hold still, so it layers structure against a looseness that keeps returning, the organizer forever tightening a coupling that will not fully take.
