# Place: The Agenda

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). Where access and timing are structured to make choices track problems, at a cost. Hosts [Setting the Agenda](plot_setting_the_agenda.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The rules being set: which problems and solutions may enter which choices, who may act on what, and the deadlines that decide when the streams meet.

## Holds

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the conditions under which structure tightens the coupling and those under which the anarchy resists it.

## Offers

The levers to channel the streams: an access rule, a decision rule, a deadline, so the right problem and the right solution can be brought to the same can.

## Withheld

A costless or complete fix, since the structure is dear to build and maintain and cannot be fully imposed where goals stay ambiguous and participation stays fluid.
