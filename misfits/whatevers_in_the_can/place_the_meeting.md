# Place: The Meeting

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). Where a choice opportunity opens and a decision is produced from whatever is present. Hosts [Whoever's in the Room](plot_whoevers_in_the_room.md) and [Decided by Oversight](plot_decided_by_oversight.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A committee convened, an agenda to work through, a decision due: the orderly surface of a room that will choose and minute its choice.

## Holds

[The Can](piece_the_can.md) as it opens, [The Coincidence](piece_the_coincidence.md) that loads it, and [The Decoupling](piece_the_decoupling.md) left when the choice is closed.

## Offers

To whoever is present, a vessel to tip their problems and solutions into, and a decision that will be made from the mix.

## Withheld

Any assurance that the problems and solutions present fit each other, so the room decides from what coincided and not from what was needed.
