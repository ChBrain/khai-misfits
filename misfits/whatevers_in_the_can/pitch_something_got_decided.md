# Pitch: Something Got Decided

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Busy and reasonable.
Meetings that run, agendas that clear, decisions produced briskly by people doing their jobs: a proposal adopted, a direction set, an item closed and minuted.
Nothing false in it: the room really did decide, on time and in order, and everyone in it acted sensibly.

## Undertow

Under the brisk decision, a coupling by timing.
What got decided turned on who was in the room and what they carried, not on the fit of a solution to a problem, so a ready answer attached to whatever choice opened and the problem it was meant to address drifted off unsolved (Cohen, March, and Olsen).
No schemer and no shirker: the one with a solution looks for a use, the one with a concern looks for a forum, the chair produces a decision from what is in front of it, all reasonable.

## Nerve

The productivity of a meeting that decides, and the catch that the deciding does not solve:
that in an organized anarchy the streams have no shared clock, so a solution looking for a problem finds a choice by coincidence, the decision reached and the problem unsolved the one coincidence seen twice,
and the room adjourns satisfied while the concern it named is still out there, in another can or in none.

## Echo

After the run: a proposal adopted for who carried it in and when, a concern decided past by oversight or drifting off by flight, a productive meeting and an unsolved problem, a loss authored by no one (Cohen, March, and Olsen; Kingdon).
And the remedy, at a cost and not certain: structure, an access rule, a decision rule, a deadline that times the streams so the right things meet, each dear to build and maintain, and narrowing the openness that let good couplings happen too (March and Olsen).
And the bound held in view, because the dynamic is conditional and not a law: it needs ambiguous goals, unclear means, and fluid participation, and lifts where those settle or a structure times the streams, so the whole of it is that a decision made from whatever was in the can is fitted to the timing and not to the problem.
