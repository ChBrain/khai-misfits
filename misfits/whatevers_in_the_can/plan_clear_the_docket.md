# Plan: Clear the Docket

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the chair's seat](position_chair.md), inside [Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Chair](position_chair.md)

## Direction

Produce a decision from whatever is in the can, and keep the meeting moving. Take the choice as it comes, loaded with whatever problems and solutions happen to be present, and reach a decision: resolve it where you can, and where you cannot, close by oversight before the problems attach or let them drift by flight so the choice is free. A decision delivered on time is the measure of the seat, so clear the item and go on.

## Orders

([The Chair](persona_the_chair.md) reaches a decision from [the Can](piece_the_can.md) through [Coinciding](process_coinciding.md) at [the Meeting](place_the_meeting.md), by resolution, oversight, or flight.)

## Implementation

([The Meeting](place_the_meeting.md) is where the decision is produced; [Coinciding](process_coinciding.md) closes the choice on the mix that coincided, often by oversight or flight, so the docket clears briskly and the problems the choice named are left behind.)

## Targets

- [ ] a decision is produced from whatever is in the can
- [ ] the choice is closed by resolution where possible, and by oversight or flight where not
- [ ] the docket clears on time, the measure of the seat
- [ ] a decision closed is not a problem solved, and the briskness is the decoupling
