# Piece: The Problem

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). A concern looking for a choice to attach to, a stream that flows on its own.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Drifting through [The Backlog](place_the_backlog.md) and, when a can opens, tipped into [The Meeting](place_the_meeting.md): the concern of someone inside or out, wanting a forum, aired where one opens and carried off again where none does (Cohen, March, and Olsen).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is nothing the decision fails to address: it is the problem, flowing free of the solutions, that the choice is supposed to solve and does not.
It bears the load by being a stream, not a starting point. Because the problem arrives on its own clock and not at the head of a march toward a solution, it may or may not be in the can when the choice is made, and its independence from the solution is half of what lets the two decouple.

## Apparent

The thing the meeting is about: the concern on the agenda, the matter to be resolved, the reason a decision is being made at all.

## Yearbook

Carried by [The Petitioner](persona_the_petitioner.md) through [Streaming](process_streaming.md) at [The Backlog](place_the_backlog.md), and tipped into [The Can](piece_the_can.md) by [Dumping](process_dumping.md).
It is what [The Decoupling](piece_the_decoupling.md) leaves behind, the concern decided past or drifted off, unsolved in another can or in none.
