# Position: Petitioner

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). The seat of the one carrying a concern that looks for a forum, whose problem is aired where a choice opens and carried off again where none does.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real concern, and a wish to see it addressed.
A stream to ride, not a march to lead: the problem flows on its own clock and lands in a can when one opens near it.
No control over which choice opportunity opens or what else is in it, so its concern's fate turns on the timing of the room.

## Orders

Find a forum, and raise the concern where a choice opens.
Carry the problem until an occasion appears, then tip it into the can, since that is the only way a concern gets addressed at all in a place like this.
Accept what the seat cannot command: that the can may close by oversight before the concern is weighed, or the concern may drift to the next can, and neither is in its hands.

## Loses

The assurance that raising the concern will get it solved.
Because it cannot ensure the can that opens is the one that will resolve its problem, its concern may be aired and then decided past or left behind, so what it loses is the link between raising a problem and solving it, lost to the timing of the room.
It keeps its voice and loses the outcome, the concern heard and not answered.

## Drives

Toward the one whose problem's fate depends on timing and not on merit,
raising the concern where a choice opens and watching it addressed or abandoned by accident of arrival,
so the seat that most wants the problem solved is the seat least able to make the room solve it, its concern only ever as answered as the mix that coincided.
