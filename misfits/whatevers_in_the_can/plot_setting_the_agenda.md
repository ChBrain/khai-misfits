# Plot: Setting the Agenda

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Agenda](place_the_agenda.md), where someone tries to make choices track problems again.

## Action

[The Convener](persona_the_convener.md) on [Structure the Access](plan_structure_the_access.md) through [Structuring](process_structuring.md) holds [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): access rules, decision rules, and deadlines can time the streams so the right problem and the right solution meet in the same can, but each is costly, narrows the useful openness, and cannot be fully imposed where goals stay ambiguous. The scene closes with the coupling improvable at a cost, and the trap shown bounded (Cohen, March, and Olsen; March and Olsen).

## Stage

Structure is the real remedy and never a free one: where goals settle, means are understood, participation steadies, or an access and timing are imposed, the streams march closer to step and choices track problems, so the trap holds only in the organized anarchy and lifts, at a price, where structure can reach.

## Tension

The convener is right that structure tightens the coupling and still cannot make it free or whole, since the anarchy routes around every rule its ambiguity and its fluid cast will not hold, so the cure buys back the coupling only in part and only while it is maintained.
