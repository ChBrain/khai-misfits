# Plan: Find It a Problem

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the peddler's seat](position_peddler.md), inside [Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Peddler](position_peddler.md)

## Direction

Carry your ready answer to every occasion and deploy it wherever a choice opens, whatever the question. A solution present in the room when the can opens is a solution adopted, so do not wait for a matching problem: bring the proposal to each meeting and offer it, and let the question be found afterward. The win is by timing and not by fit, and you bear none of the cost when the fit is wrong, so keep offering.

## Orders

([The Peddler](persona_the_peddler.md) carries [the Solution](piece_the_solution.md) through [Streaming](process_streaming.md) at [the Backlog](place_the_backlog.md), and deploys it into [the Can](piece_the_can.md) whenever a choice opens.)

## Implementation

([The Backlog](place_the_backlog.md) is where the answer waits for a use; [Streaming](process_streaming.md) carries it ready to any can, so at [the Meeting](place_the_meeting.md) it attaches to whatever choice opened, adopted for being present and not for solving what is in the room.)

## Targets

- [ ] the ready answer is carried to every choice opportunity
- [ ] it is deployed wherever a can opens, without waiting for a matching problem
- [ ] it attaches by being present when the choice opens, not by fitting the problem
- [ ] the cost of the misfit falls on the problem, not on the seat, which keeps offering
