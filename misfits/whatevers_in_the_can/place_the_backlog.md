# Place: The Backlog

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). Where problems and solutions circulate independently between choices, each on its own clock. Hosts [Four Streams](plot_four_streams.md) and [A Solution Looking for a Problem](plot_a_solution_looking_for_a_problem.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Concerns drifting in search of a forum, answers waiting for a use, participants coming and going: the standing churn of a place with more matters open than it ever closes at once.

## Holds

[The Problem](piece_the_problem.md) in its stream and [The Solution](piece_the_solution.md) in its own, each flowing free of the other.

## Offers

To a solution, the chance to wait until a choice opens; to a problem, a forum if one happens by.

## Withheld

A shared clock: nothing here brings the right problem and the right solution to the same can at the same time, so the meeting they need is left to coincidence.
