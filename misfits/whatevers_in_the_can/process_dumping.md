# Process: Dumping

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). Borne at [The Meeting](place_the_meeting.md), precipitated by the participants present tipping what they carry into an open can, in [Whoever's in the Room](plot_whoevers_in_the_room.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A choice opportunity opening with whatever participants happen to be present.

## Direction

From carried problems and solutions toward a loaded can, filled by whoever is in the room.

## Lever

That a choice opportunity is a vessel, so those present tip in the concerns and answers they are holding, and the can fills with the mix that attendance and timing supply (Cohen, March, and Olsen).

## Echo

[The Coincidence](piece_the_coincidence.md), the mix now in the can that will decide what the choice becomes.
