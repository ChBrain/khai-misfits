# Plan: Structure the Access

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the convener's seat](position_convener.md), inside [Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Convener](position_convener.md)

## Direction

Set access rules, decision rules, and deadlines that time the streams, so the right problem and the right solution meet in the same can. Decide which problems and solutions may enter which choices, and when, so that a concern is brought together with a fitting answer rather than left to coincide with whatever was present. Expect the structure to be dear and partial: it must be built and held, it narrows the openness that let good couplings happen too, and an organized anarchy resists being fully ordered.

## Orders

([The Convener](persona_the_convener.md) sets the access and timing through [Structuring](process_structuring.md) at [the Agenda](place_the_agenda.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) between where structure tightens the coupling and where the anarchy resists it.)

## Implementation

([The Agenda](place_the_agenda.md) is where the rules are set; [Structuring](process_structuring.md) channels which streams may enter which choice and when, tightening the coupling toward resolution where it holds, and leaving slack where goals stay ambiguous and participation fluid.)

## Targets

- [ ] access rules, decision rules, and deadlines are set to time the streams
- [ ] the right problem and the right solution are brought to the same can where structure holds
- [ ] the structure is costly to build and maintain and narrows the useful openness
- [ ] it cannot be fully imposed where goals stay ambiguous, so the coupling is bought back only in part
