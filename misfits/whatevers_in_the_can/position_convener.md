# Position: Convener

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). The seat of the one who would structure access and timing to make choices track problems, and finds the structure costly and partial.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The levers of structure: access rules that say which problems and solutions may enter which choices, decision rules that set who may act, and deadlines that time the streams.
The aim of tightening the coupling, so the right problem and the right solution meet in the same can.
No way to fully impose it, because goals stay ambiguous and participation stays fluid, and the structure it sets narrows the openness that let good couplings happen too.

## Orders

Structure the access, and time the streams so the right things meet.
Set the rules for what enters which choice and when, so a problem and a fitting solution are brought together rather than left to coincide, and hold the structure against the drift back toward looseness.
Accept what the seat costs: that the structure is dear to build and maintain, that it closes off useful chance couplings along with the useless ones, and that an organized anarchy resists being fully ordered.

## Loses

The hope of a costless or complete fix.
Because structure cannot be fully imposed where goals are ambiguous, and because it narrows the openness that also served, the seat cannot tighten the coupling without spending real design and losing real slack, so what it loses is the free correction, the coupling bought back only in part and at a price.
It keeps a tighter coupling and loses the ease of it, the order real and dear and never whole.

## Drives

Toward the one who tightens the coupling at a cost, and only partly,
setting access, decision, and timing so problems and solutions meet, and by setting them spending structure the anarchy resists,
so the seat that would make choices track problems is the seat that proves how dearly the coupling is bought, the fix real, costly, and incomplete.
