# Process: Structuring

## Taxonomy

[Whatever's in the Can](play_whatevers_in_the_can.md). Borne at [The Agenda](place_the_agenda.md), precipitated by [The Convener](persona_the_convener.md) imposing access and timing on the streams, in [Setting the Agenda](plot_setting_the_agenda.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The wish to make choices track problems again, once the coupling by timing is seen.

## Direction

From freely flowing streams toward channelled ones: which problems and solutions may enter which choices, and when.

## Lever

That access rules, decision rules, and deadlines can time the streams so the right problem and the right solution meet in the same can, tightening the coupling toward resolution (Cohen, March, and Olsen; March and Olsen).

## Echo

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the coupling tightened where structure holds and slack again where the organized anarchy resists it.
