# Play: The Only Sky There Is

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Only Sky There Is.
A theory of the early universe predicts a distribution, because a mechanism seeded by quantum noise cannot say which fluctuation lands where. One sky was drawn from that distribution, and it is the only one there will ever be. So the uncertainty on the largest structures is fixed by how many independent patterns a sphere admits, which is five at the largest scale, and a perfect instrument leaves it exactly where it is.

## Arc

The bend is this: the dominant uncertainty on the largest scales contains no instrumental quantity, so it survives a measurement that could not be improved.
It begins with what the theory actually says.
[The Prediction Is About an Ensemble](plot_the_prediction_is_about_an_ensemble.md). The physics fixes a variance rather than a sky, and a version that claimed to fix the sky would be the weaker theory.
And the population has one member.
[And There Is One Draw From It](plot_and_there_is_one_draw_from_it.md). [The One Realisation](piece_the_one_realisation.md) is the whole sample and the whole population, and what is missing is not data but another universe.
So the limit is a count.
[So the Floor Is Arithmetic, Not Optical](plot_so_the_floor_is_arithmetic_not_optical.md). [The Mode Count](piece_the_mode_count.md) is derived exactly, agreed by everybody, and printed as its own band beside the instrumental error.
Then [the measurement reaches it and keeps going](plot_the_measurement_reaches_it_and_keeps_going.md).
[The Sky Surveyor](persona_the_sky_surveyor.md) finishes four years of integration and the band on the left of the plot is the width it started.
And [what is found cannot be tested again](plot_and_what_is_found_cannot_be_tested_again.md). [The Alignment](piece_the_alignment.md) is real, and the statistic that measures it was chosen after the sky was seen.
And [the extra draws must come from inside](plot_so_the_extra_draws_must_come_from_inside.md). [The Reacher](persona_the_reacher.md) reads the sky from distant clusters, lowers the floor, and runs out of independent vantages.

## Stakes

Whether a quantity can be known better when it has already been measured as well as it can be.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a prediction that is a distribution, a population with one reachable member, a floor derived from a count rather than from an instrument, a complete measurement that leaves it standing, a real feature whose significance needs a draw that does not exist, and a remedy that works and stops, and away from any account in which somebody built the wrong instrument or asked the wrong question. The production cannot fault the theorist, who is stating the strongest thing the physics permits; and it cannot fault the surveyor, whose mission succeeded entirely and moved the term it was built to move. What is left to fight over is not how to reduce the uncertainty but what to call a measurement that is finished, exact, and still uncertain by an amount nobody will ever lower.
