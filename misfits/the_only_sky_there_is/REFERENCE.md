# The Only Sky There Is: Reference

The research warrant for The Only Sky There Is.
The term is cosmology's; the play runs on cosmic variance in the microwave background and on what an
uncertainty looks like when no instrument appears in it, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the error bar as **a count rather than a quality**: a theory of the early universe predicts, at each
angular scale, the variance of the fluctuations rather than their values, because a mechanism seeded by quantum
noise cannot say which fluctuation lands where and would be the weaker theory if it claimed to. One sky was
drawn from that distribution. A sphere admits a fixed number of independent patterns at each angular scale, five
at the largest, so estimating a variance from five numbers has a spread that is calculable exactly and is the
same whatever measured them. The play models the documented mechanics, the prediction that is a law about a
population, the single realisation that is the whole of the sample and the whole of the reachable population,
the floor derived from geometry with no instrumental quantity in it, the mission that succeeds completely and
leaves it standing, the feature whose significance needs a draw that does not exist, and the remedy that works
and has a ceiling, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of the wrong instrument or the
wrong question.

The governing law is that the dominant uncertainty on the largest scales is set by how many independent things
there are rather than by how well anybody saw them, so it survives a measurement that could not be improved.

**Nothing here is a shortcoming, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The theory
states the strongest thing the physics permits. The measurement is made to a precision the science no longer
requires, by two independent satellites that agree. The floor is derived exactly, agreed by everybody, and
printed as its own term beside the instrumental error rather than buried in it. There is no concealment, no
dispute and no better instrument wanted.

What removes any possibility of blame from the theorist is that predicting a distribution is the correct and
complete statement. A version of the theory that named the particular sky would not be more precise but wrong,
since the mechanism it describes does not determine which realisation arrives. So the object the prediction is
about is a population of possible universes, which is the standard reading of every statement the field makes
on these scales and not a hedge inside it.

What removes it from the surveyor is that the instrument stopped being the limitation long before the mission
ended, and continuing was still right. Integration time, mirror size and detector sensitivity all work exactly
as designed and reduce a term that is already negligible beside the other one. This is what success looks like
when the dominant quantity is not the one effort acts on, and no exposure, no technology and no future mission
changes it.

**And the sample cannot be enlarged, which is what makes this different from every small dataset.** In any
other science the answer to one draw is more draws, and the reason that answer is unavailable here has nothing
to do with cost, difficulty or ethics. There is one universe, it has produced one sky, and the last scattering
surface is a sphere whose radius is fixed by the age of the universe rather than by any decision. The word for
what is missing is not more data.

**And the anomaly closes the case against the last party, because the ordinary remedy for it needs the same
missing thing.** The lowest multipoles are aligned more closely than the model expects and the quadrupole is
low. The measurement is not disputed and the probability is correctly computed; what is disputed is what the
number means, since a statistic chosen after the sky was seen has a significance that depends on how many other
statistics would have looked equally striking. The clean test is a fresh sky with the statistic fixed in
advance, so a routine methodological question becomes a permanent one and the parties to it are not disagreeing
about rigour.

It is distinct from the house's **Overfitting**, which the slate line required it to be tested against before
authoring, and the test was run in the sweep. There a model with capacity to spare learns one sample's noise as
signal, so the defect is in the estimator, is curable by regularisation or a hold-out, and the sample could in
principle be enlarged. Remove that engine, by supposing an estimator with no capacity to overfit and no fitting
at all, and this misfit stands entire: the floor binds a perfect estimator exactly as hard, because it is a
property of the data rather than of what is done to them.

It is distinct from the house's **A Hundred Years to Vouch for a Thousand**, and the two invert. There a
relation is calibrated in a window and applied outside it, so the uncertainty that matters has **no term in the
data** and the published band is silent about it. Here the irreducible term is the one quantity that is
derived exactly, quoted in closed form and plotted as its own band. One misfit stages an uncertainty that
cannot be stated; this one stages an uncertainty that is stated perfectly and cannot be reduced.

It is distinct from the house's **Erased by What Divided Them**, which stages the other limit that no
instrument beats and does it by a different engine. There the signal is destroyed: ordinary language change
consumes the evidence, and a better instrument fails because what it would read is gone. Here nothing is
destroyed and the signal is measured essentially exactly. The uncertainty is not about the sky, which is known,
but about the distribution the sky was drawn from, so it **survives a perfect measurement**, which is the sharp
line between the two.

It is distinct from the house's **Exactly One Kilogram**, where the artefact is the unit, so drift cannot be
detected from inside the system and the trouble is that nobody can know. Here everything is known: the floor is
derived, its size is agreed and its cause is understood completely, and a fully understood limit is not thereby
a smaller one.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast anybody as having
  built the wrong instrument or asked the wrong question, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get
  wrong: a theory seeded by quantum noise predicts a distribution and would be worse if it named a sky; the
  sphere admits the number of independent patterns it admits, which is geometry and not a choice; the satellite
  succeeds completely and reduces a term that had already stopped deciding anything; the derivation of the
  floor is exact, universally accepted and printed rather than buried; the alignment is really in the map and
  its probability is correctly computed; and the remedy that reaches for more vantages is real work that
  genuinely lowers the floor. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit
  is that an uncertainty set by how many things there are survives a measurement that could not be improved.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Remote quadrupole
  measurement is the real remedy, works, and stops where the vantages stop being independent. Combining the
  microwave background with galaxy surveys and other tracers adds information about the same modes rather than
  new modes. Fixing the statistic in advance is the correct discipline and cannot be applied to a feature
  already seen. Waiting for a better mission does not help, since the term the mission would improve is not the
  binding one. Widening the survey removes the partial-sky penalty and reaches the floor rather than passing
  it. And knowing the whole structure, which every party does, leaves a spectrum with a band on the left that
  the next generation will publish unchanged.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the derivation of the variance, the separation of sample from
  cosmic variance, the philosophical statement of the uniqueness problem, the anomaly measurements and their
  critique, and the remote quadrupole proposal are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: **whether the
  large-angle anomalies are anything at all is genuinely contested**, precisely because of the difficulty this
  play stages, and the account carries the dispute rather than settling it or endorsing either side; the mode
  count is stated here in words and the exact expression, its assumptions of statistical isotropy and
  Gaussianity, and the treatment of partial sky and noise all live in the sources; the remote quadrupole method
  is at an early stage and the size of the gain it will actually deliver is not settled; the reach of the
  argument beyond the microwave background, to galaxy surveys and other large-scale probes carrying their own
  versions of the same limit, is real and is not developed here. **The play makes no claim that any anomaly is
  real or that any is spurious, and none that cosmology is limited as a science**, only that on the largest
  scales the error bar is a count of independent things, and that a measurement can be finished, exact, and
  still uncertain by an amount nobody will lower.
