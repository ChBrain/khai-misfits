# Persona: The Ensemble Theorist

## Taxonomy

[Ensemble Theorist](position_ensemble_theorist.md). The one whose prediction is a distribution rather than a sky.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A model of the early universe that predicts, for each angular scale, the **variance** of the fluctuations rather than their values.

## Action

Predicts a distribution.
Waits for a sky to compare it with.

## Shadow

Is predicting exactly the right thing, and the right thing is not directly observable.
The theory is not being vague and is not hedging: a mechanism that generates fluctuations from quantum noise cannot predict which fluctuation lands where, and would be wrong if it claimed to. What it predicts is the statistical law the fluctuations obey, which is the strongest statement the physics permits. That statement is about an ensemble of universes, and the ensemble is not a laboratory population: it is a mathematical object with one member anybody can look at.

## Tell

Says exactly what can be said, about a population of one.
