# Plot: And What Is Found Cannot Be Tested Again

## Taxonomy

[The Only Sky There Is](play_the_only_sky_there_is.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The lowest multipoles of the finished map, unexpectedly aligned.

## Action

[Testing what was already seen](process_testing_what_was_already_seen.md): [the pattern finder](persona_the_pattern_finder.md), holding [Pattern Finder](position_pattern_finder.md), measures [the alignment](piece_the_alignment.md) and computes its probability.

## Stage

A striking number that cannot be promoted to a finding or dismissed as coincidence.

## Tension

The statistic was chosen after the sky was seen, and the only clean test of that is a second sky.
The measurement is not in dispute and the probability is correctly computed. What is in dispute is what it means, because the significance of a feature noticed after the fact depends on how many other features would have looked equally striking, and the standard remedy is to fix the statistic in advance and take a fresh draw. The draw does not exist, so an ordinary methodological question becomes a permanent one, and the parties to it are not disagreeing about rigour.
