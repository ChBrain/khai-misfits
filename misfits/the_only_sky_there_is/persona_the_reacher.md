# Persona: The Reacher

## Taxonomy

[Reacher](position_reacher.md). The one who goes for more draws from inside.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A catalogue of distant galaxy clusters, and a plan to read the microwave sky as it looks from each of them.

## Action

Reads the quadrupole seen by somebody else's vantage, off the polarisation of scattered light.

## Shadow

Genuinely lowers the floor, by a bounded amount, and cannot pass the bound.
The method is real, is being pursued and works: light scattering in a distant cluster carries the imprint of the quadrupole at that place and time, so each cluster is a partly independent view of the same underlying field. What it buys is more draws from within one universe rather than a second universe, and clusters close enough together see correlated skies, so the gain stops at roughly the number of genuinely independent regions. It is a real reduction with an end to it, which is the honest shape of the remedy.

## Tell

Finds more vantages inside the one thing, and runs out of vantages.
