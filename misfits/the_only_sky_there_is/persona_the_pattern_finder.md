# Persona: The Pattern Finder

## Taxonomy

[Pattern Finder](position_pattern_finder.md). The one who sees something in the map and cannot test it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The lowest few multipoles of the map, unexpectedly aligned with each other, and a quadrupole smaller than the model likes.

## Action

Measures the alignment.
Computes how unlikely it is, and cannot answer the question that follows.

## Shadow

Has found something real in the data and has no second draw to put it to.
The alignment is in the map and the number is correctly computed. What cannot be done is the ordinary next step: the statistic was chosen after the sky was seen, so its significance depends on how many other statistics would have looked equally striking, and the only clean way to settle that is a fresh sky the statistic was fixed before seeing. There is no fresh sky. So the finding sits at a significance nobody can convert into a verdict, and this is not a dispute about rigour between people who disagree.

## Tell

Finds a pattern that can be neither confirmed nor dismissed.
