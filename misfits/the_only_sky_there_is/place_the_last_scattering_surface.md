# Place: The Last Scattering Surface

## Taxonomy

[The Only Sky There Is](play_the_only_sky_there_is.md). The one sky, measured to the end of what instruments can do.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A map of the microwave sky, the same in every direction to one part in a hundred thousand, with the departures plotted as a spectrum.
Ordinary superb work: the foregrounds are modelled, the errors are propagated, and two independent satellites agree.

## Holds

[The Sky Surveyor](persona_the_sky_surveyor.md), with nothing left to improve on the left of the plot.
[The Mode Count](piece_the_mode_count.md), which decides how wide the band there is.
[The Alignment](piece_the_alignment.md), sitting in the lowest few multipoles.

## Offers

A measurement of the largest structures in the universe, at a precision the instrument long ago stopped setting.

## Withheld

A second one.
The place is a sphere centred on here, drawn at the distance light has travelled since the universe became transparent, and its radius is fixed by the age of the universe rather than by any choice. It cannot be widened by observing longer or by building anything, and there is no other sphere available to compare it against, so everything the largest scales are ever going to say is already on it.
