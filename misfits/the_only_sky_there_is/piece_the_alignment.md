## Load Bearing

It is a real pattern whose significance depends on a second draw, which is the resource this play is about not having.
The lowest multipoles of the map are aligned with each other more closely than the model expects, and the quadrupole is low. Both are in the data and neither is disputed as a measurement. The dispute is about what the numbers mean, because the statistics were chosen after the sky was seen, and the clean answer to that is a fresh sky with the statistic fixed in advance. So a feature that would be settled anywhere else by one more observation stays exactly where it is.
