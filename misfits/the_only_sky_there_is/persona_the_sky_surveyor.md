# Persona: The Sky Surveyor

## Taxonomy

[Sky Surveyor](position_sky_surveyor.md). The one who measures the one sky, and finishes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A satellite, four years of integration, and a map of the microwave sky whose largest features are measured far better than they need to be.

## Action

Measures the sky to the instrument noise and past it.
Reports, and has nothing left to improve.

## Shadow

Has succeeded completely, and the success does not reduce the uncertainty that matters.
At the largest angular scales the instrument stopped being the limitation some time ago: the quadrupole of this sky is known, and a better telescope would return the same number with a smaller instrumental error that is already negligible beside the other term. There is no failure to point at, no exposure to extend and no technology to wait for. The seat has done the whole of what a seat can do, and the band on the left of the plot is the same width as before it started.

## Tell

Finishes the job and finds the error bar unmoved.
