## Load Bearing

It sets the precision of the estimate from the number of independent patterns available, which no instrument changes.
At each angular scale a sphere admits a fixed number of independent patterns, and the number is small at the largest scales: five at the quadrupole. An estimate of a variance from five numbers has a spread, and that spread is calculable exactly and is the same whatever measured those five numbers. So the width of the band is a property of how many things there are rather than of how well anybody saw them, which is why it is reported as its own term and why building a better satellite does not touch it.
