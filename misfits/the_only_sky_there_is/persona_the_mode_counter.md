# Persona: The Mode Counter

## Taxonomy

[Mode Counter](position_mode_counter.md). The one who shows the floor is arithmetic.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A single line of algebra: at each angular scale there are a fixed number of independent patterns available, and at the largest scale there are five.

## Action

Counts the modes.
Writes the floor down as a formula and puts it on the plot as a separate band.

## Shadow

Removes the mystery entirely, and removes no uncertainty.
This is not a caveat or an admission: it is a derivation, exact, agreed by everybody and reported as its own term beside the instrumental error. Knowing precisely why the floor is there and precisely how high it is changes nothing about how high it is. The seat's contribution is to make the limit legible rather than to soften it, and a limit that is fully understood is not thereby smaller.

## Tell

Explains the floor exactly, which is all that can be done to it.
