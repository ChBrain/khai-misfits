## Place

## Load Bearing

It is a sample of size one from the distribution the theory predicts, which is what makes the uncertainty irreducible rather than merely large.
The theory predicts a variance and the sky is one draw from it, so estimating the variance means estimating a spread from a single realisation. In any other science the answer is more realisations, and the reason it is unavailable here is not cost, difficulty or ethics: there is one universe and it has produced one sky. The sample cannot be enlarged, and the phrase for what is missing is not more data but another universe.
