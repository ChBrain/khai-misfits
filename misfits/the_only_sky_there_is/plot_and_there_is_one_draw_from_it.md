## Action

## Stage

## Tension

The sample cannot be enlarged, and what is missing is not data but another universe.
Every other science answers a small sample with a larger one, and the reason that answer is unavailable here has nothing to do with money, difficulty or ethics. There is one universe, it has produced one sky, and the last scattering surface is a sphere whose radius is set by the age of the universe rather than by any decision. So the shortfall is not in the collection.
