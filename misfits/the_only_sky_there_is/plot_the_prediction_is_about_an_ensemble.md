# Plot: The Prediction Is About an Ensemble

## Taxonomy

[The Only Sky There Is](play_the_only_sky_there_is.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A theory of the early universe, and a question about what it predicts.

## Action

[The ensemble theorist](persona_the_ensemble_theorist.md), holding [Ensemble Theorist](position_ensemble_theorist.md), fixes a variance at [the ensemble](place_the_ensemble.md): the mechanism determines a distribution and determines nothing beyond it.

## Stage

A prediction that is a distribution rather than a sky.

## Tension

The theory predicts a law and not a picture, and that is its strength rather than its evasion.
A mechanism that grows structure from quantum fluctuations cannot say which fluctuation lands where, and a version of it that claimed to would be a worse theory, not a better one. What it fixes is the statistical law the fluctuations obey. So the object the prediction is about is a population of possible universes, which is the standard and correct reading of every statement the field makes on these scales.
