# Piece: The Hidden Cost

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). The information asymmetry, the bureau alone knowing what the service truly costs to produce.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held inside [The Bureau](place_the_bureau.md), out of the sponsor's sight: the true cost of producing the service, known to the bureau that produces it and opaque to the funder that pays for it (Niskanen).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the sponsor can trim: if it could see the true cost, it would fund the efficient amount and the padding would be plain.
It bears the load as the blind spot the power of the purse rests on. Because the sponsor cannot tell an efficient budget from an inflated one, it cannot cost what it funds, so the monopoly of supply turns into a monopoly of terms, and the purse it holds cannot discipline what it cannot see.

## Apparent

The natural opacity of a complex service: of course the specialists know their costs better than the funder, an ordinary division of expertise, not a lever.

## Yearbook

Held beneath the setup in [No One to Compare](plot_no_one_to_compare.md), the asymmetry the monopoly turns to terms.
It is what [The Auditor](persona_the_auditor.md) would open and what [The Offer](piece_the_offer.md) exploits, the cost the sponsor cannot see and so cannot trim.
