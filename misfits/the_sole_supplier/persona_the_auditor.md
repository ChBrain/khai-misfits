# Persona: The Auditor

## Taxonomy

[Auditor](position_auditor.md). The one who would make the cost visible and restore the sponsor's leverage, at the cost of the audit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Rigorous, sceptical, out to price what no one has priced.
Sees a budget granted on a cost no one could check and means to check it: audit the books, compare the bureau against its peers, put the service to a market where one exists.
No zealotry in it: it is right that a cost the sponsor can see is a budget it can trim, and its aim is sound.

## Action

Works [Open the Books](plan_open_the_books.md) through [Auditing](process_auditing.md) at [the Audit](place_the_audit.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): it makes the cost visible where it can, by audit, by yardstick comparison, by contracting out to read a market price.
Cannot make the cost fully visible, since the bureau knows its own costs far better, comparison is imperfect where services differ, and some public goods cannot be bought on a market.
Does what a sound auditor does: it prices what it can and restores the sponsor's leverage exactly as far as the cost can be seen.

## Shadow

Not a zealot and not defeated: it is right that transparency disciplines the budget, and it is also unable to make the cost fully visible or cheaply.
Because the bureau outknows any auditor and some services resist comparison, the auditor proves that the power of the purse is bought, not given, so its check bounds the over-supply rather than ending it.
It is the one who can restore the sponsor's leverage, and the one who shows how dearly and how partly it is restored.

## Tell

Opens the books, prices what it can, and marks plainly where the cost still cannot be seen.
Cannot claim a full accounting, because the bureau knows more than it does, so it reports the leverage restored only as far as the audit reached, the rigorous check whose reach is exactly the reach of the visible cost.
