# Position: Auditor

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). The seat of the one who would make the cost visible and restore the sponsor's leverage, at the cost of the audit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The tools of transparency: the audit, the yardstick comparison with other jurisdictions, the option to contract the service out and read a market price.
The knowledge that a cost the sponsor can see is a budget it can trim.
No easy or complete view, since the bureau knows its own costs far better, comparison is imperfect where services differ, and some public goods cannot be bought on a market.

## Orders

Open the books: make the true cost visible so the sponsor can cost what it funds.
Audit the bureau, compare it against its peers, or price the service against a market, and hand the sponsor back the leverage its purse was supposed to carry.
Accept what the seat spends: that transparency is costly and resisted, that the bureau outknows the auditor, and that the fix reaches only as far as the cost can be seen.

## Loses

The ease of a clean correction.
Because the bureau knows its costs best and some services cannot be compared or contracted, the seat cannot make the cost fully visible for nothing, so what it loses is the costless fix, the leverage restored only in part and only where the cost can be read.
It keeps the right tools and loses their completeness, the check real, dear, and never total.

## Drives

Toward the one who restores the purse's power at the cost of the audit,
opening the books where it can and, by making the cost visible, letting the sponsor trim what it could not before,
so the seat that would discipline the budget is the seat that proves the discipline is bought, the over-supply checked only as far as the cost can be seen.
