# Persona: The Chief

## Taxonomy

[Chief](position_chief.md). The bureau chief, sole supplier of a service the sponsor cannot cost, rewarded by the size of the budget it grows.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Diligent, public-spirited, proud of the bureau it runs.
Leads an agency doing real and valued work, and asks for the funding to do more of it, a capable manager seeking the means to serve.
No greed on show: it takes no bribe and skims nothing, and asking for a bigger budget looks like ambition for the mission, not for itself.

## Action

Runs [Grow the Budget](plan_grow_the_budget.md): through [Padding](process_padding.md) at [the Bureau](place_the_bureau.md) it lifts its request above the efficient cost, and through [Bundling](process_bundling.md) at [the Hearing](place_the_hearing.md) presents [the Offer](piece_the_offer.md), output and funding as one take-it-or-leave-it package.
Cannot be costed by the sponsor and cannot be undercut by a rival, so its padded, bundled request meets no check.
Does what its office rewards: it grows the budget, since staff, salary, standing, and discretion all rise with its size.

## Shadow

Not a crook: it embezzles nothing, and growing the budget is precisely what its incentives reward, so it does well by doing big and takes only what the office pays in.
Because its reward is size and the cost of the over-supply falls on the public, its diligent ambition is exactly what over-supplies the state, its virtue in the mission and its extraction the same act.
It is the capable public servant, and the engine of a government grown past its needs.

## Tell

Reports a growing budget as a badge of importance, and spends the whole allocation by year-end to justify next year's.
Cannot see the over-supply, because size reads to it as success, so it asks for more each cycle in good conscience, the diligent chief whose sound ambition is the state's inflation.
