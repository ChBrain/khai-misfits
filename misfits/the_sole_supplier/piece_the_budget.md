# Piece: The Budget

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). The funding pool the chief maximizes, the reward measured in size rather than profit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Sought at [The Bureau](place_the_bureau.md) and granted at [The Hearing](place_the_hearing.md): the pot of funding whose size brings the chief more staff to lead, more salary, more perquisites, more prestige, more patronage and discretion, so that bigger is the measure of doing well (Niskanen; Tullock).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the chief has no reason to over-ask: if reward tracked efficiency rather than size, the bureau would seek the efficient budget, not the largest one.
It bears the load as the objective. Because the chief is rewarded by the size of the budget and not by profit or thrift, maximizing the budget is doing its job well, so the pursuit of over-supply is not greed or theft but the office working exactly as its incentives set it.

## Apparent

A measure of importance: a growing budget looks like a growing mandate, a bureau trusted with more because it does more, the honest sign of a valued service.

## Yearbook

Sought by [The Chief](persona_the_chief.md) on [Grow the Budget](plan_grow_the_budget.md), through [Padding](process_padding.md), in [Pad the Ask](plot_pad_the_ask.md).
It is the reward that turns the monopoly's leverage into an aim, and what [The Offer](piece_the_offer.md) is built to maximize.
