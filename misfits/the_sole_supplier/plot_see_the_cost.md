# Plot: See the Cost

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Audit](place_the_audit.md), where someone tries to restore the sponsor's leverage.

## Action

[The Auditor](persona_the_auditor.md), from [the auditor's seat](position_auditor.md), on [Open the Books](plan_open_the_books.md) through [Auditing](process_auditing.md) holds [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): make the cost visible by audit, yardstick comparison, or contracting out, and a budget the sponsor can cost is a budget it can trim, but the bureau knows its own costs best, comparison is imperfect where services differ, and contracting out is not possible for every good. The scene closes with the over-supply checkable at a cost, the trap bounded and its size contested (Migue and Belanger; Blais and Dion).

## Stage

The power of the purse rests on the ability to cost the service, so the remedy is to see the cost: audit it, compare it, price it against a market, and the sponsor can trim again, though each way of seeing is costly, resisted, and partial, and the pure size of the over-supply is itself contested.

## Tension

The auditor is right that a visible cost restores the leverage and cannot make the cost fully visible, since the bureau outknows it and some services cannot be compared or contracted, so the check reaches exactly as far as the cost can be seen and no further.
