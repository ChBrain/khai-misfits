# Plot: A Bigger Government

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Hearing](place_the_hearing.md), where the sponsor funds what it wants and cannot cost.

## Action

Through [Funding](process_funding.md), [The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md), from [the sponsor's seat](position_sponsor.md), on [Fund the Service](plan_fund_the_service.md) approves the package, wanting the service and unable to price it, so [The Oversupply](piece_the_oversupply.md): more of the service than its cost warrants, or the right amount at an inflated cost, government grown past the efficient size, and [The Public](persona_the_public.md), from [the public's seat](position_public.md), bears the bill. The scene closes with the over-supply funded, the surplus kept as budget, no one corrupt (Niskanen).

## Stage

No one stole and no one blundered: the chief grew the budget its office rewards, the sponsor funded a service it wanted and could not cost, and the public pays for a state larger than it needed, an extraction that reads as diligence and hides inside the impossibility of pricing it.

## Tension

The bureau looks better the bigger it grows, its rising budget a badge of importance, so the very sign the public reads as a well-run agency is the sign of the over-supply it is paying for.
