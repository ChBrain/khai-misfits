# Place: The Audit

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). Where the true cost is made visible and the sponsor's leverage might be restored, at a cost. Hosts [See the Cost](plot_see_the_cost.md).

## Owner

## Shown

The auditor's review: the books opened, the bureau set beside its peers, the service priced against a market where one can be found.

## Holds
