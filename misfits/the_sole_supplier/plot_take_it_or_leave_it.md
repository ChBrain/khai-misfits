# Plot: Take It or Leave It

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Hearing](place_the_hearing.md), where the bureau sets the terms.

## Action

Through [Bundling](process_bundling.md), [The Chief](persona_the_chief.md) presents [The Offer](piece_the_offer.md): output and funding as one package, this much service for this much money, so [The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md) can fund the whole thing or get nothing, unable to buy only the efficient amount at the efficient price. The scene closes with the surplus captured in the bundle, the sponsor with no lever to trim (Niskanen).

## Stage

The bundle is the extraction: because the bureau offers no smaller portion and there is nowhere else to buy, the sponsor cannot separate the efficient budget from the padded one, so all the surplus between them is captured in the take-it-or-leave-it, an ordinary-looking proposal that is a monopolist's ultimatum.

## Tension

The sponsor's only move is to accept the whole or refuse the whole, and refusing means forgoing a service the public needs, so the choice is rigged not by any threat but by the shape of the offer, the purse powerless against a package it cannot divide.
