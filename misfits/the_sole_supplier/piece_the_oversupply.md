# Piece: The Oversupply

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). The bureau delivering more than its cost warrants or the right amount at an inflated cost, government grown past the efficient size.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Delivered across [The Hearing](place_the_hearing.md) and out into the state: the funded package, larger than the efficient one, the extra service the public did not need at that price or the same service at a padded cost, the surplus kept as budget (Niskanen).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no misfit, only a bureau funded at cost: it is the over-supply, the state grown past the efficient size, that makes the structure a trap.
It carries the phenomenon whole. Because the sponsor funds a bundle it cannot cost from a supplier it cannot replace, the budget granted exceeds the efficient one, so government over-supplies and the difference is captured as staff and standing, a bigger and costlier state produced with no one corrupt.

## Apparent

A well-funded, active agency: from outside it looks like a diligent bureau doing a lot, its size a sign of importance rather than of extraction.

## Yearbook

Produced by [Funding](process_funding.md) in [A Bigger Government](plot_a_bigger_government.md), the bundle approved and the surplus kept.
