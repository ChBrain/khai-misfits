# Piece: The Monopoly

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). The bureau as the only provider of its service, so the sponsor has no rival to compare against and no market price to read.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Standing over [The Bureau](place_the_bureau.md): the one agency that supplies this service, with no competitor beside it and no market where the sponsor might buy the same thing elsewhere or read what it should cost (Niskanen; Downs).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the sponsor can shop: with a rival to compare or a market price to read, an inflated budget would be undercut and the extraction would fail.
It bears the load as the structural leverage. Because the bureau is the sole supplier, the sponsor cannot walk away to another provider or hold up a competing quote, so the bureau alone sets the terms, and the whole extraction rests on there being nowhere else to buy.

## Apparent

An efficiency, even: one agency for one job, no wasteful duplication, the sensible way to organise a public service.

## Yearbook

Stands over the setup in [No One to Compare](plot_no_one_to_compare.md), the sole supply the trap rests on.
It works together with [The Hidden Cost](piece_the_hidden_cost.md), the two asymmetries that let [The Offer](piece_the_offer.md) be set take-it-or-leave-it.
