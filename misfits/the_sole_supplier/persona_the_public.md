# Persona: The Public

## Taxonomy

[Public](position_public.md). The taxpayer who bears the cost of the over-supply, funding a government larger than it needed to be.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, trusting, going about its life.
Pays its taxes, wants the services the state provides, and assumes the money is spent about as well as it can be, with no reason to think otherwise.
No complaint ready: it sees a functioning bureau and a service delivered, and takes the cost as the ordinary price of government.

## Action

Bears [the Oversupply](piece_the_oversupply.md) in [A Bigger Government](plot_a_bigger_government.md): it funds, through the sponsor that acts for it, a budget it cannot see and a service larger than it needed, and pays the difference in its taxes.
Cannot see the padding or reach the bureau, standing far behind the sponsor and further from the chief, so it meets only the bill.
Does nothing that caused the over-supply: it only pays for what the monopoly and the hidden cost produced.

## Shadow

Not the author of its cost and not simply careless: the state was grown by choices it never made, and its trust in the bureau is not the failing that inflated the budget.
Because the extraction runs through a bargain it never sees, the seat bears a cost with no thief to blame, so it pays for a bigger government and cannot even name a wrong that made it so.
It is the one furthest from the bargain, and the one who funds it.

## Tell

Pays the bill without a murmur, sure the money must be going where it should.
Cannot see the over-supply, because it is hidden inside a cost no one outside the bureau can price, so it funds the extraction as an ordinary tax, the trusting public that carries a state grown past its needs.
