# Position: Sponsor

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). The seat of the funder who holds the purse but cannot cost the service or buy it elsewhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The purse, and the formal power to approve the budget or refuse it.
A genuine want of the service, which the public it answers for needs.
No sight of the true cost and no alternative supplier, so it cannot tell an efficient budget from an inflated one, nor take its custom elsewhere.

## Orders

Fund the service you want, from the only supplier there is.
Weigh the package offered, and, wanting the service and unable to price it, approve it rather than forgo it, since the alternative on the table is none.
Accept what the seat cannot do: cost the service, buy a part of it, or find it elsewhere, so its power over the purse cannot become a power over the budget.

## Loses

The discipline the purse was supposed to give.
Because it cannot cost the service, it cannot trim the budget to the efficient level, so what it loses is the very leverage of holding the purse, and it loses it not to any theft but to the two asymmetries it cannot close.
It keeps the formal power to approve and loses the real power to price, holding the purse and not the terms.

## Drives

Toward the one who holds the purse and not the power,
funding a wanted service it cannot cost and, by funding it, granting the surplus it cannot see to trim,
so the seat that looks to command the money is the seat that takes the supplier's terms, its authority real on paper and empty against a cost it cannot read.
