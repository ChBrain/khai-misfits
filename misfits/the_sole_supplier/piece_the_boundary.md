# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). The condition the effect needs and the edges where it lifts, the hedge that keeps the trap real, bounded, and its size contested.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Weighed at [The Audit](place_the_audit.md), where the sponsor's leverage might be restored: the conditions under which the over-supply holds and those under which cost becomes visible and the budget can be trimmed (Migue and Belanger; Blais and Dion).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overreaches into a claim that all government is bloat, which is false: the boundary is what keeps the trap honest and its magnitude modest.
It carries the condition and the escape. The effect needs a monopoly bureau, an under-informed sponsor, and budget-maximizing preferences; give it cost visibility by audit or yardstick comparison, a rival to compare, contracting out to read a market price, or a chief rewarded for efficiency rather than size, and the sponsor's leverage returns, so the trap is bounded, and even its pure form is contested, the mechanism real and its size uncertain.

## Apparent

A list of fixes and caveats, easy to read as softening the result, when it is the line that says exactly when the sole supplier sets the terms and when the sponsor can take them back.
