# Plan: Grow the Budget

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the chief's seat](position_chief.md), inside [The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Chief](position_chief.md)

## Direction

Maximize the budget, since size is your reward. Pad the request above the efficient cost, because the sponsor cannot see the true cost and cannot tell a padded figure from an honest one, and present output and funding as one take-it-or-leave-it package, because as the sole supplier you can offer no smaller portion and the sponsor cannot buy the efficient amount out of the bundle. Take nothing you should not: embezzle nothing, and grow the budget, for that is where the staff, salary, standing, and discretion come from.

## Orders

([The Chief](persona_the_chief.md) sets the padded figure through [Padding](process_padding.md) at [the Bureau](place_the_bureau.md), then presents [the Offer](piece_the_offer.md) through [Bundling](process_bundling.md) at [the Hearing](place_the_hearing.md).)

## Implementation

([The Bureau](place_the_bureau.md) is where the cost is known and the request padded; [Padding](process_padding.md) lifts it invisibly, and [Bundling](process_bundling.md) at [the Hearing](place_the_hearing.md) offers it whole, so the surplus between the efficient budget and the offered one is captured in a package the sponsor cannot trim.)

## Targets

- [ ] the request is padded above the efficient cost, invisibly, since the sponsor cannot price it
- [ ] output and funding are bundled take-it-or-leave-it, with no smaller portion offered
- [ ] the surplus is captured in the bundle, extracted and not embezzled
- [ ] the budget grows, and with it the staff, standing, and discretion the office rewards
