# Process: Funding

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). Borne at [The Hearing](place_the_hearing.md), precipitated by [The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md) approving the bundle, in [A Bigger Government](plot_a_bigger_government.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A service the sponsor genuinely wants and cannot cost or buy anywhere else.

## Direction

From a bundle offered toward a bundle funded, the whole package approved because the alternative is none of it.

## Lever

That wanting the service, unable to price it, and with no other supplier, the sponsor approves the package rather than forgo the service, and the surplus is granted with it (Niskanen).

## Echo

[The Oversupply](piece_the_oversupply.md), the state grown past the efficient size, the surplus kept as budget.
