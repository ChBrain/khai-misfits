# Plan: Fund the Service

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the sponsor's seat](position_sponsor.md), inside [The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Sponsor](position_sponsor.md)

## Direction

Fund the service the public needs, from the only supplier there is. Weigh the bureau's package as carefully as you can, press it on its figures, and, finding you cannot cost the service and cannot buy it elsewhere, approve the bundle rather than forgo a service that is genuinely wanted. Do not imagine the purse gives you the power to price: you can grant or refuse the whole, but you cannot trim it, because the cost is the bureau's to know and the supply is the bureau's to give.

## Orders

([The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md) approves the package through [Funding](process_funding.md) at [the Hearing](place_the_hearing.md), wanting the service and unable to cost or shop it.)

## Implementation

([The Hearing](place_the_hearing.md) is where the bundle is funded; [Funding](process_funding.md) approves the whole package because the alternative is none of it, so the sponsor grants the surplus it cannot see, its prudence stopping where the hidden cost begins.)

## Targets

- [ ] the bureau's figures are pressed and cannot be independently checked
- [ ] the service is genuinely wanted, and there is no other supplier to turn to
- [ ] the whole package is funded rather than the service forgone
- [ ] the surplus is granted with it, the purse held and the terms the bureau's
