# Plot: No One to Compare

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Bureau](place_the_bureau.md), the one agency that supplies its service.

## Action

[The Monopoly](piece_the_monopoly.md) stands: the bureau is the only provider of its service, so the sponsor has no rival to compare it against and no market price to read. And [The Hidden Cost](piece_the_hidden_cost.md) lies beneath it, only the bureau knowing what the service truly costs to produce. The scene closes with a sole supplier and an under-informed sponsor, no rival to compare and no price to read (Niskanen).

## Stage

Two asymmetries, not one: the bureau is the only place to buy, and the only one who knows what buying costs. A monopoly of supply alone might still be costed from outside, and a hidden cost alone might still be shopped elsewhere, but together they leave the sponsor with neither a price nor an exit.

## Tension

The sponsor holds the purse and looks to hold the power, but it can neither cost the service nor buy it elsewhere, so the leverage the purse should give is disarmed before a single figure is named.
