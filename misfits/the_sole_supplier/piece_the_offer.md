# Piece: The Offer

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). The take-it-or-leave-it package of output and funding, so the sponsor funds the whole thing or gets nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Presented at [The Hearing](place_the_hearing.md): the bureau's proposal bound into one bundle, this much service for this much money, offered whole so the sponsor cannot pick the efficient amount at the efficient price out of it (Niskanen).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the sponsor negotiates: able to counter, to fund a part, to price the increment, it would trim the budget to the efficient level.
It bears the load as the mechanism of capture. Because the bureau bundles output and funding and offers no smaller portion, the sponsor's only choice is the whole package or none, so all the surplus between the efficient budget and the offered one is captured in the bundling, the take-it-or-leave-it the very act that extracts it.

## Apparent

A budget proposal: from outside it looks like an ordinary funding request, a bureau stating what it needs for what it does, not a monopolist's ultimatum.
