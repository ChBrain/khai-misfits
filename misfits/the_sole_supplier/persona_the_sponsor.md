# Persona: The Sponsor

## Taxonomy

[Sponsor](position_sponsor.md). The funder who holds the purse but cannot cost the service or buy it elsewhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Prudent, accountable, guardian of the public money.
Holds the purse and means to spend it well, weighing the bureau's request with the seriousness of a funder answerable for every dollar.
No laxity in it: it takes its duty seriously and would trim any budget it could show to be too large.

## Action

Runs [Fund the Service](plan_fund_the_service.md), and through [Funding](process_funding.md) at [the Hearing](place_the_hearing.md) approves the package, wanting the service, unable to price it, and with no other supplier to turn to.
Cannot cost the service or buy a part of it, so it faces the whole bundle or none, and funds it rather than forgo a service it needs.
Does what a responsible funder does with the information it has: it approves a wanted service it cannot independently price.

## Shadow

Not a dupe: it is prudent and dutiful, and funding a service it genuinely wants and cannot cost is the reasonable act, not a failure of care.
Because it cannot cost the service and cannot shop, its prudence cannot become discipline, so the guardian of the purse grants the surplus it has no way to see, holding the money and not the terms.
It is the careful funder, and the one whose care the hidden cost renders toothless.

## Tell

Presses the bureau on its figures, is given numbers it cannot check, and approves the budget as the best it can do.
Cannot pierce the cost, because only the bureau knows it, so it signs off on a package it cannot price, the diligent sponsor whose power over the purse stops exactly where the hidden cost begins.
