# Pitch: The Funding We Need

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Responsible and public-spirited.
A bureau doing important work and asking for the funding to do it, a sponsor holding the purse and meaning to spend it well, a service the public genuinely wants.
Nothing false in it: the service is real and wanted, the bureau does the work, and asking for the funding to do it is exactly what a bureau should do.

## Undertow

Under the responsible ask, a monopoly of supply and cost.
The bureau is the only provider and the only one who knows what the service costs, and its chief is rewarded by the size of the budget, so it bundles output and funding take-it-or-leave-it and extracts more than the service is worth (Niskanen).
No crook and no dupe: growing the budget is where the chief's reward lies, funding a wanted service you cannot cost is reasonable, and both are exactly what over-supply the state.

## Nerve

The trust in a sponsor who holds the purse, and the catch that holding the purse is not the same as holding the power:
that the power of the purse rests on costing the service, and where the sponsor cannot cost it, the sole supplier sets the terms, the funding granted and the surplus kept the one hidden cost seen twice,
so a bureau reads as diligent and well-run, its growing budget a badge of importance, while the over-supply hides inside the impossibility of pricing it.

## Echo

After the run: an agency that spends its whole allocation by year-end to justify next year's, a department that measures success by headcount and budget, a government larger and costlier than it needed to be, a bill with no thief behind it (Niskanen; Tullock).
And the remedy, at a cost and not certain: make the cost visible by audit, yardstick comparison, or contracting out, or reward the chief for efficiency rather than size, though the bureau knows its own costs best and some public goods have no market price (Migue and Belanger).
And the bound held in view, because the effect is conditional and its size contested: it needs a monopoly bureau, an under-informed sponsor, and a reward in budget, so the whole of it is that whoever alone can supply a thing and alone knows its cost sets the price of it.
