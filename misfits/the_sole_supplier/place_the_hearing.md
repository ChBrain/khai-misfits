# Place: The Hearing

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). Where the budget request is presented and funded, the bureau facing the sponsor across the purse. Hosts [Take It or Leave It](plot_take_it_or_leave_it.md) and [A Bigger Government](plot_a_bigger_government.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The budget hearing: the bureau's request laid out, the sponsor weighing it, the formal ritual of a funder deciding what to grant.

## Holds

[The Offer](piece_the_offer.md), the bundle presented here, [The Budget](piece_the_budget.md) as it is granted, and [The Oversupply](piece_the_oversupply.md) once it is funded.

## Offers

To the bureau, a forum to present its package whole; to the sponsor, the choice to fund it or forgo the service.

## Withheld

The true cost of the service, and any portion smaller than the whole bundle, so the sponsor decides on a package it cannot price and cannot divide.
