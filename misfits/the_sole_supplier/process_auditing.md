# Process: Auditing

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). Borne at [The Audit](place_the_audit.md), precipitated by [The Auditor](persona_the_auditor.md) making the true cost visible, in [See the Cost](plot_see_the_cost.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The wish to restore the sponsor's leverage, once the over-supply is understood to rest on a cost it cannot see.

## Direction

From a hidden cost toward a visible one, by audit, by comparison with other jurisdictions, or by contracting the service out to read a market price.

## Lever

That a cost the sponsor can see is a budget it can trim, so making the cost visible turns the power of the purse back into a real check, at the cost of the audit and against a bureau that knows its own costs best (Migue and Belanger).

## Echo

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the leverage restored where cost can be seen and slipping back where it cannot.
