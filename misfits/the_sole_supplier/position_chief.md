# Position: Chief

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). The seat of the bureau chief, sole supplier of a service the sponsor cannot cost, rewarded by the size of the budget it grows.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A monopoly of supply, and the only true knowledge of what the service costs.
A reward measured in the size of the budget: more staff, salary, standing, and discretion the larger it grows.
No profit to keep and no market to answer to, so the budget itself, and not efficiency, is what its success is counted in.

## Orders

Grow the budget: pad the request above the efficient cost, and bundle output and funding so the sponsor cannot trim it.
Ask for more, because more is the reward, and present the whole package take-it-or-leave-it, since the sponsor cannot cost it and cannot buy elsewhere.
Accept what the seat does: that maximizing the budget is doing the job well, and that doing it well over-supplies the state.

## Loses

Nothing it feels, and that is the shape of it.
Because its reward is the size of the budget and the cost of the over-supply falls on the public, the seat loses nothing it bears, so it grows the budget with a clear conscience, the extraction costing it no more than the effort of the ask.
It keeps every gain of size and loses only the efficient budget it was never rewarded to seek.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound pursuit of its reward over-supplies the state,
padding and bundling because size is the measure of success and, by doing so, extracting a budget above the efficient one,
so the seat that only did well by doing big is the seat through which government grows past what it needed, diligent in each ask and costly in their sum.
