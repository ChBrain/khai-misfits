# Plan: Open the Books

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the auditor's seat](position_auditor.md), inside [The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Auditor](position_auditor.md)

## Direction

Make the true cost visible, so the sponsor can cost what it funds. Audit the bureau's books, compare it against other jurisdictions that run the same service, or contract the service out to read a market price, since a cost the sponsor can see is a budget it can trim. Expect the fix to be partial and dear: the bureau knows its own costs far better than you, comparison is imperfect where services differ, and some public goods have no market to be priced against.

## Orders

([The Auditor](persona_the_auditor.md) makes the cost visible through [Auditing](process_auditing.md) at [the Audit](place_the_audit.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) between where cost can be seen and where it cannot.)

## Implementation

([The Audit](place_the_audit.md) is where the cost is priced; [Auditing](process_auditing.md) opens the books, compares, or reads a market price, restoring the sponsor's leverage exactly as far as the cost becomes visible, and leaving the over-supply wherever it stays hidden.)

## Targets

- [ ] the true cost is made visible by audit, yardstick comparison, or contracting out
- [ ] a cost the sponsor can see becomes a budget it can trim
- [ ] the bureau knows its own costs best, so the view is partial and dear
- [ ] the leverage is restored only as far as the cost can be seen, and no further
