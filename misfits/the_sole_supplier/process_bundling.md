# Process: Bundling

## Taxonomy

[The Sole Supplier](play_the_sole_supplier.md). Borne at [The Hearing](place_the_hearing.md), precipitated by [The Chief](persona_the_chief.md) presenting output and funding as one package, in [Take It or Leave It](plot_take_it_or_leave_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The wish to capture the surplus, once the request is set above the efficient cost.

## Direction

From a divisible request toward an all-or-nothing bundle, output and funding offered whole with no smaller portion on the table.

## Lever

That as the sole supplier the bureau can decline to offer any part, so the sponsor's only choice is the whole package or none, and the surplus is captured in the bundling (Niskanen).

## Echo

[The Offer](piece_the_offer.md), take it or leave it, the bundle the sponsor cannot trim.
