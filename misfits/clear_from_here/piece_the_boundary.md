# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). The condition and the hedge: the trap needs a genuinely uncertain decision judged after its result is known, and lifts under records written before the outcome, never entirely.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held at [The Archive](place_the_archive.md), where a sealed contemporaneous entry is the only artefact that survives the straightening, and cannot vouch for its own honesty.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overclaims in a way that would do harm: without the boundary it reads as an argument that inquiries should not be held, that decision-makers should not be accountable, or that any adverse finding after a failure is an artefact, when plenty of criticised decisions were genuinely poor and the mechanism staged here says nothing whatever about which.
It bears the load as the honest limit and the price list. The record works, and Fischhoff and Beyth is why it must be written rather than recalled, and it costs upkeep across decisions that will never be reviewed, is composed by someone who knows it may be read at an inquiry, and can be furnished afterward, so it narrows the gap and does not close it.

## Apparent

A footnote, even: qualifications at the end of a finding whose headline is far easier to repeat than its conditions.

## Yearbook

Stands over the close in [Write It Down Before](plot_write_it_down_before.md), the remedy named with its price and its limits.
It works together with [The Straightening](piece_the_straightening.md), the only thing that outlives it and the thing it cannot fully answer.
