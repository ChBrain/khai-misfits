# Piece: The Lost Lesson

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). The understanding the review was convened to produce and replaces with a culprit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Absent from [The Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md)'s findings, and absent in a form nobody notices, since the report is complete and its recommendations are actionable.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit is only an injustice to one person: hard on the decider, certainly, but bounded, and the institution would at least come away knowing something.
It bears the load as the structural cost that outlives the individual case. Because a finding of a missed obvious sign yields the instruction to notice obvious signs, the action item teaches nothing, the sign not having been obvious then and not being obvious next time, and the genuine lesson, that this was a real gamble and here is how such gambles should be weighed, is exactly what creeping determinism makes invisible.

## Apparent

A lesson learned, formally and on paper: findings issued, actions assigned, the file closed, and every box that an institution is judged on ticked.

## Yearbook

Stands beneath the close of [Someone Should Have Known](plot_someone_should_have_known.md), the cost that survives the case.
It works together with [The Verdict](piece_the_verdict.md), what the review produced instead of what it came for.
