# Position: Decider

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). The seat that calls it under genuine uncertainty and is later read against a map with one road on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Several live endings and evidence pointing more than one way.
Two hundred signals, of which three will later be quoted and are not now distinguishable from the rest.
No record of the fog, since the fog is the ordinary condition of working and nothing about it seemed worth writing down.

## Orders

Call it as it stands: choose the action that holds up across the spread of what might happen, rather than betting the outcome.
Do not wait for certainty that is not coming, since declining to decide is itself a decision and usually the worse one.
Accept what the seat does: it takes a defensible position that a different roll would have vindicated, and cannot mark which signals will matter.

## Loses

The ability to be judged on what it actually faced.
Because the fog was never recorded and its own memory drifts toward the outcome like everyone else's, the seat loses even its own testimony, and may come to half-believe it saw the warning it did not see.
It keeps a sound decision and loses every means of demonstrating that it was one.

## Drives

Toward the one judged against a vantage it never had and cannot reconstruct,
deciding soundly because deciding soundly is the job and, by doing so, leaving behind a record that will read as a warning ignored the moment one branch arrives,
so the seat that did the work correctly is the seat that answers for it, defensible on the day and indefensible afterward.
