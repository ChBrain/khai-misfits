# Plan: Ask If It Was Foreseeable

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reviewer's seat](position_reviewer.md), inside [Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reviewer](position_reviewer.md)

## Direction

Put the question an inquiry exists to put: on what was available at the time, could this have been foreseen and should it have been acted on. Read the record carefully, quote it accurately, and reach the conclusion it supports. Do not manufacture a pattern, and note that you will not need to: the pattern is genuinely present in the record now. Accept that resolving to disregard the outcome does not remove it, since people so warned and so instructed show the effect regardless, and the standard you apply is being applied to a record the outcome has already rearranged.

## Orders

([The Reviewer](persona_the_reviewer.md) reads through [Straightening](process_straightening.md) at [the Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md), meeting [the Straightening](piece_the_straightening.md).)

## Implementation

([The Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md) is where the record is read from the far side; [Straightening](process_straightening.md) has already made the signals that pointed the way it went salient and let the rest recede, so careful reading resolves the episode into a line and the care is what sharpens it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the record is read closely and quoted accurately
- [ ] no pattern is invented, none being needed
- [ ] the outcome is not treated as set aside merely because setting it aside was intended
- [ ] the conclusion reached is the one the record now supports, which is the whole of the difficulty
