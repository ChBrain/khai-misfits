# Plan: Call It as It Stands

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the decider's seat](position_decider.md), inside [Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Decider](position_decider.md)

## Direction

Choose the action that holds up across the spread of what might happen, rather than betting on the branch you think most likely. Do not wait for certainty that is not coming: declining to decide is itself a decision and usually the worse one. Do not attempt to mark which signals will later matter, because that is knowable only from the far side, and the three that will be quoted back at you are not distinguishable now from the two hundred beside them.

## Orders

([The Decider](persona_the_decider.md) chooses through [Deciding](process_deciding.md) at [the Moment](place_the_moment.md), weighing [the Fog](piece_the_fog.md).)

## Implementation

([The Moment](place_the_moment.md) is where several endings are live at once; [Deciding](process_deciding.md) selects against the spread, producing a call that a different roll would have vindicated and that nothing in the record distinguishes, at this point, from a lucky one.)

## Targets

- [ ] the action chosen holds up across the range of live outcomes, not against one of them
- [ ] the decision is taken rather than deferred into certainty that will not arrive
- [ ] no attempt is made to guess which signals will later be salient
- [ ] the call is defensible on the information available, which is all that can be asked
