## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). The finding that follows the straightening: a sound call recorded as a warning ignored, and a reckless one that came off recorded as judgement.

## Place

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the distortion stays private: a reviewer who misread the past and drew no consequence from it would leave a bad impression and nothing else, and the misfit would not reach anyone.
It bears the load as the point where perception becomes consequence. Because the finding is binding, it converts a distortion nobody could have avoided into a career, a liability, or a precedent, and it does so in both directions at once, disciplining the careful and promoting the lucky by the same reasoning.

## Apparent

Accountability delivered: a serious failure met with a serious finding, which is precisely what an institution is criticised for failing to produce.
