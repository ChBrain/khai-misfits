# Process: Straightening

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). Borne at [The Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md), precipitated by [The Reviewer](persona_the_reviewer.md) reading a record whose ending it already knows, in [It Was Always Going to Happen](plot_it_was_always_going_to_happen.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Outcome knowledge arriving before the reading begins, which is the ordinary condition of every review ever held.

## Direction

From two hundred undifferentiated signals toward three that were pointing all along, the rest receding, the episode resolving into a line that could only have run one way.

## Lever

That the reviewer is seeing this rather than inventing it, the pattern being genuinely present in the record once the ending is known, and that warning people about the effect and instructing them to disregard the outcome does not remove it (Fischhoff).

## Echo

[The Straightening](piece_the_straightening.md), a past rearranged, and a fog that cannot be recovered by resolving to recover it.
