# Plot: Someone Should Have Known

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md), where an account is owed to someone who is entitled to it.

## Action

Through [Judging](process_judging.md), [the Claimant](persona_the_claimant.md) on [Demand an Account](plan_demand_an_account.md) presses a demand that cannot be waved off, and [the Verdict](piece_the_verdict.md) follows: a sound call recorded as a warning ignored, and elsewhere a reckless call that came off recorded as judgement. Beneath it stands [the Lost Lesson](piece_the_lost_lesson.md), since a finding that someone missed an obvious sign yields the instruction to notice obvious signs, which teaches nothing, the sign not having been obvious then and not being obvious next time. The scene closes with accountability formally delivered and understanding forgone (Baron and Hershey).

## Stage

The demand is legitimate and must be met. A system that answered every serious failure with a shrug would be no system at all, so removing the pressure would remove accountability itself, which is a worse arrangement by a wide margin.

## Tension

The propriety of the demand is what gives the distortion its binding force, so the actor with the strongest moral claim in the play is the one through whom the error becomes a finding, and the honest answer available, a description of the fog, is not an account and names nobody.
