# Persona: The Claimant

## Taxonomy

[Claimant](position_claimant.md). The one harmed, whose demand for an account is legitimate and is what makes the review necessary.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Wronged and owed, asking the question anyone in its position would ask: how did this happen and who is answerable.
Has suffered something real and is not obliged to accept that nobody is responsible for it.
No vindictiveness on show: it wants an explanation, which is the minimum a serious institution owes after a serious failure.

## Action

Runs [Demand an Account](plan_demand_an_account.md): through [Judging](process_judging.md) at [the Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md), pressing a demand that cannot be waved off, and [the Verdict](piece_the_verdict.md) follows from a record the outcome has already straightened.
Cannot accept an answer of pure misfortune, since a system that met every serious failure with a shrug would be no system at all, and it is right about that.
Does what the harmed are entitled to do, and what any decent arrangement must let them do.

## Shadow

Not unreasonable: the entitlement is genuine, an inquiry is the correct response to serious harm, and removing the demand would remove accountability itself, which is a cure far worse than the disease.
Because the demand is legitimate and the record it is answered from has been rearranged by the outcome, its rightful claim is what converts a distorted reading into a formal verdict, so the very propriety of the demand is what gives the distortion its teeth.
It is the actor with the strongest moral standing in the play and the one whose standing makes the trap bite.

## Tell

Asks who knew and when, which is exactly the right question and the one the straightened record answers most confidently and least reliably.
Cannot be answered by a description of the fog, since a fog is not an account and does not name anyone, so the honest reply is also the one that sounds like an evasion.
