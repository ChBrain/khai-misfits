# Process: Deciding

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). Borne at [The Moment](place_the_moment.md), precipitated by [The Decider](persona_the_decider.md) choosing with several endings still live, in [A Call in the Fog](plot_a_call_in_the_fog.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A choice that will not wait for certainty, and evidence pointing in more than one direction.

## Direction

From an undifferentiated field of signals toward an action selected against the spread of possible endings rather than against any single one.

## Lever

That a sound call and a lucky one are indistinguishable at this point, since what separates them is a branch that has not arrived, and no feature of the record marks which it will be.

## Echo

[The Fog](piece_the_fog.md), the state of knowledge the decision was actually made in, and the state nobody writes down.
