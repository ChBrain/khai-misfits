# Position: Reviewer

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). The seat that reads honestly from a vantage the record's author never occupied, and can occupy no other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The complete record, and the outcome, which arrived before the reading began.
An honest intention and no grievance against the decider.
No access to any vantage without the outcome in it, that not being a place a person can stand.

## Orders

Ask if it was foreseeable: put the question an inquiry exists to put, and answer it from what the record shows.
Read carefully and quote accurately, which the seat does and which is not in question.
Accept what the seat does: it applies whatever standard it holds to a record the outcome has already rearranged, so the standard is not the thing that saves it.

## Loses

Its own corrective, since care is what sharpens the error.
Because reading more closely makes the line clearer rather than fainter, the seat loses the ordinary remedy for a poor review, and being warned about the bias and resolving to discount the outcome does not restore it.
It keeps every quotation accurate and loses the state of knowledge the quotations were produced in.

## Drives

Toward the one whose diligence is the exact route the distortion travels,
reading honestly because reading honestly is the job and, by doing so, resolving two hundred signals into three that were pointing all along,
so the most conscientious seat at the table is the seat the error lives in, accurate in every citation and wrong about the decision.
