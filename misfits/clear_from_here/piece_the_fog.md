# Piece: The Fog

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). The genuine uncertainty the decision was taken in: several endings live, and two hundred signals among which three will later be famous.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Filling [The Moment](place_the_moment.md), unrecorded, because it is not an event but the ordinary condition of working and nothing about it seems worth writing down (Fischhoff).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no misfit, only a fair verdict: a decision taken in clear view, where the outcome really was foreseeable and really was ignored, deserves exactly the finding it gets, and no one needs a play about that.
It bears the load as the thing that must have existed and cannot be produced. Because the fog is what makes the decision sound and is also the first thing the outcome destroys, the play's whole injustice consists in a state of knowledge that was real, was decisive, and left no trace.

## Apparent

Nothing at all, which is its difficulty: uncertainty does not feel like a fact about the situation, it feels like not having finished thinking, so nobody logs it.

## Yearbook

Stands over the setup in [A Call in the Fog](plot_a_call_in_the_fog.md), the condition the decision was actually made in.
It works together with [The Straightening](piece_the_straightening.md), the thing that was there and the process that removes it.
