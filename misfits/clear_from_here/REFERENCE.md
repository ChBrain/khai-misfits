# Clear from Here: Reference

The research warrant for **Clear from Here**, a staging of the phenomenon the literature calls **hindsight
bias**, or **creeping determinism** (Fischhoff): that knowing how something turned out reorganises how
foreseeable it appears, so a decision made under genuine uncertainty is evaluated against a
reconstruction in which the actual outcome was always the likely one, and a sound call with a bad result
is judged as negligence while a reckless one with a good result is judged as foresight, with no evaluator
pretending and none able to set the knowledge aside. It is held with the finding that the effect is
conditional on a genuinely uncertain decision judged after its result is known, and that it lifts under
records written before the outcome, which cost upkeep, are composed by someone who knows they may be read
at an inquiry, can be curated afterward, and never close the gap entirely.

## Line of Work

Staging hindsight bias as a **vantage-does-the-work structure**: Fischhoff gave people accounts of
historical episodes and asked how probable each possible ending had been. Told which ending had actually
occurred, they rated it as having been substantially more probable than people judging the same account
without that knowledge, and they did not notice the difference, which he named creeping determinism, the
past rearranging itself into a line that could only have run one way (Fischhoff). The sharper
demonstration came the same year: Fischhoff and Beyth had people forecast the outcomes of Nixon's trips
to China and the Soviet Union, then afterward asked them to recall their own predictions, and the
recalled forecasts drifted toward what had happened, diverging from what those same people had written
down at the time. They were not lying; they could not get back to what they had thought before they knew
(Fischhoff and Beyth). This is what makes it a trap rather than a lapse: it is not produced by
carelessness and is not cured by care, since people warned of the bias and instructed to disregard the
outcome still show it, the outcome having already reorganised what the record looks like. The three
warning signs that were three signals among two hundred are now the three that pointed at it all along,
and the evaluator is not manufacturing that pattern but seeing it, because it is genuinely there once the
ending is known. The consequence runs in both directions: a sound decision with a bad result reads as a
warning ignored, and a reckless decision that happened to come off reads as judgement, the companion
effect by which decision quality is inferred from outcomes rather than from the information available at
the time (Baron and Hershey). The play models the documented mechanics: the genuine ex-ante uncertainty,
the outcome that arrives and cannot be un-known, the straightening of the record around it, the memory
that will not return to its prior state, the verdict that follows, the legitimate demand for an account
that makes the review necessary, the lesson consumed when the finding is a culprit, and the contemporaneous
record that alone hands the evaluator the fog rather than a reconstruction, as the load-bearing vectors of
the structure, and not as a claim that reviewers are unfair, that inquiries should not be held, or that
decision-makers should not be accountable. The ordinary expectation is that an honest reading of a
complete record recovers what was knowable at the time; the finding is that the reading is done from a
vantage the record's own author never occupied, and nobody can stand anywhere else. The governing law is
that **where a decision taken under genuine uncertainty is evaluated after its outcome is known, the
knowledge of the outcome raises the apparent foreseeability of that outcome, so the evaluation scores
luck as judgement and judgement as luck, and does so without any evaluator being able to correct for it
by intending to**.

It is a retrospection-and-judgement structure no staged misfit holds, and it is carefully distinct from
its nearest neighbours. It is closest to The Paper Trail, and the two are genuinely related, so the cut
must be exact: there a bad outcome is met with blame for departing from procedure, so each worker is
rational to make its acts **defensible rather than effective**, effort flows to documentation, and the
aim recedes. That is a **behavioural** trap, running forward through incentives, in which the worker
changes what it does in anticipation of how it will be judged. Here the trap is **perceptual** and runs
backward: nothing about the decider's behaviour is at issue, the distortion lives entirely in the
evaluator, and it operates on a decision already taken by someone who may never have thought about the
inquiry at all. The two compound in the world, since defensive practice is partly a response to
anticipated hindsight judgement, and the house stages them as separate mechanisms because they fail
differently and lift differently: The Paper Trail lifts by changing what is judged, and this one does not
lift by changing what is judged at all, since the evaluator applying any standard whatever is applying it
to a reconstructed record. It is distinct from The Planning Fallacy, where a planner underestimates its
own future task by taking the inside view over the base rate: there the error is **ex ante and
first-person**, committed by the person doing the thing before they do it, whereas here it is ex post and
third-person, committed by someone else about a decision already made, and the two point in opposite
temporal directions. And it is distinct from Known Too Late, the Collingridge dilemma, where a
technology's effects cannot be known early enough to control and cannot be controlled once known: there
the difficulty is a genuine **timing mismatch between knowledge and power**, and nobody misperceives
anything, whereas here the knowledge is present and it is the perception of what was knowable earlier
that is distorted.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast a
  scapegoating panel or a witch-hunt, because the finding it stages is the opposite: the reviewer is
  reading honestly and reaching the conclusion the record now supports, and people warned of the bias and
  instructed to disregard the outcome still show it, so the distortion is not a failure of good faith and
  is not fixed by more of it (Fischhoff). Nor will it cast the demand for accountability as the problem.
  The harmed are entitled to an account, and a serious failure met with no inquiry at all would be the
  worse arrangement by a wide margin, so the demand that produces the review is legitimate and must be
  met. The loss is authored by no one: it is a genuinely uncertain decision plus an outcome that cannot
  be un-known, so every part of the process works correctly and arrives at a wrong answer about a right
  decision.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. A decision log written
  before the outcome and sealed does hand the evaluator the fog rather than a reconstruction, and
  Fischhoff and Beyth is precisely the demonstration of why it must be written rather than recalled. It
  is also expensive to maintain across every decision that might one day be reviewed and mostly will not
  be; it is only as good as the honesty of an entry composed by someone who knows it may be read at an
  inquiry; and a log that can be added to is a log that can be furnished afterward. The reckoning is that
  the record narrows the gap and does not close it, and that no standard the evaluator applies repairs
  the problem, since any standard is being applied to a record the outcome has already rearranged.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The finding and the naming of creeping determinism are Fischhoff's; the
  demonstration that people cannot recover their own prior forecasts is Fischhoff and Beyth's; and the
  companion effect by which decision quality is inferred from outcomes is Baron and Hershey's. The scope
  is held to decisions taken under genuine uncertainty and evaluated after the result is known, and is
  not stretched into a claim that inquiries should not be held, that decision-makers should not be held
  to account, that every adverse finding is an artefact, or that a decision criticised after a failure
  was therefore sound, since plenty are not and the mechanism staged here says nothing about which.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per element, one per load-bearing vector, no filler.

- **The Fog**: carries the genuine ex-ante uncertainty (Fischhoff), several endings live and the evidence
  pointing more than one way, so the chosen action is sound against the spread rather than against any
  single branch, and is indistinguishable at the time from a lucky one.
- **The Outcome**: carries the arrival of one branch (Fischhoff and Beyth), the thing that happened and
  from that moment cannot be un-known, the only element that changes between a defensible call and a
  condemned one.
- **The Straightening**: carries the mechanism (Fischhoff), outcome knowledge reorganising the record so
  the signals that pointed the way it went become salient and the rest recede, a pattern genuinely seen
  rather than manufactured, and not correctable by intending to correct it.
- **The Verdict**: carries the misjudgement in both directions (Baron and Hershey), a sound call recorded
  as a warning ignored and a reckless one that came off recorded as judgement, luck scored as skill and
  skill as luck.
- **The Lost Lesson**: carries the structural cost, the review convened to teach something producing a
  culprit instead, since a finding that someone missed an obvious sign yields the instruction to notice
  obvious signs, which is no lesson, the sign not having been obvious then or next time.
- **The Boundary**: carries the condition and the hedge (Fischhoff and Beyth), that the trap needs a
  genuinely uncertain decision judged after its result is known, and lifts under contemporaneous sealed
  records, priced in upkeep, in the honesty of entries written under the shadow of a possible inquiry,
  and in the fact that a log which can be added to can be furnished.
