# Position: Recorder

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). The seat that preserves the fog in writing before an outcome exists to dissolve it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The only instrument that survives the straightening: a contemporaneous, sealed record of expectations, confidences, and live alternatives.
Proof that recollection will not substitute, since recalled forecasts drift toward what happened.
No way to verify that any entry was written honestly rather than defensively.

## Orders

Log it before you know: record what is expected, with what confidence, and which alternatives are live, at the time, and seal it.
Do not accept a later reconstruction in its place, contemporaneity being the whole of what makes the record worth anything.
Accept what the seat does: it pays on every decision, for the few that are ever reviewed, and cannot certify its own material.

## Loses

Certainty about the thing it is preserving.
Because entries are composed by people who know they may be read at an inquiry, and because a log that can be added to can be furnished afterward, the seat loses any guarantee that what it holds is the fog rather than a defence of it.
It keeps the only artefact that outlives the outcome and loses the ability to vouch for it.

## Drives

Toward the one whose correction works and cannot warrant itself,
recording because recollection provably will not serve and, by doing so, buying an inquiry the state of knowledge it would otherwise reconstruct,
so the seat holding the play's one remedy is the seat that narrows the gap at a standing cost and never closes it.
