# Plot: And Then It Happened

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The passage from [The Moment](place_the_moment.md) to [The Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md), where one branch stops being a possibility.

## Action

[The Outcome](piece_the_outcome.md) arrives. It is not disputed, not ambiguous, and not anybody's opinion: it is simply what happened, and from this point it cannot be un-known by anyone who has heard it. The scene closes with the same record on the table, the same evidence, the same reasoning, and one new item of knowledge in the room (Fischhoff and Beyth).

## Stage

This is the only alteration in the entire play. The decision has not changed, the record has not changed, and the decider's reasoning has not changed, so whatever follows is carried by this single arrival.

## Tension

The one thing that separates a defensible call from a condemned one is a fact that nobody had when the call was made and nobody can put down now, which means the difference between the two verdicts was never in the decision at all.
