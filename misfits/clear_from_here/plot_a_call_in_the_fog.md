# Plot: A Call in the Fog

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Moment](place_the_moment.md), where a choice will not wait for certainty and several endings are still live.

## Action

Through [Deciding](process_deciding.md), [the Decider](persona_the_decider.md) on [Call It as It Stands](plan_call_it_as_it_stands.md) weighs [the Fog](piece_the_fog.md): evidence pointing more than one way, two hundred signals of which three happen to point at one ending and are not distinguishable from the rest, and an action chosen because it holds up across the spread rather than against any single branch. The scene closes with a defensible call and no way, yet, to tell it from a lucky one (Fischhoff).

## Stage

Nothing here is careless. Waiting for certainty is itself a decision and usually the worse one, and choosing against the range of live outcomes is what deciding under uncertainty means rather than a compromise with it.

## Tension

The fog is decisive and goes unrecorded, since uncertainty does not feel like a fact about the situation but like not having finished thinking, so the one condition that would later exonerate the seat leaves no trace at all.
