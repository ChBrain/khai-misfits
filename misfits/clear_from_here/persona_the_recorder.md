# Persona: The Recorder

## Taxonomy

[Recorder](position_recorder.md). The one who writes the fog down before the outcome exists, so an inquiry can be handed it rather than reconstruct it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Methodical and slightly tedious, insisting that what is expected be written down at the time, with confidences and alternatives named.
Keeps a sealed log against a day that for most decisions never arrives.
No cynicism on show: it is not defending anyone in advance, it is preserving a state of knowledge that will otherwise be irrecoverable.

## Action

Runs [Log It Before You Know](plan_log_it_before_you_know.md): through [Logging](process_logging.md) at [the Archive](place_the_archive.md), recording expectations, confidences, and live alternatives at the time, and holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) against [the Straightening](piece_the_straightening.md).
Cannot rely on anyone recalling this later, since Fischhoff and Beyth is exactly the demonstration that recollection drifts toward the outcome, so the writing must be contemporaneous or it is worthless.
Does the one thing that hands an evaluator the fog instead of a reconstruction, at a price paid on every decision that never gets reviewed.

## Shadow

Not bureaucratic for its own sake: the record is the only artefact in the play that survives the straightening, and without it every review reconstructs the past from a vantage that guarantees one road.
Because its entries are written by people who know they may one day be read at an inquiry, and because a log that can be added to can be furnished afterward, its remedy is only as sound as the honesty it cannot verify.
It is the play's one working correction and it cannot certify its own material.

## Tell

Asks for a confidence and a list of alternatives on a decision nobody expects to be questioned, which is most of them, and is experienced as overhead until once it is not.
Cannot distinguish a log written honestly from one written defensively, since both look like a log, so the instrument that fixes the reviewer's problem inherits a problem of its own.
