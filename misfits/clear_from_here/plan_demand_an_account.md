# Plan: Demand an Account

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the claimant's seat](position_claimant.md), inside [Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Claimant](position_claimant.md)

## Direction

Ask who knew what and when, and insist the question be answered. The harm is real and an explanation is owed, and a system that met every serious failure with a shrug would be no system at all, so do not withdraw the demand on being told the matter was uncertain. Do not accept pure misfortune as a closing answer. Accept, too, that a description of the fog is not an account and names nobody, so the honest reply available may be the one that sounds most like an evasion.

## Orders

([The Claimant](persona_the_claimant.md) presses through [Judging](process_judging.md) at [the Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md), producing [the Verdict](piece_the_verdict.md) and, beneath it, [the Lost Lesson](piece_the_lost_lesson.md).)

## Implementation

([The Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md) is where a legitimate demand meets a straightened record; [Judging](process_judging.md) converts the reviewer's honest reading into a formal finding, so the propriety of the demand is what gives the distortion its binding force.)

## Targets

- [ ] the question of who knew what and when is asked and pressed
- [ ] the demand is not withdrawn on being told the matter was uncertain
- [ ] pure misfortune is not accepted as a closing answer
- [ ] an account is obtained, whether or not it is the explanation the seat came for
