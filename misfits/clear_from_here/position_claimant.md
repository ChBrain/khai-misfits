# Position: Claimant

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). The seat harmed, whose entitlement to an account is genuine and is what makes the review happen at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real injury and a real entitlement to know how it came about.
The standing to insist, which no decent arrangement can withdraw without withdrawing accountability itself.
No access to the fog, and no reason to accept its absence as an answer.

## Orders

Demand an account: ask who knew what and when, which is the right question and the one anyone in this seat would ask.
Do not accept pure misfortune as a closing answer, since a system that met every serious failure with a shrug would be no system at all.
Accept what the seat does: it presses a legitimate demand into a process whose record has already been rearranged by the outcome.

## Loses

The explanation it actually came for.
Because the process answers with a culprit rather than with an account of a genuine gamble, the seat loses the understanding that would have made the harm intelligible, and receives instead a name, which satisfies the form of the demand and not its substance.
It keeps its standing and loses the truth it was exercising the standing to obtain.

## Drives

Toward the one whose rightful demand gives the distortion its force,
insisting on an account because an account is owed and, by doing so, converting a straightened reading into a formal verdict,
so the seat with the strongest claim in the play is the seat through which the error is made binding, entitled in the asking and ill-served by the answer.
