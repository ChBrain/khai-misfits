# Persona: The Reviewer

## Taxonomy

[Reviewer](position_reviewer.md). The one reading the record honestly from a vantage its author never occupied, and unable to occupy any other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Diligent and fair-minded, going through the record properly and asking the question an inquiry exists to ask.
Reaches conclusions the evidence supports and can point to the passage supporting each.
No malice on show: it has nothing against the decider, wants only to understand what happened, and would say so sincerely.

## Action

Runs [Ask If It Was Foreseeable](plan_ask_if_it_was_foreseeable.md): through [Straightening](process_straightening.md) at [the Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md), meeting [the Straightening](piece_the_straightening.md) as the record resolves into a line with the three telling signals standing out from the rest.
Cannot un-know the outcome, since that is not an act available to anyone, and warned of the bias and instructed to disregard the result it shows the effect regardless.
Does honest work: it sees a pattern that is genuinely present in the record, and that was genuinely not visible before the ending was known.

## Shadow

Not a scapegoater: it invents nothing, quotes accurately, and its reading is the reading the record now supports, so an accusation of bad faith would simply miss.
Because the outcome has already rebuilt what the record looks like, its diligence is what carries it wrong, and reading more carefully sharpens rather than corrects the line, so the ordinary remedy for a poor review makes this one worse.
It is the most conscientious actor in the play and the seat the distortion lives in.

## Tell

Assembles a clear narrative in which the failure was signposted, and finds it clear because from here it is.
Cannot test its own reading against the counterfactual, since the only reader who could supply the fog is the decider, whose memory has drifted the same way for the same reason.
