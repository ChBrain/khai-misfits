# Piece: The Straightening

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). Creeping determinism: the record reorganising around the outcome so the signals that pointed its way become salient and the rest recede.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md), before the reading rather than during it, so the reader meets an already-rearranged record and takes it for the record (Fischhoff).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the trap is ordinary bias, correctable by the ordinary means: a reviewer who was tilting the evidence could be told to stop, audited, or replaced, and the problem would yield to care.
It bears the load as the reason care fails. Because the pattern is genuinely present once the ending is known, the reviewer is seeing rather than inventing, so reading more closely sharpens the line instead of dissolving it, and being warned about the effect and resolving to discount the outcome leaves it in place.

## Apparent

Clarity, and it feels exactly like clarity: a confusing episode resolving into a comprehensible sequence, which is what good analysis is supposed to feel like.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [It Was Always Going to Happen](plot_it_was_always_going_to_happen.md), the mechanism doing its work unobserved.
It works together with [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the process and the one artefact that survives it.
