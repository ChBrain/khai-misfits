# Plan: Log It Before You Know

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the recorder's seat](position_recorder.md), inside [Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Recorder](position_recorder.md)

## Direction

Write down what is expected, with what confidence, and which alternatives are live, at the time, and seal it. Do not rely on anyone recalling it later: recalled forecasts drift toward what happened, and the people whose memories drift include the decider and yourself, so contemporaneity is the whole of what makes the record worth anything. Pay the cost knowingly, since most decisions logged will never be reviewed. And state the limit rather than hiding it: an entry written by someone who knows it may be read at an inquiry is not verifiable, and a log that can be added to can be furnished.

## Orders

([The Recorder](persona_the_recorder.md) writes through [Logging](process_logging.md) at [the Archive](place_the_archive.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) against [the Straightening](piece_the_straightening.md).)

## Implementation

([The Archive](place_the_archive.md) is where the fog is preserved in a form that survives the outcome; [Logging](process_logging.md) records expectations and live alternatives before any branch arrives, so [the Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md) can be handed a state of knowledge rather than reconstruct one from a vantage that guarantees a single road.)

## Targets

- [ ] expectations, confidences, and live alternatives are recorded at the time and sealed
- [ ] no later reconstruction is accepted in place of a contemporaneous entry
- [ ] the upkeep is paid across decisions that will never be reviewed
- [ ] the limit is stated: entries cannot be verified, and a log that can be added to can be furnished
