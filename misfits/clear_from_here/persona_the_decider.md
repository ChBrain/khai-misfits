# Persona: The Decider

## Taxonomy

[Decider](position_decider.md). The one who called it in the fog, soundly, and is judged against a map drawn afterward.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent and unremarkable, doing the ordinary work of choosing under uncertainty with several endings live.
Weighs the evidence, which points more than one way, and picks the action that holds up across the spread rather than against any single branch.
No recklessness on show: the call is defensible on the information available and would have been praised had the other branch arrived.

## Action

Runs [Call It as It Stands](plan_call_it_as_it_stands.md): through [Deciding](process_deciding.md) at [the Moment](place_the_moment.md), weighing [the Fog](piece_the_fog.md) and choosing against the spread of what might happen.
Cannot mark which of the two hundred signals will later matter, since that is knowable only from the far side, and the three that will be quoted back are not distinguishable now from the rest.
Does what deciding under uncertainty is: it takes a defensible position that a different roll would have vindicated.

## Shadow

Not negligent: the call was sound on what was available, and an evaluator with the same information and no outcome would have endorsed it, which is the whole of what can be asked.
Because its own recollection drifts toward what happened, it cannot reliably defend itself either, and may come to half-believe it had seen the warning, so the one witness to the fog is a witness whose memory has also been rearranged.
It is the party wronged by the process and the party least able to establish it.

## Tell

Says it looked different at the time, which is true, and which is the least persuasive sentence available to anyone in its position.
Cannot produce the fog on demand, since nothing about it was written down, having seemed at the time like the ordinary condition of working rather than anything worth recording.
