# Piece: The Outcome

## Taxonomy

[Clear from Here](play_clear_from_here.md). The branch that became actual, and from that moment cannot be un-known by anyone who has heard it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Arriving between [The Moment](place_the_moment.md) and [The Inquiry](place_the_inquiry.md), and present in the room before any reading of the record begins (Fischhoff and Beyth).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the record reads as it read: the same pages, evaluated by someone who does not know the ending, produce a judgement close to the decider's own, which is exactly what the source experiments demonstrate by withholding it from a control group.
It bears the load as the only thing that changes. Nothing about the decision, the record, the evidence, or the decider's reasoning is different between the defensible call and the condemned one, so the entire effect is carried by a single item of knowledge that arrives afterward and cannot be put back.

## Apparent

A fact, simply: what happened, which is not in dispute and is the least controversial element in the room.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [And Then It Happened](plot_and_then_it_happened.md), the one alteration in the whole play.
It works together with [The Fog](piece_the_fog.md), the arrival that dissolves it.
