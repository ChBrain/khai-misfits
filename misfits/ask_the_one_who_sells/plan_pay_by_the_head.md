# Plan: Pay by the Head

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reformer's seat](position_reformer.md), inside [Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reformer](position_reformer.md)

## Direction

Replace the fee with a salary or a sum per patient enrolled, so that more care no longer means more pay and the reason to do too much is cut. Accept that the same stroke gives a reason to do too little, to stint and to turn the difficult patient away, and know that you are trading the risk of over-treatment for the risk of under-treatment, not ending it.

## Orders

([The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) through [Reforming](process_reforming.md) at [the Counting House](place_the_counting_house.md) replaces [the Fee](piece_the_fee.md) with [the Salary](piece_the_salary.md), a fixed sum by post or by head so that more care no longer pays.)

## Implementation

([Reforming](process_reforming.md) at [the Counting House](place_the_counting_house.md) swaps [the Fee](piece_the_fee.md) for [the Salary](piece_the_salary.md), so the pull to over-treat is removed and the pull to stint is planted, the error moved rather than ended.)

## Targets

- [ ] the fee is replaced with a salary or a sum per head, so more care no longer pays
- [ ] the pull to over-treat is understood to be removed by the change
- [ ] the pull to do too little, to stint and turn patients away, is seen to be planted in its place
- [ ] the remedy is owned as trading one risk for another, no payment wanting exactly the right amount
