# Plot: The Bed Fills

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ward](place_the_ward.md), where the recommendation written at the Clinic becomes a bed occupied, a
procedure performed, a service rendered and logged.

## Action

The recommendation acted on: [the Provider](persona_the_provider.md), from
[the provider's seat](position_provider.md), through [Filling](process_filling.md) renders
[the Extra](piece_the_extra.md), the bed that was built and so is filled, while
[the Payer](persona_the_payer.md), from [the payer's seat](position_payer.md), on
[Pay for What's Done](plan_pay_for_whats_done.md), foots the bill.

## Stage

A built bed is a filled bed; supply drives utilisation (Roemer, 1961; Evans, 1974). This is not
Rent-Seeking: real service is rendered here, the test really run, the bed really occupied, only more of it
than an informed buyer would have chosen. The harm is over-supply, not unproductive capture; something is
done for the payment, only more than was needed.

## Tension

Real care is delivered and really billed, and the only thing wrong with it is that less would have done.
