# Position: Payer

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The seat of the one who foots the bill, the insurer or public purse that pays the fee for each service rendered and so bears the money cost of the over-supply.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A duty to pay for care delivered: it honours the invoice for each service really done.
A ledger it keeps honestly, settling real claims for real work, as any payer should.
No better sight than the patient's, because it cannot tell the needed service from the induced any more than the buyer can, so it pays for both alike.

## Orders

Pay for what's done.
Honour the fee for each service rendered, since the work was really performed and a payer pays for real care.
Know that in paying every honest invoice you fund the induction you cannot see, that your spending climbs without a matching gain in health, and that the fault is not in paying but in a fee that rewards the volume you cannot referee.

## Loses

The difference between the needed and the induced, spent and unrecoverable.
Because it settles each honest claim and cannot pick out the service that need not have been done, the seat bankrolls the over-supply by doing nothing but pay for what was truly rendered, so its diligence is exactly what funds the tilt.
It keeps its honest ledger and loses the surplus, the one who pays for the extra it cannot see.

## Drives

Toward the one that funds the induction by paying honestly for real work,
settling every true invoice and unable to pick out the extra, no fault in the paying,
so the seat that pays for care delivered is the seat that bankrolls the care that need not have been, the cost of a fee that rewards the volume.
