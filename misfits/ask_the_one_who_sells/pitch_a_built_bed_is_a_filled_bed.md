# Pitch: A Built Bed Is a Filled Bed

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that where the one who says what you need is the one paid to provide it, the supply sets the demand.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reasonable, careful, clinical. An expert consulting in good faith and recommending the thorough course, a patient trusting the one who knows, a fee paid for real work done. Nothing here is a scam; the care is real and the doctor means well.

## Undertow

The provider is at once your advisor and the supplier paid per service, and you cannot judge your own need, so you defer (Arrow). Paid for what is done and believing more is safer, the provider leans to more, and the demand for care is set in part by the supplier who meets it, not by the patient who bears it (Evans). Build the bed and it fills (Roemer).

## Nerve

That the extra care is both good practice and good business, and no one, least of all the doctor, can say which moved the hand. The sincerity is not the alibi; it is the engine, since a provider who believes more is better induces demand as surely as one who does not, and more comfortably.

## Echo

Ask the one who sells whether you need it, and a built bed is a filled bed.
