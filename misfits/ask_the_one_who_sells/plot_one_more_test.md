# Plot: One More Test

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), the chart already read, the diagnosis already reasonable, and one more
order left to write or leave unwritten.

## Action

[The Provider](persona_the_provider.md) runs [Better Safe](plan_better_safe.md) through
[Recommending](process_recommending.md), taking [the Benefit of the Doubt](piece_the_benefit_of_the_doubt.md)
and, paid by [the Fee](piece_the_fee.md), recommending the extra test as the safer, more thorough course.

## Stage

A provider paid per service leans to more, and sincerely, since the extra test feels safer and more
thorough whether or not it is needed (Evans, 1974; McGuire, 2000). This is not a greedy doctor bilking a
patient; the sincerity is not an excuse offered after the fact, it is the mechanism itself, the thing that
makes the lean toward more reliable and unremarkable.

## Tension

The extra test is both good practice and good business, and nothing marks which reason moved the hand.
