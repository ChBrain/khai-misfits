# Plan: Pay for What's Done

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the payer's seat](position_payer.md), inside [Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md).

## Owner

## Direction

Honour the fee for each service rendered, since the work was really performed and a payer pays for real care. Know that in paying every honest invoice you fund the induction you cannot see, that your spending climbs without a matching gain in health, and that the fault is not in paying but in a fee that rewards the volume you cannot referee.

([The Payer](persona_the_payer.md) honours [the Fee](piece_the_fee.md) at [the Counting House](place_the_counting_house.md) for each service, and at [the Ward](place_the_ward.md) foots the bill for [the Extra](piece_the_extra.md) that [Filling](process_filling.md) rendered, paying for care that was really given.)
