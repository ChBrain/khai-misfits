# Persona: The Patient

## Taxonomy

[Patient](position_patient.md), inside [Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The archetype of the buyer who cannot judge: they came because they do not know what they need, so they take the expert's word, and they bear the bodily risk of the care they did not need.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A sensible person following doctor's orders, doing the responsible thing. It looks like nothing but good sense, trusting the qualified expert and accepting the care advised, as anyone should.

## Action

Defer, and accept. Meeting [the Information Gap](piece_the_information_gap.md) at [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), the Patient through [Deferring](process_deferring.md) takes the provider's word, on [Take Their Word](plan_take_their_word.md), unable to referee the recommendation because judging it is the very expertise they lack (Arrow). They accept the extra as needed care.

## Shadow

Its sound trust is what lets the demand be set for it. Because deferring to an expert it cannot second-guess is rational, the Patient cannot tell the needed care from the induced, so it accepts both, and the sound reliance on expertise that medicine is built on is exactly what lets the one who sells decide the amount (Evans). It keeps its good sense and loses the informed refusal, the truster who cannot know what to refuse.

## Tell

It does as the expert advises, and cannot tell what it needed from what it got.
