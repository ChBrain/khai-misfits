# Plot: No Fee for More

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Counting House](place_the_counting_house.md), where the payment itself, not just its amount, is up for
change.

## Action

[The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md), from [the reformer's seat](position_reformer.md), runs
[Pay by the Head](plan_pay_by_the_head.md) through [Reforming](process_reforming.md), replacing
[the Fee](piece_the_fee.md) with [the Salary](piece_the_salary.md), so that more care recommended no longer
means more paid.

## Stage

Paying by salary or by head removes the pull to do too much, since the return to recommending an extra
service disappears when the payment no longer follows the service (McGuire, 2000). This is not Moral
Hazard: the lever moved here is the supply-side payment to the provider, not the price the insured party
faces for their own care.

## Tension

Cut the fee for more and you cut the reason to do too much, and plant the reason to do too little.
