# Piece: The Information Gap

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The reason the buyer
cannot referee the recommendation: the whole reason they came is that they do
not know what they need.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), between what the provider knows and what
the buyer can check, carried by [Deferring](process_deferring.md).

## Load Bearing

The buyer seeks out the provider precisely because the provider knows
something the buyer does not; that asymmetry is the founding condition of the
whole relationship (Arrow, 1963). Because the buyer cannot independently judge
whether the recommended test, procedure, or visit is warranted, they defer to
the one who can, who is also the one who is paid for the answer. Guard against
reading the patient's deference as gullibility: deferring to an expert you
cannot second-guess is rational, and it is exactly the sound reliance on
expertise that lets the expert who is also the seller set the demand.

## Apparent

It looks like simple trust in a professional's judgement, no different from
trusting a mechanic to name the broken part. The gap looks like ordinary
expertise, not like an opening.
