# Position: Patient

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The seat of the buyer who cannot judge, who came for an expertise they lack and so takes the expert's word, bearing the bodily risk of care they did not need.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A need it cannot assess: the very reason it came is that it does not know what is wrong or what to do.
A sound reason to defer: the expert knows what it does not, so trusting the qualified advisor is the rational thing and the whole point of the visit.
No way to referee the advice, because judging whether the extra test is needed is the expertise it lacks, so it cannot tell the needed care from the induced.

## Orders

Take their word.
Do as the expert advises, since you came because you could not judge for yourself and trusting the one who knows is sound, not gullible.
Know that you cannot tell the care you needed from the care you were sold, that your sound trust is exactly what lets the one who sells set the amount, and that the informed refusal is the one thing your want of expertise denies you.

## Loses

The informed refusal, and it loses it for the very want of knowledge that brought it.
Because it cannot second-guess the recommendation, the seat accepts the needed care and the induced care alike, and bears the money cost and the bodily risk of treatment it did not need, undone not by any foolishness but by the plain gap in what it can know.
It keeps its good sense and loses the power to refuse, the truster who cannot know what to refuse.

## Drives

Toward the one who trusts soundly and is sold what it cannot weigh,
deferring to the expert it came to borrow, no gullibility in it,
so the seat that rightly relies on expertise is the seat that lets the one who sells decide, the informed refusal denied it by the gap it came to fill.
