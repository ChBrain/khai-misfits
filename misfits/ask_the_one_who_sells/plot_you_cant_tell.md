# Plot: You Can't Tell

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), where the case is read and a course of action is named.
The patient sits across the desk with a complaint and no way of their own to weigh it.

## Action

[The Patient](persona_the_patient.md), from [the patient's seat](position_patient.md), runs
[Take Their Word](plan_take_their_word.md) through [Deferring](process_deferring.md), meeting
[the Information Gap](piece_the_information_gap.md): unable to judge the case themselves, the Patient
takes the expert's word as the whole of the judgement.

## Stage

The patient came to the Clinic precisely because they cannot judge their own need, so they rely on the
provider's word for what that need is (Arrow, 1963). Deferring to an expert you cannot second-guess is
rational, not gullible; it is the ordinary, sound reliance on expertise that any buyer of a service they
cannot assess would extend.

## Tension

The sound reliance on expertise is exactly what lets the one who sells set the demand.
