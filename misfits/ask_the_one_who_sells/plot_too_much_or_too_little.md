# Plot: Too Much or Too Little

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Counting House](place_the_counting_house.md), the fee weighed against the salary, the ledger of one
error set beside the ledger of the other.

## Action

[The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md), from [the reformer's seat](position_reformer.md), draws the bound:
[the Salary](piece_the_salary.md), set against [the Fee](piece_the_fee.md) it was meant to answer, removes
the pull to over-treat but adds the pull to stint, so [Reforming](process_reforming.md) moves the error
rather than ending it.

## Stage

The hedge, and it is load-bearing: supplier-induced demand is real and documented, but its magnitude is
debated; not all care recommended is induced, and much of it is genuinely needed; and every payment scheme
trades one error for another, over-treatment under the fee, under-treatment under the salary (McGuire,
2000). Neither the Provider recommending under the Fee nor the Reformer paying under the Salary need be
acting in anything but good faith; the error moves with the payment, not with the person.

## Tension

There is no way to pay that wants exactly the right amount of care, only ways that err high or err low.
