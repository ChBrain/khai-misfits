# Piece: The Salary

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The fixed payment by
head or by post that removes the fee incentive to do too much, costly because
it creates the opposite incentive to do too little.

## Owner

[Reforming](process_reforming.md).

## Load Bearing

Pay the provider a fixed amount per patient or per post rather than per
service and the return to recommending more disappears; but the return to
recommending less, to seeing fewer patients or referring them elsewhere,
appears in its place (McGuire, 2000). The remedy does not remove the
mismatch between agent and supplier, it inverts which error it produces. The
bound carried here: the over-supply under fee-for-service is real and
documented, but its magnitude is debated, not all induced care is waste, and
the provider usually believes the extra warranted, so capitation is a trade
of too much care for too little, not a clean cure. Guard against reading the
salary as a clean fix: it trades the pull to over-treat for the pull to stint
and turn patients away, so it moves the risk rather than removing it.

## Apparent

It looks like the obvious solution: remove the fee and the incentive to
over-treat should vanish along with it, care returned to whatever is
actually needed.
