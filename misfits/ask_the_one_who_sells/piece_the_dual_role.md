# Piece: The Dual Role

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The one person holding
two jobs at once: the trusted advisor who is meant to recommend what the buyer
needs, and the supplier paid per service, who profits from recommending more.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), where advice and sale happen in the same
breath, carried by [Recommending](process_recommending.md).

## Load Bearing

The provider is at once the buyer's agent, asked to judge and prescribe on the
buyer's behalf, and the seller of the very service being judged, paid for each
unit supplied. The two roles do not sit in different offices; they sit in one
person, in one recommendation (Evans, 1974). The agency relationship makes the
recommendation trustworthy in the first place; the fee then bends what a
trustworthy agent has reason to recommend (McGuire, 2000). Guard against
reading the two roles as a deliberate deceit: no one need lie, the conflict is
structural, one person simply holding both the advice and the sale, and it
bites even when the provider means only well.

## Apparent

On the surface it looks like ordinary professional trust: an expert examines
you and tells you what to do, the same as any advisor would. The seam between
advising and selling is invisible from the buyer's side, since both come out
of the same mouth in the same visit.
