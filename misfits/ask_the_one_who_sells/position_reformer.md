# Position: Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The seat that would change how care is paid for, replacing the fee with a salary or a sum per head so more care no longer pays, the costly remedy that only moves the error.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one lever the structure offers: to break the link between the recommendation and the reward, by paying not per service but by post or by head.
A clean-looking design: pay a salary or a fixed sum per patient, and doing more no longer earns more, so the pull to over-treat is gone.
No payment that wants the right amount, because a flat sum rewards doing less as surely as the fee rewarded doing more, so removing one pull plants its opposite.

## Orders

Pay by the head.
Replace the fee with a salary or a sum per patient enrolled, so that more care no longer means more pay and the reason to do too much is cut.
Accept that the same stroke gives a reason to do too little, to stint and to turn the difficult patient away, and know that you are trading the risk of over-treatment for the risk of under-treatment, not ending it.

## Loses

The clean fix it seemed to hold, spent on a fault that only moves.
Because a provider paid a flat sum profits by doing less, the seat that removes the pull to over-treat plants the pull to stint, so its cure is a fresh temptation and no way of paying it can devise wants exactly the right amount of care.
It keeps the one real remedy and loses its cleanness, the fixer who moves the error rather than ending it.

## Drives

Toward the one whose cure is a fresh temptation,
cutting the reward for more and planting the reward for less, no payment that wants the right amount,
so the seat that holds the only lever is the seat that trades too much care for too little, the error moved and not undone.
