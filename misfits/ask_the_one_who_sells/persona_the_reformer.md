# Persona: The Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Reformer](position_reformer.md), inside [Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The archetype of the one who would change how care is paid for: replacing the fee with a salary or a sum per head so that more care no longer pays, the costly remedy.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A clear-eyed fixer with the obvious answer: stop paying by the service and the incentive to do too much is gone. It looks like a clean solution the system need only adopt.

## Action

Pay by the head. At [the Counting House](place_the_counting_house.md) the Reformer through [Reforming](process_reforming.md) replaces [the Fee](piece_the_fee.md) with [the Salary](piece_the_salary.md), on [Pay by the Head](plan_pay_by_the_head.md), a fixed sum by post or by patient so more care no longer means more pay (McGuire). It cuts the link between the recommendation and the reward.

## Shadow

Its clean fix plants the opposite fault. Because a provider paid a flat sum now profits by doing less, the same stroke that removes the pull to over-treat plants the pull to stint and turn the difficult patient away, so the Reformer trades the risk of too much care for the risk of too little and cannot pay in a way that wants exactly the right amount (McGuire). It keeps the one real remedy and moves the error rather than ending it, the fixer whose cure is a fresh temptation.

## Tell

It stops the reward for doing more, and starts the reward for doing less.
