# Piece: The Fee

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The payment for each
service rendered, which makes recommending more profitable, and which, when
cut, moves the provider to raise the volume to hold their income.

## Load Bearing

Payment attached to each unit of service supplied ties the provider's income
to the volume recommended, so the same dual role that lets the provider judge
need also lets that judgement move with what pays (Evans, 1974). When the fee
per service is cut, a provider aiming to hold a target income has reason to
raise the number of services rather than accept the lower income, the
target-income response (McGuire, 2000). Guard against reading the fee as a
bribe: it is the ordinary way of paying for work done, honest pay for real
service, and the trouble is only that it rewards volume, so more work means
more pay whether or not more was needed.

## Apparent

It looks like a plain, legitimate bill for services performed, the same as
paying by the hour or by the job in any other trade. Nothing about a fee
schedule looks like an incentive to do more than is needed.
