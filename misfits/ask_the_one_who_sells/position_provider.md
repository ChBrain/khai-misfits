# Position: Provider

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The seat of the expert who both advises and sells, at once the buyer's trusted advisor and the supplier paid per service, so it recommends the care it profits from and usually believes in it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Two roles in one person: the advisor trusted to say what the buyer needs, and the supplier paid for each service done.
A sincere lean toward more: more testing feels safer and more thorough, so the careful course and the profitable course are, at the margin, the same course.
No buyer who can check its word, because the buyer came precisely for the expertise it holds, so its recommendation stands unrefereed.

## Orders

Be safe, and be paid.
Recommend the thorough course, the extra test that rules out the rare disaster, since more is safer and you would rather be sure, and you are paid for the doing besides.
Know that you cannot yourself tell the diligence from the sale, that your sincere belief that more is better is what makes the induced care feel like good practice, and that this is the engine of the tilt and not its excuse.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and its good faith is exactly what carries the harm.
Because it is paid per service and truly believes the extra warranted, the seat recommends more with a clear conscience, so the induced care wears the face of diligence and the demand is set by the one who profits, no fraud and no felt conflict.
It keeps its good name and its fee and loses no cause for blame, the careful expert whose care is its trade.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sincere care is also its sale,
recommending the safer, fuller course and paid for it, no cheat and no felt conflict,
so the seat you trust to say what you need is the seat that profits from your needing more, the demand set by the one who sells.
