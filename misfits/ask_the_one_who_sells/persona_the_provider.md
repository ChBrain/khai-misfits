# Persona: The Provider

## Taxonomy

[Provider](position_provider.md), inside [Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The archetype of the expert who both advises and sells: at once the buyer's trusted advisor and the supplier paid per service, recommending more and sincerely believing it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A careful, conscientious professional, thorough and safe. It looks like the diligent expert who leaves nothing unchecked, ordering the extra test to be sure, the very picture of good and cautious practice.

## Action

Advise, and render. Wearing [the Dual Role](piece_the_dual_role.md) at [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md), the Provider through [Recommending](process_recommending.md) takes [the Benefit of the Doubt](piece_the_benefit_of_the_doubt.md) and, paid by [the Fee](piece_the_fee.md), recommends the extra as the safer course, on [Better Safe](plan_better_safe.md), then through [Filling](process_filling.md) renders [the Extra](piece_the_extra.md) at [the Ward](place_the_ward.md) (Evans; Roemer). It believes the care it recommends.

## Shadow

Its sincerity is the engine, not the excuse. Because it is paid per service and truly holds that more is safer, the Provider recommends the extra with a clear conscience, so the induced care looks like diligence and not selling, and a provider who believes more is better induces demand as surely as one who does not, and more reliably (McGuire). It keeps its good faith and owes no cause for blame, the careful expert whose care is also its trade.

## Tell

It orders the extra test to be safe, and is paid for the ordering.
