# Piece: The Extra

## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The care an informed

was built and so is filled.

## Load Bearing

Where the provider's dual role and the fee push toward more, and the buyer
cannot judge otherwise, the volume of care supplied rises to meet supply
rather than settling at what an informed buyer would have chosen; a built bed
tends to be a filled bed (Roemer, 1961; Evans, 1974). Small-area studies find
utilisation varying by supply and habit more than by underlying need
(Wennberg, cited in McGuire, 2000), though how much of that variation is
induced rather than merely discretionary is debated. Guard against reading the
extra as fraud or as pure waste: the service is really rendered and often does
some small good, so it is not nothing done for something, only more than an
informed buyer would have chosen, over-supply and not a con.

## Apparent

It looks like thoroughness, an extra measure of care taken to be safe, the
kind of diligence a buyer would normally want more of, not less.
