# Persona: The Payer

## Taxonomy

[Payer](position_payer.md), inside [Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). The archetype of the one who foots the bill: the insurer or the public purse that pays the fee for each service rendered, and so bears the money cost of the over-supply.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A diligent bookkeeper, paying honestly for care delivered. It looks like the responsible party settling real invoices for real services, doing exactly what a payer should, honouring the claim.

## Action

Pay for what's done. At [the Counting House](place_the_counting_house.md) the Payer honours [the Fee](piece_the_fee.md) for each service, on [Pay for What's Done](plan_pay_for_whats_done.md), and at [the Ward](place_the_ward.md) foots the bill for [the Extra](piece_the_extra.md) that [Filling](process_filling.md) rendered (Roemer). It pays for care that was really given.

## Shadow

Paying for real work, it funds the induction it cannot see. Because it settles each honest invoice and cannot tell the needed service from the induced any better than the patient can, the Payer bankrolls the over-supply by doing nothing but pay for what was truly done, its spending climbing without a matching gain in health (Evans; McGuire). It keeps its honest ledger and loses the difference, the one who pays for the extra it cannot pick out.

## Tell

It pays every invoice, and cannot see which service need not have been done.
