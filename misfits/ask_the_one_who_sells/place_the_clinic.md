## Taxonomy

[Ask the One Who Sells](play_ask_the_one_who_sells.md). Where [the Provider](persona_the_provider.md) advises and recommends, the two hats worn at once. Hosts [The Two Hats](plot_the_two_hats.md), [You Can't Tell](plot_you_cant_tell.md), [One More Test](plot_one_more_test.md).

## Owner

## Shown

The expert consulted: the history taken, the chart read, the recommendation given, and the fee attached to whatever is recommended.

## Holds

[The Dual Role](piece_the_dual_role.md) that the consultation is built on, [the Information Gap](piece_the_information_gap.md) that leaves the patient unable to check it, and [the Benefit of the Doubt](piece_the_benefit_of_the_doubt.md) that carries the recommendation past a fraud it was never meant to be.

## Offers

To the patient, expert judgement they cannot supply themselves: a reading of the case, and a course of action to follow.

## Withheld

Any way for the patient to tell the care they need from the care that is merely extra.
