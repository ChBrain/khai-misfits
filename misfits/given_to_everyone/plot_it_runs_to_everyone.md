# Plot: It Runs to Everyone

## Taxonomy

[Given to Everyone](play_given_to_everyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The licence file, read for who it addresses.

## Action

[The Definition](persona_the_definition.md) is consulted, and [the two clauses](piece_the_two_clauses.md) refuse discrimination against persons, against groups and against fields of endeavour.

## Stage

The grant names no addressee, and is forbidden from naming one.

## Tension

This half is constitutive, which is what makes it unfixable.
A licence carving out the party best placed to use the grant against its author would not be an open source licence, and would not have bought the adoption that made opening worth doing: the property that makes the release work and the property that makes it unaimable are the same property. So the rule is not failing to anticipate the case. It is refusing the exception it exists to refuse, and a commons that could exclude would not be a commons.
