# Persona: The Operator

## Taxonomy

[Operator](position_operator.md). The party the grant reaches furthest, breaching nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Scale the author cannot match, a service business, and a legal reading nobody disputes.
Real contributions, in some measure, and no obligation to make them.

## Action

Runs it.
Pays nothing, because nothing is owed.

## Shadow

Is doing what the licence contemplates, which is why nothing can be alleged.
The play refuses to cast this as predation: the terms were written and meant by the other party, the use is the use the definition protects, and an account that made this a wrong would be an account in which the commons was never a commons.

## Tell

Owes nothing, breaks nothing, and is the whole of the difficulty.
