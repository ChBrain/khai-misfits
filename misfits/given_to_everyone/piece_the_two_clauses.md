## Load Bearing

They are the reason the grant cannot be aimed, and they are not a gap anybody could close.
Discrimination against persons or groups and against fields of endeavour is refused outright, so a licence that carved out the party best placed to use the grant would not be an open source licence and would not have bought the adoption that made opening worth doing: the property that makes the release work and the property that makes it unaimable are one property.
