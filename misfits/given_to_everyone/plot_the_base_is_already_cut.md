# Plot: The Base Is Already Cut

## Taxonomy

[Given to Everyone](play_given_to_everyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The announcement, and a community reading it.

## Action

[Taking the base](process_taking_the_base.md): [the contributors](persona_the_contributors.md) go to [the last permissive commit](place_the_last_permissive_commit.md) and find everything the project was, on terms that cannot be revoked.

## Stage

The fork carries the users. The correction supplied both the grievance and the codebase.

## Tension

A grievance with nothing to leave with is a complaint, and this one came with the means attached.
[The Fork Base](piece_the_fork_base.md) is created at the moment of the correction and not before: nobody prepared it, nobody could have pointed to it in advance, and moving the terms at any date would have left one somewhere else. So the company did not fail to prevent the fork, it manufactured it, in the same act and on the same day, and the maintenance then lands on a foundation funded in large part by the operators the change was aimed at.
