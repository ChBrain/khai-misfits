# Play: Given to Everyone

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Given to Everyone.
A company opens its code, because open is the cheapest distribution, recruitment and credibility anybody can buy, and it works: the project becomes the default, the contributors arrive, the funding follows. Years later a provider runs the same software as a service at a scale the company cannot match, and pays it nothing, which the licence expressly permits. The company changes the terms on future versions, which is the only lever it has. The community forks from the last commit that was still open, and takes the users with it.

## Arc

The bend is this: the grant is unconditional in two directions, and the second one is not a mistake anybody made.
It runs on a release that is right on its own terms.
[The Company](persona_the_company.md) opens the code to be chosen, and is chosen. Nothing cheaper reaches that many developers, and the evidence that it works is the adoption itself, so [opening it](process_opening_it.md) is a good decision that a good decision-maker takes again.
And the grant has no expiry.
[The Perpetual Term](piece_the_perpetual_term.md) runs forever on what was released, so the position is built on an asset given away in perpetuity by the same act that built it. That much is well understood by everybody signing it, and it is only half of what is unconditional.
Then [It Runs to Everyone](plot_it_runs_to_everyone.md), and this half is constitutive.
[The Definition](persona_the_definition.md) forbids discrimination against persons, against groups and against fields of endeavour, so a licence that excluded the one party best placed to use the grant against its author would not be an open source licence. [The Two Clauses](piece_the_two_clauses.md) are not an oversight and are not patchable: a commons that could exclude would not be a commons, and the grant cannot be aimed for the same reason it cannot be withdrawn.
So [The Operator](persona_the_operator.md) runs it at scale, contributes in some measure, breaks nothing and owes nothing. Every step is permitted, and the play refuses to call it predation, because the load-bearing fact is not what anybody did.
And the correction arms the answer to it. [Changing the terms](process_changing_the_terms.md) reaches only future versions, which means it announces a grievance and leaves a complete, legally clean codebase standing at [the last permissive commit](place_the_last_permissive_commit.md), so it supplies the community both the reason to leave and the thing to leave with.
And what is left to invest in is not ownable either. Code given away puts the weight on the team, and a team is the second asset nobody holds title to, in exactly the market that made opening correct.

**[It Runs to Everyone](plot_it_runs_to_everyone.md)**

**[Nobody Broke Anything](plot_nobody_broke_anything.md)**

Cue: the announcement, and a community reading it. Closes when the correction has been shown to supply both the grievance that coordinates the fork and the clean codebase that arms it, standing at the last commit that was still open.

**[And the Team Is Not Yours Either](plot_and_the_team_is_not_yours_either.md)**
Cue: the code gone, and the question of what is left to invest in. Closes when the weight has moved onto the team and the team has been found to be the second asset nobody holds title to, most mobile in exactly the market where opening was correct.

## Stakes

Whether anything can be kept by a party whose position was built on giving it away.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a release that is right on its own terms, a grant with no expiry and no addressee, a use that breaches nothing, a correction that stops being the thing it corrects, a fork armed by that correction, and a remaining asset that walks, and away from any account in which somebody cheated. The production cannot allege a breach, because there is none, and it cannot ask the definition to admit an exception, because the exception is what it exists to refuse. What is left to fight over is not whether the company lost something real, everybody agrees it did, but what to call a loss delivered entirely by terms it wrote itself and meant.
