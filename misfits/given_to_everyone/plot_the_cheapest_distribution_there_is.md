# Plot: The Cheapest Distribution There Is

## Taxonomy

[Given to Everyone](play_given_to_everyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A company with a good technical core and no way to reach the developers who would use it.

## Action

[Opening it](process_opening_it.md): [the company](persona_the_company.md), holding [Company](position_company.md), commits a licence file and releases the core.

## Stage

It is chosen. The contributors arrive, the project becomes the default, and the funding follows the adoption.

## Tension

The strategy is correct and the correctness is the mechanism.
Nothing else reaches that many developers at that price, and the reason it reaches them is precisely that anybody may take it, so there is no arrangement in which the code is both the distribution and the moat. The company is not deceived and is not short-sighted: it trades exclusivity for adoption with its eyes open, on the day, and would be wrong to trade the other way. What it has built is a position resting on the one asset it has agreed never to hold.
