# Process: Taking the Base

## Taxonomy

[Given to Everyone](play_given_to_everyone.md). The departure, cheap because the grant was honest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An announcement, and a complete codebase granted in perpetuity.
[The Contributors](persona_the_contributors.md), holding [Contributors](position_contributors.md), at [the last permissive commit](place_the_last_permissive_commit.md).

## Direction

Toward keeping the guarantee they built on, which is what they were loyal to.
Toward a foundation, a new name, and the same software on the old terms.

## Lever

The irrevocability, which made the project safe to depend on and makes leaving it free.

## Echo

The users carried across, the maintenance landed on a foundation funded largely by the operators, and the company still holding the problem it changed the terms to solve.
