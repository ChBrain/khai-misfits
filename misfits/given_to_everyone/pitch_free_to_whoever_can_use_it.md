# Pitch: Free to Whoever Can Use It

## Taxonomy

[Given to Everyone](play_given_to_everyone.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Plain and technical. A repository, a licence file, adoption numbers, a board deciding terms.
Competence and good faith throughout, on every side, at every step.

## Undertow

Under the release, a grant with no expiry and no addressee, the second of those required rather than overlooked.
So the gift lands hardest wherever it can be used hardest, and no term in it can say otherwise.

## Nerve

The particular flatness of having nothing to allege.

## Echo

Afterward: a fork carrying the users, a foundation carrying the maintenance, and a company still holding the problem it changed the terms to solve.
