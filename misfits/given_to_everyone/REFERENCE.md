# Given to Everyone: Reference

The research warrant for Given to Everyone.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the appropriability problem under an open grant and on the
non-discrimination that makes the grant unaimable, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the open grant as **a licence that is unconditional in two directions, one of them by construction**:
opening the code is the cheapest distribution, recruitment and credibility a company can buy, and it works,
which is why the decision is taken again by anybody sensible. What is granted cannot be recalled, which
everybody knows at signing. What is less examined is that it cannot be **aimed**: clauses five and six of the
Open Source Definition forbid discrimination against persons, against groups and against fields of endeavour,
so a licence excluding the party best placed to use the grant against its author would not be an open source
licence and would not have bought the adoption that made opening worth doing. The play models the documented
mechanics, the release that is right on its own terms, the grant with no addressee, the use that breaches
nothing, the correction that stops being the thing it corrects, the fork base manufactured by that correction,
and the team as the second unownable asset, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a
large firm preying on a small one.

The governing law is that the property which makes an open grant valuable is the same property that makes it
uncapturable. Adoption arrives because anybody may take it; if anybody may take it, then the party who can
extract most from it extracts most, and no term in the licence can lean the other way without the licence
ceasing to be one. This is Teece's appropriability regime at its weakest setting, chosen deliberately and for
good reasons, with the complementary assets that decide who profits held by somebody else.

What removes any possibility of blame from the company is that the trade is made knowingly and correctly. There
is no arrangement in which the code is both the distribution and the moat, the return on the grant is the
adoption, and a company that had kept the code would not have been chosen. The decision is not short-sighted:
it is the right decision, and being right is what builds the position on an asset the company has agreed never
to hold.

What removes it from the operator is that nothing is breached. The terms were drafted, published and meant by
the other party, contributions are real in some measure and are not owed, and the use is precisely the use the
definition exists to protect. **The play refuses to stage this as predation**, and the refusal is load-bearing
rather than polite: an account in which this party is a wrongdoer is an account in which the commons was never
a commons, and the flatness of having nothing to allege is the texture of the whole case.

And the correction arms its own answer. Changing the terms reaches future versions alone, so it delivers a
public grievance beside a complete, legally clean codebase standing at the last commit that was still open. A
community that is angry and has nothing to leave with argues; a community that is angry and holds a permanently
granted codebase leaves. The fork base is created by the correction, on the same day and in the same sentence,
and moving the terms at any other date would have left one somewhere else.

It is distinct from the house's **Over a Barrel**, where the most valuable deal needs a specific investment and
once it is sunk you can be squeezed, so the deal is never made: there the exposure runs through a relationship
and the squeeze comes from a counterparty who can renegotiate, which is why the remedies are contractual,
vertical integration or a long-term agreement. Here nothing is sunk into a relationship and there is nobody to
renegotiate with. The grant ran to the world, in perpetuity, with no counterparty to sit down with, and the
deal was not avoided but made with everyone at once.

It is distinct from the house's **The Commons**, where everyone takes a fair share of the shared field and the
field goes bare because the cost of one more is split by all: there the good is rivalrous and restraint is the
remedy, so a taker who took less would leave more. Here the operator's use removes nothing from anybody, the
code is undiminished by being run, and restraint would not help the company at all. The loss is not depletion
of the good but the impossibility of charging for it.

It is distinct from the house's **The Anticommons**, where too many owners hold veto rights so the resource
goes unused: that is the exact inversion and is worth naming for it. There the fragmentation of control
paralyses the resource. Here nobody holds a veto of any kind, the resource is used more than its author can
match, and the difficulty is that no party can be excluded rather than that too many can exclude.

It is distinct from the house's **Bug for Bug**, where a behaviour nobody specified gets depended on so
matching the bug becomes required: there the binding constraint is implicit, emerged from use, and never
written anywhere. Here the constraint is a specified, deliberate, published grant that the author wrote and
meant, and the trouble is not that an unintended commitment hardened but that an intended one did exactly what
it said.

It is distinct from the house's **After You**, where a platform cannot launch because each side waits for the
other: there the difficulty is getting the thing off the ground and openness is one of the standard answers to
it. Here the launch succeeded completely and the same instrument that solved the first problem is what makes
the second one unsolvable, so the two sit at opposite ends of one strategy rather than describing rival
accounts of it.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the operator as
  predatory and the company as naive, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the release
  is the cheapest distribution available and is chosen knowingly, with the trade of exclusivity for adoption
  made with open eyes; the definition refuses the carve-out because refusing it is what constitutes the good
  the company was buying; the operator uses terms the other party wrote and meant and breaches nothing, so
  there is nothing to allege; the contributors' loyalty was always to the grant rather than to the grantor, and
  the grantor wrote the grant; and the terms move because moving them is the only lever left against a real
  problem. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that the property
  which makes an open grant work is the property that makes it uncapturable.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Relicensing solves the
  problem forward and destroys the guarantee the community was building on, in one act. Forking preserves the
  guarantee and fragments the ecosystem, landing the maintenance on a foundation funded in large part by the
  operators the change was aimed at, while the original company still holds the appropriability problem it
  relicensed to solve. And the weight moving onto the team is **not** an exit: general capital a firm cannot
  appropriate is a named regime, and the market where opening is correct is the market where that team is
  recomposed elsewhere most easily.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the appropriability regime, the economics of open source, the
  platform accounts, the human-capital regime and the Open Source Definition are theirs. The bounds are carried
  with them: the play stages the **permissive** case and the copyleft case runs differently, since reciprocal
  terms aim the grant partially and the drift toward permissive licensing through the twenty-tens is context
  the play carries rather than a claim about anybody's motives; the trap is conditional on the market, biting
  where credibility and execution speed are the binding constraint and not where the defensible position was
  regulatory standing, capital intensity, distribution scale or a data asset, so nothing here is a general law
  about software companies; intermediate strategies exist and are documented, open core and partly-open
  platforms among them, and the play stages the fully permissive pole rather than the whole space; the
  relicensing record is recent and its outcomes are still contested, with the forks' long-run health not yet
  established either way, so it is carried as a record and not as a demonstration; and the Open Source
  Definition is a definition maintained by a body rather than a research finding, cited for what it says and
  not as evidence about the world. **The play makes no claim that opening was the wrong decision**, only that
  the grant runs in two directions nobody can close, and that the correction manufactures the answer to itself.
