# Plot: Nobody Broke Anything

## Taxonomy

[Given to Everyone](play_given_to_everyone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A provider running the software as a service at a scale the author cannot match, paying nothing.

## Action

[Running it at scale](process_running_it_at_scale.md): [the operator](persona_the_operator.md), holding [Operator](position_operator.md), builds a business on terms the other party wrote and meant.

## Stage

Every step is permitted. Contributions are real in some measure and are not owed. Nothing is available to allege.

## Tension

The play refuses to call this predation, and the refusal is load-bearing rather than polite.
The terms were drafted, published and intended by the company, the use is the use the definition exists to protect, and an account in which this party is a wrongdoer is an account in which the commons was never a commons. What is happening is that a grant with no addressee lands hardest wherever it can be used hardest, which is a property of the grant and not a decision by the taker, and the flatness of having nothing to allege is the whole texture of the case.
