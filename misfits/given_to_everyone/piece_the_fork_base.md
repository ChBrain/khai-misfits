# Piece: The Fork Base

## Taxonomy

[The Last Permissive Commit](place_the_last_permissive_commit.md). The means, delivered by the correction itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Standing at [the last permissive commit](place_the_last_permissive_commit.md), created by [changing the terms](process_changing_the_terms.md) and taken by [taking the base](process_taking_the_base.md).

## Load Bearing

It converts an announcement into a departure, because a grievance with nothing to leave with is only a complaint.
A community that is angry and has no codebase argues; a community that is angry and holds a complete, working, permanently granted codebase leaves, and the second condition is manufactured by the same act that produces the first, in the same sentence, on the same day.

## Apparent

Nothing at all until the announcement. An ordinary commit in the history.
Nobody prepared it and nobody could point to it in advance.

## Yearbook

Is created at the moment of the correction and not before.
Cannot be avoided by choosing a different commit, since moving the terms at any time leaves one somewhere.
