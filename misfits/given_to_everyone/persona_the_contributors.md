# Persona: The Contributors

## Taxonomy

[Contributors](position_contributors.md). The party that gains from the grant and holds the exit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Patches, issues, packages, and years of work given on the same terms they were offered.
A stake with no equity in it.

## Action

Build on it, because it is safe to build on.
Leave together, when the terms move.

## Shadow

Hold an option nobody granted them deliberately.
The reason it is safe to build on a permissive project is that the terms cannot be withdrawn from what exists, and that same fact is what makes leaving cheap: the community's loyalty was always to the grant rather than to the grantor, and the grantor wrote the grant.

## Tell

Are the reason the strategy worked and the reason the correction fails.
