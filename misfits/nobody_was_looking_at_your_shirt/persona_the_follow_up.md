# Persona: The Follow-Up

## Taxonomy

[Follow-Up](position_follow_up.md), inside [Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The archetype of the check-in after the fact, holding the true count up against the next guess and the partial, bounded correction that follows.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

It looks like the fix, the moment the truth is told and the Self's fear is finally, permanently corrected, a lesson learned once and for all.

## Action

Check back later. At [the Next Time](place_the_next_time.md), on [Check Back Later](plan_check_back_later.md) through [Adjusting](process_adjusting.md), the Follow-Up holds [the Adjustment](piece_the_adjustment.md), the next guess coming in smaller, though not all the way down to what the room actually gave it (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000; the wider egocentric-bias and self-focus literature that followed).

## Shadow

It is not the fix it looks like. The Follow-Up's feedback narrows the anchor, but it does not remove it, since the next conspicuous moment will feel just as vivid as the last one did, and a corrected guess about one shirt does not recalibrate the guess about the next one.

## Tell

It tells the truth once, and watches the next guess come in a little smaller, and not small enough.
