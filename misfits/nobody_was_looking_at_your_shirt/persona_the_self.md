# Persona: The Self

## Taxonomy

[Self](position_self.md), inside [Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The archetype of the one living a conspicuous moment, whose own vivid attention is anchored on and projected outward as an estimate of how much anyone else noticed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person visibly self-conscious, glancing around a room, certain that every glance is landing on them. It looks like ordinary awkwardness, the kind anyone recognizes.

## Action

Guess how many noticed. At [the Room](place_the_room.md), on [Guess How Many Noticed](plan_guess_how_many_noticed.md) through [Attending](process_attending.md), the Self holds [the Blemish](piece_the_blemish.md), and it meets [the Estimate](piece_the_estimate.md), the salience of the moment projected outward onto a room that does not share it (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000).

## Shadow

Its guess is sincere and its blind spot is exactly that sincerity. The Self does not invent the feeling of being watched to flatter itself; the moment really is the whole of its own attention. What it cannot see is that this fullness is a fact about its own mind and not a fact about anyone else's.

## Tell

It feels like the center of the room, in a room where it is, to almost everyone in it, an afterthought.
