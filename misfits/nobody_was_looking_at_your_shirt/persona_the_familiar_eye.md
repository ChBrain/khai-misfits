# Persona: The Familiar Eye

## Taxonomy

[Familiar Eye](position_familiar_eye.md), inside [Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The archetype of the observer who actually knows the Self, noticing somewhat more than a stranger and still far short of what is feared.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

It looks like the exception that should break the pattern, someone who really would notice, really would remember, a friend close enough that the Self's fear finally seems justified.

## Action

Look a little closer. At [the Next Time](place_the_next_time.md), on [Look a Little Closer](plan_look_a_little_closer.md) through [Recognizing](process_recognizing.md), the Familiar Eye holds [the Nearer Look](piece_the_nearer_look.md), noticing more than a stranger would, and still recalling only a fraction of what the Self feared it would (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000).

## Shadow

It is not the exception it looks like. The Familiar Eye's closeness narrows the gap between what the Self fears and what is actually seen, but it does not close it; even someone who knows the Self well is living inside their own attention too, and familiarity buys a little more notice, not a spotlight.

## Tell

It knows the Self better than a stranger does, and still remembers less than the Self is certain it must.
