# Position: Self

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The seat of the one living the conspicuous moment, and marks that its own vivid attention is no guide to anyone else's.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A moment that is vivid and total, the whole of its own attention for a stretch of time.
No access to any other mind's attention except by guessing from what fills its own.
An honest estimate, formed from the only evidence it actually has, which is itself.

## Orders

Live the moment as it is, vivid and consuming, since that is what a conspicuous moment honestly feels like from inside it.
Guess how much others noticed, using the only measure available: how much it fills your own mind.
Know that this measure, however sincerely applied, was never built to describe anyone else's mind.

## Loses

Nothing of its own honesty, and commits no fault in guessing from the only evidence available to it.
Because its estimate is built from what actually fills its own attention, the seat takes no blame for how far that estimate strays from anyone else's experience, and yet the estimate strays exactly as far as its own vividness is not shared.
It keeps its honestly formed guess and loses no cause for blame, the one who feels the fullest spotlight of anyone in the room and is, by that same fullness, the last to see how little of it reaches anyone else.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honest fullness of attention becomes an estimate no one else's mind can confirm,
living the moment as vividly as it truly is, and guessing from nothing but that vividness,
so the seat that feels the most watched is the seat that, by the same fact, has the least evidence of anyone actually watching.
