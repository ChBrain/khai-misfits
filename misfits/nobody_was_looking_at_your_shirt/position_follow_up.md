# Position: Follow-Up

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The seat of the check-in after the fact, and marks that its honest correction is real and partial, never the whole fix it looks like.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The true count, honestly delivered, set against the guess that came before it.
No power to make the next guess start from that truth instead of from the moment's own vividness.
A correction, real and measurable, that narrows the gap without closing it.

## Orders

Deliver the true count honestly, whatever it costs the guess that came before it.
Expect the correction to shrink the next estimate, not erase the anchor that produced it.
Know that the next conspicuous moment will feel just as vivid as this one did, truth told or not.

## Loses

Nothing of its own honesty, and commits no fault for a correction that does not fully take.
Because its feedback is delivered as completely as the truth allows, the seat takes no blame for the anchor's return next time, and yet the return is easy to mistake for a correction that never worked at all.
It keeps its honestly delivered truth and loses no cause for blame, the seat that shrinks the guess and cannot promise it shrunk for good.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honest correction narrows a fear it can never fully retire,
delivering the true count, and watching the next guess land smaller, though not small enough,
so the seat that tells the truth most completely is the seat that, by the same fact, cannot make the truth outlast the next vivid moment.
