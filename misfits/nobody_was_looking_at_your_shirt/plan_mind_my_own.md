# Plan: Mind My Own

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the crowd's seat](position_crowd.md), inside [Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Crowd](position_crowd.md)

## Direction

Attend to your own concerns first, honestly, since your attention belongs to your own life before it belongs to anyone else's conspicuous moment. Know that the fraction left over will be read by someone else as a verdict on their importance, when it is only the ordinary shape of a mind occupied with its own.

## Orders

([The Crowd](persona_the_crowd.md) holds [the Glance](piece_the_glance.md) through [Noticing](process_noticing.md) at [the Debrief](place_the_debrief.md), giving exactly the attention the moment actually earns, and meets [the Count](piece_the_count.md).)

## Implementation

([Noticing](process_noticing.md) at [the Debrief](place_the_debrief.md) holds [the Glance](piece_the_glance.md), because each person present is absorbed in their own concerns and has only a fraction of attention left for anyone else, and meets [the Count](piece_the_count.md), the true tally landing well under half of what was guessed (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000).)

## Targets

- [ ] attention is given honestly, to one's own concerns before anyone else's conspicuous moment
- [ ] the glance offered is ordinary, neither more nor less than the moment earns
- [ ] the count is met: the true tally, well under half of what the Self had guessed
- [ ] it is owned that this shortfall is not coldness, only the same self-absorption turned elsewhere
