# Pitch: Everyone's Own Show

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a room full of people is a room full of people each starring in their own show, with little attention left over for anyone else's.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Wry and gentle, the small comedy of a mind that cannot stop being the center of its own attention. Nothing here is vanity or coldness; the felt spotlight is real to the one who feels it, and the room's inattention is the same ordinary self-absorption, simply not returned.

## Undertow

The salience of one's own conspicuous action or appearance is so total to the one living it that this salience is projected outward onto everyone present, an anchor set by one's own experience and insufficiently adjusted for the fact that each person in the room is, in fact, absorbed in their own concerns.

## Nerve

That a person is judged, by their own account, to be far more watched and remembered than they in fact are, no vanity or inattention required. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since the bias shrinks, though it does not vanish, with feedback and with familiarity, not a claim that others never notice anything (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000).

## Echo

Feel the whole room watching you, in a room where everyone is watching themselves.
