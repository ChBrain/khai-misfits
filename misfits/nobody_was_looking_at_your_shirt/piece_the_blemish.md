# Piece: The Blemish

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The setup: a mildly conspicuous moment, real and vivid to the one living it, the whole of their own attention for a stretch of time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Room](place_the_room.md), carried by [Attending](process_attending.md): the moment lands and takes up the whole of the Self's attention right where it happens, with nowhere else for that attention to go.

## Load Bearing

The moment is genuinely conspicuous: a shirt one would rather not be seen in, a stumble crossing the room, a wrong answer said aloud. None of this is invented or exaggerated; it is an honestly vivid thing, filling the Self's attention completely for a stretch of time (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000). For as long as it lasts, nothing else in the room competes with it. The stain is real, the stumble happened, the wrong word left the mouth and cannot be called back. Nothing about the moment itself is imagined or overstated. What happens next, in how that moment gets judged from the outside, is where the distortion enters, but the moment that starts it is exactly as vivid as it feels.

## Apparent

Looks like a small, ordinary embarrassment, nothing anyone else would dwell on for more than a passing second, if they noticed it at all.

## Yearbook

Lived by [the Self](persona_the_self.md) through [Attending](process_attending.md) at [the Room](place_the_room.md), dramatized in [The Whole of My Attention](plot_the_whole_of_my_attention.md). It becomes [the Estimate](piece_the_estimate.md), and, measured against [the Count](piece_the_count.md), it reveals a gap the Self could not see from inside the moment.
