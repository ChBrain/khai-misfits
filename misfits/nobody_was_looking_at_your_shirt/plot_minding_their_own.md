# Plot: Minding Their Own

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At the Debrief, the room as it actually was, checked after the fact.

## Action

[The Crowd](persona_the_crowd.md), from [the crowd's seat](position_crowd.md), on [Mind My Own](plan_mind_my_own.md) through [Noticing](process_noticing.md), holds [The Glance](piece_the_glance.md) at [The Debrief](place_the_debrief.md): most of each person's attention was spent on their own appearance, their own conversation, their own concerns.

## Stage

Each person in the room was, in fact, absorbed in their own appearance, their own conversation, their own worries, with only a small remainder of attention left over for anyone else, so the spotlight the Self felt so clearly was real to the Self and simply did not exist from where anyone else in the room was standing (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000). There is no villain in this. The Crowd is not being cold or dismissive; it is not withholding notice out of indifference. It is living inside its own attention exactly as fully as the Self was living inside theirs, occupied with its own shirt, its own conversation, its own small worries, and simply never got around to the Self's moment because it never gets around to most moments that are not its own. The glance that did land on the Self was brief, ordinary, and quickly spent elsewhere, the same way anyone's glance is spent.

## Tension

The same self-absorption, in every direction, and none of it returned.
