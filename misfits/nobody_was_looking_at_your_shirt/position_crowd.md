# Position: Crowd

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The seat of the people actually present, and marks that their ordinary inattention is honest, not unkind.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Its own concerns, its own conversations, its own fraction of attention, already spoken for before anyone else enters the room.
No spare attention set aside for watching a stranger's shirt or a stranger's stumble.
An honest, unremarkable glance, given and then withdrawn, exactly as much as any ordinary moment earns.

## Orders

Live your own moment as fully as the Self is living theirs, since your attention belongs to your own concerns first.
Give what attention is actually left over, honestly, without pretending to more interest than the moment earns.
Know that this ordinary inattention will be read, by someone else, as coldness or as proof they were never noticed at all.

## Loses

Nothing of its own honesty, and commits no fault in attending mostly to itself.
Because its attention is spent on what actually fills it, the seat takes no blame for how little is left for anyone else, and yet the shortfall is easily mistaken for indifference by the very person hoping for more of it.
It keeps its honestly ordinary attention and loses no cause for blame, the seat that gives exactly as much notice as any moment like this one has ever earned from it.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honest self-absorption is mistaken for a verdict on someone else's importance,
living its own moment fully, attention already spent before a stranger's shirt or stumble arrives,
so the seat that notices least is the seat that, by the same fact, is doing nothing more than what the one fearing it is also doing.
