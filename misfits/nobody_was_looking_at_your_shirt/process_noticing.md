## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The same conspicuous moment, as it actually appears to the people around the Self.

## Direction

From each person's own occupied attention toward whatever fraction is left over for anyone else.

## Lever

Each person present is absorbed in their own concerns and has only a small remainder of attention for anyone else, so the true notice given is far below what the Self assumed (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000).
