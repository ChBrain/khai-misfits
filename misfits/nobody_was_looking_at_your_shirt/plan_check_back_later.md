# Plan: Check Back Later

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the follow-up's seat](position_follow_up.md), inside [Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Follow-Up](position_follow_up.md)

## Direction

Deliver the true count honestly, and let the next estimate shrink in response. Know that the correction is real but partial, since the next conspicuous moment will feel just as vivid as this one did, truth told or not, and the anchor it sets will not simply stay retired.

## Orders

([The Follow-Up](persona_the_follow_up.md) holds [the Adjustment](piece_the_adjustment.md) through [Adjusting](process_adjusting.md) at [the Next Time](place_the_next_time.md), delivering the true count and watching the next guess come in smaller.)

## Implementation

([Adjusting](process_adjusting.md) at [the Next Time](place_the_next_time.md) holds [the Adjustment](piece_the_adjustment.md), because feedback about the true count shrinks the next estimate, though it does not erase the anchor that produces a new one each time a moment feels vivid again (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000; the wider egocentric-bias and self-focus literature that followed).)

## Targets

- [ ] the true count is delivered honestly, in full, whatever it costs the prior guess
- [ ] the next estimate is seen to shrink in response to the feedback given
- [ ] it is owned that the shrinking is real but partial, never a full retirement of the anchor
- [ ] it is seen that the next conspicuous moment will feel just as vivid, truth told or not
