# Piece: The Adjustment

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The bound: the estimate's correction, partial and never complete, once feedback is given.

## Owner

## Place

## Load Bearing

The bias shrinks, though it does not vanish, once a person is given feedback about how little was actually noticed, a real but bounded and partial correction, since the next conspicuous moment will feel just as vivid as the last one did, truth told or not (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000; the wider egocentric-bias and self-focus literature that followed). Knowing the Count does not retire the instinct that produced the Estimate; it only trims it. The next shirt, the next stumble, the next wrong word will feel, in the moment, exactly as consuming as the last one did, and the correction has to be earned again rather than banked once and kept.

## Apparent

Looks like a hedge that undercuts the whole pattern; is actually what keeps the pattern honest.
