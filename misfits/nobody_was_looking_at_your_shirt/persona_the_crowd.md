# Persona: The Crowd

## Taxonomy

[Crowd](position_crowd.md), inside [Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The archetype of the people actually present, each absorbed in their own concerns, whose real, ordinary attention reveals the gap the Self's estimate could not see.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

It looks like an audience, a room full of eyes with nothing better to do than watch whoever walks in. Up close it is nothing of the kind, each face turned toward its own conversation, its own worry, its own reflection.

## Action

Mind my own. At [the Debrief](place_the_debrief.md), on [Mind My Own](plan_mind_my_own.md) through [Noticing](process_noticing.md), the Crowd holds [the Glance](piece_the_glance.md), and it meets [the Count](piece_the_count.md), the true tally landing well under half of what the Self had guessed (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000).

## Shadow

It is not cold and it is not indifferent. The Crowd is doing exactly what the Self is doing, living inside its own attention, and it is no more watching the Self than the Self is watching any one of them; the inattention is not a verdict on the Self's importance, only the ordinary shape of being a person with one's own life filling one's own mind.

## Tell

It notices almost nothing of what someone else feared it would, because it was busy being noticed by no one, too.
