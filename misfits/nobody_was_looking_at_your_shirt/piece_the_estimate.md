# Piece: The Estimate

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The mechanism and the turn: the anchored guess of how many others noticed, salience projected outward and insufficiently adjusted.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Room](place_the_room.md), carried by [Attending](process_attending.md): the guess forms right where the moment is still vivid, before anything has had the chance to cool.

## Load Bearing

Because the moment is so salient to the Self, they anchor on that salience and adjust insufficiently for the fact that everyone else in the room is the center of their own attention and not of anyone else's, an egocentric anchor carried outward onto a perspective it does not fit (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000). The guess is sincere; the Self is not lying, only measuring with the only instrument they have, which is themselves. There is no other gauge available in the moment, no way to borrow someone else's eyes, so the fullness of one's own attention stands in for a fact about everyone else's, and stands in badly.

## Apparent

Looks like ordinary self-consciousness, a reasonable worry about being seen; is actually a real inflation, far beyond what the room will bear out.
