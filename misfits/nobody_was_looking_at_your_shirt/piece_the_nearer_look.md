# Piece: The Nearer Look

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The domain grounding: a familiar observer noticing somewhat more than a stranger, though still far short of what is feared.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Next Time](place_the_next_time.md), carried by [Recognizing](process_recognizing.md): a friend's closer notice is measured here, against both the stranger's glance and the Self's original fear.

## Load Bearing

Familiarity between observer and observed narrows the gap, a close friend or a familiar face noticing more than a stranger would, but even a familiar observer notices and recalls far less than the one at the center of the moment fears (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000; the wider egocentric-bias and self-focus literature that followed). Closeness buys a little more attention, not a spotlight of its own. The friend who knows the Self well still spends most of their attention on their own concerns, the same as anyone else in the room, only with a slightly wider margin left over for someone they actually know.

## Apparent

Looks like the exception that should prove the Self right, someone close enough to really be watching; is actually still governed by the same ordinary limits on attention.
