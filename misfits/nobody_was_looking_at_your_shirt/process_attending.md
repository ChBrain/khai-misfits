# Process: Attending

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). Borne at [The Room](place_the_room.md), precipitated by [The Self](persona_the_self.md), in [The Whole of My Attention](plot_the_whole_of_my_attention.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A conspicuous moment, something mildly embarrassing or notable happening to the Self.

## Direction

From the moment itself toward an estimate of how much anyone else noticed it.

## Lever

The moment's vividness in the Self's own mind is used, by default, as the measure of how salient it must be to everyone else (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000).
