## Shown

People asked afterward what they noticed, thinking back over the moment in question.
Answers coming back thinner than expected, most of them a shrug or a blank.

## Holds

[The Glance](piece_the_glance.md). The room's ordinary, mostly elsewhere attention, honestly given, each person absorbed in their own concerns and not in anyone else's.

[The Count](piece_the_count.md). The true tally of who noticed or remembered, well under half of what was guessed.

## Offers

The Crowd a chance to report honestly what was actually noticed, no more and no less.
