# Position: Familiar Eye

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The seat of the observer who knows the Self, and marks that closeness narrows the gap between fear and fact without ever closing it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real history with the one it is watching, enough to notice a little more than a stranger would.
No exemption from its own attention being mostly spent on its own concerns, familiarity or not.
A somewhat closer look, honestly given, still far short of the spotlight the Self imagines.

## Orders

Notice what your familiarity actually affords, a little more than a stranger, and no more than that.
Do not let closeness be mistaken for constant watching, since even a friend's attention is mostly spent elsewhere.
Know that the Self will read your closeness as proof the fear was justified, when it only narrowed the gap a little.

## Loses

Nothing of its own honesty, and commits no fault in noticing only what familiarity actually affords.
Because its attention, even for someone it knows, is still mostly spent on its own life, the seat takes no blame for falling short of the spotlight the Self imagines, and yet its closeness is easy to read as proof of exactly that spotlight.
It keeps its honestly closer look and loses no cause for blame, the seat that notices more than a stranger and still nowhere near as much as is feared.

## Drives

Toward the one whose honest closeness is mistaken for the spotlight it never actually casts,
noticing a little more than a stranger, and no more than a real, ordinary attention allows,
so the seat that comes nearest to seeing is the seat that, by the same fact, still falls short of what the Self believes it must see.
