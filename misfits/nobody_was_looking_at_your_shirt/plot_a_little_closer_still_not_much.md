# Plot: A Little Closer, Still Not Much

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At the Next Time, the same kind of moment, seen this time by someone who actually knows the Self.

## Action

[The Familiar Eye](persona_the_familiar_eye.md), from [the familiar eye's seat](position_familiar_eye.md), on [Look a Little Closer](plan_look_a_little_closer.md) through [Recognizing](process_recognizing.md), holds [The Nearer Look](piece_the_nearer_look.md) at [The Next Time](place_the_next_time.md): a familiar eye notices somewhat more of the moment than a stranger would have.

## Stage

Familiarity between observer and observed narrows the gap; a close friend or a familiar face notices more than a stranger would, simply because there is more history to notice against and more reason to look. But even a familiar observer notices and recalls far less than the one at the center of the moment fears, so the narrowing is real and still falls well short of the spotlight the Self imagined (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000; the wider egocentric-bias and self-focus literature that followed). The Familiar Eye has done nothing wrong in noticing only this much. Real closeness earns a slightly longer glance and a slightly better memory of it afterward, but even a friend's attention is still mostly spent on its own life, its own errands, its own version of the Blemish waiting somewhere down the hall.

## Tension

Closer than a stranger, and still not the spotlight that was feared.
