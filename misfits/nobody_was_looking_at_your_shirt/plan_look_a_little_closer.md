# Plan: Look a Little Closer

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the familiar eye's seat](position_familiar_eye.md), inside [Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Familiar Eye](position_familiar_eye.md)

## Direction

Notice what real familiarity actually affords, a little more than a stranger would, and no more than that. Know that closeness will be read as proof of a constant watching it does not provide, since even a familiar mind spends most of its attention on its own life.

## Orders

([The Familiar Eye](persona_the_familiar_eye.md) holds [the Nearer Look](piece_the_nearer_look.md) through [Recognizing](process_recognizing.md) at [the Next Time](place_the_next_time.md), noticing more than a stranger would, and still far short of what is feared.)

## Implementation

([Recognizing](process_recognizing.md) at [the Next Time](place_the_next_time.md) holds [the Nearer Look](piece_the_nearer_look.md), because real familiarity buys somewhat more notice, though the familiar mind's attention is still mostly spent on its own concerns, so the gap narrows without closing (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000).)

## Targets

- [ ] the notice given reflects real familiarity, more than a stranger's, no more
- [ ] the recognition is honest, not inflated into a constant watching it does not provide
- [ ] it is seen that even a familiar mind's attention is mostly spent on its own life
- [ ] it is owned that closeness narrows the gap between fear and fact without closing it
