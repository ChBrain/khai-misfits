# Plot: Surely Everyone Saw

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The moment lived, and a guess forming about who else must have seen it.

## Action

At [The Room](place_the_room.md), [The Self](persona_the_self.md) from [the self's seat](position_self.md) meets [The Estimate](piece_the_estimate.md): the anchored guess takes shape, drawn straight from how large the moment felt from inside.

## Stage

Because the moment is so salient to the Self, the Self anchors on that salience and adjusts too little for the fact that everyone else in the room is the center of their own attention, not of anyone else's, so an egocentric anchor gets carried outward onto a perspective it was never built to describe (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000). The Self is not exaggerating on purpose or fishing for reassurance; the Self is using the only measure it has ever had access to, which is its own vivid sense of the moment, and applying that measure to minds it cannot actually see into. The guess feels like an observation, but it is really an extrapolation, run from a single data point that happens to be the loudest one in the room, which is the Self's own experience of itself.

## Tension

A guess built from the one mind it cannot actually describe.
