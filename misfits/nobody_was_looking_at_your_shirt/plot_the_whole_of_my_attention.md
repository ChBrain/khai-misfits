# Plot: The Whole of My Attention

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At the Room, a conspicuous moment happens: a shirt one would rather not be seen in, a stumble crossing the floor, a wrong answer said aloud for everyone to hear.

## Action

[The Self](persona_the_self.md), from [the self's seat](position_self.md), on [Guess How Many Noticed](plan_guess_how_many_noticed.md) through [Attending](process_attending.md), holds [The Blemish](piece_the_blemish.md) at [The Room](place_the_room.md): the moment fills the Self's own attention completely, with nowhere else for that attention to go.

## Stage

The moment is real, and it is honestly vivid. The stain sits where it sits, the stumble happened in full view of the floor, the wrong word left the mouth and cannot be called back, and for a stretch of time nothing else in the room competes with it inside the Self's own head (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000). This is not performance and it is not exaggeration; it is simply what attention does when the thing it is attending to belongs to the one doing the attending. A conspicuous moment really is, for the person living it, the whole of the room for a while, because there is no second vantage point available from inside a single mind to check that feeling against. The Self is not vain for feeling this; the Self is only using the one instrument it has, which is itself, and that instrument is reporting, accurately, that something large just happened.

## Tension

A moment that fills one mind completely, and no other yet.
