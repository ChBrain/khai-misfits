# Plot: Hardly Anyone Noticed

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The guess meets the actual tally.

## Action

At [The Debrief](place_the_debrief.md), [The Crowd](persona_the_crowd.md) from [the crowd's seat](position_crowd.md) meets [The Count](piece_the_count.md): the true number lands well under half of what the Self had guessed.

## Stage

In the classic demonstration, the true number of people who noticed or later recalled a conspicuous shirt comes in well under half of what the wearer guessed, the gap between the felt spotlight and the room's actual attention laid bare in the plainest possible arithmetic (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000). No villain sits behind that gap. The Self's estimate was sincere, built honestly from the only vantage point available to it, and the Crowd's inattention was ordinary, built just as honestly from a room full of people minding their own moments, so the distance between the guess and the count is authored by no one at all. It is worth noting, in plain terms, that this is a different inflation from the one staged in a sibling misfit called Larger in the Looking, where the inflated judgment concerns how much a factor matters to one's own well-being, a self-directed importance weighing. Here the inflated judgment concerns a third party's perspective, how much someone else notices and evaluates the self, a social projection outward rather than an inward one.

## Tension

A spotlight felt by one person, and switched on for almost no one else.
