# Piece: The Glance

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Was Looking at Your Shirt](play_nobody_was_looking_at_your_shirt.md). The room's real state: the ordinary, mostly elsewhere attention of the people actually present, each absorbed in their own concerns.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Debrief](place_the_debrief.md), carried by [Noticing](process_noticing.md): this ordinary, mostly elsewhere attention is checked here, after the moment has passed and can finally be counted.

## Load Bearing

Each person in the room is, in fact, absorbed in their own appearance, their own conversation, their own concerns, and has only a small fraction of attention left over for anyone else, so the felt spotlight is real to the one who feels it and simply does not exist from where anyone else is standing (Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky, 2000). Their glances, such as they are, are brief and mostly aimed inward, at their own footing, their own next sentence, their own small worries. None of this is aimed at the Self, not out of any decision to look away, but because there was never much attention pointed outward to begin with.

## Apparent

Looks like indifference or coldness; is actually the same ordinary self-absorption the Self is feeling too, just never turned toward them.
