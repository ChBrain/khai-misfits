# Persona: The Reported Event

## Taxonomy

[Report](position_report.md). The adverse event: real, small, honestly counted, and not falling.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Genuine, and the play will not soften it.
Rates are low and are not zero, the events are described in the literature, and the surveillance that finds them exists because finding them is right.
Nobody in this production is concealing anything or would.

## Action

Occurs.
Is reported, investigated and published.
Does not decline as coverage rises, because success does not make the programme safer.

## Shadow

That its honesty is exactly what makes it the visible half.
The duty to publish is not negotiable and must not be, and it puts a current, countable, describable harm on one side of a comparison whose other side is an absence.
It ends the only harm anybody can point to, having done nothing wrong to get there.

## Tell

Has a number, a case definition and a reporting form.
The thing it is being weighed against has none of those.
