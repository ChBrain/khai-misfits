# Piece: The One Generation

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). The period-setter: the time it takes for a cohort with no memory to become the cohort deciding.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Between the disease disappearing and [the clinic](place_the_clinic.md) filling with people who never saw it.
Measured in cohorts rather than in years.

## Load Bearing

Without it this is a drift and drifts do not return to where they started.
It is load bearing because it makes the thing a **cycle**: memory goes at one cohort per generation, which is slower than the incidence curve, so the decline in confidence arrives long after the success that caused it and the return of the disease restarts the clock.

## Apparent

A slow process, and therefore one there is time to get ahead of.
The people who would act are the people who remember, and they are leaving the population at the rate that drives the whole mechanism, so the constituency for acting shrinks on the same schedule as the need for it grows.

## Yearbook

Set by demography rather than by epidemiology, spent at [the clinic](place_the_clinic.md).
It is why the same argument has to be had again roughly once every thirty years.
