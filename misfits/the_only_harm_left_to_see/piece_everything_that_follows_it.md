# Piece: Everything That Follows It

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). At high coverage, nearly every childhood misfortune follows a vaccination.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the reported caseload at [the register](place_the_register.md).
Enacted by [attributing](process_attributing.md), against the background rate of ordinary life.

## Load Bearing

Without it the attribution problem is a fixed cost and shrinks with better method.
It is load bearing because the volume is **set by coverage**: when almost every child has been vaccinated, almost everything that happens to a child happens after a vaccination, so the events needing separation grow with the programme's success rather than with any risk.

## Apparent

A reason to improve surveillance, which would help.
Better surveillance counts more of both kinds. The literature says it plainly: what accumulates is both causally and coincidentally related, and **no amount of counting separates a particular case.**

## Yearbook

Named in the same literature that describes the programme's stages, spent one anxious parent at a time.
It is the second half of the inversion and the worse half.
