# Piece: The Ratio

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). Observed harm from the disease against observed harm from the programme, inverting as coverage rises.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the two series at [the register](place_the_register.md), read together.
Produced by [immunising](process_immunising.md) succeeding, and by nothing else.

## Load Bearing

Without it this is a communication problem and communication problems have communication answers.
It is load bearing because the inversion is **arithmetic rather than rhetoric**: one series falls because the programme works and the other does not, since success does not make a vaccine safer, so the share of visible harm belonging to the programme rises mechanically toward all of it.

## Apparent

A framing that a better graph would fix.
No graph fixes it, because the numerator that fell is the one that can be counted and the benefit that replaced it cannot be. **The ratio inverts because the programme worked**, and that sentence is the whole of the play.

## Yearbook

Described in the literature on how immunisation programmes evolve, spent at [the clinic](place_the_clinic.md).
It is the difference between a trade-off and a trap.
