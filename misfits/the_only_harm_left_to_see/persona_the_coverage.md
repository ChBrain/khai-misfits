# Persona: The Coverage

## Taxonomy

[Coverage](position_coverage.md). The proportion reached: the achievement, and the variable everything else in the play moves against.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A number going up, and the play means it.
Children who would have been deaf, blind or dead are none of those, which is not a framing and not a trade-off.
Decades of work by people who were right.

## Action

Rises.
Reaches almost everybody.
Keeps reaching them, which requires the same effort every year.

## Shadow

That every consequence in this production is a consequence of its success.
The disease leaves the record because coverage rose, the attribution problem worsens because coverage rose, and the memory goes because coverage rose, so **there is no version of this that keeps the benefit and avoids the rest**.
It ends having removed the only evidence anybody had for it.

## Tell

The line reaches ninety-five per cent.
Nothing visible happens, which is the entire point and the entire difficulty.
