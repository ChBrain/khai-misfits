# Plot: It Comes Back

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Incidence rising, where coverage fell.
[The Ward](place_the_ward.md), unused for thirty years and then not.

## Action

[Disappearing](process_disappearing.md) runs backwards: the disease returns, and with it the evidence that was missing.

## Stage

[The One Generation](piece_the_one_generation.md) restarts, because the memory has been resupplied in the only way that has ever resupplied it.

## Tension

The cycle closes, and the thing that closes it is the harm.
Where coverage was maintained against the pressure, incidence ran **ten to a hundred times lower** than where it was not. That is the return leg measured, and it is the demonstration that would settle every question in this play.
**It is not a remedy.** It is precisely what the programme exists to prevent, so the one thing that reliably restores the missing side of the comparison is the outcome nobody may arrange, and every acceptable settlement runs into the same arithmetic: telling people the disease was worse is telling them about something none of them has seen; reporting fewer events would be a fraud; better attribution studies help and are themselves invisible, being null results about things that did not happen.
And then coverage recovers, and the disease goes again, and the clock starts on the next cohort who will not have seen it.
