# Process: Disappearing

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). The disease leaving the record, the clinic, and then living memory.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Coverage crossing the level at which transmission stops.
At [the register](place_the_register.md), and afterwards at every kitchen table.

## Direction

Toward absence, which is not a small number.
No ward, no cases, no cousin who died, nobody who remembers a summer of it: the benefit arrives as a set of things that did not happen to people who never knew they were at risk.
And memory goes at its own pace, one cohort at a time, which is slower than the incidence curve and is what sets the period of everything that follows.

## Lever

Success, applied for long enough.
**A counterfactual cannot be shown to anybody**, so this process converts the programme's entire product into a form that cannot be entered as evidence.

## Echo

Nothing to point at,
which is indistinguishable from there never having been a problem.
