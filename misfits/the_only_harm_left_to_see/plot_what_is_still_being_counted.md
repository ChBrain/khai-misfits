# Plot: What Is Still Being Counted

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The surveillance system, doing its duty, publishing what it finds.
[The Duty to Publish](piece_the_duty_to_publish.md), which is not negotiable.

## Action

[Reporting](process_reporting.md): [The Reported Event](persona_the_reported_event.md), in [the report seat](position_report.md), is counted, investigated and published on [Publish Every One](plan_publish_every_one.md).

## Stage

[The Ratio](piece_the_ratio.md) inverts at [the register](place_the_register.md), mechanically, while everybody does their job.

## Tension

This is the inversion, and it is arithmetic rather than rhetoric.
One series falls because the programme works. The other does not, because success does not make a vaccine safer. So the share of visible harm belonging to the programme rises toward all of it, and **the ratio inverts because the programme worked.**
And the honesty is the mechanism rather than a flaw in it: the events are current, countable and described **precisely because** the system that finds them is working, and no version of this asks anybody to count fewer. That is the difference between a public health service and a promise.
Then the second half, which is worse and is the same arithmetic. At high coverage nearly every child has been vaccinated, so nearly every childhood misfortune **follows a vaccination**: the events needing separation grow with success rather than with risk, and better surveillance counts more of both kinds. The aggregate question is answerable and has been answered. The question everybody actually asks is about one child.
