# Persona: The Vanished Disease

## Taxonomy

[Incidence](position_incidence.md). The harm the programme was built against: gone from the record, the clinic and the family.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Absent, which is not the same as small.
Not a low number in a table: no cases, no ward, no cousin who died, nobody who remembers a summer of it.
Exactly the outcome that was wanted.

## Action

Falls.
Reaches zero, or near enough.
Stays there, for as long as coverage holds.

## Shadow

That an absence cannot be shown to anybody.
The benefit the programme bought is the whole of what did not happen, and **nobody can be shown a counterfactual**, so the thing being weighed against a visible cost is not visible at all and never was.
It ends undefeated and unavailable as evidence.

## Tell

There is nothing to point at.
That is the success, and it is indistinguishable from there never having been a problem.
