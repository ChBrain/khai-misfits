# The Only Harm Left to See: Reference

The research warrant for The Only Harm Left to See.
The setting is a mature immunisation programme; the play runs on the fact that a preventive programme succeeds
by removing the harm that was visible, while its own honestly reported harms remain, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging a successful preventive programme as **a benefit made of absences weighed against a cost made of
events**: coverage rises and incidence falls to nothing, which is the achievement; the disease leaves the record
and then living memory, so the entire benefit becomes a counterfactual that no record can carry; the programme's
own adverse events are counted and published because that duty is right, and they do not fall, since success does
not make a vaccine safer; at high coverage nearly every childhood misfortune follows a vaccination, so the
attribution problem grows with the programme's success; and the person deciding has an accurate picture with one
side of the comparison unrepresented. The play models the coverage that is the engine of everything, the
disappearance that converts the benefit into a non-event, the reporting duty that supplies the visible half, the
coincidence whose volume is set by coverage, the generational lag that makes it a cycle, and the return that
restores the memory in the only way it has ever been restored, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not
as a tale of misinformation or of a credulous public.

The governing law is that a preventive programme's success removes the observable it was justified by and leaves
its own reported harms in place, so the share of visible harm belonging to the programme rises toward all of it
as a matter of arithmetic.

**Nobody in the sequence falls short, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The
programme works and is delivered competently. The surveillance that counts its harms exists because counting them
is right and reports honestly. The attribution methods are sound and answer the population question. The clinic
gives correct information to somebody who asked in good faith. And the parent reads carefully and reasons
correctly from what is in front of them.

**And the success is real, which the play establishes before anything else and does not qualify.** Coverage
rises, incidence falls, and children who would have been deaf, blind or dead are none of those. The effect is
large, measured and unambiguous, produced by decades of unglamorous work, and it has to be earned again every
year because coverage is a rate held rather than a state reached. **Every consequence in this production is a
consequence of that succeeding.**

**Then the disappearance, which changes what kind of thing the benefit is.** A disease that has gone is not a
small number in a table: it is an absence from the record, the ward and the family. So the whole of what the
programme bought is a set of things that did not happen to people who never knew they were at risk, and **nobody
can be shown a counterfactual.** Models of averted cases exist and are good and are one further thing nobody has
seen.

**Then the inversion, which is arithmetic rather than rhetoric.** The adverse events are real, are low and are
not zero, and they are counted, investigated and published because the duty to do so is not negotiable. That
series does not fall as coverage rises, because success does not make a vaccine safer. One series falls and the
other does not, so **the ratio of observed harms inverts, and it inverts because the programme worked.** The
honesty is the mechanism and not a flaw in it: the events are current, countable and described **precisely
because** the system finding them is working.

**And the second half of the inversion is worse and is the same arithmetic.** When nearly every child has been
vaccinated, nearly every childhood misfortune follows a vaccination. The literature describing how immunisation
programmes evolve says it plainly: what accumulates is both **causally and coincidentally** related. So the
caseload requiring separation grows with the programme's success rather than with any risk, and better
surveillance does not relieve it, because better surveillance counts more of both kinds. The aggregate question
is answerable and has been answered; the question anybody actually asks is about one child.

**Then the generation, which makes this a cycle rather than a drift.** Memory leaves at one cohort per
generation, which is slower than the incidence curve, so the fall in confidence arrives long after the success
that caused it. The person deciding has seen the reported harms, because those are current, and has not seen the
disease, because it is gone. **Their evidence is genuine and their conclusion follows from it**, and the people
who would argue otherwise are the people who remember, who are leaving the population at exactly the rate that
drives the mechanism.

**And the return leg is measured.** Where immunisation was disrupted by anti-vaccine movements, pertussis
incidence ran ten to a hundred times higher than in comparable countries that maintained coverage. That is the
cycle closing, and **it is not a settlement**: it is precisely the outcome the programme exists to prevent, so
the one demonstration that reliably restores the missing side of the comparison is the one nobody may arrange.
Every acceptable remedy runs into the same arithmetic. Telling people the disease was worse is telling them about
something none of them has seen. Reporting fewer events would be a fraud. Better attribution studies help and are
themselves invisible, being null results about things that did not happen.

It is distinct from the house's **Kept From Burning**, which stages the fire suppression paradox, where
suppressing small fires accumulates the fuel that makes a later one catastrophic. Remove that engine entirely, by
stipulating a hazard held exactly constant with nothing accumulating anywhere, and this misfit is unchanged,
because **measles is no more dangerous after thirty years of vaccination than before it.** That play needs the
world to change under the protection; this one needs only the evidence to.

It is distinct from the house's **Dry Enough to Build On**, which stages the levee effect, where protection
invites the development it then cannot protect. Remove that engine, by stipulating that nobody moves in and no
assets accumulate behind the protection, and this stands entire, because **the same population simply stops
seeing the disease.** Nothing here is drawn into harm's way by the defence; the exposure is unchanged and only
the record of it empties.

It is distinct from the house's **The Long Calm**, which stages the financial instability hypothesis, where a
stretch of stability is spent on leverage so the eventual break is larger for it. Remove that engine, by
stipulating that nothing whatever is accumulated during the quiet, and this is untouched, because **the epidemic
that returns is exactly the size it always was.** There the calm is converted into fragility; here it is
converted into nothing at all, which is the point.

It is distinct from the house's **Not Worth My While**, which stages Rose's prevention paradox, where a measure
bringing a large benefit to the population brings little to any individual. Remove that engine, by stipulating a
large and obvious individual benefit, which measles vaccination genuinely has, and the cycle still runs, because
**the mechanism is not that the benefit is small but that it becomes unobservable while the cost stays
observable.** That play turns on the size of the benefit; this one turns on its form.

It is distinct from the house's **As Full As I Remember**, which stages shifting baseline syndrome, where each
generation takes the state it first encountered as the reference so a decline goes unnoticed. Remove that engine,
by stipulating that every generation inherits the true historical baseline perfectly, and this stands, because
**there is no declining quantity here at all.** The hazard is constant, the vaccine is unchanged, and what moves
is which harms are visible.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the parent as
  credulous, the surveillance as a liability or anybody as misinformed, because nothing in the sequence has a
  decision to get wrong: the programme works and is delivered competently, the system that counts its harms
  exists because counting them is right and reports honestly, the attribution methods are sound and answer the
  question they can, the clinic gives correct information in good faith, and the person deciding reads carefully
  and reasons correctly from an accurate picture. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the
  structure, so the misfit is that success removes the observable it was justified by.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Better information changes
  nothing, because the information is already correct and one side of the comparison has no representative.
  Telling people the disease was worse is telling them about something none of them has seen. Reporting fewer
  adverse events would be a fraud and is not on the table in any version of this. Models of averted cases are
  good and are one more thing nobody has seen. Better attribution studies genuinely help and are themselves
  invisible, being null results about things that did not happen. And the one demonstration that reliably
  restores the missing quantity is the disease returning, which is what the programme exists to prevent.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the description of how an immunisation programme's stages unfold is
  Chen and Orenstein's, the comparison of disrupted against maintained programmes is Gangarosa and colleagues',
  and the measurement of vaccine confidence across countries is Larson and colleagues'. The bounds are carried
  with them, and one is owed plainly: **none of the cited works was read in full**, each being behind publisher
  access, so each is cited for the question it addresses and its findings as corroborated from several
  independent reports, and **no figure appears in this production that those reports did not state**. The
  others: the pertussis comparison is **observational and between countries**, so it carries the confounding
  that any such comparison does and is not a controlled result; the confidence survey is self-report at one
  period and describes variation rather than establishing what causes it; the structural halves, that the ratio
  inverts mechanically and that attribution worsens with coverage, are offered as descriptions of arithmetic and
  not as measured effects; how much of any given resurgence is attributable to confidence rather than to access,
  supply or displacement is **genuinely contested** and the play adjudicates none of it; and the account is
  written for programmes that reach very high coverage against a disease that then disappears, which is not every
  programme. **The play makes no claim about any vaccine, any schedule, any parent or any country, and gives no
  medical, public health or policy advice of any kind**, only that a programme which works by making a harm stop
  happening ends up with its own reported harms as the only ones anybody can still see.
