# Pitch: Nobody Here Has Seen Measles

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Patient and precise, explaining a graph to somebody who asked a fair question.
The register of a public health physician who will not be defensive about the part that is true.
Not persuading anybody of anything, and using numbers where numbers help.

## Undertow

Under the schedule, two facts that do not negotiate.
The disease's damage leaves the record when the disease does, and living memory goes with it.
The programme's own adverse events do not leave, because reporting them is a duty and the duty is right.

## Nerve

The unease of a comparison with only one side on the table.
A rate, a case definition and a published paper on one side.
On the other, an absence, which is what success looks like and is indistinguishable from there never having been a problem.

## Echo

After the run: a programme that worked, a surveillance system that was honest,
a parent who read everything and reasoned correctly,
and a ward that fills again, which is the only thing that has ever put the other quantity back on the table.
