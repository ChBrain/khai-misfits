# Process: Attributing

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). Separating what a vaccination caused from what merely followed one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Every event that arrived after a dose, which at high coverage is nearly every event.
At [the register](place_the_register.md), by people with methods and background rates.

## Direction

Toward saying which is which.
The methods are real and the aggregate answers are good: background rates are known, controls exist, and the population-level question is answerable and has been answered.
The question everybody actually asks is about **one** child, and that is the one the methods cannot reach.

## Lever

Coverage, working against it.
When almost every child has been vaccinated almost every childhood misfortune follows a vaccination, so the caseload grows with success rather than with risk, and **more surveillance counts more of both kinds.**

## Echo

Two piles, correct in aggregate,
and unassignable one at a time.
