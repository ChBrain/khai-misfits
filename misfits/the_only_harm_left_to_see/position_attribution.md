# Position: Attribution

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). The seat of assignment: deciding which events following a vaccination were caused by one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Every event that happened after a dose.
Methods, controls and background rates, all of them real.
A caseload that grows with coverage and not with risk.

## Orders

Receive what is reported.
Separate the caused from the coincident.
Report what cannot be separated as what it is.

## Loses

The separation itself, at exactly the moment it matters most.
When almost every child has been vaccinated, almost every childhood misfortune follows a vaccination, so **the problem grows in proportion to the programme's success**, and better surveillance does not relieve it because better surveillance counts more of both kinds.
It ends holding two piles that are correct in aggregate and unassignable one by one.

## Drives

Toward telling people what is true about a particular child,
which is the question everybody actually asks,
so the seat that would settle it is the seat success makes hardest.
