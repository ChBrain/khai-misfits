## Action

## Stage

[The Counterfactual](piece_the_counterfactual.md) is named, and has no row at [the register](place_the_register.md).

## Tension

Here the shape of the benefit becomes the problem, without anybody doing anything.
An absence is not a small number. What the programme bought is the entire set of things that did not happen to people who never knew they were at risk, and **nobody can be shown a counterfactual.**
So the two quantities in the comparison are not of the same kind: the cost is a set of events and the benefit is a set of non-events, and a record that is complete and correct still carries only one of them. Models of averted cases exist and are good, and they are a further thing nobody has seen.
**Which is what makes prevention structurally worse at arguing for itself than response is**, and it is true of every successful preventive programme rather than of this one.
