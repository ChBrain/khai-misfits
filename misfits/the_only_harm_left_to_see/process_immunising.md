# Process: Immunising

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). The programme doing its work: coverage rising, year after year, by ordinary effort.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A disease worth preventing and a vaccine that prevents it.
At [the clinic](place_the_clinic.md), one appointment at a time, for decades.

## Direction

Toward almost everybody reached.
This is the good half and the play does not qualify it: children who would have been deaf, blind or dead are none of those, and the effect is large, measured and unambiguous.
The work is unglamorous and has to be repeated every year, because coverage is not a state that is reached but a rate that is held.

## Lever

Appointments kept, supply maintained, reminders sent.
Nothing clever, and it produces the largest effect in the play.

## Echo

Ninety-five per cent,
and nothing visible happening,
which is the entire point and the entire difficulty.
