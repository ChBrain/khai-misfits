# Persona: The New Parent

## Taxonomy

[Decision](position_decision.md). The person deciding, with an accurate picture and no memory of the disease.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, informed, and reasoning correctly.
Not credulous, not ideological, and not the person a public information campaign is written for.
Somebody who has read what is published and weighed it.

## Action

Reads.
Weighs a described, countable risk against a risk nobody can describe to them.
Decides.

## Shadow

That the picture is accurate and incomplete in a way nothing available can complete.
They have seen the reported harms, because those are current; they have not seen the disease, because it is gone; so **their evidence is real and their conclusion follows from it**, and the missing half is missing because the programme succeeded.
It ends having decided well on the evidence there was.

## Tell

Asks what the risk is.
Gets a good answer, about the only risk anybody can quantify.
