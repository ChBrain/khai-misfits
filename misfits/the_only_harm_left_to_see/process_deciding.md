# Process: Deciding

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). A parent weighing a described risk against one nobody can describe to them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An appointment, for a particular child, this week.
At [the clinic](place_the_clinic.md), by somebody careful who has read what is published.

## Direction

Toward doing right by one child.
The reading is accurate. The reported harms are current, quantified and real; the disease is absent, unwitnessed and unquantifiable to somebody who has never met it.
So **the evidence is genuine and the conclusion follows from it**, and the missing half is missing because the programme worked.

## Lever

Ordinary care, applied to the information that exists.
Which is why nothing in this seat can be corrected by better information: the information is correct and one side of the comparison has no representative.

## Echo

A good question, asked,
answered well, about the only risk anybody can put a number on.
