# Process: Reporting

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). Counting the programme's own harms and publishing them, because that is the duty.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An event following a dose, of any kind, reported by anybody.
At [the register](place_the_register.md), continuously and by design.

## Direction

Toward an honest account of what the programme costs.
The rates are low and are not zero, the events are described, and the system that finds them exists because finding them is right: this is the difference between a public health service and a promise.
**Nothing here is negotiable and nothing should be.**

## Lever

A case definition, a form, an investigator and a journal.
All of which make the harm current, countable and describable, on one side of a comparison whose other side is an absence.

## Echo

A number, a rate and a paper,
about the only risk in the picture that anybody can quantify.
