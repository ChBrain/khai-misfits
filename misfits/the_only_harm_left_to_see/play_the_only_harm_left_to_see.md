# Play: The Only Harm Left to See

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Only Harm Left to See.
Put two lines on one graph. Coverage climbing. Disease falling toward the axis and staying there.
Now put a third line on it, the one nobody draws: the programme's own adverse events, real, small, honestly counted, and not falling, because nothing about the programme's success makes the programme safer.
The first two lines are the achievement. The third is what is left in the picture when the achievement is complete.

## Arc

The bend is this: a preventive programme succeeds by removing the harm it was built against, and the harm it removes is the one that was visible, so the further it succeeds the more of the remaining visible harm belongs to the programme itself.
It runs on two facts, neither of them anybody's doing: the disease's damage leaves the record when the disease does, and living memory goes with it; and the intervention's own adverse events do not leave, because they are honestly reported and the duty to report them is not negotiable.
Take the success first, because the play has to establish that it is **real**. Coverage rises, incidence falls, and children who would have been deaf or dead are neither. That is not a framing and not a trade-off: it is the thing working, and any account that opens by qualifying it has nothing to say about what follows.
Then the disappearance. A disease that has gone is not a low number in the record: it is an absence from the record and from every clinic and every family, so **the counterfactual is the whole of the benefit and nobody can see a counterfactual.**
Then the fact that inverts it. The adverse events are counted, published and discussed, because the surveillance that counts them is a duty and a good one. So as coverage rises, **the ratio of observed harms inverts, and it inverts because the programme worked.**
And the second half of the inversion is worse, and it is the same arithmetic. At high coverage, very nearly every child has been vaccinated, so very nearly every misfortune that befalls a child **follows a vaccination**. The literature says this plainly: the events that accumulate are both causally and coincidentally related. **The attribution problem gets harder in exact proportion to the programme's success**, and no better surveillance fixes it, because better surveillance counts more of both.
Then the generation, which is what makes this a cycle and not a drift. The inversion completes when the people deciding have no memory of the disease. They have seen the reported harms, because those are current, and they have not seen the disease, because it is gone, so **their picture is accurate and their conclusion follows from it.** Confidence falls, coverage falls with it, and the difference is measurable: where coverage was maintained against the pressure, incidence ran ten to a hundred times lower than where it was not.
Reach for a settlement and every one of them runs into the same arithmetic. Telling people the disease was worse is telling them about something none of them has seen. Reporting fewer adverse events is unthinkable and would be a fraud. Better attribution studies help and are themselves invisible, being a null result about something that did not happen. **And the thing that ends the cycle reliably is the disease coming back**, which is not a remedy and is what the whole programme exists to prevent.

**[It Comes Back](plot_it_comes_back.md)**

## Stakes

Whether a programme can show anybody what it is for.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the vaccine works, because the play grants that it does and needs it to, and not whether anybody is lying, because the surveillance is honest and its honesty is load-bearing, but whether a benefit made entirely of absences can be held in the same picture as a cost made entirely of events. The production cannot get it into the picture. It can only show that the success is real, that its whole product is a counterfactual, that the reporting duty is right and supplies the visible half, that at high coverage nearly every childhood misfortune follows a vaccination and no better surveillance separates them, that the person deciding has an accurate picture and reasons correctly from it, and that the one thing which restores the missing half is the harm returning.
