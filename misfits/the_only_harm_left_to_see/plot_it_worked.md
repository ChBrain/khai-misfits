## Cue

## Action

[Immunising](process_immunising.md): [The Coverage](persona_the_coverage.md), in [the coverage seat](position_coverage.md), rises at [the clinic](place_the_clinic.md) on [Reach Everybody](plan_reach_everybody.md).

## Stage

[The Vanished Disease](persona_the_vanished_disease.md), on [Fall to Nothing](plan_fall_to_nothing.md), goes.

## Tension

This plot's whole work is to establish that the success is **real and unqualified**, because a production that opens by hedging it has nothing to say about anything that follows.
Children who would have been deaf, blind or dead are none of those. The effect is large, measured and unambiguous, produced by decades of unglamorous work by people who were right, and it has to be earned again every year because coverage is a rate held rather than a state reached.
And it is the engine of everything after. **Every consequence in this production is a consequence of this succeeding**: the record empties because coverage rose, the attribution problem worsens because coverage rose, and the memory goes because coverage rose. There is no version that keeps the benefit and avoids the rest.
