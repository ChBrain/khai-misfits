# Persona: The Coincidence

## Taxonomy

[Attribution](position_attribution.md). Everything that happens to a child after a vaccination and because of nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary misfortune, arriving on its own schedule.
Seizures, developmental diagnoses, sudden deaths, illnesses that would have happened in any century.
None of it caused by anything in the programme.

## Action

Happens.
Happens after a vaccination, because at high coverage almost everything does.
Gets reported, correctly, by systems built to catch what they cannot yet explain.

## Shadow

That its volume is set by coverage.
When almost every child has been vaccinated, almost every childhood misfortune follows a vaccination, so **the attribution problem grows in exact proportion to the programme's success** and better surveillance does not fix it, because better surveillance counts more of both kinds.
It ends inseparable, at scale, from the events that are real.

## Tell

Would have happened anyway.
There is no way to say so about any particular one.
