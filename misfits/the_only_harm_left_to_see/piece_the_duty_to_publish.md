# Piece: The Duty to Publish

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). The obligation to count and report the programme's own harms, which is right and is not negotiable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the register](place_the_register.md), in the case definitions and the reporting forms.
Discharged by [reporting](process_reporting.md), continuously and by design.

## Load Bearing

Without it somebody in this production is concealing something and the play becomes an argument about honesty.
It is load bearing because it makes the visible half **a duty rather than a leak**: the events are current, countable and described precisely because the system that finds them is working, and no version of this asks anybody to count fewer.

## Apparent

The programme's vulnerability, and something a cannier operator would manage.
It is the difference between a public health service and a promise. **The honesty is the mechanism and not a flaw in it**, which is why the play has no villain available and does not want one.

## Yearbook

Built into every serious immunisation programme, spent on the side of the ledger that can be read.
It is what puts a number on one quantity and leaves the other with none.
