## Stage

Confidence falls, and coverage falls with it, in a population where nothing has gone wrong.

## Tension

This is where the harm lands, and it lands on somebody reasoning correctly.
They have read what is published. The reported harms are current, quantified and real; the disease is absent, unwitnessed and unquantifiable to anybody who never met it. **Their evidence is genuine and their conclusion follows from it**, and the missing half is missing because the programme worked.
So nothing here is corrected by better information: the information is already correct, and one side of the comparison has no representative.
And the timing is demographic rather than epidemiological. Memory leaves at one cohort per generation, which is slower than the incidence curve, so the fall in confidence arrives long after the success that caused it. **The people who would argue against it are the people who remember**, and they are leaving the population at exactly the rate that drives the mechanism.
