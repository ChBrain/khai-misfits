# Piece: The Counterfactual

## Taxonomy

[The Only Harm Left to See](play_the_only_harm_left_to_see.md). The whole of the benefit, in the one form that cannot be exhibited.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Nowhere, which is the point.
Held by [the vanished disease](persona_the_vanished_disease.md), and absent from [the register](place_the_register.md) by construction.

## Load Bearing

Without it the benefit and the cost are two quantities of the same kind and can be compared.
It is load bearing because they are not the same kind: the cost is a set of events and the benefit is a set of non-events, so **the comparison the whole programme rests on has one side that no record can carry.**

## Apparent

An accounting inconvenience, fixed by modelling the averted cases.
The models exist, are good, and are a further thing nobody has seen. An estimate of what did not happen persuades in a way that a child in a ward does, which is to say not at all comparably.

## Yearbook

Delivered by every successful preventive programme, entered in none of their records.
It is what makes prevention structurally worse at arguing for itself than response is.
