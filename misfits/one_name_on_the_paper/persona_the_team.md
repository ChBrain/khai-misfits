# Persona: The Team

## Taxonomy

[Team](position_team.md). The unit that actually performs the work.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A task, several people, and the instruments they use.
Knowledge held between them that no one of them holds.

## Action

Solves it together.
Keeps its capability in the relations rather than in the heads.

## Shadow

Is the performing unit and is not a legal person.
Nothing about this is a shortcut or a crutch: it is the ordinary architecture of skilled work and the thing an employer is buying. And it dissolves when the members disperse, so there is nobody left for a certificate to be about even where a mark was earned honestly by all of them.

## Tell

Does the work and cannot hold the qualification for it.
