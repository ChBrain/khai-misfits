# Persona: The Peer Rater

## Taxonomy

[Peer Rater](position_peer_rater.md). The remedy's instrument, and its thinnest point.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A group mark, a form, and a request to say who did what.
Whatever they happened to see of the others' work.

## Action

Rates the others.
Supplies the weighting that turns one mark into several.

## Shadow

Knows least about the others in precisely the cases the weighting exists for.
The member who contributed least has the least visibility of what anybody else did, so the informant is thinnest exactly where the splitting has to happen; and the ratings are given on scales each rater reads differently, by people with an interest in the answer, which is why no method has been made to work across the ordinary cases.

## Tell

Is asked to measure a contribution they were not present for.
