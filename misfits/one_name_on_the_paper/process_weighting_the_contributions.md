# Process: Weighting the Contributions

## Taxonomy

[One Name on the Paper](play_one_name_on_the_paper.md). The working remedy, and where it is thinnest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A group mark, and a requirement that individuals be marked.
[The Peer Rater](persona_the_peer_rater.md), holding [Peer Rater](position_peer_rater.md), after [the project](place_the_project.md).

## Direction

Toward individual marks recovered from a shared one.

## Lever

Peer observation, which is the only source of information about who did what, and is held unequally by the people asked for it.

## Echo

A defensible split in the easy cases, and an informant who saw least in exactly the cases that needed one.
