## Taxonomy

## Shown

Everything working: this is the practice the institution teaches and the employer is buying.

## Holds

## Offers

The real capability, exercised in its real form, which is the thing anybody actually wants to know about.

## Withheld

Any attribution.
The place performs the task well and leaves no record of whose the performance was, because the performance was not anybody's: what one member contributed is not separable from what the arrangement made possible, and the arrangement disperses at the end of the term with nothing to certify and nobody to certify it to.
