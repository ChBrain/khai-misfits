## Place

## Load Bearing

It is the real remedy and its informants are least informed exactly where it is needed.
Individual weighting factors from peer and self rating are the standard answer and are what makes group assessment defensible at all, and they rest on peer observation: the member who contributed least saw least of what the others did, the scales are read differently by every rater, and everyone rating has an interest in the result. So the instrument works in the cases that would have been obvious anyway and thins toward the cases it exists for.
