# Plot: The Same Act Is Collaboration and Is Cheating

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

Two people working a problem out between them.

## Action

The act is performed in [the project](place_the_project.md), and then again inside the interval that is being measured.

## Stage

The first is the practice being taught and the second is the offence.

## Tension

Nothing about the act changes, so the line is institutional rather than moral, and it has to be.
The behaviour, the reasoning and the learning are identical on both occasions; what differs is only whether a claim about a person is being constructed at the time. That is why academic-integrity codes have to define collaboration and collusion by permission rather than by conduct, and why the boundary reads as arbitrary to the people crossing it while being exactly right.
