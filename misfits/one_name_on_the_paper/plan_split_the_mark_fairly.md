# Plan: Split the Mark Fairly

## Taxonomy

A **plain intent**: the intent of [The Peer Rater](persona_the_peer_rater.md), inside [One Name on the Paper](play_one_name_on_the_paper.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The Peer Rater](persona_the_peer_rater.md)

## Direction

Turn one mark for a group into marks for the people in it.

## Orders

(Receive the group mark; rate each member; compute a weighting; multiply through; issue the individual marks.)

## Implementation

([Weighting the contributions](process_weighting_the_contributions.md); [the weighting](piece_the_weighting.md), computed from what people happened to see.)

## Targets

- [ ] the remedy is real, is the standard practice, and is what makes group assessment defensible at all
- [ ] the ratings come from members who saw different amounts of what the others did
- [ ] and the member who contributed least saw least, so the informant is thinnest where the splitting matters
- [ ] with scales read differently by each rater and an interest in the answer held by all of them
