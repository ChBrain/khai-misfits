# Play: One Name on the Paper

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

One Name on the Paper.
A certificate says that a named person can do a thing. That is what it is for, what an employer reads it as, and what the candidate came for. The work it certifies is done, everywhere it is actually done, by people together with each other and with their instruments. So on the one morning the claim is tested, the thing the claim is about has to be taken apart.

## Arc

The bend is this: a certificate is a claim about a person, and the capability it names lives in a coupling.
It runs on what a certificate is.
[The Certificate](piece_the_certificate.md) attests to an individual, because that is the only form in which it is any use: [The Employer](persona_the_employer.md) is hiring one person and cannot hire a study group, and [The Candidate](persona_the_candidate.md) came for a document with their own name on it.
And the work is done by the coupling.
[The Team](persona_the_team.md) in [the project](place_the_project.md) holds between its members what no member holds alone, which is not a shortcut but the ordinary architecture of skilled work, and the thing [The Employer](persona_the_employer.md) is actually buying.
Then [The Same Act Is Collaboration and Is Cheating](plot_the_same_act_is_collaboration_and_is_cheating.md).
Two people working a problem out between them is the practice being taught on Tuesday and the offence on Thursday, and nothing about the act changes: what changes is whether it is being measured.
And [Forbidding It Is Correct](plot_forbidding_it_is_correct.md).
[The Awarder](persona_the_awarder.md) is not being petty. A result that cannot be attributed certifies nobody, so the document the candidate came for would not exist, and the prohibition is what makes the thing worth having rather than an anxiety about honesty.
And a shared mark does not solve it. [The Group Mark Certifies Nobody](plot_the_group_mark_certifies_nobody.md): it is a true statement about a team that has already dispersed, offered to an employer hiring one of them.
And [the ones who know least are asked to split it](plot_and_the_ones_who_know_least_are_asked_to_split_it.md). [The Peer Rater](persona_the_peer_rater.md) supplies [the weighting](piece_the_weighting.md) that turns a group mark into individual ones, and the members who contributed least are the members with least visibility of what anybody else did, so the instrument is thinnest exactly where it is being asked to do the work.

## Triggers

**[A Certificate Is a Claim About One Person](plot_a_certificate_is_a_claim_about_one_person.md)**
Cue: a document with a name on it, read by somebody deciding whether to hire that name. Closes when the individual form has been shown to be the only useful one, since an employer hires one person and the candidate came for a paper of their own.

**[The Work Is Done by the Coupling](plot_the_work_is_done_by_the_coupling.md)**
Cue: a real task, in a real setting, being carried out. Closes when the performing unit has been located across people and their instruments rather than inside one head, so distributed working is the ordinary architecture of skilled work and not a shortcut.

**[The Same Act Is Collaboration and Is Cheating](plot_the_same_act_is_collaboration_and_is_cheating.md)**
Cue: two people working a problem out between them. Closes when the act has been found identical on both days, so what separates the practice from the offence is only whether it is being measured.

**[Forbidding It Is Correct](plot_forbidding_it_is_correct.md)**
Cue: an examinations regulation, read as pettiness. Closes when an unattributable result has been shown to certify nobody, so the prohibition is what makes the document worth having rather than a suspicion about anybody's honesty.

**[The Group Mark Certifies Nobody](plot_the_group_mark_certifies_nobody.md)**
Cue: a shared mark, offered as the obvious answer. Closes when it has been found to be a true statement about a team that no longer exists, handed to an employer hiring one of its members.

**[And the Ones Who Know Least Are Asked to Split It](plot_and_the_ones_who_know_least_are_asked_to_split_it.md)**
Cue: a peer rating form, which is the working remedy. Closes when the members who contributed least have been found to have least visibility of what the others did, so the instrument is thinnest exactly where the splitting has to happen.

## Stakes

Whether a document about one person can attest to a capability that belongs to several.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a certificate that is only useful in the individual form, a capability that lives between people and their instruments, an act that is the practice and the offence without changing, a prohibition that is what makes the document mean anything, a shared mark that attests about nobody, and a remedy whose informants are least informed where it matters, and away from any account in which an institution is anxious about honesty or a student is looking for an easy way through. The production cannot fault an awarding body for insisting the work be one person's, because a result nobody can attribute is a result nobody can be hired on. What is left to fight over is not whether attribution is required, it is, but what to call an institution that has to suspend the practice it teaches in order to certify that it taught it.
