# One Name on the Paper: Reference

The research warrant for One Name on the Paper.
The term is the house's; the play runs on distributed cognition and on what a certificate has to exclude in
order to attest to a person, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging certification as **a claim about a person laid over a capability that belongs to a coupling**: a
certificate is useful only in the individual form, because an employer hires one person and cannot hire a
study group, and the candidate came for a document with their own name on it. The capability it names is
exercised, everywhere it is really exercised, across people and their instruments: who knows what, who to ask,
what the record holds, how the checking is divided. So on the hours in which the claim is constructed, the
arrangement the capability consists of has to be taken apart, and the same act is the practice on Tuesday and
the offence on Thursday with nothing about it changed. The play models the documented mechanics, the document
whose usefulness and whose unit are one property, the coupling that holds what no member holds, the separation
that obtains attribution, the shared mark that attests about a team already dispersed, and the peer rating
whose informants are least informed where it matters, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a
tale of an institution anxious about honesty or a student looking for a way through.

The governing law is that attribution requires a bearer and a joint performance has none. What one member
contributed is not separable from what the arrangement made possible, so a result produced by a coupling
cannot be attributed at all, and a certificate that attributes nothing certifies nobody.

**The prohibition is what makes the document worth having, which is what removes the last party anybody could
blame.** It is not a preference for the old ways and not a suspicion about anybody: the awarding body is
buying the only property that makes its award mean anything, at the price of suspending the practice the same
institution spends the rest of the year teaching.

What removes any possibility of blame from the candidate is that they want the individual form. A certificate
about a team would not get them hired, so the unit is not imposed on them but is the thing they came for, and
what it costs them is that the best of what they can do, which is what they can do with other people, is the
part that cannot appear.

What removes it from the employer is that both of their requirements are correct. They read the individual
line because there is no way to hire a coupling, the other members being unavailable and unwilling; and what
they are paying for is how this person will work with whoever is already there, which is exactly the property
the document had to exclude to be about them.

And the obvious remedy declines the question rather than answering it. A shared mark is true, is fair, and
describes an arrangement that has ended by the time anybody reads it, so it cannot say which of the four
people in front of the employer is the reason.

It is distinct from the house's **Social Loafing**, which is not a sibling of this misfit but its
**premise**: there individual effort is unobservable inside a group and per-head contribution declines, which
is precisely why attribution is required rather than optional. This production takes that as established and
stages what the requirement costs, so the two sit end to end: one says why individual contribution must be
made visible, and this one says what making it visible removes. Distinction only.

It is distinct from the house's **Marked the Same by Anyone**, its nearest sibling in the house, where
consistency between markers caps how valid an assessment can be: both stage a constraint that an assessment
cannot relax without destroying itself, and the constraints are different. There the binding property is
**reproducibility**, and the casualty is the range of performance an instrument can reach. Here it is
**attribution**, and the casualty is a mode of working rather than a range of judgement. An assessment could
satisfy either one and fail the other, and a viva scored by one examiner attributes perfectly and reproduces
badly.

It is distinct from the house's **Too Dear to Fake**, where a signal is credible because it is costly to the
sender and cheap to nobody else: there the question is why a certificate is **believed**, and the answer is
the cost of obtaining it. Here the question is what it can be **about**, and the answer is fixed by needing a
bearer. A signalling account is compatible with everything in this play and does not reach the difficulty,
because a perfectly credible signal about a team is still a signal about nobody who can be hired.

It is distinct from the house's **The Bus Factor**, where knowledge concentrated in one person leaves an
organisation exposed when they go: there the trouble is that a capability really does sit in an individual and
the organisation wishes it did not. Here the trouble is the reverse. The capability does not sit in an
individual, and the certificate requires that it did, so one misfit is about concentration that is real and
unwanted, and this one about individuation that is wanted and unreal.

It is distinct from the house's **Treated and Counted**, where care and measurement happen in the same hour so
the patient cannot tell which they are receiving: the shape is close, two purposes inside one act, and the
resolution differs. There they run **together** and the harm is the confusion between them. Here they cannot
run together at all: the institution must stop teaching in order to measure, and everybody in the hall knows
exactly which is happening, so nothing is confused and the cost is paid in the open.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the awarding body
  as anxious about honesty or the student as looking for a way through, because nobody in the sequence has a
  decision to get wrong: the individual form is the only useful one and is wanted by every party including the
  one it costs; the coupling is the ordinary architecture of skilled work and the thing an employer is buying;
  the separation obtains the only property that makes an award mean anything; the shared mark is true and
  describes something that has dispersed; and the peer rating is a real method whose informants are unequally
  informed. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that an
  institution must suspend the practice it teaches in order to certify that it taught it.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Individual weighting factors
  from peer rating are the working remedy, are standard, and are what makes group assessment defensible at
  all, and they thin toward the cases they exist for, since the member who contributed least saw least. A
  shared group mark declines the question the certificate exists to answer. An oral defence individuates well
  and consumes examiner time that scales with the cohort, and is scored by one examiner, which trades this
  constraint for the one **Marked the Same by Anyone** stages. Certifying the team rather than the person
  produces a document nobody can hire on. And knowing the mechanism completely leaves the employer still
  hiring one person.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the distributed-cognition analysis, the transactive-memory
  account, the effort measurements, the integrity research and the peer-assessment methods are theirs. The
  bounds are carried with them: the distributed account is a **framework for analysis** rather than a claim
  that individual competence does not exist, and the play stages the difficulty of attributing a joint
  performance and never a claim that nobody knows anything on their own; **whether individual marks can be
  recovered from group work is genuinely contested**, methods vary widely in design and the evidence on their
  behaviour is mixed, so the play stages the direction and never a rate; the integrity literature is cited for
  how the collaboration boundary is drawn in practice and not for any claim about how common cheating is;
  assessment traditions differ greatly in how much joint work they certify and the play does not stage that
  variation; and treating certification as one act with one hall is the play's own simplification. **The play
  makes no claim that examinations are the wrong instrument or that group work is under-assessed**, only that
  a certificate needs a bearer, that a joint performance has none, and that the hours in which the bearer is
  established are hours in which the capability cannot be shown.
