# Plot: The Group Mark Certifies Nobody

## Taxonomy

[One Name on the Paper](play_one_name_on_the_paper.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A shared mark, offered as the obvious answer.

## Action

[The Team](persona_the_team.md) is marked as a unit, honestly, on work all of them did.

## Stage

The mark is true, and is about something that no longer exists.

## Tension

A true statement about a dispersed team is handed to an employer hiring one of its members.
Nothing is unfair in it and nothing is inaccurate: the work was good and the mark says so. What it cannot say is which of the four people in front of the employer is the reason, and by the time anybody asks, the arrangement the mark describes has ended and cannot be reassembled. So the obvious remedy is not a compromise between attribution and reality: it simply declines the question the certificate exists to answer.
