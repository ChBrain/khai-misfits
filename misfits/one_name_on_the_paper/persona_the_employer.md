# Persona: The Employer

## Taxonomy

[Employer](position_employer.md). The one who reads the document and hires one person.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A vacancy for a person, and a certificate about a person.
Work that will be done in a team from the first morning.

## Action

Reads the individual line.
Hires for a capability exercised jointly.

## Shadow

Asks for the individual form and needs the joint capability, and both are reasonable.
There is no way to hire a coupling, since the other members are not available and would not come; and the thing being paid for is how this person works with whoever is already there, which is exactly what the document had to exclude to be about them.

## Tell

Buys a joint capability using a certificate that had to leave it out.
