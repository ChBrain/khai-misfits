## Tension

The instrument thins toward exactly the cases it exists for, which is what stops this resolving.
Individual weighting factors from peer rating are the standard answer and are what makes group assessment defensible at all. They rest on peer observation, and the member who contributed least saw least of what anybody else did, so the informant is weakest in the case the weighting was built for. Add scales each rater reads differently, and an interest in the answer held by all of them, and no method has been made to work across the ordinary run of cases.
