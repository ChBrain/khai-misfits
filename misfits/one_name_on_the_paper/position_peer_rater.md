# Position: Peer Rater

## Taxonomy

[One Name on the Paper](play_one_name_on_the_paper.md). The seat asked to say who did what.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A group mark, a form, and whatever it happened to see of the others' work.

## Orders

Rate them. Supply the weighting.

## Loses

Its own standing, and it knows least where the answer matters most.
The member who contributed least has least visibility of what anybody else did, and the scales are read differently by every occupant of the seat, all of whom have an interest in the answer, which is why no method has been made to work across the ordinary cases.

## Drives

Toward a split of the mark, measured by somebody who was not there for it.
