# Piece: The Coupling

## Taxonomy

[The Project](place_the_project.md). What the team holds between its members and none of them holds alone.

## Owner

## Place

Formed in [the project](place_the_project.md) by [working the way work is done](process_working_the_way_work_is_done.md), and dispersed at the end of it.

## Load Bearing

It is the capability itself and it has no bearer, which is why nothing can be issued about it.
Knowledge in real settings sits across people and their instruments: who knows what, who to ask, what the notes hold, how the checking is divided. That is not scaffolding around a competence located in somebody, it is the competence, and it is the thing an employer is buying. And it ends when the members disperse, leaving a real performance with nobody it can be attributed to.

## Apparent

Teamwork. A soft skill, alongside the hard ones.
