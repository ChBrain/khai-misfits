# Persona: The Candidate

## Taxonomy

[Candidate](position_candidate.md). The one who came for a document with their own name on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Years of work, much of it done with other people, as work is.
A hiring decision that will turn on a single line.

## Action

Works with others while learning.
Sits alone while being measured.

## Shadow

Wants precisely the thing that requires the separation.
A certificate about a team would not get them hired, so the individual form is not imposed on this seat but is what it came for, and the price of it is that the best of what they can do, which is what they can do with other people, is the part that cannot be shown.

## Tell

Is measured alone on a capability they will never again exercise alone.
