# Process: Balancing

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). The identity holding, generation after generation, with nothing enforcing it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The fact that every offspring has one parent of each sex.
At [the next generation](place_the_next_generation.md), whenever anybody counts.

## Direction

Toward equality of the two sexes' totals, necessarily.
This is not a tendency, a pressure or an equilibrium: it is true exactly, immediately and without exception, in every species under every mating system, because it is a property of counting rather than of biology.
And it is the reason everything downstream is unavoidable, since a fact of arithmetic has nothing for adaptation to act on.

## Lever

Nothing at all: the step has no lever, which is precisely its force.
Because there is no mechanism here, there is nothing to select on, negotiate with or work around.

## Echo

The average return per individual set as the inverse of how many of that sex there are.
Darwin set this argument out in 1871 and withdrew it in 1874 as too intricate to solve, which is worth remembering before calling any of it obvious.
