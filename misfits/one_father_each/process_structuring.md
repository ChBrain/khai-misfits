# Process: Structuring

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). The population breaking into patches, and the arithmetic changing with it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Mating that happens locally, before dispersal.
At [the patch](place_the_patch.md), among the offspring of one or a few mothers.

## Direction

Toward a strongly female-biased ratio, and correctly.
The identity has not been broken and nothing here is an exception to it: a son's rivals are now his own brothers, so an extra son takes matings from the mother's other sons rather than from the population, and the return on him collapses.
So the theory predicted the departure before it was measured, and the wasps that live this way lay the clutches it predicts, which is what makes this evidence rather than an escape hatch.

## Lever

Population structure, which is not a decision and not adopted.
Because the bias follows from the structure rather than from a strategy, no species that mixes can take it up.

## Echo

The ratio that produces the most offspring, obtained by the one route that does not require anybody to want it.
The size of the bias tracks how few mothers share the patch, which is the quantitative form of the prediction and the reason it can be tested.
