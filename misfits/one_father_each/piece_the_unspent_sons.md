# Piece: The Unspent Sons

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). The reproductive investment that is made in full, grown to independence, and returns nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the lek](place_the_lek.md), in any species where a few males take nearly all the matings.
Answered nowhere: the expectation that justified the investment is unaffected by how it turned out.

## Load Bearing

Without it the misfit is a curiosity about ratios rather than a cost.
It is load bearing because it names **what the population actually pays**: half the reproductive investment, in a strongly polygynous species, lands on individuals who leave nothing, and the identity guarantees that no amount of skew makes that a worse bet, since variance does not move the average.

## Apparent

Ordinary reproductive failure, of the sort every population has.
It reads as the losing tail of a competition, and the fact that the tail is most of the distribution and was paid for at full price is not visible from any individual in it.

## Yearbook

Paid at [allocating](process_allocating.md), realised at [the lek](place_the_lek.md).
It is the quantity [the better ratio](persona_the_better_ratio.md) would remove, and the reason removing it would be worth so much.
