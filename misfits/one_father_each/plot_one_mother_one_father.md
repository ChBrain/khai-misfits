# Plot: One Mother, One Father

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The arithmetic nobody can break.
[The Identity](piece_the_identity.md), true in every species and every generation.

## Action

[Balancing](process_balancing.md): [The Tally](persona_the_tally.md), in [the ledger seat](position_ledger.md), comes out even without being kept by anybody.

## Stage

[The Tally](persona_the_tally.md), on [Balance Every Generation](plan_balance_every_generation.md), sets the total reproductive output of all males equal to the total of all females.
It does this whatever the numbers of each, whatever the mating system, and whatever anybody would prefer.

## Tension

The cause of everything that follows sits outside biology.
This is a property of counting, not of organisms, so there is nothing here for selection to act on, nothing to adapt around and no species exempt from it. And it has an immediate consequence that does not look like arithmetic at all: because the total is fixed, **the average return per individual is inversely proportional to how many of that sex there are**. A population at nine females to one male gives each male nine times the average return of a female. Nobody set that price and every parent can read it.
