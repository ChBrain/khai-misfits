# Persona: The Rare Sex

## Taxonomy

[Premium](position_premium.md). Whichever sex is currently scarcer, and therefore returns more per head without doing anything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary in every respect except how many of it there are.
Not better, not fitter and not preferred: simply fewer, against a total output fixed by the identity.
Would be worth exactly as much as the other sex if there were as many.

## Action

Averages more offspring per individual, in exact proportion to its scarcity.
Makes itself the better thing for a parent to produce.
Stops being rare, by that very fact, and stops being worth more in the same motion.

## Shadow

Its own premium, and it loses it by earning it: the advantage exists only while the scarcity does, and the advantage is precisely what removes the scarcity.
Because the premium is generated by the identity rather than by any quality of the individuals, nothing about them is improving or declining as it appears and goes.
It ends at parity, worth the same as the other sex, which is where it was always going.

## Tell

Says nothing, and needs to say nothing:
being fewer is the whole of the signal, and it is read by every parent without being sent.
