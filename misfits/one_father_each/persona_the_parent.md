# Persona: The Parent

## Taxonomy

[Allocator](position_allocator.md). The one seat with a decision in it, and the decision is correct: spend where the return is higher.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Has a finite amount to spend and spends it well.
Not choosing between the population's interest and its own, because nothing tells it there is a difference and there would be nothing to do about it.
Doing the same thing every organism does with limited resources.

## Action

Allocates reproductive effort between sons and daughters.
Puts more into whichever currently returns more, which is whichever is rarer.
Leaves more grandchildren for it, which is the only sense in which it is right.

## Shadow

Nothing, and it is the mechanism's only moving part: at the scale of one parent this is ordinary good allocation, and at the scale of a population it is the force that destroys the better ratio.
Because no parent's contribution is large enough to move anything visibly, there is no point at which any of them could reasonably decide otherwise, and nothing they could be asked to refrain from.
It ends having allocated correctly, and having helped hold the population at the allocation that produces fewest offspring.

## Tell

Says: this is where the return is.
It is. That is the whole account and there is nothing else in it.
