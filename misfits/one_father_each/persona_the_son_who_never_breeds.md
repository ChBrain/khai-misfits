# Persona: The Son Who Never Breeds

## Taxonomy

[Unreturned](position_unreturned.md). Most of them, in a species where a few males do nearly all the breeding: grown to independence, and returning nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Healthy, fully grown and entirely unremarkable.
Not defective, not weak and not unlucky in any way that could have been foreseen when the investment was made.
The expected outcome for a male in a strongly polygynous species, rather than a failure.

## Action

Is conceived, carried, fed and grown to independence, at full cost.
Competes for matings, and does not get any.
Leaves no offspring, which most males in such a species do not.

## Shadow

Everything that was spent making it, and the seat is where the population's cost is actually paid: the investment was correct in expectation and returned nothing in fact, and both of those are true at once because the expectation is an average over a distribution most of whose mass is zero.
Because variance in male success does not change the male average, which the identity fixes, no amount of skew makes producing sons a worse bet.
It ends having cost as much as a daughter and returned less than one.

## Tell

Says nothing.
There is no moment in its life at which anything went wrong, which is why nothing is recorded against it.
