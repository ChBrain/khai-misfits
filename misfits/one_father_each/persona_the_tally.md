# Persona: The Tally

## Taxonomy

[Ledger](position_ledger.md). The count that comes out even: every offspring has one mother and one father, so the two sexes' totals match exactly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not a force, not a rule and not a law of biology: a fact about counting.
True in every species, under every mating system, in every generation, with no exceptions and no conditions.
Nothing about it is contingent, which is why nothing can be done about it.

## Action

Balances, once per generation, without being kept by anybody.
Sets the total reproductive output of all males equal to the total of all females, whatever the numbers of each.
Makes the average return per individual inversely proportional to how many of that sex there are.

## Shadow

Nothing, and it authors the whole structure from outside biology: the seat has no interest, no mechanism and no location, and the outcome follows from it the way a sum follows from its terms.
Because it is arithmetic rather than adaptation, no selection can act on it, no species escapes it and no argument reaches it.
It ends having balanced, exactly, as it does every generation.

## Tell

Says nothing, being a count.
Its whole statement is that two totals are the same, which sounds like nothing and fixes everything.
