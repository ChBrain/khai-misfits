# Process: Allocating

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). A parent spending finite reproductive effort where the return is highest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A price on each sex, set by the ratio.
At [the lek](place_the_lek.md) for one and at breeding for the other, realised as grandchildren.

## Direction

Toward the rarer sex, which is the correct direction.
Fisher's refinement is what makes this exact: it is equal **investment** rather than equal numbers, measured at the age of independence, so a sex that is cheaper to rear is produced in larger numbers and the two still cost the same in total.
And nothing at this step is a judgement about the population, because no parent's allocation moves the ratio perceptibly and none could be asked to refrain from anything.

## Lever

The effort available, which is finite, and the price, which is read rather than chosen.
Because the constraint is a budget and the signal is a ratio, competence at this step consists entirely in reading the ratio correctly.

## Echo

More offspring of the rarer sex, and the ratio moving back.
The allocation is right at the scale it is made and is the force holding the population at its least productive mixture at the scale it accumulates.
