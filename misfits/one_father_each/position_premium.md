# Position: Premium

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). The seat of scarcity converted into per-head return, automatically and by nobody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Fewer individuals than the other sex.
A total output fixed by the identity regardless of that number.
Nothing else: no quality, no advantage and no merit.

## Orders

Return more per individual, in exact proportion to the scarcity.
Become the better thing for a parent to produce.
Cease to be rare, and cease to be worth more, in the same motion.

## Loses

Its own premium, by earning it: the advantage exists only while the scarcity does, and taking the advantage is what ends the scarcity, so the seat is self-cancelling by construction.
Because the premium is a property of the identity rather than of the individuals, nothing about them changes as it appears and goes.
It ends at parity, worth exactly what the other sex is worth.

## Drives

Toward nothing it wants,
never once competing for it,
so the seat that is worth most is the one with nothing to do with why.
