# Place: The Patch

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). Where the mating pool is not the population, and the ratio therefore goes where the rest of the play cannot take it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The offspring of one or a few mothers, on a single host or a single fruit.
Mating among them before the females disperse.

## Holds

The exception, and the evidence.
It holds the case where the assumption of a mixed mating pool fails: a son competes only with his brothers, so additional sons take matings from each other rather than from the population, and the favoured clutch is strongly female-biased, which is what is laid and what is observed.

## Offers

A ratio close to the one that maximises offspring, obtained without any coordination and without any species deciding anything.

## Withheld

Portability.
The bias is a consequence of the structure and not of a strategy, so nothing that mixes can adopt it, and the exception is available only to whatever already lives this way.
