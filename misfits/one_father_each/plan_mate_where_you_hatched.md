# Plan: Mate Where You Hatched

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the structured seat](position_structured.md), inside [One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Structured](position_structured.md)

## Direction

Live in a patch rather than a population. Let your sons compete only with their own brothers, and lay the strongly female-biased clutch that the arithmetic of that situation actually favours.

## Orders

([The Wasp on the Fig](persona_the_wasp_on_the_fig.md) lays mostly daughters and just enough sons to fertilise them, and sends the daughters out to found patches of their own.)

## Implementation

([The Patch](place_the_patch.md), which is not a mating pool; the [structuring](process_structuring.md) process; the departure recorded as [the broken assumption](piece_the_broken_assumption.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] mating is local and precedes dispersal, so the pool is the patch
- [ ] a son's rivals are his brothers, which changes the arithmetic rather than suspending it
- [ ] the female bias tracks how few mothers share the patch
- [ ] the exception is a fact about structure and cannot be adopted by a species that mixes
