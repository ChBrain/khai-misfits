# Plan: Balance Every Generation

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the ledger seat](position_ledger.md), inside [One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Ledger](position_ledger.md)

## Direction

Come out even, because there is no other way for the count to come out. Make the two sexes' totals equal whatever their numbers, and thereby make the average return per individual inversely proportional to how many there are.

## Orders

([The Tally](persona_the_tally.md) balances once per generation without being kept by anybody, in every species and under every mating system, with no exceptions and no conditions.)

## Implementation

([The Next Generation](place_the_next_generation.md), where the count is taken; the [balancing](process_balancing.md) process holding [the identity](piece_the_identity.md); the per-head consequence appearing as [the rare-sex premium](piece_the_rare_sex_premium.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] every offspring has exactly one mother and exactly one father
- [ ] the two sexes' total reproductive output is therefore equal by construction
- [ ] the average return per individual is inversely proportional to the number of that sex
- [ ] it is arithmetic rather than adaptation, so nothing selects on it and nothing escapes it
