# Plan: Be Worth More for Being Fewer

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the premium seat](position_premium.md), inside [One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Premium](position_premium.md)

## Direction

Return more per head, in exact proportion to how few of you there are, without being better in any respect. Lose the advantage by taking it, since being worth more is what stops you being rare.

## Orders

([The Rare Sex](persona_the_rare_sex.md) averages more offspring per individual, becomes the better thing for a parent to produce, and ceases to be rare in the same motion.)

## Implementation

([The Next Generation](place_the_next_generation.md), where the average is realised; the [pricing](process_pricing.md) process converting scarcity into return; the result driving [restoring](process_restoring.md) through [the restoring force](piece_the_restoring_force.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the premium follows from the identity and from nothing about the individuals
- [ ] it is proportional to the scarcity and to nothing else
- [ ] taking the advantage is what removes the scarcity that created it
- [ ] the seat ends at parity, worth exactly what the other sex is worth
