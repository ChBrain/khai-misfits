# Persona: The Wasp on the Fig

## Taxonomy

[Structured](position_structured.md). The exception, and it is real: offspring of one or a few mothers, mating with each other before the females disperse.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Living in a patch rather than a population.
Its sons compete for matings with their own brothers and with nobody else, which is a different arithmetic entirely.
Not doing anything cleverer than anybody else: occupying a different structure.

## Action

Lays a strongly female-biased clutch, which is what the theory predicts for this structure and what is observed.
Produces just enough sons to fertilise the daughters.
Sends the daughters out, and they found patches of their own.

## Shadow

Nothing, and the seat is what makes the rest of the play evidence rather than assertion: the female bias appears exactly where the assumption of a mixed mating pool fails, and its size tracks how few mothers share the patch.
Because the exception is a fact about population structure rather than about anybody's behaviour, it cannot be adopted by a species that mixes.
It ends producing the ratio the rest of the play cannot reach, for a reason no other species can copy.

## Tell

Says nothing, and lays a clutch that says it:
mostly daughters, and enough sons to go round the patch.
