# Plan: Wait for a Mating That Does Not Come

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the unreturned seat](position_unreturned.md), inside [One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Unreturned](position_unreturned.md)

## Direction

Be grown to independence at full cost, compete for matings, and get none, which is what most males in a strongly polygynous species do. Leave nothing, having done nothing wrong.

## Orders

([The Son Who Never Breeds](persona_the_son_who_never_breeds.md) is conceived, carried, fed and grown at full cost, competes, and leaves no offspring.)

## Implementation

([The Lek](place_the_lek.md), where the matings are settled and most of them are not his; the shortfall accumulating as [the unspent sons](piece_the_unspent_sons.md); the expectation unchanged by any of it, since [the identity](piece_the_identity.md) fixes the male average.)

## Targets

- [ ] the cost of rearing is paid in full and is the same as a daughter's
- [ ] the outcome is the expected one for a male here, not a failure
- [ ] the investment was correct in expectation and returned nothing in fact
- [ ] variance in male success does not change the male average, so the bet is unaffected
