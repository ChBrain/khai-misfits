# Piece: Equal Investment

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). Fisher's refinement: the balance is struck in cost at the age of independence, not in numbers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [allocating](process_allocating.md), wherever the two sexes differ in what they cost to rear.
Answered nowhere: the total spent on each sex comes out equal whatever the head count.

## Load Bearing

Without it the principle predicts equal numbers and is wrong wherever the sexes cost differently.
It is load bearing because it makes the account **exact rather than approximate**: a sex that is cheaper to rear is produced in larger numbers and the two still cost the same in total, which is what turns a plausible argument into a testable one.

## Apparent

A technical qualification to a simple rule.
It reads as a footnote, and the fact that it is the whole difference between the rule being right and being roughly right is not what a footnote looks like.

## Yearbook

Added by Fisher in 1930 to an argument Carl Düsing had set out in 1884 and Darwin before him.
It governs [allocating](process_allocating.md) and is what [the identity](piece_the_identity.md) is actually denominated in.
