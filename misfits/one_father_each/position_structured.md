# Position: Structured

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). The seat where the assumption fails, and the ratio therefore goes where it should.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A patch rather than a population.
Offspring of one or a few mothers, mating with each other before the females disperse.
Sons whose only rivals are their own brothers.

## Orders

Lay a strongly female-biased clutch.
Make just enough sons to fertilise the daughters.
Send the daughters out to found patches of their own.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat is what turns the rest of the play from an argument into evidence: the female bias appears exactly where a mixed mating pool does not exist, and its extent tracks how few mothers share the patch.
Because the exception is a fact about population structure rather than about conduct, no species that mixes can adopt it.
It ends producing the ratio everything else cannot reach, for a reason nothing else can copy.

## Drives

Toward the same higher return as everybody else,
never once departing from the rule,
so the seat that breaks the pattern is the one obeying it most exactly.
