# Pitch: Worth More for Being Fewer

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry, arithmetical, unhurried.
The register of a ledger held against a population: how many of each, what each returns on average, what it cost to grow one to independence.
Nothing indignant anywhere in it, because there is no decision in the play to disapprove of.

## Undertow

Under the ratio, two facts that do not negotiate.
Every offspring has one mother and one father, so the two sexes' totals are equal by construction. A parent has a finite amount to spend.
Between those two, scarcity prices itself: the fewer there are of a sex, the more each one returns, and the more each one returns the fewer there stop being.

## Nerve

The unease of an outcome that would be better for everybody,
that no agreement could hold and no cheat is required to break,
because the better it gets the more a single individual gains by leaving it.

## Echo

After the run: a population at equal investment,
half of it grown to independence and returning nothing,
and the only place the ratio goes where it should being a patch of wasps that never mixed with anybody.
