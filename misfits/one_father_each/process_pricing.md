# Process: Pricing

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). Scarcity converting into per-head return, automatically and with nobody setting a price.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A population away from equal investment.
At [the next generation](place_the_next_generation.md), as a consequence of [the identity](piece_the_identity.md).

## Direction

Toward a higher average for whichever sex is rarer.
Nothing about the rarer individuals is better: the total output of their sex is fixed from outside, so dividing it among fewer of them raises each one's share, in exact proportion.
So a population at nine females to one male makes each male worth nine times a female, which is a price nobody quoted and every parent reads.

## Lever

The ratio itself, which is the only input.
Because the premium is generated by the identity rather than by any quality, it cannot be competed away, faked or held.

## Echo

The rarer sex becoming the better thing to produce, which is what ends its rarity.
[The rare-sex premium](piece_the_rare_sex_premium.md) is self-cancelling by construction: it exists only while the scarcity does and is what removes it.
