# Plan: Be the Best Ratio and Be Unstable

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the unreachable seat](position_unreachable.md), inside [One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Unreachable](position_unreachable.md)

## Direction

Produce more offspring per generation than parity does, requiring no restraint and no sacrifice from anybody, and be abandoned at a speed proportional to how much better you are.

## Orders

([The Better Ratio](persona_the_better_ratio.md) works perfectly while it lasts, makes a son worth many times a daughter by exactly the margin of its own superiority, and collapses.)

## Implementation

([The Next Generation](place_the_next_generation.md), where the collapse is counted; [restoring](process_restoring.md) acting on it through [the rare-sex premium](piece_the_rare_sex_premium.md); nothing at any point that could be called a defection.)

## Targets

- [ ] the outcome is better for the population by a large and unsubtle margin
- [ ] it requires no cooperation, no restraint and no agreement to be reached
- [ ] the better it is, the more an individual gains by leaving it
- [ ] no promise is broken when it collapses, so nothing that repairs cooperation touches it
