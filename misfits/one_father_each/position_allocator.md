# Position: Allocator

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). The only seat in the play with a decision in it, making it correctly every time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A finite amount of reproductive effort.
A choice of what to spend it on, sons or daughters.
A return that is higher on whichever sex is currently rarer.

## Orders

Spend where the return is higher.
Leave more grandchildren for it.
Contribute, invisibly and unavoidably, to the ratio moving back.

## Loses

Nothing, and the seat is the whole mechanism's moving part: this is ordinary good allocation at the scale it is made on, and the force that destroys the better ratio at the scale it accumulates on.
Because no single allocation moves anything visibly, there is no point at which the seat could reasonably decide otherwise and nothing it could be asked to refrain from.
It ends having been right, and having helped hold the population where it produces fewest offspring.

## Drives

Toward the higher return,
right in every single instance,
so the seat that never once errs is the reason the outcome cannot be improved.
