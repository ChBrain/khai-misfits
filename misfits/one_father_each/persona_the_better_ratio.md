# Persona: The Better Ratio

## Taxonomy

[Unreachable](position_unreachable.md). The female-biased allocation that would produce more offspring per generation, and cannot be held.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Genuinely better for the population, by a large margin, and not in any way subtle.
Requires no sacrifice from anybody and involves no restraint: it is simply a different mixture of offspring.
Would work perfectly if it stayed.

## Action

Produces more offspring per generation than parity does.
Makes a son worth many times a daughter, in exact proportion to how good it is.
Collapses, at whatever speed the difference warrants.

## Shadow

Its own stability, in proportion to its own merit: **the better the ratio is for the population, the more an individual gains by leaving it**, so the quality of the outcome and the strength of the force destroying it are the same quantity.
Because no promise was broken and nobody defected, this cannot be repaired by trust, agreement, punishment or communication.
It ends having existed as a possibility and never as a state.

## Tell

Says: everybody would be better off.
Which is true, and is exactly why nobody stays.
