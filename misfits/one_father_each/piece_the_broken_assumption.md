# Piece: The Broken Assumption

## Taxonomy

[One Father Each](play_one_father_each.md). The mixed mating pool, which the whole argument requires and which some species do not have.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the patch](place_the_patch.md), where mating is local and precedes dispersal.
Answered by the observation: the ratio there goes where the theory says it should.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a claim that cannot be tested against anything.
It is load bearing because it is **the case where the mechanism does not hold, predicted in advance**: a son whose rivals are his brothers takes matings from his own mother's other sons, so the return on him collapses and the favoured clutch is strongly female-biased, which is what is laid.

## Apparent

An exception, and a limit on the theory.
It reads as the place the account breaks down, and it is the place the account is confirmed, since the departure appears exactly where the assumption fails and in proportion to how badly.

## Yearbook

Located by [structuring](process_structuring.md), against [the identity](piece_the_identity.md), which is not broken there and merely applies to a different pool.
Its size tracks how few mothers share the patch, which is what makes the prediction quantitative.
