# Plot: Only Staying Upright

## Taxonomy

[Just Keeping Their Feet](play_just_keeping_their_feet.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A surface moving faintly sideways, and a person on it who would rather not stumble.

## Action

[Walking on](process_walking_on.md): [The Ones Staying Upright](persona_the_ones_staying_upright.md), holding [Ones Staying Upright](position_ones_staying_upright.md), widen their stance and take [the balance step](piece_the_balance_step.md), below the level of noticing.

## Stage

Nothing collective is intended, attempted or perceived. Most people report the bridge moving and do not report themselves moving with it.

## Tension

The correction cannot be declined, because declining it means falling over.
This happens faster than deliberation and beneath awareness, so no announcement, sign or marshal reaches it: telling a crowd to stop reacting to a moving floor is telling them not to keep their balance. And nobody here knows anything about anybody else, so whatever they are doing together is not something any of them is doing.
