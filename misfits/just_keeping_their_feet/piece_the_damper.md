# Piece: The Damper

## Taxonomy

[The Deck](place_the_deck.md). The retrofit that absorbs what cannot be prevented.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Fitted beneath and around [the deck](place_the_deck.md): thirty-seven viscous units under the walkway and at the piers, with tuned masses alongside.

## Load Bearing

It concedes that the mechanism cannot be removed and takes the energy away instead.
The conclusion reached was that the phenomenon could not be stopped, so the response was to raise the damping until the feedback can no longer outrun it: which works, and is an admission that the crowd will go on supplying energy for as long as there is a bridge and people on it.

## Apparent

Chevron dampers under the walkway, pier dampers, vertical units, and tuned masses: unglamorous, effective, and largely invisible to anybody crossing.
The thing that reopened the bridge.

## Yearbook

Specified after two years of investigation, at a cost, on a structure that had been complete and correct.
Solves the problem by changing the structure rather than the people, because the people were never the part that could be changed.
