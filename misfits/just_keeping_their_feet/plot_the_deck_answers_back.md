# Plot: The Deck Answers Back

## Taxonomy

[Just Keeping Their Feet](play_just_keeping_their_feet.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Sideways Mode](persona_the_sideways_mode.md) swaying faintly, at a rate close to how often a walking person shifts weight between feet.

## Action

[Feeding the mode](process_feeding_the_mode.md): the balance corrections arrive in phase with the motion that prompted them, because the deck rather than the walker sets their timing.

## Stage

The crowd stops being a load on the structure and becomes part of it: the structure supplies the signal and the walkers supply the energy.

## Tension

The load is a function of the response, so it does not exist as a number until both are present.
This is why [the drawing office](place_the_drawing_office.md) could not have tabulated it: every other load can be written down before the bridge is built, and this one is a conversation between the structure and the people, which cannot be held in advance. The coincidence of frequencies is not a tuning error either, but two unrelated pieces of physics, how long legs are and how long a slender span is, landing near each other.
