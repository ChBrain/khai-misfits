# Pitch: Nobody Pushed It

## Taxonomy

[Just Keeping Their Feet](play_just_keeping_their_feet.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Structural and calm. A slender deck designed properly, analysed against the resonance every engineer is taught, signed off, opened on a Saturday to a crowd that has waited for it.
Every calculation correct and every code provision applied.

## Undertow

Under the calculations, a floor that couples the people standing on it to each other, and a balance correction nobody can feel themselves making.
The load is not a load: it is a response to the structure's own motion, so it does not exist until the crowd and the deck are put together.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a crowd that is not doing anything, moving something that nobody is pushing.

## Echo

Afterward: two days open, two years shut, thirty-seven viscous dampers and tuned masses fitted because the phenomenon could not be prevented, and a premise that had been true of every bridge anybody had ever built.
