# Plot: Two Days

## Taxonomy

[Just Keeping Their Feet](play_just_keeping_their_feet.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Opening day, a celebration, several thousand people, and a deck moving enough that they take hold of the rails.

## Action

[The Ones Who Closed It](persona_the_ones_who_closed_it.md), holding [Ones Who Closed It](position_ones_who_closed_it.md), watch [the deck](place_the_deck.md) and shut the bridge within two days.

## Stage

It stays shut for two years.

## Tension

There was no calculation available to say whether this was dangerous or merely alarming.
The mode had not been anticipated by anybody, so this is a decision taken under genuine ignorance rather than a weighing of known risks: closing was the only defensible course, cost two years and a great deal of money, and reads from outside as an admission that somebody had done something wrong. The people most visibly answerable for the failure are the ones who responded to it correctly.
