# Piece: The Balance Step

## Taxonomy

[The Deck](place_the_deck.md). The involuntary correction, which is the energy source.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Taken on [the deck](place_the_deck.md) by everybody on it, continuously, without any of them being aware of it.

## Load Bearing

It is a response rather than an action, which is why nothing addresses it.
A person on a laterally moving surface widens their stance and places the next foot differently, and the timing of that is set by the deck rather than by the walker: so the force arrives in phase with the motion that prompted it, which is the definition of the thing growing, and it is produced by people who are only avoiding a stumble.

## Apparent

Nothing at all, from inside. Most people crossing report the bridge moving and do not report themselves moving with it.
Ordinary walking, slightly adjusted.

## Yearbook

Present in every person and impossible to suppress, since suppressing it means not staying upright.
Increases as the motion increases, so the response grows with the thing it is feeding, which is what turns a small sway into a large one.
