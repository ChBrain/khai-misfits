# Persona: The Ones Who Closed It

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Closed It](position_ones_who_closed_it.md). The operator, shutting a new bridge in two days.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The people responsible for a bridge that opened to great celebration on a Saturday and is visibly moving under the crowd.
Facing a decision with no precedent and no analysis to lean on.

## Action

Watches the deck sway and people take hold of the rails.
Closes it, within two days, and keeps it closed for two years.

## Shadow

Acts correctly on evidence nobody had modelled, and pays for it publicly.
There was no calculation available to say whether the motion was dangerous or merely alarming, and the mode had not been anticipated by anybody, so this persona is deciding under genuine ignorance rather than weighing known risks: closing was right, was expensive, and looked from outside like an admission that something had been done wrong.

## Tell

Makes the only defensible decision available and is the visible face of a failure nobody committed.
