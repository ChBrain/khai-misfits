# Persona: The Ones Staying Upright

## Taxonomy

[Ones Staying Upright](position_ones_staying_upright.md). The crowd, each of them only trying not to stumble.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Several thousand people crossing a new bridge on the day it opens, strangers to each other, going both ways.
Doing nothing but walking.

## Action

Widens stance when the surface moves, and places the next foot slightly differently.
Grips the handrail when it gets worse, which does not help.

## Shadow

Cannot feel the thing it is doing, and could not stop doing it if told.
Balance correction happens below the level of noticing and faster than deliberation, so this persona is not choosing to push, is not in step with anybody by intent, and cannot be instructed out of it: telling a crowd not to react to a moving floor is telling them to fall over.

## Tell

Is doing nothing collective whatever, and is collectively the entire load.
