# Process: Feeding the Mode

## Taxonomy

[Just Keeping Their Feet](play_just_keeping_their_feet.md). The loop closing between the deck and the people on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Sideways Mode](persona_the_sideways_mode.md) swaying faintly, and setting the timing of what the crowd does next.
Balance corrections arriving in phase with the motion that prompted them.

## Direction

Toward growth, since the response is set by the deck and therefore pushes with it rather than against it.
Toward more of the crowd correcting harder as the motion increases.

## Lever

Feedback: the load is a function of the response, so it does not exist as a number until both are present.

## Echo

A crowd that is not a load on the structure but part of it, the structure supplying the signal and the walkers the energy.
