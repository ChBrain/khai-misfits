# Persona: The Sideways Mode

## Taxonomy

[Sideways Mode](position_sideways_mode.md). The lateral motion, small and cheap to excite.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A natural mode of a slender deck, swaying side to side, requiring very little energy to grow.
Moving at a rate close to how often a walking person shifts weight from one foot to the other.

## Action

Sways, faintly, whenever anything gives it energy.
Sets the timing and direction of what the crowd above it will do next.

## Shadow

Coincides with the pace of walking for a reason nobody chose.
Its frequency and the rhythm of human gait are close because of how long legs are and how long a slender span is, so the coupling is not a design error, an oversight or an unlucky tuning: it is two unrelated pieces of ordinary physics landing near each other, and no adjustment to either was available to anybody.

## Tell

Is too small to matter until it is the thing organising everybody on top of it.
