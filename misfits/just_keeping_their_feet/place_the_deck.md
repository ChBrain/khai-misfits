# Place: The Deck

## Taxonomy

[Just Keeping Their Feet](play_just_keeping_their_feet.md). The floor, which is also the wiring between everybody on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A slender span, a walking surface, handrails, and several thousand strangers crossing in both directions on an opening Saturday.
A structure that has passed every check it was given.

## Holds

[The Sideways Mode](persona_the_sideways_mode.md), small, cheap to excite, and matched by accident to the rhythm of walking.
[The Balance Step](piece_the_balance_step.md), taken by everybody standing on it, below the level of noticing.

## Offers

A crossing, which is the entire purpose, and a structure adequate to every load anybody specified.
Perfect stillness whenever the number of people on it is small.

## Withheld

Any separation between the people it carries and the thing it does.
The place is not merely a surface bearing a load: it transmits each person's motion to every other person through their own sense of balance, so the crowd on it is coupled by the floor rather than by anything they know about each other, and there is no arrangement of the structure that carries people without also connecting them.
