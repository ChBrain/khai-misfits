## Load Bearing

It is the profession's memory of its own failures, and memory only holds what has happened.
Vertical pedestrian resonance is taught, codified and checked because bridges have failed that way and soldiers are told to break step: so the check is thorough, correct and universally applied, and it is aimed at the mode that has already caused trouble, which is the only mode any body of guidance can be aimed at.

## Apparent

Rigorous, standard, independently reviewed, and passed comfortably.
Exactly what a competent design process produces.

## Yearbook

Built out of a century of vertical incidents, each one added to the guidance afterwards.
Silent on the lateral case, for the stated reason that the force is an order of magnitude smaller and pedestrians do not act coherently.
