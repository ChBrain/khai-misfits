# Process: Walking On

## Taxonomy

[Just Keeping Their Feet](play_just_keeping_their_feet.md). A crowd arriving, doing nothing collective.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An opening day, and several thousand people crossing in both directions.
[The Ones Staying Upright](persona_the_ones_staying_upright.md), holding [Ones Staying Upright](position_ones_staying_upright.md), on [the deck](place_the_deck.md).

## Direction

Toward the far side, which is the whole of anybody's intention.
Toward [the balance step](piece_the_balance_step.md), taken continuously and below the level of noticing.

## Lever

Crowd size, which crosses [the number that starts it](persona_the_number_that_starts_it.md) on the first busy day and on none of the test days.

## Echo

A structure that passed every trial honestly, meeting for the first time the only condition under which the phenomenon exists.
