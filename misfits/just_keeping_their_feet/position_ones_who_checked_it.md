# Position: Ones Who Checked It

## Taxonomy

[Just Keeping Their Feet](play_just_keeping_their_feet.md). The seat that reasoned soundly from a premise that had never failed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Standards in force, built out of everything that had gone wrong before.
A lateral walking force roughly a tenth of the vertical one, and no record of pedestrians acting coherently.

## Orders

Analyse the vertical response.
Set the lateral aside, for stated reasons.

## Loses

Only what no record could have supplied.
Both halves of the reasoning are true: the lateral force is an order of magnitude smaller, and without coherence between pedestrians the response is negligible. The second had held on every bridge ever built, so setting the mode aside was an inference from the entire available evidence rather than an oversight, and the evidence was complete right up to the moment it was not.

## Drives

Toward checking against the failure everybody has already had, which is the only kind of failure anybody can check against.
