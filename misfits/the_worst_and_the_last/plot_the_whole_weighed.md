# Plot: The Whole Weighed

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The choice to return is at hand, and it is the remembered summary, not the lived hour, that drives it.

## Action

At [the Next Time](place_the_next_time.md), [the Weigher](persona_the_weigher.md) from [the weigher's seat](position_weigher.md) on [Weigh the Whole](plan_weigh_the_whole.md) through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) takes up [the Reckoning](piece_the_reckoning.md), setting the lived sum against the peak-and-end verdict that will decide whether to go back.

## Stage

The self that chooses is not the self that lived the episode: the experiencing self is gone the instant it ends, and the remembering self, holding only the peak and the end, is the one that tells the story and decides whether to repeat it, so the episode worse to live can be the one chosen again because it is the one remembered better (Kahneman). The reckoning here honors the whole too late, setting the plain lived sum against the summary that already made the choice, a correction that is right on the merits and costly and against the grain of how the record was ever kept, since nothing in the summary makes room for it after the fact. And the reckoning holds its own bound in view: the strict claim that memory equals the plain average of peak and end is an idealization, and duration neglect is a strong tendency and not an absolute law, softening where the length is made salient or meaningful and varying with how the episode trends toward its close (Kahneman). This is distinct from More to Stand Still, a real-time adaptation dimming the felt level as it lasts, whereas here the lived feeling is whatever it is and the memory alone is skewed; distinct from Once Bitten, an asymmetric sampling across separate trials, whereas here the distortion sits within a single remembered episode; and distinct from Starting Tomorrow, a discounting of the future by delay, whereas here it is the past that is mis-summarized and the length ignored outright. No villain: the chooser trusts the only record it holds, and the lived hour left no other ledger behind for it to consult.

## Tension

The whole weighed on one side, and the memory, keeping only two moments, not feeling the difference.
