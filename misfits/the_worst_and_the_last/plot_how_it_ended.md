# Plot: How It Ended

## Owner

## Cue

The memory is building its summary, and the peak alone is not the whole of it.

## Action

At [the Looking Back](place_the_looking_back.md), [the Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md) from [the keeper's seat](position_keeper.md) on [Keep the Peak and the End](plan_keep_the_peak_and_the_end.md) through [Remembering](process_remembering.md) takes up [the Ending](piece_the_ending.md), weighting the final moments of the episode as though they were the whole of how it turned out.

## Stage

The last moments of an episode are kept not merely as one more sample of it but as its resolution, weighted far past their share of the minutes actually lived, because they read as how the whole thing came out (Fredrickson and Kahneman). In the clinic this second anchor showed the same way: patients' retrospective ratings of a colonoscopy or a lithotripsy tracked the final minutes of discomfort far more closely than they tracked the duration or the sum of the pain reported as it happened (Redelmeier and Kahneman). The end is also the most recent state and often the state most relevant to what comes next, which is why leaning on it is not an idle habit. No villain: the end is recent and often decision-relevant, sensible to weight heavily even if not proportionately to its true share of the episode.

## Tension

The last moments standing for the whole, as though how it stopped were the same as how it went.
