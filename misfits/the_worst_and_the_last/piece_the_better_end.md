# Piece: The Better End

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The mechanism: a gentler close added onto the ordeal, lengthening it while improving how it is remembered.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Ordeal](place_the_ordeal.md), carried by [Ending](process_ending.md), voiced by [the Kind Hand](persona_the_kind_hand.md): the water left to warm a little before the hand comes out, the instrument left in place a moment longer at low discomfort instead of withdrawn at once, minutes added on purpose so the last thing felt is milder than the worst.

## Load Bearing

Adding a period of milder discomfort onto the end of an ordeal makes the ordeal objectively longer, and people still prefer it, choosing to repeat the longer trial over the shorter one that stopped cold at its worst: more pain, preferred to less, because the added minutes tapered the ending down (Kahneman, Fredrickson, Schreiber and Redelmeier). Real patients, given a gentler close to a real procedure, remembered less pain and returned more willingly for the next one, and the improvement was not imagined: it showed up in what they said and in what they were willing to do again (Redelmeier, Katz and Kahneman). This is a genuine service to the self that will do the remembering, and it works because the ending is over-weighted in the first place: the same lever that eases a memory, honestly pulled here, is exactly the lever a flatterer would pull to leave a false impression instead.

## Apparent

Reads as a plain reduction of suffering: less pain at the end, what could be wrong with that. It hides that the total suffering went up even as the memory of it went down.
