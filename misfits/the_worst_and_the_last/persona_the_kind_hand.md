# Persona: The Kind Hand

## Taxonomy

[Kind Hand](position_kind_hand.md), inside [The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The archetype of the one who shapes the episode's ending, the physician or host who adds a gentler close to leave a kinder memory, a real service to the remembering self turning on the same over-weighting of the end.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A manipulator who games a memory, engineering an ending to plant a verdict the whole did not earn, as though every softened close were a trick played on the one who will recall it.

## Action

End it gently. At [the Ordeal](place_the_ordeal.md), the Kind Hand on [End It Gently](plan_end_it_gently.md) through [Ending](process_ending.md) holds [the Better End](piece_the_better_end.md), adding a stretch of lower discomfort at the close, so the episode is remembered as kinder and the one who lived it is likelier to return, though the total undergone is greater (Redelmeier, Katz and Kahneman).

## Shadow

Its kindness is real, and it is the same lever a flatterer would pull. The Kind Hand is not, in itself, a manipulator: a gentler close genuinely improves the remembered experience and eases the dread of returning, so a physician who lingers a moment at low discomfort does the patient a true service, measured in a better memory and a greater willingness to come back for care that is needed (Redelmeier, Katz and Kahneman). But the service works only because the end is weighted past its share, and the very lever that comforts could as easily flatter a memory the whole did not earn, ending a hard thing sweetly to sell the return of it. It holds a real good and a real hazard in one gesture, and neither the kindness nor the risk restores the lived episode to its rightful weight.

## Tell

It softens the close and the memory softens with it, a true kindness and the same handle a flatterer would take, the end doing the work either way.
