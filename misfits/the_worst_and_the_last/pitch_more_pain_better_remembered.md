# Pitch: More Pain, Better Remembered

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that the episode which was worse to live can be the one remembered better, and so the one chosen to repeat.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Quiet and rueful, the ache of a memory that keeps the wrong things. An episode lived moment to moment, every second counting while it lasts, and then compressed to its peak and its close by a memory doing exactly what a memory must. Nothing here is a trick or a self too foolish to recall; the compression is sensible and the divide is real.

## Undertow

Memory keeps the most intense moment and the final one and lets the long middle and the length go, so retrospective evaluation tracks the average of peak and end and is nearly blind to duration (Fredrickson and Kahneman). So a longer suffering that tapers is remembered as better than a shorter one that stopped at its worst, and preferred to repeat, more pain chosen for a gentler close (Kahneman, Fredrickson, Schreiber and Redelmeier).

## Nerve

That the self which keeps the score is the self which chooses, so the episode that was worse to live is chosen again because it is remembered better, the lived experience overruled by its own summary. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since the strict peak-end average is an idealization and duration neglect a strong tendency and not an absolute law (Redelmeier and Kahneman; Kahneman).

## Echo

Live the whole of it, and keep only the worst of it and the last of it, and go back for the one that ended kindest.
