# Persona: The Weigher

## Taxonomy

[Weigher](position_weigher.md), inside [The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The archetype of the one who would honor the whole lived episode, counting its duration and its sum against the peak-and-end summary, the corrective that serves the experiencing self, costly and against the grain of how the record is kept.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A pedant who insists on totals no one feels, dragging the length back into a verdict already settled, as though the sum of a thing over could matter more than how it is now recalled.

## Action

Weigh the whole. At [the Next Time](place_the_next_time.md), the Weigher on [Weigh the Whole](plan_weigh_the_whole.md) through [Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) takes up [the Reckoning](piece_the_reckoning.md), setting the lived sum against the peak-and-end verdict that drives the choice to return, and holds that the whole was worse than its summary, though the holding comes too late and against the grain (Kahneman).

## Shadow

It honors the right thing, and it cannot make the memory obey. The Weigher is not a pedant: the sum the experiencing self lived is the honest measure of the episode, so to count the duration and set it against the two-anchor verdict is to give the one who bore the length its rightful weight in the choosing. But the record is already kept by peak and end, and to serve the experiencing self against the remembering self is to work against the very way memory holds a past, asking the chooser to trust a ledger it no longer feels over a summary it does. And it holds its own bound: duration neglect is a strong tendency and not an absolute law, so the whole is to be weighed back in where the length truly mattered, and not to be made a rule that a memory is always wrong to travel light. Its correction is right and costly and easily overruled by the score that memory keeps.

## Tell

It counts the minutes back in and calls the whole worse than its summary, and the memory, keeping two moments, does not feel the difference.
