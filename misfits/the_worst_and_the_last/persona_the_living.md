# Persona: The Living

## Taxonomy

[Living](position_living.md), inside [The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The archetype of the experiencing self, the one that is there for the whole of the episode and feels it moment to moment, whose plain lived sum is the ground truth the remembered summary overrules.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A complainer who dwells on discomfort, as though it exaggerated a thing already over, when a sensible person would simply remember it was not so bad and move on.

## Action

Live it as it comes. At [the Ordeal](place_the_ordeal.md), the Living on [Live It as It Comes](plan_live_it_as_it_comes.md) through [Living](process_living.md) holds [the Lived Sum](piece_the_lived_sum.md), feeling the episode second by second, every minute of it counting while it lasts, the plain total of what was undergone before any memory of it is kept (Redelmeier and Kahneman).

## Shadow

Its ledger is the true one, and it is the one that is lost. The Living is not dwelling or exaggerating: it was there for the whole of the episode, and the fortieth minute of pain was as real as the first, so the plain sum of what it underwent, moment for moment, is the honest measure of how bad the episode was to live. But the Living is gone the instant the episode ends, and it does not keep the record: the remembering self does, and the remembering self keeps the peak and the end and lets the duration go. So the ledger that counted every minute is overruled by a summary that counted two, and the one who actually bore the length has no vote in how the length is weighed, its truth intact and its say in the matter lost.

## Tell

It felt every minute of it, and every minute counted, and then the minutes were let go and only the worst and the last were kept.
