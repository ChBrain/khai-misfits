# Plan: Keep the Peak and the End

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the keeper's seat](position_keeper.md), inside [The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Keeper](position_keeper.md)

## Direction

Reduce the episode to its most intense moment and its last, since a memory cannot store an hour minute by minute, and build from the two anchors the verdict by which to judge whether to return. Know that the duration and the long middle fall away in the keeping, that a long episode and a short one with the same peak and end are kept as equals, and that the summary you trust as the whole record is a compression that drops the very length the experiencing self lived.

## Orders

([The Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md) takes up [the Peak](piece_the_peak.md) and [the Ending](piece_the_ending.md) through [Remembering](process_remembering.md) at [the Looking Back](place_the_looking_back.md), keeping the most intense moment and the last, and meets [the Length](piece_the_length.md) let go.)

## Implementation

([Remembering](process_remembering.md) at [the Looking Back](place_the_looking_back.md) takes up [the Peak](piece_the_peak.md) and [the Ending](piece_the_ending.md), because a memory that cannot hold the whole keeps its two most salient anchors, so the duration falls away as [the Length](piece_the_length.md) is let go (Fredrickson and Kahneman).)

## Targets

- [ ] the most intense moment is kept as the peak, the first anchor of the summary
- [ ] the final moments are kept as the end, weighted past their share of the whole
- [ ] the duration and the long middle are let go, a long episode and a short one kept as equals
- [ ] it is owned that the compression is no lie but the only thing a memory can do
