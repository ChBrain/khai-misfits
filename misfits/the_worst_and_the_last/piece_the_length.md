# Piece: The Length

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The mechanism: the duration and the long middle, dropped almost entirely from what memory keeps.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Looking Back](place_the_looking_back.md), carried by [Remembering](process_remembering.md), voiced by [the Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md): the stretch of ordinary minutes between peak and end, present while it happened and nearly absent from the account given afterward.

## Load Bearing

How long the episode lasted barely moves how it is remembered: this is duration neglect, and it means a long ordeal and a short one that share the same peak and the same ending are recalled as roughly equal, however different the minutes they actually cost (Fredrickson and Kahneman). The long middle, every minute of it lived and logged by [the Living](persona_the_living.md) in [the Ordeal](place_the_ordeal.md), is the very thing memory lets go of first. Nothing intense happened there, by definition; that is exactly why it leaves no mark. What was heaviest to live through, its sheer length, is what weighs least in the telling.

## Apparent

Reads as unimportant, a detail memory rightly trims: nobody needs a minute-by-minute account, so why would its length matter. It looks like efficient forgetting, not a cost.
