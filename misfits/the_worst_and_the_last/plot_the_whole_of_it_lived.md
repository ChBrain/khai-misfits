# Plot: The Whole of It Lived

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The episode begins, and there is nothing yet to remember it, only the minute at hand.

## Action

At [the Ordeal](place_the_ordeal.md), [the Living](persona_the_living.md) from [the living seat](position_living.md) on [Live It as It Comes](plan_live_it_as_it_comes.md) through [Living](process_living.md) takes up [the Lived Sum](piece_the_lived_sum.md), feeling the episode second by second and letting the total accrue, minute added to minute, before any memory of it is kept.

## Stage

This is the ground truth the whole misfit turns against: an extended episode felt moment to moment, the fortieth minute of discomfort no less real than the first, so the honest measure of how bad it was to live is the plain sum across the whole of it, not an average and not a highlight (Redelmeier and Kahneman). This is the experiencing self, the one that is there for the episode and vanishes the instant it ends, and while it lasts its ledger is simply addition: more minutes of hurt make a worse episode, full stop (Kahneman). No memory is yet in play here, no compression and no summary, only the undergoing itself, which is why this ledger will later be the one overruled. No villain: the Living simply undergoes the episode as it comes, keeping no score because keeping score is not yet its job, only living it.

## Tension

The true ledger accruing minute by minute, and no one yet keeping it.
