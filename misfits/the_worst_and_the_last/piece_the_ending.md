# Piece: The Ending

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The mechanism: the final moments weighted far past their true share, taken to be how the whole episode resolved.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Looking Back](place_the_looking_back.md), carried by [Remembering](process_remembering.md), voiced by [the Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md): the last few minutes, replayed as though they were the conclusion the whole ordeal had been building toward.

## Load Bearing

The second anchor memory keeps is the ending, the last stretch of the episode, and it is weighted far past the share of time it actually occupied (Fredrickson and Kahneman). This is not arbitrary: the ending is the most recent part, and recency alone would tilt memory toward it, but it is also read as the resolution, the note the whole experience is taken to have closed on. An episode that ends worse is remembered as worse than one that ends better, even when everything before the ending was identical. The ending stands for a verdict it was never asked to carry alone.

## Apparent

Reads as the natural place to judge the whole from: of course you ask how it ended, that is what "how it went" means. It looks like sound judgment, not a bias in judgment.
