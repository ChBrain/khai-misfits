# Position: Kind Hand

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The seat of the one who shapes the ending, adding a gentler close to leave a kinder memory, and marks that the kindness is real and turns on the same over-weighting of the end that a flatterer would use.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A hold on the ending, the power to shape the final moments of the episode.
The knowledge that the end is weighted past its share, so a gentler close buys a kinder memory.
A true care for the one who will recall it, and the willingness to add to the total undergone to soften how it ends.

## Orders

End it gently, and buy a kinder memory with a softer close.
Add a stretch of lower discomfort at the end of the episode, since the end is weighted far past its share, so the whole is remembered as kinder and the one who lived it is likelier to return for what it needs (Redelmeier, Katz and Kahneman).
Know that the service is real and that it works only by the over-weighting of the end, that the total undergone is greater though the memory is better, and that the same lever you pull to comfort could flatter a memory the whole did not earn.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and this is the double edge.
Because it shapes the ending in true care and the gentler close genuinely improves the memory and the willingness to return, the seat takes no loss and does a real good, and yet the good turns on the end weighted past its share, the same lever that could sell a return the whole did not earn, so its kindness and a flatterer's craft are one gesture.
It keeps its real service and its clean intent and loses no cause for blame, the kind hand whose comfort and whose hazard are the same softened close.

## Drives

Toward the one who softens the ending and the memory softens with it,
adding a gentler close in true care, a real service to the self that will recall it and the very lever a flatterer would take,
so the seat that alone can ease the memory eases it only by weighting the end past its share, its kindness and its hazard the same gesture, no villain in the care.
