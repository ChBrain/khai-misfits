# Persona: The Keeper

## Taxonomy

[Keeper](position_keeper.md), inside [The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The archetype of the remembering self, the one that keeps the peak-and-end summary, tells the story of what happened, and chooses whether to return, valuing the episode by a record built from two moments.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A liar who rewrites the past to suit itself, softening what was hard and shortening what was long, as though it chose to distort a record it could have kept whole.

## Action

Keep the peak and the end. At [the Looking Back](place_the_looking_back.md), the Keeper on [Keep the Peak and the End](plan_keep_the_peak_and_the_end.md) through [Remembering](process_remembering.md) takes up [the Peak](piece_the_peak.md) and [the Ending](piece_the_ending.md), the most intense moment and the last, then meets [the Length](piece_the_length.md), the duration let go, and builds from them the summary it will choose by (Fredrickson and Kahneman).

## Shadow

It does not rewrite the past, it compresses it, because it cannot do otherwise. The Keeper is not a liar: no memory can hold an hour second by second, so it must reduce the episode to a few salient features, and the peak and the end are the sensible ones to keep, the mark of how far it went and the mark of how it resolved. What it drops, the duration and the long middle, it drops not by choice but by the limit of what a memory is, and it trusts the summary it builds because that summary is the only record of the episode it holds. It keeps its two honest anchors and does the only thing memory can, and the distortion is not a fault in the keeping but the price of keeping anything at all.

## Tell

It keeps the worst and the last because those are what a memory can hold, and chooses by them because they are all it has, no lie in it.
