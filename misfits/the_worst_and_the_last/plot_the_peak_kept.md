# Plot: The Peak Kept

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The episode is over, and only a memory is left of it now.

## Action

At [the Looking Back](place_the_looking_back.md), [the Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md) from [the keeper's seat](position_keeper.md) on [Keep the Peak and the End](plan_keep_the_peak_and_the_end.md) through [Remembering](process_remembering.md) takes up [the Peak](piece_the_peak.md), stamping the most intense moment of the episode into the record as the mark of how far it went.

## Stage

The memory does not hold the hour, it holds a moment plucked from it: the single most intense point of the episode, kept as the sign of its worst, or its best, extremity (Fredrickson and Kahneman). This is one of the two anchors the retrospective summary is built from, and it is chosen because it is genuinely informative: an episode's peak tells something true about what the episode contained that no ordinary minute of it would tell alone. Nothing in the choosing is careless, a memory reaching for a landmark rather than trying and failing to hold the whole terrain. No villain: keeping the peak is a sensible compression, a memory cannot hold the whole and the peak is a reasonable thing to keep of it, informative in a way an arbitrary moment would not be.

## Tension

The peak standing in for the whole, one moment doing the work of every minute that surrounded it.
