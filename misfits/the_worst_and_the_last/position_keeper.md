# Position: Keeper

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The seat of the remembering self, the one that keeps the summary and chooses whether to return, and marks that its compression to peak and end is no lie but the only thing a memory can do.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A memory that cannot hold the episode whole, and must reduce it to a few salient features.
The two anchors it keeps, the peak that marks how far the episode went and the end that marks how it resolved.
The say over the choosing, since it is the remembering self and not the experiencing one that decides whether to go back.

## Orders

Keep the peak and the end, and choose by the summary they make.
Reduce the episode to its most intense moment and its last, since a memory cannot store an hour minute by minute, and build from the two anchors the verdict by which to judge whether to return.
Know that the duration and the long middle fall away in the keeping, that a long episode and a short one with the same peak and end are kept as equals, and that the summary you trust as the whole record is a compression that drops the very length the experiencing self lived (Fredrickson and Kahneman).

## Loses

Nothing it can name, and this is the trap.
Because it keeps the two honest anchors a memory can hold and trusts the only record it has, the seat takes no loss it can see, and yet the summary it builds overrules the lived sum, so it chooses to repeat the episode that was worse to live because that episode is the one it remembers better, a harm invisible from inside the only record it keeps.
It keeps its two anchors and its honest compression and loses no cause for blame, the remembering self that distorts by doing the only thing memory can.

## Drives

Toward the one that keeps the score and cannot keep the whole,
reducing the episode to its peak and its end because a memory can hold no more, and choosing by the summary they make,
so the seat that alone decides whether to return decides by a record that drops the length, preferring the longer hurt that ended kindly, no lie in the keeping.
