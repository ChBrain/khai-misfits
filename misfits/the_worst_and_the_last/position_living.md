# Position: Living

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The seat of the experiencing self, the one that is there for the whole episode and feels it moment to moment, and marks that its lived sum is the honest measure and the one that keeps no record.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The whole of the episode as it happens, felt second by second, every minute of it as real as the first.
The plain lived sum, the honest measure of how bad or good the episode was to undergo.
No hold on the record, since it is gone the instant the episode ends and the remembering self keeps the score.

## Orders

Feel it as it comes, every minute counting while it lasts.
Undergo the episode moment to moment, letting each minute weigh as much as any other, since the plain sum of what is lived is the true measure of the whole.
Know that this ledger is honest and that it will not be the one that is kept, that the self who bore the length has no hold on the memory of it, and that the summary built after will count the peak and the end and let the minutes go.

## Loses

Its say in how the episode is weighed, though its ledger is the true one.
Because it is gone the instant the episode ends and keeps no record, the seat that felt every minute of the length has no vote in the summary that is built from the peak and the end, so its honest sum is overruled by two moments and the one who bore the whole is not the one who judges it.
It keeps its true measure and its lived truth and loses no cause for blame, the experiencing self whose faithful sum is kept by no one.

## Drives

Toward the one who feels the whole and keeps none of it,
undergoing every minute of the episode as it comes, its lived sum the honest measure of the thing,
so the seat that alone bore the length has no say in how the length is weighed, its truth intact and its record kept by the self that lets the minutes go.
