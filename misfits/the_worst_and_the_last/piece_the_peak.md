# Piece: The Peak

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The mechanism: the single most intense moment kept as memory's first anchor, standing in for the whole.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Looking Back](place_the_looking_back.md), carried by [Remembering](process_remembering.md), voiced by [the Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md): the one instant that returns unbidden, sharper than the rest, the worst (or best) the episode reached.

## Load Bearing

Memory does not average an experience; it samples it, and the sample it reaches for first is the peak, the most intense moment the episode produced, whether that intensity was pleasant or painful (Fredrickson and Kahneman). Keeping the peak is not a failure of memory: it is a sensible compression, a mark of how far the episode went, the kind of extreme worth flagging for next time. The trouble is only what it stands in for. One instant, however sharp, is asked to represent every minute that surrounded it, and it does so whether or not those minutes agreed.

## Apparent

Reads as the fair summary of the whole: surely the worst moment tells you how bad it was. It feels like the natural verdict, not a shortcut.
