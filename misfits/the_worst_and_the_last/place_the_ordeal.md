# Place: The Ordeal

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). Where the episode is lived, minute by minute, before any memory is kept of it. Hosts [The Whole of It Lived](plot_the_whole_of_it_lived.md), [A Better End](plot_a_better_end.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A procedure underway, or a hand held in cold water, or a long stretch of a hard day: the minutes passing one after another, each one felt as it comes, and the last of them shaped a little gentler than the rest.

## Holds

[The Lived Sum](piece_the_lived_sum.md).
The plain total of the episode, accrued moment by moment while it is still being undergone.

[The Better End](piece_the_better_end.md).
A stretch of milder discomfort added at the close, lengthening the ordeal but softening how it finishes.

## Offers

To the Living, every minute as it happens, honestly felt and honestly summed. To the Kind Hand, a close still open to shaping, the one part of the episode that can still be made gentler before it is sealed into memory.

## Withheld

Any hold on how the episode will be remembered. What is lived here is felt in full and counted in full, but the record that survives it is built elsewhere, from two moments this place cannot choose (Redelmeier and Kahneman).
