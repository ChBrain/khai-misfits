# Plan: Weigh the Whole

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the weigher's seat](position_weigher.md), inside [The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Weigher](position_weigher.md)

## Direction

Count the duration and the total the experiencing self underwent, and hold it against the summary the remembering self keeps, so the one who bore the length is given its rightful weight in the choice to return. Know that the memory is already kept by peak and end and will not feel the difference, that to serve the experiencing self is to work against the grain of how a past is held, and that even your correction is bounded, since the length is to be weighed where it truly mattered and not everywhere by rule.

## Orders

## Implementation

([Reckoning](process_reckoning.md) at [the Next Time](place_the_next_time.md) takes up [the Reckoning](piece_the_reckoning.md), because the choice to return is driven by the summary, so to weigh the whole is to count the duration back in against a verdict the memory has already settled (Kahneman).)

## Targets

- [ ] the duration and the lived sum are counted against the peak-and-end verdict
- [ ] the whole is held to have been worse than its summary, the length given its weight
- [ ] it is seen that the correction comes too late and against the grain of how a memory is kept
- [ ] it is owned that the bound holds, the length weighed where it mattered and not made a rule
