# Place: The Looking Back

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). Where the memory keeps its summary of the episode, once the episode itself is over and gone. Hosts [The Peak Kept](plot_the_peak_kept.md), [How It Ended](plot_how_it_ended.md), [The Length Let Go](plot_the_length_let_go.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

No episode underway here, only what is left of one: a most intense moment held up, a final moment held up beside it, and a long middle nowhere in view.

## Holds

[The Peak](piece_the_peak.md).
The most intense moment of the episode, kept as the mark of how far it went.

[The Ending](piece_the_ending.md).
The final moments, weighted past their share because they read as how the episode resolved.

[The Length](piece_the_length.md).
The duration itself, dropped from the record, a long episode and a short one remembered as equals when their peak and end agree.

## Offers

A summary a memory can actually hold: two anchors instead of an hour of minutes, light enough to carry into the choice of whether to return.

## Withheld

The length. What was lived across the whole middle of the episode finds no seat here, and a longer episode buys itself no extra weight in the memory built at this place (Fredrickson and Kahneman).
