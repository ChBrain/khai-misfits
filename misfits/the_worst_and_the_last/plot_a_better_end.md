# Plot: A Better End

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The end weighs so heavily on the record, and a hand is there that can shape what it will hold.

## Action

At [the Ordeal](place_the_ordeal.md), [the Kind Hand](persona_the_kind_hand.md) from [the kind hand's seat](position_kind_hand.md) on [End It Gently](plan_end_it_gently.md) through [Ending](process_ending.md) takes up [the Better End](piece_the_better_end.md), adding a gentler close to the episode before it is done.

## Stage

Because the end is weighted so heavily, adding a milder stretch at the close, the water made warmer, the scope left briefly in place at low discomfort, changes what the whole episode is remembered as, even though it adds to what is actually undergone: subjects offered the choice preferred to repeat the longer trial with the warmer tail over the shorter one that stopped at its worst (Kahneman, Fredrickson, Schreiber and Redelmeier). In a randomized trial this held past the laboratory: extending a colonoscopy with a gentler close genuinely improved patients' remembered experience and their willingness to return, a real gain in remembered welfare purchased with more total discomfort lived (Redelmeier, Katz and Kahneman). No villain: the kindness is real, it improves what is remembered and how willing a person is to come back, and it works by the very over-weighting of the end that any flatterer of a memory would also lean on, the same lever either hand would pull.

## Tension

The real kindness of the gentler close, and the same handle a flatterer would take.
