# Plan: End It Gently

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the kind hand's seat](position_kind_hand.md), inside [The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Kind Hand](position_kind_hand.md)

## Direction

Add a stretch of lower discomfort at the end of the episode, since the end is weighted far past its share, so the whole is remembered as kinder and the one who lived it is likelier to return for what it needs. Know that the service is real and that it works only by the over-weighting of the end, that the total undergone is greater though the memory is better, and that the same lever you pull to comfort could flatter a memory the whole did not earn.

## Orders

## Implementation
