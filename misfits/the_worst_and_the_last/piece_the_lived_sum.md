# Piece: The Lived Sum

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The mechanism: every minute of the ordeal counted honestly while it happened, and overruled the moment it was recalled.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Ordeal](place_the_ordeal.md), carried by [Living](process_living.md), voiced by [the Living](persona_the_living.md): the minute-by-minute report given moment to moment, discomfort logged as it comes and not smoothed, not yet a story, only a running total.

## Load Bearing

This is the honest measure: the moment-by-moment experience, added up across every minute the ordeal lasted, the way pain was actually rated in real time during the procedure itself (Redelmeier and Kahneman). It is the ledger the experiencing self keeps, minute by minute, without knowing yet which minute will be kept and which discarded. Nothing here is wrong: the total is a fair account of how much was lived through. But it is not the account that survives. What gets carried forward is not this sum, is not even its average, but a much thinner extract of it (Kahneman): the worst instant and the last one. The lived sum is real, and it will lose.

## Apparent

Reads as mere complaining about a thing already over: why dwell on minutes that have passed and cannot be changed. It sounds like keeping score after the game.
