# Position: Weigher

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md). The seat of the one who would honor the whole lived episode against its summary, and marks that the correction is right and costly and cannot make the memory obey, and holds even itself to a bound.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A way to honor the whole, to count the duration and the sum the experiencing self lived.
A clear sight of the divide, the lived ledger set against the peak-and-end verdict that drives the choice.
A held bound, the knowledge that duration neglect is a strong tendency and not an absolute law, so the whole is to be weighed back in where the length mattered and not made a rule.

## Orders

Weigh the whole, and set the lived sum against the two-anchor verdict.
Count the duration and the total the experiencing self underwent, and hold it against the summary the remembering self keeps, so the one who bore the length is given its rightful weight in the choice to return (Kahneman).
Know that the memory is already kept by peak and end and will not feel the difference, that to serve the experiencing self is to work against the grain of how a past is held, and that even your correction is bounded, since the length is to be weighed where it truly mattered and not everywhere by rule.

## Loses

The power to make the record obey, though it honors the true measure.
Because the summary is already built from the peak and the end and the memory no longer feels the length, the seat that counts the duration back in cannot make the chooser trust a ledger it no longer feels over a verdict it does, so its correction is right and comes too late and against the grain, honoring a whole the record will not hold.
It keeps its true measure and its held bound and loses no cause for blame, the one who weighs the whole that memory has already let go.

## Drives

Toward the one who honors the whole and cannot make it stick,
counting the duration and the sum back in against the peak-and-end verdict, right and costly and against the grain of how a memory keeps a past,
so the seat that alone would give the length its weight holds even its own correction to a bound, the whole to be weighed where it mattered and not made a rule that a memory is always wrong to travel light.
