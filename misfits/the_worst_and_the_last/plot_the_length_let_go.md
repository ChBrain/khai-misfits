# Plot: The Length Let Go

## Taxonomy

[The Worst and the Last](play_the_worst_and_the_last.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The peak and the end are kept, and the rest of the episode is still to place somewhere in the record.

## Action

At [the Looking Back](place_the_looking_back.md), [the Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md) from [the keeper's seat](position_keeper.md) through [Remembering](process_remembering.md) meets [the Length](piece_the_length.md), the duration and the long middle of the episode, and finds no place to keep it.

## Stage

With the peak and the end already stamped into the record, the duration simply has nowhere left to go: a long episode and a short one sharing the same peak and the same end are remembered as near enough equals, the neglect of length close to complete (Fredrickson and Kahneman). This is not the memory choosing to discard the minutes it lived, weighing them and setting them aside; there is no third slot in the summary for a sum to occupy, no capacity the length could have filled if only it had been valued differently. No villain: the duration is dropped not by any choice made against it but by the limit of what a two-anchor memory is built to hold.

## Tension

The lived length gone from the record, as though the minutes between the peak and the end had never been minutes at all.
