# Piece: The Loop

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). The sustaining effect: timeouts make retries, retries make load, load makes timeouts, and a cold cache misses into a backend too busy to let it warm.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Closing between [The Fleet](place_the_fleet.md) and [The Queue](place_the_queue.md), assembled entirely out of correct behaviour, the retry prescribed and the fetch contractual and neither one an error at any point in the circuit (Bronson, Aghayev, Charapko and Zhu; Beyer, Jones, Petoff and Murphy).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the trigger is just a trigger: without work amplification the load falls back when the spike ends, the queue drains as everyone expects it to, and the incident is a slow ten minutes rather than an outage.
It bears the load as the reason the failure needs no cause. Because the loop's input is the load and its output is more load, it is self-feeding from the instant it closes, and every distinctive feature of the trap, the persistence, the mystery, the uselessness of waiting, follows from that one property.

## Apparent

Diligence, even: a fleet retrying and a cache filling read as a system fighting to recover, which is exactly what they are and exactly what prevents it.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Everyone Tries Again](plot_everyone_tries_again.md), the circuit closed out of nothing but correctness.
It works together with [The Spin](piece_the_spin.md), the loop the cause and the spin what it looks like from outside.
