# Position: Backend

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). The seat that accepts the offered work and serves the queue in order, unable to tell live work from dead.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Capacity enough for the ordinary load, and margin above it, in the upper state.
A queue of accepted work, served in the order it arrived, with no marking of which entries still have someone waiting.
No standing to refuse work it is physically able to attempt, since that decision was never given to the seat.

## Orders

Take what comes: accept the connections offered and work the queue through, because refusing capacity you have is not a thing a server is built to do.
Serve in order, since the seat has no signal distinguishing a request whose caller is still waiting from one whose caller left long ago.
Accept what the seat does: that full employment is its measure of doing well, and that on the lower floor full employment yields almost nothing.

## Loses

Its whole effort, spent and unrecoverable, on work nobody is waiting for.
Because a departed caller sends no notice, the seat cannot decline the dead work and cannot prioritise around it, so it loses precisely the capacity the live requests behind it needed, and loses it while looking maximally busy.
It keeps every core saturated, and loses the goodput that saturation is supposed to mean.

## Drives

Toward the one whose willingness to serve everything is what leaves it serving almost nothing,
accepting because accepting is the ordinary and correct construction of a server and, by doing so, filling its capacity with work already worthless,
so the seat that would pass every test it has is the seat at which the service is measurably down, healthy in every component and failed as a whole.
