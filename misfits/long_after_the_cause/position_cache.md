# Position: Cache

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). The seat of the layer emptied by the trigger, whose only way to refill runs through the backend its emptiness is saturating.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An empty store, through no fault of its own, after an ordinary restart or deploy.
One defined behaviour: serve what it holds, fetch what it lacks.
No source for what it lacks except the backend that is now too busy to return it in time.

## Orders

Fill on miss: send through whatever is not held, and keep the answer for next time, since that is the entire contract of the seat.
Do not withhold a fetch, because a cache that declined to fetch what it lacked would simply be a cache that never warms and never serves.
Accept what the seat does: that its own refilling depends on the responsiveness of the very thing its missing is loading.

## Loses

Its own way back, which is the shape of it.
Because the load its coldness creates is the load that prevents the backend answering fast enough for it to warm, the seat loses the one path out of coldness that it has, its correct behaviour closing the door it is trying to walk through.
It keeps its contract intact, and loses the ability to fulfil the contract at all.

## Drives

Toward the one whose only defined behaviour locks in the condition that behaviour exists to end,
fetching because fetching is the whole of what a cache does and, by doing so, multiplying the backend load by exactly the factor it was installed to divide it by,
so the seat that made the system fast is the seat through which the system cannot get fast again, faultless in its design and self-sealing in its effect.
