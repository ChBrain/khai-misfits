# Plot: One Bad Minute

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Queue](place_the_queue.md), where something small and brief goes wrong while the margin happens to be thin.

## Action

Through [Tripping](process_tripping.md), [the Trip](piece_the_trip.md) lands: a collection pause on a few machines, or a deploy that restarts a tier and leaves its cache empty, or a dependency slow for ninety seconds, or a burst of traffic large but not enormous. It is enough, here, to push latency past the point where requests begin to time out. The scene closes with the threshold crossed by something that will be over in minutes and forgotten by morning.

## Stage

The trigger is not a disaster and would be harmless on almost any other day: the same event in the stable state does nothing at all, and nothing about it marks it as the one that mattered.

## Tension

What makes this minute different is not the minute but where the system already was, and where the system already was is a condition nobody was watching because it is not itself a failure.
