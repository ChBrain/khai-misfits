# Piece: The Trip

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). The transient trigger that pushes latency past the threshold: a pause, a deploy, a slow dependency, a burst, brief and then gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Landing at [The Queue](place_the_queue.md) while the system runs in its vulnerable band, harmless in the stable state and sufficient here, nothing about the event itself marking it as the one that mattered (Bronson, Aghayev, Charapko and Zhu).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the system stays on the upper floor indefinitely: the vulnerable band is not itself a failure, only a place where a crossing is possible, so without something to push it across the system runs on unremarkably at the same load for as long as you like.
It bears the load as the crossing and nothing more. Because its whole contribution is spent in the moment of the crossing, it is the element the play must establish precisely so that it can then be taken away, the trap's signature being what happens once it is gone.

## Apparent

A bump, even: a finished blip on a graph, the kind of thing that happens weekly and is never mentioned again.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [One Bad Minute](plot_one_bad_minute.md), the crossing made by something forgettable.
It works together with [The Loop](piece_the_loop.md), the one starting what the other then no longer needs it for.
