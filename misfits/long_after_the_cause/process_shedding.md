# Process: Shedding

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). Borne at [The Console](place_the_console.md), precipitated by [The Operator](persona_the_operator.md) refusing arriving traffic at the edge, in [Turn Away the Traffic](plot_turn_away_the_traffic.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An operator who has confirmed the trigger is gone, found no failing component, and reached at last for the only lever that touches the loop.

## Direction

From arriving load above the recovery threshold toward arriving load beneath it, requests refused at the edge, dead work drained rather than served, retries disabled while the shedding holds.

## Lever

That the loop is fed by load and nothing else, so removing load far enough starves it and the system climbs back, at the price of denying service the machines were able to provide (Beyer, Jones, Petoff and Murphy).

## Echo

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the remedy and its bill named together, the lower state still there when the shedding ends.
