# Position: Operator

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). The seat that must starve the loop by refusing traffic the machines could serve, the only move that lifts the system off the lower floor.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Levers at the edge: shed traffic, drain queues, disable retries, restart tiers.
Dashboards that show a passed trigger, healthy components, and a flat service, and show nothing at all of the loop connecting them.
No display of the recovery threshold, so no way to know in advance how hard the shedding must be.

## Orders

Shed the load: refuse a large share of arriving requests, drain the dead work, turn the retries off, and hold until the loop starves.
Go further than the situation appears to warrant, since anything above the recovery threshold leaves the loop fed and reads, wrongly, as the shedding having failed.
Accept what the seat does: that the correct move is indistinguishable, in the moment, from surrender.

## Loses

Every request it turns away that the machines were physically able to serve.
Because the only lever that works is the deliberate denial of service, the seat loses exactly what it is employed to protect, and loses it knowingly, paying in refused users for a recovery no other action buys.
It keeps the ability to bring the system back, and loses the possibility of bringing it back without cost.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound instinct to wait for a passed spike to drain is the instinct that must be overridden,
shedding because starving the loop is the only move that reaches it and, by doing so, denying service to buy service back,
so the seat that holds the play's one remedy is the seat that must spend the thing it is there to defend, correct in the end and never free.
