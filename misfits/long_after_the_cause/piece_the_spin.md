# Piece: The Spin

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). The system pinned at the lower floor, running at full effort for a fraction of its goodput, much of that effort spent on work whose callers have already gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held at [The Queue](place_the_queue.md), where every core is saturated and the served fraction is a shadow of an hour ago, dead work worked to completion beside live work because nothing distinguishes them (Huang, Magnusson and colleagues).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is nothing to explain: a system that simply stopped would point at a broken component and the incident would resolve into a fault to find, whereas the trap's whole difficulty is that it is running flat out.
It bears the load as the phenomenon whole and as the reason diagnosis fails. Because full effort and near-zero output are true at the same moment, every component passes its own tests while the service is down, so the investigation that asks what is broken here finds its answer nowhere.

## Apparent

A system fighting hard, even: pegged cores read on any dashboard as maximum effort, which is precisely what they are.
