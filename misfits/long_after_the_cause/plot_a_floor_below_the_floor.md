# Plot: A Floor Below the Floor

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Queue](place_the_queue.md), on an ordinary afternoon, with margin to spare and every board green.

## Action

[The Backend](persona_the_backend.md) on [Take What Comes](plan_take_what_comes.md) accepts the offered work and serves a short queue in order, the system running comfortably in its upper state. And beneath all of it [the Other Floor](piece_the_other_floor.md) waits: a second operating point every bit as stable as this one, in which the machines are just as up and the processes just as busy and almost nothing is served. The scene closes with a healthy system and a hollow underneath it that has never been entered and is therefore neither planned for nor tested against (Bronson, Aghayev, Charapko and Zhu).

## Stage

Nothing is wrong and nothing is being neglected: a system with margin, serving well, is exactly what good operation looks like, and the second state is not a defect anyone installed or could find on inspection.

## Tension

The one fact that would change how the system is run, that it has somewhere else it can settle and stay, is invisible from inside the state it has always been in.
