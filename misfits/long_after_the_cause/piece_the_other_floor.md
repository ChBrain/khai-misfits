# Piece: The Other Floor

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). The second stable operating point beneath the healthy one, in which every machine is up and almost nothing is served.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Waiting beneath [The Queue](place_the_queue.md), never visited in ordinary operation: a state as stable as the good one, the way a hill has two hollows and all the walking happens in the upper (Bronson, Aghayev, Charapko and Zhu).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no trap, only a slow hour: a system with one stable state returns to it the moment the excess load goes, so the trigger passing would be the end of the incident rather than the start of the mystery.
It bears the load as the thing that makes persistence possible at all. Because the lower state is genuinely stable and not merely slow, the system has somewhere to settle and stay, and every later element, the loop that holds it and the gap that seals it, is only the machinery of a floor that had to exist first.

## Apparent

Nothing at all, even: it shows on no dashboard, has never been entered, and is not a property anyone designed in or would recognise on inspection.

## Yearbook

It works together with [The Gap](piece_the_gap.md), the floor the system falls to and the distance that keeps it there.
