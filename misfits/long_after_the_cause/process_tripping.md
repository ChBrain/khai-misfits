# Process: Tripping

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). Borne at [The Queue](place_the_queue.md), precipitated by a transient trigger landing while the system runs in its vulnerable band, in [One Bad Minute](plot_one_bad_minute.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A brief and unremarkable event: a collection pause, a deploy that empties a tier's cache, a dependency slow for ninety seconds, a burst of traffic large but not enormous.

## Direction

From the healthy state toward the far side of a threshold, latency pushed past the point where requests begin to time out and the responses to those timeouts begin to add load.

## Lever

That the same trigger is harmless in the stable state and sufficient in the vulnerable one, so nothing about the event itself marks it as the one that matters (Bronson, Aghayev, Charapko and Zhu).

## Echo

[The Trip](piece_the_trip.md), crossed and then over, a bump on a graph that will be finished within minutes.
