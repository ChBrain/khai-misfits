# Process: Retrying

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). Borne at [The Fleet](place_the_fleet.md), precipitated by [The Caller](persona_the_caller.md) reissuing what timed out, in [Everyone Tries Again](plot_everyone_tries_again.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A timed-out request and the documented, default-configured instruction that a failed request should be tried again.

## Direction

From one failure toward two arriving requests or three, the reissued attempts indistinguishable at the queue from first attempts, so each timeout returns as more load than it removed.

## Lever

That the retry is correct for the single request in front of the caller and the caller has no vantage on the fleet, so the guidance that is right per request is what multiplies the work in aggregate (Beyer, Jones, Petoff and Murphy).

## Echo

[The Loop](piece_the_loop.md), half made here: timeouts making retries and retries making the load that makes timeouts.
