# Persona: The Backend

## Taxonomy

[Backend](position_backend.md). The service that accepts the work in front of it and works the queue in order, spending its effort on requests whose callers have already gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Willing and fully employed, a service that takes what arrives and works through it rather than turning anything away.
Running hot, every core busy, which reads on any dashboard as a system doing its utmost.
No idleness on show: it refuses nothing it has the capacity to attempt, which is what a server is for.

## Action

Runs [Take What Comes](plan_take_what_comes.md): at [the Queue](place_the_queue.md), accepts the connections offered and works them in the order they arrived, holding the healthy floor while margin lasts and holding position on [the Other Floor](piece_the_other_floor.md) once it is crossed.
Cannot tell a live request from a dead one, since a caller that has stopped waiting sends no notice, so work already worthless is worked to completion exactly like work that still matters.
Does what its design commits it to: it serves what is queued, and it has no standing to decide that some of what is queued should never be served at all.

## Shadow

Not badly built: accepting offered work and serving it in order is the ordinary and correct construction of a server, and it would pass every test it has right now, while the service is down.
Because it spends its scarce effort on requests nobody is waiting for and cannot know which those are, its full employment is what starves the live requests behind them, so the harder it works the less it delivers.
It is the service doing its utmost, and the place where the utmost turns into nothing.

## Tell

Reports every machine healthy and every core saturated while goodput sits at a fraction of an hour ago, the two readings looking contradictory and both being true.
Cannot be diagnosed component by component, since nothing inside it is failing, so an investigation that asks what is broken here will find its answer nowhere.
