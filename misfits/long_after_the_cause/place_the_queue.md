# Place: The Queue

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). The service's intake, where load arrives, work waits, and both stable states are held. Hosts [A Floor Below the Floor](plot_a_floor_below_the_floor.md), [One Bad Minute](plot_one_bad_minute.md), [Everyone Tries Again](plot_everyone_tries_again.md), and [The Spike Has Passed](plot_the_spike_has_passed.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Requests arriving and being worked, a depth that is short on an ordinary afternoon and long on this one, every machine behind it up and busy.

## Holds

[The Other Floor](piece_the_other_floor.md), the second stable state waiting beneath the first; [The Trip](piece_the_trip.md) that crosses into it; [The Loop](piece_the_loop.md) that sustains it; [The Gap](piece_the_gap.md) that prevents leaving it; and [The Spin](piece_the_spin.md), full effort for a fraction of the goodput.

## Offers

To the backend, work to do, without limit and without any mark of which of it still matters.

## Withheld

Which requests still have someone waiting, since a departed caller sends no notice, so dead work and live work sit in the queue indistinguishable and are served alike.
