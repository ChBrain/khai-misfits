# Plot: Turn Away the Traffic

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Console](place_the_console.md), where someone reaches past the cause and past the components for the only lever that touches the loop.

## Action

[The Operator](persona_the_operator.md) on [Shed the Load](plan_shed_the_load.md) through [Shedding](process_shedding.md) holds [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): refuse a large share of arriving requests at the edge, drain the dead work rather than serve it, turn the retries off, and hold until arriving load falls under the recovery threshold, at which point the loop starves and the system climbs back. It means denying users the machines were able to serve, and going harder and longer than the situation appears to warrant, since anything above that threshold leaves the loop fed. The standing alternatives, budgeted retries, capped queues, and headroom carried idle, are each a bill paid daily against a rare day. The scene closes with the system recoverable at a real price and the lower floor still exactly where it was (Beyer, Jones, Petoff and Murphy).

## Stage

The operator is no defeatist: the shedding is the one move that reaches the mechanism, and every cheaper action, waiting included, leaves the loop with everything it needs.

## Tension

The correct move is indistinguishable in the moment from giving up, so it must be taken against instinct and without any display of how far is far enough, and every remedy here manages the fall without removing the floor to fall to.
