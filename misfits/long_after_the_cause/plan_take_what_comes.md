# Plan: Take What Comes

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the backend's seat](position_backend.md), inside [Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Backend](position_backend.md)

## Direction

Accept the connections offered and work the queue through in the order it arrived. Refusing work you have the capacity to attempt is not a thing a server is built to do, and a service that turned away requests it could serve would be failing at the only job it has. Serve in order and without discrimination, since no signal distinguishes a request whose caller is still waiting from one whose caller left long ago, and inventing such a distinction is not within the seat's standing.

## Orders

([The Backend](persona_the_backend.md) accepts and serves at [the Queue](place_the_queue.md), holding the healthy state while margin lasts and holding position on [the Other Floor](piece_the_other_floor.md) once the threshold is crossed.)

## Implementation

([The Queue](place_the_queue.md) is where accepted work waits and is served in arrival order; once the amplified load exceeds what the capacity can clear, much of that work is dead before it is reached, so the seat's effort is spent to completion on requests nobody awaits, and the live requests behind them wait longer and become dead in their turn.)

## Targets

- [ ] every offered connection the seat has capacity to attempt is accepted
- [ ] the queue is worked in arrival order, without a distinction the seat has no signal for
- [ ] full employment is maintained, the seat's only available measure of doing well
- [ ] no request is refused on the seat's own initiative, which its design does not permit
