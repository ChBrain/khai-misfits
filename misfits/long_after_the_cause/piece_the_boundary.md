# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). The condition and the hedge: the trap needs work amplification and a vulnerable band, and lifts under load shedding, budgeted retries, capped queues, or standing headroom, none of them free.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held at [The Console](place_the_console.md), where the levers that reach the loop are also the levers that deny service, and where the standing alternatives are priced on every quiet day against a failure that arrives rarely (Beyer, Jones, Petoff and Murphy).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overclaims: without the boundary the mechanism reads as the fate of every overloaded system rather than a conditional structure needing a genuine amplification path and a system already in its vulnerable band, when a system that recovers once its trigger ends was by definition never in one.
It bears the load as the honest limit and as the price list. Because every remedy is real and every remedy costs, refused users now or idle capacity always, the boundary is what keeps the play from resolving into a free correction, and it records that none of the fixes removes the lower state, only how hard it is to fall there.

## Apparent

A footnote, even: the caveats at the end of a mechanism that otherwise reads as settled and complete.

## Yearbook

Stands over the close in [Turn Away the Traffic](plot_turn_away_the_traffic.md), the remedy named and its bill named beside it.
It works together with [The Gap](piece_the_gap.md), the gap setting how hard the shedding must go and the boundary what going that hard costs.
