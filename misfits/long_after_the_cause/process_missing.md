# Process: Missing

## Taxonomy

[Long After the Cause](play_long_after_the_cause.md). Borne at [The Queue](place_the_queue.md), precipitated by [The Cache](persona_the_cache.md), emptied by the trigger, sending every request through, in [Everyone Tries Again](plot_everyone_tries_again.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A cache emptied by an ordinary restart or deploy, holding nothing it can serve and obliged by its contract to fetch what it lacks.

## Direction

From a warm layer absorbing most reads toward a cold one passing all of them, backend load multiplied by exactly the factor a warm cache divides it by.

## Lever

That the answers needed to warm the store are the answers the saturated backend can no longer return in time, so the coldness generating the load is sustained by the load it generates (Huang, Magnusson and colleagues).

## Echo

[The Loop](piece_the_loop.md), the other half made here: a cache that cannot warm because it is cold, and is cold because it cannot warm.
