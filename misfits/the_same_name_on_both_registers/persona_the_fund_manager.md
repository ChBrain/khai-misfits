# Persona: The Fund Manager

## Taxonomy

[Fund Manager](position_fund_manager.md). The one who must hold every name in the index.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A mandate to track an index, a tracking-error limit, and a list of constituents that includes every airline there is.

## Action

Holds them all, in the weights the index specifies.
Votes the shares, on governance, and does not discuss route networks with anybody.

## Shadow

Cannot decline to own the rivals, because owning the whole list is what the product is.
A tracking fund that left out one competitor would be a different product and a worse one, and would breach the mandate it sold. So the overlap is not an investment view, a strategy or an oversight: it is the definition of the instrument, and the seat has no version of itself that avoids it while remaining the thing it was bought to be.

## Tell

Ends up the proprietor of both, by doing exactly what it promised.
