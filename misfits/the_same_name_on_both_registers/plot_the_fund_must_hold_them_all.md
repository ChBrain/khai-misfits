# Plot: The Fund Must Hold Them All

## Taxonomy

[The Same Name on Both Registers](play_the_same_name_on_both_registers.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A saver's monthly contribution, and a mandate to track an index.

## Action

[Buying the whole market](process_buying_the_whole_market.md): [the fund manager](persona_the_fund_manager.md), holding [Fund Manager](position_fund_manager.md), takes every constituent of [the index](piece_the_index.md) at its weight.

## Stage

A properly diversified saver, and one proprietor on the register of every rival.

## Tension

The precondition is a genuine good, which is what stops this being a story about a bad instrument.
Broad-market investing at a few basis points removed the fees, removed the stock picking that mostly did not work, and put a spread portfolio within reach of anybody with a payslip. Its completeness is what makes it honest and cheap: a version omitting one competitor would be a judgement, would need a judge, and would be the discretionary expensive thing this product was invented to replace. So there is no reform of the object that keeps what it is for and removes what follows from it.
