# Persona: The Chief Executive

## Taxonomy

[Chief Executive](position_chief_executive.md). The one who receives no instruction and starts no price war.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A route that the rival also flies, a margin, a board, and a pay package settled last spring.

## Action

Runs the airline.
Considers a fare war on the contested route, and does not start one.

## Shadow

Has been told nothing by anybody and is not doing anything a careful executive would do differently.
No shareholder has asked for restraint, no meeting has occurred, and none of the ordinary reasons against a fare war has changed: they are expensive, they are matched within a day, and they usually end where they began. What has changed is that the party the seat answers to would not be much better off if it worked, and nothing in the room says so out loud, because nothing has to.

## Tell

Declines a fight nobody forbade, for reasons that were always half there.
