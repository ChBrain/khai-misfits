## Cue

## Action

[Setting the pay](process_setting_the_pay.md): a remuneration committee designs [the pay package](piece_the_pay_package.md) against the executive's own results rather than against the rival's.

## Stage

A defensible package, settled for reasons that have nothing to do with any of this.

## Tension

The channel exists and carries no message, which is what separates a transmission from an instruction.
Measuring an executive against their own results is argued on its own merits, that a person should not be punished for an industry-wide bad year nor paid for a good one they did not cause, and the argument is sound and predates the question entirely. It also removes whatever reward there was for taking share from the competitor. So the objection is answered without anybody having said anything: the committee was thinking about fairness to one person, and what it built is the route by which an ownership structure reaches a fare.
