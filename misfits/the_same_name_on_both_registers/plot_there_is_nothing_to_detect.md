## Cue

## Action

## Stage

Full powers, properly exercised, over a file with nothing in it.

## Tension

The instrument is correct and aimed at an object that is not there, and no sharpening of it would help.
Competition law is built around acts: an agreement, an understanding, a concerted practice, something somebody did that can be proved and punished. Here everything is either lawful or absent. There is no agreement because none was made, and the holdings are the ordinary consequence of a product millions of people are better off for owning, so restricting them means taking that product away from those people. The seat is not failing to enforce and its powers are not weak: they were built to find conduct, and this is not conduct.
