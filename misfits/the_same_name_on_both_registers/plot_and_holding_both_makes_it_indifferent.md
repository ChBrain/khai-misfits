# Plot: And Holding Both Makes It Indifferent

## Taxonomy

[The Same Name on Both Registers](play_the_same_name_on_both_registers.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two shareholder registers, filed publicly, identical in their largest entries.

## Action

[The Overlap](piece_the_overlap.md) is read at [the register](place_the_register.md), and the arithmetic of a proprietor who owns both is done.

## Stage

An owner who gains nothing when one of its holdings takes share from another of them.

## Tension

Indifference is not hostility to competition, and the distinction is the whole of what makes this stageable.
Nobody here wants prices high or output low: the proprietor simply does not benefit from a transfer between two things it already owns, in the way a person owning both shops on a street is not served by one undercutting the other. That follows from arithmetic rather than from intention, nobody made it, nobody maintains it, and it would persist if every person involved were replaced tomorrow. It is a structure and not an act, which is why the disclosure already in place changes nothing.
