## Stage

## Tension

Careful people reach opposite conclusions from overlapping data, and neither is being careless.
Ownership is not assigned at random, the concentration measures rest on assumptions about control that are themselves argued over, and capacity, fuel, hubs, slots and demand all move together on the same routes, so the contribution of the ownership structure is the one term with no independent measurement. The play therefore stages the structure, which the disputants share, and never a magnitude, which they do not: the argument is the state of the knowledge rather than a stage on the way to one.
