## Load Bearing

Its virtue and the mechanism are one property: it holds everything, so it holds the rivals together.
Completeness is what makes the product honest, cheap and impossible to get wrong, and it is why a saver on a payslip can have what once required a private banker. A version that omitted one competitor would be a judgement, would need a judge, and would be the expensive discretionary thing this instrument was invented to replace. So there is no reform of the object that keeps what it is for and removes what follows from it.
