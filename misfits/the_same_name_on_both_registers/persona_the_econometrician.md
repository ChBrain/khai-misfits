# Persona: The Econometrician

## Taxonomy

[Econometrician](position_econometrician.md). The one asked how much of it is real.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Route-level fares, ownership data assembled from filings, and a specification that has to separate one effect from several.

## Action

Estimates.
Is answered by somebody competent estimating differently.

## Shadow

Is doing the work correctly and is up against a question that resists the instrument.
Ownership is not assigned at random, the concentration measures depend on assumptions about control that are themselves contested, and the same routes carry capacity, fuel, hubs and demand shocks moving together. Careful people reach opposite conclusions from overlapping data and neither is being careless. So the structure is agreed and the magnitude is not, and the argument is the state of the knowledge rather than a stage before it.

## Tell

Produces a defensible number that another defensible number contradicts.
