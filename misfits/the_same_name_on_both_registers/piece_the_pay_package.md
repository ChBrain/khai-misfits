## Load Bearing

It answers the objection that nobody could have told anybody, and it answers it without an instruction.
A package measured against the executive's own results rather than against the rival's is defended on its own merits, that a person should not be punished for an industry-wide bad year nor paid for a good one they did not cause, and that argument is sound and predates all of this. It also removes whatever reward there was for taking share from the competitor. So the transmission is a design choice made for unrelated reasons, which is the difference between a channel and a message.
