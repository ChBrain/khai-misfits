# Persona: The Saver

## Taxonomy

[Saver](position_saver.md). The one whose money it is, and who is better off than before.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A monthly contribution, forty years, and a product that costs a few basis points and holds the whole market.

## Action

Buys the index.
Gets diversification that was once available only to people who were already wealthy.

## Shadow

Is the reason the arrangement exists and is genuinely served by it.
Cheap broad-market investing is one of the few unambiguous improvements in the century's financial history: it removed the fees, removed the stock picking that mostly did not work, and put a spread portfolio within reach of anybody with a payslip. Nothing about this seat is a compromise or a lesser evil, which is what makes the difficulty downstream of it structural rather than a price somebody chose to pay.

## Tell

Is served well, and the serving is the precondition.
