# Persona: The Regulator

## Taxonomy

[Regulator](position_regulator.md). The one with an instrument built to find agreements.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Powers to compel documents, to interview under caution, and to fine a cartel a tenth of its turnover.

## Action

Looks for the meeting, the message, the understanding.
Finds a shareholder register, filed publicly, in order.

## Shadow

Holds an instrument that is correct, proportionate and aimed at the wrong object.
Everything the law can reach here is either lawful or absent: there is no agreement to prove because none was made, and the holdings it might restrict are the ordinary consequence of a product millions of people are better off for owning. So the seat is not failing to enforce, and its powers are not inadequate: they were built to find an act, and there is no act.

## Tell

Has full powers over conduct, and the mechanism is not conduct.
