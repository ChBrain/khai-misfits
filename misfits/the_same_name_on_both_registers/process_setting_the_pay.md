# Process: Setting the Pay

## Taxonomy

[The Same Name on Both Registers](play_the_same_name_on_both_registers.md). The ordinary decision that carries the signal, without being one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A remuneration committee with a package to design and a spring deadline.

## Direction

Toward a package that rewards what the executive controls.

## Lever

The standing argument for absolute over relative measures, that an executive should not be punished when the whole industry has a bad year nor paid for a good one they did not cause.

## Echo

[A pay package](piece_the_pay_package.md) that is defensible on its own terms, and pays the same whether the rival does badly or well.
