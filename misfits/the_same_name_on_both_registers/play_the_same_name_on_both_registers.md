# Play: The Same Name on Both Registers

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Same Name on Both Registers.
Two airlines compete on a route. Their shareholder lists are public, and the largest entries are the same handful of funds, because those funds track an index and an index holds everything. A proprietor of both is not served when one takes passengers from the other. Nobody has said this to anybody.

## Arc

The bend is this: a product that made diversification cheap for ordinary savers puts one proprietor on the register of every rival, and a proprietor of both gains nothing from the contest between them.
It runs on a genuine good.
[The Fund Must Hold Them All](plot_the_fund_must_hold_them_all.md). [The Index](piece_the_index.md) is complete because completeness is what makes it honest and cheap, and a version omitting one competitor would need a judge and would be the discretionary expensive thing this product replaced.
And holding both makes it indifferent.
[And Holding Both Makes It Indifferent](plot_and_holding_both_makes_it_indifferent.md). [The Overlap](piece_the_overlap.md) follows from arithmetic rather than intention: nobody wants prices high, the proprietor is simply not served by a transfer between two things it already owns.
And nobody instructs anybody.
[Nobody Instructs Anybody](plot_nobody_instructs_anybody.md). [The Chief Executive](persona_the_chief_executive.md) declines a fare war for reasons that were always partly good, having been told nothing by anybody.
Then [The Signal Travels Through Ordinary Pay Design](plot_the_signal_travels_through_ordinary_pay_design.md).
[The Pay Package](piece_the_pay_package.md) measures an executive against their own results rather than the rival's, which is argued on its own merits and predates the question, and which removes whatever reward there was for taking share.
And [there is nothing to detect](plot_there_is_nothing_to_detect.md). [The Regulator](persona_the_regulator.md) holds powers built to find an agreement, and everything at [the register](place_the_register.md) is either lawful or absent.
And [whether it happened cannot be settled](plot_and_whether_it_happened_cannot_be_settled.md). [The Econometrician](persona_the_econometrician.md) estimates the effect and is answered by somebody competent estimating differently, because the one term at issue has no independent measurement.

## Triggers

**[The Fund Must Hold Them All](plot_the_fund_must_hold_them_all.md)**
Cue: a saver's monthly contribution and a mandate to track an index. Closes when the precondition is established as a genuine good whose completeness cannot be reformed away without restoring the thing it replaced.

**[And Holding Both Makes It Indifferent](plot_and_holding_both_makes_it_indifferent.md)**
Cue: two registers, filed publicly, identical in their largest entries. Closes when indifference has been separated from hostility, since nobody wants prices high and a proprietor is simply not served by a transfer between its own holdings.

**[Nobody Instructs Anybody](plot_nobody_instructs_anybody.md)**
Cue: a contested route and a fare war that would be expensive and might work. Closes when the decision is found to be the shareholders' interest competently served, with no conversation anywhere.

**[The Signal Travels Through Ordinary Pay Design](plot_the_signal_travels_through_ordinary_pay_design.md)**
Cue: the proper objection, that if nobody says anything the incentive cannot arrive. Closes when a channel is found that carries no message, designed by people thinking about fairness to one person.

**[There Is Nothing to Detect](plot_there_is_nothing_to_detect.md)**
Cue: an authority with full powers over conduct. Closes when everything available is either lawful or absent, so the instrument is correct and aimed at an object that is not there.

**[And Whether It Happened Cannot Be Settled](plot_and_whether_it_happened_cannot_be_settled.md)**
Cue: a published finding and a profession that checks things. Closes when competent people are found reaching opposite conclusions from overlapping data, so the structure is agreed and the magnitude is not.

## Stakes

Whether competition can thin without anybody having agreed to anything.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a product that genuinely improved ordinary savers' lives, a completeness that cannot be reformed without restoring what it replaced, an indifference that is arithmetic rather than intention, a decision taken with no instruction, a channel built by people thinking about something else, an authority whose powers are correct and aimed at no act, and a magnitude that competent people cannot agree on, and away from any account in which funds coordinated an industry or executives were told to go easy. The production cannot fault the saver for buying the index, because it is the best cheap thing that happened to people on a payslip, and it cannot fault the fund for holding the whole list, because holding the whole list is what the product is. What is left to fight over is not who conspired but what to call a market that competes less with nothing to prosecute.
