# Persona: The One Who Reasons

## Taxonomy

[One Who Reasons](position_one_who_reasons.md). The thinker, who does everything right.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A competent mind, following the rules, checking its work, in no hurry.
With no doctrine to defend and nothing riding on the answer.

## Action

Puts the question, derives the thesis, derives the antithesis, and looks for its mistake.
Finds none, because there is none.

## Shadow

Is left holding a contradiction with a clean record.
There is no point in the sequence at which a different or more careful person would have done otherwise, so the failure cannot be assigned to haste, bias, training or want of rigour, and greater rigour produces the same two proofs faster.

## Tell

Is the one participant who could have been blamed, and cannot be.
