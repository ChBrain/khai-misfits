# Piece: The Question

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Place

## Load Bearing

It is the only thing in the room that is actually broken.
Both proofs are answers to it and both inherit its defect, which is that it presupposes [the complete series](piece_the_complete_series.md) as something given: so the contradiction is not a sign that one argument is wrong, it is a sign that the question admits no answer of the kind being offered.

## Apparent

The most innocuous item on the table. Short, grammatical, and apparently about the world.
The thing nobody thinks to inspect, because the arguments look like the thing to inspect.

## Yearbook

Takes the whole quarrel with it when it goes: two of the four conflicts have both sides false once it is withdrawn, and two may have both sides true.
Is also the question reason cannot stop asking, so withdrawing it settles the dispute and not the pull.
