# Persona: The Drive to Finish

## Taxonomy

[Drive to Finish](position_drive_to_finish.md). Reason's own demand, and the engine of the whole thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The rule that a conditioned thing must be referred to what conditions it, applied again to that, and again, until nothing is left unaccounted for.
Not an appetite and not a defect: the reason the faculty is worth having.

## Action

Asks what this rests on. Then asks what that rests on.
Declines to stop at an answer that leaves a condition standing.

## Shadow

Cannot be told where to stop without being told to stop being itself.
The demand is not calibrated to the reach of experience and was never built to be: it is a rule about conditions, and a condition does not announce whether it lies inside or outside anything checkable, so the same rule that makes the faculty rigorous is what carries it off the map.

## Tell

Is at its most rigorous exactly where it is least entitled.
