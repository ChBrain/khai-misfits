# Plot: The Question Was the Fault

## Taxonomy

[Sound Both Ways](play_sound_both_ways.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An exhausted quarrel, and the suspicion that both sides are answering something malformed.

## Action

[Throwing out the question](process_throwing_out_the_question.md): [the question](piece_the_question.md) is examined at last, rather than the answers.

## Stage

It presupposes [the complete series](piece_the_complete_series.md) as something given, so in the first two conflicts both sides are false and in the second two both may be true.

## Tension

The remedy costs the whole ambition, and cannot be declined.
The series is a task and not a thing, a rule for going on rather than an object with a size, so a question about its extent has no answer of the kind either side was offering. Dissolving the dispute therefore works, and what it establishes is that the questions reason cannot help asking are exactly the ones it is not entitled to answer.
