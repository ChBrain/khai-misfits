# Plot: Checked and Checked Again

## Taxonomy

[Sound Both Ways](play_sound_both_ways.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two conclusions that cannot both be true, and the obvious response of finding the bad step.

## Action

[The One Who Reasons](persona_the_one_who_reasons.md), on [Settle It](plan_settle_it.md), works [the proof](piece_the_proof.md) over in [the court](place_the_court.md), slowly, and then again.

## Stage

Both arguments survive. No false premise is located, because there is not one to locate.

## Tension

Every remedy available runs the search in the wrong place.
The fault is upstream of both arguments, in [the question](piece_the_question.md) they are both answering, and nothing inside an examination of the answers can point at the question. So care, rigour and time, which are the only tools the seat has, reproduce the contradiction faster rather than resolving it, and the more competent the reasoner the sooner they arrive at it.
