# Persona: The Illusion That Stays

## Taxonomy

[Illusion That Stays](position_illusion_that_stays.md). The pull, which survives being named.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The propensity to take a necessity in how we must connect our concepts for a necessity in how things are.
Inseparable from the faculty rather than laid on top of it.

## Action

Persists after exposure.
Goes on presenting the completed series as though it were an object.

## Shadow

Is not the kind of thing instruction removes.
An illusion produced by a mistake goes when the mistake is pointed out, and this one does not, because it issues from the same demand for completeness that makes reason work: exposing it lets a person name what has hold of them, and changes nothing about the grip.

## Tell

Is the reason the critique is a warning rather than a cure.
