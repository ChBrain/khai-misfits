# Place: The Court

## Taxonomy

[Sound Both Ways](play_sound_both_ways.md). Where reason is arraigned, by itself, on its own motion.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A question stated precisely, a proof entered, a counter-proof entered after it, and every step available for inspection.
No haste, no rhetoric, and no one present who is out of their depth.

## Holds

[The Two Proofs](persona_the_two_proofs.md), each formally in order, neither yielding to the other.
[The Question](piece_the_question.md), which nobody has yet thought to examine, because the arguments look like the thing to examine.

## Offers

Every remedy a careful thinker has: restate, re-derive, hunt the false premise, check the form again.
A standard of proof that has never once failed inside the country it was proved on.

## Withheld

Any test that separates the two proofs.
The court can establish that each argument is sound and cannot rank soundness against soundness, so the procedure that was supposed to settle the matter returns it twice with opposite signs: what is missing is not rigour, of which there is a surplus, but any ground on which rigour could decide.
