# Position: Unmarked Bound

## Taxonomy

[Sound Both Ways](play_sound_both_ways.md). The seat that says nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A limit on what can be confirmed, and no means of signalling itself.

## Orders

Be crossed without event.

## Loses

Nothing, since it is not an agent and not an obstacle.
What the seat withholds is a warning, and it withholds it structurally rather than by omission: a limit on confirmation cannot be encoded in the form of an inference, because form is exactly what does not vary across the border.

## Drives

Toward being locatable only in hindsight, by the wreck that shows up later.
