# Plot: A Proof, and Then Its Contrary

## Taxonomy

[Sound Both Ways](play_sound_both_ways.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A question about the world as a whole, put properly, with the time to answer it.

## Action

[Proving it](process_proving_it.md), and then [proving the contrary](process_proving_the_contrary.md), by [the one who reasons](persona_the_one_who_reasons.md) with no change of standard between them.

## Stage

[The Two Proofs](persona_the_two_proofs.md) stand together: a beginning in time is demonstrated, and then demonstrated to be impossible.

## Tension

The instrument does not fail silently, it fails confidently and in both directions at once.
A method that produced nothing past its domain would be safe, and one that produced a wrong answer would be correctable. This one produces two answers, each carrying the same certificate, so its output is not merely unreliable out here: it is self-cancelling while remaining, step by step, exactly what good reasoning looks like.
