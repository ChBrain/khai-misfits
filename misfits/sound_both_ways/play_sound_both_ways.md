# Play: Sound Both Ways

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Sound Both Ways.
Ask whether the world has a beginning in time. There is a proof that it must: an infinite series of past states could never have been completed, so a first moment is required. There is also a proof that it cannot: a first moment would need an empty time before it, and an empty time offers no reason for anything to begin then rather than earlier. Both are valid. Neither contains a step you can strike out. Put the same question about the divisibility of matter, about freedom against causation, about whether anything exists necessarily, and the same thing happens each time: a proof, and then its contrary, from the same faculty on the same day.

## Arc

The bend is this: the instrument does not fail silently, it fails confidently, and in both directions at once.
It runs on a faculty doing exactly what it is for.
[The Drive to Finish](persona_the_drive_to_finish.md) is not a flaw and not an appetite. Reason's business is to take a conditioned thing and ask what it rests on, and to go on asking, because an answer that leaves a condition unaccounted for is not yet an answer. That demand is what makes reason worth having. It is also what carries it past the last state of affairs any experience could confirm.
[The Unmarked Bound](persona_the_unmarked_bound.md) is what makes it a trap rather than an error. The edge of possible experience is not a wall and does not announce itself. Nothing in a valid inference reports which side of the edge its terms fall on, so the step that leaves the checkable world looks exactly like the step before it, and there is no moment at which the reasoner is told to stop.
So [the two proofs](persona_the_two_proofs.md) arrive, and [Checked and Checked Again](plot_checked_and_checked_again.md) is the part that closes every exit. Examine the thesis and it holds. Examine the antithesis and it holds. Look for the false premise and there is none to find, because the fault is not in either argument: it is in the question both are answering, which presupposes a completed series that neither the world nor the mind can furnish.
[The One Who Reasons](persona_the_one_who_reasons.md) has therefore made no mistake anywhere, and is left holding two conclusions that cannot both be true, with a method that has never once misfired inside its own country.
Then there is a remedy, and its price. Kant's own is to throw out the question: to show that in the first two conflicts both sides are false, since the series they quarrel over is not a thing at all, and that in the second two both may be true, once appearance and what stands behind it are told apart. That works, and it costs the whole ambition. The questions that reason cannot help asking are the ones it is now forbidden to answer.
And [And It Comes Back](plot_and_it_comes_back.md), which is the last turn and the hardest. The pull is not a habit that instruction corrects. Kant calls it an illusion inseparable from human reason, which even after its deceptiveness has been exposed will not cease to play tricks and entrap it into aberrations, so the critique does not remove the pull, it only lets you name what has hold of you while it does.

Cue: a question about the world as a whole, put properly, with the time to answer it. Closes when the thesis has been demonstrated and the antithesis demonstrated after it, by the same faculty, on the same day, with both standing.

**[Checked and Checked Again](plot_checked_and_checked_again.md)**
Cue: two conclusions that cannot both be true, and the obvious response of finding the bad step. Closes when both arguments have survived examination, no false premise has been located, and the search has been shown to be looking in the wrong place.

**[The Question Was the Fault](plot_the_question_was_the_fault.md)**

**[And It Comes Back](plot_and_it_comes_back.md)**
Cue: the critique written, the boundary drawn, the illusion named in public. Closes when the pull has returned anyway, and the gain has been shown to be the naming rather than the cure.

## Stakes

Whether a method can be trusted at a distance from the cases that made it.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a faculty following its own rule correctly, past a border that carries no marker, into a region where it goes on producing confident output, and away from any check that could have caught it from inside. The production cannot ask reason to stop demanding the condition of the condition, since that demand is the whole of what it is for, and it cannot ask the reasoner to spot an error that is not there. What is left to fight over is not whether the thinking was good, it was, but what to do with an instrument that gives no sign of leaving the ground it was proved on, and that answers a question it cannot answer rather than declining it.
