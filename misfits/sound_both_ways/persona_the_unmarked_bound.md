# Persona: The Unmarked Bound

## Taxonomy

[Unmarked Bound](position_unmarked_bound.md). The edge of possible experience, which carries no sign.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The last point at which a claim could be confirmed or refuted by something encountered.
Not a wall, not a barrier, and not visible from either side.

## Action

Sits there.
Says nothing when it is crossed.

## Shadow

Is invisible from within a valid inference, which is what makes it a trap rather than an error.
Nothing in the form of an argument reports which side of the bound its terms fall on, so the step that leaves the checkable world is indistinguishable in every respect from the step before it, and there is no moment at which the reasoner could have been warned.

## Tell

Is discoverable only by the contradiction that appears long after it was passed.
