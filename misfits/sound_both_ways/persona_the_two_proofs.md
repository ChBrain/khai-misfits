# Persona: The Two Proofs

## Taxonomy

[Two Proofs](position_two_proofs.md). The thesis and the antithesis, both standing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Two demonstrations, each formally in order, arriving at conclusions that cannot both be true.
Produced by one faculty, on one question, without a change of method between them.

## Action

Hold.
Go on holding when examined.

## Shadow

Cannot be separated by any test the reasoner has.
The usual response to a contradiction is to find the bad step, and there is no bad step: both arguments are sound relative to the question, which means the search for the error is being run in the wrong place, and running it more carefully makes it no more likely to succeed.

## Tell

Are each other's refutation and neither one's.
