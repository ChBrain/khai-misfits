# Process: Throwing Out the Question

## Taxonomy

[Sound Both Ways](play_sound_both_ways.md). The remedy, and the size of its bill.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An exhausted quarrel, and the suspicion that the fault is upstream of both arguments.
[The Question](piece_the_question.md) examined at last, in [the court](place_the_court.md).

## Direction

Toward showing that the question presupposes [the complete series](piece_the_complete_series.md) as given.
Toward both sides false in the first two conflicts, and both possibly true in the second two.

## Lever

The distinction between what appears and what stands behind it, which dissolves the dispute by denying it its object.

## Echo

A boundary drawn, an ambition surrendered to draw it, and [the illusion that stays](persona_the_illusion_that_stays.md) entirely unaffected.
