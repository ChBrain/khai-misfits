# Process: Growing

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). What comes up where the canopy opened, and who it is for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Light reaching the forest floor.
At [the cutblock](place_the_cutblock.md), for as long as the stand stays young.

## Direction

Toward abundance, of a kind that is counted as a gain everywhere it is measured.
Browse is habitat. Moose, deer and elk do better, and any manager reporting more of them is
reporting a success.
And an animal whose winter food is lichen on old trees receives none of it, which is not unfairness
but botany.

## Lever

Succession, which nobody controls and which runs on its own clock.
The question is not whether the growth is good, since it is, but who it is good for and what follows
from that being only one of the two.

## Echo

More forage than the mature stand ever offered,
more moose than there were,
and nothing at all for the herd two valleys over.
