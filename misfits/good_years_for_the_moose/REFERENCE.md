# Good Years for the Moose: Reference

The research warrant for [Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md).
The setting is woodland caribou in a logged landscape; the play runs on the fact that two prey species sharing a
predator are coupled through that predator's numbers while sharing nothing else, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging apparent competition as **a harm with no relationship between the parties**: mature forest is harvested
under a licence and a plan, which is legitimate and produces habitat many species gain by; the early-seral growth
that follows feeds moose, deer and elk and feeds nothing that winters on arboreal lichen, so the subsidy is not
divided unevenly between the two prey but is not divided at all; a predator population tracks the prey it can
feed on, which is what a functioning system does; the caribou is then met rather than sought, at a rate set by
somebody else's abundance; the ultimate cause is stand age and the proximate driver is predation, so a complete
and accurate record points at the mechanism and never at the cause; and every fast remedy is a standing removal
of one animal or another. The play models the licensed cutting, the browse addressed to one species, the
numerical response, the encounter, and the annual programme, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as
a tale of destructive forestry or surplus wolves.

The governing law is that raising one prey lowers another through a shared predator's numbers, so a species can
be removed by the prosperity of one it never meets and competes with for nothing.

It is distinct from the house's **Who Else It Was Holding Down**, which stages mesopredator release: an apex
predator is removed and the predator beneath it is unleashed on its prey. Switch that engine off, stipulating no
apex removal and no release anywhere, and this runs unchanged, because **the predator here is not unleashed but
fed**: its rank is untouched, its numbers rise from a food subsidy, and nothing has been taken out of the system.

It is distinct from the house's **Never Aimed at You**, which stages collateral selection: an agent applied to
one organism selects in another that happened to be exposed. Stipulate that nothing is applied to anybody, no
agent and no exposure, and this stands entire, because **the harm here travels through a population that grew
rather than through a treatment aimed at somebody else.** Both are harms to a non-target and the carriers are
different in kind.

It is distinct from the house's **The Lie of the Land**, which stages the ecological trap, where a cue that once
predicted quality no longer does and an animal chooses badly. Stipulate every cue perfectly honest, and this
survives, because **the caribou chooses correctly and is not attracted to anything**: it keeps to the habitat
that suits it, and the change is in how often something crosses it.

It is distinct from the house's **More Mouths Than Meat**, whose engine is the lagged oscillation of one predator
against one prey. Stipulate no lag at all, with instantaneous response, and this is unchanged, because the
mechanism needs **three** species and no cycle: the coupling is a standing relation between two prey, not a phase
relation between a pair.

It is distinct from the house's **Room to Grow**, which stages the hydra effect, where mortality raises
abundance. That misfit is a warning about culling and this play's remedy is a cull, which makes the relation
worth stating: switch the hydra engine off, stipulating that removal always lowers abundance, and this is
untouched, because the difficulty with the programme here is not that it backfires but that **it works and
cannot stop.**

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the forester as
  destructive, the wolves as surplus, the moose as invaders or the managers as squeamish, because nothing in the
  sequence has a decision to get wrong: the harvest is licensed, planned and replanted, and lists the caribou as
  a constraint which is observed; early-seral forest is habitat and many species gain by it; a predator
  population tracking its prey is what a working system has and every biologist would defend the result; the
  caribou changes nothing and has nothing to change; and the programme that removes animals is effective and is
  run by people who would rather not. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the
  misfit is that two species with no relationship are coupled through a third.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Reducing the predator works
  while it runs and must run again, because the alternative prey and the young forest are unchanged. Reducing
  the alternative prey works, lowers predator density with it, and kills a valued animal for eating what grew.
  Restoring the habitat addresses the ultimate cause on a timescale measured in the lives of trees not yet
  planted, which is the correct answer and cannot arrive. And doing none of it leaves a schedule everybody can
  already read. **The remedy's effectiveness is what makes it permanent**, since it treats the mechanism while
  the cause is a stand age.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the theory of apparent competition and the numerical coupling of
  two prey through a shared predator are Holt's, the experimental reduction of the alternative prey and its
  effect on predator density and herd trend are Serrouya and colleagues', the comparison between removal options
  is McLellan and colleagues', and the equivocal recent finding is Tjaden-McClement and colleagues'. The bounds
  are carried with them, and two are owed plainly. **None of the cited works was read in full**, each being
  behind publisher access from this environment, so each is cited for the question it addresses and its findings
  as corroborated from several independent reports, and **no figure appears anywhere in this production that
  those reports did not state**: the play carries no density, no rate and no count, and names none. And the
  second is the size of the claim. The mechanism is established in theory and documented in this system, and
  **recent work in part of the same range reports mixed evidence for the disturbance-mediated form**, which is
  carried as its own row, its own piece and a plot's closing line rather than argued past. The play stages a
  structure that occurs where its conditions hold and claims nothing about how generally they hold.
