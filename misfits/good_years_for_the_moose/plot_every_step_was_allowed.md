# Plot: Every Step Was Allowed

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A licence, an approved plan, a replanting schedule and a compliance file.

## Action

[Cutting](process_cutting.md): [The Harvest Plan](persona_the_harvest_plan.md), in [the disturbance seat](position_disturbance.md), is executed at [the cutblock](place_the_cutblock.md) on [Take the Timber](plan_take_the_timber.md).

## Stage

[The Caribou](persona_the_caribou.md), on [Keep to the Old Forest](plan_keep_to_the_old_forest.md), listed in the plan as a constraint and respected as one.

## Tension

The cutting has to be established as **allowed** before anything else is worth saying, because the
easy reading of this play is that somebody logged a herd to death.
Nobody did. The licence is real, the plan is approved, the rotation is regulated and the block is
replanted on schedule, and early-seral forest is not damage: it is habitat, and a great many species
do better for it, which is why the plan would be signed again tomorrow by anybody reading it.
The caribou are in the document. They appear as a constraint, the constraint is observed, and the
outcome is unaffected by that, which is the first thing the play has to show.
**The harm is not in a clause.** It is in what grows back, and no plan can make regeneration
conditional on which species benefits from it.
