## Tension

Here the settlements are tried, and each is a killing programme or a century.
**Reduce the wolves**: the decline slows while it runs, and the moose and the young forest are still
there next spring, so it runs again.
**Reduce the moose**: this works too, lowers wolf density with it, and kills fewer wolves by killing
a valued animal that did nothing but eat what grew where the light came in. The choice the evidence
offers is which animal, not whether.
**Restore the habitat**: correct, and it is the ultimate cause, and it arrives on a timescale
measured in the lives of trees that are not planted yet.
**Do none of it**: the herd goes, on a schedule everybody can already read.
And the size of the claim is fixed by the last row: the mechanism is established in theory and
documented here, and recent work in the western part of the range reports mixed evidence for it.
**A structure that runs where its conditions hold is the only kind this house stages**, and the play
adjudicates none of the rest.
