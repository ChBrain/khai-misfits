# Process: Cutting

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). Mature forest harvested to a plan, on a licence, with every constraint observed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A licence and an approved harvest plan.
At [the cutblock](place_the_cutblock.md), over a season, and repeated across a landscape over decades.

## Direction

Toward timber, and toward a regenerating stand.
This is a regulated industry doing a regulated thing, and early-seral forest is habitat rather than
damage: a great many species do better for it, which is why the plan is signed and why it would be
signed again.
So the play does not stage this as vandalism. It stages it as the ordinary operation of a legitimate
activity.

## Lever

The plan, which lists the caribou as a constraint and respects it as written.
The question is not whether the constraint was honoured but whether a constraint of that kind can
reach what actually happens.

## Echo

Timber out,
a block replanted on schedule,
and ten years later a stand full of things a moose eats.
