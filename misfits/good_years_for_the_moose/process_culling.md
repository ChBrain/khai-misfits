# Process: Culling

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). The removal programme: wolves or moose, annually, with results.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A herd trend nobody is prepared to watch to its end.
Across [the range map](place_the_range_map.md), every season, by helicopter and by permit.

## Direction

Toward a stabilised count, which it achieves.
Reduce the wolves and the decline slows. Reduce the moose instead and the wolves fall with them,
which is the version that kills fewer wolves and kills moose to do it.
Both work while they run, and both act on the mechanism rather than on the forest.

## Lever

Direct removal, which is the only lever that operates on the timescale the herd has left.
The question is not whether it works, since it does, but what it means that the thing which works
cannot be finished.

## Echo

A herd holding,
a budget line renewed,
and a programme with no end condition anybody has written down.
