# Persona: The Programme

## Taxonomy

[Remedy](position_remedy.md). The recovery action: annual, effective, and a killing programme whichever animal it chooses.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A budget line, a helicopter, a season, and a number of animals removed.
Evaluated, reported, and by the evidence it works: the decline slows or stops while it runs.
Deeply unpopular with people who are not wrong to dislike it.

## Action

Removes wolves, or removes moose.
Reports a stabilised herd.
Comes back next year, because nothing underneath it has changed.

## Shadow

That its success is what makes it permanent.
It acts on the mechanism and not the cause, and the cause is a forest age, so **the programme cannot
finish**: it is not a treatment with a course, it is a standing subtraction from a living population,
and stopping it returns the herd to the schedule it was on.

## Tell

Every evaluation says it works.
No evaluation says when it ends.
