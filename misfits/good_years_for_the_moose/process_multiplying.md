# Process: Multiplying

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). Wolf numbers following moose numbers, which is what predator populations do.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Growing](process_growing.md), and a larger prey base.
Across the whole range on [the range map](place_the_range_map.md), over years.

## Direction

Toward a predator population in proportion to its food.
This is the numerical response and it is not a malfunction: a system whose predators do not track
their prey is a system with something wrong with it, and the resulting population is one every
biologist would defend.
So there is no excess here to trim and no behaviour to correct.

## Lever

Prey abundance, which is the only thing the pack is responding to.
The question is not whether there are too many wolves, which is a judgement nobody can make from
inside ecology, but what their number does to an animal that is not their prey base.

## Echo

More wolves, healthily,
ranging further because there is more to range for,
and a rate of meeting that has gone up for everything in the forest.
