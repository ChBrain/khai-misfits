# Place: The Cutblock

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). Where the timber came from and the browse came up: a licensed block, regenerating on schedule.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Stumps for a year or two, then shrubs, then a dense young stand full of food.
A replanting record, a compliance file, and moose where there were none.

## Holds

The whole mechanism, in a form nobody would call damage.
This is habitat by every definition a manager uses and would be reported as a gain in any other
context: the subsidy is real, valuable and addressed to species that were not in trouble.

## Offers

Timber, jobs, and forage.

## Withheld

Any visible connection to the animal that pays.
The caribou is not here, was never here, and could not use this if it were: the link between this
place and the empty range map is three species long and passes through nothing anybody can see from
inside the block.
