# Piece: Not the Ultimate Cause

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). The split between what kills the animals and what produced the killing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the range map](place_the_range_map.md), in every cause-of-death record.
In the standard formulation: habitat disturbance the ultimate cause, predation the proximate driver.

## Load Bearing

Without it a manager who follows the evidence is doing the right thing and the play is about
insufficient effort.
It is load bearing because **the evidence is complete, accurate and points at the mechanism**: every
record says predation and every record is right, and acting on what the records show means acting on
wolves, correctly, while the cause regenerates on schedule and keeps supplying them.

## Apparent

That better data would resolve it.
More data produces more records of predation, which is what is happening. The cause is not
under-observed; it is in the past and in the age of a forest, which no field season records.

## Yearbook

The distinction ecology draws between ultimate and proximate causes, applied to a management problem.
Naming which is which is the play's whole use of it.
