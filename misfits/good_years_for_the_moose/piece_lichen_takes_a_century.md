# Piece: Lichen Takes a Century

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). The clock the caribou runs on, and the reason no remedy can operate on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [the old forest](place_the_old_forest.md), on branches of trees that were standing before any of
this began.
In every recovery plan, as the option that is correct and cannot arrive in time.

## Load Bearing

Without it habitat restoration is a plausible answer and the play has an exit.
It is load bearing because the food is a property of **stand age**: it accumulates on old trees over
a period that no budget cycle, career or government reaches, so restoring the cause is a real
proposal on a timescale where the herd is already gone.

## Apparent

That replanting is restoration.
Replanting produces a young stand, which is the thing that started this, and the difference between
a planted stand and the forest the herd needs is measured in a hundred years of not cutting it.

## Yearbook

Forest ecology, taken at its word about how long its own processes take.
The play adds nothing to it and needs nothing added.
