## Cue

## Action

## Stage

[Meeting](process_meeting.md): a wolf that is out because there are moose crosses [the caribou](persona_the_caribou.md) at [the old forest](place_the_old_forest.md).

## Tension

Here the coupling closes, through an animal nobody would want fewer of.
Predator numbers follow prey numbers. That is the numerical response, it is not a malfunction, and a
system whose predators did not track their prey would be a system with something wrong with it. The
resulting wolf population is one every biologist in the room would defend.
More wolves range further, so the rate at which they meet everything goes up, and a caribou is met.
**It is met rather than sought.** It is not the pack's quarry, not its preference and not the reason
any of them are there.
So the two prey are joined at the hip by a third animal, with **no competition, no contact and no
shared resource** between them, which is what makes the relation apparent rather than real, and what
means there is no behaviour the herd could adopt to escape it.
