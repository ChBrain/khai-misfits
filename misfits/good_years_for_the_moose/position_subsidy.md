# Position: Subsidy

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). The seat of the new food: abundant, valuable, and addressed to one species and not the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Shrubs and saplings on ground that was closed canopy.
More forage for moose, deer and elk than the mature stand ever offered.
Nothing whatever for an animal that winters on arboreal lichen.

## Orders

Grow where the light is.
Feed what browses.
Persist for as long as the stand is young.

## Loses

The possibility of being shared.
This is not a resource the two prey species divide unevenly: **it is not divided at all**, since one
of them cannot eat it, so there is no competition here to referee and no allocation to argue over.

## Drives

Toward abundance,
which is what habitat is for and what everybody counts as a gain,
so the seat that adds food to the system is the seat that removes an animal from it.
