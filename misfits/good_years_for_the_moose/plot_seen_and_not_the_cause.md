## Stage

[The Harvest Plan](persona_the_harvest_plan.md), on [Take the Timber](plan_take_the_timber.md), twenty years old, in a valley the herd never used.

## Tension

Here the play's difficulty becomes a management difficulty, and it is not a shortage of evidence.
Every cause-of-death entry says predation and every one of them is accurate. The data are good, the
sample is real and the mechanism is exactly what the record shows.
**And the record has no column for a harvest plan signed twenty years ago.** Habitat is the ultimate
cause and predation the proximate driver, so following the evidence means acting on wolves,
correctly, on the strength of a complete and honest dataset.
More data does not help, because more data is more records of predation. The cause is not
under-observed: it is in the past, and it is the age of a forest, which no field season records and
no survey design would capture.
**So the manager who does exactly what the evidence supports acts forever**, while the thing
producing the wolves regenerates on schedule.
