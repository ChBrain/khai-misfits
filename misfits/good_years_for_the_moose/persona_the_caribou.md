# Persona: The Caribou

## Taxonomy

[Encounter](position_encounter.md). The herd in the old forest, which did nothing and is the one that is gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Spread thin across mature stands, wintering on lichen that grows on trees older than anybody's career.
Not numerous, not increasing, and not in anybody's way.
Present in the harvest plan as a line item and in the outcome as the whole of it.

## Action

Keeps to the old forest.
Competes with nobody.
Meets a wolf that was out for other reasons.

## Shadow

That doing nothing is not a defence.
It did not encroach, did not change, did not take anybody's food and has no relationship of any kind
with the species whose good years killed it: **the coupling runs entirely through a third animal**,
and there is no behaviour it could have altered to escape it.

## Tell

Is the only party with nothing to answer for.
Is the only party that is gone.
