# Persona: The Pack

## Taxonomy

[Response](position_response.md). Wolves, at whatever number the food supports, doing nothing a wolf should not do.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

More of them than there were, because there is more to eat than there was.
A population any conservation biologist would describe as healthy, in a system that has its predators.
Not managed, not introduced, not misbehaving.

## Action

Raises what can be fed.
Ranges.
Takes what it meets.

## Shadow

That its health is the transmission.
Predator numbers follow prey numbers, so the moose subsidy becomes wolf numbers and wolf numbers
become encounters, and **the caribou is met rather than sought**: it is not the pack's quarry, not
its preference, and not in any sense its purpose.

## Tell

Would be defended, correctly, by everyone who studies it.
Is the animal every remedy proposes to shoot.
