# Plot: The Browse Comes Up

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Light reaching ground that was closed canopy, and shrubs on it within a season or two.

## Action

[Growing](process_growing.md): [The Browse](persona_the_browse.md), in [the subsidy seat](position_subsidy.md), fills [the cutblock](place_the_cutblock.md) on [Grow Where the Light Is](plan_grow_where_the_light_is.md).

## Stage

[Lichen Takes a Century](piece_lichen_takes_a_century.md), on the branches two valleys over, running on the other clock.

## Tension

Here the subsidy arrives, and it arrives addressed to one party.
More forage than the mature stand ever offered, and moose, deer and elk do better for it. Every
measurement of this is a gain and would be reported as one in any other context.
And the herd receives **nothing**. Its winter food is lichen growing on old trees, which is a
property of stand age and not of light, so this is not a resource the two prey species divide
unevenly: it is not divided at all.
**There is no competition here to referee.** One animal cannot eat what the other is eating, has not
been displaced from anything, and would be entirely unaffected by any number of moose if nothing
else in the system responded to them.
