# Position: Encounter

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). The seat of the meeting: not a hunt, not a competition, and the whole of the mechanism's delivery.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An animal spread thin across mature forest, doing what it has always done.
No overlap of diet with the species whose abundance is killing it.
A rate of meeting that rises with somebody else's numbers.

## Orders

Keep to the old forest.
Take nothing from anybody.
Be met.

## Loses

Every behaviour it might have changed.
There is no encroachment to withdraw, no resource to concede and no habit to alter, because the
coupling runs entirely through a third animal: **the only variable is the other prey's abundance,
and it is not this seat's to move.**

## Drives

Toward the old stands and the lichen,
which is the correct strategy and always was,
so the seat with nothing to answer for is the seat that is gone.
