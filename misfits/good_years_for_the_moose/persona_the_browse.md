# Persona: The Browse

## Taxonomy

[Subsidy](position_subsidy.md). What comes up where the light gets in: shrubs, saplings, and a great deal of food.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Green, low, dense and abundant, on ground that was closed canopy ten years ago.
Exactly what a moose wants, and what deer and elk want.
Counted by ecologists as habitat, which it is.

## Action

Grows.
Feeds.
Keeps feeding for as long as the stand is young.

## Shadow

That its generosity is addressed to one party only.
An animal that winters on lichen growing on old trees gains **nothing at all** from any of this, so
the subsidy is not shared unevenly between the two prey species: it is not shared, and one of them
would not notice it if the other were not there to eat it.

## Tell

Nobody planted it and nobody regrets it.
It is the reason there are more moose, and the reason there is a play here.
