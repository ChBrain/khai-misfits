# Piece: Which Animal to Kill

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). The two fast remedies, both of which work, and both of which are a standing subtraction.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [the programme](persona_the_programme.md), every season, by helicopter and by permit.
In the management literature, where an experimental reduction of moose lowered wolf density and
stopped an endangered herd's decline.

## Load Bearing

Without it the remedy is merely unpopular and somebody could be told to be braver.
It is load bearing because both effective options are removals and neither ends: **reduce the wolves
and the moose and the young forest remain, so it must be done again; reduce the moose and the wolves
fall with them, which is the version that kills fewer wolves and kills moose to do it.** The choice
offered by the evidence is which animal, not whether.

## Apparent

That a working remedy settles the question.
Working is what makes it permanent, since it treats the mechanism while the cause is a stand age, so
success is the reason it cannot be finished rather than the reason it can stop.

## Yearbook

Adaptive management, evaluated and reported, including by the people who ran it.
The play takes the result at its word in both directions: it works, and it does not end.
