# Process: Meeting

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). A wolf and a caribou in the same place: not a hunt, and the whole of the delivery.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Multiplying](process_multiplying.md), and geometry.
At [the old forest](place_the_old_forest.md), at whatever rate the wolf density produces.

## Direction

Toward a kill that nobody sought.
The pack is out because there are moose. It crosses a caribou and takes it, which is what a wolf
does on meeting one, and which requires no preference for caribou and no strategy involving them.
So the harm is delivered by an encounter rate rather than by a pursuit, which is why nothing the
caribou does can reduce it.

## Lever

Wolf density, which is set by moose, which is set by browse, which is set by a plan signed years ago.
The question is not whether the wolf is at fault but how far back one has to go before anything in
the chain looks like a decision about caribou, and the answer is that nothing in it ever does.

## Echo

One animal fewer,
in a herd that was already thin,
and a record in a field season showing predation, which is true and is not the cause.
