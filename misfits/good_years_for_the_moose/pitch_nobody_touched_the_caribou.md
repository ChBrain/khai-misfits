# Pitch: Nobody Touched the Caribou

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Patient and exact about which animal did what, in front of a committee that wants somebody blamed.
Unwilling to hand them a culprit, because there is not one.
Quoting the count wherever the count is the point.

## Undertow

Under the recovery plan, one relation from theory and one from the ground.
Two prey sharing a predator are coupled through that predator's numbers, and never meet.
And what grows back after a cut feeds moose, and does not feed anything that eats lichen.

## Nerve

The unease of a true sentence that settles nothing.
Nobody touched the caribou: accurate, provable, said by everybody in the room,
and the herd is gone anyway, on a mechanism every one of them helped run.

## Echo

After the run: a valley cut to a plan nobody would rewrite,
a moose population any manager would be pleased with,
a wolf population that is what a working system has,
and a range map with a line on it that stops.
