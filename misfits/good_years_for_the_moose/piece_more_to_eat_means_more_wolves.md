# Piece: More to Eat Means More Wolves

## Taxonomy

[Good Years for the Moose](play_good_years_for_the_moose.md). The numerical response: the coupling between two prey that never meet.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the theory of apparent competition, where two prey sharing a predator are joined through its numbers.
On the ground, in a wolf population that tracks a moose population that tracks the browse.

## Load Bearing

Without it the two species are unrelated and the caribou decline needs a different explanation.
It is load bearing because it is the entire connection: there is **no competition, no contact and no
shared resource** between moose and caribou, and raising one still lowers the other, through a third
animal doing exactly what a predator population does.

## Apparent

That this is competition by another name.
Competition requires something contested. Nothing here is contested: the caribou would be unaffected
by any number of moose if the wolves were held fixed, which is what makes the relation apparent
rather than real.
