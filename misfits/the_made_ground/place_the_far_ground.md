# Place: The Far Ground

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). Ground beyond the made floor, not yet worked by anyone, where the Leaver goes for reprieve.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Ground with no floor on it: whatever the equivalent of clean soil is, for whatever the tending is done in.
Open, unclaimed by any accumulated residue, so it shows exactly what the first ground once showed, before there was a first ground to compare it to.

## Holds

The same raw capacity the made ground once held: nothing built up yet, nothing standing in the way, nothing already laid down by anyone else's tending.
It holds this only as an opening balance, not a promise: what it holds now is not what it will hold once anyone lives on it.

## Offers

A test genuinely lighter than the one left behind, for exactly as long as it stays untouched.
Reaching it truly does relieve the pressure the made ground had raised, so the offer is real, not a trick: the relief is honestly there to be taken.

## Withheld

Its own future.
The far ground cannot promise to stay clean, because staying clean would mean nobody lives there, and living there is the one thing the Leaver arrives to do: it withholds any guarantee that the relief outlasts the act of taking it up.
