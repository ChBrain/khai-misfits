# Process: Bequeathing

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The traits fit for the old ground and the ground as it now stands, passed on together, in one parcel, to whoever is born next.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A birth on ground that already carries a floor.
At [the ground](place_the_ground.md), an Heir arriving where the Settler once arrived, so the condition for bequeathing is simply that the two happen to land in the same place: the traits transmitted by one line, the floor left standing by the other, and nothing coordinating them on purpose.

## Direction

Toward one bundle, not two separate gifts.
Bequeathing does not hand over traits and then, later, hand over terrain: both arrive in the same act of being born here, so its whole motion is toward a single inheritance in which body and floor cannot be pulled apart or accepted separately.

## Lever

A parent's fitness meeting a floor the parent's fitness itself raised.
There is no resistance on either side: the traits are simply what worked, the floor is simply what accumulated, and bequeathing needs no force beyond the plain fact of succession to bind the two into one handover.

## Echo

An heir carrying a body built for a test it will not be given.
What is different afterward is that the traits and the ground they were shaped against are no longer matched, and the mismatch belongs to no one, having been assembled by two honest processes that never once consulted each other.
