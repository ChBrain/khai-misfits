# Persona: The Heir

## Taxonomy

[Heir](position_heir.md). Born after the floor is laid, and answers for a test it never set.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Capable, by the only measure it has ever known.
Meets the ground it was born into as simply how ground is, and works with the traits it was given, never having stood on any other floor to compare it against.
No complaint in it: the made ground is not felt as an imposition, because nothing else was ever on offer.

## Action

Takes up the traits handed down and puts them to the ground as it finds it.
Where those traits still fit, thrives plainly. Where the floor asks something the traits were never built for, meets that too, as best it can, without naming the gap a wrong done to it.
Carries on tending the same ground the same way, because tending is still, as it was for the parent, how a life here is lived.

## Shadow

Cannot see the floor as made, only as given.
The parent's ordinary living and the Heir's inherited ground are, from the Heir's side, a single unbroken fact rather than a cause and its consequence, so the test the floor now sets reads as the world's plain nature, never as a bill arriving from a labour it did not perform. It stands measured by a standard authored by the very acts that also gave it the traits to be measured with.

## Tell

Says "this is just how it is here."
Says it about ground that was, for its parent, still becoming what it now already is.
