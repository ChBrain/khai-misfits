# Pitch: You Made Your Bed

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Patient, unhurried, faintly geological.
The sense of watching a process too slow for any single moment to hold, where nothing that happens on screen ever looks like the cause of anything, and the whole cause is only visible once you stand far enough back.
Nothing hurried and nothing sinister: the pace of soil forming, not the pace of a scheme.

## Undertow

Under the patience, a quiet closing of every door.
Because the acts that build the floor are the same acts that keep the actor alive, there is no moment inside the run where stopping would look like anything but giving up on living itself, so the floor keeps rising exactly because no one ever has a reason, in the moment, to let it stop.

## Nerve

The particular unease of realizing a thing you did correctly, every single time, is the thing that built the wall you are now standing against,
and that doing it correctly again is the only way you know how to go on,
so the wall keeps rising with you.

## Echo

After the run: a floor that was never laid on any one day, standing all the same,
an heir measured by a test its parent never wrote down anywhere, because the writing was only living,
and a door marked exit that, walked through, turns out to lead to the same room, being built again.
