# Position: Settler

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The seat that works the ground to live in it, and is right that the work is fit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The competence to work the ground it stands on: to turn it, clear it, lay down what living needs.
A place it already fits, and no reason, from inside any single act, to treat the next act as different from the last.
No power over what the acts add up to. Only over each act, one at a time.

## Orders

Tend the ground.
Do exactly what fitness requires, and do it again the next time fitness requires it.
Read each round's success as reason enough to repeat the round.

## Loses

Any claim that the acts stayed where they were made.
Because tending does not undo itself, the seat cannot keep its acts confined to the moment each was performed: they persist, and persisting, they sum, so the seat that meant only to live ends up, without ever willing it, having built.
It cannot step outside the tending to check what the tending is doing, because checking is not tending, and stopping is not fitness.

## Drives

Toward a floor no single act intended and every act, together, raised,
right in every round to do exactly what it did,
so the same competence that keeps the seat fit here is the seat's whole, unwitting authorship of the ground it will one day answer to.
