# Plan: Bear the Test

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the mandate of [the heir's seat](position_heir.md), inside [The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Heir](position_heir.md)

## Direction

Meet the ground as it is found. Take up the traits handed down and put them to the floor that arrived with them, closing what gap can be closed and standing the rest, since the floor cannot be un-laid and the traits cannot be swapped for better ones. Treat the test as simply the ground's nature, because from where the plan starts, that is all it has ever been.
