# Process: Selecting

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The crust applying its new measure to whoever meets the ground now, chosen by no one and withdrawn by no one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A floor now standing where none stood before.
At [the ground](place_the_ground.md), [the crust](piece_the_crust.md) present and load-bearing, so anyone who meets the ground meets the crust too: the condition for selecting is simply the floor's existence, nothing further needed to switch it on.

## Direction

Toward a verdict on whoever stands there, never toward the floor itself.
Selecting does not add to the crust or reshape it: it reads whoever is on the ground against the crust as it already is, so its whole motion is the sorting of fit from unfit under a standard that was set by accumulation, not by any decision.

## Lever

A measure with no author to petition and no lever to withdraw it by.
Nobody laid the crust down on purpose, and nobody standing on it now can un-lay it without stopping the very tending that keeps them alive there, so selecting runs on a pressure that is, structurally, impossible to appeal.

## Echo

A ground that sorts differently than it once did.
What passed easily before does not automatically pass now, and what the floor rewards is set by what the floor happens to be, so the echo of selecting is a test standing in the world, indifferent to whether anyone chose to write it.
