# Process: Silting

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The residue of tending settling, round on round, into a real and load-bearing layer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Residue that does not undo itself.
At [the ground](place_the_ground.md), each round of [tending](process_tending.md) leaving something behind that the next round does not erase, so the condition for silting is met the moment a second act follows a first without clearing it.

## Direction

Toward thickness, never toward a threshold.
The residue simply adds to the residue already there: silting has no register for a moment large enough to call itself a change, only a running total, so it moves steadily toward a real difference while never producing, along the way, a single instant that looks like one.

## Lever

Sums that do not forget their addends.
The force here is only that residue persists, meeting no resistance at all, because nothing on the ground is clearing it away: silting runs on the plain arithmetic fact that things which are not undone remain, and remaining, they stack.

## Echo

A layer where there used to be none.
Once enough rounds have run, the ground carries [the crust](piece_the_crust.md), a floor genuinely different from what preceded it, though its entire history is made of moments that were, each one, indistinguishable from the last.
