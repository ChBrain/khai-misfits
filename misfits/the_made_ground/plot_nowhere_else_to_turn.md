# Plot: Nowhere Else to Turn

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Far Ground](place_the_far_ground.md), offering what the made ground no longer does: a test genuinely lighter, for as long as the ground stays untouched.

## Action

[Relocating](process_relocating.md): [The Leaver](persona_the_leaver.md), holding [the leaver's seat](position_leaver.md), moves for the far ground, taking the one correction actually on offer against a test that cannot be lifted where it stands.

## Stage

[The Leaver](persona_the_leaver.md), having tabled [Try Elsewhere](plan_try_elsewhere.md), arrives on ground with no floor on it yet, and does what living anywhere well requires: begins to work it.

## Tension

The move that reaches clean ground is the same move that starts dirtying it.
The instant [Tending](process_tending.md) resumes on the far ground, a new [crust](piece_the_crust.md) begins forming under the very feet that came here to escape one. Nothing was fled successfully, only relocated, because the competence that makes the far ground livable is, unchanged, the competence that made the last ground unlivable, and there is no third act that is neither tending nor its absence.
