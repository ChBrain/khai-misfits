# Position: Leaver

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The seat that reads the floor correctly and moves for ground that has not been altered yet.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The standing to leave the made ground and reach unaltered ground elsewhere.
A true reprieve waiting there, real for as long as that ground stays untouched.
No power to take the reprieve without also taking up, on arrival, the very competence that made the first floor.

## Orders

Try elsewhere.
Move for ground that has not yet been worked, since the only ground that offers relief is ground nobody has lived on correctly yet.
Live there the way living anywhere is done: work it, exactly as fitness requires.

## Loses

The exemption it moved to find.
Because living on the new ground is worked the same way the old ground was worked, the seat cannot arrive and merely rest: arriving already begins the next floor, so the seat trades a made test for a fresh countdown to the same one, and the fresh countdown starts the moment the standing still would look like the seat.

## Drives

Toward ground it did not make, for as long as that lasts,
right to have moved rather than stayed to bear a test not its own,
so the seat carries the whole mechanism with it wherever it goes, being the one part of the floor that travels.
