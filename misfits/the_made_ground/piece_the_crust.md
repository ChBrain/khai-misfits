# Piece: The Crust

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The floor built entirely out of ordinary living, standing where nothing was chosen to stand.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the ground](place_the_ground.md), where the tending that raised it never stops, so the crust is always the ground's newest, truest surface.
Beginning again at [the far ground](place_the_far_ground.md), the moment tending resumes there, because the crust is not a single object in a single spot but whatever tending, anywhere, is quietly laying down.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the acts that built it would stay acts, real in their moment and gone the next, and there would be no accumulated ground to answer for, only a life lived and finished as it was lived.
With it, the whole structure follows: an honest sum of honest acts stands as a floor nobody laid on purpose, applies a test nobody chose, and cannot be lifted without undoing the living that raised it. The Crust is load bearing because it is the mechanism itself, made solid: the misfit is not that anyone erred, it is that this is what accumulation without a threshold produces.

## Apparent

A layer, denser and different in kind from what lies beneath it, the visible residue of everything worked into the ground over however long the tending has run.
Seen up close it looks like nothing more than settled ground, ordinary, unremarkable, the same as any other patch: the fact that it was made, rather than simply found, does not show on its surface at all.

## Yearbook

Laid down by [the settler](persona_the_settler.md) through [tending](process_tending.md) and [silting](process_silting.md), one round at a time, with no round marked as the one that mattered.
Applies [the test](piece_the_test.md) through [selecting](process_selecting.md) once it stands. Arrives whole in [the bequest](piece_the_bequest.md), passed to [the heir](persona_the_heir.md) alongside traits it was never matched to. Begins forming again wherever [the leaver](persona_the_leaver.md) settles and starts tending, the same crust, under a different sky.
