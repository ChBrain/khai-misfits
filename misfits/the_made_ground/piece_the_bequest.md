# Piece: The Bequest

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The single parcel in which the old traits and the new floor pass to the Heir together, never separately.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the moment of birth, wherever [the heir](persona_the_heir.md) is born, on [the ground](place_the_ground.md) as [the crust](piece_the_crust.md) already stands there.

## Load Bearing

Without it, traits and terrain would arrive by separate roads, and an heir could in principle be handed the one without the other, a body matched to whatever ground it happened to meet.
With it, the two are welded: the traits fit for the old ground and the floor raised past it come down as one indivisible handover, so the mismatch between them is not a misfortune that could have been packaged differently, it is the shape the bequest has always taken. The Bequest is load bearing because it is what makes the inheritance a second, ecological inheritance riding alongside the first, genetic one, rather than two separable gifts.

## Apparent

Nothing wrapped or handed over in a visible ceremony: the bequest is simply the condition the Heir is born into, traits already in the body and floor already under the feet, indistinguishable at first glance from any ordinary birth.
Only in hindsight does it read as a parcel at all, once it is clear the two halves never could have been split.

## Yearbook

Assembled by [bequeathing](process_bequeathing.md), out of traits shaped by generations of [tending](process_tending.md) and a floor raised by [silting](process_silting.md), neither side aware it was building toward this handover.
Delivered whole to [the heir](persona_the_heir.md) in [Born Into the Test](plot_born_into_the_test.md), where it becomes the ground [selecting](process_selecting.md) will measure against for as long as the Heir stays.
