# Process: Relocating

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The Leaver's move from the made ground to unaltered ground, the one correction actually on offer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A test grown heavier than the actor can, or cares to, bear, and unaltered ground known to exist elsewhere.
At [the ground](place_the_ground.md), the Leaver reading [the test](piece_the_test.md) correctly and finding [the far ground](place_the_far_ground.md) a real alternative, so the condition for relocating is simply that leaving is possible and staying is not the only option.

## Direction

Toward ground with no floor on it yet, and nothing further than that.
Relocating is a single vector, away from the made ground and toward the unmade one: it carries no plan for what happens once arrival is complete, because arriving is the whole of what it is built to do.

## Lever

A genuine reprieve pulling against nothing but the effort of the move itself.
There is no real resistance to relocating other than the crossing: the far ground truly is lighter, so the force here is almost entirely on one side, and the process runs easily once it starts.

## Echo

The Leaver standing on ground that has not been worked, for a moment.
What is different is only that the test is behind it: relocating changes where the actor stands, not what standing anywhere and living well requires of it, and that unchanged requirement is what the next act will meet.
