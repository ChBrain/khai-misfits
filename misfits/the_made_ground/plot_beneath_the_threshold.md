# Plot: Beneath the Threshold

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ground](place_the_ground.md), carrying what the tending has been leaving behind, round after round, with no round ever having announced itself as the one that changed anything.

## Action

[Silting](process_silting.md): [The Settler](persona_the_settler.md), still holding [the settler's seat](position_settler.md), keeps performing the same fit act, and the residue those acts leave keeps failing to undo itself, adding instead, round on round, into something that was not there before.

## Stage

[The Settler](persona_the_settler.md), still on [Tend the Ground](plan_tend_the_ground.md), notices nothing different in any single round and has no reason to, since no single round is different.

## Tension

What stands by the end of this plot is real, and nowhere inside its own making is there a step anyone could point to and call the change.
[The Crust](piece_the_crust.md) is now load bearing, a genuine floor built entirely from acts that were each, at the time, indistinguishable from the acts before them that had built nothing at all. There is no threshold to catch here, and so nothing here to catch it at.
