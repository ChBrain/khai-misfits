# Persona: The Settler

## Taxonomy

[Settler](position_settler.md). Works the ground exactly as fitness requires, and is right that the working is what fitness requires.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent. Steady. At home.
Does the ordinary thing the place calls for: turns, clears, digs, lays down. Nothing showy in it, nothing extra, only the plain business of living somewhere well.
No hunger to remake the world, only to keep doing what already works.

## Action

Works the ground the same way, round after round: turns it, clears it, sets down what living needs.
Repeats the act exactly because the act keeps working, and reads each round's success as reason to do it again.
Never once does something different in kind, only the same fit thing, again.

## Shadow

Cannot see that repetition is accumulation.
Each act is identical to the last and so, from inside, nothing is ever changing: but the ground does not forget an act the way a single act forgets itself, and what does not undo does not stay put either, it adds. The Settler is fit to the ground at every single moment and is, without ever choosing to, building the ground that its own fitness will not fit.

## Tell

Says "I am only doing what I always do."
Is telling the truth, and the truth is exactly what is laying the floor.
