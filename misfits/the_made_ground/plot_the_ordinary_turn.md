# Plot: The Ordinary Turn

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ground](place_the_ground.md), offering a living to whoever works it as fitness requires.
Nothing marked, nothing remarkable: the same terms it has always offered, and a Settler about to meet them the same way it always has.

## Action

[Tending](process_tending.md): [The Settler](persona_the_settler.md), holding [the settler's seat](position_settler.md), works the ground exactly as fitness requires, turning it, clearing it, laying down what living needs.

## Stage

[The Settler](persona_the_settler.md), having tabled [Tend the Ground](plan_tend_the_ground.md), does the plain thing the place calls for, and does it again the moment it is called for a second time, reading each success as reason enough to repeat it.

## Tension

Nothing in the act is wrong, and that is exactly the problem the act cannot see.
Underneath the visible tending, [Silting](process_silting.md) is already running: each round leaves something behind that the next round does not clear, and no round is different enough from the last to register, from inside, as anything but living well. There is no moment here where stopping would look like anything but giving up on the very fitness the acts exist to keep.
