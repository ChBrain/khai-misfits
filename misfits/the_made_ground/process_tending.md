# Process: Tending

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The Settler working the ground to live in it, one fit and unremarkable act at a time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A place to live in and the ordinary need to work it well.
At [the ground](place_the_ground.md), the Settler doing exactly what fitness requires, because doing anything less would mean not living there properly.

## Direction

Toward the same act, repeated.
Each round the Settler turns, clears, and lays down what is needed, and because the act keeps working, tending has every reason to run again the next time the need returns, so it repeats without ever changing in kind, only in count.

## Lever

A genuine fit, met by an act genuinely suited to it.
Because the act is correct on its own terms every time it is performed, tending is never resisted from the inside: there is no friction here to argue against doing it again, only the plain continuing rightness of the same move.

## Echo

A ground carrying more than it did.
Nothing about any single round changes, but the round leaves its residue behind and the next round leaves more, so what tending produces, unseen inside its own repetition, is [the crust](piece_the_crust.md): a floor built entirely out of acts that were each, individually, only living.
