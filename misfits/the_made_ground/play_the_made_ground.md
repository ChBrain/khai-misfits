# Play: The Made Ground

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Made Ground.
You live somewhere, and living there well means working it: turning the ground, clearing what stands in the way, laying down what the living needs and leaving behind what it does not. None of this is done to the place, exactly. It is only what living in it correctly looks like from the inside, one unremarkable act at a time. But every one of those acts leaves something: a little ground moved, a little residue set down, and no single act is large enough that anyone would call it a change. Given enough of them, though, the residue adds into a floor that was not there before, ground quite literally made from the ordinary business of living on it. That new floor does not ask permission before it starts setting terms. It applies its own pressure, chosen by no one, and it cannot simply be withdrawn, because withdrawing it would mean undoing the very living that raised it. Whoever is born after finds this made ground waiting, and inherits the traits fit for what the place used to be and the fact of what the place now is, together, in one parcel: a test its parent never meant to set and cannot excuse it from. There is no clean way out. Bear the new ground, or move to ground that has not been altered yet, except moving there means living on it, and living on it is exactly the act that starts the whole thing again.

## Arc

The bend is this: the same competence that fits an actor to its place is the mechanism that unfits the place for it, and there is no version of the act that runs any other way.
It opens on residue with no threshold.
An actor works its ground because working it is how this actor lives, and every act of that working is fit, unremarkable, indistinguishable from the last. Nothing in any single act announces a change, because nothing in any single act is one. Yet the acts do not undo each other, they add, and a sum with no visible steps is still a sum: given enough of it, the ground carries a floor that never had a moment of being made, only a great many moments of being lived on.
That floor is not neutral once it exists. It is what everyone standing on it must now clear, and the clearing was set by nobody: not chosen, the way a rule is chosen, and not able to be lifted, the way a rule can be repealed, because lifting it would mean the actor stopping the very living that is raising it further even now.
And the floor does not stay with the one who raised it. It is handed on whole, to whoever is born next, alongside the traits that were fit for the ground before the floor existed. So a descendant inherits two things in one bequest, a body built for the old test and a world already sitting the new one, and answers for a floor it never laid a single grain of.
Bind nobody here to a fault: the actor could not have declined the acts and remained the actor, the descendant could not have declined the inheritance and been born at all. The one move that looks like an exit, leaving for ground that has not been altered, only relocates the mechanism, because to live on the new ground is to work it, and working is the whole of what built the first floor. There is no place to stand that is not, eventually, ground somebody is making.

## Triggers

**[The Ordinary Turn](plot_the_ordinary_turn.md)**
The Settler works the ground exactly as fitness requires, one unremarkable act after the last, and the act is real and it is fit. Tending is the visible process, and beneath it, unseen, Silting is already running. Closes when the tending has repeated long enough that something is accumulating, though no single act inside it looked like anything but the one before.

**[Beneath the Threshold](plot_beneath_the_threshold.md)**
Cue: the ground carrying what the tending has been leaving, with no act ever having announced itself as the one that changed it. Silting completes into a real, load-bearing layer. Closes when the ground is materially different from what it was, though nowhere in the sequence that made it different is there a step anyone could point to and call the change.

**[The Ground Now Tests](plot_the_ground_now_tests.md)**
Cue: the crust standing as the operating condition, whether or not anyone addressed it directly. Selecting applies the new measure to whoever meets the ground now. Closes when the ground is plainly setting a different test than it once did, and it is plain that nobody set it and nobody can call it off.

**[Born Into the Test](plot_born_into_the_test.md)**
Cue: the made ground is the only ground a newcomer has ever known, ordinary rather than remarkable to the one born into it. Bequeathing passes the old traits and the new floor together, in one parcel, to the Heir. Closes when the Heir stands measured by a test its parent never set out to write and cannot hand back.

**[Nowhere Else to Turn](plot_nowhere_else_to_turn.md)**
Cue: the Far Ground, offering what the made ground no longer does. Relocating carries the Leaver there, away from the test, and Tending starts again the moment living on the new ground begins. Closes when it is plain that the move has not escaped the mechanism, only moved it, and a new floor is already being laid under different feet.

## Stakes

Whether an actor's own correct living ever leaves it, or its heirs, standing on the ground it was fit for, or only ever building the ground that will measure them by a harder rule.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a floor nobody laid on purpose and cannot lay down again, and toward an inheritance nobody chose to hand over and nobody can refuse to receive. The production cannot make the actor stop tending, because tending is the whole of how it lives, and it cannot make the floor un-form once it has, because the floor is only the tending, summed. It can only show that nothing here is an error: the act is fit, the accumulation is honest, the descendant is blameless, and the one door marked exit opens onto ground that will, in time, be made the same way.
