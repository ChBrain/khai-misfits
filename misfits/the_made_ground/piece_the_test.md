# Piece: The Test

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The standard the crust applies to whoever meets the ground, set by accumulation and answerable to no one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the ground](place_the_ground.md), wherever [the crust](piece_the_crust.md) already stands: the test exists only where the floor does, and nowhere the floor has not yet reached.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the crust would be a fact about the ground and nothing more, a floor that simply is, with no bearing on who thrives there.
With it, the crust becomes a filter: whoever meets the ground is sorted by what the ground now demands, and because nobody wrote the demand on purpose, nobody can be petitioned to soften it. The Test is load bearing because it is the point where an accumulated fact turns into a live consequence for a living actor, the hinge the whole misfit swings on.

## Apparent

Nothing separate to look at: no gate, no sign, no measuring stick standing apart from the ground itself. Whatever thrives on the crust and whatever struggles on it is the test, visible only in its results.
It shows itself entirely after the fact, in who is fit and who is not, never in advance, as a posted rule would.

## Yearbook

Set by [the crust](piece_the_crust.md) once [silting](process_silting.md) has finished raising it, and applied through [selecting](process_selecting.md) to whoever stands on the ground from then on.
Meets [the heir](persona_the_heir.md) first, in [Born Into the Test](plot_born_into_the_test.md), where traits built for the old ground are measured against a floor those traits never anticipated. Sends [the leaver](persona_the_leaver.md) toward [the far ground](place_the_far_ground.md), the one move that answers it without contesting it.
