# Plot: Born Into the Test

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ground](place_the_ground.md), the made ground and nothing else, the only ground [The Heir](persona_the_heir.md) has ever known, ordinary rather than remarkable from where it stands.

## Action

[Bequeathing](process_bequeathing.md): [The Settler](persona_the_settler.md) passes on the traits its own long tending was built from, and [The Heir](persona_the_heir.md), holding [the heir's seat](position_heir.md), receives them in the same parcel as the floor those traits never anticipated.

## Stage

[The Heir](persona_the_heir.md), having tabled [Bear the Test](plan_bear_the_test.md), takes up [the bequest](piece_the_bequest.md) whole, and puts the traits it was given to the ground exactly as found.

## Tension

The traits were shaped for ground that no longer exists, and the ground is the only thing the Heir was ever handed to stand on.
[The Test](piece_the_test.md) measures the Heir against a floor its own inheritance never anticipated, and there is no return address on the bequest: nobody wrote the mismatch on purpose, so there is no one for the Heir to take it up with, only a ground to keep meeting.
