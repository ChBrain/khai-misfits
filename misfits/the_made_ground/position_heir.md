# Position: Heir

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The seat born after the floor is laid, holding traits and terrain that were never matched to each other on purpose.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The traits handed down, fit for the ground as it stood before the floor.
The ground as it now stands, floor included, arriving in the same parcel as the traits and never separated from them.
No memory of the ground before, and so no basis of comparison to call the floor an imposition.

## Orders

Meet the ground as found.
Put the inherited traits to work on it, because working with what you were given is the only opening move a newcomer has.
Treat the floor's demand as simply what the place asks, since nothing marks it as anything else.

## Loses

The chance to have been measured by the test its traits were actually built for.
Because the floor was raised before it arrived and cannot be un-raised without undoing the living that raised it, the seat inherits a mismatch it did not create and cannot correct: fit traits meeting an unfit test, in the one bequest it had no say in accepting.
It cannot decline the inheritance and still be born into the place at all.

## Drives

Toward a life spent closing a gap it never opened,
right to work with exactly what it was given,
so the seat becomes proof that a floor need never be earned by the one who is tested against it.
