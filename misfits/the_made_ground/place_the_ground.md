# Place: The Ground

## Taxonomy

[The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md). The site where the tending happens and, unmarked, the floor accumulates beneath it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An ordinary place to live and work: soil, cover, whatever the tending is done in.
Nothing here announces itself as changing. Every visit shows the same kind of surface being turned the same kind of way, so what the ground shows, moment to moment, is continuity, not alteration.

## Holds

The residue of every act performed on it, kept rather than shed.
Nothing here is swept away between rounds: what is turned, cleared, or laid down stays, so the ground holds not just the current tending but the entire, uncounted record of every tending before it.

## Offers

A living, freely, to whoever works it as fitness requires.
The ground asks nothing up front and pays out at once: turn it correctly and it supports you, the same terms today as yesterday, so tending it never once feels like a cost worth weighing.

## Withheld

Any warning that the terms are shifting.
The ground never announces the moment its residue becomes a floor, because there is no such moment inside any single act: it withholds the threshold entirely, and grants only the floor itself, in full, once it is already standing, and by then the terms it withheld are the terms now in force.
