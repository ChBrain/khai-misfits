# The Made Ground: Reference

The research warrant for The Made Ground.
The term is the evolutionary biologists'; the play runs on niche construction theory, from Lewontin's
naming of the organism as both subject and object of evolution through Odling-Smee, Laland and Feldman's
formal treatment, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging niche construction as a **self-altering system**: an actor changes the world it lives in by living
correctly in it, and the changed world sets the next test it will be measured against. The play models the
documented mechanics, the act that is fit and unremarkable in itself, the alteration that accumulates
below the threshold anyone would call a change, the altered ground that now applies a selective pressure
nobody chose and nobody can withdraw, the inheritance that passes the modified world alongside the traits
so a descendant is born into a test its parent wrote, and the impossibility of stepping back, since the
actor cannot un-make the ground without ceasing the activity that made it fit, as the load-bearing vectors
of the structure, not as a tale of greed or short sight. The governing finding of the source literature is
the play's governing law: an organism is not only shaped by its environment but shapes it, so selection is
not a fixed exam set by an indifferent world but a moving one the candidate is helping to write, and fit
achieved today is a term in tomorrow's problem.

It is distinct from the house's **Fit to Fail**, where selection drives a population past its own viability
against a niche that stays put: there the ground is fixed and the population moves; here the population's
own correct behaviour moves the ground. It is distinct from the house's **The Lie of the Land**, where a
cue keeps reporting a quality the ground has lost: there the ground is changed by something outside the
settler, and the failure is a misread signal; here the ground is changed by the settler itself, and the
signal is honest throughout. It is distinct from the house's **Jevons Paradox**, where efficiency raises
the quantity consumed: there the loop runs through price and demand and the resource is merely used more;
here nothing is consumed, and what moves is the criterion of success.

## Origin

The phenomenon in practice, and the research spine beneath it.

| Source                              | Key Work / Event                                                      | Scope                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lewontin**                        | _The Organism as the Subject and Object of Evolution_ (1983)          | **The naming**: the organism is not a passive object of selection but a party to the conditions of its own selection.   |
| **Odling-Smee, Laland and Feldman** | _Niche Construction: The Neglected Process in Evolution_ (2003)       | **The formal treatment**: niche construction as a second inheritance system, ecological alongside genetic.              |
| **Jones, Lawton and Shachak**       | _Organisms as Ecosystem Engineers_ (1994)                             | **The mechanism named at scale**: the physical modification of habitat as a general ecological process.                 |
| **Darwin**                          | _The Formation of Vegetable Mould through the Action of Worms_ (1881) | **The first long observation**: the earthworm remaking the soil it lives in, over a span no single worm could perceive. |

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the actor as
  reckless or heedless, because the source finding is precisely that each act is fit: the burrow is dug
  because digging is how this animal lives, the soil is turned because turning it is feeding, and no act in
  the chain is an error or an excess. The alteration is authored by no fault and emerges from the structure,
  an actor that must act on its world in order to live in it, so the misfit is that living correctly in a
  place is the process by which the place stops being the one you were fit for.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. There is no move that
  keeps the fit and stops the making, because the making is the living. An actor that ceased to alter its
  ground would cease the activity that made it fit in the first place, so the only correction available is
  to bear the altered ground or to move, and moving begins the same process somewhere else.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; niche construction, ecological inheritance and
  ecosystem engineering are theirs. The theory's own contested edge is carried with it: how much of
  evolution niche construction explains is disputed, and the play stages the mechanism, not a claim about
  its share.
