# Plan: Tend the Ground

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the mandate of [the settler's seat](position_settler.md), inside [The Made Ground](play_the_made_ground.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Settler](position_settler.md)

## Direction

Work the ground exactly as fitness requires, and keep doing it. Turn it, clear it, lay down what living needs, and read each round's success as reason enough to run the next one. There is no clause here for checking what the rounds add up to: the plan is only ever to keep living well, one ordinary act at a time.

## Orders

([The Settler](persona_the_settler.md) turns the ground, clears what stands in the way, and lays down what living needs, again, exactly as before.)

## Implementation

([The Ground](place_the_ground.md); the [tending](process_tending.md) process; each act is fit on its own terms and, unseen, feeds [the crust](piece_the_crust.md) that the acts are jointly raising.)

## Targets

- [ ] the ground is worked exactly as fitness requires, round after round
- [ ] each round is read as proof the working still works
- [ ] no round is withheld to check what the working is accumulating
- [ ] the working continues ... and the crust rises with it, unaddressed
