# Process: Substituting

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). Treating the patient in front of you with the thing that is available instead.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An empty space where the vial goes.
At [the shelf](place_the_shelf.md), on the day, with the list already booked.

## Direction

Toward the best available alternative.
This is done well, by people who are good at it: a second-line agent, a different concentration, a
recalculated dose, a rationing rule agreed by a committee overnight.

## Lever

Clinical judgement, which is real and is the only instrument in the room.
It cannot reach the cause, because the cause is a price set in a market this process has no standing in.

## Echo

Patients treated, mostly adequately,
an outcome slightly worse and a cost slightly higher, distributed and unrecorded,
and no defective product anywhere to point at.
