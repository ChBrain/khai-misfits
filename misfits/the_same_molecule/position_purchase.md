# Position: Purchase

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). The seat that awards a year of volume, on behalf of hospitals that will not see the plant.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A quantity to buy and bids to compare.
A duty to its members to buy well, which means cheaply, for goods declared the same.
A document with columns for price, quantity, dates and terms.

## Orders

Aggregate the demand.
Award to the lowest compliant bid.
Defend the saving, which is real and is what the seat exists to produce.

## Loses

The ability to pay for anything the document has no column for.
A buyer can only purchase what the transaction contains, and **reliability of future supply is not a
term of sale**: it is a property of a plant, over a horizon longer than the award, unverifiable at
signature and unenforceable afterwards, so the seat cannot buy it however much it wants to.

## Drives

Toward the lowest defensible price,
which is correct behaviour toward an interchangeable good,
so the seat that captured the saving is the seat that removed the margin the line needed.
