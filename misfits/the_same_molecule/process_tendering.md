# Process: Tendering

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). A year of a system's volume put out to bid and awarded on the number.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A contract term ending, and a duty to buy well.
At [the price sheet](place_the_price_sheet.md), on a cycle that repeats.

## Direction

Toward the lowest compliant price.
This is correct conduct toward an interchangeable good and it is what makes the medicine affordable:
a buyer who paid more without a reason would be failing at the thing it exists to do.

## Lever

The price, which is the only column with variation in it.
A buyer who wanted a second lever would need the goods to differ, and the authoritative finding is that
they do not.

## Echo

A saving that is real and defended,
a margin that is thinner than last cycle,
and a supplier who has just learned what it must cost next year.
