# Process: Certifying

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). A regulator establishing that two versions of a medicine may be given interchangeably.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A patent expiring, and an applicant with evidence.
At the [equivalence seat](position_equivalence.md), years before any of this.

## Direction

Toward a medicine that more than one party may make.
This is done well and is done by people who know what they are doing: the standard is about the drug in
the body, the evidence is required, and the conclusion is correct.

## Lever

The evidence about the product, which is the only thing the standard is about.
There is no lever here for supply at all, because a judgement that two medicines are the same is not a
judgement about the durability of anybody's factory and was never offered as one.

## Echo

Two interchangeable products,
a price that falls because they are interchangeable,
and a comparison with one dimension left in it.
