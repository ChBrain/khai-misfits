# Persona: The Buyer

## Taxonomy

[Purchase](position_purchase.md). The party that puts a year of a hospital system's volume out to bid.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Professional, accountable, and measured on the saving it delivers.
Knows the market, runs a clean process, reads every bid.
Has never been inside any of the plants and has no basis on which to ask to be.

## Action

Aggregates demand.
Compares compliant bids.
Awards to the lowest.

## Shadow

That doing this well is what leaves nothing behind it.
**The saving is real and the margin it removes was the maintenance budget**, and the buyer has no way to
tell those apart, because the transaction contains a price and a quantity and no description of a
factory anywhere.

## Tell

Is the party whose job is to pay less.
Is the party who will be told there is none.
