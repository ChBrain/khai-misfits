# Position: Recourse

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). The seat that tries to make the supply survive the next time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Every remedy anyone has proposed, and some that are running.
A stockpile that can be built, a source that can be qualified, a premium that can be offered.
A budget that is its own and a benefit that is everybody's.

## Orders

Add redundancy.
Pay for reliability where it can be identified.
Do it before the next stoppage, not after.

## Loses

The proportion between what it spends and what it gets.
**Redundancy is a national good bought out of a local budget**: the second source takes years and serves
whoever needs it, the stockpile expires on the holder's books, and the premium buys a property that
cannot be verified at the time of purchase or attributed afterwards.

## Drives

Toward a supply that does not depend on one suite,
which is the right objective and is reachable,
so the seat with the correct answer is the seat that cannot make the arithmetic work alone.
