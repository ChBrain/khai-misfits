## Cue

## Action

## Stage

[The Price Sheet](place_the_price_sheet.md): compliance ticked, terms matched, and a difference of a few cents a vial.

## Tension

The award is correct and the saving is real, and it is worth being precise about why the buyer cannot do
better.
Every non-price term on the sheet is a property the certificate already equalised, so the sheet is not a
poor instrument badly used: it is a complete record of a purchase with exactly one variable.
The buyer has never been inside any of the plants and has no standing to ask, because the plant is not
what is being sold.
So the price falls, cycle after cycle, toward the cost of making the thing.
And **the margin that came out was also the maintenance budget**, indistinguishably, because both were
the same money and only one of them had a name in the document.
