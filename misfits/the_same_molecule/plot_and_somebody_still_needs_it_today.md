# Plot: And Somebody Still Needs It Today

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The settlements, each of which is a real answer.

## Action

[Substituting](process_substituting.md) at the bedside, while [The Second Source](persona_the_second_source.md), in [the recourse seat](position_recourse.md), works through [Qualify a Second Source](plan_qualify_a_second_source.md).

## Stage

[The Shelf](place_the_shelf.md): a resupply date that has moved twice, and a list already booked.

## Tension

Each escape gives back the thing it was for.
Stockpile it, and you have bought months, a holding cost and an expiry date, on your own books, for a
shortage that will be somebody else's too.
Qualify a second source, and you have started something correct that finishes in years, per line and per
product.
Pay a premium for reliability, and you have paid it alone, into a plant your competitors also draw on,
for a property you cannot verify at signature or attribute afterwards, which is why **the party that
waits loses nothing by waiting**.
Write a failure-to-supply penalty, and you have priced the penalty, which both sides can live with, and
not the shortage, which nobody can.
Build a manufacturer that does not need the margin, which is a real answer and exists and is working,
and it is one more supplier inside the same market on the same terms.
And meanwhile [The Prescriber](persona_the_prescriber.md) substitutes, competently, at a cost carried by
patients and recorded nowhere as a cost, because nothing that was supplied was ever defective.
