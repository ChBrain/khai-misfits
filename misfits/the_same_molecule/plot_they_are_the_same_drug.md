# Plot: They Are the Same Drug

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[Identical Is the Achievement](piece_identical_is_the_achievement.md): a patent expires, a second version is approved, and it may be given in place of the first.

## Action

[Certifying](process_certifying.md): [The Certificate](persona_the_certificate.md), in [the equivalence seat](position_equivalence.md), works through [Certify Them Identical](plan_certify_them_identical.md).

## Stage

[The Price Sheet](place_the_price_sheet.md): two names, two numbers, and nothing else that differs.

## Tension

The play has to establish first that this is a success, because everything after it depends on there
being no fault here.
The standard is about the medicine in the body, the evidence is required and supplied, and the
conclusion is right: either version may be given.
That conclusion is why a drug that cost a fortune under patent now costs almost nothing, and why it
reaches everybody who needs it.
And it is the same conclusion that finishes the comparison. **Once two things are the same, there is
nothing to weigh but price**, and a buyer who paid more anyway would not be careful, it would be
irrational, since the authoritative answer to what distinguishes them is nothing.
Nobody has been fooled. The products really are interchangeable, and what differs is not in either of
them.
