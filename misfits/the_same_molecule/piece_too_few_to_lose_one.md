# Piece: Too Few to Lose One

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). Very few makers per product, and capacity approved line by line, so one stoppage is a national absence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the fill line](place_the_fill_line.md), the day a suite goes out of service.
In the market structure of old generic injectables, where entry is rare and exit is not.

## Load Bearing

Without it a manufacturer stopping is a shift of share and the others pick it up.
It is load bearing because **the survivors are not permitted to make more**: sterile capacity is
qualified per line and per product, so the capacity that exists is approved to make something else, and
converting it is a regulatory exercise measured in years rather than a scheduling decision.

## Apparent

That high prices during a shortage will pull entry in.
Entry is what takes years, and the price returns to the floor as soon as supply does, so the return has
to be earned in a window that closes on arrival.

## Yearbook

The market-structure work reporting few producers per generic product, and injectable markets with less
entry and higher exit than the rest.
