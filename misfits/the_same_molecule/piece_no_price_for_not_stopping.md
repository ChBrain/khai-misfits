# Piece: No Price for Not Stopping

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). Reliability of future supply is not a term of sale, so a buyer who wants it has nothing to buy.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the purchase seat](position_purchase.md), at the moment the award is signed.
In every proposal to pay a premium for a more dependable maker.

## Load Bearing

Without it the remedy is obvious and the play has no second half.
It is load bearing because **the horizon of the failure is longer than the horizon of the contract**: an
award covers a year of vials, the line fails in the fourth, and no clause written today can be verified
at signature or enforced afterwards against a manufacturer whose remedy for breach is to leave.

## Apparent

That a failure-to-supply penalty solves it.
It prices the penalty, which is a number both sides can live with, and not the shortage, which is not.

## Yearbook

The empirical finding that shortages rose where reimbursement, and then manufacturer prices, fell
furthest, which is the link from a thin margin to weaker investment in reliability.
