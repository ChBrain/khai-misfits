# Process: Deferring

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). A capital committee putting money where a return can be shown, which is not here.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A budget round, and a working line that does not need replacing this year.
At [the fill line](place_the_fill_line.md), annually, for as long as the product has been thin.

## Direction

Toward capital that earns.
The suite passes inspection and makes conforming product, so replacing it is an investment with no
measurable return: nothing the buyer pays for improves, and nothing the regulator requires is currently
unmet.

## Lever

The visibility of the cost, which decides whether it can be cut.
Anything inspected is fixed and anything invisible is indistinguishable from efficiency, so the process
has exactly one direction available to it and takes it.

## Echo

A line that runs another year,
a fragility that nobody measured and nobody concealed,
and a product still shipping at the bid price.
