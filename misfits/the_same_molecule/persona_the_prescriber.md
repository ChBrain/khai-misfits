# Persona: The Prescriber

## Taxonomy

[Absence](position_absence.md). The clinician with a patient today and the drug not there.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Capable, improvising, and entirely downstream.
Knows the protocol, knows the second line, knows what the second line costs the patient.
Was not consulted about any of it and would not have known what to say.

## Action

Substitutes.
Rations, or delays, and records which.
Explains it to somebody who did not know there was a market.

## Shadow

That there is nobody to hold responsible, because nothing was defective.
**A bad batch has a recall and an owner; an absence has neither**, so the party carrying the whole of the
harm is the only party with no counterparty, no claim and no remedy.

## Tell

Is the party that finds out last.
Is the party that pays.
