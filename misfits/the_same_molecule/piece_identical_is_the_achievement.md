# Piece: Identical Is the Achievement

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). The equivalence is real, is the point, and is what leaves price as the only question.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the equivalence seat](position_equivalence.md), when the second version is approved.
In the price of every old generic injectable in the hospital.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is an argument that generic competition was a mistake, which it was not.
It is load bearing because **the mechanism runs on a success**: interchangeability is why the medicine
costs almost nothing and reaches everybody, and it is the same fact that reduces the purchase to one
dimension. Remove it and the harm goes, along with the affordability that made the drug universal.

## Apparent

That a careful buyer should weigh the manufacturers anyway.
There is nothing to weigh them on. The authoritative finding about what distinguishes the products is
that nothing does, and it is correct.

## Yearbook

The standard of therapeutic equivalence as approval actually applies it.
Naming it as an achievement is what stops the play collapsing into a complaint about cheap medicine.
