## Cue

## Action

## Stage

## Tension

Stopping is the correct response to the finding, which is the first thing to say.
The second is that the absence is national rather than local, because there were only ever two or three
makers of this and **the survivors are not permitted to make more**: sterile capacity is approved line by
line and product by product, so the capacity that exists is qualified to make something else and
converting it is measured in years.
The third is that the maker does not come back. Remediation costs money, and the product it would
restore earns nothing, because the price was set by a market that could not pay for the line in the
first place.
And through all of it, [A Good Vial Every Time, Until None](piece_a_good_vial_every_time_until_none.md):
every unit ever delivered was exactly what was certified, so there is no defective product, no recall
and no claim.
The failure is an absence, and an absence has no owner.
