# Process: Stopping

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). A suite going out of service, and a manufacturer deciding whether to come back.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A finding, an investigation, or a piece of equipment that has reached the end.
At [the fill line](place_the_fill_line.md), without warning to anybody downstream.

## Direction

Toward remediation, or toward exit.
Both are responsible. Stopping production on a quality finding is the correct response to it, and
declining to spend on a product that earns nothing is the correct response to that.

## Lever

The expected return on the remediation, against a price the market has already set.
There is no lever for the hospitals at all, since they are not party to the decision and mostly learn
of it from a shortage notice.

## Echo

A national absence produced by a local decision,
survivors whose capacity is approved to make something else,
and a maker with no reason to return.
