# Place: The Price Sheet

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). The document where a year of supply is decided: a column of names and a column of numbers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The product, the presentation, the quantity, the term, and the price.
Compliance boxes, ticked, which are about the medicine.
Two bidders, and a difference between them of a few cents a vial.

## Holds

The entire comparison, and it is one-dimensional.
**Everything the sheet can express is a thing the certificate already declared identical**, so the only
live column is price, and the sheet is not a poor instrument badly used but a complete record of a
purchase that has exactly one variable.

## Offers

A defensible award and a saving that is real.

## Withheld

Any row for the plant, the redundancy, the age of the equipment or the depth of the maintenance.
Their absence is not an oversight in the document, since none of it is a property of the goods being
bought.
