# Position: Absence

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). The seat that has to treat somebody today, with the drug not there.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A patient, a protocol, and an empty place on the shelf.
A second-line agent, which is worse, or dearer, or both.
No counterparty at all: nobody to complain to, since nothing defective was supplied.

## Orders

Treat anyway.
Substitute, ration, or delay, and record which.
Do not wait, because the resupply date moves.

## Loses

The whole of its recourse, because nothing went wrong that can be pointed at.
**There is no defective unit and therefore no claim.** A bad batch has an owner, a recall and a remedy;
an absence has a press release, and this seat carries the entire cost of a decision taken years earlier
in a market it is not party to.

## Drives

Toward the best available substitute,
which is found, and used, and mostly works,
so the seat that absorbs the harm is the only seat with no instrument of any kind.
