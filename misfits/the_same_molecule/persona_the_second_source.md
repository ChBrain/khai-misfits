# Persona: The Second Source

## Taxonomy

[Recourse](position_recourse.md). The redundancy everyone agrees is needed, measured in years and in somebody's budget.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Correct, available, and slow.
A second line, a stockpile, a contract that pays for reliability, a manufacturer that does not need the margin.
Every one of them is a real answer and several of them are running.

## Action

Qualifies capacity, suite by suite and product by product.
Holds inventory against an expiry date.
Offers to pay more, to somebody, for something.

## Shadow

That the cost is one party's and the benefit is everybody's.
**Redundancy is a national good bought out of a local budget**, so the party willing to fund it captures
a fraction of what it produces, and the party that does not fund it loses nothing by waiting.

## Tell

Is the answer nobody disputes.
Is the answer that arrives after the shortage it was for.
