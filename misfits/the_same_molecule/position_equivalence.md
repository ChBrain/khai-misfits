# Position: Equivalence

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). The seat that declares two products interchangeable, before anything is bought.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A standard for sameness that is about the medicine in the vial.
Evidence that each version meets it, which each does.
No standard at all for the plant, because the plant is not the medicine.

## Orders

Judge the product.
Say plainly when two are the same, so that either may be given.
Hold that judgement steady, because a wobbling one would be worth nothing.

## Loses

Any way to distinguish what it has just declared identical.
This seat is doing its work correctly and **its correct work removes the dimension**: once two versions
are interchangeable in fact, there is nothing left for a buyer to weigh but price, which is the whole
point and is also the mechanism.

## Drives

Toward a medicine anyone can afford,
which is what equivalence is for and what it achieves,
so the seat that made the drug cheap is the seat that made price the only question.
