# Persona: The Certificate

## Taxonomy

[Equivalence](position_equivalence.md). The document that says these two are the same, and is right.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Rigorous, evidenced, and narrow in exactly the way a good standard is narrow.
Speaks about the medicine: the molecule, the dose, the sterility, the behaviour in a body.
Says nothing about the building, because the building was never what it was asked about.

## Action

States that either version may be given.
Holds that statement steady over decades.
Makes the cheaper one usable.

## Shadow

That being right is what closes the question.
**Once two things are the same, comparing them is finished**, and a buyer who wanted to weigh anything
else is not being cautious but being irrational, since the authoritative answer to what distinguishes
them is nothing.

## Tell

Is the reason the drug is affordable.
Is the reason there is nothing to consider but price.
