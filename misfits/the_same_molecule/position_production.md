# Position: Production

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). The seat that has to make the vials for the price it bid.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A bid it must meet, on a product with almost no margin left in it.
A sterile suite that works, installed a long time ago, inspected and passing.
A capital budget that has to be spent somewhere, on some product, for some return.

## Orders

Make the medicine to specification, every batch.
Hold the cost to the bid.
Put the capital where it earns, which is not here.

## Loses

Any way to be paid for the one thing it could still spend money on.
Every cost this seat carries is either **inspected**, in which case it cannot be cut, or **invisible**,
in which case cutting it is indistinguishable from efficiency: the seat is not choosing to run a fragile
line, it is declining to fund the replacement of a working one out of a margin that no longer exists.

## Drives

Toward a product that meets the specification at the bid price,
which it does, batch after batch, for years,
so the seat that delivered exactly what was bought is the seat where the fragility accumulates.
