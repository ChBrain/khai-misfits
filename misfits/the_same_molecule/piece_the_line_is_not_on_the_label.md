# Piece: The Line Is Not on the Label

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). What the manufacturer economises on is absent from the transaction rather than hidden inside it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the price sheet](place_the_price_sheet.md), in the columns that exist.
At [the fill line](place_the_fill_line.md), in the capital that was not spent.

## Load Bearing

Without it this is a story about a buyer who did not look hard enough.
It is load bearing because **there is nothing to look at**: the age of the suite, the depth of the
redundancy and the maintenance budget are not properties of the vials being bought, so they are not
obscured within the sale, they are outside it, and no amount of diligence brings them in.

## Apparent

That inspection records close the gap.
They are about compliance on a date, arrive late, are not comparable across makers, and describe what a
line was rather than how long it can go on.

## Yearbook

The economic account of shortages that puts the cause in the market's inability to observe and reward
quality, rather than in a run of unrelated manufacturing accidents.
