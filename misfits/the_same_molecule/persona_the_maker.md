# Persona: The Maker

## Taxonomy

[Production](position_production.md). The manufacturer that bid the price and has to hold it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, compliant, and thin on this product.
Passes inspection, meets specification, ships on time, for years.
Has a capital committee that puts money where a return can be shown.

## Action

Makes the medicine to the standard.
Keeps the cost inside the bid.
Spends the capital on something with a margin.

## Shadow

That the only remaining place to economise is the only place that matters.
**Nobody decided to run a fragile line.** A working suite was not replaced, one year and then the next,
out of a margin competed away by the same process that made the product reach everybody, and each of
those decisions was correct on its own.

## Tell

Is the party that delivered exactly what was purchased.
Is the party whose exit is the shortage.
