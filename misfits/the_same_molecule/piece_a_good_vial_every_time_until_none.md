# Piece: A Good Vial Every Time, Until None

## Taxonomy

[The Same Molecule](play_the_same_molecule.md). The failure arrives as an absence, so there is no defective unit for a market to learn from.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the shelf](place_the_shelf.md), on the first day the space is empty.
Wherever a market is expected to select against poor quality and has nothing to select on.

## Load Bearing

Without it this is adverse selection and the house already stages that.
It is load bearing because **there is no bad instance**: every vial delivered was exactly what was
certified, so there is no claim, no recall, no batch to refuse and no reputation lost in the ordinary
way. What was economised is the probability of there being a vial next year, which is not a property of
any good that anyone bought.

## Apparent

That reputation will eventually price it.
A manufacturer that exits is no longer selling anything for a reputation to attach to, and the buyer
who wanted to punish it has already lost the only thing it wanted.

## Yearbook

The distinction between a market that unravels into bad goods and one that delivers good goods cheaply
until it delivers none.
