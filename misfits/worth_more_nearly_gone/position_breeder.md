# Position: Breeder

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). The seat that attacks the premium by supplying it, and cannot control what its supply legitimises.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The only lever that reaches the price rather than the poacher.
Legal stock it can produce and put into the trade until scarcity stops meaning anything.
No control over the channel once it exists, since a legal channel is also a route through which illegal stock can be laundered.

## Orders

Flood the market: supply until the premium collapses, attacking the engine instead of the symptom.
Attend to what the supply legitimises, since a larger and more normalised trade can recruit buyers faster than it satisfies them.
Accept what the seat does: it tries the one intervention aimed at the mechanism, with two documented ways of failing.

## Loses

The ability to tell its own success from its own harm.
Because unit prices can fall while total trade volume rises, and because its own stock becomes indistinguishable from laundered stock inside the channel it opened, the seat loses the audit it would need to know which way the intervention is running.
It keeps a genuine downward pressure on price and loses any confidence about the net.

## Drives

Toward the one whose correct target is reachable only by an act that may enlarge the appetite,
flooding because the premium is the engine and nothing else touches it and, by doing so, legitimising and growing the market it means to satiate,
so the seat holding the play's second remedy is the seat that cannot say whether it has broken the spiral or fed it.
