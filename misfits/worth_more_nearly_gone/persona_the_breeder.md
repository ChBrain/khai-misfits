# Persona: The Breeder

## Taxonomy

[Breeder](position_breeder.md). The one who attacks the premium directly by supplying the market, and finds the intervention capable of feeding what it meant to end.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Pragmatic and unsentimental, going after the price rather than the poacher, which is the one lever nobody else is pulling.
Breeds or farms legal stock and puts it into the trade until scarcity stops meaning anything.
No naivety on show: it knows enforcement alone has not worked and is trying the mechanism instead of the symptom.

## Action

Runs [Flood the Market](plan_flood_the_market.md): through [Flooding](process_flooding.md) at [the Auction](place_the_auction.md) it supplies legal stock against [the Premium](piece_the_premium.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) and collapsing the price where the flooding takes.
Cannot control what the larger market then does, since a legal channel can carry illegal stock laundered through it, and a bigger, more normalised trade can recruit buyers faster than it satisfies them.
Does the only thing that reaches the premium at all, with two documented ways of backfiring.

## Shadow

Not a fantasist: attacking the price is the correct target, since the price is the engine, and where the flooding has worked the premium has genuinely fallen.
Because its supply legitimises and enlarges the very market it is trying to satiate, its remedy can raise total demand faster than it lowers unit value, so the intervention aimed at the engine can end up fuelling it.
It is the play's second remedy and it turns in the hand exactly as the first one does.

## Tell

Reports falling unit prices alongside rising total trade volume and cannot say, from inside, which of those will decide the species.
Cannot distinguish its own stock from laundered stock once both are in the channel, since that is what a channel does, so the audit it would need is the one its own success makes harder.
