# Piece: The Spiral

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). The feedback whole: each loss raising the value of the remainder and financing the next loss, with no biology in it at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Running between [The Range](place_the_range.md) and [The Auction](place_the_auction.md), a price and a headcount coupled in both directions with no lag and no damping (Courchamp, Angulo, Rivalan and colleagues).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there are two separate problems, each survivable: a market that likes rare things, and a population under pressure. It is the closure of the loop that makes them one problem and makes it monotonic.
It bears the load as the thing that distinguishes this misfit from its nearest neighbour. The classical Allee effect spirals too, through mate-finding and cooperative defence failing at low density, and this one contains none of that: strip the humans out and the population is demographically fine, so the positive density dependence is supplied entirely from outside.

## Apparent

A market heating up, which is what it looks like from the saleroom and what it is, with the connection to a falling headcount visible on no document either side handles.

## Yearbook

Stands over the close of [Rarer, and Dearer](plot_rarer_and_dearer.md), the loop shown closed.
It works together with [The Chase](piece_the_chase.md), the shape and the mechanism that traces it.
