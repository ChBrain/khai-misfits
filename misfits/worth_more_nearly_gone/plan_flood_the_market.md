# Plan: Flood the Market

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the breeder's seat](position_breeder.md), inside [Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Breeder](position_breeder.md)

## Direction

Go after the price, not the poacher. The premium is the engine and nothing else in the play touches it, so breed or farm legal stock and put it into the trade until scarcity stops meaning anything and the price collapses. Watch what the supply legitimises: a legal channel is also a route through which illegal stock can be laundered, and a larger, more normalised market can recruit buyers faster than it satisfies them. Report unit price and total volume together, since the first can fall while the second rises and only both decide the species.
