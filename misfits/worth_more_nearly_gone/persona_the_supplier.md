# Persona: The Supplier

## Taxonomy

[Supplier](position_supplier.md). The one who answers the price, whose effort rises as the stock falls because the reward rises faster.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Practical and local, working ground it knows, often from a place with few other ways to earn a living.
Responds to a price that is offered, which is what anyone selling anything does.
No relish on show: this is work, and the animal is a commodity because someone a long way away has made it one.

## Action

Runs [Take What Pays](plan_take_what_pays.md): through [Taking](process_taking.md) at [the Range](place_the_range.md), producing [the Dwindling](piece_the_dwindling.md), and through [Spiralling](process_spiralling.md) intensifies [the Chase](piece_the_chase.md) as the reward per animal climbs.
Cannot be called off by scarcity, since scarcity is what raises the payment, so the ordinary signal that ends an exploitation is here the signal that funds it.
Does what any supplier does with a rising price: it supplies more effort, not less.

## Shadow

Not malicious: it did not create the demand, does not set the price, and gains nothing from the species ending, since the end of the species is the end of the earnings.
Because the price rises as the population falls, its rational response to a market signal is exactly what carries the population down, and it works hardest at the moment the stock can least afford it.
It is the hand the trap operates through and the party with the least room to choose otherwise.

## Tell

Mounts longer and costlier expeditions for fewer animals and reads that as the trade getting harder, which it is, and as still worth doing, which it also is.
Cannot switch away as the resource thins, since thinning is what makes the remaining animals valuable enough to justify the effort, so the usual exit does not open.
