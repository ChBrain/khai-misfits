# Plot: Rarer, and Dearer

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Between [The Range](place_the_range.md) and [The Auction](place_the_auction.md), where the price and the headcount have begun answering each other.

## Action

Through [Spiralling](process_spiralling.md), [the Chase](piece_the_chase.md) intensifies as effort rises with the reward, longer and costlier expeditions justified each season by a price that has climbed faster than the finding has grown hard. And [the Spiral](piece_the_spiral.md) stands revealed: each loss raising the value of the remainder and financing the next, a positive feedback with no biology in it at all. The scene closes with the economics calling the hunters in hardest exactly where the population can least bear it (Courchamp, Angulo, Rivalan and colleagues).

## Stage

Strip the humans out and the population is demographically fine: no mate-finding is failing and no cooperative defence is collapsing, so the positive density dependence here is supplied wholly from outside, by a market.

## Tension

There is no lag and no damping in the loop, so unlike a cycle that overshoots and returns, this one only tightens, each round of it permanently smaller and permanently dearer than the last.
