# Piece: The Premium

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). Rarity as the source of value rather than an obstacle to it, so willingness to pay is keyed to the headcount and rises as it falls.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [The Auction](place_the_auction.md), where a headcount becomes a price, and attacked there too if it is attacked at all (Courchamp, Angulo, Rivalan and colleagues).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the species is safe by the ordinary route: with value independent of scarcity, a thinned stock becomes expensive to work, the effort moves elsewhere, and the population recovers in the gap, which is what protects most exploited populations and why most do not vanish.
It bears the load as the single inversion the whole trap rests on. Every other element, the taking, the spiral, the failed remedies, follows from value being a function of scarcity, so this piece is not one mechanism among several but the thing that turns a brake into an accelerator.

## Apparent

Ordinary market behaviour, and it is: scarcity has commanded a premium in every market there has ever been, and nothing about this pricing is unusual or improper in itself.

It works together with [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the engine and the one intervention aimed at it.
