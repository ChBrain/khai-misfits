# Piece: The Dwindling

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). The falling population: each animal removed also raising the value of every animal that remains.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Occurring at [The Range](place_the_range.md), where the price arrives as effort and the stock is actually reduced, season by season.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the premium is harmless: a high price on an abundant thing simply transfers money, and it is only because the paying draws the population down that the valuation becomes a mechanism rather than a curiosity.
It bears the load as the coupling between the market and the animal. Because each removal is simultaneously a supply event and a scarcity event, this piece is where the loop physically closes, and it is the reason the price cannot settle.

## Apparent

A harvest, and an unremarkable one: a stock being worked, which is what stocks are for, and which in most cases would self-limit long before anything ran out.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Take While It Pays](plot_take_while_it_pays.md), the taking that raises the value of what is left.
It works together with [The Premium](piece_the_premium.md), each feeding the other with no lag between them.
