# Plan: Own the Rarest

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the collector's seat](position_collector.md), inside [Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Collector](position_collector.md)

## Direction

Bid by how few remain. Rarity is not incidental to what you are buying but constitutive of it, so a common version of the same animal would be a different object and worth a fraction as much. Pay properly, keep the specimen well, and know it better than most of the people campaigning about it, all of which this seat does and none of which is in tension with the rest. Do not pretend the valuation is about anything other than scarcity, because it is not.

## Orders

([The Collector](persona_the_collector.md) bids through [Pricing](process_pricing.md) at [the Auction](place_the_auction.md), setting [the Premium](piece_the_premium.md).)

## Implementation

([The Auction](place_the_auction.md) is where a headcount becomes a price; [Pricing](process_pricing.md) converts scarcity into willingness to pay, so the bid rises as the population falls, and nothing in the saleroom connects the sum to the expedition it will fund at [the Range](place_the_range.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the bid is set by how few remain, which is what the object is
- [ ] the specimen is paid for properly and kept well
- [ ] no claim is made that the valuation rests on anything but scarcity
- [ ] the connection to the range is not visible from here, and is not sought
