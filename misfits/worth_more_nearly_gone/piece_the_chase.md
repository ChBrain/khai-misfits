# Piece: The Chase

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). Effort rising with the price, so the next expedition is worth mounting though there is less to find.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Mounted from [The Range](place_the_range.md), longer and costlier each season, and justified each time by a reward that has climbed faster than the finding has got harder.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the spiral stalls: if effort fell as the stock thinned, in the ordinary way, the population would find shelter in its own unprofitability no matter how high the headline price went, since nobody would be out there.
It bears the load as the transmission between price and population. Because it converts a valuation into boots on the ground, and converts it hardest at the lowest population levels, it is the reason the trap does not simply run out of steam near the end.

## Apparent

Diligence, or desperation: harder work for less return reads as a trade in decline, which it is, and as one still worth pursuing, which it also is.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Rarer, and Dearer](plot_rarer_and_dearer.md), the effort that intensifies as the stock falls.
It works together with [The Spiral](piece_the_spiral.md), the mechanism and the shape it traces.
