# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). The condition and the hedge: the trap needs demand keyed to rarity as such and a reachable supply, and lifts where the premium is broken or the demand dissolved, neither reliable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held at [The Auction](place_the_auction.md), where the premium can be attacked by flooding the trade, and where the attack opens a channel that cannot afterwards be audited.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overclaims in a way that could do real damage: without the boundary it reads as an argument that listing species is counterproductive, that protection should be withheld, or that captive breeding is the wrong policy, none of which the source literature supports, since it raises these as hazards to be managed inside conservation practice rather than as arguments against it.
It bears the load as the honest limit and the price list. The remedy that reaches the engine is real, has worked where applied, and can also launder illegal stock and normalise an appetite that grows faster than it is fed, so unit price and total volume must be read together and neither alone decides the species.

## Apparent

A footnote, even: the qualifications at the end of a finding whose headline is far easier to repeat than its conditions.
