# Position: Supplier

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). The seat that answers a rising price with rising effort, at the moment the stock can least bear it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Ground it knows and skills that suit it, often with few other ways to earn.
A price it did not set, offered by buyers it will never meet.
No exit as the resource thins, since thinning is exactly what makes the remainder worth the longer expedition.

## Orders

Take what pays: answer the price, which is what anyone selling anything does.
Mount the longer and costlier trip as the reward per animal climbs, since the arithmetic of doing so keeps improving.
Accept what the seat does: it supplies more effort as the stock falls, the ordinary signal to stop having been inverted before it ever reached here.

## Loses

Its own trade, at the end, and the earnings that came with it.
Because the seat gains nothing from the species ending and cannot stop while the price climbs, it loses the livelihood it is working, and loses it by doing the only thing the market gives it to do.
It keeps what each expedition earns and loses the range that supplied them.

## Drives

Toward the one with the least room to choose and the most direct hand in the loss,
taking because the price says take and rising effort because the price keeps rising and, by doing so, thinning the stock that raises the price,
so the seat that did not create the demand and does not set the price is the seat the whole trap operates through, rational in each trip and ruinous across them.
