# Place: The Range

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). Where the price arrives as effort and the population is actually reduced. Hosts [Take While It Pays](plot_take_while_it_pays.md) and [Rarer, and Dearer](plot_rarer_and_dearer.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Ground somebody knows well, worked by people with few other ways to earn, and fewer animals in it each season.

## Holds

[The Dwindling](piece_the_dwindling.md), the falling stock, and [The Chase](piece_the_chase.md), the effort that rises as the finding gets harder.

## Offers

To the supplier, a living, for as long as there is anything left to take.

## Withheld

The ordinary exit, since thinning stock is what makes the remaining animals valuable enough to justify the longer trip, so the signal that ends other exploitations never arrives.
