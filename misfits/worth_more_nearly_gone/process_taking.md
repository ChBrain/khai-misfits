# Process: Taking

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). Borne at [The Range](place_the_range.md), precipitated by [The Supplier](persona_the_supplier.md) answering a price it did not set, in [Take While It Pays](plot_take_while_it_pays.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A price arriving from buyers the supplier will never meet, on ground it knows, with few other ways to earn.

## Direction

From a standing population toward a reduced one, each animal removed also raising the value of every animal that remains.

## Lever

That the supplier gains nothing from the species ending and cannot stop while the price climbs, so the response is ordinary and the outcome is not.

## Echo

[The Dwindling](piece_the_dwindling.md), the stock falling and the remainder growing dearer by exactly that fall.
