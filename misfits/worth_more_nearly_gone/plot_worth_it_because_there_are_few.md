# Plot: Worth It Because There Are Few

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Auction](place_the_auction.md), where a specimen is catalogued and a headcount is about to become a price.

## Action

Through [Pricing](process_pricing.md), [the Collector](persona_the_collector.md) on [Own the Rarest](plan_own_the_rarest.md) sets [the Premium](piece_the_premium.md): it wants this specimen precisely because few exist, so its willingness to pay is keyed to the population and rises as the population falls. The scene closes with the ordinary brake inverted, since a thinned stock is normally protected by becoming unprofitable to work, and here thinning raises the reward instead of the cost (Courchamp, Angulo, Rivalan and colleagues).

## Stage

Nothing improper happens here. Scarcity has commanded a premium in every market there has ever been, and valuing a thing because it is rare is close to the definition of collecting rather than a corruption of it.

## Tension

The single inversion in this scene is the whole trap, since everything that follows, the taking, the spiral, and both failed remedies, is a consequence of value being a function of scarcity rather than a cost imposed by it.
