# Plan: Take What Pays

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the supplier's seat](position_supplier.md), inside [Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Supplier](position_supplier.md)

## Direction

Answer the price. It was set by buyers you will never meet and it is the only signal reaching this ground, so respond to it as anyone selling anything responds to a rising bid. Mount the longer and costlier expedition as the reward per animal climbs, since the arithmetic keeps improving even as the finding gets harder. Do not wait for scarcity to call you off, because scarcity is what raises the payment, and the signal that ordinarily ends an exploitation has been inverted before it ever arrived here.

## Orders

([The Supplier](persona_the_supplier.md) takes through [Taking](process_taking.md) at [the Range](place_the_range.md), producing [the Dwindling](piece_the_dwindling.md), and intensifies [the Chase](piece_the_chase.md) through [Spiralling](process_spiralling.md).)

## Implementation

([The Range](place_the_range.md) is where the price lands as effort; [Taking](process_taking.md) reduces the stock, and each reduction raises the value of what remains, so the same act that thins the population finances the next trip against it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the price is answered with effort, as any supplier answers a rising bid
- [ ] longer and costlier expeditions are mounted as the reward per animal climbs
- [ ] no exit is taken on thinning stock, since thinning is what makes the remainder pay
- [ ] nothing is done to end the species, which would end the earnings
