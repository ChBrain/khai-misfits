# Process: Pricing

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). Borne at [The Auction](place_the_auction.md), precipitated by [The Collector](persona_the_collector.md) bidding by how few remain, in [Worth It Because There Are Few](plot_worth_it_because_there_are_few.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A demand for which rarity is not an obstacle to the value but the source of it, so the object being bought is partly the scarcity itself.

## Direction

From a headcount toward a willingness to pay, moving inversely, so that the fewer remain the higher the bid.

## Lever

That this inverts the brake protecting most exploited populations, where a thinned stock becomes unprofitable to work and effort moves elsewhere, since here thinning raises the reward instead of the cost (Courchamp, Angulo, Rivalan and colleagues).

## Echo

[The Premium](piece_the_premium.md), a price keyed to a headcount and rising as it falls.
