# Persona: The Collector

## Taxonomy

[Collector](position_collector.md). The one whose willingness to pay is keyed to how few remain, and who wants the thing sincerely.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Knowledgeable and devoted, often more expert on the species than most people who campaign for it.
Pays properly, keeps its specimen well, and can tell you exactly what makes this one worth having.
No cruelty on show: it buys rather than takes, values rather than despises, and would be genuinely sorry to hear the species had gone.

## Action

Runs [Own the Rarest](plan_own_the_rarest.md): through [Pricing](process_pricing.md) at [the Auction](place_the_auction.md) it sets [the Premium](piece_the_premium.md), bidding by how few exist, so its offer rises as the headcount falls.
Cannot want the thing in a way detached from its rarity, since rarity is what the wanting is about, and a common version of the same animal would not be the object it is buying.
Does what a collector does: it prizes scarcity, which is close to the definition of the practice rather than a corruption of it.

## Shadow

Not a destroyer: it lifts nothing from the wild, breaks no law it knows of, and would prefer the species to persist, since a species gone is a collection that can never be added to.
Because its valuation is a function of the headcount, its sincere appreciation is the signal that funds the taking, and the more it loves the rare thing the more it pays to make the thing rarer.
It is the most appreciative actor in the play and the origin of the price that empties the range.

## Tell

Bids higher on the news that the population has fallen, and experiences that as the specimen having become more significant, which within its own frame it has.
Cannot see the range from the saleroom, so the connection between the sum it pays and the expedition that sum funds is not visible anywhere in the transaction it makes.
