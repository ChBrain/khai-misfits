# Plot: Flood the Market

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Auction](place_the_auction.md), where someone has decided to attack the price rather than the poacher.

## Action

[The Breeder](persona_the_breeder.md) on [Flood the Market](plan_flood_the_market.md) through [Flooding](process_flooding.md) holds [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): supply the trade with legal stock until scarcity stops carrying value and the premium collapses. It can work, and it has worked. It can also give illegal stock a channel to be laundered through, and normalise a larger market that recruits buyers faster than it satisfies them, so unit price can fall while total volume climbs. The scene closes with the one intervention that reaches the engine shown to be real, unreliable, and capable of feeding what it meant to end.

## Stage

The target is correct: the premium is the engine, enforcement aimed at the taking has not reached it, and attacking the price is the only move in the play that touches the mechanism rather than its symptom.

## Tension

The seat cannot audit its own channel, since legal and laundered stock become indistinguishable inside it, so the better the intervention works at scale the harder it becomes to tell whether it is working at all.
