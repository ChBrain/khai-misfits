# Piece: The Notice

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). The protective listing: the document that mobilises enforcement and certifies scarcity to the market in the same sentence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Issued at [The Register](place_the_register.md), in public because it must be public to do anything, and read there by defenders and buyers alike (Hall, Milner-Gulland and Courchamp).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play loses its sharpest turn: a trap whose remedy simply failed would be ordinary, and what makes this one bite is that the most obviously correct protective act carries, necessarily and inseparably, an amplification of the thing it protects against.
It bears the load as the proof that the remedy is inside the trap. Because the listing is right, is the precondition of all enforcement and funding, and cannot be issued privately, there is no version of the act that carries the protection without the announcement, and withholding it would be plainly worse.

## Apparent

Protection, unambiguously: a species named, listed, and defended, which is exactly what everyone agrees ought to happen and what its absence would be criticised for.
