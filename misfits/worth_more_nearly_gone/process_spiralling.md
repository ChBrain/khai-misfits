# Process: Spiralling

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). Borne between [The Range](place_the_range.md) and [The Auction](place_the_auction.md), precipitated by each taking raising the price that funds the next, in [Rarer, and Dearer](plot_rarer_and_dearer.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A price and a headcount now coupled in both directions, each responding to the other with no lag and no damping.

## Direction

From a declining population toward a faster-declining one, effort rising with the reward exactly as the stock becomes least able to bear it.

## Lever

That the loop contains no biology at all, no mate-finding failing and no cooperative defence collapsing, the positive density dependence being supplied wholly by a market (Courchamp, Angulo, Rivalan and colleagues).

## Echo

[The Spiral](piece_the_spiral.md), each loss financing the next, and [The Chase](piece_the_chase.md) intensifying as it runs.
