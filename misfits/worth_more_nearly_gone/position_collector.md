# Position: Collector

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). The seat whose bid is a function of the headcount, rising as the headcount falls.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A sincere valuation of the thing, and expertise in it often exceeding that of its defenders.
A preference in which rarity is not incidental but constitutive, so a common version of the same animal is a different object.
No sight of the range from the saleroom, and no line on any invoice connecting a sum paid to an expedition funded.

## Orders

Own the rarest: bid by how few remain, since that is what makes the specimen the thing it is.
Pay properly and keep it well, which the seat does, and which is not in tension with anything else it is asked to do.
Accept what the seat does: it values scarcity, and valuing scarcity is the practice rather than a corruption of it.

## Loses

Nothing it can perceive, and eventually the collection's own future.
Because the seat gains an object and loses only the possibility of ever adding another once the species ends, the cost arrives too late and too diffusely to register against any single purchase, and no bid it makes is the one that ends anything.
It keeps every specimen it buys and loses the species that could have supplied the next.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sincere appreciation is the price signal that empties the range,
bidding by rarity because rarity is what it is bidding on and, by doing so, paying more for the thing precisely as there is less of it,
so the seat that loves the species best is the seat that funds its taking, devoted in each purchase and fatal in their sum.
