# Pitch: Only a Few Left

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Appreciative and lawful.
A prized specimen, a collector who genuinely loves the thing, a market pricing scarcity as markets always have, and a species formally protected by name.
Nothing false in it: the specimen is real, the affection is real, and protecting an endangered species is exactly what ought to happen.

## Undertow

Under the appreciation, a price keyed to a headcount.
Rarity is not an obstacle to the value but its source, so every animal taken raises the worth of those remaining and pays for the next expedition, and effort rises as the stock falls (Courchamp, Angulo, Rivalan and colleagues).
No monster and no conspiracy: wanting the rare thing is close to the definition of collecting, answering a price is what a supplier does, and nobody wants the species gone, a species gone being a market ended.

## Nerve

The urgency of a warning, and the catch that the warning is also a listing in a catalogue:
that the scarcity which ought to call the hunters off is the scarcity that prices them in, so the loss and the incentive are the one rarity seen twice,
and only a few left is not a plea but a valuation, spoken in a room where some of the listeners are buyers.

## Echo

After the run: record sums paid for the last populations of a listed species, and a decline financing its own continuation with no one in it behaving strangely (Courchamp, Angulo, Rivalan and colleagues).
And the remedies, both turning in the hand: listing the species is right, necessary, and an announcement to every buyer that it is officially scarce; flooding the market with bred stock can collapse the premium and can also launder illegal supply and normalise an appetite that grows faster than it is fed (Hall, Milner-Gulland and Courchamp).
And the bound held in view: it needs demand that prizes rarity as such and a supply still reachable, so the whole of it is that a thing worth more the less of it there is has been priced into its own disappearance.
