# Plan: List It and Guard It

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the warden's seat](position_warden.md), inside [Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Warden](position_warden.md)

## Direction

Name the species endangered, publish its status, and put protection and enforcement behind the name. Do not withhold the listing in the hope of keeping its scarcity quiet: an unlisted endangered species loses every protective measure a listing is the precondition of, and the funding and enforcement that follow only from a public declaration. Accept that the declaration will be read by buyers as well as by defenders, and that no version of this act carries the protection without the announcement.

## Orders

([The Warden](persona_the_warden.md) lists through [Listing](process_listing.md) at [the Register](place_the_register.md), issuing [the Notice](piece_the_notice.md).)

## Implementation

([The Register](place_the_register.md) is where a status is declared in public; [Listing](process_listing.md) mobilises protection and, in the same publication, certifies scarcity to the market, so the premium at [the Auction](place_the_auction.md) can rise in the season the protection lands.)

## Targets

- [ ] the species is named, listed, and its status published
- [ ] the listing is not withheld to keep the scarcity unadvertised
- [ ] protection and enforcement are attached to the name
- [ ] the second effect is acknowledged rather than denied, there being no act without it
