# Process: Listing

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). Borne at [The Register](place_the_register.md), precipitated by [The Warden](persona_the_warden.md) declaring a status that must be public to do anything, in [The Warning That Advertises](plot_the_warning_that_advertises.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A species plainly in decline, and an authority for whom listing it is the precondition of every protective measure that follows.

## Direction

From an undeclared decline toward a published status, mobilising enforcement and funding, and in the same act certifying scarcity to the market.

## Lever

That the warning and the advertisement are one sentence read by two audiences, and the seat cannot choose its readers, so the protective act carries the second effect necessarily (Hall, Milner-Gulland and Courchamp).

## Echo

[The Notice](piece_the_notice.md), correct and necessary, and capable of lifting the premium in the season it lands.
