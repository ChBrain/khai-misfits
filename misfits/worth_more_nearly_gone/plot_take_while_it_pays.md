# Plot: Take While It Pays

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Range](place_the_range.md), where a price set by people who will never come here arrives as a reason to go out.

## Action

Through [Taking](process_taking.md), [the Supplier](persona_the_supplier.md) on [Take What Pays](plan_take_what_pays.md) answers the signal any supplier would answer, and [the Dwindling](piece_the_dwindling.md) follows: the stock reduced season by season, and the value of every animal remaining lifted by exactly that reduction. The scene closes with a living earned and a population lowered by the earning of it.

## Stage

The supplier did not create the demand, does not set the price, and gains nothing whatever from the species ending, since a species ended is a trade ended and a livelihood with it.

## Tension

The ordinary exit is closed here: thinning stock is what makes the remaining animals valuable enough to justify the longer trip, so the signal that calls other exploitations off is the signal calling this one on.
