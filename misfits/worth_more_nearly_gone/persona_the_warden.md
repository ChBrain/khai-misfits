# Persona: The Warden

## Taxonomy

[Warden](position_warden.md). The one who lists the species and guards it, and whose listing publishes the scarcity that prices it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Dutiful and public-spirited, doing the thing everyone agrees ought to be done for a species in decline.
Names it, lists it, publishes its status, and puts protection and enforcement behind the name.
No error on show: an endangered species that went unlisted would be the plain failure, and nobody is arguing for that.

## Action

Runs [List It and Guard It](plan_list_it_and_guard_it.md): through [Listing](process_listing.md) at [the Register](place_the_register.md) it names the species endangered and issues [the Notice](piece_the_notice.md), which mobilises protection and, in the same publication, announces official scarcity to every buyer.
Cannot list quietly, since a listing that was not published would protect nothing, enforcement and funding both depending on the declaration being made in public.
Does the right thing, whose second effect is to raise the price on the thing it is protecting.

## Shadow

Not misguided: the listing is correct, necessary, and the precondition of every protective measure that follows, so withholding it would be the worse act by a wide margin.
Because the document that mobilises protection is the same document that certifies rarity to the market, its most protective act can lift the premium on the species, and it has no version of the act that carries only the first effect.
It is the play's most clearly well-intentioned actor and one of the two through which the remedy turns.

## Tell

Publishes a status change and sees, sometimes within the season, the reported price of specimens rise rather than fall.
Cannot separate the warning from the advertisement, since they are the same sentence read by two audiences, and the seat cannot choose its readers.
