# Position: Warden

## Taxonomy

[Worth More Nearly Gone](play_worth_more_nearly_gone.md). The seat whose protective declaration is also a published certificate of scarcity.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The authority to list a species, and the enforcement and funding that only a public listing unlocks.
An audience it cannot choose, since a published status is read by defenders and buyers alike.
No version of the act that carries the protection without the announcement.

## Orders

List it and guard it: name the species endangered, publish the status, and put protection behind the name.
Do not withhold the listing to keep the scarcity quiet, since an unlisted endangered species loses every protective measure that a listing is the precondition of.
Accept what the seat does: it issues the right document, whose second reading is a valuation.

## Loses

The separation between a warning and an advertisement, which it never had.
Because the sentence that mobilises protection is the same sentence that certifies rarity to the market, the seat loses any ability to reach one audience without the other, and may watch the reported price rise within the season of the listing.
It keeps every protection the listing unlocks and loses control of what else the listing does.

## Drives

Toward the one whose most protective act can raise the price on the thing it protects,
listing because listing is right and is the precondition of all enforcement and, by doing so, publishing the scarcity that is the premium's whole source,
so the seat least open to criticism is one of the two through which the remedy turns, correct in the act and compromised in its second effect.
