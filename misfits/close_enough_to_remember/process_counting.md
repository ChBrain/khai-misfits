# Process: Counting

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md). Paired sera, case counts and an effectiveness figure, all of them correct.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A season ending, and a study designed properly before it began.
At [the clinic](place_the_clinic.md), afterwards, by people who did nothing wrong.

## Direction

Toward a number that is true and is about two things at once.
The vaccine's performance and the recipients' histories are both in it, and no arm of the trial is free of
the second, because every arm is made of people who were children in some particular year.

## Lever

Randomisation, which works on everything except this.
The confounder is carried inside each participant, is not measurable at enrolment without a serological
history nobody has, and is the same variable the intervention is being tested against.

## Echo

A figure that moves between seasons for reasons that are not about the vaccine,
two studies reaching opposite conclusions and both being right,
and a disagreement resolved only by looking at the distances between the strains.
