# Process: Meeting

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md). A drifted strain arriving in a host that has met its ancestor.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Ordinary transmission, in February, from somebody on a bus.
At [the bloodstream](place_the_bloodstream.md), within hours, with nothing deciding anything.

## Direction

Toward contact between a surface that is mostly familiar and a repertoire that is mostly right about it.
There is no comparison step. Nothing measures the match, nothing sets a threshold and nothing chooses
between responding and waiting: binding is a chemical event and it either happens or does not.

## Lever

None, at any point, held by anybody.
The host cannot know which strain they met at four, the strain cannot know what the host remembers, and the
outcome is a property of the pair.

## Echo

Contact,
recognition at the conserved sites,
and no recognition at all of the difference that will decide the season.
