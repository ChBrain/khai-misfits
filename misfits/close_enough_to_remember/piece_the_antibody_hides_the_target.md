# Piece: The Antibody Hides the Target

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md). Recalled antibody bound to the conserved surface lowers the antigen available to the lineages that would have answered the changed one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the bloodstream](place_the_bloodstream.md), where the binding happens within hours.
At [the lymph node](place_the_lymph_node.md), where the shortfall is felt and nothing starts.

## Load Bearing

Without it the naive lineage is merely slow, and the play is a race rather than a structure.
It is load bearing because **the old response suppresses the new one by consuming what the new one fed on**:
the naive cells are not outcompeted on merit, not rejected and not overruled, they are given less antigen
than their selection requires, by an act that is the correct thing for an antibody to do.

## Apparent

That the new response could have been encouraged alongside the old.
The two draw on the same consumable. An antibody that binds without reducing availability is not a
different setting of the process; it is not an antibody.

## Yearbook

The epitope-masking account: pre-existing cross-reactive antibody boosting memory lineages while lowering
the antigen reaching naive B cells specific for the changed sites.
