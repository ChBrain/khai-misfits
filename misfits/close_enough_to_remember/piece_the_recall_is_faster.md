# Piece: The Recall Is Faster

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md). A memory response arrives in days and a naive one in weeks, which settles the outcome before it is contested.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the memory seat](position_memory.md), which is fast because that is what it is for.
In [the bloodstream](place_the_bloodstream.md), where the antibody is present before the other route has begun.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is about a system choosing the worse of two answers, and stops being a misfit.
It is load bearing because **nothing chooses**: the two routes are not weighed against each other, they run
at different speeds, and the fast one finishes first. Speed is also the reason the memory is worth having at
all, so it cannot be given up without giving up the thing the memory is for.

## Apparent

That the immune system preferred the old answer.
It did not prefer anything. One process completes in three days and the other takes three weeks, and by the
time the second could have begun the input it needed had been taken.

## Yearbook

The founding observation: antibody responses to a new influenza strain directed most strongly at the strain
a person met earliest in life, so a serology carries the mark of a first exposure.
