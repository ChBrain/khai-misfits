# Persona: The Middle of the Range

## Taxonomy

[Distance](position_distance.md). The gap between two strains, wearing the shape of a party because the whole outcome answers to it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Impersonal, unchosen and quantitative.
Has no view about memory, about hosts or about any particular season.
Is a relation between two surfaces and nothing more.

## Action

Sits somewhere on a range.
At one end, lets the memory be right.
At the other, lets a fresh response be raised. In between, does the only harmful thing in the play.

## Shadow

That its two ends are both safe, which is why nothing here can be declared.
**A dial harmless at zero and harmless at the far edge has no direction to name**, so the house records the
shape and takes no sign, which is the mirror of what it does with a dial that is harmful at both ends.

## Tell

Is the only element with no occupant.
Is the element every outcome in the play is a function of.
