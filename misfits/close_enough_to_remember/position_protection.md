# Position: Protection

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md). The seat where the season's outcome lands, held by whoever was infected or was not.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A history it did not choose and cannot revise.
A vaccine offered on the same terms to everybody in the queue.
An outcome that depends on which strain was circulating when it was four years old.

## Orders

Take what is offered.
Be counted afterwards.
Have no way to know, in advance or after, which of the two cases you were in.

## Loses

A season, sometimes, and never learns that this was why.
**A poor response and a good exposure produce the same person walking out of the clinic**, so nothing in the
individual case distinguishes them, and the pattern is visible only in cohorts sorted by birth year.

## Drives

Toward a measure of an intervention that is partly a measure of who received it,
which no trial design removes,
because the variable it is confounded with is the recipient's own history.
