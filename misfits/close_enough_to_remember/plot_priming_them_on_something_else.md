# Plot: Priming Them on Something Else

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The settlements, all of them serious and several of them being pursued.

## Action

[Counting](process_counting.md): [The Host](persona_the_host.md), in [the protection seat](position_protection.md), works through [Prime Them on Something Else](plan_prime_them_on_something_else.md).

## Stage

[The Clinic](place_the_clinic.md): the same offer made to everybody in the queue, each autumn, on the best information available in August.

## Tension

Each settlement gives back the thing it was for.
**Prime on a conserved or distant target**, which works and is the direction the field is going, and you are
asking a programme to vaccinate against something other than what is circulating, which is a harder case to
make every year than the one it replaces.
**Raise the dose or add an adjuvant**, and you raise the whole response including the recalled part of it.
**Skip a year** to let the imprint fade, and the skipped year is uncovered.
**Choose the strain to minimise the distance from last year's**, which the antigenic distance work actually
supports, and you are betting on next year's drift with this year's information.
And the reading that would dissolve the misfit, that imprinting is on balance protective because a fast
answer usually beats a good one, is largely correct and is why the structure survives:
[The Middle of the Range](persona_the_middle_of_the_range.md) is untouched by all four, and **the thing to be
given up is the speed that is doing the most good.**
