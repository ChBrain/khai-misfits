# Piece: Nearly Always Right

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md). The recall gives the correct answer in the overwhelming majority of cases, and is the reason immunity is worth anything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the memory seat](position_memory.md), across a lifetime of exposures that went fine.
In every case of a second infection being milder than a first.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a complaint about immune memory, which would be false and would have no misfit in it.
It is load bearing because **the mechanism that fails here is the one everything else depends on**: recall
on resemblance is what makes vaccination work, what makes childhood illnesses once-only, and what the
procedure is right about nearly every time it runs. The input it cannot distinguish is rare, and the
procedure cannot be told to run only on the others.

## Apparent

That imprinting is a liability to be corrected.
A substantial literature reads it as net protective, and the play carries that: the structure is real at
intermediate distance and the general claim is not being made.

## Yearbook

The demonstrations that moved the effect from an epidemiological pattern to a reproducible one, in man and
then under controlled sequential exposure in animals.
