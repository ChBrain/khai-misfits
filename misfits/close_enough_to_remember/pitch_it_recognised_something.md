# Pitch: It Recognised Something

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact about what was measured and about what is inferred from it.
Unwilling to call the response a mistake, because nothing in it decides anything.
Naming the strain wherever the strain is the point.

## Undertow

Under the recall, one property of memory and one property of drift.
An existing answer is recalled on resemblance and a new one is built on availability.
And drift is change at the margin of something that stays itself.

## Nerve

The unease of a defence failing by doing precisely what it is for.
Nothing was slow. Nothing was absent. Nothing chose the worse of two answers.
The response that would have worked was never built, because the one that arrived first took its material.

## Echo

After the run: a memory that has been right for thirty years and is right about most of this strain,
three days instead of three weeks, which is the whole value of having it,
a lineage that was present, specific and never engaged,
and a season's figure that is partly a figure about who was born when.
