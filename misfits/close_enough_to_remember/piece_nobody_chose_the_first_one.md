# Piece: Nobody Chose the First One

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md). The imprinting strain is whichever one was circulating in a person's childhood, so responses sort by year of birth.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the protection seat](position_protection.md), carried by everybody in the queue.
At [the clinic](place_the_clinic.md), where it is present in every arm and measurable in none.

## Load Bearing

Without it somebody could have decided differently, and the failure would be a choice.
It is load bearing because **the deciding variable was fixed before the host could remember it**: nobody
selected the strain, nobody can revise the memory, and the memory that would have to be overwritten is the
one doing the answering. A population's outcomes therefore sort by birth year, which is not a treatment
anybody assigned.

## Apparent

That a trial can control for it.
It is carried inside each participant, is not measurable at enrolment without a serological history nobody
has, and is the same variable the intervention is being tested against.

## Yearbook

The birth-year patterning of influenza antibody repertoires, which is why an effectiveness figure is partly
a statement about who received the vaccine.
