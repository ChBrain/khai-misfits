# Position: Encounter

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md). The seat where the new strain arrives and is met, occupied by whatever is circulating.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A surface that is mostly the old surface and is not the old surface where it counts.
No intent, no strategy and no knowledge of what it is walking into.
The ordinary result of copying something many times under selection.

## Orders

Arrive.
Present whatever the copying left.
Be recognised or not, which is not a thing it does but a thing done to it.

## Loses

Nothing, and it is not an adversary.
**Drift is not evasion**, since a strain that changed at the sites under pressure did not aim at anybody's
memory, and the same change would have happened in a population with no memory at all.

## Drives

Toward a host whose memory is close enough to fire and not close enough to work,
which is a coincidence of two histories,
and is the only case in the play where anything goes wrong.
