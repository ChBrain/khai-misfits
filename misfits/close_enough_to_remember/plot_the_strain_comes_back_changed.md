# Plot: The Strain Comes Back Changed

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Drift: change at the margin of something that stays itself.

## Action

[Meeting](process_meeting.md): [The Strain That Drifted](persona_the_strain_that_drifted.md), in [the encounter seat](position_encounter.md), works through [Get Into a Host That Has Met One Before](plan_get_into_a_host_that_has_met_one_before.md).

## Stage

[The Bloodstream](place_the_bloodstream.md): antibody made years ago, still in circulation, and a surface arriving that is mostly the one it was made for.

## Tension

The play has to establish that no adversary is present, and it establishes it completely.
The change accumulated at the sites under selection, which is what happens to a thing copied many times in
a population, and it would have happened identically in hosts who had never seen the ancestor. **Drift is
not evasion.** Nothing here is aimed at anybody's memory.
And there is no comparison step anywhere. Nothing measures the match, nothing sets a threshold, and nothing
weighs responding against waiting: binding is a chemical event that either happens or does not.
At the end of it the surface that remains is enough to be recognised and the surface that changed is exactly
where recognition was needed, which is a coincidence of two histories and the only thing in the play that
goes wrong.
