# Plot: The Lineage Nobody Calls

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Naive cells specific for exactly the parts that changed, present in the host throughout.

## Action

[Calling Up the Naive](process_calling_up_the_naive.md): [The One That Was Never Called](persona_the_one_that_was_never_called.md), in [the recruitment seat](position_recruitment.md), works through [Raise a Response to What Is Actually There](plan_raise_a_response_to_what_is_actually_there.md).

## Stage

[The Lymph Node](place_the_lymph_node.md): the full machinery for making exactly the right antibody, and a germinal centre that could form and does not.

## Tension

Here the failure stops being about anybody's conduct and becomes a shortage of one input.
The cells are present, specific and capable, and in a host with no prior memory this route runs and gives
the right answer. What it needs is ordinary: enough antigen, for long enough, to be selected on.
[Masking](process_masking.md) does the rest, and
[the antibody hides the target](piece_the_antibody_hides_the_target.md): the recalled antibody bound the
conserved surface within hours, which is the correct thing for an antibody to do, and **lowered the antigen
reaching these cells below what their selection requires.** They are not outcompeted on merit, not rejected
and not overruled. The process does not begin.
And a process that does not begin leaves no record. Nothing in the host's serology says a better answer was
available, so the season's result reads as the limit of this immune system rather than as what it was never
given the chance to do.
