# Close Enough to Remember: Reference

The research warrant for Close Enough to Remember.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the imprinting literature in immunology, on the antigenic
distance hypothesis, and on the epitope-masking account of why a recalled response outruns a new one, all
cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging immune imprinting as **a defence that works by being fast and is defeated by being fast enough**: a
host meets a pathogen, builds a memory against it, and for the rest of its life answers anything resembling
that pathogen out of that memory. The answer arrives in days rather than weeks, which is the whole value of
having a memory, and it is the right answer nearly every time. When the pathogen has drifted, the memory is
recalled anyway, because recall is triggered by resemblance and the drifted strain resembles the original.
The recalled antibodies bind the parts that have not changed, do not neutralise the parts that have, and
**reduce the amount of antigen available to the lineages that would have answered the parts that did**, so
the response that would have been right is not merely unbuilt: it is starved of the material it needed to
be built from. The play models the documented mechanics, the memory that is nearly always correct, the
resemblance that triggers recall, the speed that settles the race before it is a race, the masking by which
the old antibody hides the new target, the naive lineage that is never called, and the priming that alone
changes the outcome at a cost, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a lazy immune
system or a mistake.

The governing law is that **an existing answer is recalled on resemblance and a new one is built on
availability**, so an answer good enough to be triggered is an answer good enough to consume the trigger.

What makes it structural is that **the harm sits in the middle of its own dial and both ends are fine**. Meet
the identical strain again and the memory is exactly right, which is what a vaccine is for. Meet something
antigenically unrelated and nothing cross-reacts, so a naive response is mounted from scratch and it is the
correct one. The suboptimal answer appears only at **intermediate antigenic distance**, where the new strain
is close enough to be recognised and far enough to have escaped, and that is the antigenic distance
hypothesis rather than a general property of memory. A dial with a good end at each extreme and the trouble
between them **has no direction to declare**, which is why this play carries no axis.

What removes any possibility of blame is that the mechanism is the same one everything else depends on. The
speed of a recalled response is not a defect being traded against accuracy: it is the reason a second
infection is milder than a first, and it is what vaccination buys. **The immune system is not choosing the
worse of two answers**; it is running the procedure that is right in the overwhelming majority of cases, on
an input the procedure cannot distinguish from the cases where it is right.

And nobody chose the memory. The imprinting strain is whichever one a person happened to meet first, usually
in childhood, so the population's responses are sorted by birth year rather than by anything that could have
been decided. **The variable that governs the outcome was fixed before the host had any say in anything**,
and it cannot be revised afterwards, because the memory that would have to be overwritten is the one doing
the answering.

It is distinct from the house's **The Good Move**, which stages the Einstellung effect, where a solver who
knows an adequate method applies it and the better solution goes unfound: there a search is stopped, the
better answer remains available, and a solver told to keep looking would find it. Stipulate that here, an
immune system instructed in some way to keep looking, and there is nothing to instruct: **no search is
running and no lineage declines to look**. The naive cells are not choosing not to respond, they are not
receiving the antigen, because the recalled antibody has bound it first. The engine is competition for a
consumable, not a satisfied searcher.

It is distinct from the house's **Second Nature**, the curse of knowledge, where an expert cannot set aside
what they know in judging a novice: there the quantity is **the knower's own prior knowledge** and the harm
is a misjudgement of somebody else. Here the quantity is **antigenic distance**, a property of the pair of
pathogens rather than of the system holding the memory, and nothing is being judged at all.

It is distinct from the house's **Bred for the Ark**, where a population adapts to the conditions it is kept
in and is worse in the conditions it was kept for: there something is genuinely optimised for the wrong
environment over generations of selection. Here nothing is optimised for anything: the memory was correct
for the strain that made it, remains correct for that strain, and is applied to a different one because the
trigger cannot tell them apart.

It is distinct from the house's **Borrowed Immunity**, which stages the coverage of an intervention across a
population: there the quantity is how much of a population is protected and the harm falls on those outside
the coverage. Here every individual has their own memory and the difficulty is inside each one of them, so
the play would run identically in a population of one.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the immune
  system as lazy or the memory as a flaw. Recall is fast because speed is what a memory is for, it is the
  reason a second infection is milder than a first, and it gives the right answer in the overwhelming
  majority of cases. The naive lineages are not declining to respond; they are not receiving the antigen.
  The host chose nothing, since the imprinting strain is whichever one was circulating in their childhood.
  The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that **an answer good
  enough to be triggered is an answer good enough to consume the trigger**.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a better response. Priming on a distant or a
  conserved target works and asks a programme to vaccinate against something other than what is circulating,
  which is a harder case to make every year than the one it replaces. Higher doses and adjuvants raise the
  whole response including the recalled part. Skipping a year to let the imprint fade leaves the season it
  is skipped in uncovered. Choosing the strain to minimise the distance from last year's is the move the
  antigenic distance work actually supports, and it is a bet on next year's drift made with this year's
  information. And the reading that would dissolve the misfit, that imprinting is on balance protective
  because a fast answer usually beats a good one, is largely correct and is why the structure survives:
  **the thing to be given up is the speed that is doing the most good.**
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the immunology and the epidemiology are theirs. The
  bounds are carried with them. **The size and even the sign of the effect are contested**, and a substantial
  literature reads imprinting as net protective, so the play stages a structure that is real at intermediate
  distance and refuses the stronger claim that memory is generally a liability. **Most of the evidence is
  influenza's**, with dengue and coronaviruses adding cases that are argued rather than settled. **The
  mechanism is one of several proposed**, and epitope masking is carried here as the account that makes the
  structure legible rather than as a closed question. **The magnitudes those sources report are deliberately
  not carried here**, because the works themselves could not be read in the pass that cited them: publisher
  hosts and the article archives are blocked to it, so the bibliography, the findings and their directions
  were verified by search and corroborated across independent reports, which is the treatment a citation
  survives and a reported figure is not.
