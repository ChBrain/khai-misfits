## Tension

This is the plot that locates the harm, and locating it is what stops the play declaring anything.
[Harm in the middle](piece_harm_in_the_middle.md), read off [the distance seat](position_distance.md), which
holds [Land in the Middle of the Range](plan_land_in_the_middle_of_the_range.md) and no occupant: at no
antigenic distance the memory is exactly right, at
large distance nothing cross-reacts and a fresh response is raised and correct, and the suboptimal answer
lives only in the stretch between. Two vaccination studies reached opposite conclusions about repeating a
vaccine and both were right, and the disagreement resolved when somebody looked at the distances between the
strains rather than at the repetition.
And [nobody chose the first one](piece_nobody_chose_the_first_one.md), so the figure is partly a statement
about who received the vaccine. The confounder is carried inside each participant, is present in every arm,
cannot be measured at enrolment without a serological history nobody has, and **is the same variable the
intervention is being tested against.**
