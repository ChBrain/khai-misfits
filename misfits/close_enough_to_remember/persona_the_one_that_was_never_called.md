# Persona: The One That Was Never Called

## Taxonomy

[Recruitment](position_recruitment.md). A naive lineage specific for exactly the parts that changed, present throughout and never engaged.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Capable, specific and already in the host.
Would have made the antibody that neutralised this strain.
Needs the ordinary thing every naive lineage needs, which is antigen, for long enough.

## Action

Waits.
Receives less antigen than it needed, because something else bound it first.
Does nothing, which is not a refusal.

## Shadow

That its absence is indistinguishable from its non-existence.
**An uncalled lineage leaves no trace**, so nothing in the host's serology records that a better answer was
available, and the season's result reads as what this host's immune system can do rather than as what it
was not given the chance to do.

## Tell

Is the only element in the play that could have fixed it.
Is the only element in the play that was never asked.
