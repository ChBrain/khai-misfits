# Process: Masking

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md). Bound antibody covering the surface, so less of the new thing reaches anywhere it could be answered.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Recalled antibody, in circulation, doing what antibody does.
At [the bloodstream](place_the_bloodstream.md) and on the way to [the lymph node](place_the_lymph_node.md).

## Direction

Toward less free antigen, sooner.
This is not interference and not sabotage: clearing antigen is the point of an antibody response, and it is
what makes a recalled response protective in the cases where the recall is right.

## Lever

None, and the same act cannot be had selectively.
An antibody that binds the conserved parts and does not reduce their availability is not a different
setting of the same process; it is not an antibody.

## Echo

A surface partly covered,
naive cells receiving less than the amount their selection needs,
and a suppression produced entirely by a success.
