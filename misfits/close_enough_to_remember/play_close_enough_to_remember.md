# Play: Close Enough to Remember

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Close Enough to Remember.
A host met a strain in childhood and built a memory against it, and that memory has been right ever since.
This year's strain has drifted: the parts the memory binds are still there, and the parts that matter have changed.
The memory answers in three days, the answer does not neutralise, and the response that would have worked is never built.

## Arc

The bend is this: a memory is recalled on resemblance and a new response is built on availability, and the recall takes the availability with it.

Take the memory first, because the play has to establish that nothing is wrong with it. A recalled response arrives in days where a naive one takes weeks. That speed is the whole value of having been infected once, it is why a second infection is milder than a first, and it is what a vaccine buys. **The memory is the right answer nearly every time**, and it is right about this strain too, for every part of it that has not changed.

Then the trigger, which cannot tell the cases apart. Recall fires on **resemblance**, and the drifted strain resembles the original, because drift is change at the margin of something that stays itself. There is no step at which anybody or anything compares the match against a threshold and decides. The memory lineages see enough of what they were made for, and they respond.

Then the fact that makes it a structure rather than a shortfall. **The recalled antibody binds the parts that did not change**, which does not neutralise a strain that escaped at the parts that did, and it also **lowers the amount of antigen reaching the naive lineages** that would have answered the changed parts. So the response that would have been right is not merely unbuilt: it is starved of the material it needed to be built from. The old answer suppresses the new one by consuming what the new one fed on.

Then the shape of the harm, which is the reason this play declares nothing. **Both ends of the dial are fine.** Meet the identical strain and the memory is exactly right. Meet something unrelated and nothing cross-reacts, so a naive response is mounted and it is correct. The trouble appears only at **intermediate distance**, where the new strain is close enough to be recognised and far enough to have escaped, which is a documented shape and not a general property of remembering.

And nobody chose the memory. The imprinting strain is whichever one was circulating when a person was small, so a population's responses sort by year of birth. **The variable that governs the outcome was fixed before the host had any say in anything**, and it cannot be revised afterwards, because the memory that would have to be overwritten is the one doing the answering.

Reach for a settlement and each one gives back the thing that was wanted. Prime on a distant or a conserved target, which works, and you are asking a programme to vaccinate against something other than what is circulating. Raise the dose, and you raise the whole response including the recalled part of it. Skip a year to let the imprint fade, and the skipped year is uncovered. Choose the strain to minimise the distance from last year's, which the evidence actually supports, and you are betting on next year's drift with this year's information.

## Triggers

**[The Strain Comes Back Changed](plot_the_strain_comes_back_changed.md)**
Cue: drift, which is change at the margin of something that stays itself. Closes when it is clear that the new strain resembles the old at the parts that did not change and has escaped at the parts that did, and that no step in the process compares the two against a threshold.

**[What the Blood Already Has](plot_what_the_blood_already_has.md)**
Cue: circulating antibody, made years ago and still correct about what it was made for. Closes when the recall is seen as the right default, fast for the reason speed is worth having, and applied here to an input the procedure cannot distinguish from the cases where it is right.

**[The Lineage Nobody Calls](plot_the_lineage_nobody_calls.md)**
Cue: the naive cells specific for the changed parts, present and unengaged. Closes when it is clear they are not declining to respond but are not receiving the antigen, because the recalled antibody has bound it first.

**[Counting the Season](plot_counting_the_season.md)**
Cue: paired sera and a season's effectiveness figure. Closes when the harm is located at intermediate distance and nowhere else, so that both ends of the dial are seen to be fine and the measure of the intervention is partly a measure of who received it.

**[Priming Them on Something Else](plot_priming_them_on_something_else.md)**
Cue: the settlements. Closes when each is found to give back what it was for: a target that is not what is circulating, a dose that raises the recalled part too, a skipped year that is uncovered, and a strain choice that bets on next year's drift with this year's information.

## Stakes

Whether a defence built on remembering can meet something that has changed.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the memory is good, because it is, and not whether anybody erred, because nothing in the sequence decides anything, but whether an answer fast enough to be worth having can be prevented from taking the place of a better one. The production cannot settle it. It can only show that the recall is correct nearly always, that resemblance is what fires it, that the old antibody consumes what the new response needed, that the trouble lives at one stretch of the dial and not at its ends, and that the remedy costs the speed that is doing the most good.
