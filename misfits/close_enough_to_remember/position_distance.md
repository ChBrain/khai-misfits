# Position: Distance

## Taxonomy

[Close Enough to Remember](play_close_enough_to_remember.md). The seat that sets how far the new strain sits from the old, occupied by nobody.

## Owner

No occupant, no view, and no interest in either of them.
Complete control over whether anything goes wrong at all.

## Orders

Be large, and nothing cross-reacts, so a fresh response is raised and it is correct.

## Loses

Nothing, because it was never anybody's.
**Both of its ends are good**, which is the fact that keeps this play from declaring an axis: a dial that
is harmless at zero and harmless at the far edge has no direction to name, and the trouble lives in the
stretch between.
