# Plot: The Bitter Better

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The gap is felt now, not merely measured, and judged against what was expected.

## Action

At [the Gap](place_the_gap.md), [the Aggrieved](persona_the_aggrieved.md) from [the aggrieved's seat](position_aggrieved.md) on [Measure the Gap](plan_measure_the_gap.md) through [Grieving](process_grieving.md) takes up [the Discontent](piece_the_discontent.md), the shortfall between what is held and what is now looked for, felt as grievance.

## Stage

The Widening Gap that the Riser opened is here taken up and lived: the Aggrieved measures its shortfall not against having nothing, but against the standard the Improvement itself raised, and a standard that climbs faster than attainment leaves a felt shortfall that grows even while attainment grows (Stouffer; Runciman; Gurr). The discontent is therefore sharpest not among those who have least but among those who have gained the most and looked for more still, a bitterness that tracks the size of the recent gain rather than the size of the remaining want (Stouffer; Runciman; Gurr). This is why the reform that relieves can also be the reform that provokes: the relief moved the standard, and the standard now returns a verdict on what the relief left undone. No villain: the Aggrieved's grievance is honest arithmetic, a real distance measured against a real and risen standard, not the ingratitude of the comfortable complaining from plenty. It is angriest precisely where it was given the most, and that is the discontent working exactly as a rising standard says it should.

## Tension

A grievance that grows as attainment grows, sharpest where the gain was greatest.
