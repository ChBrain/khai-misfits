# Plan: Expect the Next

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the riser's seat](position_riser.md), inside [The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Riser](position_riser.md)

## Direction

Read the improvement as the promise of more, since a gain demonstrated is reasonably expected to continue and a burden shown liftable is no longer borne as inevitable, and raise your expectation as any person who saw the gain would. Know that the expectation, once raised, climbs past the condition, that your fair hope outruns the gain and opens the gap between what you have and what you now expect, and that the widening is no fault in your wanting but hope tracking a gain and then outrunning it.

## Orders

([The Riser](persona_the_riser.md) takes up [the Rising Expectation](piece_the_rising_expectation.md) through [Expecting](process_expecting.md) at [the Reform](place_the_reform.md), raising its hopes with the gain, and meets [the Widening Gap](piece_the_widening_gap.md) as expectation outruns condition.)

## Implementation

([Expecting](process_expecting.md) at [the Reform](place_the_reform.md) takes up [the Rising Expectation](piece_the_rising_expectation.md), because a gain shown is read as a gain to come, so the hope climbs on the improvement and opens [the Widening Gap](piece_the_widening_gap.md) at [the Gap](place_the_gap.md) (Tocqueville; Davies).)

## Targets

- [ ] the improvement is read as the promise of more, and the hope raised with it
- [ ] the rising expectation is understood as reasonable, a right reading of the prospects
- [ ] it is seen that the hope climbs past the condition and opens the widening gap
- [ ] it is owned that the widening is no fault in the wanting but hope outrunning the gain
