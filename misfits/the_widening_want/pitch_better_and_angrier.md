# Pitch: Better and Angrier

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a society can grow better and angrier at once, because the gain that relieves is the gain that raises the bar.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Measured and rueful, the irony of a gain that unsettles. A reform that honestly relieves, and a discontent that grows as it does. Nothing here is a mob biting the hand that fed it or a reformer who loosed the flood; the improvement is real and the rising hope is reasonable.

## Undertow

A gain once shown is a gain now expected, so expectations rise with the conditions and past a point outrun them, and the gap between what is had and what is felt owed widens as things get better (Tocqueville). A sharp reversal after a long rise opens the gap intolerably, expectation still climbing while gratification drops, and the distance between the expected and the actual turns to unrest (Davies).

## Nerve

That to make things better is to raise the bar by which they are judged, so progress can widen the want it was meant to fill and breed the discontent it was meant to quiet. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since the felt gap is a real pressure and not a guarantee of revolt, grievance widespread and revolution rare, mobilization and opportunity mediating the turn (Gurr; the mobilization critique).

## Echo

Relieve a burden, and prove the rest could be lifted too, and be judged now by the burden that remains.
