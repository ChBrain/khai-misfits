# Place: The Gap

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). Where the shortfall is felt, the space between the had and the now-expected, and where a sharp reversal opens it. Hosts [The Space Between](plot_the_space_between.md), [The Bitter Better](plot_the_bitter_better.md), [The Downturn](plot_the_downturn.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The distance between what has been given and what is now expected, measured and found still open, and in one turn found suddenly wider.
A standard risen faster than the conditions that raised it, and, when the rise checks, gratification falling while the standard keeps climbing on memory alone.

## Holds

[The Widening Gap](piece_the_widening_gap.md).
The space itself, expectation outrunning condition, growing rather than closing as things get better (Tocqueville; Davies).

[The Discontent](piece_the_discontent.md).
The grievance the gap produces, felt against a risen standard and sharpest where much was gained and more was hoped (Stouffer; Runciman; Gurr).

[The Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md).
The short sharp check after a long rise, expectation still climbing while gratification drops, the gap turned intolerable (Davies).

## Offers

The true reckoning withheld at the Reform: a place to weigh what was gotten against what was hoped for, and to feel the difference as a shortfall rather than as a gain.
When the long rise checks, it offers the clearest measure of all, the J-curve's turn laid bare (Davies).

## Withheld

Any comfort in the arithmetic.
The Gap will not credit what has already been gained against what is still wanted; it holds only the remainder, and the remainder is what is felt.
