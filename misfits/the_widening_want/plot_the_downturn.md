# Plot: The Downturn

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

After the long rise, a short sharp check in what can actually be gotten.

## Action

At [the Gap](place_the_gap.md), [the Aggrieved](persona_the_aggrieved.md) from [the aggrieved's seat](position_aggrieved.md) through [Grieving](process_grieving.md) meets [the Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md), the check that drops gratification while expectation is still climbing.

## Stage

The Discontent the Aggrieved already carries was a steady grievance, tracking a gap that widened gradually as expectation outpaced condition. The Reversal changes the pace on one side only: gratification drops, sharply and without warning, while expectation keeps its climb, still running on the memory of the long improvement that preceded the check. This is the shape Davies named a curve, a prolonged rise in both expectations and their satisfaction followed by a sudden, sharp reversal, so that the gap between what is expected and what is actually delivered opens past what the gradual widening alone had reached (Davies). It is the distance between the two lines, not the absolute height of either, that does the work: a society objectively no worse off than it was earlier can feel a gap it never felt before, because the standard it is measured against has not paused to wait for it (Davies). No villain: the downturn is an ordinary reversal, a recession, a bad harvest, a policy retracted under pressure, authored by no particular hand and aimed at no one; it is not a fresh injury, only the same standard the reform itself raised, meeting a condition that has, for once, gone the other way.

## Tension

A long rise and a sudden drop, meeting in a gap too wide for what came before it to explain.
