# Plan: Make It Better

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reformer's seat](position_reformer.md), inside [The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reformer](position_reformer.md)

## Direction

Improve the conditions in good faith, lift the oppression or extend the right or raise the standard, by the plain rule that to relieve a burden is to do good and to close a gap is the aim. Know that the gain does not only add to what people have but raises what they expect, that a burden lifted proves the rest could be lifted too, and that the parts you improve are the parts whose remainder is felt as least bearable.

## Orders

([The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) holds [the Improvement](piece_the_improvement.md) through [Improving](process_improving.md) at [the Reform](place_the_reform.md), relieving a real oppression or extending a right or raising a standard in good faith.)

## Implementation

([Improving](process_improving.md) at [the Reform](place_the_reform.md) holds [the Improvement](piece_the_improvement.md), because to relieve a burden is to do good, so the gain closes a distance and, in the closing, proves the rest removable and raises the bar (Tocqueville).)

## Targets

- [ ] a real oppression is relieved or a right extended or a standard raised, in good faith
- [ ] the gain is understood to raise what people expect, not only what they have
- [ ] it is seen that a burden lifted proves the rest could be lifted too
- [ ] it is owned that the improved parts are the parts whose remainder is felt as least bearable
