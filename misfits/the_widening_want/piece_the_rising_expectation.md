# Piece: The Rising Expectation

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The mechanism: the aspiration that climbs on the gain itself, a fair reading of prospects rather than an appetite gone loose.

## Load Bearing

An improvement shown is an improvement reasonably expected to continue: once a burden has been proven liftable, it is no longer carried as inevitable, and the standard by which the remaining burden is judged has already moved (Tocqueville, The Old Regime and the Revolution, 1856). This is not a distortion of the evidence. It is a sound reading of it: if this much could be lifted, more plausibly can be too, and the person who expects that is reasoning correctly about a trend they have just watched happen. The aspiration rises exactly where the gain rose, tracking it rather than outpacing it in any way that could yet be called unreasonable.

## Apparent

Reads as greed, as an appetite that no gain can fill, an ingrate's hunger dressed up as a claim.
