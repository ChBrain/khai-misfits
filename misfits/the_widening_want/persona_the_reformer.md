# Persona: The Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Reformer](position_reformer.md), inside [The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The archetype of the one who improves conditions in good faith, relieving an oppression or extending a right or raising a standard, and by the very gain raises the bar against which the remainder is judged.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A fool who loosed the flood, as though relieving a burden were a blunder that stirred an ungrateful people, when to lift a burden is plainly good and the gain was honest and meant only to help.

## Action

Make it better. At [the Reform](place_the_reform.md), the Reformer on [Make It Better](plan_make_it_better.md) through [Improving](process_improving.md) holds [the Improvement](piece_the_improvement.md), relieving a real oppression or extending a right or raising a standard, offered in good faith to close a gap between how people live and how they might (Tocqueville).

## Shadow

Its gain is real and its aim is to relieve, and the gain raises the bar all the same. The Reformer is no fool: to lift a burden is to do good, and the improvement it makes is honest and meant to close the distance between how people live and how they might. But a burden once lifted proves the rest could be lifted too, and a gain once shown becomes the floor from which the next is expected, so the very relief it gives raises the standard against which the remaining faults are judged, and the parts it improved are the parts whose remainder is felt as least bearable. It keeps its honest gain and its good intent, and cannot relieve a burden without proving the rest removable, the reformer whose success raises the bar it is then judged by.

## Tell

It lifted a real burden in good faith, and by lifting it proved the rest could go, and so was judged by the rest.
