# Piece: The Improvement

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The mechanism: a real gain, offered in good faith, that relieves one burden and thereby proves the rest were liftable too.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Reform](place_the_reform.md), carried by [Improving](process_improving.md), voiced by [the Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md): a burden actually lifted, a duty eased, a right actually extended, the paperwork of the old arrangement quietly retired and nobody made to miss it.

## Load Bearing

The gain is genuine: an oppression relieved, a right extended, a standard raised, and the reformer means only to relieve it. But an evil borne as inevitable is borne differently once it is shown removable (Tocqueville, The Old Regime and the Revolution, 1856); the very act of lifting one burden demonstrates, to everyone still carrying a neighboring one, that theirs was liftable too. The improvement does not simply subtract a hardship from the ledger. It reclassifies every remaining hardship from fate to choice, and choice is what a bar gets measured against. The gain, precisely by being real, raises what is now expected of the rest (Tocqueville).

## Apparent

Reads as a pure good, a straightforward relief that should, by its own logic, leave people more content than they were before it.
