# Position: Reformer

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The seat of the one who improves conditions in good faith, and marks that the gain is honest and its aim to relieve, and that it raises the bar against which the remainder is judged all the same.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real improvement to make, an oppression to relieve or a right to extend or a standard to raise.
The good and plain rule that to lift a burden is to do good, and the honest aim of closing a gap.
No way to relieve a burden without proving the rest removable, since a gain shown becomes the floor from which the next is expected.

## Orders

Make it better, relieve the burden and close the gap.
Improve the conditions in good faith, lift the oppression or extend the right or raise the standard, by the plain rule that to relieve a burden is to do good and to close a gap is the aim.
Know that the gain does not only add to what people have but raises what they expect, that a burden lifted proves the rest could be lifted too, and that the parts you improve are the parts whose remainder is felt as least bearable.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and this is the irony.
Because it improves in good faith and by the good rule that relieving a burden does good, the seat takes no loss and does real good, and yet the gain it makes raises the standard against which the remaining faults are judged, so the very relief it gives breeds the discontent that relief was meant to quiet, a harm no fault of its intent.
It keeps its honest gain and its good aim, and loses no cause for blame, the reformer whose success raises the bar it is then judged by.

## Drives

Toward the one who relieves a real burden and raises the bar by relieving it,
lifting an oppression in good faith to close a gap, blind to no one and meaning only to help,
so the seat whose gain is honest and whose aim is relief is the seat from whose very improvement the widening want grows.
