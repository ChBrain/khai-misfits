# Persona: The Aggrieved

## Taxonomy

[Aggrieved](position_aggrieved.md), inside [The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The archetype of the one who feels the widening gap as grievance, measuring what is had against a risen standard, and whom a sharp reversal tips toward the intolerable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A malcontent who complains from plenty, as though its grievance were the ingratitude of the comfortable, when the shortfall it feels is real, measured against a standard the improvement itself raised.

## Action

Measure the gap. At [the Gap](place_the_gap.md), the Aggrieved on [Measure the Gap](plan_measure_the_gap.md) through [Grieving](process_grieving.md) takes up [the Discontent](piece_the_discontent.md), feeling the shortfall between what it has and what it now expects, then meets [the Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md), the sharp check that opens the gap intolerably (Gurr; Davies).

## Shadow

Its grievance is real, and it grows precisely as things improve. The Aggrieved is no malcontent complaining from plenty: discontent is measured not against zero but against a standard, and when the standard rises faster than attainment the felt shortfall grows even as attainment grows, so it is angriest not when it has least but when it has gained much and looked for more. The gap it feels is not an appetite but a real distance between the risen expectation and the slower condition, and a short sharp reversal, expectation still climbing while gratification drops, opens that distance past bearing. It keeps its real grievance and its honest measure, and feels a shortfall that widened with the gain, the one whose discontent is sharpest where the improvement was greatest.

## Tell

It gained much and was angrier for it, because it measured the gain against a standard the gain had raised.
