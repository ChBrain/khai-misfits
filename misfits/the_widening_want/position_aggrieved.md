# Position: Aggrieved

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The seat of the one who feels the widening gap as grievance, and marks that the shortfall is real and measured against a risen standard, sharpest where the gain was greatest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real shortfall to feel, the distance between what it has and what it now expects.
A standard raised by the improvement, against which the remaining faults are judged and found wanting.
A sharp reversal ahead, expectation still climbing while gratification drops, to open the gap past bearing.

## Orders

Measure the gap, and feel the shortfall against the standard.
Judge what you have against what you now expect, since discontent is measured not against zero but against a standard, and the standard has risen with the gain.
Know that the shortfall grows even as attainment grows, that you are angriest not where you have least but where you gained much and looked for more, and that a sharp reversal will open the gap intolerably, the expected still climbing as the gotten drops.

## Loses

Its contentment, though it gained, to a shortfall it did not invent.
Because it measures what it has against a standard the improvement raised, the seat feels a real distance between the expected and the actual that widens as attainment grows, so its discontent is sharpest where the gain was greatest, a grievance not of plenty but of a standard outrun.
It keeps its real grievance and its honest measure, and loses no cause for blame, the one whose discontent grew with the very improvement.

## Drives

Toward the one whose grievance is real and grows as things improve,
measuring what it has against a standard the gain has raised, angriest where much was gained and more expected,
so the seat that feels the shortfall against the risen standard is the seat a sharp reversal tips toward the intolerable, its grievance no ingratitude but a distance truly felt.
