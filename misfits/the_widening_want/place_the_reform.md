# Place: The Reform

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). Where conditions improve and expectations rise, the scene of the good-faith gain. Hosts [The Given Inch](plot_the_given_inch.md), [The Raised Hope](plot_the_raised_hope.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An oppression eased, a right extended, a standard of living raised, the gain visible and delivered in good faith.
The relief and the hope it kindles arriving in the same hour, so close together that the one is hard to see apart from the other.

## Holds

[The Improvement](piece_the_improvement.md).
The real gain itself, the burden lifted or the right extended, honest and meant only to close a distance (Tocqueville).

[The Rising Expectation](piece_the_rising_expectation.md).
The hope that climbs on the gain shown, read as a promise of more to come and not yet measured against what follows.

## Offers

A stage where the gain and the hope it raises can be watched together, before either is judged against the other.
Here the Reformer's work is plain to see, and the Riser's hope is still reasonable, still keeping pace with what has actually been given.

## Withheld

Any accounting of the distance between them.
The Reform shows the gain and the hope it kindles side by side, but not the gap that opens once the hope outpaces the gain; that reckoning belongs elsewhere.
