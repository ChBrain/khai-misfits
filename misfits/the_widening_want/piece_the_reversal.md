# Piece: The Reversal

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The mechanism: a short sharp check after a long rise, expectation still climbing on memory while gratification drops.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Gap](place_the_gap.md), carried by [Grieving](process_grieving.md), voiced by [the Aggrieved](persona_the_aggrieved.md): the good years still fresh enough to be the baseline everyone is planning around, and the ledger for this year suddenly running the other way.

## Load Bearing

The J curve: a prolonged rise in condition builds expectation that keeps climbing on its own momentum, and when condition then turns down sharply, expectation does not turn with it. It keeps rising for a while on the memory of the trend, so the line of what is expected and the line of what is delivered, which had been close, snap apart in a single stroke (Davies, "Toward a Theory of Revolution," 1962). The gap that took years to widen gradually now opens intolerably in a season. Nothing about the reversal need be anyone's doing in a culpable sense; a harvest fails, a market turns, a war interrupts supply. The arithmetic is the same arithmetic as before, only compressed.

## Apparent

Reads as a mere hardship, an unlucky year, hard but ordinary, the kind of setback any improving trend has always absorbed and outlasted.
