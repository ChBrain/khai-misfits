# Persona: The Riser

## Taxonomy

[Riser](position_riser.md), inside [The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The archetype of the one whose expectations rise with the gain, reading a gain shown as a gain to come, and so opening the gap between what is had and what is now expected.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A greedy soul who is never satisfied, as though its rising hope were an appetite that no gain could fill, when it only read an honest improvement as the promise of more that any reasonable person would.

## Action

Expect the next. At [the Reform](place_the_reform.md), the Riser on [Expect the Next](plan_expect_the_next.md) through [Expecting](process_expecting.md) takes up [the Rising Expectation](piece_the_rising_expectation.md), raising its hopes with the gain, then at [the Gap](place_the_gap.md) meets [the Widening Gap](piece_the_widening_gap.md), its expectation outrunning the condition (Tocqueville; Davies).

## Shadow

Its hope is reasonable, and it is the hope that opens the gap. The Riser is not greedy or insatiable: an improvement demonstrated is an improvement reasonably expected to continue, and a burden shown to be liftable is one no longer accepted as fixed, so to raise its hopes with the gain is to read its prospects rightly, as any person who saw the gain would. But the expectation, once raised, climbs on the memory of the improvement and past a point faster than the improvement itself, so the gap between what it has and what it now expects widens rather than closes, opened not by any fault in its wanting but by hope tracking a gain and then outrunning it. It keeps its reasonable hope and its honest reading, and opens the gap by rising, the one whose fair expectation the conditions cannot keep pace with.

## Tell

It read an honest gain as the promise of more, as anyone would, and its fair hope outran the gain and opened the gap.
