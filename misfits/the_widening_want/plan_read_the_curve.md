# Plan: Read the Curve

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the historian's seat](position_historian.md), inside [The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Historian](position_historian.md)

## Direction

Trace across the cases the long rise of expectation and gratification, the sharp reversal, and the gap between the expected and the actual that turned to unrest, and name that gap as the pressure it was. Know that grievance and rising expectations are widespread while revolt is rare, that whether a felt gap becomes collective action turns on organization, resources, and opportunity the gap alone does not supply, and that to read every reform as the certain seed of revolution is to over-read the pattern.

## Orders

([The Historian](persona_the_historian.md) holds [the Unrest](piece_the_unrest.md) through [Reading](process_reading.md) at [the Long View](place_the_long_view.md), reading across the cases where a long rise and a sharp reversal preceded the rising, and naming the gap that drove it.)

## Implementation

([Reading](process_reading.md) at [the Long View](place_the_long_view.md) holds [the Unrest](piece_the_unrest.md), because the recurring shape names the gap as the pressure, so the outcome is held to a bound, mediated by mobilization and opportunity and not fixed (Davies; Gurr; the mobilization critique).)

## Targets

- [ ] the recurring shape of long rise, sharp reversal, and gap turned to unrest is traced across the cases
- [ ] the gap between the expected and the actual is named as the pressure that drove the rising
- [ ] it is seen that grievance is widespread while revolt is rare, so the gap is a pressure and not a trigger
- [ ] it is owned that the turn is mediated by mobilization and opportunity, and not to be read as fixed
