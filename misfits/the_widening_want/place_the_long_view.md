# Place: The Long View

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The historian's vantage over the cases, where the pattern is read and the bound held. Hosts [The Turning](plot_the_turning.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Case set against case, the improving conditions and the widening gap and, in some but not all of them, the unrest that follows.
A pattern held at a distance long enough to see its shape, and held close enough to see where the shape stops predicting.

## Holds

[The Unrest](piece_the_unrest.md).
The discontent that can turn to collective action, read across the cases where progress preceded revolt and weighed against the cases where it did not (Davies; Gurr; the mobilization critique).

## Offers

The pattern named, the J-curve seen across enough instances to be more than one story (Davies), and the bound that keeps the naming honest.
From here the gap is read as a real pressure, not assumed to be a verdict.

## Withheld

Certainty that the pattern will repeat.
Grievance is common and revolt is rare, and organization, resources, and opportunity decide whether a felt gap turns to action, so the Long View can name the pressure but never guarantee the turn (the mobilization critique).
