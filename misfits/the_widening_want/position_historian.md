# Position: Historian

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The seat of the one who reads the pattern across the cases, that progress preceded the revolt, and marks that the gap is a real pressure and not a guarantee, mobilization and opportunity mediating the turn.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A view across the cases, the recurring shape of a long rise, a sharp reversal, and a gap that turned to unrest.
A right naming of the pressure, the distance between the expected and the actual that drove the rising.
A held bound, the knowledge that grievance is widespread and revolt rare, so the gap is a pressure and not a trigger that must fire.

## Orders

Read the curve, and name the gap that drove the rising.
Trace across the cases the long rise of expectation and gratification, the sharp reversal, and the gap between the expected and the actual that turned to unrest, and name that gap as the pressure it was.
Know that grievance and rising expectations are widespread while revolt is rare, that whether a felt gap becomes collective action turns on organization, resources, and opportunity the gap alone does not supply, and that to read every reform as the certain seed of revolution is to over-read the pattern.

## Loses

Nothing it can name, and holds a truth it must not overstate.
Because it reads the pattern from above and names the gap rightly, the seat takes no loss, and yet it carries the harder discipline: to hold a real and recurring pressure to its bound, since grievance is common and revolution rare and the turn is mediated, so it must name the pressure without declaring the outcome fixed.
It keeps its true pattern and its held bound, and loses no cause for blame, the one who reads a real pressure and will not make it a law.

## Drives

Toward the one who reads the pattern and holds it to a pressure,
tracing the long rise and the sharp reversal and the gap that turned to unrest across the cases, and naming the gap that drove it,
so the seat that alone reads the recurring shape holds even its own reading to a bound, a pressure named and the outcome left mediated, not fixed.
