# Plot: The Raised Hope

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The gain is shown, and its promise is read as a promise of more.

## Action

At [the Reform](place_the_reform.md), [the Riser](persona_the_riser.md) from [the riser's seat](position_riser.md) on [Expect the Next](plan_expect_the_next.md) through [Expecting](process_expecting.md) takes up [the Rising Expectation](piece_the_rising_expectation.md), reading the gain shown as a gain to come.

## Stage

Where the Improvement fell, the Riser no longer holds the remaining burden as inevitable: a wage once raised, a burden once lifted, becomes proof that the rest could be lifted too, and what was endured as the fixed shape of things is now seen as a shape that could still change (Tocqueville). The hope this raises is not appetite outrunning sense; it is a plain reading of what has just been demonstrated. An evil patiently borne while it seemed inescapable becomes intolerable the moment its escape is conceived, and the Riser has just watched an escape be conceived and taken (Tocqueville). So the standard by which the remaining conditions are judged lifts with the gain that relieved them, quietly and at once, before any further gain has been delivered. No villain: the Riser's hope reads the prospects rightly, exactly as any reasonable person who had watched the gain would read them; it asks nothing that the reform itself had not just shown to be possible. The hope is honest and the gain is honest, and between them they set a standard that the conditions have not yet met.

## Tension

A reasonable hope, raised by a real gain, that will run ahead of the gain that raised it.
