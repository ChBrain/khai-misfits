# Plan: Measure the Gap

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the aggrieved's seat](position_aggrieved.md), inside [The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Aggrieved](position_aggrieved.md)

## Direction

Judge what you have against what you now expect, since discontent is measured not against zero but against a standard, and the standard has risen with the gain. Know that the shortfall grows even as attainment grows, that you are angriest not where you have least but where you gained much and looked for more, and that a sharp reversal will open the gap intolerably, the expected still climbing as the gotten drops.

([The Aggrieved](persona_the_aggrieved.md) takes up [the Discontent](piece_the_discontent.md) through [Grieving](process_grieving.md) at [the Gap](place_the_gap.md), feeling the shortfall against the risen standard, and meets [the Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md) that opens the gap intolerably.)
