# Plot: The Given Inch

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An oppression long borne is relieved, or a right extended, or a standard raised, and the reform is genuine.

## Action

At [the Reform](place_the_reform.md), [the Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) from [the reformer's seat](position_reformer.md) on [Make It Better](plan_make_it_better.md) through [Improving](process_improving.md) takes up [the Improvement](piece_the_improvement.md), lifting a burden that had been carried as though it could not be set down.

## Stage

The Reformer means only to relieve: a wage raised, a censorship eased, a tax lightened, a right extended to those who had gone without it. The gain is not staged and not partial in its own terms; it closes a real distance between how people live and how they might, and it is offered because closing that distance is plainly good. But the Improvement does more than it is asked to do. To show that one burden can be lifted is to show that the rest were liftable too, and a people who had carried the remaining weight as though it were simply how things are now see it as something that could be set down (Tocqueville). The reform intends only its own relief, yet it becomes evidence, proof against the fatalism that had kept the larger burden bearable. No villain: to lift a burden is plainly good, and the Reformer improves conditions in good faith and for their own sake, wanting nothing but to relieve what could be relieved. The harm the gain will do to the standard by which the remainder is judged is no part of anyone's intention, only the arithmetic of a gain, once shown, becoming evidence of what else could be shown.

## Tension

An honest gain, offered to close a gap, that will raise the very bar by which what remains unclosed is judged.
