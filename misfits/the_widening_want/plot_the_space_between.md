# Plot: The Space Between

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The expectation, once risen, keeps climbing past the condition that raised it.

## Action

At [the Gap](place_the_gap.md), [the Riser](persona_the_riser.md) from [the riser's seat](position_riser.md) through [Expecting](process_expecting.md) meets [the Widening Gap](piece_the_widening_gap.md), already open between what is held and what is now looked for.

## Stage

The Riser carried the Rising Expectation out of the Reform and into the Gap, and here the two measures, what conditions have actually delivered and what the Riser now expects them to deliver, no longer track each other. Each further sign of progress adds to the expectation faster than it adds to the condition, so the space between the had and the hoped for grows precisely as things get better, not despite it (Tocqueville; Davies). This is the shape Davies later gave a curve: expectation and gratification rising together for a stretch, then the gratification lagging while the expectation keeps its own pace, so the gap is a function of the difference between two rising lines, not of either line's height (Davies). No villain: neither the gain that opened this place nor the hope that followed it did anything wrong; the gain was real and the hope read it rightly, and the widening is simply what happens when a reasonable hope, once loosed, travels faster than the conditions that loosed it. Nobody in the Gap set the two paces; the paces are what they are.

## Tension

A gap opened by improvement itself, not by its absence, and growing as the improvement continues.
