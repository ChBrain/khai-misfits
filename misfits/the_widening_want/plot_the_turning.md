# Plot: The Turning

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The gap has gone intolerable, and the question is what, if anything, it turns into.

## Action

At [the Long View](place_the_long_view.md), [the Historian](persona_the_historian.md) from [the historian's seat](position_historian.md) on [Read the Curve](plan_read_the_curve.md) through [Reading](process_reading.md) takes up [the Unrest](piece_the_unrest.md), reading it across the cases where a long rise and a sharp reversal preceded the rising.

## Stage

The Historian looks back across the cases, France before 1789 chief among them, and finds the pattern repeating: revolt does not cluster where oppression is heaviest but where it had most recently been eased, a long stretch of rising conditions and rising hope followed by a sharp check that the risen expectation could not absorb (Tocqueville; Davies). Reading the curve, the Historian names the gap itself, the distance between what was expected and what was actually delivered, as the pressure that the discontent in the Gap had been building toward all along (Davies; Gurr). But the Historian holds the reading to its bound: grievance of this kind is common, felt in some measure almost everywhere conditions rise and then falter, while collective revolt against it is rare, so the gap cannot be read as a trigger that fires on its own (Gurr; the mobilization critique). Whether the felt gap becomes an uprising turns on organization, resources, and the political opportunity to act on the grievance, none of which the gap itself supplies (the mobilization critique). No villain: the Aggrieved presses a real grievance, the Reformer relieved in good faith at the start of it all, and the turn from discontent to unrest is mediated by forces outside anyone's grievance or intention, not fixed by either. Distinct from More to Stand Still, a backward looking, individual reset of pleasure toward a neutral baseline as a good persists, and from The Arms Race, a rivalrous outspending for rank against others that cancels itself, this misfit runs forward, a rising social expectation measured against one's own prospects over time, no rival and no rank, only a standard that climbed with the gain and then outran it.

## Tension

The gap a real pressure, documented and felt, and the outcome it points toward left unfixed.
