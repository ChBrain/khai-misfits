# Piece: The Widening Gap

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The mechanism: the space between what is had and what is now expected, growing rather than closing as conditions improve.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Gap](place_the_gap.md), carried by [Expecting](process_expecting.md), voiced by [the Riser](persona_the_riser.md): two lines drawn on the same chart, condition and expectation, both climbing, the second climbing faster, the distance between them the only number that matters.

## Load Bearing

The gap is arithmetic, not sentiment: expectation, once loosed from the belief that the old burden was inevitable, climbs on hope, while condition climbs on delivery, and hope is the faster mover (Tocqueville; Davies, "Toward a Theory of Revolution," 1962). So the distance between what is had and what is now expected widens even as both lines rise, and it widens precisely because things are getting better, not despite it. Nobody has to be worse off in any absolute sense for the gap to grow; the gap is a relation between two rising quantities, and a relation can widen while every term in it improves.

## Apparent

Reads as ingratitude, a complaint lodged from a position of plenty, evidence that the complainers have simply lost the capacity to be satisfied.
