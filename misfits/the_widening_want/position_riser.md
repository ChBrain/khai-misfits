# Position: Riser

## Taxonomy

[The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The seat of the one whose expectations rise with the gain, and marks that the rising hope is reasonable and reads the prospects rightly, and that it is the hope that opens the gap.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A gain shown, and the fair reading that a gain shown is a gain to come.
The reasonable hope that rises with the improvement, since a burden liftable is no longer accepted as fixed.
No brake on the hope, since it climbs on the memory of the improvement and past a point faster than the improvement itself.

## Orders

Expect the next, raise your hope with the gain.
Read the improvement as the promise of more, since a gain demonstrated is reasonably expected to continue and a burden shown liftable is no longer borne as inevitable, and raise your expectation as any person who saw the gain would.
Know that the expectation, once raised, climbs past the condition, that your fair hope outruns the gain and opens the gap between what you have and what you now expect, and that the widening is no fault in your wanting but hope tracking a gain and then outrunning it.

## Loses

Nothing it can name, and this is the trap.
Because its hope is reasonable and reads the prospects rightly, the seat takes no loss it can see and wants nothing it should not, and yet the hope it raises climbs faster than the condition and opens the gap that becomes the grievance, a widening no single reasonable expectation authored.
It keeps its fair hope and its honest reading, and loses no cause for blame, the one whose reasonable expectation the conditions cannot keep pace with.

## Drives

Toward the one whose fair hope opens the gap,
reading an honest gain as the promise of more, as any reasonable person would, and raising its expectation with it,
so the seat whose wanting is no greed but a right reading of its prospects is the seat from whose rising the gap is opened.
