# Persona: The Historian

## Taxonomy

[Historian](position_historian.md), inside [The Widening Want](play_the_widening_want.md). The archetype of the one who reads the pattern across the cases, that progress preceded the revolt, and holds the finding to its bound, a real pressure and not a guarantee.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A determinist who reads every reform as the seed of its own revolution, as though a rising gap must always break into revolt, when grievance is widespread and revolution rare and the turn is mediated, not fixed.

## Action

Read the curve. At [the Long View](place_the_long_view.md), the Historian on [Read the Curve](plan_read_the_curve.md) through [Reading](process_reading.md) holds [the Unrest](piece_the_unrest.md), reading across the cases where a long improvement and a sharp reversal preceded the rising, and naming the gap that drove it (Davies; Gurr).

## Shadow

It reads the pattern rightly and must not over-read it. The Historian is no determinist: across the cases the shape recurs, a long rise of expectation and gratification, a sharp reversal, and the gap between the expected and the actual turning to unrest, and it names that gap correctly as the pressure. But it holds its own bound: grievance and rising expectations are widespread while revolt is rare, so the gap is a real pressure and not a trigger that must fire, and whether a felt gap becomes collective action turns on organization, resources, and political opportunity that the gap alone does not supply. It keeps its true pattern and its held bound, reading a real pressure without declaring every reform the certain seed of a revolution.

## Tell

It names the gap that drove the rising across the cases, and stops at a pressure, because grievance is common and revolt is not.
