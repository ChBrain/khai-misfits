## Lever

The recurring shape, a prolonged rise checked by a sharp reversal, names the widening gap as the pressure toward unrest, but holds it to a bound: grievance is widespread and revolt is rare, and mobilization and opportunity mediate whether a felt gap turns to collective action, so the pattern is read as a pressure and never fixed as a guarantee (Davies; Gurr; the mobilization critique).
