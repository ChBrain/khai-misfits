# Persona: The Trainer

## Taxonomy

[Trainer](position_trainer.md). The one who must describe the work before the cohort arrives.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A course to specify, employers to consult, and openings that exist today.
Leavers who will reach the market years from now.

## Action

Reads the openings and writes the course against them.
Revises as fast as anybody can.

## Shadow

Cannot outrun a lead time, however responsive they are.
Revising the morning the market turns still leaves years of leavers already specified against the old one, so the failure is not slowness and is not fixed by speed: the pipeline is longer than the period of the thing it is aimed at, and no version of this seat has a signal about a market that does not exist yet.

## Tell

Is answerable for a forecast nobody could make and is measured on a start they do improve.
