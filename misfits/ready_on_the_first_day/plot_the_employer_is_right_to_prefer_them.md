# Plot: The Employer Is Right to Prefer Them

## Taxonomy

[Ready on the First Day](play_ready_on_the_first_day.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two candidates, one who can do the work now and one who could learn it.

## Action

[Hiring on the day](process_hiring_on_the_day.md): [the employer](persona_the_employer.md), holding [Employer](position_employer.md), fills [the opening](piece_the_opening.md).

## Stage

The specific candidate is hired, and the preference becomes the demand signal.

## Tension

The preference is sound, and by being sound it sets the specification.
Hiring for adaptability over present competence means paying to train somebody for a task that may itself move, a real cost carrying its own risk, so no reasonable employer is being asked to do otherwise. And this ordinary defensible choice, aggregated, is the entire signal the training system reads, which means the specification is set by people who were only ever answering a question about now.
