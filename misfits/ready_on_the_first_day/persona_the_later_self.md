# Persona: The Later Self

## Taxonomy

[Later Self](position_later_self.md). The same person, twenty five years on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An occupation that has moved or gone, and a skill specified against the old one.
A mortgage, dependants, and less time to retrain than at nineteen.

## Action

Looks for the next job.
Finds the match is not there.

## Shadow

Holds both signs, which is what keeps this out of every account about generations.
Nothing here is one cohort trading on another and nothing is a policy imposed by one group on a later one: the person who took the early advantage is the person who carries the late cost, and they made the decision correctly, with the information and the constraints an eighteen-year-old has.

## Tell

Is billed for a choice that was right when it was made, by nobody, at a time nobody set.
