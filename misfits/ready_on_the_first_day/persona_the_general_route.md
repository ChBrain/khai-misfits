# Persona: The General Route

## Taxonomy

[General Route](position_general_route.md). The correct alternative, and its price.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A broader education, slower to convert into a job.
Whatever the leaver has to live on while it does.

## Action

Trades a worse start for a longer reach.
Costs years of thinner employment.

## Shadow

Is the right answer and is priced out of reach of the people it would help most.
The route works, and it is bought with employment forgone at the age when a person has no savings, no reputation and no fallback, so the households that can absorb a slow start are the ones for whom the trade was least urgent, and the ones who most need the later adaptability are the ones who cannot afford the earlier gap.

## Tell

Is the remedy, sold at a price set in the years when nobody can pay it.
