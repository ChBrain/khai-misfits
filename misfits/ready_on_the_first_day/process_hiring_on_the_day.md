# Process: Hiring on the Day

## Taxonomy

[Ready on the First Day](play_ready_on_the_first_day.md). The preference that is sound and that sets the specification.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A vacancy, and two candidates who differ in how soon they can do the work.
[The Employer](persona_the_employer.md), holding [Employer](position_employer.md), with work waiting.

## Direction

Toward the candidate who can perform the task now.
Toward a filled opening and paid work.

## Lever

The cost and risk of training somebody for a task that may itself move, which makes present competence the defensible preference.

## Echo

A good hire, an early advantage that is real, and a demand signal the training system reads correctly.
