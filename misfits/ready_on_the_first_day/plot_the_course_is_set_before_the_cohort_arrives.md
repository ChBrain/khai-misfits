## Tension

The lead time survives perfect responsiveness, which is what puts the failure outside anybody's diligence.
A school that revised its course the morning the market turned would still be delivering, that year and the next and the one after, the leavers it specified before the turn. So this is not slowness and is not cured by speed. And there is no better signal to be faster about: an employer can state a vacancy precisely and cannot state the task twenty years out, not from reticence but because nobody knows.
