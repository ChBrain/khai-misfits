# Play: Ready on the First Day

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Ready on the First Day.
A young person takes the training that leads to a job, and it does: they are employable the week they leave, ahead of the ones who took the general route, and the countries that do this best have the best school-to-work transitions in the world. The thing that made them employable was that their skill matched an opening exactly. Two decades on, the task has moved and the match has not, and the same person is the one holding it.

## Arc

The bend is this: a course must be specified before the cohort that will take it arrives, and the tighter the specification the better the start and the sooner it expires.
It runs on a lead time nothing removes.
[The Specification](piece_the_specification.md) is written in [the last year of training](place_the_last_year_of_training.md) against the openings that exist when it is written, and the people it describes reach the market years later. A school that revised its course the morning the market turned would still be delivering, next year and the year after, the leavers it specified before the turn.
And the specificity is what works.
[The Leaver](persona_the_leaver.md) can do the job on Monday, which is not a small thing but the whole thing: it is the difference between earning at nineteen and not, and it is measured.
Then [The Employer Is Right to Prefer Them](plot_the_employer_is_right_to_prefer_them.md).
[The Employer](persona_the_employer.md) has [an opening](piece_the_opening.md) now and hires the person who can fill it now, which is neither short-sighted nor unkind: an employer who hired for adaptability at the cost of competence would be paying to train somebody for a task that may not be there either.
And [The Task Moves and the Skill Does Not](plot_the_task_moves_and_the_skill_does_not.md).
What technology takes first is the well-specified, routine part of a job, which is the part a course can teach precisely. So the tighter the training, the more of it sits in the part that goes, and [the closure](piece_the_closure.md) is where that becomes visible: not as a decline but as one day on which the occupation is not there.
And [The General Route](persona_the_general_route.md) costs a worse start. It is the correct answer to all of this and it is bought with two or three years of thinner employment at the age when a person has no savings, no reputation and no fallback, so it is least affordable to exactly the people the specific route serves.
And [The Later Self](persona_the_later_self.md) carries both signs. This is not one generation trading on another and not a cohort effect: it is one person, who made the right choice at eighteen with the information and the constraints of an eighteen-year-old, holding the bill at forty five.

## Stakes

Whether a person can be equipped for a working life by a system that must describe the work before the life begins.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a specification fixed ahead of the cohort, an early advantage that is real and measured, an employer right to prefer competence now, a technology that takes the best-specified part first, an alternative that is correct and costs a worse start, and one person holding both ends, and away from any account in which a school was lazy, an employer callous or a young person foolish. The production cannot fault a training system for getting leavers into work, because that is the thing it is for and the systems that do it best are the ones where the trade bites hardest. What is left to fight over is not whether the training worked, it did, but what to call an investment that pays at nineteen with the same property that fails at forty five.
