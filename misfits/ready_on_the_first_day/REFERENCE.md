# Ready on the First Day: Reference

The research warrant for Ready on the First Day.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the measured lifecycle trade between vocational and general
education, and on the lead time that fixes a course before its cohort arrives, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging vocational training as **one property with two signs, twenty five years apart, carried by one person**:
a course must be specified before the cohort that will take it arrives, against the only signal a market can
give about itself, which is the openings that exist now. The tighter that specification, the better the leaver's
start, and the early advantage is real, measured, and largest in the countries whose apprenticeship systems are
the best in the world. What delivers it is that the skill matches an opening exactly. That exactness is also
what stops matching when the task moves, and what technology takes first is the well-specified routine part of
a job, which is the part a course teaches most precisely. The play models the documented mechanics, the
specification fixed ahead of the intake, the leaver who can do the job on Monday, the employer right to prefer
them, the task that moves while the skill does not, the general alternative that is correct and costs a worse
start, and the later self who holds both ends, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a
school teaching the wrong thing.

The governing law is that tightness of training and exposure to displacement are the same property measured
twice. There is no way to buy the early advantage without buying the later exposure, because the advantage
consists of the match and the exposure consists of the match ceasing.

**The lead time survives perfect responsiveness, and that is the test that separates this from an
adaptation failure.** A school that revised its course the morning the market turned would still be delivering,
that year and the next and the one after, the leavers it specified before the turn: the pipeline is longer than
the interval over which a description of the market holds. So the failure is not slowness, is not carelessness,
and is not cured by speed. Nor is there a better signal to be faster about, since an employer can state a
vacancy precisely and cannot state the task twenty years out, not from reticence but because nobody knows.

What removes any possibility of blame from the leaver is that the decision is correct at the time it is made.
The early advantage is the difference between earning at nineteen and not, for somebody with no savings, no
reputation and nothing to fall back on, and better information at eighteen does not change the answer, because
the answer was right on the information available and on the constraints in force.

What removes it from the employer is that preferring present competence is sound. Hiring for adaptability at
the cost of doing the work now means paying to train somebody for a task that may itself move, a real cost with
its own risk, and this ordinary defensible preference, aggregated, is the whole of the demand signal the
training system reads. The specification is therefore set by people who were only ever answering a question
about the present.

And the remedy closes the case, because it is priced in the wrong years. The general route works and is bought
with employment forgone at exactly the age when a person cannot absorb it, so the households able to take a
slow start are the ones for whom later adaptability was least urgent, and the ones who most need it are the
ones for whom two thin years are not a trade but an impossibility.

It is distinct from the house's **Chasing the Price**, where production takes time so every producer answers the
current price and the synchronised response overshoots into a cycle: the reading of this candidate that
lands there, too many leavers piling into a field that was short when they enrolled, **is** the cobweb and
belongs to that misfit rather than to this one. The line is decided by removing the incumbent's engine. Take
away the synchronisation, one leaver, no cohort, no price signal, and the cobweb dissolves; this does not,
because a single person still arrives holding a specification fixed years earlier. There the casualty is a
market, oscillating; here it is a person, and nothing oscillates.

It is distinct from the house's **Right for the Last World**, where an organisation adopts the form its
environment called for, is rewarded, and holds it after the environment turns: this is the nearest misfit and
the same test separates them. There the engine is **persistence**, so an organisation that noticed the turn and
changed would escape, which is exactly what makes the failure a failure of seeing. Here noticing changes
nothing. The institution may be perfectly adaptive and still deliver into a moved world, because its output
takes twelve years to emerge, so what is load-bearing is the length of the pipeline rather than the stiffness
of the form.

It is distinct from the house's **Early and Uncounted**, where a gene favoured for an early effect is not
selected against for a late one because the cohort has thinned too far to move the tally: it is the structural
twin, from a literature sharing no vocabulary with this one, and the difference is who does the weighting.
There **nobody chooses**, and the discount on the late effect is imposed by mortality across a population.
Here a person chooses, correctly, and the discount is their own and their household's, applied to a real
present constraint, so the same individual holds both signs and there is no population in the mechanism at all.

It is distinct from the house's **Seed Corn**, where present performance is bought by consuming the capital
that would have produced future performance: there the trade is **deliberate and known**, taken by an actor who
accepts the price and could have declined it. Here nothing is consumed and no capital is spent: the early
choice is simply the right one on the information available, and the later cost is not a price anybody agreed
to pay but the second half of a property whose first half was the reason to choose.

It is distinct from the house's **Path Dependence**, where early choices raise the cost of switching until a
worse standard is locked in: there switching costs are what hold the actor, and the remedy is to lower them.
Here nothing holds anybody. Retraining is permitted, available and sometimes funded, and the difficulty is not
that the person cannot move but that what they hold has stopped being asked for, at an age with a mortgage and
less time than at nineteen.

**A disclosure about the theory underneath.** The general-versus-specific distinction that this play depends on
is Becker's, and Becker's _Human Capital_ carries the spine of the house's **Given to Everyone**, where a grant
that cannot discriminate cannot be aimed. This production does not cite that work, and rests on the empirical
lifecycle literature instead, but the two misfits sit on one theoretical floor and a reader should be told so:
there the question is what an unaimable grant can appropriate, and here it is what a person's specific skill is
worth across a working life.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the training
  system as teaching the wrong thing, the employer as short-sighted or the young person as unwise, because
  nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the course must be specified before its cohort arrives
  and no signal exists about a market that does not yet exist; the early advantage is real, measured, and the
  difference between earning at nineteen and not; preferring a candidate who can do the work now is sound,
  since training for a task that may also move carries its own cost and risk; the technology takes the routine
  part first, which is nobody's decision; and the correct alternative is priced in the years when the people
  who need it most cannot pay it. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the
  misfit is that the property which gets a leaver hired is the property that expires.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Teaching more generally is
  the right answer and is bought with thinner employment at eighteen, which is unaffordable to precisely the
  households the vocational route serves, and a system judged on placement rates cannot simply be instructed to
  do it. Making the training system more responsive does not reach the lead time, since a course revised the
  morning the market turns still delivers years of leavers specified before it. Better labour-market
  forecasting asks somebody to supply information that does not exist. Retraining at forty five is real,
  sometimes funded, and is undertaken with a mortgage, dependants and less time than at nineteen, so it is a
  price rather than a solution. And knowing the whole mechanism at eighteen does not change what an
  eighteen-year-old with no savings should do.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the lifecycle comparison, the plant-closure identification, the
  task-content framework and the dissent are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: **whether the later
  penalty generalises is genuinely contested**, findings vary by country, cohort and identification strategy,
  and the dissent cited above concludes that the observed gap is largely **selection** rather than the effect
  of the training, which the play carries as a live objection and does not settle; the play therefore stages
  the structure and never a magnitude, and makes no claim about how large the trade is in any particular
  country or trade; the task-content work is cited for the direction of technological substitution and not for
  any prediction about a named occupation; treating the choice as one decision at one age is the play's own
  simplification of systems that offer several points of entry and re-entry; and nothing here is an argument
  against apprenticeship, which the play stages as the case where the early half works best. **The play makes
  no claim that vocational training is a mistake or that anybody was mis-advised**, only that the match which
  makes a leaver employable is the match that can stop being asked for, and that both halves fall on the same
  person twenty five years apart.
