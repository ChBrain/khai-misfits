# Process: Setting the Course

## Taxonomy

[Ready on the First Day](play_ready_on_the_first_day.md). The specification, correct when written and delivered later.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A reading of the openings, and a cohort that has not started yet.

## Direction

Toward a course that places its leavers.

## Lever

The lead time between specifying a course and its leavers reaching the market, which no amount of responsiveness removes.

## Echo
