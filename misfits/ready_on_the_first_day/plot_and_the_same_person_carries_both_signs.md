# Plot: And the Same Person Carries Both Signs

## Taxonomy

[Ready on the First Day](play_ready_on_the_first_day.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A forty-five-year-old whose occupation has gone, and their own decision at eighteen.

## Action

[The Later Self](persona_the_later_self.md), in [the twentieth year](place_the_twentieth_year.md), holds what [the leaver](persona_the_leaver.md) bought.

## Stage

Both ends of the trade are held by one person.

## Tension

Nothing here is one generation trading on another, which is what closes the case against every remaining party.
The person who took the early advantage is the person who carries the late cost, and the decision behind it was made correctly, under the constraints an eighteen-year-old actually has, on information that was accurate. The trainer who set the course is retired, the employer who set the specification was describing a real vacancy, and there is no party left to whom the account could be presented. It was not a bad bargain: it was a bargain whose two halves fall due twenty five years apart.
