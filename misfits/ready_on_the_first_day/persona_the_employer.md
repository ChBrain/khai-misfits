# Persona: The Employer

## Taxonomy

[Employer](position_employer.md). The one with an opening now.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A vacancy, work waiting, and two candidates.
One who can do it on Monday and one who could learn it.

## Action

Hires the one who can do it now.
Pays them for it.

## Shadow

Is neither short-sighted nor unkind, and their preference is the whole demand signal.
Hiring for adaptability at the cost of present competence means paying to train somebody for a task that may itself move, which is a real cost carrying its own risk; and this seat's ordinary, defensible preference is what the training system reads when it sets the course, so the signal is honest and is about now.

## Tell

Wants what any competent employer wants, and by wanting it sets the specification.
