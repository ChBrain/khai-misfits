## Load Bearing

It is an honest signal and it is a description of the present, and no party in the play can supply another.
An employer can state a vacancy precisely and cannot state what the task will be in twenty years, not from reticence but because they do not know and nobody does. So the only information the training system has is accurate, current, and about the wrong moment, and asking for better forecasting asks somebody to supply a thing that does not exist.
