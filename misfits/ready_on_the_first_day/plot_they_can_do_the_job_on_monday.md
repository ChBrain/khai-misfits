## Tension

The gain is real and measured rather than promised, and it is the point of the whole system.
This is not a consolation offered to soften what follows. Vocational training delivers a measurable early-career employment advantage, largest in the countries that run apprenticeships best, which is to say the trade bites hardest exactly where the training is best done. And what delivers it is that the skill matches an opening exactly, which is the same property the rest of the play is about.
