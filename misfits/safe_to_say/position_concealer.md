# Position: Concealer

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The seat of the one who holds a private truth and professes the safe lie, sparing itself a real cost and, without meaning to, upholding the order it privately rejects.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A private preference held whole and unspoken, a view or a wish the seat genuinely holds.
A public in which honesty is dear, since saying the true thing draws a sanction, a frown or a shunning or worse.
No sight of the others' true minds, only their safe professions, so it reads the surface of lies as the feeling of the room.

## Orders

Say the safe thing, and keep the truth to yourself.
Profess the accepted view and spare yourself the sanction, since one voice would change nothing but your own fate, and weigh the certain cost of honesty against the small chance it matters, and choose silence.
Know that by giving the safe answer you add one more lie to the wall that hides how many agree with you, that reading the others' lies you believe yourself more alone than you are, and that your prudence, sensible in itself, is the very thing that keeps the order you dislike.

## Loses

Nothing it can feel now, and it forfeits the truth of its own numbers.
Because it conceals to spare itself the sanction, the seat cannot see how many conceal beside it, so it believes its view isolated when it may be the room's, and gives up the standing it would have if all the safe professions fell at once, keeping its safety at the price of its knowledge.
It keeps its peace and its good name and loses no cause for blame, a reasonable member sparing itself a real cost.

## Drives

Toward the one who keeps the truth and tells the lie,
professing the safe thing to escape the sanction, reading the others' lies as their minds, no coward in it,
so the seat that conceals to protect itself is the seat that hides from itself how many would stand with it, and the order stands on its own opponents.
