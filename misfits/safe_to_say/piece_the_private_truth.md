# Piece: The Private Truth

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The genuine preference a person actually holds: a view, a wish, a judgement, kept unspoken because saying it would cost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Heart](place_the_heart.md), in the quiet register where the view is formed and held, never yet spoken aloud.

## Load Bearing

The real preference, whole and intact, kept unsaid because honesty carries a cost (Kuran). It does not change: no revision touches the wanting itself, only its utterance is withheld. The gap that the whole misfit turns on opens here, between what is held and what will later be said.

## Apparent

Nothing shown. By definition there is no surface to read, since the private truth never crosses into view; whoever looks for it finds only its absence.

## Yearbook

Held by [The Concealer](persona_the_concealer.md) at [The Heart](place_the_heart.md), dramatized in [What I Really Think](plot_what_i_really_think.md); it is what [The Public Lie](piece_the_public_lie.md) conceals and what [The Cascade](piece_the_cascade.md) finally reveals.
