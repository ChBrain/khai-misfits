# Place: The Square

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The public arena where professions are made and read, sanctions imposed, and the false consensus stands. Hosts [The Safe Answer](plot_the_safe_answer.md), [The Cost of Honesty](plot_the_cost_of_honesty.md), [Everyone Agrees](plot_everyone_agrees.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The safe professions, offered by everyone in turn; the sanction laid on the odd voice that departs from them; and, reading the two together, an apparent agreement that looks, to anyone standing in the Square, like the room's true mind.

## Holds

[The Pressure](piece_the_pressure.md), [The Public Lie](piece_the_public_lie.md), [The False Consensus](piece_the_false_consensus.md).

## Offers

To each person present, the safety of the accepted line: say what is safe to say, here, and the sanction passes you by (Kuran).

## Withheld

Any window onto the others' true minds. The Square shows what is said, never what is held; a person standing in it can read every profession made and still not learn a single private conviction, including, in time, their own true weight against everyone else's (Katz and Allport).
