# Plot: The Cost of Honesty

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Square](place_the_square.md), a price sits on the odd voice, ready before it is even spoken: a look, a word, a colder standing, something that will fall on whoever answers wrong.

## Action

[The Enforcer](persona_the_enforcer.md), from [the enforcer's seat](position_enforcer.md), on [Make It Cost](plan_make_it_cost.md) through [Enforcing](process_enforcing.md), sets [The Pressure](piece_the_pressure.md) at [The Square](place_the_square.md): sanctioning the honest voice, and doing so, in no small part, as a concealer proving its own sincerity by punishing in another the very doubt it carries in itself.

## Stage

False enforcement makes the pressure partly endogenous, manufactured in part by the very people it binds, since a falsifier who enforces the line most loudly is often demonstrating loyalty it does not feel (Willer, Kuwabara and Macy). The room is not policed from outside by true believers alone; some of its own guards are doubters wearing the uniform.

## Tension

The pressure that binds the room is made in part by the room.
