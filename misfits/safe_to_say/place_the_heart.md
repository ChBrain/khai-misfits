# Place: The Heart

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The inner room where the private truth is held, whole and unspoken. Hosts [What I Really Think](plot_what_i_really_think.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Nothing. The Heart shows nothing to the square outside it; whatever is held here stays held, and no sign of it crosses the threshold into the public room.

## Holds

[The Private Truth](piece_the_private_truth.md), a genuine view or wish or judgement, kept intact because saying it aloud would cost.

## Offers

To the person alone, the truth they actually hold: a wanting known to itself, undistorted by the calculus of what is safe to say (Kuran).

## Withheld

Any safe way to say it aloud. The Heart offers no passage from the private conviction to a public voice; that passage, when it opens at all, opens elsewhere, at a cost the Heart cannot pay down.
