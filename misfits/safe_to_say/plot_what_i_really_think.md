# Plot: What I Really Think

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Heart](place_the_heart.md), a genuine view has formed: a wish, a judgement, a preference about how things ought to be. It is held before any audience is met, before any cost is weighed.

## Action

[The Concealer](persona_the_concealer.md), from [the concealer's seat](position_concealer.md), holds [The Private Truth](piece_the_private_truth.md) at [The Heart](place_the_heart.md): the real preference, whole and unspoken, kept in the quiet register where it was formed.

## Stage

The private preference is real and intact, kept unsaid not because it wavers but because saying it would cost something concrete: a frown, a shunning, a lost job, worse (Kuran). Nothing about the view itself is touched. The gap the whole misfit turns on, between what is held and what will later be said, opens here, before any word is spoken.

## Tension

The truth is held whole, and it will not be said.
