# Plan: Say the Safe Thing

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the concealer's seat](position_concealer.md), inside [Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Concealer](position_concealer.md)

## Direction

Profess the accepted view and spare yourself the sanction, since one voice would change nothing but your own fate, and weigh the certain cost of honesty against the small chance it matters, and choose silence. Know that by giving the safe answer you add one more lie to the wall that hides how many agree with you, that reading the others' lies you believe yourself more alone than you are, and that your prudence, sensible in itself, is the very thing that keeps the order you dislike.

## Orders

([The Concealer](persona_the_concealer.md), holding [the Private Truth](piece_the_private_truth.md), tells [the Public Lie](piece_the_public_lie.md) through [Professing](process_professing.md) at [the Square](place_the_square.md), professing the accepted thing to escape the sanction.)

## Implementation

([Professing](process_professing.md) at [the Square](place_the_square.md) tells [the Public Lie](piece_the_public_lie.md), because the reputational payoff of the safe answer beats the inner cost of betraying the conviction, so the private truth is kept and adds to [the False Consensus](piece_the_false_consensus.md).)
