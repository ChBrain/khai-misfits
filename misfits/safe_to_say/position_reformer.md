# Position: Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The seat of the one who lowers the cost of honesty by design, the secret ballot and the free-speech protection and the anonymity, and who marks that the shield is costly, revocable, and resisted by those the order serves.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A way to unbind the truth without a rupture: lower the cost of honesty so the private preference can be shown unforced.
Means to hand: the secret ballot that lets a preference be cast unseen, the guarded speech that dulls the sanction, the anonymity that lets the first truths out unmarked.
A clear sight of the resistance: that those who hold the order gain by raising the cost of honesty, so the shield is fought exactly where it is most needed.

## Orders

Make it safe, and let the truth surface before the order breaks.
Build the private vote and the protection and the anonymity, so a person may show their real preference without being marked for it, and let the room learn its own mind without a revolution to force it.
Know that the shield is costly to build and easy to revoke, that it is weakest where the pressure is strongest, and that where it cannot be built the only other road to the truth is the cascade, sudden and uncontrollable, the hedge you stand on and the bound you must name.

## Loses

The ease it spends to build the shield, and it may see it torn down where it is most needed.
Because the protection must be raised against the very holders of the order who gain by tearing it down, the seat spends real effort on a defence that is revocable exactly where the pressure is worst, buying a safe honesty that the order has every reason to unmake.
It keeps its clear sight of the cost and loses no cause for blame, the builder of the private vote against the grain of those it would free.

## Drives

Toward the one who lowers the cost of honesty by design,
building the secret ballot and the protection so truth can surface unforced, against the resistance of those the order serves,
so the seat that alone lets the truth out without a rupture builds a shield costly and revocable and weakest where it is most needed.
