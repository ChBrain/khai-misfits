## Initiated by

A public asking, put to someone who holds a private view, and honesty, in the moment, unsafe.

## Direction

From the private truth held whole toward the safe profession spoken in its place.

## Lever

The reputational payoff of saying the accepted thing outweighs the inner cost of betraying one's own conviction, so the sensible calculus resolves toward the safe answer, not the true one (Kuran).
