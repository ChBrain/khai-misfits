# Plan: Make It Safe

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reformer's seat](position_reformer.md), inside [Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reformer](position_reformer.md)

## Direction

Build the private vote and the protection and the anonymity, so a person may show their real preference without being marked for it, and let the room learn its own mind without a revolution to force it. Know that the shield is costly to build and easy to revoke, that it is weakest where the pressure is strongest, and that where it cannot be built the only other road to the truth is the cascade, sudden and uncontrollable, the hedge you stand on and the bound you must name.

## Orders

([The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md) holds [the Free Voice](piece_the_free_voice.md) through [Shielding](process_shielding.md) at [the Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), building the secret ballot and the protection and the anonymity that let the truth surface unmarked.)

## Implementation

([Shielding](process_shielding.md) at [the Threshold](place_the_threshold.md) raises [the Free Voice](piece_the_free_voice.md), because lowering the cost of honesty lets the private preference be shown without a sanction, so the truth can surface without a cascade to force it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the secret ballot and the protection and the anonymity are built, the cost of honesty lowered
- [ ] the remedy is understood to let truth surface without a rupture, the private preference shown unmarked
- [ ] the resistance is named, those who hold the order raising the cost rather than lowering it
- [ ] the bound is held, the shield revocable and weakest where most needed, the cascade the other road uncontrolled
