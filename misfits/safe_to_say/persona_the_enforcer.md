# Persona: The Enforcer

## Taxonomy

[Enforcer](position_enforcer.md), inside [Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The archetype of the pressure that makes honesty unsafe, raised not only by those the order serves but by the concealers themselves, who enforce the line on others to prove their own sincerity.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A guardian of shared values, keeping the peace. It looks like the upholder of what everyone agrees on, coming down on the odd voice that breaks the harmony, the very picture of a community defending its norms.

## Action

Raise the cost. At [the Square](place_the_square.md), on [Make It Cost](plan_make_it_cost.md) through [Enforcing](process_enforcing.md), the Enforcer sets [the Pressure](piece_the_pressure.md), sanctioning the honest voice so that the truth stays unsafe to say, and much of this enforcing comes from concealers themselves, punishing others to show their own compliance is sincere (Willer, Kuwabara and Macy).

## Shadow

Its zeal is often a concealment of its own. The fiercest enforcer is frequently one who privately dissents and enforces the harder to place itself beyond suspicion, so the pressure that binds the room is manufactured in part by the very people it binds, and the order is guarded most loudly by those who least believe in it. It takes itself for a defender of the common view and owes no cause for blame, a watchman who may be a doubter in disguise.

## Tell

It punishes the odd voice most fiercely when it fears its own doubt might show.
