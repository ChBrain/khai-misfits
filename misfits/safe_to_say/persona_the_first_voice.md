# Persona: The First Voice

## Taxonomy

[First Voice](position_first_voice.md), inside [Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The archetype of the one who speaks the truth aloud when a crack in the pressure makes it just bearable, and whose single revelation lowers the cost of the next, tipping the concealment into a cascade.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A lone dissident, reckless or brave. It looks like a single figure breaking ranks, either a fool who will pay for it or a hero who will be remembered, standing out where everyone else keeps still.

## Action

Say it out loud. At [the Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), on [Break the Silence](plan_break_the_silence.md) through [Breaking](process_breaking.md), the First Voice triggers [the Cascade](piece_the_cascade.md), speaking the hidden truth when a shock has made it just safe enough, so that the next voice finds it a little safer still, and the next, until the whole order flips at once (Kuran).

## Shadow

Its courage is real but the flood is not its doing. The First Voice does not persuade the room or move its mind; it only reveals that the mind was already there, hidden behind the safe professions, so what looks like one figure toppling an order is one figure showing that the order was hollow. Whether it is remembered as a spark or forgotten as a casualty turns on a timing it cannot control, the same words a martyr's folly one day and a revolution's opening the next.

## Tell

It says what everyone was thinking, and only afterward is it clear whether it was too early or just in time.
