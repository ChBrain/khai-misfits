# Piece: The Cascade

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The sudden tipping of hidden truth into the open when a small shock makes speaking a little safer, each revelation lowering the cost of the next.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), carried by [Breaking](process_breaking.md), the point past which concealment turns to revelation.

## Load Bearing

Because the true distribution of belief stays hidden behind a wall of safe professions, the order is brittle in a way no one, not even its opponents, can measure. A shock, often a small one, lowers the cost of the first honest word; that word lowers the cost of the next; and mass concealment can tip into mass revelation faster than anyone tracking the public record could have forecast, as in 1989 (Kuran). The whole order flips at once, and it surprises everyone, including those who brought it down.

## Apparent

A sudden revolution from nowhere, an inexplicable collapse of an order that looked, right up to the end, entirely secure.
