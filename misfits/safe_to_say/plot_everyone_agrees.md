# Plot: Everyone Agrees

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Square](place_the_square.md), the surface is read as the truth: every safe profession offered by every other person is taken, at face value, for what that person actually believes.

## Action

[The Concealer](persona_the_concealer.md), from [the concealer's seat](position_concealer.md), meets [The False Consensus](piece_the_false_consensus.md) at [The Square](place_the_square.md): reading the safe professions of others as their true feeling, and so believing itself more alone in its private view than it is, and concealing the harder for it.

## Stage

This is pluralistic ignorance: each person privately rejects the norm yet believes, wrongly, that others accept it, so an order most privately reject is upheld by the very people who reject it (Katz and Allport). Draw two distinctions here, against two different misfits. Against Not Worth Wanting: there the private wanting itself is unconsciously revised, so no gap opens between wanting and saying; here the private preference is intact and unrevised, and only its expression is falsified. Against Closing Ranks: there the team genuinely converges, each member privately coming to hold the shared view; here the inner belief still dissents, quietly, while only the outward profession conforms.

## Tension

The room agrees out loud and dissents in private, and no one can tell.
