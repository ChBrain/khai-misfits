# Position: Enforcer

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The seat of the pressure that makes honesty unsafe, raised not only by those the order serves but by the concealers themselves, who enforce the line to prove their own sincerity.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A sanction to impose, the frown or the shunning or the punishment that meets the honest voice.
A reason to enforce that is often a concealment of its own, since coming down hard on the odd voice places the enforcer beyond suspicion of sharing it.
A hold over the room that grows as more enforce, each act of enforcement raising the cost of honesty for all the rest.

## Orders

Make it cost, and let the odd voice pay.
Sanction the one who speaks the unsafe truth, so that honesty stays dear and the safe profession stays the sensible choice, and enforce the harder if your own doubt might show, to prove your compliance sincere.
Know that much of the pressure is raised by the very people it binds, that the fiercest enforcer is often a doubter in disguise, and that the order is guarded most loudly by those who least believe in it, the cost of honesty manufactured by the concealers themselves.

## Loses

Nothing it will own, and it forfeits the truth along with everyone.
Because it raises the cost of the honesty that would free it too, the seat that enforces to hide its own doubt deepens the wall that hides all doubt, so it spends its zeal to keep standing an order it may itself reject, buying its cover at the price of the truth.
It keeps its cover and its show of conviction and loses no cause for blame, a watchman who may be a doubter in disguise.

## Drives

Toward the one who raises the cost of the honest voice,
sanctioning the odd voice and enforcing the harder to hide its own doubt, taking itself for a defender,
so the seat that guards the order most loudly may believe in it least, and the pressure that binds the room is made in part by the room.
