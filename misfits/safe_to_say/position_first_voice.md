# Position: First Voice

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The seat of the one who speaks the truth aloud when a crack in the pressure makes it just bearable, whose single revelation lowers the cost of the next, tipping the concealment into a cascade.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The truth said out loud, the private thing spoken in the open where all the safe professions are made.
A moment not of its choosing, a crack in the pressure that makes speaking just bearable and its timing decisive.
No power to persuade, only to reveal, since it does not change the room's mind but shows the mind the room already hid.

## Orders

Break the silence, and say what everyone was thinking.
Speak the hidden truth when the pressure cracks, and by speaking make it a little safer for the next voice, and the next, so that revelation draws revelation until the whole order flips at once.
Know that you do not move the room but uncover it, that what looks like one figure toppling an order is one figure showing it was hollow, and that whether you are a spark or a casualty turns on a timing you cannot control.

## Loses

Perhaps everything, if the timing is wrong, and it cannot know the timing in advance.
Because its words are the same whether too early or just in time, the seat stakes its fate on a crack it did not make and cannot measure, so the identical courage is a martyr's folly one day and a revolution's opening the next, and it forfeits the safety the concealers keep for a chance it cannot reckon.
It keeps its honesty and its nerve and loses no cause for blame, the one who said the thing before it was safe to know if it was safe.

## Drives

Toward the one who speaks the truth when the pressure cracks,
revealing the hidden mind and lowering the cost for the next, not persuading but uncovering,
so the seat that breaks the silence topples nothing but shows the order was already hollow, its fate turning on a timing no one controls.
