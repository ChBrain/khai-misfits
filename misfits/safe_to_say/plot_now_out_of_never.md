# Plot: Now Out of Never

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), a crack opens in the pressure: some small event, unplanned and unbilled, makes the cost of speaking the true thing fall, just slightly, for whoever speaks next.

## Action

[The First Voice](persona_the_first_voice.md), from [the first voice's seat](position_first_voice.md), on [Break the Silence](plan_break_the_silence.md) through [Breaking](process_breaking.md), triggers [The Cascade](piece_the_cascade.md) at [The Threshold](place_the_threshold.md): one revelation lowering the cost of the next, drawing out a further voice and then another, until the order flips all at once.

## Stage

Because the true distribution of private feeling stayed hidden behind the wall of safe professions, the tipping point could not be seen coming and its timing cannot be foreseen even after the fact is understood. The revolutions of 1989 surprised a world that had read the public record and believed it, since the public record was itself the lie the cascade then unravelled (Kuran).

## Tension

The truth comes out of never, all at once, surprising everyone.
