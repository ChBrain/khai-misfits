# Persona: The Concealer

## Taxonomy

[Concealer](position_concealer.md), inside [Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The archetype of the one who holds a private truth and professes the safe lie, sparing themselves a real cost and, without meaning to, upholding the order they privately reject.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A reasonable person, going along to get along. It looks like ordinary prudence, someone keeping their head down and their opinions mild, the very picture of a sensible member of the room.

## Action

Keep the truth, tell the lie. At [the Heart](place_the_heart.md), the Concealer holds [the Private Truth](piece_the_private_truth.md), then at [the Square](place_the_square.md), on [Say the Safe Thing](plan_say_the_safe_thing.md) through [Professing](process_professing.md), tells [the Public Lie](piece_the_public_lie.md) to escape the sanction, and meets [the False Consensus](piece_the_false_consensus.md), reading the others' lies as truth and believing itself more alone than it is (Kuran; Katz and Allport).

## Shadow

Its prudence is rational and it is the whole of the trap. The Concealer spares itself a real cost that honesty would carry, and one voice would change nothing but its own fate, so the safe answer is the sensible move; yet by giving it, the Concealer adds one more safe profession to the wall that hides from everyone, itself included, how many privately agree. It means no harm and props up no order on purpose, and it owes no cause for blame.

## Tell

It says the accepted thing in the square and the true thing only at home, and cannot tell how many do the same.
