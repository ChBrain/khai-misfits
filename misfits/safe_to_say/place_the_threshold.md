# Place: The Threshold

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The tipping point where concealment turns to revelation, and where a reformer's protections would lower the cost of honesty. Hosts [Now Out of Never](plot_now_out_of_never.md), [Room to Speak](plot_room_to_speak.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The crack in the pressure where one voice first speaks; the flood that follows it, revelation drawing out revelation; and, standing apart from the flood, the private vote and the protection that let a truth surface without a rupture at all.

## Holds

[The Cascade](piece_the_cascade.md), [The Free Voice](piece_the_free_voice.md).

## Offers

To whoever would free the truth, one of two things: the moment, a shock small enough to go unremarked until it is not (Kuran), or the means, a secret ballot or a shield that lowers the cost of honesty before any shock arrives.

## Withheld

Any control over the cascade's timing. The Threshold can be crossed, but not scheduled; the shock that tips it is genuinely unforeseeable, explained after the fact and not predicted before it, so no one standing here can say when, only that it can (Kuran).
