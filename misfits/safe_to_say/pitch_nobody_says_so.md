# Pitch: Nobody Says So

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that where the truth is unsafe to say, each professes the safe thing, so a view most privately hold is one nobody says aloud.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Measured, careful, sober. A person weighing their words in public and saying the accepted thing, and the caution is understood, since honesty here costs a frown, a shunning, a job, a cell. Nothing on the surface but sensible prudence.

## Undertow

The private truth is kept and a public lie is told, because the reputational payoff of the safe answer beats the inner cost of the lie (Kuran). And each, seeing only the safe professions of others, believes their view more isolated than it is, conceals the harder, and some enforce the line to prove their sincerity (Katz and Allport; Willer, Kuwabara and Macy), so an order most reject is upheld by the very people who reject it.

## Nerve

That the concealment each chooses to protect themselves is exactly what hides from them how many would stand with them, so the order stands on its own opponents, unseen, until a small shock tips the silence into a cascade that surprises everyone. No one is a coward; one voice would change nothing but its owner's fate.

## Echo

Everyone thinks it and nobody says it, so a lie all share holds up an order all reject, until it doesn't.
