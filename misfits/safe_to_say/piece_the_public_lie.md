# Piece: The Public Lie

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The falsified public stance professed to escape the pressure, the gap between the private truth still held and the safe thing said aloud.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Square](place_the_square.md), carried by [Professing](process_professing.md), where the safe thing gets said out loud.

## Load Bearing

The deliberate misrepresentation of one's preference to escape the sanction, the reputational payoff beating the inner cost of the lie (Kuran). This differs from an information cascade, a different misfit: there the public actions are honest, a rational inference from others' visible choices, and the failure sits in the inference. Here the public stance is a deliberate lie: the true preference is actively hidden, and what drives the hiding is social fear, not Bayesian updating on what others chose.

## Apparent

Sensible prudence, going along to get along; the ordinary tact of not making a scene over a view that will change nothing anyway.
