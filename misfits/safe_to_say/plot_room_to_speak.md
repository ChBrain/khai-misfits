# Plot: Room to Speak

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), the cost of honesty is weighed once more against the cost of the lie, this time by someone trying to move the weighing itself rather than to answer it.

## Action

[The Reformer](persona_the_reformer.md), from [the reformer's seat](position_reformer.md), on [Make It Safe](plan_make_it_safe.md) through [Shielding](process_shielding.md), holds [The Free Voice](piece_the_free_voice.md) at [The Threshold](place_the_threshold.md): the secret ballot, the free-speech protection, the anonymity that together lower the cost of saying the true thing, so it can surface unforced.

## Stage

Lowering the cost of honesty lets the private preference be shown without forcing a rupture, no small shock required and no cascade risked (Kuran). Hold the bound here as well, in the same breath as the remedy. The shield is costly and revocable, and it is resisted precisely by those the standing order serves, so it is weakest exactly where the pressure is strongest and the need for it greatest. Not every conformity witnessed elsewhere is a falsification, since a good measure of agreement is genuine and needs no unmasking. And the cascade, the other road to the truth, remains sudden and uncontrollable, as able to install a new lie as to end the old one, so it is no clean substitute for the shield (Kuran).

## Tension

The way out is resisted exactly where it is most needed.
