# Persona: The Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Reformer](position_reformer.md), inside [Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The archetype of the one who lowers the cost of honesty by design, the secret ballot and the free-speech protection and the anonymity, and who marks that the shield is costly, revocable, and resisted by those the order serves.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A builder of fair institutions, ahead of the rupture. It looks like the prudent architect who sets up the private vote and the guarded speech, the calm foresight that would let a people speak their minds without a revolution to do it.

## Action

Lower the cost. At [the Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), on [Make It Safe](plan_make_it_safe.md) through [Shielding](process_shielding.md), the Reformer holds [the Free Voice](piece_the_free_voice.md), building the secret ballot and the protection and the anonymity that let the private truth be shown without its owner being marked, so the truth can surface without a cascade to force it.

## Shadow

Its shield asks a price and can be torn down. The protection that lets truth surface is costly to build and easy to revoke, and those who hold the order have every reason to raise the cost of honesty rather than lower it, so the shield is weakest exactly where the pressure is strongest and most needed. It knows the hedge it stands on and claims no certain cure, only a way of letting the truth out before the order breaks under it.

## Tell

It builds the private vote so the truth need not wait for a revolution to be safe to tell.
