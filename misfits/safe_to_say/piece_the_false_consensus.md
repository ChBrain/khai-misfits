# Piece: The False Consensus

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The surface of safe professions each reads off the others as their true feeling, so each believes their own view more isolated than it is and conceals the harder.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Square](place_the_square.md), met by [The Concealer](persona_the_concealer.md) in every face that seems, wrongly, settled.

## Load Bearing

Pluralistic ignorance: each person privately rejects a norm yet believes the others accept it, so the order is upheld by its own opponents (Katz and Allport). This differs from not worth wanting, a different misfit, where the wanting itself is unconsciously revised and there is no gap between the inner want and the outer report; here the private preference stays intact, unrevised, and only its expression is falsified, a conscious gap the person feels every time they cross it. It differs also from closing ranks, a different misfit, where a team genuinely converges and comes to privately hold the shared view; here the inner belief goes on dissenting while only the outer profession conforms, so the room's apparent unity is made entirely of people who each think they are the exception.

## Apparent

A room in genuine and settled agreement, nothing left to argue over.
