# Piece: The Free Voice

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md). The secret ballot, the free-speech protection, and the anonymity that lower the cost of honesty so the private truth can surface without a rupture.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Threshold](place_the_threshold.md), carried by [Shielding](process_shielding.md), the point where honesty is made a little safer to attempt.

## Load Bearing

Lowering the cost of honesty lets the private preference be shown unmarked, so the truth can surface without a cascade to force it. But the shield is costly to build and easy to revoke, and it is resisted, since those who hold the order gain from raising the cost of honesty rather than lowering it, so the shield is weakest exactly where the pressure is strongest. It carries the bound on the whole account: not every public conformity is a falsification, since much agreement is genuine or genuinely persuaded, and one cannot read every conformist as a secret dissident; the cascade's timing is genuinely unpredictable, and the theory explains the surprise after the fact more than it forecasts it; and private preferences are, by their nature, hidden, so the account rests partly on inference rather than direct observation (Kuran).

## Apparent

Needless institutional fuss, a private vote where an open show of hands would seem to do just as well.
