## Direction

Speak the hidden truth when the pressure cracks, and by speaking make it a little safer for the next voice, and the next, so that revelation draws revelation until the whole order flips at once. Know that you do not move the room but uncover it, that what looks like one figure toppling an order is one figure showing it was hollow, and that whether you are a spark or a casualty turns on a timing you cannot control.
