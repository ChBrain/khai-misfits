# Plot: The Safe Answer

## Taxonomy

[Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Square](place_the_square.md), the public asks, directly or by the mere fact of being present to hear an answer. Honesty is not safe here: the accepted line is known, and departing from it carries a cost.

## Action

[The Concealer](persona_the_concealer.md), from [the concealer's seat](position_concealer.md), on [Say the Safe Thing](plan_say_the_safe_thing.md) through [Professing](process_professing.md), tells [The Public Lie](piece_the_public_lie.md) at [The Square](place_the_square.md): professing the accepted thing, not because it is believed but because professing it escapes the sanction that the true answer would draw.

## Stage

The reputational payoff of the safe answer outweighs the inner cost of the lie, and the calculus is not a failure of nerve but an ordinary weighing that comes out for silence (Kuran). Set this beside a different misfit, Information Cascade: there, the public actions are honest, a rational inference drawn from others' visible choices, no deception in it anywhere. Here the public stance is a deliberate lie, the true preference hidden on purpose, driven by social fear rather than by any updating on evidence.

## Tension

The public stance is chosen to be safe, not true.
