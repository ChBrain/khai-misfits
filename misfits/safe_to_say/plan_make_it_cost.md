# Plan: Make It Cost

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the enforcer's seat](position_enforcer.md), inside [Safe to Say](play_safe_to_say.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Enforcer](position_enforcer.md)

## Direction

Sanction the one who speaks the unsafe truth, so that honesty stays dear and the safe profession stays the sensible choice, and enforce the harder if your own doubt might show, to prove your compliance sincere. Know that much of the pressure is raised by the very people it binds, that the fiercest enforcer is often a doubter in disguise, and that the order is guarded most loudly by those who least believe in it.

## Orders

([The Enforcer](persona_the_enforcer.md) sets [the Pressure](piece_the_pressure.md) through [Enforcing](process_enforcing.md) at [the Square](place_the_square.md), sanctioning the honest voice so the truth stays unsafe to say.)

## Implementation

([Enforcing](process_enforcing.md) at [the Square](place_the_square.md) raises [the Pressure](piece_the_pressure.md), because concealers punish others to prove their own compliance sincere, so the cost of honesty is manufactured in part by those it binds.)

## Targets

- [ ] the honest voice is sanctioned, honesty kept dear and the safe answer the sensible choice
- [ ] it is understood that much enforcement comes from concealers proving their own sincerity
- [ ] the fiercest enforcer is seen as often a doubter in disguise, guarding an order it may reject
- [ ] the pressure is owned as partly endogenous, made by the very room it binds, no single villain
