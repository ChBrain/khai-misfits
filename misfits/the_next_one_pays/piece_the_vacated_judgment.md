# Piece: The Vacated Judgment

## Taxonomy

[The Reports](place_the_reports.md). The ruling already made, and the attempt to buy it back out of the books.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [the reports](place_the_reports.md), where the sorting arrives too late and has to be undone rather than avoided.

## Load Bearing

It is the one place the structure was named out loud and refused.
When a losing party settles on appeal and asks the court to wipe the judgment below, it is asking to buy a precedent out of existence after the fact, and the Supreme Court held in 1994 that settlement does not justify it: judicial precedents being presumptively correct and valuable to the legal community as a whole, and not merely the property of private litigants.

## Apparent

A routine motion, agreed by both sides, in a matter that is over.
Nobody objecting, nothing contested, and the court refusing anyway.

## Yearbook

Blocked at the top of the process, and answered from the bottom.
A judgment that does not exist cannot be vacated, so the refusal does not stop the sorting: it moves it earlier, to the cases that end before anyone has written anything down, where no doctrine reaches and no motion is ever filed.
