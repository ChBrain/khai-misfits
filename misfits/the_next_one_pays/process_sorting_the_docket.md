# Process: Sorting the Docket

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md). Cases separated by what a judgment in them would do to the law.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A portfolio of similar matters, assessed together.
[The One Who Comes Back](persona_the_one_who_comes_back.md), reading them for their effect on the rule rather than for their value.

## Direction

Toward judgment in the cases where the facts are helpful and the law may move the payer's way.
Away from judgment in the cases where it may not, which are ended with [the number that ends it](piece_the_number_that_ends_it.md).

## Lever

The right to settle, which belongs absolutely to the parties and is exercised by both of them in every case.

## Echo

A published record composed of the disputes one side was content to risk, with every individual sorting decision lawful, rational and open.
