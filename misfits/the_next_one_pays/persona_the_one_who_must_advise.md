# Persona: The One Who Must Advise

## Taxonomy

[One Who Must Advise](position_one_who_must_advise.md). Counsel for the party who is here once, bound to that party alone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The lawyer across the table from the offer, competent, experienced, and entirely aware of what a judgment in this case would be worth to everyone else.

## Action

Values the claim, weighs the offer against the likely verdict and the cost of getting there, and recommends acceptance when acceptance is better.
Says so plainly, and records the advice.

## Shadow

Is the mechanism, and is required to be.
The duty runs to this client and stops there, so counsel who advised refusing a good offer in order to obtain a published ruling for future strangers would be spending the client's case on people who are not the client, which is the one thing the obligation forbids: the sorting is therefore not evaded by professional ethics but carried out by them, correctly, in every case.

## Tell

Gives advice that would be negligent to withhold, and by giving it removes the case from the law.
