# Plot: Nothing to Decide

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The notice of discontinuance, the hearing vacated, the list clear a fortnight early.

## Action

[The One at the Bench](persona_the_one_at_the_bench.md), holding [One at the Bench](position_one_at_the_bench.md), releases the date and moves on. The court cannot reach for a dispute that has ended, and enters nothing in [the reports](place_the_reports.md), because there is nothing to enter.

## Stage

The remaining cases are decided honestly and on their facts, and every judgment is correct.

## Tension

The absence leaves no shape, and an absence with no shape cannot be corrected for.
There is no volume of the cases that settled, no index of the claims withdrawn on good terms, and no mark anywhere showing where one would have gone: the sorted-out matters are not entries recorded as missing, they are nothing at all. So a court auditing its own record finds every decision in it sound, which it is, and possesses no instrument that could show it the population it was never given.
