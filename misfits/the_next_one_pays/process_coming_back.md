# Process: Coming Back

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md). The accumulation that turns a hundred separate cases into one object.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Recurrence: the same kind of claim, arriving again, at a party who has handled it before.
[The One Who Comes Back](persona_the_one_who_comes_back.md), holding [One Who Comes Back](position_one_who_comes_back.md).

## Direction

Toward expertise, advance intelligence on how these matters run, established relations with the institutions, and credibility in what it says it will and will not fight.
Toward a view of the docket as a portfolio rather than a queue.

## Lever

Frequency, which nothing forbids and nobody grants: the advantage is simply what being here often turns into.

## Echo

A party able to spend more on a case than the case is worth, because it is no longer looking at the case.
