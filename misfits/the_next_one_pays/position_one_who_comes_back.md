# Position: One Who Comes Back

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md). The seat that plays for the rule, lawfully.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A hundred matters shaped alike, counsel who have seen them all, and a view of the docket as a single object.
The capacity to spend more on one case than the case is worth.

## Orders

Manage the aggregate.
Let the helpful cases run to judgment, and end the harmful ones before there is a judgment to have.

## Loses

Money, deliberately, in the cases it most expects to lose.
The premium is a real cost, paid out of its own pocket to the party best placed to hurt it, and it pays willingly because a bad ruling is dearer than a good settlement: nothing here is concealed, nothing is against the rules, and no court is misled at any point.

## Drives

Toward a body of law composed of the cases it was content to risk, which is not a thing it asks for and is exactly what its ordinary, defensible conduct produces.
