# Persona: The One Who Came Once

## Taxonomy

[One Who Came Once](position_one_who_came_once.md). The party with a single case, who needs a number and not a rule.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person, or a small business, in the only serious dispute they will ever have.
The sum in question is the largest they will argue over in their lives, and the year it is happening in is the worst one they have had.

## Action

Brings the claim, instructs counsel, and takes the best outcome available to them.
Accepts the offer when the offer is better than the likely verdict, which is the correct thing to do with it.

## Shadow

Has no use for the rule and cannot be asked to buy one.
Whatever a judgment here would settle for others is worth nothing to a party who will never be back, so refusing a good settlement in order to build law for strangers would be spending the worst year of their life on people they will never meet: the interest they lack is not a failing, it is the accurate position of somebody who is here once.

## Tell

Comes out of it better than a verdict would have left them, which is what makes the outcome impossible to object to.
