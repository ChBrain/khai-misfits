# Plot: Nobody to Put It Back

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The structure named at last, and the three answers on the table: refuse to erase judgments, forbid the secrecy, or give the party who is here once a representative who will be back.

## Action

[Keep it on the record](plan_keep_it_on_the_record.md): [The One at the Bench](persona_the_one_at_the_bench.md) holds that [the vacated judgment](piece_the_vacated_judgment.md) is not the parties' to bargain away, precedent being valuable to the legal community as a whole and not merely the property of private litigants.

## Stage

The erasure is blocked at the top of the process. The sorting moves to the bottom of it.

## Tension

Every answer charges somebody, and the last one charges the person it was meant to protect.
A judgment that was never written cannot be vacated, so the doctrine reaches the cases that ended late and not the ones that ended early, which is most of them. Forbidding [the confidentiality clause](piece_the_confidentiality_clause.md) removes a term the claimant was paid for, so the public good comes out of the award of the person least able to fund it. And giving the one-shotter an organised representative works by manufacturing a second repeat player, one with its own stake in the rule and its own reason to trade a particular client's case for it: the asymmetry corrected by installing another instance of it, facing the other way.
