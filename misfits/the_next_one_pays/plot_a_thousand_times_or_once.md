# Plot: A Thousand Times, or Once

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The same dispute, approached from two sides. One party is here for the only time in its life. The other will be here again next month, in a hundred matters shaped like this one.

## Action

[Coming back](process_coming_back.md): [The One Who Comes Back](persona_the_one_who_comes_back.md) takes [One Who Comes Back](position_one_who_comes_back.md), where frequency has become expertise, intelligence and a portfolio view, while [The One Who Came Once](persona_the_one_who_came_once.md) takes [One Who Came Once](position_one_who_came_once.md), holding a claim of a size it will never see again.

## Stage

Both wants are stated and both are legitimate: a number to live on, and the rule the number leaves behind.

## Tension

They are not the same want, and things that are not the same can be traded.
The party who needs the rule can buy out the party who needs the number, and can overpay to do it, because one case is cheap against a hundred and the rule is not. Nothing improper has been proposed yet, and nothing needs to be: the whole mechanism is already present in the fact that one side will be back and the other will not, which nobody arranged and nobody can be asked to give up.
