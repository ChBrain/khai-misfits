# Persona: The One Who Comes Back

## Taxonomy

[One Who Comes Back](position_one_who_comes_back.md). The party who will be here again, and therefore plays for the rule.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An insurer, an employer, a lender, an agency: any party holding a hundred matters shaped alike, with counsel who have seen this claim many times before.
Manages exposure in aggregate, because exposure arrives in aggregate.

## Action

Reads the docket as one thing rather than as a hundred things.
Lets the cases go to judgment where a judgment would help, and ends the others by paying more than they are worth, which is a cost it accepts willingly.

## Shadow

Does nothing wrong at any point, and that is the difficulty.
The premium is paid to a person with a strong claim, nothing is hidden from them, no rule is broken and no court is misled: the sorting is lawful, rational and open, and it is performed by the only party in the room with any reason to perform it, so what it produces cannot be reached by asking who behaved badly.

## Tell

Is generous exactly where the law is against it, so its worst cases are the ones its opponents remember most fondly.
