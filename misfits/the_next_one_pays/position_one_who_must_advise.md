# Position: One Who Must Advise

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md). The seat whose duty runs to one client and stops.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A valuation of the claim, a reading of the odds, and a professional obligation with a boundary drawn around a single person.
Full knowledge of what a judgment here would be worth to everybody else.

## Orders

Advise this client, in this client's interest.
Recommend the offer when the offer beats the likely verdict.

## Loses

The only lever that could have kept the case alive, and loses it to its own correctness.
Recommending refusal in order to obtain a ruling for future strangers would be spending this client's case on people who are not this client, which the obligation forbids: so the seat that could in principle refuse the trade is the seat least free to, and the sorting is performed by professional duty rather than in spite of it.

## Drives

Toward advice that would be negligent to withhold, which is the same act as removing the case from the law.
