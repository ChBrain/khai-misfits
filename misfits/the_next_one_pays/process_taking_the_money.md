# Process: Taking the Money

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md). The case ending well, on advice, for everyone present.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An offer above the likely verdict, in [the room where it settles](place_the_room_where_it_settles.md).
[The One Who Must Advise](persona_the_one_who_must_advise.md), obliged to recommend it, and [The One Who Came Once](persona_the_one_who_came_once.md), right to accept.

## Direction

Toward acceptance, signature, [the confidentiality clause](piece_the_confidentiality_clause.md), and withdrawal.
Toward a claimant better off than a judgment would have left them, sooner and without the risk.

## Lever

Professional duty, running to this client and stopping there, which makes the recommendation compulsory rather than optional.

## Echo

A matter concluded properly, an obligation discharged properly, and a rule not made, the three being one act.
