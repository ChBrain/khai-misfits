# Plan: Win the Rule

## Taxonomy

A **portfolio scheme**: the intent of [The One Who Comes Back](persona_the_one_who_comes_back.md), inside [The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Comes Back](persona_the_one_who_comes_back.md)

## Direction

Manage the aggregate rather than the instance. Spend tangible outcomes to buy the rule, since the rule governs the hundred matters behind this one and the tangible outcome governs only this one.

## Orders

(Read the docket as a single object; let the helpful cases run to judgment; end the harmful ones with [the number that ends it](piece_the_number_that_ends_it.md), set above what a verdict would give; take [the confidentiality clause](piece_the_confidentiality_clause.md) as part of the bargain.)

## Implementation

([The Room Where It Settles](place_the_room_where_it_settles.md); the [coming back](process_coming_back.md) and [sorting the docket](process_sorting_the_docket.md) processes; a right to settle that belongs absolutely to the parties and is exercised by both of them every time.)

## Targets

- [ ] the exposure is priced across the portfolio, not the case
- [ ] the cases that would move the law the wrong way end before judgment
- [ ] the premium is paid willingly, in full, to a party with a strong claim
- [ ] nothing is concealed from that party, no rule is broken, and no court is misled at any point
