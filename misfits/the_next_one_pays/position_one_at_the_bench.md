# Position: One at the Bench

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md). The seat that makes the law and cannot choose its material.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The power to decide, to reason, and to have the reasons recorded and followed.
A list, which other people fill.

## Orders

Decide what is in front of you, on its facts.
Do not reach for what is not.

## Loses

The population, before it ever sees it.
The seat cannot take up a dispute that has ended, cannot rule where there is no live controversy, and keeps no register of the matters resolved before it saw them, so the cases sorted out of its path are not recorded anywhere as absent: a court reviewing its own reports finds every decision in them correct, and has no instrument that could show it what it was never given.

## Drives

Toward an honest record of everything that arrived, which is a different thing from a record of what was brought, and reads identically.
