# Plan: Keep It on the Record

## Taxonomy

A **correction**: the intent of [The One at the Bench](persona_the_one_at_the_bench.md), inside [The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md), once the structure is named.

## Owner

- Owner: [The One at the Bench](persona_the_one_at_the_bench.md)

## Direction

Hold that a precedent is not the parties' to bargain away. Refuse to erase a judgment because the losing side later settled, and where the law allows it, refuse the secrecy that keeps the sorting from being seen.

## Orders

(Settlement after judgment does not justify vacatur, since the losing party has voluntarily forfeited its remedy; [the vacated judgment](piece_the_vacated_judgment.md) stays in [the reports](place_the_reports.md); statutes restrict [the confidentiality clause](piece_the_confidentiality_clause.md) for particular harms; organised representation is funded for parties who are here once.)

## Implementation

([The Reports](place_the_reports.md), which keep what they are given; a doctrine that reaches the end of the process and not the start of it; and the [arriving after](process_arriving_after.md) process, which the correction is meant to reach and mostly cannot.)

## Targets

- [ ] a ruling already made is not bought back out of the books
- [ ] the price is accepted: the sorting moves earlier, to the cases that end before anything is written, where no doctrine reaches and no motion is filed
- [ ] the price is accepted: forbidding the secrecy takes its value off the claimant's award, so the public good is funded by the person least able to fund it
- [ ] the price is accepted: giving the one-shotter an organised representative creates a second repeat player, with its own stake in the rule and its own reason to trade a client's case for it
