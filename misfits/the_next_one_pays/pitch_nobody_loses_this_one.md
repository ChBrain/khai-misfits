# Pitch: Nobody Loses This One

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Procedural and entirely correct. An offer made in good faith, counsel discharging its duty, a client advised properly and paid more than a verdict would have given them, a matter withdrawn, a list cleared.
Everything lawful, everything documented between the parties, everything defensible in any forum that asks.

## Undertow

Under the correctness, a body of law being assembled from a population one side selected.
The cases that would have moved it are not disputed, not lost and not suppressed. They are bought, at a premium, from people who were right to take the money.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a room in which every person came out ahead, and the bill went to someone who was not born into the dispute yet.

## Echo

Afterward: a stranger with the same complaint, meeting a rule made out of the cases that were allowed to reach a judge, unable to name the transaction that shaped it, and unable to find it, because a case that settles leaves nothing behind to find.
