# Plot: Reading the File

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A docket of similar matters, opened together rather than one at a time, by counsel who have seen all of them before.

## Action

[Sorting the docket](process_sorting_the_docket.md): [The One Who Comes Back](persona_the_one_who_comes_back.md) scores each case for what a judgment in it would do to the law, and separates the ones that can be allowed to run from the ones that cannot.

## Stage

The cases are ranked by their effect on the rule rather than by their merits or their value. Nothing is done to any of them yet.

## Tension

The sorting is lawful, rational, open, and performed by the only party in the room with any reason to perform it.
No court is misled, no claimant is deceived, no rule of procedure is bent: a party assessing its own exposure across its own portfolio is doing the thing every advisor would tell it to do. The output is a list of the disputes that will be permitted to reach a judge, which is not a decision anyone has the standing to object to, and is nevertheless the moment the record is chosen.
