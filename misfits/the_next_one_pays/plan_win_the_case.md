# Plan: Win the Case

## Taxonomy

A **professional obligation**: the intent of [The One Who Must Advise](persona_the_one_who_must_advise.md), inside [The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Must Advise](persona_the_one_who_must_advise.md)

## Direction

Obtain the best available outcome for this client, and for no one else. Value the claim, weigh the offer against the likely verdict and the cost and risk of reaching it, and act on the answer.

## Orders

(Assess the claim; compare [the number that ends it](piece_the_number_that_ends_it.md) against the probable judgment; recommend acceptance when acceptance is better; record the advice; conclude the matter in [the room where it settles](place_the_room_where_it_settles.md).)

## Implementation

([The Room Where It Settles](place_the_room_where_it_settles.md); the [taking the money](process_taking_the_money.md) process; a duty with a boundary drawn around one person, which is exactly where the profession draws it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the claim is valued competently and the odds read honestly
- [ ] the better of the two outcomes is identified and recommended
- [ ] the client is advised in the client's interest, and only the client's
- [ ] nothing is weighed on behalf of parties who are not the client, because weighing it would be the breach
