# Plot: A Very Good Offer

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A case from the wrong side of the sort: strong facts, a claimant who would probably win, and a judgment that would be reported.

## Action

[Taking the money](process_taking_the_money.md): [the number that ends it](piece_the_number_that_ends_it.md) is put on the table in [the room where it settles](place_the_room_where_it_settles.md), above what a verdict would likely give. [The One Who Must Advise](persona_the_one_who_must_advise.md), holding [One Who Must Advise](position_one_who_must_advise.md), is obliged to recommend it, and [The One Who Came Once](persona_the_one_who_came_once.md) accepts, correctly.

## Stage

The matter is withdrawn, [the confidentiality clause](piece_the_confidentiality_clause.md) signed, and the claimant leaves better off than winning would have left them.

## Tension

The removal is performed by generosity and completed by professional duty, and that is the whole of the misfit.
The offer has to be above the verdict or it would be refused, so the only cases that leave the law are the ones where the claimant is made better off. Counsel who advised refusal in order to obtain a ruling for future strangers would be spending this client's case on people who are not the client, which the obligation forbids. Every person in the room acts correctly, each is better off for it, and the strongest claim of the hundred is the one that never reaches a judge.
