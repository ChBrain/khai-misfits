# Piece: The Number That Ends It

## Taxonomy

[The Room Where It Settles](place_the_room_where_it_settles.md). The offer set above the verdict, which is what makes it work.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the table in [the room where it settles](place_the_room_where_it_settles.md), between counsel, in the cases the law is most likely to go against the payer.

## Load Bearing

It is the instrument, and it is generous.
A number below the likely verdict would be refused and the case would run to judgment, so the only offers that remove a case from the law are the ones that leave the claimant better off than winning: the piece works by benefiting the person it removes, which is why nothing about its operation can be objected to by anyone present.

## Apparent

A settlement figure, arrived at by ordinary valuation and ordinary bargaining, and accepted on advice.
Unremarkable, and slightly high.

## Yearbook

Set from the aggregate rather than from the case: priced against what a judgment would cost across a hundred matters, not against what this one is worth.
Rises with the strength of the claim, so the strongest claims are the ones that never reach a judge.
