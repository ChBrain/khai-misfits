# Persona: The One at the Bench

## Taxonomy

[One at the Bench](position_one_at_the_bench.md). The court, which can only decide what arrives and stays.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A judge, and behind the judge the whole apparatus of reported decisions: the only instrument in the system that can make a rule, and the only participant with no way of choosing what to make one about.

## Action

Decides the cases that reach judgment, honestly and on their facts, and writes the reasons down.
Clears the list when a matter is withdrawn, which is the proper handling of a matter that is withdrawn.

## Shadow

Cannot reach for a case and cannot rule on a dispute that has ended.
Holds no register of the claims resolved before it saw them, so the cases removed from its path leave no gap in its record: they are not entries marked absent, they are nothing at all, and a court reading its own reports has no way to notice a population that was sorted before it arrived.

## Tell

Produces a body of correct decisions that is, taken together, a selected one, without a single incorrect decision in it.
