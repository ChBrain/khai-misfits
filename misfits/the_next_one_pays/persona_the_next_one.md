# Persona: The Next One

## Taxonomy

[Next One](position_next_one.md). The party who arrives afterward, into the law the settling left behind.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A stranger, years later, with the same complaint as the first one and none of their luck.
Has no connection to the earlier party, no knowledge of them, and no way of acquiring either.

## Action

Brings the claim, and is told what the law is.
Is advised correctly, on authorities that are correctly stated.

## Shadow

Bears the cost of a transaction they were not party to and cannot locate.
The rule that governs them was shaped by which cases were allowed to reach a judge, the decisive ones having been ended by agreement between two people who both did well out of it, and a settled case has no judgment, no reasons and often no public record at all, so there is nothing for this party to point at, appeal from, or even read.

## Tell

Is nobody's victim: no one in the earlier room acted against them, because they were not in it and could not have been.
