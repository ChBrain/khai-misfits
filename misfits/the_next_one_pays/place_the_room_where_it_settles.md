# Place: The Room Where It Settles

## Taxonomy

[The Next One Pays](play_the_next_one_pays.md). Where the case ends, and where the ending is not written down.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A conference room, or a mediator's office, or a telephone call. Counsel on both sides, a valuation, an offer, an acceptance.
No judge, no transcript, no gallery, and no obligation to produce any of the three.

## Holds

[The Number That Ends It](piece_the_number_that_ends_it.md), which is set above what a verdict would likely have given.
[The Confidentiality Clause](piece_the_confidentiality_clause.md), which decides who may ever know that any of this happened.

## Offers

A better outcome than trial for the party who is here once, sooner, and with the risk taken out.
A closed exposure for the party who will be back, at a price it has chosen to pay.

## Withheld

The event itself, from everybody who is not in the room.
Nothing that happens here is reported, indexed, numbered or reasoned, so the case does not become an authority, a statistic or a gap: it stops existing as a legal fact while remaining entirely real to the two people who agreed it, and the difference between those two conditions is the whole of what this place does.
