# Saved to Spread: Reference

The research warrant for **Saved to Spread**, a staging of the phenomenon the literature calls the
**imperfect-vaccine hypothesis** or **leaky-vaccine-driven virulence evolution**: that a vaccine which
blunts disease and death but does not block infection or transmission, a leaky or non-sterilizing
vaccine, keeps its host alive long enough to shed even a hyper-virulent strain that would otherwise
have killed the host before it could spread, so it relaxes the natural cap on virulence and lets the
pathogen population evolve deadlier, with the evolved hypervirulence falling hardest on those who are
not protected. It is held with the finding, and with unusual care, that this is a property of leaky
vaccines against transmission-limited virulence and not an argument against vaccination, since the
vaccine saves the lives it protects, sterilizing vaccines that block transmission do not drive this,
and the reading is a case for how to design vaccines, not whether to use them.

## Line of Work

Staging leaky-vaccine hypervirulence as a **protection-lifts-the-cap-on-virulence structure**: a
pathogen's virulence is naturally bounded, because a strain deadly enough to kill its host quickly kills
its own means of spreading, so the very deadliest variants burn out with the hosts they fell, and
virulence settles where the trade-off between harm and transmission pays best (Anderson and May). Onto
this comes a leaky vaccine: one that blunts the disease and prevents death in the vaccinated but does
not stop them from being infected or from passing the pathogen on. It saves lives, and it is the sound
and standard thing to give. But it changes the arithmetic of virulence, because a vaccinated host
survives an infection that would have killed an unvaccinated one, and while it survives it sheds, so a
hyper-virulent strain that could never have spread from an unvaccinated host, dead too fast, now spreads
from a vaccinated one kept alive by the shot (Read, Baigent, Powers, and colleagues). The cap is lifted:
with the cost of killing the host removed by the vaccine, selection favours strains deadlier than any
the unvaccinated world would have sustained, and the pathogen population climbs in virulence (Gandon,
Mackinnon, Nee, and Read). The ordinary expectation is that a vaccine only ever makes a disease milder;
the finding is that a leaky one can make the pathogen itself deadlier, and that the deadlier pathogen is
borne not by the protected but by the unprotected, the unvaccinated and those whose immunity has waned,
who now meet a strain more lethal than would ever have evolved. The governing law is that **a vaccine
that blunts disease without blocking transmission keeps its host alive to shed strains the cost of
virulence would otherwise have capped, so it selects the pathogen deadlier, and the evolved
hypervirulence falls on the unprotected, the protection of some raising the lethality met by others,
with no one acting wrongly and the vaccine still saving the lives it shields**. The play models the
documented mechanics: the virulence-transmission trade-off that caps virulence (Anderson and May;
Ewald), the leaky vaccine that blunts death without blocking transmission (Read and colleagues), the
theory that imperfect vaccines select for higher virulence (Gandon and colleagues), and the experimental
demonstration in Marek's disease of poultry (Read and colleagues), as the load-bearing vectors of the
structure, and not as a claim that vaccines are harmful or should be withheld.

It is an evolutionary structure no staged misfit holds, and it is carefully distinct from its nearest
neighbours. It is distinct from Sick Enough to Spread, the evolution of virulence, where a parasite
settles at the harm that spreads best because too much harm kills its host before it can transmit: that
stages the trade-off itself and its natural cap, whereas here the cap is relaxed from outside by a leaky
vaccine, a second-order shift in the same trade-off, the engine not the trade-off but the specific
property of a protection that saves the host without stopping the spread. It is distinct from Borrowed
Immunity, herd-immunity free-riding, where each skips the shot the herd already covers and coverage
slips below the line: there the harm is a shortfall of vaccination and the return of a disease,
whereas here the harm needs no free-riding at all, arising from the vaccinated themselves and evolving a
deadlier pathogen even under high coverage. It is distinct from The Spent Margin, risk compensation,
where a safety measure hands a margin the exposed spend back as risk through their own behaviour: there
the mechanism is a change in human behaviour that eats the safety gain, whereas here no one behaves any
differently, and the backfire runs through selection on the pathogen and not through the choices of the
protected.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the one who
  takes the vaccine as a spreader or the one who makes it as reckless, because the finding it stages is
  that each acts soundly: taking a life-saving vaccine is right and the vaccinated host sheds without
  choosing to, giving a leaky vaccine is the standard and correct public-health act, and the pathogen has
  no agency at all, only selection. The loss is authored by no one: it is the cost of virulence lifted by
  a protection that saves the host without stopping the spread, so a deadlier strain that could not have
  survived now does, and the burden falls on the unprotected by the working of the vaccine and not by
  anyone's fault.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. The hazard can be
  avoided, but not for nothing: a sterilizing or transmission-blocking vaccine that stops the vaccinated
  from passing the pathogen on removes the selection pressure and does not drive virulence up, but such
  vaccines are harder to make and are not available for every pathogen, and where only a leaky vaccine
  exists the right choice is still to use it, since it saves lives, while designing toward transmission
  blockade and watching virulence over time (Read and colleagues; Gandon and colleagues; Ewald). The
  reckoning is that a protection which saves the host without stopping the spread reshapes what the
  pathogen becomes, and steering that costs the harder vaccine and the longer view.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's, and carried with unusual care given how easily it is misread. The staging is the
  house's; the experimental demonstration in Marek's disease is Read, Baigent, Powers, and colleagues', the
  theory that imperfect vaccines can select for higher virulence is Gandon, Mackinnon, Nee, and Read's,
  the virulence-transmission trade-off that caps virulence is Anderson and May's, and the evolutionary
  reading of virulence is Ewald's. The mechanism is carried as carefully as the finding: that the effect
  needs a leaky vaccine, one that blunts disease without blocking transmission, and a virulence that is
  transmission-limited, that it runs through selection on the pathogen and not through any human
  behaviour, that the evolved hypervirulence is borne by the unprotected, and above all that it is
  conditional and specific, since a sterilizing vaccine, a virulence not linked to transmission, or
  coverage that removes susceptible hosts each changes the case. The scope is held to how leaky vaccines
  shape pathogen evolution, and is never stretched into a claim that vaccines are dangerous or that any
  vaccine should be refused: the vaccine saves lives, and the misfit is a case for how to design one, not
  whether to take it.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per element, one per load-bearing vector, no filler.

- **The Cap**: carries the cost of virulence, the natural ceiling set by the trade-off between harm and
  transmission (Anderson and May; Ewald), a strain too deadly killing its host before it spreads, so the
  deadliest variants self-limit and virulence stays bounded.
- **The Leak**: carries the non-sterilizing protection (Read and colleagues; Gandon and colleagues), a
  vaccine that blunts disease and prevents death in the vaccinated but does not stop them being infected
  or passing the pathogen on, the specific property that does the work.
- **The Carrier**: carries the mechanism at the host (Read and colleagues), the vaccinated host kept alive
  long enough to shed a hyper-virulent strain that would have killed an unvaccinated host too fast to
  spread, survival turned into transmission of what should have self-limited.
- **The Rise**: carries the evolutionary consequence (Gandon and colleagues), the upward selection on
  virulence once the cap is lifted, the pathogen population climbing deadlier than any the unvaccinated
  world would have sustained.
- **The Exposed**: carries the phenomenon whole (Read and colleagues), the unprotected, the unvaccinated
  and those whose immunity has waned, meeting a strain more lethal than would ever have evolved, so the
  protection of some raises the lethality met by others, a burden with no author.
- **The Boundary**: carries the condition and the hedge (Read and colleagues; Gandon and colleagues), that
  the effect needs a leaky vaccine and a transmission-limited virulence and lifts under a sterilizing,
  transmission-blocking vaccine, a virulence not tied to transmission, or coverage that removes
  susceptibles, so the trap is real but specific and never a case against vaccinating.
