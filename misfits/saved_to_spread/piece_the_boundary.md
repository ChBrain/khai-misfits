# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md). The condition the effect needs and the edges where it lifts, the hedge that keeps the trap specific and never a case against vaccinating.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Weighed at [The Bench](place_the_bench.md), where the vaccine is designed: the conditions under which a leaky vaccine drives virulence up and those under which no such hazard arises at all (Read and colleagues; Gandon and colleagues).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit is misread into a claim that vaccines are dangerous, which is false and harmful: the boundary is what keeps the trap honest and its scope exact.
It carries the condition and the escape. The effect needs a leaky vaccine and a transmission-limited virulence; give it a sterilizing vaccine that blocks transmission, a virulence not tied to how the pathogen spreads, or coverage that removes susceptible hosts, and the hazard does not arise, so the trap is specific, runs through selection and not behaviour, and is a case for how to design a vaccine and never whether to take one.

## Apparent

A pile of caveats, easy to strip away into a slogan, when it is the load-bearing line that says exactly when the effect holds and, above all, that the vaccine still saves the lives it shields.
