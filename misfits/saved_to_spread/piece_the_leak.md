# Piece: The Leak

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md). The non-sterilizing protection, a vaccine that blunts disease and death but does not block infection or transmission.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Carried in [The Blood](place_the_blood.md) of the vaccinated: a protection that spares the host the illness and its death and yet leaves it able to be infected and to pass the pathogen on, life-saving and standard and leaky at once (Read and colleagues; Gandon and colleagues).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the vaccine is sterilizing, the host non-infectious, and the cap holds: it is the leak, the saving of the host without the stopping of the spread, that does all the work.
It bears the load by its exact partiality. Because the shot blunts death but not transmission, it saves the host the strain would have killed and lets that host keep shedding, so the leak is the one property that turns a life-saving vaccine into a lift on the cap.

## Apparent

A vaccine that works: from outside it looks like plain protection, the disease made mild, and it is that, its leakiness invisible in the relief it brings.

It is what lets [The Carrier](piece_the_carrier.md) shed a strain that should have died, the protection that saves the host and frees the pathogen at once.
