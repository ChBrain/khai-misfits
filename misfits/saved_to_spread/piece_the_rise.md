# Piece: The Rise

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Place

Playing out across [The Flock](place_the_flock.md) over generations of the pathogen: with the death of the host no longer the death of the strain, the deadlier variants survive, spread, and come to dominate, so the whole population climbs in virulence (Gandon and colleagues).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and one host's shedding is a curiosity, not a trend: it is the rise, the selection playing out across the population, that turns a single surviving carrier into a pathogen evolved deadlier for all.
It bears the load as the evolutionary consequence. Because selection stops punishing lethality once the vaccine pays its cost, variants deadlier than the unvaccinated world would ever have sustained now win, so the rise is the pathogen becoming, over time, what the leak allowed.

## Apparent

A worsening disease: from outside it can look like bad luck or a nastier bug going around, not the directed outcome of a lifted cap it is.
