# Place: The Bench

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md). Where the vaccine is designed and the leaky and the sterilizing are weighed. Hosts [A Vaccine That Blocks](plot_a_vaccine_that_blocks.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The vaccine in design: the choice between blunting disease and blocking transmission, and the virulence watched over time as either is deployed.

## Holds

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the conditions under which a leaky vaccine drives virulence up and those under which a sterilizing one avoids it.

## Offers

The harder vaccine that blocks transmission, removes the selection pressure, and protects without selecting the pathogen deadlier.

## Withheld

A costless correction: a sterilizing vaccine is harder to make and does not exist for every pathogen, so the safe design is not always at hand, and the leaky vaccine remains the right thing to give where it is not.
