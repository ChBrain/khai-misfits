# Piece: The Cap

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md). The natural ceiling on virulence, the cost of virulence that keeps a pathogen from being as deadly as it could.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Holding across [The Flock](place_the_flock.md), enforced by nothing but consequence: a strain too deadly kills its host before it can spread, so the deadliest variants burn out with the bodies they fell, and virulence settles where harm and transmission trade off best (Anderson and May).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is nothing to lift: the whole misfit is the removal of this cap, so without a natural ceiling on virulence there is no ceiling for a vaccine to raise.
It bears the load as the thing kept and then taken. Because virulence is bounded only by the death of the strains that overreach, a protection that keeps the host alive pays off exactly the cost that held the cap, and the ceiling that stood on its own comes down.

## Apparent

A fact of nature: diseases are as bad as they are and no worse, a stable ceiling that looks permanent and is only ever an equilibrium held by a cost.

## Yearbook

Holds [The Strain](persona_the_strain.md) in check the whole of [The Cost of Virulence](plot_the_cost_of_virulence.md), the deadliest variants self-limiting.
It is what [The Leak](piece_the_leak.md) lifts and what [The Carrier](piece_the_carrier.md) pays off, the ceiling standing until a protection saves the host it depended on killing.
