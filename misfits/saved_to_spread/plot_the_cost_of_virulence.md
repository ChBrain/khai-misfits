# Plot: The Cost of Virulence

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Flock](place_the_flock.md), where the pathogen circulates under a natural ceiling on how deadly it can be.

## Action

[The Strain](persona_the_strain.md), from [the strain's seat](position_strain.md), is held by [The Cap](piece_the_cap.md): a variant too deadly kills its host before it can spread, so the deadliest burn out with the bodies they fell, and virulence settles where harm and transmission trade off best. The scene closes with virulence bounded, the cost of killing the host enforcing the ceiling on its own (Anderson and May).

## Stage

The cap needs no enforcer but consequence: a strain that overreaches into lethality dies with the host it kills, so the ceiling on virulence is an equilibrium held by the death of the strains that break it, stable only as long as that death holds.

## Tension

The one thing keeping the pathogen from being deadlier is the death of its own hosts, so the ceiling stands on a cost that anything sparing the host would pay off, the cap strong and yet resting on exactly what a vaccine is built to prevent.
