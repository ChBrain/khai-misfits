# Position: Designer

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md). The seat of the vaccinologist who can aim for a transmission-blocking vaccine, and finds it harder to make and not always at hand.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The choice of what vaccine to build, leaky or sterilizing, and the knowledge of the trade-off between them.
The power to remove the selection pressure, where a transmission-blocking vaccine can be made.
No sterilizing vaccine for every pathogen, and only the harder science to reach for where the leaky one is easy.

## Orders

Block the spread where you can, and use the leaky vaccine where you must.
Aim for a vaccine that stops transmission, since that removes the pressure and does not drive virulence up, and where only a leaky one exists, give it still, for it saves lives, and watch the virulence over time.
Accept what the seat costs: that the safe design is the harder one, and that the right call is never to withhold protection, only to steer how it is made.

## Loses

The ease of a costless fix.
Because a sterilizing vaccine is harder to make and does not exist for every pathogen, the seat cannot always spare the exposed, so what it loses is the guarantee of a protection that does not select the pathogen deadlier, bought only where the harder science reaches.
It keeps the right aim and loses the ease, the remedy real, dear, and not always available.

## Drives

Toward the one who can spare the exposed at the cost of the harder science,
aiming for transmission blockade where it can be reached and using the leaky vaccine, rightly, where it cannot,
so the seat that would protect without selecting the pathogen deadlier is the seat that proves how dearly that is bought, and that the answer is better vaccines and never fewer.
