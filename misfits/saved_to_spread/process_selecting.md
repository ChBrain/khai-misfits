# Process: Selecting

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md). Borne at [The Flock](place_the_flock.md), precipitated by selection favouring deadlier strains once the cap is lifted, in [The Strain Climbs](plot_the_strain_climbs.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The cost of virulence paid off by the vaccine, so lethality no longer kills the strain's own means of spreading.

## Direction

From a capped virulence toward an evolving climb, the deadlier variants surviving, spreading, and coming to dominate.

## Lever

That selection stops punishing lethality once the host survives to shed, so a deadlier strain now out-transmits a milder one and virulence is driven up (Gandon and colleagues).

## Echo

[The Rise](piece_the_rise.md), the pathogen population evolved more virulent than the unvaccinated world would have sustained.
