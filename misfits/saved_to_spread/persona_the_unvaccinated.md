# Persona: The Unvaccinated

## Taxonomy

[Unvaccinated](position_unvaccinated.md). The unprotected, the unvaccinated and those whose immunity has waned, who meet the evolved hypervirulence the shield does not cover.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Unshielded, and going about life.
Has no protection, by choice or by waning or by no vaccine to be had, and meets whatever circulates as it comes, no different in its days from anyone else.
No fault in it that bears on the harm: whether it declined the shot or simply lacked it, its exposure did not make the strain worse.

## Action

Meets the pathogen as [the Rise](piece_the_rise.md) plays out, bearing [the Exposed](piece_the_exposed.md) in [The Strain Climbs](plot_the_strain_climbs.md), and takes the full weight of a strain more lethal than would ever have evolved.
Cannot blunt what it meets, having none of the protection the vaccinated carry, so the deadlier strain lands on it undiminished.
Does nothing that caused the climb: it only stands where the evolved hypervirulence comes home.

## Shadow

Not the author of its harm and not simply careless: the strain it meets was made deadlier among the protected, and its own being unshielded, whatever the reason, did not select the pathogen up.
Because the lethality was raised by a protection it never had, the seat bears a hazard it had no hand in making, so it is pitied as unlucky or blamed as unvaccinated when it is neither the cause nor the fault.
It is the one outside the shield, and the one the lifted cap falls on.

## Tell

Falls harder ill than the disease once struck, meeting a strain worse than any the unvaccinated world had known.
Cannot have prevented the climb by any choice of its own, so its tell is the severity itself, the unshielded body bearing a virulence the protection of others made.
