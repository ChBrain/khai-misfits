# Plan: Run It Hot

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the strain's seat](position_strain.md), inside [Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md), read as the direction selection drives and not as any will of the pathogen.

## Owner

- Owner: [Strain](position_strain.md)

## Direction

Spread by whatever transmits best, since selection keeps only what survives. Under the cap, hold virulence where harm and spread trade off, because a strain that kills its host too fast dies with it. Once a leaky vaccine keeps the host alive, run as hot as transmission now allows, for the deadlier lineage no longer loses its way out and out-transmits the milder. This is not a plan the pathogen holds but the direction it is driven, whatever the cost of virulence last permits.

## Orders

([The Strain](persona_the_strain.md) climbs through [Selecting](process_selecting.md) at [the Flock](place_the_flock.md), the deadlier variants surviving and dominating once [the Cap](piece_the_cap.md) is paid off.)

## Implementation

([The Flock](place_the_flock.md) is where selection plays out; [Selecting](process_selecting.md) favours the deadlier lineage once the host survives to shed, so the strain runs hotter generation on generation and becomes [the Rise](piece_the_rise.md), driven up by nothing but what now transmits.)

## Targets

- [ ] virulence stays capped while the deadliest strains die with their hosts
- [ ] a leaky vaccine keeps the host alive, so the deadliest strains no longer self-limit
- [ ] the deadlier lineage out-transmits the milder and comes to dominate
- [ ] the climb is selection and not will, the pathogen driven up by the lifted cap
