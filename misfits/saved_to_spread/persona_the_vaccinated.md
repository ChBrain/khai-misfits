# Persona: The Vaccinated

## Taxonomy

[Vaccinated](position_vaccinated.md). The protected host, spared the disease by a leaky vaccine and still able to be infected and to shed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Well, spared, and glad of the shot.
Took a vaccine that works, came through an illness that fells the unprotected, and carries on in health, sensibly grateful for the protection.
No recklessness in it: taking a life-saving vaccine is exactly right, and there is nothing in the choice to fault.

## Action

Takes the vaccine on [Take the Shot](plan_take_the_shot.md), carrying [the Leak](piece_the_leak.md) through [Blunting](process_blunting.md) at [the Blood](place_the_blood.md), and, surviving what would have killed an unvaccinated host, through [Shedding](process_shedding.md) becomes [the Carrier](piece_the_carrier.md).
Cannot be both protected and non-infectious under a leaky shot, so it lives through the deadly strain and sheds it while it lives.
Does what any sensible host does: it takes the protection on offer, and the surviving that protection buys is exactly what passes the strain on.

## Shadow

Not a spreader and not at fault: taking the vaccine is sound, and the shedding is not a choice but a consequence of surviving.
Because it is shielded from the very strain its survival helps carry, the cost of its protection falls entirely on others, so the one the vaccine most helps is the one through whom the lifted cap does its work, well and, without willing it, a carrier.
It is the one saved by the shot, and the one whose being saved lets the deadlier strain go on.

## Tell

Feels safe, and safe to be near, and is the first of these and not the second.
Cannot see the carriage, because it is well and the disease in it is mild, so it moves through the world spared and shedding at once, the protected host unaware it is the road the strain took out.
