# Plan: Take the Shot

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the vaccinated's seat](position_vaccinated.md), inside [Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Vaccinated](position_vaccinated.md)

## Direction

Accept the vaccine and be spared the disease, for it is sound and life-saving and the right thing to take. The leaky shot blunts the illness and its death, so take it and come through what fells the unprotected. Do not mistake the sparing for being safe to be near: the same vaccine that saves you leaves you able to be infected and to shed, so you survive the strain and pass it on, not by any choice but by living through what would have killed you.
