# Plot: The Vaccine That Leaks

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Blood](place_the_blood.md), where a life-saving vaccine of the leaky kind does its work.

## Action

[The Vaccinated](persona_the_vaccinated.md) on [Take the Shot](plan_take_the_shot.md) through [Blunting](process_blunting.md) carries [The Leak](piece_the_leak.md): the vaccine blunts the disease and prevents death but does not block infection or transmission, so the host is spared the illness and still passes the pathogen on. The scene closes with the host protected and infectious both, the leak in place and nothing wrong in the shot (Read and colleagues).

## Stage

The vaccine works, and taking it is right: it spares the vaccinated the disease and its death, exactly as a vaccine should. Its one partiality, that it blunts death without blocking transmission, is invisible in the relief it brings and is the whole of what will matter.

## Tension

The protection is genuine and its leakiness is the trap: a shot that saves the host without stopping the spread is precisely what can pay off the cost of virulence, so the good it plainly does carries, unseen, the lever that lifts the cap.
