# Persona: The Designer

## Taxonomy

[Designer](position_designer.md). The vaccinologist who can aim for a transmission-blocking vaccine, and finds it harder to make and not always at hand.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, exact, out to protect well.
Understands the trade-off the leaky vaccine sets and means to design past it, toward a vaccine that stops the spread and not only the disease.
No alarm in it and no retreat: it is certain the vaccine must be given, and its aim is to make a better one, never to give fewer.

## Action

Works [Block the Spread](plan_block_the_spread.md) through [Sterilizing](process_sterilizing.md) at [the Bench](place_the_bench.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): it aims for a transmission-blocking vaccine that removes the selection pressure, uses the leaky one where only that exists, and watches the virulence over time.
Cannot always reach the safer design, because a sterilizing vaccine is harder to make and does not exist for every pathogen, so it steers where it can and gives the leaky shot, rightly, where it cannot.
Does what a sound maker does: it designs toward transmission blockade and holds firm that the answer is a better vaccine, not a withheld one.

## Shadow

Not naive and not defeated: it is right that a sterilizing vaccine avoids the climb, and it is also unable to make one for every disease.
Because the safe design is the harder science, the designer proves that the remedy is real and dear, and must guard its own finding against the misreading that would turn a case for better vaccines into a case against vaccines.
It is the one who can spare the exposed, and the one who shows how carefully that must be said.

## Tell

Aims for a vaccine that blocks transmission, watches the virulence as it deploys, and says, each time, that the leaky shot is still right where it is all there is.
Cannot let the finding be twisted, because the stakes of the misreading are lives, so it repeats the bound as often as the result, the maker who holds that the fix is better protection and never less.
