# Plot: The Strain Climbs

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Flock](place_the_flock.md), where the cap is off and lethality no longer self-limits.

## Action

Through [Selecting](process_selecting.md), [The Strain](persona_the_strain.md), from [the strain's seat](position_strain.md), on [Run It Hot](plan_run_it_hot.md), drives [The Rise](piece_the_rise.md) and [The Exposed](piece_the_exposed.md): variants deadlier than the unvaccinated world would have sustained now survive, spread, and come to dominate, so the pathogen evolves more virulent, and [The Unvaccinated](persona_the_unvaccinated.md), from [the unvaccinated's seat](position_unvaccinated.md), meets a strain more lethal than would ever have existed. The scene closes with the evolved hypervirulence borne by the unprotected, the vaccinated shielded, and no one at fault (Gandon and colleagues).

## Stage

The disease stays mild in the protected and grows worse in itself: selection, no longer punishing lethality, drives the pathogen up, and the burden of that climb lands on the unvaccinated and the waning, who meet a strain the leaky vaccine helped select and their bodies cannot blunt.

## Tension

The protection of some raises the lethality met by others, so the shielded are spared the very virulence their shielding helped create, and the harm comes home to the unprotected, authored by no one and carried where the shield never reached.
