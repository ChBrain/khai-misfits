# Process: Sterilizing

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md). Borne at [The Bench](place_the_bench.md), precipitated by [The Designer](persona_the_designer.md) building a vaccine that blocks transmission, in [A Vaccine That Blocks](plot_a_vaccine_that_blocks.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The wish to protect without selecting the pathogen deadlier, once the lifted cap is understood.

## Direction

From a leaky vaccine that saves the host toward a sterilizing one that also stops the spread.

## Lever

That blocking transmission removes the selection pressure, so a strain that kills its host still loses its way out and the cap is not lifted, at the cost of the harder science that such a vaccine takes (Read and colleagues; Gandon and colleagues).

## Echo

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the hazard avoided where a sterilizing vaccine can be made and the leaky one used, rightly, where it cannot.
