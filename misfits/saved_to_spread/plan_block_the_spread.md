# Plan: Block the Spread

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the designer's seat](position_designer.md), inside [Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Designer](position_designer.md)

## Direction

Aim for a vaccine that blocks transmission and not only the disease, since a sterilizing vaccine stops the vaccinated from passing the pathogen on, removes the selection pressure, and does not drive virulence up. Where such a vaccine can be made, make it; where only a leaky vaccine exists, give it still, for it saves lives, and watch the virulence over time. Hold the finding carefully: it is a case for how to design a vaccine, and never a case for withholding one.
