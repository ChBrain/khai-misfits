# Piece: The Carrier

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md). The vaccinated host kept alive long enough to shed a strain that would have killed an unvaccinated one too fast to spread.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [The Blood](place_the_blood.md) of the protected host: the site where a hyper-virulent infection that would have felled an unvaccinated body is survived, and where, through the survival, the strain replicates and is shed onward (Read and colleagues).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the deadliest strain dies with its host as before: it is the surviving carrier that turns a lethal dead end into a transmission route, the mechanism by which the cap is actually paid off.
It bears the load at the host. Because a vaccinated host lives through what would have killed it, and while it lives it sheds, the strain that should have self-limited finds a way out, so the carrier is where the abstract lifting of the cap becomes a real deadlier infection passed on.

## Apparent

A recovered patient: from outside the vaccinated host looks simply spared, well again, when it is also the living conduit for the strain that spared it nothing.

## Yearbook

Made by [Shedding](process_shedding.md) in [The Host That Should Have Died](plot_the_host_that_should_have_died.md), the survival of [The Vaccinated](persona_the_vaccinated.md) turned into transmission.
It is how [The Leak](piece_the_leak.md) pays off [The Cap](piece_the_cap.md), the host saved becoming the strain's road out.
