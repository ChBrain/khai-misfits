# Pitch: The Vaccine Works

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Protective and life-saving.
A vaccine that works, sparing the vaccinated the disease and its death, the sound and standard thing to give and the right thing to take.
Nothing false in it: the vaccine really does protect, and taking it is correct, and this is never a case against it.

## Undertow

Under the protection, a cost of virulence paid off.
A shot that saves the host without stopping the spread keeps it alive to shed strains the death of the host would otherwise have capped, so the pathogen is selected deadlier and the evolved hypervirulence falls on the unprotected (Read and colleagues; Gandon and colleagues).
No spreader and no reckless hand: the vaccinated shed without choosing to, the makers gave the correct protection, the pathogen only followed selection.

## Nerve

The relief of a disease made mild, and the catch that the pathogen is made worse:
that the cost of virulence the vaccine removes is exactly what kept the pathogen in check, so the protection given and the virulence raised are the one leak seen twice,
and the vaccinated stay safe while the strain their protection helped select grows deadlier for everyone the shield does not cover.

## Echo

After the run: a disease mild in the protected and grown more lethal in itself, a strain that could not have survived now dominant, the unvaccinated and the waning meeting a pathogen more deadly than would ever have evolved, a harm with no author (Read and colleagues).
And the remedy, at a cost and not always at hand: a sterilizing vaccine that blocks transmission removes the selection pressure and does not drive virulence up, but it is harder to make and does not exist for every pathogen, and where only a leaky one is at hand the right choice is still to use it (Gandon and colleagues).
And the bound held in unusual view, because the effect is specific and not a law: it needs a leaky vaccine and a transmission-limited virulence and runs through selection and not behaviour, so the whole of it is a case for how to design a vaccine and never whether to take one.
