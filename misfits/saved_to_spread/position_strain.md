# Position: Strain

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md). The seat of the pathogen lineage under selection, held down by the cost of virulence until a leaky vaccine pays it off.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Variation in virulence, from the mild to the deadly, thrown up in every generation.
A cap that was killing its deadliest, since a strain that fells its host too fast loses its way to spread.
No agency at all, only selection: it does not choose, it is chosen, whatever spreads best surviving.

## Orders

Spread by whatever works, since selection keeps only what transmits.
Under the cap, hold virulence where harm and spread trade off best, the deadliest variants dying out; and once the host is kept alive, run as hot as transmission now allows.
Answer only to what survives: the cost of virulence gone, the deadlier lineage out-transmits the milder and takes over.

## Loses

The cap that held it, once the vaccine pays the cost.
Because a protected host survives to shed what would have killed it, the strain no longer loses its deadliest to their own lethality, so what the lineage loses is the ceiling on it, and losing the ceiling it climbs.
It keeps every route the living host opens and loses only the brake, freed to evolve deadlier than it ever was.

## Drives

Toward the lineage that climbs deadlier because the vaccine removed the cost,
running hotter as survival lets it, and by out-transmitting the milder variants coming to dominate,
so the seat that was capped by the death of its hosts is the seat a protection of those hosts sets loose, selected up with no will of its own.
