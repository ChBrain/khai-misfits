# Persona: The Strain

## Taxonomy

[Strain](position_strain.md). The pathogen lineage under selection, held down by the cost of virulence until a leaky vaccine pays it off.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A variant among variants, no worse in intent than any, for it has none.
Throws up in every generation a spread of virulence from mild to deadly, and lets survival sort them, showing whichever face the cost of virulence has last allowed.
No malice in it and no plan: it does not choose to harm, it is only what selection has kept.

## Action

Held under [the Cap](piece_the_cap.md), its deadliest lineages dying with the hosts they fell, until a leaky vaccine keeps those hosts alive; then through [Selecting](process_selecting.md) it runs as hot as transmission now allows, on [Run It Hot](plan_run_it_hot.md), and becomes [the Rise](piece_the_rise.md).
Cannot climb while the cap holds, because a strain that kills too fast loses its way out, and cannot help climbing once the host survives, because the deadlier lineage now out-transmits the milder.
Does only what survives: capped by the death of its hosts, and set loose by their protection.

## Shadow

Not a villain, because it has no will: the climb is not cruelty but selection, the lineage that spreads best simply winning.
Because its lethality was viable only when the host died with it and is now viable when the host lives, the very deadliness the cap forbade is what the leaky vaccine permits, so the strain becomes worse not by scheming but by being chosen under changed conditions.
It is the pathogen the cap once held, and the pathogen a protection of its hosts lets rise.

## Tell

Shows a disease that gets worse, generation on generation, deadlier than the records knew.
Cannot be reasoned with, because there is no one there, so its only tell is the mounting virulence itself, the ceiling gone and the lineage climbing toward what the leak now allows.
