# Process: Blunting

## Taxonomy

[Saved to Spread](play_saved_to_spread.md). Borne at [The Blood](place_the_blood.md), precipitated by [The Vaccinated](persona_the_vaccinated.md) taking a leaky vaccine that reduces disease without blocking transmission, in [The Vaccine That Leaks](plot_the_vaccine_that_leaks.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A leaky vaccine given to protect, blunting the disease and its death in the host that takes it.

## Direction

From a lethal disease toward a mild one, sparing the host the illness while leaving its infectiousness untouched.

## Lever

That the vaccine reduces disease and death but not infection or transmission, so the protection is real and partial in exactly the way that matters (Read and colleagues).

## Echo

[The Leak](piece_the_leak.md), the host protected and still able to pass the pathogen on.
