# Process: Dropping Out

## Taxonomy

[What Never Arrives](play_what_never_arrives.md). The load setting down where the current stops.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A current arriving at still water.
[The Silt](persona_the_silt.md), holding [Silt](position_silt.md), entering [the still water](place_the_still_water.md).

## Direction

Downward, onto the reservoir floor, and no further.
Not removed, not diverted and not treated: simply no longer carried, because carrying is a property of moving water.

## Lever

Stillness itself, which is the whole point of the storage and cannot be had without it.

## Echo

A floor rising slowly, a river below running clear, and a delivery that has ended without anybody ending it.
