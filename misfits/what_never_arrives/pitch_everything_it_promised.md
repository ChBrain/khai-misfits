# Pitch: Everything It Promised

## Taxonomy

[What Never Arrives](play_what_never_arrives.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Solid and civic. A good work, honestly rated, delivering for eighty years without a bad report.
Floods damped, water through the dry season, power every hour. The quiet pride of infrastructure that does exactly what the case said it would.

## Undertow

Under the delivery, a subtraction that produces no waste.
Nothing is dumped, emitted or taken from anybody present: a current stops and sets down what it was carrying, which is not an act at all. So the harm has no author, no substance and no location anyone at the wall would recognise as theirs.

## Nerve

The strangeness of a damage made entirely of something not happening, a long way off, slowly.

## Echo

Afterward: an annual report with every figure correct, a riverbed a metre lower than the drawings, a bridge footing standing in air, and a shoreline further in than the maps show, with no event anywhere in the record to explain it.
