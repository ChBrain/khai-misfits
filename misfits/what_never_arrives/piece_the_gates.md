# Piece: The Gates

## Taxonomy

[The Still Water](place_the_still_water.md). The outlet works, which release water and cannot release the load.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the wall, at the downstream end of [the still water](place_the_still_water.md), drawing from near the surface where the water is cleanest.

## Load Bearing

Without them the interruption is abstract, and with them it is a fitting.
It is the piece that makes the separation physical: the works draw from where the water is clear, because clear water is what a turbine and a canal want, so the design that protects the machinery is the same design that guarantees the load stays behind.

## Apparent

Steel and concrete, operated to a published schedule.
Wholly conventional, correctly specified, and admired for the clarity of what comes out of them.

## Yearbook

Opened and closed to the same seasonal pattern for eighty years without a bad report.
Never once opened low, in flood, with the reservoir drawn down, which is the one operation that would let the load through and the one that costs the storage the wall exists to hold.
