# Plot: It Takes It From the Bed

## Taxonomy

[What Never Arrives](play_what_never_arrives.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The promised volume released on schedule, into a channel made of exactly the material it is missing.

## Action

[Taking it back](process_taking_it_back.md): [The Clear Water](persona_the_clear_water.md), holding [Clear Water](position_clear_water.md), recovers part of its load from the bed and the banks.

## Stage

The channel cuts down over years. The material left behind coarsens, the gravels that fish spawn in are gone, and the footings of a bridge built for a riverbed that used to be higher are standing in air.

## Tension

This is the near half of the harm, and it is still nobody's fault.
A flow under its capacity is not stable, so the erosion is the water doing what water does with a deficit, and no operating regime avoids it while the release stays clean. The cleaner the water, the stronger the process: the very quality that gets the release admired is the quantity that measures how much it will take out of the ground below.
