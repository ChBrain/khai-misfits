# Persona: The One Who Built It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Built It](position_one_who_built_it.md). Answers a real brief completely, and is rated on water alone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent and civic. Reads the flood record, sizes the storage, states the yield and the head, and delivers all three.
Would show anyone the figures, and the figures are right.

## Action

Builds the wall and closes it.
Operates [the gates](piece_the_gates.md) to the schedule the case set out: hold in the wet, release in the dry, generate throughout.
Reports annually against [the benefit sheet](piece_the_benefit_sheet.md), where every line is met.

## Shadow

Cannot see the conveyor, because nobody was ever asked about it.
The brief named floods, water and power, and the work answers each exactly. Sediment has no rating, no meter and no constituency, so the one output that will decide a coastline is the one output that appears nowhere in the case, the consent, the design or the report.

## Tell

Says "it has performed to specification every year since commissioning", which is true, checkable, and entirely beside the point that will matter in eighty years.
