# Plot: Clear Water Out

## Taxonomy

[What Never Arrives](play_what_never_arrives.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The current stopped behind the wall, and [the still water](place_the_still_water.md) holding what the river was carrying.

## Action

[Dropping out](process_dropping_out.md): [The Silt](persona_the_silt.md), holding [Silt](position_silt.md), settles to the floor, and the water that leaves [the gates](piece_the_gates.md) runs clean.

## Stage

Nobody stages this. Stilling water is how storage works and settling is what stilled water does, so the interruption is performed by physics and requires no decision from anyone.

## Tension

The delivery ends without anybody ending it.
Nothing is dumped, extracted or diverted, no waste is produced and nothing is taken from anyone present, so there is no act, no date and no author. And the water leaving is clearer than it used to be, which people standing beside it describe as an improvement, because on every measure anyone is taking, it is.
