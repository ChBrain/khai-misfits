# Position: Silt

## Taxonomy

[What Never Arrives](play_what_never_arrives.md). The seat that stops moving without anyone moving it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A load carried in suspension by a current, and nothing else.
No price, no meter, no contract, and no party anywhere expecting delivery.

## Orders

Travel while there is a current.
Settle when there is not.

## Loses

Its onward journey, without any act of removal.
Nothing is dumped, extracted or diverted: stilling water is how storage works and settling is what stilled water does, so the interruption requires no waste, no negligence and no decision, and there is nothing anywhere that could be pointed at as the moment it was stopped.

## Drives

Toward a floor of accumulating deposit that is read as capacity lost upstream, and away from a coast where the identical tonnes would have been land, with nothing in any accounting able to connect the two readings of the same material.
