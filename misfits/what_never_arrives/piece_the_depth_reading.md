# Piece: The Depth Reading

## Taxonomy

[The Still Water](place_the_still_water.md). The one measurement of the load, taken in the wrong place and read the wrong way.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Taken across [the still water](place_the_still_water.md) every few years, by survey, and filed with the operator.

## Load Bearing

Without it, nobody ever counts the sediment at all, and with it the counting happens and points the wrong way.
It is the piece that makes the misreading precise: the same tonnes are measured accurately and entered as storage lost upstream, when they are also land unmade downstream, and no accounting anywhere connects the two readings of one quantity.

## Apparent

A survey line and a number. The floor of the reservoir, a little higher than last time.
Discussed as a maintenance concern and a question about the useful life of the asset.

## Yearbook

Taken faithfully since commissioning, and rising steadily, exactly as expected.
Never once quoted at the mouth, where the identical figure would have described the arrival that stopped.
