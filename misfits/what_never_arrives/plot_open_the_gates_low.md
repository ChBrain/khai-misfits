# Plot: Open the Gates Low

## Taxonomy

[What Never Arrives](play_what_never_arrives.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The structure understood at last, and the remedies on the table: draw down and sluice, bypass the storage, or truck gravel into the reaches below.

## Action

[Dropping out](process_dropping_out.md) prevented rather than managed: [The One Who Built It](persona_the_one_who_built_it.md), holding [One Who Built It](position_one_who_built_it.md), opens [the gates](piece_the_gates.md) low with the reservoir drawn down, in flood.

## Stage

[The One Who Built It](persona_the_one_who_built_it.md) tables [Let It Through](plan_let_it_through.md), and pays for it in storage and head, in the season when the water is worth most.

## Tension

Every remedy works, and every one is paid at the wrong end of the river.
Sluicing spends exactly what the wall was built to hold, and must be done in flood when the water is most valuable. Gravel augmentation treats a hundred kilometres of channel a truckload at a time, forever, and never reaches the coast at all. And the payer is upstream while the saving is downstream and decades out, so the whole of the price falls on a budget answerable to people who will never see the result, which will only ever be visible as something that failed to happen.
