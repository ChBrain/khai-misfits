## Tension

The one quantity that matters is measured carefully and entered on the wrong side.
The same tonnes are storage lost upstream and land unmade downstream, and no accounting anywhere connects the two readings, because [the mouth](place_the_mouth.md) is another authority, another budget and often another country. This is not concealment: the sheet has no column for a load in suspension, and could not have, since the brief named floods, water and power, which were the things anyone was asking for.
