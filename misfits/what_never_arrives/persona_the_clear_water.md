# Persona: The Clear Water

## Taxonomy

[Clear Water](position_clear_water.md). Released without its load, and under-supplied.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Clean, and admired for it. Water leaving a reservoir runs clear, which looks like an improvement and is often described as one.
Carries capacity for a load it does not have.

## Action

Leaves [the gates](piece_the_gates.md) on schedule, in the quantity the case promised.
Takes its missing load from the only place available: the bed and the banks of the channel below.

## Shadow

Cannot leave the river as it found it, because a flow under its capacity is not stable.
It cuts down, coarsens what it leaves, strips the gravels that fish spawn in, and undermines footings built for a bed that used to be higher. Every part of that is the water doing what water does with a deficit, and none of it is a fault in the water or in whoever released it.

## Tell

Is called clean, by people standing beside it, in the years while it is quietly lowering the ground they are standing on.
