# Persona: The Delta

## Taxonomy

[Delta](position_delta.md). Not a structure but an arrival, held in balance and no longer topped up.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Flat, wide, farmed, populated, and old. Ten thousand years of material laid down where the river met the sea, and lived on for most of them.
Looks permanent, the way ground looks permanent.

## Action

Sinks, at the rate it has always sunk, under its own weight and the sea's rise.
Fails, for the first time, to be made up by the spring arrival, because there is no spring arrival.

## Shadow

Cannot register the loss as damage, because nothing has been damaged.
It was never built once and then maintained: it is a deposit whose existence is a rate, so removing the rate breaks nothing and simply stops the making. There is no event, no failure and no date, only a shoreline that is further in each decade than the maps say.

## Tell

Is explained, when it is finally noticed, by the sea rising, which is true and is not the largest term: for a good many populated deltas, what was done upstream matters more to their survival than the rise does.
