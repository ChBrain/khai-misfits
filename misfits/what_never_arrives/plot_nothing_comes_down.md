# Plot: Nothing Comes Down

## Taxonomy

[What Never Arrives](play_what_never_arrives.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Mouth](place_the_mouth.md), eight hundred kilometres downstream: flat farmed ground, villages, a shoreline that looks permanent.
A spring that brings no material, and then another.

## Action

[Falling behind](process_falling_behind.md): [The Delta](persona_the_delta.md), holding [Delta](position_delta.md), goes on sinking at the rate it always sank, and is not made up.

## Stage

Nothing happens. No flood, no failure, no collapse and no date: only a shoreline that is further in each decade than the maps show.

## Tension

The harm is made entirely of something not occurring.
A delta is not a structure that was built and can break, it is a deposit whose existence is a rate, so stopping the arrival damages nothing and simply ends the making while the unmaking continues. And when the loss is finally noticed it is explained by the sea rising, which is true and is not the largest term, so the cause that is answerable is replaced in the account by the cause that is not.
