## Tension

There is nothing here to catch, and that is the difficulty.
The brief was real, the sizing is right, the figures are checkable and every one of them is met. What has also happened, and is on no drawing, is that a conveyor has been switched off: the river was carrying two things and the works were commissioned, consented and rated against exactly one.
