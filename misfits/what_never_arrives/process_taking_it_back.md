# Process: Taking It Back

## Taxonomy

[What Never Arrives](play_what_never_arrives.md). The hungry flow recovering its load from the channel.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A flow with capacity for a load it is not carrying, running over a bed made of exactly that material.
[The Clear Water](persona_the_clear_water.md), holding [Clear Water](position_clear_water.md), below the wall.

## Direction

Into the bed and the banks, until some of the deficit is made up.
Not aimed at anything and not preventable by operation: a flow under its capacity is not stable.

## Lever

The deficit itself. The cleaner the release, the stronger the process.

## Echo

A channel cut down, a coarsened bed, spawning gravels gone, and bridge footings built for a higher riverbed standing in air.
