# Process: Falling Behind

## Taxonomy

[What Never Arrives](play_what_never_arrives.md). A deposit that was a rate, no longer being renewed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Subsidence and a rising sea, both of which were always there, meeting a spring that brings nothing.
[The Delta](persona_the_delta.md), holding [Delta](position_delta.md), at [the mouth](place_the_mouth.md).

## Direction

Landward, a little each decade, with no event to mark it.
Nothing breaks and nothing fails: the making has stopped while the unmaking continues at the rate it always ran.

## Lever

Arithmetic. The two rates were in balance, and one of them has been set to zero somewhere else entirely.

## Echo

A shoreline further in than the maps show, attributed to the sea rising, which is true and is not the largest term.
