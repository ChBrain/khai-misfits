# Persona: The Programme Officer

## Taxonomy

[Programme Officer](position_programme_officer.md). The one who has to cover the whole call with the reviewers who agreed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An official matching a stack of proposals to a panel, against conflicts, workload, availability and a closing date.

## Action

Assigns reviewers to proposals.
Places expertise where it is most needed, declares conflicts out, and fills the remainder with the nearest available reader.

## Shadow

Distributes the depth of reading unevenly and has no way not to.
The seat is not neglecting anything: a call spans more subfields than any panel has members, the closest readers are the ones most likely to be conflicted out, and the people who agree to review are a self-selected fraction of those asked. Matching expertise to proposals as well as possible is the seat's job and doing it well is what makes the depth uneven, because it concentrates the deepest readers on the proposals that need them, which is correct and is the mechanism.

## Tell

Matches well, and matching well is what makes the scale incomparable.
