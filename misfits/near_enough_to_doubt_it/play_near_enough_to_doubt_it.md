# Play: Near Enough to Doubt It

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Near Enough to Doubt It.
A proposal has to be judged by somebody competent to judge it, which means somebody close enough to the work to follow what it claims. Being that close is also being able to see how it could fail: which step is the hard one, which assay has never worked at that scale, why the obvious thing has not been done already. Those are real difficulties, correctly identified, and every one of them is a reason for a lower score. The reader further away cannot see them and scores what is in front of them, which is the writing and the plan. Both readings are honest and they are not the same instrument, and they go on one scale.

## Arc

The bend is this: the reader able to judge a novel proposal is the reader able to see why it might not work, and a score is a rank, so being understood costs it.
It begins with a requirement nobody disputes.
[A Proposal Must Be Judged by Somebody Who Understands It](plot_a_proposal_must_be_judged_by_somebody_who_understands_it.md). Assigning it to a reader who cannot follow it is the failure everybody already knows how to name.
And understanding it means seeing how it could fail.
[And Understanding It Means Seeing How It Could Fail](plot_and_understanding_it_means_seeing_how_it_could_fail.md). [The Difficulty Seen](piece_the_difficulty_seen.md) is real, is what expertise is for, and is a reason to score lower.
So the closest reader marks it down.
[So the Closest Reader Marks It Down, Correctly](plot_so_the_closest_reader_marks_it_down_correctly.md). [The Near Reviewer](persona_the_near_reviewer.md) is not protecting anything: the proposal is genuinely riskier than it reads.
And [the far reader cannot, also correctly](plot_and_the_far_reader_cannot_also_correctly.md).
[The Far Reviewer](persona_the_far_reviewer.md) reports what is visible from where they sit, which is a clear plan by a capable group.
Then [the two numbers go on one scale](plot_the_two_numbers_go_on_one_scale.md). [The Common Scale](piece_the_common_scale.md) has no column for how far away the reader was.
And [the work that most needed a reader is the work a reader costs](plot_so_the_work_that_most_needed_a_reader_is_what_a_reader_costs.md). [The Applicant](persona_the_applicant.md) proposed the thing that required expertise to assess.

Cue: a decision letter, and a proposal that was the most novel thing in the round. Closes when the penalty is found to fall hardest on the work the process exists to find.

## Stakes

Whether a genuinely new proposal can be assessed by somebody who understands it and survive the assessment.
Every plot moves the same way: toward an assignment that must place expertise where it is needed, a closeness that produces both the competence and the doubt, two honest readings taken at different depths, a scale with no column for depth, a line drawn at a percentile, and a penalty concentrated on the most novel work in the round, and away from any account in which a reviewer defended territory or an officer was careless. The production cannot fault the near reviewer, whose difficulties are real and whose job is to find them; and it cannot fault the far reviewer, who reported accurately what was visible from where they sat. What is left to fight over is not whether anybody erred but what to call a number that means two different things and is added up anyway.
