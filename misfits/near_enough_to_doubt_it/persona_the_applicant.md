# Persona: The Applicant

## Taxonomy

[Applicant](position_applicant.md). The one who proposed the thing that needs expertise to assess.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An investigator with an approach that has not been tried, a plan to try it, and a deadline.

## Action

Writes the proposal.
States the approach plainly, including the step that is hard, because concealing it would be misconduct and reviewers would find it anyway.

## Shadow

Is penalised in proportion to how well the work was understood.
The seat has no way to act on this. Writing less novelty in is writing a different proposal, and writing the difficulty out is dishonest and detectable. Requesting particular reviewers is available only to name conflicts, not to choose depth, and the seat cannot know in advance who will read it. What it can control is the quality of the argument, and the quality of the argument is precisely what a reader close enough to follow it will engage with, in detail, at the level where the difficulties live.

## Tell

Is read properly, once, and pays for it.
