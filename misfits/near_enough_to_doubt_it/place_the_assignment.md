# Place: The Assignment

## Taxonomy

[Near Enough to Doubt It](play_near_enough_to_doubt_it.md). Where proposals meet the readers who will judge them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A spreadsheet of proposals against a panel, conflicts marked out, workload balanced, a closing date.
Everything proper: the officer is matching expertise to subject as well as the panel allows.

## Holds

[The Programme Officer](persona_the_programme_officer.md), doing the job well.
[The Intellectual Distance](piece_the_intellectual_distance.md), set here and recorded nowhere afterwards.

## Offers

The nearest competent reader available for each proposal, which is what good assignment means.

## Withheld

An even depth of reading across the round.
The place is where the mechanism is set and where it is clearest that setting it well is what causes it. A call spans more subfields than a panel holds; the readers closest to a proposal are the ones most likely to be conflicted out of it; and those who agree to serve are a self-selected fraction of those asked. Matching as well as possible concentrates the deepest readers on the proposals that most need them, which is correct in every particular and means those proposals are the ones read at the depth where difficulties are visible.
