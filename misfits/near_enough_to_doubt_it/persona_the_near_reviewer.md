# Persona: The Near Reviewer

## Taxonomy

[Near Reviewer](position_near_reviewer.md). The one close enough to see what could go wrong.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A working scientist in the same subfield, who has run the method, knows what it does at that scale, and knows why the obvious version has not been done.

## Action

Reads it closely.
Names the step that is hard, the assay that has not worked at that scale, and the assumption the design rests on, and scores accordingly.

## Shadow

Does the job exactly and the doing of it is the mechanism.
The seat is not defending territory, and the evidence is against that reading: the effect appears where reviewers were assigned at random, and where the harshness tracks self-assessed expertise rather than any competitive relation. The difficulties named are real, the proposal genuinely is riskier than it reads to somebody who cannot see them, and a reviewer who suppressed them to be generous would be failing the panel. There is no version of this seat that could score higher without either lying or knowing less.

## Tell

Reports accurately, and the accurate report is the lower number.
