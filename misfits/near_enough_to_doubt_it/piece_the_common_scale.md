## Load Bearing

It records what each reader concluded and not what each reader could see, and a budget requires exactly one order.
A four from somebody who knows why the third step fails and a seven from somebody who cannot know it are not two readings of one quantity. Nothing in a percentile distinguishes them, and no available procedure removes the difference: discussion converges the panel on the better-informed account, which is right and carries the difference into the order, and averaging weights the reading that saw less equally, which is worse. The scale is not a crude instrument that a better one would replace. It is the thing a decision needs, doing what a decision needs.
