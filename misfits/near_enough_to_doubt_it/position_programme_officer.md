# Position: Programme Officer

## Taxonomy

[Near Enough to Doubt It](play_near_enough_to_doubt_it.md). The seat that must cover a whole call with the readers who agreed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A stack of proposals, a panel, a conflicts list, and a closing date.

## Orders

Match expertise to proposals. Declare conflicts out. Fill the rest.

## Loses

Any way to make the depth of reading even.
The call spans more subfields than the panel holds, the closest readers are the likeliest to be conflicted out, and matching well concentrates depth where it is needed.

## Drives

Toward a good assignment, which is what makes the scores incomparable.
