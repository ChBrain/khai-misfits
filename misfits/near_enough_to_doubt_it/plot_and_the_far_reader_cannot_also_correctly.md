# Plot: And the Far Reader Cannot, Also Correctly

## Taxonomy

[Near Enough to Doubt It](play_near_enough_to_doubt_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A reader outside the subfield, reporting in good faith.

## Action

[The Far Reviewer](persona_the_far_reviewer.md) assesses significance, argument, plan and team, and scores what is visible from there.

## Stage

A second honest reading, silent about the part that decides the outcome.

## Tension

This seat is not the weak link and treating it as one is how the structure gets mistaken for a staffing problem.
The far reader evaluates competently everything within reach: whether the question matters, whether the design follows from it, whether the group has done comparable work. What is out of reach is that the third step has never worked at that scale, and knowing that requires having been in the room where it failed. Declining the assignment moves the proposal to a reader further away still, and the panel must cover the call either way.
