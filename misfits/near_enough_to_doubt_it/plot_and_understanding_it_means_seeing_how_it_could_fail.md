# Plot: And Understanding It Means Seeing How It Could Fail

## Taxonomy

[Near Enough to Doubt It](play_near_enough_to_doubt_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A reader who has run that assay and knows what it does at that scale.

## Action

[The Difficulty Seen](piece_the_difficulty_seen.md) surfaces: the hard step, the assay that has not scaled, the assumption the design rests on.

## Stage

Expertise producing exactly what it was commissioned to produce.

## Tension

The difficulties are real, which is what makes them impossible to discount.
A reviewer close enough to follow a novel approach is close enough to know why the obvious version of it has not been done, and that knowledge is not scepticism or conservatism. It is the content of the field. A reviewer who withheld it to be encouraging would be failing the panel that asked for an assessment and the applicant who will spend three years on the answer. There is no reading of this step in which the near reviewer should have said less.
