# Plot: So the Work That Most Needed a Reader Is What a Reader Costs

## Taxonomy

[Near Enough to Doubt It](play_near_enough_to_doubt_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A decision letter, and a proposal that was the most novel thing in the round.

## Action

[The Applicant](persona_the_applicant.md) reads a summary statement naming a real weakness, below the line.

## Stage

A penalty concentrated on the work the process exists to find.

## Tension

Every seat performed correctly and the outcome is the sum of the correct performances.
The officer matched expertise where it was needed. The near reviewer named difficulties that are real. The far reviewer reported accurately what was visible. The chair converged the panel on the better-informed account. The applicant described the approach honestly, including the hard step, because doing otherwise is misconduct. And the effect falls hardest on novelty, because a novel proposal is the one whose difficulties only a close reader can see and the one that most needs a close reader to be understood at all. What is left is not a fault to allocate but a rank in which being understood and being weaker are the same number.
