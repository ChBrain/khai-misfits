# Plot: The Two Numbers Go on One Scale

## Taxonomy

[Near Enough to Doubt It](play_near_enough_to_doubt_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A percentile computed, and a line drawn across it.

## Action

[The Common Scale](piece_the_common_scale.md) receives both readings, and [the chair](persona_the_chair.md) resolves the divergence in the usual way.

## Stage

One order, built from measurements taken at different depths.

## Tension

Every available procedure carries the difference through, and the best one carries it most cleanly.
Discussion at the panel is genuinely useful and it converges the room on the near reviewer's account, because that account is better informed and everybody present can tell. So the deeper reading propagates, correctly, and the proposal that had one falls relative to proposals whose difficulties nobody in the room could see. Averaging instead would weight the reading that saw less equally, which is worse. There is no third procedure, and the scale has no column for how far away each reader stood.
