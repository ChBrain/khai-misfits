# Piece: The Intellectual Distance

## Taxonomy

[The Assignment](place_the_assignment.md). The gap between what a reader knows and what a proposal proposes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [the assignment](place_the_assignment.md), and never recorded again.

## Load Bearing

It is the quantity the whole structure runs on, it is set by an act of good administration, and no later step carries it.
Randomising which reviewer met which proposal was enough to show it operates on its own: across thousands of evaluator and proposal pairings, closeness to the proposal's own field predicted a less generous evaluation, and it did so where no competitive relation had been allowed to form. It is not a property of any person and not a bias anybody holds. It is the distance between two bodies of knowledge, and it decides how much of a proposal a reader can see.

## Apparent

An administrative detail, settled once and of no further interest.

## Yearbook

Is the depth of the reading, which the score sheet does not have a box for.
