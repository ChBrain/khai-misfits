## Withheld

Any record of how far away each reader stood.
The place is where two readings taken at different depths become comparable by being written in the same units. A four from somebody who knows why the third step fails and a seven from somebody who cannot know it are not two measurements of one quantity, and the arithmetic treats them as though they were. Discussion does not fix this and makes it cleaner: the better-informed account is more persuasive because it is better informed, so the panel converges on it, which is correct and carries the difference straight into the order.
