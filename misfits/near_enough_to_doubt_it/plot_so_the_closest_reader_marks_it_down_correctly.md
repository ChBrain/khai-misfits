# Plot: So the Closest Reader Marks It Down, Correctly

## Taxonomy

[Near Enough to Doubt It](play_near_enough_to_doubt_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A scoring rubric with a box for feasibility.

## Action

[The Near Reviewer](persona_the_near_reviewer.md) scores lower, because each difficulty named is a reason to.

## Stage

An accurate report, expressed as the number the form asks for.

## Tension

The lower score follows from perception rather than from interest, and the evidence says so.
Where evaluators and proposals were paired at random, closeness to a proposal's own field still predicted a less generous evaluation, so no competitive relation is needed to produce it. Where harshness was traced, it tracked the reviewer's own assessment of their expertise rather than any rivalry, and the reading that fits is that a better-informed reader sees more that could go wrong. The seat is not protecting anything. It is reporting, and reporting well is what lowers the number.
