# Persona: The Chair

## Taxonomy

[Chair](position_chair.md). The one who must produce one order from readings taken at different depths.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A panel chair with divergent scores, a meeting, a percentile and a payline.

## Action

Runs the discussion, invites the outlying reviewer to explain, and moves to a final score.
Produces a ranked list and applies the line.

## Shadow

Reconciles a disagreement that is not a disagreement.
The seat is doing the right thing and the material will not take it. When a near and a far reviewer diverge, the discussion is genuinely useful and it converges the panel on the near reviewer's account, because that account is better informed and everybody present can tell. So the deeper reading propagates, correctly, and the proposal that had one falls relative to proposals whose difficulties nobody in the room could see. Averaging instead would weight ignorance equally, which is worse, and there is no third procedure.

## Tell

Resolves it correctly, which completes the penalty rather than lifting it.
