# Persona: The Far Reviewer

## Taxonomy

[Far Reviewer](position_far_reviewer.md). The one who reports what is visible from where they sit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A capable scientist from a neighbouring area, assigned because a panel must cover a whole call and cannot be experts in all of it.

## Action

Reads it carefully.
Assesses the argument, the plan, the team and the significance as presented, and scores what can be assessed from there.

## Shadow

Gives an honest reading that is silent about the part that matters most.
The seat is not lazy and is not guessing: it evaluates significance, coherence, feasibility as argued, and the record of the group, and it does so competently. What it cannot do is know that the third step has never worked at that scale, because knowing that requires having been in the room where it failed. Declining the assignment does not help, since the panel still has to cover the call and the next reader out is further away still. The seat's reading is accurate about everything it can see.

## Tell

Scores higher for the same page, for reasons that are not generosity.
