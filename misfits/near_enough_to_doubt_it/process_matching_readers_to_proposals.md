# Process: Matching Readers to Proposals

## Taxonomy

[Near Enough to Doubt It](play_near_enough_to_doubt_it.md). The good assignment that distributes depth unevenly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A closed call, and a panel.
[The Programme Officer](persona_the_programme_officer.md), holding [Programme Officer](position_programme_officer.md), at [the assignment](place_the_assignment.md).

## Direction

Toward the nearest competent reader for each proposal.

## Lever

The panel's expertise, allocated against conflicts, workload and availability.

## Echo

Expertise concentrated where it is most needed, and a round read at uneven depth.
