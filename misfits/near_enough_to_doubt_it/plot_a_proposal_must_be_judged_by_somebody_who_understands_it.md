# Plot: A Proposal Must Be Judged by Somebody Who Understands It

## Taxonomy

[Near Enough to Doubt It](play_near_enough_to_doubt_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A call closed, a stack of proposals, and a panel to assemble.

## Action

[The Programme Officer](persona_the_programme_officer.md) assigns readers, placing the nearest competent one against each proposal.

## Stage

An act of good administration, performed well.

## Tension

Nobody disagrees with the requirement, and meeting it is what starts everything else.
Assigning a proposal to somebody who cannot follow it is the failure every review system already knows how to name, and every reform of the last century has pushed the other way. So closeness is not a risk being tolerated: it is the thing panels are built to supply. A call spans more subfields than a panel holds, the closest readers are the likeliest to be conflicted out, and those who serve are a self-selected fraction of those asked, so matching as well as possible is difficult work done conscientiously.
