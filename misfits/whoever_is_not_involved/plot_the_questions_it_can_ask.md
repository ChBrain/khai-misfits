# Plot: The Questions It Can Ask

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The agenda, item four.
[The Reasonable Question](piece_the_reasonable_question.md): is the form complete, is a rollback described, is the window agreed, are the right names on it.

## Action

[Sitting](process_sitting.md): [The Board](persona_the_board.md), in [the approving seat](position_approving.md), examines what is in front of it at [the board room](place_the_board_room.md) and approves or defers.

## Stage

[The Author](persona_the_author.md), on [Explain It to People Who Were Not There](plan_explain_it_to_people_who_were_not_there.md), renders the change into terms a stranger to the system can follow, and the risk does not survive the translation.

## Tension

The substitution is performed by everybody and chosen by nobody.
The board cannot assess whether this change will break this system, because that requires knowing the system, and not knowing the system is the qualification for sitting here. So it assesses what it can see, and what it can see is the paperwork.
**Those are real questions and they are not the question.** A rollback plan genuinely matters, a window genuinely matters, and asking about them well is, from inside the room, indistinguishable from doing the job. Nobody present experiences the meeting as theatre, which is why it survives review: **the seat is disqualified by the property that appoints it**, and diligence does not close that gap because diligence was never what was missing.
