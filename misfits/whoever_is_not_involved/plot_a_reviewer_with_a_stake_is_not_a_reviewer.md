# Plot: A Reviewer With a Stake Is Not a Reviewer

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Board Room](place_the_board_room.md), and a body being constituted to sit in it.
[The Independence](piece_the_independence.md), written into its terms of reference.

## Action

[Constituting](process_constituting.md): [The Board](persona_the_board.md), in [the approving seat](position_approving.md), is composed of people with no stake in the work it will review.

## Stage

[The Board](persona_the_board.md), on [Let Nothing Through Unexamined](plan_let_nothing_through_unexamined.md), meets on schedule and takes every change in front of it seriously.

## Tension

The intention is right and the composition is the correct expression of it, which is why this cannot be answered by abolishing the board.
Putting a change into a live service with no review at all is indefensible, and everybody involved knows it. So a body is created; and it is made independent, because **a reviewer who benefits from approval is not performing a review**. That sentence is true. It is the reason the arrangement exists, and it is what any responsible organisation would write.
So the trap cannot be sprung by finding the board lazy, captured or unnecessary. It has to be sprung with a competent body doing exactly what a sound principle requires of it.
