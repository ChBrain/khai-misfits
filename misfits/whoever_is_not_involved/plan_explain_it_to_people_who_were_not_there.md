# Plan: Explain It to People Who Were Not There

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the proposing seat](position_proposing.md), inside [Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Proposing](position_proposing.md)

## Direction

Write the change, complete the form honestly, and render it into terms a reader who has never operated the system can follow. Submit it, and wait for the sitting.

## Orders

([The Author](persona_the_author.md) submits at [the queue](place_the_queue.md) and explains the change at [the board room](place_the_board_room.md).)

## Implementation

([The Queue](place_the_queue.md), on the sitting's schedule; the [submitting](process_submitting.md) process; a gap in knowledge created deliberately and for a good reason.)

## Targets

- [x] the author knows what the change touches and wants the review
- [ ] the form is complete and the explanation is honest
- [ ] what survives the translation is the part of a change a stranger can follow
- [ ] the risk is usually not that part, and a better form does not change it
