# Persona: The Board

## Taxonomy

[Approving](position_approving.md). The change advisory board: competent, diligent, meeting on schedule, and constituted from people with no stake in the work.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Responsible, and correctly constituted.
Made up of people independent of the teams proposing, because a reviewer who benefits from approval is not performing a review.
Asking reasonable questions, in good faith, of every change that comes to it.

## Action

Meets, on schedule.
Reads the form, checks the rollback, confirms the window, notes the names.
Approves, or asks for more detail and approves at the next sitting.

## Shadow

The one question it exists to answer, which is whether this change will break this system.
Because answering that requires knowing the system, and not knowing the system is the qualification for sitting here, the seat is **disqualified by the property that appoints it**, and no amount of diligence closes the gap.
It ends with minutes in good order and a failure rate exactly where it would have been.

## Tell

Says: approved, subject to the rollback plan being attached.
A real condition, correctly applied, about a document.
