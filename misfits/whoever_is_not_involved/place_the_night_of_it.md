# Place: The Night of It

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). The outage: a service down, a fix ready, and a gate between them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A failure indistinguishable from one in an organisation with no board at all.
People who have just learned precisely what is wrong, holding the change that fixes it.

## Holds

Both halves of the measurement at once.
The failure arrived at the rate it would have arrived at anyway, external approval having no correlation with that rate; and the recovery is slower for the same control, which correlates negatively with time to restore service.

## Offers

Complete clarity about what went wrong, to the people who caused it and can undo it.

## Withheld

Permission to act on that clarity.
The knowledge and the authority sit in different places by design, and the design is the thing being staged.
