# Position: Proposing

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). The author's seat: holding the only knowledge that matters, and disqualified by holding it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The change, and knowledge of what it touches.
A willingness to be reviewed, and a wish to be.
A stake, which is what disqualifies it from deciding.

## Orders

Write the change.
Complete the form and explain it to a room that was not there.
Wait for the sitting.

## Loses

The knowledge, in translation, because it must be rendered into terms a stranger to the system can follow.
Because the involvement that produced the understanding is the same involvement that bars the decision, this seat cannot both know and decide, and no procedure available reconciles the two.
It ends approved by people who could not have caught what goes wrong.

## Drives

Toward a change that works,
submitted honestly to a process it believes in,
so the seat that understands the risk is the seat with no vote on it.
