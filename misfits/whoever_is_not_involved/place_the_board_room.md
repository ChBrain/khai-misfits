# Place: The Board Room

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). Where the change is examined: an agenda, a schedule, minutes, and nobody who has run the system.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A well-run standing meeting: item four, any objections, noted, approved.
Competent people asking reasonable questions in good faith.

## Holds

The play's quietest fact: nobody present experiences this as theatre.
The questions are real, the diligence is genuine, and asking them well is from inside the room indistinguishable from doing the job, which is why the arrangement survives every review of itself.

## Offers

A trustworthy review, by people with nothing to gain from the answer.

## Withheld

Any means of judging whether this change will break this system.
That requires knowing the system, which is what the room was constituted not to contain, so the missing thing is not effort or attention but the one qualification that was deliberately excluded.
