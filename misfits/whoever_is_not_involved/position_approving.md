# Position: Approving

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). The board's seat: independent by constitution, competent, and unable to judge the risk it exists to judge.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Independence from every team it reviews, written into its terms of reference.
Competent members and reasonable questions.
A schedule, an agenda and minutes.

## Orders

Meet on schedule.
Examine every change before it enters a live service.
Approve, or defer for more detail.

## Loses

The one question it exists to answer, which is whether this change will break this system.
Because answering it requires knowing the system, and not knowing the system is the qualification for sitting here, the seat is **disqualified by the property that appoints it**, and diligence closes nothing, diligence never having been what was missing.
It ends with minutes in good order and a failure rate exactly where it would have been.

## Drives

Toward letting nothing through unexamined,
by the only composition a sound principle permits,
so the seat built to catch the fault is the seat that cannot see it.
