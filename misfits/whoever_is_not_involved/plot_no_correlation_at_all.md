## Tension

The measurement settles what argument could not, and it is not a weak result.
Approval by a body external to the team has **no correlation with the rate at which changes fail**. Not a small effect, not a contested one: none. And the same data finds it correlates **negatively** with how long a change takes to reach production, with how often anything is released at all, and with how quickly service is restored when something breaks.
So the control costs three things and buys nothing measurable of the thing it was built for. **The gate and the fault never intersected**, because the gate was examining the paperwork and the fault was in the system, and no quantity of diligence at the meeting would have made them meet.
