# Piece: The Reasonable Question

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). What the board can ask: the form, the rollback, the window, the names.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the agenda at [the board room](place_the_board_room.md), item by item.
Answered honestly, every time.

## Load Bearing

Without it the meeting is obviously empty and somebody would have stopped it.
It is load bearing because these questions are **genuinely worth asking**: a rollback plan matters, a window matters, and a change without them is worse. The substitution works precisely because what replaces the question is not nonsense.

## Apparent

Diligence, which it is.
It reads as a body doing its job carefully, and from inside the room it is indistinguishable from doing the job, which is why no participant experiences the meeting as theatre.

## Yearbook

Asked by [sitting](process_sitting.md), answered by [submitting](process_submitting.md).
Real questions, asked well, none of which is whether this change will break this system.
