# Process: Breaking

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). The approved change breaking something, at the rate such changes break things.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The ordinary proportion of changes that go wrong.
At [the night of it](place_the_night_of_it.md), without regard to the minutes.

## Direction

Toward an outage indistinguishable from one in an organisation with no board at all.
The measurement is that external approval has no correlation with the rate at which changes fail: not a weak relationship, none.
So the control and the fault never intersected, the control having examined the documentation and the fault being in the system.

## Lever

The work's real failure rate, undisturbed.
Because no amount of diligence at the meeting would have made the gate and the fault meet, this step is where the whole arrangement is measured and found to have bought nothing.

## Echo

A service down,
and a signed approval for the change that did it.
