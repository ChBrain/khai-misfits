# Process: Restoring

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). The remedy being written, submitted, and held by the same gate.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The outage, and people who now know exactly what is wrong.
At [the queue](place_the_queue.md), where a fix is a change like any other.

## Direction

Toward getting the service back, by applying the best-informed change anybody will write all year.
A fix is a change and a change goes to the board; the emergency path exists and is also a procedure, needing approvers, and approvers are people who at two in the morning are asleep.
So the control that failed to prevent the failure now stands in front of the recovery.

## Lever

The same gate, applied to the opposite case: a change made in full knowledge of a specific fault, held by a process designed for changes made in ignorance of any.
Because the delay lands in the measure that counts while a service is down, the harm is **largest exactly where delay costs most**.

## Echo

A fix ready since two o'clock,
applied at nine, correctly.
