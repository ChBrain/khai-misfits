## Cue

## Action

[Submitting](process_submitting.md): the change goes to [the peer](persona_the_peer.md), in [the reviewing seat](position_reviewing.md), rather than to [the board room](place_the_board_room.md).

## Stage

[The Peer](persona_the_peer.md), on [Read It Knowing the System](plan_read_it_knowing_the_system.md), asks the question the board could not and answers it in an afternoon.

## Tension

The settlement works and its price is the principle rather than the practice.
This is the same review by different people, and the same research that found the board ineffective finds this effective. There is no scepticism to register about whether it helps.
What it spends is [the independence](piece_the_independence.md). The reviewer now has a stake: they work on the system, they want the change to go in, and their judgement is entangled with the outcome. **The organisation must give up the sentence that a reviewer with a stake is not a reviewer**, which is true, which is why the board was built, and which has to go anyway.
So the production offers no reconciliation. **The property that makes a reviewer trustworthy and the property that makes a reviewer able are the same property with opposite signs**, and any arrangement has to choose.
