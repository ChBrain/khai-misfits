# Plan: Let Nothing Through Unexamined

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the approving seat](position_approving.md), inside [Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Approving](position_approving.md)

## Direction

Meet on schedule, examine every change before it enters a live service, and approve only what has been looked at. Be composed of people with no stake, because a reviewer who benefits from approval is not performing a review.

## Orders

([The Board](persona_the_board.md) examines each item at [the board room](place_the_board_room.md) and approves or defers.)

## Implementation

([The Board Room](place_the_board_room.md), on schedule; the [constituting](process_constituting.md) and [sitting](process_sitting.md) processes; [the independence](piece_the_independence.md) written into the terms of reference.)

## Targets

- [x] an unexamined change entering a live service is indefensible, and this body exists for that reason
- [x] the composition excludes anybody with a stake in the work reviewed
- [ ] every change is examined and the questions asked of it are reasonable
- [ ] the one question it exists to answer requires knowing the system, which the composition excludes
