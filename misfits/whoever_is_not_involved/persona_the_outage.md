# Persona: The Outage

## Taxonomy

[Failing](position_failing.md). The change that breaks something: arriving at the rate it would have arrived at anyway.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, and unimpressed by process.
Not a catastrophe and not negligence: the proportion of changes that break something, which every organisation has.
Indifferent to whether the form was complete.

## Action

Arrives.
Arrives at the same rate in organisations with a board and in organisations without one.
Takes out a service that the approval said nothing about.

## Shadow

Nothing, and this seat is where the measurement lands: the rate is **uncorrelated** with whether an external body approved the change.
Because the board's questions were about the paperwork and the failure is in the system, the gate and the fault never intersected, and no diligence at the meeting would have made them.
It ends having happened on schedule, past a control designed to stop it.

## Tell

Says nothing.
A service is down, and somewhere there is a signed approval for the change that did it.
