# Persona: The Author

## Taxonomy

[Proposing](position_proposing.md). The engineer who wrote the change, knows what it touches, and is disqualified from approving it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The only person in the process who understands the risk.
Not asking to be trusted blindly: willing to be reviewed, and wanting to be.
Disqualified from the decision precisely because of the involvement that produced the knowledge.

## Action

Writes the change and knows what it touches.
Fills the form and explains it to a room that was not there.
Waits for the sitting.

## Shadow

The knowledge, which cannot be transferred at the speed the meeting runs.
Because the explanation must be rendered into terms a stranger to the system can follow, what reaches the room is the part of the change that survives translation, and the risk usually does not.
It ends approved by people who could not have caught the thing that goes wrong.

## Tell

Says: it only affects the one service.
True, believed, and the only person who could check it is the one saying it.
