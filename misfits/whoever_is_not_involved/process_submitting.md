# Process: Submitting

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). A change being rendered into a form and put in front of people who were not there.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A change, written by somebody who knows what it touches.
At [the queue](place_the_queue.md), on a schedule set by the sitting.

## Direction

Toward a fair account of the change for readers who do not know the system.
This is done honestly and done well: the author wants the review and believes in it.
What survives the translation is the part of a change that can be stated to a stranger, and the risk is usually not that part.

## Lever

Explanation, across a gap in knowledge.
Because the gap was created deliberately and for a good reason, no improvement in the writing removes it: a better form describes the change more clearly to people who still cannot evaluate it.

## Echo

A complete and honest submission,
about a system the readers have never operated.
