## Tension

This is the turn, and it is the reason the mechanism is worst at its worst moment.
The remedy for an outage is itself a change, and a change goes to the board. So the control that did not prevent the failure is now standing in front of the recovery from it. The emergency path exists and is also a procedure: it needs approvers, and approvers are people, and at two in the morning people are asleep.
And the fix waiting is **the best-informed change anybody will write all year**, produced by the people who have just learned precisely what is wrong. It is held by a process designed for changes made in ignorance of any particular fault, applied to the one change made in full knowledge of a specific one. **The delay is longest exactly where delay costs most**, which is why the measured harm shows up in time to restore service.
