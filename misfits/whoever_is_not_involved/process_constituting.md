# Process: Constituting

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). A review body being composed from people with no stake in the work it will review.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The plain fact that an unexamined change in a live service is indefensible.
At [the board room](place_the_board_room.md), by terms of reference.

## Direction

Toward a review that can be trusted, which requires reviewers who do not benefit from the answer.
A reviewer with a stake is not a reviewer: that sentence is true, it is why the arrangement exists, and any responsible organisation would write it.
So the composition is not a compromise or an oversight: it is the correct expression of a sound principle.

## Lever

Independence, deliberately selected for.
Because the knowledge needed to judge a change lives in the particulars of the system, selecting against involvement selects against the knowledge, and the two cannot be separated.

## Echo

A body that can be trusted to review,
made of the only people who cannot evaluate what they are reviewing.
