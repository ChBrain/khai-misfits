# Process: Sitting

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). The board examining what it can see and recording a decision.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The agenda, item by item.
At [the board room](place_the_board_room.md), on schedule.

## Direction

Toward letting nothing through unexamined, which is what the body was built for.
What it can examine is the form: whether it is complete, whether a rollback is described, whether the window is agreed, whether the right names are on it.
Those are real questions and they matter, and they are not the question, and asking them well is from inside the room indistinguishable from doing the job.

## Lever

Scrutiny, applied to what is visible.
Because the risk lives in the system rather than in the paperwork, more scrutiny of the paperwork does not reach it, and nobody present experiences the meeting as theatre.

## Echo

An approval, correctly recorded,
which certifies that the documentation was in order.
