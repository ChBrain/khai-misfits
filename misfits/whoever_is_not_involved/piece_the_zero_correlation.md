# Piece: The Zero Correlation

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). The measurement: external approval against the rate at which changes fail.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Outside every board room, in four years of survey data across the industry.
Read against [the outage](persona_the_outage.md), which arrives regardless.

## Load Bearing

Without it this is an opinion about meetings, and everybody has one.
It is load bearing because of **how strong the negative is**: not a weak relationship between external approval and change failure rate, not a contested one, but none, while the same data finds the arrangement costs lead time, release frequency and time to restore service.

## Apparent

A statistic about software delivery.
It reads as a finding about one industry's working practices, and it is a finding about what a control can be worth when the controller cannot see what it controls.

## Yearbook

Measured across organisations with boards and without them.
It is what tells the difference between a gate that is hard to pass and a gate that catches anything.
