# Pitch: Any Objections, Noted, Approved

## Taxonomy

[Whoever Is Not Involved](play_whoever_is_not_involved.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Procedural, courteous, on time.
The register of a standing meeting with an agenda and minutes: item four, any objections, noted, approved.
Nothing hostile anywhere in it, because everybody in the room is trying to prevent an outage.

## Undertow

Under the agenda, two facts that do not negotiate.
A reviewer with a stake is not a reviewer. The risk in a change lives in the particulars of a system.
Between those two, the room is qualified to sit and unable to judge.

## Nerve

The unease of a well-run meeting,
asking sound questions in good faith about a change nobody present could evaluate,
and recording an approval that means the form was complete.

## Echo

After the run: minutes in good order,
a change failure rate exactly where it would have been anyway,
and an outage lasting longer because the fix was on next Wednesday's agenda.
