# Persona: The Fix Waiting

## Taxonomy

[Waiting](position_waiting.md). The remedy for the outage: written, ready, and itself a change, so it goes to the board.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ready, and correct.
Written by the people who now know exactly what broke, which is the best-informed change anybody will produce all year.
Blocked by the same procedure as everything else, because procedures do not have exceptions that are not written down.

## Action

Is written quickly, by people who have just learned precisely what is wrong.
Is submitted.
Waits, while the service stays down.

## Shadow

The turn of the whole play: the control that failed to prevent the failure is now standing in front of the recovery.
Because the delay is measured in the one number that counts while a service is down, its cost is **largest exactly when the cost of delay is largest**, so the mechanism is worst at its worst moment.
It ends applied, correctly, later than it existed.

## Tell

Says: it is ready, it has been ready since two o'clock.
True, and the emergency procedure requires two approvers who are asleep.
