# Piece: The Work Is the Training

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). The capability is made of cases and is held by a group, so no curriculum contains it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the theatre](place_the_theatre.md), in the anaesthetist who has not seen this go wrong before.
On the ward, on the second night, meeting the complication for the first time.

## Load Bearing

Without it the answer is more training and the play has no difficulty in it.
It is load bearing because **the thing that accumulates is not one person's technique**: it is a group
that has been through the procedure enough times, distributed across theatre and ward and the people who
recognise a deterioration, and that is built out of repetitions rather than instruction.

## Apparent

That a course, a fellowship or a refresher closes the gap.
Each is valuable and none is a case. The training transfers perfectly and was never the constraint.

## Yearbook

The finding that outcomes for a class of complex procedures track annual volume at the level of the
institution, adjusted for case mix, rather than tracking anything measured about the individual operator.
