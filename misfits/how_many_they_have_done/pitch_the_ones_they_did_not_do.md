# Pitch: The Ones They Did Not Do

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact about what the numbers track, and about what they do not.
Unwilling to fault the surgeon or the hospital, because neither has anything to answer for.
Clear that travelling is a real cost, paid by the sick and not by the system that reorganises.

## Undertow

Under the figures, one relation from the evidence and one from how skill works.
For operations of this kind the outcome tracks how many the team has done.
And what is not exercised decays, so four a year is not a slow climb.

## Nerve

The unease of a shortfall that names nobody.
Everyone here is competent, careful, and doing what they should.
The missing thing is the operations they did not do.

## Echo

After the run: a surgeon who would be better only by operating more,
a hospital that loses more than the service if it stops,
a table showing a rate that no case in it points to,
and a centre two counties away that nobody can now afford to lose.
