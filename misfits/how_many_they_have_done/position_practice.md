# Position: Practice

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). The seat that does the operation: trained, careful, and rarely.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A completed training, a maintained registration, and a technique that would satisfy any examiner.
A team around it that is competent and constant.
Four of these a year, spread across twelve months.

## Orders

Do the operation properly.
Keep up with the literature and the technique.
Take the cases that come, because they are the cases there are.

## Loses

The one input it cannot obtain by doing its job well.
**Repetition is not a thing this seat can practise**, because a case belongs to a patient and a patient
belongs to a catchment, so the seat can be excellent at everything within its control and still be short
of the only quantity that moves the outcome.

## Drives

Toward each operation done as well as it can be done,
which it achieves, one at a time, every time,
so the seat with nothing to answer for is the seat the figures are about.
