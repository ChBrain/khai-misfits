# Piece: It Is Not Headcount

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). The shortfall is experience, so adding people does not supply it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Wherever a staffing answer is offered to a volume problem.
At [the catchment](place_the_catchment.md), which sets the number regardless of who is employed.

## Load Bearing

Without it this collapses into the house's **The Ninth Woman** and earns no separate staging.
It is load bearing because **the switch-off runs cleanly**: stipulate that adding people is free and
instantaneous and that coordination costs nothing, and the harm survives entirely, because **nine surgeons
who have each done four is not one surgeon who has done thirty-six**.

## Apparent

That a larger, better-staffed unit is a higher-volume unit.
It is a better-staffed unit performing the same four operations, since the cases come from the population
and not from the establishment.

## Yearbook

The distinction between capacity, which can be recruited, and experience, which is a count of completed
occasions and can only be waited for or taken from somewhere else.
