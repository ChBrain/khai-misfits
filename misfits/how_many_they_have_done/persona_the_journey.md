# Persona: The Journey

## Taxonomy

[Distance](position_distance.md). A hundred and forty miles, and everybody who cannot make it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Concrete, unevenly distributed, and absent from every budget.
Is a morning for some people and an impossibility for others.
Falls hardest on those with the least money, the least mobility and the fewest visitors.

## Action

Is made, by those who can.
Is not made, by those who cannot.
Is never recorded as a reason for either.

## Shadow

That it is a condition of treatment and is counted as a preference.
**A journey is not a line in a service budget**, so the cost of concentration is real, is borne entirely
here, and appears nowhere in the account of the reorganisation that produced it.

## Tell

Is what the remedy costs.
Is paid by the party with no part in choosing it.
