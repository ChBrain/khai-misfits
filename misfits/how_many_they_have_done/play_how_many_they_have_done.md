# Play: How Many They Have Done

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

How Many They Have Done.
A hospital does this operation four times a year. The surgeon trained properly, keeps up, and does each one carefully, and everybody who has watched them work would say so.
A centre two counties away does two hundred, and its results are better, by more than the difference in the patients explains.
Nothing about the local surgeon is deficient. The thing that is missing is the operations they did not do.

## Arc

The bend is this: the capability is made of cases, the cases are what a small service does not have, and the only way to get them is to take them from somewhere.

It runs on one relation from the evidence and one from how skill works. **For a class of complex procedures the outcome tracks the number done**, at the level of the institution rather than only the operator; and **what is not exercised decays**, so a team doing four a year is not accumulating slowly, it is running to stay level.

Take the local service first, because the play has to establish that there is nothing here to correct. The surgeon is competent and would be improved only by doing more of them. The referring clinician sends the patient to the nearest capable service, which is what patients ask for and what the system rewards. The hospital keeps the service because withdrawing it takes the emergency cover, the training rotation and the staff who came for the work, so a hospital that cannot do this operation is a smaller hospital in ways that reach patients who never needed it.

Then the fact that inverts it. **The training is the doing.** More courses do not close the gap, because what is missing is not instruction: it is the anaesthetist who has seen this go wrong before, the nurse who knows where the equipment is, and the ward that recognises the complication on the second night. That is held by a group rather than a person, is made of repetitions, and cannot be issued by a curriculum.

Then the sharper half, which is why nothing in the room can see it. **The harm arrives as a difference in a rate.** No case goes wrong in a way that points at volume. The complication caught a night later, the decision taken by somebody who had not seen it before, the equipment fetched from another floor: each is an ordinary piece of a hard operation, and the pattern exists only in a table nobody present has read.

And nobody has done anything wrong. Every party is right on its own terms and the terms do not meet.

Reach for a settlement and each one gives back the thing that was wanted. Set a minimum volume, which works, and you close services in the places least able to travel. Build a network with visiting teams, which is real, and the team that visits accumulates the volume while the local one still does not. Add simulation and proctoring, which help, and they are not the case. And concentrate successfully, which is the answer the evidence points at, and you have built a centre so good that losing it is unthinkable, which is the other play on this dial arriving as the price of this one's remedy.

## Triggers

**[Four a Year](plot_four_a_year.md)**
The local service established as legitimate and the surgeon as competent: trained properly, keeping up, doing each one carefully, and supported by a hospital with real reasons to hold the service. Closes when it is clear there is nothing here anybody would correct.

**[The Number That Should Not Matter](plot_the_number_that_should_not_matter.md)**

**[There Is No Course for It](plot_there_is_no_course_for_it.md)**

**[And the Other Way Round](plot_and_the_other_way_round.md)**
Cue: the settlements, and the centre that got excellent. Closes when each escape is found to give back what it was for, and when the successful concentration is recognised as the house's **The Bus Factor** arriving as the price of this play's remedy.

## Stakes

Whether a capability made of repetitions can be held anywhere it is needed.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the surgeon is good, because they are, and not whether the hospital is right to want the service, because it is, but whether a competence that exists only in the doing can be distributed to the places that need it without being destroyed in the distribution. The production cannot settle it. It can only show that the training is the doing, that what is not done decays, that no case points at the cause, that concentration works and is paid for by the sick, and that a centre good enough to justify the journey is a centre nobody can afford to lose.
