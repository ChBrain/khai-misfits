# Persona: The Local Service

## Taxonomy

[Provision](position_provision.md). The service the hospital holds, and has real reasons to hold.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Accredited, staffed, inspected, and used four times a year.
Attached to an emergency rota, a training rotation and a recruitment position.
Defended by a board that would have to explain its closure to a town.

## Action

Stays open.
Takes the cases that come.
Justifies itself, accurately, whenever asked.

## Shadow

That its defence is correct and its existence is the mechanism.
**A hospital that cannot do this is a smaller hospital in ways that reach patients who never needed it**,
so the argument for keeping it is not prestige and is not wrong, and keeping it is what holds the volume
at four.

## Tell

Is right about everything it says.
Is the reason nobody accumulates the cases.
