## Tension

The finding is the one thing in the play that nobody in the play can act on.
For procedures of this kind the outcome tracks the annual volume, adjusted for the patients, and it does so
at the level of the **institution** rather than the operator. So the figures are not an accusation about a
surgeon, and reading them as one is the first mistake available.
Nothing about any individual explains it, which is why the board hears it as unfair. Every person named in
the service is competent, and the service is accredited on every attribute an accreditation examines.
And **nothing in a referral names volume.** There is no field for it, no habit of asking, and no party
whose job is to notice, so the quantity that decides the outcome is set one correct letter at a time by
people who are optimising the attributes they can see.
The number is produced by geography and disease. It is not a target, and there is no version of doing
better locally that raises it.
