# Plot: Four a Year

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A district hospital, a properly accredited service, and a case on the list for February.

## Action

[Operating](process_operating.md): [The Surgeon](persona_the_surgeon.md), in [the practice seat](position_practice.md), works through [Do It Properly Every Time](plan_do_it_properly_every_time.md).

## Stage

[The Theatre](place_the_theatre.md): a properly equipped room and a properly prepared team.

## Tension

The play has to establish first that there is nothing here anybody would correct.
The surgeon trained properly, keeps up, prepares each case fully and operates unhurriedly, and would
satisfy any examiner who watched it. The team is competent and constant. The checklist is completed and the
audit is honest.
The referral was correct too: the local service is accredited and staffed, the patient asked to be treated
near home, and creating an unnecessary journey would have been a harm rather than a caution.
And the hospital is right to hold the service, which is the part most easily mistaken for vanity. The
emergency cover, the training rotation and the recruitment position are attached to it, so **a hospital
that cannot do this operation is a smaller hospital in ways that reach patients who never needed it**.
Four a year is what a district of this size produces. Nobody chose it and nobody inside the hospital can
change it.
