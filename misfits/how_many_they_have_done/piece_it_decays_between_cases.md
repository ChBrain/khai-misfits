# Piece: It Decays Between Cases

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). What was learned drains away in the gaps, so a low volume is a level and not a slow climb.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the list](place_the_list.md), in the months between February and May.
In every argument that a small service will get there eventually.

## Load Bearing

Without it four a year is thirty-six in nine years and patience is the answer.
It is load bearing because **organisational learning depreciates rather than persisting as a stock**, so
each gap returns part of what the last case bought and the team settles at the level its rate supports.
Waiting does not accumulate.

## Apparent

That experience, once gained, is kept.
It is kept by the person as a memory and lost by the group as a practice, and the group is where the
capability lives.

## Yearbook

The industrial finding that accumulated learning depreciates markedly when production is interrupted,
which is what makes an interval a cost rather than a pause.
