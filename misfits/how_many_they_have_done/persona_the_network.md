# Persona: The Network

## Taxonomy

[Recourse](position_recourse.md). The managed arrangement that is supposed to give capability without moving anybody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sensible, well-designed, and genuinely better than nothing.
Takes the form of visiting teams, shared rotas, joint lists, proctoring and simulation.
Aimed precisely at the gap, by people who understand it.

## Action

Sends the experienced team to the small site.
Runs the case there, with the local team assisting.
Leaves.

## Shadow

That it routes the repetitions rather than creating them.
**The team that visits accumulates the volume and the local one still does not**, so a network that
shares the work between sites has, by construction, given nobody enough of it.

## Tell

Is the answer that keeps the service open.
Is the answer that keeps the number at four.
