## Tension

Here the answer everybody reaches for is removed, and it is removed gently, because it is a good answer to
a different question.
More training does not close the gap. The training transferred perfectly and was never the constraint.
What is missing is the anaesthetist who has seen this go wrong before, the nurse who knows where the
equipment is, and the ward that recognises the complication on the second night, and none of that is
conferred by a course, a fellowship or an appointment. **It is held by a group and it is made of cases.**
And [It Decays Between Cases](piece_it_decays_between_cases.md), which removes patience as well. Four a
year is not thirty-six in nine years: learning of this kind depreciates when the work stops, so each gap
returns part of what the last case bought and the team settles at the level its rate supports.
So the small service is not climbing slowly toward the centre. It is level, and it is level at four.
