# Position: Referral

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). The seat that decides where a patient's case goes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A patient who would rather be treated near home, and usually says so.
A local service that is accredited, staffed and available.
A pathway that rewards keeping the referral in the area.

## Orders

Send the patient to the nearest capable service.
Do not create a journey where one is not needed.
Respect the preference, which is informed and is theirs.

## Loses

Any sight of the quantity that decides the outcome.
**Nothing in the referral names volume.** The local service is accredited, the surgeon is qualified, and
the annual number is not on any form this seat handles, so the decision is made correctly on every
attribute that is visible and blind to the one that is not.

## Drives

Toward care close to home,
which is what patients ask for and what the system counts as good,
so the seat that honours the preference is the seat that keeps the volume low.
