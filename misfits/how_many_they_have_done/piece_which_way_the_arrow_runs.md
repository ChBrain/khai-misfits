# Piece: Which Way the Arrow Runs

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). The causal direction is contested, and the play carries the objection rather than settling it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In every argument about whether a low-volume service should close.
In the reviews, where the relation is strong for some procedures, weak for others and absent for many.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play asserts more than the literature does.
It is load bearing because **selective referral is a serious explanation for part of the relation**: good
centres attract work, so outcome may cause volume as well as the reverse, and a production that hid that
would be staging a settled finding where the field has an argument.

## Apparent

That the objection dissolves the play.
It bounds it. Whichever way the arrow runs, the local team is short of repetitions and the parties who
could change that are each acting correctly, which is the structure regardless of the coefficient.

## Yearbook

The long-running dispute between practice-makes-perfect and selective-referral readings of the same
correlation, and the reviews reporting the relation as procedure-specific rather than general.
