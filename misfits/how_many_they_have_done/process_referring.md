# Process: Referring

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). Sending the case to the nearest capable service.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A diagnosis, and a patient who would rather stay near home.
At [the catchment](place_the_catchment.md), case by case, all year.

## Direction

Toward care that can be attended without disruption.
This is correct: the local service is accredited and staffed, the patient's preference is informed and is
theirs, and creating an unnecessary journey would be a harm rather than a caution.

## Lever

Every visible attribute of the service, all of which are satisfactory.
Volume is not among them, is on no form, and is not a thing anybody in this process has been asked about.

## Echo

A patient treated locally, as they wished,
a service that stays viable because it is used,
and an annual number that stays where the geography put it.
