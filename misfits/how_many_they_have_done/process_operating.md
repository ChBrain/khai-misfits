# Process: Operating

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). Doing the operation, properly, on the day.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A patient on the list and a team prepared for them.
At [the theatre](place_the_theatre.md), four times a year.

## Direction

Toward the operation done as well as it can be done.
This is done well and by people who know what they are doing: prepared fully, performed unhurriedly,
audited afterwards, and it would satisfy any examiner who watched it.

## Lever

Technique, preparation and care, all of which are already at their maximum.
There is no lever here for the quantity that matters, because that quantity is the number of previous
occasions and it is behind rather than ahead.

## Echo

A good operation,
a team a little more experienced than yesterday,
and three months until the next one.
