# Process: Accumulating

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). A team becoming good at a thing by doing it repeatedly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A case, then another, then another, close enough together to build on each other.
At [the theatre](place_the_theatre.md), wherever the volume is high enough for this to run.

## Direction

Toward a group that has been through the thing enough times.
What accumulates is not one person's technique: it is the anaesthetist who has seen this go wrong, the
nurse who knows where the equipment is, and the ward that recognises the complication on the second night.

## Lever

Repetition, and nothing else.
No curriculum contains it, no appointment confers it, and no amount of care substitutes for it, which is
why the process runs at some hospitals and not at others regardless of who works there.

## Echo

Capability held by a group rather than a person,
made of cases,
and therefore made of a quantity the group does not control.
