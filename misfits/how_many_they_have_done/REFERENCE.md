# How Many They Have Done: Reference

The research warrant for How Many They Have Done.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the documented relation between the volume of a procedure and
its outcomes, on the learning that accumulates with repetition and depreciates without it, and on the
contested causal direction, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging a local service as **a competence that cannot be reached from where it is offered**: a hospital
performs a complex operation four times a year, by a surgeon who trained properly, keeps up, and does each
one carefully; a centre two counties away performs it two hundred times; and the results differ, in the
direction the numbers say and by more than case mix explains. Nothing about the local surgeon is deficient
and nothing about the local team is careless. **The thing that is missing is the operations they did not
do.** The play models the documented mechanics, the volume that is the training rather than a proxy for
it, the capability that decays in the months between cases, the referral that is made locally for good
reasons, the hospital that keeps the service because a hospital that cannot do it loses more than the
service, and the journey that concentration converts into a cost on the sick, as the load-bearing vectors
of the structure, not as a tale of a bad surgeon or a vain institution.

The governing law is that for a class of complex procedures, the outcome tracks how many of them the team
has done, and it does so at the level of the institution rather than only the operator: the anaesthetist
who has seen this go wrong before, the nurse who knows where the equipment is, the ward that recognises
the complication on the second night. **Capability of this kind is not held by a person and cannot be
issued by a curriculum.** It is a property of a group that has been through the thing enough times, which
is a way of saying it is made of cases, and cases are what a small service does not have.

What makes it structural rather than a fault is that the capability **decays**. A skill that is not
exercised depreciates, and a team that does four a year spends most of its year not doing it, so it is not
accumulating slowly, it is running to stay level. That is why more training does not close the gap: the
training is the doing, the doing is the volume, and the volume is exactly what the local service does not
have and cannot manufacture without taking cases from somebody else.

What removes any possibility of blame is that every party is right on its own terms and the terms do not
meet. The surgeon is competent, is doing the operation properly, and would be improved only by doing more
of them. The referring clinician sends the patient to the nearest capable service, which is what a patient
usually asks for and what a system usually rewards. The hospital keeps the service because withdrawing it
takes the emergency cover, the training rotation and the staff who came for the work; a hospital that
cannot do this operation is a smaller hospital in ways that reach the patients who never needed it. And
the patient wants it done here, near the people who will visit.

And the harm arrives as a difference in a rate rather than as an event anybody witnesses. No case goes
wrong in a way that points at volume. The complication that was caught a night later, the decision taken
by somebody who had not seen it before, the equipment fetched from another floor: each is an ordinary
piece of a hard operation, and the only place the pattern exists is in a table nobody in the room has read.

It runs against the house's **The Bus Factor**, on the same axis and with the opposite sign, and the pair
is as tight as this mechanism produces: **each play's remedy is the other play's harm.** There, knowledge
routing to whoever already holds it hollows out everybody else and leaves an institution that cannot
survive their leaving, so the answer is to spread the work. Here, spreading the work is what stops anybody
accumulating enough of it to be good at it, and the answer is to concentrate. Neither answer is wrong and
they cannot both be taken.

**What sets the sign on this axis is what the concentration is asked to produce.** Where the obligation is
**capability**, doing the difficult thing well on the day, routing the work to whoever already does it is
what produces it, and spreading it is the harm. Where the obligation is **continuity**, surviving the loss
of a person or a site, the same routing is what makes the institution fragile, and concentrating is the
harm. Same routing decision, two obligations, and every organisation holds both.

It is distinct from the house's **Only as Good as the Worst**, which stages task complementarity: there
the quantity is how far the tasks depend on one another, so a single weak part destroys the value of the
whole. Stipulate the tasks fully separable, each step independently valuable, and this harm survives
entirely, because it is not about one weak link in a chain: it is about how many times one team has done
one thing.

It is distinct from the house's **The Ninth Woman**, which stages Brooks's law on `added-capacity`: there
adding people to late work makes it later, through communication and ramp-up. Stipulate that adding people
is free and instantaneous and that coordination costs nothing, and this harm survives, because **the
shortfall is experience and not headcount**: nine surgeons who have each done four is not one surgeon who
has done thirty-six.

It is distinct from the house's **Ready on the First Day**, which stages training specificity: there what
is taught is too particular to transfer. Here the training transfers perfectly and is not the constraint;
what is missing is the repetitions after it, which no course contains.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the local
  surgeon as inadequate or the local hospital as vain, because neither is: the surgeon trained properly,
  keeps up, and does each operation carefully, and would be improved only by doing more of them; the
  hospital keeps the service because withdrawing it takes the emergency cover, the training rotation and
  the staff who came for the work, so a hospital that cannot do this is a smaller hospital in ways that
  reach patients who never needed the operation; and the referring clinician sends the patient to the
  nearest capable service, which is what patients ask for. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges
  from the structure, so the misfit is that the missing thing is the cases that were not done.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. A minimum-volume
  standard works and closes services, which removes them from the places least able to travel. A managed
  network with visiting teams is real and partial, since the team that visits accumulates the volume and
  the local one still does not. Simulation and proctoring help and are not the same as the case. And
  concentrating successfully produces a centre so good that losing it is unthinkable, which is the other
  play on this axis arriving as the price of this one's remedy.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the health services research and the economics of
  learning are theirs. The bounds are carried with them and they are wide. **The causal direction is
  contested**: selective referral, in which better centres attract more work, is a serious explanation for
  part of the relation, and the play stages a mechanism that is supported and disputed rather than
  established. **The relation is a property of particular complex procedures**, strong for some, weak for
  others and absent for many, so nothing here licenses a general claim about low-volume services. **The
  magnitudes those sources report are deliberately not carried here**, because the papers themselves could
  not be read in the pass that cited them: publisher hosts and the article archives are blocked to it, so
  the bibliography, the findings and their directions were verified by search and corroborated across
  independent reports, which is the treatment a citation survives and a reported figure is not. **The play
  makes no recommendation about any service**, since the trade it stages is between capability and access
  and the weighing of those belongs to the people who would travel.
