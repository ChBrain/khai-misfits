## Tension

Each escape gives back the thing it was for.
Set a minimum volume, which works, and you close services in the towns least able to travel, so the
capability arrives exactly where the access leaves.
Build a network with visiting teams, which is real and better than nothing, and **the team that visits
accumulates the volume and the local one still does not**: a network that shares the work between sites
has, by construction, given nobody enough of it.
Add simulation and proctoring, which help, and they are not the case.
And succeed. Concentrate properly, and the centre becomes so good that its results justify every journey
made to it, and at that point the whole region depends on one site, one team and one rota. That is the
house's **The Bus Factor**, arriving as the price of this play's remedy: the same routing decision, the
same dial, and the other obligation.
**Spread the work and nobody accumulates enough of it to be good at it. Concentrate it and the institution
cannot survive their leaving.** Neither answer is wrong. They cannot both be taken.
