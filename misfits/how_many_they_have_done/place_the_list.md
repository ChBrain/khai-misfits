## Shown

## Holds

The decay, which is invisible because it is an absence spread evenly.
**Three months of not doing it looks exactly like three months of a full and productive list**, so the
thing that is being lost is lost during the periods that look most like competent work.

## Withheld

No system counts months since last performed, and no rota is built to shorten them.
