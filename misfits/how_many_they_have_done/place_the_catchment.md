# Place: The Catchment

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). The population the hospital serves, and the reason the number is four.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A map, a population, and an incidence rate.
Four cases a year, arithmetically, from a district of this size.
Nothing anybody has done wrong to produce that figure.

## Holds

The constraint that no effort inside the hospital can reach.
**The volume is set by the geography and the disease**, so it is not a target that better management
raises: increasing it means taking cases from another catchment, which is somebody else's service and
somebody else's town.

## Offers

A population properly served, on every measure that counts services.

## Withheld

Any route from four to forty that does not empty a map somewhere else.
