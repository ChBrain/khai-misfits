# Place: The Theatre

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). Where the operation happens, correctly, four times a year.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A properly equipped room and a properly prepared team.
A checklist completed, an anaesthetist who has read the notes, a scrub nurse who has laid out the set.
Everybody doing the thing they were trained to do.

## Holds

The whole difficulty, in a form nothing in the room can register.
**Every element here is present and correct and some of them are new to this.** The anaesthetist has not
seen this particular thing go wrong, the ward will meet the complication for the first time, and neither
absence is visible while the operation is going well.

## Offers

The operation, competently performed.

## Withheld

Any signal about what a hundredth time would look like.
There is no comparison available in the room, because the only comparison is with a place nobody here has
worked in.
