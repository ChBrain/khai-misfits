# Process: Decaying

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). What was learned draining away in the months between cases.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Nothing. It runs whenever the work is not being done.
At [the list](place_the_list.md), through February to May and May to September.

## Direction

Toward the level the current rate supports.
Organisational learning depreciates rather than persisting as a stock, so a team doing four a year is not
climbing slowly, it is running to stay level, and each gap gives back part of what the last case bought.

## Lever

The interval, which is set by the catchment.
Shortening it means more cases, more cases mean somebody else's catchment, and there is no lever inside
the hospital at all.

## Echo

A team that is as good as four a year makes it,
which is a stable level and not a slow climb,
and a gap between here and the centre that does not close by waiting.
