# Process: Concentrating

## Taxonomy

[How Many They Have Done](play_how_many_they_have_done.md). Routing the cases to one place so that the place gets good at them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A review, a standard, or a commissioner reading the same table.
At [the catchment](place_the_catchment.md), across several of them at once.

## Direction

Toward a centre with enough cases to be excellent.
This works. It is what the evidence points at, it produces the capability it was aimed at, and the centre
that results is genuinely better at the thing than any of the services it replaced.

## Lever

Where the cases are sent, which is the only lever anybody in the play holds.
Pulling it moves the repetitions to one site and the journeys to everybody else.

## Echo

A centre that is excellent,
a set of towns that no longer offer the operation and are smaller hospitals for it,
and one site whose loss has become unthinkable.
