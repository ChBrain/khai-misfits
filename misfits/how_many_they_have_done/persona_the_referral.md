# Persona: The Referral

## Taxonomy

[Referral](position_referral.md). The letter that sends the case to the nearest capable service.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Correct on every attribute it can see: accreditation, staffing, waiting time, distance.
Written by somebody who knows the patient and asked what they wanted.
Rewarded, by the system, for keeping the case local.

## Action

Names the local service.
Books the appointment.
Closes.

## Shadow

That the deciding attribute is not on the form.
**Nothing in a referral names volume**, so a decision made correctly on everything visible is blind to
the one quantity the outcome tracks, and there is no field to put it in and no habit of asking.

## Tell

Is the party doing what the patient asked for.
Is where the volume is set, one letter at a time.
