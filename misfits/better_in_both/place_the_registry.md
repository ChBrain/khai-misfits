# Place: The Registry

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). Where the staging rule meets the submitted finding and every case is filed onto a rung. Hosts [Survival, By Stage](plot_survival_by_stage.md) and [The Ones Who Move](plot_the_ones_who_move.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A long, orderly record: cases, stages, dates, outcomes, kept to a published rule and auditable case by case.

## Holds

[The Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md), the fixed rungs against which everything is reported, and [The Migrants](piece_the_migrants.md), the cases that cross a rung when newly-resolved spread is entered.

## Offers

To the field, the severity-controlled comparison that makes outcomes readable at all, since a pooled figure would track the mix of stages rather than the medicine.

## Withheld

Any field for the instrument, so a stage entered this year and one entered a decade ago are stored identically, and two eras leave the registry looking like one continuous series.
