# Better in Both: Reference

The research warrant for **Better in Both**, a staging of the phenomenon the literature calls the **Will
Rogers phenomenon**, or **stage migration** (Feinstein, Sosin and Wells): that where outcomes are
reported against a fixed ladder of categories and the instrument assigning cases to those categories
improves, the cases that migrate upward are by construction the worst of the category they leave and the
best of the category they join, so every category's average outcome improves at once though no
individual's course has changed, and a comparison across the two eras reads the arithmetic as medical
progress. It is held with the finding that the effect is conditional on stage-specific comparison across
a change in detection, and that it lifts where whole-cohort mortality is counted instead or the earlier
cohort is restaged under the newer rule, neither of them free.

## Line of Work

Staging the Will Rogers phenomenon as a **reclassification-lifts-every-group structure**: cancer is
sorted into stages by extent of spread, and survival is reported stage by stage, because that is the
only way to compare like with like, a stage I case and a stage IV case not being the same case, and a
single pooled figure tracking the mix of stages rather than the medicine. The ladder is therefore the
right yardstick and the whole field rightly uses it. Then imaging improves: a new technology resolves
deposits the old could not, so metastases that were always present but invisible are found, and a
patient who a decade ago would have been staged I is now staged II or III, correctly. No one has erred.
The radiologist reports disease genuinely present, the registrar applies the current staging rule to the
evidence before it, and the classification is more truthful than it was, not less. But the migrating
cases are not a random sample: having hidden spread makes them the sickest members of the stage they
leave, the ones who would have died soonest and dragged its average down, and having spread slight
enough to require a new machine to see makes them the healthiest members of the stage they join. So they
leave the bottom of one distribution and arrive at the top of another, and both averages rise together,
every rung of the ladder improvable at once by the single movement, with no patient living a day longer
and no treatment working better (Feinstein, Sosin and Wells). The play models the documented mechanics:
the fixed ladder that makes stage-specific reporting correct, the improved instrument, the non-random
migration and its double-edged position, the simultaneous lift of both averages, the comparison across
eras whose stage labels no longer mean the same thing, and the whole-cohort mortality count that alone
resists the artefact, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, and not as a claim that cancer
statistics are falsified or that better imaging is a mistake. The ordinary expectation is that a
stage-specific table controls for severity and so isolates the medicine; the finding is that it does so
only while the instrument assigning the stages holds still. The governing law is that **where cases are
reported against fixed categories and the assignment instrument improves, the migrating cases are the
worst of the group they leave and the best of the group they join, so every group's average improves at
once while nothing about any individual changes, and the better the new instrument the larger the
apparent gain**.

It is a statistical-artefact structure no staged misfit holds, and it is carefully distinct from its
nearest neighbours. It is distinct from The Bigger Picture, Simpson's paradox, where a trend holding in
every subgroup reverses in the pooled whole because a confounder is spread unevenly across the groups:
there the mechanism is **pooling versus stratifying one fixed population**, and the signature is a
**reversal** of direction, whereas here nothing is pooled and nothing reverses, the population is
restratified rather than aggregated, no confounder is required, and every group moves the same way, all
of them up. It is distinct from It Saved My Life, the popularity paradox of screening, where the
overdiagnosed credit the test with their lives and become the constituency that expands it: there the
engine is a **feedback loop** running through gratitude and advocacy into demand, and overdiagnosis is
the root, whereas here there is no loop, no advocacy, and no overdiagnosis at all, since the metastases
the sharper scanner finds are real disease correctly identified, and the whole effect is one pass of
arithmetic across a category boundary. It is distinct from Prevalence-Induced Concept Change, where a
category's **definition** drifts as instances of it grow scarce, so the same case is judged differently
over time: there the boundary moves and the cases stand still, whereas here the boundary is fixed and
published and the cases move across it, the exact inverse. And it is distinct from The Survivors, where
a sample is biased by attrition and an analyst reads a false lesson from the residue: there units
**leave the data**, whereas here nobody leaves, every case remains counted, and the distortion is
produced entirely by which column each one is counted in.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast a
  falsifying registry, a credulous oncologist, or a scanner salesman, because the finding it stages is
  that each party acts correctly: the radiologist reports disease that is genuinely present and would be
  negligent to omit it, the registrar applies the current staging rule to the evidence in front of it,
  and the oncologist compares stage against stage because that is the yardstick the literature uses and
  the only one that compares like with like (Feinstein, Sosin and Wells). The improved classification is
  a real improvement, not an error introduced. The loss is authored by no one: it is a fixed ladder of
  categories read across two eras of measurement, so both averages rise on arithmetic alone, and the
  better the new instrument the larger the apparent gain.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. The artefact can be
  defeated, but not for free: counting whole-cohort mortality across everyone diagnosed is immune to
  reclassification, since a patient moved between stages is still in the total, but it is slower, less
  granular, and less flattering than the stage-specific figure it replaces, and it answers a different
  question than the one clinicians want answered; and restaging the earlier cohort under the newer rule
  makes the two tables commensurable but requires retrieving decades-old imaging that was never made to
  resolve what the new rule asks of it, and often simply cannot (Welch, Schwartz and Woloshin). The
  reckoning is that the honest comparison is the one no stage-specific reporting system is built to
  produce.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The phenomenon, its mechanism in cancer staging, and its naming after Rogers's
  line are Feinstein, Sosin, and Wells's; the line itself is Will Rogers's and is public domain, credited
  and not claimed; and the wider caution that survival is the wrong endpoint where detection has changed,
  with population mortality as the count that resists it, is Welch, Schwartz, and Woloshin's. The scope
  is held to outcome comparison across a change in the assignment instrument, and not stretched into a
  claim that stage-specific survival is generally meaningless, that improvements in cancer outcomes are
  generally artefactual, or that better imaging is not worth having, since the staging it produces is
  genuinely more accurate and the artefact arises only in the comparison across eras, not in the
  classification itself.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per element, one per load-bearing vector, no filler.

- **The Ladder**: carries the fixed categories (Feinstein, Sosin and Wells), the stages by extent of
  spread against which outcomes are reported, correct to use and correct to report against, the structure
  the whole trap requires in order to have anything to migrate across.
- **The Sharper Eye**: carries the improved instrument (Feinstein, Sosin and Wells), the new imaging that
  resolves deposits the old could not, making the classification genuinely more truthful and supplying
  the one change the trap needs.
- **The Migrants**: carries the non-random movement (Feinstein, Sosin and Wells), the patients who cross
  a rung upward and who are, by construction, the sickest of the stage they leave and the healthiest of
  the stage they join, the double-edged position that is the whole engine.
- **The Double Lift**: carries the arithmetic (Feinstein, Sosin and Wells; Will Rogers), both averages
  rising from the single movement, the lower stage improved by shedding its worst and the upper by
  gaining better than its own, every rung liftable at once.
- **The Empty Progress**: carries the phenomenon whole (Welch, Schwartz and Woloshin), improvement real
  in the table and absent from the world, no patient living a day longer and no treatment working better,
  with a protocol or a machine liable to be credited for it.
- **The Boundary**: carries the condition and the hedge (Welch, Schwartz and Woloshin), that the trap
  needs stage-specific comparison across a change in detection, and lifts where whole-cohort mortality is
  counted, which no reclassification can touch, or the earlier cohort is restaged under the newer rule,
  the first less flattering and the second often impossible.
