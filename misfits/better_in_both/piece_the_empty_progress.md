# Piece: The Empty Progress

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). Improvement real in the table and absent from the world: no patient living a day longer, no treatment working better, the same people dying at the same times.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Standing unremarked beneath the slide at [The Podium](place_the_podium.md), invisible in a comparison that has no column for it, and detectable only from outside the stage tables entirely (Welch, Schwartz and Woloshin).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit dissolves into a technicality: an artefact that flattered a number while the medicine also genuinely improved would be a measurement quibble, worth a footnote and no more.
It bears the load as the actual cost, and the cost is not confined to the page. A protocol can be credited with a gain it did not produce and adopted on that strength; a scanner can be justified by survival figures that are its own artefact; historical comparison becomes untrustworthy in the one direction everyone wants it to point; and a patient staged III today under a rule that would have said stage I a decade ago may be treated for the stage rather than for the disease.

## Apparent

Nothing at all, which is its whole difficulty: it has no representation anywhere in the tables, and shows only as the absence of a change that was never there.

## Yearbook

Stands beneath the close of [Progress on Paper](plot_progress_on_paper.md), the announced advance with nothing inside it.
It works together with [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the emptiness and the only count that can reveal it.
