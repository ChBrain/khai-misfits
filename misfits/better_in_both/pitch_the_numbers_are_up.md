# Pitch: The Numbers Are Up

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Clinical and progressive.
A better scanner installed, disease found more accurately than a decade ago, staging more truthful than it was, and survival figures improving at every stage on the chart.
Nothing false in it: the imaging really is better, the staging really is more accurate, and stage-by-stage reporting really is the right way to compare like with like.

## Undertow

Under the rising figures, a migration doing all the work.
The patients moved up a rung are the sickest of the stage they left and the healthiest of the stage they joined, so subtracting them lifts one average and adding them lifts the other, and every rung improves at once with no patient living a day longer (Feinstein, Sosin and Wells).
No fraud and no error: the radiologist reports real disease, the registrar applies the current rule, and the oncologist uses the yardstick the literature uses.

## Nerve

The reassurance of a table improving across the board, and the catch that across the board is exactly the tell:
that the same accuracy which makes this decade's staging truer is what makes it incomparable with last decade's, so the improved classification and the inflated figures are the one migration seen twice,
and the numbers are up is not the medicine working but the arithmetic of moving people between the columns they are counted in.

## Echo

After the run: a protocol credited with a gain it did not produce, a scanner justified by survival figures that are its own artefact, and a patient given the treatment its new stage warrants for the disease its old stage described (Welch, Schwartz and Woloshin).
And the remedy, at a cost and often out of reach: count whole-cohort mortality, which no reclassification can touch, slower and less flattering than the figure it replaces, or restage the old cohort under the new rule, which needs decades-old imaging that was never made to answer the question.
And the bound held in view, because the artefact is real and conditional at once: it needs stage-specific comparison across a change in detection, so the whole of it is that you can improve every group at once by moving people between them and improve nothing at all.
