# Position: Radiologist

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). The seat that reads the new imaging and reports the disease the old machine could not resolve.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An instrument that resolves smaller deposits than the one it replaced.
A duty to report what is present, with no licence to withhold a visible finding.
No view downstream of the report, and no field in which to record which machine produced it.

## Orders

Report what you see: record every deposit the imaging resolves, since a visible metastasis omitted is a falsification and a clinical failure at once.
Do not read to an older standard for the sake of comparability, because a reader who suppressed real findings to keep this year's series matching last year's would be doing the one genuinely wrong thing available here.
Accept what the seat does: it supplies accuracy, and accuracy is not a thing a reader may decline to supply.

## Loses

Nothing it can be asked to keep, and that is the shape of it.
Because the seat's only duty is to the image in front of it, and that duty is discharged perfectly, it loses nothing at all in its own terms, and the cost it creates lands entirely in a comparison it never makes and cannot see.
It keeps a clean record and loses only the commensurability of two tables it was never asked to reconcile.

## Drives

Toward the one whose plain improvement is the hinge the whole artefact turns on,
reporting truthfully because reporting truthfully is the entire job and, by doing so, moving patients across a line that has not itself moved,
so the seat that only did well by seeing better is the seat through which two decades of tables stop meaning the same thing, correct in every reading and decisive in their sum.
