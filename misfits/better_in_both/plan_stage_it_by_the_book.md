# Plan: Stage It by the Book

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the registrar's seat](position_registrar.md), inside [Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Registrar](position_registrar.md)

## Direction

Apply the current staging rule to the finding submitted, uniformly, case after case. Sort by extent of spread as the system directs, and file what the evidence supports and nothing beyond it. Do not stage a visible metastasis as though it were invisible in order to protect a series, since that would be entering a known falsehood into the permanent record. Record the stage, which is the field the ladder provides, and accept that there is no field for the instrument that resolved it.

## Orders

([The Registrar](persona_the_registrar.md) sorts onto [the Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md) at [the Registry](place_the_registry.md), and through [Migrating](process_migrating.md) files [the Migrants](piece_the_migrants.md) a rung higher than the same case would have gone before.)

## Implementation

([The Registry](place_the_registry.md) is where the rule meets the finding; [Migrating](process_migrating.md) carries a case up a rung when newly-resolved spread is entered against a boundary that has not itself moved, so the record stays uniform within its era and diverges quietly from the era before.)

## Targets

- [ ] the current rule is applied to every case, uniformly and without exception
- [ ] no case is staged against an older rule to protect the continuity of a series
- [ ] the entry records the stage the evidence supports, and nothing beyond it
- [ ] consistency within the era is held, that being the whole of what the seat can offer
