# Persona: The Registrar

## Taxonomy

[Registrar](position_registrar.md). The keeper of the ladder, sorting each case onto a rung by the current rule and the evidence in front of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Exact and impartial, the keeper of a record that does not editorialise, applying a published rule to a submitted finding.
Sorts by extent of spread, as the staging system directs, and files what the evidence supports and nothing beyond it.
No judgement on show: it does not decide what disease a patient has, only which rung the reported disease places them on.

## Action

Runs [Stage It by the Book](plan_stage_it_by_the_book.md): at [the Registry](place_the_registry.md) it sorts each case onto [the Ladder](piece_the_ladder.md) by the current rule, and through [Migrating](process_migrating.md) files [the Migrants](piece_the_migrants.md) a rung higher than the same case would have been filed a decade ago.
Cannot apply an older rule to a newer finding, since the rule it holds is the current one and staging a visible metastasis as though it were invisible would be plainly wrong.
Does what a registry is for: it applies the rule consistently, and consistency within an era is exactly what makes the record incomparable across eras.

## Shadow

Not careless: every case is filed correctly, the rule is applied uniformly, and an auditor checking any single record against the evidence would find it right.
Because the ladder's rungs are fixed labels while the evidence assigned to them has improved, its faithful filing is what carries patients across a boundary that never moved, the record more accurate and less comparable in the same stroke.
It is the most exact actor in the play and the one through whom the migration passes.

## Tell

Reports a stage distribution shifting upward year on year and reads it as the population being staged more accurately, which it is.
Cannot mark the record with the instrument that produced it, since the staging field records a stage and not the machine that resolved it, so the two eras leave the registry looking like one continuous series.
