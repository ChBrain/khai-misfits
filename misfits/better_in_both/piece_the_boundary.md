## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overclaims: without the boundary it would read as a charge that cancer statistics are generally false or that better imaging is a mistake, when the classification the sharper instrument produces is genuinely more accurate and the artefact arises only in the comparison across eras, never in the staging itself.
It bears the load as the honest limit and the price list together. The remedy exists and is exact, since a figure that never stratified cannot be moved by restratification, but it costs precisely the resolution the ladder was built to supply, and the alternative, restaging the old cohort, needs imaging that was never made to answer the question and frequently cannot.
