# Process: Migrating

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). Borne at [The Registry](place_the_registry.md), precipitated by [The Registrar](persona_the_registrar.md) entering newly-resolved spread against a rung that has not moved, in [The Ones Who Move](plot_the_ones_who_move.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A finding more detailed than the same patient would have received before, filed under a staging rule whose boundaries are unchanged.

## Direction

From a lower rung to a higher one, for exactly those cases whose spread was too slight to have been seen by the older instrument.

## Lever

That the movement is not random in either direction: hidden spread makes these cases the sickest of the rung they leave, and slight spread makes them the healthiest of the rung they join, so they exit one distribution at the bottom and enter the other at the top (Feinstein, Sosin and Wells).

## Echo

[The Migrants](piece_the_migrants.md), correctly filed to a rung above, and every case still counted.
