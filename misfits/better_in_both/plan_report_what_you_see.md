# Plan: Report What You See

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the radiologist's seat](position_radiologist.md), inside [Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Radiologist](position_radiologist.md)

## Direction

Record every deposit the imaging resolves. A visible metastasis left off the record is a falsification and a clinical failure at once, and the patient in front of you is entitled to the finding whatever it does to a series. Do not read to the older machine's standard for the sake of comparability: suppressing real disease so this year's figures match last year's is the one genuinely wrong act available in this seat. Judge nothing about what the registry or the podium will do downstream, since neither is the reading room's business and neither is visible from it.

## Orders

([The Radiologist](persona_the_radiologist.md) reads through [Detecting](process_detecting.md) at [the Reading Room](place_the_reading_room.md), wielding [the Sharper Eye](piece_the_sharper_eye.md).)

## Implementation

([The Reading Room](place_the_reading_room.md) is where the new instrument meets the old population; [Detecting](process_detecting.md) resolves deposits that were always present and never visible, so the finding submitted to [the Registry](place_the_registry.md) is more accurate than the same patient's finding would have been a decade ago.)

## Targets

- [ ] every deposit the imaging resolves is recorded, without exception
- [ ] no finding is suppressed to preserve comparability with an earlier era
- [ ] the report states disease present, not disease suspected
- [ ] accuracy is supplied in full, downstream use being outside this seat's view and its remit
