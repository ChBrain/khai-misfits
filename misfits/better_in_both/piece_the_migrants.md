# Piece: The Migrants

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). The cases that cross a rung upward: by construction the sickest of the stage they leave and the healthiest of the stage they join.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Filed at [The Registry](place_the_registry.md), each one correctly, each one a rung above where the same case would have gone a decade ago (Feinstein, Sosin and Wells).

## Load Bearing

Remove the double edge and the arithmetic fails: if the migrating cases were a random sample of the lower stage, moving them would leave that stage's average untouched, and if they were not the mildest arrivals in the upper stage they would drag its average down rather than up. It is the fact that they are simultaneously the worst of one group and the best of the other that lifts both.
It bears the load as the whole engine. Hidden spread is what makes them the sickest below, and spread slight enough to have needed a new machine is what makes them the healthiest above, so the same property that moves them guarantees the direction of both effects.

## Apparent

A correction, and truly one: these are the patients whose staging was wrong before and is right now, the clearest beneficiaries of the better instrument.
