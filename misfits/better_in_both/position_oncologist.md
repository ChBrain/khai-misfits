# Position: Oncologist

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). The seat that compares stage-specific survival across eras and reads the arithmetic as advance.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Two tables, each internally consistent and correctly computed, carrying the same stage labels.
The standard yardstick of the field, stage-specific survival, chosen precisely because it controls for severity.
No column on either table naming the instrument that assigned its stages.

## Orders

Compare to last decade: set stage against stage, since comparing across stages would compare unlike cases and pooling everything would track the mix rather than the medicine.
Report what the comparison shows, because a field that declined to measure its own progress would be failing a different and worse way.
Accept what the seat does: it trusts the stage label to mean one thing in both tables, which is the assumption the whole yardstick rests on and the only one it cannot check.

## Loses

The very rigour it selected for, spent on a comparison the rigour cannot survive.
Because the seat controls for severity by stratifying on a label whose evidentiary basis has shifted, it loses more by being careful than a cruder seat would lose by pooling, so its discipline is what carries it wrong.
It keeps a severity-controlled comparison and loses the ground that made control mean anything.

## Drives

Toward the one whose methodological care is the exact route the artefact travels,
stratifying because stratifying is the more rigorous move and, by doing so, reading a migration as medicine,
so the seat that only did well by comparing like with like is the seat at which arithmetic is announced as progress, careful in method and wrong in conclusion.
