# Persona: The Statistician

## Taxonomy

[Statistician](position_statistician.md). The one who reaches past the stage tables for a count no reclassification can move, and pays for it in granularity.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sceptical and unwelcome, the one asking why every rung improved at once when no single treatment changed.
Reaches for the total rather than the strata, and for the mortality rather than the survival, which is the less interesting number and the harder one to move.
No cynicism on show: it does not doubt the imaging or the staging, only what a comparison across them can support.

## Action

Runs [Count the Whole Cohort](plan_count_the_whole_cohort.md): at [the Podium](place_the_podium.md), through [Recounting](process_recounting.md), holds [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) by counting total mortality across everyone diagnosed, which no migration touches since a patient moved between rungs is still in the total.
Cannot restage the old cohort in most cases, since that would need decades-old imaging retrieved and re-read against a question it was never made to resolve, and often the films answer nothing.
Does the one thing that works and is least wanted: replaces a granular, flattering, severity-controlled figure with a blunt, unflattering, uncontrolled one.

## Shadow

Not a naysayer: the artefact is real and arithmetic, and the count it proposes is the one endpoint the migration provably cannot move, so the scepticism answers a demonstrable problem and not a mood.
Because its remedy answers a different and cruder question than the one clinicians need answered, its correctness costs exactly the resolution the stage tables existed to provide, and it can defend the number without making it useful.
It is the play's only remedy and it trades away the thing that made the yardstick worth having.

## Tell

Asks which scanner built each table, the one question neither table records, and is met with the reasonable answer that this has never been a column.
Cannot make the whole-cohort count say what stage-specific survival says, since the migration it defeats is the same movement that gave the stage figures their apparent detail, so the honest number is permanently the less informative one.
