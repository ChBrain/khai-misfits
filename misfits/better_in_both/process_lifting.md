# Process: Lifting

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). Borne at [The Podium](place_the_podium.md), precipitated by [The Oncologist](persona_the_oncologist.md) setting the two tables side by side, in [Progress on Paper](plot_progress_on_paper.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two internally consistent tables carrying the same stage labels, built a decade apart by different instruments, and a field that rightly wants to know whether it is winning.

## Direction

From a stage-specific comparison toward a reading of advance, every rung improved at once, the lower having shed its worst cases and the upper having gained cases better than its own.

## Lever

That the arithmetic is genuine and the medicine is not: removing the lowest members of one group and adding them at the top of another raises both averages with no individual altered, and the better the new instrument the larger the apparent gain (Will Rogers; Feinstein, Sosin and Wells).

## Echo
