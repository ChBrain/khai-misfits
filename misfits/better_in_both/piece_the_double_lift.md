# Piece: The Double Lift

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). Both averages rising from the one movement: the lower stage improved by shedding its worst, the upper by gaining better than its own.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Read at [The Podium](place_the_podium.md), across every rung at once, the improvement uniform in a way no single treatment could produce (Will Rogers; Feinstein, Sosin and Wells).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the migration is merely a reclassification: cases moved between columns with the averages unchanged would be an administrative detail, noticed by nobody and costing nothing.
It bears the load as the thing that converts an accounting change into an apparent discovery. Because both groups improve rather than one improving at the other's expense, the result looks like advance from every angle and offers no internal contradiction to catch, which is exactly why a careful reader is not protected by care.

## Apparent

Success across the board, the most persuasive shape a result can take, since a gain confined to one stage would invite the question this one never provokes.
