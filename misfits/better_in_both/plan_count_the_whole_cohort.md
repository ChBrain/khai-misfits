# Plan: Count the Whole Cohort

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the statistician's seat](position_statistician.md), inside [Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Statistician](position_statistician.md)

## Direction

Take the total. Count mortality across everyone diagnosed rather than survival within each rung, because a patient moved between rungs is still inside the total and a figure that never stratified cannot be moved by restratification. Where the films allow it, restage the earlier cohort under the current rule so both tables are built by one instrument, and say plainly where they do not allow it, since decades-old imaging was never made to resolve what the new rule asks of it. Name the price without softening it: the number this yields is blunter, slower, and less useful to a clinician than the one it replaces.

- [ ] mortality is counted across the whole diagnosed cohort, unstratified by stage
- [ ] the earlier cohort is restaged where the imaging permits, and the limit stated plainly where it does not
- [ ] the cost is named: a blunter and less clinically useful figure than the one replaced
- [ ] no claim is made that the remedy restores the resolution it gives up
