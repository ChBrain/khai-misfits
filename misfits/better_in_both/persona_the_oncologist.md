# Persona: The Oncologist

## Taxonomy

[Oncologist](position_oncologist.md). The one who sets this decade's stage-specific survival beside last decade's and reads advance across every rung.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Rigorous and encouraged, comparing like with like exactly as the literature prescribes, stage against stage rather than pooling unlike cases.
Reads improvement across the board and reports it, since improvement across the board is what a field working well should produce.
No credulity on show: it is using the most severity-controlled yardstick available, and refusing to compare at all would be its own failure.

## Action

Runs [Compare to Last Decade](plan_compare_to_last_decade.md): at [the Podium](place_the_podium.md), through [Lifting](process_lifting.md), reads [the Double Lift](piece_the_double_lift.md) across the table and presents it as advance, with [the Empty Progress](piece_the_empty_progress.md) standing unseen beneath it.
Cannot detect the artefact from inside the comparison, since both tables are internally consistent, correctly computed, and carry the same stage labels, and nothing on either page records which instrument assigned them.
Does what the yardstick was built for: it controls for severity by stratifying on stage, which isolates the medicine only while the instrument assigning the stages holds still.

## Shadow

Not credulous: stage-specific survival is the standard comparison, more careful than the pooled figure it replaced, and choosing it over a crude overall rate is the more rigorous move, not the lazier one.
Because its rigour consists in trusting the stage label to mean the same thing in both tables, the very discipline that makes the comparison careful is what makes it wrong, and a cruder analyst pooling everything would have stumbled nearer the truth.
It is the most methodical reader in the play and the one the arithmetic passes through undetected.

## Tell

Presents gains at every stage at once and reads the uniformity as strength, when uniformity across all rungs is the signature of the artefact rather than of the medicine.
Cannot ask the tables which scanner built them, since that is not a column either one carries, so the question that would settle it is not available at the podium where it arises.
