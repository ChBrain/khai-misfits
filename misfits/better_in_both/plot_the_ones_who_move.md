# Plot: The Ones Who Move

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Registry](place_the_registry.md), where the newly-resolved findings meet a staging rule whose boundaries have not moved.

## Action

Through [Migrating](process_migrating.md), [the Migrants](piece_the_migrants.md) go up a rung: patients who a decade ago would have been filed one stage lower, because nothing showed, now filed correctly one stage higher, because something shows. And they are not a random sample in either direction. Having hidden spread makes them the sickest of the stage they leave, the cases that would have died soonest and dragged its average down; having spread slight enough to have needed a new machine makes them the healthiest of the stage they join. The scene closes with the movement made and every case correctly filed.

## Stage

No one is misclassified at any point. Each migrant is filed accurately under the current rule, and an auditor checking any single record against its evidence would find it right.

## Tension

The one property that moves these cases upward is the same property that places them at the bottom of the group they leave and the top of the group they enter, so the direction of both effects is guaranteed by the migration itself and cannot come out any other way.
