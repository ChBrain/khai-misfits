# Piece: The Ladder

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). The fixed rungs of stage, sorted by extent of spread, against which every outcome is reported.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Standing over [The Registry](place_the_registry.md), published and stable: labels that do not move, each one meaning whatever the evidence assigned to it means (Feinstein, Sosin and Wells).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is nothing to migrate across: with no categories, outcomes could only be reported for the whole cohort at once, which is precisely the figure the migration cannot touch, so the trap would have no surface to run on.
It bears the load as the structure the whole artefact requires, and it is not a flaw. Stage-specific reporting is the correct way to compare like with like, since a pooled figure tracks the changing mix of stages rather than the medicine, so the ladder is both the right instrument and the necessary condition of the trap.

## Apparent

Rigour, and rightly so: stratifying by severity is the more careful analysis, the one a serious field insists on over a crude overall rate.

## Yearbook

Stands over the setup in [Survival, By Stage](plot_survival_by_stage.md), the yardstick everyone correctly uses.
It works together with [The Migrants](piece_the_migrants.md), the fixed rungs and the cases that cross them, neither meaningful without the other.
