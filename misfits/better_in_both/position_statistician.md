# Position: Statistician

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). The seat that reaches for a count no reclassification can move, and pays in granularity for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

One endpoint the migration provably cannot touch: total mortality across everyone diagnosed.
A second remedy mostly out of reach, restaging the old cohort under the new rule, which needs imaging that often cannot answer.
No way to make either remedy as informative as the stage-specific table it replaces.

## Orders

Count the whole cohort: take the total, since a patient moved between rungs is still inside it and no migration can shift a number that never stratified.
Restage the earlier cohort where the films allow, so both tables are built by one instrument, and say plainly where they do not allow it.
Accept what the seat does: it trades resolution for validity, and the number it defends is the one nobody came to hear.

## Loses

The granularity that made the stage tables worth reading in the first place.
Because the migration it defeats is the same movement that gave the stage figures their apparent detail, the seat cannot defeat the artefact and keep the detail, so its correctness costs exactly the resolution clinicians need, and it answers a cruder question than the one that was asked.
It keeps a valid number and loses the ability to make that number useful.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound scepticism can only be vindicated in a currency nobody wants,
counting the total because the total is the one thing the migration cannot move and, by doing so, replacing a flattering, detailed figure with a blunt and unwelcome one,
so the seat that holds the play's only remedy is the seat that must spend the yardstick's whole value to prove the yardstick wrong.
