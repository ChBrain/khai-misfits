# Persona: The Radiologist

## Taxonomy

[Radiologist](position_radiologist.md). The reader of the new imaging, finding deposits the old machine could not, and making the record more truthful than it was.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Precise and improving, the practitioner whose instrument got better and whose readings got better with it.
Finds what is genuinely there, including what nobody could see a decade ago, which is the entire point of a new scanner and the reason it was bought.
No overreach on show: it reports disease, not suspicion, and would be negligent to leave a visible deposit off the record.

## Action

Runs [Report What You See](plan_report_what_you_see.md): through [Detecting](process_detecting.md) at [the Reading Room](place_the_reading_room.md), reads [the Sharper Eye](piece_the_sharper_eye.md) and records the small metastases that older imaging missed entirely.
Cannot report less than it sees, since omitting real disease to preserve comparability with an older era would be a falsification and a clinical failure both.
Does the unambiguously right thing: it makes the classification more accurate, and accuracy is not a thing a reader may decline to supply.

## Shadow

Not overzealous: every deposit it reports is real, present, and would have been present a decade ago too, unseen, so the finding corrects an old undercount rather than inventing a new disease.
Because a truer reading is what moves a patient across a stage line, its improvement is the precise mechanism that renders the two eras incomparable, its accuracy and the artefact the same act.
It is the reason the staging is better, and the reason the tables cannot be set beside each other.

## Tell

Reports more advanced disease this year than last from the same underlying population, and reads that as the machine doing its job, which it is.
Cannot see downstream of its own report, since what a registry does with a stage and what a podium does with a registry are not the reading room's business, so the improvement leaves its hands correct and arrives elsewhere as inflation.
