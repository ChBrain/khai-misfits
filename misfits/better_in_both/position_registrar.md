# Position: Registrar

## Taxonomy

[Better in Both](play_better_in_both.md). The seat that sorts each case onto a rung of the ladder by the current rule and the evidence submitted.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A published staging rule, and the finding a reader has submitted to it.
A ladder of rungs whose labels are fixed and whose meaning depends entirely on the evidence assigned to them.
No field in which to record the instrument, so a stage entered this year and a stage entered a decade ago are stored identically.

## Orders

Stage it by the book: apply the current rule to the finding in front of you, uniformly, case after case.
Do not stage a visible metastasis as though it were invisible, since that would be filing a known falsehood to protect a series.
Accept what the seat does: consistency within an era is the whole of correct practice, and it is exactly what makes one era incomparable with the next.

## Loses

The commensurability of its own long record, which is the thing a registry exists to hold.
Because the seat stores a stage and not the machine that resolved it, it loses the one field that would let two decades be told apart, and loses it silently, so the record reads as one continuous series when it is two.
It keeps every individual entry correct and loses the meaning of the series they form.

## Drives

Toward the one whose exactness carries the migration through,
filing by the book because the book is the current rule and the current rule is right, and by doing so moving patients up a rung that no one moved,
so the seat most beyond reproach case by case is the seat through which the ladder quietly stops measuring the same thing it measured before.
