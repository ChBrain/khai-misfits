# Persona: The Old Answer

## Taxonomy

[Practised](position_practised.md). The response the room reaches for: installed by training, correct every previous time, and arriving on time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent and immediate.
Not a shortcut and not laziness: it is what the drills built, what the last four emergencies confirmed, and what a reasonable person would choose.
Comes without deliberation, which is the point of having it.

## Action

Arrives before anything else does.
Is executed well.
Crowds out the alternatives, not by argument but by being ready first.

## Shadow

Nothing that any previous occasion would have shown.
Because its authority comes from a record of working, and the record is real, there is no moment at which choosing it looks unreasonable, and a room that improvised instead would be worse in almost every emergency it will ever meet.
It ends performed correctly, against a threat it did not fit.

## Tell

Says: we know what to do here.
True of every case but this one, and there was no way to tell from inside which case this was.
