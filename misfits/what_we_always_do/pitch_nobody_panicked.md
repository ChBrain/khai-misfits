# Pitch: Nobody Panicked

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Clipped, operational, unhurried in the way trained people are.
The register of a room that has stopped taking questions: shorter sentences, fewer voices, decisions moving up the table.
Nothing panicked anywhere in it, because panic is not what this is and never was.

## Undertow

Under the competence, two facts that do not negotiate.
Under pressure, attention goes to the cues that have mattered before. Under pressure, authority goes to whoever is accountable.
Between those two, the range narrows exactly when the situation calls for it to widen.

## Nerve

The unease of a room performing well,
answering fast, deciding cleanly, everybody doing what they were trained to do,
and having quietly put away the two things that would have told them it was the wrong answer.

## Echo

After the run: a decision made quickly by the right person,
on the cues that have always mattered,
about a situation that was not the usual kind.
