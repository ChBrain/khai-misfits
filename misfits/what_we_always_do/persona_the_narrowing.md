# Persona: The Narrowing

## Taxonomy

[Narrowed](position_narrowed.md). The restriction of what gets attended to: fewer cues, trusted channels only, the marginal set aside.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Efficient, and necessary.
A body that weighed every marginal signal equally in a crisis would never act at all, so restricting attention is how anything gets done under load.
Feels like focus, because it is focus.

## Action

Drops the peripheral.
Works the channels that have carried the important thing before.
Speeds everything up, measurably and genuinely.

## Shadow

The one cue that mattered this time, set aside with all the others and by the same rule that correctly set them aside.
Because the restriction is what makes acting possible, it cannot be removed without removing the capacity to respond at speed, and there is no version of it that keeps the useful margin and discards the rest.
It ends efficient, and having filtered out the thing that would have changed the answer.

## Tell

Says: let us not get distracted.
Correct, and the distraction was the evidence.
