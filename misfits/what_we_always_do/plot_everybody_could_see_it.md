# Plot: Everybody Could See It

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Emergency](place_the_emergency.md), begun.
[The Threat](persona_the_threat.md), arriving and recognised.

## Action

[Arriving](process_arriving.md): [The Threat](persona_the_threat.md), in [the faced seat](position_faced.md), presents itself at [the emergency](place_the_emergency.md) and is assessed accurately by everyone present.

## Stage

[The Threat](persona_the_threat.md), on [Be Seen for What I Am](plan_be_seen_for_what_i_am.md), is neither exaggerated by an anxious room nor minimised by a complacent one.

## Tension

Denial is eliminated here, and the play needs it gone before anything else happens.
Nothing in this production is unseen, nothing is talked down, and nobody is protecting a position by pretending things are fine. The assessment is correct, it is shared, and it is arrived at quickly.
**So the accuracy of the perception buys nothing.** Every remedy addressed to awareness is spent in this plot: better reporting changes nothing when the report was right, and a franker room changes nothing when the room was already frank. What follows is not caused by anybody failing to see.
