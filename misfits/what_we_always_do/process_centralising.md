# Process: Centralising

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). The decision moving up the table: authority concentrated, discretion withdrawn.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The need for somebody to own the call.
At [the top of the table](place_the_top_of_the_table.md), by consent rather than by seizure.

## Direction

Toward an answer that is both quick and answerable, which a dispersed decision is neither.
The seniors did not ask for it and the juniors handed it up: in an emergency, ownership is a service rather than a prize.
A body that spread authority the moment things got serious would answer slowly and answer for nothing.

## Lever

Concentration, which shortens the path from question to answer.
Because what it removes is the standing to object rather than any particular objection, its cost leaves no trace: there is no dissent on the record, dissent requiring a place to be entered.

## Echo

A decision owned and made quickly,
by the smallest number of people who have ever made one here.
