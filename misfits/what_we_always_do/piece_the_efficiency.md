# Piece: The Efficiency

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). What restriction buys: the capacity to act at all when everything cannot be weighed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the channels](place_the_channels.md), where it is produced.
Spent nowhere, because it is not consumed: it is simply what narrowing is.

## Load Bearing

Without it the narrowing is a failure of attention and the play is about a distracted room.
It is load bearing because it makes the restriction **correct**: a body weighing every marginal signal equally in a crisis would never act, so this is the mechanism of responding rather than a lapse in it.

## Apparent

Focus, which is what it is.
It reads as a virtue because it is one, and there is no version of this play in which the room would have been better off attending to everything.

## Yearbook

Produced by [narrowing](process_narrowing.md), required by [the emergency](place_the_emergency.md)'s clock.
It cannot be separated from what it costs, since the useful margin and the rest of the margin are indistinguishable in advance.
