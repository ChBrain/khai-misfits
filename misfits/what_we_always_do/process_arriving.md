# Process: Arriving

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). The threat presenting itself and being assessed accurately.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Something going genuinely wrong.
At [the emergency](place_the_emergency.md), in front of everybody.

## Direction

Toward a correct and shared assessment, arrived at quickly, which is what a well-run room produces.
Nobody exaggerates and nobody minimises; there is no denial anywhere in the sequence and no position being protected.
So every remedy addressed to awareness is spent at this step, and none of them touches what follows.

## Lever

Accuracy, achieved.
Because the response is selected by the speed the threat demands rather than by the kind of threat it is, improving this step further changes nothing downstream.

## Echo

A room that understands its situation exactly,
and is about to answer a different question.
