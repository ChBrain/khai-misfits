## Tension

The restriction is the mechanism of acting at all, which is why it cannot simply be removed.
[The efficiency](piece_the_efficiency.md) is real: a body that weighed every marginal signal equally in a crisis would never act, and under load attending to fewer cues is how anything gets done. It feels like focus because it **is** focus.
And the cue that mattered this time is set aside by the same rule that correctly sets aside all the others, so it is not an ignored warning: **it is one of the many correctly ignored things, which this once was not.** Nothing distinguishes it in advance, because what would distinguish it is the knowledge that this threat is a new kind, which is the thing the weighing was going to establish.
