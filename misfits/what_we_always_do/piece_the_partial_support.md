# Piece: The Partial Support

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). The finding's honest shape: one half of the account confirmed, the other not found.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Inside [the twenty-four groups](piece_the_twenty_four_groups.md), inseparable from them.
Carried by the play and closed by it nowhere.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play overstates its evidence and claims a whole mechanism where the record shows half of one.
It is load bearing because it **names the gap in its own case**: information processing restricted and stress rose, as predicted, and influence inside the groups did **not** concentrate, which the account predicted and the test did not find.

## Apparent

A weakness, of the kind an author would rather leave out.
It reads as undermining, and it is the opposite: a finding that reports where it failed is worth more than one that reports only where it held.

## Yearbook

Reported by the study that produced it, at group level and in one design.
It leaves the narrowing well supported and the concentration standing on the theory and on other evidence rather than on this test.
