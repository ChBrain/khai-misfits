# Position: Faced

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). The seat of the threat: real, arriving, and correctly assessed by everybody present.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A genuine emergency, neither exaggerated nor talked down.
Accurate and shared perception, arrived at quickly.
A clock, which selects the response before the kind of threat does.

## Orders

Arrive.
Be recognised for what you are.
Demand an answer fast enough that the answer will be the practised one.

## Loses

Nothing, and this seat is what removes denial from the play: nobody is unseeing anything and nobody is protecting a position.
Because the response is selected by the speed demanded rather than by the kind of threat presented, seeing it exactly is compatible with meeting it wrongly, so the accuracy buys nothing.
It ends met, decisively, by the answer to a different question.

## Drives

Toward being taken seriously,
and succeeding at that completely,
so the seat that is correctly understood is the seat that is wrongly answered.
