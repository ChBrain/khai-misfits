# Persona: The Grip

## Taxonomy

[Held](position_held.md). The decision moving up the table: authority concentrated, discretion withdrawn, one person answerable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Accountable, and fast.
Somebody has to own this, and a body that dispersed authority the moment things got serious would answer slowly and answer for nothing.
Not a power grab: the seniors did not ask for it and the juniors handed it up.

## Action

Concentrates the decision.
Shortens the path from question to answer.
Removes the standing of everyone who is not at the top of the table.

## Shadow

The dissent that never becomes dissent, because the people who would have offered it are now outside the decision rather than losing an argument inside it.
Because concentration is how a body answers quickly and stays answerable, dispersing it costs both, and the play does not pretend otherwise.
It ends decisive, with fewer people who could have said so.

## Tell

Says: I will take this one.
Correct, responsible, and the reason nobody else weighs in.
