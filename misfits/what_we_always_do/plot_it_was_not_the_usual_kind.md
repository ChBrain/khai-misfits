# Plot: It Was Not the Usual Kind

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Novelty](piece_the_novelty.md), which was there from the start and is legible only now.
[The Old Answer](persona_the_old_answer.md), performed correctly and against the wrong thing.

## Action

[Missing](process_missing.md): [The Signal Not Taken](persona_the_signal_not_taken.md), in [the unread seat](position_unread.md), stays unweighed at [the channels](place_the_channels.md) while the practised response runs to completion.

## Stage

[The Novelty](piece_the_novelty.md) is what makes the repertoire wrong, and [the narrowing](persona_the_narrowing.md) and [the grip](persona_the_grip.md) are what removed the two means of noticing it.

## Tension

This is the whole trap, and its shape is that the error hides inside its own correction.
The practised answer is right when the threat is one the repertoire contains, and wrong when it is not. The two things that would establish which case this is are a wider sweep of cues and more people with the standing to say so. **Both were withdrawn on the way in**, by moves that were correct, that a trained room makes automatically, and that make the body better at nearly every other emergency.
So the narrowing removes the means of noticing that the narrowing is wrong, and there is no moment inside the sequence at which anybody had what they needed to object. The room finds out which answer it had **afterwards**, which is the only place that information exists.
