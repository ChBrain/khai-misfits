# Play: What We Always Do

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

What We Always Do.
Something has gone badly wrong, and everybody can see that it has. Nobody is in denial and nobody is panicking.
The room does what rooms do: it stops entertaining the marginal, it works the channels it trusts, and it moves the decision to whoever is most senior.
Every one of those is the correct response to a threat the room has met before.

## Arc

The bend is this: the response to threat narrows the range of what is considered and concentrates who considers it, at exactly the moment when the situation calls for more of both, and the narrowing is not a failure of nerve but the well-learned answer arriving on time.
It runs on two facts, neither of them anybody's doing: under pressure, attention goes to the cues that have mattered before, and under pressure, authority goes to whoever is accountable.
Take the efficiency first, because the play has to establish that both moves are **right**. Attending to fewer cues is how anything gets done under load: a body that weighed every marginal signal equally in a crisis would never act at all. And concentrating the decision is how an organisation answers quickly and how it stays accountable for what it did. Neither is a vice, both are what training installs, and a room that did the opposite, widening its attention and dispersing its authority the moment things got serious, would be worse in almost every emergency it will ever face.
Then the condition that turns them, which is the whole play. The moves are correct when the threat is one the repertoire already contains. When the threat is **new**, the well-learned response is the wrong response, and the two things that would reveal that, a wider sweep of cues and more people with the standing to say so, are precisely what has just been withdrawn. **The narrowing removes the means of noticing that the narrowing is wrong.**
And nothing here is anybody's error. There is no denial: the threat is seen accurately. There is no cowardice: the room acts, quickly and decisively. There is no incompetence: the response it reaches for is the one that has worked every previous time. The room is doing the thing that made it worth trusting.
The evidence is a group under a real load, and it is honest about its own limits. Twenty-four groups, put under threat in a management simulation, showed restricted information processing and raised stress, as the account predicts. They did **not** show the second half: influence inside the groups did not concentrate. So the account holds in part and is not confirmed whole, and the play carries the gap rather than closing it.
Reach for a settlement and every one costs something real. Widen attention in a crisis and the room is slower when speed is what a crisis takes. Disperse authority and nobody is accountable for the decision. Rehearse the novel case in advance and it stops being novel, which works exactly to the degree that you guessed right about which novelty would come. **The reliable answer and the answer to this are different answers, and the room finds out which it had afterwards.**

## Triggers

**[Everybody Could See It](plot_everybody_could_see_it.md)**
A real threat, arriving, and correctly perceived by everyone in the room. Closes when denial is eliminated: nothing here is unseen, nothing is minimised, and the accuracy of the perception is established before anything else happens.

**[The Thing That Has Always Worked](plot_the_thing_that_has_always_worked.md)**
Cue: the repertoire, reached for. Closes when the fallback is established as correct rather than lazy: it is what training installed, it has worked every previous time, and a room that improvised instead would be worse in almost every emergency it will meet.

**[Fewer Things to Weigh](plot_fewer_things_to_weigh.md)**
Cue: the channels, narrowing. Closes when the restriction is shown to be the mechanism of acting at all under load, so that attending to fewer cues is efficiency and not blindness, and the signal that would have mattered is simply one of the many now not weighed.

**[Whoever Is Accountable](plot_whoever_is_accountable.md)**
Cue: the decision, moving up the table. Closes when concentration is shown to be how a body answers quickly and stays answerable for what it did, and when the people who would have dissented are shown to be outside the decision rather than overruled inside it.

**[It Was Not the Usual Kind](plot_it_was_not_the_usual_kind.md)**
Cue: the threat turning out to be novel. Closes when the trap is complete: the well-learned answer is wrong here, and the wider sweep and the extra voices that would have revealed it are exactly what was withdrawn on the way in.

## Stakes

Whether a body can meet something new with the competence it built on everything old.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the room saw the threat, because it saw it exactly, and not whether it acted, because it acted fast and decisively, but whether the response that makes an organisation reliable in every familiar emergency can be told apart, at the time, from the response that is wrong in this one. The production cannot tell them apart and neither can the room. It can only show that both moves are correct and installed by training, that they are correct precisely because the threat is usually familiar, that a novel threat inverts them, that the narrowing removes the means of noticing the inversion, and that the evidence for the account confirms one half and not the other, which the play carries rather than settles.
