## Stage

## Tension

The fallback is right, and the play has to establish that before it can be the problem.
It is what the drills installed, what the last four emergencies confirmed, and what any reasonable person in the room would choose. It arrives without deliberation, which is the entire point of having trained: deliberation is what a body cannot afford when the clock is the constraint.
**A room that improvised instead would be worse in almost every emergency it will ever meet.** So the trap cannot be sprung by finding the response lazy or the room complacent, and the production never asks for the repertoire to be abandoned. It has to be sprung with the practised answer arriving on time and performed well.
