# Place: The Top of the Table

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). Where the decision arrives once it has moved up, and where it is owned.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A short path from question to answer.
One person answerable, by consent: handed up rather than seized.

## Holds

The reason the alternative is not free.
Concentration is how a body answers quickly and how it stays accountable, so dispersing authority in an emergency costs both the speed and the ownership, and the play does not pretend otherwise.

## Offers

A decision made fast, and somebody to answer for it afterwards.

## Withheld

The standing to object.
What is removed is not any particular argument but the place an argument could be entered, so the cost leaves no trace: there is no dissent on the record, because dissent requires somewhere to be recorded.
