# Place: The Emergency

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). Where the threat lands and the room answers it, on a clock that forbids deliberation.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A real situation, correctly assessed, with nobody in denial and nobody panicking.
A room performing well: fast, competent, doing what it was trained to do.

## Holds

The play's quietest fact: everything visible here is being done correctly.
The perception is accurate, the response is the practised one, the speed is what the situation demands, and none of it is a failure of nerve, attention or competence.

## Offers

A fast, owned, well-executed answer.

## Withheld

The time in which the kind of threat could be established.
The clock selects the response before anybody can determine whether the repertoire contains this case, so the choice is made by the pace rather than by the diagnosis.
