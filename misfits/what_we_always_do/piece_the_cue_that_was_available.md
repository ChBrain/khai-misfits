# Piece: The Cue That Was Available

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). The signal that would have shown the threat was a new kind: in the room, peripheral, never weighed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the channels](place_the_channels.md), among the many things correctly set aside.
Held by [the signal not taken](persona_the_signal_not_taken.md), who is present throughout.

## Load Bearing

Without it the room never had a chance and the play is about bad luck.
It is load bearing because the information **was there**: the failure is not one of availability but of weighing, which is what makes it structural rather than accidental.

## Apparent

Marginal, exactly like everything else that is marginal.
It reads as one more low-value signal, which is what it looks like and what it would have been on any other day, and there is no rule available in advance that separates it from the rest.

## Yearbook

It is not the ignored warning: it is one of the many correctly ignored things, which this once was not.
