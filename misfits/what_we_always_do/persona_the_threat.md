# Persona: The Threat

## Taxonomy

[Faced](position_faced.md). Something that has genuinely gone wrong, arriving, and seen for exactly what it is by everyone in the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Real, and correctly perceived.
Not exaggerated by an anxious room and not minimised by a complacent one: the assessment is accurate.
Nobody is in denial, and the play has no use for anybody who is.

## Action

Arrives.
Is recognised.
Demands a response quickly enough that the response will be the practised one.

## Shadow

Nothing while it is the usual kind, and everything when it is not.
Because the room's answer is selected by the speed the threat demands rather than by the kind of threat it is, the accuracy of the perception buys nothing: seeing it exactly is compatible with meeting it wrongly.
It ends met, decisively, by the answer to a different question.

## Tell

Says: this is serious and we all know it.
True, and being right about that is where the trouble starts rather than ends.
