# Plan: Decide Where the Accountability Sits

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the held seat](position_held.md), inside [What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Held](position_held.md)

## Direction

Take the decision, shorten the path from question to answer, and be the one who answers for it afterwards. Supply the accountability a dispersed decision does not have.

## Orders

([The Grip](persona_the_grip.md) concentrates the decision at [the top of the table](place_the_top_of_the_table.md), by consent rather than by seizure.)

## Implementation

([The Top of the Table](place_the_top_of_the_table.md), where the decision arrives; the [centralising](process_centralising.md) process; ownership handed up by the juniors rather than claimed by the seniors.)

## Targets

- [x] somebody has to own the call, and a dispersed decision answers slowly and for nothing
- [ ] the path from question to answer is shortened
- [ ] what is removed is the standing to object rather than any particular objection
- [ ] no dissent appears on the record, dissent requiring a place to be entered
