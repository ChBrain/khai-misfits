# Piece: The Repertoire

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). The set of well-learned responses: installed by drill, confirmed by outcome, and ready without deliberation.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the emergency](place_the_emergency.md), reached for first.
Held by [the old answer](persona_the_old_answer.md), who arrives before anything else.

## Load Bearing

Without it the room is improvising and the play is about an unprepared body, which is a different and lesser thing.
It is load bearing because its authority is **earned**: the record of working is genuine, so choosing from it is never unreasonable, and the trap has to be sprung with a competent room doing the competent thing.

## Apparent

Habit. Doing it the way it has always been done.
It reads as the absence of thought rather than as thought already completed and stored, which is why the first instinct is to ask for more deliberation, exactly when there is no time for any.

## Yearbook

Built by drill, confirmed by [arriving](process_arriving.md) four times before this one.
It contains the answer to every threat it contains, and [the novelty](piece_the_novelty.md) is the case it does not.
