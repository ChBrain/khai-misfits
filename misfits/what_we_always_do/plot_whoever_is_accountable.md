## Cue

## Action

[Centralising](process_centralising.md): [The Grip](persona_the_grip.md), in [the held seat](position_held.md), concentrates the decision at [the top of the table](place_the_top_of_the_table.md) and shortens the path from question to answer.

## Stage

[The Grip](persona_the_grip.md), on [Decide Where the Accountability Sits](plan_decide_where_the_accountability_sits.md), owns the call, which nobody resents and several are relieved by.

## Tension

Concentration is how a body answers quickly and how it stays answerable, so dispersing it costs both.
This is not a power grab: the seniors did not ask for it and the juniors handed it up, because in an emergency somebody has to own the decision and a body that spread authority the moment things got serious would answer slowly and answer for nothing.
What it removes is not an argument but the **standing to have one**. The people who might have said this is not the usual kind are now outside the decision rather than losing a debate inside it, which is quieter and harder to notice: there is no dissent on the record, because dissent requires a place to be entered.
