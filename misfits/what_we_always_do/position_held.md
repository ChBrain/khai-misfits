# Position: Held

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). The seat of concentrated authority: the decision moved up, owned, and answerable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Ownership of the call, handed up rather than seized.
A short path from question to answer.
Accountability, which a dispersed decision does not have.

## Orders

Take the decision.
Shorten the path.
Be the one who answers for it afterwards.

## Loses

Not an argument but the standing to have one: those who might have objected are outside the decision rather than losing a debate inside it.
Because concentration is how a body answers quickly and stays answerable, dispersing it costs both, and the play does not pretend the alternative is free.
It ends decisive, with no dissent on the record, because dissent requires a place to be entered.

## Drives

Toward a fast and owned decision,
which is what an emergency requires,
so the seat that supplies accountability removes the voices that would have corrected it.
