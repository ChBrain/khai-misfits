# Persona: The Signal Not Taken

## Taxonomy

[Unread](position_unread.md). The cue that would have shown the threat was a new kind: available throughout, peripheral, and never weighed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Present and unremarkable.
Not hidden, not suppressed, not withheld by anybody: it is in the room, and it looks exactly like the many marginal things that correctly get set aside.
Distinguishable afterwards and not before.

## Action

Sits available.
Is not attended to.
Would have been attended to on an ordinary day, when there was room to weigh the marginal.

## Shadow

Everything, and none of it visible at the time.
Because what makes it decisive is only knowable once the threat is known to be novel, and the threat is known to be novel only by weighing it, the play offers no rule that would have caught it: **it is not the ignored warning, it is one of the many correctly ignored things, which this once was not.**
It ends unread, and legible in the review.

## Tell

Says nothing, being a cue.
Somebody mentioned it early on and the room had already stopped weighing that kind of thing.
