# Process: Falling Back

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). The practised response being reached for, without deliberation, because that is what it is for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The clock, which forbids deliberation.
At [the emergency](place_the_emergency.md), from [the repertoire](piece_the_repertoire.md).

## Direction

Toward a fast, competent answer by the route that has produced one every previous time.
It is what the drills installed and what the last four occasions confirmed, and it arrives before the alternatives by being ready rather than by winning an argument.
A room that improvised instead would be worse in almost every emergency it will ever meet.

## Lever

Readiness, which is the whole return on having trained.
Because the authority of the response rests on a genuine record of working, there is no moment at which choosing it looks unreasonable from inside.

## Echo

The right answer, performed well,
to whichever question this turns out to have been.
