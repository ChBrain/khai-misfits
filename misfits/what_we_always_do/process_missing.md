# Process: Missing

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). The available cue going unweighed, and the novelty going unestablished.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Narrowing](process_narrowing.md) and [centralising](process_centralising.md), both correct, both complete.
At [the channels](place_the_channels.md), while the practised response runs.

## Direction

Toward completing the response that was chosen, without the two things that would have shown it was the wrong one.
A wider sweep of cues and more people with the standing to speak are precisely what establishes whether a threat is the usual kind, and both were withdrawn on the way in.
So the narrowing removes the means of noticing that the narrowing is wrong.

## Lever

Absence, which leaves no trace and raises no alarm.
Because nothing was suppressed and nobody was overruled, there is no moment inside the sequence at which anybody had what they needed to object.

## Echo

A response completed correctly,
and a room that learns which answer it had afterwards, that being the only place the information exists.
