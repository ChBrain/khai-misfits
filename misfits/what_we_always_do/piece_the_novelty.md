# Piece: The Novelty

## Taxonomy

[What We Always Do](play_what_we_always_do.md). The property that makes this threat one the repertoire does not contain, present from the start and legible only afterwards.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [the emergency](place_the_emergency.md) throughout, undetected.
Established only by the weighing that [narrowing](process_narrowing.md) has already stopped.

## Load Bearing

Without it the practised response is simply correct and there is no misfit at all.
It is load bearing because it is the **switch**: the same two moves that make a room excellent at familiar threats make it wrong at this one, and nothing about the moves changes, only what they are applied to.

## Apparent

Nothing. It does not present as novelty.
A new kind of threat looks, at the start, exactly like a known kind, which is why the diagnosis has to be done rather than seen, and why the clock is what decides.

## Yearbook

Named by [missing](process_missing.md), once the response has run.
It is what [the cue that was available](piece_the_cue_that_was_available.md) would have established, had there been room to weigh it.
