# Persona: The Intention

## Taxonomy

[Intention](position_intention.md). The deliverable, and the harm.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A settled conditional commitment to do something monstrous if a condition holds.
Real rather than performed, since performance is what is being checked for.

## Action

Exists.
Gets read, and works by being read.

## Shadow

Is the entire product, which is what makes it different from a means.
An instrument is used and set down; this is never used, because using it is the failure case, so the whole of what the arrangement ever manufactures is a state of mind held by a person, and the ordinary defence that it was only a tool has nothing to point at.

## Tell

Is judged by a rule that says it is wrong to intend what it would be wrong to do, and breaks the rule by working.
