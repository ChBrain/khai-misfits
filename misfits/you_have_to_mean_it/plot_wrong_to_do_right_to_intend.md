# Plot: Wrong to Do, Right to Intend

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Mean It](play_you_have_to_mean_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The ordinary rule that it is wrong to intend what it would be wrong to do, applied to this.

## Action

[The Ones Who Judge It](persona_the_ones_who_judge_it.md), on [Judge the Intention](plan_judge_the_intention.md), take the rule and read what it returns.

## Stage

It condemns the party whose intention is the reason nothing happened, and the repair costs more than the verdict.

## Tension

The rule cannot be relaxed in a way that stays a rule.
Weakening it to permit an intention whose purpose is prevention licenses anybody who claims a sufficient purpose, which is the exact move the principle exists to refuse: its whole work is declining to weigh intentions by their consequences. Keeping it condemns somebody who reasoned correctly and prevented the catastrophe. Neither is a small adjustment, so the case is held rather than solved.
