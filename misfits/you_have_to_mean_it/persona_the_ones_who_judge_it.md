# Persona: The Ones Who Judge It

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Judge It](position_ones_who_judge_it.md). The moral account, arriving to find the usual rule broken.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Anybody applying the ordinary principle that it is wrong to intend what it would be wrong to do.
A rule that is sound almost everywhere and is why we hold people to their intentions at all.

## Action

Applies the rule.
Finds it returns that the person who prevented the catastrophe did the wrong thing.

## Shadow

Cannot repair the rule without giving up something it needs.
Weakening it to permit an intention whose purpose is prevention licenses anybody who claims a good enough purpose; keeping it condemns the person whose intention was the reason nothing happened; and neither of those is a small adjustment, because the rule's whole work is refusing to weigh intentions by their consequences.

## Tell

Is left with a verdict that neither answer makes comfortable.
