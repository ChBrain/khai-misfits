# Plan: Judge the Intention

## Taxonomy

A **plain intent**: the intent of [The Ones Who Judge It](persona_the_ones_who_judge_it.md), inside [You Have to Mean It](play_you_have_to_mean_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The Ones Who Judge It](persona_the_ones_who_judge_it.md)

## Direction

Apply the rule that it is wrong to intend what it would be wrong to do.

## Orders

(Take the rule, which is sound almost everywhere; apply it to a conditional intention aimed at prevention; read the verdict; look for the repair.)

## Implementation

([Standing down](process_standing_down.md), which is available and costly; the rule's own refusal to weigh intentions by their consequences, which is what it is for.)

## Targets

- [ ] the rule is applied honestly and returns its answer
- [ ] the answer condemns the party whose intention is the reason nothing happened
- [ ] relaxing it for a preventive purpose licenses anybody who claims a sufficient purpose
- [ ] so neither keeping the rule nor amending it is a small adjustment, and the case has to be held rather than solved
