# Piece: The Standing Order

## Taxonomy

[The Room Where It Is Formed](place_the_room_where_it_is_formed.md). The hand tied, in advance and on purpose.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Written in [the room where it is formed](place_the_room_where_it_is_formed.md), and running on [the morning after](place_the_morning_after.md) whether anybody wants it to or not.

## Load Bearing

It works by removing the capacity to reconsider from the person who would reconsider, and there is no other way.
An intention the moment will argue you out of does not deter, because the other side can see the argument coming as clearly as you can: so the only commitment that reads as credible is one arranged so that the moment cannot reach the decision, which means delegating, pre-releasing, or building something that proceeds unless stopped.

## Apparent

Procedure. Delegation, predelegation, a system that continues absent an intervention.
Sensible contingency planning, indistinguishable in form from ordinary preparedness.

## Yearbook

Is the only device that makes the intention hold, and the device that guarantees the act if the intention is ever tested.
Cannot be made reversible without becoming useless, since reversibility is exactly what the other side is looking for.
