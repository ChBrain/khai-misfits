# Plot: Nobody Decides on the Day

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Mean It](play_you_have_to_mean_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An intention the moment will argue anybody out of, and the need for it to hold anyway.

## Action

[Tying the hand](process_tying_the_hand.md): [the standing order](piece_the_standing_order.md) is written so that [the morning after](place_the_morning_after.md) cannot reach the decision.

## Stage

The capacity to reconsider is removed in advance from the person who would reconsider, because nothing else makes it stick.

## Tension

The device that makes the threat credible is the device that guarantees the act if it is ever tested.
Reversibility is exactly what the other side looks for, so a commitment that can be unwound does not deter and a commitment that deters cannot be unwound. Which means the arrangement's success and its worst outcome run through one mechanism: the same standing order that bought the peace is what proceeds, from nobody's decision, on the one morning everybody would want somebody to decide.
