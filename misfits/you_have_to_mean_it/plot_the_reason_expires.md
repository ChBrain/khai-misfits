# Plot: The Reason Expires

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Mean It](play_you_have_to_mean_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The morning after, and the question of what carrying it out would now buy.

## Action

[The Reason That Expires](piece_the_reason_that_expires.md) is examined on [the morning after](place_the_morning_after.md), and found already spent.

## Stage

Nothing. Deterrence had one purpose, the attack has already defeated it, so the threat is rational to make and irrational to keep.

## Tension

The asymmetry is visible in advance to both parties, which is what forces everything else.
Every reason to carry the threat out was a reason to have intended it, and none survives into the moment when acting is possible. Both sides can see that coming with equal clarity, so a threat left in the hands of whoever will be standing there on the morning is a threat the other side has already discounted: the argument that undoes it is available before it is needed.
