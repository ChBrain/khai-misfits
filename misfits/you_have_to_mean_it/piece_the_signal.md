# Piece: The Signal

## Taxonomy

[The Room Where It Is Formed](place_the_room_where_it_is_formed.md). The threat as it reaches the other side.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Sent from [the room where it is formed](place_the_room_where_it_is_formed.md), read by [the deterred](persona_the_deterred.md), and worth exactly what the reading makes it worth.

## Load Bearing

It cannot carry its own verification, which is why the intention has to be real.
Words, postures and deployments can all be arranged by somebody who does not mean them, and the reader knows that, so nothing inside the signal settles the only question being asked: what remains is an estimate of a person, and the cheapest way to pass an estimate of a person is to be the person.

## Apparent

Deliberate, legible, and reviewed. Everything a serious commitment looks like.
Identical, on its face, to a bluff prepared by somebody competent.

## Yearbook

Works only while it is believed, and is believed only while it is meant.
Is never tested by the party it is aimed at, since testing it is the thing it exists to prevent.
