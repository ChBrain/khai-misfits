# Pitch: If It Works, Nothing Happens

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Mean It](play_you_have_to_mean_it.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Cold and procedural, the register of a staff paper. A threat drafted, a posture arranged, a chain of command written so that nobody has to decide anything on the day.
Every step deliberate, reviewed, and defensible.

## Undertow

Under the procedure, a state of mind that has to be real to work, because the other side is estimating a person rather than reading a sentence.
And a morning that never comes, precisely because it was made real.

## Nerve

The vertigo of producing nothing but an intention, and the intention being the part that is wrong.

## Echo

Afterward: no attack, no retaliation, no event, decades of it, and a hand tied in advance so that the one who would reconsider cannot.
