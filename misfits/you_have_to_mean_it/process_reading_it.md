# Process: Reading It

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Mean It](play_you_have_to_mean_it.md). The examination that makes a performance insufficient.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A decision with everything at stake, and no obligation to take anything at face value.
[The Deterred](persona_the_deterred.md), holding [Deterred](position_deterred.md), with [the signal](piece_the_signal.md).

## Direction

Toward an estimate of a person rather than a reading of a sentence.
Toward declining to attack, if the estimate comes back the right way.

## Lever

Care, applied by a party with more at stake in the estimate than anybody, which is the one thing it cannot be asked to stop doing.

## Echo

A bluff that does not survive being examined, and a trap closed by somebody doing nothing wrong.
