# Process: Standing Down

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Mean It](play_you_have_to_mean_it.md). The exit, priced honestly rather than raised to be dismissed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The option of not doing any of this, considered by somebody who can see what it costs.
[The Ones Who Judge It](persona_the_ones_who_judge_it.md), holding [Ones Who Judge It](position_ones_who_judge_it.md).

## Direction

Toward keeping the rule that it is wrong to intend what it would be wrong to do.
Toward not manufacturing a state of mind as an instrument.

## Lever

The refusal itself, which is available and which is read by [the deterred](persona_the_deterred.md) exactly as it is meant.

## Echo

A clean intention, and the decision handed to whoever is still willing to make it.
