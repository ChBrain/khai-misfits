# Persona: The Deterred

## Taxonomy

[Deterred](position_deterred.md). The reader, whose reading is the whole test.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The party the threat is for: an aggressor with a decision to make and every reason to make it carefully.
Watching, and not obliged to accept anything at face value.

## Action

Estimates a person rather than reading a sentence.
Declines to attack, if the estimate comes back the right way.

## Shadow

Is doing nothing wrong and is the reason the trap closes.
A threat could be faked if nobody examined it, and this party's whole position is examining it, with more at stake in the estimate than anybody: so the care that makes deterrence work at all is the same care that makes a performance insufficient, and it cannot be asked to look less hard.

## Tell

Never learns whether it was right, because it did not test it.
