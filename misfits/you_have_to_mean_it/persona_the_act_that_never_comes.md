# Persona: The Act That Never Comes

## Taxonomy

[Act That Never Comes](position_act_that_never_comes.md). The success condition, and the reason nothing can be shown.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The retaliation: prepared for, provided for, and not carried out.
The outcome everybody wanted, indistinguishable from an outcome nobody arranged.

## Action

Does not happen.
Leaves no evidence that the arrangement was what prevented it.

## Shadow

Removes its own justification by arriving.
On the morning after an attack, the deterrence has already failed and the one purpose the threat had is gone, so carrying it out buys nothing: the case for acting was entirely a case for having intended, and it does not survive into the moment when acting is possible.

## Tell

Is the proof of success and cannot be produced, since its absence is the outcome.
