# Process: Tying the Hand

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Mean It](play_you_have_to_mean_it.md). Making it hold against the moment, by removing the moment's reach.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An intention the morning will argue anybody out of, and the need for it to hold anyway.
[The Standing Order](piece_the_standing_order.md), drafted in [the room where it is formed](place_the_room_where_it_is_formed.md).

## Direction

Toward delegation, predelegation, and systems that proceed unless stopped.
Toward a commitment the other side cannot see a way out of, because there is not one.

## Lever

Removal of the capacity to reconsider from the person who would reconsider, which is the only device that works.

## Echo

A credible threat, and a guarantee that if it is ever tested the act follows from nobody's decision.
