# Persona: The One Who Must Mean It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Must Mean It](position_one_who_must_mean_it.md). The party that has to form the thing, and cannot form it lightly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A state, a command, a person with the authority to answer an attack, drafting a threat meant to prevent one.
Competent, deliberate, and with no appetite for what is being threatened.

## Action

Forms a settled conditional intention, of a kind that would carry through.
Arranges, afterwards, that it cannot be dropped.

## Shadow

Cannot hold it provisionally, which is where every ordinary escape closes.
A reserved intention is not an intention: to intend on condition that you may reconsider at the moment is to have decided nothing, and the moment is precisely when reconsidering is correct, so the reservation is not a small qualifier on the state of mind but its removal.

## Tell

Produces one thing only, and it is the thing that would be wrong to produce.
