# Piece: The Reason That Expires

## Taxonomy

[The Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md). The case for acting, which does not survive the case arising.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held on [the morning after](place_the_morning_after.md), and already spent by the time it is needed.

## Load Bearing

It is the whole asymmetry, and it is what the moral rule was not built for.
Deterrence has exactly one purpose, and an attack has already defeated it, so every reason to carry the threat out was a reason to have intended it and none of them is a reason to act now: the threat is rational to make and irrational to keep, which is not a weakness in this arrangement but the shape of all of them.

## Apparent

A question with an obvious answer, asked by anybody standing there.
The most reasonable thought available on the morning, which is why it has to be made unreachable.

## Yearbook

Is visible in advance to both parties, which is why the hand must be tied before it matters rather than after.
Makes the intention irrational to hold and the holding of it the only thing that worked.
