# Plot: Only If You Mean It

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Mean It](play_you_have_to_mean_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A threat drafted, and somebody on the other side with every reason to work out whether it is a posture.

## Action

[Reading it](process_reading_it.md): [the deterred](persona_the_deterred.md), holding [Deterred](position_deterred.md), estimates a person rather than a sentence.

## Stage

The bluff does not survive that examination, so [the intention](persona_the_intention.md) has to be formed for real rather than performed.

## Tension

The trap is closed by a party doing nothing wrong.
[The Signal](piece_the_signal.md) carries no verification of itself: words, postures and deployments can all be arranged by somebody who does not mean them, and the reader knows it. So the only question left is an estimate of a person, and the cheapest way to pass an estimate of a person is to be the person. Nobody can ask the deterred to examine less carefully, since examining carefully is the whole of its position and the reason deterrence works at all.
