# Position: One Who Must Mean It

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Mean It](play_you_have_to_mean_it.md). The seat that has to hold it, and cannot hold it lightly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Authority to answer an attack, and no appetite for answering one.
A threat that only works if it is meant.

## Orders

Mean it. Then make it undroppable.

## Loses

Every provisional version of itself.
An intention held on condition that it may be reconsidered is not one, and the moment at which reconsidering would happen is exactly the moment at which reconsidering is correct, so the reservation does not soften the seat, it empties it.

## Drives

Toward producing a state of mind and nothing else.
