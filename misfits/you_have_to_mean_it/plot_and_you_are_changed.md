# Plot: And You Are Changed

## Taxonomy

[You Have to Mean It](play_you_have_to_mean_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The arrangement working, for decades, with nothing to show for it.

## Action

[The Intention](persona_the_intention.md), holding [Intention](position_intention.md), goes on existing in somebody, because that is the only form in which it works.

## Stage

The corrupted intention is seen to be the product rather than a side effect, held by a person who did the calculation correctly.

## Tension

There is no version of this that buys the peace with something else.
Meaning less is read as meaning less. Meaning it and reserving the right to reconsider is not meaning it. [Standing down](process_standing_down.md) keeps the intention clean and hands the decision to whoever is still willing to make it, which is a real option with a real price and not a way of having both. So what is left is somebody who reasoned their way, step by defensible step, into being a person who holds that.
