# Place: The Studio

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). Where the content is made, shaped to gather and hold the audience advertisers will pay for. Hosts [The Bait](plot_the_bait.md), [The Buying Mood](plot_the_buying_mood.md), [What Goes Unmade](plot_what_goes_unmade.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A production floor, turning out the paper, the broadcast, the feed, each piece built to attract a desirable audience and hold it in a buying frame.
Nothing here looks like a trap: writers write, editors edit, the work is made the way work is made anywhere.

## Holds

The free lunch that draws the crowd, and the mood that keeps it once drawn.
Here sits [the Free Lunch](piece_the_free_lunch.md), the content given away to gather an audience, and here is worked [the Receptive Mood](piece_the_receptive_mood.md), the engaged, buying-frame state the content is shaped to produce. And here, unmet, waits [the Unserved Interest](piece_the_unserved_interest.md), what the audience needs but the studio has no reason to make.

## Offers

To the audience, a free lunch worth taking, genuinely made, genuinely enjoyed.
To the outlet, a crowd worth selling: the same production that offers the audience its show delivers the outlet the audience it needs at [the Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md).

## Withheld

What the audience needs but will not attend to, and what would hold attention but unsettle the advertisers paying for it.
Both go undermade here, not withdrawn by anyone's hand but simply never commissioned, since the studio answers to what draws and holds a desirable audience and not to what that audience is owed (Smythe; Herman & Chomsky, structurally).
