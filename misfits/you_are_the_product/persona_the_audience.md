# Persona: The Audience

## Taxonomy

[Audience](position_audience.md), inside [You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The archetype of the reader or viewer: given the free lunch and glad of it, its attention the product sold, its own interest served only where it coincides with what holds attention.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The customer, the one the whole thing is for. It feels itself served, courted even, handed good content for nothing, and takes the outlet's generosity at face value as a gift with no bill attached.

## Action

Take the free lunch. The Audience comes to [the Studio](place_the_studio.md) for [the Free Lunch](piece_the_free_lunch.md) and stays in [the Receptive Mood](piece_the_receptive_mood.md) the content is shaped to hold, on [Take the Free Lunch](plan_take_the_free_lunch.md), a trade it enters freely and enjoys (Smythe). It is no dupe: the lunch is real and the bargain open to anyone who looks.

## Shadow

It is the product, not the customer, and cannot see the difference. Because the outlet is paid to deliver it and not to serve it, the Audience is left with [the Unserved Interest](piece_the_unserved_interest.md), what it needs but will not attend to going unmade, and it loses nothing it can name, since no story is forbidden, only never made (Smythe). It keeps its free content and loses its own interest, the guest who was the meal.

## Tell

It feels served, and it is being sold.
