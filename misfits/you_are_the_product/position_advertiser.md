# Position: Advertiser

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The seat of the paying customer, which buys the audience's attention in a receptive mood, and whose money, not any order it gives, decides whose interest the outlet serves.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A need for attention: a large and desirable audience, held in a frame to buy, to set its message before.
The money that funds the outlet, paid not for the content but for the audience the content gathers, so its coin is the outlet's living.
No need to command, because the outlet, living on that money, will make what keeps the audience and the advertiser content of its own accord, so the coin does the work an order would.

## Orders

Buy the attention.
Pay for the audience you need, large, desirable, and in a buying mood, since that attention is the product on sale and your message needs it.
Know that you give no orders and need none, that your money aligns the outlet to you by the plain logic of who pays, and that the tilt it produces is the market's and not a censorship of yours.

## Loses

Nothing at all, and its clean hands are part of the trap.
Because it shapes the content by funding it and never by commanding it, the seat bears no visible mark of the tilt it causes, so the outlet makes what pleases it without an order given and the harm attaches to no instruction anyone can point to.
It keeps its money's power and loses no cause for blame, the customer whose coin is the only instruction and whose hands stay clean.

## Drives

Toward the one that shapes all it touches and touches nothing,
buying the attention it needs and giving no order, no censorship in it,
so the seat that funds the content is the seat it is all made to please, the tilt a matter of who pays and not of any command.
