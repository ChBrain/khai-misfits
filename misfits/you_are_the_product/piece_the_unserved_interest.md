# Piece: The Unserved Interest

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The audience's own informational or civic interest, served only where it happens to coincide with attention-capture.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Studio](place_the_studio.md), the residue [Shaping](process_shaping.md) leaves behind.
Not a place anything is made, but the shape of what never gets commissioned: the story that would inform but not hold attention, the report that would matter but unsettle the advertisers paying the bills.

## Load Bearing

Without it the tilt would be invisible, since a studio that served the audience perfectly well by accident would leave nothing missing.
With it the misfit shows its cost: what the audience needs to know as citizens or as people making decisions is made only where that need happens to also draw and hold [the Receptive Mood](piece_the_receptive_mood.md) advertisers will pay for, and what would hold attention but unsettle the advertisers paying for it goes undermade for the same structural reason (Smythe; Herman & Chomsky, structurally, credited for the no-villain market-structure claim only). Guard against reading the gap as a conspiracy or a deliberate suppression: nothing here is censored, no one refuses to run the story; the interest simply is not what is being paid for, so it is made only where it happens to also capture attention, and where it does not, it is never commissioned at all rather than blocked.

## Apparent

Just what the audience wants, or does not want: a niche subject with no readership, a story people were never going to click.
It looks like ordinary editorial judgement responding to demand, the studio giving the audience what it asks for and skipping what it does not; nothing marks the missing story as a casualty of who pays rather than a fair read of what the audience prefers.

## Yearbook

The residue [Shaping](process_shaping.md) leaves at [What Goes Unmade](plot_what_goes_unmade.md), unmet in [the Audience](persona_the_audience.md).
It is what [the Receptive Mood](piece_the_receptive_mood.md) does not require and [the Advertiser's Coin](piece_the_advertisers_coin.md) does not reward, and it is what [the Reader's Coin](piece_the_readers_coin.md) exists to try to recover.
