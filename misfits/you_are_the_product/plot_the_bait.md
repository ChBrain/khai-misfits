# Plot: The Bait

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Studio](place_the_studio.md), before the audience arrives. The outlet has no crowd yet, so its opening move is to give something away: a paper, a broadcast, a feed, made to be worth the reader's time and asking nothing for it.

## Action

[Gathering](process_gathering.md): [the Outlet](persona_the_outlet.md), holding [the outlet's seat](position_outlet.md), runs [Deliver the Audience](plan_deliver_the_audience.md), making [the Free Lunch](piece_the_free_lunch.md) that draws the crowd, while [the Audience](persona_the_audience.md), holding [the audience's seat](position_audience.md), runs [Take the Free Lunch](plan_take_the_free_lunch.md) and comes for what is on offer.

## Stage

Smythe's term for the content an advertising-funded medium gives away is the free lunch: not the end of the outlet's business but its cost of production, spent to assemble an audience that can then be sold. The show, the paper, the feed is genuinely made and genuinely worth having, which is exactly why it works as bait; nothing about taking it announces that the taking is the gathering, or that what is gathered is not the audience's readership but the audience itself (Smythe).

## Tension

The audience believes it is being served a free lunch, and it is right, the lunch is real. It is also being gathered, and neither the noticing nor the being gathered is optional: to take the one is to become the other.
