# Piece: The Sold Attention

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The audience's own attention, produced and packaged and sold to advertisers, the thing the outlet is actually in the business of making.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md), carried there by [Selling](process_selling.md).
The audience [the Free Lunch](piece_the_free_lunch.md) gathered at [the Studio](place_the_studio.md), now counted, sorted by demographic and minute of engagement, and offered as inventory.

## Load Bearing

Without it the outlet has a crowd and nothing to do with it.
With it the crowd becomes a commodity: Smythe's account holds that what an advertising-funded medium produces is not its content but its audience, gathered by the content and sold as attention to whoever will pay for it, and Jhally and Livant carry the mechanism further, the audience's own watching the labour that manufactures the very commodity being sold (Smythe; Jhally & Livant). The Sold Attention is the pivot of the whole structure: it is the point at which the audience stops being who the outlet serves and becomes what the outlet has to offer. Guard against reading the audience as the customer: the audience is the product, priced by the thousand and packaged for delivery, and the customer, the one actually paying, is the advertiser waiting at [the Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md).

## Apparent

Popularity: a large readership or a strong rating, the plain measure of a show or a paper doing well.
Counted this way it looks like nothing more sinister than success, the audience a sign the outlet is liked; nothing about the number itself shows that the number's real function is as a price tag, quoted to a buyer who is not the audience at all.
