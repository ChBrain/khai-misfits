# Piece: The Advertiser's Coin

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The advertiser's money that funds the outlet and so decides whose interest the outlet serves.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md), paid in by [the Advertiser](persona_the_advertiser.md).
The revenue line the outlet lives on, entered against the audience just delivered and not against anything the audience felt about the content it received.

## Load Bearing

Without it the outlet has an audience and no way to keep making the content that gathers one.
With it the outlet has a customer, and the customer is who the revenue tells it to serve: Smythe's account of the audience commodity holds that the outlet's income comes from delivering the audience to the advertiser, not from pleasing the audience directly, so the money that actually funds the next issue or the next broadcast rewards delivery and is indifferent to satisfaction (Smythe). The Advertiser's Coin is load bearing because it is the plain mechanism of the tilt: whoever pays the bill is whose interest the bill answers to, and here the bill is paid by the advertiser. Guard against reading the advertiser as a censor or a villain: it buys the attention it needs and nothing more, and its money aligns the outlet to it by the ordinary logic of who pays, not by any order given or influence exerted.

## Apparent

Ordinary commerce: an advertiser buying space or airtime, the outlet selling it, a transaction like any other in a market.
It looks like a simple exchange of value for value, no different from any vendor being paid by any customer; nothing about the transaction itself reveals that this particular customer is not the party the content is nominally made for.
