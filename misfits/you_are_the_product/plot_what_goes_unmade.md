# Plot: What Goes Unmade

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Studio](place_the_studio.md), and what never reaches its production floor. No memo forbids any story; the assignment simply never gets made.

## Action

[the Audience](persona_the_audience.md), holding [the audience's seat](position_audience.md), is left with [the Unserved Interest](piece_the_unserved_interest.md): what it needs but will not attend to, and what would hold its attention but unsettle the advertisers paying for [the Receptive Mood](piece_the_receptive_mood.md) that [the Outlet](persona_the_outlet.md) makes instead, both left undermade.

## Stage

The harm follows from no one's censorship, only the plain logic of who pays: an outlet funded by delivering a desirable audience to advertisers has no revenue line for content that fails to draw that audience or that draws it while unsettling the advertiser footing the bill, so both go undermade (Smythe; Herman & Chomsky, structurally, credited only for the market-structure claim, never for the reading of a press conspiring by design). This is not The Filter Bubble: no feed narrows by the reader's own clicks here, no personalisation is doing the work. The funding model tilts what is made at all, before any reader ever clicks on anything.

## Tension

No one forbids the story that goes unmade, and no one pays for it either, so it simply is not there, and its absence looks exactly like an ordinary day's output.
