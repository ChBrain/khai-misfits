# Process: Gathering

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). Borne at [The Studio](place_the_studio.md), precipitated by [The Outlet](persona_the_outlet.md), in [The Bait](plot_the_bait.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The outlet, making the content that will draw an audience before it has one to sell.
There is no crowd yet, so the outlet's first move is to build the thing that will gather one: a paper, a broadcast, a feed, offered free or near enough to it.

## Direction

From an empty room toward a gathered audience, drawn by content given free.
Nothing is charged for the show itself; the audience arrives because the content is worth its time, and its arrival is the whole point of making it.

## Lever

The content is the free lunch, worth making because it draws the crowd whose attention is the real product, so the show is a cost of production and not the thing sold (Smythe).
The outlet spends on the content the way a shop spends on a window display: not to sell the display, but to bring the customer in for what is actually for sale.
