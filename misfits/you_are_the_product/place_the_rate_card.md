# Place: The Rate Card

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). Where the gathered audience's attention is priced and sold to advertisers. Hosts [Delivering the Audience](plot_delivering_the_audience.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A price list, audiences quoted by the thousand, by the demographic, by the minute of engagement.
Nothing here is content: it is inventory, the same audience the studio gathered now counted, sorted, and offered for a price.

## Holds

The audience packaged for delivery, and the money that packaging is made to draw.
Here sits [the Sold Attention](piece_the_sold_attention.md), the audience's attention produced at [The Studio](place_the_studio.md) and now priced as a commodity, and here is counted [the Advertiser's Coin](piece_the_advertisers_coin.md), the revenue that funds the outlet and so decides whose interest it serves.

## Offers

To the advertiser, the attention of a desirable audience, delivered at a stated rate.
To the outlet, the revenue that funds it, earned by delivering the audience rather than by pleasing it (Smythe).

## Withheld

Any revenue line for pleasing the audience on its own terms.
The rate card prices delivery, not satisfaction, so nothing here rewards the outlet for serving the reader's interest where that interest fails to translate into attention a buyer wants.
