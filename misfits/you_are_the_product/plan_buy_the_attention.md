# Plan: Buy the Attention

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the advertiser's seat](position_advertiser.md), inside [You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Advertiser](position_advertiser.md)

## Direction

Pay for the audience you need, large, desirable, and in a buying mood, since that attention is the product on sale and your message needs it. Know that you give no orders and need none, that your money aligns the outlet to you by the plain logic of who pays, and that the tilt it produces is the market's and not a censorship of yours.

## Orders

([The Advertiser](persona_the_advertiser.md) pays [the Advertiser's Coin](piece_the_advertisers_coin.md) at [the Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md) for [the Sold Attention](piece_the_sold_attention.md) that [Selling](process_selling.md) delivers, buying a desirable audience held in [the Receptive Mood](piece_the_receptive_mood.md) and giving no order at all.)

## Implementation

([the Advertiser's Coin](piece_the_advertisers_coin.md) at [the Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md) funds the outlet, so the outlet of its own accord makes what keeps the audience and the advertiser content, the coin doing the work an order would and leaving the advertiser's hands clean.)

## Targets

- [ ] the attention of a large, desirable audience in a buying mood is paid for
- [ ] no order is given, the money left to align the outlet by the plain logic of who pays
- [ ] the tilt is understood as the market's doing and not a censorship of the advertiser's
- [ ] the clean hands are seen as part of the trap, the coin the only instruction given
