# Plot: Where Interests Meet

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Paywall](place_the_paywall.md), two outlets set side by side: one advertising-funded whose audience and advertiser want the same thing, one whose interests pull apart.

## Action

The bound is drawn at the gate itself: [the Reader's Coin](piece_the_readers_coin.md) realigns the customer outright, and even where [the Advertiser's Coin](piece_the_advertisers_coin.md) still funds the outlet, the tilt worked by [Shaping](process_shaping.md) is felt only where [the Audience](persona_the_audience.md)'s interest and [the Advertiser](persona_the_advertiser.md)'s diverge, softening wherever the two coincide.

## Stage

The hedge is load bearing and not a footnote: advertising funding can make excellent content exactly where attention-capture and quality point the same way, a receptive audience and a well served audience turning out, in those cases, to be one and the same (Smythe; Wu). The harm staged across [The Bait](plot_the_bait.md), [Delivering the Audience](plot_delivering_the_audience.md), [The Buying Mood](plot_the_buying_mood.md), and [What Goes Unmade](plot_what_goes_unmade.md) is specific to the divergence cases, not a law that every ad-supported outlet is worthless or that every audience is helpless before it. This is not After You: the market here forms and clears, its failure is not that it never comes into being but that its ordinary functioning, in the divergence cases, tilts the product against the very audience it was built to gather.

## Tension

The trap is exactly as deep as the gap between what holds a reader and what serves one, no deeper, and the gap is the whole of the misfit: not a verdict on advertising, a measurement of the distance in any one case.
