# Process: Shaping

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). Borne at [The Studio](place_the_studio.md), precipitated by [The Outlet](persona_the_outlet.md) answering [the Advertiser's Coin](piece_the_advertisers_coin.md), in [The Buying Mood](plot_the_buying_mood.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The outlet, adjusting what it makes to answer what the advertiser is paying for.
The rate card has already told the outlet what sells: not any audience, but an audience held in an engaged, receptive state, so the outlet reaches back into production and shapes the content to make that state more reliably.

## Direction

From content that might serve the audience toward content that holds it in the mood advertisers pay for.
The drift is not away from quality as such, but away from whatever quality does not also produce a receptive, buying-frame audience.

## Lever

It is the receptive, engaged, buying-frame audience the advertiser pays to reach, so the content is shaped to make that mood rather than to serve understanding (Jhally & Livant).
The audience's watching is itself a kind of labor the outlet extracts and refines, engagement is the output, and understanding rides along only when it happens to coincide with holding attention in that mood.
