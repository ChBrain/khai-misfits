# Position: Outlet

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The seat of the advertising-funded medium, which gives away content to gather an audience and sells that audience to advertisers, so the party it serves is the party who pays.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Content to give away, a free lunch worth making because it draws the crowd whose attention is the real product.
A revenue that comes from delivering the audience and not from pleasing it, so its customer is the advertiser and its living depends on the advertiser's coin.
No income from the reader to answer to, because the reader pays nothing, so nothing in the funding obliges it to serve the reader except where serving also gathers a saleable crowd.

## Orders

Deliver the audience.
Make the free lunch that gathers a large and desirable audience, hold it in a receptive mood, and sell its attention to advertisers, because that is where the revenue is and a business serves its customer.
Know that this is not a swindle but the plain logic of who pays, and that the reader's own interest is served only where it coincides with the attention you sell, no story of theirs forbidden, only unmade.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and yet what it serves is set by who funds it.
Because its revenue is the advertiser's and not the reader's, the seat rationally makes what keeps a desirable audience and its advertisers content, so its generosity to the reader and its service to the advertiser are one act, and the reader's interest is served only by accident.
It keeps its free reach and loses no cause for blame, the giver of a lunch paid for by selling the guest.

## Drives

Toward the one that serves whoever pays it, and is paid by the advertiser,
giving the reader content for free and selling the reader on, no swindle in it,
so the seat that looks the reader's servant is the seat the market makes the advertiser's, what it makes serving who pays and not who reads.
