# Position: Patron

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The seat of the one who pays to be served, the subscriber, member, or public funder who would make the audience the customer, the costly remedy that realigns who is served.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one realignment the tilt allows: to become the payer, so the one served is the one consuming.
A set of costly means: a subscription or membership that makes the reader the customer, or a public and licence-fee model that makes the public the customer.
No free version of it, because a paywall shrinks the audience and shuts out the unpaying, public money courts capture of what it funds, and donor money tilts to donors, so the alignment is always bought at a price.

## Orders

Pay to be served.
Become the payer, by subscription or public funding, so that the medium answers to the one who consumes and the product is aligned to them, since only then is the reader the customer and not the goods.
Accept the cost, and know that your paying shuts the door on everyone who will not or cannot pay, forfeiting the free reach the advertising model gives, so the cure is real and never free.

## Loses

The free reach, and the many it shuts out, spent to buy the audience's place as the customer.
Because the alignment costs, the seat that makes the reader the payer loses those who will not or cannot pay, and where the public purse pays instead it risks the capture of what it funds, so the remedy that ends the tilt is bought with reach or with independence.
It keeps the one real cure and bears its whole price, the one who pays so as not to be sold.

## Drives

Toward the one that buys back the audience's place at a real cost,
becoming the payer so the served and the paying are one, no free version of the cure,
so the seat that holds the only fix is the seat that forfeits the free reach to have it, the reader made the customer at the price of the crowd.
