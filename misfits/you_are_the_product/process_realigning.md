# Process: Realigning

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). Borne at [The Paywall](place_the_paywall.md), precipitated by [The Patron](persona_the_patron.md), in [The Reader Pays](plot_the_reader_pays.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The patron, offering to pay for the content directly rather than take it as the free lunch.
Where the advertiser's coin has set the terms until now, the patron steps in with a subscription, a licence fee, or a membership, and asks to be served on that basis instead.

## Direction

From an audience that is the product toward an audience that is the customer, by paying.
Nothing about the content changes by itself; what changes is who the outlet is answerable to once that party is the one whose money it lives on.

## Lever

Only when the reader or the public becomes the payer is the one served the one consuming, and that realignment is costly, a paywall shutting out the unpaying, public money courting capture (Wu).
The remedy is real and not free: it trades the advertiser's tilt for a narrower reach or a new dependency, so realigning is a genuine cost paid to buy back alignment, not a correction that arrives for nothing.
