# Piece: The Free Lunch

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The content given away free, the bait made only to draw the crowd whose attention is the real thing sold.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Studio](place_the_studio.md), turned out by [Gathering](process_gathering.md).
The paper, the broadcast, the feed: whatever the outlet hands the reader at no charge, built to be worth taking and so worth gathering an audience around.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is no crowd to sell, and nothing downstream to sell it to.
With it the outlet has its draw: the Free Lunch is Smythe's term for the content an advertising-funded medium gives away, not as an end but as the cost of production it pays to assemble an audience it can then sell (Smythe). It has to be genuinely good enough to take, or no one gathers, but its worth to the outlet is measured by the crowd it pulls in, not by any value the content holds on its own. Guard against reading the content as the product being sold: it is the bait, a line item spent to gather the audience, and what the outlet actually sells downstream is [the Sold Attention](piece_the_sold_attention.md) that gathering produces.

## Apparent

Generosity: a paper or a broadcast handing over its work for nothing, journalism or entertainment made for its own sake.
It reads as an outlet giving the audience something for free because it can, or because that is simply what the medium does; the free lunch looks like the product itself, the very thing the outlet is in business to make, and nothing about the page or the screen announces that its true purpose is to gather a crowd for sale elsewhere.

## Yearbook

Taken by [the Audience](persona_the_audience.md) through [Take the Free Lunch](plan_take_the_free_lunch.md), staged at [the Bait](plot_the_bait.md).
It is the first move of the structure: the Free Lunch gathers what [the Studio](place_the_studio.md) then holds in [the Receptive Mood](piece_the_receptive_mood.md), and what [the Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md) prices as [the Sold Attention](piece_the_sold_attention.md).
