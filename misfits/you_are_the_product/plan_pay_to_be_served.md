# Plan: Pay to Be Served

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the patron's seat](position_patron.md), inside [You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Patron](position_patron.md)

## Direction

Become the payer, by subscription or public funding, so that the medium answers to the one who consumes and the product is aligned to them, since only then is the reader the customer and not the goods. Accept the cost, and know that your paying shuts the door on everyone who will not or cannot pay, forfeiting the free reach the advertising model gives, so the cure is real and never free.

## Orders

([The Patron](persona_the_patron.md) pays [the Reader's Coin](piece_the_readers_coin.md) through [Realigning](process_realigning.md) at [the Paywall](place_the_paywall.md), making the audience the customer so the one served is the one who pays, at the cost of the reach the paywall shuts out.)

## Implementation

([Realigning](process_realigning.md) at [the Paywall](place_the_paywall.md) makes the reader or the public the payer, so [the Reader's Coin](piece_the_readers_coin.md) buys back the audience's place as the customer, the tilt softening where payer and consumer are one and the reach forfeited where the paywall shuts the unpaying out.)

## Targets

- [ ] the reader or the public is made the payer, so the served is the one who consumes
- [ ] the cost is accepted, the paywall's forfeited reach and the public purse's risk of capture owned
- [ ] the realignment is understood as the one real fix, and as no free hero
- [ ] the tilt is seen to soften where payer and consumer are the same party
