# Plot: The Buying Mood

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Studio](place_the_studio.md), production resumed, now with a rate card behind it. The outlet already knows what sells: not any audience, but an audience held engaged, in a frame receptive to buying.

## Action

[Shaping](process_shaping.md): [the Outlet](persona_the_outlet.md), holding [the outlet's seat](position_outlet.md), answering [the Advertiser's Coin](piece_the_advertisers_coin.md), runs [Deliver the Audience](plan_deliver_the_audience.md), making [the Receptive Mood](piece_the_receptive_mood.md), content that holds the audience engaged and primed to buy.

## Stage

The audience's watching is itself a kind of labour the outlet extracts: it is the receptive, engaged audience the advertiser pays to reach, so content is shaped to produce that mood rather than to serve understanding, engagement the output and comprehension only along for the ride when the two happen to coincide (Jhally & Livant). This is not Metric Fixation: nothing here is measured and gamed, no proxy stands in for a goal and gets hit while the goal dies. The served party was simply the payer from the start; there was never a purer target to game away from.

## Tension

The content is good at holding a reader and need not be good for one, and nothing on the page marks which is which.
