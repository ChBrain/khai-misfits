# Plot: The Reader Pays

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Paywall](place_the_paywall.md), a gate asking for a subscription, a licence fee, a membership, before the content opens.

## Action

[Realigning](process_realigning.md): [the Patron](persona_the_patron.md), holding [the patron's seat](position_patron.md), runs [Pay to Be Served](plan_pay_to_be_served.md), paying [the Reader's Coin](piece_the_readers_coin.md) so the audience becomes the customer.

## Stage

Only when the reader or the public is the one paying is the one served the one consuming; the remedy is documented across the same field that documents the trap, from penny press to feed, and it is never free (Wu). A paywall shuts out whoever will not or cannot pay, narrowing reach to buy back alignment; public or licence-fee funding asks a collective willingness to fund and courts a capture of its own; membership or endowment money tilts toward the donors and members who supply it. No hero arrives at [the Paywall](place_the_paywall.md); realigning is a cost paid, not a correction that arrives for nothing.

## Tension

To be the customer instead of the product, you must pay, and the paying itself shuts the door on everyone who will not or cannot follow you through it.
