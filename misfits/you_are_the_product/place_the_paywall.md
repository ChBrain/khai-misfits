# Place: The Paywall

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). Where the reader can become the payer and so the customer, at the cost of shutting out the unpaying. Hosts [The Reader Pays](plot_the_reader_pays.md), [Where Interests Meet](plot_where_interests_meet.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A gate, standing between the reader and the content, asking for a subscription, a licence fee, a membership, before it opens.
Nothing free crosses here; what crosses is money, from the reader instead of the advertiser.

## Holds

The payment that would remake who the outlet answers to.
Here is deposited [the Reader's Coin](piece_the_readers_coin.md), the subscription or public or membership funding that, once taken, makes the audience the one the outlet is paid to serve (Wu).

## Offers

To those who pay, a product aligned to them: the realignment [The Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md) cannot offer, since here the one served is the one who paid.

## Withheld

The free lunch, and reach to those who will not or cannot pay.
The paywall trades the advertiser's tilt for a narrower audience, shutting out whoever stays outside the gate, so the realignment is bought, never given.
