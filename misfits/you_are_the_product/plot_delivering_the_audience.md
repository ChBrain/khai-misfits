# Plot: Delivering the Audience

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md), where the crowd [the Studio](place_the_studio.md) gathered is counted, sorted, and quoted a price. Nothing here is content anymore; it is inventory.

## Action

[Selling](process_selling.md): [the Outlet](persona_the_outlet.md), holding [the outlet's seat](position_outlet.md), delivers [the Sold Attention](piece_the_sold_attention.md) to [the Advertiser](persona_the_advertiser.md), who, holding [the advertiser's seat](position_advertiser.md), runs [Buy the Attention](plan_buy_the_attention.md), paying [the Advertiser's Coin](piece_the_advertisers_coin.md) for it.

## Stage

The outlet's revenue comes from delivering the audience to the advertiser, not from pleasing the audience directly, so the money that funds the next issue rewards delivery and is indifferent to satisfaction: the advertiser is the customer, and the reader is the product (Smythe). This is not Regulatory Capture: nothing here turns over time, no watchdog drifts from an original mandate. The outlet serves the party who pays from the very first rate card it ever issues, the alignment built in rather than corrupted.

## Tension

The reader is the one thing in the room being bought and sold, quoted by the thousand at [the Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md), and the only party present who paid nothing for the seat and is owed nothing for occupying it.
