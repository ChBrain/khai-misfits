# Persona: The Patron

## Taxonomy

[Patron](position_patron.md), inside [You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The archetype of the one who pays to be served: the subscriber, member, or public funder who would make the audience the customer, the costly remedy that realigns who is served.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A mark, or a mug, paying for what others get free. It looks like the one who did not have to pay and did, buying a thing the outlet next door gives away, generous or gullible depending on who is asked.

## Action

Pay to be served. The Patron pays [the Reader's Coin](piece_the_readers_coin.md) through [Realigning](process_realigning.md) at [the Paywall](place_the_paywall.md), on [Pay to Be Served](plan_pay_to_be_served.md), so that the one who consumes is the one who pays and the product is aligned to them (Wu). It buys back the audience's place as the customer.

## Shadow

Its cure is real and it is dear. Because the alignment costs, the Patron's paywall shrinks the audience and shuts out everyone who will not or cannot pay, forfeiting the free reach the advertising model gives, and where the public purse pays instead it courts capture of what it funds, so the remedy that makes the reader the customer is bought at a price and is no free hero (Wu). It keeps the one real fix and bears its whole cost, the one who pays so as not to be sold.

## Tell

It pays for what others get free, and is the only one being served.
