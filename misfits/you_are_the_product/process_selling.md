# Process: Selling

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). Borne at [The Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md), precipitated by [The Outlet](persona_the_outlet.md) toward [The Advertiser](persona_the_advertiser.md), in [Delivering the Audience](plot_delivering_the_audience.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The outlet, turning the audience it has gathered into inventory for the advertiser.
Having drawn the crowd at [The Studio](place_the_studio.md), the outlet's next move is to count it, describe it, and quote a price for reaching it.

## Direction

From a gathered audience toward that audience sold, its attention delivered to the advertiser.
The audience does not change hands physically; what moves is a slot of its attention, priced and delivered against the rate card's terms.

## Lever

The outlet's revenue comes from delivering the audience and not from pleasing it, so the advertiser is the customer and the reader the product (Smythe).
Every incentive the outlet answers to from this point runs through the advertiser's willingness to pay for what has been delivered, not through the reader's satisfaction with what it received.
