# Piece: The Reader's Coin

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The subscription, public, or membership funding that would make the audience itself the customer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Paywall](place_the_paywall.md), paid in by [the Patron](persona_the_patron.md).
The gate standing between the reader and the content, taking money from the reader instead of the advertiser, and so answering, at last, to the reader.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is no way out of the structure at all, the audience permanently the product and never the customer.
With it there is a real if costly realignment: Wu's history of the attention merchants traces the recurring business of gathering attention with a free lunch and reselling it, and traces too its counter-model, media paid for by the audience it serves rather than by the advertiser it delivers to (Wu). Wherever the reader's coin replaces [the Advertiser's Coin](piece_the_advertisers_coin.md), the one paying and the one consuming become the same party, and the outlet's rational interest turns toward serving them. Guard against reading it as a free fix: each realignment buys back the audience's place at a real cost, a paywall shutting out whoever will not or cannot pay, public funding courting political capture, donor or membership money tilting toward donors and members, and the advertising model persists at large because the free lunch outcompetes the paid one for reach. This piece also carries the bound of the whole structure: the tilt softens exactly where the payer and the consumer are the same, which is what paying the Reader's Coin buys.

## Apparent

A clean solution: just charge for it, and the whole problem is solved.
Paying to read looks like it simply removes the advertiser and the misfit along with it; what the sticker price hides is that the fix is bought, not given, traded for reach among those unwilling or unable to pay, so the realignment is real but never costless.
