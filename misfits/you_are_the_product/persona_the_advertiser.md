# Persona: The Advertiser

## Taxonomy

[Advertiser](position_advertiser.md), inside [You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The archetype of the paying customer: it buys the audience's attention in a receptive mood, and its money, not any order it gives, decides whose interest the outlet serves.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A mere sponsor, off to the side, politely funding content it has nothing to do with. It looks like a benefactor whose name appears in the margins and whose only wish is to be seen, touching the content not at all.

## Action

Buy the attention. The Advertiser pays [the Advertiser's Coin](piece_the_advertisers_coin.md) at [the Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md) for [the Sold Attention](piece_the_sold_attention.md) that [Selling](process_selling.md) delivers, on [Buy the Attention](plan_buy_the_attention.md), seeking a large and desirable audience held in a frame to buy (Jhally and Livant). It gives no orders and needs none.

## Shadow

Its coin aligns the outlet to it without a word. Because the outlet lives on the advertiser's money, it rationally makes what keeps a desirable audience and its advertisers content, so the advertiser shapes the content by paying for it and never by commanding it, the tilt a matter of who funds and not of any censorship (Smythe). It keeps its clean hands and owes no cause for blame, the customer whose money is the only instruction given.

## Tell

It gives no orders, and everything is made to please it.
