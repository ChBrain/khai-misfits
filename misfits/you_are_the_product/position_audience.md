# Position: Audience

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The seat of the reader or viewer, given the free lunch and glad of it, its attention the product sold, its own interest served only where it coincides with what holds attention.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A free lunch to enjoy, real content handed over at no charge, a bargain open to anyone who looks.
An attention that is the product, harvested as it watches and sold to advertisers, so its watching is the work the outlet lives on.
No standing as the customer, because it pays nothing and so is owed nothing, its own interest served only where it happens to coincide with the attention being sold.

## Orders

Take the free lunch.
Enjoy the content given for nothing, since it is real and freely offered and you are no dupe for taking it.
Know that you are the product and not the customer, that your attention is what is sold and your own interest is served only by accident, and that you will lose nothing you can name, because no story of yours is forbidden, only never made.

## Loses

Its own interest, served only where it coincides with the sale, and unnamed because unmade.
Because the outlet is paid to deliver it and not to serve it, the seat is left with what it needs but will not attend to going undermade and what unsettles advertisers going undermade, a loss it cannot point to since nothing was taken, only never offered.
It keeps its free content and loses its own service, the guest who was the meal.

## Drives

Toward the one that feels served and is sold,
taking a free lunch it enjoys and being the product it does not see, no dupe in it,
so the seat the whole thing seems to be for is the seat it is not for at all, the reader the product until the reader becomes the payer.
