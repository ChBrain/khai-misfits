# Plan: Deliver the Audience

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the outlet's seat](position_outlet.md), inside [You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Outlet](position_outlet.md)

## Direction

Make the free lunch that gathers a large and desirable audience, hold it in a receptive mood, and sell its attention to advertisers, because that is where the revenue is and a business serves its customer. Know that this is not a swindle but the plain logic of who pays, and that the reader's own interest is served only where it coincides with the attention you sell, no story of theirs forbidden, only unmade.

## Orders

([The Outlet](persona_the_outlet.md) makes [the Free Lunch](piece_the_free_lunch.md) through [Gathering](process_gathering.md) at [the Studio](place_the_studio.md), then through [Selling](process_selling.md) delivers [the Sold Attention](piece_the_sold_attention.md) to [the Advertiser](persona_the_advertiser.md), and through [Shaping](process_shaping.md) holds the crowd in the mood the advertiser pays to reach.)

## Implementation

([Gathering](process_gathering.md) makes the bait and [Selling](process_selling.md) delivers the crowd at [the Rate Card](place_the_rate_card.md), because the revenue is [the Advertiser's Coin](piece_the_advertisers_coin.md) and not the reader's, so the audience is the product and its interest is served only where it coincides with the attention on sale.)
