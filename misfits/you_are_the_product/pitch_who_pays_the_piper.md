# Pitch: Who Pays the Piper

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a medium serves the one who pays it, and under advertising that is not the reader.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reasonable, even generous. An outlet giving away good content for free, an advertiser buying the attention it needs, an audience gladly taking the lunch. Nothing here is a swindle; the content is real and often good, and the trade is open to anyone who looks.

## Undertow

The free content is the bait, and the audience it gathers is the product, sold to advertisers (Smythe). The outlet's revenue comes from delivering the audience, not from pleasing it, so what it makes is shaped to hold a desirable audience in a buying mood, and the reader's own interest is served only where it coincides with holding attention.

## Nerve

That the reader who felt served was the thing being sold, and lost nothing they can name, since no story is forbidden, only unmade. Who pays the piper calls the tune, and the reader is not paying.

## Echo

The lunch is free because you are not the guest, you are the meal.
