# Plan: Take the Free Lunch

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the audience's seat](position_audience.md), inside [You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Audience](position_audience.md)

## Direction

Enjoy the content given for nothing, since it is real and freely offered and you are no dupe for taking it. Know that you are the product and not the customer, that your attention is what is sold and your own interest is served only by accident, and that you will lose nothing you can name, because no story of yours is forbidden, only never made.

## Orders

([The Audience](persona_the_audience.md) comes to [the Studio](place_the_studio.md) for [the Free Lunch](piece_the_free_lunch.md) and stays in [the Receptive Mood](piece_the_receptive_mood.md) the content is shaped to hold, taking a bargain it enjoys while its attention is [the Sold Attention](piece_the_sold_attention.md) delivered to the advertiser.)

## Implementation

([the Free Lunch](piece_the_free_lunch.md) at [the Studio](place_the_studio.md) is real and freely taken, and the same watching that enjoys it harvests [the Sold Attention](piece_the_sold_attention.md), so the audience is the product it does not see and is left with [the Unserved Interest](piece_the_unserved_interest.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the free content is enjoyed, real and freely offered, the taker no dupe
- [ ] the audience is understood to be the product and not the customer
- [ ] the attention that watching harvests is seen to be what is sold to the advertiser
- [ ] the loss is grasped as unnameable, no story forbidden, only never made
