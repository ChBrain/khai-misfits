# Persona: The Outlet

## Taxonomy

[Outlet](position_outlet.md), inside [You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The archetype of the advertising-funded medium: it gives away content to gather an audience, sells that audience's attention to advertisers, and so serves the advertiser who pays it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A generous public servant, giving its content away for free. It looks like the one doing the audience a service, informing and entertaining at no charge, asking nothing of the reader but their time.

## Action

Gather, and sell. The Outlet makes [the Free Lunch](piece_the_free_lunch.md) through [Gathering](process_gathering.md) at [the Studio](place_the_studio.md) to draw a crowd, then through [Selling](process_selling.md) delivers [the Sold Attention](piece_the_sold_attention.md) to [the Advertiser](persona_the_advertiser.md), on [Deliver the Audience](plan_deliver_the_audience.md) (Smythe). Answering the advertiser's coin, it shapes the content through [Shaping](process_shaping.md) to hold a desirable audience in a buying mood.

## Shadow

Whom it serves is decided by who pays it. Because its revenue comes from delivering the audience and not from pleasing it, the outlet rationally serves the advertiser, exactly as any business serves its customer, and the reader's own interest is served only where it coincides with the attention it sells, so its generosity to the reader and its service to the advertiser are one act (Smythe). It keeps its free reach and owes no cause for blame, the giver of a lunch paid for by selling the guest.

## Tell

It gives you the content for free, and sells you to someone else.
