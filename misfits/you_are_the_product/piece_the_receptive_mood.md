# Piece: The Receptive Mood

## Taxonomy

[You Are the Product](play_you_are_the_product.md). The engaged, desirable, buying-frame state the content is shaped to produce in the audience it holds.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Studio](place_the_studio.md), worked into the content by [Shaping](process_shaping.md).
Not a separate output but a quality bred into the same show or story or feed the audience takes as [the Free Lunch](piece_the_free_lunch.md): the pacing, the tone, the placement, all tuned toward a watching that stays open and receptive.

## Load Bearing

Without it the audience is merely gathered, present but not necessarily worth much to anyone paying for it.
With it the audience is worth more, because Jhally and Livant's account of watching as working holds that it is the audience's attentive, engaged consciousness itself that the medium harvests and sells, so content is made not simply to be seen but to hold viewers in the state most valuable to deliver: attentive, engaged, and open to being sold to (Jhally & Livant). The Receptive Mood is the load-bearing refinement of the Sold Attention: it is not raw eyeballs that fetch the best price but eyeballs held in the right frame of mind. Guard against reading the shaping as manipulation of a helpless audience: the audience is not duped, it takes a free lunch it genuinely enjoys, and the tilt lies not in coercion but in what gets made, content built to hold a buying mood rather than content built to serve understanding.

## Apparent

Good content: a show that is simply engaging, well paced, hard to look away from, the craft any maker would be proud of.
It reads as quality, the ordinary aim of anyone making something worth watching; nothing distinguishes, from the inside, content shaped to serve the audience's understanding from content shaped to hold the audience in a state a buyer will pay to reach.

## Yearbook

Produced at [the Buying Mood](plot_the_buying_mood.md), the state [Shaping](process_shaping.md) is tuned to reach.
It refines what [the Free Lunch](piece_the_free_lunch.md) gathers into what [the Sold Attention](piece_the_sold_attention.md) prices, and its residue, what the mood leaves out, is [the Unserved Interest](piece_the_unserved_interest.md).
