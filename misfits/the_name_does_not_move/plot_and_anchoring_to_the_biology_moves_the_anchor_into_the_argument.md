## Cue

## Action

## Stage

A serious, worked answer that removes the arbitrariness of the specimen entirely.

## Tension

The reform trades an anchor that is fixed and sometimes wrong for one that is correct and moves, and the two are not comparable on a single scale.
Tying a name to ancestry is right in principle and answers the objection completely. It also makes the name's meaning depend on a phylogenetic hypothesis, and revising phylogenetic hypotheses is what the discipline does: the anchor lands on the one thing under active argument. So this is not a bad proposal being resisted by conservatives. It is the same quantity traded the other way, and whichever way it is traded somebody downstream is holding a string that either points at the wrong animal or stops pointing at the same one.
