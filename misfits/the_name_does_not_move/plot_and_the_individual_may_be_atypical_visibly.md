# Plot: And the Individual May Be Atypical, Visibly

## Taxonomy

[The Name Does Not Move](play_the_name_does_not_move.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two hundred specimens, a sequencing run, and a type that sits outside the cloud.

## Action

[Revising the group](process_revising_the_group.md): [the reviser](persona_the_reviser.md), holding [Reviser](position_reviser.md), shows conclusively that [the holotype](piece_the_holotype.md) is not representative.

## Stage

A finding accepted at once by everybody in the field, and a name exactly where it was.

## Tension

Everybody can see the error, and that is what separates this from a system whose errors are invisible.
The demonstration is a measurement, is checkable and is believed immediately: nobody is deceived, nothing is hidden, and no better instrument is wanted. What follows is that the name applies to something other than what it has been used for, which is a fact established rather than a fault corrected, because the name goes with the specimen. So the field knows precisely what is wrong and the knowing changes nothing, which is the opposite of a system that cannot detect its own drift.
