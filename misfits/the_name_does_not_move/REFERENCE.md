# The Name Does Not Move: Reference

The research warrant for The Name Does Not Move.
The term is the house's; the play runs on biological nomenclature and on what a discipline pays for a referent
nobody can argue about, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging nomenclature as **a handle bought with accuracy, knowingly**: a species name is fastened to one animal
in a jar and to the date somebody published it. If the animal turns out to be a juvenile, a hybrid or an
outlier, everybody can see that, and the name stays on it. If a forgotten description from 1830 turns up, that
name wins, however long the current one has been in use. The play models the documented mechanics, the referent
that must be uncontestable rather than representative, the second anchor that removes discretion as completely
as the first, the error that is fully visible and cannot be acted on, the rule producing an outcome nobody wants
while working exactly as designed, the commission that relocates discretion rather than removing or restoring
it, and the alternative code that trades fixity for correctness without escaping the trade, as the load-bearing
vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a badly written code or an obstinate committee.

The governing law is that both anchors were chosen for being uncontestable rather than for being right, and
being uncontestable is what makes them sometimes wrong and always immovable.

**The error is fully visible, and that is what distinguishes this from a system that cannot detect its own
drift.** A reviser who shows the type to be unrepresentative is believed at once, because the demonstration is a
measurement and the measurement is good. Nobody is deceived, nothing is concealed, and no better instrument is
wanted. What follows is that the name applies to something other than what it has been used for, which is a fact
established rather than a fault corrected, because the name goes with the specimen. The field knows precisely
what is wrong and the knowing changes nothing.

What removes any possibility of blame from the codes is that a name with no fixed referent is not a name. A
description needs interpreting and two workers will interpret it differently; a population's boundary is the
thing under dispute. An individual in a jar cannot drift, be reinterpreted or be argued about, which is the
entire specification, and nothing in that specification asks the individual to be typical, because typicality is
a judgement and a judgement is what was being avoided.

What removes it from the constituency that suffers is that the same constituency requires the cause. A name
tracking the current best phylogeny would change with every revision, and every schedule, statute, dataset and
citation keyed to it would quietly come to mean something else, with nothing announcing the change. That is a
larger problem than an inaccurate anchor, not a smaller one, which is why rigidity is the codes' first principle
rather than a convenience.

And the commission closes the case against the last party, because its power must stay scarce to remain worth
having. It exists precisely because the rules do sometimes produce results nobody wants, and it works: names in
universal use are conserved and the literature is spared. A rule set aside whenever it is inconvenient offers
nobody anything to rely on, which was the reason for having one, so the number of names that ought to be moved
will always exceed the number that can be. **The discretion is not removed by the codes and not restored by the
commission: it is relocated, rationed and made expensive.**

It is distinct from the house's **Exactly One Kilogram**, which the slate line required it to be tested against
before authoring, and the test was run in the sweep. There the artefact **is** the unit, so if it gains or loses
mass the unit moves with it and no comparison inside the system can detect the drift: the failure is that
nobody can know. Remove that engine, by supposing an error that everybody can see, and this misfit stands
entire, because seeing it is the normal case here. A reviser measures the holotype against two hundred
specimens, publishes, and is believed the same afternoon. So one misfit stages an error that cannot be detected
and this one stages an error that is detected, published, agreed and then knowingly left in place, which is a
different structure carrying a different remedy: theirs needed a new definition, and no definition helps here.

It is distinct from the house's **Grandfathering in Codes**, where a prospective standard binds new stock and
exempts what already exists, so the old thing is **released** from a rule everybody agrees with. Here nothing is
exempted and the old thing is what everybody is bound **to**: the anchor is not spared the rule, it is the rule.

It is distinct from the house's **Bug for Bug**, where an implementation must reproduce a predecessor's defects
because observable behaviour rather than specification is what everything downstream depends on: that is the
closest mechanism in the house, since both stage stability preferred over correctness. The difference is who
chose. There the constraint is somebody's earlier mistake, faithfully preserved because too much was built on
it, and a clean world would not have it. Here nobody made a mistake and the constraint was **written down on
purpose**, by people who considered exactly this case and decided that a name which moved when the biology moved
would not be a name.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the codes as badly
  written or the commission as obstinate, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: a name
  with no fixed referent is not a name, and every alternative anchor needs interpreting or is itself the dispute;
  the type is arbitrary by design and has not failed at anything it was asked to do; priority ends disputes by
  removing exactly the judgement that would let it tell a used name from a forgotten one; the reviser's
  demonstration is a measurement and is believed immediately; the constituency injured by the rigidity requires
  it more than it requires the accuracy; the commission's power works and must stay scarce to keep working; and
  the alternative code is a serious answer that relocates the difficulty. The failure is authored by nobody and
  emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a fixed handle and a true description are bought with each
  other.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Petitioning the commission is
  the real remedy, works, and cannot be granted in every deserving case without destroying what makes a grant
  worth having. Designating a neotype repairs a lost or inadequate type and does not reach a type that is
  present, adequate and unrepresentative. Anchoring names to clades removes the arbitrariness completely and
  fastens the name to a hypothesis under active revision. Better original designation would have required
  describers to know what later material would show. Keeping two vocabularies, one stable for law and one
  current for science, is what happens informally and doubles the thing that was supposed to be a single handle.
  And knowing the whole structure, which every party does, leaves the jar in the drawer.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the codes, the type method, the principle of priority, the
  plenary powers and the alternative system are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: **whether names should
  be anchored to specimens or to clades is genuinely contested**, the alternative code is a serious and
  developed proposal rather than a foil, and the play carries the argument rather than settling it; the codes
  differ between zoology, botany and bacteriology in provisions this account treats as one, which is the play's
  own simplification; the frequency with which types prove unrepresentative is not quantified here and the play
  makes no claim about how common it is; and the account of priority omits the many detailed provisions by
  which the codes already soften it. **The play makes no claim that any name is wrong, or that any code should
  be changed**, only that the anchors were chosen for being uncontestable, and that a discipline which needs a
  handle that holds still is paying for it in accuracy and knows the price.
