# Persona: The Reformer

## Taxonomy

[Reformer](position_reformer.md). The one who would anchor names to the biology instead.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A worked alternative code, in which a name is tied to a clade defined by an explicit statement rather than to a jar.

## Action

Proposes anchoring names to ancestry.
Publishes a full parallel system rather than a complaint.

## Shadow

Moves the anchor onto the one thing the field is actively arguing about.
Tying a name to a clade is right in principle and removes the arbitrariness of the jar completely. It also makes the name's meaning depend on a phylogenetic hypothesis, and phylogenetic hypotheses are revised constantly, which is what the discipline is for. So the reform trades an anchor that is fixed and sometimes wrong for one that is correct and moves, and the two failures are not comparable on a single scale.

## Tell

Removes the arbitrary anchor by fastening the name to the argument.
