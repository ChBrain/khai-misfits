# Process: Designating the Type

## Taxonomy

[The Name Does Not Move](play_the_name_does_not_move.md). The act that fixes the name, once.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A new species being described, and a requirement to say what the name refers to.
A describer, at [the type drawer](place_the_type_drawer.md), often long ago.

## Direction

Toward a single named individual that the name will mean for ever.

## Lever

The code's requirement of a name-bearing type, which exists so the name has a referent nobody can argue about.

## Echo

A name anchored to one animal, chosen with the knowledge of its day.
