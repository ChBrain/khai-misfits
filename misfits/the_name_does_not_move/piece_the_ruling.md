# Piece: The Ruling

## Taxonomy

[The Literature](place_the_literature.md). The override, granted sparingly and for that reason.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Sought by [petitioning the commission](process_petitioning_the_commission.md) and issued by [the commission](persona_the_commission.md).

## Load Bearing

It is discretion reintroduced deliberately, and it must stay scarce or it undoes what it protects.
The codes removed judgement with the type and the date, found the results sometimes intolerable, and built a body that may set the rules aside case by case. That body works: names in universal use are conserved and the literature is spared. It also cannot grant every deserving case, because a rule disapplied whenever it is inconvenient offers nobody anything to rely on, which was the point of having it. So the discretion is not removed by the codes and is not restored by the commission: it is relocated, rationed and made expensive.

## Apparent

The safety valve. Proof the system is not rigid after all.

## Yearbook

Restores the judgement the rules removed, in quantities small enough not to remove the rules.
