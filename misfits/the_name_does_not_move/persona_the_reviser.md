# Persona: The Reviser

## Taxonomy

[Reviser](position_reviser.md). The one who finds the type unlike the rest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Two hundred specimens, a sequencing run, and a holotype that sits outside the cloud.

## Action

Revises the group.
Shows, publicly and conclusively, that the name-bearing individual is not representative of what the name is used for.

## Shadow

Is right, is believed, and has not moved the name an inch.
The finding is accepted immediately by everybody in the field, because it is a measurement and the measurement is good. What it changes is the biology and not the nomenclature: the name goes with the specimen, so demonstrating that the specimen is atypical demonstrates that the name applies to something other than what everybody has been using it for, which is a fact about the name rather than a correction to it.

## Tell

Proves the point completely and changes nothing by proving it.
