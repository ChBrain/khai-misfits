# Persona: The User

## Taxonomy

[User](position_user.md). Everybody downstream, who needs the name to hold still.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A conservation schedule, a trade regulation, a database, and thirty years of papers using one binomial.

## Action

Cites the name.
Builds a legal instrument, an ecological time series and a field guide on it.

## Shadow

Needs the property that produces the error, and needs it more than the accuracy.
A name that tracked the current best phylogeny would change with every revision, and every schedule, statute, dataset and citation built on it would silently come to mean something else. That is not a smaller problem than an inaccurate anchor: it is a larger one, and it is the reason this seat's requirement is the code's first principle rather than a convenience. So the constituency harmed by the misfit is also the constituency that requires its cause.

## Tell

Is served by the rule and injured by it, in the same property.
