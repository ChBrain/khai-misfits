# Piece: The Holotype

## Taxonomy

[The Type Drawer](place_the_type_drawer.md). One individual, carrying a name for ever.

## Owner

## Place

## Load Bearing

Its authority comes from having been designated and from nothing else, which is what makes it usable and what lets it be wrong.
The code needed a referent that could not drift, be reinterpreted or be argued over, and one animal in a jar is exactly that. It never required the individual to be typical, well preserved or even mature, because those are judgements and a judgement is the thing being avoided. So a holotype that turns out to be a juvenile, a hybrid or an outlier has not failed at anything it was asked to do.

## Apparent

The specimen. Presumably the best one they had.

## Yearbook

Is arbitrary by design, and the arbitrariness is the property that works.
