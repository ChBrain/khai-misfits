# Persona: The Keeper

## Taxonomy

[Keeper](position_keeper.md). The one who holds the object the name hangs on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A drawer, controlled humidity, a jar with a label written in 1847, and a loan register.

## Action

Keeps it.
Sends it out to anybody with a reason to look at it.

## Shadow

Curates an object whose authority comes from nothing about the object.
It is not the best specimen, the most typical or the best preserved: it is the one somebody designated, and that is the whole of its standing. This seat can say precisely what is in the jar and that is exactly what the code wants of it, because the code needs a fixed thing to point at rather than a good one. So the care is real and the object it protects is arbitrary by design.

## Tell

Preserves perfectly a thing chosen for being pointed at rather than for being right.
