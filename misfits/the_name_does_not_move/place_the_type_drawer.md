## Withheld

Any reason to think it is representative.
The place is a store of designated objects rather than of good ones, and that is what the code asked for: it needed something that could not drift, be reinterpreted or be argued about, and an individual animal in a jar is exactly that. Whether it resembles the rest of its kind was never part of the specification, which is why it can fail to and nothing about the arrangement is thereby broken.
