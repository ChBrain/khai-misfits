# Place: The Moment

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). Where the sooner reward has drawn near and spiked, the ranking reverses, and the immediate reward is taken. Hosts [The Nearer It Comes](plot_the_nearer_it_comes.md), [Just This Once](plot_just_this_once.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The near reward, looming large now that it is within reach.
The plan, made in the calm, met here by a self that stands much closer to the reward than the self who made it.

## Holds

[The Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md).
Here the smaller-sooner reward's recovered value overtakes the larger-later, and the ranking set in the calm flips, though nothing about either reward has changed.

[The Now](piece_the_now.md).
Here the immediate reward is seized, the plan broken not by a change of mind but by the mere approach of what was, from a distance, the lesser choice.

## Offers

To the present self, the immediate reward within reach: near, vivid, and larger from here than the larger-later looks from this same distance.

## Withheld

The distance that made the larger-later look greater.
Once the sooner reward has neared, the calm's far view is no longer available to check against, and the moment shows only what looms.
