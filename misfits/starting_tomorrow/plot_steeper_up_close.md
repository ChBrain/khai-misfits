# Plot: Steeper Up Close

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Still at [the Calm](place_the_calm.md), the plan is set, and something in the ground beneath it has not yet been named. It is already there, quiet at this distance, shaping what the plan cannot yet feel.

## Action

[The Planner](persona_the_planner.md), from [the planner's seat](position_planner.md), meets [The Curve](piece_the_curve.md) at [The Calm](place_the_calm.md): the hyperbolic discount that falls far more steeply over the near future than the far, mild and unremarkable from here, nothing like what it will become.

## Stage

The near steepness means a reward's value does not fall off smoothly with distance but spikes sharply as it comes within reach, the engine beneath every reversal that follows in this play (Ainslie). The exact functional form, whether truly hyperbolic or the simpler quasi-hyperbolic approximation, remains debated, and no single equation is claimed here as settled.

## Tension

The curve looks gentle from afar and will spike up close.
