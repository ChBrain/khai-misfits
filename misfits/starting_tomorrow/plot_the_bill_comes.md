# Plot: The Bill Comes

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md), the larger-later reward chosen in the calm and lost in the moment comes due. Someone has to answer for it, though no one present at this hour chose to lose it.

## Action

[The Later Self](persona_the_later_self.md), from [the later self's seat](position_later_self.md), on [Start Again Tomorrow](plan_start_again_tomorrow.md) through [Re-Resolving](process_re_resolving.md), inherits [The Bill](piece_the_bill.md) at [The Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md): the larger-later reward lost, taken in full, and answered with the next plan, the one the next moment will break in turn.

## Stage

This is the war between the self that plans and the self that acts, waged again each time the later self, if naive, trusts a future self to keep a plan that self will reverse exactly as the last one did, since it will stand just as near its own reward when its own moment arrives (O'Donoghue and Rabin). The harm is authored by no one: the later self's persistence is real and blameless, its fresh resolve as sincerely meant as the first, and still it only refills the trap the curve will spring again.

## Tension

The persistence only refills the plan the moment will break again.
