# Position: Present Self

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The seat of the self at the moment of choice, for whom the near reward has spiked and overtaken the later, and who takes the sooner and breaks the plan.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A place on the clock, standing near the smaller-sooner reward where the Planner stood far from it.
A reward that looms large up close, its steeply-recovered value now above the larger-later's, so its own ranking has flipped.
The same rule the Planner used, to choose what looks best from where it stands, only where it stands is nearer.

## Orders

Take it now, and resolve to start tomorrow.
Choose what looks best from where you stand, and since the near reward now outweighs the later, take it, break the plan, and set a fresh resolution for tomorrow, which will meet the same near reward and flip the same way.
Know that your preference has genuinely reversed and not merely slipped, that you are not failing to hold a want you still have but acting on a want that has truly changed with the distance, and that you break the plan in good faith, reversed by the curve.

## Loses

The larger-later reward, and it loses it without feeling it lost.
Because from where it stands the near reward really is the larger, the seat takes the sooner in good conscience and forgoes the greater good the Planner chose, so the loss falls due later, to a self not yet on the clock, and is never weighed against the near reward in the moment it is taken.
It keeps its clear conscience and its reasons and loses no cause for blame, a self choosing rightly from where it wrongly stands.

## Drives

Toward the self on the clock for whom the near reward looms largest,
taking the sooner because its value has truly overtaken the later, no weakness in it,
so the seat that breaks the plan breaks it in good faith, its preference reversed by the distance and not by any want of resolve.
