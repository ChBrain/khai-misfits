# Pitch: The Nearer It Comes

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a reward's value spikes as it comes within reach, so the plan made in the calm is reversed by the moment.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Rueful, humane, sincere. An ordinary person who means the plan they make, to save, to quit, to rise early, and who breaks it in the moment and resolves, honestly, to start again tomorrow. Nothing here is laziness or bad faith; the resolution is real.

## Undertow

People discount the near future far more steeply than the far, so a reward's value does not fall smoothly with its distance but spikes as it comes within reach (Ainslie). So when both rewards are far off the larger-later wins, but as the smaller-sooner nears its recovered value overtakes the later, and the person's own ranking reverses, the plan broken not by a change of mind but by the mere approach (Laibson; O'Donoghue and Rabin).

## Nerve

That the self who plans and the self who acts are not the same self and want different things, so the far-sighted resolution is forever outvoted by the near-sighted moment on the clock. No one is weak; it is the shape of the curve, held against its limit, since the form is debated and not all impatience is a bias.

## Echo

The larger reward wins from a distance, but the nearer the smaller one comes the larger it looms, so the plan is always for tomorrow.
