# Persona: The Planner

## Taxonomy

[Planner](position_planner.md), inside [Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The archetype of the far-sighted self who, in the calm and with both rewards distant, chooses the larger-later and resolves to save or study or quit, and means it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person of resolve and foresight. It looks like the sensible self at its best, weighing the long run, choosing the greater good, and setting a firm intention, the very picture of someone in command of their life.

## Action

Resolve for later. At [the Calm](place_the_calm.md), on [Plan for Later](plan_plan_for_later.md) through [Resolving](process_resolving.md), the Planner makes [the Plan](piece_the_plan.md), choosing the larger-later reward while both are far off, and it meets [the Curve](piece_the_curve.md), the discount whose near steepness it cannot yet feel from this distance (Laibson; Ainslie).

## Shadow

Its resolve is sincere and it does not hold the clock. The Planner genuinely means the plan, and from the calm the larger-later plainly wins; but it makes the plan for a future moment it will not itself be present at, handing it to a present self who will stand much nearer the reward and see it much larger. It is not weak and not deceiving anyone, only a self planning for a self that will want something else when the time comes.

## Tell

It makes an honest plan for a moment it will not be the one to live.
