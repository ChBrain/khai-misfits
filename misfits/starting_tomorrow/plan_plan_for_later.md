# Plan: Plan for Later

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the planner's seat](position_planner.md), inside [Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Planner](position_planner.md)

## Direction

Weigh the long run, choose the larger-later reward while it plainly wins from here, and set a firm resolution, since from the calm the far-sighted choice is obvious and honest. Know that you make the plan for a moment you will not live, that the self who lives it will stand near the reward and see it much larger than you do now, and that your sincere resolution is handed to a self who will want something else when the near reward looms.
