# Position: Later Self

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The seat of the self who inherits the forgone larger-later reward and pays the bill, and who, if it does not foresee the reversal, becomes the next Planner and resolves again.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The bill of yesterday's moment, the larger-later reward lost to the near one the Present Self took.
A fresh resolve, the will to start again, the new plan for the greater good.
A blind trust in its own future self, if it is naive, expecting that self to keep the plan though that self will stand as near the reward as the last one did.

## Orders

Pay the bill, and resolve again for tomorrow.
Take the loss of the larger-later reward, dust yourself off, and make the next plan, choosing the greater good once more, since persistence is a virtue and tomorrow is a fresh start.
Know that unless you foresee the reversal your fresh resolve only refills the plan the moment will break again, that you are trusting a future self who will reverse exactly as the last one did, and that persistence alone runs the war between the self that plans and the self that acts one more round.

## Loses

The larger-later reward it inherits lost, and it re-stakes it on a self that will lose it again.
Because it pays the bill and, being naive, trusts its future self to keep the next plan, the seat forgoes the greater good twice over, once to yesterday's moment and again to tomorrow's, its persistence refilling the very plan the curve will break.
It keeps its fresh resolve and its blameless persistence and loses no cause for blame, a self that pays and plans and pays again.

## Drives

Toward the self who pays the bill and resolves anew,
inheriting the loss and trusting its future self to keep the next plan, persistent and blameless,
so the seat that starts again tomorrow, unless it foresees the reversal, only hands the same plan to the same moment, and pays once more.
