# Piece: The Mast

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The remedy and its bound: the commitment a foreseeing self makes to bind its future self in advance.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md), carried by [Binding](process_binding.md), voiced by [the Steward](persona_the_steward.md): the illiquid account, the deleted app, the mast Ulysses is tied to, set up where the near reward is not yet in reach to fight.

## Load Bearing

Taking the choice out of the moment removes the reward the reversal would flip, so the plan is kept by removing the moment's power to break it rather than by strengthening anyone's will (Laibson; O'Donoghue and Rabin). This is the remedy, but it carries its own bound, and the bound is part of the piece, not an afterthought to it.

The binding is open only to the sophisticated self that foresees its own bias in advance, not to the naive self that keeps trusting a future self who will reverse just as this one did. It spends real effort and real freedom: locking an account, deleting an app, tying a knot, all cost something to set up and something to live inside afterward. It can be undone or worked around, since a commitment device made by a person can usually be unmade by that same person given enough motive, and a self that can never change its mind is not always the wiser one for it. And the phenomenon it answers is itself contested: the functional form of the discount curve is debated, and careful elicitation has raised doubts about the size and even the presence of present bias in some settings, since not all impatience is a bias to begin with (Andreoni and Sprenger). The mast is real and it works when it holds, but it is not a cure for a diagnosis everyone agrees on, and it is not free.

## Apparent

Needless self-restriction, a person locking themselves out of their own choices. It looks like distrust turned inward, or an odd kind of fussiness, tying a knot against a wind that might never blow.
