# Plot: The Nearer It Comes

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Moment](place_the_moment.md), the smaller-sooner reward has drawn near enough to be taken. The far view that made the larger-later look plainly greater is no longer there to check against.

## Action

[The Present Self](persona_the_present_self.md), from [the present self's seat](position_present_self.md), meets [The Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md) at [The Moment](place_the_moment.md): the nearing reward's recovered value overtaking the larger-later, so the ranking set in the calm flips, though nothing about either reward has changed.

## Stage

This is the dynamic inconsistency of preference under a present-biased curve, the same two rewards ranked one way from a distance and the other way from close in (Ainslie). It is not Time Inconsistency: both trace to the same root in Strotz, but Time Inconsistency is strategic and outward, a promise made to others whose credibility fails when the promiser later deviates from it. Here the reversal is intrapersonal, a self overriding the plan it made for itself, with no counterparty to disappoint. Nor is it mere impatience: preferring the sooner reward is not by itself the misfit, since a person may rationally prefer sooner for reasons that have nothing to do with bias. The misfit is the reversal, the ranking flipping as time passes while the rewards themselves stay exactly what they were.

## Tension

The ranking flips though nothing about the rewards has changed.
