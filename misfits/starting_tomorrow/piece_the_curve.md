# Piece: The Curve

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The engine: the hyperbolic discount that falls far more steeply over the near future than the far.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

## Load Bearing

People discount the near future far more steeply than the far, hyperbolically or quasi-hyperbolically rather than at a steady exponential rate, so a reward's value does not fall off smoothly with distance but spikes sharply as the reward comes within reach (Ainslie). This is the mechanism beneath every reversal in the play: not a change of heart but a curve that is shallow far out and steep up close, so the same two rewards can be ranked one way from a distance and the other way from close in, without anything about the rewards themselves changing. The exact functional form, whether truly hyperbolic or the simpler quasi-hyperbolic approximation, is debated and no single equation is claimed here as settled.

## Apparent

Looks like ordinary impatience or discounting, nothing unusual. Everyone prefers a reward sooner to some degree, and the curve, glimpsed only in its distant, shallow stretch, looks like the same mild preference anyone would report.
