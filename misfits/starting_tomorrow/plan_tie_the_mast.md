# Plan: Tie the Mast

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), inside [Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Steward](position_steward.md)

## Direction

Foresee your own reversal, and bind your future self in advance, locking away the reward it would spend and the temptation it would take, so that when the moment comes it finds no near reward to spike and no choice left to make. Know that the binding spends real effort and real freedom, that it is open only to a self that foresees its own bias, that it can be undone or worked around, and that a self which can never change its mind is not always the wiser, the hedge you stand on and the bound you must name.

## Orders

([The Steward](persona_the_steward.md) sets [the Mast](piece_the_mast.md) through [Binding](process_binding.md) at [the Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md), binding the future self so the moment finds no near reward to spike.)

## Implementation

([Binding](process_binding.md) at [the Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md) sets [the Mast](piece_the_mast.md), because taking the choice out of the moment denies [the Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md) any reward to flip, so the plan is kept by removing the moment's power to break it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the future self is bound in advance, the reward locked away or the temptation removed
- [ ] the remedy is understood to work by taking the choice out of the moment
- [ ] the cost is named, the effort and the freedom spent and the binding open only to the sophisticated
- [ ] the bound is held, the binding circumventable and a self that cannot change its mind not always wiser
