# Persona: The Present Self

## Taxonomy

[Present Self](position_present_self.md), inside [Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The archetype of the self at the moment of choice, for whom the near reward has spiked and overtaken the later, and who takes the sooner and breaks the plan.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person giving in, weak in the moment. It looks like a lapse of willpower, someone who knows better and does worse, the familiar face of the self that cannot hold the line when the temptation is right there.

## Action

Take it now. At [the Moment](place_the_moment.md), the Present Self meets [the Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md), the nearing reward's value overtaking the larger-later, then on [Take It Now](plan_take_it_now.md) through [Grabbing](process_grabbing.md) takes [the Now](piece_the_now.md), the immediate reward seized and the plan broken, resolving all the while to start tomorrow (Ainslie).

## Shadow

Its lapse is a reversal and not a weakness. The Present Self is not failing to hold a preference it still has: its preference has genuinely flipped, because from where it stands, on the clock, the near reward really is larger, its steeply-recovered value really above the later's. So it chooses what looks best from where it stands, exactly as the Planner did, only where it stands is nearer. It breaks the plan in good faith, reversed by the curve and not by any want of resolve.

## Tell

It does what looks best from where it stands, and where it stands the near reward looms largest.
