# Persona: The Steward

## Taxonomy

[Steward](position_steward.md), inside [Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The archetype of the sophisticated self who foresees its own reversal and binds its future self in advance, and who marks that the binding is costly, circumventable, and not always the wiser course.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A wise and disciplined planner, master of itself. It looks like the calm foresight that sets up the standing order and locks away the temptation, the very picture of a self in command of its own future.

## Action

Tie the mast. At [the Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md), on [Tie the Mast](plan_tie_the_mast.md) through [Binding](process_binding.md), the Steward sets [the Mast](piece_the_mast.md), the commitment that binds the future self, the retirement locked away or the app deleted or the temptation not bought, so that the moment finds no near reward to spike (Laibson; O'Donoghue and Rabin).

## Shadow

Its foresight is real but its shield is costly and not always right. The Steward alone escapes the trap, because it does not trust its future self and binds it before the moment; but the binding spends real effort and real freedom, it is open only to the sophisticated who foresee their own bias and not to the naive who do not, it can be undone or worked around, and a self that can never change its mind is not always the wiser self. It claims no clean cure, only a way of taking the choice out of the moment.

## Tell

It does not trust its future self, so it ties that self to the mast before the song begins.
