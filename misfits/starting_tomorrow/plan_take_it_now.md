# Plan: Take It Now

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the present self's seat](position_present_self.md), inside [Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Present Self](position_present_self.md)

## Direction

Choose what looks best from where you stand, and since the near reward now outweighs the later, take it, break the plan, and set a fresh resolution for tomorrow, which will meet the same near reward and flip the same way. Know that your preference has genuinely reversed and not merely slipped, that you are not failing to hold a want you still have but acting on a want that has truly changed with the distance, and that you break the plan in good faith, reversed by the curve.

## Orders

([The Present Self](persona_the_present_self.md), having met [the Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md) at [the Moment](place_the_moment.md), takes [the Now](piece_the_now.md) through [Grabbing](process_grabbing.md), the immediate reward seized and the plan broken.)

## Implementation

([Grabbing](process_grabbing.md) at [the Moment](place_the_moment.md) takes [the Now](piece_the_now.md), because along [the Curve](piece_the_curve.md) the near reward's recovered value has overtaken the larger-later, so choosing what looks best from here breaks the plan.)

## Targets

- [ ] the immediate reward is taken and the plan broken, with a fresh resolve for tomorrow
- [ ] the lapse is understood as a genuine reversal of preference, not a want still held and dropped
- [ ] it is seen that the same rule the planner used chooses the sooner from nearer
- [ ] the break is owned as good-faith and curve-driven, no want of resolve in it
