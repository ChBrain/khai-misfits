# Piece: The Plan

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The setup: the resolution made in the calm, sincerely, to choose the larger-later reward.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Calm](place_the_calm.md), carried by [Resolving](process_resolving.md): the place where both rewards are still far off and nothing is yet at hand to grab.

## Load Bearing

From a distance, when both the smaller-sooner and the larger-later reward are equally far off, the larger-later plainly outweighs the smaller-sooner, so the far-sighted choice is made and sincerely meant: to save, to study, to quit, to rise early (Laibson). Nothing in the plan itself is false or thin. It is the correct answer to the question as it looks from where the planner stands, and the planner is not lying to themselves in making it. What the plan cannot see, because it is not yet in view, is the shape of the curve that will meet it later.

## Apparent

Admirable resolve and foresight. It looks like the person simply deciding, once, what they want, the way a reasonable person weighs a small thing against a large one and picks the large one. Nothing about it looks fragile.

## Yearbook

Made by [the Planner](persona_the_planner.md) through [Resolving](process_resolving.md) at [the Calm](place_the_calm.md), dramatized in [From a Distance](plot_from_a_distance.md). It rests on the shape of [the Curve](piece_the_curve.md), which the plan cannot yet feel, and it is broken by [the Reversal](piece_the_reversal.md), not because the planner changed their mind but because the ground under the plan moved.
