# Piece: The Reversal

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The flip: as the smaller-sooner reward nears, its recovered value overtakes the larger-later, and the person's own ranking reverses.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Moment](place_the_moment.md), met by [the Present Self](persona_the_present_self.md): the point where the sooner reward has closed enough distance for its value to have climbed past the later one.

## Load Bearing

This is the dynamic inconsistency of preference: the ranking of the same two rewards flips with the passage of time alone, under a present-biased discount curve, though neither reward has changed (Ainslie). Two distinctions matter here, because the reversal is easy to mistake for either.

It is not the strategic dynamic inconsistency that shares its root in Strotz: that version faces outward, a promise made to others, a policy or a commitment whose credibility fails because reneging becomes optimal once the moment to keep it arrives. This reversal has no counterparty and no promise made to anyone else. It is intrapersonal: a self overriding the plan its own earlier self made, the argument entirely within one person across two moments.

Nor is it mere impatience. Preferring a reward sooner is not automatically a bias; given uncertainty or the plain fact of one's own mortality, it can be the rational thing to want. What is at stake here is narrower and stranger than a preference for the sooner: it is that the same person's own ranking of the same two rewards flips as time passes, though the rewards themselves stand still. The misfit is the reversal, not the wanting-it-now.

## Apparent

Looks like a change of mind, or a lapse of willpower. From outside, and often from inside too, it reads as the person simply deciding differently now than they did then, or as their resolve giving out.
