# Piece: The Bill

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The harm: the larger-later reward forgone, come due to a later self who was not on the clock when the sooner was taken.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md), carried by [Re-Resolving](process_re_resolving.md), voiced by [the Later Self](persona_the_later_self.md): the place where the forgone reward is felt, after the fact, by whoever inherits the ledger.

## Load Bearing

This is the deferred cost of the broken plan: the greater good lost, paid by a self who did not take the near reward and cannot undo the taking. Being naive rather than fully foreseeing, this self trusts its own future self to keep the next plan, and so makes it again, sincerely, in the same way the last plan was made (O'Donoghue and Rabin). The bill is not paid by the self that spent it. It is paid by the one who comes after, which is the whole of why the loss keeps recurring: every payer is also, unknowingly, the next planner.

## Apparent

Bad luck, or the natural price of a lapse. It reads as simple consequence, the sort of cost anyone would expect to follow from giving in once, rather than as the structural output of a curve that will do the same thing again the next time a reward nears.
