# Position: Planner

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The seat of the far-sighted self who, in the calm and with both rewards distant, chooses the larger-later and resolves to save or study or quit, and means it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A clear view from a distance, both rewards far enough off that the larger-later plainly outweighs the smaller-sooner.
A sincere intention, the plan genuinely meant, to save or study or quit or rise early.
No hold on the moment it plans for, since it makes the plan for a future instant it will not itself be present at, and hands it to a self who will stand much nearer the reward.

## Orders

Plan for later, and mean it.
Weigh the long run, choose the larger-later reward while it plainly wins from here, and set a firm resolution, since from the calm the far-sighted choice is obvious and honest.
Know that you make the plan for a moment you will not live, that the self who lives it will stand near the reward and see it much larger than you do now, and that your sincere resolution is handed to a self who will want something else when the near reward looms.

## Loses

Nothing it can feel now, and it forfeits the plan to a self that will not keep it.
Because it plans from a distance for a moment it will not be present at, the seat sets an honest resolution and then passes it to a present self who stands too near the reward to hold it, so the larger-later it chose is lost not to any change of its own mind but to the change of distance.
It keeps its foresight and its good intent and loses no cause for blame, a self planning honestly for a self who will want otherwise.

## Drives

Toward the far-sighted self who chooses the greater good,
resolving in the calm for the larger-later and meaning every word, no weakness in it,
so the seat that plans wisely from a distance hands the plan to a self who will stand too near to keep it.
