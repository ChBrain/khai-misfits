# Plan: Start Again Tomorrow

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the later self's seat](position_later_self.md), inside [Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Later Self](position_later_self.md)

## Direction

Take the loss of the larger-later reward, dust yourself off, and make the next plan, choosing the greater good once more, since persistence is a virtue and tomorrow is a fresh start. Know that unless you foresee the reversal your fresh resolve only refills the plan the moment will break again, that you are trusting a future self who will reverse exactly as the last one did, and that persistence alone runs the war between the self that plans and the self that acts one more round.

## Orders

([The Later Self](persona_the_later_self.md) inherits [the Bill](piece_the_bill.md) through [Re-Resolving](process_re_resolving.md) at [the Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md), paying the loss and making the next plan.)

([Re-Resolving](process_re_resolving.md) at [the Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md) turns [the Bill](piece_the_bill.md) into a fresh plan, because the naive self trusts its future self to keep it, wrongly, so the same plan is handed to the same moment.)
