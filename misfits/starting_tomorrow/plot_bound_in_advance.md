# Plot: Bound in Advance

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Also at [the Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md), a self who has watched the plan break before chooses not to leave the next one to the moment. The choice is taken out of the moment before the moment can take it.

## Action

[The Steward](persona_the_steward.md), from [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), on [Tie the Mast](plan_tie_the_mast.md) through [Binding](process_binding.md), sets [The Mast](piece_the_mast.md) at [The Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md): the commitment that binds the future self in advance, so that when the moment comes it finds no near reward left to spike.

## Stage

Taking the choice out of the moment removes the very reward the reversal would flip, since there is no decision left there for the near reward to act on (Laibson; O'Donoghue and Rabin). Hold the bound in view: the binding is open only to the sophisticated self that foresees its own bias, not to the naive self that keeps trusting a future self who will reverse just the same; it spends real effort and real freedom; it can be undone or worked around; and a self that can never change its mind is not always the wiser self for it. And the phenomenon itself is contested, the functional form debated and the size, even the presence, of present bias questioned by careful elicitation, so not all impatience is a bias (Andreoni and Sprenger).

## Tension

The only escape is to spend one's own freedom binding a future self one does not trust.
