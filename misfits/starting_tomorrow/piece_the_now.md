# Piece: The Now

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The reward taken when the moment comes: the plan broken, and a fresh resolution made to start tomorrow.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Moment](place_the_moment.md), carried by [Grabbing](process_grabbing.md), voiced by [the Present Self](persona_the_present_self.md): the same ground the reversal happened on, now acted from.

## Load Bearing

The sooner reward is seized once its value has spiked above the later one, the plan broken in good faith by a self choosing, correctly, what looks best from where it now stands (Ainslie). It is worth holding the distinction this misfit turns on. In Seed Corn the present is favoured over the future deliberately, a chosen response to a metric or an incentive that rewards the present on purpose. Here the person means to favour the future, sincerely, and is reversed against their own stated intent by the mere approach of the near reward. The plan is not broken on purpose. It is broken by the curve, and the person taking the cigarette or hitting the snooze is not the same person, in the sense that matters, as the one who resolved not to.

## Apparent

Giving in, a lapse of willpower. It looks, to the person as much as to anyone watching, like weakness finally showing itself, the plan too thin to survive contact with the moment.
