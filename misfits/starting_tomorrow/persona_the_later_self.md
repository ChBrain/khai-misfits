# Persona: The Later Self

## Taxonomy

[Later Self](position_later_self.md), inside [Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The archetype of the self who inherits the forgone larger-later reward and pays the bill, and who, if it does not foresee the reversal, becomes the next Planner and resolves again.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person turning over a new leaf. It looks like renewed resolve, the fresh start, the Monday diet, someone dusting themselves off and committing again, admirable in its persistence.

## Action

Pay, and resolve again. At [the Morning After](place_the_morning_after.md), on [Start Again Tomorrow](plan_start_again_tomorrow.md) through [Re-Resolving](process_re_resolving.md), the Later Self inherits [the Bill](piece_the_bill.md), the larger-later reward lost to yesterday's moment, and makes the next plan, which the next moment will break in turn (O'Donoghue and Rabin).

## Shadow

Its fresh resolve repeats the trap unless it foresees it. The Later Self pays for the broken plan and, being naive, trusts its own future self to keep the next one, wrongly, since that self will stand as near the reward as the last one did and reverse just the same. Its persistence is real and blameless, but persistence alone only refills the plan the moment will break again, so the war between the self that plans and the self that acts goes another round.

## Tell

It pays yesterday's bill and makes tomorrow's plan, trusting a self that will do exactly as the last one did.
