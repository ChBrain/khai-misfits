## Owner

## Initiated by

Two rewards seen from a distance, a smaller-sooner and a larger-later, both far enough off to be weighed on their merits alone.

## Direction

From the far view toward a plan for the larger-later, set down while the choice is still easy and the far-sighted reward still plainly wins.

## Lever

From a distance the larger-later plainly outweighs the smaller-sooner, since neither reward has yet entered the range where the discount steepens (Laibson).
