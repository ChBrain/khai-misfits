# Plot: Just This Once

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Still at [the Moment](place_the_moment.md), the plan made in the calm meets the reward it was made against. What looked, from a distance, like the lesser choice now stands close enough to take.

## Action

[The Present Self](persona_the_present_self.md), from [the present self's seat](position_present_self.md), on [Take It Now](plan_take_it_now.md) through [Grabbing](process_grabbing.md), takes [The Now](piece_the_now.md) at [The Moment](place_the_moment.md): the immediate reward seized and the plan broken, with a fresh resolution, sincerely meant, to start tomorrow.

## Stage

The sooner reward is taken once its value has spiked above the later's, and the plan is broken in good faith: not against the present self's own judgment but by its exact application, choosing what looks best from where it now stands (Ainslie). This is not Seed Corn: there the present is favoured over the future deliberately, a chosen response to a metric that rewards the near term. Here the person means to favour the future, sets the plan meaning to keep it, and is reversed against that very intent by the curve. The plan is broken not on purpose, but broken all the same.

## Tension

The plan is broken not on purpose but by the mere approach of the near reward.
