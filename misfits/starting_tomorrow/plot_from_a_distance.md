# Plot: From a Distance

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Calm](place_the_calm.md), the smaller-sooner and the larger-later reward are both still far off, laid out where the reasoning is easy. Nothing yet stands near enough to be grabbed, so the choice can be weighed on its merits alone.

## Action

[The Planner](persona_the_planner.md), from [the planner's seat](position_planner.md), on [Plan for Later](plan_plan_for_later.md) through [Resolving](process_resolving.md), makes [The Plan](piece_the_plan.md) at [The Calm](place_the_calm.md): choosing the larger-later reward while both are distant, and resolving to save, or to study, or to quit.

## Stage

From a distance, the larger-later reward plainly outweighs the smaller-sooner, so the far-sighted choice is the obvious one, and the resolution the planner sets down is sincerely meant, not a performance of virtue but the correct answer to the question as it looks from here (Laibson). Nothing in the plan is thin or self-deceiving; what it cannot yet see is the shape of the curve waiting to meet it later.

## Tension

The plan is made for a moment the planner will not itself live.
