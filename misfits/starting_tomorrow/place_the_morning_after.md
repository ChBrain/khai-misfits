# Place: The Morning After

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). Where the later self inherits the bill and re-plans, and where the steward binds the future self in advance. Hosts [The Bill Comes](plot_the_bill_comes.md), [Bound in Advance](plot_bound_in_advance.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The forgone reward, come due: the larger-later that the plan chose and the moment gave away.
A fresh resolve taking shape, and, in the other plot this place hosts, a mast being tied.

## Holds

[The Bill](piece_the_bill.md).
Here the larger-later reward lost in the moment is inherited, tallied, and answered for by a self that did not spend it.

[The Mast](piece_the_mast.md).
Here the future self is bound in advance, the choice taken out of the moment so that no near reward is left to spike when the time comes again.

## Offers

To whoever foresees the reversal, the means to bind: a place where a plan can be made to hold, not by resolve alone but by removing the choice from the moment that would break it.

## Withheld

Any undoing of yesterday's forgone reward.
The bill is real and stands paid; the morning after can set the next plan or set a mast, but it cannot hand back what the moment already spent.
