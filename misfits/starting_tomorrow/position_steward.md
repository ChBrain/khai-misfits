# Position: Steward

## Taxonomy

[Starting Tomorrow](play_starting_tomorrow.md). The seat of the sophisticated self who foresees its own reversal and binds its future self in advance, and who marks that the binding is costly, circumventable, and not always the wiser course.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A foresight the naive self lacks, the knowledge that its own future self will reverse just as its past selves did.
A means to bind that self before the moment: the retirement locked away, the app deleted, the temptation not bought, the mast made ready.
A distrust of its own future will, choosing to remove the choice rather than to rely on holding the line when the near reward looms.

## Orders

Tie the mast, and do not trust the moment.
Foresee your own reversal, and bind your future self in advance, locking away the reward it would spend and the temptation it would take, so that when the moment comes it finds no near reward to spike and no choice left to make.
Know that the binding spends real effort and real freedom, that it is open only to a self that foresees its own bias, that it can be undone or worked around, and that a self which can never change its mind is not always the wiser, the hedge you stand on and the bound you must name.

## Loses

The freedom it spends to bind itself, and it loses that knowingly.
Because it will not trust its future will, the seat gives up the flexibility to change its mind and the effort of setting the binding, buying a kept plan at the price of a self locked out of its own later choices, which is not always the choice a wiser self would want.
It keeps its foresight and its clear sight of the cost and loses no cause for blame, the self that removes its own temptation before the moment can.

## Drives

Toward the sophisticated self who binds its future in advance,
foreseeing its own reversal and tying the mast before the song, distrusting the moment,
so the seat that alone escapes the trap escapes it only by spending its own freedom, and only if it foresees the reversal the naive self never will.
