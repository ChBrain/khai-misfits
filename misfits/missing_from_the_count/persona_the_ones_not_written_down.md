# Persona: The Ones Not Written Down

## Taxonomy

[Ones Not Written Down](position_ones_not_written_down.md). The missed, for reasons that are nobody's fault.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A family doubled up in a flat the address list holds as one household. A baby born in March whose mother did not think the form meant babies.
A renter who moved twice that spring. A household that does not open the door to anybody official, often for reasons well earned.

## Action

Lives there, uses the schools and the clinics, and is absent from the return.
Is missed again at the next census, for the same reasons.

## Shadow

Is not evenly distributed, which is the whole of the problem.
Random error cancels and would leave the shares intact, but every one of these mechanisms concentrates in particular households, particular ages and particular streets: so the count does not come out fuzzy, it comes out tilted, and the tilt is stable enough that the Supreme Court could name the affected groups in a judgment.

## Tell

Is entirely real, entirely present, and produces no evidence of itself in the only document that counts.
