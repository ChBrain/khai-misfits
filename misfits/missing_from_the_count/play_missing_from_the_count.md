# Play: Missing From the Count

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Missing From the Count.
A census is a head count, and it is supposed to be a head count: the constitution calls for an actual enumeration, not an estimate, and the reason is not pedantry. A number that can be adjusted by whoever holds the adjusting machinery is a number that can be adjusted toward whoever holds it, and representation is the thing being distributed. So the rule is that you go and knock on doors, and what you find is the answer. And then the answer is wrong in a particular direction, every single time, about the same people.

## Arc

The bend is this: the error in the count is not random, and the instrument that measures it is barred from correcting it where it matters most.
It runs on a protection that is genuinely a protection.
The Actual Enumeration is not a technicality. Insisting on a physical count rather than a modelled one is a real guarantee against a party in office improving its own position by choosing a method, and anybody who has watched a boundary commission understands why the guarantee is there. The rule is doing exactly the work it was written to do.
The Ones Not Written Down are missed for reasons that are nobody's fault. A family doubled up in one flat that the address list has as a single household. A child born in March to a mother who did not think the form meant babies. A renter who moved twice that spring. Distrust of anyone official, which is often well earned. Not one of these is negligence, and not one is evenly distributed across the population.
The One Who Knocks does the job properly. Enumerators walk the streets in the heat, return six times, leave notices and follow the procedure exactly, and the procedure is good. What they cannot do is enumerate somebody whose existence nobody in the building will confirm.
So the miss lands on the same groups every decade: minorities, children and renters, as the Supreme Court itself put it in describing the problem the Bureau was trying to solve. In 2020 the net undercount of children under five ran at about five and a half per cent, roughly a million children, the largest undercount of any age group and the highest rate recorded since tracking began in 1950.
And The One Who Knows the Number can prove it. Demographic analysis builds an independent estimate from birth records, death records and migration, and comparing it against the count gives the gap to a decimal place, state by state and county by county. This is not a suspicion. It is a measurement, published by the counting agency itself.
And it may not be applied. The Census Act prohibits sampling or statistical procedure in determining population for apportioning representatives, and the Supreme Court held so in 1999, striking down the Bureau's plan for exactly this correction. So the error is known, quantified, published, and carried into ten years of seats and formulas uncorrected, by a rule that exists for good reasons and would be dangerous to remove.
And the corrections eat themselves. Outreach is the obvious answer, and outreach is planned from the previous count, so the areas hardest to count are the areas the plan sizes smallest. Administrative records are the other answer, and the people missing from the census are disproportionately the same people missing from administrative records. Doing nothing costs a decade.

## Stakes

Whether a number can be called an enumeration when its error is known to a decimal place and must be carried anyway.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a constitutional guarantee worth keeping, enumerators who follow a good procedure exactly, and a measurement of the gap that is honest and public, and away from any instrument that could put the two together, because joining them is precisely what the guarantee forbids. The production cannot enumerate a person nobody will confirm, and it cannot hand the adjusting machinery to whoever stands to gain by it. What is left to fight over is not whether to count heads, that is the protection, but what is owed to a million children who are known to exist, known in what numbers, and absent from the table for ten years.
