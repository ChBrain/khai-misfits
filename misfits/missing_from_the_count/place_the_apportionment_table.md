# Place: The Apportionment Table

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md). Where the number becomes seats and money for ten years.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A column of state populations, a divisor, and a list of how many representatives each state receives.
Behind it, formulas distributing federal funds by population, running annually until the next census.

## Holds

[The Bar on Sampling](piece_the_bar_on_sampling.md), which fixes what number may be used here.
A total that is known, by the agency that produced it, to be short in a measured direction.

## Offers

Absolute regularity: the same arithmetic applied to every state, checkable by anybody, immune to argument.
A result nobody can lean on, which is exactly what the guarantee was for.

## Withheld

The correction, specifically and by law.
The published measurement of the shortfall exists, is undisputed and sits in the same agency's own release, and may not enter this room: so the table is the one place in the play where the error is both fully known and formally irrelevant, and it is also the only place where the error turns into anything.
