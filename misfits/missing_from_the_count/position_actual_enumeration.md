# Position: Actual Enumeration

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md). The seat that protects the number by forbidding its repair.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A constitutional instruction to conduct an actual enumeration, and a statute barring sampling in determining population for apportionment.
The strongest argument in the play behind it.

## Orders

Count people.
Do not estimate them.

## Loses

Accuracy, in exchange for something worth more.
An enumeration that may be adjusted is an enumeration that may be adjusted by whoever holds the machinery, and what is being distributed is political representation: so the seat is not refusing a fix out of formalism, it is declining to hand a party in office a knob on its own share of power, and nobody in this play argues it should yield.

## Drives

Toward a number nobody can interfere with, which is therefore a number nobody can correct.
