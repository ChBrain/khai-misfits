# Piece: The Questionnaire

## Taxonomy

[The Front Step](place_the_front_step.md). The form, asking a question people answer differently than it intends.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the front step](place_the_front_step.md), on paper, online or read aloud, asking who was living or staying at this address on the reference day.

## Load Bearing

It relies on a household's own idea of who counts as living here, and that idea varies.
A grandmother staying six months, a partner between addresses, a newborn, a child who splits the week between two homes: each is a person the form intends to capture and a person a respondent may reasonably not list, so the instrument's coverage is decided by an interpretation it cannot control.

## Apparent

Short, plain, tested extensively, translated widely and about as clear as such a question can be made.
Genuinely well designed, and better with each decade.

## Yearbook

Revised repeatedly to make the residence rules plainer, including explicit prompts about babies and about people staying temporarily.
Still answered by a person deciding, in their own kitchen, what the word here means.
