# Process: Missing Them

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md). A shortfall assembling itself out of innocent causes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A doubled-up household on an address list that holds it as one. A baby born in March. A move in April. A door that does not open to anybody official.
[The Ones Not Written Down](persona_the_ones_not_written_down.md), holding [Ones Not Written Down](position_ones_not_written_down.md).

## Direction

Toward absence from the return, with every individual cause entirely innocent.
Toward a total that is tilted rather than fuzzy, since none of the causes is evenly spread.

## Lever

Concentration: every mechanism here clusters in particular households, ages and streets, so the errors reinforce instead of cancelling.

## Echo

A shortfall stable enough across decades that a Supreme Court judgment could name the groups it falls on.
