# Plan: Adjust It

## Taxonomy

A **correction**: the intent of [The One Who Knows the Number](persona_the_one_who_knows_the_number.md), inside [Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md), once the gap is measured.

Apply the measurement. The shortfall is known to a decimal place and published: use it to correct the totals that decide seats and money, rather than carrying a figure everybody agrees is wrong.
