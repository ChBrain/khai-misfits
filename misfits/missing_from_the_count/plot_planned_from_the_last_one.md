# Plot: Planned From the Last One

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The next census being designed, and the corrections on the table: more enumerators where counting is hardest, administrative records, or adjusted figures.

## Action

[Plan the outreach](plan_plan_the_outreach.md): [The Ones Who Plan the Count](persona_the_ones_who_plan_the_count.md), holding [Ones Who Plan the Count](position_ones_who_plan_the_count.md), identify the hard-to-count areas and concentrate field staffing, advertising and partnerships on them.

## Stage

The designations and the population denominators behind the staffing come from the previous census, because that is the best data anybody has.

## Tension

The remedy is scaled by the error it exists to remove.
An area understated last time is allocated fewer enumerators, less advertising and less partnership money this time, in exact proportion to how badly it was missed, and the targeting is deliberate, competent and aimed correctly the whole while. The other answers close no better: administrative records miss disproportionately the same people the census missed, and doing nothing carries a measured shortfall into a decade of seats and formulas that nothing will reopen.
