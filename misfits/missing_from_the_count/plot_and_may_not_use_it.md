# Plot: And May Not Use It

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A plan to apply the published correction to the totals that decide seats, and a statute providing that no sampling or statistical procedure may be used in determining population for that purpose.

## Action

[Adjust it](plan_adjust_it.md): [The One Who Knows the Number](persona_the_one_who_knows_the_number.md) proposes the adjusted figures, and [the bar on sampling](piece_the_bar_on_sampling.md) keeps them out of [the apportionment table](place_the_apportionment_table.md). In 1999 the Supreme Court struck exactly this plan down.

## Stage

[Carrying it forward](process_carrying_it_forward.md): the uncorrected total does all the work, for ten years, and nothing revises it until the next census.

## Tension

The error is known, published, undisputed and legally irrelevant in the only room where it turns into anything.
And the rule keeping it out is not a mistake: a method somebody chooses is a method somebody can choose in their own favour, and the guarantee against that is worth having. So the play arrives at a place where the correction and the protection are competing for the same slot, both are right, and the cost of keeping the protection is paid entirely by the people the count already missed.
