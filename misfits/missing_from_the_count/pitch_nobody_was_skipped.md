# Pitch: Nobody Was Skipped

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Civic and procedural. A duty discharged every ten years, enumerators walking streets in the heat and returning six times, a form anybody can fill in, a statute protecting the count from anybody who might want to improve it.
Every part of it done properly, and most of it genuinely admirable.

## Undertow

Under the procedure, an error that is not random: it falls on the same groups every decade, and it is not a suspicion but a published measurement.
And a rule, written for good reasons, requiring that the measurement be set aside where the stakes are highest.

## Nerve

The vertigo of knowing the size of your mistake to a decimal place and being obliged to carry it for ten years.

## Echo

Afterward: about a million children under five missing from the 2020 count, the largest undercount of any age group and the worst since 1950, and an outreach plan for the next census sized by the count that missed them.
