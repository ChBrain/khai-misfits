# Persona: The Actual Enumeration

## Taxonomy

[Actual Enumeration](position_actual_enumeration.md). The requirement for a head count, and the protection it really is.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A constitutional instruction to conduct an actual enumeration, and a statute forbidding sampling in determining population for apportionment.
Written so that the number distributing political representation cannot be produced by a method somebody chooses.

## Action

Requires people to be counted rather than estimated.
Holds, against every argument that a better number could be modelled.

## Shadow

Is a genuine safeguard, which is what makes it immovable.
An enumeration that may be adjusted is an enumeration that may be adjusted by whoever holds the machinery, and representation is the thing being handed out: so this persona is not obstructing a fix out of formalism, it is refusing to hand a party in office a knob on its own share of power, and nobody in the play actually wants it to yield.

## Tell

Protects the count from interference by forbidding the one intervention that would make it accurate.
