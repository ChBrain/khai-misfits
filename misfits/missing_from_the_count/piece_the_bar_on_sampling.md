# Piece: The Bar on Sampling

## Taxonomy

[The Apportionment Table](place_the_apportionment_table.md). The rule that keeps the correction out of the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Governing what may be used at [the apportionment table](place_the_apportionment_table.md), and only there with full force.

## Load Bearing

It is the lock, and it is not a mistake.
The Census Act provides that no sampling or statistical procedure may be used in determining population for apportioning representatives, and the Supreme Court held in 1999 that the Bureau's plan to do exactly that was unlawful: so the correction is barred precisely where the stakes are highest, by a provision written to stop the number being shaped by whoever is in a position to shape it.

## Apparent

A short statutory prohibition, litigated to the top and settled.
Defended by people who are not wrong about the danger it addresses.

## Yearbook

Written against a real hazard: a method that can be chosen is a method that can be chosen in somebody's favour.
Blocks the correction at apportionment, which is the use nobody can afford to get wrong and the only one where a decade of error cannot be revised.
