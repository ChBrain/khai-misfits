# Plot: The Ones the Form Misses

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A family doubled up in a flat the address list holds as one household. A baby born in March. A renter who moved twice that spring. A door that does not open to anybody official.

## Action

[Missing them](process_missing_them.md): [The Ones Not Written Down](persona_the_ones_not_written_down.md), holding [Ones Not Written Down](position_ones_not_written_down.md), go unrecorded on [the questionnaire](piece_the_questionnaire.md) at [the front step](place_the_front_step.md).

## Stage

Every individual cause is innocent, and several of them are the household exercising a choice it is entitled to make.

## Tension

Random error would cancel. None of this is random.
Every mechanism here clusters in particular households, particular ages and particular streets, so the errors reinforce rather than average out and the count comes out tilted instead of merely fuzzy. The tilt is stable enough across decades that a Supreme Court judgment could name the affected groups in passing, as a settled description of the problem rather than as a contested allegation.
