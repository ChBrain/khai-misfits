# Piece: The Demographic Analysis

## Taxonomy

[The Apportionment Table](place_the_apportionment_table.md). The independent estimate that proves the gap and may not close it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Produced alongside the count and published afterwards, and admitted nowhere near [the apportionment table](place_the_apportionment_table.md).

## Load Bearing

It converts a suspicion into a measurement, which is what makes the situation intolerable rather than merely unfortunate.
Births, deaths and migration assemble into a population estimate owing nothing to the enumeration, so comparing them yields the shortfall to a decimal place: on the 2020 count it put the net undercount of children under five at about five and a half per cent, roughly a million children, the largest of any age group and the highest recorded since 1950.

## Apparent

A technical release from the counting agency, thorough, cautious, and published in ranges rather than as a single figure.
Work that makes the publishing agency look worse, published anyway.

## Yearbook

Available since the middle of the last century, and improving with the administrative records it rests on.
Strongest for exactly the group it most embarrasses, since births are among the best recorded events there are.
