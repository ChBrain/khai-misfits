# Plan: Plan the Outreach

## Taxonomy

A **corrective effort**: the intent of [The Ones Who Plan the Count](persona_the_ones_who_plan_the_count.md), inside [Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The Ones Who Plan the Count](persona_the_ones_who_plan_the_count.md)

## Direction

Put the effort where the counting is hardest. Identify the areas likely to be missed, and concentrate enumerators, advertising, language provision and community partnerships on them.

## Orders

(Derive hard-to-count designations and population denominators; allocate field staffing and advertising accordingly; fund partners in the areas identified; brief them before [knocking](process_knocking.md) begins.)

## Implementation

(Budgets sized per head of recorded population; designations and denominators taken from the previous census, which is the best data anybody has.)

## Targets

- [ ] the hard-to-count areas are identified and prioritised, which they are
- [ ] resources are concentrated there rather than spread evenly, which is correct
- [ ] the price is accepted: the denominators come from the count that did the missing, so an understated area is allocated less
- [ ] the price is accepted: the remedy is scaled by the error, in exact proportion to how badly the area was missed
