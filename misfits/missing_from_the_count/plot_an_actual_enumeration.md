# Plot: An Actual Enumeration

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A constitution calling for an actual enumeration rather than an estimate, and a statute forbidding sampling in determining population for apportionment.

## Action

[The Actual Enumeration](persona_the_actual_enumeration.md), holding [Actual Enumeration](position_actual_enumeration.md), fixes what may be used at [the apportionment table](place_the_apportionment_table.md) through [the bar on sampling](piece_the_bar_on_sampling.md).

## Stage

The number that distributes political representation cannot be produced by a method somebody chooses.

## Tension

The guarantee is real, and nobody in the play wants it withdrawn.
An enumeration that may be adjusted is an enumeration that may be adjusted by whoever holds the adjusting machinery, and what is being handed out is seats. So this is not formalism standing in the way of a technical fix: it is a refusal to give a party in office a knob on its own share of power, argued by people who are right about the danger. The rule is doing precisely the work it was written to do, and the cost of that work is the whole of this play.
