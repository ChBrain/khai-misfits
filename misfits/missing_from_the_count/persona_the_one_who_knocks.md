# Persona: The One Who Knocks

## Taxonomy

[One Who Knocks](position_one_who_knocks.md). The enumerator, following a good procedure exactly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A temporary field worker with a case list, an address register and a handheld device, walking a district in the heat.
Trained, supervised, and paid by the hour.

## Action

Returns to a non-responding address up to six times, leaves notices, asks neighbours and the building manager, and records the outcome honestly.
Enumerates everybody who can be established to live there.

## Shadow

Cannot enumerate a person nobody will confirm.
The procedure is genuinely good and the effort is genuinely made, and a household that will not open the door, or that has good reason to omit the extra family sleeping in the back room, defeats all of it: so the failure is not in the diligence, the training or the design, and no amount of any of them reaches somebody whose existence is not going to be stated to a stranger with a badge.

## Tell

Does the job correctly and leaves the district with a number that is wrong in a direction anybody could predict.
