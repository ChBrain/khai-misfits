# Plot: We Know Exactly

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Births, deaths and migration, assembled into a population estimate that owes nothing at all to the enumeration.

## Action

[The One Who Knows the Number](persona_the_one_who_knows_the_number.md), holding [One Who Knows the Number](position_one_who_knows_the_number.md), compares the two and publishes the gap state by state and county by county.

## Stage

The comparison is made by the counting agency itself, and released including the parts that make it look bad.

## Tension

This is not a suspicion, an allegation or a model of somebody's opinion. It is a measurement.
Two independent instruments disagree by a known amount in a known direction, and the disagreement is published by the party with the strongest interest in it being small. There is nobody to argue with, no methodology dispute to have and no further study to commission: the finding is settled, and being settled is what makes what follows intolerable rather than merely regrettable.
