# Position: Ones Who Plan the Count

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md). The seat calibrating the remedy with the error.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Budgets for field staffing, advertising, language provision and community partnerships.
Hard-to-count designations and population denominators, both derived from the previous census.

## Orders

Find the hard-to-count areas.
Concentrate the effort there.

## Loses

The proportionality of the remedy, to the instrument that measures the need.
An area understated last time is an area allocated fewer enumerators, less advertising and less partnership money this time, in exact proportion to how badly it was missed: the targeting is deliberate, competent and aimed correctly, and its scale is set by the very failure it exists to reverse.

## Drives

Toward a well-designed correction sized from a map the failure drew.
