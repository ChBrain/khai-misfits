# Plot: Five and a Half Per Cent

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The 2020 count, complete and certified, examined afterwards by age group.

## Action

[Measuring the gap](process_measuring_the_gap.md): [the demographic analysis](piece_the_demographic_analysis.md) sets an independent estimate against the enumeration, and [The One Who Knows the Number](persona_the_one_who_knows_the_number.md) publishes what it shows.

## Stage

Children under five were missed at about five and a half per cent, roughly a million of them: the largest undercount of any age group, and the highest rate recorded since tracking began in 1950.

## Tension

The worst-counted group is the one with the least capacity to be anything else.
A five-year-old cannot fill in a form, cannot confirm their own existence to an enumerator, and depends entirely on an adult understanding that the question includes them. And the evidence is strongest exactly here, because births are among the best recorded events there are: so the group the count fails hardest is also the group whose absence can be demonstrated most rigorously, which is what turns an unfortunate margin into an unbearable one.
