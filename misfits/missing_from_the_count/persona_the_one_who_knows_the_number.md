# Persona: The One Who Knows the Number

## Taxonomy

[One Who Knows the Number](position_one_who_knows_the_number.md). The demographer, holding a measurement that may not be applied.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A statistician at the counting agency, assembling births, deaths and migration into an independent estimate of the population.
Publishing the comparison openly, including the parts that make the agency look bad.

## Action

Establishes the gap between the estimate and the count, to a decimal place, state by state and county by county.
Publishes it.

## Shadow

Holds a proof that changes nothing where it matters.
The gap is not an allegation, a model of opinion or a contested inference: it is a measurement, made by the agency being measured, and it is legally inapplicable to apportionment, so the persona's entire professional output is a precise account of an error that must be carried into ten years of seats and formulas exactly as it stands.

## Tell

Is believed by everybody, contradicted by nobody, and cannot alter a single seat.
