# Plan: Count Everyone

## Taxonomy

A **public duty**: the intent of [The One Who Knocks](persona_the_one_who_knocks.md), inside [Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Knocks](persona_the_one_who_knocks.md)

## Direction

Reach every household and record everybody who lives there. Go back until somebody answers, ask the people who would know, and write down honestly what you find.

## Orders

(Work the case list at [the front step](place_the_front_step.md); return up to six times; leave notices; ask neighbours and building managers; complete [the questionnaire](piece_the_questionnaire.md) for everybody the household names.)

## Implementation

([The Front Step](place_the_front_step.md); the [knocking](process_knocking.md) process; training, supervision, translated materials and a procedure that is genuinely well designed.)

## Targets

- [ ] every address on the list is worked to the full number of visits
- [ ] every outcome is recorded honestly, including the refusals and the non-contacts
- [ ] everybody the household names is enumerated
- [ ] nobody the household does not name is reached, because no part of this plan operates on that side of the door
