# Persona: The Ones Who Plan the Count

## Taxonomy

[Ones Who Plan the Count](position_ones_who_plan_the_count.md). The planners, sizing the effort from the last answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The agency designing the next census: address canvassing, field staffing, language provision, advertising and community partnerships.
Competent, well intentioned, and working from the best data anybody has.

## Action

Identifies hard-to-count areas and concentrates resources on them.
Derives those designations, and the population denominators behind the staffing, from the previous census.

## Shadow

Sizes the effort with the instrument that did the missing.
An area whose population was understated last time is an area allocated fewer enumerators, less advertising and less partnership funding this time, in exact proportion to how badly it was undercounted: so the correction is real, deliberate and aimed correctly, and it is calibrated by the very error it exists to remove.

## Tell

Targets the hard-to-count areas accurately, using a map drawn by the failure it is targeting.
