# Position: One Who Knocks

## Taxonomy

[Missing From the Count](play_missing_from_the_count.md). The seat whose diligence is not the binding constraint.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A case list, an address register, a handheld device and a procedure that is genuinely well designed.
Training, supervision, and up to six return visits per address.

## Orders

Knock.
Establish who lives here, and record it honestly.

## Loses

Nothing to any failing of its own.
The procedure is good, the effort is real and the recording is honest, and none of that reaches a household that will not open the door or that has sound reasons not to mention the family sleeping in the back room: so the shortfall cannot be closed by more training, more visits or better design, because the constraint is not on this seat's side of the door.

## Drives

Toward a district worked correctly and a number wrong in a direction anybody could have predicted in advance.
