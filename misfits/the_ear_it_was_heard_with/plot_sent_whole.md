## Tension

The message has to be established as ordinary before anything else is worth saying, because the
easy reading is that somebody wrote carelessly.
Nobody did. A sign is a symbol of what it is about, a symptom of the one who sent it and a signal to
the one who receives it, and the three are operative in any speech action at any moment, varying in
strength and never in presence.
So the writer did not smuggle anything in. **There is no version of the message with one side**, and
the careful drafting everybody recommends moves weight between them without removing any, which
means an author who follows that advice perfectly still sends four things.
This is not ambiguity, which careful wording reduces: ambiguity is one sign that might mean either
of two things, and this is one sign doing several things and doing all of them.
