# Process: Withdrawing

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). Both of them writing less, and the messages getting louder.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A week of it.
Across [the thread](persona_the_thread.md), in shorter and shorter messages.

## Direction

Toward safety, by subtraction, which subtracts the wrong thing.
Cutting a message removes content and leaves the disclosure, the positioning and the ask exactly
where they were, with less around them to dilute either: **a three-word message is not less
relational than a thirty-word one, it is more.**
Both of them are doing the responsible thing and both are doing it in the direction that concentrates
what is hurting.

## Lever

Brevity, which everybody believes reduces risk.
The question is not whether they should say more, since more is more messages, but why less does
not help.

## Echo

Two people who work well together,
writing to each other in the fewest possible words,
each of which lands harder than the sentence that started it.
