# Piece: The First One Good Enough

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). The receiver's stopping rule, which is the receiver's, and which ends the search.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In relevance theory's account of comprehension.
Running, invisibly and correctly, in [the reader](persona_the_reader.md) at [the desk](place_the_desk.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the reader can be asked to have tried harder and the play has somebody to blame.
It is load bearing because the procedure is **a path of least effort through readings in order of
accessibility, stopping when expectations of relevance are satisfied**: understanding is that
procedure rather than a first pass at it, so there is no later stage in which the intended reading
is recovered and preferred.

## Apparent

That a careful reader would check.
Checking is another comprehension, run the same way, on the same message, with the same order of
accessibility. It arrives where it arrived.

## Yearbook

A cognitive account of how anybody understands anything, taken here at its word.
The play does not claim the reader is biased; it claims the reader is working.
