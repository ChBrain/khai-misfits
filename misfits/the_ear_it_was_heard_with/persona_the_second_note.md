# Persona: The Second Note

## Taxonomy

[Correction](position_correction.md). The clarification, written carefully, twice, and sent on Monday morning.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

I was only asking about the file.
Nine words, drafted at length, with three earlier versions deleted for sounding worse.
The most reasonable sentence in the whole exchange.

## Action

Corrects.
Arrives.
Carries four sides, being an utterance.

## Shadow

That it is **more relational than the thing it corrects**.
On its content side it is about a file. On its relationship side it says you read me wrong, which
the first message never got round to saying, and on its disclosure side it says the writer noticed
and minded. It is entirely true and there is no wording of it that is not.

## Tell

Both of them reread it.
Neither of them can find the sentence that would have been better.
