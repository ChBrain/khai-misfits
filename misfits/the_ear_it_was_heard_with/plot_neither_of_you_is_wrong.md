## Tension

Here the harm lands, and it lands with nobody at fault.
The writer meant the file and did. The reader was positioned and was. Both accounts survive every
test either would accept, and each will be told to a friend and will hold up.
There is **no single thing meant** for a better reading to have recovered, because the message did
not contain one intention surrounded by three accidents. It contained several things sent.
And the effect was not the writer's to perform: what an utterance brings about is not conventional
and is not under the speaker's control, so the intention is true, is believed, and settles nothing.
The disagreement is therefore not about what happened. **It is about which of the things that
happened counts, and neither of them has any standing to settle that for the other.**
