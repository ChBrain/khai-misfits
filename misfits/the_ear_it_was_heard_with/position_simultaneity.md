# Position: Simultaneity

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). The seat of the message's several sides: sent together, in one act, and not separable by any wording.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Four things in seven words: what it is about, what it shows about the sender, where it puts the reader, and what it asks for.
The founding model of language as an instrument, in which a sign is symbol, symptom and signal together.
No off switch.

## Orders

Carry every side.
Carry them at once.
Vary their strength and never their number.

## Loses

The possibility of the careful version.
There is no sentence with only the content side in it, so the instruction to be clearer moves
weight between the sides and cannot remove one, and an author following that instruction perfectly
sends exactly as many things as before.

## Drives

Toward being one act,
which is what makes it a message rather than four,
so the seat that makes communication possible is the seat that makes it undecidable.
