# Piece: Not the Sender's to Perform

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). The line between what a speaker does in saying something and what they bring about by saying it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the philosophy of speech acts, where the effect of an utterance is separated from the act.
Sitting between [intent](position_intent.md) and everything that happened afterwards.

## Load Bearing

Without it the writer's intention would be the fact of the matter and the reader would simply be
wrong. It is load bearing because the consequence of an utterance is **not conventional and not
under the speaker's control**, belonging to the circumstances it lands in, so the writer's account
of what they meant is true, is offered honestly, and settles nothing about what the message did.

## Apparent

That this excuses the reader and convicts the writer.
It does neither. It says the effect had no author, which is why there is nobody to apologise to and
nobody who can accept an apology on the point actually in dispute.

## Yearbook

A distinction made in 1962 and used here for exactly what it says.
The play takes no side in the long argument about how far it can be pushed, and needs none.
