# Process: Selecting

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). A reading arriving, being found good enough, and the reading stopping there.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The message, opened at [the desk](place_the_desk.md).
Late on a Thursday, after a week of being asked things.

## Direction

Toward the first reading relevant enough to act on.
Readings are tested in the order they come to mind and the search ends when expectations of
relevance are satisfied, so what arrives first is decided by the afternoon rather than by the file.
This is not carelessness and not suspicion: it is the procedure by which anybody understands
anything, running exactly as it should.

## Lever

Accessibility, which is the reader's and is set by everything that is not the message.
The play's question is not whether the reader read properly but what reading properly yields when
four things were sent.

## Echo

A reading, held with no sense of having chosen it,
which is the reading,
because there is no later stage in which it is checked against what was meant.
