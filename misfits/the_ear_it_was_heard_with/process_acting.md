# Process: Acting

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). The reader answering the side they selected, briefly, and going home.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Selecting](process_selecting.md), completed.
At [the desk](place_the_desk.md), in under a minute.

## Direction

Toward a reply that answers the message as received.
The reply is short, correct and slightly cool, because the side answered was the one about who gets
to ask, and a short cool reply to that side is a perfectly proportionate response to it.
Nothing here is retaliation. It is an accurate answer to a question that was really asked.

## Lever

The reply, which is the first thing the writer will see and the only evidence they will have.
The question is not whether the reader was fair but what a fair answer to one side looks like to
somebody who sent four.

## Echo

Seven words back,
read on Friday morning by the writer,
who cannot see the reading it answers and can see only that something has gone wrong.
