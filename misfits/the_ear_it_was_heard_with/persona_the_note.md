# Persona: The Note

## Taxonomy

[Simultaneity](position_simultaneity.md). Seven words about a report, sent once and arriving as four messages.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Is this the final version?
Composed in about four seconds, read in about one, and defensible in every syllable.
The kind of thing sent forty times a day by everybody in the building.

## Action

Carries.
Arrives whole.
Cannot be opened partly.

## Shadow

That it has no version with one side.
The message about the file only, with nothing in it about who is asking or what is wanted, was
not written carelessly: it does not exist, and no wording produces it, which is what makes every
instruction to be clearer land somewhere other than the problem.

## Tell

Everybody can say what it was about.
Nobody can say what it was only about.
