# Process: Correcting

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). The writer explaining what was meant, and the explanation being a message.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A reply that reads wrong, and a weekend.
At [the draft](place_the_draft.md), over about twenty minutes and three deleted versions.

## Direction

Toward closing it, and into another turn of the same thing.
The sentence is true, kind and carefully made, and on its relationship side it says you read me
wrong, which is a firmer positioning than the original ever attempted.
So the recursion is not a failure of the correction. **A better correction has the same shape**,
because the shape belongs to utterances and not to this one.

## Lever

Meta-communication, which is permitted, encouraged and available.
Nothing here is forbidden or unspeakable: the play's question is what happens when commenting on
the message is allowed and is itself a message.

## Echo

Nine words on Monday,
the most reasonable sentence in the exchange,
and a second reading, arriving first, in a reader who has now been told they read wrong.
