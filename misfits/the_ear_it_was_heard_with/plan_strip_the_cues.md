# Plan: Strip the Cues

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the channel seat](position_channel.md), inside [The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Channel](position_channel.md)

## Direction

Deliver exactly what was typed, drop everything that was not, and keep all of it so either party
can read it again and be confirmed.

## Orders

([The Thread](persona_the_thread.md) delivers, timestamps and retains, without a face, a pause or a tone.)

## Implementation

([The Inbox](place_the_inbox.md) and [the desk](place_the_desk.md); the [withdrawing](process_withdrawing.md) process; [shorter is not fewer](piece_shorter_is_not_fewer.md).)

## Targets

- [x] fidelity to the words is complete
- [x] every cue that was not typed is gone
- [ ] removing cues makes the selection harder and leaves the number of sides untouched
- [ ] and a richer channel supplies more to read rather than less to choose between
