# Persona: The Thread

## Taxonomy

[Channel](position_channel.md). Written, timestamped, and stripped of everything that is not words.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A column of messages with times against them.
No face, no pause, no tone of voice, and no way to ask a small question in a small way.
Universally described as the problem.

## Action

Delivers exactly what was typed.
Removes every cue that was not typed.
Keeps all of it, so both of them can read it again on Monday and each be confirmed.

## Shadow

That removing cues does not remove sides.
The channel takes away what would have narrowed the reading and leaves untouched the fact that
every side was sent, so it makes the selection harder without making the plurality smaller: fewer
signals, the same number of things signalled.

## Tell

Everybody says this would not have happened in a room.
In a room there is a face to read as well.
