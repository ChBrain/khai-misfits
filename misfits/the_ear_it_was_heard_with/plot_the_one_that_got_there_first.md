## Cue

## Action

[Selecting](process_selecting.md): [The Reader](persona_the_reader.md), in [the selection seat](position_selection.md), takes the first adequate reading on [Stop at Good Enough](plan_stop_at_good_enough.md).

## Stage

[The Thread](persona_the_thread.md), on [Strip the Cues](plan_strip_the_cues.md), having delivered the words and nothing else.

## Tension

Here the selection is put in the open, and it has to be shown to be **right**.
The reader is not choosing among labelled options and is not being suspicious. Comprehension is a
path of least effort through readings in the order they come to mind, ending when expectations of
relevance are satisfied, and that is what understanding **is** rather than a first pass at it.
So the reading that arrives first is the reading, and what makes it arrive first is the afternoon,
the week and everything else that is not the message. Sent on a Tuesday to somebody rested, the same
seven words are about a file.
And the reader has no experience of having interpreted anything, which is exactly what the procedure
running properly feels like from inside. **The only person who could report the selection is the one
person it is invisible to.**
