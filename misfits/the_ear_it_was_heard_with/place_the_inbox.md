# Place: The Inbox

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). Where messages are made and sent: forty a day, four seconds each, nothing weighed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A window, a cursor and a send button.
Colleagues writing to colleagues in the shortest form that will do, all day, without incident.

## Holds

The scale that makes the play ordinary rather than exceptional.
This happens with one message in some large number, and the number of messages is enormous, so
what looks like a rare failure of expression is the ordinary rate of a thing that runs every time.

## Offers

Speed, which is real, and which nobody would give up.

## Withheld

The version with one side.
No feature of the composing window produces it, no amount of care produces it, and the writer who
believes they have produced it has moved weight between the sides.
