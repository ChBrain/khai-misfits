# Persona: The Reader

## Taxonomy

[Selection](position_selection.md). The one it arrived at, on a Thursday afternoon, with a difficult week behind them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, unsuspicious and busy.
Reads the way everybody reads: takes what comes, decides whether it is enough to act on, and acts.
Would be astonished to be told they interpreted anything.

## Action

Takes the reading that arrives first.
Checks it against what is going on, which is a week of being asked things.
Stops, because it is good enough.

## Shadow

That stopping is not a shortcut but the procedure itself.
Comprehension is a path of least effort through readings in the order they come to mind, ending
when expectations of relevance are satisfied, so **the reading that arrives first is the reading**,
and there is no further step in which the intended one is compared with it and preferred.

## Tell

Never says which reading they took.
Has no sense of having taken one.
