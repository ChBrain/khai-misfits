# Pitch: I Was Only Asking

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Patient and exact, writing up a disagreement neither party will accept a verdict on.
Unwilling to say that either of them misread anything, because neither did.
Quoting the message wherever the message is the point, which is most of the time.

## Undertow

Under seven words, three findings that do not negotiate.
A sign is a symbol, a symptom and a signal at once, in any speech action, at any moment.
A hearer stops at the first reading good enough, by their own reckoning of enough.
And what a message does is not the sender's to perform.

## Nerve

The unease of a sentence that is true and makes it worse.
I was only asking about the file: accurate, said in good faith, and on its relationship side
it says you read me wrong, which the first message never got round to saying at all.

## Echo

After the run: two accounts of one message, both correct,
a correction more relational than the thing it corrected,
advice to be clearer that is itself four-sided,
and two people who work well together not writing to each other for a week.
