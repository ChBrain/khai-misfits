# Position: Channel

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). The seat of the medium: what it strips, what it keeps, and what it cannot touch.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Words, exactly as typed, with a time against them.
No face, no pause, no tone, and a permanent record both parties will reread.
A universal reputation as the cause.

## Orders

Deliver what was typed.
Strip everything else.
Keep it all, for later.

## Loses

The credit for the trouble, which belongs elsewhere.
Removing cues makes the selection harder and leaves the number of sides exactly as it was, so a
richer channel supplies more to read and not less to choose between: in a room there is a face to
interpret as well, and it is one more thing sent that was not chosen.

## Drives

Toward fidelity,
which it achieves completely,
so the seat that transmits perfectly is the seat that settles nothing.
