# The Ear It Was Heard With: Reference

The research warrant for [The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md).
The setting is a written exchange between two colleagues; the play runs on the fact that an utterance carries its
sides together and the receiver stops at the first reading good enough, so both accounts of what was said are
accurate, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging ordinary miscommunication as **a plurality that no care removes**: a sign is at once a symbol of what it
is about, a symptom of the one who sent it and a signal to the one who receives it, and the three run in any
speech action at any moment, varying in strength and never in presence; comprehension is a path of least effort
through readings in order of accessibility, ending when expectations of relevance are satisfied, so the reading
that arrives first is the reading and what makes it arrive first is not in the message; the effect of an
utterance is not the speaker's to perform, so an honest account of what was meant settles nothing about what was
done; both parties are therefore accurate and neither has standing over the other; the correction is itself an
utterance and its relationship side is stronger than the original's; and shortening subtracts from the content
side alone, so the responsible move concentrates what is hurting. The play models the message that cannot be sent
with one side, the reader's stopping rule, the intention that is true and does not govern, the correction that
recurses, and the channel that strips cues without removing sides, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure,
not as a tale of careless writing or thin skin.

The governing law is that the sides of an utterance are sent together and selected singly, so agreement about
what was said is unavailable to two people who are both telling the truth.

It is distinct from the house's **The Double Bind**, whose sealing condition is a prohibition on commenting: the
victim there cannot step outside and name the contradiction. Remove that engine, by stipulating that
meta-communication is fully permitted and nothing is unspeakable, and this play is untouched, because **commenting
is permitted here and simply recurses**. There the door is locked; here it is open and leads into the same room.

It is distinct from the house's **Anyone Can See It**, which stages naive realism, a construal taken for plain
fact. Remove that engine, by stipulating that both parties know perfectly well that their reading is a construal,
and this survives, because knowing a reading is a construal does not say which side was sent, and the receiver
must still act on one. The sharper line is what each play supposes about the fact of the matter: **there a fact
exists and a construal is mistaken for it, and here there is no single thing meant**, because the message
contained several things sent rather than one intention surrounded by three accidents.

It is distinct from the house's **The Illusion of Shared Understanding**, which was the precondition on this
play's slate line and could not be cut until the spine was found. That play runs on an **overestimate** of how
well meaning transmits, and its remedy is grounding, the mutual confirmation that a contribution has been
understood well enough for present purposes. Remove that engine entirely, by stipulating perfectly calibrated
parties and unlimited grounding, and this play stands: **grounding converges on the reading that was taken and
cannot unsend the sides that were not.** In that play the parties are wrong about transmission and more
confirmation closes the gap; here both are right and more confirmation is more utterances. The cut is now made,
and it is made by the spine rather than asserted.

It is distinct from the house's **Whatever's in the Can**, whose engine is four independent streams meeting by
timing in an organised anarchy. Stipulate perfect coupling of problems to solutions and this is unchanged,
because nothing here arrives by coincidence: one act carries every side by construction, and the plurality is
inside the utterance rather than in the queue it sits in.

formulation of the first, the tradition the third revises, and the grounding account cited to hold the line.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the writer as
  passive-aggressive, the reader as thin-skinned, or the channel as the culprit, because nothing in the sequence
  has a decision to get wrong: seven words carry every side by construction and no drafting removes one; the
  reader's stopping rule is what comprehension is rather than a shortcut through it, and it runs invisibly to the
  only person who could report it; the writer's intention is single and honest and would survive any examination;
  the correction is true and was drafted three times in good faith; and the channel transmits with perfect
  fidelity. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a message is
  sent whole and read singly.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Saying less subtracts from the
  content side alone and concentrates the rest. Saying more is more utterances with the same sides. Meeting in
  person supplies a face and a pause to be read as well, which is more sent rather than fewer sides. Asking what
  was meant is a question with a relationship side of its own, and the answer is an utterance. And the standing
  advice to be clearer moves weight between the sides without removing any, so an author who follows it perfectly
  still sends four things.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed as
  the house's. The staging is the house's; the Organon model and the simultaneity of the three functions are
  Bühler's, the comprehension procedure and its stopping rule are Sperber and Wilson's, the separation of the act
  from its effect is Austin's, the four-sided formulation is Schulz von Thun's, the cooperative account it
  revises is Grice's, and the grounding process held against this one is Clark and Brennan's. The bounds are
  carried with them, and two are owed plainly. **None of the cited works was read in full**, each being behind
  publisher access from this environment, so each is cited for the question it addresses and its findings as
  corroborated from several independent reports, and **no numerical parameter appears anywhere in this
  production** that those reports did not state: the play carries no figure for anything and does not need one.
  And the second bound separates what is established from what is drawn. The literature establishes the
  simultaneity, the stopping rule and the separation of act from effect. **That both parties are therefore
  accurate and neither has standing over the other, that the correction is more relational than the thing it
  corrects, and that shortening concentrates what is hurting, are the play's readings of those findings taken
  together and are not reported results.** They follow from the conjunction rather than from any measurement, and
  they are marked as the house's throughout: in [both are right](piece_both_are_right.md), in [shorter is not
  fewer](piece_shorter_is_not_fewer.md), and here. The setting is the house's own extension too: the cited work is
  in language theory, pragmatics and the philosophy of language, and a written exchange between two colleagues is
  where the play puts the mechanism, not where anybody studied it.
