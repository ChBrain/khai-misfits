# Process: Sending

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). Seven words leaving, carrying every side, in about four seconds.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A report on a desk and a question about printing.
At [the inbox](place_the_inbox.md), on a Thursday, among forty others.

## Direction

Toward one thing wanted and four things sent.
The writer is not encoding a subtext and is not being careless: a sign is a symbol of what it is
about, a symptom of the one who sent it and a signal to the one who receives it, all at once, in
any speech action.
So this is not a lapse in expression. It is what expression is.

## Lever

Wording, which moves weight between the sides and removes none of them.
The question is not whether it could have been phrased better but what the better phrasing would
have subtracted, and the answer is nothing.

## Echo

A message in a thread,
complete, brief and defensible,
already carrying more than its author put in it and no more than anybody's does.
