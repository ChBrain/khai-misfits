# Position: Intent

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). The seat of what was meant: genuine, provable to nobody, and not what governs.

## Has

## Orders

## Loses

Its authority over the outcome, which it never had.

outside the speaker's control and belongs to the circumstances it lands in, so intending is real
and is evidence of nothing about what the message did.

which it deserves and gets,
so the seat that is telling the truth is the seat with nothing to show.
