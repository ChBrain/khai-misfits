# Piece: Four at Once

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). The claim the play rests on: a message carries its sides together, in one act, always.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the founding model of language as an instrument, where a sign is at once a symbol of what it is
about, a symptom of the sender and a signal to the receiver.
Carried, unknowingly, by everybody at [the inbox](place_the_inbox.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a story about a badly worded message and the remedy is better wording.
It is load bearing because the three functions are **operative in any speech action at any moment**,
varying in strength and not in presence, so there is no utterance with one side and no wording
that makes one, and the whole advice tradition addresses the wrong quantity.

## Apparent

That this is a claim about ambiguity, which careful drafting reduces.
Ambiguity is one sign standing for two possible things, and this is one sign doing several things at
once and doing all of them: reduce the ambiguity to nothing and every side is still sent.

## Yearbook

Stated in language theory in 1934 and given its widely taught four-sided form much later.
The play stages the finding and does not extend it; what it adds is the workplace it is put in.
