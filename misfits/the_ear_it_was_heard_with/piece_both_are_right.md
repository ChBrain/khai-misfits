# Piece: Both Are Right

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). The shape of the harm: two accurate accounts of one message, and no standing between them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the two versions told afterwards, each to a friend, each holding up.
Nowhere in the message, which contains no fact that would decide it.

## Load Bearing

Without it this is a misunderstanding and misunderstandings get cleared up.
It is load bearing because there is **no single thing meant** for a better reading to have
recovered: the message did not contain one intention surrounded by three accidents, it contained
several things sent, so the disagreement is not about what happened but about which of the things
that happened counts, and neither party has any standing to settle that for the other.

## Apparent

That somebody must be more right.
Both accounts survive every test either would accept, which is the position the play is about and
is not a stalemate to be broken by a third party, since the third party's ruling is an utterance.

## Yearbook

The house's own reading, drawn from the three findings and not reported by any of them.
It is the consequence of their conjunction, and it is marked as the play's throughout.
