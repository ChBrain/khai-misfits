# Plot: Saying It Again

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A weekend, and twenty minutes at [the draft](place_the_draft.md) with three versions deleted.
Nine words that survive: I was only asking about the file.

## Action

[Correcting](process_correcting.md): [The Second Note](persona_the_second_note.md), in [the correction seat](position_correction.md), is sent on [Say What I Meant](plan_say_what_i_meant.md).

## Stage

[The Reader](persona_the_reader.md), on [Stop at Good Enough](plan_stop_at_good_enough.md), receiving it the same way they receive anything.

## Tension

Here the remedy recurses, and the recursion is not a fault in the remedy.
A message about a message is a message. On its content side the second note is about a file. On its
relationship side it says you read me wrong, which is a firmer positioning than the first note ever
attempted, and on its disclosure side it says the writer noticed and minded.
**The correction is more relational than the thing it corrects, and it is entirely accurate.**
The three deleted drafts are the evidence that nobody is careless: that is a person doing exactly
what everybody is told to do, attending at length to how it will land, and the sentence that
survived still says it.
And nothing here is forbidden. Commenting on the message is permitted, encouraged and available,
which is what separates this from a bind: **the door is open and it leads into the same room.**
