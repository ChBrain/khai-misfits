# Piece: Shorter Is Not Fewer

## Taxonomy

[The Ear It Was Heard With](play_the_ear_it_was_heard_with.md). What brevity does: it removes content and concentrates everything else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [withdrawing](process_withdrawing.md), as both parties get careful.
In every three-word message anybody has ever received from somebody they had annoyed.

## Load Bearing

Without it the obvious remedy works and the play has an exit.
It is load bearing because the sides do not scale together: **shortening subtracts from the content
side and leaves the disclosure, the positioning and the ask where they were**, with less around them
to dilute either, so the responsible move concentrates exactly what is doing the damage.

## Apparent

That saying more must therefore help.
Saying more is more utterances, each with the same sides, so the two directions fail differently
rather than one of them working.

## Yearbook

Not a measured finding and not offered as one: it follows from the sides being present rather than
proportional, which is what the founding model says.
Named here because the settlement it kills is the one everybody reaches for first.
