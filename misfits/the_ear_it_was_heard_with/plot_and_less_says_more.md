## Tension

Here the settlements are tried, and each costs what it was for.
**Say less**: shortening subtracts from the content side and leaves the disclosure, the positioning
and the ask where they were, with less around them to dilute either, so the responsible move
concentrates what is doing the damage.
**Say more**: more is more utterances, each with the same sides, so the two directions fail
differently rather than one of them working.
**Meet in person**: a richer channel supplies a face and a pause to read as well, which is more sent
and not fewer sides, and the selection still happens.
**Ask what was meant**: that is a question with a relationship side of its own, and the answer is an
utterance, and so on for as long as either of them keeps trying.
Underneath all four, the thing none of them reaches: the sides are sent together and no wording
removes one, so the structure is not escaped by trying harder. **It is iterated by it.**
