# Persona: The Writer

## Taxonomy

[Intent](position_intent.md). The one who sent it, correct about what they meant, and holding the half of the message that does not travel.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Wanted to know whether to print it.
Nothing else in mind, no history being invoked, no point being made.
Would say all of this under oath and be telling the truth.

## Action

Means one thing.
Sends four.
Learns, on Monday, that a different one landed.

## Shadow

That meaning it is real and does not govern.
What the message did belongs to the reading it got, and the effect of an utterance is not
something the sender performs, so the writer's intention is genuine, is evidence of nothing about
what happened, and is the only thing they have to offer.

## Tell

Says I only meant, and means it.
Has nothing to point at that would show it.
