# Plan: Answer the Complaint

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the officer's seat](position_officer.md), inside [No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Officer](position_officer.md)

## Direction

Take in a complaint specific enough to log, answer only what it names, and close the case with a brief the designer can build to. Never take on the condition behind the complaint: the seat has no budget, and no order, to.

## Orders

([The Officer](persona_the_officer.md) logs [The Complainant](persona_the_complainant.md)'s account, drafts [The Brief](piece_the_brief.md), forwards it to [The Designer](persona_the_designer.md), and closes the case once the fix is installed.)

## Implementation

(At [The Frontage](place_the_frontage.md); the [Complaining](process_complaining.md) and [Briefing](process_briefing.md) processes; the case closes when [The Divider](piece_the_divider.md) goes in.)

## Targets

- [ ] the complaint is logged, specific and true
- [ ] the brief is drafted, naming a function and never a person
- [ ] the brief reaches the designer and the divider is installed
- [ ] the case is closed... and the condition behind the complaint stays open, because no order ever reached it
