# Process: Displacing

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md). What a person does once a place stops being usable, carried out alone, off every book the scheme kept.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A surface that no longer holds a body the way it used to, discovered by trying it, not by being told.

## Direction

Toward another doorway, chosen without a brief, a budget, or a remit behind it: whatever can be found, on whatever night it is found on.

## Lever

Whatever the person can carry meeting an object built precisely, and lawfully, against them.

## Echo

An empty frontage, read elsewhere as success, and a person now somewhere no ledger connected to this scheme will ever look.
