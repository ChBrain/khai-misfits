# Plot: The Count

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Frontage](place_the_frontage.md), months on, [The Divider](piece_the_divider.md) settled into the bench, the doorway empty on most mornings now.
A report is due, and the commission named exactly what it wants counted.

## Action

[Counting](process_counting.md): [The Manager](persona_the_manager.md), holding [Manager](position_manager.md), counts the incidents logged at the frontage against last quarter's, and tallies the fall.

## Stage

[The Manager](persona_the_manager.md), having tabled [Report the Figure](plan_report_the_figure.md), carries [The Figure](piece_the_figure.md) to publication: a number, genuinely down, exactly what the scheme was commissioned to reduce.

## Tension

The figure is not massaged and it is not wrong: incidents at this address really have fallen. What it counts is the frontage, and only the frontage, so a person who no longer sleeps there does not register as a fall, only as an absence the count has no column to hold.
