# Process: Complaining

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md). The step that turns a private nuisance into an official record.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A fact specific enough to name and true enough to stand behind, at [The Frontage](place_the_frontage.md), held by someone the remit is built to hear.

## Direction

Toward a logged case naming the address and the nuisance, and nothing else.
The complaint travels only as far as the words that carry it, so what enters the record is exactly, and only, what was said.

## Lever

A real, standing inconvenience meeting a remit built exactly to receive it, and built to receive no further than that.

## Echo

A case open at the officer's desk, and a doorway now marked for a fix rather than merely lived in.
