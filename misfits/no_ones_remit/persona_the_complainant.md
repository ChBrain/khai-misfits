# Persona: The Complainant

## Taxonomy

A shopkeeper on [The Frontage](place_the_frontage.md), entitled to complain about a fact of her own doorway and holding no office beyond that entitlement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Plain, civic, unapologetic.
Names the fact of her doorway the way she would name a broken lock: specific, dated, and owed a response.
Nothing dressed up in it. She wants the shutter clear in the morning, and she is right to want it.

## Action

Brings her account to [The Officer](persona_the_officer.md), true and specific: this doorway, these mornings, this stranger.
Asks for exactly what her own entitlement covers, and no further.
Reads a fix at the address as the complaint answered, and closes the matter in her own mind.

## Shadow

Never asked, because no one put the question to her, what becomes of the person she is stepping over.
Her complaint is entitled and true in every particular, and it is also the first true link in a chain she never sees past her own doorway, so the harm the chain produces later is not a thing she willed, only a thing her honesty made possible.
The doorway clear again reads to her as the whole story closed, not as a story that only just left her sight.

## Tell

Still checks the recess out of habit each morning, months after the fix goes in, the same glance she used to give the stranger sleeping there.
Finds it empty every time, and never once wonders where the glance would need to travel to find out why.
