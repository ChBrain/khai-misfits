# Plot: The Complaint

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Frontage](place_the_frontage.md), a doorway that fills most nights, and a morning routine that starts by stepping over whoever is there to lift the shutter.
The inconvenience is specific and recurring, not imagined, and it belongs to someone entitled to name it.

## Action

[Complaining](process_complaining.md): [The Complainant](persona_the_complainant.md), holding no seat but entitled to be heard, brings her account, true and specific, to [The Officer](persona_the_officer.md), holding [Officer](position_officer.md). The Officer's remit takes in the complaint exactly as written, and nothing behind it.

## Stage

[The Officer](persona_the_officer.md), having tabled [Answer the Complaint](plan_answer_the_complaint.md), logs the case and drafts [The Brief](piece_the_brief.md): an instruction for a surface that discourages lying down, aimed at the doorway the complaint named.

## Tension

The complaint is real and the shopkeeper is not lying: she is owed a doorway she can open in the morning. The Officer is not required, and has no power, to weigh what happens to the person who was sleeping there; its remit is answerable only because it stops at the complaint, and stopping there is exactly what lets it miss what comes next.
