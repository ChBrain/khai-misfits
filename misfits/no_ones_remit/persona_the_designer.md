# Persona: The Designer

## Taxonomy

[Designer](position_designer.md). Turns a written brief into a built thing, competently, whatever the brief asks for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Practical, unhurried, a tradesperson who takes pride in meeting a spec exactly.
Reads [The Brief](piece_the_brief.md) as a function to solve, a surface that discourages lying down, and solves it the way any brief gets solved.
No relish in the outcome and no distaste either: a job is a job, met on schedule.

## Action

Drafts and fabricates [The Divider](piece_the_divider.md) to the brief's spec.
Installs it at [The Frontage](place_the_frontage.md), tests it against the drawing, signs it off.
Moves to the next contract the moment this one closes.

## Shadow

Does not see that meeting the brief exactly, without cruelty and without excess, is what makes the object work at all.
Believes the refusal to add anything harsher than the spec requires proves the hands are clean, when the object built to the letter of the spec is already, on its own, sufficient to do everything the spec was written for.
Competence completes the exclusion here; no further decision was needed, and none was made.

## Tell

Measures the gap in the bench twice against the printed drawing, and never once against a body.
Signs the completion certificate with the same care given to every other job on the books.
