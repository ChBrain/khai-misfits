# Position: Officer

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md). The seat that answers a complaint about a place, and closes when a fix is proposed for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Authority to log a complaint, open a case against an address, and issue a written brief in answer to it.
No housing budget, no housing power, no authority over what happens to a person once the case against the place is closed.

## Orders

Take in a complaint specific enough to log.
Answer only what the complaint names, and draft a brief that fixes the address, never the person found there.
Close the case once the brief is met.

## Loses

Any claim to have addressed the condition behind the complaint.
The seat can only ever answer what was written to it, so it stays innocent, by design, of what a resolution costs anyone the complaint never named, and cannot be held to a harm its own file never mentions.

## Drives

Toward a fix at the address named, and nowhere past its edges,
a remit exactly wide enough to be checked and, by the same measure, exactly narrow enough to miss whoever the fix moves.
