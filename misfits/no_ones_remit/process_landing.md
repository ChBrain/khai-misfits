# Process: Landing

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md). What happens to a moved harm once it arrives somewhere that never ordered it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A person, and nothing else: no brief, no case number, no commission trailing behind them.

## Direction

Toward a doorway in a place with no remit open to receive either of them, the person or the harm they carry.

## Lever

A harm that has to land somewhere meeting a place that was never asked whether it could hold it.

## Echo

A ward carrying, whole, what a frontage two miles off spent a whole chain of honest decisions moving off its own count.
