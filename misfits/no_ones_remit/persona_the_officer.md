# Persona: The Officer

## Taxonomy

[Officer](position_officer.md). Answers a complaint about a place, one case at a time, exactly as far as the case is written.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Procedural, courteous, unhurried.
Logs what is reported, in the words it is reported in, and treats the file as the whole of the matter until the file says otherwise.
Nothing cold in it: a case answered well is, to this seat, a job done right.

## Action

Opens the case [The Complainant](persona_the_complainant.md) brings, and drafts [The Brief](piece_the_brief.md) that answers it: a surface, a function, a spec.
Forwards the brief to [The Designer](persona_the_designer.md) once it is written.
Closes the case the day [The Divider](piece_the_divider.md) goes in, and marks the file done.

## Shadow

Cannot see that the very narrowness that lets the seat be held to an answer is what keeps the seat from ever being asked about the person on the other side of the fix.
Reads a closed file as a solved problem, not as a problem relocated past the edge of what the file was ever built to reach, so the closing itself hides the part that never got opened.
The remit was drawn this size on purpose, to be checkable, and being checkable is exactly what stops it from being wide.

## Tell

Files the case shut the moment the brief is sent, a single motion, the same motion whatever the brief turns out to build.
Never once revisits a closed file to see what stands where the complaint used to be.
