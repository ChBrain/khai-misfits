# Position: Manager

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md). The seat that measures a scheme against what it was commissioned to change.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A metric, a reporting line, and the authority to publish a figure once it is counted.
No authority to count anything the commission did not name.

## Orders

Count the condition the commission named, at the address the commission named.
Report the change, honestly, whichever way it runs.
File the figure, and let it stand as the record of the scheme.

## Loses

Any obligation to count what the commission never asked it to keep.
The seat answers for the number, not for where the number went, so a fall it reports in full good faith can be true of the address and silent about everyone the address let go of.

## Drives

Toward a report that is true of the frontage and only the frontage,
a figure exact enough to be trusted and, by that same exactness, narrow enough to never once ask where the difference between two counts actually walked to.
