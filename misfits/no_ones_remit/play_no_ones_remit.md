# Play: No One's Remit

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

No One's Remit.
A shopkeeper's doorway fills with a stranger most nights, and she names the fact of it, truthfully, to whoever answers complaints for the street. The one who answers holds a remit built exactly to that size: to take the complaint, not to take the person's whole condition, so it passes forward the only kind of order its seat can write, a brief for a surface no one can lie flat on. A designer meets the brief the way any competent hand meets a brief, because refusing it only hands the job to whoever will not, and what goes up is a bench with one bar too many. A manager, months later, counts the doorway against last quarter and reports what the count shows: fewer people sleeping rough at that address, exactly the number the scheme was asked to move. Every seat in the chain did its own job well, and somewhere past the edge of all of their remits a person who was never party to any of it picks up two bags and walks to a doorway in a ward that asked for none of this, and gets, whole, everything the frontage moved off its books.

## Arc

The bend is this: a remit exists so someone can be held to an answer, and it can only be held if it is narrow enough to check, so every remit built to be answerable is built, by the same stroke, to leave someone standing just outside its edge.
It runs on the fact that a remit is not a wall. It is a shape, and a shape excludes as certainly as a wall does, only without meaning to.
The complainant asks only for what the fact of her own doorway entitles her to ask for, and she is owed an answer. The officer answers only the complaint, because that is the whole of what the seat was built to hold, and the condition behind the complaint, the reason someone was sleeping there at all, sits outside the paper the officer was handed. The officer's only lawful move forward is a brief, and a brief that fixes a doorway can only describe the doorway, never the person who used it, so the brief asks for a surface and gets, honestly, a surface. The designer, handed a brief that names a function and not a person, builds to it the way the trade builds to any brief: competently, and would lose nothing but the contract by refusing, so refusing is not integrity, it is only unemployment with an extra step. The manager counts what the scheme was commissioned to reduce, and the count is not massaged, it is real, so the report the manager files is the truth about the frontage and only the frontage.
None of it is folly, and nowhere in the chain is a decision that weighs the complainant's doorway against the person who slept in it, because no seat in the chain was ever built with the scale to hold both sides of that weighing at once. The exclusion is not a mistake any office made. It is the sum every office's narrowness adds up to, once each has done its own job in full.
Widen any single remit to take in the displaced and it stops being answerable: the officer would need a housing budget it was never given, the designer a client no one is paying, the manager a column the scheme never asked it to keep. So the fix is not a better decision inside the chain, it is a new seat with a wider remit than any of these, funded against a harm that shows up nowhere on the books it is meant to watch, and that seat is expensive, unpopular, and built against every incentive that made the narrow chain work as well as it did.

## Triggers

**[The Complaint](plot_the_complaint.md)**
Cue: the shopkeeper's doorway, mornings that start by stepping over a stranger to lift the shutter. The Complainant brings the fact of it, true and specific, to The Officer, whose remit is built to take exactly that and nothing behind it. Closes when the complaint is logged and a brief is drafted to answer it.

**[The Instruction](plot_the_instruction.md)**
Cue: the frontage marked now as a job, the complaint closed on the officer's side and waiting on a fix. The Officer hands The Brief to The Designer, who meets it the way any brief is met, and The Divider goes up at the doorway. Closes when the surface that discourages lying down is built and installed.

**[The Count](plot_the_count.md)**
Cue: the frontage months on, The Divider settled into the bench, the doorway empty on most mornings now. The Manager counts what the scheme was commissioned to reduce and files The Figure, true and falling. Closes when the report is published and read as a success.

**[The Displacement](plot_the_displacement.md)**
Cue: the same frontage, the same divider, and a person who has tried and failed to lie flat against it once too often. The Displaced, party to none of the plans that reached this doorway, gathers what fits in two bags and leaves. Closes when the doorway is empty of the one thing none of the scheme's ledgers were ever built to hold.

**[No One Commissioned This](plot_no_one_commissioned_this.md)**
Cue: a doorway two miles off, in The Ward, that ordered no brief and read no figure about a frontage it has never heard named. The Displaced arrives, still working Find Another Doorway, and the whole of what the frontage moved lands there entire. Closes when the harm sits whole in a place that answers for none of it, and the chain that produced it has nowhere left to reach.

## Stakes

Whether a person can be moved by an exclusion that no seat in the chain was ever asked to see, and whether the ward the harm lands in ever gets a remit wide enough to be blamed for it.
Every plot moves the same way: toward an answer that is honest and complete within its own remit, and away from any seat wide enough to hold the whole of what the answer costs. The production cannot make any office in the chain corrupt or cruel, because widening the account to find one would lose the mechanism it is staging: the complaint stays true, the brief stays legitimate, the count stays accurate, and still a person is moved off every ledger that was ever kept about the doorway. It can only show that a remit answerable enough to be held is narrow enough to miss what it moves, that the sum of every seat doing its job is an exclusion no seat authored, and that the only seat that could answer for the whole of it, wide enough to weigh the complainant against the displaced, does not yet exist, and would cost exactly what none of the narrower seats were ever funded to spend.
