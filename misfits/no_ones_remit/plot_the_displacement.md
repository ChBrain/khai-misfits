## Action

## Stage

## Tension

Nothing here forces the move the way a rule forces a fine, and no one arranged it: every seat that touched this doorway was finishing its own remit, and finishing it discharged The Displaced without ever addressing them. No brief was written about them, no figure was kept for them, no complaint was ever made against them; there is no seat in the whole chain whose job it was to see them, only seats whose job it was to solve someone else's problem near them.
