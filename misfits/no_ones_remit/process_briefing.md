# Process: Briefing

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md). The officer's answer to a logged case, turned into an instruction a trade can build to.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A case with nowhere further inside the officer's own remit to go, and a physical fix that could plausibly answer it.

## Direction

Toward a written spec naming a surface and a behavior to discourage, and nothing about who the surface is meant for.
The brief can only describe the fix; it has no section for the person the fix removes.

## Lever

The officer's need to close the case meeting its own inability to touch the condition behind it, so a brief is the one move left that both acts and stays inside the remit.

## Echo

A brief in a designer's hands, and a contract that now depends on meeting it exactly as written.
