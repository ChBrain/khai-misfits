# Position: Designer

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md). The seat that turns a written brief into a built thing, paid to meet the spec and nothing else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A trade, a client, and a contract that runs exactly as long as the brief in hand is met.
No standing to ask what the brief is for beyond the function it names.

## Orders

Read the brief as written.
Design and fabricate to its function, on schedule, to a professional standard.
Install, test against the drawing, and sign the job off.

## Loses

Any standing to weigh the brief's purpose against its effect.
Refusing a brief costs this seat the contract, not the outcome: the job goes to whichever firm will build it, so declining buys nothing but unemployment, and the seat loses the very leverage that would let refusal mean anything.

## Drives

Toward a competent object that satisfies exactly what was asked and nothing the brief did not ask,
so the object built is precisely as effective, and precisely as blameless, as the spec that ordered it.
