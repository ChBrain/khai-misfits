# Piece: The Brief

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md). The document that turns a closed complaint into an instruction a designer can build to.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Drafted at the officer's desk, held by [The Designer](persona_the_designer.md) once issued, checked again against the finished divider at [The Frontage](place_the_frontage.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the designer has nothing lawful to build to, and the officer has no way to close a case it cannot otherwise touch.
It is the one document that lets a narrow remit reach past its own edge without the remit itself ever having to widen: everything the play stages downstream depends on this single instruction existing.

## Apparent

A page naming a function, a location, and a spec: a surface that discourages lying down, dimensions given, a deadline attached.
Nothing on the page describes who might have used the surface before.

## Yearbook

Drafted the morning [The Complaint](plot_the_complaint.md) closes, handed across a desk to [The Designer](persona_the_designer.md), referenced once more when [The Divider](piece_the_divider.md) is signed off, and filed, finally, in a case marked resolved.
