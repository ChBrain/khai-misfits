# Persona: The Manager

## Taxonomy

[Manager](position_manager.md). Counts a scheme against what it was commissioned to change, and reports the figure honestly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Numerate, satisfied, careful with figures.
Treats a falling count as the sentence the whole scheme was written to earn, and reports it plainly once it is earned.
Nothing invented in the number: the count is exact, and exactness is the whole of the job.

## Action

Counts [The Frontage](place_the_frontage.md) against its own history, tallies the change, files [The Figure](piece_the_figure.md).
Publishes the report once the tally is checked.
Cites the figure afterward, whenever the scheme is asked about, as the answer to the question.

## Shadow

Cannot see that the count is faithful to the frontage and blind to everywhere else the missing number simply walked to.
Reads a fall in the figure as the condition solved, because the figure is the only place the condition was ever asked to show up, so a person who moved off the count entirely reads, to this seat, exactly like a person who was helped.
The report is true and complete about its own address, and that completeness is what lets it stand in for a story it never measured.

## Tell

Circles the falling number in the report and never once circles the address it fell out of.
Files the figure with the commission and closes the folder the same afternoon.
