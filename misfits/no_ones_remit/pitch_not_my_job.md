# Pitch: Not My Job

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Procedural, reasonable, mild.
Everyone in the chain is doing exactly what their seat was built to do, and doing it correctly, so the register never once has to raise its voice.

## Undertow

Under the reasonableness, a completeness that never quite closes.
Every remit answers cleanly, on its own terms, and something keeps landing just past the edge of all of them at once, unaddressed by any of the clean answers that produced it.

## Nerve

The specific unease of watching several people be entirely right, one after another, and watching the same harm arrive somewhere none of them can see from their own desk.

## Echo

After the run: a doorway fixed, a figure published, a case closed, and a person carrying two bags into a street that was never asked whether it could take them.
