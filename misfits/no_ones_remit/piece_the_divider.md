# Piece: The Divider

## Taxonomy

[No One's Remit](play_no_ones_remit.md). The object the whole chain produces, and the only piece that states, in itself, what the scheme does.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set into the bench at [The Frontage](place_the_frontage.md), fixed, load bearing, a few centimeters wide.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is no scheme left to trace at all: every complaint, brief and count in the play exists only because this single bar sits exactly where a body used to lie.
It reads honestly to anyone who has never had to lie against it, and completely differently to anyone who has, and that difference in reading is the whole finding the play stages.

## Apparent

A plain steel armrest set mid bench, painted to match, catching the morning light, indistinguishable at a glance from a hundred ordinary benches.

## Yearbook

Fabricated to spec by [The Designer](persona_the_designer.md), installed the week [The Instruction](plot_the_instruction.md) closes, counted against in [The Count](plot_the_count.md), left behind by [The Displaced](persona_the_displaced.md), and, per the field record the house credits, not the only one of its kind ever set into a bench by a chain that ran exactly this way.
