# Persona: The Displaced

## Taxonomy

Holds no seat and was invited to none of the plans that reached [The Frontage](place_the_frontage.md); never a party to the scheme run there.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Practical, unhurried by habit, someone who has done this before and treats it as a chore rather than a catastrophe.
Tries the bench at [The Frontage](place_the_frontage.md) out of routine, finds [The Divider](piece_the_divider.md) where it was not before, and does not argue with a bar of steel.
Packs what fits in two bags and moves, the way the last move went and the one before it.

## Action

Leaves the frontage once the divider makes it unusable.
Tries the next doorway the same way: alone, on no schedule any office is keeping, off every list any of the seats behind the divider ever opened.
Arrives, eventually, at a doorway in [The Ward](place_the_ward.md) that has never heard the frontage named.

## Shadow

Reads the run of closed doorways as bad luck repeating, not as a run of doors built, in sequence, exactly against the shape of a person carrying two bags and nowhere fixed to be.
Never suspects that a complaint was filed, a brief was written and a figure was published about the last doorway, because nothing in any of those documents ever named a person to suspect it on behalf of.
Takes the erasure for chance, and the chance keeps repeating itself with a precision that pure chance would not carry.

## Tell

Checks the same short list of streets in the same order every time before settling, an old habit from when the order used to matter.
Never once revises the list to ask why so many of the doorways on it are narrower than they used to be.
