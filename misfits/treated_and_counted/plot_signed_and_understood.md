# Plot: Signed and Understood

## Taxonomy

[Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A table in [the unit](place_the_unit.md), and [the signed page](piece_the_signed_page.md) between two people, unhurried.
A sentence in it saying this is a study of dose and tolerance. A sentence saying benefit is not promised. A sentence saying the compound may do nothing.

## Action

[Seeing to](process_seeing_to.md): [The One Who Treats](persona_the_one_who_treats.md), holding [One Who Treats](position_one_who_treats.md), reads the page aloud in full and stops where it matters, while [The Enrolled](persona_the_enrolled.md), holding [Enrolled](position_enrolled.md), follows every sentence and repeats the design back correctly.

## Stage

[The One Who Treats](persona_the_one_who_treats.md), having tabled [Say It Plainly](plan_say_it_plainly.md), asks for the design back in the person's own words and gets it, accurately, before the signature.

## Tension

Everything a document can do has now been done, and the thing it was written to prevent is not prevented.
The comprehension is real: the sentences were followed and could be repeated. What the page cannot reach is not a fact but a frame, because the person is not misjudging the odds and is not hoping wrongly, they are simply sitting in a clinic being spoken to by their own doctor, and a page has no way to say "and yet this hour is not for you" loudly enough to be heard over the room it is read in.
