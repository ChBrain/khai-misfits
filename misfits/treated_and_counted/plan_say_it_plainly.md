# Plan: Say It Plainly

## Taxonomy

A **personal scheme**: the intent of [The One Who Treats](persona_the_one_who_treats.md), inside [Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Treats](persona_the_one_who_treats.md)

## Direction

Leave nothing implied. Read the design aloud rather than handing it over, stop at the sentence that matters most, check that it was heard, and say it again in ordinary words. Then, when the gap shows anyway, say it plainer still.

## Orders

(The One Who Treats reads [the signed page](piece_the_signed_page.md) aloud in full, stops where benefit is not promised, asks for the design back in the person's own words, and repeats the exercise at the next visit when the word "treatment" is used again.)

## Implementation

([The Unit](place_the_unit.md); the [seeing to](process_seeing_to.md) process; [the signed page](piece_the_signed_page.md) carries the words, and the room carries the argument against them.)

## Targets

- [ ] the design is read aloud in full, not handed across
- [ ] the sentence on benefit is stopped at and checked
- [ ] the design is repeated back in the person's own words
- [ ] the plainer version is tried when the gap shows, and its cost is noticed
