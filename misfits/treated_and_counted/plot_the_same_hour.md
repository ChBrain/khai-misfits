# Plot: The Same Hour

## Taxonomy

[Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Four hours in the chair in [the unit](place_the_unit.md), with [the drip](piece_the_drip.md) running.
An interval falling due every so often, and an observation owed to it.

## Action

[Seeing to](process_seeing_to.md) and [adding up](process_adding_up.md) at once: [The One Who Treats](persona_the_one_who_treats.md), holding [One Who Treats](position_one_who_treats.md), adjusts the blanket, treats the side effect that arrives at hour three, asks after the weekend and wants the answer, and logs each interval into [the casebook](place_the_casebook.md).

## Stage

[The One Who Treats](persona_the_one_who_treats.md) discharges both duties completely, in the same visit, with the same hands, and neither duty takes anything from the other.

## Tension

Both accounts of the hour are now complete, and neither has displaced the other.
There is no moment where the care turns out to have been cover for the collection, and none where the collection compromises the care: that is exactly why nothing can be caught. The contradiction does not sit in the doctor, who owes both and pays both, nor in the room, which is doing both honestly. It has only one place left to sit, and it is the chair.
