# Place: The Unit

## Taxonomy

[Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md). Where the hour is felt, and where every furnishing argues that this is care.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A chair that reclines. A blanket, offered before it is asked for. A stand, a bag, a line taped to the back of a hand.
A nurse who checks the rate on the way past. A doctor who knows the whole case and asks after the weekend.
Whatever a person takes in on sitting down, all of it at once, before a word is said.

## Holds

[The Drip](piece_the_drip.md), running at the side of the chair, saying what it has always said.
[The Signed Page](piece_the_signed_page.md), filed, having been read aloud in full.
[The Dose Card](piece_the_dose_card.md), clipped to the stand, carrying the amount the study had reached.

## Offers

Care, without reservation, to whoever is in the chair.
The side effect at hour three treated exactly as it would be treated in any ward.
The right to stop, on any day, and keep the doctor.

## Withheld

Any signal that would let the person tell which of the two accounts they are inside.
There is nothing to notice, because there is nothing hidden: the room is not disguising a study as care, it is doing both, so the setting cannot be read for a clue it does not contain.
