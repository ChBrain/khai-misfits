# Persona: The Protocol

## Taxonomy

[Protocol](position_protocol.md). The design itself, which assigns by where it has reached and not by who has arrived.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Impersonal, and not cold: written with great care by people who wanted no one hurt.
Holds the steps, the intervals, the stopping rules, the amounts. Everything in it exists because someone once was harmed in a way it now prevents.

## Action

Sets the amount on [the dose card](piece_the_dose_card.md) from its own position: the cohort ahead came through, so this cohort steps up.
Starts low on purpose, so that nobody meets the ceiling while the ceiling is unknown.
Stops, when the rule says stop, regardless of what anyone in the room hopes.

## Shadow

Cannot aim at the person in front of it, and would be worthless if it could.
A design that gave each arrival the amount that arrival seemed to need would never learn where the ceiling is, and every person after would take the risk instead. So the one thing that makes it trustworthy, that it answers a question rather than a face, is the same thing that makes the hour not about the face.

## Tell

Has no way of saying "for you", and does not pretend to.
Its silence on the point is honest, and is read, in a room full of care, as though the answer were yes.
