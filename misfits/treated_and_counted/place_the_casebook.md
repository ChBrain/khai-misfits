# Place: The Casebook

## Taxonomy

[Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md). Where the hour becomes a row, and the knowledge accrues for someone not present.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Columns, and one line per person.
An amount, a set of intervals, a mark against each. What was observed, at what hour, at what dose.
Nothing about the weekend, the blanket, or the doctor remembering the case, none of which the columns have a place for.

## Holds

The filled rows of the cohorts ahead, which are the reason this cohort steps up.
The empty rows below, which are the reason the study is still running.
[The Dose Card](piece_the_dose_card.md) in its finished form, once the ceiling is finally known.

## Offers

An answer that is general and durable, to [the one after](persona_the_one_after.md), who will receive it as an ordinary number on an ordinary prescription.

## Withheld

Any account of the hour as the person in it experienced it.
Not by neglect: the columns are exactly the columns the question needs, and a column for what the hour felt like would answer no question the study is able to ask. So the record is complete and true and contains no trace of the half of the hour that was care.
