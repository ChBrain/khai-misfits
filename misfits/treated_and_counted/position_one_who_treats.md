# Position: One Who Treats

## Taxonomy

[Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md). The seat that owes care to the person and data to the question, in the same visit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The whole case in memory, and the standing that comes with it.
Authority over the care: the rate, the blanket, the side effect at hour three, the decision to hold or reduce or stop.
No authority at all over the amount, which [the protocol](persona_the_protocol.md) has set.

## Orders

Read [the signed page](piece_the_signed_page.md) aloud, in full, and stop where it says benefit is not promised.
See to the person as any doctor would.
Log the observation at every interval into [the casebook](place_the_casebook.md).

## Loses

The power to be heard as only one of its two roles.
It can state the design accurately, and does, and it cannot make the statement carry, because the voice saying it is the voice that has been treating this person for years, and a familiar voice in a familiar room is heard as the thing it has always been.

## Drives

Toward two duties that do not conflict and do not merge: nothing the seat does for the study costs the person, and nothing it does for the person is why the study is there, so it discharges both perfectly and still hands the contradiction to the chair.
