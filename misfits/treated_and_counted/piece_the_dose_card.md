# Piece: The Dose Card

## Taxonomy

[The Unit](place_the_unit.md). The amount, set by where the study has reached rather than by who has arrived.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Clipped to the stand beside the chair, at eye height, where anyone can read it.
Nothing about it is concealed. It simply does not say why.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the two accounts of the hour could still be told apart by feel, and the design's indifference would stay abstract.
It is the piece that makes the indifference physical: the number on it is the study's position made into millilitres, so the clearest statement of what the hour is for is the one thing going into the arm.

## Apparent

A cohort letter, an amount, a rate. Printed, checked, initialled twice.
Reads exactly like the card that would hang there if this were treatment, because in every respect except the one that matters, it is the same card.

## Yearbook

Lower, a month ago, and deliberately so: the early cohorts take less on purpose, while the ceiling is unknown.
Higher next month, if this cohort comes through.
Fixed at last, when the ceiling is found, into the number [the one after](persona_the_one_after.md) will be given without ever being told it was ever in doubt.
