# Pitch: Nobody Lied

## Taxonomy

[Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Quiet and clinical, and warmer than clinical usually sounds.
A room where the blanket is offered before it is asked for, where the document is read aloud in full rather than slid across a desk, and where the person doing the reading knows the whole case by heart. Nothing furtive in it: everything said is true, and said kindly.

## Undertow

Under the kindness, an hour that answers to two different questions and settles neither.
Because the care is real and the measurement is real and both are running at once, there is no moment where one turns out to have been a cover for the other, and so no moment where anything can be caught. The unease has nowhere to land, which is why it lands on the only person required to hold both accounts at the same time.

## Nerve

The small, repeated turn of finding that a sentence you understood perfectly did not do to you what it was written to do.

## Echo

Afterward: a table with its rows filled in, a number that will be given to people not yet ill, a document still on file with every sentence accurate, and no one anywhere in the record who did anything wrong.
