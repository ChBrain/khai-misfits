# Treated and Counted: Reference

The research warrant for Treated and Counted.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the therapeutic misconception, named by Appelbaum, Roth and Lidz
and worked on for forty years since, with the ethics of the double role and the measured stakes of an
early-phase study carried alongside it, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the therapeutic misconception as a **structure with two true accounts of one hour**: a person is
cared for and measured by the same hand at the same time, and the design doing the measuring was never
built around that person's good. The play models the documented mechanics, the exhausted alternative that
makes enrolling the rational act, the design that assigns by its own position rather than by the person's
need, the sincere double role of one who treats and collects in the same visit, the document that states
all of it truly and does not land, the setting that outvotes the document because everything standing in
the room is an instrument of care, and the beneficiary who is not present because they are not yet ill, as
the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of deception.

The governing law is that the two accounts do not resolve into one. Care answers to the person in front of
it; a study answers to a question, and a question is not a person. Both are honest, both are running, and
the only place they are required to be held at once is inside the one who consented. So the person with the
fewest remaining options is systematically the one who carries the stage of a compound's life with the
least expected benefit and the least known risk, and no one at any point has lied to them.

Appelbaum's group found the shape by asking: most could not say how they had been assigned and few grasped
randomisation, while believing the treatment in front of them had been chosen because it suited them. The
finding that makes it structural rather than clerical is what followed it. The misconception survives
disclosure. A person can read the document, understand its sentences, repeat them back correctly, and go on
reasoning as a patient in a clinic with their doctor, because that is exactly where they are sitting and
everything in the room agrees with them.

It is distinct from the house's **Ask the One Who Sells**, where the one who advises is paid to supply, so
the advice bends: there the interest is adverse and financial. Here both roles are sincere, the one who
treats gains nothing from the second role that the first would not have given, and the tension is in the
design rather than in the pocket.

It is distinct from the house's **True of No One**, where a finished trial reports one valid average and
the many are treated for a benefit only the few were going to get: there the study is over and its result
is being applied outward. Here the study is running and the person is inside it, and there is no result
yet to misapply.

It is distinct from the house's **No One's Remit**, where each remit answers narrowly and honestly and the
person no remit was built to see falls through all of them at once: there the person is unseen. Here the
person is seen precisely, assessed, measured, entered and followed, and the misfit is what that seeing is
for.

It is distinct from the house's **First Do Harm**, where a remedy given in good faith inflicts a separate
new injury: there the harm is the misfit. Here the harm may never arrive, the compound may even work, and
the structure stands unchanged either way, because it is made of what the hour is _for_, not of what it
costs.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the one who
  treats as a deceiver or the enrolled as credulous, because the source finding is precisely that both act
  well: the document is accurate and is read aloud, the hope is not a delusion and by Horng and Grady's cut
  is not even an error, and entering a study when the shelf is empty is the reasonable move and not a
  failure of nerve. The design is not a trick either: assigning by the study's position is the only way a
  dose is ever learned, and a compound that skipped it would injure everyone who came after. The
  misfit is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, two honest accounts of one hour that meet
  only inside the person consenting, so the care and the counting are not rivals to be separated but the
  same visit described twice.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Saying it harder is the
  obvious answer and it is the one the record already tested: comprehension of the sentences rises and the
  misconception does not go with it, because the room is making the stronger argument. And a disclosure
  blunt enough to actually dissolve it would do its work by dissolving the hope alongside, which would turn
  away the person who might have gained and would take from everyone else the thing that makes the hour
  bearable. Separating the roles, so that one clinician treats and a stranger enrols, buys clarity by
  removing the person who knows the case, which is a real loss charged to the same patient. There is no
  arrangement that is both fully honest and fully kind.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the naming, the measurement scales, the ethical
  argument and the outcome figures are theirs. The bounds are carried with them. **The play makes no claim
  that an early-phase study cannot help the person in it**: the 2005 figures come from an era of
  cytotoxic single agents, and the expansion-cohort record since is markedly different, so a reading of
  this misfit as "the drug will not work" has left the mechanism and is wrong on the house's own terms.
  What is staged is the double account of the hour, which holds whether the compound works or not.
