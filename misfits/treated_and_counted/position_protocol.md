# Position: Protocol

## Taxonomy

[Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md). The seat that assigns by its own position, because that is the only way an amount is ever learned.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The steps, the intervals, the stopping rules, and the authority to set the amount without consulting anyone in the room.
A ceiling it does not yet know, which is the whole reason it exists.

## Orders

Start low, so no one meets the ceiling while it is unknown.
Step up when the cohort ahead comes through.
Stop when the rule says stop, whatever anyone hopes.

## Loses

Any capacity to be about the person in front of it.
The moment it began setting amounts by what an individual seemed to need, it would stop learning where the ceiling is, and the risk it declined to take would fall on everyone who came after: so its indifference is not a defect to be corrected but the property that makes it worth trusting.

## Drives

Toward an answer that is durable and general, held by [the one after](persona_the_one_after.md), and away from the particular person whose arm is receiving it, so the design's virtue and the misfit are the same property seen from two sides.
