# Piece: The Signed Page

## Taxonomy

[The Unit](place_the_unit.md). The document that states the whole design truthfully, and does not land.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Read aloud in [the unit](place_the_unit.md), across a table, and then filed.
Ten minutes in a room that will go on speaking for four hours.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the misfit would be an ordinary deception and would dissolve the moment anyone told the truth.
It is the piece that makes the structure structural: everything the person needed to know was said, in order, in plain words, and checked. What fails is not the telling.

## Apparent

Several pages, plainly set. A sentence saying this is a study of dose and tolerance. A sentence saying benefit is not promised. A sentence saying the compound may do nothing at all.
A line at the end, and a date, and a signature that was not hurried.

## Yearbook

Rewritten many times, each revision plainer than the last, each one added after someone somewhere was found not to have understood.
Understood correctly, now, by nearly everyone who signs it.
Still filed, still accurate, and still doing less than it was written to do.
