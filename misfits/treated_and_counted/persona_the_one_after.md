# Persona: The One After

## Taxonomy

[One After](position_one_after.md). The party the design actually serves, who is not in the room and cannot be asked.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Absent. Not indifferent, not ungrateful: simply not present, and not consultable, because they are not ill yet.
May be perfectly well today. May be a child now. May be the person who, in eleven years, is handed an amount that is known to be safe and never learns how it came to be known.

## Action

Receives, later, the one durable product of the hour: a number that was not guessed.
Takes it without ceremony, as an ordinary prescription, from a doctor who states the dose plainly because by then it is plain.

## Shadow

Cannot consent, cannot thank, and cannot decline on anyone's behalf.
So the beneficiary of the design is the one party with no seat at it, and the person who does have a seat is asked to carry a study whose gains are addressed elsewhere. Nobody arranged that. It falls out of the calendar: illness arrives in an order, and knowledge has to be made before it can be given.

## Tell

Says nothing at all, in the whole production, and is the reason for all of it.
