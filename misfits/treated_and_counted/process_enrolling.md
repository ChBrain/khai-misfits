## Taxonomy

## Owner

[The Enrolled](persona_the_enrolled.md), holding [Enrolled](position_enrolled.md), at [the unit](place_the_unit.md).

## Direction

Toward the only remaining place, and toward it clear-eyed.

## Lever

Neither condition is anyone's doing, and both have to hold at once for the door to be a door.
