# Persona: The Enrolled

## Taxonomy

[Enrolled](position_enrolled.md). Comes to the unit because the shelf is empty, and reasons the whole way in like the patient they have been for years.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Attentive. Well informed by now, after a long time being ill: reads what is handed over, asks good questions, knows the vocabulary.
Not credulous, and not desperate in the way the word is usually meant. Arrives at the unit having worked through what is left, found it to be nothing, and concluded that a place in a study is the only place there is.

## Action

Follows [the signed page](piece_the_signed_page.md) sentence by sentence, repeats the design back correctly, and signs.
Sits four hours with [the drip](piece_the_drip.md) in the back of the hand and answers every question the interval asks.
Watches for a change, the way anyone would.

## Shadow

Cannot stop reading the room, and the room is a treatment room.
Every furnishing argues that this is a place where people are made better: the chair, the blanket, the checked rate, and above all the presence of a doctor who knows the whole case. So the design stated on the page is understood and does not land, because the page had ten minutes and the room has four hours and says the same thing every one of them.

## Tell

Says "my treatment" and means the study, and would correct it if asked, and would say it again next visit.
Says it without any false idea of the odds: the misconception is not about how likely the good outcome is, it is about what the hour is for.
