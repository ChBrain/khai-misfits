# Persona: The One Who Treats

## Taxonomy

[One Who Treats](position_one_who_treats.md). Cares for the person and collects for the study, in the same visit, sincerely in both.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Warm and exact. Knows the case without opening the file, remembers what happened at the last visit and asks after it.
Reads [the signed page](piece_the_signed_page.md) aloud in full rather than sliding it across the desk, and stops at the sentence about benefit not being promised to make sure it was heard.

## Action

[Seeing to](process_seeing_to.md) the person: the rate, the blanket, the side effect that arrives at hour three and is treated as it would be treated anywhere.
Logging the observation at every interval against the dose, into [the casebook](place_the_casebook.md), because the interval is when the study asks.

## Shadow

Cannot be only one of the two, and cannot make the room say so.
The second role costs the first nothing and gains it nothing, so there is no bent advice to catch and no interest to declare. But the knowing of the case, which is what makes the care good, is also exactly what makes the study's account inaudible: the person hears their doctor, because that is who is speaking.

## Tell

Says "we'll see how you do on this dose", meaning both things at once, and could not have said it in a way that meant only one.
