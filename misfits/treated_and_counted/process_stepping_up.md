# Process: Stepping Up

## Taxonomy

[Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md). The assignment of the amount, by the study's position and by nothing else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A cohort ahead that came through without reaching the ceiling.
[The Protocol](persona_the_protocol.md), holding [Protocol](position_protocol.md), writing to [the dose card](piece_the_dose_card.md).

## Direction

Toward the ceiling, one increment at a time, from deliberately below.
Aimed at finding where the compound stops being tolerable, which is a question about the compound and not about anyone receiving it.

## Lever

A rule written in advance, applied without exception, which is the only thing that makes the answer worth having.

## Echo

An amount on the card, higher than last month's and lower than next month's, going into an arm that had no say in which month it arrived.
