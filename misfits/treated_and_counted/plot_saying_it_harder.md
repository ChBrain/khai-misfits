# Plot: Saying It Harder

## Taxonomy

[Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The word "treatment" used again, at a later visit, about the study.
The gap noticed, and the obvious remedy reached for.

## Action

[Seeing to](process_seeing_to.md): [The One Who Treats](persona_the_one_who_treats.md), holding [One Who Treats](position_one_who_treats.md), rewrites [the signed page](piece_the_signed_page.md) plainer, reads it again, and checks sentence by sentence that each one was understood.

## Stage

[The One Who Treats](persona_the_one_who_treats.md), still working [Say It Plainly](plan_say_it_plainly.md), gets what the plan asked for: comprehension measurably higher than before, every sentence returned accurately.
And the frame does not move, because [the drip](piece_the_drip.md) is still running and the voice reading the page is still the voice that has been treating this person for years.

## Tension

Every remaining correction is paid for by the same person it is meant to protect.
A version blunt enough to actually dissolve the frame would work by dissolving the hope with it, and Horng and Grady's cut says the hope was never the error: so the cure would take the load-bearing thing and might turn away the one person who would have gained. Splitting the roles, so that a stranger enrols and the doctor only treats, buys the clarity by removing the one who knows the case. There is no arrangement here that is both fully honest and fully kind, and the choice between them is made in a room where only one of the two people present has to live inside the result.
