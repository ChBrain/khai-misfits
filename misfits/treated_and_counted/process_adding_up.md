# Process: Adding Up

## Taxonomy

[Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md). The accrual: rows filling until a number can be carried out of the room.

## Owner

[The One Who Treats](persona_the_one_who_treats.md), holding [One Who Treats](position_one_who_treats.md), writing into [the casebook](place_the_casebook.md).

## Direction

Toward generality, and away from this hour in particular.
Each row is worth having only because it will be read alongside rows from people who never met, so the process is finished exactly when the individual stops being visible in it.

## Lever

Consistency: the same interval, the same question, the same column, whoever is in the chair.

## Echo
