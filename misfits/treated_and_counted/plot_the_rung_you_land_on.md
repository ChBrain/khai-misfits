# Plot: The Rung You Land On

## Taxonomy

[Treated and Counted](play_treated_and_counted.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The cohort ahead came through, and nobody in it reached the ceiling.
[The Dose Card](piece_the_dose_card.md), clipped to the stand at eye height, carrying a number that is higher than last month's.

## Action

[Stepping up](process_stepping_up.md): [The Protocol](persona_the_protocol.md), holding [Protocol](position_protocol.md), sets the amount from its own position, while [The Enrolled](persona_the_enrolled.md), holding [Enrolled](position_enrolled.md), takes it.

## Stage

[The Protocol](persona_the_protocol.md), having tabled [Finish the Steps](plan_finish_the_steps.md), assigns without consulting anyone present, because consulting anyone present is the one thing that would make the answer worthless.

## Tension

The amount is the clearest statement in the room of what the hour is for, and it is printed, unhidden, at eye height.
A month earlier the same person would have been given deliberately less, not through caution about them but because the early steps are low while the ceiling is unknown. A month later, more. Nothing about the number is a response to the person receiving it, and nothing about it is concealed from them: it is simply the study's position, made into millilitres, going into an arm.
