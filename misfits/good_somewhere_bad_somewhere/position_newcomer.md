# Position: Newcomer

## Taxonomy

[Good Somewhere, Bad Somewhere](play_good_somewhere_bad_somewhere.md). The seat any diner takes on arriving, free to come and just as free to leave.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A choice, nothing more: where to eat tonight, and a reason for it, the ease a fix has just bought.
No claim on the room beyond the table taken, no more standing than the diner who was already seated.

## Orders

Come where coming is easiest.
Take the table on offer.
Say what worked, to whoever asks.

## Loses

The very ease that drew it, the moment enough others answer the same call.
It gets the good table it came for, tonight; it does not get to keep the empty room that made the table good.

## Drives

Toward whichever gauge is currently easiest, so the seat's own honest choice, multiplied by every other honest choice like it, is what carries the fix's gain back out of the room.
