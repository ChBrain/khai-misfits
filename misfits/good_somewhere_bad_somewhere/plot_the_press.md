# Plot: The Press

## Taxonomy

[Good Somewhere, Bad Somewhere](play_good_somewhere_bad_somewhere.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Dining Room](place_the_dining_room.md), fuller on an ordinary night than it used to get on the busiest one.
Every table, new and old, seated, so the room now carries more covers a night than it has ever carried.

## Action

[Leveling](process_leveling.md): [The Level](persona_the_level.md), holding [Level](position_level.md) and tabled on [Hold the Level](plan_hold_the_level.md), draws down Speed and Quiet exactly as far as Room was raised, while [The Regular](persona_the_regular.md), holding [Regular](position_regular.md), sits through a slower plate and a louder room neither the Keeper nor the kitchen meant to give her.

## Stage

[The Regular](persona_the_regular.md), still on no plan but her own habit, keeps the same table and the same hour, and finds the wait for the plate longer than it was and the talk around her harder to hear over.
[The Line](piece_the_line.md) is not yet back at the door, but the room behind it no longer moves the way it did.

## Tension

Nobody slowed the kitchen on purpose, and nobody raised their voice on purpose.
The redraw runs by itself, once the room fills, cook for cook and table for table, with no single decision to point at and undo: the same fullness that rewards the Keeper's fix is what the kitchen and the room are quietly paying for, and there is no one moment either the Regular or the Newcomer could name as the mistake.
