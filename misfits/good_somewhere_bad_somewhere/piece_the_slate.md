# Piece: The Slate

## Taxonomy

[The Kitchen](place_the_kitchen.md). Where the house writes down, for anyone to read, which gauge it has chosen to leave short.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

By the door of [the dining room](place_the_dining_room.md), where the wait, on a bad night, is easiest to see.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the choice the Keeper makes stays private, and a private choice not to fix Room reads, to a waiting diner, exactly like neglect: it is the one piece that turns a deliberate shortfall into an announced one.

## Apparent

Chalk, or a printed card. A short line: "we do not add tables past this count." Changed rarely, and only on purpose.

## Yearbook

Blank, while every gauge was still being chased at once. Written, the season the Keeper stopped trying to fix all four, and left standing since.
