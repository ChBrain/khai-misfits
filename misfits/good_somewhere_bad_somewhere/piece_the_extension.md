# Piece: The Extension

## Taxonomy

[The Dining Room](place_the_dining_room.md). The added room, built once, to answer a wait that was real.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the back of [the dining room](place_the_dining_room.md), where the storeroom used to be.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the fix the Keeper made is only a plan, and nothing draws the Newcomer at all: it is the one thing every diner who "heard the wait was over" can point to and mean.

## Apparent

New tables, still smelling faintly of paint. A wider aisle where boxes used to sit. The same menu, printed fresh, laid on tables that were not there last month.

## Yearbook

Framed as the fix for a wait that once ran the length of the block. Opened quietly, on a night the regulars would see it first. Full, within a season, on the very nights it was built to keep empty.
