# Persona: The Regular

## Taxonomy

[Regular](position_regular.md). Was already coming for the quiet table and the fast plate, before the fix and before the crowd.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Settled. Knows the menu, the hour to avoid, the corner table.
Comes for the same two things every week: a plate that arrives quick, and a room quiet enough to talk in.
Notices the new tables approvingly, at first: more room is more room, and she expects nothing else to change.

## Action

Keeps the same table, the same hour.
Orders the same plate.
Says nothing when the room starts to fill differently, at first.

## Shadow

Cannot see that the crowd she is glad of is drawing down the very quiet and speed she never asked anyone to touch.
She holds no grudge, and there is none to hold: every diner around her has as fair a claim to a table as she does. What she cannot see is that the fullness she welcomes is spending, meal by meal, the two gauges that were never part of the fix and never meant to move.

## Tell

Says, some weeks later, "it did not used to take this long," surprised each time, since nobody touched the kitchen and nobody turned the room up on purpose.
