# Position: Level

## Taxonomy

[Good Somewhere, Bad Somewhere](play_good_somewhere_bad_somewhere.md). The seat that reads all four gauges as one total, and holds that total where a free door has always held it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

No table of its own, and no diner's loyalty. Only the sum: Room, Speed, Quiet, and Ease read together as one figure, [the rating](piece_the_rating.md), rather than four.

## Orders

Grant a real gain to a real fix.
Draw the untouched gauges down to match, once the diners drawn by the fix have arrived.
Settle the total at the level it has always held.

## Loses

Any claim to raise the whole.
It can move the weight from one gauge to another as often as a fix invites it to, but it cannot add to what a free door will not let grow, so every gain it grants is already spoken for elsewhere before the season is out.

## Drives

Toward the same total, however the mix beneath it shifts, so a place that improves one gauge without choosing which other to spend is driven, by this seat alone, back to exactly where it started.
