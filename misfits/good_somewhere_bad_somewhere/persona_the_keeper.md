# Persona: The Keeper

## Taxonomy

[Keeper](position_keeper.md). Reads the wait plainly and fixes what a fix can fix, in good faith.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Practical. Attentive to complaints. Good with a hammer and a ledger both.
Sees the line at the door on a busy night and reads it plainly: too few tables for too many diners, so more tables is the honest answer.
No spin in it: the fix he makes is exactly the fix the wait calls for.

## Action

Builds [the extension](piece_the_extension.md) where the storeroom stood.
Opens it the same week the wait was longest, so relief is felt by everyone who was standing in it.
Reads the empty wait, once it comes, as proof the fix has worked.

## Shadow

Cannot see that the ease he built is itself the advertisement.
He fixed the room for the diners already waiting, and never budgeted for the diners the fix would go on to invite, so what he built to empty the line is the very thing that will fill it again from a wider circle. He is not naive: the relief he built, working, was real and worth building, only its half life is shorter than a full one.

## Tell

Says, of a full room, "we needed more tables, and now we have them."
Says it again a season later, meaning to build a second extension, before he lets himself notice that the wait is back and the room is already bigger than the one that used to hold it.
