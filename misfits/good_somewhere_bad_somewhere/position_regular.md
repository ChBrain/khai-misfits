# Position: Regular

## Taxonomy

[Good Somewhere, Bad Somewhere](play_good_somewhere_bad_somewhere.md). The seat held by whoever was already coming, for the two gauges the fix never touched.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A standing habit, the same table and the same hour, and a stake in exactly the two gauges nobody built anything for: Quiet and Speed.
No say in how many tables get added, only in whether the two gauges it came for are still there once they have.

## Orders

Keep coming, for the quiet and the speed, as long as they hold.
Notice when they do not.
Say so, plainly, when asked.

## Loses

The two gauges it never spent anything to earn and never agreed to give up.
A full room borrows against them without asking, so a seat that wanted only room finds itself paying, in quiet and in speed, for a table it never needed.

## Drives

Toward the two untouched gauges as the plain measure of whether the diner is still the diner it was, so its contentment or its complaint is the readout the redraw shows first.
