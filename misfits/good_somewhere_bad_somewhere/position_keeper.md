# Position: Keeper

## Taxonomy

[Good Somewhere, Bad Somewhere](play_good_somewhere_bad_somewhere.md). The seat that answers a real shortage with a real fix, and later must answer for the shortage that follows it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The tools to relieve one gauge at a time: a crew, a build budget, a slate by the door.
Authority over the room itself, and none at all over who chooses to walk through the door once it is easier to.

## Orders

Fix what is genuinely short.
Build [the extension](piece_the_extension.md) when the wait is the complaint.
Post the truth on [the slate](piece_the_slate.md) once a fix has run its course and the shortage has only moved.

## Loses

The right to call any single fix the last one.
Because the ease it buys draws the very diners who go on to fill it, the seat can build room without ever buying a lasting slack, so it ends owning a bigger dining room and the same nightly wait it started with, on the nights that matter.

## Drives

Toward a level that a free door holds fixed no matter how well it builds, so its own competence becomes the reason the next season needs fixing too.
