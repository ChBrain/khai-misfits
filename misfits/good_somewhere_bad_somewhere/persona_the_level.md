# Persona: The Level

## Taxonomy

[Level](position_level.md). The draw itself, held level by whoever is free to come and just as free to go.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Even. Indifferent to which gauge carries the weight. Answers every improvement with a matching draw somewhere else.
Reads Room, Speed, Quiet, and Ease as one number, not four, and holds that number where the diners, coming and going, have always held it.

## Action

Grants [the extension](piece_the_extension.md) its real gain in Room, the night it opens.
Draws that same gain back out of Speed and Quiet once the new tables are as full as the old ones.
Settles, every time, at the same total it started from, made of a different mix.

## Shadow

It is the reason no single fix ever raises the whole.
A total that a free door holds level cannot be added to, only rearranged, because being worth the trip is a comparison against every other place a diner could be, and that comparison is set by how many diners choose to come, not by how good any one gauge gets. It is exactly as generous at the end of the season as at the start, and only what it is made of has changed.

## Tell

Says "there is no more of me to have, you can only move me from one gauge to another," and holds the same size however hard the Keeper builds.
