# Plot: Choosing the Poor One

## Taxonomy

[Good Somewhere, Bad Somewhere](play_good_somewhere_bad_somewhere.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Kitchen](place_the_kitchen.md), where a second extension sits on paper, next to the same numbers that emptied the first one's gain.
The choice not yet made plain to anyone but the Keeper, standing at the pass.

## Action

[Choosing](process_choosing.md): [The Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md), holding [Keeper](position_keeper.md), weighs a second build that would only move the shortage again, against naming one gauge to hold deliberately short.

## Stage

[The Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md), tabling [Hold the Line](plan_hold_the_line.md) in place of [Add the Tables](plan_add_the_tables.md), chooses Room, and writes it on [the slate](piece_the_slate.md) by the door: no more tables past this count.
[The Regular](persona_the_regular.md) reads it on her way to her usual seat, and finds the room, some nights, exactly as loud and exactly as quick as it used to be.

## Tension

The choice is not free, and nobody thanks the Keeper for making it.
A second extension would look, on paper, like more relief, and would only summon the next round of arrivals; choosing instead to leave Room visibly short protects Quiet and Speed at the plain cost of a wait some nights will always show, a deficiency any diner can point at and blame on the house. The alternative, chasing all four gauges at once and choosing none of them to be poor on purpose, was never free either: it would have spent the same total, spread thin enough that no single failing had a name, and left mediocre exactly what a deliberate choice leaves merely short in one place.
