# Process: Choosing

## Taxonomy

[Good Somewhere, Bad Somewhere](play_good_somewhere_bad_somewhere.md). Naming, in the open, which gauge stays short on purpose, so the other three can hold.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A rating that will not rise no matter which gauge is fixed next, and a Keeper who has run the sum.
[The Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md), holding [Keeper](position_keeper.md), at [the kitchen](place_the_kitchen.md).

## Direction

Toward one gauge, held deliberately below what a diner might want, so the other three are protected from ever being spent to buy it back.

## Lever

A house willing to be blamed for a wait it could, on paper, build its way out of, meeting a Regular and a Newcomer who both keep their quiet and their speed only because it does not.

## Echo
