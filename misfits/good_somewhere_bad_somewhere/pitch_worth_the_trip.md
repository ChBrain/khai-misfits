# Pitch: Worth The Trip

## Taxonomy

[Good Somewhere, Bad Somewhere](play_good_somewhere_bad_somewhere.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Warm and practical, the plain pleasure of a table that used to be hard to get.
Nothing smug in it: only the satisfaction of a fix that plainly works, tonight, for the diner standing in front of it.

## Undertow

Under the satisfaction, an arithmetic nobody ordered.
Because an easier table is worth mentioning, and mentioning it costs nothing, the very ease that pleases tonight's diner is what tomorrow's diner will spend it on, so the pleasure and its own quiet erosion travel together, table by table, in the same sentence.

## Nerve

The small, repeated surprise of a comfort that was fixed and is, later, somehow missing again, though nothing was ever taken back on purpose.

## Echo

After the season: a bigger room, worth exactly what the old one was worth, a slate that finally says which comfort will stay short, and a house neither blamed for the fix nor thanked for the choice, only trusted, or not, to keep making it in the open.
