# Plot: Word Gets Around

## Taxonomy

[Good Somewhere, Bad Somewhere](play_good_somewhere_bad_somewhere.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Dining Room](place_the_dining_room.md), a night that used to be quiet, now holding an ease worth talking about.
[The Line](piece_the_line.md) is gone at the door, and staying gone is news.

## Action

[Drawing](process_drawing.md): [The Newcomer](persona_the_newcomer.md), holding [Newcomer](position_newcomer.md), comes for the ease she heard about, while [The Level](persona_the_level.md), holding [Level](position_level.md), answers her arrival with everyone else's who heard the same thing.

## Stage

[The Newcomer](persona_the_newcomer.md) takes the table [the extension](piece_the_extension.md) provided, and tells a friend, in turn, exactly what she was told.
[The Regular](persona_the_regular.md), on no plan but her own habit, keeps her usual table and notices, for now, only that the room looks fuller than it used to.

## Tension

Each arrival, alone, is exactly right, and the sum of them is what will spend the very ease that drew them.
Nobody who comes is wrong to come: an easier table is a fair reason to choose one place over another, and a free diner choosing it is doing precisely what a free diner should. But the ease is only there because the room, until now, was not full, and every diner who arrives for it is one fewer empty seat standing behind the reason she came.
