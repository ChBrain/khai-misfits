# Persona: The Newcomer

## Taxonomy

[Newcomer](position_newcomer.md). Hears the wait is gone and comes for exactly that.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Easy to please. Practical about a night out. Chooses a table the way anyone free to choose does, by what is on offer tonight.
Hears from a friend that the dining room now seats you without the old wait, and decides, plainly, that tonight is the night to try it.

## Action

Walks in on a night that used to be quiet.
Takes the table [the extension](piece_the_extension.md) made room for.
Tells a friend, in turn, exactly what she was told: the wait is gone, go now.

## Shadow

Cannot see that her own arrival is what will bring the wait back.
She is not a burden: she is one diner choosing one good table on one good night, and the choice is entirely hers to make. What she cannot see is that a hundred diners making the same good choice, for the same good reason, refill the room that was emptied for them and slow the kitchen that was fast for them, so the very ease that drew her is the first thing her own arrival spends.

## Tell

Says "I heard you do not have to wait here anymore," pleased, the week before the wait quietly returns.
