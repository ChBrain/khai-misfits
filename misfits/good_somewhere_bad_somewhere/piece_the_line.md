# Piece: The Line

## Taxonomy

[The Dining Room](place_the_dining_room.md). The wait made visible, at the door, whatever room stands behind it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the sidewalk outside [the dining room](place_the_dining_room.md), when it forms, and nowhere when it does not.

## Load Bearing

Without it, nobody could see, at a glance, that the room's true size is not the number of tables but the number of diners who fit their evening around it: it is the plainest reading the gauge Ease has.

## Apparent

A short queue, or none at all. Coats in the cold. A hand held up at the door, counting how many are still ahead.

## Yearbook

Long, the season before the extension was built. Gone, the week it opened. Back, most busy nights, once word of the extension had done its work.
