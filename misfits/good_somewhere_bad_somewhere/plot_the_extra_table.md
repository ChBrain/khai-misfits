# Plot: The Extra Table

## Taxonomy

[Good Somewhere, Bad Somewhere](play_good_somewhere_bad_somewhere.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Dining Room](place_the_dining_room.md), a line long enough, and often enough, that more tables reads as the honest answer.
The room, for now, holding steady at its old size, so a fix made here truly buys back the wait it targets.

## Action

[Expanding](process_expanding.md): [The Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md), holding [Keeper](position_keeper.md), builds where the storeroom stood, while [The Level](persona_the_level.md), holding [Level](position_level.md), grants Room its real rise, for the moment, since nothing else has moved yet.

## Stage

[The Keeper](persona_the_keeper.md), having tabled [Add the Tables](plan_add_the_tables.md), opens [the extension](piece_the_extension.md) the week the wait was worst, reading the empty line at the door as proof the fix has worked.

## Tension

The fix works, and working is exactly what will undo it.
There is no error to find here: the wait was real, the storeroom was idle, the Keeper's read was sound, and the relief, tonight, is genuine. But an easier table is worth mentioning to a friend, so the very thing that proves the fix a success, an empty line where a long one stood, is the thing that will carry word of it out past the door.
