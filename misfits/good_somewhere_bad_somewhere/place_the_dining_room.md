# Place: The Dining Room

## Taxonomy

[Good Somewhere, Bad Somewhere](play_good_somewhere_bad_somewhere.md). Where every gauge is felt at once, by whoever is sitting in it tonight.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Tables, full or open. The line at the door, long or gone. The noise of the room, low or loud. The clock from order to plate, quick or slow.
Whatever a diner notices on walking in, all four at once, before a word is said.

## Holds

[The Extension](piece_the_extension.md), the room itself, which can grow.
[The Slate](piece_the_slate.md), by the door, posting what the house has chosen to be short at.
[The Rating](piece_the_rating.md), by the till, telling what the room has always been worth.

## Offers

A table to whoever walks in and finds one open, no matter how they heard about the room.
Nothing barred to a first visit that a regular does not also get.

## Withheld

More of all four gauges at once.
The room can be built bigger, and often is, but Room, Speed, Quiet, and Ease are not one thing to hand out together, so making one plentiful is never free: it is paid for on one of the other three, withheld the moment the room fills to match.
