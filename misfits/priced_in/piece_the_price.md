# Piece: The Price

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The market price, the public conveyor that carries what [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) paid to learn out to every reader at once, for nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set by [The Market](persona_the_market.md) through [Reflect the Trades](plan_reflect_the_trades.md), where orders taken at [The Floor](place_the_floor.md) are folded into the running print on [The Tape](place_the_tape.md). Moved by [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) the instant their trades leave [The Desk](place_the_desk.md), through [Gathering](process_gathering.md) and then [Revealing](process_revealing.md); read back by [The Uninformed](persona_the_uninformed.md) through [Reading](process_reading.md).

## Load Bearing

The Price is the engine of the paradox, not its casualty: it aggregates costly private research and hands the answer to anyone who looks, at no charge, the moment the informed act on what they paid to find out (Grossman and Stiglitz). HEDGE: it does this only partly, never completely, the transmission is never a clean copy of what was learned. Guard against reading the Price as a settled verdict that has already absorbed everything knowable; it is an active conveyor, and its very success at conveying is what starves the gathering that fills it.

## Apparent

It looks finished, a number that has already priced in whatever there is to know, so that further looking seems wasted. That look is the misdirection: the Price only ever carries a partial trace of what the informed learned, informative enough to seem complete, never complete enough to make the gathering behind it either pointless or worthless to keep doing.

## Yearbook

Carried by [Revealing](process_revealing.md), the core conveyance the play turns on; read off in [The Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) instead of paid for again. Its growing accuracy drives [The Return](piece_the_return.md) toward [Withering](process_withering.md), and only stays short of complete because [The Cover](piece_the_cover.md) keeps it from fully revealing, which is what settles it at [The Disequilibrium](piece_the_disequilibrium.md) rather than at full efficiency. Behind every print stands [The Cost](piece_the_cost.md) already paid at [The Desk](place_the_desk.md).
