# Persona: The Noise Trader

## Taxonomy

[Noise Trader](position_noise_trader.md). The trader who buys and sells for reasons unrelated to value, liquidity, habit, need, whim, and whose trades are the noise that lets the informed hide and profit, so that on average this trader loses to them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, various, unconcerned with the number.
Is everyone trading for reasons that have nothing to do with what a thing is worth: cash needed now, a rule to rebalance, a hunch, a habit, orders that arrive for their own sake and not to price anything.
No folly in it as such: these are real reasons to trade, and there must be many such traders for a market to have anyone to trade with at all.

## Action

Trades on [Trade Blind](plan_trade_blind.md), supplying [the Cover](piece_the_cover.md) that mixes into the flow [the Market](persona_the_market.md) reads, so [the Informed](persona_the_informed.md)'s trades cannot be told from the rest.
Cannot be picked out from the informed: because its trades look no different at the tape, they are the crowd the informed disappear into, and without that crowd the informed's every move would be read at once and their edge erased before they earned on it.
Does what a value-blind trader does in a market: it trades for its own reasons, and in doing so, unknowing, funds the whole arrangement.

## Shadow

Not a dupe and not a sacrifice made in bad faith: the Noise Trader has its own reasons and trades on them, and it is no one's design that it lose.
Because the informed earn precisely on the traders who trade without knowing the value, and because the noise is what keeps the price from revealing everything at once, this trader on average loses to the informed and, in losing, pays for the whole market's efficiency: the return that keeps anyone gathering is drawn, in the end, from the ones who trade blind, so the price is informative only to the degree that these traders keep supplying the cover the informed hide in.
It is the one who funds the finding-out, and the one who, on average, is out of pocket for it.

## Tell

Says it traded because it needed to, or chose to, and thinks no more about the number than that.
Cannot see that its untargeted trades are the cover the informed need and the source of what the informed earn, so that the market works at all only because it and its like keep trading for reasons that are not about being right.
