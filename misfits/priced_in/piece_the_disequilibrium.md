# Piece: The Disequilibrium

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The equilibrium degree of disequilibrium: a price informative enough to be useful and noisy enough that gathering still pays.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Reached by [The Market](persona_the_market.md) through [Reflect the Trades](plan_reflect_the_trades.md), the resting state of [The Price](piece_the_price.md) once [The Cover](piece_the_cover.md) has done its work at [The Floor](place_the_floor.md): never fully settled toward complete revelation, never collapsed to none.

## Load Bearing

The Disequilibrium is the resolution the paradox forces, not a flaw left unfixed: there is no arrangement that gives both a fully informative price and a standing incentive to inform it, so the market settles where [The Return](piece_the_return.md) stays just above zero and [The Price](piece_the_price.md) stays just short of complete (Grossman and Stiglitz). It holds permanently, not as a transitional stage on the way to full efficiency, because moving toward full efficiency is exactly what would erase the incentive that sustains it. Guard against reading the Disequilibrium as a market failure awaiting a fix; a fix that made the price fully informative would be the failure, since it would leave nothing for anyone to be paid for finding out.

## Apparent

It looks like an unfinished job, a market still catching up to what it should already know. That reading assumes the destination is reachable; it is not, the gap is not a lag to be closed but the condition under which the market can keep functioning as an information system at all.

## Yearbook

Set by [The Market](persona_the_market.md) on [Reflect the Trades](plan_reflect_the_trades.md); held open by [The Cover](piece_the_cover.md), which keeps [The Price](piece_the_price.md) short of fully revealing and [The Return](piece_the_return.md) short of [Withering](process_withering.md) to zero. It is the state in which [The Cost](piece_the_cost.md) still finds a payer and [The Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) still finds something worth reading, the whole structure resting where neither can win outright.
