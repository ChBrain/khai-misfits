# Pitch: It's All Priced In

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Cool, knowing, unbothered.
A market where the price already tells you what a thing is worth, so paying to work it out yourself looks like effort spent on an answer that is right there in the number.
Nothing false in it: the price really does carry what the informed have found out, and reading it off is genuinely the sensible thing to do.

## Undertow

Under the knowing, a price that only knows what someone paid to find out.
The moment a trader acts on research, the number moves and the research leaks into it for free, so the better the price reveals what the informed know, the less the informed are paid to know it.
A price that revealed everything would pay nothing for finding anything out, so it would leave no one any reason to find out, and then it could reveal nothing.

## Nerve

The comfort of reading the answer off the price, and the catch that the answer is only there because someone paid to put it there:
that every free read is a reward withheld from the one who did the work, so the more everyone reads and the fewer pay, the less the number has behind it,
and the market's own success at spreading what is known is what quietly stops it being known.

## Echo

After the run: a price informative enough to be worth reading and never complete, kept a little wrong on purpose so the finding-out still pays (Grossman and Stiglitz).
And the only thing that keeps it from collapsing into a number with nothing behind it, the noise of traders who buy and sell for reasons unrelated to value,
cover the informed hide in and the market's efficiency is paid for by, the losses of the ones who supply it, an incompleteness that is the condition of the price working at all.
