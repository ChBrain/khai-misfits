# Piece: The Return

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The compensation [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) need to justify [The Cost](piece_the_cost.md) of gathering, falling as the price grows more revealing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Earned by [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) on [Gather and Trade](plan_gather_and_trade.md), the gap between what they learned at [The Desk](place_the_desk.md) and what [The Price](piece_the_price.md) had already reflected before they traded; worn down by [Withering](process_withering.md).

## Load Bearing

The Return is the hinge the whole paradox turns on: it must exceed The Cost or gathering stops, but every trade that reveals information through [Revealing](process_revealing.md) narrows the very gap that pays for the next trade (Grossman and Stiglitz). A price that fully reflected all information would drive the Return to zero, and at zero no one gathers, so a fully informative price is self-defeating. HEDGE: the Return does not vanish outright, because [The Cover](piece_the_cover.md) holds it above zero; it only falls toward that limit as revelation improves. Guard against reading the Return as a fixed reward for skill; it is a moving residual, set by how much noise still hides the trade.

## Apparent

It looks like a payoff for being right, proof that research pays. That reading forgets what sets its size: the Return is not a wage for accuracy, it is what is left over once [The Price](piece_the_price.md) has already given away most of what was learned, and that leftover shrinks as the giving gets better.

## Yearbook

Driven toward zero by [Withering](process_withering.md) as [The Price](piece_the_price.md) grows more revealing; kept above zero only by [The Cover](piece_the_cover.md), without which [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) could not profit at all. Must clear [The Cost](piece_the_cost.md) paid at [The Desk](place_the_desk.md) or [Gather and Trade](plan_gather_and_trade.md) stops, the collapse [The Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) presses toward and [The Disequilibrium](piece_the_disequilibrium.md) is the only thing that arrests.
