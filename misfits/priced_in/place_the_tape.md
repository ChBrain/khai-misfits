# Place: The Tape

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The published stream of prices that anyone can read for the cost of looking. Hosts [Off the Price](plot_off_the_price.md), [The Necessary Noise](plot_the_necessary_noise.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The price itself, ticking, free to any eye that lands on it, carrying inside it whatever [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) already paid at [The Desk](place_the_desk.md) to learn.

## Holds

The gathered information, already spent, now folded into a number and handed out to every reader at once, [The Uninformed](persona_the_uninformed.md) included, at no charge to any of them.

## Offers

A free education in what the well-informed currently believe. Reading the tape substitutes, imperfectly but cheaply, for doing the research oneself, which is exactly what makes it worth reading instead of paying to gather.

## Withheld

The full picture. The tape never shows all the information, only a partly revealing trace of it, so it rewards a reader without ever making the reading equivalent to the gathering, and that gap is what keeps the tape worth publishing at all.
