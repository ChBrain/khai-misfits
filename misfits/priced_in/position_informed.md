# Position: Informed

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The seat of the trader who pays to find out what a thing is worth and trades on it, whose finding leaks into the price the instant it is used, and who gathers only while the return covers the cost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An edge, bought at a price: knowledge of what a thing is worth that the market has not yet shown, won by paying a real cost others declined.
A genuine claim: the edge is honestly earned, and it lets the seat trade ahead of the number and earn on the gap between what the price shows and what is true.
No way to keep the edge while using it, because trading on it moves the price and reveals it, so the seat holds its advantage only in the moment before the number catches up.

## Orders

Gather and trade.
Pay the cost to find out what the market has not priced, then trade on it to earn the gap between belief and price, before the number moves.
Gather only while it pays, because the cost is real and the edge leaks the moment it is used, so where the price reveals too quickly, stop.

## Loses

The edge, the instant it trades, and the reason to gather, as the price learns to reveal it faster.
Because the finding leaks into the price the moment the seat acts, and because the more the price reveals the less the seat earns, the return the seat needs falls as the market it feeds grows efficient, so the diligence that fills the price with meaning is the diligence the filled price stops paying for.
It keeps only what it earns before the number catches up, and loses the incentive itself where the number catches up at once.

## Drives

Toward the researcher who makes the price worth reading and is retired by the reading,
its finding given away in the act that pays for it and its return thinning as more read the price and fewer pay,
so the seat that puts the answer into the number is the seat the answered number leaves with nothing to earn.
