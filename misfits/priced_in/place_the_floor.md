# Place: The Floor

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). Where the informed, the uninformed, and the noise traders all place their orders and the price forms out of the mix. Hosts [The Reveal](plot_the_reveal.md), [The Vanishing Return](plot_the_vanishing_return.md), [No One Left to Look](plot_no_one_left_to_look.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Every order that crosses: size, side, timing, the running print. Nothing here carries a label of who sent it or why, only that a trade happened at a price.

## Holds

The mix underneath the print: how much of today's flow came from [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) trading on gathered information and how much from [The Noise Trader](persona_the_noise_trader.md) trading on none. That mixture is what the floor never discloses directly, only lets the price partly encode.

## Offers

A single number where three different motives settle. Anyone watching gets a price that has absorbed real research without paying for it, the floor's whole function is to let that absorption happen as trades clear.

## Withheld

Certainty about the price's own accuracy. Because informed and noise orders arrive indistinguishable, no one on the floor, not even [The Market](persona_the_market.md), can say how much of today's move is signal and how much is noise, so the price stays informative without ever becoming provably complete.
