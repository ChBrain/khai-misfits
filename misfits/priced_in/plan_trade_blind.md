# Plan: Trade Blind

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the noise trader's seat](position_noise_trader.md), inside [Priced In](play_priced_in.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Noise Trader](position_noise_trader.md)

## Direction

Buy and sell for whatever reason you actually have, cash to raise, a rule to follow, a habit, a whim, and pay no mind to what the thing is truly worth. Supply the flow the market needs, trading for your own sake and not to price anything, indistinguishable at the tape from every other order.

## Orders

([The Noise Trader](persona_the_noise_trader.md) trades for its own reasons and supplies [the Cover](piece_the_cover.md) into the flow [the Market](persona_the_market.md) reads, so [the Informed](persona_the_informed.md)'s trades cannot be told apart.)

## Implementation

([The Floor](place_the_floor.md) mixes value-blind orders with informed ones into a single flow; because the [cover](piece_the_cover.md) hides the informed's trades in the crowd, their edge is not read off at once, so [the Return](piece_the_return.md) stays above zero and the seat, trading without knowing the value, on average loses to the informed and funds the whole arrangement.)

## Targets

- [ ] trades are made for reasons unrelated to value, cash or rule or habit or whim
- [ ] the flow the market needs is supplied, indistinguishable at the tape from informed orders
- [ ] the noise is the cover the informed hide in, without which their edge would be read off at once
- [ ] the seat on average loses to the informed and, in losing, funds the return that keeps gathering alive
