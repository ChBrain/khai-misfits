# Persona: The Market

## Taxonomy

[Market](position_market.md). The mechanism that folds every trade into a single published price and so conveys the informed's findings to everyone for free, doing exactly what a market is for, and in doing it undercutting the finding-out it spreads.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Impartial, efficient, doing its one job well.
Is the machine that takes all the buying and selling and settles it into one number, moving the price toward what the trades imply and publishing it for anyone to read.
No design against anyone: it simply reflects the orders it receives, and the better it does so the more faithfully the price carries whatever the informed knew when they traded.

## Action

Reflects the trades on [Reflect the Trades](plan_reflect_the_trades.md), through [Revealing](process_revealing.md) at [the Floor](place_the_floor.md), moving [the Price](piece_the_price.md) and showing it on [the Tape](place_the_tape.md).
Cannot reveal the informed's findings without also giving them away: conveying information is the whole of what it does, so the same efficiency that makes the price worth reading is the efficiency that strips the informed of the edge they paid for the instant they use it.
Does what a good market does with the trades it is handed: it prices them faithfully, and in pricing them faithfully hands everyone, for nothing, what someone paid to find out.

## Shadow

Not rigged and not broken: the Market does precisely what it is admired for, spreading what is known as widely and cheaply as it can, and every step of that is correct.
Because conveying the finding is the same act as giving it away, the Market's very success is what removes the reward for producing the finding, so the more perfectly it prices, the less anyone is paid to inform it, and a market that priced perfectly would leave itself nothing to price: the machine cannot be made to reward the research it reveals, because revealing the research is what it is.
It is the efficiency everyone wants, and the efficiency that, carried all the way, would empty the number it publishes.

## Tell

Says the price reflects all available information, and the better it works the truer that sounds and the less there is behind it.
Cannot hold a fully informative price, because at that price no one is paid to inform it, so it settles for one a little short of the answer, noisy enough that the finding-out still pays, and could settle nowhere else.
