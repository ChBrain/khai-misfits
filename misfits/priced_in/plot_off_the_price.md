# Plot: Off the Price

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Tape](place_the_tape.md), moments after [The Reveal](plot_the_reveal.md). A print sits on the feed, unexplained but legible to anyone who reads prints for a living.

## Action

[Reading](process_reading.md): [The Uninformed](persona_the_uninformed.md), holding [the uninformed's seat](position_uninformed.md), runs [Read the Price](plan_read_the_price.md), taking [the free ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) instead of paying [the cost](piece_the_cost.md).

## Stage

[The Uninformed](persona_the_uninformed.md) watches [the tape](place_the_tape.md), sees [the price](piece_the_price.md) move against the old level, and reasons backward: something is known that was not known before, and the move itself says which way. No filing needed, no call made, no model built.
It declines to open [The Desk](place_the_desk.md) at all. Why pay [the cost](piece_the_cost.md) for an answer the market is already handing over, stamped in the print, for the price of watching.

## Tension

The choice is not lazy, it is correct: reading the price is cheaper than gathering and gets most of the way there. But every trader who reasons this way is one fewer trader paying [the cost](piece_the_cost.md), and the tape [The Uninformed](persona_the_uninformed.md) leans on exists only because someone, somewhere, still pays.
