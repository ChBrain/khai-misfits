# Plot: The Vanishing Return

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Desk](place_the_desk.md), some seasons on. The same nightly work, the same filings read, but the edge it used to buy has thinned.

## Action

[Withering](process_withering.md): [The Return](piece_the_return.md) to [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md), holding [the informed's seat](position_informed.md), falls as more of the market runs [Read the Price](plan_read_the_price.md) instead of [Gather and Trade](plan_gather_and_trade.md).

## Stage

Each round of [The Reveal](plot_the_reveal.md) leaves [the price](piece_the_price.md) a little more informative than the round before, so each round of [Off the Price](plot_off_the_price.md) has less left to infer and more reason to lean on the tape instead of the desk. Fewer traders now pay [the cost](piece_the_cost.md); more take [the free ride](piece_the_free_ride.md).
[The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) still does the work, but the price has already moved most of the way there by the time the trade goes in, so what the trade earns keeps shrinking against what the research cost.

## Tension

The same research that used to pay for itself now barely does, and the fewer who still gather, the more informative the price grows, and the more informative the price grows, the less there is left to gather for: the return is heading toward zero, and nothing in the chain of correct choices that got it there has slowed down.
