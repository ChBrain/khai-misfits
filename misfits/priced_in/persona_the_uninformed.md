# Persona: The Uninformed

## Taxonomy

[Uninformed](position_uninformed.md). The trader who does not pay to find out what a thing is worth, reading the answer off the price instead, a choice that is correct for each and that removes the demand for the finding-out.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sensible, thrifty, unbothered.
Is the trader who declines the research and trusts the number: the price already carries what the informed have found, so reading it off costs nothing and paying to redo the work looks like waste.
No laziness in it: the answer really is in the price, and taking it from there rather than paying again for it is the rational move.

## Action

Reads [the Price](piece_the_price.md) on [Read the Price](plan_read_the_price.md), through [Reading](process_reading.md) at [the Tape](place_the_tape.md), taking [the Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md).
Cannot be faulted for it: the finding is already public, revealed for nothing the moment the informed traded, so there is no reason to pay the cost again for an answer the number already shows.
Does what a rational reader does with a price that reveals: it reads, and pays nothing, and is right to.

## Shadow

Not a free-loader in bad faith and not a thief: the Uninformed takes only what the price freely offers, and its refusal to pay is exactly the correct response to a number that already tells it what it would have learned.
Because every free read is a reward the informed do not get, the Uninformed's rational thrift is what thins the return to gathering, so the more sensibly it and its like read the price rather than pay, the less anyone is paid to put anything into the price, and the answer it reads off grows steadily emptier behind the number.
It does nothing wrong and takes something real, and the taking, summed, is the trap.

## Tell

Says it is all priced in, and means it, and is not wrong that the price says so.
Cannot see that its own sensible refusal to pay, multiplied across everyone who reads rather than researches, is what leaves the price with less and less behind it to read.
