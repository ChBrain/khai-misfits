# Process: Revealing

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). Run by [The Market](persona_the_market.md) on [Reflect the Trades](plan_reflect_the_trades.md), at [The Floor](place_the_floor.md) and [The Tape](place_the_tape.md), in [The Reveal](plot_the_reveal.md). The core mechanism of the misfit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Market, the moment The Informed's order crosses The Floor and enters the print alongside everyone else's.

## Direction

Toward the tape, folding a trade shaped by gathered information into a public price no different in appearance from one shaped by noise.

## Lever

The price moves with the trades that cross it, and because informed orders are mixed with [The Noise Trader](persona_the_noise_trader.md)'s, the movement partly, never fully, reveals what The Informed paid to learn (Grossman and Stiglitz).

## Echo

A price on The Tape carrying, for free, part of what was bought at [The Desk](place_the_desk.md), available to any reader who did not pay for it.
