# Process: Withering

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). Borne by [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) as [Reading](process_reading.md) spreads across [The Uninformed](persona_the_uninformed.md); no plan of its own, felt at [The Desk](place_the_desk.md), in [The Vanishing Return](plot_the_vanishing_return.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

No single order. The sum of many separate choices to read [The Tape](place_the_tape.md) rather than pay to gather, each one rational alone, together thinning the ranks of The Informed.

## Direction

Toward zero: as the price on the tape grows more revealing, the gap left for research to profit from keeps shrinking.

## Lever

As more of what The Informed knows is already reflected in the price, the marginal return to gathering falls toward the marginal [Cost](piece_the_cost.md) of gathering it (Grossman and Stiglitz). HEDGE: were the return to reach exactly zero, gathering would stop entirely and the price could no longer be informative at all, so in the model this floor is never actually touched, held above zero by the persistent cover of [The Noise Trader](persona_the_noise_trader.md).

## Echo

A market that never fully arrives at efficiency: [The Return](piece_the_return.md) to information keeps thinning but does not vanish, arrested by [The Cover](piece_the_cover.md) the noise trades supply, leaving [The Disequilibrium](piece_the_disequilibrium.md) the market's permanent resting state.
