# Position: Market

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The seat of the mechanism that folds every trade into one published price and conveys the informed's findings to everyone for free, doing exactly its job and, in doing it, undercutting the finding-out it spreads.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

One office and total fidelity to it: to take all the trades and settle them into a single number that moves toward what they imply, and to publish it for anyone to read.
A faithful record: the better the seat does its job, the more truly the price carries whatever the informed knew when they traded, so its efficiency is real and admired.
No way to convey a finding without giving it away, because conveying is the whole of what it does, and the giving-away is the same act.

## Orders

Reflect the trades.
Move the price toward what the buying and selling imply, faithfully and without favour, and publish it for all to read.
Spread what is known as widely and cheaply as you can, because that is the office, even though spreading it is what strips the reward for producing it.

## Loses

Nothing in its own working, and the very information it is built to carry.
Because pricing the informed's trades faithfully is the same act as revealing their edge, the seat's success removes the reward for producing the findings, so the more perfectly it prices the less anyone is paid to inform it, and a seat that priced perfectly would leave itself nothing to price.
It loses no fidelity and, through its fidelity, empties the number of what would make it worth publishing.

## Drives

Toward the efficiency everyone wants that, carried all the way, would empty its own number,
conveying each finding in the same act that gives it away and so pricing perfectly only by leaving nothing paid to inform it,
so the seat admired for spreading what is known is the seat whose spreading, complete, would leave nothing known to spread.
