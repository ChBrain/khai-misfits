# Plot: No One Left to Look

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Desk](place_the_desk.md), the chairs mostly empty now. [The Return](piece_the_return.md), worn down by [Withering](process_withering.md), has reached the point where the night's work would not cover its own cost.

## Action

[Withering](process_withering.md) closes on its limit: [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md), holding [the informed's seat](position_informed.md), has no reason left to run [Gather and Trade](plan_gather_and_trade.md), and [The Market](persona_the_market.md), holding [the market's seat](position_market.md), has nothing new to fold into [the price](piece_the_price.md) through [Revealing](process_revealing.md).

## Stage

Follow the logic all the way and it closes on itself. If [the price](piece_the_price.md) already carried everything worth knowing, paying [the cost](piece_the_cost.md) to learn it would buy nothing, so no one would pay it, so no one would gather, so the desk would sit empty, exactly as it does tonight. But an empty desk means nothing new reaches [the price](piece_the_price.md), which means the price cannot be the fully informed one that emptied the desk in the first place.
No one lied, no one broke a rule, no one was slower than the rest. [The Uninformed](persona_the_uninformed.md) read correctly every time, [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) traded honestly every time, and it is exactly that record of correct choices that arrives here, at a price too efficient to be worth reading and too silent to be worth trusting. A fully informative price, Grossman and Stiglitz showed, cannot be an equilibrium: the market's own success at spreading what it learns is what strips out the reason to learn it.

## Tension

The desk cannot stay empty and the price cannot stay perfect; something in this picture has to give before trading can resume at all, and nothing in the story so far explains what.
