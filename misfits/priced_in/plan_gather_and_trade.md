# Plan: Gather and Trade

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the informed's seat](position_informed.md), inside [Priced In](play_priced_in.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Informed](position_informed.md)

## Direction

Pay the cost to find out what the market has not yet priced, then trade on the finding to earn the gap between what the number shows and what is true, before the number moves. Gather only while it pays: the cost is real and the edge leaks the instant it is used, so where the price reveals too quickly, stop paying for research the price will give away for free.

## Orders

([The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) pays [the Cost](piece_the_cost.md) through [Gathering](process_gathering.md) at [the Desk](place_the_desk.md), then trades so [the Market](persona_the_market.md) moves [the Price](piece_the_price.md), the edge leaking through [Revealing](process_revealing.md).)

## Implementation

([The Desk](place_the_desk.md) is where the cost is paid to learn what the price does not show; the finding is used at [the Floor](place_the_floor.md), where trading on it moves the number and, through [Revealing](process_revealing.md), hands it to everyone, so [the Return](piece_the_return.md) lives only in the gap before the price catches up and thins as the price reveals faster.)

## Targets

- [ ] the cost is paid to learn what the market has not yet priced
- [ ] the finding is traded on to earn the gap between belief and price, before the number moves
- [ ] the edge is understood to leak into the price the instant it is used, kept only until the number catches up
- [ ] gathering continues only while the return covers the cost, and stops where the price reveals too quickly
