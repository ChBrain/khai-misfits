# Process: Gathering

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). Run by [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) on [Gather and Trade](plan_gather_and_trade.md), at [The Desk](place_the_desk.md), in [The Edge](plot_the_edge.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Informed, at The Desk, when the expected gain from finding out still exceeds [The Cost](piece_the_cost.md) of finding out.

## Direction

Toward a private estimate of what the asset is really worth, held before any of it has reached [The Floor](place_the_floor.md) or shown up on [The Tape](place_the_tape.md).

## Lever

Paying for research is worthwhile only while the price has not yet caught up to what the research would show (Grossman and Stiglitz).

## Echo

A purchased estimate, still private, about to start informing the price the moment The Informed trades on it.
