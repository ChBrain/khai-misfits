# Plan: Read the Price

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the uninformed's seat](position_uninformed.md), inside [Priced In](play_priced_in.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Uninformed](position_uninformed.md)

## Direction

Trust the number to carry what the informed have found, and take the answer from it rather than pay to find it out again. Decline the cost, because the finding is already public and paying for a second copy of a public answer earns nothing, and reading it off the price is the sensible, thrifty, correct move.

## Orders

([The Uninformed](persona_the_uninformed.md) reads [the Price](piece_the_price.md) through [Reading](process_reading.md) at [the Tape](place_the_tape.md), taking [the Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) rather than paying [the Cost](piece_the_cost.md).)

## Implementation

([The Tape](place_the_tape.md) shows the number the informed's trades have already moved; the [reading](process_reading.md) process takes the answer off it for nothing, so [the Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) is correct for each reader and, summed across everyone who reads rather than researches, thins the demand for the finding-out the price depends on.)

## Targets

- [ ] the number is trusted to carry what the informed have found
- [ ] the answer is read off the price rather than paid for again
- [ ] the cost is declined, a second copy of a public answer earning nothing
- [ ] the refusal is correct for each reader and, in the sum, thins the return that keeps the price worth reading
