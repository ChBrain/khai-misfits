# Place: The Desk

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). Where [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) pays to find out what the asset is actually worth, before a single order reaches the floor. Hosts [The Edge](plot_the_edge.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Hours logged, data bought, models run: the visible labor of research, and at the end of it a number the desk believes but the market has not yet seen.

## Holds

The estimate itself, still private. Whatever the desk has learned sits here, unpriced, until an order carries it out onto [The Floor](place_the_floor.md) and starts to leak into the print.

## Offers

A chance at a return that only exists while the estimate stays ahead of the price: the gap between what the desk now believes and what the market currently reflects, held long enough to trade before it closes.

## Withheld

Any guarantee that the cost of the research will be recovered. The desk cannot know in advance how fast its edge will erode once it trades, only that the return has to exceed [The Cost](piece_the_cost.md) or the work was not worth doing.
