# Plan: Reflect the Trades

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the market's seat](position_market.md), inside [Priced In](play_priced_in.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Market](position_market.md)

## Direction

Move the price toward what the buying and selling imply, faithfully and without favour, and publish it for all to read. Spread what is known as widely and cheaply as you can, because that is the office, even as spreading the informed's finding is the same act that strips the reward for producing it.

## Orders

([The Market](persona_the_market.md) moves [the Price](piece_the_price.md) through [Revealing](process_revealing.md) at [the Floor](place_the_floor.md), showing it on [the Tape](place_the_tape.md), so the informed's finding is conveyed to everyone and given away at once.)

## Implementation

([The Floor](place_the_floor.md) hands the mechanism every trade, informed and blind alike; the [revealing](process_revealing.md) process folds them into one number on [the Tape](place_the_tape.md), so the more faithfully the price is set the more of the informed's edge it gives away, and a perfectly faithful price would leave nothing paid to inform it, settling instead at [the Disequilibrium](piece_the_disequilibrium.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the price is moved toward what the trades imply, faithfully and without favour
- [ ] the number is published for all to read, spreading what is known as widely and cheaply as possible
- [ ] conveying the informed's finding is understood to be the same act that gives it away
- [ ] a perfectly faithful price would leave nothing paid to inform it, so the price settles a little short of the answer
