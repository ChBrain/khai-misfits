# Position: Noise Trader

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The seat of the trader who buys and sells for reasons unrelated to value, whose trades are the noise the informed hide in, and who on average loses to them and, in losing, funds the market's efficiency.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Reasons of its own to trade, none of them about the number: cash to raise, a rule to follow, a habit, a whim, orders that arrive for their own sake.
A real place: a market needs many such traders to have anyone to trade with, so the seat's value-blind flow is not waste but the liquidity the whole floor runs on.
No claim on being right about worth, because it is not trying to be, so it carries no edge and expects none.

## Orders

Trade blind.
Buy and sell for whatever reason you actually have, cash, rule, habit, whim, and pay no mind to what the thing is truly worth.
Supply the flow the market needs, trading for your own sake and not to price anything, indistinguishable at the tape from everyone else.

## Loses

On average, to the informed, and does not know it is the one paying.
Because the informed earn precisely on the traders who trade without knowing the value, and because the seat's noise is the cover the informed hide in, the seat is the source of the return that keeps anyone gathering, so the market's efficiency is drawn, in the end, from its losses.
It loses quietly and steadily, and its losses are what the whole arrangement is paid with.

## Drives

Toward the value-blind trader who funds the finding-out and is out of pocket for it,
its untargeted trades the cover without which the informed's every move would be read at once and their edge erased,
so the seat that is not trying to be right is the seat whose losses buy the market whatever rightness it has.
