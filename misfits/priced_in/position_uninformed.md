# Position: Uninformed

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The seat of the trader who does not pay to find out what a thing is worth, reading the answer off the price instead, a refusal correct for each that removes the demand for the finding-out.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A free reading of the answer: the price already carries what the informed found, so the seat can take it from the number at no cost.
A sound claim: redoing research whose result is already public would be waste, so reading it off the price rather than paying again is the rational move.
No stake in producing the finding, because it can be had for nothing, so the seat carries no reason of its own to pay the cost.

## Orders

Read the price.
Trust the number to carry what the informed have found, and take the answer from it rather than pay to find it out again.
Decline the cost, because the finding is already public and paying for a second copy of a public answer earns nothing.

## Loses

Nothing it can see, and a little of what the price is worth reading for.
Because its free reading is a reward the informed do not get, the seat's rational thrift thins the return to gathering, so each correct refusal to pay, summed across everyone who reads rather than researches, leaves the price with less behind it.
It loses nothing in the moment and helps empty, over the rounds, the very number it reads.

## Drives

Toward the rational reader whose thrift is corrosive in the sum,
taking for free an answer someone paid to place and, in taking it, thinning the reason to place any more,
so the seat that does nothing wrong is the seat whose correct refusals leave the price steadily emptier behind the number.
