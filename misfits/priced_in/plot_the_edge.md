# Plot: The Edge

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Desk](place_the_desk.md), before the open. A filing, a rumor, a set of numbers nobody has yet reconciled: something knowable but not yet known, and known only to whoever pays to find it out.

## Action

[Gathering](process_gathering.md): [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md), holding [the informed's seat](position_informed.md), runs [Gather and Trade](plan_gather_and_trade.md), spending [the cost](piece_the_cost.md) to convert the unknown into a position.

## Stage

[The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) works [the desk](place_the_desk.md) late: calls made, filings read, a model rebuilt until the number it returns is worth more than what it took to build it. Nothing here trades yet, so nothing here is public; the knowledge sits in one head, purchased at [the cost](piece_the_cost.md) and owed a return for the sitting.
It is a small, local, unglamorous fact: research is not free, and someone has to front for it before any price can move on what they learn.

## Tension

The informed now knows something the market's published price does not yet show, and the only way to collect on that gap is to trade, which is the one act that will start giving the knowledge away.
