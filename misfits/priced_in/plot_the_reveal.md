# Plot: The Reveal

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Floor](place_the_floor.md), the open. [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) steps in and places the order that [The Edge](plot_the_edge.md) bought.

## Action

[Revealing](process_revealing.md): [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md), holding [the informed's seat](position_informed.md), trades on [Gather and Trade](plan_gather_and_trade.md), and [The Market](persona_the_market.md), holding [the market's seat](position_market.md), runs [Reflect the Trades](plan_reflect_the_trades.md), folding that order into [the price](piece_the_price.md).

## Stage

The order lands on [the floor](place_the_floor.md) and the market does what a market does: it clears trades and prints a number. That number is not the informed's secret, but it is not silent about it either; a price that moves carries the direction, and often the size, of whatever moved it.
By the time [the tape](place_the_tape.md) shows the new print, the information the informed paid to gather is sitting in public view, available to anyone watching, at no charge to them at all.

## Tension

What cost [the informed](persona_the_informed.md) real money to learn now reaches every reader of [the tape](place_the_tape.md) for free, the instant it is printed: the same act that lets the informed collect on its research is the act that gives the research away.
