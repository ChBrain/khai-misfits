# Piece: The Cover

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The noise that keeps [The Price](piece_the_price.md) from fully revealing, letting [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) hide their trades and profit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Provided by [The Noise Trader](persona_the_noise_trader.md) on [Trade Blind](plan_trade_blind.md), orders placed at [The Floor](place_the_floor.md) for reasons unrelated to value, mixed into the same print as the informed trades on [The Tape](place_the_tape.md).

## Load Bearing

The Cover is what makes the whole arrangement possible at all: without random supply or noise trading to blend into, an informed trade would announce itself and [The Price](piece_the_price.md) would move to absorb it entirely before any return could be captured (Grossman and Stiglitz). It must persist, not as an unfortunate defect but as a structural requirement, because a market with no noise has no way to pay its informed traders and so no way to stay informed at all. Guard against treating the Cover as inefficiency to be engineered away; removing it would remove [The Return](piece_the_return.md) along with it, and with it the information the price depends on.

## Apparent

It looks like waste, trades that add nothing but static to the tape and cost the traders who place them. That reading is accurate about the cost, the noise traders do lose to the informed on average, but it misses that this loss is the price the system pays to keep any information flowing into the market at all.

## Yearbook

Supplied through [Trade Blind](plan_trade_blind.md) by [The Noise Trader](persona_the_noise_trader.md) at [The Floor](place_the_floor.md); it is what holds [The Return](piece_the_return.md) above the zero that [Withering](process_withering.md) would otherwise reach, and by keeping [The Price](piece_the_price.md) short of fully revealing it is what sustains [The Disequilibrium](piece_the_disequilibrium.md) as a standing state rather than a passing one.
