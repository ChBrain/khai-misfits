# Persona: The Informed

## Taxonomy

[Informed](position_informed.md). The trader who pays the cost to find out what a thing is worth and trades on it, moving the price and so leaking the finding into it for free, and who keeps gathering only while the return covers the cost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Diligent, early, quietly ahead.
Is the trader who does the work: pays for the research, learns what the market has not yet priced, and trades ahead of the number to earn on the gap between what it shows and what is true.
No trick in it: the edge is real and honestly won by paying a cost others did not, and it is only worth paying while there is a gap left to trade on.

## Action

Pays [the Cost](piece_the_cost.md) on [Gather and Trade](plan_gather_and_trade.md), through [Gathering](process_gathering.md) at [the Desk](place_the_desk.md), and trades on the edge so [the Market](persona_the_market.md) moves [the Price](piece_the_price.md).
Cannot keep the edge it uses: the instant it trades, the finding leaks into the price through [Revealing](process_revealing.md) and shows on [the Tape](place_the_tape.md), so the very act that pays for the research is the act that gives it away.
Does what a researcher does with a costly edge: it spends the edge to earn, and in spending it hands it to everyone, keeping only what it made before the number caught up.

## Shadow

Not a manipulator and not a mark: the Informed earns honestly on work it paid for, and asks only to be paid enough to keep paying for it.
Because its finding leaks into the price the moment it trades, and because the better the price reveals the less it can earn, the Informed's own success feeds the number that undercuts it, so as more read the price off [the Tape](place_the_tape.md) and fewer do the work, [the Return](piece_the_return.md) it needs shrinks toward nothing, and the diligence that fills the price with meaning is the diligence the filled price stops rewarding.
It is the one who makes the number worth reading, and the one the reading quietly retires.

## Tell

Says the research still pays, for now, and watches the margin on it thin as the price grows quicker to reflect what it finds.
Cannot go on paying the cost once the edge is gone, so where the price reveals too well it simply stops gathering, and takes with it the finding the price needed to hold.
