# Plot: The Necessary Noise

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Floor](place_the_floor.md), reopened. Orders arrive that answer to no filing and no model: a fund rebalancing, a household selling to cover a bill, a position closed for reasons that have nothing to do with what anything is worth.

## Action

[The Noise Trader](persona_the_noise_trader.md), holding [the noise trader's seat](position_noise_trader.md), runs [Trade Blind](plan_trade_blind.md), and its orders give [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) [the cover](piece_the_cover.md) to trade without being read outright.

## Stage

Because [the noise trader](persona_the_noise_trader.md)'s orders move [the price](piece_the_price.md) too, no one on [the tape](place_the_tape.md) can tell, order by order, whether a print carries real information or none at all. That cover is what [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) trades inside of: mixed in with the blind flow, its edge survives long enough at each print to be worth having paid [the cost](piece_the_cost.md) for, so [the return](piece_the_return.md) settles above zero instead of withering to nothing.
The market lands, and stays, at [the disequilibrium](piece_the_disequilibrium.md): a price informative enough that [The Uninformed](persona_the_uninformed.md) is right to read it, and noisy enough that [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) is still paid to gather what it reads. Grossman and Stiglitz named this the resting point itself, not a flaw in it: no arrangement gives a market both a fully efficient price and a standing reason to keep informing it, so the market instead holds a degree of disequilibrium, permanently short of perfect and permanently short of blind.

## Tension

The noise traders who supply [the cover](piece_the_cover.md) are, on average, the ones who pay for it: they trade against better information more often than they trade against worse, and lose accordingly, an ordinary cost that never shows up on anyone's tape as a line item. Whatever efficiency the price does reach, someone financed by losing, and an analyst who reads that price and calls it all priced in has simply stopped being one of the people keeping it that informed.
