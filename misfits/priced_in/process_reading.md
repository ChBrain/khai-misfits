# Process: Reading

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). Run by [The Uninformed](persona_the_uninformed.md) on [Read the Price](plan_read_the_price.md), at [The Tape](place_the_tape.md), in [Off the Price](plot_off_the_price.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Uninformed, checking the tape instead of commissioning research, because the tape already carries part of what the research would find.

## Direction

Toward a belief updated at no cost, standing in for the estimate The Informed paid to build at [The Desk](place_the_desk.md).

## Lever

Reading a partly revealing price is rational once its marginal advantage over guessing exceeds the marginal advantage of paying [The Cost](piece_the_cost.md) to gather firsthand (Grossman and Stiglitz).

## Echo

An inference formed for free, [The Free Ride](piece_the_free_ride.md) taken, and one fewer trader in the market with any reason to pay for information of its own.
