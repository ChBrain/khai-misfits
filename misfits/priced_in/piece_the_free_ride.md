# Piece: The Free Ride

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The uninformed's rational choice to read the answer off [The Price](piece_the_price.md) rather than pay to gather it themselves.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Taken by [The Uninformed](persona_the_uninformed.md) on [Read the Price](plan_read_the_price.md), through [Reading](process_reading.md), at [The Tape](place_the_tape.md): the print is checked, not researched.

## Load Bearing

The Free Ride is the correct move, not a shortcut taken out of laziness: when the price already carries a trace of what the informed learned, paying [The Cost](piece_the_cost.md) again to learn it independently is throwing money at an answer already visible for free (Grossman and Stiglitz). It is load bearing because it is what removes the demand side of information: every uninformed trader who reads instead of gathers is one less buyer for research, and the choice is rational for each of them taken alone. Guard against casting the free rider as a freeloader; the same logic would bind The Informed too, if the price ever became fully revealing.

## Apparent

It looks like laziness or a shortcut, a trader skipping homework that a diligent one would do. That reading misses the arithmetic underneath it: reading the tape is not cutting a corner, it is optimizing against a price that has already made the corner disappear.

## Yearbook

Taken through [Reading](process_reading.md) on [Read the Price](plan_read_the_price.md) at [The Tape](place_the_tape.md); depends entirely on [The Price](piece_the_price.md) carrying enough of a trace to be worth reading, and by declining [The Cost](piece_the_cost.md) it is the choice that starts driving [The Return](piece_the_return.md) toward [Withering](process_withering.md).
