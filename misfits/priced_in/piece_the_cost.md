# Piece: The Cost

## Taxonomy

[Priced In](play_priced_in.md). The real expense of gathering information: the research and analysis that must be paid for before [The Price](piece_the_price.md) can carry any of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Paid by [The Informed](persona_the_informed.md) on [Gather and Trade](plan_gather_and_trade.md), at [The Desk](place_the_desk.md), through [Gathering](process_gathering.md): time, data, and analysis spent before a single order reaches [The Floor](place_the_floor.md).

## Load Bearing

The Cost is what makes information a produced good rather than a free fact lying in the world: someone has to pay it before the market can have anything true to reveal (Grossman and Stiglitz). It is the reason the paradox bites at all, because if learning were free, the price's giving it away for nothing would cost nobody anything, and there would be no incentive to erode. Guard against treating the Cost as a nuisance the informed simply absorb; it is the standing bill that [The Return](piece_the_return.md) must clear or the gathering stops.

## Apparent

It looks like overhead, a line item the well-resourced simply pay and the rest skip. That reading hides the hinge: the Cost is not incidental to the system, it is the very thing whose payment the market needs and whose payment the market's own efficiency works to make unrewarding.
