# On Good Authority: Reference

The research warrant for **On Good Authority**. The title is a reading of the phenomenon named the
**Woozle effect**, or evidence by citation: a claim gains the look of established evidence not from
data but from being cited and re-cited, each author trusting a prior source in good faith, so a weak
or unsupported assertion accretes a dense network of references until it reads as settled fact, and
belief in it is manufactured by the repetition of the citation rather than by any accumulation of
evidence, all cited below, held with the finding that this is a mechanism attested in case analyses
of specific literatures rather than a measured rate at which literatures distort.

## Line of Work

Staging the Woozle effect as a **citation-manufactures-authority structure**: a claim is made once, on thin or absent
evidence, a single small study, a speculation, a figure with a decimal in the wrong place. Someone cites it, in good
faith, because a working scholar cannot re-derive every upstream claim and must at some point trust a cited source as
the ordinary practice of the field allows. Then a second author cites the first, and a review cites the second as
established, and a model builds on the review, and each of these is a reasonable act: to cite a prior source is to stand
on the record, and to summarize a body of work in a review is to compress it, and neither citer is positioned to walk
the whole chain back to the root. But as the references accrete, the claim takes on a standing the data never gave it,
because belief tracks the density of citations and not the weight of evidence, and past a point the sheer count of
references reads as many confirmations when it is only many echoes of the one source (Greenberg). Worse, the network does
not merely repeat but distorts: authors cite the supportive over the critical, reviews report a tentative claim as
settled, and now and then a paper is cited for a claim it never made, so the record hardens around an assertion that no
single study established (Greenberg). And the name is older than the diagnosis and honest about the shape of it: the
Woozle is Milne's, the creature Pooh and Piglet track round and round a spinney by following their own footprints,
growing surer a whole pack walks ahead as the ring of tracks deepens, which is the trap exactly, a circle of one's own
making mistaken for a crowd (Milne; and the naming of the effect in the family-violence literature, Houghton; Gelles and
Straus). It is authored by no villain: no one fabricates, and each citation is an ordinary and defensible act of
scholarship, an author trusting a source as the practice of citing allows and as re-verifying every upstream claim would
make the work impossible. The reviewer who calls a claim established is compressing honestly, and the reader who takes a
much-cited claim as sound is reading the record as it is meant to be read. The governing law is not that anyone lies or
is lazy, but that **a claim cited and re-cited in good faith accretes an authority the evidence never gave it, because
belief tracks the count of citations and not the weight of data, so a weak assertion can be repeated into fact with no
villain and no fabrication**. The play models the documented mechanics: the thin original claim, the good-faith citation
that stands on the record, the accretion of references where the data does not grow, the amplification and invention
that harden the record, the manufactured authority the claim now carries, and the trace-back to the root that alone
exposes it and is rarely walked (Greenberg; Rekdal; Simkin and Roychowdhury), as the load-bearing vectors of the
structure, not as a tale of a fraud dressed up as a fact.

It is a citation-manufactures-authority structure no staged misfit holds, and it is carefully distinct from its nearest
neighbours. It is distinct from The File Drawer, where null results go unpublished so the printed record over-represents
the positive: there the distortion is in which studies reach print, and a claim can be false because its disconfirmations
were never seen, whereas here every study may be published and honest and the distortion is in how the existing claims
are cited and amplified, a claim woozled by its citations and not by a hidden drawer of nulls. It is distinct from
Information Cascade, where each actor copies the visible choice of those ahead and rationally discards its own private
signal in a live sequential decision: there the copying is a real-time choice and a signal is withheld, whereas here each
scholar cites openly and withholds nothing, and the accretion builds in a written record over time, so what looks like
many independent confirmations is many honest re-citations of one source. And it is distinct from Too Sure to Be True,
where genuinely independent judgments agree so perfectly that the agreement itself indicts a shared hidden bias: there
the observers are independent and their improbable concord is the tell, whereas here the references are not independent
at all but echoes of a single upstream claim, so the many citations are not confirmations whose agreement is suspicious
but repetitions that were never confirmations to begin with.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast any author as a fraud or the
  citing as a deceit, because the source finding it stages is that the trap needs no fabrication: a scholar cannot
  re-derive every upstream claim and must trust a cited source as the ordinary practice of the field allows, a review
  compresses a body of work honestly, and a reader takes a much-cited claim as sound because that is how the record is
  meant to be read. No one lies, and each citation is a defensible act. The authority is authored by no one: it accretes
  from the aggregate of individually reasonable citations, and the misfit is that a claim repeated in good faith can carry
  a weight the evidence never gave it.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. There is a way to expose a woozled claim,
  to walk the citation chain back to the root and find the data was never there, as the citation-network dissection does
  (Greenberg), but it is slow, thankless, and rarely done, since to trace one claim to its source is a research project in
  itself and the practice of citing exists precisely so that no one must. The escape asks a reader to distrust the very
  signal, a dense record of citations, that ordinarily marks a claim as sound, and it does not scale to a literature in
  which thousands of claims each rest on chains no one has walked.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed as the
  house's. The staging is the house's; the citation-network dissection and the account of citation bias, amplification,
  and invention are Greenberg's, the Woozle image is Milne's, the naming of the effect in research method is Houghton's
  and Gelles and Straus's, the documented academic-urban-legend cases are Rekdal's, and the citation-copying estimate is
  Simkin and Roychowdhury's. The mechanism is carried as carefully as the finding: this is a mechanism attested in case
  analyses of specific literatures and in documented instances, not a measured rate at which literatures distort, so the
  house stages the structure, that a claim can be cited into authority with no villain, and holds that not every
  much-cited claim is a woozle and that a dense citation record is more often earned than manufactured, the effect a real
  and demonstrated hazard and not a licence to distrust every well-cited fact.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per element, one per load-bearing vector, no filler.

- **The Claim**: carries the origin, the single thin or unsupported assertion, the small study or speculation or
  misplaced decimal that will be cited into fact, the root of the chain (Greenberg; Rekdal).
- **The Citation**: carries the good-faith act (Greenberg; Simkin and Roychowdhury), the author trusting a prior source
  as the practice of citing allows, standing on the record because re-deriving every upstream claim would make the work
  impossible.
- **The Accretion**: carries the mechanism (Greenberg), the growing network of references where the data does not grow,
  the count of citations read as many confirmations when it is only many echoes of the one source.
- **The Amplification**: carries the distortion (Greenberg), the review that reports a tentative claim as established and
  the paper cited for a claim it never made, the citation bias and invention that harden the record around the assertion.
- **The Authority**: carries the harm (Greenberg; Houghton; Gelles and Straus), the manufactured standing, the weak claim
  now reading as settled fact on the weight of its citations, drawn distinct from The File Drawer, Information Cascade, and
  Too Sure to Be True.
- **The Trace-Back**: carries the remedy and the bound (Greenberg; Rekdal), the walking of the citation chain to its root
  that alone exposes the empty claim, costly and thankless and rarely done, and the marker of the hedge, since the effect
  is a mechanism attested in cases and not a rate, and a dense citation record is more often earned than manufactured.
