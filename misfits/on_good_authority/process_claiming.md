# Process: Claiming

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). Borne at [the Literature](place_the_literature.md), precipitated by [the Source](persona_the_source.md), in [Said Once](plot_said_once.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Source, publishing an honest result with its qualifications intact: a small study reported as small, a speculation offered as a speculation.

## Direction

From the honest result toward the modest claim set on the record.

## Lever

To say a result once, with its hedges attached, is to enter an assertion no larger than the evidence, the root a later citing will build on and, in time, strip of the very qualifications that made it honest (Greenberg).
