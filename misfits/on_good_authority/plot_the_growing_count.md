# Plot: The Growing Count

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The first citation made, and others arriving behind it, each one reasonable on its own.

## Action

At [the Literature](place_the_literature.md), [the Citer](persona_the_citer.md) from [the citer's seat](position_citer.md), through [Citing](process_citing.md), meets [the Accretion](piece_the_accretion.md), the network of references thickening around the claim where the data behind it does not.

## Stage

A second citer cites the first citation rather than the original study, and a review cites the second, and each of these, taken alone, is the same ordinary act of standing on the record that made the first citation reasonable. But none of them is placed to see the shape the aggregate is taking, and what accretes is a count of references growing while the underlying data stays exactly as thin as it was at the root. Past a point the sheer number of citations reads as many confirmations, when a citation-network dissection of one such claim shows it is only many echoes of the one source, footnote citing footnote back to a single study (Greenberg). The picture is Milne's before it was method: the Woozle that Pooh and Piglet track round and round a spinney, growing surer a whole pack walks ahead as the ring of their own footprints widens, a circle of their own making mistaken for a crowd (Milne). No villain: each citer here cites in good faith, standing on a source the way the practice allows, and not one of them holds a vantage from which the network's shape, or its distance from the data, is visible.

## Tension

The count of references climbs with every reasonable citation.
The data beneath it has not moved at all, and nothing in the growing count says so.
