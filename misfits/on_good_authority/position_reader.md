# Position: Reader

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The seat of the one who takes the much-cited claim as fact, and marks that reading the record by its citations is the sound and ordinary practice, not a credulity.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A claim it needs to use and no time to chase every fact to its primary source.
The density of citations before it, the record's own mark that a claim has been checked and held by others.
No way from where it reads to tell a hundred confirmations from a hundred echoes of one source.

## Orders

Take it as read, and credit the claim the whole field cites.
Believe a claim reported settled in the reviews and cited across the literature, since the density of citations is the ordinary signal that others have checked it, and a reader cannot re-verify every fact it uses.
Know that this sound reading is exactly what the woozle exploits, that the count you trust as confirmations may be echoes, and that nothing in the record from where you stand distinguishes an earned authority from a manufactured one.

## Loses

The truth it meant to take on, though it read the record rightly.
Because the density of citations is the record's own mark of soundness and a reader must trust it to use the record at all, the seat credits a claim built by repetition and takes a manufactured authority for an earned one, deceived by no one and reading exactly as it should.
It keeps its sound reading and loses no cause for blame, the reader who trusted the fact the whole field cited.

## Drives

Toward the one who reads the record rightly and is misled by it,
crediting a densely cited claim as any careful reader should, unable to tell confirmations from echoes,
so the seat that trusts the count because the count is the record's mark of soundness is the seat the woozle deceives, believing on good authority that was never the evidence.
