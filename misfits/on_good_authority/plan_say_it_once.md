# Plan: Say It Once

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the source's seat](position_source.md), inside [On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Source](position_source.md)

## Direction

Set down the result with its qualifications, the small study as small and the speculation as speculation, so the claim on the page is honest and no larger than its ground. Know that the hedges are the first thing dropped in the citing, that a careful maybe is cited as a settled yes, and that the authority built on the claim will be built on a version you did not write.

## Orders

([The Source](persona_the_source.md) holds [the Claim](piece_the_claim.md) through [Claiming](process_claiming.md) at [the Literature](place_the_literature.md), stating the thin result plainly with its qualifications, claiming no more than the evidence allows.)

## Implementation

([Claiming](process_claiming.md) at [the Literature](place_the_literature.md) holds [the Claim](piece_the_claim.md), because to say a result once with its hedges is to put an honest and modest assertion on the record, the root the later citing will build on and strip.)

## Targets

- [ ] the result is stated once, plainly, with its qualifications attached
- [ ] the claim is held no larger than the evidence allows
- [ ] it is seen that the hedges are the first thing dropped in the retelling
- [ ] it is owned that the source cannot govern what the citing makes of the claim
