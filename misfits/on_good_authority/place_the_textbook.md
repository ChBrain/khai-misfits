# Place: The Textbook

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). Where the claim has hardened into received fact, taken as given by a reader who has no reason to look for its origins. Hosts [Taken as Read](plot_taken_as_read.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A page of settled knowledge, the claim stated plainly, a reference number its only visible trace, the long chain of citing that produced it nowhere in view.

## Holds

[The Authority](piece_the_authority.md).
The claim as it is now held: not argued afresh here but simply known, its standing inherited from a record this page does not show.

## Offers

To the Reader, a claim ready to build on, sparing the labour of tracing every fact taught here back to its origin, the same trust in the record that let the citations accrete in the first place; a dense citation is, in the ordinary run of scholarship, exactly the mark of a claim worth crediting (Greenberg).

## Withheld

Sight of the chain behind the claim. The textbook does not show whether what stands behind a fact is many independent studies or one study cited and re-cited, so it withholds the one distinction that would tell an earned authority from a manufactured one, and leaves the reader nothing to read but the confidence of the page.
