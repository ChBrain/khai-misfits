# Plot: Walked Back

## Taxonomy
[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md).

## Owner
- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue
A doubt, small and specific, and someone willing to follow the references home instead of citing them onward.

## Action
At [the Root](place_the_root.md), [the Tracer](persona_the_tracer.md) from [the tracer's seat](position_tracer.md) on [Walk It Back](plan_walk_it_back.md) through [Tracing](process_tracing.md) takes up [the Trace-Back](piece_the_trace_back.md), following the citation chain past every echo to the primary study at its base.

## Stage
The Tracer sets aside the density of citations that would ordinarily settle the question, and walks the chain back one reference at a time, past the reviews and the citers of citers, to the study the whole record is standing on. What it finds there, as a citation-network dissection of one biomedical claim found, is a primary result smaller than the record built on it, or absent altogether, or saying something the citations above it do not say (Greenberg). Documented cases confirm the chain can run this thin all the way down, a misplaced decimal in an iron measurement, carried by citation through decades of reviews and textbooks until someone finally traced it home (Rekdal). The corrective is slow and thankless, a research project spent to check the one claim that the practice of citing exists so that no one ordinarily has to, and it does not scale to a literature in which every claim rests on a chain nobody has walked. No villain: the field trusted the record exactly as it is built to be trusted, and the Tracer's doubt is warranted here only because this particular chain ran thin, not because every dense citation record is hollow, since most such records are earned rather than manufactured, and this is a mechanism attested in case analyses and documented instances, not a measured rate at which literatures distort (Greenberg; Rekdal).

Distinct from The File Drawer, where the distortion is in which studies reach print, while here the published record is distorted by how existing claims are cited and amplified; from Information Cascade, a live sequential choice in which a private signal is rationally withheld, while here each scholar cites openly and the accretion builds in a written record, so what looks like many independent confirmations is many honest re-citations of one source; and from Too Sure to Be True, where the judgments are genuinely independent and their improbable agreement is the tell, while here the references are not independent at all but echoes of one upstream claim, never confirmations to begin with.

## Tension
The root, once reached, is found thin or empty.
The walking that found it took a season, and a citation that undoes it takes a moment, so the trace-back that alone can tell an earned authority from a manufactured one is too costly to run on more than a doubt at a time.
