# Piece: The Trace-Back

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The mechanism: the citation chain walked to its root, where the primary study turns out small or absent or saying something the citations do not.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Root](place_the_root.md), carried by [Tracing](process_tracing.md), voiced by [the Tracer](persona_the_tracer.md): a table of every reference laid out and followed backward, one by one, past the reviews and past the citing papers, to whatever is or is not actually there at the bottom.

## Load Bearing

This is the corrective, and it is exactly the labour the practice of citing exists to spare everyone else. A citation-network dissection can walk a claim's whole chain back to source and show the many references collapsing onto one thin root, or none (Greenberg); documented cases confirm the method finds real error at the bottom, a misplaced decimal in an iron measurement propagated across decades of citation before someone tracked it home (Rekdal). Most citers, reasonably, never make this trip themselves; that is what makes the trace-back slow, thankless, and rare rather than routine (Simkin and Roychowdhury). It carries the hedge the whole misfit is built to hold: this is a mechanism attested in citation-network case analyses and in documented instances, not a measured rate at which literatures distort, and most authorities in the record are earned rather than manufactured, so the trace-back is a check on a particular claim, never a licence to distrust the record wholesale (Greenberg; Rekdal).
