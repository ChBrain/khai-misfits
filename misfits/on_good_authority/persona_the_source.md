# Persona: The Source

## Taxonomy

[Source](position_source.md), inside [On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The archetype of the one who makes the original claim, a small study or a speculation offered as no more than it is, the modest root of the chain that the citations will build into fact.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A careless author who put a weak claim into the record, as though the whole edifice were its fault, when it claimed no more than its evidence and hedged what it could not show.

## Action

Say it once. At [the Literature](place_the_literature.md), the Source on [Say It Once](plan_say_it_once.md) through [Claiming](process_claiming.md) holds [the Claim](piece_the_claim.md), the single thin assertion made on a small study or a speculation, stated plainly with its qualifications, claiming no more than the evidence allows (Greenberg).

## Shadow

Its claim was honest, and it does not control what is made of it. The Source is not careless: it offered a tentative result as tentative, a small study as small, a speculation flagged as one, and by itself such a claim would sink for want of support or be checked at its root. What happens after is out of its hands, because it cannot govern who cites it or how, and the qualifications it set down are the first thing dropped in the retelling, its careful maybe cited as a settled yes. It keeps its honest modest claim and its hedges intact on the page, and loses its say over the authority the citations will give the claim it never made.

## Tell

It said the thing once, plainly, with its doubts attached, and the doubts were the first thing the citing left behind.
