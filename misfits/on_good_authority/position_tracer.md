# Position: Tracer

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The seat of the one who walks the citation chain to its root, and marks that the trace-back is right and costly and rarely done, and holds even itself to a bound.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A way to test the claim, to follow the references home and read the primary source they rest on.
A clear sight of the chain, the citations leading back toward a root that may be small or absent or say what they do not.
A held bound, the knowledge that the trace-back is warranted where the chain runs thin and not a rule that every well-cited claim is hollow.

## Orders

Walk it back, and read the root the citations rest on.
Follow the chain of references to the primary study and read it, since only the source can tell an authority the evidence earned from one the citations built, and the dissection shows the record can harden around a claim the data never carried (Greenberg; Rekdal).
Know that the work is slow and thankless, a research project to check one fact, that it asks the field to distrust the dense record it ordinarily and rightly trusts, and that your own doubt is bounded, warranted where the chain is thin and not everywhere by rule.

## Loses

The ease the practice of citing was meant to give, spent to check what should not need checking.
Because to trace one claim to its root is a labour the whole practice of citing exists to spare, the seat that walks the references home spends a season to unsettle what a citation made in a moment, and its correction, right as it is, does not scale to a literature of chains no one has walked.
It keeps its right doubt and its held bound and loses no cause for blame, the one who found the root empty at a cost the citing never paid.

## Drives

Toward the one who walks the chain back and pays for the walking,
following the references home to a root the citations rest on, right where it runs thin and bounded where it does not,
so the seat that alone can tell an earned authority from a made one spends more to check the claim than the whole field spent to build it, its correction right and costly and rarely done.
