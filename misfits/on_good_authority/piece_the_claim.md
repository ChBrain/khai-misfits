# Piece: The Claim

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The mechanism: a single thin assertion, made once and hedged, that by itself would sink or be checked, but does not stay by itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Literature](place_the_literature.md), carried by [Claiming](process_claiming.md), voiced by [the Source](persona_the_source.md): one line entered into the record, a small study reported as small, a speculation offered as a speculation, a figure carrying a decimal in the wrong place and no one yet the wiser.

## Load Bearing

This is the root of the chain, and it is honest. A small study, a speculation flagged as one, or a number transcribed with an error not yet caught: the claim states no more than its evidence and hedges what it cannot show (Greenberg). Nothing here is fabricated or overstated; the whole misfit depends on that, because a claim that lied at the root would make every citer after it a dupe rather than a good-faith actor. Documented cases show the root can be this thin: a misplaced decimal in a measurement, carried forward for decades before anyone traced it home (Rekdal). By itself, thin evidence like this would ordinarily sink for want of support, or be checked and corrected at the source. What it needs to become fact instead of a footnote is not more data. It needs to be cited.

## Apparent

Reads already as the settled fact it will become: a claim on the record, ready to be used, its hedges not yet stripped and its thinness not yet hidden by anything.
