## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). Borne at [the Literature](place_the_literature.md), precipitated by [the Citer](persona_the_citer.md), in [Stood On](plot_stood_on.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Citer, reaching for a prior source rather than re-deriving what it found, the ordinary and defensible practice a working scholar depends on.

## Direction

From standing on the record toward the accreting references.

## Lever

To cite a prior source rather than re-derive it is the practice that makes the work possible at all, since most citers reasonably copy a reference without reading it, so each new citation adds to a count that, read back, resembles independent confirmation when it is only the one source echoed forward (Greenberg; Simkin and Roychowdhury).
