# Place: The Root

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). Where the citation chain is followed back to the primary source and found small, absent, or saying something other than what the citations above it claim. Hosts [Walked Back](plot_walked_back.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The original study opened at last: a sample size, a hedge still attached in the original words, a passage that says less than the claim now resting on it, or, now and then, no paper at all where the chain assumed one (Rekdal).

## Holds

[The Trace-Back](piece_the_trace_back.md).
The chain followed link by link to its first link, and the gap measured between what is claimed now and what was actually said there.

## Offers

To the Tracer, the one place the question can be settled directly: what the source itself says, read rather than inferred from how densely it is cited, as a citation-network dissection can show for a single claim (Greenberg; Simkin and Roychowdhury).

## Withheld

Any shortcut. The root cannot be reached by counting references, only by opening the source and reading it, so it withholds the very convenience that let the accretion happen upstream, and offers no comfortable middle ground between having read it and not.
