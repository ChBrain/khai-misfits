# Persona: The Tracer

## Taxonomy

[Tracer](position_tracer.md), inside [On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The archetype of the one who walks the citation chain back to its root, finding the primary study small or absent or saying what the citations do not, the corrective that alone exposes the woozle and is rarely done.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A contrarian who doubts a settled fact out of pique, as though chasing references were a stunt, when the trace-back is the one thing that can tell an earned authority from a manufactured one.

## Action

Walk it back. At [the Root](place_the_root.md), the Tracer on [Walk It Back](plan_walk_it_back.md) through [Tracing](process_tracing.md) holds [the Trace-Back](piece_the_trace_back.md), following the references home to the primary source, and finding the data small or absent or other than the citations claim, as the citation-network dissection does (Greenberg; Rekdal).

## Shadow

Its doubt is right and it costs more than it should have to. The Tracer is not a contrarian: to trace a claim to its root and read the primary study is the only way to tell an authority the evidence earned from one the citations built, and when it does so it finds, as the dissection found, that the record hardened around a claim the data never carried. But the work is slow and thankless, a research project to check a single fact that the practice of citing exists precisely so that no one need do, and it asks the field to distrust the dense record that ordinarily and rightly marks a claim as sound. And it holds its own bound: the trace-back is warranted where the chain runs thin, not a rule that every well-cited claim is hollow, since most are earned. Its correction is right and costly and does not scale to a literature of chains no one has walked.

## Tell

It walked the references home and found the root was empty, and the walking took a season to unsettle what a citation made in a moment.
