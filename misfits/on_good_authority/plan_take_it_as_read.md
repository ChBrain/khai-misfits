# Plan: Take It as Read

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reader's seat](position_reader.md), inside [On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reader](position_reader.md)

## Direction

Believe a claim reported settled in the reviews and cited across the literature, since the density of citations is the ordinary signal that others have checked it, and a reader cannot re-verify every fact it uses. Know that this sound reading is exactly what the woozle exploits, that the count you trust as confirmations may be echoes, and that nothing in the record from where you stand distinguishes an earned authority from a manufactured one.

## Orders

([The Reader](persona_the_reader.md) takes up [the Authority](piece_the_authority.md) through [Crediting](process_crediting.md) at [the Textbook](place_the_textbook.md), crediting the claim as established on the weight of its citations.)

## Implementation

([Crediting](process_crediting.md) at [the Textbook](place_the_textbook.md) takes up [the Authority](piece_the_authority.md), because to read the record rightly is to trust a densely cited claim, so the manufactured standing is credited as an earned one (Greenberg).)

## Targets

- [ ] the claim is credited as established on the density of its citations
- [ ] it is seen that trusting a densely cited claim is the sound and ordinary way to read the record
- [ ] it is understood that the count may be echoes of one source and not confirmations
- [ ] it is owned that the reader is deceived by no one, only by a record whose weight was repetition
