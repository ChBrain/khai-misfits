# Plan: Walk It Back

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the tracer's seat](position_tracer.md), inside [On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Tracer](position_tracer.md)

## Direction

Follow the chain of references to the primary study and read it, since only the source can tell an authority the evidence earned from one the citations built, and the dissection shows the record can harden around a claim the data never carried. Know that the work is slow and thankless, a research project to check one fact, that it asks the field to distrust the dense record it ordinarily and rightly trusts, and that your own doubt is bounded, warranted where the chain is thin and not everywhere by rule.
