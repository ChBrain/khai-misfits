# Process: Tracing

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). Borne at [the Root](place_the_root.md), precipitated by [the Tracer](persona_the_tracer.md), in [Walked Back](plot_walked_back.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Tracer, doubting not the claim itself but its standing, and starting where the citations point rather than where they lead.

## Direction

From the settled claim toward the primary source, read.

## Lever

Only the source itself can tell an authority the evidence earned from one the citations built, so walking the chain link by link back to its first link finds the root small, absent, or silent on what the record above it claims (Greenberg; Rekdal).
