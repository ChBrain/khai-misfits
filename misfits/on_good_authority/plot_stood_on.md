# Plot: Stood On

## Taxonomy
[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md).

## Owner
- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue
The claim sits on the record, there to be used, and a new piece of work needs a source for the point it makes in passing.

## Action
At [the Literature](place_the_literature.md), [the Citer](persona_the_citer.md) from [the citer's seat](position_citer.md) on [Stand on the Record](plan_stand_on_the_record.md) through [Citing](process_citing.md) takes up [the Citation](piece_the_citation.md), setting down a footnote to the claim without a detour back to the study behind it.

## Stage
The Citer needs a source for a point that is not the paper's own argument, and the claim is on the record, so it cites the claim and moves on, trusting the reference as the ordinary practice of the field allows. This is not corner-cutting: a working scholar cannot re-derive every upstream claim it touches, and most citers, reasonably, copy a reference rather than chase it back to the primary study (Simkin and Roychowdhury). The citation it enters is defensible entirely on its own terms, one working paper standing on a prior source the way the practice of citing is built to allow, exactly the kind of individual reference a citation-network dissection can trace without finding fault in it (Greenberg). Nothing is wrong yet, because a claim cited once has gained a witness and not an authority, and the Citer is not placed to see what its citation will become once it is not alone. No villain: to cite a prior source rather than rebuild it from scratch is the ordinary practice that makes scholarship possible at all, and this citation asks no more of the record than the record is meant to give.

## Tension
The citation is sound and reasonable and stands entirely on its own.
It will not stay alone: it is the first stone of a pile it cannot see itself become.
