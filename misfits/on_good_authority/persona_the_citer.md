# Persona: The Citer

## Taxonomy

[Citer](position_citer.md), inside [On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The archetype of the author who cites a prior source in good faith, standing on the record because it cannot re-derive every upstream claim, the ordinary act whose aggregate accretes the authority.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A lazy scholar who cites what it has not read and repeats what it has not checked, as though the whole distortion were its negligence, when to trust the record is the practice that makes any work possible.

## Action

Stand on the record. At [the Literature](place_the_literature.md), the Citer on [Stand on the Record](plan_stand_on_the_record.md) through [Citing](process_citing.md) takes up [the Citation](piece_the_citation.md), trusting a prior source and standing on it, then meets [the Accretion](piece_the_accretion.md) as the references grow and [the Amplification](piece_the_amplification.md) as the tentative is reported settled (Greenberg; Simkin and Roychowdhury).

## Shadow

It cannot re-derive the record, and no field that demanded it could move. The Citer is not lazy or negligent: to cite a prior source is to stand on the record rather than repeat every proof from the beginning, and a scholar who re-verified every upstream claim before using it would never finish a paper, so trusting a cited source, and copying a reference it has not chased to the root, is the ordinary and necessary practice of the field (Simkin and Roychowdhury). Each citation it makes is defensible on its own, and none of them is placed to see that the many references have become echoes of one source. It keeps its good faith and its sound practice and loses no cause for blame, one honest citation among the many whose sum builds the authority the evidence never gave.

## Tell

It stood on the record as anyone must, and cited in good faith, and its one reasonable citation joined the many that made a fact of a maybe.
