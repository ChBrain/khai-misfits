# Position: Source

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The seat of the one who makes the original claim, and marks that the claim was honest and hedged and that what the citing makes of it is out of its hands.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A single result or speculation to set down, no more than a small study or a tentative reading supports.
The qualifications that belong to it, the doubts and the smallness stated plainly on the page.
No hold on what follows, since it cannot govern who cites the claim or how, or whether its hedges survive the retelling.

## Orders

Say it once, plainly, and claim no more than the evidence allows.
Set down the result with its qualifications, the small study as small and the speculation as speculation, so the claim on the page is honest and no larger than its ground.
Know that the hedges are the first thing dropped in the citing, that a careful maybe is cited as a settled yes, and that the authority built on the claim will be built on a version you did not write.

## Loses

Its say over the claim, though the claim it made was honest.
Because it cannot control who cites the result or how, the seat that stated a tentative finding with its doubts attached watches the doubts fall away in the retelling and the claim harden into more than it ever said, so its honest modest assertion becomes the root of an authority it did not build and would not endorse.
It keeps its honest claim and its hedges on the page and loses no cause for blame, the source of a fact it never quite claimed.

## Drives

Toward the one who states the claim honestly and cannot hold its shape,
setting down a small result with its qualifications, no more than the evidence allows,
so the seat that hedged what it could not show loses those hedges to the citing, its careful maybe carried off as a settled fact it never made.
