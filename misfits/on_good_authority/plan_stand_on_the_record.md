# Plan: Stand on the Record

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the citer's seat](position_citer.md), inside [On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Citer](position_citer.md)

## Direction

Trust the cited claim and build on it, since to re-derive every upstream result before using it would make the work impossible, and copying a reference you have not chased to its root is the ordinary practice of the field. Know that the references accrete past what any of you can see, that the count of citations reads as confirmations when it is only echoes, and that the tentative hardens into the settled as the record is summarized and re-summarized.

## Orders

([The Citer](persona_the_citer.md) takes up [the Citation](piece_the_citation.md) through [Citing](process_citing.md) at [the Literature](place_the_literature.md), standing on the prior source in good faith, and meets [the Accretion](piece_the_accretion.md) and [the Amplification](piece_the_amplification.md) as the record grows and hardens.)

## Implementation

([Citing](process_citing.md) at [the Literature](place_the_literature.md) takes up [the Citation](piece_the_citation.md), because to stand on the record is to cite a prior source rather than re-derive it, so the references accrete into [the Accretion](piece_the_accretion.md) and harden through [the Amplification](piece_the_amplification.md) (Greenberg; Simkin and Roychowdhury).)

## Targets

- [ ] the prior source is cited in good faith, standing on the record
- [ ] it is seen that re-deriving every upstream claim would make the work impossible
- [ ] it is understood that the accreting references read as confirmations when they are echoes
- [ ] it is owned that no single citer authors the distortion or is placed to see it
