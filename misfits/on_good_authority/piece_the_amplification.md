# Piece: The Amplification

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The mechanism: the distortion layered onto the count, a tentative claim reported as settled, the supportive cited over the critical, and now and then a source cited for a claim it never made.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Literature](place_the_literature.md), carried by [Citing](process_citing.md), voiced by [the Citer](persona_the_citer.md): a review paragraph that states the claim flatly, no longer a maybe, its qualifying clause quietly gone from this telling and the one after it.

## Load Bearing

Where the accretion is a matter of count, the amplification is a matter of shape: the citation-network dissection of one biomedical claim documents citation bias (the supportive study cited far more than the critical one), citation amplification (a review calling a tentative finding established), and even citation invention (a source cited for a claim it does not contain) (Greenberg). Each of these is still an ordinary editorial act; a review compresses a literature honestly as reviews are meant to, and compression drops qualifications the way any summary drops detail. But summed across a chain of reviews citing reviews, the qualifications do not thin, they vanish, and the record hardens around an assertion that no single study behind it actually established (Greenberg). This is the piece where the hedge the source set down is fully gone from the page.

## Apparent

Reads as a fair summary of a settled literature, a review doing exactly its job of distilling many findings into one clean, citable sentence.
