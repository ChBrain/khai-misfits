# Persona: The Reader

## Taxonomy

[Reader](position_reader.md), inside [On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The archetype of the one who takes the much-cited claim as established fact, reading the record as it is meant to be read and believing by the count of citations.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A credulous reader who swallowed a claim without checking it, as though a stronger mind would have doubted a fact the whole field cites, when to trust a densely cited claim is exactly how the record is meant to be used.

## Action

Take it as read. At [the Textbook](place_the_textbook.md), the Reader on [Take It as Read](plan_take_it_as_read.md) through [Crediting](process_crediting.md) takes up [the Authority](piece_the_authority.md), crediting the claim as established because it is cited everywhere and reported settled, believing it on the weight of its references (Greenberg).

## Shadow

It read the record rightly, and the record was the thing at fault. The Reader is not credulous: a claim cited across a literature and reported as established in the reviews is, by every ordinary and sound rule of reading, a claim one may take as known, since a reader cannot chase every fact to its primary source and the density of citations is precisely the signal that says a claim has been checked by others. To distrust it would be to distrust the whole practice of standing on a shared record. It believes by the count because the count is the record's own mark of soundness, and it has no way from where it reads to tell a hundred confirmations from a hundred echoes of one source. It keeps its sound reading and loses no cause for blame, deceived by no one, only by a record whose weight was repetition.

## Tell

It trusted the fact the whole field cites, as a careful reader should, and the whole field was citing one another back to a claim no one had shown.
