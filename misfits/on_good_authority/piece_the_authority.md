# Piece: The Authority

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The mechanism: the manufactured standing, a weak claim believed on the weight of its citations rather than the weight of its evidence.

## Place

## Load Bearing

By the time it reaches the textbook, the claim is read as knowledge: dense citation, in the ordinary run of scholarship, is exactly the mark of a claim worth trusting, so a reader who credits a much-cited statement is reading the record precisely as it is meant to be read (Greenberg). The term for this outcome was carried into research method from Milne's image of the Woozle, the creature Pooh and Piglet track round and round a spinney by following their own deepening prints, growing surer a whole pack walks ahead as the ring of tracks widens, a circle of one's own making mistaken for a crowd (Milne); researchers in family violence named the pattern after it, a statistic or claim that gains standing by being cited rather than by being shown (Houghton; Gelles and Straus). Nothing in the reading is careless. The authority was manufactured upstream, in the accretion and the amplification, and arrives at the reader already finished.

## Apparent

Reads as knowledge earned, a fact settled by the weight of scholarship behind it, no different in appearance from any claim that was actually shown.
