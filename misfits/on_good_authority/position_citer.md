# Position: Citer

## Taxonomy

[On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md). The seat of the author who cites a prior source, and marks that standing on the record is no negligence but the ordinary practice that makes the work possible.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A prior source to build on, and a claim it needs but cannot re-derive from the beginning.
The sound practice of citing, the standing on the record that lets a field advance without proving everything anew.
No sight of the whole network, no way from one citation to tell whether the source is one confirmation among many or the one echo they all repeat.

## Orders

Stand on the record, and cite the prior source in good faith.
Trust the cited claim and build on it, since to re-derive every upstream result before using it would make the work impossible, and copying a reference you have not chased to its root is the ordinary practice of the field.
Know that the references accrete past what any of you can see, that the count of citations reads as confirmations when it is only echoes, and that the tentative hardens into the settled as the record is summarized and re-summarized (Greenberg; Simkin and Roychowdhury).

## Loses

Nothing it can name, and this is the trap.
Because each citation it makes is defensible and standing on the record is the practice that makes the work possible, the seat takes no loss it can see, and yet its one reasonable citation joins the many whose sum accretes an authority the evidence never gave, a distortion no single citer authored and none is placed to observe.
It keeps its good faith and its sound practice and loses no cause for blame, the honest citation whose aggregate makes a fact of a maybe.

## Drives

Toward the one who stands on the record as any scholar must,
citing a prior source in good faith because it cannot re-derive it all, blind to the network its citation joins,
so the seat whose every citation is defensible on its own is the seat from whose honest aggregate the unearned authority accretes.
