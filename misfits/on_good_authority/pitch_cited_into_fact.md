# Pitch: Cited Into Fact

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [On Good Authority](play_on_good_authority.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a claim can be cited into fact, its authority built by the count of references and not by the weight of the evidence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Dry and careful, the unease of a fact that no one checked. A record built on good faith, each author standing on prior work because it cannot re-derive it all, and a weak claim gathering the references that read as confirmations. Nothing here is a fraud or a lazy scholar; the citing is the ordinary practice that makes the work possible.

## Undertow

A second author cites the first, a review calls the tentative established, a model builds on the review, and belief tracks the density of citations and not the data beneath them, the count read as many confirmations when it is only many echoes of the one source (Greenberg). Now and then a paper is cited for a claim it never made, and the record hardens around an assertion no single study established.

## Nerve

That the same practice that lets a scholar stand on the record rather than re-derive it all is the practice by which a weak claim is repeated into fact, believed on good authority that was never the evidence but only the count. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since this is a mechanism attested in cases and not a measured rate, and a dense citation record is more often earned than manufactured (Rekdal).

## Echo

Cite a thin claim in good faith, and cite the citation, and the footprints deepen into a fact no one ever showed.
