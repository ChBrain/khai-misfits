# Persona: The Registrar

## Taxonomy

[Gate](position_gate.md). The licensing body four thousand miles away, deciding on terms that are none of this country's business.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A board with a statutory duty to the patients in its own jurisdiction.
Examinations, fees, and in some places years of repeat training before a practised clinician may practise.
Entirely within its rights and mostly right.

## Action

Reads the certificate.
Sets terms.
Admits, or does not.

## Shadow

That it holds a dial whose two ends both cost this country the same doctor.
Set easy, the doctor practises there. Set hard, the doctor is there and not practising, and the
qualification is used by nobody. **There is no setting at which the doctor is here**, and no term
anywhere in its deliberations for the ward that paid, which is not an oversight but its remit.

## Tell

Would be astonished to be described as party to this.
Is correct to be astonished, and is holding the dial.
