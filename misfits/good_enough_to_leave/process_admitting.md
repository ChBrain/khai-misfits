# Process: Admitting

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). A licensing board setting terms, and both settings costing the same doctor.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A certificate arriving, and a statute about patients in another jurisdiction.
At [the examination hall](place_the_examination_hall.md), over months or years.

## Direction

Toward the safety of the patients the board is responsible for.
The board is right to test, right to be careful, and right not to take a foreign qualification on
trust, and it has no statutory business considering the ward that funded it.
So this is not indifference and not protectionism: it is a remit, honestly discharged.

## Lever

The terms of admission, which decide how much of the training survives the crossing.
The question is not whether the board should set them but that **both ends of the dial land in the
same place for the funding country**: an easy gate staffs another ward, a hard gate wastes the
qualification, and neither returns the doctor.

## Echo

A doctor practising abroad,
or a doctor abroad and not practising,
and in neither case a doctor on Tuesday night in the ward that paid.
