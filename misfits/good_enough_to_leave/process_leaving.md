# Process: Leaving

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). A doctor going, having paid something themselves for the privilege.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A certificate, and everything that is true about wages, equipment, safety and a career.
From [the ward](place_the_ward.md) toward [the examination hall](place_the_examination_hall.md), over a year or two.

## Direction

Toward a life anybody may choose.
Nothing here is ingratitude or bad faith: the person leaving usually pays fees, sits examinations
and accepts years of repeated training, which is a considerable price for a thing they are entitled
to do.
So the play has no complaint against them and does not make one, and a version of this that blamed
them would be a different play with a villain in it.

## Lever

The right to leave, which is the thing that would have to be removed for any remedy to work.
The question is not whether they should go but what it means that the document making it possible
is the same one that made the training worth having.

## Echo

One name off the rota,
one queue joined abroad,
and a country that has funded a doctor for somebody, and not for the ward that paid.
