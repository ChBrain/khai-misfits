## Tension

Here the inversion arrives, and it arrives as an identity rather than a leak.
The certificate says a stranger could check this. That sentence is the whole of what makes it worth
anything to the patient it was issued for, and it is also the whole of what makes it worth something
to a licensing board four thousand miles away.
**The examiner certifying and the registrar admitting are performing the same act on the same
document.** There is no misuse to prevent, no loophole to close and nobody to blame for the
plumbing, because the export is the intended use of the qualification arriving from an unexpected
direction.
And the alternative is not a tighter certificate but a weaker one. A document a foreign board could
not read would not be evidence to the ward either, which puts the question straight back where the
last plot left it.
