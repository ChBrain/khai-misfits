# Piece: Neither Setting Returns Them

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). The dial the receiving country holds, and the fact that both of its ends cost the same doctor.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the examination hall](place_the_examination_hall.md), in the terms of admission.
In the recognition literature, where a steep gate is recorded as de-skilling highly trained migrants.

## Load Bearing

Without it there would be an obvious remedy, which is to lobby for harder recognition abroad.
It is load bearing because it closes that exit: an easy gate staffs another country's wards, and a
hard gate produces a clinician who has practised for a decade sitting examinations and then not
practising at all. **The funding country loses the doctor at both ends**, and only the receiving
country's patients are better or worse off in between.

## Apparent

That a hard gate at least deters the leaving.
It raises the price and the migration it was supposed to price did not stop, which is the point of
the row below about the code: the people leaving are paying a considerable price already.

## Yearbook

Assembled from the recognition literature on one side and the migration figures on the other.
Putting the two together is the play's extension and is marked as such.
