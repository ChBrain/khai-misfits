## Action

## Stage

[The Curriculum](persona_the_curriculum.md) takes them all, and cannot tell which are which.

## Tension

Here the play states the case against itself, because a warrant that did not would be an advocacy.
The prospect of leaving is a reason to train. People apply who otherwise would not, the queue is
longer than it would be, and **not all of them go**, so the country ends with more trained people
than it would have had without the possibility that took them.
That is measured and it is not in dispute here. It is also measured what it comes to. Across a
hundred and twenty seven countries the same work that establishes the gain finds **more losers than
winners, and the losers losing more than the winners gain.**
So the play does not claim the counter-current is imaginary. It claims the net, which is a narrower
thing to claim and the only thing the evidence supports. **A version of this play without this plot
in it would be making a larger claim on less.**
