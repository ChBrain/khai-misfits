# Good Enough to Leave: Reference

The research warrant for [Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md).
The setting is publicly funded health worker training in a country that loses clinicians to emigration; the play
runs on the fact that the property making a training worth having is the property making it legible abroad, all
cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging health worker migration as **a quality that cannot be issued in a domestic edition**: a qualification no
outsider can evaluate is a claim rather than a standard, so a country owing its own patients a checked clinician
must issue a credential a stranger can check; the examiner certifying and a foreign registrar admitting read that
credential by the same means, so the export is the intended use arriving from an unexpected direction; the
receiving board sets the terms of admission under a statute that correctly contains no term for the funding
country, and both settings of that dial cost it the same clinician, an easy gate to another ward and a hard one
to no ward at all; the prospect of leaving induces training that would not otherwise happen and some of the
induced stay; and the measured net across a hundred and twenty seven countries still runs against the losers. The
play models the standard that is owed, the certificate with two readers, the gate held abroad, the rota that
paid, and the queue that lengthens, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of ungrateful
emigrants or predatory recruiters.

The governing law is that recognisability is the whole content of a certification, so the quality of a training
and its portability are one property and cannot be separated by any wording of the certificate.

It is distinct from the house's **The Dead Sea Effect**, whose engine is a talent differential: the ablest have
outside options and evaporate first. Hold talent flat, stipulating every graduate equally able, and this play
runs unchanged, because **it is the certificate and not the ability that a foreign board can read**, so the
credentialed leave and the uncredentialed cannot while ability is constant. There the differential is in the
person; here it is in the document.

It is distinct from the house's **Ready on the First Day**, which stages the specificity trade in vocational
training, where a tightly specified course buys an early advantage and a later adaptability cost. Remove the
adaptability cost entirely, stipulating a leaver who adapts perfectly for twenty five years, and this is
untouched: **the payer here is the funding country and not the trained person**, and the clock is a labour market
rather than a career.

It is distinct from the house's **No One's Remit**, where each mandate is honest and narrow and the exclusion is
in the design. Broaden every remit, stipulating that the receiving board must weigh the funding country's loss,
and this survives, because the clinician leaves for wages, equipment, safety and a career rather than for the
gate: a wider remit changes **whether the training is used**, which is the dial in the third plot, and does not
change whether it goes.

It is distinct from the house's **The Lowest Bar**, which stages regulatory competition, jurisdictions bidding
standards downward to attract what is mobile. Switch that engine off, stipulating no competitive pressure on
standards anywhere, and this stands entire, and it runs the other way: nothing here is lowered to attract
anything, and **the funding country raises its standard unilaterally, at its own cost, and that is what exports.**

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the emigrating
  clinician as ungrateful, the receiving country as predatory, the school as naive or the regulator as
  indifferent, because nothing in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the school teaches what its own
  patients are owed and would teach it if emigration were impossible; the certificate is legible because
  legibility is what certification means; the clinician who leaves exercises a right and usually pays fees and
  examinations for it; the receiving board discharges a statutory duty to its own patients and would exceed its
  remit by weighing anybody else's; and the applicant drawn in by the prospect, who then stays, is a gain nobody
  planned. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that quality and
  portability are one property.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. The code exists, was adopted by
  almost every state, is voluntary and unenforced, and the migration it addressed did not slow. Giving it teeth
  means restricting a person's right to leave, which is the measure that would work and the one nobody will
  write. Funding a transfer requires a payer to accept a loss they did not cause and which appears on no budget
  line. Bonding graduates lowers applications, so the country trains fewer and loses by another route. And
  teaching to something unrecognised certainly works and is paid for by the patients in the ward downstairs,
  who were the reason for the training.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the measurement of skilled migration against human capital
  formation and the netting of winners against losers are Beine, Docquier and Rapoport's, the code and its terms
  are the World Health Organization's member states', the post-adoption evaluation is the migration researchers'
  who conducted it, and the account of recognition barriers and de-skilling is the credential recognition
  literature's. The bounds are carried with them, and three are owed plainly. **None of the cited works was read
  in full**, each being behind publisher access from this environment, so each is cited for the question it
  addresses and its findings as corroborated from several independent reports, and **no figure appears anywhere
  in this production that those reports did not state**: the play carries the count of countries, the count of
  states adopting the code and the direction of the net, and nothing finer. The second: the harm is **contested**,
  and the contest is staged rather than answered, since the counter-current has its own seat, persona, plan,
  process, piece and plot in the production, and the play's claim is the sign of the net and not a magnitude.
  And the third is that **the definition doing the most work is the house's own**: that checkability by a
  stranger is the whole content of a certification, and therefore that quality and portability cannot be
  separated, is stated in the warrant as a definition rather than cited as a result, because it is one. Every
  measured claim in the production rests on the rows above; that one rests on what accreditation is.
