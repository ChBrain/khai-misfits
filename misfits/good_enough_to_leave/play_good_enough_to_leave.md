# Play: Good Enough to Leave

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Good Enough to Leave.
A country trains doctors, and it trains them properly, because the people in its own wards are owed somebody who has been examined against a real standard.
A standard is a thing other people can check. That is what makes it a standard and not a local habit.
So the same document that tells a patient here their doctor was trained tells a hospital four thousand miles away the same thing, and it is the only document that can.

## Arc

The bend is this: the property that makes the training worth having is the property that makes it travel, and they are not two properties.

It runs on one fact about standards and one about who decides. **A qualification nobody outside can evaluate is not a standard**: it is a claim, and a country that issues those has no way of telling its own citizens that its doctors are any good. And the country that receives the doctor sets the terms of admission, for its own patients, by its own regulator, with no term anywhere in the calculation for the country that paid for the training.

Take the standard first, because the play has to establish that it is **right**. The school could teach to something idiosyncratic and unexaminable, and it would be cheaper and the graduates would stay. It would also be a school whose graduates nobody can check, in a country whose patients cannot be told what they are getting, and no health system that has tried this has been proud of it. Teaching to a recognised standard is the correct thing to do and would be the correct thing to do if nobody ever emigrated.

Then the fact that inverts it. **Recognisability has one meaning and two uses.** The examiners at home use it to certify; a registrar abroad uses it to admit. Nothing distinguishes the two uses, because they are the same property being read by different people, and there is no version of the document that a foreign registrar cannot read and a domestic patient can rely on.

Then the sharper half, which is the part that removes the last exit. The receiving country holds a dial. Set it low, with easy recognition, and the doctor arrives and practises: the funding country has paid to staff somebody else's wards. Set it high, with a re-examination and years of retraining, and the doctor arrives anyway and drives a taxi, or waits: the funding country has paid for a qualification that is now being used by nobody at all. **Neither setting returns them**, and the setting is chosen abroad, honestly, on grounds that have nothing to do with the loss, because the receiving regulator is protecting its own patients and that is its job.

Then the counter-current, which is real and has to be carried honestly rather than argued past. The possibility of leaving is itself a reason to train, so more people apply than would have, and some of them do not go. Read across a hundred and twenty seven countries the effect exists and does not settle it: **there are more losers than winners, and the losers lose more than the winners gain.** The play stands on that sentence and not on the absence of the other one.

And nobody has done anything wrong. The school teaches to the standard. The examiners examine. The doctor who leaves is doing what any person may do and has usually paid something themselves for the privilege. The foreign registrar admits or refuses on grounds that concern their own patients. The applicant who came because they might leave, and stayed, is a gain nobody planned.

Reach for a settlement and each one costs something nobody will give. A code exists: a hundred and ninety three states adopted one in twenty ten, and it is voluntary, unenforced, and the migration it addressed did not slow. Make it binding and it becomes a restriction on a person's right to leave, which is the thing that would work and the thing that will not be written. Fund a transfer and somebody must agree to pay for a loss they did not intend and cannot see on any budget line. Bond the graduates and the applications fall, which lowers the number trained. And teach to something unrecognised, which is the only measure that certainly works, and the patients in the ward downstairs are the ones who pay for it.

## Triggers

**[What the Patients Here Are Owed](plot_what_the_patients_here_are_owed.md)**
The standard established as right rather than as the defect: a qualification nobody outside can evaluate is a claim and not a standard, and a country issuing those cannot tell its own citizens what their doctors are. Closes when it is clear the school would teach this way if nobody ever emigrated.

**[Readable Anywhere](plot_readable_anywhere.md)**
Cue: the certificate, and the two people who can read it. Closes when the inversion is in the open: recognisability has one meaning and two uses, the examiner's and the registrar's, and no version of the document exists that only one of them can read.

**[Waste or Drain](plot_waste_or_drain.md)**
Cue: a registrar abroad, setting terms of admission for their own patients. Closes when both settings are shown to lose: an easy gate staffs somebody else's wards, a hard gate wastes the qualification entirely, and neither returns the doctor to the ward that paid.

**[More Come Because More Leave](plot_more_come_because_more_leave.md)**
Cue: an applications office with more applications than last year. Closes when the counter-current is carried honestly and found insufficient: the prospect of leaving does induce people to train and some do stay, and across a hundred and twenty seven countries there are more losers than winners and the losers lose more.

**[And the Code Was Voluntary](plot_and_the_code_was_voluntary.md)**
Cue: the settlements. Closes when each is found to cost what it was for: a binding code restricts a right to leave, a transfer needs a payer for an invisible loss, bonding lowers the number trained, and an unrecognised qualification is paid for by the patients in the ward downstairs.

## Stakes

Whether a country can train people well for itself when good training is by definition legible to everybody else.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the doctors who leave are ungrateful, because they are doing what anybody may do, and not whether receiving countries are predatory, because their regulators are protecting their own patients exactly as they should, but whether the quality of a training and its portability can be separated at all. The production cannot separate them. It can only show that a standard is a thing others can check, that being checkable is the whole of the export licence, that the terms on which the export is used are set by somebody with no term for the loss, that both settings of that dial leave the funding ward empty, that the counter-current is real and measured and does not cover it, and that every remedy either restricts a person, invents a payer, shrinks the intake, or breaks the standard.
