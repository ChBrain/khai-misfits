# Process: Certifying

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). The document issued, and the two uses it has from the moment it exists.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Teaching](process_teaching.md), completed and examined.
At [the medical school](place_the_medical_school.md), on one afternoon, for everybody at once.

## Direction

Toward evidence rather than assertion.
The certificate says a stranger could check this, which is the only thing that makes it worth
anything to the patient it was issued for.
And a stranger four thousand miles away is also a stranger, reading the same sentence in the same
way, which is not a misuse of the document but the use it was built for arriving from an unexpected
direction.

## Lever

Accreditation, which is the thing that cannot be issued locally.
The play's question is not whether the school should certify but whether a certificate can be made
that only one kind of stranger can read.

## Echo

A page with a seal,
meant for a ward,
and readable at a licensing board on another continent without translation.
