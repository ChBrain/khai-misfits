# Piece: A Standard Is Checkable

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). What makes a qualification a standard rather than a claim, and why it cannot be issued locally.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the accreditation framed in the corridor at [the medical school](place_the_medical_school.md).
In every sentence a health system says to its own citizens about its own doctors.

## Load Bearing

Without it the school could teach something unexaminable, keep its graduates, and the play would be
a story about a country that chose badly.
It is load bearing because **checkability by a stranger is the whole content of the certification**:
a qualification no outsider could evaluate would be evidence to nobody, including the patient it was
issued for, so there is no version that certifies at home and does not travel.

## Apparent

That portability is a feature that could be withheld.
Withholding it means withholding the property that made the training worth having, which is why the
only measure that certainly retains graduates is the one paid for by the ward downstairs.

## Yearbook

Not a finding but a definition, and the play uses it as one.
Everything measured below is what happens once it is true.
