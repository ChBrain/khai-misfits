# Piece: More Losers Than Winners

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). The counter-current measured and netted: the strongest argument against the play, carried inside it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the human-capital literature, across a hundred and twenty seven countries.
At [the queue at the gate](persona_the_queue_at_the_gate.md), which is longer than it would be.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play would be arguing a loss while ignoring a documented gain, which is not a warrant
but an advocacy.
It is load bearing because it is the honest arithmetic: the prospect of emigration does raise human
capital formation and some who trained for it stay, and the same work finds **more losers than
winners, with the losers losing more than the winners gain.** The play stands on the second half
and would not stand without the first being stated.

## Apparent

That admitting the gain concedes the argument.
It concedes that the gain is real and denies that it covers the loss, which is what the measurement
says and is a narrower claim than the play would need if the gain did not exist.

## Yearbook

Economic research on brain drain and human capital formation, taken at its word in both directions.
The play claims the sign of the net and not a figure for it.
