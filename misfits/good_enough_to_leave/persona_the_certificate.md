# Persona: The Certificate

## Taxonomy

[Legibility](position_legibility.md). One document, two readers, and no way to tell them apart.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A page with a seal on it, and behind the page an accreditation nobody in the room arranged.
Meant for a patient, a hospital and a regulator here.
Readable, without translation, by a licensing board on another continent.

## Action

Certifies.
Travels, being paper and being legible.
Opens a queue somewhere else.

## Shadow

That it cannot be issued in a domestic edition.
Legibility is not a feature added to the qualification for export: it **is** the qualification, since
a credential that could not be checked by a stranger would not be evidence of anything to anybody,
including the patient it was issued for.

## Tell

Nobody designed it to be an export licence.
Nobody can design one that is not.
