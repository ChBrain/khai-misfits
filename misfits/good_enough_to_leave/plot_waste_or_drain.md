## Stage

[The Rota](persona_the_rota.md), in [the coverage seat](position_coverage.md), short either way, with no standing in the decision.

## Tension

Here the last exit closes, and it closes at both ends.
The obvious move, once the previous plot has landed, is to want the receiving countries to make
recognition harder. Follow it. **A hard gate does not return the doctor.** It produces a clinician
who has practised for a decade sitting examinations about their own profession, repeating years of
training, and in many cases not practising at all, which the recognition literature records as the
de-skilling of exactly the people whose training was most expensive.
An easy gate does not return them either. It puts them in somebody else's ward, working, which is
the version where at least a patient somewhere benefits.
**So the funding country loses the doctor at both settings**, and the only thing the dial changes is
whether anybody gets the use of the training. And the dial is held by a board doing its job, whose
statute correctly contains no term for this ward, and which would be exceeding its remit if it did.
