# Position: Gate

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). The seat of the receiving terms: a dial held abroad, with no term for the loss it causes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A statutory duty to patients in another jurisdiction.
Examinations, fees and, in some places, years of repeat training for a clinician who has practised for a decade.
Complete discretion over how much of the training survives the crossing.

## Orders

Admit on our terms.
Protect our patients.
Consider what our statute tells us to consider.

## Loses

Nothing, which is the difficulty.
Both settings of the dial cost the funding country the same doctor, one to another ward and one to
no ward at all, and **there is no setting at which the doctor is here**, so the choice that decides
the size of the loss is made honestly, elsewhere, by somebody whose remit correctly excludes it.

## Drives

Toward its own patients' safety,
which is its whole job and which it does well,
so the seat that is behaving properly is the seat that disposes of what somebody else paid for.
