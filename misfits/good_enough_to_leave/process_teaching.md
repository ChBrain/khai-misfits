# Process: Teaching

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). Six years, examined externally, against a standard the profession would recognise anywhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A cohort arriving, and a duty to the ward downstairs.
At [the medical school](place_the_medical_school.md), over six years.

## Direction

Toward a doctor a patient can be told something true about.
The alternative is a syllabus nobody outside can evaluate, which is cheaper and keeps its graduates,
and which leaves a country unable to say what its doctors are.
So this is not an ambition or a vanity: it is the minimum a health system owes the people in it.

## Lever

The curriculum, which can be weakened and only in the one direction that matters.
The question is not whether to teach well but whether teaching well can be done without also
producing a document a stranger can read.

## Echo

A cohort examined and passed,
a school that has done its job,
and a qualification that is now evidence to anybody who asks, wherever they are asking from.
