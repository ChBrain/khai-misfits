# Persona: The Curriculum

## Taxonomy

[Standard](position_standard.md). What is taught, and the reason it is taught that way.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Six years, examined externally, mapped against what the profession requires anywhere.
Written by people who have seen what happens where it is not.
The thing the school is proudest of and would defend first.

## Action

Teaches to the standard.
Is examined against it.
Would be the same document if nobody ever left.

## Shadow

That its quality is not separable from its portability.
There is no curriculum that produces a doctor a patient here can rely on and a registrar abroad
cannot read, because **the reliance and the reading are the same act performed by different people.**

## Tell

Everybody agrees it should not be weakened.
Nobody can name the part that could be weakened without weakening the ward.
