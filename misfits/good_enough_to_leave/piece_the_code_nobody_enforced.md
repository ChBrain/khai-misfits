# Piece: The Code Nobody Enforced

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). The remedy that exists, was adopted almost universally, and did not work.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the global code on the international recruitment of health personnel, adopted in 2010.
Signed by a hundred and ninety three states and binding on none of them.

## Load Bearing

Without it somebody can say that the problem is simply unaddressed and the answer is to address it.
It is load bearing because it shows the addressed version: **soft law, voluntary, no enforcement
mechanism**, and the sub-Saharan-Africa-to-United-States physician migration it was written about
did not slow after it. So the remedy is not missing. It is present and inert.

## Apparent

That it merely needs teeth.
Teeth means restricting a person's right to leave, or a transfer somebody must agree to fund for a
loss they did not cause and cannot see on a budget line, and the reason it was written voluntary is
that neither was available.

## Yearbook

An instrument of international soft law, evaluated after adoption and found not to have moved the
thing it addressed.
It is the no-hero section's best evidence and is why the play has one.
