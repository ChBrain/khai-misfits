## Tension

The standard has to be established as **right** before anything else is worth saying, because the
easy reading of this play is that the country over-invested in training.
It did not. A qualification nobody outside can evaluate is a claim rather than a standard, and a
country that issues those cannot tell its own citizens anything true about its own doctors.
The cheaper school exists and is available: teach something unexaminable, keep the graduates, and
accept that the ward downstairs is staffed by people nobody has checked.
**No health system that has tried that has been proud of it**, and the school here would teach the
way it does if emigration were impossible, which is the test that matters: the standard is not a
concession to a global market, it is what the ward is owed.
