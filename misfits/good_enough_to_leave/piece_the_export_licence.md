# Piece: The Export Licence

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). One document, two readers, and the discovery that they are performing the same act.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [the certificate](persona_the_certificate.md), from the afternoon it is issued.
Read at home by an employer and abroad by a licensing board, identically.

## Load Bearing

Without it the export would be a leak to be plugged and somebody would be at fault for the plumbing.
It is load bearing because the examiner certifying and the registrar admitting are **reading the
same sentence in the same way**: the export is not a misuse of the qualification but its intended
use arriving from an unexpected direction, so there is nobody to blame and nothing to tighten.

## Apparent

That a country could issue a certificate valid only within its borders.
It could, and it would be a claim rather than a standard, which returns the whole question to the
piece above.
