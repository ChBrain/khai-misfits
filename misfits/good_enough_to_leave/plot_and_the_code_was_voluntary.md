## Tension

Here the settlements are tried, and each costs something nobody will give.
**The code**: it exists, it was adopted almost universally, it is soft law with no enforcement
mechanism, and the migration it was written about did not slow. The remedy is not missing. It is
present and inert.
**Give it teeth**: teeth means restricting a person's right to leave, which is the measure that
would work and is the one nobody will write, and the reason the code was drafted voluntary in the
first place.
**Fund a transfer**: somebody must agree to pay for a loss they did not cause and which appears on
no budget line anywhere, and a cost with no ledger has no claimant.
**Bond the graduates**: applications fall, so the country trains fewer, which is the loss arriving
by another route.
**Teach something unrecognised**: this certainly works, and it is paid for by the patients in the
ward downstairs, who were the reason for all of it.
