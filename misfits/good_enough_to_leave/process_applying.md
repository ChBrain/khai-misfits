# Process: Applying

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). More people entering training than would have, because of where the training can be used.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The visible fact that this degree is usable elsewhere.
At [the medical school](place_the_medical_school.md), every admissions round.

## Direction

Toward a longer queue, which is a real gain and is counted as one.
Some of these applicants would not have applied at all, and not all of them will leave, so the
country ends with more trained people than it would have had, which is the strongest argument
against the play and is made here rather than answered elsewhere.

## Lever

The prospect itself, which cannot be offered selectively.
The question is not whether the gain is real, since it is measured, but whether it covers the loss.

## Echo

A longer list than last year,
a share who stay and were not planned for,
and an arithmetic across a hundred and twenty seven countries in which more countries lose than win
and the losers lose more.
