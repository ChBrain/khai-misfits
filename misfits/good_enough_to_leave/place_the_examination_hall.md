# Place: The Examination Hall

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). Where the crossing is priced: a licensing examination in the receiving country, and the dial set by whoever set it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Fees, papers, and clinicians who have practised for a decade sitting an examination about their own
profession.
In some jurisdictions, years of repeated training afterwards before any of it counts.

## Holds

The dial, and the fact that both ends of it land in the same place.
Set easy, the doctor practises here and the funding ward is short. Set hard, the doctor is here and
not practising, the funding ward is short, and nobody has the doctor at all.

## Offers

Protection to the patients of the receiving country, which it genuinely provides.

## Withheld

Any term for the ward that paid.
That is not an oversight in the statute. It is the statute, and a board that included such a term
would be exceeding its remit and would be told so.
