# Place: The Medical School

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). Where the training is made: six years, external examination, and a prospectus everybody is proud of.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Lecture theatres, an accreditation certificate framed in the corridor, and an admissions office with
a longer queue than last year.
Staff who could name every part of the syllabus and why it is there.

## Holds

The whole of the mechanism, in a form nobody would want changed.
The accreditation on the wall is the reason a patient can be told something true and the reason a
board on another continent can read the graduate's certificate, and it is one accreditation.

## Offers

Doctors trained to a standard, which is what it was built to offer.

## Withheld

A domestic edition of its own qualification.
There is nothing it could teach that would make a good doctor and an unreadable certificate, and the
one syllabus that would keep its graduates is the one its own wards could not accept.
