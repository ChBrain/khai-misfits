# Persona: The Queue at the Gate

## Taxonomy

[Prospect](position_prospect.md). The applicants, more of them than last year, and the reason there are more.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A line outside an admissions office, longer than the places available.
Some who would have applied anyway, and some who are here because of where this degree can be used.
Nobody in it will say which they are, and most do not know.

## Action

Applies.
Trains.
Splits, at the end, into those who go and those who do not.

## Shadow

That it is a real gain and does not cover the loss.
The prospect of leaving induces people to train who otherwise would not, and not all of them leave,
so some of this queue is a windfall the country never planned. Read across a hundred and twenty
seven countries, **there are more losers than winners and the losers lose more than the winners
gain**, which is the honest arithmetic and is why this persona is here rather than absent.

## Tell

Is the strongest argument against the play, made inside it.
Is not strong enough, and the play says by how much.
