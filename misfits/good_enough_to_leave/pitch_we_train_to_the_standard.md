# Pitch: We Train to the Standard

## Taxonomy

[Good Enough to Leave](play_good_enough_to_leave.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Proud of the school and precise about what a standard is.
Talking to a minister who wants the number fixed, and unwilling to pretend that any available answer is one.
Quoting the figure wherever the figure is the point.

## Undertow

Under the prospectus, one fact about standards and one about who decides.
A qualification nobody outside can evaluate is a claim rather than a standard.
And the terms on which it is used abroad are set abroad, by people protecting their own patients.

## Nerve

The unease of a sentence said with pride.
We train to the standard: true, correct, the right thing to have done,
and the exact reason the fourth-year list and the departures list are the same list.

## Echo

After the run: a certificate two people can read and only one of them was meant to,
a registrar four thousand miles away holding a dial with no term for this ward,
more applicants than last year, some of whom will stay,
and a rota that still does not cover Tuesday night.
