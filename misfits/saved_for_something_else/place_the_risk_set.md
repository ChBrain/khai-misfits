# Place: The Risk Set

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md). Where a subject is either in or out, and where both kinds of leaving look identical. Hosts [Count the Time and Let Them Go](plot_count_the_time_and_let_them_go.md) and [They Did Not Stop Being Watched](plot_they_did_not_stop_being_watched.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Careful bookkeeping: every subject's contributed time counted, every exit recorded at its last observation, nothing dropped and nothing invented.

## Holds

[The Censoring](piece_the_censoring.md), the correct rule for an unfinished observation, and [The Other Death](piece_the_other_death.md), the case it was never written for.

## Offers

To the analysis, an honest account of how long each subject was actually watched, which is what a survival model needs and is exactly what it gets.

## Withheld

Any field separating a study that closed from a patient who died, so the two arrive through one door and are handled by one rule, and the room cannot notice a distinction it has no way to record.
