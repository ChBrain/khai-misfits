# Persona: The Oncologist

## Taxonomy

[Oncologist](position_oncologist.md). The one reporting a true count that means something other than what it appears to mean.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Attentive and candid, following a survivor cohort for years and noticing that cardiac events in it are rising.
Reports the rise because it is real and because not reporting it would be concealing something.
Takes no position on causation, only on what the cohort shows.

## Action

Runs [Report What the Cohort Shows](plan_report_what_the_cohort_shows.md): through [Inflating](process_inflating.md) at [the Follow-Up Clinic](place_the_follow_up_clinic.md), setting [the Inflation](piece_the_inflation.md).
Counts the events, compares them with the historical cohort, and finds the increase both real and substantial.
Writes it up plainly, without speculation and without softening.

## Shadow

Not alarmist and not mistaken about anything it says: every event it counted happened, and the rise it reports is present in the data and would be present for any other observer.
Because the patients who would have died of the tumour are alive to die of something else, the rise is a consequence of the therapy working rather than of the therapy harming, and no amount of care in the counting distinguishes those two from inside the count.
It is the seat that supplies a true number to a false conclusion without asserting the conclusion.

## Tell

Publishes a figure nobody can fault and that means the opposite of what it will be read to mean.
Cannot separate deaths caused by the therapy from deaths uncovered by it, since the cohort contains only people the therapy kept alive long enough to have them.
