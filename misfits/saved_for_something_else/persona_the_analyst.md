# Persona: The Analyst

## Taxonomy

[Analyst](position_analyst.md). The one applying the taught rule, correctly, to a case it was not built for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent and unhurried, censoring the subjects it stopped observing exactly as the textbook says, and able to explain why.
Regards censoring as the honest treatment of an unfinished observation, which for administrative loss it is.
Has no view about what killed anyone, that not being the kind of question a survival model asks.

## Action

Runs [Count the Time They Gave You](plan_count_the_time_they_gave_you.md): through [Censoring](process_censoring.md) at [the Risk Set](place_the_risk_set.md), setting [the Censoring](piece_the_censoring.md), and through [Competing](process_competing.md) at the same place, meeting [the Other Death](piece_the_other_death.md).
Counts each subject's contributed time and releases them at their last observation.
Treats a death from another cause the same way it treats a study closing, there being one mechanism in the model for both.

## Shadow

Not sloppy and not ignorant: it knows the assumption censoring rests on and would state it if asked, and the assumption is exactly right for the loss the method was designed around.
Because the model has one mechanism for leaving the data and two quite different things arrive through it, the seat's correct handling of the first is what mishandles the second, and applying the rule more carefully applies it more thoroughly.
It is the seat where a right rule meets a case it does not fit, with nothing in the rule to warn it.

## Tell

Produces a curve in which every subject is accounted for and the incidence of the studied event is above the truth.
Cannot see the difference from inside the model, since a subject who died of something else and a subject whose study ended arrive as the same record, and the model was never given a field that distinguishes them.
