# Plan: Ask If the Treatment Did It

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the regulator's seat](position_regulator.md), inside [Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Regulator](position_regulator.md)

## Direction

Put the question a rising death rate obliges: did the therapy cause these deaths. Require the analysis that answers it, check every figure, and do not shrug, because a regulator that shrugged at a rising death rate in a treated population would be failing at the only thing it exists for. Carry no hostility to the therapy and be glad to clear it if the evidence allows. And when the analysis comes back with every number correct and the question no nearer settled, notice what has happened: asking for more data will return more of the same, because the fault is in what the risk set is taken to contain and not in how much of it there is.

## Orders

([The Regulator](persona_the_regulator.md) reads through [Inflating](process_inflating.md) at [the Safety Review](place_the_safety_review.md), taking [the Inflation](piece_the_inflation.md) as a signal.)

## Implementation

([The Safety Review](place_the_safety_review.md) is where a true count is weighed as evidence of harm; [Inflating](process_inflating.md) has already made the signal, so the room is deciding on numbers that are correct and cannot separate a therapy's success from its harm.)

## Targets

- [ ] the question is put, a rising death rate obliging it
- [ ] the analysis is required, received and checked, and every figure is correct
- [ ] no hostility is carried toward the therapy
- [ ] more data is recognised as returning more of the same
- [ ] the signal and the success are indistinguishable in the evidence held, which is the whole of the difficulty
