# Plot: Did the Treatment Do This

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Safety Review](place_the_safety_review.md), where a rising death rate in a treated population has reached the people whose job is to notice it.

## Action

Through [Inflating](process_inflating.md), [the Regulator](persona_the_regulator.md), from [the regulator's seat](position_regulator.md), on [Ask If the Treatment Did It](plan_ask_if_the_treatment_did_it.md) reads [the Inflation](piece_the_inflation.md) as a signal: more deaths, in a treated population, from a cause the therapy was not meant to touch, reported by clinicians with no motive to exaggerate. The seat asks whether the therapy caused them, which is precisely the question it exists to ask, and commissions the analysis that would answer it. Every figure in that analysis is correct. The scene closes with a safety signal that is real in the numbers and false about the world, and with every check anyone runs on it coming back clean.

## Stage

A regulator that shrugged at a rising death rate would be failing at the one thing it is for, and the room would be glad to clear the therapy if the evidence allowed. The evidence does not allow it, because the evidence is not wrong.

## Tension

Asking for more data returns more of the same, since the fault is in what the risk set is taken to contain rather than in how much of it there is, so the diligence that ought to resolve the question is the diligence that confirms the signal.
