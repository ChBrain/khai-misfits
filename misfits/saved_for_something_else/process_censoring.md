# Process: Censoring

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md). Borne at [The Risk Set](place_the_risk_set.md), precipitated by [The Analyst](persona_the_analyst.md) releasing a subject at its last observation, in [Count the Time and Let Them Go](plot_count_the_time_and_let_them_go.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A subject who stops being observed, and a method built to handle exactly that: the study closed, the calendar ran out, the family moved away.

## Direction

From an unfinished observation toward an honest accounting of it: count the time contributed, release the subject, assume they remain at risk beyond the data.

## Lever

That the assumption is exactly right for administrative loss, so the rule is not an approximation but the correct handling, and there is nothing in it to doubt (Prentice, Kalbfleisch and colleagues).

## Echo

[The Censoring](piece_the_censoring.md), a correct rule sitting at the one door out of the data, waiting for a kind of leaving it was never written for.
