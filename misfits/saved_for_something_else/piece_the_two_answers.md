# Piece: The Two Answers

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md). Cause-specific and subdistribution hazards: both correct, answering different questions, and disagreeing here.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Reanalysis](place_the_reanalysis.md), where the two models are fitted side by side on one dataset (Andersen, Geskus, de Witte and Putter).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing one right analysis, and the play resolves into a lesson about using the correct method, which is a smaller thing and a solved one.
It bears the load as the reason the correction is not an answer. Because no experiment settles which hazard was wanted, the choice is not empirical and cannot be delegated to the data, so an analyst who reports one without naming the question has answered something nobody asked, and the room that commissioned the work is handed a decision rather than a result.

## Apparent

A disagreement between methods, and it looks like the ordinary kind, which is usually resolved by finding out which one was misapplied. Neither was.
