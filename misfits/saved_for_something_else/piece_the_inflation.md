## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfiling is a technicality inside a model that harms nobody, since a bias that changes no decision costs nothing.
It bears the load as the reason the trap reaches the world. Because the rise is genuine and would be found by any observer, it cannot be dismissed as anyone's artefact, and because the patients it is counted in exist only because the therapy kept them alive, the therapy's success and the appearance of its harm are one fact counted twice, with no way to separate them from inside the count.

## Apparent
