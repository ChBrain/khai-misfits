# Position: Regulator

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md). The seat with the clearest public duty and evidence that cannot discharge it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A rising death rate in a treated population, and the standing to demand an explanation for it.
An analysis it commissioned in which every number is correct.
No hostility to the therapy, and every wish to be able to clear it.

## Orders

Ask if the treatment did it: put the question a rising death rate obliges, and require the analysis that answers it.
Do not shrug, since a regulator that shrugged at a rising death rate would be failing at the only thing it exists for.
Accept what the seat does: it is weighing a signal produced by the therapy's success against the possibility of the therapy's harm.

## Loses

Its own remedy, since asking for more data returns more of the same: the fault is in what the risk set is taken to contain, not in how much of it there is.
Because the standard analysis counts a patient dead of another cause as though they might still have had the event, the signal and the success are indistinguishable in the evidence the seat holds.
It keeps the right question and loses any answer the submission can give it.

## Drives

Toward the seat that must decide and cannot,
receiving a correct analysis, checking it, finding it sound, and being no closer than before,
so the question a regulator exists to ask is the question this evidence is built to be unable to answer.
