# Plot: Keep the Dead in the Risk Set

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Reanalysis](place_the_reanalysis.md), after somebody finally asks what the correct analysis would have been.

## Action

Through [Retaining](process_retaining.md), [the Methodologist](persona_the_methodologist.md) on [Name Which Hazard You Want](plan_name_which_hazard_you_want.md) produces [the Ghost](piece_the_ghost.md): the cumulative incidence function, and regression on the subdistribution hazard, estimate the quantity that actually answers the clinical question, and they do it by keeping in the risk set subjects who have already died of the competing cause. Those subjects are not at risk of anything. They are held there so the population arithmetic comes out right. The scene closes with a correct population answer bought at an exact price, a hazard ratio that has no interpretation for any individual patient at all (Jason Fine and Robert Gray).

## Stage

The correction is real, it works, and it is not a compromise reached under pressure. It is the estimate the clinical question was asking for, and it arrives holding its own cost in plain sight, in the methods section, where it will be read as a technicality.

## Tension

What is bought and what is given up are the same operation, so there is no version of the fix that keeps the population answer and returns the individual reading, and the clinician who wanted a number for the patient in front of them leaves without one.
