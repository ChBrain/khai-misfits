# Plan: Count the Time They Gave You

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the analyst's seat](position_analyst.md), inside [Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Analyst](position_analyst.md)

## Direction

Take each subject's contributed time and release them at their last observation, on the assumption that they carry on being at risk beyond the data. This is the honest treatment of an unfinished observation and it is exactly correct for the loss the method was built around: the study closed, the calendar ran out, the family moved away, and none of those has anything to do with whether the person was going to have the event. State the assumption if asked, because you know it. And note the thing the model cannot help you with: a patient who died of another cause arrives through the same mechanism as a study that closed, since there is one way out of the data and no field distinguishing them.

## Orders

([The Analyst](persona_the_analyst.md) censors through [Censoring](process_censoring.md) at [the Risk Set](place_the_risk_set.md), setting [the Censoring](piece_the_censoring.md), and meets [the Other Death](piece_the_other_death.md) through [Competing](process_competing.md) at the same place.)

## Implementation

([The Risk Set](place_the_risk_set.md) is where a subject is either in or out, and where both kinds of leaving look identical; [Censoring](process_censoring.md) applies the correct rule and [Competing](process_competing.md) is the case it was not written for, arriving through the same door.)

## Targets

- [ ] each subject's contributed time is counted and the subject released at last observation
- [ ] the censoring assumption is known and could be stated
- [ ] the rule is applied as taught, being correct for the administrative loss it was designed around
- [ ] a death from another cause arrives through the same mechanism, there being no field that separates them
- [ ] applying the rule more carefully applies it more thoroughly, which is the whole of the difficulty
