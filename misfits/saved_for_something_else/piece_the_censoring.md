## Load Bearing

Remove it and survival analysis cannot handle an unfinished observation at all, which is most of the data most of the time, so the rule is not optional and is not an approximation.
It bears the load as a correct rule in the wrong place. Because the assumption is exactly right for the administrative loss the method was designed around, there is nothing in the rule to doubt and nothing in its application to audit, so applying it more carefully applies it more thoroughly rather than catching the case it does not fit.
