## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play overreaches into a claim it cannot support, that survival analysis is unreliable or that the reported signals were fabricated, neither of which the source literature says and neither of which the house will stage.
It bears the load as the limit that keeps this a design fault. Because the trap needs an event able to remove a subject from risk other than the one being counted, and needs no collider, no confounder and no missing data, it cannot be dismissed as a symptom of weak observational work and would appear in a randomised trial with complete follow-up; and because it lifts wherever the question is stated precisely enough to name which hazard answers it, the cure is a clearer question rather than a better estimate.
