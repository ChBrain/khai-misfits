# Persona: The Methodologist

## Taxonomy

[Methodologist](position_methodologist.md). The one who can fix it and can only fix it by handing back something harder to use.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Precise and slightly unwelcome, arriving after the signal and asking what the risk set is taken to contain.
Concedes at once that the analysis was correctly executed and that nobody made an error.
Does not claim the therapy is safe, only that the analysis on the table cannot say either way.

## Action

Runs [Name Which Hazard You Want](plan_name_which_hazard_you_want.md): through [Retaining](process_retaining.md) at [the Reanalysis](place_the_reanalysis.md), producing [the Ghost](piece_the_ghost.md), and through [Choosing](process_choosing.md) at the same place, holding [the Two Answers](piece_the_two_answers.md) and [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md).
Fits the cumulative incidence function and the subdistribution regression, and gets the answer to the clinical question.
Runs the cause-specific analysis beside it, and gets a different answer that is equally correct.

## Shadow

Not a debunker and not a rescuer: it removes a false reading and hands back a model whose hazard ratio no clinician can apply to a patient, because the risk set that makes the population arithmetic right holds people who are already dead.
Because the two hazards answer different questions and disagree here, the seat cannot deliver one number and be done: it must ask what was being asked, and that is not a question the data contains.
It is the only actor who can end the trap and cannot end it with an answer.

## Tell

Produces two analyses of one dataset that disagree, and can say exactly why, and cannot say which one belongs in the label.
Cannot make the correct estimate interpretable for an individual, since the interpretability was what was spent to buy the correctness.
