# Position: Methodologist

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md). The seat that can end the trap and cannot end it with an answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The same data, and the one question nobody asked: what the risk set is taken to contain.
Two correct analyses, cause-specific and subdistribution, that answer different questions.
No third analysis that answers both, and no experiment that chooses between them.

## Orders

Name which hazard you want: fit the cumulative incidence function and the subdistribution regression, and get the answer to the clinical question.
Run the cause-specific analysis beside it, and report that it is equally correct and points the other way.
Accept what the seat does: it replaces one number with a question about what was being asked.

## Loses

The usability of what it hands back, since the subdistribution risk set retains subjects already dead of the competing cause, so its hazard ratio has no reading for any individual patient.
Because interpretability is precisely what was spent to buy correctness, the seat cannot return both, and the clinician who needs a number for the person in front of them is the one who pays.
It keeps the true population estimate and loses the sentence a label could carry.

## Drives

Toward the only actor who can end it and the only one who must hand back a harder object than it received,
asking what the risk set contains, producing two right answers that disagree, and declining to pretend one of them is the answer,
so the play closes on a choice about the question rather than a correction to the arithmetic.
