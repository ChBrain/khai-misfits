# Persona: The Regulator

## Taxonomy

[Regulator](position_regulator.md). The one asking the right question of a number that cannot answer it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sober and duty-bound, seeing a rising death rate in a treated population and opening the question of whether the treatment caused it.
Can say exactly why the question must be asked: a regulator that shrugged at a rising death rate would be failing at the only thing it is for.
Has no hostility to the therapy and would be pleased to clear it.

## Action

Runs [Ask If the Treatment Did It](plan_ask_if_the_treatment_did_it.md): through [Inflating](process_inflating.md) at [the Safety Review](place_the_safety_review.md), reading [the Inflation](piece_the_inflation.md) as a signal.
Requests the analysis, receives it, and finds every number in it correct.
Weighs a real rise in deaths against a therapy that works, on evidence that will not separate the two.

## Shadow

Not obstructive and not credulous: the question is the right question, the evidence it was given is accurate, and the analysis it commissioned would pass any audit.
Because the standard analysis counts a patient who died of another cause as though they might still have had the event, the signal it is weighing is produced by the therapy's success and is indistinguishable, in the numbers it holds, from the therapy's harm.
It is the seat with the clearest public duty and the one holding evidence that cannot discharge it.

## Tell

Asks for more data and receives more of the same, since the fault is in what the risk set is taken to contain and not in how much of it there is.
Cannot resolve the question without being told which hazard answers it, and nothing in the submission tells it, because the submission does not know the question has two answers.
