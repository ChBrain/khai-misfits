# Piece: The Other Death

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md). The competing event: a removal from risk rather than a pause in observation, permanent and not independent of the outcome.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Risk Set](place_the_risk_set.md), indistinguishable from an administrative exit because the model has no field that separates them.

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by supposing a study in which no subject can die of anything else, and the censoring rule is simply correct and the whole trap dissolves: that is why it does not arise in a short trial with one possible outcome.
It bears the load as the falsified assumption. Because a patient who died of another cause is counted as though they might still have the event, the estimated incidence of the studied event rises above the truth by exactly the amount the model refused to concede, and nothing anywhere in the analysis records that a concession was made.

## Apparent

A censored observation, and it is entered as one: a subject, a last date, and no event, which is what the record has room to say.
