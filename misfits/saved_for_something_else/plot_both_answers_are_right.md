# Plot: Both Answers Are Right

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Reanalysis](place_the_reanalysis.md), with both models now fitted on the same subjects and the same dates, and pointing opposite ways.

## Action

Through [Choosing](process_choosing.md), [the Methodologist](persona_the_methodologist.md) on [Name Which Hazard You Want](plan_name_which_hazard_you_want.md) holds [the Two Answers](piece_the_two_answers.md) and [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): cause-specific hazards answer the aetiological question and the subdistribution answers the question a patient faces, both are correct, and on this dataset they disagree. Neither was misapplied, so the usual repair does not apply. And the scope is stated with the fix rather than after it: the trap needs an event able to remove a subject from risk other than the one being counted, needs no collider and no confounder and no missing data, would appear in a randomised trial with complete follow-up, and lifts wherever the question is stated precisely enough to name which hazard answers it. The scene closes with the analyst obliged to choose, and the choice being a question about what was asked rather than one the data can settle (Andersen, Geskus, de Witte and Putter).

## Stage

This is where the arithmetic ends and the question begins. Nothing further can be computed, no further data would help, and the room that commissioned a result is handed a decision instead.

## Tension

The cure is a clearer question rather than a better estimate, which is the one thing a room asking for a number is not equipped to receive, and the seat that names the limit is the same seat that has just priced the fix.
