# Plot: They Did Not Stop Being Watched

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Risk Set](place_the_risk_set.md) again, and a subject who leaves it by dying of something other than the thing being counted.

## Action

Through [Competing](process_competing.md), [the Analyst](persona_the_analyst.md) on [Count the Time They Gave You](plan_count_the_time_they_gave_you.md) meets [the Other Death](piece_the_other_death.md): a subject, a last date, no event, entered exactly as an administrative exit is entered, because the record has no field that separates them. But this subject did not stop being observed. They stopped being at risk, permanently, for a reason that is not independent of the outcome under study. Censored, they are written down as someone who might still have the event and might not. The scene closes with the estimated incidence of the studied event pushed above the truth by exactly the amount the model refused to concede, and with nothing anywhere in the analysis recording that a concession was made.

## Stage

No step here is a mistake in its own terms. The entry is accurate, the date is right, the rule is the correct one, and the one assumption the whole handling rests on is the one thing that does not hold.

## Tension

The failure is invisible from inside the data, since the two kinds of leaving arrive through one door and are handled by one rule, and a room cannot notice a distinction it has no way to record.
