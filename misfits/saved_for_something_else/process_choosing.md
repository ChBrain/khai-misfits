# Process: Choosing

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md). Borne at [The Reanalysis](place_the_reanalysis.md), precipitated by both analyses being run on one dataset, in [Both Answers Are Right](plot_both_answers_are_right.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two correct models, cause-specific and subdistribution, fitted side by side and disagreeing.

## Direction

From a search for the right number toward a question about what was being asked, since each hazard answers a different question and both answer it correctly.

## Lever

That no experiment settles it, so the choice is not empirical and cannot be delegated to the data, and an analyst who reports one without naming the question has answered something nobody asked (Andersen, Geskus, de Witte and Putter).

## Echo

[The Two Answers](piece_the_two_answers.md) and [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), a room handed a decision instead of a result, and a trap that lifts only once the question is stated precisely enough to name its own hazard.
