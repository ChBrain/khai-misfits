# Plan: Report What the Cohort Shows

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the oncologist's seat](position_oncologist.md), inside [Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Oncologist](position_oncologist.md)

## Direction

Follow the survivor cohort, count the cardiac events, compare them against the historical cohort, and publish what you find. The rise is real, it is substantial, and it would be present for any observer who looked. Do not soften it and do not decline to publish it, because a clinician who suppressed a rise in deaths among their own patients would be concealing something. Take no position on causation: report what was observed and leave the question of what caused it to the people whose job that is. And understand what you cannot do from here, which is separate deaths the therapy caused from deaths it merely uncovered, since this cohort contains only people the therapy kept alive long enough to have them.

## Orders

([The Oncologist](persona_the_oncologist.md) reports through [Inflating](process_inflating.md) at [the Follow-Up Clinic](place_the_follow_up_clinic.md), setting [the Inflation](piece_the_inflation.md).)

## Implementation

([The Follow-Up Clinic](place_the_follow_up_clinic.md) is where a population that exists because of the therapy is counted; [Inflating](process_inflating.md) is the plain arithmetic that everybody dies of something, so removing one cause raises the observed rate of all the rest.)

## Targets

- [ ] the events are counted and compared against the historical cohort
- [ ] the rise is published plainly, without speculation and without softening
- [ ] no position is taken on causation, that being someone else's question
- [ ] the figure would survive any audit, and will be read as harm
- [ ] deaths caused cannot be separated from deaths uncovered, the cohort containing only survivors
