# Position: Oncologist

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md). The seat that supplies a true number to a false conclusion without asserting the conclusion.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A survivor cohort followed for years, and a rise in cardiac events within it that is real and substantial.
A duty to report what the cohort shows, and no licence to soften it.
A population that exists only because the therapy kept it alive.

## Orders

Report what the cohort shows: count the events, compare against the historical cohort, and publish the increase plainly.
Take no position on causation, only on what was observed.
Accept what the seat does: it reports a figure that will be read as harm and that is a consequence of success.

## Loses

Any means of separating deaths caused by the therapy from deaths uncovered by it, since the cohort contains only people the therapy kept alive long enough to have them.
Because the rise would be present for any observer and would survive any audit, the seat cannot qualify the finding without appearing to soften it, and cannot decline to publish without concealing something.
It keeps an unimpeachable count and loses the meaning of it.

## Drives

Toward the seat whose honesty is the mechanism,
counting what happened, comparing it fairly, and writing it up without speculation or softening,
so a number nobody can fault carries a conclusion nobody intended, and the more scrupulously it is reported the more firmly it lands.
