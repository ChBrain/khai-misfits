# Process: Competing

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md). Borne at [The Risk Set](place_the_risk_set.md), precipitated by a patient dying of a cause other than the one being counted, in [They Did Not Stop Being Watched](plot_they_did_not_stop_being_watched.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An event that removes a subject from risk permanently, and that is not independent of the outcome under study.

## Direction

From a subject who has stopped being at risk toward a record that says they stopped being observed, the two being indistinguishable at the one door the model provides.

## Lever

That the censoring assumption is the single thing the handling rests on and is the single thing that does not hold here, so the estimated incidence rises above the truth by exactly what was not conceded.

## Echo

[The Other Death](piece_the_other_death.md), counted as though it might still have the event, and a curve that overstates the thing it was built to measure.
