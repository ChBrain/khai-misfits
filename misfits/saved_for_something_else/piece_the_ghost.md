# Piece: The Ghost

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md). The price of the correction: a risk set that retains subjects already dead of the competing cause.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Reanalysis](place_the_reanalysis.md), inside the subdistribution model, holding people who are not at risk of anything (Jason Fine and Robert Gray).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no way to estimate the quantity that answers the clinical question, since the cumulative incidence a patient actually faces is what the retained subjects are there to make come out right.
It bears the load as the no-free-hero, and unusually exactly. Because the retained subjects are held in the risk set purely for the arithmetic, the hazard ratio the model produces has no interpretation for any individual patient at all, so a correct population answer is bought by giving up the ability to say what the model means for the person in front of you.
