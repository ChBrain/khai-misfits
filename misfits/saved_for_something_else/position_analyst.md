# Position: Analyst

## Taxonomy

[Saved for Something Else](play_saved_for_something_else.md). The seat where a right rule meets a case it does not fit, with nothing in the rule to warn it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

One mechanism for a subject leaving the data, and two quite different things arriving through it.
A censoring assumption it knows and would state: that the released subject carries on being at risk beyond the observation.
No field anywhere in the model distinguishing a study that closed from a patient who died of something else.

## Orders

Count the time they gave you: take each subject's contributed time, release them at their last observation, and assume they remain at risk.
Apply the rule as taught, since for administrative loss it is exactly correct and is the honest treatment of an unfinished observation.
Accept what the seat does: it handles the second kind of loss the way it handles the first, there being one mechanism for both.

## Loses

Any way to see the difference from inside the model, since the two losses arrive as the same record.
Because the assumption is right for the case the method was designed around, applying the rule more carefully applies it more thoroughly, so diligence deepens the error rather than catching it.
It keeps a curve in which every subject is accounted for and loses the truth of the incidence it reports.

## Drives

Toward the seat that is correct in its handling and wrong in its result,
counting the time, releasing the subject, and writing down that a dead patient might still have the event,
so the incidence of the studied event stands above the truth by exactly the amount the model refused to concede.
