# Plot: It Pushes the Sheet Out

## Taxonomy

[Right Apart, Wrong Together](play_right_apart_wrong_together.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A century on. The shellac disintegrated, the asbestos dry and porous, the salt air through, and the cell running at every touching place.

## Action

[Giving way](process_giving_way.md) and [growing thicker](process_growing_thicker.md): [The Inner Rib](persona_the_inner_rib.md), holding [Inner Rib](position_inner_rib.md), wastes to as little as a third of its thickness while its rust swells some bars to twice their original size.

## Stage

[The Pushed Line](piece_the_pushed_line.md) appears on the outer face, following the ribs beneath, and the silhouette of the work begins to change.

## Tension

The first visible damage in the whole production is on the member that was being protected.
The consumed part is invisible, inside, under a fitting, still carrying load and still passing as a material. The preserved part is on the outside, where everyone can see it, going out of shape under the pressure of the other one's remains. So the evidence points precisely at the wrong component, and reads to anyone looking at it as a problem with the skin.
