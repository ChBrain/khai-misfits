# Piece: The Parting Layer

## Taxonomy

[The Touching Place](place_the_touching_place.md). The guard against exactly this, with a life shorter than the work.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Between saddle and rib at every [touching place](place_the_touching_place.md), inside, where it cannot be seen once the sheet is closed.

## Load Bearing

Without it the misfit is ignorance, and it is not.
It is the piece that makes the structure structural: shellac-impregnated asbestos was specified against this exact mechanism by people who understood it, so what failed was never the knowledge, only the assumption that a remedy installed once stays installed.

## Apparent

A thin separator, doing nothing mechanical, carrying no load, and required to be present rather than to perform.
Correctly specified and correctly fitted.

## Yearbook

Sound for decades. Then the shellac disintegrating and the asbestos going dry and porous, over a century, in the dark, at a rate nobody measured.
Never inspected, because nothing that carries no load is on an inspection list, and it was the only thing standing between the two metals the entire time.
