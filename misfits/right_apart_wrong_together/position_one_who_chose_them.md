# Position: One Who Chose Them

## Taxonomy

[Right Apart, Wrong Together](play_right_apart_wrong_together.md). The seat that specifies each part correctly and owns no part of the joint.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Two requirements and the knowledge to meet both: a skin that must be thin, formable and weatherproof, a frame that must be strong, stiff and affordable.
Knowledge of the couple, and a remedy for it.

## Orders

Specify copper for the skin and iron for the frame, each on its merits.
Draw [the parting layer](piece_the_parting_layer.md) between them, against exactly this mechanism.
Sign off an assembly in which every part is right.

## Loses

The ability to give the guard the same life as the work.
The knowledge is present and the remedy installed, and nothing anywhere asks how long the remedy lasts, so the real design life of the assembly quietly becomes the service life of a consumable buried in a joint, unstated, unwatched and unowned.

## Drives

Toward two selections that are each correct and would be made the same way today, and into a property that belongs to the pair, which is the one thing neither specification describes and no component test can reach.
