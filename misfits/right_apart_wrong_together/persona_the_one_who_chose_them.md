# Persona: The One Who Chose Them

## Taxonomy

[One Who Chose Them](position_one_who_chose_them.md). Specifies each material correctly for its own job, and knows about the couple.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Rigorous, and informed. Picks copper for a skin that must be thin, formable and stand a century of weather, and iron for a frame that must be strong, stiff and affordable in quantity.
Knows what happens when the two touch in damp, which is why the drawings carry an insulator.

## Action

Specifies both metals, on their merits, for their jobs.
Draws [the parting layer](piece_the_parting_layer.md) between them, of shellac-impregnated asbestos, against exactly this mechanism.
Signs off an assembly in which every part is right and the risk has been addressed.

## Shadow

Cannot give the guard the same life as the work.
The knowledge is present and correct and the remedy is installed, and what is not asked anywhere is how long the remedy lasts: so the true design life of the assembly becomes the service life of a consumable buried inside a joint, and nobody writes that number down or watches it.

## Tell

Says "the metals are insulated from each other", in the present tense, about a condition that is true on the day it is said and is not a property of the structure.
