# Plot: Down to the Ribs

## Taxonomy

[Right Apart, Wrong Together](play_right_apart_wrong_together.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The mechanism understood again, a century later, and three answers on the table: insulate afresh, replace the frame, or hang an anode and feed it forever.

## Action

[Giving way](process_giving_way.md) ended by changing one member of the pair: [The One Who Chose Them](persona_the_one_who_chose_them.md), holding [One Who Chose Them](position_one_who_chose_them.md), opens the work and takes the ribs out.

## Stage

[The One Who Chose Them](persona_the_one_who_chose_them.md) tables [Take It Apart](plan_take_it_apart.md): the wasted members removed, replacements fitted in stainless steel, and a fresh separator of a different material put in behind them.

## Tension

Every answer works and each one either dismantles the thing or subscribes to the problem.
Insulating again installs a consumable in a place nobody can inspect, and hands the next century the same unanswered question about how long it lasts. Replacing the frame means opening the entire work, which is what it took. And the sacrificial anode accepts the mechanism outright, choosing a member to be destroyed on purpose and replacing it for as long as the structure stands, which is not a cure but a subscription.
