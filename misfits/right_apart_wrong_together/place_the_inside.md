# Place: The Inside

## Taxonomy

[Right Apart, Wrong Together](play_right_apart_wrong_together.md). The interior, where the process runs and nobody looks.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Ribs and saddles, the back of the sheet, and a great deal of dark.
Nothing changing at any speed a visit would register.

## Holds

Every touching place in the assembly, all of them behind the sheet.
[The Parting Layer](piece_the_parting_layer.md), ageing, in the one location where its ageing cannot be observed.

## Offers

Access, in principle, to anyone who climbs in with a light.
Nothing that would look wrong to them on any single visit, for the first several decades.

## Withheld

A rate. There is no way to see how far along it is.
The metal loss is at the contact faces, under fittings, and the only signal it produces is one that will appear on the outside much later, so the place where the whole production happens is a place that reports nothing while it is happening.
