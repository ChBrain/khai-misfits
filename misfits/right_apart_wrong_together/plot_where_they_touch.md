# Plot: Where They Touch

## Taxonomy

[Right Apart, Wrong Together](play_right_apart_wrong_together.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Touching Place](place_the_touching_place.md), repeated hundreds of times: a saddle riveted to the sheet and closed around the rib.

## Action

[Setting them together](process_setting_them_together.md): [The Outer Sheet](persona_the_outer_sheet.md) and [The Inner Rib](persona_the_inner_rib.md), holding [Outer Sheet](position_outer_sheet.md) and [Inner Rib](position_inner_rib.md), are brought into contact by [the saddle](piece_the_saddle.md).

## Stage

The work stands. The sheet is held in shape against wind and its own weight, exactly as intended, by a connection any engineer would call sound.

## Tension

The connection the structure requires is the connection the cell requires.
Fastening two parts together means putting them in contact, so there is no version of this assembly that holds the sheet up and does not join the metals. A property now exists that belongs to the pair and to neither member, and it has no part number, no supplier and no line in any specification, because it is not a component.
