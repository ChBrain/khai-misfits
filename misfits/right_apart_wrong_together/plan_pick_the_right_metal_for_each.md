# Plan: Pick the Right Metal for Each

## Taxonomy

A **personal scheme**: the intent of [The One Who Chose Them](persona_the_one_who_chose_them.md), inside [Right Apart, Wrong Together](play_right_apart_wrong_together.md).

## Owner

## Direction

Specify each part on its merits. The skin must be thin, formable and able to stand a century of weather, so copper. The frame must be strong, stiff and affordable in quantity, so iron. Let each requirement choose its own material and do not compromise either.

## Orders

(The One Who Chose Them writes the requirement for each part separately, selects copper for the sheet and iron for the ribs, and defends both selections on their own terms, which hold.)
