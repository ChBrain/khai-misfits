# Position: Outer Sheet

## Taxonomy

[Right Apart, Wrong Together](play_right_apart_wrong_together.md). The seat that is preserved, and disfigured by the preserving.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The right properties for a skin, and a century of weather to stand.
A position on the outside, where everything about it is visible.

## Orders

Take the shape and hold it.
Weather, and protect what is behind.

## Loses

Its shape, to the thing that is protecting it.
The rust replacing the iron occupies more room than the iron did, so the wasting member swells and jacks it outward, and the seat cannot decline the protection or the deformation that comes with it.

## Drives

Toward being the only visible member, and therefore the one that appears to have failed, when what has appeared on its surface is the record of another part being consumed on its behalf.
