# Persona: The Outer Sheet

## Taxonomy

[Outer Sheet](position_outer_sheet.md). The nobler metal: preserved by the cell, and deformed by its preservation.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Thin, formable, and weatherproof. Copper, which takes a shape and holds it, and which weathers to a surface that protects itself.
Exactly right for the job, and admired.

## Action

Takes the shape it was made to take and holds it for a century.
Is protected, at every touching place, by the metal behind it giving way instead.

## Shadow

Cannot decline the protection, and is disfigured by it.
The rust that replaces the iron occupies more room than the iron did, so the wasting member swells and pushes outward, and the first visible damage in the whole production appears on the surface of the part that the cell was preserving.

## Tell

Shows [the pushed line](piece_the_pushed_line.md) on its face and is read as the thing that has gone wrong, being the only member anyone can see.
