# Piece: The Pushed Line

## Taxonomy

[The Touching Place](place_the_touching_place.md). The deformation on the outside: the first visible sign, on the preserved member.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the outer face of the sheet, following the ribs beneath, visible from outside the work once it is pronounced enough.

## Load Bearing

Without it nothing surfaces at all, and with it the surfacing points at the wrong part.
It is the piece that carries the asymmetry: rust occupies more room than the metal it replaced, so the consumed member's product deforms the protected member, and the only evidence anyone can see is on the surface of the thing the cell was preserving.

## Apparent

A line, a bulge, a seam standing slightly proud of where the drawings put it.
Read, by anyone looking at the outside, as a problem with the skin.

## Yearbook

Absent for most of the century, while the whole of the process ran.
Present at the end, following the ribs exactly, and pointing precisely at the members that are no longer there.
