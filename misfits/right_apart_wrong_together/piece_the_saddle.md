# Piece: The Saddle

## Taxonomy

[The Touching Place](place_the_touching_place.md). The fitting that holds sheet to frame, and therefore makes the contact.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Riveted flush to the back of the sheet and closed around the rib, at [the touching place](place_the_touching_place.md), repeated across the whole assembly.

## Load Bearing

Without it the sheet is not held and the work does not stand, and with it the two metals are joined.
It is the piece that shows the contact is not incidental: fastening two parts together means putting them in contact, so the connection the structure requires is the same connection the cell requires, and there is no version of the assembly that has one without the other.

## Apparent

Copper, formed and riveted, entirely conventional.
Correctly executed, hundreds of times, and admired by anyone who looks at the workmanship.

## Yearbook

Installed at construction and never altered.
Still holding, at the end, when the rib inside it has been reduced to a third of its thickness and swollen to twice its size, having done its own job faultlessly throughout.
