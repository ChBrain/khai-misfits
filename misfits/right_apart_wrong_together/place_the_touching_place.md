# Place: The Touching Place

## Taxonomy

[Right Apart, Wrong Together](play_right_apart_wrong_together.md). Where the two metals meet, and where the property that belongs to neither exists.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A saddle riveted to the sheet and closed around the rib, repeated hundreds of times over the whole assembly.
Neat, conventional, and correctly executed.

## Holds

[The Saddle](piece_the_saddle.md), which is what fastening means and therefore what contact means.
[The Parting Layer](piece_the_parting_layer.md), between the two, for as long as it lasts.

## Offers

A sound mechanical connection, exactly as designed, holding the sheet in shape against wind and its own weight.

## Withheld

Any separation between the metals that does not depend on a consumable.
The joint must transmit load, which means the parts must bear on each other, so the only thing standing between them is a material that is there to be nothing, and a material that is there to be nothing has no structural reason to be inspected.
