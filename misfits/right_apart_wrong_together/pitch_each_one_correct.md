# Pitch: Each One Correct

## Taxonomy

[Right Apart, Wrong Together](play_right_apart_wrong_together.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact and admiring. Good material science and a beautiful assembly: a thin formable skin over a strong stiff frame, fitted by people who knew what they were doing and who thought about this.
Every decision in it holds up. Any of them would be made the same way today.

## Undertow

Under the exactness, a cell nobody built.
It has no part number, no supplier and no specification, because it is not a component: it is what two components are when they touch, and it runs continuously in a place nobody can see, for a hundred years, on electricity nobody supplied.

## Nerve

The particular discomfort of an assembly in which every part passes and the assembly does not.

## Echo

Afterward: a bar wasted to a third of itself, a skin pushed out of shape by the rust of the thing that was holding it, a layer of insulation that did its job for as long as it lasted, and a specification in which every line is still correct.
