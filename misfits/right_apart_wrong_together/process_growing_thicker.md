# Process: Growing Thicker

## Taxonomy

[Right Apart, Wrong Together](play_right_apart_wrong_together.md). The corrosion product taking more room than the metal it replaced.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Iron converted to rust at the contact faces, under fittings, with nowhere to expand except outward.
[The Inner Rib](persona_the_inner_rib.md), holding [Inner Rib](position_inner_rib.md), inside [the touching place](place_the_touching_place.md).

## Direction

Outward, against the sheet, with the force of a material occupying more volume than its parent.
Some bars swelling to twice their original size.

## Lever

Volume. The one quantity in the whole production that increases while everything else is lost.

## Echo

[The Pushed Line](piece_the_pushed_line.md) standing proud on the outside, following the ribs exactly, and the silhouette of the work beginning to change.
