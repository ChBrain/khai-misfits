# Persona: The Inner Rib

## Taxonomy

[Inner Rib](position_inner_rib.md). The less noble metal: consumed, out of sight, while doing its job perfectly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Strong, stiff, cheap in quantity, and entirely fit for a frame.
Would pass any test put to it, at any point in the century, as a material.

## Action

Carries the load it was specified to carry, continuously and without complaint.
Gives way at the touching places, atom by atom, because that is what the less noble member of a couple does.

## Shadow

Cannot be examined where it matters.
The wasting happens at the joint, on the inside, under a fitting, in a place with no line of sight and no access, so a member reduced to a third of its thickness is still carrying load and still invisible, and nothing about its own specification is implicated at any point.

## Tell

Is found, when it is finally uncovered, both far thinner and far thicker than it was: metal gone, and the rust that replaced it swollen to twice the original size.
