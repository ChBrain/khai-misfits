# Persona: The Damp

## Taxonomy

[Damp](position_damp.md). The third condition, supplied free by the setting.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Salt air off a harbour, and weather. Ordinary, daily, and nobody's doing.
Not a contaminant, not a failure, and not something anyone could have declined to install.

## Action

Arrives, and keeps arriving.
Finds its way through [the parting layer](piece_the_parting_layer.md) once the shellac has gone and the asbestos is dry and porous, and closes the circuit.

## Shadow

Cannot be excluded for as long as the structure stands.
Every barrier against it is a consumable and every consumable has a life, so the only real question is which ages first, and the setting has no deadline while everything installed against it does.

## Tell

Requires nothing and does nothing visible, and is one of the three things the cell needs, the only one that arrives without a drawing.
