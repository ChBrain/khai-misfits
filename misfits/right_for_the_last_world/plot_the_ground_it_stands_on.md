# Plot: The Ground It Stands On

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Chart](place_the_chart.md), the shape of the place drawn out, seats and the paths between them, each path already carrying someone who climbed it.
A vacancy opening at the top of one path, and a rule already in place for who is next.

## Action

[Hardening](process_hardening.md): [The Auditor](persona_the_auditor.md), holding [Auditor](position_auditor.md)'s seat, writes the credited read into the criteria the Chart promotes by, while [The Successor](persona_the_successor.md), holding [Successor](position_successor.md)'s seat, rises by exactly those criteria.

## Stage

[The Auditor](persona_the_auditor.md), on [Read the Record](plan_read_the_record.md), fixes [The Rulebook](piece_the_rulebook.md) into the Chart's own promotion path, so following it well is no longer one way to do the job, it is the job.
[The Successor](persona_the_successor.md), having tabled [Earn the Rung](plan_earn_the_rung.md), climbs the path exactly as drawn and arrives at the top holding a record that says, correctly, that the path works.

## Tension

The Successor's whole career is evidence for the very thing that will need to be argued against later.
There is nothing to fault in the climb: the criteria were real, the Successor met them honestly, rung by rung, and every rung is a fact, not a favour. But a person who rose by proving a structure sound cannot be asked to doubt it on the same footing as someone who never climbed it, because doubting it means doubting the ground their whole record stands on, and no one can be expected to find that an easy thing to weigh evenly.
