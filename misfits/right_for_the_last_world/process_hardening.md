# Process: Hardening

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md). The turning of a credited structure into the criteria the place is run and promoted by.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A read that has already been credited and needs no further defending.
At [the Chart](place_the_chart.md), the Auditor writing the credited terms into the path a seat is won by, and a first climber meeting them.

## Direction

Toward a structure no longer held by argument, only by position.
Because the criteria are drawn straight from a result already certified, the hardening runs toward permanence, each promotion made on the same terms adding one more person whose own standing depends on the terms having been right, so the process does not merely keep the structure in place, it recruits its future defenders.

## Lever

A career built honestly on the criteria it hardens.
Because the Successor's climb is real, met rung by rung and not handed over, the hardening is driven by genuine merit within its own terms, and the lever is that very genuineness: a criteria proven fair is a criteria no one who met it fairly wants to see loosened.

## Echo

A structure that is no longer a choice anyone remembers making.
The path is staffed, the seats are filled by people who earned them on its terms, and what began as one answer to one requirement is now simply how the place works, so the hardening leaves behind an arrangement that any later challenge to the form must also challenge every career standing on it.
