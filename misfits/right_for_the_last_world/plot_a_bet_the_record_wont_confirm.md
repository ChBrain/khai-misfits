# Plot: A Bet the Record Won't Confirm

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Review](place_the_review.md), the same room the first Scorecard was read aloud in, called again, this time on [The Backlog](piece_the_backlog.md)'s account rather than the Scorecard's.
The numbers on the wall still reading clean, and the case for the room being here at all resting on nothing the wall shows.

## Action

[Abandoning](process_abandoning.md): [The Scout](persona_the_scout.md), holding [Scout](position_scout.md)'s seat, argues for dismantling the arrangement ahead of any proof the Scorecard will ever produce, while [The Successor](persona_the_successor.md), holding [Successor](position_successor.md)'s seat, argues against it, correctly, from a record the structure is still producing.

## Stage

[The Scout](persona_the_scout.md), on [Move Before Proof](plan_move_before_proof.md), lays out [The Backlog](piece_the_backlog.md) and names it for what the Rulebook cannot: [The Overhang](piece_the_overhang.md), a structure still executing well, aimed at a market that has already moved.
[The Successor](persona_the_successor.md), still on [Earn the Rung](plan_earn_the_rung.md), sets [The Scorecard](piece_the_scorecard.md) beside it, clean as ever, and asks the room to say, honestly, which document it trusts.

## Tension

Both figures are reading real evidence, and the evidence points two ways.
There is no bad faith on either side of the table: the Scout's read of the Backlog is sound, and the Successor's read of the Scorecard is sound, and the room cannot settle which one describes the world as it now stands, because the only instrument built to measure that question is the Scorecard, and the Scorecard is the very thing on trial. The room closes on a decision, or does not, and either way it closes without the one piece of evidence that would have made the decision easy: a number the old form was ever built to produce.
