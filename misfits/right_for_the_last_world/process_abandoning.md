# Process: Abandoning

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md). The dismantling of a hardened structure on a read of the market that no scorecard has yet confirmed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A Backlog grown past what any single exception explains.
At [the Review](place_the_review.md), the Scout bringing the count forward and asking the room to act on it ahead of the Scorecard.

## Direction

Toward dismantling arrangements that the structure's own success built, before the numbers that built them say to.
Because the only proof the room fully trusts is produced by the very structure now in question, the abandoning has to run without that proof, so it drives toward a decision made on a different kind of evidence entirely, one the place has never had to weigh before.

## Lever

A bet against the exact record the structure is still correctly producing.
Because the Scorecard keeps reading clean and the Successor's record keeps being real, the abandoning is not driven by any failure in either, it is driven by a claim that both are measuring the wrong thing now, and the lever is whether the room will act on that claim before it can be shown.

## Echo

A structure dismantled ahead of its own proof, or held exactly as it was.
Either way, the room closes on a decision made without the one document that would have made it easy, so what the abandoning leaves behind is either a correction nobody can yet justify by the numbers, or a structure held for another quarter on numbers that were never built to answer the question that was actually asked.
