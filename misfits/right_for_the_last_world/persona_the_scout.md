# Persona: The Scout

## Taxonomy

[Scout](position_scout.md). Meets the market at the boundary, where the case arrives before it ever reaches a scorecard, and reads a shift no instrument in the building has confirmed yet.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Practical. A little worn. Handles what comes rather than what the manual says should come.
Sees a case that does not fit the channel and forces it through anyway, because the case is in front of it and the channel is not going to widen itself.
No alarmism in it: only the flat, repeated experience of things taking longer to force than the Rulebook says they should.

## Action

Logs every hard case as an exception, because that is the box the Rulebook provides, and closes it well, because closing it badly would help no one.
Keeps its own count, off the books, of how often the hard case is turning up.
Brings that count to the Review room when the count stops looking like noise.

## Shadow

Being closest to where the market actually is does not make its word count as evidence.
The Backlog it carries is real, but it is not the instrument the place was built to trust, so however plainly the Scout can see the shape of what is changing, it cannot make that seeing arrive as proof, only as a claim set against a Scorecard that keeps reading clean, and it is the Scout's own good handling of every single hard case that keeps the count from ever forcing itself onto that Scorecard.
It is right early, which in this structure is indistinguishable, for a long while, from being wrong.

## Tell

Says "something's taking longer than it should, and it isn't one thing anymore."
Says it before it can point to a number for it, because the number that would prove it does not exist inside a structure built to prove the opposite.
