# Process: Fitting

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md). The building of a structure to match a real requirement, and its being correct at the moment it is built.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A requirement in the Market steady enough to build against.
At [the Market](place_the_market.md), the Steward reckoning that a rule for the order and a channel for the exception will answer what is actually arriving, and being right that it does.

## Direction

Toward a structure cut to exactly what is being asked for.
Because the requirement really is stable enough to design around, the fitting runs toward precision, a rule tuned to this shape of demand and no other, so the structure that results is not a rough approximation kept because nothing better exists, it is a close match, built because a close match was possible.

## Lever

A requirement that will hold still long enough to be worth answering exactly.
Because the market is not yet moving under the structure being raised against it, the fitting is rewarded for precision rather than punished for it, and the process turns on that stillness holding, not on any error in reading it while it does.

## Echo

A structure that answers the requirement as well as a structure can.
The first results land in the shape the Market asked for, and the fit stands proven in its own terms, so what looks, in the moment, like a job finished is, unseen, the opening of a structure's whole future dependence on a requirement that was never promised to hold still.
