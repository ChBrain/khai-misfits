# Piece: The Rulebook

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md). The codified structure itself: a rule for the order, a channel for the exception, a chain of command for the case that needs a decision above the floor.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Chart](place_the_chart.md), where it sets the seats and the paths between them.
Carried onto [the Market](place_the_market.md) every time a case arrives and is answered by whatever channel the Rulebook provides for it.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is no structure, only whoever happens to be handling a case deciding on the spot how to handle it.
With it the whole mechanism has its instrument: a form specific enough to answer a real, repeated requirement precisely, and general enough to be taught, checked, and promoted by. The Rulebook is load bearing because it is the mechanistic form itself (Burns and Stalker): a structure built for a stable, predictable environment, and precisely because it is built for that shape of environment, it carries no provision within itself for recognising a different one, so the piece is both the whole reason the fit worked and the whole reason the fit's ending goes unread.

## Apparent

A manual, dry, procedural, unremarkable, the kind of document nobody reads twice.
Seen day to day, the Rulebook looks like plain infrastructure, and the truth that it is the exact shape of the last world's requirement, frozen, still being followed after the requirement it was shaped to has moved, stays hidden behind how ordinary a rulebook is supposed to look.

## Yearbook

Raised by [the Steward](persona_the_steward.md) through [fitting](process_fitting.md), against a requirement that was genuinely there.
It is what [crediting](process_crediting.md) certifies as sound rather than merely matched, what [hardening](process_hardening.md) fixes into the Chart's own promotion path, and the exact shape [the Backlog](piece_the_backlog.md) is measured against every time a case does not fit it.
