# Pitch: Don't Fix What Isn't Broken

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Confident, competent, plainly vindicated.
The steady sense that a structure doing exactly what it was built to do, and proving it every quarter, has earned the right to keep doing it. Nothing defensive in it: just the calm of a case that keeps being made in your favour.

## Undertow

Under the confidence, an instrument that can only ever confirm itself.
Because the scorecard was built by the same fit it is now asked to judge, every reading it returns is a reading in the form's own terms, so the very evidence that earns the trust is evidence that could never have said otherwise, whether the world outside still matches it or not.

## Nerve

The comfort of a number that keeps coming back clean,
and the thin, hard-to-place unease of a case or two lately that took longer than a case like that should,
filed as an exception, closed, and never once counted against the number that stayed clean.

## Echo

After the run: a structure executing better than it ever has, a scorecard still reading exactly right,
and a question nobody inside is positioned to ask, because asking it would mean doubting the very numbers that ask nothing:
right for what, and for how much longer.
