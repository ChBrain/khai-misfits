# Process: Crediting

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md). The reading of a good result back to the structure that produced it, rather than to the match between the structure and the moment.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A result that lands the way the requirement predicted it would.
At [the Review](place_the_review.md), the Auditor asking what produced the number, and finding, honestly, the structure standing there.

## Direction

Toward the structure itself, and away from the fit that made it work.
Because a result cannot say, on its own terms, whether it reflects a sound design or a design that merely still matches its moment, the crediting has nowhere else correct to point, so it runs toward the structure, every time, not from carelessness but because the structure is the only cause the number is built to show.

## Lever

A number that cannot distinguish soundness from fit.
Because the Scorecard measures output and not the match producing it, the crediting is driven by the honest limits of its own instrument, and the process turns on exactly that limit, not on any wish to overstate what the structure has proven.

## Echo

A structure certified, not merely matched.
The credit is entered, the room agrees, and what was earned as a fit for this quarter is carried forward as a soundness good for the next, so the crediting leaves behind a form now trusted on terms wider than the ones it was actually tested on.
