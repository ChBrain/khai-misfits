# Position: Scout

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md). The seat closest to the market as it now runs, and answers for what it notices there before any scorecard does.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Direct contact with the case as it actually arrives, before it is filed into a channel.
A count of its own, kept outside the structure's own instruments.
No standing to make that count count as proof, only to keep it and bring it forward.

## Orders

Handle every hard case well, whatever it costs to force it through.
Log it as the structure provides for, and no further.
Bring the pattern forward when the pattern stops looking like noise.

## Loses

The power to turn a true observation into a decision-grade one, exactly because the seat sits outside the instrument the place trusts.
Because handling each hard case well is also what keeps it from ever forcing itself onto the Scorecard, the seat's own competence is what erases the evidence its own position is best placed to gather, so it loses the very thing its vantage would otherwise be worth.
It ends the run holding the clearest read in the building and the least standing to make anyone else act on it.

## Drives

Toward a claim that is true and cannot yet be proven,
right about the shape of what is coming and unable to certify it,
so the seat closest to the market is the seat furthest from the one kind of evidence the place will act on.
