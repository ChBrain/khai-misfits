# Play: Right for the Last World

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Right for the Last World.
A market asks for something specific: high volume, routine work, the same order repeated at scale, and an organisation answers it the way that market asks to be answered, with a rule for the order, a channel for the exception, a chain of command for the case that needs a decision above the floor. The answer works. Costs fall, throughput climbs, the numbers come back exactly the shape the market wanted, and every one of those numbers is read, correctly, as proof that the answer was right. So the answer hardens: the rule becomes the manual, the manual becomes the way a career is built inside the place, and the chain of command becomes the only chain there is. Nobody chose badly and nobody is coasting. The market that called this structure into being does not hold still, though, and it turns to something else, faster, less routine, less patient with a rule cut for a different world, and it turns without sending word, because the turning happens out there, not in here, and nothing in here has changed to register it. The structure keeps running exactly as well as it ever did. That is the whole of the misfit: not a broken machine, a working one, aimed now at a world that has already left.

## Arc

The bend is this: the very quality that makes a form worth keeping is the quality that makes it impossible to leave in time.
It runs on a fit that is real, not imagined.
Some matches between a structure and its environment are lucky guesses, replaceable the moment the luck runs out. This one is not: the market genuinely asked for routine, predictability, and repeatable output, and the mechanistic answer, a rule for the order and a channel for every case, genuinely delivered it, so the reward that followed was earned, not awarded on faith.
And so the reward is trusted, and trust hardens into structure: the manual is written down, the chain of command is staffed, and the people who rise are the people who rose by following it, so every promotion becomes one more vote that the form is simply how the work is done here. None of it is complacency. Reading a scorecard that says the numbers are good and concluding that the structure producing them is sound is exactly the correct inference, drawn correctly, every time it is drawn, for as long as the scorecard is measuring a world that still wants what it always wanted.
The scorecard, though, measures only what it was built to measure, and the world it was built to measure has moved. The volume that was routine is not routine anymore, the case that used to fit the channel now takes the channel apart to force itself through, and every one of those frictions gets absorbed at the point where it happens, logged as an exception, handled by hand, and never once summed into a number the scorecard was built to read. So the turn arrives and produces no signal that the instrument built for the last world is able to register, because the instrument was built to be exactly that precise, and precision cut to one shape is blindness to every other.
What is left standing at the end is not failure. It is a structure executing its own design flawlessly, faster and cleaner than it has ever run, aimed at a requirement that used to exist. Correcting it means dismantling arrangements that its own success built, careers that its own criteria made, on a read of the new world that no scorecard has yet confirmed, against people who can point, correctly, at the numbers the old world's structure is still producing. There is no cost-free version of this. The cure is to leave the form before the numbers say to leave it, and the numbers, by the very design that made the form work, are the last thing in the building that will ever say so.

## Triggers

**[The Fit](plot_the_fit.md)**
The market is running at a volume steady enough that a rule written for it this quarter is still the right rule next quarter. The Steward reads the requirement and raises the Rulebook to match it, the Auditor watching the first cases come back. Closes when the structure is in place and its first results land in exactly the shape the market asked for.

**[The Proof](plot_the_proof.md)**
Cue: the first full quarter's numbers are in, and the room exists to ask one question of them. The Auditor reads the results and credits them to the structure itself, not merely to the match, and the Steward receives the credit the same way. Closes when the form is certified sound rather than merely fitted.

**[The Ground It Stands On](plot_the_ground_it_stands_on.md)**
Cue: a certified form becomes the standard the place is run by, and a seat opens with a rule already set for who is next. The Auditor writes the credited read into the promotion criteria, and the Successor rises by exactly those criteria. Closes when the form is no longer a choice anyone remembers making, only the way advancement works.

**[Nothing Inside Has Changed](plot_nothing_inside_has_changed.md)**
Cue: a case arrives at the boundary that the Rulebook was not built for, while nothing on the Chart and nothing in the Review room has changed since the day the first Scorecard was read aloud. The Scout meets the case and forces it through by hand, while the Steward keeps running the Rulebook exactly as written. Closes when the exception is logged, the Scorecard reads clean, and the Backlog is one entry heavier with nothing to show for it upstream.

**[A Bet the Record Won't Confirm](plot_a_bet_the_record_wont_confirm.md)**
Cue: the Backlog has grown past what any single exception explains, and the Review room is called again, this time on its account rather than the Scorecard's. The Scout argues for dismantling the arrangement ahead of any proof the Scorecard will ever produce, and the Successor argues against it, correctly, from a record the structure is still producing. Closes when the room decides, or does not, on evidence that will not exist until after the decision is made.

## Stakes

Whether a structure that is executing correctly can ever be told, from the inside, that it has become the wrong structure, or whether the very success that built it is what keeps that news from arriving in a form the structure was ever built to read.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a fit that was earned and is now outrun, and away from a correction that no instrument in the building can yet certify. The production cannot make the Scorecard measure a market it was never built to measure, because the Scorecard is itself a product of the fit it is now being asked to judge, so more good numbers only ever deepen the case for the form and never once test whether the form still answers the world outside it. It can only show that no one erred: the Steward was right to build, the Auditor was right to credit, the Successor was right to climb, and the Scout is right to worry without yet being able to prove it. And it can show that the one move that frees the structure, leaving the form before the numbers confirm the need to, must be made against the exact evidence the structure is still correctly, honestly producing, so the correction is a bet, and the bet is the hardest thing in the whole run to place.
