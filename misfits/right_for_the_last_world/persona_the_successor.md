# Persona: The Successor

## Taxonomy

[Successor](position_successor.md). Rose through the criteria the hardened form set, and holds, honestly, a record that says the form works.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Earned. Steady. Answers a challenge to the structure with a record, not a defence.
Climbed the path the Chart laid out rung by rung, and every rung is a fact: a target met, a case closed, a review passed.
No entitlement in it: the seat was won on the same terms it is now held by.

## Action

Runs the structure the way it was run when the running proved it out.
Points, when asked, to the Scorecard behind the seat, because the seat and the Scorecard were built by the same climb.
Trains the next rung of hires on the path that raised this one.

## Shadow

A record built by proving a structure sound cannot easily be turned around to doubt the structure it proved.
Because the Successor's whole standing rests on the criteria having been the right criteria, doubting the criteria now means doubting the ground under the seat itself, so the resistance to a claim against the form is not defensiveness, it is the same honest reading of the evidence that built the career, applied consistently, to a moment where the evidence has quietly stopped being enough.
It is the person best placed, by every fact of the record, to be the last one able to see past it.

## Tell

Says "I got here by following it, and it hasn't let me down yet."
Says it while holding the same Scorecard that raised it, offered now as the answer to a question the Scorecard was never asked to answer.
