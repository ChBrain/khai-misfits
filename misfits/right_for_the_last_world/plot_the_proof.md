# Plot: The Proof

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Review](place_the_review.md), the first full quarter's numbers laid out, the kind of room built to ask one question of them: did it work.
Everyone present already sensing, from the shape of the numbers, roughly what the answer will be.

## Action

[Crediting](process_crediting.md): [The Auditor](persona_the_auditor.md), holding [Auditor](position_auditor.md)'s seat, reads the results and credits them to the structure itself, while [The Steward](persona_the_steward.md), holding [Steward](position_steward.md)'s seat, receives the credit and reads it the same way.

## Stage

[The Auditor](persona_the_auditor.md), still on [Read the Record](plan_read_the_record.md), sets [The Scorecard](piece_the_scorecard.md) in front of the room: throughput up, cost down, every line reading the way the market's requirement predicted it would.
[The Steward](persona_the_steward.md), on [Hold the Fit](plan_hold_the_fit.md), hears the numbers named as proof that the Rulebook is sound, not merely that it was matched well, and does not correct the difference, because from inside the room there is none to correct.

## Tension

The number cannot say what it is a number of.
There is no dishonesty in the reading: the results are real, the structure produced them, and crediting the structure for them is the plain, reasonable inference from data that cannot distinguish a structure that is sound from a structure that merely still fits. But the two readings diverge the moment the fit stops holding, and nothing about the number itself carries any warning of which one it will turn out to have been.
