# Place: The Chart

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md). The organisation's own structure, where the fitted form is drawn as seats and the paths that lead to them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Seats and the lines between them, each line already carrying someone climbing it.
The chain of command the Rulebook calls for, staffed and running, every box filled by someone whose being there is itself a small piece of evidence that the boxes are the right ones to have drawn.

## Holds

The promotion path, fixed to the criteria the fitted form earned.
Here the Chart holds the one route upward there is, so every hire who wants to rise studies the path rather than the market it was drawn from, and holding only the path, and not the requirement behind it, is exactly how the path outlives the requirement.

## Offers

Advancement to whoever follows the path as drawn.
Because the criteria really did track a real fit once, the Chart offers a fair, checkable route up, earned rung by rung, so it hands every climber a record that is honestly theirs and that argues, by its very existence, for the soundness of the path that produced it.

## Withheld

Any route that argues against itself.
The Chart has no path for rising by doubting the structure the path was drawn from, because the path was drawn to reward following it, not questioning it, so it withholds, from everyone who has ever succeeded on it, the standing to be the one who calls it into question, and hands that standing instead to whoever never climbed it at all.
