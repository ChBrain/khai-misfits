# Persona: The Steward

## Taxonomy

[Steward](position_steward.md). Reads the market's real requirement, builds the structure it calls for, and holds that structure faithfully after the market has moved past it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Methodical. Unshowy. Trusts the rule over the improvisation.
Built the Rulebook against a requirement that was really there, and reads a clean number as license to keep running the way that produced it.
No arrogance in it: only the plain confidence of someone whose read of the world has, so far, been confirmed every time it was checked.

## Action

Writes a rule for the order and a channel for the exception, and keeps both running exactly as written.
Trains every new hand into the Rulebook rather than around it, because around it is where the errors used to live.
Answers a question about why things are done this way with the numbers, not with a story.

## Shadow

The precision that let the fit take hold is the precision that will not let it feel the fit end.
Because the Rulebook was built to read one shape of demand exactly, it has no calibration for any other, so the Steward is not failing to look, there is nothing built into what it looks with that could show it a different shape even if it turned to look straight at one.
It holds the form not out of habit but out of the same rigour that built it, and the rigour is exactly what will not let go.

## Tell

Says "the rule works, look at the numbers."
Says it in the same tone at the ribbon-cutting and years later, and the tone has not changed because, from where the Steward is standing, nothing that would change it has ever come through.
