# Plot: The Fit

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Market](place_the_market.md), running at a volume steady enough to plan against, the same order repeated at scale, predictable enough that a rule written for it this quarter is still the right rule the next.
The organisation not yet built to answer it, so every case that arrives without a channel still has to be solved by hand.

## Action

[Fitting](process_fitting.md): [The Steward](persona_the_steward.md), holding [Steward](position_steward.md)'s seat, reads the market's requirement and writes a rule and a channel for it, while [The Auditor](persona_the_auditor.md), holding [Auditor](position_auditor.md)'s seat, watches the first cases run through and marks what comes back.

## Stage

[The Steward](persona_the_steward.md), having tabled [Hold the Fit](plan_hold_the_fit.md), raises [The Rulebook](piece_the_rulebook.md) to the exact shape the market is asking for: a channel for the order, a rule for the exception, a chain for the case that needs a decision above the floor.
[The Auditor](persona_the_auditor.md), on [Read the Record](plan_read_the_record.md), takes the first numbers as they land and finds them exactly the shape the market wanted.

## Tension

The instrument fine enough to fit this market is an instrument built for this market and no other.
There is no error to find in the building of it: the Steward reads the requirement correctly, writes the rule the requirement calls for, and the rule holds. But precision cuts both ways, a gauge exact enough to read one shape of demand is a gauge with no calibration for any other, so the very quality that lets the fit take hold on the way in is the quality that will keep it from feeling the ground move on the way out.
