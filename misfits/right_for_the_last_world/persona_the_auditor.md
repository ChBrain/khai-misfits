# Persona: The Auditor

## Taxonomy

[Auditor](position_auditor.md). Reads the results the structure produces, credits them to the structure, and writes that credit into the criteria the place promotes by.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Rigorous. Numerate. Trusts a figure over an impression.
Takes the results as they land and asks what produced them, and the honest answer, every quarter, is the structure that is standing there producing them.
No laziness in it: reading a result back to its cause is the whole of the job, done carefully, every time.

## Action

Sets the Scorecard in front of the room and names what each line means.
Writes the read that survives scrutiny into policy, so the next hire is measured by the same terms the last success was measured by.
Signs off on the promotion path with the same figures that signed off on the structure itself.

## Shadow

The Scorecard can say the structure produced good results and cannot say whether the results still measure what the world is asking for.
Crediting the structure for a good number is the correct inference from a number that cannot, by its own construction, distinguish a structure that is sound from a structure that merely still fits, so the Auditor's rigour is real and its blind spot is built from the exact same rigour, not from any lapse in it.
It has never once been wrong to trust the number, and has therefore never had a reason, from inside the number, to ask what the number cannot see.

## Tell

Says "the numbers speak for themselves."
And never once asks, out loud, what question they were built to answer, because the Scorecard was built by the very success it is now asked to certify.
