# Piece: The Backlog

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md). The accumulating pile of cases the Rulebook has no clean channel for, each one handled well and closed, none of them ever summed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Market](place_the_market.md), where each hard case is met and forced through by hand.
Carried by [the Scout](persona_the_scout.md) to [the Review](place_the_review.md), off the books, until the count stops looking like noise.

## Load Bearing

Without it the drift in the Market would leave no trace anywhere in the structure, and the misfit would have no evidence at all, not even contested evidence.
With it there is, finally, something to bring into the room. The Backlog is load bearing because it is the lag between a form and a world it no longer fits, borne while structural adjustment has not yet occurred (Donaldson): each entry in it is a real friction, handled correctly and closed, and it is precisely their being closed correctly, one at a time, that keeps the pile from ever forcing itself onto the Scorecard the place actually trusts.

## Apparent

A normal cost of doing business, the ordinary residue every operation leaves behind.
Seen individually, each entry in the Backlog looks like nothing more than a hard case handled well, and the truth that the pile is the market's own drift, arriving one exception at a time and adding, unread, to a pattern the Rulebook was never built to see, stays hidden behind how unremarkable any single entry is.

## Yearbook

Filed one case at a time through [drifting](process_drifting.md), while [the Steward](persona_the_steward.md) keeps [the Rulebook](piece_the_rulebook.md) running exactly as written.
It is what [the Scout](persona_the_scout.md) carries into [the Review](place_the_review.md) and sets beside [the Scorecard](piece_the_scorecard.md), and the raw material [the Overhang](piece_the_overhang.md) is finally named from, once the pile is large enough to be named.
