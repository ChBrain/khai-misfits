# Piece: The Overhang

## Taxonomy

[Right for the Last World](play_right_for_the_last_world.md). The phenomenon whole: a structure still executing well, aimed at a market that has already moved past what it was built to answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Named at [the Review](place_the_review.md), where the Backlog is finally set beside the Scorecard and called by what it is.
Standing over [the Chart](place_the_chart.md) and [the Market](place_the_market.md) alike, since it is the gap between what the one still runs and what the other now asks for.

## Load Bearing

Without it the other pieces are parts without the mechanism that binds them into one story.
With it the whole misfit stands in a single object: a Rulebook that fits a requirement no longer arriving in that shape, a Scorecard that certifies the fit is running and cannot say whether it is still needed, a Chart of careers built on the certifying, and a Backlog that has been quietly counting the difference all along. The Overhang is load bearing because it is structural contingency's own governing finding, staged whole (Burns and Stalker; Lawrence and Lorsch; Woodward): there is no one best way to organise, a form is right only for a world, and a world does not hold still, so the piece carries the exact shape of a structure that was correct, is still competent, and is now, without anyone's fault, the wrong one.

## Apparent

A success story still in progress, a structure that has simply kept doing what it was built to do.
Seen from inside the Chart, the Overhang looks like nothing at all, since every instrument the place trusts is reading fine, and the truth that the requirement behind those readings has already changed, that competence and correctness have quietly come apart, stays hidden behind how well the machine is still running.

## Yearbook

Built, unnamed, across every plot of the run: raised in [fitting](process_fitting.md), certified in [crediting](process_crediting.md), staffed in [hardening](process_hardening.md), and widened, entry by entry, in [drifting](process_drifting.md).
It is what [the Scout](persona_the_scout.md) finally names in [abandoning](process_abandoning.md), set beside [the Scorecard](piece_the_scorecard.md) in [the Review](place_the_review.md), the whole misfit made, at last, visible enough to argue over.
