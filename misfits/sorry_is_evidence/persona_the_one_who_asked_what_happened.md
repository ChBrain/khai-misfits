# Persona: The One Who Asked What Happened

## Taxonomy

[One Who Asked What Happened](position_one_who_asked_what_happened.md). The harmed party, asking for the ordinary thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A patient or a family, seriously affected, wanting to be told what happened and why.
Wanting an apology, an assurance that it will not happen to the next person, somebody held to account, and compensation, in roughly that order.

## Action

Asks, repeatedly, in corridors and in letters.
Receives courtesy, sympathy, and no account of what occurred.

## Shadow

Is asking for the cheapest thing in the play and cannot be given it.
The request costs nothing to satisfy in money and everything to satisfy in liability, because the explanation and the admission are the same sentence: so a person who mostly did not want to sue is refused the one thing that would have prevented it, by people who are not refusing out of malice and cannot do otherwise.

## Tell

Ends up suing for a conversation, which is the most expensive way anybody has ever obtained one.
