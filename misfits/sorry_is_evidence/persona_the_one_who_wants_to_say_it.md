# Persona: The One Who Wants to Say It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Wants to Say It](position_one_who_wants_to_say_it.md). The clinician who was there, and is not the obstacle.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The person who was in the room when it happened, who has gone over it many times since, and who often carries it worse than anybody realises.
Trained to be honest with patients, and wanting to be here.

## Action

Prepares to explain, and is advised not to.
Says that they are sorry this happened, which is the most that is permitted, and watches it land as nothing.

## Shadow

Is willing, present and stopped by something that is not in them.
Nothing about this persona's character, training or intention obstructs the disclosure, and removing every reluctance they might have would change nothing at all: the constraint sits in the admissibility of what they would say, so the play cannot be resolved by finding a braver clinician.

## Tell

Is the one person in the building who both knows what happened and wants to say it.
