# Position: Statute That Protects the Words

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md). The seat that reaches the sentence and not the fact.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A provision making expressions of sympathy or regret inadmissible in a claim.
Authors who understood the problem precisely and legislated at it directly.

## Orders

Make saying sorry safe.
Keep the words out of evidence.

## Loses

The half that mattered, and loses it by construction.
An apology conveys knowledge the harmed party did not have, and it is the knowledge rather than the admissibility that generates a claim, so this seat can make candour safe in form while leaving it fully consequential in substance: the measured result is a rise in liability risk, produced by a reform that was aimed correctly and drafted competently.

## Drives

Toward a well-designed correction that increases the thing it was written to reduce.
