# Position: One Who Asked What Happened

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md). The seat asking for the cheapest thing and refused it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A serious harm, and a question about how it occurred.
A wish for an apology, for others to be protected, for somebody to be accountable, and for compensation, in roughly that order.

## Orders

Ask what happened.
Ask again.

## Loses

The explanation, which costs nothing in money and everything in liability.
The account and the admission are the same sentence, so the request cannot be granted by anybody who understands its consequences: this seat mostly did not want to litigate, is refused the single thing that would have prevented it, and is refused by people acting neither out of malice nor out of indifference.

## Drives

Toward suing for a conversation, which is the most expensive method anybody has devised for obtaining one.
