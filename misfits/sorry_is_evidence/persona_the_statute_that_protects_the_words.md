# Persona: The Statute That Protects the Words

## Taxonomy

[Statute That Protects the Words](position_statute_that_protects_the_words.md). The apology law, aimed correctly and reaching the wrong half.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A statute providing that expressions of sympathy or regret are inadmissible in a claim, so that saying sorry is safe.
Passed by people who understood the problem exactly and set out to fix it.

## Action

Removes the words from evidence.
Leaves the information those words carry entirely where it was.

## Shadow

Protects the sentence and cannot protect the fact.
An apology tells the harmed party something they did not know, and it is the knowledge rather than the admissibility that produces a claim: so the statute makes candour legally safe in form while leaving it fully consequential in substance, and the measured effect is a rise in liability risk rather than the intended fall.

## Tell

Is a well-aimed correction that increases the thing it was passed to reduce.
