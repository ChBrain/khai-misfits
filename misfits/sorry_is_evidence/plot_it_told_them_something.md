# Plot: It Told Them Something

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The effect of the apology statutes measured across ninety per cent of the physicians in a country practising within a single specialty, over eight years.

## Action

The comparison is made. For surgeons, no substantial effect on the probability of a claim or on the average payment. For non-surgeons, a higher probability of facing a claim and a higher average payment.

## Stage

On balance the laws increase liability risk rather than reducing it, which is the opposite of what they were passed to do.

## Tension

The reform did exactly what it said and produced the reverse of what it intended, and the mechanism is the misfit rather than an error in the drafting.
The apology supplies information the patient did not have, which is precisely the thing that makes an apology worth having, so it can prompt a claim even though it cannot be put in evidence. The statute therefore succeeds completely at its stated task and fails at its purpose, and [The Statute That Protects the Words](persona_the_statute_that_protects_the_words.md) cannot be redrafted to fix it without removing the reason for apologising at all.
