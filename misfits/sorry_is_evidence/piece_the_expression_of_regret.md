# Piece: The Expression of Regret

## Taxonomy

[The Bedside Conversation](place_the_bedside_conversation.md). The approved sentence, which concedes nothing and lands as nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Spoken in [the bedside conversation](place_the_bedside_conversation.md), cleared in advance, and carefully constructed to express sorrow at an event rather than responsibility for it.

## Load Bearing

It is the largest thing that may be said, and it is audibly the largest thing that may be said.
Sorrow that something happened is not an account of why it happened, and a family that came for the second hears the first as a refusal: so the piece is simultaneously a genuine expression of sympathy and a signal that an explanation is being withheld, and it communicates the withholding more clearly than silence would.

## Apparent

Warm, sincere, and meant. The person saying it usually means every word.
Correct, approved, and unobjectionable to anybody.

## Yearbook

Refined over years by legal and communications teams into a form that carries no admission.
Recognised on arrival by anybody who has heard one before, which is increasingly everybody.
