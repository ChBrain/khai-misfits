# Plan: Tell Them

## Taxonomy

A **duty of candour**: the intent of [The One Who Wants to Say It](persona_the_one_who_wants_to_say_it.md), inside [Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Wants to Say It](persona_the_one_who_wants_to_say_it.md)

## Direction

Say what happened. Sit down with the family, explain it in words they can follow, say plainly that you are sorry, and tell them what is being changed so it does not happen again.

## Orders

(Attend [the bedside conversation](place_the_bedside_conversation.md); answer what is asked; apologise; describe what has changed; do none of it without approval, which is the instruction that arrives first.)

## Implementation

([The Bedside Conversation](place_the_bedside_conversation.md), which holds the knowledge, the willingness and the audience; and an approval process that permits sympathy and not account.)

## Targets

- [ ] the family is met by somebody who was actually there
- [ ] everything permitted to be said is said, warmly and sincerely
- [ ] the professional duty of candour is discharged as far as approval allows
- [ ] the account itself is not given, and the constraint is not in this plan's owner, so no amount of courage in them changes it
