# Pitch: Nobody Said Anything

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Clinical and careful. An incident reviewed properly, a family met with courtesy, counsel giving accurate advice about admissibility, an insurer discharging a duty it genuinely holds, a legislature passing a law to make kindness safe.
Nobody lies, nobody is cruel, and every step is defensible.

## Undertow

Under the courtesy, a question with an answer and no permitted route to it.
The cheapest thing the harmed person wants is the most expensive thing the institution can give, because the explanation and the admission are the same sentence.

## Nerve

The vertigo of watching somebody sue for a conversation.

## Echo

Afterward: fewer than fifteen per cent of the explanations that were given found satisfactory, apology statutes that raise liability rather than lowering it, and disclosure in litigation as the first place anybody says what happened.
