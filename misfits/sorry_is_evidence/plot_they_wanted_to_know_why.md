# Plot: They Wanted to Know Why

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A serious harm, and a family who do not understand how it happened, asking in corridors and then in letters.

## Action

[Asking what happened](process_asking_what_happened.md): [The One Who Asked What Happened](persona_the_one_who_asked_what_happened.md), holding [One Who Asked What Happened](position_one_who_asked_what_happened.md), requests an explanation, an apology, an assurance that others will be protected, and accountability.

## Stage

When claimants are studied rather than assumed, those are the reasons they give, and the decision to sue turns not only on the injury but on how they were handled afterwards.

## Tension

The cheapest thing in the play is the one thing that cannot be supplied.
The request costs nothing in money and everything in liability, because the explanation and the admission are the same sentence. Of those who were given an explanation at all, fewer than fifteen per cent found it satisfactory. So a person who mostly did not want to litigate is refused the single thing that would have prevented it, by people who are not refusing out of malice and who cannot do otherwise.
