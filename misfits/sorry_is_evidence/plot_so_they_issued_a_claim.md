# Plot: So They Issued a Claim

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A question with a knowable answer, and the discovery that nothing outside a court will compel anybody to give it.

## Action

[Suing to find out](process_suing_to_find_out.md): [The Only Way to Find Out](persona_the_only_way_to_find_out.md), holding [Only Way to Find Out](position_only_way_to_find_out.md), opens, and proceedings are issued to obtain disclosure, records and witness statements.

## Stage

The account arrives at [the disclosure list](place_the_disclosure_list.md) two years later, complete, verified, and at many times the cost of the conversation that was refused.

## Tension

A compensation mechanism is being operated as an information service, because nothing else in the system will do it.
The instrument exists to determine liability and award damages, and it holds the one power that matters here, which is compulsion. So a person who wanted twenty minutes and an apology buys two years and an adversarial posture, and the file records a claim for damages rather than a request for an explanation that nobody was able to answer.
