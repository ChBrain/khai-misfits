# Process: Bundling It With Money

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md). The answer that works, at a price nobody imposed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An institution deciding that the litigation costs more than the candour would.
[The Early Offer](piece_the_early_offer.md), made alongside a full account rather than instead of one.

## Direction

Toward all four of the things the harmed party said they wanted, delivered together and early.
Toward a measurable reduction in litigation, which these programmes do achieve.

## Lever

Compensation supplied voluntarily, which converts disclosure from an exposure into a resolution.

## Echo

An institution acting as its own tribunal, finding its own fault before anybody compels it, and paying some claims that would never have been brought.
