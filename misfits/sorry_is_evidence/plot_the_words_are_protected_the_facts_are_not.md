# Plot: The Words Are Protected, the Facts Are Not

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A legislature that has understood the problem exactly and sets out to fix it, so that kindness is no longer legally dangerous.

## Action

[The Statute That Protects the Words](persona_the_statute_that_protects_the_words.md), holding [Statute That Protects the Words](position_statute_that_protects_the_words.md), makes expressions of sympathy or regret inadmissible in a claim.

## Stage

Saying sorry is now safe in form. Nothing at all has changed about what saying sorry conveys.

## Tension

The reform reaches the sentence and cannot reach the fact the sentence carries.
An apology tells the harmed party something they did not know, and it is the knowledge rather than the admissibility that produces a claim. So the statute makes candour legally safe and leaves it fully consequential, which is not a drafting failure: no provision could have protected the words while withholding the information, because the information is what the words are for.
