# Persona: The One Who Guards the Claim

## Taxonomy

[One Who Guards the Claim](position_one_who_guards_the_claim.md). Counsel and insurer, advising accurately.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The institution's lawyer and its indemnifier, asked whether an explanation may be given to the family this afternoon.
Sympathetic, experienced, and answerable for the consequences.

## Action

Advises that an account of fault is admissible and an apology may be read as an admission.
Approves an expression of sympathy that concedes nothing, and nothing further.

## Shadow

Is correct, and the correctness is the whole obstruction.
The duty here runs to the institution and to every future claimant whose cover depends on the fund, and the advice is an accurate prediction of what happens to a sentence spoken in a corridor once it appears in a witness statement: so this is not cowardice or callousness, and a braver lawyer giving different advice would simply be giving wrong advice.

## Tell

Protects the institution from the sentence, and delivers it the claim.
