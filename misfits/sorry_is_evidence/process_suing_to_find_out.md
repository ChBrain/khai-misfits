## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md). A question becoming a claim, for want of any other instrument.

## Owner

[The Only Way to Find Out](persona_the_only_way_to_find_out.md), holding [Only Way to Find Out](position_only_way_to_find_out.md).
