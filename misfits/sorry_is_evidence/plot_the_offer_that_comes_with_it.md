# Plot: The Offer That Comes With It

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An institution that has decided the litigation is costing more than the candour would, and the programmes that pair disclosure with early compensation.

## Action

[Say it and pay it](plan_say_it_and_pay_it.md): the account is given in full, the apology is unhedged, and [the early offer](piece_the_early_offer.md) is put on the table before anybody asks for it, through [bundling it with money](process_bundling_it_with_money.md).

## Stage

It works. Giving the harmed party all four of the things they said they wanted, rather than one, reduces the litigation.

## Tension

The answer that works is the one that stops treating this as a legal problem and starts paying for it.
It requires the institution to investigate its own conduct, find its own fault before any tribunal has, decide its own liability and put a number on it, which is a governance question rather than a legal one and is where most attempts stop. And it means paying some claims that would never have been brought at all, out of a fund that was never sized for them, so the cost of candour is real and falls first on the party being asked to be candid.
