# Place: The Bedside Conversation

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md). Where the answer is present and cannot be given.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A side room, chairs brought in, a senior clinician, a family, and forty minutes set aside.
Everybody present who knows what happened, and everybody willing to be there.

## Holds

[The Expression of Regret](piece_the_expression_of_regret.md), approved in advance and conceding nothing.
[The One Who Wants to Say It](persona_the_one_who_wants_to_say_it.md), who could answer every question in the room.

## Offers

Time, courtesy, sympathy, and the undivided attention of the people involved.
A genuine willingness to sit there for as long as it takes.

## Withheld

The account, which is the only thing anybody came for.
The room contains the knowledge, the willingness and the audience, and the one sentence that would satisfy it is the sentence that establishes liability, so the meeting can supply everything except its purpose: and a family that leaves having been treated kindly and told nothing will remember the second part.
