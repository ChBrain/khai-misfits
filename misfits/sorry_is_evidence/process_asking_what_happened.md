# Process: Asking What Happened

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md). The request that starts everything, and is mostly not about money.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A serious harm, and a family who do not understand how it occurred.
[The One Who Asked What Happened](persona_the_one_who_asked_what_happened.md), holding [One Who Asked What Happened](position_one_who_asked_what_happened.md).

## Direction

Toward an explanation, an apology, an assurance that others will be protected, and accountability.
Toward [the bedside conversation](place_the_bedside_conversation.md), where everybody who knows is present and willing.

## Lever

A request that costs nothing in money and everything in liability, because the account and the admission are one sentence.

## Echo

Courtesy, sympathy and time given generously, and the single thing asked for withheld.
