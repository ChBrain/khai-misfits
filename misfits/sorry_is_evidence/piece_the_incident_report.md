# Piece: The Incident Report

## Taxonomy

[The Disclosure List](place_the_disclosure_list.md). The internal account, written to learn and read to litigate.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Filed internally within days, and appearing at [the disclosure list](place_the_disclosure_list.md) two years later if privilege does not hold.

## Load Bearing

It is where the truth is written down, by people who need it to be true.
Safety improvement requires a frank internal account, and a frank internal account is a document about fault, so the same page serves the review that prevents the next harm and the claim arising from this one: and every protection extended to it, so that staff will write frankly, is a protection that keeps it from the person it is about.

## Apparent

A candid, detailed, timely record, written by the people who were there for the purpose of stopping it happening again.
Exactly what a learning organisation should produce.

## Yearbook

Protected by privilege or by statute in many systems, precisely so that it can be candid.
Withheld, by that same protection, from the family who asked what happened.
