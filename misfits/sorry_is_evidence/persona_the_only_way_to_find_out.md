# Persona: The Only Way to Find Out

## Taxonomy

[Only Way to Find Out](position_only_way_to_find_out.md). Litigation, functioning as an information service.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A claim: pleadings, disclosure of records, witness statements, an expert report, and eventually an account of what happened.
Slow, adversarial, ruinously expensive, and effective at the one thing.

## Action

Compels the production of everything the corridor conversation would have contained.
Delivers it two years later, through solicitors, at a cost many times the value of the answer.

## Shadow

Is being used for a purpose it was never designed to serve, because nothing else will do it.
The instrument exists to determine liability and award damages, and here it is the only mechanism in the system capable of making somebody explain: so a person who wanted twenty minutes and an apology buys two years and a trial, and the system records the outcome as a compensation claim rather than as a request for information that went unanswered.

## Tell

Answers the question at last, having been the most expensive way of asking it.
