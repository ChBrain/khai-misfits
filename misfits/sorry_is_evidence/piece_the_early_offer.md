# Piece: The Early Offer

## Taxonomy

[The Disclosure List](place_the_disclosure_list.md). The compensation that makes the explanation possible.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Made months before anything reaches [the disclosure list](place_the_disclosure_list.md), alongside a full account of what happened.

## Load Bearing

It is what turns disclosure from an exposure into a resolution, and it costs money nobody was ordered to spend.
Pairing the explanation with compensation gives the harmed party all four of the things they said they wanted rather than one, and the programmes that do it reduce litigation: which requires the institution to find its own fault before any tribunal has, and to pay some claims that would never have been brought at all.

## Apparent

Prompt, generous, and accompanied by a genuine account and an assurance about future practice.
The most effective answer anybody has to this misfit, and known to work.

## Yearbook

Adopted by institutions that decided the litigation was costing more than the candour would.
Requires a body willing to be its own tribunal, which is a governance question rather than a legal one and is where most attempts stop.
