# Position: One Who Wants to Say It

## Taxonomy

[Sorry Is Evidence](play_sorry_is_evidence.md). The seat that is willing and is not the constraint.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

First-hand knowledge of what happened, and a professional duty of candour.
A wish to explain, and often more distress about the event than anybody has noticed.

## Orders

Be honest with the patient.
Say nothing that has not been approved.

## Loses

The two orders against each other, and cannot reconcile them alone.
Nothing in this seat's character, training or intention obstructs the disclosure, so removing every reluctance it might hold changes nothing whatever: the constraint lives in the admissibility of the sentence rather than in the person who would speak it, which means the play cannot be resolved by finding a braver clinician.

## Drives

Toward being the one person who both knows what happened and wants to say it, and saying the approved sentence instead.
