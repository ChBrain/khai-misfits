## Tension

The advice is accurate, which is what makes it the whole of the obstruction.
An account of fault is admissible and an apology may be read as an admission, and the duty runs to the institution and to the fund every future claimant draws on: so this is not cowardice, and a braver lawyer giving different advice would simply be giving advice that is wrong. Nobody lies. [The One Who Wants to Say It](persona_the_one_who_wants_to_say_it.md) is in the room, willing, and is not the constraint, so no amount of courage anywhere in the building changes the outcome.
