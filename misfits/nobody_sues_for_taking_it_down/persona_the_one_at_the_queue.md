# Persona: The One at the Queue

## Taxonomy

[Custody](position_custody.md). Well advised, entirely solvent, and answering more complaints in a day than anybody could read in a month.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, unhurried, and not remotely frightened.
Has read the statute, has counsel who has read it more carefully, and knows precisely what the harbour is worth.
Would keep lawful material up if keeping it up were free.

## Action

Checks the notice against the list of required elements.
Finds them all present.
Removes the material, inside the hour, and logs the ticket as resolved.

## Shadow

That the removal is a correct reading and not a flinch.
**Leaving it up risks the thing the whole business stands on and taking it down risks nothing**, so the
decision is arithmetic, an adviser recommending otherwise would be recommending a liability, and what looks
from outside like timidity is the only defensible answer.

## Tell

Is the party everybody blames.
Is the party whose behaviour would not change if you replaced them with anybody better.
