# Process: Pricing

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). Two errors being valued against each other, by a statute rather than by anybody.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Text enacted long before any of the parties arrived.
At [the exposure seat](position_exposure.md), identically for everybody, with no participants.

## Direction

Toward the protected error, for every competent occupant of every other seat.
This is not selection by anybody: it is what a rational party does when one of its two available mistakes
costs it the thing it operates on and the other is immunised in terms.

## Lever

None that any party in the play holds.
The pricing completes before the first notice is opened, which is why no meeting is ever about it and why
its result arrives looking like a set of company policies that happen to agree.

## Echo

A dominant move that is the same for every host,
an outcome that does not improve when the operator improves,
and a cost that lands on somebody who was not consulted.
