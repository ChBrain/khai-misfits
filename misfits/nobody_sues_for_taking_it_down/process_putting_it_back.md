# Process: Putting It Back

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). The remedy the statute provides, working exactly as written, on the terms it sets.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A speaker who has read the page, understood the procedure, and decided the work is worth a lawsuit.
At [the recourse seat](position_recourse.md), free of charge, available to everybody.

## Direction

Toward the material coming back.
It works. It is not a formality, it is not obstructed, and hosts that receive a valid counter-notice
restore on schedule, which is why the failure here is not a failure of the remedy.

## Lever

The price of entry, which is a consent to jurisdiction and is not adjustable.
Neither of the parties who produced the removal is asked for anything comparable, so the one requirement
in the remedy falls entirely on the one party with nothing at stake but the work itself.

## Echo

A procedure with nothing wrong in it,
a rate of use low enough to be read as agreement,
and a conclusion drawn from that silence by everybody except the people who were silent.
