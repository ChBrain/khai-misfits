# Position: Custody

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). The seat that holds other people's material and answers for holding it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A protection that makes the whole activity possible, and conditions attached to keeping it.
A queue of complaints arriving faster than anybody could read them.
Two ways to be wrong, and only one of them usable against it.

## Orders

Act expeditiously on anything compliant.
Do not weigh the claim, because nothing asks you to and the volume forbids it.
Keep the harbour, because without it there is no seat.

## Loses

Nothing, when it is wrong in the protected direction.
**That is the whole of the difficulty**: the seat is not exposed to the error it makes most often, so no
count of those errors reaches it, no cost of them appears in its accounts, and it can be entirely diligent
about every risk it actually carries.

## Drives

Toward a queue answered by rule rather than by reading,
which is the only way a queue that size can be answered,
so the removal is a policy and the policy is correct.
