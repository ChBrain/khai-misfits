# Piece: Ten Days and a Courtroom

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). The remedy works, restores on a fixed timetable, and asks its user to consent to a federal court.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the recourse seat](position_recourse.md), which is the seat that works.
At [the federal court](place_the_federal_court.md), named on a form by somebody who has not been sued.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is about a system with no remedy, which would be a simpler and lesser thing.
It is load bearing because **a settlement that works and is not used is harder to argue with than one that
fails**: nothing is wrong with the counter-notice except who is asked to pay for it, and a consent to
jurisdiction is trivial for a catalogue and decisive for a person, so the requirement lands as unevenly as
the harm did.

## Apparent

That low use means the notices were sound.
It means the price of contesting one is a lawsuit, borne by the only party in the play who lost something
and the only one with no counsel.

## Yearbook

The counter-notification provisions: restoration after ten to fourteen business days absent suit, on a
form requiring a name, an address and a consent to the jurisdiction of a federal district court.
