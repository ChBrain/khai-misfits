# Plot: Making It Cost Something

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The settlements, taken one at a time and in good faith.

## Action

[Sending](process_sending.md): [The One Who Sends It](persona_the_one_who_sends_it.md), in [the claim seat](position_claim.md), works through [Make a Wrong Notice Cost Something](plan_make_a_wrong_notice_cost_something.md).

## Stage

[The Notice Form](place_the_notice_form.md): the one surface where accuracy could have been required, and where it is not.

## Tension

Each settlement gives back the thing it was for.
**Read the merits**, and the host takes on precisely the liability the harbour was enacted to remove, which
converts a legal question into a business decision nobody in the seat can justify.
**Penalise bad notices**, and the provision that does this already exists, has been used successfully, and
asks [what the sender believed](piece_what_the_sender_believed.md), so it reaches the sender who knew and leaves the sender who was merely wrong, which is the population producing the volume.
**Add human review**, and it scales with the notices rather than with the errors, so the cost rises with
the sending and the sending is what a widened net produces.
**Restore first and ask later**, and the exposure moves back onto the host, which is where the harbour was
written to take it from.
[The Two Prices](persona_the_two_prices.md) is untouched by all four, because none of them changes what the
two errors cost the party choosing between them, and **the one that would is the one that puts the host
back in the position the statute exists to spare it.**
