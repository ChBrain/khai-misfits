# Persona: The One Who Sends It

## Taxonomy

[Claim](position_claim.md). An agent with real authority over a real catalogue, working at a volume that only a machine can reach.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Sincere, authorised, and correct about most of what it sends.
Signs the statement about authority truthfully, because it has the authority.
Would not knowingly send a notice about lawful material.

## Action

Matches. Files. Sends.
Meets every element the form requires.
Does not ask, because nothing asks it to, whether the use it found was one the law permits.

## Shadow

That being wrong is not the same as being dishonest, and only one of them is priced.
**A notice is compliant if it is complete**, so accuracy is a courtesy rather than a requirement, and an
operation optimising what it is actually measured on will sweep wide and send too many.

## Tell

Is doing the job it was retained to do.
Is not, at any point in the process, asked to be right.
