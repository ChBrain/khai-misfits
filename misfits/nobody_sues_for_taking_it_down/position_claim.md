# Position: Claim

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). The seat that asserts a right and is judged on the shape of the assertion.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A catalogue to protect and a duty to whoever owns it.
A form with a list of required elements, and a statement made under penalty of perjury about authority.
No obligation, anywhere in the process, to be right about the underlying claim.

## Orders

Find the material. Send the notice. Meet every element.
Sweep widely, because a match missed is a loss and a match made in error is not.
Do it at the volume the catalogue requires, which means do it by machine.

## Loses

Almost nothing when the claim is bad, unless it knew.
The misrepresentation provision is real and reaches the sender who knew, so the seat is not lawless: it is
**unpriced for being wrong and priced only for being dishonest**, and those are different populations.

## Drives

Toward more notices rather than better ones,
because the return on accuracy is small and the return on coverage is not,
so the seat is doing its job properly when it sends too many.
