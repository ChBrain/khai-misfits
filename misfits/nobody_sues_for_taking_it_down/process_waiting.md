# Process: Waiting

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). The statutory period between a counter-notice and a restoration, counted in business days.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A form filed by somebody who has agreed to be sued over it.
At [the federal court](place_the_federal_court.md) in prospect, and nowhere physical at all.

## Direction

Toward restoration, if nobody sues.
The wait is short in a calendar and long against anything the material was timely about, so a piece of
commentary can be entirely restored and entirely too late.

## Lever

Nobody's, and it is not a discretionary period.
It runs the same length for a wholesale copy and for a quotation the law plainly permits, because the
process has no way to tell those apart and never had.

## Echo

A restoration on a fixed timetable,
material that comes back to an audience that has moved,
and a wait borne by the only party who did nothing.
