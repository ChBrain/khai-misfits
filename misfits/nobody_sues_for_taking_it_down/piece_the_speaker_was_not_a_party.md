# Piece: The Speaker Was Not a Party

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). The cost of the free error falls entirely outside the exchange that produced it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the absence seat](position_absence.md), which has no representation in the decision.
In the automated message that arrives after the material has already gone.

## Load Bearing

Without it the asymmetry is just a company bearing its own risks, which is ordinary and not a misfit.
It is load bearing because **an error whose cost lands on a non-party is not an error the system can
learn from**: the removal that should not have happened closes the same ticket as the one that should, the
loss appears in nobody's accounts, and a process improving on everything it measures will not improve on
this.

## Apparent

That the speaker is a customer whose interests the host is balancing.
The speaker is not a party to the notice, was not told before the removal, and is owed nothing by either of
the two parties who decided.

## Yearbook

The structure of the notice procedure itself: a communication between a complainant and a service
provider, with the affected user reached afterwards and by notification.
