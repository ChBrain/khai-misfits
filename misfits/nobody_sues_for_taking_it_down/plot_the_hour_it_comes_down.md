## Cue

## Action

## Stage

## Tension

The second obvious answer closes here, and it closes on the operator's competence rather than in spite of
it. This is not a frightened company. It is solvent, well advised, and reading its position exactly.
[One error is free](piece_one_error_is_free.md): **leaving the material up puts the harbour in question and taking it down is immunised**, so the decision
is arithmetic and an adviser recommending the other way would be recommending a liability.
And the volume finishes the argument. At this rate the merits are not being neglected here and considered
somewhere else: there is no somewhere else, and a system answering at seconds per complaint has already
settled that the form is the whole of the test.
The material goes inside the hour. The ticket closes within the service level. **The record of this removal
is indistinguishable from the record of a correct one**, and the metric improves as the answer gets faster.
