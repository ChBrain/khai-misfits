# Plot: The Notice Arrives

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A catalogue with a duty attached to it, and an agent retained to protect it.

## Action

[Sending](process_sending.md): [The One Who Sends It](persona_the_one_who_sends_it.md), in [the claim seat](position_claim.md), works through [Protect the Catalogue](plan_protect_the_catalogue.md).

## Stage

[The Notice Form](place_the_notice_form.md): an identification of the work, an identification of the material, contact details, two statements of belief, and a signature under penalty of perjury.

## Tension

The play has to close the obvious answer before anything else, and it closes it completely.
The authority is real. The statement about it is true. Every element the statute asks for is present and
correctly filled in. The sender is not lying and is right about most of what it sends.
And [the form is the test](piece_the_form_is_the_test.md): **nothing on it asks whether the use complained of is one the law permits.** The statement of
belief is about the sender's own mind, and a sincere mistake satisfies it, so a notice about a wholesale
copy and a notice about a lawful quotation arrive as the same document.
At the end of it the sender has done the job it was retained to do, correctly, at the volume the catalogue
requires, and has not at any point been asked by anybody to be right.
