## Tension

Here the failure stops being about anybody's conduct.
One provision makes the protection conditional on acting expeditiously against material identified in a
compliant notice. Another says no claim lies against a service provider for a good faith removal of
material claimed to be infringing, **whether or not the material turns out to infringe.**
So the two errors are not comparable and were never meant to be. **The asymmetry was enacted rather than
decided**: there is no meeting where it was agreed, no party who can be asked to reconsider it, and no
occupant of the seat that sets it.
And the cost of the cheap error does not fall on the party making it. It falls at [the absence seat](position_absence.md), on [somebody who is not in the exchange](piece_the_speaker_was_not_a_party.md), which is why it can be made at volume, indefinitely, by parties who are all behaving
correctly, and why the total never appears in any of their accounts.
