# Plot: The Form Nobody Files

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The remedy, offered in plain language on the page that explains the removal.

## Action

[Putting It Back](process_putting_it_back.md): [The Form That Costs a Courtroom](persona_the_form_that_costs_a_courtroom.md), in [the recourse seat](position_recourse.md), works through [Have It Back Up](plan_have_it_back_up.md).

## Stage

[The Federal Court](place_the_federal_court.md): a district named on a form, a consent given in advance, and a wait of ten to fourteen business days.

## Tension

This is the plot that has to be careful, because the remedy is not broken.
It exists, it is free, it is statutory, and hosts that receive a valid counter-notice restore on schedule.
Nothing in it is obstructed and nothing in it is a formality. **The counter-notice works.**
Its price is [ten days and a courtroom](piece_ten_days_and_a_courtroom.md): a name, an address, and a consent to be sued in a federal district court, after the [waiting](process_waiting.md) the statute sets. Neither of the two
parties who produced the removal is asked for anything comparable, and **a consent to jurisdiction is cheap
to a catalogue and decisive to a person**, so the one requirement in the remedy falls on the only party
with no counsel and nothing at stake but the work.
[The One Who Was Not Asked](persona_the_one_who_was_not_asked.md) reads the page and does not file. The
rate of non-use is later offered as evidence that the notices were sound, which is the conclusion drawn
from a silence by everybody except the people who were silent.
