# Process: Removing

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). A compliant notice being answered the way the statute asks and the exposure requires.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A notice with every element present.
At [the queue](place_the_queue.md), inside the hour, by an operator reading its position exactly.

## Direction

Toward expeditious action, which is the condition on the protection.
The material goes, the ticket closes, and the record shows a complaint received and resolved within the
service level, which is what a well run operation looks like.

## Lever

The operator's judgement, which exists and cannot be exercised here.
Exercising it means weighing a claim the process never asked to be weighed, at a volume that forbids it,
and taking on precisely the liability the harbour was enacted to remove.

## Echo

Material gone before its author knows a notice exists,
a ticket indistinguishable from every correct one,
and a metric that improves as the answer gets faster.
