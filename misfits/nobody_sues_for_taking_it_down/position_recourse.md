# Position: Recourse

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). The seat that gives the material back, and the price of sitting in it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A working procedure, written into the statute, that restores the material.
A waiting period counted in business days.
A requirement that whoever uses it give a name, an address, and a consent to a federal district court.

## Orders

Restore, if the form is filed and nobody sues.
Ask first for the consent, because the statute asks for it.
Do not weigh whether the person filing can afford what they are consenting to.

## Loses

Nothing, and it is the seat that works.
**Its cost is paid entirely by the party with the least at stake**, so the remedy is real, available, free
of charge and largely unused, and the non-use is read afterwards as evidence that the notices were sound.

## Drives

Toward a settlement that exists and is not taken,
which is a harder thing to argue with than a settlement that fails,
because nothing is wrong with it except who is asked to pay.
