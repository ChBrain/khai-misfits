# Piece: The Form Is the Test

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). A notice is judged by whether it contains the required elements, and nothing in the process asks whether the claim is good.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the notice form](place_the_notice_form.md), where accuracy would have to live.
At [the queue](place_the_queue.md), where the checklist is applied.

## Load Bearing

Without it the sender is a liar and the play has its second villain.
It is load bearing because **compliance and correctness are different properties and only one of them is
required**: the statement of belief is about the sender's own mind, a sincere mistake satisfies it, and the
empirical studies of notices in practice report substantial shares raising questions about validity while
remaining formally complete.

## Apparent

That a compliant notice is a checked one.
It is a complete one. Nobody at any point in the sequence has been asked whether the use complained of is
one the law permits.

## Yearbook

The two empirical accounts of notices in practice, the 2006 study of an archive of notices and the 2016
study of senders and recipients, reporting questionable notices from automated and human senders in
different patterns.
