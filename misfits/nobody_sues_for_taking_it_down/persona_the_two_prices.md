# Persona: The Two Prices

## Taxonomy

[Exposure](position_exposure.md). The difference between two sentences, wearing the shape of a party because every other party is answering to it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Impersonal, enacted, and identical for everybody.
Has no view about speech, about catalogues, or about any particular removal.
Was written to protect rightsholders and to make hosting possible, and does both.

## Action

Prices one error at the loss of the harbour.
Prices the other at nothing, expressly, provided the removal is in good faith.
Applies both to every notice that has ever been sent.

## Shadow

That nobody chose the outcome and everybody is producing it.
**The asymmetry was enacted rather than decided**, so there is no meeting where it was agreed, no party
who can be asked to reconsider it, and the result arrives looking like a series of separate corporate
policies that happen to agree.

## Tell

Is the only element in the play with no occupant.
Is the element every other one is reading correctly.
