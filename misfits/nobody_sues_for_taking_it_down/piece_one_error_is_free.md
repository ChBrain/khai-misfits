# Piece: One Error Is Free

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). Leaving infringing material up can cost the protection the business runs on, and taking lawful material down is immunised in terms.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the exposure seat](position_exposure.md), where the two are priced.
In every decision made at [the queue](place_the_queue.md), by everybody who ever sat there.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play has a villain, the platform that removes too readily, and stops being a misfit.
It is load bearing because **the asymmetry is in the text and not in anybody's disposition**: a host that
acts on a compliant notice in good faith is protected from claims arising out of the removal whether or not
the material infringed, and a host that does not act expeditiously puts the harbour itself in question, so
the dominant move is the same for every occupant of the seat.

## Apparent

That the host is being cautious, and could be persuaded to be braver.
It is not caution. It is a correct reading of two different prices, and bravery here means accepting a
liability that the statute was written to remove.

## Yearbook

The safe harbour structure of section 512: expeditious action as the condition of the protection, and an
express immunity for good faith removal of material claimed to be infringing.
