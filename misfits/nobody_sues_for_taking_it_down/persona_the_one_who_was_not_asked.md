# Persona: The One Who Was Not Asked

## Taxonomy

[Absence](position_absence.md). The author of the material, who was not a party to anything that happened to it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, lawful, and right about the law as it applies to the work.
Quoted what the law permits quoting, and could say why.
Has no counsel, no catalogue, and no relationship with either of the parties who decided.

## Action

Publishes.
Finds out, from an automated message, that the work is gone.
Reads a page explaining a procedure and what the procedure requires them to agree to.

## Shadow

That the loss is real and the process that caused it cannot see it.
**A removal that should not have happened leaves the same record as one that should**, so nothing counts
what this party is out, no report contains it, and the absence of a complaint is later offered as evidence
that there was nothing to complain about.

## Tell

Is the only party in the play who did nothing at all.
Is the only party in the play who lost something.
