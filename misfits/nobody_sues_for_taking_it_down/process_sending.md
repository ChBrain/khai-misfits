# Process: Sending

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). A catalogue being matched against the open world, at machine rate, and the matches being filed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A catalogue with a duty attached to it and a budget for protecting it.
At [the notice form](place_the_notice_form.md), continuously, by an agent with real authority.

## Direction

Toward coverage rather than toward precision.
A match missed is a loss the sender carries and a match made in error is a cost somebody else carries, so
an operation optimising what it is measured on will widen the net, and it is doing its job correctly when
it does.

## Lever

The sender's own care, which is real and is unpriced.
Nothing in the process requires it, nothing in the process rewards it, and the provision that punishes bad
notices reaches the sender who knew rather than the sender who was wrong.

## Echo

A form with every element present,
a statement of belief that is sincere,
and a claim nobody has been asked to evaluate.
