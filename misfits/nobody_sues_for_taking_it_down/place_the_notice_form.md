# Place: The Notice Form

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Sues for Taking It Down](play_nobody_sues_for_taking_it_down.md). The document the statute describes, and the entire surface on which a complaint is judged.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An identification of the work, an identification of the material, contact details, two statements of belief, and a signature.
Every element present and correctly filled in.
A statement about authority made under penalty of perjury, which is true.

## Holds

The place where accuracy would have to live and does not.
**Nothing on the form asks whether the use complained of is one the law permits**, so a notice about a
lawful quotation and a notice about a wholesale copy are the same document, and the party receiving them
has been given nothing to tell them apart with.

## Offers

A complete, compliant, checkable assertion.

## Withheld

Any assertion that the claim behind it is good.
The statement of belief is about the sender's own mind, and a sincere mistake satisfies it.
