# Persona: The Form That Costs a Courtroom

## Taxonomy

[Recourse](position_recourse.md). The counter-notice, which works exactly as written and is almost never used.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Free, available, statutory, and effective.
Restores the material if it is filed and nobody sues.
Is offered to everybody, in plain language, on the page explaining the removal.

## Action

Asks for a name.
Asks for an address.
Asks for a consent to the jurisdiction of a federal district court.

## Shadow

That its price is paid by the party least able to price it.
**The two parties who made the decision are asked for nothing and the party who lost the work is asked to
accept a lawsuit**, so a remedy with nothing wrong in it goes unused, and the non-use is read afterwards as
agreement.

## Tell

Is the answer to every objection raised against the system.
Is the answer that requires the objector to consent to being sued.
