# Piece: The Filter Is Correct

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). The discount applied to a dissimilar adopter's success is accurate, so the waiting is arithmetic rather than resistance.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the kitchen table](place_the_kitchen_table.md), where the question is asked and answered honestly.
In the head of everybody who attended the field day and did not plant.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play has a villain, the stubborn holdout, and stops being a misfit.
It is load bearing because **an adviser recommending otherwise would be wrong**: a result obtained where a
failure was survivable is weaker evidence about a place where it is not, so the discount is a correct
reading of what the case supports and cannot be argued away by anybody honest.

## Apparent

That the caution is a disposition, to be softened by encouragement.
It is a calculation about a specific downside on a specific holding, and encouragement does not change
either.

## Yearbook

The homophily finding: similarity between a source and a receiver treated as a property of the pair
rather than as a quality of either, so nobody in the exchange is deficient and the pairing is what fails.
