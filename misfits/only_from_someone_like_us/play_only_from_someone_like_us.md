# Play: Only From Someone Like Us

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Only From Someone Like Us.
The practice is better. The leaflet reached every holding in the county, the figures in it are honest, and the officer who wrote it is competent.
Two growers planted it. Both farm three times the median acreage and both have a bank line that survives a bad season.
Everybody else read the same leaflet, watched the same harvest, and did not plant, and every one of them was right to wait.

## Arc

The bend is this: information travels one way and influence travels another, and the influence channel only carries between people who resemble each other.

It runs on one relation from the founding study and one from the ground. **The salesmen informed and the neighbours persuaded**, so a practice does not spread because it is known but because somebody comparable did it and it worked for them; and **whatever lets a person absorb a bad year is what lets them go first**, so the people who have done it are not comparable.

Take the information first, because the play has to establish that nothing failed there. The leaflet arrived. The figures are right. The field day was held and attended. Nobody is ignorant of the practice, nobody misunderstood it, and no amount of further explanation is waiting to be delivered.

Then the fact that inverts it. **The adoption destroys the comparability it would need to spread.** The early adopters were younger, better educated and farming more land, which the founding study reported and which is not an accident of two counties: slack is what buys the risk, so the population sorts on exactly the trait that makes the early experience a poor guide for everybody else.

Then the sharper half, which is why persuasion does not reach it. **The discount is correct.** A grower with two hundred acres can carry a failed season; a grower with forty cannot, and the first one's success says less about the second one's prospects than the first one's confidence implies. Declining to act on it is an accurate reading of what the example is evidence for, and an adviser who said otherwise would be wrong.

And the person sent to close the gap is selected against closing it. The change agent is trained, credentialed, salaried by an institution and usually from somewhere else, and each of those is a dimension of difference. **They are least like the people who most need convincing**, so the professional whose whole function is persuasion holds the one position from which persuasion does not carry.

Reach for a settlement and each one gives back the thing that was wanted. Send more information, and it arrives through the channel that was never the constraint. Pay the first adopters, and you have made them a party with an interest, which is what the neighbour's example was trusted for not being. Recruit an opinion leader, and you need one who has already adopted. Run a demonstration on a comparable holding, which is the remedy the field actually uses and which works, and you need a comparable holder willing to go first, so the remedy consumes the thing it produces.

## Triggers

**[The Leaflet Arrives](plot_the_leaflet_arrives.md)**
Information established as complete and correct: every holding reached, the figures honest, the field day held and attended. Closes when it is clear that nobody is ignorant of the practice and that no further explanation is waiting to be delivered.

**[The Ones Who Could Afford It](plot_the_ones_who_could_afford_it.md)**
Cue: two growers plant it. Closes when it is shown that they went first because they could carry a bad season, that the founding study reports exactly that sorting, and that the population has now divided on the trait that decides whose experience transfers.

**[Not Someone Like Me](plot_not_someone_like_me.md)**
Cue: the harvest, watched by everybody. Closes when the discount applied to it is understood as accurate rather than stubborn, and when it is clear that an adviser recommending otherwise would be wrong.

**[The Agent at the Gate](plot_the_agent_at_the_gate.md)**
Cue: the officer, doing the job properly. Closes when the credentials, the salary and the origin are seen as dimensions of difference, so that the party whose function is persuasion occupies the position from which the influence channel carries least.

**[The Plot at the End of the Lane](plot_the_plot_at_the_end_of_the_lane.md)**
Cue: the settlements. Closes when each is found to give back what it was for: information through the channel that was never the constraint, a paid adopter who is now an interested party, an opinion leader who must already have adopted, and a demonstration that works and consumes the thing it produces.

## Stakes

Whether a practice can reach the people it would help most.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not whether the practice is better, because it is, and not whether anybody is ignorant or foolish, because nobody is, but whether evidence can cross a difference that adopting is what creates. The production cannot settle it. It can only show that the information arrived, that the first adopters were sorted by what they could afford to lose, that the discount on their example was right, that the messenger sent to bridge it is the least similar person available, and that the remedy which works needs the thing it is trying to produce.
