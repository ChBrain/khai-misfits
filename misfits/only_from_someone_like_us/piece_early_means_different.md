# Piece: Early Means Different

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). Whatever lets a person absorb a bad year is what lets them go first, so the adopters are sorted on the trait that decides whose experience transfers.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the sorting seat](position_sorting.md), before any meeting is called.
In the founding study's own description of who planted first.

## Load Bearing

Without it the early adopters are simply braver and the play is about temperament.
It is load bearing because **the sorting is on slack rather than on character**: the first movers were
younger, better schooled and farming more land, which is what makes a failed season survivable, so the
population divides on exactly the variable that makes an early result a poor guide for a late decider.

## Apparent

That the difference narrows as adoption spreads.
It does eventually and it widens first, because every adoption moves one person from the side whose
experience would transfer to the side whose experience does not.

## Yearbook

The founding study's report that the early adopters were younger, better educated and operating larger
farms, stated at the origin of the paradigm rather than discovered as a criticism of it.
