# Persona: The Demonstration Plot

## Taxonomy

[Recourse](position_recourse.md). The remedy the field actually uses: the practice run on a holding like theirs, by somebody like them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Modest, local, and by a distance the most effective thing in the play.
Two acres at the end of the lane, farmed by a neighbour, at the neighbour's scale.
Endorsed by every extension service that has ever been evaluated.

## Action

Runs the practice where the doubters can walk to it.
Produces a result on land they recognise.
Settles the question, when it happens.

## Shadow

That it needs the thing it is meant to produce.
**A demonstration by a comparable holder requires a comparable holder willing to go first**, so the
remedy is a witness and the difficulty is that there is no witness, which makes it a description of the
solution rather than a route to it.

## Tell

Is the answer, and is known to be the answer.
Is the answer that has the same precondition as the problem.
