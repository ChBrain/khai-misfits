# Pitch: The Leaflet Reached Everybody

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact about what reached people and about what moved them, which are not the same thing.
Unwilling to call anybody backward, because the discount they applied was correct.
Naming the acreage wherever the acreage is the point.

## Undertow

Under the stall, one relation from the founding study and one from the ground.
The salesmen informed and the neighbours persuaded.
And whatever lets a person absorb a bad year is what lets them go first.

## Nerve

The unease of a practice that is better, known to everybody, and not spreading.
Nobody is ignorant. Nobody is foolish. Nobody needs it explained again.
The witness who would settle it is the person who has not done it yet.

## Echo

After the run: a leaflet delivered to every holding in the county,
two harvests that proved it on land nobody else farms,
a discount that any adviser would endorse,
and an officer whose credentials are the reason they cannot land it.
