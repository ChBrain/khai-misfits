# Position: Recourse

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). The seat trying to produce a witness the next grower will accept.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Every remedy the field has developed, and several of them work.
A demonstration budget, a payment it could offer, an opinion leader it could recruit.
A requirement it cannot meet by wanting to.

## Orders

Produce a comparable case.
Produce it on a holding like theirs, at their scale, under their constraints.
Do it without paying so much that the case stops being comparable.

## Loses

The distinction between the remedy and the thing the remedy needs.
**Every route to a credible witness requires a credible witness**: the demonstration needs a comparable
holder willing to go first, the opinion leader must already have adopted, and the payment that makes
somebody willing is what turns them into an interested party.

## Drives

Toward one neighbour, at this scale, who has done it and will say so,
which is all that was ever needed,
so the seat with the correct answer is the seat whose answer is its own precondition.
