# Piece: The Agent Is Least Like Them

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). Every qualification for the persuading post is a dimension of difference from the audience.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the field day](place_the_field_day.md), in front of forty attentive people.
In the appointment criteria for the post, all of which are reasonable.

## Load Bearing

Without it the remedy is a better campaign and the play has no second half.
It is load bearing because **improving the appointment makes it worse**: training, credential, salary and
origin are each a distance, so a more qualified officer stands further from the influence channel, and the
one professional whose function is persuasion holds the position from which persuasion carries least.

## Apparent

That the agent should be more relatable, or better trained at communicating.
Both are worth doing and neither closes a gap made of acreage and credit.

## Yearbook

The change agent's heterophily gap as the diffusion literature describes it, the party sent to bridge a
difference being the one least like the people on the far side of it.
