## Tension

The play has to close the obvious answer before anything else, and it closes it completely.
The figures are checked and come from trials that were properly run. The writing is clear. The list is
complete. The field day is held and forty people attend, ask sensible questions, and get proper answers
from somebody who knows the agronomy.
At the end of it **nobody in the county is ignorant of the practice.** There is no misunderstanding to
correct and no further explanation waiting to be delivered, so every remedy that consists of saying it
again, or saying it better, has already been spent.
And the planting does not change. The service records good attendance, books the same day for next year,
and has no way to tell a failure to persuade from a failure to inform, because both look like a county
that did not plant.
