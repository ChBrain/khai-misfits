# Process: Informing

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). Getting accurate knowledge of the practice to everybody who could use it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A practice that works and a service whose job is to say so.
At [the bulletin](place_the_bulletin.md), each season, to a list with nobody missing.

## Direction

Toward complete and accurate coverage.
This is done well: the figures are checked, the trials were properly run, the writing is clear, and the
postal list is complete. Nobody in the county is ignorant of the practice at the end of it.

## Lever

Reach, clarity and accuracy, all of which are already at their maximum.
There is no lever here for belief, because the process was only ever able to carry knowledge, and
knowledge was not what was missing.

## Echo

A county that knows,
a field day that is well attended,
and a planting decision that is not affected.
