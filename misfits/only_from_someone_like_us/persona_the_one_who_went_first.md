# Persona: The One Who Went First

## Taxonomy

[Precedent](position_precedent.md). The grower who tried it, honestly reported it, and cannot make it count.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Younger, better schooled, farming more land, with a bank that would carry a failure.
Not reckless: the risk was real and was affordable, which is a different thing.
Entirely willing to show the crop and the books to anybody who asks.

## Action

Plants it.
Gets a good year, and says so.
Cannot understand why nobody follows.

## Shadow

That the qualification to go first is the disqualification as a witness.
**The slack that bought the risk is the difference the next grower is discounting**, so the more clearly
this persona demonstrates the practice, the more clearly it demonstrates it for somebody with slack.

## Tell

Is the proof everybody said was needed.
Is proof about a farm nobody else is running.
