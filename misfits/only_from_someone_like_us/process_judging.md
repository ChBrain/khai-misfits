# Process: Judging

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). Weighing what somebody else's result is evidence for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A good harvest on a holding three times the size of this one.
At [the kitchen table](place_the_kitchen_table.md), and in the head of anybody who attended.

## Direction

Toward an accurate reading of what the case supports.
This is done correctly. A result obtained where a failure was survivable is weaker evidence about a place
where it is not, and the reader who marks it down is doing the arithmetic properly.

## Lever

The comparability of the case, which is not a property of the reader.
Nothing the reader can do improves it, and nothing the reporter can do either, since the difference is in
the holdings and not in the honesty.

## Echo

A discount that any adviser would endorse,
applied to the only evidence that exists,
and a decision that stays where it was.
