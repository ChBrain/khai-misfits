# Persona: The Careful One

## Taxonomy

[Decision](position_decision.md). Forty acres, no credit line, and a season's margin between a decision and a disaster.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Informed, numerate, and reading the same figures as everybody else.
Went to the field day. Believed what was shown. Asked a good question about the second year.
Would plant it tomorrow on evidence from a holding of this size.

## Action

Reads the leaflet.
Watches the harvest at the far end of the parish.
Plants what was planted last year.

## Shadow

That the waiting is a correct reading and not a temperament.
**A result from two hundred acres with a bank line is weak evidence about forty acres without one**, so
the discount is accurate, an adviser recommending otherwise would be wrong, and the caution that looks
like resistance is arithmetic.

## Tell

Is the party the whole campaign is aimed at.
Is the party whose question the campaign cannot answer.
