# Process: Waiting

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). Holding the decision open until a comparable case appears.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An honest answer of not yet, given at a kitchen table.
At [the kitchen table](place_the_kitchen_table.md), and then again the following season.

## Direction

Toward a decision made on evidence that applies.
Waiting is the correct move for somebody who cannot carry a failure, is what any adviser would counsel,
and costs nothing that a wrong decision would not cost more of.

## Lever

Time, which everybody assumes is on the practice's side.
It is not, particularly: the case that would end the wait has to be produced by somebody still waiting,
and each person who stops waiting stops being that somebody.

## Echo

Another season of the old practice,
a gap that is no smaller,
and a service that records the campaign as continuing.
