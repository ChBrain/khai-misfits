## Tension

This is the step everybody misreads, and the play spends its weight here.
The careful one is not resisting. They went to the day, believed the figures, and asked a good question
about the second year. What they are doing is arithmetic: **a result obtained where a failure was
survivable is weaker evidence about a place where it is not**, and forty acres without a credit line is
that place.
An adviser who told them to plant on that evidence would be wrong. The discount is accurate, would be
endorsed by anybody honest, and is indistinguishable from stubbornness to everybody watching from outside.
So the question goes to the person across the table, at the same scale, and the honest answer is not yet.
[Salesmen Inform, Neighbours Persuade](piece_salesmen_inform_neighbours_persuade.md): this room is where
the decision is made and no institution is present in it, and what the room needs is not information but a
neighbour who has done it.
