# Piece: Salesmen Inform, Neighbours Persuade

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). Information and influence run on different channels, and only one of them decides anything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the bulletin](place_the_bulletin.md), which reaches everybody and moves nobody.
At [the kitchen table](place_the_kitchen_table.md), which reaches one person and decides it.

## Load Bearing

Without it the stall is a communication failure and the answer is a better leaflet.
It is load bearing because **the two channels are separate and only the second is homophilous**: knowledge
arrives from anywhere, and the decision to act arrives from somebody who resembles the decider, so a
campaign can succeed completely at the thing it is funded to do and change nothing.

## Apparent

That a sufficiently good explanation persuades.
The explanation was good, was understood, and was believed. What was missing was not clarity.

## Yearbook

The founding study of the paradigm, which traced a seed's spread across two counties and reported the
salesmen as the source of the farmers' first knowledge and the neighbours as what moved them to plant it.
