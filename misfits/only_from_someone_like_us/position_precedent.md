# Position: Precedent

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). The seat held by whoever has already done it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Two hundred acres, a bank line, and a season it could afford to lose.
A result, honestly reported, and a willingness to show anybody who asks.
No idea that its own position is the thing being weighed.

## Orders

Try it.
Report what happened, accurately.
Show the crop to whoever comes.

## Loses

Its standing as evidence, in the eyes of the people who most need evidence.
**What made this seat available is what makes its result untransferable**: the slack that bought the risk
is the difference the next grower is correctly discounting, and no honesty in the reporting can close it.

## Drives

Toward a practice tried and a result published,
which is exactly what everybody said was needed,
so the seat that produced the proof produced it in a form nobody can use.
