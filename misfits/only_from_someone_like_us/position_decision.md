# Position: Decision

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). The seat deciding whether to change what it does this season.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Forty acres, a season's margin, and no line of credit that survives a failure.
The leaflet, read, and the figures in it, believed.
One question that the leaflet does not answer: what happens to somebody in this position.

## Orders

Read the evidence.
Weigh it against what a bad year would cost here.
Act when the answer is clear, and not before.

## Loses

Access to the only evidence that would settle it.
**The seat is not short of information and is short of a comparable case**, because the cases that exist
were produced by people who could afford the risk, which is why they exist, so the seat is asking a
question the record cannot answer.

## Drives

Toward a decision that survives a bad season,
which is the whole of prudence at this scale,
so the seat that reasons correctly is the seat that does not move.
