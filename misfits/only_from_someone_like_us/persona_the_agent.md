# Persona: The Agent

## Taxonomy

[Advocacy](position_advocacy.md). The extension officer, competent, credentialed, and from somewhere else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Knows the agronomy properly and can answer any question about it.
Salaried, trained, degree on the wall, car provided, three counties to cover.
Sincere, and would say the same thing if nobody were paying.

## Action

Prints the bulletin.
Holds the field day.
Comes back next season and does it again.

## Shadow

That every qualification for the post is a dimension of difference.
**The credential that makes this persona worth listening to is what places them outside the channel that
carries**, so improving the appointment, more training, a better speaker, a bigger budget, moves the post
further from the people it exists to reach.

## Tell

Is the only party here whose job is persuasion.
Is the party least able to perform it.
