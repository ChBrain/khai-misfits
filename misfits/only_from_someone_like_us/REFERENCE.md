# Only From Someone Like Us: Reference

The research warrant for Only From Someone Like Us.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the homophily finding in the diffusion literature, on the
founding hybrid seed corn study's division between informing and persuading, and on the change agent's
heterophily gap, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the spread of a better practice as **a proof that can only come from a witness who does not
exist**: a practice is genuinely better, the information about it reaches everybody who would benefit,
and it does not spread, because the people deciding will not act on a demonstration by somebody unlike
them and everybody who has already adopted is, by construction, unlike them. The play models the
documented mechanics, the channel that informs and cannot persuade, the early adopters who were early
because they had slack, the discount applied to their example that is accurate rather than stubborn, the
change agent whose credentials make them the least similar person in the room, and the demonstration that
works and needs an adopter to run it, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of
backward farmers or a failure of communication.

The governing law is the founding study's, and it survives every restatement since: **the salesmen
informed and the neighbours persuaded**. Information and influence run on different channels, and the
influence channel is **homophilous**, which is to say it carries between people who resemble each other.
So a practice does not spread because it is known. It spreads because somebody comparable did it and it
worked for them.

What makes it structural is that the comparability is destroyed by the adoption itself. The founding
study reports that the early adopters were younger, better educated and farming more land, and that is
not a coincidence attached to those two counties: **whatever lets a person absorb a bad year is what
lets them go first**. So the population sorts, and it sorts on exactly the trait that makes their
experience a poor guide for everybody else. The person waiting is not waiting for evidence. They are
waiting for evidence from somebody in their position, and the process that produces evidence removes
people from that position.

What removes any possibility of blame is that the discount is **correct**. A grower with two hundred
acres and a bank line can carry a failed season; a grower with forty cannot, and the fact that the first
one did well says less about the second one's prospects than the first one's confidence implies.
Declining to act on that example is not stubbornness, it is an accurate reading of what the example is
evidence for, and an adviser who told them otherwise would be wrong.

And the party sent to close the gap is selected against closing it. A change agent is trained,
credentialed, salaried by an institution and usually from somewhere else, and every one of those is a
dimension of difference from the people they are sent to. **They are least like the people who most need
convincing**, which is where the influence channel is weakest, so the professional whose entire function
is persuasion holds the one position from which persuasion does not carry.

It is distinct from the house's **After You**, which stages the two-sided market's chicken-and-egg: there
each side waits because the other side is not there yet, and stipulating a **single side** removes the
harm entirely, since there is nobody left to wait for. Stipulate that here, one population of growers and
no complementary party anywhere, and the harm survives, because the wait is not for a counterparty but
for a **witness**, and a witness is not the other half of a market.

It is distinct from the house's **Information Cascade**, where visible prior choices crowd out a
decider's own information: there the quantity is how much weight the private signal keeps, and
stipulating that everybody weights their own information fully removes the harm. Stipulate that here, and
the harm survives intact, because the private signal is **accurate and is the difficulty**: what it says
is that the only available evidence comes from people whose position is not this one, and acting on it is
correct.

It is distinct from the house's **What Everyone Else Does**, where custom sets the standard of care and
following it is a defence: there the quantity is the legal weight of the customary practice, and the harm
is that the custom can lag what a reasonable actor should do. Here nothing is being defended and no
standard is at issue; the difficulty is that the evidence a decision needs cannot be produced by anybody
in a position to have produced it yet.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the waiting
  grower as backward or the change agent as ineffective, because neither is: the discount applied to an
  early adopter's success is an accurate reading of what that success is evidence for, given who could
  afford to run the risk, and the agent is doing the job competently from the one position where the
  influence channel does not carry. The early adopter is not reckless either; they had the slack and
  used it. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that the
  evidence which would move the next person can only be produced by somebody already in their position,
  and adopting is what takes people out of it.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into better communication. More information reaches
  the same people through the same channel and changes nothing, since the channel was never the
  constraint. Paying early adopters converts them into a party with an interest, which is exactly the
  thing a neighbour's example was trusted for not being. Recruiting an opinion leader works and requires
  one who has already adopted, which is the quantity in question. A demonstration run on a comparable
  holding is the remedy the field actually uses, it works, and it needs a comparable holder willing to go
  first, so the remedy consumes the thing it produces. And subsidising the first adopters in each stratum
  is the move that reaches the mechanism, at the cost of paying for what some of them would have done
  anyway.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the rural sociology, the communication research and
  the network studies are theirs. The bounds are carried with them. **The chasm is a popular framing and
  not the mechanism**: much of the field reads the adoption curve as continuous, so the play rests on the
  homophily result and treats the discontinuity as a description of how the stall feels. **Homophily is a
  tendency and not a law**, and the weak-tie literature is the standing counterweight, since the ties that
  carry novelty are the dissimilar ones and a network of only similar people learns nothing new. **The
  founding study is one crop in two counties in one decade**, and its authority comes from what it
  started rather than from its own coverage. **The magnitudes those sources report are deliberately not
  carried here**, because the works themselves could not be read in the pass that cited them: publisher
  hosts and the article archives are blocked to it, so the bibliography, the findings and their directions
  were verified by search and corroborated across independent reports, which is the treatment a citation
  survives and a reported figure is not.
