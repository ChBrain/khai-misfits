# Persona: The Gap

## Taxonomy

[Sorting](position_sorting.md). The difference between whoever has adopted and whoever has not, which the adopting creates.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Impersonal, arithmetical, and present before anybody decides anything.
Made of acreage, credit, age and schooling, none of which anybody chose this season.
Would be the same in any county, for any innovation, in any decade.

## Action

Sorts the population by what a failure would cost them.
Lets through whoever can carry one.
Widens, every time somebody adopts.

## Shadow

That it grows by being crossed.
**Each adoption moves a person from the undecided side to the demonstrated side**, so the pool of people
whose experience would transfer is depleted by exactly the process that is supposed to be filling it.

## Tell

Is not a party and takes no decisions.
Is what decides which evidence the county will ever have.
