# Process: Dividing on What Can Be Lost

## Taxonomy

[Only From Someone Like Us](play_only_from_someone_like_us.md). The population dividing on who can afford a bad season, before anybody deliberates.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A distribution of acreage, credit, age and schooling that was there already.
At [the sorting seat](position_sorting.md), continuously, with no participants.

## Direction

Toward a set of first adopters who could carry a failure.
This is not selection by anybody: it is what a population does when an option carries risk, and it would
happen identically in any county, for any innovation, in any decade.

## Lever

None that anybody holds.
The process completes before the first meeting is called, which is why no party is accountable for it and
why its result arrives looking like a fact about who is forward-thinking.

## Echo

Two adopters with two hundred acres each,
a demonstrated case that is about two hundred acres,
and a gap that widened by exactly the two people who crossed it.
