## Tension

Neither of them is reckless, which the play insists on.
The risk was real and it was affordable, and those are different things. Both are younger, better
schooled, farming more land, and both would show the books to anybody who asked.
And that is the whole of the difficulty. **Whatever lets a person absorb a bad year is what lets them go
first**, so the county has not been divided into the bold and the timid: it has been divided on the one
variable that decides whether an early result says anything about a late decider.
Nobody selected them. **The sorting completed before the first meeting was called**, on acreage and credit
and schooling that were there already, which is why no party is accountable for it and why the outcome
arrives looking like a fact about who is forward-thinking.
And the gap has just widened by exactly the two people who crossed it.
