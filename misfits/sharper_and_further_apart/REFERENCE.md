# Sharper and Further Apart: Reference

The research warrant for **Sharper and Further Apart**, a staging of the phenomenon the literature calls
**motivated numeracy**, an instance of identity-protective cognition (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic):
that where a question is tied to a reasoner's identity, analytical skill is deployed to reach the
conclusion the identity requires rather than the one the evidence supports, so the most capable
reasoners diverge furthest and shared evidence widens the disagreement it was meant to settle, with
nobody lying and nobody failing to think. It is held with the finding that the effect is conditional on
a live identity stake and a question able to carry one, that its magnitude and reach are argued over in
the literature, and that it lifts where the data is stripped of its identity cue or where being right
costs the reasoner nothing among their own, neither of them easy to arrange outside a study.

## Line of Work

Staging motivated numeracy as a **skill-serves-the-side structure**: the promise the effect tests is
that disagreement is a shortage of clear thinking, so people taught to handle evidence properly will,
given the same evidence, converge. Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic put a two-by-two contingency table
to subjects whose numeracy had been measured separately. The table is a genuine trap, since the raw
counts point one way and the ratios the other, so a reader who compares the large numbers instead of
dividing them arrives backwards, and handling it correctly takes real ability. Labelled as a trial of a
skin-rash cream, it behaved as an ability test should: the more numerate got it right and the less
numerate were caught by the counts. Relabelled, with the identical cells and the identical arithmetic,
as a study of a gun-control ban, the answers sorted by politics rather than by skill, and the gap
between opposed groups was widest among the most numerate, so the capability that had corrected the
error in the neutral frame amplified the division in the charged one, without a single faked
calculation (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic). The mechanism the source literature gives is
identity-protective: being right about a table is worth almost nothing to an individual, while being out
of step with their own community costs standing, trust, and belonging, so the sound personal investment
is the belief that fits, and a reasoner with more analytical power simply has more of it to spend on
that investment (Kahan and the Cultural Cognition Project). The play models the documented mechanics:
the ability that genuinely works, the identity cue that changes what an answer is worth, the stake that
dwarfs the value of accuracy, the turn by which real analysis is pointed at reaching a required reading,
the widening gap that grows with capability, and the identity-neutral framing that alone restores the
skill to the answer, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, and not as a claim that partisans are
stupid, dishonest, or arguing in bad faith. The ordinary expectation is that reasoning ability is a
corrective; the finding is that it is a tool, and a tool serves whoever holds it. The governing law is
that **where a conclusion bears on a reasoner's standing among their own people, analytical skill is
turned to reaching that conclusion rather than to testing it, so capability predicts divergence rather
than convergence, and shared evidence widens the disagreement it was supposed to close**.

It is a reasoning-and-identity structure no staged misfit holds, and it is carefully distinct from its
nearest neighbours. It is distinct from Anyone Can See It, naive realism, where each party takes its own
construal of a shared situation for plain objective fact and reads a sincere disagreement as the other
side's bias: there the failure is at the level of **perception**, each side unable to see that it is
construing at all, whereas here both sides can and do compute correctly whenever nothing rides on the
answer, the construal is not the problem, and what turns is the deliberate application of a measured
capability once a stake appears. It is distinct from The Filter Bubble, where a personalised feed
supplies each reader different evidence and the divergence follows from what each was shown: there the
**information supply differs**, whereas here it is held identical by construction, the same cells and
the same arithmetic on one page, so the divergence is produced entirely downstream of the evidence. It
is distinct from Too Sure to Be True, the paradox of unanimity, where agreement so complete that it
outruns the noise is itself evidence of a systematic fault: there the signal is the **absence of
disagreement**, whereas here disagreement is the outcome and its correlation with skill is the finding.
And it is distinct from Don't Look, the ostrich effect, where an agent declines to acquire information
that is cheaply available: there the trap is **avoidance**, whereas here the reasoner engages the data
harder than anyone, and the engagement is what does the damage. It is worth marking, since the source
literature reaches for the phrase, that Kahan describes the aggregate result as a tragedy of the science
communication commons; the house holds this misfit clear of The Commons all the same, since nothing is
depleted here and no shared stock is drawn down by use, the loss being a failure to converge rather than
a resource consumed.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast a liar, a
  fool, or a partisan arguing in bad faith, because the finding it stages is the opposite of all three:
  no calculation is faked, the working is real, and the reasoners who diverge most are the ones who
  worked hardest and had the most ability to work with (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic). Nor will it
  cast the identity stake as a weakness. A person gains almost nothing from being right about a
  contingency table and stands to lose trust, standing, and belonging by holding a belief out of step
  with their own people, so weighting the second above the first is a sound personal judgement and not a
  failure of character (Kahan and the Cultural Cognition Project). The loss is authored by no one: it is
  an ability, a stake, and a question able to carry one, so individually sound belief-formation sums to
  a public that cannot converge, and converges least where its reasoners are strongest.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Stripping the identity
  cue does restore the skill to the answer, and it works cleanly, and it is close to useless where it is
  most wanted: the questions a society actually has to settle arrive already flagged and cannot be
  laundered into rash creams, and an audience that suspects the framing has been managed discounts it on
  that ground alone. The deeper remedy, lowering what a correct answer costs a person among their own,
  is not something a communicator can arrange or an individual can choose. The reckoning is that the
  laboratory fix does not survive contact with the conditions that make the trap matter.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The contingency-table demonstration and the naming of motivated numeracy are
  Kahan, Peters, Dawson, and Slovic's; the identity-protective account of why capability is spent on
  defence is Kahan's and the Cultural Cognition Project's; and the older demonstration that shared
  evidence can widen a disagreement is Lord, Ross, and Lepper's. The scope is held to questions carrying
  a live identity stake, and the magnitude is carried lightly rather than asserted: the size, generality,
  and interpretation of the effect are argued over in the literature, and the house stages the
  mechanism's shape without claiming it settles how much of any real disagreement it explains, or that
  numeracy is generally a liability, or that any particular political camp reasons worse than another,
  since the finding is symmetric and the play stages it symmetrically.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per element, one per load-bearing vector, no filler.

- **The Table**: carries the evidence held identical (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic), the two-by-two
  whose raw counts point one way and whose ratios point the other, a genuine trap with a correct answer
  available to anyone who will divide, and the same cells under either label.
- **The Craft**: carries the ability (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic), numeracy measured separately and
  genuinely effective, which in the neutral frame does exactly what an ability should and supplies the
  power that is later spent the other way.
- **The Stake**: carries the mechanism (Kahan and the Cultural Cognition Project), the standing, trust,
  and belonging that ride on holding the belief one's community holds, against which being right about a
  table is worth almost nothing, so the identity-consistent answer is the sound personal investment.
- **The Turn**: carries the moment the tool changes hands (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic), real
  analysis pointed at reaching a required reading rather than at testing it, with no step faked and the
  working sound throughout.
- **The Widening**: carries the phenomenon whole (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic; Lord, Ross and
  Lepper), answers sorting by politics rather than by skill and the gap between opposed groups greatest
  among the most capable, the corrective running as an amplifier.
- **The Boundary**: carries the condition and the hedge, that the trap needs a live identity stake and a
  question able to carry one, that its magnitude is contested, and that it lifts where the identity cue
  is stripped, which works in a study and rarely in the world, or where being right stops costing the
  reasoner anything at home, which no one can arrange.
