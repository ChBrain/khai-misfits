# Plot: The Better You Are, the Further Apart

## Taxonomy

[Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Square](place_the_square.md), where the answers are gathered and set against the ability that produced them.

## Action

Through [Widening](process_widening.md), [the Widening](piece_the_widening.md) shows: the answers sort by politics rather than by skill, and the distance between opposed groups is greatest among the most numerate, while [the Plain Reader](persona_the_plain_reader.md), who could not work the table in either frame, sits nearer the middle. The scene closes with the corrective running as an amplifier and the remedy tracking the disease (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic; Lord, Ross and Lepper).

## Stage

The comparison is not rhetorical. Ability was measured before the question was asked, so the line can be drawn against it directly, and it runs the wrong way.

## Tension

The standard prescription for public disagreement is more reasoning ability distributed more widely, and this is the one measurement that shows the prescription predicting more distance rather than less, with the least equipped reader the least divided and no one able to recommend that.
