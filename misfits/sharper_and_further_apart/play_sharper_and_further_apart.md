# Play: Sharper and Further Apart

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Sharper and Further Apart.
Here is a promise the modern world runs on: that disagreement is a shortage of clear thinking, and that people taught to handle evidence properly will, given the same evidence, come nearer to each other and nearer to the truth. Teach the reasoning, supply the data, and the argument resolves. It is the premise beneath science education, beneath fact-checking, beneath every attempt to settle a public question by putting the numbers where everyone can see them.
And here is the table that tests it. Kahan, Peters, Dawson, and Slovic gave people a two-by-two contingency table, the kind that shows how many improved and how many worsened, with treatment and without. It is a genuine trap for the unwary: the raw counts point one way and the ratios point the other, so anyone who compares the big numbers instead of dividing them gets it backwards. Handling it correctly takes real skill, and the study measured that skill separately, so it knew who had it (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic).
And in the neutral version, skill did exactly what skill is supposed to do. The table was labelled as a trial of a skin-rash cream. People with more numeracy got it right more often; people with less got caught by the raw counts. Nothing surprising, and everything encouraging: the ability works, it can be taught, and where it is present the answer follows.
And then the identical numbers were labelled as a study of a gun-control ban. The same cells, the same arithmetic, the same trap. Now the answers sorted by politics rather than by skill, and the most capable reasoners did not correct the pull, they served it, arriving more reliably at whichever reading their own side needed, so the gap between opposed groups was widest among those best equipped to close it. The ability that had been a corrective became an amplifier, and it did so without a single faked calculation (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic).
And the reason is not stupidity, because there was none, and not laziness, because the skilled reasoners worked harder. It is that being right about a table is worth almost nothing to a person, while being out of step with their own people is worth a great deal. Nobody's mortgage depends on reading a contingency table correctly. What does depend on their beliefs is who trusts them, who they sit with, who they are taken to be. So for an individual, holding the belief that fits their community is the sound investment and getting the table right is the indulgence, and a person with more analytical power simply has more of it to spend on the investment (Kahan and the Cultural Cognition Project).
And the loss falls where no individual can feel it. Each reasoner is doing well by their own lights, and the sum is a public that cannot converge on a shared fact no matter how much evidence is put in front of it, and that converges least where its reasoners are strongest. The thing which was supposed to be the remedy turns out to track the disease. Nobody chose that, and no one in the room is behaving badly: it is what individually sound belief-formation adds up to when the reward for being right is small and the reward for belonging is not.
And it is older than the numbers. Lord, Ross, and Lepper gave supporters and opponents of capital punishment the identical mixed evidence and watched both sides come away more convinced than before, each finding the studies that agreed with them rigorous and the ones that did not flawed, so the shared evidence widened the gap it should have closed (Lord, Ross and Lepper). The contingency table is that finding sharpened to a point, because it isolates the skill and shows the skill running the wrong way.
And here is what lifts it, and why it costs. Strip the flag: present the same data without the identity cue, as a rash cream rather than a gun ban, and the numeracy goes back to serving the answer. That is a real remedy and it works, and it is close to useless where it is most wanted, because the questions a society actually has to settle come pre-flagged and cannot be laundered, and an audience that suspects the framing has been managed discounts it on that ground alone. Or lower what the answer costs a person at home, so that being right no longer risks their standing, which is not something a communicator can arrange and not something an individual can choose.
And so the misfit is not that people reason badly but that reasoning well is a tool, and a tool serves whoever holds it. The contingency-table finding and its naming are Kahan, Peters, Dawson, and Slovic's; the identity-protective account of why the tool turns is Kahan and the Cultural Cognition Project's; and the older demonstration that shared evidence can widen a disagreement is Lord, Ross, and Lepper's. The house stages the amplifier and holds the bound in view: that it needs an identity genuinely at stake and a question able to carry one, that the effect's size and reach are argued over, and that it lifts where the flag comes off or the answer stops costing anything, so the trap is real and common and bounded, and the whole of it is that a sharper instrument does not find a truer answer, it finds a better-defended one.

## Arc

The bend is this: where a question is tied to a reasoner's identity, analytical skill is deployed to reach the conclusion that identity requires rather than the one the evidence supports, so the most capable reasoners diverge furthest, and shared evidence widens the disagreement it was meant to settle, with no one lying and no one failing to think.
It runs on a skill that genuinely works.
Numeracy handles a contingency table where raw counts mislead and ratios do not, and in a neutral frame the more numerate get it right and the less numerate are caught, exactly as an ability should behave (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic).
And a question that carries a flag. The identical table, relabelled from a rash cream to a gun ban, becomes a thing on which a person's community has a position, so an answer is no longer only an answer.
And a stake that dwarfs the answer. Being right about a table is worth almost nothing to an individual; being out of step with their own people costs standing, trust, and belonging, so the sound personal investment is the belief that fits (Kahan and the Cultural Cognition Project).
And the skill turns to serving it. Nothing is faked and the working is real, but the analysis is pointed at reaching the required reading, and more capability means more power to reach it.
And the gap widens with ability. Answers sort by politics rather than by skill, and the distance between opposed groups is greatest among the most numerate, so the corrective runs as an amplifier (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic).
And it is not new. Opposed partisans given identical mixed evidence on capital punishment both came away more convinced, each crediting what agreed and faulting what did not (Lord, Ross and Lepper).
And the loss is authored by no one. Every reasoner does well by their own lights, and the sum is a public that cannot converge on a shared fact, converging least where its reasoners are strongest.
And the whole is held against its own limit. It needs a live identity stake and a question able to carry one, its magnitude is contested, and it lifts where the data is stripped of its flag or being right stops costing anything at home, neither easy to arrange.

## Stakes

Whether shared evidence can settle a disagreement, when the people best equipped to read it have most to lose by reading it a particular way.
Every plot but the last moves the same way: toward the right answer, which the skill genuinely can reach and does reach whenever nothing rides on it, and toward the required answer, because a reasoner's community holds a position and the reasoner's standing within it does not survive the wrong conclusion. The production cannot make the evidence decide the question, because the skill that would decide it is the same skill that defends against it: analysis pointed at finding and analysis pointed at defending are the one craft seen twice, and adding more of it adds to both. The remedy, stripping the identity cue so the data arrives unflagged, works cleanly in a study and is close to useless where it is most wanted, since the questions a society has to settle come already flagged and cannot be laundered, and an audience that suspects the framing has been managed discounts it on that ground; the deeper remedy, making it cost a person nothing at home to be right, is not something a communicator can arrange or an individual can choose. It can only show that no one lied and no one failed to think, that the working was real and the arithmetic sound, that a person who values belonging over being right about a table is not thereby irrational, and that the failure is what individually sound belief-formation sums to when the reward for accuracy is small and the reward for belonging is not. And it holds the bound in view, because the effect is conditional and its size is argued over: it needs an identity genuinely at stake and a question able to carry one, and it lifts where the flag comes off or the answer stops costing anything, so the whole of the misfit is that a sharper instrument does not find a truer answer, it finds a better-defended one.
