# Process: Widening

## Taxonomy

[Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md). Borne at [The Square](place_the_square.md), precipitated by the answers being sorted against ability rather than assumed to follow from it, in [The Better You Are, the Further Apart](plot_the_better_you_are_the_further_apart.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two opposed groups of reasoners, each having worked the charged table soundly toward the reading its side required.

## Direction

From a disagreement expected to shrink with ability toward one that grows with it, the distance between the groups greatest where the craft is strongest and smallest where it is absent.

## Lever

That capability is spent on defence rather than on correction, so more of it produces more distance, and shared evidence widens the gap it was meant to close (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic; Lord, Ross and Lepper).

## Echo

[The Widening](piece_the_widening.md), the corrective shown running as an amplifier.
