# Position: Opposite

## Taxonomy

[Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md). The seat facing the Numerate across the same table, identical in every respect but which community it answers to.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The same measured ability as the seat opposite, and the same right to be believed about its own working.
Its own community, whose regard is as real and whose loss would cost as much.
A clear view of the other seat's motivated reasoning, and none at all of its own.

## Orders

Work it through, exactly as the seat opposite does: apply the craft to the cells and reach the reading your side requires, by steps that will survive checking.
Diagnose the other seat accurately, since from here its defence is plainly visible as a defence.
Accept what the seat does: it performs the identical operation in the other direction, which is what makes the trap structural and not partisan.

## Loses

The ability to apply its own accurate diagnosis to itself.
Because the seat can see motivated reasoning perfectly well when it is done by someone else and not at all when it is done here, it loses the one insight that would free it while holding the very concept that names it, and holds it as a weapon rather than a mirror.
It keeps a correct account of the other side's failure and loses any purchase on its own.

## Drives

Toward the one whose symmetry removes the last place blame could land,
reasoning soundly toward its own required conclusion and, by doing so, making the pattern a structure rather than one side's fault,
so the seat that could have been cast as the villain is the seat that proves there is none, equally able and equally caught.
