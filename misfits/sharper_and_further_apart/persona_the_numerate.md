# Persona: The Numerate

## Taxonomy

[Numerate](position_numerate.md). The capable reasoner, right on the neutral table and furthest from its opposite number on the charged one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful and equipped, the one who divides where dividing is called for and is not caught by a large raw count.
Measured as skilled before the question was ever put, so the ability is a fact about it and not a claim it makes.
No arrogance on show: it works the problem rather than pronouncing on it, and in a neutral frame it is simply right.

## Action

Runs [Work It Through](plan_work_it_through.md): through [Computing](process_computing.md) at [the Rash Study](place_the_rash_study.md) it brings [the Craft](piece_the_craft.md) to the ratios and reaches the correct answer; then at [the Gun Study](place_the_gun_study.md), through [Sifting](process_sifting.md), it applies the identical craft to the identical cells and reaches the reading its own side requires.
Cannot notice the difference between the two performances from inside either, since both feel like working a problem carefully and both are, in every checkable respect, exactly that.
Does what the seat rewards: it spends real capability on the answer that protects its standing, which is the answer worth having.

## Shadow

Not dishonest: no step is faked, no figure altered, and the working would survive audit line by line, so an accusation of bad faith would be simply false.
Because the ability that corrects an error in the neutral frame is the ability that constructs a defence in the charged one, its greatest strength is what carries it furthest from the truth, and it is worse off for being better equipped.
It is the best reasoner in the play and the one that ends up furthest from its opposite.

## Tell

Produces a longer and more sophisticated justification on the charged question than on the neutral one, and reads that as having taken the harder problem seriously.
Cannot be corrected by more evidence, since more evidence is more material for the craft to work with, so the ordinary remedy for error feeds the mechanism instead of checking it.
