# Persona: The Plain Reader

## Taxonomy

[Plain Reader](position_plain_reader.md). The one without the craft, caught by the raw counts in the neutral frame and, for want of the tool, nearer the middle in the charged one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Straightforward, comparing the big numbers to the big numbers, which is what a table looks like it is asking for.
Takes the counts at face value because ratios are not a thing this reader reaches for, and the surface reading is honest and immediate.
No pretence on show: it does not claim an ability it lacks, and it answers the question as it appears.

## Action

Runs [Read It Plain](plan_read_it_plain.md): at [the Rash Study](place_the_rash_study.md), through [Computing](process_computing.md), it compares raw counts rather than dividing and is caught by the trap the table was built to set.
Cannot construct an elaborate defence of a required conclusion at [the Square](place_the_square.md), because building one takes the very capability it does not have.
Does what it can with what it has, and by that limitation ends up nearer its opposite numbers than the skilled do.

## Shadow

Not the play's fool: it gets the neutral table wrong, plainly and measurably, and would be better off with the craft in every ordinary sense.
Because the tool it lacks is the tool the others spend on defence, its incapacity is the only thing in the play that limits divergence, so the least equipped reasoner is the least polarised one, which is not a recommendation and is the finding.
It is worse at the question and closer to the other side.

## Tell

Gives a short answer where the skilled give long ones, and the shortness is not modesty but the absence of material to build with.
Cannot be improved without being drawn into the mechanism, since teaching it the craft would equip it to defend rather than merely to compute, which is the uncomfortable shape of the whole result.
