# Pitch: Just Look at the Data

## Taxonomy

[Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reasonable and analytic.
A table of numbers, a person willing to work it through properly rather than eyeball it, and the ordinary confidence that a disagreement survives only as long as the evidence is unclear.
Nothing false in it: the table has a correct answer, the skill to reach it is real and teachable, and in a neutral frame the skilled do reach it.

## Undertow

Under the working, an answer already required before the arithmetic starts.
Being right about a table is worth almost nothing to a person, while being out of step with their own people costs standing and trust and belonging, so the analysis is pointed at the reading the side needs, and more ability means more power to get there (Kahan and the Cultural Cognition Project).
No liar and no fool: nothing is faked, the working is sound, and preferring to belong is not a failure of character.

## Nerve

The faith that better reasoning closes a gap, and the catch that reasoning is a tool and a tool serves whoever holds it:
that the same craft which finds the answer in a neutral frame defends against it in a charged one, so the corrective and the amplifier are the one capability seen twice, and adding more of it adds to both,
and just look at the data is not the end of the argument but the point at which the skilled start pulling hardest in opposite directions.

## Echo

After the run: a public question that will not close however many numbers are put in front of it, an expert audience polarising on a chart, and the widest gap sitting exactly where the sharpest readers are (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic; Lord, Ross and Lepper).
And the remedy, clean in a study and thin in the world: strip the identity cue and the skill serves the answer again, though the questions a society must settle arrive already flagged, cannot be laundered, and are discounted by anyone who suspects the framing was managed.
And the bound held in view, its magnitude argued over: it needs a live identity stake and a question able to carry one, so the whole of it is that a sharper instrument does not find a truer answer, it finds a better-defended one.
