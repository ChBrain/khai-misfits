# Position: Plain Reader

## Taxonomy

[Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md). The seat without the craft, wrong on the neutral table and, for want of the tool, nearer the middle on the charged one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

No measured facility with ratios, and a straightforward willingness to compare the numbers as given.
The same identity stake as every other seat, and no instrument with which to serve it.
An answer that is short because there is nothing to build a longer one out of.

## Orders

Read it plain: compare the counts as they stand, which is what the table appears to ask.
Answer the question you can see, without claiming a method you do not have.
Accept what the seat does: it will be caught by a trap built precisely for it, and it will not be able to construct a defence when a defence is what the stake calls for.

## Loses

The neutral question, plainly and measurably, which is a real loss and not a disguised advantage.
Because the seat lacks the craft, it loses the answer whenever the answer requires dividing, and it would be better off in every ordinary sense with the ability the other seats have.
It keeps only a smaller distance from the seats opposite, which it did not choose and cannot claim credit for.

## Drives

Toward the one whose incapacity is the only thing in the play that limits divergence,
reading plainly because plainly is all it can read and, by that limitation, never acquiring the tool that would carry it further from the other side,
so the seat that is worst at the question is the seat that ends nearest its opposites, an outcome nobody would prescribe and the finding all the same.
