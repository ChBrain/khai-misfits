# Plan: Read It Plain

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the plain reader's seat](position_plain_reader.md), inside [Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Plain Reader](position_plain_reader.md)

## Direction

Compare the numbers as they are given. The larger count against the larger count, which is what a table of this shape appears to be asking for, and answer accordingly. Do not claim a method you do not have, and do not pretend to a calculation you cannot perform. Accept that this will be caught by a table built to catch it, and accept too that when a stake appears you will have nothing elaborate to build with, and will answer briefly where others answer at length.

## Orders

([The Plain Reader](persona_the_plain_reader.md) reads through [Computing](process_computing.md) at [the Rash Study](place_the_rash_study.md), and appears again at [the Square](place_the_square.md) as the comparison against which [the Widening](piece_the_widening.md) is measured.)

## Implementation

([The Rash Study](place_the_rash_study.md) is where the raw-count reading meets a trap built for it and fails; [the Square](place_the_square.md) is where that same failure shows its other face, since the seat has no craft to spend on defence and so sits nearer the seats opposite than the skilled do.)

## Targets

- [ ] the counts are compared as given, without a method the seat does not hold
- [ ] no ability is claimed that has not been measured
- [ ] the neutral answer is wrong, plainly and without disguise
- [ ] no elaborate defence is constructed on the charged question, there being nothing to construct one from
