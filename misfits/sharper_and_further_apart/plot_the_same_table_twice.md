## Cue

## Action

[The Table](piece_the_table.md) stands: a two-by-two of improved and worsened, with treatment and without, whose raw counts point one way and whose ratios point the other, so a reader who compares the large numbers instead of dividing arrives backwards. It is a genuine trap with a genuine answer, and the ability to reach it has been measured separately in every reader. The scene closes with one dataset, a correct conclusion available to anyone who will do the arithmetic, and the identical cells waiting to be relabelled (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic).

## Stage

Nothing is hidden and nothing is rigged. The numbers are the numbers, the correct answer exists, and the skill that reaches it is real, teachable, and known in advance for each reader.

## Tension

Everything that follows will be produced by a label, since the evidence is about to be held exactly constant, which forecloses in advance every explanation that turns on what either side was shown.
