# Piece: The Turn

## Taxonomy

[Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md). The moment real analysis is pointed at reaching a required reading rather than at testing one, with no step faked.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Occurring at [The Gun Study](place_the_gun_study.md), inside the working rather than around it, so nothing on the page marks where it happened (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit is a story about people refusing to look: without the turn, a partisan would simply decline the arithmetic, and the remedy would be to make them do it, which is the remedy everyone already believes in.
It bears the load as the reason the obvious fix fails. Because the analysis genuinely happens, and happens well, insisting on more rigour supplies more capability to the defence, so the standard prescription for error feeds the mechanism rather than checking it.

## Apparent

Diligence, and undetectably so: a longer justification on a harder question reads as having taken it seriously, and from inside the seat it is exactly that.
