# Piece: The Table

## Taxonomy

[Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md). The two-by-two of improved and worsened, with treatment and without, whose raw counts point one way and whose ratios point the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Laid out at [The Rash Study](place_the_rash_study.md) and again, cell for cell identical, at [The Gun Study](place_the_gun_study.md), the same arithmetic under two labels (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic).

## Load Bearing

Remove its identity across the two conditions and the finding evaporates: if the charged version showed different numbers, the divergence would be ordinary, since people given different evidence may reasonably differ, and there would be nothing to explain.
It bears the load as the control that makes the result mean anything. Because the evidence is held exactly constant while only the label moves, everything that follows is produced downstream of the data, which is what rules out every explanation involving what each side was shown.

## Apparent

A test of ability, and genuinely one: it has a correct answer, it catches the unwary on raw counts, and reaching it takes a skill that can be measured.
