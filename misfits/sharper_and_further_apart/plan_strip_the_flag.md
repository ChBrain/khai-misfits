# Plan: Strip the Flag

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the framer's seat](position_framer.md), inside [Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Framer](position_framer.md)

## Direction

Present the identical data without the cue that tells a reader which side the answer serves. Alter nothing in the cells, since the remedy is the absence of the label and not any change to the evidence, and a communicator who touched the numbers would be doing the one dishonest thing available here. Expect it to work, because the neutral condition is the finding's own control and the effect is demonstrated. And name what it cannot do: the questions a society actually has to settle arrive already flagged and cannot be laundered, and an audience that suspects the framing was managed discounts it on that ground alone.
