# Piece: The Widening

## Taxonomy

[Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md). The gap between opposed groups growing with ability rather than shrinking, greatest among the most capable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Read at [The Square](place_the_square.md), where answers are sorted against measured skill and the line runs the wrong way (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic; Lord, Ross and Lepper).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the trap is merely disappointing: skill failing to help would leave the case for teaching it untouched, since a neutral instrument that does nothing is not an argument against instruments.
It bears the load as the finding that inverts the prescription. Because divergence tracks capability positively, the standard remedy for public disagreement, more reasoning ability distributed more widely, is shown to predict more distance rather than less, and the corrective and the disease are measured by the same variable.

## Apparent

Sophistication, from either side: the most capable produce the most elaborate and defensible cases, which reads as the argument being taken seriously by serious people.

## Yearbook

Stands over the close in [The Better You Are, the Further Apart](plot_the_better_you_are_the_further_apart.md), the pattern that names the play.
It works together with [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the divergence and the one condition under which it lifts.
