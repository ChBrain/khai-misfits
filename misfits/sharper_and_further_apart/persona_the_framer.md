# Persona: The Framer

## Taxonomy

[Framer](position_framer.md). The one who takes the identity cue off the data so the skill serves the answer again, and finds the remedy thin exactly where it is needed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Constructive and undramatic, presenting the same numbers without the label that tells a reader which side they are on.
Reads as good practice rather than intervention: a communicator choosing a neutral example is doing something every teacher does.
No manipulation on show: the data is unaltered, and only the flag is absent.

## Action

Runs [Strip the Flag](plan_strip_the_flag.md): at [the Square](place_the_square.md), through [Stripping](process_stripping.md), it presents the identical cells without the identity cue and holds [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), restoring the craft to the answer where the framing can be controlled.
Cannot launder the questions that matter, since a society's live disputes arrive already flagged, already known, and already attached to sides, and there is no neutral label available for them.
Does the one thing that demonstrably works, in the one setting where it is least needed.

## Shadow

Not naive: the remedy is real and it is the finding's own control condition, so it is not a hopeful guess but a demonstrated effect.
Because its method depends on an audience not knowing which side the data serves, it is defeated the moment the audience wonders, and a reader who suspects the framing has been managed discounts it for that reason alone, so its success requires an innocence its own use erodes.
It is the play's only remedy and it works best where nothing is at stake.

## Tell

Reaches for a rash cream when the question is a gun ban, and is told, reasonably, that the rash cream is not the question.
Cannot repeat the trick on the same audience, since a framing recognised as a framing carries its own flag, so the remedy is spent in the using of it.
