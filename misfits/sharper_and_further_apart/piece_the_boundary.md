## Place

Held at [The Square](place_the_square.md), where the remedy is real, demonstrated, and reaches only questions nobody was divided about (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overclaims badly: without the boundary it reads as a verdict that numeracy is a liability, that education makes disagreement worse, or that one political camp reasons worse than another, none of which the source supports and the last of which the symmetry of the finding directly contradicts.
It bears the load as the honest limit on a result that invites overreach. It also carries the magnitude hedge, since the effect's size, generality, and interpretation are argued over in the literature, so the house stages the mechanism's shape without claiming it settles how much of any real disagreement it explains.

## Apparent
