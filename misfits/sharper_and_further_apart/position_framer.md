# Position: Framer

## Taxonomy

[Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md). The seat that takes the identity cue off the data, restoring the craft to the answer wherever the framing can be controlled.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Control of the label, and none at all of the data, which it presents unaltered.
A demonstrated remedy, since the neutral condition is the finding's own control and is known to work.
No purchase on the questions that matter, which arrive already flagged and already attached to sides.

## Orders

Strip the flag: present the identical cells without the cue that tells a reader which side the answer serves.
Alter nothing else, since the remedy is the absence of the label and not any change to the evidence.
Accept what the seat does: it works cleanly where nothing is at stake, and the questions worth settling are the ones it cannot reach.

## Loses

The remedy itself, in the act of using it.
Because the method depends on an audience not knowing which side the data serves, it is defeated the moment the audience wonders, and a framing recognised as a framing carries a flag of its own, so the seat spends its one instrument each time it deploys it and cannot deploy it twice on the same room.
It keeps a real and demonstrated effect and loses the ability to apply it where the effect would matter.

## Drives

Toward the one whose genuine remedy is thinnest exactly where it is most needed,
stripping the flag because stripping the flag demonstrably restores the skill to the answer and, by doing so, buying convergence only on questions nobody was divided about,
so the seat holding the play's one working correction is the seat that cannot bring it to bear on anything that was ever in dispute.
