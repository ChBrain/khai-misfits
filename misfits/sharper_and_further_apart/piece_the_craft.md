# Piece: The Craft

## Taxonomy

[Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md). Numeracy: real analytical ability, measured before the question was put, which resolves the table in a neutral frame.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Brought to bear at [The Rash Study](place_the_rash_study.md), where it produces the right answer, and carried unchanged into [The Gun Study](place_the_gun_study.md), where it produces a different one (Kahan, Peters, Dawson and Slovic).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no paradox, only ordinary partisanship: unskilled reasoners diverging on a charged question would surprise nobody and would say nothing about reasoning at all.
It bears the load as the thing that was supposed to be the remedy. Because the divergence grows with the ability rather than shrinking, the misfit lands on the one faculty every account of good public reasoning depends on, and it does so without that faculty failing at anything it was measured for.

## Apparent

A corrective, and rightly so: it is teachable, it is valuable, and in every setting without a stake it delivers exactly what is claimed for it.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Skill Tells](plot_skill_tells.md), doing its proper work before it is turned.
It works together with [The Stake](piece_the_stake.md), the tool and the reason it changes hands.
