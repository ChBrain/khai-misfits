# Persona: The Opposite

## Taxonomy

[Opposite](position_opposite.md). The equally capable reasoner on the other side, doing the identical thing in the other direction, and the proof that neither side is the villain.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Just as careful and just as equipped, indistinguishable from its counterpart on every measure except which community it answers to.
Divides where dividing is called for, is not caught by raw counts, and reaches its conclusion by real work.
No malice on show, and no less rigour: whatever is said of its ability is true of the other's too.

## Action

Runs [Work It Through](plan_work_it_through.md), the same plan as [the Numerate](persona_the_numerate.md): at [the Gun Study](place_the_gun_study.md), through [Sifting](process_sifting.md), it applies the identical craft to the identical cells and arrives, by sound working, at the reading its own side requires.
Cannot be distinguished from its counterpart by any test of method, since both worked correctly, both spent real effort, and both produced defensible arithmetic.
Does exactly what the other does, from the other direction, which is what makes the mechanism structural rather than partisan.

## Shadow

Not the mirror image of a wrongdoer, because there is no wrongdoer to mirror: its stake is as real, its community as genuine, and its reasoning as sound as its counterpart's.
Because its presence makes the pattern symmetric, it removes the last place blame could land, so a play that could otherwise be read as an indictment of one side becomes an account of a structure both sides sit inside.
It is the persona whose only job is to make the trap unattributable.

## Tell

Reads its counterpart's sophisticated justification as motivated reasoning, correctly, and its own as careful analysis, also from inside, incorrectly.
Cannot see the symmetry from within it, since the one thing invisible from either seat is that the seat opposite is doing the same thing, so each can diagnose the other exactly and neither can diagnose itself.
