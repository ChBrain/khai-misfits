# Plan: Work It Through

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the numerate's seat](position_numerate.md), and of [the opposite's seat](position_opposite.md) in the other direction, inside [Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Numerate](position_numerate.md)

## Direction

Do the arithmetic properly. Divide where dividing is called for and do not be caught by a large raw count that points the wrong way, since the table is built to catch exactly that. Where the question carries no stake, this delivers the answer and delivers it reliably. Where it does carry one, apply the same care to reaching the reading your standing can survive, because being right about a table buys nothing and being out of step with your own people costs a great deal, and fake no step along the way: the working must hold up, and it will.

## Orders

([The Numerate](persona_the_numerate.md) computes through [Computing](process_computing.md) at [the Rash Study](place_the_rash_study.md), then sifts through [Sifting](process_sifting.md) at [the Gun Study](place_the_gun_study.md), where [the Opposite](persona_the_opposite.md) runs the identical plan the other way.)

## Implementation

([The Rash Study](place_the_rash_study.md) is where [the Craft](piece_the_craft.md) meets a question with nothing riding on it and produces the correct answer; [the Gun Study](place_the_gun_study.md) is where the same craft meets [the Stake](piece_the_stake.md) and produces [the Turn](piece_the_turn.md), the analysis pointed at a required reading with every step still sound.)
