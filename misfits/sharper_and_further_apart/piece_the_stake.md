## Owner

## Place

Entering at [The Gun Study](place_the_gun_study.md) with nothing but a label, since the numbers do not change and the cost does (Kahan and the Cultural Cognition Project).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the craft never turns: with nothing riding on the conclusion, ability is spent on reaching the answer, which is precisely what the neutral condition shows and why it is the control.
It bears the load as the reason the turn is rational rather than weak. Being right about a contingency table is worth almost nothing to any individual, while being out of step with one's own people costs a great deal, so the identity-consistent conclusion is the sound personal investment, and this piece is what makes the misfit an account of incentives instead of an accusation of character.

## Apparent

Not visible at all as a stake: from inside, it feels like knowing which answers are serious and which are not, which is indistinguishable from judgement.
