# Position: Numerate

## Taxonomy

[Sharper and Further Apart](play_sharper_and_further_apart.md). The seat of the capable reasoner, whose capability serves the answer when nothing rides on it and the side when something does.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Measured analytical ability, established before the question was put and not in doubt.
A community whose regard depends in part on what it concludes.
No way, from inside, to tell working a problem from working toward a conclusion, since both consist of correct steps correctly taken.

## Orders

Work it through: divide where dividing is called for, and do not be caught by a raw count that points the wrong way.
Reach the answer your standing can survive, since being right about a table buys nothing and being out of step costs a great deal.
Accept what the seat does: it spends real capability, and the capability goes where the stake is.

## Loses

Its own corrective, which is the shape of it.
Because the seat's ability is what would catch an error and also what constructs a defence, it loses the use of its best faculty precisely on the questions where that faculty was most needed, and loses it without ever feeling the loss, since defending feels exactly like reasoning.
It keeps every unit of skill it ever had and loses the one function skill was supposed to serve.

## Drives

Toward the one whose competence carries it furthest from the truth,
working carefully because working carefully is what it does and, by doing so, building the better-defended conclusion rather than the truer one,
so the seat best equipped to close a disagreement is the seat through which the disagreement widens, sound in every step and furthest apart in the sum.
