# Persona: The Watch

## Taxonomy

[Watch](position_watch.md), inside [Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The archetype of the late hand that sees the harm at last and reaches to steer, holding the knowledge of where the technology should go at the very stage when the power to move it has gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A timid overseer who waited too long, who should have moved sooner and lost the nerve, as though the technology could still be turned if only the hand on the wheel were firmer, and the failure were one of courage rather than of leverage.

## Action

Turn the wheel. At [the Ground](place_the_ground.md), the Watch meets [the Reckoning](piece_the_reckoning.md), the harm now plain and measurable, then at [the Bind](place_the_bind.md) on [Turn the Wheel](plan_turn_the_wheel.md) through [Steering](process_steering.md) meets [the Locked Wheel](piece_the_locked_wheel.md), the control grown slow and costly and resisted by all who are built around the technology (Collingridge).

## Shadow

Its lateness is the structure's and not a want of nerve. The Watch is not failing to act boldly on a thing it could still move: the knowledge that warrants steering is made only by the very deployment that entrenches the technology, so by the time the harm is plain enough to act on, the thing is already set, and no firmness of hand will make a locked wheel turn cheaply. It reaches at the first moment it can know to reach, and finds the power spent exactly as the knowledge arrives. Its lateness is not delay but the order of knowing and doing.

## Tell

It sees the harm at last and reaches for the wheel, and the wheel will not turn for any nerve.
