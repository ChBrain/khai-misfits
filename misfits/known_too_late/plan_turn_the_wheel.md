# Plan: Turn the Wheel

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the watch's seat](position_watch.md), inside [Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Watch](position_watch.md)

## Direction

Reach for the controls and try to steer the technology from its harm, since you have the knowledge that warrants it and the duty to act on what you now plainly see. Know that the knowledge you now hold was made only by the deployment that entrenched the thing, that the wheel you reach for has grown slow and costly and resisted by all who are built around it, and that no firmness of hand will turn a locked wheel cheaply, your lateness the order of knowing and not a want of nerve.

## Orders

([The Watch](persona_the_watch.md) meets [the Reckoning](piece_the_reckoning.md) at [the Ground](place_the_ground.md), then works [the Locked Wheel](piece_the_locked_wheel.md) through [Steering](process_steering.md) at [the Bind](place_the_bind.md), reaching to steer the entrenched technology and finding the wheel too costly and slow to turn.)

## Implementation

([Steering](process_steering.md) at [the Bind](place_the_bind.md) meets [the Locked Wheel](piece_the_locked_wheel.md), because the control that warrants using has grown slow and costly and resisted by all built around the thing, so the reach to steer finds no cheap purchase.)

## Targets

- [ ] the harm is seen plain and the reach for control is made, the knowledge now warranting it
- [ ] the reach is understood as timely, made at the first moment the harm can be known
- [ ] it is seen that the wheel has grown slow and costly and resisted, the power gone as the knowledge comes
- [ ] the lateness is owned as the order of knowing and doing, not a failure of nerve
