# Persona: The Steward

## Taxonomy

[Steward](position_steward.md), inside [Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The archetype of the one who foresees the dilemma itself and builds and adopts the technology to keep the later decision open, and who marks that corrigibility is costly, imperfect, and not always the wiser course.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A brake on progress, a worrier who would slow every promising thing for fear of a harm no one can name, trading away the gains of commitment and scale out of an excess of caution, as though flexibility were free and holding options open cost nothing.

## Action

Keep it open. At [the Bind](place_the_bind.md), the Steward on [Keep It Open](plan_keep_it_open.md) through [Keeping](process_keeping.md) holds [the Corrigible Hand](piece_the_corrigible_hand.md), building and adopting the technology to stay flexible and reversible so that when the knowledge arrives the power to act has not wholly gone (Collingridge; Genus and Stirling).

## Shadow

Its caution is the one real answer, and it is dearly bought. The Steward is not slowing progress out of nameless fear: it foresees the structure, that knowledge and power never coincide, and so keeps the later decision open on purpose, favouring the reversible over the committed. But this spends a real cost, the gains of commitment and standardisation and scale forgone, and the foresight it leans on is itself imperfect and can never fully close the gap, and a technology kept forever unset never delivers what commitment would. Its remedy works against the grain of the efficiency it gives up, and it is not the clean foreknowledge of harm anyone would wish.

## Tell

It keeps the thing bendable on purpose, buying a later power to steer at the price of the gains of setting it firm.
