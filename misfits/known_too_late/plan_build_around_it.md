# Plan: Build Around It

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the builder's seat](position_builder.md), inside [Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Builder](position_builder.md)

## Direction

Sink your investment into the technology, rest your systems on it, settle your standards and habits around it, because at the stage you adopt it there is no reason not to and every reason to. Know that each sound decision to use it adds to the weight that will hold it in place, that the dependency you grow is what will make it costly to change when the harm shows, and that you harden the thing not by carelessness but by using it well.

## Orders

([The Builder](persona_the_builder.md) lays [the Setting](piece_the_setting.md) through [Settling](process_settling.md) at [the Ground](place_the_ground.md), sinking investment and growing dependency around the technology that works, hardening it against later change.)

## Implementation

([Settling](process_settling.md) at [the Ground](place_the_ground.md) lays [the Setting](piece_the_setting.md), because building on what works is sound, so the sunk cost and grown dependency harden the technology into place beyond cheap change.)

## Targets

- [ ] the world builds on the technology, sinking investment and growing dependency and settling standards
- [ ] the building is understood as sound, the thing working and showing no harm at the stage of adoption
- [ ] it is seen that this sound building is the very weight that sets the thing beyond cheap reach
- [ ] the hardening is owned as a byproduct of using it well, not a mob's carelessness
