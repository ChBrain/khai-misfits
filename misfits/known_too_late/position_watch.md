# Position: Watch

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The seat of the late hand that sees the harm at last and reaches to steer, and marks that its power is least exactly when its knowledge is at last enough.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The knowledge at last, the harm now plain and measurable and no longer deniable.
A wheel to reach for, the levers of control by which the technology might be steered or curbed.
A world already built around the thing, its investment sunk and its dependency grown, resisting the change.

## Orders

Turn the wheel, now that the harm is plain and you know at last where the thing should go.
Reach for the controls and try to steer the technology from its harm, since you have the knowledge that warrants it and the duty to act on what you now plainly see.
Know that the knowledge you now hold was made only by the deployment that entrenched the thing, that the wheel you reach for has grown slow and costly and resisted by all who are built around it, and that no firmness of hand will turn a locked wheel cheaply, your lateness the order of knowing and not a want of nerve.

## Loses

The power to steer, gone exactly as the knowledge to steer by arrives.
Because the harm becomes knowable only through the use that sets the technology hard, the seat reaches for the wheel at the first moment it can know to reach and finds the thing already entrenched beyond cheap change, so the knowledge and the power pass each other and it is left holding one without the other.
It keeps its clear sight of the harm and its will to act and loses no cause for blame, a watch that moved as soon as it could know to and found the wheel locked.

## Drives

Toward the late hand that knows at last and cannot move the thing,
seeing the harm plain and reaching for the wheel only to find it grown costly and slow and resisted, the power spent as the knowledge comes,
so the seat that alone knows where to steer can no longer steer, its knowledge full exactly where its power is gone.
