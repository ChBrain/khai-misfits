# Plot: It Hardens

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The technology is adopted and taken up, because it works.

## Action

At [the Ground](place_the_ground.md), [the Builder](persona_the_builder.md) from [the Builder's seat](position_builder.md) on [Build Around It](plan_build_around_it.md) through [Settling](process_settling.md) lays [the Setting](piece_the_setting.md), the sunk investment and grown dependency and settled standards that harden the technology against change.

## Stage

The clay turns to stone. Investment sinks into the technology, other systems come to depend on it, standards and habits and whole industries settle around it, and what was once soft and open now sets into place. None of this is reckless: each decision to build on what works, to standardize around what is already spreading, to invest further in what has already proven itself, is sound on its own terms. But the sum of those sound decisions is entrenchment, a technology that has grown too costly to unmake, and it hardens exactly during the stretch when no harm is yet in view to have warranted restraint (Collingridge; Marchant). No villain: the builder lays the setting in good faith, on the ground as it stands.

## Tension

The thing set beyond cheap reach, and no harm yet in view to have warned against it.
