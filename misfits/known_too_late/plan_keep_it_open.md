# Plan: Keep It Open

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), inside [Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Steward](position_steward.md)

## Direction

Build and adopt the technology to stay flexible and reversible, favouring the corrigible over the committed, so that when the knowledge of the harm arrives the power to act on it has not wholly gone. Know that this spends a real cost, the gains of commitment and standardisation and scale given up, that the foresight you lean on is itself imperfect and never closes the gap fully, and that a thing kept forever unset never delivers what setting it firm would, the hedge you stand on and the bound you must name.
