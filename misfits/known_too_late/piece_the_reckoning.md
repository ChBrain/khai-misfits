# Piece: The Reckoning

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The late arrival of knowledge: the harm at last plain, measurable, no longer deniable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Ground](place_the_ground.md), met by [the Watch](persona_the_watch.md): the count finally comes in, long after the wager was placed.

## Load Bearing

The harmful consequences at last emerge from use at scale and are plainly seen: the knowledge that would have warranted steering arrives only now, when the thing is already set (Collingridge). This is not a discovery that anyone withheld. It is the information problem resolving itself the only way it could, through the very scale and time that also spent the power to act on what it reveals. The Reckoning is real knowledge, hard won and accurate, and it is also, structurally, too late to be cheap.

## Apparent

It looks like a problem discovered, as if discovery itself meant the problem could now be fixed.

## Yearbook

Met by [the Watch](persona_the_watch.md), staged at [the Ground](place_the_ground.md), dramatized in [Now It Shows](plot_now_it_shows.md). It is the knowledge that comes too late, and it sends the Watch to [The Locked Wheel](piece_the_locked_wheel.md).
