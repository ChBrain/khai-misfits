# Plot: Nothing Yet to Show

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The plastic thing sits in hand, and with it the wish to know what it will do.

## Action

At [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), [the Maker](persona_the_maker.md) meets [the Blind Window](piece_the_blind_window.md), the power to steer high but the knowledge of where to steer absent, since the consequences do not yet exist to be measured.

## Stage

This is the first horn: the information problem. The technology can still be controlled, its clay soft, its course open, but no one can yet say which way it ought to be steered, because the harms that would warrant steering have not happened and cannot be observed before they happen. They emerge only once the technology is developed and used at scale, so at this stage there is nothing yet to show, no evidence, no measurable consequence, only possibility. The reasonable response is to let the thing grow, since restraining every new technology on the mere chance of an unknown future harm would be a strange and paralyzing rule (Collingridge). No villain: the blindness here is the order of things, not a failure to look. The window is real, and it is closed by the nature of the moment, not by neglect.

## Tension

The thing let grow, the cheap window spent blind.
