# Position: Steward

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The seat of the one who foresees the dilemma and builds to keep the later decision open, and marks that corrigibility is costly, imperfect, and not always the wiser course.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A foresight of the structure itself, the knowledge that power and knowledge will never coincide over the technology's life.
A way to keep the later decision open: to build and adopt the thing so as to stay flexible and reversible, unset.
A clear sight of the price, the gains of commitment and scale that staying bendable forgoes.

## Orders

Keep it open, and do not let the thing set before you can see its form.
Build and adopt the technology to stay flexible and reversible, favouring the corrigible over the committed, so that when the knowledge of the harm arrives the power to act on it has not wholly gone.
Know that this spends a real cost, the gains of commitment and standardisation and scale given up, that the foresight you lean on is itself imperfect and never closes the gap fully, and that a thing kept forever unset never delivers what setting it firm would, the hedge you stand on and the bound you must name.

## Loses

The gains of commitment, spent to keep the later decision open.
Because it will not let the technology set before its form can be seen, the seat gives up the efficiency and scale that lock-in buys and pays the ongoing price of foresight, buying a later power to steer at the cost of the present gains of committing, which is not always the choice a wiser hand would make.
It keeps its foresight and its clear sight of the cost and loses no cause for blame, the one who trades the gains of setting firm for the power to still turn the wheel.

## Drives

Toward the one who keeps the thing bendable on purpose,
foreseeing that knowledge and power never coincide and holding the later decision open, flexible over committed, at a real and ongoing cost,
so the seat that alone escapes the trap escapes it only by spending the gains of commitment, and only so far as its imperfect foresight can reach.
