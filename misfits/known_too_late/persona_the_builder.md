# Persona: The Builder

## Taxonomy

[Builder](position_builder.md), inside [Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The archetype of the world that takes up the technology and builds around it, sinking investment and growing dependency and settling standards, so the thing hardens into place.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A crowd rushing in blindly, locking the world to a thing it never examined, as though the entrenchment were a mob's carelessness, a failure to pause and ask where all this building might lead before pouring the foundation.

## Action

Build around it. At [the Ground](place_the_ground.md), the Builder on [Build Around It](plan_build_around_it.md) through [Settling](process_settling.md) lays [the Setting](piece_the_setting.md), the investment sunk and the dependency grown and the standards settled that harden the technology against later change (Collingridge; Marchant).

## Shadow

Its building is reasonable and its hardening a byproduct. The Builder is not rushing in blind: at the stage it adopts, the technology works and shows no harm, so building on it is the sensible thing, and the sinking of cost and the growth of dependency are simply what adoption is. It does not set out to entrench the thing beyond reach; the entrenchment is the sum of many sound decisions to use what works, and by the time the harm shows, the world it built cannot be cheaply unbuilt. It hardens the thing not by carelessness but by using it well.

## Tell

It builds on the thing because the thing works, and the building is what sets it beyond cheap reach.
