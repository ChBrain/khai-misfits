## Cue

## Action

At [the Bind](place_the_bind.md), [the Steward](persona_the_steward.md) from [the Steward's seat](position_steward.md) on [Keep It Open](plan_keep_it_open.md) through [Keeping](process_keeping.md) holds [the Corrigible Hand](piece_the_corrigible_hand.md), the building and adopting of the technology to keep the later decision open, flexible and reversible over efficient and committed.

## Stage

The remedy, and the bound. Collingridge's own answer is corrigibility: to build and adopt technologies so as to keep the later decision open, favouring the flexible and reversible over the efficient and committed, so that when the knowledge finally arrives the power to act on it has not wholly gone. This is not free. It forgoes the gains of commitment, standardization, and scale that lock-in buys, and it slows and complicates deployment; and the foresight that would bring the knowledge earlier, real-time and anticipatory assessment, is itself imperfect and costly and can never fully close the gap between what can be known and what can still be changed (Genus and Stirling; Guston and Sarewitz). No villain: the steward does not defeat the dilemma, only holds it at a distance, paying in advance for what commitment would otherwise have bought cheap. And the bound is held in view: the dilemma is a tendency, not an iron law, its horns softened wherever real foresight and things built to stay corrigible are at work, and not every technology locks in with equal severity.

## Tension

The way out real but dearly bought, and the dilemma a tendency and not a law.
