# Position: Maker

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The seat of the early hand that shapes the young technology while it is still plastic, and marks that the power to steer is highest exactly when the knowledge of where to steer is absent.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A young technology still soft, its course open to being shaped or redirected or stopped at little cost.
The power to steer it cheaply, the full plasticity of a thing not yet settled into the world.
No way to know what it will do, since its consequences do not yet exist to be measured, only emerging as it is widely used.

## Orders

Shape it while it is soft, and let it grow, since no harm is yet known to hold your hand.
Work the young technology while it is still plastic and cheap to steer, and release it, because at this stage there is no evidence of a harm that would warrant restraining it, and to hold back every new thing on the chance of an unknown harm is no rule at all.
Know that the power you hold now is highest exactly where your knowledge is least, that the consequences you would need to steer by cannot be known until the thing has grown and set, and that your blindness here is the order of things and not a fault of care.

## Loses

The one cheap chance to steer, spent in the necessary dark.
Because the technology is soft and steerable only at the stage when its harms cannot yet be known, the seat shapes and releases it blind, and the cheap early window of control is spent before there is anything to warrant spending it on, the steering that would have mattered impossible to aim.
It keeps its reasonable care and its good faith and loses no cause for blame, a maker who acts well on what can be known and cannot know what would warrant more.

## Drives

Toward the early hand that shapes the soft thing in the dark,
holding the full power to steer at the one stage when nothing can yet be known of where it should go, and letting it grow reasonably,
so the seat that alone could have steered cheaply spends that chance blind, its power highest exactly where its knowledge is least.
