# Piece: The Blind Window

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The first horn: power to steer held in a hand that cannot yet see what it is steering toward.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), met by [the Maker](persona_the_maker.md): the window of cheap control, open now, closing without anyone marking the hour.

## Load Bearing

This is the information problem: while the technology can still be controlled, not enough is known to warrant controlling it, because its consequences do not yet exist to be measured and only emerge through wide use (Collingridge). There is no data to weigh, no harm to point to, no case to make for restraint that would not also apply to any untried thing. So the young technology is let grow, and letting it grow is the reasonable choice, not a lapse. The window is blind not because anyone refuses to look through it, but because there is nothing yet on the other side to see.

## Apparent

It looks like negligence, a failure to look ahead, as if foresight had simply been skipped.

## Yearbook

Met by [the Maker](persona_the_maker.md), staged at [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), dramatized in [Nothing Yet to Show](plot_nothing_yet_to_show.md). It is the window of cheap control spent blind, and it closes as [The Setting](piece_the_setting.md) hardens.
