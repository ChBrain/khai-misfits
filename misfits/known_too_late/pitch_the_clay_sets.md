# Pitch: The Clay Sets

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a technology is soft to shape when its form cannot yet be seen, and set hard by the time it can.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Grave and measured, the long view told without blame. A world doing the reasonable thing at every step: letting a promising young technology grow because no harm is yet known, building on it because it works, and reaching to steer it only once the harm is plain. Nothing here is recklessness or timidity; each hand acts well on what it has.

## Undertow

Early a technology can still be controlled but its harms cannot yet be known, since the consequences do not exist to be measured; late the harms are plain but the technology is so entrenched that control has become costly and slow (Collingridge). The power to steer and the knowledge of where to steer are inversely available across its life, and they never meet in the middle.

## Nerve

That the same growth which reveals a technology's harm is the growth that sets it beyond cheap reach, so the knowledge always arrives just as the power departs. No one is a villain; it is the shape of the control problem, held against its limit, since the dilemma is a tendency and not a law, its horns softened by foresight and by things built to stay corrigible (Genus and Stirling).

## Echo

Soft when you cannot see the form, set hard by the time you can, so the harm is always known too late to steer.
