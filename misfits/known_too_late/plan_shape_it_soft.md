# Plan: Shape It Soft

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the maker's seat](position_maker.md), inside [Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Maker](position_maker.md)

## Direction

Work the young technology while it is still plastic and cheap to steer, and release it, because at this stage there is no evidence of a harm that would warrant restraining it, and to hold back every new thing on the chance of an unknown harm is no rule at all. Know that the power you hold now is highest exactly where your knowledge is least, that the consequences you would need to steer by cannot be known until the thing has grown and set, and that your blindness here is the order of things and not a fault of care.

## Orders

([The Maker](persona_the_maker.md) works [the Clay](piece_the_clay.md) through [Shaping](process_shaping.md) at [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), shaping the plastic technology while it is cheap to steer, and meets [the Blind Window](piece_the_blind_window.md), the power without the knowledge.)

## Implementation

([Shaping](process_shaping.md) at [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md) works [the Clay](piece_the_clay.md), because the young technology is soft and cheap to steer, until [the Blind Window](piece_the_blind_window.md) shows nothing yet to warrant steering it and the thing is let grow.)

## Targets

- [ ] the young technology is shaped while it is still plastic and cheap to steer, then released
- [ ] the release is understood as reasonable, no harm yet known to restrain it
- [ ] it is seen that the power to steer is highest exactly where the knowledge of where to steer is least
- [ ] the blindness is owned as the order of things, the consequences not yet in being to be read
