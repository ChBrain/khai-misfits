# Position: Builder

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The seat of the world that takes up the technology and builds around it, and marks that the hardening which sets it beyond reach is the sum of sound decisions to use what works.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A technology that works and shows no harm at the stage it is adopted, worth building on.
Investment to sink into it, systems to rest on it, standards and habits to settle around it.
No sight yet of the harm to come, only the plain usefulness of a thing that does its job.

## Orders

Build around it, since it works and shows no harm, and building on what works is the sensible thing.
Sink your investment into the technology, rest your systems on it, settle your standards and habits around it, because at the stage you adopt it there is no reason not to and every reason to.
Know that each sound decision to use it adds to the weight that will hold it in place, that the dependency you grow is what will make it costly to change when the harm shows, and that you harden the thing not by carelessness but by using it well.

## Loses

The later freedom to change the thing, spent unknowing in the building.
Because the seat builds on the technology while it works and shows no harm, sinking cost and growing dependency, the world it makes cannot be cheaply unmade when the harm at last appears, so the very soundness of its adoption is what sets the thing beyond reach.
It keeps its good sense and its working systems and loses no cause for blame, a builder that used what worked and could not have used the future it could not see.

## Drives

Toward the world that builds on the thing because the thing works,
sinking investment and growing dependency and settling standards around a technology that shows no harm, reasonably and well,
so the seat whose sound building is what hardens the thing sets it beyond cheap reach, the entrenchment the sum of good decisions and not a mob's carelessness.
