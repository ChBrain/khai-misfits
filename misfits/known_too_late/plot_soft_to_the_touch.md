# Plot: Soft to the Touch

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A new technology enters the world, still plastic, its course not yet fixed.

## Action

At [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), [the Maker](persona_the_maker.md) from [the Maker's seat](position_maker.md) on [Shape It Soft](plan_shape_it_soft.md) through [Shaping](process_shaping.md) works [the Clay](piece_the_clay.md), the young technology still open to being shaped or stopped at little cost.

## Stage

This is the moment when steering is cheapest: the thing soft in the hand, its form not yet settled, its course still open to redirection or to being stopped outright. Nothing here forecloses anything; every choice remains inexpensive because nothing has yet been built around it, sunk into it, or made to depend on it. This is the condition Collingridge names as the precondition of control: a technology can be controlled precisely because it has not yet hardened into the world (Collingridge). No villain stands here either, only a maker doing the ordinary, reasonable work of shaping something new while it is still shapeable.

## Tension

The thing soft now, and the one cheap chance to steer it at hand.
