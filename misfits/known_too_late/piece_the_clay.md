# Piece: The Clay

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The young technology, still soft, before its course is set.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), carried by [Shaping](process_shaping.md), voiced by [the Maker](persona_the_maker.md): the thing still takes the hand's pressure, its shape not yet fixed.

## Load Bearing

At the early stage the technology is soft and cheap to steer: its course is open, every option still on the table, and this is the one moment when redirection or restraint costs little (Collingridge). Nothing has hardened yet. No commitment has been made that cannot be unmade at small cost, no standard settled, no dependency grown. The clay takes whatever shape the hand gives it, and giving it a different shape tomorrow would cost almost nothing more than giving it this one today.

## Apparent

A harmless novelty, a thing of no consequence yet, not worth anyone's careful attention.
