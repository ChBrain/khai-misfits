# Plot: Now It Shows

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The harms at last emerge from use at scale.

## Action

At [the Ground](place_the_ground.md), [the Watch](persona_the_watch.md) meets [the Reckoning](piece_the_reckoning.md), the harmful consequences now apparent and measurable and no longer deniable.

## Stage

The knowledge that would have warranted steering arrives only now, once the technology has been widely developed and used long enough for its consequences to surface and be counted. What was invisible at the workshop is plain at the ground: the form of the harm, finally seen, finally measurable, finally undeniable. But the thing it describes is already set, already built around, already depended upon. The knowledge has come, but it has come to a world that has already hardened, so the very fact that made it knowable, the scale of use, is the same fact that made it costly to change (Collingridge). No villain: the watch does not fail to see; the watch sees exactly what could not have been seen any sooner.

## Tension

The knowledge arrived, and the thing already hardened around it.
