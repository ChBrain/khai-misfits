# Piece: The Setting

## Taxonomy

[Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The entrenchment: sunk investment, grown dependency, settled standards hardening the once soft thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Ground](place_the_ground.md), carried by [Settling](process_settling.md), voiced by [the Builder](persona_the_builder.md): what once took any shape now holds one shape only.

## Load Bearing

The world builds on the technology because it works, and the sunk cost, the grown dependency, the settled standards are the sum of sound decisions made one at a time, each reasonable on its own, together hardening the thing beyond cheap change: the clay turned to stone (Collingridge; Marchant, the pacing problem). No single builder chose to lock the world in place. Each one only built on what was already there, and what was already there kept being there for the next builder too, until the weight of everyone's ordinary work became a structure nobody built on purpose and nobody can cheaply move.

## Apparent

It looks like a mob locking the world to a thing it never examined, entrenchment mistaken for stubbornness.
