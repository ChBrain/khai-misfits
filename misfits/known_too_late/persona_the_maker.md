# Persona: The Maker

## Taxonomy

[Maker](position_maker.md), inside [Known Too Late](play_known_too_late.md). The archetype of the early hand that shapes the young technology while it is still plastic, holding the power to steer it at the very stage when nothing can yet be known of where it should be steered.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A reckless creator, loosing a thing on the world without a thought for what it will do. It looks like heedlessness, an inventor who should have known better, who should have foreseen the harm and held back, as though the future were there to be read and simply went unread.

## Action

Shape it soft. At [the Workshop](place_the_workshop.md), the Maker on [Shape It Soft](plan_shape_it_soft.md) through [Shaping](process_shaping.md) works [the Clay](piece_the_clay.md), the young technology still open to being shaped or stopped at little cost, then meets [the Blind Window](piece_the_blind_window.md), the power to steer without the knowledge of where, and lets the thing grow since no harm is yet knowable to restrain it (Collingridge).

## Shadow

Its heedlessness is a limit and not a fault. The Maker is not failing to read a future that could be read: the consequences of the technology do not yet exist to be measured, because they emerge only as it is widely used, so at the one stage when the thing is soft enough to steer cheaply there is nothing yet to warrant steering it. It acts reasonably on what can be known, and what would have warranted restraint cannot be known until the window has closed. It shapes in good faith, blind not by neglect but by the order of things.

## Tell

It shapes the thing while it is soft, at the one stage when no harm is yet knowable to hold its hand.
