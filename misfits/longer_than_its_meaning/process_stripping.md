# Process: Stripping

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The marker cleared of ornament, scale, and finish, so its own craft cannot be mistaken for value.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A marker whose care would read as worth having.
At [the site](place_the_site.md), [the warder](persona_the_warder.md) recognizing that any sign built to hold attention will hold the wrong kind of attention too: the stripping starts the moment the danger in being well made is seen for what it is.

## Direction

Toward the plainest form the marker can take and still stand.
Ornament goes, scale goes, polish goes, whatever a passerby might read as effort spent for a reason worth stealing, so the stripping drives the marker toward ordinary material and away from anything that would ask to be admired.

## Lever

The wish to stop one danger, meeting the fact that the same signal guards against two different ones.
Because removing the mark of value also removes the mark that would make a passerby care whether the thing survives, the stripping cannot choose only the danger it is aimed at: it buys safety from the taking with safety from the neglect.

## Echo

A marker no one wants, and for the same reason, no one minds.
The stripping succeeds exactly to the degree that the marker becomes uninteresting, so what is left standing is safer from a thief and no safer at all from the weather, the plough, or simple indifference.
