# Piece: The Marker

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The carrier chosen for one property alone: that it will still be standing when the maker is not.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [the site](place_the_site.md) by [the maker](persona_the_maker.md), and left there.
Later found at the same site by [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md), unmoved in every sense that matters: it has not travelled, only the world around it has.

## Load Bearing

Without it, [the warning](piece_the_warning.md) dies with whoever first knew it, a fact like any other, gone the ordinary way facts go. With it, the knowledge survives past every person who could explain it, which is exactly the point and exactly the cost: a carrier is chosen for endurance because endurance is the one requirement a living witness cannot meet, and every property that buys it that endurance, being fixed, being hard to alter, being built to be noticed and to last, is also the property that will later read as worth having or worth ignoring on its own terms, with no maker present to correct the reading either way.

## Apparent

A worked surface at [the site](place_the_site.md): cut, shaped, or raised by a hand rather than by weather, plainly the product of effort and plainly still standing.
What it looks like changes with what [stripping](process_stripping.md) has or has not done to it: fine and deliberate if the craft was kept, plain and unremarkable if the craft was cut away on purpose.

## Yearbook

Chosen by [the maker](persona_the_maker.md) for endurance, then carries [the warning](piece_the_warning.md) through [compressing](process_compressing.md), holding only what the surface would allow.
Possibly reworked by [the warder](persona_the_warder.md) through [stripping](process_stripping.md), losing ornament to lose the reading that would draw a hand toward it.
Found, eventually, by [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md), who reads it through [inverting](process_inverting.md) and takes from it a meaning [the maker](persona_the_maker.md) never gets to hear.
