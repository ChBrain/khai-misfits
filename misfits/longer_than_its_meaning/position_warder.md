# Position: Warder

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The seat that strips the marker of the qualities that would draw a hand toward it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A clear view of the danger a well-made warning carries: that its own craft will be read as worth having.
The authority to plan the marker's material, scale, and finish before it is ever cut, and no authority over how it will be read once it stands.

## Orders

Make it worthless.
Strip the marker of ornament, scale, and polish, anything a stranger might read as value, and leave only what the warning needs.
Hold the plainness deliberate, not accidental, so it reads as a choice a stranger might one day recognize as one.

## Loses

The one quality that would have made a passerby stop and choose to protect it.
Because plainness that repels a thief also fails to summon a keeper, the seat cannot buy safety from looting without spending safety from neglect, and it has no way to choose only one loss without the other.
It ends having removed one danger by opening the door to its opposite.

## Drives

Toward a marker no one has reason to want and therefore no reason to take,
right to fear the invitation a fine warning sends,
so the very plainness that closes that door is what leaves the marker with nobody left to mind it.
