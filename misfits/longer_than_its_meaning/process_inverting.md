# Process: Inverting

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The marker's own qualities read backward once the frame that gave them their intended sense is gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A marker read outside the frame it was cut in.
At [the site](place_the_site.md), [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md) meeting the marker with no memory of the occasion it answers: the reading begins the moment a mind supplies its own frame in place of the one that is missing.

## Direction

Toward a meaning exactly opposite the one intended.
Care reads as skill, skill reads as expense, expense reads as value, and a shape meant to repel a hand instead calls it closer, so the inverting drives the reading further from the source the harder the marker tries to signal its own seriousness.

## Lever

A signal built to be noticed, meeting a mind with nothing to notice it correctly with.
Because the marker cannot help but show the effort spent on it, and effort is the one thing every frame reads as worth something, the inverting needs no error on the stranger's part: the same signal that would have meant danger inside the right frame means value inside any other.

## Echo

A warning taken up as a find, or a plain stone walked past as nothing.
Either way, the marker's intended meaning does not survive contact with a mind it was not built for; what the stranger carries away, taken or ignored, is a reading the inverting produced, not the one the maker cut.
