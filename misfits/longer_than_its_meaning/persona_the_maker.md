# Persona: The Maker

## Taxonomy

[Maker](position_maker.md). Holds knowledge worth more than one lifetime and cuts it into the one carrier built to outlast one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Capable. Unhurried. Certain of the choice, if not the outcome.
Knows the knowledge is worth more than the years left to carry it, and picks the one surface that will still be standing after those years run out. No fear in the work, only the care of someone doing it once and doing it right.

## Action

Chooses the carrier built to outlast a body, and cuts the knowledge into it, line by line, present tense, watching each cut narrow what will be left to read.
Leaves the marker finished and walks away from it, trusting the stone to hold what a voice could not.

## Shadow

Cannot see the frame it leans on.
The cuts make sense only inside an understanding shared by everyone alive to see them made, and that understanding rides along unspoken because writing it down is exactly what the stone has no room for. The Maker never doubts the marks will still say what they say. It never occurs to it that saying and being understood come from different places.

## Tell

Explains everything except the one thing a stranger would need first: not what the marks mean, but what kind of mind is meant to read them.
Says "anyone will see it," meaning anyone who already sees the world the way I do.
