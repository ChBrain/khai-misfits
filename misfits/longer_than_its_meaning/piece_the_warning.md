# Piece: The Warning

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The knowledge itself: real, load-bearing, and known to be worth more than the one lifetime holding it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held first by [the maker](persona_the_maker.md), before it is anywhere else.
Cut into [the marker](piece_the_marker.md) at [the site](place_the_site.md), where it stays whether or not anyone left to carry it in mind is still alive.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is nothing worth the trouble of a carrier that outlasts a body: an ordinary fact would ride along in memory and die out with no great loss. With it, the whole mechanism is forced: because the knowledge genuinely outvalues one lifetime, it must be set into something that outlasts a lifetime, and every cost the rest of the structure pays, the compression, the lost frame, the inversion, follows from that one requirement and would not exist without it. The Warning is load bearing because it is the reason durability is worth buying at any price, including the price of legibility.

## Apparent

Whatever the site shows before it is read: lines cut into a surface, a shape set where it will be seen, nothing more than marks on stone until a mind supplies what they are for.
Its worth is not visible in the marks themselves; it has to be taken on the evidence of how much care went into leaving them.

## Yearbook

Known first only to [the maker](persona_the_maker.md), then set down through [compressing](process_compressing.md) into [the marker](piece_the_marker.md), where it has stood, unchanged, since the last cut was made.
Read once, eventually, by [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md), through [inverting](process_inverting.md), in whatever form survived [the compression](piece_the_compression.md) and the loss of [the frame](piece_the_frame.md) that would have unpacked it correctly.
