# Process: Compressing

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The knowledge cut down to what the durable carrier will hold, line by line, until only the hard edges remain.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A carrier chosen for endurance, and a surface that will not hold everything.
At [the site](place_the_site.md), [the maker](persona_the_maker.md) facing a stone that will outlast a lifetime and a message that, whole, would not fit it: the choice to cut is already made the moment the choice of carrier is.

## Direction

Toward the shortest form the warning can take and still be a warning.
Each pass removes what is not essential in the moment of cutting, the caveats, the softenings, the parts that only made sense to say out loud, so the compressing runs the message down and down until what is left is hard, certain, and standing in for everything it no longer says.

## Lever

A surface that will not be revised, meeting a message that must still work when it is not.
Because nothing more can be added once the stone is cut, the compressing is driven by the one chance it gets, and it presses hardest exactly where the medium is least forgiving, sanding away anything that depended on being said twice.

## Echo

A marker that says less than the maker knew, and says it forever.
What is cut remains exact and permanent; what is cut away leaves no mark of its absence, so a reader meeting the result has no way to tell a complete short message from a long one with its middle missing.
