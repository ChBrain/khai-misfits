# Plan: Read What's Here

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the mandate of [the stranger's seat](position_stranger.md), inside [Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Stranger](position_stranger.md)

## Direction

Find the marked place and make sense of it in the only frame available. Weigh what it shows against everything already known, reach the reading that fits, and act on it, because a reading withheld for a better frame that may never come is no reading at all.

## Orders

([The Stranger](persona_the_stranger.md) finds the marker where it stands, reads it against its own frame rather than the one it was cut in, and acts on whatever sense that reading makes.)

## Implementation

([The Site](place_the_site.md); the [inverting](process_inverting.md) process; [the marker](piece_the_marker.md) is read through [the inversion](piece_the_inversion.md), since [the frame](piece_the_frame.md) it was cut in did not survive to meet it.)

## Targets

- [ ] the marked place is found
- [ ] the marker is read against the only frame on hand
- [ ] a reading is reached and acted on
- [ ] the reading closes as certain, whether or not it is faithful
