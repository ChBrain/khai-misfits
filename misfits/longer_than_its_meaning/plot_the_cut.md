# Plot: The Cut

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Site](place_the_site.md), open ground, and a maker who knows the knowledge in hand will outlast neither of them if it is left where it is.
No stone yet marked, no message yet shortened: only the knowledge, and the choice of what to cut it into.

## Action

[Compressing](process_compressing.md): [The Maker](persona_the_maker.md), holding [the maker's seat](position_maker.md), cuts the knowledge into the carrier line by line, keeping only what the surface will hold.

## Stage

[The Maker](persona_the_maker.md), having tabled [Make It Last](plan_make_it_last.md), deploys [the warning](piece_the_warning.md) into [the marker](piece_the_marker.md), choosing the durable surface over any living one and trusting it to carry what a voice cannot.
The cutting runs through [the compression](piece_the_compression.md), narrowing the message to its hardest edges, while [the frame](piece_the_frame.md) that would explain those edges is never carved in, because it does not occur to the maker that it needs to be.

## Tension

The choice is exactly right, and that is what dooms the message.
There is no better carrier to have picked and no gentler way to have cut it: the knowledge really does outlast one lifetime only by being reduced to what the stone will hold, so the maker's care and the maker's own erasure are the same act seen from two sides. The marker will stand. What it will still say, once the frame that made it sayable is gone, is a question the cutting never had a way to answer.
