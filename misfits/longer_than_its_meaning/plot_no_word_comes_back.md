# Plot: No Word Comes Back

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Site](place_the_site.md), the whole exchange already run, the cutting done, the stripping done, the reading done, and one question left standing over all three: did any of it work.
No one left at the site who could ask, and no one who set the marker left anywhere to answer.

## Action

[Compressing](process_compressing.md) and [inverting](process_inverting.md), both already spent: [the maker](persona_the_maker.md), holding [the maker's seat](position_maker.md), and [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md), holding [the stranger's seat](position_stranger.md), stand at opposite ends of an exchange that ran to completion without the two of them ever occupying the same moment.

## Stage

[The Maker](persona_the_maker.md), still on [Make It Last](plan_make_it_last.md), and [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md), having closed [Read What's Here](plan_read_whats_here.md), are laid side by side with [the trade](piece_the_trade.md) between them: the whole ledger, what was spent on durability, what it cost in legibility, stated whole for the first time.
[The Silence](piece_the_silence.md) sits over both ends of it, unbroken by anything either persona has done.

## Tension

The account is complete and it will never balance.
Every step was sound: the carrier was the right choice, the compression was forced, the stripping answered a real danger, the reading was the only reading a stranger could reach. None of that soundness builds a path back, because the one report that would say whether the message survived intact requires a reader who could reach the maker, and the marker was built, from the first cut, for a reader who never could.
