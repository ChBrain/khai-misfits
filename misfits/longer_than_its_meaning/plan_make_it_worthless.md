# Plan: Make It Worthless

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the mandate of [the warder's seat](position_warder.md), inside [Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Warder](position_warder.md)

## Direction

Strip the marker of anything a stranger might read as value: the fine cutting, the scale, the polish. Leave only what the warning needs, deliberately plain, so that no hand is drawn toward it by the very craft meant to warn it off.

## Orders

([The Warder](persona_the_warder.md) strips the marker of ornament and finish before it is ever cut, holding the plainness as a considered choice rather than an absence of effort.)

## Implementation

([The Site](place_the_site.md); the [stripping](process_stripping.md) process; stripped of what would trigger [the inversion](piece_the_inversion.md), [the marker](piece_the_marker.md) is left plain, at the cost of the one quality that would have made it worth protecting.)

## Targets

- [ ] the marker's ornament, scale, and finish are stripped before it stands
- [ ] the plainness is held as deliberate, not accidental
- [ ] the marker no longer reads as something worth taking
- [ ] whether it still reads as worth keeping is left open
