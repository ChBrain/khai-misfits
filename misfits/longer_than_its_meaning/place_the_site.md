# Place: The Site

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The one marked place, held across the whole gap between the cutting and the finding.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A worked surface standing in open ground: cut lines, or a built shape, plainly not the work of weather alone.
Something someone meant to leave, present to anyone who comes near it, unguarded and unexplained, exactly as it was left the day the last hand touched it.

## Holds

[The marker](piece_the_marker.md), and through it, [the warning](piece_the_warning.md) it was cut to carry.
Nothing else: no attendant, no sign pointing elsewhere, no second copy kept safe in case this one fails. Whatever the site can offer a later visitor, it offers through this one worked surface alone.

## Offers

Itself, to anyone who happens by: to look, to walk its edges, to notice it and wonder.
No entry is barred and no toll is asked; the site puts the marker in front of whoever arrives and leaves the rest to them, exactly as free to the maker's intended reader as to the stranger who was never meant to find it at all.

## Withheld

The occasion it was cut for.
The site can show the marks and cannot show the moment that made them make sense: who stood here, what they already knew, what emergency or season or warning made the cutting worth the effort. That context is not hidden the way a locked door hides a room; it is simply gone, and the site has no way to hand over what it no longer holds.
