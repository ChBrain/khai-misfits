# Longer Than Its Meaning: Reference

The research warrant for Longer Than Its Meaning.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the problem of the durable message, from the rock-art record of
mobile foragers to the nuclear semiotics of the long repository warning, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the message to an unmeetable reader as a **system with no return channel**: knowledge worth passing
is set into a carrier chosen to outlast its maker, and every property that buys the carrier its life costs
the message its legibility. The play models the documented mechanics, the knowledge that is real and
load-bearing and known to be worth more than one lifetime, the carrier chosen for endurance because
endurance is exactly the requirement, the compression that endurance forces since what must be cut into
stone cannot be long, the shared frame that compression silently assumes and that is the one thing certain
not to survive, the inversion by which the marker's own qualities turn against it so that mass reads as
importance and importance reads as treasure and a warning becomes an invitation, and the absence of any
reply, since the only reader who could confirm receipt is the reader the maker will never meet, as the
load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of naivety.

The governing law is that durability and legibility are bought from different accounts and each is paid for
with the other. A carrier survives by being inert, massive and cheap to no one; a meaning survives only
inside a frame of reference that is none of those things. So the maker must optimise against a model of a
stranger's mind that cannot be tested, and must do it in a medium whose whole virtue is that it cannot be
revised once the stranger arrives.

The Sandia panel convened for the Waste Isolation Pilot Plant met the same wall the carvers did and wrote
it down: most monuments say remember this, value this, come closer, and this one had to say the opposite.
Their conclusion that the markers should be of ordinary materials and absent of beauty, lest a finder see
value in removing them, is the inversion stated as a design rule. The thing must be made worthless on
purpose or it will be carried off, and a message carried off is a message unsent.

It is distinct from the house's **The Common Object**, where one artefact carries different meanings across
a boundary and the coordination is believed rather than real: there the parties are contemporaries who
could in principle talk, and the misfit is that they do not. Here there is no conversation available at any
price, because the reader is not yet born.

It is distinct from the house's **Second Nature**, where a knower cannot perceive the gap between what they
know and what a learner does: there the learner is in the room and could ask, and the account fails a
person who is present. Here the learner cannot ask, and the maker cannot answer, and the failure cannot
even be observed.

It is distinct from the house's **The Decision Travels**, where a decision moves down a chain and the
format cannot carry what mattered: there the chain sits inside one organisation and one lifetime, and
someone could walk back up it.

It is distinct from the house's **The Horizon Problem**, where a plan is complete to its edge and silent
past it: there the beyond is excluded and unnamed. Here the beyond is addressed directly, deliberately and
at great cost, and the message fails anyway.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the maker as
  naive or the later reader as careless, because the source finding is precisely that both act well: cutting
  into rock is the correct choice for a message that must outlive you, compressing it is forced by the
  medium and not by haste, and a stranger who finds a marked place and reads it in the frame they have is
  doing the only thing a reader can do. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, a
  channel with no return path and two requirements that are bought from different accounts, so the misfit is
  that the care taken to make the message last is the same care that makes it unreadable.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Every correction pays.
  Redundancy across media and scales, which the WIPP panel recommended, multiplies the chances and also the
  surfaces that can be misread. Making the marker worthless so that it will not be plundered means making it
  ugly, which is to spend the one quality that would have made later people choose to keep it. And a living
  transmission, a priesthood or an institution charged with retelling, trades a medium that cannot be
  revised for a custodian that can, which is to say it can be forgotten, or edited, or turned. There is no
  arrangement that is both unrevisable and still understood.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the site, the information models, the task force, the
  semiotic argument and the marker report are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: what any particular
  petroglyph meant is interpretation and not decipherment, and the play does not assert a reading of any
  specific carving. The repository markers have not been built and cannot be tested, so their efficacy is a
  matter of expert judgement by the panel's own description. The play stages the structural problem both
  cases share, not a claim to know what either message says.
