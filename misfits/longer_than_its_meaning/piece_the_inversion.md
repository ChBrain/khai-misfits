# Piece: The Inversion

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The marker's own qualities turning against it: mass reads as importance, importance reads as treasure, a warning reads as an invitation.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Happens nowhere but inside [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md), reading [the marker](piece_the_marker.md) at [the site](place_the_site.md) with [the frame](piece_the_frame.md) gone missing.
Leaves its mark not on the stone but on what the stranger does next: taking the marker up, or setting it down as nothing.

## Load Bearing

Without it, a missing frame would simply leave a stranger uncertain, guessing at a blank rather than committing to a wrong answer. With it, the guess is not neutral: every signal the maker built in to be noticed, care, scale, evident effort, is read by any mind as evidence of worth, so the very qualities chosen to make the warning taken seriously are the qualities that make it taken at all. The Inversion is load bearing because it is what turns a lost frame from an inconvenience into a trap: the marker does not fail by saying nothing, it fails by saying something, just the wrong thing, and saying it clearly.

## Apparent

Nothing different in the marker itself: the same cut lines, the same worn stone, unchanged from the day the maker left it.
What is apparent is only in the stranger, a hand reaching where a warning meant to stop it, or a glance passing over where a warning meant to be seen, either way the marker's surface giving no outward sign that anything has gone wrong.

## Yearbook

Waits dormant in [the marker](piece_the_marker.md) from the moment [the compression](piece_the_compression.md) leaves [the frame](piece_the_frame.md) uncarved, and activates the instant [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md) supplies a frame of its own.
Where [stripping](process_stripping.md) has run first, it turns the other way: plainness reads as nothing rather than mass reading as treasure, the same mechanism producing a different failure from the opposite design.
