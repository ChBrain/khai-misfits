# Position: Stranger

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The seat that finds the marker with no memory of what it was cut for, and must read it anyway.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A marked place in front of it, and a frame of its own to read that place with, built from everything except the one occasion the marker was cut for.
No way to know what it is missing, since a gap in understanding does not announce itself from inside.

## Orders

Read what's here.
Take the marker as it stands and make sense of it in the only frame available, because a reading held back for a better frame is not a reading at all.
Act on the sense made, whatever it turns out to be.

## Loses

Any certainty that the reading is the one intended.
Because the frame it reads with is not the frame the marks were cut in, the seat cannot tell a faithful reading from an inverted one from where it stands, so it loses the very check that would tell it which reading it has made.
It ends having read the marker completely, and possibly backward.

## Drives

Toward a reading that closes the marker's meaning for good,
right to read a real thing in front of it the only way a reader can,
so the same completeness that lets it act at all is what keeps it from ever noticing the reading might be wrong.
