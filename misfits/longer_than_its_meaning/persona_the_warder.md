# Persona: The Warder

## Taxonomy

[Warder](position_warder.md). Sees the danger in the marker's own craft and strips it, to keep the wrong hand from being drawn in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Deliberate. Suspicious of beauty. Working against the instinct that a warning should be built to impress.
Reasons that a mark worth admiring is a mark worth taking, so it plans the opposite of every monument it has ever seen: ordinary stone, no polish, nothing a passerby would want to own.

## Action

Strips the marker of the qualities that would read as value: the fine cutting, the scale, the show of effort, leaving only what the warning needs and nothing that could be mistaken for treasure.
Sets the plainness in place and stands by the reasoning behind it, aware that plainness is a choice too, not an absence of one.

## Shadow

Cannot see that the same plainness meant to stop a hand also stops an eye.
It sets out to remove one failure, the marker read as an invitation, and does not notice it is buying that removal with the marker's other chance of surviving: being cared for, remembered, and kept standing by people who thought it worth the keeping.

## Tell

Argues for the plainness at length, and grows quiet on what happens to a plain thing nobody has any reason to protect.
Says "better unnoticed than unearthed," and does not finish the sentence where unnoticed also means unread.
