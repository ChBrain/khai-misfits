# Persona: The Stranger

## Taxonomy

[Stranger](position_stranger.md). Finds the marker with no memory of what it was cut for, and reads it in the only frame on hand.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Curious. Careful, by its own lights. Reading a real thing in front of it, doing the only thing a reader can do with what is actually there.
Sees a marked place, weighs what it says against everything it already knows, and reaches a reading that fits, because a reading that does not fit is no reading at all.

## Action

Finds the marker where it stands and reads it against the only frame it owns, the one it was raised in, not the one it was cut in.
Acts on the reading: takes the marker up, or takes it apart, or takes it as nothing worth the detour, whichever the reading points toward.

## Shadow

Cannot see the frame it is missing, only the one it has.
There is no way, standing where it stands, to tell an intact message read wrong from a message that was never whole to begin with, so every reading feels complete from the inside, and the Stranger has no way to notice the parts that are silently doing all the work of filling a gap it does not know is there.

## Tell

Explains its reading with total confidence and no reference to what it does not know it is missing.
Says "this clearly means," about the one thing it is least equipped to be sure of.
