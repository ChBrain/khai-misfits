# Piece: The Compression

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The narrowing endurance forces: what must be cut into a surface that cannot be revised cannot be long.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Happens at [the site](place_the_site.md), in the moment [the maker](persona_the_maker.md) cuts [the warning](piece_the_warning.md) into [the marker](piece_the_marker.md).
Leaves no trace of itself once finished: only its result, the shortened message, remains to be found.

## Load Bearing

Without it, a durable carrier could hold a message exactly as full as any spoken one, and nothing would be lost in the choice to make it last. With it, the choice to endure and the choice to explain fully are shown to draw on the same limited surface, so more of one is always less of the other. The Compression is load bearing because it is the mechanism that turns a sound decision, choose the carrier that lasts, into an unavoidable cost, lose the parts that needed room: the maker does not choose to compress badly, the surface simply will not hold what a voice could have said.

## Apparent

The gap between what a full account would need and what the cut lines actually show: fewer words than the occasion had, fewer marks than the knowledge contained, a message visibly shorter than its subject.
Nothing about the marker announces what was left out; the shortness itself is all that is visible.

## Yearbook

Forced the moment [the maker](persona_the_maker.md) chose a durable carrier over any living one, and completed the moment the cutting stopped.
Its result stands unchanged at [the site](place_the_site.md) from that day to the one [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md) finds it, carrying [the frame](piece_the_frame.md) it silently assumed and never carved in.
