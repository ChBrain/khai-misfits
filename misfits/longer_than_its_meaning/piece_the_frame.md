# Piece: The Frame

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The shared understanding [the compression](piece_the_compression.md) silently assumes, and the one thing certain not to survive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Present at [the site](place_the_site.md) only while [the maker](persona_the_maker.md) and everyone who shares its understanding are alive to hold it.
Absent from the same site by the time [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md) arrives: not removed, simply never renewed.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the short cut left by [the compression](piece_the_compression.md) would mean nothing to anyone, since a fragment needs a context to expand back into a whole. With it, present at the moment of cutting, the fragment reads perfectly, because the maker never had to carve in what everyone around already knew. The Frame is load bearing because it is the missing half of every compressed message: it is what lets a short cut stand in for a long meaning, and its absence is what turns the same short cut into a puzzle with no key supplied.

## Apparent

Nothing at all: the Frame is never carved into the marker, so there is no mark that shows it was ever there or ever needed.
Its only visible trace is negative, the places in [the warning](piece_the_warning.md) that read as complete to one mind and as gapped to another.

## Yearbook

Shared by everyone alive at [the site](place_the_site.md) when [the maker](persona_the_maker.md) does the cutting, and gone the ordinary way any unwritten understanding goes: not destroyed, just never passed on past the people who already had it.
Missing entirely by the time [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md) meets [the marker](piece_the_marker.md), which is exactly what opens the door to [the inversion](piece_the_inversion.md).
