# Piece: The Trade

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The phenomenon whole: durability and legibility bought from different accounts, each paid for with the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Struck at [the site](place_the_site.md) the moment [the maker](persona_the_maker.md) chooses a carrier for endurance, and never renegotiated afterward.
Read out, finally, wherever [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md) stands when the reading lands, treasure taken, danger ignored, or nothing noticed at all.

## Load Bearing

Without it the other pieces are parts without the ledger that binds them. With it the whole exchange stands in one accounting: [the warning](piece_the_warning.md) worth more than a lifetime, set into [the marker](piece_the_marker.md) chosen for its endurance, narrowed by [the compression](piece_the_compression.md) that endurance forces, emptied of [the frame](piece_the_frame.md) that would have carried its full sense, read back through [the inversion](piece_the_inversion.md) its own qualities set up, with [the silence](piece_the_silence.md) closing every path that could have caught the error. The Trade is load bearing because it is the structure entire: not a failure of care on either end, but its sum, since spending on durability is spending against legibility by the nature of the accounts, not by anyone's mistake.

## Apparent

A marker still standing, exactly as durable as it was built to be, saying exactly what it was cut to say.
The trade itself is never visible in the stone: only its two outcomes are, a message that survived and a message that was not received, sitting in the same object at once.

## Yearbook

The trade entire, staged across the run.
The Trade is [the warning](piece_the_warning.md) that could not travel any medium but a hard one, narrowed by [the compression](piece_the_compression.md) that hard medium demands, stripped of [the frame](piece_the_frame.md) no cut could carry, read backward through [the inversion](piece_the_inversion.md) its own care invited, and closed, on the maker's side, by [the silence](piece_the_silence.md) no report will ever cross.
