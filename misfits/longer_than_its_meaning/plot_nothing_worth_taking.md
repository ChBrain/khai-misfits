# Plot: Nothing Worth Taking

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Site](place_the_site.md), the marker finished but for one question left open: whether its own craft will get it carried off before anyone reads it right.
The warder weighing a marker built to be noticed against everyone who will notice it for the wrong reason.

## Action

[Stripping](process_stripping.md): [The Warder](persona_the_warder.md), holding [the warder's seat](position_warder.md), clears the marker of ornament, scale, and finish, while [the maker](persona_the_maker.md), still on [Make It Last](plan_make_it_last.md), holds the durability the stripping is not allowed to touch.

## Stage

[The Warder](persona_the_warder.md), having tabled [Make It Worthless](plan_make_it_worthless.md), works [the marker](piece_the_marker.md) plain, removing anything a passerby might read as value and leaving only what the warning strictly needs.
What is stripped away is weighed against [the inversion](piece_the_inversion.md) it is meant to prevent, the fine cutting that would read as treasure, but the plainness left behind carries no mark of the reasoning behind it, only the reasoning's result.

## Tension

The fix for one failure is the cause of another.
Stripping the marker's craft does keep a hand from reaching for it as treasure, exactly as intended, and it also strips the one quality that would have made anyone care whether the thing survived at all: cared for, remembered, kept standing. There is no way to hold both, being safe from the taking and safe from the neglect, with the same stone, so the warder buys one danger down by opening the other.
