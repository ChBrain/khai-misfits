# Piece: The Silence

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The absence of any reply: the only reader who could confirm receipt is the reader the maker will never meet.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Sits between [the site](place_the_site.md) and wherever [the maker](persona_the_maker.md) has long since gone, a gap with no path across it in either direction.
Present from the moment the marker is finished, whether or not anyone ever finds it, and unchanged by whatever [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md) eventually does there.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the whole structure would resolve: a maker who could hear back would learn whether the compression held, whether the frame carried, whether the reading landed right or inverted, and correct the next attempt. With it, none of that reaches back, because the reader who could report is, by the terms of the carrier itself, someone the maker is already gone before meeting. The Silence is load bearing because it is what makes every other cost in the structure permanent rather than merely risky: a mistake with no feedback cannot be found, so it is never fixed, only ever repeated by whoever cuts the next one.

## Apparent

Nothing to see: no unanswered letter, no unopened door, nothing marking the gap where a report should have arrived.
Its only visible form is negative, an absence that shows up as nothing happening rather than as something failing to happen.

## Yearbook

Opens the moment [the maker](persona_the_maker.md) finishes the cut and walks away, and holds, unbroken, through every year [the marker](piece_the_marker.md) stands untouched.
Still holds after [the stranger](persona_the_stranger.md) reads it, whatever the reading turns out to be, because a report requires a path back and none was ever built.
