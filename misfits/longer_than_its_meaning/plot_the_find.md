# Plot: The Find

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Site](place_the_site.md), long after anyone who could explain it is gone, come upon by a stranger carrying a frame the marker was never cut inside.
The marker exactly as it was left, whatever [stripping](process_stripping.md) did or did not do to it, waiting on a reading it cannot supply for itself.

## Action

[Inverting](process_inverting.md): [The Stranger](persona_the_stranger.md), holding [the stranger's seat](position_stranger.md), reads the marker's own qualities against its own frame, while nothing in [the marker](piece_the_marker.md) itself can correct the direction the reading takes.

## Stage

[The Stranger](persona_the_stranger.md), having tabled [Read What's Here](plan_read_whats_here.md), meets [the marker](piece_the_marker.md) at [the site](place_the_site.md) and reads it the only way a reader can, in the frame on hand rather than the one it was cut in.
Where the craft was kept, [the inversion](piece_the_inversion.md) runs one way, care read as value, a warning read as an invitation; where [stripping](process_stripping.md) ran first, it runs the other, plainness read as nothing worth a second look.

## Tension

The stranger is doing the only thing a reader can do, and it is exactly wrong.
There is no carelessness here to name: the reading is careful, consistent, and built from everything the stranger actually has to work with, since [the frame](piece_the_frame.md) that would have supplied the rest never reached this far. Whichever way the marker was made, kept fine or stripped plain, the reading lands on a meaning the maker did not intend, and nothing in the moment tells the stranger, or ever will, that it has.
