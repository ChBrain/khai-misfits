# Pitch: No One Left to Ask

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Still, exact, unhurried.
The quiet of someone doing careful work for a person they will never meet, cutting each line with the patience of knowing it has to be right the first time because there is no second pass.

## Undertow

Under the stillness, a question with nobody left to answer it.
Every choice the maker makes, what to keep, what to cut, how plain or how fine, is a bet placed against a mind that cannot be consulted and will not be met, so the calm of the cutting sits directly over the one thing calm cannot fix: there is no way to check the bet before it is the only copy left standing.

## Nerve

The specific hush of watching someone finish a message they know they will not see delivered,
sure the stone will still be standing when it matters,
and just as sure that standing and being understood were never the same promise.

## Echo

After the read: a marker still upright, weathered but whole, saying exactly what it always said,
and a reader walking away from it changed, but not by what it meant to say,
the two of them never quite in the same room.
