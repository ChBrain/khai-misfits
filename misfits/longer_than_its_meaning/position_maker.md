# Position: Maker

## Taxonomy

[Longer Than Its Meaning](play_longer_than_its_meaning.md). The seat that cuts knowledge into a carrier built to outlast the one holding the seat.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Knowledge worth more than the years left to carry it, and a carrier chosen for one property alone: that it will still be standing when the years run out.
No way to test the choice against the reader it is made for, since that reader does not exist yet.

## Orders

Make it last.
Cut the knowledge into the carrier, keeping only what the surface will hold, and set it where it will be found rather than lost.
Trust the stone to do, alone, what no voice will be present to do.

## Loses

Any chance to revise.
Once the cut is made, the seat cannot soften a line, add a caveat, or answer a question it did not think to anticipate, because the choice that gives the marker its life also removes the maker from every future draft of it.
It ends having said everything it could fit, and nothing more, ever again.

## Drives

Toward a message that survives its own maker,
right to choose durability over any promise of being understood,
so the very cut that lets it outlast a lifetime is the cut that empties it of what only a lifetime could have explained.
