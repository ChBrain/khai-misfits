# Piece: The No-Go Sector

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). The arc, roughly forty-five degrees either side of the wind, into which no vessel can be steered.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Opposite the wind, wherever the wind is.
Carried by [the sector](persona_the_sector.md), and moving when the weather does.

## Load Bearing

Without it a vessel simply sails away from a lee shore and there is no play.
It is load bearing because it is **the same fact as the lift**: a sail makes force from flow across it, so aiming one at the wind produces nothing, and the arc is not a defect that better design removes.

## Apparent

A limitation, and therefore something engineering ought eventually to fix.
It has not and will not, because a rig that could point at the wind would be a rig generating no force. **The capability and the exclusion are one object seen from two sides.**

## Yearbook

A property of how lift is made, spent at [the windward leg](place_the_windward_leg.md).
Ninety degrees out of three hundred and sixty, unremarkable in open water and exactly the same size on a coast.
