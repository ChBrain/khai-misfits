## Stage

## Tension

This plot's work is to establish that the rig is **right**, because a play that opens by faulting it has nothing to say later.
A sail is a wing. It makes force from wind flowing across it, and that is what carries a vessel toward the weather, which nothing else aboard can do. The destination is reached by never once pointing at it, at a cost of half again the distance, and that price is paid gladly and daily.
Then the sector, stated as a price and not a flaw. **A rig that could be aimed at the wind would be a rig generating nothing**, so the arc is not a defect better building removes: it is the same fact as the lift, seen from the other side. In open water it is ninety degrees nobody has any use for.
