## Tension

This is where the harm lands, and it lands with nobody steering at anything.
The keel holds the vessel up against the wind's sideways push by generating side force, and that force varies with the **square of her speed through the water**. So the seaway that takes her from four knots to two does not halve the resistance to going sideways: it quarters it, and the leeway rises to make up the difference. **She is set toward the shore by her own loss of way.**
And it is invisible from on board. The heading has not changed and the helm feels the same, because what has changed is the gap between where she is pointed and where she is going. The compass reports the first and nothing aboard reports the second. **The only instrument that reports it is the land**, which is why the news arrives as breakers on a bearing that has not moved.
