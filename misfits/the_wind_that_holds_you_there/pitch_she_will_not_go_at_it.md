# Pitch: She Will Not Go At It

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Plain, unhurried, stated.
A master saying what is so: the wind is in the west, the land is in the east, she will not lie closer than this.
Nothing raised and nothing dramatised, because the facts do not need any help.

## Undertow

Under the deck, two facts that do not negotiate.
A sail is a wing, so it makes nothing aimed at the wind, and there is an arc she cannot be steered into.
The keel's grip on the water goes as the square of her way, so as the seas slow her she is pushed sideways faster.

## Nerve

The unease of watching everybody do the right thing
and watching the land come nearer anyway,
with the compass reading true, the helm where it should be, and nobody steering at the shore.

## Echo

After the run: an accurate chart, a sound rig, a master who chose well,
two readings that were both correct,
and an offing that was kept or not kept on a Tuesday, in a light breeze, for no reason anybody could have given.
