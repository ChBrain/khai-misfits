# Piece: The Square of the Speed

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). The keel's side force varying with the square of the way, so leeway rises as speed falls.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Under the water, where nobody is looking.
Enacted by [the lost way](persona_the_lost_way.md), whenever the seas take her speed.

## Load Bearing

Without it losing way is merely going slower, and going slower is not fatal.
It is load bearing because it makes speed into **the resistance to going sideways**: the keel holds her up against the wind's push by generating side force, and that force goes as the square, so halving the way quarters it and the leeway rises to make up the shortfall.

## Apparent

A technicality of hydrodynamics, of interest to designers.
It is the mechanism by which a vessel is set ashore without being steered there, and it operates most strongly in exactly the seaway that is taking her way.

## Yearbook

Measured in the aerodynamics and hydrodynamics of sailing, paid at [the deck](place_the_deck.md).
It is why the compass can be right and the vessel still be closing.
