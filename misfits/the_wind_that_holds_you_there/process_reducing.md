# Process: Reducing

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). Taking sail off as the wind rises: correct seamanship, and the removal of the drive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Wind enough that the vessel is overpressed.
At [the deck](place_the_deck.md), before it is too late to send people aloft.

## Direction

Toward keeping the rig standing and the vessel upright.
This is not caution and it is not a failure of nerve: a rig carried too long in rising wind comes down, and a vessel pressed too hard goes over, and both of those end the question immediately.
So sail comes off, at the right time, by people who know exactly why.

## Lever

Less area, less force.
Which is the entire point, and is also the drive that was being used to work off the land, so **the correct act and the costly act are the same act**.

## Echo

A rig that will still be there in the morning,
aboard a vessel that is going ashore tonight.
