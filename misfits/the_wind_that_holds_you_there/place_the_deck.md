# Place: The Deck

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). Where the situation is read and the orders are given: competent, well found, and out of options.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A master, a mate, a glass falling, a coast on the chart and not yet in sight.
Sail coming off at the right time by people who know why.

## Holds

The play's quietest fact: **nothing here is being done badly.**
The position is known. The weather is read correctly. The seamanship is sound and would be praised by anybody watching. The trap does not need an error and is not given one.

## Offers

Every action a vessel in this position can take, all of them available, none of them withheld by ignorance.

## Withheld

A third reading.
Carry sail and reduce sail are both right about the danger each is looking at, so there is nowhere to stand from which one of them is the mistake, and no amount of skill produces an option that is simply correct.
