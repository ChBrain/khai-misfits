# Persona: The Sector

## Taxonomy

[Bearing](position_bearing.md). The arc no vessel can be steered into: roughly forty-five degrees either side of where the wind is coming from.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Not a flaw. A price, and a small one on most days.
Ninety degrees of the compass, out of three hundred and sixty, unavailable as a heading.
Every other direction is open, which is why nobody thinks about this one.

## Action

Sits where the wind is.
Moves when the wind moves.
Excludes.

## Shadow

That it points at whatever the wind is blowing away from, which on a coast is the sea, and therefore the safety.
The arc is not aimed at anything by intent: it simply lies opposite the wind, and a shore to leeward is by definition a shore the wind is blowing toward.
It ends covering the only exit, having been given that job by nothing but geometry.

## Tell

Nobody notices it in open water.
It is the same size there.
