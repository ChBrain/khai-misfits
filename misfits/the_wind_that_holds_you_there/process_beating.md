# Process: Beating

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). Working to windward by tacking: the capability in action, and the achievement of the whole rig.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A destination to windward.
At [the windward leg](place_the_windward_leg.md), one board at a time.

## Direction

Toward a place the wind is coming from, which nothing else on board can reach.
Every leg points somewhere other than the destination, and the destination is reached anyway: this is not a workaround but the ordinary and correct way a sailing vessel goes to weather, and it is why a rig is worth having.
The distance sailed is perhaps half again the distance made. That is the price and it is paid gladly.

## Lever

Lift, from wind flowing across the sail, converted into ground gained toward the weather.
It works, reliably, on any ordinary day, which is the fact the play establishes before it establishes anything else.

## Echo

A coast weathered, a harbour entered against the wind,
by a vessel that could not have been steered at either of them.
