# Piece: The Jury Rig

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). Damage already done by the same weather, having removed exactly the capability that weather is about to demand.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Where the masts were.
Aboard a vessel that has already survived one thing and is now short of what the next thing requires.

## Load Bearing

Without it the coupling is an argument and with it the coupling is an object.
It is load bearing because it is the same variable acting twice in the plainest possible form: **the storm that is setting her down took the rig she would leave with**, and no separate misfortune is required.

## Apparent

Bad luck compounding, one thing after another.
Not compounding and not two things. A jury rig is what a gale leaves behind, and needing to beat off a lee shore is what a gale creates, so the shortfall and the demand have one cause.

## Yearbook

Rigged by a competent crew doing well with what is left, spent on a lee shore.
HMS St George was under one when she went ashore on the Jutland coast in December 1811.
