# Piece: The Chart

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). The shore correctly marked, accurately known, and of no assistance whatever.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the table, open, correct.
Read by [the master](persona_the_master.md), who knows exactly where the danger is.

## Load Bearing

Without it this is a play about not knowing, and not knowing has a remedy.
It is load bearing because it removes ignorance from the account entirely: **the position is known, the shore is charted, and the trap is unaffected.** Everything in this production is visible to everybody in it.

## Apparent

The instrument that prevents this, which is what charts are for and what they usually do.
Here it tells the master precisely which direction the vessel cannot be steered in, which is knowledge and is not capability.

## Yearbook
