## Tension

This is the coupling, and it is what makes the production a structure rather than a hazard.
**One variable moves both quantities.** As the wind rises the vessel is set toward the land faster, and her power to work off it falls, and these are not two misfortunes arriving together: they are one fact seen twice. There is no version of this weather that brings the danger without the incapacity.
The first mechanism is [the reefed sail](piece_the_reefed_sail.md), and it is where the play refuses the master a free move. Reducing sail is not the timid option: a rig carried too long in rising wind comes down and ends the question. So the act is right, and the drive it removes is the drive that was working her off the land. **The correct act and the costly act are one act.**
