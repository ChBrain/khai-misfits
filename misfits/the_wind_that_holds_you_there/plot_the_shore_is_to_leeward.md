# Plot: The Shore Is to Leeward

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Chart](piece_the_chart.md), open, correct, and consulted.
A wind that has gone into the west, with the coast in the east.

## Action

[Closing](process_closing.md): the geometry arrives at [the deck](place_the_deck.md), where [The Master](persona_the_master.md), in [the command seat](position_command.md), reads the position and knows it exactly.

## Stage

[The Sector](persona_the_sector.md), in [the bearing seat](position_bearing.md), is discovered to be covering the only way out, having been put there by nothing.

## Tension

Here the property becomes a danger, and it does so without anybody doing anything.
A shore to leeward is a shore the wind is blowing onto. Leaving it means going to windward. Going to windward means entering the arc, and the arc is exactly what cannot be entered. **Nothing has gone wrong yet and the trap is already complete.**
And the account contains no ignorance to remove. The chart is correct, the position is known, the shore was surveyed properly and is marked where it is. The master can name precisely which direction the vessel cannot be steered in, which is **knowledge, and is not capability**, and no better information exists to be supplied.
