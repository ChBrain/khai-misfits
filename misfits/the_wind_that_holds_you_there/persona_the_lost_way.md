# Persona: The Lost Way

## Taxonomy

[Speed](position_speed.md). The vessel's way through the water going off, and the leeway coming up behind it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Speed, and what speed was quietly buying.
The keel resists being pushed sideways by generating side force, and side force varies with the **square** of the speed through the water.
So way is not only progress. It is the whole of the resistance to going sideways.

## Action

Falls, as the seas stop her.
Falls faster than anybody is watching for.
Hands the difference to leeway.

## Shadow

That losing it is indistinguishable, from on deck, from simply going slower.
The heading does not change. The helm feels the same. The vessel is being carried bodily to leeward at a rate that has risen while her course over the ground has quietly swung, and the only instrument that reports it is the land.
It ends putting her ashore without anyone having steered there.

## Tell

Four knots became two.
The side force did not halve.
