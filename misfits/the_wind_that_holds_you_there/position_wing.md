# Position: Wing

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). The seat of the aerofoil: the capability, and the source of the incapacity, in one object.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Force generated from wind passing across it.
The ability to make ground toward the weather, which is the whole achievement of a sailing rig.
One angle at which it makes nothing at all.

## Orders

Generate.
Work to windward.
Stall, at the arc, and stall completely.

## Loses

Nothing, and this seat is where the play gives the rig its due: **the wing is not the fault.**
A rig that could be aimed at the wind would be a rig producing no force, so the sector is not a defect that better building removes; it is the same fact as the lift, seen from the other side.
It ends correct, and unable to point at the one bearing it is best at approaching.

## Drives

Toward getting somewhere the wind is not sending you,
which nothing else on board can do,
so the seat that supplies the freedom is the seat that fixes where the freedom stops.
