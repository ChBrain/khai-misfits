# Position: Command

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). The seat that must choose, between two readings that are both correct.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An accurate picture of the situation.
Experience of this exact situation, and of getting off.
Two options, each right about the danger it is looking at.

## Orders

Read the weather and the coast.
Weigh carrying sail against reducing it.
Decide, now.

## Loses

Nothing to any error, which is the seat's whole difficulty.
Carrying sail is right about the shore and wrong about the rig; reducing sail is right about the rig and wrong about the shore, and there is no vantage point from which either is the mistake, so being better at this changes nothing about what is available.
It ends having decided well.

## Drives

Toward getting the ship off,
by the only kind of judgement the situation admits,
so the seat that is answerable for the outcome is the seat with no option that is simply correct.
