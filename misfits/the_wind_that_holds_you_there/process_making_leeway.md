# Process: Making Leeway

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). The vessel carried bodily sideways, at a rate set by how much way she has left.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Wind on the sails pushing sideways as well as forward.
At [the deck](place_the_deck.md), continuously, in every condition, unremarkably.

## Direction

Toward wherever the wind is going, at some angle off the heading.
Every sailing vessel makes some, always, and a good one is not one that makes none but one that makes less than others.
So it is present on the finest day and nobody mentions it.

## Lever

The shortfall between the wind's sideways push and the keel's side force.
And the keel's side force goes as the **square of the speed**, so the seaway that takes her way from four knots to two does not halve the resistance: it quarters it, and the leeway rises to make up the difference.

## Echo

The same heading on the compass,
and a course over the ground that has quietly swung toward the land.
