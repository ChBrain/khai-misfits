# Process: Closing

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). The shore coming nearer, with nobody steering at it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A shore to leeward and a wind that has risen.
At [the deck](place_the_deck.md), while every hand is doing the right thing.

## Direction

Toward the land, which is not moving and is correctly charted.
There is no error in this sequence to find. The helm is where it should be, the sails are set as they should be, the position is known, and the vessel closes anyway, because her course over the ground is her heading plus her leeway and the second term has grown.
That is the whole of it: **nobody steers at the shore, and she arrives at it.**

## Lever

The gap between where she is pointed and where she is going.
Invisible from on board, because the compass reports the heading and the heading is fine, and the only instrument that reports the difference is the land itself.

## Echo

Breakers, closer than the last time anybody looked,
on a bearing that has not changed, which is the worst news a bearing can carry.
