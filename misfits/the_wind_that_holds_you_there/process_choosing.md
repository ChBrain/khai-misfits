# Process: Choosing

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). The master weighing two readings, and finding both of them right.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A situation that will not wait.
At [the deck](place_the_deck.md), in the time it takes to say it.

## Direction

Toward whichever danger is nearer, which is a genuine question with no general answer.
Carry sail: the drive to claw off, and the risk of losing the rig, after which nothing else matters. Reduce sail: the rig preserved, and the ground given away.
**Each branch is correct about the danger it is looking at**, so this is not a test of nerve and not a test of prudence.

## Lever

Judgement, exercised well, on a choice that does not contain a right answer.
Which is why the play refuses to give this seat an error: being better at it would change the wording of the order and not the position of the vessel.

## Echo

An order given levelly,
because there is nothing in it that argument would improve.
