# Persona: The Rig

## Taxonomy

[Wing](position_wing.md). The sail as an aerofoil: the reason a vessel can go somewhere other than downwind.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The achievement, and the production says so before it says anything else.
A wing, generating force from wind passing across it, which is what lets a vessel make ground toward the weather.
Without it there is no sailing, only drifting.

## Action

Generates.
Works to windward, a leg at a time.
Refuses, at one particular angle, and refuses absolutely.

## Shadow

That the refusal is not a limitation of this rig but of every rig.
Lift needs flow across the sail, so a sail aimed at the wind makes nothing, and no amount of building it better moves the arc: a rig that could face the wind would be a rig that generated no force at all.
It ends unable to go the one direction it is best at going toward.

## Tell

Draws beautifully at forty-five degrees.
Shakes, and stops, at thirty.
