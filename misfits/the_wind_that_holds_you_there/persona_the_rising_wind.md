# Persona: The Rising Wind

## Taxonomy

[Weather](position_weather.md). One variable, moving two quantities in opposite directions.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Weather. Not malice, not a test, not an antagonist.
It has no information about the vessel and no interest in the shore.
It does exactly one thing, which is blow harder.

## Action

Rises.
Sets her down toward the land.
Takes her power to leave it.

## Shadow

That both of those are the same act.
Nothing has to go wrong twice: the strength that increases the danger is the strength that forces sail off and raises the sea, and there is no version of it that brings only the first half.
It ends having authored a trap without an intention anywhere in it.

## Tell

The glass falling.
That is all the notice there is, and it is notice of one thing, not two.
