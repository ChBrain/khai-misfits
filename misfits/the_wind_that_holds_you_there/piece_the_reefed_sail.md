# Piece: The Reefed Sail

## Taxonomy

[The Wind That Holds You There](play_the_wind_that_holds_you_there.md). Sail taken off at the right time: correct, necessary, and the drive gone with it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Aloft, then on deck.
Ordered by [the master](persona_the_master.md) at [the deck](place_the_deck.md), at the moment good practice requires.

## Load Bearing

Without it the master has a free move and the play collapses into a lesson about being bold.
It is load bearing because reducing sail is **not the timid option**: a rig carried too long in rising wind comes down and ends the question, so the act is right, and the drive it removes is the drive that was working her off the land.

## Apparent

Prudence, traded against progress, with a brave choice available to whoever wants it.
There is no brave choice. **Carrying on is right about the shore and wrong about the rig**, and losing the rig does not leave a vessel that can still try.

## Yearbook

Ordered by good seamanship, spent at [the deck](place_the_deck.md).
It is the play's proof that the correct act and the costly act can be one act.
