# Plot: And the Deep Parameters Are Deep Only Until Somebody Checks

## Taxonomy

[True Until It Was Used](play_true_until_it_was_used.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The remedy, which is to model what sits underneath the rules.

## Action

[Going after the deep parameters](process_going_after_the_deep_parameters.md): [the deep parameter](persona_the_deep_parameter.md) is sought, estimated and built upon.

## Stage

A generation of models rests on quantities meant to hold across arrangements.

## Tension

Whether those quantities are more invariant is an empirical question, and it has been asked.
The reasoning is correct and the whole discipline turned on it: if the rules are regime-dependent, model what the rules are derived from. What that rests on is a claim about the world rather than a definition, and tests of whether the deeper quantities are stable across regimes have not come back as the programme needed. So the remedy is real, is the right move, and inherits the same question one level down.
