## Stage

The relation between the aggregates is what those rules add up to.

## Tension

The equation describes the arrangement as much as the economy, and nothing on its face distinguishes the two.
People decide how much to save, how much to borrow and what to expect by working out what the standing arrangement rewards, so an aggregate relation is a summary of answers to a particular question. The coefficients, the fit and the residuals all look exactly as they would if the relation were a law, because in a record where the question never changed there is no way for them to look otherwise.
