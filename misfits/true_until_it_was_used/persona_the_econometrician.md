# Persona: The Econometrician

## Taxonomy

[Econometrician](position_econometrician.md). The one whose estimate is right about every year of the sample.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Twenty years of data, two aggregates that move together, and every diagnostic passing.
A committee that will ask what the relation is.

## Action

Specifies, estimates, tests, reports.
States the relation and its standard errors.

## Shadow

Does nothing wrong and hands over an object with a property nobody can see in it.
The estimate fits, predicts within the sample, and is correct about the whole of the period it covers; and what it summarises is behaviour under one arrangement, so it carries an unstated condition that no diagnostic tests and no better econometrics removes.

## Tell

Is right about the past and is asked for something about the future.
