# Place: The Old Regime

## Taxonomy

[True Until It Was Used](play_true_until_it_was_used.md). Where the relation is true, is measured, and is measured under one arrangement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Twenty years of two aggregates moving together, through recessions and recoveries, with the relation holding.
Everything working: honest series, a sound specification, diagnostics passing, forecasts landing.

## Holds

[The Household](persona_the_household.md), living sensibly under an arrangement it has worked out.
[The Regime](persona_the_regime.md), constant throughout and therefore invisible.
[The Data](piece_the_data.md), which records everything except the one thing that never varied.

## Offers

A real relation, correctly estimated, that will predict well for as long as nothing about the arrangement changes.

## Withheld

Any variation in the arrangement.
The place cannot show what it does not contain: one standing policy across the whole sample means the estimate carries no coefficient on the policy, so there is nothing in the data, in the diagnostics or in the residuals that could reveal how much of the relation is the economy and how much of it is the arrangement.
