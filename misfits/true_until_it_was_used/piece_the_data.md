## Load Bearing

It cannot show the effect of the one thing that never changed, so the condition is unfalsifiable from inside the sample.
Twenty years under one standing arrangement records twenty years of variation in everything except the arrangement, so there is no coefficient to estimate on it, no residual pattern to reveal it and no stability test that could fail on it. The condition is not hidden by anybody: it is absent from the record because it was constant, which is the same reason the relation looked like a law.
