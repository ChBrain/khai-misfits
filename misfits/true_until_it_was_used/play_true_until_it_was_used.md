# Play: True Until It Was Used

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

True Until It Was Used.
A relation between two aggregates holds for twenty years. It is measured carefully, tested honestly, and it is real. A committee with a decision to make uses it, because it is the best account anybody has of how the economy behaves. And the relation is a summary of what households and firms decided to do under the arrangement that was in force, so the decision that leans on it alters the arrangement, the decisions change, and the relation is a summary of nothing.

## Arc

The bend is this: an estimated relation is made of answers, and using it changes the question.
It runs on a relation that is genuinely there.
[The Relation](piece_the_relation.md) is estimated by [The Econometrician](persona_the_econometrician.md) from [the data](piece_the_data.md) and is correct about every year in it. Nothing here is a bad model, a misspecification or a fluke: it fits, it predicts within the sample, and it goes on holding.
And it is made of rules formed under a regime.
[The Household](persona_the_household.md) holds [The Rule](piece_the_rule.md), which is a sensible answer to [The Regime](persona_the_regime.md) as it stands, and the relation between the aggregates is what those rules add up to. So the equation is a description of the arrangement quite as much as of the economy.
Then [The Policy Is Chosen With the Best Model There Is](plot_the_policy_is_chosen_with_the_best_model_there_is.md).
[The Policymaker](persona_the_policymaker.md) has to decide, has to decide on something, and the only accounts of how the economy behaves are estimated from a regime. Declining to use a model is not available: it is choosing by a worse one.
And [The Household Revises, and Nobody Games Anything](plot_the_household_revises_and_nobody_games_anything.md).
The rule was an answer to the old arrangement and the arrangement has changed, so the household changes its answer. That is not gaming, not evasion and not a response to being measured: it is the same prudence that produced the rule in the first place, and it would happen if nobody had ever estimated anything.
And [The Relation Stops Holding at the Moment It Is Used](plot_the_relation_stops_holding_at_the_moment_it_is_used.md). Not slowly and not because the world drifted: the act of leaning on the relation is the act that removes the conditions under which it was true, so the instrument fails on the one occasion anybody needed it.
And [the deep parameters are deep only until somebody checks](plot_and_the_deep_parameters_are_deep_only_until_somebody_checks.md). [The Deep Parameter](persona_the_deep_parameter.md) is the real remedy, sought by modelling what is meant to sit underneath the rules rather than the rules themselves, and whether the things found down there are any more invariant is an empirical question with an unwelcome answer.

## Triggers

**[The Relation Is Real and Was Measured Correctly](plot_the_relation_is_real_and_was_measured_correctly.md)**
Cue: two aggregates that have moved together for twenty years. Closes when the estimate has been shown to be sound, tested, and right about every year it covers, so nothing here is a bad model and no better econometrics reaches it.

**[It Is Made of Rules People Formed Under a Regime](plot_it_is_made_of_rules_people_formed_under_a_regime.md)**
Cue: the households and firms whose behaviour the aggregates aggregate. Closes when the relation has been located in decision rules that are sensible answers to the arrangement in force, so the equation describes the arrangement as much as the economy.

**[The Policy Is Chosen With the Best Model There Is](plot_the_policy_is_chosen_with_the_best_model_there_is.md)**
Cue: a committee that must decide, and a decision that cannot wait. Closes when using an estimated relation has been shown to be the responsible course, since declining to use a model is choosing by a worse one and every available model is estimated from a regime.

**[The Household Revises, and Nobody Games Anything](plot_the_household_revises_and_nobody_games_anything.md)**
Cue: an arrangement that has changed, and a rule that was an answer to the old one. Closes when the revision has been shown to be ordinary prudence rather than evasion, indistinguishable from what produced the rule, and unrelated to anybody being measured.

**[The Relation Stops Holding at the Moment It Is Used](plot_the_relation_stops_holding_at_the_moment_it_is_used.md)**
Cue: the first year after, and a forecast that misses. Closes when the failure has been traced to the act of leaning on the relation rather than to drift, so the instrument breaks on the one occasion anybody needed it.

**[And the Deep Parameters Are Deep Only Until Somebody Checks](plot_and_the_deep_parameters_are_deep_only_until_somebody_checks.md)**
Cue: the remedy, which is to model what sits underneath the rules. Closes when whether those quantities are any more invariant has been found to be an empirical question that has been asked, and answered less favourably than the programme needs.

## Stakes

Whether a decision can be taken on the only kind of evidence there is about how an economy behaves.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a relation that is real and correctly estimated, decision rules that are sensible answers to an arrangement, a committee that must choose and has nothing better to choose with, a revision that is prudence rather than evasion, and a failure that arrives with the use rather than with the years, and away from any account in which somebody gamed a number, misspecified a model or was slow to update. The production cannot fault an econometrician whose estimate was right about every year of the sample, and it cannot fault a committee for using the best account anybody has. What is left to fight over is not whether the relation was true, it was, but what to call an instrument that is valid until it is picked up.
