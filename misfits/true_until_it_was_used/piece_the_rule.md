## Load Bearing

It changes for reasons that have nothing to do with being measured, which is what removes the last agent anybody could blame.
The rule is an answer to a question about what is taxed, what is promised and what happens when things go badly. Change the question and the answer changes, by the same prudence that produced it. Nobody is gaming a target, nobody is evading a measurement, and the household need never have heard of the estimate: the revision would happen in a world where no economist existed.
