# True Until It Was Used: Reference

The research warrant for True Until It Was Used.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the Lucas critique and on what an estimated relation between
aggregates is actually made of, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging policy evaluation as **an instrument that is valid until it is picked up**: a relation between two
aggregates holds for twenty years, is measured carefully, is tested honestly, and is real. It is a summary of
what households and firms decided to do, and what they decided is a sensible answer to the standing
arrangement they live under: what is taxed, what is promised, what is done when things go badly. So the
equation describes the arrangement as much as it describes the economy. A committee that must decide leans on
it, because it is the best account anybody has, and the leaning changes the arrangement, so the answers change
and the relation is a summary of nothing. The play models the documented mechanics, the estimate that is right
about every year of its sample, the decision rules the relation is made of, the arrangement that is invisible
because it never varied, the responsible use that removes the condition, the revision that is prudence rather
than evasion, and the deeper quantities the remedy reaches for, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure,
not as a tale of a bad model or a gamed number.

The governing law is that an estimated relation between aggregates is a summary of behaviour under a regime,
so it is a description of the regime as much as of the economy, and using it to change the regime changes what
it was a description of. The use and the invalidation are one event.

**Nobody games anything, and that is what separates this production from the misfit next to it.** The household
revises because the question it was answering has changed, by the same prudence that produced the original
answer. It has no interest in any published number, may never have heard of one, and would revise identically
in a world containing no economists. There is no pressure to relieve and no proxy to correct.

What removes any possibility of blame from the econometrician is that the estimate is sound. It fits, it
predicts within the sample, the diagnostics pass and it is correct about every year it covers. Better technique
would produce a better estimate of the same conditional object, because the difficulty is not in the estimate
but in what the estimated thing is made of.

What removes it from the policymaker is that declining to use a model is choosing by a worse one. Every account
of how an economy behaves was estimated from a period in which some arrangement was in force, so there is no
unconditioned instrument on the shelf: the real choice is between a model carrying the property and a guess
carrying no information, and taking the model is right. The failure is entered by doing the correct thing.

And the record closes the case, because it could not have warned anybody. Twenty years under one standing
arrangement contain twenty years of variation in everything except the arrangement, so there is no coefficient
to estimate on it, no residual pattern to reveal it and no stability test that could fail on it. The condition
is not concealed: it is absent from the data because it was constant, which is the same reason the relation
looked like a law.

It is distinct from the house's **Metric Fixation**, which has been narrowed in this pull request to say so in
its own warrant. There a measure becomes a target and somebody with an interest in the number optimises
against it, so there is pressure to relieve and a proxy to change, and the remedy reaches the failure. Here
there is no target, no pressure and no interested party: the household is not responding to a measurement at
all. Goodhart's sentence is often stretched to cover both, and it should not be, because one failure is
removed by taking the incentive away and the other is not touched by it.

It is distinct from the house's **The Weather We Make**, where risk models in common use drive correlated
selling that manufactures the very risk they measure: this is the closest mechanism in the house and the
objects differ. There the joint use of a model **creates a phenomenon** that was not there, through a feedback
between measurement and behaviour, so the model becomes true of a world it made. Here nothing is created and
there is no feedback loop: the parameters were never invariant, the use reveals that rather than causing it,
and what fails is the standing of an old measurement rather than the emergence of a new hazard.

It is distinct from the house's **Chasing the Price**, which carries the cobweb and holds Muth's rational
expectations as its remedy row: there producers plan on a price they can see and the lag makes the synchronised
response overshoot, so the failure is a cycle in a quantity. Here nothing oscillates and nothing overshoots.
One decision is taken once, and what fails is not a quantity but the truth of an equation.

It is distinct from the house's **Time Inconsistency**, which holds Kydland and Prescott: there a policymaker's
optimal plan for later stops being optimal when later arrives, so the difficulty is **commitment** and the
remedy is a rule that binds. Here commitment is not at issue and a policymaker who could bind themselves
completely gains nothing: the instrument they would bind themselves to is the thing that stops being true.

It is distinct from the house's **Right for the Last World**, where an organisation holds a form that fitted an
environment which has since turned: there the environment turns **on its own** and the failure is persistence,
so noticing and changing would escape it. Here the environment turns because the actor acted, and noticing
changes nothing, since the act of using the model is what moves the world the model described.

## Origin

The critique, the tradition it came out of, the case it was written about, the remedy, and the argument that
is still open.

| Source                               | Key Work / Event                                                                                                       | Scope                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Robert E. Lucas**                  | "Econometric Policy Evaluation: A Critique" (Carnegie-Rochester Conference Series on Public Policy, 1976)              | **The spine**: the parameters of an estimated macroeconomic relation are not invariant to policy, because they embed decision rules formed as optimal responses to the regime in force, so the relation cannot evaluate a change to it.      |
| **Jacob Marschak**                   | "Economic Measurements for Policy and Prediction" (in _Studies in Econometric Method_, 1953), and the Cowles programme | **The tradition it came out of**: the distinction between structural and reduced-form parameters and the requirement of autonomy, which shows the problem was named and worked on before it was made famous.                                 |
| **The Phillips-curve episode**       | a stable inflation and unemployment relation exploited for policy, and what followed                                   | **The case it was written about**: a relation that held across decades, was used as a menu of choices, and did not survive being treated as one, which is the empirical occasion for the argument.                                           |
| **The microfoundations programme**   | the turn to deep parameters, preferences and technology, and the models built on them                                  | **The remedy, and its shape**: the correct response to the failure, which is to model what the decision rules are derived from rather than the rules, and which the discipline adopted at scale.                                             |
| **Arturo Estrella & Jeffrey Fuhrer** | "Are 'Deep' Parameters Stable? The Lucas Critique as an Empirical Hypothesis" (Federal Reserve Bank of Boston, 1999)   | **The remedy's own premise, tested**: whether the deeper quantities are in fact invariant across regimes examined as an empirical claim rather than assumed, which puts the question one level down instead of answering it.                 |
| **Neil Ericsson & John Irons**       | "The Lucas Critique in Practice: Theory Without Measurement" (Federal Reserve Board, 1995)                             | **The dissent, cited to hold the line**: a survey of the literature citing Lucas finding virtually no evidence that empirically substantiates the critique, with super-exogeneity tests refuting it for various relations. Distinction only. |

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the econometrician
  as building a bad model or the household as gaming a number, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to
  get wrong: the estimate fits, predicts within its sample and is right about every year it covers; the
  arrangement is invisible in the data because it never varied, so no diagnostic could have flagged it; the
  committee must decide and declining to use a model is choosing by a worse one; and the household revises for
  the reason the rule existed, with no interest in any published number. The failure is authored by nobody and
  emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that an estimated relation is a summary of behaviour under an
  arrangement, and using it to change the arrangement ends what it summarised.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Modelling the deeper
  quantities is the right response, was adopted by the discipline at scale, and rests on the claim that those
  quantities are invariant, which is an empirical hypothesis that has been tested rather than a definition.
  Better econometrics produces a better estimate of the same conditional object. Waiting for more data
  accumulates more observations of the same unvaried arrangement. Announcing the policy in advance does not
  help, since the revision is a response to the arrangement rather than to the announcement. And knowing the
  critique completely, which every party may, still leaves the committee choosing between a conditional model
  and no model at all.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the critique, the structural-parameter tradition, the
  microfoundations programme, the stability tests and the dissent are theirs. The bounds are carried with them:
  **whether the critique bites empirically is genuinely contested**, and the dissent cited above surveyed the
  literature citing it and found virtually no evidence substantiating it, with tests refuting it for various
  relations, which the play carries as a live objection and does not settle; the play therefore stages the
  **structure**, that an estimated relation is conditional on the arrangement that generated it, and never a
  claim about how large the effect is in any economy or period; the microfoundations remedy is described as the
  discipline's response and not endorsed, and the stability of deep parameters is cited as a question that was
  asked rather than as a verdict; the Phillips-curve episode is carried as the occasion for the argument and
  not as a demonstration of it, since what happened there is itself argued over; and treating the arrangement
  as one thing that changes once is the play's own simplification. **The play makes no claim that macroeconomic
  models are useless or that any policy was wrongly chosen**, only that a relation estimated under one
  arrangement is a summary of answers to that arrangement, and that the decision which uses it is the decision
  that changes the question.
