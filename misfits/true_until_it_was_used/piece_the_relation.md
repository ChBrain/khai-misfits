# Piece: The Relation

## Taxonomy

[The Old Regime](place_the_old_regime.md). The estimated equation, true of its sample and conditional on something absent from it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Estimated in [the old regime](place_the_old_regime.md) by [estimating the relation](process_estimating_the_relation.md), and used in [the first year after](place_the_first_year_after.md).

## Load Bearing

It is a summary of decisions rather than a law, and nothing on its face says so.
Two aggregates move together because the people underneath them are following rules, and the rules are sensible answers to a standing arrangement. So the equation is a description of the arrangement as much as of the economy, and it carries that condition invisibly: the coefficients, the fit and the diagnostics all look exactly as they would if it were a law.

## Apparent

A finding. How the economy works.

## Yearbook

Is true of every year it was estimated on, and of no year in which it is used to choose.
Fails for a reason that is not drift, not misspecification and not noise.
