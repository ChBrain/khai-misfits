# Persona: The Household

## Taxonomy

[Household](position_household.md). The one whose rule the relation is made of.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An arrangement in force, and a sensible way of living with it.
Savings, borrowing, wages, and expectations formed the way anybody forms them.

## Action

Holds a rule that answers the arrangement.
Revises it when the arrangement changes.

## Shadow

Revises for exactly the reason the rule existed, and is not responding to being measured.
Nothing here is gaming, evasion or a reaction to a target: the same prudence that produced the rule produces the new one, the seat has no interest in any published number and may never have heard of one, and the revision would happen if nobody had ever estimated anything.

## Tell

Does the sensible thing twice, and breaks an equation by doing it.
