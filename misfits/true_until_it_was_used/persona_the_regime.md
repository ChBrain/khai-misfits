# Persona: The Regime

## Taxonomy

[Regime](position_regime.md). The arrangement the rules are answers to.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A standing policy: what is taxed, what is promised, what is done when things go badly.
Stable long enough for people to have worked out how to live under it.

## Action

Sets the question the rules answer.
Changes when the policymaker acts.

## Shadow

Is invisible in the data it generates, because it is constant throughout.
A period in which one arrangement held records no variation in the arrangement, so it appears in the estimate as nothing at all, and the relation cannot carry a coefficient on the thing that was never different.

## Tell

Is the condition on every estimate and appears in none of them.
