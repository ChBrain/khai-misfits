## Action

## Stage

## Tension

The act of leaning on the relation is the act that removes the conditions under which it was true.
Not drift, not decay, not a world that moved on its own: the use and the invalidation are one event. And there was no warning available beforehand, because the condition is invisible in a record where the arrangement never varied, so nobody could have said in advance how much of the relation was the economy and how much of it was the arrangement.
