# Place: The Reanalysis

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). Where one dataset yields two analyses that disagree, and the disagreement is fully explained. Hosts [Count the Time Instead](plot_count_the_time_instead.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The same subjects, the same dates and the same endpoint, with each life broken into the months before the treatment and the months after it.

## Holds

[The Landmark](piece_the_landmark.md), the cheaper correction and what it discards, and [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) that bounds the whole account.

## Offers

The true answer, and an exact account of why the other one differed, with no accusation attached to anybody.

## Withheld

The legibility of the thing it corrects, since the wrong analysis compared two groups of people and the right one compares two kinds of time, so what is handed back is a harder sentence to carry into a clinic than the one it replaces.
