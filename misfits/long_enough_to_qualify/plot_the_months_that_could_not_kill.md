# Plot: The Months That Could Not Kill

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Table](place_the_table.md), where person-time is totalled by group and the comparison everyone came for is finally made.

## Action

Through [Crediting](process_crediting.md), [the Analyst](persona_the_analyst.md) on [Compare the Treated with the Untreated](plan_compare_the_treated_with_the_untreated.md) assigns each subject's whole follow-up to the arm the register names, and [the Gift](piece_the_gift.md) passes: one arm's total now contains a stretch in which nobody could die and the other's does not. [The Finding](piece_the_finding.md) follows, a survival advantage equal to the length of the queue, and the scene closes with a result that is exact, tight, reproducible by anyone who repeats it, and false (Sylvestre, Huszti and Hanley).

## Stage

Nothing is dropped, no covariate is fudged, no specification is searched over, and the limitations section is written in good faith. A hostile reanalyst working the same way returns the same number, which is ordinarily the strongest evidence a result can have.

## Tension

The fault lies in the step from a record to a denominator, and that step is experienced as data handling rather than as inference, so no amount of statistical care is ever aimed at it, and the audit that would expose a fabricated finding is the audit that certifies this one, exact in every step and wrong in the whole.
