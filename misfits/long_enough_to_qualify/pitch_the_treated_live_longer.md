# Pitch: The Treated Live Longer

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Sober and quantitative, the tone of a result that has been checked.
A cohort of reasonable size, a clear endpoint, a treated group and an untreated one, and a survival difference large enough to matter and tight enough to believe.
Nothing false in it as a matter of procedure: every subject is present, no data are missing, no measurement is wrong, and the arithmetic is exact and will be exact again for anyone who repeats it.

## Undertow

Under the comparison, a denominator made of two different kinds of time.
Being counted as treated required surviving until the treatment arrived, so the months of waiting could not contain a death, and the record, having one field per person rather than one per stretch of a life, files those months in the treated column (Suissa).
No careless analyst and no padded study: the physician treated when the moment came, the registrar wrote down who was treated, and the analyst compared the only two groups the data offers.

## Nerve

The appeal of the result, and the catch that the checking is what confirms it:
that the advantage is the length of the queue rather than the effect of the treatment, so the more the arithmetic is audited the more solid a false finding becomes,
and the treated live longer is the sentence in which a definition is mistaken for a benefit.

## Echo

After the run: a guideline moved, and a physician acting on an artefact of his own scheduling, with no way from inside the paper to see it (Sylvestre, Huszti and Hanley).
And the remedy, real and priced: count the time and not the person, letting each subject contribute untreated months until the treatment and treated months after, which works and is harder to explain to anyone who has to act on it; or set a landmark, which is simple, defensible, and buys its correctness by discarding every event before a date that has no correct value, the early and highest-hazard period going with it (Lévesque, Hanley, Kezouh and Suissa).
And the bound held in view: it needs exposure defined by an event after entry, it needs no confounder and would survive randomisation and perfect measurement, so the whole of it is that the months you had to survive in order to qualify are credited to the thing you qualified for.
