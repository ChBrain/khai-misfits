# Plot: You Had to Live to Get It

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ward](place_the_ward.md), where a patient is waiting for something that is not yet available.

## Action

Through [Waiting](process_waiting.md), [the Physician](persona_the_physician.md) on [Treat When the Moment Comes](plan_treat_when_the_moment_comes.md) treats in month seven, because month seven is when the organ arrived, and [the Waiting](piece_the_waiting.md) is set: the six months before it are months this patient had to survive in order to be treated at all. The scene closes with a window that cannot contain a death, not as a matter of luck but as a matter of construction, since a patient who died inside it would never have been recorded as treated and would sit among the untreated (Suissa).

## Stage

There is no clinical argument for delay and none is made. The treatment is given at the first moment it exists to be given, which is the best any ward can do and exactly what everyone in it is trying to do.

## Tension

The raw material of the artefact is produced by the one practice nobody would change, so the interval cannot be shortened by anyone acting better, and the only person who could see what it will become is not in this room and will not arrive for two years.
