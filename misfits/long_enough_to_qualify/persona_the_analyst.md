# Persona: The Analyst

## Taxonomy

[Analyst](position_analyst.md). The one who compares the only two groups the data offers, and computes correctly on them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent and careful, checking the numbers, reporting the interval, and stating the limitations section honestly.
Compares the treated with the untreated, that being the comparison the study was mounted to make and the classification the register supplies.
Would welcome an audit and would pass one.

## Action

Runs [Compare the Treated with the Untreated](plan_compare_the_treated_with_the_untreated.md): through [Crediting](process_crediting.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md), handing over [the Gift](piece_the_gift.md) and producing [the Finding](piece_the_finding.md).
Takes each patient's follow-up from entry to endpoint and assigns it to the group the register puts them in.
Reports a survival advantage that is exact, tight, and reproducible by anyone who repeats the calculation.

## Shadow

Not sloppy and not motivated: no subject is dropped, no covariate is fudged, no specification is searched over, and the result would be identical from a hostile reanalyst working the same way.
Because the fault is in the step from a record to a denominator, and that step feels like data handling rather than inference, its diligence never reaches the thing that is wrong, and checking the arithmetic again returns the same number with more confidence attached.
It is the seat where an exact calculation and a false conclusion meet without contradiction.

## Tell

Presents a result that survives every check the field routinely applies.
Cannot see the immortal span, since nothing in the table is labelled as time that could not contain a death, and the only question that would reveal it, what is this person-time made of, is not a question the design invites.
