# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). The condition and the hedge: it needs exposure defined by an event occurring after entry, and it needs nothing else at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Reanalysis](place_the_reanalysis.md), where the corrected account states its own scope rather than claiming more than it holds.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play overreaches into a claim it cannot support, that observational research is generally untrustworthy or that the affected findings were dishonest, which the source literature does not say and the house will not stage.
It bears the load as the limit that keeps the finding sharp in both directions. Because the bias needs no confounder, no collider and no missing data, and would appear in a cohort with randomised exposure and perfectly measured covariates, it cannot be dismissed as a symptom of weak observational work; and because it needs exposure defined after entry, it does not reach a design in which everyone is exposed from the first day, so it is a specific and correctable design fault rather than a general doubt.

## Apparent

A scope note, and an easy one to skip: the kind of qualification that reads as caution and is in fact what separates a correctable fault from a slander on a whole method.
