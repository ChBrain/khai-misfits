# Plan: Compare the Treated with the Untreated

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the analyst's seat](position_analyst.md), inside [Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Analyst](position_analyst.md)

## Direction

Make the comparison the study was mounted to make, between the two groups the register supplies, which are the only two groups the data offers. Take each subject's follow-up from entry to endpoint and assign it to the group they are recorded in. Drop nobody, fudge no covariate, search over no specifications, report the interval honestly and write the limitations section in good faith. Expect the result to survive checking, because it will: a hostile reanalyst working the same way will get the same number. And notice that nothing in the table is labelled as time in which death was impossible, because nothing in the table could be.

## Orders

([The Analyst](persona_the_analyst.md) credits through [Crediting](process_crediting.md) at [the Table](place_the_table.md), handing over [the Gift](piece_the_gift.md) and producing [the Finding](piece_the_finding.md).)

## Implementation

([The Table](place_the_table.md) is where person-time is totalled by group; [Crediting](process_crediting.md) assigns each subject's whole follow-up to the arm the register names, so one arm's total contains a stretch in which nobody could die and the other's does not, and the difference is reported as an effect.)

## Targets

- [ ] the comparison is made between the groups the data offers
- [ ] no subject is dropped and no specification is searched over
- [ ] the arithmetic is exact and reproducible by anyone who repeats it
- [ ] every check the field routinely applies is passed
- [ ] the step from a record to a denominator is never examined, not being experienced as inference at all
