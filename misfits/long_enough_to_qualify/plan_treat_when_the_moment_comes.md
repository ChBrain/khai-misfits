# Plan: Treat When the Moment Comes

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the physician's seat](position_physician.md), inside [Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Physician](position_physician.md)

## Direction

Give the treatment at the first moment it can be given. The organ arrives when it arrives, the indication declares itself when it declares itself, and the prescription is written when writing it becomes right. There is no clinical argument for delay and none is being made. Read the literature and move practice when the evidence says to, since a physician who did not would be failing the patient in front of him. And note what is not on the list of things you are asked to consider: what the follow-up time in a paper's two arms is made of, which is not a clinical question and has never been put to you.

## Orders

([The Physician](persona_the_physician.md) waits through [Waiting](process_waiting.md) at [the Ward](place_the_ward.md), setting [the Waiting](piece_the_waiting.md), and later adopts through [Adopting](process_adopting.md) at [the Round](place_the_round.md), meeting [the Finding](piece_the_finding.md).)

## Implementation

([The Ward](place_the_ward.md) is where the interval that cannot be avoided accrues; [the Round](place_the_round.md) is where the finding built from that interval comes back and changes what is done, so the seat supplies the raw material at one end and consumes the product at the other, with no vantage joining the two.)

## Targets

- [ ] the treatment is given at the first moment it can be given
- [ ] the interval before it is understood as unavoidable, since treating sooner is already the intent
- [ ] the literature is read and practice moves when it says to
- [ ] the finding is applied to the patient in front of you, accurately cited
- [ ] the loop is not visible from either end, which is the whole of the difficulty
