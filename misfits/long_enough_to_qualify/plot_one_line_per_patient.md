# Plot: One Line Per Patient

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Register](place_the_register.md), where what happened has to be written down.

## Action

Through [Filing](process_filing.md), [the Registrar](persona_the_registrar.md) on [Record What Happened](plan_record_what_happened.md) sets [the One Column](piece_the_one_column.md): the patient who was untreated in March and treated in September is entered as treated, once, because the field asks what became of this patient and that question has one true answer. The scene closes with the six months of waiting travelling into the treated column alongside the patient, entered by nobody as such and visible to nobody as such.

## Stage

The register is complete, accurate and auditable. Every date is right, no line is missing, nothing is guessed, and an auditor would pass it without a note, which is the highest praise a register can receive and is deserved.

## Tension

The shape of a field is not a decision anybody took, so there is no choice here to have made differently, no error to correct and nothing to mark, which leaves the most accurate actor in the play as the seat where two kinds of time are merged, and leaves the fault with no author and no moment at which it was committed.
