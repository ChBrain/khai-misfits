# Persona: The Physician

## Taxonomy

[Physician](position_physician.md). The one whose entirely proper waiting creates the window, and who later acts on the finding the window produced.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Attentive and unhurried, treating when the moment arrives, which is the only time it can be done and the right time to do it.
Reads the literature, changes practice when the evidence says to, and would be criticised if it did not.
Has no view about denominators and has never been asked to have one.

## Action

Runs [Treat When the Moment Comes](plan_treat_when_the_moment_comes.md): through [Waiting](process_waiting.md) at [the Ward](place_the_ward.md), setting [the Waiting](piece_the_waiting.md), and later through [Adopting](process_adopting.md) at [the Round](place_the_round.md), meeting [the Finding](piece_the_finding.md).
Treats in month seven because the organ arrived in month seven, and the six months before it were months the patient had to survive to be treated at all.
Reads a paper that says the treatment helps and moves practice toward it, which is what a paper that says that is for.

## Shadow

Not negligent and not credulous: nothing in the treating was wrong, nothing in the reading was lazy, and the paper it acted on had been checked.
Because the interval it could not avoid is the interval the finding is made of, its own good scheduling is the raw material of the artefact, and treating more promptly would shrink the bias while treating as soon as possible is already what it does.
It is at both ends of the loop and can see neither end from the other.

## Tell

Cites the finding accurately and applies it to the patient in front of it.
Cannot detect the fault from inside the paper, since the paper contains no missing subject, no wrong measurement and no arithmetic error, and the only trace of the problem is a question about what the person-time is made of that the paper never raises.
