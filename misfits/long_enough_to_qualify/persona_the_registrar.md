# Persona: The Registrar

## Taxonomy

[Registrar](position_registrar.md). The one who writes down what happened, in a record with one field per person.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Meticulous, unglamorous, and completely reliable: every patient entered, every treatment recorded, every date correct.
Takes pride in a register with no gaps and no guesses, and would notice at once if a line were missing.
Has never falsified anything and would not know how to begin.

## Action

Runs [Record What Happened](plan_record_what_happened.md): through [Filing](process_filing.md) at [the Register](place_the_register.md), setting [the One Column](piece_the_one_column.md).
Writes each patient down once, under what happened to them, because a register records people and what became of them.
Enters a patient untreated in March and treated in September as treated, there being one field and one answer.

## Shadow

Not a poor record-keeper: the register is complete, accurate and auditable, and an auditor would pass it without a note.
Because a field holds a person and not a period, the six months of waiting travel into the treated column alongside the patient, and no decision was made anywhere that could be revisited, since the shape of the field is not a decision anyone took.
It is the most accurate actor in the play and the seat where the two kinds of time are merged.

## Tell

Produces a register in which every fact is true and no fact is a duration.
Cannot record a patient as two things at once, since the form does not permit it and asking for a form that did would be asking a question nobody in the ward has reason to ask.
