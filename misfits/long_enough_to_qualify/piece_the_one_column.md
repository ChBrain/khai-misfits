# Piece: The One Column

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). The record's shape: one field per person, not one per stretch of a person's life, so a life containing two states is filed under one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Register](place_the_register.md), in entries that are complete, accurate, dated correctly, and would pass any audit without a note.

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by giving the record a field per interval rather than a field per person, and the waiting stays where it was lived and the bias never forms.
It bears the load as the reason nobody can be blamed. Because the shape of a field is not a decision anybody took, there is no choice here to have made differently, no error to correct and nothing an auditor could mark, so the fault has no author and no moment at which it was committed.

## Apparent

Completeness, and it is completeness: a register with no gaps and no guesses, in which every single fact recorded is true.
