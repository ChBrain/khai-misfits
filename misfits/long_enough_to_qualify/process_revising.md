# Process: Revising

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). Borne at [The Reanalysis](place_the_reanalysis.md), precipitated by [The Reviser](persona_the_reviser.md) asking what the person-time is made of, in [Count the Time Instead](plot_count_the_time_instead.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The one question the design does not invite, put to a dataset that answers it completely.

## Direction

From people counted toward time counted: each subject broken into untreated months before the treatment and treated months after it, and the advantage returning to where it was lived.

## Lever

That the correction is complete and its cost is real, the time-varying analysis being harder to act on and the landmark buying its correctness by discarding every event before an arbitrary date, which is the early and highest-hazard period (Lévesque, Hanley, Kezouh and Suissa).

## Echo

[The Landmark](piece_the_landmark.md) and [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), a true answer handed back in a form less usable than the false one, and a choice between a legible falsehood and an awkward truth.
