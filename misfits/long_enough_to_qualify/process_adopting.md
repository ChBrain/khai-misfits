# Process: Adopting

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). Borne at [The Round](place_the_round.md), precipitated by [The Physician](persona_the_physician.md) reading a finding and doing what a finding is for, in [It Changes What We Do](plot_it_changes_what_we_do.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A published result that is legible, plausible, useful, and says the treatment helps.

## Direction

From a paper toward practice: a guideline moved, a threshold lowered, a treatment offered a little more readily than it was last year.

## Lever

That acting on it is correct conduct, since a physician who ignored a checked and published finding would be failing the patient, so the trap is entered by doing the right thing with the evidence available.

## Echo

[The Finding](piece_the_finding.md) arriving from the other side, and the loop shutting on the seat that supplied its raw material, with no vantage joining the ward to the round (Sylvestre, Huszti and Hanley).
