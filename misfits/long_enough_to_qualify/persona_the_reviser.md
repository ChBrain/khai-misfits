# Persona: The Reviser

## Taxonomy

[Reviser](position_reviser.md). The one who asks what the person-time is made of, and whose correct answer costs evidence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Methodological rather than clinical, arriving after the result and interested in the denominator rather than the treatment.
Asks the one question the design does not invite, which is what the follow-up time in each arm consists of.
Carries no grievance against anyone and is not proposing that the study was badly done.

## Action

Runs [Count the Time, Not the Person](plan_count_the_time_not_the_person.md): through [Revising](process_revising.md) at [the Reanalysis](place_the_reanalysis.md), holding [the Landmark](piece_the_landmark.md) and [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md).
Breaks each subject into untreated months before the treatment and treated months after it, and the advantage goes.
Sets out the simpler alternative honestly, a fixed landmark date with whoever is alive at it, and states what it costs rather than recommending it plainly.

## Shadow

Not a debunker and not a hero: it removes a false finding and hands back an analysis that is harder to explain to the people who must act on it, which is a real loss and not a rhetorical one.
Because the landmark buys its correctness by discarding every event before an arbitrary date, and the discarded period is the early, highest-hazard one, the cheap version of the fix throws away the evidence the question most needed, and a landmark chosen after the curves are seen readmits the selection it was brought in to close.
It is the only actor who can end the trap and cannot end it for free.

## Tell

Produces two analyses of the same data that disagree, and can say exactly why, and cannot say which one a clinician should carry into the room.
Cannot make the corrected result as legible as the wrong one was, since the wrong one compared two groups of people and the right one compares two kinds of time.
