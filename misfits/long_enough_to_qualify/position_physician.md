# Position: Physician

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). The seat at both ends of the loop: the waiting it cannot avoid, and the finding that waiting produced.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A patient, a treatment that becomes available when it becomes available, and no way to make it available sooner.
An obligation to read the literature and to move practice when the evidence says to.
No view about denominators, and no occasion on which anyone has asked it to form one.

## Orders

Treat when the moment comes: give the treatment at the first moment it can be given, which is the right moment on every clinical ground there is.
Read the finding and act on it, since a finding that a treatment helps is a finding a physician is meant to use.
Accept what the seat does: the interval it cannot avoid is the interval the finding is made of.

## Loses

Any vantage from which the loop is visible, since the paper it acts on contains no missing subject, no wrong measurement and no arithmetic error.
Because treating as promptly as possible is already what it does, the seat holds no lever that would shrink the bias, and the only remedy available to it is a question about person-time that nothing in its training or its reading prompts.
It keeps every clinical judgement sound and loses the ability to tell the treatment's effect from its own scheduling.

## Drives

Toward the one seat that appears twice, first supplying the raw material and then acting on the product,
treating in month seven because the organ arrived in month seven, and later changing practice on a paper built from months one to six,
so the loop shuts on the most ordinary conduct in the play, correct at both ends and mistaken across them.
