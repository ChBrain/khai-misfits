# Plan: Record What Happened

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the registrar's seat](position_registrar.md), inside [Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Registrar](position_registrar.md)

## Direction

Enter every patient, record every treatment, get every date right, and leave no gaps and no guesses. Where a patient was untreated in March and treated in September, enter them as treated: the field asks what became of this patient and that has one true answer. Do not invent a representation the form does not have, and do not treat the absence of one as an error, since the shape of a field is not a decision anybody took and there is nothing here to have decided differently. Produce a register that would pass any audit, which it will.

## Orders

([The Registrar](persona_the_registrar.md) files through [Filing](process_filing.md) at [the Register](place_the_register.md), setting [the One Column](piece_the_one_column.md).)

## Implementation

([The Register](place_the_register.md) carries one line per patient and one field for what became of them; [Filing](process_filing.md) writes the person down under the state their life ended in, so the months before the treatment travel into the treated column alongside them, entered by nobody as such and visible to nobody as such.)

## Targets

- [ ] every patient is entered and every treatment recorded
- [ ] every date is correct and no line is missing
- [ ] a patient who was one thing and then another is entered under the one field provided
- [ ] the register is complete, accurate and auditable, and would pass without a note
- [ ] the distinction the analysis will need is not represented anywhere, there being no field for it
