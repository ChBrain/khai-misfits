# Plot: Count the Time Instead

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Reanalysis](place_the_reanalysis.md), and the one question the design does not invite: what the follow-up time in each arm is made of.

## Action

Through [Revising](process_revising.md), [the Reviser](persona_the_reviser.md) on [Count the Time, Not the Person](plan_count_the_time_not_the_person.md) breaks each subject into untreated months before the treatment and treated months after it, and the advantage goes back to where it was lived. Then it holds [the Landmark](piece_the_landmark.md) honestly rather than recommending it: the simpler fix starts a clock at a fixed date and keeps whoever is alive at it, and buys its correctness by discarding every event before that date, which is the early and highest-hazard period, on a date with no correct value, so one chosen after the curves are seen readmits the selection it was brought in to close. It closes on [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): the bias needs exposure defined after entry and needs nothing else, no confounder, no collider, no missing data, and would appear in a randomised cohort with perfect measurement (Lévesque, Hanley, Kezouh and Suissa).

## Stage

No accusation is made and none is warranted. The study was properly done, the arithmetic was exact, and the correction is a matter of counting the same lives a different way, which is why it can be offered without anybody being wrong in the way people are usually wrong.

## Tension

The corrected analysis is right and less usable, since the wrong one compared two groups of people and the right one compares two kinds of time, so what is handed back is a harder sentence to carry into a clinic than the one it replaces, and the play closes not on a rescue but on a choice between a legible falsehood and an awkward truth.
