# Position: Analyst

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). The seat where an exact calculation and a false conclusion meet without contradiction.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A complete register, two groups, and follow-up time from entry to endpoint for every subject.
The only classification the data offers, which is the one the register supplies.
No label anywhere marking a stretch of person-time as time in which death was impossible.

## Orders

Compare the treated with the untreated: make the comparison the study was mounted to make, on the groups it recorded.
Drop nobody, fudge nothing, search over no specifications, and report the interval honestly.
Accept what the seat does: it assigns each subject's whole follow-up to the group the register puts them in.

## Loses

Its own corrective, since every check the field routinely applies confirms the result.
Because the fault lies in the step from a record to a denominator, and that step is experienced as data handling rather than inference, the seat loses the possibility of catching it by diligence, and recomputing returns the same number with more confidence attached to it.
It keeps an arithmetic that is exact and loses the conclusion it supports.

## Drives

Toward a result reproducible by anyone, including a hostile reanalyst working the same way,
comparing the two groups the data offers and computing correctly on them,
so the audit that would expose a fabricated finding is the audit that certifies this one, exact in every step and wrong in the whole.
