# Position: Reviser

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). The seat that can end the trap and cannot end it for free.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The same data, and the one question the design does not invite: what the follow-up time in each arm is made of.
Two correct handlings, a time-varying exposure and a fixed landmark, and no third that is both correct and cheap.
No grievance against anyone, and no claim that the study was badly done.

## Orders

Count the time, not the person: break each subject into untreated months before the treatment and treated months after it, and let the advantage go where it belongs.
Set out the landmark alternative honestly, including what it discards, rather than recommending it plainly.
Accept what the seat does: it returns an analysis that is right and harder to act on than the one it replaces.

## Loses

The legibility of the thing it corrects, since the wrong analysis compared two groups of people and the right one compares two kinds of time.
Because the cheap version of the fix works by discarding every event before an arbitrary date, and that period is the early and highest-hazard one, the seat cannot deliver correctness without either spending evidence or spending clarity, and a landmark placed after the curves are seen readmits the selection it was brought in to close.
It keeps the truth and loses the sentence a clinician could carry into the room.

## Drives

Toward the only actor who can end it and the only one who must price the ending,
asking what the denominator consists of, producing two analyses of one dataset that disagree, and being able to say exactly why,
so the correction arrives as a genuine loss rather than a rescue, and the trap closes on a choice between a legible falsehood and an awkward truth.
