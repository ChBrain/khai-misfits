# Position: Registrar

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). The seat that records people accurately in a structure that has no place for periods.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A register with one line per patient and one field for what became of them.
Complete and accurate entries, every date right, no gaps and no guesses.
No field that can hold a patient who was one thing and then another.

## Orders

Record what happened: enter every patient, record every treatment, get every date right.
Write a patient untreated in March and treated in September down as treated, there being one field and one true answer to what it asks.
Accept what the seat does: it files a person, and a person is not a duration.

## Loses

Any representation of the two states a life passed through, since the form permits one and the question it asks has a single correct answer.
Because the shape of a field is not a decision anybody took, the seat loses the possibility of having decided otherwise, and there is no error here to find, correct or apologise for.
It keeps a register in which every fact is true and loses the distinction the analysis will need.

## Drives

Toward the most accurate actor in the play and the seat where two kinds of time are merged,
entering what happened, once, under the heading the form provides,
so a record that would pass any audit carries six months of impossibility into the treated column with nobody having put it there.
