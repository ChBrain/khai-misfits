# Long Enough to Qualify: Reference

The research warrant for **Long Enough to Qualify**, a staging of the phenomenon the literature calls
**immortal time bias**, also the time-dependent bias and the survivor-treatment selection bias (Suissa):
that where being counted as treated requires surviving until the treatment occurs, the span between
entering the study and receiving it is time in which the subject could not have died, since dying would
have removed them from the treated group, so assigning that span to the treated arm manufactures a
survival advantage out of the definition alone. It is held with the finding that the bias needs no
confounder, no collider and no missing data, that it survives perfect measurement and would appear in a
randomised cohort, and that the remedies cost real evidence rather than merely effort.

## Line of Work

Staging immortal time bias as a **bookkeeping structure**: the ordinary expectation is that a study
goes wrong through something absent or something crooked, a confounder nobody measured, a group that
never made it into the sample, a number somebody shaded. This one goes wrong with every subject
present, every measurement correct and every arithmetic step sound. The mechanism is exact. A cohort
opens, and exposure is defined by an event that takes time to arrive: the organ becoming available, the
prescription being filled, the award being won. Between the moment a subject enters and the moment that
event occurs, the subject cannot die, not as a matter of luck but as a matter of construction, because
a subject who died in that window would never have been recorded as treated at all and would sit in the
other column. That window is immortal by definition. The record, however, carries one field per person
and not one field per stretch of a person's life, so a life containing both states is filed under the
state it ended in, and the immortal window is credited to the treatment (Suissa). The comparison that
follows is then between a group whose person-time includes a stretch in which death was impossible and
a group whose person-time does not, and the treated group wins by exactly that much. The play models
the waiting that must be survived, the record that classifies a person rather than a period, the
crediting that moves the window into the wrong column, the finding that follows and is believed, and
the correction that costs, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, and not as a tale of a careless
analyst. The ordinary expectation is that a fair comparison needs the right people; the finding is that
it also needs their time filed in the right place. The governing law is that **where being counted as
exposed requires surviving until the exposure occurs, the waiting cannot contain a death, so crediting
the waiting to the exposed manufactures a survival advantage from the definition and not from the
treatment**.

It is an inference structure no staged misfit holds, and it is carefully distinct from its nearest
neighbours. It is distinct from The Survivors, Wald's survivorship bias, where the units that never
appear are the entire problem and the remedy is to reason about the absent: there the sample is drawn
once and the missing are missing, whereas here **every subject is present and counted**, nobody is
absent from anything, and the error lies in how each observed subject's time is apportioned between two
columns. It is distinct from Better in Both, the Will Rogers phenomenon, where patients are
reclassified from one stage to another and both stages' averages improve although no one's course
changed: there it is **people** that move between categories, whereas here nobody moves at all, every
patient stays exactly where they are, and it is their **time** that is filed under the wrong heading. It
is distinct from The False Trade-Off, collider and selection bias, which requires a common effect of
two variables to be conditioned on: immortal time requires no collider, no confounder and no
conditioning of any kind, and would appear in a cohort with randomised exposure and perfectly measured
covariates, which is what makes it a bookkeeping fault rather than a causal-structure fault. And it is
distinct from The Bounce, regression to the mean, which needs selection on an extreme value plus noise
to produce a spurious improvement: here there are no extremes, no noise is required, and the advantage
is not a chance reversion but a deterministic consequence of arithmetic that would reproduce exactly in
a dataset with no randomness in it at all.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast a sloppy
  analyst or a study padded to please a sponsor, because the finding it stages needs neither. The
  physician treats when the moment arrives, which is the only time it can be done and the right time to
  do it. The registrar records who was treated, that being what the record is for and what its fields
  permit. The analyst classifies subjects by treatment status because it is the only classification the
  dataset offers, and computes correctly on it. Nobody has mis-recorded anything, no data are missing,
  no measurement is wrong, and the resulting number is arithmetically exact. The fault lives in the step
  between a record and a denominator, which is not a step anybody experiences as an inferential choice
  at all, and that is precisely why it is not caught.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. The correct handling
  is to let subjects contribute unexposed person-time until the moment of treatment and exposed
  person-time thereafter, and it works, and it is harder to explain to the clinicians who must act on it
  than the wrong analysis was. The alternative, a landmark analysis that begins the clock at a fixed
  date and keeps only those alive at it, is simple and defensible and buys its correctness by
  **discarding every event before that date**, which is precisely the early, highest-hazard period the
  question most needs; and the date itself is arbitrary, so a landmark chosen after the curves have been
  seen readmits by the back door the very selection it was brought in to remove. The reckoning is that
  the fix costs evidence, that the amount of evidence it costs is set by a choice with no correct
  answer, and that the wrong analysis was the more legible one.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The naming and general statement of immortal time bias in pharmacoepidemiology
  is Suissa's; the paired demonstration in a statin cohort and the setting out of the time-varying and
  landmark handlings are Lévesque, Hanley, Kezouh and Suissa's; and the reanalysis showing an
  award-winner survival advantage to be the artefact is Sylvestre, Huszti and Hanley's, revisiting the
  earlier Redelmeier and Singh result, which is cited as the finding reanalysed and not as an error
  attributed to anyone. The scope is held to cohort designs in which exposure is defined by an event
  occurring after entry, and is not stretched into a claim that observational research is generally
  untrustworthy, that the affected findings were dishonest, or that the treatments studied do not work,
  since the source literature presents this as a correctable design fault and the play stages it exactly
  as one.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per element, one per load-bearing vector, no filler.

- **The Waiting**: carries the span that must be survived before exposure can occur (Suissa), the organ
  becoming available, the prescription being filled, the award being won, a stretch in which death is
  impossible by construction rather than by luck, since a death inside it would have placed the subject
  in the other column.
- **The One Column**: carries the record's shape, one field per person and not one per stretch of a
  person's life, so a subject who was untreated and then treated is filed under a single heading and the
  waiting goes wherever the person goes.
- **The Gift**: carries the transfer itself, the immortal span credited to the treated arm, so the
  comparison runs between a group whose person-time contains a period in which nobody could die and a
  group whose person-time does not.
- **The Finding**: carries the result (Sylvestre, Huszti and Hanley), a survival advantage exact in
  every arithmetic step, reproducible by anyone who repeats the calculation, and absent from the world,
  which is why checking the work does not find it.
- **The Landmark**: carries the remedy that costs (Lévesque, Hanley, Kezouh and Suissa), a fixed date
  that removes the bias by discarding every event before it, which is the early and highest-hazard
  period, and whose placement is arbitrary, so one chosen after the curves are seen restores the
  selection it was meant to remove.
- **The Boundary**: carries the condition and the hedge, that the trap needs exposure defined by an
  event occurring after entry, that it needs no confounder or collider and survives randomisation and
  perfect measurement, and that it lifts where time is counted rather than people, at a price in
  evidence and legibility that has to be paid rather than argued away.
