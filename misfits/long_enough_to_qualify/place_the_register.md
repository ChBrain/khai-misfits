# Place: The Register

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). Where what happened is written down, one line per patient and one field for what became of them. Hosts [One Line Per Patient](plot_one_line_per_patient.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A complete and accurate record with no gaps and no guesses, which would pass any audit without a note.

## Holds

[The One Column](piece_the_one_column.md), a field that holds a person and not a period.

## Offers

To everyone downstream, a true account of who was treated and when, which is exactly what a register exists to provide.

## Withheld

Any representation of a life that passed through two states, so the months before the treatment travel into the treated column with the patient, entered by nobody as such and available to nobody as such.
