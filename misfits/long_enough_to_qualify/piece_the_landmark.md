# Piece: The Landmark

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). The cheaper correction: start the clock at a fixed date, keep whoever is alive at it, and count from there.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Reanalysis](place_the_reanalysis.md), offered honestly and with its price attached rather than recommended plainly (Lévesque, Hanley, Kezouh and Suissa).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the play has only the costless-seeming fix, counting time instead of people, and the misfit resolves into a lesson about doing the analysis properly.
It bears the load as the no-free-hero. Because it removes the bias by discarding every event before the landmark date, and that stretch is the early, highest-hazard period the question most needs, the simple correction buys its validity with the evidence that mattered most; and because the date has no correct value, a landmark placed after the curves have been seen readmits by the back door the selection it was brought in to close.

## Apparent

Rigour, and a tidier design: a clean start, a defined risk set, and a method that can be stated in one sentence to people who found the time-varying version hard to follow.

## Yearbook

Stands over the close of [Count the Time Instead](plot_count_the_time_instead.md), where the correction arrives as a genuine loss rather than a rescue.
It works together with [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the two of them marking what the fix costs and where the trap does not reach.
