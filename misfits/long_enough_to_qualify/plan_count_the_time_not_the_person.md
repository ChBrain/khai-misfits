# Plan: Count the Time, Not the Person

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the reviser's seat](position_reviser.md), inside [Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Reviser](position_reviser.md)

## Direction

Ask what the follow-up time in each arm is made of, which is the one question the design does not invite. Then break each subject into untreated months before the treatment and treated months after it, so the waiting is counted where it was lived and the advantage goes where it belongs. Do not present this as an exposure of bad work: the study was done properly and the arithmetic was exact. Set out the landmark alternative honestly, including that it removes the bias by discarding every event before a fixed date, that the discarded stretch is the early and highest-hazard one, that the date has no correct value, and that a landmark chosen after the curves are seen readmits the selection it was brought in to close. Then say plainly which analysis you would carry into a clinic, and why that is a harder sentence to write than the wrong one was.

## Targets

- [ ] the question about person-time is asked, having been asked nowhere else
- [ ] each subject contributes untreated time until the treatment and treated time after it
- [ ] the correction is offered without any accusation, the study having been properly done
- [ ] the landmark alternative is stated with what it discards, rather than recommended plainly
- [ ] the cost is named: the right analysis is the harder one to act on, and that loss is real
