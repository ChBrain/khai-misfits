# Piece: The Waiting

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). The span between entering the study and receiving the treatment: months a patient had to survive in order to be counted as treated at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Operating at [The Ward](place_the_ward.md), accruing while everyone does the right thing, since treating at the first possible moment is already the intent (Suissa).

## Load Bearing

Remove it, by making the treatment available on the day of entry, and the trap does not exist: there is no interval to misfile and the two arms are comparable from the first day.
It bears the load as the source of the whole effect. Because a patient who died inside the span would never have been recorded as treated and would sit among the untreated, the span cannot contain a death by construction rather than by luck, so it is not merely a period of good fortune but a period in which the outcome was impossible.

## Apparent

A queue, and nothing more: an unavoidable delay that everyone regrets and nobody can shorten, which is how it is experienced by every person in the ward.
