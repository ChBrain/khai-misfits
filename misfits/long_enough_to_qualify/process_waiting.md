# Process: Waiting

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). Borne at [The Ward](place_the_ward.md), precipitated by [The Physician](persona_the_physician.md) treating at the first moment the treatment can be given, in [You Had to Live to Get It](plot_you_had_to_live_to_get_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An exposure that has to be waited for: the organ becoming available, the indication declaring itself, the prescription becoming right to write.

## Direction

From cohort entry toward the moment of treatment, accruing a span that the patient must survive in order to be counted as treated at all.

## Lever

That the span cannot contain a death by construction rather than by luck, since a patient who died inside it would never have been recorded as treated and would sit among the untreated (Suissa).

## Echo

[The Waiting](piece_the_waiting.md), an interval of guaranteed survival produced by the one practice nobody would change, treating as soon as it is possible to treat.
