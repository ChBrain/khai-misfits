# Place: The Ward

## Taxonomy

[Long Enough to Qualify](play_long_enough_to_qualify.md). Where the treatment is given at the first moment it can be given, and where the interval before it accrues. Hosts [You Had to Live to Get It](plot_you_had_to_live_to_get_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A patient waiting for something that is not yet available, and a physician who will give it the day it is.

## Holds

[The Waiting](piece_the_waiting.md), the months that must be survived before the treatment can be received.

## Offers

To the patient, the treatment at the earliest moment it exists to be given, which is the best any ward can do.

## Withheld

Any way of shortening the interval, since treating as soon as possible is already the intent, so the raw material of the artefact is produced by the one practice nobody would change.
