# Piece: The Extra Effort

## Owner

## Place

## Load Bearing

It is real, small, entirely about transmission, and indistinguishable at the point of use from effort the claim caused.
Unfamiliar phonetics take marginally longer to resolve, and the resolution succeeds: the meaning arrives intact and nothing is misheard. That is what makes it so hard to address. It is not a comprehension failure that better articulation would fix, and it is not a judgement about the speaker that goodwill would fix. It is a quantity of work, paid out of the same budget as the work the proposition itself demands, arriving at the same place, with nothing attached saying which part came from where.
