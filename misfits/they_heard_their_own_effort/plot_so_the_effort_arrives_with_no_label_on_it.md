# Plot: So the Effort Arrives With No Label On It

## Taxonomy

[They Heard Their Own Effort](play_they_heard_their_own_effort.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A judgement formed in the moment after hearing.

## Action

[Reading the effort as the claim](process_reading_the_effort_as_the_claim.md): one impression of how readily it landed, converted into a view about whether it is true.

## Stage

Two causes, one sensation, and no instrument for the division.

## Tension

The listener is not ignoring the distinction: the distinction is not present to be ignored.
Effort caused by an implausible proposition and effort caused by unfamiliar phonetics are spent in the same moment, out of the same budget, and arrive as a single impression. There is no itemisation, no residue that says which part came from where, and no amount of attention recovers the split, because the split was never encoded. That is why this cannot be characterised as carelessness. A careful listener is a listener who attends harder to a total that includes the wrong term.
