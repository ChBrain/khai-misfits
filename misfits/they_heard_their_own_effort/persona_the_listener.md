# Persona: The Listener

## Taxonomy

[Listener](position_listener.md). The one using a cue that works nearly everywhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Somebody in the room with a claim to assess, no time to check it, and the ordinary equipment for that.

## Action

Listens, understands, and forms a view.
Weighs how readily the claim sits, which is what anybody does with a claim they cannot verify.

## Shadow

Uses a good heuristic correctly and is wrong in this one place.
The seat is not prejudiced and the evidence is what establishes that rather than the play asserting it: the effect appears where the speaker is visibly reciting somebody else's words, so there is no motive to distrust and no judgement being made about the person. What the seat has is a single sensation of how easily the claim landed, produced by two different causes, and no instrument for splitting it. Abandoning the cue is not an improvement either, because the cue is right nearly everywhere else and there is nothing to put in its place.

## Tell

Judges honestly with a good instrument, in the one place it reads the wrong quantity.
