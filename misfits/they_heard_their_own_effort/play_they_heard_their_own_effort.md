# Play: They Heard Their Own Effort

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

They Heard Their Own Effort.
How easily something is understood is a real cue to whether it is true, and using it is not a mistake: claims that are familiar, coherent and easy to follow are more often correct than claims that are none of those things. An accent adds a small cost to understanding and adds nothing to the claim. The cost arrives with no label saying where it came from, so it is felt as a property of what was said, and a true sentence spoken in a second language is believed less than the same sentence spoken in a first.

## Arc

The bend is this: the difficulty a listener has in processing a sentence is felt as a fact about the sentence, so the speaker who is hardest to follow is believed least, and the listener is not prejudiced.
It begins with a cue that works.
[Ease Is a Truth Cue, and It Usually Works](plot_ease_is_a_truth_cue_and_it_usually_works.md). [The Fluency Cue](piece_the_fluency_cue.md) is a good heuristic and is why nobody has any reason to abandon it.
And an accent costs the listener effort.
[And an Accent Costs the Listener Effort](plot_and_an_accent_costs_the_listener_effort.md). [The Extra Effort](piece_the_extra_effort.md) is real, small, and about the transmission rather than the content.
So the effort arrives with no label on it.
[So the Effort Arrives With No Label On It](plot_so_the_effort_arrives_with_no_label_on_it.md). [The Listener](persona_the_listener.md) experiences one quantity and has no way to see it in two parts.
And [it is not prejudice, which is the hard part](plot_and_it_is_not_prejudice_which_is_the_hard_part.md).
[The Source](persona_the_source.md) wrote the sentence and is a native speaker, so the person saying it is not claiming anything of their own and the effect appears anyway.
Then [the warning helps where it is least needed](plot_the_warning_helps_where_it_is_least_needed.md). [The Warning](piece_the_warning.md) lets a listener correct for a mild accent and not for a heavy one.
And [the true thing is believed less for being said in a second language](plot_so_the_true_thing_is_believed_less.md). [The Speaker](persona_the_speaker.md) said something correct, carefully, in a language learned on purpose.

## Stakes

Whether somebody speaking a language they learned can be believed on the same terms as somebody who did not.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a truth cue that is generally sound, an accent that costs the hearer and not the claim, a difficulty that arrives undivided, an effect that survives the removal of any motive to distrust, a warning that works inversely to the need for it, and a correct statement discounted, and away from any account in which a listener held anything against anybody. The production cannot fault the listener, who is using a heuristic that serves them well nearly everywhere; and it cannot fault the speaker, who said something true in a language they went to the trouble of learning. What is left to fight over is not whether anybody erred but what to call a discount that nobody applied.
