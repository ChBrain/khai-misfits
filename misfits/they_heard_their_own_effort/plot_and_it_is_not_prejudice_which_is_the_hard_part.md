# Plot: And It Is Not Prejudice, Which Is the Hard Part

## Taxonomy

[They Heard Their Own Effort](play_they_heard_their_own_effort.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A speaker reciting a statement written by somebody else.

## Action

[The Source](persona_the_source.md) is the acknowledged author, so the speaker claims nothing and stands to gain nothing, and the discount appears regardless.

## Stage

The removal of every account that runs through distrust of a person.

## Tension

The design that established this took the villain away on purpose, and the effect stayed.
Where the person speaking is openly not the author of the claim, they have no expertise being assessed, nothing at stake in whether it is believed, and no reason anybody could impute to them for saying it. Every explanation that runs through what listeners think of foreigners becomes unavailable at that point. The effect is still there. So it is localised precisely, and not where the ordinary account puts it: not in anybody's view of the speaker, but in what the sentence cost to hear.
