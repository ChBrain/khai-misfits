# Plot: The Warning Helps Where It Is Least Needed

## Taxonomy

[They Heard Their Own Effort](play_they_heard_their_own_effort.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A listener told in advance that accents affect credibility.

## Action

[The Warned Listener](persona_the_warned_listener.md) applies [the warning](piece_the_warning.md) in good faith, adjusting upward.

## Stage

A remedy that succeeds for the mild case and fails for the heavy one.

## Tension

Knowing the mechanism is genuinely worth something and is worth least where it would be worth most.
Given the finding, believing it, and intending to allow for it, a listener does correct where the accent is light. Where the accent is heavy the correction does not hold, and a heavy accent is where the discount is largest. So the remedy runs backwards along the quantity it addresses. This is the honest shape of what is on offer: not a fix that somebody has failed to apply, but an adjustment whose reach is smallest at the point where the effect is greatest.
