# Place: The Meeting

## Taxonomy

[They Heard Their Own Effort](play_they_heard_their_own_effort.md). Where the claim is made and weighed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An ordinary room, an ordinary agenda, several people who know the subject, and claims arriving faster than anybody can check them.
Everything proper: nobody is hostile, nobody is inattentive, and everything said is understood.

## Holds

[The Speaker](persona_the_speaker.md), saying something correct.
[The Listener](persona_the_listener.md), assessing it with the usual equipment.
[The Native Colleague](persona_the_native_colleague.md), who will say something equally correct at no extra cost.

## Offers

A decision made on the best available reading of claims nobody has time to verify.

## Withheld

Any sign that a discount was applied.
The place is where the effect is complete and invisible at the same time. Nothing observable distinguishes a claim that was weighed at the ordinary rate from one that was weighed a little low: there is no gesture, no remark, no moment anybody could point to afterwards. The listener does not experience having discounted anything, because from the inside a claim that landed less readily simply landed less readily. So the room can contain the whole mechanism and hold nobody in it who could report on it.
