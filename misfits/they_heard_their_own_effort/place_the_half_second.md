## Withheld

An itemised bill.
The place is where two costs are added together and stop being two. Some of the effort belongs to the claim, because an implausible or tangled proposition really is harder to take in and really is likelier to be wrong. Some of it belongs to the sound, because unfamiliar phonetics take marginally longer to resolve. Both are spent in the same moment, out of the same budget, and arrive at the same place as a single impression of how readily the thing went down. Nothing in the experience distinguishes them, which is why no amount of attention recovers the split.
