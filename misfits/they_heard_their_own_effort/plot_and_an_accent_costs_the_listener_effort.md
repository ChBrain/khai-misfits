# Plot: And an Accent Costs the Listener Effort

## Taxonomy

[They Heard Their Own Effort](play_they_heard_their_own_effort.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The same sentence, in a second language.

## Action

[The Extra Effort](piece_the_extra_effort.md) is spent at [the half second](place_the_half_second.md): unfamiliar phonetics, resolved successfully and slightly more slowly.

## Stage

A real cost, attached to the transmission and not the content.

## Tension

Nothing goes wrong here, which is exactly the difficulty.
The meaning arrives intact. Nothing is misheard, no word is lost, and the listener could repeat the sentence back. So this is not a communication failure and cannot be met by speaking more clearly, more slowly or with better vocabulary: the seat that would have to improve has already succeeded. What has happened is that a small quantity of work was done, and the work was about the sound rather than about the claim, and the difference between those two facts is not available anywhere in the experience.
