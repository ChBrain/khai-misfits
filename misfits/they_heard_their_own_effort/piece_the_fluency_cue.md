# Piece: The Fluency Cue

## Taxonomy

[The Half Second](place_the_half_second.md). The heuristic that works nearly everywhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Applied at [the half second](place_the_half_second.md), through [reading the effort as the claim](process_reading_the_effort_as_the_claim.md).

## Load Bearing

It is a good instrument, which is why nobody has any reason to put it down.
How readily a claim is taken in tracks, in general, whether it is right: propositions that are coherent, familiar and consistent with what is already known go down easily and are more often true, and ones that are tangled or unprecedented go down slowly and are more often wrong. A listener with no time to verify anything has to weigh claims somehow, and this is a real signal rather than laziness. Discarding it would leave nothing in its place and would make the listener worse nearly everywhere else.

## Apparent

A bias, of the kind a careful person would simply switch off.

## Yearbook

Is a sound cue reading a total, in the one case where the total includes the listener's own hearing.
