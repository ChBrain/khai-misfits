# Persona: The Native Colleague

## Taxonomy

[Native Colleague](position_native_colleague.md). The one who says the same words and is believed more.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Another person in the same room, on the same topic, speaking their first language.

## Action

Says the same sentence.
Is understood at no extra cost, and is believed at the ordinary rate.

## Shadow

Receives a benefit that is invisible from inside and is not a benefit anybody granted.
The seat has no advantage it could decline, disclose or share. Nothing was awarded to it, nothing was withheld from anybody, and it is not aware of receiving anything at all: the sentences simply arrive at the ordinary cost. That is what makes it the comparator rather than a beneficiary in any sense that could be addressed. The gap is measured against this seat and this seat cannot see it, which is why nobody in the room can.

## Tell

Is the baseline, and cannot tell that it is one.
