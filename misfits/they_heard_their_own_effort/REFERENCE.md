# They Heard Their Own Effort: Reference

The research warrant for They Heard Their Own Effort.
The term is the house's; the play runs on accented speech and on what a listener's own processing cost does to a
claim it had nothing to do with, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the fluency heuristic as **a sound instrument reading a total that includes the listener**: how readily a
claim is taken in is a real cue to whether it is right, because propositions that are coherent, familiar and
consistent with what is already known go down easily and are more often correct. Somebody with a decision to make
and no way to verify anything has to weigh claims somehow, and this is a signal rather than a lapse. An accent
adds a small cost to understanding and adds nothing to the content: unfamiliar phonetics take marginally longer
to resolve, and the resolution succeeds, so the meaning arrives intact and nothing is misheard. Both costs are
spent in the same moment out of the same budget and arrive as a single impression of how readily the thing went
down, with nothing attached saying which part came from where. The play models the documented mechanics, the cue
that works nearly everywhere, the effort that belongs to the sound, the sensation that cannot be divided, the
design that removed every account running through distrust of a person, the warning that corrects the mild case
and not the heavy one, and the correct statement discounted, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as
a tale of a listener holding something against anybody.

The governing law is that the difficulty a listener has in processing a sentence is felt as a fact about the
sentence, so the speaker who is hardest to follow is believed least, and the listener is not prejudiced.

**Nobody in the sequence falls short, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The speaker
says something true, clearly, in a language learned on purpose. The listener uses a heuristic that serves them
well nearly everywhere. The source supplied the claim and is acknowledged. The warned listener corrects in good
faith as far as the correction reaches.

What removes any possibility of blame from the speaker is that clarity is not the quantity that is short. The
sentence is correct and is understood in full, so this is not a communication failure and cannot be met by
speaking more slowly, more clearly or with better vocabulary. The seat that would have to improve has already
succeeded, and what costs it is a residue of work in another person's head that has no connection to the content
and is unreachable by anything the seat says.

**And the design that established it took the villain away on purpose, which is the finding rather than the
play's assertion.** In the study, speakers merely recited statements supplied by a native speaker, so the person
talking was openly not the author, had no expertise being assessed, nothing at stake in whether the claim was
believed, and no motive anybody could impute. Every explanation running through what listeners think of
foreigners becomes unavailable at that point. The discount appears anyway, which locates it precisely: not in
anybody's view of the speaker, but in what the sentence cost to hear.

**Then the remedy runs backwards along the quantity it addresses.** Telling listeners that accent affects
credibility, and having them believe it and intend to allow for it, produces a real correction where the accent
is mild. Where the accent is heavy the correction does not hold, and a heavy accent is where the discount is
largest. So knowledge of the mechanism is genuinely valuable and is worth least exactly where it would be worth
most.

It is distinct from the house's **The Illusion of Shared Understanding**, which is the nearest incumbent and
shares a scholar, staging illusory transparency: hearers resolve what an utterance leaves open egocentrically and
speakers overestimate how well their intention was conveyed, so the engine is **comprehension going wrong while
everybody believes it went right**. Remove that engine, by stipulating an utterance that is entirely unambiguous
and is understood exactly as intended, and this misfit stands entire: the sentence here is understood in full,
nothing is misheard, and the discount is produced by the **cost of achieving that understanding** rather than by
any failure of it.

It is distinct from the house's **In Somebody Else's Words**, where a second speaker re-authors the manner a room
reads credibility from, so there are two speakers and the record keeps one. Remove that engine, by stipulating no
intermediary at all, and this stands: here the person speaks their own words in their own voice, there is nobody
between them and the room, and the accent itself does the work.

It is distinct from the house's **Naive Realism**, where construal is taken for plain fact and the disagreement
is about the world. Nothing here is construed differently by anybody: the parties agree entirely about what was
said and what it means, and what differs is a quantity of effort nobody is aware of having spent.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the listener as
  prejudiced, because the evidence rather than the play's preference removes that reading, and because nobody in
  the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the fluency cue is generally valid and there is nothing to replace it
  with; the extra effort is real, small and about the sound; the two costs are spent in one moment and arrive as
  one impression that no amount of attention can itemise; the speaker's sentence is correct and understood, so
  clarity is not what is short; the source is the acknowledged author, which removes every account running
  through distrust; the native colleague received nothing anybody granted and cannot tell it is the baseline; and
  the warned listener corrects in good faith as far as the correction reaches. The failure is authored by nobody
  and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a listener's own effort is charged to the speaker.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Speaking more clearly does not
  help, because the sentence was already understood. Abandoning the fluency cue makes the listener worse nearly
  everywhere else and supplies no substitute. Being told about the effect produces a real correction that holds
  for a mild accent and not a heavy one. Putting the claim in writing removes the accent and removes the speaker
  from the room, which is a different conversation with different costs. Exposure to a voice reduces the cost
  over time and takes time nobody has in a first meeting. And knowing the whole structure, which the field does
  and has documented, leaves a room, a claim, and a decision to take before anybody can check it.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the accent-credibility experiments and the processing-fluency
  literature are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: **how far the result generalises across listeners,
  languages and settings is genuinely contested**, and the play carries that argument rather than settling it;
  the statements used were laboratory trivia items and the play makes no claim about the size of the effect in
  consequential settings, which is not established here; **prejudice against non-native speakers is a real and
  separate phenomenon, and this play neither models it nor denies it**, since the finding it stages is precisely
  the residue that remains when every motive to distrust has been designed out; and the account treats one
  experimental result and a wider literature as one structure, which is its own simplification. **The play makes
  no claim about any person, any group, any hiring, immigration or legal decision, and gives no advice of any
  kind**, only that a listener's own effort in understanding is felt as a fact about what was said.
