# Plot: Ease Is a Truth Cue, and It Usually Works

## Taxonomy

[They Heard Their Own Effort](play_they_heard_their_own_effort.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A claim arriving, and no time to check it.

## Action

[The Fluency Cue](piece_the_fluency_cue.md) does what it always does: how readily the thing went down informs how far it is believed.

## Stage

A sound instrument, in ordinary use.

## Tension

This is the part that is not a mistake, and it is where the mechanism starts.
Claims that are coherent, familiar and consistent with what a listener already knows are taken in easily and are more often correct; claims that are tangled or unprecedented are taken in slowly and are more often wrong. Somebody with a decision to make and no way to verify anything has to weigh claims somehow, and this is a real signal rather than a lapse. Any account that opens by calling the cue a bias has already lost the thing that makes this hard: there is nothing better to use, and using it makes the listener right more often nearly everywhere.
