# Persona: The Source

## Taxonomy

[Source](position_source.md). The one who wrote the sentence and is not in the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A native speaker who supplied the statement, whose authorship is known to everybody present.

## Action

Supplies the claim.
Hands it over to be said by somebody else, and takes no further part.

## Shadow

Exists to remove the last available explanation, and does.
This seat is the reason the play is not about prejudice. When the person speaking is openly not the author, they have nothing to gain from the claim, no expertise being assessed, and no credibility of their own at stake in it. Every account that runs through distrust of the speaker is unavailable at that point. And the discount happens anyway, which localises it precisely: not in what anybody thinks of the speaker, but in what the sentence cost to hear.

## Tell

Removes the villain by being the author.
