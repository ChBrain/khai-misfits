# Persona: The Warned Listener

## Taxonomy

[Warned Listener](position_warned_listener.md). The one who has been told, and can act on it only halfway.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A listener who knows the finding, believes it, and intends to correct for it.

## Action

Discounts the discount.
Adjusts upward for the accent, deliberately, having been told to expect the effect.

## Shadow

Succeeds where the accent is mild and fails where it is heavy.
The seat is doing exactly what the finding recommends, in good faith, and the correction holds for a light accent and does not hold for a strong one. So the remedy is available in inverse proportion to the need for it: where the difficulty is small the seat can allow for it, and where the difficulty is large, which is where the discount is largest, the allowance does not reach. Knowing the mechanism is genuinely worth something and is worth least exactly where it would be worth most.

## Tell

Corrects for the small case and cannot reach the large one.
