## Load Bearing

It works, and it works where it is least required.
Telling listeners that accent affects credibility, and having them believe it and intend to allow for it, produces a real correction where the accent is mild. Where the accent is heavy the correction does not hold, and a heavy accent is where the discount is largest. So knowledge of the mechanism is genuinely valuable and its value runs the wrong way along the thing it addresses: the seats it can rescue were only lightly affected, and the seats most affected are the ones it cannot reach.
