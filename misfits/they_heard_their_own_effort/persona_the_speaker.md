# Persona: The Speaker

## Taxonomy

[Speaker](position_speaker.md). The one saying something true in a language they learned.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A colleague, expert in the subject, speaking a language acquired deliberately and well, with an accent.

## Action

Says the true thing.
Says it clearly, in order, with the right words, and is understood.

## Shadow

Is understood completely and believed less.
The seat has done nothing available to be done differently. The sentence is correct, the grammar is correct, the vocabulary is correct, and the listener understands it: this is not a failure of communication and cannot be fixed by speaking more clearly, because clarity is not what is short. What costs the seat is a residue of effort in somebody else's head that has no connection to the content and cannot be addressed by anything the seat says. Speaking the first language instead is available only where the room shares it, which is the one condition the seat cannot supply.

## Tell

Is right, is understood, and is discounted for neither.
