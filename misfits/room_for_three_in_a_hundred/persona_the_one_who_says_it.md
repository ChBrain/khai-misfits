# Persona: The One Who Says It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Says It](position_one_who_says_it.md). The defendant who takes the number, and pays for it in words.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person facing a certain small sentence or an uncertain large one, advised competently, often held in custody while deciding, and sometimes innocent.
Ninety-seven of every hundred convictions in the federal system, and ninety-four of every hundred in the states.

## Action

Accepts the number, and then stands up in open court and states that they did it, because the discount is conditional on the admission.
Answers that the plea is voluntary, which it is, in the sense the question means.

## Shadow

Is the instrument of their own erasure, and cannot decline to be without paying the gap.
The only record capable of showing a wrongly convicted person who pleaded is the record this persona completes, correctly and under oath, as the price of the deal: so an innocent plea does not enter the system as a doubtful entry or a marked exception, it enters as a voluntary, counselled, judicially examined admission of guilt, indistinguishable in every particular from a true one.

## Tell

Says the words that close the case and, in the same breath, remove the only evidence that closing it was wrong.
