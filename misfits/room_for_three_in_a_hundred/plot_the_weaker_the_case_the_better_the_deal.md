# Plot: The Weaker the Case, the Better the Deal

## Taxonomy

[Room for Three in a Hundred](play_room_for_three_in_a_hundred.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A charge that might well fail at trial: thin evidence, a shaky identification, a laboratory result nobody has seen yet.

## Action

[Pricing the case](process_pricing_the_case.md): the prosecution values its own prospects honestly and prices accordingly, so the offer drops. [The One Who Counts the Odds](persona_the_one_who_counts_the_odds.md) reads it and finds the arithmetic more favourable than usual.

## Stage

The most attractive number in the building is now sitting in front of the defendant with the strongest defence.

## Tension

The pressure to plead is heaviest exactly where guilt is least certain, and it got there by everyone reasoning correctly.
Pricing a weak case low is not a trick, it is what expected value means, and it is not about the defendant at all: it is the prosecution valuing its own risk. But the effect is a sorting, and the sorting runs the wrong way, drawing the people most likely to be innocent toward the deal with the largest discount and away from the process that could have established it.
