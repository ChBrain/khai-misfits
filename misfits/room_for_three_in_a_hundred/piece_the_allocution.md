## Place

## Load Bearing

It converts the transaction into evidence against the only person who could contradict it.
The discount is conditional not on silence but on admission, so the defendant must state that they did it in order to receive the number: an innocent plea therefore enters the record as a voluntary, counselled, judicially examined confession, and the single document capable of showing the system's error is completed, accurately and under oath, by the person the error was made about.

## Apparent

A short, dignified exchange. Do you understand, is this voluntary, did you do it, yes.
Everything correct, everything on the record, and everything true to the question asked.

## Yearbook

Designed as a protection, and it is one: it stops a defendant pleading to something they do not understand or that never happened at all.
Its factual basis is satisfied by the account the parties agree on, so it tests whether the story hangs together, never whether this person is the one who did it.
