# Persona: The One Who Asked

## Taxonomy

[One Who Asked](position_one_who_asked.md). The defendant who used the right, and paid the undiscounted number.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The rare defendant who declines the offer: fewer than three in a hundred.
In 1978, a man charged over a forged cheque for eighty-eight dollars and thirty cents, offered five years, told plainly that refusal would bring a habitual offender charge.

## Action

Refuses, and goes to trial, which is the thing the constitution names.
Receives the sentence that exists when nothing has been discounted: in that case, life.

## Shadow

Demonstrates the price rather than escaping it.
The Supreme Court held that the threat and its execution were not vindictiveness but the normal give-and-take of plea negotiation, so this outcome is not a scandal that slipped through the net, it is the net, described accurately and upheld: the persona exists in the play to show that the gap is enforceable, published in advance, and lawful.

## Tell

Exercised a right and was sentenced to the difference, which is what makes the right expensive rather than absent.
