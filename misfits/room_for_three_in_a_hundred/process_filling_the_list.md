# Process: Filling the List

## Taxonomy

[Room for Three in a Hundred](play_room_for_three_in_a_hundred.md). Arrivals meeting a capacity that was set elsewhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Charges arriving at a rate nobody in the building controls.
[The Courtrooms](place_the_courtrooms.md), with a fixed number of judges, rooms and hours in a year.

## Direction

Toward a list far longer than the hours available to try it.
Toward roughly three cases in a hundred receiving a trial, and ninety-seven needing some other disposal.

## Lever

Capacity, which is a budget and a building rather than a policy about anybody.

## Echo

A shortfall that never appears as a refusal, because nothing is denied to anyone: it appears only as a number offered in a hallway.
