# Place: The Hallway Outside

## Taxonomy

[Room for Three in a Hundred](play_room_for_three_in_a_hundred.md). Where the case is actually decided, off the record.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A corridor, a cell, a telephone call, a few minutes before the list is called. Counsel, a client, and a number.
No judge, no jury, no transcript, and no gallery.

## Holds

[The Gap Between the Numbers](piece_the_gap_between_the_numbers.md), which decides the outcome.
[The Offer That Expires](piece_the_offer_that_expires.md), often before the evidence has been seen.

## Offers

A resolution today, at a price, with the risk taken out.
Certainty, which is the only commodity anybody here is really trading.

## Withheld

Every protection the building was built to provide.
The proof, the confrontation, the jury and the standard of proof all live inside the courtrooms and none of them operate here, so the decision that determines ninety-seven cases in a hundred is taken in the one part of the courthouse where nothing is tested, and is then carried inside to be ratified by an examination that can only ask whether it was made freely.
