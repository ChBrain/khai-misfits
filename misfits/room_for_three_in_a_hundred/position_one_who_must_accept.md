# Position: One Who Must Accept

## Taxonomy

[Room for Three in a Hundred](play_room_for_three_in_a_hundred.md). The seat that examines the plea and passes it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A duty to ensure the plea is knowing, voluntary and factually supported, and the power to reject one that is not.
A list, and no authority to enlarge the building.

## Orders

Ask whether it is voluntary, whether it is understood, and whether the defendant did it.
Accept the plea when the answers are right.

## Loses

The distinction it exists to protect, to a test that cannot reach it.
Voluntariness in law means free of improper coercion, and a large lawful discount is not improper coercion, so the examination passes a plea made under enormous pressure exactly as it passes a free one: the seat is thoroughly equipped to catch a defective plea and holds no instrument whatever for a valid plea that is false.

## Drives

Toward a properly conducted examination, correctly recorded, in which every question asked was answered truthfully and the one that mattered was not among them.
