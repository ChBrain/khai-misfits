# Piece: The Offer That Expires

## Taxonomy

[The Hallway Outside](place_the_hallway_outside.md). The deadline that makes the decision before the evidence arrives.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In [the hallway outside](place_the_hallway_outside.md), early, frequently before disclosure is complete and often while the defendant is held.

## Load Bearing

It removes the one resource that could have separated the innocent from the guilty, which is time.
An offer good only today is answered on what is known today, so the defendant chooses before the laboratory reports, the disclosure or the witness statements exist: in one documented run of drug cases a hundred and fifty-seven people pleaded guilty to possessing substances that later testing showed were not drugs at all, each of them having decided while the answer was still in a queue.

## Apparent

An ordinary term of business, and a real one: an offer priced for early resolution genuinely is worth less later, because the saving it buys is smaller.
Nothing about it is a trick, and nothing about it is hidden.

## Yearbook

Sharpened by custody, since a defendant held before trial can be offered a sentence shorter than the wait itself, which makes pleading the fastest way out of a cell for a person with nothing to admit.
Entirely lawful, and unreviewable, because the charging and offering decisions behind it are the prosecutor's own.
