# Persona: The One Who Offers

## Taxonomy

[One Who Offers](position_one_who_offers.md). The prosecutor, holding a caseload no courtroom can absorb.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A public official with a list far longer than the hours available to try it, and the authority to resolve most of it by agreement.
Charged with disposing of the whole list, not with trying a representative sample of it.

## Action

Prices each case and puts a number on the table, discounted against what a trial would produce.
Prices the weak ones lower, because a case that might fail is worth less and has to settle cheaper.

## Shadow

Is doing the design rather than evading it.
The Supreme Court has said plainly that this bargaining is an essential component of the administration of justice, and that trying every charge would require multiplying the number of judges and court facilities many times over, so the discount is not a corner being cut: it is the mechanism the system is built on, operated by an official who did not choose the caseload, did not set the sentencing ranges, and cannot conjure a courtroom.

## Tell

Offers most generously in the cases the evidence supports least, which is rational, lawful, and points the pressure at exactly the wrong people.
