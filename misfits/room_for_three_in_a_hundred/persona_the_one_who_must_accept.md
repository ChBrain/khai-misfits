# Persona: The One Who Must Accept

## Taxonomy

[One Who Must Accept](position_one_who_must_accept.md). The judge, examining a plea that satisfies every test.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A judge with a list, a duty to ensure that any plea is knowing, voluntary and supported by a factual basis, and no power to conjure a courtroom.
The one participant who could refuse, and the one with the least ground to.

## Action

Asks the questions: is this voluntary, do you understand, did you do it.
Receives correct answers to all three, and accepts the plea, which is the proper handling of a plea that is knowing, voluntary and factually supported.

## Shadow

Has every test satisfied and no test for the thing that matters.
Voluntariness in law means free of improper coercion, and a large lawful discount is not improper coercion, so a plea taken under enormous pressure passes the examination exactly as a plea taken freely does: the bench is equipped to detect a defective plea and has no instrument at all for a valid plea that is false.

## Tell

Conducts the examination properly, records that everything was in order, and is right about every question that was asked.
