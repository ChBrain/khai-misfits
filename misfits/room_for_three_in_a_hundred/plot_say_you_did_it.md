## Stage

## Tension

The only record that could show the error is completed, under oath, by the person the error is about.
An innocent plea does not enter the system flagged, doubted or marked as exceptional: it enters as a voluntary, counselled, judicially examined confession, indistinguishable in every particular from a true one. Voluntariness in law means free of improper coercion, and a large lawful discount is not improper coercion, so the examination passes it exactly as it passes a free one. The bench is thoroughly equipped to catch a defective plea and holds no instrument at all for a valid plea that is false.
