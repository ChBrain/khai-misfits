# Persona: The One Who Counts the Odds

## Taxonomy

[One Who Counts the Odds](position_one_who_counts_the_odds.md). Defence counsel, with an arithmetic that only goes one way.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A competent lawyer, often carrying more clients than hours, holding an offer on one side and an exposure on the other.
Fully aware of what the trial penalty is and fully unable to make it smaller.

## Action

Multiplies the exposure by the odds, compares it against the certain number, and advises accordingly.
Recommends the certain thing when the certain thing is lower, which is nearly always.

## Shadow

Cannot prefer the principle without spending the client's years on it.
Two certain years against a fifteen per cent chance of twenty is not a close question of arithmetic, and counsel who steered a client past a good offer on a matter of principle would be the one found ineffective: so the advice that empties the courtrooms is not a failure of the defence function, it is the defence function performed correctly.

## Tell

Gives the advice that competence requires, and it is the same advice the throughput requires, every time.
