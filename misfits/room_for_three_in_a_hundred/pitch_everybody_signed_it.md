# Pitch: Everybody Signed It

## Taxonomy

[Room for Three in a Hundred](play_room_for_three_in_a_hundred.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Administrative and entirely lawful. An offer made openly, competent advice given and taken, a plea entered by a defendant who confirms in open court that it is voluntary and that they did it, a judge satisfied, a list disposed of on time.
Every signature real, every form complete, every step endorsed by the highest court that has looked at it.

## Undertow

Under the paperwork, a number that came from the estate department: the size of the inducement needed to clear a list the buildings cannot try, applied identically to people who did it and people who did not.
And the discount improving as the case weakens, so the sweetest offer in the building lies in front of the person with the best defence.

## Nerve

The vertigo of a record that is complete, accurate, voluntary and signed by everyone, and cannot tell you the one thing you would want to know.

## Echo

Afterward: a hundred and fifty-seven people who pleaded guilty to possessing substances that later testing showed were not drugs at all, each admission spoken in open court, each plea voluntary, each file closed correctly.
