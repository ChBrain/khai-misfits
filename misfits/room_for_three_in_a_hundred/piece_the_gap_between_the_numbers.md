# Piece: The Gap Between the Numbers

## Taxonomy

[The Hallway Outside](place_the_hallway_outside.md). The distance between the offer and the sentence after trial.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

On the table in [the hallway outside](place_the_hallway_outside.md), in every case, quoted as two figures and understood as one.

## Load Bearing

It is the whole mechanism, and its size is a logistics number.
The gap has to be large enough to move ninety-seven cases in a hundred, which is a fact about hours and rooms rather than about anybody's conduct: at the federal level it runs at roughly three times the plea sentence and sometimes eight or ten times, and nothing in that ratio was derived from culpability, deterrence or desert.

## Apparent

Two ordinary sentencing figures, both lawful, neither of them secret.
A discount for saving the court the trouble, described in exactly those terms.

## Yearbook

Grew as sentencing ranges lengthened and mandatory minimums arrived, since the gap is measured against the maximum exposure rather than the expected one.
Applied identically to the guilty and the innocent, because nothing in it is capable of distinguishing them.
