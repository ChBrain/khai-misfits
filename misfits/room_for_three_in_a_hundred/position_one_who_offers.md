# Position: One Who Offers

## Taxonomy

[Room for Three in a Hundred](play_room_for_three_in_a_hundred.md). The seat that must clear a list the buildings cannot try.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A caseload set by arrests rather than by capacity, and the authority to resolve most of it by agreement.
Sentencing ranges written by somebody else, and a courtroom allocation written by somebody else again.

## Orders

Dispose of the list.
Discount against what a trial would produce, and discount further where the case is weaker.

## Loses

Nothing it can identify, which is the difficulty.
The seat did not choose the caseload, did not set the ranges, cannot build a courtroom, and is operating the mechanism the Supreme Court has called an essential component of the administration of justice: so a seat behaving impeccably, within a design endorsed at the highest level, produces the pressure without anywhere along the way making a decision that could be called wrong.

## Drives

Toward a cleared list at a price, with the price highest for the people whose cases were weakest.
