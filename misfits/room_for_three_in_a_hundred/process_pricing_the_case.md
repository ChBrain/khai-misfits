# Process: Pricing the Case

## Taxonomy

[Room for Three in a Hundred](play_room_for_three_in_a_hundred.md). The discount set from the odds, and set deepest where the evidence is thinnest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A list that must clear, and a case whose prospects at trial can be estimated.
[The One Who Offers](persona_the_one_who_offers.md), holding [One Who Offers](position_one_who_offers.md).

## Direction

Toward a number below what a trial would produce, large enough to be taken.
Toward a lower number still where the case is weak, because a case that might fail is worth less and must settle cheaper.

## Lever

Expected value, applied to a prosecution's own prospects, which is ordinary rational conduct and is not about the defendant at all.

## Echo

The most attractive offer in the building sitting in front of the defendant with the strongest defence, arrived at without a single improper step.
