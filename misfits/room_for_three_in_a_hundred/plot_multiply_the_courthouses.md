# Plot: Multiply the Courthouses

## Taxonomy

[Room for Three in a Hundred](play_room_for_three_in_a_hundred.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The structure understood at last, and the answers on the table: close the gap, cap it, police the offers, or build and staff enough courtrooms to try what arrives.

## Action

[Try them all](plan_try_them_all.md): [The One Who Must Accept](persona_the_one_who_must_accept.md) reaches for the only lever the bench holds, and finds that each version of it is paid for by somebody who is not in the room.

## Stage

The gap is what clears the list, so shrinking it stops the list clearing, and the Court has already priced that in judges and court facilities multiplied many times over.

## Tension

Every correction lands on a party the correction was not aimed at.
Capping the discount moves the pressure into the charging decision, where almost nothing reviews it. A judge who polices the offer becomes a party to the bargain they must afterwards examine as voluntary. And funding the trials requires a sum somebody must vote for, while every case that goes to trial spends the time of victims and witnesses that the plea was sparing them and lengthens the wait of everyone still held before their own. There is no version in which the capacity number is paid by the people who set it.
