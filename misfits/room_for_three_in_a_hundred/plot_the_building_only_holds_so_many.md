# Plot: The Building Only Holds So Many

## Taxonomy

[Room for Three in a Hundred](play_room_for_three_in_a_hundred.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A courthouse with a fixed number of rooms, judges and hours in the year, and charges arriving at a rate set by something entirely outside it.

## Action

[Filling the list](process_filling_the_list.md): [The Courtrooms](place_the_courtrooms.md) offer the full apparatus, jury and proof and confrontation, to anybody who asks, while the list grows past anything those hours can absorb.

## Stage

Roughly three cases in a hundred can be tried. The other ninety-seven must be disposed of by something that is not a trial.

## Tension

The shortfall never appears as a refusal, so it never appears as anything that could be appealed.
Nothing is denied to anyone and nothing needs to be: the right stays open on demand, unrationed on its face, genuinely available to every defendant individually and impossible for all of them at once. So the constraint does not present as a closed door, it presents as a number offered in a hallway, and a number is not a thing anyone can be said to have been refused.
