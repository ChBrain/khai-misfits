# Position: One Who Counts the Odds

## Taxonomy

[Room for Three in a Hundred](play_room_for_three_in_a_hundred.md). The seat whose arithmetic only ever points one way.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An offer, an exposure, a probability, and a duty to one client.
Frequently a caseload of its own, and a client in custody while the clock runs.

## Orders

Multiply the exposure by the odds.
Compare, advise, and recommend the lower number.

## Loses

Any standing to prefer the principle.
Two certain years against a fifteen per cent chance of twenty is not a close question, and counsel who talked a client past a good offer to make a point would be the one later found ineffective: so the seat that might in principle resist the throughput is professionally required to serve it, and does so competently, in case after case.

## Drives

Toward advice that is correct on the arithmetic and identical, every time, to what the capacity constraint needs.
