# Plan: Wait for the Ground to Turn

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the emerger's seat](position_emerger.md), inside [Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md).

## Owner

## Direction

Stay under until the soil in contact with the body reaches the temperature at which flight works. Do not shave the threshold, because the cost of being wrong is not a poor season but a dead queen and a colony that never existed.

## Orders

([The Queen](persona_the_queen.md) waits while the soil sum climbs, emerges at her threshold, and forages from that moment on whatever bloom remains, having no way to learn beforehand what remains.)
