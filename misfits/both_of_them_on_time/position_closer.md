# Position: Closer

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The seat overhead whose leafing ends the window on a schedule of its own, making the bloomer's early threshold correct and patience impossible.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An accumulation of its own, on its own timetable, ending in leaf.
Command of the light reaching the floor, entire, from the moment it closes.
No coordination with anything below and no capacity for any.

## Orders

Leaf out on the warmth it has taken in.
Take the light.
Do this identically in the year the meeting held and the year it did not.

## Loses

Nothing, and it takes something: it is not harmed by the mismatch and it is what makes the mismatch unrecoverable, since a flower that could wait would simply wait.
Because it is the reason the early threshold is right, it cannot be removed from the account as an aggravating factor: it is the same fact, read at the other end.
It ends every season having closed on time, which is the only thing it does.

## Drives

Toward a canopy closed on its own accumulated warmth,
indifferent in every year to what was or was not pollinated beneath it,
so the seat that justifies the flower's timing is the same seat that forecloses the flower's only answer to it.
