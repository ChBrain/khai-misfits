# Position: Emerger

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The seat that waits on soil warmth, correctly for its own survival, with no sense of the bloom and no margin to shave.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A threshold in soil temperature below which flight is not survivable.
A whole colony held in one body, so that the cost of emerging early is total rather than marginal.
No sense organ for the surface, for the air, or for what is flowering above.

## Orders

Wait while the soil sum climbs.
Come up when the ground reaches the temperature at which the body works.
Forage from that moment at the ordinary rate, on whatever bloom remains.

## Loses

The early bloom, in the years the surface ran ahead of the soil, and with it the forage a founding colony is built on.
Because the threshold is a survival limit and not a preference, there is nothing to trade: coming up sooner does not cost less, it costs everything.
It ends those seasons correct about its own body and short of the resource, having done the identical right thing it does every year.

## Drives

Toward an emergence timed exactly to when flight becomes possible,
right in every single year to wait as long as it waits,
so the seat that is never reckless is the seat that arrives to a wood already going over.
