# Piece: The Long Series

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). Two decades of the same variables in the same wood, plus a patch where the cover is cleared by hand, which together are the only instrument that can see the thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the quadrat](place_the_quadrat.md), accumulating one row a year.
Answered nowhere faster: there is no shortcut, because the signal being resolved is a week wide and the noise it sits in is a whole spring.

## Load Bearing

Without it the mechanism is unobservable and the wood simply has good years and bad ones.
It is load bearing because the misfit is not merely hard to fix but hard to see: it takes nineteen springs to separate the gap from weather, and a deliberate clearing of the snow to turn a correlation across decades into a cause demonstrated in one season.

## Apparent

Patience, or stubbornness, or an ordinary long study of no particular urgency.
Nothing about keeping a count looks like the decisive instrument in the play, and it is, because every other position in the wood is structurally blind to the quantity that matters.

## Yearbook

Built by returning to [the quadrat](place_the_quadrat.md) through every [melting](process_melting.md), ordinary and early alike.
It resolves [the gap](piece_the_gap.md) against noise, ties it to [the seed count](piece_the_seed_count.md), and ends by naming a mechanism that implicates [the flower](persona_the_flower.md) in nothing and [the queen](persona_the_queen.md) in nothing.
