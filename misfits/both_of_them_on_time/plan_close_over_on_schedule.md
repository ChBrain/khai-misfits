# Plan: Close Over on Schedule

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the closer's seat](position_closer.md), inside [Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Closer](position_closer.md)

## Direction

Leaf out on accumulated warmth and take the light, on a timetable of the canopy's own, in every year alike. Do not adjust for what is happening on the floor, because there is no mechanism by which the floor could be consulted.

## Orders

([The Canopy](persona_the_canopy.md) leafs out on its own accumulation and shades the floor for the rest of the year, ending the window in the good years and the bad ones identically.)

## Implementation

([The Forest Floor](place_the_forest_floor.md), darkened from above; the [warming](process_warming.md) that drives the canopy's own leafing; the hard end it sets on the season in which [the seed count](piece_the_seed_count.md) can still be filled.)

## Targets

- [ ] the canopy leafs out on its own accumulated warmth
- [ ] the light on the floor ends on that date and not on any other
- [ ] the window closes whether or not anything came to the flowers
- [ ] waiting is thereby unavailable to the bloomer as a strategy at all
