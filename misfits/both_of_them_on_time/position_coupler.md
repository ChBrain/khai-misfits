# Position: Coupler

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The seat that suppressed both signals and released them together, doing the coordinating work that nothing in either lineage was doing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An even cover over surface and soil alike, holding both accumulations at zero while it lies.
A single release moment, shared by both, on the day it goes.
No relationship to either party: it is weather on ground, and it is not for anything.

## Orders

Lie, and hold both clocks stopped.
Go, and start both clocks from the same instant.
Go early over cold ground, and start one of them far ahead of the other.

## Loses

Nothing, because it holds nothing and is owed nothing: it is the one seat in the play with no stake in the outcome.
What is lost when it goes early is lost by the other two, and neither of them can see that it was ever the reason they agreed.
It ends the season having been the whole of the mechanism and no part of the account.

## Drives

Toward simply being weather,
never once acting on behalf of the pairing it happened to hold together,
so the seat that was never anybody's guarantee is the one whose loss nothing was built to survive.
