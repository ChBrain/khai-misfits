# Place: The Quadrat

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). Marked ground returned to every spring for two decades, and a patch beside it where the cover is cleared by hand, so the mismatch can be forced rather than waited for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Pegged corners in the same wood, revisited each year: melt date, temperatures above and below, first flower, first bee, fruits opened and seeds counted.
Beside it, a plot where the snow is shovelled off early on purpose, and a plot left alone as its control.

## Holds

The only instrument in the play capable of seeing the thing at all, because a week's gap in one spring is indistinguishable from weather and only becomes a mechanism across twenty of them.
It also holds the manipulation, which converts a correlation observed over decades into a cause demonstrated in a season.

## Offers

Resolution, bought with time: enough years that the noise averages out, plus one cleared patch that makes the early melt happen on demand instead of waiting for a warm decade to supply it.

## Withheld

Anything to do about it.
The quadrat can establish exactly what happens and exactly why, and it cannot produce a party in the wood who could have acted otherwise, so the better the measurement gets the more completely it confirms that nobody erred.
