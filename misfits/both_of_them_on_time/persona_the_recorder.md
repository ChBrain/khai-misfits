# Persona: The Recorder

## Taxonomy

[Witness](position_witness.md). Keeps the count across two decades and clears snow off a patch on purpose, because the gap cannot be told from weather in any single year.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Methodical, slow, and constitutionally unwilling to conclude anything from one season.
Returns to the same marked ground every spring for years: melt date, warmth above, warmth below, first flower, first bee, seed per fruit.
No thesis being defended, only a series being extended.

## Action

Records the same variables in the same place year after year, through good springs and bad, accumulating the only instrument that can see a seven day gap against the noise of weather.
Shovels the snow off a marked patch to bring the early melt on deliberately, and counts what follows against the ground left alone.
Publishes the mechanism: one sum above, one sum below, and a gap that predicts the seed.

## Shadow

Not detached, and with nothing to do about it: the finding is clean, the mechanism is named, and it names no party who could act differently.
So the record's whole achievement is to establish that nobody in the wood made an error, which is exactly the result that leaves nothing to recommend.
It converts an invisible loss into a measured one, and a measured loss with no author is still a loss.

## Tell

Says: one year is weather, nineteen is a mechanism.
Says it in a table of melt dates and seed counts, and offers no advice at the end of it, because there is nobody in the table to advise.
