# Plan: Hold Them Both Under

## Taxonomy

A **mandate**: the intent of [the coupler's seat](position_coupler.md), inside [Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Coupler](position_coupler.md)

## Direction

Lie evenly over surface and soil, holding both accumulations at zero, and go in one moment. Do not coordinate anything on purpose, because nothing here is on purpose: the coordination is a side effect of being one object lying over two things.

## Orders

([The Cover](persona_the_cover.md) suppresses both sums while it lies, releases both at the moment it goes, and does so without reference to either party or to the pairing it is holding together.)

## Implementation

([The Forest Floor](place_the_forest_floor.md) and [the soil column](place_the_soil_column.md) beneath it, held together by one object; the [melting](process_melting.md) process ending the suppression; the release starting [warming](process_warming.md) above and [conducting](process_conducting.md) below at rates that are no longer the same.)

## Targets

- [ ] the cover lies evenly and both accumulations stand at zero
- [ ] the release is one event, shared by the surface and the soil
- [ ] an early release over cold ground starts the two at different rates
- [ ] nothing in the wood registers that a coupling has been removed
