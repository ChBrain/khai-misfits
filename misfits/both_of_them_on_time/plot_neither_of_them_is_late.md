# Plot: Neither of Them Is Late

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Surface Sum](piece_the_surface_sum.md) reaching the figure at which this plant has always opened.
Four centimetres down, [the threshold](piece_the_threshold.md) short of the temperature at which this queen has always emerged.

## Action

[Flowering](process_flowering.md): [The Flower](persona_the_flower.md), holding [the bloomer's seat](position_bloomer.md), opens at its threshold across the patch, while [The Queen](persona_the_queen.md), holding [the emerger's seat](position_emerger.md), stays underground because [conducting](process_conducting.md) has not yet brought the soil to hers.

## Stage

[The Flower](persona_the_flower.md), on [Open on the Warmth I Can Feel](plan_open_on_the_warmth_i_can_feel.md), opens at exactly the sum it has always opened at, having no sense by which a bee could be anticipated and no margin, since [The Canopy](persona_the_canopy.md) on [Close Over on Schedule](plan_close_over_on_schedule.md) will take the light on its own date regardless.
[The Queen](persona_the_queen.md), on [Wait for the Ground to Turn](plan_wait_for_the_ground_to_turn.md), holds under, correctly, because the ground still says that flying would kill her.

## Tension

Both of them are on time, and the wood is empty.
This is the whole misfit standing still for a moment: two thresholds, both reached exactly when they should be, in two places that no longer agree. Neither party is capable of the other's reading, and that is not a shortcoming either could have made up: a queen at depth cannot sense the air, a shoot at the surface cannot sense the soil below, and nothing in either lineage was ever selected to watch the other, because for the whole of their shared history [the cover](persona_the_cover.md) did that work for nothing. There is no schedule here that anyone is late for. There was never a schedule, only two clocks that used to agree.
