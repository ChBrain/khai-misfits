# Process: Conducting

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). Heat working downward from the surface into the soil, slowly, and reaching the overwintering queen weeks after it reached the flower.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A warm surface over cold ground.
At [the soil column](place_the_soil_column.md), heat beginning to move down from the bared surface toward the depth the queen lies at.

## Direction

Toward the queen's threshold, slowly.
Soil conducts heat far more slowly than a bare surface absorbs it, so the sum at depth climbs behind the sum above, and the size of the shortfall depends on how cold the ground was when the cover left it.
This is the misfit's engine in motion: the same warmth, arriving at two depths at two different times, with a creature keyed to each.

## Lever

The thermal properties of soil, which are fixed, and the state of the ground at the melt, which the winter sets.
Because neither is adjustable by anything in the play, the delay is not a friction anybody could reduce: it is what a few centimetres of earth does to heat.

## Echo

A queen still underground while the wood is in flower.
From the soil's side nothing is late: the sum is where the sum is, and the threshold is where it has always been, and the only thing that has changed is a quantity in a different place.
