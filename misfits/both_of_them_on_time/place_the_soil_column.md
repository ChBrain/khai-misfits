# Place: The Soil Column

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The few centimetres between the surface and the overwintering queen, across which heat travels slowly, and which is the whole of the distance between the two clocks.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The ground immediately below the floor, from the surface down to where a queen lies through the winter.
Cold through the melt and for weeks after it, warming from the top downward at the pace conduction allows.

## Holds

The queen's threshold, the soil temperature at which a body her size can fly, and therefore the entire basis of her timing.
It also holds the mechanism itself: the delay between warmth arriving at the surface and warmth reaching the depth she is at is the misfit, expressed as a distance.

## Offers

An honest reading of the queen's own survival prospects, which is precisely what she needs and precisely what it is for.
It answers the only question she has to get right, and answers it correctly every year.

## Withheld

Any signal from above.
The column cannot report the surface it is under, so a queen reading it perfectly learns nothing about whether the wood above her is in bloom, and no depth exists at which she could read both.
