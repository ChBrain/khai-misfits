# Play: Both of Them on Time

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Both of Them on Time.
A flower opens when the ground it stands on has taken in enough warmth, which is the only signal it has and the right one, because it must be finished before the trees overhead close and take the light. A bee waits underground until the soil around her reaches the temperature at which a body her size can fly, which is the only signal she has and the right one, because coming up into cold is how a queen dies with her whole colony still inside her. For as long as anyone has counted, those two readings arrived together, not because either creature was watching the other, and not because anything in either lineage was keeping the appointment, but because one blanket of snow lay over both signals and lifted off them at the same moment. Take the snow away three weeks early and the bare surface warms in days while the soil beneath it is still cold, and the flower opens into an empty wood. Neither of them is late. There was never a time to be late for.

## Arc

The bend is this: the meeting was never held by anything, so when the two signals part there is nothing that notices and nothing that could correct it.
It runs on a synchrony that is a by-product and was mistaken, by everybody including the two parties, for a relationship.
The flower reads accumulated warmth at the surface where it stands. The queen reads accumulated warmth in the soil where she lies. Both readings are correct, both are the only reading available from where that body is, and neither creature can take the other's: a queen four centimetres down cannot feel the air, and a shoot at the surface cannot feel the soil at depth. That is not a limitation either could work around. It is where they are.
So the synchrony was never an agreement, never a signal passed between them, never anything either lineage was selected to maintain. It was two accurate instruments reading two quantities that happened to move together, because the same snow held both of them down and released both at once.
Then the snow goes early, over ground that is still cold. The surface bares and starts counting warmth at once. The soil, insulated by nothing now but its own mass, takes weeks to catch up. The two sums come apart, and each creature acts on its own, correctly, at a different time. The flowers open. The wood stays empty. Seed set falls by half at a gap of a single week, and the trees close overhead on their own schedule, so there is no waiting it out: the window shuts whether or not anything came.
Look for the party who got it wrong and there is nobody in the frame. The flower cannot delay, because the canopy is coming. The queen cannot come up, because the ground says it would kill her. The snow did not decide to leave. And the difference is invisible from inside a single spring: one bad year is weather, and it takes a record two decades long, and a patch of snow shovelled off on purpose, before the gap can be told from the noise at all.
Reach for the obvious fix and each one turns in your hand. Adaptation is what both of them are already doing, perfectly, and doing it is what carries them apart. Selection could move a cue, and it moves slower than the thing moving the cues. Pollinate the plants by hand and you have proved the limitation and fed nobody's bees. Nothing here is broken enough to repair, which is the difficulty: two correct clocks, and no clock keeping them together.

## Triggers

**[The Snow Holds Both](plot_the_snow_holds_both.md)**
Winter, and nothing is running. The Cover lies over the whole floor, and under it the surface takes in no warmth and the soil takes in no warmth, so both clocks are stopped at once by the same object. Closes when the melt begins and the two of them start again together, as they always have.

**[The Melt Comes Early](plot_the_melt_comes_early.md)**
Cue: a warm few weeks, and the cover is gone three weeks before its date, off ground that has not thawed. The bared surface begins counting warmth immediately. The soil below begins far slower, insulated by its own depth. Closes when the two sums have visibly parted and nothing in the wood has registered that anything happened.

**[Neither of Them Is Late](plot_neither_of_them_is_late.md)**
Cue: the surface sum reaches the figure at which this plant has always flowered. It flowers. Four centimetres down the soil is short of the temperature at which this queen has always emerged, so she stays where she is. Both of them are exactly on time by the only clock either has. Closes when the flowers are open and the ground is still shut.

**[Nothing Comes to the Flowers](plot_nothing_comes_to_the_flowers.md)**
Cue: open flowers, and an empty wood. The queen comes up a week later to a bloom already going over, the seed count falls to about half what a matched year returns, and the canopy closes overhead on its own schedule, which was never negotiable. Closes when the season's seed is counted and the window is shut.

**[Nineteen Years to See It](plot_nineteen_years_to_see_it.md)**
Cue: a single bad count, which means nothing, because one spring is weather. The Recorder has the series instead, and a patch where the snow was cleared away on purpose to force the thing to happen on demand. The mechanism comes out clean: one sum above, one sum below, and a gap between them that predicts the seed. Closes when the finding is written down and there is nobody it can be handed to.

## Stakes

Whether a synchrony that nothing was ever maintaining can survive the thing that incidentally maintained it.
Every plot moves the same question forward: not which of the two got it wrong, because neither did and neither could, but whether the word _mismatch_ is even about them. The production cannot put the clocks back together, because there is no mechanism between them to repair: the flower has no way to sense the soil at depth, the queen has no way to sense the air, and nothing in either lineage was ever selected to watch the other, because for the whole of their shared history the snow did that work for free. It can only show that both readings stay correct the entire time, that the harm is measured in seed and in worker bees and not in anybody's error, that the record has to be twenty years deep before the gap is even visible, and that the fix everybody reaches for first, adapt to the conditions, is the exact thing both parties are already doing flawlessly and the exact thing carrying them apart.
