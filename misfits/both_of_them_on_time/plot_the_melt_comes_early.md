# Plot: The Melt Comes Early

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A warm few weeks, and [the cover](persona_the_cover.md) gone three weeks ahead of its date, off ground that has not thawed.
Bare earth in full sunlight, with cold soil a few centimetres beneath it.

## Action

[Melting](process_melting.md) completes early and releases both clocks together, as it always does. Then [warming](process_warming.md) begins at [the forest floor](place_the_forest_floor.md) at the rate bare ground in sun allows, while [conducting](process_conducting.md) begins at [the soil column](place_the_soil_column.md) at the rate earth allows, which is far slower.

## Stage

[The Cover](persona_the_cover.md), having completed [Hold Them Both Under](plan_hold_them_both_under.md), goes, and in going starts two accumulations that will not keep pace.
[The Surface Sum](piece_the_surface_sum.md) climbs fast on exposed ground. [The Threshold](piece_the_threshold.md) waits on heat that has to travel down through cold earth to reach it.

## Tension

The release is identical to every other year, and its consequences are not.
There is nothing anomalous to see: bare ground in March looks like bare ground, and both parties are accumulating exactly as they always accumulate. What has changed is that [the lag](piece_the_lag.md), which in an ordinary spring is a few days and matters to nobody, has opened wide, because the cover came off ground that was still frozen. Two quantities that have moved together for the whole history of the pairing have just come apart, and there is no position in the wood from which that is visible.
