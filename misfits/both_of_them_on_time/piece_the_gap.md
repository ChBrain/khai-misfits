# Piece: The Gap

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The days between the flower opening and the queen emerging, which nothing in the wood measures and which predicts the season's seed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Between [the forest floor](place_the_forest_floor.md) and [the soil column](place_the_soil_column.md), and observed only from [the quadrat](place_the_quadrat.md).
Answered by neither party: the flower cannot perceive it and the queen cannot perceive it, so it exists as a quantity only for the one who is counting.

## Load Bearing

Without it the two arrivals coincide and there is no misfit, only a partnership that was never a partnership.
It is load bearing because it is the misfit's single measurable term, the thing that turns two individually correct timings into one outcome, and because it is invisible from every position in the play except the observer's.

## Apparent

A near miss, a slightly early spring, the sort of variation any wood has always had.
A week is nothing to look at, and a week is where the seed halves, so the quantity that carries the whole harm is also the one least likely to be noticed by anybody standing in it.

## Yearbook

Opened by [the lag](piece_the_lag.md) after an early [melting](process_melting.md).
It is what [the long series](piece_the_long_series.md) resolves against weather, what the cleared patch at [the quadrat](place_the_quadrat.md) forces on demand, and what finally explains [the seed count](piece_the_seed_count.md).
