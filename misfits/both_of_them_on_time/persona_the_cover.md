# Persona: The Cover

## Taxonomy

[Coupler](position_coupler.md). The winter snow, which suppressed both signals at once and released both at once, and so did for free the coordinating that nothing else was doing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Uniform, indifferent, and quietly load bearing.
Lies over the whole floor at an even depth, insulating surface and soil alike from whatever the air is doing above it.
Nothing in it is directed at either party: it is weather, sitting on ground.

## Action

Holds both clocks stopped for as long as it lies, because under snow neither the surface nor the soil accumulates the warmth either party is counting.
Lifts, and starts both of them again from the same moment.
Lifts early, over cold ground, and starts one of them far ahead of the other.

## Shadow

Not a mechanism, and doing a mechanism's work: for the whole history of the pairing it was the only thing making the two arrivals coincide, and nothing in the wood knew that, including the two arrivals.
So its going is not a disturbance to a relationship, it is the removal of the entire basis of one, and there is no residue underneath to keep working.
It was never anybody's guarantee, which is exactly why nobody noticed losing it.

## Tell

Says nothing and is spoken to by no one.
Its presence looks like winter and its absence looks like spring, and neither reading contains any information about what it was holding together.
