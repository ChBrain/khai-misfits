# Plot: The Snow Holds Both

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Forest Floor](place_the_forest_floor.md) and [the soil column](place_the_soil_column.md) beneath it, both under an even winter cover.
Nothing accumulating anywhere: the surface takes in no warmth and the soil takes in no warmth, and both clocks stand at zero because one object is lying on both of them.

## Action

[Melting](process_melting.md) has not begun. [The Cover](persona_the_cover.md), holding [the coupler's seat](position_coupler.md), suppresses both accumulations at once, while [The Flower](persona_the_flower.md) in [the bloomer's seat](position_bloomer.md) waits under litter and [The Queen](persona_the_queen.md) in [the emerger's seat](position_emerger.md) waits four centimetres down.

## Stage

[The Cover](persona_the_cover.md), on [Hold Them Both Under](plan_hold_them_both_under.md), lies evenly and does the whole of the coordinating work without being for anything.
[The Flower](persona_the_flower.md), on [Open on the Warmth I Can Feel](plan_open_on_the_warmth_i_can_feel.md), and [The Queen](persona_the_queen.md), on [Wait for the Ground to Turn](plan_wait_for_the_ground_to_turn.md), each hold at a threshold neither can reach yet, for reasons that have nothing to do with each other.

## Tension

The pairing looks like a partnership and there is no partnership here to look at.
Nothing passes between the two of them, in this season or in any other: no signal, no cue about the other, no trait in either lineage shaped by the other's timing. What makes them arrive together is [the cover](persona_the_cover.md), one object suppressing two quantities, and neither creature has any way to know that. The synchrony everybody would call a relationship is, at this moment, a fact about snow.
