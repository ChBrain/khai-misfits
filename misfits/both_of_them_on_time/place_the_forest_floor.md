# Place: The Forest Floor

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The few centimetres of ground and air where the flower stands, bared by the melt and warming fast, and shaded out by the canopy on its own date.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A patch of deciduous woodland in the weeks between the snow going and the trees leafing out.
Bare ground, full light, and a shoot coming up through litter, with the whole of its year's light available and a hard end on it.

## Holds

The surface accumulation, which is the only quantity the flower can read, and the entire window in which anything on the floor can flower, be pollinated, and fill seed.
It holds both halves of the flower's bind at once: the reason its early threshold is correct, and the reason waiting is not available.

## Offers

Warmth, quickly, the moment the cover comes off it, because bare ground in sunlight takes in heat far faster than ground under snow.
That speed is exactly what it offers and exactly what parts it from the soil beneath.

## Withheld

Any signal from below.
The floor carries no information about the temperature four centimetres down, so a plant reading the floor perfectly learns nothing whatever about whether the queen can fly, and there is no version of the floor that would tell it.
