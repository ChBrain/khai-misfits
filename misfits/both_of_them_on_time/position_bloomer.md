# Position: Bloomer

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The seat that opens on surface warmth, correctly for its own season, with no sense of the pollinator and no option to wait.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A threshold in accumulated surface warmth, reached or not reached.
An optimum that is genuinely its own: finishing before the canopy takes the light.
No sense organ of any kind for the state of the soil at depth, or for the presence of a bee.

## Orders

Sum the warmth at the surface from the day the cover leaves.
Open when the sum reaches the figure.
Fill what seed can be filled, and die back when the light goes, whichever way the season went.

## Loses

Seed, in the years its opening lands ahead of the emergence, at roughly half the rate a matched year returns.
Because it has no way to perceive the bee and no way to hold on past the canopy, it cannot spend anything to avoid this: the loss is taken by a seat that had no move available.
It ends those seasons correct about its own optimum and short of offspring, and the two facts do not contradict.

## Drives

Toward an opening timed exactly to the light it needs,
right in every single year to open when it opens,
so the seat that never mistimes anything is the seat the shortfall is finally counted in.
