# Process: Flowering

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The plant opening at its surface threshold, into whatever wood exists at that moment, with the canopy's date already fixed behind it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A surface sum reaching the figure the plant has always opened at.
At [the forest floor](place_the_forest_floor.md), the flowers opening across the patch.

## Direction

Toward seed, if anything comes.
The opening is unconditional on visitors, because there is no sense by which visitors could be anticipated and no state in which holding back would help: the canopy's date is fixed and the light is finite.
So the flower commits the whole of its reproductive season at a moment chosen entirely by its own correct reading.

## Lever

The threshold itself, which is a real optimum given the canopy and is not a preference the plant could relax.
Because the deadline overhead is genuine, the early threshold is right, and the rightness is exactly what removes any margin for arriving with the bees instead.

## Echo

Either a normal season or a very poor one, decided by something the flowering itself never touched.
The two look identical on the day of opening, and the difference appears weeks later, in the count of seed, which is the first moment anything in the wood registers that a coupling failed.
