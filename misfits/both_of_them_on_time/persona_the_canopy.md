# Persona: The Canopy

## Taxonomy

[Closer](position_closer.md). The trees overhead, whose leafing out ends the flower's light on a schedule of its own and so removes the option of waiting.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Overhead, seasonal, and utterly uninterested.
Comes into leaf on its own accumulated warmth, in its own time, closing the forest floor into shade for the rest of the year.
Not opposed to anything below it: simply on a schedule that was never coordinated with one.

## Action

Leafs out, and takes the light.
Sets a hard end to the window in which anything on the floor can photosynthesise, flower, or fill seed.
Does this in the year the bees came and the year they did not, without reference to either.

## Shadow

Not hostile, and structurally decisive: it is what makes the flower's early threshold correct in the first place, and it is what makes patience unavailable as a strategy.
So the same fact that justifies the flower's timing forecloses the flower's only possible response to being early, and both halves of that come from one indifferent thing overhead.
It is the reason there is no waiting it out, and it never enters the scene as an actor at all.

## Tell

Says nothing.
Closes over on its own date, and the floor beneath it is dark by midsummer whether the season below went well or badly.
