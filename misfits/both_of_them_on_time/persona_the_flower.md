# Persona: The Flower

## Taxonomy

[Bloomer](position_bloomer.md). Opens on accumulated warmth at the surface it stands in, which is the only quantity it can sense and the correct one for its own season.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Patient, precise, entirely local.
Reads one thing: how much warmth has passed through the few centimetres of ground and air it occupies since the snow came off it.
No improvidence and no impatience in it, only a threshold reached or not reached.

## Action

Sums the warmth at the surface from the day the cover leaves, and opens when the sum reaches the figure it has always opened at.
Holds nothing back for a pollinator it has no way to detect, because there is no sense by which it could detect one and no state in which waiting would help.
Sets seed if something comes, and sets far less if nothing does, and dies back either way when the light goes.

## Shadow

Not eager, and not capable of any other setting: its threshold is a real optimum, since a plant on a forest floor has to be finished before the trees leaf out and take the light.
So the very accuracy that makes it right about its own season is what carries it away from the bee, and it cannot trade one for the other, because the canopy is not negotiable and the bee is not perceivable.
It is on time and it is alone, and both of those are the same reading.

## Tell

Says, in effect, nothing: there is no bee it consults before opening.
Opens exactly when its sum says to, in the year the bee is there and in the year it is not, and the two openings are indistinguishable from the inside.
