# Process: Melting

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The cover leaving the ground, which ends the suppression of both accumulations at once and starts them both from the same instant.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Warm air over lying snow.
At [the forest floor](place_the_forest_floor.md), the cover thinning and going, in its ordinary week or three weeks before it.

## Direction

Toward bare ground, and toward two clocks restarted together.
While the cover lies, neither the surface nor the soil takes in the warmth either party counts, so the melt is the single event that releases both, and it releases them at the same moment in every year.
This is the coupling in motion: not a mechanism between the two creatures, but one object lifting off both of them.

## Lever

The date the cover goes, which is set by the winter's weather and by nothing in the wood.
Because that date is the shared zero for both accumulations, everything downstream is decided by when it falls and by what state the ground is in when it does, neither of which anything in the play can influence.

## Echo

Two accumulations beginning at once, and then running at different speeds.
An early melt over cold ground looks, on the day, exactly like an ordinary melt: bare earth and sunlight, with nothing to indicate that the two things just released are no longer going to arrive together.
