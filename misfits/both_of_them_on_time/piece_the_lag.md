# Piece: The Lag

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The delay between warmth arriving at the surface and warmth reaching the depth the queen lies at, physical and not decisional, and the reason two correct readings come apart.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the soil column](place_the_soil_column.md), spanning the few centimetres between the two clocks.
Answered nowhere: no party can shorten it, because it is what earth does to heat and not a distance anything could close.

## Load Bearing

Without it the two sums climb together, the two thresholds are reached together, and the pairing holds with no mechanism between the parties at all, which is exactly how it has always held.
With it the same warmth arrives twice at two different times, and each creature acts on its own arrival, correctly. It is load bearing because it is the governing law itself made concrete (Kudo; Cooper): the synchrony was a coincidence of two correlated quantities, and this is the thing that decorrelates them.

## Apparent

Basic physics, unremarkable, the plain fact that soil warms slower than the air above it.
Nothing about it looks like a mechanism of harm, and in every ordinary spring it is not one, because the cover comes off warm ground and the shortfall never opens far enough to matter.

## Yearbook

Opened wide by [melting](process_melting.md) early over cold ground, then held open by [conducting](process_conducting.md).
It is what turns [the surface sum](piece_the_surface_sum.md) and [the threshold](piece_the_threshold.md) from two names for one date into two dates, and what [the long series](piece_the_long_series.md) had to be twenty years deep to see.
