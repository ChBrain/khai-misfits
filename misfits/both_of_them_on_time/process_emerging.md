# Process: Emerging

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The queen coming up at her soil threshold, at the ordinary rate, into a bloom that is either beginning or already going over.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A soil sum reaching the temperature at which flight is survivable.
At [the soil column](place_the_soil_column.md), the queen coming up through the ground she wintered in.

## Direction

Toward founding a colony on what the first weeks provide.
The emergence is unconditional on the bloom, because the soil carries no information about the surface and the threshold is a survival limit rather than a preference: coming up sooner is not a worse trade, it is a fatal one.
So the queen commits her colony's founding at a moment chosen entirely by her own correct reading.

## Lever

The temperature at which her body works, which is fixed by her size and physiology.
Because it is a hard limit and not a margin, there is nothing here to optimise: every year she waits exactly as long as she must, including the year that waiting costs her the early bloom.

## Echo

Either a founding on full forage or a founding on what is left.
From underground the two are indistinguishable in advance, and the queen who arrives to a spent wood has performed the identical correct action as the one who arrives to a full one.
