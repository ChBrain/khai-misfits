# Position: Witness

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The seat that keeps the series long enough, and forces the melt on a marked patch, to separate a mechanism from a run of bad weather.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A marked ground returned to every spring, and the discipline to return.
A series long enough that a seven day gap clears the year to year noise.
A patch where the cover can be cleared on purpose, so the early melt happens on demand rather than being waited for.

## Orders

Record melt date, warmth above, warmth below, first flower, first bee and seed per fruit, in the same place, every year, for as long as it takes.
Clear the snow from a marked patch and count what follows against ground left alone.
Publish the mechanism, and the size of the gap that predicts the seed.

## Loses

Any recommendation, which is the seat's own particular loss: the cleaner the finding, the more completely it establishes that no party in the wood made an error.
Because the mechanism names two correct readings and one absent coupler, there is nobody in it who could be advised to act differently.
It ends with a measured loss and no author, which is a result and not an answer.

## Drives

Toward a series long enough to be believed,
right to refuse every conclusion a single season offers,
so the seat that finally makes the thing visible is the seat that finds there is nothing in it to address.
