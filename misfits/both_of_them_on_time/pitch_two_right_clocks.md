# Pitch: Two Right Clocks

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact, unhurried, instrumental.
The register of a notebook kept in the same wood for twenty springs: the date the snow went, the running sum of warmth above and below, the date of first flower, the date of first bee, the count of seed.
No urgency anywhere in the telling, because nothing in the scene is in a hurry and nothing in it is aware.

## Undertow

Under the measurements, a meeting that was never an arrangement.
Every year the two of them arrive together and it reads as partnership, as something evolved and held: and it was only ever one blanket of snow lying over two separate instruments, lifting off both at once.
The synchrony and its failure are not two states. They are the same absence of a mechanism, seen in a year when the coincidence held and a year when it did not.

## Nerve

The unease of watching two correct readings produce a wrong outcome,
each party doing precisely the thing that has always been right for it,
and no third thing anywhere in the wood whose business the meeting ever was.

## Echo

After the run: a season's seed counted at half, a queen who lost nothing by waiting and would have died by not waiting,
a flower that could not have held on past the canopy whatever it sensed,
and a record long enough to prove that the two of them were never keeping an appointment, only sharing a roof.
