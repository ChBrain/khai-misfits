# Piece: The Seed Count

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). Seed set per fruit, running near three in five when the two arrivals are close and near three in ten at a gap of a week, which is where the loss finally appears.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the quadrat](place_the_quadrat.md), counted weeks after the flowering, once the fruits have filled or failed to.
Held nowhere else: it is the only place in the play where the mismatch becomes a number.

## Load Bearing

Without it the whole structure is an interesting coincidence of dates and nobody can say it costs anything.
It is load bearing because it converts two correct readings into a measured harm, and because the size of the drop, roughly a halving at a single week, is what rules out reading the gap as ordinary variation.

## Apparent

A poor year, of the kind every wood has.
On its own it is entirely consistent with weather, drought, a cold snap or a bad crop, and it takes the date of the melt beside it before it stops looking like luck.
