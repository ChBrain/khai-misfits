# Piece: The Threshold

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The soil temperature at which the queen can fly, which she reads, correctly, and which carries no information about the wood above.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the soil column](place_the_soil_column.md), a few centimetres down, reached weeks after the surface has warmed.
Answered nowhere: no depth in the column reports the surface, so there is no better place for her to have wintered.

## Load Bearing

Without it the queen has no timing and does not survive her first cold emergence, and with it she has a timing that is right about her own body and silent about the bloom.
It is load bearing because it is the other half of the misfit: a second accurate instrument, correctly read, in a place from which the first cannot be seen.

## Apparent

Simple caution, a creature waiting for the ground to warm.
Seen from below it looks like nothing but prudence, and the truth that this prudence is unconditional, and rightly unconditional, is what makes it impossible to trade against a bloom she cannot perceive.

It triggers [emerging](process_emerging.md), stands weeks behind [the surface sum](piece_the_surface_sum.md) once [the lag](piece_the_lag.md) opens, and is the lower term in [the gap](piece_the_gap.md).
