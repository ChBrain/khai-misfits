# Plot: Nineteen Years to See It

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

One poor count, which means nothing, because a single spring is weather.
[The Quadrat](place_the_quadrat.md), pegged in the same wood, with a row added every year for two decades, and a patch beside it where the snow is cleared by hand.

## Action

[The Recorder](persona_the_recorder.md), holding [the witness's seat](position_witness.md), extends [the long series](piece_the_long_series.md) through ordinary springs and early ones alike, and forces [melting](process_melting.md) on the cleared patch to bring the early melt on demand rather than waiting for a warm decade to supply one.

## Stage

[The Recorder](persona_the_recorder.md), on [Keep the Count Long Enough](plan_keep_the_count_long_enough.md), refuses every conclusion a single season offers and accumulates instead, until [the gap](piece_the_gap.md) clears the year to year noise and predicts [the seed count](piece_the_seed_count.md).
The cleared patch returns what the series predicted, which turns a correlation observed across decades into a cause demonstrated in one.

## Tension

The finding is clean, and it names nobody.
This is the last thing the production can do and it is not a remedy: the mechanism comes out as one sum above, one sum below, and a lag between them that no party can perceive or shorten. Adaptation is not the missing piece, because both parties are adapting perfectly and that is what carries them apart. Selection could move a cue and moves slower than the thing moving the cues. A hand with a paintbrush proves the pollen limitation and feeds no bees. And knowing the whole structure, which the field now does and has published, leaves a wood where the snow will go early again next year, two creatures who will each read their own instrument correctly, and nobody at all to tell.
