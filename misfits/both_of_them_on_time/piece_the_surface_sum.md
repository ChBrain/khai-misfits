# Piece: The Surface Sum

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). The accumulated warmth at the bared surface, which the flower reads, correctly, and which carries no information about the soil below.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the forest floor](place_the_forest_floor.md), climbing from the day [the cover](persona_the_cover.md) goes.
Read nowhere else: no other party in the play consults it, and the flower consults nothing else.

## Load Bearing

Without it the flower has no timing at all, and with it the flower has a timing that is right about its own season and silent about the bee.
It is load bearing because it is one half of the misfit made concrete: an accurate instrument, correctly read, measuring a quantity that used to stand in for another and no longer does.

## Apparent

Ordinary spring warmth, the plainest thing in the wood.
Seen from the surface, its climb looks the same in a year when the bees arrive with the bloom and a year when they do not, and the fact that it has come loose from the sum below stays hidden behind how normal a warm spring looks while it is happening.

## Yearbook

Driven by [warming](process_warming.md) on bare ground, started by [melting](process_melting.md).
It is what triggers [flowering](process_flowering.md), what parts from [the threshold](piece_the_threshold.md) once [the lag](piece_the_lag.md) opens, and the upper term in [the gap](piece_the_gap.md) that finally predicts [the seed count](piece_the_seed_count.md).
