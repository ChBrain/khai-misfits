# Process: Warming

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md). Accumulated warmth at the bared surface, climbing fast in sun and driving both the flower's threshold and the canopy's own leafing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Bare ground in spring sunlight.
At [the forest floor](place_the_forest_floor.md), the surface taking in heat from the moment the cover is off it.

## Direction

Toward the flower's threshold, quickly.
Bare ground in full light accumulates warmth at a rate that has nothing to do with how cold the mass beneath it is, so the surface sum climbs on its own terms from the day of the melt.
The same accumulation, read by the trees, is what will later close the canopy: one quantity, driving the flower's opening and the flower's deadline both.

## Lever

Insolation on exposed ground, which is fast and which no party sets.
Because the rate depends on the surface being bare rather than on the soil being thawed, an early melt buys the flower a full speed climb over ground that is still frozen a few centimetres down.

## Echo

A flower opening on schedule.
Read from the surface, the year looks entirely normal right up to the opening, because every quantity the flower can sense is behaving exactly as it does in a year when the bees are there.
