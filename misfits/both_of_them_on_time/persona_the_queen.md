# Persona: The Queen

## Taxonomy

[Emerger](position_emerger.md). Emerges when the soil around her reaches the temperature a body her size can fly at, which is the only quantity she can sense and the correct one for staying alive.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Still, buried, conservative in the exact degree the situation warrants.
Reads one thing: the temperature of the soil in immediate contact with her, a few centimetres down, where she has spent the winter.
Nothing hesitant about it: a threshold that has kept queens alive for as long as there have been queens.

## Action

Waits underground while the soil sum climbs, and comes up when it reaches the temperature at which flight is possible and survivable.
Forages from the moment she is up, at the ordinary rate, on whatever is in bloom.
Founds a colony on what she can gather in her first weeks, and founds a smaller one, or none, when the early bloom is already over.

## Shadow

Not sluggish, correctly cautious: a queen who comes up into cold does not merely suffer, she is lost, and the whole colony inside her with her.
So the threshold is not a margin she could shave, and the soil is not a proxy she chose for the flowers, it is the direct measure of whether her own body will work.
She is right to wait every single time she waits, including the year that waiting costs her the bloom.

## Tell

Says, in effect, nothing: there is no flower she consults before emerging.
Comes up exactly when the ground says she can, to a full wood in one year and a spent one in another, having done the identical correct thing in both.
