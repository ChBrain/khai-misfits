# Both of Them on Time: Reference

The research warrant for Both of Them on Time.
The term is the house's; the play runs on plant and pollinator phenology and on what happens when two correct clocks
stop agreeing, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the spring meeting as **a coincidence that everybody mistook for a relationship**: a spring ephemeral opens
when accumulated warmth at the surface reaches the figure it has always opened at, which is the correct reading for a
plant that must finish before the canopy closes and takes the light. An overwintered bumble bee queen emerges when the
soil around her reaches the temperature at which a body her size can fly, which is the correct reading for an insect
whose alternative is dying with a whole colony inside her. Those two readings arrived together for as long as anybody
counted, not because either creature was watching the other and not because anything in either lineage was maintaining
it, but because one blanket of snow lay over both signals and lifted off both at once. The play models the documented
mechanics, the surface sum the plant can sense, the soil threshold the queen can sense, the cover that suppressed and
released both together, the lag that opens when the melt comes early over cold ground, the canopy that forecloses
waiting, and the record that has to be two decades deep before any of it is visible, as the load-bearing vectors of the
structure, not as a tale of a species that failed to keep up.

The governing law is that the synchrony was a by-product of two correlated quantities rather than a mechanism between
two parties, so when the quantities part there is nothing that notices and nothing that could correct it.

**Nobody in the sequence falls short, and that is what puts the failure outside anybody's conduct.** The plant reads
the only signal available where it stands. The queen reads the only signal available where she lies. The snow does not
decide to leave. The canopy closes on its own accumulation. The observer needs nineteen springs to see it.

What removes any possibility of blame from either party is that **neither is capable of the other's reading, and that
is a fact about where they are rather than a shortcoming either could make up.** A queen a few centimetres down cannot
sense the air; a shoot at the surface cannot sense the soil at depth. There is no better place for either of them to
have been, and no version of either signal that carries information about the other. Nothing in either lineage was ever
selected to watch the other, because for the whole of their shared history the snow did that work for free.

What removes it from the plant in particular is that its early threshold is a real optimum and not a preference. A
plant on a deciduous forest floor has to flower, be pollinated and fill seed before the trees leaf out, so the same
fact that makes its timing correct is the fact that makes waiting unavailable. **The argument for patience is an
argument against the light**, and there is no season in which holding back would help.

What removes it from the queen is that her threshold is a survival limit rather than a margin. Emerging into cold is
not a worse trade for a founding queen, it is a fatal one, so there is nothing here to optimise: she waits exactly as
long as she must, in the year the wood is full and the year it is already going over.

**And the mechanism is measured rather than asserted, which is where the structure becomes concrete.** Flowering onset
follows an accumulated surface degree-day model after snowmelt, while queen emergence follows an accumulated soil
degree-day model to a threshold near six degrees. The mismatch is generated when snowmelt is early **but the
subsequent soil warming is slow**, because bare ground in sunlight takes in heat far faster than that heat conducts
downward through cold earth. Seed set under natural pollination ran near 60 per cent where the mismatch was small and
fell to around 30 per cent at a gap of seven days.

**Then the loss is not the plant's alone.** Worker population dynamics in four bumble bee species answer the same
divergence from the other side, in a system where one flowering community tracks early-season air temperature and
another tracks snowmelt: the mutualism is staged here because both parties are hurt and neither can be charged.

**The literature does contest how general this is in mutualisms, and the play does not dispute that.** The prediction
is met most clearly in antagonistic interactions at high latitudes, with limited evidence in mutualistic ones, and
across 27 pairs of interacting species the changes had no consistent direction, 31 interactions moving closer and 23
moving further apart. That finding is not a refutation of what is staged here and is closer to its premise: **a
synchrony held by an agreement would not drift in either direction.** One that is an incidental consequence of two
organisms reading two correlated quantities drifts whichever way the correlation happens to break, which is what the
absence of a mechanism looks like from outside.

It is distinct from the house's **The Lie of the Land**, which is the nearest incumbent and stages the ecological
trap, where a cue that once predicted habitat quality no longer does and the organism is drawn to a place that will
not support it. Remove that engine entirely, by stipulating that every cue perfectly predicts the thing it is a cue
for, and this misfit stands whole: **both cues here remain perfectly accurate about their own referent** throughout,
since surface warmth still predicts the plant's own season and soil warmth still predicts whether the queen's body will
work. Nobody is misled about anything. What has moved is the relation between two referents, and neither organism was
ever reading a cue about the other in the first place.

It is distinct from the house's **More Mouths Than Meat**, which stages the predator-prey lag, where two stocks govern
each other through a delay that carries both past the turn they should have made. Remove that engine, by stipulating an
instantaneous response, and this stands: **neither party responds to the other at all**, with lag or without, so there
is no coupling here for a delay to spoil. Nor does it oscillate. It is a one-way divergence under an external trend,
and next spring is a fresh draw rather than the far side of a cycle.

It is distinct from the house's **A Hundred Years to Vouch for a Thousand**, which stages the calibration window, where
a relationship established over one stretch of record is assumed to hold outside it. Remove that engine, by stipulating
that nothing is ever extrapolated beyond the range it was calibrated on, and this stands: **nothing here estimates
anything.** There is no reconstruction, no inference and no knower. The flower is not using surface warmth as a proxy
for the bee's emergence, and could not be, because it has no representation of a bee at all.

It is distinct from the house's **True Until It Was Used**, which stages the Lucas critique, where an estimated
relation stops holding because policy is set on it. Remove that engine, by stipulating that nobody acts on the relation
at all, and this stands: the correlation between surface and soil warming breaks because the climate moved, **not
because anything was done with it.** No party in the wood knows the relation exists.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast either party as having
  failed to keep up, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the plant reads accumulated warmth at
  the surface, which is the only quantity it can sense and the right one for a plant that must finish before the
  canopy takes the light; the queen reads soil temperature, which is the only quantity she can sense and a survival
  limit rather than a margin she could shave; neither is capable of the other's reading, because a queen at depth
  cannot sense the air and a shoot at the surface cannot sense the soil below, and no place exists from which either
  could read both; nothing in either lineage was selected to watch the other, because the snow did that work for
  nothing; the snow does not decide when to leave; and the canopy closes on its own accumulation, which is the same
  fact that makes the plant's early threshold correct and the fact that forecloses waiting. The failure is authored by
  nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a meeting nothing was maintaining does not survive the
  loss of the thing that incidentally maintained it.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Adapting to conditions is what both
  parties are already doing, accurately, and doing it is what carries them apart, so the remedy everybody reaches for
  first is the mechanism of the harm. Evolutionary adjustment of a cue is possible and runs slower than the trend
  moving the cues. Hand pollination demonstrates the pollen limitation cleanly and scales to nothing and feeds no
  bees. Generalist pollination networks buffer a community and not a specialist pairing, and the specialists are where
  the risk concentrates. Planting or moving populations addresses where the parties are and not when they arrive.
  And knowing the whole structure, which the field now does and has published, leaves a wood where the snow will go
  early again next spring, two creatures who will each read their own instrument correctly, and nobody at all to tell.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed as the
  house's. The staging is the house's; the phenology monitoring, the snow-removal experiment, the yardstick argument,
  the specimen-record analysis and the trophic-mismatch synthesis are theirs. The bounds are carried with them:
  **whether phenological mismatch is general in mutualisms is genuinely contested**, the evidence is clearest in
  antagonistic interactions and limited in mutualistic ones, and across a set of interacting pairs the shifts have run
  in both directions, so the play carries that argument rather than settling it; the degree-day models, the six-degree
  threshold and the seed-set figures come from particular systems in particular places and are offered as the
  documented shape of the mechanism rather than as constants; a spring ephemeral under snow at high latitude is an
  unusually clean case and the account treats it as the structure rather than as one end of a range; and phenological
  shifts have consumer-resource and competitive forms as well as mutualistic ones, only the mutualistic form being
  staged here. **The play makes no claim about any particular species, population, site or projection, and gives no
  ecological, conservation or land-management advice of any kind**, only that the two of them arrive by separate
  clocks, and the thing that used to hold those clocks together was never keeping anybody's appointment.
