# Plot: Nothing Comes to the Flowers

## Taxonomy

[Both of Them on Time](play_both_of_them_on_time.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Open flowers across the patch, and no bees in the wood.
[The Gap](piece_the_gap.md), some days wide, measured by nobody standing in it.

## Action

[Flowering](process_flowering.md) proceeds unpollinated while [emerging](process_emerging.md) waits on the soil. A week on, [The Queen](persona_the_queen.md) comes up to a bloom already going over, and forages at the ordinary rate on what is left, while [The Canopy](persona_the_canopy.md) in [the closer's seat](position_closer.md) begins to leaf out on its own accumulation.

## Stage

[The Queen](persona_the_queen.md), completing [Wait for the Ground to Turn](plan_wait_for_the_ground_to_turn.md), emerges the moment the ground permits and founds her colony on the forage that remains, which is less.
[The Flower](persona_the_flower.md), having committed the whole of its season under [Open on the Warmth I Can Feel](plan_open_on_the_warmth_i_can_feel.md), fills [the seed count](piece_the_seed_count.md) at roughly half what a matched year returns, inside a window [The Canopy](persona_the_canopy.md) closes on schedule.

## Tension

The harm arrives, and there is nowhere to send it.
Both parties are hurt and neither can be charged: the seed halves at a gap of a single week, the founding queen builds on short forage, and every action either of them took was the correct one taken at the correct moment. [The Canopy](persona_the_canopy.md) closes overhead exactly as it always has, which forecloses the one remedy that would not have required either creature to sense something it cannot sense, and it does so as the same fact that made the flower's early threshold right in the first place. The loss is real, measured in seed and in workers, and it is authored by nobody.
