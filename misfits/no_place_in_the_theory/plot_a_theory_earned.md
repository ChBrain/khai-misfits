# Plot: A Theory Earned

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The Discipline before any anomaly has arrived to test it, its record already long and its predictions already confirmed.

## Action

At [the Discipline](place_the_discipline.md), [the Theory](persona_the_theory.md) from [the theory's seat](position_theory.md) on [Stand Confirmed](plan_stand_confirmed.md) through [Confirming](process_confirming.md) takes up [the Theory](piece_the_theory.md), standing on a record built trial by trial into the field's working account of how things work.

## Stage

The Discipline holds a paradigm earned honestly, through a long accumulation of confirmed prediction, tested and refined by successive trials until trusting it has become, in the great majority of cases still to come, the correct thing to do (Kuhn, The Structure of Scientific Revolutions, 1962). Every prediction that held, every rival account it explained away, added another layer to a record long enough that no single result could reasonably overturn it. The Theory is not dogma clung to out of habit or institutional pride: it stands because it has predicted correctly again and again, and its authority is real, right far more often than wrong across the whole of its record. No villain: the standing is earned, not asserted, and defending a record this well confirmed is the ordinary, warranted confidence of a paradigm that has done its work well, not stubbornness and not fear of the new. What the record does not yet contain, because nothing has yet asked it to, is a place prepared for a result it cannot explain.

## Tension

An authority earned this completely leaves no place ready for what it does not yet explain. The very accumulation that makes the Theory trustworthy is the accumulation with nothing prepared for what falls outside it.
