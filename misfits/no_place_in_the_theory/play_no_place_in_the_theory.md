# Play: No Place in the Theory

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

No Place in the Theory.
A field holds a working theory, built from a long accumulation of evidence and refined by successive tests, and the theory has earned its authority honestly, explaining what its predecessors could not and predicting what experiment later confirmed. It is not dogma held for its own sake; it is a real, hard-won account of how things work, and it is right far more often than it is wrong.
A researcher within the field observes something the theory does not predict and cannot explain, a correlation, an effect, a pattern in the data, real and repeatable under test, but with no mechanism the theory recognizes as legitimate. Ignaz Semmelweis, a physician at the Vienna General Hospital, found that maternity ward mortality from childbed fever fell sharply when doctors washed their hands in a chlorinated solution before examining patients, a striking and repeatable effect. But he could offer no mechanism the medicine of 1847 could accept, since germ theory did not yet exist and the prevailing account of disease gave no reason hand washing should matter, and the practice, and Semmelweis himself, were largely rejected and ridiculed by his medical peers.
The rejection is not malice but ordinary and usually correct inference. An expert who encounters a claim that contradicts a well-supported theory reasonably treats the theory, backed by a great weight of confirmed prediction, as more likely correct than a single surprising result, and this is the right default in the overwhelming majority of cases: most claims that contradict an established theory are themselves mistaken, artifacts of a flawed study or a coincidence read as a pattern, and a field that abandoned its theory at every surprising result would abandon good theories constantly.
The trap is that this correct general policy misfires on the rare occasion when the anomaly is genuine. The very reasoning that protects a field from a flood of false alarms is the reasoning that also delays or buries a true one, since the field cannot tell, from inside the rejection, which kind of anomaly it is looking at. A true finding with no accepted mechanism looks, by the field's own best evidence at the time, exactly like a false one, and there is no reliable, in-the-moment way to sort the rare true anomaly from the far more common false one while the mechanism is still missing.
And here is why no one in it is a villain. The theory the field defends was built honestly and has earned its authority by real predictive success, and each individual expert who doubts the anomalous claim is applying the same sound epistemic practice that ordinarily serves the field well, weighing a surprising result against an established body of confirmed prediction and finding the established body more credible, the correct inference nearly every time it is drawn. No one conspires against the discoverer and no one refuses evidence out of pride or malice; the rejection is authored by no one, the predictable cost of a sound general policy meeting its rare true exception.
And here is the way out, and why it cannot simply be argued into being. The anomaly is eventually accepted once a mechanism is found or enough independent confirmation accumulates past a threshold, Semmelweis's own finding vindicated decades later once germ theory arrived to explain why the chlorinated wash worked. But this remedy takes years or decades, and the discoverer cannot simply argue harder in the meantime, since the case against them is not a failure of argument but a correct weighing of priors that the missing mechanism does not supply. There is no reliable shortcut to the mechanism arriving sooner.
And here is the bound that keeps it honest. This is a documented pattern traced through specific historical cases of genuine anomaly, and not a measured general rate at which theories wrongly reject true findings. The same sound default that occasionally buries a true discovery rightly rejects the great majority of anomalies that really are error, so the play is not a brief for every dissenter against every consensus. The case and its name belong to the historical record of Semmelweis's own discovery and its reception, the framework of paradigms and anomaly resistance is Kuhn's, and the epistemic defense of resisting isolated anomalies is the philosophy and sociology of science literature's.

## Arc

The bend is this: a well-supported theory rightly commands more credence than a single anomalous finding, so a valid discovery that lacks an accepted mechanism is indistinguishable, from inside the field's own best evidence, from a false one, and can be rejected by entirely sound reasoning applied to a true claim.
It runs on a good rule meeting its rare bad case.
A theory stands, earned honestly by a long accumulation of confirmed prediction.
And an anomaly is found. Real and repeatable, and offering no mechanism the theory recognizes as legitimate.
And the anomaly is weighed. The established theory found more credible than the single surprising result, the sound default in the overwhelming majority of cases.
And the anomaly is set aside. Rejected by reasoning that is correct nearly every time it is applied.
And the mechanism eventually arrives. The claim vindicated once germ theory, or its equivalent, supplies what was missing.
And the true scope is held. A documented pattern in genuine anomaly, not a general indictment of a sound and necessary caution.

## Stakes

Whether a field can be trusted to recognize a true discovery, when the very reasoning that protects it from false alarms is the reasoning that can also bury a genuine one.
Every plot but the last moves the same way: toward the rejection and away from recognition, because the theory's authority is real and earned, and weighing a surprising anomaly against that authority is the correct thing to do in the great majority of cases where the anomaly is error. The structure cannot both protect the field from the flood of false anomalies and reliably admit the rare true one, because the protecting is the burying: the same weighing that correctly turns away a hundred false claims cannot, from inside itself, tell the hundred and first true one apart, since a true anomaly with no mechanism looks exactly like a false one by the field's own best evidence at the time. The way out, waiting for a mechanism or for evidence to accumulate past a threshold, is real but slow, arriving only after the interval has already cost what it cost, and the discoverer cannot argue their way past a rejection that was never a failure of argument. It can only show that no one is a villain, that the theory was built honestly and each expert applies a rule that ordinarily serves the field well, and that the rejection is authored by no one, the cost of a sound policy meeting its rare true exception. And it holds the bound in view, hedged, because this is a documented pattern in genuine anomaly and not a general indictment of scientific caution, the same default that buries a rare true finding rightly rejecting the far more common false ones (Kuhn; the philosophy and sociology of science literature), so the whole of the misfit is that a discovery can be entirely true and still have no place in the theory that would need to hold it, rejected by reasoning sound enough to be right almost every other time it is used.
