## Has

A long accumulation of confirmed predictions, tested and refined across successive trials.
An authority that is real and earned, right far more often than wrong across the whole of its record.
No place prepared for a result outside what it explains, since a theory is defined by what it accounts for.

## Orders

Stand confirmed, on the record you have honestly earned.
Hold your place as the working account of how things work, built from a long accumulation of evidence and refined by successive tests, explaining what came before and predicting what experiment later confirmed.
Know that this authority is real and not mere habit, that it will be right in the great majority of cases still to come, and that its very structure leaves no ready place for a true anomaly it has not yet been built to explain.

## Loses

Nothing of its own standing, and offers no shelter to what it cannot yet explain.
Because its authority is earned honestly through a real record of confirmed prediction, the seat takes no loss and commits no fault, and yet the very completeness that makes it trustworthy is the completeness that has no ready place for a genuine result outside its own account, so a true anomaly finds nowhere to stand within it.
It keeps its honestly earned authority and loses no cause for blame, the paradigm that is right almost always and, for that reason, has no place ready for the rare time it is not yet right.

## Drives

Toward the one whose earned authority leaves no room for what it does not explain,
standing on a real record of confirmed prediction, right far more often than wrong, built honestly and not out of habit,
so the seat that has earned every ounce of its standing is the seat that, by that very earning, offers no place to a true finding its own account cannot yet hold.
