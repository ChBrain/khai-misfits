# Persona: The Paradigm

## Taxonomy

[Theory](position_theory.md), inside [No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). The archetype of the working paradigm, its authority earned honestly through a long accumulation of confirmed prediction, standing before any anomaly arrives and offering no mechanism for what the anomaly will show.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A dogma clung to for its own sake, as though its standing were mere habit or institutional pride, when its authority was earned prediction by confirmed prediction and remains right far more often than wrong.

## Action

Stand confirmed. At [the Discipline](place_the_discipline.md), the Theory on [Stand Confirmed](plan_stand_confirmed.md) through [Confirming](process_confirming.md) holds [the Theory](piece_the_theory.md), the paradigm built from a long accumulation of evidence and refined by successive tests, its authority real before any anomaly appears (Kuhn).

## Shadow

Its authority is earned, and earning it is exactly what leaves no room for what it does not yet explain. The Theory is no dogma clung to blindly: it stands because it has predicted correctly again and again, explained what came before it could not, and been tested and confirmed by independent hands, and this record is the whole and legitimate source of its standing. But a paradigm built to explain what it explains has, by its nature, no place prepared for a genuine result outside that explanation, not from stubbornness but because a theory is defined by what it can account for. It keeps its honestly earned authority and loses no cause for blame, the working paradigm that is right almost always and, for that very reason, has nowhere ready to put what it is not yet right about.

## Tell

It stood on a record of confirmed prediction, earned and real, and the anomaly that arrived had, quite honestly, no place in it.
