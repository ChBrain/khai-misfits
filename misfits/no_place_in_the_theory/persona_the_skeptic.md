# Persona: The Skeptic

## Taxonomy

[Skeptic](position_skeptic.md), inside [No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). The archetype of the expert who weighs the anomalous claim against the theory's accumulated confirmed prediction, and by the ordinary and usually correct rule, sets the true anomaly aside.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A closed mind defending its turf, as though the rejection were pride or fear of being wrong, when it is the same weighing of evidence that correctly turns away nearly every other claim like this one.

## Action

Trust the weight. At [the Society](place_the_society.md), the Skeptic on [Trust the Weight](plan_trust_the_weight.md) through [Weighing](process_weighing.md) takes up [the Sound Default](piece_the_sound_default.md), weighing the anomaly against the theory's confirmed record and finding the theory more credible, then meets [the Rejection](piece_the_rejection.md) (Kuhn; the philosophy and sociology of science literature).

## Shadow

Its weighing is sound, and sound weighing is not the same as being right. The Skeptic is no closed mind: it applies the same rule that ordinarily serves the field well, that a well-confirmed theory outweighs a single surprising result, because most claims that contradict an established theory really are mistaken, artifacts of a flawed study or a coincidence read as a pattern, and a field that abandoned good theories at every surprise would abandon them constantly. The rule is correct in the overwhelming majority of cases it is applied to. What it cannot do, from inside a single application, is tell this one case apart from the many others where the rule was right, since a true anomaly with no mechanism looks exactly like a false one from where the Skeptic stands. It keeps its sound reasoning and loses no cause for blame, the expert whose correct general practice failed only because this was the rare case it was never built to catch.

## Tell

It weighed the claim against the theory's earned record, as it should, and set aside the one time in many that the claim happened to be true.
