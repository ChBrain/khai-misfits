# Plot: The Mechanism Found

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Time passes, and evidence or explanation accumulates past the threshold the earlier weighing was missing.

## Action

At [the Society](place_the_society.md), [the Historian](persona_the_historian.md) from [the historian's seat](position_historian.md) on [Wait for the Mechanism](plan_wait_for_the_mechanism.md) through [Reading](process_reading.md) takes up [the Vindication](piece_the_vindication.md), tracing the case forward to the point the mechanism finally arrives.

## Stage

The Historian reads the case from later vantage, tracing it past the rejection to the point where a mechanism is found or independent confirmation accumulates past a threshold, and records what the moment of rejection could not: that the claim was true all along. Semmelweis's own finding was vindicated only decades later, once germ theory arrived to supply the account the medicine of 1847 lacked, explaining at last why a chlorinated wash between patients would matter (the historical record). The vindication is real, and it changes nothing about what came before it: the earlier weighing was still sound when it was made, and the earlier report was true from the moment it was given, the interval between the two simply the cost of a mechanism not yet found. No villain: no one in the chain was wrong to have waited, since the mechanism could not be argued into existence sooner, only reached, and the Historian's part is to record that it arrived, not to indict the caution that came before it. What the Historian sets down is a claim finally given the place it always deserved, late.

## Tension

A truth finally recognized, at a cost the interval before it already paid.
