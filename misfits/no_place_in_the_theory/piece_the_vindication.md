## Load Bearing

Anomalies a paradigm cannot accommodate are typically set aside only until they accumulate past a threshold the paradigm can no longer absorb (Kuhn, 1962). For Semmelweis, that threshold was crossed decades later, when germ theory supplied the mechanism his own finding had lacked, and the effect he had documented was vindicated as genuine (the historical record). The vindication is real and it is also late: the interval between the finding and its acceptance was paid for in the mortality the practice would have prevented had it been adopted sooner. This piece is load bearing because it closes the historical arc honestly, crediting the correction without pretending the delay was costless.

## Apparent

Reads as proof that the whole system of caution was always wrong, that the field should simply have believed the discoverer from the start.
