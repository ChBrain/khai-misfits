# Plan: Stand Confirmed

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the theory's seat](position_theory.md), inside [No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Theory](position_theory.md)

## Direction

Hold your place as the working account of how things work, built from a long accumulation of evidence and refined by successive tests, explaining what came before and predicting what experiment later confirmed. Know that this authority is real and not mere habit, that it will be right in the great majority of cases still to come, and that its very structure leaves no ready place for a true anomaly it has not yet been built to explain.

## Orders

([The Paradigm](persona_the_paradigm.md) holds [the Theory](piece_the_theory.md) through [Confirming](process_confirming.md) at [the Discipline](place_the_discipline.md), standing on its earned authority before any anomaly appears.)

## Implementation

([Confirming](process_confirming.md) at [the Discipline](place_the_discipline.md) holds [the Theory](piece_the_theory.md), because a paradigm built from confirmed prediction earns real authority, and that same earning leaves no place prepared for a genuine result outside its own account (Kuhn).)

## Targets

- [ ] the theory holds its place on a real, long-earned record of confirmed prediction
- [ ] the authority is understood as earned honestly, not habit or institutional pride
- [ ] it is seen that the theory is right far more often than wrong across its whole record
- [ ] it is owned that this very completeness leaves no ready place for a true anomaly not yet explained
