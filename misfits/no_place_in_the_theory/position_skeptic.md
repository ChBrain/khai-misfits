# Position: Skeptic

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). The seat of the expert who weighs the anomaly against the theory and sets it aside, and marks that the weighing is sound and correct in the great majority of cases it is applied to.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A well-confirmed theory to weigh the anomalous claim against, backed by a great record of prediction.
A sound and ordinary rule, that an established theory outweighs a single surprising result.
No way, from inside a single weighing, to tell a true anomaly apart from the many false ones the rule correctly rejects.

## Orders

Trust the weight, and weigh the anomaly against the theory's earned record.
Compare the surprising claim to the theory's accumulated confirmed prediction, and judge the theory more credible, since most claims that contradict an established theory really are mistaken.
Know that this rule protects the field from abandoning good theories at every surprise, that it is correct nearly every time it is applied, and that you cannot tell, from where you weigh, whether this is the rare time it is not.

## Loses

Nothing in its own reasoning, and rejects a truth all the same.
Because it applies the same sound rule that ordinarily serves the field well, weighing a well-confirmed theory over a single surprising result, the seat commits no error of method, and yet that correct method, applied to the rare true anomaly, yields a wrong conclusion it had no way to foresee from inside the weighing itself.
It keeps its sound and ordinary reasoning and loses no cause for blame, the expert whose correct general practice failed only on the case it was never built to catch.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound reasoning is right nearly every time and wrong this once,
weighing the anomaly against the theory's earned authority, exactly as the rule that serves the field well requires,
so the seat that reasons correctly by every ordinary standard is the seat that rejects a true claim indistinguishable, from where it stands, from the false ones the same reasoning rightly turns away.
