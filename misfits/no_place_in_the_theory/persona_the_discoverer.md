# Persona: The Discoverer

## Taxonomy

[Discoverer](position_discoverer.md), inside [No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). The archetype of the researcher who finds and reports a real, repeatable finding the theory cannot explain, offering the result honestly with no mechanism to defend it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A crank chasing a coincidence, as though the effect it reports were an artifact of a flawed study, when it is real and repeatable under test and the discoverer reports it exactly as found.

## Action

Report what you found. At [the Ward](place_the_ward.md), the Discoverer on [Report What You Found](plan_report_what_you_found.md) through [Discovering](process_discovering.md) holds [the Anomaly](piece_the_anomaly.md), the effect observed and confirmed under repeated test, offered honestly with no mechanism the field's theory recognizes (the historical record of Semmelweis's discovery).

## Shadow

Its finding is real, and reality alone is not enough to give it a place. The Discoverer is no crank: the effect is repeatable, tested, and true, a mortality rate that falls and falls again under the same intervention, and it reports the result exactly as observed, claiming no more than the data shows. What it lacks is not honesty or rigor but a mechanism, an account of why the effect occurs that the field's theory can recognize as legitimate, and without that account a true result and a false one look, from outside, exactly alike. It keeps its honest, repeatable finding and loses no cause for blame, the one who found something real and could not yet say why.

## Tell

It reported a result that held up every time it was tested, and could offer no reason the theory would accept, and so offered nothing the theory could use.
