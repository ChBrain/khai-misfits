# Plan: Trust the Weight

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the skeptic's seat](position_skeptic.md), inside [No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Skeptic](position_skeptic.md)

## Direction

Compare the surprising claim to the theory's accumulated confirmed prediction, and judge the theory more credible, since most claims that contradict an established theory really are mistaken. Know that this rule protects the field from abandoning good theories at every surprise, that it is correct nearly every time it is applied, and that you cannot tell, from where you weigh, whether this is the rare time it is not.

## Orders

([The Skeptic](persona_the_skeptic.md) takes up [the Sound Default](piece_the_sound_default.md) through [Weighing](process_weighing.md) at [the Society](place_the_society.md), weighing the anomaly against the theory's confirmed record, and meets [the Rejection](piece_the_rejection.md).)

## Implementation

([Weighing](process_weighing.md) at [the Society](place_the_society.md) takes up [the Sound Default](piece_the_sound_default.md), because a well-confirmed theory rightly outweighs a single surprising result in the great majority of cases, so [the Rejection](piece_the_rejection.md) follows correctly from a rule that cannot tell this case apart from the many it correctly turns away (Kuhn; the philosophy and sociology of science literature).)

## Targets

- [ ] the anomalous claim is weighed against the theory's accumulated confirmed prediction
- [ ] the theory is judged more credible, following a rule sound in the great majority of cases
- [ ] it is seen that this rule protects the field from abandoning good theories at every surprise
- [ ] it is owned that the weighing cannot, from inside itself, tell a true anomaly from a false one
