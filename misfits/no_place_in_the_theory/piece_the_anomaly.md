# Piece: The Anomaly

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). The mechanism: a real, repeatable effect the theory does not predict and offers no accepted mechanism to explain, reported in good faith.

## Load Bearing

In 1847 Ignaz Semmelweis found that having attendants wash their hands in a chlorine solution before examining mothers sharply cut deaths from childbed fever, a real and repeatable effect that showed up ward after ward, month after month (the historical record of Semmelweis's discovery). What he did not have, and what no one at the time could give him, was an accepted mechanism: germ theory did not yet exist, so there was no reason recognized by the medicine of the day for washed hands to matter at all. The result was genuine. It simply had no place in the theory available to explain it. This piece is load bearing because it is the honest half of the trap: a true finding, offered without a story the field can yet accept.
