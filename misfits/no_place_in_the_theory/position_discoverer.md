# Position: Discoverer

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). The seat of the one who finds and reports a real, repeatable anomaly, and marks that the finding is honest and true and lacks only a mechanism the field's theory can yet recognize.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real, repeatable result, confirmed under test again and again.
An honest report, claiming no more than the observed data shows.
No mechanism the field's theory recognizes as legitimate, and so no accepted account of why the result occurs.

## Orders

Report what you found, exactly as you found it.
Observe the effect, test it under repetition, and report it honestly, claiming nothing beyond what the data plainly shows, whether or not you can explain why it occurs.
Know that a true result offered without a mechanism the theory can recognize will look, from outside, exactly like a false one, and that this is not a fault in your reporting but the shape of the trap itself.

## Loses

The credit its true finding deserves, though the finding itself stands unchanged.
Because it reports honestly and claims no more than the data shows, the seat commits no error in the reporting, and yet without a mechanism the theory can recognize, the true result is read no differently than a false one would be, so it loses the credibility a correct finding should earn, for want of an explanation it does not yet have.
It keeps its honest, repeatable finding and loses no cause for blame, the one who found something real and could not yet say why it was so.

## Drives

Toward the one whose true finding is read exactly like a false one for want of a mechanism,
observing and reporting a real, repeated effect honestly, claiming no more than the evidence shows,
so the seat that discovers something genuinely true is the seat left indistinguishable, by the field's own best evidence, from one that discovered nothing at all.
