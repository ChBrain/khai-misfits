# Piece: The Rejection

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). The mechanism: a correct seeming inference, authored by no one, meeting its rare true exception, so a true finding is set aside and its finder discredited.

## Place

At [the Society](place_the_society.md), carried by [Weighing](process_weighing.md), voiced by [the Skeptic](persona_the_skeptic.md): the paper unread past its summary, the practice unadopted, the name that becomes a byword for obstinacy rather than for the finding it belonged to.

## Load Bearing

The sound default, applied to Semmelweis's finding, produced the wrong answer: the anomaly was set aside and Semmelweis himself was rejected and ridiculed by the medical establishment of his day (the historical record). No one conspired to do this. Each expert who dismissed the claim was applying the same rule that, applied to the far larger number of false anomalies any field encounters, protects it well. The reasoning was sound at every step and the conclusion was still wrong, because this was the rare case where the anomaly was true. This piece is load bearing because it is the trap closing: the cost of a policy that is right almost always, paid by the one case where it is not, with no villain anywhere in the chain.

## Apparent

Reads as closed mindedness, or as a discipline protecting its own authority against an inconvenient challenger.
