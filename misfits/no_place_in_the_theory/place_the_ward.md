# Place: The Ward

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). Where the researcher observes and tests a real, repeatable result the reigning theory does not predict. Hosts [A Repeatable Result](plot_a_repeatable_result.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Patients, records, and a practice tried and retried until its effect holds under repetition, the maternity ward where Semmelweis tracked mortality against a chlorinated hand wash he could not yet explain (the historical record).

## Holds

[The Anomaly](piece_the_anomaly.md).
A finding real and repeatable under test, with no mechanism the reigning theory recognizes as legitimate (the historical record).

## Offers

Direct contact with the result itself: repetition enough to know the effect is real, independent of whether anyone yet has an account of why.

## Withheld

A mechanism. The ward can confirm the effect holds; it cannot supply the explanation the theory would need in order to accept it.
