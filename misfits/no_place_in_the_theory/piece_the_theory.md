## Load Bearing

A theory is not believed on faith. It is believed because it has predicted correctly, again and again, across a long run of tests that could each have broken it and did not (Kuhn, The Structure of Scientific Revolutions, 1962). That accumulated record is what "normal science" means: a paradigm mature enough to tell its practitioners what to expect, what counts as a competent result, and what an error looks like when one occurs. The authority is earned, not asserted, and it is earned before the discipline has ever met the case that will strain it. This piece is load bearing because everything that follows depends on the theory being genuinely well confirmed and not a strawman: the trap only works if the paradigm being defended is sound.

## Apparent

Reads as institutional dogma, a habit of mind mistaken for knowledge, the profession simply repeating what it was taught because that is what professions do.
