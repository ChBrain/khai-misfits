## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

The Skeptic, encountering the anomalous claim and measuring it against the theory's accumulated weight.

## Direction

From the anomalous claim toward a correctly reasoned rejection.

## Lever

A well-confirmed theory rightly outweighs a single surprising result in the great majority of cases, so the rejection follows correctly from a rule that cannot tell this case apart from the many it correctly turns away (Kuhn; the philosophy and sociology of science literature).
