# Piece: The Sound Default

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). The mechanism: the ordinary rule that a well confirmed theory outweighs a single surprising result, sound in the great majority of the cases it is applied to.

At [the Society](place_the_society.md), carried by [Weighing](process_weighing.md), voiced by [the Skeptic](persona_the_skeptic.md): the same careful hearing given to every claimed exception, the same question asked of each: what is more likely, that the whole accumulated record is wrong, or that this one result is.

## Load Bearing

An anomaly the reigning paradigm cannot accommodate is not, ordinarily, grounds to overturn that paradigm. Normal science sets such anomalies aside and keeps working within the paradigm until anomalies accumulate past some threshold (Kuhn, 1962). This is not obstruction, it is the correct default: most claims that contradict a well confirmed theory really are mistaken, the product of error, confound, or coincidence rather than genuine discovery, and the philosophy and sociology of science literature documents that resisting an isolated anomaly is, in the great majority of cases, the epistemically sound response. This piece is load bearing because it names the rule that makes the whole trap possible: a rule that is right almost every time, applied honestly, by people who mean well.

## Apparent

Reads as an infallible or purely objective procedure, a mechanical test that simply sorts truth from error, rather than a probabilistic default that is usually right and occasionally wrong.
