# Persona: The Historian

## Taxonomy

[Historian](position_historian.md), inside [No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). The archetype of the one who reads across the cases from later vantage, records the vindication once a mechanism arrives, and holds the pattern to its true and bounded scope.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A prosecutor of the field's past errors, as though every rejected anomaly were proof the field is generally wrong to resist novelty, when most anomalies that were ever rejected turned out to be exactly the errors the rejection took them for.

## Action

Wait for the mechanism. At [the Society](place_the_society.md), the Historian on [Wait for the Mechanism](plan_wait_for_the_mechanism.md) through [Reading](process_reading.md) holds [the Vindication](piece_the_vindication.md), recording the anomaly's eventual acceptance once a mechanism is found, then meets [the Conditions](piece_the_conditions.md), the true scope of the pattern (the historical record; Kuhn).

## Shadow

It records the vindication and must not turn it into a verdict on caution itself. The Historian is no prosecutor: it can trace, case by documented case, the true anomalies that were correctly rejected in their moment and only later vindicated once germ theory or its equivalent supplied what was missing, and this record is real and worth telling. But it holds its own bound: for every Semmelweis, many more anomalies rejected by the same reasoning really were error, and the same sound default that buried the rare true finding rightly turned those away, so to read every past rejection as a future vindication waiting to happen is to mistake the exception for the rule. It keeps its true record of vindication and its held bound, and loses no cause for blame, the one who honors the discoveries rescued by time without indicting the caution that, far more often, was simply right.

## Tell

It named the cases where a true anomaly was rejected and later proven, and stopped there, because most rejected anomalies never needed proving at all.
