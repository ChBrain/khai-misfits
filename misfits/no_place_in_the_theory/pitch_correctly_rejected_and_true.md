# Pitch: Correctly Rejected, and True

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a discovery can be entirely true and still be rejected by reasoning that was, by every ordinary standard, sound.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Measured and clinical, the quiet tragedy of a good rule meeting its rare bad case. A theory earned honestly, an anomaly reported honestly, and a rejection that follows from correct reasoning applied to the wrong case. Nothing here is closed-mindedness or cruelty; the theory is right far more often than wrong.

## Undertow

An expert who encounters a claim that contradicts a well-supported theory reasonably treats the theory, backed by a great weight of confirmed prediction, as more likely correct than a single surprising result, and this is the right default in the overwhelming majority of cases. A true finding with no accepted mechanism looks, by the field's own best evidence, exactly like a false one, since it cannot be told apart from inside the rejection itself.

## Nerve

That the very reasoning protecting a field from a flood of false alarms is the reasoning that can also bury a rare true one, so a valid discovery can have no place in the theory that would need to hold it. No one is a villain, held against its limit and hedged, since this is a documented pattern in genuine anomaly and not a claim that consensus is generally wrong to resist novelty, the same default rightly rejecting most false anomalies (Kuhn; the philosophy and sociology of science literature).

## Echo

Find something true the theory cannot explain, and be turned away by reasoning sound enough to be right almost every other time.
