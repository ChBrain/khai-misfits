# Plot: A Repeatable Result

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A ward where mortality falls, and falls again, under a practice the reigning theory offers no reason to expect.

## Action

At [the Ward](place_the_ward.md), [the Discoverer](persona_the_discoverer.md) from [the discoverer's seat](position_discoverer.md) on [Report What You Found](plan_report_what_you_found.md) through [Discovering](process_discovering.md) takes up [the Anomaly](piece_the_anomaly.md), observing and reporting the effect exactly as it was found.

## Stage

At the Vienna General Hospital in 1847, Ignaz Semmelweis found that having attendants wash their hands in a chlorinated solution before examining mothers sharply cut deaths from childbed fever, a striking effect that held ward after ward and month after month (the historical record of Semmelweis's discovery). The Discoverer reports it honestly: the mortality tables are counted and recounted, the practice tried and retried, and the number does not move no matter how many times the comparison is run. What cannot be offered is a mechanism the medicine of the day would recognize, since germ theory does not yet exist and nothing in the reigning account of disease gives a reason for washed hands to matter at all. No villain: the report claims no more than the data shows, no exaggeration and no overreach into an explanation not yet available, only an effect that repeats under every honest test put to it. What it lacks is not rigor but a place to be believed in, since a true result offered without a mechanism the theory can accept looks, from outside, exactly like a false one.

## Tension

A true finding that, without a mechanism, cannot yet be told apart from a false one. It is exactly as real as it will later prove to be, and exactly as unbelievable as it now appears.
