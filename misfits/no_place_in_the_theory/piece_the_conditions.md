## Load Bearing

The pattern this misfit stages is narrow by design. It applies to a documented case of genuine anomaly: a real, repeatable effect, honestly reported, that later turned out to be true (the historical record; Kuhn, 1962). It is not a claim that consensus is generally wrong to resist novelty, and it is not license to treat every rejected claim as a future Semmelweis. The philosophy and sociology of science literature is clear that the same default which occasionally buries a true finding is, in the great majority of cases it is applied to, rightly turning away claims that really are mistaken. This piece is load bearing because it is the boundary that keeps the whole misfit honest: it names exactly what the pattern covers and refuses what it does not.
