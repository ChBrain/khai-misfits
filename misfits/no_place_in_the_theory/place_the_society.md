# Place: The Society

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). Where the field's experts weigh the anomalous claim and reject it by sound reasoning, and where, much later, the vindication and the pattern's true scope are recorded. Hosts [The Sound Weighing](plot_the_sound_weighing.md), [Set Aside](plot_set_aside.md), [The Mechanism Found](plot_the_mechanism_found.md), [Bound to the Cases](plot_bound_to_the_cases.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A body of peers, correspondence, and published judgment, where a claim is measured against the theory's accumulated weight, first found wanting, and later, once the missing piece arrives, found true.

## Holds

[The Sound Default](piece_the_sound_default.md).
The ordinary and usually correct rule that weighs an established theory over a single surprising result (Kuhn; the philosophy and sociology of science literature).

[The Rejection](piece_the_rejection.md).
The anomaly set aside and its discoverer discredited, a correct-seeming inference applied to a claim that happened to be true (the historical record).

[The Vindication](piece_the_vindication.md).
The claim accepted once a mechanism is found or independent confirmation accumulates past a threshold, arriving late and at real cost to the interval before it (the historical record).

[The Conditions](piece_the_conditions.md).
The bound that holds the pattern to documented cases of genuine anomaly, not a general indictment of scientific caution (Kuhn; the philosophy and sociology of science literature).

## Offers

Judgment: the standing to weigh a claim against the field's whole accumulated record, and, eventually, the standing to revise that judgment once the record changes.

## Withheld

Any way to sort, from inside the rejection itself, the rare true anomaly from the far more common false one. The missing mechanism is not a gap in argument the Society can reason its way past; it is a fact the Society does not yet have.
