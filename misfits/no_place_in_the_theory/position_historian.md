# Position: Historian

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). The seat of the one who records the eventual vindication once a mechanism arrives, and marks that the record is real and that it must not be read as proof consensus is generally wrong to resist novelty.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A later vantage, from which the mechanism the field once lacked has since been found.
A true record of vindication, cases where a rejected anomaly was later proven correct.
A held bound, the knowledge that most rejected anomalies were rightly rejected and never needed proving at all.

## Orders

Wait for the mechanism, and record the vindication when it comes.
Trace the case forward to the point where a mechanism is found or independent confirmation accumulates past a threshold, and record the anomaly's acceptance, late and real.
Know that this record, though true, is the rare exception and not the rule, that far more rejected anomalies stayed rejected because they were genuinely wrong, and that to read every past rejection as a future vindication waiting to happen is to mistake the exception for the rule.

## Loses

Nothing it can name, and holds a truth it must not overstate.
Because it records a real vindication once the mechanism arrives, the seat takes no loss, and yet it carries the harder discipline: to honor the discoveries rescued by time without indicting the caution that, far more often, was simply right, naming the exception without making it the rule.
It keeps its true record and its held bound, and loses no cause for blame, the one who reads the vindication and stops there.

## Drives

Toward the one who records a true vindication and will not overreach it into a verdict,
tracing the case forward to the mechanism that finally arrives, honoring the discovery rescued by time,
so the seat that alone can name the exception holds it to a bound, a rare truth recorded and not made proof that the sound caution around it was ever wrong to exist.
