# Plan: Report What You Found

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the discoverer's seat](position_discoverer.md), inside [No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Discoverer](position_discoverer.md)

## Direction

Observe the effect, test it under repetition, and report it honestly, claiming nothing beyond what the data plainly shows, whether or not you can explain why it occurs. Know that a true result offered without a mechanism the theory can recognize will look, from outside, exactly like a false one, and that this is not a fault in your reporting but the shape of the trap itself.

## Orders

([The Discoverer](persona_the_discoverer.md) holds [the Anomaly](piece_the_anomaly.md) through [Discovering](process_discovering.md) at [the Ward](place_the_ward.md), observing and reporting a real, repeated effect the theory cannot explain.)

## Implementation

([Discovering](process_discovering.md) at [the Ward](place_the_ward.md) holds [the Anomaly](piece_the_anomaly.md), because a result tested and confirmed under repetition, reported honestly, is real regardless of whether a mechanism yet exists to explain it (the historical record of Semmelweis's discovery).)

## Targets

- [ ] the effect is observed and tested under repeated trial, confirmed each time
- [ ] the finding is reported honestly, claiming no more than the data shows
- [ ] it is seen that no mechanism the theory recognizes as legitimate is yet available
- [ ] it is owned that a true result without a mechanism looks, from outside, exactly like a false one
