# Place: The Discipline

## Taxonomy

[No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md). Where the working theory stands, its authority earned honestly through a long accumulation of confirmed prediction, before any anomaly arrives to test it. Hosts [A Theory Earned](plot_a_theory_earned.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A body of confirmed prediction, laid down trial by trial and refined by successive tests, standing as the field's working account of how things work. No anomaly has yet arrived to test it.

## Holds

[The Theory](piece_the_theory.md).
The paradigm itself, built honestly from real predictive success, its authority earned and not assumed (Kuhn).

## Offers

A seat whose confidence is warranted: a record long enough and consistent enough that trusting it is, in the great majority of cases still to come, the correct thing to do.

## Withheld

Any place already prepared for a result the theory does not predict. The same accumulation that earns its authority is the accumulation that leaves no room ready for what falls outside it.
