# Plan: Wait for the Mechanism

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the historian's seat](position_historian.md), inside [No Place in the Theory](play_no_place_in_the_theory.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Historian](position_historian.md)

## Direction

Trace the case forward to the point where a mechanism is found or independent confirmation accumulates past a threshold, and record the anomaly's acceptance, late and real. Know that this record, though true, is the rare exception and not the rule, that far more rejected anomalies stayed rejected because they were genuinely wrong, and that to read every past rejection as a future vindication waiting to happen is to mistake the exception for the rule.

## Orders

([The Historian](persona_the_historian.md) holds [the Vindication](piece_the_vindication.md) through [Reading](process_reading.md) at [the Society](place_the_society.md), tracing the case forward to the mechanism's eventual arrival, and meets [the Conditions](piece_the_conditions.md).)

## Implementation

([Reading](process_reading.md) at [the Society](place_the_society.md) holds [the Vindication](piece_the_vindication.md), because once a mechanism is found or evidence accumulates past a threshold, the rejected anomaly is accepted, and [the Conditions](piece_the_conditions.md) hold the true scope of the pattern, documented in genuine cases and not a general indictment (the historical record; Kuhn).)
