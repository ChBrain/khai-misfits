# Position: Explorer

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The seat that re-samples what burned the learner, the costly remedy that goes back against avoidance to give a shunned option a fair second draw, the bound on the trap.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one cure the sampling lacks: to draw again what a bad outcome sealed, so a good option written off by luck can be found and reinstated.
A set of costly means: second chances given by policy, a few trials randomised, the dropped options re-explored, or another's experience borrowed where the record is honest.
No cheap or certain version, because most of what burned the learner really was bad, so every retrial mostly confirms the first draw, and the good option rescued is one paid for among many that were not.

## Orders

Try it again anyway.
Go back against the flinch and re-sample what was written off, since a sealed error can be opened only by the second draw the learner refused to take.
Accept the cost and the odds, and know that you cannot tell the unlucky-good from the truly-bad before you retry it, so you must pay for the many to recover the one.

## Loses

The cost of every retrial, most of which end where the first draw did.
Because the seat cannot sort the good written off by luck from the bad written off by desert until it samples them again, it spends real effort on options that mostly stay bad, buying back the few sealed errors at the price of retouching many stoves that truly are hot.
It keeps the only remedy there is and bears its whole cost, the one who pays for the many second draws to find the one that was only unlucky.

## Drives

Toward the one who buys back the sealed error at real cost,
re-sampling against the pull of avoidance, no free version of the cure,
so the seat that holds the only way out is the seat that must pay for the many to recover the one, the bound on the trap bought one costly second draw at a time.
