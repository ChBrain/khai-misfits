# Position: Learner

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The seat of the one who learns by sampling, keeping the options that go well and avoiding the ones that go badly, and forming its view of the world from the record that leaves.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A sound rule for learning: try options, read the outcomes, keep what serves and drop what hurts.
A real reason to avoid what burned it: repeating a bad experience costs, and there is no way to test whether the burn was a fluke without risking the burn again.
No sight of what it does not sample, because its record holds only the options it kept trying, so the evidence that would correct a bad first draw is exactly the evidence its own avoidance never gathers.

## Orders

Go by what you have seen.
Keep sampling what has served you and decline to repeat what has hurt you, since that is how experience teaches and prejudging is worse than learning from outcomes.
Know that this fair rule reads from a censored record, so the picture you form runs low through no fault of your judgement, only through what a sampler who avoids what burns it never comes back to see.

## Loses

Its accuracy, not through carelessness but through the very prudence that protects it.
Because it stops sampling what drew badly, the low-side errors it makes are never corrected while the high-side ones are, so its honest average of a truncated record is biased downward, and it thinks worse of the world than the world deserves.
It keeps its prudence and loses its aim, the fair judge undone by a sample its own sound rule truncates.

## Drives

Toward the one who learns soundly and still misjudges,
keeping what served and dropping what hurt, no prejudice in it,
so the seat whose rule is exactly what good judgement means is the seat whose avoidance seals the errors it can never see corrected, the bias a property of the sampling and not of the mind.
