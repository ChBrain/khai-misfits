# Process: Revisiting

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). Borne at [the Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md), precipitated by [the Explorer](persona_the_explorer.md) choosing to return, in [A Second Draw](plot_a_second_draw.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Explorer](persona_the_explorer.md), deliberately spending on a second trial of an option that already burned it once.

## Direction

From a shunned option toward a fair second sample, at real cost.
The option enters revisiting with a record of one bad draw and nothing since; the Explorer pays to add a second entry, so the process drives from a single, censored mark toward a record that can, at last, be read against a true average.

## Lever

Only deliberate re-sampling of what burned it can rescue the few unlucky options among the many that truly were bad, and the agent cannot tell which is which in advance, so the remedy is dear (Denrell).
Nothing in ordinary sampling ever returns to a shunned option, so correcting its record takes an act outside that logic, a trial paid for without the guidance of a prior good result, spent on options most of which will confirm the first bad draw and only a few of which will overturn it.
