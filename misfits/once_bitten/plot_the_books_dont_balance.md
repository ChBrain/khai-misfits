# Plot: The Books Don't Balance

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md) totalled honestly, column by column, and still coming out low.
Every figure entered is a true figure; nothing here has been fudged, and the sum still reads worse than the world actually is.

## Action

[The Learner](persona_the_learner.md) reads its belief straight off [the Censored Sample](piece_the_censored_sample.md), the record built only from what stayed in play, and forms [the Downward Bias](piece_the_downward_bias.md): variable options get shunned across the board, though the Learner carries no fear of risk at all going in.

## Stage

Beliefs built from an asymmetrically truncated sample come out biased downward, and because variability is what produces the early bad draw that ends sampling, risk aversion emerges from the sampling asymmetry itself, with no intrinsic risk preference required (Denrell & March; Denrell, 2007).

## Tension

The average of what remains is honest and still wrong, because the losers were never entered in the book.
