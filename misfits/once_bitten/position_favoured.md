# Position: Favoured

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The seat of the option kept in play by a good first draw, sampled again and again so that its early luck is exposed and its true worth found, the arm of the mechanism that self-corrects.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A good first outcome and the sampling it earns: chosen once and so chosen again, tried until the truth comes out.
A fair exposure to correction, because continued use regresses a lucky high draw toward what the option is really worth, so a first impression too kind does not survive the trials.
No permanence to its luck, because the same repeated sampling that keeps it in play is what would find it out if it were only lucky.

## Orders

Stay in play.
Take the trials your good first draw earns and let them run, since being sampled again is what turns a first impression into a fair estimate, whether it lifts you or lowers you to your true worth.
Know that this correction you receive is exactly what the burned option is denied, so your fair hearing is the measure of the other's silence and not a favour you were owed.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and yet its fair correction exposes the asymmetry.
Because the seat is tested until the truth catches up, it shows what a full sampling does, and that very fairness throws into relief that the burned option never gets it, so the seat that is corrected is the mirror in which the sealed error is seen.
It keeps its many looks and loses no cause for blame, the option whose fair hearing reveals the other's frozen verdict.

## Drives

Toward the one sampled to the truth while the other is sealed at a guess,
kept in play by a good draw and corrected by the trials it earns, no unfairness in the correcting,
so the seat that receives the full hearing is the seat that shows what the burned option is denied, the asymmetry and not the correction the whole of the trap.
