# Plot: The Hot Stove

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Stove](place_the_stove.md), the lid untried, its heat unknown until a hand goes down on it.
One reach, nothing held in reserve, the whole question of hot or cold about to be settled by a single touch.

## Action

[The Learner](persona_the_learner.md), from [the learner's seat](position_learner.md), runs [Go by What I've Seen](plan_go_by_what_ive_seen.md) through [Burning](process_burning.md): the hand goes down, takes [the Bad Draw](piece_the_bad_draw.md), and comes back up already deciding, settling into [the Flinch](piece_the_flinch.md) that will not let it reach again.

## Stage

Denrell's hot-stove effect, after Twain's image: a cat that sits on a hot stove-lid will not sit on a stove-lid again, hot or cold, so it never learns that the cold one was safe (Twain; Denrell). One draw from a variable option is mostly noise, the lid may run hot only sometimes, so the burn the Learner reads as the lid's whole nature may be nothing more than an unlucky instant, and there will be no second touch to check it.

## Tension

The very prudence that keeps the Learner from a second burn keeps it from ever learning the burn was chance.
