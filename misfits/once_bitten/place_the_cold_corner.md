# Place: The Cold Corner

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). Where the shunned options sit untested, never revisited, their true worth unknown; it hosts [Never Asked Back](plot_never_asked_back.md) and [A Second Draw](plot_a_second_draw.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The place options are set aside after one bad draw, gathering no new evidence, a corner the Learner does not walk back into.
Options shelved and left, no second trial ever run against them; [the Burned](persona_the_burned.md) waits here, unvisited, its true value exactly what it was the day it was shelved.

## Holds

The shunning, and what it leaves untouched.
Here an option sits exactly where the bad draw left it, so the Cold Corner holds the point where avoidance becomes permanent: nothing tried here changes, because nothing here is tried, and the option's one bad outcome stands as its entire record, unrevised by anything that has not happened since.
Holds [the Untested](piece_the_untested.md).

## Offers

To anyone who returns, the truth a second sample would reveal.
The Cold Corner keeps every shunned option intact and available, so it offers whatever visits it the chance to find out, at last, whether the first draw was the rule or the exception, an answer sitting here the whole time, unclaimed.

## Withheld

That second sample, since the Learner never comes back.
The Cold Corner can offer the truth but cannot make anyone collect it, so it withholds the one thing that would matter, an actual second draw, because the Learner who shunned this place has no reason, by its own lights, to return to it, and the truth waits here uncollected.
