# Plan: Try It Again Anyway

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the explorer's seat](position_explorer.md), inside [Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Explorer](position_explorer.md)

## Direction

Go back against the flinch and re-sample what was written off, since a sealed error can be opened only by the second draw the learner refused to take. Give second chances by policy, randomise a few trials, re-explore the dropped options, or borrow another's honest experience of them. Accept the cost and the odds, and know that you cannot tell the unlucky-good from the truly-bad before you retry it, so you must pay for the many to recover the one.

## Orders

([The Explorer](persona_the_explorer.md) returns to [the Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md) and through [Revisiting](process_revisiting.md) draws [the Untested](piece_the_untested.md) again, undoing [the Downward Bias](piece_the_downward_bias.md) where a good option was written off by luck, at real cost.)

## Implementation

([Revisiting](process_revisiting.md) at [the Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md) re-samples what the flinch sealed, because only a second draw opens a frozen record, and it undoes [the Downward Bias](piece_the_downward_bias.md) for the few unlucky-good options at the price of retouching the many that truly were bad.)

## Targets

- [ ] what was written off is deliberately re-sampled against the pull of avoidance
- [ ] the remedy is spent knowing the unlucky-good cannot be told from the truly-bad in advance
- [ ] the cost of retouching many hot stoves to find the one is accepted, not wished away
- [ ] the downward bias is undone only where a good option was sealed by luck, not everywhere
