# Pitch: Never Tried Again

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a good thing can be lost to one unlucky draw because the draw is never repeated.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Prudent, even wise. An agent learns from experience and sensibly avoids what has burned it, forming its view of the world from what it has actually seen. Nothing here is careless; the rule is exactly what good judgement is supposed to be.

## Undertow

Avoidance stops the sampling. A good first draw is kept and, if it was luck, corrected by continued use; a bad first draw is dropped, and the experience that would show it was luck is never gathered, so negative errors seal themselves while positive ones wash out, and beliefs from experience run low (Denrell; Denrell & March).

## Nerve

That a good option, or a good person, can be written off forever on one bad day, and written off by a learner doing everything right. No prejudice, no unfair hearing, just a fair rule applied to a sample the rule itself truncates.

## Echo

Once bitten, never tried again, so the burn is never found to have been chance.
