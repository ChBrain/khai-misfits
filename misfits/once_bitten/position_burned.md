# Position: Burned

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The seat of the option written off after a bad draw, perhaps genuinely good, whose worth is frozen at one unlucky result because it is never sampled again.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A worth that may be real and a record that shows one bad mark.
A single draw against it, real or merely unlucky, taken by the learner as a verdict though one sample from a variable option is mostly noise.
No way to compel a second try, because the proof that would clear it is a sampling only the learner can give, and the learner is precisely the one who has resolved to avoid it.

## Orders

Seek somewhere you are new.
Since the learner who burned once will not draw you again, carry your unproven worth to a learner who never saw the bad draw and might sample you afresh, for that is the only opening a sealed record leaves.
Know that your low standing is a verdict frozen and not a verdict reached, and that nothing you can do from where you sit will reopen it, only a stranger's fresh trial.

## Loses

The chance to show what it is worth, held shut by the one draw on its record.
Because the correction it needs is a second sample and the sample never comes, the seat loses not in proportion to any true failing but for a single unlucky result never revised, condemned by a draw that was never repeated.
It keeps whatever worth it truly has and loses every means to prove it, the option good or bad alike sealed at its first bad outcome.

## Drives

Toward the one written off for one bad draw and never asked back,
maybe better than its record and unable to show it, no fault of its own in it,
so the seat that might be good is the seat that stays condemned, its worth unlearned because the one who could learn it is the one who will not try.
