# Plot: Never Asked Back

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md), where the option that burned the Learner sits exactly where it was left, no new trial run against it since.
Elsewhere, in some room that never met this option at its worst, an opening the option might still fill.

## Action

[The Learner](persona_the_learner.md), running [Shunning](process_shunning.md), simply does not return; the option stays [the Untested](piece_the_untested.md), its single bad draw standing as its whole record.
Meanwhile [the Burned](persona_the_burned.md), from [the burned's seat](position_burned.md), runs [Somewhere I'm New](plan_somewhere_im_new.md), seeking out a learner who has never sampled its bad draw and so carries no verdict on it at all.

## Stage

The negative error self-seals because the option is never revisited: one bad draw ends the sampling that could have corrected it, and the low impression stands unrevised (Denrell & March). This is not The Survivors: the censoring there is exogenous attrition destroying the record from outside, planes that went down never returning to be counted, while here the censoring is endogenous, the Learner's own choice to withhold the second sample that would have tested the first.

## Tension

The option's low standing is not a verdict reached but a verdict frozen, and its only escape is a stranger who never saw it burn.
