# Piece: The Second Look

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The continued sampling of an option that looked good, which exposes a lucky first draw to regression and washes the positive error out.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), carried by [Correcting](process_correcting.md), where an option kept in play accumulates entry after entry.
[The Favoured](persona_the_favoured.md) is who receives it, tried again and again until its luck settles toward its true worth.

## Load Bearing

An option that gives a good result is chosen again, and being chosen again, it is sampled again, so a first draw that was merely lucky meets a second and a third that regress it toward what the option actually is (Denrell).
Guard against reading the self-correction of good options as fairness in the system: it is only half of it, and the very mechanism that corrects the lucky high leaves the unlucky low uncorrected, so the asymmetry, not the correction, is the point.

## Apparent

Diligence rewarded, the system working as it should. It looks like proof the process is fair, giving every option the repeated look that lets its real quality show.

## Yearbook

Runs through [the Favoured](persona_the_favoured.md) on [Stay in Play](plan_stay_in_play.md), staged in [Kept in Play](plot_kept_in_play.md).
Set against [the Censored Sample](piece_the_censored_sample.md) and [the Untested](piece_the_untested.md), the correction the low side never gets, the imbalance [The Books Don't Balance](plot_the_books_dont_balance.md) names.
