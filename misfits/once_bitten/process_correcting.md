# Process: Correcting

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). Borne at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), precipitated by [the Favoured](persona_the_favoured.md) kept in play by a good first draw, in [Kept in Play](plot_kept_in_play.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Favoured](persona_the_favoured.md), its first draw lucky and high, kept in the running and sampled again.

## Direction

From a lucky high first draw toward its true, lower value as sampling continues.
The Favoured opens with an outcome better than its average, and every further draw pulls the running figure a little closer to what the option is actually worth, so correcting drives from a flattering first mark toward an accurate one.

## Lever

An option that looked good is kept and sampled again, so continued use exposes a lucky first draw to regression and washes the positive error out, the correction the low side never gets (Denrell).
Keeping a good-looking option in play costs the Learner nothing it would not spend anyway, so the same sampling that would have corrected a bad first draw, had the option been kept, is freely available here, and the positive error does not survive it.
