# Plot: A Second Draw

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md), the shunned option still sitting where its bad draw left it, untouched since.
Someone willing to spend a trial on a question everyone else considers already settled.

## Action

[The Explorer](persona_the_explorer.md), from [the explorer's seat](position_explorer.md), runs [Try It Again Anyway](plan_try_it_again_anyway.md) through [Revisiting](process_revisiting.md): the option that burned the Learner is drawn on again, at the Explorer's own cost, chipping at [the Downward Bias](piece_the_downward_bias.md) one re-sample at a time.

## Stage

Only deliberate re-sampling can rescue the rare unlucky option among the many shunned options that truly were bad, and there is no way to tell in advance which is which, so the correction is never free (Denrell). The costly correction, no free hero: every retouched stove that turns out to be exactly as hot as it looked is the price paid for the one that was not.

## Tension

To find the one good option written off by chance, the Explorer must pay to retouch many stoves that really are hot.
