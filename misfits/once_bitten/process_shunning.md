# Process: Shunning

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). Borne at [the Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md), precipitated by [the Learner](persona_the_learner.md) declining to return, in [Never Asked Back](plot_never_asked_back.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Learner](persona_the_learner.md), carrying the flinch forward as a standing avoidance of the option that burned it.

## Direction

From an option tried once toward an option never tried again, its record frozen at the bad draw.
The option was open to a fresh trial the day it was sampled; from that day on it receives none, so shunning drives an option from a single, closed episode toward a permanent absence of any episode that could reopen it.

## Lever

Because the Learner avoids what burned it, the option is never sampled again, so a negative impression is never corrected and the error seals itself (Denrell & March).
Avoidance is the sound response to a bad outcome, so the very prudence that keeps the Learner from repeating a painful trial is what withholds the second sample that would have shown whether the first was the rule or the exception.
