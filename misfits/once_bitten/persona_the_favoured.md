# Persona: The Favoured

## Taxonomy

[Favoured](position_favoured.md), inside [Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The archetype of the option that drew well early and stays sampled: kept in play by a good first outcome, so that continued use exposes its luck and finds its true worth.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The proven winner, the safe pick that keeps being chosen. It looks like the option experience has vindicated, tried again and again and still preferred, whatever its true merit.

## Action

Stay in play and be corrected. Kept by a good first draw, the Favoured goes on being sampled, and through [Correcting](process_correcting.md) at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md) it meets [the Second Look](piece_the_second_look.md), its early luck regressing toward the truth as the trials mount, on [Stay in Play](plan_stay_in_play.md) (Denrell). Whether genuinely good or merely lucky at first, its record comes to say what it is.

## Shadow

Its fair correction is only half the mechanism. That the Favoured is tested until the truth catches up is exactly right, and it is exactly what the burned option never gets, so its self-correcting is the mirror that reveals the asymmetry: the high side is checked and the low side is not. It keeps its many looks and shows no fault, the option whose fair hearing throws the other's silence into relief.

## Tell

It is tried again and again, so whatever it is comes out in the end.
