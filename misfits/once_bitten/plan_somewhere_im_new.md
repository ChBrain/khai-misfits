# Plan: Somewhere I'm New

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the burned's seat](position_burned.md), inside [Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Burned](position_burned.md)

## Direction

Since the learner who burned once will not draw you again, carry your unproven worth to a learner who never saw the bad draw and might sample you afresh, for that is the only opening a sealed record leaves. You cannot compel the second try that would clear you, because the proof is a sampling only the one who avoids you can give. Know that your low standing is a verdict frozen and not a verdict reached, and that a stranger's fresh trial, not any argument from you, is the one way the record reopens.

## Orders

([The Burned](persona_the_burned.md) waits as [the Untested](piece_the_untested.md) at [the Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md), left there by [Shunning](process_shunning.md), and seeks a fresh learner who never took [the Bad Draw](piece_the_bad_draw.md) against it and might draw it anew.)

## Implementation

([Shunning](process_shunning.md) at [the Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md) freezes the option at its one bad result, because [the Untested](piece_the_untested.md) is never sampled again by the learner who burned, so only a stranger who never saw the draw can reopen what the record sealed.)

## Targets

- [ ] the sealed record is understood as a verdict frozen, not a verdict reached
- [ ] the second try is seen to lie beyond the option's own power to compel
- [ ] a fresh learner who never saw the bad draw is sought as the one opening
- [ ] the option's true worth is held to be unproven, not disproven, by its one mark
