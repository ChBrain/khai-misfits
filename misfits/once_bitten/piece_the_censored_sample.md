# Piece: The Censored Sample

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The record built only from what stayed sampled, truncated on the low side, from which the Learner forms a picture biased downward.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), the running account that only ever grows on one side.
[The Learner](persona_the_learner.md) reads it as the total of its experience, though the entries it is missing are as real as the ones it keeps.

## Load Bearing

Adaptation restricts information: what is dropped after a poor result is never sampled again, so the record an agent accumulates contains many entries for options that kept drawing and one entry, never revised, for every option a bad draw ended (Denrell & March).
Guard against reading the sample as a fair record of experience: it is a censored one, missing every option dropped after a bad draw, so the honest average of what remains is still a biased estimate of the world.

## Apparent

An honest tally, everything counted as it came in. It looks like an objective account, since every entry it holds is true and nothing in it has been falsified.

## Yearbook

Totalled at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md) through [Correcting](process_correcting.md), set against what [Shunning](process_shunning.md) withheld from it.
Its imbalance between [the Second Look](piece_the_second_look.md) and [the Untested](piece_the_untested.md) is what [The Books Don't Balance](plot_the_books_dont_balance.md) stages, and what it yields is [the Downward Bias](piece_the_downward_bias.md).
