# Plot: Kept in Play

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), an entry already good running, one more trial about to be added to a growing column.
The option that drew well the first time is reached for again, the reach unremarkable, nothing in it flagged as luck.

## Action

[The Favoured](persona_the_favoured.md), from [the favoured's seat](position_favoured.md), runs [Stay in Play](plan_stay_in_play.md) through [Correcting](process_correcting.md), meeting [the Second Look](piece_the_second_look.md): a good first draw keeps the option sampled, and each further draw pulls a lucky first result back toward what the option is actually worth.

## Stage

The positive error self-corrects because sampling continues; a good early outcome earns the option more trials, and those trials regress its luck to the truth (Denrell). This is not The Bounce, where a reversal is seen and misread as caused by some reward or punishment that did nothing: here the reversal on the high side is genuinely witnessed and rightly read as regression, the whole point being that only this side ever gets watched at all.

## Tension

The lucky are corrected and the unlucky are not, and that asymmetry, not any unfairness in the correcting itself, is the whole trap.
