# Piece: The Bad Draw

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The early negative outcome, real or merely unlucky, that turns the Learner away from an option before its true worth is known.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Stove](place_the_stove.md), carried by [Burning](process_burning.md), the instant a first touch resolves into a hand pulled back for good.
[The Learner](persona_the_learner.md) is the one who draws it; the Bad Draw is the whole verdict, formed on a sample of one.

## Load Bearing

A single trial of a variable option is mostly noise, not signal, so an outcome drawn from it says as much about chance as about the option itself; yet because it is the only draw taken, the Learner has nothing to weigh it against and reads it as the truth of the thing (Denrell).
Guard against reading the bad draw as proof the option is bad: a single draw from a variable option is mostly noise, so an unlucky one condemns a good option as surely as a truly bad one, and nothing in the draw itself tells the Learner which case it is.

## Apparent

Straightforward information, learned the hard way. It looks like the Learner has simply found out what the option is worth, an honest fact earned by direct experience rather than a guess.

## Yearbook

Lands on [the Learner](persona_the_learner.md) at [the Stove](place_the_stove.md) and drives straight into [the Flinch](piece_the_flinch.md) through [Burning](process_burning.md).
It is the opening move of [The Hot Stove](plot_the_hot_stove.md), the single draw the whole misfit turns on.
