# Piece: The Untested

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The shunned option never sampled again, whose real value is never learned, so an impression that was only unlucky stands uncorrected forever.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md), carried by [Shunning](process_shunning.md), sitting exactly where the bad draw left it.
[The Burned](persona_the_burned.md) is what waits here; nothing that happens elsewhere revises it, because nothing here is tried again.

## Load Bearing

An option that gives a poor result is sampled less, and once it is no longer sampled, no further evidence about it can arrive; its standing is fixed at whatever the last draw showed, not because that draw was representative but because it was final (Denrell).
Guard against reading the untested option's low standing as a verdict: it is not a judgement reached but a judgement frozen, a single bad draw never revised because it was never revisited.

## Apparent

Settled business, a known quantity. It looks like the matter has already been decided, closed by evidence rather than left open by an evidence gathering that simply stopped.

## Yearbook

Left behind by [the Flinch](piece_the_flinch.md) through [Shunning](process_shunning.md), it is what [Never Asked Back](plot_never_asked_back.md) names and what [A Second Draw](plot_a_second_draw.md) would, if taken, finally test.
[The Explorer](persona_the_explorer.md) alone, on [Try It Again Anyway](plan_try_it_again_anyway.md), is who might still collect it.
