# Piece: The Flinch

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The rational avoidance that follows the bad draw, the sound decision not to repeat what hurt, which is also the decision that stops the sampling.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Stove](place_the_stove.md), carried by [Burning](process_burning.md) into [Shunning](process_shunning.md), the hand pulled back that hardens into a distance kept.
[The Learner](persona_the_learner.md) is who flinches; [the Burned](persona_the_burned.md) is who the flinch settles into.

## Load Bearing

Adaptive choice restricts the information an agent gathers: an option that yields a poor result is chosen less, and the reduced choosing is itself what prevents the poor result from ever being corrected (Denrell & March).
Guard against reading the flinch as cowardice or prejudice: pulling back from what burned you is rational, the sound response any careful learner would give. The flaw is not in the flinch but in what the flinch prevents, the second sample that would correct the error.

## Apparent

Prudence, plainly. It looks like nothing more than a sensible party declining to touch the same hot thing twice, caution earned and applied.

## Yearbook

Follows [the Bad Draw](piece_the_bad_draw.md) at [the Stove](place_the_stove.md), and through [Shunning](process_shunning.md) leaves [the Untested](piece_the_untested.md) behind at [the Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md).
It is the hinge of [The Hot Stove](plot_the_hot_stove.md), the reasonable step that opens onto [Never Asked Back](plot_never_asked_back.md).
