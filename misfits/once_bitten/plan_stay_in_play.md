# Plan: Stay in Play

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the favoured's seat](position_favoured.md), inside [Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Favoured](position_favoured.md)

## Direction

Take the trials your good first draw earns and let them run, since being sampled again is what turns a first impression into a fair estimate, whether it lifts you or lowers you to your true worth. A kind first result keeps you in the rotation, and the rotation is exactly what exposes a lucky high draw to correction. Know that this correction you receive is precisely what the burned option is denied, so your fair hearing is the measure of the other's silence and not a favour you were owed.

## Orders

([The Favoured](persona_the_favoured.md) stays sampled by a good first draw and through [Correcting](process_correcting.md) at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md) meets [the Second Look](piece_the_second_look.md), its early luck regressed toward the truth by the trials it earns.)

## Implementation

([Correcting](process_correcting.md) at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md) regresses a lucky high draw toward the option's true worth, because [the Second Look](piece_the_second_look.md) is the continued sampling a good first outcome earns, the correction the sealed low side never gets.)
