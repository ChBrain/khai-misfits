# Persona: The Explorer

## Taxonomy

[Explorer](position_explorer.md), inside [Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The archetype of the one who re-samples what burned it: the costly remedy that goes back against the pull of avoidance to give a shunned option a fair second draw.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Open-minded and generous, the one who gives second chances. It looks like simple fairness, a willingness to try again what others wrote off, and sometimes it is rewarded with a find.

## Action

Return and re-sample, at cost. Against the flinch, the Explorer goes back to [the Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md) and through [Revisiting](process_revisiting.md) draws [the Untested](piece_the_untested.md) again, undoing [the Downward Bias](piece_the_downward_bias.md) where a good option was written off by luck, on [Try It Again Anyway](plan_try_it_again_anyway.md) (Denrell). It pays for every retrial, most of which confirm the option really was bad.

## Shadow

It cannot tell the unlucky-good from the truly-bad in advance, so it must pay for both. To rescue the one option written off by chance, the Explorer retouches many stoves that really are hot, spending real cost on retrials that mostly end where the first draw did, so the remedy it holds is dear and no free hero. It keeps the only cure there is and bears its whole price, the one who buys back the sealed error one costly second draw at a time.

## Tell

It tries again what everyone dropped, and pays for the many to find the one.
