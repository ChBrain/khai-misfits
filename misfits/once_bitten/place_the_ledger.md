# Place: The Ledger

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). Where the Learner's censored record accumulates and its belief is read off, truncated on the low side; it hosts [Kept in Play](plot_kept_in_play.md), [The Books Don't Balance](plot_the_books_dont_balance.md), and [When a Bad Draw Means Bad](plot_when_a_bad_draw_means_bad.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The place every trial the Learner has run is entered and summed, a running account that only ever grows on one side.
The kept options sampled again and again, their luck regressing toward what they are actually worth, while the shunned ones show a single low mark and no more, an entry made once and never updated.

## Holds

The correcting, and the imbalance it cannot reach.
Here the record is totalled exactly as it was gathered, so the Ledger holds the point where the asymmetry becomes visible as a number: an option kept in play carries many entries, averaging toward the truth, while an option shunned after one bad draw carries a single entry that stands in for its whole worth.
Holds [the Second Look](piece_the_second_look.md), [the Censored Sample](piece_the_censored_sample.md), and [the Downward Bias](piece_the_downward_bias.md).

## Offers

An accurate read of whatever stayed in play.
The Ledger totals exactly what was sampled, honestly and without distortion, so for every option that kept drawing it offers a figure that converges, entry by entry, on the option's real worth, a correct account of everything the Learner did not flinch away from.

## Withheld

Any entry at all for what was dropped after one draw.
The Ledger can only total what was sampled, so for an option shunned after its first bad draw it withholds every entry a second, third, or hundredth trial would have added, and the single low mark stands, unrevised and unaveraged, as if it were the whole account.
