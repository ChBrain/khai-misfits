# Persona: The Burned

## Taxonomy

[Burned](position_burned.md), inside [Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The archetype of the option written off after a bad draw: a thing or a person, perhaps genuinely good, that drew badly once and is never sampled again, its worth frozen at that one result.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A poor option, fairly judged and rightly avoided, or so it looks from the outside. Nothing marks it as a good thing that merely had a bad day, because the one look on record is the bad one.

## Action

Wait unsampled, and seek a stranger. Set aside as [the Untested](piece_the_untested.md) at [the Cold Corner](place_the_cold_corner.md) by [Shunning](process_shunning.md), the Burned cannot compel the second sample that would clear it, so on [Somewhere I'm New](plan_somewhere_im_new.md) it seeks a learner who never saw it burn and might draw it afresh (Denrell). Its true worth stays unlearned until someone tries it again.

## Shadow

Its low standing is a verdict frozen, not a verdict reached. It may be better than its one bad draw, and it can do nothing from where it sits to prove it, because the proof requires a sampling the Learner will not give, so it carries a judgement it never earned and cannot appeal. It keeps whatever worth it truly has and loses the chance to show it, the option condemned by a draw that was never repeated.

## Tell

It has one bad mark and no second, and only a stranger will draw it again.
