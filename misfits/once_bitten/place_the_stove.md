# Place: The Stove

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). Where the bad draw lands and the Learner is burned, the single first touch that resolves, on the spot, into a hand pulled back for good; it hosts [The Hot Stove](plot_the_hot_stove.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The place a first touch is paid for in real pain, the lid reached for once and once only, the sting arriving before any second reading is possible.
[The Learner](persona_the_learner.md) stung and pulling its hand back, the reach that does not return, a single encounter already closing on itself.

## Holds

The burning, and the resolve it leaves behind.
Here the first outcome is drawn and felt in the same instant it is learned from, so the Stove holds the point where a bad draw becomes a rule: one touch, real pain, and a decision taken before there is any second touch to weigh it against, the whole verdict formed on a sample of one.
Holds [the Bad Draw](piece_the_bad_draw.md) and [the Flinch](piece_the_flinch.md).

## Offers

To a first touch, one real outcome, good or bad.
The Stove hands over exactly what a single trial can give, an honest result of that one draw, so it offers the Learner true information about this instant, and nothing withheld about what just happened here, the pain or the safety of this one touch reported straight.

## Withheld

Any sign of whether the burn was the rule or a fluke.
The Stove gives the outcome of one draw and no way to read it against a second, so it withholds the very thing that would settle whether this lid is hot every time or was hot only once, leaving the Learner a true report of a single instant mistaken, by necessity, for a verdict on the lid itself.
