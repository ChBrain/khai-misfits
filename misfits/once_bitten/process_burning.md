# Process: Burning

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). Borne at [the Stove](place_the_stove.md), precipitated by [the Learner](persona_the_learner.md) drawing [the Bad Draw](piece_the_bad_draw.md), in [The Hot Stove](plot_the_hot_stove.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Bad Draw](piece_the_bad_draw.md) landing on [the Learner](persona_the_learner.md), a single outcome arriving before there is any second draw to weigh it against.

## Direction

From a first, open trial toward a hand pulled back for good.
Before the touch the option is untested and open to any outcome; the instant the bad result lands, the Learner is already moving away from it, so burning runs straight from an open question to a closed one, with no pause in between for a second reading.

## Lever

One bad outcome, real or merely unlucky, is enough to trigger avoidance, because the pain of repeating it outweighs the value of testing whether it was a fluke (Denrell; Twain).
A single trial is cheap to read as a verdict and dear to re-run for confirmation, so the Learner closes the question on one draw rather than spend a second touch finding out whether the first was representative or a stroke of bad luck.
