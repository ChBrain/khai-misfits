# Piece: The Downward Bias

## Taxonomy

[Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The systematic undervaluation the asymmetry produces, and the risk aversion it breeds, variable options shunned though some are better than they first seemed.

## Load Bearing

Because variable options are the ones most likely to produce the early bad draw that ends sampling, adaptive learning drives an agent to avoid variability even with no intrinsic risk preference at all, so a measured risk aversion emerges from the sampling asymmetry itself rather than from any taste for safety (Denrell, 2007).
Guard against reading the resulting risk aversion as a stable taste or an intrinsic preference: it is manufactured by the sampling asymmetry, an agent with no fear of risk coming to shun variance because variance is what produces the bad draw that ends sampling. The bias bites hardest where outcomes are variable and the sampling is the agent's own choice, and softens where outcomes are close to certain, sampling is forced, or another's experience can be watched instead of lived.

## Apparent

Sound conservatism, a settled preference for the safe and proven. It looks like the Learner has simply learned to value certainty, a mature calibration rather than a distortion built into how it learns.
