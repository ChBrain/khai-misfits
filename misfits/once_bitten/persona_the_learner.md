# Persona: The Learner

## Taxonomy

[Learner](position_learner.md), inside [Once Bitten](play_once_bitten.md). The archetype of the one who learns by sampling: it tries options, keeps the ones that go well, and avoids the ones that go badly, forming its view from its own experience.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Prudent and fair-minded, the very picture of good judgement. It does not prejudge; it goes by what it has seen, keeps an open door to what has served it, and declines only to repeat what has hurt it, which sounds like nothing but sense.

## Action

Sample, keep, and avoid. The Learner tries an option and reads the outcome, and it carries [the Bad Draw](piece_the_bad_draw.md) through [Burning](process_burning.md) at [the Stove](place_the_stove.md) into [the Flinch](piece_the_flinch.md), never touching again what burned it, on [Go by What I've Seen](plan_go_by_what_ive_seen.md) (Denrell). Then it reads its belief off [the Censored Sample](piece_the_censored_sample.md) at [the Ledger](place_the_ledger.md).

## Shadow

Its avoidance stops the sampling that would correct it. Declining to repeat a bad experience is sound, and that very soundness is what leaves the low-side error unchecked, so the picture it forms is biased downward through no gloom of its own, only through what an adaptive sampler goes on to see (Denrell & March). It keeps its prudence and owes no cause for blame, the fair judge working from a sample its own rule truncates.

## Tell

It never prejudges, and it never asks the burned option back.
