# Process: Investing

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The floor being raised back to the old standard and the capacity funded ahead of any demand yet on record to justify it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A reading of the loop rather than a reading of the ledger: the claim that the fall is an effect of the withholding, not a verdict on it, argued in the one room where every current number says otherwise.

## Direction

Toward a standard held in visible breach for a while, since the old floor, reinstated ahead of the capacity to meet it, will be missed before it is met, and toward capital spent on a demand that has not yet shown itself.

## Lever

A conviction that the loop, once seen, cannot be answered on its own terms, set against every incentive in the room to wait one more quarter for a case that the waiting itself keeps from arriving.

## Echo

Either a floor held long enough for the demand it was built for to return, and a case made after the fact on evidence that did not exist when the decision was taken, or a correction abandoned for lack of the proof it was always going to lack until it was tried.
