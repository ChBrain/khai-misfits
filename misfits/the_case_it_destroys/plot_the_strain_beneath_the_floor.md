# Plot: The Strain Beneath the Floor

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Counter](place_the_counter.md), the load still climbing behind capacity that never moved, and the queue building again beneath a floor with nowhere further down to go this round without becoming a second, visible lapse.

## Action

[Straining](process_straining.md): [The Grower](persona_the_grower.md), holding [the grower's seat](position_grower.md), holds effort flat against a load it has no capacity to meet, while [The Patron](persona_the_patron.md), holding [the patron's seat](position_patron.md), waits longer than the floor still promises, for the first time since the floor moved.

## Stage

[The Grower](persona_the_grower.md), still on [Meet the Load](plan_meet_the_load.md), watches [the queue](piece_the_queue.md) lengthen past the number it only just set, with no capacity behind it to absorb the difference and no appetite, yet, to write the number down again.

## Tension

Nothing here is hidden, and that is exactly what makes it survivable for one more round.
The wait is plain to anyone standing in it, but the metric the boardroom reads still shows the floor as met more often than not, so the strain is carried by the people in the line rather than by any number a sponsor will see before the next brief is due.
Closes when the wait is being felt directly, by the people standing in it, whatever the floor still claims on paper.
