# Piece: The Standard

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The published service floor, the number the operation promises and is measured against.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Posted at [the counter](place_the_counter.md), where it is checked against the wait in the room. Referenced at [the boardroom](place_the_boardroom.md), where it is the line a brief is judged clean or not against.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is no line to be honest or dishonest about, no lapse to record and no relaxing of a pressure that was never fixed anywhere. With it, the whole mechanism has its hinge: lowering the standard is what turns a real shortfall into a clean report, and a clean report is what removes the one signal that would otherwise force the capacity question. It is load bearing because it is the single number the entire loop is read through, on the way down and on the way back up.

## Apparent

A figure on a wall or a page, a wait time, a service level, plain and countable, nothing more than a number until it moves.

## Yearbook

Set once, high, when the operation was smaller. Revised down when the load first outran it, quietly, honestly, without ceremony. Held there through [declining](process_declining.md), strained again through [straining](process_straining.md), and reinstated, in [investing](process_investing.md), at the old, higher figure, this time on a promise rather than a proof.
