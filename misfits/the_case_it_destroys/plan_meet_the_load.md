# Plan: Meet the Load

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the grower's seat](position_grower.md), inside [The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Grower](position_grower.md)

## Direction

Keep the published floor true to what the operation can actually do. Where growth outruns the old number, set a new one it can hold, and hold that one cleanly, round after round, rather than report a lapse against a promise the operation has already outgrown.

## Orders

([The Grower](persona_the_grower.md) revises the standard down when the load demands it, meets the new floor, and reads each clean report as the job done.)

## Implementation

([The Counter](place_the_counter.md); the [lowering](process_lowering.md) and [straining](process_straining.md) processes; [the standard](piece_the_standard.md) and [the queue](piece_the_queue.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] the floor is revised down to whatever the growth can currently hold
- [ ] the new floor is met cleanly, with nothing reading as a lapse
- [ ] the load climbs past even the revised floor
- [ ] a second revision is weighed against how visible a second lapse would be
