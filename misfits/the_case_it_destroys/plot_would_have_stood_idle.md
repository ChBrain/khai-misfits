# Plot: Would Have Stood Idle

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md), the falling demand laid for the first time beside the capacity that was never built, the two lines finally read together.

## Action

[Vindicating](process_vindicating.md): [The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md), holding [the sponsor's seat](position_sponsor.md), reads the falling demand as proof that the declined capacity would have gone unused, while [The Grower](persona_the_grower.md), holding [the grower's seat](position_grower.md), confirms that the floor, such as it now is, is being held easily.

## Stage

[The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md), still on [Wait for the Case](plan_wait_for_the_case.md), builds [the clean chart](piece_the_clean_chart.md) out of [the ledger](place_the_ledger.md) and every [empty seat](piece_the_empty_seat.md) it never counted as one, and the case, from where the sponsor sits, reads closed.

## Tension

The reading is sound, and that is the whole trap of it.
Nobody in the room is wrong: the demand really is down, the capacity really was never built, and the two lines really do sit together the way the chart shows them. What the chart cannot show is that the first line is made almost entirely of people the second line's absence sent away, so the proof and the cause are the same event, read back as if they were two.
Closes when the case is read as settled, and the underinvestment is taken, in the room, for the prudent call it always looked like.
