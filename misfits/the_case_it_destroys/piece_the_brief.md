# Piece: The Brief

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The capital request for added capacity, made and answered on whatever the current numbers show.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Tabled at [the boardroom](place_the_boardroom.md), read against the standard and the demand on record each time it is raised.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is no single moment where the decision to withhold capacity gets made and recorded, only a drift with no hinge to point at. With it, the loop has its decision point: the brief carries whatever case exists at the moment it is read, and because the standard has already been lowered and the demand has not yet fallen, the brief that gets declined is, on its own terms, correctly declined.

## Apparent

A short document, a request for capital, a table of current service and demand figures, nothing in it older than the last quarter.

## Yearbook

First raised while the load was still visibly outrunning the old floor, before [lowering](process_lowering.md) made the case for it disappear. Declined, cleanly, in [declining](process_declining.md). Not raised again until [vindicating](process_vindicating.md) has already closed the case the other way, and then only by [the advocate](persona_the_advocate.md), carrying no brief of its own at all.
