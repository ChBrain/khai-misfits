# Process: Straining

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The service continuing to slip once the load outgrows even the floor that was already lowered for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A capacity that was never added, meeting a load that never stopped climbing, so the room the last revision bought is used up and the wait starts to lengthen again, this time against a floor with nowhere further down to go without becoming visible as a second lapse.

## Direction

Toward a wait felt directly rather than caught by a metric, since setting the floor down a second time would be the second lapse in a row, the kind that does read as one.

## Lever

The same honest instinct that lowered the floor once, now weighed against how conspicuous lowering it twice would be, so the strain is held rather than recorded, and it is patrons rather than the ledger who feel it first.

## Echo

A queue that answers to no metric anyone is tracking, and a service that is worse in fact before it is worse on any page a sponsor will read.
