# Persona: The Advocate

## Taxonomy

[Advocate](position_advocate.md). Holds the case for building ahead of the numbers, and argues it with nothing on the ledger to back it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Insistent, a little out of step with the room. Reads the same falling chart everyone else reads and names a different cause for it.

## Action

Argues to raise the floor back to the old standard and fund the capacity before the demand that would justify it on paper exists. Asks the room to trust a reading the numbers themselves seem to deny.

## Shadow

Cannot make its case land as anything but a hunch, because the evidence that would prove it right is exactly the evidence its own argument says the room destroyed, so it cannot be won by the room's own rules.

## Tell

Says "it's down because we didn't build it, not the other way round" and says it again after being outvoted, because the numbers never once come around to agree.
