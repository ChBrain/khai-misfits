# Plot: Held in Breach

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Boardroom](place_the_boardroom.md), [the clean chart](piece_the_clean_chart.md) on the table, read by the room as closed.

## Action

[Investing](process_investing.md): [The Advocate](persona_the_advocate.md), holding [the advocate's seat](position_advocate.md), argues to raise the floor back to the old standard and fund the capacity ahead of any demand yet on record, while [The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md), holding [the sponsor's seat](position_sponsor.md), holds the chart against it, every current figure still reading the other way.

## Stage

[The Advocate](persona_the_advocate.md), having tabled [Invest Ahead of the Fall](plan_invest_ahead_of_the_fall.md), makes the case with no brief of its own and no chart on its side, only a reading of how [the clean chart](piece_the_clean_chart.md) came to look the way it does.

## Tension

The correction cannot be won on the evidence in the room, because the evidence in the room is exactly what the thing being corrected produced.
Holding the old standard again means failing it in public, on purpose, before a single number arrives to say the failure was worth it, and investing ahead of the fall means spending against a demand that, by the room's own numbers, is not there.
Closes when the floor is held in breach and the capital is spent against a case no chart yet supports, or the room keeps its reading, the chart wins, and the spend stays declined.
