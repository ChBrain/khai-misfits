# Persona: The Sponsor

## Taxonomy

[Sponsor](position_sponsor.md). Holds the purse, and funds only what the numbers in front of it can justify.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Careful, numerate, fair minded. Weighs the capital request against the service and demand data on the table and funds what the data supports, no more and no less.

## Action

Declines the capacity request when nothing in the brief is failing. Later reads the falling demand against the capacity never built and calls the reading proof.

## Shadow

Cannot see that the data it trusts was produced by the very decisions it is using the data to justify, so the case it keeps waiting for can never arrive: arriving would require the customers the last decision already sent away.

## Tell

Says "show me where this is failing" and means the metric on the page, not the queue no one still stands in.
