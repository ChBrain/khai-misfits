# Plot: Ordinary Prudence

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Boardroom](place_the_boardroom.md), the ledger clean against the revised floor, and a capital request for added capacity sitting on the table beside it.
Nothing in the room is currently failing.

## Action

[Declining](process_declining.md): [The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md), holding [the sponsor's seat](position_sponsor.md), reads [the brief](piece_the_brief.md) and finds no breach in it to fund against, while [The Grower](persona_the_grower.md), holding [the grower's seat](position_grower.md), stands down the case for expansion, since nothing the floor now measures is coming up short.

## Stage

[The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md), having tabled [Wait for the Case](plan_wait_for_the_case.md), declines the capital and files [the brief](piece_the_brief.md) as answered, the decision made clean on everything the room can currently see.

## Tension

Every figure in the room argues for exactly this decision, and every figure in the room was produced by the decision before it.
There is no imprudence to find: funding a request that nothing is failing would be the harder position to defend, not the easier one. But the numbers that make the no easy are the numbers the last lowering was built to produce, so the room is being prudent about evidence it has already, quietly, arranged not to have.
Closes when the capacity is not funded, and the budget it would have spent is preserved for another round.
