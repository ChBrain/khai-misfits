# Piece: The Empty Seat

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The custom a patron has already taken elsewhere, present in the room only as an absence.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the counter](place_the_counter.md), where it is never seen, only felt, in a queue one shorter than it would have been. Counted, at last, at [the ledger](place_the_ledger.md), where it appears as a number and nothing else.

## Load Bearing

Without it the falling demand has no origin, only a trend; with it, every point on the falling line is a patron who was, at some counter, made to wait too long, so the ledger's whole downward slope is built out of these, one at a time, each one indistinguishable, once counted, from a customer who simply never wanted to come.

## Apparent

Nothing, in the room. Only a shorter line than the day before, and later, a smaller number on a chart.

## Yearbook

Left behind, one at a time, through [leaving](process_leaving.md), never logged as a departure. Gathered, in total, into [the clean chart](piece_the_clean_chart.md), where it stops being an absence and starts being read as evidence.
