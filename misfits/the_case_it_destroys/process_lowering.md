# Process: Lowering

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The published standard being revised down to what the growing operation can actually hold.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A load that has outrun the capacity serving it, so the old floor is no longer being met and something has to give: the floor, since the capacity cannot move as fast as the load did.

## Direction

Toward a standard that always reads as current. Each revision resets the floor to whatever the operation can do right now, so the record stays honest and the gap it is honest about stays exactly where it was.

## Lever

The plain fact that a growing operation genuinely cannot hold its old standard, set against the plain fact that lowering it removes the only reading that would call the gap a failure.

## Echo

A floor being met again, cleanly, and a history quietly rewritten to match: what was a lapse a season ago is now simply the going rate, and no one downstream will read it as anything else.
