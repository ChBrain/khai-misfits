# Persona: The Grower

## Taxonomy

[Grower](position_grower.md). Holds the operation as it grows, and keeps the published floor honest to what the growth can actually carry.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Busy, capable, unbothered. Watches the load climb and adjusts what can be adjusted, the promise, not yet the plant. No panic in it, only the steady work of keeping the numbers true to what is happening on the floor.

## Action

Revises the published standard down when the old one can no longer be held. Meets the new floor cleanly, round after round, and reports every metric it now owns as met.

## Shadow

Cannot see that meeting the floor and building the floor are two different jobs, and that doing the first one well removes the only signal that would have forced the second. Reads a clean report as a job done, not as a debt deferred.

## Tell

Says "we are holding the standard" and means the one revised last quarter, not the one the patrons still remember.
