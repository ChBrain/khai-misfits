# Position: Patron

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The seat that carries the demand the operation is built to serve, and answers a slipping wait with its feet.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The custom, and the freedom to withdraw it. No standing to change the floor, and no channel that reads a quiet departure as anything but softening demand.

## Orders

Return while the wait is bearable. Stop, without announcement, the moment it is not.

## Loses

Nothing it can be charged for and nothing it announces, so the seat's whole exit is invisible to the very ledger it will later appear on, as a number and not as a reason.

## Drives

Toward an absence that reads, downstream, as demand simply not having been there, rather than as demand that left.
