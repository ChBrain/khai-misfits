# Process: Leaving

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). Patrons ceasing to return, quietly, once the wait outlasts what brought them back the last time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A strain long enough, and often enough, that the ordinary patience it draws on runs out, with no lapse on record to complain about and no channel built to catch an absence instead of a complaint.

## Direction

Toward a thinning load that looks, from the operation's side, exactly like room opening up, since nothing marks a departure as caused rather than simply demand easing off.

## Lever

The patron's plain right to take its custom wherever the wait is shorter, set against the operation's total blindness to why the numbers it watches are moving.

## Echo

A ledger reading lighter, and a queue reading shorter, both true, and both about to be read as the demand having been softer all along.
