# Place: The Counter

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). Where the service is actually delivered, and whatever floor currently holds is felt directly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A line of patrons and whatever standard is meeting them today: fast if the floor is high enough for the load, slower the moment it is not. Nothing hidden here, only whatever the operation can currently do, in full view.

## Holds

The queue, growing and shrinking with the gap between load and capacity. The published standard, whatever it currently reads, posted where anyone waiting can measure the wait against it.

## Offers

A clean reading of exactly how the operation is doing right now, to anyone willing to stand in the line and watch it.

## Withheld

Any record of how long the wait used to be. The counter shows only the present floor and the present queue, and withholds the history that would let anyone see the floor has already moved once, and is about to be asked to move again.
