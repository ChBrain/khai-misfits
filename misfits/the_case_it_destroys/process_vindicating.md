# Process: Vindicating

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The falling demand being read against the capacity never built, and the reading landing as proof the capacity would have stood idle.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A ledger with two lines that finally sit side by side, demand down and capacity flat, and nothing in the room to say the first caused the second rather than confirmed the wisdom of it.

## Direction

Toward a case considered closed. The absence of the customers the decision drove away is read as the absence of customers who were never coming, since the only record in the room is the one the decision produced.

## Lever

A reading that is sound on its own terms, every figure real and every inference ordinary, set against the fact that the figures are themselves the decision's own output, so the soundness and the error occupy the same numbers.

## Echo

A decision that now looks vindicated rather than merely made, the loop's whole history compressed into a chart that shows only the last two points and calls that proof.
