# Persona: The Patron

## Taxonomy

[Patron](position_patron.md). Holds the demand the whole operation is built to serve, and answers a worsening wait the only way most people do.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Ordinary, unhurried, forgiving up to a point. Keeps coming while the wait is bearable and says nothing about it either way.

## Action

Waits, once, twice, longer than expected, and then simply does not come back. Takes its custom elsewhere without a word to anyone who could log it.

## Shadow

Cannot see that its own quiet departure is about to be read, downstream, as evidence that the room it left was never wanted in the first place.

## Tell

Never complains. The absence is the only complaint it ever files, and it reads to everyone left behind as silence, not as a message.
