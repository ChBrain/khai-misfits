# Plot: Outgrowing the Floor

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Counter](place_the_counter.md), the queue lengthening past what the current floor can clear, the load arriving faster than the operation was ever built to hold.
The wait is real and growing, and nothing behind the counter has changed to meet it.

## Action

[Lowering](process_lowering.md): [The Grower](persona_the_grower.md), holding [the grower's seat](position_grower.md), revises the published floor down to what the operation can actually carry, while [The Patron](persona_the_patron.md), holding [the patron's seat](position_patron.md), is met at the new number without ever having seen the old one written down.

## Stage

[The Grower](persona_the_grower.md), having tabled [Meet the Load](plan_meet_the_load.md), records [the standard](piece_the_standard.md) at the level the growth can actually sustain, and [the queue](piece_the_queue.md) clears back to a length the new floor calls normal.

## Tension

The lowering is the honest thing to do, and it is also the one move that removes the evidence anyone would need to ask for more.
There is no dishonesty in the record: the old floor truly cannot be held, and writing down the floor that can be held is a fair account of where the operation stands. But a floor that is always set to whatever is currently true can never itself say build more, so the same honesty that keeps the record clean is what quietly banks the shortfall for later, unread by the one number built to catch it.
Closes when the new floor is being met, and nothing in the operation's own numbers reads as a lapse anymore.
