# Place: The Boardroom

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). Where the capacity decision is made and remade, on whatever brief the room has been given.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A request for capital beside the numbers meant to justify it: demand, service, cost, laid out clean and current, never carrying the history of how they got that way.

## Holds

The brief, the budget, the chart, whichever one the room is currently reading. Whatever was decided last round, filed and not reopened unless the numbers move.

## Offers

A decision that will be right on everything the room can see, every time it is made, because the room only ever sees what the current numbers show it.

## Withheld

The cost of the last decision, since that cost shows up, if it shows up at all, as softer numbers next round, indistinguishable in the room from demand that was simply never there.
