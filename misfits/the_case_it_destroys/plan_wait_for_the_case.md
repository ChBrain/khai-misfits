# Plan: Wait for the Case

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the sponsor's seat](position_sponsor.md), inside [The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Sponsor](position_sponsor.md)

## Direction

Fund capacity only when the numbers in front of the seat require it. Decline what a clean brief does not support, and revisit the question each round on whatever the freshest figures show, never on the history behind them.

## Orders

([The Sponsor](persona_the_sponsor.md) reads each brief against the current standard and demand, declines what shows no breach, and reads the falling demand, when it comes, as confirmation rather than consequence.)

## Implementation

([The Boardroom](place_the_boardroom.md) and [The Ledger](place_the_ledger.md); the [declining](process_declining.md) and [vindicating](process_vindicating.md) processes; [the brief](piece_the_brief.md) read against the standard, and [the clean chart](piece_the_clean_chart.md) built from the ledger and the empty seat.)

## Targets

- [ ] the capital request is declined for lack of a failing number
- [ ] the decision is remade each round on the freshest figures alone
- [ ] the falling demand is read against the flat capacity
- [ ] the reading closes the case in the decision's own favor
