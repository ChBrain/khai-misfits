# Play: The Case It Destroys

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Case It Destroys.
A service is growing faster than anyone built room for, and the floor it promises quietly drops to the level the growth can actually hold: not a lapse, a fair record of what is true now. Nobody is failing the new floor, so the pressure to add capacity, which only ever came from someone failing it, eases off, and the capacity stays exactly what it was. The load keeps climbing behind a floor that has already given once, the wait grows again, and this time it is felt rather than fixed by a second honest correction. Some who feel it stop coming back, quietly, without a complaint to file, because there is no lapse to complain about, only an absence. The falling numbers land on the ledger beside a capacity that was never built, and read there, together, they say only one thing: the room would have stood empty anyway. The case for building it was made of exactly the people the not building sent away, so the longer it waits the better it looks, right up until it does not.

## Arc

The bend: the decision to withhold capacity is judged by demand, and demand is what the withheld capacity itself suppresses, so the longer the withholding runs the more innocent it looks, each stage produced honestly by the one before it and each one erasing the evidence that would have overturned it.
Reordering loses this. The floor cannot lower before growth outpaces it. The pressure cannot ease before nobody is failing the lower floor. The strain cannot resume before the pressure that would have fixed it is gone. The leaving cannot read as proof before the capacity was withheld on the leaving's account. Every stage manufactures the premise of the next, and the case against the whole sequence is only ever findable in the sequence, never in any single reading inside it.
It closes on the one move built against every number the sequence produced: holding an old standard in visible breach of it, and spending on a demand no chart in the room yet supports.

## Triggers

**[Outgrowing the Floor](plot_outgrowing_the_floor.md)**
Cue: the queue at the counter lengthening past what the current floor can clear, growth arriving faster than the operation was built for. The Grower lowers the published standard to what the operation can actually hold, and the Patron is met there without ever seeing the old number. Closes when the new floor is being met and nothing reads as a lapse anymore.

**[Ordinary Prudence](plot_ordinary_prudence.md)**
Cue: the ledger clean against the revised floor, a capital request for capacity on the table beside it. The Sponsor reads the brief, finds no breach to fund against, and declines it; the Grower stands the case for expansion down. Closes when the capacity is not funded and the budget is preserved for another round.

**[The Strain Beneath the Floor](plot_the_strain_beneath_the_floor.md)**
Cue: the load still climbing behind capacity that never moved, the queue building again beneath a floor with nowhere further down to go without a second, visible lapse. The Grower holds effort flat; the Patron waits longer than the floor still promises. Closes when the wait is felt directly by the people standing in it, whatever the floor still claims on paper.

**[Quietly Elsewhere](plot_quietly_elsewhere.md)**
Cue: the wait now routine, one patron among many not returning, no complaint filed. The Patron takes its custom elsewhere without a word; the Grower reads the thinning line as room opening up. Closes when the falling numbers reach the ledger, read only as demand, never as an effect of the floor that drove it away.

**[Would Have Stood Idle](plot_would_have_stood_idle.md)**
Cue: the ledger laying the falling demand beside the capacity never built, the two lines read together for the first time. The Sponsor reads the fall as proof the capacity would have gone unused, and builds the clean chart from the ledger and the empty seats it never counted as one. Closes when the case is read as settled, and the underinvestment is taken, in the room, for the prudent call it always looked like.

**[Held in Breach](plot_held_in_breach.md)**
Cue: the clean chart on the table, read by the room as closed. The Advocate argues to raise the floor back to the old standard and fund the capacity ahead of any demand yet on record; the Sponsor holds the chart against it. Closes when the floor is held in breach and the capital is spent against a case no chart yet supports, or the room keeps its reading and the spend stays declined.

## Stakes

Whether a decision that starves its own evidence can ever be caught before the numbers run out, and whether the case for investing can be made on anything other than the very departures the underinvestment causes.
Every plot moves it. The floor's honest fall narrows what could ever count as proof. The declined capital banks that narrowing. The strain beneath the new floor spends the last of the slack. The quiet departures hand the loop its clean numbers. The vindication spends them. And the one plot that tries to break the loop must pay for it in a breach with nothing on the ledger yet to justify it.
The production cannot make the case appear in the numbers, because the numbers are exactly what the mechanism has already eaten. It can only show that every reading along the way was sound, and that the single move which frees the operation, holding an old standard in visible breach of it and investing on a reading no chart yet supports, is bought with the one thing every other move in the play was made to avoid: being wrong on paper, for a while, on purpose.
