# Pitch: Nobody Failed

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Careful, current, reasonable. Every reading taken in the room is a fair one, made on the numbers actually in front of whoever is reading them, and nobody is ever asked to defend a number they do not have.

## Undertow

Under the reasonableness, a set of numbers each quietly produced by the reading before it: the floor honestly lowered, the brief honestly clean, the demand honestly thinner, each one true and each one the product of the last true thing, so the case that would catch the pattern needs a memory none of the individual readings are built to carry.

## Nerve

The unease of watching a string of sound decisions add up to a result none of them, alone, could be blamed for, and the suspicion, never quite provable in the room, that the cleanliness itself is the tell.

## Echo

After the run: a chart that reads as vindication to anyone who trusts it, and a case, for anyone who does not, that can only be made by doubting every figure the room was ever shown, on the strength of nothing but the shape of how they got there.
