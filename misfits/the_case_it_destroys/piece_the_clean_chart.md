# Piece: The Clean Chart

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The report that lays the falling demand beside the capacity never built and reads the pairing as proof.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Presented at [the boardroom](place_the_boardroom.md), built from [the ledger](place_the_ledger.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it the loop has no closing move, only a slow decline with no one drawing the conclusion from it. With it, the whole mechanism completes itself: the chart takes exactly the customers the underinvestment drove away and reads their absence as the reason the investment was never needed, so the decision is not merely repeated, it is confirmed, by the one kind of evidence the decision itself was capable of producing.

## Apparent

Two lines on a graph, demand and capacity, drifting apart in a way that looks, to anyone reading only the chart, like capacity was simply ahead of a need that never came.

## Yearbook

Assembled in [vindicating](process_vindicating.md) from every quarter's [ledger](place_the_ledger.md) line and every [empty seat](piece_the_empty_seat.md) the queue never showed. Held up, at last, against [the advocate](persona_the_advocate.md), who has no chart of its own to answer it with, only a different reading of the one on the table, argued through [investing](process_investing.md).
