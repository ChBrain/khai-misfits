# Piece: The Queue

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The wait itself, the visible gap between the load arriving and the capacity ready to meet it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the counter](place_the_counter.md), lengthening and shortening as the floor and the load move against each other.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is nothing for a floor to be measured against and no wait for a patron to grow tired of: it is the one thing in the whole structure that every persona reads differently and none reads wrong. With it, the gap between what is promised and what is delivered has a shape anyone standing in it can feel, whether or not it shows up anywhere the sponsor is looking.

## Apparent

A line of people, a hold time, a backlog, whatever form the wait takes in the operation it sits in.

## Yearbook

Long, once, before [lowering](process_lowering.md) reset what counted as long. Short again after the floor moved. Long a second time in [straining](process_straining.md), this round with nowhere left to lower to without the lapse becoming impossible to miss, and thinner, at the last, only because [leaving](process_leaving.md) has started to remove the people who would have been standing in it.
