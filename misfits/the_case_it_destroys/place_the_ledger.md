# Place: The Ledger

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). Where demand and capacity are tallied against each other, and where the case, for or against investing, is read off the total.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two lines over time: what came through the door, and what was ever built to hold it. Wherever they sit, the ledger shows them exactly as they are.

## Holds

Every quarter's total, kept in order, available to be read forward as a build-up or backward as a verdict, with nothing in the arrangement to say which reading is the honest one.

## Offers

A clean comparison, demand against capacity, to anyone who wants to know how the operation is doing, this quarter, on its own terms.

## Withheld

Any line that shows why demand moved. The ledger holds the numbers and not their causes, so a fall read here looks exactly like softening interest and exactly like a consequence, and it withholds the one thing that would tell them apart.
