# Plot: Quietly Elsewhere

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Counter](place_the_counter.md), the wait now routine, and one patron among many not returning, with no complaint filed and nothing behind the counter built to notice an absence.

## Action

[Leaving](process_leaving.md): [The Patron](persona_the_patron.md), holding [the patron's seat](position_patron.md), takes its custom elsewhere without a word, while [The Grower](persona_the_grower.md), holding [the grower's seat](position_grower.md), reads the thinning line as room finally opening up.

## Stage

[The Patron](persona_the_patron.md), having tabled [Go Elsewhere](plan_go_elsewhere.md), leaves [the empty seat](piece_the_empty_seat.md) behind, one more absence with nothing behind the counter marking it as caused by anything at all.

## Tension

The departure and the relief arrive in the same moment, read by two different people as two different things.
The patron leaves because the wait finally cost more than the service was worth; the grower reads the shorter queue as slack returning to the system, and nothing at the counter is built to tell one story from the other.
Closes when the falling numbers reach [the ledger](place_the_ledger.md), where they will be read only as demand, and never as an effect of the floor that drove it away.
