# Position: Sponsor

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The seat that funds capacity only on a case the current numbers make.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The capital, and the standing to release or withhold it. No visibility into what the numbers would show under an older, unrevised floor.

## Orders

Fund what the brief supports. Decline what it does not, and revisit the question when the numbers change.

## Loses

Any case built on a floor already revised down, and any case built on demand already thinned by the last decline, so each round the seat has less to found a yes on than the round before.

## Drives

Toward a decision that is right on every page in front of it, and toward a ledger that, read back, seems to confirm it, because the seat never sees the demand its own no was built to suppress.
