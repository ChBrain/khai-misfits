# Process: Declining

## Taxonomy

[The Case It Destroys](play_the_case_it_destroys.md). The capacity request being turned down for lack of a failing number to justify it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A brief with nothing in breach: the revised floor is being met, the demand on record looks steady, and the capital sits beside a request the current numbers do not require.

## Direction

Toward capital undeployed and a budget preserved, each round decided again on whatever the freshest numbers show, none of which yet carry the cost of the last decline.

## Lever

The genuine prudence of not spending against a problem the data does not show, set against the fact that the data cannot show a problem the last decline is already working to remove.

## Echo

A capacity gap held exactly where it was, now backed by a decision that will read, later, as the reasonable one, because reasonable is what it was, on everything the room could see.
