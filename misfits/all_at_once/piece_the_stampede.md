# Piece: The Stampede

## Taxonomy

[All at Once](play_all_at_once.md). The emergent flood, the dogpile, of simultaneous recomputations arriving on the backend together.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Produced by [Flooding](process_flooding.md) at [The Window](place_the_window.md), the brief span after [The Expiry](piece_the_expiry.md) where every [Miss](piece_the_miss.md) lands together; central to the beat the play stages as Everyone Misses.

## Load Bearing

The Stampede is not a new action, it is the sum of the same one: every recompute inside it is [The Miss](piece_the_miss.md) done correctly, and the Stampede is nothing more than many of those correct misses reaching the backend inside the same brief window (Vattani, Chierichetti, and Lowenstein; the caching instance of the older thundering herd family). HEDGE: the name marks the shape of the arrival, not a defect in any single arrival within it. Guard against searching the Stampede for a bad actor; it has none, it is coincidence at scale, produced by popularity and a shared clock and nothing else.

## Apparent

Looks, from the backend's side, indistinguishable from an attack or a sudden burst of real demand, an unexplained wall of identical requests arriving together. The resemblance misleads: an attack is driven by intent and ordinary demand grows, but the Stampede is manufactured by the system's own cache, a flood with a known cause and a known clock, that subsides on its own the moment the value is recomputed and back in place.
