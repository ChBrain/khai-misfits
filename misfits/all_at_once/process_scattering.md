# Process: Scattering

## Taxonomy

[All at Once](play_all_at_once.md). Run by [The Warden](persona_the_warden.md) on [Break the Sync](plan_break_the_sync.md), at [The Key](place_the_key.md) and [The Front](place_the_front.md), in [Scatter the Herd](plot_scatter_the_herd.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A key known in advance to be popular enough that its lapse would draw a crowd.
Scattering is never a reaction to a flood already underway; it is built into the key and the front before any expiry, or it does nothing.

## Direction

Toward recomputations that no longer land in the same instant: spread earlier across many separate moments, or collapsed down to the single one that was strictly necessary.
Probabilistic early recomputation moves individual refreshes back before the cliff (Vattani, Chierichetti, and Lowenstein); leases and coalescing hold every request but one back until that one has finished (Nishtala and colleagues); both directions end at the same place, a backend that never sees more than it can answer.

## Lever

A little staleness, a wait, or an early refresh, spent deliberately to buy de-synchronization (Vattani, Chierichetti, and Lowenstein; Nishtala and colleagues).
Nothing here makes the value cheaper to compute; the lever only changes when and by whom the computing happens, trading a small, chosen cost in the ordinary case for the absence of a large, unchosen one in the rare case.

## Echo

[The Jitter](piece_the_jitter.md): a crowd that no longer arrives together.
The window at [The Window](place_the_window.md) still opens on every lapse, but scattering leaves it holding far fewer coincident misses, one recompute instead of a thousand, or a thousand refreshes spread wide enough that the backend never meets more than a handful at once.
