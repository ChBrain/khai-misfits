# Position: Warden

## Taxonomy

[All at Once](play_all_at_once.md). The seat of the remedy discipline built into the cache ahead of time, which breaks the coincidence rather than the correctness, scattering the refreshes off the shared cliff or collapsing the misses to a single recompute.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A watch on the clock and a lever on when the work lands: a rule that can refresh a value before it expires, or let one request recompute while the rest wait, or spread the expiries so keys do not fall due together.
A precise aim: it does not make the value cheaper to compute, it only keeps the correct recomputations from all landing at once, which is the whole of what the stampede needs.
No power after the instant, because breaking a coincidence must be done before it happens, so it holds only what it was built in to hold ahead of time.

## Orders

Break the sync.
Scatter the refreshes off the shared cliff, letting one caller refresh early with a chance that climbs as expiry nears, or collapse the misses by letting a single request recompute while the rest briefly wait.
Build it in before the hot key and the shared expiry meet, because a coincidence can only be prevented in advance, never cured once it lands.

## Loses

The look of thrift, and a little of something in every ordinary hour.
Because it buys de-synchronization with a value served a little stale, a request made to wait, or a refresh done early, the seat pays a standing cost against a spike that comes rarely, and does nothing at all for a cache that shipped without it.
It gives up a little in the common case to spare the backend in the rare one, and protects only what it was designed into ahead of time.

## Drives

Toward the remedy that is neither obvious nor free,
breaking the coincidence and not the correctness because the flood is made of correct actions and only their timing is left to change,
so the seat that spends a little staleness or waiting is the one that hands the backend a trickle where a spike would have formed, effective only where it was foreseen.
