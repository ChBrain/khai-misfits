# Process: Flooding

## Taxonomy

[All at Once](play_all_at_once.md). Run by [The Request](persona_the_request.md) on [Recompute on Miss](plan_recompute_on_miss.md), at [The Window](place_the_window.md), in [Everyone Misses](plot_everyone_misses.md) and [The Backend Buckles](plot_the_backend_buckles.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[The Miss](piece_the_miss.md), taken independently by every request that arrives inside [The Window](place_the_window.md).
Each initiation is separately correct and separately unaware of the others; nothing coordinates the start, the shared expiry already did that work upstream.

## Direction

Toward [The Backend](persona_the_backend.md), all at once, from every direction the popularity of the key was drawing traffic from.
What arrives one at a time under ordinary load arrives as a single burst here, because the misses that triggered it were never spread across time to begin with.

## Lever

The size of the crowd the key's own popularity built (Vattani, Chierichetti, and Lowenstein; Nishtala and colleagues), now converted from an asset into a load.
The more valuable the caching was, the larger the crowd it was quietly serving, and the larger the flood the moment that crowd all misses together; the lever is popularity itself, turned against the backend it was sparing.

## Echo

[The Stampede](piece_the_stampede.md) and [The Overload](piece_the_overload.md): a spike the backend may or may not absorb.
HEDGE: if the backend cannot absorb the burst, the failure can cascade into a persistent overload of its own, but that further collapse is flooding composing with a separate mechanism, not flooding itself, which ends the moment the recomputations resolve and [The Key](place_the_key.md) is repopulated.
