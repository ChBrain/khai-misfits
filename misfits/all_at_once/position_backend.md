# Position: Backend

## Taxonomy

[All at Once](play_all_at_once.md). The seat of the expensive origin the cache was placed to shield, which computes the value from scratch and answers every request handed to it, struck by the whole coincident crowd at once when the cache expires.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The value at its source, and a real cost to produce it: it computes the answer from scratch and serves whatever requests reach it, as they come.
An honest capacity: it is sized for the ordinary trickle of misses the cache leaves it, which it handles without strain, that being the whole point of the cache.
No power to refuse or reshape the load it is handed, so it meets a synchronized crowd at full width, having no way to spread a burst that arrives already together.

## Orders

Answer every call.
Compute the value for each request that reaches you, and serve them all as they come, one at a time or many.
Rely on the cache to keep the trickle a trickle, because that is what your capacity was sized for, and answer whatever the cache lets through.

## Loses

Its footing the instant the trickle becomes a flood.
Because it answers every call and cannot spread a burst that arrives synchronized, the seat is struck by the whole undamped crowd at the moment the cache stops shielding it, and buckles under a load its average capacity could have carried spread out.
It loses nothing while the cache holds and everything the instant it expires, wronged by no request and struck by all of them at once.

## Drives

Toward the spared origin that the sparing itself delivers to the spike,
sized for a trickle and handed the whole crowd in one instant, hardest hit where it was most shielded,
so the seat the cache exists to protect is the seat the cache's one expiry exposes, buckling on a burst and not on its average load.
