## Load Bearing

It makes the decision concrete, because what is being set aside is not a possibility but a count.
The party's support is not a projection or a fear: it is a number of ballots already marked by people who were entitled to mark them, for reasons of their own, most of which have nothing to do with the clause. That is what turns the question from doctrine into a decision with a named cost, and it is also what makes the timing impossible, since the clause is designed to act before the programme is enacted and the mandate exists precisely because it has not been.
