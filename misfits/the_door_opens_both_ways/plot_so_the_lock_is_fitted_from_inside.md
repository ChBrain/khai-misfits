# Plot: So the Lock Is Fitted From Inside

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The memory of a procedure used, lawfully, to end the order it belonged to.

## Action

[Fitting the lock](process_fitting_the_lock.md): [the framer](persona_the_framer.md) places [the lock](piece_the_lock.md) in [the text](place_the_text.md), naming what amendment may not reach.

## Stage

A core the procedure cannot touch, written by the people who wrote the procedure.

## Tension

The clause is not theoretical and names the specific route it closes, which is what makes it hard to argue against on its own terms.
It is drafted by parties who watched the door used, and it is aimed at that use rather than at a class of ideas. Whatever is said against it afterwards, it is answering something that happened. That is why the bind cannot be resolved by pointing out that entrenchment is illiberal: everybody involved already knows, and wrote it anyway.
