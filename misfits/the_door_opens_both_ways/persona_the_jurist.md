# Persona: The Jurist

## Taxonomy

[Jurist](position_jurist.md). The one who names the bind and cannot dissolve it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A body of scholarship on how a constitutional order defends itself, and a shelf of cases where it tried.

## Action

Names the structure.
Sets out the tests, the thresholds and the proportionality, and hands back a decision that still has to be taken.

## Shadow

Describes the bind exactly, which is the most that description does.
The literature is precise and honest: it says plainly that a democracy defending itself must use means it would otherwise condemn, and it works hard on when that is warranted, how imminent the danger must be, how narrow the measure. All of that is real and improves the decision. What none of it produces is a version of the decision that costs nothing, because the cost is in the structure rather than in the doctrine, and a better test tells the seat when to pay rather than how to avoid paying.

## Tell

Makes the choice better informed and no less a choice.
