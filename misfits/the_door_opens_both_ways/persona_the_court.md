# Persona: The Court

## Taxonomy

[Court](position_court.md). The one that cannot abstain.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An application to dissolve a lawful party, an entrenched clause, and a hearing date.

## Action

Reads the programme.
Decides, knowing that not deciding is also a decision.

## Shadow

Has two available rulings and no neutral one.
Dissolving the party removes an organisation that has broken no law and voids the choice of everybody who voted for it, which is the thing courts exist to prevent. Declining removes the entrenchment's whole point, since a clause that can only be applied after the programme is enacted is a clause that arrives too late by construction. The seat is not weighing law against politics: it is applying a text that requires it to act on an intention, and the requirement is deliberate, because waiting for the act was what the clause was written to avoid.

## Tell

Rules either way and cannot rule neutrally.
