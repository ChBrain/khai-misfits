# Plot: The Door Has to Be There

## Taxonomy

[The Door Opens Both Ways](play_the_door_opens_both_ways.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A constitution being drafted, and the question of how it may later be changed.

## Action

[Writing the door](process_writing_the_door.md): [the framer](persona_the_framer.md), holding [Framer](position_framer.md), sets out [the door](piece_the_door.md) at [the text](place_the_text.md).

## Stage

An amendment procedure, in a document that would not survive without one.

## Tension

A text that cannot be amended governs the living by the settled preferences of the dead, which is why nobody proposes one.
It could not absorb anything its authors failed to foresee, and it would hold its authority by enforcement rather than by consent. So the procedure is not a concession to weakness but a condition of the thing lasting at all, and every serious drafter writes one.
