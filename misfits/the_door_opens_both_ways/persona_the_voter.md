# Persona: The Voter

## Taxonomy

[Voter](position_voter.md). The one whose vote is at stake in both directions.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A ballot paper, an ordinary morning, and a preference formed for ordinary reasons.

## Action

Votes.
Finds out afterwards whether the vote produced a government.

## Shadow

Exercises the franchise, and the franchise is what is being spent either way.
The seat has done nothing but the thing the arrangement asks of it. If the party is dissolved, a lawful choice made by millions is set aside by an institution whose purpose is to protect that choice. If the party is not dissolved and the programme is carried out, the same seat loses the ability to change its mind at the next election, which is the whole of what a vote is for. There is no version in which the franchise is untouched, and the seat is not the party's programme and cannot be held to it.

## Tell

Casts the vote the arrangement asked for, and pays whichever way it lands.
