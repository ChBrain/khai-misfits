# Plan: Leave It Open to Change

## Taxonomy

## Owner

- Owner: [The Framer](position_framer.md)

## Direction

Write a constitution that later generations can alter without breaking it.

## Orders

(Set out who may propose, what majority is needed, and what procedure is followed.)

## Implementation

([Writing the door](process_writing_the_door.md); [the text](place_the_text.md).)

## Targets

- [ ] a text that cannot be amended governs the living by the settled preferences of the dead
- [ ] it cannot absorb anything its authors did not foresee, and loses the consent it depends on
- [ ] so every serious drafter provides a procedure, and none proposes doing without one
- [ ] and the procedure that lets the text be improved is the same procedure that can be used against it
