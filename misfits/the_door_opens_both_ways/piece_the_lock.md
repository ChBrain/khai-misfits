## Load Bearing

It closes the route out and does so by binding every later generation, which is the objection it cannot answer.
The clause is not speculative: it is written by people who watched an amendment procedure used to end the order it belonged to, and it names the route it is closing. What it also does is remove a question from every future majority, including one that is unanimous, well informed and acting in good faith, so the argument that a constitution should not govern the living by the preferences of the dead applies to it exactly. The defence against being ended by procedure is a limit that no procedure can lift.

## Apparent

A guarantee. The part of the constitution that cannot be taken away.
