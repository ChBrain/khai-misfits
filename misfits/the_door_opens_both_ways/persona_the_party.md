# Persona: The Party

## Taxonomy

[Party](position_party.md). The one that uses the procedure, and is the reason for the lock.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A registered organisation, a published programme, candidates on the ballot, and a share of the vote that is rising.

## Action

Stands. Campaigns. Wins seats, lawfully, in numbers nobody disputes.

## Shadow

Breaks no rule and is the case the entrenchment was written against.
Everything the seat does is what the arrangement invites: forming, publishing a programme, persuading people and standing for election are not merely permitted but are the activity the whole apparatus exists to make possible. The programme is the difficulty, since carrying it out would use the amendment procedure to remove the amendment procedure, and there is no moment at which the seat is doing anything other than what a party does. So the objection cannot be to conduct and has to be to a programme that has not yet been enacted.

## Tell

Does exactly what the arrangement invites, toward an end the arrangement cannot survive.
