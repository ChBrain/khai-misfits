# Plot: And the Lock Binds Whoever Comes After

## Taxonomy

[The Door Opens Both Ways](play_the_door_opens_both_ways.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A later generation, unanimous, well informed, and wanting the clause revised.

## Action

[The Lock](piece_the_lock.md) is read against the objection that produced [the door](piece_the_door.md): a limit no procedure can lift, standing over every future majority.

## Stage

The argument for amendability, turned against the article that protects amendability.

## Tension

The defence commits the objection it was built to answer, which is what makes this a bind and not a policy question.
The case for an amendment procedure is that the dead should not govern the living. An entrenched clause is the dead governing the living, at one point, on purpose. Both statements are true at once and neither party is confused: the framers knew, the critics are right, and no drafting removes it, because a protection that a later majority can lift is not a protection against a later majority.
