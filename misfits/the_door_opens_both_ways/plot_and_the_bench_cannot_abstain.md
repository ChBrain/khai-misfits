# Plot: And the Bench Cannot Abstain

## Taxonomy

[The Door Opens Both Ways](play_the_door_opens_both_ways.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An application to dissolve, a hearing date, and a clause that anticipated this.

## Action

[Ruling on the party](process_ruling_on_the_party.md): [the court](persona_the_court.md), holding [Court](position_court.md), weighs [the mandate](piece_the_mandate.md) at [the bench](place_the_bench.md).

## Stage

A reasoned judgment that voids a lawful choice or forgoes the protection.

## Tension

There is no third ruling, because declining to rule is a ruling.
Dissolution removes an organisation that has broken no law and sets aside ballots already counted, which is the thing the institution exists to prevent. Declining forgoes the protection entirely, since a clause that can only be applied once the programme is enacted arrives too late by construction and would never have been worth writing. The seat is not weighing law against politics: it is applying a text that requires it to act on an intention, and the requirement is deliberate.
