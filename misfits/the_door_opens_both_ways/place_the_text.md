# Place: The Text

## Taxonomy

[The Door Opens Both Ways](play_the_door_opens_both_ways.md). Where the door and the lock are the same document.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A printed constitution. An article setting out how it may be amended, and an article naming what amendment may not touch.
Both are ordinary drafting, both are publicly debated, and both are in force at once.

## Holds

[The Framer](persona_the_framer.md), who wrote each of them for a good reason.
[The Door](piece_the_door.md), the procedure by which the text can be changed.
[The Lock](piece_the_lock.md), the part the procedure may not reach.

## Offers

An arrangement that can be improved by those who live under it, and a core that cannot be removed by those who do not like it.

## Withheld

Any reading on which the two do not point at each other.
The place holds a procedure whose whole merit is that it can change anything, beside a clause whose whole merit is that one thing cannot be changed. Neither is a drafting error and neither could be dropped without losing something the other cannot supply. So the difficulty is not hidden in the text and is not a matter of interpretation: it is on the page, in two articles, written deliberately by people who understood both.
