# Play: The Door Opens Both Ways

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Door Opens Both Ways.
A constitution must say how it can be changed, because one that cannot be changed governs the living by the settled preferences of the dead. The same article can be used to change everything, including the arrangement itself. So a clause is written putting the core beyond amendment, and that clause binds every later generation, including one that is unanimous. The defence against being ended by procedure is a limit no procedure can lift.

## Arc

The bend is this: the procedure that keeps the arrangement alive is the procedure by which it can be ended, and the only defence is the objection the procedure was written to answer.
It begins with a door that has to be there.
[The Door Has to Be There](plot_the_door_has_to_be_there.md). A text with no amendment procedure cannot absorb what its authors did not foresee and holds its authority by enforcement rather than consent.
And the door opens both ways.
[And a Door Opens Both Ways](plot_and_a_door_opens_both_ways.md). [The Door](piece_the_door.md) cannot be drafted to admit only improvements, because nothing inside a procedure can tell them apart.
So a lock is fitted, from inside.
[So the Lock Is Fitted From Inside](plot_so_the_lock_is_fitted_from_inside.md). [The Lock](piece_the_lock.md) is written by people who watched the door used, and names the route it closes.
And [the lock binds whoever comes after](plot_and_the_lock_binds_whoever_comes_after.md).
The case for amendability is that the dead should not govern the living, and an entrenched clause is exactly that, at one point, on purpose.
Then [the party arrives lawfully and wins](plot_the_party_arrives_lawfully_and_wins.md). [The Mandate](piece_the_mandate.md) is ballots already counted, cast mostly for reasons that have nothing to do with the clause.
And [the bench cannot abstain](plot_and_the_bench_cannot_abstain.md). [The Court](persona_the_court.md) has two rulings and no neutral one, since declining to rule is a ruling.

## Triggers

**[The Door Has to Be There](plot_the_door_has_to_be_there.md)**
Cue: a constitution being drafted, and the question of how it may later be changed. Closes when the amendment procedure is established as a condition of the text lasting rather than a concession.

**[And a Door Opens Both Ways](plot_and_a_door_opens_both_ways.md)**
Cue: the same article, read by somebody who wants the arrangement gone. Closes when no drafting is found that admits improvements and refuses replacements.

**[So the Lock Is Fitted From Inside](plot_so_the_lock_is_fitted_from_inside.md)**
Cue: the memory of a procedure used, lawfully, to end the order it belonged to. Closes when the entrenchment is shown to answer something that happened rather than a theory.

**[And the Lock Binds Whoever Comes After](plot_and_the_lock_binds_whoever_comes_after.md)**
Cue: a later generation, unanimous and well informed, wanting the clause revised. Closes when the defence is found to commit the objection it was built to answer.

**[The Party Arrives Lawfully and Wins](plot_the_party_arrives_lawfully_and_wins.md)**
Cue: a registered organisation with a published programme and a rising share of the vote. Closes when the objection is established as being to a programme rather than to any conduct.

**[And the Bench Cannot Abstain](plot_and_the_bench_cannot_abstain.md)**
Cue: an application to dissolve, and a hearing date. Closes when both available rulings are shown to spend something the arrangement was built to protect.

## Stakes

Whether an arrangement can protect itself without using the thing it exists to prevent.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a procedure that is a condition of lasting, a procedure that cannot distinguish improvement from replacement, an entrenchment written against something that happened, an entrenchment that binds the unanimous, a mandate that is ballots already counted, and a bench with two rulings and no neutral one, and away from any account in which somebody acted badly. The production cannot fault the framer, who is right twice and hands on both rightnesses; and it cannot fault the voter, who did the one thing the arrangement asked and pays whichever way the question is answered. What is left to fight over is not who is at fault but what to call a protection that can only be had by spending the thing protected.
