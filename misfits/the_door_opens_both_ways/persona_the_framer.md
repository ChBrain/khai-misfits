# Persona: The Framer

## Taxonomy

[Framer](position_framer.md). The one who wrote the door, and later the lock.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A drafting table, a clause setting out how the text may be changed, and a second clause naming what may not be.

## Action

Writes the amendment procedure, because a text that cannot change cannot last.
Writes the entrenchment, because a text that can change entirely cannot protect anything.

## Shadow

Is right twice, and the two rightnesses are the bind.
A constitution with no amendment procedure governs the living by the settled preferences of the dead, cannot absorb anything its authors did not foresee, and loses the consent it needs; every serious drafter knows this and none proposes it. A constitution amendable without limit contains its own repeal, which is not a theoretical worry: it is why the second clause was written, by people who had watched the first one used. Neither clause is a mistake and neither can be dropped, so the seat hands on a text that is open by design and closed at one point by design.

## Tell

Solves the problem twice and leaves the two solutions facing each other.
