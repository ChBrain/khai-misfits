# Piece: The Door

## Owner

## Place

## Load Bearing

It is indispensable and it is the mechanism by which the arrangement can be ended lawfully.
A constitution with no amendment procedure governs the living by the settled preferences of the dead, cannot absorb what its authors did not foresee, and forfeits the consent it needs to be obeyed rather than merely enforced. Every serious drafter provides one. The same article, used to its limit by whoever holds the required majority, is the route by which everything else in the document can be replaced, and the article cannot be written so as to admit only improvements, because nothing in a procedure can tell which is which.

## Apparent

A safeguard against rigidity. The clause that keeps a constitution alive.

## Yearbook

Cannot be omitted and cannot be limited to good changes, since a procedure cannot know which they are.
