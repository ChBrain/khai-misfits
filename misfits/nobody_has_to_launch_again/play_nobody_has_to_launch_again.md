# Play: Nobody Has to Launch Again

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Nobody Has to Launch Again.
An operator puts a satellite into a band that is already busy, which is what the band is for and is why it is busy. Everything up there is tracked as well as anybody can track it, conjunctions are screened, manoeuvres are made. Two objects meet anyway, once, and what was two objects is now some thousands, each of which is now something else's conjunction. Past a certain density the population of fragments grows from collisions among fragments, so the arithmetic no longer has a term for anybody launching, and stopping the launches removes a term that had already stopped mattering.

## Arc

The bend is this: past a density the thing consuming the commons is no longer its users, so restraint stops being a move.
It runs on a use that is the point of the place.
[The Operator](persona_the_operator.md) is not overusing anything. [The Launch](persona_the_launch.md) is what the band exists for, each one is screened, tracked and manoeuvred, and an operator who declined would be declining the purpose rather than moderating it.
And a collision does not remove objects, it multiplies them.
[The Wreckage](piece_the_wreckage.md) is the whole difficulty: two tracked objects become thousands of untracked ones, and every fragment is now a hazard to everything sharing the band. Nothing was consumed. The count went up.
Then [Past the Density It Runs Itself](plot_past_the_density_it_runs_itself.md).
[The Threshold](piece_the_threshold.md) is where fragments collide with fragments often enough to replace themselves, and past it [the cascade](persona_the_cascade.md) needs no further input: the objects are already there, on trajectories nothing anybody does can address, so the loop has no behaviour in it to withdraw.
And that is what removes restraint from the table. Ceasing all launching leaves the population growing, because what is growing is not the population anybody is adding to.
And nobody can be billed. [The Unowned Volume](piece_the_unowned_volume.md) has no owner to charge and no register of who left what, and a fragment cannot be traced to a payer even where everybody agrees whose it was.
And the one repair is somebody else's bill. [The Removal](persona_the_removal.md) works in models, has never been done at scale, and takes objects belonging to whoever launched them out of a band that everybody uses, so the party who would pay is not the party who benefits, and it must run for decades.

## Stakes

Whether a commons can be defended once its users stop being what is emptying it.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a use that is the purpose of the place, a collision that multiplies rather than consumes, a density past which the wreckage supplies its own input, a restraint that reaches nothing, a cost that cannot be assigned to anybody, and a repair paid for by whoever is not protected by it, and away from any account in which somebody was reckless with a band everybody needs. The production cannot ask the operators to stop using the thing the band is for, and it cannot point at the party whose fragment did it, because the fragment has no name on it. What is left to fight over is not whether the band is filling up, everybody can count it, but what to call a commons that goes on emptying after every user has gone home.
