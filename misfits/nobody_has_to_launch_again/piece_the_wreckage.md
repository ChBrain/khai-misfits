# Piece: The Wreckage

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Band](place_the_crowded_band.md). What a collision makes, which is more of the hazard.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Produced in [the crowded band](place_the_crowded_band.md) by [breaking into more](process_breaking_into_more.md), from objects that were being tracked.

## Load Bearing

A collision multiplies objects rather than removing them, which is what makes this unlike every other depletion.
Two tracked items become some thousands of untracked ones, each a hazard to everything sharing the altitude, so nothing was consumed and the count went up: the event that damages the commons increases the number of things in it, and every one of those things is now an input to the next event.

## Apparent

Debris. A catalogue entry that splits into many, most too small to track.
The visible consequence of an accident.

## Yearbook

Persists for centuries at these altitudes, so it is not waiting anything out.
Becomes the population that produces the next collision, which produces more of it.
