# Position: Operator

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Has to Launch Again](play_nobody_has_to_launch_again.md). The seat that does the whole job and holds no lever.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A payload, a window, a catalogue, and a manoeuvre budget.

## Orders

## Loses

Any purchase on the outcome.
The seat performs every duty the work defines and none of them touch the mechanism, since declining to launch would decline the purpose of the band rather than moderate a demand on it, and the growth past the threshold is not fed by arrivals at all.

## Drives

Toward doing everything correctly and changing nothing.
