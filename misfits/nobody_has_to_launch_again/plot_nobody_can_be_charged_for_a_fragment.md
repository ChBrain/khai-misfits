# Plot: Nobody Can Be Charged for a Fragment

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Has to Launch Again](play_nobody_has_to_launch_again.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The question of who should pay for what is up there.

## Action

[The Unowned Volume](piece_the_unowned_volume.md) is examined for an owner, a register, or a route from a fragment to a payer.

## Stage

There is none. A fragment carries no name, and the volume has no authority that could levy anything.

## Tension

The polluter is unidentifiable in principle rather than in practice.
Even where everybody agrees which object a cloud came from, the individual fragment cannot be attached to a payer, and there is no owner of the band to levy on anybody's behalf. So the standard commons remedy has nothing to fasten to. And it would not have reached this anyway: rules users write bind users, and past the threshold the users are not the mechanism, so the instrument is missing and the target has moved.
