# Plot: What a Collision Makes

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Has to Launch Again](play_nobody_has_to_launch_again.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Two tracked objects on paths that intersect, once, despite the screening.

## Action

[Breaking into more](process_breaking_into_more.md): the event happens in [the crowded band](place_the_crowded_band.md), at orbital velocity.

## Stage

[The Wreckage](piece_the_wreckage.md): some thousands of fragments where there were two objects, most too small to track.

## Tension

The event multiplies objects rather than removing them, which is what makes this unlike every other depletion.
A grazed field has less grass and a drawn aquifer has less water, so the damage and the loss are the same quantity. Here nothing is consumed and the count goes up: every fragment is a hazard to everything sharing the altitude and an input to the next event. Mass that was two tracked items becomes a population, and at these altitudes it does not decay for centuries.
