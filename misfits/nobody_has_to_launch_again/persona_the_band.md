# Persona: The Band

## Taxonomy

[Band](position_band.md). The commons, rivalrous and unowned.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A shell of altitudes that does what no other altitude does, shared by everybody who needs it.
Finite in the only sense that counts.

## Action

Fills.
Keeps filling with nobody adding.

## Shadow

Has no owner to charge and no register of what was left in it.
A fragment carries no name, cannot be traced to a payer even where everybody agrees whose object it came from, and the volume has no authority that could levy anything: so the standard remedy for a commons, rules the users make and enforce upon themselves, has no object to attach to once the users are not the problem.

## Tell

Is emptied by something that is not using it.
