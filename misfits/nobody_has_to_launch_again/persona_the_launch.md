# Persona: The Launch

## Taxonomy

[Launch](position_launch.md). The use, which is the point of the place.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A payload into a band that is already busy, screened against everything catalogued.
Communications, weather, navigation, observation.

## Action

Goes up.
Joins a population that is tracked as well as anybody can track it.

## Shadow

Adds a term to an equation that will stop containing it.
Every launch is a real contribution to the density while the density is what matters, and past the threshold the growth comes from collisions among fragments rather than from arrivals: so the act that everybody would name as the cause is the one term that drops out of the arithmetic exactly when the arithmetic starts to matter.

## Tell
