# Piece: The Threshold

## Taxonomy

[The Crowded Band](place_the_crowded_band.md). The density past which the loop needs no user.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Crossed somewhere in [the crowded band](place_the_crowded_band.md), on a date nobody can name and models disagree about.

## Load Bearing

Past it the term for launching drops out of the arithmetic, which is what removes restraint from the table.
Fragments collide with fragments often enough to replace what they consume, so the growth rate stops depending on arrivals: the quantity everybody would regulate is the one that stops mattering exactly when regulating starts to matter, and no amount of restraint reaches a population nobody is adding to.

## Apparent

Nothing. A number in a model, disputed between models.
No event, no marker, and no announcement.

## Yearbook

Is approached by ordinary use and crossed without a signal.
Cannot be uncrossed by any behaviour, since behaviour was never what carried it.
