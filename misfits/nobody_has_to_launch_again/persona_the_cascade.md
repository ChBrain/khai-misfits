# Persona: The Cascade

## Taxonomy

[Cascade](position_cascade.md). The loop with no behaviour in it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Fragments meeting fragments, often enough to replace what they consume.
Ballistic trajectories, decaying over centuries at these altitudes.

## Action

Runs.
Does not require anybody to do anything.

## Shadow

Cannot be withdrawn from, because nobody is contributing to it.
A loop sustained by behaviour ends when the behaviour stops, which is what a circuit breaker is for and what self-governance does to a commons: this loop is sustained by objects already in flight, so there is no participant whose restraint reaches it and no agreement anybody could sign that would.

## Tell

Is the only party in the play that is not a party.
