# Persona: The Operator

## Taxonomy

[Operator](position_operator.md). The user, doing everything the work asks.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A payload, a window, a tracking service, and a manoeuvre budget.
Screening run before every pass, and the passes taken.

## Action

Launches.
Avoids what is tracked.

## Shadow

Is not overusing anything and has no lever on the outcome.
The band is busy because it is useful, screening and manoeuvring are performed properly, and an operator who declined to launch would be declining the purpose of the place rather than moderating a demand on it: so the party with the decision has neither caused the growth nor any means of slowing it.

## Tell

Does the whole job correctly and changes nothing either way.
