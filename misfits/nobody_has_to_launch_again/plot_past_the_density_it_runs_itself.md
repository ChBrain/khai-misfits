## Action

## Stage

The population replaces what it consumes from collisions among what is already there. The arithmetic loses its term for anybody launching.

## Tension

The quantity everybody would regulate is the one that stops mattering when regulating starts to matter.
While density is what counts, every launch is a real contribution and the obvious lever is the obvious lever. Past the threshold the growth rate no longer depends on arrivals, so the term drops out: what is left is a loop whose inputs are objects already in flight, on trajectories that respond to nothing anybody does. There is no date on this and the models disagree about where it sits, which means nobody is told when the lever stopped working.
