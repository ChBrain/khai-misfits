# Plot: Every Launch Is the Point

## Taxonomy

[Nobody Has to Launch Again](play_nobody_has_to_launch_again.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An operator with a payload, a window, and a band that is busy because it is useful.

## Action

[Putting it up](process_putting_it_up.md): [the operator](persona_the_operator.md), holding [Operator](position_operator.md), screens against the catalogue and launches.

## Stage

A working satellite, correctly placed, doing what no other altitude allows.

## Tension

Nobody here is overusing anything.
The band is crowded because it is useful, and the use is the purpose of the place rather than a demand upon it, so an operator who declined would be declining the thing itself. [Avoiding the others](process_avoiding_the_others.md) is performed in full, tracking is used, warnings are acted on and disposal follows the guidelines. Every duty the work defines is discharged by somebody competent, and not one of them touches the quantity that ends up mattering.
