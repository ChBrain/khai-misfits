## Place

## Load Bearing

It removes the one remedy a commons reliably has, which is rules its users write and enforce on themselves.
There is no owner to levy, no register of what was left, and no way to attach a fragment to a payer even where everybody agrees whose object it came from: so the self-governance answer has nothing to fasten to, and it would have failed here anyway, since the parties it binds are not the ones producing the growth.

## Apparent

A legal frame that has worked for sixty years, written when the band was empty.
Nobody's failure and nobody's fault.

## Yearbook

Makes the polluter unidentifiable in principle rather than in practice.
Leaves the only repair as a gift from whoever chooses to pay for it.
