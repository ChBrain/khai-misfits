# Nobody Has to Launch Again: Reference

The research warrant for Nobody Has to Launch Again.
The term is the field's; the play runs on the collision cascade in low Earth orbit and on the commons it
empties, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging the debris cascade as **a commons in which, past a density, the depleting agent stops being the
users**: a band of altitudes is crowded because it is useful, every operator screens against the catalogue,
manoeuvres when warned and disposes at end of life, and none of that reaches the mechanism. A collision at
orbital velocity does not consume objects, it divides them, so two tracked items become thousands of untracked
ones and the count goes up. Past a density at which fragments meet fragments often enough to replace what they
consume, the growth rate stops depending on arrivals, and what is left is a loop whose inputs are objects
already in flight. The play models the documented mechanics, the use that is the purpose of the place, the
event that multiplies rather than consumes, the threshold that drops the launch term out of the arithmetic, the
restraint that reaches nothing, the fragment that cannot be billed to anybody, and the removal that is
somebody else's expense, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of an industry littering a
band everybody needs.

The governing law is that a loop sustained by behaviour ends when the behaviour stops, and this loop is not.
Every other depletion the house holds has a participant whose restraint reaches it: a grazing commons is saved
by rules the users write and enforce on themselves, and an overload clears the moment the clients back off.
Here the objects are already up, on trajectories that respond to nothing anybody does, so universal cessation
by every party that ever launched withdraws a term the arithmetic had already dropped.

What removes any possibility of blame from the operator is that they are not overusing anything and hold no
lever. The band is busy because it does what no other altitude does, an operator who declined to launch would
be declining the purpose rather than moderating a demand, and every duty the work defines, tracking, screening,
avoidance, end-of-life disposal, is discharged in full without touching the quantity that ends up mattering.

What removes it from the instrument is that the commons remedy has nothing to fasten to. A fragment carries no
name and cannot be attached to a payer even where everybody agrees which object the cloud came from, and the
volume has no owner to levy on anybody's behalf. Rules that users write bind users, and past the threshold the
users are not the mechanism, so the instrument is missing and the target has moved.

And the one repair that reaches what is already in orbit is homeless. Removing large derelicts would hold the
line on the models, and every object worth removing belongs to whoever launched it, sitting in a volume with no
jurisdiction: so a mission needs somebody else's permission to take somebody else's property out of nobody's
place, for a benefit spread across everybody, on a bill carried by whoever volunteers, for decades.

It is distinct from the house's **Held Under**, where a brief blip tips a sound system into failure and its
clients' own correct retries become the load that holds it under: the shape looks identical and the difference
is what the loop is made of. There it is made of **behaviour**, so every client could stop and the state
clears, which is exactly what a circuit breaker does. Here it is made of **objects already on ballistic
trajectories**, so there is no participant whose restraint reaches it and no agreement anybody could sign that
would. That is not the same mechanism made harder to clear, which would be a difference of degree: it is the
same shape with the behavioural lever removed.

It is distinct from the house's **The Commons**, where everyone takes a fair share of the shared field and the
field goes bare because the cost of one more is split by all: there the good is consumed by drawing and the
remedy is rules the users make, monitor and enforce upon themselves, so restraint restores the resource. Here
restraint restores nothing. Past the threshold the resource is consumed by its own wreckage rather than by
anybody's draw, and self-governance binds precisely the parties who have stopped being the mechanism.

It is distinct from the house's **Onto the Survivors**, where a failed member's load falls onto the survivors
so past a critical fraction the sharing that made it safe ruptures the bundle: there a **fixed** load is
redistributed and each transfer raises the share carried by what remains. Here nothing is redistributed and no
load is conserved. The hazard population **grows**, because the event that damages the commons increases the
number of things in it.

It is distinct from the house's **The Anticommons**, where too many owners hold veto rights so the resource
goes unused: there fragmentation of control paralyses a resource that is otherwise intact. Here nobody holds a
veto and the resource is used heavily, and the difficulty is the opposite one, that no party can be excluded,
charged or made to consent to a repair.

It is distinct from the house's **Kept From Burning**, where every small fire put out leaves its fuel standing
until the load is beyond suppression: the accumulation is physical in both, and there the load is built by a
protective act that could be stopped, and the remedy, putting fire back, does reduce it. Here no protective act
is building anything, the accumulation is produced by the failures themselves, and the corresponding remedy has
never been run at scale.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the operators as
  littering a band everybody needs, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get wrong: the band is
  crowded because it is useful and the launch is the purpose of the place rather than a demand on it; tracking,
  screening, avoidance and end-of-life disposal are performed in full and reach only what is catalogued; a
  collision at orbital velocity multiplies objects rather than consuming them, which is physics rather than
  anybody's choice; past the threshold the growth no longer depends on arrivals, so the term everybody would
  regulate has already dropped out; and a fragment cannot be attached to a payer even where its origin is
  agreed. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a commons can
  go on emptying after every user has gone home.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. Launching less reaches a term
  the arithmetic has dropped. Self-governance by the users binds the parties who have stopped being the
  mechanism. Active debris removal is the only repair that touches what is already in orbit, and it requires
  taking somebody else's property out of a volume with no jurisdiction, for a benefit spread across everybody,
  paid for by whoever volunteers, continuously, for decades. And better tracking reaches what is catalogued,
  which is not what most of the population is made of.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never claimed
  as the house's. The staging is the house's; the cascade model, the no-launch simulations, the removal
  sensitivity study and the observed events are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: **whether the
  critical density has been passed is genuinely disputed** and models disagree on where the threshold sits, so
  the play stages the structure and never a claim that the cascade is currently running; the no-launch result
  is a simulation under stated assumptions about fragmentation, solar activity and compliance, not an
  observation, and the play carries it as the finding that isolates the mechanism rather than as a measurement
  of the future; the removal figures are model outputs and no programme has tested them; mitigation practice
  has improved materially since 1978 and the play does not stage that improvement, which is a real omission
  rather than a denial; and treating a band of altitudes as one commons is the play's own simplification of a
  volume whose density varies sharply with altitude and inclination. **The play makes no claim that any
  operator acted wrongly or that the band is already lost**, only that a collision multiplies what it damages,
  and that past a density the parties who could exercise restraint are no longer the ones emptying it.
