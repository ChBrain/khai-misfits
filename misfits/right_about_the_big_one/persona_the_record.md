# Persona: The Record

## Taxonomy

[Record](position_record.md). The track history, as it is actually read.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A list of calls, and the two or three anybody can name.
People deciding who to hire, publish, quote and keep.

## Action

Carries every forecast and is read for the famous ones.
Stands in for judgement.

## Shadow

Is complete and is consulted selectively, and the selection is the mechanism.
Nothing is missing from it and nothing is falsified: the quiet weeks are all there, available to anybody, and simply not what a reader looks up. So the object that decides careers is accurate, public, and answered by the same conditional average that broke the rule.

## Tell

Is a full and honest file that functions as a highlight reel.
