# Persona: The Calibrated Forecaster

## Taxonomy

[Calibrated Forecaster](position_calibrated_forecaster.md). The one who reports the number they actually hold.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Years of forecasts, all filed, all honest.
A rule that exists so that this is the best available answer.

## Action

Says what they believe, at the length they believe it.
Does not lean, and does not hedge.

## Shadow

Is doing the one thing the instrument was designed to induce, and loses the review.
Nothing in their conduct can be improved: propriety means the honest report maximises the expected score, and it does, over all the cases. The average that decides their standing is not taken over all the cases, and there is no report they could have made that is both honest and competitive there.

## Tell

Is exactly right for years and comes second on the only day anybody counts.
