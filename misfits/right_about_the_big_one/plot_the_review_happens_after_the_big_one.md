## Tension

Caring about the extremes is correct rather than lazy, and that is what makes this irreducible.
A forecast earns its keep in the tail: that is what it is commissioned for, what it is funded by and what anybody would sensibly want to know about it. No institution has ever convened a review after an ordinary week and none should, so the selection is not a shortcut anybody took and there is no version of a serious evaluation culture that does not do this.
