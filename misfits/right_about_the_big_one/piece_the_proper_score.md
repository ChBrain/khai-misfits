## Load Bearing

It is the correct instrument and its guarantee is conditional, and the condition is the part nobody carries.
Propriety means the expected score is maximised by the truthful report, and expectations are taken over a distribution: so the guarantee is a property of the rule **and the sample together**, and holds over the full set of cases. Nothing about the rule fails when the sample changes. It simply stops making the promise anybody was relying on.

## Apparent

The right tool, chosen by a careful evaluator. The thing that makes an evaluation trustworthy.
