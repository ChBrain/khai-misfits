# Persona: The Reviewer

## Taxonomy

[Reviewer](position_reviewer.md). The one who asks who was right, on the case that mattered.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A storm nobody forecast, and a room that wants to know why.
Every forecast on file, and the ones from the quiet weeks of no interest to anybody.

## Action

Convenes after the extreme.
Scores the forecasts on those days, honestly and correctly.

## Shadow

Is right to care about the extremes, and that is what does the damage.
A forecast earns its keep in the tail, no review is ever called after an ordinary week and none should be, and the score they compute is a proper one computed properly. There is no negligence to find and no bias to correct: an evaluator with no hindsight at all, doing the arithmetic perfectly, produces the same ranking.

## Tell

Asks the only question worth asking and gets an answer that rewards exaggeration.
