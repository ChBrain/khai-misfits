# Pitch: You Missed the Big One

## Taxonomy

[Right About the Big One](play_right_about_the_big_one.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Plain and professional. A scoring rule, a quiet week nobody reviews, a room convened after the storm, a record read by people deciding who to keep.
Years of honest forecasts, every one of them on file.

## Undertow

Under the review, an average taken over the days the extreme happened.
So the rule that made honesty the best answer quietly stops saying so, and nothing about it was changed.

## Nerve

The flatness of a verdict that is arithmetically correct and rewards the wrong answer.

## Echo

Afterward: a record that reads as judgement, a distortion widest where the noise is worst, and a remedy that needs somebody to say in advance what will count.
