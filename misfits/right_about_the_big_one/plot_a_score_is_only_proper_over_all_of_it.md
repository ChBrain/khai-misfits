## Tension

The guarantee is a property of the rule and the sample together, never of the rule alone.
Propriety means the expected score is maximised by the truthful report, and an expectation is taken over a distribution of cases. So the promise the forecaster is relying on carries a condition attached to which cases get averaged, and that condition is not written on the instrument, is not violated by anybody, and is not something the forecaster has any control over.
