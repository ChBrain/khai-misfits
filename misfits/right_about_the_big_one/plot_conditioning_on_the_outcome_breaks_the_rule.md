# Plot: Conditioning on the Outcome Breaks the Rule

## Taxonomy

[Right About the Big One](play_right_about_the_big_one.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An honest score, computed honestly, over the days the extreme happened.

## Action

[The Extreme Subset](piece_the_extreme_subset.md) is scored with a strictly proper rule, correctly, by somebody with nothing to gain.

## Stage

The report that maximises the expected score is no longer the honest one.

## Tension

The arithmetic does this on its own, and it would survive a perfect evaluator.
Selecting on the realised outcome makes the average a different average from the one the rule is proper against, so the optimal report shifts toward the extreme. Nobody erred, nobody hedged and nobody perceived anything wrongly: an evaluator with no hindsight at all, using a strictly proper rule and doing the sums correctly, produces a ranking in which exaggeration comes first. That is what separates this from every account that needs somebody to be fooled.
