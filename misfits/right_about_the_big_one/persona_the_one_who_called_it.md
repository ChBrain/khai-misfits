# Persona: The One Who Called It

## Taxonomy

[One Who Called It](position_one_who_called_it.md). The one whose forecasts lean, and whose record shines.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A habit of forecasting extremes more readily than the evidence supports.
A call that landed, and is remembered.

## Action

Puts more weight on the tail than they should.
Is right about the big one.

## Shadow

Is not cheating, and need not have decided anything.
Leaning is the strategy the conditional review rewards, so a rational forecaster who wants to keep the job adopts it, and a person who sincerely holds wilder beliefs arrives at the same record having chosen nothing at all. Neither of them is gaming a measure: the measure is correct and the sample is what favours them.

## Tell

Has the record everybody wants, and it signals a strategy that is worse over everything.
