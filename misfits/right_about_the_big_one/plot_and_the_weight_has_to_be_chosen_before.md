## Action

## Stage

## Tension

The remedy works and relocates the judgement rather than removing it.
Weighted and censored scoring rules are real, are published and do the job. What they require is that somebody declare in advance which outcomes count and how much, before anybody knows what will happen, and different weight functions rank forecasters differently, so the choice is substantive rather than technical. And it must then be read: an audience that wants to know who called the crash is being handed a weighted average instead, which is the correct answer to a question nobody asked.
