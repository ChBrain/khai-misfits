## Action

## Tension

An accurate call on an extreme is evidence of a strategy that scores worse over everything.
The forecaster who leans is more likely to be the one holding the correct extreme prediction when an extreme arrives, so success on the memorable case is a signal about method rather than about judgement. And the seat need not have chosen anything: somebody who sincerely believes wilder things produces the same record without optimising against any review, which is why nothing here is a measure being gamed.
