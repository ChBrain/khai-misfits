# Persona: The Noise

## Taxonomy

[Noise](position_noise.md). The share of the outcome no forecast could have carried.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A domain where the signal is thin and the variance is not.
Two forecasters, and outcomes that mostly are not about either of them.

## Action

Swamps the signal in the cases selected.
Widens the gap between the conditional ranking and the honest one.

## Shadow

Grows the distortion exactly where forecasting is hardest.
Where a domain is nearly deterministic the conditional ranking is close to the honest one and little is lost; where it is barely predictable the two come apart, and that is the domain in which forecasts are commissioned, argued over and acted on, so the failure is worst in every case anybody built a forecasting function for.

## Tell

Is largest where the stakes are, and is nobody's doing.
