# Plot: It Bites Hardest Where the Noise Is

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

## Action

## Stage

The conditional ranking departs further from the honest one as the signal falls.

## Tension

The distortion is largest exactly where forecasting is hardest and matters most.
Where outcomes are close to deterministic the two rankings nearly agree and little is lost. Where they are dominated by variance no forecast could carry, they come apart, and that is the description of every domain in which forecasts are commissioned, argued over and acted on. So the failure is not a corner case of the method: it is concentrated in the cases the method exists for.
