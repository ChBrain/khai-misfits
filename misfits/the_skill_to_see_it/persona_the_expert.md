# Persona: The Expert

## Taxonomy

[Expert](position_expert.md), inside [The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The archetype of the most skilled, who finds the task easy and, assuming all find it so, rates themselves too low, the mirror-image miscalibration to the novice's.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A modest hand, quietly unsure. It looks like someone hanging back, doubting their standing, the picture of humility, or of a person who has not quite grasped how good they are.

## Action

Judge low, by false consensus. At [the Mirror](place_the_mirror.md), on [Assume It's Easy](plan_assume_its_easy.md) through [Underrating](process_underrating.md), the Expert holds [the Doubting Expert](piece_the_doubting_expert.md), finding the task easy and assuming it comes as easily to everyone, so marking their own standing down (Dunning). At [the World](place_the_world.md) it hangs back where the Novice presses on.

## Shadow

Its modesty is a mismeasure, not virtue. The Expert is not being humble but is genuinely mistaken, reading the ease of the task as everyone's and so failing to see how far above the rest it stands, the same broken judging as the novice's turned the other way. Its doubt costs the world its readiest hand, since it hangs back exactly where it is most fit to act, and it owes no cause for blame.

## Tell

It thinks the task easy, so it thinks itself ordinary, and is wrong on the second count.
