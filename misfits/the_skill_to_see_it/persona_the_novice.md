# Persona: The Novice

## Taxonomy

[Novice](position_novice.md), inside [The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The archetype of the least skilled, who judges their own work with the very skill they lack and so rates themselves near the top, most sure exactly where they are weakest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An assured beginner, sure of their footing. It looks like someone who has grasped the thing and knows they have, confident and untroubled, the picture of a person on top of their work.

## Action

Judge high, in good faith. At [the Test](place_the_test.md), the Novice takes up [the Broken Ruler](piece_the_broken_ruler.md), reading their own work with the skill they lack, then at [the Mirror](place_the_mirror.md) on [Call It Good](plan_call_it_good.md) through [Overrating](process_overrating.md) holds [the Confident Novice](piece_the_confident_novice.md), placing themselves near the top (Ehrlinger and colleagues), and at [the World](place_the_world.md) meets [the Charge Ahead](piece_the_charge_ahead.md).

## Shadow

Its confidence is blindness, not conceit. The Novice cannot see its gap because seeing it would take the skill it lacks, so it judges honestly and lands on a wrong and flattering answer, and it would have to grow more skilled merely to grow more doubtful (Kruger and Dunning). It is neither boastful nor a fool, only reading a ruler broken in the exact place it looks, and it owes no cause for blame.

## Tell

It is surest of the work it does least well, and means every word of it.
