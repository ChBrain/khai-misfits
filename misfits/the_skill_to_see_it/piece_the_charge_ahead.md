# Piece: The Charge Ahead

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The consequence piece:
confidence and competence pulled apart, so that at the margin the least able
press on surest and the most able hang back, and the readiest hand turns out
to be the least fit one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Met at [The World](place_the_world.md), where a rating stops being a private
reading of the mirror and becomes an act with consequences beyond the person
who made it.

## Load Bearing

The two miscalibrations, taken together, leave decisions in the hands of
those least able to make them well and withheld from those most able, an
unhappy handoff produced without any single villain: it is a tendency in
self-assessment held across many people and many sittings, not a verdict
passed on any one novice or any one expert. Neither side chose the mismatch;
each simply read their own mirror as everyone reads it, and the two readings,
run together, hand the floor to the wrong party more often than chance would.

## Apparent

Looks like the ordinary shape of leadership: the bold stepping forward, the
cautious stepping back, confidence sorting itself into its natural order.
