# Piece: The Doubting Expert

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The most skilled
underrating themselves: finding the task easy, and assuming by false consensus
that it must come as easily to everyone else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Staged at [The Mirror](place_the_mirror.md), the same glass the novice reads
falsely upward; carried here by [Underrating](process_underrating.md).

## Load Bearing

Top performers mark themselves down, reading their own ease at the task as
evidence that the task is simply easy, universally, and not as evidence of
their own standing (Dunning): so they miss how far above the rest they truly
stand. This is a self-rating error, not a communication failure, and the
distinction matters. It is not the curse of knowledge staged elsewhere as
Second Nature, where an expert who knows a thing so well cannot imagine not
knowing it, and so explains over the heads of novices: that deficit is about
other people's minds, and it shows up as a failure to reach them. Here the
expert's error concerns their own standing alone; nothing is said, nothing is
taught, no one is talked over. The expert simply looks at an easy task, feels
it as easy, and reads that ease outward onto everyone else, quietly
underwriting their own rank.

## Apparent

Looks like modesty: humility, a person hanging back, letting others take the
credit they might just as well have claimed.
