# Plot: Easy for Anyone

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Mirror](place_the_mirror.md), the top scorer looks back at their own
performance and rates it.

## Action

[The Expert](persona_the_expert.md), from
[the expert's seat](position_expert.md), on
[Assume It's Easy](plan_assume_its_easy.md) through
[Underrating](process_underrating.md), holds
[The Doubting Expert](piece_the_doubting_expert.md) at
[The Mirror](place_the_mirror.md), finding the task easy and assuming by
false consensus that it is easy for all comers, and so marking their own
standing down toward the middle.

## Stage

The top performers underrate themselves, reading their own ease as universal
rather than earned (Dunning). This is not Second Nature, the curse of
knowledge in which an expert misjudges other minds and explains over their
heads; the failure there is one of communication. Here the error is a
self-rating one about the expert's own standing: not a failure to reach
others, but a failure to see what sets their own performance apart from
theirs.

## Tension

It finds the task easy, so it thinks itself ordinary, and is wrong on the
second count.
