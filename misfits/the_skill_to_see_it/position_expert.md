# Position: Expert

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The seat of the most skilled, finding the task easy and, assuming all find it so, rating themselves too low, the mirror-image miscalibration to the novice's.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The skill in full, so a true ruler for its own work and a task that comes to it easily.
A false consensus close at hand, the quiet assumption that what is easy for it is easy for all, since it cannot feel the difficulty others meet.
A standing it cannot quite see, because the ease that marks its mastery is the very thing that hides how far above the rest it sits.

## Orders

Assume it's easy, and mark yourself down.
Judge the task by how it feels to you, and since it feels easy, take it to be easy for everyone and rate your own standing as ordinary, hanging back where you might have led.
Know that this is a mismeasure and not modesty, that you read the ease of the task as everyone's and so miss how far you stand above, the same broken judging as the novice's turned the other way.

## Loses

Its true standing in its own eyes, and it gives the world its steadiest hand too late.
Because it marks itself ordinary, the seat hangs back exactly where it is most fit to act, ceding the ready confidence to those less able, so its doubt costs not itself alone but the decisions it declines to lead.
It keeps its skill and its modesty's good name and loses no cause for blame, the master who underrates the mastery it cannot feel.

## Drives

Toward the one who thinks itself ordinary,
finding the task easy and taking it as easy for all, doubting where it is strongest,
so the seat most fit to judge and to act marks itself down and stands aside, the mirror of the novice's blindness.
