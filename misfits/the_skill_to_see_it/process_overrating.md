# Process: Overrating

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). Borne at [The Mirror](place_the_mirror.md), precipitated by [The Novice](persona_the_novice.md), in [Sure at the Bottom](plot_sure_at_the_bottom.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A self-rating made with the very skill that is missing.
No other instrument is on hand, so the missing skill is asked to weigh itself.

## Direction

From the broken ruler toward a self-placement near the top.
The reading climbs not because anything was hidden, but because nothing was there to hold it down.

## Lever

The broken ruler cannot catch the flaws the same missing skill produced, so it reads high, honestly, on a scale it cannot correct (Ehrlinger and colleagues).
The error and the means of catching the error are one and the same absence.
