# Position: Novice

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The seat of the least skilled, judging their own work with the very skill they lack and so rating themselves near the top, most sure exactly where they are weakest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Little of the skill the task takes, so poor work it cannot itself grade.
The same broken ruler for the grading as for the doing, since to judge the work takes the skill that does it, and that is the skill it is short of.
A confidence that comes free, because nothing it can see tells it the work is poor, so it feels, in good faith, on top of the thing.

## Orders

Call it good, and mean it.
Judge your own work with the eye you have, and since that eye cannot catch the flaws it made, rate yourself near the top and rest easy, honestly sure of a thing you did poorly.
Know that the sureness is blindness and not pride, that the ruler is broken in the exact place you read it, and that you would have to grow more skilled merely to grow more doubtful, so no telling will move you until the skill beneath the seeing is built.

## Loses

Nothing it can feel, and it forfeits the very knowledge of its need.
Because it cannot see the gap, the seat does not seek to close it, resting confident where it is weakest and pressing on where it is least fit, so the miscalibration costs it the correction it never knows to want.
It keeps its ease and its good faith and loses no cause for blame, the honest hand reading a ruler broken where it looks.

## Drives

Toward the one surest where it is weakest,
judging its poor work with the skill it lacks and landing near the top, blind and not proud,
so the seat that cannot do the thing cannot see that it cannot, and the less it has of the skill the less it knows it lacks it.
