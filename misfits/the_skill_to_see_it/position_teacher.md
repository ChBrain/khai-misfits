# Position: Teacher

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The seat of the one who mends the misjudgement the only way it can be mended, by building the skill beneath it, and who marks that no telling can stand in for the teaching.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one road that works: to build the skill itself, since the self-insight comes bundled with the competence and by no shorter path.
A closed door to the easy fix, because telling the unskilled they fall short asks them to judge the claim with the faculty they lack, so the news does not land.
A long and patient labour ahead, the skill raised piece by piece until the ruler mends of its own accord.

## Orders

Teach the skill, and do not merely tell the verdict.
Build the competence up until the seeing comes with it, and spend no breath simply informing the novice they are short, since that message must be judged by the very skill it is about and will be waved away.
Know that the remedy is singular and slow, that self-insight arrives only with the skill and cannot be delivered as news, and that you stand on a contested finding and may claim no quick or certain cure.

## Loses

The ease of a quick correction, and it spends the long cost of real teaching instead.
Because the judgement cannot be fixed without the skill beneath it, the seat forgoes the shortcut of simply telling and takes on the patient work of building competence, buying a mended ruler only at the price of the training that makes one.
It keeps its clear sight of what will and will not land and loses no cause for blame, the mentor who teaches the skill because it knows the telling cannot.

## Drives

Toward the one who builds the skill and not the verdict,
raising competence until the self-insight comes with it, spurning the telling that cannot land,
so the seat that alone can mend the misjudgement mends it only by the long road, the skill itself, and never by the word.
