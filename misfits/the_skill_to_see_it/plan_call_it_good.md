# Plan: Call It Good

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the novice's seat](position_novice.md), inside [The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Novice](position_novice.md)

## Direction

Judge your own work with the eye you have, and since that eye cannot catch the flaws it made, rate yourself near the top and rest easy, honestly sure of a thing you did poorly. Know that the sureness is blindness and not pride, that the ruler is broken in the exact place you read it, and that you would have to grow more skilled merely to grow more doubtful, so no telling will move you until the skill beneath the seeing is built.

## Orders

([The Novice](persona_the_novice.md), having taken up [the Broken Ruler](piece_the_broken_ruler.md) at [the Test](place_the_test.md), holds [the Confident Novice](piece_the_confident_novice.md) through [Overrating](process_overrating.md) at [the Mirror](place_the_mirror.md), placing themselves near the top.)

## Implementation

([Overrating](process_overrating.md) at [the Mirror](place_the_mirror.md) reads high, because [the Broken Ruler](piece_the_broken_ruler.md) cannot catch the flaws the same missing skill made, so the self-rating lands near the top exactly where the skill is lowest.)

## Targets

- [ ] the work is rated near the top, in good faith, by the one who did it poorly
- [ ] the sureness is understood as blindness and not pride, the ruler broken where it is read
- [ ] the confidence is seen to run highest exactly where the skill runs lowest
- [ ] the block is owned, that no telling lands until the skill beneath the seeing is built
