# Plan: Teach the Skill

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the teacher's seat](position_teacher.md), inside [The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Teacher](position_teacher.md)

## Direction

Build the competence up until the seeing comes with it, and spend no breath simply informing the novice they are short, since that message must be judged by the very skill it is about and will be waved away. Know that the remedy is singular and slow, that self-insight arrives only with the skill and cannot be delivered as news, and that you stand on a contested finding and may claim no quick or certain cure.

## Orders

([The Teacher](persona_the_teacher.md) holds [the Lesson](piece_the_lesson.md) through [Teaching](process_teaching.md) at [the World](place_the_world.md), building the skill so the self-insight arrives bundled in, rather than merely telling the Novice they fall short.)

## Implementation

([Teaching](process_teaching.md) at [the World](place_the_world.md) raises the skill, because the same competence that mends the doing mends the ruler that judges it, so [the Lesson](piece_the_lesson.md) lifts the self-insight only by the long road and never by the telling.)

## Targets

- [ ] the skill is built up, the self-insight arriving bundled with the competence
- [ ] the telling is set aside, understood to bounce off the faculty it is about
- [ ] the remedy is seen as singular and slow, no shorter road than real learning
- [ ] the bound is held, the finding contested and the cure neither quick nor certain
