# Place: The Mirror

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). Where each rates their own standing against others. Hosts [Sure at the Bottom](plot_sure_at_the_bottom.md), [Easy for Anyone](plot_easy_for_anyone.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The self-ratings laid side by side: the novice placed near the top, the expert marking down.
Not a boast and not false modesty. Each reads honestly off a glass ground by their own hand.

## Holds

[The Confident Novice](piece_the_confident_novice.md), [The Doubting Expert](piece_the_doubting_expert.md).
Two readings of the same glass, one inflated by a flaw it cannot see, one deflated by an ease it wrongly assumes is shared.

## Offers

A reflection of one's own standing.
Everyone who steps up gets a number, and the number feels earned, because the same mind that would catch the error is the mind giving the answer.

## Withheld

A true reflection, since the glass is the skill itself.
No comparison against others is on offer here, only the picture the self can produce alone, and that picture is only as good as what ground it.
