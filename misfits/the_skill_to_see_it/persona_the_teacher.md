# Persona: The Teacher

## Taxonomy

[Teacher](position_teacher.md), inside [The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The archetype of the one who mends the misjudgement the only way it can be mended, by building the skill beneath it, and who marks that no telling can stand in for the teaching.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A patient mentor with good news to give. It looks like someone who can simply set the novice straight, point out the gap, and have them see it, the ordinary work of correction.

## Action

Build the skill, not the verdict. At [the World](place_the_world.md), on [Teach the Skill](plan_teach_the_skill.md) through [Teaching](process_teaching.md), the Teacher holds [the Lesson](piece_the_lesson.md): that only gaining the skill lifts the self-insight with it, so it trains the competence and the seeing arrives bundled in, and it does not waste breath merely telling the Novice they fall short (Kruger and Dunning).

## Shadow

Its correction cannot be delivered as a message. The Teacher knows that to tell the Novice they are unskilled is to ask them to judge that claim with the faculty they lack, so the news bounces off and is waved away, and the only correction that lands is the slow one, the skill itself built up until the ruler mends. It claims no quick cure and stands on a contested finding, and it owes no false certainty.

## Tell

It never just tells them they are wrong, because it knows telling cannot land where the skill is missing.
