# Piece: The Confident Novice

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The least skilled rating
themselves near the top: the confidence held most firmly exactly where the
skill to earn it is lowest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Staged at [The Mirror](place_the_mirror.md), where the novice's answer is
turned into a self-rating; carried there by [Overrating](process_overrating.md).

## Load Bearing

Bottom performers place themselves near the top of the group, and the finding
holds up robustly across settings and retests (Ehrlinger and colleagues): this
is not a one-off fluke of one unlucky sitting but a steady pattern. The
overconfidence on display is blindness and not conceit. It cannot be conceit,
properly speaking, because conceit presumes the person can see the gap between
their standing and others' and chooses to overstate it anyway; here the person
cannot see the gap at all, since seeing it would itself take the skill they are
short of. What looks from outside like a swagger is, from inside, simply the
absence of anything to swagger against.

## Apparent

An assured beginner, plainly pleased with their own work, standing on top of
it without a second thought.

## Yearbook

Carried by [Overrating](process_overrating.md), staged at
[The Mirror](place_the_mirror.md), voiced by [The Novice](persona_the_novice.md),

goes on to act.
