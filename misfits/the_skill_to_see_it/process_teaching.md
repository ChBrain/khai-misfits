# Process: Teaching

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). Borne at [The World](place_the_world.md), precipitated by [The Teacher](persona_the_teacher.md), in [Learn It to See It](plot_learn_it_to_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A misjudgement that telling cannot fix.
The word has already been tried and has already bounced off; something slower has to start.

## Direction

From the missing skill toward the skill built, and the self-insight that arrives bundled with it.
Nothing is corrected by announcement; the ground itself is rebuilt, and only then does the reading on it change.

## Lever

Self-insight comes bundled with competence and by no shorter path; simply telling the novice bounces off the very faculty the telling is about (Kruger and Dunning).
The lesson has to raise the skill first, because the skill is the only thing that can recognize the correction as a correction.
