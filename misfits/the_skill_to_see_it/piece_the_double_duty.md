# Piece: The Double Duty

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The engine piece: the skill
that produces a good answer in a domain is the same skill that recognises a
good answer, so performing the task and judging the performance are, at
bottom, one faculty wearing two hats.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [The Test](place_the_test.md), where an answer is given and, in the same
motion, graded; carried through the sitting by [Doubling](process_doubling.md).

## Load Bearing

To tell a good answer from a bad one draws on the same knowledge that makes a
good answer in the first place (Kruger and Dunning): the grader's eye is not a
separate instrument brought in afterward, it is built from the identical
competence the task itself demands. This is not the illusion of explanatory
depth staged elsewhere as I Know How It Works, which concerns a felt grasp of
how a mechanism works, punctured the moment it is traced step by step, and
which catches expert and novice alike. Here the deficit sits one level up: it
is in the self-assessment of one's own skill at the task, not in the tracing
of a mechanism. And it is self-masking in its own
particular way. Since the very faculty that would notice the shortfall is the
one in short supply, the shortfall cannot be caught by simply trying harder or
looking again; the tool for looking is broken along with the thing it would
look at. The double duty falls on everyone who attempts the task, but its
weight is not shared evenly: it presses hardest on those least skilled, since
they are furthest from the competence that would let them see how far short
they fall.

## Apparent

A plain, fair task: anyone may sit it, and anyone may look over what they
wrote and say how they did. Nothing about the arrangement looks rigged.

## Yearbook

Set by [The Task](persona_the_task.md) through [Doubling](process_doubling.md)
at [The Test](place_the_test.md), and dramatized in
[The Same Skill](plot_the_same_skill.md), this piece is the engine the rest of
the misfit runs on: it makes [The Broken Ruler](piece_the_broken_ruler.md),
the ruler being nothing other than the double duty applied to a particular
sitting, and it underlies both [The Confident Novice](piece_the_confident_novice.md)
and [The Doubting Expert](piece_the_doubting_expert.md), the two ways the same
one-skill arrangement can misfire in opposite directions.
