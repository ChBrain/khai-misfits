# Plot: Measured by What's Missing

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Test](place_the_test.md), the answer is in, and the self-assessment
must be made with the same skill that fell short of producing a right one.

## Action

[The Novice](persona_the_novice.md), from
[the novice's seat](position_novice.md), takes up
[The Broken Ruler](piece_the_broken_ruler.md) at [The Test](place_the_test.md),
judging their own work with the very skill they lack, since no other
instrument is on offer in the room.

## Stage

The measure is only as sound as the thing measured, so where the skill is
missing, the deficit cannot see itself: the reading comes back honest and
wrong at once, not fudged, not gamed, simply taken on a ruler that is short in
the exact place it is asked to read (Kruger and Dunning). Nothing in the
sitting singles this out as failure; the ruler reports what it can, and what
it can is bounded by what it lacks.

## Tension

The ruler is broken in the exact place it is read.
