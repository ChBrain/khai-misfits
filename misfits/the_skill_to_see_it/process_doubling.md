# Process: Doubling

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). Borne at [The Test](place_the_test.md), precipitated by [The Task](persona_the_task.md), in [The Same Skill](plot_the_same_skill.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A task done, and now to be judged.
The answer is barely given before it is asked to stand as its own witness.

## Direction

From the doing toward a judging drawn from the same skill.
Nothing new is called in to check the work; the hand that wrote the answer is handed the pen to grade it too.

## Lever

To tell a good answer from a bad one in a field is itself an exercise of that field, so one skill serves both jobs at once (Kruger and Dunning).
There is no second faculty waiting in reserve for the checking; the checking borrows the very competence in question.
