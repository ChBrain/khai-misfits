# Plan: Assume It's Easy

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the expert's seat](position_expert.md), inside [The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Expert](position_expert.md)

## Direction

Judge the task by how it feels to you, and since it feels easy, take it to be easy for everyone and rate your own standing as ordinary, hanging back where you might have led. Know that this is a mismeasure and not modesty, that you read the ease of the task as everyone's and so miss how far you stand above, the same broken judging as the novice's turned the other way.

## Orders

([The Expert](persona_the_expert.md) holds [the Doubting Expert](piece_the_doubting_expert.md) through [Underrating](process_underrating.md) at [the Mirror](place_the_mirror.md), finding the task easy and assuming all find it so, so marking their own standing down.)

## Implementation

([Underrating](process_underrating.md) at [the Mirror](place_the_mirror.md) marks the self down, because a false consensus reads the task's ease as everyone's, so the expert's true standing above the rest goes unseen by the expert.)

## Targets

- [ ] the standing is rated ordinary by the one who is in fact well above it
- [ ] the underrating is understood as a false consensus, the task's ease taken for everyone's
- [ ] the error is seen as a mismeasure and not modesty, the novice's blindness turned the other way
- [ ] the cost is owned, the steadiest hand hanging back where it is most fit to act
