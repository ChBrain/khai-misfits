# Piece: The Broken Ruler

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The self-assessment made
with the very skill it is meant to check: a ruler bent by the same hand that
holds it to the work.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [The Test](place_the_test.md), where the answer already given is turned
back on itself and read over.

## Load Bearing

A person short of the skill judges their own work with that same short skill,
and so cannot catch the flaws that short skill produced (Kruger and Dunning):
the ruler used to measure the work is bent in exactly the shape of the work's
own errors, so it lies flat against them and finds nothing wrong. The deficit
therefore hides itself, not by any effort of concealment but structurally: the
instrument of detection and the source of the fault are the same instrument.

## Apparent

An honest self-check, nothing more: the ordinary, unremarkable act of looking
back over one's own work before calling it finished.

## Yearbook

Taken up by [The Novice](persona_the_novice.md) at [The Test](place_the_test.md),
and dramatized in [Measured by What's Missing](plot_measured_by_whats_missing.md),
this piece follows directly from [The Double Duty](piece_the_double_duty.md),
the ruler being that same one-skill arrangement caught in the act of
misreading itself, and it yields [The Confident Novice](piece_the_confident_novice.md),
the reading the broken ruler hands back.
