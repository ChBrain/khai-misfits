# Position: Task

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). The seat of the thing done and judged, whose doing and whose judging draw on one skill, so it hands whoever attempts it a ruler made of the very thing being measured.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A demand that is single: the skill it takes to do it is the skill it takes to grade it, one competence serving both.
No grader outside the skill, since to tell a good answer from a bad one in its field is itself an exercise of the field, so it cannot be judged from without.
An even face to all comers, offering the same test and the same grading to the skilled and the unskilled alike, favouring none by design.

## Orders

Demand one skill for both, and grade each in their own hand.
Ask of the judging the same competence you ask of the doing, so that whoever would rate the work must be able to do the work, and hand each attempter a ruler that is only as true as the skill they brought.
Know that your fairness is the source of the trap, that by asking one skill for both you give the unskilled a broken ruler and the skilled a true one, not by design but by the plain shape of what you ask.

## Loses

Nothing, having nothing of its own to lose, and it is the blind structure that sets the trap.
Because the seat can be judged only by the skill that does it, it cannot help but measure its worst students in their own faulty hand, delivering to each a verdict only as good as the skill under test, so the trap is built into the task and needs no ill will.
It keeps its evenness and its innocence and bears no cause for blame, a fair test whose fairness is the whole of the snare.

## Drives

Toward the thing judged only by the skill that does it,
asking one competence for both the doing and the grading, favouring no one,
so the seat that is fair to all hands each a ruler made of their own skill, true for the able and broken for the rest.
