# Place: The Test

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). Where the task is performed and self-judged, the doing and the judging drawn from one and the same skill. Hosts [The Same Skill](plot_the_same_skill.md), [Measured by What's Missing](plot_measured_by_whats_missing.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The task attempted, the answer given, the self-grading that follows it.
Nothing here separates the performance from the appraisal; both pass through the same hands in the same sitting.

## Holds

[The Double Duty](piece_the_double_duty.md), [The Broken Ruler](piece_the_broken_ruler.md).
The one instrument asked to do the answering and the checking both, and what happens to the check when the instrument itself is short.

## Offers

To any comer, a task to do and to rate.
The room does not discriminate by standing; it hands out the same two jobs, doing and judging, to whoever sits down.

## Withheld

Any grader from outside the skill, and so any true measure to the one who lacks it.
There is no second instrument waiting in reserve. The only ruler in the room is the one the task itself requires, and a short ruler cannot report its own shortness.
