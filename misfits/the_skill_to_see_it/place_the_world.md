# Place: The World

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md). Where the consequence lands, confidence and competence pulled apart, and where the teacher gives the lesson. Hosts [Confidence Where Skill Is Least](plot_confidence_where_skill_is_least.md), [Learn It to See It](plot_learn_it_to_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The confident but unable pressing ahead, the able hanging back, and between them the slow work of teaching.
Here the mismatch stops being a number on a page and starts being who moves first and who is believed.

## Holds

[The Charge Ahead](piece_the_charge_ahead.md), [The Lesson](piece_the_lesson.md).
The forward step taken on a ruler that cannot measure its own error, and the patient rebuilding of the ruler itself.

## Offers

To whoever would mend it, the one road: to build the skill.
Nothing shorter is on the table. The world will let the mismatch play out before it lets it be corrected any faster than that.

## Withheld

Any quick correction by telling.
A word from outside does not reach the faculty that is missing; it has to be built, not announced, and the world keeps that difference honest.
