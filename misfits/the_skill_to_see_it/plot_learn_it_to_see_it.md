# Plot: Learn It to See It

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The World](place_the_world.md), the mismatch between confidence and skill
stands, and only one road runs out of it.

## Action

[The Teacher](persona_the_teacher.md), from
[the teacher's seat](position_teacher.md), on
[Teach the Skill](plan_teach_the_skill.md) through
[Teaching](process_teaching.md), holds [The Lesson](piece_the_lesson.md) at
[The World](place_the_world.md): that only gaining the skill lifts the
self-insight along with it, and no telling in its place can do the same work.

## Stage

The same training that builds competence builds the metacognition to see
where one fell short, so self-insight arrives bound to the skill and by no
shorter road; simply being told bounces off the very faculty that would need
to change to hear it (Kruger and Dunning). Held against this: the pattern is
heavily contested, much of its classic size reproducible by regression to the
mean plus the better-than-average tendency, even from data with no skill in
it at all, so its magnitude and its metacognitive reading both remain disputed
(Krueger and Mueller; Gignac and Zajenkowski).

## Tension

The only fix is the long one, since news of the gap bounces off the very
faculty it is about.
