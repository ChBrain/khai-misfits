# Plot: Confidence Where Skill Is Least

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The World](place_the_world.md), confidence and competence, once pulled
apart at the mirror, now go out to act.

## Action

[The Novice](persona_the_novice.md), from
[the novice's seat](position_novice.md), meets
[The Charge Ahead](piece_the_charge_ahead.md) at [The World](place_the_world.md):
the least able pressing forward surest of all, while the most able hang back
in their own doubt, so the readiest hand in the room is the least fit one.

## Stage

The two miscalibrations, run together, leave decisions made by those least
able to make them and doubted by those most able, since one side is emboldened
past its competence and the other restrained past its own. This is held as a
tendency across a group, not a verdict on any one person in it; no vanity
authors it, and no single loss can be pinned to one hand.

## Tension

The hand least fit to act is the readiest.
