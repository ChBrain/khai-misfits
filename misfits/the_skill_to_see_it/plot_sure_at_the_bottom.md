# Plot: Sure at the Bottom

## Taxonomy

[The Skill to See It](play_the_skill_to_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [The Mirror](place_the_mirror.md), the lowest scorer looks back at their
own performance and rates it.

## Action

[The Novice](persona_the_novice.md), from
[the novice's seat](position_novice.md), on
[Call It Good](plan_call_it_good.md) through
[Overrating](process_overrating.md), holds
[The Confident Novice](piece_the_confident_novice.md) at
[The Mirror](place_the_mirror.md), placing themselves near the top of the
group they in fact sit near the bottom of.

## Stage

The bottom performers rate themselves near the top, confidence running
highest exactly where skill runs lowest (Ehrlinger and colleagues). The
overconfidence is blindness, not conceit: there is no vanity in the reading,
only a ruler with nothing to measure the shortfall by, returning the best
answer it has.

## Tension

Surest exactly where weakest.
