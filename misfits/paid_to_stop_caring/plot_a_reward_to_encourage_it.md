# Plot: A Reward to Encourage It

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A valued thing someone wishes to encourage, and the ordinary means at hand: offer something for it.

## Action

At [the Playroom](place_the_playroom.md), [the Patron](persona_the_patron.md) from [the Patron's seat](position_patron.md) on [Reward It](plan_reward_it.md) through [Rewarding](process_rewarding.md) offers [the Reward](piece_the_reward.md), the extrinsic incentive given in good faith by the rule that a reward strengthens a behaviour.

## Stage

The reward is well meant, an ordinary application of a rule that ordinarily works: pay for a behaviour and you get more of it. But for a thing already loved, the offering is the beginning of the turn, not because it fails to reward but because it supplies a rival motive where the doing needed none, a second reason crowding in beside the first (Deci). No villain: the rule is sound, the Patron follows it in good faith, and it is only this one case, the already-loved task, that the rule does not cover.

## Tension

The incentive well meant, and the one case the ordinary rule does not cover.
