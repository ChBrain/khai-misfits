# Persona: The Steward

## Taxonomy

[Steward](position_steward.md), inside [Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The archetype of the one who would encourage without undermining, rewarding in a way that informs rather than controls, or leaving the freely given unpaid, and who marks that the way is narrow, costly, and its effect once contested.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A withholder who will not pay what is owed, a stingy hand dressing up its meanness as wisdom, as though refusing the tangible reward were only an excuse to give less than the doing deserves.

## Action

Reward it right. At [the Empty Table](place_the_empty_table.md), the Steward on [Reward It Right](plan_reward_it_right.md) through [Informing](process_informing.md) holds [the Right Reward](piece_the_right_reward.md), the praise or the unexpected acknowledgement that feeds competence rather than controls, or the leaving of the loved thing unpaid (Deci, Koestner and Ryan).

## Shadow

Its withholding is a careful giving, not a meanness. The Steward is not dressing up stinginess: it gives what encourages without supplying a controlling reason, a word that tells the person it did well and feeds the sense of doing the thing rightly, or an acknowledgement it did not expect, or it leaves the love of a thing to be its own wage. But this is narrow and easily missed, and it costs: it gives up the simple lever of the expected reward, it demands the giver first read whether a thing is already loved, and it forgoes the control that paying buys. It rewards well against the grain of the ordinary rule, and holds that its own remedy is real but bounded and was once sharply contested.

## Tell

It gives the word that informs and withholds the wage that controls, encouraging the love without pricing it, narrowly and at a cost.
