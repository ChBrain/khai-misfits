# Persona: The Onlooker

## Taxonomy

[Onlooker](position_onlooker.md), inside [Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The archetype of the self as its own observer, which reads the person's motive from what it sees the person do and be paid for, and infers the reward as the reason.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A cynic whispering that nothing is done for love, a corrosive doubt that talks the person out of caring, as though the reframing were a bad faith the person chose or a suspicion it should have resisted.

## Action

Read the reason. At [the Second Thought](place_the_second_thought.md), the Onlooker on [Read the Reason](plan_read_the_reason.md) through [Inferring](process_inferring.md) makes [the Reframing](piece_the_reframing.md), reading a reason from the reward, that the person must do the thing for the reward and not for love, an external motive supplied where none was needed (Lepper, Greene and Nisbett).

## Shadow

Its cynicism is ordinary self-reading, not a chosen bad faith. The Onlooker is not a cynic talking the person out of caring: it infers the person's motive the way anyone infers a motive, from the visible act and its visible circumstances, and when the visible circumstance is a reward, the reasonable reading is that the person acts for the reward. It does not doubt the love to spite it; it simply cannot see an inner motive directly, so it reads from the outside, and the outside now shows a wage. The reframing is the plain work of a self making sense of itself, and it is blameless, only mistaken by the reward it was handed to reason from.

## Tell

It reads the motive from the visible act and its wage, and where a reward is paid it reasonably, and wrongly, reads the reward as the reason.
