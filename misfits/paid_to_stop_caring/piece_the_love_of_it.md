# Piece: The Love of It

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The intrinsic motive, the thing done for its own sake, before any reward is offered.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Playroom](place_the_playroom.md), carried by [Playing](process_playing.md), voiced by [the Lover](persona_the_lover.md): the drawing done for the delight of drawing, the puzzle worked for the pleasure of the puzzle.

## Load Bearing

The motive inside the act, the drawing delighted in and the puzzle enjoyed, needing no reward because the act is its own reward, the love of it wage enough (Lepper, Greene and Nisbett). Nothing here is waiting to be paid. The child at the easel is not withholding effort until a fee arrives; the doing and the wanting to do are the same motion, closed on themselves, complete before anyone thinks to add anything to them.

## Apparent

It looks like a thing that could only be strengthened by a reward, since the ordinary rule says a reward strengthens everything it touches.

## Yearbook

Carried by [Playing](process_playing.md), staged at [the Playroom](place_the_playroom.md), voiced by [the Lover](persona_the_lover.md), dramatized in [For Its Own Sake](plot_for_its_own_sake.md). It is the love that needs no wage; it is what [The Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md) supplants.
