# Pitch: When Play Becomes Work

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that a reward turns a thing done for its own sake into a thing done for pay, and play becomes work.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Rueful and humane, the small tragedy of a good intention. A person doing a thing purely for the love of it, and someone rewarding the valued thing in good faith, exactly as one should. Nothing here is a bribe or a corruption; the reward is well meant and the ordinary rule that incentives help is almost always right.

## Undertow

A reward offered for what was already done freely supplies a reason where none was needed, so the person reads its own motive from the reward, that it does the thing for the pay and not for love, and the inner motive is crowded out by the outer (Lepper, Greene and Nisbett; Deci). When the reward stops, the doing falls below the baseline it had before any reward.

## Nerve

That the very reward meant to feed a love is the reward that hollows its reason, so paying for the freely given can make it less freely given, and the thing loved is done less once it has been paid for. No one is a villain, held against its limit, since the effect is real but bounded, weak or absent for praise or the unexpected, and its size was once sharply contested (Deci, Koestner and Ryan; Cameron and Pierce).

## Echo

Pay a person for what they did for love, and they come to do it for the pay, and do it less when the pay is gone.
