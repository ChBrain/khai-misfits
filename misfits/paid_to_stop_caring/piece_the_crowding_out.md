# Piece: The Crowding-Out

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The substitution, the inner motive supplanted by the outer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Second Thought](place_the_second_thought.md), met by [the Lover](persona_the_lover.md): the moment the Lover finds its own reason for playing has quietly changed hands.

## Load Bearing

The inner motive supplanted by the outer, the act now done for the reward and the love of it quietly gone, the controlling reward having undermined the autonomy the intrinsic motive rested on, no measure and no gaming involved (Deci; Deci, Koestner and Ryan). Nothing is faked and nothing is corrupted in the doing itself; the drawing is still a drawing. What has moved is only the reason behind it, and that is enough.

## Apparent

It looks like a will grown weak, a person who stopped caring, as though caring were a resource that ran out on its own.
