# Persona: The Patron

## Taxonomy

[Patron](position_patron.md), inside [Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The archetype of the one who offers the reward in good faith to encourage the valued thing, by the sound and ordinary rule that a reward strengthens a behaviour.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A briber, a corrupter buying with money what should be given for love, cheapening a pure thing by putting a price on it, as though the reward were meant to control or to buy off and not simply to encourage.

## Action

Reward it. At [the Playroom](place_the_playroom.md), the Patron on [Reward It](plan_reward_it.md) through [Rewarding](process_rewarding.md) offers [the Reward](piece_the_reward.md), the extrinsic incentive given to encourage the valued thing, the gold star or the payment or the fee, added by the rule that a reward strengthens a behaviour (Deci).

## Shadow

Its bribery is a good intention following a sound rule. The Patron is not a corrupter cheapening a pure thing: it means only to encourage a valued behaviour, and it follows the rule that incentives strengthen behaviour, which is true of almost everything a person does not already love. It cannot see from the outside that this particular thing was already done for its own sake, that here the reward will not add to the motive but supply a rival one, and that for a loved thing a wage is not a help but a hollowing. It rewards in good faith, and the harm is not in its intent but in the one case the ordinary rule does not cover.

## Tell

It rewards to encourage, by the rule that works for all but the loved thing, and cannot see it has priced the one thing that needed no price.
