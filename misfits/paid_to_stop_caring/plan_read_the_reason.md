# Plan: Read the Reason

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the onlooker's seat](position_onlooker.md), inside [Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Onlooker](position_onlooker.md)

## Direction

Infer the person's motive as anyone infers a motive, from the visible behaviour and its visible circumstances, and where a reward is paid, read the reward as the reason the act is done. Know that you cannot see the inner motive directly, that the reward you reason from was handed to you and not chosen, and that the reading, though it is the plain work of a self and blameless, supplies an external reason where the true one was within.

## Orders

([The Onlooker](persona_the_onlooker.md) makes [the Reframing](piece_the_reframing.md) through [Inferring](process_inferring.md) at [the Second Thought](place_the_second_thought.md), reading a reason from the reward, that the person acts for the reward and not for love.)

## Implementation

([Inferring](process_inferring.md) at [the Second Thought](place_the_second_thought.md) makes [the Reframing](piece_the_reframing.md), because the self reads its motive from the visible act and its wage, so where a reward is paid it reasons the reward to be the reason.)

## Targets

- [ ] the person's motive is read from the visible act and its reward
- [ ] the reading is understood as ordinary self-making, how any self infers a motive
- [ ] it is seen that where a reward is paid the self reasonably reads the reward as the reason
- [ ] it is owned that the inference is honest and blameless, made false only by the reward it reasons from
