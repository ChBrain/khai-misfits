# Plot: So That Is Why

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The person now paid to do what it once did freely, looking at its own doing and the reward beside it.

## Action

At [the Second Thought](place_the_second_thought.md), [the Onlooker](persona_the_onlooker.md) from [the Onlooker's seat](position_onlooker.md) on [Read the Reason](plan_read_the_reason.md) through [Inferring](process_inferring.md) makes [the Reframing](piece_the_reframing.md), reading a reason from the reward, that it must do the thing for the reward and not for love.

## Stage

The inference is ordinary and blameless, the plain work of a self reading its own motive off the visible act and its wage, the way anyone reads their reasons from what they can see of themselves: an external reason supplied where the true one was within, and taken as the whole of the answer (Lepper, Greene and Nisbett). No villain: this is ordinary self-reading, not a chosen bad faith, the same inference anyone would draw watching a paid hand at a once-free task.

## Tension

The reason read from the reward, and the love it quietly displaces.
