# Plot: For Its Own Sake

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A person doing a thing purely for the love of it, no one watching, no one paying, the doing its own reason.

## Action

At [the Playroom](place_the_playroom.md), [the Lover](persona_the_lover.md) from [the Lover's seat](position_lover.md) on [Do It for Love](plan_do_it_for_love.md) through [Playing](process_playing.md) holds [the Love of It](piece_the_love_of_it.md), the thing done for its own sake and needing no reward.

## Stage

The love of it is whole here, freely given and asking nothing back: the act is its own reward, the delight in the doing its own wage, no ledger kept and none needed (Lepper, Greene and Nisbett). This is the baseline the rest of the misfit measures against, the doing at its fullest before anyone thinks to price it. No villain: there is nothing here to blame or credit, only a person and a thing it loves, doing it because it loves it.

## Tension

The love whole and unpaid, and a reward about to be offered.
