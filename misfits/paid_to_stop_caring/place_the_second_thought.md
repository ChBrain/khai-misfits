# Place: The Second Thought

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The inner space of self-perception, where the person reads its own reasons and the motive is crowded out. Hosts [So That Is Why](plot_so_that_is_why.md), [Now It Is for the Reward](plot_now_it_is_for_the_reward.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The self looking at its own act and the wage beside it, inferring why it does what it does.
The love quietly giving way to the reward as the reason, no announcement made, no argument won.

## Holds

[The Reframing](piece_the_reframing.md).
The reason read from the reward, that the act must be for the reward and not for love of it.

[The Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md).
The inner motive supplanted by the outer, the love of it quietly gone from where it once stood.

## Offers

To the Onlooker, a plausible reason ready to hand in the visible reward, no need to look further.

## Withheld

The inner motive itself, never visible direct, only ever inferred from what is seen from outside.
