# Piece: The Reframing

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The reason read from the reward, the over-justification supplying an external motive where none was needed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Second Thought](place_the_second_thought.md), carried by [Inferring](process_inferring.md), voiced by [the Onlooker](persona_the_onlooker.md): the pause in which the self looks at its own rewarded act and asks why it is doing this.

## Load Bearing

The over-justification, the person reading a reason from the reward, that it must do the thing for the reward and not for love, an external motive supplied where none was needed, the plain and blameless work of a self inferring its motive from the visible act and its wage (Lepper, Greene and Nisbett). The Onlooker does not invent a lie; it reasons the way anyone reasons about anyone, from behaviour and its visible cause, and here the visible cause is the reward, so the reward is what gets credited.

## Apparent

It looks like cynicism, a bad faith the person chose, as though it decided to care less on purpose.
