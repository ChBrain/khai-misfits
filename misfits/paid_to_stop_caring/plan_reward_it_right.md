# Plan: Reward It Right

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the steward's seat](position_steward.md), inside [Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Steward](position_steward.md)

## Direction

Give the word that tells the person it did well and feeds the sense of doing the thing rightly, or the acknowledgement it did not expect, or withhold the wage entirely and let the love be its own reward, so as to encourage without supplying a controlling reason. Know that this way is narrow and easily missed, that it forgoes the simple lever of the expected reward and asks you first to read whether a thing is already loved, and that the effect you steer by is real but bounded and was once sharply contested.

## Orders

([The Steward](persona_the_steward.md) holds [the Right Reward](piece_the_right_reward.md) through [Informing](process_informing.md) at [the Empty Table](place_the_empty_table.md), the praise or unexpected acknowledgement that informs rather than controls, or the freely given left unpaid.)

## Implementation

([Informing](process_informing.md) at [the Empty Table](place_the_empty_table.md) holds [the Right Reward](piece_the_right_reward.md), because a reward that feeds competence rather than controls behaviour encourages without a rival reason, bought by forgoing the simple lever of the tangible reward.)

## Targets

- [ ] the reward informs competence rather than controls, or the loved thing is left unpaid
- [ ] the remedy is understood to work by not supplying a controlling reason where the love is
- [ ] the cost is named, the simple lever forgone and the reading of the motive required first
- [ ] the bound is held, the effect real but bounded, weak or absent for praise or the unexpected, once contested
