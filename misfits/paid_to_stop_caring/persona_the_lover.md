# Persona: The Lover

## Taxonomy

[Lover](position_lover.md), inside [Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The archetype of the one who does the thing for its own sake, whose intrinsic motive is crowded out by the reward, and who does the thing less once it has been paid for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A person made lazy or greedy by pay, who once worked for love and now will not lift a finger without a wage, as though the reward had exposed a mercenary heart or a will too weak to keep caring without being paid.

## Action

Do it for love. At [the Playroom](place_the_playroom.md), the Lover on [Do It for Love](plan_do_it_for_love.md) through [Playing](process_playing.md) holds [the Love of It](piece_the_love_of_it.md), the thing done for its own sake, then at [the Second Thought](place_the_second_thought.md) meets [the Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md) and at [the Empty Table](place_the_empty_table.md) meets [the Falling-Off](piece_the_falling_off.md), doing less than ever it did unpaid (Lepper, Greene and Nisbett).

## Shadow

Its falling-off is a displaced motive, not a mercenary heart. The Lover is not lazy or greedy: it read its own reasons the way anyone does, from what it saw itself doing and why, and being paid to do a thing is a real reason to think one does it for the pay, so the love was not betrayed but quietly crowded out. When the reward stops, the inner motive that once carried the act does not spring back, because it was displaced and not merely outbid, so the Lover does the loved thing less than before any reward, not from a weak will but because the reason it acted on has been hollowed.

## Tell

It did the thing for love, and once paid comes to do it for pay, and does it less than ever when the pay is gone.
