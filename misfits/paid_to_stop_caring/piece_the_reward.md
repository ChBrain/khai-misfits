# Piece: The Reward

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The extrinsic incentive offered in good faith to encourage what was already freely done.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Playroom](place_the_playroom.md), carried by [Rewarding](process_rewarding.md), voiced by [the Patron](persona_the_patron.md): the gold star produced, the fee named, the offer made in plain good faith.

## Load Bearing

The gold star or the payment or the fee, added by the ordinary rule that a reward strengthens a behaviour, well meant and, for a thing already loved, the beginning of the turn, since it supplies not more motive but a rival one (Deci). The Patron means only to encourage; the rule that guides the offer is sound in general and only misfires here, on a thing that did not need it.

## Apparent

It looks like plain help, a reward that must make the wanted thing wanted more, since more incentive should mean more of the behaviour.
