# Plot: Now It Is for the Reward

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The external reason read and settling in, the reward no longer beside the doing but under it.

## Action

At [the Second Thought](place_the_second_thought.md), [the Lover](persona_the_lover.md) meets [the Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md), the inner motive supplanted by the outer, the act now done for the reward and the love of it quietly gone.

## Stage

The controlling reward has undermined the autonomy the love rested on: no measure is gamed and nothing is corrupted, only the motive substituted, the reward not merely added to the love but standing where the love used to stand (Deci; Deci, Koestner and Ryan). The doing looks the same from outside, still performed, still apparently willing, but its inner reason has changed hands. No villain: nothing was taken by force, the substitution happened quietly, in the ordinary course of a person making sense of its own paid behaviour.

## Tension

The act still done, but now for the reward, the love gone quiet beneath it.
