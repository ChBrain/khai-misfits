# Plot: Praise, Not Pay

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The love spent, and a way sought to encourage a thing without undermining it further.

## Action

At [the Empty Table](place_the_empty_table.md), [the Steward](persona_the_steward.md) from [the Steward's seat](position_steward.md) on [Reward It Right](plan_reward_it_right.md) through [Informing](process_informing.md) holds [the Right Reward](piece_the_right_reward.md), the praise or unexpected acknowledgement that informs competence rather than controls, or the freely given left unpaid.

## Stage

This is the remedy, and the bound it is held to. The informing reward is narrow and costly: it forgoes the simple lever of the expected controlling reward and asks the giver first to read whether a thing is already loved before pricing it, offering praise or an unlooked-for recognition of real competence, or simply leaving the freely given unpaid (Deci, Koestner and Ryan). And the bound holds too: the effect is real but bounded, strongest for an expected tangible controlling reward laid on an already-loved task, weak or absent for informational praise, for the unexpected, or for tasks with no love to begin with, its overall size once sharply contested (Cameron and Pierce). No villain: the Steward means only to help, and the remedy is offered in the same good faith the original reward was.

## Tension

The way out real but narrow, the simple lever forgone and the effect once contested.
