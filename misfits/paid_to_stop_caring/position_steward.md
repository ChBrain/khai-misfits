# Position: Steward

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The seat of the one who encourages without undermining, and marks that the informing reward is narrow, costly, and its effect once contested.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A way to encourage that does not control: a word of praise, an unexpected acknowledgement, a recognition of real competence.
The option of leaving the freely given unpaid, letting the love of a thing be its own wage.
A clear sight of the price and the bound, the simple lever forgone and the effect real but limited and once fought over.

## Orders

Reward it right, informing rather than controlling, or leave the loved thing unpaid.
Give the word that tells the person it did well and feeds the sense of doing the thing rightly, or the acknowledgement it did not expect, or withhold the wage entirely and let the love be its own reward, so as to encourage without supplying a controlling reason.
Know that this way is narrow and easily missed, that it forgoes the simple lever of the expected reward and asks you first to read whether a thing is already loved, and that the effect you steer by is real but bounded and was once sharply contested.

## Loses

The simple lever of the expected reward, given up to keep the love intact.
Because it will not price a thing already loved, the seat forgoes the plain and reliable incentive and the control that paying buys, and takes on the harder work of reading the motive and informing rather than controlling, buying an unhollowed love at the cost of the easy wage.
It keeps its careful giving and its clear sight of the bound and loses no cause for blame, the one who encouraged the love without pricing it.

## Drives

Toward the one who encourages the love without pricing it away,
giving the reward that informs and withholding the one that controls, or leaving the freely given unpaid, narrowly and at a cost,
so the seat that alone keeps the love intact keeps it only by forgoing the simple lever, and only so far as the bounded and once-contested effect can be steered by.
