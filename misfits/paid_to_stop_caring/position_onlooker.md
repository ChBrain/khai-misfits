# Position: Onlooker

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The seat of the self as its own observer, and marks that reading the reward as the reason is ordinary self-making and not a chosen bad faith.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A view only from the outside, of the visible act and its visible circumstances, never of the inner motive direct.
The ordinary work of a self making sense of itself, inferring why it acts from what it does.
A reward now in the picture, a wage attached to what was once done for love.

## Orders

Read the reason for the act from what you can see: the deed, and its wage.
Infer the person's motive as anyone infers a motive, from the visible behaviour and its visible circumstances, and where a reward is paid, read the reward as the reason the act is done.
Know that you cannot see the inner motive directly, that the reward you reason from was handed to you and not chosen, and that the reading, though it is the plain work of a self and blameless, supplies an external reason where the true one was within.

## Loses

The truth of the motive, misread from the outside by the wage in view.
Because it can see only the act and its reward and never the love direct, the seat reasons the reward to be the reason, and so replaces a true inner motive with a plausible outer one, an honest inference made false by the reward it was given to reason from.
It keeps its ordinary self-reading and its blamelessness and loses no cause for blame, a self that made sense of itself and was misled by a wage.

## Drives

Toward the self that reads its motive from the outside and is fooled by the wage,
inferring the reason for the act from the visible deed and its reward, as any self makes sense of itself,
so the seat whose ordinary self-reading is no bad faith supplies the reward as the reason and crowds the true motive out, honest and mistaken.
