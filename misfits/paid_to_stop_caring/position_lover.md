# Position: Lover

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The seat of the one who does the thing for its own sake, and marks that its falling-off is a displaced motive and not a mercenary heart.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A love of the doing, an intrinsic motive that needs no reward because the act is its own reward.
A habit of reading its own reasons from what it sees itself do, as anyone reads a motive.
No sight, from within, of the difference between doing a thing for love and doing it for a wage, once the wage is there.

## Orders

Do the thing for its own sake, needing no reward.
Draw because drawing delights you, work the puzzle because it is fun, give because giving feels right, since the act is its own reward and the love of it is wage enough.
Know that once a reward is offered you will read a reason from it, that being paid to do a thing is a reason to think you do it for the pay, so the inner motive may be crowded out, and that when the reward stops the doing may fall below where it began.

## Loses

The love of the thing, crowded out by the reward that meant to feed it.
Because it reads its own motive from the visible wage and comes to do the thing for the reward, the seat forfeits the intrinsic reason that once carried the act, and when the reward is withdrawn the love does not spring back, so it does the loved thing less than ever it did unpaid.
It keeps its ordinary self-reading and its blameless heart and loses no cause for blame, a doer whose love was not betrayed but quietly spent.

## Drives

Toward the one whose love of a thing is crowded out by being paid for it,
doing the thing for its own sake until a reward supplies a rival reason, then doing it for the reward and less when the reward is gone,
so the seat that did the loved thing freely comes to do it for pay and does it less than before, its motive displaced and not its will grown weak.
