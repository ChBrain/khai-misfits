# Position: Patron

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The seat of the one who offers the reward in good faith to encourage the valued thing, and marks that it follows a sound rule that fails only for the loved thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A valued behaviour it wishes to encourage, and a reward to encourage it with.
The sound and ordinary rule that a reward strengthens a behaviour, true of almost everything.
No sight, from the outside, that this particular thing is already done for its own sake and needs no reward.

## Orders

Reward the valued thing, to encourage it, by the rule that a reward strengthens a behaviour.
Offer the gold star or the payment or the fee, meaning only to see more of the good behaviour, since incentives work and a reward added to a wanted thing should make it wanted more.
Know that this one thing is already done for love, that for a loved thing the reward supplies not more motive but a rival one, and that you cannot see from the outside that you have priced the one thing that needed no price.

## Loses

The very behaviour it meant to encourage, hollowed by the reward it offered.
Because it rewards a thing already loved and cannot see the love from outside, the seat supplies a controlling reason that crowds the love out, so its good intention buys less of the behaviour and not more, the encouragement turned to a hollowing.
It keeps its good faith and its sound rule and loses no cause for blame, a giver undone by the one case the rule does not cover.

## Drives

Toward the one who rewards in good faith and hollows what it meant to feed,
offering the incentive by the sound rule that works for all but the loved thing, blind from outside to the love already there,
so the seat whose good intention should have helped instead supplies the rival reason that crowds the love out, harmed by the single exception to a rule that is otherwise true.
