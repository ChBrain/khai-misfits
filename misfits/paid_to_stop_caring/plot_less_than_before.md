# Plot: Less Than Before

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

The reward stopped, or come to feel too small, and the doing left to stand on whatever moves it now.

## Action

At [the Empty Table](place_the_empty_table.md), [the Lover](persona_the_lover.md) meets [the Falling-Off](piece_the_falling_off.md), the doing fallen below the baseline it had before any reward.

## Stage

This is the harm. The love does not spring back when the reward is withdrawn, because it was displaced and not merely outbid: the reward crowded out the inner motive rather than sitting alongside it, so when the reward is gone there is no motive left underneath to resume, and the thing once loved for itself is now done less than ever it was unpaid (Lepper, Greene and Nisbett; Deci, Koestner and Ryan). This is distinct from The Cobra Effect, where a gamed incentive produces more of the very thing it meant to stop, an outcome inflated by cunning; here the incentive produces less of the thing it meant to encourage, a motive crowded out, no gaming involved. It is distinct from Metric Fixation, where the measure itself is corrupted, chased instead of the goal it stood for; here no measure is in play at all, only the motivation for a single, ungoverned act. And it is distinct from Moral Licensing, where a virtue in one act funds licence for a vice in another, a ledger kept across acts; here there is only one act, its inner motive displaced by an outer one, no ledger and no later withdrawal to spend. No villain: not a mercenary heart revealed but a displaced motive, authored by no one's ill will.

## Tension

The loved thing now done less than before it was ever paid for.
