# Plan: Reward It

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the patron's seat](position_patron.md), inside [Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md).

## Owner

## Direction

Offer the gold star or the payment or the fee, meaning only to see more of the good behaviour, since incentives work and a reward added to a wanted thing should make it wanted more. Know that this one thing is already done for love, that for a loved thing the reward supplies not more motive but a rival one, and that you cannot see from the outside that you have priced the one thing that needed no price.
