# Plan: Do It for Love

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the lover's seat](position_lover.md), inside [Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Lover](position_lover.md)

## Direction

Draw because drawing delights you, work the puzzle because it is fun, give because giving feels right, since the act is its own reward and the love of it is wage enough. Know that once a reward is offered you will read a reason from it, that being paid to do a thing is a reason to think you do it for the pay, so the inner motive may be crowded out, and that when the reward stops the doing may fall below where it began.

## Orders

([The Lover](persona_the_lover.md) holds [the Love of It](piece_the_love_of_it.md) through [Playing](process_playing.md) at [the Playroom](place_the_playroom.md), doing the thing for its own sake, and later meets [the Crowding-Out](piece_the_crowding_out.md) and [the Falling-Off](piece_the_falling_off.md).)

## Implementation

([Playing](process_playing.md) at [the Playroom](place_the_playroom.md) holds [the Love of It](piece_the_love_of_it.md), because the act is its own reward, until a reward supplies a rival reason and the love is crowded out and the doing falls off.)

## Targets

- [ ] the thing is done for its own sake, the intrinsic motive whole and needing no reward
- [ ] the love of it is understood as wage enough, the act its own reward
- [ ] it is seen that once paid the self reads a reason from the reward and the motive may be crowded out
- [ ] it is owned that the falling-off is a displaced motive and not a mercenary or weakened heart
