# Place: The Playroom

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). Where the thing is done for love and then, in good faith, rewarded. Hosts [For Its Own Sake](plot_for_its_own_sake.md), [A Reward to Encourage It](plot_a_reward_to_encourage_it.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A person absorbed in a thing for the delight of it, drawing or solving or giving because the doing itself is the wage.
A well-meant reward held out beside it, offered to encourage the very thing that needed no encouragement.

## Holds

[The Love of It](piece_the_love_of_it.md).
The intrinsic motive, the thing done for its own sake, needing no reward because the act is its own reward.

[The Reward](piece_the_reward.md).
The extrinsic incentive offered in good faith, by the ordinary rule that a reward strengthens a behaviour.

## Offers

To the Lover, the delight of the act as its own wage, before any other reason is laid beside it.

## Withheld

Any sign, from outside, that this thing was already loved and needs no reward at all.
