# Piece: The Falling-Off

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The harm, the doing fallen below its unrewarded baseline once the reward stops.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Empty Table](place_the_empty_table.md), met by [the Lover](persona_the_lover.md): the table where the reward no longer sits and the drawing does not resume.

## Load Bearing

When the reward stops or comes to feel too small, the doing falls below the baseline it had before any reward, because the inner motive that once carried it was displaced and does not spring back on the reward's withdrawal, so the thing loved for itself is done less than ever it was unpaid (Lepper, Greene and Nisbett; Deci, Koestner and Ryan).

This is distinct from three neighbouring shapes that look related but are not. It is distinct from The Cobra Effect, where a reward for a good outcome breeds a workaround that games it, producing more of the very thing it meant to reduce, as a bounty on dead cobras breeds cobra farming; here the incentive is not gamed at all, and the behaviour collapses rather than multiplying perversely. It is distinct from Metric Fixation, where a measure made into a target ceases to measure, effort bending to the number; there the measure is corrupted, while here the motivation is corrupted, the inner reason for the act, and no measure need be involved. And it is distinct from Moral Licensing, where a good deed is banked as moral credit that a later lapse draws down, a moral accounting across acts, a virtue funding a vice; here a single act's inner motive is displaced by an outer one, with no ledger of credit and no later lapse bought.

## Apparent

It looks like proof of a mercenary heart, that the person only ever wanted the pay and never the doing.
