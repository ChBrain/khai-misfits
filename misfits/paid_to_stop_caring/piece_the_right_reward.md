# Piece: The Right Reward

## Taxonomy

[Paid to Stop Caring](play_paid_to_stop_caring.md). The remedy and the bound, the reward reshaped to inform rather than control, and the limits of the whole effect.

## Owner

## Place

At [the Empty Table](place_the_empty_table.md), carried by [Informing](process_informing.md), voiced by [the Steward](persona_the_steward.md): the same table, set differently, where a word is offered instead of a wage.

## Load Bearing

The reward that informs competence rather than controls behaviour, a word of praise, an unexpected acknowledgement, a recognition of real competence, that encourages without supplying a controlling reason because it feeds the sense of doing the thing well rather than of doing it for pay, or the leaving of the freely given unpaid, costly because it forgoes the simple lever of the expected reward and asks the giver first to read whether a thing is already loved (Deci, Koestner and Ryan).

And the effect it answers has a bound, which the Steward holds as carefully as the remedy itself: it is real but bounded, strongest for expected tangible controlling rewards on already-loved tasks, and weak or absent for dull tasks, for unexpected rewards, and for verbal praise; its magnitude was once sharply contested (Cameron and Pierce). The Steward's caution is not a loophole, it is the shape of the whole claim, no wider than the evidence.

## Apparent

It looks like withholding, a meanness dressed as wisdom, a Steward too stingy to simply pay for good work.
