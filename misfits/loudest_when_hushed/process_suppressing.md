## Initiated by

## Direction

From the wish to remove toward the move that removes, and, by removing, advertises.

## Lever

Removing a thing should leave fewer eyes on it, and that is the whole of the intent; but the removal is not a private act. A suit is filed on the record, a notice is sent and answered, an order is sought and granted or refused, and each of these is itself visible: an act that anyone watching can read as a thing someone did not want seen (the coinage; Jansen and Martin).
