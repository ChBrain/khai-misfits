# Place: The Wild

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). Where the wanting sparked at [the Square](place_the_square.md) turns to copies scattered past any gathering: mirrors, reposts, screenshots kept beyond the reach of any single takedown. Hosts [Beyond Recall](plot_beyond_recall.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The mirrors standing up as fast as the original comes down. The reposts, each a small act of its own. The screenshots, saved once and passed on forever.

## Holds

[The Copies](piece_the_copies.md).

## Offers

To the copier, free and unbounded means to keep a thing and pass it on: no fee, no gatekeeper, no limit on how many hands it can pass through.

## Withheld

Any point of recall. The wild has no ledger and no single shelf; nothing gathered here can be gathered back, by suit or by notice or by any hand that once tried to take it down.
