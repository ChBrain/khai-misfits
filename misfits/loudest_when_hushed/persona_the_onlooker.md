# Persona: The Onlooker

## Taxonomy

[Onlooker](position_onlooker.md), inside [Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The archetype of the one who would never have looked, until told they may not, and who reads the move to hide as the sign that here is a thing worth seeing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An idle bystander, no stake in the thing. It looks like someone who would have scrolled past without a second glance, indifferent to an obscure photograph or a stray post, until something turns the indifference into attention.

## Action

Notice, and go looking. At [the Square](place_the_square.md), the Onlooker meets [the Signal](piece_the_signal.md), the removal read as here is something someone did not want seen, then on [Go and Look](plan_go_and_look.md) through [Reacting](process_reacting.md) takes up [the Reactance](piece_the_reactance.md), wanting the withheld thing the more for being told it is off limits (Brehm). What would have gone unseen it now seeks out.

## Shadow

Its wanting is stirred by the ban, not by the thing. The Onlooker is not drawn by the photograph itself, which it would have ignored, but by the freedom the takedown seems to threaten, and it looks to reassert that freedom as much as to see the thing (Brehm). It is no conspirator and no mob, only a person reacting to what looks like overreach, and it owes no cause for blame.

## Tell

It did not care about the thing until it was told the thing was forbidden.
