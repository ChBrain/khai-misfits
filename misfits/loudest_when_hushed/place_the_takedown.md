# Place: The Takedown

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). Where the move to remove is made: the letter written, the suit filed, the notice sent, the order sought, and, in the same breath, where the move can be refused instead. Hosts [The Move to Hide](plot_the_move_to_hide.md), [Let It Lie](plot_let_it_lie.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The filing on the desk. The notice drafted and ready to send. The order asked of a court or a platform. A hand hovering over the choice: send it, or set it down.

## Holds

[The Suppression](piece_the_suppression.md), the move made. [The Shrug](piece_the_shrug.md), the move refused.

## Offers

To the one with a real grievance, the lawful and locally sensible means to have the offending thing gone: the letter that can be sent, the suit that can be filed, the ban that can be sought.

## Withheld

Any way to make the removal itself unseen. Whatever is filed here becomes a matter of record the moment it is filed; the place has no quiet channel, only ones that leave a trace once used.
