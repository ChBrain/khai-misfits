# Position: Onlooker

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The seat of the one who would not have looked until told they may not, and who reads the move to hide as the sign the thing is worth seeing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

No prior stake in the thing: an obscure photograph or a stray post it would have scrolled past without a glance.
A freedom it feels threatened the moment a thing is put off limits, and a pull to reassert that freedom by exercising it.
A view of the move to hide, since the suppression is visible, so the seat sees the ban and reads it as a sign before it ever sees the thing.

## Orders

Go and look, and see what they did not want seen.
Seek out the thing you are told you cannot have, because being told sharpens the wanting and the looking reasserts the freedom the ban seemed to threaten.
Know that it is the ban and not the thing that stirred you, that you would have ignored the photograph itself, and that you are reacting to a perceived overreach and not conspiring, a bystander made a seeker by the telling.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and its reaction is the thing's amplification.
Because the withheld thing is wanted the more for being withheld, the seat that had no interest acquires one the instant the thing is forbidden, and its looking, multiplied across a networked crowd, is the attention the suppression drew to the thing.
It keeps its innocence of any plot and loses no cause for blame, the idle bystander the ban turned into an audience.

## Drives

Toward the one the ban made curious,
wanting the withheld thing the more for being told, reacting to overreach and not scheming,
so the seat that would have looked away looks hard, the freedom reasserted by the very looking, the forbidden made a draw.
