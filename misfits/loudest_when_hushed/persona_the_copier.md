# Persona: The Copier

## Taxonomy

[Copier](position_copier.md), inside [Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The archetype of the many hands that mirror and repost and screenshot the thing, so that once the eye is drawn the thing passes beyond any recall.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A preserver, saving something before it vanishes. It looks like someone doing a small and harmless thing, keeping a copy, sharing a link, taking a screenshot, each act trivial on its own and none of it meaning any harm.

## Action

Copy, and spread. At [the Wild](place_the_wild.md), on [Get It Out](plan_get_it_out.md) through [Copying](process_copying.md), the Copier makes [the Copies](piece_the_copies.md), the mirrors and reposts and screenshots that cost nothing to make, so that no later takedown can find every one and the thing is beyond recall (Jansen and Martin).

## Shadow

Its power is in the cheapness and the number, not in any one hand. No single copy matters and no single copier means to defeat the suppressor, but copying is free and the copiers are many, so the sum is a thing that can never be gathered back, and the removal that might have worked on one host fails against a thousand. It is many ordinary people, each doing a trivial thing, and no villain among them.

## Tell

Each copy is nothing, and together they are past recall.
