# Plot: Beyond Recall

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Wild](place_the_wild.md), the open ground where the wanting turns to copying, and where a copy, once made, cannot be gathered back in.

## Action

[The Copier](persona_the_copier.md), from [the copier's seat](position_copier.md), on [Get It Out](plan_get_it_out.md) through [Copying](process_copying.md), makes [The Copies](piece_the_copies.md) at [The Wild](place_the_wild.md): the mirrors and reposts and screenshots that cost nothing to make, so that no later takedown can find every one.

## Stage

Irrecoverability: copying costs the copier almost nothing, and copiers are many, so a takedown that must find and remove every copy fails against a copier who need only make and keep one (Jansen and Martin). The thing is amplified in place, the same item multiplied where it already stood, not relocated to some new corner as displacement would move it.

## Tension

Once the copies are out, no takedown can gather them back.
