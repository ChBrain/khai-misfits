# Position: Copier

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The seat of the many hands that mirror and repost the thing at no cost, putting it beyond any recall once the eye is drawn.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A means of copying that costs nothing: the mirror, the repost, the screenshot, each made in a moment and needing no leave.
A number that no removal can match, since the copiers are many and scattered and a takedown must find every one to succeed while a copier need only miss one.
A small and innocent motive in each hand, to save a thing, to share it, to keep a record, none of it meaning to defeat the suppressor.

## Orders

Get it out, and keep a copy before it is gone.
Mirror the thing, repost it, screenshot it, because it costs you nothing and because it may vanish, so save it while you can.
Know that no one copy matters and the sum is past recall, that your trivial act joined to a thousand others is what makes the thing ungatherable, and that you are preserving and sharing, not conspiring, the harm in the number and not in the hand.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and the sum of its trivial acts is the point of no return.
Because copying is free and the copiers are many, the thing the suppressor moved to remove is scattered past any gathering back, and the removal that might have worked against one host fails against a thousand mirrors.
It keeps its innocent motive and loses no cause for blame, the many small hands whose sum no takedown can undo.

## Drives

Toward the many who copy at no cost,
each saving or sharing a thing in a trivial harmless act, none meaning the suppressor any harm,
so the seat that is nothing alone is beyond recall together, the thing put past any taking-back the moment the eye is drawn.
