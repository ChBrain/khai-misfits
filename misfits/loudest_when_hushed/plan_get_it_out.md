# Plan: Get It Out

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the copier's seat](position_copier.md), inside [Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Copier](position_copier.md)

## Direction

Mirror the thing, repost it, screenshot it, because it costs you nothing and because it may vanish, so save it while you can. Know that no one copy matters and the sum is past recall, that your trivial act joined to a thousand others is what makes the thing ungatherable, and that you are preserving and sharing, not conspiring, the harm in the number and not in the hand.

## Orders

([The Copier](persona_the_copier.md) makes [the Copies](piece_the_copies.md) through [Copying](process_copying.md) at [the Wild](place_the_wild.md), the mirrors and reposts and screenshots that cost nothing, so no later takedown can find every one.)

## Implementation

([Copying](process_copying.md) at [the Wild](place_the_wild.md) spawns [the Copies](piece_the_copies.md) at no cost and in great number, so the thing is scattered past any gathering back and the removal that worked on one host fails against a thousand.)

## Targets

- [ ] the thing is mirrored, reposted, screenshotted, a copy kept before it can vanish
- [ ] the cheapness is understood as the lever, each copy free to make and needing no leave
- [ ] the number is seen to be past recall, the sum of trivial acts no takedown can undo
- [ ] the motive is owned as preserving and sharing and not defeating anyone, no villain in the hand
