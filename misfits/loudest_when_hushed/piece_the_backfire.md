# Piece: The Backfire

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The amplified reach the suppression wins for the very thing it meant to bury: an obscure item seen far more than it would ever have been left alone, the outcome the reverse of the intent. The harm.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Lands at [The Square](place_the_square.md), where the once-obscure thing is now the thing everyone has seen.

## Load Bearing

The net effect across the chain: the attempt to suppress produces more attention than silence would have (the coinage; Brian Martin). It is authored by no one and lands as no one's comeuppance; the amplified reach is the structure's doing, not a punishment dealt out by the crowd or the copiers.

## Apparent

Looks like the thing suddenly everywhere: on every feed, in every mouth, as though it had been famous all along.
