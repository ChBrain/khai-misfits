# Position: Adviser

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The seat of the one who counsels the strategic silence, and who marks that the silence is costly and available only before the move to hide is made.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one course that does not signal: to say nothing, leave the thing unremarked, and deny the fight the oxygen that spreads it.
A narrow window for it, since the silence works only before the takedown is filed, once the move is made the signal is already sent and cannot be recalled.
A real price attached, because saying nothing means tolerating the very exposure the wronged party wanted gone.

## Orders

Say nothing, and let the thing lie.
Counsel silence, leave the photograph up and the post live, and deny the fight the attention that would spread it, since the backfire runs on the visibility of the move to hide and only silence withholds it.
Know that the silence costs the exposure endured, that its window closes the moment the takedown is filed, and that where a genuine and urgent harm demands removal the silence is no counsel at all, the hedge you stand on and the bound you must name.

## Loses

The exposure it chooses to tolerate, and it loses that knowingly.
Because the only move that does not backfire is the move not made, the seat buys the quiet at the price of leaving the thing seen, and forfeits the remedy it could have reached for, keeping the peace by keeping still.
It keeps its clear sight of the cost and loses no cause for blame, the cool head that counsels the dear and narrow way out.

## Drives

Toward the one who counsels doing nothing,
saying nothing and letting the thing lie so the fight is denied its oxygen, at the price of the exposure endured,
so the seat that alone escapes the backfire escapes it only before the move is made and only by tolerating the thing, the way out real and dear and narrow.
