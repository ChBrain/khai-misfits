## Action

## Stage

The hiding advertises. Being visible, the act of suppression carries information in itself, that the thing removed matters enough to someone that they moved against it, and that carried information draws the eye the move meant to turn away (Jansen and Martin). Distinct from the Cobra Effect: no reward is offered and no incentive is farmed here, nothing is gamed for a bounty. What grows is attention to the one thing, not more of the thing itself.

## Tension

The surest sign a thing is worth seeing is that someone tried to hide it.
