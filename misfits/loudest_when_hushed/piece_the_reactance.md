# Piece: The Reactance

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The forbidden made desired: those told they cannot have a thing want it the more, and look for a way to reassert the freedom the telling threatened.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Square](place_the_square.md), carried by [Reacting](process_reacting.md): the crowd that reads the notice and turns to look for what it named.

## Load Bearing

Psychological reactance: when a freedom is threatened or removed, the wanting of the threatened thing rises, and the person moves to restore it (Brehm, 1966). It is the ban, and not the thing itself, that stirs the wanting; take the ban away and the pull goes with it.

## Apparent

Looks like sudden, genuine curiosity in the thing itself, as though it had always been this interesting.
