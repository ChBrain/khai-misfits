# Plan: Say Nothing

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the adviser's seat](position_adviser.md), inside [Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Adviser](position_adviser.md)

## Direction

Counsel silence, leave the photograph up and the post live, and deny the fight the attention that would spread it, since the backfire runs on the visibility of the move to hide and only silence withholds it. Know that the silence costs the exposure endured, that its window closes the moment the takedown is filed, and that where a genuine and urgent harm demands removal the silence is no counsel at all, the hedge you stand on and the bound you must name.

## Orders

([The Adviser](persona_the_adviser.md) holds [the Shrug](piece_the_shrug.md) through [Shrugging](process_shrugging.md) at [the Takedown](place_the_takedown.md), urging the thing be left unremarked so the fight is denied the oxygen that would spread it.)

## Implementation

([Shrugging](process_shrugging.md) at [the Takedown](place_the_takedown.md) withholds [the Signal](piece_the_signal.md), because the move never made emits nothing, so the thing stays as obscure as it was, at the price of the exposure tolerated and only while the window is open.)

## Targets

- [ ] the thing is left unremarked, the photograph up and the post live, no move made against it
- [ ] the silence is understood to work by withholding the signal the move would have sent
- [ ] the price is named, the exposure endured and the window closing once the takedown is filed
- [ ] the bound is held, that where an urgent harm demands removal the silence is no way out at all
