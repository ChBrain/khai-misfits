# Plan: Go and Look

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the onlooker's seat](position_onlooker.md), inside [Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Onlooker](position_onlooker.md)

## Direction

Seek out the thing you are told you cannot have, because being told sharpens the wanting and the looking reasserts the freedom the ban seemed to threaten. Know that it is the ban and not the thing that stirred you, that you would have ignored the photograph itself, and that you are reacting to a perceived overreach and not conspiring, a bystander made a seeker by the telling.

## Orders

([The Onlooker](persona_the_onlooker.md), having met [the Signal](piece_the_signal.md) at [the Square](place_the_square.md), takes up [the Reactance](piece_the_reactance.md) through [Reacting](process_reacting.md), wanting the withheld thing the more for being told it is off limits.)

## Implementation

([Reacting](process_reacting.md) at [the Square](place_the_square.md) turns the ban into a draw, because [the Reactance](piece_the_reactance.md) makes the forbidden thing wanted the more, so the one who would have looked away goes looking.)

## Targets
