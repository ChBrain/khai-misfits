# Plot: See What They Hid

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Square](place_the_square.md), where those who read the signal are now told plainly they may not have the thing.

## Action

[The Onlooker](persona_the_onlooker.md), from [the onlooker's seat](position_onlooker.md), on [Go and Look](plan_go_and_look.md) through [Reacting](process_reacting.md), takes up [The Reactance](piece_the_reactance.md) at [The Square](place_the_square.md): wanting the withheld thing the more for being told it is off limits, the ban turned into a draw.

## Stage

This is psychological reactance: a threatened or withdrawn freedom raises the wanting of the threatened thing, and the person moves to reassert it (Brehm). It is the ban, and not the thing itself, that stirs the wanting, no plot in it, no conspiracy, only a bystander reacting to what looks like overreach.

## Tension

The telling that it is forbidden is what makes them look.
