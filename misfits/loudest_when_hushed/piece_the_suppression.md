# Piece: The Suppression

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The move to remove: the letter written, the takedown filed, the suit brought, the injunction sought, the ban imposed. The trigger the whole misfit turns on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Made at [The Takedown](place_the_takedown.md), carried by [Suppressing](process_suppressing.md): the desk where the filing is drafted and sent.

## Load Bearing

Locally rational on its own terms: removing a thing should leave fewer eyes on it, and that is the whole of the intent. But the removal is not a private act. A suit is filed on the record, a notice is sent and answered, an order is sought and granted or refused, and each of these is itself a visible act that anyone watching can read (the coinage; Jansen and Martin). It cannot be made unseen once made. This is distinct from the Balloon Effect: nothing is pushed elsewhere and popping up in a new place. The same thing, in the same place, is amplified.

## Apparent

The obvious lawful remedy. A wronged party doing the reasonable, sensible thing: asking that an offending item be taken down, exactly as anyone with a real grievance would.
