# Plan: Take It Down

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the suppressor's seat](position_suppressor.md), inside [Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Suppressor](position_suppressor.md)

## Direction

File the takedown or the suit, since removing a thing should leave fewer to see it, and you would rather it gone. Know that the removal is visible, that filing it says here is a thing worth hiding and so worth seeing, and that the more openly and heavily you move the louder the thing grows, the visibility of the act and not any malice the engine of the harm.

## Orders

([The Suppressor](persona_the_suppressor.md) holds [the Suppression](piece_the_suppression.md) through [Suppressing](process_suppressing.md) at [the Takedown](place_the_takedown.md), moving to remove the thing because removing it should leave fewer eyes on it.)

## Implementation

([Suppressing](process_suppressing.md) at [the Takedown](place_the_takedown.md) is itself a visible act, so it emits [the Signal](piece_the_signal.md) that here is a thing someone did not want seen, and the move to remove becomes the move that advertises.)

## Targets

- [ ] the thing is moved against, the takedown or the suit filed to have it gone
- [ ] the move is understood as locally rational, removing a thing should leave fewer to see it
- [ ] the removal is seen to be itself visible, an act on the record that cannot be made unseen
- [ ] the visibility of the move is owned as the engine of the harm, not any malice, no villain in it
