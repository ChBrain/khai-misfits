# Pitch: The Surest Sign It Matters

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that the act of hiding a thing is itself the surest sign the thing is worth seeing, so the hiding advertises.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Quiet, reasonable, sympathetic. Someone with a real interest to protect, a privacy, a reputation, a secret, doing the sensible thing and moving to take a thing down, since removing it should leave fewer to see it. Nothing here is a scheme; the interest is real and the move is the obvious one.

## Undertow

The removal is itself a visible act, and a visible act carries a signal: that here is a thing worth the trouble of hiding, which is the surest sign it is worth seeing (Jansen and Martin). Those told they cannot have it want it the more (Brehm), and on a medium where copying is free, the wanting spawns mirrors that put the thing beyond recall. The suppression amplifies the one thing, in the place it already was, far past what silence would.

## Nerve

That the very act meant to hide a thing is the surest sign it matters, so the more visibly you move to suppress, the louder the thing you wanted quiet. The suppressor is no villain and means only to protect what is theirs; the visibility of the move, not any malice, is what turns the hiding into an advertisement.

## Echo

Move to hide it and you have told the world it is worth seeing, and the thing is loudest when hushed.
