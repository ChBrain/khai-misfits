# Piece: The Signal

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The meaning the move broadcasts: here is a thing someone did not want seen, and that alone is the surest sign it is worth seeing. The reason the whole thing backfires.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Read at [The Square](place_the_square.md), where anyone watching sees the suit filed, the notice sent, the ban sought.

## Load Bearing

The act of suppression, being visible, carries information in itself: that the thing removed matters enough to someone that they moved against it. That carried information draws the eye the act meant to turn away (Jansen and Martin). This is the pivot of the whole misfit: the content of the thing suppressed matters less than the fact that suppressing it was tried.

## Apparent

Looks like ordinary news: word of a lawsuit, a takedown notice, a ban, nothing more than a fact reported.
