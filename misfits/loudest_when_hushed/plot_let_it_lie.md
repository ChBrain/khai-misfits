# Plot: Let It Lie

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Takedown](place_the_takedown.md), the desk where the filing was drafted, weighed now against the shrug that never sends it.

## Action

[The Adviser](persona_the_adviser.md), from [the adviser's seat](position_adviser.md), on [Say Nothing](plan_say_nothing.md) through [Shrugging](process_shrugging.md), holds [The Shrug](piece_the_shrug.md) at [The Takedown](place_the_takedown.md): the strategic silence that leaves the thing unremarked and denies the fight the oxygen that would spread it.

## Stage

The way out is real but costly, since it means tolerating the very exposure the suppressor wanted gone, and it works only before the move is made, once a filing is on the record the signal is already sent and cannot be recalled. And here the bound holds: the backfire needs the suppression to be visible, to read as heavy-handed or unjust, and to meet a networked audience able and willing to copy and resist, so quiet, lawful, unremarked, or widely-accepted removal need not backfire at all, and much is taken down every day and stays down. Where a genuine and urgent harm demands removal regardless of cost, the silence is no option (Jansen and Martin).

## Tension

The only move that does not backfire is the move not made, and it costs the exposure endured.
