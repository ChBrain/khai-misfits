# Piece: The Shrug

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The strategic silence: say nothing, leave the thing unremarked, deny the fight the oxygen that would spread it. The remedy, and the bound on the whole misfit.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Takedown](place_the_takedown.md), the move refused there instead of made, carried by [Shrugging](process_shrugging.md).

## Load Bearing

The only course that does not signal, because the move never made emits nothing (Jansen and Martin). It is costly: it means tolerating the very exposure the suppressor wanted gone, and it is available only before the takedown is filed, never after. It also carries the bound on the whole misfit: the backfire needs the suppression to be visible, to read as heavy-handed or unjust, and to meet a networked audience able and willing to copy and resist. Quiet, lawful, unremarked, or widely-accepted removal need not backfire at all, and much is taken down every day and stays down. Where a genuine and urgent harm demands removal regardless of the cost, the silence is no option.

## Apparent

Looks like doing nothing, or like weakness: a wronged party who had every means to act and chose not to.
