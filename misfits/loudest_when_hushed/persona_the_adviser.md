# Persona: The Adviser

## Taxonomy

[Adviser](position_adviser.md), inside [Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The archetype of the one who counsels the strategic silence, to say nothing and let the thing lie, and who marks that the silence is costly and available only before the move to hide is made.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A cool head counselling patience. It looks like the calm counsel that says do nothing, the wise restraint that sees further than the wronged party's first urge to strike back and remove what stings.

## Action

Counsel silence. At [the Takedown](place_the_takedown.md), on [Say Nothing](plan_say_nothing.md) through [Shrugging](process_shrugging.md), the Adviser holds [the Shrug](piece_the_shrug.md), urging that the thing be left unremarked so the fight is denied the oxygen that would spread it, the one course that does not signal (Jansen and Martin).

## Shadow

Its counsel asks a real price and has a short window. To say nothing is to tolerate the very exposure the suppressor wanted gone, to leave the photograph up and the post live, and it works only before the takedown is filed, since once the move is made the signal is already sent and cannot be recalled. Where a genuine and urgent harm demands removal, its silence is no counsel at all, and it knows the hedge it stands on.

## Tell

Its whole advice is to do nothing, and it admits doing nothing is not free.
