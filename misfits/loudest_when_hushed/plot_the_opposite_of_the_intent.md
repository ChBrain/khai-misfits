# Plot: The Opposite of the Intent

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Square](place_the_square.md), where the reach the suppression won for the thing is now plain to see.

## Action

[The Suppressor](persona_the_suppressor.md), from [the suppressor's seat](position_suppressor.md), meets [The Backfire](piece_the_backfire.md) at [The Square](place_the_square.md): the once-obscure thing now seen far more than it would ever have been left alone, the outcome the reverse of the intent.

## Stage

The attempt to suppress produced more attention than silence would have (the coinage; Brian Martin). It is authored by no one: the Suppressor kept a real grievance and lawful means throughout, the crowd only reacted to overreach or fresh news, and the amplified reach is the structure's doing, not a punishment dealt out to anyone, no comeuppance in it. Distinct from Fixes that Fail: there is no delayed rebound running through a symptom quietly relieved first. The backfire is immediate and direct, the act of hiding itself the advertisement.

## Tension

The thing is famous for the very move that meant to bury it.
