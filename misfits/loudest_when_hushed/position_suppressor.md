# Position: Suppressor

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The seat of the one who moves to remove a thing to protect a real interest, whose visible move to hide it is itself the act that spreads it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real and sympathetic interest to protect: a privacy, a reputation, a secret, harmed by the thing and worth defending.
A lawful and obvious means to hand: the takedown, the letter, the suit, the ban, each of which should, all else equal, leave fewer eyes on the thing.
No way to make the removal unseen, because the means are themselves acts on the record, so the seat cannot both remove the thing and keep the removing quiet.

## Orders

Take the thing down, and protect what is yours.
File the takedown or the suit, since removing a thing should leave fewer to see it, and you would rather it gone.
Know that the removal is visible, that filing it says here is a thing worth hiding and so worth seeing, and that the more openly and heavily you move the louder the thing grows, the visibility of the act and not any malice the engine of the harm.

## Loses

Nothing of its grievance, and its very defence is what carries the harm.
Because the move to hide is itself an act on the record, the seat that means only to protect broadcasts that here is something someone did not want seen, so the thing it wanted quiet is amplified in the place it already was, seen far more for the trying to hide it.
It keeps its real interest and its good grounds and loses no cause for blame, the wronged party whose remedy is the wrong's advertisement.

## Drives

Toward the one who moves to hide and by moving makes seen,
protecting a real interest by the obvious lawful means, no malice and no lie in it,
so the seat that tries to take the thing down is the seat that spreads it, the suppression the surest signal the thing is worth seeing.
