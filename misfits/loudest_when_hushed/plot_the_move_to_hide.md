# Plot: The Move to Hide

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Takedown](place_the_takedown.md), the desk where a filing is drafted and sent. There is a thing someone wants gone: an obscure photograph, a document in a corner of the web, a stray post that names them.

## Action

[The Suppressor](persona_the_suppressor.md), from [the suppressor's seat](position_suppressor.md), on [Take It Down](plan_take_it_down.md) through [Suppressing](process_suppressing.md), holds [The Suppression](piece_the_suppression.md) at [The Takedown](place_the_takedown.md): the letter written, the notice filed, the suit brought, moving to remove the thing because removing it should leave fewer eyes to see it.

## Stage

The move is locally rational and no lie sits in it: a real interest, a privacy or a reputation or a secret, is genuinely harmed, and the removal is the obvious lawful step to take. But the filing is not a private act. A suit is a matter of record, a notice is sent and answered, and each of these is itself a visible act on the record that anyone watching can read (the coinage; Jansen and Martin). Distinct from the Balloon Effect: nothing here is displaced to a new corner of the web, nothing pops up elsewhere while the total holds steady. The same thing, in the same place, is what gets amplified.

## Tension

The move to remove is itself the act that cannot be made unseen.
