# Persona: The Suppressor

## Taxonomy

[Suppressor](position_suppressor.md), inside [Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The archetype of the one who moves to remove a thing to protect a real and sympathetic interest, and whose visible move to hide it is exactly what makes it seen.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A wronged party setting a wrong right. It looks like someone with a genuine grievance taking the obvious and lawful step, defending a privacy or a reputation or a secret against a thing that harms it, the very picture of a reasonable person protecting what is theirs.

## Action

Move to remove. Holding [the Suppression](piece_the_suppression.md) at [the Takedown](place_the_takedown.md), the Suppressor through [Suppressing](process_suppressing.md), on [Take It Down](plan_take_it_down.md), files the takedown or the suit to have the thing gone, since removing it should leave fewer eyes on it (the coinage). Then at [the Square](place_the_square.md) it meets [the Backfire](piece_the_backfire.md), the thing seen far more for the move to hide it.

## Shadow

Its visible move is the engine, not its malice. Because the removal is itself an act on the record, it signals that here is a thing someone did not want seen, the surest sign it is worth seeing, so the more openly and heavily the Suppressor moves, the louder the thing grows (Jansen and Martin). It keeps its real grievance and owes no cause for blame, the wronged party whose defence is what publishes the wrong.

## Tell

It moves to take the thing down, and by moving makes the thing seen.
