# Piece: The Copies

## Taxonomy

[Loudest When Hushed](play_loudest_when_hushed.md). The mirrors, reposts, and screenshots that cheap copying spawns once a networked audience goes looking, putting the thing beyond any single recall.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [The Wild](place_the_wild.md), carried by [Copying](process_copying.md): the open ground where a copy, once made, cannot be gathered back in.

## Load Bearing

Irrecoverability: copying costs the copier almost nothing, and copiers are many, so a takedown that must find and remove every copy fails against a copier who need only make and keep one (Jansen and Martin). This is distinct from the Cobra Effect: no incentive is gamed, no bounty perversely chased. It is only free copying, multiplying the one thing that was to be reduced.

## Apparent

Looks like ordinary, harmless sharing: someone saving a page, someone reposting a link, the kind of small preserving anyone does without thinking twice.

## Yearbook

Carried by [Copying](process_copying.md), staged at [The Wild](place_the_wild.md), voiced by [The Copier](persona_the_copier.md), dramatized in [Beyond Recall](plot_beyond_recall.md). It is fed by [The Reactance](piece_the_reactance.md), and it produces [The Backfire](piece_the_backfire.md).
