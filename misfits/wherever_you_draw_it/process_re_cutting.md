# Process: Re-cutting

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). Redrawing the areas and computing it all again.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A doubt about the units, held by somebody with the data and the time.
At [the map](place_the_map.md), as many times as anybody is willing to.

## Direction

Toward knowing how much the answer depends on the drawing.
It is the right thing to do and it works: the dependence becomes visible, its size becomes known, and
the analyst who does it understands their own finding better than the one who does not.

## Lever

The number and shape of the areas, both of which move the estimate and **move it separately**.
Neither lever has a correct setting, which is why turning them is diagnosis and not correction.

## Echo

A distribution of coefficients, all admissible,
no rule for choosing among them,
and an analyst who now has to publish one anyway.
