# Place: The Map

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). The territory with its lines on it: continuous ground, divided.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A complete tiling, every point in exactly one area.
Lines following rivers, roads, old parish edges and negotiated compromises.
Nothing marking which lines matter to the question.

## Holds

The whole mechanism, in the form of something nobody would call data.
**The map is upstream of every number in the study** and is not treated as an input to it: it appears
in a methods section as provenance, if at all, and never as a parameter.

## Offers

A way to count a continuous thing.

## Withheld

Any indication that a different tiling was available.
The ground does not come with its own divisions, so the absence of an alternative on the page is a
property of the page and not of the world.
