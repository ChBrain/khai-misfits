# Place: The Boundary File

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). The published geometry: the lines as a dataset, versioned and dated.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Polygons with identifiers, a release year, and a note on what changed since the last one.
Documentation of how the areas were constituted and for what.
Everything an honest analyst would want, published openly.

## Holds

The proof that nothing is hidden.
**The purpose of every boundary is written down and available**, which is what makes the play a
structural one rather than a story about opacity: the analyst can read exactly why these lines are
where they are, and reading it does not help, because the reason is administrative and the question
is not.

## Offers

Complete transparency about the partition.

## Withheld

A second partition with equal standing.
Alternatives can be constructed and none of them is published, endorsed or shared, so the one that
comes with the data is the one the literature will be built on.
