# Pitch: Nobody Drew It Against You

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Exact about what was computed and about what it was computed over.
Unwilling to call any zoning the right one, because none of them is.
Quoting the coefficient wherever the coefficient is the point.

## Undertow

Under the finding, one relation from method and one from the ground.
A statistic computed over aggregates is a statistic about the aggregates.
And the aggregates were already there, drawn by somebody for something else.

## Nerve

The unease of a number that is correct and is not about the world.
Nobody drew it against you: true of every boundary in the file,
and the estimate is still an artefact of where the lines fell.

## Echo

After the run: a coefficient that survives every check the method has,
a confidence interval that answers a question nobody asked,
a second zoning that would have returned something else,
and a committee that needs one number by Thursday.
