## Tension

Here each settlement returns less than the question asked for.
Report the spread across zonings and you have replaced an estimate with a range that is not a
confidence interval, contains no estimator, and gives a committee nothing to act on.
Model at the individual level and you have left the question, since the question was about places.
Find a natural unit and there usually is not one, because the ground does not come with divisions.
Publish one coefficient with a note, which is what almost everybody does, and the note lands in the
slot readers use to check provenance.
And the general form has no geography in it at all: a case belongs to many classes, the probability
moves with the classification, and the argument that this afflicts **every** interpretation of
probability rather than one of them is what puts the difficulty in the method.
**So somebody still has to publish one number, and the one they publish is correct.**
