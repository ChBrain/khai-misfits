# Persona: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Partition](position_partition.md). The line on the map, drawn for a purpose that was not this one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A census tract sized for enumeration, a ward drawn for representation, a postcode built for delivery.
Each defensible on its own terms and well made for the job it was made for.
Older than the question, and unaware of it.

## Action

Encloses.
Divides what is continuous.
Determines what is averaged with what.

## Shadow

That it is doing statistical work it was never appointed to.
**Nothing was drawn against this analysis**, and the line still decides which households are summed
together before anything is measured, so a boundary commission's compromise about representation
becomes a term in somebody's regression thirty years later.

## Tell

Is innocent of the question.
Is inside the answer.
