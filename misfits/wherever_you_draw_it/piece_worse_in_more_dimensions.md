# Piece: Worse in More Dimensions

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). The multivariate case, where the movement stops being predictable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the table](place_the_table.md), in every regression with more than one predictor.
In the finding that the effect is essentially unpredictable in multivariate work.

## Load Bearing

Without it an experienced analyst could learn the direction the bias runs and allow for it.
It is load bearing because **the direction is not learnable**: with several variables the intensity
and even the sign of the movement are not predictable from the units, so intuition trained on
bivariate cases does not transfer, and the researcher most familiar with the data is not better
placed than anybody else.

## Apparent

That the problem is a known bias to be corrected.
A bias has a direction. This has a distribution over admissible partitions, and no estimator of the
thing it is a distribution around.

## Yearbook

The multivariate result, reported by its authors as evidence of the unreliability of such analyses.
The play carries their assessment rather than softening it.
