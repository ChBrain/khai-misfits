## Cue

[Drawn for Something Else](piece_drawn_for_something_else.md): a dataset published at areas, and a question about a relationship across a territory.

## Action

[Drawing](process_drawing.md): [The Boundary](persona_the_boundary.md), in [the partition seat](position_partition.md), was set on [Use the Units You Have](plan_use_the_units_you_have.md) long before.

## Stage

[The Boundary File](place_the_boundary_file.md): polygons, identifiers, a release year, and a written account of what each area was constituted for.

## Tension

Here the play establishes that no choice was made badly, because no choice was made.
The tracts are sized for enumeration, the wards drawn to hold communities together, the rounds built
for a van to cover in a day. Every one is well made for its purpose and none was drawn against this
question, and the reasons are **published**, which is what stops this being a play about opacity.
The analyst can read exactly why the lines are where they are. Reading it does not help.
**The one input that will determine the answer is the one input the study contains no decision
about**, and an analyst asked to justify these areas can say only that these are the areas the data
come at, which is true and is not a justification.
