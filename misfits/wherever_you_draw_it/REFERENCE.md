# Wherever You Draw It: Reference

The research warrant for Wherever You Draw It.
The term is the house's; the play runs on the modifiable areal unit problem, on its two limbs of scale
and zonation, and on the general form of a partition with no principled setting, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging spatial statistics as **an answer conditional on a choice nobody can defend**: a relationship
is measured across a territory, the territory arrives already cut into areas by a bureau or a boundary
commission for reasons unrelated to the question, the coefficient is computed correctly, and redrawing
the areas returns a different coefficient that is also correct. The play models the documented
mechanics, the units that are inherited rather than chosen, the aggregation that is the analysis's
first operation rather than a step before it, the two limbs that move the estimate separately, the
report that has no column for the dependence, and the sensitivity run that measures the difficulty and
cannot resolve it, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a tale of a careless analyst or
a gerrymandered map.

The governing law is that a statistic computed over aggregates is a statistic about the aggregates. The
grouping is not preliminary to the analysis: the variance it removes and the covariance it creates are
properties of the boundaries rather than of the people, so the relationship is constituted by the
partition and only measured by the model. And the phenomenon supplies no partition, so one must be
taken from somewhere, and everywhere it can be taken from drew it for something else.

What removes any possibility of blame is that no choice was made badly, because no choice was made. The
tracts were sized for enumeration, the wards drawn to hold communities together, the delivery rounds
built for a van to cover in a day, each well made for its own purpose and none drawn against this
question. The reasons are published, which is what stops this being a play about opacity: an analyst
can read exactly why the lines are where they are, and reading it does not help. The data exist at
those units and usually not below them, so an analyst who wanted others cannot have them.

What makes it structural is that the effect has two limbs and only one of them can be reasoned about.
Change the size of the areas and the estimate moves, which is the scale effect: coarser loses variation,
finer keeps it, and a view can be formed. Change only the shape, holding the number of areas fixed, and
it moves again, and **there is no ordering of those answers**. The same territory cut into the same
number of pieces a different way returns a different coefficient, nothing ranks the tilings, and so
finer is not truer and there is no limit the sequence approaches. In more than two variables the
movement is unpredictable in intensity and in direction, so the analyst most familiar with the data is
no better placed than anybody else.

And the correction returns less than the question asked for. A sensitivity run across many zonings is
the right thing to do and it works, in that the dependence becomes visible and its size becomes known;
what it returns is a spread that is not a confidence interval and contains no estimator, delivered to
somebody who still has to choose. Modelling at the individual level leaves the question, which was about
places. A natural unit mostly does not exist. And publishing one coefficient with a note on the units,
which is what almost everybody does, puts the true statement in the slot readers use to check
provenance.

It is distinct from the house's **Reasonable Either Way**, where undisclosed analytic flexibility lets a
result be chosen after the fact: there the quantity is the flexibility being **hidden**, and stipulating
full pre-registration removes the harm. Here pre-registering the zoning changes the analyst's standing
and not the estimate, because naming in advance which arbitrary partition will be used does not make it
the right one. **The forking-paths play is about a choice that should have been declared; this one is
about a choice with no correct option to declare.**

It is distinct from the house's **The Bigger Picture**, which stages Simpson's paradox: there the quantity
is a confounder that reverses an association when the groups are combined, and stipulating no such
subgroup structure removes the harm. Here the estimate still moves when the zones are redrawn with no
lurking variable anywhere, because the units are themselves the aggregation rather than a thing
aggregated across.

It is distinct from the house's **The Streetlight Effect**, where the search goes where the looking is
easy: there the quantity is the ease of a place, and stipulating every place equally easy removes the
harm. Here every zoning is equally available in principle and the difficulty is that none of them is
correct, so the analyst is not looking in the wrong place but computing over a division the world did
not make.

And it is the shadow of the house's **The Planning Fallacy**, whose remedy is this play's problem.
Reference-class forecasting answers the inside view by pricing a case from the class of cases it
resembles, which presumes that the class can be settled. That is the same difficulty without the
geography, and it is carried here as an Origin row rather than as a second play, since one engine in two
domains is a row.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the
  analyst as careless or the boundaries as rigged, because nobody in the sequence has a decision to get
  wrong: the areas were drawn well for purposes that are documented and published; the data exist at
  those units and usually not below them; the model is correctly specified and passes every diagnostic
  the method offers, all of which are computed inside the partition; and the reader takes a number from
  a table that gives no sign it should be read differently. The failure is authored by nobody and
  emerges from the structure, so the misfit is that a correct estimate can be an artefact of a division
  the world did not make.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. A sensitivity run
  across zonings is the right response, is genuinely informative, and returns a spread that is not a
  confidence interval and contains no estimator. Modelling individuals abandons a question that was
  about places. Choosing units on substantive grounds is available, is better than not thinking about
  it, and produces one more admissible partition, with the added hazard that a partition selected to
  suit a question is the worse failure. Pre-registration fixes the analyst's standing and leaves the
  number as dependent as it was.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the quantitative geography and the philosophy of
  probability are theirs. The bounds are carried with them: the size of the effect varies enormously by
  dataset and by the relationship being estimated, and some findings are robust across zonings, so the
  play stages a hazard that must be checked for rather than a defect present in every areal study; the
  field has developed real partial answers, among them substantively motivated zoning design, explicitly
  multi-scale methods and analysis at the finest available resolution, and none of them is nothing; and
  the reference class row is a philosophical argument about the interpretation of probability rather
  than an empirical result, and is cited for the shape of the difficulty and not as evidence of the
  spatial one. **The magnitudes those papers report are deliberately not carried here**, because the
  papers themselves could not be read in the pass that cited them: publisher hosts and the article
  archive are blocked to it, so the bibliography, the results and their directions were verified by
  search and corroborated across independent reports, which is the treatment a citation survives and a
  reported effect size is not. **The play makes no claim that areal analysis should not be done**, since
  most questions about places have no other form, only that its answers carry a condition the page has
  no room for.
