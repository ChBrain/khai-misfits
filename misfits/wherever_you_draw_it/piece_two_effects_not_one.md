# Piece: Two Effects, Not One

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). Scale and zonation: the estimate moves with the size of the areas and again with their shape.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the map](place_the_map.md), whenever the same territory is cut a second way.
In the standard decomposition of the problem into its two limbs.

## Load Bearing

Without it the difficulty is a resolution question and might be answered by going finer.
It is load bearing because **the second limb has no ordering**: change only the shape, holding the
number of areas fixed, and the estimate moves again, so there is no direction in which the answer
gets better and no limit it approaches. Finer is not truer; it is different.

## Apparent

That the smallest available units are the safest.
They are one more admissible partition. The scale limb makes them a defensible default and the
zonation limb means a different tiling at the same fineness would have returned something else.

## Yearbook

The two-limbed statement of the problem, scale and zoning, as the literature sets it out.
Naming both is what stops the play collapsing into an argument about resolution.
