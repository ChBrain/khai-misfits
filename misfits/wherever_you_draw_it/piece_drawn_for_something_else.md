# Piece: Drawn for Something Else

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). The inheritance: the partition arrives from a purpose unrelated to the question.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the boundary file](place_the_boundary_file.md), documented and dated.
In every dataset published at areas rather than at people.

## Load Bearing

Without it the analyst chose the units and the play is about a bad choice.
It is load bearing because **there was no choice to make well**: the areas were constituted for
enumeration or representation, the data exist only at them, and an analyst who wanted others usually
cannot have them. So the one input that determines the answer is the one input the study does not
contain a decision about.

## Apparent

That a better-motivated set of areas would fix it.
It would move the estimate, which is not the same thing. Any partition drawn deliberately for this
question would still be one of many admissible ones, and choosing it to suit the question is the
worse failure.

## Yearbook

The standard observation that areal units in social data are administrative artefacts.
The play uses it to remove the decision, and with it the blame.
