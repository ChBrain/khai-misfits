# Process: Reporting

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). Putting the estimate into a table for other people to use.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[Estimating](process_estimating.md), and a finding worth communicating.
At [the table](place_the_table.md), once, and thereafter quoted.

## Direction

Toward a legible, comparable, quotable result.
This is what publication is for and the format is not a failing: a field in which every finding is
reported the same way is a field whose findings can be compared, which is most of what makes a
literature.

## Lever

The note on data sources, which is where the units get mentioned.
It is the only slot the format has for this, and it is a slot readers use to check provenance rather
than to bound a claim, so **the true statement lands in the place reserved for uninteresting ones**.

## Echo

A row in a table,
identical in form to a row that would not move,
and a finding that travels without its condition.
