# Persona: The Estimate

## Taxonomy

[Publication](position_publication.md). The coefficient: correct, reproducible, and conditional on a choice it does not mention.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A number with a sign, a magnitude and a standard error.
Reproducible exactly by anybody with the same data and the same units.
Carries no mark of the partition it was computed over.

## Action

Comes out of the model.
Goes into the table.
Moves when the units are redrawn.

## Shadow

That its precision is real and is about the wrong thing.
The standard error says how far it would move if the sample were drawn again, and **nothing here was
sampled again**: the movement that matters comes from re-cutting a territory that was fully observed,
so the quantity most likely to be read as the uncertainty is the one that does not measure it.

## Tell

Is correct.
Is not a fact about the world it appears to describe.
