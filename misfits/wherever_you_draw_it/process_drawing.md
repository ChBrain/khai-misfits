# Process: Drawing

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). Somebody cutting a territory into administrative areas, for administrative reasons.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A need to enumerate, to represent, or to deliver.
At [the boundary file](place_the_boundary_file.md), decades before the analysis.

## Direction

Toward areas that are workable for their purpose.
This is done well and is done by people who know what they are doing: tracts of roughly equal
population, wards that hold communities together, rounds that a van can cover in a day.

## Lever

The purpose being served, which is never this one.
There is no lever here for the analyst at all, because the process completed before the question
existed and its participants are not available to be consulted about it.

## Echo

A complete, non-overlapping partition,
defensible on its own terms,
and a statistical instrument nobody built.
