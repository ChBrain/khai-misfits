# Position: Publication

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). The seat where the estimate becomes a row in a table.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A coefficient, a sign, a standard error and a note on the data source.
A format shared with every other finding in the field.
Space for one number, because that is what the table has room for.

## Orders

Report the result.
Note the units the data came at.
Do not clutter the finding.

## Loses

The distinction between two kinds of movement.
The interval printed beside the estimate describes resampling and the movement that matters is
re-cutting, and **the page has one slot and prints the wrong one**, so the more carefully the
uncertainty is reported the more confidently the dependence is hidden.

## Drives

Toward a legible finding,
which is what publication is for,
so the seat that makes the work usable is the seat that strips the condition off it.
