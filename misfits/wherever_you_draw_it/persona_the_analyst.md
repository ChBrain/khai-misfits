# Persona: The Analyst

## Taxonomy

[Computation](position_computation.md). The person who runs the model and reports what it returns.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent, careful, and correct at every step that has a correct answer.
Specifies the model well, checks the assumptions, reports the standard errors.
Did not draw the boundaries and in most cases cannot obtain others.

## Action

Takes the units the data come in.
Computes the estimate.
Reports it.

## Shadow

That the one choice with no right answer is the one nobody made.
**The partition was not selected and cannot be defended**, because it was inherited, and an analyst
asked why these areas can answer only that these are the areas the data are published at, which is
true and is not a justification.

## Tell

Is the party who did everything correctly.
Is the party whose number moves when somebody else draws the map.
