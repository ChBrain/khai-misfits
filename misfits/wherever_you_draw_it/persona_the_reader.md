# Persona: The Reader

## Taxonomy

[Reading](position_reading.md). Whoever takes the number out of the table and acts on it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A committee, a minister, a journalist, a later analyst building on the finding.
Competent to read a coefficient, a sign and an interval.
Not in a position to re-run the study at other units, and with no reason to think it would matter.

## Action

Reads the number.
Reads the interval beside it.
Concludes something about people rather than about areas.

## Shadow

That there is nothing in the report to read differently.
**The dependence leaves no trace on the page**: the estimate looks like every other estimate, the
interval looks like every other interval, and the note about units, where there is one, reads as a
description of the data rather than as the boundary of the claim.

## Tell

Is not being misled by anybody.
Is the party the harm lands on.
