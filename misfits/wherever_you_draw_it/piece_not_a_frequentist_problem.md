# Piece: Not a Frequentist Problem

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). The same engine without the geography, which is what shows it is not about maps.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Outside the map entirely, in the reference class problem.
Wherever a probability is assigned to a case that belongs to many classes at once.

## Load Bearing

Without it the play is a geographer's difficulty and other fields may reasonably ignore it.
It is load bearing because **the general form is the same and has no geography in it**: a case can be
classified many ways, the probability moves with the classification, and nothing in the method
selects one. The areas are one instance of a partition the phenomenon does not supply, and the
argument that this afflicts every interpretation of probability rather than one of them is what
places the difficulty in the method and not in the data.

## Apparent
