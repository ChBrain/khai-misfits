# Process: Aggregating

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). Summing individuals into area totals, which is where the estimate is really made.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Data published at the units and not below them.
At [the table](place_the_table.md), before any model is fitted.

## Direction

Toward one row per area.
This is not a preliminary to the analysis: **it is the analysis's first operation**, and the variance
it removes and the covariance it creates are both properties of the boundaries rather than of the
people, so the relationship being estimated is constituted here and only measured later.

## Lever

Which households fall together, which is decided by [drawing](process_drawing.md).
The analyst has no lever on it, and the model downstream has no way to represent that it happened.

## Echo

Area means that are all correct,
a covariance structure that came out of the map,
and a dataset that looks like a sample of anything.
