# Position: Recourse

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). The seat of the answers: what an analyst can actually do about it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The option of re-cutting and recomputing, at whatever cost.
The option of modelling individuals, where individual data exist.
The option of a note.

## Orders

Do the most rigorous thing available.
Report it honestly.
Still deliver a finding.

## Loses

The form the question was asked in.
Each answer trades the thing that was wanted: the spread is a range and not an estimate, the
individual model is about people and the question was about places, and **the note is a caveat in a
document whose purpose is to be quoted from**.

## Drives

Toward honesty about the difficulty,
which is achievable and is achieved,
so the seat that behaves best is the seat that returns least.
