# Position: Partition

## Taxonomy

[Wherever You Draw It](play_wherever_you_draw_it.md). The seat where the territory is cut into areas, before any measurement happens.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A set of boundaries, complete and non-overlapping.
A purpose, which is enumeration or representation or delivery.
No relationship whatever to the question that will be asked of it.

## Orders

Divide the ground.
Serve the purpose it was drawn for.
Persist, because redrawing is expensive and disruptive.

## Loses

Its own standing to be questioned.
This seat is occupied **before the analysis begins and by somebody else**, so there is no moment in
the study at which the partition is chosen, and therefore no moment at which it could be justified:
it arrives as a condition rather than as a decision.

## Drives

Toward stable, administrable areas,
which is what a boundary is for and what these ones achieve,
so the seat that makes the territory countable is the seat that decides the count.
