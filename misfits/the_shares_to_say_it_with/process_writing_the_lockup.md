# Process: Writing the Lockup

## Taxonomy

[The Shares to Say It With](play_the_shares_to_say_it_with.md). The commitment, which the offering requires and which freezes what it binds.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An untested company, and buyers who need a reason to believe the insiders will not leave in the morning.
[The Underwriter](persona_the_underwriter.md), holding [Underwriter](position_underwriter.md), before the listing.

## Direction

Toward an offering that can be sold.
Toward [the lockup](piece_the_lockup.md), binding the holders for a stated term.

## Lever

The absence of any weaker instrument that still binds: a commitment that leaves the shares saleable is not a commitment.

## Echo

A credible offering, a public date, and a register most of which cannot reach the market.
