# Process: Borrowing to Sell Short

## Taxonomy

[The Shares to Say It With](play_the_shares_to_say_it_with.md). The correcting trade, and the supply it is rationed by.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A printed date, a view about it, and the intention to sell it forward.
[The Short](persona_the_short.md), holding [Short](position_short.md), in [the quiet six months](place_the_quiet_six_months.md).

## Direction

Toward a position that puts the coming fall into today's price.
Toward [the borrow](piece_the_borrow.md), without which nothing can be sold.

## Lever

The rule that a share must be located and borrowed before it is sold short, and the fact that a locked share can no more be lent than sold.

## Echo

A trade that is right, is made, rises into the date, and is capped and priced by the scarcity it exists to correct.
