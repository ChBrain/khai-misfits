# Piece: The Lockup

## Taxonomy

[The Quiet Six Months](place_the_quiet_six_months.md). The instrument that fixes the date and withholds the shares, in one clause.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Written in [writing the lockup](process_writing_the_lockup.md) before the listing, and carried in the prospectus.

## Load Bearing

It is the answer to a real problem and it is the cause of the trap, and there is no separating the two.
Buyers of an untested company need the insiders bound, and the only way to bind them is to make the shares unsaleable for a term: so the same clause that earns the offering its credibility publishes an exact future date and removes, for the whole interval, every share that could be used to trade against it.

## Apparent

A standard term. Six months, typically, printed with the rest of the offering.
Exactly what a careful underwriter is supposed to insist on.

## Yearbook

Does two jobs that cannot be given to two instruments.
Ends on a day chosen in advance and known to everyone, which is what makes the day both foreseeable and untradable.
