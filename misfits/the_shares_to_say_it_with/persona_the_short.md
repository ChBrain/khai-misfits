# Persona: The Short

## Taxonomy

[Short](position_short.md). The correcting trade, made and rationed.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A fall in plain view, months ahead, on a printed date.
An account, a view, and the intention to sell it forward.

## Action

Borrows what can be borrowed and sells it.
Increases into the date, then closes.

## Shadow

Is doing the right trade against a supply that does not exist yet.
A share must be borrowed before it can be sold short, and the lendable shares are the free float, because a locked share can no more be lent than sold: so the size of the correction is capped by the same quantity whose arrival is the event, and the trade is made, and made in the wrong amount, and costs what a scarce borrow costs.

## Tell

Is the market's answer to the problem, sized by the problem.
