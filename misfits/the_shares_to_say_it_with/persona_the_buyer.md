# Persona: The Buyer

## Taxonomy

[Buyer](position_buyer.md). The holder on the day, who read everything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A published date, understood.
A share bought at the price the market was quoting.

## Action

Reads the disclosure and buys anyway.
Holds through the day.

## Shadow

Has no error to be found in them and carries the loss.
They were told, they understood, and the alternatives were not owning the thing at all or paying a price nobody was offering: the quoted price was the price of a share in a market with few to sell, and there was no instrument for buying the company at its post-unlock float.

## Tell

Is fully informed, entirely reasonable, and on the wrong side of a printed date.
