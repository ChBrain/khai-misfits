# Persona: The Underwriter

## Taxonomy

[Underwriter](position_underwriter.md). The party who must make the offering saleable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A company with no trading history and insiders who could leave the morning after.
Strangers being asked to fund it.

## Action

Writes the promise into the deal, and prints it.
Sells the offering on the strength of it.

## Shadow

Is solving a real problem, and the solution is the trap.
The commitment is what makes the offering credible, and there is no version of it that binds the holder without also withholding the shares: a promise not to sell is a promise the shares cannot move, and shares that cannot move cannot be lent, quoted against or traded into a price.

## Tell

Fixes a public date by the only means that also empties the market for it.
