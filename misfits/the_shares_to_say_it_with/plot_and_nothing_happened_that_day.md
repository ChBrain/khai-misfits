## Action

## Stage

[The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) holds through it and carries the loss.

## Tension

A price moves on a day with nothing in it, and the whole market saw the day coming.
No accounts were published, no guidance revised, no fact about the business learned. What changed is the number of shares that could be sold, and that change was printed on page one months earlier. So the fall was foreseeable, disclosed and untradable at once, and the loss lands on the party who read the disclosure and had no instrument for acting on it, which is every party who owned the stock that morning.
