## Direction

Place the offering, and give the buyers a reason to believe the insiders will still be there.

## Orders

## Targets

- [ ] the commitment is real, and the offering is credible because of it
- [ ] a promise not to sell is a promise the shares cannot move
- [ ] so the shares under it cannot be lent, sold or quoted against for the whole term
- [ ] and the day the promise ends is fixed, public, and unpriceable until it arrives
