# Persona: The Locked Holder

## Taxonomy

[Locked Holder](position_locked_holder.md). The insider bound by the promise, and released by the calendar.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A whole working life standing in one company's paper.
A date, agreed to in advance, on which that stops being compulsory.

## Action

Waits exactly as long as agreed.
Sells on the first day selling is permitted.

## Shadow

Is behaving prudently and is the thing that moves the price.
Holding one undiversified position is the risk any adviser would tell them to reduce, the wait was honoured in full, and the first legal day is simply the first day: nothing here is opportunism, and there is no later date that is any less the day everybody was watching.

## Tell

Does the careful thing on the one morning it is visible to everyone.
