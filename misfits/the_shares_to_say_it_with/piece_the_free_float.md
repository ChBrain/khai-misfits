# Piece: The Free Float

## Taxonomy

[The Quiet Six Months](place_the_quiet_six_months.md). The shares that may actually be sold, which is what the price is made from.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Formed in [the quiet six months](place_the_quiet_six_months.md) by [pricing the free float](process_pricing_the_free_float.md), and multiplied on the day.

## Load Bearing

The price is a price for this quantity and not for the company, and nothing on the screen says so.
A quote is where the shares on offer meet the money on offer, and a share that may not be sold is in neither: so a small float and a large float produce different prices for the same business, and the difference is not an error in anybody's valuation but the arithmetic of what was available to trade.

## Apparent

The number of shares in issue, near enough. The figure a screen reports as the company.

## Yearbook

Is a fraction of the register during the term and several times itself after it.
Changes on a date rather than on any fact about the business.
