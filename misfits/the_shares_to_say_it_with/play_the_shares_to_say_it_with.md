# Play: The Shares to Say It With

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

The Shares to Say It With.
A company goes public, and the people who already own most of it promise in writing not to sell for a fixed period. The promise is printed, the date is printed, and everybody who buys can read both. On that date the promise ends, the shares arrive, and the price falls. No accounts were published that morning, no guidance changed, nothing was learned. The fall was legible to the whole market for six months and stayed out of the price until the day it happened.

## Arc

The bend is this: a price is made by the shares that can trade, and the instrument that publishes the date is the instrument that withholds them.
It runs on a promise somebody has to make.
[The Underwriter](persona_the_underwriter.md) cannot sell the offering without it. A company with no trading history is asking strangers to fund insiders who could leave the next morning, so [the lockup](piece_the_lockup.md) is the answer to a real problem and the offering is worse without it.
And the price during it is set by what can trade.
[The Free Float](piece_the_free_float.md) is a fraction of the shares that exist, and a fraction is what the market has to work with, so the quoted price is a price for the small quantity available and not for the whole company.
Then [The Date Is on Page One](plot_the_date_is_on_page_one.md).
[The Prospectus](persona_the_prospectus.md) discloses the promise, the count and the day, in plain terms, to everyone, months ahead. This is disclosure working exactly as intended, and it changes no price.
And [You Cannot Borrow What Is Locked](plot_you_cannot_borrow_what_is_locked.md).
[The Short](persona_the_short.md) is the party who would sell the coming fall into the present, and to sell a share you must first borrow one. The lendable shares are the free float and nothing else, because a locked share cannot be lent any more than it can be sold. So the trade that would price the event in is rationed by the same quantity the event releases.
And [the first legal day](place_the_unlock_day.md) is the first day. [The Locked Holder](persona_the_locked_holder.md) has one company's paper standing where a life's savings would be, has waited exactly as long as they agreed to wait, and sells: which is prudence, not opportunism, and it is what moves the price.
And [The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md) carries it. They read the disclosure, understood the date, and had no instrument for acting on it: buying later means not owning it now, and the price they paid was the price of a share in a market that had few to sell.

## Stakes

Whether a fall the whole market can read can reach the price before the day it arrives.
Every plot moves the same way: toward a promise that had to be made, a price made by the shares that can trade, a disclosure that is complete and inert, a correcting trade rationed by the object it would correct, and a holder who waited exactly as agreed, and away from any account in which somebody concealed something or dumped on somebody. The production cannot fault a prospectus that said everything, and it cannot fault a buyer who read it, because reading it and acting on it are different problems and only the first was available. What is left to fight over is not whether the drop was foreseeable, the date was printed, but what to call a foreseeable loss that no party in the market had the instrument to price.
