# Plot: Somebody Has to Promise Not to Sell

## Taxonomy

[The Shares to Say It With](play_the_shares_to_say_it_with.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A company with no trading history, asking strangers to fund insiders who could leave the next morning.

## Action

[Writing the lockup](process_writing_the_lockup.md): [the underwriter](persona_the_underwriter.md), holding [Underwriter](position_underwriter.md), binds the holders before the listing.

## Stage

[The Lockup](piece_the_lockup.md) is written into the deal and printed with it.

## Tension

The commitment is the answer to a real problem rather than a restraint imposed for advantage.
Without it the offering is asking buyers to fund people free to leave with the proceeds, and no wording binds a holder without making the shares unsaleable, because a promise that leaves them saleable is not a promise. So the clause that earns the offering its credibility is the same clause that fixes a public date and removes every share that could be traded against it.
