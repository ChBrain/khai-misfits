# Position: Underwriter

## Taxonomy

[The Shares to Say It With](play_the_shares_to_say_it_with.md). The seat that must make an untested company saleable.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An offering to place, and buyers who need a reason to believe the insiders will stay.

## Orders

Bind the holders. Print the terms.

## Loses

Nothing, and that is the difficulty: the seat's solution is correct and complete.
There is no form of the commitment that binds a holder without freezing the shares, so the seat cannot both solve the problem it exists to solve and leave a market able to price the day the solution ends.

## Drives

Toward an offering that stands up, and a date that cannot be traded.
