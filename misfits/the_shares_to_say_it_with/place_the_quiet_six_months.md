# Place: The Quiet Six Months

## Taxonomy

[The Shares to Say It With](play_the_shares_to_say_it_with.md). Where a price is made for a company most of whose shares cannot be sold.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A newly listed company trading normally: a quote, a spread, volume, coverage, an orderly market.
Everything working: full disclosure filed, the terms public, the date known to anyone who looks.

## Holds

[The Free Float](piece_the_free_float.md), a fraction of the shares that exist and the whole of what the market has.
[The Buyer](persona_the_buyer.md), informed and buying at the price on the screen.
[The Short](persona_the_short.md), who can see the date and can only borrow what is loose.

## Offers

A real price, honestly made, for the quantity actually available.
A market that does everything a market is supposed to do with the shares in front of it.

## Withheld

Any way to trade the company at the float it will have.
The place prices the shares that can move, and the shares that cannot are not absent from the register, only from the market: so there is no instrument here for buying or selling the company as it will stand after the date, because that company's shares are not yet permitted to exist as tradable objects.
