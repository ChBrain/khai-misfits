## Load Bearing

It is what makes the correcting trade impossible to size, and it is scarce for the same reason the fall is coming.
Nothing can be sold short that has not first been borrowed, and a locked share cannot be lent any more than it can be sold, so the pool available to the party who would price the event in consists precisely of the shares the event is not about: the correction is therefore capped by the small float, and costs what borrowing a scarce share costs, and both of those tighten as more people see the date.
