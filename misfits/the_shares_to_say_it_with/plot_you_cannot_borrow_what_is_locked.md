# Plot: You Cannot Borrow What Is Locked

## Taxonomy

[The Shares to Say It With](play_the_shares_to_say_it_with.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A trader who can see the fall coming and wants to sell it forward.

## Action

[Borrowing to sell short](process_borrowing_to_sell_short.md): [the short](persona_the_short.md), holding [Short](position_short.md), goes looking for [the borrow](piece_the_borrow.md).

## Stage

The trade is made, and rises into the date, and cannot be made in the size the event warrants.

## Tension

The correcting trade is rationed by the same quantity whose release is the event.
A share must be borrowed before it can be sold short, and a locked share can no more be lent than sold, so the lendable pool is exactly the free float: small, and small for precisely the reason the fall is coming. The trade is therefore capped where the case for it is strongest and costs what a scarce borrow costs, and the market's own instrument for pricing a known future supply is denied the supply until it has arrived.
