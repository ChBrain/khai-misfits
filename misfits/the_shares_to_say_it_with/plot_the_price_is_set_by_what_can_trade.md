# Plot: The Price Is Set by What Can Trade

## Taxonomy

[The Shares to Say It With](play_the_shares_to_say_it_with.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A quoted price on a company whose shares are mostly promised away.

## Action

[Pricing the free float](process_pricing_the_free_float.md): the market clears [the free float](piece_the_free_float.md) in [the quiet six months](place_the_quiet_six_months.md).

## Stage

The quote is a real price, honestly made, for the quantity actually available.

## Tension

The price is made from what may be sold, and nothing on the screen distinguishes that from the company.
A quote is where the shares on offer meet the money on offer, and a share under the lockup is in neither, so the same business produces a different price at a small float than at a large one. That is not a misvaluation anybody committed: it is the arithmetic of a market working correctly on the quantity it was given, and the quantity was set by a clause everybody had read.
