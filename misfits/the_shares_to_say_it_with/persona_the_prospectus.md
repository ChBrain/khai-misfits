# Persona: The Prospectus

## Taxonomy

[Prospectus](position_prospectus.md). The disclosure, complete and early and universal.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A filing, read by anybody who wants it, months before the day.
The promise, the count of shares under it, and the date.

## Action

States all of it, plainly, in advance.
Withholds nothing.

## Shadow

Does everything asked of disclosure and reaches no price.
There is no wording that would work better, no earlier date on which to say it, and no reader who was missed: the information is already perfect and public, so the gap is not in what anybody knows but in what anybody can do with it.

## Tell

Is the case where full disclosure is achieved and is not enough.
