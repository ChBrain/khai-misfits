# Persona: The Naval Architect

## Taxonomy

[Naval Architect](position_naval_architect.md). The one who put the tanks where they are.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A designer working to a classification society's rules, a cargo capacity, a beam restriction and a set of loading conditions.

## Action

Sizes and places the ballast tanks so the hull is sound loaded and sound empty.
Makes the ballast condition a designed condition rather than an improvisation.

## Shadow

Designs the transfer into the ship and could not have designed it out.
The seat is not overlooking anything: a hull that needs no ballast has been drawn, and it buys that freedom with extra beam, lost cargo capacity, added drag and internal framing a classification society may decline to approve. The ballast tank is not a shortcut around a better answer. It is the answer that fits the beam the ports allow and the cargo the trade requires, and every alternative moves the cost somewhere else rather than removing it.

## Tell

Draws a correct ship whose correctness is the mechanism.
