# Plot: So It Is Released Where the Cargo Is

## Taxonomy

[Heavy Enough to Cross](play_heavy_enough_to_cross.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A berth, a cargo waiting, and tanks that must be emptied to take it.

## Action

The pumps run out through [releasing it to load](process_releasing_it_to_load.md), and the water leaves where the freight comes in.

## Stage

A discharge that lands, without anybody aiming it, on the most receptive water available.

## Tension

The destination is not random and could not be made random.
Ballast must come out where cargo goes in, and cargo goes in at working coasts: sheltered, shallow, productive, near estuaries, which is also the description of habitat. So the transfer's endpoint is chosen by the shape of trade and lands on exactly the water most able to receive a coastal assemblage and most valuable to the people who live from it. Moving the discharge elsewhere means moving the port, which is the one thing a voyage cannot do.
