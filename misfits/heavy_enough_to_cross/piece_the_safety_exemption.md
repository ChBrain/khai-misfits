## Load Bearing

The instrument drafted to stop the transfer provides that the transfer need not be stopped where stopping it would endanger the ship.
A ship is not required to conduct an exchange where the master reasonably decides that doing so would threaten the safety or stability of the ship, its crew or its passengers, on account of adverse weather, ship design or stress, equipment failure or other extraordinary condition. That provision is not a loophole and removing it would be indefensible: emptying and refilling tanks in open water reduces stability, raises structural stress, produces sloshing loads, and can cut the forward draught enough to invite bow slamming. So the exemption is the same seamanship that made the ballast necessary, appearing a second time, and the drafters wrote it in because the alternative was worse.
