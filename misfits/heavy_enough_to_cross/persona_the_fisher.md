# Persona: The Fisher

## Taxonomy

[Fisher](position_fisher.md). The one on the receiving coast, party to nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A working fishery on a coast with a port, a boat, a licence and a ground that has always been fished.

## Action

Fishes the ground.
Goes back to it season after season, on the assumption that it is the same ground.

## Shadow

Loses the fishery without any transaction having touched them.
The seat is not a party to the voyage, the fixture, the classification rules or the convention. It has no standing in any of them and no information about what was in a tank. The change arrives through a food web rather than through a market: the zooplankton the larvae need is eaten by something that was not there before, and the catch falls for reasons that are not visible from a boat. There is nothing this seat could have done differently, because there was nothing for it to do.

## Tell

Pays the whole cost and appears in none of the documents.
