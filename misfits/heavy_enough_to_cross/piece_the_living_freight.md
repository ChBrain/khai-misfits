## Place

## Load Bearing

It is not loaded, not declared and not chosen: it is simply what lives in a port, taken up with the port's water.
The sampling work found hundreds of taxa in a single ocean crossing's ballast, spanning every major marine habitat and trophic group, and essentially anything with a planktonic phase in its life cycle. The mechanism has no selectivity and needs none. It moves whole coastal assemblages across a barrier that separated them for as long as the oceans have been where they are, and it delivers them to the kind of water they came from, because ports resemble ports.
