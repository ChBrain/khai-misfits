# Place: The Loading Port

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Shown

Everything proper: the ship is certified, the discharge is logged, the standard is met.

## Withheld

Anywhere else for the discharge to go.
The place is not incidental to the mechanism, it is entailed by it. Ballast must come out where cargo goes in, and cargo goes in at working coasts: sheltered, productive, near estuaries and shallows, which is the same description as habitat. So the discharge is aimed, without anybody aiming it, at exactly the water most able to receive what is in it and most valuable to the people who live from it.
