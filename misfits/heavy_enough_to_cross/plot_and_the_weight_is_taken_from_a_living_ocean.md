# Plot: And the Weight Is Taken From a Living Ocean

## Taxonomy

[Heavy Enough to Cross](play_heavy_enough_to_cross.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

An open sea chest, and a tank to fill.

## Action

[The Living Freight](piece_the_living_freight.md) comes aboard with the water: larvae, cysts, plankton, everything with a drifting phase, and the bacteria besides.

## Stage

A cargo that is loaded by nobody and appears on no manifest.

## Tension

The contents arrive by the same act as the mass and cannot be declined.
The sampling work recovered hundreds of taxa from one crossing's ballast, spanning every major marine habitat and trophic group. That is not a failure of housekeeping and no port is cleaner than this: it is what a productive coastal water contains. The ship is not selecting anything and has no way to take the weight without the population, because they are the same volume of the same substance.
