# Pitch: The Emptier the Ship, the More It Carries

## Taxonomy

## Tenor

A bulk carrier riding high off a berth, tanks filling, a sea chest open below the waterline.
Nothing on the manifest, because none of what comes aboard is cargo.

## Undertow

Under a hull made safe for the crossing, a volume of another coast's water going with it.

## Nerve

The particular quiet of a voyage in which nothing at all went wrong.

## Echo

Afterward: a passage logged as uneventful, and a bay that does not fish the way it did.
