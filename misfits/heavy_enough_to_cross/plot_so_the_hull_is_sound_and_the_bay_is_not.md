# Plot: So the Hull Is Sound and the Bay Is Not

## Taxonomy

[Heavy Enough to Cross](play_heavy_enough_to_cross.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A voyage completed with nothing gone wrong aboard.

## Action

The passage is logged as uneventful, the certificates are in order, and [the fisher](persona_the_fisher.md) works a ground that is changing.

## Stage

A faultless voyage and a coast that is not what it was.

## Tension

Every seat performed correctly and the outcome is the sum of the correct performances.
The master ballasted for safety and may lawfully decline an exchange that would endanger the ship. The architect drew a hull that is sound loaded and empty, and the ballast-free alternative buys its freedom with beam, cargo capacity and drag. The charterer moved freight in the direction trade moves it. The inspector verified the standard the convention sets. The fisher was party to nothing. What is left is not a fault to allocate but a coast changed by ships that did everything right, and a remedy that is real, partial, and exempted whenever performing it would put a ship at risk.
