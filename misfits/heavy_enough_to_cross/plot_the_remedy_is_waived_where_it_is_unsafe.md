# Plot: The Remedy Is Waived Where It Is Unsafe

## Taxonomy

[Heavy Enough to Cross](play_heavy_enough_to_cross.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A mid-ocean exchange due, and a sea running.

## Action

[The Safety Exemption](piece_the_safety_exemption.md) applies: the ship is not required to exchange where the master reasonably decides it would threaten the ship, the crew or the passengers.

## Stage

An instrument written to stop the transfer, releasing the ship from stopping it.

## Tension

The exemption is correct and its correctness is what closes the case.
Exchanging ballast in open water empties and refills tanks in sequence, which lowers stability, raises structural stress, produces sloshing loads and can reduce the forward draught enough to invite slamming. A rule with no exemption would order masters to endanger ships, and no drafter could write that. So the same seamanship that made the ballast necessary reappears as the ground for waiving the remedy, and the convention says so in its own text: the ship's safety is why the water moves, stated by the instrument that exists to stop it moving.
