# Place: The Ballast Leg

## Taxonomy

[Heavy Enough to Cross](play_heavy_enough_to_cross.md). The crossing made with nothing aboard but water.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

An ocean passage with empty holds and full tanks, days of it, the ship at a designed draught it reaches by carrying the sea.
Everything proper: the loading condition is computed, the record book is written up, the passage is uneventful.

## Holds

[The Master](persona_the_master.md), who ballasted correctly.
[The Ballast](piece_the_ballast.md), which is doing exactly what it is for.
[The Living Freight](piece_the_living_freight.md), which came aboard with it and is not on any manifest.

## Offers

A hull that steers, a propeller under the surface, and a structure inside its design loads.

## Withheld

Any separation between the weight and its contents.
The place is where the two things the play holds together are literally the same volume. There is no arrangement in which the ship takes the mass and leaves the organisms, because the mass is the water and the organisms are in it. Filtration and disinfection act on the volume after it is aboard and reduce what is in it; they do not turn the ballast into something other than a piece of one coast's sea, carried across an ocean because the ship would not be safe without it.
