# Process: Releasing It to Load

## Taxonomy

[Heavy Enough to Cross](play_heavy_enough_to_cross.md). Emptying the tanks, because the cargo is waiting.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A berth, and freight to lift.
[The Master](persona_the_master.md), at [the loading port](place_the_loading_port.md).

## Direction

Toward empty tanks and a ship able to take cargo.

## Lever

The ballast pumps, run in reverse.

## Echo

A discharge into a sheltered, productive, worked piece of coast, which is where cargo is loaded and where habitat is.
