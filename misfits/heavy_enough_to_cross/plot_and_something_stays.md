# Plot: And Something Stays

## Taxonomy

[Heavy Enough to Cross](play_heavy_enough_to_cross.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A bay that receives the same discharge, voyage after voyage, for years.

## Action

[The Living Freight](piece_the_living_freight.md) meets the receiving water: almost everything released dies, and occasionally something does not, and finds food, and reproduces.

## Stage

A process that fails overwhelmingly and only has to succeed once.

## Tension

The arithmetic of the failure rate is what makes the outcome certain rather than what makes it unlikely.
Establishment is stochastic and rare per organism, and the number of organisms is enormous and the number of arrivals is continuous. When it succeeds the cost does not fall on the ship, the charterer or the port: a comb jelly established in the Black Sea in the early 1980s, ate the zooplankton the anchovy larvae depended on, and the anchovy fishery collapsed between 1989 and 1991. Nobody in the sequence intended, foresaw or profited from that, and [the fisher](persona_the_fisher.md) was not a party to any of it.
