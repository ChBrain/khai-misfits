# Persona: The Master

## Taxonomy

[Master](position_master.md). The one who decides how much water comes aboard.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A shipmaster with empty holds, an ocean ahead, a loading condition to satisfy and a convention to comply with.

## Action

Ballasts the ship until it is safe to cross.
Manages the water as the convention requires, and declines the exchange where the sea makes it dangerous.

## Shadow

Does the seamanlike thing and moves a coast in doing it.
The seat is not cutting a corner: an unballasted hull on an ocean passage lifts its propeller clear, loses steerage, slams at the bow and carries a bending moment it was not built for. The quantity of water is set by how empty the ship is, which is set by where the cargo is. Declining a mid-ocean exchange in a heavy sea is not an evasion either: the convention itself provides for it, and the alternative is a stability and stress problem in open water. There is no version of this seat that could have crossed lighter without becoming a different ship.

## Tell

Takes on exactly the weight that is needed, which is the whole of the problem.
