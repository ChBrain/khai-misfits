# Plot: An Empty Ship Must Be Made Heavy, and That Is Right

## Taxonomy

[Heavy Enough to Cross](play_heavy_enough_to_cross.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A hull that has discharged its cargo, and an ocean to cross.

## Action

[The Ballast](piece_the_ballast.md) is taken aboard: seawater pumped into the tanks until the ship sits at a draught it can cross in.

## Stage

A requirement that is a condition of seaworthiness, not a preference.

## Tension

There is nothing to argue with in the requirement, which is what makes the rest inescapable.
An unballasted hull on an ocean passage lifts its propeller toward the surface, loses rudder authority, slams at the bow and carries the hull girder in a condition it was not designed for. No standard of seamanship removes this and no owner's diligence substitutes for it. The mass has to come from somewhere, and the only material available in that quantity, at that place, at no cost, is the sea the ship is floating in.
