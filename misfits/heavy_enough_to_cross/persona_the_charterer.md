# Persona: The Charterer

## Taxonomy

[Charterer](position_charterer.md). The one whose trade decides which leg is empty.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A party with cargo at one end of an ocean and none at the other, fixing a ship to move it.

## Action

Fixes the vessel for the loaded leg.
Accepts the ballast leg as the cost of getting the ship to where the cargo is.

## Shadow

Sets the volume of the transfer without touching the water.
The seat is not doing anything questionable: cargo moves in the direction the world's trade moves it, and the return leg is empty because there is nothing to carry back. Nobody chose the imbalance and no party to the fixture can correct it. The seat's decision is about freight and dates, and the quantity of foreign water crossing an ocean is downstream of it in a way nothing in the contract records.

## Tell

Books a voyage, and the ballast follows from the direction of trade.
