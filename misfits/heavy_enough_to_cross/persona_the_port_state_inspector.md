# Persona: The Port State Inspector

## Taxonomy

[Port State Inspector](position_port_state_inspector.md). The one who has to verify it at the berth.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

An official with a discharge standard expressed as organisms per volume, a record book to read, and the time a ship is alongside.

## Action

Checks the management plan and the record book, and samples what can be sampled.
Holds the ship to the standard the convention sets.

## Shadow

Verifies a number that does not translate cleanly into the thing that matters.
The seat is not being lax: the standard is a concentration, the question is whether anything establishes, and the relation between the two is species-specific, stochastic and contested in the literature. A sample drawn at a berth is small against a tank, and a count of organisms per volume says nothing about how many distinct species were delivered. The seat enforces the written standard exactly, which is the only thing a seat at a berth can enforce.

## Tell

Confirms compliance with a figure that is not the question.
