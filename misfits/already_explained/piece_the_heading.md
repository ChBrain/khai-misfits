# Piece: The Heading

## Taxonomy

[The Notes](place_the_notes.md). The diagnosis at the top of the record, true and read first.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At the top of [the notes](place_the_notes.md), on the screen in [the small room](place_the_small_room.md), read before the first word of the consultation.

## Load Bearing

Without it there is no prior and no misfit, and with it hidden the misfit would be a records failure.
It is the piece that shows the problem is not concealment but position: the label is accurate, useful, and correctly placed at the top, and being at the top is what makes everything under it a variation rather than a new thing.

## Apparent

A line of text. A condition, a date, a clinician's name.
Uncontroversial, never disputed, and exactly what any good record should carry at the top.

## Yearbook

Written years ago by someone careful, on good evidence.
Correct then, correct now, and never once the thing that needed changing, which is why nobody has ever had reason to look at it again.
