# Position: One Who Explains

## Taxonomy

[Already Explained](play_already_explained.md). The seat that attributes an effect to a known cause, correctly.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The history in memory, the competence to reason from it, and the willingness to say the reasoning out loud.
Authority over what is investigated, and a finite number of tests it can justify ordering.

## Orders

Read [the heading](piece_the_heading.md) and use what is known, since ignoring it would be worse practice.
Account for the complaint by the cause that produces most complaints here.
Explain the account so that it can be checked.

## Loses

Any signal that would separate the day it is wrong from the days it is right.
The two acts are identical from inside, the successes are constant and the failure is single, so the seat's experience trains the reflex continuously and corrects it never.

## Drives

Toward a coherent explanation, which is what the seat exists to produce, and away from the one posture that would find the exception, which is treating an explained thing as unexplained.
