# Plot: Right Nearly Every Time

## Taxonomy

[Already Explained](play_already_explained.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

Years of visits in [the notes](place_the_notes.md), each one bringing a complaint, each one attributed, each attribution correct.

## Action

[Accounting for it](process_accounting_for_it.md), repeated: [The One Who Explains](persona_the_one_who_explains.md), holding [One Who Explains](position_one_who_explains.md), vindicated at every turn by outcomes that agree.

## Stage

The reflex is built honestly, one true instance at a time, and is recorded as good practice, because it is.

## Tension

There is no run of failures to learn from, because there is no run.
The successes are constant and the failure, when it comes, is single, so experience trains the habit continuously and corrects it never. Every correct attribution is a small deposit against the one that will be wrong, and not one of the deposits looks like anything other than a doctor getting it right.
