# Persona: The Label

## Taxonomy

[Label](position_label.md). A true diagnosis that explains almost everything, and by explaining, closes.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Correct. Arrived at properly, years ago, by someone careful, and never since disputed.
Names something real that genuinely produces a wide range of symptoms, which is why it is useful and why it is dangerous.

## Action

Sits at the top of [the notes](place_the_notes.md) and colours everything beneath.
Absorbs each new complaint that it could plausibly produce, which is most of them, and does so correctly.
Lowers the weight of anything the person says, without disbelieving any of it.

## Shadow

Cannot decline a symptom that fits, and its fitting is not evidence.
Its explanatory reach is exactly what makes it worth having and exactly what makes it unfalsifiable in the room: a condition that accounts for many things will account for the one thing it should not, in the same tone and by the same reasoning, and nothing in the encounter can distinguish the two.

## Tell

Explains everything offered to it, which is read as the sign of a good diagnosis and is the property that makes this one unable to be surprised.
