## Tension

The correction is worse medicine on average, in exchange for better medicine in the rare case.
The prior is genuinely informative, so setting it aside means testing for what has already been explained: more scans, more incidental findings, more procedures on people for whom procedures are harder, most of it confirming what the reasoning already said. And the cases it saves can never be produced as evidence, because a thing found early leaves no record of the disaster it was not, so the clinician who does this pays visibly and is credited invisibly.
