# Plan: Account for It

## Taxonomy

A **personal scheme**: the intent of [The One Who Explains](persona_the_one_who_explains.md), inside [Already Explained](play_already_explained.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [The One Who Explains](persona_the_one_who_explains.md)

## Direction

Give the person a real answer. Use what is known about them rather than pretending not to know it, reason from the established cause to the present effect, say the reasoning aloud so it can be challenged, and do not order a test for something already explained.

## Orders

(The One Who Explains reads [the heading](piece_the_heading.md), hears the complaint in full, attributes it to the known condition, explains the attribution out loud, and closes with [the discharge letter](piece_the_discharge_letter.md).)
