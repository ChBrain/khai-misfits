# Piece: The Blank Form

## Taxonomy

[The Small Room](place_the_small_room.md). The request that would have found it, never filled in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

In the drawer and in the system, in [the small room](place_the_small_room.md), one click from being sent.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the miss looks like scarcity, and it is not.
It is the piece that locates the failure precisely: the test is available, cheap, routine, and would settle the question, and it is not refused, not rationed and not delayed. It is simply never requested, because a finding that is already accounted for does not generate a request.

## Apparent

An ordinary form. A box for the indication, which is the only box that cannot be filled, since the indication would have to be that something is unexplained.

## Yearbook

Filled in thousands of times a year for people whose complaint has no ready explanation.
Never once for this person, across years of the same complaint, and no entry anywhere records the not-requesting, because nothing happened.
