# Plot: It Fits What We Know

## Taxonomy

[Already Explained](play_already_explained.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

A new complaint, described carefully, of a kind the existing condition could plausibly produce.

## Action

[Accounting for it](process_accounting_for_it.md): [The One Who Explains](persona_the_one_who_explains.md), holding [One Who Explains](position_one_who_explains.md), attributes the complaint to the known cause and says the reasoning out loud, while [The Label](persona_the_label.md), holding [Label](position_label.md), takes it in.

## Stage

[The One Who Explains](persona_the_one_who_explains.md), having tabled [Account for It](plan_account_for_it.md), gives a coherent answer rather than a shrug, and invites the question back.

## Tension

This is diagnosis, not dismissal, and the difference matters.
Attributing an effect to an established cause is the work, done correctly and in the open, by someone who would be the doctor anyone would want. And [the label](persona_the_label.md) cannot decline a symptom that fits, so the fitting is not evidence of belonging: the same act, performed identically and to the same standard, is right today and will be wrong on a day nobody will be able to pick out in advance.
