## Shown

Visit after visit in which a complaint was brought, attributed, and resolved, with the attribution correct nearly every time and recorded as such.

## Holds

## Offers

A complete and accurate history to whoever opens it, which is what a record is for and what makes care continuous.

## Withheld

Any way of reading an entry except downward from the heading.
The record is honest and well kept, and its structure is the argument: the label is the first thing on the page, so it is the first thing in the room, and nothing in the file has a position from which to contradict it.
