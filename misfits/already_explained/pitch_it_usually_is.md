# Pitch: It Usually Is

## Taxonomy

[Already Explained](play_already_explained.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Attentive and reasonable. An unhurried consultation with someone who knows the history without opening the file, thinks aloud, and invites the question back.
Nothing brusque in it. The person is listened to, examined, and given a coherent account of what is happening to them, by someone competent enough to give one.

## Undertow

Under the competence, an instrument doing the only thing it can do.
The label explains, and explaining is not noticing, so the better it performs the more firmly it holds the door it is standing in. Every correct attribution is a small deposit against the one that will be wrong, and none of the deposits look like anything but good practice.

## Nerve

The quiet disquiet of hearing a true and complete explanation and knowing, without being able to say why, that it is answering a different question.

## Echo

Afterward: a letter closing the episode, a heading unchanged at the top of the notes, an illness found later by somebody else, and a chain of reasoning that would survive any review it were put through.
