# Persona: The One Who Explains

## Taxonomy

[One Who Explains](position_one_who_explains.md). Reasons correctly from a known cause to a present effect, and is right nearly every time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Competent and unhurried. Knows the history without the file, thinks aloud, invites questions, and gives a coherent account rather than a shrug.
Would be the doctor anyone would want, and is regarded that way by colleagues and by the person in front of them.

## Action

Reads [the heading](piece_the_heading.md), hears the complaint, and places it where it belongs: attributed to the condition that produces most of what this person brings.
Explains the attribution out loud, so that it can be checked.
Does not order the test, because the finding is already accounted for.

## Shadow

Cannot tell the day it fails from the days it does not, because from inside they are the same act.
The reflex was earned honestly, one correct instance at a time, and there is no run of errors to learn from: the successes are constant and the failure is single, so experience trains the habit and never corrects it.

## Tell

Says "that will be the [condition] again", accurately, in a tone of reassurance, having just performed the reasoning that is the reason nobody will look.
