# Process: Accounting For It

## Taxonomy

[Already Explained](play_already_explained.md). Attributing a present effect to a known cause, which is diagnosis.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A complaint that the existing condition could plausibly produce.
[The One Who Explains](persona_the_one_who_explains.md), holding [One Who Explains](position_one_who_explains.md), in [the small room](place_the_small_room.md).

## Direction

Toward a coherent explanation of what the person is experiencing, offered out loud and open to challenge.
Aimed at the complaint and answering it, correctly, from what is already known.

## Lever

A real diagnosis with wide explanatory reach, and the professional obligation to use what is known about a patient rather than ignore it.

## Echo

An answer given, a person reassured, and a chain of reasoning that would survive any review it were put through.
