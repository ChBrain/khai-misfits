# Position: One Already Named

## Taxonomy

[Already Explained](play_already_explained.md). The seat whose every account arrives attached to a true label.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A real diagnosis, correctly made, and years of practice describing what happens to them.
The right to be heard, which is honoured: they are listened to, examined, and answered.

## Orders

Come and say it.
Say it again, and differently, when it does not land.
Answer whatever is asked.

## Loses

The power of their own testimony, in proportion to how firmly it is offered.
Insistence is a symptom the label can absorb, so pressing harder makes the report fit better, and the one instrument that could contradict [the label](persona_the_label.md) is the instrument the label attenuates.

## Drives

Toward being believed, which they already are, and away from being investigated, which is the different thing they actually need, so the courtesy they receive and the omission that harms them arrive in the same encounter.
