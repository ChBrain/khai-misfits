# Persona: The One Already Named

## Taxonomy

[One Already Named](position_one_already_named.md). Carries a real diagnosis, and every account they give arrives attached to it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Practised. Has said this kind of thing many times, in many rooms, and knows the vocabulary.
Not vague and not exaggerating: describes what is happening as precisely as anyone could, and can tell that this one is not like the others, though not why.

## Action

Comes to [the small room](place_the_small_room.md) and says it.
Says it again at the next visit, and differently, reaching for a word that will land.
Answers every question asked, none of which is the question that would find it.

## Shadow

Cannot get out from under the heading, and the harder they press the more the pressing fits.
Insistence is itself a symptom the label can absorb, so the one instrument that could contradict the account, their own report, loses weight in proportion to how firmly it is offered. There is no way to say it that the explanation cannot include.

## Tell

Says "this is different", which is the truest sentence available and carries no diagnostic weight at all, since it is exactly what the label would predict them to say.
