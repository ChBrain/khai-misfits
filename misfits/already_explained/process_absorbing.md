# Process: Absorbing

## Taxonomy

[Already Explained](play_already_explained.md). The label taking in a symptom that belongs to something else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A new complaint that falls inside the label's range, which most complaints do.
[The Label](persona_the_label.md), holding [Label](position_label.md), at the top of [the notes](place_the_notes.md).

## Direction

Inward, without discrimination between what belongs to it and what does not.
The label cannot decline a symptom that fits, so fitting is not evidence of belonging and there is no reading that would separate the two.

## Lever

Explanatory reach: the same property that makes the diagnosis worth having.

## Echo

A complaint accounted for, correctly in almost every instance, and identically in the instance where it is wrong.
