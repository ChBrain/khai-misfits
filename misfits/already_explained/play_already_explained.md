# Play: Already Explained

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

Already Explained.
A person arrives with a complaint, and at the top of their notes is a condition they have carried for years. The condition is real, it was correctly diagnosed, and it produces a great many symptoms, including ones very like the complaint being made today. So the complaint is placed where it belongs: accounted for by the thing that accounts for almost everything this person brings. The reasoning is sound, it is spoken aloud, and it can be examined by anyone. It is also right, in this person's case, on nearly every previous occasion it has been applied. And underneath it, this time, is something else entirely: common, unrelated, treatable, and never once looked for.

## Arc

The bend is this: a strong prior is an instrument for explaining, and an instrument for explaining is not an instrument for noticing.
It runs on being right nearly every time.
The Label is not a mistake. It was arrived at properly, it names something real, and it genuinely produces most of what The One Already Named brings through the door. So when a new complaint arrives that it could account for, accounting for it is not laziness: it is diagnosis, done the way diagnosis is done, by attributing an effect to a known cause.
And the attribution is correct, again and again, which is the whole difficulty. Every time it is right, it earns the reflex a little more. There is no run of failures to learn from, because there is no run: the successes are constant and the failure is single, and the single failure looks exactly like the successes from inside.
Reiss and his colleagues showed the shape by changing nothing but the label. The same described case, the same phobia, was less likely to be called an emotional disturbance when the person was also described as having an intellectual disability. Nothing about the symptom moved. What moved was what it could be read against.
The One Already Named goes on saying it, and the saying is weighed light. Not disbelieved: weighed light, because a report that arrives attached to a condition known to produce reports is heard as more of the condition, so the person's own account, which is the only instrument that could contradict The Label, is the instrument The Label attenuates.
Then The Other Illness runs its course unattended, and it is ordinary. It is the kind of thing that is straightforward when found and serious when not, and what makes it serious here is nothing about the illness at all.
And the correction costs. Investigate every new complaint as though the label were absent, and you are testing for what has already been explained: more scans, more incidentals, more procedures on people for whom procedures are harder, most of it confirming what the prior already said. The prior is genuinely informative. Setting it aside is worse medicine on average, in exchange for better medicine in the rare case, and nobody can point at the rare cases afterwards and show they were saved, because a thing found early leaves no record of the disaster it was not.

**[A Name Already There](plot_a_name_already_there.md)**
A condition correctly diagnosed years ago, real, and productive of many symptoms, standing at the top of the notes before today's complaint is spoken. Closes when the reading of anything said in this room has a place to start from, and starting from it is correct.

**[It Fits What We Know](plot_it_fits_what_we_know.md)**
Cue: a new complaint that the existing condition could plausibly produce. The One Who Explains reasons aloud, attributes it, and is doing diagnosis rather than dismissing anyone. Closes when the complaint has been accounted for by a cause that genuinely accounts for most things here.

**[Right Nearly Every Time](plot_right_nearly_every_time.md)**
Cue: the same attribution, across years of visits, being correct. The reflex is earned honestly, one true instance at a time, and there is no run of failures anywhere to learn from. Closes when the habit is strong, well founded, and indistinguishable from the reasoning that will miss.

**[Told and Not Weighed](plot_told_and_not_weighed.md)**
Cue: The One Already Named says it again, plainly, and says it differently this time. **[The One Nobody Looked For](plot_the_one_nobody_looked_for.md)**
Cue: an ordinary illness, common and treatable, running its course underneath. No test is refused, because no test is requested. Closes when it is found late, or found by accident, and what made it serious turns out to be nothing about the illness.

**[As If It Were New](plot_as_if_it_were_new.md)**
Cue: the structure understood, and the correction reached for: investigate each complaint as though the label were absent. Closes when the cost is accepted, in tests that confirm what was already known and procedures on people for whom procedures are harder, or the prior is trusted again because on average it deserves to be.

## Stakes

Whether an instrument that explains this person correctly almost every time can ever be the instrument that notices the time it should not.
Every plot moves the same way: toward an attribution that is sound, spoken aloud and repeatedly vindicated, and away from any moment at which being wrong would look different from being right. The production cannot make the label uninformative, because it is informative, and it cannot make the person's account carry more weight, because the weight it loses is lost to the same true fact that makes the label useful. What is left to fight over is not whether to use what is known about a patient, everyone agrees that ignoring it would be worse, but who pays for the tests that mostly confirm what was already known, and how anyone would ever show that paying was right.
