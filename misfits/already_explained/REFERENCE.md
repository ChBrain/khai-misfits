# Already Explained: Reference

The research warrant for Already Explained.
The term is the house's; the play runs on diagnostic overshadowing, named by Reiss, Levitan and Szyszko in
1982 and carried into physical medicine by Jones, Howard and Thornicroft in 2008, with the stigma reading
of the same evidence carried alongside it, all cited below.

## Line of Work

Staging diagnostic overshadowing as **a correct prior that closes the only door the exception could come
through**: a person carries a diagnosis that is real, and a new complaint arrives that the diagnosis could
account for, so it is accounted for, correctly and by a competent reading, and the separate ordinary
illness underneath it is never looked for. The play models the documented mechanics, the label that is true
and genuinely explanatory, the new complaint that fits inside it, the attribution that is right nearly
every time and is right for good reasons, the account whose weight is lowered by the label it comes with,
the second illness that is common and treatable and goes unfound, and the correction that works by
investigating as though the label were not there, as the load-bearing vectors of the structure, not as a
tale of an uncaring doctor.

The governing law is that a strong prior is an instrument for explaining, and an instrument for explaining
is not an instrument for noticing. Where a person has a condition that produces many symptoms, attributing
a new symptom to it is not a lapse in reasoning: it is the reasoning, and it returns the right answer in
the great majority of cases, which is exactly what makes the minority uncatchable. Every correct
attribution strengthens the habit that will make the incorrect one.

Reiss and colleagues found the shape experimentally, and the design is what makes it structural rather
than anecdotal: the same described case was judged differently depending only on whether the person was
also described as having an intellectual disability, with a debilitating phobia less likely to be called an
emotional disturbance when the disability was present. A second experiment reproduced it and extended it to
schizophrenia and personality disorder, and later work found the same pattern across disciplines. Nothing
in the vignette changed except the label.

Jones, Howard and Thornicroft carried it out of psychiatry and into the body: physical symptoms
misattributed to a mental illness, in a population that dies earlier and carries more medical comorbidity
than the general population. What is being missed is not exotic. It is ordinary disease, of the kind that
is treatable when found.

It is distinct from the house's **The Rare Disease Test**, where an accurate test meets a rare condition
and a positive reading is believed past what the base rate supports: there the error is arithmetic on a
result that was actually obtained. Here no test is run at all, and the base rate is being used correctly,
which is why nothing in the reasoning can be shown to be wrong.

It is distinct from the house's **The Proxy**, where an unobservable individual is priced by the group's
average and the discount makes the average come true: there the person cannot be seen and the mechanism is
allocation. Here the person is seen, examined and listened to, and the label does not price them, it
explains them.

It is distinct from the house's **The Empty Seat**, where an aid that is right so often that deferring
becomes rational lulls the watch until the rare wrong output is followed: there the trusted thing is an
external aid that could in principle be checked against. Here the trusted thing is the clinician's own
correct inference, so there is no second opinion built into the loop to disagree with.

It is distinct from the house's **No One's Remit**, where each remit answers narrowly and honestly and the
person falls through all of them at once: there nobody's brief covers the person. Here one brief covers
them completely, and covering them completely is the problem.

It is distinct from the house's **Second Nature**, where a knower cannot picture not knowing and explains
over a learner's head: there the failure is in transmission. Here the transmission is fine and the
reception is what is discounted.

## Restrictions

What the house refuses to model, and how it credits what it stages.

- **No villains**: the elements are reduced to load-bearing vectors. The play refuses to cast the clinician
  as dismissive or the labelled person as unreliable, because the source structure is precisely that the
  reasoning is sound: a diagnosis that produces many symptoms really does account for most new complaints
  from the person who carries it, and attributing a complaint to a known cause is what diagnosis is. The
  attribution is right nearly every time, and being right nearly every time is not a defect to be trained
  away, it is the value of the prior. The failure is authored by nobody and emerges from the structure, an
  instrument built to explain being asked to notice, so the misfit is that every correct attribution
  reinforces the reflex that will make the one incorrect one, and the one incorrect one is the whole cost.
- **No hero**: the play refuses to resolve the misfit into a costless correction. The correction is to
  investigate each new complaint as though the label were absent, which means testing for what has already
  been explained: more investigations, more incidental findings, more procedures on people for whom
  procedures are harder, and a great deal of money spent confirming what the prior already said. And the
  prior is genuinely informative, so a clinician who sets it aside is practising worse medicine on average
  in order to practise better medicine in the rare case, which is a trade nobody can make visibly, because
  the cases it saves are never identified as having been saved.
- **Credit, not claim**: the research above is other people's work, packaged and attributed here, never
  claimed as the house's. The staging is the house's; the naming, the experiments, the extension to
  physical health and the outcome record are theirs. **The contested edge is carried rather than smoothed**:
  a large part of this literature reads the effect as stigma, as attitudes toward a labelled group rather
  than as an artefact of correct inference, and that reading is not displaced by this one. The play stages
  the structural component, which is the part that survives when nobody holds a poor opinion of anybody,
  and takes no position on how the total divides. A staging that reads as a denial that stigma operates
  here has left the mechanism and is wrong on the house's own terms.
