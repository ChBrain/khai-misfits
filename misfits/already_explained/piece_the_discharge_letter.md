# Piece: The Discharge Letter

## Taxonomy

[The Notes](place_the_notes.md). The document that closes the episode and reaffirms the heading.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Written at the end of the visit, filed into [the notes](place_the_notes.md), and copied to everyone who will read this person next.

## Load Bearing

Without it the attribution stays in one room, and it does not.
It is the piece that makes the reading travel: every clinician who sees this person afterward begins from a letter in which the complaint was considered, explained, and resolved by a competent colleague, so the next reader inherits the conclusion along with the courtesy of not repeating work already done.

## Apparent

A short, well-written letter. History, examination, impression, plan.
Entirely accurate. Nothing in it is overstated and nothing is left out.

## Yearbook

One of many, each closing an episode the way the last one closed.
Read by the next clinician as continuity of care, which is what it is, and as a reason not to start again, which is also what it is.
