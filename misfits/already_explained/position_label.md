# Position: Label

## Taxonomy

[Already Explained](play_already_explained.md). The seat that is true, useful, and unable to be surprised.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A correct diagnosis and a wide explanatory reach across the symptoms this person presents.
Standing at the top of [the notes](place_the_notes.md), read before anything else in the room is said.

## Orders

Explain what can be explained.
Be used, because using what is known about a patient is better medicine than not using it.

## Loses

The ability to decline a symptom that fits, which means it can never be the thing that raises an alarm.
Its reach is the source of its value and the source of the failure at once: a condition that accounts for many things will account for the one it should not, by the same reasoning and in the same tone.

## Drives

Toward completeness of explanation, which reads as the mark of a good diagnosis, and away from any state in which something remains unaccounted for, which is the only state that would have prompted a look.
