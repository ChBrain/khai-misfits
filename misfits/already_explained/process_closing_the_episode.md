# Process: Closing the Episode

## Taxonomy

[Already Explained](play_already_explained.md). Ending the visit with the attribution recorded and passed on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

An account given and accepted, and a consultation reaching its end.
[The One Who Explains](persona_the_one_who_explains.md), holding [One Who Explains](position_one_who_explains.md), writing [the discharge letter](piece_the_discharge_letter.md).

## Direction

Outward, to every clinician who will read this person next.
The conclusion travels with the courtesy of not repeating work a competent colleague has already done.

## Lever

Continuity of care, which is a virtue, and which here means the next reader begins where the last one finished.

## Echo

A heading unchanged, an episode resolved, and a slightly stronger reason for the next person not to start again.
