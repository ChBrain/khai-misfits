# Place: The Small Room

## Taxonomy

[Already Explained](play_already_explained.md). Where the account is given, heard, and answered.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two chairs, a screen, a couch behind a curtain, and a clock that is not looked at more than it should be.
A person describing what is happening to them, and someone listening properly and asking follow-up questions.

## Holds

[The Heading](piece_the_heading.md), on the screen, read before the first word is spoken.
[The Blank Form](piece_the_blank_form.md), in the drawer and in the system, one click away and never clicked.

## Offers

Real attention. Time, examination, an answer rather than a shrug, and an invitation to come back.
Everything a person is entitled to expect from a consultation, delivered.

## Withheld

Any moment at which the explanation could fail to be available.
There is no silence in the room for something unaccounted-for to occupy, because the account is ready before the complaint is finished, and it is a good account.
