# Persona: The Other Illness

## Taxonomy

[Other Illness](position_other_illness.md). Ordinary, unrelated, treatable, and never looked for.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Unremarkable. The kind of thing that turns up in any clinic on any day, is straightforward when caught, and is caught by a test nobody would think twice about ordering.
Carries no unusual presentation and hides nothing: it presents exactly as it presents in everybody.

## Action

Runs its course underneath, at its own pace, producing the complaint that keeps being brought and keeps being accounted for.
Is not refused a test, because a test is never requested.

## Shadow

Cannot announce itself past the explanation, because everything it does is something the explanation covers.
What makes it dangerous here is nothing about the illness: it is common, it is well understood, and the delay that harms the person is manufactured entirely by the correctness of somebody else's reasoning.

## Tell

Is found late, or found by accident by someone who did not have the notes to hand, and is then unremarkable again.
