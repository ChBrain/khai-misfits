# Position: Clinician

## Taxonomy

[Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md). The seat that screens for the overshoot, catching gestational diabetes and preeclampsia without touching the imprint that presses them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Tools that read the overshoot after it appears: a glucose tolerance test, a blood pressure trend, urine tested for protein.
No tool that reaches the imprint itself, fixed in the genome before the pregnancy began.
The standing to treat, once the overshoot shows: insulin, monitoring, early delivery when the risk is severe.

## Orders

Watch for the overshoot: screen every pregnancy on the same schedule, since the imprint gives no warning beforehand and the numbers are the only signal available.
Treat what is found: manage the mother's blood sugar or blood pressure back toward a safe range, buying time and safety rather than removing the cause.
Accept what the seat does: that vigilance catches the acute risk and never the tug that produced it.

## Loses

The standing to prevent, holding only the standing to catch and to manage.
Because the imprint that sets the press is fixed before the pregnancy is screened, the seat loses any chance to intervene upstream of it, arriving only once the numbers already show the gap between press and cover.
It keeps every case the screening catches in time, and loses the cases where the overshoot arrives before the schedule does.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound vigilance meets a tug it cannot reach at the source,
screening every pregnancy the same because the overshoot gives little warning and, by doing so, catching real risk at a real and recurring cost,
so the seat that only did well by watching closely is the seat through which the trap is managed and never resolved, sound in its own reckoning and bounded by what a checkup can see.
