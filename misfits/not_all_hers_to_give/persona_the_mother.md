# Persona: The Mother

## Taxonomy

[Mother](position_mother.md). The maternal system, related by half to this fetus and by that same half to every offspring she will ever have, present and future.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Carrying, providing, the ordinary work of gestation: insulin secreted, vasculature adjusted, a body doing exactly what a healthy pregnancy asks of it.
Reads as generous without limit, the body that simply gives what the fetus needs.
No withholding on show: her cover meets the press in the great majority of pregnancies, and what she gives looks like all there is to give.

## Action

Runs [Ration the Broods](plan_ration_the_broods.md): through [Countering](process_countering.md) at [the Bloodstream](place_the_bloodstream.md), raises her insulin output and adjusts her vasculature to meet [the Push](piece_the_push.md) from [the Paternal Gene](persona_the_paternal_gene.md).
Cannot see the imprint that set the press, since it was fixed in the fetus's genome before the pregnancy began, and can only answer the demand as it arrives, not the mechanism behind it.
Does what her own relatedness rewards: she is related by half to this offspring and by that same half to every offspring she will ever have, so she covers the present demand without giving past what the whole ledger can bear.

## Shadow

Not withholding: her cover is calibrated to her own relatedness across every offspring she will ever have, present and future, so meeting the press without exceeding it is exactly the sound response, not a stinginess toward the one in front of her.
Because her ledger is real and the fetus's is not the same ledger, her soundest response is a cover that, in a harder pregnancy, is not quite enough, the same calculus that serves her well in the ordinary case failing her only where the press is strongest.
She is the reason most pregnancies read as nothing but growing, and the reason a few do not.

## Tell

Reports a normal glucose tolerance test and a stable blood pressure as the sign the pregnancy is going well, and in most pregnancies it is.
Cannot tell, from inside, whether her cover is meeting an ordinary press or already straining against one stronger, until the numbers at [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md) say so.
