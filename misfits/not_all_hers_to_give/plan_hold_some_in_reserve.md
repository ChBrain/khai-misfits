# Plan: Hold Some in Reserve

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the maternal gene's seat](position_maternal_gene.md), inside [Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Maternal Gene](position_maternal_gene.md)

## Direction

Where you are heard, hold resource back for a future sibling you are related to through the mother, since that sibling's fitness is worth something to your own relatedness. Where imprinting has silenced you, stay silent: a mixed signal at those loci would blunt the paternal copy's press without stopping it, and the locus has already resolved against your voice. Accept what the seat allows: restraint where the locus lets you speak, and none where it does not.

## Orders

([The Maternal Gene](persona_the_maternal_gene.md) is silenced through [Imprinting](process_imprinting.md) at [the Placenta](place_the_placenta.md) wherever the growth loci favor the paternal copy, and heard only at the loci imprinting leaves open to it.)
