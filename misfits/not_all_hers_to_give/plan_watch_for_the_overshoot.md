# Plan: Watch for the Overshoot

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the clinician's seat](position_clinician.md), inside [Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Clinician](position_clinician.md)

## Direction

Screen every pregnancy on the same schedule, since the imprint gives no warning beforehand and the numbers, a glucose tolerance test, a blood pressure trend, are the only signal available. Treat what is found, managing blood sugar or blood pressure back toward a safe range with insulin, monitoring, or early delivery, buying safety rather than removing the cause. Accept what the seat can do: catch the overshoot when the push outruns the cover, and leave the imprint that produced it untouched.

## Orders

([The Clinician](persona_the_clinician.md) screens at [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md) through Overshooting made visible in the numbers, holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) against [the Overshoot](piece_the_overshoot.md).)

## Implementation

([The Clinic](place_the_clinic.md) is where the overshoot is caught, not prevented; screening reads the gap between [the Push](piece_the_push.md) and [the Counter](piece_the_counter.md) after it shows in the mother's blood sugar or blood pressure, and treatment manages the acute risk once found.)

## Targets

- [ ] every pregnancy is screened on the same schedule, regardless of how it reads
- [ ] gestational diabetes and preeclampsia are caught when the numbers cross the threshold
- [ ] treatment manages the overshoot, at a real and recurring cost
- [ ] the imprint that produced the press is left untouched, since no clinic visit reaches it
