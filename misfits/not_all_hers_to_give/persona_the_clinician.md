# Persona: The Clinician

## Taxonomy

[Clinician](position_clinician.md). The one who screens for the overshoot, catching gestational diabetes and preeclampsia without touching the imprint that presses them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A routine checkup: a glucose tolerance test, a blood pressure cuff, numbers read and charted, the ordinary vigilance of prenatal care.
Reads as precaution more than intervention, the visit that confirms a pregnancy is going well.
No alarm on show: most tests come back normal, and the visit that finds nothing is not wasted, it is the visit doing its job.

## Action

Runs [Watch for the Overshoot](plan_watch_for_the_overshoot.md): screens at [the Clinic](place_the_clinic.md) for [the Overshoot](piece_the_overshoot.md) through Overshooting made visible in the numbers, then treats with insulin, monitoring, or early delivery when the push has outrun the mother's cover.
Cannot reach the imprint itself, silenced or active in the fetus's genome before the pregnancy began, and so can only manage the overshoot each time it appears, not prevent the press that produces it.
Does what the seat allows: catches the acute risk early, buying safety at the cost of real, ongoing medical effort.

## Shadow

Not a zealot: the pathology it screens for is real, gestational diabetes and preeclampsia both measurable and both dangerous untreated, so its vigilance answers an actual risk and not an imagined one.
Because it can treat the overshoot but not the asymmetry beneath it, its every success is a management and never a cure, the same tug playing out again in whatever pregnancy comes next.
It is the closest thing the play allows to a remedy, and it is bounded on every side.

## Tell

Orders the same screening in every pregnancy regardless of how it reads, because the imprint cannot be seen directly and the overshoot, when it comes, gives little warning beforehand.
Cannot tell a pregnancy that will overshoot from one that will not, until the glucose tolerance test or the blood pressure trend says so, so the vigilance is constant and the finding, when it comes, decisive.
