# Piece: The Ledger

## Taxonomy

[Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md). The relatedness asymmetry between a mother, related by half to every offspring she will ever have, and a fetus, related to itself completely.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Standing beneath everything at [The Placenta](place_the_placenta.md) and [The Bloodstream](place_the_bloodstream.md): the arithmetic of relatedness that neither side chose and neither can revise (Trivers; Hamilton).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the two sides want the same thing: if the mother were related to this fetus alone, her giving and the fetus's asking would balance at the same point, and no press would run past the cover.
It bears the load as the structural mismatch the whole trap rests on. Because the mother's ledger is spread across every offspring she will ever have and the fetus's is held to itself alone, the two were never going to set the same price on the same resource, and the whole tug follows from that single difference.

## Apparent

A closeness, even: mother and fetus related by half is no small thing, closer kin than almost any other relationship either will have.
