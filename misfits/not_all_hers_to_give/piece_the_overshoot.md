# Piece: The Overshoot

## Taxonomy

[Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md). Gestational diabetes and preeclampsia, arising when the fetal push outruns the mother's cover, with no toxin, no infection, and no one to blame.

## Owner

## Place

Read at [The Clinic](place_the_clinic.md): a glucose tolerance test crossing its threshold, a blood pressure trend that will not settle, the numbers naming what the bloodstream has already produced (Haig).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the tug stays invisible: without a real, measurable pathology, the conflict between the two ledgers would be a theory with no case to point to, felt nowhere and costing no one anything real.
It bears the load as the proof the tug is not metaphorical. Because the overshoot is a treatable, dangerous condition and not a figure of speech, it is what shows the misfit is a genuine trap and not merely a clever reading of ordinary biology.

## Apparent

A complication, even: a pregnancy that went wrong, read as if something failed, when both sides performed exactly as their own relatedness selected them to.
