# Piece: The Imprint

## Taxonomy

[Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md). The silencing of one parental gene copy at growth loci, leaving the other alone active, set by which copy has a stake in a possible future half sibling.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Set at [The Placenta](place_the_placenta.md), locus by locus, before the pregnancy is screened at all: the paternal copy switched on where it has nothing to lose, the maternal copy silenced where its restraint would otherwise be heard (Haig; Haig and Moore).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the fetus's own genome speaks with one voice: if both copies were active and equally weighted, their signals would partly cancel, and the press would fall closer to what a single, unified interest would set.
It bears the load as the mechanism that turns a statistical asymmetry into an actual, unopposed signal. Because the maternal copy's restraint is silenced rather than merely outvoted, the paternal copy's press reaches the mother's system uncontested, the imprint the reason a difference in relatedness becomes a difference in hormones.

## Apparent

A redundancy resolved, even: two copies of the same gene, only one active, read as ordinary economy rather than as a conflict decided in advance.

## Yearbook

Stands over the turn in [Silence the Generous Copy](plot_silence_the_generous_copy.md), the moment the fetus's own voice divides.
It works together with [The Push](piece_the_push.md), the imprint the reason the push goes uncontested from within.
