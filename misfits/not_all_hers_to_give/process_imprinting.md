# Process: Imprinting

## Taxonomy

[Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md). Borne at [The Placenta](place_the_placenta.md), precipitated by the growth loci resolving which parental copy speaks, in [Silence the Generous Copy](plot_silence_the_generous_copy.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A relatedness split between the fetus's two gene copies: the paternal copy with no stake in a possible future half sibling, the maternal copy with a real one.

## Direction

From two competing copies at a single locus toward one copy switched on and one silenced, the split fixed before the pregnancy begins and unchanging through it.

## Lever

That a mixed signal from both copies would blunt the paternal copy's press without stopping it, so evolution silences the copy whose interest is restraint rather than let it compete locus by locus (Haig; Haig and Moore).

## Echo

[The Imprint](piece_the_imprint.md), set at the growth loci, one voice active and one switched off.
