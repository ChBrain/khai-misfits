# Plan: Ration the Broods

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the mother's seat](position_mother.md), inside [Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Mother](position_mother.md)

## Direction

Cover the present demand from the resources your whole ledger can bear, since you are related by half to this offspring and by that same half to every offspring you will ever have, present and future. Raise insulin and adjust the vasculature to meet the press as it arrives, since the imprint that set it is invisible to you and only its effect can be answered. Give what a fair share of the whole ledger allows, not the full amount any single offspring's genes would ask for, and accept that this will meet the demand in most pregnancies and fall short in some.
