## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Shown

A glucose tolerance test, a blood pressure cuff, a chart of numbers tracked across the pregnancy, the ordinary vigilance of prenatal care.

## Holds

[The Overshoot](piece_the_overshoot.md), read here in the numbers before it is read anywhere else, and [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), what screening can and cannot reach.

## Offers

To the mother, an early warning and a real treatment, insulin, monitoring, or early delivery, when the push has outrun the cover.

## Withheld

Any access to the imprint itself, fixed in the fetus's genome before the pregnancy began, so the clinic can only manage what the placenta and the bloodstream have already produced.
