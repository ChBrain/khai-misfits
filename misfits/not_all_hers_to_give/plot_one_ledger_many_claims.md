# Plot: One Ledger, Many Claims

## Taxonomy

[Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Placenta](place_the_placenta.md), where two bodies share one project and two different ledgers.

## Action

[The Ledger](piece_the_ledger.md) stands over the whole pregnancy: the mother related by half to this offspring and by that same half to every offspring she will ever have, present and future, the fetus related to itself completely. The scene closes with two ledgers that were never going to balance at the same point for both, the arithmetic set before either side does anything at all (Trivers; Hamilton).

## Stage

Neither ledger is wrong: the mother's is the sound accounting of a parent with more than one offspring to weigh, and the fetus's is the sound accounting of a gene related to itself alone, each correct by its own reckoning.

## Tension

The one fact that would resolve the mismatch, that the fetus is worth exactly what the mother's wider ledger says it is worth, is the one fact the fetus's own genes are not selected to accept.
