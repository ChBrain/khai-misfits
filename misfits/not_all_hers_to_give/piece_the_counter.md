# Piece: The Counter

## Taxonomy

[Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md). The mother's insulin and vascular response meeting the fetal push, calibrated to her whole ledger and not to this offspring alone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Answered at [The Bloodstream](place_the_bloodstream.md): insulin rising and vasculature adjusting against the placental signal, the response of a system rationing across every offspring it will ever have (Haig).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and every pregnancy overshoots: without a counter-regulating response, the fetal push would land unopposed, and gestational diabetes or preeclampsia would follow in the ordinary case rather than the exception.
It bears the load as the reason most pregnancies read as nothing but growing. Because the mother's cover meets the push with room to spare far more often than not, the tug stays invisible, the counter doing exactly enough work to keep the conflict from ever showing in the numbers.

## Apparent

A given, even: a healthy mother's body simply handles pregnancy, and the counter reads as no more than that handling.
