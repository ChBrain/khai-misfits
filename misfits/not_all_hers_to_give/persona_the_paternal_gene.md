# Persona: The Paternal Gene

## Taxonomy

[Paternal Gene](position_paternal_gene.md). The gene copy inherited from the father, active at growth loci, unrelated to any half sibling the mother might have by a different man.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A grower, nothing more: the copy behind a healthy weight, a strong birth, a fetus doing well.
Its work reads as the ordinary business of growth, the thing every prenatal visit hopes to see more of.
No excess on show: growth is what a fetus is supposed to do, and asking for the resources to do it looks like nothing but that.

## Action

Runs [Draw It All Now](plan_draw_it_all_now.md): stays switched on through [Imprinting](process_imprinting.md) at [the Placenta](place_the_placenta.md), the more restrained maternal copy silenced beside it, then drives [the Push](piece_the_push.md) through [Extracting](process_extracting.md) at [the Bloodstream](place_the_bloodstream.md), lifting the mother's blood sugar and blood pressure.
Cannot be talked down by the maternal copy at its own locus, since that copy has already been silenced, and cannot be reasoned with by the mother's system except through the slower channel of her own counter-regulation.
Does what its relatedness rewards: it presses for extraction, since it shares nothing with a possible future half sibling by a different father and has nothing to lose by asking for everything now.

## Shadow

Not greedy: it has no stake in the mother's future offspring by another man, so pressing for more here costs it nothing it values, and doing so is exactly what its own relatedness selects for.
Because its interest is calculated fully in this fetus and not at all in any half sibling to come, its sound pressing is exactly what pushes the mother's blood sugar and blood pressure past her ledger's own setting.
It is the ordinary engine of growth, and the engine of the overshoot when the push outruns the cover.

## Tell

Reads, on any single scan, as nothing but a fetus growing well, the pressing indistinguishable from health until the numbers are checked.
Cannot see the mismatch, because pressing is simply what it does, so it keeps drawing at the same rate whether the mother's cover is ample or already stretched, the same sound calculus behind an easy pregnancy and a hard one.
