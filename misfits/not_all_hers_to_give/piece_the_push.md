# Piece: The Push

## Taxonomy

[Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md). The placental hormones that raise the mother's blood sugar and blood pressure, carrying the paternal copy's demand into her own circulation.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Carried from [The Placenta](place_the_placenta.md) into [The Bloodstream](place_the_bloodstream.md): a paternally expressed growth gene shown moving fetal growth directly in the laboratory, the mechanism observed and not merely inferred (DeChiara, Robertson and Efstratiadis; Haig).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the imprint has no channel: an active paternal copy with no way to reach the mother's own physiology would press against nothing, and the demand would stay confined to the fetus's own tissue.
It bears the load as the physical carrier of the trap. Because the placenta is built from the fetus's own tissue and sits inside the mother's circulation, it is the one structure able to turn a genetic imprint into an actual hormonal signal, and the whole misfit passes through it.

## Apparent

Ordinary gestation, even: hormones rising through pregnancy are expected, and a placenta doing its job looks like nothing more than that.
