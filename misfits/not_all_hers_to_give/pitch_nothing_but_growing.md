# Pitch: Nothing but Growing

## Taxonomy

[Not All Hers to Give](play_not_all_hers_to_give.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Ordinary and biological.
A healthy pregnancy, a fetus growing, a mother's body carrying it, blood sugar and blood pressure the routine business of gestation and a checkup that reads well.
Nothing false in it: the fetus really is growing, the mother's body really is carrying it, and a pregnancy that reads as ordinary usually is.

## Undertow

Under the ordinary growing, two ledgers that do not match.
The fetus is related to itself completely, but the mother is related by half to it and by half to every offspring she will ever have, so a gene copy inherited from the father, with nothing riding on any future half sibling, presses her blood sugar and blood pressure higher than her own genes are selected to give (Trivers; Haig).
No greed and no withholding: the pressing copy is sound by its own relatedness, the mother's cover is sound by hers, and the mismatch is arithmetic, not appetite.

## Nerve

The comfort of a body simply growing another, and the catch that growing is never quite as simple as one project shared:
that the mother's ledger is spread across every offspring she will ever have and the fetus's ledger is held to itself alone, so the very hormones that read as ordinary gestation are the currency of a press already under way, the one relatedness asymmetry seen twice, once between mother and fetus and once between the fetus's own two gene copies,
and nothing but growing is not neutral biology but a demand already pressed past what the mother's own ledger would set.

## Echo

After the run: a glucose tolerance test that comes back high, a blood pressure reading that will not settle, gestational diabetes or preeclampsia named with no toxin and no infection behind either (Haig).
And the remedy, at a cost and never complete: insulin, monitoring, or early delivery catches the overshoot, but the imprint that pressed it was set in the genome before the pregnancy began, and no clinic visit un-silences a gene copy (Haig and Moore; DeChiara, Robertson and Efstratiadis).
And the bound held in view, because the mechanism is real and conditional at once: it needs a viviparous kin system with loci under imprinting control, and it lifts toward convergence wherever a mother's every offspring shares the one father, so the whole of it is that a resource is never quite as free to give as it looks, once you ask whose future it was also owed to.
