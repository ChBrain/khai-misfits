# Persona: The Maternal Gene

## Taxonomy

[Maternal Gene](position_maternal_gene.md). The gene copy inherited from the mother, silenced at the same growth loci where the paternal copy presses, related through the mother to any future sibling.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A restraint that is never seen acting, because at the loci that matter it does not act at all.
Where it is active, at other loci, it reads as the counterweight, the copy that would hold some resource back if it could be heard.
No neglect on show: it is silenced, not absent, its interest still real even where its voice is switched off.

## Action

Would run [Hold Some in Reserve](plan_hold_some_in_reserve.md), preserving resource for a future sibling it is related to through the mother, but at the growth loci [Imprinting](process_imprinting.md) at [the Placenta](place_the_placenta.md) switches it off, leaving [the Paternal Gene](persona_the_paternal_gene.md) alone active.
Cannot press its own case at a silenced locus, since evolution has already resolved which copy speaks there, and cannot be restored to voice by anything the mother or the clinic does after conception.
Does, at the loci where it is active instead of silenced, exactly the moderating work its relatedness to future siblings selects for.

## Shadow

Not weak: it is related through the mother to whatever sibling might follow, so holding back here is exactly what its own relatedness rewards, the same sound calculus as its paternal counterpart, aimed the other way.
Because a mixed signal from both copies would only blunt the press without stopping it, evolution silences the very copy whose restraint would otherwise have moderated the demand, so its soundest instinct is the one instinct the locus never lets it use.
It is the counterweight the system evolved away from where the press is strongest.

## Tell

Cannot be observed directly at a silenced locus, its absence indistinguishable, on a single scan, from a fetus with no restraint gene at all.
Shows itself only where it is active, at loci restraining growth from the maternal side, the same relatedness logic run the other direction and visible there instead.
