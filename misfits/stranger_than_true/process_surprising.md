# Process: Surprising

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). Borne at [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), precipitated by [the Tale](persona_the_tale.md), in [Free of the Facts](plot_free_of_the_facts.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[the Tale](persona_the_tale.md), unbound by the facts.
Not invented to deceive, only free of the one constraint a true account must keep: that it match what occurred.

## Direction

From a story that must be surprising to be shared, toward a false one made or found more surprising than any true one could be.
Every story here competes on novelty; the false story simply enters that competition without truth's handicap.

## Lever

Truth is bound by what happened while a falsehood is free to be as novel as it can be, so the false out-novels the true at the one thing the filter selects (Vosoughi, Roy & Aral).
No one sets out to make the lie stranger; strangeness is simply cheaper to reach when nothing has to have actually occurred.

## Echo

[the Novelty Edge](piece_the_novelty_edge.md).
The edge that follows a falsehood into every later share it wins.
