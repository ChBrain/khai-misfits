# Plot: What Slows the Share

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The one friction that could slow the false, and everything it costs to apply it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), a share is about to be made: the story has already passed the eye's test for the striking, and the finger is a moment from passing it on.

## Action

[the Checker](persona_the_checker.md) applies [the Brake](piece_the_brake.md) through [Chasing](process_chasing.md): a prompt to read or verify before the pass, catching the share for a moment before it is made, at the cost of the reach and speed that sharing lives on.

## Stage

This is the hedge, and it is load bearing: the friction fights the grain of the ordinary human preference for the novel, and slowing the false this way also slows the true, since both are caught by the same pause. The tilt it is fighting is a tendency, strongest in political news, and novelty is the authors' best-supported explanation of it, not a settled causal certainty; false news is more likely to spread, not that all falsehood spreads, and truth is never shown to be unable to catch up (Vosoughi, Roy & Aral).

## Tension

The only brake that slows the false also slows the true, because both are carried by the same appetite, and that appetite, the ordinary and useful love of the new, is not itself the trouble.
