# Piece: The Brake

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The friction laid on the pass: a prompt to read, to check, to pause before sharing, and the mark of where the tendency's bound sits.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), applied by [the Checker](persona_the_checker.md) through [Chasing](process_chasing.md): the moment inserted between the surprise and the pass, where a share can still be reconsidered.

## Load Bearing

Vosoughi, Roy and Aral (Science, 2018) describe a tendency, strongest in political news and best explained by the novelty of false stories, not a rule that every falsehood outruns every truth. The Brake is the piece that keeps that bound visible: it is what a check or a pause can still catch, and where it works, the differential the study measured narrows.
Guard against reading the brake as a free or certain fix: it fights the grain of the same preference for the novel that Sharing runs on, and using it costs the pause, the reach and the speed that sharing lives on, so it is applied at a price, not for free. And it addresses a tendency, strongest in political news and driven by novelty, not a law that all falsehood spreads; the brake slows a bias, it does not cancel one.

## Apparent

It looks like simple diligence: a moment's pause before passing something on, the responsible thing anyone would do. Nothing about stopping to check looks costly from where the Checker stands; the cost only shows up in the reach given up while everyone else has already passed the tale on.
