# Persona: The Checker

## Taxonomy

[Checker](position_checker.md), inside [Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The archetype of the one who chases the lie: the fact-checker whose correction sets off after the false has spread and arrives late, and whose only brake on the false also slows the true, the costly remedy.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The spoilsport, arriving to say the good story is not true. It looks like the one who turns up after the party to correct the record, tiresome and late, wanting everyone to slow down and check.

## Action

Chase it down, and reach for the brake. The Checker sets off after the spread lie through [Chasing](process_chasing.md) at [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), on [Chase It Down](plan_chase_it_down.md), the correction arriving long after, and it reaches for [the Brake](piece_the_brake.md) at [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), a prompt to verify before the pass (Brandolini). It works against the current the filter runs.

## Shadow

Its correction lags and its brake slows the true as well. Because refuting the surprising costs far more than telling it, the Checker always arrives after the lie has done its work, and the only friction it can put on the false, a check before the share, fights the ordinary love of the new and slows the true story along with the false (Brandolini; Vosoughi, Roy, and Aral). It keeps the one real remedy and bears its whole cost, the late corrector whose brake catches everyone.

## Tell

It arrives with the truth after the lie has been believed, and its brake slows them both.
