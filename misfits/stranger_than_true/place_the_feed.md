# Place: The Feed

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). Where every story meets the same test, not whether it is true but whether it is worth passing on. Hosts [What Gets Passed On](plot_what_gets_passed_on.md), [Free of the Facts](plot_free_of_the_facts.md), [What Slows the Share](plot_what_slows_the_share.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A stream of stories passing by, each read in an instant and either shared or let go.
No two treated alike: some pulling at the eye, most scrolled past without a second look.

## Holds

[the Surprise](piece_the_surprise.md), [the Social Currency](piece_the_social_currency.md), [the Novelty Edge](piece_the_novelty_edge.md), [the Brake](piece_the_brake.md).
The filter that every story here must clear, the value a sharer draws from clearing it, the edge a falsehood carries into the test, and the one piece of friction that could slow a pass.

## Offers

To the sharer, the standing chance to be first with something new, the social value of passing on the surprising (Berger & Milkman).
To the surprising story, true or false, a crowd ready to carry it further, no gate asking first whether it happened.

## Withheld

Any weight given to a story for being true rather than novel.
The feed asks how striking a story is, not how accurate; accuracy earns nothing here that surprise does not already pay.
