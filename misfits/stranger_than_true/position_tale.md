# Position: Tale

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The seat of the false story that wins the sharing filter, more surprising than any true one because unbound by what happened, and so it travels far and fast, no schemer's weapon but the tale that is stranger than true.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A freedom the truth lacks: bound by nothing that happened, it can be made or happen to be as surprising as anyone likes.
An edge at the one contest the filter runs: because it can be more novel than any true rival, it wins the share the true story cannot.
No need of an author's intent, because an honest rumour or an innocent error is novel too, so it travels for being surprising and not for being a deliberate lie.

## Orders

Travel far.
Be the striking story, stranger than any true one because you are not held to the facts, and let the shares carry you farther and faster than the truth.
Know that you win for being novel and not for being false, that no one need craft you to deceive, and that being unbound by what happened is the whole of your advantage over the true.

## Loses

Nothing at all, and it carries no author's guilt.
Because it beats the true only by being more surprising, and a falsehood is free to be surprising as a truth is not, the seat wins the race without anyone having to lie, so the harm it does attaches to no deceiver and the tale travels clean of intent.
It keeps its wide reach and loses no cause for blame, the story that outruns the truth for being stranger than it.

## Drives

Toward the one that outruns the truth for being unbound by it,
stranger than true because it is not held to the facts, no schemer behind it,
so the seat that wins the share is the seat the filter favours for its novelty, the false ahead of the true and no one having lied.
