# Position: Sharer

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The seat of the one who passes on the surprising, rational and social, and so the engine that carries the novel ahead of the true.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

An appetite for the striking: news worth passing on is news that surprises, and the surprising is what it reaches for.
A sound reason to share it: passing on the novel marks it as informed and first, and gives the people it tells something new, real social value and not idle chatter.
No habit of weighing truth before novelty, because the filter it applies is surprise, and a story's being true or false is not what decides whether it is worth the pass.

## Orders

Pass it on.
Share the striking story, since to pass on the surprising is to be informed and first and to give your friends something new, a sound and social thing to do.
Know that the filter you apply selects for surprise and not for truth, and that this ordinary, useful impulse is exactly what carries the false ahead of the true, so you are the engine of the tilt by doing only what a good sharer does.

## Loses

Nothing of its own, and yet its sound sharing is the engine of the tilt.
Because it passes on what surprises and the false can be more surprising than the true, the seat carries the false farther than the true not by any wish to deceive but by the plain exercise of a good and social habit, so its very soundness feeds the filter that favours the false.
It keeps its good name and loses no cause for blame, the informed friend whose sharing is the tilt.

## Drives

Toward the one whose sound impulse carries the false ahead of the true,
passing on the surprising to be informed and first, no wish to deceive in it,
so the seat that shares as any good sharer does is the engine of the tilt, the filter selecting for surprise and the sharer feeding it.
