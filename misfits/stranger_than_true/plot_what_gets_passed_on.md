# Plot: What Gets Passed On

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The opening test every story meets before it goes anywhere: not whether it happened, but whether it is worth passing on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), stories flow past in a stream, each read in an instant, and the sharer must decide, story by story, what is worth passing on and what is scrolled past and let go.

## Action

[the Sharer](persona_the_sharer.md), from [the sharer's seat](position_sharer.md), runs [Pass It On](plan_pass_it_on.md) through [Sharing](process_sharing.md), taking [the Surprise](piece_the_surprise.md) as the test a story must clear, and earning [the Social Currency](piece_the_social_currency.md) by passing on the striking.

## Stage

Sharing the novel is rational: it carries social value, marking the sharer as informed and in the know, and it gives the receiver something new (Berger & Milkman). The feed's filter is built to select for surprise, not for truth, so the sharer's ordinary and sound impulse to pass on the striking thing is doing exactly what it looks like it is doing, and nothing more sinister.

## Tension

Nothing here is careless, no liar and no dupe; the sound impulse to pass on the striking is the very engine that will, story by story, tilt the whole feed toward the stranger tale.
