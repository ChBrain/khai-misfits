# Pitch: New Beats True

## Taxonomy

The single hook of [Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md): the default key the misfit is played in, that what a sharing network carries is chosen for being new, not for being true, and the false can be newer.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Reasonable, sociable, even generous. People passing on the striking thing they just heard, glad to be first with it, giving their friends something new. Nothing here is a lie told on purpose, and the crowd is nobody's fool.

## Undertow

The sharing network is a filter, and it selects for surprise (Vosoughi, Roy & Aral). A true story is bound by what happened; a false one is bound by nothing, so it can be stranger than true, and stranger is what wins the share. So the false outruns the true on the appetite for the novel alone, and bots have nothing to do with it.

## Nerve

That the truth loses the race for being true, since being true is a constraint on how surprising it can be, and the lie is round the world before the correction has its boots on. No villain: the appetite that carries the false is the ordinary, useful love of the new.

## Echo

New beats true, because the truth has to be true and the lie does not.
