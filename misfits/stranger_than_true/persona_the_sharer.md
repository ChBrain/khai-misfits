# Persona: The Sharer

## Taxonomy

[Sharer](position_sharer.md), inside [Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The archetype of the one who passes on the surprising: rational and social, sharing what strikes them to be informed and first, and so the engine that carries the novel ahead of the true.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A well-informed friend, generous with the news. It looks like the one who keeps you up to date, first with the striking story, glad to give you something new, and no one thinks the worse of it for that.

## Action

Pass on the surprising. The Sharer takes [the Surprise](piece_the_surprise.md) at [the Feed](place_the_feed.md) and through [Sharing](process_sharing.md) passes it on, earning [the Social Currency](piece_the_social_currency.md) of being informed and first, on [Pass It On](plan_pass_it_on.md) (Berger and Milkman). It shares what struck it, and often believes it.

## Shadow

Its sound impulse is the engine of the tilt. Passing on the striking is ordinary and sensible, and that very soundness is what feeds the filter that selects for surprise, so the Sharer carries the false ahead of the true not by any wish to deceive but by doing exactly what a good sharer does (Vosoughi, Roy, and Aral). It keeps its good name and owes no cause for blame, the informed friend whose sharing is the tilt.

## Tell

It is first with the striking story, and never asked if it was true.
