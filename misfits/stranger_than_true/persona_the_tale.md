# Persona: The Tale

## Taxonomy

[Tale](position_tale.md), inside [Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The archetype of the false story that wins the sharing filter: more surprising than any true one because it is unbound by what happened, and so it travels far and fast, no schemer's weapon but the tale that is stranger than true.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

A great story, the one everyone is talking about. It looks like the striking, share-worthy news of the hour, the thing too good to keep to yourself, and nothing on its face marks it false.

## Action

Out-surprise the true, and travel. Free of the facts, the Tale holds [the Novelty Edge](piece_the_novelty_edge.md) through [Surprising](process_surprising.md) at [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), stranger than any true story, and carried by the shares it wins [the Wide Reach](piece_the_wide_reach.md) through [Outrunning](process_outrunning.md), on [Travel Far](plan_travel_far.md) (Vosoughi, Roy, and Aral). It is often an honest rumour or an innocent error, novel and so passed on.

## Shadow

It wins for being novel, not for being false. Because a falsehood is bound by nothing while a truth is bound by the facts, the Tale can be made or happen to be more surprising than any true rival, so it beats the true at the one thing the filter selects, and it need be no one's deliberate lie to do it (Vosoughi, Roy, and Aral). It keeps its wide reach and carries no author's guilt, the story that travels because it is stranger than true.

## Tell

It is the best story going, and being true was never why.
