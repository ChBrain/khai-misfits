# Piece: The Novelty Edge

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The advantage a falsehood holds over a true story at the one test that decides the pass: unbound by what happened, it can be as surprising as the telling requires.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), held by [the Tale](persona_the_tale.md), pressed through [Surprising](process_surprising.md): where a true story is finished once the facts are told, a tale can keep bending until it clears the bar.

## Load Bearing

Vosoughi, Roy and Aral (Science, 2018) found false stories were reliably rated more novel than the true stories they displaced, and that this novelty gap tracked the differential in how far, fast, deep and broadly falsehood spread. A truth is bound by what happened; a tale answers only to what will travel, so it can be made, or can simply happen, to be more surprising than any fact-bound account of the same event.
Guard against reading the false story as a schemer's weapon, built by someone to deceive: no one need craft it for that purpose, honest rumours and plain errors are novel too and carry the same edge, and a tale wins at the Feed not because it is false but because, being free of the facts, it is freer to be surprising.

## Apparent

It looks like a good story, well told: sharper details, a cleaner twist, the version that finally makes sense of something confusing. Nothing on the surface flags it as untethered from what happened; it just reads as the better telling.
