# Process: Outrunning

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). Borne at [the Open Road](place_the_open_road.md), precipitated by [the Tale](persona_the_tale.md), in [Halfway Around the World](plot_halfway_around_the_world.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[the Tale](persona_the_tale.md), carried by the shares.
Not running on its own; carried onward at every hand it passes through, none of them acting as anything but an ordinary sharer.

## Direction

From a story shared once toward a story reaching farther, faster, and deeper than the true one.
Each pass hands the story to a fresh set of readers, and a fresh set of readers means a fresh chance to be passed on again.

## Lever

Because the false is more novel it is shared more, and each share carries it on, so it outruns the true across the whole network (Vosoughi, Roy & Aral).
The advantage is small at any one hand and large only in aggregate, the same rational preference for the novel repeated at scale.

## Echo

[the Wide Reach](piece_the_wide_reach.md).
The distance and speed the false story banks while the true one is still starting out.
