# Plot: Lacing the Boots

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The cost of being true, paid at the doorstep before the truth has even left.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), the true story is still getting ready to leave, mundane, accurate, and in no hurry, while the road ahead is already busy with something else.

## Action

[the Plodder](persona_the_plodder.md), from [the plodder's seat](position_plodder.md), runs [Lace the Boots](plan_lace_the_boots.md), the mundane true story wearing [the Boots](piece_the_boots.md), slow to spread because it is less novel to tell and dearer to verify before it is told.

## Stage

The truth is not less wanted, only less surprising, bound as it is to what actually happened, and costlier to check before passing it on, so it is slower off the mark than a story free to be as striking as it likes (the folk saying; Brandolini). This is not The Availability Cascade: the point here is not a belief made to feel more true by being retold, but a true story that is simply slower to travel in the first place.

## Tension

The truth is still lacing its boots while the lie is already gone, and being true turns out to be no help at all in the race the novelty filter runs.
