# Piece: The Social Currency

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The payoff a sharer collects for passing on the surprising: marked as informed, marked as first.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), earned by [the Sharer](persona_the_sharer.md) through [Sharing](process_sharing.md): each pass spends a little of the sharer's own standing and, when the story lands, earns it back with interest.

## Load Bearing

Berger and Milkman (2012) found that the content people share tends to be content that carries social value for the sharer: the surprising, the arousing, the useful, because passing it on marks the sharer as informed and gives the receiver something they did not already have. Vosoughi, Roy and Aral's later finding, that novelty predicts sharing, rests on this same mechanism: the pass is not idle, it is a small transaction, and the more surprising the story, the more currency it is worth.
Guard against reading the sharer as vain or careless for wanting this payoff: passing on the striking is ordinary and sensible social behaviour, the same impulse that keeps any network worth belonging to alive, and it is that same sound impulse, not a defect in the sharer, that ends up carrying the false ahead of the true.

## Apparent

It reads as generosity: here is something worth knowing, passed along for the receiver's benefit. The standing gained by being first with it is invisible from the inside; it looks like sharing, not earning.
