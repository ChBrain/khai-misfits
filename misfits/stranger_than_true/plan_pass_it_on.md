# Plan: Pass It On

## Taxonomy

## Direction

Share the striking story, since to pass on the surprising is to be informed and first and to give your friends something new, a sound and social thing to do. Know that the filter you apply selects for surprise and not for truth, and that this ordinary, useful impulse is exactly what carries the false ahead of the true, so you are the engine of the tilt by doing only what a good sharer does.
