# Position: Checker

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The seat that chases the lie, the fact-checker whose correction arrives after the false has spread and whose only brake on the false also slows the true, the costly remedy.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one remedy the filter allows: to slow the share, by a prompt to read or verify before the pass, or a correction sent after.
A pace it cannot win at: refuting a surprising falsehood costs far more than telling it, so its correction always sets off late and arrives later.
No brake that catches only the false, because friction on sharing fights the ordinary love of the new and slows the true story along with the false.

## Orders

Chase it down.
Send the correction after the spread lie and put friction on the share, a prompt to verify before the pass, since that is the only remedy the filter allows.
Accept that your correction lags by the bullshit asymmetry and that your brake slows the true as well as the false, and know that neither catches the lie already round the world.

## Loses

The race and the clean fix both, spent chasing what novelty already carried away.
Because refuting costs far more than asserting and any brake on the false also slows the true, the seat arrives after the lie has done its work and cannot slow the false without slowing the truth, so the remedy it holds is late and blunt and dear.
It keeps the one real remedy and bears its whole cost, the late corrector whose brake catches everyone.

## Drives

Toward the one whose correction lags and whose brake slows the true,
chasing the lie that novelty already carried off, no free fix in hand,
so the seat that holds the only remedy is the seat that arrives too late and catches too much, the cost of braking a filter that rewards surprise.
