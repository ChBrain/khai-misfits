# Plot: Halfway Around the World

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The reach the novelty edge buys, measured in how far and how fast one story travels past the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

On [the Open Road](place_the_open_road.md), the shares have already carried the story out past any single reader, each pass opening the road a little further ahead of where the truth has got to.

## Action

Carried by the shares, [the Tale](persona_the_tale.md), through [Outrunning](process_outrunning.md), wins [the Wide Reach](piece_the_wide_reach.md): farther, faster, and deeper than the true story manages, reaching more people, and reaching them sooner.

## Stage

Falsehood diffused farther, faster, and deeper than truth, most markedly in political news, and the study's own bot control shows that automated accounts spread true and false news at the same rate, so the reach here is ordinary human sharing of the surprising, not any engineered push (Vosoughi, Roy & Aral). This is not Information Cascade: no one here discards a private signal or copies another's observed choice; each person shares what strikes them as novel, and the road opens by that alone.

## Tension

The lie is halfway round the world on nothing but the appeal of the surprising, and no one pushed it there; ordinary people simply kept passing on the striking, each one sure of nothing but that the story was worth telling.
