# Plan: Lace the Boots

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the plodder's seat](position_plodder.md), inside [Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Plodder](position_plodder.md)

## Direction

Set out as the true story must, held to the facts and no more surprising than they allow, and verified before you go. Know that being true is no help in this race, that the filter rewards surprise and your constraint to the facts caps your novelty, so you leave the doorstep slow and the lie is already gone.

## Orders

([The Plodder](persona_the_plodder.md) wears [the Boots](piece_the_boots.md) at [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), the true story bound to what happened and dearer to verify, setting out slow while the tale is already on [the Open Road](place_the_open_road.md).)

## Implementation

([the Boots](piece_the_boots.md) at [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md) slow the true story, because it can be no stranger than the facts allow and costs time to verify that the tale never spent, so it leaves late and the lie is already gone.)

## Targets

- [ ] the true story is set out held to the facts and verified before it goes
- [ ] being true is understood to add nothing to the story's novelty or reach
- [ ] the truth is seen as no less wanted, only less surprising and costlier to check
- [ ] the constraint to the facts is owned as exactly what caps the novelty and slows the spread
