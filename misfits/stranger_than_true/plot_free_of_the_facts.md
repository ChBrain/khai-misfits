# Plot: Free of the Facts

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The edge one story carries into the feed's test that the other cannot buy at any price.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), a true story and a false story arrive at the same test of what is worth passing on, and only one of them is free to make itself stranger to pass it.

## Action

[the Tale](persona_the_tale.md), from [the tale's seat](position_tale.md), runs [Travel Far](plan_travel_far.md) through [Surprising](process_surprising.md), holding [the Novelty Edge](piece_the_novelty_edge.md): unbound by what happened, and so free to be more surprising than any true story could ever be.

## Stage

Truth is constrained by the facts; a falsehood is free to be as novel as it can be made or happens to be, so the false out-novels the true at the one thing the feed's filter selects (Vosoughi, Roy & Aral). This is not a schemer's weapon: honest rumours and simple errors carry the same freedom and the same edge, wearing no more intent than the truth they outrun.

## Tension

The false wins not because it is false but because, being free of the facts, it can be stranger than true, and nothing in the telling has to mean anyone any harm at all.
