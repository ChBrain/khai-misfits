# Process: Chasing

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). Borne at [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), precipitated by [the Checker](persona_the_checker.md), in [The Late Correction](plot_the_late_correction.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[the Checker](persona_the_checker.md).
Not slow by carelessness; the correction is thorough because it has to be, and thorough takes time the lie never had to spend.

## Direction

From a lie already spread toward a correction that sets off after it and arrives late.
The checker starts only once the lie is visible, so the chase always opens from behind.

## Lever

Refuting a surprising falsehood costs far more than telling it, so the truth reaches its audience long after the lie has, and the correction always lags (Brandolini).
Making a claim needs no evidence; unmaking one does, and gathering evidence is the slower trade.

## Echo

[the Brake](piece_the_brake.md).
The one piece of friction the chase leaves behind for the next surprising story to meet.
