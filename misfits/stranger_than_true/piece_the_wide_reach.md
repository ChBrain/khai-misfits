# Piece: The Wide Reach

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The documented outcome: falsehood spreading farther, faster and deeper than the truth it displaced.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Open Road](place_the_open_road.md), won through [Outrunning](process_outrunning.md): where the Tale travels once loosed from any single feed, into cascades wider and quicker than the true story ever reaches.

## Load Bearing

Vosoughi, Roy and Aral (Science, 2018) tracked roughly 126,000 cascades of news on Twitter and found that false stories reached more people, reached them faster, spread deeper and diffused more broadly than true ones, the effect strongest in political news. This is the Wide Reach: not a single viral fluke but a measured, repeated differential across a huge sample of stories.
Guard against reading the wide reach as proof of a coordinated campaign or a bot-driven push: the study's own control found bots amplified true and false news at about the same rate, so the differential is not automation, it is the sum of ordinary human choices to pass on the surprising, made one Feed at a time.

## Apparent

It looks like a story that simply mattered more to people, one that struck a nerve the true version never did. The size of the reach reads as significance, as if the crowd's scale were a verdict on the story's importance rather than a record of what it happened to win at the Feed.
