# Place: The Doorstep

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). Where the true story is still getting ready to leave, and the correction sets out after a lie already gone. Hosts [Lacing the Boots](plot_lacing_the_boots.md), [The Late Correction](plot_the_late_correction.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The plodding truth lacing its boots, unhurried because it has nothing to invent.
The checker reading where the lie has already reached, and starting out anyway.

## Holds

[the Boots](piece_the_boots.md).
The lag itself: the truth bound to what happened, taking the time that binding costs, while the falsehood it answers is already round the world (the folk saying; Brandolini).

## Offers

To the truth and the correction, accuracy, in time: what finally arrives here is right, even if it is not first.
To the checker, ground to stand on and the reason to still make the trip, however late.

## Withheld

The speed to catch what the novelty filter already carried away.
No boot is laced fast enough to close a head start bought by being unbound by fact; the doorstep offers accuracy, never a shortcut to the distance already lost.
