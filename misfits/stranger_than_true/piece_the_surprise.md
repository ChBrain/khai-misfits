# Piece: The Surprise

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The novelty test a story must clear before the network decides it is worth passing on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), applied to every story that arrives there, run through [Sharing](process_sharing.md): before a story moves on, it is weighed against everything else on offer for how surprising it is.

## Load Bearing

Across roughly 126,000 cascades, Vosoughi, Roy and Aral (Science, 2018) found that falsehood was rated more novel than the truth it competed with, and that novelty was the strongest predictor of whether a story was shared onward. The Surprise is the filter itself: not a bias applied to a story after the fact, but the standing test every story meets at the point of the pass.
Guard against reading the preference for the surprising as a flaw or a failing in the people who apply it: sharing the novel is rational and useful, it is how a network finds what is worth anyone's attention, and the trouble is only that a true story cannot always be the most surprising thing on offer that day.

## Apparent

It looks like curiosity, nothing more: people notice and pass on what catches the eye, the way anyone would. Nothing about attending to the surprising looks like a trap in the moment; it looks like ordinary attention doing its ordinary job.
