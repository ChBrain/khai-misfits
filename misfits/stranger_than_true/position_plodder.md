# Position: Plodder

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The seat of the true story, mundane and slow, still lacing its boots while the lie is gone, less shared for being less novel and dearer to verify.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A constraint the false escapes: bound to what actually happened, it can be no more surprising than the truth allows.
A worth that does not help it race: it is accurate and wanted, but accuracy is not what the filter rewards, and being true adds nothing to its novelty.
No cheap way to leave the doorstep, because verifying it costs time the tale never spent, so it sets out slow and later.

## Orders

Lace the boots.
Set out as the true story must, held to the facts and no more surprising than they allow, and verified before you go.
Know that being true is no help in this race, that the filter rewards surprise and your constraint to the facts caps your novelty, so you leave the doorstep slow and the lie is already gone.

## Loses

The race, and it loses it for the very thing that makes it worth winning.
Because the filter rewards surprise and the truth is bound to be no stranger than the facts, the seat is outrun not despite being true but because being true limits how novel it can be, so its accuracy is exactly what slows it.
It keeps its truth and loses the reach, the true story that being true only holds back.

## Drives

Toward the one outrun for being bound to the facts,
true and so no stranger than the truth allows, no less wanted for it,
so the seat that carries the truth is the seat the filter leaves behind, still lacing its boots while the lie is round the world.
