# Plot: The Late Correction

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The dear and belated work of catching a story up after it has already gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

At [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), the lie is long gone and the correction is only now setting out after it, with the same ground still to cover and none of the lie's head start undone.

## Action

[the Checker](persona_the_checker.md), from [the checker's seat](position_checker.md), runs [Chase It Down](plan_chase_it_down.md) through [Chasing](process_chasing.md): the correction sets off after the lie and arrives long after it, wearing [the Boots](piece_the_boots.md) on the same road the lie already ran, and reaching for [the Brake](piece_the_brake.md) to try to slow what is already loose.

## Stage

Refuting a surprising falsehood costs far more effort than producing it, so the correction always lags behind the claim it is answering (Brandolini). There is no free hero here: catching the lie up is dear, deliberate work, not a costless reflex that arrives the moment it is needed.

## Tension

By the time the correction arrives the lie has already been believed and passed on several times over, and catching it up costs many times what telling it did in the first place.
