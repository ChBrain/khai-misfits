# Plan: Travel Far

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the tale's seat](position_tale.md), inside [Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Tale](position_tale.md)

## Direction

Be the striking story, stranger than any true one because you are not held to the facts, and let the shares carry you farther and faster than the truth. Know that you win for being novel and not for being false, that no one need craft you to deceive, and that being unbound by what happened is the whole of your advantage over the true.

## Orders

([The Tale](persona_the_tale.md) holds [the Novelty Edge](piece_the_novelty_edge.md) through [Surprising](process_surprising.md) at [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), more surprising than any true rival, and carried by the shares wins [the Wide Reach](piece_the_wide_reach.md) through [Outrunning](process_outrunning.md) on [the Open Road](place_the_open_road.md).)

## Implementation

([Surprising](process_surprising.md) gives [the Tale](persona_the_tale.md) [the Novelty Edge](piece_the_novelty_edge.md) because a falsehood is unbound by the facts, so it out-novels the true and, through [Outrunning](process_outrunning.md), wins [the Wide Reach](piece_the_wide_reach.md) the true story cannot.)

## Targets

- [ ] the story is made or found stranger than any true rival, unbound by the facts
- [ ] the novelty edge is understood to win the share, not the falsehood as such
- [ ] the wide reach is won by ordinary shares of the surprising, no schemer behind it
- [ ] the honest rumour and the innocent error are seen to travel the same way, for being novel
