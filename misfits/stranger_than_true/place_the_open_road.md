# Place: The Open Road

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). Where the chosen story runs, carried on from share to share with nothing left to stop it. Hosts [Halfway Around the World](plot_halfway_around_the_world.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

A story out ahead of its own origin, reaching readers who never saw where it started.
Cascades of shares branching wider and deeper the further it travels.

## Holds

[the Wide Reach](piece_the_wide_reach.md).
The measured distance, speed, and depth the novelty edge buys a story once it is loose on the network (Vosoughi, Roy & Aral).

## Offers

To the surprising story, distance and speed: more people reached, and reached sooner, than a mundane one could manage.
Every share a fresh launch, so the road only lengthens the further the story has already come.

## Withheld

The same reach to the mundane true story left behind.
The road runs as far as the novelty carried it and no farther, so a plain account, however accurate, is not granted the crowd or the speed a stranger tale wins by right of being stranger.
