# Process: Sharing

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). Borne at [the Feed](place_the_feed.md), precipitated by [the Sharer](persona_the_sharer.md), in [What Gets Passed On](plot_what_gets_passed_on.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

[the Sharer](persona_the_sharer.md).
Reading a story, finding it surprising, and passing it on for the same reason anyone passes on the interesting: no deceiver, no dupe.

## Direction

From a surprising story seen toward a surprising story passed on.
The story does not change hands unread; it changes hands because reading it already paid off in the noticing.

## Lever

Sharing the novel carries social value, marking the sharer as informed and giving the receiver something new, so passing on the surprising is rational and not idle (Berger & Milkman).
The act rewards the sharer before it ever tests true or false, so the reward is collected the same way either way.

## Echo

[the Social Currency](piece_the_social_currency.md).
The value banked with every pass and spent again on the next.
