# Persona: The Plodder

## Taxonomy

[Plodder](position_plodder.md), inside [Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The archetype of the true story, mundane and slow, still lacing its boots while the lie is gone, less shared for being less novel and dearer to verify.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

The dull one, the story no one hurries to pass on. It looks like old news, or unremarkable news, the true thing that is simply not as striking as the tale beside it, and so it waits.

## Action

Lace the boots and set out slow. Bound to what actually happened, the Plodder can be no more surprising than the truth allows, so it wears [the Boots](piece_the_boots.md) at [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), on [Lace the Boots](plan_lace_the_boots.md), slow off the mark because it is less novel and because verifying it costs time the tale never spent (Brandolini). It travels, but late.

## Shadow

Its truth is no help in the race. The Plodder is not less wanted or less valued, only less surprising and costlier to check, and the filter rewards surprise, so being true does nothing for its reach and its constraint to the facts is exactly what caps its novelty (Vosoughi, Roy, and Aral). It keeps its accuracy and loses the race, the true story that being true only slows.

## Tell

It is still lacing its boots, and the lie is already round the world.
