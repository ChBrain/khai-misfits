# Piece: The Boots

## Taxonomy

[Stranger Than True](play_stranger_than_true.md). The lag itself: the mundane true story still lacing up while the tale it corrects is already gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At [the Doorstep](place_the_doorstep.md), worn by [the Plodder](persona_the_plodder.md) and by [the Checker](persona_the_checker.md): one setting out with what actually happened, the other setting out to prove what did not.

## Load Bearing

The folk saying gives the picture: a lie is halfway around the world before the truth has got its boots on. Brandolini's observation supplies the mechanism: the effort needed to refute a claim runs far larger than the effort it took to make it, so a correction is slower to assemble even once someone sets out to write it. Vosoughi, Roy and Aral's finding that falsehood reaches its audience faster than truth gives the lag a measured shape rather than leaving it a proverb.
Guard against reading the lag as the truth being weaker, less wanted, or less true for arriving late: the true story is not devalued by its delay, it is only less novel to begin with and dearer to check before telling, so it is slower off the mark and slower still to close the distance once it starts.

## Apparent

It reads as thoroughness: the true story takes longer because it is being done right, checked and confirmed before it is told. The delay looks like care, not disadvantage, right up until the tale it is meant to catch has already reached everyone it will reach.
