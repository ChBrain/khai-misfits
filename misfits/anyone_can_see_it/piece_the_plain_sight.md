# Piece: The Plain Sight

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). The conviction that one's own construal is not a reading but the unmediated facts, the first tenet of naive realism.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held in each seat at [The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md): the felt transparency of one's own view, the sense that one is simply looking and reporting, so that the reading carries the authority of reality itself and needs no defending (Ross and Ward).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and a disagreement is just two views: it is the plainness, the taking of one's construal for the facts, that turns a different vantage into an error to be explained.
It bears the load by feeling like perception. Because one's own reading arrives as reality and not as interpretation, a reasonable person's disagreement cannot be another truth but must be a failure to see it, and from that felt plainness the bias-read follows without being chosen.

## Apparent

Clear sight: the ordinary confidence of a person who has looked and seen, not stubbornness, just the fair report of what is there.

## Yearbook

Carried by [The Convinced](persona_the_convinced.md) and [The Opposed](persona_the_opposed.md) at [The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md), the felt reality [Construing](process_construing.md) produces.
It is what makes [The Bias Read](piece_the_bias_read.md) feel not like a slur but like the only explanation, the plainness from which the misfiling follows.
