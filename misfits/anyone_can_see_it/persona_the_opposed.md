# Persona: The Opposed

## Taxonomy

[Opposed](position_opposed.md). The mirror of the Convinced: reads the situation the other way, equally sure it sees plainly, and equally sure the first is the biased one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Equally reasonable, equally level, just as sure it is telling it like it is.
Reads the same situation the opposite way and finds its own account just as obvious, wondering how the other could see it so wrongly.
No malice in it: only the same calm certainty, aimed the other direction, a fair person reporting the facts as they plainly are from here.

## Action

Reads on [Consider the Source](plan_consider_the_source.md), carrying its own [Plain Sight](piece_the_plain_sight.md) from [Construing](process_construing.md) at [the Dispute](place_the_dispute.md), and through [Attributing](process_attributing.md) reaches [the Bias Read](piece_the_bias_read.md), discounting the other's view as what an interested party would say.
Cannot catch its own lens any more than the Convinced can, so its certainty is the exact twin of the certainty across the table, and it cannot see the symmetry.
Does what a reasonable person does: it holds its reading as the facts and reads the other's as bias, in equal good faith and mirror image.

## Shadow

Not the biased one it is taken for, and not free of the read it aims: it is doing precisely what the Convinced does, honestly, and the mirror is invisible from inside it.
Because it denies its own slant as readily as it spots the other's, the quarrel looks one-sided from its seat when it is perfectly symmetric, two honest vantages each convicting the other.
It is the reflection that cannot see the glass, sure it is the fair one exactly as its opposite is sure of the same.

## Tell

Says consider who is telling you that, and cannot see that the same could be said of itself.
Cannot suspect its own source, because its reading feels like the facts, so it discounts the other for bias with the same clear conscience the other discounts it, each certain the slant is all across the table.
