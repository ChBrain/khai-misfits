# Piece: The Bias Read

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). The attribution of the other's disagreement to bias, ignorance, or irrationality rather than to a different vantage, the third tenet.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Reached in each seat at [The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md) and hardened at [The Record](place_the_record.md): the verdict that the one who disagrees is not seeing another truth but failing to see the truth, from a distortion the reader is sure it does not share (Ross and Ward).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and a difference of vantage stays a difference of vantage: it is the bias-read that converts an honest disagreement into a charge against the other's character.
It bears the load as an honest inference, not a slur. From a seat that takes its own view as objective, a reasonable person's disagreement can only be explained by ignorance or bias, so the read is the sound conclusion of a false premise, sincerely reached and aimed the wrong way.

## Apparent

A fair judgement of the other: they are letting their interest, their politics, their feelings distort what is plainly there. It looks like discernment, and it is a misfiling.
