# Plan: Lay Out the Facts

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the arbiter's seat](position_arbiter.md), inside [Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Arbiter](position_arbiter.md)

## Direction

Offer a balanced account of the situation and expect its evenness to settle the dispute. Credit each side with what it gets right, name the facts plainly, and trust that a fair summary, visibly fair, will bring two reasonable people together. Do not anticipate the catch: that each side, reading the even account from a seat that takes its own view as objective, will find the evenness a slant against it, so the neutrality earns distrust rather than assent.

## Orders

([The Arbiter](persona_the_arbiter.md) lays out a balanced account at [the Record](place_the_record.md), expecting the facts to close the gap.)

## Implementation

([The Record](place_the_record.md) is where the account is offered; the even summary is construed by each side as slanted against it, so the arbiter is distrusted by both benches, and the facts, far from settling the dispute, become fresh evidence to each of the other's bias.)

## Targets

- [ ] a balanced, even-handed account is offered to settle the dispute
- [ ] each side is credited with what it gets right
- [ ] both sides read the evenness as a slant against them
- [ ] the neutral is distrusted for being neutral, the facts failing to close the gap
