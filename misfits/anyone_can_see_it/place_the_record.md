# Place: The Record

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). Where a fair account and the evidence are laid out to settle the dispute, and construed by each instead. Hosts [The Neutral Read as Biased](plot_the_neutral_read_as_biased.md) and [The Wider It Gets](plot_the_wider_it_gets.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The balanced summary, the evidence on the table, the neutral account offered as the thing that will bring two reasonable people together.

## Holds

[The Widening](piece_the_widening.md) as it grows with every exchange, the bias-reads hardening as the record is construed.

## Offers

To the arbiter, the hope that a fair statement of the facts will close the gap that argument opened.

## Withheld

A reading of the record that both sides accept as fair, since each finds an even account slanted against it and reads the evenness itself as bias.
