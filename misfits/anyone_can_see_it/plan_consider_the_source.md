# Plan: Consider the Source

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the opposed's seat](position_opposed.md), inside [Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Opposed](position_opposed.md)

## Direction

Discount the other's reading by the interest or angle that must be warping it, and hold your own as the simple facts. Consider the source: a view so wrong, to you, is best explained by what the one holding it stands to gain or wants to believe, so file it as bias rather than as a reasonable vantage. Hold your own reading as plain, since from where you sit it is, and do not notice that the very same could be said of you, because your own source is as invisible to you as the other's is plain.

## Orders

([The Opposed](persona_the_opposed.md) reads its own [Plain Sight](piece_the_plain_sight.md) through [Construing](process_construing.md) at [the Dispute](place_the_dispute.md), and through [Attributing](process_attributing.md) files the other's view as [the Bias Read](piece_the_bias_read.md).)

## Implementation

([The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md) is where the discounting is done; [Construing](process_construing.md) makes the seat's own reading feel like the facts, so considering the other's source reads as discernment and not as the mirror of the charge aimed back at it, the symmetry invisible from inside.)

## Targets

- [ ] the other's view is discounted by the source that must be warping it
- [ ] the seat's own reading is held as the plain facts
- [ ] the same discounting could be turned on the seat, and is not
- [ ] the quarrel looks one-sided from here though it is perfectly symmetric
