# Process: Hardening

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). Borne at [The Record](place_the_record.md), precipitated by continued contact and evidence between the parties, in [The Wider It Gets](plot_the_wider_it_gets.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

More engagement, offered as the natural way for two reasonable people to converge.

## Direction

From a gap toward a wider one, each exchange adding distance rather than closing it.

## Lever

That each construes the new facts through the same lens and reads the other's persistence as fresh proof of the bias already inferred, so contact confirms rather than corrects (Vallone, Ross, and Lepper).

## Echo

[The Widening](piece_the_widening.md), the two further apart for having tried to close the gap.
