# Piece: The Construal

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). The interpretive lens each party reads the situation through, the reason the same events yield two honest readings.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

At work in each seat at [The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md), unseen by the one who uses it: a lens of interest, history, and expectation through which the events arrive already meaning something, so that two people take in the same scene and register two different things (Ross and Ward).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is nothing to mistake for reality: if both read the situation the same way, there is no gap to misfile as the other's bias.
It is the construal that does the seeing and is not itself seen. Because the lens is invisible from inside as a lens, its reading feels like perception and not interpretation, and the whole trap rests on this: a vantage that presents itself as the plain facts.

## Apparent

Simply what happened: from each seat it does not look like a reading at all, only the events as they were, obvious to anyone with clear eyes.

## Yearbook

Applied by [The Convinced](persona_the_convinced.md) and [The Opposed](persona_the_opposed.md) through [Construing](process_construing.md) at [The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md).
It is the lens beneath [The Plain Sight](piece_the_plain_sight.md), the difference of vantage that the whole production mistakes for a difference of honesty.
