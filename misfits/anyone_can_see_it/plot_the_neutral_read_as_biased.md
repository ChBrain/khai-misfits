# Plot: The Neutral Read as Biased

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Record](place_the_record.md), where a fair account is brought in to settle the dispute.

## Action

[The Arbiter](persona_the_arbiter.md) on [Lay Out the Facts](plan_lay_out_the_facts.md) offers a balanced account, and both sides read it as slanted against them, since from each seat a fair account gives the other too much, so [The Blind Spot](piece_the_blind_spot.md) is laid bare in the mirror. The scene closes with the neutral party distrusted by both, the facts failing to close the gap (Vallone, Ross, and Lepper).

## Stage

The evenness is genuine and read as bias by all of it: the same balanced account is called slanted by both benches, so the neutrality that should have settled the dispute becomes fresh evidence, to each, of a thumb on the scale for the other.

## Tension

The natural remedy of a fair account fails precisely because it is fair, so the more even the arbiter is, the more both sides distrust it, and the settling move deepens the split instead.
