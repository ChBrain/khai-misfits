# Plan: See It Plainly

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the convinced's seat](position_convinced.md), inside [Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Convinced](position_convinced.md)

## Direction

Hold your reading of the situation as the plain facts, and explain any disagreement by the other's defect. Your view arrives as reality and not as a view, so treat it as the obvious truth a fair mind would share, and when a reasonable person disagrees, do not read it as another vantage but as ignorance or bias, since from where you sit it can be nothing else. Do not look for your own lens, because you cannot see it, and its invisibility is exactly what makes the reading feel plain.

## Orders

([The Convinced](persona_the_convinced.md) reads [the Plain Sight](piece_the_plain_sight.md) through [Construing](process_construing.md) at [the Dispute](place_the_dispute.md), and through [Attributing](process_attributing.md) files the other's disagreement as [the Bias Read](piece_the_bias_read.md).)

## Implementation

([The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md) is where the reading is held; [Construing](process_construing.md) delivers it as reality itself, so the bias-read follows as the only explanation for a reasonable person's disagreement, and the seat's own lens stays invisible to it throughout.)

## Targets

- [ ] the reading is held as the plain facts, not as a view
- [ ] a reasonable person is expected to share it, given the same facts
- [ ] the other's disagreement is filed as bias, not as a different vantage
- [ ] the seat's own lens stays invisible to it, so the charge is never turned inward
