# Plot: Trying It Their Way

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Other Chair](place_the_other_chair.md), where someone tries the one move that helps.

## Action

[The Bridge](persona_the_bridge.md), from [the bridge's seat](position_bridge.md), on [Take the Other Chair](plan_take_the_other_chair.md) through [Bridging](process_bridging.md) holds [The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): getting each to construe the situation as the other does and to grant a reasonable person could dissolves the bias-read, but the move is effortful and resisted, and lifts on its own where a fact is checkable or one side truly errs. The scene closes with the gap closable only by costly perspective-taking, the trap shown bounded (Ross and Ward).

## Stage

Not more facts but a taken perspective: the bridge does not referee the account, it moves each into the other's chair until the charge of bias softens into a difference of vantage, and it works only where the perspective is genuinely taken and not merely professed.

## Tension

The one move that closes the gap is the one each is surest it does not need, since each believes it already sees clearly, so the cure is real and dear at once, resisted precisely by the plainness that caused the trap.
