# Plot: The Same Scene, Two Sights

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md), where two parties take in the same situation at once.

## Action

Through [Construing](process_construing.md), [The Convinced](persona_the_convinced.md) and [The Opposed](persona_the_opposed.md) read the same events oppositely, each carrying [The Plain Sight](piece_the_plain_sight.md), certain its reading is simply what happened. The scene closes with two honest, incompatible accounts, each taken by its holder for the plain truth (Ross and Ward).

## Stage

The lens does its work unnoticed: each reads through interest, history, and expectation, and the reading arrives feeling like perception and not interpretation, so neither experiences its account as a construal at all, only as the facts.

## Tension

Both are in good faith and both are sure, so the disagreement is real and each already holds the seed of the trap: a reading that feels like reality, and no way, from inside, to see it as a reading.
