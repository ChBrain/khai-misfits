# Place: The Dispute

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). Where the two sides face off over the same situation and read it oppositely. Hosts [The Same Scene, Two Sights](plot_the_same_scene_two_sights.md) and [You Must Be Biased](plot_you_must_be_biased.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Two people and one situation, and two accounts of it that do not match: the meeting each remembers differently, the argument each is sure the other started.

## Holds

[The Construal](piece_the_construal.md) at work in each seat, [The Plain Sight](piece_the_plain_sight.md) each carries, and [The Bias Read](piece_the_bias_read.md) forming on both sides.

## Offers

To each party, the felt authority of its own clear reading: the sense of simply reporting what is there.

## Withheld

Any sight, from inside a seat, of one's own lens as a lens, so that each can see the other's slant and never its own.
