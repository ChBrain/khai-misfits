# Process: Construing

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). Borne at [The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md), precipitated by [The Convinced](persona_the_convinced.md) and [The Opposed](persona_the_opposed.md) reading the same situation, in [The Same Scene, Two Sights](plot_the_same_scene_two_sights.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

Two parties taking in the same events, evidence, and words at once.

## Direction

From a shared situation toward two divergent readings, each arrived at through a lens the reader does not notice using.

## Lever

That the lens of interest, history, and expectation works unnoticed, so its reading feels like perception and not interpretation (Ross and Ward).

## Echo

[The Plain Sight](piece_the_plain_sight.md), each reading felt by its holder as the unmediated facts.
