# Piece: The Widening

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). The escalation by which contact and evidence deepen the disagreement rather than close it, the phenomenon whole.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Spreading across [The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md) and [The Record](place_the_record.md), through every exchange: each new fact construed the same way, each show of persistence read as fresh proof of the bias inferred, each neutral account called slanted, so the distance grows with the talking (Vallone, Ross, and Lepper; Robinson and colleagues).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and there is no misfit, only two people who disagree and let it lie: it is the widening that makes the structure a trap, the gap that grows exactly where good faith should have closed it.
It carries the phenomenon whole. Because each construes the contact and reads the other's persistence as bias, the natural remedy of more engagement adds evidence, to each, that the other is as biased as suspected, so the two end further apart for having tried, a discord deepened by the reasonableness of both.

## Apparent

The other digging in: from each seat it looks like the other growing more stubborn and more biased the more they talk, proof that reason cannot reach them.
