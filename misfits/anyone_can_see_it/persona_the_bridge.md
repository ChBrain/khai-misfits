# Persona: The Bridge

## Taxonomy

[Bridge](position_bridge.md). The one who closes the gap by perspective-taking, getting each to construe as the other does and grant a reasonable person could.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Patient, unhurried, uninterested in who is right.
Does not add facts or referee the account, but asks each side to say the other's view in its strongest form and to grant that a reasonable person could hold it.
No neutrality to be distrusted: it is not judging the dispute, only moving each into the other's chair, and it knows that is the one thing that helps.

## Action

Works on [Take the Other Chair](plan_take_the_other_chair.md) through [Bridging](process_bridging.md) at [the Other Chair](place_the_other_chair.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md): it brings each to construe the situation as the other does, until the bias-read softens into a difference of vantage.
Cannot make the move cheap, because perspective-taking is effortful and resisted by parties each sure it is the other who needs correcting, so it spends real effort to buy what facts could not.
Does what actually closes the gap: not insisting on the plain facts, since the plainness is the illusion, but getting each genuinely into the other's eyes.

## Shadow

Not naive and not a miracle-worker: it is right that only perspective-taking dissolves the charge, and it is also unable to make that cheap or certain.
Because each believes it already sees clearly, the one move that would help is the one each is surest it does not need, so the bridge proves, against the wish for an easy fix, how dearly a construal disagreement is closed.
It is the one who knows what works, and the one who shows how much it costs.

## Tell

Asks each to state the other's case until the other says yes, that is what I mean, and only then speaks of agreement.
Cannot shortcut the work, because a perspective told is not a perspective taken, so it waits out the resistance rather than arguing through it, the one who closes the gap by refusing to add another fact to it.
