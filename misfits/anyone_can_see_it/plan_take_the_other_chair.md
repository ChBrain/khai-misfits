# Plan: Take the Other Chair

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the bridge's seat](position_bridge.md), inside [Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Bridge](position_bridge.md)

## Direction

Do not add facts, since facts are construed too; instead get each party to construe the situation as the other does and to grant that a reasonable person could land there. Have each state the other's view in its strongest form until the other agrees that is what it means, and only then speak of agreement, because a perspective told is not a perspective taken. Expect resistance, since each is sure it already sees plainly and that it is the other who must move, and spend the effort anyway, for it is the one thing that closes the gap.

## Orders

([The Bridge](persona_the_bridge.md) runs [Bridging](process_bridging.md) at [the Other Chair](place_the_other_chair.md), holding [the Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md) between where perspective-taking is genuinely done and where it is only professed.)

## Implementation

([The Other Chair](place_the_other_chair.md) is where the move is made; [Bridging](process_bridging.md) brings each into the other's construal until the bias-read softens into a difference of vantage, effortful and resisted, and holding only where the perspective is actually taken and not merely named.)

## Targets

- [ ] each is brought to construe the situation as the other does
- [ ] each grants that a reasonable person could hold the other's view
- [ ] the effort is real and resisted, since each believes it already sees clearly
- [ ] where a fact is plainly checkable or one side truly errs, the trap was never this one
