# Plot: The Wider It Gets

## Owner

## Cue

[The Record](place_the_record.md), where the two keep at it and it worsens.

## Action

Through [Hardening](process_hardening.md), [The Widening](piece_the_widening.md): each construes the new exchanges through the same lens and reads the other's persistence as fresh proof of the bias already inferred, so contact and evidence widen the gap rather than close it. The scene closes with two reasonable people further apart for having engaged, the escalation authored by no one (Robinson, Keltner, Ward, and Ross).

## Stage

The remedy is the poison here: more talk, offered as the way two fair minds converge, adds to each the very evidence that the other is as biased as suspected, so engagement confirms the bias-read instead of correcting it.

## Tension

The harder they try to settle it with facts, the wider it gets, because every fact is construed and every show of conviction is read as stubbornness, so good faith on both sides deepens the discord it meant to end.
