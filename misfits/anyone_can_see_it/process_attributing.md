# Process: Attributing

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). Borne at [The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md), precipitated by each party accounting for the other's disagreement, in [You Must Be Biased](plot_you_must_be_biased.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A reasonable person disagreeing with what one takes to be the plain facts.

## Direction

From a difference of vantage toward a charge against the other: ignorance, muddle, or bias, anything but another truth.

## Lever

That from a seat which takes its own view as objective, a reasonable person's disagreement can only be distortion, so the read is a sound conclusion from a false premise (Ross and Ward).

## Echo

[The Bias Read](piece_the_bias_read.md), the disagreement misfiled as a defect in the other.
