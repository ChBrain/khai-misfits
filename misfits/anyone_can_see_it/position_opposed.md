# Position: Opposed

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). The mirror seat of the party that reads the situation the other way, equally sure it sees plainly, and equally sure the first is the biased one.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

Its own clear reading of the situation, felt as reality and not as a view.
A ready explanation for the other's stance: consider the source, the interest, the angle that must be warping it.
No sight of its own lens either, so its certainty is the exact twin of the certainty across the table.

## Orders

Consider the source, and hold your own reading as plain.
Read the other's view as what an interested or biased party would say, and hold your own as the simple facts, since from here it is.
Accept what the seat does: that it is doing precisely what the other is doing, in equal good faith, and that the symmetry is invisible from inside it.

## Loses

The chance to see the quarrel as symmetric.
Because it cannot catch its own construal, it cannot notice that its bias-read is the mirror of the one aimed at it, so what it loses is the recognition that both are honest vantages, and it loses it by the same plain sight the other has.
It keeps its certainty and loses the symmetry, sure it is the fair one exactly as its opposite is sure of the same.

## Drives

Toward the equally fair-minded party equally sure the other is biased,
considering the source and, by considering it, missing that its own source is just as readable, and hardening in turn,
so the two seats drive apart together, each the other's reflection and neither able to see the glass.
