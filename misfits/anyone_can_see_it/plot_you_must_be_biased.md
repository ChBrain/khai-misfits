# Plot: You Must Be Biased

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md), where each party accounts for the other's disagreement.

## Action

Through [Attributing](process_attributing.md), [The Convinced](persona_the_convinced.md) on [See It Plainly](plan_see_it_plainly.md) and [The Opposed](persona_the_opposed.md) on [Consider the Source](plan_consider_the_source.md) reach [The Bias Read](piece_the_bias_read.md), each filing the other's honest vantage as ignorance or bias, and [The Blind Spot](piece_the_blind_spot.md) keeps the charge from turning inward. The scene closes with the disagreement pinned on the other's defect, both sure they are the fair one (Pronin, Lin, and Ross).

## Stage

The bias-read is an honest inference, not a slur: from a seat that takes its own view as objective, a reasonable person's disagreement can only be a distortion, so each reaches the sound conclusion of a false premise and aims it the wrong way.

## Tension

Each detects the slant in the other and none in itself, so the charge runs one way from both seats at once, and the very symmetry that should reveal the trap is the thing neither can see.
