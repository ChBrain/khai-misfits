# Piece: The Boundary

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). The condition the effect needs and the edges where it lifts, the hedge that keeps the trap real but bounded.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Weighed at [The Other Chair](place_the_other_chair.md), where the bridge works to dissolve the bias-read: the conditions under which the gap closes and those under which it was never this trap at all (Ross and Ward).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the misfit overreaches into a claim that no one is ever biased and no disagreement is ever the other's error, which is false: the boundary is what keeps the trap honest.
It carries the condition and the escape. The effect needs a disagreement over construal and low insight into one's own; give it a plainly checkable fact, a genuine act of perspective-taking that grants the other a reasonable vantage, or a disagreement that really does rest on one side's error, and the bias-read dissolves or was never warranted, so the trap is bounded and not every disagreement is symmetric construal.

## Apparent

A set of let-offs where the quarrel resolves, easy to read as softening the result rather than as the line that says when the bias-read is a misfiling and when it is simply right.
