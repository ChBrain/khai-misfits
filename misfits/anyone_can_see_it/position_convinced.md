# Position: Convinced

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). The seat of a party that reads the situation one way, takes its reading for the plain facts, and can explain the other's disagreement only as a defect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A clear reading of the situation, felt not as a view but as reality itself.
The confidence of a reasonable person: any fair mind, given the same facts, would surely land here.
No sight of its own construal as construal, so no way to grant the other a vantage without surrendering the plainness it is sure of.

## Orders

See it plainly, and account for disagreement by the other's bias.
Hold your reading as the facts, since that is how it arrives, and explain the one who disagrees as uninformed or slanted, since from here they can be nothing else.
Accept what the seat does: that its reasonableness is exactly what convicts the other, and that the charge feels like discernment and not like a misfiling.

## Loses

The chance of reading the disagreement rightly.
Because it cannot see its own lens, it cannot file the difference as vantage rather than defect, so what it loses is the true account of why the other disagrees, and it loses it precisely by being sure it sees clearly.
It keeps its plain sight and loses the other's reasons, the fairness it feels the very thing that blinds it.

## Drives

Toward the fair-minded party whose fairness indicts the other,
seeing plainly and, by seeing plainly, reading the other as biased, and hardening as the other persists,
so the seat surest it is the fair one in the room is the seat least able to grant that the other might be too.
