# Pitch: Just Look at It

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Clear-eyed and reasonable.
A person simply seeing what is there and saying the obvious, sure that any fair mind given the same facts would land where they have.
Nothing false in it: most of the time we do just see what is there, and asking someone to look plainly is a fair request.

## Undertow

Under the plain seeing, a lens that does its work unnoticed.
The reading is a construal and not the facts, and the other party is equally clear-eyed and equally sure, so each reads the other's honest disagreement not as a different vantage but as bias, and each detects the bias in the other and never in itself (Ross and Ward; Pronin, Lin, and Ross).
No liar and no fool: both are in good faith, and the very reasonableness of each is exactly what convicts the other.

## Nerve

The plainness of just looking, and the catch that two people looking plainly see opposite things:
that the seeing is a vantage taken for the view, so the more sure each is that it sees clearly, the surer it is that the other is the biased one, the fairness felt and the bias read the one construal seen twice,
and so more contact and more facts widen the gap, each exchange fresh proof, to each, that the other is just as biased as suspected.

## Echo

After the run: two reasonable people further apart for having talked, a neutral account both benches called slanted, a disagreement of vantage misfiled as the other's stubbornness, a loss authored by no one (Vallone, Ross, and Lepper; Robinson, Keltner, Ward, and Ross).
And the remedy, at a cost and not certain: perspective-taking, being brought to construe the thing as the other does and to grant that a reasonable person could, effortful and resisted because each is sure it is the other who needs correcting (Ross and Ward).
And the bound held in view, because the effect is conditional and not a law: it needs a construal disagreement and low self-insight, and lifts under a checkable fact, a real act of perspective-taking, or a disagreement that truly is the other side's error, so the whole of it is that anyone can see it is a vantage mistaken for the view.
