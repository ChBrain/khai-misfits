# Place: The Other Chair

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). Where perspective-taking happens and the bias-read is dissolved, at a cost. Hosts [Trying It Their Way](plot_trying_it_their_way.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Each party asked to construe the situation as the other does, to sit in the other's seat and grant that a reasonable person could see it from there.

## Holds

[The Boundary](piece_the_boundary.md), the conditions under which the gap closes and those under which it was never this trap at all.

## Offers

The one move that dissolves the bias-read: not another fact, but a genuine seeing through the other's eyes.

## Withheld

A costless fix, since sitting in the other chair is effortful and resisted by each party sure it already sees clearly and that the other is the one who needs to move.
