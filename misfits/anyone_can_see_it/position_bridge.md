# Position: Bridge

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). The seat of the one who closes the gap by perspective-taking, getting each to construe as the other does and grant a reasonable person could.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one move that works: not more facts, but a genuine seeing of the situation through the other's eyes.
The willingness to trade insistence on the plain facts for the effort of granting the other a vantage.
No cheap way to do it, because perspective-taking is effortful and is resisted by parties each sure it is the other who needs correcting.

## Orders

Get each to sit in the other's chair, and to grant that a reasonable person could land there.
Do not add facts, since facts are construed too; instead bring each to construe as the other does, until the bias-read dissolves into a difference of vantage.
Accept what the seat costs: that the work is real and resisted, and that it succeeds only where each is actually brought to take the perspective, not merely told to.

## Loses

The ease of the fix, always.
Because each believes it already sees clearly, the move that would help is the one each is surest it does not need, so the bridge spends real and resisted effort to buy what a neutral account could not, and there is no shortcut through.
It keeps the one thing that works and loses the hope it is cheap, the cure real and dear at once.

## Drives

Toward the reconciler who spends what facts and neutrality cannot,
trading insistence for perspective and, by getting each into the other's chair, dissolving the charge of bias into a difference of view,
so the seat that closes the gap is the seat that proves it never closed on facts alone, the bridge built only of the effort each was surest it could skip.
