# Persona: The Convinced

## Taxonomy

[Convinced](position_convinced.md). The one who reads the situation one way, takes its reading for the plain facts, and can explain the other's disagreement only as a defect.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Reasonable, level, just telling it like it is.
Sees the situation clearly and states the obvious, sure that any fair mind given the same facts would agree, and puzzled that anyone does not.
No heat in it at first, only the calm of a person reporting what is plainly there, with nothing to defend because it is just the truth.

## Action

Reads on [See It Plainly](plan_see_it_plainly.md), carrying [the Plain Sight](piece_the_plain_sight.md) that [Construing](process_construing.md) delivers at [the Dispute](place_the_dispute.md), and through [Attributing](process_attributing.md) reaches [the Bias Read](piece_the_bias_read.md), filing the other's disagreement as ignorance or slant.
Cannot see its own construal as construal, so it cannot grant the other a vantage without giving up the plainness it is sure of, and does not.
Does what a reasonable person does with a view that feels like reality: it holds it as the facts, and explains the one who disagrees as failing to see them.

## Shadow

Not a bully and not a fool: it really does see its reading as the plain facts, since a lens is invisible from inside, and it really does infer the other's bias, since from its seat a reasonable person's disagreement can only look like distortion.
Because it cannot catch its own lens, its fairness is the very thing that convicts the other, and it is doing, in perfect good faith, precisely what the party across the table is doing to it.
It is the one surest it is fair, and the one least able to grant the other might be too.

## Tell

Says anyone can see it, and means it, and cannot say why the other does not except that they are biased.
Cannot suspect itself, because its reading arrives as reality and not as a view, so it levels the charge of bias with a clear conscience and never turns it inward, sure the stubbornness is all on the other side.
