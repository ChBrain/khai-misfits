# Piece: The Blind Spot

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). The asymmetry that keeps the bias-read one-directional, the bias readily seen in the other and denied in the self.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Sitting in each seat wherever the reading is done, at [The Dispute](place_the_dispute.md) and [The Record](place_the_record.md): the ease of spotting the slant in the other and the impossibility of spotting one's own, since a construal cannot be caught from inside as a construal (Pronin, Lin, and Ross).

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the bias-read would rebound: if each could see its own slant as readily as the other's, the charge would soften into a shared difference of vantage.
It bears the load by running one way. Because each party detects bias in the other and denies it in itself, no seat turns the read inward, so both are sure they are the fair one in the room, and the misfiling is never corrected by the one who could correct it.

## Apparent

Simple perceptiveness: the clear sight of how the other's interests are warping their judgement, with no matching sight of one's own.

## Yearbook

Held in every seat, laid bare in [The Neutral Read as Biased](plot_the_neutral_read_as_biased.md) when both call the same fair account slanted.
It is what keeps [The Bias Read](piece_the_bias_read.md) aimed outward and what lets [The Widening](piece_the_widening.md) run, the reason neither party ever suspects the lens is its own.
