# Position: Arbiter

## Taxonomy

[Anyone Can See It](play_anyone_can_see_it.md). The seat of the neutral third who lays out a fair account to settle the dispute, and is read as biased by both sides.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A balanced account, honestly even-handed, and the standing of a neutral.
The reasonable hope that a fair summary of the facts will bring two reasonable people together.
No way to be seen as fair by either, because from each seat a fair account gives the other side too much.

## Orders

Lay out the facts evenly, and expect the evenness to settle it.
Offer a balanced account, name what each side gets right, and trust that neutrality will be read as neutrality.
Accept what the seat discovers: that both benches boo, that its fairness is taken for a thumb on the scale, and that the neutral is distrusted precisely for being neutral.

## Loses

Its credibility with both, by the act of being fair to both.
Because each side reads an even account as slanted against it, the arbiter's neutrality earns not trust but suspicion from every seat, so what it loses is the standing to settle anything, lost by doing its job well.
It keeps its even hand and loses the room, the fairness that should have closed the gap read as bias by all of it.

## Drives

Toward the honest broker read as partisan by everyone,
laying out the facts and watching both sides find them slanted, and by being distrusted from both benches showing the bias is in the eye,
so the seat that took no side is the seat both sides take for the enemy, the neutrality that proves the misfit rather than curing it.
