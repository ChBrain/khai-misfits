# Persona: The Arbiter

## Taxonomy

[Arbiter](position_arbiter.md). The neutral third who lays out a fair account to settle the dispute, and is read as biased by both sides.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Even-handed, fair, above the fray.
Comes in with a balanced account, credits each side with what it gets right, and expects the evenness to be recognised and to settle things.
No stake in it: only the reasonable confidence of a neutral that a fair statement of the facts will bring two reasonable people together.

## Action

Offers a balanced account on [Lay Out the Facts](plan_lay_out_the_facts.md) at [the Record](place_the_record.md), and watches both sides read it as slanted against them, since from each seat a fair account gives the other too much.
Cannot be seen as fair by either, because the evenness that is its neutrality is, to each partisan, a thumb on the scale for the other.
Does what an honest broker does: it states the facts evenly, and is distrusted from both benches for exactly that.

## Shadow

Not slanted, and not able to be seen as unslanted: its account is genuinely even, and its evenness is precisely what earns it the charge of bias from all sides.
Because each partisan reads a fair account as unfair to it, the arbiter's neutrality does not close the gap but deepens the distrust, so the honest broker becomes, to both, the enemy.
It is the one who took no side, and the one both sides take for the other's ally.

## Tell

Protests that it is being fair to both, and is booed by both for saying so.
Cannot win the room, because the fairness is read as bias, so its very neutrality is offered as proof of a slant, the even hand taken by each for a hand against it.
