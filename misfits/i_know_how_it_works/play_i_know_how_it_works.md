# Play: I Know How It Works

## Estate

[khai-misfits](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-misfits): the house that holds this production and answers for the whole run.

## Name

I Know How It Works.
Ask a person whether they understand how a bicycle works, or a zipper, or a flush toilet, or the policy they argue for every week, and they will say yes, and mean it: they feel the understanding, detailed and coherent and solid, a sense that if pressed they could lay the whole mechanism out. And on most things they are not pressed. Life asks them for their view, their reasons, their side, and they have all of those fluently, so the felt understanding is never tested against the thing it claims to be, and it holds, comfortably, for years. The feeling of understanding how something works is one of the most convincing feelings the mind produces, and it is produced whether or not the understanding is there.
Because it is very often not there, or not nearly to the depth it feels. The felt grasp is far larger than the actual grasp, and this gap is widest exactly for explanatory knowledge, for how a thing works, wider there than for plain facts, or memorized procedures, or stories, because with a mechanism it is easy to mistake knowing what it does for knowing how it does it. A person can picture a bicycle perfectly, name every part, use one daily, and still be unable to draw a working one from memory, and be genuinely surprised, when they try, that the chain and the frame and the pedals will not come out right under their own hand. The knowing-what stood in, all along, for a knowing-how that was never actually assembled.
And here is the turn that makes it a trap and not just a quirk. The felt understanding survives because of what conversation asks for. When people argue, when they hold and defend a position, they are asked for reasons: why do you support this, what is wrong with the other side, and reasons are cheap and plentiful and do nothing to test the mechanism, so giving them, again and again, leaves the illusion perfectly intact and can even harden the position, each round of justification feeling like more understanding. The one thing that would test it, being asked to trace the actual mechanism, step by step, how the policy would move from enacted to its promised effect, how the machine gets from the crank to the wheel, is almost never asked, because it is awkward and slow and not how argument is done. So the felt grasp is never put to the one question it cannot answer.
And when it is, it goes. Sit a confident person down and ask them, gently, to walk through the mechanism in full, and somewhere partway the fluency stops, the next step will not come, and they discover in real time that the understanding they were certain of a minute ago is not there, the middle of it simply blank. The felt grasp collapses, the self-assessment drops, they rate their own understanding far lower than they did before the tracing, and often the strong position, no longer resting on an understanding that felt total, eases a little too, though that easing is the more fragile part of the finding and does not always come. What is reliable is the collapse of the certainty when the mechanism is finally asked for.
And none of it is folly or dishonesty. The felt understanding is sincere, not a bluff, and it visits the careful and the expert as readily as anyone, in any domain where they know what a thing does without having assembled how. The confident position is held in good faith on a grasp that genuinely felt complete. And the discourse that results, a public square of firm convictions on mechanisms almost no one could trace, polarizing as each side trades reasons that never test the understanding underneath, is authored by no one and full of no liars: only a great many people, each sincerely feeling they understand how it works, arguing from a depth that is not there and that nothing in the arguing will ever ask them to show.

## Arc

The bend is this: the sense of understanding a mechanism runs far deeper than the mechanism actually held, an illusion specific to explanatory knowledge that survives because tracing the mechanism is almost never asked, so confident and even extreme positions rest on an understanding that is not there and that only a demand to explain, not to justify, exposes.
It runs on a felt grasp larger than the actual.
People feel they understand how things work in far more detail than they do, and this illusion of explanatory depth is far stronger for explanatory, how-it-works knowledge than for facts or procedures or stories, because a grasp of what a thing does is easily mistaken for a grasp of how it does it (Rozenblit and Keil). The feeling of understanding is vivid and convincing and produced whether or not the understanding is there.
And the illusion survives on what is asked. Ordinary argument asks for reasons and a side, which are cheap and never test the mechanism, so the felt grasp is confirmed rather than probed, round after round, and the position can even harden as each justification feels like more understanding (Fernbach, Rogers, Fox, and Sloman). The one question that would test it, a demand to trace the mechanism step by step, is awkward and slow and almost never made.
And when it is made, the grasp collapses. Asked to generate the actual causal account, not the reasons, the person meets the mechanism they cannot produce, the fluency stops partway, and the felt understanding falls, the self-rating dropping sharply from where it stood before the tracing (Rozenblit and Keil). And a demand for mechanism, unlike a demand for reasons, can soften the position as it falls, though that moderation is the more contested part of the finding and the reliable core is the collapse of the certainty itself (Fernbach and colleagues).
And the sum is a discourse on unexamined conviction. Because the mechanism is almost never asked and the reasons always are, a whole public can hold firm and opposed views on how things work that almost none of them could trace, and polarize by trading justifications that never touch the understanding beneath, each side sincere and no one lying (Fernbach and colleagues).
None of it is folly or a lie. The felt understanding is sincere and universal, visiting the careful and the expert, and the confident position is held in good faith: the unexamined conviction emerges from the structure, a felt grasp that runs ahead of the actual and a discourse that asks for reasons and never for the mechanism, so no one is foolish and no one deceives.
Ask for the mechanism, of yourself before you are certain and of others before you dismiss them, trace how it actually works step by step, and the illusion can be punctured and the certainty right-sized: but asking for the mechanism is uncomfortable, slow, and the opposite of how argument is done, it is rarely offered and less often invited, and the reasons that everyday discourse runs on will never do it, so the puncture that would right-size the conviction is exactly the move the discourse is built not to make.

## Stakes

Whether a conviction can be right-sized to the understanding beneath it, when the feeling of understanding runs far ahead of the mechanism and only a question no one asks would reveal the gap.
Every plot but the last moves the same way: toward the confident position held on a felt grasp, and away from the mechanism that would test it, because argument asks for reasons and a side, which the felt grasp supplies fluently, and almost never for the step-by-step how, which it cannot. The production cannot make the reasons expose the illusion, because reasons are exactly what the felt understanding produces without effort and defending them only hardens it: the one solvent is a demand to trace the mechanism, and that demand is awkward, slow, and rarely made. It can only show that no one is a fool and no one lies, that the felt understanding is sincere and visits the careful and the expert alike, and that the one move which would right-size the conviction, asking for the mechanism rather than the reasons, of oneself before certainty and of others before dismissal, is precisely the move a discourse built to trade justifications is built not to make, so the convictions stand at a depth that is not there, the collapse always one unasked question away.
