# Pitch: Walk Me Through It

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The default key the whole production is read in.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Tenor

Confident, fluent, sincere.
A person who feels they understand how the thing works, the device, the process, the policy, and holds a firm view on it, ready with reasons and a side.
Nothing false in it: the felt understanding is genuine, not a bluff, and the position is held in good faith on a grasp that feels complete.

## Undertow

Under the confidence, the mechanism they cannot actually produce.
The felt grasp runs far deeper than the grasp behind it, widest exactly for how-it-works knowledge, and it survives because argument asks for reasons and a side, which come easily and never test the mechanism, and almost never for the step-by-step how, which the grasp was never assembled to answer.
Knowing what a thing does has stood in, all along, for knowing how it does it.

## Nerve

The feeling of being certain you understand, and being asked, gently, to walk through it step by step,
and finding partway that the next step will not come, that the middle of the understanding is simply not there,
so the certainty of a minute ago drops away in real time, and you rate your own grasp far below where you were sure it stood.

## Echo

After the run: a public square of firm, opposed convictions on mechanisms almost no one could trace,
polarizing as each side trades reasons that never touch the understanding beneath, and no one lying, every conviction sincere,
because the one question that would right-size it, walk me through how it actually works, is awkward and slow and the move the discourse is built not to make.
