# Process: Questioning

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). Run by [The Questioner](persona_the_questioner.md) on [Ask for the Mechanism](plan_ask_for_the_mechanism.md), at [The Whiteboard](place_the_whiteboard.md), in [Show Me](plot_show_me.md) and [The Blank](plot_the_blank.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A firm position, stated once too fluently, and a Questioner willing to ask the awkward thing: not why, but how, step by step.
Not a challenge to the side taken, a request to see the mechanism the side is supposed to rest on.

## Direction

The demand to trace the mechanism, not to give reasons: walk through how it actually works, from here to there, with nothing skipped.
Carries [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md) onto [the Whiteboard](place_the_whiteboard.md), and the attempt to meet it produces [the Collapse](piece_the_collapse.md), the fluency stopping partway and the middle of the understanding turning out not to be there.

## Lever

A demand for the mechanistic explanation, not for reasons, is what exposes the gap and drops the self-rating of understanding; reasons, however many, never do (Rozenblit and Keil; Fernbach, Rogers, Fox, and Sloman). HEDGE: the reliable effect is the collapse of the certainty itself; a further softening of the position that sat on it is Fernbach's more contested finding and is not guaranteed to follow.

## Echo

The felt grasp gone, the self-assessment dropped sharply, the certainty right-sized to what the mechanism could actually show.
And the move itself rarely made: this is the one process in the whole discourse that punctures the illusion, and it runs only when someone, awkwardly, asks for the how instead of the why.
