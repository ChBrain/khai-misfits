# Place: The Exchange

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The ordinary setting where a view is asserted and reasons are traded, one on one. Hosts [The Strong View](plot_the_strong_view.md) and [Trading Reasons](plot_trading_reasons.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Fluent argument, confident positions, reasons and a side.
Two people, or one and an audience, each stating a view on how the thing works and defending it, ably, with the reasons ready to hand.

## Holds

[The Position](piece_the_position.md), held on [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md), and the reasons traded to defend it.
Here the conviction is real and the case for it well made, round after round, and every round that is answered with a reason is a round the felt grasp is not asked to do anything harder than it already does.

## Offers

A full hearing for a view, and every reason it needs to feel confirmed.
The Exchange gives a position everything argument usually gives: a side to take, an opponent to meet, and reasons enough to hold the line, so leaving it undefeated feels, to anyone in it, like having been right all along.

## Withheld

Any demand for the mechanism.
The Exchange asks only for reasons and a side, never for the step-by-step how, so the felt grasp is confirmed here and never once tested, the position it anchors mistaking an unbroken record of reasons for an examined understanding.
