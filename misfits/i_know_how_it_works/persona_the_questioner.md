# Persona: The Questioner

## Taxonomy

[Questioner](position_questioner.md). The one who asks not for reasons but for the mechanism, walk me through step by step how it actually works, the rare and awkward request that punctures the illusion, the one who tries the fix that few will make.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Curious. Patient. Genuinely asking.
Is the one who does not counter the position but requests the how, who wants the causal account traced out in full rather than the reasons rehearsed, and asks it plainly and without hostility.
No trap in the asking: the Questioner is not scoring a point but making the one request that would test the understanding, the request ordinary argument almost never contains.

## Action

Asks for the mechanism, on [Ask for the Mechanism](plan_ask_for_the_mechanism.md), through [Questioning](process_questioning.md) at [the Whiteboard](place_the_whiteboard.md), laying down [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md), the demand to walk the how step by step.
Does not ask for reasons or a side, because those never test [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md), and asks instead for the one thing that meets [the Shallows](piece_the_shallows.md) and brings [the Collapse](piece_the_collapse.md).
Does what almost no one in an argument does: requests the mechanism rather than the justification, the slow, awkward question that the felt grasp cannot answer.

## Shadow

Not a hero who fixes the discourse, a person making a move that is rarely welcome and rarely made, and that is the difficulty: the puncture works and is almost never offered or invited.
Because asking for the mechanism is slow, uncomfortable, and the opposite of how argument runs, the Questioner's one effective move is exactly the one a discourse of reasons is built not to make, so the fix exists and sits unused, effective on the rare occasion it is tried and absent almost everywhere else.
It asks the one question that would right-size the conviction, and asking the one question that would right-size the conviction is the thing no one is inclined to do.

## Tell

Says "I hear the reasons, but walk me through how it actually works, step by step," and means it without malice.
Is met, often, with discomfort rather than gratitude, because the request is not how the game is played, and finds that the move which alone punctures the illusion is the move the room was least expecting and least wanting to make.
