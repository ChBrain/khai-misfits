# Persona: The Knower

## Taxonomy

[Knower](position_knower.md). The person who feels they understand how the thing works and holds a firm view on it, sincerely and in good faith, whose felt grasp runs far ahead of the mechanism they can actually produce, the self the trap runs through.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Confident. Fluent. Certain they understand.
Is the ordinary person asked their view on the device or the process or the policy, who feels the understanding as detailed and solid and ready to hand, and who could, they are sure, lay out the whole of how it works if pressed.
No bluff in it: the felt understanding is genuine, sincerely held, and it visits them as it visits the careful and the expert, in any domain where they know what a thing does without having assembled how.

## Action

Holds the view, on [Hold the View](plan_hold_the_view.md), through [Grasping](process_grasping.md) at [the Exchange](place_the_exchange.md), carrying [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) and the confident [Position](piece_the_position.md) it anchors.
Never traces the mechanism, because argument asks for reasons and a side, which come easily, and almost never for the step-by-step how, which would meet [the Shallows](piece_the_shallows.md) beneath the grasp.
Does what a sincere, articulate person does with a view they feel they understand: holds it firmly, defends it fluently, and is never once asked the one question that would test it.

## Shadow

Not a fraud pretending to understand, a person who genuinely feels they do, and that is the difficulty: the felt grasp is convincing precisely because the mind produces the feeling of understanding whether or not the understanding is there.
Because they know what the thing does and have mistaken that for knowing how it does it, the Knower holds a real conviction on an assembly they never actually built, so the confidence is honest and the grasp behind it is thin, and nothing in their ordinary life will ever put the two side by side.
It feels certain in good faith, and feeling certain in good faith is exactly what the illusion is.

## Tell

Says "of course I understand how it works," and believes it, and is not lying.
Cannot see, until asked to trace it, that knowing what a thing does has stood in all along for knowing how, that the fluency is in the reasons and not the mechanism, and that the certainty of a minute ago is one unasked question from being gone.
