# Process: Polarizing

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). Run by [The Public](persona_the_public.md) on [Argue from Conviction](plan_argue_from_conviction.md), at [The Forum](place_the_forum.md), in [No One Lying](plot_no_one_lying.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A question the whole square has an opinion on, and enough Knowers and Opponents in it to fill every seat.
Not one exchange but many at once, each carrying its own firm position into a shared square that never convenes a Whiteboard.

## Direction

A whole square sorting into opposed, certain camps on mechanisms almost none of them have traced.
Carries [the Discourse](piece_the_discourse.md), the sum of every [Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) held and every [Position](piece_the_position.md) defended, none of it tested, all of it hardening as the sides sort further apart.

## Lever

Because the mechanism is asked of no one and reasons are asked of everyone, a public can polarize entirely on a felt understanding almost none of it actually holds, each side sincere and no one lying (Fernbach, Rogers, Fox, and Sloman).

## Echo

A firm collective conviction over a thin collective understanding, camps further apart than the sum of what either side could actually explain.
One unasked question away from the same collapse on every side: the Forum could in principle convene [the Whiteboard](place_the_whiteboard.md) for any of it, and does not, because that is not how a public square argues.
