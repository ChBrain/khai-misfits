# Position: Public

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The seat of the whole discourse, firm and opposed convictions on mechanisms almost none of its members could trace, sincere throughout and polarising as it trades reasons, the collective the misfit is drawn down through.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A great many members and a great many firm views: the standing of a public square, and in each member a felt grasp of how the thing works that is never traced.
A sincere case: every member argues in good faith, holding a real position and fluent reasons, so the discourse is composed of nothing but honest and often able people.
No mechanism asked of anyone, because argument runs on reasons and a side, so the seat sums firm convictions without ever testing the understanding beneath a single one.

## Orders

Argue from conviction.
Let each member hold their firm view and defend it with reasons across the divide, and sort the square into opposed and certain camps.
Ask no one to trace the mechanism, because that is not how public argument is done, so let the disagreement sharpen on justifications that never touch the how.

## Loses

The competence its confidence implies, and loses it with no member at fault.
Because each member holds a felt grasp they are never asked to trace and defends it with reasons that never test it, the seat sums a firm collective conviction over a thin collective understanding, so what it surrenders is any grounding for the certainty it argues with, lost not to folly anywhere in it but to a discourse that asks for reasons and never the mechanism.
It keeps the heat and the sincerity of the debate and loses the understanding the debate claims to rest on, hardened by exactly the arguing that felt like reaching the bottom of it.

## Drives

Toward a public square certain and polarised on a depth that is not there,
each side sincere, no one lying, and the mechanism asked of no one,
so the seat made of nothing but honest and able people argues from an understanding almost none of them hold, one unasked question from the same collapse on every side.
