# Piece: The Tracing

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The demand to explain the mechanism step by step, the causal account rather than the reasons: the one act that exposes the gap.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Laid down by [the Questioner](persona_the_questioner.md) through [Questioning](process_questioning.md), at [the Whiteboard](place_the_whiteboard.md), where the marker is picked up and the how is asked for plainly.

## Load Bearing

The tracing is the single instrument in the whole misfit that actually measures the felt grasp against the mechanism: sitting someone down and asking them to explain, in full and in order, how the thing works, is the task that punctures the illusion of explanatory depth and drops the self-rating sharply (Rozenblit and Keil), and it is a demand for mechanism specifically, not for reasons or a side, that does this work: a request for justification leaves a position untouched or even firmer, while a request to trace the causal account is what a study of political attitudes found actually undermines the felt understanding beneath it (Fernbach, Rogers, Fox, and Sloman). It is load bearing because it is the only vector in the misfit capable of collapsing another: without it, the felt grasp and the shallows beneath it never meet. Guard against reading the tracing as an argument, a rebuttal, or a test of the position itself. It asks nothing about whether the view is right, only how the mechanism gets from here to there, and that is precisely why reasons cannot substitute for it.

## Apparent

Just another question in the argument, one more thing to answer.
It sounds, on first hearing, like a variant of "why do you believe that," answerable the same way reasons are answered, and that resemblance is what lets it be asked so rarely: it looks like it belongs to the register of justification, when it is asking for something justification has never touched.

## Yearbook

Requested through [Ask for the Mechanism](plan_ask_for_the_mechanism.md), the tracing is what [Show Me](plot_show_me.md) puts on the table and what [the Whiteboard](place_the_whiteboard.md) withholds nothing else for: no side invited, no rebuttal welcome, only the how.
It meets [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) at [the Shallows](piece_the_shallows.md) and produces [the Collapse](piece_the_collapse.md), the one outcome [Trade Reasons](plan_trade_reasons.md) and [Argue from Conviction](plan_argue_from_conviction.md) are built never to reach.
