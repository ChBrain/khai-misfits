# Plot: The Blank

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Whiteboard](place_the_whiteboard.md), where the tracing begins.
The Knower starts to draw, or to speak it out loud in order, the marker or the sentence moving with the same fluency as at [the Exchange](place_the_exchange.md): this part first, then this.

## Action

[Grasping](process_grasping.md), pressed past where it has ever been pressed: [The Knower](persona_the_knower.md), still holding [the Knower's seat](position_knower.md), attempts [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md) the Questioner asked for, and somewhere partway [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) meets [the Shallows](piece_the_shallows.md) it was standing on all along.

## Stage

The opening steps come easily, because the start of a mechanism often looks exactly like knowing what the thing does, and for a moment it still resembles the confidence of a minute before. Then the next step will not come. Not a wrong answer, no answer: the fluency simply stops, mid-sentence or mid-line, and the Knower hears themselves not finishing a thought they were certain, a breath ago, they already had. [The Collapse](piece_the_collapse.md) follows directly on that silence.

## Tension

The felt grasp is gone and the self-rating drops with it, the Knower rating their own understanding far below where they rated it a minute earlier, and there is no fault to find in the gap: the grasp felt complete and the feeling was sincere the entire time, right up until the moment it had to produce what it claimed to hold. The strong view, perhaps, eases with it, no longer resting on an understanding that felt total. Carry that easing lightly: it is the further, more contested part of the finding, and what is reliable here is only the collapse of the certainty itself, not what, if anything, the Knower goes on to believe.
