# Persona: The Opponent

## Taxonomy

[Opponent](position_opponent.md). The one on the other side, who argues by trading reasons and counter-reasons rather than asking for the mechanism, and so entrenches both positions instead of testing either, a Knower in their own right.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Engaged. Well-argued. On the other side.
Is the person who disagrees and says why, marshalling reasons against the Knower's reasons, fluent in the case for their own view and the flaws in the other.
No bad faith in it: the Opponent argues sincerely and often ably, a Knower too, holding their own [Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) of a mechanism they also could not trace.

## Action

Trades reasons, on [Trade Reasons](plan_trade_reasons.md), through [Debating](process_debating.md) at [the Exchange](place_the_exchange.md), meeting the Knower's [Position](piece_the_position.md) with counter-reasons and never with [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md).
Asks for a side and a justification, which the felt grasp supplies without effort, and never for the step-by-step how, so the exchange leaves both illusions intact and both positions harder.
Does what a good debater does: argues the case, rebuts the other, and, by asking only for reasons, tests neither side's understanding of how the thing actually works.

## Shadow

Not a foil who exposes the Knower, a mirror who spares them, and that is the difficulty: opposition by reasons is exactly the pressure the felt grasp is built to withstand.
Because the Opponent demands justifications rather than the mechanism, each round of argument confirms rather than probes both sides, and the defending itself can harden the convictions, so the fiercest disagreement leaves the understanding beneath each position wholly untouched.
It pushes as hard as it can on the reasons, and pushing as hard as it can on the reasons is precisely what never reaches the how.

## Tell

Says "you are wrong, and here is why," at length and in earnest.
Does not think to say "walk me through how yours would actually work," because that is not how the argument is fought, and so leaves the encounter with a firmer view and an understanding as thin as the one across from it.
