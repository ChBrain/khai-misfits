# Piece: The Felt Grasp

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The subjective sense of understanding a mechanism: vivid, detailed-seeming, confident, produced by the mind whether or not the mechanism is actually held.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Carried by [the Knower](persona_the_knower.md) through [Grasping](process_grasping.md), at [the Exchange](place_the_exchange.md), where a view is asserted and the felt grasp behind it is never asked to do more than sound sure.

## Load Bearing

The felt grasp is the illusion itself, the thing the whole misfit is about: a person's rating of how well they understand a mechanism runs far ahead of what they can actually produce, and this gap is far wider for explanatory, how-it-works knowledge than for plain facts, memorized procedures, or stories (Rozenblit and Keil). It is load bearing because everything else in the production, the confident position, the reasons traded to defend it, the discourse built on both, stands on this one feeling and on nothing beneath it until something asks. Guard against reading it as a bluff or a performance: the feeling is sincere, produced by the mind the same way whether or not the mechanism is there, and that sincerity is exactly what lets it hold for years without being tested.

## Apparent

Real understanding. Solid, ready to hand, the kind a person could lay out on request.
It presents as knowledge already assembled, not knowledge yet to be checked, because the feeling of understanding a mechanism is one of the most convincing feelings the mind produces, convincing to the very person having it, first of all.

## Yearbook

Rests on nothing but itself until [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md) is laid down by [the Questioner](persona_the_questioner.md) through [Questioning](process_questioning.md), at which point it meets [the Shallows](piece_the_shallows.md) it was always standing on and falls into [the Collapse](piece_the_collapse.md).
It is what [the Position](piece_the_position.md) is anchored to, what [Hold the View](plan_hold_the_view.md) and [Trade Reasons](plan_trade_reasons.md) leave undisturbed, and what a whole [Public](persona_the_public.md) carries into [the Discourse](piece_the_discourse.md), each member's own, never summed against the mechanism any of them claims.
