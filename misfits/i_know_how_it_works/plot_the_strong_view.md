# Plot: The Strong View

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Exchange](place_the_exchange.md), where a view is asked for and a side is expected.
Someone wants to know what the Knower thinks about how the thing works, the device, the process, the policy, and the Knower has an answer ready before the question finishes.

## Action

[Grasping](process_grasping.md): [The Knower](persona_the_knower.md), on [Hold the View](plan_hold_the_view.md), takes up [the Knower's seat](position_knower.md) and states a firm [Position](piece_the_position.md), fluent and certain, anchored on [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) that feels, to the one holding it, complete.

## Stage

The Knower answers without hesitation, laying out the view in full sentences that arrive already finished, the reasons for it close behind. Nothing here asks for more than the view and its defense, so nothing here meets [the Shallows](piece_the_shallows.md) waiting under the confidence. The felt grasp does what it always does, unprompted: it supplies the sense of a mechanism understood, whole, ready to hand if anyone should ask for it. No one does.

## Tension

The position stands, strongly held on an understanding that has never once been asked to show itself, and so it feels complete because nothing has yet found the edge of it. The felt grasp behind the view is sincere, not a bluff: the Knower is not performing certainty, they are reporting it, honestly, from the inside of a feeling the mind produces whether or not the mechanism is there to back it.
