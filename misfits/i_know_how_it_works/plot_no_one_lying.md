# Plot: No One Lying

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Forum](place_the_forum.md), where the tracing is almost never asked.
A great many exchanges are running at once, each one its own Knower meeting its own Opponent, and nowhere in the square is there a whiteboard.

## Action

[Polarizing](process_polarizing.md): [The Public](persona_the_public.md), on [Argue from Conviction](plan_argue_from_conviction.md), holds [the Public's seat](position_public.md), each member carrying a firm [Position](piece_the_position.md) on [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) of a mechanism almost none of them could trace, trading reasons across the divide and never [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md), hardening into [the Discourse](piece_the_discourse.md).

## Stage

Camp answers camp with reasons, and the reasons are able, sincere, and endless, each round read from inside as getting closer to the bottom of it. At this scale the debate looks settled by sheer duration: a view argued this long, by this many people, must by now rest on something examined. It rests on what every single exchange inside it rested on, a felt grasp defended and never once probed, multiplied by every member and never summed against the mechanism any of them actually claims to hold.

## Tension

The discourse is seen, at last, for what it is: not folly and not lies, but a great many sincere people arguing from a felt understanding that is not there, each one as honest as the Knower ever was, none of them bluffing, none of them fools. Nothing in the arguing will ever ask them to show it, because the Forum, like the Exchange it is built from, asks only for reasons and a side. The one puncture, a demand for the mechanism rather than the case, is rarely made here, and rarer still received well: the fix exists, plainly, and sits almost entirely unused, and the discourse closes on that unresolved distance, not on a remedy.
