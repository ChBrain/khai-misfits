# Plot: Trading Reasons

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Exchange](place_the_exchange.md), where someone has come in arguing the other side.
The Knower's view meets a view opposed to it, and both are stated with the same fluency, the same readiness, the same sense of a case already made.

## Action

[Debating](process_debating.md): [The Opponent](persona_the_opponent.md), on [Trade Reasons](plan_trade_reasons.md), holds [the Opponent's seat](position_opponent.md) and meets the Knower's [Position](piece_the_position.md) with counter-reasons of their own, never with [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md); the Knower answers reason with reason in turn, and neither asks the other to walk through the mechanism.

## Stage

Round follows round: a claim, a rebuttal, a case for one side and a case against, each reason met with a sharper one. It reads, from either seat, like the argument getting somewhere, because every reason that lands feels like ground gained. But the Exchange only ever asks for a side and a justification, so no round reaches [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) underneath either position, let alone [the Shallows](piece_the_shallows.md) it is standing on. The Opponent is not a foil sent to expose the Knower; the Opponent is a Knower too, holding their own felt grasp of a mechanism they also have never traced, arguing just as sincerely from just as thin a floor.

## Tension

The reasons run out before either side does, and both positions leave the Exchange firmer than they entered it, the felt understanding on each side untouched by the whole encounter and, if anything, hardened by the defending: each round of justification felt like more understanding, and was not. The fiercest disagreement here has tested nothing, because nothing here was built to test the one thing that needed testing.
