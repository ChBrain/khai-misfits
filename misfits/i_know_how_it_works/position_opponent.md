# Position: Opponent

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The seat on the other side of the argument, which pushes by trading reasons and never by asking for the mechanism, so it entrenches both positions instead of testing either, a Knower in its own right.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A case of its own and the reasons to press it: the standing to disagree, and its own felt grasp of a mechanism it also could not trace.
A sincere case: the seat argues in good faith and often ably, holding a real position on how the thing works and marshalling reasons against the other side.
No inclination to ask for the how, because the argument is fought on reasons, so the seat brings the one kind of pressure the felt grasp is built to withstand.

## Orders

Trade reasons.
Meet the position across from you with counter-reasons, argue the case, rebut the other, and press your side as hard as the reasons allow.
Ask for a justification and never for the mechanism, because that is how the argument is fought, and so leave both understandings untested and both convictions firmer.

## Loses

The one pressure that would have reached the how, and loses it by pressing the wrong one hard.
Because the seat demands reasons rather than the mechanism, its fiercest opposition confirms both illusions instead of probing either, and the defending it provokes hardens the very positions it means to shake, so what it surrenders is any test of the understanding beneath the argument.
It wins and loses rounds of reasons and never once touches the mechanism, so the harder it argues the more firmly the illusion sits on both sides.

## Drives

Toward a disagreement that sharpens the convictions and spares the understanding,
reasons traded and hardened and the mechanism never asked,
so the seat that opposes as fiercely as it can is the seat that leaves the other's felt grasp, and its own, wholly intact.
