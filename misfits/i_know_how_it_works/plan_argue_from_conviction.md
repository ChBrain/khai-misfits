# Plan: Argue from Conviction

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the public's seat](position_public.md), inside [I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Public](position_public.md)

## Direction

Let each member hold their firm view and defend it with reasons across the divide, and sort the square into opposed and certain camps. Ask no one to trace the mechanism, because that is not how public argument is done, so let the disagreement sharpen on justifications that never touch the how, each side sincere and none the wiser.

## Orders

([The Public](persona_the_public.md) sums firm [Positions](piece_the_position.md) held on untraced [Felt Grasps](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) through [Polarizing](process_polarizing.md), hardening into [the Discourse](piece_the_discourse.md).)

## Implementation

([The Forum](place_the_forum.md) is where the square argues; the [polarizing](process_polarizing.md) process sorts sincere convictions into opposed camps; the mechanism is asked of no one, so [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md) never comes and [the Discourse](piece_the_discourse.md) hardens on an understanding almost none of its members hold.)

## Targets

- [ ] each member holds a firm view and defends it with reasons across the divide
- [ ] the square sorts into opposed and certain camps
- [ ] no one is asked to trace the mechanism, the disagreement sharpening on reasons alone
- [ ] the discourse hardens on a felt understanding almost no member could actually trace, each side sincere and no one lying
