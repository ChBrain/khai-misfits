# Persona: The Public

## Taxonomy

[Public](position_public.md). The whole body of the discourse, firm and opposed convictions on mechanisms almost none of its members could trace, polarising as each side trades reasons, sincere throughout, the collective the misfit is drawn down through.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Projection

Engaged. Opinionated. Sincere on every side.
Is the public square of a hundred thousand Knowers, each with a firm view on how the policy or the system works, each fluent in reasons, each certain, arguing in earnest across the divide.
No mob in it and no liars: only a great many people, each sincerely feeling they understand, holding views in good faith on how things work.

## Action

Argues from conviction, on [Argue from Conviction](plan_argue_from_conviction.md), through [Polarizing](process_polarizing.md) in [the Forum](place_the_forum.md), each member holding a [Position](piece_the_position.md) on a [Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) they do not trace.
Trades reasons across the divide and never [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md), so the disagreement sharpens without ever testing the understanding beneath it, hardening into [the Discourse](piece_the_discourse.md).
Does what a public square does when the mechanism is never asked: argues louder and firmer on convictions no one is called to show, and sorts itself into opposed and certain camps.

## Shadow

Not a foolish crowd, a sincere one arguing from a depth that is not there, and that is the difficulty: nothing is wrong with any member and the whole discourse still runs on understanding it does not have.
Because each member holds a felt grasp they are never asked to trace and defends it with reasons that never test it, the Public can polarise entirely without any member being a fool or a liar, the collective conviction firm and the collective understanding thin, hardened by exactly the arguing that feels like getting to the bottom of it.
It is made of nothing but sincere and able people, and being made of nothing but sincere and able people is exactly why its discourse rests on so little.

## Tell

Speaks in the confident voice of a public that has debated a thing to death and surely understands it by now.
Cannot show that the debating was all reasons and never mechanism, that the understanding it argues from was never once traced by almost anyone in it, and that the certainty on every side is one unasked question from the same collapse.
