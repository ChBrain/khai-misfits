# Place: The Whiteboard

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). Where the mechanism must be drawn out step by step, the one place the how is actually asked for. Hosts [Show Me](plot_show_me.md) and [The Blank](plot_the_blank.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

The request to trace, not to justify: walk me through, step by step, how it actually gets from here to there.
An attempt begun in the same confident voice as the Exchange, the marker moving, the first steps coming easily, because the opening of a mechanism often looks exactly like knowing what the thing does.

## Holds

[The Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md), demanded plainly and without hostility, and [the Collapse](piece_the_collapse.md) that follows it, the point where [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) meets [the Shallows](piece_the_shallows.md) and the next step will not come.
Here the position cannot answer with a reason, because no reason was asked for, only the how, so whatever the Whiteboard holds at the end is exactly what was actually there.

## Offers

The one honest reading the illusion ever gets.
Nowhere else does the felt grasp meet a request it cannot satisfy with fluency alone, so the Whiteboard is the single place a person's sense of understanding and their actual understanding are made to stand side by side.

## Withheld

The comfort of the reasons-argument.
No side is asked for here, no rebuttal invited, so the position cannot hide behind a justification: it must produce the mechanism or run out partway, and either way the outcome is not a matter of who argued better.
