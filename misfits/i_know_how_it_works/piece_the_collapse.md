# Piece: The Collapse

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The fall of the felt grasp when the tracing fails: the fluency stopping partway, the self-rating dropping sharply, the certainty gone.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Occurs at [the Whiteboard](place_the_whiteboard.md), in the middle of an attempt begun in the same confident voice as [the Exchange](place_the_exchange.md), the moment the marker stops moving.

## Load Bearing

The collapse is the measured effect behind the whole misfit, and it is well founded: asked to explain a mechanism step by step, people who moments before rated their understanding as strong watch the explanation run out partway and, asked again, rate their own understanding markedly lower, a drop specific to explanatory tasks (Rozenblit and Keil). That much is the reliable core, and it is load bearing on its own: the certainty falls, reliably, the instant the mechanism is actually asked for. Guard carefully against reaching further than the evidence does. Whether the strong or extreme position built on top of the felt grasp also softens as the certainty under it falls is Fernbach and colleagues' further and more contested claim, found in part and not fully replicated since, so it belongs here hedged: the collapse of the felt understanding is the solid ground, the accompanying ease in the position is the part that sometimes follows and sometimes does not.

## Apparent

A stumble, a momentary loss for words, nothing more than needing a second to think.
It can look, from outside and even to the person inside it, like a pause before the explanation resumes, and only when the resumption does not come does the pause reveal itself as the floor giving way rather than a breath being taken.
