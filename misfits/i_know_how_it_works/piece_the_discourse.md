# Piece: The Discourse

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The collective outcome: a polity holding firm, opposed convictions on mechanisms almost none of its members could trace, polarizing as each side trades reasons, each side sincere, no one lying.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Rendered in [the Forum](place_the_forum.md) through [Polarizing](process_polarizing.md), the public square a great many private exchanges sum to.

## Load Bearing

The discourse is where the illusion stops being one person's private overestimate and becomes a social pattern: the same illusion of understanding that supports a single strong position, multiplied across a public that trades reasons across the divide and never the mechanism, is put forward as contributing to political polarization (Fernbach, Rogers, Fox, and Sloman), standing on the general illusion of explanatory depth beneath every individual position in it (Rozenblit and Keil). It is load bearing because it is the scale at which the misfit's stakes become public rather than personal, a whole polity certain on mechanisms it has, collectively, never traced. Guard against overclaiming here in two directions at once: the contribution to polarization is the more contested layer of the underlying research, offered as an application rather than a settled result, and the discourse itself is not evidence that its members are foolish, only that the one question capable of testing any of their positions is not the question the discourse asks.

## Apparent

A public that has debated the issue thoroughly and, by the volume and duration of the arguing, has surely gotten to the bottom of it.
The reading is honest as far as it goes: a great many reasons really were exchanged, at length, by sincere and able people, and that record can look indistinguishable from an examined understanding until it is noticed that reasons were all that was ever asked for.
