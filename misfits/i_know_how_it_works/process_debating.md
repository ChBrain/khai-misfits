# Process: Debating

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). Run by [The Opponent](persona_the_opponent.md) on [Trade Reasons](plan_trade_reasons.md), at [The Exchange](place_the_exchange.md), in [Trading Reasons](plot_trading_reasons.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A position stated, and an Opponent who disagrees and says why.
Two views, two sets of reasons, and an exchange that begins the way arguments usually begin: with a case and a case against it, no mechanism named on either side.

## Direction

Trading reasons and counter-reasons, never asking the how.
Each side answers the other's justification with a justification of its own, the [Position](piece_the_position.md) on each side met and pressed and never once brought to [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md) it would need to actually settle anything.

## Lever

Opposition by reasons is exactly the pressure the felt grasp is built to withstand, and defending a position under that pressure can harden it further, so the fiercest disagreement leaves both understandings exactly as thin as they began (Fernbach, Rogers, Fox, and Sloman).

## Echo

Both convictions firmer, both illusions intact, the mechanism never reached by either side.
The debate ends the way it started, with two confident positions, except each has now survived a contest and reads its survival as vindication, one more round of reasons mistaken for one round closer to the how.
