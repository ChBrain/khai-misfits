# Position: Knower

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The seat of the one who feels they understand the mechanism and holds a firm view on it, sincere and never tested, whose felt grasp runs far ahead of the mechanism it could actually produce.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

A vivid sense of understanding and a view built on it: the standing to hold and defend a position, and a felt grasp of how the thing works that feels detailed, coherent, and complete.
An honest case: the feeling of understanding is genuine and sincerely held, and knowing what the thing does is real, so the seat holds its view in good faith.
No actual mechanism assembled behind the feeling, only the feeling, and no occasion in ordinary life that ever asks the seat to produce the how rather than the reasons.

## Orders

Hold the view.
Feel the understanding, take the firm position it supports, and defend it with reasons and a side, because that is what argument asks for and the felt grasp supplies without effort.
Never trace the mechanism, not from evasion but because no one asks it, so the grasp is confirmed by every exchange and tested by none.

## Loses

The certainty, the moment the mechanism is finally asked for.
Because the seat's confidence rests on a felt grasp and not an assembled one, it holds firm only so long as it is asked for reasons, and gives the whole of its certainty up the instant it is asked to trace the how and cannot, the middle of the understanding meeting a blank.
It keeps its conviction for years on an understanding never examined, and loses it in a minute when the one unasked question is finally put.

## Drives

Toward a firm view resting on a grasp that is not there,
held in good faith and confirmed by every argument and tested by none,
so the seat that sincerely feels it understands is the one the collapse waits on, always a single unasked mechanism away.
