# Plan: Trade Reasons

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the opponent's seat](position_opponent.md), inside [I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Opponent](position_opponent.md)

## Direction

Meet the position across from you with counter-reasons, argue the case, rebut the other, and press your side as hard as the reasons allow. Ask for a justification and never for the mechanism, because that is how the argument is fought, and so leave both understandings untested and, in the defending, both convictions firmer than before.

## Orders

([The Opponent](persona_the_opponent.md) meets the Knower's [Position](piece_the_position.md) with counter-reasons through [Debating](process_debating.md), never with [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md), so both felt grasps go untouched.)

## Implementation

([The Exchange](place_the_exchange.md) runs on reasons and a side; the [debating](process_debating.md) process trades justifications and never asks the how, so [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) on both sides withstands the pressure and hardens, the mechanism never reached and [the Collapse](piece_the_collapse.md) never brought on.)

## Targets

- [ ] a justification is demanded and never the step-by-step mechanism
