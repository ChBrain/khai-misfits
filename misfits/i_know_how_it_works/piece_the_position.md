# Piece: The Position

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The confident, often strong or extreme stance held on the felt grasp: a firm view resting on an understanding that is not there, and one that defending with reasons only hardens.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Place

Held by [the Knower](persona_the_knower.md), hardened through [Debating](process_debating.md) against [the Opponent](persona_the_opponent.md), at [the Exchange](place_the_exchange.md).

## Load Bearing

The position is what turns a private illusion into a public and consequential one: strong and even extreme stances on policy are supported by an illusion of understanding the mechanism, and asking for reasons rather than for the mechanism does not test that support, it exercises it, so each round of justification can leave the position firmer than before, not merely unchanged (Fernbach, Rogers, Fox, and Sloman). It is load bearing because it is the visible, consequential layer of the misfit, the thing other people actually meet and argue with, while the felt grasp underneath stays invisible until asked for directly. Guard against treating the position itself as the error. The view can be reasonable, even correct, and still be held at a depth of understanding the holder has not actually got: the fault the misfit stages is not the position but the felt grasp it is resting on, unexamined.

## Apparent

Conviction earned by argument: a view that has survived every challenge put to it and so has been proven out.
Every reason it has answered reads as a test passed, and an unbroken record of reasons answered looks, from inside the exchange, exactly like an examined understanding, though the exchange asked only for reasons and never once for the mechanism the position claims to rest on.
