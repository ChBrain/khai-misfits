# Place: The Forum

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The collective public square, where a whole polity argues from conviction and polarizes. Hosts [No One Lying](plot_no_one_lying.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Shown

Opposed and certain camps, sincere on every side, trading reasons across the divide.
A great many exchanges at once, each one a Knower meeting an Opponent, each one fluent, each one convinced, summing to a square that looks, from any single seat in it, like a debate that has been thoroughly had.

## Holds

[The Discourse](piece_the_discourse.md): firm convictions on mechanisms almost no member could trace, hardened by exactly the arguing that feels like getting to the bottom of them.
Here the Forum holds what every Exchange inside it holds, a position on a felt grasp, multiplied by every member and never once summed against the mechanism any of it claims.

## Offers

The appearance of a settled understanding.
A view debated at this scale, this long, by this many sincere and able people, reads as thoroughly examined, and the reading is honest as far as it goes: only reasons were ever asked for, and reasons were supplied in full.

## Withheld

The mechanism, asked of no one.
So the Forum can polarize entirely without any member being a fool or a liar, the certainty on every side never once tested, the collapse that would meet it at [the Whiteboard](place_the_whiteboard.md) simply never convened here.
