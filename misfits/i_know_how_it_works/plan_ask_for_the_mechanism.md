# Plan: Ask for the Mechanism

## Taxonomy

A **standing plan**: the intent of [the questioner's seat](position_questioner.md), inside [I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md).

## Owner

- Owner: [Questioner](position_questioner.md)

## Direction

Do not counter the position or trade a reason, ask instead for the step-by-step how, the account of the way the thing actually gets from here to there. Put the one question the felt grasp cannot answer, and accept that it will be met more often with discomfort than with thanks, because the request breaks the etiquette of an argument built on reasons.

## Orders

([The Questioner](persona_the_questioner.md) lays down [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md) through [Questioning](process_questioning.md), asking for the mechanism rather than the reasons, and brings on [the Collapse](piece_the_collapse.md).)

## Implementation

([The Whiteboard](place_the_whiteboard.md) is where the how must be drawn out; the [questioning](process_questioning.md) process asks for the mechanism and not the justification, so [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) meets [the Shallows](piece_the_shallows.md) and [the Collapse](piece_the_collapse.md) follows, the one solvent the discourse is built not to offer.)

## Targets

- [ ] the request is for the step-by-step mechanism, not for reasons or a side
- [ ] the question is put in good faith, to test the how and not to score a point
- [ ] the one question the felt grasp cannot answer is placed on the table
- [ ] the ask is understood to be awkward and rarely welcome, the effective move the discourse rarely makes
