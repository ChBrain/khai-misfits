# Plot: Show Me

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Cue

[The Whiteboard](place_the_whiteboard.md), where the one awkward question finally gets asked.
A marker is in someone's hand, or offered to the Knower's, and the room shifts from taking sides to being asked, plainly, to explain.

## Action

[Questioning](process_questioning.md): [The Questioner](persona_the_questioner.md), on [Ask for the Mechanism](plan_ask_for_the_mechanism.md), holds [the Questioner's seat](position_questioner.md) and asks not for reasons but for [the Tracing](piece_the_tracing.md): walk me through, step by step, how it actually gets from here to there.

## Stage

The Questioner does not counter the Knower's view or take the other side; there is no rebuttal in the request, no side being argued. The ask is quieter than anything at [the Exchange](place_the_exchange.md) and stranger for it: not why do you believe this, but how does it actually work, in order, from the first step to the last. The Knower, still in the confident register of a moment ago, agrees, because agreeing is easy and the felt grasp gives no warning that anything here is different from what came before.

## Tension

The demand is now on the table, and it is not a challenge to the position, only a request to trace the how, the one question [the Felt Grasp](piece_the_felt_grasp.md) has never once been put to. It lands, more often, as discomfort than as welcome: the room was built for reasons and a side, and this is neither, so the Questioner's plain and patient ask sits oddly in it, the rare move a discourse of justifications was never set up to receive well.
