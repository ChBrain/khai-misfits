# Position: Questioner

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). The seat that asks for the mechanism rather than the reasons, holding the one move that punctures the illusion and the awkwardness that keeps it rarely made.

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Has

The one effective question and little standing to ask it: the request to trace the mechanism step by step, and no ordinary occasion that welcomes it.
A clean case: the seat wants the how, not a point, and asks in good faith for the causal account the position was never made to show.
No easy way to make the ask, because it is slow, uncomfortable, and the opposite of how argument runs, so the seat holds the solvent and rarely gets to use it.

## Orders

Ask for the mechanism.
Do not counter the position or trade a reason, ask instead for the step-by-step how, the account of the way the thing actually gets from here to there.
Put the one question the felt grasp cannot answer, and accept that it will be met more often with discomfort than with thanks.

## Loses

The comfort of the room, and much of the chance to ask at all.
Because the request for the mechanism breaks the etiquette of an argument built on reasons, the seat pays in awkwardness for making it and is invited to make it almost never, so what it surrenders is the ease of a discourse that would rather trade justifications than trace a how.
It holds the one move that works and gives up, to the awkwardness of it, nearly every occasion to make the move.

## Drives

Toward a fix that is real and almost never applied,
the one question that punctures the illusion sitting unwelcome and unasked,
so the seat that could right-size any conviction is the seat the discourse is least built to make room for, effective when tried and absent nearly everywhere.
