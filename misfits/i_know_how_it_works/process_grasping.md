# Process: Grasping

## Taxonomy

[I Know How It Works](play_i_know_how_it_works.md). Run by [The Knower](persona_the_knower.md) on [Hold the View](plan_hold_the_view.md), at [The Exchange](place_the_exchange.md), in [The Strong View](plot_the_strong_view.md).

## Owner

- Project: khai-misfits

## Initiated by

A question about how something works: a bicycle, a policy, a device used daily and never opened.
Asked for a view, the Knower reaches for the sense of understanding it, and the sense arrives at once, vivid and entire.

## Direction

The forming of the felt understanding, into a position held with confidence.
Knowing what the thing does, having seen it work, having used it, hardens into a feeling of knowing how it does it, and the feeling is fluent enough to carry a firm, defensible view without ever being unpacked.

## Lever

The mind produces the feeling of understanding a mechanism whether or not the mechanism is there, and knowing-what is easily taken for knowing-how, most of all for explanatory knowledge, stronger here than for facts, procedures, or stories (Rozenblit and Keil). Nothing in the Exchange asks the feeling to prove itself, so it does not have to.

## Echo

A firm position, held in good faith, on a grasp that was never actually assembled.
The Knower leaves the Exchange as certain as they arrived, the felt grasp confirmed by having gone unchallenged, and carries that certainty into every reason they will trade next, the assembly still not built, the confidence in it untouched.
